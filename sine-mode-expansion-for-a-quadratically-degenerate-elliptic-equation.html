Sine-mode expansion for a quadratically degenerate elliptic equation (bn​(y)=An​y(1+1+4n2​)/2)

For uxx​+y2uyy​=0 with zero vertical-side values on 0<x<π, the Fourier coefficient bn​(y) obeys the Cauchy-Euler differential equation y2bn′′​=n2bn​. Boundedness at y=0 removes the negative indicial root, leaving bn​(y)=An​y(1+1+4n2​)/2. Bounded top coefficients give absolute convergence of the Fourier sine series below the top boundary.
