Hard sphere (U(R)=∞ (R<d),U(R)=0 (R≥d))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 227 Articles: 4
A hard sphere has an impenetrable core and otherwise no pair interaction. Two identical spheres of diameter d cannot have centre separation below d. Even without attractive potential energy, crowding can produce fluid–crystal coexistence because available configuration space and entropy differ between the phases.
