Scalar spin-spin coupling

 Home Chemistry Nuclear magnetic resonance
OurBigBook.com Words: 549 Articles: 23
Through-bond interaction between nuclear spins that splits NMR resonances. In the weak-coupling limit, n equivalent spin-21​ neighbours produce n+1 lines with binomial coefficient intensities. Equivalent nuclei do not split each other.
