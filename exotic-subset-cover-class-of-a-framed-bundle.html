Exotic subset-cover class of a framed bundle (lk​(η)=(pk​)!​(1))

OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The degree-zero framed cobordism class is defined by the transfer of the subset cover of a framed vector bundle. With the canonical relative framing it equals (kn​)⋅1 for a globally ordered framing of rank n. Put l0​=1 and lk​=0 outside the rank range. Disjoint-union additivity and fiber-product multiplicativity of transfer give lk​(η⊕ζ)=∑i+j=k​li​(η)lj​(ζ).
