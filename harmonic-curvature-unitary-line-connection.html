Harmonic-curvature unitary line connection (iFA​=η)

 ... Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle) Metric connection Unitary connection
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
The real curvature iFA​ of a unitary line connection has a fixed de Rham class. Let η be its harmonic representative and write η−iFA​=dβ. Then A−iβ has curvature η. Any two such connections differ by a closed imaginary one-form. If b1​=0, that form is exact and comes from a circle-valued gauge transformation. In general their gauge classes form a torsor for H1(X;R)/(2πH1(X;Z)).
