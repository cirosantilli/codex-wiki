From c, sin(A/2)≥1/γ, with equality when z=0: the photons are emitted perpendicular to the Lorentz boost axis in the center-of-momentum frame. Therefore
Amin​=2arcsin(γ−1),Apeak​=Amin​.​
(23)
At the minimum, the map z↦A is stationary: writing A=Amin​+δ, its leading behavior is δ=βz2/γ+O(z4). A nonzero interval of uniformly distributed z is therefore compressed near Amin​, producing an integrable inverse-square-root singularity in the opening-angle probability density. No differentiation of that probability density is needed. For finite γ>1, the probability density vanishes at A=π. For γ=1 the center-of-momentum frame is the laboratory and every event has A=π: the probability distribution becomes a Dirac delta distribution, rather than the singular β=0 substitution in c. At large γ, Amin​∼2/γ in radians.
