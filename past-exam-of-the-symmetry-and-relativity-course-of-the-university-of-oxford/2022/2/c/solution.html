In the center-of-momentum frame, four-momentum conservation gives two opposite photon momenta, each of energy Mπ​/2. Let z=cosθ∗​, with the axis along the Lorentz boost from that frame to the laboratory. Reversing the axis specified in the source changes z to −z and has no effect on the following product. The Lorentz transformation of four-momentum gives
E1​=2γMπ​​(1+βz),E2​=2γMπ​​(1−βz).

The isotropy assumption is dN=a∣dz∣ on −1≤z≤1. Each interior opening angle has two inverse branches, z and −z, both of which must enter the change of variables for probability densities. The positive branch has derivative
​dAdz​​=2βγsin2(A/2)γ2sin2(A/2)−1​cos(A/2)​.
(21)
Consequently the diphoton opening-angle distribution is
dAdN​=γa​βsin2(A/2)γ2sin2(A/2)−1​cos(A/2)​,f(γ)=γ−1.​
(22)
Its support is 2arcsin(1/γ)<A<π for 0<β<1, and its integral is 2a, the integral of the original angular probability density before normalization. Thus the normalized probability density is (2a)−1dN/dA. Omitting one inverse branch would incorrectly halve the total count.
