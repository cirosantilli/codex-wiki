The extra term is vT∗xx​, the rest-frame stress multiplied by the velocity. This shows why treating stored field energy as an unstressed parcel moving rigidly with the plates misses part of the energy transport.

The electric field is parallel to the Lorentz boost, so F01=E/c remains unchanged and B=0. The cancellation between T∗00​ and T∗11​ gives
Tμν=u0​diag(1,−1,1,1),u=u0​,S=0.​
(38)
There is no Poynting flux in the ideal interior in this orientation, although the region containing the electric field moves. The longitudinal negative stress contribution cancels the convective term: Sx​=γ2v(u0​+T∗xx​)=0. Field energy is removed at the receding boundary and supplied at the advancing boundary by work on the charged plates; it need not flow through the interior to accompany the moving region. At edges, omitted fringing fields provide additional local structure.
For a finite idealized interplate rest volume V0​, length contraction gives V=V0​/γ. The field-only integrated laboratory energies would consequently be Uxy​=γ(1+β2)U0​ and Uyz​=U0​/γ, with U0​=u0​V0​. Their different transformation laws are not paradoxical: the field alone is not a closed system. Include the plates and the supports holding them apart, whose mechanical stresses balance the Maxwell stress tensor. The total closed-system four-momentum then has the ordinary Lorentz transformation law. This is the same distinction between a subsystem and a conserved whole that appears in b.
