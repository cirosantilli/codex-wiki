With c=1, the total four-momentum is Ptot​=∑i​Pi​. A sum of Lorentz four-vectors transforms as a four-vector, so
s2=Ptot​⋅Ptot​=(i∑​Wi​)2−​i∑​pi​​2​
(20)
is a Lorentz invariant. Its value is the squared invariant mass of the complete system, irrespective of whether its constituents interact. Isolation makes the total four-momentum constant in time.
For a photon gas, write pi​=Wi​ni​ with ∣ni​∣=1. Its individual null norms do not make the total norm vanish:
s2=2i<j∑​Wi​Wj​(1−ni​⋅nj​)≥0.​
(21)
For an isotropic gas in its momentum rest frame, ∑i​pi​=0 and s2=Wtot2​. Zero is possible only when all nonzero photons propagate in the same direction. Two equal-energy photons at right angles, for example, give s2=2W2, rather than zero. Without an angular distribution, the pairwise expression is the general answer; isotropy is an additional assumption.
