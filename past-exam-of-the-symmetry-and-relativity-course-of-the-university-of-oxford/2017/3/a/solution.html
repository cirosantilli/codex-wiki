Three relevant observable effects are the relativistic Doppler effect in spectral-line shifts, including the transverse time-dilation contribution; relativistic aberration, observable as a change of apparent light direction with observer motion; and relativistic beaming of moving emitters, associated with strongly asymmetric jet brightness. Apparent superluminal motion supplies a related light-travel-time test: vapp​/c=βsinϑ/(1−βcosϑ) can exceed one without a material speed exceeding c. These interpretations require an emission and source-motion model; a cosmological or gravitational redshift by itself is not a direct test of special-relativistic kinematics.
