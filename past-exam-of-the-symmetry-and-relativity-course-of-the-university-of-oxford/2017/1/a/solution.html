For a massive particle on a timelike world line, the four-velocity is Uμ=dXμ/dτ=γ(c,v), with U2=c2. Its four-momentum is Pμ=mUμ=(E/c,p) and its four-force is Fμ=dPμ/dτ. Here τ is proper time. A massless trajectory does not admit this massive-particle proper-time parametrization.
The asserted classification of arbitrary orthogonal vectors is false. With the chosen Minkowski metric, D=(0,1,0,0) and G=(0,0,1,0) are nonzero, orthogonal and both spacelike vectors. Also D=(1,1,0,0) and G=2D are orthogonal null vectors. The valid statement is that a nonzero vector orthogonal to a timelike vector is spacelike. To prove it, choose the rest frame of the timelike vector, D=(d,0,0,0); then D⋅G=dG0=0 forces G0=0, so G2=−∣G∣2<0.
The relativistic energy-momentum relation gives
P⋅P=c2E2​−∣p∣2=m2c2.
(1)
Therefore P2=0 ⟹ m=0​. A photon is an example: E=c∣p∣. A collection of photons need not itself have zero invariant mass.
