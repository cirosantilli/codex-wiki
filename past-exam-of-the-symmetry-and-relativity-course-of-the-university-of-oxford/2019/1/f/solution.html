For causal reachability with periodic time, let Pt​=2πT. The lower-right panel of part c identifies its top and bottom edges. The vertical curve at fixed x is a closed timelike curve: after a positive proper duration Pt​, it returns to the same spacetime event. The quotient is a mathematically well-defined flat Lorentzian manifold, but it violates chronology and admits no usual global causal ordering or ordinary global initial-value evolution.
These are physical causality problems, rather than an algebraic contradiction in the metric. Fields and particle histories must satisfy periodic consistency conditions; an arbitrary initial state followed by irreversible evolution will generally fail to return to itself after a temporal lap. The fact that the causal past and causal future overlap everywhere, established below, explains why the ordinary three-way causal partition degenerates.
