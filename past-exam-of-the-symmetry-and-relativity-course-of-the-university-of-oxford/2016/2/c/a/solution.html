Each photon has energy ε=ℏω. Their total four-momentum is
P=cε​(2,1+cosθ,sinθ,0).
(18)
Its squared invariant is P2=2ε2(1−cosθ)/c2. Therefore the system's rest energy is
Mc2=Etot2​−c2∣ptot​∣2​=2ℏω​sin2θ​​.​
(19)
Two individually massless particles can thus have positive total invariant mass. Back-to-back photons give rest energy 2ℏω, whereas exactly parallel photons have zero invariant mass and no massive-system rest frame.
