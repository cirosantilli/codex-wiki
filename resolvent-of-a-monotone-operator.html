Resolvent of a monotone operator (JτA​=(I+τA)−1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Monotone operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For an operator A, its resolvent with step τ>0 is JτA​=(I+τA)−1. A maximal monotone operator has an everywhere-defined single-valued firmly nonexpansive mapping as its resolvent. For A=∂f, the resolvent is the proximal map of τf. This monotone-operator resolvent uses a different convention from the spectral resolvent of a linear operator.
