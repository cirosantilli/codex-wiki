Conway polynomial (knot theory, ∇L​(z))

The Conway polynomial is normalized by ∇U​=1 for the unknot, zero for a split unlink with more than one component, and the skein relation ∇L+​​−∇L−​​=z∇L0​​. For a knot, the symmetric Alexander polynomial with value one at t=1 is ΔK​(t)=∇K​(t1/2−t−1/2). This fixes the Laurent-unit ambiguity of the Seifert-matrix presentation of the Alexander module.
