Connectivity (graph theory)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 5
Connectivity studies the existence of paths between vertices of a graph and the persistence of paths after vertex or edge deletions. A connected graph is the basic case in which every pair of vertices is linked by a path.
