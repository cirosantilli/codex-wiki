Boundary-pressure virial theorem. Multiply hydrostatic equilibrium by 4πr3 and integrate. The left side is [4πr3p]0r0​​−3∫0r0​​4πr2pdr, while the right side is Ω. The central boundary contribution vanishes when r3p→0 and the outer pressure is p(r0​)=p0​. Thus
3∫ρp​dM+Ω=4πr03​p0​=3p0​V​.
(5)
A finite external pressure is compatible with a gravitationally self-contained cloud, but is not the zero-surface-force notion of isolation. External ram stress or mass flow would require additional terms.
radiation budget of monatomic cloud collapse. For an ideal gas with fixed γ, the specific internal energy is u=p/[ρ(γ−1)]. Therefore the pressure integral is 3(γ−1)U. Cold monatomic hydrogen has γ=5/3 when only translational degrees of freedom matter. With negligible final surface pressure, 2U+Ω=0, so Ef​=U+Ω=Ω/2. If the initial cloud is sufficiently extended and cold that Ei​≃0, the emitted energy is
Erad​=Ei​−Ef​≃−2Ω​>0​.
(6)
Being initially at rest alone does not set its binding energy to zero. If the initial total energy is appreciable, retain Erad​=Ei​−Ω/2; a different equation of state also changes the half-energy result.
Inside-out accretion. The combination of aT​ and G with dimensions of mass per time is
M˙=CGaT3​​​,
(7)
where dimensional analysis leaves the dimensionless coefficient C undetermined. An isothermal gas keeps aT​ constant, giving constant supply in the self-similar idealization. A front at radius aT​t and an isothermal mass scale M(r)∼aT2​r/G give the same scaling. The front's swept mass is not automatically the accreted central mass; the coefficient requires a dynamical similarity solution.[ref]
constant-radius accretion energy budget. If all available accretion power is radiated at the fixed radius, Lacc​=GMM˙/r0​. Neglecting the seed mass,
∫Lacc​dt=∫0M​r0​GM′​dM′=2r0​GM2​.
(8)
This equals −Ω/2 precisely when
Ω=−r0​GM2​​.
(9)
The positive sign in the final printed energy condition is inconsistent with the earlier use of negative gravitational potential energy. Equivalently its positive right side must denote the binding-energy magnitude ∣Ω∣. A general structural coefficient Ω=−qGM2/r0​ leaves a mismatch unless q=1 under these other idealizations. Radiation trapped in the object, outflows or release over a different radius also change the budget.
Which temperature? With M=Ms​+M˙t and fixed r0​, Lacc​∝M. If this power emerges thermally from area 4πr02​, the accretion-powered effective-temperature scaling is
Teff​=(4πσSB​r03​GMM˙​)1/4 ∝ t1/4​
(10)
when the seed mass is negligible. This is the radiating surface's effective temperature, inferred from the preceding accretion-luminosity calculation. A homologous ideal-gas interior instead has virial temperature Tc​∼μmH​GM/(kB​r0​)∝t. Thus the surface-effective and interior-virial scalings are t1/4 and t, respectively, under their stated assumptions. Inferring a detailed interior temperature profile would additionally require a structural and transport model.
