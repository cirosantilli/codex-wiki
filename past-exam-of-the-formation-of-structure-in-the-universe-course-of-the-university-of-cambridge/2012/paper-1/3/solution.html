The normalized cycloid has
Rm​Rs​​=21−cosη​,tm​t​=πη−sinη​.
(14)
The initially expanding perturbation reaches maximum radius at η=π, t=tm​, then formally collapses to zero radius at η=2π, t=2tm​. A finite virialized object is assigned the conventional formation epoch tf​=2tm​; random motions replace the pressureless point-collapse singularity. The cycloid alone does not describe that relaxation.
For the standard top-hat virialization estimate, write U=−CG​GM2/R and retain the same gravitational structure factor CG​ at turnaround and final equilibrium. The coherent kinetic energy vanishes at turnaround, whereas the virial theorem gives 2Kv​+Uv​=0 in the final isolated equilibrium. conservation of energy gives
Um​=Kv​+Uv​=2Uv​​,Rv​=Rm​/2​.
(15)
The final density is eight times the turnaround density. Meanwhile ρˉ​∝a−3∝t−2, so ρˉ​(tf​)=ρˉ​(tm​)/4. The Einstein-de Sitter virial overdensity follows by combining these factors with the supplied turnaround contrast:
Δf​=8×4(43π​)2=18π2≃177.65≈200,​ρv​=Δf​ρ0​(1+zf​)3.​
(16)
The value 200 is a convenient rounded overdensity convention, not exactly the Einstein-de Sitter result.
The virial radius of a dark-matter halo follows from Mv​=4πRv3​ρv​/3:
Rv​=(4πΔf​ρ0​3Mv​​)1/31+zf​1​.​
(17)
The hydrostatic temperature of an inverse-square halo follows from hydrostatic equilibrium and the ideal gas law:
drdP​=−ρg​r2GM(r)​,P=μρg​kB​Tv​​.
(18)
Since ρg​∝r−2 and M(r)=Mv​r/Rv​, cancellation of ρg​/r gives 2kB​Tv​/μ=GMv​/Rv​. Therefore
Tv​=2kB​Rv​GMv​μ​=2kB​GMv2/3​μ​(34πΔf​ρ0​​)1/3(1+zf​).​
(19)
Here μ is a dimensionful mean particle mass, as specified in the paper; a dimensionless mean molecular weight would need multiplication by a particle-mass unit. The isothermal interior is a further halo idealization, not an exact continuation of the uniform top-hat profile used in the virial-overdensity estimate. Changing its gravitational structure factor in a literal energy comparison would change the turnaround-radius relation.
The formation redshift from halo mean density uses the cluster data, which imply ρv​=3Mv​/(4πRv3​)=5.37148×1013M⊙​Mpc−3. Thus, using the requested rounded convention,
1+zf​=(4πRv3​Δf​ρ0​3Mv​​)1/3=1.91908,zf​≃0.92(Δf​=200).​
(20)
Using 18π2 instead gives zf​≃1.00. In the Einstein-de Sitter time scaling the rounded-convention result is tf​/t0​=(1+zf​)−3/2≃0.376. Directly from the mass and radius,
Tv​=2.34478×108(mp​μ​)K.​
(21)
For the estimate, take mean particle mass μ=0.6mp​, appropriate approximately to a hydrogen-helium mixture with complete ionization. This gives Tv​≃1.41×108K, or kB​Tv​≃12.1keV. The composition choice is additional input, not a value supplied by the examination; the boxed expression retains the result for any μ.
