At distance r, one grain intercepts power through its projected area and emits through its full surface:
Pabs​=πa24πr2L​,Pem​=4πa2σSB​Td4​.
(11)
The blackbody dust sublimation radius follows by setting Td​=Tsub​:
rsub​=(16πσSB​Tsub4​L​)1/2≃1.62×1015m≃0.0526pc.​
(12)
Here the supplied luminosity is 3.8×1037W. The grain radius cancels because both cross-sections scale as a2. The temperature exterior to this boundary scales as Td​∝r−1/2. This estimate assumes isotropic illumination, no shielding, and unit absorption/emission efficiency as specified; real grain emissivities can change it. The numerical evaluation uses the external conversion values σSB​≃5.6704×10−8Wm−2K−4 and 1pc≃3.0857×1016m.[ref]
