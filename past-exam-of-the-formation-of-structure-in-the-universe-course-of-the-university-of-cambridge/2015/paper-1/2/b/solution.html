For inside-out collapse of a singular isothermal sphere, the expansion front is at rf​=aT​t. The original mass swept by this front is
Mswept​=G2aT2​rf​​=G2aT3​t​.
(8)
Thus the only time-independent accretion scale is aT3​/G. The front-sweeping estimate has coefficient two, but gas takes time to fall from the front to the object; it is not an exact central accretion coefficient. Write
M˙⋆​=m0​GaT3​​∝(μmH​kB​T​)3/2∝T3/2,​
(9)
for fixed mean particle mass μmH​. Determining the dimensionless coefficient m0​ requires the collapse solution; it is not fixed by front propagation alone and is not needed for the requested temperature scaling.[ref]
Well inside the front, where the point mass dominates and infall is approximately pressureless and locally steady, ∣vr​∣≃2GM⋆​/r​. Conserving the radial mass flux gives the free-fall density profile around a growing point mass:
ρ(r,t)=4πr2∣vr​∣M˙⋆​​≃4π2GM⋆​(t)​M˙⋆​​r−3/2.​
(10)
The inner slope is −3/2, versus −2 in the undisturbed outer cloud. For constant M˙⋆​ and negligible initial object mass, M⋆​∝t and the inner normalization varies as t−1/2. These are inner asymptotes, not an assertion of an abrupt exact power-law join at the front.
