Stabilizing terms. In the given local fluid dispersion relation, κ2 is rotational support, important at long wavelength; a02​k2 is random-motion or pressure support, important at short wavelength. long-wave and short-wave branches at marginal Toomre stability. For Q=1 and n≥1,

(14)
These endpoints are the inner and outer Lindblad resonance conditions. The stated dispersion relation does not restrict every wave solution to that interval. Its plus-sign short-wavelength branch has q>0 even outside it: for example, ΩP​=Ω+2κ/n gives q=3κ/a0​ and satisfies the equation. Additional branch selection or resonant absorption physics would be needed for an absolute confinement claim. No such condition is supplied.
Toomre stability radius of a flat-rotation disk. With Ω=v0​/r, κ=2​v0​/r. Using G=4.3009×10−3 pc(kms−1)2M⊙−1​,

The supplied local model therefore predicts stable radii below about 4.19 kpc, marginality at that radius, and axisymmetric instability farther out while its assumed constant surface density and thin-disk approximation remain applicable. An infinite constant-density, flat-rotation disk is an idealization, not a global self-consistent galaxy model.
