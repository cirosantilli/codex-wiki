p0​=r03​A​−r04​B​,A=4π3cs2​M0​​,B=20π3GM02​​​.

then decreases toward zero at large radius. Since p0′​=(4B−3Ar0​)/r05​, a cloud displaced outward at fixed external pressure has a restoring pressure deficit only on the descending branch. Thus the homologous, isothermal radial mode is stable for r0​>rc​, unstable for rz​<r0​<rc​, and marginal at rc​. The negative-pressure branch is not a physical equilibrium with a positive confining pressure. This conclusion uses the stipulated gravitational-energy approximation, not the exact isothermal density profile.

mass and luminosity weighting of a stellar initial mass function. Impose finite birth-mass cutoffs ml​,mu​. The stellar birth mass and main-sequence luminosity scale as
Mborn​=0.35ξ0​​(ml−0.35​−mu−0.35​),Ltot​∝2.65ξ0​​(mu2.65​−ml2.65​).

exponential closed-box gas consumption. The gross formation rate is Ψ=ϵMg​, but the permanent locking rate is αΨ. The instantaneous recycling approximation and mass conservation give M˙g​=−αϵMg​, M˙∗​=αϵMg​. Therefore
Mg​=M0​e−αϵt,M∗​=M0​(1−e−αϵt),fg​=e−αϵt​.
(6)
Here M∗​ means surviving locked stellar mass in the galactic gas fraction, not the cumulative gross mass that has passed through star formation.
locked-mass and returned-mass yield conventions. Pre-existing metals are locked at rate αZΨ; the stipulated newly produced metal source is b(1−α)Ψ. Thus
dtd(Mg​Z)​=−αZΨ+b(1−α)Ψ,Mg​Z˙=b(1−α)Ψ.
(7)
For α>0 and initially pristine gas,
Z=ylnMg​M0​​=ylnfg​1​,y=αb(1−α)​,Z(t)=b(1−α)ϵt​.
(8)
The effective yield y is per unit permanently locked mass; it is not the source parameter b. For α=0, Mg​=M0​ and the direct time equation gives Z=bϵt. For α=1, the explicitly stipulated metal source vanishes, so this particular enrichment model gives Z=0. Constant yield is a trace-enrichment approximation; the formal logarithmic divergence as gas is exhausted must not be interpreted as a physical metal fraction exceeding one.
backward extrapolation of exponential closed-box star formation. Denote the present age of the universe by tnow​. With the last paragraph's α=1,
Ψ(z≃3)=(1M⊙​yr−1)exp[0.5(Gyrtnow​​−2)]​.
(9)
Adopting a present age of the universe tnow​=13.8Gyr for the estimate gives about 365M⊙​yr−1, requiring 7.3×1011M⊙​ of gas at that epoch in the same model. The boxed expression makes the dependence on the adopted present age explicit. This is an extreme history for a present Milky-Way-like system, although intense high-redshift star formation by itself is not forbidden.
The extrapolation forces a fixed gas reservoir to decline exponentially over many gas-depletion times. Gas accretion, outflows, evolving gas density and star-formation efficiency, delayed recycling, and galaxy assembly break that assumption. The empirical disk-averaged relation is not a universal constant-efficiency law for all gas: the original measurements combined Balmer series H-alpha, atomic gas, carbon monoxide and infrared data, with tracer calibration and dust corrections entering the inference.[ref] Cosmological calculations explicitly support continuing cold-gas supply to rapidly forming high-redshift galaxies, providing a physical alternative to this closed-box backward extrapolation.[ref]
