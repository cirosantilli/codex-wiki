Furstenberg multiple recurrence theorem (μ(⋂j=0k−1​T−jnB)>0)

 ... Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Ergodic theory Measure-preserving transformation Poincaré recurrence theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 2
For a probability measure-preserving system, every measurable B of positive measure and every integer k≥2 admit an integer n≥1 with μ(⋂j=0k−1​T−jnB)>0. The sets are preimages; neither invertibility nor an ergodic transformation is required. The Furstenberg correspondence principle converts this simultaneous return into arithmetic progressions and yields the Szemerédi theorem. The case k=2 follows from the Poincaré recurrence theorem.
