Flux-lattice spacing (a=2Φ0​/(3​B)​)

Assume a triangular array of straight, singly quantized Abrikosov vortices with nearest-neighbor distance a. Here B>0 is the magnitude of the spatially averaged internal magnetic field, and Φ0​=h/(2e) is the superconducting flux quantum; e>0 is the elementary charge. Two lattice vectors of length a meeting at 60∘ span area Ac​=3​a2/2. One flux quantum per cell gives
BAc​=Φ0​,nv​=Φ0​B​,a=3​B2Φ0​​​.
(8)
Thus multiplying B by five decreases a by 5​; it does not multiply the flux of each vortex by five. Using the exact SI Planck constant and elementary charge, Φ0​≃2.06783×10−15Wb.[ref]
For chosen internal fields 0.20T and 1.0T, the spacings are approximately 109nm and 48.9nm. These are geometric model calculations, not measurements from a particular sample. The local magnetic field varies within a cell; replacing B by the field at a core would not give this counting relation.
