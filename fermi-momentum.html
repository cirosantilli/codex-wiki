Fermi momentum (pF​)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Fermi-Dirac distribution Fermi gas Fermi energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Fermi momentum is the momentum at the boundary of occupied one-particle states in a zero-temperature Fermi gas. For spin-one-half particles in three dimensions, counting the two spin states gives pF​=ℏ(3π2n)1/3, where n is number density.
