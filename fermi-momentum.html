Fermi momentum (pF​)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Fermi-Dirac distribution Fermi gas Fermi energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 281 Articles: 3
Fermi momentum is the magnitude of the momentum at the boundary of filled one-particle states in an isotropic zero-temperature Fermi gas. For the uniform three-dimensional two-component electron model, a short state count gives
N≃2(2πℏ)3V​34πpF3​​,n=3π2ℏ3pF3​​,pF​=ℏ(3π2n)1/3.
(20)
To see the factors, impose periodic boundary conditions on a cubic box of side L, so V=L3 and each momentum component is pi​=2πℏji​/L for an integer ji​. Each allowed momentum point occupies a cell of volume (2πℏ/L)3 in momentum space. Divide the sphere volume 4πpF3​/3 by this cell volume and multiply by two for the two spin states. Replacing the discrete count with a volume is the large-box approximation; surface and shell effects are neglected.[ref]
The factor two is not automatic for every Fermi gas. If only one internal state is populated, its number density nσ​ instead satisfies pF,σ​=ℏ(6π2nσ​)1/3. For two equally populated states, nσ​=n/2 recovers the displayed result. A Fermi surface in a crystal can have a more complicated shape, so a single spherical Fermi momentum is a property of this isotropic model.
