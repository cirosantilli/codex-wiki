Radio astronomy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Observational astronomy
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 30
Radio astronomy studies celestial sources through their radio waves. The long wavelengths make angular resolution expensive: a filled telescope of diameter D has a characteristic diffraction scale of order λ/D. Aperture synthesis instead combines measurements from separated antennas, using their separations to measure fine structure while their individual collecting areas determine how much radiation they receive. A kilometre-wide arrangement of small antennas does not collect the power intercepted by a filled kilometre-wide dish.
