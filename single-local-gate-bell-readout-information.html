Single-local-gate Bell readout information (I=1−h2​((1+nz​)/2))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bell state Superdense coding
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For four uniformly distributed Bell states, measure one qubit along Bloch vector n and the other along z. The sign within each Φ± or Ψ± pair is invisible and the displayed mutual information is at most one bit. A local Hadamard gate before a computational detector changes nz​ from one to zero and destroys the remaining information. An entangling Bell decoder falls outside this product-measurement restriction and recovers two bits.
