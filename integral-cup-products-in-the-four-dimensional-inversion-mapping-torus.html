Integral cup products in the four-dimensional inversion mapping torus (H∗(M4​;Z))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Mapping torus Inversion mapping torus of a torus Integral cohomology of an inversion mapping torus
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
Choose the base class a, degree-two torsion classes τi​=β​(xi​), and degree-two free lifts bij​ reducing to xi​xj​. A generator κ of the top Z/2 reduces to tx1​x2​x3​. The mod-two cohomology ring of an inversion mapping torus determines products in top integral degree, since reduction there is injective. The products of distinct b12​,b13​,b23​ equal κ; so does bij​τk​ when i,j,k are all distinct. Their other degree-four products vanish. Also aτi​=0 because degree-three integral cohomology is torsion-free.
