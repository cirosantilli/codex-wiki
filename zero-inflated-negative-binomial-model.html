Zero-inflated negative binomial model

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Zero inflation
OurBigBook.com Words: 235 Articles: 3
With structural-zero probability π, size r>0 and count mean λ, the model assigns π+(1−π)(r/(r+λ))r to zero and (1−π)fNB​(y;r,λ) to positive counts. Its expectation is (1−π)λ and its variance is (1−π)(λ+λ2/r)+π(1−π)λ2, by the law of total variance. A logarithmic link function can relate the count-component mean to predictors.
