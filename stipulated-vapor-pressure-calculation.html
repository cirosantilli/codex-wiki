Consider an unnamed model substance with molar latent heat Lm​=40.0kJmol−1 and saturation pressure p1​=10.0kPa at T1​=300K. Stipulate the ideal-vapor Clausius-Clapeyron approximation throughout 280≤T≤340K, constant Lm​, and R=8.314Jmol−1K−1. These parameters define an illustrative model, not a measured dataset or a fit to water. At T2​=330K,
lnp1​p2​​=8.31440000​(3001​−3301​)≃1.458,p2​≃42.97kPa.

At the reference state the gas molar volume is RT1​/p1​=0.24942m3mol−1, and the local slope is dp/dT≃0.5346kPaK−1. The tangent estimate p2​≈10.0+30(0.5346)=26.04kPa is much lower than the integrated result: a local derivative should not be extrapolated over this whole interval. By contrast, over a small temperature increment, the fractional sensitivity is dln(p/p1​)/dT=Lm​/(RT12​)≃0.05346K−1.
