Pyramid (V=Ah/3)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Polyhedron
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 1
A pyramid is a polyhedron with a polygon as its base and triangular faces joining the base edges to one apex outside the base plane. If its base area is A and perpendicular height is h, its volume is Ah/3. At height z above the base, similarity gives cross-sectional area A(1−z/h)2. Integrating this area from 0 to h proves the formula.
