Three-isogeny descent

OurBigBook.com Words: 37
For a degree-three isogeny whose kernel becomes μ3​ under the Weil pairing, the Kummer connecting map embeds the quotient by the isogeny into K×/(K×)3. Valuations outside the primes of bad reduction restrict its image to an S-unramified power class group.
