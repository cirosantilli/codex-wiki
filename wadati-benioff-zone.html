Wadati-Benioff zone

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Seismology Seismicity
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
An inclined distribution of earthquake sources associated with a descending slab. Shallow interface earthquakes and deeper intraslab earthquakes need not share the same failure mechanism. The pattern helps trace subduction, subject to the limits of source location and the distribution of seismically active material. Named for Kiyoo Wadati and Hugo Benioff.
