Complete one-loop connected cubic three-point graphs

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram Connected Feynman diagram
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
For a real phi cubed theory with labelled external points, the connected three-point function at third order in the coupling constant has seven labelled one-loop Feynman diagrams: one triangle, three external-branch bubble insertions, and three tadpole-ended chains. The symmetry factors are respectively 1,2,2, with graph weight 1/S. To prove completeness, use 3V=2I+3 and L=I−V+1: V=3 gives I=3 and L=1. The unique internal cycle is then a triangle, a pair of parallel edges, or a self-loop; the degree-three condition fixes the external attachments. Only the triangle remains one-particle irreducible after amputation of external propagators. A tadpole-free prescription removes the three chains but must be stated separately.
