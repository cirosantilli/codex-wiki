Transvections generate the special linear group

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Transvection Elementary transvection matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Row additions reduce an invertible matrix to diagonal form without changing determinant. A determinant-one diagonal matrix is a product of diagonal two-coordinate blocks diag(a,a−1), each expressible as a product of six elementary transvection matrices. Therefore these matrices generate the special linear group over a field.
