Normal form theorem for an amalgamated free product

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Bass-Serre theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 1
In A∗C​B, an alternating product of elements of A∖C and B∖C is nonidentity. More generally, choosing coset representatives gives a unique reduced normal form.
This is the amalgamated case of normal form for free groups and free product of groups.
