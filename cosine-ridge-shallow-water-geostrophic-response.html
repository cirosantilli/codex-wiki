For a uniform upstream current whose free-surface and bottom slopes compensate, steady quasi-geostrophic approximation flow with uniform incoming potential vorticity obeys the displayed inversion equation. Let b=ϵcos(x/L) on ∣x∣<a=πL/2 and zero outside. With C=(fϵ/h0​)/(L−2+LR−2​), the decaying even response is ϕ=C[cos(x/L)+(LR​/L)e−a/LR​cosh(x/LR​)] inside and ϕ=C(LR​/L)e−a/LR​cosh(a/LR​)e−(∣x∣−a)/LR​ outside. Continuity of ϕ and ϕ′ fixes the coefficients. Its streamlines satisfy y=y∞​+ϕ(x)/U, so the response extends beyond the compact ridge through exponentially decaying balanced tails.
