PI3K regulatory subunit p85

 Home Biology Enzyme Phosphoinositide 3-kinase
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
A regulatory subunit associated with a class I phosphoinositide 3-kinase complex. A band of its approximate mass does not establish its identity or whether a detected phosphorylation is activating.
