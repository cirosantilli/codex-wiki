Laurent series field (k((t)))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field
OurBigBook.com Words: 301 Articles: 4
The Laurent series field k((t)) consists of formal series ∑n≥N​an​tn with finitely many negative-degree terms. Its t-adic valuation is the least exponent with nonzero coefficient.
