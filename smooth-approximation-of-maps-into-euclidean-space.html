Smooth approximation of maps into Euclidean space

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Smooth manifold Smooth map between manifolds
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
A continuous map from a smooth manifold to Rd can be approximated by a smooth map with any prescribed positive continuous pointwise error bound. In coordinate charts, smoothing by local convolution and then combining with a smooth partition of unity gives the approximation. On a compact subset of an open chart domain, a smooth cutoff allows the resulting straight-line homotopy to be supported in that domain. This permits the Sard theorem to be used after approximation; the original continuous map can be space-filling and need not omit a point.
