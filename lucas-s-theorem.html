Lucas's theorem ((kn​)≡∏i​(ki​ni​​)(modp))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Binomial coefficient
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For a prime number p, the binomial coefficient modulo p factors into the corresponding digit binomial coefficients in the base-p expansions of the nonnegative integers n,k. Pad both expansions to the same length and use (ba​)=0 when b>a. The identity follows by factoring (1+x)n into digit powers and using the Frobenius endomorphism; digit uniqueness identifies each coefficient.
