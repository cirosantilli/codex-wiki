Marginal density (fX​(x)=∫fX,Y​(x,y)dy)

OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A marginal density is a probability density function for one component of a joint probability distribution. Integrating the joint probability density over the other coordinates gives it; for two components, fX​(x)=∫fX,Y​(x,y)dy.
