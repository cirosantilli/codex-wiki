Barker equation

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Kepler orbit Parabolic trajectory
OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 2
For a parabolic trajectory with periapsis radius rp​, put D=tan(f/2), where f is true anomaly. The Barker equation is t−tp​=2rp3​/(GM)​(D+D3/3). Since r=rp​(1+D2), it is equivalent to the radius-time relation for a parabolic Kepler orbit. The sign of D distinguishes approach from recession; tp​ is the periapsis time.
