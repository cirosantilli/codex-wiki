Summable-memory coupling (∥q(⋅∣η)−q(⋅∣η′)∥TV​≤br​,∑r​br​<∞)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Coupling
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
Suppose a finite-alphabet transition kernel is uniformly positive and histories agreeing in their most recent r symbols have total variation distance at most br​. Choose decreasing bounds with br​<1 and summable tail. A maximal coupling can be dominated by a match-length chain that increments with probability 1−br​ and resets with probability br​. Its chance of never resetting is ∏r≥0​(1−br​)>0. Successive failed attempts therefore end almost surely, leaving a final infinite run of matches. The dominating chain does not depend on the histories, so future-block laws forget the remote past uniformly. This proves uniqueness of a two-sided law with the prescribed transition kernel without assuming stationarity first.
