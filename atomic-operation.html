Atomic operation

 Home Computer science Operating system Process (computing) Thread (computing) Synchronization (computing)
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 2
An operation that takes effect indivisibly relative to the specified competing accesses. An atomic increment protects the whole read-modify-write; separate atomic loads and stores do not make their combined sequence an atomic operation.
