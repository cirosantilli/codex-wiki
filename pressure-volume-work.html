Pressure-volume work (δW=PdV)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Energy Work
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 1
The work done by a uniform pressure P on an expanding boundary is PdV. With internal energy U, the first law of thermodynamics for an adiabatically expanding region reads dU=−PdV. For a homogeneous cosmological perfect fluid, U=ρV and V∝a3 give the cosmological continuity equation ρ˙​+3H(ρ+P)=0.
