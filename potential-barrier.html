Potential barrier

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 50
A potential barrier is a region where the potential energy exceeds that in the surrounding regions.
A rectangular potential barrier is the piecewise-constant special case used for exact transmission and reflection calculations.
