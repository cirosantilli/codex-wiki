Cosine (cos)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Trigonometric function
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 1
For a real angle θ, cosθ is the horizontal coordinate on the unit circle reached by a counterclockwise rotation through θ radians. For an acute angle in a right triangle, it is the adjacent side divided by the hypotenuse. Basic values include
cos0=1,cos(π/3)=21​,cos(π/2)=0,cosπ=−1.
(68)
At θ=2π/3, the horizontal coordinate is −1/2 even though the vertical sine coordinate remains positive. A positive angle need not produce a positive cosine.
Reflection across the horizontal axis leaves this coordinate unchanged, giving cos(−θ)=cosθ: cosine is an even function. A full turn gives cos(θ+2π)=cosθ. For real arguments, its range is [−1,1] and its zeros of a function occur at θ=π/2+kπ. A quarter-turn shifts between the two coordinate traces, with cosθ=sin(θ+π/2).
