Linearize around A=ReiqX and write r=R2>0. For sideband wavenumber l, the sum and difference of the two amplitude sidebands, together with the field amplitude b, evolve under
Ml​=​−l2−2r−2ql−μrl2​−2ql−l20​−20−σl2​​.
(26)
Expanding det(λI−Ml​) gives
(λ+σl2)[(λ+l2)(λ+l2+2r)−4q2l2]−2μrl2(λ+l2)=0.
(27)
At l=0, the eigenvalues are −2r,0,0; the field's constant mode must be removed if its mean is prescribed. Nonzero long-wave field modes are still dynamically active.
