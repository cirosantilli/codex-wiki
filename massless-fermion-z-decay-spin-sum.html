For the Z boson vertex gγμ(cv​−ca​γ5)/(2cW​), sum over the three initial polarizations and final fermion spins. The symmetric Dirac trace is 4(cv2​+ca2​)(k1μ​k2ν​+k1ν​k2μ​−gμνk1​⋅k2​). The vector-axial interference is antisymmetric and drops out of the massive vector polarization sum. The longitudinal term drops out by current conservation for massless final particles. Contracting the remaining trace gives the displayed result. The massive-vector spin average divides it by three; two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space then gives Γ=g2MZ​(cv2​+ca2​)/(48πcW2​) per color. These coefficients are twice those used with a vertex prefactor g/cW​.
