Surface coverage (θ)

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Adsorption Adsorption site
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
In a one-molecule-per-adsorption site description, surface coverage is θ=Noccupied​/Nsites​, with 0≤θ≤1. For a macroscopic sample at chemical equilibrium, θ usually denotes the mean occupied fraction. Inferring it from total uptake also requires the capacity of a complete monolayer.
