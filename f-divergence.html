f-divergence (Df​(P∥Q))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 35
For a convex function f satisfying f(1)=0, the f-divergence between probability distributions P and Q is
Df​(P∥Q)=∫qf(p/q)dλ,
(22)
where p=dP/dλ, q=dQ/dλ, and the integrand uses the lower-semicontinuous perspective convention at q=0.
