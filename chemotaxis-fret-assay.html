Chemotaxis FRET assay

 Home Biology Signal transduction Signaling feedback Negative feedback Chemotaxis feedback regulation
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
The chemotaxis FRET assay uses Förster resonance energy transfer between fluorescent tags on CheY and CheZ to report their association. Interpreting this emission ratio as CheA activity requires the coupling between CheY phosphorylation and CheZ binding described in the experimental sources above.
