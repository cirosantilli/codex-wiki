Reading frame

 ... Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Gene expression Translation (biology) Genetic code
OurBigBook.com Words: 370 Articles: 5
A reading frame specifies how consecutive nucleotides are grouped into codons. Starting at position 1 of AUGGCUUAA gives AUG / GCU / UAA; starting at position 2 gives UGG / CUU with AA left over. The letters are unchanged but the groups differ. A possible grouping alone does not establish an actual biological translation initiation site.
For the twelve-nucleotide example, number positions from the 5′ end. The three possible forward reading frames start at positions 1, 2 and 3:
1:2:3:​AUGGCUUUCUGA,UGGCUUUCU(GA),GGCUUUCUG(A).​
(14)
Parentheses enclose incomplete trailing groups. The second and third rows do not assert initiation of biological translation in a biological cell. A chosen start fixes which positions belong together; the ribosome then advances through consecutive codons rather than searching each three-letter window independently.
