Completion of a number field at a prime ideal (Kp​)

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Absolute value on a field Extension of an absolute value Local factorization and extended absolute values
OurBigBook.com Words: 181 Articles: 5
For a number field K and a nonzero prime ideal p⊂OK​, the completion Kp​ is the completion for the p-adic absolute value. It is a finite extension of Qp​, where pZ=p∩Z.
