Partition regular matrix

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Ramsey theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 270 Articles: 8
A rational matrix A is partition regular when every finite coloring of the positive integers admits a nonzero monochromatic vector x satisfying Ax=0.
