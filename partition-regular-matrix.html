Partition regular matrix

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Ramsey theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 19
A matrix A over the rational numbers is partition regular when every finite coloring of the positive integers admits a monochromatic vector x satisfying Ax=0, with every coordinate a positive integer. Repeated coordinates are permitted.
