Strong epimorphism

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Epimorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 350 Articles: 7
A strong epimorphism has the left lifting property with respect to every monomorphism. Thus every commutative square with the strong epimorphism on the left and a monomorphism on the right has a diagonal filler.
