Donor-wind angular-momentum loss (J˙=(1−f)M˙d​ad2​Ω)

 ... Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Binary star Roche lobe Roche-lobe overflow Nonconservative binary mass transfer
OurBigBook.com Words: 435 Articles: 6
An isotropic escaping stellar wind has the donor star's mean specific orbital angular momentum. With M˙d​<0, retained fraction f, negligible spin and no additional wind torque, J˙=(1−f)M˙d​ad2​Ω. The retained matter redistributes angular momentum internally. Unlike isotropic re-emission from a binary star, the escaping material originates at the donor rather than the accretor. With no retention this reduces to Jeans-mode mass loss.
