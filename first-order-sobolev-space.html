First-order Sobolev space (W1,p(U))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space
OurBigBook.com Words: 961 Articles: 20
For an open set U⊆Rn and 1≤p≤∞, the first-order Sobolev space is
W1,p(U)={u∈Lp(U):Di​u∈Lp(U) for 1≤i≤n},
(8)
where each Di​u is a weak derivative. Its standard norm is ∥u∥W1,p​=∥u∥p​+∑i​∥Di​u∥p​.
