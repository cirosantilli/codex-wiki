Markov chain state (Xt​=i)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
A possible value of a Markov chain at one time. The collection of such values is its state space. A pair of molecule counts is one Markov chain state when two chemical species are tracked together.
