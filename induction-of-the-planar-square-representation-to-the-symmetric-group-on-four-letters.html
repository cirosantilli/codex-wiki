Induction of the planar square representation to the symmetric group on four letters (IndD8​S4​​W≅C⊕(C⊗sgn))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Induced representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
Embed the order-eight dihedral group as H=⟨(1234),(12)(34)⟩ in the symmetric group S4​, and let W be its two-dimensional complex representation obtained from its square action. Let C be the three-dimensional complex representation from the rotational symmetry group of a cube. In the class order 1,(12),(12)(34),(123),(1234), the induced character is (6,0,−2,0,0): only the identity and the square's half-turn have nonzero character values. This equals the character of C⊗(1⊕sgn), since χC​=(3,−1,−1,0,1). Characters distinguish finite-dimensional complex semisimple representations, giving the displayed isomorphism. Its trivial character multiplicity is (6−6)/24=0.
