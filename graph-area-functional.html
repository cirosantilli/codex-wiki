Graph area functional (F(u)=∫Ω​1+∣∇u∣2​dx)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Second fundamental form Fundamental forms of a graph surface Minimal surface equation for a graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
This functional measures the surface area of the graph of a function. On a bounded domain it is finite for u∈H1. Its directional derivative is ∂v​F(u)=∫∇u⋅∇v/1+∣∇u∣2​, by the dominated convergence theorem, since the pointwise difference quotients are bounded by ∣∇v∣. The resulting dual-valued operator is monotone: the scalar integrand p↦1+∣p∣2​ is convex and its gradient is p/1+∣p∣2​. It is not a nonlinear coercive operator on the full H1 space, since it vanishes on constant functions whose H1 norms can tend to infinity.
