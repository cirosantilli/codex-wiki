Walsh-Hadamard transform (H⊗n)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Hadamard transform
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
On n qubits, the Walsh-Hadamard transform satisfies
H⊗n∣x⟩=2−n/2∑y∈{0,1}n​(−1)x⋅y∣y⟩,
(3)
where the dot product is evaluated modulo two.
This is the Hadamard transform acting on the computational basis of n qubits.
