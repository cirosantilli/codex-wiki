Gödel-Löb logic (GL)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Modal logic Provability logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 484 Articles: 7
The Gödel-Löb logic has propositional tautologies, □(A→B)→(□A→□B) and □(□A→A)→□A as axiom schemes. Its rules are modus ponens rule and the necessitation rule; uniform propositional substitution is built into the schemes. Under an arithmetic realization, each axiom and each rule preserves provability in a theory with the Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions and diagonalization. This proves arithmetic soundness by induction on derivations; it is distinct from claiming that every true arithmetic sentence is provable.
