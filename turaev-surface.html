Turaev surface (2−2gT​(D)=sA​+sB​−c(D))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Link Link diagram
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
Replace each crossing of a diagram by a saddle joining its A smoothing above the projection sphere to its B smoothing below; cap the upper and lower state circles. For a connected classical link diagram this constructs a connected closed orientable surface. Its Euler characteristic is sA​+sB​−c(D), so sA​+sB​≤c(D)+2. For an alternating diagram the surface has genus zero.
