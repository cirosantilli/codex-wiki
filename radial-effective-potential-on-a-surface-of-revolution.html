Radial effective potential on a surface of revolution (Uh​(r)=h2/(2r2)+gf(r))

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Effective potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
A particle constrained to a stationary smooth surface of revolution z=f(r) under uniform gravity conserves h=r2θ˙ and mechanical energy per unit mass. Substitute z˙=f′(r)r˙ and θ˙=h/r2 into that energy to obtain
21​[1+f′(r)2]r˙2+Uh​(r)=E.
(9)
This proves the radial reduction. Simple roots of E−Uh​ are turning points; a stationary point Uh′​(r0​)=0 supports an exact circular orbit. At a local maximum of Uh​, another trajectory at the same energy can approach the orbit asymptotically as a separatrix. Existence of roots elsewhere is insufficient for confinement: their dynamically accessible ordering relative to the launch radius matters.
