Ionic strength (Ic​=21​∑i​ci​zi2​)

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry)
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
The concentration-based ionic strength is Ic​=21​∑i​ci​zi2​, where ci​ is each dissolved ion's concentration and zi​ its signed electric charge number. It has the same units as ci​; some formulas instead use the dimensionless ratio Ic​/c∘. All dissolved ions contribute, including those that balance the electric charge of a buffer solution's conjugate base. Ionic strength helps describe the interactions represented by activity coefficients. See Hippler and Metcalfe, activity coefficients and dimensionless ionic strength.
