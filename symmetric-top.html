Symmetric top

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 355 Articles: 9
A symmetric top is a rigid body with two equal principal moments of inertia, conventionally I1​=I2​. Its third principal axis is its symmetry axis.
