Integer multiplication map on the circle (TK​(x)=Kx(mod1))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Ergodic theory Measure-preserving transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 65 Articles: 1
For an integer K≥2, multiplication by K on the circle group preserves normalized Lebesgue measure. Each inverse image consists of K branches, each scaling lengths by 1/K.
