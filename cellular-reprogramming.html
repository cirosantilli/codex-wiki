Cellular reprogramming

 Home Biology Organism Multicellular organism Developmental integration Cellular differentiation
OurBigBook.com Words: 607 Articles: 3
Cellular reprogramming changes an established cell state so that a different developmental program becomes accessible. Experimentally induced cellular reprogramming must be distinguished from the ordinary progression of cell differentiation. An ovum's cytoplasm can reset aspects of a transferred cell nucleus; this does not mean that a specialized donor normally returns to an early developmental state in its original surroundings. [ref]
