Vertical ascent under linear drag (T=(m/α)log(1+αu0​/(mg)))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Drag (physics) Linear drag
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For constant downward gravitational acceleration g and linear drag −αv, the upward velocity solves mv˙=−mg−αv with v(0)=u0​≥0. Its first zero occurs at the displayed time. The dimensionless control variable is αu0​/(mg), and the limit α→0 gives u0​/g.
