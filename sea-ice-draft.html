Sea-ice draft (h)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Sea ice
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
Sea-ice draft is the depth of the ice underside below the local water surface. Total thickness also includes the part above the water. A sea-ice pressure ridge has a spatially variable draft, and its peak draft differs from its mean along a track.
