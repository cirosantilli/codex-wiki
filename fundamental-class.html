Fundamental class ([M])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Cohomology Cap product
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 26
For an oriented closed connected d-manifold, the fundamental class is the unique generator of Hd​(M;Z) that restricts to the orientation generator in every local homology group.
