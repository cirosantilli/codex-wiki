Left and right translation on a Lie group (Lg​,Rg​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 4
For a Lie group G, left and right translation are the diffeomorphisms Lg​(h)=gh and Rg​(h)=hg. They commute: Lg​Rh​=Rh​Lg​.
