Free sphere actions exclude elementary abelian subgroups of rank two (Cp​×Cp​↷free​Sn(n>1))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group action Free group action
OurBigBook.com Words: 94
Suppose a finite group G acts freely on Sn, n>1. The quotient has fundamental group G and higher homotopy groups equal to those of Sn. Attach one (n+1)-cell killing a generator of πn​≅Z, then attach higher cells successively to kill all remaining higher homotopy. This constructs a Eilenberg–MacLane space K(G,1) with only one (n+1)-cell. The one-cell bound on next-degree cohomology gives dimension at most one in degree n+1. But if G=Cp​×Cp​, the mod-p cohomology ring of a cyclic group and Künneth theorem give dimension n+2 there. This contradiction excludes that group, and also excludes such a subgroup of any group acting freely on a sphere.
