Product of scalar superfields

 ... Branches of physics Supersymmetry Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry Superspace Superfield Scalar superfield transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A product of scalar superfields is a scalar superfield: the pullback of a product is the product of the pullbacks at the same transformed superspace point. Infinitesimally, the even supersymmetry variation obeys δ(ST)=(δS)T+S(δT). This follows from the graded Leibniz rule for the odd supercharges, after including their odd parameters.
