Ribosome profiling

 Home Biology Biology laboratory technique
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 4
Sequencing of RNA fragments protected by ribosomes to measure their positions on transcripts. Occupancy measures a combination of entry, passage and dwell time; more footprints can reflect slower elongation rather than more completed protein.
