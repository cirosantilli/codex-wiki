Finite lifetime threshold for a power diffusion (P(T<∞)=1{α<1}​(0<α≤1))

OurBigBook.com Words: 87
For the positive-domain power diffusion with 0<α≤1, assuming the lifetime is the limit of the hitting times of 1/n, it is finite almost surely exactly when α<1. For α<1, the Itô formula gives
d(X1−α)=(1−α)dB−2X1−αα(1−α)​dt.
(19)
The negative drift bounds this positive process above by X01−α​+(1−α)B, whose first hit of zero is finite by recurrence of one-dimensional Brownian motion. The lifetime must precede that hit. At α=1, Xt​=X0​eBt​−t/2 is positive and finite on every compact time interval, so the lifetime is infinite. Approaching zero only as t→∞ is not a finite boundary lifetime.
