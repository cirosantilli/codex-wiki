Kinetic magnetohydrodynamics (KMHD)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 2
A magnetized long-wavelength plasma description with a common leading perpendicular flow, magnetic flux freezing and a gyrotropic pressure tensor. The kinetic magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation couples to a drift-kinetic equation for each species; parallel plasma pressure and perpendicular plasma pressure are calculated as velocity moments, rather than prescribed by an isotropic adiabatic closure. Relative species drifts enter at higher order in the ratio of gyroradius to macroscopic length.
