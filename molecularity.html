Molecularity

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Chemical kinetics Reaction mechanism Elementary reaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
Molecularity counts the reacting entities in one elementary reaction: one for a unimolecular step, two for a bimolecular step. An entity can be a molecule, ion, or chemical atom. Solvent entities involved only through solvation are excluded. Molecularity describes a microscopic event; order of reaction describes the concentration dependence of a rate law. Assigning molecularity to a net multi-step chemical equation is therefore inappropriate.[ref]
