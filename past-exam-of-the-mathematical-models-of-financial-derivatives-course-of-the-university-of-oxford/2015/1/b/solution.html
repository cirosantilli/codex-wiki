A replicating strategy holds Δ shares and a current cash amount b in the bank account. Matching the two next values gives
ΔSt+1u​+Rb=Vt+1u​,ΔSt+1d​+Rb=Vt+1d​.
(2)
Solving this linear system,
Δ=St+1u​−St+1d​Vt+1u​−Vt+1d​​,b=R(St+1u​−St+1d​)St+1u​Vt+1d​−St+1d​Vt+1u​​.
(3)
By no-arbitrage, the claim costs Vt​=ΔSt​+b. Collecting the coefficients of its two future values yields
Vt​=R−1[qVt+1u​+(1−q)Vt+1d​],q=St+1u​−St+1d​RSt​−St+1d​​.​
(4)
The physical probability p does not appear: replication fixes the price of this contingent claim from traded prices.
