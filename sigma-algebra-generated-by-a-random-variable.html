Sigma-algebra generated by a random variable (σ(X)={X−1(B):B∈B(R)})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Sigma-algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 179 Articles: 1
For a real random variable X on a probability space, σ(X) is the smallest sigma-algebra making X a measurable function into the Borel sigma-algebra on R. The collection of all inverse images of Borel sets is already a sigma-algebra, since inverse images preserve complements and countable unions; this proves the displayed formula without completing any measure.
