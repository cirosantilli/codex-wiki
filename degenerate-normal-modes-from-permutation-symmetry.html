Degenerate normal modes from permutation symmetry

 ... Physics Branch of physics Classical mechanics Lagrangian mechanics Small oscillation Generalized eigenvalue problem for small oscillations
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
If the mass and stiffness matrices commute with a permutation representation, each symmetry subspace is invariant under the generalized eigenvalue problem for small oscillations. On an irreducible subspace, Schur lemma forces a commuting operator to be scalar, producing a degenerate normal mode multiplet.
