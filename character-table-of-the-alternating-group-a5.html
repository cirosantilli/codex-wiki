The class sizes are 1,20,15,12,12. Restricting the degree-four and degree-five irreducible characters of S5​ gives rows of squared character inner product one. The degree-six restriction has squared norm two and is orthogonal to those rows and the trivial row, so it splits into the two remaining irreducibles. The sum of squared degrees forces their degrees to be three. Orthogonality forces their last two values to have sum one and product minus one, producing the displayed roots. Since a normal subgroup is a union of classes and its order divides sixty, these class sizes also prove the Simplicity of the alternating group A5.
