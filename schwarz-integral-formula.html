Schwarz integral formula

The Schwarz integral formula reconstructs a holomorphic function from boundary values of its real part. For a real boundary function f on the complex upper half-plane satisfying ∫R​∣f(s)∣/(1+s2)ds<∞, the regularized form is
W(w)=πi1​∫R​f(s)[s−w1​−1+s2s​]ds.
(39)
Its real part is the Poisson integral of f. The subtraction makes the integral converge and changes only the imaginary normalization. Integration by parts, when the boundary terms vanish, gives W′(w)=(πi)−1∫f′(s)/(s−w)ds; jumps of f are included as Dirac delta function terms in its distributional derivative.
