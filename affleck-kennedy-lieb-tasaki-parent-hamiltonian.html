Affleck--Kennedy--Lieb--Tasaki parent Hamiltonian (AKLT parent Hamiltonian)

OurBigBook.com Words: 38
The AKLT parent Hamiltonian is H=∑j​Pj,j+1(2)​, where P(2) projects two neighboring spin-one particles onto total spin two. It is frustration free, and the periodic AKLT MPS is its unique ground state on a sufficiently long chain.
