Miscibility criterion for a two-component condensate (U11​>0,U22​>0,U11​U22​>U122​)

In a homogeneous overlapping two-component Bose-Einstein condensate, the long-wavelength energy change at fixed species numbers is one half the integral of the density perturbation quadratic form with matrix (U11​U12​​U12​U22​​). Strict positive definiteness gives the displayed criterion. Strong positive cross-repulsion favors separation; sufficiently strong cross-attraction instead produces an unstable joint density mode. Equality is marginal at this order, not strict stability.
