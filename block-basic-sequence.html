Relative to a Schauder basis (ej​), a block basic sequence consists of nonzero finite combinations supported on successive disjoint coordinate intervals. In the l-p sequence space, disjointly supported vectors un​ of norm one satisfy ∥∑an​un​∥pp​=∑∣an​∣p. Their closed span is consequently isometric to the l-p sequence space.
