Characteristic speed (λj​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation System of conservation laws Flux Jacobian
OurBigBook.com Words: 62 Articles: 1
A characteristic speed is an eigenvalue of the flux Jacobian. Along a corresponding characteristic curve, an associated wave variable obeys an ordinary differential equation obtained by projecting the governing system onto the matching left eigenvector.
