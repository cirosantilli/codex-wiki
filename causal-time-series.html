Causal time series

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series
OurBigBook.com Words: 67 Articles: 1
A causal time series can be expressed using present and past innovations, without future innovations. The autoregressive process of order one with ∣α∣<1 has the mean-square convergent causal representation Yt​=∑j≥0​αjεt−j​.
