Two distinguished classes of discriminant minus eight q (D=−8q)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Binary quadratic form Reduced positive definite binary quadratic form Class number of a negative discriminant
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
For an odd prime number q, the primitive positive definite forms
[1,0,2q]and[2,0,q]
(55)
have discriminant −8q and lie in distinct proper equivalence classes. The first represents one, whereas the second has minimum positive value two.
