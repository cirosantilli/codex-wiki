Creation operator (a†)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum harmonic oscillator Creation and annihilation operators
OurBigBook.com Words: 15
A creation operator raises the occupation number operator eigenvalue of a quantum harmonic oscillator by one. Repeated application builds Fock states.
