Samelson product (⟨a,b⟩)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Loop space
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
For sphere maps into a loop space or a group-like homotopy-associative space, take their pointwise commutator. It is trivial on the wedge sum of the two sphere factors, so descends to their smash product and defines ⟨a,b⟩∈πp+q​(ΩY). This product obeys graded antisymmetry and the graded Jacobi identity. Suspension-loop adjunction identifies it with the sign-adjusted Whitehead product. Under the Hurewicz homomorphism it becomes the graded commutator of the Pontryagin product.
