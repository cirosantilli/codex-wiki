Chemical relaxation time (τchem​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Chemical potential Chemical equilibrium
OurBigBook.com Words: 124
The chemical relaxation time estimates how rapidly a perturbed abundance returns toward chemical equilibrium. Comparing it with the eddy mixing time identifies a chemical quench level.
For the A-to-B model in chemical equilibrium, deviations decay by exponential decay with τchem​=1/(kf​+kr​)=2.5s. After one such interval, 1/e of the initial deviation remains; reaching one percent takes ln(100)τchem​≃11.51s. Multiplying both rate constants by two preserves their ratio and the equilibrium composition, but halves τchem​ to 1.25s. The equilibrium exchange rates double to 0.15M/s. Faster exchange therefore coexists with the same final composition and the same free energy minimum.
