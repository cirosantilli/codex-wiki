Period of a Brauer class (per[A]=ord[A])

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Central simple algebra Brauer group
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The period is the order of a class in the Brauer group. It divides its index. Over a local field with finite residue field, the period and index are both the reduced denominator of its local Brauer invariant.
