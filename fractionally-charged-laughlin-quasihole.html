A quasihole adds one zero at η per electron; its local deficit carries positive electric charge e/m.[ref]
Here is a transport counting outline, conditional on an incompressible quantum Hall fluid. Inserting one magnetic flux quantum ΔΦ=h/e slowly compared with ℏ/Δ, where Δ is the excitation energy gap, produces a circulating electric field by Faraday's law. The transverse sheet conductivity sends a radial electric current whose time integral has magnitude
∣ΔQ∣=∣σxy​∣ΔΦ=mhe2​eh​=me​.

The opposite electric charge moves to the boundary, so the total electric charge remains conserved. The counted deficit belongs to a collective quasiparticle; no individual electron has been cut into pieces. The argument assumes the gapped fluid and its Hall response, and is not a consequence of the finite two-electron normalization integral.
