Flavor eigenstate

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
A flavor eigenstate is a state with a definite flavor label in the specified interaction or flavor-charge basis. For neutral kaons, K0=dsˉ and Kˉ0=sdˉ have definite opposite strangeness. Weak interactions mix them, so these flavor states are not the mass eigenstates. The precise flavor basis must be specified when discussing mixing.
