Framed instanton moduli space (dimR​Mkframed​=8k)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field-theory soliton Gauge-theory soliton Yang-Mills instanton Instanton moduli space
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
Fix a trivialization at infinity and divide smooth finite-action instantons only by gauge transformations tending to the identity there. Global gauge orientations remain genuine parameters. For regular SU(2) instantons of charge magnitude k, the real dimension is 8k by the ADHM construction. Forgetting the framing divides by the three-dimensional global group action of SU(2), giving 8k−3 on the irreducible locus. For one instanton the framed parameters are four center coordinates, one positive size, and three orientation coordinates.
