Du Jiao sieve

 Home Computer science Algorithm Number-theoretic sieve
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A Du Jiao sieve computes a summatory arithmetic function using a Dirichlet convolution identity and memoization of repeated quotient arguments. For the Mertens function M(n)=∑k≤n​μ(k), the identity ∑d∣k​μ(d)=[k=1] gives M(n)=1−∑i=2n​M(⌊n/i⌋). Grouping equal quotients and precomputing small arguments makes this recurrence relation useful for large inputs.
