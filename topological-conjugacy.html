Topological conjugacy (H∘f=g∘H)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Topological dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
Two continuous maps are topologically conjugate if a homeomorphism H between their state spaces satisfies the displayed intertwining identity. It is conjugate maps with a topology-preserving conjugating map. On compact metric spaces, pulling back a compatible metric through H preserves every Bowen metric, and hence topological entropy.
