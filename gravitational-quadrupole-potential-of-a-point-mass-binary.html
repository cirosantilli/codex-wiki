Gravitational quadrupole potential of a point-mass binary (Φ2​=−Gμ[3(x⋅r)2−x2r2]/(2x5))

For a binary star viewed far from its centre of mass, let r be the separation and μ the reduced mass, M=M1​+M2​ the total mass, r=∣r∣ and x=∣x∣. The Taylor expansion of the point-mass Newtonian gravitational potential has zero dipole because ∑A​MA​xA​=0. Its second moment is ∑A​MA​xAi​xAj​=μri​rj​, so
Φ=−xGM​−2x5Gμ​[3(x⋅r)2−x2r2]+O(GMr3/x4).
(4)
This holds for an instantaneous separation in any orbit, not only a circular orbit. The mass quadrupole moment convention Qij​=μ(ri​rj​−r2δij​/3) gives Φ2​=−3GQij​xi​xj​/(2x5).
