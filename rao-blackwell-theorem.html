Rao-Blackwell theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 305 Articles: 6
If T is a sufficient statistic and U is an estimator with finite variance, then U∗=E[U∣T] has the same expectation and no greater variance. Indeed, the tower property of conditional expectation preserves the mean and the law of total variance gives
Var(U)=Var(U∗)+E[Var(U∣T)].
