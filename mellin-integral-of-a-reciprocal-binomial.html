Mellin integral of a reciprocal binomial (∫0∞​1+xpxs−1​dx=psin(πs/p)π​)

For real p>0 and 0<s<p, the displayed improper integral converges: its endpoint powers are xs−1 near zero and xs−p−1 near infinity. The change of variables t=xp gives
p1​∫0∞​1+tts/p−1​dt=p1​B(s/p,1−s/p)=pΓ(s/p)Γ(1−s/p)​=psin(πs/p)π​.
(5)
Here the beta function, the beta-gamma identity, and the Gamma reflection formula evaluate the integral. The same formula holds for complex s in the Mellin fundamental strip 0<Res<p, by absolute convergence and analytic continuation within the strip. For integer p, a sector contour of angle 2π/p gives a direct residue theorem proof, with a consistent branch of zs−1.
