Porosity (ϵ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Darcy law Permeability of a porous medium
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
Porosity is the fraction of a representative material volume occupied by pore space. In a saturated medium, it is also the liquid volume fraction; it relates Darcy velocity to the mean pore-fluid velocity.
