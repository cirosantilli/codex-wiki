Small-cell deletion in weighted regularity (∣Vi​∣<εn/M⟹at most εn deleted vertices)

OurBigBook.com Words: 57
A partition with at most M cells has at most εn vertices in cells smaller than εn/M. Deleting all edges incident to those vertices costs at most εn2 edges. Every remaining cell has a fixed positive fraction of all vertices, which permits a regular triangle counting lemma to give a cubic count without requiring equal cell sizes.
