With Bx​=0, a fast simple wave in magnetohydrodynamics has constant uy​ and By​/ρ=K. Its longitudinal velocity satisfies dux​/dρ=±cf​/ρ, and ρt​+(ux​±cf​)ρx​=0. Along this state curve the speed derivative is ±[cf​/ρ+K2/(2μ0​cf​)], which is nonzero. Compressive profiles therefore exhibit shock formation by characteristic intersection, unlike a linearly degenerate characteristic field.
