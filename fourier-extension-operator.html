Fourier extension operator (Eσ​f(x)=∫e−ix⋅ωf(ω)dσ(ω))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Fourier restriction theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 343 Articles: 4
A Fourier extension operator forms an oscillatory integral from a density on a frequency surface with measure σ. The opposite sign in the phase merely reflects the output variable, so it gives identical Lp norms. In the usual dual formulation it is the adjoint of restricting the Fourier transform to that surface.
