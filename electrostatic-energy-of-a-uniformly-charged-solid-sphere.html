Electrostatic energy of a uniformly charged solid sphere (U=20πϵ0​R3Q2​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrostatics Electrostatic energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A solid sphere of radius R and uniform charge density has total electric charge Q. Gauss law gives the radial electric field Er​=Qr/(4πϵ0​R3) inside and Er​=Q/(4πϵ0​r2) outside. The electric potential vanishing at infinity is Q(3−r2/R2)/(8πϵ0​R) inside and Q/(4πϵ0​r) outside. Integrating the electrostatic energy density ϵ0​E2/2 over both regions gives U=3Q2/(20πϵ0​R). In a fixed-density family with Q∝Z and R3∝Z, the electrostatic energy scales as Z5/3.
