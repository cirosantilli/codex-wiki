Metacognition

 Home Biology Animal behaviour
OurBigBook.com Words: 907 Articles: 24
Monitoring or control of one's own cognition. Metacognitive monitoring estimates aspects of current or future performance, while metacognitive control uses such estimates to guide behaviour. Metamemory applies these functions to remembering; uncertainty responses provide a way to study some of them without verbal reports. Successful behaviour alone need not establish introspection or subjective awareness.
