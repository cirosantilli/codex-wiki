Pusey-Barrett-Rudolph theorem (PBR)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Foundations of quantum mechanics Ontological model of a quantum system
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 2
Under preparation independence, an ontological model of a quantum system reproducing exact quantum measurement in quantum mechanics probabilities cannot assign overlapping preparation measures to distinct pure states. An exclusion measurement in quantum mechanics on independent copies makes every outcome impossible for one possible preparation; a common ontic region would require every response to vanish there. Thus the conclusion is a psi-ontic model, not a blanket exclusion of hidden variables or all informational interpretations.
