Helicity invariant of an inviscid shearing wave (H(t)∣k(t)∣2=H(0)(kx2​+kz2​))

For a solenoidal velocity amplitude v with k=(kx​,−Ωtkx​,kz​) and v˙+Ωvy​x=−ikq, differentiating k⋅v=0 gives q=2iΩkx​vy​/K2, where K2=∣k∣2. Thus v˙y​=2Ωkx​ky​vy​/K2 and v˙z​=2Ωkx​kz​vy​/K2. For the real helicity quantity H=ikx​(vy​vz∗​−vz​vy∗​), the mixed forcing terms cancel, leaving H˙=2Ωkx​ky​H/K2=−K˙2H/K2. Consequently
H(t)K(t)2=H(0)(kx2​+kz2​).
(10)
The invariant is a consequence of inviscid linear shear dynamics, not a claim that H itself is constant under the shear.
