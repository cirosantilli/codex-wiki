Silicon microstrip detector

 Home Physics Particle detection Particle detector
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 2
A semiconductor particle detector uses narrow conducting strips on depleted silicon to measure the position of an ionizing particle. The collecting electric field transports the generated electrons and holes; the induced signals identify the traversed strips.
