Jeans instability

Jeans instability is the gravitational growth of density perturbations whose self-gravity overcomes pressure support. For physical sound speed cs​, the comoving threshold is
kJ​=cs​a4πGρˉ​​​.
(9)
Modes with k≪kJ​ grow gravitationally, whereas modes with k≫kJ​ undergo pressure-supported acoustic oscillations.
