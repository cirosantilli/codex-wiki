Perfect-conductor sphere dipole interference (σback​=9πk4a6)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic radiation Rayleigh scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
An electrically small perfect conductor sphere has both an induced electric dipole moment and magnetic dipole moment. The latter contributes half the electric scattering amplitude in magnitude. The two amplitudes add in the backward direction and partially cancel in the forward direction. Thus the backward intensity is 9/4 of the electric-only value and its radar cross section is 9πk4a6. The correction persists at leading order as ka→0; discarding the magnetic term is a separate response approximation.[ref]
