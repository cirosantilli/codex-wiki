Connection components (Γabc​)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle) Affine connection
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For a local basis ea​ of the tangent bundle, an affine connection has components defined by ∇ec​​eb​=Γabc​ea​. If V=Vbeb​, then (∇ec​​V)a=ec​(Va)+Γabc​Vb. The connection components depend on the chosen basis and have an inhomogeneous transformation law; they are not themselves a tensor field. In a coordinate basis, a torsion-free connection has symmetry in the two lower indices.
