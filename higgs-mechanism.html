Higgs mechanism

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Electroweak interaction Higgs field
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 25
In the Higgs mechanism, gauge fields associated with broken generators acquire longitudinal polarizations and masses by absorbing Goldstone modes. Gauge fields of the unbroken subgroup remain massless.
The phrase “absorbing” describes a rearrangement of degrees of freedom, not a collision in which a gauge boson swallows another particle. The gauge symmetry remains a redundancy of the description. A chosen vacuum expectation value leaves a smaller subgroup manifest, while gauge-invariant observables describe the physical masses.
A one-field calculation isolates the mechanism. In an Abelian Higgs model, write a complex scalar field locally as Φ=(v+H)eiθ/2​, use Dμ​=∂μ​−ieAμ​, and define Bμ​=Aμ​−∂μ​θ/e. Then
∣Dμ​Φ∣2=21​(∂μ​H)2+21​e2(v+H)2Bμ​Bμ.
(28)
The second term contains a Proca field mass mB​=ev and its interactions with the radial Higgs boson. A local gauge transformation removes θ from the scalar field but leaves its information in the longitudinal vector polarization. The electroweak symmetry breaking calculation applies this idea to three broken directions.
