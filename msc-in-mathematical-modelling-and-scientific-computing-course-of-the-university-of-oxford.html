Official course description.
Required applied partial differential equations and perturbation methods; choose two or three numerical-analysis cores from numerical solution of partial differential equations, numerical linear algebra and continuous optimisation. At least two special-topic units, including modelling/methods and computation. One scientific-computing case study in Michaelmas term and one mathematical-modelling case study in Hilary term. Compulsory four-unit dissertation, normally written in Trinity term and the summer.
