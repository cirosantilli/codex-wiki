Splitting of an idempotent morphism (sr=e,rs=1H​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Idempotent morphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
An idempotent morphism e:E→E splits if there are r:E→H and s:H→E with sr=e and rs=1H​. The object H is a retract of E. In a functor category, the splitting maps must be natural transformations; objectwise splittings without compatible functorial choices are not enough.
