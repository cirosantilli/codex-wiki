Type-I band alignment

 Home Physics Semiconductor Semiconductor heterostructure Heterojunction
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
A type-I band alignment confines electrons and electron holes together: its inner conduction band edge is lower, its valence band edge higher.[ref]
In the chosen band offset example, the electron barrier is 0.6, while the electron hole barrier is 0.4. A hole's excitation energy is measured downward from a valence band maximum: reversing that convention would incorrectly make the two barriers appear to repel opposite carriers. An electrostatic shift −qϕ moves both electron energy band edges together; changing the band gap also changes their separation. Thus material composition supplies a control beyond the electric potential ϕ alone.
