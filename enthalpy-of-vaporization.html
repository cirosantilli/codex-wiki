Enthalpy of vaporization (ΔHvap​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Phase transition Vaporization
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
The molar enthalpy difference between vapor and liquid at specified conditions, evaluated on coexistence when it is a latent heat. Treating it as constant and treating the vapor as ideal gives ln(P2​/P1​)=(ΔHvap​/R)(1/T1​−1/T2​).
