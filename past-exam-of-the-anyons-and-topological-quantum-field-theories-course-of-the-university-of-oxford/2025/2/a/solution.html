Cut one nonseparating circle on each handle of the closed orientable surface Σg​ of genus g. The cut surface is a sphere with 2g boundary circles. Gluing a pair back together requires an anyon label ai​ on one boundary and the antianyon label aˉi​ on the oppositely oriented boundary. Sum over these labels independently for the g handles.
For a fixed labeling, the dimension of the resulting anyon fusion space on the sphere, with total vacuum anyon charge, is
[Na1​​Naˉ1​​⋯Nag​​Naˉg​​]11​.
(17)
The gluing rule of the topological quantum field theory therefore gives the handle-cut formula for anyon ground-state degeneracy:
GSD(Σg​)=a1​,…,ag​∑​[Na1​​Naˉ1​​⋯Nag​​Naˉg​​]11​=[(a∑​Na​Naˉ​)g]11​.​
(18)
Here 1 denotes the vacuum anyon, and the anyon fusion matrices have entries (Nb​)ac​=Nabc​, as in the source. At g=0, the empty product is the identity matrix, giving one ground state. At g=1, (Na​Naˉ​)11​=1 for each simple charge, so the ground-state degeneracy of the torus is the number of anyon types.
