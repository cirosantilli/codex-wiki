Use the Witten-Reshetikhin-Turaev invariant with the source's normalized Kirby color on all 2g+1 surgery components. Since the signature of the linking matrix is zero, the framing-anomaly factor is one, and the TQFT partition function is
Z(Σg​×S1)=D1​⟨L(Ω,…,Ω)⟩.
(26)
All evaluations in this part use the isotopy normalization of anyon diagrams and preserve the indicated blackboard framing.
Expand the long component as D−1∑a​da​a and fix its charge a. Each pair of auxiliary components is in the Borromean rings arrangement with this component. Eliminate such a pair using the two-strand Kirby projector from c: bend the long component to expose its two oppositely oriented portions, viewed as equally oriented strands labeled a,aˉ. The first encircling normalized Kirby color loop replaces these by a vacuum cup-cap with coefficient D/da​. This vacuum connection opens the second encircling loop so that the same two-strand Kirby projector applies once more. Removing the resulting vacuum edges and straightening the bends restores the original charge-a strand. Thus the Borromean handle-pair evaluation multiplies the bare strand by
Ha​=(da​D​)2.
(27)
There is no residual twist. This is a relative strand evaluation: the final closure of the long component is counted separately. As a normalization check, when a=1 the two auxiliary components become two unlinked normalized Kirby color loops, each of value D, giving H1​=D2.
The g pairs occupy disjoint portions of the long component, so their factors multiply. Closing that component gives its quantum dimension da​. Combining this with its normalized Kirby color coefficient da​/D gives
⟨L(Ω,…,Ω)⟩​=a∑​Dda​​da​(da2​D2​)g=D2g−1a∑​da2−2g​.​
(28)
Applying the overall surgery prefactor yields
Z(Σg​×S1)=D2g−2a∑​da2−2g​.​
(29)
Finally, the topological quantum field theory assigns the identity map to a cylinder over Σg​. Closing its time direction takes the trace of that identity, so
GSD(Σg​)=dimV(Σg​)=Z(Σg​×S1).​
(30)
This independently reproduces b. For g=0 the surgery link is one zero-framed normalized Kirby color unknot: its value D cancels the surgery prefactor and gives Z(S2×S1)=1. For g=1 the result is the number of simple anyons, as required for the torus.
