CRC generator polynomial (G(x))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Coding theory Error detection and correction Cyclic redundancy check
OurBigBook.com Words: 19
The CRC generator polynomial is the agreed divisor in a cyclic redundancy check. Under the basic convention, undetected error polynomials are precisely its nonzero multiples.
