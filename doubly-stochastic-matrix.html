Doubly stochastic matrix

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 282 Articles: 4
A square real matrix is doubly stochastic if its entries are nonnegative and every row and column sums to 1. Its positive-entry support satisfies the condition of the Hall marriage theorem: for a row subset S, ∣S∣≤∣N(S)∣ by summing column capacities. Consequently its support contains a permutation matrix.
