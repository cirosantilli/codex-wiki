Radiative-convective boundary (RCB)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Irradiated planetary atmosphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 251 Articles: 6
The radiative-convective boundary is where radiative transport first requires a temperature gradient steeper than the adiabatic gradient, causing convection below.
