Adiabatic bulk modulus (KS​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Bulk modulus
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The pressure response to compression at fixed entropy, KS​=ρ(∂P/∂ρ)S​. Seismic compressions are commonly approximately adiabatic; a background density gradient follows the corresponding derivative only when composition and entropy assumptions also apply.
