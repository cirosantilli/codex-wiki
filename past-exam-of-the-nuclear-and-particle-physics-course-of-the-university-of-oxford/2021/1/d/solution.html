For a purely electromagnetic interaction continuum with five active quark flavors, the usual estimate is Ne+e−​/Nhad​=3/11. The unspecified particle resonance and the word “total” require qualifications before this can be treated as a unique physical prediction.
In natural units, neglecting final masses, single-photon annihilation gives
σ(μ+μ−→e+e−)=3s4πα2​,σ(μ+μ−→qf​qˉ​f​)=3Qf2​3s4πα2​.
(11)
Here α is the fine-structure constant, Qf​ the quark electric charge in elementary-charge units, and the factor three counts orthogonal colour final states. Consequently the inverse hadronic R ratio is
Nhad​Ne+e−​​=3∑f active​Qf2​1​.​
(12)
With u,d,s,c,b accessible, the denominator is 3[2(2/3)2+3(1/3)2]=11/3. The corresponding values for u,d,s and u,d,s,c are 1/2 and 3/10. Inclusive Quantum chromodynamics corrections increase the hadronic denominator, approximately by 1+αs​/π away from thresholds and particle resonances. These are annihilation ratios before selection and detection efficiency corrections, not ratios of uncorrected detector counts.
A future muon-collider Higgs resonance is a plausible reading of part (c), since its narrow decay width makes a detuning of a few MeV relevant. Even then, two further assumptions are necessary. First, being a few MeV below the Higgs boson peak does not eliminate it: for detuning Δ=s​−mH​, the ideal narrow Breit-Wigner resonance cross section retains the relative factor
σH​(0)σH​(Δ)​≃4Δ2+ΓH2​ΓH2​​.
(13)
For ΓH​=4MeV and Δ=−4MeV this is 1/5, not zero. Resonant hadronic production can still change the requested denominator substantially.
Second, at s​≃125GeV the Z boson continuum is not negligible. A more complete annihilation estimate including both tree-level Feynman diagrams uses
σf​=3s4πα2​Ncf​Ff​(s),Ff​=Qf2​−2Qf​vμ​vf​Reχ+(vμ2​+aμ2​)(vf2​+af2​)∣χ∣2,
(14)
vf​=T3f​−2Qf​sW2​,af​=T3f​,χ=4sW2​(1−sW2​)(s−mZ2​+imZ​ΓZ​)s​.
(15)
Here sW2​ is the squared sine of the weak mixing angle, T3f​ is weak isospin, and Ncf​ is one for a lepton and three for a quark. Thus the continuum ratio is Fe​/[3(2Fu​+3Fd​)], rather than necessarily 3/11. For example, sW2​=0.231, mZ​=91.1876GeV and ΓZ​=2.4952GeV give approximately 0.102 at 125GeV before Quantum chromodynamics corrections.
If the unspecified particle resonance instead means the Z boson, detuning by a few MeV is tiny compared with its 2.5GeV decay width. The resonant ratio remains approximately Γ(Z→e+e−)/Γ(Z→hadrons)=1/20.767≃0.0482.[ref] This is a concrete counterexample to treating any slight downward detuning as permission to discard the particle resonance. The source supplies neither its identity nor the detuning and selection needed for a unique all-process ratio. The boxed continuum formula is the charge-counting answer under its stated approximation; a literal total must also include remaining resonant and other accepted hadronic channels.
