The labels X and Y cannot be uniquely assigned from the surviving text: their decay vertices and daughter tracks were contained in the missing figure. The available conservation laws nevertheless specify how the assignment would be obtained.
The initial π−p state has (Q,B,S)=(0,1,0), with S denoting strangeness. At a primary strong interaction vertex all three numbers must be reproduced. A final Lambda baryon or Sigma baryon has B=1, S=−1 and therefore needs accompanying S=+1, for example a neutral kaon K0=dsˉ or a positive kaon K+=usˉ, with the remaining electric charge balanced. At later weak interaction decay vertices, strangeness need not be conserved; it would be wrong to enforce it there.

A labelled answer is therefore a source gap, rather than a completed identification.
