One possible strong interaction quark-line flavour rearrangement is
(uud)+(uud)⟶puud​​+Λ0uds​​+π+udˉ​​+K0dsˉ​​.

It needs newly created ddˉ and ssˉ pairs, with no net change of any quark flavor. For example, keep the first uud as the outgoing proton; allocate the second incoming u,d and the new s to Λ0, the remaining incoming u and new dˉ to π+, and the new d,sˉ to K0. Two gluon splittings illustrate this flow. This is a possible hadronization topology, not a unique perturbative description of the proton collision.
After several short mean lifetimes, the surviving coherent beam is approximately ∣KL​⟩=(∣K0⟩−∣Kˉ0⟩)/2​. In ordinary matter the forward scattering amplitudes of K0 and Kˉ0 differ. For example, the s in a neutral antikaon can enter a strange baryon through strong absorption; the opposite strangeness assignment has different available channels. If their forward transmission amplitudes through a thin block are t0​ and tˉ0​, respectively, then
∣KL​⟩⟼2t0​+tˉ0​​∣KL​⟩+2t0​−tˉ0​​∣KS​⟩.​
(7)
Thus the transmitted forward beam contains a regenerated short-lived component, with prompt two-pion decays after the block. Differential absorption changes the intensities as well as the relative phase. Scattered particles outside the forward beam can provide an incoherent component. This neutral kaon regeneration does not require CP violation: the material itself distinguishes matter and antimatter.
