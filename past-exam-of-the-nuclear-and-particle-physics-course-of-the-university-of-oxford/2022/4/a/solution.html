Each quark has baryon number 1/3, and each antiquark has baryon number −1/3. A strange quark has strangeness −1, a strange antiquark has strangeness +1, and the light u,d have zero strangeness. The isospin projections of u,d,s are +1/2,−1/2,0, with the opposite values for their antiquarks. Thus
B=3nq​−nqˉ​​​,S=nsˉ​−ns​,Iz​=2nu​−nd​−nuˉ​+ndˉ​​.
