Binary branching model of an electromagnetic shower (nstop​=⌈log2​(E0​/Ec​)⌉)

 Home Physics Particle detection Particle shower Electromagnetic shower
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
If every particle branches into two equal-energy daughters after one radiation length, the multiplicity is 2n and each energy is E0​/2n. Multiplication stops at the critical energy of an electromagnetic shower. This predicts a branching depth, not a realistic containment quantile: stochastic interaction distances, unequal radiation spectra and the residual stopping range produce a longitudinal tail.
