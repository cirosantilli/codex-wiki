Square-basin topographic Rossby mode (ω2=β2/[4(π2(m2+n2)/L2+LD−2​)])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Rossby wave Topographic Rossby-wave dispersion relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
In a square with impermeable walls and background topographic potential-vorticity gradient β, the linear shallow-water quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity equation is ∂t​(∇2−LD−2​)ψ+βψx​=0. For a nonzero-frequency normal mode, setting ψ​=ψeiβx/(2ω) gives a Helmholtz equation with homogeneous wall data. The eigenfunctions are ψ​=sin(mπx/L)sin(nπy/L), with m,n≥1, and the displayed frequencies. The positive and negative frequency representations are complex conjugates for a real wave.
