Fold-Hopf bifurcation (λ0​=0,λ±​=±iω,ω=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory Codimension-two bifurcation
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 1
A fold-Hopf bifurcation combines a simple zero eigenvalue and a nonzero imaginary pair at an equilibrium point. In a three-dimensional centre manifold, a normal form of a dynamical system separates a real fold amplitude from a complex oscillatory amplitude. A rotationally symmetric normal form permits reduction to two real amplitude equations, while the oscillation phase continues to evolve. Secondary bifurcations of the amplitude flow can produce invariant tori in the full dynamical system.
