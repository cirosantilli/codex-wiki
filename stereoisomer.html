Stereoisomer

 Home Chemistry Stereochemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 225 Articles: 9
Stereoisomers have the same chemical bond connectivity but different spatial arrangements. A mirror pair of distinct stereoisomers comprises enantiomers; diastereomers have no mirror-pair relation.
