Covariance matrix

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Expected value Variance Covariance
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 30
For a random vector X with finite second moments, its covariance matrix is
cov(X)=E[(X−EX)(X−EX)T].
(13)
It is positive semidefinite, and the variance of aTX is aTcov(X)a.
