Cartan formula (algebraic topology)

The Cartan product formula says that total Steenrod squares and total Steenrod reduced powers preserve the cup product:
Sqk(ab)=∑i+j=k​Sqi(a)Sqj(b),Pk(ab)=∑i+j=k​Pi(a)Pj(b).
(3)
It is named after Henri Cartan and is distinct from Cartan's magic formula for the Lie derivative of a differential form, named after Élie Cartan.
