Formal power series over a local ring ((A[[x]],mA[[x]]+(x)))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Ring Commutative ring Formal power series
OurBigBook.com Words: 191 Articles: 1
If (A,m) is a local ring, then B=A[[x]] is local with maximal ideal I=mB+xB and residue field A/m. A series f=∑an​xn is a unit if and only if a0​ is a unit. Necessity follows by taking constant coefficients of an inverse; sufficiency follows from the recursion b0​=a0−1​ and bn​=−a0−1​∑i=1n​ai​bn−i​ for its inverse. Thus the nonunits are precisely series with a0​∈m, which is exactly I, since f=a0​+xg. The constant-coefficient map followed by reduction modulo m has kernel I and image A/m.
