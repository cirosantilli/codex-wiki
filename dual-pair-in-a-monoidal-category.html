Dual pair in a monoidal category ((X,Y,e,n))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Monoidal category
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 4
Objects X,Y with an evaluation morphism e:X⊗Y→I and a coevaluation morphism n:I→Y⊗X satisfying both snake identities. These are categorical duality data, distinct from the locally convex dual pair.
