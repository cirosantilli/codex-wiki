For a dilute ideal gas vapor whose specific volume greatly exceeds that of its condensate, the Clausius-Clapeyron relation integrates to the displayed formula. If L(T)=L∗​(Tc​−T)/(Tc​−T∗​), the result is ln(p/p∗​)=L∗​[Tc​(1/T∗​−1/T)−ln(T/T∗​)]/[R(Tc​−T∗​)]. Near the liquid-gas critical point, both the ideal gas approximation and the neglect of condensed-phase volume fail; zero latent heat alone does not determine the limiting coexistence slope.
