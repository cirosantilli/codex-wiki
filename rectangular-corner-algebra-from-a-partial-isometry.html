Rectangular corner algebra from a partial isometry (pAq≅qAq,a⋅b=au∗b,a†=ua∗u)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra C-star algebra Partial isometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
For a nonzero partial isometry u in a C-star algebra, put p=uu∗ and q=u∗u. The closed linear space pAq becomes a unital C-star algebra with the displayed operations and inherited norm, with unit u. The map a↦u∗a is an isometric -isomorphism onto the ordinary corner qAq, with inverse b↦ub. The identities uu∗a=a and u∗ub=b verify the inverse maps, while contractivity of both multiplications gives isometry. Products and involutions transfer directly, giving all algebra and C-norm axioms. This construction turns a rectangular operator corner into a square algebra by using the partial isometry as its unit.
