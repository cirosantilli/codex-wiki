Number of one Poisson type before another

For independent Poisson processes of rates λ1​ and λ2​, the number K of type-two arrivals before the first type-one arrival satisfies
P(K=k)=(λ1​+λ2​λ2​​)kλ1​+λ2​λ1​​.
(46)
This follows by marking the superposed process by its arrival type.
