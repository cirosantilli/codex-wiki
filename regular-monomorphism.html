Regular monomorphism

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Monomorphism Strong monomorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
A regular monomorphism is a morphism that occurs as the equalizer of some parallel pair. Every regular monomorphism is a strong monomorphism.
This is the equalizer-defined subclass of monomorphisms.
