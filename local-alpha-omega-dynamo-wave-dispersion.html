Local alpha-Omega dynamo wave dispersion ((p+ηT​k2)2=iαSk)

OurBigBook.com Words: 100
Take a right-handed local Cartesian frame with x towards the equator, y azimuthal and z radially outwards in the northern hemisphere. A local radial shear Uy​=Sz and a poloidal magnetic field represented by Ay​ give the alpha-Omega dynamo equations At​=αB+ηT​Axx​ and Bt​=SAx​+ηT​Bxx​. Substitution of a Fourier mode proportional to ept+ikx gives the displayed dispersion relation. The growing branch has Rep=−ηT​k2+∣αSk∣/2​ and Imp=sgn(αSk)∣αSk∣/2​. Its phase velocity is −Imp/k: it propagates equatorwards when αS<0, polewards when αS>0. The result assumes local constant coefficients and no meridional circulation in a star; advection and spatially separated field regeneration can alter migration.
