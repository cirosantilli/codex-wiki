Quark mixing (V=UuL†​UdL​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Weak interaction Charged current
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
The up-type and down-type fermion mass matrices need different left-handed transformations represented by unitary matrices to diagonalize them. Their relative transformation appears in the charged weak current as the Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix. The weak neutral currents remain diagonal in the minimal Standard Model, since the same-charge couplings are generation-independent. Three nondegenerate generations give three mixing angles and one physical weak CP phase after quantum field rephasings.
