Rademacher function (rn​(t)=(−1)⌊2nt⌋)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Symmetric probability distribution Rademacher distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
On [0,1) with Lebesgue measure, the functions rn​(t)=(−1)⌊2nt⌋, n≥1, are independent symmetric Rademacher random variables. Every prescribed pattern for the first n functions occupies a dyadic interval of measure 2−n. Their closed span in L1 is isomorphic to the l2 sequence space by the Khintchine inequality.
