Kerr ergoregion

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole Kerr black hole
OurBigBook.com Words: 302 Articles: 5
The Kerr ergoregion is the region where the asymptotically timelike stationary Killing field becomes spacelike. Its outer boundary is r=M+M2−a2cos2θ​.
The Kerr realization of an ergosphere lies outside the outer horizon where the stationary Killing vector is spacelike; its outer boundary is r=M+M2−a2cos2θ​.
