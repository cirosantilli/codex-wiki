Degree as a sum of local degrees (degf=∑x∈f−1(y)​degx​f)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Degree of a continuous mapping Local degree of a continuous map
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
For a continuous map f:M→N of closed connected oriented d-dimensional manifolds, suppose f−1(y)={x1​,…,xs​} is finite. Then
degf=∑j=1s​degxj​​f.
(6)
By excision, the degree-d relative homology group Hd​(M,M∖f−1(y);Z) is the direct sum of the local groups at xj​. The fundamental class maps to the tuple of local orientation generators. Mapping this tuple to Hd​(N,N∖{y};Z) adds their local degrees. Naturality identifies the result with the image of (degf)[N]. If the fiber is empty, the same argument gives degree zero.
