Covariance of the Jaumann derivative

Use the derivative-index-first velocity gradient Gij​=∂i​uj​ and ω=G−GT. For an objective time derivative the tensor transformation A∗=QAQT under x∗=Q(t)x+c(t) must imply DA∗/Dt=Q(DA/Dt)QT. If R=Q˙​QT, then G∗=QGQT−R and
Dt​A∗=Q(Dt​A)QT+RA∗−A∗R,ω∗=QωQT−2R.
(8)
Consequently the extra terms cancel in Dt​A+21​(ωA−Aω), proving covariance. With the component-index-first gradient Lij​=∂j​ui​, the same physical rate is Dt​A−WA+AW, with spin tensor W=(L−LT)/2=−ω/2. Changing the gradient convention without changing the commutator sign destroys objectivity.
