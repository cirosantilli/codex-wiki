Relative Proj construction (ProjX​A)

 ... Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Proper morphism Projective scheme Proj construction
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For a quasi-coherent graded OX​-algebra A, the relative Proj is obtained by gluing the schemes ProjA(U) over affine open subsets U⊆X. If A is of finite type and generated in degree one, a surjection Sym(E)↠A realizes ProjX​A as a closed subscheme of the projective bundle PX​(E).
