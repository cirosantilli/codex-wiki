Split epimorphism

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Epimorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
A split epimorphism r:B→A has a right inverse s:A→B with rs=1A​. Every functor preserves split epimorphisms.
