Credit derivative

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Financial asset
OurBigBook.com Words: 518 Articles: 7
A credit derivative transfers exposure to deterioration of credit quality or a specified credit event of a reference borrower without necessarily transferring the underlying loan or bond. Credit default swaps transfer a defined loss exposure; total return swaps transfer a wider asset return. Funded structures such as credit-linked notes combine the exposure with an investor's initial principal. Documentation specifies the reference entity, obligations, credit events and settlement; a hedge can leave counterparty credit risk and mismatch with the original exposure.
