Dense bipartite common-neighbour lemma

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Probabilistic combinatorics Dependent random choice
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
In a bipartite graph with parts of sizes m,n and density δ>0, a neighbourhood V′ of size at least δm/2 can be chosen so that at least a proportion 1−2ϵ/δ2 of its ordered pairs have at least ϵn common neighbours. Choose a random vertex on the other side, use the second moment of its degree, and penalize pairs with few common neighbours. This is a simple dependent random choice tool for controlling restricted sumsets.
