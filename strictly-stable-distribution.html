Strictly stable distribution (α)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Cauchy distribution Stable distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A probability distribution is strictly stable with index α if n−1/α(T(1)+⋯+T(n))=dT for every positive integer n, for independent copies. Here no centering term is allowed, unlike in the general definition of a stable distribution.
