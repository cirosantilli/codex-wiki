Cauchy subsequence

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Cauchy sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 111
A Cauchy subsequence is a subsequence that is a Cauchy sequence. Every subsequence of a Cauchy sequence is a Cauchy sequence, because its increasing indices eventually lie beyond any index required by the original pairwise bound. Every convergent subsequence is also a Cauchy sequence.
The converse about the original sequence fails: an​=(−1)n has a constant subsequence at its even integers, hence a Cauchy sequence, while an​ itself is not a Cauchy sequence. If the original sequence is already a Cauchy sequence and one subsequence converges to L, however, the whole sequence converges to L. Choose a late subsequence term close to L and use the triangle inequality to compare it with every sufficiently late original term. [ref].
