Iwasawa module structure theorem

A finitely generated module over Λ=Zp​[[T]] is pseudo-isomorphic to
Λr⊕⨁i​Λ/(pai​)⊕⨁j​Λ/(fjbj​​),
(1)
where the fj​ are irreducible distinguished polynomials. Height-one localizations are discrete valuation rings, which give the elementary divisors; the remaining errors are supported only at the maximal ideal and are finite. This is a finite-error classification, not necessarily an actual direct-sum decomposition.
