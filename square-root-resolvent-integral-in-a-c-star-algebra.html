Square-root resolvent integral in a C-star algebra (x1/2=π−1∫0∞​t−1/2x(t1+x)−1dt)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra C-star algebra Continuous functional calculus
OurBigBook.com Words: 124 Articles: 1
For a Positive element of a C-star algebra x, the displayed improper integral converges in norm. Near zero its integrand has norm at most t−1/2; near infinity it has norm at most ∥x∥t−3/2. The scalar identity follows by setting t=su2 for s>0 and using ∫0∞​2/(1+u2)du=π; for s=0 both sides vanish. The bounds are uniform on the compact spectrum of an element, so continuous functional calculus gives the operator identity.
