Premelted-film particle pushing (V=9πμa2A​(1−c)(c+3)c+2​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For a sphere partially below a planar melting isotherm, write c=cosθ0​ and depth a(cosθ−c). Local premelting gives film thickness d3=K/(cosθ−c). Conservation in the thin spherical film gives flux Vasinθ/2 and pressure p=3μVa2(cosθ−c)2/K. Integrating vertical repulsive and hydraulic stresses over the wetted cap yields the displayed speed. The unknown thermodynamic constant K cancels, but a narrow rim where the film ceases to be thin is excluded from the leading approximation.
