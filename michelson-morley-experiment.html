Michelson-Morley experiment

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Tests of special relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 187
An interferometer compares round-trip light travel times along perpendicular arms as its orientation changes. The absence of the classical stationary-aether fringe shift at the expected scale constrains orientation dependence of the round-trip speed of light. The result is consistent with special relativity, but alone does not select it uniquely from every alternative theory.[ref]
The instrument's stone support and floating mounting allowed the experimenters to change its orientation while limiting mechanical disturbance. The measured quantity was the fringe shift produced as the apparatus rotated, rather than a direct timing of a single light pulse.[ref]
