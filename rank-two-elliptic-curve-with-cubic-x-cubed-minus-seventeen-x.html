Rank-two elliptic curve with cubic x cubed minus seventeen x (E:y2=x3−17x,r=2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 61
The two-torsion square-class homomorphism on E has candidate classes {1,−1,17,−17} by the prime-support bound in two-isogeny descent. The identity, (0,0) and (−1,4) realize 1,−17,−1, so multiplicativity gives all four. The two-isogeny formula companion E′:y2=x3+68x has only positive candidate classes {1,2,17,34}; (0,0) realizes [68]=17 and (2,12) realizes 2. Thus both images have order four, and the two-isogeny rank formula gives 2r=4⋅4/4=4. The rank of an elliptic curve is therefore two, without any unresolved covering class.
