Lundberg inequality (ψ(u)≤e−Ru)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Classical risk model Adjustment coefficient
OurBigBook.com Words: 290 Articles: 5
For a classical risk model with adjustment coefficient R>0, the ultimate ruin probability from capital u≥0 satisfies ψ(u)≤e−Ru. Stop the exponential continuous-time martingale at ruin or a finite horizon, bound its value on the ruin event, and then increase the horizon.
