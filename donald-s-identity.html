Donald's identity

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Von Neumann entropy Quantum relative entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 1
For an ensemble of density operators with average ω=∑j​pj​ωj​,
∑j​pj​D(ωj​∥ρ)=∑j​pj​D(ωj​∥ω)+D(ω∥ρ).
(12)
Expanding the trace definitions cancels the logωj​ terms, and the remaining sum combines to a trace against ω. Positive-weight ensemble states have their support contained in that of the average, so the first sum on the right is finite in finite dimension. If the reference-state support condition fails, both sides are +∞. The identity is associated with Donald's work on relative entropy.
