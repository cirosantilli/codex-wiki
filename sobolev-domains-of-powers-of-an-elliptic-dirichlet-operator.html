Sobolev domains of powers of an elliptic Dirichlet operator (Xk​=D(ADk/2​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Elliptic differential operator Elliptic regularity
OurBigBook.com Words: 97
For a strictly positive Dirichlet realization of an elliptic operator with smooth coefficients and smooth boundary, put X0​=L2(U). For each integer k≥1,
Xk​={u∈Hk(U):T(Lju)=00≤j≤⌊2k−1​⌋}.
(21)
These are the domains D(ADk/2​), with norms equivalent to the indicated Sobolev space norms. The bilinear forms ((u,v))2l​=(Llu,Llv)L2​ and ((u,v))2l+1​=B[Llu,Llv] are inner products on X2l​ and X2l+1​. The higher boundary conditions are essential: for L=−d2/dx2 and u=x(π−x), u is smooth and zero at the boundary but L2u=0, so ∥L2u∥2​ is not a norm on all of H4∩H01​. Repeated elliptic regularity proves AD​:Xk+2​→Xk​ is an isomorphism, which gives the norm equivalence inductively from X0​ and X1​=H01​.
