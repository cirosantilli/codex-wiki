Blackbody dust sublimation radius (rsub​∝L1/2Tsub−2​)

A spherical black grain of radius a absorbs luminosity L through projected area πa2 and emits over 4πa2. Radiative balance at sublimation temperature gives rsub​=[L/(16πσSB​Tsub4​)]1/2. Grain size cancels only under the stated unit-efficiency approximation; shielding and wavelength-dependent emissivity change the result.
