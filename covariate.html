Covariate

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model
OurBigBook.com Words: 114 Articles: 1
A covariate is an observed explanatory variable used to model the conditional distribution of a response. A vector of covariates can enter a linear predictor, a treatment-effect adjustment or a hazard multiplier. Conditioning on a covariate does not itself establish that its effect is causal.
