Sun-synchronous orbit

 ... Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Kepler orbit Circumplanetary orbit Polar orbit
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
An orbit whose nodal precession tracks the central planet's annual motion, keeping approximately the same local solar time at corresponding crossings. Near-Earth implementations are nearly polar and exploit the planet's nonspherical gravitational field. Fixed local-time sampling supports comparison between passes while limiting observations of the diurnal cycle.
