Truth value (F,T)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Proposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
In classical propositional logic, a truth value is false or true, encoded by a bit as zero or one. A Boolean valuation assigns these values to primitive propositions and extends to compound propositional formulas through logical connectives.
