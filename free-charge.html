Free charge

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
In macroscopic electromagnetism, free charge is the part of the charge density represented explicitly as ρf​, separately from bound charge associated with the electric polarization of matter. The electric displacement field satisfies ∇⋅D=ρf​. The word free does not imply zero force or zero energy: it distinguishes this source from the polarization response in the chosen material description.
