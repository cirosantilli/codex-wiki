Trust-region acceptance ratio (ρk​=mk​(0)−mk​(sk​)f(xk​)−f(xk​+sk​)​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Trust region
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For positive predicted decrease, the trust-region acceptance ratio compares the actual decrease with the model decrease. A ratio near one supports the local model; a negative ratio means the trial increased the objective function. A typical algorithm rejects and shrinks the trust region when ρk​<η1​, accepts it when ρk​≥η1​, and enlarges the radius after a very successful boundary step with ρk​>η2​, where 0<η1​<η2​<1.
