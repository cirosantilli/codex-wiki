Reduced topological K-theory (K∗(X))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological K-theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
For a based compact space, reduced K-theory is the kernel of restriction to the basepoint. It satisfies
Ki(Sd)≅Ki−d(pt).
