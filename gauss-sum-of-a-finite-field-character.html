Here ψ is a nontrivial additive character of the finite field. For nontrivial χ, substituting x=ty in the squared absolute value gives
∣G(χ)∣2=∑t=0​χ(t)∑y=0​ψ((t−1)y)=q.
(2)
The inner sum is q−1 at t=1 and −1 otherwise; multiplicative character orthogonality completes the identity.
