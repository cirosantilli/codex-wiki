Mahler-measure norm argument at measure two

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Mahler measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
Let an irreducible f∈Z[X] have M(f)=2 and a root outside the closed unit disk. Its leading coefficient must have absolute value one. The product B of all roots outside the unit disk is then an algebraic integer with ∣B∣=2; conjugate pairs make B real, so B=±2. The quotient of the norm of any root by B is both rational and an algebraic integer, and its absolute value is at most one. It is therefore ±1, so every root has absolute norm two.
