Lift the Levi-Civita connection to the spinor bundle using the spin representation of the Lorentz algebra. Under a local Lorentz transformation, its matrix Ωμ​=41​ωμAB​γAB obeys Ωμ′​=SΩμ​S−1−(∂μ​S)S−1, so ∇μ′​ψ′=S∇μ​ψ. The Clifford algebra identity [γC,γAB]=2(ηCAγB−ηCBγA) fixes the coefficient. The tetrad postulate then gives ∂μ​γν+Γμρν​γρ+[Ωμ​,γν]=0, establishing compatibility with curved Clifford multiplication.
