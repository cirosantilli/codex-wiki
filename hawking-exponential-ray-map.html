Hawking exponential ray map (UH​−U=Ae−κu)

 ... Branches of physics General relativity Black hole Black-hole thermodynamics Hawking temperature Hawking radiation
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
The late outgoing rays from a nonextremal collapsing black hole have an exponential relation between a regular early affine null coordinate U and late retarded time u. Logarithmic divergence of the tortoise coordinate at the horizon gives UH​−U=Ae−κu up to subleading terms. Backward propagation turns e−iωu into (UH​−U)iω/κ; its positive- and negative-frequency Fourier parts give a thermal Bogoliubov transformation.
