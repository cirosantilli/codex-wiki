Dual of a maximum of affine functions (max{bTy:y≥0, 1Ty=1, ∑i​yi​ai​=0})

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Linear programming Linear programming duality
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
Minimizing maxi​(aiT​x+bi​) over unrestricted x is a linear program after introducing an epigraph variable t and inequalities aiT​x+bi​≤t. Its optimization Lagrangian has finite infimum over x,t exactly when the nonnegative multipliers sum to one and their weighted slopes sum to zero. The displayed Lagrangian dual problem maximizes the resulting constant lower bound. It identifies the role of a zero-slope convex combination of the affine functions.
