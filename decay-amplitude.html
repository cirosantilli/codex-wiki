Decay amplitude (M(i→f))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Decay width
OurBigBook.com Words: 148 Articles: 1
The invariant decay amplitude is the transition matrix element for one incoming particle and a specified on-shell final state, with the overall momentum delta function factored out. For covariantly normalized states, write the connected S-matrix element as i(2π)4δ4(pi​−pf​)M(i→f). Its overall phase does not affect the decay width. The rate is Γ=(2Mi​)−1∑spins​∫dΦf​∣M∣2, with the initial spin average included when the initial state is unpolarized. For a spin-zero parent and an angle-independent two-body amplitude, the two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space gives Γ=∣k∣∑∣M∣2/(8πMi2​). In leptonic pion decay, contracting the axial hadronic matrix element with the chiral weak charged current makes this amplitude proportional to the charged-lepton mass, proving helicity suppression.
