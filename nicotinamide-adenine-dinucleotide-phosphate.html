Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 2
Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate is a cofactor used in metabolism. Its oxidized and reduced forms, NADP+ and NADPH, carry electrons between coupled chemical reactions. In oxygenic photosynthesis, light-dependent reactions produce NADPH for the Calvin cycle, which returns NADP+ for reuse. [ref].
