Flat normalization is an isomorphism

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
If the normalization of an integral scheme is flat, then it is an isomorphism. On an affine chart, let A⊆B⊆Frac(A) be the integral closure. Integrality gives surjectivity on spectra, so flatness makes B faithfully flat. For b=u/v∈B with u,v∈A and v=0, faithful flatness gives vB∩A=vA. Thus u=va for some a∈A, and b=a. Consequently B=A. This proof does not need finiteness of the normalization.
