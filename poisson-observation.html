Poisson observation (Y∣λ∼Poisson(λ))

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution Poisson distribution Poisson observation model
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
A Poisson observation is an integer-valued count modeled by a Poisson distribution of specified intensity, possibly conditionally on a latent field. Independent Poisson observations motivate Poisson data fidelity through their log-likelihood.
