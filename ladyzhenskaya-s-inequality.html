Ladyzhenskaya's inequality

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space
OurBigBook.com Words: 78 Articles: 1
Ladyzhenskaya's inequality controls the Lp norm of a function by its Lp norm and the Lp norm of its gradient: in dimension two, ∥u∥4​≤C∥u∥21/2​∥∇u∥21/2​; in dimension three the exponents are 1/4 and 3/4. These estimates hold for smooth compactly supported functions and extend using density of smooth functions in a Sobolev space.
