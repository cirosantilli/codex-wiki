Doeblin's condition

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Markov chain Monte Carlo Geometric ergodicity Minorization condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
A Doeblin condition is a minorization of the whole state space. It implies uniform geometric convergence in total variation distance.
