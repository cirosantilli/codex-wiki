Linear differential operator (L=∑j=0m​aj​(x)Dj)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differential operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
A linear differential operator is a finite linear combination of derivatives with specified coefficient functions. It obeys L(αf+βg)=αLf+βLg for scalar constants α,β. Variable coefficients need not commute with differentiation: D(af)=a′f+af′.
