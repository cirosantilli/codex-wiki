Exterior power (ΛkV)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Exterior algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 348 Articles: 4
The kth exterior power ΛkV is the quotient vector space of the tensor power V⊗k that makes every elementary tensor with two equal factors zero. More precisely, take the quotient vector space by the linear span of all such tensors. Its elementary products v1​∧⋯∧vk​ turn alternating multilinear maps into linear maps: every alternating k-linear map on V factors uniquely through ΛkV.[ref]
If e1​,…,en​ is an ordered basis, the increasing products
ei1​​∧⋯∧eik​​,1≤i1​<⋯<ik​≤n
(27)
form a basis of ΛkV. There is one for each k-element choice of basis directions, so
dimΛkV=(kn​)​.
(28)
Repeated directions vanish; products with a different order can be sorted using their sign. Coordinate determinants distinguish the remaining increasing products, establishing linear independence.[ref]
For V=R3, a basis of Λ2V is e1​∧e2​,e1​∧e3​,e2​∧e3​. This explains the three bivector components in the worked example. The top exterior power Λ3V has one basis element, e1​∧e2​∧e3​, while Λ4V=0: four independent directions cannot fit into that vector space. By convention Λ0V is the scalar field, even when V has dimension zero.
