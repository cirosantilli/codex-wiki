For a unit vector, the radial coordinate is r=1 and the spherical coordinates give
(nx​,ny​,nz​)=(sinθcosϕ,sinθsinϕ,cosθ).
(12)
The Pauli matrices therefore give
H=(cosθeiϕsinθ​e−iϕsinθ−cosθ​).​
(13)
The diagonal entries are real and the off-diagonal entries are complex conjugates, so H∗=H. Its trace is zero and its determinant is −1, giving eigenvalues +1 and −1. A convenient orthonormal eigenbasis is
v+​=(eiϕsin(θ/2)cos(θ/2)​),v−​=(eiϕcos(θ/2)−sin(θ/2)​).​
(14)
The half-angle identities verify Hv±​=±v±​, v±∗​v±​=1 and v+∗​v−​=0. Multiplication of either vector by any unit-modulus phase gives an equivalent normalized eigenvector. At a pole, the azimuth is undefined geometrically, but the remaining phase freedom makes the chosen expressions consistent with the same one-dimensional eigenspaces.
