For A′, the centre and constant remain a=(−1,0,0) and C=3. Its eigenvalues are 3,1,−1, so rotated coordinates put the surface in the form 3u2+w2−y2=3. This is a one-sheet hyperboloid. Since 3∥x∥2≥xTA′x=3, the nearest centre-to-surface distance is at least one, attained in the eigen-direction with eigenvalue three. The coordinate y is unbounded, because arbitrarily large ∣y∣ can be balanced by u or w. Thus the nearest distance is one, and there is no maximum value of y.
