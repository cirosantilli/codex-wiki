It is zero at ω=0, rises to one at ω0​, and decays to zero as ω→∞. The two positive half-power angular frequencies satisfy

On the low-frequency side, ω02​−ω12​=γω1​; on the high-frequency side, ω22​−ω02​=γω2​. Solving these quadratic equations gives
ω1​=24ω02​+γ2​−γ​,ω2​=24ω02​+γ2​+γ​.​
(45)
Consequently the frequency bandwidth of viscous oscillator power absorption obeys the stronger exact result
ω2​−ω1​=γ.​
(46)
For a sharp resonance, γ≪ω0​, expansion gives ω1,2​=ω0​∓γ/2+O(γ2/ω0​), which recovers the requested approximation. The exact width above assumes the constant viscous damping coefficient in this model. Although the curve is approximately symmetric near a sharp peak, the exact half-power points satisfy ω1​ω2​=ω02​ rather than exact symmetry about ω0​.
