Induction from M2=M gives Mj=M for every integer j≥1. Separate the zeroth term of the matrix exponential series:
eαM​=I+j=1∑∞​j!αjMj​=I+Mj=1∑∞​j!αj​=I+(eα−1)M.​
(58)
Therefore the exponential of an idempotent linear map is
eαM=I+(eα−1)M=(I−M)+eαM.​
(59)
The last form shows its action directly: it multiplies the image of a linear map of M by eα and fixes its kernel of a linear map. No Hermiticity assumption on the idempotent matrix is needed.
