At critical damping, the characteristic polynomial has the repeated root r=−ω0​. Its independent solutions are e−ω0​t and te−ω0​t, giving
x(t)=(A+Bt)e−ω0​t.​
(17)
The factor t supplies the second independent solution that would be lost by using just one exponential. There is no oscillatory factor at this boundary between the underdamped and overdamped regimes.
