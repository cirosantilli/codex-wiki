The three vectors are coplanar precisely when their scalar triple product vanishes:
a⋅(b×c)=0.​
(13)
This includes linearly dependent or zero vectors. It expresses that the determinant with those vectors as columns is zero.
For the two moving particles, distinguish closest approach at a common time from the geometric distance between their supporting lines. For fixed reference times, put
d0​=a1​−a2​−b1​t1​+b2​t2​,v=b1​−b2​.
(14)
Their relative position is d0​+tv. If v=0, the closest approach of two uniformly moving particles occurs at
t∗​=−∣v∣2d0​⋅v​,dmin2​=∣d0​∣2−∣v∣2(d0​⋅v)2​.​
(15)
If v=0, the distance is constant. If only t≥0 is permitted, replace t∗​ by max(0,t∗​) before evaluating the distance.
Now suppose the velocities are nonparallel, and allow the relative starting-time offset to be chosen so that the particles reach the closest points of the two lines simultaneously. Write Δ=a2​−a1​, A=∣b1​∣2, B=b1​⋅b2​, C=∣b2​∣2 and D=AC−B2>0. The independent line parameters s1​,s2​ at the closest points satisfy
As1​−Bs2​=b1​⋅Δ,Bs1​−Cs2​=b2​⋅Δ.
(16)
These equations make the connecting vector perpendicular to both velocities. Solving the two-by-two linear system gives
s1​=DCb1​⋅Δ−Bb2​⋅Δ​,s2​=DBb1​⋅Δ−Ab2​⋅Δ​.
(17)
The corresponding distance between skew lines is ∣Δ⋅(b1​×b2​)∣/∣b1​×b2​∣. Simultaneous arrival requires t1​+s1​=t2​+s2​, so t1​−t2​=s2​−s1​. Since the vector triple product gives
(b1​×b2​)×(b1​−b2​)=(A−B)b2​+(C−B)b1​,
(18)
this condition is precisely
t1​−t2​=B2−ACΔ⋅[(b1​×b2​)×(b1​−b2​)]​.​
(19)
Thus the printed formula has a valid interpretation as a synchronization condition for attaining the line-distance lower bound. It is not an identity that must hold for arbitrary fixed reference times at the time of closest approach.
For a counterexample to that stronger reading, take a1​=0, a2​=(1,0,1), b1​=(1,0,0), b2​=(0,1,0) and t1​=t2​=0. The actual squared distance is 2(t−1/2)2+3/2, minimized at t=1/2. The printed right-hand side is −1, whereas the fixed difference t1​−t2​ is zero. The line-distance bound is one, but it is not attained by that synchronized motion. Parallel velocities also require separate treatment because the displayed denominator then vanishes.
