Let M be the square matrix whose jth column is Aj​. The Leibniz formula for determinants is precisely the stated contraction:
S=∑i1​,…,in​​ϵi1​⋯in​​Mi1​,1​⋯Min​,n​=detM.
(33)
A square matrix has zero determinant exactly when its columns are linearly dependent, proving the required equivalence. One direction also follows directly from multilinearity and antisymmetry of a multilinear map: replacing one column by a linear combination of the others leaves only terms with repeated columns, each zero. Conversely, nonzero determinant gives an inverse matrix and hence independent columns.
