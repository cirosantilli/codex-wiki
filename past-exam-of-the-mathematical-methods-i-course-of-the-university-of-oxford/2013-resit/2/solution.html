Write 27i=27ei(π/2+2πk). Every cube root has complex modulus 3 and an angle one third of one of these complex arguments:
zk​=3ei(π/6+2πk/3),k=0,1,2.​
(2)
Equivalently the three complex roots are
233​​+23​i,−233​​+23​i,−3i.​
(3)
They lie on the circle of radius 3 centred at the origin in the Argand plane, spaced by 2π/3. Further integer values of k repeat these three points. Cubing any of them multiplies its angle by three and cubes its complex modulus from 3 to 27, giving 27i.
