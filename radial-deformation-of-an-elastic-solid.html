Radial deformation of an elastic solid (Dy=(r/R)I+(r′−r/R)θ⊗θ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Finite elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 319 Articles: 2
A spherical radial deformation is y(x)=r(R)x/R, with R=∣x∣ and θ=x/R. Differentiation gives the displayed symmetric gradient. Its radial eigenvalue is r′ and its two tangential eigenvalues are r/R; when these are positive, they are the principal stretches. The determinant is r′(r/R)2. Positivity distinguishes an orientation-preserving radial elastic deformation from a map that collapses an interior sphere.
