Descending chain condition

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Noetherian ring Artinian ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A family of ideals satisfies the descending chain condition when every descending sequence of ideals eventually becomes constant. A ring is Artinian precisely when all its ideals satisfy this condition.
