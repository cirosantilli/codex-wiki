Geometric partition bounds for a power-law improper integral (∫1∞​x−1−αdx=1/α,α>0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Riemann integration Riemann sum
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
On the geometric progression xi​=(1+ϵ)i, with ϵ>0, the interval widths are ϵ(1+ϵ)i. The left-endpoint Riemann sum for the decreasing integrand x−1−α is the geometric series
Uϵ​=∑i=0∞​ϵ(1+ϵ)−αi=1−(1+ϵ)−αϵ​.
(81)
The right-endpoint sum is Lϵ​=(1+ϵ)−1−αUϵ​. Integral comparison on each interval gives Lϵ​≤I≤Uϵ​. Both bounds tend to 1/α as ϵ→0+, proving the improper integral by the squeeze theorem. Although the largest interval width is unbounded on the infinite domain, these explicit upper and lower bounds justify the limit without incorrectly applying a finite-interval mesh argument to the entire half-line.
