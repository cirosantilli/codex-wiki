Local wavelength (λloc​=2π/∣∇ϕ∣)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Wavenumber Wavelength
OurBigBook.com Words: 134
For a locally oscillatory wave written u(x,t)=A(x,t)cosϕ(x,t), the local wavevector is kloc​=∇ϕ, where ∇ is the spatial gradient. The local wavelength 2π/∣kloc​∣ measures local wave phase spacing normal to the wavefront. Interpreting it as a crest separation requires the amplitude and local wavevector to vary little over one wavelength, as in the WKB approximation. Along a unit direction e the corresponding local repeat distance is 2π/∣kloc​⋅e∣, if that dot product is nonzero; oblique sampling measures this projected spacing.
A real snapshot alone does not uniquely specify A and ϕ: their meaning requires a chosen oscillatory component or physical approximation. Strong interference, zeros of amplitude, and rapidly changing media can defeat a single-carrier description. If ∇ϕ=0, the local reciprocal formula gives no finite wavelength; it does not establish an exact global spatial period.
