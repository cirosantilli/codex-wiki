Weak-ball metrizability requires a norm-separable dual (BE​ weakly metrizable⟺E′ norm separable)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Weak topology Metrization of the weak topology on a bounded set
OurBigBook.com Words: 110
If the relative weak topology on the unit ball of a normed vector space is first countable at zero, choose a countable collection of finite families of continuous linear functionals defining a local base. For any f∈E′ and ϵ>0, one family F has common kernel M on which ∥f∣M​∥≤ϵ. Extend that restriction to h∈E′ with ∥h∥≤ϵ using the Hahn-Banach theorem. Then f−h vanishes on M, so it belongs to spanF by finite-dimensional factorization. Consequently the countable union of these families has norm-dense linear span in E′. The converse is the metrization of the weak topology on a bounded set construction from a norm-dense sequence in E′. In particular, separability of E alone does not suffice: ℓ1 has nonseparable dual ℓ∞.
