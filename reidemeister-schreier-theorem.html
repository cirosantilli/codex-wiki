Reidemeister–Schreier theorem

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Quasi-isometry Finite-index subgroup quasi-isometry Schreier's lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
Given a presentation of a group and a Schreier transversal for a subgroup, the Reidemeister–Schreier theorem gives a presentation of the subgroup. Its generators are the Schreier generators tsts−1, and its relators are rewritten conjugates of the original relators.
