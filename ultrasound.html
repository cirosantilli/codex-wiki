Ultrasound

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Acoustic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 5
Ultrasound is sound above the conventional upper limit of human hearing, approximately 20kHz. In a crystal, ultrasonic travel-time measurements determine long-wavelength elastic wave speeds, which are the small-wavevector slopes of the corresponding acoustic phonon branches.
