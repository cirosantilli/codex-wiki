RLC circuit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 5
An RLC circuit combines electrical resistance R, inductance L, and capacitance C. The capacitor stores energy in its electric field; the inductor stores energy in its magnetic field. Their exchange can produce an oscillation, while Joule heating removes energy. A series RLC circuit gives a particularly clear example: changing R turns ringing into a direct return to equilibrium, and changing L or C tunes a resonance.
These circuits connect two useful viewpoints. In time, they describe how stored energy is released after switching. In frequency, they select a range of signals: the resonant series RLC response explains radio tuning and simple filters. The same mathematics describes a damped harmonic oscillator, so insight about mechanical ringing transfers directly to an electrical circuit. [ref]; [ref].
