Entropy method for certifiable functions

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability inequality Concentration inequality Certifiable function
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
The entropy method combines a coordinatewise certificate with tensorization of entropy to bound the moment-generating function of a certifiable random quantity and hence its tails.
