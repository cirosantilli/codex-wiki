Parity as odd-mode number parity (P=eiπNodd​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Parity Parity operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
For a free intrinsic-even real scalar field, momentum reflection Rf(p)=f(−p) is a self-adjoint involution on the one-particle space. Its odd orthogonal projector is (1−R)/2. The corresponding second quantization is Nodd​=21​∫d3p/(2π)3ap†​(ap​−a−p​), with integration over all momentum space. Conjugation by eiπNodd​ fixes even modes and reverses odd modes, hence sends ap​ to a−p​ and implements the parity operator. Its square is one because Nodd​ has integer eigenvalues. The equivalent positive expression is Nodd​=41​∫d3p/(2π)3(ap†​−a−p†​)(ap​−a−p​).
