Graph of a function (graph(f))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Function
OurBigBook.com Words: 128 Articles: 2
For a function f:A→B, its graph is the subset {(x,f(x)):x∈A} of A×B. It differs from a graph in graph theory. A solution curve of a scalar differential equation can be a graph over its independent coordinate.
