Renormalization-group transformation (g′=Rb​(g))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Renormalization group
OurBigBook.com Words: 184 Articles: 2
A renormalization-group transformation integrates out selected short-distance degrees of freedom and restores chosen cutoff and field-normalization conventions. It defines a map Rb​ on couplings at a length factor b. Its discrete Jacobian matrix multipliers differ from eigenvalues of the generator of a continuous renormalization-group flow; the relation for a scaling direction is λ=by.
