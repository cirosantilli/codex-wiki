Electrical reference potential (Φref​=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric field Electric potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 102
An electrical reference potential fixes the arbitrary additive constant of an electric potential. Assigning zero to ground makes other potentials differences from that baseline; it does not assert that the electric field vanishes there. If a source calculation gives Φ(a)=89.9V and Φ(b)=179.8V relative to infinity, choosing a as the zero instead gives Φ′(a)=0 and Φ′(b)=89.9V, with the same electric potential difference. Grounding an electrical conductor additionally connects it to a reservoir that can exchange electric charge to maintain the prescribed electric potential; that physical boundary condition differs from merely relabeling an unchanged solution.[ref]; [ref].
