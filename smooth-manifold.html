Smooth manifold

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 991 Articles: 29
A smooth k-manifold is a Hausdorff second-countable space locally homeomorphic to Rk, equipped with smoothly compatible coordinate charts.
