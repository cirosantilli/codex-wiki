Mean curvature of a sphere and cylinder (Hsphere​=−1/R,Hcylinder​=−1/(2R))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Second fundamental form Mean curvature
OurBigBook.com Words: 150
For a sphere of radius R>0, its outward unit normal is N(p)=p/R. Applying the shape operator gives S(v)=−v/R, so the two principal curvatures coincide:
k1​=k2​=−R1​,H=−R1​,K=R21​.
(28)
For the lateral surface of a circular cylinder with outward unit normal, moving around a circle turns N at rate 1/R, while moving along the axis leaves N constant. Thus the circumferential and axial principal curvatures give
kcirc​=−R1​,kaxial​=0,H=−2R1​,K=0.
(29)
Here K is Gaussian curvature. The circular cylinder bends in only one principal direction, so its mean curvature is half that of the equally sized sphere. These formulas concern the smooth lateral surface, excluding any edges where end caps meet it.
The common length scale is ℓ, equal to the radius R for the sphere and circular cylinder, and to the waist radius a for the catenoid. The mean curvature marker is halfway between the two principal curvature markers. Gaussian curvature has a different unit and is listed separately beneath each panel.
