Exoplanet transit depth (δ)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Exoplanet atmospheric observatory Exoplanet transit photometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
The fractional decrease in observed radiative flux during an exoplanet transit, relative to the out-of-transit baseline. For a uniform star, a dark planet, full disc overlap, and no transit dilution, the exoplanet transit depth is (Rp​/R⋆​)2. For a nonuniform star it depends on the blocked specific intensity as well as the radius ratio.
