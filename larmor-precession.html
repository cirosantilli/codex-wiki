Larmor precession

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Spin
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 3
Larmor precession is the precession of a magnetic moment about an applied magnetic field. For a spin with gyromagnetic ratio γ and Hamiltonian H=−γB⋅S, its expectation obeys d⟨Sangle/dt=γ⟨SangleimesB. A spin coherent state retains its coherent form during this rotation.
