Spin-orbit interaction (Hso​∝L⋅S)

 Home Physics Atomic physics Fine structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 626 Articles: 7
The spin-orbit interaction couples orbital angular momentum to spin. In chemical atoms it helps determine fine structure and the coupling scale that separates weak-field Zeeman splitting from the Paschen-Back effect.
