For RNA birth/death rates λ1​,β1​x1​ and protein rates λ2​x1​f(x2​),β2​x2​, linearize at stationary means m1​,m2​. Put H=1−∂logf/∂logx2​=1+h. The relative-fluctuation drift is (−β1​β2​​0−β2​H​) and the diffusion matrix is diag(2β1​/m1​,2β2​/m2​). The stationary covariance equation gives the displayed normalized protein variance. Its extrinsic part factors as H−2[β2​H/(β1​+β2​H)]m1−1​: static susceptibility squared times a time-averaging factor times environmental noise. Faster response alone weakens averaging; feedback reduces susceptibility and thereby lowers noise. The formula is exact for affine rates when H=1, and is a linear noise approximation for nonlinear feedback.
