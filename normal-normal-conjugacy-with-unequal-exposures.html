For independent normal observations conditional on Θ, with mean Θ and variances v/mj​, and normal prior mean μ and variance a, write W=∑j​mj​. Multiplying likelihood and prior adds precisions W/v+1/a. Completing the square gives the displayed posterior distribution. Its posterior mean is ZXw​+(1−Z)μ, where Z=aW/(v+aW) and Xw​=∑j​mj​Xj​/W. Thus the Bayes estimator under squared error loss is exactly the Bühlmann–Straub credibility estimate, not merely an approximation to it. Its posterior variance is av/(v+aW).
