Formal power series preserve regular local rings (dimA[[x]]=dimA+1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Local ring Regular local ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 99
Let (A,m) be a regular local ring of Krull dimension d. Its formal power series ring B=A[[x]] is Noetherian and local, with maximal ideal I=mB+(x) and embedding dimension d+1. Since I is generated by d+1 elements, the Krull height theorem gives dimB≤d+1. For the reverse inequality choose a strict prime chain p0​⊊⋯⊊pd​=m in A. Finite generation of each pi​ gives B/pi​B≅(A/pi​)[[x]], an integral domain, so each extended ideal is prime. The inclusions remain strict by contraction to A, and mB⊊I adds one more step since x∈/mB. This proves dimB=d+1, equal to its embedding dimension, and hence regularity.
