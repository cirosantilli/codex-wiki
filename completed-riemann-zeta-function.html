Completed Riemann zeta function (Λ(s)=π−s/2Γ(s/2)ζ(s))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Riemann zeta function
OurBigBook.com Words: 223 Articles: 4
The completed zeta function Λ(s)=π−s/2Γ(s/2)ζ(s) satisfies Λ(s)=Λ(1−s) and has poles at zero and one. It is also sometimes denoted ξ(s); this differs from the entire xi function obtained by multiplying by s(s−1)/2. The distinction matters in the constant term of a nonholomorphic Eisenstein series.
