Fermentation

 Home Biology Metabolism
OurBigBook.com Words: 222 Articles: 9
In its biochemical sense, fermentation is metabolism that obtains ATP from breakdown of organic substrates without a respiratory electron transport chain. The alcoholic route is one example; not every fermentation produces ethanol. See Clark, Douglas and Choi, Biology 2e, 7.5: fermentation versus anaerobic respiration.
