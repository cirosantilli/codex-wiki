Approximate inverse of a Gram matrix

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Lasso Debiased Lasso
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A matrix Θ is an η-approximate inverse of a Gram matrix Σ in entrywise maximum norm when
∥ΣΘ−I∥max​≤η.
(16)
This turns the bias remainder of a Debiased Lasso into a product of η and the coefficient ℓ1 error.
