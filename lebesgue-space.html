Lebesgue space

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 87 Articles: 2
The space Lp consists of measurable functions with finite p-norm, identified when they agree almost everywhere. This quotient is necessary because the integral seminorm vanishes on functions supported on null sets.
