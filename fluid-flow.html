Fluid flow

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 190 Articles: 4
A fluid flow is motion described by a velocity field u(x,t). The trajectories of fluid elements satisfy dx/dt=u(x,t); the mass density and other fields obey their conservation laws and constitutive equations. This physical motion differs from a flow on a flow network.
