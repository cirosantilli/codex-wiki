Competing exponential clocks

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Continuous probability distribution Exponential distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For independent exponential variables Si​ of rates qi​ and Q=∑i​qi​, let T=mini​Si​ and let K be the minimizing index. Then
P(K=k,T≥t)=Qqk​​e−Qt.
(16)
Consequently T is exponential with rate Q, P(K=k)=qk​/Q, and K and T are independent.
