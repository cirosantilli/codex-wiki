A horizontal bar of mass M hangs from a spring of incremental stiffness κ0​>0, and two equal masses m hang separately from the bar through springs of stiffness κ1​>0. The bar remains horizontal and all motion is vertical. For physical displacements q=(q1​,q2​,q3​) from the loaded equilibrium, the kinetic energy and quadratic potential energy are
T=21​[Mq˙​12​+mq˙​22​+mq˙​32​],V=21​κ0​q12​+21​κ1​[(q2​−q1​)2+(q3​−q1​)2].
(29)
Gravity determines the static extensions; its linear terms cancel at equilibrium. Exchange of the lower masses splits the normal modes into the antisymmetric mode (0,1,−1) with ω2=κ1​/m, and two symmetric modes (A,B,B). The latter obey (κ0​+2κ1​−Mω2)A−2κ1​B=0 and −κ1​A+(κ1​−mω2)B=0. Their determinant gives the title quadratic in ω2, and B/A=κ1​/(κ1​−mω2). For M=2,m=1,κ0​=4,κ1​=1, their squared angular frequencies are 2±2​, with displacement ratios (1,1∓2​,1∓2​) respectively. The lower symmetric mode moves all masses in phase; the higher one moves the lower pair against the bar.
