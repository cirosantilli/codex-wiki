Conformal invariance of SLE

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Schramm–Loewner evolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 3
Chordal SLE is defined on any simply connected domain with two marked boundary points by applying a conformal map from the upper half-plane. Its law is independent of the chosen map, up to a deterministic change of parameterization.
