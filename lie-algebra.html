Lie algebra

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 184
A Lie algebra over a field is a vector space g with a bilinear operation [x,y], called the Lie bracket, that is alternating and satisfies the Jacobi identity.
