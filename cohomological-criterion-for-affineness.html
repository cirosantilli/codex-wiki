Cohomological criterion for affineness (X affine⟺H1(X,I)=0 for all coherent ideals I)

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Spectrum of a commutative ring Affine scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 185 Articles: 3
A Noetherian scheme is affine exactly when every coherent ideal sheaf has zero first sheaf cohomology. One direction is vanishing of quasi-coherent cohomology on an affine scheme. For the converse, ideal-sheaf vanishing produces affine principal neighbourhoods from ideal-sheaf vanishing. A finite such cover yields a unit-ideal certificate from a principal affine cover, and those affine charts glue to the spectrum of a commutative ring of global sections. The criterion, including the finite-type ideal version for quasi-compact quasi-separated schemes, is recorded in Stacks Project, Section 30.3.
