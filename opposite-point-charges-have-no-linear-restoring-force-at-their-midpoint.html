Opposite point charges have no linear restoring force at their midpoint (F(f)=2ke​qq3​L−2(1+3f2+O(f4)))

OurBigBook.com Words: 54
Place +q at −L and −q at +L. For a nonzero point charge q3​ between them, the signed force at x=fL is 2ke​qq3​(1+f2)/[L2(1−f2)2]. It is nonzero at f=0 and even in f, so its first nonconstant term is quadratic. The midpoint is not an equilibrium and cannot be treated as a harmonic restoring centre.
