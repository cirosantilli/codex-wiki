Triangle count (T(G))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Graph Triangle in a graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
The triangle count of a finite simple graph is the number of unordered triples of vertices inducing a triangle in a graph. In a tripartite graph with parts X,Y,Z and adjacency indicator functions gXY​,gYZ​,gXZ​, it equals ∣X∣∣Y∣∣Z∣Ex,y,z​gXY​(x,y)gYZ​(y,z)gXZ​(x,z), using uniform expectations on nonempty parts.
