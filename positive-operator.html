Positive operator (T≥0)

 ... Analysis Functional analysis Hilbert space Riesz representation theorem Adjoint operator Hermitian operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 356 Articles: 7
A bounded Hermitian operator T on a Hilbert space is positive when ⟨Tx,x⟩≥0 for every x. Its spectrum is contained in [0,∞).
