Squirmer reflection from a held sphere

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Method of reflections for Stokes flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
For a two-mode tensorial squirmer flow of radius a and a passive equal sphere held at X=Re, with a/R≪1, let s=e⋅Be. Faxén's first law gives holding force Fh​=−9πμa3se/R2+O(μa4∣A∣/R3+μa5∥B∥/R4). Its reflected Stokeslet changes swimming velocity by −9a3se/(4R3). Since this leading force is radial, its vorticity at the swimmer vanishes. The next holding force, generated by the potential dipole, yields rotation a4A×X/(4R6). Terms from the passive sphere's finite radius, reflected stresslet and rotlet are needed at higher order. These formulas assume fixed slip and no externally applied swimmer force or torque.
