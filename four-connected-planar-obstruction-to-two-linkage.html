Four-connected planar obstruction to two-linkage

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Menger theorem Linked graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
On vertices 0,…,7, join cyclic indices differing by one or two modulo eight. The resulting planar graph has a square face bounded in order by 0,2,4,6. Deleting at most three vertices cannot disconnect it: each break in the surviving cyclic chain requires two consecutive deleted vertices, and two separate breaks require four deletions. It is therefore four-connected. Disjoint paths joining 0 to 4 and 2 to 6 would cross in the complementary disk of the square face, contrary to the Jordan curve theorem. Thus four-connectivity does not guarantee two-linkage.
