The mature experiment uses channel-template comparison, pulsar dedispersion, GPS clock-offset transfer, Solar system barycenter corrections and connected pulse counts.[ref]
For a geometric illustration, let a perfectly uniform source emit pulse n at tn​=t0​+nPs​, with spin period Ps​, and let z1​ increase away from the observer. At leading order in orbital velocity divided by the speed of light, an extra path length z1​ adds ΔR​=z1​/c. Consecutive arrivals then obey
Papp​≃Ps​(1+dtdΔR​​)=Ps​(1+cv∥​​).
(24)
A receding source has v∥​>0 and a longer apparent period. This derivation isolates the geometric Doppler effect; a precision relativistic clock model also needs gravitational redshift and time dilation.
