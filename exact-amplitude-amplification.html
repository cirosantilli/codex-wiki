Exact amplitude amplification

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Amplitude amplification
OurBigBook.com Words: 181 Articles: 2
If the initial good amplitude p=sinθ is known, an ancillary qubit can reduce it to sinθ′=pc with θ′=π/(4k+2) and 0<c≤1. After k ordinary amplitude-amplification iterations the good amplitude is sin((2k+1)θ′)=1, so the target state is prepared exactly.
