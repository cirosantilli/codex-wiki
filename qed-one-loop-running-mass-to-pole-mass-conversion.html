In quantum electrodynamics, the modified minimal subtraction scheme running mass differs from the perturbative pole mass by a finite correction. The displayed relation follows by evaluating the renormalized fermion self-energy at the mass shell and imposing a zero of the inverse Dirac propagator. The sign assigned to the self-energy insertion must be matched to its denominator convention; changing that naming convention does not change the conversion. A wave-function coefficient counterterm and multiplicative mass renormalization are also distinct: the coefficient of the mass term includes both at first order.
