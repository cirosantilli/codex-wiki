Vector superfield

 ... Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry Superspace Superfield
OurBigBook.com Words: 845 Articles: 16
A vector superfield is a real superfield, V=V†, used to describe a supersymmetric gauge multiplet. In Wess-Zumino gauge its components are a gauge field, a gaugino and a real auxiliary field.
