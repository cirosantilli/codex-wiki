Brain lesion

 Home Biology Neuroscience Brain
OurBigBook.com Words: 39 Articles: 2
Damage to a region of the brain. An observed deficit can reflect local tissue loss, disconnected pathways or remote dysfunction; preserved performance can reflect redundant routes or compensation. Lesion extent and appropriate sensory and motor controls matter for causal inference.
