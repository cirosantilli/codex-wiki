Grassmann-valued classical supersymmetric mechanics

A classical supersymmetric mechanical model takes its coordinates in a Grassmann algebra: the position is even and the fermionic coordinates are odd. Time differentiation is even, while variation with respect to an odd coordinate uses Grassmann derivatives. For example, moving an odd variation to the left and integrating by parts gives δ∫ψψ˙​dt=2∫δψψ˙​dt.
For the real variant L=x˙2/2+U2/2−ψ1​ψ˙​1​/2+ψ2​ψ˙​2​/2+U′ψ1​ψ2​, the Euler-Lagrange equations are
