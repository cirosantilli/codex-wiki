Collocation Runge-Kutta method

Given distinct nodes ci​, a collocation Runge-Kutta method uses the Lagrange basis ℓj​ with aij​=∫0ci​​ℓj​(τ)dτ and bj​=∫01​ℓj​(τ)dτ. Its stages enforce the differential equation at the collocation nodes.
This applies the collocation method to the time-step polynomial of an ordinary differential equation.
