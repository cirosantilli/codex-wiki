Extremal derivative of a disc map into a symmetric annulus (max∣f′(0)∣=4L/π)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Schwarz lemma Schwarz-Pick theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
Let L>0 and consider holomorphic functions f from the unit disc into e−L<∣w∣<eL, normalized by f(0)=1. Their largest possible derivative modulus is 4L/π. Lift f to its holomorphic logarithm h with h(0)=0, so ∣Reh∣<L. The function tan(πh/(4L)) maps the disc into itself and fixes zero. The Schwarz lemma gives ∣h′(0)∣≤4L/π. Equality holds precisely for
fθ​(z)=exp(π4L​arctan(eiθz)),θ∈R.
(146)
Here arctan is its holomorphic branch vanishing at zero; (4L/π)arctanz is a conformal map from the disc onto the vertical strip. Thus the maximal derivative modulus is attained, with a circle of distinct extremizers rather than a unique map.
