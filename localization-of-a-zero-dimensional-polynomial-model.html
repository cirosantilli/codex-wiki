Localization of a zero-dimensional polynomial model (⟨f⟩=π∑a​ma​f(za​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supersymmetric localization
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
With P=W′ a nonconstant polynomial, positive area measure and Berezin integration oriented positively, a holomorphic polynomial insertion in S=∣P∣2−P′θ1​θ2​−P′θˉ1​θˉ2​ equals π∑a​ma​f(za​), where P(za​)=0 and ma​ is the multiplicity of a root. Rescaling P by t>0 leaves the insertion fixed and localizes it as t→∞.
