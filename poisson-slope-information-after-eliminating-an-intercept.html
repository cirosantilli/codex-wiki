Poisson slope information after eliminating an intercept (Var(β​)≈[∑i​μi​(xi​−xˉμ​)2]−1)

In Poisson regression with log means a+βxi​, the Fisher information matrix is (S0​S1​​S1​S2​​), where Sj​=∑i​xij​μi​. Inverting this matrix gives slope variance S0​/(S0​S2​−S12​). Writing xˉμ​=S1​/S0​ reduces it to the displayed reciprocal weighted sum of squares. Thus a common intercept consumes information about the overall rate, while slope information comes from the spread of the covariates under the mean-count weights. Constant covariates make the two parameters unidentifiable.
