Euler's critical load (Pcr​=π2EIA​/(KL)2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Euler-Bernoulli beam equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 2
A straight slender elastic beam under axial compression loses stability when bending can decrease its total potential energy. With pinned ends a distance L apart, its quadratic potential is 21​∫0L​[EIA​(y′′)2−P(y′)2]dx. Stationarity gives EIA​y′′′′+Py′′=0, with y=y′′=0 at each end. The first nontrivial mode is sin(πx/L), at Pcr​=π2EIA​/L2. More generally Pcr​=π2EIA​/(KL)2, with an effective-length factor K determined by endpoint constraints: K=1 for pinned-pinned, 2 for fixed-free and 1/2 for fixed-fixed. Real imperfections remove the perfectly sharp onset, and short columns can yield before this elastic instability.
