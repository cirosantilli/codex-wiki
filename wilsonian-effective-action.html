Wilsonian effective action

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Effective action
OurBigBook.com Words: 265 Articles: 5
A Wilsonian effective action is obtained by integrating out field modes above a chosen momentum scale while retaining lower-momentum modes as backgrounds. Its local couplings change with the scale according to a renormalization-group flow.
