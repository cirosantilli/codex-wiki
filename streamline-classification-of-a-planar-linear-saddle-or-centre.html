Streamline classification of a planar linear saddle or centre

 Home Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Stream function
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
For (u,v)=(y,ax), the stream function ψ=(y2−ax2)/2 gives hyperbolic streamlines and a saddle when a>0, while a<0 gives elliptical streamlines and a centre.
