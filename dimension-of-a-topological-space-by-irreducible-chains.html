Dimension of a topological space by irreducible chains (dimX)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Irreducible topological space
OurBigBook.com Words: 183 Articles: 4
This is the supremum of lengths of strict chains of nonempty irreducible closed subsets. Closedness is relative to the given space. This is the relevant dimension for locally closed subsets of algebraic varieties, rather than a general Hausdorff-space notion of dimension.
