Pointwise closure of the bounded Lipschitz unit ball (fi​→f pointwise,∥fi​∥BL​≤1 ⟹ ∥f∥BL​≤1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Lipschitz continuity Bounded Lipschitz norm

For every fixed x=y and z, the sum ∣fi​(x)−fi​(y)∣/d(x,y)+∣fi​(z)∣ is at most one. Pointwise convergence preserves this inequality. Taking the independent suprema over the pair (x,y) and the point z proves the assertion. Taking each bound separately would only give the weaker estimate two.
