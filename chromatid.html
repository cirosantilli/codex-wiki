Chromatid

 Home Biology Genetics Chromosome
OurBigBook.com Words: 39 Articles: 1
A chromatid is one of the two copies of a replicated chromosome. Each homologous chromosome contributes two sister chromatids before separation in meiosis; chromosomal crossovers can exchange segments between nonsister chromatids.
