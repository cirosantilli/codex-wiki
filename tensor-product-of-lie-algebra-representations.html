Tensor product of Lie algebra representations (V⊗W)

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Lie algebra homomorphism Lie algebra representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 377 Articles: 6
The tensor product of Lie algebra representations has action x(v⊗w)=xv⊗w+v⊗xw. The Lie algebra representation identity follows because operators on the two different tensor factors commute. This action descends to exterior powers and symmetric powers.
