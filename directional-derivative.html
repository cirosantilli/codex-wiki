Directional derivative (Dv​f)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Partial derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 134 Articles: 2
The directional derivative of f at x in the direction v is
Dv​f(x)=dtd​f(x+tv)​t=0​.
(35)
For a differentiable scalar function on Euclidean space, it equals v⋅∇f(x).
