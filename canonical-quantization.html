Canonical quantization

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 270 Articles: 6
Canonical quantization promotes canonical variables to operators and their Poisson brackets to canonical commutation relations. Composite observables require an ordering prescription, and quantum anomalies can modify their algebra.
