Riesz dual basis in an inner product space ((ui​,uk​)=δik​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Inner product Gram matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For a basis (ui​) of a finite-dimensional inner product space with conjugate-linear first argument, define Gij​=(ui​,uj​) and uk​=∑l​(G−1)lk​ul​. Then (ui​,uk​)=δik​ and (uj​,uk​)=(G−1)jk​. This is the inner-product representation of the dual basis; requiring the dual vectors to lie in the given span is essential.
