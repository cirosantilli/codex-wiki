Saffman integral (L)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Homogeneous turbulence Isotropic turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 2
The Saffman integral is L=∫⟨u(x)⋅u(x+r)⟩d3r. With integrable trace correlation it equals the large-volume limit of ⟨∣∫V​udV∣2⟩/∣V∣. It is nonnegative and measures momentum-fluctuation density. Under appropriate unforced far-field flux conditions it is invariant.
