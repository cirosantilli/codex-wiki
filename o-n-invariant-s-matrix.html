O(N)-invariant S-matrix (S=S2​I+S3​P+S1​K)

For particles in the vector representation of the orthogonal group, the two-body S-matrix is a linear combination of the identity I, permutation P and trace contraction K: S=S2​I+S3​P+S1​K. Here K(ei​⊗ej​)=δij​∑a​ea​⊗ea​, P2=I, PK=KP=K and K2=NK. The orthogonal-invariant scattering channels diagonalize these three operators simultaneously.
