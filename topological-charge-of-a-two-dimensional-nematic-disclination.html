Topological charge of a two-dimensional nematic disclination (q=Δθ/(2π))

 ... Critical phenomenon Order parameter Liquid crystal Nematic liquid crystal Nematic director Nematic disclination
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
For a continuous director angle around a closed counterclockwise circuit, the enclosed topological charge is q=Δθ/(2π). Since θ is defined modulo π, elementary nematic disclinations can have q=±1/2.
