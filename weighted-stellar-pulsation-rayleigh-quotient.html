Weighted stellar pulsation Rayleigh quotient (R[ξ])

For radial adiabatic stellar oscillations, the quotient is R[ξ]=∫[γpr4∣ξ′∣2+r3((4−3γ)p)′∣ξ∣2]dr/∫ρr4∣ξ∣2dr. Regular-centre and free-surface endpoint conditions remove the boundary term. The infimum is the fundamental squared frequency; any negative trial value proves radial instability. A variable stellar adiabatic exponent must remain inside the derivative.
