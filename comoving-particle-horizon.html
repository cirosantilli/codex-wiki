Comoving particle horizon (rp​(t)=c∫0t​a(t′)dt′​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Particle horizon
OurBigBook.com Words: 140 Articles: 2
The comoving particle horizon is the particle horizon expressed in comoving coordinates, assuming a finite initial cosmic-time boundary at zero. In a spatially flat FLRW metric, it is the distance a photon could have travelled since that boundary. The corresponding proper distance in cosmology at time t is a(t)rp​(t). It differs from the future-directed cosmological event horizon and from the instantaneous comoving Hubble radius.
