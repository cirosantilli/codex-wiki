Normal-ordered free scalar four-momentum (Pμ=∫(2π)3d3p​pμap†​ap​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 1
For a canonically quantized real scalar field, the quadratic Hamiltonian contains 21​∑p​Ep​ in a regulated box. Normal ordering subtracts this zero-point energy, leaving
Pμ=∫(2π)3d3p​pμap†​ap​.
(11)
The vacuum momentum is zero with an inversion-symmetric regulator. The canonical commutation relation then gives [Pμ,ap†​]=pμap†​, identifying the momentum and energy added by one particle. The un-subtracted energy differs by a constant and is not literally the same operator.
