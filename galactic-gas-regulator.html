Galactic gas regulator

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy evolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 219 Articles: 5
A gas-budget model in which inflow competes with star formation, gas return and outflows. A zero instantaneous gas derivative defines a balance nullcline; it need not define a fixed point of the evolving stellar and black-hole masses.
