Phase matching

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Interference (wave propagation)
OurBigBook.com Words: 298 Articles: 6
A relation between wave phases that lets contributions from successive interaction regions add coherently. In Cherenkov radiation, the condition along the particle trajectory is ω=k⋅v, relating angular frequency, wave vector and particle velocity.
