Ambivalent group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Finite group
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A finite group is ambivalent when every element is conjugate to its inverse. Since every complex character satisfies χ(g−1)=χ(g)​, an ambivalent group has real values for every irreducible character. Conversely, if every irreducible value is real, irreducible characters separate conjugacy classes and force every g to be conjugate to g−1.
