Decimal period from multiplicative order (ordq​(10))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Multiplicative order
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For q>1 coprime to 10, long division of 1/q has remainders rn​≡10n(modq) in 1,…,q−1. Its digits satisfy 10rn−1​=qan​+rn​. A congruence 10m≡1(modq) therefore makes the remainders and digits periodic with period dividing m. The least period is the multiplicative order of 10 modulo q.
