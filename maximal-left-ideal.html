Maximal left ideal (L⊊A)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Left ideal
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 1
A maximal left ideal is a proper left ideal contained in no larger proper left ideal. In a unital Banach algebra it is norm closed: its closure is a left ideal, and if that closure were the entire algebra it would contain elements of L within distance less than one of 1, hence invertible by the Neumann series. A proper left ideal contains no invertible element. The quotient A/L is consequently a quotient Banach space and a simple module.
