Variational problem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Calculus of variations
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
A variational problem seeks a stationary point or minimizer of a functional on a specified function space. For a symmetric coercive bilinear form a and bounded linear functional ℓ on a real Hilbert space, minimizing J(v)=a(v,v)/2−ℓ(v) is equivalent to the weak formulation a(u,v)=ℓ(v) for every test vector. The Lax-Milgram theorem gives a unique solution, and J(u+w)−J(u)=a(w,w)/2 proves unique minimization. Specifying the admissible space and its boundary constraints is part of the problem.
