Equitable regularity energy (q(P))

For a set partition P of the vertices of a graph of order n, define
q(P)=n21​∑A,B∈P​∣A∣∣B∣d(A,B)2,
(13)
where the sum is ordered, the adjacency indicator defines the density also on diagonal pairs, and exceptional vertices are treated as singleton parts. Refinement cannot decrease this energy, by the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality. An irregular pair of equal cells of size m, with witnesses of relative size at least ε and density discrepancy greater than ε, increases the energy by more than ε4m2/n2 for each orientation. Splitting refined atoms into equal chunks and leftover singleton parts is a further refinement, so restoring equitability causes no energy loss.
