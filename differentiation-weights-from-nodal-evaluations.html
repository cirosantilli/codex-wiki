At distinct nodes a0​,…,an​, the Lagrange interpolation polynomial has basis ℓj​(x)=∏i=j​(x−ai​)/(aj​−ai​). Differentiating its exact expression for a polynomial of degree at most n gives the displayed weights. Their uniqueness follows because the nodal evaluations form a basis of the dual space of that polynomial space.
