Thermal radiation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic radiation
OurBigBook.com Words: 62 Articles: 1
Thermal radiation is electromagnetic radiation emitted by matter because of its temperature. Matter in thermal equilibrium emits a spectrum governed by its absorptivity, approaching the Planck law for a blackbody.
