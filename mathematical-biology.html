SIR model with demography and permanent immunity

Words: 102 Articles: 1
Compartmental model OurBigBook.com
With susceptible, infective, and immune populations X,Y,Z, equal per-capita birth and death rate μ, mass-action transmission coefficient β, and recovery rate ν, the equations are
X′=μN−βXY−μX,Y′=βXY−(μ+ν)Y,Z′=νY−μZ.
(20)
The conserved population is N=X+Y+Z, and the threshold population is Nc​=(μ+ν)/β.

Endemic equilibrium of the SIR model with demography

Words: 49
SIR model with demography and permanent immunity OurBigBook.com
When N>Nc​=(μ+ν)/β, the positive equilibrium is
X∗=Nc​,Y∗=μ+νμ​(N−Nc​),Z∗=μ+νν​(N−Nc​).
(21)
On the invariant plane X+Y+Z=N, the (X,Y) Jacobian at this equilibrium has negative trace −(μ+βY∗) and positive determinant βY∗(μ+ν), so both eigenvalues have negative real part and the equilibrium is locally asymptotically stable.

SIR model with waning immunity

For susceptible, infective, and recovered populations S,I,R, mass-action infection coefficient β, recovery rate ν, and immunity-loss rate f, the SIRS equations are
S′=fR−βIS,I′=βIS−νI,R′=νI−fR.
(22)
They conserve S+I+R=N and have basic reproduction number R0​=βN/ν.

Endemic equilibrium of the SIR model with waning immunity

Words: 59
SIR model with waning immunity OurBigBook.com
When βN>ν and f>0, the positive equilibrium is
S∗=βν​,I∗=β(f+ν)f(βN−ν)​,R∗=β(f+ν)ν(βN−ν)​.
(23)
Writing a=βI∗>0, the reduced (S,I) Jacobian has characteristic polynomial
λ2+(f+a)λ+a(f+ν).
(24)
