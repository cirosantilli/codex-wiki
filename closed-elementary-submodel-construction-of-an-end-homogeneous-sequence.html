Closed elementary-submodel construction of an end-homogeneous sequence

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Partition relation Erdős-Rado theorem for finite arities
OurBigBook.com Words: 122
Put θ=(2μ)+ for an infinite cardinal μ. Choose M≺Hχ​ of size 2μ, containing all ordinals below μ+, and closed under externally given sequences of length at most μ. Such a model is built by μ+ successive Skolem hull closures, since (2μ)μ=2μ. For a coloring of [θ]r+1 with at most μ colors in M, put β=sup(M∩θ)<θ. Recursively choose xα​∈M∩θ for α<μ+, above all earlier points, so that every earlier r-tuple has the same color with xα​ as with β. There are at most μ constraints, their code belongs to M by closure, and β witnesses their consistency. The elementary embedding supplies the required witness in M. The resulting sequence is end-homogeneous, providing the induction step in the Erdős-Rado theorem for finite arities.
