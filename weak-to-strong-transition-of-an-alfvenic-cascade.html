Weak-to-strong transition of an Alfvénic cascade (l⊥,∗​∼L⊥​χ02​)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Magnetohydrodynamic turbulence Alfvénic turbulence Weak Alfvénic cascade
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
In a weak Alfvénic cascade at fixed l∥​, χ=τA​/τnl​∝l⊥−1/2​ grows as the perpendicular scale shrinks. If its outer value is χ0​≪1 at L⊥​, it reaches order one at the displayed scale. Beyond it the weak-interaction ordering fails and a critical balance model may become appropriate.
