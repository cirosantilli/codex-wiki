Stochastic integral under an absolutely continuous measure change

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Semimartingale Semimartingale stability under an absolutely continuous measure change
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
If Q≪P and X is a semimartingale under both measures, the two integrals of any locally bounded predictable H against X agree up to Q-indistinguishability. Elementary integrals are the same increment sums. Bounded pointwise convergence of integrands, stochastic dominated convergence, and transfer of probability convergence from P to Q extend equality by the monotone-class theorem. Localize to remove boundedness.
