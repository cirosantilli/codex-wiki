Equip the finite-dimensional real vector space V with a positive-definite inner product. A reduced crystallographic root system is a finite set R⊂V∖{0} spanning V such that, for every α∈R,
R∩Rα={α,−α},sα​(R)=R,(α,α)2(β,α)​∈Z(β∈R),

where sα​(v)=v−2(v,α)α/(α,α) is the orthogonal reflection in α⊥. The Weyl group is W(R)=⟨sα​:α∈R⟩. It permutes the finite set R. Its action is faithful because R spans V: an element fixing every root fixes every vector. Hence it embeds in the finite symmetric group of R and is finite.
A Cartan subalgebra is a nilpotent self-normalizing Lie subalgebra. For a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra it is equivalently a maximal toral subalgebra: a maximal abelian subalgebra whose elements have diagonalizable adjoint actions. The commuting semisimple operators ad(h) give the root-space decomposition
g=h⊕⨁α∈R​gα​,gα​={x:[h,x]=α(h)x for every h∈h}.
(17)
The nonzero weights α are the roots. Their real span inside the complex dual h∗, with the positive-definite real inner product induced by the Killing form, is the Euclidean space in which they form a reduced crystallographic root system. In particular one uses this real span, rather than treating all of h∗ as a real vector space of twice the rank.

Its Dynkin diagram is the connected chain α2​−α1​−α3​. A decomposition into mutually orthogonal nonempty root systems would separate this connected set of simple roots, which is impossible. Thus the root system is irreducible. The chain is also the diagram A3​, which is the root system of sl4​(C). The classification of complex semisimple Lie algebras by their root systems yields the Lie algebra isomorphism
so6​(C)≅sl4​(C).​
(23)
An explicit explanation of this coincidence uses the exterior-power Lie algebra representation of sl4​ on ⋀2C4, a six-dimensional space. After choosing a volume form, u∧v defines a nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form on that exterior square. The induced action preserves it because a traceless endomorphism acts trivially on the top exterior power. This gives a nonzero homomorphism sl4​→so6​. Its kernel is an ideal in the simple algebra sl4​, so it is zero; equal dimensions 15 then make it an isomorphism. Over C all nondegenerate symmetric forms in dimension six are equivalent, including the form with matrix J.
