In the simple module Lm​, weight 0 occurs once if m is even and never if m is odd. Weight 1 occurs once if m is odd and never if m is even. Thus each simple summand contributes exactly one to dimV0​+dimV1​.
Complete reducibility of semisimple Lie algebra representations writes V=⨁m​Lm⊕am​​. Taking the two weight spaces commutes with this finite direct sum, so
dimV0​+dimV1​=m∑​am​.​
(9)
The right side counts irreducible summands with multiplicity, independently of a chosen decomposition.
