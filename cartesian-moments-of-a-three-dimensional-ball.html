Cartesian moments of a three-dimensional ball (∫∣x∣≤a​xi​xj​dV=154πa5​δij​)

Spherical symmetry makes moments over a ball isotropic tensors. Odd moments vanish under x↦−x, while the second and fourth moments are
∫∣x∣≤a​xi​xj​dV=154πa5​δij​,∫∣x∣≤a​xi​xj​xk​xl​dV=1054πa7​(δij​δkl​+δik​δjl​+δil​δjk​).
(23)
For the second moment, a tensor contraction gives 3α=∫r2dV=4πa5/5. For the fourth, complete index symmetry makes the three coefficients equal and a double tensor contraction gives 15β=∫r4dV=4πa7/7. These identities turn polynomial volume integrals into scalar radial integrals and are useful for inertia and rotational averages.
