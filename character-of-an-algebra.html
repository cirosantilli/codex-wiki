Character of an algebra

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 727 Articles: 14
A character on a complex algebra is a nonzero multiplicative linear functional φ:A→C.
Algebra characters form the domain of the Gelfand representation; they are distinct from traces of group representations.
