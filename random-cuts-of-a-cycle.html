Random cuts of a cycle (Pr(i↔j)=(rn​)(rk​)+(rn−k​)​)

Choose r distinct edges uniformly from an n-vertex cycle graph, with 1≤r≤n, and delete them. Two specified vertices at oriented distance k remain connected exactly when one of their two complementary edge-disjoint paths is uncut. All cuts must then lie in the other path, giving the displayed probability. The two events are disjoint because at least one edge is cut. Set (ra​)=0 when a<r.
