Orbital period (T)

 ... Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Kepler orbit Orbital element Mean motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 1
The orbital period is the time required to complete one orbit. It is related to mean motion by T=2π/n.
