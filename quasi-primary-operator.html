Quasi-primary operator ((h,h))

 ... Physics Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Worldsheet Two-dimensional conformal field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
A quasi-primary operator transforms with weights (h,h) under global conformal, or Möbius, transformations. A primary operator has this covariance under every locally invertible holomorphic and antiholomorphic coordinate change. In radial quantization, quasi-primary states are annihilated by L1​,L1​, whereas primary states are annihilated by every positive Virasoro mode. The stress tensor at nonzero central charge is quasi-primary but not primary because its transformation under a general coordinate change includes a Schwarzian term.
