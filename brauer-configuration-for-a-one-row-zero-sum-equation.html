Brauer configuration for a one-row zero-sum equation

 ... Combinatorics Ramsey theory Partition regular matrix Columns property Rado's theorem Rado theorem for one equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
Suppose nonzero integer coefficients have a nonempty zero-sum subset I, and fix i0​∈I. Put s=∣ai0​​∣ and C=∑j∈/I​aj​. A Brauer progression theorem configuration containing a sufficiently long monochromatic progression of step d and the point sd gives a positive solution: take every outside variable equal to sd, and the inside variables from the progression with offsets λi​, where λi0​​=−sgn(ai0​​)C and the other offsets are zero. Shifting all inside offsets by the same integer keeps their weighted sum unchanged, because ∑i∈I​ai​=0. This removes negative offsets while preserving the equation.
