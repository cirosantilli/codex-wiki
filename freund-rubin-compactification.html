Freund-Rubin compactification (F4​=fvol(AdS4​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supergravity Eleven-dimensional supergravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
A product compactification supported by a four-form proportional to one factor's volume form. For the maximally supersymmetric membrane throat, the other factor is a round S7 and the radii obey RS7​=2RAdS​. The flux links the curvature scales through the supergravity field equations.
