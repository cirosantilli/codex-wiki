Projective gradient criterion in characteristic dividing the degree

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Projective hypersurface
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For a reduced projective hypersurface X=V(f) over an algebraically closed field,
Sing(X)=X∩V(fx0​​,…,fxn​​).
(35)
The Euler homogeneous function theorem makes the intersection redundant only when the characteristic does not divide the degree. In characteristic p, the irreducible polynomial x0p​+x1p−1​x2​ has zero gradient at [1:0:0], which is not on X.
