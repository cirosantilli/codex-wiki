Zero modes of a positive transverse Pauli field (χk​=e−ky−∫0y​a(s)ds)

For B=B(y)ez​ with B(y)≥B0​>0, take a(y)=(e/ℏ)∫0y​B(s)ds. The spin-down transverse Hamiltonian at momentum label −k is proportional to M†M, where M=∂y​+k+a(y). It is nonnegative, and Mχk​=0 gives the displayed zero mode. Since a′≥eB0​/ℏ, its exponent has a negative quadratic upper bound at both infinities, proving square integrability for every k. The continuously varying momentum label supplies an infinitely degenerate zero-energy space.
