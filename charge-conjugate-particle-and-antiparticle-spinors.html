Charge-conjugate particle and antiparticle spinors (v(p,s)=Cuˉ(p,s)T)

OurBigBook.com Words: 73
Choose the negative-frequency Dirac spinors as the charge conjugates of the positive-frequency spinors. Transposing uˉ(p−m)=0 and using the charge-conjugation matrix identity gives (p+m)CuˉT=0, so the displayed choice solves the correct antiparticle equation. In the mode expansion of a Dirac field, the unitary charge-conjugation operator then exchanges particle and antiparticle annihilation operators, with conjugate phases on their creation operators. A different spinor basis can insert conventional phases or a spin-index relabeling; the displayed equality specifies a compatible choice.
