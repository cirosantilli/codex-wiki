Isothermal sound speed (ai​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Compressible flow Speed of sound
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
The isothermal sound speed is ai2​=(∂p/∂ρ)T​. For an ideal gas it is ai2​=p/ρ=RT/μ.
