Apparent horizon

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Null hypersurface Trapped surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
An apparent horizon is the outer boundary of the future-trapped region on a chosen spatial slice, under the usual regularity conditions. Its location depends on the slice; it is not the globally defined event horizon. At a regular boundary the outward null expansion vanishes while the inward null expansion is negative. Its area cannot be substituted into every version of the Penrose inequality without the appropriate area and initial-data hypotheses.
