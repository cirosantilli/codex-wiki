Fusion of Jones-Wenzl colors

In the reduced theory the product of colors a and b decomposes with multiplicity one into colors c satisfying ∣a−b∣≤c≤min(a+b,2r−4−a−b) and a+b+c even. These rules follow by iterating 1⊗j=(j−1)⊕(j+1), with color r−1 set to zero. The closure traces give da​db​=∑c​Nabc​dc​, and self-duality makes the multiplicities symmetric in all three colors.
