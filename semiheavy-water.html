Semiheavy water (HDO)

OurBigBook.com Words: 16
Semiheavy water contains one ordinary hydrogen nucleus and one deuterium nucleus. Dilute semiheavy water in heavy water provides relatively isolated O–H groups for infrared spectroscopy.
