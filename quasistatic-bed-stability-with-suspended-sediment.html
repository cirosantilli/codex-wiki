Quasistatic bed stability with suspended sediment (σ=−4κ2/(1+κ2)−iκ[3β+4/(1+κ2)])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Sediment transport Exner equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
A leading fixed-discharge, level-surface approximation to a shallow erodible-bed flow gives perturbation equations Cx​=4U−C and Ut​+3βUx​=−4U+C. The displayed Fourier growth rate has negative real part for every nonzero wave number, independently of bedload parameter β. Bedload changes the propagation speed but not this damping. The zero-wave-number mode is neutral, so absence of growing modes should not be confused with strict asymptotic decay of every spatially uniform perturbation.
