Energy eigenstate

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Energy eigenvalue
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
An energy eigenstate is a nonzero quantum state represented by an eigenvector of the Hamiltonian operator. Its time evolution changes only its overall phase.
