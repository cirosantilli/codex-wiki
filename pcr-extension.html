PCR extension

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Polymerase chain reaction PCR thermal cycle
OurBigBook.com Words: 383
PCR extension is template-directed construction of DNA from an annealed PCR primer by a DNA polymerase. Incoming deoxynucleoside triphosphates supply the new nucleotides. Each addition lengthens the growing strand at its 3′ end, so the product grows 5′→3′ while the template is read 3′→5′. The direction refers to the nucleic acid backbone, not to left or right on a page.[ref]
For a complete worked copying step, start with template 3′-TCAGGGAC-5′ and paired PCR primer 5′-AGTC-3′. The next four template letters are GGAC, encountered in the template's 3′-to-5′ direction. The base pairing rule therefore selects CCTG in order, giving
5′-primerAGTC​​extensionCCTG​​-3′.
(11)
The PCR primer supplies four nucleotides, and PCR extension adds four more; this copying event consumes four deoxynucleoside triphosphates. On the oppositely oriented template 5′-AGTCCCTG-3′, the other PCR primer is 5′-CAGG-3′. It pairs at the right and extends leftward. Reading that template from right to left encounters CTGA after the paired region, so addition of GACT gives 5′-CAGGGACT-3′. Both finished products are the reverse complements of their respective templates.
The direction argument is short: the PCR primer and its template are antiparallel nucleic acid strands; extension takes place at the PCR primer's 3′ end; hence forward progress along the new strand is backward progress along the template. Reflecting a drawing reverses its arrows on the page without changing either chemical direction. This is why a leftward arrow can represent 5′→3′ synthesis.
The time needed depends on how much DNA remains to be copied and the chosen DNA polymerase, while temperature must support that enzyme's activity.[ref] Separating PCR annealing and PCR extension into different holds is convenient, but some compatible conditions allow a combined hold. The three chemical jobs therefore need not always require three distinct temperatures.[ref]
