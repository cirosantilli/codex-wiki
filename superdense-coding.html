Superdense coding

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bell state
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
Superdense coding uses a shared Bell state to communicate two classical bits by transmitting one qubit. The sender selects one of the four Bell states with a local Pauli operation, and the receiver performs a Bell-basis measurement.
