Brauer first main theorem

 ... Algebra Representation theory Modular representation theory Block of a group algebra Defect group of a block Brauer correspondence
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
Brauer's first main theorem gives a bijection between the blocks of kG with defect group D and the blocks of kNG​(D) with defect group D. Corresponding blocks are related by their images under the Brauer morphism.
