Stone-Čech compactification (βX)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Compactification (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 234 Articles: 2
For a completely regular Hausdorff space, the Stone-Čech compactification is the weak-star closure of the evaluation functionals in the dual unit ball of Cb​(X). The Banach-Alaoglu theorem gives compactness; complete regularity makes evaluation a topological embedding. Every bounded real continuous function extends by f​(p)=p(f), yielding an isometric isomorphism Cb​(X)≅C(βX). For a continuous map into a compact Hausdorff space K, the adjoint of pullback sends this closure into the closed evaluation copy of K, yielding the unique continuous extension. Uniqueness follows from density. It is a universal compactification, not a homeomorphism between X and the whole dual space.
