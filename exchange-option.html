Exchange option ((S1​(T)−S2​(T))+)

OurBigBook.com Words: 59
An exchange option permits delivery of one asset in exchange for another at maturity, producing the positive-part payoff (S1​(T)−S2​(T))+. In a complete finite market its price is the discounted expected value under the unique risk-neutral measure. For a two-period joint binomial model, evaluating this payoff at all four terminal paths and weighting by their risk-neutral path probabilities gives its price directly.
