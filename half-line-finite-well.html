Half-line finite well

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state Bound state
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 1
A one-dimensional well V=−V0​ on 0<x<a, with an infinite wall at zero and V=0 beyond a. For −V0​<E<0, the interior wavefunction is proportional to sinkx and the exterior decays as e−qx. Continuity gives kcotka=−q, where k2=2m(E+V0​)/ℏ2 and q2=−2mE/ℏ2. Its half-line finite-well bound-state count has a nonzero minimum depth, unlike a full-line attractive one-dimensional well.
