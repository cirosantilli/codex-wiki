Nonlinear sigma model

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Spontaneous symmetry breaking
OurBigBook.com Words: 976 Articles: 17
A nonlinear sigma model has fields valued in a curved target space such as a vacuum manifold G/H. Its leading action is quadratic in spacetime derivatives but nonlinear in any unconstrained coordinates used for the target.
