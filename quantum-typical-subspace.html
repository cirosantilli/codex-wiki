Quantum typical subspace (Tε(n)​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Schumacher compression
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 1
For a density operator π=∑i​qi​∣ϕi​⟩⟨ϕi​∣, the quantum typical subspace is spanned by product eigenvectors of π⊗n whose eigenvalues qi1​​⋯qin​​ satisfy
​−n1​log2​(qi1​​⋯qin​​)−S(π)​≤ε.
(2)
It applies the classical weakly typical sequence definition to the spectrum. If Pε(n)​ is its orthogonal projection, then
dimTε(n)​≤2n(S(π)+ε),Tr(π⊗nPε(n)​)⟶1.
(3)
The first statement follows from the typical-set cardinality bounds, and the second from the weak law of large numbers. This subspace holds nearly all the source probability while using exponentially fewer dimensions than the whole space when S(π)<log2​d.
