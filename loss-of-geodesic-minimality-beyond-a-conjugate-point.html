Loss of geodesic minimality beyond a conjugate point

 ... Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Energy of a curve Variation vector field Riemannian index form Conjugate-point criterion for the Riemannian index form
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
Let a nonzero Jacobi field J vanish at times zero and a. On a longer geodesic segment ending at b>a, extend it by zero to a continuous piecewise smooth field Z. Then I(Z,Z)=0. Choose an endpoint-vanishing field P with P(a)=−Dt​J(a); integration by parts gives I(Z,P)=−∣Dt​J(a)∣2<0. Thus I(Z+εP,Z+εP)<0 for small positive ε. Smooth approximation preserves negativity. The second variation of geodesic energy supplies a shorter path, so a minimizing geodesic cannot extend beyond its first conjugate point.
