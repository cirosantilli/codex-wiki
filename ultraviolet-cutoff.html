Ultraviolet cutoff (Λ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Effective field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
An ultraviolet cutoff removes modes above a chosen energy or momentum scale. An effective field theory encodes the influence of those omitted short-distance modes in its couplings.
