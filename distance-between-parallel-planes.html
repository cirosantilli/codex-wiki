Distance between parallel planes (∣(a1​−a2​)⋅n∣)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Plane
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
Planes with common unit normal n have distance ∣(a1​−a2​)⋅n∣. Every joining displacement has this normal component, giving a lower bound by its absolute value; a displacement parallel to the normal attains the bound.
For a common nonzero normal vector n, not necessarily of unit Euclidean norm, the planes n⋅x=d1​ and n⋅x=d2​ have distance ∣d2​−d1​∣/∥n∥. Thus 2x−y+2z=3 and 2x−y+2z=9 are distance 6/3=2 apart. First put both plane equations into the same normal-vector scale: subtracting offsets from independently rescaled equations need not give a geometric distance.
