OurBigBook.com Words: 33
For real x>0, its small-argument expansion is Ci(x)=γE​+logx+O(x2). Integrating Ci′(x)=cosx/x separates the logarithm from the regular correction. Its conventional constant is the Euler--Mascheroni constant; the primary series normalization is dlmf.nist.gov/6.6.E6 .
