Direct CP asymmetry (ACP​=(Γ−Γˉ)/(Γ+Γˉ))

OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A direct CP asymmetry compares decay widths for a process and its CP symmetry conjugate. Let two decay amplitudes contribute with real nonnegative magnitudes a1​,a2​, absorptive phases δi​ and weak phases ϕi​. Then A=a1​ei(δ1​+ϕ1​)+a2​ei(δ2​+ϕ2​) and A=a1​ei(δ1​−ϕ1​)+a2​ei(δ2​−ϕ2​). Expanding the absolute squares gives
∣A∣2−∣A∣2=−4a1​a2​sin(δ1​−δ2​)sin(ϕ1​−ϕ2​).
(7)
Thus two nonzero contributions and both kinds of relative phase are required in this example. Equal total decay widths under CPT symmetry constrain the sum over channels and do not forbid individual channel asymmetries.
