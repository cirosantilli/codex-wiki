K correction (mR​=MR​+5log10​(dL​/10pc)+KR​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astronomical magnitude Absolute magnitude
OurBigBook.com Words: 114 Articles: 1
The K-correction converts an observed photometric passband to a specified emitted photometric passband, accounting for cosmological redshift and the source spectrum. For a narrow same-band measurement, KR​=−2.5log10​[(1+z)Lν​((1+z)νR​)/Lν​(νR​)]. Thus its sign depends on the spectrum, not on redshift alone. For Lν​∝να, it is −2.5(1+α)log10​(1+z). Finite-band corrections use the actual detector response and magnitude zero point.
