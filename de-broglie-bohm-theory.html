de Broglie-Bohm theory

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Foundations of quantum mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 520 Articles: 10
A deterministic interpretation with a definite particle configuration guided by the wavefunction. The latter obeys the Time-dependent Schrödinger equation, while the particle follows the guidance equation. A Born rule distribution of initial positions is preserved by quantum equilibrium equivariance. The guidance law restricts admissible initial velocities, even when trajectories are rewritten as a second-order force equation.
