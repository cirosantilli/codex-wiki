Unit index of a CM-field ([OK×​:μK​OK+×​]∈{1,2})

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Number field Totally imaginary number field CM-field
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For a CM-field, put c for conjugation and EK​=OK×​. The map u↦u/c(u) takes values in the cyclic group μK​ of roots of unity: all conjugates have absolute value one, and the Kronecker theorem on algebraic integers in the unit disk applies. Its kernel is EK+​, while its image on μK​ is μK2​. It therefore embeds EK​/(EK+​μK​) into μK​/μK2​, a group of order two. This proves the index bound without assuming that every unit is a real unit times a root of unity.
