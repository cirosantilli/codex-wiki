Half-line mKdV spectral global relation (S−1s=e4ik3Tσ3​μ3​(0,T,k))

For the three corner-normalized Lax pair eigenfunctions, let s=μ3​(0,0,k) and S=e4ik3Tσ3​μ2​(0,T,k)−1. Their constant connection matrices give the displayed global relation. If their second columns are (b,a)T and (B,A)T, its upper-right entry is Ab−Ba=e8ik3Tμ3,12​(0,T,k). The right side is analytic in the lower half-plane. In the linear limit this is the backward-sign Airy half-line global relation at Fourier parameter 2k, coupling all three boundary traces rather than allowing three independent boundary conditions.
