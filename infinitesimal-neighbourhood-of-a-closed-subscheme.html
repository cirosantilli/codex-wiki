Infinitesimal neighbourhood of a closed subscheme (Xn​=V(In+1))

 ... Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Closed immersion Closed subscheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For a closed subscheme defined by an ideal sheaf I, its nth infinitesimal neighbourhood has structure sheaf OX​/In+1. All these neighbourhoods have the same underlying topological space because the radicals of I and its positive powers agree. Their inverse limit of structure sheaves is the formal completion of a scheme.
