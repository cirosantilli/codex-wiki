Geometric distribution

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 496 Articles: 7
A geometric distribution models the number of independent Bernoulli trials needed to obtain the first success. On the convention supported by 1,2,…, its mass is (1−p)k−1p and its mean is 1/p. On the failures-before-first-success convention supported by 0,1,…, its mass is (1−p)kp and its mean is (1−p)/p. Both conventions have variance (1−p)/p2.
