Two-orbital fermionic pairing Hamiltonian (H=ε(a†a+b†b)+Δ(ab+b†a†))

For distinct fermionic orbitals obeying the full canonical anticommutation relations, fix the Fock state order ∣11⟩=a†b†∣0⟩. With real ε,Δ>0, the even-fermion parity matrix is (0−Δ​−Δ2ε​), since ab∣11⟩=−∣0⟩. The two odd-fermion parity energy eigenvalues are both ε. The even-fermion parity energy eigenvalues are ε±ε2+Δ2​. This finite model isolates the relative sign and constant shift required in a fermionic Bogoliubov transformation without any momentum sum or particle-hole doubling factor.
