Stochastic SIR model

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Compartmental models (epidemiology) SIR model
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 2
The stochastic SIR model is a continuous-time Markov chain with infection jump (−1,1,0) at rate βSI and recovery jump (0,−1,1) at rate γI. The state space consists of nonnegative integer compartment counts with conserved total population.
