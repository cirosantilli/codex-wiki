For x′′+ε(x2−1)x′+x=0, the averaged amplitude for position-dependent damping has the displayed logistic-type equation. With A(0)=1, its positive solution is A(T)=2/1+3e−T​. Small amplitudes grow and large ones decay toward the stable value two; the leading oscillation is A(εt)cost.
