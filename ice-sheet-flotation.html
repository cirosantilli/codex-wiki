Ice-sheet flotation (ρH=ρw​b)

 ... Branches of physics Geophysics Glaciology Ice sheet dynamics Ice shelf Grounding line
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
At a grounding line, hydrostatic pressures balance when the weight of an ice column of thickness H and mass density ρ equals the weight of its displaced water, of depth b and mass density ρw​. Thus flotation gives ρH=ρw​b. Its time derivative along a moving grounding line relates local thickness changes to grounding-line speed; it supplies a different boundary condition from the unbuttressed Newtonian grounding-line stress.
