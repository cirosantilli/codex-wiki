Comodule ((M,ρ))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Coalgebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 170 Articles: 5
A right comodule is an object M with a coaction ρ:M→M⊗C satisfying the coassociativity and counit laws. This definition extends from coalgebras to comonoids in a monoidal category.
