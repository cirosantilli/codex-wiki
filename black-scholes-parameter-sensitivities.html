Black-Scholes parameter sensitivities (pσ​=σ(T−t)x2pxx​,pρ​=(T−t)(xpx​−p))

For a fixed twice-differentiable terminal payoff with sufficient growth control, differentiate its lognormal distribution pricing integral. Gaussian integration by parts gives the displayed identities. Convexity implies pxx​≥0, so increasing spot volatility cannot lower the price. The interest rate sensitivity depends on the sign of the cash position p−xpx​. Calendar-time sensitivity is pt​=ρ(p−xpx​)−21​σ2x2pxx​. Thus a convex payoff with a negative cash position has a nonincreasing price at fixed spot when ρ≥0. Negative rates invalidate that conclusion: f(x)=x−K gives p=x−Ke−ρ(T−t) and pt​=−ρKe−ρ(T−t)>0 when ρ<0.
