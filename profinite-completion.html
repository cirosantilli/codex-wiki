Profinite completion (G)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Profinite group
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 1
The profinite completion of a group G is the inverse limit of its finite quotient groups:
G=lim​N◃f​G​G/N.
(5)
The canonical map G→G has dense image and is injective exactly when G is residually finite.
