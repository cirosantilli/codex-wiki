Graph Ramsey number

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Ramsey theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 191 Articles: 5
For a finite graph G, its two-colour Ramsey number R(G) is the least n such that every red-blue colouring of Kn​ contains a monochromatic copy of G. It exists because a monochromatic clique on ∣V(G)∣ vertices contains a copy of G.
