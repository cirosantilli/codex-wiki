Measure-preserving transformation (T)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Ergodic theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 551 Articles: 16
A measurable transformation T:X→X preserves a measure μ when μ(T−1A)=μ(A) for every measurable set A.
