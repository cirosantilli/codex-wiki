Iwasawa theorem on local cyclotomic units (U∞1​/C∞1​≃Zp​[[G]]/(I(G)ζp​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Iwasawa theory Main conjecture of Iwasawa theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For the real p-power cyclotomic tower, take norm limits of local principal units and closed cyclotomic-unit subgroups. The corrected Coleman logarithm identifies U∞1​ with the Iwasawa algebra of G=Zp×​/{±1}. On a symmetric cyclotomic unit it gives ([b]−[a])ζp​. A topological generator generates the cyclotomic-unit limit and the augmentation ideal, yielding the quotient description. This local calculation does not alone identify the characteristic ideal of the global Galois module.
