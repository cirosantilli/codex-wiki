Modular layer vanishing lemma

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Frankl-Wilson theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
Let p be a prime number, 0≤a<p, 1≤s<p, and n≥a+s. A multilinear polynomial over Fp​ of degree less than s that vanishes on every Boolean vertex whose weight is not congruent to a modulo p vanishes everywhere. Alternating sums over intervals of s free coordinates eliminate the possible nonzero weight levels one at a time. This proves independence of the auxiliary functions used for the uniform Frankl-Wilson theorem bound.
