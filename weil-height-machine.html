Weil height machine (L⟼hL​(modO(1)))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Height function
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
On a projective variety over a number field, a very ample line bundle gives a logarithmic projective height by an embedding. Tensor products correspond to sums by the Segre embedding, and a sufficiently ample twist expresses any line bundle as a difference of very ample ones. This defines its height modulo bounded functions. It is additive and functorial under morphism pullback. An ample bundle has the Northcott theorem finiteness property for bounded height and bounded point degree; a trivial bundle need not. The product formula makes these absolute heights independent of scalar coordinates and of the number field used to compute them.
