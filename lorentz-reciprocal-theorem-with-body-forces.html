Lorentz reciprocal theorem with body forces

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Lorentz reciprocal theorem for Stokes flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For two incompressible Stokes flows with the same dynamic viscosity, ∇⋅σ(a)+f(a)=0. The divergence of ui(2)​σij(1)​−ui(1)​σij(2)​ equals −u(2)⋅f(1)+u(1)⋅f(2). The cross-strain-rate tensor products cancel by reciprocity. Integrating gives equality of the two surface cross-work terms after adding the corresponding volume body-force work. The outward domain normal must be used consistently, especially at an exterior domain's inner surface.
