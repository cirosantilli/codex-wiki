Atomic approximation on finite-dimensional spaces of continuous functions (​∫fdμ−∑i​ti​f(wi​)​≤ε∥f∥)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A finite regular complex measure of total variation one can be approximated on a finite-dimensional vector subspace of continuous functions by a finite sum of phased point evaluations with ∑i​∣ti​∣=1. The Krein-Milman theorem and the extreme points of the dual unit ball of C(K) give weak-star approximation by convex combinations of phased point masses. A finite norm net of the vector subspace unit ball turns finitely many scalar approximations into one uniform estimate. Repeated nodes may be kept separate to retain the exact coefficient-magnitude sum.
