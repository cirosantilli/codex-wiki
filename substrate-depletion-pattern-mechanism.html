Substrate-depletion pattern mechanism (J∼(+−​+−​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Diffusion equation Reaction–diffusion system
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
An autocatalytic species promotes its own growth while consuming a substrate that promotes that growth. Its reaction Jacobian has the displayed signs after choosing the activator first. This differs from the activator-inhibitor sign pattern (++​−−​), where the activator stimulates its inhibitor. Appropriate magnitudes, diffusivities and admissible spatial modes are needed for a Turing instability; signs alone are insufficient.
