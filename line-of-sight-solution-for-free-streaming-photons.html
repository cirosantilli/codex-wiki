Line-of-sight solution for free-streaming photons

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Free-streaming photon Boltzmann equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 218 Articles: 3
The line-of-sight solution integrates the gravitational source along the unperturbed photon trajectory. In Fourier space the integrating factor eikμη accounts for free streaming between emission and observation.
