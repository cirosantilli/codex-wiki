Klein paradox

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Klein-Gordon equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 179 Articles: 2
In a sufficiently strong electrostatic step, the positive-energy incident channel overlaps a negative-kinetic-energy channel on the far side. For a charged scalar field the outgoing antiparticle branch has negative charge current, allowing reflected particle current larger than the incident current while signed charge is conserved. The external source supplies energy for particle-antiparticle creation. This requires a multiparticle interpretation; negative transmitted charge current is not a negative probability.
