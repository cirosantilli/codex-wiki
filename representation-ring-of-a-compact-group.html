Representation ring of a compact group (R(G))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 144 Articles: 1
The representation ring is the Grothendieck group of finite-dimensional complex unitary representations of a compact group, with addition induced by direct sum and multiplication by tensor product. As an abelian group it is freely generated by the irreducible unitary representations. Taking dimension gives a ring homomorphism R(G)→Z.
