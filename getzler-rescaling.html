Getzler rescaling

 ... Riemannian geometry Riemannian manifold Spin manifold Spinor field Dirac operator Atiyah-Singer index theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
Getzler rescaling combines the change x↦t​x in geodesic normal coordinates with the Clifford algebra filtration, assigning degree one to a derivative and to a Clifford generator and degree minus one to a coordinate. The associated graded Clifford algebra is the exterior algebra. This is useful because the spinor supertrace annihilates every Clifford monomial except the top-degree one. Applied to the Lichnerowicz formula for a twisted Dirac operator, it retains a quadratic curvature model, whose heat kernel is computed by a Gaussian ansatz; all terms of smaller rescaling degree disappear from the local index limit. Thus the cancellations in a supersymmetric heat trace can be computed before solving the complete curved heat equation.
