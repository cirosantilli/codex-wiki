Deformation radius (Rd​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 69 Articles: 1
A horizontal scale comparing gravitational or buoyancy restoring forces with rotation. For a shallow-water gravity mode with propagation speed c, the deformation radius is c/∣f∣. The barotropic deformation radius uses c=gH​; internal layered modes use a speed set by reduced gravity and layer depths.
