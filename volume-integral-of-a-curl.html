Volume integral of a curl (∫V​∇×GdV=∫∂V​n×GdS)

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus Curl
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
The divergence theorem applied to G×c for every constant vector c gives ∫V​∇×GdV=∫∂V​n×GdS, with outward unit normal n. Indeed ∇⋅(G×c)=c⋅(∇×G), while (G×c)⋅n=c⋅(n×G). Equality of the scalar projections for every c proves the vector identity. Reversing the surface cross product reverses the sign.
