Area bound for an axisymmetric Kerr merger

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole Kerr black hole Irreducible mass
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For initially well-separated Kerr black holes with no orbital angular momentum, exact axisymmetry gives Jf​=J1​+J2​. Put X=(A1​+A2​)/(8π). The black-hole area theorem and the irreducible mass identity imply
Mf2​≥21​(X+X(J1​+J2​)2​).
(99)
Indeed, X≥∣J1​∣+∣J2​∣≥∣Jf​∣, and 21​(Y+Jf2​/Y) is nondecreasing for Y≥X. Opposite spin signs lower Jf2​ at fixed initial areas and hence raise the allowed radiated-energy bound; this need not be achievable in an actual merger.
