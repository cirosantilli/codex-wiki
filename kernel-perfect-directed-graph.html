Kernel-perfect directed graph

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Directed graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 1
A digraph kernel is an independent set K of vertices such that each vertex outside K has a directed edge from itself to a vertex in K. A directed graph is kernel-perfect if every induced subgraph has a digraph kernel. Preference orientations of the line graph of a bipartite graph are kernel-perfect: a stable matching in a bipartite graph is independent in the line graph, and stability supplies the required outgoing edge at every unmatched edge.
