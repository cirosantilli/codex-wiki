Quadratic differential (q=ϕ(z)dz2)

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Canonical bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 161 Articles: 4
A section of the square of the canonical bundle, locally q=ϕ(z)dz2; its coefficient changes by the square of the coordinate derivative. A holomorphic quadratic differential has holomorphic coefficient, and its flat metric of a quadratic differential is ∣q∣1/2.
