Crystal tensor-product rule

Write εi​(b) and φi​(b) for the numbers of raising and lowering steps of color i available from a vertex of a crystal basis. One convention for the tensor product of crystals is
f​i​(a⊗b)={f​i​(a)⊗b,a⊗f​i​(b),​φi​(a)>εi​(b),φi​(a)≤εi​(b).​
(55)
The raising operator uses the first factor for φi​(a)≥εi​(b) and the second otherwise. The different strictness makes raising and lowering inverse along every edge. An edge is absent if the chosen Kashiwara operator vanishes. Stating the convention is essential when identifying the highest-weight vectors as ordered tensor vertices.
