Geometric Asian option ((exp(T−1∫0T​logSt​dt)−K)+)

An Asian option whose averaging uses the geometric mean. In the constant-coefficient Black-Scholes model, the logarithm of the continuous geometric average is Gaussian with mean logS0​+(r−σ2/2)T/2 and variance σ2T/3, because Var(∫0T​Wt​dt)=T3/3. Its forward mean is S0​erT/2−σ2T/12. The discounted lognormal call formula gives its price. It is strictly cheaper than the terminal European call when σ,K,T>0 and r≥−σ2/6, since both its forward mean and log-variance are no larger, with strictly smaller variance. No unconditional comparison holds for arbitrary negative interest rates: for r<−σ2/6, sufficiently small positive strikes reverse the inequality.
