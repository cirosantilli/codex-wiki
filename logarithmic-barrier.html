Logarithmic barrier (fμ​(x)=f(x)−μ∑i​logci​(x))

For optimization constraints ci​(x)≥0 and μ>0, the logarithmic barrier objective is fμ​(x)=f(x)−μ∑i​logci​(x) on D={x:ci​(x)>0 ∀i}. Its derivatives are defined only on this strictly feasible open subset. One may extend its value to +∞ off D to formulate an extended-real convex function. The penalty alone is −∑i​logci​, while fμ​ includes the original objective function.
When f is convex and each ci​ is concave, D is a convex set and
∇fμ​=∇f−∑i​ci​μ​∇ci​,∇2fμ​=∇2f−∑i​ci​μ​∇2ci​+∑i​ci2​μ​∇ci​∇ciT​.
(22)
Every term of this Hessian matrix is a positive semidefinite matrix, so fμ​ is convex. Its critical point satisfies the stationarity part of the Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions with estimates λi​=μ/ci​(x)>0 and perturbed complementary slackness λi​ci​(x)=μ.
