Local isometry between the helicoid and catenoid

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry First fundamental form Local isometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
The helicoid H(s,v)=(sinhscosv,sinhssinv,v) and catenoid C(s,v)=(coshscosv,coshssinv,s) have the same first fundamental form cosh2s(ds2+dv2). Therefore matching equal parameter pairs gives a local isometry. The result is local: a full turn changes the height on the helicoid while the angular coordinate on the catenoid repeats. Equal intrinsic lengths do not require the same embedding into Euclidean space.
