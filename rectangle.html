Rectangle

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Polygon Quadrilateral
OurBigBook.com Words: 105 Articles: 3
A Euclidean polygon with four sides and four right angles. Opposite sides are parallel and have equal lengths. A rectangle is a square exactly when its adjacent side lengths agree.
