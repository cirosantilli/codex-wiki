Joint conditional independence test for stratified contingency tables (Xjoint2​=∑k=1K​Xk2​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical hypothesis test Pearson's chi-squared test
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For K independent strata, each containing an r×c contingency table, test independence of the two table variables within every stratum. Under adequate expected counts and the usual multinomial distribution sampling assumptions, each stratum's Pearson chi-squared statistic has asymptotic chi-squared distribution with (r−1)(c−1) statistical degrees of freedom. Their sum has asymptotic chi-squared distribution with K(r−1)(c−1) statistical degrees of freedom, by the additivity of independent chi-squared distributions. This is a test of conditional independence, not of equality of a common effect across strata; the latter needs an interaction contrast or another specified homogeneity test.
