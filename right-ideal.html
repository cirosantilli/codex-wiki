Right ideal

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
A right ideal I in a unital associative algebra A is a vector subspace with IA⊆I, equivalently a submodule of the right regular module. It need not be a left ideal. Its finite generation refers to right multiplication by elements of A.
