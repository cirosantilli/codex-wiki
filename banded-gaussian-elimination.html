Banded Gaussian elimination

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Numerical linear algebra Gaussian elimination
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For a band matrix, elimination without pivoting retains bounded lower and upper bandwidths in its triangular factors. A tridiagonal system therefore needs only linear work and storage, as in the Thomas algorithm. Pivoting can change the precise fill pattern and must be included in the bandwidth and cost analysis when required. The inverse need not be banded, so solving through the factors preserves sparsity that explicit inversion would lose.
