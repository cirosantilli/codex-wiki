Counterterm closure of a massive Yukawa theory

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Renormalization Counterterm Counterterm closure
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
Without a symmetry excluding odd scalar powers, a real-scalar Yukawa interaction with a massive Dirac field requires a general scalar potential through degree four. For a constant background, the fermion mass becomes M+yϕ, and its divergent local determinant contribution contains (M+yϕ)4. Linear and cubic terms are therefore generated when M=0. A zero renormalized tadpole is a renormalization condition, not a prohibition of its counterterm. An exact discrete chiral symmetry in a massless fermion theory can instead enforce an even scalar potential.
