Hadamard differentiability (Φ˙s​(a))

OurBigBook.com Words: 208 Articles: 2
A map on a subset of a normed vector space is Hadamard differentiable at s if its directional difference quotients converge to a continuous linear map uniformly along all convergent sequences of directions: tj​→0, aj​→a, and s+tj​aj​ in the domain imply (Φ(s+tj​aj​)−Φ(s))/tj​→Φ˙s​(a). Tangential differentiability restricts the limit directions to a specified subspace. This is the regularity used by the functional delta method.
