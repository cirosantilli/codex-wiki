Mean transverse force from a rocking rod (⟨Fy​⟩)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biological fluid dynamics Resistive-force theory Oscillating rigid rod in resistive-force theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
For x=ϵacosωt and a tangent (sinθ,cosθ) with θ=ϵcos(ωt+ϕ), the leading force on the fluid is ⟨Fy​⟩=−(c⊥​−c∥​)Lϵ2aωsinϕ/2. The sign changes under reversal of the tilt convention. Quadrature strokes maximize its magnitude; reciprocal strokes give zero, consistent with kinematic reversibility of Stokes flow.
