Two-component superspace contraction signs (θθ,θˉθˉ)

For left Grassmann derivatives, ϵ12=−ϵ12​=1, θθ=θαθα​ and θˉθˉ=θˉα˙​θˉα˙, the antisymmetric products are θαθβ=−ϵαβθθ/2 and θˉα˙θˉβ˙​=ϵα˙β˙​θˉθˉ/2. With contractions ∂2=∂α∂α​ and ∂ˉ2=∂ˉα˙​∂ˉα˙, both squared derivatives give −4 on the matching quadratic monomial. If Tr(σμσˉν)=2ημν, the mixed bilinear product is +(θθ)(θˉθˉ)ημν/2. Reversing the metric-relative trace convention reverses this last sign.
