Rest frame (p=0)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Reference frame Inertial frame
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 1
The rest frame of a massive particle is an inertial frame in which its spatial momentum is zero. Its four-momentum is then (mc,0) and its energy is mc2. Such a frame exists for a future-directed timelike four-momentum: the Lorentz transformation with boost velocity v=c2p/E makes the spatial momentum zero, and the mass shell relation implies ∣v∣<c. A massless particle has no rest frame, because E=c∣p∣ would require a boost with speed c.
