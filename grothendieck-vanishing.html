Grothendieck vanishing

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Sheaf cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
For a coherent sheaf on a Noetherian scheme, its sheaf cohomology vanishes in degrees greater than its support dimension. In particular, a zero-dimensional projective scheme has no positive-degree coherent cohomology.
