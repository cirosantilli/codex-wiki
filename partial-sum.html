Partial sum (Sn​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Series (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 1
The Nth partial sum of a series with terms (an​) is the finite sum SN​=∑n=1N​an​. It records the accumulated total after N additions. The terms and their partial sums are different sequences: the terms of the harmonic series get small, while their partial sums grow without bound.
For the telescoping series with terms an​=1/[n(n+1)], the first three partial sums are
S1​=21​,S2​=21​+61​=32​,S3​=21​+61​+121​=43​.
(34)
By contrast, the harmonic series gives H1​=1, H2​=3/2, and H3​=11/6, where HN​ is the harmonic number. Computing more partial sums approximates an infinite sum only when a limit of a sequence exists, and the tail of a telescoping reciprocal series shows how an error estimate can certify the approximation.
