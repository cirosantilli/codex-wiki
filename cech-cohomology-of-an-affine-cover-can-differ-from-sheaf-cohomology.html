Čech cohomology of an affine cover can differ from sheaf cohomology (Hˇ1({X},F)=0,H1(X,F)=k(t))

OurBigBook.com Words: 118
On X=Ak1​, let K=k(t) and let K be its constant sheaf. For the two closed rational points 0,1, take skyscraper sheaves S0​,S1​ with value K, and put F=ker(K→S0​⊕S1​). The maps on the two stalks are identities, so this is a sheaf surjection. All three displayed outer sheaves are flasque. The induced global map is the diagonal K→K2, so H1(X,F)=K2/ΔK≃K. The one-member affine cover has zero positive Čech cochains. This even gives an OX​-module example: let regular functions act on all copies of K by multiplication as rational functions, not by evaluation at the closed point. The kernel is not quasi-coherent.
