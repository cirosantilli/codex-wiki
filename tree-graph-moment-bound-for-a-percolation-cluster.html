Tree-graph moment bound for a percolation cluster

OurBigBook.com Words: 95
For translation-invariant independent bond percolation with finite percolation susceptibility χ=∑y​P(0↔y), the BK inequality gives E∣C0​∣k≤(2k−3)!!χ2k−1 for k≥1, with (−1)!!=1. Prune a connecting open tree for the k+1 labelled vertices, suppress unmarked degree-two vertices, and split higher branching into binary branching with zero-length connections. There are (2k−3)!! binary tree topologies and 2k−1 connections. The connections have disjoint bond witnesses, so the BK inequality bounds their joint probability by a product of two-point connection probabilities. Summing successive leaves contributes χ for each connection. Since (2k−3)!!≤2k−1(k−1)!, this proves Eet∣C0​∣≤1−(2χ)−1log(1−2tχ2) for 0<t<(2χ2)−1. Finite percolation susceptibility therefore gives an exponential cluster-volume tail.
