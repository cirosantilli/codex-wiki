Projective modules are flat (P projective⟹P flat)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Projective module
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
A projective module is a direct summand of a free module. Free modules are flat because tensoring with a free module produces a direct sum of copies of the original module and map. If F=P⊕Q is flat, tensoring an injection with F is the direct sum of its tensor maps with P and Q, so each component is injective. Applying this to a free module containing P proves that P is flat.
