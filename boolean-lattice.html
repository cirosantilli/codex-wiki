Boolean lattice (P([n]))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Set family
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 27
The Boolean lattice is the power set of a finite set ordered by inclusion. Its rank-h level consists of all h-element subsets.
