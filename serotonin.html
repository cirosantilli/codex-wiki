Serotonin

 Home Biology Neuroscience Neuron Synapse Neurotransmitter
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A monoamine transmitter also called 5-hydroxytryptamine. Distinct receptors and circuits can have different effects; blood levels are not a direct reading of synaptic transmission in the brain.
