Power transform of a uniform random variable (U∼Uniform(0,1), a>0 ⟹ Ua∼Beta(1/a,1))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Continuous probability distribution Continuous uniform distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The cumulative distribution function of Ua on [0,1] is z1/a. Differentiating gives density z1/a−1/a on (0,1), which is the beta distribution probability density function with parameters (1/a,1). This also gives a simple instance of inverse transform sampling: to sample a density αzα−1 on (0,1), raise a uniform draw to the power 1/α.
