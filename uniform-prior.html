Uniform prior

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Prior probability
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
A uniform prior on a finite-volume parameter region assigns constant probability density there and zero outside. On [0,1] it is Beta(1,1), giving a Beta distribution posterior after binomial distribution data. Uniformity depends on the coordinate: a nonlinear reparametrization introduces a Jacobian and generally does not preserve a uniform density.
