Wave absorption

 Home Physics Wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
Wave absorption transfers energy from a propagating wave to other degrees of freedom of a medium. For a plane wave that is a monochromatic wave, with amplitude proportional to ei(k′+ik′′)z and k′′>0, its wave intensity decreases as I(z)=I(0)e−2k′′z. The intensity absorption coefficient is therefore 2k′′ when this loss is due to absorption. A decreasing transmitted wave intensity can also result from wave reflection or wave scattering, which redistribute energy rather than necessarily converting it into heat.
