Shared zero-inflated Gamma-Poisson count model (Yj​∣B∼Pois(Bμj​))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Zero inflation Zero-inflated negative binomial model
OurBigBook.com Words: 137 Articles: 1
Let B=0 with probability π, and otherwise let B have a Gamma distribution with mean one and variance τ. Given B, counts Yj​ are conditionally independent Poisson random variables with means Bμj​. For q=1−π, the law of total variance and law of total covariance give
EYj​=qμj​,Cov(Y)=diag(qμ)+q(τ+π)μμT.
(1)
Each component has a zero-inflated negative binomial distribution, but the components are not independent after marginalizing B. The zero component is shared by the whole profile. At τ=0, interpret the positive Gamma component as a point mass at one.
