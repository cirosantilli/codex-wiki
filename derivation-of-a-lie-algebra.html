Derivation of a Lie algebra (D[x,y]=[Dx,y]+[x,Dy])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 201 Articles: 5
A derivation of a Lie algebra is an algebraic derivation, namely a linear map D:L→L satisfying
D[x,y]=[Dx,y]+[x,Dy].
(5)
Every adjoint map adz:x↦[z,x] is a derivation.
