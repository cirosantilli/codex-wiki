Local periodogram smoothing (f​(ω)=∑j​wTj​(ω)IT​(ωj​))

 ... Probability and statistics Time series Stationary process Weakly stationary process Spectral density of a stationary process Periodogram
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
Average neighboring periodogram ordinates with nonnegative weights summing to one. Let the number LT​ of averaged ordinates tend to infinity while their frequency span, of order LT​/T, tends to zero. For a smooth spectral density and suitable short-memory or fourth-order dependence conditions, the bias tends to zero and variance is of order 1/LT​. Smooth second-order spectral density alone does not ensure this: a time-constant random scale multiplying independent white noise has a smooth unconditional spectrum but a nonergodic random limiting local power.
