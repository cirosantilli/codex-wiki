Doubling of variables for scalar conservation laws

 ... Transport equation Scalar conservation law Inviscid Burgers equation Entropy solution Entropy flux for a scalar conservation law Kruzhkov entropy flux
OurBigBook.com Words: 229 Articles: 3
Apply the entropy solution inequality for u(t,x) with level v(s,y) and for v(s,y) with level u(t,x), integrate both in the other pair of variables, and add them. Symmetry of the Kruzhkov entropy flux produces derivatives ∂t​+∂s​ and ∂x​+∂y​. A nonnegative mollifier concentrated near the diagonal then yields the Kato inequality for scalar conservation laws. Boundary-time terms require an initial trace argument, not merely interior translation continuity.
