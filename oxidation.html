Oxidation

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Redox
OurBigBook.com Words: 955 Articles: 11
An oxidation increases an oxidation state, recording loss from the formal electron assignment. For Zn→Zn2++2e−, the increase is 0→+2. Oxidation need not involve oxygen.[ref]
In the electrochemistry examples here, oxidation is loss of electrons by a chemical species; it occurs at the anode. That electrode definition holds in both a galvanic cell and an electrolytic cell; it does not fix terminal polarity. The oxidized chemical species serves as the reducing agent in the coupled redox.
