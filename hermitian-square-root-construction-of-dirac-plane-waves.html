Hermitian square-root construction of Dirac plane waves (u=(p⋅σˉ​ξp⋅σ​ξ​))

In mostly-minus signature and the Weyl representation, take p0=E=∣p∣2+m2​>0. The Hermitian matrices A=p⋅σ=E−p⋅σ and B=p⋅σˉ=E+p⋅σ commute and obey AB=m2I. Their positive square roots of a matrix therefore obey A​B​=mI. It follows that u=(A​ξ,B​ξ)T solves (p−m)u=0 and v=(A​ζ,−B​ζ)T solves (p+m)v=0. They multiply e−ipx and eipx respectively. For normalized two-spinors, u†u=v†v=2E. The formula extends by positive-semidefinite square roots to the massless limit.
