Maximum degree (Δ(G))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
The maximum degree of a finite graph is the largest number of edges incident to one vertex. A bound d on degree bounds the number of vertices within distance k of a fixed vertex by 1+d+⋯+dk. Such counting bounds turn a large vertex count into a lower bound on diameter.
