Mole (unit, mol)

 Home Physics System of units International System of Units
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The mole is the SI base unit of amount of substance. One mole contains exactly 6.02214076×1023 specified entities, as fixed by the Avogadro constant. It counts entities rather than a fixed mass or volume. [ref]
