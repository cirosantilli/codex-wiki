Iterated quotient equals a power quotient up to permutation

OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For every e,k≥1, the ek-quotient Qek​(λ) is a permutation of level k of the e-quotient tower. Writing a runner residue modulo ek in base e shows that taking one quotient chooses one digit at a time; iteration may reverse the order of those digits but selects the same runner partitions.
