Reversible Markov chain

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 957 Articles: 21
A stationary Markov chain (Xt​:0≤t≤T) is reversible in equilibrium when it has the same finite-dimensional distributions as its time reversal (XT−t​:0≤t≤T). Equivalently, its stationary flow satisfies detailed balance.
