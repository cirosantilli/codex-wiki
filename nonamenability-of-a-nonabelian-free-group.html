Nonamenability of a nonabelian free group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Amenable group
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The free group F2​=⟨a,b⟩ is not an amenable group. Partitioning reduced words by their first letters gives two translated decompositions of F2​ whose invariance equations add to 2=1, contradicting the existence of an invariant mean.
