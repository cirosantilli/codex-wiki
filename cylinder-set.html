Cylinder set (πF−1​(A))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Product topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A cylinder set in a product of spaces restricts a finite collection of coordinates F and leaves all other coordinates free. It has the form πF−1​(A), where πF​ is the coordinate projection onto the finite subproduct. Taking A to be a product of open sets gives the basic open cylinders for the product topology. In a finite-alphabet full shift, specifying exact letters at finitely many coordinates gives a clopen set.
