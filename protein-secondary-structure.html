Protein secondary structure

 Home Biology Protein Protein structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 874 Articles: 6
Protein secondary structure consists of recurring local arrangements of the protein backbone, notably the alpha helix and beta sheet. Backbone hydrogen bonds help stabilize these patterns. A beta sheet can bring together stretches far apart in the protein primary structure; local refers to the pattern of backbone geometry, not necessarily neighboring sequence positions. See Alberts et al.: alpha helices, beta sheets and backbone interactions.
