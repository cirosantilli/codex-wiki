Finite-dimensional distribution

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process
OurBigBook.com Words: 65 Articles: 1
For times t1​,…,tn​, the corresponding finite-dimensional distribution of a stochastic process X is the joint probability distribution of the random vector (Xt1​​,…,Xtn​​). The collection of these distributions records every finite set of coordinates of the process.
