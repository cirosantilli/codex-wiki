Jacobi reciprocity law ((a/b)(b/a)=(−1)(a−1)(b−1)/4)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Jacobi symbol
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 1
For coprime positive odd integers a,b, the Jacobi symbol satisfies (a/b)(b/a)=(−1)(a−1)(b−1)/4. It follows by factoring a and b into primes and multiplying the quadratic reciprocity for each prime pair; the parity of the sums of prime half-differences agrees with (a−1)/2 and (b−1)/2. A Jacobi symbol equal to one does not imply that its numerator is a quadratic residue for a composite denominator.
