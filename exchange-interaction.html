Exchange interaction

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 776 Articles: 10
The exchange interaction is an effective interaction between spins originating in the quantum mechanics of electrons. Its sign convention must accompany any formula. For the illustrative classical fixed-length spin pair used here, write
E=JS1​⋅S2​=JS2cosθ.
(120)
With this plus-sign convention, J>0 favors antiparallel spins: E(π)=−JS2, compared with E(0)=+JS2. The energy difference is 2JS2. If instead the Hamiltonian is written with an overall minus sign, the same physical preference has a negative coupling. This prevents a sign convention in the Ising model from being mistaken for a disagreement about the ordering.
