Nonzero ultraviolet fixed point of a cubic-quintic beta function (β(g)=Bg3−Ag5,g∗2​=B/A)

For A,B>0, the nonzero zero of the renormalization-group beta function has slope β′(g∗​)=−2B2/A<0 and is ultraviolet attractive. The origin is infrared attractive. A fixed-point field-renormalization anomalous dimension gives a power-law quantum field theory propagator factor d(z)∼Czγϕ​(g∗​). A weak fixed point is needed for control by a perturbative truncation.
