By conformal invariance of extremal length, work first on 1<∣w∣<R. For a conformal metric ρ, let Lρ​ be the infimum of lengths of curves joining the two circles, and Aρ​ its area. Each radial segment is admissible, so the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality gives, for every angle,
Lρ2​≤(∫1R​ρ(seiθ)ds)2≤logR∫1R​ρ(seiθ)2sds.
(12)
Integrating in angle gives 2πLρ2​≤Aρ​logR. Conversely, ρ(w)=1/∣w∣ has shortest joining length logR and area 2πlogR, attaining the bound. Thus the conformal modulus of an annulus gives
dΩ​(E,F)=2πlogR​,ρΩ​(z)=c​ψ(z)ψ′(z)​​,c>0​.
(13)
The second formula is the pullback of c/∣w∣ by ψ. Pullback preserves curve lengths and metric area, so every displayed positive multiple is an extremal metric in Ω.
