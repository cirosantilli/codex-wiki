On each smaller closed disk ∣z∣≤s<r, its kernel and all its derivatives in z are uniformly bounded. Since the boundary values of the real harmonic function u are continuous on this circle, differentiation under the integral sign proves that g is holomorphic. Taking the real part gives the Poisson integral of those boundary values. It agrees with u by uniqueness of the Dirichlet problem on the radius-r disk. Hence g is holomorphic and Reg=u on ∣z∣<r. Its imaginary part is the harmonic conjugate normalized by Img(0)=0.
