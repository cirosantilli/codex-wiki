Strong-field quenching of an isotropic electromotive force (α∥​∝∣B∣−3)

 ... Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Dynamo action Mean-field dynamo Alpha effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
For isotropic monochromatic forcing, the longitudinal coefficient in a mean-field electromotive force is proportional to ∫01​μ2(1+h2μ2)−2dμ=[arctanh−h/(1+h2)]/(2h3), where h is proportional to mean-field strength. This gives generic ∣B∣−3 quenching. The natural angular alpha tensor is anisotropic in a nonzero mean field: its transverse coefficient instead contains ∫01​(1−μ2)(1+h2μ2)−2dμ and scales as ∣B∣−1. Only the longitudinal contraction enters E=αB. An effective scalar representation may therefore have every diagonal entry equal to the longitudinal coefficient without being the unique natural response tensor.
