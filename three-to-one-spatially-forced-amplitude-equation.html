Three-to-one spatially forced amplitude equation (AT​=μA−c∣A∣2A+FA2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory Amplitude equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 279 Articles: 3
A critical pattern Fourier mode transforms under a spatial translation as A↦eiϕA. A forcing at three times the critical wavenumber transforms as F↦e3iϕF. Their lowest resonant product with phase weight one is FA2. This determines the form of the leading weak-forcing amplitude equation, while projection onto an adjoint eigenfunction determines its coefficient. Symmetry permits this coupling but does not ensure that it is nonzero. Travelling forcing gives a time-dependent phase to F.
