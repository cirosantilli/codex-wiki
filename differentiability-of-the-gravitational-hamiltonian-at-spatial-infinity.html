Differentiability of the gravitational Hamiltonian at spatial infinity

OurBigBook.com Words: 84
For asymptotically Cartesian spatial metrics hij​=δij​+O(r−1), unit asymptotic lapse and vanishing asymptotic shift, the curvature term in the bulk gravitational Hamiltonian has variation
δHbulk​=bulk functional derivatives−16πG1​limr→∞​∫Sr​​ni(∂j​δhij​−∂i​δhjj​)dA.
(102)
The surface variation is finite under the usual differentiable asymptotic falloff and is not removed by simply taking the boundary to infinity. Adding Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy cancels it and makes Hamilton's equations well defined for allowed variations that need not fix the energy. On the constraint surface, the total Hamiltonian for that time translation equals the boundary energy.
