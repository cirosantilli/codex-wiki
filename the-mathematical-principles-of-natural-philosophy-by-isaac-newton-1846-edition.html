An English edition of the Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica by Isaac Newton. The cited opening Book III propositions are available in the linked facsimile extract; the full edition permits verification of the Laws of Motion and Book I. Later additions must not be silently dated to the first edition: the General Scholium was added in 1713.
