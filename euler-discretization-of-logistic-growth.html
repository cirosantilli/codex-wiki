Euler discretization of logistic growth (λ=1+rΔt)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Nonlinear ordinary differential equation Logistic differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
The Forward Euler method applied to the logistic differential equation y′=ry(1−ay) becomes the logistic map after u=a(λ−1)y/λ, λ=1+rΔt. The positive equilibrium point of the differential equation has perturbations decaying as e−rt. Its discrete fixed-point multiplier is 1−rΔt=2−λ: for rΔt>2, the discretization has an artificial oscillatory instability, leading initially to alternation on successive steps. This contrasts with the positive perturbation multiplier e−rΔt of the exact time-step map.
