Geometric intersection number (i(α,β))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological surface Mapping class group
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 2
The geometric intersection number i(α,β) is the minimum number of transverse intersection points among representatives of the isotopy classes of α and β. Representatives in minimal position realize it.
