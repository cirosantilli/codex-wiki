Weak Alfvénic cascade (E⊥​(k⊥​)∼(ϵvA​/l∥​)1/2k⊥−2​)

For balanced anisotropic Alfvénic turbulence, suppose τA​=l∥​/vA​≪τnl​=l⊥​/δz and resonant interactions preserve the parallel scale during the perpendicular cascade. Random encounter accumulation gives τcas​=vA​l⊥2​/(δz2l∥​). Constant energy flux then gives δz∝l⊥1/2​ and the displayed perpendicular turbulent energy spectrum. Unlike isotropic phenomenology, the fixed parallel scale is independent of the decreasing perpendicular scale.
