Permittivity tensor (Di​=∑j​ϵij​Ej​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Permittivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The permittivity tensor is the linear material-response matrix relating electric displacement field to electric field at a specified frequency. An isotropic local medium has ϵij​=ϵδij​. A magnetically biased transparent medium can have an imaginary antisymmetric transverse contribution; its eigenmodes can have opposite circular polarizations and different refractive indices.
