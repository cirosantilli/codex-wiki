Spectral filtering of Hamiltonian terms (gZ​=∫w(t)eitHhZ​e−itHdt)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Hamiltonian simulation Local Hamiltonian
OurBigBook.com Words: 282 Articles: 4
A real nonnegative normalized filter w defines gZ​=∫w(t)eitHhZ​e−itHdt. In an energy eigenbasis, it multiplies each matrix element by w(Ei​−Ej​). A Fourier cutoff at the spectral gap removes couplings between a unique ground state and excitations. The filtered terms still sum to H, because the total Hamiltonian is unchanged by its own unitary time evolution. Each term commutes with the ground projector, but need not be minimized there.
