Proper mean lifetime (τ0​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Decay width Mean lifetime (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 79 Articles: 1
The mean lifetime of an unstable particle measured in its rest frame, using elapsed proper time. In the exponential decay model, survival to proper time τ has probability e−τ/τ0​. For constant Lorentz factor γ, time dilation gives laboratory mean lifetime γτ0​. A mean lifetime is a statistical mean, rather than a fixed decay time.
