Positive square root of an operator (T1/2)

 ... Functional analysis Hilbert space Riesz representation theorem Adjoint operator Hermitian operator Positive operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
Every bounded positive operator T has a unique positive operator T1/2 satisfying (T1/2)2=T. The spectral theorem for normal operators on a separable Hilbert space constructs it by applying the scalar square-root function to the spectrum.
