Acting with bs2​ on the vacuum produces cs​∣0⟩ and a two-particle vector whose squared norm is 2ns2​, because its two possible vacuum contractions have equal contributions. This proves the displayed second moment and Var(Ns​)=ns​(ns​+1)+∣cs​∣2. A thermal-mode formula requires cs​=0; a general squeezed vacuum state need not satisfy that restriction.
