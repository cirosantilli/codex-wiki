Critical point of a Flory–Huggins polymer solution (ϕc​=1/(1+N​),χc​=(1+N−1/2)2/2)

For composition-independent χ, the spinodal of Flory–Huggins solution theory is 2χ=1/(Nϕ)+1/(1−ϕ). At its thermodynamic critical point, a second condition −1/(Nϕ2)+1/(1−ϕ)2=0 gives 1−ϕc​=N​ϕc​, proving the displayed values. Hence ϕc​∼N−1/2 and χc​−1/2∼N−1/2. Under χ=C/T, the theta temperature is Θ=2C and Tc​=Θ/(1+N−1/2)2. Thus Tc​ approaches a constant while Θ−Tc​ scales as N−1/2. Converting χc​ to a temperature always requires a specified or inferred χ(T) law.
