Convection amplitude coupled to a conserved field

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Magnetoconvection
OurBigBook.com Words: 307 Articles: 3
A representative scaled amplitude model has complex A and real B with
AT​=A+AXX​−A∣A∣2−AB,BT​=σBXX​+μ(∣A∣2)XX​,σ>0.
(25)
Periodic boundary conditions conserve ∫BdX, because the right-hand side is a total second derivative. The slow field can change long-wave convection roll stability and permit localized pulses. For zero mean B, the uniform convection rolls are A=ReiqX, R2=1−q2, B=0.
