Interest-rate cap

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fixed-income security Interest rate
OurBigBook.com Words: 166 Articles: 2
A cap limits the floating rate on a notional principal by paying the excess over a strike for successive accrual periods. It is a sum of caplets. For accrual fraction δ and reset at T with payment at U, the unit-notional payoff is δ(L(T;T,U)−K)+ at U, where L(T;T,U)=[P(T,U)−1−1]/δ and P is a zero-coupon bond price.
