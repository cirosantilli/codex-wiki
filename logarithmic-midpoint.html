Logarithmic midpoint (m=ab​)

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Exponential function Logarithm Logarithmic scale
OurBigBook.com Words: 144
For positive endpoints a,b, halfway along a logarithmic scale means
logβ​(m/x0​)=2logβ​(a/x0​)+logβ​(b/x0​)​.
(29)
Here β>1 is the base. Equivalently, using the natural logarithm, 2ln(m/x0​)=ln(a/x0​)+ln(b/x0​). The product of numbers identity for logarithms gives m2=ab, so the midpoint is the geometric mean ab​. The two endpoints lie at equal multiplicative distances because m/a=b/m.
For a=10 and b=100, this midpoint is 1000​≈31.6228, giving a factor of 10​ on each side. The arithmetic mean 55 is halfway on a linear scale, but its two ratios are 55/10=5.5 and 100/55≈1.81818. Thus reading halfway between the marks 10 and 100 as 55 on a logarithmic scale gives the wrong value.
