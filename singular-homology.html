Singular homology

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 437 Articles: 9
Singular homology forms a chain complex from formal sums of continuous maps from standard simplices into a topological space. Its homology groups are invariant under homotopy.
