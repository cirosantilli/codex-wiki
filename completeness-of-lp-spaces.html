Completeness of Lp spaces (1≤p<∞ ⟹ Lp is Banach)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Lp space
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
From an Lp norm Cauchy sequence choose a subsequence with successive difference norms at most 2−j. Minkowski inequality and monotone convergence theorem make the sum of the absolute differences an Lp function, hence finite almost everywhere. The telescoping series defines an Lp limit; the geometric bound on its tail proves norm convergence of the subsequence, and the original Cauchy property proves convergence of the whole sequence. No sigma-finiteness assumption is needed.
