Shear flow

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 301 Articles: 4
A shear flow has a velocity field varying across adjacent fluid layers, giving a nonzero shear rate. A parallel example is u=U(z)ex​, with vertical shear U′. Linear shear flow has constant shear, while a shear layer concentrates the velocity change in a narrow region.
