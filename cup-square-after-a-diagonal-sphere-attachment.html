Cup square after a diagonal sphere attachment (u2=−2v)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Cohomology Cup product Cohomology ring Cohomology ring of a product of two spheres
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For even n≥2, attach an (n+1)-cell to Sn×Sn along the diagonal. The cellular chain complex has boundary (1,1) for the new cell. The degree-n cohomology generator restricts to a−b, and the top generator restricts to ab. Naturality of the cup product therefore gives u2=−2v, since (a−b)2=−2ab. Thus the resulting cohomology ring is Z[u,v]/(u2+2v,uv,v2), with ∣u∣=n and ∣v∣=2n. This distinguishes the attachment from a wedge of spheres, whose positive-degree products vanish.
