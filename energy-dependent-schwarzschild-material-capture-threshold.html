For an incoming timelike Schwarzschild geodesic with asymptotic Lorentz factor k≥1, the critical unstable circular orbit obeys
k2=rc​(rc​−3μ)(rc​−2μ)2​,hc2​=rc​−3μμc2rc2​​.
(23)
At k=1, rc​=4μ and ∣hc​∣=4μc. For k>1, the critical radius lies between 3μ and 4μ and ∣hc​∣>4μc. The marginally bound angular-momentum bound remains sufficient at larger energies but is no longer the exact threshold. Origin at infinity and an inward initial branch are essential; an orbit already inside a barrier is a different initial value problem.
