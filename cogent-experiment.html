CoGeNT experiment

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Dark matter Dark-matter direct detection
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A low-threshold point-contact germanium ionization search. The small readout capacitance supports sensitivity to low-energy interactions. Bulk/surface charge collection, internal lines, threshold response and time dependence must be controlled before interpreting an excess or modulation as a WIMP recoil.[ref]
