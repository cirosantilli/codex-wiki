Electron carrier

 Home Chemistry Biochemistry Energy metabolism Electron transport chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
An electron carrier cycles between oxidation and reduction. Ubiquinone carries two electrons, while cytochrome c carries one at a time.[ref]
For example, transferring the two electrons supplied by one NADH requires two one-electron deliveries by cytochrome c. It does not require two permanently consumed cytochrome c molecules: an available electron carrier can unload, return and reload. Electron carriers recycle material while the net redox conversion supplies free energy.
