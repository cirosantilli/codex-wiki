SU(3) Lie algebra (su(3))

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Lie algebra of a matrix Lie group Unitary Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 73 Articles: 1
The compact real Lie algebra su(3) consists of traceless anti-Hermitian 3×3 matrices. Physicists often specify Hermitian generators ta​ instead, so the genuine Lie algebra elements are ita​. The compact real algebra and its complexification sl3​(C) should be distinguished.
