Riemannian gradient (∇g​f)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
For a smooth function f on a Riemannian manifold, its Riemannian gradient is the unique vector field satisfying g(∇g​f,V)=df(V) for every vector field V. It depends on the Riemannian metric, and its negative generates the downward gradient flow.
