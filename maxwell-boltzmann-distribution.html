When E−μ≫kB​T, either quantum occupation law reduces to the classical factor
e−(E−μ)/(kB​T).
(14)
For a classical ideal gas the associated velocity distribution is f(v)=(m/(2πkB​T))3/2exp[−m∣v∣2/(2kB​T)]. The occupation factor is the corresponding Boltzmann distribution for a one-particle energy.
