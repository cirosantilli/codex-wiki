Kernel sheaf (kerϕ)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Presheaf of sets on a topological space Sheaf (mathematics) Morphism of sheaves
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For a morphism of sheaves of abelian groups or modules, the kernel sheaf has sections ker(F(U)→G(U)). These already satisfy the sheaf gluing axiom. Its stalk at x is the kernel of Fx​→Gx​, since filtered colimits of modules preserve exactness. A sheaf morphism is injective exactly when its kernel sheaf is zero.
