Gauge protection of a vector mass

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Hierarchy problem Naturalness (physics) Symmetry protection of a small mass
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
An unbroken gauge symmetry forbids an explicit local Proca mass for its gauge vector. A Higgs mechanism can generate that mass through a charged vacuum expectation value, but the stability of the resulting vector scale then depends on the scalar sector that fixes the expectation. Gauge invariance alone does not protect a fundamental scalar mass.
