Chromatic polynomial of a cycle (pCℓ​​(x)=(x−1)ℓ+(−1)ℓ(x−1))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Graph coloring Chromatic polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
For a cycle graph of length ℓ≥3, the chromatic polynomial is pCℓ​​(x)=(x−1)ℓ+(−1)ℓ(x−1). The graph triangle gives the base case x(x−1)(x−2). For ℓ≥4, the deletion-contraction recurrence for the chromatic polynomial gives pCℓ​​(x)=x(x−1)ℓ−1−pCℓ−1​​(x), since deleting an edge leaves a path graph and contracting it gives the shorter cycle graph. Substitution proves the formula by mathematical induction.
