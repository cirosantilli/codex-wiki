Orthogonal group over a finite field (O(V,Q)={g∈GL(V):Q(gv)=Q(v)})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory General linear group Matrix group
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The group of invertible linear maps preserving a specified quadratic form over a finite field. In characteristic two one must preserve the quadratic form itself, not merely its polar alternating bilinear form. For a nonsingular form, any two nonzero isotropic vectors can be incorporated into hyperbolic pairs and standard adapted bases, producing an isometry between them.
