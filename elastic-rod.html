Elastic rod

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Elastic filament
OurBigBook.com Words: 277 Articles: 4
An elastic rod is a slender elastic body represented by a centreline and an attached orthonormal director frame. Its elastic energy penalizes stretching, bending and twisting relative to a preferred configuration. The director kinematics of a rod describe changes of the frame along the centreline. An unshearable elastic rod has its third director parallel to the centreline tangent vector.
