Planck-constant loop counting with source normalization (ℏL−1)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram Loop order
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
In a scalar field path integral with weight exp[i(S+∫Jϕ)/ℏ], each internal propagator contributes ℏ, each interaction vertex contributes ℏ−1, and each source insertion contributes ℏ−1. A connected graph with I internal lines and V interaction vertices has loop order L=I−V+1. Its amputated amplitude, or its coefficient in logZ[J] with the source insertions included, is proportional to ℏI−V=ℏL−1. An ordinary unamputated connected n-field correlation function instead has the additional n external propagator factors and scales as ℏL+n−1. Multiplication by −iℏ to form the connected generating functional shifts the source-kernel counting to ℏL. These are compatible normalization conventions.
