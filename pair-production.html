Pair production

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Quantum electrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 180 Articles: 3
Pair production creates a particle and its antiparticle while conserving energy, momentum and the relevant charges. The available invariant mass must reach the sum of their rest masses.
