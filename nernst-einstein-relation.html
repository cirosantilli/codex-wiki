Nernst-Einstein relation (σ=nq2D/(kB​T))

 ... Electric current Electrical resistance and conductance Electrical resistance Ohm's law Electrical conductivity Ionic conductivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
For dilute, independently migrating carriers of number density n, charge q and diffusion coefficient D, balancing drift with diffusion gives mobility D/(kB​T) per applied force. Multiplying drift velocity by charge density yields σ=nq2D/(kB​T). State which species defines n and D: a vacancy diffusivity must be combined with vacancy density. Correlated motion requires a correction rather than automatic use of a tracer diffusivity.
