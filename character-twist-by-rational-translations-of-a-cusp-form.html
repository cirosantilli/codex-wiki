For a level-one cusp form and any Dirichlet character modulo N>1, this translation sum is a cusp form on Γ1​(N)∩Γ0​(N2). Conjugating that subgroup by (10​j/N1​) yields integral determinant-one matrices; cusp holomorphy under rational slash operators supplies all cusp conditions. Its exact Fourier coefficients at positive indices are an​(f)∑j∈(Z/NZ)×​χ(j)−1e2πinj/N. For a primitive Dirichlet character they equal g(χ​)χ(n)an​(f), by the finite Fourier transform of a primitive Dirichlet character. For imprimitive characters the sum can be nonzero at nonunits, so the simplified twist formula need not hold.
