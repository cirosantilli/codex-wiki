For steady injection into a Keplerian disk between two zero-torque boundaries, conservation of mass gives M˙=M˙in​+M˙out​. Conservation of angular momentum gives M˙h0​=M˙in​hin​+M˙out​hout​, where h(R)=GMR​. Eliminating the outward mass flux gives the displayed inward fraction. This fraction does not depend on the viscosity law: the two boundary fluxes must carry away the injected mass and angular momentum. Equivalently, continuity of the viscous torque in an accretion disk joins νΣR​=M˙in​(R​−Rin​​)/(3π) inside the source to M˙out​(Rout​​−R​)/(3π) outside.
