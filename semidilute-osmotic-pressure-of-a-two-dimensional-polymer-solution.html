Semidilute osmotic pressure of a two-dimensional polymer solution (Π∼(kB​T/ℓ2)ϕ3)

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry) Polymer solution Semidilute polymer solution
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
In a planar good-solvent solution, a correlation blob of g monomers has size ξ∼ℓg3/4. Its area fraction is ϕ∼gℓ2/ξ2∼g−1/2, giving g∼ϕ−2 and ξ∼ℓϕ−3/2. Each blob contributes order kB​T to the osmotic free energy, so the surface osmotic pressure is Π∼kB​T/ξ2∼(kB​T/ℓ2)ϕ3. This regime requires many blobs per chain and area fraction below close packing. In the dilute regime entire chains are the independently translating objects and Π∼kB​Tϕ/(Nℓ2). The two expressions match at the two-dimensional polymer overlap fraction ϕ∗∼N−1/2.
