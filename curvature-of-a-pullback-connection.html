Curvature of a pullback connection (Rϕ∗∇=ϕ∗R∇)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle) Pullback connection
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For a smooth map ϕ:M′→M and a connection on a vector bundle E→M, the curvature form of a connection on the pullback vector bundle satisfies
Rpϕ∗∇​(u,v)=Rϕ(p)∇​(dϕp​u,dϕp​v)
(23)
as an endomorphism of Eϕ(p)​. In a frame of a vector bundle written as a row, with section components written as columns, the connection one-form is Ω and its curvature form of a connection is dΩ+Ω∧Ω. The pullback connection has matrix ϕ∗Ω; commutation of the pullback of a differential form with the exterior derivative and wedge product of differential forms proves the identity. No pushforward to an ambient vector field is required: dϕ(X) is a vector field along a map.
