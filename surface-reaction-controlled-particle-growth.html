OurBigBook.com Words: 57
When attachment is much slower than exterior diffusion, the surface concentration stays close to the reservoir value. An attachment coefficient ks​ with units of velocity gives nearly constant radial growth in the dilute-feed approximation. For a sphere the ratio ks​a/D distinguishes attachment control from transport control. A finite attachment rate leads to a Robin boundary condition rather than a fixed equilibrium surface concentration.
