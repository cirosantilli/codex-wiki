Antiperiodic boundary condition (f(x+L)=−f(x))

 ... Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Boundary condition Periodic boundary conditions Twisted boundary condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
An antiperiodic boundary condition is a phase twist of π across the boundary. A Fourier mode then has k=2π(n+1/2)/L. It occurs in the even-fermion sector of a periodic Jordan–Wigner transformation and in imaginary-time fermionic thermal fields, for different underlying reasons.
