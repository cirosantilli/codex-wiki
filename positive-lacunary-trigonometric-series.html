Positive lacunary trigonometric series (En​(f)=∑qk>n​ak​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation Equioscillation theorem Trigonometric Chebyshev alternation theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 210 Articles: 2
For an odd integer q≥3 and positive summable coefficients ak​, the Weierstrass M-test makes f(x)=∑k≥0​ak​cos(qkx) continuous. Its partial sum over qk≤n is the unique best uniform approximation by degree-at-most-n trigonometric polynomials, for n≥1. If Q is the first omitted frequency, at xj​=jπ/Q every omitted cosine equals (−1)j because all frequency ratios are odd. Thus the tail attains alternating extrema equal to its coefficient sum at 2Q≥2n+2 points. The trigonometric Chebyshev alternation theorem proves the formula. For ak​=r−k, r>1, the largest decay exponent is logr/logq.
