Grothendieck topos

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Elementary topos
OurBigBook.com Words: 861 Articles: 14
A Grothendieck topos is a category equivalent to the sheaves on a site for a small Grothendieck topology. It is an elementary topos with all small colimits and a small generating family. Small disjoint coproducts, effective quotients and the set of subobjects of any fixed object allow many constructions by unions.
