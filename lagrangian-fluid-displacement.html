Lagrangian fluid displacement (ξ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Displacement field (mechanics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
The Lagrangian fluid displacement ξ compares neighbouring fluid trajectories. Linear velocity perturbations about a steady flow U obey δu=(∂t​+U⋅∇)ξ−(ξ⋅∇)U. For a background scalar f0​, its material perturbation is Δf=δf+ξ⋅∇f0​. In an axisymmetric normal mode about cylindrical rotation, the radial and vertical velocities are −iωξr​ and −iωξz​. The displacement of a free surface is needed to impose the Lagrangian pressure perturbation condition.
