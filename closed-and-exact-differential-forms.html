Closed and exact differential forms

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form Exterior derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 28
A closed differential form satisfies dα=0; an exact differential form has α=dβ. The identity d2=0 makes every exact form closed. Global failure of the converse is measured by de Rham cohomology; the Poincaré lemma gives the local converse in positive degrees.
