No-scale identity from degree-one homogeneity (Ki​Kij−1​Kj​=3)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supergravity No-scale supergravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
Let K=−3logΓ where Γ>0 is twice differentiable and homogeneous of degree one. If the Kähler Hessian is invertible, the Euler theorem for homogeneous functions identities give τi​Kij​=3Γj​/Γ, then Kij−1​Γj​/Γ=τi​/3 and Γi​Kij−1​Γj​/Γ2=1/3. Multiplication by nine gives the no-scale identity. A positive degree-one Γ need not induce an invertible or positive Kähler metric: Γ=τ1​+τ2​ gives a rank-one metric for K. The Hessian matrix (Γij​) itself always has the radial null vector τ, whereas the Hessian matrix of K can be invertible. In a larger theory the relevant full inverse metric, or a decoupled block, must obey the identity.
