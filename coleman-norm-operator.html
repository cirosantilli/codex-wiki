Coleman norm operator (φ(Nf)=∏ξp=1​f(ξ(1+T)−1))

The determinant norm for the finite free extension R/φ(R) defines N on units. After adjoining the p-th roots of unity, the norm is the displayed product of conjugates. A Coleman power series of a norm-compatible unit sequence satisfies Nf=f. For symmetric cyclotomic units, the product relation follows directly from ∏ξp=1​(1−ξaXa)=1−Xap when p∤a.
