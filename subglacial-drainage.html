Subglacial drainage

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Glaciology
OurBigBook.com Words: 481 Articles: 8
Subglacial drainage transports water beneath a glacier. The water pressure affects basal sliding through the effective pressure in subglacial drainage. Röthlisberger channel drainage concentrates discharge in melt-enlarged conduits; a linked cavity drainage system distributes it through connected spaces maintained by sliding and creep.
