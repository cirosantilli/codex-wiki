Euclidean invariance of the shape operator

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Gauss map Second fundamental form Shape operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
For a proper Euclidean motion of Euclidean three-space E(x)=Ax+b, transport the unit normal by N(E(p))=AN(p). The corresponding shape operators are orthogonally conjugate:
SE(p)​=ASp​A−1.
(70)
Their determinant and trace, hence Gaussian curvature and mean curvature, are unchanged.
