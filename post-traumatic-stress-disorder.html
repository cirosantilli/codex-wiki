Post-traumatic stress disorder

 Home Biology Medicine Psychiatry Mental disorder
OurBigBook.com Words: 238 Articles: 5
A disorder defined by a qualifying traumatic exposure together with the required subsequent symptom, duration and impairment criteria. The exposure requirement is a diagnostic condition, not a claim that exposure alone is sufficient or that every psychological disorder has the same prerequisite.[ref]
