Poisson-gamma conjugacy with unequal exposures (Θ∣Y∼Gamma(α+∑j​Yj​,β+∑j​mj​))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Bayesian posterior Poisson-gamma conjugacy
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
Given Θ, independent observations Yj​ have Poisson distribution with means mj​Θ for known exposures. A gamma distribution prior of shape α and rate β yields a gamma posterior with shape α+∑j​Yj​ and rate β+∑j​mj​. The likelihood kernel is θ∑j​Yj​e−θ∑j​mj​. Thus the posterior mean depends on total claims and total exposure, not the number of periods alone.
