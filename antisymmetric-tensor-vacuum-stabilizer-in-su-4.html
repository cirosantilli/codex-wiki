Antisymmetric tensor vacuum stabilizer in SU(4) (A=diag(aϵ,bϵ))

For a complex antisymmetric tensor A∈∧2C4, the SU(4) action is A↦UAUT. The unitary skew-diagonalization of an antisymmetric matrix gives A=diag(aϵ,bϵ) in suitable coordinates, where ϵ=(0−1​10​). If ∣a∣=∣b∣, preserving AA† forces a stabilizing unitary matrix to preserve the two coordinate planes. The identity VϵVT=(detV)ϵ then forces both two-by-two blocks to have determinant one, using detU=1 also when one of a,b vanishes. The exact stabilizer is SU(2)×SU(2). If ∣a∣=∣b∣=0, the stabilizer is the larger compact symplectic group USp(4). If a=b=0, it is all of SU(4). The real structure of the SU4 exterior square imposes b=aˉ in this form, so a nonzero real six-dimensional field has the larger symplectic stabilizer, whereas unequal paired singular values are generic for a complex six-dimensional field.
