Condensation graph

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Directed graph Strongly connected component
OurBigBook.com Words: 196 Articles: 2
Contract each strongly connected component of a directed graph to one vertex, retaining edges between different components. A directed cycle between contracted vertices would make those components mutually reachable, contradicting their distinctness; thus the condensation graph is a Directed acyclic graph. Parallel edges can be retained by algorithms that count them consistently.
