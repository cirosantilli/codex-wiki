Ray class group (Clm​(K))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Class field theory Modulus of a number field
OurBigBook.com Words: 316 Articles: 6
The ray class group modulo m is IK​(m)/PK​(m), where the numerator consists of fractional ideals prime to m0​ and the denominator consists of (α) with α≡1 at the finite modulus and positive at every real place in m∞​.
