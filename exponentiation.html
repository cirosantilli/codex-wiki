Exponentiation (ab)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Multiplication
OurBigBook.com Words: 326 Articles: 13
For a nonnegative integer exponent, exponentiation iterates multiplication: a0=1 and ab+1=aab. This convention defines 00=1 in primitive recursive arithmetic. Extensions to other exponents may use different domains and conventions.
