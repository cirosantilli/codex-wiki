Power iteration eigenpair diagnostics (ρk​=xk∗​Axk​,rk​=Axk​−ρk​xk​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Numerical linear algebra Power iteration
OurBigBook.com Words: 183
For each unit vector iterate compute the Rayleigh quotient ρk​=xk∗​Axk​ and the eigenpair residual rk​=Axk​−ρk​xk​. Use the same multiplication Axk​ for both diagnostics and the next normalized iterate. For any complex matrix, this Rayleigh quotient minimizes ∥Axk​−μxk​∥2​ over complex scalars μ, because xk∗​rk​=0. Under power iteration convergence for a diagonalizable matrix, ρk​→λ1​ and ∥rk​∥2​→0, even when the vectors alternate signs or phases.[ref]
A dimensionless stopping quantity is
ηk​=∥A∥2​+∣ρk​∣∥rk​∥2​​,
(32)
when the denominator is positive; ∥A∥2​ is the operator norm, and a known upper bound may replace it if computing it is costly. A small eigenpair residual certifies a small backward error of an approximate eigenpair, but does not establish that the eigenvalue found is dominant.
For a Hermitian matrix with orthonormal eigenbasis uj​, write xk​=∑j​aj​uj​. Then the independently useful identity
∥rk​∥22​=∑j​∣aj​∣2∣λj​−ρk​∣2
(33)
shows that some eigenvalue lies within ∥rk​∥2​ of ρk​. If a specific target eigenvalue λ1​ is separated from all the others by δk​=minj>1​∣λj​−ρk​∣>0, it also gives εk​≤∥rk​∥2​/δk​. This separation is measured from the computed Rayleigh quotient; it differs from the dominant-modulus spectral gap. For a nonnormal matrix, the same orthogonal expansion is unavailable and a small eigenpair residual alone need not imply a comparably small distance to the spectrum.
