Exponential integral (E1​, Ei)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 3
For real x>0, the decaying exponential integral is
E1​(x)=∫x∞​te−t​dt,E1′​(x)=−xe−x​.
(24)
The other standard real convention satisfies Ei(−x)=−E1​(x). The NIST definitions specify the analytic continuation and branch conventions.
