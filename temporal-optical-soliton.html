Temporal optical soliton

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Nonlinear optics Optical soliton
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 3
A localized temporal wave envelope supported by a balance of group velocity dispersion and self-phase modulation. Bright solitons decay to zero background; dark solitons are dips on a nonzero background. This temporal balance differs from the diffraction balance of a spatial optical soliton.
