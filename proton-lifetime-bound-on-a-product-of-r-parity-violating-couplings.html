Proton-lifetime bound on a product of R-parity-violating couplings (∣λ′λ′′∣<M2(ch​τmin​mp5​)−1/2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Exchanging a down-type squark of mass M between LQD and UDD vertices produces a dimension-six effective operator with coefficient C∼λ′λ′′/M2. Dimensional analysis then gives a proton decay width Γp​=ch​∣λ′λ′′∣2mp5​/M4, with a dimensionless factor ch​ containing hadronic matrix elements and phase space. A lifetime bound τp​>τmin​ implies ∣λ′λ′′∣<M2/ch​τmin​mp5​​. The bound is on the relevant flavour product and depends on the exchanged mass; dimensional analysis alone does not determine ch​.
