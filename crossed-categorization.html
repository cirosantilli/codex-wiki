Crossed categorization

 Home Science Psychology Cognitive psychology Categorization Social categorization
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
An arrangement in which group boundaries on different dimensions do not coincide. A former outgroup member may share another valued identity with the observer. This can soften a single division, but need not eliminate exclusion of people outside both categories.
