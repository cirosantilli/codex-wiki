DNA polymerase

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics DNA replication
OurBigBook.com Words: 136 Articles: 7
A replicating DNA polymerase is an enzyme that extends a paired strand end with nucleotides selected against a DNA template. New DNA grows 5′→3′ while the template is read 3′→5′. Base pairing specifies the partner; the DNA polymerase catalyzes its incorporation. See Alberts et al.: DNA polymerase.
