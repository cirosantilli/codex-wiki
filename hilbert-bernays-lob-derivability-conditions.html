Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions (□(A→B)→(□A→□B),□A→□□A)

 ... Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order theory Gödel numbering Arithmetical provability predicate
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For an arithmetical provability predicate write □A=PrT​(┌A┐). The conditions are: if T⊢A then T⊢□A; T⊢□(A→B)→(□A→□B); and T⊢□A→□□A. The first is an external rule, while the latter two are internal theorems. They formalize proof verification, closure under modus ponens rule and positive introspection. None licenses reflection from a proved sentence □A to A.
