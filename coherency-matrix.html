Coherency matrix (Cij​=⟨Ei​Ej∗​⟩)

 Home Physics Wave Polarization (waves)
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The optical coherency matrix records the second moments of transverse complex amplitudes. Normalize its trace to light intensity. Under a deterministic Jones matrix M, C↦MCM†. Unpolarized light has C=(I0​/2)I2​, so its output intensity is (I0​/2)tr(MM†); it cannot be represented by one fixed Jones vector.
