Evaluation onto the dual of a reflexive dual subspace (T:Z↠X∗,(Tz)(x)=x(z))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Reflexive space Reflexive Banach space
OurBigBook.com Words: 151
If Z is a Banach space and X is a norm-closed reflexive linear subspace of Z∗, evaluation gives the displayed surjection. Restriction R:Z∗∗→X∗ is weak-star continuous and maps the closed unit ball onto the target ball by the Hahn-Banach theorem. The Goldstine theorem then makes T(BZ​) weak-star dense in BX∗​, where T=RJZ​. Reflexivity of X makes this target weak-star topology equal to its weak topology. Convexity and the Hahn-Banach separation theorem convert weak density of this ball image to norm density.
To pass from density to surjectivity, approximate a desired f by Tz1​ with ∥z1​∥≤∥f∥ and residual at most ∥f∥/2. Repeat on each residual. The chosen vectors have summable norms, with sum at most 2∥f∥, and the residuals tend to zero geometrically. Completeness of Z produces z=∑n​zn​ with Tz=f. Using any smaller residual ratio proves the infimum lifting norm equals ∥f∥, although that infimum need not be attained. This argument proves onto-ness, rather than merely density of the evaluation functionals.
