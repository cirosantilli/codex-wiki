Uniaxial nematic order (Qij​=23​λ(ni​nj​−δij​/3))

 ... Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Order parameter Liquid crystal Nematic liquid crystal Nematic order parameter
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A uniaxial nematic order parameter has one distinguished nematic director and equal eigenvalues in its perpendicular plane. In the displayed normalization the eigenvalues are λ,−λ/2,−λ/2, giving TrQ2=3λ2/2 and TrQ3=3λ3/4. Positive and negative λ describe prolate and oblate orientational anisotropy, respectively; reversing the nematic director leaves the tensor unchanged.
