Let xˉ=m−1∑i​xi​ and S=∑i​(xi​−xˉ)2>0. With Lebesgue base measure dμdv for v=σ2, the likelihood-power law has conditionals
μ∣v∼N(xˉ,Tv/m),v∣μ∼InvGamma(2Tm​−1,2TS+m(μ−xˉ)2​).
(3)
The joint law is proper for T<m/3, since its marginal v is inverse-gamma distribution with shape m/(2T)−3/2 and scale S/(2T). There is also a direct convergence proof for successive Gibbs sampling updates with deterministic 0<Tn​≤m/10 and Tn​→0. Update μn​ from vn−1​, then vn​ from μn​. Inverse-gamma distribution moments and conditional normal moments give
Evn​=m−4Tn​S+Tn​Evn−1​​,Evn2​=(m−4Tn​)(m−6Tn​)S2+2STn​Evn−1​+3Tn2​Evn−12​​.
(4)
The coefficients of the preceding moments are bounded by 1/6 and 1/8, so these moments stay bounded from a finite deterministic initial variance. Letting Tn​→0 gives Evn​→S/m and Evn2​→(S/m)2. Also E(μn​−xˉ)2=Tn​Evn−1​/m→0. Thus the iterates converge in mean square to the normal maximum-likelihood estimate.
