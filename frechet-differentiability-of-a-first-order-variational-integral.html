Fréchet differentiability of a first-order variational integral (DI(u)w=∫(fu​w+fv​w′))

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Differentiable map Fréchet derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
For I(u)=∫ab​f(x,u,u′)dx on W1,∞, continuity of f,fu​,fv​ on [a,b]×R2 gives a bounded Fréchet derivative in the displayed form. Fixing u and restricting ∥w∥W1,∞​ to a bounded neighborhood confines all intermediate pairs (u+tw,u′+tw′) to a compact range up to null sets. Uniform continuity of the partial derivatives bounds the integrated first-order remainder by (b−a)ω(∥w∥)(∥w∥∞​+∥w′∥∞​), where ω(s)→0. This is little-oh of the Sobolev norm. The result needs no continuous derivative representative for u; essential boundedness suffices.
