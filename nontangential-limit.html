Nontangential limit (∠limz→0​f(z))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 79 Articles: 1
A boundary limit taken while approaching within a cone that stays away from tangency. At zero in the complex upper half-plane, every fixed cone ∣Rez∣≤AImz is allowed. A nontangential limit need not be a limit along all paths approaching the boundary.
