Relativity of simultaneity (Δt′=−γvΔx/c2(Δt=0))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 177 Articles: 2
Spatially separated simultaneous events in one inertial frame are generally not simultaneous in a relatively moving frame. The Lorentz transformation explicitly mixes position into time. This is essential when comparing distances between moving light pulses: one must compare their positions at the same time in the measuring frame.
