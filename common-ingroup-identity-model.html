Common ingroup identity model

 ... Science Psychology Cognitive psychology Categorization Social categorization Recategorization
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
An account of prejudice reduction in which former outgroup members become included in a common ingroup. The proposed mechanism extends favorable ingroup responses across a previous boundary. Whether the common identity recognizes or suppresses subgroup identity is a substantive boundary condition.
