Negative-vacuum Friedmann lifetime bound (tlife​≤π3/(8πG∣ρΛ​∣)​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 89
For a FRW metric with positive scale factor, nonnegative dust and radiation, and negative vacuum density, the Friedmann acceleration equation gives att​≤−ω2a, with ω2=8πG∣ρΛ​∣/3. At maximum expansion, a(0)=amax​ and at​(0)=0. The forced-oscillator integral formula gives a(t)≤amax​cos(ωt) until the first zero, since its forcing is nonpositive and its sine kernel is nonnegative for 0≤t≤π/(2ω). Thus each half of the expansion-recollapse history lasts at most π/(2ω). Nonzero dust or radiation makes the endpoint a physical density singularity; the matter-free limit instead bounds the open anti-de Sitter coordinate patch.
