Reduced osmotic-pressure intercept (limc→0​Π/c=RT/Mn​)

 ... Cell membrane Membrane transport Passive transport Osmosis Osmotic pressure Osmometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For polymer mass concentration c, the leading ideal chain pressure is RTc/Mn​. Plotting Π/c against c and extrapolating removes the leading virial correction. The intercept has units of energy per mass; without the measurement temperature it determines Mn​/T, not an absolute molar mass.
