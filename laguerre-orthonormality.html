Laguerre orthonormality (∫0∞​Ln​(x)Lm​(x)e−xdx=δnm​)

The ordinary Laguerre polynomials, normalized by Ln​(0)=1, are orthonormal for the weighted inner product displayed above. Their coefficient formula is Ln​(x)=∑k=0n​(−1)k(kn​)xk/k!. To prove the relation, use Rodrigues' formula e−xLn​=(n!)−1(xne−x)(n) and integrate by parts n times against Lm​. For n>m its nth derivative vanishes. At zero every derivative of xne−x of order less than n vanishes, and at infinity the exponential kills every polynomial boundary term. For n=m, Ln(n)​=(−1)n, so the integral is (n!)−1∫0∞​xne−xdx=1. Symmetry covers m>n. Thus these are orthonormal without an extra normalization factor.
