Boussinesq top-hat plume in a stratified ambient (Qz​=2αM​,Mz​=FQ/M,Fz​=−N2Q)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulent plume Top-hat plume model
OurBigBook.com Words: 297 Articles: 5
With common factors of π removed, steady kinematic fluxes satisfy Qz​=2αM​, Mz​=FQ/M and Fz​=−N2Q. The Batchelor entrainment hypothesis supplies volume, the integrated buoyancy force supplies momentum, and the environmental mass density gradient reduces buoyancy flux. This uses the Boussinesq approximation.
