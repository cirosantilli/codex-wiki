Fairness assumption

 Home Computer science Algorithm Algorithm correctness Formal verification
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A condition selecting admissible infinite paths in a transition system, often to exclude executions that postpone an enabled activity forever. The notation TS⊨fair​φ quantifies only over initial paths in a transition system satisfying the chosen condition. For example, GFp requires p infinitely often, while FGp requires p permanently from some point onwards. Neither condition implies the other.
