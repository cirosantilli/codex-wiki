Compositional inverse of a formal power series (f(T)=a1​T+O(T2),a1​∈R×)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Ring Commutative ring Formal power series
OurBigBook.com Words: 163 Articles: 1
A formal power series with zero constant coefficient has a compositional inverse over R exactly when its linear coefficient is a unit. Starting with b1​=a1−1​, the degree-k coefficient of f(b1​T+⋯+bk​Tk) is a1​bk​ plus an expression already determined by lower coefficients, so recursion gives a unique inverse. For a homomorphism of formal group laws, cancellation in the homomorphism identity proves that this inverse is also a formal-group homomorphism.
