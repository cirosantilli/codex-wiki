Dual conic (ℓTQ−1ℓ=0)

 ... Algebraic geometry Degree of an algebraic variety Degree of a projective curve Bézout's theorem Projective plane curve Smooth plane conic
OurBigBook.com Words: 178 Articles: 2
The tangent lines of a smooth plane conic form a smooth plane conic in the dual projective space. If the original equation is vTQv=0, its tangent has coefficient vector Qv, giving the displayed equation. The matrix Q is symmetric and invertible, so this correspondence is a projective linear isomorphism of the conics.
