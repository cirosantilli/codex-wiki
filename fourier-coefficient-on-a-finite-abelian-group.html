Fourier coefficient on a finite abelian group (f​(χ)=Ex​f(x)χ(x)​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Normalized Fourier analysis on a finite abelian group
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
For a scalar function on a finite abelian group, its coefficient at an additive character χ is the displayed uniform expectation. On F3d​, write χξ​(x)=e2πiξ⋅x/3. The coefficient at the constant character is the mean of f, and the Parseval identity on a finite group becomes ∑χ​∣f​(χ)∣2=E∣f∣2 after relabelling characters to match the transform convention.
