Dickey–Fuller test

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Autoregressive moving-average model Autoregressive model Unit-root autoregressive process
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The Dickey–Fuller test detects a unit-root autoregressive process against a stationary causal alternative. Regress ΔXt​ on Xt−1​, with deterministic terms appropriate to the model. Under the null, the usual regression statistic has a nonnormal Brownian motion functional limit, so ordinary normal critical values are inappropriate.
