Scientific confirmation

 Home Philosophy Philosophy of science
OurBigBook.com Words: 648 Articles: 13
Evidence supports a scientific hypothesis without necessarily proving it. Distinguish increasing its credibility from showing it highly credible in absolute terms; a low prior probability can rise substantially while remaining low.
