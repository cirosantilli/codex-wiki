Locally of finite type

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Morphism of finite type
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A morphism of schemes f:X→S is locally of finite type if, over every affine open subscheme SpecA⊆S, its inverse image is covered by affine schemes SpecB with B a finitely generated A-algebra. It is of finite type exactly when it is also a quasi-compact morphism.
