Piecewise-exponential mortality comparison (P(T≤τ)=1−exp(−∑k​hk​Δtk​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis Piecewise-exponential survival model
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For specified exposure periods with constant death hazard functions hk​, the integrated hazard through the endpoint is ∑k​hk​Δtk​. The displayed event probability follows from the survivor function identity. For different latent exposure schedules mixed with probabilities wj​, average the schedule-specific probabilities, ∑j​wj​(1−e−Hj​), rather than exponentiating the average integrated hazard. When the hazards are small, 1−e−Hj​≃Hj​ gives a person-time approximation. A comparison must specify the entire custody or treatment schedule; later changes to exposure cannot be inferred from an endpoint rate alone.
