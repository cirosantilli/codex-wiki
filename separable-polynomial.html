Separable polynomial

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 2
A polynomial over a field is separable when it has no repeated root in a splitting field. Equivalently, it is coprime to its formal derivative.
