Extra dissipation due to a rigid inclusion (D−D0​≥0)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Viscous dissipation Minimum-dissipation theorem for Stokes flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 211 Articles: 2
At fixed outer boundary velocity, extending the inclusion velocity rigidly through its interior makes the particle flow an admissible competitor for the particle-free Minimum-dissipation theorem for Stokes flow. Its interior rate-of-strain tensor vanishes. For a linear background, the increase in viscous dissipation is force times relative translation, torque times relative rotation, plus the contraction of the particle stresslet tensor with background strain.
