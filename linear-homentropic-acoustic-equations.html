Linear homentropic acoustic equations

For perturbations (ρ′,p′,u) about a uniform fluid of density ρ0​, the linearized continuity equation, momentum equation, and homentropic pressure perturbation relation are
ρt′​+ρ0​∇⋅u=0,ρ0​ut​=−∇p′,p′=c02​ρ′.
(4)
For irrotational flow u=∇ϕ, they imply p′=−ρ0​ϕt​ and ϕtt​=c02​∇2ϕ.
