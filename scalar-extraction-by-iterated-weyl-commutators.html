Scalar extraction by iterated Weyl commutators ((adD)α(adx)β(a)=cαβ​α!β!(−1)∣β∣)

For a nonzero element of a Weyl algebra over a characteristic-zero field, choose a monomial xαDβ of maximal total degree with coefficient cαβ​=0. The displayed iterated commutators kill every lower-degree monomial. Another monomial of the same degree survives only if all its exponents dominate (α,β), which forces equality. Hence the result is a nonzero scalar. If the element has filtered degree at most j, expansion of these commutators gives a sum of terms uav with both u and v of degree at most j. This proves faithful finite-step action of a Weyl algebra over any characteristic-zero field.
