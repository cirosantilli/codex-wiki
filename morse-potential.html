Morse potential (V(x)=De​(1−e−a(x−xe​))2)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state Observable Hamiltonian (quantum mechanics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 141 Articles: 2
The Morse potential models an anharmonic molecular bond with dissociation energy De​>0, equilibrium separation xe​, and inverse length a>0. Unlike a quantum harmonic oscillator, it has finitely many bound levels and a dissociation continuum.
