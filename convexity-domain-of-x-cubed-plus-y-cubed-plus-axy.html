Convexity domain of x cubed plus y cubed plus Axy

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Convex function
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The hessian matrix of
f(x,y)=x3+y3+Axy
(362)
is positive semidefinite exactly when
x≥0,y≥0,36xy≥A2.
(363)
This region is convex: for A=0 it is the epigraph of A2/(36x) in the positive quadrant, and for A=0 it is the closed first quadrant.
