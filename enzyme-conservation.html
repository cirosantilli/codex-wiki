Enzyme conservation (∑j=0n​Cj​=E0​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Enzyme kinetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
When each chemical reaction only changes the state of one enzyme molecule, summing all enzyme-state concentrations gives a conserved total. For sequential complexes satisfying C˙0​=−R1​, C˙i​=Ri​−Ri+1​, C˙n​=Rn​, the sum telescopes to zero. Bound substrate must be included separately in substrate mass conservation.
