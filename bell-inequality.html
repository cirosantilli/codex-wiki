Bell inequality

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Foundations of quantum mechanics Bell theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 2
A Bell inequality is a constraint on observable correlations satisfied by the specified class of local hidden-variable models. In finite-setting scenarios such constraints bound linear combinations of probabilities or expectation values. The CHSH inequality is one example, following from Bell factorization, measurement independence and bounded outcomes. A violation rules out the conjunction of the assumptions defining that model class; it does not alone identify a unique false premise.
