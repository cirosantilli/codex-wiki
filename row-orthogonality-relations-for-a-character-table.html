Row orthogonality relations for a character table

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Character orthogonality
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
For irreducible complex characters χ,ψ of a finite group,
∑g∈G​χ(g)ψ(g)​=∣G∣δχψ​.
(175)
Equivalently, summing over conjugacy classes C with representatives c gives
∑C​∣C∣χ(c)ψ(c)​=∣G∣δχψ​.
(176)
