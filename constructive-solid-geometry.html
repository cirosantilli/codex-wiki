Constructive solid geometry

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Computer aided geometric design
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
Constructive solid geometry describes a solid by combining primitive regions with Boolean set union, set intersection and difference. With primitive defining functions nonpositive inside, minimum implements union and maximum implements intersection. These formulas encode membership rather than signed distance functions. Solid modellers often regularize Boolean operations to remove isolated lower-dimensional remnants.
