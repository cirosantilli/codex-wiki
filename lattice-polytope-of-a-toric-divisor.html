Lattice polytope of a toric divisor (PD​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 29
For an invariant divisor D=∑ρ​aρ​Dρ​, its lattice polytope is
PD​={m∈MR​:⟨m,vρ​⟩≥−aρ​ for every ρ}.
(317)
Its lattice points index torus-character sections of the associated line bundle.
