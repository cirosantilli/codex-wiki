The broad feature has the scale expected for a carrier plasmon, a longitudinal collective oscillation of the mobile electron density. Using the parameters just estimated and N=1024m−3 gives
νp​=2πcωp​​=2πc1​ϵ0​ϵ∞​me∗​Ne2​​≃316cm−1​.

Conversely a bare screened plasma oscillation at 340cm−1 would require N≃1.16×1018cm−3 for the same parameters, consistent with the source's approximate density. Its broadness is also consistent with carrier damping, unlike the much sharper lattice line near 270cm−1.
This identifies a plasmon-related feature, not a uniquely determined undamped pure electronic mode. Gallium arsenide is polar, and its longitudinal optical phonon couples to the carrier plasmon. A more complete dielectric model has longitudinal eigenfrequencies
ω±2​=21​[ωLO2​+ωp2​±(ωLO2​+ωp2​)2−4ωp2​ωTO2​​].

The measured Raman scattering maximum can also shift with damping and the scattering matrix elements. Experimental Raman scattering from these coupled modes and carrier-collision broadening is established independently.[ref] The supplied approximate density and one broad spectrum do not determine a unique coupled-mode fit. Treating 340cm−1 as an exact pure-plasmon eigenfrequency would overstate the available information.
