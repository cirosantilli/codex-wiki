The term −αϵ is the leading electronic energy gain from splitting the initially degenerate orbitals and occupying the lower levels. Its negative sign expresses the lowering of the occupied-electron energy for the chosen elongation branch of the Jahn-Teller effect. The positive coefficient α is a linear electron-lattice coupling in this reduced model.
The term βϵ2/2 is the harmonic elastic cost of moving the ligands away from the undistorted reference structure. Mechanical stability requires β>0. The competition between linear gain and quadratic cost produces a finite equilibrium distortion rather than indefinite elongation.
