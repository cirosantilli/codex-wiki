For axial compression along z, the ordering is reversed: dxy​ lies below the dxz​,dyz​ doublet, and dx2−y2​ lies below dz2​ within eg​. One electron occupies dxy​ and the other occupies either dxz​ or dyz​, with parallel spins in the weak-distortion regime. The orbital degeneracy is therefore two, with S=1.
The retained orbital degeneracy allows further symmetry lowering and explains why this compressed arrangement does not provide the fully quenched equilibrium of the elongated case in the specified simple model.
