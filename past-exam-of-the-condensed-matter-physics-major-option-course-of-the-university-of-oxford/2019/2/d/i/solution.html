The photon energy fixes the resonant cyclotron frequency by ℏω=575μeV. Consequently
me​mcr​​=me​(575μeV)eℏB​≃0.2013TB​.
(16)
Read the two absorption maxima as approximately BL​=0.95--0.97T and BH​=2.10T. For B∥[001], the two valleys whose long axis is along z have mcr​=m1​, whereas the other four have mcr​=m1​m2​​. Hence
m1​≃0.19me​,m2​=m1​/me​[0.2013BH​/T]2​me​≃0.92–0.94me​.​
(17)
For the representative reading BL​=0.96T, the values are m1​=0.193me​ and m2​=0.925me​. The graph does not warrant additional precision. The larger longitudinal effective mass means flatter dispersion and a longer constant-energy ellipsoid along the valley axis.
