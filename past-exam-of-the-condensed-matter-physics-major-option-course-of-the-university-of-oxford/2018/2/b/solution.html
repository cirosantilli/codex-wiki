A linear combination of atomic orbitals expands a crystal wavefunction in localized orbitals on all sites,
Ψk​(r)=∑R,α,η​cαη​(k)eik⋅(R+dα​)φαη​(r−R−dα​).
(13)
Here α labels the basis site, η the retained atomic orbitals and R=ma1​+na2​. For the two-band model retain one relevant orbital on each B or N atom, usually the out-of-plane pz​ orbital, and neglect other orbital manifolds. Define normalized Bloch sums for Nc​ cells,
Φα​(k,r)=Nc​​1​∑m,n​eik⋅(ma1​+na2​+dα​)φα​(r−ma1​−na2​−dα​),α=B,N.
(14)
Then Ψ=αB​ΦB​+αN​ΦN​. Bloch periodicity puts the same two coefficients in every cell. We adopt the stated orthonormality, neglect longer-range hopping, and approximate the diagonal matrix elements by the supplied atomic energies ϵB​,ϵN​. More generally the crystal environment shifts these on-site energies, so equality with isolated-atom expectation values is an additional tight-binding approximation.
For equal real nearest-neighbour transfer integral tnn​, this phase convention gives
γ(k)=∑j=13​eik⋅nj​,H(k)=(ϵB​tnn​γ∗​tnn​γϵN​​).
(15)
The secular determinant is (ϵB​−E)(ϵN​−E)−tnn2​∣γ∣2=0. Thus
E±​(k)=2ϵB​+ϵN​​±4(ϵB​−ϵN​)2​+tnn2​∣γ∣2​.​
(16)
Since (ϵB​−ϵN​)2/4=(ϵB​+ϵN​)2/4−ϵB​ϵN​, this is the requested algebraic form. A hopping-sign convention or rephasing of the Bloch sums changes off-diagonal phases but not these eigenvalues. Explicitly,
