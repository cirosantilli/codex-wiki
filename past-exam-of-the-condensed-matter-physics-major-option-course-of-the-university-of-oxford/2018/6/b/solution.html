The Hall-plateau voltmeter calibration is obtained as follows. Let Dp​ be a plateau height in displayed units after any voltage offset is removed, with identified integer plateau index p. The true Hall voltage is Ix​RK​/p, so the calibration factor would be
λ=pDp​Ix​RK​​=pDp​3.8719×10−4​V/(displayed unit).​
(48)
The nominal current is 15nA. If several plateau heights are available, their inverse-integer ratios help identify indices and a fit of Dp​ against 1/p can separate a constant offset from the gain. Reversing magnetic field and antisymmetrizing the voltage also removes an even offset. A unique absolute index should not be asserted solely from an unspecified sequence of steps.
For an established exact integer p and independent current and height errors,
(λδλ​)2=(150.3​)2+(Dp​δDp​​)2.​
(49)
The current alone contributes 2% relative uncertainty; it is a common calibration uncertainty and does not average away by fitting more plateaux. Voltage-offset uncertainty and deviations from quantization must also be included where relevant. The 25±2μm width cancels because the Hall voltage divided by current is a sheet Hall resistance; it would enter the transverse electric field, not this calibration.
