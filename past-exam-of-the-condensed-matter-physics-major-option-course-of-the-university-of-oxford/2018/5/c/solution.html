The displayed horizontal variable is μ0​H in tesla. The source formulas written with a flux quantum actually have the dimensions of magnetic flux density, so define Bc1​=μ0​Hc1​ and Bc2​=μ0​Hc2​ when applying them:
Bc1​≃4πλ2Φ0​​lnκ,Bc2​=2πξ2Φ0​​,κ=λ/ξ.
(39)
From the onset of curvature away from the low-field line in panel A, Bc1​ is roughly 0.06T with uncertainty of order 0.01T. Panel B's branches approach the normal background over approximately 2–3T; use Bc2​≃2.5T as a coarse central estimate. Resolving irreversible loop closure from the true thermodynamic upper field, and correcting demagnetization, would require better data and geometry. These readings support scales, not precise material constants.
The upper field gives
ξ=2πBc2​Φ0​​​≃11.5nm.​
(40)
The leading-log critical-field inversion starts by dividing the two field formulas to eliminate ξ:
Bc2​Bc1​​=2κ2lnκ​≃0.024.
(41)
The large-κ solution is κ≃6.15, hence
λ≃71nm,ξ∼10–13nm,λ of order 70–80nm.​
(42)
The penetration-depth range is an indicative graph/model scale, not a propagated confidence interval. There is a mathematical ambiguity worth making explicit: f(κ)=lnκ/(2κ2) rises from zero at one, peaks at e​, then falls. The same central ratio also gives κ≃1.055 and λ≃12.1nm, so the printed restriction κ>1 alone does not select a unique solution. The leading-log lower-field formula is intended for appreciably large κ, which motivates the κ≃6 branch; near one it needs the omitted core-energy correction. Even the larger-branch estimate has systematic uncertainty from that approximation.
λ is the London penetration depth, the magnetic screening scale, while ξ is the superconducting coherence length, the recovery scale of the superconducting order parameter after spatial suppression. In the mixed state the complex order parameter vanishes in vortex cores of radius of order ξ, recovers between separated cores and winds in phase by 2π around each singly quantized vortex. Currents and magnetic field spread over scale λ; vortex density grows with the mean internal field according to nv​Φ0​=B. Near Hc2​ cores overlap and the order-parameter amplitude becomes small throughout the sample. A schematic core profile appears in solution.
The hysteresis is not the reversible equilibrium magnetization: defects pin vortices, so entry and exit leave different spatial flux distributions. Macroscopic currents oppose changes in flux, trapped vortices give remanence, and depinning and creep depend on history and temperature. Surface barriers can also contribute. The loop width probes an irreversible current scale, but its conversion to current density additionally needs sample geometry and an appropriate critical-state model.
