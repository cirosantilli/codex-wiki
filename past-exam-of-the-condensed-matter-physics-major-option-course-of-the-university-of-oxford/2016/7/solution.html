Between the transverse and longitudinal frequencies the undamped dielectric function is negative, giving an evanescent transmitted wave and nearly total reflection: the Reststrahlen band. Graphical uncertainty in the transverse edge makes this only a few-picosecond estimate: readings RTO​≃0.85–0.93 give approximately 2–11 ps. The displacement amplitude decays as e−γt/2 and has lifetime 2/γ; phonon occupation/energy decays as e−γt and has lifetime 1/γ. Anharmonic forces allow an optical phonon to decay into two lower-energy phonons when energy and crystal momentum are conserved. Spontaneous decay can persist at low temperature; defects and electron–phonon scattering can add further linewidth.
