For the body-centered tetragonal direct lattice, conventional reciprocal labels (h,k,l) are integer triples satisfying h+k+l even. This follows because the extra translation (1/2,1/2,1/2) must have phase one for a reciprocal lattice vector. In the k=0 section the condition is h+l even. The reciprocal steps are
a∗=0.3532π​=17.799 nm−1,c∗=1.052π​=5.984 nm−1.​
(12)
Within the strict index window −2<h,l<2, the reciprocal points are (h,l)=(0,0) and (±1,±1). The neighboring (±2,0) and (0,±2) points lie on the excluded boundary but are useful for constructing the zone bisectors.
At the requested drawing scale, one conventional step in h is a∗/5=3.560 cm and one step in l is c∗/5=1.197 cm. Thus the four off-origin included points have drawing coordinates (±3.560,±1.197) cm. Keeping these unequal physical scales avoids drawing a fictitious square reciprocal section.
