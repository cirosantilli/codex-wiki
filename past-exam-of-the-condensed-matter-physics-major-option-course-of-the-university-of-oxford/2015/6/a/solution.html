Choose orthonormal localized orbitals ∣R⟩. The on-site energy is E0​=⟨R∣H∣R⟩, and the sign convention in the question defines t(T)=−⟨R∣H∣R+T⟩. Bloch state diagonalizes this translationally invariant hopping problem. For real, inversion-symmetric nearest-neighbor amplitudes, t(±ax^)=tx​ and t(±by^​)=ty​, so the exponential pairs yield
E(k)=E0​−2tx​cos(kx​a)−2ty​cos(ky​b).​
(37)
The first zone is the rectangle ∣kx​∣≤π/a, ∣ky​∣≤π/b, wider in kx​ because a<b. Use Γ=(0,0), X=(π/a,0), Y=(0,π/b) and M=(π/a,π/b).
For positive ty​=t and tx​=2t, the four corner energies and the requested path are
EΓ​=E0​−6t,EM​=E0​+6t,EX​=E0​+2t,EY​=E0​−2t;Γ→M→X→Γ→Y→M→Γ.​
(38)
Along a path parametrized in reduced coordinates x=kx​a, y=ky​b, the full curve is simply E−E0​=−4tcosx−2tcosy. In particular the two appearances of Γ and the two diagonal traversals must have consistent endpoint energies.[ref] A reversed hopping sign changes which point is the band minimum; the usual positive-hopping convention is stated explicitly here.
