A magnetic field quantizes closed semiclassical orbits into Landau levels. Their passage through the Fermi energy modulates the density of states and scattering, producing Shubnikov-de Haas oscillations of resistivity. The fundamental frequency for a regular extremal orbit perpendicular to the field is
F=2πeℏ​Aext​,Δ(1/B)=ℏAext​2πe​.​
(42)
Thus two distinct fundamentals ordinarily identify two inequivalent extremal areas, rather than two arbitrary points or two spin directions automatically giving two different geometric areas.
The particle-hole equality of tetragonal extremal orbit areas is a special constraint of the exact half-filled nearest-neighbor band stated here. For B∥z, its regular closed endpoint sections are the electron contour at kz​c=π and the hole contour at kz​c=0 described in b. Translation (kx​a,ky​a)↦(kx​a+π,ky​a+π) changes the sign of both cosines and maps one enclosed pocket onto the other. Their areas are equal. Direct integration gives
Ae​a2=∫−2π/32π/3​2arccos[21​−cosx]dx=12.17169,Ah​=Ae​.
(43)
Consequently this bare model predicts
Fh​Fe​​=1,​
(44)
not two distinct regular fundamental frequencies. The intermediate kz​c=π/2 contour is a separatrix with a divergent cyclotron period, not another ordinary smooth extremal pocket to insert into the Onsager formula. The larger occupied-area slice at kz​=0 is the complement of a hole pocket on the Brillouin torus; counting that complement as a second enclosed planar orbit would give a spurious ratio.
For comparison, an additional small-kx​,ky​ parabolic approximation would replace the exact surface by (kx​a)2+(ky​a)2≃4+cos(kz​c) and suggest a rough belly-to-neck ratio 5/3. That approximation is uncontrolled at half filling and erases the actual topology; it cannot be asserted as an exact prediction of the supplied cosine band. The observation of genuinely different frequencies in a real material therefore needs further information, such as longer-range hopping, a departure from half filling, extra bands, symmetry breaking or a resolved spin-dependent effect. The universal area-ratio inference is valid, but a nonunit numerical ratio is not determined consistently by the complete stated band assumptions alone.[ref]
