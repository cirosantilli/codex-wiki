At high density the plasma scale remains large, but a strong magnetic field separates the two circular dielectric responses. Ignoring damping, the positive plasma cutoffs are
ωH,L​=ωp2​/ϵ∞​+ωc2​/4​ ± ωc​/2.​
(35)
Between suitable cutoffs one circular wave can propagate while the other is excluded; reflection and transmission are strongly polarization dependent. Around the cyclotron frequency the active response is resonant, while at optical frequencies far above ωc​ the splitting can be a relatively small correction to a large plasma edge.
There is also a low-frequency window: for the active component, 0<ω≪ωc​ gives ϵ≃ωp2​/(ωωc​)>0 when losses are small. This supports a helicon-like mode even when ω≪ωp​. That does not make an ordinary metal broadly transparent; the large index, boundary coupling, collisions and skin geometry still matter. In the common regime ωc​≪ωp​, bulk cyclotron absorption remains hidden from straightforward transmission, motivating a surface-sensitive experiment. Thus the relevant comparisons are among ω, ωc​, ωp​ and 1/τ, not carrier density and field in isolation.
