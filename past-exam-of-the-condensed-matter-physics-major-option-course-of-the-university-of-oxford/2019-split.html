The cover identifies the Part C major option, Trinity Term 2019, 17 June 2019, A11046W1; the bundle's 2018 filename does not determine the examination year. All six questions are treated. Figure 2(A) has missing image tiles, including part of its lower-left plot and some path labels. The readable portions and the standard silicon valley model support the conclusions below; the missing image content is not reconstructed. Numerical values read from plotted curves are estimates. Write e>0 for the elementary electric charge.
