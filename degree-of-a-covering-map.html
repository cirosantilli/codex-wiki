Degree of a covering map (degp=∣p−1(x)∣)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Covering space
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
For a covering map with nonempty connected base, the degree of a covering map is the common cardinality of its fibres. Each evenly covered neighbourhood makes this number locally constant, so connectedness makes it constant. The total covering space need not be connected. On a path-connected base the path lifting theorem gives a fibre bijection by path lifting. A disconnected base may have different degrees on different connected components.
