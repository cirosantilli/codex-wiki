Quantum Hall effect

 ... Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force Hall effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 33
The quantum Hall effect connects an electrical measurement to a quantum counting rule: a two-dimensional electron gas in a perpendicular magnetic field can exhibit plateaus of transverse electrical resistance. The integer case supplies a resistance scale built from the Planck constant and elementary charge, rather than the sample's dimensions. The fractional quantum Hall effect involves additional many-electron physics.
