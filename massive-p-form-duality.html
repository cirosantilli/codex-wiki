Massive p-form duality (BD−p−1​=m−1∗dAp​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Abelian p-form gauge field Massive p-form field
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
For a free massive p-form field, the displayed dual has degree D−p−1. The massive field equation reconstructs A locally from m−1∗dB with the signature-dependent sign. It also gives δB=0 and δdB+m2B=0. The polarization counts agree because (pD−1​)=(D−p−1D−1​). The construction depends on nonzero mass and therefore differs from massless p-form duality by one potential rank.
