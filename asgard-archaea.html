Asgard archaea

OurBigBook.com Words: 37
Asgard archaea are an archaeal group important in studies of eukaryotic origins. Molecular comparisons connect their genes to the nuclear lineage of eukaryotes, rather than making modern Asgard archaea the direct ancestors of modern animals or plants. See the original analysis cited under two-domain system.
