Airy wave theory

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Free surface Surface gravity wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 224 Articles: 6
Airy wave theory linearizes the motion of small-amplitude surface gravity waves in an inviscid, incompressible, irrotational fluid. At depth h, its dispersion relation is ω2=gktanh(kh); deep water is the limit kh→∞.
