Fan subdivision

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Toric geometry Fan in toric geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 626 Articles: 11
A subdivision of a fan replaces its cones by smaller cones with the same support and compatible intersections. It induces a proper birational toric morphism.
