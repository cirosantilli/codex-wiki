Fixed field

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 2
For a group G of automorphisms of a field L, the fixed field is
LG={x∈L:g(x)=x for every g∈G}.
(35)
For one automorphism σ, it is also written Lσ.
