Vacuum permeability (μ0​)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Maxwell equations Maxwell equations in matter Constitutive relations Permeability (electromagnetism)
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Vacuum permeability is the magnetic permeability appearing in the vacuum Maxwell equations. In SI, it relates vacuum magnetic field strength to magnetic flux density through B=μ0​H and obeys μ0​ε0​c2=1, where c is the speed of light and ε0​ the vacuum permittivity.
