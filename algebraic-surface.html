Algebraic surface

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 46
An algebraic surface is a two-dimensional algebraic variety. The birational classification of smooth projective surfaces uses their curves, divisor intersections and pluricanonical maps.
