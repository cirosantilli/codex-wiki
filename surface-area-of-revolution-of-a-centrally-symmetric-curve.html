Surface area of revolution of a centrally symmetric curve (A=2πdL)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Surface of revolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 98
Let a simple closed regular planar curve be invariant under point reflection through a center and remain strictly on one side of a coplanar axis of rotation, at distance d from its center. If L is its arc length, its surface of revolution has surface area 2πdL. To prove this, choose coordinates with the center at the origin and the axis of rotation at y=−d. The surface area formula is 2π∮(d+y)ds. Pair each point with its reflected point: the arc length element ds is unchanged and y changes sign. Thus ∮yds=0, proving the result. The one-side condition prevents the surface from passing through the axis of rotation or requiring an absolute value with changing sign.
