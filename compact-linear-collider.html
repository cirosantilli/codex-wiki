Compact Linear Collider

 Home Physics Experimental apparatus Particle accelerator Linear particle accelerator Linear collider
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
A proposed electron–positron linear collider using normal-conducting RF structures powered through a separate drive-beam deceleration system. The 2012 conceptual design studied multi-TeV operation and lower-energy stages; its 3 TeV design used approximately 100 MV/m main-linac gradients. Historical design targets should be distinguished from later staging revisions and from operating measurements. [ref]
