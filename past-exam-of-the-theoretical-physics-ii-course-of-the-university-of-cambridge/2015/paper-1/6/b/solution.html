The association between four real coordinates and Hermitian two-by-two matrices is a real-linear bijection. Congruence by S preserves Hermiticity, so X′=SXS† defines another real four-vector depending linearly on the original. Its determinant is
detX=(ct)2−x2−y2−z2,detX′=detSdetXdetS†=detX.
(59)
Thus the induced invertible linear map preserves the Minkowski quadratic form:
ΛTηΛ=η,η=diag(1,−1,−1,−1).​
(60)
The maps obtained continuously from the identity are proper and orthochronous. For example, a future timelike vector gives a positive-definite X, which remains positive definite under invertible congruence; its time direction is preserved. The matrices S and −S induce the same four-vector transformation, illustrating the Lorentz spinor double cover.
The source uses x+iy in the upper-right entry, corresponding to X=ctI+xσx​−yσy​+zσz​ for the standard Pauli matrices. This is a permissible basis convention, but its minus sign in the y basis must also be retained in the spinor identification.
