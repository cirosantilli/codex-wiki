The canonical commutation relation [x′,p′]=iℏI and hamiltonian H′=p′2/(2m)+mω2x′2/2 give
[H′,x′]=−miℏ​p′,[H′,p′]=iℏmω2x′.
(15)
Substitution in the Heisenberg equation of motion yields x˙′=p′/m and p˙​′=−mω2x′. Differentiating once more gives the harmonic-oscillator equation for each operator. Its initial-value solution is
x′(t)p′(t)​=x′(0)cosωt+mωp′(0)​sinωt,=p′(0)cosωt−mωx′(0)sinωt.​​
(16)
Differentiating these expressions at zero verifies both prescribed initial derivatives. They also preserve [x′(t),p′(t)]=iℏI.
