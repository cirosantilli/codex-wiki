For a time-independent Hamiltonian operator, the time-evolution operator is U(T)=e−iHT/ℏ, with T=t−t′. Insert its resolution of identity in normalized energy eigenstates:
⟨x∣U(T)∣x′⟩=∑α​⟨x∣α⟩e−iEα​T/ℏ⟨α∣x′⟩.
(25)
Since ⟨x∣α⟩=φα​(x), the retarded quantum propagator in the question is
K(x,t∣x′,t′)=Θ(T)α∑​φα​(x)φα∗​(x′)e−iEα​T/ℏ.​

The Heaviside step function imposes the chosen retarded convention. If the Hamiltonian operator has a continuous spectrum, the spectral sum includes its continuum integral; it must not be restricted to bound states. The spectral resolution of a quantum propagator also shows that K tends to δ(x−x′) as T↓0. With the step factor it obeys (iℏ∂t​−H)K=iℏδ(T)δ(x−x′); the corresponding Green function with a unit delta source is K/(iℏ).
