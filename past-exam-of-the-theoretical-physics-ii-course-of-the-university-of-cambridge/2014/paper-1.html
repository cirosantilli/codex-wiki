An initially occupied, isolated instantaneous energy eigenstate follows its changing eigenspace, acquiring a dynamical phase and generally a Berry phase. For smooth nondegenerate evolution, a useful local smallness condition is
∣En​(t)−Em​(t)∣2ℏ∣⟨m(t)∣H˙(t)∣n(t)⟩∣​≪1(m=n)​.
(1)
A nonvanishing spectral gap and suitably smooth, slow driving throughout the evolution are needed; the local inequality alone is not a universal long-time error bound. Gap closure invalidates the simple nondegenerate approximation. A separated degenerate eigenspace may mix internally even when transitions out of it are suppressed.

For an interaction of finite range b, higher partial waves are suppressed at kb≪1 by the centrifugal barrier, away from exceptional higher-wave resonances. Outside the potential, the zero-energy radial S-wave scattering equation is u′′=0, where ψ=u/r. Its solution is proportional to r−a, so normalization of the constant term gives
ψ≃1−ra​​.
(15)
The scattering length a is the extrapolated zero of u(r). At nonzero small k, this form applies in the overlap region b≪r≪k−1; it is not the far-field limit at fixed k. Equivalently, kcotδ0​→−1/a and f0​→−a.
