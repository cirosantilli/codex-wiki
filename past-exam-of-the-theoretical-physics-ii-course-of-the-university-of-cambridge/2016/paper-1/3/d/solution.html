Here S is the first-order generator. The full second-order commutator includes terms changing the number difference by four. To retain only fixed site occupancies, keep the products XY and YX.
With f=f(Δ) and f+​=f(Δ+2), the mixed commutators give
[fX,Y]=fXY−f+​YX,[−f+​Y,X]=fXY−f+​YX.
(46)
Thus occupancy-preserving second-order bosonic hopping gives
Hocc(2)​=t[f(Δ)XY−f(Δ+2)YX].​
(47)
For bosons, normal ordering gives
XY=Na​+P,YX=Nb​+P,P=∑s,s′​as†​as′​bs′†​bs​.
(48)
Consequently an explicit form in terms of the supplied function is
Hocc(2)​=t[f(Δ)Na​−f(Δ+2)Nb​+{f(Δ)−f(Δ+2)}P].​
(49)
The operator P exchanges spin labels between sites while preserving their total occupancies. Defining dimensionless Schwinger-boson spins sa​=21​as†​σss′​as′​ and similarly for B,
P=21​Na​Nb​+2sa​⋅sb​.
(50)
No factor of ℏ2 is needed for these dimensionless spins; for physical spin operators Sa​=ℏsa​ it becomes 2Sa​⋅Sb​/ℏ2. The spin exchange belongs in the occupancy-preserving part, whereas two hops in the same direction do not.
