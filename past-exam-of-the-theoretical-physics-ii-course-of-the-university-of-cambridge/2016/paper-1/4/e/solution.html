The ultraviolet divergence of an instantaneous vector-potential quench qualifies this formal product. For an exactly instantaneous vector-potential step in three spatial dimensions, at large k the mixing behaves as vk​∼eA0​⋅k/(2ℏk). Thus ∫d3k∣vk​∣2 diverges in the ultraviolet. In a finite quantization box, a finite momentum cutoff makes the product a normalized Fock-space vector; a sufficiently smooth finite-duration pulse removes this particular instantaneous-quench pathology. The per-mode distribution remains the result above, while the unregulated infinite product should not be asserted to have a finite nonzero global normalization.
