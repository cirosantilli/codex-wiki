On periodic ring states the angular-momentum index m is an integer. At the stated resonance,
e−iαm2=e−2πim2=1,
(13)
so UT​ is the identity on every orbital state. After N steps from the normalized ground state,
ψN​(θ)=2π​1​eiNηcosθ.
(14)
Its phase varies in space but its position probability density remains uniform. Periodicity permits integration by parts, giving
⟨K⟩N​=2Iℏ2​∫02π​∣∂θ​ψN​∣2dθ=2Iℏ2N2η2​2π1​∫02π​sin2θdθ.
(15)
Consequently
⟨K⟩N​=4Iℏ2η2​N2.​
(16)
Equivalently, the angular-momentum probabilities are Pm​=Jm​(Nη)2, with ∑m​m2Jm​(z)2=z2/2. The mean angular momentum remains zero while its variance grows as N2. This quantum resonance of the kicked rotor gives coherent ballistic energy growth. The Bessel second-moment identity connects the momentum spreading to that quadratic energy law; an incoherent random walk would instead give linear energy growth.
