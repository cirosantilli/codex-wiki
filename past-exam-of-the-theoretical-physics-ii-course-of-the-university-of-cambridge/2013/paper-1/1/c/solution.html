The oriented solid angle A is swept out by the field direction. These are the Berry phases for the eigenstates parallel and antiparallel to that direction. Retaining the spinor sign makes the quantum phase vanish modulo 2π when the field does not trace a nontrivial loop. The assumption ω≪B suffices for adiabatic following; near degeneracy it must not be silently dropped.[ref]
