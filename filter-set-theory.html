Filter (set theory)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 40
A filter F on a set X is a nonempty family of subsets that excludes the empty set, is closed under finite intersections, and is upward closed under inclusion.
