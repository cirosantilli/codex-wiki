Wave reflection by odd extension (u(x,t)=21​[fodd​(x−ct)+fodd​(x+ct)])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation D'Alembert's formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
For the half-line wave equation utt​=c2uxx​ with a zero Dirichlet boundary condition at x=0, extend both the initial displacement and initial velocity as odd functions to the full line. D'Alembert's formula then gives an odd solution in x, so u(0,t)=0. Restriction to x≥0 solves the desired initial-boundary value problem. With zero initial velocity, the displayed formula shows reflection at the fixed endpoint with reversal of the displacement sign. Continuous piecewise smooth displacement data give a weak solution; a classical solution requires sufficient additional regularity. In particular, a kink in the initial displacement can prevent the pointwise time derivative at that kink from taking the assigned initial velocity, even though the initial velocity is attained in the L2 sense.
