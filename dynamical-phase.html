Dynamical phase (δ=−ℏ−1∫E(t)dt)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state Quantum phase
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
For an energy eigenstate with instantaneous energy eigenvalue E(t), the dynamical phase is
δ(T)=−ℏ1​∫0T​E(t)dt.
(26)
It comes from the Schrödinger equation and depends on the time spent evolving. In an ideal cycle under the adiabatic theorem, a periodic choice of instantaneous energy eigenstate gives ∣Ψ(T)⟩=ei(δ+γ)∣Ψ(0)⟩, with Berry phase γ.
