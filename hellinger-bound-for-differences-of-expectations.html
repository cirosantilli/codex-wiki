Hellinger bound for differences of expectations

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics f-divergence Squared Hellinger distance Hellinger distance
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
If f is a measurable function into a separable Banach space and has finite second moments under probability measures μ,μ′, then their Bochner integral expected values satisfy
∥Eμf−Eμ′f∥≤2(Eμ∥f∥2+Eμ′∥f∥2)1/2dHell​(μ,μ′).
(40)
Here the Hellinger distance includes the factor 1/2 in its square. For a proof, use ν=(μ+μ′)/2, p=dμ/dν, and q=dμ′/dν. Apply the norm bound for a Bochner integral and the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality to f(p−q)=f(p​−q​)(p​+q​), then use (p​+q​)2≤2(p+q). This explains why stability of a Bayesian posterior in Hellinger distance controls posterior expected values when the relevant second moments remain bounded.
