Surface correction to thermodynamic free energy (F=Vf+O(A))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamic limit
OurBigBook.com Words: 286
For finite-range interactions and bounded wall couplings, only sites within an interaction range of the wall have altered local environments. In a regular container of linear size L and dimension d, the surface area scales as A∼Ld−1 while volume scales as V∼Ld. A boundary contribution of order A therefore gives an order-1/L correction to free-energy density. Away from a critical phenomenon, a regular surface contribution often has the form F=Vf+Aγ+⋯, with γ a wall Helmholtz free energy per area. Critical finite-size rounding, edges and long-range interactions require separate care; vanishing relative boundary size does not guarantee a simple surface expansion in every regime.
An exact check uses the zero-field nearest-neighbour Ising model on a chain of N≥3 sites with fixed coupling J>0 and K=J/(kB​T). The open chain has N−1 bonds, while the periodic ring has N. Their finite partition functions and dimensionless Helmholtz free energies per spin gN​=FN​/(NkB​T) are
ZNopen​=2(2coshK)N−1,ZNring​=(2coshK)N+(2sinhK)N,
(39)
g∞​=−log(2coshK),gNopen​−g∞​=NlogcoshK​,gNring​−g∞​=−Nlog[1+(tanhK)N]​.
(40)
For the open chain, sum the last Ising spin successively: each removed bond contributes 2coshK, leaving a factor of two. For the ring, the transfer matrix for the one-dimensional Ising model has eigenvalues 2coshK and 2sinhK, and its Nth power's trace gives the displayed sum. The two ends are a constant-size boundary in one dimension, so the open-chain total correction is kB​TlogcoshK, and its per-spin correction is exactly proportional to 1/N.
Both sets of finite results approach the same g∞​ at the fixed plotted K=0.8. Multiplying the correction by N exposes the open-chain boundary term, while the ring correction vanishes exponentially at fixed finite K. This model's thermodynamic limit remains a real analytic function at every positive temperature: having a thermodynamic limit does not require a phase transition.
