Villain model (ZV​=∫∏x​2πdθx​​∏e​∑ne​∈Z​2πβ​e−ne2​/(2β)+ine​Δe​θ​)

A Villain model replaces the cosine Boltzmann factor of the XY model by a periodic Gaussian weight. The Poisson summation formula makes its bond weight equal to ∑p∈Z​exp[−β(ψ−2πp)2/2]. The periodic Gaussian retains integer winding defects and permits an explicit separation into spin waves and vortices in two dimensions. Its replacement of the cosine model is an approximation; subsequent transformations within the Villain model can be exact.
