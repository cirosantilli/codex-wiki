Regular functor

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Functor Finite-limit-preserving functor
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A functor between regular categories is regular if it preserves finite limits and regular epimorphisms. It then preserves image factorizations, since finite-limit preservation preserves monomorphisms. A representable functor C(P,−) is regular exactly when P is projective with respect to regular epimorphisms. Regularity of a functor does not alone imply faithfulness or reflection of isomorphisms.
