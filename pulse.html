Pulse

 Home Physics Wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A pulse is a localized disturbance that passes a fixed location during a bounded or effectively bounded time interval. A travelling wave pulse may be written y(x,t)=f(x−ct) with localized f. Its width and peak amplitude describe its extent and size; a wave packet additionally emphasizes a carrier and an envelope.
