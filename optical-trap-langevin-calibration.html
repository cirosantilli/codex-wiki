Optical trap Langevin calibration (ζx˙=−kx+ξ(t),fc​=k/(2πζ))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical tweezers Harmonic optical trap Thermal calibration of an optical trap
OurBigBook.com Words: 503 Articles: 1
Add the random thermal force to the deterministic optical trap relaxation time model. In one stationary coordinate of a harmonic optical trap, the overdamped Langevin equation is
ζx˙=−kx+ξ(t),⟨ξ(t)⟩=0,⟨ξ(t)ξ(t′)⟩=2kB​Tζδ(t−t′).
(29)
Here ξ is Gaussian white noise understood through its integrals, δ is the Dirac delta function, and ζ is the linear friction coefficient. The covariance coefficient has units N2s; multiplying by δ(t−t′), of units s−1, gives squared force. This fluctuation-dissipation relation for a Langevin particle makes the stationary variance σ2=kB​T/k, consistent with the equipartition theorem. The solution is an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process with autocovariance function
Cx​(t)=σ2e−∣t∣/τ,τ=kζ​.
(30)
Indeed, at positive lag the noise has zero correlation with the initial position, so ζCx′​=−kCx​; its initial value is σ2. This supplies both a decay time and an amplitude, making a stronger calibration test than a position histogram alone.
Specify the Fourier transform convention before using a power spectral density. For frequency f in hertz, define Sx(2)​(f)=∫−∞∞​Cx​(t)e−2πiftdt and the one-sided density Sx(1)​(f)=2Sx(2)​(f) for f>0. Integration of the exponential gives
Sx(1)​(f)=k2+(2πfζ)24kB​Tζ​=π2ζ(f2+fc2​)kB​T​,fc​=2πζk​,∫0∞​Sx(1)​(f)df=kkB​T​.
(31)
The position power spectral density has units m2/Hz; its integral is a variance, not a standard deviation. At f=fc​ it is half its zero-frequency plateau. Angular frequency conventions require a corresponding 2π conversion in both axis and integration measure. If x=β(V−V0​) with position sensitivity β in m/V, then SV(1)​=Sx(1)​/β2 in V2/Hz before independent detector noise is added.
For an original invented calibration example, choose a=0.500μm, dynamic viscosity η=1.00×10−3Pas, T=300K, k=0.0200pN/nm=2.00×10−5N/m, and β=100nm/V. The unbounded no-slip Stokes drag law gives
ζ=9.42478×10−9Ns/m,τ=0.471239ms,fc​=337.737Hz,σ=14.391nm.
(32)
The corresponding voltage standard deviation is 0.14391V, and the position power spectral density plateau is 0.390369nm2/Hz, or 3.90369×10−5V2/Hz in the detector. Recovering both k=kB​T/σ2 and k=2πζfc​ is an internal model check; actual agreement also tests the assumed temperature, drag and detector response.
The generator uses the exact stationary update xj+1​=ρxj​+σ1−ρ2​Zj​, with ρ=e−Δt/τ and independent standard normal random variables Zj​. For a demeaned block of N samples and its unnormalized discrete Fourier transform Xj​, the two-sided periodogram is Δt∣Xj​∣2/N; doubling positive bins except the zero and Nyquist endpoints gives a one-sided density. Summing those bins times Δf=1/(NΔt) returns the block mean square by discrete Parseval identity. Independent detector noise, finite exposure and aliasing would alter a measured spectrum and must enter a fit before interpreting fc​.
