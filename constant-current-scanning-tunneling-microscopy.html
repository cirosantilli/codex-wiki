Constant-current scanning tunneling microscopy (I(x,y,z)=I0​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Potential barrier Quantum tunnelling Tunnel junction Scanning tunneling microscopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 116
Choose a fixed tip-sample electric potential difference and a positive target electric current magnitude I0​. At each lateral position (x,y), feedback control adjusts z until the measured electric current approaches I0​. Since increasing the gap reduces quantum tunnelling, excessive electric current calls for retraction; insufficient electric current calls for approach. The image is the calibrated vertical displacement z(x,y), while the residual electric current error tests how closely the loop followed its target.
This is a contour of equal electric current, not necessarily a contour of equal separation. The electronic contrast in scanning tunneling microscopy model below shows why. It also explains the value of vibration isolation: an unintended displacement changes the same sensitive gap as the feature being measured. A scan must be slow enough for the finite bandwidth of feedback control; otherwise z(x,y) lags the desired contour.
