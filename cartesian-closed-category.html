Cartesian closed category

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Adjoint functor
OurBigBook.com Words: 413 Articles: 10
A category with finite products is cartesian closed when every product functor −×A has a right adjoint (−)A, called exponentiation by A.
