Capacitor bridging a divider and a return resistor (Vth​=2V/3,Rth​=5R/3)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Capacitor
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
Feed a node through resistance R from an ideal voltage V, connect that node to ground through 2R, and connect a capacitor from it to a second node grounded through R. The capacitor sees a Thévenin equivalent with open-circuit voltage 2V/3 and resistance (R∥2R)+R=5R/3. For zero initial capacitor voltage, IC​=2Ve−t/τ/(5R) and τ=5RC/3. The 2R branch current is V/(3R)−IC​/3, so the two branch currents coincide at t=τlog(8/5). This illustrates why the source-side resistor alone does not determine a charging time constant.
