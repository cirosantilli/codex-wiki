Interval map

 Home Physics Branch of physics Dynamical systems Discrete dynamical system
OurBigBook.com Words: 217 Articles: 6
An interval map is a continuous function F:I→I from a real interval to itself. Its iterates form a one-dimensional discrete dynamical system.
