Sampling uncertainty of an endpoint diffusivity estimate (3nD/D∼χ3n2​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 62
Each Cartesian Brownian increment has variance 2Dt. Summing their normalized squares gives a chi-squared distribution with 3n degrees of freedom. Hence D is unbiased and has relative standard deviation 2/(3n)​. Its inverse is not unbiased: for 3n>2, E[1/D]=[3n/(3n−2)]/D. Thus the direct inverse-diffusivity radius estimate has appreciable finite-sample bias when only a few trajectories are observed. Measurement noise or drift adds separate systematic error.
