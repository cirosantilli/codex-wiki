Circular torus coordinates (x=((A+rsinα)cosβ,(A+rsinα)sinβ,rcosα))

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Change of variables formula Orthogonal coordinates
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
For a ring torus of major radius A>0, circular torus coordinates use distance r and angle α in the circular cross-section, and azimuth β around the central axis. The coordinate tangent vectors are the three partial derivatives of the displayed position. They are mutually orthogonal, with scale factors of orthogonal coordinates 1,r,A+rsinα. For 0<r<A their ordered Jacobian determinant is r(A+rsinα)>0, so dV=r(A+rsinα)drdαdβ. At r=0 the cross-section angle degenerates. These are tubular coordinates for a circular torus, rather than the confocal coordinate convention also called toroidal coordinates.
