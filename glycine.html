Glycine (Gly, G)

 Home Biology Protein Protein structure Protein primary structure Amino acid composition
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Glycine is an amino acid, represented by Gly or G for an incorporated amino acid residue. Its amino acid side chain is a hydrogen chemical atom. It was identified at one insulin N-terminus in Sanger's insulin sequencing experiment. See IUPAC Blue Book, P-103.1: amino-acid names, symbols and structures.
