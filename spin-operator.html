Spin operator (S^)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Spin
OurBigBook.com Words: 155 Articles: 3
A spin operator describes intrinsic angular momentum. Its components obey angular-momentum commutators; for spin one half, S^j​=ℏσj​/2 in terms of the Pauli matrices.
