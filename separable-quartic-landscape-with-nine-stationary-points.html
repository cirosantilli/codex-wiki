Separable quartic landscape with nine stationary points (F(x,y)=x4−ax2−y4+by2,a,b>0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Critical point
OurBigBook.com Words: 147
The gradient of this polynomial vanishes exactly when x=0,±a/2​ and y=0,±b/2​, giving nine stationary points. Its diagonal Hessian matrix has entries 12x2−2a and 2b−12y2. The second-derivative test therefore gives two strict local maxima at (0,±b/2​), with value b2/4, and two strict local minima at (±a/2​,0), with value −a2/4. The origin and the four points (±a/2​,±b/2​) are saddle points of a scalar function, with values zero and (b2−a2)/4, respectively. Every stationary point is nondegenerate.
Completing the squares organizes the contour plot:
F=4b2−a2​+(x2−2a​)2−(y2−2b​)2.
(54)
The level set through the four outer saddle points of a scalar function factors as
F−4b2−a2​=(x2−y2+2b−a​)(x2+y2−2a+b​).
(55)
It is a circle together with a hyperbola when a=b; for a=b, the latter factor's complementary zero set is the pair of lines y=±x. Neither the local maxima nor the local minima are global, since the restrictions to the two coordinate axes are unbounded in opposite directions. The case a=1, b=4 gives four outer saddle values 15/4 and an exact circle-hyperbola contour crossing at all four outer saddle points of a scalar function.
