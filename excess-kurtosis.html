Excess kurtosis (γ2​=κ−3)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Kurtosis
OurBigBook.com Words: 157
Excess kurtosis subtracts 3 from kurtosis, making the normal distribution baseline zero. The non-excess convention is sometimes called raw kurtosis; this still uses a fourth central moment, not the uncentered fourth moment E[X4]. Software may report either kurtosis or excess kurtosis, so the convention must be stated.[ref]
For a standard normal distribution, the moment-generating function of a standard normal variable gives E[Z4]=3, hence excess kurtosis zero. For a continuous uniform distribution on [−3​,3​], the mean is zero, the variance is one, and direct integration gives
κ=23​1​∫−3​3​​z4dz=59​,γ2​=−56​.

The rare-outlier three-point distribution can instead have kurtosis 100 and excess kurtosis 97 with mean zero and variance one. These are calculated properties of specified probability distributions, rather than a measured statistic.
