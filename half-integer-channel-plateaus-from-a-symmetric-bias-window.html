For rigid spin-degenerate channel transmissions and μS,D​=EF​±eV/2, differentiating I=(2e/h)∑n​∫μD​μS​​Tn​(E)dE gives Gd​=(e2/h)∑n​[Tn​(μS​)+Tn​(μD​)]. If the two edges sample neighboring integer channel counts, Gd​ is a half-integer multiple of 2e2/h. Explicit bias dependence of the potential adds terms; the apparent fraction does not imply fractional carriers.
