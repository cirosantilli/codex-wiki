Kinematic magnetic dynamo (Dt​B=(B⋅∇)u)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 217 Articles: 4
A kinematic magnetic dynamo studies magnetic field amplification by a prescribed fluid flow, neglecting the field's feedback on the velocity through the Lorentz force. With incompressibility and zero magnetic diffusivity, a Lagrangian field vector evolves by its local velocity-gradient matrix. A growth law derived in this approximation cannot hold indefinitely once magnetic stresses significantly change the flow.
