Strengthened Sylow congruence from intersections (np​≡1(modpa))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Sylow theorems Conjugation action on Sylow subgroups
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For a finite group and a Sylow subgroup P, conjugation by P on all Sylow subgroups has stabilizer P∩Q at Q: the normal Sylow subgroup Q of NG​(Q) contains every p-subgroup of that normalizer. Thus the orbit length is [P:P∩Q]. If every Q=P has this index at least pa, each such orbit length is divisible by pa, whereas P is a singleton orbit. Summing orbit lengths proves the displayed stronger integer congruence.
