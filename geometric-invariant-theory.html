Geometric invariant theory

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation Invariant theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 478 Articles: 7
Geometric invariant theory constructs algebraic quotients of group actions by a reductive algebraic group using invariant sections and a chosen linearization of a line bundle. An affine quotient is the spectrum of the polynomial invariant ring; a projective quotient uses homogeneous invariants and excludes the points where all positive-degree invariants vanish. Stability separates the orbits which give well-behaved geometric quotient points.
