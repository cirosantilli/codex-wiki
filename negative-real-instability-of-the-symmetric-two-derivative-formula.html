Negative-real instability of the symmetric two-derivative formula (Fz​(−1)=2z<0)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Linear multistep method Multiderivative multistep method Symmetric two-step two-derivative formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
On the Dahlquist test equation, the symmetric two-step two-derivative formula has amplification polynomial Fz​(w)=(z2−7z+15)w2−16zw−(z2+7z+15). For every real z<0, its leading coefficient is positive and Fz​(−1)=2z<0, whereas Fz​(w)→+∞ as w→−∞. The intermediate value theorem therefore gives a real root below minus one. Thus the method is not A-stable, without any appeal to a multistep order barrier.
