Cumulative and birth stellar metallicities (Zbirth​(δ)=dδd​[δZ∗​(δ)])

 ... Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy evolution Galactic chemical evolution Galactic metallicity Stellar metallicity
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
In a well-mixed closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution, gas consumed into locked stellar mass supplies a natural cumulative mass coordinate δ. The mean stellar metallicity is Z∗​(δ)=δ−1∫0δ​Zbirth​(s)ds, giving the displayed derivative relation. If the birth abundance increases linearly, Zbirth​=bδ, its cumulative mean is half the current maximum. More generally the maximum of any nonnegative abundance distribution cannot lie below its mean. A fixed initial mass function and the same survival selection are required to identify this mass-weighted distribution with a star-count distribution.
