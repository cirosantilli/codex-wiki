Cauchy kernel fundamental solution (∂zˉ​(1/(πz))=δ0​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Fundamental solution of a linear differential operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
The locally integrable function E(z)=1/(πz) on the plane is a fundamental solution for the Wirtinger derivative ∂zˉ​=(∂x​+i∂y​)/2. Let L=(4π)−1log(x2+y2) be the fundamental solution of the Laplace equation. Its first distributional derivatives agree with its ordinary ones: deleting a radius-ε disk in integration by parts gives an error O(ε∣logε∣), tending to zero. Hence E=4∂z​L and
∂zˉ​E=4∂zˉ​∂z​L=ΔL=δ0​.
(25)
The analogous fundamental solution for ∂z​ is 1/(πzˉ). The singular support of each kernel is exactly {0}: they are smooth elsewhere, and smoothness near zero would make their Wirtinger derivatives smooth there, contradicting the Dirac delta distribution identity.
