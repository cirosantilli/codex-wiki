Normal modes of an anchored complete spring graph (K=k[(n+1)I−11T])

Connect every pair among n moving scalar coordinates and one fixed coordinate by identical springs of stiffness k. The coupling pattern is the complete graph Kn+1​ with one vertex fixed. Its potential energy is k∑i<j​(Xi​−Xj​)2/2. The moving-coordinate stiffness matrix is the principal submatrix displayed in the header. For equal masses m, the uniform normal mode has squared angular frequency k/m, while every sum-zero vector has squared angular frequency (n+1)k/m. There are n−1 such degenerate modes. This follows directly from the Laplacian spectrum of a complete graph and its rank-one matrix form. Constant gravitational forces shift the equilibrium without changing the stiffness.
