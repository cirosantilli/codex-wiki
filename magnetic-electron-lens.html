Magnetic electron lens

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Electron optics Electron lens
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
A magnetic electron lens uses a solenoid around the beam axis; iron pole pieces concentrate its magnetic field.[ref] A strictly axial trajectory in a perfectly uniform axial magnetic field experiences no Lorentz force, since v×B=0. Near the entrance, a radial field component turns an advancing electron azimuthally; the resulting sideways velocity in the axial magnetic field also produces a radial Lorentz force. This couples image rotation and focusing. Real trajectories bend smoothly, whereas a thin lens diagram compresses that bending into one effective plane.
