Thermal diffusion time (L2/κ)

 ... Thermodynamics Heat Thermal conduction Fourier's law Thermal conductivity Thermal diffusivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 44 Articles: 1
The thermal diffusion time across a distance L is L2/κ, where κ is the thermal diffusivity. It is the time required for a temperature disturbance to spread over that distance by thermal conduction.
