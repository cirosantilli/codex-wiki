Black-Scholes equation with constant cash storage costs (Vt​+(rS+δ)VS​+21​σ2S2VSS​=rV)

OurBigBook.com Words: 119
If holding one unit of a commodity costs a constant cash amount δdt for storage, its gain is dS−δdt. A delta hedge long a derivative and short VS​ commodity units has riskless drift Vt​+21​σ2S2VSS​+δVS​ and value V−SVS​. Equating the drift to the bank return gives the displayed Black-Scholes equation. This is an additive cash carry term, unlike a proportional dividend or storage yield.
The corresponding pricing diffusion is dS=(rS+δ)dt+σSdW. A fixed-strike forward has value S+δ(1−e−rτ)/r−Ke−rτ, and put-call parity gives that same value for a matching call minus put. Replication buys the commodity and a bank reserve equal to the present value of its future storage payments. For r=0, the reserve is δτ.
