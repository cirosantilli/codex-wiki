Feasibility of donor-wind binary contact (min(ζ0​,ζ1​)≤ζ∗​≤max(ζ0​,ζ1​))

A donor star with stellar radius response exponent ζ∗​ can maintain exact contact in the donor-wind Roche-lobe response model for some retained fraction 0≤f≤1 precisely when ζ∗​ lies between ζ0​ and ζ1​. This follows because the attainable Roche-lobe radius response exponents form that closed interval. If its endpoints coincide, all fractions work when ζ∗​ equals the common value and none work otherwise. With negative donor mass-loss rate, dlog(Rd​/RL​)/dt=(ζ∗​−ζL​)M˙d​/Md​ distinguishes detachment from increasing overfill. Dynamical stability of binary mass transfer requires the adiabatic donor response.
