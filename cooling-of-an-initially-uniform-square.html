Cooling of an initially uniform square (T∼(16T0​/π2)sin(πx/ℓ)sin(πy/ℓ)e−2κπ2t/ℓ2)

For a square of side ℓ initially at constant temperature T0​, held at zero boundary temperature for t>0, the heat equation Tt​=κΔT with κ>0 has solution
T=π216T0​​∑m,n≥1m,n odd​​mnsin(mπx/ℓ)sin(nπy/ℓ)​e−κπ2(m2+n2)t/ℓ2.
(17)
The double Fourier sine series of the initial constant gives the coefficients. The spatial modes are Dirichlet Laplacian eigenfunctions, so separation of variables gives their decay rates. At late times the (1,1) mode dominates, giving the displayed profile; the next nonzero decay exponent is 10κπ2/ℓ2. At early times the series approaches the constant at each interior point and in L2 space, while the boundary remains at zero. An instantaneous change to the boundary condition therefore need not produce a solution continuous at the initial boundary in space-time.
