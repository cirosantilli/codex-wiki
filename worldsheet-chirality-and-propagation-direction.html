Worldsheet chirality and propagation direction (ψ+​=F(τ−σ), ψ−​=G(τ+σ))

OurBigBook.com Words: 41
In signature (−,+), choose real gamma matrices γ0=(01​−10​), γ1=(01​10​) and γ∗​=−γ0γ1. The Dirac equation then gives (∂τ​+∂σ​)ψ+​=0 and (∂τ​−∂σ​)ψ−​=0. Positive chirality is right-moving and negative chirality is left-moving in this orientation. Reversing the definition of γ∗​ exchanges the labels without changing the physics.
