Alfvén radius (RA​)

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Axisymmetric magnetohydrodynamic wind Alfvén surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
The Alfvén radius characterizes where a magnetized stellar wind crosses its Alfvén surface. For a roughly radial wind, vwind​≃B/μ0​ρ​ and M˙w​≃4πRA2​ρvwind​. The wind's angular-momentum lever arm is of order RA​: its torque is J˙∼−M˙w​ΩRA2​, up to geometry factors. The precise wind invariant uses the cylindrical radius of the surface crossing, rather than necessarily its spherical radius.
