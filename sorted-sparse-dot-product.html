Sorted sparse dot product (x⋅y=∑i∈suppx∩suppy​xi​yi​)

 ... Analysis Numerical analysis Sparse optimization Compressed sensing Sparse vector Sparse linked vector representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 97
For two sparse vectors with the same dimension and sorted nonzero entries, merge their coordinate lists. Equal indices contribute their product and advance both cursors. If one index is smaller, advance only that cursor: the other vector has zero at that coordinate, because all its smaller indices have already been processed and all remaining indices are larger. This proves a loop invariant consisting of the accumulated inner product over settled indices. Every iteration consumes at least one stored entry, giving time complexity O(kx​+ky​+1) and constant auxiliary space for kx​,ky​ nonzero entries. The method avoids work proportional to the ambient dimension. Its floating-point arithmetic error remains distinct from its exact algorithm correctness.
