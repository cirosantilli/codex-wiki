Adapted process

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process
OurBigBook.com Words: 313 Articles: 4
A stochastic process X is adapted to a filtration (Ft​) when Xt​ is Ft​-measurable at every time t. Its present value therefore depends only on information already available.
