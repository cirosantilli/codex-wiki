Positive linear map

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 657 Articles: 23
A positive linear map sends every positive semidefinite matrix to a positive semidefinite matrix. Positivity need not persist after tensoring with the identity map on an ancillary system; requiring that stronger property gives a completely positive map.
