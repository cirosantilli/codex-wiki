Recursive half-space clipping of a subdivision curve

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Computer aided geometric design Subdivision curve Subdivision curve interrogation
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For an oriented plane function ℓ, certified extrema over a bounding volume allow a subdivision curve piece to be rejected when maxℓ<0, or wholly retained when minℓ≥0. Otherwise subdivide. At an explicitly chosen rendering tolerance, a sufficiently flat, sufficiently short piece can be replaced by its clipped chord. This is an approximation, not a proof of exact crossing topology. Exact treatment of tangent contacts and small excursions requires root isolation of ℓ(C(t)) and splitting into sign-constant intervals, when the curve representation supports such isolation.
