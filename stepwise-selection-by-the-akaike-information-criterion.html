Stepwise selection by the Akaike information criterion

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Model selection Akaike information criterion
OurBigBook.com Words: 160 Articles: 1
Starting from a fitted model, compare admissible single-term additions and deletions within specified lower and upper scopes, retaining hierarchical lower-order terms when an interaction requires them. Select a change reducing the Akaike information criterion and repeat until no admissible change improves it. For a Gaussian normal linear model with N observations and k fitted mean coefficients, the criterion used by R's linear-model stepwise search is Nlog(RSS/N)+2k, up to constants common to the models. The search is greedy and need not find the best model in the whole scope. Ordinary coefficient tests in the selected model do not account for this model selection. The software arguments are documented in the MASS documentation.
