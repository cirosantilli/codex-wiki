Thermodynamic limit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
The thermodynamic limit sends particle number and volume to infinity at fixed intensive quantities such as number density. Distinct symmetry-breaking phases become sharply defined in this limit.
