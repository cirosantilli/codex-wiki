G2 root system (G2​)

The G2​ root system has six short and six long roots. For a short simple root α1​ and a long simple root α2​, its positive roots are
α1​, α2​, α1​+α2​, 2α1​+α2​, 3α1​+α2​, 3α1​+2α2​.

These are the root data associated with G2.
