Write gC​=L(GC​) and h=L(H). A root is a nonzero weight α∈h∗ of the adjoint action. Its root space is
gα​={Y∈gC​:[Z,Y]=α(Z)Y for all Z∈h}.
(9)
At the group level these weights come from nontrivial characters of the Cartan subgroup acting by conjugation. The Cartan root-space decomposition is
gC​=h⊕⨁α∈Φ​gα​.
(10)
The zero-weight space is h; for the complexification of a compact group the algebra is reductive, with any centre contained in this zero-weight space.
For the product in question,
GC​=SL2​(C)×SL2​(C),gC​=sl2​(C)⊕sl2​(C).
(11)
Take the diagonal Cartan subalgebra generated by H1​=(diag(1,−1),0) and H2​=(0,diag(1,−1)). On Z=xH1​+yH2​, define α1​(Z)=2x and α2​(Z)=2y. The roots are exactly
Φ={α1​,−α1​,α2​,−α2​}​.
(12)
If E=E12​ and F=E21​, their four root spaces are respectively C(E,0), C(F,0), C(0,E), and C(0,F). These four lines and the two-dimensional diagonal subalgebra form the full six-dimensional decomposition. There are no mixed roots: the two direct summands commute. This is the root system A1​×A1​.
