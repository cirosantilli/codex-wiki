OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For the frame fixed by zero mean height and zero first height moment, the variance profile is P(u)=1/105−11u/105+13u2/35−u3/3−u4/3+3u5/5−u6/5. It obeys P(1−u)=P(u), P(0)=1/105 and P(1/2)=1/320. Integrating a white noise curvature field twice, subtracting its affine projection and integrating the squared kernel proves the profile. These free-end conditions must not be confused with physically clamping the ends.
