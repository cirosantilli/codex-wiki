Darcy velocity (u=ϵ(vl​−vs​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Darcy law
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
The Darcy velocity is liquid volume flux relative to the solid skeleton, divided by total cross-sectional area. If porosity is ϵ, liquid velocity is vl​, and skeleton velocity is vs​, then u=ϵ(vl​−vs​). Thus pore-fluid speed relative to the skeleton is u/ϵ, rather than u.
