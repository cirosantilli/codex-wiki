Stone's theorem on one-parameter unitary groups (Ut​=eitA,A=A∗)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis C0-semigroup Strongly continuous unitary group
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
Every strongly continuous unitary group has a unique possibly unbounded self-adjoint operator A as its generator. Its domain consists of the vectors for which the norm limit of (Ut​ξ−ξ)/(it) exists as t→0. Conversely, the Borel functional calculus for a normal operator constructs a strongly continuous unitary group from every self-adjoint A. Smooth averaging supplies a dense generator domain; Laplace integrals of Ut​ and U−t​ prove that A±i are surjective, which establishes self-adjointness.
