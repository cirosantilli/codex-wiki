Sanger sequencing controls

OurBigBook.com Words: 136
The endpoint argument identifies what its scientific controls must interrogate. A known-template comparison tests whether preparation, copying and detection can produce an interpretable ladder. A no-template comparison tests background arising without the intended template. Agreement from an independently primed opposite-strand read tests the proposed nucleotide sequence through its reverse complement. These are distinct analytical checks; none follows merely from seeing a gel band.
An ambiguous ladder should retain its ambiguity. More than one band at a nominal position can reflect mixed templates or unwanted stops; a missing band can reflect failed detection rather than an absent nucleotide. Four clean lanes are an assumption of the ideal example. They do not establish a unique genome, a biological function, or the frequency of a mutation in a population.
