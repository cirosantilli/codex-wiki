Connection 1-form (ωab​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Orthonormal frame in spacetime Orthonormal coframe in spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 25
Connection one-forms are defined by ∇eb​=ωab​⊗ea​. For the Levi-Civita connection they obey Cartan's torsion-free equation dθa+ωab​∧θb=0 and metric compatibility ωab​=−ωba​.
