Polychromatic single-slit diffraction

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Fraunhofer diffraction Single-slit diffraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For mutually incoherent wavelengths, intensities add rather than amplitudes: I(θ)=∫S(λ)sinc2[πasinθ/λ]dλ, where sincq=sinq/q and S includes source strength and detector response. All wavelengths share the central maximum but their minima occur at different angles. A smooth white spectrum therefore has a white centre, wavelength-dependent edge colours, no common first dark minimum and increasingly overlapped, low-contrast outer fringes. A particular hue needs the actual spectrum and colour response.
