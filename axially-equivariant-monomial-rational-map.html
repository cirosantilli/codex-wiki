Axially equivariant monomial rational map (R(z)=zn)

 ... Isolated singularity Classification of isolated singularities Pole Meromorphic function Rational map (complex analysis) Rotational symmetry of a rational map
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
For positive integer n, R(eiαz)=einαR(z) and R(1/z)=1/R(z). For n>1 its angular Jacobian of a rational map vanishes at the poles and has axial symmetry. Its combined rotational group is the group of rotations preserving an axis; for n=1 it enlarges to all domain rotations paired with identical target rotations.
