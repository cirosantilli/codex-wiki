Hydrostatic approximation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Hydrostatic pressure
OurBigBook.com Words: 58 Articles: 1
The hydrostatic approximation neglects vertical acceleration relative to gravity and vertical pressure gradients. It reduces vertical momentum balance to pz​=−ρg.
