Bipyramid

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Polyhedron
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A polyhedron formed by joining two pyramids along a common polygonal base on opposite sides of its plane. The common base is internal; the external boundary consists of the two sets of triangular side faces. A triangular bipyramid has five vertices of a polyhedron, nine edges and six triangular faces.
