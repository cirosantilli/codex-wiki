A transition matrix P defines an irreducible Markov chain if every state is accessible from every other state:
for every i,j∈S, there is n≥0 with (Pn)ij​>0.​
(27)
Equivalently, all states belong to one communicating class. When i=j, a positive number of steps is necessarily used.
