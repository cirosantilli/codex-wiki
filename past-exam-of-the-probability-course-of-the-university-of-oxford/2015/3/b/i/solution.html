Put q=1−p and ξi​=Yi​−Yi−1​. The Markov property and the state-independent transition probabilities show that, conditional on the whole previous history, ξi​ is +1 with probability p and −1 with probability q. Multiplying these conditional probabilities for any finite step sequence proves that the ξi​ are independent and identically distributed random variables, with
Eξi​=p−q=2p−1>0.
(28)
Because Yn​=k+∑i=1n​ξi​, the strong law of large numbers gives
nYn​​=nk​+n1​i=1∑n​ξi​a.s.​μ=2p−1.​
(29)
In particular, Yn​→+∞ almost surely. This stronger pathwise consequence will determine both eventual crossings and the asymptotic first-passage times.
