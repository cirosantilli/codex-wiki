The recurrence criterion by return probabilities proves the recurrent property; by the irreducible Markov chain property established in (i), every state is recurrent. The equivalence of positive recurrence and existence of a stationary distribution for an irreducible Markov chain on a countable state space, together with (iii), makes this chain null recurrent.
For completeness, the zero occupation proportion follows directly by treating returns as a renewal process. Let L1​,L2​,… be successive strictly positive return-cycle lengths to zero.
