Fix any two integers i,j. By Bézout's identity, there are integers u,v with au−bv=j−i. Replacing (u,v) by (u+kb,v+ka) leaves this identity unchanged. Choose k large enough that both entries are nonnegative. A path with u+kb upward steps and v+ka downward steps then takes i to j, with strictly positive probability pu+kbqv+ka. There are no boundary restrictions on this state space.
This applies in both directions, so every pair of states communicates. Hence
The Markov chain is irreducible, with sole communicating class Z.​
