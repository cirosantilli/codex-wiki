Network dual potential (πi​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Minimum-cost flow problem Uncapacitated minimum-cost flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 2
A vertex potential in the dual of an uncapacitated minimum-cost flow yields the edge inequalities πi​−πj​≤cij​. Its objective is ∑i​πi​bi​. Adding a common constant leaves all network reduced costs and the objective unchanged when total net supply is zero. Potentials tight on a spanning tree are fixed up to this common constant.
