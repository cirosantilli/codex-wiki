Egyptian fraction

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Rational number Fraction Unit fraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 197 Articles: 1
An Egyptian fraction expresses a nonnegative rational number as a finite sum of positive unit fractions. A computational representation may permit repeated denominators, while the classical distinct-denominator convention adds a further requirement. The empty sum represents zero. For example, 3/7=1/3+1/11+1/231; each denominator is a positive integer. A representation that permits repetition also has the simple expansion n/d=∑j=1n​1/d for nonnegative integers n and positive d.
