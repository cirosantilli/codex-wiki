Orthonormal coordinates (xi​=(x−o)⋅ei​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Inner product Orthonormal set Orthonormal basis
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
Coordinates relative to an origin o and an orthonormal basis e1​,…,en​ preserve Euclidean distances: if x−y=∑i​di​ei​, then ∥x−y∥2=∑i​di2​. This matters when finding the eccentricity of a conic section in an oblique plane: arbitrary projected coordinates need not preserve the ratio of its semi-major axis to its semi-minor axis.
