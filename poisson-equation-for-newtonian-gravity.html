Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity (∇2Φ=4πGρ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
For mass density ρ, the Newtonian gravitational potential satisfies the Poisson equation ∇2Φ=4πGρ. The gravitational acceleration is −∇Φ. An isolated system also requires the exterior vacuum solution and decay at infinity; solving the interior equation alone does not fix a globally physical field.
