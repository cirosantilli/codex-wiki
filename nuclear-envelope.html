Nuclear envelope

 Home Biology Biological cell Cell nucleus
OurBigBook.com Words: 646 Articles: 4
The nuclear envelope is the double-membrane boundary around a cell nucleus. Regulated passage connects nuclear processes to the surrounding cytoplasm: transcription produces RNA in the cell nucleus, while exported messenger RNA can undergo biological translation on ribosomes outside it. See NHGRI: nuclear membrane.
