Cryogenics

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Temperature
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 10
Cryogenics studies the production and use of very low temperatures. Its experimental challenge is a balance of rates: a refrigerator must remove energy from a cold region faster than unwanted heat transfer supplies it. A low temperature reading alone does not establish a useful cooling capacity. A maintained cold liquid bath is especially valuable because latent heat allows it to absorb heat while its phase fraction changes at nearly fixed temperature and pressure.
Helium liquefaction illustrates why staged cooling and recovery of a returning stream's cooling capacity matter. A counterflow heat exchanger transfers heat from the incoming stream to the colder returning stream without mixing them. This reduces the incoming specific enthalpy before expansion. The heat exchanger recovers cooling already produced; the surrounding refrigerator still requires input work and rejection of heat to a warmer environment.
