Unprojected low levels of the open RNS string (α′M2≤1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Spinning string
OurBigBook.com Words: 115
Before the GSO projection, the Neveu–Schwarz sector has string oscillator levels 0,1/2,1,3/2, with physical multiplicities 1,8,36,128 in ten-dimensional light-cone gauge in string theory. The eight transverse worldsheet Majorana fermions supply eight single ψ−1/2​ states. Level one has eight α−1​ states and (28​)=28 pairs of distinct ψ−1/2​ modes. Level 3/2 has 8 single ψ−3/2​ modes, 8⋅8 mixed boson–fermion modes and (38​)=56 triples. Their squared masses are respectively −1/(2α′),0,1/(2α′),1/α′. In the Ramond sector, the Ramond zero-mode Clifford algebra gives a sixteen-dimensional ground space. At level one, α−1i​ and ψ−1i​ each provide 8⋅16 states, hence multiplicities 16,256 at squared masses 0,1/α′. No half-integer Ramond levels occur. The GSO projection keeps odd NS fermion number and one total Ramond chirality, giving 8 bosons and 8 fermions at zero mass, then 128 of each at the first massive level.
