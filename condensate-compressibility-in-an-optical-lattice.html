Here n is average number density and μ the ground-state chemical potential. This commonly used condensate response has units of inverse energy; thermodynamic isothermal compressibility is instead κ/n. In a weakly interacting repulsive condensate, the normalized one-cell ground state fs​, with average ∣fs​∣2=1, gives μ=E0​(s)+UnI(s)+⋯, I(s)=d−1∫0d​∣fs​∣4dx. Localization raises I and reduces κ at fixed n,U,d. In deep harmonic wells I∝s1/4, hence κ∝s−1/4. This is a ground-band, repulsive-superfluid statement with the held variables specified, not a theorem for arbitrary signs of the interaction or arbitrary excited branches.
