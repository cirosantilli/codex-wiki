Integrated electromotive response of a finite cyclonic event (Ex​=πB0​a4(xcosx−sinx)/x2)

For the ideal magnetic response to a cylindrical cyclonic event, the plane-integrated x-component of u×B at duration t is
Ex​(t)=πB0​∫0∞​[tr2(fg′−gf′)cos(ft)−2rgsin(ft)]dr.
(15)
This follows from (u×B)x​=(rfBz​+gAr​)cosϕ−(g/r)Aϕ​sinϕ and azimuthal integration. If boundary terms vanish, integration by parts gives the equivalent form πB0​∫0∞​r2g′[sin(ft)+tfcos(ft)]dr. For f=g=a2−r2 on r<a, zero outside, the response is
Ex​(t)=πB0​a4x2xcosx−sinx​,x=a2t.
(16)
The value at zero is its continuous limit zero. It is initially −πB0​a6t/3, but changes sign repeatedly at the nonzero roots of tanx=x, with oscillatory envelope of order t−1. The flow's kinetic helicity density is 2f2≥0, showing that the sign of a finite-duration ideal response is not fixed by the instantaneous helicity sign.
