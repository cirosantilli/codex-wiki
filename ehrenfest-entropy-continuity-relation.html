Ehrenfest entropy-continuity relation (dT/dP=TVΔαT​/ΔCP​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Phase transition Phase coexistence
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
If two equilibrium phases have continuous entropy and volume but jumps in second derivatives of Gibbs free energy, differentiate their entropy equality along the transition line. Since dS=(CP​/T)dT−VαT​dP, one obtains the title slope, for nonzero ΔCP​. Before imposing equal volume, the numerator is TΔ(VαT​). Equality of Gibbs free energies with equal entropy implies equal volume along a differentiable coexistence curve on which pressure varies. A constant-pressure curve or zero numerator and denominator needs separate analysis. This entropy-continuity condition is one Ehrenfest relation; volume continuity gives another independent relation.
