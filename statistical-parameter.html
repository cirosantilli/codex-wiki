Statistical parameter (θ∈Θ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model
OurBigBook.com Words: 127 Articles: 2
A statistical parameter indexes a family of probability distributions in a statistical model. It is fixed in a frequentist sampling model; a prior distribution makes it random for Bayesian statistics. Different statistical parameter values should produce different observable distributions when identifiability is claimed.
