Matching one specified birthday (1−(1−1/m)n−1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Birthday problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 157
In a group of total size n≥1, designate one member and ask whether any of the other n−1 labels equals that member's label. Conditional on the designated label, all the other draws avoid it with probability 1−1/m. Independence gives
P(a match with the designated member)=1−(1−m1​)n−1.​
(9)
For n=23, this is approximately 5.86%, while the probability of some matching pair is approximately 50.73%. The designated-member question examines 22 pairs; the any-pair question examines all 253. A repeated label elsewhere in the group does not require a match with its designated member.
