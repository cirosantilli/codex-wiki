Predictable-coefficient localization of a martingale transform (σj​=inf{t≥0:∣Kt+1​∣>j})

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Predictable process Martingale transform
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For a finite-valued predictable process K, this is a stopping time and increases to infinity. The stopped martingale transform uses coefficients Ks​1{s≤σj​}​, which are predictable and bounded by j. Thus a transform of a discrete-time martingale is a local martingale without a global boundedness assumption. The stop is before, not after, the first large coefficient is used.
