Pseudo-arclength continuation (F(z)=0,tkT​(z−zk​)−h=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Continuation method
OurBigBook.com Words: 396 Articles: 1
Pseudo-arclength continuation allows both the state and the parameter to change. Write z=(u,λ) and choose a unit vector tk​ in the kernel of the full Jacobian matrix DF(zk​). A continuation predictor-corrector step seeks the next solution on a hyperplane perpendicular to tk​, a distance h>0 along the old tangent vector. This adds one equation to F(z)=0 and makes the correction a square system. The constraint approximates arc length; h is the displacement projected onto the old tangent vector, rather than the exact curved distance.[ref]
