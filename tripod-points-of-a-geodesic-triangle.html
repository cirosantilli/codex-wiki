Tripod points of a geodesic triangle

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Quasi-isometry Hyperbolic metric space
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
For a geodesic triangle with side lengths a,b,c, its tripod points divide the three sides into lengths
2a+b−c​,2b+c−a​,2c+a−b​.
(25)
In a δ-hyperbolic metric space the three tripod points have pairwise distance at most 4δ.
