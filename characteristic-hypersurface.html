Characteristic hypersurface

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Quasilinear partial differential equation Principal symbol of a partial differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 3
A hypersurface Σ={ϕ=0} is characteristic for a differential equation at x∈Σ when its principal symbol vanishes on the conormal dϕ(x):
p(x,dϕ(x))=0.
(753)
It is non-characteristic where this quantity is nonzero.
