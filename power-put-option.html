Power put option (gn​(S)=(K−S)+n​)

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fundamental theorem of asset pricing Contingent claim Put option
OurBigBook.com Words: 336 Articles: 2
A power put has financial payoff (K−S)n below the strike price K and zero at or above it, where n is a positive integer. For n=1 this is the ordinary put option. For every n≥1 the payoff is decreasing and is a convex function of the stock price. A European option permits exercise only at expiry; a perpetual power put option permits exercise at any finite time without a fixed expiry.
