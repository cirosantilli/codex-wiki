Moment of inertia of a uniform square plate (I=Ms2/6)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics Moment of inertia
OurBigBook.com Words: 180 Articles: 2
A uniform square of side s has the displayed moment of inertia about the perpendicular axis through its centre. Integrating x2+y2 over [−s/2,s/2]2 gives s4/6; multiplying by the surface mass density M/s2 gives the result. A square prism of any uniform thickness has the same axial formula when expressed in terms of its total mass. If the side is denoted 2a instead, the result is 2Ma2/3, so the side-length convention must be specified before substituting numerical data into a normal mode calculation.
