Burgers' equation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 600 Articles: 13
The one-dimensional equation ut​+uux​=νuxx​ combines nonlinear transport with diffusion. Positive ν gives the viscous Burgers equation; setting ν=0 gives the Inviscid Burgers equation.
