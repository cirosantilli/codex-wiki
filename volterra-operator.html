Volterra operator ((Af)(x)=∫0x​f(t)dt)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Integral operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 387 Articles: 6
The Volterra integration operator on L2([0,1]) is compact and injective, but its inverse is differentiation and is unbounded. Its singular values 2/((2n−1)π) tend to zero, making recovery of a function from noisy indefinite-integral data an ill-posed inverse problem.
