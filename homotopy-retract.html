Homotopy retract (ri=idA​, ir≃idX​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Homotopy equivalence
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
In the Oxford course's convention, a homotopy retract is a retraction r:X→A such that the inclusion map i:A↪X also satisfies ir≃idX​. It gives a homotopy equivalence between A and X. Unlike the deformation retraction convention used here, the homotopy need not fix A at intermediate times. [ref]
