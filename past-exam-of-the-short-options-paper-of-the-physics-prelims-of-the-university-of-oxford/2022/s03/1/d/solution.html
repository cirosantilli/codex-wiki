For a monochromatic photon of vacuum wavelength λ, a plate of thickness d replaces air by glass and adds phase ϕ=2π(n−1)d/λ. Neglect losses and reflections, or assume antireflection coatings. Between the first beam splitter and mirror 1, the affected arm is the second basis component, so Dϕ​=diag(1,eiϕ). The successive quantum states, omitting common propagation phases, are
(01​)​B​2​1​(11​)Dϕ​​2​1​(eiϕ1​)M​−2​1​(1eiϕ​)B​−21​(eiϕ+1eiϕ−1​).​
(42)
The first output component feeds detector 2 and the second feeds detector 1, giving
p2​=sin2(ϕ/2),p1​=cos2(ϕ/2).​
(43)
The imbalance gives p1​−p2​=cosϕ. Thus the measured probabilities determine only ϕ up to ϕ↦±ϕ+2πm, and hence only a corresponding family of thicknesses. There is no unique absolute thickness from this one measurement. The zero-plate limit recovers p1​=1, p2​=0.
