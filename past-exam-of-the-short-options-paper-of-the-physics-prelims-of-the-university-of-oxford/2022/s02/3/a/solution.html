Use ρr​,ρm​ as energy per volume, as specified in the prose, and let R have dimensions of length so that the positive curvature magnitude k is dimensionless. The Friedmann equation for spatial curvature −k is
H2=(RR˙​)2=3c28πG​(ρr​+ρm​)+R2kc2​+3Λc2​.​

For an energy density the cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation is ρ˙​+3H(ρ+P)=0. Nonrelativistic matter has negligible pressure and radiation has Pr​=ρr​/3. Thus the four contributions to H2 scale respectively as
R−4,R−3,R−2,R0.​
(28)
For nonzero positive densities and Λ>0, the usual possible sequence is radiation domination, matter domination, curvature domination, then cosmological-constant domination. A distinct era for each is not guaranteed: coefficients may cause curvature to be overtaken by the cosmological constant before it ever dominates. If Λ=0, curvature dominates asymptotically; negative Λ does not give a late positive vacuum-dominated expansion.
The printed auxiliary relation Pr​=ρr​c2/3 uses mass-density notation, in conflict with the stated energy-density definition. It is consistent if ρr​ there is understood as energy density divided by c2; in that convention the density term in the Friedmann equation is 8πGρ/3. The two conventions must not be mixed.
