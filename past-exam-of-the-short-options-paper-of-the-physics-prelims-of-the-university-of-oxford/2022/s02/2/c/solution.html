The same gas-pressure stellar homology estimates give T∝μM/R∗​ and ρ∝M/R∗3​. With Kramers' opacity law κ∝ρT−7/2, radiative diffusion gives
L∝κρR∗​T4​∝ρ2R∗​T15/2​∝μ15/2M11/2R∗−1/2​.
(25)
Consequently the claimed exponent five additionally requires the approximate low-mass main-sequence mass-radius relation R∗​∝M and fixed composition:
R∗​∝M⟹L∝M5.​
(26)
The supplied opacity and pressure assumptions alone do not establish that mass-radius relation. For example, a homologous fixed-radius family obeys those scaling assumptions but gives L∝M11/2, not M5. More generally R∗​∝Mq gives L∝M(11−q)/2. Very low-mass stars can be convective, where this radiative model ceases to apply.
