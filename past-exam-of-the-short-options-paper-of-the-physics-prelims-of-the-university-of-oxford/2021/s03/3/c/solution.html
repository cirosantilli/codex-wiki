Applying the Hamiltonian operator to ψn​ gives ψn′′​=−n2ψn​, and therefore
En​=2Iℏ2n2​=2mr2ℏ2n2​,n∈Z.​
(71)
The ground state n=0 has zero kinetic energy. Every positive energy level is of quantum degeneracy two, because n and −n correspond to opposite angular momenta with equal squared magnitude.
The confinement scale suggests a tangential momentum scale ℏ/r and thus an excitation energy scale ℏ2/(mr2), consistent with the Heisenberg uncertainty principle. More precisely the de Broglie wavelength must fit an integer number of times around the circumference: 2πr=∣n∣λ, so ∣p∣=h/λ=∣n∣ℏ/r. This explains both the r−2 dependence and the n2 sequence.
One must not infer a nonzero ground-state energy by applying ΔϕΔL≥ℏ/2 naively. Angle is periodic and is not a globally canonical position operator on the circle. The uniform n=0 wavefunction, with ΔL=0, is a concrete counterexample to that naive inequality. The ordinary Cartesian Heisenberg uncertainty principle is not violated by the zero-angular momentum state.
