Conformational isomerism

 Home Chemistry Stereochemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 136 Articles: 8
Conformational isomerism concerns spatial arrangements interconverted by internal motion, such as turning about a single chemical bond. Moving the entire molecule or applying a rotation changes its position or viewing direction, not its internal arrangement.
