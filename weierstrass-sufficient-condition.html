Weierstrass sufficient condition

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Calculus of variations Weierstrass excess function Strengthened Weierstrass condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
Under the usual C2 integrand regularity, an admissible C1 curve satisfying the weak Euler-Lagrange equation, a coercively positive second variation, and the strengthened Weierstrass condition is a strong local minimizer of a variational integral. Strict excess away from the reference slope gives strict minimality. Coercive positivity means δ2I(u)[η,η]≥c∥η∥W1,22​ for every zero-trace variation and some c>0.
The second variation provides a neighboring field of extremals with slope p(x,y). The associated endpoint-invariant Hilbert integral yields I(v)−I(u)=∫E(x,v,p(x,v),v′)dx for uniformly close admissible v. The tube condition makes this integral nonnegative even when v′ is large, explaining the passage from weak to strong minimality.
