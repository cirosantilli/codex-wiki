Roots of unity at the tame ramification boundary

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Roots of unity in a p-adic field Valuation of a primitive p-power root of unity
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For odd p, both possibilities occur at v(p)=p−1: Qp​(ζp​) contains p-torsion, while Qp​(p−1p​) does not. Both extensions are totally ramified of degree p−1 by the Eisenstein criterion. In the second field, if ζp​=1+uπ with πp−1=p, the cyclotomic equation reduced after division by p would give 1+uˉp−1=0 in Fp​, contradicting uˉp−1=1. For p=2, v(2)=1 always permits the root −1, so the torsion exponent is exactly one and the zero-exponent alternative is impossible.
