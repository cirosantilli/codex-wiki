Conic support of an oscillatory amplitude (Ca​⊂X×Sk−1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Oscillatory integral Amplitude of an oscillatory integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 294 Articles: 3
A closed conic enlargement of an oscillatory integral amplitude support is obtained by taking the closure of its frequency-direction projection in X×Sk−1 and lifting that set radially to nonzero frequencies. It records limiting spatial locations and frequency directions, including limits reached only at arbitrarily large frequency. Ordinary support in X×Rk need not record those limits. This distinction is necessary in singular support bounds for an oscillatory integral.
