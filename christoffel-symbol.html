Christoffel symbol (Γijk​)

In a coordinate frame, the Levi-Civita connection is determined by
∇∂i​​∂j​=Γijk​∂k​.
(454)
The coefficients Γijk​ are the Christoffel symbols. They are not the components of a tensor because a coordinate change contributes second derivatives.
