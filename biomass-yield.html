Biomass yield (Y=biomass produced/nutrient consumed)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Chemostat
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
The biomass yield Y converts nutrient consumption into biomass production: consuming nutrient mass m produces dry biomass mass Ym under the assumed constant conversion. For Y=0.5g biomass/(g nutrient), consumption of 8g of nutrient produces 4g of dry biomass. Its numerical value depends on the measurement conventions; it is not a fraction of all feed mass converted into cells. With no separate maintenance consumption, biomass production μ(s)x requires nutrient consumption μ(s)x/Y in the chemostat balance.
