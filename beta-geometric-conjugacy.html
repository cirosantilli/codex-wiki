Beta-geometric conjugacy (p∣D∼Beta(a+d,b+F))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Bayesian posterior Beta-binomial conjugacy
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
For independent geometric random variables with success probability p, observing d terminal events and F preceding failed Bernoulli trials gives likelihood function proportional to pd(1−p)F. Multiplication by a beta distribution prior Beta(a,b) gives the posterior distribution Beta(a+d,b+F). With complete waiting times x1​,…,xn​, take d=n and F=∑i​(xi​−1).
A waiting time that is right-censored after c failed trials contributes (1−p)c: increase F by c without increasing d. This requires the censoring mechanism to be ignorable, as with an externally fixed observation horizon. Counting a censored waiting time as an observed event changes the likelihood function and biases the update.
