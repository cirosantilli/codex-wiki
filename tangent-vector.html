Tangent vector

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 453 Articles: 11
A tangent vector to a smooth curve is the derivative of a parametrization of that curve. Tangent vectors to a surface form its tangent plane.
