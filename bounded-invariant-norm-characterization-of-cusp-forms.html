Bounded invariant norm characterization of cusp forms (f∈Sk​(Γ)⟺supτ∈H​∣f(τ)∣(Imτ)k/2<∞)

OurBigBook.com Words: 88
For k>0, a meromorphic function satisfying the modular transformation law is a cusp form exactly when its invariant norm is bounded. The bound first excludes interior poles. At each cusp a genuine periodic slash translate is bounded by Cy−k/2, which tends to zero. Its punctured-disk expression therefore has a removable singularity with zero value. Conversely cusp Fourier expansions decay exponentially, and finitely many compact core regions control the rest. Positive weight is essential: a nonzero constant in weight zero satisfies the bound but is not a cusp form.
