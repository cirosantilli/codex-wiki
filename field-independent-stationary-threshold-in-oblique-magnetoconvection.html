Field-independent stationary threshold in oblique magnetoconvection (α=arctan2​,kc​=−1/2​,Rc​=27/4)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Magnetoconvection Oblique-field plane-wave magnetoconvection
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
At inclination α=arctan2​ and vertical wavenumber one, the nonmagnetic minimizing wavenumber k=−1/2​ also satisfies c⋅k=0. The stationary threshold is the sum of F(k)≥27/4 and a nonnegative magnetic penalty that vanishes there. Therefore kc​=−1/2​ and Rc​=27/4 for every Q≥0. The selected disturbance is constant along the imposed field, so the linear resistive induction equation produces no magnetic perturbation and there is no field-line tension penalty.
