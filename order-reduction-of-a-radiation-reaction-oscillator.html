Order reduction of a radiation-reaction oscillator

For ∣εω∣≪1, use the zeroth-order equation x¨=f−x inside the small third-derivative term, giving
x¨+εx˙+x=f+εf˙​.
(3)
For positive ε this reduced equation has a stable retarded response with no high-frequency runaway. Its response Gred​=(1−iεω)/(1−ω2−iεω) agrees with the unreduced oscillator to first order at fixed nonresonant frequency. Order reduction deliberately removes the singular fast mode outside the assumed low-frequency regime; it is an approximation rather than an exact alternative boundary condition for the third-order equation.
