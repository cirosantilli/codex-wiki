Elliptic cylinder (u2/a2+v2/b2=1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Quadric surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For a,b>0, an elliptic cylinder is a quadric surface with the displayed equation in suitable Cartesian coordinates, independent of the third coordinate. It is formed by translating an ellipse parallel to a line perpendicular to its plane. The generator direction is the zero-eigenvalue direction of its quadratic part, while the transverse semiaxes are a,b. It is circular if a=b.
