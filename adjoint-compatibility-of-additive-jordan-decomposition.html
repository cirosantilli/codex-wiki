Adjoint compatibility of additive Jordan decomposition ((adX)s​=adXs​,(adX)n​=adXn​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Jordan normal form Jordan–Chevalley decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 1
The commutator action of Xs​ is diagonalizable on endomorphisms: on Hom(Vλ​,Vμ​) it acts by μ−λ. The action of Xn​ is nilpotent by nilpotence of commutation by a nilpotent endomorphism. The two actions commute, so uniqueness of the Additive Jordan decomposition identifies them as the parts of adX.
