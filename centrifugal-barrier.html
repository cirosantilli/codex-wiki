Centrifugal barrier (Veff​(r)=Φ(r)+L2/(2r2))

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Effective potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For a central gravitational potential and nonzero specific angular momentum L, the positive L2/(2r2) contribution to the radial effective potential can prevent the orbit reaching the centre at finite energy. It dominates the attractive −μ/r term at small r in a Kepler orbit. A more singular attractive term may overcome it: for Φ=−μ/r−μa/r2, the net inverse-square coefficient is (L2−2μa)/2, so a central barrier exists only when that coefficient is positive.
