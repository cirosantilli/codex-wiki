Adic completion of a module (M=lim​n​M/InM)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Ring Topological ring Adic topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 193 Articles: 2
The adic completion is the inverse limit of the quotient modules M/InM. An element is a compatible family of residue classes at every order. For R=k[t] and M=V⊗k​k[t], this gives the formal power series module V[[t]]. The canonical map from M need not be injective in general: its kernel is ⋂n​InM. It is injective for this polynomial example because a nonzero polynomial has finite degree. Successive changes of coordinates in formal rigidity from vanishing second Hochschild cohomology converge in this completion because the order-r change is the identity modulo tr.
