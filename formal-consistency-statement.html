Formal consistency statement (Con(T))

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order theory Consistent first-order theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 419 Articles: 8
After fixing a Gödel numbering, the formal consistency statement Con(T) asserts that no natural number codes a T-proof of a contradiction. For a recursively axiomatized theory, this is an arithmetical sentence expressible inside any sufficiently strong base theory.
