Use {QαA​,QβB​}=ϵαβ​ZAB. Unitary skew-diagonalization of an antisymmetric matrix gives two-by-two blocks with entries Zr​=2zr​. At rest, the paired supercharges have anticommutators 2(M±∣zr​∣). The squared norms of an operator and its adjoint sum to the expectation of their anticommutator, so positive norm requires M≥∣zr​∣ for every block. A convention with 2Z in the algebra instead writes M≥max∣Zr​∣.
