DLVO theory

 Home Chemistry Mixture Colloid
OurBigBook.com Words: 342 Articles: 5
DLVO theory combines screened electrostatic repulsion from overlapping electric double layers with attractive Van der Waals forces to model colloid stability. A repulsive barrier can inhibit aggregation even when a deep short-distance attractive minimum exists. Increasing electrolyte concentration reduces the screening length and can lower that barrier. Molecular surface forces and polymer-mediated interactions require additions to this description.
