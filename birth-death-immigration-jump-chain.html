Here 0<p<1, θ>0, and the upward step is either a birth or immigration, the latter having conditional probability θ/(k+θ). At zero immigration occurs with probability one. Put q=1−p, a=θ/p and r=p/q. The stationary weights of a reflected birth-death chain are
wk​=θk+θ​Γ(a)k!Γ(k+a)​rk.
(29)
Their successive ratio tends to r, so they are summable for p<1/2. For p≥1/2, uk​>1/2>dk+1​ makes the weights strictly increasing, ruling out a stationary distribution. Thus positive recurrence holds exactly for p<1/2. In that case the binomial series gives ∑k​wk​=2(1−r)−a−1, and
πk​=2θΓ(a)(1−r)a+1​k!(k+θ)Γ(k+a)​rk.
(30)
Every step reverses parity and a two-step return has positive probability, so the period of a state in a Markov chain is two. Starting at zero, P(X2n​=0)→2π0​=(1−r)a+1, whereas P(X2n+1​=0)=0.
