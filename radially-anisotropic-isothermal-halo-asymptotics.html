Radially anisotropic isothermal halo asymptotics (ρ∼σ2/[4πGr2log(r/r∗​)])

OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For a self-consistent Gaussian angular-momentum suppression in a stellar distribution, put y=[ψ(r)−ψ(0)]/σ2 and s=log(r/ra​). At large radius the Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity reduces to yss​+ys​∼−λey, where λ=4πGρc​ra2​/σ2. The asymptotic balance ys​∼−1/s gives ey∼1/(λs), the displayed density, and circular speed vc2​∼σ2/log(r/r∗​). A sufficiently small regular core can have an intermediate isotropic singular isothermal sphere envelope with ρ≃σ2/(2πGr2) and vc2​≃2σ2. At the actual regular centre, however, ρ→ρc​ and vc​∝r; the intermediate power law must not be mistaken for a central limit.
