Space-filling SLE above parameter eight

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Schramm–Loewner evolution Phase classification of the SLE trace
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
For κ>8, the Loewner trace visits every point of the complex upper half-plane almost surely. Choose ρ=κ−8 in the SLE interior-point martingale to obtain Mt​=Υt(κ−8)/8​St−(κ−8)/κ​. The maximal inequality for a nonnegative supermartingale bounds its running supremum. If a fixed interior ball were avoided, its center would have Υt​≥c1​>0 before swallowing, forcing St​≥c2​>0. But the Chordal Loewner equation gives dYt2​/dt=−4St2​, forcing a finite lifetime with Yt​→0, and dlogΥt​/dlogYt​=2St2​ then forces Υt​→0, a contradiction. Countably many rational balls give a dense trace; continuity and Transience of chordal SLE make its image relatively closed, so it is all of the complex upper half-plane. This argument does not cover the critical parameter eight.
