Shock wave

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation Scalar conservation law
OurBigBook.com Words: 160 Articles: 2
A shock wave is a moving discontinuity of a weak solution to a conservation law. Its speed is fixed by the Rankine-Hugoniot condition, while an entropy condition selects the physically admissible direction of compression.
