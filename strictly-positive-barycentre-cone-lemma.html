Strictly positive barycentre cone lemma ({E[νX]:ν>0, E[ν∥X∥]<∞}=riK)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex set Convex cone
OurBigBook.com Words: 215 Articles: 1
For a finite-dimensional random vector X, let K be the closed cone generated by the support of a probability distribution of X. Every integrable strictly positively weighted barycentre lies in the relative interior of K: every nonzero supporting functional on its span is positive on a set of positive probability. Conversely, put w=(1+∥X∥)−1 and D={E[wfX]:0≤f∈L∞}. Its dual cone equals that of K, so D=K and riK⊆D. For z∈riK, subtract a sufficiently small positive multiple of E[wX] and represent the remainder using D; adding the constant positive weight back gives ν=w(f+ε)>0.
