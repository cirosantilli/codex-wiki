Local principal series of GL2 (V(χ)=IndBGL2​(F)​χ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Induced representation Unnormalized parabolic induction
OurBigBook.com Words: 169 Articles: 2
The local principal series of GL2 induces a smooth linear character of its diagonal torus, inflated to its upper-triangular subgroup. Its unnormalized Jacquet module has two one-dimensional composition factors, χ and χwδ−1, where δ(diag(a,d))=∣d/a∣. When these coincide, the module is a nonsplit self-extension, so one must not count the linear character twice as two independent quotients.
