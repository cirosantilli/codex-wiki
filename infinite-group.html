Infinite group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group
OurBigBook.com Words: 147 Articles: 2
An infinite group is a group with infinitely many elements. An element of infinite order proves that its group is infinite, but the converse need not hold: an infinite group can have all elements of finite order.
