Cotangent roots of a powered Möbius transformation (zk​=−icot[(π/4+πk)/n])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Projective linear group Möbius transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
For a nonzero integer n, the equation ((z+1)/(z−1))n=i has ∣n∣ distinct roots given by the displayed formula for 0≤k<∣n∣. Put w=(z+1)/(z−1); its roots have unit modulus and angles (π/2+2πk)/n. The inverse map is (w+1)/(w−1)=−icot(arg(w)/2), proving that all these roots are purely imaginary. The value w=1 never occurs. For n=0 there are no solutions; for negative n the original map's zero at z=−1 is excluded as well as its pole at z=1.
