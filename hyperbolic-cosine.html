Hyperbolic cosine (cosh)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Exponential function Hyperbolic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 586 Articles: 4
The hyperbolic cosine is the even function
coshx=2ex+e−x​.
(40)
It has derivative sinhx and second derivative coshx>0, so it is a strictly convex function. Its unique global minimum is cosh0=1, and its image is [1,∞). For example, cosh(−1)=cosh1≈1.543081. On the unit hyperbola, the hyperbolic cosine is the horizontal coordinate; as a graph against x, it is a symmetric bowl.
Its Taylor series begins
coshx=1+2!x2​+4!x4​+⋯.
(41)
The positive fourth-order term explains why the bowl resembles a parabola near its minimum yet differs from one away from it. Restricting to x≥0 gives the inverse hyperbolic cosine.
