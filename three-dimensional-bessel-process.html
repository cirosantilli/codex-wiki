Three-dimensional Bessel process (dRt​=dBt​+Rt−1​dt)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Bessel process
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 1
The radial part of three-dimensional Brownian motion started away from the origin is a Bessel process of dimension three. The radial Laplacian is Δ∣x∣=2/∣x∣, so the Itô formula gives the displayed equation. The radial martingale has unit quadratic variation rate and is Brownian motion by the Lévy characterization of Brownian motion. The reciprocal 1/R is a local martingale. Stopping it on (ε,M) gives Px​(Tε​<TM​)=ε(M−x)/(x(M−ε)). Letting ε↓0 shows that zero is not hit.
