Hadronization

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 81 Articles: 1
Hadronization is the conversion of colored quarks and gluons into color-neutral hadrons. It affects exclusive final-state distributions even when an inclusive observable can be approximated through a short-distance partonic calculation. A tree-level partonic decay width does not by itself model this conversion.
