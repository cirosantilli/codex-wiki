Linear congruential generator (Sn+1​=(aSn​+c)modM)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Monte Carlo method Pseudorandom number generator
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 1
Choose integers M>1, a, c and a seed S0​. Iterate Sn+1​=(aSn​+c)modM and return Un​=Sn​/M. This gives a deterministic finite-state pseudorandom number generator with output in [0,1). A nonzero increment gives a mixed generator; c=0 gives a multiplicative generator. Its period is at most M, but even a full period does not guarantee good multidimensional uniformity or statistical independence.
