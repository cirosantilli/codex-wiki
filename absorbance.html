Absorbance (A)

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 6
Absorbance is the dimensionless quantity A=log10​(P0​/P), where P0​ and P are reference and transmitted radiant powers at the chosen wavelength. For example, A=1 means one tenth of the reference power is transmitted. Under the Beer-Lambert law, absorbance can determine an absorbing species' concentration; attenuation by cloudiness or unrelated colored solutes needs separate controls. [ref]
