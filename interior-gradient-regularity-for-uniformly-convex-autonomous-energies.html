Interior gradient regularity for uniformly convex autonomous energies (λI≤D2F≤MI⟹u∈Cloc1,β​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Convex function Strongly convex function Uniformly convex variational integrand
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
For scalar minimizers of ∫F(Du) with F∈C2(Rn) and globally bounded uniformly positive Hessian matrix, no higher differentiability of F is needed for interior gradient Hölder continuity. The weak Euler-Lagrange equation is divDF(Du)=0. Difference quotients solve uniformly elliptic equations with coefficient matrices obtained by averaging D2F along gradient segments. The Caccioppoli inequality and difference quotient characterization give u∈Hloc2​. The Sobolev chain rule then gives div(D2F(Du)D(Dk​u))=0. The De Giorgi-Nash-Moser theorem makes each derivative Hölder continuous, with exponent depending only on dimension and the ellipticity ratio. This scalar conclusion should not be assumed for vector-valued elliptic systems.
