Endomorphism ring of an elliptic curve (End(E))

 ... Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Isogeny of elliptic curves
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
The endomorphism ring of an elliptic curve consists of its self-isogenies together with the zero map, with pointwise addition and composition as multiplication.
