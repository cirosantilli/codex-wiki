Three-ball (B3)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological manifold 3-manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A three-ball is a three-manifold homeomorphic to the closed unit ball in R3. Its boundary is a two-sphere. Attaching a three-ball to a spherical boundary component is called capping that boundary; marked endpoints may be joined by a straight arc inside the cap.
