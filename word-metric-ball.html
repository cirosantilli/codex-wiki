Word metric ball (BS​(g,r))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Cayley graph Word metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 282 Articles: 1
The closed word metric ball of radius r≥0 centred at a group element g is
BS​(g,r)={h∈G:dS​(g,h)≤r}=g{u∈G:∣u∣S​≤r}.
(22)
This is a closed ball for the word metric. A finite generating set of a group gives a finite word metric ball, because at most ∑k=0⌊r⌋​∣S∣k words over an alphabet can represent its elements. Different words over an alphabet may represent the same element, so this is an upper bound. Word metric balls in the integer lattice give exact counts for two choices of moves.
