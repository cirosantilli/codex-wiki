Freiling axiom of symmetry (A<ω1​​(R))

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cardinal number Cardinal arithmetic Continuum hypothesis
OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 1
Every function f:R→[R]<ω1​ has reals x,y with x∈/f(y) and y∈/f(x). The values are countable subsets. Requiring distinct witnesses is equivalent, by first adjoining x to f(x). The Freiling theorem identifies this axiom with the negation of the Continuum hypothesis in ZFC.
