Protein structure

 Home Biology Protein
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 53
Protein structure describes both the chain's chemical organization and its spatial arrangement. Four levels answer different questions: protein primary structure gives the order; protein secondary structure gives recurring local patterns; protein tertiary structure gives one chain's overall arrangement; protein quaternary structure gives the assembly of multiple chains. These are levels of description, not four mandatory stages that every protein must pass through. See Clark, Douglas and Choi: four levels of protein structure.
