Random coding

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Coding theory Channel coding Channel code
OurBigBook.com Words: 110
Random coding constructs a channel code by sampling its codewords from a specified probability distribution and bounding the expected decoding error over that ensemble. If the expected average error is at most ϵ, at least one deterministic codebook has average error at most ϵ; otherwise every book would exceed the expectation bound. This is the probabilistic method applied to communication codes.
For independently uniform binary codewords, a fixed competing word falls in any received word's radius-r Hamming ball with probability 2−n∑j=0r​(jn​). The union bound over competing messages, together with a bound on excessive channel noise, yields the basic random-coding error estimate. Expurgation of a codebook can then convert average-error control to maximal decoding error probability with negligible loss of asymptotic rate.
