Rank-two complex vector bundles over the four-sphere (VectC2​(S4)≅Z)

 ... Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Tautological bundle Complex tautological bundle on a Grassmannian Classification of complex vector bundles by a Grassmannian
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
The contractible universal frame bundle gives π4​(BU2​)≅π3​(U2​) and simple connectedness of BU2​. The determinant fibration SU2​→U2​→S1 identifies π3​(U2​) with π3​(SU2​). Since SU2​≅S3, this is Z. Hence the classification of complex vector bundles by a Grassmannian gives the displayed bijection of isomorphism classes; fixed-rank classes do not acquire a group operation from Whitney sum.
