Rectangular function (rect(t)=1∣t∣<1/2​)

 Home Electrical engineering Signal processing Signal
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
The rectangular function has value one on ∣t∣<1/2 and zero on ∣t∣>1/2. Endpoint values are convention-dependent and do not affect its ordinary integrals or Fourier transform. Shifts, amplitude changes and time rescaling produce a rectangular pulse of arbitrary height, center and duration. With f​(k)=∫f(t)e−iktdt, for a>0 the width-2a pulse f(t)=1∣t∣<a​ has Fourier transform 2sin(ak)/k, with value 2a at zero by a limit. Its convolution with itself is a rescaled triangular function, (2a−∣t∣) on ∣t∣<2a and zero outside, because the convolution measures the overlap length of two intervals.
