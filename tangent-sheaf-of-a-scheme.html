Tangent sheaf of a scheme (TX/S​=Hom(ΩX/S1​,OX​))

 ... Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Kähler differential Sheaf of relative Kähler differentials
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The relative tangent sheaf is the dual of a sheaf of Kähler differentials. Its sections are derivations of the structure sheaf that kill base scalars. For a smooth morphism it is locally free and describes first-order deformations of sections into the scheme.
