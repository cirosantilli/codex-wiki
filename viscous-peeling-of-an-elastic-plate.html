Viscous peeling of an elastic plate (Up​∼Bhf3​/(μlp5​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Elastic plate
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
A fluid advancing under an elastic plate can be resisted mainly by lubrication theory flow near its front. For front gap hf​ and peeling length lp​, the elastic pressure gradient is of order Bhf​/lp5​, while the gap volume flux is of order Bhf4​/(μlp5​). Mass balance therefore gives peeling speed Up​∼Bhf3​/(μlp5​).
