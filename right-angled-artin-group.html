Right-angled Artin group (AΓ​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 4
For a graph Γ, the right-angled Artin group is
AΓ​=⟨v∈V(Γ)​[v,w]=1 whenever {v,w}∈E(Γ)⟩.
