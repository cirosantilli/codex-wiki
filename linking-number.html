Linking number (lk(K1​,K2​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Link
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The linking number of two disjoint oriented closed curves in S3 is the signed intersection number of one curve with a Seifert surface for the other. In an oriented diagram it is half the signed sum of crossings between the two components.
