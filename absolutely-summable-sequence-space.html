Absolutely summable sequence space (ℓ1={x:∑n​∣xn​∣<∞})

The Banach space ℓ1 consists of absolutely summable scalar sequences, with norm ∥x∥1​=∑n​∣xn​∣. Its continuous dual is ℓ∞, under the pairing ∑n​xn​yn​. Finitely supported sequences are norm dense, which identifies a linear functional from its values on coordinate vectors. The space of sequences converging to zero inside this dual is a norming subspace of infinite codimension.
