Travelling salesman problem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 15
Given finitely many locations and pairwise travel costs, find a cyclic visiting order minimizing the sum of its costs. For Euclidean distance this becomes the Euclidean travelling salesman tour problem.
