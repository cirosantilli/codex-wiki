Electromagnetic coupling of a sine-Gordon topological current (jμ=(θx​,−θt​),Q=∫θx​dx)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Real scalar field Sine-Gordon theory Sine-Gordon kink
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The identically conserved topological current j couples to an external electromagnetic gauge potential through −Aμ​jμ=−A0​θx​+A1​θt​. A gauge transformation changes this coupling only by a divergence. The Euler-Lagrange field equation becomes θtt​−θxx​+sinθ+E=0, with E=∂t​A1​−∂x​A0​. A unit Sine-Gordon kink has unnormalized charge Q=2π and mass 8. For spatially uniform forcing, its field momentum balance is P˙=QE when the boundary stress contributions cancel.
