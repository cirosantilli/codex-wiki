Equivariant Fredholm index (indG​T=[kerT]−[cokerT])

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Lower norm of an operator Fredholm operator Fredholm index
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
An equivariant Fredholm operator between unitary representations of a compact group has finite-dimensional invariant kernel and cokernel. Their formal difference is its index in the representation ring of a compact group. If domain and target decompose into irreducibles with finite multiplicities mπ​,mπ′​, Schur's lemma makes each intertwining block a matrix on the multiplicity spaces. Its rank cancels between kernel and cokernel, giving indG​T=∑π​(mπ​−mπ′​)[π], with finitely many nonzero terms.
