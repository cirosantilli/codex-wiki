Thin-shell approximation for a spherical blast wave (α=1/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Compressible flow Self-similar blast wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
Let a strong shock wave of radius R and speed V sweep a uniform mass density ρ0​ into a shell of mass 4πρ0​R3/3 and speed 2V/(γ+1). If the cavity pressure is α times the immediate post-shock pressure, shell momentum balance gives RV˙=3(α−1)V2. Constant total energy gives R3V2=constant and hence α=1/2. Including shell internal and kinetic energy, and treating the cavity volume as 4πR3/3 to thin-shell accuracy, yields R=ξ(E/ρ0​)1/5t2/5 with ξ5=75(γ−1)(γ+1)2/[16π(5γ−3)]. This coefficient is approximate; resolving the shell's finite volume changes it.
