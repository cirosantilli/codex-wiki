Randomized block coordinate descent (Xt+1=Xt−β−1∇Bt​​f(Xt))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Coordinate descent
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
A coordinate descent method that randomly selects a block and updates only its coordinates. Suppose each coordinate is selected with probability 1/ℓ, every block has Lipschitz constant at most β, and the objective function is a strongly convex function with modulus γ>0. The block descent lemma and the strong convexity gradient inequality give
E[f(Xt+1)−f(x∗)∣Xt]≤(1−ℓβγ​)(f(Xt)−f(x∗)).
(52)
For uniform blocks of size m in n=mℓ coordinates, ℓ updates constitute an optimization epoch, and the epoch complexity is O((β/γ)log(Δ0​/ε)). Here Δ0​ is the initial objective function gap, and the target is its expected gap at most ε. A coordinate-based common bound is β=mmaxi​Li​; actual constants can improve it.
