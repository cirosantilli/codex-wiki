Microfluidics

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 393 Articles: 5
Microfluidics controls and studies small fluid volumes in confined channels. In cellular assays it can impose reproducible flow and chemical fields, but channel geometry, wall interactions and flow-induced stresses can themselves alter behaviour.
