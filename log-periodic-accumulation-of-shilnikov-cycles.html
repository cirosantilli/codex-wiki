Log-periodic accumulation of Shilnikov cycles

 ... Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Homoclinic orbit Shilnikov bifurcation Shilnikov return map
OurBigBook.com Words: 112
At a saddle-focus homoclinic orbit, the leading Shilnikov return map is f(x)=Axδcos(qlogx+Φ), x>0, with q>0. If 1/2<δ<1, its fixed points solve cos(qlogx+Φ)=x1−δ/A. Infinitely many solutions accumulate at zero near the zeros of the cosine, with successive size ratios tending to e−π/q. The derivative is Axδ−1[δcos(qlogx+Φ)−qsin(qlogx+Φ)], so their expanding multiplier is unbounded. Narrow monotone strips near successive oscillations map across a common interval; inverse branches contract, supplying arbitrarily long symbolic itineraries and infinitely many saddle periodic orbits. Varying the splitting parameter shifts the graph vertically, producing accumulating fold and period-doubling thresholds and stable windows. The condition δ>1/2 makes the local flow volume-contracting, whereas δ<1 makes its saddle value positive.
