Eventual domination (f≤∗g)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 140 Articles: 4
For functions f,g:ω→ω, write f≤∗g when f(n)≤g(n) for all sufficiently large n. A dominating family is cofinal for this relation.
