Twistor correspondence (Gr(2,C4)←F1,2​(C4)→CP3)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Twistor theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 600 Articles: 8
The flag manifold of a line ℓ contained in a two-plane W has projections (ℓ,W)↦W and (ℓ,W)↦ℓ. Their fibers are respectively CP1 and CP2. This is the flat twistor correspondence: complexified compactified Minkowski space is the Grassmannian of two-planes in twistor space, while projective twistor space is its projective space of lines.
