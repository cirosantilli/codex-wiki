Planetary crust

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics
OurBigBook.com Words: 214 Articles: 11
The planetary crust is the outer compositional rock layer of a differentiated rocky planet. Its thickness need not equal either the lithosphere's thermal thickness or its effective elastic thickness.
