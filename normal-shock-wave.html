Normal shock wave

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Compressible flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 868 Articles: 21
A normal shock is a discontinuity perpendicular to the flow direction across which mass, momentum, and total energy fluxes are conserved.
Property ratios for this discontinuity are tabulated in normal shock tables.
