Tip of the red giant branch

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmological distance measure Cosmic distance ladder
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
The termination of the first-ascent red giant luminosity function near helium ignition provides a calibrated population-II distance indicator. In suitable passbands the tip luminosity varies relatively weakly over a useful old, metal-poor population range. Colour/composition corrections, foreground extinction, photometric completeness and contamination by other evolved stars must be controlled; an arbitrary brightest red star is not a reliable tip measurement.
