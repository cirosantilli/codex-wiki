Pressure-area law of a collapsible tube (p−pe​=P(α),c2=αP′(α))

OurBigBook.com Words: 86 Articles: 1
The pressure-area law of a collapsible tube describes transverse mechanical equilibrium of a deformable tube wall. On a stable branch P′(α)>0. Linearizing mass and momentum conservation about a stationary uniform tube gives the displayed wave speed. External pressure is prescribed separately and is not part of the elastic wall stiffness.
