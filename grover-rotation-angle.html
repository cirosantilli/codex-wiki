Grover rotation angle

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum theory Grover search algorithm
OurBigBook.com Words: 55 Articles: 1
If M of N items are marked and sinθ=M/N​, each Grover iteration rotates the state by 2θ in the marked--unmarked plane, so after r iterations the marked amplitude is sin((2r+1)θ).
