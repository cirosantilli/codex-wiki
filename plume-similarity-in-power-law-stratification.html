Plume similarity in power-law stratification ((q,m,f)=(3+β/2,4+β,4+3β/2))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulent plume Top-hat plume model Boussinesq top-hat plume in a stratified ambient
OurBigBook.com Words: 262 Articles: 4
For N2=Kzβ and positive pure-power fluxes, the similarity solution exponents are q=3+β/2, m=4+β and f=4+3β/2. Increasing volume and momentum and decreasing positive buoyancy flux require −4<β<−8/3. At fixed nonzero K the endpoint coefficient limits are singular.
