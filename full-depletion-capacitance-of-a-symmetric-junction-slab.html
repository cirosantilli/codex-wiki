Full-depletion capacitance of a symmetric junction slab (Cfd​=εs​A/(2d))

 Home Physics Semiconductor P-n junction Depletion region Junction capacitance
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
Let each side of a symmetric abrupt p-n junction have thickness d, area A and doping density N. Partial depletion gives w2=εs​(Vbi​−V)/(eN). Both sides become fully depleted at Vfd​=Vbi​−eNd2/εs​. Beyond that point, the ionized dopant charges are fixed, but an incremental terminal voltage changes the electric field uniformly across the full thickness 2d. Gauss's law then gives dQterminal​=εs​AdV/(2d), up to terminal orientation. The measured positive capacitance approaches Cfd​ rather than vanishing. This geometrical result assumes ideal contacts, negligible mobile carriers, no breakdown and no other series capacitances.
