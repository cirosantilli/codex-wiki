Central angle between independent uniform sphere points (fΘ​(θ)=21​sinθ,0<θ<π)

OurBigBook.com Words: 205 Articles: 2
For two independent uniform points on the surface of a sphere, rotational invariance permits fixing the first at the north pole. The surface area of a latitude band of angular width dθ is 2πR2sinθdθ, yielding the displayed density for their central angle. Equivalently their unit position vectors have a dot product uniformly distributed on [−1,1]. The law is independent of sphere radius.
