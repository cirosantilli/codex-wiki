Dimension of an algebraic set (dimX)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Irreducible topological space Dimension of a topological space by irreducible chains
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 3
The supremum of the lengths of strict chains of nonempty irreducible closed subsets. It is the maximum of the dimensions of the irreducible components. For an affine algebraic set it equals the Krull dimension of its coordinate ring; for a quasi-projective algebraic set it is the supremum of these dimensions over affine open subsets.
