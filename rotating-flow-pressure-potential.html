Rotating-flow pressure potential (ψ=p/ρ+Φ−21​∣Ω×r∣2)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Effective potential Rotating-frame effective potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
For a uniformly rotating incompressible fluid of constant mass density, pressure and conservative body forces combine as the gradient of this scalar. The centrifugal term has a negative sign. Pressure enforces incompressible flow, whereas the velocity-dependent Coriolis force remains explicit and cannot be included in a scalar position potential. Adding a spatially constant function of time to ψ does not change the motion.
