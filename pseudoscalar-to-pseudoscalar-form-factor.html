Pseudoscalar-to-pseudoscalar form factor (f+​(q2), f−​(q2))

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron Meson
OurBigBook.com Words: 19
Between spin-zero pseudoscalars, Lorentz covariance decomposes a vector current matrix element into (p+k)μf+​(q2)+(p−k)μf−​(q2), with q=p−k. Strong interaction parity symmetry in quantum field theory makes the corresponding axial current matrix element vanish.
