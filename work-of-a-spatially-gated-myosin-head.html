Work of a spatially gated myosin head (W=κpb​[x02​/2−(v/koff​)2])

 Home Biology Biological cell Cytoskeleton Actomyosin Spatially gated myosin attachment
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For attached-state elastic energy U=κ(x−x0​)2/2 and a binding window much narrower than x0​, the expected assisting work before x0​ is κpb​x02​/2. The detachment tail produces resisting work −κpb​v2/koff2​. Mean force per head is W/D for site spacing D. The detachment-time second moment, not the square of its mean, fixes the resisting term. These are idealized prescribed-velocity kinetics, not a complete muscle energy budget.
