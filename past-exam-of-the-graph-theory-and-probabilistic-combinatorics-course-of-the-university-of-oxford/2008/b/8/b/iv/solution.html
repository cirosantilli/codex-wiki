It makes EX=e−ω(n). Thus ω→+∞ gives no runs with high probability, and ω→−∞ gives runs and relative concentration. For sufficiently large n, p≤1/2, so the same uniform variance bound applies. Definitions are made from the actual integer k; no continuous rounding assumption is required. These conclusions retain the stipulated range 1≤k≤n/2.
