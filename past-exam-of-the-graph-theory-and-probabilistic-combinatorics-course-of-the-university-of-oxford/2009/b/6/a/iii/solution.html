For distinct indices put arcs in both directions whenever Si​∩Sj​=∅. If j is not an outgoing neighbour of i, every variable determining Aj​ lies outside Si​. The entire family of such events is measurable with respect to variables outside Si​, whose sigma-algebra is independent of the variables inside Si​. Thus Ai​ is independent of their whole generated sigma-algebra, establishing the required dependency graph of events. This justifies joint independence rather than just checking each pair.
