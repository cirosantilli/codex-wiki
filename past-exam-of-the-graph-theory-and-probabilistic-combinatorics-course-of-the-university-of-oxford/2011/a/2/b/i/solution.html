Take a proper colouring c:V(G)→{1,…,k} and orient every edge from its lower-coloured endpoint towards its higher-coloured endpoint. Along a directed path the colour strictly increases, so such a graph path has at most k vertices. A directed cycle is impossible for the same reason. Thus
D is acyclic and λ(D)≤k.​
