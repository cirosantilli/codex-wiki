The requested scale is k=Θ(logn​)​. To see its first-moment origin, the number Xk​ of k-cliques has expectation
EXk​=(kn​)2−(3k​).
(28)
For k on the proposed scale, taking base-two logarithms gives
log2​EXk​=klog2​n−6k3​+O(k2+klogk).
(29)
The leading terms balance at k≈6log2​n​. In particular, for any fixed c>6​, taking k near clog2​n​ makes the expectation tend to zero, so such cliques are absent with probability tending to one.
Thus the first-moment estimate places the largest-clique scale at a constant multiple of logn​, as requested. A diverging expectation below that balance does not itself prove existence; a corresponding probabilistic lower-bound proof would require an additional argument and is not part of the requested first-moment estimate.
