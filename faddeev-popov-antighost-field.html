Faddeev-Popov antighost field (ba=cˉa,gh(b)=−1)

 ... Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing Faddeev-Popov determinant Faddeev-Popov ghost
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The Grassmann-odd partner of the Faddeev-Popov ghost field in the gauge-fixing determinant representation. In an auxiliary-field BRST symmetry description it obeys sb=h, where h is the Nakanishi-Lautrup field. Its ordinary spacetime derivative remains odd, which matters when moving an anticommuting BRST parameter past it.
