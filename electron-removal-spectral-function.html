Electron removal spectral function (f(ϵ)A(k,ϵ))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Finite-temperature quantum field theory Retarded Green function Spectral function
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
The electron single-particle spectral function A(k,ϵ)=−ImGR(k,ϵ)/π, in a convention normalized to unit energy integral per orbital and spin, describes addition and removal energies. For the fermionic retarded Green function, GR(k,t)=−iΘ(t)⟨{ck​(t),ck†​(0)}⟩, energy is referenced to the chemical potential. The occupied removal spectrum is f(ϵ)A(k,ϵ), with Fermi-Dirac distribution f. In the noninteracting limit, A is a delta peak at the band energy; interactions can shift, broaden, and redistribute its weight.
