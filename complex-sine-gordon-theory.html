Complex sine-Gordon theory (L=∣∂ψ∣2/(1−λ2∣ψ∣2)−M2∣ψ∣2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Complex scalar field
OurBigBook.com Words: 221 Articles: 3
A nonlinear complex scalar field theory with a global phase symmetry and a curved target-space kinetic coefficient. In physical coordinates its classical density is displayed above; dimensionless coordinates measured in M−1 put a common M2 outside the reduced density. Its regular coordinate domain is λ2∣ψ∣2<1. Rotating localized solutions are charged complex sine-Gordon solitons. The local classical density does not by itself specify the treatment of the singular coordinate boundary or a global quantum completion.
