Phosphorylation

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
Phosphorylation attaches a phosphate group to a molecule. Transfer from ATP to a substrate or enzyme can participate in energy coupling; the name alone does not specify the complete reaction mechanism. See OpenStax Biology 2e, 6.4: phosphorylation.
