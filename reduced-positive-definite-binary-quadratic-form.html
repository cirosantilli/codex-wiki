Reduced positive definite binary quadratic form

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Binary quadratic form
OurBigBook.com Words: 641 Articles: 13
A positive definite integral form [a,b,c] is reduced when ∣b∣≤a≤c, with b≥0 if ∣b∣=a or a=c. Every proper equivalence class has a unique reduced representative under this boundary convention.
