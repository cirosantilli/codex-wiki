Comparison tournament (n−1 comparisons to find the maximum)

 Home Computer science Algorithm Selection algorithm
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 1
Pair candidates from a totally ordered set and retain the greater one from each comparison until one winner remains. Every other candidate loses once, so n−1 key comparisons suffice to find the maximum of n distinct values. A balanced tournament on 2m inputs gives the winner m direct losers. Retaining each loser's own defeat record preserves information for finding later order statistics. The comparison records form a rooted tree and are distinct from a graph-theoretic tournament, which includes a directed comparison between every pair.
