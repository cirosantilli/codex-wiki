Tadpole integral (Td​(m)=∫(2π)dddk​k2+m21​)

 ... Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Renormalization Regularization in quantum field theory Dimensional regularization Euclidean massive loop integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The one-propagator Euclidean loop momentum integral associated with a tadpole diagram. Dimensional regularization gives Td​(m)=Γ(1−d/2)(m2)d/2−1/(4π)d/2. Near d=3 this has no pole and T3​(m)=−m/(4π) for m>0. A momentum-cutoff definition instead contains a linear ultraviolet divergence; the absence of a dimensional-regularization pole here concerns logarithms, not the positivity or convergence of the unregularized integral.
