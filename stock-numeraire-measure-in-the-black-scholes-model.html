Stock-numeraire measure in the Black-Scholes model (dQS/dQ=e−ρTST​/S0​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Numéraire Change of numeraire
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
For a non-dividend-paying stock in the Black-Scholes model, use density dQS/dQ=e−ρTST​/S0​. Under QS, WtS​=WtQ​−σt is Brownian motion, and logSt​=logS0​+(ρ+σ2/2)t+σWtS​. Thus e−ρTEQ​[ST​H]=S0​EQS​H. This change of numeraire is particularly useful for payments delivered in stock units.
