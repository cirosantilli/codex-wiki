Green function on a Riemann surface (GR​(q,p))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Green's function
OurBigBook.com Words: 94
With the positive logarithmic convention, a Green function with pole p on a noncompact Riemann surface is positive and harmonic away from p, has local form −log∣ζ∣ plus a harmonic function in a coordinate ζ(p)=0, and is minimal among positive functions with these properties. It is obtained, when finite, as the increasing limit of zero-boundary Green functions on an exhaustion by compact bordered subdomains. On the unit disk it is
GD​(z,p)=log​z−p1−p​z​​.
(117)
Its sign convention is −ΔG=2πδp​ in a conformal coordinate, with the opposite overall sign from conventions using log∣ζ∣ as the singular part.
