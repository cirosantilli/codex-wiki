Dispersive swell source inversion (t=t0​+4πL/(gT))

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Free surface Surface gravity wave Airy wave theory Deep-water gravity wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For an impulsive distant source of deep-water gravity waves, a component of period T travels at group velocity gT/(4π). Its arrival at distance L obeys the displayed law, so inverse period increases linearly with arrival time. Two arrivals determine both L and t0​. Finite source duration, currents, refraction and passage through sea ice can invalidate the simple inversion.
