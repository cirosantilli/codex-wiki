Variance-stabilizing transformation (g′(μ)∝V(μ)−1/2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Asymptotic normality Delta method
OurBigBook.com Words: 166 Articles: 1
If a response has mean μ and variance V(μ), the delta method gives Var(g(Y))≈g′(μ)2V(μ). Choosing g′(μ) proportional to V(μ)−1/2 makes this first-order variance independent of μ. For V(μ)=kμ2 on positive means, the resulting transformation is the natural logarithm. This is a local approximation and requires the transformation to be defined on the observations; it does not make arbitrary normal responses positive or preserve an exact normal distribution.
