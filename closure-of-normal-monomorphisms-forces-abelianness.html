Closure of normal monomorphisms forces abelianness

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Pointed category Normal monomorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
In an additive category with finite categorical limits and colimits in which every epimorphism is a conormal epimorphism, closure of normal monomorphisms under composition forces every monomorphism to be normal. To prove this, write a monomorphism m as kg, where k=kercokerm, and put l=kercokerg. If kl=kert were normal, tm=0 would force tk=0, hence l would be an isomorphism. This makes cokerg=0, so g is epic as well as monic. An epic monomorphism which is a cokernel is invertible, forcing m to be normal. Contrapositively, any failure of normality provides two normal monomorphisms whose composite is not normal.
