Curvature-matching diffeomorphism between two surfaces of revolution

OurBigBook.com Words: 33
For the surfaces generated by (eu,0,u) and (coshs,0,s),
KR​(u)=−(1+e2u)−2,KS​(s)=−cosh−4s.
(105)
The diffeomorphism u=logsinhs matches these curvatures, although comparison of the first fundamental forms shows that no local isometry exists.
