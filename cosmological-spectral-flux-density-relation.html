Cosmological spectral flux-density relation (Fν0​​=(1+z)L(1+z)ν0​​/(4πdL2​))

A comoving source's spectral luminosity Lνe​​ specifies emitted power per unit emitted frequency. The cosmological redshift reduces photon energy and arrival rate by two factors of 1+z, but also narrows the received frequency interval by one factor. Consequently its observed spectral flux density is Lνe​​/[4πa02​r2(1+z)], with νe​=(1+z)ν0​. Substituting luminosity distance dL​=(1+z)a0​r gives the displayed relation. Integrating over received frequency recovers the bolometric radiative flux L/(4πdL2​). A fixed-frequency comparison must evaluate the source spectrum at the shifted emitted frequency.
