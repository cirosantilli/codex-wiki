Resonant triple-alpha temperature sensitivity (r3α​∝nα3​T−3e−Er​/(kB​T))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar nuclear fusion Fowler nuclear astrophysics
OurBigBook.com Words: 288
In a dilute thermal helium gas with rapid formation–decay balance of beryllium-8, write its small equilibrium abundance schematically as n8​∝nα2​T−3/2exp[−Δ8​/(kB​T)]. A narrow isolated Hoyle state gives a second-step coefficient proportional to T−3/2exp[−ΔC​/(kB​T)]. Multiplication yields
r3α​∝nα3​T−3exp[−kB​TEr​​],Er​=Δ8​+ΔC​,
(12)
with the stabilizing decay strength absorbed into the coefficient. Here kB​ is the Boltzmann constant. The two positive offsets count energy relative to their respective entrance thresholds, not relative to the carbon-12 ground state. A resonance enhances capture at the relevant energy; the Boltzmann factor still selects how many encounters reach it. This is a sequential narrow-level approximation with fixed nuclear widths, excluding plasma screening and changes of composition or mass density.
William Alfred Fowler lists the Hoyle state 7.654MeV above the carbon-12 ground state, 0.2875MeV above beryllium-8 plus an alpha particle, and 0.3794MeV above three alpha particles.[ref] Subtraction gives Δ8​=0.0919MeV for the diagram.
Normalize the calculated rate at T0​=108K and Er​=0.3794MeV. Holding alpha particle number density and the decay strength fixed gives the original sensitivity calculation
r(T0​,Er​)r(T,Er​+δE)​=(T0​T​)−3exp[−kB​TEr​+δE​+kB​T0​Er​​].
(13)
At T0​, a deliberately chosen shift δE=+100keV multiplies the rate by 9.12×10−6; −100keV gives 1.10×105. These offsets are hypothetical perturbations, not alternative measured nuclear levels. The reference curve has local temperature exponent of a thermonuclear reaction −3+Er​/(kB​T0​)=41.03: raising temperature by 10% gives a factor 41.12 in this fixed-environment calculation. A real star changes its stellar structure and composition as it burns, so these factors do not directly predict final carbon abundance or a constraint on life.
