Compact harmonic-spinor rigidity under nonnegative scalar curvature (∇ψ=0⟹∇ψ=0)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Dirac equation Lichnerowicz spinor-square formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 1
On a compact boundaryless Riemannian spin manifold with R≥0, integrating the Lichnerowicz spinor-square formula against a harmonic spinor gives ∫(∣∇ψ∣2+R∣ψ∣2/4)=0. Positivity forces the spinor to be parallel. Its norm is constant on each connected component, so a nontrivial solution is nowhere zero there. Strictly positive scalar curvature at any point on such a component excludes nonzero harmonic spinors.
