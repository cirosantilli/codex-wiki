Dynamo action

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 52
Dynamo action is growth or sustained maintenance of a magnetic field by conducting-fluid motion despite magnetic diffusion. In the kinematic problem the velocity is prescribed and the resistive induction equation is linear in the magnetic field. Magnetic feedback through the Lorentz force density matters once the field becomes dynamically important.
