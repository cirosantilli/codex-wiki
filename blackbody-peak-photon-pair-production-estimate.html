Blackbody-peak photon pair-production estimate (E2,th​=(me​c2)2/E1​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Quantum electrodynamics Pair production Photon-photon pair production
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
Two head-on photons can create an electron-positron pair at the limiting product E1​E2​=(me​c2)2. A target at the peak of the frequency-density blackbody spectrum has E1​=2.82144kB​T, whereas the wavelength-density peak corresponds to E1​=4.96511kB​T. At T=3K these conventions give incident thresholds approximately 3.58×1014eV and 2.03×1014eV respectively. A real blackbody has a continuum of target energies and angles, so an absorption calculation integrates the spectrum and cross section rather than imposing a sharp peak-photon opacity boundary.
