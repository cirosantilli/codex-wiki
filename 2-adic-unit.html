2-adic unit (Z2×​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field p-adic unit
OurBigBook.com Words: 281 Articles: 4
A 2-adic unit is an odd element of Z2​. It is a square in Z2×​ exactly when it is congruent to 1 modulo 8.
