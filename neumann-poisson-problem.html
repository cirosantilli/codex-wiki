Neumann Poisson problem (−Δu=f,∂n​u=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Poisson equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
On a smooth bounded connected domain, the homogeneous Neumann problem seeks u∈H1(U) such that ∫∇u⋅∇v=∫fv for every v∈H1(U). For f∈L2(U) it is solvable exactly when ∫f=0, and the weak solution is unique up to constants. The Poincare-Wirtinger inequality and the Riesz representation theorem give existence on the mean-zero Sobolev space. The test function one gives necessity of the compatibility condition.
