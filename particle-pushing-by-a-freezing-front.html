Particle pushing by a freezing front (V=A/(36πμad))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Stefan problem Phase boundary
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 1
A planar freezing front can push a spherical particle steadily when repulsive sphere-plane dispersion force balances lubrication resistance. The thin parabolic gap gives hydraulic drag 6πμVa2/d, and balancing it against Aa/(6d2) gives the displayed speed. This balance assumes a nonretarded interaction, small gap and negligible inertial and other particle forces.
