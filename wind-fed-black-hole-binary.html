Wind-fed black-hole binary

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Binary star X-ray binary
OurBigBook.com Words: 91 Articles: 1
A binary in which a black hole captures part of its companion's stellar wind. The captured gas can dissipate energy and, with sufficient specific angular momentum, form an accretion disk. This differs from gas overflowing a Roche lobe through the inner Lagrange point. The fraction captured, its angular momentum and changes in the wind affect the accretion flow and emitted X-rays.
