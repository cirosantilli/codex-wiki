Q-switching

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Laser
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 1
Q-switching creates a short high-energy optical pulse by first storing population inversion while an optical cavity has high loss, then increasing its quality factor so that stimulated emission rapidly extracts the stored excitation. Its duration depends on photon lifetime, excess optical gain, inversion depletion and switching time; it is not fixed by a universal time-bandwidth equality.
