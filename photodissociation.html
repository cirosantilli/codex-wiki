Photodissociation

 Home Chemistry Chemical bond
OurBigBook.com Words: 461 Articles: 5
Photodissociation separates a molecule into fragments after photon absorption. For an isolated parent with a single absorbed photon, conservation of energy gives
hν+Einitial,int​=D0​+Eproducts,int​+Erecoil​.
(11)
Internal excitation energies here are relative to the parent and product ground states used to define D0​; Erecoil​ is the total product translational kinetic energy in the parent's centre of mass frame. Measuring one product's kinetic energy is insufficient by itself. For two fragments of masses mA​,mB​, conservation of momentum gives Erecoil​=EA​(mA​+mB​)/mB​ when A is detected. Unknown product excitation can conceal part of the available energy.
