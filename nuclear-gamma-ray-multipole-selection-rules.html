Nuclear gamma-ray multipole selection rules (∣Ji​−Jf​∣≤L≤Ji​+Jf​,L≥1)

 ... Physics Nuclear physics Atomic nucleus Nuclear structure Nuclear de-excitation Gamma decay
OurBigBook.com Words: 196 Articles: 2
A real photon emitted in gamma decay carries a radiation multipole of integer angular momentum L≥1. The triangle rule gives the displayed range. An electric multipole EL requires πf​/πi​=(−1)L, while a magnetic multipole ML requires πf​/πi​=(−1)L+1. There is no real single-photon E0 decay; an electric monopole transition can instead proceed through internal conversion or, above threshold, pair creation. In the long-wavelength regime kR≪1, increasing L usually strongly suppresses the rate, but nuclear transition matrix elements and collective enhancement can make an allowed lower multipole weaker than a higher one.
