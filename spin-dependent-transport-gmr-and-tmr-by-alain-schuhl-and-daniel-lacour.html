Spin dependent transport: GMR & TMR by Alain Schuhl and Daniel Lacour (2005, 10.1016/j.crhy.2005.10.010)

Alain Schuhl and Daniel Lacour's authored article in C. R. Physique6,pp.945–955. Section2.1 discusses Mott two-current model transport parameters and the spin asymmetry of defect scattering;§2.2 describes averaging scattering when carriers sample several layers. This supports the model qualifications without asserting a universal iron channel ordering.
