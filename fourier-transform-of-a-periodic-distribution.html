Fourier transform of a periodic distribution (u=2π∑n∈Z​cn​δn​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Schwartz space Tempered distribution Fourier transform of a tempered distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For a 2π periodic distribution u∈S′(R), choose a smooth periodic partition of unity Ψ, and define cn​=(2π)−1⟨u,Ψe−inx⟩. A finite Schwartz seminorm bound on the fixed compact support gives ∣cn​∣≤C(1+∣n∣)N. For a Schwartz function ϕ, the Poisson summation formula and Fourier inversion imply Pϕ​(x)=∑n​ϕ(−n)einx. Localizing the pairing for u gives
⟨u,ϕ⟩=⟨u,ΨPϕ​⟩=2π∑n​cn​ϕ(n),
(60)
proving the displayed Fourier transform formula. The series converges in tempered distributions because its coefficients have polynomial growth while ϕ(n) decreases rapidly. Applying the inverse Fourier transform gives u=∑n​cn​einx in tempered distributions. These are the smooth-window Fourier coefficients of a periodic distribution, and the transform formula shows independence of Ψ.
