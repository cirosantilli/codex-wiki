Mechanical configuration (q∈Q)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Mechanical configuration space
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A mechanical configuration specifies the positions and orientations of all parts of a system, subject to its constraints. It is a point of the mechanical configuration space Q; generalized coordinates label such points locally. A configuration does not by itself specify velocities, so it generally does not determine the system's full dynamical state. For a bead on a fixed circular hoop, its angle specifies its configuration, while its angular velocity is additional data.
