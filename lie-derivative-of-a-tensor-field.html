Lie derivative of a tensor field (LX​)

 ... Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Tensor product of vector bundles Tensor field Tensor derivation
OurBigBook.com Words: 348 Articles: 7
For a smooth vector field X, the Lie derivative is the tensor derivation determined by LX​f=X(f) and LX​Y=[X,Y]. The identity [X,fY]=f[X,Y]+X(f)Y permits its unique extension. On a differential one-form,
(LX​ω)(Y)=X(ω(Y))−ω([X,Y]).
(13)
On differential forms it agrees with the usual Lie derivative of a differential form and Cartan's magic formula.
