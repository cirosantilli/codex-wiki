Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle

A Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle is the long exact sequence induced by the Gysin homomorphism and the Euler class of a vector bundle.
For the unit sphere bundle S(E)→B of an oriented rank-d vector bundle, the Gysin sequence contains
⋯→Hq−d(B)⌣e(E)​Hq(B)→Hq(S(E))→Hq−d+1(B)⌣e(E)​Hq+1(B)→⋯.
