Function of bounded variation on a domain (BV(Ω))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Regularization of an inverse problem Variational regularization Total variation seminorm on a domain
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 41
A function u∈L1(Ω) has bounded variation when TV(u)<∞. The space BV(Ω) is a Banach space with norm ∥u∥L1​+TV(u).
