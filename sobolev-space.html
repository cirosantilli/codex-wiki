Zero-trace Sobolev space (H01​(Ω))

Words: 62
Sobolev space OurBigBook.com
The zero-trace first-order Sobolev space is the closure of Cc∞​(Ω) in the H1 norm. On a bounded Lipschitz domain it is the kernel of the Sobolev trace operator. On an interval, its elements have absolutely continuous representatives with both endpoint values zero. The Poincaré inequality makes the derivative norm equivalent to the full Sobolev norm. This is the natural form domain for an elliptic problem with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions.

Trace operator (T:H1(Ω)→L2(∂Ω))

Words: 287 Articles: 4
Sobolev space OurBigBook.com
On a bounded Lipschitz domain, the Sobolev trace operator is the continuous linear extension to H1(Ω) of the boundary restriction of smooth functions. Continuity and density make this extension unique; its existence is the Sobolev trace theorem. The kernel prescribes a homogeneous Dirichlet boundary condition. Vanishing Sobolev trace on only part of the boundary instead gives the form domain for a mixed boundary condition, with the natural Neumann boundary condition on the remainder. On an assembly of congruent tiles, taking a Sobolev trace commutes with a constant matrix acting on the tile restrictions. This lets a transplantation theorem be proved on quadratic form domains even when corner eigenfunctions have singular classical derivatives.

Conformal invariance of the planar Dirichlet inner product
