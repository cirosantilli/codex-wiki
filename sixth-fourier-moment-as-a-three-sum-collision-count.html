Sixth Fourier moment as a three-sum collision count

For A in a finite abelian group and normalized Fourier transform 1A​​, the normalized number of sextuples satisfying
x1​+x2​+x3​=x4​+x5​+x6​
(25)
is
∑γ∈G​∣1A​​(γ)∣6.
(26)
This follows by inserting orthogonality of complex exponentials for the displayed equation: the three variables on one side contribute 1A​​(γ)3 and those on the other side contribute its complex conjugate.
