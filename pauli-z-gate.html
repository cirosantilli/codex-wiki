Pauli Z gate (Z)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The Pauli Z gate fixes ∣0⟩ and maps ∣1⟩ to −∣1⟩.
This gate acts by the corresponding Pauli matrix, viewed as a unitary operator on one qubit.
