Weak Mordell-Weil theorem

 ... Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Mordell-Weil group
OurBigBook.com Words: 474 Articles: 7
For an elliptic curve over a number field and an integer n≥2, the quotient E(K)/nE(K) is finite. Combining this finite quotient with a height descent proves finite generation of E(K).
