Arithmetic circuit

 Home Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Circuit complexity
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
An arithmetic circuit is a Directed acyclic graph whose input nodes are variables or constants in a field, and whose internal gates perform addition, subtraction, or multiplication. Gates may reuse earlier outputs, so the circuit can represent a polynomial without listing all its monomials.
