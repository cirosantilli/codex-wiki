Absorption line (Iline​<Icontinuum​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar atmosphere Spectral line
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
An absorption line is a deficit of observed spectral specific intensity relative to the surrounding continuum. In a photosphere with temperature falling outward, increased line opacity makes photons emerge from cooler layers than the continuum. For an absorption-dominated LTE source, the Planck function then supplies a smaller line specific intensity. A foreground layer with source below the incident continuum gives another direct transfer description.
