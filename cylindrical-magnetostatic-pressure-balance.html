Cylindrical magnetostatic pressure balance

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic field Magnetostatic equilibrium Cylindrical magnetostatic equilibrium
OurBigBook.com Words: 300 Articles: 4
For an axisymmetric vector field independent of the axial coordinate and regular on the axis, Gauss's law for magnetism gives BR​=0. The magnetostatic Ampère-Maxwell equation then gives jR​=0, jϕ​=−cBz′​/(4π), and jz​=c(RBϕ​)′/(4πR) in Gaussian units. Radial magnetostatic equilibrium is
dRd​(p+8πBz2​+Bϕ2​​)+4πRBϕ2​​=0.
(9)
For enclosed axial electric current I(R), the magnetostatic Ampère-Maxwell equation gives Bϕ​=2I/(cR), hence
dRd​(p+8πBz2​​)+2πc2R21​dRdI2​=0.
(10)
The first term contains pressure and axial magnetic pressure; the second combines toroidal magnetic pressure with the hoop force from magnetic tension.
