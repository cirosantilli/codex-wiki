Choice-function well-ordering construction

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Axiom of choice Well-ordering theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Given a choice function on all nonempty subsets of X, recursively choose the next point from the complement of all earlier choices. Hartogs theorem forces the recursion to exhaust X before it defines an injection from h(X) into X, producing a bijection from an ordinal to X.
