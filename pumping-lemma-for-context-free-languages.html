Pumping lemma for context-free languages

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Formal language theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 175 Articles: 4
For some p, every sufficiently long w in a context-free language has w=uvxyz with ∣vxy∣≤p, ∣vy∣>0, and uvixyiz in the language for all i≥0.
