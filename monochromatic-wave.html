Monochromatic wave (ω)

 Home Physics Wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
A monochromatic wave has a single angular frequency. Its sinusoidal components can have different wave amplitudes and wave phases while sharing that angular frequency, as in the polarization of waves examples.
