Quicksort

 Home Computer science Algorithm Sorting algorithm
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
A divide-and-conquer algorithm that partitions entries relative to a pivot, recursively sorts the smaller and larger partitions, and combines their results. A three-way partition keeps all equal keys in a central bucket. Correctness follows from the partition order and recursive correctness; excluding the pivot from child inputs provides decreasing finite lengths. The choice of pivot controls balance and therefore running time.
