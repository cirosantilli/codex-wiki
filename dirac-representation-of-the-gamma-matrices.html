Dirac representation of the gamma matrices (γ​μ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Clifford algebra Gamma matrices
OurBigBook.com Words: 76 Articles: 1
With signature (+,−,−,−), one standard representation is γ​0=diag(I2​,−I2​) and γ​i=(0−σi​σi0​). Block multiplication and the Pauli matrix multiplication law give time-time anticommutator 2I4​, mixed anticommutators zero, and spatial anticommutators −2δijI4​. Thus these gamma matrices represent the spacetime Clifford algebra.
