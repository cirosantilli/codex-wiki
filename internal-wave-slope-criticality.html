Internal-wave slope criticality

 Home Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Internal gravity wave Reflection of an internal-wave ray
OurBigBook.com Words: 107 Articles: 2
Let an internal gravity wave energy ray make angle θ with the horizontal, so that sinθ=ω/N. A boundary of local slope magnitude s is subcritical, critical, or supercritical according as s is smaller than, equal to, or larger than tanθ. At criticality the inviscid reflected wavelength tends to zero.
