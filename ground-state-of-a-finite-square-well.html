Ground state of a finite square well (ktan(ka/2)=κ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Finite square well
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 1
For V(x)=0 on 0<x<a and V(x)=Vw​>0 outside, the lowest bound state is even about a/2, has no nodes, and has 0<E<Vw​. Write k=2mE​/ℏ and κ=2m(Vw​−E)​/ℏ. The interior wavefunction is proportional to cos[k(x−a/2)]; the exterior tails decay exponentially. Wavefunction matching at a finite potential step gives the displayed condition. Its lowest root has 0<ka<π, so E<π2ℏ2/(2ma2), the infinite square well ground state energy. As Vw​ increases, the penetration length 1/κ tends to zero and that limiting energy is approached from below.
