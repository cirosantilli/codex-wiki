Pressure tensor (Pij​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
In the local rest frame of a gas, the pressure tensor is the directional momentum flux Pij​=∫pi​vj​f(p)d3p, where f is the phase-space distribution function per unit momentum volume and v is particle velocity. For an isotropic distribution, Pij​=Pδij​, recovering the kinetic pressure of an isotropic gas. An anisotropic distribution can exert different normal pressures along different directions.
