First-order theory

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 53
A first-order theory is a set of sentences in one first-order language. Depending on convention, the term may instead mean its deductive closure.
This is a theory formulated in first-order logic.
