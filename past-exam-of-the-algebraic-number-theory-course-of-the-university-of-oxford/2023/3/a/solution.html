Let dK​ denote the field discriminant, and put n=[K:Q]≥2. Let r1​ be the number of real field embeddings and r2​ the number of conjugate pairs of nonreal field embeddings, so n=r1​+2r2​. The Minkowski bound for ideal classes provides a nonzero integral ideal A satisfying
1≤N(A)≤(π4​)r2​nnn!​∣dK​∣​.
(46)
Rearranging gives the Minkowski lower bound for a number-field discriminant
∣dK​∣≥(4π​)2r2​(n!nn​)2.
(47)
Because 2r2​≤n and π/4<1, this is at least
(4π​)n(n!nn​)2.
(48)
Apply the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality to 1,2,…,n:
n!≤(2n+1​)n.
(49)
It follows that
∣dK​∣≥((n+1)2πn2​)n≥(94π​)n>1,
(50)
where n/(n+1)≥2/3 for n≥2 was used. Thus
∣dK​∣>1​.
(51)
The source writes ∣Δ(K)2∣>1. If Δ(K) denotes the embedding determinant, its square is dK​; if it denotes the field discriminant, the displayed result also implies the stated inequality. The conclusion therefore does not depend on that notational convention.
