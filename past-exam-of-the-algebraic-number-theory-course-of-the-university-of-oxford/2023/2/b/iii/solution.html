The printed domain assertion is false. For example, take K=Q and I=(4). Then OK​/I=Z/4Z has the nonzero zero divisor 2+(4), so it is not an integral domain and cannot be a principal ideal domain. The valid conclusion is that OK​/I is a principal ideal ring. This error is present in the original PDF, not just its transcription.

H1​×⋯×Hs​=((h1​,…,hs​)),
