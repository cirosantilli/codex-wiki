Put α=m1​​, β=m2​​ and F=Q(α). The hypotheses make L/Q a biquadratic extension. To check the degree of a field extension is four, if β=x+yα with rational x,y, squaring gives xy=0. The case y=0 would make m2​ a rational square; the case x=0 would make m2​/m1​ a rational square. Neither can occur for the specified distinct coprime square-free integers. Hence [L:F]=2.
The nonidentity field automorphism of L/F fixes α and sends β to −β. Thus, for γ=(r+sα)+(t+uα)β, the field norm and field trace are
NL/F​(γ)=(r+sα)2−m2​(t+uα)2,TrL/F​(γ)=2(r+sα)​.
(13)
If γ is an algebraic integer, so is its conjugate, and their sum and product are algebraic integers. Conversely, put T=TrL/F​(γ) and N=NL/F​(γ). The identity
γ2−Tγ+N=0
(14)
shows that γ is integral over Z[T,N]. If T,N are algebraic integers, the ring generated by algebraic integers Z[T,N] is integral over Z. Since integral dependence is transitive, γ is an algebraic integer. This proves the trace and norm integrality criterion for a quadratic extension, including elements already in F.
