Photon-number reference for a Hadamard gate (rk​=1−1/k,sk​=max(k−2,0)/k)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Hadamard transform Hadamard gate
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
A flat reference over k adjacent Fock states has overlap rk​ with a one-photon shift and sk​ with a two-photon shift. These overlaps, obtained by counting shared number states, determine the reduced qubit channel of a number-conserving Hadamard interaction. For k≥2, its squared fidelity with the desired pure output is 1−(1+xz)/(2k) for input Bloch vector (x,y,z). Its worst-case infidelity is 3/(4k) because ∣xz∣≤1/2. At k=1 the two-shift overlap is zero, not −1, and the fidelity is 1/2+(y2−xz)/4. Broad coherent number support, rather than merely a large fixed photon number, suppresses distinguishability of the outgoing field states.
