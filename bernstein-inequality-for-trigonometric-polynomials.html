Bernstein inequality for trigonometric polynomials (∥Tn′​∥∞​≤n∥Tn​∥∞​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier series Trigonometric polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A trigonometric polynomial of degree at most n obeys the displayed derivative bound. Iteration gives ∥Tn(r)​∥∞​≤nr∥Tn​∥∞​. Applied to differences of approximants at dyadic degrees, this inequality underlies the inverse theorem for trigonometric approximation. It differs from the endpoint-weighted Bernstein inequality for algebraic polynomials.
