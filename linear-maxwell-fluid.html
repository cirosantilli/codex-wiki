Linear Maxwell fluid

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 332 Articles: 4
A linear Maxwell fluid has constitutive equation σ+λ∂t​σ=μγ˙​, with γ˙​ twice the rate-of-strain tensor. Here μ is the zero-frequency shear viscosity and λ>0 is the viscoelastic relaxation time. Stress at fixed strain decays as e−t/λ; the corresponding series spring and dashpot have shear modulus G=μ/λ. In small-amplitude simple shear from an isotropic relaxed state, the first-order forcing is off-diagonal, so both normal-stress differences vanish at linear order. This is not a statement about the nonlinear objective model at second order in shear rate.
