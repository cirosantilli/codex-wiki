Metric tensor (gμν​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 625 Articles: 17
A metric tensor is a nondegenerate symmetric covariant tensor that defines squared line elements by ds2=gμν​dxμdxν.
