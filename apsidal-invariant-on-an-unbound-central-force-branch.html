Apsidal invariant on an unbound central-force branch (ψperi​=ψ−Arg(u−uc​−iu′/ν)/ν)

OurBigBook.com Words: 101
For u=uc​[1+ecosν(ψ−ψ0​)] with ν>0 and e≥1, the positive-radius scattering branch has ∣ν(ψ−ψ0​)∣<arccos(−1/e)≤π. Thus the principal complex argument in the displayed expression is continuous throughout that branch, unlike at an apocentre of a bound orbit. Its derivative is ν, so the angle modulo 2π, or the associated unit apsidal vector in the orbital plane, is a stationary integral of motion fixing the periapsis direction. Together with energy and three components of specific angular momentum, it gives five regular scattering labels even for irrational ν. Periodicity is therefore necessary for five orbit labels in recurrent bound motion, not in general for escaping trajectories.
