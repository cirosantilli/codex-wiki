Saddle growth rate along a ray (σ(V)=Im(ω⋆​−Vk⋆​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Hydrodynamic stability
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
On a ray x/t=V, a localized wave packet is governed by a physically selected saddle point satisfying dω/dk=V in its analytic dispersion relation. The exponent's real growth is Im(ω⋆​−Vk⋆​). The fixed-frame case V=0 gives the absolute growth rate. This ray formulation makes the frame dependence of convective hydrodynamic instability explicit.
