Spectrogram

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Frequency spectrum
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A spectrogram displays how a local frequency spectrum changes over time, usually using colour for squared Fourier transform magnitude. Longer analysis windows give finer frequency spacing while averaging over a longer time interval.
