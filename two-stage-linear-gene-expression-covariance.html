Two-stage linear gene-expression covariance (varP=qP​/(2γP​)+kP2​qR​/[2γR​γP​(γR​+γP​)])

For linear RNA removal and protein production driven by independent additive white noises, the stationary RNA variance is qR​/(2γR​) and the protein variance is the displayed expression. Steady count birth-and-death intensities qR​=2γR​⟨R⟩, qP​=2γP​⟨P⟩ give the protein Fano factor 1+kP​/(γR​+γP​). This specializes arbitrary noise strengths; it is not an identity valid for every additive-noise model.
