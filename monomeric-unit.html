Monomeric unit

 Home Chemistry Polymer Monomer
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
An incorporated monomeric unit is the largest constitutional unit identifiable as derived from one monomer molecule. It need not be a whole unreacted molecule or the smallest constitutional repeating unit. See IUPAC: monomeric unit.
