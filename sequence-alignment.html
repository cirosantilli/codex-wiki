Sequence alignment

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 550 Articles: 22
A sequence alignment places nucleotide sequences or protein sequences in correspondence for comparison. The choice of corresponding positions affects evolutionary inference; matching a short stretch alone does not establish common descent of entire organisms.
