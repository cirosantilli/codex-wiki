Shamir's secret sharing

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Coding theory Secret sharing Threshold secret sharing
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 1
Shamir's secret sharing places a secret N at the constant term of a random polynomial f∈Fp​[X] of degree at most r−1 and issues distinct nonzero evaluation pairs (xi​,f(xi​)). Any r shares recover f by polynomial interpolation. Given fewer than r shares, every candidate constant term has the same number of compatible coefficient tuples, which gives perfect secrecy.
