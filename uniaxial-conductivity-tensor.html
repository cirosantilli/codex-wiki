Uniaxial conductivity tensor (σ=αI+γnnT,∣n∣=1)

OurBigBook.com Words: 82
A uniaxial electrical conductivity has one distinguished direction n and equal response in its orthogonal plane. Its eigenvalues are α in n⊥ and α+γ along n, so it is invertible precisely when α(α+γ)=0. For an electric field perpendicular to n, the electric current density is J=αE. Positive dissipation requires both eigenvalues to be nonnegative, a physical constraint separate from algebraic invertibility. For Dij​=ϵijk​nk​, one has D2=nnT−I, and σD2=−σ is equivalent to γ=−α; the conductivity then vanishes along n.
