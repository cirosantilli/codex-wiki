Conditioning on selecting item i leaves capacity B−wi​. Its fractional knapsack problem bounds that item's total interaction with the other selected items. Thus the ordered-pair quadratic objective is at most ∑i​(vi​+qi​)xi​. Remove overweight items before defining their residual-capacity problems.
