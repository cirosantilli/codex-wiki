Franck-Condon principle

 Home Physics Atomic physics Electronic transition
OurBigBook.com Words: 156 Articles: 3
Electronic excitation occurs rapidly compared with nuclear motion. In the Condon approximation, vibronic intensities are proportional to squared overlaps of initial and final vibrational wavefunctions; vertical arrows on a potential diagram summarize this overlap rather than a nuclear trajectory.
