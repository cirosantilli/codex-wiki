Quantum register

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum circuit
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A quantum register is a named tensor product factor of a quantum circuit's Hilbert space, usually a collection of qubits. Registers separate data, control, output and workspace roles; uncomputation returns workspace to a fixed quantum state without destroying coherence in the data.
