Unit normal

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Normal vector
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 4
A unit normal is a normal vector of norm one.
It is a normalized normal vector; changing its sign changes the chosen normal orientation.
