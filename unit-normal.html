Unit normal (n)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Normal vector
OurBigBook.com Words: 571 Articles: 5
A unit normal is a normal vector of Euclidean norm one. Given a nonzero normal vector n0​, normalization gives n=n0​/∥n0​∥. This makes a dot product w⋅n measure the signed length of the normal component, without an extra factor from the length of n0​. On a hypersurface, the two opposite choices n and −n correspond to opposite normal orientations; a continuous choice on an orientable surface fixes its orientation of a surface.
