Kahn-Kalai-Linial theorem (maxi​Ii​(f)≥cVar(f)log(n)/n)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Analysis of Boolean functions
OurBigBook.com Words: 179 Articles: 2
For a Boolean function f:{0,1}n→{0,1} under the uniform probability measure, some coordinate has influence of a variable at least cVar(f)log(n)/n, where c>0 is an absolute constant and Ii​(f) is the probability that flipping coordinate i changes f. For variance bounded below, this is order log(n)/n. The tribes function shows that this order cannot be improved for general balanced Boolean functions.
