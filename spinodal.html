Spinodal

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Landau theory Landau free energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 2
The spinodal is the boundary where a metastable phase loses local stability. In a mean-field free-energy description the relevant curvature vanishes there, separating local minima from unstable configurations.
