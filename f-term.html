F-term

 ... Supersymmetry Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry Superspace Superfield Chiral superfield Superpotential
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
An F-term is the highest θ2 component of a chiral superfield, equivalently an integral over chiral half of superspace. Its supersymmetry variation is a spacetime total derivative.
