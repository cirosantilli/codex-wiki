Measurement of linear photon polarization (M(ϕ)=cos2ϕZ+sin2ϕX)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum measurement Projective measurement
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
With ∣V⟩=∣0⟩ and ∣H⟩=∣1⟩, an analyzer oriented at angle ϕ from vertical has positive-outcome state cosϕ∣0⟩+sinϕ∣1⟩. Subtracting the two orthogonal rank-one projection operators gives M(ϕ)=cos2ϕZ+sin2ϕX, where X,Z are Pauli operators. Physical polarization angles are therefore half their corresponding Bloch sphere angles.
