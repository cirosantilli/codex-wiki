Subalgebra

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
A linear subspace of an algebra over a field closed under multiplication is a subalgebra. If a unital subalgebra is intended, it must also contain the ambient identity. A closed subalgebra of a normed algebra is closed in the norm topology.
