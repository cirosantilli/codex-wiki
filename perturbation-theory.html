Perturbation theory (u(ϵ)∼u0​+ϵu1​+⋯)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Asymptotic expansion
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 1
Perturbation theory develops approximate solutions in a small parameter around a simpler reference problem. A power-series asymptotic expansion gives equations for successive correction coefficients; a matched asymptotic expansion handles cases where one expansion is not uniformly valid.
