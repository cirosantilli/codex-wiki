Propagator

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Quantized field
OurBigBook.com Words: 687 Articles: 14
A propagator is a two-point kernel describing propagation and, in a free theory, a Green function for the quadratic kinetic operator. Its boundary prescription determines its type: the time-ordered Feynman propagator uses an iϵ prescription, while retarded and advanced propagators impose causal boundary conditions.
