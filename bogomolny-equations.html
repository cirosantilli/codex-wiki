Bogomolny equations

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 2
Bogomolny equations are first-order field equations obtained by setting every nonnegative square in a Bogomolny bound to zero. Their solutions automatically satisfy the corresponding second-order Euler-Lagrange equations.
