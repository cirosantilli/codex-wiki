Early stopping of Landweber iteration

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Normal equation for a linear inverse problem Landweber iteration
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
The Landweber spectral filter progressively admits smaller singular-value components. Its approximation bias tends to zero on exact admissible data, but its noise amplification grows. With ∥A∥≤1, a sufficient a priori regularization parameter choice is n(δ)→∞ and n(δ)​δ→0. Taking regularization parameter α=1/n expresses this as α→0 and δ/α​→0; the noise-bias decomposition for linear regularization proves convergence.
