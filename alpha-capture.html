Alpha capture (ZA​X+α⟶Z+2A+4​Y+γ)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Nuclear fusion
OurBigBook.com Words: 58 Articles: 1
Capture of an alpha particle increases the mass number by four and atomic number by two. Radiative alpha capture releases a photon, while reactions such as (α,n) can instead emit a neutron. Carbon-12 alpha capture competes with helium production of carbon during core helium burning.
