Isotropic third-rank tensor (Tijk​=bϵijk​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor Isotropic tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
A rank-three Cartesian tensor invariant under every proper rotation matrix in three dimensions is a multiple of the Levi-Civita symbol. Half-turns about coordinate axes force every nonzero component to contain each coordinate label once, and quarter-turns make these components alternating. Conversely, the determinant formula Ria​Rjb​Rkc​ϵabc​=(detR)ϵijk​ proves invariance when detR=1. Requiring invariance under reflections as an ordinary tensor forces b=0; invariance under proper rotations alone permits nonzero b.
