Fermi transition (MF​=⟨f∣∑k​τk±​∣i⟩)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Radioactive decay Beta decay Beta decay transition
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 1
The spin-independent vector contribution to an allowed beta decay. The isospin operator τk±​ changes a neutron into a proton or the reverse; the sign convention depends on the assigned nucleon isospin projection. Its rotational rank is zero, so Jf​=Ji​ and the parity is unchanged. The phrase Fermi-type forbidden contribution extends the S=0 classification to higher orbital angular momentum.
