Background potential energy (Pb​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Energy Potential energy Available potential energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
The background potential energy is the gravitational potential energy of the statically stable state obtained by adiabatically sorting a fluid's parcels. The difference A=P−Pb​ is available potential energy. Pure rearrangement changes A but not Pb​; irreversible density mixing can increase Pb​. Molecular exchange with internal energy must be separated when defining an irreversible mixing rate.
