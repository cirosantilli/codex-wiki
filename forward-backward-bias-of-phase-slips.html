Forward-backward bias of phase slips (p+​/p−​=e2πω/Teff​)

 ... Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Phase oscillator Phase slip Thermally activated phase slip
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
In a mobility-one noisy Adler phase equation, matching Kramers escape rate prefactors and a barrier difference 2πω give p+​/p−​=e2πω/Teff​. Positive mismatch favors forward winding. The ratio is a low-noise barrier-crossing result, not a claim that the particle remains in one well at arbitrarily long times.
