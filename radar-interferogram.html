Radar interferogram (Δϕ=±4πdLOS​/λr​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Radiometry Remote sensing Interferometric synthetic-aperture radar
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
An image of the relative radar phase between co-registered coherent observations. A displacement changes the two-way path length, giving phase change proportional to 4πdLOS​/λr​, with sign set by the adopted range convention. A full wrapped phase cycle therefore corresponds to half a radar wavelength of line-of-sight displacement. Orbital geometry, topography, atmospheric delay and scattering changes must be separated from deformation; a fringe is not uniquely an elevation contour or an earthquake displacement.[ref]
