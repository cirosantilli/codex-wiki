Cramer-Rao bound

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 2
For a regular scalar model with score function Sθ​ and Fisher information I(θ)=Eθ​Sθ2​, an estimator T with mean ψ(θ) satisfies
Varθ​(T)≥I(θ)ψ′(θ)2​.
(589)
Indeed, differentiating Eθ​T=ψ(θ) gives Covθ​(T,Sθ​)=ψ′(θ), and the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality gives the bound.
