Chemical polarity

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 2
Chemical polarity concerns unequal distribution of electron density in a chemical bond or molecule. A polar molecule has a permanent electric dipole moment; the effects of individual bonds must be combined with their spatial arrangement. In a symmetric linear carbon dioxide molecule, opposing bond-dipole contributions cancel. In water, its bent molecular geometry prevents that cancellation, giving a permanent electric dipole moment.[ref]
