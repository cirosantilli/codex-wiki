Rotating Rayleigh-Bénard convection

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Navier-Stokes equation Rayleigh-Bénard convection
OurBigBook.com Words: 648 Articles: 9
A thermally driven fluid layer rotating about the vertical axis experiences a Coriolis force that couples vertical velocity to vertical vorticity. Its onset depends on the Rayleigh number, Prandtl number and Taylor number, as well as thermal and mechanical boundary conditions. The force does no mechanical work, but changes the linear mode balance and nonlinear convection roll competition.
