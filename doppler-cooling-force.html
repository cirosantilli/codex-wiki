Doppler cooling force (f(v)≃−αv)

OurBigBook.com Words: 289
Use a closed two-level system with spontaneous emission population decay Pe​(t)=Pe​(0)e−Γt. Here Γ is a decay rate in inverse seconds and also the full width at half maximum of the natural linewidth expressed in angular frequency. The corresponding full width in hertz is Γ/(2π); the angular half width is Γ/2. These conventions determine every factor of two and 2π below.
Let s be the on-resonance saturation parameter of each beam, with total excitation weak enough that saturation and coherent coupling between beams can be neglected. The independent-beam scattering rates and net force are
R±​(v)=2Γs​1+4(δ∓kv)2/Γ21​,f(v)=ℏk(R+​(v)−R−​(v)).
(45)
The force is the photon momentum per absorption multiplied by the difference in absorption rates. With dimensionless laser detuning d=δ/Γ and dimensionless velocity u=kv/Γ, write
f(v)=2ℏkΓs​F(u),F(u)=1+4(d−u)21​−1+4(d+u)21​.
(46)
For the example d=−0.5 and u=0.25, the positive and negative beam factors are respectively 4/13 and 4/5. Thus F(0.25)=−32/65≃−0.4923: the atom is moving right and the net force points left. All values in this example are dimensionless.
Subtracting the two fractions gives an especially useful sign check:
F(u)=(1+4(d−u)2)(1+4(d+u)2)16du​,F′(0)=(1+4d2)216d​.
(47)
For red detuning, the force opposes nonzero v throughout this ideal model; its useful magnitude eventually falls at large ∣u∣. Near zero, a Taylor series gives damping
f(v)=−αv+O(v3),α=−(1+4d2)28ℏk2sd​>0.
(48)
At d=−0.5, F′(0)=−2 and α=ℏk2s. For blue detuning the sign reverses and the small-velocity motion is amplified; at exact resonance the opposing mean forces cancel in this symmetric model. Its derivation assumes weak excitation, a closed two-level system, equal beam intensities and nonrelativistic velocities; real multilevel atoms need additional physics.
