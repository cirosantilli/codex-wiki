Assume an ideal-gas grey atmosphere with P(0)=0, constant surface gravity of a star g, T4=Te4​(1+3τ/2)/2 and κ=κ0​ρT4β+1 for β>1. Using ρ=μP/(RT) in dP/dτ=g/κ and integrating gives
P2=3κ0​(β−1)μTe4β​2β+2Rg​[1−(1+3τ/2)1−β].
(22)
Differentiation yields dlogT/dlogP=[(1+3τ/2)β−1−1]/[2(β−1)]. Applying the Schwarzschild criterion with monatomic adiabatic gradient 2/5 gives τb​=32​[((4β+1)/5)1/(β−1)−1]. Since τb​ depends only on β, matching to P=KT5/2 fixes K∝g1/2Te−2β−5/2​.
