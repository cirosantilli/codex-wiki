Deligne-Lusztig theory

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Linear algebraic group Reductive group Finite group of Lie type
OurBigBook.com Words: 395 Articles: 6
Deligne-Lusztig theory constructs virtual characters of finite groups of Lie type from compactly supported étale cohomology of varieties associated with Frobenius and flags. Rational maximal tori and their finite-group characters provide induction data, while the geometry explains character orthogonality and cuspidality.
