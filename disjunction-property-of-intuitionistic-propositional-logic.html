Disjunction property of intuitionistic propositional logic

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Intuitionistic propositional logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
The disjunction property says that if ⊢IPC​A∨B, then ⊢IPC​A or ⊢IPC​B.
This is the disjunction part of disjunction and existence properties, specialized to Intuitionistic propositional logic.
