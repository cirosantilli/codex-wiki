Radiant intensity (I=dΦ/dΩ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Radiometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 1
Radiant intensity describes how a source distributes its radiant flux over directions. A small cone of solid angle ΔΩ carries approximately IΔΩ when I varies little across it; integrating I over all emitting directions recovers the total radiant flux. For a source with isotropy emitting 4πW over a full sphere, I=1Wsr−1 and a 0.01sr cone carries 0.01W. A planar source satisfying Lambert's cosine law instead has direction-dependent radiant intensity, even though its radiance is direction-independent.
