Electrophoretic mobility (v=μE)

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Electrophoresis
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 2
The electrophoretic mobility μ is the proportionality coefficient between drift velocity and a weak applied electric field. Its sign specifies motion relative to the field. When only travel distances are used, the mobility magnitude is often intended. For long linear DNA in a weak-field reptation regime, ∣μ∣ can scale inversely with chain length, whereas free-solution mobility is approximately length-independent. Neither law supplies a universal gel electrophoresis size calibration for arbitrary gel concentration, field strength or molecular topology.
