Restricted mean survival time (RMST(τ)=∫0τ​S(t)dt)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis Survival function
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The restricted mean survival time to a fixed cutoff τ is E[min(T,τ)]=∫0τ​S(t)dt for a nonnegative event time. This follows by the tail integral formula for moments. Unlike the unrestricted mean, it uses no tail beyond τ; under independent censoring it can be estimated by integrating a Kaplan–Meier estimator over adequately observed follow-up.
