By William Lawrence Bragg, credited as W. L. Bragg, pp. 531–539 in Fifty Years of X-ray Diffraction, edited by Paul Peter Ewald. The chapter author is distinct from the volume editor. First published in 1962; digitized in 1999.
Institutional PDF: www.iucr.org/__data/assets/pdf_file/0014/761/bragg_wl.pdf
Institutional transcription: www.iucr.org/what-we-do/publications/books/Fifty-Years-of-X-ray-Diffraction/w-l-bragg
Cited passage: printed p. 533, PDF p. 3, retrospective account of sodium chloride and potassium chloride analysis using Laue photographs in late 1912 and early 1913, followed by work with the spectrometer. This is a first-person retrospective, not the original structure-determination paper.
