Space of infinite subsets of the natural numbers ([N]ω)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Ramsey theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 28
The space [N]ω, also written N(ω), consists of all infinite subsets of the positive integers. An element is identified with its strictly increasing enumeration. If s is finite, write s⊏X when s is the finite initial segment of this enumeration.
