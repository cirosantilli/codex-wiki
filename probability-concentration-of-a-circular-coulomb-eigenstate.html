Probability concentration of a circular Coulomb eigenstate (ψℓ​∝rℓsinℓθeiℓϕ−r/[(ℓ+1)a])

For a nonnegative integer ℓ, this Coulomb potential energy eigenstate has principal quantum number ℓ+1 and maximal magnetic quantum number. Put b=(ℓ+1)a. Including the spherical volume element, its radial probability density is a gamma distribution proportional to r2ℓ+2e−2r/b; its polar density is proportional to sin2ℓ+1θ, while its azimuth is uniform. Radial and polar variables are independent. Their moments satisfy
μ=E[r]=22ℓ+3​b,μ2Var(r)​=2ℓ+31​,E[cos2θ]=2ℓ+31​.

The radial identities follow by successive integration by parts; the angular identity follows from ∫0π​sinj+2θdθ=(j+1)∫0π​sinjθdθ/(j+2).
Set h=(2ℓ+3)−1/3. Chebyshev's inequality and Markov's inequality give P(∣r/μ−1∣>h)≤h and P(∣cosθ∣>h)≤h. By the union bound, outside an event of probability at most 2h, the point lies at distance O(μh) from the equatorial circle of radius μ. The quantum state therefore concentrates near a classical circular orbit in relative units as ℓ grows. The absolute orbit radius and absolute transverse width both grow; this statement does not assert localization in a fixed narrow tube.
