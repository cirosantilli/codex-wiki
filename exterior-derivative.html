Exterior derivative (d)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 32
The exterior derivative is the unique graded derivation d:Ωp(M)→Ωp+1(M) that agrees with the differential on functions and satisfies d2=0.
