Successive over-relaxation (ω)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Numerical linear algebra Stationary iterative method for a linear system
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
For a splitting A=D+L+U, successive over-relaxation updates
(D+ωL)x(k+1)=[(1−ω)D−ωU]x(k)+ωb.
(88)
It reduces to the Gauss-Seidel method at ω=1. If A is symmetric positive definite, the Householder-John theorem applies to M=D/ω+L, N=(1/ω−1)D−U, since MT+N=(2/ω−1)D is positive definite for 0<ω<2.
