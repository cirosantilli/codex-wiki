Trigonometric polynomial

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier series
OurBigBook.com Words: 389 Articles: 7
A trigonometric polynomial is a finite linear combination of complex exponentials einx, or equivalently of sines and cosines. If it vanishes on an interval, all its coefficients vanish.
