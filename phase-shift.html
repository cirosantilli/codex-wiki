Phase shift (Δϕ)

 Home Physics Wave Phase (waves)
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A phase shift is a change in an oscillation's wave phase, specified modulo 2π. It can arise from propagation, reflection or an imposed response. With the time convention e−iωt, a delay τ multiplies the complex amplitude by eiωτ. A sign reversal is a π phase shift.
