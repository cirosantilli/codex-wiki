Lagrange, Euler, and Kovalevskaya tops

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
The Lagrange, Euler, and Kovalevskaya tops are three integrable cases of fixed-point rigid body dynamics. The Euler top has no external torque; the Lagrange top has axial symmetry and its center of mass on the symmetry axis; the Kovalevskaya top has two equal principal moments twice the third and its center of mass in the corresponding principal plane.
