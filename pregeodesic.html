Pregeodesic

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Geodesic
OurBigBook.com Words: 65 Articles: 1
A pregeodesic is a curve whose tangent obeys ∇U​U=κU for some scalar κ. A change of parameter makes it a geodesic with an affine parameter. A varying speed along a geodesic changes its parameter, not its image.
