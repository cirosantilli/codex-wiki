Monte Carlo standard error (MCSE(hˉ)≃σh2​/Neff​​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Monte Carlo method Monte Carlo error
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
The Monte Carlo standard error estimates the standard deviation of a simulation-based estimate across repeated simulation runs. For an average of N independent random variables with finite variance σh2​, it is σh​/N​. Under a suitable central limit theorem for Markov chain Monte Carlo, correlated draws instead give the approximation σh​/Neff​​, where Neff​ is the effective sample size of a Markov chain for the particular estimated function h. This measures numerical uncertainty in an estimate, not uncertainty in the unknown parameter itself.
