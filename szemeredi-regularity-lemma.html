Szemerédi regularity lemma

OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For every ε>0 and m0​ there are M,n0​ such that every graph on at least n0​ vertices has a partition
V=V0​⊔V1​⊔⋯⊔Vm​,
(154)
where m0​≤m≤M, ∣V0​∣≤ε∣V∣, the other parts have equal size, and all but at most εm2 pairs (Vi​,Vj​) are ε-regular.
