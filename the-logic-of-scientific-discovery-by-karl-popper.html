The Logic of Scientific Discovery by Karl Popper (1934 German publication with 1935 imprint; English translation 1959)

OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by Karl Popper Words: 46
Primary selection, especially part A, II, Deductive Testing of Theories.
The selection's opening identifies the original 1959 text and its later editorial arrangement. Sections 29–30 of the full book distinguish acceptance of basic statements from conclusive justification; sections 82–84 discuss scientific corroboration. An indexed full-text transcription is fliphtml5.com/jbdh/wxak/The_Logic_of_Scientific_Discovery_%28Popper_2002%29/ .
