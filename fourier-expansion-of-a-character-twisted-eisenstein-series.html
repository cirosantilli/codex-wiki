With Sχ​(r) the finite Fourier transform in Gauss sum of a Dirichlet character, the constant coefficient of Gk​(χ,z) in e2πiz/N is 2L(χ,k), and its positive coefficients are
cn​=(k−1)!Nk2(−2πi)k​∑r∣n​rk−1Sχ​(r).
(19)
For a primitive Dirichlet character this simplifies to 2(−2πi)kg(χ)((k−1)!Nk)−1∑r∣n​χ​(r)rk−1. Without primitivity the simplified formula is generally false.
