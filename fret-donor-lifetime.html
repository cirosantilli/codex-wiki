FRET donor lifetime (τD​,τDA​)

 Home Biology Biophysical measurement Förster resonance energy transfer FRET efficiency
OurBigBook.com Words: 158
The FRET donor lifetime is the mean time spent in the excited state. For the constant-rate model of FRET efficiency,
τD​=kD​1​,τDA​=kD​+kT​1​,E=1−τD​τDA​​.
(7)
After a sufficiently brief excitation pulse, the FRET donor's fluorescence follows exponential decay, I(t)=I(0)e−t/τ. With the illustrative values τD​=4 nanoseconds and τDA​=2 nanoseconds, the inferred FRET efficiency is 1/2. Multiplying I(0) changes brightness without changing this decay time.
The lifetime comparison requires the same nontransfer decay rates in the reference and paired samples. Changes in the FRET donor's environment or a different nonradiative loss can also shorten the FRET donor lifetime. Heterogeneous FRET efficiencies or slowly varying FRET dipole orientation factors need an appropriate decay model; substituting any fitted average lifetime into the single-pair formula can mislead.
