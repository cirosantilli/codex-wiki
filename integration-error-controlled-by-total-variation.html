Let f have bounded variation, and let g,h be continuous functions on [a,b]. In any tagged sum for the Riemann-Stieltjes integral,
​∑j​(g(ξj​)−h(ξj​))(f(tj​)−f(tj−1​))​≤∥g−h∥∞​∑j​∣f(tj​)−f(tj−1​)∣≤∥g−h∥∞​Vab​(f).
(86)
Taking the limit proves the displayed error bound. Thus approximating g uniformly to accuracy ε controls this integration error by εVab​(f), even when the integrator is not differentiable. For the piecewise linear function in partition example of total variation, accuracy 0.001 gives an error at most 0.011. Total variation supplies exactly the movement budget that an endpoint difference would miss.
