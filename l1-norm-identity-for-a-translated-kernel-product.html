L1 norm identity for a translated kernel product (∥k(x−y)g(y)∥L1(R2)​=∥k∥1​∥g∥1​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Convolution Convolution kernel
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
Let k be a Borel measurable function in L1 space on R, and let g be Lebesgue integrable. The function f(x,y)=k(x−y)g(y) is measurable. Tonelli's theorem for ∣f∣ and translation invariance of Lebesgue measure give
∫R2​∣k(x−y)g(y)∣dxdy=∫R​∣g(y)∣(∫R​∣k(x−y)∣dx)dy=∥k∥1​∥g∥1​.
(78)
Finiteness justifies Fubini's theorem for the signed product, yielding ∫R2​f=(∫k)(∫g). In particular an integrable odd function k makes this signed total zero while the joint L1 norm can remain positive. The triangle inequality for an integral also gives the usual convolution estimate ∥k∗g∥1​≤∥k∥1​∥g∥1​.
