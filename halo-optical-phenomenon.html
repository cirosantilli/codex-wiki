Halo (optical phenomenon)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Meteorology
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
An atmospheric optical feature produced by interactions with ice crystals. A common 22∘ halo comes from a 60∘ prism path in hexagonal crystals; minimum deviation concentrates rays near its inner angular boundary. This differs from the liquid-water path of a rainbow.[ref]
