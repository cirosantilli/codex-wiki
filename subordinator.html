Subordinator

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Lévy process
OurBigBook.com Words: 234 Articles: 2
A subordinator is a Lévy process with nondecreasing paths. Its nonnegative stationary increments have a Laplace transform of the form
Ee−λTt​=e−tΦ(λ),λ≥0,
(48)
where Φ is called its Laplace exponent. Its standard path convention is càdlàg. Subordinators provide random clocks for subordination of a Lévy process.
