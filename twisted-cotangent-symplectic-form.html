Twisted cotangent symplectic form

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Lagrangian submanifold Cotangent bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 134 Articles: 3
If σ is a closed two-form on X, then ω+π∗σ is a symplectic form on T∗X, called a twisted cotangent symplectic form. Nondegeneracy follows from the horizontal-vertical block form of the canonical symplectic form.
