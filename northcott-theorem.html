Northcott theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Height function Absolute multiplicative Weil height
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
There are only finitely many algebraic numbers of bounded degree and bounded Absolute multiplicative Weil height. The finite set can be enumerated effectively from the bounded coefficients of their primitive minimal polynomials.
The degree and Absolute multiplicative Weil height bounds are both essential.
