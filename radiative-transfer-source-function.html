Radiative transfer source function (Sν​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Radiative transfer Radiative transfer equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The radiative transfer source function is emission per unit extinction. If jν​ is emission per unit path length and ρκν​ is extinction per unit path length, Sν​=jν​/(ρκν​) and the transfer equation is μ∂τν​​Iν​=Iν​−Sν​. Under thermal absorption in local thermodynamic equilibrium it is the Planck function; for conservative coherent isotropic scattering it is the mean intensity.
