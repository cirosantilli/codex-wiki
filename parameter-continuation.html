Parameter continuation (F(u(λ),λ)=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Continuation method
OurBigBook.com Words: 146
Parameter continuation changes λ by a small amount and solves again for u, using the previous solution as the initial guess for Newton method. If Fu​ is an invertible matrix, the implicit function theorem gives a local solution branch u(λ), with
dλdu​=−Fu−1​Fλ​.
(24)
Local convergence of Newton method also needs regularity and a sufficiently small step: the cited result assumes a Lipschitz continuous derivative and an invertible Fu​ at the seed.[ref]
For the cubic polynomial,
Fx​=3x2−1,Fλ​=1,dλdx​=1−3x21​.
(25)
The derivative becomes unbounded at either continuation fold, and the scalar Newton method divides by Fx​=0 there. Just beyond the upper continuation fold, no nearby real solution exists at the increased λ, although a distant negative-x solution still exists. Converging to that solution would jump away from the local solution branch. Decreasing the parameter step cannot make x(λ) a valid smooth local description at the bend.
