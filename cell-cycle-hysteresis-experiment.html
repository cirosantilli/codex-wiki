Cell-cycle hysteresis experiment

 Home Biology Signal transduction Signaling feedback Positive feedback Cell-cycle activation feedback
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 3
A 2003 study split a Xenopus laevis egg extract into portions with different histories. One received calcium to destroy endogenous cyclin B before receiving nondegradable cyclin B; the other received nondegradable cyclin B first, then calcium. The same sustained cyclin B dose was approached from low or high Cdc2 activity. A phosphorimager quantified histone H1 phosphorylation, sampling every 15–30 minutes until an apparent stationary response. At 45–60nM, the dose maintained high activity but failed to induce it from the low state.[ref]
Controls tracked labeled endogenous cyclin B disappearance after calcium treatment and persistence of nondegradable cyclin B, addressing unequal remaining input. The comparison supports hysteresis under extract conditions; finite observations cannot establish indefinite persistence. It does not isolate each reinforcing path or establish thresholds in intact biological cells.[ref]
