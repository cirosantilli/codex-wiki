Standard linear solid model (G∗=GL​+(GH​−GL​)iωτ/(1+iωτ))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 246 Articles: 3
A standard linear solid model has a finite instantaneous stiffness, a nonzero long-time stiffness and one exponential stress-relaxation mode. A Kelvin-Voigt model with spring G0​ and viscosity η in series with spring G1​ gives
ησ˙+(G0​+G1​)σ=G1​ηϵ˙+G0​G1​ϵ,GL​=G0​+G1​G0​G1​​,GH​=G1​,τ=G0​+G1​η​.
(5)
These expressions follow by adding series strains under common stress. Its storage modulus is GL​+(GH​−GL​)x2/(1+x2) and its loss modulus is (GH​−GL​)x/(1+x2) for x=ωτ. Its relaxation modulus is G(t)=GL​+(GH​−GL​)e−t/τ, while its creep compliance is J(t)=G1−1​+G0−1​(1−e−G0​t/η). Thus creep retardation and stress-relaxation times differ. This solid has bounded creep and cannot represent terminal fluid flow.
