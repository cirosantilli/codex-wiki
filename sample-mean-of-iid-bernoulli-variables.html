Sample mean of iid Bernoulli variables (X=K/n,K∼Bin(n,p))

For n≥1 independent and identically distributed Bernoulli random variables with success probability p, the sum K=∑i​Xi​ has a binomial distribution. Every length-n pattern with k successes has probability pk(1−p)n−k, and the binomial coefficient counts its (kn​) possible success positions. Therefore
P(X=k/n)=(kn​)pk(1−p)n−k,E[X]=p,Var(X)=np(1−p)​.
(23)
The usual endpoint convention includes p=0 and p=1 as deterministic cases. For p=1/2 and n=4, the five probability masses are 1,4,6,4,1 divided by 16. The sample mean takes values 0,1/4,1/2,3/4,1, with variance 1/16 and standard deviation 1/4. At n=64, its variance is 1/256 and its standard deviation is 1/16.
