Scaling function (φ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Wavelet Multiresolution analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 657 Articles: 9
A scaling function generates the approximation space V0​ of a multiresolution analysis by its integer translates. In an orthonormal construction those translates form an orthonormal basis, and φj,k​(x)=2j/2φ(2jx−k) generate Vj​. Integrable orthonormal scaling functions have ∣φ​(0)∣=1, as follows from density and the MRA projection Fourier identity.
