Quasi-phase-matching (Δk+mK=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Interference (wave propagation) Phase matching
OurBigBook.com Words: 105 Articles: 1
Compensation of phase mismatch by a spatial modulation of the coupling coefficient. Write d(z)=∑m​dm​eimKz. The driven amplitude contains ei(Δk+mK)z, so the selected Fourier component accumulates coherently when Δk+mK=0. For first-order compensation the full spatial period is 2π/∣Δk∣, twice the nonlinear optical coherence length.
An ideal equal-duty pattern alternating +d and −d has first Fourier coefficient of magnitude 2d/π. Hence its conversion intensity is 4/π2 of a uniformly phase-matched crystal using that same coefficient, under the same undepleted plane-wave assumptions. The modulation can nevertheless allow a larger tensor coefficient or an otherwise inaccessible wavelength. Periodically poled lithium niobate implements such sign reversals using ferroelectric domains.
