Projection with a compact factor is closed (X compact⟹πY​:X×Y→Y closed)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Homeomorphism Closed map
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For metric spaces X,Y with X compact, the projection with a compact factor is closed: if F⊂X×Y is a closed set and yn​∈πY​(F) converges to y, choose xn​ with (xn​,yn​)∈F. Sequential compactness supplies xnk​​→x, so (xnk​​,ynk​​)→(x,y)∈F, proving y∈πY​(F). The projection map is also continuous for the sum metric. Without compactness, the closed set {(x,y)∈R2:xy=1} projects to the nonclosed set R∖{0}.
