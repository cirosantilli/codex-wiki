Fourier series of a half-wave rectified sinusoid

OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For V(t)=V0​max(sinωt,0), with period 2π/ω, half-wave rectification gives
V(t)=πV0​​+2V0​​sinωt−π2V0​​∑m=1∞​4m2−1cos(2mωt)​.
(11)
The Fourier coefficients are a0​=2V0​/π, b1​=V0​/2, a2m​=−2V0​/[π(4m2−1)], with all remaining coefficients zero. To derive the cosine coefficients, integrate sinscosns over 0<s<π and use the product-to-sum identity. The sine coefficients follow from orthogonality. A half-period delay reverses only the fundamental sine term and leaves the constant and even-frequency cosine terms unchanged, by translation of a Fourier series.
