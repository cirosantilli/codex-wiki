Maslov cycle

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Lagrangian subspace Lagrangian Grassmannian
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
Relative to a fixed Lagrangian subspace L0​, the Maslov cycle consists of Lagrangian subspaces L with L∩L0​=0. In a two-dimensional symplectic space all lines are Lagrangian, and the cycle is the single line L0​ in the projective line. Fibrewise versions yield sections of a projective-line bundle and record passage through a prescribed vertical direction.
