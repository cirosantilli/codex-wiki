For Φ=−μ/r+α2μ(3z2−r2)/r5, a nearly circular equatorial orbit has n2=μ/R3 and azimuthal frequency Ωc2​=n2(1+3α2/R2). The radial epicyclic frequency is κ2=RdΩc2​/dR+4Ωc2​, and the vertical epicyclic frequency is ν2=∂z2​Φ∣z=0​. Radial stability requires R2>3α2. The nodal precession rate Ωc​−ν is negative and the apsidal precession rate Ωc​−κ positive. These sign statements concern small eccentricity and inclination. For an arbitrary inclined orbit, the averaged argument of periapsis instead evolves as (3/2)nα2[5cos2i−1]/[a2(1−e2)2] and can regress.
