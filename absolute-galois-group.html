Absolute Galois group (GK​=Gal(Ksep/K))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The absolute Galois group of a field K is GK​=Gal(Ksep/K). It is a profinite group, the inverse limit of the Galois groups of finite Galois extensions. Its continuous actions on discrete modules are the coefficients for Galois cohomology.
