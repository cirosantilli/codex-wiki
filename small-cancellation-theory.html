Small cancellation theory

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 415 Articles: 5
Small cancellation theory controls overlap between relators or attaching paths of polygonal two-cells. Requiring overlaps to be small compared with cell perimeters gives strong disc diagram structure, shortening algorithms and geometric restrictions on subgroups. The metric small cancellation condition C′(1/6) is a central example.
