into wSS​−wXX​=0. Indeed, with D=∂t​+ρ∂ρ​, the original operator is D2+D−∂ρ2​, while D=−S∂S​ and ∂ρ​=S∂X​. Its characteristic curves are ρ=−1+Cet and ρ=1+Cet, including the characteristic lines ρ=±1. Zero Cauchy data on −1<ρ<1 at t=0 force vanishing throughout that strip for t≥0, but not for all negative time: u=(e−t(ρ+1)−2)+3​ is a global C2 counterexample. Thus the characteristic barriers describe a forward domain of dependence, not a barrier in both time directions.
