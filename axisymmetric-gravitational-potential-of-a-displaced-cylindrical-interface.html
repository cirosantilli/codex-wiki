Axisymmetric gravitational potential of a displaced cylindrical interface (ψin​=−4πGρRK0​(kR)I0​(kr)η)

Consider a uniform-density cylinder with an axisymmetric radial Fourier mode η=ϵeikz. Its displaced boundary gives a first-order surface mass density ρη, although the interior density perturbation vanishes. The gravitational Poisson equation reduces to Laplace equation away from the interface. Regularity internally and decay externally give ψin​=AI0​(kr)eikz and ψout​=BK0​(kr)eikz. Continuity at R and the derivative jump ψout′​−ψin′​=4πGρϵeikz, together with I0​(κ)K0′​(κ)−I0′​(κ)K0​(κ)=−1/κ, give the title amplitude. The prime differentiates the modified Bessel function argument. This axial mode is different from an azimuthal mode on a gravitational potential of a displaced cylindrical interface.
