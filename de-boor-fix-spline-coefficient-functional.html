This linear functional extracts one coefficient of a B-spline expansion using local polynomial data. With ψi​(x)=∏ℓ=1k−1​(x−ti+ℓ​)/(k−1)! and a point ξ inside a knot cell in the support, it is ∑r=0k−1​(−1)rs(r)(ξ)ψi(k−1−r)​(ξ). The local polynomial identity for the Marsden dual functional gives the desired coefficient. Its bounded restriction to the local spline space can be extended to continuous data on [ti​,ti+k​] by the Hahn-Banach theorem, with the same bound. Such extensions supply spline quasi-interpolation coefficients without requiring derivatives of the data.
