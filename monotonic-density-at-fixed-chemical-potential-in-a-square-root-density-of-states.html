Monotonic density at fixed chemical potential in a square-root density of states

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Fermi-Dirac distribution Sommerfeld expansion Fixed-chemical-potential Sommerfeld density increase
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The temperature derivative of the Fermi occupation has opposite signs below and above μ. Pairing energies μ±x leaves a positive contribution because the density of states increases with energy. The unpaired higher-energy tail is also positive. Hence the density increases at fixed μ for an unbounded three-dimensional parabolic band.
