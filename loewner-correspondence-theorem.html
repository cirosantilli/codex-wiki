OurBigBook.com Words: 55
An increasing family of compact H-hulls of half-plane capacity 2t with the Loewner local growth property corresponds uniquely to a continuous real Loewner driving function. Its mapping-out functions solve the Chordal Loewner equation up to the maximal lifetime of each point. Conversely each continuous driver generates that hull family. A continuous Loewner trace is an additional property, not a consequence for every continuous driver. For SLE it is supplied by the trace-existence theorem.
