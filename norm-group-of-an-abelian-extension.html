Norm group of an abelian extension (NL/K​CL​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 2
For a finite abelian extension L/K, the norm group is the image of the idele-class norm CL​→CK​. Class field theory gives CK​/NL/K​CL​≅Gal(L/K).
