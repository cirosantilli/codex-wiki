Barotropic stellar equation of state (p=p(ρ))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure Equation of state
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A perfect fluid in general relativity stellar model in which pressure depends only on energy density, p=p(ρ). A positive derivative makes this relation locally invertible and turns outward-decreasing pressure into outward-decreasing density. This general relation need not have the constant ratio p/ρ used for a constant cosmological fluid parameter.
