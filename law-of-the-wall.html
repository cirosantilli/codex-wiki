Law of the wall (U/u∗​=κ−1log(z/z0​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Boundary layer Turbulent boundary layer
OurBigBook.com Words: 126 Articles: 2
In a neutral turbulent constant-stress region, the stress velocity and wall distance give mean shear Uz​=u∗​/(κz). Its logarithmic integral uses the roughness length. The law applies outside the inner sublayer and below the outer boundary-layer scale, not all the way to a smooth wall.
