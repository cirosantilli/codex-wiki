For a simple spin-triplet p-wave pairing description, let the 3×3 matrix A, with complex number entries, map an orbital direction k on the Fermi surface into a triplet gap vector d=Ak. The indices refer to spin and orbital components. A scalar amplitude cannot retain all these relative orientations. Anthony James Leggett's account connects that extra structure to the magnetic responses of the different phases.[ref]
Here are two explicit idealizations, with a common positive scale Δ and an overall quantum phase omitted. They show how odd orbital pairing can have either nodes or an isotropic gap. They do not calculate which state has the lower Helmholtz free energy.
