Harmonic chain (H=∑j​[pj2​/(2m0​)+κ(uj+1​−uj​)2/2])

OurBigBook.com Words: 227 Articles: 4
A uniform harmonic chain couples neighbouring displacements by quadratic spring energies. With periodic boundaries and spacing a, a Fourier transform gives ωk​=2κ/m0​​∣sin(ka/2)∣. Quantization makes each nonzero normal mode a phonon. The zero-frequency uniform displacement is a free centre-of-mass coordinate unless pinned, not an ordinary oscillator.[ref]
