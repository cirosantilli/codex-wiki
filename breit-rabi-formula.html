Breit-Rabi formula (x=(gJ​−gI​)μB​B/Δhf​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Hyperfine structure Hyperfine interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 136 Articles: 1
For electronic J=1/2, nuclear spin I and conserved angular momentum component m, define Δhf​=A(I+1/2) and the dimensionless field x shown in the title. The hyperfine-plus-magnetic energies are
E±​(m)=−2(2I+1)Δhf​​+gI​μB​mB±2Δhf​​1+2I+14mx​+x2​.
(1)
The factors gI​,gJ​ use the same Hamiltonian convention and Bohr-magneton units. The two stretched states require the branch consistent with their unique spin configuration. The formula follows by diagonalizing each two-dimensional fixed-m block; it connects weak-field coupled-spin levels to decoupled high-field states. The name refers to Gregory Breit and Isidor Isaac Rabi.[ref]
