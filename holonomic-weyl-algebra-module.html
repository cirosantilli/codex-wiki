Holonomic Weyl algebra module (d(M)=n)

 ... Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Gelfand–Kirillov dimension Gelfand–Kirillov dimension of a module Bernstein growth dimension of a Weyl algebra module
OurBigBook.com Words: 108 Articles: 1
A nonzero finitely generated Weyl algebra module over An​(C) is holonomic when it attains the lower bound in the Bernstein inequality for Weyl algebra modules. The zero module is also conventionally called holonomic. Nonzero subquotients of a holonomic module remain holonomic. Additivity and integrality of the leading Hilbert-polynomial multiplicity then bound the number of nonzero factors in any submodule chain, giving finite composition length.
