Mutual genericity for product forcing

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Forcing Product forcing
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
If H=G0​×G1​ is generic for product forcing over M, then G1​ is generic over M[G0​]. For a name D˙ for a dense subset of the second factor, take p0​∈G0​ forcing density. Ground-model pairs with first coordinate incompatible with p0​, or with first coordinate below p0​ forcing the second into D˙, form a dense product set. The existential clause of syntactic forcing and the membership clause for a canonical forcing name give the density witnesses. The product filter meets this set, cannot use the incompatible case, and hence its second projection meets D˙G0​.
