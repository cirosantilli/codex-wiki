Retract in a category

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Monomorphism Split monomorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
An object A is a retract of B when there are morphisms s:A→B and r:B→A with rs=1A​. The map s is a split monomorphism and r is a split epimorphism.
