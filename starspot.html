Starspot

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Exoplanet atmospheric observatory Exoplanet transit photometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A starspot is a relatively cool, dark region on a star's surface associated with a magnetic field. Starspots change the light curve as the star rotates. A planet crossing a starspot blocks less light than it would from an unspotted region, producing a local upward anomaly during an exoplanet transit; unocculted starspots can instead deepen the normalized exoplanet transit depth. They can also distort spectral lines and imitate a Doppler spectroscopy signal.
