Columns property

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Ramsey theory Partition regular matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 851 Articles: 14
If c1​,…,cn​ are the columns of a matrix, the matrix has the columns property when [n] has an ordered partition B1​⊔⋯⊔Bs​ such that
∑i∈B1​​ci​=0
(11)
and, for j>1, ∑i∈Bj​​ci​ belongs to the linear span of the columns indexed by B1​∪⋯∪Bj−1​.
