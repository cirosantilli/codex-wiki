Reciprocal zeta bounds near the line one (∣ζ(σ+it)−1∣≪(σ−1)−3/4log1/2(2+∣t∣))

For 1<σ≤2, the three-four-one inequality for Euler products gives ∣1/ζ(σ+it)∣≤ζ(σ)3/4∣ζ(σ+2it)∣1/4. For ∣t∣≥1, the fractional-part continuation formula for the Riemann zeta function, truncated at H≍2+∣t∣, gives ζ(j)(σ+it)=Oj​(logj+1(2+∣t∣)). Thus the reciprocal bound even holds with exponent 1/4 instead of 1/2 in this range. On bounded-height compact sets, three-four-one product proof of zeta boundary nonvanishing and removal of the reciprocal's zero at the pole give uniform bounds. Differentiating 1/ζ repeatedly yields
​(ζ1​)(k)(σ+it)​≪k​(σ−1)−3(k+1)/4log3k+1(2+∣t∣).
(58)
The range restriction matters: 1/ζ(σ)→1 as σ→∞, so the first displayed bound cannot hold uniformly for all σ>1. A global version replaces (σ−1)−3/4 with 1+(σ−1)−3/4.
