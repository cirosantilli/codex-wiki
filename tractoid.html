Tractoid (ds2=cot2θdθ2+sin2θdv2)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Surface of revolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 163 Articles: 1
The tractoid is the surface of revolution of a unit tractrix. For X(θ,v)=(h(θ),sinθcosv,sinθsinv) with 0<θ<π/2, its coordinate tangent vectors are orthogonal and have squared lengths cot2θ,sin2θ. The coordinates x=v, y=cscθ transform this first fundamental form into (dx2+dy2)/y2, giving a local isometry to the hyperbolic plane. Thus its Gaussian curvature is minus one. The angular coordinate is periodic globally, while its unwrapped coordinate is a real variable; this distinguishes the local metric identification from a global embedding of the whole hyperbolic plane.
