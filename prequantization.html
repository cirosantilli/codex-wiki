Prequantization ([ω/(2πℏ)]∈H2(P,Z))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Geometric quantization
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 2
Prequantization realizes a symplectic manifold using a Hermitian line bundle with connection whose curvature is iω/ℏ in the convention ιXf​​ω=df. Its existence requires integral symplectic periods. On sections, the Kostant-Souriau prequantum operator represents every classical observable and its Poisson bracket, but the resulting states depend on the full phase space. A polarization in geometric quantization removes this excess of variables.
