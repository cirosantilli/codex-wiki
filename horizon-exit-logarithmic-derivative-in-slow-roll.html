Horizon-exit logarithmic derivative in slow roll (dlogkd​=1−ϵH​1​Hϕ˙​​dϕd​≃−VV′​dϕd​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic inflation Slow-roll approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
Along cosmological horizon exit k∝aH, differentiation gives dlogk/dloga=1−ϵH​, where ϵH​=−H˙/H2. The chain rule gives the first expression. In reduced Planck units, the slow-roll equations 3Hϕ˙​≃−V′ and 3H2≃V give the second expression to leading order. Applied to a quantity already of slow-roll order, the omitted factor changes the answer only at second order.
