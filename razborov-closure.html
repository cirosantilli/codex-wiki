Razborov closure

 ... Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Circuit complexity Monotone circuit complexity Razborov approximation method
OurBigBook.com Words: 123 Articles: 2
Fix integers r and l. The Razborov closure of a family of vertex sets of size at most l repeatedly adjoins a set W whenever there are r existing sets W1​,…,Wr​ whose pairwise intersections are contained in W. A family equal to its closure is called r-closed.
