Delayed feedback characteristic root (λ)

 Home Biology Signal transduction Signaling feedback Negative feedback Delayed negative feedback model
OurBigBook.com Words: 15
A delayed feedback characteristic root solves the characteristic equation of a delay differential equation and supplies an exponential function mode. Its real part determines decay or growth of that mode.
