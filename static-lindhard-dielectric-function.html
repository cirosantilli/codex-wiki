Static Lindhard dielectric function (ϵ(q,0)=1+κ2F(q/2kF​)/q2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
The static independent-electron density response of a uniform three-dimensional, two-spin Fermi gas, combined with self-consistent Poisson equation screening, gives the displayed dielectric function. For bare electron mass and vacuum background, κ2=me​e2kF​/(ϵ0​π2ℏ2) and
F(x)=21​+4x1−x2​ln​1−x1+x​​.
(20)
Here F(0)=1, F(1)=1/2 by continuous extension, and F(x)∼1/(3x2) at large x. A host dielectric background and band effective mass require replacing the vacuum parameters appropriately. This nonlocal response contains Friedel oscillations, which its long-wavelength Thomas-Fermi screening limit misses.
