For a positively oriented curvilinear polygon bounding a disk D in one coordinate patch, with exterior turning angles θi​, the Gauss-Bonnet theorem states
∫D​KdA+∫∂D​κg​ds+i∑​θi​=2π.​
(16)
For a closed compact surface the global formula is
∫S​KdA=2πχ(S).​
(17)
Triangulate the surface with each triangle contained in a coordinate patch. For each face, writing its exterior angles as π minus its interior angles gives ∫K+∫κg​=∑interior angles−π. On summing, the two contributions on each internal edge cancel, and all angles at each vertex sum to 2π. Thus ∫S​K=2πV−πF. Since 3F=2E, this equals 2π(V−E+F), as required. For a nonorientable surface apply the orientable version to its orientation double cover: both the curvature integral and Euler characteristic double. With boundary, the same summation retains the boundary geodesic-curvature integral and the exterior angles at boundary corners, giving the corresponding global boundary formula with right side 2πχ(S).
