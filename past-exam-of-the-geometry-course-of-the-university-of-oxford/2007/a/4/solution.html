Parametrize a nonconstant hyperbolic geodesic by unit speed. Its energy is (x˙2+y˙​2)/(2y2), and the Euler-Lagrange equation in the cyclic coordinate x gives x˙/y2=c. Unit speed gives x˙2+y˙​2=y2. If c=0 the curve is vertical. Otherwise, on either side of a possible highest point,
dydx​=1−c2y2​±cy​,(x−a)2+y2=c−2.
(6)
The two branches join smoothly at the top. Conversely these curves with their unit-speed parametrizations satisfy the energy equations. Thus the unparametrized complete geodesics are vertical half-lines and upper semicircles centered on the real axis.
Let the tractrix point have positive height r(θ). Its tangent segment to the axis has length one and makes angle θ, so r=sinθ. Since its slope is tanθ, h′(θ)=cos2θ/sinθ for its axial coordinate. Rotating X(θ,v)=(h(θ),sinθcosv,sinθsinv) gives orthogonal coordinate vectors with squared lengths cot2θ and sin2θ. Hence the stated first fundamental form follows.
Now put x=v, y=cscθ. Direct substitution gives
y2dx2+dy2​=sin2θdv2+cot2θdθ2.
(7)
On the specified intervals this coordinate map is a diffeomorphism onto the stated rectangle, so is an isometry there and a local isometry on the tractoid.
Set b=cscβ, a=cscα. In the unwrapped coordinate, connect (x0​,b) to (x0​+2π,b) along the semicircle centered at (x0​+π,0) of radius b2+π2​. Its height lies between b and that radius. The hypothesis a2−b2>π2 puts the whole arc in the closed height strip b≤y≤a. Projecting the angular coordinate modulo 2π therefore gives a geodesic segment in the closed tractoid annulus which winds once and returns to its starting point. Taking x0​ to represent any chosen angular position proves the assertion for every point of its θ=β boundary. The returned tangent need not equal the starting tangent, so this proves the claimed returning segment without asserting a periodically smooth closed geodesic.
