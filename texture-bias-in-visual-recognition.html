Texture bias in visual recognition

 Home Biology Neuroscience Sensory system Vision
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A tendency for object decisions to follow local texture rather than global shape under cue conflict. Human–model comparisons on matched conflict images can reveal different strategies despite similar ordinary classification accuracy. Altered training distributions can change the bias without changing the network architecture.[ref]
