Locality property of SLE

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Schramm–Loewner evolution Conformal invariance of SLE
OurBigBook.com Words: 238 Articles: 3
The locality property says that an SLE does not detect a hull lying away from its starting point until the curve reaches that hull. Among chordal Schramm-Loewner evolutions, this property holds for SLE6​.
