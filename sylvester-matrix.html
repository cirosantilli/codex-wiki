Sylvester matrix

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial Resultant
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
For polynomials p,q of degrees m,n, the Sylvester matrix has n shifted coefficient rows of p and m shifted rows of q, with coefficients in descending-power order. It represents, after transposition, the map (A,B)↦Ap+Bq for degA<n, degB<m. Its determinant is the resultant. Its kernel is nonzero exactly when a common polynomial factor exists: divide p,q by that factor to obtain a nonzero cancelling pair; conversely, coprimality and Ap=−Bq imply p∣B, forcing the degree-bounded B and then A to vanish.
