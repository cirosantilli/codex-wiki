Dihedral group (Dn​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 326 Articles: 4
The dihedral group D2n​ is generated by a rotation r of order n and a reflection s with srs=r−1; it has order 2n.
