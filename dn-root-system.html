For the complexified Special orthogonal Lie algebra so2n​, n≥2, choose positive roots of a root system ei​−ej​ and ei​+ej​ for i<j. Its Weyl vector is ρ=(n−1,n−2,…,0). Dominant coordinates satisfy λ1​≥⋯≥λn−1​≥∣λn​∣. The Weyl dimension formula becomes ∏i<j​((λi​+ρi​)2−(λj​+ρj​)2)/(ρi2​−ρj2​). The case D2​ is two independent A1 root systems, while D3​ is isomorphic to the A3 root system.
