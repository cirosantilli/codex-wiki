Dense subset of a forcing order

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Forcing
OurBigBook.com Words: 166 Articles: 3
A subset D of a forcing order P is dense if every p∈P has a stronger condition q≤p in D. This order-density condition is the one used by a generic filter; it is distinct from density in an arbitrarily specified topology.
