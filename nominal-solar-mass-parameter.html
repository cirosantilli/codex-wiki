Nominal solar mass parameter ((GM)⊙N​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Solar mass
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The exact conversion factor 1.3271244×1020m3s−2. Dividing a measured gravitational parameter by this factor expresses a mass in nominal solar units, avoiding a separate conversion through the measured gravitational constant.[ref]
