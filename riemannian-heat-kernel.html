Riemannian heat kernel (H(t,x,y))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Diffusion equation Heat equation Heat kernel
OurBigBook.com Words: 404 Articles: 6
For the positive Laplace-Beltrami operator, the kernel solving (∂t​+Δx​)H=0 with initial Dirac delta distribution on the diagonal represents the heat semigroup. On a closed manifold with a Riemannian metric it is smooth for t>0, symmetric, nonnegative, preserves constants, and obeys the semigroup property.
