Extension of a unitary character from a finite abelian subgroup

 ... Group theory Group Abelian group Pontryagin duality Character group Character group of a finite abelian group
OurBigBook.com Words: 102
Let H≤G be finite abelian and let χ:H→T be a character of a finite abelian group. To adjoin x∈G, let d be the least positive integer with dx∈H and choose a complex number z of modulus one with zd=χ(dx). Then χ​(h+jx)=χ(h)zj is well-defined and extends χ to H+⟨x⟩. There are exactly d choices, corresponding to the d roots. Iterating proves character extension to G, proves that G has exactly ∣G∣ characters, and proves separation of points by extending a primitive character of the cyclic subgroup generated by a nonzero point. No structure theorem is needed for this argument.
