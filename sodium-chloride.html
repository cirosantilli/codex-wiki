Sodium chloride (NaCl)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound
OurBigBook.com Words: 13
Sodium chloride contains sodium cations Na+ and chlorine anions Cl− in a 1:1 ratio; its formula unit has zero net electric charge.
