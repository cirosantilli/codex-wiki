Interferometric phase (Δϕ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Radiometry Remote sensing Interferometric synthetic-aperture radar
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
The phase difference of complex radar returns. It includes deformation, topography, orbital geometry and propagation contributions; the displayed wrapped phase is defined modulo 2π.
