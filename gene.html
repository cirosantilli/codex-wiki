Gene

 Home Biology Genetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 42
A gene is an inherited functional unit of genetic material. In cellular organisms, a gene is a region of DNA whose information produces a functional RNA or contributes to a protein. This connects inheritance to cellular chemistry: a change in a gene can alter the amount or activity of a protein, and thereby a phenotype. See NHGRI: gene.
Alternative inherited versions at a genetic locus are alleles. The distinction matters: two parents can carry the same gene but different alleles, and a genetic locus can have variation outside a gene. Neither the word gene nor a change in DNA alone specifies the resulting phenotype.
