Fixed-volume capillary spreading in a cylindrical cusp (w=[21μa(zN2​−z2)/(8γt)]+1/2​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Lubrication theory Cusp flow between touching cylinders
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For fixed volume V and symmetric spreading, conservation of mass and the cusp flow between touching cylinders equation give width proportional to t−1/8 and support length proportional to t3/8. The source-type similarity solution has the displayed profile and zN​=(8Va/π)1/4(8γt/(21μa))3/8. It is a formal outer lubrication solution; its microscopic nose requires a local description when the axial slope becomes large.
