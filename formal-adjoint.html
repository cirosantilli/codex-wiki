Formal adjoint (L∗)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Hilbert space Riesz representation theorem Adjoint operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 241 Articles: 3
The formal adjoint of a differential operator L is characterized by moving L between factors under an integral by integration by parts, while discarding boundary terms for compactly supported functions.
