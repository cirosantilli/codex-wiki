Razor-thin disk relaxation (trel​∼λtdyn​)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics Two-body relaxation
OurBigBook.com Words: 99
In an unsoftened plane with number surface density n2​ and relative speed u, an encounter strip has area 2udtdb. Thus the velocity diffusion rate is 8G2m2n2​/u∫db/b2, dominated by the small-impact cutoff. Setting this cutoff to Gm/u2 and requiring a change of order u2 gives trel​∼u/(Gmn2​). With u=λv, n2​∼N/R2 and v2∼GNm/R, this is of order λR/v, independent of N at fixed total mass. The point-particle, zero-thickness limit is singular; fixed softening or nonzero thickness changes it. Numerical coefficients are not controlled by extending weak kicks down to order-one deflections.
