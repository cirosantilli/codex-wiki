Nonlinear R-xi gauge in polar Higgs variables (H=∂⋅A−ξef(1+h/f)2π)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing Scalar-dependent gauge fixing
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 1
For the polar parametrization of the Abelian Higgs field with D=∂+ieA, adding −H2/(2ξ) cancels quadratic vector-phase mixing. The cross terms reduce, modulo a total derivative, to −2e(1+h/f)πAμ∂μ​h; interactions remain. The Faddeev-Popov operator is proportional to □+ξe2(f+h)2, so the Faddeev-Popov ghosts couple to h. The vector propagator falls as k−2 at fixed finite ξ, but the polar kinetic and nonlinear gauge fixing vertices include terms of mass dimension above four. Manifest polynomial power counting instead uses Cartesian scalar variables with conventional R-xi gauge.
