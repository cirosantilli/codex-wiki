Non-uniform rational B-spline (F(u,v))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation Spline (mathematics) Spline approximation B-spline
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A non-uniform rational B-spline surface is
F(u,v)=∑i,j​wij​Ni,p​(u)Mj,q​(v)∑i,j​wij​Ni,p​(u)Mj,q​(v)Pij​​.
(14)
Here the Ni,p​ and Mj,q​ are B-spline basis functions for possibly nonuniform spline knot sequences, and the wij​ are weights. Positive weights make each evaluated point a convex combination of the active control points, supplying bounding volumes for geometric searches. The denominator must remain nonzero for the parametrization to be defined.
