Conformal weight ((h,h))

 ... Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Worldsheet Two-dimensional conformal field theory Primary field
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The conformal weights determine the response of a primary operator to holomorphic and antiholomorphic coordinate rescaling. Its scaling dimension is Δ=h+h and its spin is h−h.
