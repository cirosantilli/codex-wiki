Pseudoclassical spinning particle (L=x˙⋅p+2i​ζ⋅ζ˙​−2e​p2−iχp⋅ζ)

A pseudoclassical spinning particle supplements ordinary position and momentum by odd Grassmann variables. An even multiplier imposes p2=0 and an odd multiplier imposes p⋅ζ=0. The odd symplectic term gives {ζm,ζn}D​=−iηmn. Quantization represents these variables by a Clifford algebra, producing a spinor wavefunction.
