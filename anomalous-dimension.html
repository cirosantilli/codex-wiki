Anomalous dimension (η)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Renormalization group Engineering dimension
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 2
An anomalous dimension is the interaction-generated difference between a full scaling dimension and its engineering dimension. For a scalar field, momentum-dependent self-energy corrections require wave-function renormalization and produce Δϕ​=(d−2+η)/2.
