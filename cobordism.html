Cobordism

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 964 Articles: 19
A cobordism from a closed oriented n-manifold X0​ to X1​ is a compact oriented (n+1)-manifold W whose oriented boundary is −X0​⊔X1​.
