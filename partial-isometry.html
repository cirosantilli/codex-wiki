Partial isometry

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra C-star algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 2
An operator U on a Hilbert space is a partial isometry when it is isometric on (kerU)⊥. Equivalently, U∗U is the orthogonal projection onto this initial space.
