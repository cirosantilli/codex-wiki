N-terminus

 Home Biology Protein
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
The N-terminus is the amino end of a protein or amino acid chain. Sequence notation lists residues from the N-terminus toward the C-terminus, the same direction in which biological translation builds a chain. The two-residue example under gene expression places methionine at the N-terminal end before any subsequent processing. [ref]
