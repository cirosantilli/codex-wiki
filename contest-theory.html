Contest theory

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 28
Contest theory studies how prize allocation rules and effort costs influence strategic effort investment, participation, and the resulting Nash equilibria or Bayesian Nash equilibria. Unlike a sale in which only the winner pays, an all-pay auction charges every participant for its effort.
