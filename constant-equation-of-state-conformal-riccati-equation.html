Constant-equation-of-state conformal Riccati equation (H′+α(H2+k)=0,α=(1+3w)/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Friedmann equations
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
For a perfect fluid in general relativity with constant equation-of-state parameter w, use conformal time dt=adτ and the conformal Hubble parameter H=a′/a=aH. The Friedmann acceleration equation gives H′=aa¨=−4πGa2(1+3w)ρ/3. The Friedmann equation eliminates ρ and gives this Riccati equation. For positive energy density, α=0 and appropriately shifted conformal time, its usual branches are cot(ατ) for k=1, 1/(ατ) for k=0, and coth(ατ) for k=−1. Their scale factors are proportional to sin1/α(ατ), (ατ)1/α and sinh1/α(ατ) respectively on intervals where the bases are positive. At α=0 the conformal Hubble parameter is constant and the separate solution is a=Aehτ.
