Simple J-holomorphic sphere

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Pseudoholomorphic curve Simple J-holomorphic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 69 Articles: 1
A simple J-holomorphic sphere is a J-holomorphic sphere that is a simple J-holomorphic curve. Its automorphism group as a parametrized map is trivial, because any automorphism fixing the map fixes its locally injective points.
