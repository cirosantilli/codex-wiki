Bending moment (M)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 1
A bending moment is an internal torque transmitted across a cut through a beam or elastic plate. For a slender linear elastic beam, M=EIκ up to sign convention, where E is Young's modulus, I the second moment of area, and κ the centreline curvature. The corresponding bending response of an elastic plate is described by its bending stiffness and curvature tensor.
