Sheaf cohomology under a closed inclusion (Hi(Y,i∗​F)≅Hi(X,F))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Direct image sheaf
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For a closed inclusion i:X↪Y, direct image sheaf formation is exact: stalks on X are unchanged and stalks outside X are zero. It also preserves flasque sheaves. Pushing forward a flasque resolution thus gives a resolution computing cohomology on Y, and its complex of global sections is identical to the original one on X. This proves the displayed isomorphism in every nonnegative degree.
