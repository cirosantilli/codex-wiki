Bending and torsion of filament bundles (B,C∼Er2R2, ErR3, ER4)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biopolymer mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For independent sliding filaments in a filled bundle, bending energies add and B∼NEr4∼Er2R2. A coherently bonded one-filament-thick tube has B∼ErR3, while a bonded filled rod has B∼ER4. The same torsional scalings apply with the shear modulus instead of Young's modulus if individual sliding filaments are forced to undergo a common material twist. Completely free filament spin/slip does not define that torsional response: collective helical bending alone begins at fourth order in the twist rate.
