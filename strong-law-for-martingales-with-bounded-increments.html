Strong law for martingales with bounded increments (Mn​/n→0 a.s.)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Martingale difference sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
If a martingale has increments bounded by a single deterministic constant, then Mn​/n→0 almost surely. Its increments form a uniformly L2-bounded martingale difference sequence; apply the strong law for uniformly L2-bounded uncorrelated random variables to their partial sums and note M0​/n→0. No square-integrability of the initial value is needed beyond the usual martingale integrability.
