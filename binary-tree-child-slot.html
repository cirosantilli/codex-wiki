Binary tree child slot ((L,R))

 Home Computer science Data structure Binary tree
OurBigBook.com Words: 227 Articles: 1
One of the two distinguished positions, left or right, at a vertex of a binary tree. Each binary tree child slot is occupied by a child and its subtree, or is empty. A reference representation can use null for an empty binary tree child slot. Two slots are capacity for at most two children in a rooted tree, rather than a requirement for two actual vertices.
