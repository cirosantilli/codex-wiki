Hyperbolic equation reduced by an exponential characteristic coordinate (e2yuxx​+uy​=uyy​ ⟹ Uξη​=0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Linear partial differential equation Canonical form of a second-order partial differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For ξ=x+ey, η=x−ey, the Jacobian determinant is −2ey and the transformed domain is ξ>η. The chain rule gives uyy​=e2y(Uξξ​−2Uξη​+Uηη​)+ey(Uξ​−Uη​), while uy​=ey(Uξ​−Uη​). The drift terms cancel, leaving Uξη​=0. Thus the general solution is F(x+ey)+G(x−ey); its characteristic curves are the level sets of the two new coordinates.
