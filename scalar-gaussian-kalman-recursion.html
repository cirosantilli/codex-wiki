Scalar Gaussian Kalman recursion (Kt​=FRt​/(F2Rt​+V))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Control theory State estimation Kalman filter
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 1
For St​=GSt−1​+wt​ and Xt​=FSt​+vt​, assume independent Gaussian process and observation noises, independent of the prior state and previous observations. A normal filtering law with mean m and variance P predicts mean Gm and variance R=G2P+W. Gaussian conditioning then gives posterior mean Gm+K(X−FGm) and variance RV/(F2R+V). Mere weak white noise assumptions do not imply normal posterior laws: independent Rademacher process noise makes the next state a mixture of two shifted normal distributions. The Kalman filter can still describe a best linear predictor under appropriate second-order assumptions, but that is distinct from an exact Gaussian conditional law.
