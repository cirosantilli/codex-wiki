Cone (geometry)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 1
A geometric cone joins a fixed point to points of a plane curve by straight lines. A circular cone uses a circle as that curve; its solid version includes the enclosed circular cross-sections. For a right circular cone of height h and base radius R, the cross-section radius varies linearly along the axis, and its volume is πR2h/3 by integration. An elliptic cone allows elliptical cross-sections. The construction differs from the abstract topological cone, which does not specify lengths or angles.
