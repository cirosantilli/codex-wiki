Telescoping product (∏i=mn​bi+1​/bi​=bn+1​/bm​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A product in which numerator and denominator factors cancel between successive terms. For nonzero bm​,…,bn+1​,
∏i=mn​bi​bi+1​​=bm​bn+1​​.
(1)
One can prove the identity directly by cancellation or by mathematical induction on n. Factoring a more complicated product into two such ratios often reveals an otherwise hidden closed expression.
