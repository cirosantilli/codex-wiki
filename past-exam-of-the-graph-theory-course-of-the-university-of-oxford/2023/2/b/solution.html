Let the nonempty connected components of a graph of G be G1​,…,Gt​. A proper vertex colouring can be chosen separately on each connected component of a graph, since no edge joins different connected components of a graph. The multiplication principle gives, first at positive integers and then as a polynomial identity,
pG​(x)=∏j=1t​pGj​​(x).
(11)
It remains to justify that every factor has zero constant term. More generally, for every nonempty simple graph H, prove pH​(0)=0 by mathematical induction on the number of edges. If there are no edges, then pH​(x)=x∣V(H)∣ with ∣V(H)∣≥1. Otherwise choose an edge e. Both H−e and the simplified edge contraction H/e are nonempty graphs with fewer edges. The deletion-contraction recurrence for the chromatic polynomial and mathematical induction give
pH​(0)=pH−e​(0)−pH/e​(0)=0.
(12)
Thus pGj​​(x)=xqj​(x) for a polynomial qj​, and
pG​(x)=xtj=1∏t​qj​(x),xt∣pG​(x).​
(13)
For the empty graph, t=0 and pG​(x)=1, so the conclusion also holds. This is the chromatic polynomial product over components.
