Principal Eilenberg–MacLane fibration (K(A,n)→E→B)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Homotopy group Postnikov tower
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
For an abelian group A, a principal fibration with fibre K(A,n) is a homotopy pullback of the path fibration PK(A,n+1)→K(A,n+1). It is therefore the homotopy fibre of a map k:B→K(A,n+1) and is classified by [k]∈Hn+1(B;A). The fibre acts by translation up to homotopy; in this untwisted description the fundamental group acts trivially on A. This is the principal-fibration form used for simply connected Postnikov towers.
