No early exercise of a call without dividends

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance American option American call option
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For a non-dividend-paying stock and nonnegative deterministic interest, the European call lower bound is CE​≥S−XB, where B≤1 is the zero-coupon bond price. This is at least the in-the-money exercise payoff S−X; the call also preserves the possibility of avoiding payment of the strike. Positive interest makes the comparison strict when exercise would have positive payoff. At zero interest there can be indifference, and with negative interest the statement can fail: a deterministic stock Su​=S0​eru with r<0 can give a larger payoff by immediate exercise.
