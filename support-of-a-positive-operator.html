Support of a positive operator (suppA=(kerA)⊥)

 ... Functional analysis Hilbert space Riesz representation theorem Adjoint operator Hermitian operator Positive operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
The support of a positive operator is the orthogonal complement of its kernel. In finite dimension it is the span of eigenvectors with positive eigenvalues, equivalently the operator's image. Quantum relative entropy is finite only when the first state's support lies in the reference state's support.
