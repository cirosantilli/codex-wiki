Parametric curve (Q(t))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 325 Articles: 6
A parametric curve is described by a vector-valued map t↦Q(t). Its derivative gives a tangent vector wherever it is nonzero.
