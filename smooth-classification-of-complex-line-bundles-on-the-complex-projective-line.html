Smooth classification of complex line bundles on the complex projective line (L≅OCP1​(k))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Complex line bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
Every smooth complex line bundle on CP1 is isomorphic to exactly one twisting bundle O(k). Trivializing on the two standard affine charts leaves a transition function on C∗; its winding number determines k, and a partition of unity removes the zero-winding factor.
