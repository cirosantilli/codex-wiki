Invariant-rectangle criterion for underdominant coexistence (σmax(q,1−q)<s/8)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Metapopulation Globally coupled underdominant metapopulation
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
In the two-class globally coupled underdominant metapopulation, the rectangle 3/4≤x≤1, 0≤y≤1/4 is forward invariant under the displayed bound. On its inner edges, selection points inward with magnitude 3s/32 while outward migration is at most (3/4)σ(1−q) or (3/4)σq; the outer edges also point inward. Thus prescribed initial state (1,0) cannot approach global allele fixation when 0<q<1. Maximizing f(u)/u=s(1−u)(2u−1) gives s/8 at u=3/4, making the bound optimal within this particular symmetric worst-case rectangle argument, not a universal sharp migration threshold.
