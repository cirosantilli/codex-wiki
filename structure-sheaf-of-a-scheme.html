Structure sheaf of a scheme (OX​)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 357 Articles: 6
The structure sheaf OX​ records the regular functions on every open subset of a scheme X. Its stalk OX,x​ is the local ring of functions defined near x.
