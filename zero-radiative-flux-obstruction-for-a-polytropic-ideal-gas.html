Zero-radiative-flux obstruction for a polytropic ideal gas

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Thin disk Vertical structure of an astrophysical disk Polytropic vertical structure in stellar gravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
For positive finite opacity and nonvacuum ideal gas satisfying radiative diffusion, zero flux implies dT/dz=0. Write P=RρT, where R=kB​/(μm​mp​) for constant mean molecular weight μm​ in proton-mass units. If a polytropic equation of state P=Kργ with constant K>0 is also imposed, then T=(K/R)ργ−1. For γ=1, constant temperature would force constant density, contradicting vertical hydrostatic equilibrium P′=−ρΩ2z on an open interval away from the midplane. The familiar finite polytropic slab for γ>1 is therefore a solution of the hydrostatic/adiabatic closure, not of this finite-opacity zero-radiative-flux system. For γ=1, the consistent isothermal profile is ρ=ρm​exp[−Ω2z2/(2RTm​)], with no finite surface.
