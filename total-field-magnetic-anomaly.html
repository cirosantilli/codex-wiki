Total-field magnetic anomaly (ΔT)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Palaeomagnetism Magnetic anomaly
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The difference ΔT=∣B0​+b∣−∣B0​∣ between measured scalar field strength and a specified background. For ∣b∣≪∣B0​∣, it is approximately B0​⋅b, not an arbitrary fixed Cartesian component.
