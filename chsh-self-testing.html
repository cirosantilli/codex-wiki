CHSH self-testing

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Foundations of quantum mechanics Bell theorem CHSH inequality
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
Maximal quantum CHSH inequality violation certifies an embedded maximally entangled qubit pair and its ideal measurement action, up to local isometries and unused ancillary systems, under the separated-device quantum assumptions. It does not identify every microscopic component of an arbitrary device. Near-maximal observed correlations require robust error bounds rather than exact identification.[ref]
