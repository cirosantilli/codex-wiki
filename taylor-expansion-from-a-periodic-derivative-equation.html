Taylor expansion from a periodic derivative equation (f(r)=f)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Taylor theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For a smooth real function satisfying the displayed equation with positive integer r, differentiating makes f(j+r)=f(j). On any compact interval, all derivatives are therefore bounded by the maximum of the bounds of f,f′,…,f(r−1). The Taylor formula with integral remainder consequently has error at most MRn+1/(n+1)!, which tends to zero uniformly for ∣x∣≤R. Hence the function equals its globally convergent Taylor series. For r=3 and initial derivatives (1,0,0), it is ∑k≥0​x3k/(3k)!.
