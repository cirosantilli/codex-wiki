Solenoidal time-of-flight energy window (K∈[K0​,K0​+q2B2a2/(8m)])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Charged particle in a uniform magnetic field Guiding center On-axis entry into a solenoidal tube
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
A nonrelativistic particle accepted only at a fixed flight time through an ideal uniform-field tube has fixed parallel speed v∥​=l/t0​. On-axis entry and wall clearance bound the independent perpendicular speed by ∣q∣Ba/(2m), provided a full gyration occurs before exit. Adding parallel and perpendicular kinetic energies gives the displayed ideal energy window, where K0​=mv∥2​/2. The upper endpoint is a grazing limit; strict wall exclusion makes it a supremum.
