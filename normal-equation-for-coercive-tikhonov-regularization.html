Normal equation for coercive Tikhonov regularization ((K∗K+αB∗B)uα​=K∗f)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Regularization of an inverse problem Tikhonov regularization Tikhonov regularization with a coercive penalty operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For bounded linear operators K:U→V and B:U→W between real Hilbert spaces, α>0 and ∥Bu∥≥β∥u∥ with β>0, the unique minimizer of ∥Ku−f∥2/2+α∥Bu∥2/2 satisfies (K∗K+αB∗B)uα​=K∗f. Differentiate the objective in every direction and use the adjoint operator to obtain this equation. The coercive quadratic penalty makes its left-hand operator boundedly invertible. Setting B=I gives the Tikhonov normal equation; omitting the penalty instead gives the normal equation for a linear inverse problem.
