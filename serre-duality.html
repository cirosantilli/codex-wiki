Serre duality

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Sheaf cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 362 Articles: 5
For a smooth projective n-dimensional variety X over a field and a coherent sheaf F, Serre duality gives a perfect pairing between Hq(X,F) and Extn−q(F,ωX​), where ωX​ is the canonical sheaf.
