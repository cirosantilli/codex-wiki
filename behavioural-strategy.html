Behavioural strategy

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 191 Articles: 1
An assignment of an action probability distribution to every information set of a player in an extensive-form game. Randomization is fresh at each encounter. It differs from a mixed strategy, which chooses a complete deterministic plan once before play. Perfect recall is central to equivalence of their induced outcome distributions.
