Lorentz reality condition on chiral generators (Ai†​=Bi​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For Hermitian rotations Ji​ and boosts Ki​, the complex combinations Ai​=(Ji​+iKi​)/2 and Bi​=(Ji​−iKi​)/2 are adjoints of one another. Although their complex brackets split into two commuting sl2​ factors, they are not two independent Hermitian compact rotation algebras. The real Lorentz spin group is SL(2,C); SU(2)×SU(2) is a different compact real form.
