Delay differential equation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
A delay differential equation relates a derivative at the current argument to values at earlier arguments. For example, the Buchstab function obeys dud​(uw(u))=w(u−1) for u>2.
