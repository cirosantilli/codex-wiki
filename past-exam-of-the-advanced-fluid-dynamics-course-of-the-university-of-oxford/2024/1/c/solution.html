Take outward radial force as positive and keep the displaced element's angular rate fixed at Ω0​=Ω(R0​). Its imbalance is
ΔfR​=ρ[(R0​+ξR​)Ω02​−Φ′(R0​+ξR​)].
(5)
Use the equilibrium relation from part b, Φ′(R)=RΩ(R)2, and a first-order Taylor expansion:
ΔfR​=−ρR0​dRdΩ2​​R0​​ξR​+O(ξR2​),∣ΔfR​∣≃ρ​R0​dRdΩ2​ξR​​.​
(6)
The absolute values are needed for a magnitude. As printed, the proposed magnitude has a signed right side; it would be negative for outward displacement in a Keplerian disk. For decreasing Ω2, the signed imbalance above points outward and therefore amplifies that fixed-angular-rate displacement. This calculation fixes the angular rate, rather than the specific angular momentum; the latter is the conserved quantity for an uncoupled inviscid radial displacement and leads to the Rayleigh centrifugal stability criterion.
