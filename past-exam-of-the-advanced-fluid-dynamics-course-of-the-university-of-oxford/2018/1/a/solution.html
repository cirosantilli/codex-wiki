Let u and p​ be the complex plane wave amplitudes and set kx​=k⊥​, kz​=k∥​. The linearized Euler equations in the rotating frame are
−iωux​−iωuy​−iωuz​k⊥​ux​+k∥​uz​​=−ik⊥​p​+2Ωuy​,=−2Ωux​,=−ik∥​p​,=0.​
(1)
Here p is pressure per unit mass density, including the centrifugal potential. The last line is incompressibility, not an additional equation of state.
Taking the curl eliminates pressure. For constant Ω and a divergence-free vector field, ∇×(2u×Ω)=2(Ω⋅∇)u. Thus the inertial-wave vorticity equation gives
−iω(ik×u)=2iΩk∥​u,ωk×u=2iΩk∥​u.
(2)
Apply k× once more and use k×(k×u)=−k2u. For a nonzero oscillatory amplitude,
−ωk2u=2iΩk∥​ω2iΩk∥​​u,
(3)
so the inertial-wave dispersion relation is
ω2=4Ω2k2k∥2​​,ωσ​=σ2Ωkk∥​​,σ=±1.​
(4)
The restoring influence is the Coriolis acceleration. In particular ∣ω∣≤2∣Ω∣. For k∥​=0, the projected linear dynamics have zero frequency; these are steady balanced disturbances rather than the nonzero-frequency branch used in the division above. The spatially uniform k=0 mode must be treated separately from this dispersion relation.
