Past exam of the advanced fluid dynamics course of the University of Oxford / 2018 / 2 / a / Solution

OurBigBook.com Words: 122
Use the same constant dynamic viscosity μ for both incompressible Stokes flows, and let f(a) be body force per unit volume. Their equations and Newtonian fluid stress tensor are
∂j​σij(a)​+fi(a)​=0,∂i​ui(a)​=0,σij(a)​=−p(a)δij​+2μeij(a)​,
(26)
where eij(a)​=(∂i​uj(a)​+∂j​ui(a)​)/2 is the strain-rate tensor. Consider the cross-work flux
Qj​=ui(2)​σij(1)​−ui(1)​σij(2)​.
(27)
By the product rule,
∂j​Qj​=(∂j​ui(2)​)σij(1)​−(∂j​ui(1)​)σij(2)​−ui(2)​fi(1)​+ui(1)​fi(2)​.
(28)
The pressure terms vanish by incompressibility. Symmetry of the stress tensor removes antisymmetric velocity gradient contributions, leaving 2μe(1):e(2) in both cross terms; they cancel. Apply the divergence theorem, with n outward from V, and rearrange:
∫V​f(1)⋅u(2)dV+∫∂V​u(2)⋅σ(1)ndS=​∫V​f(2)⋅u(1)dV+∫∂V​u(1)⋅σ(2)ndS.​​
(29)
This is the Lorentz reciprocal theorem with body forces. It depends on reciprocal linear viscous response in the same fluid; arbitrary unrelated constitutive laws would not supply the cross-term cancellation.
