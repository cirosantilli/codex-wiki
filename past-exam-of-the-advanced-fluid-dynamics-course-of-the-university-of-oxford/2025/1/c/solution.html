A gauge transformation changes H=∫V​A⋅BdV by a surface integral of the normal magnetic field, which is zero here. Thus the integrated magnetic helicity has gauge invariance under the stated boundary conditions.
Differentiate both factors in H. Use the stated vanishing-boundary assumption to remove the helicity surface flux; this can be implemented in the usual closed-boundary gauge n×A=0, or by extending the localized field and integrating over all space with sufficient decay. The curl integration by parts identity then gives
