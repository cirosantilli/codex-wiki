The stress tensor of the incompressible Newtonian fluid is σ=−pI+2μe, where e=(∇u+∇uT)/2 is the rate-of-strain tensor. The homogeneous Stokes equations give ∇⋅σ=0. Apply the divergence theorem to the fluid between the body and a surrounding sphere SR​. If nB​ points from the body into the fluid, the fluid-domain normal on the inner boundary is −nB​. Hence
0=∫SR​​σndS−∫∂B​σnB​dS,F=∫SR​​σndS.​
(9)
This is the force flux at infinity in Stokes flow. A nonzero resultant force must be supported by a stress of order R−2 on a surface of area of order R2. More precisely, the leading exterior solution is a Stokeslet with force on the fluid f=−F:
u(x)∼8πμR(I+xxT)f​,p(x)−p∞​∼4πR2f⋅x​.
(10)
Thus u=O(R−1), p−p∞​=O(R−2) and e=O(R−2). These are leading generic orders for a translating body with nonzero drag; the integral alone does not determine every angular component, and force-free disturbances may decay faster.
