Hold the two-sphere object fixed in a uniform stream V. In the free-draining approximation, its drag forces are fi​=ζi​V, with ζi​=6πμai​. The hydrodynamic torque about rc​ is
Tc​=∑i=12​(ri​−rc​)×ζi​V=[ζ1​r1​+ζ2​r2​−(ζ1​+ζ2​)rc​]×V.
(16)
To make it zero for every incoming-stream direction, choose the hydrodynamic centre of a two-sphere dumbbell:
rc​=ζ1​+ζ2​ζ1​r1​+ζ2​r2​​=a1​+a2​a1​r1​+a2​r2​​.​
(17)
It lies closer to the larger sphere, whose larger drag requires a shorter lever arm. Uniform translation does not spin an isolated sphere, so no additional isolated-sphere spin torque enters this leading approximation.
Finite-separation hydrodynamic interactions and connector drag can change the displayed location; the calculation has the same approximation as part c.
