For rotation about the hydrodynamic centre of a two-sphere dumbbell, the sphere centres have velocities vi​=Ω×(ri​−rc​). Their resultant drag force in the free-draining approximation is
F=−∑i​ζi​Ω×(ri​−rc​)=−Ω×(∑i​ζi​ri​−(ζ1​+ζ2​)rc​)=0.​
(18)
Each sphere also experiences a spin drag torque, but its isolated rotation causes no net force. Those spin contributions therefore do not alter the cancellation.
This illustrates translation-rotation reciprocal coupling. About any fixed origin, write the fluid's rigid-body force and torque as
(FT​)=−(ABT​BC​)(UΩ​).
(19)
The transposed cross-blocks follow from the Lorentz reciprocal theorem. If pure translation causes zero torque about an origin for every U, then BT=0, hence pure rotation about that origin causes zero force. This implication is general for linear Stokes flow; the existence and leading location of such an origin here use the two-sphere geometry and the approximation in part c.
