Dielectric anisotropy of a nematic (Δϵ=ϵ∥​−ϵ⊥​)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Order parameter Liquid crystal Nematic liquid crystal
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
The dielectric response differs along and perpendicular to the nematic director. In Gaussian units the orientational electric contribution at prescribed field is −Δϵ(E⋅n)2/(8π); positive anisotropy favors alignment with the electric field. The electric term does not receive the additional one-half convention used in writing the Frank elastic terms.
