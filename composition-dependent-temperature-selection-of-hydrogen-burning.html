Composition-dependent temperature selection of hydrogen burning (ϵCNO​/ϵpp​)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar nuclear fusion CNO cycle CNO crossover temperature
OurBigBook.com Words: 299
A hotter stellar core can favor a higher-barrier route despite fewer catalyst nuclei. To isolate this competition, prescribe a local hydrogen burning toy model at fixed mass density ρ, with reference T0​=15MK and
Fa​(T)=θ−2/3exp[−τa0​(θ−1/3−1)],θ=T/T0​,τa0​=3(4kB​T0​EG,a​​)1/3.
(18)
Choose EG,pp​=493keV and EG,CNO​=45000keV, rounded barrier parameters for a proton pair and proton capture on nitrogen-14. These prescribe the temperature dependence, while the following freely chosen heating normalization incorporates effective nuclear factors and deposited energy:
ϵpp​=ϵ∗​ρ0​ρ​(0.70X​)2Fpp​(T),ϵCNO​=0.01ϵ∗​ρ0​ρ​0.70X​0.01XCNO​​FCNO​(T).
(19)
Here X is hydrogen mass fraction, XCNO​ is CNO mass fraction, and ϵ∗​ is an arbitrary positive unit of stellar energy-generation rate. For X=0.70 and XCNO​=0.01, the reference ratio is 0.01 by construction. It is not a measured solar ratio. The CNO cycle bottleneck is represented by one effective rate, without evolving the existing proton-proton chain branches or catalyst network.
At T0​, the logarithmic temperature slopes are (τpp,0​−2)/3=3.90 and (τCNO,0​−2)/3=19.90. The larger Coulomb barrier gives a steeper fractional response. Because these slopes change with temperature, this example retains the exponential rather than extending two fixed power laws. Writing R0​=0.01(XCNO​/0.01)(0.70/X) and D=τCNO,0​−τpp,0​ gives
ϵpp​ϵCNO​​=R0​exp[−D(θ−1/3−1)],T∗​=T0​(1+DlogR0​​)−3,
(20)
provided the bracket is positive. With the chosen composition, T∗​=20.30MK. Reducing XCNO​ to 0.001 at unchanged X shifts it to 23.91MK. For XCNO​=0, this catalytic route supplies no heat in this model. These are local toy crossovers: a whole star's luminosity requires integrating its stellar energy-generation rate through its stellar composition profile and stellar structure. Nuclear features, plasma screening and changes of the dominant bottleneck limit the model's extrapolation.
