Angular astrometry determines the combination displayed by substituting a=Dα into Kepler's third law. Keeping the fitted shape, orientation and orbital period fixed, the rescaling
D↦λD,a↦λa,μ↦λ3μ
(7)
leaves the entire angular time series unchanged. A longer physical orbit around a larger mass can trace precisely the same angular path. More angular samples improve the fitted combination; by themselves they cannot break this scaling symmetry of the model.
The radial-velocity semi-amplitude is
K=P1−e2​2πasini​.

The spectral velocity scale supplies the missing physical ruler. For a face-on orbit, sini=0, this radial signal vanishes and that method cannot supply a distance. In a partial or noisy data set, fitted orientation and systemic motion also correlate with the inferred scale.
