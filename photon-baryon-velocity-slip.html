Photon-baryon velocity slip (s=vγ​−vb​)

 ... Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Photon-baryon fluid Tight-coupling approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The velocity slip is the difference between the bulk velocities of photons and baryons. Thomson scattering suppresses it at a rate Γ(1+R−1), where R=3ρb​/(4ργ​) and Γ=−τ˙>0. Its first correction in the tight-coupling approximation produces heat-conduction damping of acoustic waves.
