Top Ext detects finite projective dimension (pdA​M=n<∞⟹ExtAn​(M,A)=0)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Projective module Projective dimension
OurBigBook.com Words: 274 Articles: 2
Let M be a nonzero finitely generated module over a commutative Noetherian ring. Take a length-n resolution with finite projective terms. If its top Ext functor with coefficients in A vanished, the dual of its last injection would be surjective. The dual of a finite projective module is projective, so that surjection splits. Finite projective modules are isomorphic to their double duals, so dualizing gives a splitting of the original injection. The resolution then shortens, contradicting minimality of n. When n=0, a nonzero coordinate of a finite free module containing M as a summand gives a nonzero map M→A.
