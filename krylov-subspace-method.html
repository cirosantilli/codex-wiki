Krylov subspace method

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Numerical linear algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
A Krylov subspace method seeks approximations in spaces generated by repeated matrix applications to a starting vector. For a linear system with starting point x0​ and residual r0​=b−Ax0​, the affine search space is x0​+Kk​(A,r0​). Choosing an optimality or orthogonality condition within it gives different algorithms. The conjugate gradient method minimizes the energy norm of the error for a symmetric positive-definite matrix; other methods use residual minimization or different projections. The growth of the Krylov subspace can stop early when an invariant subspace is reached.
