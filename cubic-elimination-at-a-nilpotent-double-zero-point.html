Cubic elimination at a nilpotent double-zero point (P=a2​,Q=b2​+3a1​)

For a vector field with linear part u˙=v, v˙=0 and cubic terms ai​u3+bi​u2v+ci​uv2+di​v3, write (u,v)=(x,y)+(h1​,h2​) with homogeneous cubic hi​. The transformed cubic field is f3​+Ah−DhA(x,y)T, where A(x,y)T=(y,0)T. Taking the coefficients of h2​ to be (−a1​,c2​/2,d2​,0) and those of h1​ to be ((b1​+c2​/2)/3,(c1​+d2​)/2,d1​,0) eliminates every cubic term in x˙ and the xy2,y3 terms in y˙​. The remaining normal form is x˙=y, y˙​=Px3+Qx2y through cubic order. The invariant damping coefficient contains the contribution 3a1​ from differentiating the first equation.
