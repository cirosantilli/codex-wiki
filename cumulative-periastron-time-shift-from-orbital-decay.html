Cumulative periastron-time shift from orbital decay (δT≃21​(P˙b​/Pb0​)t2)

A tiny orbital period change accumulates into a larger timing shift. Choose the original model Pb​(t)=Pb0​+P˙b​t, with Pb0​=28,800s and P˙b​=−2×10−12s/s. Expanding the accumulated cycle count gives
N(t)=∫0t​Pb​(t′)dt′​≃Pb0​t​−2Pb02​P˙b​t2​,δT≃2Pb0​P˙b​t2​.
(28)
Negative δT means an event of fixed orbital cycle count arrives earlier than a constant-period prediction. After twenty Julian years, the chosen period has decreased by 1.262ms, while the accumulated event shift is about −13.83s. This is a time shift of periapsis passage; it is different from a rotation of the direction of periapsis.
