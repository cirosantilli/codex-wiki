Constant-entropy magnetic cooling example (Tf​=Ti​Bf​/Bi​)

 ... Statistical physics Thermodynamics Temperature Adiabatic demagnetization Magnetic entropy Two-level paramagnetic cooling model
OurBigBook.com Words: 346
Choose a fictional two-level refrigerant with μ/kB​=1KT−1, where the upright T denotes tesla. This chosen moment is a convenient model parameter, not a fitted property of the historical salt. At bath temperature Tb​=1K, raise B from 0.20T to 1.00T. Then x increases from 0.20 to 1.00, so
s(0.20)=0.673540…,s(1)=0.365334…,NkB​Tb​Qin​​=s(1)−s(0.20)=−0.308206….
(11)
The last equality assumes a reversible isothermal process of magnetization; negative Qin​ means the refrigerant rejects heat. The bath gains 0.308206…NkB​ of entropy, balancing the stage's loss in this reversible limit. An appreciable temperature difference during real heat transfer adds entropy production.
Now open the heat switch and reduce B from 1.00T to 0.10T. Constant x=1 gives Tf​=0.10K. Throughout this ideal cooling leg, p−​=0.880797…, p+​=0.119203…, and Smag​=0.365334…NkB​. Neither state becomes more probable. Field reduction shrinks the energy gap by ten while preserving the Boltzmann distribution's dimensionless ratio of gap to temperature.
There is a subtle energy check. For the spin Hamiltonian convention used here, external-field interaction energy belongs to Umag​, so reversible work done on the spin subsystem is δWon​=−mdB and
dUmag​=TdSmag​−mdB.
(12)
On the isolated leg, Q=0 but Umag​ increases from −0.761594…NkB​K to −0.0761594…NkB​K, becoming less negative as the gap shrinks. Integrating −mdB at constant x=1 supplies exactly +0.685435…NkB​K. Cooling therefore need not decrease this chosen spin internal energy. An account of work exchanged with a magnet circuit must specify where field interaction energy is assigned before comparing signs. The occupation ratio and temperature prediction do not depend on that bookkeeping choice.
For the isothermal preparation, direct integration gives Won​/(NkB​Tb​)=−ln[cosh(1)/cosh(0.20)]=−0.413913…. The endpoint internal energy difference is −0.722119…NkB​Tb​, equal to Qin​+Won​. This independently checks both the rejected heat and the magnetic-work sign in the chosen convention.
