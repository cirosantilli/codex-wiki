Stellar angular diameter from a visibility envelope (α=1.21967λ/Y0​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum optics Hanbury Brown and Twiss effect Stellar intensity interferometry Uniform-disk stellar visibility
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For a uniform circular stellar disc, the first zero of J1​ at x=3.831706… gives Y0​=1.21967λ/α when α is full angular diameter. In contrast a one-dimensional top-hat angular profile of full width α has a sinc envelope with Y0​=λ/α. Diameter estimates therefore require a brightness-profile convention. A binary additionally multiplies the envelope by its separation-dependent interference factor.
