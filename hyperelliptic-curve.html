Hyperelliptic curve

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 165 Articles: 4
A hyperelliptic curve of genus at least two admits a degree-two map to P1 and can be represented in characteristic other than two by an equation y2=f(x) with f square-free.
