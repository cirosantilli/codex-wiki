Dyadic power variation of a continuous local martingale (∑i​∣Δi​M∣p)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Quadratic variation
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
Along the dyadic partitions of an interval of [0,1], the sums ∑i​∣Δi​M∣p of a continuous local martingale tend to zero in probability for p>2. If 0<p<2 and these sums are eventually bounded almost surely, then M is constant on [0,1]: compare them with the sums defining quadratic variation, using the maximum increment, which tends to zero by uniform continuity.
