Filtered colimits commute with finite limits in sets

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Colimit Filtered category
OurBigBook.com Words: 141 Articles: 2
In the Category of sets, filtered colimits commute with finite limits. Elements in a finite limiting diagram involve only finitely many representatives and finitely many equalities, all of which can be realized at one common stage of a filtered diagram.
