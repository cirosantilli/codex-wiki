Centre of percussion (b=IO​/(Mh))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 141 Articles: 1
For a body constrained to rotate about a fixed pivot, a perpendicular impulse K delivered at distance b from the pivot produces ω=Kb/IO​. If the centre of mass is distance h from the pivot, its momentum is Mωh. The pivot impulse is JO​=Mωh−K, and vanishes when b=IO​/(Mh). This impact location is the centre of percussion. The result assumes the stated planar rigid-body geometry and a short impulse; it is not a claim that all forces at the pivot vanish afterward.
