Surface divergence (∇s​⋅v)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Geodesic Surface covariant derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
The surface divergence of an ambient vector field is the tangential trace ∇s​⋅v=(δij​−ni​nj​)∂j​vi​. For a tangent vector field it is the intrinsic divergence. For v=n it gives the sum of the two principal curvatures; a sphere with outward normal has ∇s​⋅n=2/a.
