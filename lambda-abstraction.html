Lambda abstraction (λx.t)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Computability theory Lambda calculus Lambda term
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
A lambda abstraction λx.t binds the variable x in the lambda term t. In the simply typed lambda calculus, if t:B under x:A, the abstraction has type A→B; this is the implication introduction rule under the Curry-Howard correspondence.
