Determinant of a complex block representation (detM=det(A+iB)det(A−iB))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Determinant
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The block matrix M=(AB​−BA​) is similar over C to diag(A+iB,A−iB) via T=(I−iI​IiI​). Therefore its determinant is det(A+iB)det(A−iB) for arbitrary square matrices A,B. The argument uses a constant change-of-basis matrix and multiplicativity of the determinant.
