Lower ramification numbering (Gs​={σ:vL​(σa−a)≥s+1 for every a∈OL​})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Ramification group
OurBigBook.com Words: 123 Articles: 3
For all real s≥0, this definition uses the normalized integral valuation on L. Equivalently Gs​=G⌈s⌉​. The group G0​ is the inertia group and G1​ is the wild inertia group.
