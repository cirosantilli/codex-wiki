Orientation-preserving affine group of the real line (Aff+(R))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 2
The real affine group used here is the orientation-preserving subgroup of the affine group on the real line. It acts by x↦eax+b and has multiplication (a,b)(a′,b′)=(a+a′,b+eab′). The positive dilation distinguishes this connected component from the full group, which also contains negative dilations.
