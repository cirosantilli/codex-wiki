Resolvent bound for a discrete SIR epidemic (EZ≤1T(I−βA)−1X(0))

When βρ(A)<1, summing the adjacency-matrix bound for a discrete SIR epidemic gives the displayed Neumann series bound on the expected total infected count Z. For an undirected graph, the adjacency matrix of a graph is a symmetric matrix, so its Euclidean operator norm equals its spectral radius. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality then yields EZ≤nm​/(1−βρ) for m initially infected vertices.
