Sums of two values of x cubed plus three x over the 3-adic integers (n≡0,±1,±4(mod9))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Hensel's lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
For n∈Z3​, the equation x3+3x+y3+3y=n has a solution in the p-adic integers exactly for the displayed residue classes. The polynomial F(t)=t3+3t has values 0,4,5 modulo nine, whose pairwise sums are 0,1,4,5,8. For sufficiency choose initial pairs (0,0),(−1,−1),(1,0),(−1,0),(1,1) for those five classes respectively, fix the second coordinate, and lift the first. Its derivative is 3(x2+1), always of 3-adic norm 1/3, while its second Taylor coefficient is 3x, of norm below one. The residual is divisible by nine, so either the strict or the Boundary case of Hensel's lemma with a small quadratic coefficient applies.
