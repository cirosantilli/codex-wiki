Vertical-field magnetoconvection dispersion relation ((λ+σs)(λ+s)(λ+ζs)+σQζπ2(λ+s)−σRk2(λ+ζs)/s=0)

For a stress-free, fixed-temperature layer with vertical magnetic perturbation at its boundaries, take vertical velocity and temperature proportional to sinπz, vertical magnetic perturbation proportional to cosπz, and horizontal magnetic perturbations proportional to sinπz. With s=k2+π2, the vertical velocity, temperature and magnetic amplitudes form a three-variable linear system whose determinant is the stated polynomial. The Prandtl number is σ, magnetic-to-thermal diffusivity ratio is ζ, and field strength is the Chandrasekhar number Q. The steady marginal curve is R(e)=[s3+Qπ2s]/k2.
