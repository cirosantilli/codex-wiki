Function Hopf algebra of a finite group (kG)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Hopf algebra Commutative Hopf algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
Functions on a finite group form a Hopf algebra under pointwise multiplication, with (Δf)(g,h)=f(gh), ε(f)=f(1) and (Sf)(g)=f(g−1). The coproduct is cocommutative exactly when the group is abelian, since delta functions distinguish distinct products gh,hg.
