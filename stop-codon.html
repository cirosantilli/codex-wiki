Stop codon

 ... Genetics Molecular genetics Gene expression Translation (biology) Genetic code Codon
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 1
In the standard genetic code, UAA, UAG and UGA are stop codons: they signal termination of biological translation rather than an amino acid assignment. See Clark, Douglas and Choi: stop codons.
A stop codon supplies no additional amino acid residue. In the constructed AUG GCU UUC UGA example, replacing UGA by UAA preserves termination after the same three amino acid residues. Replacing UUC by UAA instead terminates after methionine and alanine. These are consequences of the stated genetic code, not measurements of expression efficiency.
At a standard stop codon, a release factor promotes release of the polypeptide from the ribosome. Ordinary delivery of an amino acid by transfer RNA does not supply a “stop amino acid”.[ref]
