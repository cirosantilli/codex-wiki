Discrete Gaussian white noise (εt​∼iidN(0,σ2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 36
Discrete Gaussian white noise is a jointly Gaussian sequence with expected value zero and covariance σ2 at lag zero and zero at other lags. Joint normality makes the components independent. This is a sequence of ordinary random variables, unlike the generalized continuous-time white-noise process.
