Total variation norm of a measure (∥μ∥TV​=∣μ∣(K))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Measure Variation measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The total variation norm of a finite signed or complex measure is the total mass of its variation measure. Under the Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem, it equals the norm of the corresponding member of C(K)∗.
