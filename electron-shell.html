Electron shell (n)

 Home Chemistry Quantum chemistry Electron configuration
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 2
An electron shell collects the allowed electronic quantum states sharing a principal quantum number n. Its occupancy is the number of electrons assigned to those states in an electron configuration. An electron shell is not a material surface or a prescribed orbit.
