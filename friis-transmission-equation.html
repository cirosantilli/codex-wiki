Friis transmission equation (Pr​/Pt​=Gt​Gr​[λ/(4πd)]2)

The Friis transmission equation describes a far-field free-space antenna link with aligned directions, matched polarizations and negligible additional loss. It follows by multiplying the transmitter's axial energy flux Pt​Gt​/(4πd2) by the receiver's antenna effective area λ2Gr​/(4π). A receiving impedance mismatch multiplies the available received power by 1−∣ΓL​∣2. Wavelength scaling must state which gains, apertures and impedances are held fixed.
