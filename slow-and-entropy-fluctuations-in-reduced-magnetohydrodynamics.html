In ideal reduced magnetohydrodynamics with constant background sound and Alfvén speeds, perpendicular total-pressure balance gives δp+ρ0​vA2​δB∥​/B0​=0. Put s=δp/p0​−γδρ/ρ0​, r=δρ/ρ0​+s/γ, cT​=vA​cs​/(vA2​+cs2​)1/2 and h=cs2​r/cT​. Then the parallel momentum, continuity and induction equations yield
Du∥​=−cT​∇∥​h,Dh=−cT​∇∥​u∥​,Ds=0.
(33)
Thus f±=u∥​±h satisfy Df±=∓cT​∇∥​f±. The two slow-wave populations propagate along bent magnetic field lines and are mixed by the perpendicular Alfvénic turbulence, while the entropy fluctuation is simply advected. They do not force the perpendicular Alfvénic system at this leading order.
