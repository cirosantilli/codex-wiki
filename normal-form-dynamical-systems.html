Normal form (dynamical systems)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 194 Articles: 2
A simplified local vector field obtained by smooth changes of state variables, and sometimes time or parameter, retaining the terms that cannot be removed at the required order. Near a simple zero eigenvalue, reduction to a centre manifold can give the transcritical form v˙=ϵv−v2 or a reflection-symmetric pitchfork form x˙=ϵx±x3. The nonzero leading coefficients and permitted coordinate changes are part of the classification.
