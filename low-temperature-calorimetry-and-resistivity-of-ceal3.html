Low-temperature calorimetry and resistivity of CeAl3

 Home Physics Branches of physics Condensed matter physics Electron correlation Heavy fermion material
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
The 1975 measurements of CeAl3 below 0.2K found an electronic heat capacity coefficient of 1.62Jmol−1K−2 and a quadratic electrical-resistivity coefficient of 35μΩcmK−2. These are complementary thermal and transport signatures of an unusually dense low-energy electronic spectrum.[ref] A temperature sweep and a C/T versus T2 fit separate the electronic intercept from a Debye lattice term; the transport comparison tests a Fermi liquid temperature law.
