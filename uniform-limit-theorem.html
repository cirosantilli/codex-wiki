Uniform limit theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Uniform convergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
A uniform limit of continuous functions is a continuous function. No compactness assumption on the domain of a function is required.[ref]
For a proof, fix an input x and tolerance ε>0. Uniform convergence supplies one fN​ with ∥fN​−f∥∞​<ε/3. Its continuity at x supplies δ>0 with ∣fN​(y)−fN​(x)∣<ε/3 for allowed inputs satisfying ∣y−x∣<δ. The triangle inequality gives
∣f(y)−f(x)∣≤∣f(y)−fN​(y)∣+∣fN​(y)−fN​(x)∣+∣fN​(x)−f(x)∣<ε.
(81)
One approximating function controls both endpoints of this estimate. Under pointwise convergence, selecting an index for x supplies no common control at nearby y. This explains why the polynomial functions xn can have a discontinuous pointwise limit on [0,1].
