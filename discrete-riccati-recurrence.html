Discrete Riccati recurrence

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Dynamic programming Bellman equation Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation Linear-quadratic optimal control
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A backward scalar or matrix recurrence for the quadratic coefficients in a value function for a linear-quadratic optimal control problem. It results from completing the control square in the Bellman equation. Multiplicative noise modifies the quadratic coefficients through its second moments.
