Energy balance (ΔE=Ein​−Eout​)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Conservation of energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
An energy balance accounts for the change of stored energy as inputs minus outputs. For mechanical motion, externally supplied power and dissipative losses must be included before imposing a conserved mechanical energy.
