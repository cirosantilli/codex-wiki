Modulus powers of holomorphic functions are subharmonic (Δ∣f∣p=p2∣f∣p−2∣f′∣2≥0(f=0, p>0))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Harmonic function Subharmonic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For every holomorphic function and p>0, ∣f∣p is subharmonic. Away from its zeros the displayed Laplacian calculation proves this. At the zeros, use the smooth approximations (∣f∣2+ε)p/2, whose Laplacians are nonnegative, and let ε↓0 in their mean inequalities. This permits Poisson integral comparison for fractional modulus powers even when a global analytic fractional power does not exist.
