Tangent function (tanx=sinx/cosx)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Trigonometric function
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
A trigonometric function defined where cosx=0. It has real simple poles at (n+1/2)π, and tan((n+1/2)π+δ)=−cotδ. This local pole identity generates the large roots near tangent poles.
