Quenched disorder (Fq​=−kB​TEJ​logZJ​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Spin model
OurBigBook.com Words: 281 Articles: 2
Quenched disorder is randomness held fixed while the other degrees of freedom equilibrate. For a sample with couplings J, first compute its partition function ZJ​ and Helmholtz free energy −kB​TlogZJ​, then average over samples. One must not silently replace EJ​logZJ​ by logEJ​ZJ​.[ref]
