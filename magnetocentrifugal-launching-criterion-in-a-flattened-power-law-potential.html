Magnetocentrifugal launching criterion in a flattened power-law potential (αcrit​=arctan(∣λ∣/β+2​))

In a flattened power-law gravitational potential Φ=Φ0​R0β​(R2+λ2z2)−β/2, a magnetic surface corotating with its disk footpoint has an effective potential with meridional Hessian matrix Ωf2​diag[−(β+2),λ2] there. A cold outward displacement along a field inclined by α to the vertical is downhill when tan2α>λ2/(β+2). Equality is marginal and can depend on higher derivatives and field-line curvature; this is a local launching criterion, not a guarantee of a global escaping wind.
