Research deception

 Home Science Psychology Psychological research methods Research ethics
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
Intentionally misleading participants or withholding relevant information about a study. Its necessity and risks require justification and appropriate review; less deceptive alternatives should be considered. Research debriefing can explain what was withheld but does not automatically remove all consequences.
