Polarized Hodge structure ((−1)w(w−1)/2ip−qQ(v,vˉ)>0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Hodge theory Hodge structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 258 Articles: 4
A polarization is a nondegenerate integral bilinear form of symmetry (−1)w, orthogonal on Hodge summands except complementary bidegrees, satisfying the displayed positivity for every nonzero v∈Hp,q. This convention takes the cup-product pairing as the primitive geometric polarization. Omitting the constant sign factor and rescaling Q is an equivalent convention. The full intersection pairing of a K3 surface is not itself a polarization: its real (1,1) space has one positive direction as well as nineteen negative directions.
