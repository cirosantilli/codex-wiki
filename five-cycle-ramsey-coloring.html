Five-cycle Ramsey coloring (R(3,3)>5)

 ... Combinatorics Ramsey theory Ramsey's theorem Finite Ramsey theorem Ramsey number Ramsey number for triangles
OurBigBook.com Words: 112
Place five labeled vertices around a pentagon. In the complete graph K5​, color the five perimeter edges red and the five diagonals blue. Writing ij for the unordered pair {i,j}, the red pairs are 12,23,34,45,15, and the blue pairs are 13,14,24,25,35. This assigns a color to every pair, including every diagonal.
The red subgraph is the cycle graph 1,2,3,4,5,1; the blue subgraph is the cycle graph 1,3,5,2,4,1. Neither contains a triangle in a graph. Consequently, none of the ten triples has all three edges red or all three blue, proving the lower bound for the Ramsey number for triangles. Crossings in the drawing are not additional vertices.
