Baryon-to-photon ratio (η)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology
OurBigBook.com Words: 127 Articles: 2
The baryon-to-photon ratio is the number density of baryons divided by the number density of photons. In the present universe it is of order 10−9.
