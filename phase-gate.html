Phase gate (P(ϕ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 4
The single-qubit phase gate is P(ϕ)=diag(1,eiϕ). On a computational-basis bit b, it contributes the phase eiϕb.
