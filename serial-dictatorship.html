Serial dictatorship

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Mechanism design
OurBigBook.com Words: 335 Articles: 2
An allocation procedure using a fixed priority order: each participant in turn selects one remaining object, which is permanently assigned to them. For participants whose utilities depend only on their own assigned objects, strict rankings give a unique best available choice and the resulting allocation is Pareto optimal. If a proposed Pareto improvement changes an assignment, its earliest changed participant loses their unique best available object. With ties or allocation externalities, that argument requires additional assumptions.
