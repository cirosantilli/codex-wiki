Surface density of a size-sorted dust burst (Σ=π(r02​−r2)[1−η4−α]md​(4−α)​(t/Tr​)4−α)

OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For initial dust mass md​ in a ring at r0​ with a fixed-diameter power-law size distribution of index α<4, burst transport under inverse-size radial drag maps radius to diameter Dr​=2γt/(r02​−r2). Set Tr​=tr​(Dmax​) and η=Dmin​/Dmax​. The surface density is the azimuthal average: 2πrΣdr=(πρK/6)Dr3−α​dDr​. Since dDr​/dr=2rDr​/(r02​−r2), mass normalization of a power-law size distribution gives the displayed expression. It is supported where η≤t/Tr​≤1. It applies at t>0 before removal at the radius in question; the initial state is a delta-function ring. A local angular density needs additional phase information.
