Occultation spectroscopy

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Exoplanet secondary eclipse
OurBigBook.com Words: 85 Articles: 1
The difference between star-plus-planet light outside secondary eclipse and star-only light during eclipse isolates the planet's dayside spectrum. Thermal radiation probes its temperature-pressure structure and opacity, while optical reflected light probes scattering and albedo. Contrast and the subtraction of stellar photons limit sensitivity.
