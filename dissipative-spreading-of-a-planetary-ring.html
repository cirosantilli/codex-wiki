Dissipative spreading of a planetary ring

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Gravitational instability of an astrophysical disk Shearing sheet
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
In a Keplerian shearing sheet, an epicyclic guiding center x0​ carries rotating-frame energy −3Ω2x02​/8, plus nonnegative radial and vertical oscillation energies. Inelastic collisions dissipate the total Jacobi energy in a shearing sheet, while momentum conservation preserves ∑x0​. Thus ∑x02​ must grow: the ring spreads about its fixed mean radius. The effect is an angular momentum transport process, with some particles moving inward and others outward.
