Diamond anvil cell

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure Pressure measurement High-pressure experimental metrology
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 6
A diamond anvil cell compresses a sample between opposed diamond tips. The flat diamond anvil culets load a metal gasket whose hole holds the specimen and a pressure-transmitting medium. Transparent diamond admits excitation and returning fluorescence, enabling an internal ruby pressure sensor.[ref]
The functions in the drawing answer different questions. The drive applies force, the gasket confines the sample, the medium transmits stress, and the ruby pressure scale assigns a local pressure from an optical signal. A large drive reading cannot establish either the sample's pressure or the absence of deviatoric stress. Optical access also permits repeated readings without unloading the sample: a change during a long measurement can be detected rather than silently folded into a material-property curve.
