For an orbit of radius b in the xy plane, a=−bω2(cosωt,sinωt,0). In spherical coordinates, at angle θ from the orbit normal, ⟨(n⋅a)2⟩=b2ω4sin2θ/2. The acceleration covariance in nonrelativistic radiation therefore gives the displayed pattern. Its axial power per solid angle is twice its equatorial value; no observation direction has zero time-averaged power. Integrating gives ⟨P⟩=q2b2ω4/(6πϵ0​c3). The condition bω≪c is essential: relativistic synchrotron radiation has a different beamed pattern.
