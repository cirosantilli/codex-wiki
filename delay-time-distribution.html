Delay-time distribution (Ψ(τ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar population
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The event rate per unit initial stellar mass and per unit delay after star formation. An extended star formation history produces a rate R(t)=∫SFR(t−τ)Ψ(τ)dτ. Measuring rates versus age in old populations constrains progenitor channels only after accounting for stellar lifetimes, age spreads and selection.
