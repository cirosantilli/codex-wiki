Exceptional divisor (E)

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Blowup of a complex manifold at a point
OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 1
The exceptional divisor of the blowup of a complex manifold at a point p is the fiber over p. In complex dimension n it is naturally the projective space P(Tp​X)≅CPn−1 of tangent directions at the center.
