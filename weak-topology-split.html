Weak topology

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 37
The weak topology σ(X,X∗) on a normed space X is the coarsest topology making every member of X∗ continuous. A net converges weakly exactly when every bounded linear functional converges on it.
