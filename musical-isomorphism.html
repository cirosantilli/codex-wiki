Musical isomorphism (♭g​,♯g​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A Riemannian metric gives inverse vector bundle isomorphisms
♭g​:TM→T∗M,v↦g(v,⋅),♯g​:T∗M→TM.
(24)
In coordinates they have matrices gij​ and gij. Every smooth manifold admits a Riemannian metric by a partition of unity, so its tangent and cotangent bundles are isomorphic as real smooth vector bundles. This isomorphism depends on the metric, not merely on the manifold.
