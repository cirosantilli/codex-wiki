Wheel graph (Wn​)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Path in a graph Cycle in a graph Cycle graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A wheel graph is the join of graphs of a cycle graph and a single vertex. Every wheel with a rim of at least three vertices has a K4​ graph minor: partition the rim into three nonempty consecutive connected sets and use its centre as the fourth branch set of a graph minor.
