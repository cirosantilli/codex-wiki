Closed cone of curves (NE(X))

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Cartier divisor Positivity of divisors Numerical equivalence of divisors Real numerical divisor classes
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The closed cone of curves is the closure in N1​(X)R​ of the convex cone generated by classes of integral curves. A nef divisor pairs nonnegatively with this entire closed cone. Its boundary can contain limiting classes which are not represented by one curve.
