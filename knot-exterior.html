Knot exterior (EK​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Knot
OurBigBook.com Words: 431 Articles: 9
The knot exterior is EK​=S3∖intνK, where νK is an open tubular neighborhood of the knot. It is homotopy equivalent to the knot complement S3∖K.
