Stiff fluid (P=ρ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Stress-energy tensor Perfect fluid in general relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 238 Articles: 4
A perfect fluid with equation of state P=ρ is called a stiff fluid. For positive energy density, its constant equation-of-state parameter is w=1. The cosmological continuity equation gives ρ∝a−6. In a flat universe dominated by this fluid, the Friedmann equation then gives a(t)∝(t−tB​)1/3. This statement assumes a nonzero fluid density and negligible other components.
