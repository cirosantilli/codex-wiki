Cauchy-complete category

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Idempotent morphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 1
A category is Cauchy-complete when every idempotent morphism splits: for e:A→A with e2=e, there are r:A→B and i:B→A with ir=e and ri=1B​. Its Karoubi envelope freely supplies these splittings. For a small category, this condition makes every retract of a representable functor representable.
