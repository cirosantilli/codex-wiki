Conditional modus ponens (Γ⊢A→B, A→(B→C)⟹Γ⊢A→C)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Deduction theorem for propositional logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
Conditional modus ponens derives A→C from A→B and A→(B→C). It is the step which translates a modus ponens rule inference when discharging a common assumption. In the case-elimination Hilbert calculus, the third scheme first gives A→(¬B→C); the second scheme combines the two conditionals to give A→C.
