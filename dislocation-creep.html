Dislocation creep

 Home Engineering Materials science Creep (deformation)
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
Dislocation creep is time-dependent crystal deformation through motion and interactions of dislocations. A common constitutive model is ϵ˙=Aσnexp[−(E∗+PV∗)/(RT)] with n>1, giving stress-dependent effective viscosity. Unlike diffusion creep, it often has weak grain-size dependence over the regime being modelled.
