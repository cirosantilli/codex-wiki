Electroweak representation and hypercharge table (Q=T3​+Y)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Standard Model representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
In normalization Q=T3​+Y, the left quark doublet has Y=1/6, right up/down singlets have 2/3,−1/3, the left lepton doublet has −1/2, the right charged lepton has −1, and the Higgs doublet has 1/2. The alternative convention Q=T3​+Y/2 uses Y=2Y. Up-type Yukawa interactions use the conjugate doublet of hypercharge −1/2.
