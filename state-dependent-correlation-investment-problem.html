State-dependent correlation investment problem (ρ=ρ(M))

 ... Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Utility function Expected utility hypothesis Expected utility maximization Investment-consumption problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 91 Articles: 1
When asset correlation varies with a traded market index, the index is both an asset and a state variable. Writing m=logM and optimizing Brownian portfolio exposures accounts for the cross derivative between wealth and m. Power homogeneity reduces the Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation to an ordinary differential equation in m.
