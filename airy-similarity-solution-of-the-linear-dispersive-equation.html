Airy similarity solution of the linear dispersive equation (u(x,t)=t−1/3y(x/(3t)1/3))

Let y solve the Airy ordinary differential equation y′′(s)=sy(s). For t>0, set s=x/(3t)1/3 and define the displayed similarity solution. Differentiating the profile equation gives y′′′=y+sy′, so the chain rule yields
ut​=−31​t−4/3(y+sy′),uxxx​=31​t−4/3y′′′=31​t−4/3(y+sy′).
(27)
Their sum is zero, proving the Airy equation ut​+uxxx​=0. Every real linear combination of the two standard Airy functions gives such a solution. This is a solution of the linear dispersive equation; the nonlinear Korteweg-De Vries equation includes an extra term and does not follow from the same arbitrary profile construction.
