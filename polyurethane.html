Polyurethane

 Home Chemistry Polymer
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A polyurethane contains repeated connections of the form −O−C(=O)−NH−. Suitable hydroxy groups and isocyanate groups can produce those connections by polyaddition; exactly two suitable groups per feed molecule give the bifunctional linear model only when competing reactions and ring closure are excluded.
