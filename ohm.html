Ohm (Ω=V/A)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Electrical resistance and conductance Electrical resistance
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The ohm is the SI unit of electrical resistance, one volt per ampere. A component of electrical resistance 2Ω needs an electric potential difference of 2V to carry electric current 1A under Ohm's law. [ref].
