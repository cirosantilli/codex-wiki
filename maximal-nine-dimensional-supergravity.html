Maximal nine-dimensional supergravity (N=2,32 real supercharges)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supergravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The ungauged maximal nine-dimensional supergravity zero-mode multiplet has one metric, three vectors, two two-forms, one three-form and three scalars, giving 128 bosonic states. Two Majorana gravitini and four Majorana spin-one-half fields give 128 fermionic states. Flat-circle reductions of massless type IIA and type IIB theories, and a flat-two-torus reduction of eleven-dimensional supergravity, give this same nonchiral spectrum.
