The sum of the two modal exponentials supplies a hyperbolic cosine, not its reciprocal. The determinant is e−2γz, and J(z1​+z2​)=J(z1​)J(z2​). Real modal coefficients give circular dichroism; purely imaginary coefficients give lossless circular birefringence. Complex modal labels do not have an intrinsic numerical ordering.
