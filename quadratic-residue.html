Quadratic residue

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 400 Articles: 15
For an odd prime p, a nonzero class a is a quadratic residue when a≡x2(modp). These residues form an index-two subgroup of (Z/pZ)×.
