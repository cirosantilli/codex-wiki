Dense Bogolyubov-Ruzsa lemma

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Bogolyubov lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
For every α>0, if A has density at least α in a cyclic group of prime order, then 2A−2A contains a proper generalized arithmetic progression of rank Oα​(1) and size Ωα​(∣G∣).
