Commutator subgroup

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 295 Articles: 6
The commutator subgroup G′ is generated by all [x,y]=x−1y−1xy. It is characteristic, G/G′ is abelian, and every normal subgroup with abelian quotient contains G′.
