Two-sample exact period recovery (r=N/gcd(N,c1​,c2​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum Fourier transform Quantum period finding Exact period recovery from a Fourier sample
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
Suppose a function has least period r∣N and distinct values on distinct period cosets. Two independent Fourier samples have the form cj​=sj​N/r with uniform sj​∈Zr​. The displayed candidate is r/gcd(r,s1​,s2​), so exact recovery occurs when no prime divisor of r divides both residues. The Chinese remainder theorem gives probability ∏ℓ∣r​(1−ℓ−2)≥1/ζ(2)=6/π2, using the Euler product. Unlike a single-sample coprimality probability, this bound is uniformly greater than one half. A candidate-period test makes successful recovery heralded when such a test is available.
