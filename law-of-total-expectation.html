Law of total expectation (E[E[X∣G]]=E[X])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Conditional expectation
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
Taking expectation after conditioning recovers the original expectation. More generally, if H⊆G, then
E[E[X∣G]∣H]=E[X∣H].
