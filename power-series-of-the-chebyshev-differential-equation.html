Power series of the Chebyshev differential equation (an+2​=(n+2)(n+1)n2−k2​an​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Chebyshev polynomial Chebyshev differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
For the Chebyshev differential equation (1−x2)y′′−xy′+k2y=0, a power series about the ordinary point zero splits into even and odd solutions with the displayed recurrence relation. Every nonterminating parity series has radius of convergence one. If k2=m2 for a nonnegative integer m, the parity series containing xm terminates, giving a polynomial with infinite radius of convergence; the other independent series still has radius one. Normalized solutions are cos(karcsinx) and sin(karcsinx)/k, with the latter replaced by arcsinx when k=0.
