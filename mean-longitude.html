Mean longitude (λ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Kepler orbit Orbital element
OurBigBook.com Words: 17
Mean longitude is an angle that advances uniformly at the mean motion in an unperturbed Kepler orbit.
