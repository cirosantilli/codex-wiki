Gauge covariant derivative (Dμ​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field
OurBigBook.com Words: 297 Articles: 6
A gauge covariant derivative combines an ordinary derivative with a gauge field so that differentiating a field preserves its gauge-transformation law. In a matrix representation one common convention is Dμ​=∂μ​−igAμ​.
