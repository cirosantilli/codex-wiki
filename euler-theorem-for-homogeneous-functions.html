Euler theorem for homogeneous functions (∑i​xi​∂i​f=kf)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Homogeneous function
OurBigBook.com Words: 214 Articles: 2
Let f be a differentiable function on an open domain stable under positive rescaling, with f(λx)=λkf(x) for λ>0. The chain rule differentiates this homogeneous function identity at λ=1 to give
∑i​xi​∂i​f(x)=kf(x).
(2)
If f is twice differentiable, differentiating once more gives ∑i​xi​∂i​∂j​f=(k−1)∂j​f. In degree one its Hessian matrix therefore annihilates the radial vector x. This is the identity used in the no-scale identity from degree-one homogeneity. For an extensive quantity, the degree-one formula also supplies the thermodynamic identity behind the Gibbs-Duhem equation.
