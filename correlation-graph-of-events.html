Correlation graph of events

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Probabilistic combinatorics Lovász local lemma Lopsided Lovász local lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For bad events Ai​, a correlation graph is sufficient for the Lopsided Lovász local lemma when, for every set S of nonneighbors and every positive-probability conditioning event, P(Ai​∣⋂j∈S​Ajc​)≤P(Ai​). A dependency graph of events satisfies this with equality. The condition concerns simultaneous avoidance, not just signs of pairwise correlations.
