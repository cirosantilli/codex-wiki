Banach limit (L:ℓ∞(N;R)→R)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Normed vector space Banach space l-infinity sequence space
OurBigBook.com Words: 196 Articles: 2
A Banach limit is a positive linear functional on the real l-infinity sequence space that extends the ordinary limit on the convergent sequence space, has operator norm one, and is invariant under the left shift on bounded sequences. The Hahn-Banach theorem constructs one by separating the constant sequence 1 from the subspace (I−S)ℓ∞, whose distance from 1 is one. The functional is generally not unique.
