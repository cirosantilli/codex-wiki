Three-mode porous magnetoconvection (a˙=σ(r−1)a−σζqb,b˙=a−ζb−ac,c˙=−ζc+3ab)

A Galerkin method using one convective streamfunction mode and two Cartesian magnetic flux function modes yields the displayed system. With α=π/3​, use ψ=42​asin(αx)sin(πz) and A=(32​/π)bsin(αx)cos(πz)+(3​/π)csin(2αx), time τ=(4π2/3)t, q=9Q/(16π2) and r=9R/(64π4). The origin's planar trace is σ(r−1)−ζ and determinant σζ(1+q−r); the third eigenvalue is −ζ. The stationary threshold is r=1+q, and the oscillatory threshold is r=1+ζ/σ when q>ζ/σ. They meet in a symmetric double-zero point at (q,r)=(ζ/σ,1+ζ/σ). Higher spatial harmonics generated by the quadratic terms are discarded, so this is a projected approximation rather than an exact invariant subspace of the original equations.
