Centrifugal force (−mΩ×(Ω×r))

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Reference frame Rotating reference frame
OurBigBook.com Words: 37 Articles: 1
In a rotating reference frame, this apparent force acts away from the rotation axis. The corresponding centrifugal acceleration is the force per unit mass.
