Equality-constrained convex optimization

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
An equality-constrained convex optimization problem has the form
minx​f(x)subject to Ax=b,
(35)
where f is convex and the constraint is affine. Its stationarity and feasibility equations can be combined into a monotone primal-dual operator.
