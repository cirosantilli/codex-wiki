Planetary ice shell

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics
OurBigBook.com Words: 7k Articles: 125
A planetary ice shell is a solid water-rich layer overlying an ocean or deeper planetary interior. Its thickness can be controlled by thermal conduction, basal heat flux, surface radiation, and solid-state mantle convection.
