Cramér-Lundberg workload exponent (ΛA​(θ∗​)=cθ∗​,θ∗​=inft>0​tIA​(c+1/t))

For nondegenerate independent and identically distributed arrivals with mean below c, suppose the positive root is interior to the moment-generating function domain. Then ΛA′​(θ∗​)>c. The Fenchel–Young inequality gives tIA​(c+1/t)≥θ∗​ for every t>0, and equality occurs at t=[ΛA′​(θ∗​)−c]−1. The exponential workload bound from cumulative arrival moments gives the upper logarithmic tail bound for every θ<θ∗​. A horizon of the indicated length times buffer size gives the matching lower bound by Cramér's theorem. A positive interior root is an assumption; if arrivals never exceed capacity, positive workload cannot arise.
