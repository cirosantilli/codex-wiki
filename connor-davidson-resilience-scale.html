Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale

 Home Science Psychology Clinical psychology Psychological resilience Measurement of psychological resilience
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A self-report measure of coping-related qualities developed by Kathryn M. Connor and Jonathan R. T. Davidson. The original version has 25 items scored from zero to four. Measurement reliability and treatment-associated change concern the measured self-reports; they do not substitute for prospective evidence of adaptation to a documented adversity.[ref]
