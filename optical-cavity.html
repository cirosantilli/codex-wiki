Optical cavity

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument
OurBigBook.com Words: 477 Articles: 7
An optical cavity supports optical fields whose phases and spatial profiles reproduce themselves after a round trip through its reflecting or guiding elements. Its resonance conditions include propagation and reflection phases. A cavity bounded by two plane mirrors is a simple example; Fabry–Pérot resonance spacing describes the frequency intervals of an ideal two-mirror arrangement.
