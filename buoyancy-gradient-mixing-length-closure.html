Buoyancy-gradient mixing-length closure (K∼d2Gz​​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Eddy diffusion
OurBigBook.com Words: 172 Articles: 2
For positive dense-fluid reduced gravity G and upward coordinate z, an unstable gradient Gz​>0 supplies buoyant work of order d2Gz​ over mixing length d. velocity u∼dGz​​ and eddy diffusivity K∼ud follow. Order-one constants depend on the turbulence model. This closure is for an unstable gradient; its square root must not be applied to a stable negative gradient.
