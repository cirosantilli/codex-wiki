Seismic layer transfer matrix (Mj​=(cosδj​iqj​sinδj​​isinδj​/qj​cosδj​​))

At normal incidence, a uniform lossless elastic wave layer of thickness hj​, speed cj​ and seismic impedance qj​=ρj​cj​ has wave phase thickness δj​=ωhj​/cj​. With phasors proportional to e−iωt, particle velocity V and compressive traction P=−T, the displayed matrix maps (V,P) from the top to the bottom. It has determinant one. Multiplying these matrices in physical order accounts for all coherent multiple reflections. Continuity of V,P connects adjacent layers without another propagation factor.
