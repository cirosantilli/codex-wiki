Reactive strategy (game theory, (p,q)=(CC​,CD​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Prisoner's dilemma Iterated prisoner's dilemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 349 Articles: 7
A reactive strategy cooperates with probability p after the opponent cooperated and probability q after the opponent defected. It ignores its own previous action. Independent behavioural randomization gives a four-state joint-action Markov chain. The reduced marginal recurrences depend on the prescribed initial action distribution.
