Antibody affinity (Kd​)

 Home Biology Protein Antibody
OurBigBook.com Words: 537 Articles: 2
Antibody affinity concerns one paratope binding one epitope. A Fab fragment supplies a single-site comparison. [ref] In the dilute chemical equilibrium model below, smaller dissociation constant Kd​ means larger antibody equilibrium occupancy at the same free antigen concentration. State the antigen, temperature and buffer solution: a dissociation constant is not a context-free score. It does not alone specify how fast attachment or detachment occurs.
