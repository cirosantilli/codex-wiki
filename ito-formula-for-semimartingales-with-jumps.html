Itô formula for semimartingales with jumps

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Itô's lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
For a semimartingale with càdlàg paths X and f∈C2, the Itô formula uses the left-limit gradient in ∫f′(Xs−​)dXs​, the continuous quadratic variation in its second-order integral, and the jump correction ∑s≤t​[f(Xs​)−f(Xs−​)−f′(Xs−​)ΔXs​]. In several dimensions use the corresponding gradient, Hessian and continuous bracket matrix. The correction is the part of each jump not captured by first-order linearization.
