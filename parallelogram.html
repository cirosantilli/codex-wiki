Parallelogram

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Polygon Quadrilateral
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
A parallelogram is the convex set with vertices p,p+u,p+v,p+u+v for two linearly independent planar vectors u,v. Its opposite edges are parallel and its centre p+(u+v)/2 gives central symmetry. Its area is ∣det[u,v]∣, so an invertible linear map changes its area by the absolute value of that map's determinant. These facts make parallelograms convenient convex bodies for two-dimensional applications of the Minkowski convex body theorem.
