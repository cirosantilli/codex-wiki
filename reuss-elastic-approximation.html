Reuss elastic approximation (WR​=(⟨W∗⟩)∗)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Composite material Effective elastic energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
The Reuss elastic approximation assumes uniform stress. The local strain is the derivative of the convex conjugate W∗(S,x); its mean is D⟨W∗⟩(S). Under invertible differentiable duality, this mean determines S=DWR​(E). For positive linear elasticity, CR​=⟨C−1⟩−1. Uniform stress is generally not accompanied by a compatible local displacement field, so this approximation is distinguished from an exact heterogeneous solution.
