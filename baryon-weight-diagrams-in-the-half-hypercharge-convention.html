Baryon weight diagrams in the half-hypercharge convention

 ... Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Flavor symmetry Flavor hypercharge Half-hypercharge convention
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
The baryon octet has rows Y=1/2,0,−1/2 with isospins 1/2, 1⊕0, 1/2. The central weight has two states, Σ0 and Λ0. The baryon decuplet has rows Y=1/2,0,−1/2,−1 with isospins 3/2,1,1/2,0. The three-quark flavour singlet has one state at the origin. Both abstract octet copies in 3⊗3 share the same weights.
