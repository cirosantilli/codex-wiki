Congestion control

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory Stochastic network
OurBigBook.com Words: 972 Articles: 14
Congestion control adjusts user transmission rates in response to load signals from shared resources. A resource can compute its price from local aggregate load, while each user sums prices along its route. Primal congestion potential and dual congestion potential formulations explain equilibrium and stability for suitable monotone response functions.
