Magnetic helicity (H=∫V​A⋅BdV,B=∇×A)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 399 Articles: 4
The magnetic helicity integrates the dot product of a magnetic vector potential and its magnetic field. It measures linkage and twisting of magnetic flux. For a solenoidal vector field B tangent to the boundary, it is unchanged by the gauge transformation A↦A+∇χ, since the change is ∫V​∇⋅(χB)dV=∫∂V​χB⋅dS=0. If its density satisfies ∂t​h=∇⋅(fB−hu) with both vector fields tangent to the boundary of a fixed volume, the divergence theorem gives conservation of its integral. This is an instance of helicity conservation by tangent boundary conditions.
