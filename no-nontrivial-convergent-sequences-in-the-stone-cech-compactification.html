No nontrivial convergent sequences in the Stone-Čech compactification

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Filter (set theory) Ultrafilter Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
Every convergent sequence in the Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers is eventually constant. If distinct ultrafilters Un​ converged, isolate each from the other sequence terms using convergence and finitely many separating clopen sets. Finite disjointification gives disjoint Bn​∈Un​. The union of the even-indexed Bn​ then belongs exactly to the even-indexed ultrafilters, contradicting convergence of membership in a clopen set.
