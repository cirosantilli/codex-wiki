Positive-diagonal QR decomposition preserves a complex structure

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Inner product Gram-Schmidt process QR decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 94
Let K be block diagonal with 2×2 blocks J=(0−1​10​). If a real invertible matrix A commutes with K, its QR decomposition A=BC, with B orthogonal and C upper triangular with positive diagonal, has both BK=KB and CK=KC. Indeed CKC−1=B−1KB, so E=K−1CKC−1 is orthogonal and upper triangular in 2×2 blocks. Orthogonality forces its off-diagonal blocks to vanish. Its diagonal blocks are J−1Ci​JCi−1​. For Ci​=(a0​bd​) with a,d>0, this block is (d/a−b/a​−b/a(a2+b2)/(ad)​). Orthogonality of its columns forces b=0,d=a, and hence each block is the identity. Thus E=I, proving the claim.
