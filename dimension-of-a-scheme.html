Dimension of a scheme (dimX)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The dimension of a scheme is the supremum of the lengths of strict chains of irreducible closed subsets of its underlying topological space. On an affine scheme it is the Krull dimension of its coordinate ring. Taking the reduced subscheme leaves the dimension unchanged.
