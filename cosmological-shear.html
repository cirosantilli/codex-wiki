Cosmological shear

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Spatial homogeneity
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The traceless symmetric part of the galaxy velocity-gradient matrix changes shape without isotropic expansion. An antisymmetric part instead represents rigid rotation. A matrix proportional to the identity plus an antisymmetric matrix has no shear.
