For a three-dimensional isotropic comparison material, the contraction of its elastic strain Green operator is the displayed distributional derivative. Its double trace is Γiikk​=δ/L, because Δ∣x∣−1=−4πδ. Away from zero the contraction is (δij​∣x∣2−3xi​xj​)/(4πL∣x∣5); the full distribution also includes δij​δ/(3L). This contact term must not be discarded when computing hydrostatic strain.
