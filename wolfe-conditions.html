Wolfe conditions

For a continuously differentiable function, a descent direction s and 0<β1​<β2​<1, the Wolfe conditions require both the Armijo condition and
∇f(x+αs)Ts≥β2​∇f(x)Ts.
(4)
This second inequality is the weak curvature condition: the slope must have become less negative. The strong variant instead requires ∣∇f(x+αs)Ts∣≤β2​∣∇f(x)Ts∣. The weak version already guarantees a positive curvature pair for the Quasi-Newton secant equation.
