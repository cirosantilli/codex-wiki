Neighbour of a vertex

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Graph neighbourhood
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
A neighbour of a vertex v in a graph is a vertex joined to v by an edge. These vertices form the graph neighbourhood N(v).
