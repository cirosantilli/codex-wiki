Rank-one elliptic curve with coefficients eight and minus seven (E:y2=x3+8x2−7x)

The two-isogeny formula gives the partner E′:y2=x3−16x2+92x. The two-torsion square-class homomorphism image for E is {1,−7}: prime support leaves only {1,−1,7,−7}, the modulo-sixteen obstruction for the negative unit descent class excludes −1, and the subgroup property excludes 7. For E′, (x−8)2+28>0 forces nonnegative x, so the image is contained in {1,2,23,46}. The two-torsion point realizes 23 and the rational point (18,48) realizes 2, giving all four classes. The two-isogeny rank formula yields 2r=2⋅4/4=2, hence rank one. The dual isogeny sends (18,48) to (16/9,116/27); this statement does not assert that the resulting point is a generator of the full free part.
