Binary family of nonhomeomorphic Sunada quotients (2n examples in dimension 4)

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Spectral geometry Isospectral manifolds Sunada theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
Choose distinct odd primes and take products of the nonisomorphic Gassmann equivalent regular subgroups, selecting one of the two groups at each prime. Product conjugacy-class counts give 2n Gassmann equivalent subgroups of the same finite product of symmetric groups. Their fundamental groups are distinguished by whether the unique Sylow subgroup at each chosen prime is abelian. The closed-manifold realization of a finitely presented fundamental group and Sunada theorem therefore produce 2n pairwise nonhomeomorphic, and hence nonisometric, isospectral closed four-manifolds. Gassmann equivalence alone, without a topology distinction, does not imply nonhomeomorphism.
