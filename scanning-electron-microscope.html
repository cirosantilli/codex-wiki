Scanning electron microscope

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Electron optics Electron microscope
OurBigBook.com Words: 365 Articles: 10
A scanning electron microscope scans a focused electron probe across a specimen and assigns a detector signal to each probe position. Secondary electrons leaving the irradiated region often reveal surface relief; backscattered electrons can provide composition-dependent contrast. Image brightness records the selected signal, rather than a transmitted projection enlarged by two lenses. Display enlargement is therefore set by the ratio of display width to scanned specimen width. Surface geometry and composition both affect brightness.[ref]
