Constant score baseline (Eq​[(h−b)∇logq]=Eq​[h∇logq])

OurBigBook.com Words: 101
For a normalized differentiable sampling law on fixed support, Eq​[∇logq]=∇∫q=0. Consequently any deterministic scalar b, independent of the sampled value, can be subtracted from the reward in a score-function gradient estimator without changing its expectation. This is an identity for the estimator, not a licence to differentiate an added parameter-dependent objective without its other terms. For a scalar parameter and score S with E[S2]>0, minimizing the estimator's variance gives b∗=E[hS2]/E[S2], provided these moments exist: expand E[(h−b)2S2] and differentiate the quadratic in b. An arbitrary baseline need not reduce variance.[ref]
