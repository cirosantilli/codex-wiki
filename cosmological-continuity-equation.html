Cosmological continuity equation (ρ˙​+3H(ρ+P)=0)

In natural units, conservation of the stress-energy tensor for a homogeneous perfect fluid in an FLRW metric gives the displayed equation for its energy density and pressure. It is equivalent to d(ρa3)=−Pd(a3). For a constant equation-of-state parameter w=P/ρ, separation gives ρ∝a−3(1+w). If ρ instead denotes mass density, the pressure term is P/c2.
This is local stress-energy conservation; it does not in general define a globally conserved total energy for an expanding universe. The adiabatic work in a comoving volume makes this distinction explicit.
