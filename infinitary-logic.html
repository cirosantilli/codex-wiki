Infinitary logic

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 207 Articles: 5
Infinitary logic permits infinitary syntactic operations, such as infinite conjunctions, together with an interpretation of its formulas. The infinitary language Lκ,λ​ specifies the permitted conjunction and quantifier lengths.
