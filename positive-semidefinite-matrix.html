Positive semidefinite matrix

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Quadratic form Definite matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 302 Articles: 9
A real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix A is positive semidefinite when x†Ax≥0 for every vector x, equivalently when all its eigenvalues are nonnegative.
