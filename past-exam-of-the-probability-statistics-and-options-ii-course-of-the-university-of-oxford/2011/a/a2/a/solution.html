A splitting field of f over F is an extension field in which f factors into linear factors and which is generated over F by those roots. The generation requirement excludes unnecessary extra elements.
For existence, induct on the degree. An irreducible factor p of f gives a field F1​=F[X]/(p), since an irreducible polynomial generates a maximal ideal in the polynomial ring over a field. The class α of X is a root of p and hence of f. By the factor theorem, f=(X−α)g over F1​, with degg=degf−1. Induction gives a splitting field of g over F1​. It contains all roots of f; taking the subfield generated by these roots over F gives exactly a splitting field of f. The degree-one case is immediate. Thus
Every positive-degree polynomial over a field has a splitting field.​
