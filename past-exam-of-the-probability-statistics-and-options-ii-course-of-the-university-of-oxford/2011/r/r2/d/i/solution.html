Root the tree at any vertex and order vertices so every parent precedes its children. The root has k colour choices. Each of the other n−1 vertices has exactly one earlier adjacent vertex, its parent, and therefore exactly k−1 choices. There are no other restrictions because a tree has no cycles and no extra edges between those branches. Multiplication yields
PG​(k)=k(k−1)n−1.​

The formula also includes the one-vertex tree.
