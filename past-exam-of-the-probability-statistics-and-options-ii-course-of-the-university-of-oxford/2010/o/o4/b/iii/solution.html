Under the null put p=q=r. The likelihood becomes the same two binomial constants times rm+n(1−r)2M+2N−m−n. Maximization gives the pooled estimate
r^=2(M+N)m+n​.​
(57)
Under an interior null, (ii) has zero mean and variance r(1−r)(1/(2M)+1/(2N)). Statistical consistency of r^ and Slutsky's theorem permit replacement of r by r^ in the denominator, giving the stated Z⇒N(0,1). For a two-sided level-α test, reject when ∣Z∣>Φ−1(1−α/2)​. The large-sample approximation requires both expected counts of each type to grow; if all observed alleles are identical the stated denominator degenerates and this approximation is not applicable.
