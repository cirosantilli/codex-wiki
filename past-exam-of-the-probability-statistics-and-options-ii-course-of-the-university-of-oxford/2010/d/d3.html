The monotone convergence theorem states that if nonnegative measurable functions hn​ increase almost everywhere to h, then ∫h=limn​∫hn​, allowing infinity. The dominated convergence theorem states that if measurable hn​→h almost everywhere and ∣hn​∣≤g for an integrable g, then h is integrable and ∫hn​→∫h.
