Regard K as a vector space over F. Since K is finite, a maximal linearly independent list has finite length d and is a basis. Every element is uniquely a linear combination of its d basis vectors, with q choices for each coefficient. Hence ∣K∣=qd​, with d=[K:F]. This includes d=1 when the extension is trivial.
