For n=1 there are no edges. Every finite tree with at least two vertices has a leaf of a graph: an endpoint of a longest simple path cannot have another neighbor, since that would either create a cycle or extend the path. Delete that leaf of a graph and its sole edge. The remaining graph is still a tree, so induction gives n−2 remaining edges and n−1​ original edges. Alternatively the decoding construction in (i) inserts exactly n−2 edges and then the final edge.
