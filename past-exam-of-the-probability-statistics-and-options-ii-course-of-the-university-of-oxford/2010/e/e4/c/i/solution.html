Yes. A Cauchy sequence of continuous functions in the supremum norm is Cauchy in the given complete space of bounded functions, so it has a uniform limit there. A uniform limit of continuous functions is continuous: at a point, use two uniform errors smaller than ε/3 and continuity of one sufficiently late term for the remaining error. Thus the limit is in C([0,1]), proving completeness. Equivalently this is a closed subspace of the given complete space.
