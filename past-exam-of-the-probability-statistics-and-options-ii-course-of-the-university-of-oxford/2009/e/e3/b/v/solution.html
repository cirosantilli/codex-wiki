An isolated point outside B cannot belong to its closure. Thus the only possible extra closure point is a. The balls at a meet B exactly when arbitrarily small original-metric balls at a do so. Consequently
Bda​=⎩⎨⎧​B,B,B∪{a},​a∈B,a∈/B, d(a,B)>0,a∈/B, d(a,B)=0.​​
(12)
In the first case a is already included. In the second case a ball smaller than the positive distance separates it from B; in the last case every ball meets B. If B is empty its closure is empty, with distance convention d(a,∅)=+∞.
