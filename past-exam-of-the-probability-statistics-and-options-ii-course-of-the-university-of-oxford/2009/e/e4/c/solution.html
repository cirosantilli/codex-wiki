The difference map (z,w)↦z−w is continuous and X is its inverse image of the open set C∖{0}, so X is open in C2. For fixed z, the equivalence class is {z}×(C∖{z}), homeomorphic to a punctured plane. It is path-connected: two points can be joined by a polygonal path avoiding the missing point. Thus all equivalence classes are connected.
The projection π is continuous and onto, since (z,z+1)∈X. If U is open in X, it is open in C2 because X is open. Product-coordinate projection is open: around any (z,w)∈U there is a product of open disks contained in U, whose first disk is a neighborhood inside π(U). Hence π(U) is open.
Its fibers are precisely the equivalence classes, so it induces a bijection πˉ:X/R→C. Continuity follows from the quotient topology and π=πˉ∘q. If V is open in X/R, then q−1(V) is open in X and πˉ(V)=π(q−1(V)) is open. Thus the inverse is continuous too, proving X/R≅C​ as topological spaces. The plane is connected, so the connected-fiber equivalence in (b)(ii) gives X is connected​.
