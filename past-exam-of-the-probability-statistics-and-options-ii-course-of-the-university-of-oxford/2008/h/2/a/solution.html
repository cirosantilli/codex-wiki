Measure the axial coordinate z from the vertex. The slice radius is ztanα and its mass is dm=3mz2H−3dz, since cross-sectional area is proportional to z2. Its axial moment is 21​(ztanα)2dm. Therefore
CO​=103mH2​tan2α.​
(30)
For an axis through the vertex perpendicular to the cone axis, a thin disc contributes [z2+41​z2tan2α]dm: the second term is its diametral moment. Integration gives the two equal transverse principal moments
AO​=BO​=53mH2​(1+4tan2α​).​
(31)
Rotational symmetry eliminates the mixed moments and makes these the principal axes. The centre of mass is at 3H/4, so the parallel-axis theorem would give AG​=3mH2/80+3mH2tan2α/20 and CG​=CO​.
