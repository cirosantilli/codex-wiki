OurBigBook.com Words: 76
Take a shortest allowed simple graph path. If it does not use k as an intermediate vertex, its length is at least uij(k−1)​. If it does, simplicity makes k occur only once. Splitting there gives an i--k path and a k--j path whose intermediate vertices are in {1,…,k−1}. Their lengths are at least the respective two minima. In either case,
uij(k)​≥min{uij(k−1)​,uik(k−1)​+ukj(k−1)​}.​
(74)
For identical endpoints use the usual empty path of length zero.
