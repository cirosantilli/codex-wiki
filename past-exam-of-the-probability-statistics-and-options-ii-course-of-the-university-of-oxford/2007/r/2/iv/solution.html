Take a root r with children a,b. Give a the children c,1, give c the children 2,3, and give b the children 4,5. The root of a tree has degree two, the three vertices a,b,c have degree three, and 1,…,5 are the five leaves of a graph. There are nine vertices and eight edges, as required by the handshaking lemma and the edge count for a tree.
