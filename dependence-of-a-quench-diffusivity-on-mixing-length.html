Dependence of a quench diffusivity on mixing length (Kzz​≳ℓ2/τchem​)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Disequilibrium chemistry in an exoplanet atmosphere Chemical quench level
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For a vertical eddy diffusivity Kzz​, mixing over a length ℓ takes τmix​∼ℓ2/Kzz​. The condition that transport outrun a chemical relaxation time therefore gives Kzz​≳ℓ2/τchem​. Taking ℓ=0.1H instead of one atmospheric scale height H changes this estimate by a factor of one hundred. The atmospheric scale height itself depends on both temperature and gravity.
