Modal energy of a string (En​=μLωn2​(An2​+Bn2​)/4)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Wave equation on a string
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 1
For a uniform fixed-end mechanical string of length L, linear mass density μ and wave speed c, let its nth normal mode be yn​=[An​cosωn​t+Bn​sinωn​t]sin(nπx/L), where ωn​=cnπ/L. The conserved energy is the sum of kinetic energy and elastic potential energy,
En​=2μ​∫0L​[(yn​)t2​+c2(yn​)x2​]dx.
(37)
The sine and cosine squared integrals are L/2, so substitution proves the title formula. Orthogonality makes energies of distinct normal modes add. For zero initial displacement and initial modal velocity Vn​, An​=0 and Bn​=Vn​/ωn​, giving En​=μLVn2​/4.
