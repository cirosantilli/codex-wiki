Cohomology growth for nef twists (hi(X,F(mN))=O(md−i))

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Sheaf cohomology Serre vanishing Fujita vanishing
OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 1
Let N be a nef Cartier divisor and let the coherent sheaf F have support dimension d. Then hi(X,F(mN))=O(md−i) for 1≤i≤d. This does not require reducedness or smoothness.
Choose a very ample A and fix a sufficiently large for Fujita vanishing. Choose a section of aA avoiding the associated points of F. Its multiplication gives 0→F→F(aA)→Q→0, where Q has support dimension at most d−1. After tensoring with OX​(mN), the middle term has zero higher sheaf cohomology, uniformly in m. Hence hi(F(mN))≤hi−1(Q(mN)). Induct on support dimension: for i=1 use the polynomial bound for sections of a fixed divisor, and for i>1 use the induction hypothesis. Degree greater than d vanishes by Grothendieck vanishing.
