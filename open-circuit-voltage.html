Open-circuit voltage (Voc​)

 Home Electrical engineering Electrical measurement
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
The terminal electric potential difference with zero external electric current, when a finite value exists. A reading from a finite-input-electrical resistance voltmeter approximates it subject to voltmeter loading.
