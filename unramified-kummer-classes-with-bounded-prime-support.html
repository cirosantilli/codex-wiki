Unramified Kummer classes with bounded prime support (K(S,m)={[a]:vp​(a)≡0(modm) outside S})

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Kummer theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For a number field K, a finite set S and m≥2, let K(S,m) consist of classes [a]∈K∗/K∗m whose valuations outside S are divisible by m. It fits into 0→OK,S×​/(OK,S×​)m→K(S,m)→Cl(OK,S​)[m]→0. The S-unit group is finitely generated and the ideal class group is finite, so K(S,m) is finite. When S contains primes dividing m, this controls unramified multiplicative Kummer classes outside S.
