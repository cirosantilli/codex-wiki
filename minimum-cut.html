Minimum cut

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Flow network Cut of a flow network
OurBigBook.com Words: 206 Articles: 1
A minimum cut is a cut of a flow network of least cut capacity for the prescribed source s and sink t. The max-flow min-cut theorem equates that least cut capacity with the greatest flow value.[ref]
To certify a particular minimum cut, exhibit a feasible flow whose flow value equals its cut capacity. Every feasible flow is bounded above by every cut capacity, so this single equality proves both optimalities.[ref]
In the running example, the four possible source sides {s}, {s,a}, {s,b} and {s,a,b} have cut capacities 5,5,6,5, respectively. The feasible flow of flow value 5 therefore certifies three different minimum cuts. A minimum cut need not be unique; the optimum cut capacity is the same for all of them. Merely saturating many edges does not give this certificate: flow conservation and the particular cut of a flow network must also be checked.
