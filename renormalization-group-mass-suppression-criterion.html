Renormalization-group mass suppression criterion (∫0t​(δ(g(u))−1)du→−∞)

OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The effective dimensionless mass is e−tm(t)/μ. It vanishes precisely when this integrated exponent tends to minus infinity, for positive initial mass. An eventual bound δ≤1−η is sufficient. Replacing the massive correlator by its massless limit additionally requires regularity uniform along the coupling trajectory.
