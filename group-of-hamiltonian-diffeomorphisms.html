Group of Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms (Ham(M,ω))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Symplectomorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 421 Articles: 6
This group consists of time-one maps of Hamiltonian isotopies whose flows are defined throughout the specified time interval. With ιXH​​ω=−dH, composition of isotopies generated by Ht​,Kt​ has generator Ht​+Kt​∘ϕt−1​, where ϕt​ is the first isotopy. The inverse path has generator −Ht​∘ϕt​. These formulas prove closure under composition and inversion. On a closed symplectic manifold, every smooth time-dependent Hamiltonian has a complete flow over a bounded time interval.
