Diode minority-carrier diffusion current (IS​=eAni2​[Dn​/(Ln​NA​)+Dp​/(Lp​ND​)])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Diode Shockley diode equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For a long, nondegenerate, ideal p-n junction under low injection, the diode saturation current is set by minority carriers supplied from the neutral regions. Their equilibrium densities are np0​=ni2​/NA​ and pn0​=ni2​/ND​. Solving the steady diffusion and recombination equations gives IS​=eA(Dn​np0​/Ln​+Dp​pn0​/Lp​), where A is junction area, Dn​,Dp​ are diffusion coefficients, and Ln​,Lp​ are diffusion lengths. Reverse bias suppresses opposite majority-carrier injection exponentially while collecting this minority supply. Thus IS​ is approximately bias independent before breakdown, but depends strongly on temperature through ni2​ and on transport, doping, and geometry.
