Exponential tightness

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Convergence of random variables Large deviation principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
A family with large-deviation speed aN​ is exponentially tight if for every M>0 there is a compact set KM​ such that
limsupN​aN−1​logP(XN​∈/KM​)≤−M.
(18)
Local upper bounds then imply the closed-set upper bound of a large deviation principle: intersect the closed set with KM​, cover that compact intersection by finitely many neighborhoods on which the local bounds hold, and use a finite union bound. The remaining probability has exponent at most −M; let M→∞.
