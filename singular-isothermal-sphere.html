Singular isothermal sphere (SIS)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 142 Articles: 3
A singular isothermal sphere with one-dimensional velocity dispersion σ has ρ(r)=σ2/(2πGr2), enclosed mass M(<r)=2σ2r/G, and constant circular speed 2​σ.
