One-way normal linear model

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Normal linear model
OurBigBook.com Words: 397 Articles: 10
A one-way normal model assigns each factor level its own mean while assuming independent Gaussian errors with a common variance.
This Gaussian model is the setting for a one-way instance of analysis of variance.
