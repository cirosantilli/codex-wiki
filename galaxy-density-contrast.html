Galaxy density contrast (δg​=(ng​−nˉg​)/nˉg​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Galaxy clustering
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
The galaxy density contrast measures a fractional departure of galaxy number density from its ensemble or survey mean. Linear galaxy bias relates its large-scale three-dimensional component to the matter density contrast by δg​=bg​δm​. An angular sample averages this field with its normalized redshift selection; a probability density p(z) per unit cosmological redshift corresponds to a distance kernel H(z)p(z)/c in a spatially flat background.
