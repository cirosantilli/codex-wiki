Blowup of a complex manifold at a point

 ... Geometry and topology Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 219 Articles: 7
The blowup replaces a point of a complex n-manifold by the projective space of complex tangent directions CPn−1. A biholomorphism carrying one center to another lifts to a biholomorphism of their blowups.
