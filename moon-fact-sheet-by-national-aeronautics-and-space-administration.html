nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html
The bulk-parameter table gives the Moon's volumetric mean radius as 1737.4 km and solar irradiance as 1361Wm−2. It separately lists Bond albedo 0.11 and geometric albedo 0.12; these are different measures of reflection.
