Word normalization proof of monoidal coherence

Associate to a finite ordered word w the right-associated tensor N(w), retaining a terminal unit: N(∅)=I and N(Aw)=A⊗N(w). Define concatenation maps recursively by
c∅,v​=λN(v)​,cAu,v​=(1A​⊗cu,v​)aA,N(u),N(v)​.

The unit identities derived from the monoidal pentagon and triangle give cu,∅​=ρN(u)​. Induction on u, using the pentagon in the induction step and left-unitor compatibility in the base case, gives
cuv,w​(cu,v​⊗1)=cu,vw​(1⊗cv,w​)aN(u),N(v),N(w)​.
(5)
Normalize a letter by ρA−1​, a formal unit by 1I​, and a tensor expression recursively using c. The displayed identities show that normalization commutes with each generating associator or unitor, and therefore also with inverses, tensor products and composites of them. Every structural arrow from expression S to expression T is consequently νT−1​νS​, proving monoidal coherence without presupposing it.
