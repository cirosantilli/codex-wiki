Turning level of a stationary internal wave (m2(zt​)=0)

 ... Gravity wave Internal wave Atmospheric internal gravity wave Taylor–Goldstein equation Scorer parameter Vertical trapping of an atmospheric gravity wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A vertically propagating stationary topographic internal gravity wave meets a turning level when its squared vertical wavenumber changes from positive to negative. The stationary internal-wave WKB solution then fails locally. A simple zero is resolved by an Airy function transition connecting incident and reflected waves below to a decaying evanescent wave above. For negligible wind curvature the condition is ∣kU(zt​)∣=N(zt​), distinct from the zero-intrinsic-frequency critical layer in a shear flow.
