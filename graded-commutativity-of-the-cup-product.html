Graded commutativity of the cup product (a⌣b=(−1)∣a∣∣b∣b⌣a)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Cohomology Cup product
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
Homogeneous cohomology classes satisfy
a⌣b=(−1)∣a∣∣b∣b⌣a.
(18)
In particular, an odd-degree class has square killed by two, and its square vanishes whenever two is invertible in the coefficient ring.
