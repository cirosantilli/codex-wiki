Coadjoint orbit

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Lie group action Coadjoint representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 1
A coadjoint orbit is the orbit of ℓ∈g∗ under Adg∗​ℓ, defined by ⟨Adg∗​ℓ,ξ⟩=⟨ℓ,Adg−1​ξ⟩. For a connected Lie group these orbits are the symplectic leaves of its Lie-Poisson manifold. Their tangent vectors are infinitesimal coadjoint actions, and their natural two-form is the Kirillov–Kostant–Souriau symplectic form.
