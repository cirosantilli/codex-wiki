Orbital parity ((−1)ℓ)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum mechanics Parity
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
An orbital angular momentum eigenstate with quantum number ℓ changes by the factor (−1)ℓ under spatial reflection. Equivalently,
Yℓm​(−r)=(−1)ℓYℓm​(r).
