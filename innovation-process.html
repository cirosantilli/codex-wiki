Innovation process

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series
OurBigBook.com Words: 426 Articles: 6
An innovation measures the part of the current observation not predicted from past observations. The linear innovation process uses orthogonal projection onto the past linear span. A conditional expectation predictor can use nonlinear information; the two predictors agree for a Gaussian process.
