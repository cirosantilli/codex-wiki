Homogeneous turbulence

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 860 Articles: 21
Homogeneous turbulence has velocity statistics unchanged by translating all observation points. Mean spatial divergences vanish when the moments exist and averaging commutes with derivatives. Homogeneity is distinct from isotropy: it does not require equal statistics in different directions.
