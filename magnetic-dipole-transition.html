Magnetic dipole transition

 Home Physics Atomic physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
A transition driven by an oscillating magnetic field through H′=−μ⋅B. The rank-one magnetic operator preserves parity and allows changes in total angular momentum of zero or one, excluding zero-to-zero. Its spherical components select changes in the angular momentum component of 0,±1. Hyperfine clock states can therefore couple even when each has zero first-order static magnetic moment.
