Fermi contours of an isotropic Rashba band

OurBigBook.com Words: 117
For the Rashba effect with kR​>0, put D=kR2​+2mEF​/ℏ2​. If −ER​<EF​<0, both Fermi contours belong to the lower branch and bound the singly occupied annulus kR​−D<k<kR​+D; the central disk and exterior are empty. At EF​=0, the lower occupied disk has outer radius 2kR​, while the origin is a degenerate point at the Fermi energy. For EF​>0, the lower Fermi contour has radius k−​=D+kR​ and the upper Fermi contour radius k+​=D−kR​. The inner disk has two occupied spin-split eigenstates at each momentum, the intervening annulus has one, and the exterior is empty. Consequently k−​−k+​=2kR​, whereas below the crossing the two lower-branch radii add to 2kR​. At zero temperature, changing occupation values only on these Fermi contours affects a null set and leaves the integrated carrier density unchanged.
