In On a Mechanical Theorem applicable to Heat by R. Clausius (1870, pp. 123-124), the author defines stationary motion through bounded positions and velocities, includes planetary orbits and elastic vibrations, and names the average negative half of the force-position sum the “virial”. That historical convention differs from the auxiliary G(t) defined above. His proof on pp. 126-127 keeps an endpoint term, then explains why it vanishes over a complete period or becomes small in a long bounded irregular motion. The same paper separates internal and external forces and includes uniform boundary pressure on pp. 125-126: confinement was already part of the formulation, rather than a reason to omit external stresses.[ref]
