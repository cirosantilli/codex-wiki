Variable-Coriolis shallow-water height equation (Fy​=∂t2​+f(y)2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 51
On a beta plane with f=f0​+βy, set Fy​=∂t2​+f(y)2. Exact elimination of the depth-integrated shallow-water transports gives
Fy​[∇h2​η−c2Fy​η​]t​=β(ηxtt​+2fηyt​−f2ηx​).
(42)
The essential commutator is ∂y​(Fy​V)=Fy​Vy​+2fβV. Freezing undifferentiated f factors to f0​ is a local approximation. The slow-wave limit gives the shallow-water Rossby-wave dispersion relation with a negative zonal phase velocity for β>0.
