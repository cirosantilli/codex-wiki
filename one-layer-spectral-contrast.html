One-layer spectral contrast

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Radiative transfer Radiative transfer equation Formal solution of the radiative transfer equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
A non-scattering isothermal atmospheric layer in local thermodynamic equilibrium, of temperature Ta​ and slant optical depth tλ​, lies above a blackbody background at Tb​. The specific intensity is
Iλ​=Bλ​(Tb​)e−tλ​+Bλ​(Ta​)(1−e−tλ​).
(11)
Thus an increase in opacity shifts the specific intensity toward the layer's Planck function. If a line is more opaque than its continuum, it appears in absorption for Ta​<Tb​, in emission for Ta​>Tb​, and has no contrast for Ta​=Tb​. This is a local explanation, not a claim that all features diagnose one uniform layer.
