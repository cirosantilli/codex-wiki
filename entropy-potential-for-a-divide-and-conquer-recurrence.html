Entropy potential for a divide-and-conquer recurrence (nlog2​n−mlog2​m−(n−m)log2​(n−m)=nH2​(m/n))

 Home Computer science Algorithm Recursion (computer science) Recursion tree
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
If 0<m<n, the displayed identity expresses the decrease of the potential nlog2​n across a split. Here H2​(a)=−alog2​a−(1−a)log2​(1−a) is the binary entropy function. If every split fraction stays in [δ,1−δ] for a fixed 0<δ≤1/2, the potential decreases by at least nH2​(δ)>0. Consequently, for a recurrence relation with linear work at each split, the inductive hypothesis T(n)≤Anlog2​n+Bn closes when AH2​(δ)≥1 and B covers the base cases. This is a proof by the substitution method for a recurrence of an O(nlogn) time complexity bound without requiring equal subproblem sizes.
