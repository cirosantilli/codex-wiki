CNO cycle

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar nuclear fusion
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 3
The CNO cycle catalyses hydrogen fusion through carbon, nitrogen, and oxygen nuclei. Near 2×107K, its specific energy generation is approximately proportional to ρT17 and is therefore strongly concentrated near a star's centre.
