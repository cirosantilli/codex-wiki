The fiber-supported measurable probability kernel supplied by the disintegration theorem for a probability measure for a factor map. For integrable f, E[f∣φ−1BY​](x)=∫fdμφ(x)​ almost everywhere. The same construction applies to general measurable maps and countably generated sub-sigma-algebras modulo null sets.
