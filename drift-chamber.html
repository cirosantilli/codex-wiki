Drift chamber

 Home Physics Particle detection Particle detector
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
A drift chamber uses the drift times of ionization electrons in a gas to infer positions along charged particle tracks. In a known magnetic field, track reconstruction can estimate momentum and the sign of electric charge.
