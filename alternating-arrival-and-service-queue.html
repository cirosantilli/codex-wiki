Alternating arrival and service queue (θ=λ(μ+β)/[μ(λ+α)])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
A continuous-time Markov chain may alternate between an arrival phase C, with upward transitions at rate λ, and a service phase W, with downward transitions at rate μ when the level is positive. Switching rates C→W and W→C are α,β. Its stationary balance equations give a geometric invariant tail with ratio θ=λ(μ+β)/[μ(λ+α)]. A stationary probability exists exactly when μα>λβ, consistent with the stationary average service rate μα/(α+β) exceeding the arrival rate λβ/(α+β).
