Primitive unit cell

 ... Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering Scattering amplitude Crystal scattering Bravais lattice Unit cell
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A fundamental cell of a Bravais lattice, of volume equal to the absolute determinant of any primitive lattice basis. Direct and reciprocal lattice primitive cell volumes multiply to (2π)d in dimension d with the angular-wavenumber convention.
