For bending stiffness D and mass per area m, a plane wave of surface displacement η satisfies mηtt​+D∇H4​η=p. An inviscid deep-water velocity potential has vertical dependence ekz; the kinematic boundary condition and linear Bernoulli equation give p=(ρw​ω2/k−ρw​g)η. Eliminating p gives the displayed dispersion relation. With water depth d, replace it by ω2=(gk+Dk5/ρw​)tanh(kd)/[1+(m/ρw​)ktanh(kd)].
