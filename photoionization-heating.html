Photoionization heating

 Home Physics Atomic physics Ionization Photoionization
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
Absorption of an ionizing photon leaves energy in a photoelectron above the binding energy. Thermalization supplies heat to the gas. Photoionization heating competes with the astrophysical cooling function, can establish thermal equilibrium, and can raise the pressure enough to inhibit baryon accretion into shallow dark-matter haloes.
