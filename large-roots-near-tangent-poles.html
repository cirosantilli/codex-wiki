For fixed real μ=0, roots of tanx=μx tending to positive infinity approach tangent poles an​=(n+1/2)π. Expanding −cot(x−an​) gives
xn​=an​−μan​1​+an3​1/(3μ3)−1/μ2​+O(an−5​).
(35)
The expansion works for either sign of μ when ∣μ∣an​≫1, but is nonuniform at μ=0, where the roots lie at nπ instead. The pole labeling need not equal the ordered root index at small n.
