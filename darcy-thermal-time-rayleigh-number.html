Darcy thermal-time Rayleigh number (R=gαΔTKd/(νκT​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Darcy law Darcy-Bénard convection
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
With permeability K, depth d, kinematic viscosity ν and the relevant effective thermal diffusivity κT​, the Darcy Rayleigh number compares buoyancy-driven Darcy advection to thermal diffusion. It differs from the clear-fluid Rayleigh number by replacing d3 with Kd. Effective heat capacity and porosity factors may be absorbed into the definition of κT​ and the thermal-time scale, and must be kept consistent with the chosen Darcy law convention.
