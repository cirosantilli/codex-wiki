Weak-fluctuation memory equation for the coherent field

 ... Physics Wave Wave scattering Random medium Wave propagation in a random medium Coherent wave field
OurBigBook.com Words: 178 Articles: 1
For a parabolic wave equation with n=1+μW, a zero-mean stationary real field W of unit variance, and L0​=i∂z2​/(2k), finite-correlation perturbation theory gives
mx​​=L0​m+2ikμ2​m−k2μ2∫x0​x​∫R​C(x−s,z−ζ)Gx−s​(z−ζ)m(s,ζ)dζds+O(μ3).​
(52)
Here m is the coherent field, C is the autocorrelation function of a random field and G is the one-coordinate Fresnel propagator. Expand the random solution once using the Duhamel principle, multiply by W and average to obtain the memory term. The local phase term comes from the μ2W2 term in n2. The expansion is for fixed propagation distances with suitable covariance regularity and moment bounds. A Markov approximation is an additional scale assumption that can turn this integral equation into a local attenuation equation.
