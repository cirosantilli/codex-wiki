K-theory of the mapping torus of the factor swap on two complex projective planes (K0(Tswap​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 25
For the factor swap on CP2×CP2, the invariant subgroup of
Z[x,y]/(x3,y3)
(143)
has basis 1,xy,x2y2,x+y,x2+y2,xy2+x2y. Hence the mapping torus has K0≅Z6.
