Free-slip convection neutral curve (R0​(k)=(k2+π2)3/k2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Navier-Stokes equation Rayleigh-Bénard convection
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For nonrotating convection under the Boussinesq approximation with fixed-temperature impermeable stress-free boundary conditions, a marginal vertical mode sin(nπz) has neutral Rayleigh number (k2+n2π2)3/k2. The minimum is at n=1, k=π/2​, and Rc​=27π4/4. These follow by eliminating the temperature from (D2−k2)2w=Rk2θ and (D2−k2)θ=−w, then minimizing over k.
