For an outward sightline, Fourier velocity vbi​=ikiθb​, and sudden last scattering, intrinsic photon temperature projects with a Spherical Bessel function, while the Doppler term projects with its derivative. Density and velocity acoustic sources are approximately a quarter cycle out of phase. Their amplitude is suppressed by photon diffusion, giving a power damping envelope e−2k2/kD2​. Projection maps the sound scale to peak spacing approximately πD∗​/rs​ and the damping scale to angular multipoles of order kD​D∗​.
