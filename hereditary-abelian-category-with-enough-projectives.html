Hereditary abelian category with enough projectives (L2​F=0)

 ... Foundations of mathematics Category theory Enriched category Semi-additive category Additive category Abelian category
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
An abelian category with enough projectives is hereditary when every subobject of a projective object is projective. For 0→K→P→A→0 with P projective, dimension shifting gives L2​F(A)≅L1​F(K). Hence hereditaryness is equivalent to vanishing of L2​F for every additive right exact functor into any abelian category, by projectivity detected by all first left derived functors. It is also equivalent to left exactness of every L1​F: the derived long exact sequence gives injectivity of L1​F(K)→L1​F(P) when L2​F vanishes, and applying left exactness to the displayed sequence proves the converse.
