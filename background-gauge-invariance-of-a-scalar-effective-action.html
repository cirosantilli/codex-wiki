Background gauge invariance of a scalar effective action (Wa​Γ=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Effective action
OurBigBook.com Words: 118
For real scalar multiplets with antisymmetric generators ta​, transforming a source J in the same representation leaves J⋅ϕ invariant. With an invariant functional measure, local changes of integration variables imply the Ward identity for the quantum effective action. In the convention Z=eiW, φ=WJ​, Γ=−W+∫Jφ, it is
[(ta​φ)⋅δφδ​+∂μ​δAμa​δ​+fabc​Aμb​δAμc​δ​]Γ=0.
(11)
The Legendre transform gives Γφ​=J and ΓA​∣φ​=−WA​∣J​. Its classical limit is Γ=−S0​, not S0​, for the source term +Jϕ. Differentiating twice in φ produces two contact terms and the momentum-space identity p1μ​τa,ijμ​=i(ta​)ik​τkj​−τik​i(ta​)kj​ with the appropriate shifted two-point momenta. Changing the overall convention for Γ changes both two-point and mixed vertices together; changing just one violates the identity. A gauge anomaly would invalidate the assumed invariant-measure argument.
