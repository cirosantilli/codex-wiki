Line with two origins

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Non-Hausdorff manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Take R×{0,1} with its Euclidean topology and identify (t,0) with (t,1) for every t=0, retaining two distinct origins. This quotient space is locally homeomorphic to the real line but is not Hausdorff. Any open neighbourhoods of its origins have preimages containing (−ε,ε)×{0} and (−δ,δ)×{1}, so a nonzero ∣t∣<min(ε,δ) gives a shared image point. All equivalence classes in the original metric space are finite and closed. This shows that closed equivalence classes do not suffice for a Hausdorff quotient space.
