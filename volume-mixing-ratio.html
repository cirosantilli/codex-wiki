Volume mixing ratio (xi​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 33
For an ideal gas mixture, the volume mixing ratio xi​ equals the mole fraction, number fraction, and ratio of partial to total pressure: xi​=Ni​/N=ni​/n=Pi​/P, with ∑i​xi​=1. This equivalence fails for general nonideal mixtures.
