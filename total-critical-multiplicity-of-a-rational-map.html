A degree-d rational map has total critical multiplicity 2d−2. Here is an algebraic proof. Critical points are isolated zeros of local derivatives and are finite in number by compactness. Change domain and range Möbius coordinates so that the poles are d simple finite poles and infinity is a regular point with a finite image. Write R=P/Q, with degQ=d, and R(z)=a+b/z+O(z−2), b=0. Then W=P′Q−PQ′ has degree exactly 2d−2, since R′=W/Q2∼−b/z2. At a pole, W=−PQ′=0. Thus the roots of W are precisely the critical points and their zero orders are the critical multiplicities. The fundamental theorem of algebra gives the count; the coordinate changes preserve it.
