For standard tableaux of the same shape and ht​=ct​rt​, t>u in column-reading order of standard Young tableaux implies ht​hu​=0. If there were no row-column collision, each column of u would select one entry from each eligible row of t. Its sorted first column is componentwise at least the row minima of t; equality fixes those selected entries and permits induction after deleting the column. Thus no collision implies u≥t. A collision gives rt​cu​=0.
