Indicial root (P(p)=0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 33
An indicial root is a root of the indicial equation obtained from a power-law ansatz. For a Cauchy-Euler differential equation, such roots give the exponents of its power solutions. Their sign determines which modes remain bounded near a singular endpoint.
