Crossed-product algebra of a Galois extension (A(L,G,ϕ))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Central simple algebra Brauer group
OurBigBook.com Words: 246 Articles: 4
For a finite Galois extension L/k, Galois group G, and normalized two-cocycle ϕ:G×G→L×, the crossed-product algebra is
A(L,G,ϕ)=⨁σ∈G​Luσ​,
(4)
with uσ​a=σ(a)uσ​ and uσ​uτ​=ϕ(σ,τ)uστ​. It is a central simple k-algebra split by L.
