Cohomology class of the diagonal (εΔ​)

Let M be a closed oriented n-manifold, let ep,i​ be a homogeneous basis of Hp(M;Q), and choose its Poincare-dual basis ep,i∗​∈Hn−p(M;Q) by ⟨ep,i​ep,j∗​,[M]⟩=δij​. With the product-orientation convention, the Poincare dual of the diagonal is
εΔ​=∑p,i​(−1)npep,i∗​×ep,i​.
