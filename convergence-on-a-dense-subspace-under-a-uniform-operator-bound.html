Convergence on a dense subspace under a uniform operator bound (suph​∥Ah​∥<∞,Ah​d converges on dense D ⟹ Ah​x converges for every x)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Topological vector space Continuous linear operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
Let Ah​:X→Z, 0<h<1, be bounded linear operators from a normed space into a Banach space, with M=suph​∥Ah​∥<∞. If Ah​d converges as h→0+ for each element of a dense subspace D, then Ah​x converges for every x∈X. The limit defines a bounded linear operator A with ∥A∥≤M.
For d∈D, the triangle inequality gives
∥Ah​x−Ak​x∥≤2M∥x−d∥+∥Ah​d−Ak​d∥.
(15)
Approximate x by d and use the Cauchy criterion for a function limit on D. The completeness of Z supplies the limit; passing to the limit preserves linearity and the bound. When every limit on D is zero, the inequality ∥Ah​x∥≤M∥x−d∥+∥Ah​d∥ shows A=0.
