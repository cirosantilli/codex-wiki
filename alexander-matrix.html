Alexander matrix

Given a presentation ⟨x1​,…,xn​∣r1​,…,rm​⟩ of a knot group, apply abelianization to the Fox derivatives ∂rj​/∂xi​. The resulting matrix over Z[t±1] is an Alexander matrix; suitable maximal minors recover the Alexander polynomial of a knot.
