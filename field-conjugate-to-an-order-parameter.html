Field conjugate to an order parameter (−hm)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Order parameter
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
A field conjugate to a scalar order parameter m contributes −hm to its free-energy density. Differentiating the equilibrium free energy with respect to h gives −m, with density or extensive normalization stated consistently. In a ferromagnet the conjugate field is magnetic; for a fluid-density order parameter it is related to a chemical-potential difference. It can explicitly break the symmetry whose spontaneous breaking defines the ordered phase.
