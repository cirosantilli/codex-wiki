Polynomial invariant

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation Invariant theory Polynomial invariant ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 1
For a group representation on V, a polynomial invariant is a polynomial p satisfying p(gv)=p(v) for every g∈G. Homogeneous degree-d invariants form (SymdV∗)G. Using the symmetric power matters: an alternating invariant tensor contracted with repeated copies of the same commuting vector can vanish.
