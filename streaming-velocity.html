Streaming velocity (⟨vϕ​⟩)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics Galactic distribution function
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
The streaming velocity is the local mean stellar velocity obtained by averaging with the galactic distribution function. In an axisymmetric disk it usually denotes ⟨vϕ​⟩. It differs from the circular speed, which is set by the force field, and from the velocity dispersion, which measures fluctuations about the mean.
