Dielectric relaxation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Permittivity Complex permittivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
Delayed adjustment of electric polarization after a change in electric field. A simple illustrative model lets the slowly adjusting part Ps​ obey τdPs​/dt+Ps​=ϵ0​Δϵr​E, with a positive response time τ. A harmonic electric field then gives a contribution Δϵr​/(1+iωτ) to complex permittivity divided by vacuum permittivity. This model is an assumption, not a claim that all dielectrics have one response time.
