Convected acoustic wave equation ((∂t​+U0​∂x​)2ρ1​=c02​∂x2​ρ1​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Linear acoustics
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For a uniform background velocity U0​ and speed of sound c0​, linearized conservation of mass, the Euler equation and the local equation of state p1​=c02​ρ1​ give this wave equation. In the coordinate x−U0​t it is the ordinary one-dimensional wave equation. Its two characteristic curves travel at laboratory velocities U0​+c0​ and U0​−c0​; both point downstream in supersonic flow.
