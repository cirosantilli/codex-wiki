Labelled tree

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Tree (graph theory)
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
A tree whose vertices carry distinct labels from a fixed finite set, as a labelled combinatorial structure. Two labelled trees on the same vertex set are equal when their sets of edges are equal: any comparison must keep every label fixed. Cayley's formula counts these trees.
