Ornstein-Uhlenbeck stochastic differential equation (dYt​=σdWt​−λYt​dt)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The linear stochastic differential equation has the explicit solution Yt​=e−λtY0​+σ∫0t​e−λ(t−s)dWs​. Its integrating factor is eλt. For λ>0, an independent initial normal variable of variance σ2/(2λ) makes its zero-mean Gaussian process stationary.
