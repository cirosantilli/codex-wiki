σx​σp​=2ℏ​1+η2​,σx2​σp2​−Cxp2​=4ℏ2​.
(45)
Thus free spreading preserves equality in the Robertson-Schrödinger uncertainty relation while producing a nonzero quadratic position-dependent phase and a product σx​σp​ larger than ℏ/2.
