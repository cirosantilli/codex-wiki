One-dimensional Sobolev representative

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space First-order Sobolev space
OurBigBook.com Words: 73 Articles: 1
Every element of W1,p(a,b) for 1≤p<∞ has an absolutely continuous representative. Its ordinary derivative exists almost everywhere, equals its weak derivative, and belongs to Lp(a,b).
