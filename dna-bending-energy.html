DNA bending energy (Gb​=2kB​Tlp​​∫κ2dℓ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biopolymer mechanics Worm-like chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The curvature-dependent elastic energy of a semiflexible DNA polymer in the isotropic worm-like chain model. Here lp​ is the persistence length, κ the curvature and ℓ the polymer contour length. A complete circular loop of radius R costs πkB​Tlp​/R, obtained by integrating constant curvature 1/R over length 2πR.
