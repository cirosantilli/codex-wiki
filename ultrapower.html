Ultrapower (MI/U)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Model theory Ultraproduct
OurBigBook.com Words: 311 Articles: 4
An ultrapower is an ultraproduct whose factors are all one structure M. Its elements are equivalence classes [f] of functions f:I→M, where f∼g exactly when {i∈I:f(i)=g(i)} belongs to the chosen ultrafilter U.
