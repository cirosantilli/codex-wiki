Infrared spin-wave correlations of the XY model (⟨m(x)⋅m(0)⟩=mˉ2e−⟨(θ(x)−θ(0))2⟩/2)

For the Gaussian functional integral with dimensionless phase stiffness K and a microscopic cutoff a, the Gaussian phase averaging identity gives the displayed correlation function. In two dimensions the phase-difference variance is (πK)−1log(r/a)+O(1), so the XY model has algebraic spin wave correlations with exponent 1/(2πK). In one dimension the variance is r/K, giving exponential decay. Above two dimensions the variance has a finite large-distance limit within the low-temperature spin wave approximation. The Laplacian Green function solves −K∇2G=δ; integration over a sphere fixes G(r)=r2−d/[(d−2)Sd​K]+C for d=2 and G(r)=−(2πK)−1log(r/a)+C for d=2. A fixed uniform phase and ultraviolet regularization are required to define G itself; phase differences eliminate the constant.
