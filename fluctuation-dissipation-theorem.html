Fluctuation-dissipation theorem

OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The fluctuation-dissipation theorem relates an equilibrium correlation function to the dissipative part of the corresponding linear response. If ρ=−2ImGR​, one common convention is S(ω)=ρ(ω)/(1−e−ω/T), which becomes S(ω)≃Tρ(ω)/ω in the classical limit ∣ω∣≪T.
