Ligand in coordination chemistry

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 67
A ligand in coordination chemistry is an atom, ion or molecule attached to a central atom through one or more donor sites. This use is distinct from a protein-binding ligand. A neutral CO ligand and an anionic chloride ligand can both donate two electrons in ionic electron counting.
