Sum of squares (∑i​ai2​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A sum of squares adds the squares of numbers ai2​ for specified real numbers ai​. It is nonnegative and vanishes exactly when every term is zero. For the coordinates of a vector, it is the squared Euclidean norm; for regression residuals, it gives the residual sum of squares minimized by ordinary least squares.
