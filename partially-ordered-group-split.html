Partially ordered group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 40
A group with a partial order preserved by multiplication on both sides: a≤b implies xay≤xby for all x,y. In additive notation this says a≤b⇒x+a+y≤x+b+y. Preserving only one side is a different orderability condition.
