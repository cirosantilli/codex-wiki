Stein's lemma (probability)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Normal distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 1
For Z∼N(0,1) and a sufficiently regular function g,
E[Zg(Z)]=E[g′(Z)].
(72)
It follows by integrating zg(z)e−z2/2 by parts.
