Roots of a Gegenbauer polynomial

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Sturm-Liouville theory Gegenbauer polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For α>0, all n roots of Cnα​ are simple and lie in (−1,1). If the product q of its interior linear factors had degree below n, orthogonality would make ∫Cnα​qw=0, while Cnα​q=q2(Cnα​/q) has a constant strict sign away from finitely many points.
