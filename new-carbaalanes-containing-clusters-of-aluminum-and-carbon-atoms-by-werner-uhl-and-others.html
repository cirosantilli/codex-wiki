New Carbaalanes Containing Clusters of Aluminum and Carbon Atoms by Werner Uhl and others (2000)

 Home Chemistry Chemistry bibliography
OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by Werner Uhl Words: 47
doi.org/10.1021/om000244s
Primary study in Organometallics, volume 19, pages 4536–4543. Its abstract identifies the open carbaalane with two C=CHPh groups and reports their hydroalumination during conversion to a capped aluminium cube. This establishes that the unsaturated groups are real, but the abstract alone does not establish which electron counting convention the examination intended.
