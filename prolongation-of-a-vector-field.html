Prolongation of a vector field

For V=ξ(x,u)∂x​+η(x,u)∂u​, its prolongation to derivatives through order n is
pr(n)V=V+∑j=1n​η(j)∂u(j)​,η(j)=Dx​η(j−1)−u(j)Dx​ξ.
(279)
It generates a symmetry of Δ=0 exactly when pr(n)V(Δ) vanishes on the equation manifold.
