Strassen's algorithm

OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The matrix multiplication algorithm of Volker Strassen using seven half-size block products and quadratically many scalar additions per recursion level. Its recurrence is T(n)=7T(n/2)+Θ(n2), giving Θ(nlog2​7) arithmetic operations by the Master theorem.[ref]
