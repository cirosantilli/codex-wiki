Orthogonal direct sum (V⊕⊥W)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Direct sum
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
An orthogonal direct sum is a direct sum whose distinct summands are mutually orthogonal. For v∈V and w∈W, the Pythagorean identity gives ∥v+w∥2=∥v∥2+∥w∥2. In a Hilbert space, a closed subspace of a Hilbert space and its orthogonal complement give an orthogonal direct sum of the entire space. The component maps are orthogonal projections and have operator norm one when their ranges are nonzero.
