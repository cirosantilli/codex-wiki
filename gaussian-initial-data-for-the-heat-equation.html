Gaussian initial data for the heat equation (u(x,t)=A(1+4γDt)−1/2exp[−γ(x−x0​)2/(1+4γDt)])

For D,γ>0, the displayed function solves the heat equation ut​=Duxx​ and has initial condition u(x,0)=Ae−γ(x−x0​)2. Set s=1+4γDt. Direct differentiation gives
ut​=(−s2γD​+s24γ2D(x−x0​)2​)u,uxx​=(−s2γ​+s24γ2(x−x0​)2​)u.
(35)
These identities prove the equation. The total integral is Aπ/γ​, independent of time. The real smooth formula is valid for t>−1/(4γD), including a finite interval of negative time for this particular initial profile. For nonzero A, its value at x0​ diverges as the lower time endpoint is approached. The superposition principle combines finitely many such profiles with different centers or widths.
