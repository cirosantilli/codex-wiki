Innovation exponential for Gaussian drift filtering (dZt​=−Zt​mtT​dWt​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Innovation process Gaussian Brownian drift filter
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 1
For the Gaussian Brownian drift filter with positive definite V0​, define Zt​=det(I+tV0​)1/2exp[−mtT​Vt−1​mt​/2+m0T​V0−1​m0​/2]. Because Vt′​=−Vt2​ and dmt​=Vt​dWt​, the Itô formula gives dlogZt​=−mtT​dWt​−∣mt​∣2dt/2. Thus Z is a positive stochastic exponential, hence a nonnegative local martingale and a supermartingale. For a singular prior, the inverse-matrix expression is undefined; the same process can instead be defined by Zt​=(Eλ∼N(m0​,V0​)​exp[λTYt​−t∣λ∣2/2])−1, with Yt​ treated as fixed inside this Gaussian integral.
