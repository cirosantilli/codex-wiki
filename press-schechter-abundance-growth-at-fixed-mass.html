For a fixed narrow mass bin with time-independent smoothing slope, the Press-Schechter halo mass function has time dependence n∝νe−ν2/2, with halo peak height ν∝D−1. Differentiating gives the displayed rate. It is positive only for ν>1; the bin can lose objects to higher masses later. Cumulative counts require integrating the differential abundance, and a moving mass boundary adds a selection term.
