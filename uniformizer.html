Uniformizer

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Local ring Valuation ring Discrete valuation ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A uniformizer of a discrete valuation ring R is a generator π of its unique nonzero maximal ideal. Every nonzero x in the fraction field has a unique expression x=uπn with u a unit and n∈Z.
