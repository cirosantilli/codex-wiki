Giant magnetoresistance

 ... Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Electrical resistance and conductance Electrical resistance Magnetoresistance
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 12
Giant magnetoresistance connects the relative magnetizations of adjacent magnetic layers to their electrical transport. In a suitable magnetic-metal/nonmagnetic-metal stack, aligning the layers can leave one spin channel with an easy conducting path through both magnetic layers; opposite alignments make each channel encounter a more resistive layer. Albert Fert's account traces the spin-dependent-conduction idea to Nevill Mott and distinguishes the later device geometries.[ref]
The beauty is that a magnetic arrangement becomes an electrical readout. Preparing reproducible layers, establishing their magnetizations, and measuring electrical resistance are separate parts of that inference. The Fe-Cr giant magnetoresistance experiments show how those parts were joined; the two-current resistor model isolates a simple transport mechanism under stated assumptions.
