Amplifier

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
An amplifier uses supplied energy to increase a signal amplitude or delivered power. An electronic amplifier is an electrical circuit; real devices add noise and have a frequency-dependent frequency response.
