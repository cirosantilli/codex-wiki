An isolated conducting sphere of radius R initially at potential V0​ has charge Q=4πϵ0​RV0​. Inserting it without contact into the hollow inner conductor of a long coaxial cylindrical capacitor conserves that charge. The neutral inner conductor acquires −Q on its cavity wall and +Q outside. With the outer conductor grounded, the annular electric field is Q/(2πϵ0​lr) and the inner potential is the displayed result. The original electric potential is not conserved. End effects are neglected; exact cavity fields are geometry-dependent.
