Skywave

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Upper atmosphere Ionosphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A skywave is a radio wave returned toward Earth through interaction with the ionosphere. In a smoothly varying plasma, the ray can bend continuously through refraction before returning: there need be no sharp material surface at the apparent point of reflection. Different frequencies, launch directions and wave polarizations can have different paths and losses.
