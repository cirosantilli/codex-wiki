Unit-ideal certificate from a principal affine cover (∑i​gi​fi​=1)

 ... Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Spectrum of a commutative ring Affine scheme Cohomological criterion for affineness
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
If finitely many Xfi​​ cover X, the morphism OXr​→OX​ with coefficients fi​ is onto. If its kernel has vanishing first sheaf cohomology, the long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology lifts the global section 1 to coefficients gi​. This turns a geometric cover into a unit ideal in the ring of global sections.
