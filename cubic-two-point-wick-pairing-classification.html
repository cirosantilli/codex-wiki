Cubic two-point Wick-pairing classification

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Wick's theorem Wick contraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 94
At second order in the coupling constant of a real phi cubed theory, the two-point numerator has eight fields, hence 7!!=105 Wick contractions. Of these, six give a free external line times a vacuum theta graph, nine give a free line times a vacuum dumbbell, 36 give a two-line bubble, 36 give a connected tadpole attachment, and 18 give two separate one-point tadpoles. Dividing by 2!(3!)2=72 gives weights 1/12,1/8,1/2,1/2,1/4. The normalized vacuum generating functional cancels the first two contributions, while the last remains in the full two-point correlation function as the product of one-point functions. Thus cancellation of vacuum bubbles does not, by itself, take the connected part.
