Baryon operator in supersymmetric quantum chromodynamics (B,B)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supersymmetric gauge theory Supersymmetric quantum chromodynamics Chiral ring of a supersymmetric gauge theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
An SQCD baryon contracts Nc​ fundamental fields with the gauge epsilon tensor; an antibaryon similarly contracts antifundamentals. Such operators exist when enough flavors are available and obey relations with the mesons.
