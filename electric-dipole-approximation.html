Electric-dipole approximation (Hint​≃−d⋅E(R,t))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Time-dependent perturbation theory Electric-dipole interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 110
For a localized charge distribution in an external field varying little across its extent, expand the external scalar potential about a reference point R. The constant term couples to total charge; the first spatially varying term gives the electric-dipole interaction −d⋅E(R,t), where d is the electric dipole moment. Higher spatial multipoles are neglected.
For a passing charge at minimum distance b, the atomic extent must be small compared with b. For a plane electromagnetic wave, the analogous long-wavelength condition is ka≪1, as in the electric-dipole approximation for radiation. A short interaction time and a spatially uniform field across the atom are different assumptions; neither implies the other.
