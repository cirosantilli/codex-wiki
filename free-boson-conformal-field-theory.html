Free boson conformal field theory

 ... Physics Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Worldsheet Two-dimensional conformal field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 23
The free boson conformal field theory has action quadratic in a scalar X and central charge one. Its oscillator partition function is expressed through the Dedekind eta function.
