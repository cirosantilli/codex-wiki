Cole-Hopf transformation (u=−2ν∂x​logϕ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Burgers' equation Viscous Burgers equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 167 Articles: 3
The Cole-Hopf transformation u=−2νϕx​/ϕ converts the viscous Burgers equation into the heat equation ϕt​=νϕxx​. Initial data u(x,0)=g(x) correspond to ϕ(x,0)=Cexp[−(2ν)−1∫xg(s)ds].
