Wilcoxon signed-rank statistic (W+​=∑Xi​>0​rank(∣Xi​∣))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Nonparametric statistics Wilcoxon signed-rank test
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For a continuous sample, the statistic also equals ∑i​1{Xi​>0}​+∑i<j​1{Xi​+Xj​>0}​. In each pair the sign of the sum is the sign of the observation with larger absolute value, proving the identity by counting its smaller-rank partners. Under a continuous symmetric null, the pair kernel has projection F(x)−1/2 and projection variance 1/12. The U-statistic central limit theorem yields asymptotic normality of W+​, with variance asymptotic to n3/12.
