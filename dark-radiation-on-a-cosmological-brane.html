Dark radiation on a cosmological brane (ρE​∝Ca−4)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Compactification (physics) Randall–Sundrum model High-energy braneworld expansion
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
Projected bulk Weyl tensor can contribute a radiation-scaling term to the brane Friedmann equation, even without ordinary brane photons. For an anti-de Sitter black-hole bulk the integration constant is related to its mass. This dark radiation on a cosmological brane changes the expansion rate tested by Big Bang nucleosynthesis and Cosmic microwave background anisotropy; it should not automatically be identified with a freely specifiable thermal particle species.
