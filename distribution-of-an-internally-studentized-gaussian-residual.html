Distribution of an internally studentized Gaussian residual (ηi2​/ν∼Beta(1/2,(ν−1)/2))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Regression residual Standardized regression residual
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
In a normal linear model with residual degrees of freedom ν≥2, an internally studentized residual satisfies ∣ηi​∣≤ν​ and ηi2​/ν∼Beta(1/2,(ν−1)/2), provided its leverage is less than one. Choose an orthonormal basis of the residual space with its first direction proportional to the projection of the ith coordinate vector. The Gaussian coordinates in this basis are independent standard normal random variables Z1​,…,Zν​, and ηi​=ν​Z1​/(∑j​Zj2​)1/2. The Beta distribution follows from the independent chi-squared distributions of Z12​ and ∑j=2ν​Zj2​. The bounded residual is not exactly distributed according to Student's t-distribution; it approaches the standard normal distribution as ν→∞.
