Twin image in holography

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Holography In-line holography
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
For a uniform on-axis plane wave reference, choose its constant wave phase to be zero, so R=AR​>0. The reconstructed cross terms are then proportional to O and its complex conjugate O∗. This pair creates the twin image in holography. An outgoing curved wavefront and its conjugate have opposite curvature: under forward propagation, one can appear to diverge from a virtual image while the other converges to a real image. In in-line holography their depths differ, but a defocused member still overlaps the other. This is the same conjugation of spatial wave phase used in phase conjugation, although a transmission hologram here launches both components forward rather than automatically creating a returning phase-conjugate mirror.
