For E=Cμ/(β+Cμ)<1 and mean lifetime τ=(β+Cμ)−1, the normalized restoring matrix is M=τ−1(1E​E1​) and the diffusion matrix is D=(τμ)−1(2E​E2​). Solving the normalized stationary fluctuation-dissipation relation for a reaction network gives the displayed covariance. The sum mode remains damped, but the difference mode's restoring rate is β=(1−E)/τ. Its noise grows without bound relative to that restoring rate as E→1, giving strong negative correlations and invalidating small-fluctuation closure near exclusive joint removal.
