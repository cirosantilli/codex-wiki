Fourier transform of a compactly supported distribution

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Support of a distribution Compactly supported distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 154 Articles: 2
For u∈E′(Rn), its Fourier transform is the smooth function
u(λ)=⟨u(x),e−iλ⋅x⟩.
(16)
It has at most polynomial growth, and its extension to complex frequency is controlled more precisely by the Paley–Wiener–Schwartz theorem.
