For a weakly absorbing doped semiconductor with one dominant carrier species and approximately constant background dielectric function ϵb​>1, the Drude model gives a normal-incidence reflectivity minimum near ϵr​=1. Thus ωpc2​=(ϵb​−1)ωmin2​, with ωpc2​=ne2/(ϵ0​m∗) and ωmin​=2πc/λmin​. Eliminating the plasma frequency proves the displayed mass estimate. The real-dielectric zero instead has ω0​≃ωpc​/ϵb​​ and is the nearby screened plasma edge, so its wavelength requires a different denominator. With appreciable loss, fit the full complex Fresnel equations rather than identifying the minimum with an exactly real dielectric value.
