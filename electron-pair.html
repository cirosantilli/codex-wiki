Electron pair

 Home Chemistry Chemical bond
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
An electron pair consists of two Electrons with opposite spins sharing the same spatial part of their quantum state. The Lewis structure distinction between a bond pair and a lone pair records whether that pair is assigned to a connection between chemical atoms or to one chemical atom.
