Addition on the Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers (+ on βN)

For ultrafilters U,V∈βN, define U+V by
A∈U+V⟺{x:{y:x+y∈A}∈V}∈U.
(50)
This operation is associative, and each right translation U↦U+V is continuous, so βN is a compact Hausdorff left-topological semigroup.
