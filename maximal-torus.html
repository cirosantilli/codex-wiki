Maximal torus (T)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 197 Articles: 2
A maximal torus is a connected compact abelian Lie subgroup maximal among such subgroups. Restricting a finite-dimensional unitary representation to it splits into one-dimensional weight characters. For special unitary group SU(2) it is the diagonal circle diag(z,z−1).
