Temperley-Lieb diagram algebra (ei2​=δei​,ei​ei+1​ei​=ei​)

The Temperley-Lieb diagram algebra is generated by planar cap-cup tangles, with composition by stacking and each closed circle replaced by δ. Its local relations ei2​=δei​ and ei​ei+1​ei​=ei​ make the second and third Reidemeister moves direct algebraic identities for Ri​=AI+A−1ei​ when δ=−A2−A−2.
