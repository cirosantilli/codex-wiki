Exact Bühlmann–Straub credibility for Poisson-gamma counts (μ​cred​=μ​Bayes​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Bühlmann model Bühlmann–Straub model Bühlmann–Straub credibility estimate
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
With a Poisson-gamma conjugacy with unequal exposures model, m0​=v=α/β and a=α/β2, so the Bühlmann–Straub credibility factor is W/(W+β). The credibility estimate simplifies to (α+∑j​Yj​)/(β+W), exactly the posterior mean and hence the Bayes estimator under squared error loss. The agreement is exact because that posterior mean is already affine in the exposure-weighted data.
