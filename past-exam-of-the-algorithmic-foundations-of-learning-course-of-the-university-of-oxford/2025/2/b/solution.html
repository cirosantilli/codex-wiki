Fix a realization in E. The action space is nonempty and compact, since it is a closed subset of the finite-dimensional unit ball. The Lipschitz continuous reward function is continuous, so there is an optimal action x⋆​∈argmaxx∈X​μ(⟨x,θ⋆​⟩). Because the same reward function is used at every time, the pseudoregret is Rn​=∑t=1n​rt​, where
rt​=μ(⟨x⋆​,θ⋆​⟩)−μ(⟨Xt​,θ⋆​⟩)≥0.

The pair (x⋆​,θ⋆​) is feasible in the optimization defining (Xt​,θt​). This optimism in the face of uncertainty gives
μ(⟨x⋆​,θ⋆​⟩)≤μ(⟨Xt​,θt​⟩),rt​≤L∣⟨Xt​,θt​−θ⋆​⟩∣.

∣⟨x,z⟩∣≤∣x∣V−1​∣z∣V​.
