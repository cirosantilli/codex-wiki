Let Gt​=σ(X1​,…,Xt​). Under Pθ⋆​​, the true probability density function is positive and finite at every observation almost surely. Define the sequential likelihood ratio
M0​=1,Mt​=∏s=1t​pθ⋆​​(Xs​)pθs−1​​(Xs​)​.
(35)
A zero numerator sets the product to zero thereafter. Thus Mt​ is a well-defined nonnegative process, and Lt​(θ⋆​)=logMt​, with log0=−∞.
The next observation is independent of Gs−1​, while θs−1​ is measurable with respect to that sigma-algebra. Its conditional expectation is therefore
Eθ⋆​​[pθ⋆​​(Xs​)pθs−1​​(Xs​)​​Gs−1​]​=∫{pθ⋆​​>0}​pθs−1​​(x)dμ(x)≤1.​
(36)
Consequently E(Ms​∣Gs−1​)≤Ms−1​; induction also gives EMs​≤1. Thus (Mt​) is a nonnegative supermartingale for the filtration (Gt​). It need not be a martingale: an alternative distribution can put mass outside the support of the true distribution.
The maximal inequality for a nonnegative supermartingale, the corresponding form of the Ville inequality, gives
Pθ⋆​​{∃t≥0:θ⋆​∈/Θt​}​=Pθ⋆​​{∃t≥0:Lt​(θ⋆​)≥log(1/δ)}≤Pθ⋆​​{t≥0sup​Mt​≥1/δ}≤δEM0​=δ​.​
(37)
The strict inequality in the definition of the confidence set correctly becomes a non-strict crossing event.
There is a minor definitional omission in the source: for false candidates θ, the printed sum of log-likelihood ratios can involve 0/0 or both infinite signs. To define the entire confidence set without a positivity assumption, use the logarithm of the ratio of the two full products, ∏s​pθs−1​​(Xs​) and ∏s​pθ​(Xs​), taking 0/a=0 for a>0, a/0=+∞ for a>0, and, for example, 0/0=1. This is a measurable extension of the printed formula wherever that formula is defined. The choice at 0/0 cannot affect coverage of the truth, since its full denominator is positive almost surely. Alternatively, strict positivity of every density on the common support removes the omission.
