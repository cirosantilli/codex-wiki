Gravity-current box model

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Reduced gravity Gravity current
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 30
A gravity-current box model replaces the current by a well-mixed region with uniform depth and density. Integral volume, scalar, and front-speed balances then reduce the spreading problem to ordinary differential equations.
