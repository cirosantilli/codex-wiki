Tightness of a probability measure (∀ε>0 ∃K compact:μ(K)>1−ε)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability space Probability measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 205 Articles: 3
A probability measure is tight if compact sets carry arbitrarily nearly all its mass. Every Borel probability measure on a Polish space is tight. The stronger family condition is uniform tightness.
