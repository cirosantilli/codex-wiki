Stratification–rotation analogy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Boussinesq approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 241 Articles: 2
Uniformly stratified, nonrotating, two-dimensional ideal Boussinesq equations in coordinates (x,z) are equivalent to unstratified rotating equations independent of Y, with (X,Y,Z)=(z,y,−x), rotation 2Ω=NeZ​, velocity (U,V,W)=(w,σ/N,−u), and the same reduced pressure. The transverse rotating velocity represents buoyancy perturbation, while the rotation-axis velocity represents the stratified horizontal velocity. Matching prescribed forces may have components in the invariant plane, but no transverse component. Physical free surface conditions are not preserved: the physical vertical direction and its normal velocity are different under the mapping.
