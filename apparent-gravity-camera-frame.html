Apparent-gravity camera frame

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Computer aided geometric design
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
If g is gravitational acceleration and a is the camera's acceleration, the locally felt upward vector is U=a−g. Choose the travel unit vector X, set W=U−(U⋅X)X, and, when W=0, take Z=W/∥W∥ and Y=Z×X. These form an orthonormal basis with U in the XZ plane. If U is parallel to X, the condition leaves the roll angle free even when U=0; transport a previous transverse axis or choose a well-conditioned coordinate axis and project it into X⊥.
