Linear matroid

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Matroid
OurBigBook.com Words: 348 Articles: 1
A linear matroid has a finite ground set indexing a family of vectors in a vector space over a specified field. A subset of labels is a matroid independent set precisely when its vectors are linearly independent. Labels distinguish repeated vectors; the ground set is not required to consist of distinct numerical vectors. The matroid bases correspond to bases for the linear span of all the supplied vectors.
Exchange outline. The linear span of ∣I∣ linearly independent vectors has dimension ∣I∣. If ∣J∣>∣I∣, not all the linearly independent vectors indexed by J can lie in that linear span. A vector outside it can be added to I. Heredity follows because a linear dependence in a subset would also be a linear dependence in the full selection.
