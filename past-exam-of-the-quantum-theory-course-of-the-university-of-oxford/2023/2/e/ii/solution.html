Choose the first three energy eigenstates consistently as ψ0,0​, ψ1,0​=a1,+​ψ0,0​ and ψ0,1​=a2,+​ψ0,0​. The Fock vacuum is annihilated by both lowering operators, so
L3​ψ0,0​=0,L3​ψ1,0​=iℏψ0,1​,L3​ψ0,1​=−iℏψ1,0​.​
(31)
For example a1,−​a1,+​ψ0,0​=ψ0,0​, while a2,−​ψ1,0​=0, giving the middle formula. Thus the simultaneous eigenstates can be chosen as
χ0​=ψ0,0​,χ+​=2​ψ1,0​+iψ0,1​​,χ−​=2​ψ1,0​−iψ0,1​​.​
(32)
Their pairs of eigenvalues (H,L3​) are respectively (ℏω,0), (2ℏω,+ℏ) and (2ℏω,−ℏ). The factor 1/2​ normalizes the combinations when the original three states are normalized. Mixing only inside the degenerate first-excited energy eigenspace preserves the energy eigenvalue while diagonalizing L3​.
