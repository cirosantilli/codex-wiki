Grey-atmosphere convection onset with seventeenth-power opacity (Tb​=(17/40)1/12Te​)

For a grey atmosphere with T4=Te4​(2+3τ)/4, an ideal gas and κ=κ0​ρT17, hydrostatic equilibrium in optical depth integrates to P2=512Rg[1/8−(2+3τ)−3]/(9μκ0​Te16​), taking P(0)=0. The logarithmic radiative gradient is [(2+3τ)3−8]/48. Equating it to the monatomic adiabatic temperature gradient 2/5 gives (2+3τb​)3=136/5 and the displayed onset temperature. Beyond this point the radiative profile must be replaced by convective transport.
