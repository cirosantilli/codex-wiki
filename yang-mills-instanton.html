Yang-Mills instanton

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field-theory soliton Gauge-theory soliton
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 24
A Yang-Mills instanton is a finite-action Euclidean gauge field with self-dual or anti-self-dual curvature. On R4, its asymptotic pure gauge defines a map S∞3​→SU(2) whose degree is the instanton number.
It is a gauge-field instanton solving the Euclidean Yang-Mills equations, with additional finite-action and duality conditions.
