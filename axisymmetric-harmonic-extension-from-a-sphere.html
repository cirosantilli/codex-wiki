Axisymmetric harmonic extension from a sphere (Ψin​=∑ℓ​fℓ​(r/a)ℓPℓ​,Ψout​=∑ℓ​fℓ​(a/r)ℓ+1Pℓ​)

For boundary data f(u) on the sphere r=a, with u=cosθ, define its Legendre polynomial coefficients by fℓ​=(2ℓ+1)∫−11​f(u)Pℓ​(u)du/2. Separation of variables in the Laplace equation gives radial factors rℓ and r−ℓ−1. Regularity at the origin selects the first for the interior Dirichlet problem; decay at infinity selects the second for the exterior problem. Matching each boundary coefficient gives the displayed series. For continuous boundary data the resulting harmonic functions attain those data, with convergence interpreted through their harmonic extension rather than requiring uniform convergence of the unweighted boundary series. The maximum principle for harmonic functions proves uniqueness in the bounded interior and decaying exterior classes.
