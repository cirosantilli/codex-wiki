Density-weighted mean kinematic viscosity (νˉ=Σ−1∫ρνdz)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Dynamic viscosity Kinematic viscosity
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
For a vertically stratified accretion disk with surface density Σ=∫ρdz, the density-weighted mean kinematic viscosity is νˉ=Σ−1∫ρνdz. In an alpha disk with ν=αp/(ρΩ), this becomes νˉ=α(ΣΩ)−1∫pdz.
