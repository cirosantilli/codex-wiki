Plasma (physics)

 Home Physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 49
A plasma is a gas of mobile charged particles whose collective electromagnetic response is important to its dynamics. It commonly contains ions and electrons, and may also contain a neutral component: complete ionization is not required. A multi-species fluid description has current density J=∑s​qs​ns​us​. The relative motion of the species can matter even when a single-fluid magnetohydrodynamics approximation describes the large-scale dynamics.
