Gaussian quadratic exponential moment (Ee⟨b,Z⟩+⟨Z,QZ⟩)

For a real standard Gaussian random vector Z, a real symmetric matrix Q, and I−2Q strictly positive,
EebTZ+ZTQZ=det(I−2Q)−1/2exp[21​bT(I−2Q)−1b].
(65)
Diagonalize Q and complete the square in each coordinate. Failure of strict positivity makes the positive exponential integral divergent. The same formula extends to a self-adjoint trace-class quadratic form of an isonormal Gaussian process, with square-summable linear coefficients and a Fredholm determinant. A linear-plus-quadratic Gaussian random field functional is reduced to this form by its covariance square root.
