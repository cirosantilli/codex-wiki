Rocket exhaust speed (u>0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Momentum Rocket equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The rocket exhaust speed is the magnitude of the emitted material's velocity relative to the instantaneous rocket. In the ideal rearward-ejection convention of the rocket equation, a rocket with inertial velocity v emits material with inertial velocity v−u to first order. A constant u does not make v−u constant.
