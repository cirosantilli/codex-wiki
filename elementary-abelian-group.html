Elementary abelian group

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Abelian group Finite abelian group
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 1
An elementary abelian p-group is a finite abelian group in which every nonidentity element has order p. It is equivalently a finite-dimensional vector space over Fp​ under addition.
