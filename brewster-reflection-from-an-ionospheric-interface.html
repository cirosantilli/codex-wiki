Brewster reflection from an ionospheric interface (D=2h1−ωp2​/ω2​)

 ... Electromagnetic wave Plane electromagnetic wave Interface conditions for electromagnetic fields Dielectric-interface boundary conditions Fresnel equations Brewster's angle
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
In the sharp planar, nonmagnetic ionosphere model with frequency above the plasma frequency, the refractive index is n=1−ωp2​/ω2​<1. From air, the Brewster angle satisfies tanθB​=n. At that angle the reflected p-polarized wave vanishes, while s polarization retains a nonzero reflected component. A ground-to-ground single reflection from height h spans D=2htanθB​=2hn. This is below the critical angle for total internal reflection for total internal reflection, which reflects both polarizations in this lossless isotropic model. The distinction diagnoses ordinary Brewster reflection rather than total internal reflection.
