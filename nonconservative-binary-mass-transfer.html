Nonconservative binary mass transfer

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Binary star Roche lobe Roche-lobe overflow
OurBigBook.com Words: 588 Articles: 9
In nonconservative binary evolution, matter can escape the system and/or external processes change its orbital angular momentum. Escaping overflow, a stellar wind and a common envelope can invalidate the fixed-total-mass and period-product invariants of conservative mass transfer.
