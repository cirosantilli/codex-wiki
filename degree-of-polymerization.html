Degree of polymerization (X, Xn​)

 Home Chemistry Polymer
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The degree of polymerization counts monomeric units in a chain. Xn​ is its number average over a specified collection of chains. State whether unreacted monomers are included and distinguish this count from constitutional repeating units or statistical segments. See IUPAC: degree of polymerization.
