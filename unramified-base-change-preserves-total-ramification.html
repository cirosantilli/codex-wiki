Unramified base change preserves total ramification

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Ramification index Totally ramified extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 1
If L/K is an unramified extension and M/K is a totally ramified extension of non-Archimedean local fields inside a fixed algebraic closure, then LM/L is totally ramified of degree [M:K]. A uniformizer of M has an Eisenstein polynomial over K. Since a uniformizer of K remains a uniformizer in L, that polynomial remains Eisenstein over L and generates LM.
