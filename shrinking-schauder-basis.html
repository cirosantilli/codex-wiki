Shrinking Schauder basis

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Schauder basis Coordinate functional of a Schauder basis Dual sequence of a Schauder basis
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A Schauder basis is shrinking when its dual sequence is a Schauder basis of the whole dual space. Every Schauder basis of a reflexive Banach space is shrinking.
