Rotational latency (Trot​/2)

 Home Computer science Input-output Data storage device Hard disk drive
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The wait for the requested sector to reach the disk head after positioning on its track. For a uniformly distributed arrival phase and rotation period Trot​, its expected value is half a rotation. At r revolutions per minute, this is 30/r seconds.
