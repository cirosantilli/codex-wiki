Correlation length (ξ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Correlation function
OurBigBook.com Words: 114 Articles: 2
The correlation length is the characteristic distance over which a connected correlation function decays. It diverges at an ordinary continuous critical point as ξ∼∣t∣−ν.
