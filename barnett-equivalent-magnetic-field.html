Barnett equivalent magnetic field (BΩ​=Ω/γ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential Magnetic dipole moment Barnett effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 271
Let the specimen rotate uniformly with angular velocity Ω=Ωz. In its rotating reference frame, the internal Hamiltonian contains
Hrot​=H0​−ΩJz​.
(66)
Here Jz​ is the internal angular momentum operator about the rotation axis, and H0​ contains the internal interactions and any imposed magnetic field expressed in that frame. It does not include the separately treated rigid body's rotational kinetic energy. For a sector with magnetic moment mz​=γJz​, the rotational term has exactly the form of a magnetic dipole potential energy:
−ΩJz​=−mz​BΩ​,BΩ​=γΩ​.
(67)
This Barnett equivalent magnetic field has tesla units, with the angular convention for the signed gyromagnetic ratio: Ω is in radians per second, not revolutions per second. Its sign matters. For an electronic contribution with γ<0, positive Ω favors positive Jz​ and negative mz​. A sector with γ>0 has the opposite magnetic sign. Equal Ω need not correspond to equal Barnett fields for sectors having different gyromagnetic ratios.
A short derivation fixes the sign independently of any material model. Put U(t)=exp(−iΩtJz​/ℏ) and transform a laboratory quantum state by ∣ψrot​⟩=U†∣ψlab​⟩. The Schrödinger equation gives
Hrot​=U†Hlab​U−iℏU†U˙=H0​−ΩJz​.
(68)
The transformed laboratory term is denoted by H0​; a laboratory perturbation not stationary in the rotating frame can make it time dependent. Establishing a steady thermal response also requires exchange of internal angular momentum with the rotating body, for example through spin-lattice relaxation. A mere relabeling of coordinates cannot polarize isolated, unrelaxed spins.
The Barnett field is a useful description of spin-rotation coupling, not a claim that empty laboratory space acquires a uniform magnetic flux density Ω/γ. In particular, inserting it into the Lorentz force on an unrelated test electric charge is unjustified. A nearby magnetometer detects the specimen's actual stray magnetic field and backgrounds, rather than directly detecting this internal Hamiltonian parameter.
