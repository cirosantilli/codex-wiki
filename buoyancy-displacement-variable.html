Buoyancy displacement variable (bx​=−N2θ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Buoyancy Buoyancy perturbation
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
In a radially stratified shearing sheet, one can normalize the scalar buoyancy perturbation by writing bx​=−N2θ and Dt​θ=ux​+ξ∇2θ. With this convention θ has dimensions of length, rather than temperature; ξ is thermal diffusivity.
