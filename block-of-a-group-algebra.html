Block of a group algebra

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Modular representation theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 44
A block of a finite group algebra is an indecomposable two-sided ideal RGe determined by a primitive central idempotent e. An RG-module M lies in this block when eM=M.
