Mathematical induction

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical proof
OurBigBook.com Words: 189 Articles: 5
A mathematical induction proof establishes a property P(n) for every integer n≥n0​ by proving its induction base case and its inductive step. If a counterexample existed, the well-ordering principle would give a least one; its predecessor and the inductive implication exclude it. The starting case excludes a counterexample at n0​.
