Doppler-free saturation spectroscopy

 Home Physics Atomic physics Atomic measurement methods Laser spectroscopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 8
Doppler-free saturation spectroscopy pairs a strong pump with an opposed weak probe. Shared populations produce a narrow Lamb dip within the Doppler broadening of a spectral line.
