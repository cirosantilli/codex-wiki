Local extremum

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Maximum and minimum
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A local extremum of a real-valued function is either a local maximum or a local minimum, with comparisons restricted to a neighbourhood in its domain. A differentiable interior local extremum is a stationary point, but the converse can fail, as f(x)=x3 at zero shows.
