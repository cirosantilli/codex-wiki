Tau neutrino (ντ​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Lepton number Neutrino
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 3
The neutrino flavor associated with the tau lepton in the weak charged current. Its direct detection through interactions producing a tau lepton was announced by the DONUT experiment in 2000.[ref]
