Kripke model for intuitionistic propositional logic

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Intuitionistic propositional logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 221 Articles: 7
A Kripke model is a poset of worlds with a persistent valuation of atoms. Conjunction and disjunction are forced pointwise, while w⊩α→β exactly when every v≥w that forces α also forces β.
