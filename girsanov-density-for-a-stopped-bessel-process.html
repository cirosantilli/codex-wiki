For reference Brownian motion R=x+W, stopped at T=t∧τ(a,b)​ with 0<a<x<b, this expression is the Radon-Nikodym derivative of the stopped Bessel process law of dimension δ. The bounded stopped drift verifies the Novikov condition. The comparison laws must have the same initial value and the same stopping rule.
