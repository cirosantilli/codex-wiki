The five-point Laplacian on the square grid is
Δh​Wi,j​=h2Wi+1,j​+Wi−1,j​+Wi,j+1​+Wi,j−1​−4Wi,j​​,1≤i,j≤N−1.​
(22)
It adds the second-order central differences in the two coordinate directions and uses the given zero Dirichlet boundary conditions when a neighbouring node lies on the boundary.
The sine addition formula implies
sin((i+1)α)+sin((i−1)α)−2sin(iα)=2(cosα−1)sin(iα)=−4sin2(α/2)sin(iα).
(23)
Apply this identity with α=mπh in the x direction and with α=nπh in the y direction. For Ui,j(m,n)​=sin(mπih)sin(nπjh), it gives
−Δh​Ui,j(m,n)​+Ui,j(m,n)​=[1+h24​(sin22mπh​+sin22nπh​)]Ui,j(m,n)​.
(24)
Hence the discrete eigenvalues are
Λm,n​=1+h24​(sin22mπh​+sin22nπh​)>0,1≤m,n≤N−1.​
(25)
Both sine factors vanish on their respective boundary sides. Also U1,1(m,n)​>0, since mπ/N and nπ/N lie strictly between zero and π. Thus each array is a nonzero discrete eigenvector, rather than merely the zero solution of the homogeneous system. The separated structure is also described by the five-point Dirichlet Laplacian as a Kronecker sum.
