Bloch sphere (S2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Bloch vector
OurBigBook.com Words: 142 Articles: 2
The Bloch sphere is the unit sphere of Bloch vectors, corresponding to pure qubit states through ρ=(I+r⋅σ)/2. A local unitary change of qubit basis induces a rotation of this sphere, with its common phase physically irrelevant. Mixed states occupy the interior of the unit ball.
