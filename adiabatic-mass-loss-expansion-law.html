Adiabatic mass-loss expansion law

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy evolution Stellar feedback
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
If a self-gravitating system loses mass slowly compared with its orbital times, adiabatic invariance gives MR=constant in the homologous approximation, so retaining fraction ϵ expands the system by R′/R=1/ϵ.
