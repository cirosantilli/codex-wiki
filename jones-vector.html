Jones vector (J=(Jx​,Jy​)T)

 Home Physics Wave Polarization (waves) Jones calculus
OurBigBook.com Words: 128 Articles: 1
A Jones vector records the two transverse complex amplitudes of a completely polarized monochromatic wave. Multiplication by a common nonzero complex number preserves its polarization of waves, while changing overall amplitude and phase. With the convention Ψ(t)=Re[Jeiωt], J=(1,2i)T gives (cosωt,−2sinωt), an ellipse with the longer axis along y. Handedness labels require both a viewing direction and a naming convention.
