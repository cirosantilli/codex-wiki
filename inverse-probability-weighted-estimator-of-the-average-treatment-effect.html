Inverse-probability-weighted estimator of the average treatment effect (ATEIPW​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Causal inference Inverse probability weighting
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
For binary treatment, outcomes Yi​, and estimated propensity score e(Xi​), the inverse-probability-weighted estimator is
ATEIPW​=n1​∑i=1n​{e(Xi​)Ai​Yi​​−1−e(Xi​)(1−Ai​)Yi​​}.
(7)
It is consistent under conditional exchangeability, consistency of potential outcomes, positivity in causal inference, and a consistent propensity-score estimator.
