Independent censoring

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis Censoring (statistics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 284 Articles: 5
Independent censoring means that, conditional on the variables used in the analysis, the censoring time carries no further information about the event time. It lets the observed risk set represent those who would remain at risk without censoring.
