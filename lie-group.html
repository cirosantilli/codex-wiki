Lie group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 690 Articles: 26
A Lie group is a group that is also a smooth manifold, with smooth multiplication and inversion. Its tangent space at the identity carries a natural Lie algebra structure.
