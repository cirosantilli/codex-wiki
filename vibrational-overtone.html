Vibrational overtone (Δv=2,3,…)

 Home Chemistry Molecule Molecular vibration
OurBigBook.com Words: 121
A vibrational overtone excites more than one vibrational quantum: v=0→2 is the first overtone, and 0→3 the second. Such transitions vanish in the combined harmonic-potential and linear-dipole approximation. Anharmonicity changes wavefunctions and level spacing; nonlinear terms in the electric dipole moment can also supply otherwise missing transition matrix elements.[ref]
For an original illustrative level model, set
Ev​−V0​=ℏω[(v+1/2)−ϵ(v+1/2)2],0<ϵ≪1.
(35)
Then E1​−E0​=ℏω(1−2ϵ) and E2​−E0​=2ℏω(1−3ϵ). With chosen ϵ=0.02, the fundamental gap is 0.96ℏω and the first-overtone gap 1.88ℏω, below twice the fundamental, 1.92ℏω. This low-level illustration does not determine transition intensity or justify extrapolation to arbitrarily large v. It shows why even a well-resolved fundamental band origin need not equal the harmonic curvature scale νharm​.
