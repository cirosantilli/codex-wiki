Magnetic spherical-shell matching

 Home Physics Branch of physics Electromagnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
Across an interface with no free surface current, the normal component of B and tangential component of H are continuous. For an axisymmetric spherical shell, harmonic scalar potentials have dipole form (ar+b/r2)cosθ in each radial region.
