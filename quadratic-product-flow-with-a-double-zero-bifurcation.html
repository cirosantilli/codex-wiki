Quadratic-product flow with a double-zero bifurcation (u˙=u(μ+u−v),v˙=λ(u2−v))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory Codimension-two bifurcation Bogdanov–Takens bifurcation
OurBigBook.com Words: 185 Articles: 1
For λ>0, the equilibrium points are the origin and (s,s2) with s=(1±1+4μ​)/2. The nonzero branches have Jacobian trace s−λ and determinant λs(2s−1). They have a saddle-node bifurcation at μ=−1/4, s=1/2; the origin exchanges stability with the lower branch at the transcritical bifurcation μ=0. A Hopf bifurcation occurs on the upper branch at μ=λ(λ−1) for λ>1/2. The point (μ,λ)=(−1/4,1/2) has a double zero eigenvalue and a nontrivial Jordan block. Its generic unfolding includes a saddle-loop curve and the intervening stable periodic orbit region.
