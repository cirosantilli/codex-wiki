Mixed-normalization spline Gram matrix (G=D−1H)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation Spline (mathematics) Spline approximation B-spline
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 2
For partition-normalized B-splines Ni​ and integral-normalized Mi​=kNi​/(ti+k​−ti​), let Hij​=(Ni​,Nj​) and Dii​=(ti+k​−ti​)/k. The mixed matrix Gij​=(Mi​,Nj​) equals D−1H. It is invertible whenever the splines form a basis and their support widths are positive. It need not be symmetric, unlike the ordinary Gram matrix.
