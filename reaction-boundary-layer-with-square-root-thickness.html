Reaction boundary layer with square-root thickness (ϵy′′=y−f(x),X=(1−x)/ϵ​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Singular perturbation Matched asymptotic expansion
OurBigBook.com Words: 101
For a positive small parameter ϵ, an order-one mismatch between the reduced solution y0​=f(x) and a prescribed right endpoint value y(1)=b creates a right boundary layer. Balancing ϵy′′ against the order-one reaction term gives width ϵ​. In the inner variable X=(1−x)/ϵ​, the leading equation is Y0′′​=Y0​−f(1). Its boundary and matching conditions are Y0​(0)=b and Y0​→f(1) as X→∞, so
Y0​(X)=f(1)+(b−f(1))e−X.
(5)
The leading additive composite expansion is f(x)+(b−f(1))e−(1−x)/ϵ​, obtained by subtracting the common overlap value. For the nonlinear perturbation ϵy′′=y−x2+ϵ(2+y2) with zero endpoint values, the first two outer expansion terms are x2−ϵx4, and the leading right-layer term is 1−e−X.
