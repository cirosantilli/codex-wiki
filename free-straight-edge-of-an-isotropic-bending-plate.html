Free straight edge of an isotropic bending plate (wxx​+νwyy​=0,wxxx​+(2−ν)wxyy​=0)

For a plate occupying x>0, the boundary part of the first variation of its isotropic thin-plate bending energy is, up to an overall nonzero sign,
D∫x=0​[ηx​(wxx​+νwyy​)−η(wxxx​+(2−ν)wxyy​)],dy.
(22)
At a free edge the variation and its normal derivative can be prescribed independently. Their coefficients must therefore vanish, giving the two displayed natural boundary conditions: zero bending moment and zero effective transverse shear. The mixed-derivative coefficient includes the derivative along the edge of the twisting moment; keeping only the normal derivative of wxx​+νwyy​ would miss that contribution.
