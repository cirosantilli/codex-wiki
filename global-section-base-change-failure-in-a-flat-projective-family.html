Global-section base-change failure in a flat projective family (H0(X,OX​)⊗R​k≅H0(Xk​,OXk​​))

Flatness of a projective family does not by itself force global sections to commute with base change. For a discrete valuation ring with uniformizer π, glue SpecR[πt,t2,t3] to SpecR[t−1] along SpecR[t,t−1]. In the projective model with coordinates (1,πt,t2,t3), the global ring is R. The special-fibre first chart has an additional square-zero element a annihilated by t2,t3; it vanishes on the overlap and therefore extends by zero to a global section. The special-fibre global ring is k[a]/(a2), and base change from R reaches only its constants.
