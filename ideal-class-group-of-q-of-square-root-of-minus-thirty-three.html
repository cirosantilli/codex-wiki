Ideal class group of Q of square root of minus thirty-three (Cl(Q(−33​)))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Ideal class group Minkowski bound for ideal classes
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The field has ring of integers Z[−33​], discriminant −132, and Minkowski bound below eight. The nonprincipal ramified ideals above two and three generate four classes, and the split primes above seven introduce no new class. Hence
Cl(Q(−33​))≅C2​×C2​.
