Calibrated photometer model

Use the triangular w in equal radiant power under visual weighting and stipulate the detector's spectral responsivity
r(λ)=(0.5AW−1)w(λ)(1+150nmλ−555nm​).
(32)
This model has nonnegative response: w is zero outside 405–705nm. For a 1mW ideal calibration line at 555nm, iA​=0.5mA and Φv,A​=0.683lm, so CA​=1366lmA−1. An equal-radiant flux test line at 630nm gives w=1/2, r=0.375AW−1 and iT​=0.375mA. The unchanged conversion indicates 0.51225lm, whereas the model's intended weighted result is 0.3415lm. The required spectral mismatch correction is F=2/3: the uncorrected result is 50% too large. All these numbers are declared model outputs, not instrument measurements or standard photometry data.
