Landau quasiparticle energy functional (δ(E−μN))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Fermi liquid Landau quasiparticle
OurBigBook.com Words: 192 Articles: 2
Changing one Landau quasiparticle occupation changes the energies of others. To second order around equilibrium,
δ(E−μN)=∑pσ​ξp​δnpσ​+2V1​∑pσ,p′σ′​fσσ′​(p,p′)δnpσ​δnp′σ′​.
(11)
Here V is volume, occupations are per momentum and spin state, and f has units of energy times volume.[ref]
For a toy two-state change at fixed volume, take δna​=δnb​=η, faa​=fbb​=0, fab​=fba​=gV, and unperturbed energies ξa​=ξb​=e. The expression gives 2eη+gη2. The factor 1/2 counts the interaction of the same pair once; it does not halve each excitation's energy. This finite-state illustration only explains the bookkeeping of the Landau quasiparticle energy functional.
