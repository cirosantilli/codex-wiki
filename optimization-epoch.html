Optimization epoch

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Gradient descent
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
A unit of work corresponding to one full pass through the components used to measure an iterative method. For coordinate descent in n coordinates, count n coordinate partial derivative evaluations as an optimization epoch: updates on blocks of size m require n/m updates per optimization epoch. For stochastic gradient descent on a finite sum of losses, a common different convention counts one evaluation of every loss component. The convention must be specified. Random sampling with replacement need not visit every component during one such unit of work.
