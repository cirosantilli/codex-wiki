Slater determinant (Ψ(x1​,…,xN​)=det[uj​(xi​)]/N!​)

 ... Multiparticle quantum state Particle exchange operator Indistinguishable particles Fermion Pauli exclusion principle Two-particle antisymmetrization
OurBigBook.com Words: 79 Articles: 1
A Slater determinant extends two-particle antisymmetrization to N fermions. Here xi​ contains position and spin or other internal variables. Distinct orthonormal one-particle states give the normalized wavefunction displayed in the title. Repeating a one-particle state repeats a column of the determinant, so the wavefunction vanishes. Exchanging particle slots exchanges rows and reverses its sign. A general fermionic quantum state can be a quantum superposition of Slater determinants; it need not be one Slater determinant.
