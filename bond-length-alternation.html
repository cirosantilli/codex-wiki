Bond-length alternation (βS​−βL​)

 Home Chemistry Chemical bond Conjugated system
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
Alternation between shorter and longer chemical bonds along a conjugated system. In the Hückel method, unequal nearest-neighbour couplings split the frontier molecular orbitals. An electronic energy gain must be compared at a stated constraint on the mean coupling and against the geometric distortion cost.
