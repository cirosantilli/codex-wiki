Two hinged rods with both ends sliding on a floor (vhinge​=6glsinα​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
Two identical uniform rigid bodies of rod shape, each of length 2l and mass M, form a symmetric arch with a free common hinge and both outer ends sliding without friction on a horizontal floor. At angle θ, each center has speed l∣θ˙∣, giving total kinetic energy 4Ml2θ˙2/3. The total gravitational potential energy is 2Mglsinθ. Conservation of energy from rest at α gives the displayed hinge impact speed. Unlike fixing one outer endpoint, allowing both endpoints to slide keeps the arch center horizontally fixed. The floor reaction on each rod is Mg[1+9sin2θ−6sinαsinθ]/4, nonnegative throughout this fall.
