Expand the two observations in order using the chain rule for mutual information:
I(X,Y;Z)=I(X;Z)+I(Y;Z∣X).
(2)
The remaining conditional mutual information is
I(Y;Z∣X)=∑x​pX​(x)D(pY,Z∣x​​pY∣x​pZ∣x​)≥0.
(3)
Relative entropy nonnegativity proves the inequality. Equality requires every positive-weight relative entropy to vanish, which is equivalent to p(y,z∣x)=p(y∣x)p(z∣x) for every x of positive probability. Thus equality holds exactly when Y and Z have conditional independence given X. No unconditional independence is required.
