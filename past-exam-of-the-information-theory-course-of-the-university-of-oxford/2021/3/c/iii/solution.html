Choose noise values 0,2, giving exactly the outputs 0,1,2,3,4. The transition matrix, in that column order, is
M=​1/200​01/20​1/201/2​01/20​001/2​​.
(32)
An input probability mass function (1/2,1/2,0) makes outputs 0,1,2,3 equiprobable, so H(Y)=2 and H(Y∣X)=1. Hence C≥1 bit per use, as required for comparison with the four-output case.
There is a strict improvement, which can also be computed exactly. Reflection of the input labels 0↔2, accompanied by output reflection y↔4−y, leaves mutual information unchanged. Concavity of information entropy makes mutual information a concave function of the input probability mass function for a fixed transition matrix. Averaging an input law with its reflection therefore cannot decrease it. It suffices to consider
pX​=(s/2,1−s,s/2),0≤s≤1.
(33)
The outputs whose labels are even integers have total mass s and conditional probability mass function (1/4,1/2,1/4), with information entropy 3/2. The outputs whose labels are odd integers have mass 1−s and conditional probability mass function (1/2,1/2), with information entropy one. Splitting first by output parity using the chain rule for information entropy yields
H(Y)=h2​(s)+23​s+(1−s),I(X;Y)=h2​(s)+2s​.
(34)
Its derivative is log2​((1−s)/s)+1/2, and its second derivative is negative. The unique maximum occurs at s=2​/(1+2​), giving
C=log2​(1+2​)≈1.271553,pX​=(2+2​1​,1+2​1​,2+2​1​).​
(35)
This additional optimization explains the collision pattern: outputs 1,3 identify input 1, outputs 0,4 identify the two extreme inputs, and only output 2 is ambiguous.
