Cubic-scalar exchange corrections at zero momentum

OurBigBook.com Words: 162
In the six-dimensional cubic scalar field theory, use full external-propagator amputation and tadpole subtraction. The exchange part of the zero-momentum four-point amplitude is
A4,exchange​=m23g2​+m26g4​I3​+2m43g4​I2​+O(two loops).
(8)
The triangle corrects either end of each of three exchanges, and the symmetry factor of an internal bubble is 1/2. Equivalently insert Γ(3)=g+g3I3​ and Γ(2)=m2−g2I2​/2 into 3[Γ(3)]2/Γ(2). Without tadpole subtraction, the shift v=−gI1​/(2m2) adds 3g4I1​/(2m6). None of these is an additional local 1PI quartic coupling; zero-momentum bridges are also excluded from a strict high-mode Wilsonian shell. If bare rather than full external propagators are amputated, external-line decorations additionally contribute 6g4I2​/m4, and 6g4I1​/m6 without tadpole subtraction. Every displayed term has mass dimension minus two. In a renormalized expansion, the exchange diagrams also receive the counterterm insertions 6gδg/m2−3g2δm2/m4. Without enforcing a zero one-point function, a linear counterterm δhϕ contributes 3g3δh/m6 through the background shift; choosing δh=−gI1​/2 cancels the attached tadpole. A kinetic counterterm is proportional to the exchanged momentum squared, so its insertion vanishes at this zero-momentum point.
