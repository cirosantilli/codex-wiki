Contact process

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Interacting particle system
OurBigBook.com Words: 890 Articles: 13
In the contact process, infected vertices recover at rate μ, while each oriented nearest-neighbour edge transmits infection at rate λ. The empty configuration is absorbing.
