Retarded Green function (GR​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Finite-temperature quantum field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 154 Articles: 5
For a bosonic operator A, the retarded Green function is GR​(t)=−iθ(t)⟨[A(t),A(0)]⟩. Causality makes its Fourier transform analytic in the upper half frequency plane.
