Sylow subgroups of A6 (n5​=36,n3​=10)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Sylow theorems Sylow subgroup
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
In the alternating group A6​, of order 360, a Sylow subgroup of order five is generated by a five-cycle. There are 6⋅4!=144 five-cycles, four in each such subgroup, giving 36 subgroups. A Sylow subgroup of order nine is generated by disjoint three-cycles, hence is C3​×C3​. The unordered partitions of six letters into two triples give all such subgroups by Sylow conjugacy, and their single three-cycles recover the partition uniquely. There are (36​)/2=10 partitions.
