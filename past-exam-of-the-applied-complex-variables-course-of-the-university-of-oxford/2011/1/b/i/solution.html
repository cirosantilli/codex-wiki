The imaginary part of the complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow is the stream function. Its constant values on the wall and the free boundary make the potential domain the horizontal strip
0<Imw<1.​
(10)
The lower edge is the rigid wall, with B at w=0. The upstream end has Rew→−∞, and the downstream end has Rew→+∞. The upper edge is the free streamline. Thus AB and BC are the negative and positive portions of the real w axis, respectively.
Write q=w′=u−iv for the complex velocity. Downstream q→1. Upstream the fluid moves towards the corner along a wall of inclination π/4, so its physical velocity has argument −3π/4 and its complex velocity has argument 3π/4. Along each straight wall the velocity direction is fixed. At the corner the fluid angle is π/4, giving local behavior w∼K(z−B)4 and hence q→0: B is a stagnation point. On the free streamline, the speed is one, so ∣q∣=1.
The corresponding simple-jet hodograph plane is therefore the circular sector
0<argq<43π​,0<∣q∣<1.​
(11)
The radius at argument 3π/4 represents AB, the positive real radius represents BC, and the unit-circle arc represents the free boundary. The upstream and downstream ends are e3πi/4 and one, respectively; the corner is the sector's origin.
