Kummer-Dedekind theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 155 Articles: 2
The factorization of a defining polynomial modulo a prime, provided the index is coprime to that prime, determines the prime ideals, ramification indices and residue degrees in the corresponding ring of integers.
