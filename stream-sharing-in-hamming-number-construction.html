Stream sharing in Hamming-number construction

 Home Computer science Programming language Functional programming Lazy feedback stream Sorted multiplicative closure stream
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
The Hamming number stream merges its shared multiples by two, three and five. To emit its first n values, each of the fixed number of mapped streams and merge nodes advances at most n+O(1) positions. With lazy evaluation that shares each evaluated list cell, this takes Θ(n) comparisons and fixed-factor multiplications. The statement counts arithmetic operations, rather than bits: the growing exact integers make their bit-operation costs larger. Recomputing independent copies of the recursively defined stream loses this sharing argument.
