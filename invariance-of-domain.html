Invariance of domain

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
Invariance of domain says that a continuous map that is an injective function between topological manifolds without boundary of the same dimension is an open map. It is useful in the Penrose singularity theorem: projecting a compact achronal boundary along timelike curves onto a Cauchy hypersurface gives a nonempty subset that is both open and closed, forcing that connected Cauchy hypersurface to be compact.
