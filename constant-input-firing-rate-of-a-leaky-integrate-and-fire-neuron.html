Constant-input firing rate of a leaky integrate-and-fire neuron

 Home Biology Neuroscience Computational neuroscience Leaky integrate-and-fire model
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For Cm​V˙=−gL​(V−VL​)+I, reset V0​<Vθ​ and no explicit refractory interval, define Ic​=gL​(Vθ​−VL​) and τm​=Cm​/gL​. Constant current gives
r(I)={0,[τm​log((I−gL​(V0​−VL​))/(I−Ic​))]−1,​I≤Ic​,I>Ic​.​
(1)
The threshold is approached only asymptotically at I=Ic​. At large current, r∼I/[Cm​(Vθ​−V0​)]. Adding an absolute neuronal refractory period changes the denominator to T(I)+tref​ and makes the firing rate saturate rather than grow without bound.
