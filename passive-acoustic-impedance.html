Passive acoustic impedance (ReZ≥0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 56
With pressure amplitude P=ZV and velocity V directed into a boundary, the mean absorbed acoustic energy flux is 21​Re(PV∗)=21​ReZ∣V∣2. A passive boundary cannot supply net energy, so ReZ≥0 at a real frequency. Its reflection from a propagating acoustic plane wave has magnitude at most one. A pole continued to a complex incidence angle or frequency must be interpreted separately from real-frequency passive scattering.
