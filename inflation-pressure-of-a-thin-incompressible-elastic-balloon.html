At leading thin-shell order, a spherical balloon with reference radius a0​, thickness h0​ and current radius a=λa0​ has reference elastic energy 4πa02​h0​W(λ,λ,λ−2). Equating its differential to pressure work p4πa2da gives the displayed pressure difference. Its thickness becomes h0​λ−2 by incompressibility. The same result follows from the spherical membrane force balance p=2hσt​/a, with current tangential Cauchy stress tensor component σt​=λN and the equibiaxial nominal tension of an incompressible sheet. Thin-shell validity requires h/a≪1.
