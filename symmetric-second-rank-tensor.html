Symmetric second-rank tensor (Tij​=Tji​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor Symmetric tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
A second-rank tensor is symmetric when Tij​=Tji​. Every second-rank tensor has the unique decomposition
Tij​=2Tij​+Tji​​+2Tij​−Tji​​
(20)
into symmetric and antisymmetric parts.
