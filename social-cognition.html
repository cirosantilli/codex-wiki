Social cognition

 Home Science Psychology Social psychology
OurBigBook.com Words: 844 Articles: 26
The processes through which people represent other individuals, relationships and groups. Social cognition includes social attribution, emotion recognition and theory of mind, which need not rely on identical mechanisms.
