Hazel model

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Hydrodynamic stability Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow Inviscid parallel shear flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 192 Articles: 2
The Hazel model is a smooth stratified shear flow whose base velocity has a hyperbolic-tangent profile and whose squared buoyancy frequency has a hyperbolic-secant-squared profile. The equal-width nondimensional example is U=tanhz and N2=Jsech2z. For J≥0, its gradient Richardson number is Jcosh2z, with minimum J.
