Magnetic field-line curvature (κ=b⋅∇b)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic field Magnetic field line
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The curvature vector of a magnetic field line parametrized by arc length. For circular lines, it points towards their center and has magnitude 1/R. The curvature contribution to magnetic tension is B2κ/(4π) in Gaussian units.
