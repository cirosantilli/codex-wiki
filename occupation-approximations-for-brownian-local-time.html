Occupation approximations for Brownian local time (Lt0​=limn→∞​2n​∫0t​1{∣Bs​∣<1/n}​ds)

For Brownian motion started at zero, let fn​ equal ∣x∣ outside (−1/n,1/n) and nx2/2+1/(2n) inside. Its derivative is the clipped sign function gn​(x)=max{−1,min{1,nx}}. The Itô integral Mt(n)​=∫0t​gn​(Bs​)dBs​ converges uniformly on bounded time intervals in L2 to a continuous martingale M, by the Itô isometry and Doob L2 maximal inequality: the squared maximal error is at most 82T/π​/n. The Itô formula gives 2n​∫0t​1{∣Bs​∣<1/n}​ds=fn​(Bt​)−fn​(0)−Mt(n)​. Along n=k2, the maximal errors are summable, so First Borel-Cantelli lemma gives uniform almost-sure convergence. The ratio-one interpolation for shrinking-window occupation integrals extends it to every integer n. The limit is ∣Bt​∣−Mt​=Lt0​, the local time of a semimartingale in the Tanaka formula convention. The unnormalized factor n gives twice this limit.
