Ionospheric interference fringe spacing (Δf≃c/ΔL)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Upper atmosphere Ionosphere Appleton-Barnett radio interference experiment
OurBigBook.com Words: 458
Consider an original two-path model with a locally flat ground baseline d and a symmetric equivalent return height h. Replace the skywave by two straight segments traveling at the speed of light c, and replace the ground wave by a straight segment of length d. Their lengths and positive excess distance are
Ls​=2h2+(d/2)2​,Lg​=d,ΔL=Ls​−Lg​>0.
(54)
This is an equivalent geometric construction, not a metallic layer overhead. It neglects the curvature of Earth, refraction within the return region, ground-induced wave phase shifts and optical dispersion.
For received complex amplitudes Ag​ and As​, absorb their constant relative wave phase into ϕ0​ and choose their magnitudes nonnegative. An ideal detector reading proportional to squared received field has
P(f)=K[Ag2​+As2​+2Ag​As​cosΦ(f)],Φ(f)=c2πfΔL​+ϕ0​,K>0.
(55)
Assume ΔL and ϕ0​ are independent of frequency across the sweep, with slowly changing amplitudes. Consecutive maxima differ by 2π in phase difference, so
Φ(f+Δf)−Φ(f)=c2πΔfΔL​=2π,Δf=ΔLc​.
(56)
The often-used approximation Δf≃c/ΔL therefore carries a frequency-independent-path assumption. Counting N complete fringe periods between f1​ and f2​ gives ΔL≃Nc/∣f2​−f1​∣; equivalently, N=ΔL∣1/λ2​−1/λ1​∣ when the vacuum wavelength is λ=c/f. Count whole periods, not maxima plus minima as though each were a whole period.
Choose d=150km and h=100km, with c≃3.00×108ms−1. Then Ls​=250km, ΔL=100km and Δf=3.00kHz. A 12.0kHz sweep covers four full fringe periods. Conversely, the inferred equivalent height is
h=21​(d+ΔL)2−d2​=100km.
(57)
These chosen values illustrate the inversion; they are not a transcription of the 1924 measurements. With Ag​=1 and As​=0.7, the normalized reading ranges from (1−0.7)2=0.09 to (1+0.7)2=2.89: incomplete cancellation does not remove the distance information.
For a sweep with linearly changing frequency f(t)=f0​+f˙​t and two constant delays τs​>τg​, the received instantaneous frequency difference has magnitude ∣f˙​∣(τs​−τg​). In this model τs​−τg​=ΔL/c, giving a beat frequency ∣f˙​∣ΔL/c. Equivalently, traversing each Δf of the sweep crosses one fringe. This calculation requires a sufficiently slow sweep that propagation, tuning and amplitude changes can be treated consistently; it is not a beat between two independent transmitters.
More generally, measure the total phase difference Φ(f), including propagation and any reflection or apparatus contribution. Define the differential group delay
Δτg​=2π1​dfdΦ​.
(58)
For a locally monotone wave phase with slowly varying derivative, the local spacing is Δf≃1/∣Δτg​∣. If Φ=2πfD(f)/c+ϕr​(f), this becomes
Δτg​=cD+fD′​+2π1​ϕr′​(f).
(59)
Here D(f) is a phase-equivalent optical path difference, not necessarily the geometric excess distance ΔL. A drifting ionosphere, several simultaneous skywaves, changing wave polarization, a frequency-dependent reflection wave phase or significant loss can invalidate the simple constant-spacing inversion.
