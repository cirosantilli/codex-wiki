Fermi wavevector (kF​=pF​/ℏ)

 ... Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Fermi-Dirac distribution Fermi gas Fermi energy Fermi momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 1
The Fermi wavevector is the radius of the occupied sphere in wavevector coordinates for an isotropic Fermi gas. In the uniform two-component electron model, kF​=(3π2n)1/3 and EF​=ℏ2kF2​/(2me​) for nonrelativistic particles. Its units are inverse length, whereas the Fermi momentum has units of momentum.
