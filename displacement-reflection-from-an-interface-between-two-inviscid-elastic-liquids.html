For a P-wave incident at angle θ from a liquid (ρ,c,λ) onto (ρ′,c′,λ′), with transmitted angle θ′ obeying Snell law for elastic and acoustic waves,
R=λ′sin2θ+λsin2θ′λ′sin2θ−λsin2θ′​.
(14)
No reflection is equivalent to ρ′c′cosθ=ρccosθ′.
