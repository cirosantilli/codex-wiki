For a continuous local martingale starting at zero, finite expected terminal quadratic variation is equivalent to being an L2-bounded continuous martingale. Stop at bounded path and bracket levels and use E(Mtτ​)2=E[M]t∧τ​. Uniform integrability removes the localization; Doob L2 maximal inequality justifies convergence of squares.
