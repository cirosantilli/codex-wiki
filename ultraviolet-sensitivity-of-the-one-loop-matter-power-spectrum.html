Ultraviolet sensitivity of the one-loop matter power spectrum

OurBigBook.com Words: 38
For internal momentum q≫k, the P22 contribution to the one-loop matter power spectrum starts at k4, while twice the P13 contribution to the one-loop matter power spectrum starts at k2P(k). The latter has the same momentum dependence as the effective sound-speed counterterm in large-scale structure. Short-scale stochastic contributions start at k4 by mass and momentum conservation.
