Normalization

 Home Mathematics
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Choosing the multiplicative scale of a mathematical or physical quantity by a stated convention. Examples include a probability density integrating to one, a quantum state having unit norm, and relativistic spinors satisfying uˉu=2m. A quoted normalization must specify the constraint and the underlying units or state convention.
