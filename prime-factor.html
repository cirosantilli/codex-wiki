Prime factor

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Prime number
OurBigBook.com Words: 51 Articles: 1
A prime factor of a nonzero integer n is a prime number p satisfying p∣n. For 360=23⋅32⋅5, the distinct prime factors are 2,3,5, while counting repeated factors gives six prime entries. A prime factorization retains their multiplicities: knowing only the distinct prime factors does not distinguish 12=22⋅3 from 18=2⋅32. A negative integer has the same prime factors as its absolute value.
