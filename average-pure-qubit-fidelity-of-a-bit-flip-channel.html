Average pure-qubit fidelity of a bit-flip channel (⟨F2⟩=1−2p/3)

OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For pure input ∣ψ⟩, squared quantum fidelity after the bit-flip channel is (1−p)+p∣⟨ψ∣X∣ψ⟩∣2. The last factor is the square of the input's Bloch x component. Rotational symmetry of the uniform pure-qubit average gives ⟨rx2​⟩=1/3, because rx2​+ry2​+rz2​=1. Hence the average squared fidelity is 1−2p/3. Averaging an unsquared fidelity or over a different ensemble gives a different quantity.
