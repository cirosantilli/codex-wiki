Negation introduction rule (¬P[P] ⋮ ⊥​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Natural deduction
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
If a natural deduction subproof derives logical falsity from a temporary assumption P, discharge that assumption to conclude ¬P. Other undischarged assumptions remain available. This gives a constructive proof of a negation and does not require double-negation elimination.
