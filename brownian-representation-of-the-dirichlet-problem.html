Brownian representation of the Dirichlet problem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Dirichlet problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 91 Articles: 1
Let D be bounded, let u be harmonic on D and continuous on D, and let τD​ be the first exit time of Brownian motion. The Itô formula makes u(Bt∧τD​​) a bounded martingale, so
u(x)=Ex​[u(BτD​​)].
(507)
If u=f on the boundary, this becomes u(x)=Ex​[f(BτD​​)].
