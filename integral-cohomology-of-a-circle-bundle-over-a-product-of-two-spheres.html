Let a,b be the standard basis of H2(S2×S2;Z) and let E→S2×S2 be an oriented circle bundle with Euler class pa+qb=0. If d=gcd(∣p∣,∣q∣), its Gysin sequence gives
Hi(E;Z)≅⎩⎨⎧​Z,Z⊕Z/d,Z/d,0,​i=0,3,5,i=2,i=4,otherwise.​
(50)
For zero Euler class the bundle is trivial and its cohomology is that of (S2×S2)×S1.
