Novikov's theorem

OurBigBook.com Words: 39
For a centered Gaussian random field W with covariance C and a sufficiently regular functional F[W], the Furutsu--Novikov formula is the functional integration by parts identity
E[W(x)F[W]]=∫C(x,y)E[δW(y)δF​]dy.
(198)
It closes moment equations for systems driven multiplicatively by Gaussian fluctuations.
