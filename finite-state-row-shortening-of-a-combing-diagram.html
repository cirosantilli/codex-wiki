Finite-state row shortening of a combing diagram

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Combing of a group Combable group Finite presentation from a synchronous combing
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
For a null loop of length n, a horizontal row of its combing diagram is encoded by an ordered n-tuple of rung words of length at most K. If there are D such words, there are at most Dn row types. Between two identical labelled rows one can delete the entire intervening annular band and glue the equal boundary paths. Repeating this leaves at most Dn layers of n bounded-perimeter cells. Thus a synchronous combable group has a recursive exponential isoperimetric bound. This does not require the combing language to be regular or computable.
