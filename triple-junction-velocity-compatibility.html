Triple-junction velocity compatibility

 ... Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth Mantle convection Plate tectonics Triple junction
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
For a boundary with in-plane normal n, an admissible junction velocity U obeys (U−vboundary​)⋅n=0. A ridge uses the mean of adjacent plate velocities in the symmetric model, a trench uses the overriding plate velocity, and a transform uses either adjoining plate velocity. Compatibility requires a common intersection of the three velocity-space lines.
