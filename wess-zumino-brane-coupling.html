Wess-Zumino brane coupling (qTp​∫Σ​X∗Ap+1​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Brane
OurBigBook.com Words: 240 Articles: 3
A charged brane couples to an appropriate differential-form gauge potential through its pullback. Under A↦A+dΛ, a closed worldvolume changes the action by a boundary term, by Stokes theorem. Reversing the brane charge reverses this coupling, which matters in probe-force and supersymmetry calculations.
