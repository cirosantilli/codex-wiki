Singular value decomposition

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 144 Articles: 4
Every complex matrix has a factorization A=VΣU† with U,V unitary and Σ diagonal with nonnegative entries. The squared singular values are the eigenvalues of A†A.
