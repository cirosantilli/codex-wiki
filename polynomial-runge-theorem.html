Polynomial Runge theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Runge's theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 627 Articles: 10
If K⊂C is compact with connected complement and f is holomorphic near K, then for each ε>0 there is a polynomial p such that supK​∣p−f∣<ε.
