Morse-Smale complex (∂p=∑indq=indp−1​n(p,q)q)

Here n(p,q) is the signed number of unparametrized trajectories of a Morse-Smale gradient flow from p to q. Compactifying one-dimensional trajectory moduli spaces gives ∂2=0. The generators coincide with the cells furnished by the unstable manifolds, and the trajectory count is their cellular incidence number, so the complex computes integral homology.
