Incremental cost-effectiveness ratio (ICER=ΔC/ΔE)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Statistical decision theory Cost-effectiveness analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
The incremental cost-effectiveness ratio divides an intervention's additional mean cost by its additional mean effect relative to a comparator. Its units are cost per outcome unit. When both increments are positive, comparison with a threshold λ is equivalent to checking whether the incremental net monetary benefit λΔE−ΔC is positive. When ΔE<0 the inequality reverses on division, and when ΔE=0 the ratio is undefined. A ratio alone cannot identify the quadrant of the cost-effectiveness plane.
