Unramified residue generator integral basis (OL​=⨁j=0f−1​OK​αj)

OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 1
For an unramified extension of local fields L/K, an integral element whose residue generates kL​/kK​ generates L/K: its degree is at least the residue degree f=[L:K]. The reduced powers 1,αˉ,…,αˉf−1 are a basis of the residue field extension. If x=∑cj​αj, factor out a coefficient of least valuation. Reduction of the remaining sum is nonzero by that residue basis, so vL​(x)=minj​vK​(cj​). Consequently x is integral exactly when all cj​ are integral.
