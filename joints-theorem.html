Joints theorem (∣J∣≲n​Ln/(n−1))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Incidence geometry Joint of a line collection
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 1
The number of joints of a line collection formed by L distinct real affine lines in Rn is at most Cn​Ln/(n−1). The pruning and minimal-degree polynomial argument proves this bound. The exponent is sharp: the coordinate-line grid has mn joints of a line collection and nmn−1 lines. In three dimensions the bound is CL3/2.
