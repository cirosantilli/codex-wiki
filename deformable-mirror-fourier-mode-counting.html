Deformable-mirror Fourier-mode counting

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Adaptive optics Deformable mirror
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
For actuator pitch D/N, full-cycle spatial frequency components are limited to N/2 by sampling. Integer lattice counting in a circular frequency disk gives approximately πN2/4 signed points; conjugate pairing halves the number of pairs, and two real wave phase quadratures per pair restore approximately πN2/4 real coefficients. Counting only positive-quadrant frequency representatives gives πN2/16, but it omits other orientations or quadratures unless a restricted convention is specified. Area approximations have boundary corrections and do not give exact integer ranks. A square sampling cutoff and a circular illuminated actuator footprint are distinct geometric restrictions.
