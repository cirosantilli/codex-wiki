Picard scheme of a curve (Picd(C))

 ... Ringed space Sheaf of modules Locally free sheaf Line bundle Picard group Picard functor of a curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 1
For a smooth projective algebraic curve of genus g, this smooth proper g-dimensional scheme represents degree-d line bundles in families. Charts come from nonspecial degree-g bundles: their first sheaf cohomology vanishes, so the Riemann-Roch theorem gives exactly one independent global section, whose zero Cartier divisor varies algebraically. Twisting by fixed line bundles gives charts covering every degree-d class. For degree zero the tensor product gives the Jacobian variety; other components are its torsors.
