Exceptional curve of the first kind

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 221 Articles: 4
An exceptional curve of the first kind is a smooth rational curve E on a smooth surface with E2=−1. It can be contracted to a smooth point.
This is precisely the curve contracted by the Castelnuovo contraction criterion.
