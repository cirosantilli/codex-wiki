Let β(q) be the centered nondimensional cubic B-spline, even and supported on [−2,2]. The outer cubic must be b(2−∣q∣)3 to make the function and its first two derivatives vanish at the support edge. Write its inner part as A+Bq2+C∣q∣3. Matching the value and first two derivatives at ∣q∣=1 gives A=4b, B=−6b, C=3b. Its nonzero knot values are then 4b,b,b, so requiring their sum to be one fixes b=1/6. Thus
β(q)=⎩⎨⎧​(4−6q2+3∣q∣3)/6,(2−∣q∣)3/6,0,​∣q∣≤1,1≤∣q∣≤2,∣q∣≥2.​​
(33)
In particular β(0)=2/3, β(±1)=1/6.
With h=1/m, represent the spline on [0,1] as s(x)=∑j=−1m+1​cj​β(x/h−j). There are m+3 unknown coefficients. interpolation at xi​=ih, 0≤i≤m, gives
ci−1​+4ci​+ci+1​=61−ih​.
(34)
The natural endpoint conditions, using β′′(0)=−2 and β′′(±1)=1, give
c−1​−2c0​+c1​=0,cm−1​−2cm​+cm+1​=0.
(35)
These m+3 equations are the required matrix system: the m+1 interpolation rows have coefficients (1,4,1) at consecutive positions and right sides 6u(xi​); the two endpoint rows have (1,−2,1) and zero right sides. No solution of that system is required. The scaling uses β(x/h−j), without an extra 1/h, because normalization is by knot values rather than by integral.
