Expand the fifth-degree Taylor polynomial p5​ in Chebyshev polynomials, using the identities for powers obtained from part (a):
p5​=−3211​T0​+811​T1​−83​T2​+487​T3​−321​T4​+801​T5​.
(4)
Delete the last two Chebyshev terms and convert back to powers. The economized cubic is
p3​(x)=321​+1615​x−43​x2+127​x3.​
(5)
Since ∣Tj​∣≤1,
∥p5​−p3​∥∞​≤321​+801​=1607​.
(6)
Equality holds at x=−1, where both deleted terms have negative sign. Thus the economization changes the polynomial by at most 7/160, exactly in supremum norm on [−1,1].
For the target itself, ∣log(1+x)−p3​(x)∣≤∣log(1+x)−p5​(x)∣+7/160 wherever x>−1. The logarithm is singular at −1, so its total uniform error on all of (−1,1] is infinite; the finite estimate concerns the additional polynomial change, or the increase on any smaller interval where the original error is bounded.
