Use the full continuous piecewise linear space Vh​⊂H1(0,1), including endpoint basis functions, because the boundary conditions are Neumann. Define
a(w,v)=∫01​(w′v′+exwv).

Choose uh0​ as the L2 projection of u0​ onto Vh​. For each time step seek uhm+1​∈Vh​ with
(Δtuhm+1​−uhm​​,vh​)+a(2uhm+1​+uhm​​,vh​)=0(vh​∈Vh​).​
(52)
This is the conforming Crank-Nicolson method. The reaction coefficient and the diffusion are both treated at the midpoint; integration by parts has removed the boundary flux through the homogeneous Neumann condition.
