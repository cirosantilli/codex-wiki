Holomorphic disk detects a nonzero Liouville class

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Liouville class of a Lagrangian submanifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For a smooth nonconstant holomorphic disk h in Cn with boundary on a Lagrangian submanifold L, its symplectic area is ∫D2​∣∂s​h∣2dsdt>0. The Cauchy-Riemann equations give ∂t​h=J∂s​h, so this is the pullback of the standard symplectic form. The Stokes theorem identifies the area with the period of a primitive along the boundary loop. Thus the Liouville class of a Lagrangian submanifold evaluates nontrivially on this loop and cannot vanish.
