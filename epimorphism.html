Epimorphism

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category
OurBigBook.com Words: 576 Articles: 13
An epimorphism e:A→B is right-cancellative: fe=ge implies f=g. In the Category of sets and in abelian groups, epimorphisms are precisely the surjective homomorphisms.
