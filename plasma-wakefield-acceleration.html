Plasma wakefield acceleration

 Home Physics Experimental apparatus Particle accelerator
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 3
A driver excites charge-density oscillations in a plasma whose longitudinal electric field transfers energy to a trailing witness beam. Density fixes a characteristic plasma frequency and wavelength, while driver shape and loading determine the actual field and transfer efficiency.
