Every closed walk
J0​⟶J1​⟶⋯⟶Jn−1​⟶J0​
(3)
in a directed covering graph of an interval map has a point x∈J0​ with Fj(x)∈Jj​ and Fn(x)=x. This follows by successively pulling J0​ back through the covering relations and applying the fixed-point property of a closed interval. If the itinerary has least period n and avoids shared endpoints, x lies on an n-cycle.
