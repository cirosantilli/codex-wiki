Upper unitriangular group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory General linear group over a finite field
OurBigBook.com Words: 218 Articles: 3
The upper unitriangular group consists of upper triangular matrices with every diagonal entry equal to one. Over Fp​ it has order pn(n−1)/2 and is a Sylow subgroup of GLn​(Fp​).
Its elements form the upper triangular, diagonal-one subset of triangular matrices.
