An integer interval is a disjoint union of aligned binary blocks with at most two of each length. On [a2j,(a+1)2j) the Thue–Morse sequence sign factors as f(a)f(r), so the Thue–Morse Fourier product bounds its exponential sum by O(2σj). Sum the geometric series over block lengths to obtain the uniform bound, for σ=2−43​log2​3.
