Link of a simplex (lkK​(σ))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Simplicial complex
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
For a simplex σ in a simplicial complex K, its link consists of the simplexes disjoint from σ whose union with σ is a simplex of K:
lkK​(σ)={ρ∈K:ρ∩σ=∅, ρ∪σ∈K}.
(52)
This is a simplicial subcomplex, because taking a nonempty face preserves both conditions. At a vertex of a closed combinatorial surface its link is a cycle graph; at a boundary vertex in a triangulated surface with boundary it is a graph path. Geometrically, the incident triangles form a cyclic fan or a fan with two ends. Deleting one incident triangular face from a cyclic fan changes the vertex link into a path.
