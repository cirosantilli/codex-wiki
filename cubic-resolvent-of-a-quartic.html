Cubic resolvent of a quartic

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Polynomial discriminant
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
For a depressed quartic x4+px2+qx+r with roots α1​,…,α4​, the three pair-partition quantities α1​α2​+α3​α4​ and its two analogues are the roots of
y3−py2−4ry+(4pr−q2).
(42)
The Galois action on these roots is the quotient of its action on the four quartic roots by the Klein four-group.
