Surjectivity of good reduction over a local field

OurBigBook.com Words: 51
If an elliptic curve has good reduction over a local field K, its integral projective model is smooth. Every point over the residue field lifts by the Hensel lemma, applied in a chart with a unit partial derivative. Thus reduction gives an exact sequence 0→E1​(K)→E(K)→E(k)→0. The kernel of reduction of an elliptic curve is the maximal ideal of the valuation ring, with the formal group of an elliptic curve as its group operation.
