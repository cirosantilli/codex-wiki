Pole mass (mpole​)

 ... Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram Connected Feynman diagram One-particle-irreducible Feynman diagram One-particle-irreducible correlation function Self-energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The pole mass is the value of the invariant momentum at a pole of the full propagator. It is independent of the renormalization scheme, whereas a running mass parameter generally depends on the renormalization scale.
