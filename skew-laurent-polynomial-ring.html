Skew Laurent polynomial ring (A[t,t−1;σ])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Polynomial ring Skew polynomial ring of an automorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 188 Articles: 2
Adjoining an inverse of t to the skew polynomial ring of an automorphism allows finite sums ∑j∈Z​aj​tj, with ta=σ(a)t. Every element can be moved into the polynomial subring by multiplying by a sufficiently large power of t. Consequently, for a right ideal I of this ring, I is generated by its intersection with the polynomial subring: if ftN lies there, then f=(ftN)t−N. This proves transfer of right Noetherianity from the polynomial subring, and multiplication on the left proves the left-handed version.
