Cycle-sum identity for Young–Jucys–Murphy elements (X2​⋯Xn​=∑π an n-cycle​π)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra Jucys–Murphy element
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
In the group algebra of Sn​, the product of the Young–Jucys–Murphy elements X2​,…,Xn​ is the sum of all n-cycles, each with coefficient one. To prove it, multiply the sum of all m-cycles by Xm+1​. Right multiplication by (j m+1) inserts m+1 immediately after j in the cycle. Every (m+1)-cycle has a unique predecessor of m+1, so deletion inverts this insertion bijectively. Induction starts at X2​=(1 2). The identity turns a product of cell contents into a central character value of a conjugacy-class sum.
