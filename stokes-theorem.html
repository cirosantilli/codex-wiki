Stokes theorem

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus
OurBigBook.com Words: 548 Articles: 6
Let S be an oriented smooth surface with piecewise smooth boundary, and let F be a continuously differentiable vector field on a neighborhood of S. With the compatible boundary direction determined by the right-hand rule,
∫S​(∇×F)⋅ndS=∮∂S​F⋅dx.
(69)
For G=(−y,x,0) on the horizontal unit disc with upward normal, the left side is 2 times its area, giving 2π. The counterclockwise boundary has tangential field of magnitude one and circumference 2π, giving the same line integral. Reversing the surface normal reverses the boundary direction and both signs. The theorem replaces a circulation calculation with a surface integral of the curl, whichever is easier.
