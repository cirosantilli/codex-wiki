Diffusion image processing

 Home Computer science Image processing
OurBigBook.com Words: 264 Articles: 6
Diffusion filtering evolves an observed image signal using a diffusion equation. The heat equation gives Gaussian image smoothing, while gradient-dependent or tensor-dependent diffusion can reduce transport across image edges. A image smoothing scale, stopping rule and appropriate boundary conditions are part of the filter.
