Mass flux

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Conservation law
OurBigBook.com Words: 79 Articles: 1
Mass flux is the mass crossing a unit area per unit time. In a one-dimensional flow it is ρu. Integrating its normal component over a surface gives the mass flow rate.
