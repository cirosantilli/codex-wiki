Coarse uniqueness of a closest point in a hyperbolic metric space

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Quasi-isometry Hyperbolic metric space Convex subset of a geodesic metric space
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 1
If Y is convex in a δ-hyperbolic metric space and two points of Y both minimize distance from x, then their distance is at most 4δ. Thus a closest-point projection to Y, when it exists, is unique up to uniformly bounded error.
