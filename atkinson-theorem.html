Atkinson theorem (T Fredholm⟺[T]∈Q(H)×)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Lower norm of an operator Fredholm operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 99
A bounded operator is Fredholm if and only if it has a bounded two-sided inverse modulo compact operators. For the forward implication invert its restriction from the orthogonal kernel complement to its closed range and extend by zero. Conversely a parametrix makes the identity compact on the kernel, forcing finite dimension. A failure of a lower bound on the kernel complement would yield unit vectors xn​ with Txn​→0; the compact error then forces a norm-convergent subsequence, whose nonzero limit lies both in the kernel and its complement. This proves closed range; applying the other parametrix identity to the adjoint gives finite cokernel.
