Simple compressible system

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Thermodynamic system
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A thermodynamic system for which the reversible mechanical work is entirely pressure-volume work. At fixed composition its internal energy is specified by entropy and volume; additional work modes require additional state variables.
