Magnetic dipole

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetostatics
OurBigBook.com Words: 391 Articles: 7
A localized magnetic source described at leading nonvanishing dipole order by a magnetic dipole moment. A small current loop is one classical model. The source object, its moment vector and the resulting magnetic dipole field are distinct concepts. At large distance from a source with nonzero dipole moment, higher multipoles give smaller corrections; if the dipole moment vanishes, a higher-order term can lead instead.
