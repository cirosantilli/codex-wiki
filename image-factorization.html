Image factorization (A↠imf↪B)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Subobject
OurBigBook.com Words: 261 Articles: 4
An image of f:A→B is the least subobject m:I↪B through which f factors. In a category with pullback in a category constructions this gives a strong epimorphism–monomorphism factorization f=me: pull back any mono in a lifting square for e to I; minimality of the image forces the resulting mono to be invertible, producing a diagonal. Conversely, the lifting property of the strong part proves minimality. No stability under pullback is asserted; that is an additional property in a regular category.
