Clathrate compound

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound
OurBigBook.com Words: 69 Articles: 1
A clathrate compound has a host structure with cavities containing guest atoms or molecules. The host and guest motions can have very different stiffnesses and characteristic frequencies. Cage-like germanium frameworks with metal guests provide examples with low-energy rattling modes.
