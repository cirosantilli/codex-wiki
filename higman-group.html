Higman group (Q)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Group presentation Finite group presentation Finitely presented group
OurBigBook.com Words: 244 Articles: 2
The Higman group has the finite group presentation Q=⟨a1​,a2​,a3​,a4​∣ai​ai+1​ai−1​=ai+12​ (imod4)⟩. Its subgroup ⟨a1​,a3​⟩ is a rank-two free group, and the normal closure of each ai​ is all of Q. It has no nontrivial finite quotients of a group: a least prime divisor among the four generator orders in a finite image contradicts the multiplicative-order constraints imposed by conjugation to squares. These properties give finite-presentation constructions embedding arbitrary finitely presented groups into groups with no nontrivial finite quotients.
