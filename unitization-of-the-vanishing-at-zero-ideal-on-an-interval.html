Unitization of the vanishing-at-zero ideal on an interval ({f∈C[0,1]:f(0)=0}∼≅C[0,1])

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra C-star algebra Unitization of a C-star algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
Evaluation at zero is a bounded homomorphism on C[0,1]. Its kernel I is a closed self-adjoint ideal and hence a C-star subalgebra. It has no identity: applying a proposed identity to f(t)=t would force that identity to equal one at every positive point, contradicting continuity and its value zero at zero. The map (f,λ)↦f+λ1 from the unitization of a C-star algebra is a bijective star homomorphism, with inverse h↦(h−h(0),h(0)). Pulling back the supremum norm verifies the C-star identity, so this is the prescribed unitization norm. Thus adjoining the missing constant direction recovers all continuous functions on the interval.
