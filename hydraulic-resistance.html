Hydraulic resistance (R=Δp/Q)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Hydraulic resistance relates the pressure drop across a resistive fluid element to its volumetric flow rate. In a linear model, Δp=RQ with R>0, so dissipated mechanical power is ΔpQ=RQ2. A peripheral circulatory bed can be represented by such a resistance when its detailed dynamics are not retained. This terminal pressure-to-flow resistance differs from the area-dependent friction rate multiplying velocity in a distributed tube momentum equation.
