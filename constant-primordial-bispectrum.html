Constant primordial bispectrum (B∝(k1​k2​k3​)−2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A constant primordial bispectrum means a constant bispectrum shape function, not a bispectrum independent of wavenumber. With P(k)=2π2Δζ2​/k3 and scale-independent Δζ2​, one normalization is B=fNL​[P(k1​)P(k2​)P(k3​)]2/3=4π4fNL​Δζ4​/(k1​k2​k3​)2.
