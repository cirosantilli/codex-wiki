Genus of a symplectic Lefschetz pencil on the complex projective plane

OurBigBook.com Words: 35
Every symplectic smooth fiber in a Lefschetz pencil on CP2 represents dH for an integer d≥1. The Symplectic adjunction formula gives
g=2(d−1)(d−2)​.
(489)
Conversely, generic pencils of degree-d complex plane curves realize every value in this list.
