Subgradient

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Convex function
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 4
A subgradient of a convex function f at x is a vector g such that f(y)≥f(x)+gT(y−x) for every y in the domain.
