Histone H1 kinase assay

 ... Biology Signal transduction Signaling feedback Positive feedback Cell-cycle activation feedback Cyclin-dependent kinase 1
OurBigBook.com Words: 272
A histone H1 kinase assay measures protein kinase action on supplied histone H1, a protein substrate. When the transferred phosphate carries a radioactive isotope label, label incorporated into histone H1 reports catalytic action over the assay interval. It does not count CDK1 molecules, and a crude preparation can contain other protein kinases acting on histone H1. Attribution therefore needs controls.
A 2010 study prepared human HeLa S3 extracts after a double thymidine block, using swelling, a Dounce homogenizer, centrifugation, dialysis and concentration. Added recombinant cyclin B1 incubated for 20 minutes.[ref]
Aliquots received histone H1 and terminal-32P-labeled ATP for a 15-minute room-temperature reaction, stopped with Laemmli buffer. Dried SDS-PAGE gels exposed a phosphorimager screen; a BioRad Molecular Imager FX quantified labeling. No-added-cyclin B activity set the normalization baseline; RO-3306 reduced H1 activity approximately 85%. Companion Western blots monitored Tyr15 phosphorylation, with actin Western blot loading control.[ref]
Mixed extracts lose compartment boundaries; inhibitor sensitivity supports a major CDK1 contribution, with residual signal.[ref] Substrate choice and inhibitor specificity limit attribution. The cell-cycle hysteresis experiment supplies a separate history comparison with this kind of histone H1 readout; its dose thresholds should not be transferred to human biological cells.
