Elementary extension

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Model theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 25
An elementary extension is an extension preserving all first-order truth with parameters, hence giving elementary equivalence. An extension N⊇M is elementary when every formula with parameters from M has the same truth value in both structures.
