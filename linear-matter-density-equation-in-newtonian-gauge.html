Linear matter density equation in Newtonian gauge (δm′′​+Hδm′​)

 ... Branches of physics Cosmology Linear cosmological perturbation theory Scalar cosmological perturbation Newtonian gauge Cosmological Euler equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For pressureless matter in Newtonian gauge in cosmology with a common metric potential Φ, let θ=∇⋅v. The linear equations δm′​=−θ+3Φ′ and θ′=−Hθ−∇2Φ, where primes denote conformal time, yield
δm′′​+Hδm′​=∇2Φ+3(Φ′′+HΦ′).
(6)
This follows by differentiating the first equation and eliminating θ. On deeply subhorizon scales with a slowly varying matter potential, the metric time derivatives are small compared with its spatial Laplacian.
