Plane Poiseuille flow of a Bingham fluid (u(y)=2μ0​G​[(h−y0​)2−(∣y∣−y0​)+2​])

For fixed plates y=±h, no slip, viscosity μ0​>0, yield stress τ0​≥0 and G=−p,x​>0, force balance gives shear stress −Gy. The central plug flow of a yield-stress fluid has half-width y0​=τ0​/G. If y0​<h, the velocity is
u(y)=2μ0​G​[(h−y0​)2−(∣y∣−y0​)+2​].
(2)
It is even in y; its derivative is (−Gy+τ0​signy)/μ0​ in the yielded layers and zero in the plug. If Gh≤τ0​, there is no flow. Odd velocity is incompatible with this pressure-driven no-slip solution at nonzero flow.
