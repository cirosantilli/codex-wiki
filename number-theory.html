Strong-pseudoprime bases for a product of two primes with common predecessor divisor ten

Words: 75
Strong pseudoprime OurBigBook.com
Let N=pq for distinct odd prime numbers p,q with gcd(p−1,q−1)=10. Exactly 100 unit bases make N a Fermat pseudoprime, and exactly 50 of these make it a strong pseudoprime. The Fermat bases form C10​×C10​ under the Chinese remainder theorem for unit groups. If N−1=2sm, then 5∣m and m is odd, so the mth-power map sends five elements of each C10​ to 1 and five to −1. The strong condition requires equal signs in both components, giving 52+52=50.
