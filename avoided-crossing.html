Avoided crossing

 ... Branches of physics Quantum theory Bloch's theorem Electronic band structure Energy band Band gap
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
An avoided crossing occurs when two parameter-dependent eigenvalues approach one another but an interaction prevents equality. Near a two-level crossing, a nonzero off-diagonal matrix element opens a positive minimum gap.
