Toomre's stability criterion (Q)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Gravitational instability of an astrophysical disk

For a razor-thin isothermal gas disk,
Q=πGΣcs​κ​.
(1)
Axisymmetric disturbances are stable when Q≥1: pressure stabilizes short wavelengths, epicyclic motion stabilizes long wavelengths, and self-gravity drives intermediate wavelengths.
