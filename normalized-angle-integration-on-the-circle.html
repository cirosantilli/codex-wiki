Normalized angle integration on the circle (dμ=dθ/(2π))

On the circle group T, normalized Haar measure is the pushforward measure of uniform angle on [0,2π). For a continuous function f:T→C, define

The factor 1/(2π) makes the integral of the constant function 1 equal to 1. For g=eiϕ, a change of angle gives
∫T​f(gz)dμ(z)=2π1​∫ϕ2π+ϕ​f(eiu)du=2π1​∫02π​f(eiu)du.

The last equality uses the 2π-periodic function u↦f(eiu). Since the circle group is an abelian group, this also verifies right invariance. An arc of angular length L has normalized Haar measure L/(2π). In particular, the arc A={eiθ:π/6≤θ≤π/2} and its quarter-turn iA both have measure (π/2−π/6)/(2π)=1/6.
