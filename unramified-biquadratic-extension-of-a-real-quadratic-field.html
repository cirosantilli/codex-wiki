Unramified biquadratic extension of a real quadratic field (Q(p​,q​)/Q(pq​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Biquadratic extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 138
For distinct primes p,q≡1(mod4), put E=Q(p​,q​) and K=Q(pq​). The algebraic integers a=(1+p​)/2 and b=(1+q​)/2 generate an order whose basis has discriminant of elements of a number field p2q2: the trace pairing matrix is, up to basis order, the tensor product of the quadratic trace matrices of determinants p and q. If its index in OE​ is j, the discriminant-index formula for an integral lattice and the relative discriminant tower formula give
p2q2/j2=dE​=(pq)2NK/Q​(dE/K​).
(8)
Both j and this ideal norm are positive integers, forcing j=1 and dE/K​=OK​. Thus E/K is unramified at all finite primes. It is also totally real, so no real place ramifies. This quadratic abelian extension is contained in the ordinary Hilbert class field of K, and Artin reciprocity gives a quotient of its ideal class group of order two. The extensions K(p​) and K(q​) are equal to E, not two different extensions of K.
