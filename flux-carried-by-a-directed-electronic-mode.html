Flux carried by a directed electronic mode (dN˙=dE/h)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum transport Landauer formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For one directed spin-resolved channel, the density of longitudinal states per unit length and wave number is 1/(2π). Since v=ℏ−1dE/dk, velocity times density per energy is 1/h. A reservoir occupation imbalance therefore contributes particle flux ∫δf(E)dE/h; in zero-temperature linear response it is δμ/h. Multiplying by charge and transmission gives the Landauer current per mode.
