p-adic metric (dp​(x,y)=∣x−y∣p​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis P-adic number p-adic absolute value
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
The p-adic metric on the P-adic numbers is induced by the p-adic absolute value. Its ultrametric inequality is dp​(x,z)≤max(dp​(x,y),dp​(y,z)). On p-adic integers, digit agreement and p-adic distance expresses metric precision as shared low-order digits.
