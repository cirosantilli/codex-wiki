Generalized Gaussian white noise over a real Hilbert space H=L2 is an isonormal Gaussian process W(h) with covariance ⟨h,g⟩H​. Its law P0​ is carried on a larger observation space; the identity is not the covariance of an infinite-dimensional H-valued Gaussian measure because it is not a trace-class operator. A deterministic signal h∈H defines a translated observation law Ph​ with Radon-Nikodym derivative
dP0​dPh​​(m)=exp(Wm​(h)−21​∥h∥H2​).
(60)
In an orthonormal basis, Wm​(h)=∑j​mj​hj​ is a stochastic series with total variance ∥h∥H2​, not an inner product of two H-valued observations. The formula follows from the Cameron-Martin theorem for a Gaussian measure in its generalized white-noise version, or from finite-dimensional Gaussian likelihood ratios. On R2, generalized noise can be realized on tempered distributions; an unweighted global negative Sobolev space is not automatically a suitable almost-sure support on an unbounded domain. The stochastic series avoids imposing that unsupported regularity.
