Perfect semidirect product criterion ([g⋉V,g⋉V]=[g,g]⊕gV)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semidirect product of a Lie algebra and a module
OurBigBook.com Words: 98
For a Lie algebra representation of g on V, let gV denote the linear span of xv for x∈g and v∈V. The derived algebra of the semidirect product of a Lie algebra and a module is the displayed direct sum of vector spaces. Every Lie bracket has first component in [g,g] and second component in gV. Conversely, brackets of (x,0) with (y,0) span the former component, and brackets of (x,0) with (0,v) span the latter. Therefore this semidirect product of a Lie algebra and a module is a perfect Lie algebra exactly when g is a perfect Lie algebra and gV=V. When V=0, its nonzero abelian Lie algebra ideal prevents it from being a semisimple Lie algebra. The sl2 Lie algebra acting on its two-dimensional defining representation supplies a five-dimensional example.
