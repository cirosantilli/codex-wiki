One-particle-irreducible correlation function (Γ(n))

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram Connected Feynman diagram One-particle-irreducible Feynman diagram
OurBigBook.com Words: 389 Articles: 9
An n-point one-particle-irreducible correlation function is obtained by differentiating the quantum effective action n times. Its diagrammatic expansion contains the amputated one-particle-irreducible diagrams with n external legs.
