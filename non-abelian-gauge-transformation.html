Non-Abelian gauge transformation (Aμ​↦RAμ​R−1−g−1(∂μ​R)R−1)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Yang-Mills theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
For the convention Dμ​=∂μ​+gAμ​, a gauge group transformation Φ↦RΦ acts on the gauge potential by Aμ​↦RAμ​R−1−g−1(∂μ​R)R−1. The gauge field strength transforms by conjugation. This makes Dμ​Φ transform in the same gauge group representation as Φ.
