Indexed lambda term (Λ⊥N​)

 ... Foundations of mathematics Computability theory Lambda calculus Lambda model Reflexive domain Graph model of lambda calculus
OurBigBook.com Words: 147 Articles: 1
An indexed lambda term assigns a natural number to every subterm occurrence; one also admits a constant ⊥ with empty interpretation. In the graph model of lambda calculus, every syntax constructor is interpreted as usual and then intersected with {0,…,n} at its occurrence index n. All indexed interpretations are finite subsets of the unindexed interpretation, and their directed union recovers that interpretation.
