Roughness length (z0​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Boundary layer Turbulent boundary layer Law of the wall
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
The extrapolation height at which a logarithmic wall profile vanishes. For a fully rough wall it is set mainly by the geometry; for a smooth wall it scales with ν/u∗​. It is not the thickness of the molecular viscous sublayer or the literal location of a no-slip surface.
