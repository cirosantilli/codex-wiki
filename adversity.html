Adversity

 Home Science Psychology Developmental psychology Developmental psychopathology
OurBigBook.com Words: 39 Articles: 1
Experiences or conditions threatening relevant development, safety or functioning. Exposure should be characterized by nature, severity, duration, timing and context rather than assumed identical across people with the same event count. Objective circumstances and their subjective appraisal provide different information.
