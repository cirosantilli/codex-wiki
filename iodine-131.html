Iodine-131

 Home Physics Medical imaging Nuclear medicine Radiopharmaceutical
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
An iodine radionuclide with approximately eight-day half-life, emitting beta particles and gamma rays. Its uptake and retention depend strongly on the carrier and tissue physiology.
