Claims inflation of a fixed reinsurance layer (E[LM,A​(hX)∣hX>M]=hC(μ,σ;M/h,A/h))

OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For positive inflation factor h, LM,A​(hX)=hLM/h,A/h​(X). The conditioning event also changes to X>M/h. If claims have a lognormal distribution, the same result follows by replacing their log-mean μ by μ+logh and keeping the contractual attachment and limit fixed. Merely multiplying the original conditional mean by h would hold those effective thresholds incorrectly fixed.
