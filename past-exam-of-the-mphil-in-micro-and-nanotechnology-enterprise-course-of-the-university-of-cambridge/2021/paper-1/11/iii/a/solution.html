If the voltage were held at 4.57V for the entire ideal discharge, then
E=VQ=(4.57)(175.2)=800.664Wh=0.801kWh​,
(36)
and the electrical power would be
P=VI=(4.57)(0.010)=0.0457W=45.7mW​.
(37)
These are constant-voltage estimates, not a determination of the actual energy from the fresh-cell open-circuit voltage. Under load the voltage depends on state of discharge, electric polarization and internal resistance. The physical result is E=I∫0tf​​Vload​(t)dt, and P(t)=IVload​(t). The supplied single open-circuit value does not specify that voltage curve; practical delivered energy will generally be lower than the simple estimate.
