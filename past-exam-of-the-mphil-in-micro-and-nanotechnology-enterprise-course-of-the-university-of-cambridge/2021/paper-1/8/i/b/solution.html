The ordering is gold brightest, then copper, NiPz​, NiPx​ and NiPy​. The phosphorus-rich layer has the lowest effective atomic number.
There is a wording ambiguity in “atomic fraction of P relative to Ni.” If those numbers instead mean r=NP​/NNi​, first convert xP​=r/(1+r), giving Zˉ=(28+15r)/(1+r). The three alloy contrasts then become 0.4243, 0.4361, 0.4129, respectively. The qualitative ordering is unchanged. Both calculations explicitly use η(Zˉ); this simple alloy approximation is not a full electron-transport simulation.
