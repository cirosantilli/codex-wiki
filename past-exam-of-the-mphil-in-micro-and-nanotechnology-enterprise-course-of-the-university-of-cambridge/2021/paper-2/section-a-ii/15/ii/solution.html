Assume the usual one-lead-biased measurement, Vs​=Vsd​ with drain grounded and the gate referred to that ground; the printed map does not separately specify the wiring. This assumption is needed to assign individual source/drain capacitances. In this Coulomb diamond capacitance extraction, the smaller zero-bias gate intervals are 5 mV wide. In the constant interaction model with no orbital gap between those additions,
Cg​=ΔVg​e​=5×10−31.602×10−19​=32.04 aF.
(7)
An upper diamond tip lies at gate 123.75 mV between the zero-bias vertices 120 and 125 mV and at bias 2.50 mV. Its positive and negative edge slopes are therefore s+​=2.50/3.75=2/3 and s−​=−2.50/1.25=−2. Reservoir alignment gives
s−​=−Cs​Cg​​,s+​=CΣ​−Cs​Cg​​=Cg​+Cd​Cg​​.
(8)
The gate capacitance is not negligible in the second denominator. Consequently
Cs​≃16.0 aF,Cd​≃16.0 aF,Cg​≃32.0 aF,CΣ​≃64.1 aF.​
(9)
The consistency check is e2/CΣ​=2.50 meV, matching the smaller tip's bias energy. The conventional electrostatic charging energy EC​=e2/(2CΣ​) is 1.25 meV; it is half the charging energy contribution to the addition energy. A different applied-bias convention changes the slope formulas, so the convention must accompany the extraction.
