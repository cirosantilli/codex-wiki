t=50×10−65.25​=105000h≃12.0 years.​
(6)
This is an ideal energy-budget estimate using 365.25 days per year. It assumes the stated energy is usable at this load, constant current and voltage, and negligible self-discharge, ageing and reserve capacity. No biological operating model is required.
