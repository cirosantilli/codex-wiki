A liquid crystal flows while retaining partial molecular order. A nematic liquid crystal has long-range orientational order but no crystal-like positional lattice. Its local mean axis is the nematic director n, with n≡−n for an ordinary apolar nematic. Order leads to anisotropic optical and dielectric response and an elastic cost for director distortions.
Nematic order selects an axis, not a common molecular head direction or density-layer stack. The original sketch uses short rods as a coarse-grained orientation map.
