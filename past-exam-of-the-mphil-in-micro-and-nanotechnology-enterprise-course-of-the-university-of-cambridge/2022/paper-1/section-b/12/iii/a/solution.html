For the thermoelectric couple figure of merit, the coefficient αnp​ denotes a pair difference, whereas the table gives one value each for resistivity and thermal conductivity without saying whether they are per-leg or effective pair values. To keep the factors consistent, first take both legs to have the listed ρ and κ, and equal A,L as stated. Then
R=2ρL/A,K=2κA/L,Zpair​=RKαnp2​​=4ρκαnp2​​=3.83×10−4K−1.​
(34)
This is identical to treating each symmetric leg as having Seebeck-coefficient magnitude αnp​/2. The mean temperature is T=(523.15+323.15)/2=423.15K, giving Zpair​T=0.162.
If the examination instead intends its ρ and κ as already combined effective pair coefficients in R=ρL/A and K=κA/L, the alternative convention gives Z=1.53×10−3K−1, with ZT=0.649. That convention must be declared; using the full pair Seebeck difference with individual-leg ρ,κ while omitting both factors of two would overestimate the device figure of merit by four.
