One mole of the overall discharge reaction transfers one mole of electrons. Count both active electrode reactants, including the graphite host:
Mactive​=MLiC6​​+MCoO2​​=6.94+6(12.01)+58.93+2(16.00)=169.93gmol−1.
(26)
Using the preceding constant-voltage approximation,
W=FE≃(96500)(4.2)=405300Jmol−1=112.58Whmol−1,
(27)
so the ideal active-material specific energy is
εsp​≃0.16993112.58​=663Whkg−1.​
(28)
This assumes complete one-electron utilization and the proxy 4.2V throughout discharge. It is not a packaged-cell value or a cathode-only value. A varying equilibrium voltage requires integrating ∫VdQ; using a maximum voltage as a constant average overestimates the delivered energy.
