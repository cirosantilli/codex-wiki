Symmetric wetting and an A-preferring substrate imply an A-preferring free surface as well. Parallel lamellae satisfy both preferences. For equal-width A and B domains, a thickness 2L0​ contains two repeats, with terminal A half-domains:
AL0​/4​∣BL0​/2​∣AL0​/2​∣BL0​/2​∣AL0​/4​.​
(14)
The sequence is written from substrate to atmosphere. Its total thickness is 2L0​, the total A and B thicknesses are each L0​, and both interfaces contact A. A diagram using five full half-period domains would instead give 2.5L0​ and would not match the stated thickness.
The quarter-period end domains place an A-domain centre at each boundary in this ideal symmetric-lamella model. Real surface reconstruction and non-equal block fractions can change their detailed widths without changing the key requirement of identical terminations.
