After the loading impulse, the stress remains Gγ0​. The model does not produce a gradual stress-relaxation tail at held strain. A real finite-rate ramp replaces the impulse by a finite dashpot stress during loading, but the subsequent held-strain stress is still constant. Thus this model describes material creep retardation but not a solid's genuine time-dependent relaxation toward another finite modulus.
