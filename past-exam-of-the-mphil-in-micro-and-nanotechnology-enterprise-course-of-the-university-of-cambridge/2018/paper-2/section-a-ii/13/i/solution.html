Only the magnetic-response physics is addressed here; biological and clinical application is user-deferred.
A single-domain particle has an approximately coherent magnetic dipole moment, but single-domain does not by itself imply rapid thermal reversal. If moments equilibrate within the measurement time, a dilute, noninteracting ensemble is superparamagnetic: its quasistatic magnetization is reversible, odd in field and approaches saturation with negligible remanence or magnetic coercivity.
