Read the displayed axes as horizontal [001] and vertical [010]; the viewing direction is therefore consistent with [100]. crystal facets edge-on in this projection have normals in the yz plane. The flat upper/lower boundaries are approximately horizontal, the end face vertical, and the bevels approximately 45∘ to both.
A probable low-index assignment is upper face (010), end face (001) and lower-right bevel (01ˉ1), with the upper-right bevel (011) if it is also indexed. Thus the flat faces belong to {100} and the diagonal faces to {110}.
For example,
cosϕ(010),(001)​=0,cosϕ(001),(011)​=2​1​,

giving 90∘ and 45∘ between normals. The upper (010) normal and lower bevel (01ˉ1) normal instead make 135∘; their acute unoriented plane angle is 45∘. Adjacent polygon corners between a flat edge and bevel have internal angles near 135∘.
The distinction between a normal angle, an acute plane angle and an internal polygon angle avoids a supplementary-angle error. This is a probable projected indexation, not a unique full three-dimensional crystal facet reconstruction. The associated work uses tomography precisely to recover information absent from one projection.[ref] The original facet-normal diagram in the growth-and-LED figure shows the 45∘ relation independently of the source micrograph.
