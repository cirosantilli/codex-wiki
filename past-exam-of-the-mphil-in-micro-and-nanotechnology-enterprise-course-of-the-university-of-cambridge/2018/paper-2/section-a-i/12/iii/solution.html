Since energy and angular frequency are related by E=ℏω, the given eigenvalue relation yields
Ei​=EP​21+λi​​​.
(3)
With EP​=4.3 eV,
E1​=4.30.285​≃2.296 eV,E2​=4.30.325​≃2.451 eV,E3​=4.30.400​≃2.720 eV.​
(4)
The λi​ are dimensionless modal eigenvalues, not optical wavelengths despite the symbol. The same ℏ multiplies both mode and bulk angular frequencies and cancels in the energy ratio. These are bare-core predictions of the stipulated lossless Drude model; adding a dielectric/metallic shell, interband response or damping changes the actual resonances.
