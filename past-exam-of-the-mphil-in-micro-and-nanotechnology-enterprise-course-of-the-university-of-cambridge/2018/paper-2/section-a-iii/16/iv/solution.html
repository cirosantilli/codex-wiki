For large positive dimensionless surface potential u0​=eΦ0​/(kB​T)≳1, use the nonlinear Boltzmann distribution rather than their linear expansion:
nco​=n0​e−u(x),ncounter​=n0​eu(x).
(18)
The counterions become strongly enriched near the wall and co-ions strongly depleted. A planar mean-field solution is
u(x)=4artanh[tanh(u0​/4)e−x/λD​].
(19)
It approaches the linear screening form far from the wall. Nonlinearity preserves positive densities and produces a much stronger near-wall asymmetry; an extrapolated linear co-ion profile could incorrectly become negative. The total local ion density is 2n0​coshu, no longer constant.
The original figure compares weak- and strong-potential profiles. At very large local concentrations, finite ion size, specific adsorption and ion correlations limit this ideal point-ion mean-field model; it does not predict unlimited physically realizable counterion packing.
