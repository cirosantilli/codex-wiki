x(t)=x0​e−t/τ+γ1​∫0t​e−(t−s)/τξ(s)ds.
(20)
For a particle initially at x0​=0,
⟨∣x(t)∣2⟩=γ26γkB​T​∫0t​e−2(t−s)/τds=k3kB​T​(1−e−2t/τ).
(21)
Hence its long-time displacement about the trap/elastic equilibrium has
xrms​=3kB​T/k​​.
(22)
This also follows from equipartition theorem: each of three quadratic coordinates contributes kB​T/2.
A different common experimental quantity is the stationary time-lag displacement. It obeys
⟨∣x(t+Δ)−x(t)∣2⟩=k6kB​T​(1−e−Δ/τ),
(23)
with long-lag RMS 6kB​T/k​. The factor of two distinguishes fluctuations about a mean position from differences between two decorrelated positions. The observation convention must be stated rather than silently interchanged.
