Relative to conventional crystalline-silicon technology, many organic absorbers offer low-temperature thin-film processing and lightweight flexible devices, but have more strongly bound excitons and more disorder-limited charge transport. These are common material trends, not universal statements about every organic or inorganic device.
Relevant semiconductor properties include the absorption spectrum and optical gap, donor/acceptor energy levels and offsets, dielectric screening and exciton dissociation, electron/hole mobilities, recombination lifetimes, traps and morphological stability.
