The tabulated free energies are per mole of O2​, not per mole of NiO. Values normalized to one mole of NiO would be half as large. A reliable oxygen concentration cell measurement requires reversible interfaces, calibrated uniform temperature, a known reference oxygen pressure and predominantly ionic rather than electronic conduction through yttria-stabilized zirconia. Air is not pure oxygen: dropping the RTln0.21 term changes the inferred entropy.
