Approximate the reaction enthalpy and entropy as constant over the measured interval, so Δr​G∘=Δr​H∘−TΔr​S∘. A linear least squares fit of all three free energies against temperature gives
Δr​G∘≃−478580.96+183.9664TJmolO2​−1​,
(10)
and hence
Δr​H∘≃−478.6kJmolO2​−1​,Δr​S∘≃−184.0JK−1molO2​−1​​.
(11)
The negative entropy change is reasonable because oxygen gas is incorporated into solids. The fit residuals are +0.129,−0.257,+0.129kJmolO2​−1​, so the data support this approximate straight-line description. Extrapolating its intercept to zero temperature is a way of estimating a constant local enthalpy, not evidence that the heat capacities vanish or that the same phases persist to zero temperature. More temperatures and repeated heating/cooling measurements would test curvature and equilibration.
