A faceted, approximately equiaxed crystal is indicated. A truncated octahedron bounded by low-energy {111} and {100} facets is a plausible three-dimensional shape for an fcc metal particle; the hexagonal section alone does not uniquely distinguish it from other faceted polyhedra.

Low-surface-energy orientations survive as large facets. Annealing permits diffusion toward this equilibrium morphology, provided the time is sufficient. For a supported or strained alloy, substrate adhesion and elastic energy modify this simple free-particle construction.
