A Ragone plot has specific energy es​=E/m on its horizontal logarithmic axis and specific power ps​=P/m on its vertical logarithmic axis, normally in Whkg−1 and Wkg−1. The comparison must use the same mass convention: complete devices cannot fairly be compared with electrode-active-material values. At approximately constant delivered power, the discharge time is
th​=ps​es​​,ts​=3600ps​es​​.
(10)
Thus constant-time lines slope upward on the log–log plot. Ordinary capacitors lie toward high power and low energy; batteries toward higher energy and lower power; supercapacitors bridge the two. Technology and operating limits produce overlapping regions, not universal sharp boxes.
