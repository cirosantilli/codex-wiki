Thus Mc​=0.6991 and 0.2006 for the lower- and higher-contrast materials. Reading those levels on the supplied modulation transfer function gives maximum normalized spatial frequencies approximately ν/ν0​=0.40 and 0.60, respectively. The stated partial coherence is already represented by that curve; do not multiply the read frequencies by another coherence factor.
The unambiguous pattern dimensions are full pitch P=1/(xν0​) and equal line/space width w=P/2, where x is the read normalized frequency. To convert the paper's Rayleigh-frequency label into lengths, explicitly use the circular-pupil Rayleigh criterion dR​=0.61λ/NA and ν0​=1/dR​. The pitch and half-pitch distinction matters: the point-separation Rayleigh criterion is not itself an equal-line/space width. If a course defines ν0​ as an optical cutoff rather than 1/dR​, use that specified frequency in w=1/(2xν0​); the normalized graph readings and their ratio are unchanged.
