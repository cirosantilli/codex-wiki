The duty cycle D=tactive​/(tactive​+tsleep​) is the fraction of time spent active. At a constant supply voltage and with switching energy neglected,
Pˉ=V[DIa​+(1−D)Is​].
(30)
For D=0.01, the average current is 0.01(25 mA)+0.99(6.1 μA)=256.039 μA. Thus Pˉ=512.078 μW≃0.512 mW. Multiplying only the active power by the duty cycle would omit the sleep contribution.
