For two equal spheres, the Derjaguin approximation uses Reff​=R/2. A planar nonretarded Van der Waals force interaction has energy per area W(h)=−AH​/(12πh2), so
F=2πReff​W(h)=−12h2AH​R​=−12(10−9)2(15.6×10−20)(10−3)​=−1.30×10−5 N​.
(18)
The force is attractive, with magnitude 13.0 μN, taking separation increase as positive. Using the sphere–plane value of Reff​ would overestimate it by a factor of two. The gap is much smaller than either radius, as required. Treat the supplied Hamaker constant as the effective one for the intervening medium; roughness, solvation and molecular-scale contact can modify a literal 1 nm experiment.
