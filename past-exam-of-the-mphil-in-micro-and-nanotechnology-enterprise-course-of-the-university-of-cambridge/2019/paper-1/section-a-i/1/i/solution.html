 ... Past exam of the MPhil in micro- and nanotechnology enterprise course of the University of Cambridge 2019 Paper 1 Section A(i) 1 i
OurBigBook.com Words: 194
The repeated, similarly oriented rounded triangular outlines in panel (a) are characteristic of AFM tip convolution: a narrow raised object can image the shape of a blunt or contaminated AFM probe, rather than reveal its own lateral shape. A finite tip radius broadens lateral dimensions. Replace or clean the AFM probe, check it on an independently characterized sharp-feature standard, and repeat at low load; agreement after a tip change is stronger evidence than a visually pleasing image. Image processing cannot uniquely recover unmeasured sidewalls or prove that every particle with a similar outline is an artefact.
Panel (b) contains horizontal streaks associated with steep features. The natural instrumental diagnosis is AFM feedback artefact: the height controller does not track a rapid change faithfully, or rings after it. Reduce the scan speed and adjust proportional/integral gains below the oscillation threshold. Compare trace and retrace and rotate the fast-scan direction: a streak following the scan rather than the specimen is instrumental. Loose contamination can also produce scan-line streaks, so clean or change the AFM probe if tuning does not remove them. Tip-shape broadening in (a), and scan-direction tracking/streaking in (b), are the principal diagnoses; the images alone do not uniquely establish every underlying cause.
