On an integer plateau the sheet transverse resistivity has magnitude
∣ρxy​∣=νe2h​,
(10)
where the integer ν counts occupied resolved quantum Hall edge states. Increasing B at fixed sheet density reduces the Landau filling factor νfill​=ns​h/(eB). The observed trace is therefore a staircase toward larger ∣ρxy​∣, with transitions between plateaux rather than the classical straight line. In the plateau regions ρxx​≃0; dissipative peaks occur at transitions.
It is not correct to demand an exactly integer value of ns​h/(eB) everywhere along a finite-width plateau. Localization accommodates changes in total population without changing the number of extended current-carrying channels. If spin remains unresolved, the usual spin-degenerate sequence contains even integers rather than every integer.
