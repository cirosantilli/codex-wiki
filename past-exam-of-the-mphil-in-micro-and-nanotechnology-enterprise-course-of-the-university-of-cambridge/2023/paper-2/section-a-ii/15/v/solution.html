Using the magnitude of the classical slope,
ns​=e(4000m2/C)1​=1.56×1015m−2.
(11)
The Landau filling factor at 1T is
νfill​=eBns​h​=6.47.
(12)
Under the convention that the “last Landau level” means the last resolved spin branch, its degeneracy per area is eB/h. Setting νfill​=1 gives
B1​=ens​h​=6.47T.
(13)
Above this threshold all carriers fit into that lowest resolved branch, which is partially filled when νfill​<1.
There is a spin convention to state explicitly: if “Landau level” instead means one orbital level with unresolved twofold spin degeneracy, its capacity is 2eB/h, and all carriers first fit into orbital index n=0 at B=3.24T, where νfill​=2. The slope alone determines ns​ and νfill​, but does not specify the spin splitting.
