The zero-bias gate period in the first plot is ΔVg​=20mV. In the constant interaction model, changing the gate-induced island charge by one electron gives
Cg​=ΔVg​e​=8.0aF.
(25)
The tip is at ∣Vsd​∣=5.0mV above or below zero; use this half-height, not the full 10mV vertical extent. The addition energy is Eadd​=e(5.0mV)=e2/CΣ​, giving
CΣ​=32aF,Cg​/CΣ​=0.25.
(26)
To distinguish the junction capacitances, explicitly adopt the circuit convention Vs​=Vsd​ and Vd​=0. The diamond with zero-bias endpoints 20 and 40mV has its positive tip near (Vg​,Vsd​)=(35,5)mV. Its two edge slopes are therefore +1/3 and −1.
Writing the island electrochemical potential as a charge-dependent constant minus e(Cs​Vsd​+Cg​Vg​)/CΣ​, alignment with the drain gives slope −Cg​/Cs​, while alignment with the source gives Cg​/(CΣ​−Cs​). Thus
Cs​=Cg​=8aF,Cd​=CΣ​−Cs​−Cg​=16aF.
(27)
The source/drain names are exchanged if the opposite lead is biased under the voltage convention. The figure alone establishes the pair {8,16}aF unless that wiring convention is specified. Symmetric-looking diamond heights do not imply equal junction capacitances.
