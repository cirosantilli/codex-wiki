B retains the terminal alkyne and replaces the tertiary propargylic chlorine with ethoxy. D replaces the benzylic chlorine while retaining the aromatic chlorine; its meta arrangement follows from the drawn starting material, rather than from four aromatic protons alone.

For B, the strong 3300cm−1 band is the terminal sp C–H stretch, the 2950cm−1 band is aliphatic C–H stretching, and the weak 2100cm−1 band is the alkyne stretch. The terminal proton and alkyne band together distinguish this from an alcohol interpretation of the high-frequency absorption.
For D, 3050cm−1 is aromatic C–H stretching, 2950cm−1 is aliphatic C–H stretching, and the 1600 and 1580cm−1 bands are aromatic skeletal stretches. The listed spectrum is a selected peak list, so omission of a C–O band is not evidence that the ether bond is absent.
