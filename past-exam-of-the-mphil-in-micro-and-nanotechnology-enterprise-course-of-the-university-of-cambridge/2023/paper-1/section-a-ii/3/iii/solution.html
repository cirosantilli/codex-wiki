Depth of focus is the axial tolerance over which the projected pattern remains acceptable. Wafer topography, resist thickness, wafer tilt and focus-setting errors can move part of the film outside that range, reducing contrast and changing printed linewidth. Increasing numerical aperture can improve lateral resolution while reducing this axial process window.
