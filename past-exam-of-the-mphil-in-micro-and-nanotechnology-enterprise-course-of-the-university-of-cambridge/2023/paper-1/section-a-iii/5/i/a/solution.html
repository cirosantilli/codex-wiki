The oxygen atom has 1s22s22p4. Each oxygen contributes six valence electrons, so the molecular diagram contains twelve. Taking the internuclear direction as z, the relevant ordering for oxygen is
σg​(2s)<σu∗​(2s)<σg​(2pz​)<πu​(2px​)=πu​(2py​)<πg∗​(2px​)=πg∗​(2py​)<σu∗​(2pz​).
(15)
The valence occupation is
[σg​(2s)]2[σu∗​(2s)]2[σg​(2pz​)]2[πu​(2px​)]2[πu​(2py​)]2[πg∗​(2px​)]1[πg∗​(2py​)]1​.
(16)
The final two electrons occupy the degenerate antibonding orbitals singly with parallel spin, following Hund's rules. Thus oxygen has two unpaired electrons and is paramagnetic. The 1s core orbitals are deliberately omitted from the valence-only molecular orbital diagram.
