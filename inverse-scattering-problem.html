Inverse scattering problem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 23
An inverse scattering problem reconstructs a medium or obstacle from measured scattered waves. Under the Born approximation, fixed-frequency far-field data sample the Fourier transform of the scattering potential on a restricted frequency surface.
