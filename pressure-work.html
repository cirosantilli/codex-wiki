Pressure work (δW=pdV)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
Mechanical work transferred by a pressure acting through a displaced volume. In steady incompressible flow, the Bernoulli equation contains p/ρ as flow work per unit mass; it is not an independently conserved internal energy of an isolated parcel.
