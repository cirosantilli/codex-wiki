Lightest supersymmetric particle (LSP)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supersymmetry breaking Soft supersymmetry breaking Sparticle
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The lightest supersymmetric particle is the least massive sparticle in a given spectrum. Exact R-parity makes it stable: a lighter final state cannot contain another odd particle, while a final state of ordinary particles is even. A neutral weakly interacting lightest supersymmetric particle is a possible dark matter constituent, but its cosmological abundance must be computed separately.
