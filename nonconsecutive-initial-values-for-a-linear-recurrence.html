Nonconsecutive initial values for a linear recurrence

A second-order linear recurrence relation normally uses two consecutive initial conditions, but two specified nonconsecutive values may also determine a unique solution of the linear recurrence relation. For two independent homogeneous solutions of the linear recurrence relation vn​,wn​, the coefficients in un​=unp​+Avn​+Bwn​ are determined by uj​,uk​ exactly when vj​wk​−vk​wj​=0. Otherwise the data can be inconsistent or can leave freedom. A nonconsecutive subscript is therefore not by itself evidence of an error.
For a repeated characteristic root of a linear recurrence r=0, use vn​=rn, wn​=nrn. The determinant is (k−j)rj+k=0 for distinct j,k, so any such pair of prescribed values determines the two coefficients.
