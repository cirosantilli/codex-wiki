Chromosome

 Home Biology Genetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 795 Articles: 21
A chromosome carries genes and other genetic material. A haplotype records the alleles carried on one chromosome at specified genetic loci.
In a typical eukaryotic cell nucleus, an unreplicated chromosome comprises one long double-stranded DNA molecule with associated proteins; a replicated chromosome comprises two associated sister chromatids. It need not be X-shaped: that familiar outline depicts a condensed, replicated state.[ref]
For these ordinary centromere-bearing chromosomes, count by centromere units before separation: the paired sister centromere regions count together as one replicated chromosome. Once sister chromatids separate in anaphase of mitosis or meiosis II, each becomes a daughter chromosome. Thus the undivided anaphase biological cell in the 2n=6 mitotic example transiently contains twelve daughter chromosomes, but each pole receives six and each completed daughter cell nucleus remains diploid. State the counting compartment and stage; summing both poles is not the ploidy of one daughter.
