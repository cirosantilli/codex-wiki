Projective state exclusion (⟨bj​∣ψj​⟩=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Quantum state discrimination Quantum state exclusion
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For d labelled pure states in dimension d, an orthonormal basis {∣bj​⟩}j=0d−1​ supplies error-free projective state exclusion when ⟨bj​∣ψj​⟩=0 for every j. The Born rule gives zero probability to reporting j on input ψj​; orthonormal-basis completeness ensures that an outcome is always obtained. Conversely, zero error for this labelled basic measurement forces precisely those zero overlaps. The matrix of transition probabilities is therefore a matrix of column probability distributions with zero diagonal.
