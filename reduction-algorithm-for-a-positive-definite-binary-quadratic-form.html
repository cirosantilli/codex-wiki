Reduction algorithm for a positive definite binary quadratic form

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Binary quadratic form Reduced positive definite binary quadratic form
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
For a positive definite integral form [a,b,c], a unimodular shear can replace b by its representative b′ with ∣b′∣≤a. If the resulting c′=(b′2−D)/(4a) is smaller than a, swapping the variables replaces the leading coefficient by the smaller positive integer c′. Repeating terminates and produces ∣b∣≤a≤c; determinant-one sign changes enforce the boundary convention. Thus every positive definite integral binary quadratic form is properly equivalent to a reduced form.
