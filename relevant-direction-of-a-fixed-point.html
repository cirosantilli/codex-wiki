Relevant direction of a fixed point

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Renormalization group Renormalization-group relevance Relevant operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 165 Articles: 1
A perturbation of a renormalization-group fixed point is relevant if it grows as the coarse-graining length increases. For a continuous flow dδg/dℓ=Mδg, an eigenvector of the linearized matrix with eigenvalue σ grows as eσℓ; a positive real part is relevant, a negative real part is irrelevant and a zero real part requires nonlinear analysis. For a discrete transformation with length factor b>1, its Jacobian matrix multiplier λ instead gives δgn​=λnδg0​: ∣λ∣>1 is relevant, ∣λ∣<1 is irrelevant and ∣λ∣=1 requires nonlinear analysis. For a positive multiplier the scaling exponent is y=logλ/logb. Thus a positive discrete eigenvalue smaller than one is an irrelevant operator direction, while the sign criterion belongs to the continuous generator.
