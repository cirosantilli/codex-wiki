Brauer group (Br(k))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Central simple algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 816 Articles: 15
The Brauer group of a field k consists of Morita equivalence classes of central simple k-algebras. Multiplication is induced by tensor product, the identity is [k], and the inverse of [A] is the class of the opposite algebra Aop.
