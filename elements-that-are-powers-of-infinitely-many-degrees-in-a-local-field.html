Elements that are powers of infinitely many degrees in a local field

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Hensel's lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For a non-Archimedean local field K, an element of K× is an mth power for infinitely many positive integers m exactly when it belongs to OK×​. Valuations prove necessity; for sufficiency, choose infinitely many m coprime to the residue characteristic and to ∣k×∣, for which exponentiation by m is an automorphism of the unit group.
