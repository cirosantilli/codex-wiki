Axon initial segment

 Home Biology Neuroscience Neuron Axon
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The axon initial segment is the specialized proximal region of an axon near the soma. In many neurons, its voltage-gated ion channels make it the site where an action potential begins. [ref]
