Empirical measure (νN​=N1​∑j=0N−1​δxj​​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability space Probability measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
For observations or orbit points x0​,…,xN−1​, the empirical measure is νN​=N−1∑j=0N−1​δxj​​. Integrating a test function gives its sample average. For orbit points xj​=Tjx, the difference between T∗​νN​ and νN​ telescopes to (δTNx​−δx​)/N, so on a compact metric space with continuous T, every weak limit is invariant.
