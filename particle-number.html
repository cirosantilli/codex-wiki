Particle number (N)

 Home Physics Particle (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 90 Articles: 1
A particle number counts the particles of a specified kind in a system. For a fixed collection, N is a nonnegative integer. In a variable-number quantum system, distinguish a measured particle number, the particle number operator, and its expected value.
