Correlation function

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon
OurBigBook.com Words: 795 Articles: 15
A correlation function measures the joint fluctuation of observables at separated points. For a scalar field, the connected two-point function is G(x−y)=⟨ϕ(x)ϕ(y)⟩−⟨ϕ(x)⟩⟨ϕ(y)⟩.
