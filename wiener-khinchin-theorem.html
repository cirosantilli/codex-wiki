Wiener-Khinchin theorem (Γ(τ)=∫S(Ω)e−iΩτdΩ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The Wiener-Khinchin theorem relates the autocorrelation of a weakly stationary process to its power spectral density by a Fourier transform. The displayed convention is appropriate when the optical correlation is Γ(τ)=⟨f∗(t)f(t+τ)⟩ and the analytic field contains e−iωt. A Lorentzian line shape of half-width κ gives exponential correlation decay e−κ∣τ∣, including the carrier factor e−iωτ when the full field is correlated.
