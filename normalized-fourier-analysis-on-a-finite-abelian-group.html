Normalized Fourier analysis on a finite abelian group

For a finite abelian group G, use the normalized average Ex​=∣G∣−1∑x​, normalized convolution
(f∗g)(x)=Ey​f(x−y)g(y),
(31)
and Fourier transform f​(γ)=Ex​f(x)γ(x)​. Then Parseval's identity and the convolution identity take the forms
⟨f,g⟩=∑γ∈G​f​(γ)g​(γ)​,f∗g​(γ)=f​(γ)g​(γ).
(32)
