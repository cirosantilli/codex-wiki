Reciprocal-state construction of unambiguous discrimination (∣χi​⟩=∑j​(G−1)ji​∣ψj​⟩,Ei​=ϵ∣χi​⟩⟨χi​∣)

For normalized linearly independent pure states, their Gram matrix Gij​=⟨ψi​∣ψj​⟩ is positive definite. The displayed reciprocal vectors satisfy ⟨χi​∣ψj​⟩=δij​. The operator R=∑i​∣χi​⟩⟨χi​∣ has nonzero eigenvalues equal to those of G−1: use the polar factorization of the matrix whose columns are ψi​. Choosing 0<ϵ≤λmin​(G) gives the POVM effects Ei​ and E?​=I−ϵR. Each conclusive outcome has probability ϵ on its own state and zero on every other input. The inconclusive effect preserves positivity, so the construction is a physical measurement in quantum mechanics.
