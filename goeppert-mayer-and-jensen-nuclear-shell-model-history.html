Goeppert Mayer and Jensen nuclear shell model history

OurBigBook.com Words: 112
The 1948 empirical synthesis preceded the 1949 strong spin-orbit interaction proposals. Johannes Hans Daniel Jensen recalled developing that idea in a discussion with colleagues, independently of Maria Goeppert Mayer's work; their shared problem was to account for the observed magic numbers and spins.[ref]
Johannes Hans Daniel Jensen emphasized that even nuclear ground states retain correlations and that independent-particle motion becomes less adequate at higher excitation. This helps explain why the nuclear shell model needs residual interactions and complementary collective descriptions.[ref]
The 1963 Nobel Prize in Physics recognized Maria Goeppert Mayer and Johannes Hans Daniel Jensen for nuclear shell structure. Eugene Paul Wigner's share recognized his contributions to nuclear and elementary-particle theory, especially fundamental symmetry principles, a distinct award motivation.[ref]
