Lensing convergence (κ=21​∇θ​⋅α)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Gravitational lensing Weak gravitational lensing
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
The trace of the angular derivative of the reduced deflection defines convergence in the standard thin gravitational lens equation convention. It equals projected surface mass density divided by the critical surface density, Σcrit​=c2Ds​/(4πGDd​Dds​). If deflection is instead defined as source minus image angle, the divergence relation has a minus sign.
