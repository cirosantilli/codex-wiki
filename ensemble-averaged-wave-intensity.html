Ensemble-averaged wave intensity (E[∣E∣2]=∣E[E]∣2+E[∣E−E[E]∣2])

 Home Physics Wave Wave scattering Random medium Wave propagation in a random medium
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For a random complex wave amplitude E, its mean squared amplitude is I=E[∣E∣2]. Expanding E=m+(E−m), with m=E[E], gives
E[∣E∣2]=∣m∣2+E[∣E−m∣2].
(50)
The mixed terms vanish. Thus the squared coherent field is only one part of the mean intensity; the other is the diffuse contribution. A fixed acoustic or optical normalization converts this squared-amplitude quantity to physical energy flux. This ensemble mean differs from the angularly averaged radiative-transfer mean intensity.
