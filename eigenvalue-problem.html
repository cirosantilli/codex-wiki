Eigenvalue problem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Eigenvalue
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
An eigenvalue problem asks for the eigenvalues and corresponding eigenvectors or eigenfunctions of a linear operator. It is commonly written as the eigenvalue equation Av=λv.
