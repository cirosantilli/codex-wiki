Gradient-like vector field (df(V)<0 off Crit(f))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Morse function
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A descending gradient-like field decreases f away from its critical points and agrees with a negative gradient in chosen Morse lemma coordinates near those points. It transports attaching spheres and belt spheres across regular levels. Perturbations away from the critical points can arrange the transverse intersections used by the Morse-Smale complex, while preserving the prescribed local models.
