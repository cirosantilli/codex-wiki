Transported addition on a uniform pro-p group (x+n​y=(xpnypn)p−n)

For a uniform pro-p group, the power map x↦xpn is a homeomorphism onto Gpn. Transport multiplication back to G by
x+n​y=(xpnypn)p−n.
(4)
Unique roots make this an associative group operation. For odd p, group commutator collection gives x+n​y≡y+n​x(modGpn+1) and x+n​y≡x+n−1​y(modGpn). The operations converge to addition in the Lie algebra of a uniform pro-p group.
