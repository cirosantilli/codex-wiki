For an integrable discrete trading strategy under an equivalent martingale measure, discounted portfolio wealth plus accumulated discounted strategy payments is a martingale. Conditioning its terminal value gives the displayed identity. A formula involving future payments alone requires terminal liquidation, Vn​=0, or inclusion of that terminal value in the final payment. For an attainable claim paid only at maturity, liquidation yields its usual conditional discounted expected value.
