Poincare disc automorphism (fa​(z)=(z−a)/(1−az))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Hyperbolic geometry Poincaré disk model
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
For ∣a∣<1, the Möbius transformation fa​(z)=(z−a)/(1−az) is an isometry of the Poincare disc model sending a to zero. Rotations combined with these transformations describe its orientation-preserving isometries.
