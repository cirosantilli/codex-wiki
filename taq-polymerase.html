Taq polymerase

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics DNA replication DNA polymerase
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
Taq polymerase is a heat-resistant DNA polymerase used in PCR amplification. It requires a paired strand end and nucleotide substrates; heat resistance does not confer target recognition.
