Exponentially narrow gluing-map cusp (∣μ∣<C(δ),C(1−η)∼ηA1/η/e)

OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For fixed 0<A<1 and δ<1, the two folds of the signed gluing-map reduction enclose three scalar fixed-point branches. Writing δ=1−η gives width C=η(1−η)−1[A(1−η)]1/η. Since (1−η)1/η→e−1, the displayed essential asymptotic follows. The fold curves meet with exponentially small width; this is not the ordinary algebraic cusp normal form of a smooth cubic vector field. A negative fixed point lifts to two symmetry-related flow cycles, so the cusp describes distinct return-map branches rather than counting every physical cycle separately.
