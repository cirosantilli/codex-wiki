Puiseux series (∑n≥n0​​an​zn/q)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Laurent series
OurBigBook.com Words: 123 Articles: 2
A Puiseux series permits fractional exponents with a fixed common denominator: ∑n≥n0​​an​zn/q. These are natural local expansions of branches of algebraic functions. A fractional-power root expansion uses such powers to resolve roots that coalesce as a parameter tends to zero.
