Parallel photon beams in linearized gravity (hik​=H(u,y,z)ℓi​ℓk​,Δ⊥​H=16πGρ(u))

For signature (+−−−) and the curvature convention giving □hˉik​=−16πGϵ−1Tik​, take u=t−x, ℓi​=(1,−1,0,0) and Tik​=ϵρ(u)ℓi​ℓk​. A trace-free Lorenz-gauge particular solution has the displayed form. The vector ℓi=(1,1,0,0) is null, and Γijk​ℓjℓk=0, so co-propagating test photons are undeflected by this beam field. For a transversely uniform source, H=4πGρ(u)(y2+z2) is one particular solution. A function of u alone has vanishing wave operator and cannot source nonzero ρ; homogeneous gravitational perturbations and boundary conditions remain separate data.
