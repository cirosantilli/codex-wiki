Phase speed (∣ω∣/∣k∣)

 ... Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Dispersion relation Phase velocity and group velocity Phase velocity
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The magnitude of the phase velocity of a real-frequency plane Fourier mode is ∣ω∣/∣k∣. In an anisotropic medium it depends on the direction of the wave vector and need not equal the group velocity magnitude.
