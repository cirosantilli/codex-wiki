Diagonal of a polygon

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Polygon
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
A diagonal of a polygon joins two vertices of a polygon that are not endpoints of the same side of a polygon. A square has two diagonals of a polygon, forming a two-element group orbit under its dihedral group of symmetries.
