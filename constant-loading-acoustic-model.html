A deliberately restricted forced harmonic oscillator shows two effects of the baryon loading parameter without duplicating the full photon Boltzmann hierarchy. Use conformal time dη=dt/a in seconds, a comoving wavenumber k in inverse metres, constant R=3ρb​/(4ργ​), and equal constant dimensionless Newtonian gauge gravitational potentials Φ=Ψ=0. The energy densities ρb​ and ργ​ use the same units. Neglect expansion drag, Silk damping and evolution of the potentials. The photon-baryon sound speed is cs​=c/3(1+R)​. Writing Θ0​ for the photon monopole temperature perturbation, the photon-baryon acoustic oscillator reduces to
Θ0′′​+k2cs2​Θ0​=−3k2c2​Ψ.
(1)
For y=(Θ0​+Ψ)/Ψ this gives the displayed forced equation. Constant loading and the neglected expansion drag make this a local teaching model, not a self-consistent expanding cosmology with R fixed forever.
Choose y(0)=1/3 and y′(0)=0, the static matter-era Sachs-Wolfe combination normalized by Ψ. With q=kcs​η, direct substitution gives
y(q)=(31​+R)cosq−R,yq​(0)=0.
(2)
At R=0, successive extrema have equal magnitudes 1/3. At the stipulated R=0.6, y(π)=−23/15 whereas y(2π)=1/3. The shifted equilibrium y=−R makes alternating extrema unequal. This is an elementary illustration of how the baryon loading parameter changes acoustic amplitudes; these extrema are neither measured peak heights nor a complete Cosmic microwave background power spectrum. Comparing curves at fixed q isolates the response; at fixed k and η, increasing R also reduces the photon-baryon sound speed and hence the phase.
For example, holding cη∗​=300Mpc gives sound horizons rs​=173.2Mpc at R=0 and 136.9Mpc at R=0.6. Their ratio is 1/1.6​. Each term of the forced equation has units of inverse seconds squared because y and R are dimensionless and kcs​ is an inverse time. In the actual photon-baryon fluid, R grows with the scale factor and the sound horizon is the integral of the evolving photon-baryon sound speed.
