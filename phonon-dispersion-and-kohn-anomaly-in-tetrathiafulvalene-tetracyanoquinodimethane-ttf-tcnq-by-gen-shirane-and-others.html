Phonon dispersion and Kohn anomaly in tetrathiafulvalene-tetracyanoquinodimethane (TTF-TCNQ) by Gen Shirane and others (1976, 10.1103/PhysRevB.14.2325)

 Home Physics Physics bibliography
OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by Gen Shirane Words: 33
An inelastic neutron scattering measurement of phonon dispersion in deuterated TTF-TCNQ, including the temperature-dependent anomaly near 2kF​. The abstract specifies the 60–300K measurement range and the transition at 54K.
Publisher: doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.14.2325
