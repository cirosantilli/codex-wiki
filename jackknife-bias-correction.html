Jackknife bias correction

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference
OurBigBook.com Words: 61 Articles: 1
For an estimator Tn​ and leave-one-out versions T(−i)​, the jackknife bias estimate and corrected estimator are
Bn​=(n−1)(n1​∑i​T(−i)​−Tn​),TJACK​=Tn​−Bn​.
(242)
If Bn​=a/n+b/n2+O(n−3), the correction leaves bias O(n−2).
