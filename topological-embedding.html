Topological embedding (f:X↪Y)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological space Continuous map
OurBigBook.com Words: 313 Articles: 5
A continuous map injective map f:X→Y that is a homeomorphism onto its image with the subspace topology. A continuous map injective map from a compact space into a Hausdorff space is a topological embedding.
