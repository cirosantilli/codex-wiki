Reflection-symmetric cubic double-zero normal form (q˙​=p,p˙​=−λq+κp+σq3−2σq2p)

The sign σ=±1 distinguishes an inverted quartic potential from a double-well potential. The origin has determinant λ and trace κ. Nonzero equilibria obey q2=λ/σ, with determinant −2λ and trace κ−2λ. Its energy p2/2+λq2/2−σq4/4 has derivative (κ−2σq2)p2. The origin's Hopf bifurcation is supercritical for σ=1 and subcritical for σ=−1. In the latter case the nonzero equilibria have supercritical Hopf bifurcations at κ=2λ<0.
