Polystyrene

 Home Chemistry Polymer
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 1
Polystyrene is a polymer used as a calibration-standard family in organic-solvent size-exclusion chromatography. Its hydrodynamic size in a specified solvent supplies a conditional SEC molar mass calibration for other dissolved chains.
