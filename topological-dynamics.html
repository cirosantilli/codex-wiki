Topological dynamics

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 20
Topological dynamics studies continuous maps and their orbits on topological spaces. For a compact metric space X and a continuous map T:X→X, the pair (X,T) is a compact Hausdorff topological dynamical system.
