Sheaf of modules

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space
OurBigBook.com Words: 9k Articles: 167
For a ringed space (X,OX​), a sheaf of OX​-modules is a sheaf F such that every F(U) is an OX​(U)-module and restriction maps preserve scalar multiplication.
The underlying object is a Sheaf (mathematics) with compatible local module actions.
