Sodium (Na)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
Sodium is the chemical element of atomic number eleven. Carbon-fusion channels in carbon burning include 12C+12C⟶23Na+p, producing sodium-23 and a proton. Its abundance in expelled stellar material depends on later reactions and mixing, rather than on this one production channel alone.
