Charge selection rule for complex scalar correlators (⟨ϕr(ϕ∗)s⟩=0(r=s))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Complex scalar field Global phase symmetry of a complex scalar field
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
In a phase-invariant vacuum, a correlator with r insertions of ϕ and s of ϕ∗ gains the phase eiα(r−s). Its invariance for all real α forces it to vanish unless r=s. Balanced local interactions and the absence of same-charge free contractions give the same rule order by order in Wick's theorem. A symmetry-breaking vacuum is an essential exception to the vacuum-invariance premise.
