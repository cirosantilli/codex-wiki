Use v for molar volume to distinguish it from the fluctuating parcel volume below. The equation is p=RT/(v−b)−a/v2. The parameter b>0 models excluded volume, while a>0 models the reduction in pressure from attractive interactions.
Below the critical temperature, a raw isotherm has a loop. The positive-slope segment is mechanically unstable and surrounding metastable portions do not give the stable equilibrium mixture. Replace the loop by a constant coexistence pressure ps​ satisfying the Maxwell equal-area condition,
∫vl​vg​​[p(v,T)−ps​],dv=0.
(19)
The endpoints are liquid and vapour molar volumes; intermediate mean volumes correspond to phase mixtures with changing proportions. Above the critical temperature, liquid-like and gas-like states connect continuously without a coexistence plateau.
In the pressure-temperature diagram, the liquid-vapour coexistence line ends at the critical point. Below it, the low-pressure side is vapour and the high-pressure side liquid. “Gas” conventionally denotes the region above the critical temperature, where compression alone cannot cross a liquid-vapour transition. The model has no solid phase.
The critical isotherm has an inflection: pv​=pvv​=0. Thus
−(vc​−b)2RTc​​+vc3​2a​=0,(vc​−b)32RTc​​−vc4​6a​=0,
(20)
which give
vc​=3b,Tc​=27Rb8a​,pc​=27b2a​.​
(21)
For fluctuations about a stable equilibrium value x0​, the system plus reservoir has probability proportional to e−A(x)/(kB​T). Expanding availability,
A(x)=A(x0​)+21​A′′(x0​)(x−x0​)2+⋯,
(22)
gives a Gaussian distribution and, to this order, ⟨x⟩=x0​ with
⟨(x−⟨x⟩)2⟩≃A′′(x0​)kB​T​.​
(23)
This requires positive curvature and sufficiently small fluctuations; it is not exact when higher-order terms matter.
The whole cylinder's volume is fixed. For the volume fluctuations of a small fluid parcel, interpret the small quantity as a material parcel containing s moles, whose volume can fluctuate against the much larger remainder. Its mean volume at the critical density is Vˉ=svc​=3sb. At fixed temperature its availability is A(V)=Fs​(V,T)+pbath​V, so
AVV​=−(∂V∂p​)T,s​=−s1​(∂v∂p​)T​.
(24)
On the critical isochore, approached from T>Tc​,
(∂v∂p​)vc​,T​=−4b2R(T−Tc​)​.
(25)
Consequently
⟨δV2⟩≃R(T−Tc​)4kB​sb2T​=3R(T−Tc​)4kB​bVˉT​.​
(26)
Equivalently, ⟨δV2⟩=kB​TκT​Vˉ, with κT​=4b/[3R(T−Tc​)]. The mean-field variance diverges as (T−Tc​)−1, so its critical exponent is 1; the root-mean-square fluctuation has exponent 1/2. A fixed spatial observation box instead has number fluctuations, not fluctuating geometric volume. Finite size and non-Gaussian terms cut off the literal divergence; the approximation fails exactly at the critical point.
