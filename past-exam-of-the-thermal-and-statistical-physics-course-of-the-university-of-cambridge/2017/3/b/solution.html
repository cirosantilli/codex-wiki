Gibbs free energy and its minimum. For fixed atom number N, there are N+n sites and
W(N,n)=(nN+n​)
(29)
configurations with n indistinguishable vacancies. Their configurational entropy is kB​lnW. Relative to the vacancy-free Gibbs free energy G0​, creating n vacancies costs energy nϵ and pressure-volume work npv. Put A=ϵ+pv>0. Then
G(n)=G0​+nA−kB​TlnN!n!(N+n)!​.
(30)
At leading thermodynamic order, Stirling's approximation gives
G(n)≃G0​+nA−kB​T[(N+n)ln(N+n)−NlnN−nlnn].
(31)
The minimum obtained from the stationary point below is
Gmin​≃G0​+NkB​Tln(1−e−A/(kB​T)).​
(32)
If the perfect crystal's internal energy and other entropy are chosen as zero, its pressure term is G0​=pNv. More generally the information given fixes the vacancy contribution rather than the absolute bulk free energy. The pressure-volume term must be retained because the number of sites, and hence the volume, changes.
Equilibrium vacancy population. Differentiating the extensive free energy at fixed N,T,p gives
∂n∂G​=A+kB​TlnN+nn​,∂n2∂2G​=n(N+n)kB​TN​>0.
(33)
The stationary point is consequently the unique interior minimum. Write q=e−A/(kB​T); its condition is n/(N+n)=q, giving the requested thermodynamic result for equilibrium vacancies with variable lattice size,
n∗​≃1−qNq​=e(ϵ+pv)/(kB​T)−1N​.​
(34)
Substitution back into G cancels the terms containing n∗​ and yields the boxed minimum. In the dilute-defect limit A≫kB​T, n∗​≃Ne−A/(kB​T). The Bose-like denominator is a consequence of the changing number of lattice sites and combinatorial entropy; it does not assert that vacancies here are an ideal gas of bosonic particles.
There is a finite-size correction to a variable-site vacancy model if “average” is interpreted exactly. The microscopic count written above gives the relative isothermal-isobaric sum
Y=∑n=0∞​(nN+n​)qn=(1−q)−N−1,
(35)
so
⟨n⟩exact​=q∂q​lnY=1−q(N+1)q​.​
(36)
Its relative equilibrium free energy is −kB​TlnY=(N+1)kB​Tln(1−q). Thus the source's N numerator is correct to leading extensive order, but is not the exact finite-N mean for that stated configuration count. A free-energy minimum is likewise not generally identical to the mean of a finite distribution. These distinctions vanish relatively as N→∞.
Thermal volume. Each atom or vacancy occupies volume v, so at the same thermodynamic accuracy
⟨V(T)⟩≃v(N+n∗​)=1−e−(ϵ+pv)/(kB​T)Nv​.​
(37)
For the exact counting model, replace n∗​ by (N+1)q/(1−q) in v(N+⟨n⟩). The vacancy-free limit is Nv as T→0. This model isolates expansion from vacancies, taking v and ϵ constant; it omits ordinary phonon thermal expansion. Its growth at high temperature should not be extrapolated to arbitrarily large vacancy fractions in a stable crystal, and A>0 is essential for a normalizable variable-site ensemble.
