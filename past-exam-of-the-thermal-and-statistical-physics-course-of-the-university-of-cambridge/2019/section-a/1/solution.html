OurBigBook.com Words: 88
Use the Gibbs-Duhem equation for a homogeneous, extensive, single-component thermodynamic phase:
SdT−Vdp+Ndμ=0.
(1)
At fixed temperature, this becomes dp=ndμ, with number density n=N/V. Separately, at fixed particle number,
(∂p∂n​)T,N​=−V2N​(∂p∂V​)T,N​=nκT​.
(2)
The chain rule along the same isothermal process in the equation of state now gives
(∂μ∂n​)T​=(∂p∂n​)T​(∂μ∂p​)T​=nκT​n.
(3)
Consequently
κT​=n21​(∂μ∂n​)T​.​
(4)
This is isothermal compressibility from chemical-potential response. Since chemical potential has units of energy per particle, the right-hand side has units of volume per energy, or Pa−1, as required. The derivation assumes a smooth bulk equation of state; changing the fractions of coexisting thermodynamic phases at fixed pressure needs separate treatment.
