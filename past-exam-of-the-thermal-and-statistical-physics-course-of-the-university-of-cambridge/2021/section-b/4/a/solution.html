Put β=1/(kB​T) and define the thermal de Broglie wavelength of species α by
λα​=2πmα​kB​T​h​=mα​2πℏ2β​​.
(12)
Ignoring the internal degrees of freedom as stipulated, each molecule's translational canonical partition function is V/λα3​. The two species do not interact, so their partition functions multiply; the treatment of indistinguishable particles requires a separate factorial for each species, not a factorial of the total particle number. Thus
ZCO−O2​​=NCO​!(V/λCO3​)NCO​​NO2​​!(V/λO2​3​)NO2​​​.​
(13)
Using F=−kB​TlnZ and Stirling's approximation gives the extensive Helmholtz free energy
FCO−O2​​=kB​Tα=CO,O2​∑​Nα​[ln(VNα​λα3​​)−1].​
(14)
These are ideal gas expressions, valid for nα​λα3​≪1, with nα​=Nα​/V. The energy zero has been chosen so that separated CO and oxygen molecules have zero internal reference energy.
