Quantum harmonic chain

 ... Branches of physics Classical mechanics Particle dynamics One-dimensional conservative motion Simple harmonic motion Harmonic chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 89 Articles: 1
A quantum harmonic chain has quadratic couplings between canonical coordinates and momenta. A normal mode transformation turns it into independent quantum harmonic oscillators. A continuum elastic action with time coefficient χ and spatial stiffness κ has long-wave frequency ω2=(κ/χ)q2, the structure of a smooth condensate phase action.
