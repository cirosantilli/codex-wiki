Parabolic wave equation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Helmholtz equation Phase screen
OurBigBook.com Words: 682 Articles: 12
The parabolic wave equation is a one-way, slowly varying envelope approximation to the Helmholtz equation. For carrier wavenumber k and transverse coordinate z, a common form is 2ikEx​+Ezz​=0.
