Stoichiometry of ionic compounds (∑i​νi​zi​=0)

 Home Chemistry Chemical bond Ionic bonding Salt (chemistry)
OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 1
For a neutral formula unit, let νi​ count ions of type i with electric charge zi​e, where e is the elementary charge. The stoichiometry must satisfy
∑i​νi​zi​=0.
(1)
For Na+ and Cl−, the smallest positive counts are 1:1. For Ca2+ and Cl−, they are 1:2, giving calcium chloride CaCl2​: 1(2)+2(−1)=0. The formula counts ions, not the number of nearest neighbours. A counterion is the oppositely charged partner needed to balance a charged species; its identity can change solubility and other properties without changing the identity of that species.[ref]
