Abacus of a partition

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Beta set of a partition
OurBigBook.com Words: 852 Articles: 16
An e-runner abacus arranges the nonnegative integers by their residues modulo e and places beads at the positions in a beta set. Moving a bead up one place on its runner removes an e-hook.
An abacus represents the beta set of a partition by beads at integer positions. Positions with the same residue modulo the chosen runner count lie on one runner; moving a bead to an empty position above it represents the corresponding rim-hook removal.
