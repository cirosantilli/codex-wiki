Explicit separatrices of the marginally bound Schwarzschild orbit (m/r=41​tanh2[(ϕ−ϕ0​)/(22​)] or 41​coth2[(ϕ−ϕ0​)/(22​)])

OurBigBook.com Words: 72
A timelike equatorial Schwarzschild geodesic with specific energy E=1 and angular momentum J=4m obeys (du/dϕ)2=2u(u−1/4)2 for u=m/r. Substituting q=2u​ reduces it to (dq/dϕ)2=(q2−1)2/8, integrating to the two displayed branches. One lies outside r=4m and the other inside; each approaches the same unstable circular orbit exponentially in angle and only after infinite proper time. Linearization of u′′=1/16−u+3u2 about u=1/4 gives δu′′=δu/2, confirming instability. These are separatrix approaches or departures, not stable oscillations around the circular orbit.
