For a uniform guide field, write the transverse perturbation in velocity units as b. Linear momentum gives ωu=−k∥​vA​b. Linear induction gives ωb=−k∥​vA​u+ik∥​vA​di​k×b. The helicity decomposition of a transverse Fourier mode then yields the displayed dispersion relation. Its two positive frequency magnitudes are ∣k∥​∣vA​[1+(kdi​)2/4​±kdi​/2], approaching the Alfvén wave frequency for kdi​≪1. For kdi​≫1, the fast branch is a whistler wave and the slow branch tends to ∣k∥​∣vA​/(kdi​).
