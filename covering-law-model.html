Covering-law model

 Home Philosophy Philosophy of science Scientific explanation
OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 2
An approach to scientific explanation that subsumes a phenomenon under general laws and specified circumstances. Its deductive form is the deductive-nomological model; statistical forms permit probabilistic support rather than entailment.
