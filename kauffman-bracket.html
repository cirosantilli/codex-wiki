Kauffman bracket (⟨D⟩)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Link Link diagram
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 25
The Kauffman bracket is the unoriented diagram invariant determined by resolving each crossing into its two smoothings and replacing every additional disjoint circle by the loop factor −A2−A−2. It is invariant under the second and third Reidemeister moves.
