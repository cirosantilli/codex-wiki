Off-shell component count of minimal supergravity (6 bosonic+12 fermionic components)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supergravity Minimal four-dimensional supergravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
A four-dimensional Majorana spinor gravitino has 4×4=16 real components, minus four local supersymmetry gauge functions, leaving twelve before using any Euler-Lagrange field equation. A vierbein has sixteen real components, minus six local Lorentz transformation and four diffeomorphism gauge functions, leaving six. Equivalently, a symmetric metric tensor has 10−4=6. The propagating graviton and massless gravitino each have two states on shell. Six bosonic auxiliary field components complete an off-shell supergravity multiplet.
