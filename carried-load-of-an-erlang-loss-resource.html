Carried load of an Erlang loss resource (mC​(a)=a[1−E(a,C)])

For offered load a and capacity C≥1, the carried load equals the mean occupancy:
mC​(a)=a[1−E(a,C)].
(19)
Differentiating the truncated-Poisson equilibrium weights gives mC′​(a)=Vara​(N)/a>0 for a>0. The carried load increases from zero to C. This monotonicity supplies the strictly increasing term in the convex potential for the Erlang fixed point.
