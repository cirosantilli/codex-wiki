Correlation matrix (C=D−1/2ΣD−1/2)

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Expected value Variance Covariance Covariance matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For a random vector with covariance Σ and positive coordinate variances, let D=diag(Σ). Its correlation matrix is the covariance of D−1/2(X−EX), so it is positive semidefinite with unit diagonal. Each entry is the correlation of the corresponding pair of variables. In particular tr(C)=p, explaining why an eigenvalue in correlation-based principal component analysis represents that eigenvalue divided by p of the total variance. The empirical version is the sample correlation matrix.
