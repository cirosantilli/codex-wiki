Matter-radiation equality

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology
OurBigBook.com Words: 54 Articles: 1
Matter-radiation equality is the epoch at which the background matter and radiation densities are equal. Its horizon wavenumber keq​ sets the turnover scale of the cold-dark-matter transfer function.
