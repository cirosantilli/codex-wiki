Sherrington-Kirkpatrick model (Jij​=J0​/N+Jzij​/N​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Spin model Spin glass
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
The Sherrington-Kirkpatrick model couples every distinct pair of Ising spins using independent standard normal random variables zij​ for i<j, with Jji​=Jij​. Here J0​ and J have units of energy. David Sherrington and Scott Kirkpatrick introduced this scaling in 1975; their original replica-symmetric calculation gave an unphysical negative low-temperature entropy. The model and that initial proposed solution must be distinguished.[ref]
