Modal logic

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 967 Articles: 17
A modal logic extends propositional connectives by operators such as □ and its dual ◊A=¬□¬A. An interpretation determines their meaning: necessity, knowledge and formal provability lead to different axiom systems. In a relational semantics, □A holds at a world when A holds at every accessible world. Provability logic interprets the box through arithmetically coded proofs.
