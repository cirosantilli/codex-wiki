Unitary orbit of a density operator (O(ρ)={UρU†:U∈U(N)})

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The unitary orbit consists exactly of density operators with the same spectrum. If distinct eigenvalues have multiplicities nj​, its stabilizer is ∏j​U(nj​) and its real dimension is N2−∑j​nj2​. A reachable compact group with Lie algebra g is transitive on this orbit exactly when dimg−dim(g∩{X:[X,ρ]=0}) equals the orbit dimension. Global phases act trivially by conjugation.
