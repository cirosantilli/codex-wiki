Empty-box indicators in uniform allocation (p=(1−1/m)n,q=(1−2/m)n)

OurBigBook.com Words: 75
Place n≥1 balls independently and uniformly into m≥2 boxes. If Ii​ indicates that box i is empty, then EIi​=p, var(Ii​)=p(1−p), and, for i=j,
cov(Ii​,Ij​)=q−p2<0.
(61)
Indeed all balls avoid one specified box with probability p and two specified boxes with probability q. The indicator random variable identities give the expectations, variances, and covariance. Finally 1−2/m<(1−1/m)2 proves strict negativity. Thus the number K of empty boxes has EK=mp and var(K)=mp(1−p)+m(m−1)(q−p2) by the variance of a sum.
