Dust gravitational dispersion relation with gas drag (s[(s+ϵΩ)2+Ω2]+(c2k2−2πGσ0​∣k∣)(s+ϵΩ)=0)

The local axisymmetric density/velocity system of a pressured dust layer with linear gas drag and a fixed Keplerian shearing sheet gas flow has this cubic dispersion relation. The Ω/2 azimuthal velocity coefficient includes advection of the Keplerian shear. The razor-thin disk Poisson kernel supplies the ∣k∣ self-gravity term. Keeping the full amplitude determinant retains the zero-frequency branch and avoids dividing out the secular mode.
