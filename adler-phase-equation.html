Adler phase equation (θ˙=ω−ϵsinθ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Phase oscillator
OurBigBook.com Words: 123 Articles: 2
The relative-phase drift θ˙=ω−ϵsinθ describes competing frequency mismatch and phase coupling. For 0<ω<ϵ, the equilibrium points are arcsin(ω/ϵ) and π−arcsin(ω/ϵ), stable and unstable respectively. They merge in a saddle-node bifurcation at ω=ϵ; greater mismatch gives running phase dynamics. Adding Gaussian white noise gives overdamped Langevin dynamics in a tilted washboard potential.
