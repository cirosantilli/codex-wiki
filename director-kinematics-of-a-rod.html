Director kinematics of a rod (di′​=u×di​)

 ... Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Elastic filament Elastic rod Material frame of a rod
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
For a differentiable material frame of a rod, there is a unique vector u such that di′​=u×di​. To see this, form the orthogonal matrix R=(d1​,d2​,d3​). Differentiation of RRT=I shows that R′RT is a skew-symmetric matrix; every three-dimensional skew-symmetric matrix has the unique form v↦u×v. Primes may denote differentiation with respect to reference or current arc length, but that choice must be stated. In a planar bend about d2​, reference differentiation gives d3′​=ud1​ and d1′​=−ud3​.
