Fourier solution of the strip Dirichlet problem (u(s,y)=g​(s)sinh(s(1−y))/sinhs)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Laplace operator Laplace equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
For the Laplace equation on 0<y<1, prescribe bottom data g and zero top data. A horizontal Fourier transform reduces the equation to uyy​−s2u=0, giving the displayed multiplier and its limit 1−y at s=0. Integrable transformed data give a bounded inverse-transform solution. The bounded solution is unique by the maximum principle for harmonic functions, using barriers εcosh(δx)cos(δ(y−1/2)) for 0<δ<π on expanding rectangles. Without a growth condition, one can add eπxsin(πy), so the two boundary traces alone do not determine an arbitrary harmonic function on an infinite strip.
