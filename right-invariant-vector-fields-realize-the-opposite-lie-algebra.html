Right-invariant vector fields realize the opposite Lie algebra ([rξ​,rη​]=−r[ξ,η]​)

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Left and right translation on a Lie group Right-invariant vector field
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For a Matrix Lie group, the right-invariant vector field is rξ​(g)=ξg. Differentiating these ambient matrix-valued functions in the definition of the Lie bracket of vector fields gives [rξ​,rη​](g)=(ηξ−ξη)g. Thus ξ↦rξ​ is an antihomomorphism, and ξ↦−rξ​ is a homomorphism. In contrast, ξ↦lξ​ for left-invariant vector fields is a homomorphism.
