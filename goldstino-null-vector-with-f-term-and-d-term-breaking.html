For canonical global four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry, define Fi=Wi​​, Hij​=Wij​ and Gia​=gaϕˉ​j​(Ta)ji​ at a stationary vacuum. Therefore the canonically normalized fermion mass matrix, after a harmless gaugino phase choice, obeys
(H−2​GT​−2​G0​)(FD/2​​)=0.

If supersymmetry is broken, this vector is nonzero and specifies a massless Goldstino. The matrix with off-diagonal blocks −G instead acts on (F,D); it is related to the canonical matrix by a change of normalization, not by equality of canonical entries. Additional zero modes can coexist with the Goldstino.
