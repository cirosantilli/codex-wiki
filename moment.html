Moment (E[Xn])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Expected value
OurBigBook.com Words: 216 Articles: 7
The nth raw moment of a random variable X is E[Xn] when this expectation exists.
More generally, a moment is an integral weighted by a power of a coordinate against a measure or density; raw, central, and standardized moments are distinguished by the coordinate and normalization used.
