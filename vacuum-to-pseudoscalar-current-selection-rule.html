Vacuum-to-pseudoscalar current selection rule (⟨0∣Vμ∣P(p)⟩=0,⟨0∣Aμ∣P(p)⟩∝pμ)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Pion Leptonic pion decay
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
Lorentz covariance allows only a multiple of pμ for a vacuum-to-spin-zero one-particle current. In its rest frame only the temporal component can remain. A parity-even temporal vector current between a parity-even vacuum and parity-odd particle changes sign, forcing that component to vanish. A temporal axial current is parity-odd and is allowed. This explains why the pion decay constant enters the axial, rather than vector, part of the weak charged current.
