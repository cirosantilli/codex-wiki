Collisionless phase mixing

 Home Physics Plasma (physics) Landau damping
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For freely streaming particles, a spatial Fourier perturbation of a velocity distribution evolves with factors e−ikvt. Contributions from different velocities acquire different phases and can cancel in a density or field moment even while the fine-grained distribution retains structure. In a self-consistent plasma, resonant field–particle exchange gives Landau damping. This is distinct from collisional thermalization and from orbital phase mixing in a planetary ensemble.
