Impulse approximation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 391 Articles: 7
In a rapid encounter, the impulse approximation holds stellar positions fixed while integrating an external acceleration over time. A stellar velocity kick is Δv=∫atidal​(t)dt. If the encounter time p/v is much shorter than the orbital time Ω−1, this gives reliable leading tidal heating for a weak, distant encounter. Slow perturbations require adiabatic shielding of tidal encounters instead.
