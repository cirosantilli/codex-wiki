Shannon's block-entropy approximations

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Entropy rate
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
In A Mathematical Theory of Communication by Claude Shannon, Claude Shannon compares block uncertainty per symbol, denoted GN​, with conditional uncertainty given the preceding symbols, denoted FN​. Theorems 5 and 6 connect both decreasing approximations to the source's limiting uncertainty. He uses symbol statistics at increasing block lengths to expose structure missed by single-letter counts.[ref] The fair marginal bits of the binary Markov source provide a small exact example of that distinction.
