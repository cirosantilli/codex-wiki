At expiry, the difference of a European call and put with matching strike is (ST​−K)+−(K−ST​)+=ST​−K. Linearity of pricing therefore makes their difference equal the forward-contract value from part (c):
C(t,S)−P(t,S)=S+rδ​(1−e−r(T−t))−Ke−r(T−t).​
(35)
A direct put-call parity replication gives the same result. Buy one unit of the commodity and deposit δ∫tT​e−r(u−t)du in the bank. Withdraw the fixed cash storage payments from this reserve; the reserve is exhausted at expiry, leaving one commodity unit. Its initial cost is S+δ(1−e−rτ)/r. Borrow Ke−rτ as well, leaving terminal value ST​−K. The law of one price proves the parity identity. If r=0, replace the storage reserve by δτ.
