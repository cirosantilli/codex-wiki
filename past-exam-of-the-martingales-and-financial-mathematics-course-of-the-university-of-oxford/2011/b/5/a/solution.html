Put σ=σ12​+σ22​​>0. The linear combination W3​ starts at zero and has continuous paths. An increment over an interval of length h is a linear combination of independent centred normal random variables, so is centred normal with variance
(σ2σ12​​+σ2σ22​​)h=h.
(31)
Increments on disjoint intervals are independent, since they depend on disjoint increments of the two independent Brownian motions. These are the defining properties, so W3​ is a standard Brownian motion.
