Let μX​ be the distribution measure of X. The measurable change-of-variables rule for expectations gives
EX=∫R​xμX​(dx)=∫R​xdF(x).
(11)
For the second equality, let U be uniform on (0,1) and Q=F←. The quantile function is measurable and satisfies
Q(u)≤x⟺u≤F(x).
(12)
This equivalence uses monotonicity and right-continuity of F. Consequently P(Q(U)≤x)=F(x): Q(U) has the same distribution as X. In particular ∫01​∣Q(u)∣du=E∣X∣<∞, so the signed integral is legitimate. Hence the quantile expectation formula is
EX=∫R​xdF(x)=∫01​F←(u)du.​
(13)
No continuity or strict increase of the distribution function is required.
