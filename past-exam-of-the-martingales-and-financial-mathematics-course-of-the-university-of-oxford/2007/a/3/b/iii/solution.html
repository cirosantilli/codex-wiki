There is an indexing error at the printed initial time. The dyadic sigma-algebra F0​ is trivial, whereas the stated formula gives
X0​=1(0,1/2]​.
(25)
This is not F0​-measurable. Moreover EX0​=1/2 and EX1​=1, so the process cannot be a martingale on all of N0​ as printed.
For every n≥1, its support In​=(1/2−2−n,1/2] is one atom of Fn​, so Xn​ is adapted and integrable with expected value one. At the next dyadic level, In​ splits into two equal-measure children; Xn+1​ is zero on its left child and 2n+1 on its right child In+1​. Its conditional average on the parent is 2n, and on every other parent it is zero. Hence
E(Xn+1​∣Fn​)=Xn​(n≥1).
(26)
Thus the printed process is a martingale from time one onward. The intended full-index repair is to define X0​≡1 and leave all n≥1 terms unchanged, since then E(X1​∣F0​)=1.
If ω=1/2, it eventually lies outside the shrinking intervals, so Xn​(ω)→0. At the single null set ω=1/2, the values tend to infinity. Therefore
Xn​→0 almost surely,nlim​EXn​=1.​
(27)
The repaired martingale is not uniformly integrable and does not converge in L1. This is the time-zero correction for a half-point dyadic spike martingale.
