Let Δt​ be the number of stock units and βt​ the number of bank-account units, with predictable holdings satisfying the integrability conditions for the price integrals. The portfolio value is Vt​=Δt​St​+βt​Bt​. It is self-financing if
Vt​=V0​+∫0t​Δu​dSu​+∫0t​βu​dBu​.​
(34)
Thus changes in value come only from gains on the assets held. Rebalancing transfers money between the stock and bank account; it does not inject or withdraw cash. The definition is an identity of stochastic integrals and automatically includes the bookkeeping needed when holdings themselves vary randomly.
