Negative-degree divisor has no sections (degD<0⟹ℓ(D)=0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Divisor on an algebraic curve Riemann-Roch space
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
If a divisor on an algebraic curve D on a smooth projective curve has negative degree of a divisor, then its Riemann-Roch space is zero. Otherwise a nonzero f∈L(D) would make (f)+D an effective divisor of degree degD<0, since a principal divisor has degree zero. This is impossible. In genus one the Riemann-Roch theorem consequently gives ℓ(D)=degD for every divisor of positive degree.
