Gravitational redshift (νr​/νe​=Ne​/Nr​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 198 Articles: 2
In a static spacetime with gtt​=−N2, a photon has conserved Killing energy E=−pt​. A static observer with four-velocity N−1∂t​ measures frequency proportional to E/N. Hence light emitted at one lapse and received at another obeys the displayed ratio. For N=1+Φ/c2+O(Φ2/c4) it gives the weak-field shift (νr​−νe​)/νe​=(Φe​−Φr​)/c2. This local clock effect does not require light-ray bending.
