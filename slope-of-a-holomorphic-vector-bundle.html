Slope of a holomorphic vector bundle (μ(E)=degE/rankE)

 ... Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Holomorphic vector bundle Degree of a holomorphic vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 2
The slope of a nonzero holomorphic vector bundle on a compact Riemann surface is its degree of a holomorphic vector bundle divided by its rank of a vector bundle. It measures degree per fibre dimension. Comparing subbundle slopes defines semistable holomorphic vector bundles.
