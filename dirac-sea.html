Dirac sea

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Mode expansion of a Dirac field Antiparticle
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
The historical interpretation fills the negative-energy electron states of the Dirac equation and uses the Pauli exclusion principle to prevent additional electrons from falling into them. Removing an electron of energy −E and charge −e gives a hole of excitation energy E and charge +e, interpreted as a positron. A quantum field theory instead treats negative-frequency field coefficients as independent antiparticle creation operators in a Fock space, with positive excitation energies after normal ordering. No literal infinite material sea is needed. Bosonic antiparticles also exist, although a filled-sea exclusion argument cannot stabilize bosonic negative-energy levels.
