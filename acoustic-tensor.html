Acoustic tensor (Qij​(ν)=cαiβj​να​νβ​)

 ... Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Finite elasticity Hyperelastic material Incremental elastic moduli
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The acoustic tensor determines the plane waves of a locally uniform incremental elastic medium. For a unit material direction ν, its eigenvalues are ρ0​c2 and its eigenvectors are displacement polarizations. Major symmetry of the incremental elastic moduli makes Q a real symmetric matrix. Positivity on every rank-one matrix m⊗ν makes all its wave speeds real and nonzero.
