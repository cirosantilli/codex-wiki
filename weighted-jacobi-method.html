Weighted Jacobi method

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Numerical linear algebra Stationary iterative method for a linear system Jacobi method
OurBigBook.com Words: 430 Articles: 5
With relaxation parameter ω, weighted Jacobi updates by
x(ν+1)=x(ν)+ωD−1(b−Ax(ν)).
(128)
Its iteration matrix is Hω​=I−ωD−1A.
Equivalently, the weighted Jacobi method takes a weighted average of the old vector and its full Jacobi method update: x(k+1)=(1−ω)x(k)+ωxJacobi(k+1)​. If Bv=λv, its iteration matrix multiplies that eigenvector by 1−ω+ωλ. For the diagonal-4 Jacobi iteration for a three-variable tridiagonal system, the factors are 1−ω and 1−ω±ω2​/4; a Poisson equation smoothing parameter must not be inferred from this different matrix.
