Galaxy cluster

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe
OurBigBook.com Words: 264 Articles: 4
A galaxy cluster is a gravitationally bound collection of galaxies, diffuse gas and dark matter. Much of its ordinary matter can reside in hot intracluster gas, visible through thermal bremsstrahlung and the Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect. The two signals weight the electron density differently, making the cluster useful both as a structure-formation probe and, with a geometrical model, as a distance indicator.
