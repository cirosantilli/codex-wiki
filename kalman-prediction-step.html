Kalman prediction step (mt−​=At​mt−1​+Bt​ut−1​,Pt−​=At​Pt−1​At⊤​+Qt​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Control theory State estimation Kalman filter
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The Kalman prediction step advances the previous posterior mean and posterior covariance matrix through the state transition:
mt−​=At​mt−1​+Bt​ut−1​,Pt−​=At​Pt−1​At⊤​+Qt​.
(39)
The minus superscript marks information before observing yt​. The transpose and matrix multiplication propagate the old uncertainty; Qt​ adds the uncertainty of the new process noise. A known control input changes the mean but contributes no additional variance. If the control input itself is uncertain, that uncertainty needs its own model.
