Spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Symmetric matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
Every real symmetric matrix has an orthonormal basis of real eigenvectors. Equivalently, it admits an orthogonal diagonalization A=OTDO with D real diagonal and O an orthogonal matrix.
This is the finite-dimensional real-symmetric case of the spectral theorem.
