Temporal sensing in bacterial chemotaxis

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Chemotaxis
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
Temporal sensing in bacterial chemotaxis compares changing stimulation during movement with a recent adapted baseline. Attractant increases can lengthen runs and decreases can increase tumbling, producing biased movement up a chemical gradient. Rapid spatially uniform concentration steps distinguish a temporal response from a requirement for front-to-back spatial comparison.[ref]
