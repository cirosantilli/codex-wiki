Orthogonal polynomial contrast

 ... Statistical modelling Normal linear model One-way normal linear model Cell-means parametrization Linear contrast of cell means Treatment contrast
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
At equally replicated quantitative treatment levels, evaluations of orthogonal polynomials generate pairwise orthogonal treatment contrasts, using the replication-weighted inner product. Five equally spaced levels give four contrast directions representing degrees one through four, besides the constant direction. This decomposes treatment variation without assuming the response has low polynomial degree.
