Gaussian phase averaging (Eeiϕ=e−Var(ϕ)/2)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Helmholtz equation Phase screen Random phase screen
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
For a centered real Gaussian random variable ϕ, its characteristic function gives Eeiϕ=exp[−Var(ϕ)/2]. Applied to a random phase screen, this controls the coherent and diffuse wave fields.
