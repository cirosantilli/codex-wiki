Chemical reaction network

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 27
A chemical reaction network specifies chemical species, reactions between them, and their stoichiometric vectors. It can be modelled deterministically by reaction-rate equations or stochastically by molecule-copy-number jumps.
