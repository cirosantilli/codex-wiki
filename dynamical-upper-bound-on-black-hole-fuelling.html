Dynamical upper bound on black-hole fuelling

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical black hole Black-hole accretion
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For a singular isothermal sphere of dispersion σ and gas fraction fg​, placing all gas inside radius R onto a hole in time R/σ gives M˙dyn​∼2fg​σ3/G. This is an optimistic supply bound that omits angular momentum transport, star formation, and active-galactic-nucleus feedback. A numerical comparison with the Eddington accretion rate requires a gas fraction, reference radiative efficiency, opacity, and crossing-time convention.
