Pulley

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
A wheel guiding a rope or cable so that it can redirect a tension force. An ideal fixed pulley changes the force direction without multiplying its magnitude. A freely rotating pulley with negligible moment of inertia and friction has equal rope tensions on its two sides; a finite moment of inertia instead relates their difference to its angular acceleration through the net torque.
