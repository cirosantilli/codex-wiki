Phonon

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Bose-Einstein statistics Bose-Einstein distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 46
A phonon is a bosonic quantum of a crystal's normal modes. Because phonon number is not conserved in thermal equilibrium, its chemical potential is zero.
