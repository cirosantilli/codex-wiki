Pathwise uniqueness

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Stochastic differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
Pathwise uniqueness holds when any two solutions with the same initial value and driven by the same Brownian motion on the same filtered probability space are indistinguishable.
