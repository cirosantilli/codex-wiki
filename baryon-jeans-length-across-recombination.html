Baryon Jeans length across recombination

 ... Branches of physics Cosmology Linear cosmological density perturbation Jeans wavenumber Jeans instability Comoving Jeans length
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
Before cosmological recombination, photon-baryon sound speed controls the baryonic comoving Jeans length. The tightly coupled photon-baryon fluid has cs2​=c2/[3(1+Rb​)], where Rb​=3ρˉ​b​/(4ρˉ​γ​)∝a. Using total background density in the instantaneous dynamical Jeans estimate, the scale grows as a during radiation domination, approaches a plateau in strongly baryon-loaded matter domination, and drops when photon decoupling removes photon pressure support. Subsequently, adiabatic monatomic-gas cooling gives Tb​∝a−2 and a comoving Jeans length proportional to a−1/2. Residual Compton scattering can delay that cooling law.
