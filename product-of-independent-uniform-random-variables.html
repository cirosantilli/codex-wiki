For independent random variables X,Y with the uniform distribution on (0,b), use the probability density of a product of random variables and integrate 1/(b2x) over z/b<x<b. The displayed probability density function is zero off (0,b2) and has integral one. More generally, for independent and identically distributed factors Uj​ with this uniform distribution, Wn​=∏j=1n​Uj​ satisfies
E[Wnα​]=(1+αbα​)n(α>−1),EWn​=(b/2)n,Var(Wn​)=(b2/3)n−(b2/4)n.
(71)
The expectation of a product of independent random variables proves these identities directly.
