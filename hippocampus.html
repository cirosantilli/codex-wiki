Hippocampus

 Home Biology Neuroscience Brain
OurBigBook.com Words: 352 Articles: 7
A brain structure involved in episodic memory and spatial processing. In computational accounts, dentate gyrus input transformation, recurrent hippocampal CA3 processing and hippocampal CA1 output processing supply complementary operations; these assignments are hypotheses constrained by recordings and interventions.
