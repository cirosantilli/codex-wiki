Equilibrium heat capacity of interconverting states (CV​=N∑i​xi​ci​+NVari​(ui​)/(kB​T2))

For independent particles with interconverting state families i, the equilibrium partition function is a sum q=∑i​qi​ and the family probabilities are xi​=qi​/q. Let ui​ be the conditional mean one-particle energy in family i and ci​=dui​/dT. The total canonical energy variance separates into within-family and between-family contributions, giving
CV​=N∑i​xi​ci​+kB​T2N​[∑i​xi​ui2​−(∑i​xi​ui​)2].
(41)
The final positive term is absent if family fractions are held fixed. A frozen ortho-para mixture and an equilibrating mixture can therefore have different heat capacities at the same instantaneous composition.
