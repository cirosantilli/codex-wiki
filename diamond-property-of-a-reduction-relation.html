Diamond property of a reduction relation

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Computability theory Lambda calculus Untyped lambda calculus Beta reduction
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
A reduction relation → has the diamond property if a→b and a→c imply a common one-step successor b→d←c. For relations allowing an identity step, the same definition includes zero-length joins. A finite grid of these diamonds proves confluence of →∗. Ordinary one-step beta reduction fails this property because contracting before duplication can save two separate contractions.
