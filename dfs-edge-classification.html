DFS edge classification

 Home Computer science Algorithm Depth-first search
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 4
Classify directed edges relative to a depth-first search forest. Tree edges discover a new vertex; forward edges reach a proper descendant without being its discovery edge; back edges reach an active ancestor; cross edges connect completed branches or trees. Discovery and finish intervals are nested for ancestry. The classification depends on the chosen search order, not only on the graph.
