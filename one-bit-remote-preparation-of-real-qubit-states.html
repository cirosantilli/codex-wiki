One-bit remote preparation of real qubit states (iY∣βθ​⟩=∣αθ​⟩)

OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For ∣αθ​⟩=cosθ∣0⟩+sinθ∣1⟩, Alice measures her half of a Bell state in the real basis {∣αθ​⟩,∣βθ​⟩}, with ∣βθ​⟩=−sinθ∣0⟩+cosθ∣1⟩. Bob receives one outcome bit and applies I or iY. Since iY∣βθ​⟩=∣αθ​⟩ for every θ, the preparation is exact and the correction is independent of the unknown parameter.
