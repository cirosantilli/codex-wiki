Constant-coefficient elliptic second-derivative estimate (θ∥D2u∥2​≤∥AijDij​u∥2​)

If the constant symmetric matrix A satisfies Aξ⋅ξ≥θ∣ξ∣2, then every smooth function u with compact support satisfies
θ∥D2u∥2​≤∥AijDij​u∥2​.
(762)
Indeed, the Fourier transform of a derivative and the Plancherel theorem turn the squared norms into integrals with Fourier multipliers ∣ξ∣4 and (Aξ⋅ξ)2.
