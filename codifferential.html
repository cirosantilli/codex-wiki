Codifferential (δ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form Hodge star operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 3
The codifferential is the formal L2 adjoint of the exterior derivative. On p-forms in dimension n, one convention is
δ=(−1)n(p+1)+1∗d∗.
