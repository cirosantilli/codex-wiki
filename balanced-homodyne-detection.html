Balanced homodyne detection

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum optics Optical homodyne detection
OurBigBook.com Words: 167 Articles: 1
Mix a signal mode a and an independent coherent reference mode b on a balanced beam splitter, detect both outputs, and subtract the currents. For c=(a+ib)/2​ and d=(ia+b)/2​, the difference is I=c†c−d†d=i(a†b−ab†). A reference β=∣β∣eiϕ gives mean 2∣β∣⟨Xϑ​⟩ with ϑ=ϕ+π/2. Other beam-splitter conventions shift this phase calibration. The exact finite-reference balanced homodyne variance quantifies the strong-reference approximation.
Matched subtraction cancels the shared total-power term. It therefore suppresses common reference intensity noise, while imbalance and phase fluctuations reduce this advantage. Repeated samples measure a distribution, not just its mean; scanning reference phase supports optical homodyne tomography.
