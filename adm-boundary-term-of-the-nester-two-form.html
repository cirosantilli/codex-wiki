For asymptotically constant spinors, the leading intrinsic spin connection term gives half the ADM metric integrand times K∞0​. The Sen spinor connection correction gives −(kij​−khij​)K∞j​. The constant-spinor derivative term integrates to zero by antisymmetry of the gamma matrices, and the remaining quadratic terms vanish under the standard decay. This identifies the Nester two-form flux with the ADM energy-momentum contraction, including momentum rather than just energy.
