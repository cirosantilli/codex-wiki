Rational generating function

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Power series Generating function
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A rational generating function is a formal power series equal to a quotient of two polynomials, with denominator nonzero at the origin. Multiplying by the denominator shows that its coefficients eventually satisfy a linear recurrence with constant coefficients.
