Partial inner product ((⟨a∣⊗I)∣Ψ⟩)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor product
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A partial inner product contracts one factor of a bipartite state against a fixed bra, leaving a vector in the other factor. For ∣Ψ⟩=∑i,j​cij​∣i⟩⊗∣j⟩ and ∣a⟩=∑i​ai​∣i⟩, the result is ∑i,j​ai​​cij​∣j⟩. It is linear in ∣Ψ⟩ and conjugate-linear in ∣a⟩. For a normalized rank-one projective outcome on the first factor, its squared norm is the outcome probability; divide by that norm to obtain the conditional pure state when it is nonzero.
