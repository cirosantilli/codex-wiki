Phase-reversed double-slit diffraction

 ... Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Interference (wave propagation) Double-slit experiment Double-slit interference
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 1
Two equal rectangular apertures of width a, centre spacing D, and relative phase π have Fraunhofer diffraction amplitude proportional to 2iasinc(qa/2)sin(qD/2), with q=(2π/λ)sinθ and sincz=sinz/z. Hence
I(θ)∝sin2(qD/2)sinc2(qa/2).
(35)
The interference zeros satisfy Dsinθ=mλ, including the central dark fringe. The single-aperture envelope vanishes at asinθ=nλ for nonzero integer n. A uniform phase reversal translates the narrow-slit fringes by half a fringe without changing their spacing.
