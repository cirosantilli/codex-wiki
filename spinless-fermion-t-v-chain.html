OurBigBook.com Words: 77
A tight binding chain of one species of fermions with nearest-neighbour hopping and density interaction. At half filling and V/∣t∣→+∞, minimizing the interaction requires alternating occupied and empty sites. An even periodic chain has two such configurations at t=0; finite hopping admixes defects through virtual hopping. A finite ring can select superpositions rather than one ordered product state. Two uncoupled copies have four limiting ordered choices when each is half filled, without a general claim of exact finite-hopping fourfold state degeneracy.
