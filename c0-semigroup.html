C0-semigroup (C0​-semigroup)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 50
A C0-semigroup is a family of bounded operators U(t) for t≥0 satisfying U(0)=I, U(t+s)=U(t)U(s), and U(t)x→x in norm as t↓0 for every x.
