Synaptic release probability (p)

 ... Neuroscience Neuron Synapse Chemical synapse Synaptic vesicle Quantal neurotransmitter release
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
Synaptic release probability is the probability of release at a specified site during a specified opportunity, such as one presynaptic action potential. It depends on the site's state and conditions; it is not the probability that the receiving neuron fires. In the example under synaptic vesicle, p refers to one available site, whereas 1−(1−p)4 refers to any of four independent sites releasing.
