Crystallographic defect

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Crystal structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 52
A crystallographic defect is a departure from ideal repeated structure. In an ionic crystal, a missing ion can provide an available site for another ion to move into; movement still requires overcoming an activation energy. This is why an ideal immobile-site model is a limit rather than a universal law for solid electrical conductivity.[ref]
