J-minimizing solution (uJ†​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Regularization of an inverse problem Variational regularization
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
For an exact equation Au=f and a penalty J, a J-minimizing solution is a feasible point uJ†​ such that
J(uJ†​)=inf{J(u):Au=f}.
