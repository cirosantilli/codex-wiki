Ancillary statistic (Lθ​(A) is independent of θ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Sufficient statistic
OurBigBook.com Words: 450 Articles: 4
A statistic is ancillary for a statistical model if its probability distribution is the same for every parameter value. Its marginal law carries no information about the parameter, but its observed value can identify the precision or configuration of the experiment. Fisherian conditional inference uses the conditional distribution of an informative statistic given the observed ancillary value. Ancillarity does not mean that the ancillary is independent of every informative statistic.
