Lorentz spinor double cover (SL(2,C)⟶SO+(1,3))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz transformation Lorentz group Proper orthochronous Lorentz group
OurBigBook.com Words: 163 Articles: 2
Identify Minkowski vectors with Hermitian matrices X=x0I+xaσa​. The action X↦SXS† preserves detX=(x0)2−∣x∣2. It gives a surjective homomorphism from SL(2,C) to the Proper orthochronous Lorentz group with kernel {I,−I}. Polar decomposition retracts the covering group onto SU(2), making it simply connected. This cover permits half-integer spin group representations that do not descend to the Lorentz group itself.
