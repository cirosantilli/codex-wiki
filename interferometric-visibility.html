Interferometric visibility (V=(Imax​−Imin​)/(Imax​+Imin​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Interference (wave propagation)
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 1
Interferometric visibility measures the contrast of a scanned interference fringe. For the mutually coherent, equally polarized two-beam model in wave interference, with fixed individual wave intensities and I1​+I2​>0, the constructive interference intensity and destructive interference intensity give
V=Imax​+Imin​Imax​−Imin​​=I1​+I2​2I1​I2​​​.
(36)
Equal nonzero wave intensities give V=1; I1​=1,I2​=0.25 give V=0.8. Unequal wave intensities can therefore reduce interferometric visibility even with perfect mutual optical coherence. Random phase differences or distinguishable wave polarizations can reduce it further; zero contrast alone does not identify the cause. Both intensities zero leave the ratio undefined.
For normalized paths with complex amplitudes α,β and identical internal states, the output probability is 1/2+Re(α∗βeiθ), so V=2∣αβ∣. A photon polarization fringe alone determines transverse quantum coherence but not the sign of the population difference.
