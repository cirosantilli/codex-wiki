Scalar-QED photon-tadpole symmetry factor (Sgraph​=1/2)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Quantum electrodynamics Perturbative interactions in scalar electrodynamics Seagull vertex
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The two identical photon fields at a scalar electrodynamics seagull give a vertex multiplicity two. Joining them into one closed photon line gives Feynman-diagram symmetry factor 1/2. Directly expanding −e2A2ϕ†ϕ gives only one photon Wick contraction, independently checking the compensation. In a transverse gauge the remaining tensor trace is d−1; a massless photon tadpole is a scaleless integral in dimensional regularization.
