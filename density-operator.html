Density operator

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 208 Articles: 6
A finite-dimensional density operator is Hermitian, positive semidefinite, and has trace one. In dimension N it has N2−1 real parameters. Schrödinger evolution is
ρS​(t)=U(t)ρH​U(t)†
(2)
when ρH​ denotes the time-independent Heisenberg-picture state.
