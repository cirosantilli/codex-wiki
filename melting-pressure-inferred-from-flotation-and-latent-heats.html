Floating ice with submerged volume fraction f has density fρl​ by Archimedes' principle. Its volume change on melting is therefore vl​−vs​=(1−1/f)/ρl​<0. An enthalpy cycle gives Lf​=Ls​−Lv​ for quantities consistently expressed per mass at the same temperature. Treating these latent heats and specific volumes as constant and integrating the Clausius-Clapeyron relation gives the displayed pressure estimate. Using a vaporization latent heat extrapolated from a different temperature carries that approximation into the inferred fusion heat.
