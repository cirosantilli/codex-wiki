Cabibbo angle (θC​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 202 Articles: 3
The Cabibbo angle θC​ describes the real two-generation quark mixing rotation
V=(cosθC​−sinθC​​sinθC​cosθC​​).
(56)
Thus Vud​=Vcs​=cosθC​, Vus​=sinθC​ and Vcd​=−sinθC​. Nicola Cabibbo formulated the mixing of weak charged current couplings before the three-generation mechanism of CP violation; this foundational contribution is distinct from the later extension.[ref]
