Gell-Mann and Zweig quark models

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron Quark model
OurBigBook.com Words: 101
In his 1964 paper, Murray Gell-Mann proposed fractional-charge quarks, constructing baryons and mesons from them. He also distinguished a mathematical constituent model from the hypothesis of finite-mass physical particles. The successful bookkeeping did not itself settle that distinction.[ref]
George Zweig's 1964 CERN model used three constituents called aces, with fractional electric charge and baryon number 1/3. Its abstract also proposed an experimental search.[ref] The theoretical construction and the experiment that might discriminate between interpretations are separate parts of the argument. The Omega baryon discovery experiment illustrates the power of testing an incomplete particle pattern against new observations.
