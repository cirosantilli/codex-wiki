Bipartite Szemerédi regularity lemma

OurBigBook.com Words: 216
For every ε>0 there is a positive integer K(ε) such that every finite bipartite graph has an ε-regular pair of partitions with at most K(ε) cells on each side.
To prove this, give X×Y the uniform probability measure, let f be the indicator function of the edge set, and for partitions P,Q define their energy by
E(P,Q)=∥E(f∣P⊗Q)∥22​=∑i,j​∣X∣∣Y∣∣Xi​∣∣Yj​∣​d(Xi​,Yj​)2.
(10)
This conditional expectation is an orthogonal projection, so 0≤E≤∥f∥22​≤1. If the partitions are not ε-regular, choose witnesses Aij​⊆Xi​ and Bij​⊆Yj​ in every irregular cell pair and refine each Xi​ by all its Aij​ and each Yj​ by all its Bij​. The refined energy exceeds the old energy by
∥E(f∣P′⊗Q′)−E(f∣P⊗Q)∥22​.
(11)
On Aij​×Bij​ the absolute value function of the mean of the difference is greater than ε, while this rectangle occupies at least an ε2 fraction of Xi​×Yj​. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality therefore contributes more than ε4∣Xi​∣∣Yj​∣/(∣X∣∣Y∣) from that cell pair. Irregular pairs have total weight greater than ε, so every refinement raises the energy by more than ε5. The process stops after at most ⌈ε−5⌉ refinements. If the current cell counts are r,s, the construction gives at most r2s,s2r new cells, so a finite iterated bound depending only on ε supplies K(ε).
