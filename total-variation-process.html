For a path of finite variation, its total-variation process is
Vt​(A)=supπ​∑[u,v]∈π​∣Av​−Au​∣,
(1)
where the supremum is over every partition of an interval of [0,t]. It is an increasing process, and the signed measure dA satisfies ∣dA∣=dV(A).
