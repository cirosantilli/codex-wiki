Geometrized units

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 74 Articles: 1
Geometrized units set G=c=1, allowing mass, length, and time to be expressed in common units. Restore GM/c2 for a mass parameter in a radial metric tensor.
