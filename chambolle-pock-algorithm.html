Chambolle–Pock algorithm

For a saddle point problem minu​maxv​{F(u)+⟨Du,v⟩−G(v)}, one update ordering is
uk+1=proxτF​(uk−τD∗vk),vk+1=proxσG​(vk+σD(2uk+1−uk)).
(1)
The proximal operators treat both nonsmooth convex terms, while the extrapolation 2uk+1−uk couples the primal and dual variables. The primal objective is F(u)+G∗(Du), with G∗ the convex conjugate. A dual-first ordering is obtained by exchanging roles; consistent indexing is needed when comparing the formulas.
