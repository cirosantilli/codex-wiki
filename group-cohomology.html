Group cohomology (Hn(G,M))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 70
Group cohomology studies a group through cochain complexes built from its actions on abelian groups. In degree one, H1(G,M) identifies one-cocycles c:G→M modulo one-coboundaries.
