Delay quantization and bandwidth decorrelation (V/V0​∝sinc(πΔνδτ))

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Observational astronomy Radio astronomy Aperture synthesis Radio interferometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
A residual delay δτ in a rectangular band of width Δν attenuates the correlated amplitude by sinc(πΔνδτ). Coarse delay quantization must keep this factor near one; the relevant path scale is c/Δν. Separately, an untracked carrier phase has error 2πδℓ/λ. Fine phase rotation can remove that central-frequency phase while leaving the band-dependent decorrelation, so delay-step specifications must state both bandwidth and compensation architecture.
