Fermi window (w(ϵ)=−∂ϵ​nF​=[4kB​T]−1sech2[(ϵ−μ)/(2kB​T)])

The Fermi window measures sensitivity of occupation to energy near the chemical potential. It is positive, even about μ, has width of order kB​T, and integrates to one over the full energy line. Its centred variance is π2(kB​T)2/3. A band restricted to nonnegative energy contains weight nF​(0,μ) instead of exactly one; extending to the full line is exponentially accurate when μ≫kB​T. At zero temperature the window tends to a Dirac delta function at the chemical potential.
