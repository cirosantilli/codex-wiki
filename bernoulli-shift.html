Bernoulli shift

 ... Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Ergodic theory Measure-preserving transformation Ergodicity
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 2
On a one-sided sequence space EN with product measure, the Bernoulli shift is
S(x0​,x1​,x2​,…)=(x1​,x2​,x3​,…).
(23)
It preserves the product measure. Every invariant event lies in the tail sigma-algebra of the coordinate process, so the Kolmogorov zero-one law makes the shift an ergodic transformation.
