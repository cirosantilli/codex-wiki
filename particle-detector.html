Particle detector

 Home Physics Particle detection
OurBigBook.com Words: 8k Articles: 68
A particle detector converts an interaction involving a particle into a readable signal. A cloud chamber is a particularly direct example: tiny liquid drops turn an invisible ion trail into a photographable line.
