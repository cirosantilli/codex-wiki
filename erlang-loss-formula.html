Erlang loss formula (E(a,C))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory Stochastic network Loss network
OurBigBook.com Words: 444 Articles: 8
For capacity C and offered traffic a, the probability of a full single-resource loss system is E(a,C)=(aC/C!)/(∑k=0C​ak/k!). The stable recursion is E(a,0)=1, E(a,k)=aE(a,k−1)/(k+aE(a,k−1)).
