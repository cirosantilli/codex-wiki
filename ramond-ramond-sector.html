Ramond–Ramond sector

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Type II string theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 79 Articles: 1
The closed-string sector with Ramond sector states on both chiral sides. The left and right spinor tensor product describes differential-form gauge fields. Opposite chiralities in type IIA superstring theory give the massless one-form and three-form, with transverse dimensions 8 and 56. Same chiralities instead give the type IIB form-field content.
