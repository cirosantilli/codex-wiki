Fourier coefficient (f​n​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier series
OurBigBook.com Words: 361 Articles: 6
For an integrable function of period L>0, its complex Fourier coefficients measure its inner product with each complex exponential mode:
f​n​=L1​∫x0​x0​+L​f(x)e−2πinx/Ldx.
(28)
For a real function of period 2π, the real coefficient convention of Fourier series gives f​0​=a0​/2 and f​n​=(an​−ibn​)/2 for n≥1, with f​−n​=f​n​​. For f(x)=1/2+cosx+21​sin(2x), the only nonzero complex Fourier coefficients are f​0​=1/2, f​±1​=1/2, f​2​=−i/4, and f​−2​=i/4. This explains why the real and complex lists look different while representing the same function.
