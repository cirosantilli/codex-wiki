A uniform-density inviscid shear flow with U=U0​z/d for ∣z∣<d and U=±U0​ outside has two vorticity jumps but no walls. Decaying normal modes satisfy w′′−k2w=0 in each region. Continuity of w and pressure matching at a piecewise-linear shear interface gives a two-by-two determinant whose diagonal entries are 1−2kd±2kdc/U0​ and off-diagonal entries e−2kd. Its vanishing gives the displayed dispersion relation. The Kelvin-Helmholtz instability band is 0<kd<αs​, where 2αs​−1=e−2αs​ has its unique positive root above 1/2, approximately 0.63923227. Short waves decouple the two edges and remain neutral; long waves approach the vortex sheet instability with c=±iU0​.
