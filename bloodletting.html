Bloodletting

 Home Biology Medicine History of medicine
OurBigBook.com Words: 107 Articles: 4
Deliberate removal of blood for a therapeutic purpose. Historical practices include venesection, wet cupping and medicinal leeches; their historical rationale must be separated from present-day indications for venesection.
