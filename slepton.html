Slepton (ℓ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supersymmetry breaking Soft supersymmetry breaking Sparticle
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
A slepton is a scalar sparticle partner of a lepton. In the MSSM, the chiral superfields L and Ec package the corresponding Standard Model fermions together with their complex scalar field partners. Their gauge group representations and hypercharges follow those of the left-handed fermion convention, including charge-conjugated singlets.
