Elliptic paraboloid (z=x2/A2+y2/B2)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 431 Articles: 4
An elliptic paraboloid is a smooth bowl-shaped quadratic surface with elliptical horizontal sections. In the circular case A=B, it is a surface of revolution. For z=x2+y2, polar coordinates give the parametrization (rcosθ,rsinθ,r2) and upward vector area element (−2r2cosθ,−2r2sinθ,r)drdθ. A band between two positive heights has two boundary circles and no cap.
