Simultaneous triangularization of a Lie algebra representation (0=V0​⊂V1​⊂⋯⊂Vn​=V)

 ... Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Derived series of a Lie algebra Solvable Lie algebra Lie's theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A Lie algebra representation is simultaneously upper triangular precisely when it preserves a complete flag, with dimVj​=j. A basis adapted to the invariant flag makes every representing matrix upper triangular. For a complex finite-dimensional representation of a solvable Lie algebra, the Lie theorem provides a common eigenvector; iteration on the quotient representations constructs the flag.
