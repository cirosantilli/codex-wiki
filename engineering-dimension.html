Engineering dimension ([X]eng​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Renormalization group
OurBigBook.com Words: 149 Articles: 3
The engineering dimension of a field, parameter or operator is its dimension under the rescaling that leaves the quadratic theory invariant. Interactions can change it into a full scaling dimension.
