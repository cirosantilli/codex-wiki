Spin four-vector (sμ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 1
The spin of a small torque-free test gyroscope is represented by a spacelike vector orthogonal to its four-velocity, sμuμ​=0. Its temporal component vanishes in its own rest frame. Along a geodesic, Dsμ/dτ=0: parallel transport preserves the orthogonality and the invariant spin magnitude. For an accelerated observer the corresponding nonrotating transport is Fermi-Walker transport.
