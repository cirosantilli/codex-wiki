Symmetric product (Symg(X))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
The gth symmetric product of a space X is Symg(X)=Xg/Sg​, where the symmetric group permutes the factors. Its points are unordered g-tuples, with repetitions allowed. If X is a Riemann surface, elementary symmetric functions in local complex coordinates make its symmetric product a smooth complex manifold, including along the locus of repeated points. A collection of disjoint circles in X gives a product torus in this symmetric product.
