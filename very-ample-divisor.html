Very ample divisor

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Cartier divisor Complete linear system of a divisor
OurBigBook.com Words: 64 Articles: 1
A very ample divisor is a divisor whose associated line bundle is a very ample line bundle. A divisor is very ample when its complete linear system defines a closed embedding into projective space.
