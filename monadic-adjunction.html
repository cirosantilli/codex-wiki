Monadic adjunction

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Monad Eilenberg-Moore category
OurBigBook.com Words: 177 Articles: 4
An adjunction is monadic when its comparison functor from the right-hand category to the Eilenberg-Moore category of the induced monad is an equivalence.
The induced monad determines this comparison; Beck's monadicity theorem gives a criterion for it to be an equivalence of categories.
