Lagrangian integral time (tL​=∫0∞​RL​(τ)dτ/RL​(0))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Turbulent mixing Taylor turbulent dispersion
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
The Lagrangian integral time is the area under a normalized velocity autocorrelation along a Lagrangian trajectory. If this correlation is integrable, Taylor turbulent dispersion has asymptotic mean-square displacement 2RL​(0)tL​t. The integral time need not equal every short-time decorrelation scale, particularly when the autocorrelation oscillates.
