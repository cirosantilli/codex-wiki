Consequently [:H:,ap†​]=Ep​ap†​: one application of a creation operator adds precisely the required quasiparticle energy. Before normal ordering there is the usual constant zero-point vacuum energy. If m=0, the exactly zero-momentum mode has E0​=0 and is a free canonical coordinate, not an oscillator with a normalizable oscillator vacuum state; in a finite box handle that mode separately or introduce a small positive mass. The formula applies directly to all modes with Ep​>0.
