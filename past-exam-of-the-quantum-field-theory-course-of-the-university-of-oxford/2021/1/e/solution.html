Put Q=M−3m. For Q<0 the decay width is zero by conservation of energy. Just above the three-particle decay threshold, all three daughters are nonrelativistic. The decay amplitude of iv remains finite: the internal scalar propagator denominator approaches (2m)2−m2=3m2, and the Dirac scalar bilinear does not vanish at rest. Thus, for nonzero fixed g and λ, the leading suppression comes entirely from three-body threshold phase space.
Use the spatial Dirac delta function to eliminate one daughter momentum. The remaining integral has six independent momentum coordinates and one conservation of energy constraint,
∫d3pd3qδ(Q−2mp2+q2+(p+q)2​).
(12)
Rescaling both momenta by mQ​ gives Q3 from the measure and Q−1 from the Dirac delta function. The smooth factors 1/(2E) do not change the power. Consequently
Γ=C(M,g,λ)(M−3m)2[1+O((M−3m)/M)]as m↑M/3,​
(13)
with a finite positive leading coefficient when gλ=0; Γ=0 for m≥M/3. The 1/2! for the identical bosons changes only this coefficient. Equivalently, at fixed M the opening is quadratic in M/3−m. If gλ=0, this leading tree-level Feynman diagram vanishes, so the stated leading coefficient is zero.
