Label the external four-momenta by Ψ(p1​)+χ(p2​)→Ψ(p3​)+χ(p4​). The only tree-level Feynman diagram exchanges a scalar field particle between the transitions Ψ(p1​)→χ(p4​) and χ(p2​)→Ψ(p3​). Its internal four-momentum is q=p1​−p4​=p3​−p2​. Thus its scattering amplitude, with a consistent external-state ordering, is
iM=(−ig)2[uˉχ​(p4​)uΨ​(p1​)](p1​−p4​)2−m2+i0i​[uˉΨ​(p3​)uχ​(p2​)].
(2)
It is the crossed, or u-channel, exchange with these labels. A direct t-channel scalar field exchange would need a flavour-preserving Yukawa coupling, which the Lagrangian density does not contain. There is no annihilation Feynman diagram for two incoming fermions.
