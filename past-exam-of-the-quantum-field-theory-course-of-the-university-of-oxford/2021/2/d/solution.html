Read the unsuperscripted ap​ in the printed Hamiltonian as aps​: the repeated spin label is otherwise missing. Relabel p↦−p in its antiparticle term, and insert both projections from c. Keeping the original operator ordering gives
H​=∫(2π)3d3p​ψ​†(t,p)[21​s∑​(us(p)us†(p)−vs(−p)vs†(−p))]ψ​(t,p)=∫(2π)3d3p​ψ​†(t,p)(α⋅p+βm)ψ​(t,p).​
(23)
The difference of the two orthogonal projectors in a is h(p)/E, explaining the cancellation of the factors E. Under inverse Fourier transformation, p becomes −i∇ acting on ψ. Hence
H=∫d3xH,H=ψ†(−iα⋅∇+βm)ψ=ψˉ​(−iγi∂i​+m)ψ.​
(24)
Spatial indices run from 1 to 3. This is the local Hamiltonian density of the free Dirac field. It also follows from H=πψ˙​−L with π=iψ†.
The original antiparticle term is −bb†, so the result retains its infinite additive vacuum energy. If the Hamiltonian is normal-ordered, its occupation-number expression becomes ∫d3p/(2π)3E∑s​(as†as+bs†bs) and its local expression is :H:, with the same subtraction. The displayed unsymmetrized Hamiltonian density is Hermitian after spatial integration under vanishing boundary terms; a manifestly Hermitian local form replaces the derivative term by −2i​ψ†α⋅∇ψ and differs by a spatial divergence.
