The single-vertex scalar two-point graph at first order in g2k​ has one 2k-valent interaction vertex. Attach the two external lines to it and pair its remaining 2k−2 legs into k−1 tadpole diagrams. The graph is connected, has L=k−1 independent loop momenta, and is momentum independent after amputation apart from the overall Dirac delta function. In particular, first order in the coupling need not mean one loop.
The number of contractions with fixed external labels is
(2k)(2k−1)2k−1(k−1)!(2k−2)!​.
(17)
Dividing by the interaction factorial (2k)! gives the inverse Feynman-diagram symmetry factor 1/[2k−1(k−1)!]. Use m>0 for the mass in the lowest-order propagator and define the tadpole integral
TD​(m)=∫(2π)DdDq​q2+m21​.
(18)
The linear-order correction to the amputated function is consequently
Γlin(2)​(p1​,p2​)=−(2π)Dδ(D)(p1​+p2​)2k−1(k−1)!μdk​g2k​​TD​(m)k−1.​
(19)
This is the correction to the paper's amputated connected correlator, not a definition of an inverse-propagator self-energy with an unspecified sign. At this order using m0​ instead of m gives the same lowest-order integral.
For completeness, Schwinger parameterization and a Gaussian integral give
TD​(m)​=(4π)D/21​∫0∞​dαα−D/2e−αm2=(4π)D/2(m2)D/2−1​Γ(1−D/2).​
(20)
This first converges for 0<D<2 and then defines its dimensional regularization continuation. At D=4−2ϵ, the Gamma function expansion yields
μ2ϵTD​(m)=−16π2m2​[ϵ1​+1−γE​+logm24πμ2​+O(ϵ)].
(21)
Thus its highest ultraviolet pole is
Γlin(2)​=(2π)Dδ(D)(p1​+p2​)2k−1(k−1)!(16π2)k−1ϵk−1(−1)kg2k​μ4−2k(m2)k−1​+O(ϵ−(k−2)).​
(22)
For k=2 the remainder means finite terms. For k>2 the unrenormalized graph has a higher-order pole from its independent ultraviolet subdivergences, rather than only a simple pole. A massless tadpole integral is scaleless and vanishes in dimensional regularization; that does not justify discarding the massive ultraviolet divergence by setting m=0 before separating ultraviolet and infrared effects.
