Use mass dimensions with [x]=−1, [∂μ​]=1 and dimensionless action. The Lagrangian density in Euclidean space has dimension D, and the kinetic term fixes 2+2[ϕ0​]=D. Thus
[ϕ0​]=2D−2​,[λ2k​]=D−k(D−2)=2k−(k−1)D≡dk​.​
(15)
A dimensionless coupling constant is therefore
g2k​(μ)=μ−dk​λ2k​,λ2k​=μdk​g2k​.​
(16)
Here μ is a positive regularization scale with mass dimension one. The interaction is marginal at its upper critical dimension Dc​=2k/(k−1), relevant below it and irrelevant above it by this engineering-dimension test. In particular, d2​=4−D, while dk​=4−2k+2(k−1)ϵ near D=4−2ϵ. At this stage g2k​ is the dimensionless version of the displayed bare coupling; after introducing counterterms one may use the same scale factor for the renormalized coupling.
