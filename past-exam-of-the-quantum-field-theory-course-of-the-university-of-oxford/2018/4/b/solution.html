⟨Tϕ(y1​)ϕ∗(y2​)⟩=C12​−iλ∫dDzC(y1​,z)C(z,z)C(z,y2​)+O(λ2).​

The local quartic interaction vertex has factor −iλ, since 2!2! cancels its denominator 4. The oriented tadpole diagram has Feynman-diagram symmetry factor 1; the isolated two-loop vacuum vertex has factor 1/2 in its coefficient. Coincident kernels and the integrals require a common regularization; none is being assigned a finite numerical value here.
More generally, vacuum bubbles exponentiate and factor from every numerator. Here global U(1) symmetry also makes all one-point functions zero. Thus a surviving graph with the two external insertions in separate connected components would have a component with one unbalanced charge and vanish. This proves that only connected graphs contribute to this particular two-point function to all perturbative orders. Vacuum normalization alone would not remove disconnected components containing external legs in a general four-point function. If one formally chooses a different free kernel, the displayed Wick multiplicities are unchanged; replacing C by Clit​ gives a formal oscillator series, not the Gaussian integral of the action as printed.
