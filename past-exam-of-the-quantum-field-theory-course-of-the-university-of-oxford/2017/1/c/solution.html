The vacuum subtraction of a complex scalar charge leaves all commutators unchanged.[ref]
Scope of the expansion: a fixed-frequency expansion with time-independent oscillator coefficients is the free-field or asymptotic-field form. For arbitrary interacting f, it can be used as canonical oscillator coordinates at a chosen time, rather than as the exact interacting time dependence. For example, f(s)=m2s+λs2 gives (□+m2)ϕ+2λϕ∗ϕϕ=0, which a generic free plane-wave superposition does not satisfy. The Noether charge and its generator action remain valid in the interacting theory; the symmetry does not depend on using a free Hamiltonian.
