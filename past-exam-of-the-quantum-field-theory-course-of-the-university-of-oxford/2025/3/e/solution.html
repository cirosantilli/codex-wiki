The corrected expression in d is a vacuum two-point function, or Wick contraction, of the free Dirac field. It equals the negative spinor transpose of the usual Dirac propagator with reversed spacetime arguments. It supplies the internal fermion lines in Feynman diagrams, rather than a position-space detection probability.
