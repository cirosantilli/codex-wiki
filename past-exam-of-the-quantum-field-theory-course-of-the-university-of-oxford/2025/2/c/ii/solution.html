Keep the particle labels specified under 2. There are two connected tree-level Feynman diagrams: the quartic contact interaction vertex and an s-channel light antiparticle exchange. The initial Noether charge is 1−2=−1, exactly the charge of ϕˉ​. At the first cubic Feynman vertex, the incoming ϕ and Φ leave the second ϕ leg internal; the conjugate Feynman vertex supplies both outgoing particles.
Thus
iM=−ig+(−ih)s−m2+i0i​(−ih),M(ϕΦ→ϕΦ)=−g−s−m2+i0h2​.​
(19)
Here s=(p1​+p2​)2 for incoming ϕ(p1​),Φ(p2​). Both terms have the same sign in the chosen S-matrix convention.
The distinction from antiparticles matters. If the paper's heavy “Φ particle” is instead intended to mean the charge-+2 state Φˉ, so that its printed decay into ϕϕ is allowed, the exchange diagram is crossed. For that alternative physical process,
M(ϕΦˉ→ϕΦˉ)=−g−u−m2+i0h2​,u=(p1​−p4​)2.​
(20)
This is a consistent alternative naming convention only if it is also applied to the decay in iii; one cannot use the same heavy particle label for both charge assignments.
