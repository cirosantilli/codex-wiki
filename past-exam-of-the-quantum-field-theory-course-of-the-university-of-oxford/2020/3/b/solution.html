For the interacting vacuum state defined perturbatively by the same Feynman i-epsilon prescription, the normalized time-ordered product is
⟨Tr=1∏m​ϕ(xr​)⟩=Z[λ;0]1​im1​δj(x1​)⋯δj(xm​)δmZ[λ;j]​​j=0​.​
(32)
Each functional derivative inserts iϕ, accounting for i−m. The denominator implements cancellation of vacuum bubbles, while disconnected pieces carrying external insertions still contribute. The lower limit i=i in the printed product is a typographical error; the product here runs from 1 to m.
At order V in the cubic scalar field interaction term, the expansion of the generating functional supplies 1/[V!(3!)V]. The V! counts permutations of indistinguishable integrated interaction vertices, and 3! counts permutations of the three field slots at each interaction vertex. Applying the Wick theorem pairs fields into Feynman propagators. Several pairings produce the same Feynman diagram; their number NG​ multiplies the expansion coefficient. For fixed labelled external points, the net combinatorial weight is
wG​=V!(3!)VNG​​=SG​1​.​
(33)
Here SG​ is the order of the graph's automorphism group when the external labels are fixed, with permutations of identical parallel lines and reversal of a self-loop included as applicable. This is the Feynman-diagram symmetry factor convention in which a Feynman diagram is divided by SG​. Each cubic interaction vertex then contributes −iλ, each line contributes ΔF​, and each internal vertex is integrated over its position. External labelled points are not divided by m!.
