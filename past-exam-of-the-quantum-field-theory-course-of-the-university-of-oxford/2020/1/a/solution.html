The scalar propagator and Dirac propagator invert their respective quadratic kernels. The interaction vertex follows by expanding exp(i∫Lint​), fixing its sign. Species is conserved along each open fermion line; there is no vertex changing species 1 into species 2.
An interaction vertex carries (2π)4δ(4)(∑k) with inward four-momenta. Impose momentum conservation and integrate each independent loop momentum with d4k/(2π)4. Include the closed fermion loop sign −1 for every fermion loop, the relevant fermionic signs when rearranging external states, and the Feynman-diagram symmetry factor. External incoming and outgoing fermions carry us​(p) and uˉs​(p); incoming and outgoing antifermions carry vˉs​(p) and vs​(p). An external real scalar field leg has factor 1 in an amputated scattering amplitude. The Feynman i-epsilon prescription specifies the poles.
