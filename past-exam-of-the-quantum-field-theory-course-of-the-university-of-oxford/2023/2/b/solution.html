The two complex scalar fields have scalar propagators
⟨Tϕϕ†⟩(p)=p2−m2+iϵi​,⟨TΦΦ†⟩(p)=p2−M2+iϵi​;
(17)
contractions of two unconjugated fields or two conjugated fields vanish. Draw an arrow on each line in the direction of positive conserved charge flow: a solid line carries one unit and a dashed line two units. A particle arrow follows its physical momentum; an antiparticle arrow opposes it.
There are two conjugate three-leg interaction vertices, both with Feynman rule −ig: one has a charge-two arrow entering and two charge-one arrows leaving, and the other reverses every arrow. These describe the two conjugate terms ϕ2Φ† and ϕ†2Φ. The two identical ϕ attachments cancel the 1/2 in the Lagrangian density. All crossed assignments are obtained by moving external lines between initial and final states while maintaining the arrows. There is no interaction vertex with three equal-sign charges.
Each interaction vertex conserves four-momentum, supplying (2π)4δ4(∑pj​) with all momenta entering. Integrate each independent loop momentum with d4k/(2π)4 and divide by the Feynman-diagram symmetry factor. No minus sign is assigned to a closed scalar loop. These rules are for iM after amputating external lines and removing the overall four-momentum conservation factor.
