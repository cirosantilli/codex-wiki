The mass counterterm Feynman rule is −iδm​, cancelling the tadpole diagram. A convention defining a positive mass subtraction by mB2​=m2−δmsub​ instead has δmsub​=−δm​; specifying this convention avoids a spurious sign disagreement.
There is no one-loop wave-function renormalization because the tadpole diagram is independent of momentum. Each one-loop four-point bubble diagram has two scalar propagators, so its large Euclidean loop momentum integral behaves as ∫d2kE​/kE4​ and is ultraviolet finite. Thus no divergent quartic interaction counterterm is required. Finite coupling redefinitions remain possible. At higher loop orders, finite wave-function renormalization may be needed if unit pole residue is imposed; power counting only rules out its ultraviolet divergence.
