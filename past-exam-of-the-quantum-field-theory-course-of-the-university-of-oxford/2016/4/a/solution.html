For incoming four-momenta, the momentum-space Feynman rules in the chosen Minkowski metric convention are:
  • Each internal line contributes the scalar propagator i/(p2−m2+i0).
  • Each quartic interaction vertex contributes −iλ0​ and (2π)DδD(∑p) for momentum conservation.
  • Each independent loop momentum is integrated with dDk/(2π)D; after eliminating redundant Dirac delta functions, one overall conservation factor remains.
  • Each graph is divided by its symmetry factor S, with external labels held fixed.
There is one connected one-loop one-particle irreducible two-point graph: a single quartic vertex with two external lines and one tadpole diagram. Its symmetry factor is S=2 from interchange of the two loop half-edges.
There are three connected one-loop one-particle irreducible four-point graphs: two quartic vertices joined by two parallel internal lines, with external partitions (12∣34), (13∣24) and (14∣23). These are the s, t and u scattering channels; each bubble diagram has symmetry factor S=2. External-branch insertions are excluded by the one-particle irreducible requirement.
