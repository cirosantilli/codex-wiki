Combine the two equal-mass Dirac fields into Ψ=(ψ1,ψ2)T. A constant unitary matrix U acting on this flavour index commutes with the common Dirac Hamiltonian, so Ψ↦UΨ gives the unitary flavour symmetry of degenerate Dirac fields U(2). Its real Lie algebra has dimension four. A convenient Hermitian generator basis is T0=12​/2, Ta=τa/2, a=1,2,3, where τa are the Pauli matrices.
For each generator the canonical anticommutation relations give
ρA​=Ψ†TAΨ,jAk​=ΨˉγkTAΨ,[ρA​(x),H]=−i∂k​jAk​(x).

The calculation is exactly the one in b, since TA commutes with both the Dirac gamma matrices and the common fermion mass matrix. Thus
Four independent number-preserving vector currents:jAμ​=ΨˉγμTAΨ,A=0,1,2,3.​
(41)
Explicitly their densities are
ρ0​=21​(ψ1†ψ1+ψ2†ψ2),ρ3​=21​(ψ1†ψ1−ψ2†ψ2),
(42)
ρ1​=21​(ψ1†ψ2+ψ2†ψ1),ρ2​=21​(−iψ1†ψ2+iψ2†ψ1).
(43)
The two off-diagonal conserved currents are why counting only the individual fermion numbers misses two generators. The singlet generates U(1); the other three generate special unitary group SU(2). Unequal masses would break the off-diagonal conservation laws.
Scope of the count. Four is the count of currents of the displayed ψˉ​AγμTAB​ψB form. The completely free system also has an enhanced internal symmetry of free Dirac fields if mixing with charge-conjugate fields is admitted. For m=0, write its two Dirac fields as four equal-mass Majorana spinors χI. Their orthogonal group O(4) rotations have six continuous generators, with conserved currents proportional to iχˉ​IγμχJ for I<J; the four U(2) currents form a subgroup. This larger count is relevant if “similar” means all local internal currents of the free system, without preserving a chosen fermion number. If m=0, the usual number-preserving chiral flavour symmetry of massless Dirac fields is U(2)L​×U(2)R​ with eight currents; allowing charge conjugation mixing enlarges it to U(4) with sixteen. These counts concern internal currents, excluding spacetime Noether currents.
