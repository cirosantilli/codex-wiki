The auxiliary field σ has quadratic kernel −1 and no kinetic term. Inverting that kernel, rather than introducing an artificial scalar mass, gives the momentum space Feynman rules
σ line: −i,ψ line: p2−m2+i0i(p+m)​,σψˉ​ψ vertex: ig.​
(15)
The auxiliary-field propagator is independent of momentum; in position space it is −iδ(4)(x−y). At each interaction vertex attach (2π)4δ(4)(∑p) for momentum conservation. Integrate independent loop momenta with d4p/(2π)4, respect fermion flow, include the closed fermion loop sign −1, and use the appropriate symmetry factor. Incoming and outgoing fermions carry us​(p) and uˉs​(p); incoming and outgoing antiparticles carry vˉs​(p) and vs​(p). Exchanging external fermions introduces the fermionic sign. Here p=γμpμ​ is Feynman slash notation.
For a dimensional check, [ψ]=3/2, [σ]=2, [g]=−1 in mass dimensions. Eliminating σ consequently produces a four-fermion interaction of negative coupling mass dimension, not a propagating scalar degree of freedom.
