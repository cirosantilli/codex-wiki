Set B(x)=ψˉ​(x)ψ(x), a Grassmann-even Dirac scalar bilinear. Completing the square gives
−21​σ2+gσB=−21​(σ−gB)2+2g2​B2.
(27)
Introduce external Grassmann sources η,ηˉ​ for the Dirac field, but no source for σ. The generating functional is
Z[η,ηˉ​]=∫Dψˉ​DψDσexp{i∫d4x[ψˉ​(i∂−m)ψ−21​(σ−gB)2+2g2​B2+ηˉ​ψ+ψˉ​η]}.
(28)
At a finite regulator the change of variable σ′=σ−gB has unit Jacobian: it translates a bosonic integration variable by a Grassmann-even expression independent of that variable. Its oscillatory Gaussian integral can be defined with a convergence prescription and then continued. It is a constant N independent of ψ,η,ηˉ​. Thus
Z[η,ηˉ​]=N∫Dψˉ​Dψexp{i∫d4x[ψˉ​(i∂−m)ψ+2g2​(ψˉ​ψ)2+ηˉ​ψ+ψˉ​η]}.
(29)
The factor N cancels in Z[η,ηˉ​]/Z[0,0]. Every normalized Green function with only external fermion lines is a Grassmann source derivative of this same normalized generating functional, so
Gauxiliary(n)​=Gfour-fermion(n)​.​
(30)
This is an exact Hubbard–Stratonovich transformation in reverse, rather than just an approximation based on tree-level Feynman diagrams. At the diagrammatic level the contact auxiliary-field propagator −iδ(4)(x−y) collapses each exchanged line into the same local four-fermion interaction. The equality uses a common regulator and does not assert perturbative renormalizability of that four-dimensional four-fermion interaction.
