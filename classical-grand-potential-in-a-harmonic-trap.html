Classical grand potential in a harmonic trap (Φ=−kB​Teβμ[1−e−βℏω]−3)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Ideal gas

For noninteracting particles in a three-dimensional isotropic quantum harmonic oscillator, measure the one-particle ground energy as zero. The single-particle partition function is Z1​=(1−e−βℏω)−3, from the product of three geometric series. In the dilute Maxwell–Boltzmann statistics regime, lnΞ=eβμZ1​, so the grand potential is the displayed expression and N=eβμZ1​. For kB​T≫ℏω,
Φ≃−(ℏω)3(kB​T)4​eβμ,μ≃kB​Tln[N(kB​Tℏω​)3].
(1)
The same approximation follows from integrating the density of states g(E)≃E2/[2(ℏω)3] against e−βE. Extrapolating the classical formula until the fugacity is one gives the scale of Bose condensation in a three-dimensional harmonic trap, but the quantitative transition temperature requires the Bose occupation law rather than Maxwell-Boltzmann statistics.
