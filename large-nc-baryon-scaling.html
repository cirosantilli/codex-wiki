Large-Nc baryon scaling (MB​,Λ,Fπ2​,e−2=O(Nc​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
In the large-color limit of QCD with gs2​Nc​ fixed, baryon masses scale as Nc​ while meson masses remain of order one. A Skyrme model action with coefficients of order Nc​ has soliton mass and rotational moment of inertia of order Nc​, and rotor splittings of order 1/Nc​. This motivates a semiclassical mesonic description of baryons; it does not uniquely determine every term in a finite-color effective theory.
