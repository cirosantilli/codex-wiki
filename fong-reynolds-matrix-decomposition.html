Fong–Reynolds matrix decomposition

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Modular representation theory Block of a group algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
Let N◃G, let b be a block of a group algebra of kN, and let I be its inertia subgroup. A block of kG covering b is a full matrix algebra of degree [G:I] over its corresponding block of kI. When I=N, the corner is just kNb. Let eB​ be the global block idempotent and put c=eB​b. With coset representatives gi​, the elements gi​cgj−1​ are matrix units in that global block because different conjugates of b are orthogonal. When I=N, one has c=b.
