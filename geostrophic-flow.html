Geostrophic flow

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Quasi-geostrophic approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 91 Articles: 1
A geostrophic flow is a horizontal flow in leading geostrophic balance. Its velocity follows pressure contours rather than pointing directly down the pressure gradient.
