Microswimmer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 37
A microswimmer is a microscopic body that propels itself through a fluid. At small Reynolds number its motion is governed by Stokes flow, and self-propulsion must respect the force-free and torque-free conditions.
