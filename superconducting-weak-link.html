Superconducting weak link

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Superconductivity Josephson junction
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
A superconducting weak link is a connection between regions with superconductivity whose coupling is weak enough that a substantial difference of quantum phase can develop across the connection. An insulating tunnel barrier is one realization; a narrow conducting constriction can also couple the regions. The current-phase relation of a superconducting weak link depends on the connection. A sinusoid is the leading tunnel-junction approximation, not a universal law for every superconducting weak link. [ref]
