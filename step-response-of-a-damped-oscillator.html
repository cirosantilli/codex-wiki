Step response of a damped oscillator (H(t)K(t),K(t)=∫0t​g(s)ds)

Integrating the smooth positive-time impulse kernel gives the zero-data response to the Heaviside step function. For nonzero ω,
K(t)=k2+ω21−e−kt[cosωt+(k/ω)sinωt]​.
(78)
At zero frequency and nonzero k the limit is [1−e−kt(1+kt)]/k2; when both parameters vanish it is t2/2. The integral representation avoids ambiguous divisions in these limiting cases.
