Fixed-target production threshold (E1​≥2m2​(∑i​μi​)2−m12​−m22​​c2)

For an incident particle of rest mass m1​ and total energy E1​ hitting a stationary target of mass m2​>0, four-momentum conservation gives W2=m12​c4+m22​c4+2m2​c2E1​. Applying the relativistic collision mass threshold gives the displayed necessary condition. The incoming energy must also satisfy E1​≥m1​c2; other conserved quantum numbers or reaction dynamics can impose further restrictions.
