A supersymmetry transformation is the infinitesimal action generated by an odd supercharge, with constant anticommuting spinor parameter in a global theory. In superspace it acts as δS=(ϵQ+ϵˉQˉ​)S, with possible conventional overall factors of i. On the fermion of a chiral superfield, the transformation includes δψ=2​ϵFaux​+i2​σμϵˉ∂μ​ϕ. A nonzero vacuum auxiliary field therefore gives an inhomogeneous fermion shift, identifying the Goldstino direction when global supersymmetry is spontaneously broken.
