For a nondegenerate parity-symmetric ground state in an external electric field, write the perturbation as V=−Ed^. The first-order state correction gives

The positive coefficient α is the electric polarizability along the applied direction. Meanwhile second-order nondegenerate perturbation theory gives δE0(2)​=−αE2/2. Thus the induced electric dipole moment satisfies d=−2δE0(2)​/E, not −δE0(2)​/E. The Hellmann–Feynman theorem independently gives d=−∂E0​/∂E.
