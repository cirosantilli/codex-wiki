Dynamic modulus (G∗(ω)=G′(ω)+iG′′(ω))

The dynamic modulus is the complex stress-amplitude to strain-amplitude ratio in steady oscillatory response. With the convention eiωt, the real part is the storage modulus and the imaginary part is the loss modulus. For ϵ=ϵ0​cosωt, σ=ϵ0​(G′cosωt−G′′sinωt). The average supplied power is ωϵ02​G′′/2, fixing the loss-sign convention. The elastic coefficient is a shear or extension modulus according to the deformation measured; it is unrelated to the complex modulus meaning absolute value of a complex number.
