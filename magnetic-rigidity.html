Magnetic rigidity (Br=p⊥​/∣q∣)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force
OurBigBook.com Words: 931 Articles: 3
Magnetic rigidity measures the resistance of a charged particle's momentum to bending by a magnetic field. The relativistic cyclotron orbit gives p⊥​=∣q∣Br, where r is the transverse gyroradius. For motion perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field, p⊥​=p is the whole momentum. A measured magnetic rigidity fixes momentum per magnitude of electric charge; it does not by itself fix mass, kinetic energy, or the sign of the electric charge. This distinction makes the Anderson positron track experiment an especially revealing application of the Lorentz force.
