Nobel Lecture in Physiology or Medicine by Gerald M. Edelman, delivered 12 December 1972, as stated on printed p. 31. This is the author’s retrospective account of experiments. The lecture date is distinct from an online or reprint publication date, neither of which is inferred.
Inspected University of Washington mirror: krishna.gs.washington.edu/content/members/cholilee/sequencing_files/alumni_akash/vol7/fun/1972_Edelman_structureofantibodies.pdf
The university hosts the mirror; it is not the author or lecture issuer. The original Nobel-hosted PDF returned 403, so the authored lecture text was inspected at the mirror.
Cited passages: pp. 33–34: reduction in dissociating solvents, including 6 M urea, ultracentrifugal analysis, Figure 2a’s four comparison lanes and Figure 2b’s two-dimensional peptide electrophoresis; pp. 35–37: heavy-chain classes, Table 1 and Figure 3’s chain and disulfide arrangement; p. 37: complement binding by Fc fragments. Figure legends and surrounding text were inspected; no source band positions or intensities are copied or inferred.
