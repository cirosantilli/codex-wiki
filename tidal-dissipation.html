Tidal dissipation

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planetary system dynamics Tidal heating
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
Tidal dissipation converts mechanical energy associated with a time-varying tidal deformation into heat. It can reduce orbital eccentricity, drive synchronous rotation, and contribute deep planetary heating. A circular orbit with exact synchronous rotation, perpendicular spin axis and no additional forcing has a static tidal deformation in the body's frame and need not sustain substantial tidal heating.
