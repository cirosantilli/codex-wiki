Finite-interval exit times have geometric tails

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Birth-death chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For a nearest-neighbour Markov chain on {L,…,U} with positive right-step probabilities in its interior, let m=U−L and p∗​>0 be their minimum. From every interior state, at most m consecutive right steps reach U, with probability at least p∗m​. The Markov property then gives P(T>km)≤(1−p∗m​)k for the first boundary hitting time. Thus T is almost surely finite and has finite expectation, regardless of the holding probability of a Markov chain at each interior state.
