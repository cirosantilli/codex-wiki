Cox process (N∣Λ∼Poisson process(Λ))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Poisson process
OurBigBook.com Words: 174 Articles: 2
A counting process which, conditional on a random nonnegative locally integrable intensity Λ, is an Inhomogeneous Poisson process. For Lt​=∫0t​Λ(s)ds, conditioning gives EN(t)=ELt​ and VarN(t)=ELt​+VarLt​. Thus finite-variance counts are never underdispersed. Randomness of the intensity may induce dependence between increments which are independent conditionally.
