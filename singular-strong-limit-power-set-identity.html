Singular strong limit power-set identity (2λ=λcf(λ))

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cardinal number Large cardinal Strong limit cardinal
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For a singular strong limit cardinal λ, let μ=cf(λ) and choose cofinal cardinals λi​<λ. Subset restrictions inject P(λ) into ∏i<μ​P(λi​), whose cardinal is at most λμ by the strong-limit property. Conversely λμ≤(2λ)μ=2λ. This equality alone does not imply the singular cardinals hypothesis.
