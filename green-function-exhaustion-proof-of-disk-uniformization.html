Green-function exhaustion proof of disk uniformization

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Uniformization theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 110
Exhaust a simply connected hyperbolic Riemann surface in potential theory by compact bordered Jordan domains Rn​. Their Green functions on a Riemann surface Gn​(⋅,p) have logarithmic poles and zero boundary values. Exponentiating their harmonic conjugates produces proper degree-one maps fn​:Rn​→D with fn​(p)=0. If Gn​=−log∣ζ∣+cn​+o(1), normalize fn′​(p)=e−cn​>0. Domain monotonicity and a global positive Green function bound cn​ increasingly from above. Thus Montel theorem gives a nonconstant limit F, which is injective by the locally uniform limit of univalent functions theorem. The Schwarz lemma makes F′(p) extremal among normalized disk-valued maps. If F omitted a=0, taking a global holomorphic square root of a disk automorphism applied to F, then renormalizing at p, would multiply its derivative by (1+∣a∣)/(2∣a∣​)>1. Thus F is onto and gives the desired biholomorphism.
