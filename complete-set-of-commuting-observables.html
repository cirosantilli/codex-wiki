Complete set of commuting observables

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state Observable
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
A complete set of commuting observables consists of mutually commuting quantum observables whose joint eigenvalues distinguish all vectors of a simultaneous eigenbasis, up to phase. For a finite-dimensional Hilbert space, commuting self-adjoint operators admit a simultaneous orthonormal basis of eigenvectors; completeness means that every joint eigenspace is one-dimensional. An additional quantum observable that is already a function of the others supplies no independent quantum number.
For the addition of angular momentum, the uncoupled labels (j1​,j2​,m1​,m2​) correspond to {J12​,J22​,J1z​,J2z​}, whereas the coupled labels (j1​,j2​,j,m) correspond to {J12​,J22​,J2,Jz​}. Within a fixed pair of irreducible angular momentum spaces each choice gives a natural eigenbasis. The Clebsch-Gordan coefficients implement the change between them.
