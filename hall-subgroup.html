Hall subgroup

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Subgroup
OurBigBook.com Words: 250 Articles: 3
A Hall subgroup has order coprime to its index. For a set of primes π, a Hall π-subgroup has order using only primes of π and index using only primes outside π. Thus its order contains the full prime-power contributions belonging to π. A Sylow subgroup is the single-prime instance. Hall subgroup existence in soluble groups generalizes Sylow existence to every prime set.
