Seifert surface

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Knot
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 30
A Seifert surface for an oriented knot K is a compact connected oriented surface F⊂S3 with oriented boundary ∂F=K.
