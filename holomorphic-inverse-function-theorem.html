Holomorphic inverse function theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Conformal map
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
If a holomorphic function f satisfies f′(z0​)=0, then it has a holomorphic local inverse near z0​ and is conformal there. Its possible failures of local conformality are therefore its critical points.
