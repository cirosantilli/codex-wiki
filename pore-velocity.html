Pore velocity (v=q/ϕ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Darcy law Darcy velocity
OurBigBook.com Words: 129
Pore velocity is the fluid velocity averaged over pore volume relative to the solid framework. For uniform porosity ϕ, full saturation and all pores participating, v=q/ϕ, with Darcy velocity q defined per bulk area. The identity follows from volume averaging: extending the relative fluid velocity by zero in the solid gives a bulk average equal to the pore fraction times the pore-volume average. It does not assert that every local passage has open area ϕA or identical speed.
In a uniform advective model, travel over length L takes L/v=ϕL/q. If some pore volume is inaccessible to transport, the corresponding estimate uses effective porosity ϕe​ and v=q/ϕe​. A connected but nearly stagnant passage can exchange solute slowly, so even this mean does not describe all arrival times. Flux-weighted residence time in a porous layer explains one precise averaging convention.[ref]
