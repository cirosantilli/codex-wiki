Electron electromagnetic vertex form factors (F1​(q2),F2​(q2))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Quantum electrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 282 Articles: 2
For external electrons on shell, factor out signed electric charge in the parity-preserving interaction vertex:
uˉ(p′)Γμu(p)=uˉ(p′)[γμF1​(q2)+2miσμνqν​​F2​(q2)]u(p),q=p′−p,p2=p′2=m2,σμν=2i​[γμ,γν].
(32)
The u are Dirac spinors; γμ are gamma matrices. With on-shell renormalization scheme normalization F1​(0)=1, the Gordon identity gives gs​/2=F1​(0)+F2​(0) and ae​=F2​(0).[ref]
This explains a subtlety: the Dirac equation already predicts a spin magnetic dipole moment with F2​=0. Rewriting its Dirac electromagnetic current with the Gordon identity produces a spin-dependent term even before any loop order correction. F2​ measures the additional response after that baseline is included. The limit q→0 is taken after extracting its coefficient; setting q=0 in the interaction vertex at the outset would hide it. This electromagnetic anomalous magnetic moment is not a failure of gauge invariance of the kind called a quantum anomaly.
