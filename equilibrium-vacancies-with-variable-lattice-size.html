Equilibrium vacancies with variable lattice size (n∗​≃N/[e(ϵ+pv)/(kB​T)−1])

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Crystal structure Crystallographic defect Vacancy defect
OurBigBook.com Words: 166 Articles: 1
Consider fixed N atoms and variable n vacancies among N+n sites, each of volume v, with vacancy energy ϵ. The configuration count is (N+n)!/(N!n!). At fixed pressure and temperature, leading Stirling's approximation gives the excess Gibbs free energy n(ϵ+pv)−kB​T[(N+n)ln(N+n)−NlnN−nlnn]. Its stationary point is the displayed n∗​, and its second derivative is kB​TN/[n(N+n)]>0. Relative to the vacancy-free Gibbs free energy, the minimum is NkB​Tln(1−e−(ϵ+pv)/(kB​T)). The resulting mean-field volume is v(N+n∗​). Normalizability requires ϵ+pv>0, and large vacancy fractions lie outside a realistic dilute-defect crystal model.
