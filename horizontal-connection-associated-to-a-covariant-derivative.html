Horizontal connection associated to a covariant derivative (H(x,v)​={(ξ,−Γx​(ξ)v)})

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle) Horizontal subspace of a vector bundle connection
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
In a frame of a vector bundle, write a vector bundle covariant derivative as ∇(ev)=e(dv+Γv). The associated horizontal subspace at (x,v) consists of tangent pairs (ξ,−Γx​(ξ)v). Under a frame change e′=eg, the connection one-form changes by Γ′=g−1Γg+g−1dg, and this horizontal equation changes to the corresponding one in the new frame. Thus the subspaces glue to a unique smooth linear connection on a vector bundle. Its vertical projection gives back the original derivative. The Leibniz rule ensures locality, allowing this construction from an operator initially defined on global sections.
