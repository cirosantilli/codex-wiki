Right singular vector (vj​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Singular value decomposition Singular value
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A unit right singular vector of a matrix or compact operator A is an eigenvector of A∗A with eigenvalue σj2​. For σj​>0, uj​=Avj​/σj​ is the corresponding left singular vector. The largest singular value is the operator norm of A, attained on its corresponding right singular vectors.
