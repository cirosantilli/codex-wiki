Comparator composition (L=LH​∨(EH​∧LL​),G=GH​∨(EH​∧GL​))

To combine more and less significant digital comparators, compute EH​=¬(LH​∨GH​) and use the displayed formulas. Unequal upper blocks decide the order, because their difference outweighs every possible lower-block difference; equal upper blocks delegate the decision to the lower block. This is an associative operation on the three comparison outcomes. A balanced binary tree of these combinations has O(n) circuit size and O(logn) circuit depth for n-bit inputs.
