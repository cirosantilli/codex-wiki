Stationary law of a two-resource linear flow network (π(a,b,c))

For route offered loads ρ1​,ρ2​,ρ0​, the balance function of a flow-level network Ψ(a,b,c)=(ca+b+c​) gives the stationary distribution
π(a,b,c)=B(ca+b+c​)ρ1a​ρ2b​ρ0c​.
(14)
The negative binomial series sums over c, and two geometric sums give B=(1−ρ0​−ρ1​)(1−ρ0​−ρ2​)/(1−ρ0​) under the stability conditions for a two-resource linear flow network.
