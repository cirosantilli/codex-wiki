Sagnac interferometer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Sagnac effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 325 Articles: 1
A Sagnac interferometer uses a beam splitter to send light with optical coherence around a common closed optical path in opposite directions, then recombines the beams to read their phase difference. Mirrors or a guiding path define the loop; the source, beam splitter, loop and readout rotate together in the basic experiment. A small relative beam tilt makes spatial interference fringes whose displacement reveals a change of phase difference. At one fixed output, a photodetector can instead record the corresponding change in light intensity.
