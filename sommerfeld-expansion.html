Sommerfeld expansion (∫0∞​f(E)pF​(E)dE=∫0μ​f(E)dE+6π2​(kB​T)2f′(μ)+⋯)

The Sommerfeld expansion approximates integrals weighted by the Fermi-Dirac distribution when kB​T≪μ and f is smooth near μ. Applying it to the number integral at fixed particle number gives μ−EF​=−(π2/6)(kB​T)2g′(EF​)/g(EF​)+⋯. The sign depends on the slope of the density of states. For an unbounded parabolic band, g(E)∝Ed/2−1, yielding opposite quadratic shifts in one and three dimensions. In two dimensions the leading algebraic shift vanishes, but the finite band-bottom cutoff still gives an exponentially small negative shift.
