Zimm-Bragg model (fh​=21​+(s−1)/(2(s−1)2+4sσ​))

A homogeneous Zimm-Bragg chain assigns propagation weight s>0 to each helical unit and initiation factor σ>0 to each helical run. With states ordered as coil and helix, one possible transfer matrix for a classical spin chain is
W=(11​σss​).
(60)
Its largest eigenvalue is λ+​=(1+s+(s−1)2+4sσ​)/2. In the long-chain limit the partition function grows as λ+N​, so logarithmic differentiation gives the helical fraction fh​=s∂lnλ+​/∂s, equal to the displayed expression. If Q=(s−1)2+4sσ, then
dsdfh​​=Q3/2σ(s+1)​>0,fh​(1)=21​,fh′​(1)=4σ​1​.
(61)
For σ=1, fh​=s/(1+s) and units are noncooperative. A small initiation factor suppresses boundaries and sharpens the crossover around s=1, with width of order σ​. For every fixed σ>0 the long-chain expression is smooth; a discontinuous step appears only in the singular σ↓0 limit. [ref]
