Shallow-water potential vorticity (q=(f+ζ)/h)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Potential vorticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 790 Articles: 13
Shallow-water potential vorticity is materially conserved by inviscid, unforced shallow water equations. Linearizing about rest with depth H0​ gives an anomaly proportional to
ζ−H0​fη​.
