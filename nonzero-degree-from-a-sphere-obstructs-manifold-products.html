Nonzero degree from a sphere obstructs manifold products (degf=0⟹M≅Xk×Ym−k)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Degree of a continuous mapping
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
Suppose a map Sm→M of closed connected oriented manifolds has nonzero mapping degree. A product decomposition with 0<k<m would have oriented closed factors: an orientation-reversing loop in either factor, with the other coordinate fixed, would reverse the product orientation. Pull back their top orientation classes to obtain α∈Hk(M;Z) and β∈Hm−k(M;Z) with α⌣β a generator of Hm(M;Z). Both pullbacks to the sphere vanish in their intermediate degrees, contradicting the nonzero-degree pullback of their product.
