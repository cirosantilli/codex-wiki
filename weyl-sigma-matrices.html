Weyl sigma matrices (σαβ˙​μ​)

For mostly-minus metric signature, the Weyl sigma matrices are σμ=(I,σ1​,σ2​,σ3​) and σˉμ=(I,−σ1​,−σ2​,−σ3​), where σi​ are the Pauli matrices. The Pauli matrix multiplication law proves
σμσˉν+σνσˉμ=2ημνI.
(6)
A real Lorentz four-vector is equivalently a Hermitian matrix X=x0I+xiσi​ with detX=(x0)2−∣x∣2. The transformation X↦MXM† for M∈SL(2,C) preserves this determinant and gives the Lorentz spinor double cover. The indices σαβ˙​μ​ therefore convert a vector index into one undotted and one dotted Weyl spinor index. They are the invariant tensors in the mixed supercharge anticommutator {Qα​,Qˉ​β˙​​}=2σαβ˙​μ​Pμ​.
