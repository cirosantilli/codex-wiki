Knapsack problem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Integer programming
OurBigBook.com Words: 220 Articles: 5
The knapsack problem selects items of prescribed weights and profits under a total-weight capacity. The 0-1 knapsack problem permits each item at most once; bounded and unbounded multiplicity variants use different integer restrictions. Nonnegative weights and capacity are assumed here.
