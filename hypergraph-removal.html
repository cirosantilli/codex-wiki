Hypergraph removal

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Hypergraph Uniform hypergraph
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 1
For each fixed finite uniform hypergraph F, having few copies of F forces small edge-edit distance from an F-free hypergraph. More precisely, for every η>0 there is ρ>0 such that fewer than ρv∣V(F)∣ copies can be eliminated by deleting fewer than ηvr edges in an r-uniform hypergraph. Strong regularity for three-uniform hypergraphs and a relative counting lemma prove the case r=3.
