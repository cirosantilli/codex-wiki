Its orthonormal eigenbasis consists of u1​=(1,1)T/2​ and u2​=(1,−1)T/2​, with eigenvalues 3 and 1. Since x0​=(u1​+u2​)/2​, direct multiplication gives
Akx0​=21​(3k+13k−1​),xk​=1+3−2k​u1​+3−ku2​​.

The first unnormalized vectors are (1,0)T, (2,1)T, (5,4)T, and (14,13)T. If θk​ is the acute angle from the line spanned by the dominant eigenvector, then tanθk​=3−k. Every application divides the unwanted-to-wanted component ratio by three: normalization changes lengths, while the spectral gap selects the direction.
The right panel compares the power iteration direction error, Rayleigh quotient error, and eigenpair residual. With tk​=3−k, their exact values are
εk​=1+tk2​​tk​​,3−ρk​=1+tk2​2tk2​​,∥rk​∥2​=1+tk2​2tk​​.
(265)
Thus a very accurate Rayleigh quotient can appear well before the eigenvector is comparably accurate. This is an example of quadratic Rayleigh-quotient improvement for the power method.
