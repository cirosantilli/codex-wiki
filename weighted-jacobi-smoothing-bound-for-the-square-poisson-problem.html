Weighted Jacobi smoothing bound for the square Poisson problem (ω=4/5 ⟹ ∣μr,s​∣≤3/5 on high modes)

OurBigBook.com Words: 89
For the five-point Dirichlet Poisson matrix, weighted Jacobi iteration has sine-mode multiplier 1−ω+(ω/2)(cosθr​+cosθs​). Call a mode high-frequency if at least one cosine is nonpositive. Then the sum lies in [−2,1], so for ω=4/5 the multiplier lies in [−3/5,3/5]. This uniform damping makes the iteration a useful smoother for a multigrid method, even though low-frequency multipliers approach one on fine grids. Balancing ∣1−2ω∣ and ∣1−ω/2∣ shows why 4/5 is the minimax parameter for this high-frequency interval. The two-dimensional high-frequency set gives a different optimum from the familiar one-dimensional value 2/3.
