Hadronization time (thad​∼ℏ/ΛQCD​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Hadronization
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A characteristic proper timescale for strong bound-state formation, estimated by dimensional analysis from the QCD scale. A scale of a few tenths of a GeV corresponds to a few 10−24s. It is an order-of-magnitude guide rather than a universal sharply defined formation time.
