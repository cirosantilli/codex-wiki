Spectral luminous efficacy (K(λ)=Km​V(λ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Photometry (optics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
Spectral luminous efficacy converts radiant flux at one wavelength to luminous flux. The candela convention fixes Kcd​ exactly at 540×1012Hz, not at an exactly specified wavelength of 555nm. [ref]
In vacuum, that frequency corresponds to c/(540×1012Hz)≃555.1712nm, using the speed of light. In standard air it is approximately 555.017nm, close to the 555nm maximum of the photopic luminous efficiency function. Thus Km​ is slightly larger than 683lmW−1; using Km​≃683lmW−1 in ordinary calculations is a rounded convention. The exact anchor is K(λa​)=683lmW−1. [ref]
