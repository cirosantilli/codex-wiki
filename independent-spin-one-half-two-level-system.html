Independent spin-one-half two-level system (E↑​=+ϵ, E↓​=−ϵ)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Statistical physics Canonical ensemble
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For N distinguishable noninteracting spins, let a fraction α occupy the level +ϵ and the remainder occupy −ϵ. The Stirling formula gives
S=−NkB​[αlogα+(1−α)log(1−α)],E=Nϵ(2α−1).
(67)
The relation 1/T=∂S/∂E yields
α=1+e2ϵ/(kB​T)1​,E=−Nϵtanh(kB​Tϵ​).
(68)
Positive temperature corresponds to 0≤α<1/2; a population inversion 1/2<α≤1 has negative temperature.
