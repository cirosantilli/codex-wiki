Fresnel diffraction

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 20
Fresnel diffraction describes diffraction when the quadratic propagation wave phase across an aperture must be retained. Its Fresnel propagator becomes the Fraunhofer diffraction description when that curvature is negligible.
A Fresnel diffraction pattern is a map of how propagation changes the wave interference of light from an aperture. It can resemble the opening, develop bright and dark structure near its edges, and eventually approach a Fraunhofer diffraction pattern. The controlling comparison involves wavelength, opening size and propagation distance together; a distance large compared with the opening need not give the far field.
For a monochromatic wave, use time dependence e−iωt, wavenumber k=2π/λ and complex amplitude U. Let U0​(ξ,η) be the transmitted field immediately behind an aperture in the plane z=0. In a homogeneous medium, the scalar paraxial approximation gives
U(x,y,z)≃iλzeikz​∬R2​U0​(ξ,η)exp[λziπ​((x−ξ)2+(y−η)2)]dξdη,z>0.
(1)
This is the two-transverse-coordinate Fresnel propagator, with its carrier eikz restored. It adds complex amplitudes before taking their squared complex modulus to obtain light intensity. It is a scalar paraxial approximation, rather than a full vector solution of Maxwell equations.[ref]
The source in this constructed geometry supplies a plane wave; the observation screen is a plane perpendicular to the optical axis. The right panel isolates the aperture-dependent wave phase that distinguishes Fresnel diffraction from Fraunhofer diffraction. Its vertical scale is in radians, and the drawing's lengths are schematic.
Keeping this wave phase is useful when transporting a field between nearby optical components: both the shape and the complex amplitude matter for subsequent wave interference. A particularly striking feature is that propagation can create a dark centre even from a uniformly illuminated symmetric aperture; the incident wave amplitudes need not be unequal for their outgoing contributions to cancel.
