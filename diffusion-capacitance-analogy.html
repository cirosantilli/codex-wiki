Diffusion-capacitance analogy

OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A quasi-static chemical potential outside a fixed-shape droplet solves the same Laplace equation as an electrostatic potential outside an equipotential conductor. If μ=μs​ on the droplet and zero on the surrounding reservoirs, its outward diffusive current is
I=−M∫∂D​∂n​μdS=ϵM​Cμs​,
(20)
where C is the corresponding electrostatic capacitance in a medium of permittivity ϵ. The arbitrary ϵ cancels against the capacitance's proportionality to ϵ. Thus electrostatic geometry directly determines evaporation rates.
