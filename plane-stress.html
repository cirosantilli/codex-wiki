Plane stress (τzz​=τxz​=τyz​=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A stress tensor state with all traction components involving one normal direction zero. In an isotropic solid, its two remaining normal stresses obey τx​=E(ex​+νey​)/(1−ν2) and τy​=E(ey​+νex​)/(1−ν2). The out-of-plane strain need not vanish: ez​=−ν(τx​+τy​)/E. Constraining the out-of-plane deformation instead would be a different boundary condition.
