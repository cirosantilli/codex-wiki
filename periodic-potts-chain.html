Periodic Potts chain (E=−J∑n=1L​δσn​,σn+1​​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Spin model Potts model
OurBigBook.com Words: 147 Articles: 2
The ferromagnetic Potts model on a labelled ring has Q possible site states, J>0 and σL+1​=σ1​. Its spin-chain transfer matrix is T=(eβJ−1)I+P, with P the Q-dimensional all-ones matrix. The canonical partition function is (eβJ+Q−1)L+(Q−1)(eβJ−1)L. The convention counts two bonds when L=2.
