Spherical-geodesic first integral (h=sin2θdϕ/ds)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Surface of revolution Clairaut first integral for a surface of revolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 2
For a sphere with metric a2(dθ2+sin2θdϕ2), physical arc length s gives h=sin2θdϕ/ds. The cyclic coordinate ϕ proves conservation of a2h, while unit-speed normalization gives
(dϕdθ​)2=a2h2sin4θ​−sin2θ.
(91)
This azimuthal form requires h=0. The solutions are great circles; meridians have h=0 and require another parameter.
