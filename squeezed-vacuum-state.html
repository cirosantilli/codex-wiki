The squeezed vacuum is the zero-displacement squeezed coherent state ∣γ⟩=S(γ)∣0⟩. Its position and momentum variances are rescaled in opposite directions,
(ΔX)2=2mωℏ​e−2γ,(ΔP)2=2mℏω​e2γ,

so it remains an minimum-uncertainty state with ΔXΔP=ℏ/2.
