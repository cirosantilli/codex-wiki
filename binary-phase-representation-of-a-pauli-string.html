Binary phase representation of a Pauli string (P=is⨂j​Xaj​Zbj​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum circuit Pauli group
OurBigBook.com Words: 197 Articles: 3
Store a Pauli group element with binary exponents aj​,bj​ and a phase exponent s modulo four. This ordered XZ convention uses 2n+2 bits for n qubits. Since XZ=−iY, phase factors are essential when converting to Hermitian Pauli operators and evaluating measurement probabilities.
