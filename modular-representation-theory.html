Modular representation theory

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 89
Modular representation theory studies representations of a finite group G over a field whose characteristic divides ∣G∣. The group algebra is then generally nonsemisimple, and projective modules, radicals, blocks and vertices replace complete reducibility as central tools.
