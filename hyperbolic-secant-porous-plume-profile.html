Hyperbolic-secant porous plume profile (w=Wsech2(x/b))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Darcy law Porous thermal plume
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
The symmetric steady porous thermal plume has w=Wsech2(x/b), T−T0​=w/β and ψ=−Wbtanh(x/b). Its exact normalizations are Wb2=6κz and (4/3)W2b=Q, so b=(48κ2z2/Q)1/3 and W=(3Q2/(32κz))1/3. The streamfunction supplies lateral entrainment required by continuity.
