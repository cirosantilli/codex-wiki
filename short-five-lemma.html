Short five lemma

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Short exact sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The Five lemma has a short form: in a commutative diagram of short exact sequences, if the maps on both outer terms are isomorphisms, then the map on the middle terms is an isomorphism.
