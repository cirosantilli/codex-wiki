Virtual valuation (ϕ(v)=v−f(v)1−F(v)​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Mechanism design Single-parameter mechanism
OurBigBook.com Words: 201 Articles: 4
For an absolutely continuous valuation distribution with density f(v)>0, its virtual valuation is v−(1−F(v))/f(v). The virtual-surplus revenue identity converts expected incentive-compatible payments into expected allocations weighted by virtual valuations. A regular prior makes this quantity nondecreasing.
