Affine module sheaf (M(D(f))=Mf​)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Quasi-coherent sheaf
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 1
For an affine variety with coordinate ring A and an A-module M, localization defines sections on principal open subsets by the displayed formula. Restrictions are the natural localization maps. Equivalently, a section is a family of stalk values which locally come from one fraction m/fr. These data form a quasi-coherent sheaf, with global sections M and stalk MmP​​ at P. Sheaf tensor products correspond to tensor products over A; pullback of a sheaf of modules corresponds to extension of scalars, and affine direct image to restriction of scalars.
