Chern-Simons form

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Chern-Weil homomorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 160 Articles: 3
A Chern-Simons form is an odd-degree transgression form whose exterior derivative is an invariant polynomial in the curvature form. For the trace polynomial of degree k, dω2k−1​=Tr(Fk); the Chern-Simons 3-form is the degree-three case.
