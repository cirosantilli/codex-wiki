Thermodynamic system

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 899 Articles: 11
A thermodynamic system is the part of the physical world selected for an energy and matter balance. Draw its boundary before classifying an interaction as heat, work or transported matter. The boundary may move: a sealed piston encloses a closed system even while its volume changes. An isolated system additionally excludes all external energy transfers.[ref]
Boundary choice changes the ledger. If an electrical heater warms water, choosing only the water counts incoming heat; including the heater counts electrical work entering through its leads. The combined internal energy records storage, without separate stored categories for the two transfer mechanisms.[ref]
