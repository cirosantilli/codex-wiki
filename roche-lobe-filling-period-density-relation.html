Roche-lobe-filling period-density relation (P∝(R23​/M2​)1/2)

If R2​=RL​≃Ca(M2​/M)1/3, Kepler's third law gives
P=GC3​2π​(M2​R23​​)1/2.
(12)
Thus a Roche lobe-filling star's period measures its inverse square-root mean mass density, largely independently of the companion mass in this approximation. A local mass-radius relation R2​∝M2ζ​ gives P∝M2(3ζ−1)/2​.
