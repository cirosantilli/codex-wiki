Sampling order-three Cardinal B-splines Nj​ at xi​=i+3/2 gives Ni​(xi​)=3/4, Ni−1​(xi​)=Ni+1​(xi​)=1/8, and all other entries zero. The B-spline collocation matrix therefore has row strict diagonal dominance margin at least 1/2, giving inverse operator norm at most two. More precisely, with q=3−22​, its absolute inverse row sums are vi​=2[1−(qi+qn+1−i)/(1+qn+1)]. To see this, change signs by Dii​=(−1)i: DAD has negative off-diagonal entries and a nonnegative inverse given by a convergent Neumann series. Its inverse row sums solve 6vi​−vi−1​−vi+1​=8, v0​=vn+1​=0. Thus ∥A−1∥∞​=v⌊(n+1)/2⌋​<2, tending to two as the dimension grows.
