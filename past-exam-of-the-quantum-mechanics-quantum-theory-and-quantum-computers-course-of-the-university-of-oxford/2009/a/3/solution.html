With the specified ladder-operator normalization, A+†​=A−​ and
A+​A−​=P2+m2ω2X2−mℏω=2mH−mℏω.
(12)
Therefore ∥A−​ψ∥2=2m(E−ℏω/2)∥ψ∥2≥0. This proves E≥ℏω/2​, with equality exactly when A−​ψ=0. In position representation this equation is ℏψ′+mωxψ=0, whose general solution is
ψ(x)=Cexp[−mωx2/(2ℏ)].​
(13)
For a unit vector ∣C∣=(mω/πℏ)1/4; its phase is arbitrary. The commutators [H,A±​]=±ℏωA±​ make A+​ raise and A−​ lower energies by ℏω. Repeated raising of the Gaussian produces all levels (n+1/2)ℏω. Conversely lowering any nonzero energy eigenvector must eventually terminate, since otherwise it would produce an energy below the lower bound. A terminating vector is annihilated by A−​ and hence has energy ℏω/2. Thus no other eigenvalues occur.
Bilinearity of the two-dimensional commutators gives
[Pθ​,Xφ​]=−iℏ(cosθcosφ+sinθsinφ)1=−iℏcos(θ−φ)1,
(14)
while all momentum-momentum and position-position commutators remain zero. Equal angles give a canonical position-momentum pair. Perpendicular angles give commuting components, which belong to independent coordinate directions.
The orthogonal coordinates X+​=(X1​+X2​)/2​, X−​=(X1​−X2​)/2​ preserve the kinetic sum, and
5(X12​+X22​)−8X1​X2​=X+2​+9X−2​.
(15)
Hence ω+​=ω,ω−​=3ω​ and H=H+​+H−​. Every cross-mode position/momentum pair commutes, so [H+​,H−​]=0. On the tensor-product oscillator basis,
E=ℏω(n+​+21​)+3ℏω(n−​+21​)=(n+​+3n−​+2)ℏω.
(16)
For each N≥0, allowed pairs have n−​=0,1,…,⌊N/3⌋ and n+​=N−3n−​. Thus
EN​=(N+2)ℏω,gN​=⌊N/3⌋+1.​
(17)
Each pair contributes one product eigenvector; counting them accounts for the energy-level degeneracy.
