Use periodic plane waves on the ring. For n∈Z,
ϕn​(x)=2πa​einx/a​,Hϕn​=2ma2ℏ2n2​ϕn​.
(1)
They are orthonormal on one circumference and complete by Fourier series. Thus Ek​=ℏ2k2/(2ma2)​, with degeneracy 1 for k=0,2 for k≥1​: the two signs of nonzero angular momentum have the same energy. Equivalently one may use normalized sine and cosine eigenfunctions for each positive k.
For the position measurement put θ=x/a. Direct integration gives
P(21​πa≤x≤23​πa)=π1​∫π/23π/2​sin2(θ/2)dθ=21​+π1​.​

The energy measurement requires projection onto whole degenerate eigenspaces, rather than just onto one traveling wave. The expansion coefficients are
cn​=⟨ϕn​,ψ⟩=π2​1​∫02π​sin(θ/2)e−inθdθ=π(1−4n2)22​​.
(3)
For instance the imaginary integral vanishes by reflection about θ=π, and the product-to-sum identity evaluates the real integral as 4/(1−4n2). Consequently
p0​=π28​,pk​=∣ck​∣2+∣c−k​∣2=π2(4k2−1)216​(k≥1).​
(4)
Every numerator is positive and no integer k makes a denominator zero, so every energy has nonzero probability. Parseval's identity guarantees that these probabilities sum to one. The supplied state is a valid normalized periodic L2 state; its derivative need not obey the operator-domain periodic condition for the spectral probabilities to be defined.
