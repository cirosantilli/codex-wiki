Hasse-Weil L-function (L(E/F,s)=∏v​det(1−Frobv​Nv−s∣Vℓ​(E)Iv​)−1)

OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For an elliptic curve over a number field, its Hasse-Weil L-function is the product of the local factors determined by its Tate module. At a good finite place, the factor is (1−av​Nv−s+Nv1−2s)−1, with av​=Nv+1−#E(κ(v)). At a bad place use the inertia-invariant subspace and an auxiliary prime different from the residue characteristic. For a CM elliptic curve with all endomorphisms defined over the base, this factors as the product of the two conjugate Hecke L-functions.
