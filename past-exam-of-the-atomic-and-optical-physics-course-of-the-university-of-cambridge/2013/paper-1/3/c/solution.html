For a nucleus of spin I>0 and electronic J=1/2, the magnetic-dipole hyperfine interaction is AI⋅J, using dimensionless angular-momentum operators. The electric-quadrupole hyperfine contribution vanishes for J=1/2. In zero field, F=I±1/2 labels the levels:
EF​=2A​[F(F+1)−I(I+1)−3/4],EI+1/2​=AI/2,EI−1/2​=−A(I+1)/2.​
(20)
Their signed separation is Δ=A(I+1/2). For A>0, the larger-F multiplet lies higher; negative A reverses this order. If I=0 there is no such hyperfine splitting.
Let the field define z and write
H=AI⋅J+μB​B(gJ​Jz​+gI​Iz​).
(21)
Here gI​ is the signed nuclear Zeeman coefficient expressed in Bohr-magneton units, including its nuclear-to-Bohr-magneton factor; it is not an unconverted nuclear g factor. Total projection m=mI​+mJ​ is conserved, while F need not be. In the basis ∣m−1/2,+1/2⟩, ∣m+1/2,−1/2⟩, the two-state block is
Hm​=(−4A​+gI​μB​Bm)1+21​(Am+(gJ​−gI​)μB​BA(I+1/2)2−m2​​A(I+1/2)2−m2​−Am−(gJ​−gI​)μB​B​).
(22)
For A>0, define x=(gJ​−gI​)μB​B/Δ. Diagonalization gives the Breit-Rabi formula,
E±​(m)=−4A​+gI​μB​Bm±2Δ​1+2I+14m​x+x2​.​
(23)
For either sign of A, the block itself remains valid; its ordered eigenvalues are −A/4+gI​μB​Bm±21​Δ2+2Am(gJ​−gI​)μB​B+(gJ​−gI​)2μB2​B2​.
For a stretched hyperfine Zeeman state at projection m=±(I+1/2) there is only one state each. Their energies are exactly
Estretched​=AI/2±μB​B(gJ​/2+gI​I).​
(24)
Assigning two branches to each stretched projection would double-count states.
In weak fields the shift is gF​μB​mB, with gF​=gI​±(gJ​−gI​)/(2I+1) for F=I±1/2>0; an F=0 state has no first-order Zeeman shift. At strong hyperfine-decoupling fields the eigenstates approach product states with
E≃AmI​mJ​+μB​B(gJ​mJ​+gI​mI​).
(25)
This is the hyperfine regime of the Paschen-Back effect, still assuming the field is weak enough not to invalidate the electronic J=1/2 manifold. States of the same m mix and avoid crossing when their off-diagonal coupling is nonzero; different-m levels can cross.
