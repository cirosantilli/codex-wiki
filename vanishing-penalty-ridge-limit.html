OurBigBook.com Words: 46
In an eigenbasis of G=XTX, ridge regression divides the corresponding component of XTY by γ+λ. If γ=0, its eigenvector v satisfies Xv=0, and hence vTXTY=0 exactly. Only positive eigenvalues contribute to the limit, giving the Moore-Penrose inverse expression for the minimum-norm least-squares solution. No full-rank or sample-size assumption is needed.
