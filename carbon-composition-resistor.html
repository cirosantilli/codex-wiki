Carbon composition resistor

OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A carbon composition resistor uses a bulk mixture of carbon and insulating binder. The conducting network sets its electrical resistance. Its compact geometry has little winding inductance, but composition and contact fluctuations can cause excess electronic noise and changes of electrical resistance with temperature and aging. It differs from a carbon-film resistor.
