Sonic boom

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Acoustic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
A sonic boom is the sound associated with shock waves from a body moving at a supersonic Mach number. The weak-wave Mach cone describes a propagation geometry rather than the finite pressure signature determining the sound strength.
