Mobility gap

 ... Physics Wave Wave scattering Random medium Wave propagation in a random medium Anderson localization
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
An energy interval with no extended bulk energy eigenstates available for transport. It can contain localized eigenstates and have a nonzero density of states; a mobility gap need not be a spectral gap.
