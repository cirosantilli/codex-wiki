Leaking and trapped SH-wave reverberations

OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For subcritical incidence, a reflected SH-wave loses energy into the faster substrate. Postcritical incidence has an evanescent substrate tail and no outgoing normal energy flux in the ideal lossless model. Repeated reflections can then interfere to form Love waves. Individual reflected rays are contributions to the field; normal-mode frequencies require the reflection-phase condition and are not supplied by a geometrical travel-time curve alone.
