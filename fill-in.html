Fill-in

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Numerical linear algebra Gaussian elimination Sparse Gaussian elimination
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
Fill-in consists of factor entries that become nonzero despite being zero in the original sparse matrix. In symmetric elimination these are the added edges of the elimination graph. Removing a high-degree vertex can create many fill entries, whereas a perfect elimination ordering creates none. Symbolic fill predicts possible nonzeros before floating-point arithmetic; special cancellation can reduce the numerical pattern.
