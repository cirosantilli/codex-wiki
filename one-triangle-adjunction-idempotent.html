One-triangle adjunction idempotent (e=εF​Fη)

OurBigBook.com Words: 107 Articles: 1
Let F:C→D, G:D→C, η:1⇒GF and ε:FG⇒1 be natural transformations. If GεηG​=1G​, then e=εF​Fη:F⇒F is an idempotent morphism in the functor category. Naturality implies Geη=η and εeG​=ε; these absorption identities prove e2=e. The omitted triangle measures exactly whether e=1F​.
