Degree of a holomorphic vector bundle (degE=degdetE=2πi​∫X​trFE​)

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Holomorphic vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 3
On a compact Riemann surface, the degree of a holomorphic vector bundle is the degree of a line bundle of its determinant line bundle. A Hermitian metric on a holomorphic vector bundle and its Chern connection express this degree by the trace-curvature integral. The value is independent of the metric because the normalized trace curvature represents the First Chern class.
