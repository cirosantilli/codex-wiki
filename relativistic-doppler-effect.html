Relativistic Doppler effect (ν=γν′(1+βcosθ′))

 Home Physics Wave Doppler effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 497 Articles: 6
The Lorentz transformation changes the photon four-momentum and therefore its frequency, producing the displayed formula for a boost along the first axis. Here the primed ray angle is measured in the emitting frame and β=v/c. The angle and frequency transforms must be used together: aberration of light changes the direction, and a photon-number angular fraction need not equal the corresponding radiant-energy fraction.
Here c is the vacuum speed of light, γ is the Lorentz factor, and the two frequencies are measured in different inertial reference frames. Unlike the classical Doppler effect for sound, there is no material rest reference frame for light in vacuum. For small radial recession speed vr​≪c, the wavelength shift satisfies Δλ/λ≃vr​/c. This small-speed limit underlies Doppler spectroscopy; it is not an exact sound formula.
