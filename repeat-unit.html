Repeat unit

 Home Chemistry Polymer
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A constitutional repeating unit is the smallest chemical unit whose repetition describes a regular chain. Its boundaries mark repetition, not necessarily the origin of the chemical atoms from a single monomer. See IUPAC: constitutional repeating unit.
