Strictly monotone operator (u=v⟹⟨A(u)−A(v),u−v⟩>0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Monotone operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 131 Articles: 2
A strictly monotone operator A:X→X∗ on a real Banach space satisfies strict operator monotonicity for every pair of distinct inputs. It is injective: Au=Av would make the pairing zero and hence force u=v. In particular Au=f has at most one solution for each f∈X∗.
