Irreducible variety

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic variety
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
An algebraic variety whose underlying topological space with its Zariski topology is an irreducible topological space: it is nonempty and cannot be a union of two proper closed subsets. For a reduced affine variety, this is equivalent to its coordinate ring being an integral domain.
