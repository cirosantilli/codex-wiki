Infrared-safe observable

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Effective field theory Infrared divergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
An observable unchanged by adding a particle with vanishing energy or replacing one massless particle by exactly collinear particles with the same total momentum. This property permits cancellation of real and virtual infrared divergences and collinear divergences in its perturbative cross-section. Fully inclusive electron-positron hadronic rates and suitably defined jet observables are examples.
