Suppose a finite population is split into m categories with fixed sizes c1​,…,cm​ summing to n. A uniform size-r sample drawn by sampling without replacement has category counts K1​,…,Km​ with the displayed joint probability mass function, for integers 0≤ki​≤ci​ summing to r. It extends the two-category hypergeometric distribution; replacing the sampled objects instead gives a multinomial distribution.
