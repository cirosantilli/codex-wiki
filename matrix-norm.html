Matrix norm

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 174 Articles: 3
A matrix norm is a norm on a vector space of matrices; common choices include induced operator norms and the Frobenius norm. A submultiplicative matrix norm additionally satisfies ∥AB∥≤∥A∥∥B∥. The matrix 2-norm is the norm induced by Euclidean norms and equals the largest singular value.
