SLAC-MIT spectrometer readout and corrections

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Deep inelastic scattering SLAC-MIT deep inelastic electron scattering experiment
OurBigBook.com Words: 145
A movable magnetic spectrometer selected scattered electrons by momentum and scattering angle. Magnetic fields dispersed momenta at the focal plane. With relativistic electrons, the inferred momentum determines outgoing energy E′. Commissioning included steering the beam through the spectrometer at zero angle to check its optics and accepted solid angle. Counters sorted events into momentum and angle bins. The acceptance entered the conversion from counts to differential scattering cross-sections.[ref]
Radiative corrections matter because photon emission can move events from an elastic scattering peak into lower outgoing-energy bins. The analyses subtracted the elastic radiative tail before unfolding inelastic radiation. Independent MIT and SLAC procedures provided a check. An uncorrected low-E′ tail alone therefore cannot establish deep inelastic scattering.[ref]
