Conservative mass-transfer critical mass ratio (qcrit​=(ζ∗​+5/3)/2)

 ... Stellar astrophysics Binary star Roche lobe Roche-lobe overflow Conservative binary mass transfer Dynamical stability of binary mass transfer
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
With fixed total mass and orbital angular momentum and RL​∝a(Md​/M)1/3, the Roche-lobe radius response exponent is 2q−5/3. A donor with stellar radius response exponent ζ∗​ is stable to infinitesimal mass loss when ζ∗​>2q−5/3, giving q<(ζ∗​+5/3)/2. The threshold depends on the donor response.
