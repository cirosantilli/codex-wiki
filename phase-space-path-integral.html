Phase-space path integral (K=∫DqDpei∫(pq˙​−H)dt)

A phase-space path integral represents a quantum transition kernel by integrating both coordinates and conjugate momenta with action ∫(pq˙​−H)dt. It follows from inserting position and momentum completeness relations between short-time evolution operators. For a quadratic momentum dependence the momentum variables can be integrated by a Gaussian integral.
