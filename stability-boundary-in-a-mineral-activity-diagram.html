Stability boundary in a mineral activity diagram

 ... Thermodynamic potential Thermodynamic free energy Gibbs free energy Phase coexistence curve Phase diagram Mineral activity diagram
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 1
A pairwise equilibrium line restricted to conditions where both minerals are stable against the other allowed phases. Compute the Gibbs energy of reaction for independent conversions to compare them. A pairwise line can continue into a region where a third mineral is favoured; this continuation is a metastable equilibrium relation, rather than a boundary of stable fields.
