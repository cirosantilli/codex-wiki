Gershgorin stability bound for a variable-coefficient diffusion stencil

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Gershgorin circle theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 144
A symmetric matrix obtained from a conservative five-point discretization of a diffusion equation has row centre −si​/h2 and radius at most si​/h2, where h>0 is the grid spacing and si​ is the sum of its four nonnegative edge coefficients. Here the matrix represents the diffusion generator, with nonnegative off-diagonal entries; boundary elimination may reduce the row radius. If every coefficient is at most amax​>0, then si​≤4amax​, and the Gershgorin bounds for symmetric matrices place every eigenvalue in [−8amax​/h2,0].
For the forward Euler method, an eigenvalue λ≤0 has amplification factor 1+Δtλ. Requiring ∣1+Δtλ∣≤1 for every such eigenvalue gives the sufficient absolute stability condition Δt/h2≤1/(4amax​). Symmetry makes this also prevent growth of the Euclidean norm. For amax​=2 and h=0.1, the bound is λ∈[−1600,0] and Δt≤0.00125. At that step size the interval maps to amplification factors in [−1,1]. This illustrates why cheap Gershgorin disc bounds can select a stable time step before any eigenvalues are computed.
