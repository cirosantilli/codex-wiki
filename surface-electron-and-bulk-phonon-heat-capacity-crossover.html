Surface-electron and bulk-phonon heat-capacity crossover (Csurf​/Cbulk​∝A/(NT))

A neutral two-dimensional linear electronic spectrum has heat capacity proportional to AT2, while three-dimensional acoustic phonons have Debye heat capacity proportional to NT3/ΘD3​. Thus their ratio scales as AkB2​ΘD3​/(ℏ2v2NT). At fixed crystal composition and shape, A/N∝1/L for linear size L, favoring the electronic surface contribution in smaller or colder specimens. The continuum laws require thermally populated wavelengths small compared with sample size; their formal low-temperature crossover need not lie inside that regime.
