Here ∥g∥22​=∫−11​g(x)2dx is the squared L2 norm, without division by the interval length. Each row retains the previous Legendre polynomial coefficients and appends one new term. The ordinary power coefficients can change: adding 32​P2​ changes the constant from 4/3 to 1, and adding 52​P3​ changes the linear coefficient from 8/5 to 1. Independence belongs to the chosen orthogonal polynomial coordinates.
The full squared L2 norm is ∥f∥22​=192/35, equal to 32/9+128/75+8/45+8/175. The residual budgets are the omitted terms:
E0​=75128​+458​+1758​=315608​,E1​=458​+1758​=1575352​,E2​=1758​,E3​=0.

For a direct check independent of summing the budgets, f−q2​=x3−3x/5, and integrating its square gives 2/7−12/25+6/25=8/175. This residual is orthogonal to 1,x,x2, exactly the conditions for the best quadratic least-squares solution. An orthogonal projection need not be a polynomial interpolant: for example, q2​(1)=18/5 while f(1)=4. The approximation allocates error across the interval according to the chosen weighted inner product.
