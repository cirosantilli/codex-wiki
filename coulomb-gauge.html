Coulomb gauge

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 65 Articles: 1
The Coulomb gauge imposes
∇⋅A=0
(53)
on the magnetic vector potential. A gauge transformation A↦A+∇χ reaches this gauge when χ solves
∇2χ=−∇⋅A.
(54)
