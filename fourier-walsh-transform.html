Fourier-Walsh transform

For f:{−1,1}n→R, the characters χS​(x)=∏i∈S​xi​ form an orthonormal basis, and the Fourier-Walsh expansion is
f=∑S⊆[n]​f​(S)χS​,f​(S)=E[fχS​].
(47)
On the finite Boolean hypercube, these coefficients are the Hadamard transform of the vector of function values, with expectation normalization.
