Schmidt rank (r)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Von Neumann entropy Entanglement entropy Schmidt decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 198 Articles: 3
The Schmidt rank is the number of nonzero terms in a Schmidt decomposition, equivalently the rank of either reduced density matrix of a bipartite pure state.
