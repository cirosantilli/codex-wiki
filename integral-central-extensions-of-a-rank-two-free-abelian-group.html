Integral central extensions of a rank-two free abelian group (En​=⟨x,y,z: z central, [x,y]=zn⟩)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group cohomology Group extension Second group cohomology classifies group extensions
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
With a fixed identification of the kernel with Z and quotient with Z2, these central group extensions are classified by n∈Z. Choose lifts x,y of the quotient generators; their commutator is zn and is unchanged by central changes of lifts. Every element has unique normal form zmybxa, proving this invariant is complete. A cocycle representative is c((a,b),(c,d))=nad, whose cocycle identity follows from bilinearity. Its extension multiplication is (a,b,m)(c,d,l)=(a+c,b+d,m+l+nad). The zero parameter gives Z3 and parameter one gives the integer Heisenberg group. The Koszul resolution for a rank-two free abelian group independently computes H2(Z2,Z)≅Z.
