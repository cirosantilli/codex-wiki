Velocity correlation tensor (Qij​(r))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Homogeneous turbulence Isotropic turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 342 Articles: 8
The velocity correlation tensor is Qij​(r)=⟨ui​(x)uj​(x+r)⟩. For isotropic turbulence it splits into longitudinal and transverse parts. Incompressibility constrains them, while the Fourier transform gives the turbulent energy spectrum. Its trace is the scalar velocity-dot-product correlation.
