Permutation module (R[X])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Permutation representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For a finite group action on a finite set X, R[X] is the free R-module with basis X, with the group permuting that basis. For a transitive set G/H it is IndHG​R. Disjoint unions of sets give direct sums of permutation modules.
