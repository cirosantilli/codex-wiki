FRET dipole orientation factor (κ2)

 Home Biology Biophysical measurement Förster resonance energy transfer FRET efficiency Förster distance
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
The FRET dipole orientation factor describes the geometry of the two transition electric dipoles relative to their separation. Let μ​D​, μ​A​ and r be their unit vectors. Then
κ2=[μ​D​⋅μ​A​−3(μ​D​⋅r)(μ​A​⋅r)]2,0≤κ2≤4.
(6)
For a separation along z, parallel electric dipoles along z give κ2=4, parallel ones along x give 1, and one along x with the other along y gives 0. Thus rotating a pair can change FRET efficiency without changing its separation.
The familiar value κ2=2/3 requires suitable rapid, independent orientation averaging during the FRET donor lifetime. Random orientations across a population of slowly rotating fluorescent proteins do not establish that dynamic condition. [ref]
