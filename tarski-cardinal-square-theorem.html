Tarski cardinal-square theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
In ZF, if X×X≅X for every infinite X, apply this to X⊔h(X). The product-sum comparison lemma applied to X and its Hartogs ordinal gives either an impossible injection h(X)→X or a surjection h(X)→X. In the latter case, ordering each x∈X by its least ordinal preimage well-orders X. The converse is the well-orderable-cardinal identity κ2=κ.
