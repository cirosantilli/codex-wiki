Magnetic diffusion (∂t​B=ηm​∇2B)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 435 Articles: 7
Finite electrical conductivity permits resistive smoothing of a magnetic field. With uniform coefficients and no motion the field satisfies a diffusion equation; with motion the diffusion term supplements the curl of u×B. The magnetic Reynolds number compares these effects.
