Burnside basis theorem (d(G)=dimFp​​G/Φ(G))

 ... Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Finite p-group Frattini subgroup Frattini quotient
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A subset generates a finite p-group precisely when its images span the Frattini quotient. Indeed ⟨S⟩Φ(G)=G implies ⟨S⟩=G, since otherwise a maximal subgroup contains both factors. Minimal generating sets correspond to vector-space bases in the quotient.
