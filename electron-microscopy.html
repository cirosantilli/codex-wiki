Electron microscopy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Electron optics Electron microscope
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 27
Electron microscopy is the practice of preparing specimens, producing images with an electron microscope, and interpreting their contrast. A dark region can reflect electron deflection out of the admitted beam rather than complete absorption. A useful image therefore needs a stated preparation, detection signal and length calibration; brightness by itself does not identify a material. Transmission electron microscopy probes thin specimens, whereas a scanning electron microscope can examine the exposed surface of a bulk specimen.[ref]
