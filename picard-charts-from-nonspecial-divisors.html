Picard charts from nonspecial divisors (H1(C,O(E))=0,degE=g)

 ... Sheaf of modules Locally free sheaf Line bundle Picard group Picard functor of a curve Picard scheme of a curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 97
A degree-g line bundle with vanishing first sheaf cohomology has exactly one section up to scale, by Riemann-Roch theorem. Its zero divisor is unique and varies in families by cohomology and base change for line bundles on a curve. For a fixed divisor B of degree N−g, the open subset of C(g) where O(E) has vanishing first cohomology gives a chart in degree N, by E↦O(B+E). Different charts are glued by the unique residual divisor of O(B+E−B′) wherever that line bundle is again nonspecial. Normalizing the universal bundles along a fixed rational point gives their canonical compatible gluing and a representing scheme for the Picard functor of a curve.
