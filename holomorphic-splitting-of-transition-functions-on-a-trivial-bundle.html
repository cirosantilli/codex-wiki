Holomorphic splitting of transition functions on a trivial bundle (Fαβ​=Hβ​Hα−1​)

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Holomorphic vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
For a holomorphically trivial vector bundle, choose a global holomorphic bundle frame. Let the columns of Hα​ be the coordinates of that frame in the prescribed frame over Uα​. If fiber coordinates transform as sβ​=Fαβ​sα​, then Hβ​=Fαβ​Hα​. Each matrix is holomorphic and invertible, giving the displayed splitting. Conversely such matrices give agreeing global holomorphic sections forming a frame. Topological triviality alone does not supply this conclusion.
