Positivity of divisors

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Cartier divisor
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 31
The positivity of a Cartier divisor can be measured by sections, intersection numbers, or its numerical class. Ample divisors give projective embeddings after taking a multiple; nef divisors are their numerical limits; big divisors have the maximum possible order of section growth. Nefness and bigness are different conditions.
