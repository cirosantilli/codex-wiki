Quartic-inflation prompt-reheating estimate (TR​∼λ1/4g∗−1/4​MP​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic inflation Reheating Reheating temperature
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For a quartic inflaton potential, an endpoint field of order the unreduced Planck mass leaves density of order λMP4​. If prompt, efficient reheating converts it to equilibrium radiation, ρ=(π2/30)g∗​TR4​ gives the displayed scaling. With the potential-only endpoint estimate ϕe​=MP​/π​, the coefficient is [15/(2π4)]1/4. End-of-inflation kinetic energy changes that coefficient by order one, and delayed transfer or incomplete thermalization requires a reheating model rather than the potential alone.
