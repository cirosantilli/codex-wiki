Brownian portfolio exposures (dw=⋯dt+∑j​yj​dWj)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Investment portfolio
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
Brownian portfolio exposures are the coefficients yj​ of independent Brownian motions in the wealth equation. An invertible asset volatility matrix lets one optimize directly over these exposures. The drift risk premium is then the dot product of exposures with the market price of risk vector.
