Uncorrelated random variables

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Expected value Variance Covariance
OurBigBook.com Words: 86 Articles: 1
Two square-integrable random variables X,Y are uncorrelated when Cov(X,Y)=0. Independent random variables with finite second moments are uncorrelated, but the converse generally fails.
