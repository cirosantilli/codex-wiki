Extensive-form game

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 625 Articles: 6
A game described by a game tree, with the acting player and available actions specified at each decision node, payoffs at terminal nodes, and information sets indicating which decision nodes a player can distinguish. A pure strategy specifies an action at every information set belonging to that player, including ones not reached during realized play.
