Finite invariant sets in a countable saturated structure are definable

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Model theory Elementary extension Saturated model Omega-saturated model
OurBigBook.com Words: 113
Let M be a countable omega-saturated model and D⊆M finite. Then D is a parameter-free definable set exactly when every structure automorphism preserves it setwise. One direction is automorphism invariance of parameter-free definability. For the converse, saturation implies homogeneity and the back-and-forth method show that equal empty-set types are in the same automorphism orbit. For a∈D, if no formula in tp(a) has its realization set contained in D, the partial type tp(a)∪{x=d:d∈D} is finitely satisfiable. Its parameters form a finite set, so saturation realizes it outside D, contradicting invariance. Choose a defining neighbourhood for each a∈D and take their finite disjunction. The empty set is defined by x=x. Setwise invariance permits automorphisms to permute points within D.
