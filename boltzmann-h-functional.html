Boltzmann H functional (H(f)=∫flogfdxdv)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Boltzmann equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 2
The integral uses 0log0=0 when it is defined. For the Boltzmann equation, the Boltzmann H theorem makes it nonincreasing under suitable regularity and integrability. This functional is distinct from the microstate-counting Boltzmann entropy; the physical kinetic entropy has the opposite sign.
