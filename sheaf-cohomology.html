Sheaf cohomology (Hp(X,F))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 55
Sheaf cohomology consists of the right derived functors of global sections. The zeroth group is H0(X,F)=Γ(X,F), and higher groups measure obstructions to gluing local sections.
