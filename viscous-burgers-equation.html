Viscous Burgers equation (ut​+uux​=νuxx​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Burgers' equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 580 Articles: 12
The viscous Burgers equation adds diffusion with viscosity ν>0 to nonlinear transport. Smooth initial data remain smooth, and the diffusion regularizes the shocks of the Inviscid Burgers equation.
