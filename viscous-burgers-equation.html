Viscous Burgers equation (ut​+uux​=νuxx​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Burgers' equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 14
The viscous Burgers equation combines a nonlinear transport equation with diffusion with constant ν>0. On the infinite line, a bounded initial condition gives a smooth function solving the equation for every positive time; a jump in the initial condition is smoothed rather than retained as a discontinuity. Small ν can nevertheless produce a steep layer, as the Viscous Burgers travelling shock illustrates. The Cole-Hopf transformation u=−2ν∂x​logϕ, with positive ϕ, converts this equation to the heat equation ϕt​=νϕxx​.[ref]
Positive diffusion also explains the admissibility of the inviscid limit. For the entropy flux for a scalar conservation law associated with η(u)=u2/2, direct multiplication by u gives
∂t​2u2​+∂x​3u3​=ν∂xx​2u2​−νux2​.
(4)
The last term is nonpositive. With convergence sufficient to pass to the limit, the vanishing viscosity approximation therefore retains an entropy inequality rather than just the weak solution equation. For general bounded data, the full Kruzhkov entropy inequalities specify the entropy solution; the quadratic identity is a useful illustration of dissipation.[ref]
