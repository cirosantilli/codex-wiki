Superspace differential realization of N=1 supercharges ({Qα​,Qˉ​β˙​​}=2σαβ˙​μ​Pμ​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry Super-Poincaré algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
With left Grassmann derivatives, define Pμ​=−i∂μ​, Qα​=−i∂θα​−σαγ˙​μ​θˉγ˙​∂μ​ and Qˉ​β˙​​=i∂θˉβ˙​​+θγσγβ˙​μ​∂μ​. The anticommutators of the derivative parts and of the coordinate parts vanish. Each mixed derivative/coordinate pair contributes −iσμ∂μ​ by the graded product rule, giving the displayed mixed anticommutator. Equal-chirality anticommutators vanish, and all these operators commute with translations. Under Lorentz transformations their spinor indices transform in conjugate Weyl spinor representations, completing the minimal Super-Poincaré algebra. A convention for left derivatives is essential to the signs.
