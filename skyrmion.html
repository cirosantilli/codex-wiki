Skyrmion

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field-theory soliton Skyrme model
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 15
A Skyrmion is a finite-energy topological soliton of the Skyrme model. Its baryon number is the degree of the compactified spatial map S3→SU(2)≃S3.
