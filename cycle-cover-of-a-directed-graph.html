Cycle cover of a directed graph

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Directed graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
A cycle cover of a finite directed graph is a collection of vertex-disjoint directed cycles containing every vertex exactly once. Cycle covers of the weighted directed graph with adjacency matrix A are the terms in the permanent of a matrix permA.
This is a vertex cycle cover with the additional requirements of directed cycles and vertex disjointness.
