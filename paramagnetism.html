Paramagnetism (χ>0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Bound charge and bound current Magnetization
OurBigBook.com Words: 275 Articles: 7
In a linear isotropic regime, a paramagnetic material has positive magnetic susceptibility: its induced magnetization is aligned with the applied magnetic field intensity. Molecular oxygen supplies an example discussed through molecular orbital theory in the covalent bond article.
