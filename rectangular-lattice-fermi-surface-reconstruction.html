Rectangular-lattice Fermi-surface reconstruction

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bloch's theorem Nearly-free electron model
OurBigBook.com Words: 233 Articles: 2
Consider a spin-degenerate two-dimensional free electron model with rectangular periods a>b and two electrons per unit cell. Write K=π/a, L=π/b, and kF2​=4π/(ab). If K<kF​<L, the circular Fermi contour crosses only the vertical boundaries of the first Brillouin zone. Folding translates its protruding caps back by a reciprocal lattice vector (2K,0). A weak periodic potential mixes the intersecting plane waves, reconnecting the contours into two open first-band sheets and a closed second-band pocket. The pocket is centred on a zone boundary: its halves on opposite sides of a reduced-zone plot are identified periodically and belong to one closed sheet.
