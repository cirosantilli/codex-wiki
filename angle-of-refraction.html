Angle of refraction (θt​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Refraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
The angle of refraction is the angle between the transmitted light ray and the surface normal vector, measured on the transmitted side. With positive refractive indices, a propagating transmitted light ray satisfies ni​sinθi​=nt​sinθt​ in Snell's law. The incident and transmitted angles are measured from the same normal line, not from the surface and not from the emergent light ray at a second surface.
