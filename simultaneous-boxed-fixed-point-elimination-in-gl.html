Simultaneous boxed fixed-point elimination in GL (Fi​↔□Ci​(F1​,…,Fm​))

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Modal logic Provability logic Gödel-Löb logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 123 Articles: 1
In Gödel-Löb logic, the one-variable identity □C(⊤)↔□C(□C(⊤)) solves a boxed equation with a formula omitting its unknown letter. A finite simultaneous system can be solved by elimination. First solve its last equation with the other unknowns treated as parameters, substitute that formula into the preceding equations, and apply induction. Substituting the resulting solutions back into the last formula completes the system. This construction introduces no new parameter letters.
