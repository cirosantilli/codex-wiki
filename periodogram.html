Periodogram (IT​(λ))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Stationary process Weakly stationary process Spectral density of a stationary process
OurBigBook.com Words: 257 Articles: 3
For a centered record of length T, the periodogram is the squared modulus of its discrete Fourier transform, with normalization
IT​(λ)=2πT1​​∑t=1T​Xt​e−itλ​2.
(4)
Under suitable short-memory assumptions its expectation approaches the spectral density of a stationary process, but its variance generally does not vanish. Smoothing nearby frequencies can provide an estimate with statistical consistency.
