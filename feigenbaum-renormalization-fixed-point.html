Feigenbaum renormalization fixed point (g=T(g))

OurBigBook.com Words: 242 Articles: 3
A Feigenbaum fixed point is an even analytic unimodal interval map g with g(0)=1, a critical maximum of even order d, a=g(1)∈(−1,0) and
g(x)=a−1g(g(ax)).
(16)
In the quadratic class g′′(0)<0. Its spatial scaling uses the Feigenbaum constants convention α=−1/a; the values depend on the critical order. The central restrictive intervals are successively reduced by ∣a∣ under renormalization. This fixed point describes the limiting shape of repeatedly renormalized maps at a period-doubling accumulation.
