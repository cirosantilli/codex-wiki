Agglomerative hierarchical clustering

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical learning Cluster analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 280 Articles: 6
Start with singleton clusters and repeatedly merge the two clusters with the smallest chosen intercluster dissimilarity. Single-linkage clustering uses the smallest cross-pair distance; complete-linkage clustering uses the largest. Merge heights and sensitivity to ties help assess a proposed number of groups.
