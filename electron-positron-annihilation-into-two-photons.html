Electron-positron annihilation into two photons (e−e+→γγ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Quantum electrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The leading scattering amplitude is the sum of two tree-level Feynman diagrams, with the outgoing photons attached in either order to the fermion line. The Dirac propagator carries momentum p−k1​ or p−k2​ for incoming electron momentum p. Their sum obeys the two-photon fermion Ward identity, although individual diagrams need not. Crossing symmetry relates this amplitude to Compton scattering.
