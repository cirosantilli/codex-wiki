Tidal acceleration (arela​=−Rabcd​UbξcUd)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Riemann curvature tensor Geodesic deviation
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 1
The relative acceleration of neighboring freely falling bodies is measured by geodesic deviation, comparing their infinitesimal separation ξ in a parallel transport frame along a reference timelike geodesic with tangent U. With Rabcd​ defined by the covariant derivative commutator, it has the displayed form. In the Newtonian limit the corresponding tidal tensor is the spatial gradient of the gravitational acceleration. A single freely falling body can remove local gravitational acceleration by its frame choice, whereas relative tidal acceleration measures curvature.
