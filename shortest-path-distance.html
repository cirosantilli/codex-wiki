Shortest-path distance (d(s,t)=infP:s⇝t​∑e∈P​w(e))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Shortest path problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
The infimum of total edge weights over allowed walks from a source to a target. The value is +∞ when no route exists. A negative cycle reachable from the source and able to reach the target makes it −∞. In a finite graph without such a cycle, a finite distance is attained by a graph path, after removing non-improving repetitions.
