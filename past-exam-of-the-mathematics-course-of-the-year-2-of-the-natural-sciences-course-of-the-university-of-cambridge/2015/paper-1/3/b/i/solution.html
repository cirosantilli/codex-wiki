Use the zero initial displacement and velocity given in the PDF. The characteristic roots of the homogeneous equation are −1±iμ. Its causal Green function of a damped oscillator is
G(t)=H(t)μe−tsin(μt)​,
(16)
where H is the Heaviside step function. The positive-time kernel has G(0+)=0 and G′(0+)=1, so its derivative jump generates a unit impulse. The zero-data forced solution is consequently the causal convolution
x(t)=μ1​∫0t​e−(t−s)sin[μ(t−s)]g(s)ds,t≥0.​
(17)
Differentiating once gives x′(0)=0; differentiating twice supplies the endpoint term g(t) and verifies the differential equation. No homogeneous transient is required with these initial data.
