The ratio test therefore gives R=1​ for each nonterminating series. The nearest singular coefficient points are x=±1, both distance one from the expansion centre.
The polynomial exception is essential here. If n is an integer, the series of the same parity as ∣n∣ terminates and has R=∞​; the opposite-parity independent series remains nonterminating with radius one. For noninteger n, both have radius one. A regular singular point can be removable for one particular solution, so its presence does not force every individual solution to have finite convergence radius.
