Perform the product and inverse formulas from part (b) in the commutative ring Z/4Z. They involve only addition, multiplication and negatives, so closure and inverses remain valid; no division by a nonunit is needed. Associativity again comes from matrix multiplication, and A(0,0,0) remains the identity. The Heisenberg group over a commutative ring therefore has
∣G∣=43=64.​
(65)
These are residues modulo four, not arithmetic in the finite field of four elements.
For the additional subgroup task, write an element of H as A(t,y,t). Its product and inverse are
A(t,y,t)A(s,v,s)=A(t+s,y+v+ts,t+s),A(t,y,t)−1=A(−t,t2−y,−t).
(66)
Both remain in H, and the product is unchanged on exchanging the factors because ts=st. The identity is included, so H is an Abelian subgroup. There are four independent choices each for t,y, giving
∣H∣=16,[G:H]=1664​=4.​
(67)
Thus there are four distinct left cosets. Explicitly the surjective group homomorphism A(x,y,z)↦x−z(mod4) has kernel H; the four cosets are the level sets of that difference, with representatives A(d,0,0), d=0,1,2,3. This is the Abelian plane subgroup of the Heisenberg group modulo four.
