ψ0​(x,y)=e−k3x/2eiky.​
(17)
The decay of a Fourier mode in a third-order spatial evolution equation requires a sign check: this satisfies the requested limit at positive infinity precisely when k>0​. For k=0 it remains identically one, while for k<0 its amplitude grows exponentially. Hence for those nonpositive values there is no separated solution meeting both prescribed boundary conditions; the printed assumption that k is merely real needs this additional restriction for decay.
