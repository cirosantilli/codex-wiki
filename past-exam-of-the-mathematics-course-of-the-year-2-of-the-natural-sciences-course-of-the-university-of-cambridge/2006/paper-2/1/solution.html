Away from its source, this fundamental solution of the Laplace equation is harmonic. Its radial derivative is 1/(4πρ2), so the flux of its gradient through a small source-centred sphere is one. The divergence theorem therefore supplies exactly the unit Dirac delta function required by the equation. Decay at infinity excludes an added globally regular harmonic term.
