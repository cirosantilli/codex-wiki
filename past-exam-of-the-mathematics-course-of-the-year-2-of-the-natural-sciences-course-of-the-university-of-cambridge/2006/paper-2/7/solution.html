For small oscillations with quadratic kinetic and potential energies, a normal mode has a fixed spatial amplitude pattern and one angular normal frequency. Its amplitude is a normal coordinate; choosing all modes diagonalizes the two quadratic forms and decouples their equations. A zero frequency describes a free symmetry motion.

The normal mode coordinates are Qα​=uα​⋅q, with equations Q¨​α​+ωα2​Qα​=0. The zero mode rotates all beads together without stretching a spring and has Q0​=A+Bt. In each of the two degenerate middle modes, opposite beads have opposite signed arc displacements while the other pair remains stationary. Any fixed linear combination is another mode of that frequency. The last mode alternates the signed displacement of adjacent beads. The radius cancels from the frequencies because arc length supplies the same factor R2 to the angular kinetic and spring energies.
