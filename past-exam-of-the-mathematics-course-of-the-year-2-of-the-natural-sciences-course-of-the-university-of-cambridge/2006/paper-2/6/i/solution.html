For a Cartesian tensor under a proper orthogonal change R, the rank-n law is Ti1​⋯in​′​=Ri1​j1​​⋯Rin​jn​​Tj1​⋯jn​​, with repeated indices summed. An isotropic tensor has T′=T for every such rotation.
For rank two, T=RTRT. A half-turn about a coordinate axis, obtained by applying a quarter-turn twice, reverses each off-diagonal component with exactly one index on that axis. Invariance forces every off-diagonal entry to vanish. A quarter-turn about the third axis exchanges T11​ and T22​; quarter-turns about the other axes equate them with T33​. Thus the isotropic second-rank tensor is
Tij​=λδij​.​
(15)
Conversely R(λI)RT=λI, proving sufficiency.
