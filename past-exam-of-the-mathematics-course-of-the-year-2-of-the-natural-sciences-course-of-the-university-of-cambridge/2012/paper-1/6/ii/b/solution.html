β=j∑​∣cj​∣2(λj​−a)(λj​−b).​
(34)
Since the spectrum avoids [a,b], each λj​ is either below a or above b, making every product strictly positive. This proves positivity of a quadratic polynomial across a spectral gap:
β≥δ∥v∥2,δ=jmin​(λj​−a)(λj​−b)>0.​
(35)
In particular β>0 for every nonzero v. For v=0, β=0; the printed strict claim for an arbitrary vector must exclude this case.
