For a nonnegative integer ℓ, the chain of the same parity as ℓ reaches n=ℓ, where the numerator of the recurrence relation vanishes. The next coefficient and every later coefficient in that chain are zero, producing a polynomial of degree ℓ. Normalizing it at x=1 gives the Legendre polynomial Pℓ​.

Choose a family satisfying the essential boundary conditions and finite energy, evaluate the quotient, and minimize over its parameters.
