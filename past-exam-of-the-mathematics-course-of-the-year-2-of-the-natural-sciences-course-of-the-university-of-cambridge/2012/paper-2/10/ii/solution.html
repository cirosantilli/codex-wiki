Let B=(0c​b0​) be the lower block. Then
B2=bcI2​,T(a)2=diag(1,bc,bc),T(a)4=diag(1,(bc)2,(bc)2).
(57)
The defining relation a4=I requires T(a)4=I3​, so for real constants bc=±1. Both possibilities ensure invertibility.
If bc=−1, the generator matrix has order four, because its square is not the identity while its fourth power is. Its four powers are distinct, giving a faithful representation. If bc=1, its square is the identity but it is not itself the identity; its lower diagonal entries are zero. The image then has order two and the kernel of a group homomorphism is {I,a2}. Thus the faithfulness criterion for a block representation of the cyclic group of order four gives
faithful exactly when bc=−1;unfaithful representation exactly when bc=1.​
(58)
Other parameter values give no representation of Z4​, not additional unfaithful cases; bc=0 also makes the assigned matrix singular. No orthogonality of these matrices was required.
