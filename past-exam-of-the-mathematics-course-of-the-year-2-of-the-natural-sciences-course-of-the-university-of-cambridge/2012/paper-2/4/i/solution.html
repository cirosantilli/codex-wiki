For a counterclockwise simple contour containing z0​, and f analytic on a neighbourhood of its interior and boundary, the Cauchy integral formula is
2πi1​∮C​z−z0​f(z)​dz=f(z0​).​
(20)
For the required rational integral, use partial fraction decomposition:
z2−11​=21​(z−11​−z+11​).
(21)
Both points 1,−1 lie inside ∣z∣=2. Applying the formula with the constant analytic function one gives 2πi for each of the two terms. They cancel, so
∮∣z∣=2​z2−1dz​=21​(2πi−2πi)=0.​
(22)
Reversing the circle's orientation would leave this zero result unchanged.
