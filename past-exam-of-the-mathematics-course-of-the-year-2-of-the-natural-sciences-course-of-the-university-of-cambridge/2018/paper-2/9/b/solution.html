The two diagonal generators are independent involutions, so
H1​={diag(ϵ1​,ϵ2​):ϵ1​,ϵ2​∈{1,−1}},∣H1​∣=4.
(84)
It is a Klein four-group. Write the swapping generator as J=(01​10​). Then J2=I, so H2​={I,J} has order two. Moreover,
Jdiag(ϵ1​,ϵ2​)J=diag(ϵ2​,ϵ1​)∈H1​.
(85)
Thus J normalizes H1​. The set
K={DJη:D∈H1​, η∈{0,1}}
(86)
is a group: its products reduce using
(DJη)(D′Jζ)=D(JηD′J−η)Jη+ζ,
(87)
and inverses reduce in the same way. It contains both generating subgroups, so minimality gives G⊆K; conversely every displayed product is in G, giving G=K.
There are four diagonal matrices D and four off-diagonal matrices DJ, all distinct. Consequently
∣G∣=8​.
(88)
Also H1​∩H2​={I}, since J is not diagonal, and every element of G has the form DJη∈H1​H2​. This proves condition (I), hence the exact factorization of a group. Here H1​ is a normal subgroup and G=H1​⋊H2​ is a semidirect product. Its matrices are the two-dimensional signed permutation matrices, also realizing the dihedral group of the square.
