Classification and existence. For y′′+p(x)y′+q(x)y=0, an ordinary point x0​ is one where p,q are analytic. There are two independent analytic power series solutions, with arbitrary initial values y(x0​),y′(x0​). A regular singular point is a singular point where (x−x0​)p(x) and (x−x0​)2q(x) are analytic. The Frobenius method then constructs solutions beginning with (x−x0​)σ, where σ solves the indicial equation. At least one Frobenius solution exists; two such solutions exist without logarithms when the two exponents differ by a noninteger. Repeated exponents or an integer difference can require a logarithmic second solution.
