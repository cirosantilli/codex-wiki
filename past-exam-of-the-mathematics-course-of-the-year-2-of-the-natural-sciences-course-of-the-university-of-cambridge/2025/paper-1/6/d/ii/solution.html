Let K(t)=etRSe−tR. The product rule and differentiation of the matrix exponential give
K′(t)=RK(t)−K(t)R=[R,K(t)],K′′(t)=[R,[R,K(t)]],
(60)
where brackets are the operator commutator. Evaluating at zero gives K(0)=S, K′(0)=[R,S] and K′′(0)=[R,[R,S]]. The Taylor series at zero is consequently
etRSe−tR=S+t[R,S]+2t2​[R,[R,S]]+O(t3).​
(61)
In particular [R,[R,S]]=R2S−2RSR+SR2, which also follows by multiplying the two matrix exponential series through quadratic order. For fixed finite matrices the remainder has the stated Big O notation in any matrix norm.
