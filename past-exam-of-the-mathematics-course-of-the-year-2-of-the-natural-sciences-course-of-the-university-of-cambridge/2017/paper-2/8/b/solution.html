Observe that A=−I, B2=I and C=−B. Thus A2=B2=C2=I, while AB=BA=C, AC=CA=B and BC=CB=A. The multiplication table, with the left factor labeling the row, is
IABC​IIABC​AAICB​BBCIA​CCBAI​​.​
(63)
It proves closure of the set. Associativity is inherited from matrix multiplication, I is the identity element, and every element is its own inverse element. Therefore this set is a group. The table is symmetric, so it is an Abelian group; its three nonidentity elements have order two, identifying it as the Klein four-group C2​×C2​.
