Let d=b−a. In the mixed Dirichlet-Neumann problem in an elliptic annulus, the angular Fourier modes separate. The outer data excite the cosν mode, while the inner derivative excites cos2ν.
For the first mode, a radial factor with zero derivative at μ=a is cosh(μ−a). Normalizing its outer value gives F1​(μ)=cosh(μ−a)/coshd. For the second mode, a radial factor vanishing at μ=b is sinh[2(b−μ)]. Its derivative at a must be −1, fixing the coefficient to 1/[2cosh(2d)]. Therefore
