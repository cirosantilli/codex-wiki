Differentiate the position vector with respect to the three coordinates:
rρ​ru​rv​​=(sinhρcosucosv,sinhρcosusinv,coshρsinu),=(−coshρsinucosv,−coshρsinusinv,sinhρcosu),=(−coshρcosusinv,coshρcosucosv,0).​
(4)
Their pairwise dot products vanish. For example, rρ​⋅ru​=−sinhρcoshρsinucosu+sinhρcoshρsinucosu=0. Thus these are orthogonal coordinates wherever the coordinate tangents are nonzero. Their scale factors of orthogonal coordinates are
hρ2​=hu2​=sinh2ρcos2u+cosh2ρsin2u=sinh2ρ+sin2u,hv​=coshρcosu.
(5)
The absolute Jacobian determinant is their product, giving
dV=coshρcosu(sinh2ρ+sin2u)dρdudv.​
(6)
The ordered coordinate system is left handed: rρ​⋅(ru​×rv​)=−hρ​hu​hv​. The volume measure uses the absolute value. The axis and the focal circle contain coordinate degeneracies, as expected for oblate spheroidal coordinates. This is the hyperbolic parametrization of oblate spheroidal coordinates. The local TeX has an OCR substitution sinh2u in the volume formula; the original PDF correctly prints sin2u.
