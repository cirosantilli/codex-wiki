Let f=u+iv be analytic on a neighbourhood of a bounded planar region Ω and its piecewise smooth boundary C, oriented with Ω on the left. The Cauchy integral theorem states
∮C​f(z)dz=0.​
(26)
For a region with holes, C includes the clockwise inner boundaries as well as the counterclockwise outer one. In particular, the integral around a closed contour bounding a region throughout which f is analytic is zero.
To prove this classical form, expand the line integral and apply Green's theorem to its real and imaginary parts:
∮C​fdz​=∮C​(udx−vdy)+i∮C​(vdx+udy)=∬Ω​(−vx​−uy​)dxdy+i∬Ω​(ux​−vy​)dxdy.​
(27)
Both integrands vanish by the Cauchy-Riemann equations, ux​=vy​ and uy​=−vx​. Analyticity supplies the continuous first partial derivatives needed by this Green's theorem proof. The requirement that the enclosed region contain no singularities is essential.
