For a nonterminating parity series, ∣an+2​/an​∣→1, so the ratio test in x2 gives R=1​. The regular singular points at ±1 explain the natural obstruction.
There is an important exception to an unconditional radius-one answer. If k2=m2 for an integer m≥0, the series of parity m terminates at degree m and is a multiple of a Chebyshev polynomial. Its radius of convergence is R=∞​; the other independent parity solution still has radius one. For m=0 the even solution is constant. Any nonzero combination containing the nonterminating solution has radius one. These are the full power series of the Chebyshev differential equation, including the terminating cases.
