In the lower-frequency mode, the masses move vertically in phase, with the lower mass having the larger amplitude. In the higher-frequency mode they move oppositely, with lower-to-upper amplitude ratio 1/φ. Their Euclidean inner product is zero because 1+φ(−1/φ)=0; since M=mI, this is also mass-matrix orthogonality of normal modes.
