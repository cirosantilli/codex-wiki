For sufficiently smooth functions satisfying the same boundary conditions, integration by parts gives the boundary form
⟨f,Lg⟩−⟨Lf,g⟩​=∫−LL​[−f∗(pg′)′+(pf∗′)′g]dx=[p(f∗′g−f∗g′)]−LL​.​
(5)
The stated endpoint relation annihilates this expression. This proves the required self-adjoint differential operator identity; for the usual regular Sturm-Liouville problem, separated real boundary conditions give a self-adjoint realization on its domain.
In this problem the transformed diffusive flux is J=−e−λtpy′. Thus the physical boundary conditions are
py′(±L)=0,​
(6)
equivalently y′(±L)=0 when the endpoint values of p are positive. In particular pf′=pg′=0 at the endpoints, which makes the boundary form vanish directly. The inner product used here is the ordinary integral; the weight w enters the generalized eigenvalue problem, not this boundary-form calculation.
