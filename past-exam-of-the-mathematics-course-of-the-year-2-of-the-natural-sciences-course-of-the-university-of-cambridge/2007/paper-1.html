The matrix trace is TrA=∑i​Aii​. Expanding the matrix products gives Tr(AB)=∑i,j​Aij​Bji​=∑j,i​Bji​Aij​=Tr(BA). Thus the cyclic property of the trace gives zero trace to every commutator. If AB−BA=I, taking the trace would give 0=n, impossible for positive finite n over the real or complex field.
For real A,B, let p(λ)=det(A+λB), a real polynomial of degree at most n. Since p(i)=detC=0, it is not the zero polynomial. It has at most finitely many real roots of a polynomial, so A+λB is an invertible matrix for some real λ, in fact for all but finitely many.
Finally write the invertible intertwiner as R=A+iB. The relation PR=RQ and the reality of P,Q give PA=AQ and PB=BQ. Choose a real λ for which S=A+λB is invertible. Then PS=SQ, so P=SQS−1. This proves that complex similarity of real matrices implies real similarity.
