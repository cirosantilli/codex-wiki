If f is analytic on and inside a positively oriented simple closed contour except for finitely many isolated poles zj​ strictly inside it, then the residue theorem states
∮C​f(z)dz=2πizj​ inside C∑​Resz=zj​​f.​
(81)
There must be no singularity on the contour. More generally each residue is multiplied by the contour's winding number about its point.
