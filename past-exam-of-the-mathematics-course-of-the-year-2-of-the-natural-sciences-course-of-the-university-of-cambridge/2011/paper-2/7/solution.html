In the lowest normal mode, all three masses move in phase, with the lower masses moving about 2.414 times as far as the bar. In the antisymmetric normal mode, the bar stays still and the lower masses move equally in opposite directions; their changing forces on the bar cancel. In the highest normal mode, the lower masses move together against the bar, with magnitude about 0.414 of its displacement. Any small motion is a superposition of these three modes.
