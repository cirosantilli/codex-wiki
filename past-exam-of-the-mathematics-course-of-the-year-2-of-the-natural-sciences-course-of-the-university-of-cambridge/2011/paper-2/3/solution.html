For the two insulating walls, successive Euclidean reflections in x=b and x=−b generate sources of strength q at (2nb,0) for every integer n. Only n=0 lies inside the physical strip. This is the Neumann image lattice for a point source in a strip. Set

In the corresponding x-derivative, the images pair as n and 1−n at x=b, and as n and −1−n at x=−b. Their opposite horizontal separations make the derivatives cancel. Thus both homogeneous Neumann boundary conditions hold. The constructed solution is even in y; without a condition at infinity the side conditions alone would allow further homogeneous harmonic functions.
