The radius of convergence one is the distance from the expansion point to the nearest singular points of a differential equation, x=±1. Convergence of function values at those points does not make the solutions analytic there; endpoint derivatives can diverge. The terminating Chebyshev polynomial is an exception with infinite radius of convergence. For example, at m=0 the other solution is arcsinx, whose endpoint values are finite while its first derivative diverges.
