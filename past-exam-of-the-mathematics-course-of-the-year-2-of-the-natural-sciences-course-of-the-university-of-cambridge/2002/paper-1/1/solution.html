Coordinate surfaces and sketch. Adding and subtracting the defining relations gives s=u2+v2 and x=u2−v2. Since y2=s2−x2, we have y2=4u2v2. For u2=c>0, eliminate v2 to obtain
x=c−4cy2​.
(1)
This is a parabolic cylinder parallel to the z axis, opening towards negative x, with cross-sectional vertex (c,0) and focus at the origin. Similarly, v2=d>0 gives
x=4dy2​−d,
(2)
a parabolic cylinder opening towards positive x, with vertex (−d,0) and the same focus. The degenerate u2=0 surface is the half-plane y=0,x≤0; v2=0 gives y=0,x≥0. Constant w gives the horizontal planes z=w.
Thus the requested cross-sections consist of x=1−y2/4, x=2−y2/8, x=y2/4−1, x=y2/8−2 and the two horizontal half-axes. The parabolas of the two families meet orthogonally.
Signed coordinates. Take u≥0 and choose the sign of v to agree with y away from y=0. Then the Cartesian coordinates are
x=u2−v2,y=2uv,z=w.​
(3)
Every coordinate triple gives exactly one point. For u>0, v and y have the same sign. A fixed signed v=0 selects the upper or lower half of its parabolic cylinder; the entire cylinder corresponds to fixed v2. On the cut u=0, the two values v=±−x​ represent the same point of the negative x half-axis. This boundary identification is unavoidable: there y=0 even when v=0, so the sign prescription is understood by approaching from either side of the cut. The interior u>0 gives a unique coordinate chart off that half-axis.
Orthogonality and metric. Differentiate the position vector:
ru​=(2u,2v,0),rv​=(−2v,2u,0),rw​=(0,0,1).
(4)
The pairwise dot products vanish. Their lengths give the scale factors of orthogonal coordinates and hence the Riemannian metric:
hu​=hv​=2u2+v2​,hw​=1,dl2=4(u2+v2)(du2+dv2)+dw2.​
(5)
These are parabolic cylindrical coordinates. The differential calculation is regular for u2+v2>0; the z axis is the coordinate singularity where the two planar tangent vectors vanish.
Laplacian. Put h=2u2+v2​. The physical components of the gradient of a w-independent scalar field are ϕu​/h,ϕv​/h,0. Applying the divergence formula cancels the two equal scale factors of orthogonal coordinates inside the derivatives:
∇2ϕ=h21​[∂u​(hhϕu​​)+∂v​(hhϕv​​)].
(6)
Therefore, away from the coordinate singularity,
∇2ϕ=4(u2+v2)ϕuu​+ϕvv​​.​
