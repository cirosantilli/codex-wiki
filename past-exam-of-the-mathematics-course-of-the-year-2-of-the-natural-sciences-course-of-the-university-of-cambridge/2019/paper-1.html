For an oriented piecewise smooth surface S with boundary curve C=∂S, and a continuously differentiable vector field F near S, Stokes theorem states
∫S​(∇×F)⋅ndS=∮C​F⋅dr.​

The adjoint operator A†, when it exists, is defined by
⟨Ax,y⟩=⟨x,A†y⟩​
(38)
