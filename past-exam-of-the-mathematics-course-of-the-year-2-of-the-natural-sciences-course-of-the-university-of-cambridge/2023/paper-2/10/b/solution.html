The relations in (a) reduce every word in the generators to UaVb, with 0≤a<4 and b∈{0,1}. These eight matrices are distinct: the b=0 elements have third diagonal entry 1 and the b=1 elements have entry −1, while the four planar blocks are distinct in each family. Their multiplication is
(UaVb)(UcVd)=Ua+(−1)bcVb+d,​

with exponents reduced modulo four and two. This proves closure. The identity element is U0V0, the inverse of Ua is U−a, and each UaV is its own inverse. Associativity is inherited from matrix multiplication, proving the group axioms. This is the eight-element dihedral group D8​.
