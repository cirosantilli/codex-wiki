Apply the product rule to the vector field Φ∇Ψ−Ψ∇Φ:
∇⋅(Φ∇Ψ−Ψ∇Φ)=ΦΔΨ−ΨΔΦ.
(18)
The gradient cross terms cancel. Integrating and applying the divergence theorem with outward unit normal n proves Green's second identity:
∫V​(ΦΔΨ−ΨΔΦ)dV=∮S​(Φ∂n​Ψ−Ψ∂n​Φ)dS.​
(19)
Choose the Dirichlet Green function GD​(r,r′) with Δr​GD​=δ(r−r′) and GD​=0 on S. Taking Ψ=GD​ and using the Dirac delta distribution, the left side becomes Φ(r′) while the second boundary term vanishes. Hence
Φ(r′)=∮S​f(r)∂nr​​GD​(r,r′)dS.​
(20)
The plus sign is tied to the convention ΔGD​=+δ, whose free-space fundamental solution is negative in three dimensions. The singularity may equivalently be handled by excising a small sphere about r′ before applying the smooth identity.
