Discrete Sobolev norm (∥W∥1,h2​=h∑i=1N−1​∣Wi​∣2+h∑i=1N​∣Dh−​Wi​∣2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A discrete Sobolev norm combines weighted norms of nodal values and their finite differences. For a real sequence W0​,…,WN​ with zero endpoints, the displayed expression is a norm: it is the Euclidean norm of one vector containing both weighted lists, and zero norm forces all nodal values to vanish. It approximates the Sobolev space H1 norm. Endpoint weights or prescribed zero endpoints must be specified when calling a discrete expression a norm.
