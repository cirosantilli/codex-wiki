Horizontally integrated plume concentration (c(z,t)=∫C(x,z,t)dx)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Scalar transport Passive scalar
OurBigBook.com Words: 116 Articles: 1
The horizontally integrated plume concentration is the integral of a local concentration C across the plume. It measures scalar amount per unit vertical length and unit span, and its vertical integral is total scalar amount per unit span. Unlike a local concentration, its dimensions include one horizontal length. It must be distinguished from horizontally averaged plume concentration when a plume's width varies with height.
