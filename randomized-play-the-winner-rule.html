Randomized play-the-winner rule (RPW(a,b))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Clinical trial Response-adaptive randomization
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
In a two-treatment urn design, begin with a balls of each type, draw and replace a ball to allocate treatment, then add b balls of the same type after success or of the opposite type after failure. This response-adaptive randomization favours treatments with more observed successes. For RPW(1,1), type-0 probability after counts (s0​,f0​,s1​,f1​) is (1+s0​+f1​)/(2+s0​+f0​+s1​+f1​).
