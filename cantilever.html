Cantilever

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Elastic beam
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
A beam fixed at one end and free at the other. A transverse end force F gives bending moment M(x)=F(L−x) and, for a rectangular section, maximum bending stress 6FL/(wh2) at the fixed end. Longitudinal tension and compression are greatest on opposite outer fibres.
