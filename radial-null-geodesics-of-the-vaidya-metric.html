Radial null geodesics of the Vaidya metric (v=const,dr/dv=(1−2M/r)/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Vaidya metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 79 Articles: 1
The two radial null geodesic families are v=constant and dr/dv=(1−2M(v)/r)/2. The first has affine radius, while the second has tangent k=∂v​+(f/2)∂r​ obeying ∇k​k=(M/r2)k, so advanced time is generally a nonaffine parameter. An affine parameter λ for the outgoing family satisfies dlog(dv/dλ)/dv=−M/r2.
