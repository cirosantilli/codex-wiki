Automorphism-count divisibility theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
For a finite extension K/F, the automorphism group G=AutF​(K) has order dividing [K:F]. Indeed, Artin's theorem gives [K:KG]=∣G∣, while F⊆KG and the tower law gives the divisibility.
