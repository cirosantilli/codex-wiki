Ramification of a power map of the Riemann sphere (f(z)=zd,Rf​=2d−2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Riemann-Hurwitz formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 118
For an integer d≥2, extend f(z)=zd to a holomorphic map from the Riemann sphere to itself with f(∞)=∞. A nonzero finite regular value has d distinct preimages, so the degree of a holomorphic map is d. At zero the analytic ramification index is d. At infinity, use local coordinates u=1/z upstairs and v=1/f(z) downstairs: the same holomorphic map becomes v=ud, so e∞​=d too. Everywhere else the derivative dzd−1 is nonzero.
There are two distinct ramification points and two distinct branch values of a holomorphic map, but
Rf​=(d−1)+(d−1)=2d−2,−2=d(−2)+2d−2.
(115)
For d=3, the total ramification of a holomorphic map is four, not two. The local-circle panel shows three nearby preimages converging to the single ramification point. For d=1 the holomorphic map is the identity and neither zero nor infinity is ramified.
