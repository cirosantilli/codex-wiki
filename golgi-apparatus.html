Golgi apparatus

 Home Biology Biological cell Organelle
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 6
The Golgi apparatus modifies and sorts cargo arriving from the endoplasmic reticulum for delivery to other cellular destinations. The eponym refers to Camillo Golgi.
Its usual stacked organization is polarized: cargo enters on the cis side, encounters medial processing regions, and leaves through the trans side and trans-Golgi network. Golgi cisternae enclose a Golgi lumen separated from the surrounding cytosol. Processing changes cargo; sorting selects its destination.[ref]
This division of labor explains why reaching the Golgi apparatus does not imply being secreted: cargo can take different onward routes, while resident enzymes continue working there. The conventional route discussed here is part of secretory protein routing; many cytosol proteins never enter it.
