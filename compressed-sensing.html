Compressed sensing

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Sparse optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 19
Compressed sensing reconstructs a sparse vector, or an approximately sparse one, from fewer linear measurements than its ambient dimension. Basis pursuit replaces counting nonzero coordinates by convex ℓ1 minimization. A null space property characterizes exact uniform recovery, while a robust null space property controls errors from noise and nonsparse tails.
