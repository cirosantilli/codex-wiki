Consistent heuristic (h(s)≤c(s,a,s′)+h(s′))

 Home Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Computational problem Search problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A heuristic function is consistent if h(g)=0 at every goal and
h(s)≤c(s,a,s′)+h(s′)
(23)
for every permitted transition sa​s′ with step cost c(s,a,s′). Summing these inequalities along a finite route to a goal gives h(s)≤ its total cost. Taking the minimum over such routes proves that every consistent heuristic is an admissible heuristic.
