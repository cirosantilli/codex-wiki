Parallel transport (Pγ∇​)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle) Horizontal lift
OurBigBook.com Words: 339 Articles: 6
Parallel transport along a path γ sends an initial fiber vector to the endpoint of the unique horizontal lift through that vector. Reversing the path gives the inverse linear map.
