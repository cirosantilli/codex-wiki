Phase-diffusion coefficient from a Bloch cell problem (D=⟨w,L2​v0​+L1​v1​⟩)

Suppose a Bloch stability operator has expansion L(q)=L0​+iqL1​−q2L2​+⋯, with a simple neutral eigenfunction v0​ and an adjoint neutral function w normalized by ⟨w,v0​⟩=1. Expand v(q)=v0​+iqv1​+⋯ and λ(q)=icq−Dq2+⋯. The Fredholm alternative gives c=⟨w,L1​v0​⟩ and the cell problem L0​v1​=cv0​−L1​v0​, ⟨w,v1​⟩=0. Applying the adjoint solvability condition at second order gives the displayed phase-diffusion coefficient. Multiple translation phases or a conserved mean require a coupled matrix problem rather than this scalar formula.
