Mott criterion (nc1/3​aB∗​≃0.25)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Phase transition Metal-insulator transition
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
The approximate donor metal-insulator threshold is often written nc1/3​aB∗​≃0.25. It estimates when overlap and screening destroy isolated donor localization. The numerical coefficient is empirical and not a universal exact result; disorder, compensation and anisotropy can shift the threshold.[ref]
