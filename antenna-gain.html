Antenna gain (G(r)=4πr2Sr​/Pin​)

 Home Electrical engineering Antenna (radio)
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 1
Antenna gain compares the far-field energy flux in a specified direction with the flux that an isotropic radiator would produce using the same input power. Thus Sr​=GPin​/(4πr2). Gain includes radiation efficiency; it must be distinguished from a voltage or current amplifier gain and from directivity when losses matter.
