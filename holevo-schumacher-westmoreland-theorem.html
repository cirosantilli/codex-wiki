For a finite-dimensional memoryless quantum channel,
C(Λ)=limn→∞​n1​χ∗(Λ⊗n).
(12)
The Holevo bound controls information obtainable from an output ensemble, while collective decoding makes rates below the corresponding coding expression achievable. The Holevo capacity needs regularization because input states can be entangled across channel uses. Watrous's channel-coding chapter states and develops the classical-capacity theorem.
