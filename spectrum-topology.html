Spectrum (topology, E)

OurBigBook.com Words: 924 Articles: 24
A sequential model of a topological spectrum consists of based spaces Ej​ and structure maps S1∧Ej​→Ej+1​. Indexed models use finite-dimensional subspaces of a universe for spectra and structure maps SW⊖V∧E(V)→E(W). Different standard models present the same stable homotopy category.
