Late-time CMB-matter cross correlation

 ... Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Photon temperature multipole Photon monopole Sachs-Wolfe combination Integrated Sachs-Wolfe effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
Cross-correlating a cosmic microwave background temperature map with a low-redshift matter tracer can isolate a late gravitational contribution from the much larger primordial anisotropy. Decaying potential wells generally produce a positive correlation of matter overdensity with temperature. Tracer bias, overlapping redshift distributions, foreground emission, masks and chance correlations limit the inference. Potential evolution also depends on curvature and gravity assumptions, so this is not a dark-energy measurement independent of a cosmological model.
