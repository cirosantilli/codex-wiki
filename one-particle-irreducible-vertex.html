One-particle-irreducible vertex (Γ(n))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Effective action
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
A functional derivative of the quantum effective action gives an amputated one-particle-irreducible correlation function. Its perturbative graphs stay connected after cutting any single internal propagator. A free quadratic effective action has no vertices of order greater than two; interactions generate higher vertices.
