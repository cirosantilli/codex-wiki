Torsion-freeness of the formal group over Qp for odd p (E(pZp​) is torsion-free)

OurBigBook.com Words: 81
For odd p, a one-dimensional formal group law over Zp​ has a formal logarithm logF​(T)=T+∑j≥2​cj​Tj/j with cj​∈Zp​. This follows by integrating its integral invariant differential. For t∈pZp​∖{0}, vp​(cj​tj/j)>vp​(t) for j≥2. Hence the series converges and vp​(logF​(t))=vp​(t), so its kernel is zero. As it is a homomorphism into a characteristic-zero additive group, there is no nonzero torsion. In particular, at an odd prime of good reduction over Q, reduction injects the whole rational torsion subgroup, including its p-primary part.
