Riemann–Roch theorem for algebraic surfaces (χ(OX​(D))=χ(OX​)+21​D⋅(D−KX​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 65 Articles: 1
For a Cartier divisor on a smooth projective surface, the displayed formula computes its Euler characteristic of a coherent sheaf. Combined with Serre duality, it turns intersection numbers into lower bounds for section spaces.
