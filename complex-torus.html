Complex torus (Cn/Λ)

 ... Geometry and topology Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 491 Articles: 10
A complex torus is a quotient Cn/Λ by a discrete subgroup Λ≅Z2n spanning Cn over R. Translation-invariant differential forms descend to the quotient.
