Quotient Hermitian metric (hQ​([v],[w])=hE​((I−πS​)v,(I−πS​)w))

OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For a smooth subbundle S of a Hermitian vector bundle E, orthogonal projection identifies E/S smoothly with S⊥. The quotient metric is hQ​([v],[w])=hE​((I−πS​)v,(I−πS​)w). It is independent of representatives and positive definite. For a holomorphic subbundle the resulting smooth quotient metric is defined on the holomorphic quotient, but the orthogonal identification itself need not be holomorphic.
