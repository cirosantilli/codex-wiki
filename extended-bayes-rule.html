Extended Bayes rule

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Bayes estimator Bayes decision rule
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
A decision rule δ is extended Bayes if, for every ε>0, there is a proper prior π with r(π,δ)≤r∗(π)+ε, where r∗(π) is the infimum Bayes risk. Equivalently a sequence of priors makes the rule's excess Bayes risk tend to zero. If its risk function is also the finite constant c, then r∗(π)≥c−ε, so every rule has maximum risk at least c−ε. Letting ε→0 proves that this equalizer rule is minimax.
