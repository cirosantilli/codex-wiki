Comoving volume (V=a3Vc​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Scale factor (cosmology) Comoving coordinate
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 2
A comoving volume has fixed boundaries in comoving coordinates. Its physical volume grows as the cube of the scale factor. In a homogeneous adiabatic gas, cosmological entropy conservation makes sV constant.
