Electric dipole transition

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Time-dependent perturbation theory Electric-dipole interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 285 Articles: 6
An electric dipole transition is a transition driven, in the long-wavelength approximation, by the electric-dipole interaction Hint​=−d⋅E(t). Its leading amplitude is proportional to a matrix element of the electric dipole moment between the initial and final states. A vanishing element gives an electric-dipole selection rule; a nonzero element alone does not ensure a large probability, since the drive frequency, duration and strength also matter. For a charged quantum harmonic oscillator driven along its axis, x=ℏ/(2mω)​(a+a†) gives the single-quantum rule Δn=±1.
