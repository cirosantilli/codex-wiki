Ideal of a Lie algebra

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 60 Articles: 1
A vector subspace I⊆g is an ideal when [g,I]⊆I. It is therefore the kernel of a Lie-algebra quotient map.
