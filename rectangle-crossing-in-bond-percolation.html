Rectangle crossing in bond percolation (hp​(m,n))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Percolation theory Bond percolation
OurBigBook.com Words: 123 Articles: 1
For the induced square lattice subgraph in [0,m]×[0,n], hp​(m,n) is the probability of an open graph path from its left boundary to its right boundary under independent bond percolation of density p. Width and height count lattice spacings. Dual bond percolation exchanges failure with a top-to-bottom dual crossing of dimensions (m−1)×(n+1), with the usual exterior boundary vertices.
