Four-leg vertex of a factorial-normalized scalar interaction (V4​=iλ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram Feynman rule
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
An interaction Lint​=+λϕ4/4! produces a four-leg momentum-space vertex iλ. The 4! assignments of external legs cancel the coefficient factorial. With a negative interaction sign the vertex is −iλ. Graph Feynman-diagram symmetry factors are a separate combinatorial issue.
