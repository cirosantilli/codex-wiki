Volume-preserving vector field (LX​ω=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form Volume form
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 1
A vector field preserves a chosen volume form ω when its local flow pulls ω back to itself. Infinitesimally this is LX​ω=0, using the Lie derivative. The condition depends on the chosen volume form.
