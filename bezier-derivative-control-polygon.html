Bézier derivative control polygon (C(r)=(n−r)!n!​∑j=0n−r​ΔrPj​Bjn−r​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Computer aided geometric design Bézier curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 87 Articles: 1
For a degree-n Bézier curve, the rth derivative has degree at most n−r and control points n!ΔrPj​/(n−r)!. The first derivative follows by differentiating the Bernstein basis and shifting one summation index; iteration proves the general formula. A parameter interval of length h multiplies these derivative controls by h−r.
