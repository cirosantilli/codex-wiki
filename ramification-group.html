Ramification group (Gi​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field
OurBigBook.com Words: 356 Articles: 12
For a finite Galois extension of local fields, the lower ramification groups measure how closely automorphisms fix the valuation ring. The inertia group is G0​, and G1​ is the wild inertia group.
