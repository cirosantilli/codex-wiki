Reciprocal climate-sensitivity uncertainty (ΔT2×​=F2×​/λ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Climate change Climate model Climate sensitivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
A symmetric positive uncertainty interval for a stabilizing feedback coefficient produces asymmetric uncertainty in its reciprocal. With both cooling magnitude x and forcing magnitude f uncertain, constant-sensitivity lines are rays x/f=constant; tangents from the origin to a Gaussian covariance ellipse give a one-parameter profile interval for the ratio. A unit-radius two-variable covariance ellipse is not itself a 68 percent joint confidence region.
