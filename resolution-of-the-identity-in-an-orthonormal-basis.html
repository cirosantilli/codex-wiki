Resolution of the identity in an orthonormal basis (I=∑j=1n​uj​ujT​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Inner product Orthonormal set Orthonormal basis
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
For a real orthonormal basis u1​,…,un​, each vector has expansion x=∑j​uj​(ujT​x). Since this holds for every x, the sum of the rank-one orthogonal projection matrices uj​ujT​ is the identity matrix. Their pairwise products vanish for distinct basis vectors. For a complex inner product space, replace the transpose by the conjugate transpose.
