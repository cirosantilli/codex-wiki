Electrical conductor

 ... Electric charge Electric current Electrical resistance and conductance Electrical resistance Ohm's law Electrical conductivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
An electrical conductor supports mobile charge carriers and hence an electric current under a field. In a band description, a partially filled band or available overlapping bands supply nearby accessible states. The contrast with a band insulator depends on band filling as well as spectral gaps; semiconductors provide carriers through thermal excitation or doping.
