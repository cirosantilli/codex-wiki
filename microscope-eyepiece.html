Microscope eyepiece (Me​=D/fe​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Optical microscope
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
The viewing optical lens assembly that acts as a magnifier of the intermediate microscope image. With that image at its front focal plane, the outgoing light rays from each point are parallel. In the small-angle approximation, the viewed angle is hi​/fe​, versus hi​/D for the same image-sized object at reference distance D, giving Me​=D/fe​.[ref]
A finite-distance virtual image requires a different eyepiece conjugate. In the ideal thin lens model, with the eye close to the eyepiece and the virtual image at distance D, the angular magnification becomes 1+D/fe​. State the viewing condition before multiplying by the objective's transverse magnification; the relaxed-eye example above uses D/fe​.
