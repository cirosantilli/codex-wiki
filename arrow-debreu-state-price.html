Arrow–Debreu state price (qs​=ps​Zs​)

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Risk-neutral measure Martingale deflator State-price density
OurBigBook.com Words: 127 Articles: 1
On a finite state space, the Arrow–Debreu state price is the current price of a payoff equal to one in state s and zero elsewhere. It equals the physical state probability times the state-price density in that state. With a riskless asset of deterministic prices B0​,B1​, the Arrow state prices sum to B0​/B1​. Normalizing them gives the risk-neutral probabilities, while division by the physical probabilities instead recovers the pricing density.
