Full-weighting restriction (R=2−dPT)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Multigrid method
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
Full-weighting restriction transfers a fine-grid residual to a coarser grid by local averaging. In one dimension its weights are (1,2,1)/4; in two dimensions they are the tensor-product stencil (121​242​121​)/16. For linear or bilinear interpolation P and coarsening by two in every direction, it equals 2−dPT in unweighted coordinates. This scaling makes restriction the adjoint of interpolation for mesh-weighted inner products.
