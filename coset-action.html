Coset action (g⋅(aH)=(ga)H)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group action
OurBigBook.com Words: 533 Articles: 1
A group acts on the left cosets of a subgroup by left multiplication; its kernel is the core of that subgroup.
Let H≤G be a subgroup. The notation G/H here means the set of left cosets {aH:a∈G}, even when H is not a normal subgroup. Define
g⋅(aH)=(ga)H,ρH​:G⟶Sym(G/H).
(52)
This gives a group action without needing a quotient group structure on G/H.[ref]
The representative check is essential: if aH=bH, then a=bh for some h∈H, and (ga)H=(gbh)H=(gb)H. Thus equal input cosets give equal output cosets. The identity element fixes every coset, and associativity gives g1​⋅(g2​⋅aH)=(g1​g2​)⋅aH. Each induced map is a permutation, with inverse induced by g−1. This is a transitive group action because ba−1 sends aH to bH.
The stabilizer subgroup of H is H itself. At a general coset, the stabilizer subgroup and the group kernel are
GaH​=aHa−1,kerρH​=⋂a∈G​aHa−1=CoreG​(H).
(53)
Consequently the coset action is a faithful group action exactly when the subgroup core is trivial.[ref]
Indeed, g⋅(aH)=aH exactly when a−1ga∈H, or g∈aHa−1. Intersecting these membership conditions over all cosets gives the group kernel. Conjugation permutes the subgroups in this intersection, making it a normal subgroup of G contained in H. Conversely, any normal subgroup of G contained in H lies in every aHa−1. This explains why the subgroup core is the largest such normal subgroup: it is exactly the portion of H invisible at every label, rather than merely at the label H.
For a normal subgroup H, every aHa−1 equals H, so kerρH​=H. For H={e}, the construction is the regular group action behind Cayley's theorem. Between these extremes, a nontrivial subgroup with trivial subgroup core can give a faithful group action on fewer labels. If the index of a subgroup is finite, [G:H]=n, the first isomorphism theorem gives
G/CoreG​(H)≅imρH​≤Sn​.
(54)
Thus a subgroup of small index of a subgroup exposes a small permutation group associated with G. The two examples below and in nonfaithful coset action of the symmetric group on three letters show exactly which information can survive.
