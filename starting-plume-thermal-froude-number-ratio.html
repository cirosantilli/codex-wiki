Starting-plume thermal Froude-number ratio

For a spherical buoyant thermal fed only through the moving top of a Boussinesq point-source plume, put λ=R/B and B=(6α/5)Z. Relative inflow πB2(wp​−Z˙) and V˙=4πR2R˙ imply
wp​Z˙​=5+24αλ35​.
(10)
The ratio of Froude numbers is 5/[λ​(5+24αλ3)] when both use plume reduced gravity gp​. Normalizing the thermal with its own gt​=9gp​/4 multiplies this by 2/3. These two definitions must not be interchanged when stating bounds.
