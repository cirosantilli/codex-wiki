Executive function

 Home Science Psychology Cognitive psychology
OurBigBook.com Words: 369 Articles: 10
Processes supporting goal-directed control, including maintaining and updating relevant information, inhibiting competing responses and changing task sets. Their importance depends on what a task requires: an initially effortful procedure may need less executive control after practice. The unity-and-diversity framework distinguishes correlated components without equating a single test score with a pure executive capacity.[ref]
