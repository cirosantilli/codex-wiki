Polarization field of a conductivity inclusion (P=(a−a0​I)∇u)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Composite material Effective conductivity Conductivity homogenization
OurBigBook.com Words: 276 Articles: 5
Relative to a uniform reference conductivity a0​, this polarization is the excess positive conductivity flux. The equation becomes a0​Δu+∇⋅P=0. For a prescribed mean gradient E, averaging gives a∗E=a0​E+⟨P⟩. This sign convention differs from the physical heat-flux sign.
