Polytrope of index one (P=Kρ2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure Stellar polytrope
OurBigBook.com Words: 251 Articles: 3
A stellar polytrope with polytropic index one has ∇Φ=−2K∇ρ in hydrostatic equilibrium. Combining this with the Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity gives the Helmholtz equation
∇2ρ+k2ρ=0,k2=K2πG​.
(8)
The spherical solution regular at the origin is ρ(r)=ρc​sin(kr)/(kr). Its first zero gives R=π/k=πK/(2G)​, independent of central mass density, and ρˉ​/ρc​=3/π2.
