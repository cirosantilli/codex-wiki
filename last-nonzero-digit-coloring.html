Last nonzero digit coloring

 ... Area of mathematics Combinatorics Ramsey theory Partition regular matrix Columns property P-adic columns lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For a prime number p, write a positive integer as x=pvp​(x)u with p∤u. Its last nonzero base-p digit is umodp, an element of {1,…,p−1}. Assigning this digit as the color gives the last nonzero digit coloring used in the necessity proof of Rado's theorem.
