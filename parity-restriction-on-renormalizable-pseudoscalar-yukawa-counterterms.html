Parity restriction on renormalizable pseudoscalar Yukawa counterterms (Lct​⊃ϕ4,Lct​⊃ϕ,ϕ3)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Yukawa interaction Pseudoscalar Yukawa interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For an unbroken parity symmetry in quantum field theory with ϕ a pseudoscalar, pure-scalar local counterterms of mass dimension at most four are even in ϕ. A four-vertex fermion loop requires a quartic counterterm, even when the initial Lagrangian density shows only a Yukawa interaction. The one-point and three-point pure-boson vertices vanish at all orders: their available external four-momenta cannot form a parity-odd Lorentz scalar.
