Closed system

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Thermodynamic system
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
A closed system exchanges no matter with its surroundings, but can exchange energy through heat or work. A sealed cylinder with a moving piston is a typical idealized example. “Closed” fixes the material inventory, rather than the volume or the internal energy. An insulated cylinder can receive compression work; a rigid sealed vessel can receive heat. Neither is necessarily an isolated system.
