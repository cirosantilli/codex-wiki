Two-loop sextic counterterm in quartic Yukawa theory (δλ6​∝g4)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Yukawa interaction Quartic Yukawa interaction in three spacetime dimensions
OurBigBook.com Words: 98
Four quartic Yukawa vertices on a fermion loop with one internal scalar chord give six external scalar legs, two loops and overall logarithmic degree. The coefficient is nonzero: in a constant scalar background v, the induced cubic Yukawa coupling is y=gv and the fermion mass is μ=m+gv2/2. The two-loop sunset diagram in the quantum effective potential has a logarithmic pole proportional to y2(4μ2−B2), where B2 is the background scalar squared mass. Its v6 term is g4v6 when the tree sextic coupling vanishes. Thus zero renormalized sextic coupling at one scale does not eliminate the sextic counterterm or make that zero stable under renormalization.
