Driven two-level Rabi model (Hrot​=ℏ[δσz​+Ω(cosϕσx​+sinϕσy​)]/2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 792 Articles: 7
Choose an isolated pair of energy levels, with ground state ∣g⟩=(0,1)T and excited state ∣e⟩=(1,0)T. Use the Pauli matrices in the ordered basis (∣e⟩,∣g⟩), so σz​=diag(1,−1). After subtracting the mean energy, prescribe the laboratory Hamiltonian operator
Hlab​(t)=2ℏω0​​σz​+ℏΩcos(ωt+ϕ)σx​,δ=ω0​−ω,Ω≥0.
(76)
The unperturbed energy gap is ℏω0​, the drive has angular frequency ω, and ϕ fixes its oscillation phase at the chosen time origin. All of ω0​, ω, the Rabi angular frequency Ω and the Rabi detuning δ are in radians per second; ordinary frequency is angular frequency divided by 2π. In particular, Ω here is the coefficient multiplying the cosine in Hlab​/ℏ, not twice that coefficient.
Write ∣ψlab​⟩=R(t)∣ψrot​⟩ with R(t)=exp(−iωtσz​/2). Transforming the Schrödinger equation gives Hrot​=R†Hlab​R−iℏR†R˙. The rotating-wave approximation drops terms at angular frequency 2ω, leaving
Hrot​=2ℏ​K,K=(δΩeiϕ​Ωe−iϕ−δ​),Ωeff​=Ω2+δ2​.
(77)
This approximation requires Ω,∣δ∣≪ω0​,ω, and an interaction duration over which omitted corrections remain small. It also assumes that other energy levels can be neglected. Resonance alone does not justify either assumption. An exactly co-rotating drive has no discarded counter-rotating component; the linearly oscillating laboratory drive above does.
The Pauli matrix multiplication law gives K2=Ωeff2​I. Separating even and odd terms of the matrix exponential therefore proves
Urot​(t)=Icos2Ωeff​t​−iΩeff​K​sin2Ωeff​t​,
(78)
with the continuous limit U=I when Ω=δ=0. Starting from ∣g⟩, the upper probability amplitude is −ie−iϕ(Ω/Ωeff​)sin(Ωeff​t/2). The Born rule gives
Pg→e​(t)=Ω2+δ2Ω2​sin2(2Ω2+δ2​t​).
(79)
The rotating transformation only changes the quantum phases of these basis states, so their laboratory populations are the same. Rabi detuning tilts the rotation axis of the Bloch vector: it increases the oscillation rate to Ωeff​ but caps the transferred population at Ω2/(Ω2+δ2). A fixed drive oscillation phase cancels from this single-pulse population when the initial state is ∣g⟩. It still fixes the relative quantum phase of a resulting quantum superposition and matters when another pulse supplies a phase reference.
