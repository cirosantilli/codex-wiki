Frame homomorphism (h:A→B)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Complete Heyting algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A map between frames preserving every join and every finite meet, including the empty join 0 and empty meet 1. Such a map is automatically order-preserving. Inverse image under a continuous map is an example.
