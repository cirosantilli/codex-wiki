Uniform material thinning under planar-sheet tension (h=h0​(z0​)−Δ(t),Δ(t)=∫0t​F(s)ds/(4μ))

For a thin planar viscous sheet with zero effective gravity and prescribed tensile force F(t) per unit transverse width, force balance gives 4μhwz​=F(t). Conservation of mass gives Dt​h=−hwz​=−F/(4μ), proving uniform subtraction from the initial material thickness. The material map satisfies dz/dz0​=h0​/[h0​−Δ]. Thus the same material thinning creates greater extension wherever the initial sheet is thinner.
