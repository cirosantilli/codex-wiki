Chromatic number of the half-density binomial random graph (χ(G(n,1/2))∼2log2​nn​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Binomial random graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
For the binomial random graph G(n,1/2), the chromatic number is (1+o(1))n/(2log2​n) with high probability. A first moment method bounds the independence number above. Edge-disjoint clique packing in the complement graph, followed by an edge-exposure martingale and a union bound over moderately large vertex sets, supplies independent sets for greedy colouring by removing independent sets.
