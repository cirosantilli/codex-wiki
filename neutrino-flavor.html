Neutrino flavor

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Lepton number Neutrino
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
A neutrino label defined by the associated charged lepton in weak charged current production or detection. A produced flavour state can evolve through neutrino oscillation.
