Plastid

 Home Biology Biological cell Organelle
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A plastid is an organelle of the lineage that includes chloroplasts. A chloroplast is a photosynthetic plastid; the terms are not interchangeable because some plastids are nonphotosynthetic. Endosymbiotic theory connects plastid origins to cyanobacteria, while endosymbiotic gene transfer and organelle protein import explain genetic and functional integration with the host.
