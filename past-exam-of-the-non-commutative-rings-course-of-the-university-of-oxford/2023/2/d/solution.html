Take D=R^. We first check that this noncommutative formal power series ring is a noncommutative domain. For nonzero F,G∈D, let p,q be the smallest lengths of words with nonzero coefficients in F,G, respectively. Their degree-p and degree-q parts Fp​,Gq​ are nonzero homogeneous polynomials in the free associative algebra.
Choose words u,v with nonzero coefficients in Fp​,Gq​. In Fp​Gq​, the coefficient of uv is the product of those two coefficients: a word of length p+q has a unique splitting into a prefix of length p and a suffix of length q. This product is nonzero in Q. The degree-p+q part of FG is exactly Fp​Gq​, since all lower parts of F and G vanish. Thus FG=0, proving that D is a noncommutative domain.
By the defining property of the free associative algebra, there is a ring homomorphism
f:R⟶D,f(x1​)=1+x1​,f(x2​)=2+x2​.
(25)
The same substitution defines a ring automorphism of R, with inverse x1​↦x1​−1, x2​↦x2​−2. Composing this ring automorphism with the natural inclusion R↪D proves that f is injective.
Finally,
f(x1​+x2​)f(x1​)​=3+x1​+x2​,=1+x1​,​f(x1​−x2​)f(x2​)​=−1+x1​−x2​,=2+x2​.​
(26)
Their constant terms are 3,−1,1,2, respectively, all nonzero. By the unit criterion for noncommutative formal power series proved in c, all four elements are units. Therefore D and f have every required property. The embedding changes the images of the generators, so the failure of the original monomial left Ore set in b presents no obstruction.
