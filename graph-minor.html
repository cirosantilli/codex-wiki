Graph minor (H≼G)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 6
A graph minor is obtained by deleting vertices, deleting edges, and performing edge contractions. Equivalently, its vertices can be represented by disjoint nonempty connected sets of vertices in the original graph, with an edge joining two sets whenever the corresponding vertices are adjacent. These are branch sets of a graph minor.
