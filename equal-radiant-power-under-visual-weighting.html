An original declared model makes the distinction visible without substituting invented numbers for the photopic luminous efficiency function. Define a triangular weight
w(λ)=max(0,1−150nm∣λ−555nm∣​),Φv​=(683lmW−1)∫w(λ)Φe,λ​(λ)dλ.
(12)
This w is an illustrative function, not a fit or table of V. Three ideal spectral lines, each carrying radiant flux 1mW, at 450, 555 and 650nm have weights 3/10, 1 and 11/30. Their model weighted outputs are respectively 0.2049, 0.6830 and approximately 0.25043lm. These are model calculations, not standard luminous fluxes or measured data. For mixtures of these lines the model outputs add, just as the integral adds independent spectral contributions.
