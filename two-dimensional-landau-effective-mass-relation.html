Two-dimensional Landau effective-mass relation (m∗/m=1+F1​/2)

For an isotropic spinless continuum Fermi liquid with the Principle of Galilean relativity, let D∗=Am∗/(2πℏ2) be the total density of states and use a Landau quasiparticle energy functional whose double sum has no explicit 1/A. Then
mm∗​=1+D∗∫02π​2πdϕ​f(ϕ)cosϕ.
(14)
Indeed, a small boost has radial displacement p0​cosθ. The one-quasiparticle term costs Np02​/(2m∗), and the quadratic interaction multiplies that by D∗∫fcosϕdϕ/(2π), since ∫cos(θ+ϕ)cosθdθ=πcosϕ. Matching the Galilean boost energy of an interacting gas proves the formula. With Fourier series D∗f=F0​+∑ℓ≥1​Fℓ​cos(ℓϕ), the angular average is F1​/2. If the functional instead contains 1/A, use its intensive kernel Af and the density of states per area.
