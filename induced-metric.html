Induced metric

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Riemannian manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
An immersion into a Riemannian manifold inherits a metric by taking ambient inner products of tangent vectors. For a parametrized curve r(u) in Euclidean space, the induced metric coefficient is g=∂u​r⋅∂u​r.
