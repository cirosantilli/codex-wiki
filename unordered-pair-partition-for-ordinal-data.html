Unordered-pair partition for ordinal data ((2n​)=C+D+TX​+TY​+TXY​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Categorical variable Ordinal categorical variable
OurBigBook.com Words: 126
For n observations of two ordered variables, every unordered pair is concordant, discordant, tied only on the first variable, tied only on the second variable, or tied on both. Consequently
(2n​)=C+D+TX​+TY​+TXY​.
(27)
The proof is an exhaustive partition: if neither coordinate is tied the two nonzero comparison signs agree or disagree; otherwise exactly one or both coordinates are tied. For contingency-table counts nij​ with row totals ni+​ and column totals n+j​,
TXY​=∑ij​(2nij​​),TX​=∑i​(2ni+​​)−TXY​,TY​=∑j​(2n+j​​)−TXY​.
(28)
Two records tied on both variables belong to each marginal tie total, so subtracting them once from each produces the exclusive TX​,TY​. Using incompatible exclusive and inclusive conventions corrupts Somers' D and Kendall's tau-b even if the untied concordances are correct. The identities also provide a direct arithmetic check on a printed statistical summary.
