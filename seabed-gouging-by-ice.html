Seabed gouging by ice

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A moving ice keel can gouge sediment when its draft exceeds water depth. In an exponential ridge-draft model, a point crossed by ice at relative speed ∣V∣ experiences candidate encounters at rate ∣V∣μexp[−(max(D,h0​)−h0​)/(hm​−h0​)]. Actual erosion also depends on grounding, deformation of the keel and sediment strength.
