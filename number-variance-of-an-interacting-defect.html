Number variance of an interacting defect (Varn=kB​T∂μ​nˉ)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum magnetism Hubbard model On-site Coulomb repulsion Single-orbital interacting defect
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
With z=eβ(μ−ϵ), q=e−βU and Ξ=1+2z+qz2, direct four-state averaging gives
Varn=Ξ22z(1+2qz+qz2)​=kB​T∂μ​nˉ.
(89)
For βU≫1 it has two separated peaks of height 1/4 at the spin-degeneracy-shifted charge steps. At the lower bare crossing μ=ϵ, three microstates share equal limiting weight, giving nˉ=2/3 and variance 2/9. The upper bare crossing similarly gives mean 4/3 and variance 2/9. Between crossings occupation is pinned near one and fluctuations are suppressed. The identity is grand-canonical; fixing total particle number would change the fluctuation question.
