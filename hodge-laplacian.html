Hodge Laplacian (Δ=dδ+δd)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form Hodge star operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 410 Articles: 10
The Hodge Laplacian acts on differential forms by Δ=dδ+δd. On a compact manifold,
⟨Δα,α⟩=∥dα∥2+∥δα∥2.
(16)
It is one of the Laplace operators in differential geometry.
