Point group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 865 Articles: 24
The point group of a Euclidean crystallographic group is the group of its orthogonal linear parts, equivalently its quotient by the translation subgroup. It preserves the translation Euclidean lattice. In an arbitrary dimension it is a finite orthogonal group; for a plane crystallographic group this lattice preservation restricts the allowed rotation orders.
