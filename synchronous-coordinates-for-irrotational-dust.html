Synchronous coordinates for irrotational dust (u♭=−dt,g=−dt2+gij​dxidxj)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Synchronous coordinates
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For relativistic dust with nowhere-zero density, stress-energy conservation implies zero four-acceleration. If its normalized four-velocity u has hypersurface orthogonality, then u♭∧du♭=0 and ιu​du♭=0. The interior product of the first identity gives du♭=0. The Poincaré lemma supplies u♭=−dt locally; spatial coordinate functions constant along the smooth flow of u give u=∂t​, gtt​=−1 and gti​=0. The resulting synchronous coordinates describe the dust until the local coordinate construction fails.
