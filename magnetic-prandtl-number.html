Magnetic Prandtl number (Pm=u/η)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Magnetic diffusion Magnetic diffusivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The magnetic Prandtl number compares kinematic viscosity with magnetic diffusivity. It is the ratio of the viscous and magnetic diffusion coefficients; equivalently it is the magnetic Reynolds number divided by the Reynolds number when the same speed and length scale are used.
