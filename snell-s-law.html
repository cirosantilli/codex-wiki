Snell's law

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Helmholtz equation Wave scattering at a planar interface

Snell's law follows from conservation of tangential wavenumber across a planar interface. If angles are measured from the interface normal, it reads n1​sinθ1​=n2​sinθ2​.
