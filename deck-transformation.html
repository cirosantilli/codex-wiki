Deck transformation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Covering space
OurBigBook.com Words: 310 Articles: 6
A deck transformation of a covering p:X→X is a homeomorphism g:X→X satisfying p∘g=p. Every double covering has a canonical nonidentity deck transformation that exchanges the two points in each fibre.
