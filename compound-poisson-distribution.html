Compound Poisson distribution (S=∑i=1N​Xi​,N∼Poisson(λ))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Aggregate claims model Random sum of independent claims
OurBigBook.com Words: 559 Articles: 8
The law of a sum of independent identically distributed claims with an independent Poisson distribution count of parameter λ. If the claim moment-generating function is MX​, the aggregate transform is exp(λ(MX​(r)−1)) wherever finite. It has an atom at zero when the claims are positive. This is the fixed-time law of a Compound Poisson process.
