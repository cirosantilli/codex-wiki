Elliptic characteristics of a planar transport equation

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation Characteristic curve Method of characteristics
OurBigBook.com Words: 111
For the linear partial differential equation 2yux​−xuy​=0, the method of characteristics gives x˙=2y, y˙​=−x, u˙=0. Thus ϕ=x2+2y2 is constant on each characteristic curve, whose nonzero level sets are ellipses. On a domain containing entire such ellipses, the solutions have the form u=F(ϕ), subject to the desired regularity at the origin.
For the inhomogeneous equation 2yux​−xuy​=xy(2y2−x2), a particular solution is up​=x2y2/2, so the general solution is u=x2y2/2+F(x2+2y2). Prescribing u(x,1)=x2 fixes F(s)=(s−2)/2 only for s≥2. The inner ellipses s<2 never intersect that data line, so the boundary values leave their solution values undetermined. Smooth extensions must match the prescribed F at s=2; choosing the same linear formula everywhere gives one polynomial extension.
