User-network decomposition of concave utility maximization (y∗=ATp∗,νr∗​=xr∗​eyr∗​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 76
Maximize a sum of increasing strictly concave utilities Ur​(xr​) subject to Ax≤C, x≥0. Suppose capacities are positive, every route is nonempty, and Ur′​(0)=∞. At its unique positive optimizer x∗, the Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions give nonnegative resource congestion prices p∗ and route congestion prices yr∗​=Ur′​(xr∗​). Choosing νr∗​=xr∗​eyr∗​ makes each user maximize Ur​(νr​e−yr∗​)−yr∗​νr​e−yr∗​ at νr∗​. It also makes x∗ maximize the entropy maximization for loss-network occupancy objective, since its gradient is log(νr∗​/xr∗​)=(ATp∗)r​. Thus user and network optima implement the same system optimum.
