Lower triangular matrix

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Triangular matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 175 Articles: 3
A lower triangular matrix has zero entries above its main diagonal. Its determinant is the product of its diagonal entries, and an invertible lower triangular linear system is solved by forward substitution.
