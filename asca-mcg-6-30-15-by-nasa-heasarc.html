ASCA: MCG-6-30-15 by NASA HEASARC (1995 observation; institutional page updated 2005)

 Home Physics Physics bibliography
OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by NASA HEASARC Words: 52
The mission result description reports a broad asymmetric iron K-alpha profile with a low-energy wing relative to the approximately 6.4 keV rest energy. It discusses gravitational redshift and the relativistic Doppler effect in a disk model. The page presents the original observational result and its interpretation, rather than establishing that every possible absorption or illumination model is excluded.
