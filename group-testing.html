Group testing

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A testing strategy that pools samples to determine which individuals satisfy a binary condition. A negative pool rules out every member, whereas a positive pool requires more information to identify the affected individuals. Conditional probabilities and dynamic programming can determine an efficient adaptive strategy under a specified model.
