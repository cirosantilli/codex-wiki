Modular symbol ({α,β})

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 1
A modular symbol is the relative homology class of a path between two modular cusps. Oriented paths satisfy {α,β}+{β,γ}={α,γ}. Continued fractions subdivide rational-endpoint paths into unimodular edges, giving a finite presentation through Manin symbols. The Hecke operators act by finite sums of transformed paths, so their matrices can be computed by rational linear algebra.
