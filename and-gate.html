AND gate

 ... Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Proposition Logical connective Logical conjunction
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
An AND gate computes a∧b=ab for bits a,b. It is irreversible when only its one-bit output is retained. The Toffoli gate embeds the operation reversibly as (a,b,c)↦(a,b,c⊕ab); setting c=0 preserves the inputs while recording their logical conjunction.
