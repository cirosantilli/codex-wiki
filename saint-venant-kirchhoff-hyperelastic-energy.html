Saint Venant–Kirchhoff hyperelastic energy (W=λ(trE)2/2+μtr(E2))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Finite elasticity Hyperelastic material
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
This objective isotropic constitutive model uses the Green-Lagrange strain tensor E=(ATA−I)/2. It reproduces linear elasticity at small strain. Under simple shear A=[(1,q,0)T,e2​,e3​], its longitudinal nominal stress is (λ+2μ)q2/2. Nonuniform finite shear therefore generates longitudinal acceleration, even though the corresponding infinitesimal transverse mode is uncoupled.
