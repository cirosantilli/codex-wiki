Zero- and two-fermion vertices of an internally fermionized bosonic string (M2=−4/α′, −2/α′, 0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Internal free fermions in a four-dimensional bosonic string
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
In the unprojected polynomial Neveu–Schwarz vertex sector, zero fermion insertions give the usual closed-string tachyon and the massless graviton, two-form and dilaton. Two insertions split into a left-right pair ψiψˉ​jeipX of internal weights (1/2,1/2) and a same-chirality current pair of weight one. The first has M2=−2/α′ and 44-squared scalar components. The second requires a bosonic derivative on the opposite side for level matching and gives the two sets of 946 massless gauge vectors. Four-fermion scalar-current products belong to a different insertion count. A chosen global projection may retain a subset, so these operators do not by themselves specify a projected string spectrum.
