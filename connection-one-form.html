Connection one-form (A)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle)
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
In a local frame e, a connection on a vector bundle is written ∇e=Ae, where A is a matrix-valued one-form. Under a change of frame e′=eg, it transforms as A′=g−1Ag+g−1dg.
