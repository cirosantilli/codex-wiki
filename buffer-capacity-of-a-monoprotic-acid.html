Buffer capacity of a monoprotic acid

 Home Chemistry Acid-base chemistry Buffer solution Buffer capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 229
Assume an ideal dilute aqueous chemical solution with just one monoprotic acid pair HA/A−, self-ionization of water, and other ions that balance electric charge without undergoing further chemical reactions. Hold the analytical concentration C=[HA]+[A−] constant. Keep the acid dissociation constant Ka​ and the self-ionization of water constant Kw​ dimensionless, consistently with pH:
c∘=1molL−1,H=10−pH=c∘[H+]​,Kw​=(c∘)2[H+][OH−]​.
(42)
Thus H denotes normalized hydrogen ion concentration, not a dimensional concentration. The chemical equilibrium and conserved analytical concentration give
[A−]=Ka​+HCKa​​,[OH−]=Hc∘Kw​​,[H+]=c∘H.
(43)
Adding chemical base converts weak acid to conjugate base, increases free hydroxide, and decreases free hydrogen ion concentration. Balancing electric charge, including the added reagent's accompanying ions, gives the signed dose relative to any initial chemical equilibrium at p0​:
b(p)−b(p0​)=Q(p)−Q(p0​),Q(p)=[A−]+[OH−]−[H+].
(44)
Differentiate with respect to pH, using dH/dp=−(ln10)H. The result is
β=(ln10)((Ka​+H)2CKa​H​+c∘H+Hc∘Kw​​).​
(45)
The first term is the monoprotic acid pair's contribution, βpair​; the remaining terms are the free hydrogen ion and hydroxide contributions, βwater​. Each has units molL−1 per pH. Equivalently, with dimensional Hc​=[H+], Ka,c​=c∘Ka​ and Kw,c​=(c∘)2Kw​, the expression is (ln10)(CKa,c​Hc​/(Ka,c​+Hc​)2+Hc​+Kw,c​/Hc​). Mixing the dimensional and dimensionless conventions would give incorrect units.
For r=Ka​/H=10pH−pKa​,
βpair​=(ln10)C(1+r)2r​≤4ln10​C,
(46)
because (1+r)2−4r=(1−r)2≥0. Equality occurs at r=1: equal weak acid and conjugate base concentrations, or pH equal to pKa. This is the exact maximum of the pair contribution in this model. The full buffer capacity need not have its maximum there: free hydrogen ions and hydroxide make it grow toward the acidic and alkaline extremes. When these contributions are small, the central peak lies close to pKa.
