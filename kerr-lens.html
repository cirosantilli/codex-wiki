Kerr lens

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Nonlinear optics Kerr effect Optical Kerr effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A beam's transverse intensity changes its own refractive index. For positive nonlinear index and a bright centre, the accumulated phase has the curvature of a converging lens. For I(r)=I0​e−2r2/w2 over a short length L, the in-medium thin-lens approximation gives 1/f=4n2​I0​L/(n0​w2). Negative n2​ instead produces self-defocusing.
