For a Euclidean embedded submanifold,
⟨R(X,Y)Z,W⟩=⟨A(X,W),A(Y,Z)⟩−⟨A(X,Z),A(Y,W)⟩.
(395)
It expresses intrinsic curvature in terms of the second fundamental form.
