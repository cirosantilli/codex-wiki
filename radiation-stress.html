Radiation stress (Sxx​=E(2cg​/cp​−1/2))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Free surface Surface gravity wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
The excess depth-integrated, time-averaged momentum flux of a surface gravity wave is its radiation stress. In deep water cg​/cp​=1/2, so Sxx​=E/2=ρw​ga2/4. The force per transverse width on a reflecting object is the incident plus reflected minus transmitted radiation stress. Reflection increases force because the reflected wave carries momentum in the opposite direction.
