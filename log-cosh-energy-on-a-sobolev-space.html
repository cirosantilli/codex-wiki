Log-cosh energy on a Sobolev space (I(v)=∫Ω​[∣∇v∣2/2+logcoshv])

OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The inequalities 0≤logcosht≤t2/2 make this energy finite on H1. Since the second derivative of its scalar potential is sech2t∈(0,1], its Fréchet derivative is DI(v)[h]=∫(∇v⋅∇h+tanh(v)h), with Taylor remainder between zero and half the squared H1 norm of h. The derivative operator is strictly monotone because the hyperbolic tangent is strictly increasing.
