Pull-off force

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Contact mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The tensile force magnitude needed to detach contacting surfaces during retraction. A force-probe measurement reads it from the force minimum relative to the noncontact baseline; roughness, capillary forces and contact geometry affect its interpretation.
