Landauer-Büttiker formula (Ii​=(e2/h)[Ni​Vi​−∑j​Tij​Vj​])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum transport Landauer formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 154 Articles: 2
A multi-reservoir quantum transport relation. Here Ii​ is conventional current injected from reservoir i, Ni​ counts outgoing spin-resolved modes and Tij​ counts transmission from reservoir j into i. Ideal voltage probes draw zero net current and equilibrate incident carriers. On a chiral integer Hall edge with N modes, Ii​=(Ne2/h)(Vi​−Vi−1​) for the stated circulation order.
