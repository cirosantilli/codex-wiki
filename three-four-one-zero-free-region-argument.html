Three-four-one zero-free-region argument

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Riemann zeta function Nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function Zero-free region of the Riemann zeta function
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
For F=−ζ′/ζ and σ>1, positivity of
3+4cosθ+cos2θ=2(1+cosθ)2
(69)
term by term in the Euler product gives
3F(σ)+4ℜF(σ+it)+ℜF(σ+2it)≥0.
(70)
Comparing this with the pole of F at one and the local poles of ζ′/ζ at zeros proves the classical zero-free region.
