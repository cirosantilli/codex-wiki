Deuterium equilibrium abundance (XD​/(Xn​Xp​)≃(12ζ(3)/π​)η(T/mN​)3/2eεD​/T)

Use natural units and a positive nuclear binding energy εD​=mn​+mp​−mD​. Chemical equilibrium of n+p↔D+γ requires μD​=μn​+μp​. The nonrelativistic Maxwell--Boltzmann number density gives
nn​np​nD​​=gn​gp​gD​​(mn​mp​mD​​)3/2(T2π​)3/2eεD​/T.
(51)
Here gD​=3 and gn​=gp​=2 count the spin states. Taking mn​≃mp​≃mN​ and mD​≃2mN​, then writing Xi​=ni​/nB​, nB​=ηnγ​ and using the photon number density yields the displayed abundance. The coefficient is approximately 8.14. Omitting the spin-state ratio 3/4 gives the rougher prefactor 16ζ(3)/π​. A convention B=mD​−mn​−mp​<0 instead writes the exponential as e−B/T. The small baryon-to-photon ratio suppresses stable deuterium until the temperature is well below the binding scale, producing the deuterium bottleneck.
