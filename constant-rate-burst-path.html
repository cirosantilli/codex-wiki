Constant-rate burst path (t=x/(v−C),v>C)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory Queue (queueing theory) Workload of a queue
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
Use arrival rate v during the last t time units and the normal stable rate mu before that, where μ<C. The lookback net input increases from zero to x over the burst, then decreases because the earlier net drift is negative. Its infinite-buffer workload is x, as is the finite-buffer workload map for capacity at least x. For a local convex path cost with density g and g(μ)=0, its cost is tg(v)=xg(v)/(v−C). These paths realize or approximate the optimal workload cost even if an optimizing duration is not attained.
