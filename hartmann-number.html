Hartmann number (H=∣B0​∣L/μ0​ρνη​)

The Hartmann number measures the strength of magnetic coupling relative to viscous and resistive effects in a channel:
H=μ0​ρνη​∣B0​∣L​=∣B0​∣Lρνσc​​​.
(18)
Here L is the channel half-width, ν the kinematic viscosity, η the magnetic diffusivity and σc​ the electrical conductivity. In Hartmann flow, large H produces thin magnetic-viscous wall layers and a flatter, slower core for fixed pressure gradient.
