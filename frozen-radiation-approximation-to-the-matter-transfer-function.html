Frozen-radiation approximation to the matter transfer function (T(k)=Gmin{1,(keq​/k)2})

Assume all relevant modes begin outside the horizon at common conformal time τi​, grow as τ2 until radiation-era horizon entry, then freeze until matter-radiation equality, and again grow as τ2 in matter domination. With horizon entry τh​=2π/k, keq​=2π/τeq​ and final time τ0​, direct multiplication of the growth factors gives the displayed transfer with G=(τ0​/τi​)2. A primordial density power spectrum Pi​=Ak becomes P0​=AG2k below equality wavenumber and AG2keq4​k−3 above it. This approximation neglects the true logarithmic radiation-era matter growth, baryonic acoustic structure and late acceleration; a conventionally normalized transfer divides out G.
