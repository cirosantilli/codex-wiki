Type IIA superstring theory (N=(1,1) in ten dimensions)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Type II string theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 2
A closed superstring theory whose left and right Ramond sector projections have opposite ten-dimensional Majorana-Weyl chiralities. It has 32 real spacetime supercharges and is nonchiral. Its massless fields form type IIA supergravity; the RR gauge potentials have odd degrees, including a one-form and a three-form.
