For the internal rotation symmetry of two real scalar fields with δϕ1​=αϕ2​ and δϕ2​=−αϕ1​, the Noether charge is Q=i∫(a1†​a2​−a2†​a1​)d3p/(2π)3. Its commutators satisfy [Q,b±†​]=±b±†​, so these creation operators produce charge eigenstates in the one-particle state sector. Substitution gives Q=N+​−N−​, converting the rotation of real components into opposite charges in a complex basis.
