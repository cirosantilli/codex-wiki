Optical tensor (Bab​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Geodesic congruence Null geodesic congruence Screen-space projector

For Bab​=∇b​ka​, the optical tensor is the screen projection Bab​=Pa​cPb​dBcd​. Its trace, symmetric trace-free part, and antisymmetric part are the expansion, shear, and twist of the null congruence.
