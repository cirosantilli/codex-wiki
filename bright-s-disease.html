Bright's disease

 Home Biology Medicine Pathology Renal pathology Nephritis
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
Historical category of kidney disease associated with albuminuria and often oedema, named after Richard Bright. It should not be equated with one modern microscopic diagnosis.[ref]
