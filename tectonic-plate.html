Tectonic plate

 ... Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth Mantle convection Plate tectonics
OurBigBook.com Words: 136 Articles: 5
A region of lithosphere approximated as rigid on the spatial and temporal scale of a plate-kinematic model. Its instantaneous motion on a sphere is a rotation; deformation within boundary zones limits the rigid-plate approximation.
