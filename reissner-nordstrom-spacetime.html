Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime (r±​=M±M2−Q2​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole
OurBigBook.com Words: 508 Articles: 8
The four-dimensional charged spherically symmetric electrovacuum solution has radial function f=1−2M/r+Q2/r2. For 0<∣Q∣<M, its outer and inner horizon radii are r±​=M±M2−Q2​. A complete two-ended spatial bridge has a maximal Cauchy development ending at smoothly extendible inner Cauchy horizons in the exact solution.
