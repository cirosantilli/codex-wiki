Aubin–Nitsche duality argument

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Finite element method Galerkin orthogonality Céa's lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 1
For a symmetric elliptic variational problem with Galerkin orthogonality, let e=u−uh​ and solve the dual problem a(v,z)=(e,v)L2​ for every v∈H01​(Ω). If elliptic regularity gives ∥z∥H2​≤C∥e∥L2​, then a finite element interpolation estimate gives
∥e∥L22​=a(e,z−Ih​z)≤Ch∥e∥H1​∥z∥H2​≤Ch∥e∥H1​∥e∥L2​.
(23)
Thus ∥e∥L2​≤Ch∥e∥H1​. Combining this with the Céa lemma and an O(h) energy norm error gives an O(h2) L2 norm error. The additional order depends on the dual regularity assumption, which can fail on unsuitable domains.
