Algebraic one-parameter subgroup (λ:Gm​→G)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Linear algebraic group
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
An algebraic one-parameter subgroup is a regular group homomorphism from the multiplicative algebraic group. In SLn​, a change of basis makes it t↦diag(ta1​,…,tan​) for integers with sum zero. This is the algebraic multiplicative notion, distinct from an additive real-parameter subgroup of a Lie group.
