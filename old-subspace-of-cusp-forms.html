Old subspace of cusp forms (Sk​(Γ1​(N))old)

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Fourier expansion of a modular form Cusp form
OurBigBook.com Words: 256 Articles: 3
The old subspace is spanned by h(tz) for cusp forms h of level M<N, where M∣N and t∣N/M. It is stable under the Hecke operators, but need not have a basis of eigenvectors for every bad-prime operator. Its Petersson inner product orthogonal complement is the new subspace of cusp forms.
