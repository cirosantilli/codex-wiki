Tensor-product basis ({ei​⊗fj​},dim(V⊗F​W)=dimVdimW)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor product
OurBigBook.com Words: 225 Articles: 1
If (e1​,…,em​) and (f1​,…,fn​) are bases of finite-dimensional vector spaces V,W over F, then the mn products ei​⊗fj​ form a basis of V⊗F​W. There is one independent coordinate for every pair of indices.[ref]
For a proof outline, bilinearity expands each pure tensor as
(∑i​vi​ei​)⊗(∑j​wj​fj​)=∑i,j​vi​wj​(ei​⊗fj​),
(7)
so these products form a spanning set. For linear independence, take the coordinate functionals ek,fℓ from the two dual bases. The bilinear map (v,w)↦ek(v)fℓ(w) induces a linear functional λkℓ​ on the tensor product, satisfying λkℓ​(ei​⊗fj​)=δki​δℓj​. Applying it to a proposed zero linear combination selects just ckℓ​, forcing every coefficient to vanish. Thus dimensions multiply, rather than add. This finite-dimensional count includes a zero factor, whose tensor product has dimension zero.
