Photon arrival from an Einstein-de Sitter particle horizon (tarrival​=8t0​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Particle horizon Comoving particle horizon
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For an Einstein-de Sitter universe normalized by a(t0​)=1, a=(t/t0​)2/3. Its current comoving particle horizon is 3ct0​. A photon emitted inward at that location at t0​ covers 3ct0​[(t/t0​)1/3−1] in comoving distance by time t. Equating these distances gives arrival at 8t0​, a travel time of 7t0​.
