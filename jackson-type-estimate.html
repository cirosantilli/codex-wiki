Jackson-type estimate

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 230 Articles: 4
A Jackson-type estimate bounds an approximation error by a smoothness quantity such as a modulus of continuity, with the smoothness evaluated at a scale comparable to the reciprocal of the approximating degree.
