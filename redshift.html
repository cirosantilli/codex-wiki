Redshift (1+z=umem​/umrec​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 52 Articles: 1
The redshift z compares emitted and received local frequencies or wavelengths: 1+z=λrec​/λem​=νem​/νrec​. Motion, gravitation, and cosmological expansion can all contribute.
