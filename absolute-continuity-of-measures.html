Absolute continuity of measures

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 564 Articles: 16
A measure ν is absolutely continuous with respect to μ, written ν≪μ, when every μ-null measurable set is also ν-null. This is distinct from the regularity property of functions called absolute continuity.
