Five lemma via image factorization

 ... Enriched category Semi-additive category Additive category Abelian category Exact sequence in an abelian category Five lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 106
For commuting five-term exact sequences in an abelian category, the first vertical map being epic and the second invertible give an invertible induced map on the images entering the middle objects, because these images are the cokernels of the first horizontal maps. The fourth vertical map being invertible and the fifth monic similarly give an invertible map on the images leaving the middle objects, because these are the kernels of the last horizontal maps. The image factorization in an abelian category packages each middle object between these two images in a short exact sequence. The short five lemma then makes the middle vertical map invertible. The proof is categorical and does not require an embedding into a category of modules.
