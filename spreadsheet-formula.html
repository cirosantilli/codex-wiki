Spreadsheet formula

 Home Computer science Spreadsheet Spreadsheet cell
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
A mathematical expression stored in a spreadsheet cell, with references to other spreadsheet cells. In this example =A1+A2 asks for addition, =2*A1 for multiplication, and =A2^2 for exponentiation. The evaluator below assumes these spreadsheet formulas have already been represented by their ordered lists of referenced spreadsheet cells and operations on the resulting numbers; it does not execute arbitrary formula text.
