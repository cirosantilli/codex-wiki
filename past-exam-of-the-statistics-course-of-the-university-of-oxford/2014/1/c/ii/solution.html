At the conventional 5% significance level, Student 1's p-value 0.72 gives no rejection of equal mean wear, while Student 2's p-value 0.009 gives rejection of equal mean wear. The latter also rejects at the 1% significance level. More generally, compare each p-value with the prespecified level α; no decision threshold was fixed in the data supplied.
Failure to reject does not establish equality, and a p-value is not the posterior probability that the null hypothesis is true. These two-sided p-values alone also do not identify which material has larger mean wear.
