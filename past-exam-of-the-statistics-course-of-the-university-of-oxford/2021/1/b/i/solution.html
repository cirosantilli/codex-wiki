Put A=−∑i​logxi​>0, B=−∑j​logyj​>0, and N=n+m. The two independent statistical samples give the joint likelihood function
L(θ,τ)=θnτmexp{−(θ−1)A−(τ−1)B}.
(13)
The unrestricted maximum-likelihood estimators are θ=n/A and τ=m/B. Under the null hypothesis, the common statistical parameter η has log-likelihood function Nlogη−(η−1)(A+B), maximized at η​=N/(A+B). With the likelihood ratio defined as the null maximum divided by the unrestricted maximum,
λ=(n/A)n(m/B)me−N+A+B(N/(A+B))Ne−N+A+B​=nnmmNN​(A+B)NAnBm​.
(14)
The statistic in the paper is T=A/(A+B), since both original sums of natural logarithms are negative. Hence
λ=c(n,m)Tn(1−T)m,c(n,m)=nnmm(n+m)n+m​​.
(15)
It has maximum one at T=n/N, corresponding to equal fitted rates. This is the likelihood-ratio test for equality of beta shape parameters.
