The stated prior density factors into independent normal distributions for the regression intercept α and regression coefficient β. Multiplication by the likelihood function therefore leaves separate quadratic expressions in the two coefficients. Define the positive posterior variances
vα​=(σ2n​+τ02​1​)−1,vβ​=(σ2Sxx​​+τ12​1​)−1,

mα​=vα​(σ2nyˉ​​+τ02​α0​​),mβ​=vβ​(σ2Sxy​​+τ12​β0​​).

By completing the square, the joint posterior density is
π(α,β∣y)=2πvα​vβ​​1​exp{−2vα​(α−mα​)2​−2vβ​(β−mβ​)2​}​.
(45)
Equivalently,
α∣y∼N(mα​,vα​),β∣y∼N(mβ​,vβ​),α⊥β∣y​.
(46)
This is Gaussian conjugacy for a normal linear model. Each posterior precision parameter is the sum of its data and prior precision parameters. The formulas remain valid when Sxx​=0: all xi​ are then zero, so the posterior distribution of β equals its prior distribution.
