Both weights are positive and sum to one, so the mean lies between r/λ and xˉ, strictly between them unless they coincide. If all counts are zero, xˉ=0 is the boundary estimate in the usual nonnegative Poisson parameter space; with a strictly positive parameter restriction it represents the likelihood supremum at that boundary. The displayed posterior-mean relation is valid in either interpretation.
