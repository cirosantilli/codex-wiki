Anticommutator ({A,B})

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Linear operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 53 Articles: 1
The anticommutator of two operators is {A,B}=AB+BA. They anticommute when this operator vanishes, equivalently when AB=−BA.
