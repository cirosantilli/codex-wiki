Double-precision floating-point format (binary64)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Floating-point arithmetic
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
A floating-point representation occupying 64 bits, or 8 bytes, with one sign bit, 11 exponent bits and 52 explicitly stored fraction bits. The implicit leading bit of a normal number gives 53 bits of significand precision. Near one, adjacent numbers are separated by 2−52; rounding to nearest has unit roundoff 2−53≈1.11×10−16 in the normal range. These are relative precision scales, distinct from underflow limits or a universal absolute error bound.[ref]
