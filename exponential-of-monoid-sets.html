Exponential of monoid sets (BA≅HomM​(M×A,B))

 ... Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Adjoint functors Cartesian closed category Exponential object
OurBigBook.com Words: 182 Articles: 2
For left M-sets, BA consists of equivariant maps of monoid sets M×A→B for the diagonal action on the domain. Its action is (m⋅f)(w,a)=f(wm,a) and evaluation is f(1,a). The curry of an equivariant h:C×A→B is h(c)(w,a)=h(w⋅c,a). This construction can have noninjective action maps even when both A and B are decidable.
