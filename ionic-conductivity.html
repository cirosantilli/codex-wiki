Ionic conductivity (σion​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 189 Articles: 4
Electrical conductivity arising from motion of ions. In a dilute, uncorrelated vacancy model, a mobile oxygen vacancy has effective charge +2e and moves opposite to the oxygen ions that fill it.
