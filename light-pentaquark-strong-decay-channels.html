Light pentaquark strong-decay channels

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron Baryon Pentaquark
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
Exotic flavour weights can decay into a baryon and a meson while preserving every strong quantum number: uuddsˉ→pK0 or nK+, ddssuˉ→Ξ−π−, and uussdˉ→Ξ0π+. These are quark rearrangements. If above threshold with unsuppressed couplings, such states are short-lived strong resonances. Flavour symmetry alone fixes neither masses nor widths.
