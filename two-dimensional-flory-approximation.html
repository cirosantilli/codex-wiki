Two-dimensional Flory approximation (R∼(v2​ℓ2)1/4N3/4)

 Home Chemistry Polymer Flory approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For a planar polymer with N segments of length ℓ, approximate its coil free energy by F/(kB​T)∼R2/(Nℓ2)+v2​N2/R2. The first term is the entropic stretching cost of an ideal chain, while v2​ is an excluded-area coefficient. Minimizing gives R4∼v2​ℓ2N3 and size exponent ν=3/4. For v2​∼ℓ2, this becomes R∼ℓN3/4. A parameter multiplying N2/R2 must carry area units; using an unsquared segment length there would be dimensionally inconsistent.
