Two-pion isospin decomposition (1⊗1=2⊕1⊕0)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Flavor symmetry Isospin
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
Two pions, each of isospin one, have a nine-dimensional charge-state space. Clebsch-Gordan coefficients decompose it into an isospin-two quintet, an isospin-one triplet and an isospin-zero singlet. Under exchange of the two particle slots, the I=2,0 states are symmetric and the I=1 states are antisymmetric. Because pions have zero spin and obey bosonic exchange symmetry, the I=1 two-pion channel requires odd relative orbital angular momentum, while I=0,2 require even orbital angular momentum.
