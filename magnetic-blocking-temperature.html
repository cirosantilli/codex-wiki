Magnetic blocking temperature (TB​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The observation-timescale-dependent temperature below which a magnetic configuration no longer relaxes readily during a measurement. For exchange bias it can mark stable pinning of antiferromagnetic interfacial domains and can be below the bulk Néel temperature. It is not identical to the thermodynamic onset of magnetic order.
