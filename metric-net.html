Metric net (∀x∈T ∃u∈U: d(x,u)≤ε)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Topological analysis Metric space
OurBigBook.com Words: 215 Articles: 3
An ε-net of a metric space (T,d) is a subset U⊆T such that every x∈T has d(x,u)≤ε for some u∈U. Its least possible finite size is the metric covering number with closed balls. A maximal set with pairwise distances greater than ε is an ε-net.
