Quantization (signal processing, Q(x))

 Home Computer science Image processing
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 1
Quantization maps numerical values to a specified discrete collection of representable values. In lossy block averaging, Q(m)=⌊m+1/2⌋ maps block arithmetic means in [0,255] to eight-bit integers. Thus Q(40.25)=40 and Q(40.5)=41. Different means can share a stored value, making this step irreversible.
