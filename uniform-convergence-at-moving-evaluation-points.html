Uniform convergence at moving evaluation points (fn​(xn​)→f(x))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Uniform convergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
Suppose continuous functions fn​ converge by uniform convergence to f on a domain of a function X, and xn​→x with all these inputs in X. The uniform limit theorem makes f a continuous function, and the triangle inequality gives
∣fn​(xn​)−f(x)∣≤∥fn​−f∥∞​+∣f(xn​)−f(x)∣⟶0.
(79)
The first term handles the changing approximation and the second handles the changing input. For fn​(x)=xn on [0,1], the inputs xn​=2−1/n instead give xn​→1, fn​(xn​)=1/2, and f(1)=1. Pointwise convergence cannot supply the first uniform bound, and this pointwise limit also lacks continuity at one.
