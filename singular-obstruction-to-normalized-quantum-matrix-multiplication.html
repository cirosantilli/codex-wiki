Singular obstruction to normalized quantum matrix multiplication

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum Fourier transform Quantum phase estimation Quantum spectral filtering
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
If ∣b⟩ lies in the kernel of A, then A∣b⟩=0 has no normalized quantum state. A request to produce that normalized vector with positive probability is consequently undefined. For example, A=diag(0,1/2) on one qubit has distinct dyadic eigenvalues, but annihilates ∣0⟩. The corrected filtering assertion requires A∣b⟩=0; it cannot be repaired by assigning a positive success probability to this input.
