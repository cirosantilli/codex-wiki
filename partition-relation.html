Partition relation (λ→(μ)βα​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 747 Articles: 9
The notation λ→(μ)βα​ says that every β-coloring of the α-element subsets of λ has a homogeneous subset of order type μ. At infinite arity one specifies whether the domain subsets have a fixed cardinality or a fixed order type; the usual finite-arity notation has no such ambiguity.
