Rest-frame reduction of scalar transition matrix elements (E′=p⋅q/m)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Quantum field Translation covariance of a quantum field
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For p2=m2>0, let a unitary operator U implement a Lorentz boost taking p to (m,0). For a Lorentz scalar field, ⟨q∣ϕ(x)∣p⟩=⟨Ψ∣ϕ(0)∣m,0⟩ with ∣Ψ⟩=e−i(q−p)⋅xU∣q⟩. The phase follows from translation covariance of a quantum field; its complex conjugate enters the bra. The transformed energy eigenvalue is (Λq)0=p⋅q/m by invariance of the Minkowski inner product. The reduction requires a massive initial state.
