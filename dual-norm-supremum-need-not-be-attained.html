Dual norm supremum need not be attained (∥p∥∗​=1without a maximizing unit vector)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Normed vector space Norm Dual norm
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
On the real space of sequences converging to zero c0​, use the supremum norm and define the linear functional
p(x)=∑n=1∞​2−nxn​.
(5)
The geometric series gives ∣p(x)∣≤∥x∥∞​, so p is a bounded linear functional and ∥p∥∗​≤1. The sequence with its first N entries equal to one and all later entries zero belongs to c0​, has unit supremum norm, and gives p(x)=1−2−N. Hence ∥p∥∗​=1. But if ∥x∥∞​≤1 and xn​→0, not all coordinates can equal one or all equal minus one. Since every weight 2−n is positive, −1<p(x)<1. The supremum is approached but never attained on the unit ball.
