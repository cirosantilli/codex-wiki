Radius deformation as a Higgs mechanism (mW2​=(R/α′−1/R)2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 76
At the self-dual circle, left and right dimension-one currents generate SU(2)L​×SU(2)R​. The compact-coordinate vertex ∂YL​∂ˉYR​ is a Cartan–Cartan component of a scalar field in the (3,3) representation. Its expectation value changes the circle radius and leaves U(1)L​×U(1)R​ unbroken. The four extra charged vectors acquire the displayed mass, and four scalar field polarizations become their longitudinal modes. This realizes the Higgs mechanism geometrically. In a bosonic string the ever-present tachyon and additional scalar field instabilities prevent interpreting the whole family as a stable vacuum.
