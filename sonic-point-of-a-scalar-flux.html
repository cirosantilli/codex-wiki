Sonic point of a scalar flux (f′(s∗​)=0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation Scalar conservation law
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For a differentiable scalar conservation law ut​+f(u)x​=0, a sonic point of a scalar flux is a state s where its characteristic speed f′(s) vanishes. If f is convex and this zero is unique, f′<0 on the left and f′>0 on the right, so s is the unique minimum of the flux. The definition refers to a vanishing transport speed in a scalar conservation law. In compressible fluid mechanics, a sonic point instead refers to flow at the local sound speed.
