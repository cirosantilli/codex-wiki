Isotropic thin-plate bending energy (U=2D​∫[(Δw)2−2(1−ν)detD2w])

For a homogeneous isotropic plate of thickness h, Young's modulus E and Poisson's ratio ν, the bending stiffness is D=Eh3/[12(1−ν2)]. Its quadratic bending energy is
U[w]=2D​∫Ω​[wxx2​+2νwxx​wyy​+wyy2​+2(1−ν)wxy2​],dx,dy.
(21)
It follows by integrating the strain energy density 21​τij​eij​ through the thickness: linear bending strains are proportional to z, and ∫−h/2h/2​z2dz=h3/12. Taking a first variation and integrating by parts twice gives DΔ2w=0 without applied transverse load. Clamped boundary variations have both zero value and zero normal derivative.
