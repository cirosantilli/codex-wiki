Quadratic penalty method (f(x)+2μ1​(∥h(x)∥2+∥g(x)−∥2))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For constraints h=0, g≥0, minimize the displayed merit function for progressively smaller μ>0, where gi−​=min(gi​,0). Increasing the quadratic penalty discourages constraint violation. In a violated inequality, differentiation gives the multiplier estimate λi​=−gi​/μ; an inactive inequality has estimate zero. Thus obtaining small violations by this method alone typically requires a very small μ, which may cause a large condition number in the inner minimization.
