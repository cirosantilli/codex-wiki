Comoving observer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Scale factor (cosmology) Comoving coordinate
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A comoving observer has constant comoving coordinates in a homogeneous expanding universe. Its physical separation from another comoving observer grows in proportion to the scale factor, giving relative recession governed by the Hubble parameter. Light exchanged between these observers undergoes cosmological redshift.
