Marginal equilibrium at a mush-liquid boundary (Tz​=−mCz​)

 ... Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Stefan problem Mushy layer Stagnant mushy-layer model
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
At a sharp mush–liquid edge with linear liquidus TL​=Tm​−mC, marginal equilibrium makes the liquid temperature gradient tangent to the local liquidus: Tz​=−mCz​. Together with T=TL​ at the edge and T≥TL​ in the exterior liquid, it supplies the usual absence-of-constitutional supercooling closure. When solute diffusion is neglected inside the mushy layer but retained outside it, interfacial solute conservation can require a small solid fraction jump; imposing unit porosity as an additional finite-diffusivity condition generally overdetermines this sharp-edge reduction.
