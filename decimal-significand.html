Decimal significand (S10​(x)=10{log10​x})

For x>0, the decimal significand is the unique S10​(x)∈[1,10) such that x=10kS10​(x) for an integer k. Explicitly, k=⌊log10​x⌋ and S10​(x)=10{log10​x}, using the floor function and the fractional part. For 0.0042, 4.2 and 4200, the decimal significand is 4.2 and the first nonzero digit is ⌊S10​(x)⌋=4. In the classical terminology of logarithm tables, the logarithmic mantissa is {log10​x}, which differs from the decimal significand 10{log10​x}.
