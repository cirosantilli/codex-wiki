Binding energy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 2
Binding energy is the energy required to separate a bound state into specified free constituents. For an electron bound in a chemical atom, neglecting this cost is an assumption of the free-electron Compton scattering model.
