Steric sea level change (Δηsteric​=−∫Δρ/ρdz)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Climate change Sea level rise
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
Steric sea level change results from changes of seawater mass density at fixed ocean mass. To first order, thermal expansion gives Δηthermal​=∫αΔTdz; changes in salinity give a halosteric contribution. The reference column, pressure and equation of state must be specified.
