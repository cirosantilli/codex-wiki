Fresnel equations

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Plane electromagnetic wave Interface conditions for electromagnetic fields Dielectric-interface boundary conditions
OurBigBook.com Words: 691 Articles: 13
The Fresnel equations give reflection and transmission amplitudes for both transverse-electric and transverse-magnetic polarization of an electromagnetic wave at a planar interface. They follow by applying interface conditions for electromagnetic fields to incident, reflected and refracted plane electromagnetic waves.
