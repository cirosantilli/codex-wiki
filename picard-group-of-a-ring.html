Picard group of a ring (Pic(A))

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Locally free sheaf Line bundle Picard group
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
The Picard group of a commutative ring A consists of isomorphism classes of finitely generated projective modules that have rank one at every prime ideal. Tensor product is the group law, [A] is the identity, and the module dual supplies the inverse. For an integral domain, every such module can be represented by an invertible fractional ideal after choosing a basis over the fraction field.
