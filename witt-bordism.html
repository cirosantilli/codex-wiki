Witt bordism (ΩnWitt​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Cobordism
OurBigBook.com Words: 261 Articles: 3
Two closed oriented rational Witt spaces are Witt bordant if their signed disjoint union is the collared boundary of a compact oriented Witt space with collared boundary. Disjoint union gives the bordism group ΩnWitt​.
