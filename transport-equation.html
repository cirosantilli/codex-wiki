Transport equation (ut​+b⋅∇u=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 566 Articles: 19
A transport equation carries a quantity along the flow of a velocity field b. For a smooth solution of ut​+b⋅∇u=0, the value of u is constant along each characteristic curve.
