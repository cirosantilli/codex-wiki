Reusable locator for Table 1, “Geomorphic features associated with active faults”, in the primary mapping report Late Quaternary Faulting, Death Valley–Furnace Creek Fault System, California-Nevada, USGS Bulletin 1991. The normal-slip column lists scarps, faceted spurs, oversteepened mountain-front bases and rejuvenated valley floors. The official publication record confirms the report identity and 1991 publication year: pubs.usgs.gov/publication/b1991 .
