Intersection pairing on an oriented surface (ω:H1​(Σg​;Z)2→Z)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Cohomology Cap product Fundamental class Poincare duality
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
Represent one-dimensional homology classes on a closed oriented topological surface by transverse oriented curves and sum their signed crossing points. This gives a bilinear form that is skew-symmetric. A symplectic handle basis has matrix (0−Ig​​Ig​0​), which is unimodular. In particular, a class detected with intersection 1 or −1 by another integral class is a primitive homology class: the detecting homomorphism splits its rank-one subgroup.
