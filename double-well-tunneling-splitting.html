Double-well tunneling splitting (E±​=Ewell​∓ℏKe−SI​/ℏ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Potential barrier Quantum tunnelling
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A symmetric two-well system has nearly localized left/right states coupled by a positive quantum tunnelling magnitude Δ=ℏKe−SI​/ℏ. Its effective Hamiltonian is Ewell​I−Δσx​, so the superposition with an even spatial wavefunction is lower, and the level splitting is 2Δ. The dilute instanton gas reproduces these two exponents through even/odd crossing sums.
