Indefinite Hermitian form (⟨v,v⟩ can have either sign)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Sesquilinear form Hermitian form
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
An indefinite Hermitian form takes both positive and negative values on ⟨v,v⟩ for nonzero vectors. For example, −∣v0​∣2+∣v1​∣2+∣v2​∣2+∣v3​∣2 is indefinite. It is not a positive inner product defining a Hilbert space, and a nonzero vector can have zero norm without being orthogonal to all vectors. A positive semidefinite restricted form becomes a positive inner product only after quotienting its radical of a Hermitian form.
