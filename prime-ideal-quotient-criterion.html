Prime ideal quotient criterion

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Prime ideal
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
For a commutative unital ring R, an ideal I is prime exactly when R/I is an integral domain. The zero-product condition in the quotient translates directly to ab∈I.
