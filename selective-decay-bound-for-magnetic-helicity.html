Selective decay bound for magnetic helicity (∣H˙∣≤2μ0​2ηm​EB​DB​​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential Magnetic helicity Resistive magnetic-helicity balance
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
With the closed-boundary assumptions of the resistive magnetic-helicity balance, define the nonnegative resistive magnetic energy loss DB​=ηm​∫V​∣∇×B∣2dV/μ0​. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality gives the displayed bound. Consequently, in a family of fields with bounded EB​,DB​ as ηm​→0, resistive magnetic helicity loss tends to zero even when DB​ remains finite. This is an absolute conservation estimate; it is not a universal comparison of fractional decay times. For a single Beltrami field with ∇×B=kB and no motion, both EB​ and H have the same fractional decay rate 2ηm​k2.
