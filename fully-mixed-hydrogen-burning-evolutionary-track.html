OurBigBook.com Words: 63
With negligible heavy-element contribution to the mean molecular weight, μ=4/(3+5X) and κ∝1+X. At fixed mass, composition-dependent CNO homology gives L∝(1+X)−1(3+5X)−4 and R∝[X(1+X)]1/16(3+5X)−9/16. The Stefan–Boltzmann law then gives Te​∝X−1/32(1+X)−9/32(3+5X)−23/32. From the formal X=1 reference, declining X initially increases luminosity and effective temperature, unlike the usual redward main-sequence turn-off. A trace CNO catalyst abundance is implicit in the burning coefficient; an exactly metal-free pure-hydrogen star cannot burn through that cycle.
