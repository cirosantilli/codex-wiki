H1 space on the unit disk (H1(D))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Harmonic analysis Hardy space
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 2
This Hardy space consists of holomorphic functions with ∥f∥1​=supr<1​∫∂D​∣f(rξ)∣dm(ξ)<∞, where m is normalized angular measure. The H1 non-tangential maximal inequality on the disk controls their approach to the boundary, and the Carleson embedding theorem on the disk characterizes measures against which all these functions remain integrable.
