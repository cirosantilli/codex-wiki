Perfect fluid in general relativity (Tab​=(ρ+p)ua​ub​+pgab​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Stress-energy tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 28
A relativistic perfect fluid has energy density ρ, isotropic pressure p and unit timelike four-velocity u. Projecting ∇a​Tab=0 along and orthogonally to u gives its energy and Euler equations.
For a perfect fluid, the relativistic stress tensor is Tab​=(ρ+p)ua​ub​+pgab​ in signature (−,+,+,+) with uaua​=−1.
