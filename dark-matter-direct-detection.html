Dark-matter direct detection

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Dark matter
OurBigBook.com Words: 406 Articles: 9
A search for interactions of passing dark matter with a terrestrial target, for example nuclear recoils from elastic scattering. Predicting a count requires the local density and velocity distribution, interaction cross section, target response and detector acceptance. A background-compatible count gives a conditional interaction bound, rather than a measurement of the cosmological relic density.
