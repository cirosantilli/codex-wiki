Electromotive force (E)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current
OurBigBook.com Words: 629 Articles: 6
Electromotive force is the driving work per unit electric charge around an electrical circuit, measured in volts, despite its name containing "force". For a stationary loop its electromagnetic contribution is E=∮C​E⋅dl; for a moving electrical conductor, include the motional electromotive force. A nonzero closed-loop line integral of the electric field cannot be represented by a single-valued electric potential alone. An electromotive force specifies the drive; the resulting electric current also depends on the electrical resistance and self-inductance. [ref].
