Positive-degree norm-one virtual characters are irreducible (⟨ψ,ψ⟩=1, ψ(1)>0⟹ψ∈Irr(G))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Character theory Character of a representation Virtual character
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Write the virtual character as ψ=∑χ​aχ​χ with integer coefficients and irreducible characters χ. Character orthogonality gives ⟨ψ,ψ⟩=∑χ​aχ2​. Norm one forces exactly one coefficient to be 1 or −1, and positive degree selects the positive sign.
