Screen-space projector (Pab​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Geodesic congruence Null geodesic congruence
OurBigBook.com Words: 472 Articles: 11
Given null vectors k and n with k⋅n=−1, the screen-space projector is
Pab​=δba​+kanb​+nakb​.
(4)
It annihilates k and n and, in D spacetime dimensions, projects onto the (D−2)-dimensional spacelike space transverse to them.
