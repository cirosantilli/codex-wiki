For a torsion Spin-c structure, the relative grading of Heegaard Floer homology lifts canonically to a rational absolute grading, normalized by the bottom plus generator of S3 having degree zero. Multiplication by U has degree −2, and a Heegaard Floer cobordism map has degree (c12​−2χ−3σ)/4. The correction term d(Y,s) is the lowest grading of the image of HF∞ in HF+ for a rational homology sphere. A pure tower then has bottom plus degree d and top minus degree d−2 in the i<0 minus convention. Keeping that shift explicit prevents errors of two in surgery and mixed-invariant calculations.
