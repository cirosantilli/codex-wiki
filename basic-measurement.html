Basic measurement ({∣bj​⟩⟨bj​∣}j​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum measurement Projective measurement
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A rank-one projective measurement associated with an orthonormal basis {∣bj​⟩}j​. For a density matrix ρ, outcome j has probability ⟨bj​∣ρ∣bj​⟩ and leaves the measured system in the pure state ∣bj​⟩ whenever that probability is nonzero. It can be implemented by mapping this orthonormal basis to the computational basis with a unitary transformation and performing a computational-basis measurement.[ref]
