Condensate density (n0​=N0​/V)

 ... Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Bose-Einstein statistics Bose-Einstein distribution Bose-Einstein condensation Bose-Einstein condensate
OurBigBook.com Words: 94
For a homogeneous gas, the condensate density is the macroscopic occupation N0​ of its condensate mode divided by volume. More generally its spatial density is N0​∣φ0​(x)∣2 for a normalized condensate orbital. The mode and occupation are defined by a macroscopic eigenvalue of the one-body density matrix, so a finite fixed-number state need not have a nonzero field expectation. Condensate density is distinct from the superfluid density defined by response to a phase twist; interactions can deplete the former even when the latter equals the total density at zero temperature in a uniform Galilean-invariant gas.
