Brans-Dicke theory (Lg​=ΦR+ωΦ−1(∇Φ)2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Einstein field equations Modified gravity Scalar-tensor theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 177 Articles: 2
The Brans-Dicke theory adds a dynamical scalar field Φ to a metric tensor theory of gravity, with constant dimensionless coupling ω. In a (+−−−) signature and the reversed-curvature convention used here, the displayed Lagrangian is supplemented by matter and integrated against the metric tensor volume density with prefactor 1/(16πG). Its scalar multiplier of the Ricci scalar lets the curvature coupling vary in spacetime. Both the metric tensor and scalar are varied; the scalar equation of Brans-Dicke theory follows by eliminating curvature between the scalar equation and the trace of the metric tensor equation. The original construction is described in Brans and Dicke's 1961 article.
