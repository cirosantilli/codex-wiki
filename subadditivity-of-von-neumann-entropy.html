Subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Von Neumann entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 4
For a bipartite density operator ρAB​,
S(ρAB​)≤S(ρA​)+S(ρB​).
(3)
Equality holds exactly when ρAB​=ρA​⊗ρB​.
