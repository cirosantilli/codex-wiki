Eddy turnover time (τl​∼l/δul​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Energy cascade
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
The eddy turnover time estimates how long a velocity difference δul​ takes to distort a structure of size l. In a local strong energy cascade, the transfer time is assumed comparable to it. A weak wave cascade can transfer energy much more slowly because a single encounter makes only a small fractional change.
