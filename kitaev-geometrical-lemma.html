Kitaev geometrical lemma (λmin​(A+B)≥2γsin2(ϑ/2))

 ... Functional analysis Hilbert space Hilbert projection theorem Orthogonal decomposition by a closed subspace Orthogonal projection Smallest angle between two subspaces
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
Suppose positive operators A,B have positive eigenvalues at least γ and their nullspaces meet only at zero. If ϑ is their smallest angle between two subspaces, then A+B≥2γsin2(ϑ/2)I. Indeed A+B≥γ(2I−P−Q), while (P+Q)2≤(1+cosϑ)(P+Q) gives P+Q≤(1+cosϑ)I. With a common nullspace, apply the same proof on its orthogonal complement to bound the positive spectral gap.
