Phototactic concentration in a vertical channel (n(x)=n0​aβeβx/sinh(aβ))

In a long channel −a<x<a, uniform horizontal mean swimming Vs​K0​ex​ and isotropic translational diffusion coefficient D give zero transverse cell flux when n′=βn, β=Vs​K0​/D. Normalizing the transverse mean concentration to n0​ gives the displayed exponential. For βa≫1, cells form a layer of thickness β−1 at the illuminated wall. This is a bulk approximation; exact closed-end cell conservation equation also requires the integrated axial cell flux to vanish.
