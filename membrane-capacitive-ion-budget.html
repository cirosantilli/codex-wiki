Membrane capacitive ion budget (Δc=Cm​AΔV/(zFV))

 Home Biology Neuroscience Patch clamp Whole-cell recording Membrane capacitance
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
When a single ion species supplies the net charging of a cell membrane, its bulk concentration increment is Δc=Cm​AΔV/(zFV). Opposing ionic Electric currents can make gross entry exceed this minimum net electric charge budget. A large membrane potential change can coexist with approximate bulk electroneutrality.
