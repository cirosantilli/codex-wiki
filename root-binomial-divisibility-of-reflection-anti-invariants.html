Root-binomial divisibility of reflection anti-invariants (sα​u=−u ⟹ u/(1−e(α))∈Z[P])

For a root reflection on the weight lattice, sα​λ=λ−mα with m=λ(Hα​)∈Z. A finite anti-invariant element of the group ring of a weight lattice is a sum of terms aλ​[e(λ)−e(sα​λ)]; fixed weights have zero coefficient. Each pair is divisible by 1−e(α), because 1−tm is divisible by 1−t in Z[t,t−1] for every integer m. Thus the quotient has finite support.
The formal expression (∑n≥0​e(nα))u is interpreted coefficientwise. Along each coset of Zα, only finitely many coefficients of u are nonzero and their total is zero by reflection anti-invariance. Partial coefficient sums therefore vanish before and after that finite interval. The result has finite support, even though the individual summands e(nα)u do not eventually vanish.
