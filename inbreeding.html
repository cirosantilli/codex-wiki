Inbreeding

 Home Biology Genetics Population genetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 169 Articles: 3
Inbreeding is reproduction between genetically related individuals. It increases the chance that an offspring's maternal and paternal copies at a locus are identical by descent. Such homozygosity by descent is autozygosity.
