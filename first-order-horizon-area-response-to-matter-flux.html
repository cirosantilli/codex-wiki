For a small perturbation of a stationary horizon, the linearized Null Raychaudhuri equation gives δθ′=−8πδTkk​. With vanishing boundary term [λδθ], integration by parts gives δA=8π∫λδTkk​dλdA0​. Combining this with affine horizon-generator scaling converts area response to horizon Killing-energy flux.
