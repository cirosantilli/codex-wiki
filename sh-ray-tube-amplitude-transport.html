SH ray-tube amplitude transport (ρβa2J=constant)

 ... Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Elastic wave S wave SH-wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 1
For a leading wavefront singularity or discontinuity in a smooth isotropic shear-horizontal wave field, the ray transport equation conserves the displayed quantity along a tube of elastic rays. Here J is transverse tube width in two dimensions and area in three. Cylindrical spreading has J∝r, so the leading wave amplitude is proportional to r−1/2. This rule excludes the immediate source neighborhood and caustics.
