Coverage coalitional game (v(S)=​⋃i∈S​Bi​​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Cooperative game theory Transferable utility game
OurBigBook.com Words: 155 Articles: 2
Players hold finite sets of resources, and their coalition value is the number of distinct resources they collectively cover. The value is generally subadditive rather than superadditive: overlapping resources are counted only once. It is a superadditive coalitional game exactly when the players' resource sets are pairwise disjoint.
