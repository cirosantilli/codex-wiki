Lorentzian resonance approximation (γ/(x2+γ2)→πδ(x))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Dirac delta function
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For γ>0, the kernels γ/[π(x2+γ2)] have unit integral and concentrate at zero as γ→0+. Splitting the integral into a neighborhood of zero and its complement proves convergence against bounded continuous test functions. Thus [(kv−ω)2+γ2]−1≃πδ(kv−ω)/∣γ∣ under an integral, with δ(kv−ω)=δ(v−ω/k)/∣k∣. This is the narrow-width limit of a Cauchy distribution, not a pointwise replacement away from the peak.
