Hecke operator on lattice functions (Tn​)

One standard normalization is
(Tn​F)(Λ)=n1​∑[Λ′:Λ]=nΛ′⊃Λ​​F(Λ′).
(42)
With lattice model of a modular form homogeneity F(uL)=u−kF(L), the factor 1/n gives the usual weight-k Hecke operator. For prime p, the overlattices contribute f((τ+b)/p) and pkf(pτ); division by p produces the standard Fourier coefficients.
