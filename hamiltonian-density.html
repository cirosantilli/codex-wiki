 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 62 Articles: 1
A Hamiltonian density is a local integrand for the field-theory Hamiltonian. For canonical fields with invertible velocity-momentum relation it is the Legendre transform in mechanics of the Lagrangian density, summing over all conjugate field components. Gauge theories require constraints and multiplier terms as well.
