Thiol (R−SH)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound
OurBigBook.com Words: 48 Articles: 1
A thiol is a chemical compound of the form R−SH, with an organic group R attached to sulfur. The conjugate base is R−S−; cysteine and mercaptoethanol supply examples of thiol groups. Two thiol groups can undergo oxidation to form a disulfide bond. [ref]
