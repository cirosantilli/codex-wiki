Coarse exponential bracketing (C(m)≤(1+1/k)log2​m+Ok​(1))

 Home Computer science Algorithm Exponential search
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
To discover a positive unknown integer m by comparisons, test the thresholds 2k,22k,… for fixed k≥1. If the first threshold not smaller than m has index j, fewer than 2kj candidates remain and binary search needs at most kj+O(1) comparisons. Since j≤log2​(m)/k+1, the total is at most (1+1/k)log2​m+Ok​(1). Choosing k≥1/ϵ makes the leading constant at most 1+ϵ; the additive constant may depend on ϵ.
