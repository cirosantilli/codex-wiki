Resonance envelope of a Rabi transition (Pmax​(δ)=Ω2/(Ω2+δ2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 75
In the Driven two-level Rabi model, the transition probability is P(t)=Ω2sin2(Ω2+δ2​t/2)/(Ω2+δ2). Maximizing over time at each detuning gives a Lorentzian line shape, with first maximizing time π/Ω2+δ2​ and full width at half maximum 2∣Ω∣ in angular frequency. The amplitude parameter Ω must use the specified Rabi angular frequency convention.
This envelope differs from a spectrum obtained with a fixed pulse duration: optimizing the time separately removes the sine-squared factor, while fixing time retains it. A finite linewidth here describes detuning-limited transfer under coherent driving, without requiring dissipative decay.
