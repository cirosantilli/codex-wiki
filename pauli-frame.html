Pauli frame (XaZb)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum circuit Pauli group
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
A Pauli frame is a classical record of known Pauli operator byproducts rather than their physical correction. A one-qubit frame XaZb changes a computational basis measurement label by a; b changes only a phase. Conjugation by Clifford gates maps Pauli operators to Pauli operators, so the frame can be updated by classical binary arithmetic throughout a Clifford circuit.
