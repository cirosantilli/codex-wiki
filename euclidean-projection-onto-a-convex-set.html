Euclidean projection onto a convex set

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex set
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 3
For a nonempty closed convex subset C of a finite-dimensional inner-product space, ΠC​(x) is the unique point of C minimizing ∥x−z∥.
This closest-point map is the metric projection onto a closed convex set. Repeated projections onto several sets form the separate projections onto convex sets method.
