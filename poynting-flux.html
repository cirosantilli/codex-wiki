Poynting flux (Sn​=S⋅n^)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Poynting vector
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
The Poynting flux through an oriented surface is the normal component of the electromagnetic energy-flow vector. Integrating it over area gives outward electromagnetic power. A scale estimate of the form energy density times propagation speed must still specify the field and geometric regime.
