Three-wave mixing

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Nonlinear optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 5
An interaction among three waves mediated by a second-order nonlinear optical susceptibility. In sum-frequency generation, two input angular frequencies satisfy ω3​=ω1​+ω2​. The same coupling permits reverse conversion. Phase matching determines whether successive generated contributions reinforce; the Manley-Rowe relations express the associated lossless exchange constraints.
