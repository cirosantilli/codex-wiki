Fay solution (q=2δ∑n≥1​sin(nθ)/sinh(nδZ))

The Fay solution is a periodic solution of the negative-flux viscous Burgers equation for Z>0. Its Fourier series has exponentially decreasing coefficients at each positive δZ. Expanding 1/sinh(ns)=2∑j≥0​e−(2j+1)ns and summing the sine series gives
q=2δsinθ∑j≥0​cosh((2j+1)s)−cosθ1​,s=δZ.
(2)
For s≫1, the first harmonic gives q∼4δe−ssinθ. The Fay shock-layer asymptotics describe its opposite, small-s regime.
