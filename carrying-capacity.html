Carrying capacity (K)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Population dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A carrying capacity is an abundance scale at which a specified environment's net population growth vanishes. In the logistic growth equation, positive unharvested populations approach K. It depends on the species, resources and environment rather than being an immutable universal population limit.
