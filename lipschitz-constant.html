Lipschitz constant (L)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Lipschitz continuity
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
The least L≥0 for which d(f(x),f(y))≤Ld(x,y) for every x,y is the Lipschitz constant of f. Any admissible L is a Lipschitz bound.
