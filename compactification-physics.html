Compactification (physics)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 28
Compactification makes some spatial dimensions compact, so modes along them acquire discrete momenta. At energies below the first nonzero compact-mode mass, a dimensional reduction to the zero modes gives a lower-dimensional effective field theory. The compact volume fixes the gravitational kinetic coefficient in an unwarped product background.
