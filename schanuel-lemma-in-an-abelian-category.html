Schanuel lemma in an abelian category (K⊕P′≅K′⊕P)

Two projective presentations of the same object in an abelian category, with kernels K,K′ and middle objects P,P′, satisfy K⊕P′≅K′⊕P. Form their pullback in a category. Its two induced short exact sequences in an abelian category split because each quotient is a projective object in a category. The two resulting biproduct descriptions of the pullback give the isomorphism.
