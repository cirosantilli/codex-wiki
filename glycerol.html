Glycerol (C3​H8​O3​)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
Glycerol has three carbon chemical atoms and three OH groups. It supplies the scaffold of triglycerides and glycerophospholipids, but is not itself a long hydrocarbon chain. See Biology 2e: glycerol.
