Crystal momentum (ℏk)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bloch's theorem Bloch state
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Crystal momentum is the translation quantum number ℏk of a Bloch state. Since k and k+G give the same translation character whenever G belongs to the reciprocal lattice, crystal momentum is defined modulo reciprocal-lattice vectors.
