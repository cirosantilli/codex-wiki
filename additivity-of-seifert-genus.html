Additivity of Seifert genus

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Knot Seifert surface Seifert genus
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The Seifert genus is additive under connected sum of knots:
gs​(K#J)=gs​(K)+gs​(J).
(3)
The upper bound comes from boundary-connected-summing minimal Seifert surfaces, while the reverse inequality follows by cutting any Seifert surface for the connected sum along a splitting sphere.
