Conditional expectation of a monochromatic clique count

 Home Computer science Algorithm Randomized algorithm Derandomization Method of conditional probabilities
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
In a partially fixed two-colour edge coloring of a complete graph, complete all unassigned edges independently and fairly. For a fixed clique with r unassigned edges, its conditional probability of becoming monochromatic is zero if both colours already occur; 2−r if a colour is fixed and no conflicting colour occurs; and 21−r if none is fixed. Sum these conditional probabilities over the cliques to obtain a computable conditional expectation. Fix each next edge to a colour that does not increase this conditional expectation, yielding a deterministic minimization construction.
