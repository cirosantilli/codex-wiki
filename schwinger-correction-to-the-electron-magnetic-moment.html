Schwinger correction to the electron magnetic moment (ae​=α/(2π)+O(α2))

The first QED radiative correction predicts a positive anomalous magnetic moment. In Julian Seymour Schwinger's 1948 paper On Quantum-Electrodynamics and the Magnetic Moment of the Electron, the extra spin moment is δμ/μ=α/(2π). His e2/(ℏc) uses the unrationalized charge convention; in rationalized natural units the same fine-structure constant is α=e2/(4π). He explicitly connected the result to mass and charge renormalization.[ref]
In the electron spin g-factor convention used here,
ae(1)​=2πα​,gs​=2(1+2πα​+O(α2)).
(28)
For the deliberately rounded teaching input α=1/137, ae(1)​≃0.0011617149 and gs(1)​≃2.0023234298. The correction is about 0.116% of the Dirac equation spin moment. These digits describe the stipulated calculation, not the precision of a measured fine-structure constant or a current experimental comparison.
