Convex cone

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex set
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 26
A convex cone is a subset C of a real vector space closed under nonnegative linear combinations: ax+by∈C whenever x,y∈C and a,b≥0. The positive semidefinite cone and the copositive cone are examples, ordered by inclusion through the positive-semidefinite-plus-nonnegative cone.
