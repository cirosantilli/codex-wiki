Force balance

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Force
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Force balance equates the rate of change of momentum to the sum of applied forces. A static continuum segment with internal resultant F(s) and force density f(s) obeys ∂s​F+f=0.
