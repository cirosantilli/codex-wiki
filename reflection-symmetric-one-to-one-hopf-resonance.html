Reflection-symmetric one-to-one Hopf resonance

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory Hopf bifurcation
OurBigBook.com Words: 175 Articles: 1
Two complex Hopf amplitudes of even and odd spatial parity transform under reflection as (A,B)↦(A,−B). In a nonresonant double Hopf bifurcation, the cubic terms are A∣A∣2,A∣B∣2 and their B counterparts. At equal temporal frequencies, common phase covariance also permits B2A and A2B. Distinct frequencies alone do not guarantee nonresonance: a ratio of two allows the quadratic terms B2 and AB. The frequency assumptions must therefore be included when deriving the normal form of a dynamical system.
