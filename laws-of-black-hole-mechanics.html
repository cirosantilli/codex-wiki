Laws of black-hole mechanics

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole Black-hole thermodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The laws of black-hole mechanics relate surface gravity, horizon area, mass, angular momentum, and charge in close analogy with the laws of thermodynamics. Quantum theory identifies the analogy physically through Hawking temperature and Bekenstein-Hawking entropy.
