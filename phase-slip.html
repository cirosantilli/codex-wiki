Phase slip (Δθ=±2π)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Phase oscillator
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 2
An event changing the unwrapped relative phase by one cycle, usually 2π, compared with a phase-locked state. Thermally activated phase slips correspond to crossing neighboring barriers of a tilted washboard potential. Successive slips can produce long-term drift even while small fluctuations appear confined on shorter times.
