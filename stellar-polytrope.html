Stellar polytrope

 Home Physics Branch of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 241 Articles: 8
A stellar polytrope obeys P=Kρ1+1/n for constant K and polytropic index n. Its dimensionless density profile satisfies the Lane-Emden equation.
