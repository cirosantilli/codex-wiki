Liouville vector field (ιZ​ω=λ,LZ​ω=ω)

For an exact symplectic manifold with ω=dλ, the Liouville vector field is defined by ιZ​ω=λ. Cartan's magic formula gives LZ​ω=ω. On a cotangent bundle using ω=−dα, take λ=−α; then Z=∑i​pi​∂pi​​ generates the cotangent fiber-dilation flow.
