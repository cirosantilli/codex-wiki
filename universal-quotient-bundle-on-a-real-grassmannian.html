Universal quotient bundle on a real Grassmannian (QW​=RN/W)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Vector subbundle Quotient vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 160 Articles: 2
Over Grk​(RN), quotient the trivial bundle by the tautological bundle. The quotient is a vector bundle because it identifies continuously with the orthogonal-complement bundle via [v]↦PW⊥​v. Its rank is N−k.
