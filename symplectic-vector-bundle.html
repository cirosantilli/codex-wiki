Symplectic vector bundle

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
A symplectic vector bundle has a smooth fiberwise symplectic form, making each fiber a symplectic vector space. Its structure group reduces to the symplectic group. A symplectic normal bundle is an important example.
