Continuity into a product space (f:Y→∏i​Xi​ continuous⟺∀i, πi​∘f continuous)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Product topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 127
For a topological space Y and topological spaces Xi​, a function f:Y→∏i​Xi​ is continuous into the product topology exactly when every coordinate function fi​=πi​∘f is continuous.[ref]
Proof outline: one direction uses function composition with the continuous projection maps. For the other, the preimage of a basic cylinder set is
f−1(B(F,U))=⋂i∈F​fi−1​(Ui​),
(47)
a finite set intersection of open sets in Y. This gives continuity on every basic test. It explains, for example, why t↦(t,t2,t3,…) from the real line to RN is continuous in the product topology: each coordinate is a polynomial function. The general coordinate criterion fails for the box topology, where infinitely many preimages can be required at once. For example, g(t)=(t,t,…) has continuous coordinate functions, but the preimage of the box ∏j≥1​(−1/j,1/j) is {0}, which is not an open set in the real line.
