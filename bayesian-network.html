Bayesian network (p(x)=∏j​p(xj​∣xpa(j)​))

A Bayesian network consists of a Directed acyclic graph G whose nodes are random variables, together with conditional distributions satisfying p(x)=∏j​p(xj​∣xpaG​(j)​). Its Directed acyclic graph encodes conditional independence constraints. For unrestricted binary variables it has ∑j​2∣paG​(j)∣ free conditional probabilities. A Directed acyclic graph used this way need not have a causal interpretation.
