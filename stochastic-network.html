Stochastic network

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 6k Articles: 114
A stochastic network combines resource constraints, routing, and random arrivals or service requirements. Loss networks reject requests that cannot acquire all required resources; flow-level network models let ongoing transfers share capacities.
