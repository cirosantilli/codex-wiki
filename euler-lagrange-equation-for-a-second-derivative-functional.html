Euler-Lagrange equation for a second-derivative functional (Fy​−(Fy′​)′+(Fy′′​)′′=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Calculus of variations
OurBigBook.com Words: 224 Articles: 3
For a sufficiently smooth functional I[y]=∫ab​F(x,y,y′,y′′)dx, the first variation in direction η is ∫(Fy​η+Fy′​η′+Fy′′​η′′)dx. Twice applying integration by parts gives the interior coefficient Fy​−(Fy′​)′+(Fy′′​)′′ and boundary expression [(Fy′​−(Fy′′​)′)η+Fy′′​η′]ab​. Variations supported in the interior and the fundamental lemma of the calculus of variations force the displayed equation. The remaining boundary terms give the natural boundary conditions for second-derivative functionals.
