Holding force on a contracting nozzle (F=ρQ2(A0​/A1​−1)2/(2A0​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Euler equations for an inviscid fluid Integral momentum equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For steady ideal flow through a horizontal contracting nozzle, Bernoulli equation gives inlet gauge pressure p0​=ρQ2(A1−2​−A0−2​)/2. The integral momentum equation gives the fluid's force on the nozzle as p0​A0​−ρQ2(A1−1​−A0−1​) in the outlet direction. Simplification yields the displayed nonnegative magnitude. The external holding force has equal magnitude and the opposite direction.
