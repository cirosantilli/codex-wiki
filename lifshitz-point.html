Lifshitz point (K(p)∼p⊥2​+p∥4​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon
OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 1
A multicritical point where disordered, spatially uniform ordered and spatially modulated phases meet. The quadratic stiffness in one or more directions vanishes, so a positive higher-derivative term stabilises the Landau-Ginzburg theory. For a uniaxial case the kernel at the point has p⊥2​+p∥4​, with the coefficient of p∥2​ tuned to zero.
