Real Ginzburg–Landau equation (AT​=rA+ξAXX​−g∣A∣2A)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory Amplitude equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 416 Articles: 5
The real-coefficient Ginzburg–Landau amplitude equation has complex A and real parameters, usually ξ,g>0 near a supercritical stationary pattern onset. A detuned plane wave A=ρeiQX has ρ2=(r−ξQ2)/g. Its phase and amplitude sidebands determine the narrower Eckhaus instability stability band. It is a local envelope model; additional conserved fields or mean flows may need their own equations.
