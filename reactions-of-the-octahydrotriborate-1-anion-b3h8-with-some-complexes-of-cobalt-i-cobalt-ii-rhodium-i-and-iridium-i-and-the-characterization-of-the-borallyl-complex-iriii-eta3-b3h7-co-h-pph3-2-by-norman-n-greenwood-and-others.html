Journal of the Chemical Society, Dalton Transactions, 196–200. The published title uses bracketed formulas and superscript oxidation states. The original abstract records octahydrotriborate reactions and a borallyl iridium product; it must not be cited as direct crystallographic proof of a different rhodium compound.
