Inverse Galois problem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 131 Articles: 1
The inverse Galois problem asks which finite groups occur as Galois groups over a given field, with the classical problem taking the base field to be Q. A regular realization over K(T) and Galois-preserving Hilbert specialization solve an instance over a Hilbertian field. Realization over an algebraically closed constant field's rational function field alone does not supply descent to Q.
