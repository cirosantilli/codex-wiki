Cross-spectrum of two stationary time series (fXY​(λ)=2π1​∑h​E[Xt​Yt+h​]e−ihλ)

OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For zero-mean real weakly stationary processes whose cross-covariances are summable, the cross-spectrum is the Fourier series of their lagged covariance, with the displayed convention. Reality gives fXY​(−λ)=fXY​(λ)​, but the cross-spectrum need not be real or even. Exchanging the two series gives fYX​(λ)=fXY​(λ)​. The entries form a Hermitian spectral density of a stationary process matrix.
