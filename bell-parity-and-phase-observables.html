Bell parity and phase observables (Bparity​=(I−Z⊗Z)/2,Bphase​=(I−X⊗X)/2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Maximally entangled state Generalized Bell basis Bell basis
OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 1
The Bell basis diagonalizes the commuting Pauli operators Z⊗Z and X⊗X. Two local anticommutations make the joint operators commute. Their signs encode parity and relative phase. The displayed projectors turn the signed eigenvalues into literal zero-or-one bit eigenvalues: even parity and plus phase have bit zero.
