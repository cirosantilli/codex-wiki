Atmospheric isobar

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Shallow water equations Geostrophic balance
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
An atmospheric isobar is a line of equal pressure on a specified atmospheric map. Its spacing indicates the horizontal pressure gradient at that map's reference level; a closer spacing means a larger gradient for the same contour interval.
