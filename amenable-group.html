Amenable group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 507 Articles: 13
A discrete group G is amenable when it admits a left-invariant finitely additive probability measure on all subsets of G. Equivalently, it admits an invariant mean on the bounded real-valued functions on G.
