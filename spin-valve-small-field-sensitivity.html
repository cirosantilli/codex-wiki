Spin-valve small-field sensitivity (dR/dB=(dR/dθ)(dθ/dB))

 ... Electric charge Electric current Electrical resistance and conductance Electrical resistance Magnetoresistance Spin valve
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
A spin valve detects a small field by rotating a free layer relative to a pinned magnetic reference. The chain rule separates transport angular response from the magnetic rotational susceptibility. An angular response proportional to 1−cosθ has zero slope at perfect parallel and antiparallel alignment but a nonzero slope near perpendicular alignment. The smallest detectable field also depends on electronic and magnetic noise, thermal stability, hysteresis and the chosen bias current; a large magnetoresistance ratio alone does not determine sensitivity.
