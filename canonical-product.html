Canonical product (∏n​Epn​​(z/an​))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Holomorphic function Entire function Weierstrass factorization theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 207 Articles: 2
A canonical product builds a prescribed discrete zero set from Weierstrass elementary factors. Choose the orders pn​ so that the logarithms of tail factors converge uniformly on each compact subset. Infinite product convergence from logarithmic tails then gives an entire function with exactly those zeros, counted with the prescribed multiplicities. When ∑n​∣an​∣−1<∞, factors of order zero suffice.
