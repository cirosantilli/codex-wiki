Rational Whitehead theorem (H∗​(f;Q) invertible⇒π∗​(f)⊗Q invertible)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Homotopy group Whitehead theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
For maps between simply connected spaces, a rational homology equivalence is a rational homotopy equivalence. It follows from the relative Hurewicz theorem modulo torsion, or from rationalization and the homological Whitehead theorem.
