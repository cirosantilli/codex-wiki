Scalar wave separation in Kerr spacetime (Ψ=e−iωt+imϕR(r)Θ(θ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole Kerr black hole
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
For the massless Klein-Gordon equation on a Kerr black hole, the covariant wave operator separates into radial and angular ordinary differential equations. With K=(r2+a2)ω−am and angular separation constant Λ, the radial equation is (ΔR′)′+[K2/Δ−a2ω2+2amω−Λ]R=0. The angular equation is (sinθΘ′)′/sinθ+[a2ω2cos2θ−m2/sin2θ+Λ]Θ=0. No extra radial factor is included in the displayed ansatz.
