Heterotrimeric G protein

 Home Biology Protein G protein
OurBigBook.com Words: 89 Articles: 5
A heterotrimeric G protein is a signaling assembly with α, β and γ subunits. Receptor-promoted nucleotide exchange on α regulates interactions with βγ and effectors; both signaling components can contribute downstream.
