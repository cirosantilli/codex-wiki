Constrained order-parameter free energy (Fc​(M)=−kB​TV−1logZM​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Landau theory Landau free energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 147 Articles: 1
Fix the spatial mean of a scalar order parameter to M and sum the Boltzmann weights of all configurations with that mean. The resulting constrained partition function ZM​ defines this free-energy density, with an additive normalization fixed consistently. For an applied conjugate field h, Z(h)=∫dMe−βT​V[Fc​(M)−hM], up to the chosen measure normalization. The thermodynamic limit gives f(h)=infM​[Fc​(M)−hM]. A homogeneous Landau free energy is an approximation to this constrained potential; phase-separated configurations can convexify it in the full thermodynamic limit.
