Lévy reflection theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Von Neumann hierarchy
OurBigBook.com Words: 181 Articles: 3
For every finite collection Φ of first-order formulas, there are arbitrarily large ordinals α such that, for every φ∈Φ and all parameters in Vα​,
Vα​⊨φ⟺V⊨φ.
(11)
The reflecting ordinals for Φ form a closed unbounded class.
