Mordell-Weil saturation (Hsat={P∈E(K):nP∈H for some n≥1})

 ... Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Mordell-Weil group
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
The saturation of a subgroup H of the Mordell-Weil group consists of all points with a positive multiple in H. Saturation includes the full torsion subgroup. For a full-rank H containing that torsion, it equals E(K), but the index of H may exceed one. Checking that ℓP∈H forces P∈H for every prime divisor of a certified index bound proves that H is already the whole group. A canonical height pairing proves independence of found points; saturation answers the separate question of whether a full-rank subgroup is the full group.
