Jacobi truncation of two singular isothermal spheres (rt​=Dσd​/(2​σh​))

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Tidal radius Jacobi tidal radius
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For a low-mass singular isothermal sphere satellite on a circular orbit of radius D in a singular isothermal sphere host, Ω2=2σh2​/D2 and Φh′′​=−2σh2​/D2. The radial differential effective acceleration is (Ω2−Φh′′​)r. Balancing it with satellite gravity 2σd2​/r gives the displayed Jacobi tidal radius and bound mass mt​=2​Dσd3​/(Gσh​). Equal mean density gives a different order-one approximation, rt​=Dσd​/σh​; eccentric orbits require an orbital-history prescription.
