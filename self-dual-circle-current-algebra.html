Self-dual circle current algebra (SU(2)L​×SU(2)R​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 63
At R=α′​, a chiral compact boson has operator product expansion YL​(z)YL​(w)∼−α′log(z−w)/2. The currents J3=i∂YL​/α′​ and J±=:e±2iYL​/α′​: have conformal weight one. Contractions give J3(z)J3(w)∼1/[2(z−w)2], J3(z)J±(w)∼±J±(w)/(z−w) and J+(z)J−(w)∼1/(z−w)2+2J3(w)/(z−w). These are the level-one SU(2) Lie algebra currents. The right-moving copy supplies a second factor. Combining a current with a noncompact coordinate derivative gives a massless string vertex operator for each of the six vectors; four are additional to the generic two Cartan vectors.
