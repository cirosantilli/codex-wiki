Universal kriging

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Geostatistics Kriging
OurBigBook.com Words: 161
Universal kriging allows an unknown regression drift:
E[Z]=Xβ,E[Z0​]=x0T​β.
(12)
The design matrix X has full column rank, its entries and x0​ are known, and the regression coefficients β are unknown. For example, rows (1,si​) and target row (1,s0​) allow a linear spatial trend. Requiring wTZ to have zero bias for every β gives XTw=x0​. With known positive-definite matrix observation covariance matrix Σ, the minimizing weights solve
(ΣXT​X0​)(wη​)=(cx0​​),
(13)
where η is a vector of Lagrange multipliers. Equivalently, the generalized least squares estimate and prediction are
β​=(XTΣ−1X)−1XTΣ−1z,Z0​=x0T​β​+cTΣ−1(z−Xβ​).
(14)
The first term is the estimated trend alone; the second predicts the correlated regression residuals. With q=x0​−XTΣ−1c, the error variance is
sUK2​=v0​−cTΣ−1c+qT(XTΣ−1X)−1q.
(15)
The last, nonnegative term accounts for estimating the specified drift coefficients. Merely inserting a fitted drift into simple kriging and keeping its known-mean error variance would omit this term. For a Gaussian random field, the same expressions arise by integrating over drift coefficients in a diffuse prior distribution limit; this is an additional inferential interpretation, not an assumption required by the linear optimization.[ref]
