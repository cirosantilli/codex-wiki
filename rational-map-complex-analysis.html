Rational map (complex analysis)

 ... Analysis Complex analysis Isolated singularity Classification of isolated singularities Pole Meromorphic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 336 Articles: 6
A rational map of the Riemann sphere is a quotient of two complex polynomials, extended to infinity. A nonconstant map has a well-defined positive degree and is a branched covering of the sphere.
