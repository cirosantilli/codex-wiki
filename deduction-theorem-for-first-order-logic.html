Deduction theorem for first-order logic (Γ,A⊢B⟹Γ⊢A→B)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Deduction theorem for propositional logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 1
In the usual first-order Hilbert system, logical implication discharge requires the appropriate generalization restriction: a variable generalized in the derivation must not be free in the assumption being discharged. Equivalently use a proof system whose generalization rule already forbids variables free in open assumptions. One can first discharge a temporary assumption and then generalize if the quantified variable is not free in the remaining assumptions. Ignoring this side condition would allow deriving universal conclusions from a single arbitrary instance as an open hypothesis.
