Rosser proof-order dependence

 ... Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order theory Gödel numbering Arithmetical provability predicate Rosser provability predicate
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 1
The internal derivability conditions of a Rosser provability predicate depend on the proof numbering, even when the ordinary provability predicate is unchanged up to provable equivalence. Joining proofs of A and A→B can increase their codes beyond the two bounds in the Rosser hypotheses, leaving the absence of earlier proofs of ¬B unestablished. Internal modus ponens rule therefore needs a separate argument for the chosen numbering. Proof predicates can be constructed with different combinations of closure and introspection properties.
