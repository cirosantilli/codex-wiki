p-adic unit (Zp×​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field
OurBigBook.com Words: 681 Articles: 14
A p-adic unit is an invertible element of the ring Zp​. For a p-adic integer x, this is equivalent to vp​(x)=0, ∣x∣p​=1, or a nonzero initial p-adic digit b0​. A rational number a/b is a p-adic unit when neither its reduced numerator nor its denominator is divisible by p.
Thus 3 and 1/3 are p-adic units at p=2, while 2 is a p-adic integer with no inverse inside Z2​. The modular inversion converges to a p-adic inverse construction computes the inverse of an integer unit to arbitrary finite precision.
