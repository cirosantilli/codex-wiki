Metric compatibility makes the curvature action on a two-component spinor symplectic, so its lowered curvature spinor is symmetric in each pair. The pair-exchange symmetry of the Riemann curvature tensor makes XABCD​=XCDAB​. This six-component tensor decomposes into the five-component totally symmetric Weyl curvature spinor ΨABCD​=X(ABCD)​ and one scalar term. In the displayed normalization, with alternating forms satisfying ϵABϵAB​=2, its scalar is Λ=XABCD​ϵACϵBD/3. This coefficient follows by contracting the displayed scalar term; different normalizations of that term give different scalar conventions.
