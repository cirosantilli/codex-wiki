A massless fixed-axis pulley carries a rim point mass M at radius R. A mechanical string gripping its rim without slipping passes over a fixed guide pulley to a hanging mass m. Choose the angle θ from downward vertical so the hanging mass descends by Rθ. The Lagrangian is (M+m)R2θ˙2/2+MgRcosθ+mgRθ. The stable equilibrium satisfies sinθe​=m/M with cosθe​>0, and exists for 0<m<M. Its small-amplitude angular frequency is [Mgcosθe​/((M+m)R)]1/2.
