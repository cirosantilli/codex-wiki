Sedimentation drift of a weighted two-rod body (Δx=2L/3)

 ... Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Slender-body theory Slender-body force density Right-angle two-rod resistance matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
Attach weights mg at the joint and λmg at each outer endpoint of the right-angle two-rod resistance matrix geometry. With the first rod initially horizontal and the second vertically upwards, quasistatic Stokes flow gives θ˙=(1−λ)mg(cosθ−sinθ)/(4CL2). For λ<1 the orientation tends to π/4; for λ>1 it tends to −3π/4. Eliminating time gives x−x0​=(2L/3)(1+sinθ−cosθ) and the same rightward net displacement 2L/3 in both cases. For λ>1 the initial drift is leftwards before reversing. At λ=1 there is no rotation or lateral drift, illustrating noncommuting infinite-time and parameter limits.
