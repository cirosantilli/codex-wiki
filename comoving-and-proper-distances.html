Comoving and proper distances

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
Comoving and proper distances are two cosmological distance conventions. For simultaneous radial separations in an FLRW model, proper distance equals the comoving radial distance multiplied by the scale factor at that cosmic time. A comoving coordinate remains fixed on an ideal comoving observer's worldline.
