Body force

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 67 Articles: 1
A body force acts throughout a fluid volume. Gravity and electromagnetic forces are standard examples; a body force per unit mass f contributes ρf to the momentum equation.
