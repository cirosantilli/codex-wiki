Representable functor

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Functor Functor category
OurBigBook.com Words: 163 Articles: 4
A covariant functor is representable when it is naturally isomorphic to C(A,−) for some object A; a contravariant functor is representable when it is naturally isomorphic to C(−,A).
