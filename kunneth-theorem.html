Künneth theorem

For chain complexes of free modules over a principal ideal domain R, the Künneth theorem gives a natural split short exact sequence
0→⨁i+j=n​Hi​(C)⊗R​Hj​(D)→Hn​(C⊗R​D)→⨁i+j=n−1​Tor1R​(Hi​(C),Hj​(D))→0,

although the splitting is not natural. Over a field the Tor term vanishes, and the cohomological cross product gives H∗(X×Y;k)≅H∗(X;k)⊗k​H∗(Y;k) as graded rings under standard finiteness hypotheses.
