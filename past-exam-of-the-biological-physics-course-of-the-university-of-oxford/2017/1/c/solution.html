For the parameter sensitivity of autorepressed expression, unregulated production has Yst​=β/α, so its logarithmic sensitivity is one. In the strong Hill approximation, Yst​=(βKn/α)1/(n+1), with K,α,n held fixed. Hence
S(Yst​,β)=1without feedback,S(Yst​,β)=n+11​with strong Hill repression.​
(6)
The ideal logic level Yst​≃K instead gives S≃0, because changing production primarily changes the switching duty fraction, not the threshold. Finite Hill repression has a residual production sensitivity.
The exact Hill steady-state relation makes the comparison continuous. Write s=(Yst​/K)n; then β=αYst​(1+s) and dβ/dYst​=α[1+(n+1)s]. Thus
S(Yst​,β)=1+(n+1)s1+s​.​
(7)
It tends to one for weak repression and to 1/(n+1) for strong repression. The calculation concerns a local relative parameter change, not the response to an arbitrary large production perturbation.
