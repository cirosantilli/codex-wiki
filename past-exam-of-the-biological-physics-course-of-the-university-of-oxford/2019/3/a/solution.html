Past exam of the biological physics course of the University of Oxford / 2019 / 3 / a / Solution

Let the common production and removal constants be β and α, so an unrepressed protein approaches S=β/α. A simple constructive motif is
X⟶Y,X⟶Z1​,X⟶Z2​,Y⟶Z3​.
(17)
Choose thresholds KXY​=KXZ1​​=0.2S, KXZ2​​=0.4S, and KYZ3​​=0.5S. The logic approximation for gene regulation gives Y˙=βH(X−0.2S)−αY, Z˙1​=βH(X−0.2S)−αZ1​, Z˙2​=βH(X−0.4S)−αZ2​, and Z˙3​=βH(Y−0.5S)−αZ3​. These unequal thresholds are permitted in this part; the production and degradation rates remain equal.
After input onset, X=S[1−e−α(t−T1​)]. Production of Z1​ and Y begins at T1​+0.2231/α, Z2​ at T1​+0.5108/α, and Z3​ at T1​+(0.2231+ln2)/α=T1​+0.9163/α. Thus the required on-order is obtained. Take an input long enough that all these events occur, for example T2​−T1​=6/α.
At input termination, X=X2​e−α(t−T2​), where X2​=S[1−e−α(T2​−T1​)]. The higher direct threshold is crossed first: Z2​ production stops at t2​=T2​+α−1ln[X2​/(0.4S)], and Z1​ and Y production stop at t1​=T2​+α−1ln[X2​/(0.2S)]. At that instant Y1​=S{1−e−α[t1​−T1​−0.2231/α]}, so Z3​ stops later at t3​=t1​+α−1ln[Y1​/(0.5S)]. For the example, the delays after T2​ are approximately 0.914, 1.607 and 2.300 in units of α−1:
Z1​ on before Z2​ on before Z3​;Z2​ off before Z1​ off before Z3​.​
(18)
