Treat the target as a stationary perfectly absorbing sphere of reaction radius r0​, surrounded by an effectively uniform reservoir of protein number density CA​. At steady state the radial diffusion equation gives
∇2c=0,c(r0​)=0,c(∞)=CA​,c(r)=CA​(1−rr0​​).
(1)
The inward capture rate per target is the integral of the Fick's first law:
J=4πr02​DA​drdc​​r0​​=4πDA​r0​CA​.
(2)
For dilute independent targets of number density CB​, the volume association rate is JCB​, so
ka​=4πDA​r0​.​
(3)
This is the Smoluchowski association rate, in volume per time because the concentrations are number densities. If the target also diffuses, use the relative diffusion coefficient DA​+DB​. Orientation constraints, incomplete absorption and chromosome geometry can reduce the actual association rate relative to this ideal collision limit.
