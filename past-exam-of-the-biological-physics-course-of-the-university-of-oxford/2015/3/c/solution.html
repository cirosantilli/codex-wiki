Assume the structural surface charge density stays fixed while a divalent chloride is added, without changing T or ϵ. Let v=eϕ0​/(kB​T) and δc be the increment of the bulk divalent concentration. Electroneutral addition also increases bulk chloride by 2δc. Varying the squared Grahame equation at fixed σ gives
0=δc(e−2v+2ev−3)−kB​Te​[c1+​(0)+2c2+​(0)−c1−​(0)−2c2−​(0)]δϕ0​.
(15)
At a strongly negative wall, coions are small. Dropping their contribution to the denominator and 2ev in the numerator gives the requested leading approximation
δϕ0​≃ekB​T​c1+​(0)+2c2+​(0)δc(e−2v−3)​.​
(16)
The −3 includes the change in bulk osmotic concentration of one added divalent counterion and its two monovalent coions. The numerator's increment is a bulk concentration; the denominator contains surface concentrations.[ref] Extra counterions screen the wall and make its negative electric potential less negative. At fixed bulk calcium, cCa​(0)=cCa∞​e−2ϕ0​/VT​ therefore decreases. Under the stipulated proportionality, synaptic activity decreases as well; this argument isolates electrostatic accumulation rather than assuming a separate specific magnesium effect on the ion channels.
For δc=5mM, the printed linear approximation gives δϕ0​=9.53434mV. Linearizing the activity too gives δA/A=−2δϕ0​/VT​=−0.7422, or approximately a 74% reduction. Exponentiating this approximate voltage instead gives A′/A=0.4761. Neither is a controlled finite-increment prediction: the added divalent concentration is 2.5 times its initial value.
A consistent finite-change calculation is possible within the same Poisson-Boltzmann equation. Define, using mM throughout,
Sm​(u)=160(eu−1)+(2+m)(e2u−1)+(114+2m)(e−u−1)+25(e−2u−1).
(17)
Fixed σ requires S5​(u′)=S0​(2.84129). The physical positive root is u′=2.58241, giving
ϕ0′​=−66.3488mV,Δϕ0​=6.65122mV,AA′​=e2(u′−u)=0.595857.​
(18)
Thus the self-consistent finite-change estimate is about a 40.4% reduction. Both estimates rely on fixed surface charge, ideal ionic activities, and unchanged bulk calcium; the limitations of the large local concentrations remain.
