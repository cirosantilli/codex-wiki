An inextensible worm-like chain describes entropic elasticity and diverges as x→L. Real protein backbones and folded protein domains also deform energetically under large forces. For an axial stretch modulus Ks​, a simple extensible-chain correction is
Lx​≃1−21​fLp​kB​T​​+Ks​f​.
(20)
The last term supplies extension through bond and structural deformation that the inextensible model excludes. It becomes especially noticeable on the high-force ends of rising branches.[ref] An unfolding transition itself is also outside a single equilibrium fixed-L branch. These mechanisms explain why the source explicitly distinguishes energetic and entropic elasticity.
