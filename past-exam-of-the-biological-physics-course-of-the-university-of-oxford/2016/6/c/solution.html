For independent available acceptors at equal distance, the multiple-acceptor FRET efficiency follows by adding the n parallel exit rates. Each contributes the same rate kT​, so the total rate is nkT​, rather than n times the single-pair transfer probability. Hence
En​=kD​+nkT​nkT​​,kD​kT​​=1−EE​,En​=1+(n−1)EnE​.​
(33)
Equivalently, En​=n/[n+(R/R0​)6]. Inverting gives
R=R0​[En​n(1−En​)​]1/6.​
(34)
With n=3 and En​=0.75, the bracket is exactly one and R=R0​=6.5 nm. Each isolated pair would have efficiency 1/2; the three transfer rates together give 3/(1+3)=3/4. Multiplying 1/2 by three would violate the probability bound and is not a valid calculation.
This model assumes one donor excitation, identical pairwise transfer rates, no coherent coupling between acceptors, no acceptor saturation and no change in donor photophysics or orientation on adding labels. Different separations instead require summing kT,j​ individually. Acceptor-to-acceptor transfer can alter which acceptor emits; it does not automatically change the donor's first-transfer probability when the donor exit rates remain those specified.
