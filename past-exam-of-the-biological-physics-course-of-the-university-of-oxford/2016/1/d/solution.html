Rapid permeation is possible because the hydrated pore and coordinating groups of the selectivity filter provide a favorable free-energy landscape for repeated ion transit. Several ions can occupy the filter and their interactions favor onward passage, while the channel remains open for many successive transits. Selectivity need not mean that each transported ion remains tightly trapped for a long time. This is passive downhill transport; it does not consume one ATP molecule per ion crossing.
