The controlled weak-force condition is fb≪kB​T, not merely that a short part of a measured curve looks straight. With the supplied b, kB​T/b≃10.3pN at 25∘C. A raw force near 30pN, as in the figure's labelled low-extension portion, would give u≃2.9 and is not asymptotically weak for those literal segment parameters. A force offset or different effective model might change that interpretation, but no such correction should be invented from the plot.
