Reinsurance

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 954 Articles: 18
Reinsurance transfers part of an insurer’s claim liability to another insurer. If aggregate claims are S, a retained payout g(S) with 0≤g(S)≤S leaves the reinsurer with S−g(S). Quota share reinsurance retains a fixed fraction, whereas aggregate stop loss reinsurance retains losses only up to a fixed aggregate threshold.
