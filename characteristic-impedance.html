Characteristic impedance (Z0​=V+/I+)

 Home Electrical engineering Transmission line
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 1
The characteristic impedance is the voltage-to-current ratio of a single forward travelling wave on a uniform transmission line. For real Z0​ and a real load resistance RL​, the voltage-wave reflection coefficient is (RL​−Z0​)/(RL​+Z0​) and the power accepted by the load is the incident power times 4RL​Z0​/(RL​+Z0​)2.
