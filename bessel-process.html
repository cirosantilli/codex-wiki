Bessel process

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 18
A Bessel process of dimension d is the radial part of d-dimensional Brownian motion. Away from zero it solves the stochastic differential equation
dRt​=2Rt​d−1​dt+dWt​.
(14)
This equation also defines noninteger dimensions through its weak solutions.
