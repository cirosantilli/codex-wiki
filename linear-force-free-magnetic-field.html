Linear force-free magnetic field (∇×B=λB,λ constant)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Force-free magnetic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
A force-free magnetic field is linear when the proportionality between its curl and itself is a spatial constant. The curl of the curl identity and zero divergence give the vector Helmholtz equation ∇2B+λ2B=0. Here λ has inverse-length units; specifying a constant current-to-field coefficient with another normalization changes its numerical definition.
