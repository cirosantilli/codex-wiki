Causal Green function of a damped oscillator (G(t)=H(t)e−ktsin(ωt)/ω)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Damped harmonic oscillator
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 2
For L=Dt2​+2kDt​+k2+ω2, the causal Green function satisfies LG=δ(t). It is continuous at zero, with a unit jump in its first derivative. Its smooth positive-time kernel g obeys g(0)=0,g′(0)=1. The zero-frequency limit is H(t)te−kt. Shifting G gives the zero-initial-data response to a delayed Dirac delta function.
