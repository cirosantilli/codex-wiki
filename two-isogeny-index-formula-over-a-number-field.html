For the two-isogeny formula ϕ:E→E′ over a number field, the two-torsion square-class homomorphisms have kernels ϕ​E′(K) and ϕE(K). Consequently
[E(K):2E(K)]=δ#α(E(K))#α′(E′(K))​,δ=[kerϕ​:kerϕ​∩ϕE(K)].

Indeed 2E(K)=ϕ​ϕE(K), and the surjection from E′(K)/ϕE(K) to ϕ​E′(K)/2E(K) has kernel kerϕ​/(kerϕ​∩ϕE(K)). Combined with the Mordell-Weil theorem, the index is 2r#E(K)[2], where r is the rank of an abelian group. This formula prevents an erroneous extra factor of two in an isogeny descent.
