Improper integral

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Riemann integration Riemann integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 658 Articles: 9
An improper integral is defined by taking a limit when an integration endpoint is infinite or the integrand is unbounded. Its convergence must be established before ordinary integral manipulations are applied.
