Centered polynomial birth move in reversible-jump sampling (β0′​=β0​−cz)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Markov chain Monte Carlo Reversible-jump Markov chain Monte Carlo
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
A polynomial regression birth move can introduce a coefficient z while shifting the intercept by −cz, with c the sample average of the new monomial. The fitted-value increment is z(xk+1−c), whose sample average vanishes. The Jacobian determinant of the coefficient transformation is one. A reversible-jump Markov chain Monte Carlo acceptance ratio must still include the normalized dimension-dependent prior distributions, the model-order prior distribution, the birth/death selection probabilities and the proposal density for z.
