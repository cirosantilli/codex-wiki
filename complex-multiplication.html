Complex multiplication

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 216 Articles: 3
An abelian variety of dimension g has complex multiplication when its rational endomorphism ring contains a commutative semisimple Q-algebra of dimension 2g. For an elliptic curve in characteristic zero, this means its geometric endomorphism ring of an elliptic curve is an order in an imaginary quadratic field. The resulting rank-one Tate module representations connect torsion points to class field theory.
