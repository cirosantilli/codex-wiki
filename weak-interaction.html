Weak interaction

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model
OurBigBook.com Words: 849 Articles: 18
The weak interaction is the short-range chiral interaction mediated by the W and Z bosons. Above the electroweak scale it is part of the unbroken SU(2)L​×U(1)Y​ electroweak gauge theory.
