For the planar porous medium equation nt​=[D0​/(3n02​)]Δ(n3), the mass-Q point-source similarity solution is n=n0​λ−2[1−r2/(r02​λ2)]+​​, with λ=(6D0​t/r02​)1/6 and r02​=3Q/(2πn0​). Its front grows as t1/6 and its central density falls as t−1/3. The mass conservation integral uses the planar area element 2πrdr. Although the density slope diverges at the front, its diffusion flux vanishes there, giving a compactly supported weak solution with initial measure Qδ(2).
