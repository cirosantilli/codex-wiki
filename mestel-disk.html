Mestel disk (Σ(R)=vM2​/(2πGR))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 247 Articles: 4
A Mestel disk is a thin axisymmetric astrophysical disk with surface density inversely proportional to radius. Its galaxy rotation curve is exactly flat, vc​=vM​, and vc2​=GM(R)/R despite its nonspherical geometry. In the Legendre expansion of thin-disk gravity, all positive-degree interior and exterior terms cancel. Its total mass is infinite, so only potential differences, rather than a potential zero at infinity, are defined.
