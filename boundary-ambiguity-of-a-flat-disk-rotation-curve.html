Boundary ambiguity of a flat disk rotation curve

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Mestel disk Mestel disk potential-density pair
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The midplane galaxy rotation curve fixes the radial derivative of the Newtonian gravitational potential, not every exterior boundary condition. Adding a∣z∣ leaves that radial derivative unchanged but adds a uniform sheet of surface density of a disk a/(2πG). The pure scale-free Mestel disk excludes such extra sources.
