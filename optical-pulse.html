Optical pulse (τ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Light
OurBigBook.com Words: 500 Articles: 11
An optical pulse is a finite-duration envelope of light. A nearly monochromatic pulse has many carrier oscillations within its duration τ: in units with light speed one, its wavelength obeys λ≪τ. A slowly varying propagation-time map stretches the pulse duration by its derivative and divides the observed frequency by the same factor.
