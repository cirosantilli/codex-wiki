Prize allocation rule (xi​(b)≥0,∑i​xi​(b)≤1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Contest theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
A prize allocation rule assigns winning probabilities or divisible prize shares to a profile of efforts. In an all-pay auction, the greatest effort wins; a proportional allocation contest instead assigns shares continuously according to relative effort. The rule is essential data of a contest model.
