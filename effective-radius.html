Effective radius (Re​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The projected radius enclosing half an object's total luminosity in a specified photometric passband. For circular apertures, the mean linear surface brightness inside it is Ie​=L/(2πRe2​). Elliptical apertures require an axis-ratio factor or a circularized radius; that convention must be stated when comparing calibrations.
