Random-cluster model

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Percolation theory Site percolation Dependent percolation
OurBigBook.com Words: 952 Articles: 21
On a finite graph, the random-cluster model assigns an edge configuration ω probability proportional to
po(ω)(1−p)c(ω)qk(ω),
(1)
where o,c,k count open edges, closed edges, and open connected components.
