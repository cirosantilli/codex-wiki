Paschen-Back effect

 Home Physics Atomic physics Zeeman splitting
OurBigBook.com Words: 272 Articles: 3
In the Paschen-Back effect, the external magnetic field interaction becomes large compared with the spin-orbit interaction that holds L and S coupled. Then J ceases to be a good approximate quantum number; mL​,mS​ become more useful, with leading electronic shifts ΔE≃μB​B(mL​+gs​mS​). In the ideal complete-decoupling limit an electric-dipole interaction preserves mS​, so its spin contribution cancels between the levels and orbital triplet spacing can reappear. Intermediate fields require mixing the coupled states. This is a change of coupling regime, distinct from the weak-field anomalous Zeeman effect; still larger fields require additional terms beyond this linear model.
