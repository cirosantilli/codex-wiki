Indoor-temperature limit of a Carnot heat pump (Tc,min​=Th​−W˙max​Th​/λ​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat pump
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
A reversible heat pump maintaining Th​ against an external Tc​<Th​ has heating coefficient of performance Th​/(Th​−Tc​). If its maximum work input is W˙max​ and the building loses heat at rate λ(Th​−Tc​), maintaining equilibrium requires λ(Th​−Tc​)2≤W˙max​Th​. This yields the displayed minimum external temperature, provided it lies in the physical positive-temperature range. If the rating instead specifies maximum delivered heat, the bound is Th​−Tc​≤Q˙​h,max​/λ; a heat-output rating cannot be substituted for a work-input rating in the Carnot formula.
