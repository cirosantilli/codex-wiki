Salt budget

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Conservation law Concentration Salinity
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
The conservation law for salt in a reservoir: the rate of change of its salt content equals inflowing minus outflowing salt transport and any explicit salt sources. Freshwater addition changes salinity without directly adding salt. For a well-mixed fixed-volume reservoir, salt content is proportional to its volume times salinity.
