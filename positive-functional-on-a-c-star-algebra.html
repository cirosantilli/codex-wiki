Positive functional on a C-star algebra

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra C-star algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 73 Articles: 2
A bounded linear functional τ on a C-star algebra is positive when τ(x)≥0 for every Positive element of a C-star algebra. Positivity implies
∣τ(y∗x)∣2≤τ(x∗x)τ(y∗y)
(91)
and ∥τ∥=τ(1); conversely, ∥τ∥=τ(1) implies positivity.
