Triplet helium state (S=1)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Helium Helium atom
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A helium atom electronic state with total spin one. Its symmetric spin function requires an antisymmetric spatial wavefunction, excluding two electrons in the same spatial orbital. The three spin projections are magnetic substates of the same spin multiplet, not three separately distinguishable particles.
