Dimension theorem for Noetherian local rings

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Krull dimension
OurBigBook.com Words: 73 Articles: 1
For a Noetherian local ring (A,m), the Krull dimension of A, the Krull dimension of the associated graded ring grm​(A), and the order of the pole at t=1 of its Hilbert series are equal.
