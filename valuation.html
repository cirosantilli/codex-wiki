Valuation (v)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Field
OurBigBook.com Words: 228 Articles: 5
A valuation assigns sizes to elements of a field in a way compatible with multiplication and addition. In additive notation, v(xy)=v(x)+v(y) and v(x+y)≥min{v(x),v(y)}.
