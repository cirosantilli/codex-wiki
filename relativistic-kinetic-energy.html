Relativistic kinetic energy (K=(γ−1)mc2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Energy Kinetic energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
For a free particle of rest mass m, subtract its rest energy mc2 from its total energy γmc2, where γ is the Lorentz factor. The low-speed binomial series gives K=mv2/2+3mv4/(8c2)+O(mv6/c4), recovering classical kinetic energy.
