Differential pom-pom model (σ=Gλ2A/trA−pI)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 179 Articles: 2
This differential model separates normalized molecular orientation B=A/trA from scalar stretch λ. With component-first velocity gradient K, it evolves by Dt​A=KA+AKT−(A−I)/τ1​ and Dt​λ=λB:K−(λ−1)/τ2​. Its relaxed state is A=I, B=I/3, λ=1. The displayed constant-relaxation stretch law is an idealization; variants can add saturation or nonlinear relaxation.
