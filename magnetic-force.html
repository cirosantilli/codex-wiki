Magnetic force (FB​=qv×B)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
The magnetic contribution to the Lorentz force on a point charge. It is perpendicular to that charge's instantaneous velocity, so its direct work on the charge is zero. Summing contributions in a conductor leads to the magnetic force on a current-carrying wire; mechanical work in moving circuits must also account for the electric fields and constraints that maintain the current.
