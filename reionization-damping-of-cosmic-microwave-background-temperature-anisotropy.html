Reionization damping of cosmic microwave background temperature anisotropy (e−τre​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Reionization
OurBigBook.com Words: 93 Articles: 1
Ignoring velocities and evolving potentials, a thin reionization screen transmits the primary Cosmic microwave background anisotropy with amplitude e−τre​ and adds a direction-averaged source. A plane wave propagating a distance Δχ from recombination to the screen has monopole factor j0​(kΔχ)=sin(kΔχ)/(kΔχ). This suppresses the rescattered source at kΔχ≫1, while coherent large-scale modes are unchanged to leading order.
