First-order linear differential equation (y′+p(x)y=q(x))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Linear ordinary differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 246 Articles: 3
A first-order linear differential equation has the form y′+p(x)y=q(x). Multiplication by an integrating factor μ satisfying μ′=pμ turns it into (μy)′=μq.
