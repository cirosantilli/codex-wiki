Dilation of a distribution (δt​u)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 2
For t>0, the dilation δt​u of a distribution on Rn is defined by
⟨δt​u,φ⟩=t−n⟨u,φ(⋅/t)⟩.
(868)
For a regular distribution this agrees with the function x↦u(tx).
