Ocean transport streamfunction (Hu=−ψy​, Hv=ψx​)

 ... Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Ocean circulation Wind stress Ocean gyre Sverdrup balance
OurBigBook.com Words: 58 Articles: 1
An ocean transport streamfunction defines depth-integrated horizontal transport. For constant depth H, it is H times the velocity streamfunction. Its difference between two boundaries gives signed transport across a connecting line.
