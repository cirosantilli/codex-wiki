Type I migration

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planetary system dynamics Planetary migration
OurBigBook.com Words: 420 Articles: 10
Orbital migration of a low-mass embedded planet driven by its interaction with disk gas, without a deep gap. Spiral wave torques and corotation-region torques exchange orbital angular momentum. The sign depends on the disk gradients and thermodynamics; this regime is not restricted to inward migration.
