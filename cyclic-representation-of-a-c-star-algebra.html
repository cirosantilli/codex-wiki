Cyclic representation of a C-star algebra (π(A)ξ​=H)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Representation of an associative algebra Representation of a Banach algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
A representation of a C-star algebra on a Hilbert space is a star-homomorphism π:A→B(H). It is cyclic if some vector ξ has dense orbit span as displayed; the orbit π(A)ξ is itself a linear subspace. For a unital representation, a unit cyclic vector defines a state on a C-star algebra by a↦⟨π(a)ξ,ξ⟩. Conversely, the Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction realizes every state this way. Cyclicity means density under the action of the whole algebra, not necessarily under powers of a single chosen element.
