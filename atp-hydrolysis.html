ATP hydrolysis

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Hydrolysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
ATP hydrolysis converts ATP with water to products commonly including ADP and inorganic phosphate. Its available Gibbs free energy depends on chemical conditions and can drive a coupled process; ATP binding and hydrolysis can have distinct structural roles.
