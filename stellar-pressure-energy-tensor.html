Stellar pressure-energy tensor (Πij​=∫ρσij​d3x)

 ... Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics Collisionless Boltzmann equation Jeans equation Tensor virial theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
This tensor integrates the local pressure tensor, where σij​=⟨(vi​−ui​)(vj​−uj​)⟩ is the velocity covariance. Half its trace is the random kinetic energy. It enters the tensor virial theorem with coefficient one.
