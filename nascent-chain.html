Nascent chain

 Home Biology Protein Polypeptide
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
A nascent chain is a polypeptide chain still being synthesized by a ribosome. Its exposed targeting information can direct entry into the endoplasmic reticulum before biological translation finishes.
