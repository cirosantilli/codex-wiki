Degree of a holomorphic map (degf)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Valency theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
For a nonconstant holomorphic map f:X→Y between compact connected Riemann surfaces, its degree is
degf=∑p∈f−1(y)​mf​(p).
(55)
The valency theorem says that this integer is independent of y∈Y.
