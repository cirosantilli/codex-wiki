Equivalent width (W)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Radiative transfer
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
An absorption equivalent width is Wλ​=∫[1−Iλ​/Iλ0​]dλ, the wavelength width of a completely black interval that removes the same continuum-normalized area. For a narrow spectral line profile, small optical depth and negligible stimulated emission, the energy-density convention for Einstein coefficients gives Wλ​=hB12​N1​/ν12​. This follows by replacing 1−e−τν​ by τν​ and using ∣dλ/dν∣=c/ν2. At redshift z, the observed and rest-frame values satisfy Wobs​=(1+z)Wrest​.
