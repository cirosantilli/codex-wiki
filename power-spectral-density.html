Power spectral density (Sx​(f))

 Home Electrical engineering Signal processing Signal
OurBigBook.com Words: 138 Articles: 2
A power spectral density distributes the mean square of a signal over frequency. Its integral over the specified frequency domain gives that mean square. State whether the convention is one-sided or two-sided and whether frequency is measured in hertz or as angular frequency. For a zero-mean stationary signal, the mean square is its variance; spectral density of a stationary process gives the discrete-time convention.
