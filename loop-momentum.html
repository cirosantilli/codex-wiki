Loop momentum

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram Loop order
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A loop momentum is an independent momentum integrated over in a Feynman diagram. Momentum conservation leaves one independent loop momentum for each cycle counted by the loop order.
