Affine combination

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Affine geometry Affine space
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
An affine combination of points Pi​ is ∑i​ai​Pi​ with ∑i​ai​=1, allowing negative coefficients. It is independent of the choice of origin: translating every point by T translates the result by T. It also commutes with every affine map. When all coefficients are nonnegative, it is a convex combination, placing the result inside the convex hull of the points.
