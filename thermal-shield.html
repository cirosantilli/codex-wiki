Thermal shield

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Heat transfer Thermal insulation
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
A thermal shield reduces coupling to unwanted environmental temperature changes. Low-conductance supports and thermal insulation reduce heat transport, low-emissivity surfaces reduce thermal radiation, and a conducting inner enclosure can reduce temperature gradients. Combined with heat capacity these give a thermal low-pass response. Passive shielding cannot indefinitely reject a slow change in the entire environment and may increase the temperature rise from internal dissipation; active regulation addresses a different frequency range.
