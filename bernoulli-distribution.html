Bernoulli distribution (Bernoulli(p))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 185 Articles: 3
A Bernoulli random variable equals one with probability p and zero with probability 1−p. Its mean is p and its variance is p(1−p).
