Multiplying the two matrix exponentials with the Pauli matrix multiplication law gives
UF​=CI−iv⋅σ,C=cosacosb,v=(cosasinb,−sinasinb,−sinacosb).
(45)
One has C2+∣v∣2=1. If ∣v∣=0, put θ=2arccosC and n=v/∣v∣. The exponential of a Pauli vector gives UFN​=Icos(Nθ/2)−in⋅σsin(Nθ/2). Its action on a Bloch vector is a rotation through Nθ about n, and its quasienergies are ±ℏθ/(2T) modulo 2πℏ/T. When UF​=±I, its axis is arbitrary and the Bloch vector is unchanged.
