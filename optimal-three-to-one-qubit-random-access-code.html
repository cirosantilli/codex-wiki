Optimal three-to-one qubit random access code (p∗​=21​(1+1/3​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Quantum random access code
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
Encode signs sj​=(−1)bj​ by the unit Bloch vector (s1​,s2​,s3​)/3​. Perform the requested Pauli measurement and interpret a positive outcome as zero. Every input and index has success p∗​. To prove optimality, write each binary-measurement difference as Dj​=tj​I+vj​⋅σ, where positivity gives ∣vj​∣≤1−∣tj​∣. Averaging over all eight inputs cancels the tj​ terms. For any encoding vectors of norm at most one, the average success is at most 1/2+(1/48)∑s​∣∑j​sj​vj​∣. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality bounds the sum by 8∑j​∣vj​∣2​≤83​, proving the optimum even for mixed encodings and general binary measurements. The constant-success construction also maximizes worst-case success.
