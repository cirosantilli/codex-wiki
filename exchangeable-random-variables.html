Exchangeable random variables

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Joint probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 342 Articles: 3
A family of random variables is exchangeable if its joint probability distribution is unchanged by finite permutations of its coordinates. Independent and identically distributed random variables are exchangeable, but exchangeable random variables need not be independent random variables.
