Characteristic of an integral domain (charD=0 or a prime)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Ring Characteristic of a ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
The positive characteristic of a ring which is an integral domain is a prime number. The characteristic cannot be one because 1=0. If its least positive value were n=ab with 1<a,b<n, minimality would make a1 and b1 nonzero, while (a1)(b1)=n1=0 would give zero divisors.
The analogous result holds for a nonzero commutative nonunital ring without zero divisors, using the characteristic of a rng. If n=ab were composite and minimal, choose r with ar=0. For every s, (ar)(bs)=n(rs)=0, so cancellation forces bs=0 for every s, contradicting minimality of n.
