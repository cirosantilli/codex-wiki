Seismic reflection survey

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Seismology Seismic reflection
OurBigBook.com Words: 147 Articles: 4
A controlled-source survey recording reflected elastic waves to infer subsurface interfaces and their properties. Travel times constrain geometry through a velocity model; amplitudes also depend on source, propagation and impedance contrasts.
