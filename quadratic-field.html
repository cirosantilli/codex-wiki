Quadratic field

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Number field
OurBigBook.com Words: 197 Articles: 3
A quadratic number field is a number field of degree of a field extension two, necessarily of the form Q(d​) for a square-free integer d=0,1. Its two field embeddings send a+bd​ to a±bd​. If d>0, both images lie in the real numbers, giving a totally real number field; if d<0, they form a pair related by complex conjugation, giving an imaginary quadratic field. The ring of integers of a quadratic field depends on d modulo four.[ref]
