Adaptive optics

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument
OurBigBook.com Words: 306 Articles: 8
Adaptive optics measures changing wavefront errors and corrects them using a deformable mirror. A wavefront sensor supplies measurements to a fast feedback controller; a guide star provides the reference. It improves astronomical seeing toward diffraction-limited resolution.
