Doppler-free absorption line model (As​(x)=B(x)−1+4x2/ws2​Cs​​)

Construct an original illustrative spectrum using ordinary frequency detuning x=νL​−ν0​. Write the longitudinal Doppler shift in frequency units as u=v/λ0​, where λ0​=c/ν0​. Choose a normal distribution with standard deviation σD​ and a weak-probe Lorentzian line shape with half width at half maximum γν​:
g(u)=2π​σD​e−u2/(2σD2​)​,L(y)=y2+γν2​γν2​​,B(x)=∫−∞∞​g(u)L(−u)du∫−∞∞​g(u)L(x−u)du​.
(36)
Thus B(0)=1. Choose σD​=120 MHz and natural linewidth Γ/(2π)=6 MHz, so γν​=3 MHz. The Gaussian-only full width at half maximum is 22ln2​σD​≃282.58 MHz; the averaged profile also includes the finite Lorentzian line shape width.
Add a deliberately phenomenological Lamb dip:
As​(x)=B(x)−1+4x2/ws2​Cs​​,Cs​=0.241+ss​,ws​=2πΓ​1+s​.
(37)
Here As​ is the absorption coefficient normalized to the pump-off line-centre value. The factor 0.24 is chosen, not inferred from populations or a fit. The dip's assigned full width at half maximum ws​ borrows the single-atom power broadening scale purely to illustrate contrast versus width. It is not the preceding overlap integral or a fit to historical spectra. There are no crossover features in this isolated-transition toy spectrum.
For s=1, the subtracted dip has depth 0.12 and width 8.49 MHz; for s=9, depth 0.216 and width 18.97 MHz. Its half-depth points are x=±ws​/2. Measure that width relative to the curved background B(x), not a horizontal line through the total absorption minimum.
For a chosen cell optical depth τs​(x)=0.4As​(x), the line-centre pump-off transmission is e−0.4≃0.6703. At s=1 it is e−0.352≃0.7033. The transmitted probe therefore increases by about 4.92 percent relative to pump off. Absorption contrast, transmission contrast and spectral width are three different quantities.
