Parsec (pc)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar parallax
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
The parsec is a distance unit defined by the exact convention
1pc=π648000​au≃3.085677581×1016m.
(4)
The astronomical unit is exactly 149597870700m; one arcsecond is exactly π/648000 radians. The IAU resolution explicitly records the parsec convention. A megaparsec is one million parsecs. [ref]. [ref].
The familiar description "the distance giving one arcsecond of stellar parallax" uses the small-angle approximation. Exact right triangle geometry instead gives au/tan(π/648000), slightly shorter than the conventional parsec. The relative difference is approximately (π/648000)2/3≃7.83×10−12. This is negligible for the examples here, but distinguishing an exact unit convention from an approximate geometric interpretation prevents a misleading equality.
