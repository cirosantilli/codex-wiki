Potential-vorticity evolution equation (Dq/Dt=Sq​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Potential vorticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 220 Articles: 3
A potential vorticity budget has the form
DtDq​=Sq​.
(6)
The material derivative follows the fluid motion and Sq​ collects forcing or dissipation. With Sq​=0 it becomes potential-vorticity conservation. For a quasi-geostrophic streamfunction, horizontal advection is J(ψ,q)=ψx​qy​−ψy​qx​.
