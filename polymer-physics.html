Polymer physics

 Home Chemistry Polymer
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 39
Polymer physics connects the conformations and motion of long molecules to the properties of a material. A polymer can change shape through many segment rotations while its chemical backbone remains intact. This makes the competition between flow and relaxation important: the same chain can behave as a coil in one experiment and an extended object in another. Soft matter includes both these flexible structures and liquid crystals, whose orientational order produces a different route to strong mechanical and optical response.
