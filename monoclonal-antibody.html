Monoclonal antibody

 Home Biology Protein Antibody
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A monoclonal antibody preparation derives from one selected antibody-producing biological cell and its descendants, or reproduces their specified antibody sequences. Monoclonal describes the origin and composition of the preparation; it does not guarantee absence of cross-reactivity or establish antibody affinity. [ref]
