Topographic buoyancy boundary condition for quasi-geostrophic flow (Dg​[(f0​/N2)ψz​+b]=0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 52
For a small steady lower-boundary height b(x,y), impermeability gives w=ug​⋅∇b at the reference plane. Substitution in the buoyancy equation Dg​(f0​ψz​)+N2w=0 yields the displayed boundary condition. For nonzero Fourier modes in a uniform crossing current, this fixes ψz​(0)=−N2b/f0​. An upper radiation condition or decay condition is still needed to select the physical response.
