Edge cutset

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Cut (graph theory)
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
An edge cutset separating two vertices is a set of edges whose removal leaves no path in a graph between those vertices. For a vertex set containing exactly one of the two vertices, its edge boundary is such a cutset.
