On polynomials with weighted inner product
⟨f,g⟩=∫0∞​f(x)g(x)e−xdx,
(122)
the operator
Lf=xf′′+(1−x)f′
(123)
is self-adjoint because e−xLf=(xe−xf′)′. Its eigenvalues on polynomials of degree at most n are 0,−1,…,−n; its eigenvectors are scalar multiples of the Laguerre polynomials.
