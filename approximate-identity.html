Approximate identity

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Convolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 397 Articles: 6
An approximate identity is a family of integrable kernels Kε​ whose total mass is one and whose mass concentrates near zero as ε→0. Under standard hypotheses, f∗Kε​ converges to f in norm and at almost every Lebesgue point.
