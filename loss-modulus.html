Loss modulus (G′′)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Rheometer Small-amplitude oscillatory shear
OurBigBook.com Words: 16
The loss modulus is the quadrature coefficient G′′ in oscillatory shear. It measures viscous energy dissipation.
