RKKY interaction

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetism Exchange interaction Indirect exchange
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
A local moment induces conduction-electron spin polarization, which mediates a second-order coupling to another moment. The static electronic spin susceptibility fixes the spatial interaction. For a three-dimensional spherical Fermi surface its leading large-distance form oscillates as cos(2kF​R)/R3, up to the overall sign convention and prefactor. The sign alternates with separation. Other band structures and dimensions change this form. [ref]
