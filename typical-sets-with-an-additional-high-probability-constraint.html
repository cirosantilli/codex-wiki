Typical sets with an additional high-probability constraint (∣An,ε∩Bn​∣≥2n(H−ε)−δ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Asymptotic equipartition property Typical set
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
Let An,ε be the weak typical set of a finite alphabet independent and identically distributed source with Shannon entropy H, and let Bn​ be any sets with Pr(Xn∈Bn​)→1. The asymptotic equipartition property and union bound give Pr(Xn∈An,ε∩Bn​)→1. Every word in the intersection has probability mass between 2−n(H+ε) and 2−n(H−ε). Summing these bounds gives
Pr(Xn∈An,ε∩Bn​)2n(H−ε)≤∣An,ε∩Bn​∣≤2n(H+ε).
(142)
For every fixed δ>0, the first probability eventually exceeds 2−δ, proving the title bound. A sample mean within a fixed positive tolerance of the expected value supplies Bn​ by the strong law of large numbers.
