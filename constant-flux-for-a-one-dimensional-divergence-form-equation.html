Constant flux for a one-dimensional divergence-form equation (a(x)u′(x)=J)

On an interval, the weak equation (au′)′=0 makes au′ constant almost everywhere. If λ≤a≤Λ, the derivative energy on concentric subintervals satisfies E(r)≤(Λ/λ)2(r/R)E(R). This supplies dyadic energy decay even though a one-dimensional annulus is disconnected.
