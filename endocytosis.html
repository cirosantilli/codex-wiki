Endocytosis

 Home Biology Biological cell Cell membrane Membrane transport
OurBigBook.com Words: 126 Articles: 3
Endocytosis takes material into a biological cell by inward budding and separation of part of its cell membrane. At a chemical synapse, it helps recover membrane and proteins after synaptic vesicle exocytosis. This recovery is distinct from uptake of individual neurotransmitter molecules through membrane transport proteins.
