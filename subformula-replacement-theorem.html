Subformula replacement theorem

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Propositional formula Subformula
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
Replacing one designated subformula occurrence in a propositional formula by another propositional formula preserves well-formedness. Induct on the context surrounding the occurrence: at the root the result is the replacement formula; under a connective apply the induction hypothesis to the affected child and rebuild the parent using its formation rule.
