Airy global modes of a linearly confined Ginzburg-Landau equation (ωn​=i[μ0​−U2/(4γ)+(ϵ2γλ2)1/3zn​])

On x>0 with a homogeneous wall condition, positive diffusion γ and growth μ(x)=μ0​−ϵλx, remove constant drift by A=eUx/(2γ)−iωtf. The scale ξ=(ϵλ/γ)1/3x reduces the eigenvalue problem to the Airy ordinary differential equation. Decay selects f=Ai(ξ+zn​), where Ai(zn​)=0 enforces the wall. The least negative zero z1​ sets the global instability threshold μ0​>U2/(4γ)+∣z1​∣(ϵ2γλ2)1/3. A locally absolutely unstable pocket can therefore exist without an unstable global mode.
