Magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation (ρDt​u=−∇p+J×B+ρν∇2u)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For constant mass density ρ, kinematic viscosity ν and magnetic permeability μ0​, the incompressible momentum equation is
∂t​u+(u⋅∇)u=−ρ−1∇p+(μ0​ρ)−1(∇×B)×B+ν∇2u.
(7)
It is the Navier-Stokes equation with Lorentz force density. The magnetic force decomposes into magnetic tension and a magnetic pressure gradient. A uniform streamwise pressure gradient need not make ordinary pressure independent of transverse coordinates.
