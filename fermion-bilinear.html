Fermion bilinear (ψˉ​Γψ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field
OurBigBook.com Words: 42 Articles: 1
A local expression containing two fermion fields and a matrix acting on Dirac spinor indices. The choices Γ=γμ and Γ=γμγ5 give the Dirac current and axial current.
