Multiset

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Set
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
A multiset records a nonnegative integer multiplicity for each element of a set. A finite multiset has finite support and finite total multiplicity. Its cardinality is the sum of its multiplicities; its disjoint union with another multiset adds multiplicities. The hook lengths of a Young diagram form a multiset, since different cells can have equal lengths.
