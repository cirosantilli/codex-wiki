Rational interpolation (q(xi​)/r(xi​)=fi​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Interpolation
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 1
Rational interpolation seeks a rational function q/r with prescribed bounds on the polynomial degrees of its numerator and denominator and prescribed values at distinct interpolation nodes. A rational interpolant must have a well-defined value at each interpolation node. With a specified numerator-denominator representation, this requires r(xi​)=0 for every i. A normalization such as a monic polynomial denominator removes the common scaling freedom but does not guarantee existence or uniqueness. Multiplying by a zero denominator can create spurious solutions to the rational interpolation linearization.
