Autoregressive process of order one (AR(1))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Autoregressive moving-average model Autoregressive model
OurBigBook.com Words: 404 Articles: 9
An autoregressive process of order one satisfies Xt​=ϕXt−1​+εt​. It is causal and weakly stationary when ∣ϕ∣<1.
