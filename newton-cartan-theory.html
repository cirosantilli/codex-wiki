Newton–Cartan theory

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newtonian mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 2
Newton–Cartan theory describes Newtonian mechanics geometrically using absolute time, a degenerate spatial inverse metric, and a compatible torsion-free affine connection. It is not a theory with a nondegenerate Lorentzian metric. Gravitationally accelerated particles are geodesics of that connection, whose curvature measures tidal gravity.
