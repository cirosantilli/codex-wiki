Four-acceleration (Aμ=DUμ/dτ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 584 Articles: 10
The four-acceleration is the covariant derivative of four-velocity along a massive observer's worldline: Aμ=dUμ/dτ+Γνρμ​UνUρ. In Cartesian Minkowski spacetime coordinates the Christoffel symbols vanish, leaving the ordinary proper time derivative. Differentiating the constant velocity norm gives U⋅A=0; in the instantaneous rest frame, A=(0,a), so A⋅A=−a2 with +−−− metric signature. The magnitude a is the proper acceleration.
