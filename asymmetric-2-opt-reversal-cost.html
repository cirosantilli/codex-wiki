Asymmetric 2-opt reversal cost (Δ=Δboundary​+Δinternal​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 51
A 2-opt schedule move reverses a consecutive block vp​,…,vq​, reconnecting its boundary arcs. With directed costs, its change is
Δ=cvp−1​,vq​​+cvp​,vq+1​​−cvp−1​,vp​​−cvq​,vq+1​​+∑r=pq−1​(cvr+1​,vr​​−cvr​,vr+1​​).
(5)
Omitting the internal sum is valid only for symmetric costs. A simulated annealing step accepts an increase with probability exp(−Δ/T); a strictly positive temperature permits escape from local minima.
