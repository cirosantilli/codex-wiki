Poincaré line bundle (PA​ on A×A)

OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The normalized universal line bundle restricts to the class ξ on A×{ξ} and is trivialized on both zero sections. For a line bundle L on an abelian variety, its homomorphism associated to a line bundle on an abelian variety satisfies
(id,ϕL​)∗PA​≅m∗L⊗p1∗​L−1⊗p2∗​L−1
(2)
as Picard group classes. A choice of trivialization of L0​ normalizes the right side; uniqueness then follows from the Seesaw theorem.
