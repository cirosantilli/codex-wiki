The quotient PN/N is a p-group, being the image of P, and
[G/N:PN/N]=[G:PN]∣[G:P].

Its index is prime to p, so PN/N is Sylow in G/N.
If P≤A and Q≤B are Sylow p-subgroups, their product has the full p-part of ∣A×B∣, hence is Sylow. Conversely, for any Sylow R≤A×B, its two projections are p-subgroups and lie in some Sylows P,Q. Then R⊆P×Q, and equality follows by order. Distinct pairs give distinct subgroups because their projections recover P and Q. Thus
