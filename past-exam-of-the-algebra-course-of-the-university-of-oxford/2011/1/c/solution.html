The Artin–Wedderburn theorem says that a finite-dimensional semisimple algebra over C is a finite product ∏j​Mdj​​(C): the division algebras in the general theorem reduce to C because the base field is algebraically closed. Each factor has center of an associative algebra CIdj​​. Consequently a central element corresponds to (λj​Idj​​)j​. If it is nilpotent, then λjn​=0 for every j, whence every λj​=0. Therefore z=0​. The centrality assumption matters: a full matrix algebra has nonzero nilpotent elements.
