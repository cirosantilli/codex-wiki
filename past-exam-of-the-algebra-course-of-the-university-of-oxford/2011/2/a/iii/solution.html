By the Artin–Wedderburn theorem, a finite-dimensional complex simple algebra is Md​(C), with a unique simple module up to isomorphism, namely the column module S=Cd. Its left regular module is S⊕d. The Schur lemma gives EndA​(S)=C, so
dimC​VM​=d=dimC​M=dimC​A​.​
(8)
The right action can be seen explicitly. For a row vector r, the map fr​(m)=mr is A-linear, and all such maps are obtained this way, since the row space and VM​ both have dimension d. Moreover fr​⋅x=frx​. Any nonzero row generates the whole row space under right multiplication by matrices: if rj​=0, suitable matrix units send it to each coordinate row. Thus VM​ is a simple right A-module.
