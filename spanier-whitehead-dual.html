Spanier-Whitehead dual (A∨)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Stable homotopy theory Spectrum (topology) Cell spectrum Finite spectrum
OurBigBook.com Words: 55 Articles: 1
The Spanier-Whitehead dual of a finite spectrum A is A∨=F(A,S). It is finite, and evaluation and coevaluation give the natural Spanier-Whitehead duality adjunction.
