Constant presheaf of sets (KT​(U)=T)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Presheaf of sets on a topological space
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
Every restriction is the identity. Its stalk at each point is T, and its étale space of a presheaf is X×T with T discrete. It is generally not a sheaf; in particular its value on the empty open set is T. Its sheafification is the constant sheaf of sets.
