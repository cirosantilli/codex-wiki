Free-pair dynamic vibration absorber (k=mω2)

 Home Physics Wave Oscillation Oscillator Coupled oscillator
OurBigBook.com Words: 112
A free mass M driven by F0​cosωt can be coupled by a single spring of stiffness k to an auxiliary free mass m. With no wall attachment or damping, the periodic displacement amplitude of M is A=F0​(k−mω2)/{ω2[Mmω2−k(M+m)]}. Tuning k=mω2 gives antiresonance: A=0 while the auxiliary mass has signed amplitude −F0​/k, supplying an exactly cancelling spring force. A nearby pole occurs at k=Mmω2/(M+m). For relative tuning error ϵ=(k−mω2)/(mω2) sufficiently small, the primary amplitude relative to its free response grows as (M/m)∣ϵ∣. A light absorber is therefore narrowband and may require large auxiliary travel. This cancellation dissipates no energy, and undamped free modes persist unless excluded by initial conditions.
