Photoevaporative gravitational radius (rg​=GM/cs2​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Photoevaporation
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
The thermal binding scale defined by equating heated gas sound speed to circular Keplerian orbit speed. It is rg​=GM/cs2​. The circular-orbit binding energy is GM/(2r) per unit mass, while the gas specific enthalpy is of order cs2​. A heated disk surface at radii comparable to or larger than rg​ can therefore drive photoevaporation. The actual launching radius can differ by a factor of order unity; cs​ is not the ballistic escape speed.
