A short concentration inequality argument explains the bound. For a fixed nonzero vector u, the normalized row products ⟨Ga​,u⟩/∥u∥2​ are independent random variables with the standard normal distribution, so
Ru​=∥u∥22​∥Tu∥22​​ ∼ kχk2​​,ERu​=1,Var(Ru​)=k2​.

Use the moment-generating function of a chi-squared distribution and the Chernoff bound to obtain
P(Ru​≥1+ε)P(Ru​≤1−ε)​≤exp[−2k​(ε−log(1+ε))],≤exp[−2k​(−ε−log(1−ε))].​
(28)
For 0<ε<1, elementary logarithm inequalities bound their sum by 2e−kc(ε). Apply the union bound to the (2n​) nonzero pair differences to get failure probability at most n(n−1)e−kc(ε)≤δ. Independence of different pairs is unnecessary; they use the same map. This combines fixed-vector concentration with a finite-set union bound. [ref]
