Weighted hypergraph ((V,E,w))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Hypergraph
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A hypergraph with a specified numerical weight w(e) for each hyperedge e∈E. In a hypergraph cut function, the weight records the cost or benefit of splitting a hyperedge between two sides. Signed weights and nonnegative weights lead to different submodularity properties.
