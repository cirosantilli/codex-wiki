For component-first velocity gradient Lij​=∂j​ui​, ideal incompressible magnetic field stretching gives the displayed evolution of Mij​=Bi​Bj​. It has the same upper-convected derivative as a polymer conformation tensor with negligible relaxation. Finite polymer relaxation time introduces a local return toward thermal equilibrium, whereas magnetic diffusion acts through spatial gradients and preserves the solenoidal magnetic-field constraint. The analogy does not identify those dissipative mechanisms.
