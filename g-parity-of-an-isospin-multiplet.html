G-parity of an isospin multiplet (G=η(−1)I)

 ... Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Flavor symmetry Isospin G parity
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 1
For integer isospin I and neutral-state charge conjugation value η, the entire isospin multiplet has G=η(−1)I. Both charge conjugation and the second-axis half-turn reverse the first and third generators, so their product commutes with the full isospin algebra. Evaluate it on the M=0 state and use ladder connectivity to obtain the value for every M.
