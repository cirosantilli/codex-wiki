Cubic-time resistive damping in a differentially rotating cell (a∼a0​exp[−ηm2(Ω′)2t3/3])

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Flux expulsion Phase mixing in magnetic flux expulsion
OurBigBook.com Words: 101
In a smooth interior region with locally constant shear Ω′, a wound azimuthal Fourier mode has radial wavenumber ks​≃−mΩ′t. Integrating its resistive damping rate ηks2​ gives the exponential decay in the title. The characteristic time is τc​=[3/(ηm2Ω′2)]1/3. For m=1 and Ω′=−Ω0​/d, Ω0​τc​=(3Rm)1/3, where Rm=Ω0​d2/η. This is a high-magnetic Reynolds number interior transient on a time long compared with the rotation time but short compared with d2/η. Physical magnetic field components may have additional algebraic factors from taking derivatives of the wound magnetic vector potential. A maintained exterior field, an axial core and a circulation-edge boundary layer prevent interpreting this estimate as an exact, uniform, infinite-time solution.
