Conditional mutual information (I(X;Y∣Z))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Mutual information
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
Conditional mutual information is the average information shared by X and Y after Z is known:
I(X;Y∣Z)=H(X∣Z)−H(X∣Y,Z)≥0.
