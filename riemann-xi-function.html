Riemann xi function (ξ(s)=21​s(s−1)π−s/2Γ(s/2)ζ(s))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Riemann zeta function Completed Riemann zeta function
OurBigBook.com Words: 183 Articles: 3
Removing the completion's poles produces an entire function with ξ(0)=ξ(1)=1/2 and ξ(s)=ξ(1−s). Its zeros are the nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function. Its finite order allows Hadamard factorization, while the xi modulus criterion for the Riemann hypothesis characterizes their horizontal location.
