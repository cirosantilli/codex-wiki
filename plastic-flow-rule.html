Plastic flow rule (e˙p=λ˙∂g/∂τ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Plasticity (physics) Plastic strain
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
A plastic flow rule prescribes the direction and rate of plastic straining once a yield condition is active. In a plastic-potential model its direction is the stress gradient of g, multiplied by a nonnegative plastic multiplier. An associated flow rule takes g equal to the yield function. The stress-strain split, loading conditions and hardening evolution are needed along with this equation to close an elastoplastic model.
