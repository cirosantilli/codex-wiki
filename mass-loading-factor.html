Mass-loading factor (λ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy evolution Galactic outflow
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
The mass-loading factor is the ratio of outflow rate to star-formation rate, λ=M˙out​/M˙∗​. Its value can depend on galaxy mass, radius, gas phase, and the surface through which the flow is measured.
