Algebraic cotangent bundle (ΩX/k1​)

 ... Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Kähler differential Sheaf of relative Kähler differentials Sheaf of Kähler differentials over a field
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For a smooth algebraic variety of dimension r over a field, the sheaf of Kähler differentials over a field is locally free of rank r and defines its algebraic cotangent bundle. Its dual is the tangent sheaf of a scheme. For a smooth hypersurface of degree d in Pn, the Conormal exact sequence for Kähler differentials is the short exact sequence 0→OX​(−d)→ΩPn1​∣X​→ΩX1​→0.
