Observed flux of an unresolved spherical emitter

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Radiative transfer Radiation-field moment Radiative flux
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For a uniformly emitting spherical body of radius R at Euclidean distance d≫R, integration of its specific intensity over the apparent solid angle gives fλ​=πIλ​(R/d)2. For a blackbody, Iλ​=Bλ​(T) and the bolometric result is f=σSB​T4(R/d)2=L/(4πd2). A grey emissivity factor multiplies this expression; isothermality alone does not imply blackbody emission.
