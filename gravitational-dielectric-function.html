Gravitational dielectric function (ϵk​(ω))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For a homogeneous collisionless stellar model with its mean field subtracted, the response to an imposed Newtonian gravitational potential is Φktot​=Φkext​/ϵk​, where
ϵk​(ω)=1+k24πG​∫d3vk⋅v−ωk⋅∂v​f0​​.
(24)
The phase-space distribution function f0​ is mass-normalized, and k=0. Causal analytic continuation uses the Landau contour wherever the equilibrium permits it. Zeros describe collective Landau modes. This response function is a gravitational analogue of the electrostatic dielectric function; its susceptibility sign reflects attraction.
