Angular relation for two-photon electron-positron annihilation (cosθ1​+cosθ2​1+cos(θ1​+θ2​)​=γ+1γ−1​​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Four-momentum Four-momentum conservation
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
When an electron of Lorentz factor γ>1 annihilates with a stationary equal-mass positron, transverse four-momentum conservation makes the photons' transverse components opposite. Eliminating their positive frequencies gives the displayed relation for noncollinear photon angles to the incoming electron. The exactly collinear forward/backward configuration is physically allowed but makes the quotient 0/0; its undivided cross-multiplied identity remains valid. The angle relation thus requires nondegeneracy or an appropriate limit, rather than an assertion that every allowed angle pair defines the quotient.
