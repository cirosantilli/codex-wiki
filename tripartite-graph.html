Tripartite graph

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 81 Articles: 1
A tripartite graph has a set partition of its vertices into three classes, with every edge joining different classes. Each pair of classes induces a bipartite graph, and every triangle in a graph uses one vertex from each class.
