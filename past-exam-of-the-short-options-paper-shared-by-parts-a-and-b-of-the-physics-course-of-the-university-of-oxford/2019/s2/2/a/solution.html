A thin spherical shell has outward pressure force −4πr2(dP/dr)dr and inward gravitational force GM(r)4πr2ρ(r)dr/r2. Hydrostatic equilibrium requires
drdP​=−r2GM(r)ρ(r)​,drdM​=4πr2ρ(r).​
(19)
A uniform-density solar model gives M(r)=M⊙​(r/R⊙​)3, ρˉ​=3M⊙​/(4πR⊙3​), and after integration from R⊙​ to zero,
Pc​=8πR⊙4​3GM⊙2​​=1.34×1014Pa,Tc​=ρˉ​kB​Pc​μmp​​=6.9×106K(μ=0.6).
(20)
Thus the appropriate orders of magnitude are 1014–1016Pa and 107K. The uniform-density approximation underestimates the actual central pressure because the Sun is centrally concentrated. Radiation pressure is neglected in applying the ideal gas law.
