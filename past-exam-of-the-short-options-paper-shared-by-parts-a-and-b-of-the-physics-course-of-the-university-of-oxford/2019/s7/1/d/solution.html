The orbiting planet produces a potential V(r,ϑ,φ−Ωt). It has explicit time dependence and exerts an azimuthal torque, so generally E˙=Vt​ and L˙z​=−Vφ​ are nonzero. Nevertheless a simultaneous time-translation symmetry δt=λ and rotation δφ=Ωλ leaves the Lagrangian and dt unchanged. Noether's theorem gives Q=Ωpφ​−E and hence
EJ​=E−ΩLz​=constant.​
(34)
Equivalently Vt​=−ΩVφ​ implies d(E−ΩLz​)/dt=0. This Jacobi integral assumes a rigidly rotating potential with constant Ω and negligible back-reaction from the test particle.
