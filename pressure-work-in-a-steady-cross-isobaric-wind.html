Pressure work in a steady cross-isobaric wind (−ρ−1u⋅∇h​p=r∣u∣2)

 ... Physics Mathematical physics Shallow water equations Geostrophic balance Geostrophic wind Geostrophic wind with linear drag
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Dot the steady linear-drag momentum equation with the velocity. The Coriolis force does no work, so pressure-gradient work balances frictional dissipation. In a strictly geostrophic flow the wind is along isobars and pressure work vanishes. Cross-isobar motion supplies the positive pressure work needed to maintain a dissipative equilibrium.
