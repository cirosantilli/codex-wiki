Uniform partial-sum criterion for a Schauder basis (supn​∥Pn​∥<∞⟺x=∑n​fn​(x)xn​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Schauder basis
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
For a countable minimal total family in a normed space in a Banach space, let (xn​,fn​) be its unique biorthogonal system and let Pn​x=∑j≤n​fj​(x)xj​. Uniform boundedness of these projections implies Pn​x→x for every x: approximate x by a finite linear combination y, eventually fixed by Pn​, and bound the error by (1+supn​∥Pn​∥)∥x−y∥. Conversely, if every vector has a norm-convergent expansion in this family, applying each continuous fj​ shows that its coefficients must be fj​(x). Thus Pn​x→x, so the projections are pointwise bounded. The Uniform boundedness principle gives their uniform operator-norm bound. This proves the criterion and uniqueness of all expansions.
