Probability (P(E))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 3
The probability of an event is its value under a probability measure, a real number between zero and one. It compares the mass assigned to that event with the unit mass of the whole sample space. For a finite sample space with equally likely outcomes, P(A)=∣A∣/∣Ω∣. For unequal outcome masses, add their individual probabilities instead.
For example, selecting a coin with head probability 1/2 with chance 4/5, or a coin with head probability 3/4 with chance 1/5, gives head probability (4/5)(1/2)+(1/5)(3/4)=11/20. This is the law of total probability: averaging over the hidden selection is part of calculating the outcome's probability.
