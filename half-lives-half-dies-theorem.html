Half-lives-half-dies theorem (dimkeri∗​=21​dimH1​(∂W;F2​))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Intersection form Intersection pairing
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
For a compact three-manifold W, let L=ker(H1​(∂W;F2​)→H1​(W;F2​)). The boundary intersection pairing satisfies L⊥=L: the long exact sequence in relative homology identifies L with boundaries of relative surfaces, and the boundary-interior adjoint identity identifies its orthogonal complement with the same kernel. Hence 2dimL=dimH1​(∂W;F2​). This also implies that a closed surface with odd first mod-two Betti number, such as RP2, cannot be the entire boundary of a compact three-manifold.
