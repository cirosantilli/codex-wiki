Umklapp scattering (q1​+q2​−q3​=G=0)

 ... Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Bose-Einstein statistics Bose-Einstein distribution Phonon Phonon scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
An Umklapp process conserves reduced crystal momentum modulo a nonzero reciprocal lattice vector. It can occur in electron-phonon scattering as well as phonon-phonon scattering; the lattice takes up the reciprocal-lattice momentum.
In a three-phonon Umklapp scattering process, the first-zone wavevectors obey the displayed equation for a nonzero reciprocal lattice vector G, together with conservation of energy. The crystal momentum transferred to the lattice allows these processes to relax a phonon heat current. They are suppressed at sufficiently low temperature and often determine the approximate 1/T high-temperature thermal conductivity of a crystalline electrical insulator.
