Radiative relaxation time in a planetary atmosphere (τrad​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Exoplanet atmospheric dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A column near pressure p contains mass per area of order p/g. Linearizing its blackbody cooling around temperature T gives the estimate
τrad​∼4gσSB​T3cp​p​,
(14)
where cp​ is specific heat capacity at constant pressure. This estimate omits details of wavelength-dependent radiative transfer, but captures faster relaxation in hotter and lower-pressure atmospheric layers.
