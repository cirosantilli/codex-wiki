Incompressible inextensible-fibre plane strain (F=R(θ)(10​γ1​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Finite elasticity Inextensible fibre constraint
OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 1
Let e=(cosθ,sinθ) be the current fibre direction and n=(−sinθ,cosθ). Inextensibility fixes the first column of the deformation gradient to e, and incompressibility fixes the second to γe+n. Commuting mixed derivatives gives γ,1​=θ,1​ and θ,2​=γθ,1​. Thus γ=θ+f(X2​), and curves tangent to (−γ,1) map to straight transverse curves with constant θ.
