Idempotent ultrafilter (U+U=U)

 ... Foundations of mathematics Set theory Filter (set theory) Ultrafilter Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers Addition on the Stone-Čech compactification of the natural numbers
OurBigBook.com Words: 243 Articles: 3
An additive idempotent ultrafilter satisfies U+U=U. On positive integers it is necessarily nonprincipal, since a principal ultrafilter at n adds to itself to give the principal ultrafilter at 2n. The idempotent-ultrafilter star-set lemma converts this algebraic property into a recursive construction of finite sums. If zero is included, the trivial principal idempotent at zero must be excluded for that increasing-sequence application.
