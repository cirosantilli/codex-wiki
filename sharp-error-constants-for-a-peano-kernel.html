If λ(f)=k!−1∫Kf(k+1), the best unrestricted derivative-norm bounds are ∣λ(f)∣≤∥K∥∞​∥f(k+1)∥1​/k! and ∣λ(f)∣≤∥K∥1​∥f(k+1)∥∞​/k!. Continuous concentrated bumps approach the first constant, and continuous approximations to sgnK approach the second. For the three-point asymmetric second-derivative formula, these constants are 5/6 and 7/9.
