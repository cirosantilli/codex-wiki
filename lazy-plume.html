Lazy plume (Γ>1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulent plume
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
A buoyant plume with insufficient momentum for the corresponding pure plume balance. In the constant-entrainment top-hat model it has plume balance parameter Γ>1, unlike the momentum-excess forced plume. The equations accelerate the plume and drive the balance parameter toward one downstream. The definition depends on the chosen flux and entrainment convention; it is not merely a synonym for slow vertical speed.
