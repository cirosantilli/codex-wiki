Representation ring of a semisimple Lie algebra (R(g)≅Z[P]W)

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Lie algebra homomorphism Lie algebra representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
The representation ring of finite-dimensional modules for a complex semisimple Lie algebra is the abelian group generated by module classes, with [V⊕U]=[V]+[U], and multiplication [V][U]=[V⊗U]. Weyl complete reducibility theorem makes the classes of irreducible representations a free integral basis. The formal character map identifies this ring with the Weyl group invariant part of the group ring of a weight lattice. Injectivity follows from distinct highest weights; surjectivity follows by subtracting the character with each maximal dominant weight, whose highest-weight coefficient is one, from a Weyl-invariant finite sum.
