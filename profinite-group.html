Profinite group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group
OurBigBook.com Words: 357 Articles: 11
A profinite group is an inverse limit of finite discrete groups. Equivalently, it is a compact Hausdorff totally disconnected topological group.
