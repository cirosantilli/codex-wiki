Enthalpy of atomization (ΔHat∘​)

 Home Chemistry Thermochemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
An enthalpy of atomization is the enthalpy change for making one mole of separated gaseous chemical atoms from a specified starting chemical substance. For sodium, the relevant step is sublimation to monatomic gas; for diatomic chlorine, it is half the compatible molar bond-dissociation energy expressed as an enthalpy.
