Entropy production in successive weak shocks

OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For N equal weak shocks with individual pressure ratio 1+δ and total ratio Ps​=(1+δ)N, entropy production in a perfect-gas shock gives
cv​Δs​=12γ2γ2−1​δ2logPs​[1+O(δ)].
(24)
At fixed Ps​>1, this tends to zero as the compression is divided into increasingly many smaller weak shocks. A single finite normal shock wave instead gives the positive value logPs​−γlogD(Ps​). Gradual compression can consequently approach reversible isentropic flow while an abrupt compression generates finite entropy.
