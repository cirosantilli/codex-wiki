Bacillus subtilis

 Home Biology Organism Prokaryote Bacteria
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
A bacterial species that is a natural host of Bacillus virus phi29. The host identity matters when distinguishing that bacteriophage from unrelated Escherichia coli bacteriophages.
