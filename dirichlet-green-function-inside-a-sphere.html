Dirichlet Green function inside a sphere (GD​(x,y)=−4π1​[∣x−y∣−1−∣y∣a​∣x−a2y/∣y∣2∣−1])

For the three-dimensional ball ∣x∣<a, the displayed Dirichlet Green function has convention Δx​GD​=+δ(x−y). Its method of images uses the exterior inverse point y∗=a2y/∣y∣2. On ∣x∣=a, the distance identity ∣x−y∗∣=(a/∣y∣)∣x−y∣ makes the boundary value zero. The image lies outside the ball and therefore adds no source singularity to the domain. At a central source the weighted image term tends to 1/a, giving GD​(x,0)=−(4π∣x∣)−1+(4πa)−1. Spherical inversion with the required radial weight is the Kelvin transform.
