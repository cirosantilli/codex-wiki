Prandtl normal-mode equation (νv′′′=iα[(U−c)v′−U′v])

 ... Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Viscous boundary layer Prandtl boundary-layer equation Unsteady Prandtl equation Linearized unsteady Prandtl equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For a frozen real parallel profile and modes proportional to eiα(x−ct), continuity eliminates tangential velocity and gives this third-order equation. Wall conditions are v=v′=0; outer tangential matching gives v′→0, with bounded v. Real coefficients give the spectral symmetry (α,c)↦(−α,c∗). For positive real wavenumber, growth means Imc>0.
