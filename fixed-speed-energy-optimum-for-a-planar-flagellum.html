Fixed-speed energy optimum for a planar flagellum (β∗​=B/(B+B2+AB​))

In resistive-force theory with 0<γ<1, head drag δ≥0 and the ideal constant-inclination condition α2=β, put r=1−γ, d=1+δ, A=r(δ2−γ) and B=γd2. At prescribed swimming speed U, eliminating wave speed using linear resistive-force propulsion of a travelling filament gives
κN​LU2E​=r2β(1−β)(d−rβ)(γd+δrβ)​.
(40)
Its derivative has numerator Aβ2+2Bβ−B. This numerator is strictly increasing on (0,1), is negative at zero, and equals (δ+γ)2 at one. Thus its unique zero is the displayed global minimum. When A=0 the formula gives β∗​=1/2; when δ=0 it gives 1/(1+γ​). A planar sawtooth realizes constant inclination in the ideal model; smooth rounding and elastic costs change that idealization.
