Finite intersection property

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Compact space
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A family of sets has the finite intersection property when every finite subfamily has nonempty intersection. A topological space is compact exactly when every family of closed subsets with the finite intersection property has nonempty total intersection.
