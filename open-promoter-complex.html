Open promoter complex

 ... Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Gene expression Transcription (biology) Transcription initiation
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 1
An open promoter complex is a promoter-bound RNA polymerase assembly in which a short region of DNA is unwound and the template strand is accessible to the active site. This distinguishes it from the initially bound closed complex; it need not yet have escaped into productive elongation.
