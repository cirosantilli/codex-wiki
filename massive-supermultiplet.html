Massive supermultiplet

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry Supermultiplet
OurBigBook.com Words: 193 Articles: 3
A massive supermultiplet is a unitary irreducible representation of the Super-Poincaré algebra with positive timelike momentum. At rest, its nonzero supercharges become fermionic creation operators and fermionic annihilation operators. Without central charges in supersymmetry, four-dimensional extended supersymmetry has 2N complex oscillators; a spin-j Clifford vacuum gives (2j+1)22N states.
