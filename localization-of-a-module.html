Localization of a module (S−1M)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Localization of a ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 382 Articles: 5
For a multiplicative subset S⊆R and an R-module M, the localization S−1M consists of fractions m/s, with m/s=m′/s′ when some u∈S satisfies u(s′m−sm′)=0. It is an S−1R-module, and the canonical map is m↦m/1. If M is finitely generated over a Noetherian ring, then S−1M is a Noetherian module over S−1R.
