Relative cohomology (H∗(X,A;R))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 68 Articles: 1
Relative cohomology is the cohomology of cochains on X vanishing on chains in A. A pair has a natural long exact sequence in cohomology, and excision identifies relative groups for suitable neighborhood pairs.
