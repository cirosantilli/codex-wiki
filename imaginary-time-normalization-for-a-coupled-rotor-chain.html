Imaginary-time normalization for a coupled rotor chain (S=∫dτdx[Iϕ˙​2/(2ℏ2)+Jϕx2​/2])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum rotor Coupled quantum rotor chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
When imaginary time is divided by ℏ and ranges from zero to β, integrating rotor momenta gives Iϕ˙​2/(2ℏ2) but leaves the potential stiffness term J(∂x​ϕ)2/2. Factoring 1/ℏ2 out of both terms requires spatial coefficient ℏ2J inside the bracket. Setting ℏ=1 hides this distinction, but restoring units must preserve it.
