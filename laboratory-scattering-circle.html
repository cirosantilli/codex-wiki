Laboratory scattering circle (∣v1′​−V∣=qM2​u1​/(M1​+M2​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Particle dynamics Collision mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
When a particle of mass M1​ and initial speed u1​ strikes a stationary mass M2​, the centre-of-momentum frame moves at V=M1​u1​x/(M1​+M2​). If the relative speed retention in an oblique collision is q, its possible outgoing velocity vectors lie on the displayed circle in a scattering plane. Intersect a ray of fixed laboratory direction with that circle to obtain the quadratic formula for outgoing speed. A negative root is inadmissible, and the origin has no defined scattering angle. Tangency gives the maximum laboratory angle when the circle excludes the origin.
