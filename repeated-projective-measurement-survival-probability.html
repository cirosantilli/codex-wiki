Repeated projective-measurement survival probability (PN​(T)=​⟨ψ,e−iHT/(Nℏ)ψ⟩​2N)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum measurement Projective measurement
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For a normalized quantum state ψ and a time-independent Hamiltonian operator H, consider ideal projective measurements of the orthogonal projection P=∣ψ⟩⟨ψ∣ at intervals Δt=T/N. Write U=e−iHΔt/ℏ. A successful check leaves the post-measurement state ψ up to global phase. Thus each successive success, conditional on earlier successes, has conditional probability ∣⟨ψ,Uψ⟩∣2, and multiplication gives the displayed formula. Equivalently,
(PU)Nψ=⟨ψ,Uψ⟩Nψ,PN​(T)=∥(PU)Nψ∥2.
(13)
This is the probability of the entire sequence of successful checks. It underlies the quantum Zeno effect.
