Characteristic zero (charK=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Field Characteristic of a field
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A field has characteristic zero when no positive integer multiple of its identity vanishes. Equivalently, its prime field is isomorphic to Q. Every irreducible polynomial over such a field is a separable polynomial.
