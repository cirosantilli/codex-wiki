Category of finite sets (FinSet)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Category of sets
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The category whose objects are finite sets and whose morphisms are all functions between them. Finite categorical limits and colimits are the ordinary set constructions. It is essentially small: a skeleton of a category consists of the sets {0,…,n−1} for n∈N. Its Yoneda embedding preserves limits but generally does not preserve coproducts: evaluating y1⨿y1 at a two-element set gives two elements, while evaluating y2 gives four.
