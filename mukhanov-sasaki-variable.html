Mukhanov-Sasaki variable (v=zR)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic inflation Slow-roll approximation Comoving curvature perturbation
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For single-field adiabatic inflation, the Mukhanov-Sasaki variable rescales the comoving curvature perturbation by z=a2ϵ​MPl​, where MPl​ is the reduced Planck mass. Its quadratic bulk action is 21​∫[(v′)2−(∇v)2+(z′′/z)v2], after a boundary term is removed. A Fourier transform mode obeys vk′′​+(k2−z′′/z)vk​=0 and has canonical Wronskian normalization. The mass scale and sound-speed factors depend on the underlying scalar theory; this definition assumes the canonical single-field action and positive slow-roll kinetic coefficient.
