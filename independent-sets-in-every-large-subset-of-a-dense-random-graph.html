Independent sets in every large subset of a dense random graph (∣U∣≥n/(logn)2⟹α(G[U])≥(2−γ)log1/(1−p)​n)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Binomial random graph Chromatic number of a binomial random graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For fixed p and γ>0, Janson inequality bounds the failure probability for a given large vertex subset by exp(−c∣U∣2/(logn)4). A union bound over all at most 2n subsets still tends to zero. This uniform statement is essential because vertex sets left by a greedy colouring procedure depend on the graph; one cannot assume each adaptively chosen remainder is an independent fresh random graph.
