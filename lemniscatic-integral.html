Lemniscatic integral (F(z)=∫0z​(1−w4)−1/2dw)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Elliptic integral Elliptic integral of the first kind
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
Choose the holomorphic square root equal to one at zero on the unit disc. The resulting elliptic integral maps that disc conformally onto the square with vertices ±B,±iB, where B=∫01​dt/1−t4​. For the inverse Cayley transform between the half-plane and disk h(z)=i(1+z)/(1−z), the square of the derivative of F1​∘h, with F1′​(w)=1/w(1−w2)​, is 2i/(1−z4). Thus the two square maps differ by an affine change of coordinate.
