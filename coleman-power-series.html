Coleman power series (fu​(T))

 ... Algebra Algebraic number theory Iwasawa theory Iwasawa module p-ramified Iwasawa module Class-field unit sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 15
A Coleman power series encodes a norm-compatible sequence of local units in a cyclotomic tower by a single integral power series evaluated at ζpn​−1. A logarithmic derivative and trace correction give a measure, relating the local images of cyclotomic units to p-adic L-functions.
