Isentropic flow

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Compressible flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 76 Articles: 2
An isentropic flow has constant entropy along the fluid and contains no entropy-producing shocks or dissipation. For a perfect gas, p/ργ is constant and cs2​=γp/ρ∝ργ−1.
