Linear drag (Fd​=−αv)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Drag (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 3
Linear drag opposes the velocity relative to the surrounding medium with magnitude proportional to its speed, where α>0. Its power is Fd​⋅v=−α∣v∣2, so it dissipates kinetic energy. Under a constant external force, the resulting linear ordinary differential equation relaxes exponentially on the time scale m/α.
