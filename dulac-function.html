Dulac function (B(x,y))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bendixson-Dulac theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A multiplier B(x,y) whose rescaled planar vector field has divergence of one strict sign can exclude periodic orbits in a simply connected region. For a system x˙=xF, y˙​=yG in the positive quadrant, B=1/(xy) is often effective.
