Cassie–Baxter state (cosθ∗​=fs​(1+cosθY​)−1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Surface tension Wetting
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
A Cassie–Baxter state is a composite wetting state in which a drop contacts solid asperities over area fraction fs​ and trapped gas over the remainder. An ideal area-weighted contact-angle model gives the displayed apparent angle from the smooth-solid Young's equation angle θY​. Reducing solid contact can strongly enhance apparent hydrophobicity. Such a state can be metastable and can collapse when liquid enters the texture; roughness alone does not guarantee it.
