Angular frequency

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Lagrangian mechanics Small oscillation
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
Angular frequency measures phase advance per unit time. For x(t)=Acos(ωt+φ) it is ω=2π/T, where T is the period.
