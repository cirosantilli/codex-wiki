For teleportation with an ambiguous Bell-state phase label, write ρ=∣ψ⟩⟨ψ∣. The Bell-basis measurement outcomes ϕ+ and ϕ− each occur with probability 1/4. In either case a correct guess gives Bob ρ, while the wrong guess differs by the Pauli Z gate and gives ZρZ. The other two Bell states are identified correctly. Since Alice guesses each ambiguous label with probability 1/2, the overall wrong-correction probability is (1/2)(1/2)=1/4.
