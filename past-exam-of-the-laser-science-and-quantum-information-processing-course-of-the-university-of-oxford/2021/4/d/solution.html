The extra Pauli gate on the control is removed by Za​G=CZ​. Conjugating the target by a Hadamard gate then gives
CX​=(I⊗H)(Z⊗I)G(I⊗H).​
(45)
Thus, from left to right in the network, apply Hb​, then G, then Za​ and Hb​; the last two gates commute.
For a direct proof, the control-zero block of G is Ib​ and its control-one block is −Zb​. Multiplication by Za​ changes the latter into Zb​. Since Hb​Ib​Hb​=Ib​ and Hb​Zb​Hb​=Xb​, the result is
∣g⟩⟨g∣a​⊗Ib​+∣e⟩⟨e∣a​⊗Xb​,
(46)
which is exactly the CNOT gate on all inputs, including quantum superpositions.
