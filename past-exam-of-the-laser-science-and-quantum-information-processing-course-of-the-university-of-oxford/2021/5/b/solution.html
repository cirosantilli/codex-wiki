For an ideal half-wave plate whose fast axis makes angle θ with horizontal polarization, discard the common phase and use the Jones matrix
W(θ)=(cos2θsin2θ​sin2θ−cos2θ​).

It implements Z at 0∘ and X at 45∘. Thus for outcome Φ+ use two half-wave plates, first 0∘ then 45∘ in the propagation direction; for Φ− use one at 45∘; for Ψ+ one at 0∘; for Ψ− use no plate. Ideal retardance is assumed at the photon wavelength, and the setting is chosen after receiving the detector's classical result.
