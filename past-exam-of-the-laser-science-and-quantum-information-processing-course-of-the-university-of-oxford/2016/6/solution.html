The Hadamard gate H and T gate T=diag(1,eiπ/4) form a universal quantum gate set for single-qubit operations: finite sequences approximate any target single-qubit unitary arbitrarily well, up to a physically irrelevant global phase. Universality is not a claim that every arbitrary rotation has a finite exact H,T word. With products acting right to left, exact Pauli constructions are
Z=T4,X=HT4H,Y=T2HT4HT6.​
(28)
Indeed T2=S, T6=S†, and SXS†=Y.
Initialize the search register in ∣00⟩ and the output qubit in ∣1⟩, then apply H to all three. The search state becomes ∣s⟩=(∣00⟩+∣01⟩+∣10⟩+∣11⟩)/2, and the output becomes ∣−⟩. The oracle obeys
Uf​∣x⟩∣−⟩=(−1)f(x)∣x⟩∣−⟩.
(29)
This phase kickback leaves the output qubit factored out, so subsequent analysis can use the phase oracle Of​ on the two search qubits alone. Apply one Grover diffusion operator D=2∣s⟩⟨s∣−I, then measure the search register.
A promise restricts which Boolean functions may be supplied. For the exact one-iteration four-entry search here, promise exactly one marked input. The four functions are fjk​(x)=1 precisely when x=jk, for jk∈{00,01,10,11}. Other known numbers of marked entries require a correspondingly chosen iteration count; uniqueness is the promise for this particular exact circuit.
In basis order 00,01,10,11, the requested explicit calculation is
O10​=diag(1,1,−1,1),D=21​​−1111​1−111​11−11​111−1​​,
(30)
∣s⟩⟼21​​11−11​​⟼​0010​​=∣10⟩.​
(31)
Thus the promised marked entry is found with probability one.
For two weakly coupled spin-half nuclei in nuclear magnetic resonance, a useful rotating-frame Hamiltonian is
ℏH​=Ω1​Iz​+Ω2​Sz​+2πJIz​Sz​=2Ω1​​Z1​+2Ω2​​Z2​+2πJ​Z1​Z2​,
(32)
where J is in hertz and Iz​=Z1​/2, Sz​=Z2​/2. Resonant pulses and refocusing separate local rotations from the scalar coupling. For positive J, evolution for t=1/(2J) supplies e−iπZ1​Z2​/4. Combining it with local Z phases gives the phase oracle
O11​=diag(1,1,1,−1)=e−iπ/4eiπZ1​/4eiπZ2​/4e−iπZ1​Z2​/4.
(33)
The scalar global phase is immaterial. Local π pulses interchange zero and one, so
O00​=(X⊗X)O11​(X⊗X).​
(34)
This implements the desired change of marked input without an additional output qubit.
