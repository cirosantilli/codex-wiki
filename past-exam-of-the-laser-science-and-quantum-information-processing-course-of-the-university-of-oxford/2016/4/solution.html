A crystal needs a nonzero effective second-order nonlinear optical susceptibility for the chosen polarizations. In the bulk electric-dipole approximation, centrosymmetry forbids this response. Transparency at the relevant frequencies and phase matching over the interaction length permit efficient buildup. Nonzero local generation can occur without exact phase matching, but contributions then tend to cancel as they propagate.

The spatial walk-off angle is the angle between the extraordinary wave's Poynting vector and its wavevector. For an optic-axis angle θ, the extraordinary dispersion surface gives
