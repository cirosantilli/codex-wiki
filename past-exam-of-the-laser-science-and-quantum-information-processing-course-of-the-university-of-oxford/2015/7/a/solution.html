The orthonormal diagonal linear polarizations ∣D⟩=(∣H⟩+∣V⟩)/2​ and ∣A⟩=(∣H⟩−∣V⟩)/2​ are the Pauli X eigenstates. Each is transmitted perfectly as a density operator for every p; the possible minus sign on ∣A⟩ is global. Encode a classical zero and one as D and A, then measure in that basis at the receiver. This sends one classical bit per transmitted qubit without error. General superpositions of D and A need not be preserved, because their relative phase is randomized.
