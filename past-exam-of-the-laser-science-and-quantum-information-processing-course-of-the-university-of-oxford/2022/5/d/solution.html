For second-qubit inputs ∣0⟩ and ∣1⟩, c gives control expectation values ReU00​ and ReU11​. Preparing the maximally mixed state I/2 is equivalent to choosing these inputs independently with equal probability and discarding the choice. Linearity of the density operator evolution and quantum measurement probabilities therefore gives
⟨Z⟩I/2​=21​ReU00​+21​ReU11​=21​ReTrU.
(52)
Hence
ReTrU=2(P0​−P1​).​
(53)
The factor two is essential: the Hadamard test directly estimates the normalized trace. More generally, a target density operator ρ gives ⟨Z⟩=ReTr(ρU). This argument never requires knowledge of the eigenvectors of U.
