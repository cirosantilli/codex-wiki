The same result is the secular approximation for scalar-coupled spins. In the interaction picture of the Zeeman energy terms,
Vint​(t)=J(eiΔt∣01⟩⟨10∣+e−iΔt∣10⟩⟨01∣).
(9)
These terms average rapidly on the coupling timescale 1/∣J∣ when ∣Δ∣≫∣J∣, while JZ⊗Z/2 survives.
For fixed nuclei and electronic environments, the individual NMR angular frequencies scale with the static magnetic field B0​: schematically ωi​=κi​B0​, where κi​ includes the gyromagnetic ratio and magnetic shielding. The NMR scalar coupling J in angular frequency units is approximately independent of B0​. Therefore a nonzero separation ∣Δ∣=∣κ1​−κ2​∣B0​ increases with magnetic field, improving the approximation. In particular, a fixed difference of chemical shifts gives a larger separation in hertz at higher magnetic field. If the two resonances remain exactly equal, increasing their common frequency does not suppress their exchange.
