First massive level of a chiral RNS sector (NNS​=3/2,NR​=1,M2=4/α′)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Type II string theory Type II superstring mass spectrum
OurBigBook.com Words: 192 Articles: 3
After the GSO projection, the first positive shifted level is one: NNS​=3/2 and NR​=1. In a closed-string mass convention it corresponds to M2=4/α′. The transverse oscillator basis has 128 NS states and 128 R states. They form the bosonic and fermionic pieces of a massive supersymmetry multiplet.
