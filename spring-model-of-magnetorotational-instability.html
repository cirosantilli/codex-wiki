Spring model of magnetorotational instability

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Accretion disk Alpha disk Magnetorotational instability
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For an incompressible vertical-field mode, frozen-in magnetic tension supplies a spring of frequency ωA​. Its displacement satisfies ξ¨​x​−2Ωξ˙​y​−4ΩAξx​=−ωA2​ξx​ and ξ¨​y​+2Ωξ˙​x​=−ωA2​ξy​. Their determinant gives the ideal magnetorotational dispersion relation. A weak spring transfers angular momentum from the faster inner element to the slower outer element, causing inward and outward migration that increase their separation when angular velocity decreases outward. A sufficiently strong spring instead supplies a restoring force.
