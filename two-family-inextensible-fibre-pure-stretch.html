Two-family inextensible-fibre pure stretch (α2=2cos2θλ±λ2−sin22θ​​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 97
For two distinct reference fibre directions l±​=(cosθ,±sinθ,0), with 0<θ<π/2, consider the volume-preserving deformation with right stretch tensor U=diag(λ−1/2α,λ−1/2α−1,λ), with positive λ,α. The inextensible fibre constraint gives α2cos2θ+α−2sin2θ=λ. Setting b=α2 gives a quadratic with the displayed roots. There are two distinct positive branches for λ>sin2θ. The smallest possible axial stretch is λ=sin2θ, at which α2=tanθ and (Ul+​)⋅(Ul−​)=0. Since both image fibres have unit length, their mutual area cannot exceed one; their area is sin2θ/λ, which gives the same lower bound. Coincident or antiparallel fibres at the angle endpoints do not give the two-family orthogonality conclusion.
