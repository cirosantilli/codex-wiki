Quotient map

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Quotient topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 131 Articles: 1
A quotient map is a surjective continuous map q:X→Y for which U⊆Y is open exactly when q−1(U) is open.
