Lysine (Lys, K)

 ... Chemical substance Organic acid Carboxylic acid Amino acid Alpha-amino acid Proteinogenic amino acid
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A proteinogenic amino acid represented by K or Lys in protein primary structure. Its amino acid side chain carries predominantly positive electric charge near neutral pH.[ref]
Trypsin preferentially cleaves after its amino acid residues.
