Gamma prior for an inverse-gamma scale (Θ∣x∼Gamma(α+nk,λ+∑i​xi−1​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For inverse-gamma distribution observations of known shape k and scale Θ, their likelihood function in Θ is proportional to Θnkexp(−Θ∑i​xi−1​). A shape-α, rate-λ gamma distribution conjugate prior therefore gives the displayed Bayesian posterior. The Bayes estimator under squared error loss of the conditional claim expected value Θ/(k−1) is (α+nk)/[(k−1)(λ+∑i​xi−1​)]. It depends on reciprocals, rather than the sample mean of the claims.
