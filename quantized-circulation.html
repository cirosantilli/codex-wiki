Quantized circulation (∮u⋅dl=2πℏq/m)

 ... Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Order parameter Topological defect Phase vortex Quantum vortex
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 2
Single-valuedness of the condensate field gives total complex argument change 2πq on a closed loop avoiding zeros, where q is an integer winding number. Integrating the superfluid velocity around that loop gives the displayed circulation. It is unchanged by smooth deformations of the loop that do not cross a quantum vortex.
