Composition of rotation matrices (v′′=R2​R1​v)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Rotation matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 251 Articles: 1
Apply R1​ to a column coordinate vector, then apply R2​ in the same fixed basis. The function composition is
v′′=R2​(R1​v)=(R2​R1​)v.
(209)
Thus the rightmost matrix acts first. The product is again a rotation matrix, since (R2​R1​)T(R2​R1​)=I and det(R2​R1​)=1. For planar rotations about the same origin, the sine addition formula and cosine addition formula give
Rβ​Rα​=Rα+β​=Rα​Rβ​.
(210)
For a concrete composition of rotation matrices, first rotate (2,0)T by π/3 radians, reaching (1,3​)T, then by π/6 radians:
(3​/21/2​−1/23​/2​)(13​​)=(02​).
(211)
The total angle is π/2. Reversing these two planar rotations gives the same answer, but noncommuting rotations about fixed axes show why one cannot assume that property for rotations in three dimensions.[ref]
