OurBigBook.com Words: 443 Articles: 7
Hamiltonian engineering designs accessible interactions or their effective time average to implement desired quantum states or unitary operators. The available control inputs, Quantum Hamiltonian generators and laboratory constraints determine which transformations can actually be achieved. Model-based open-loop control computes a waveform before running it; adaptive experiments instead evaluate its performance on the physical system.
