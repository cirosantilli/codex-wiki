Allowed beta decay

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Radioactive decay Beta decay Beta decay transition
OurBigBook.com Words: 190 Articles: 4
A beta decay transition whose leading lepton orbital term has l=0. Fermi transitions allow ΔJ=0 and Gamow-Teller transitions allow ΔJ=0,1, excluding a Gamow-Teller 0→0 transition. Both preserve nuclear parity. Allowed describes the selection rules; cancellations in the nuclear matrix element can still make an allowed transition slow.
