Discrete valuation (v:K×→Z)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Local ring Valuation ring Discrete valuation ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 85 Articles: 2
A discrete valuation on a field K is a surjective group homomorphism v:K×→Z satisfying v(x+y)≥min(v(x),v(y)) whenever x+y=0. Its valuation ring is {0}∪{x:v(x)≥0}.
