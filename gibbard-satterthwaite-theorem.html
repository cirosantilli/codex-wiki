Gibbard-Satterthwaite theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Mechanism design Strategyproofness
OurBigBook.com Words: 293 Articles: 3
With a finite set of at least three alternatives and unrestricted strict preference orders, every onto deterministic strategyproof social choice function is a dictatorship in social choice. Restricting to two alternatives or to a restricted preference domain removes essential hypotheses.
