Spherical elastic ray invariant (p=rsini/α(r))

 ... Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Dispersion relation Ray tracing Elastic ray
OurBigBook.com Words: 267 Articles: 3
For an isotropic radial wave speed α(r), the elastic ray equations imply conservation of x×y, where y is the slowness vector. Indeed, its derivative is αy×y+x×∇(1/α)=0. Thus each nonradial elastic ray lies in a fixed plane through the centre and p=∣x×y∣ is constant. The angle i is measured from the radial direction; tangential slowness continuity preserves p across spherical interfaces.
