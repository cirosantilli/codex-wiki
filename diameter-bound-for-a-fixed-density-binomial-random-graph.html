Diameter bound for a fixed-density binomial random graph (Pr(diamG(n,p)≤2)→1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Binomial random graph Missing common-neighbour count in a binomial random graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
For fixed 0<p<1, the missing common-neighbour count in a binomial random graph has expectation tending to zero. The Markov inequality therefore shows that every pair has a common neighbour with probability tending to one. On that event every graph distance is at most two, so the graph is connected and its graph diameter is at most two.
