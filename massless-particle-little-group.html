Massless particle little group (ISO(D−2))

 ... Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz transformation Lorentz group Poincaré group Little group
OurBigBook.com Words: 221 Articles: 4
The stabilizer of a nonzero null four-momentum has connected group ISO(D−2). For ordinary finite-polarization particles its translation part acts trivially, leaving a representation of SO(D−2).
