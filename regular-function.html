Regular function

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Structure sheaf of a scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 223 Articles: 3
A regular function on an open subset U of a scheme X is a section of OX​(U). On an affine variety it is locally a quotient of polynomial functions with a denominator that does not vanish.
On a variety, a regular function is a regular map to the affine line; the arbitrary-target regular-map concept is broader.
