Equal-time momentum of an externally driven particle pair

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Four-momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 97
A sum of particle four-momenta at specified events is a four-vector, but sums recomputed at simultaneous events in different inertial frames need not correspond to that same four-vector. For opposite equal electric charges initially at rest a distance d apart, a longitudinal electric field switched on simultaneously in the laboratory produces zero total laboratory three-momentum. In a frame moving at v>0, the switch-on events are separated by γv​vd/c2; during that interval only one force acts. The late equal-time total three-momentum is −2γv​mv+γv​eEvd/c2, while the Lorentz transformation of an equal-laboratory-time sum varies as the laboratory pair's energy grows. Closed-system four-momentum conservation requires including the field and external apparatus.
