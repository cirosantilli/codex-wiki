Solutions and source-qualified methods for the Experimental Psychology paper Experimental design and methods, dated 25 April 2022. Source release: Oxford Experimental Psychology examination release, member a16816w1.pdf.
The separate supplementary data sheet is absent from the held release. Consequently all eight parts of Question 1, parts (a)–(b) of Question 2, and all six parts of Question 5 retain explicit TODOs; conditional explanations at these leaves do not constitute recovered empirical answers. All available theory parts, including every option in the choice questions, are answered.
