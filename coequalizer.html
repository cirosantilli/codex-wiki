Coequalizer

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Colimit
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 2
The coequalizer of parallel arrows f,g:A⇉B is a universal arrow q:B→Q satisfying qf=qg. Every other arrow equalizing f and g factors uniquely through q.
