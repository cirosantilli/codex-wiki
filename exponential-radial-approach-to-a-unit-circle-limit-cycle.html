Exponential radial approach to a unit-circle limit cycle (r˙=1−r,θ˙=1)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Periodic orbit Limit cycle Unit-circle attracting limit cycle
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
On the punctured plane, the vector field x˙=(1/r−1)x−y, y˙​=(1/r−1)y+x has the displayed polar equations. Its exact trajectories satisfy r(t)=1+(r(0)−1)e−t and θ(t)=θ(0)+t. Every forward trajectory approaches the unit circle, rotating counterclockwise, from inside or outside according to its initial radius. The circle is an attracting limit cycle with least period 2π. The origin is not an equilibrium: the original vector field is undefined there. The singularity prevents an application of a whole-plane Bendixson-Dulac criterion to this periodic orbit.
