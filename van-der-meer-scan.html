Van der Meer scan

OurBigBook.com Words: 40
An absolute collider luminosity calibration that scans the transverse separation of two colliding beams while measuring an interaction rate. Known bunch populations and the overlap integral determine the visible monitor cross section. Correlations between horizontal and vertical profiles require a joint overlap treatment.
