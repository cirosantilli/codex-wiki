A shearing-wave ansatz in a uniform inviscid isothermal shearing sheet removes background advection when the radial wavevector grows as kx​(t)=kx​(0)+Sky​t. The razor-thin disk Poisson kernel reduces the pressure-plus-gravity amplitude to [cs2​−2πGΣ0​/k]Σ′/Σ0​. Continuity couples it to ikx​vx​+iky​vy​, while the two momentum equations have rotational couplings −2Ωvy​ and (2Ω−S)vx​. The linearized vortensity amplitude remains constant, because the barotropic pressure and gravitational forces have zero curl and the background vortensity is uniform.
