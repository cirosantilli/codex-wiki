Trace distance for qubit dephasing (d(ρ,Dc​(ρ))=∣αβ∣∣1−c∣)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Trace distance
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For the pure qubit α∣0⟩+β∣1⟩, subtracting the output of dephasing channel leaves a Hermitian matrix with zero diagonal and off-diagonal modulus ∣αβ∣∣1−c∣. Its two eigenvalues are that number and its negative, giving the displayed trace distance. For real c=1−2p, this is 2p∣αβ∣.
