Finite-source forced-plume quadrature (z=c−1∫Q0​Q​[M05/2​+5F0​(q2−Q02​)/(4c)]−1/5dq)

For an axisymmetric top-hat plume model in uniform ambient density, use full kinematic fluxes and c=2αe​π​. The equations Q′=cM​, M′=F0​Q/M conserve M5/2−5F0​Q2/(4c). Solve this invariant for M(Q), then integrate dz=dQ/(cM​) to obtain the displayed exact quadrature. It determines the transition from the near-source jet limit of the top-hat plume model to the distant pure plume. The two regimes cannot be added by linear superposition because the flux equations are nonlinear.
