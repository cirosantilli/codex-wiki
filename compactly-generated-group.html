Compactly generated group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group
OurBigBook.com Words: 141 Articles: 1
A topological group is compactly generated in the algebraic sense when a compact subset generates its underlying group. Adjoining its inverse and the identity gives a compact symmetric generating set K, and G=⋃n≥1​Kn is a sigma-compact space. Some conventions restrict the term to locally compact groups or require a compact generating neighbourhood; those stronger conventions supply local compactness. Compact generation by an arbitrary compact subset alone does not.
