Cesium-beam clock procedure

 ... Special relativity Lorentz factor Time dilation Twin paradox Twin paradox clock experiments Atomic clock
OurBigBook.com Words: 152
An oven supplies a collimated caesium beam. State-selecting magnets precede and follow two coherent microwave interaction regions; a weak static magnetic field separates sublevels. The clock transition connects F=3,mF​=0 and F=4,mF​=0. Transferred atoms reach a heated ionizing collector. A detector scan identifies the resonance before locking.[ref]
Here F labels the total angular momentum and mF​ its projection. The selected pair is a two-level system inside a larger set of atomic energy levels; treating every oven atom as a prepared qubit would overstate the preparation. A measured detector signal may be modeled as S(ν)=Sbg​+AP(2π(ν0​−ν)), with background Sbg​ and gain A>0. Background subtraction and stable transmission are necessary before interpreting the signal as a transition probability. The velocity-averaged Ramsey line provides one explicit ideal example; its chosen dimensions are not historical specifications.
