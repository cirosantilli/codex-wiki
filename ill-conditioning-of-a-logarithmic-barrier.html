Ill-conditioning of a logarithmic barrier

OurBigBook.com Words: 90
Near an active constraint with limiting Lagrange multiplier λi∗​>0, a convergent barrier sequence has ci​(xk​)∼μk​/λi∗​. Its Hessian matrix therefore contains the term
ci​(xk​)2μk​​∇ci​(xk​)∇ci​(xk​)T∼μk​(λi∗​)2​∇ci​(x∗​)∇ci​(x∗​)T.
(24)
For a nonzero constraint gradient, this gives large curvature in a direction normal to the boundary. If a nonzero common tangent direction retains positive curvature of order one, while the remaining terms stay bounded, the condition number typically grows like 1/μk​. This is a mechanism rather than a universal rate: there may be no tangent direction, all curvatures may grow together, or an active constraint may have zero limiting Lagrange multiplier.
