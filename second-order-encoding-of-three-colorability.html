Second-order encoding of three-colorability (Φ3​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Second-order logic Existential second-order logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 254
Represent a finite simple graph by a domain D of vertices and a symmetric, irreflexive binary relation E for graph adjacency. Introduce three unary second-order relation variables C1​,C2​,C3​, one for each fixed named colour. Define the following abbreviations for first-order formulas in this expanded first-order signature:
One(x)Different(x,y)​:=(C1​(x)∨C2​(x)∨C3​(x))∧¬(C1​(x)∧C2​(x))∧¬(C1​(x)∧C3​(x))∧¬(C2​(x)∧C3​(x)),:=¬(C1​(x)∧C1​(y))∧¬(C2​(x)∧C2​(y))∧¬(C3​(x)∧C3​(y)).​
(3)
Then the existential second-order logic sentence
Φ3​:=∃C1​∃C2​∃C3​(∀xOne(x) ∧ ∀x∀y(E(x,y)→Different(x,y)))
(4)
asserts that every vertex receives exactly one of the three colours and adjacent vertices receive different colours. The first clause gives coverage and pairwise disjointness of the three subsets; the second makes each subset an independent set. Empty colour classes are allowed, so Φ3​ expresses existence of a proper vertex coloring using at most three colours.
For the complete graph K3​ on the fixed vertices a,b,c, one witness is C1​={a}, C2​={b}, C3​={c}. Each vertex satisfies exactly one predicate, and each of the three edges joins different predicates. Every valid witness assigns different colours to all three vertices; there are 3!=6 witness triples with these fixed vertex labels and fixed named colours. We are counting assignments, without identifying them under colour permutations or graph isomorphisms. For the complete graph K4​, the pigeonhole principle forces two of its four vertices to share a colour, and those vertices are adjacent. Thus it has no witness triple. This calculation illustrates a complexity certificate without requiring the reader to reconstruct a machine computation.
Reproduce the diagram by running python ../second-order-coloring-witness.py from the repository's _media directory. The script checks all witness triples for these two complete graphs before drawing them.
