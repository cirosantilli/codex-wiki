For upper concentration a, lower concentration b<a and L=H−zm​, define A=aL and D=(1−b)zm​. Once inside the settling rarefaction fan, clearing and deposition shocks obey zu​=zm​−us​t+2us​At​ and zd​=zm​+us​t−2us​Dt​. These curves apply only while their neighboring concentration remains within the fan.
