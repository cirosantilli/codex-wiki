Dimerized tight-binding chain (E±​(k)=±2t2cos2(ka)+δ2sin2(ka)​)

For spin-degenerate electrons at atomic spacing a, alternate the hopping amplitudes −(t+δ) and −(t−δ), with t>0 and ∣δ∣<t. The doubled primitive unit cell gives an off-diagonal Bloch Hamiltonian entry −(t+δ)−(t−δ)e−2ika. Its two eigenvalues are the displayed energy bands, for ∣k∣≤π/(2a). The band gap at the edge of the Brillouin zone is 4∣δ∣. At half filling there are two electrons per doubled primitive unit cell, so the lower band is full and the upper empty. This explicit model realizes the period-doubling form of a Peierls distortion.
