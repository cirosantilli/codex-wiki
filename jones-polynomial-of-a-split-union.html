Placing the two link diagrams in disjoint disks keeps all state circles separate. The reduced Kauffman bracket therefore acquires one extra loop factor −A2−A−2. Correcting writhe of a link diagram and setting t=A−4 gives the displayed formula. It concerns a split link, not an arbitrary union of linked components.
