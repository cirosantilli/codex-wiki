Dissipative Liouville equation (∂t​P+∇z​⋅(bP)=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Kinetic theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 69 Articles: 1
Conservation of probability under a deterministic characteristic flow map with velocity b(z,t) gives the displayed continuity equation on phase space. Along a characteristic, dP/dt=−P∇z​⋅b. Unlike the Hamiltonian Liouville equation, it allows contraction of phase-space volume and increasing density along trajectories. It still preserves total probability when boundary fluxes vanish.
