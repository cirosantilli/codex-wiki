Subtangent (CE)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Trigonometric function Tangent
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
For a point B of a curve, let its ordinate meet the chosen coordinate axis at C, and its tangent meet that axis at E. The segment CE is the subtangent. It depends on the chosen axis. For the standard parabola, CE=2CD, where D is its vertex of a parabola.
