Perpendicular plasma pressure (p⊥​=m∫(v⊥2​/2)fd3v)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure Pressure tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The equal normal stresses in the two directions perpendicular to the local magnetic field for a gyrotropic phase-space distribution function. The factor one half averages the two perpendicular velocity components. With first adiabatic invariant μ=v⊥2​/(2B), the integrand is mμBf.
