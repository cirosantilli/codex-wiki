Cut locus (Cut(p))

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Arc length Riemannian distance Minimizing geodesic
OurBigBook.com Words: 278 Articles: 3
On a complete connected Riemannian manifold, let c(v) for a unit tangent vector v be the supremum of times for which t↦expp​(tv) minimizes Riemannian distance from p. The cut locus consists of the endpoints expp​(c(v)v) with c(v)<∞. Radial minimization fails beyond this first cut time; distance spheres can cease to be smooth there.
