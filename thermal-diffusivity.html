Thermal diffusivity (κ)

 ... Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Thermal conduction Fourier's law Thermal conductivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 3
Thermal diffusivity is κ=k/(ρcp​), the ratio of thermal conductivity to volumetric heat capacity. It sets the diffusion length κt​ over time t.
