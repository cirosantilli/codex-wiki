Split-coequalizer lifting for a fixed-point-domain operation (βQ​(z)=qβB​(sz))

In the nested partial unary operation category, suppose the lower-operation diagram A⇉Bq​Q has a split coequalizer with qs=1, ft=1 and gt=sq. The section s takes eligible fixed points to eligible fixed points, so the displayed next operation is defined on exactly the required domain. For eligible x∈B, the equations give
qβB​(x)=qfβA​(tx)=qgβA​(tx)=qβB​(sqx).
(1)
Thus q preserves the next operation. Every factored morphism preserves it by evaluating at sz, giving creation of the specified coequalizer. Together with reflection of isomorphisms, the Beck monadicity theorem proves one-step monadicity.
