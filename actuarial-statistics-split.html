Actuarial statistics

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 6k Articles: 114
Actuarial statistics uses probability theory and statistical inference to quantify insurance claims, premiums, and the uncertainty of future losses. Aggregate claims model describes portfolios, reinsurance redistributes losses, and the Bühlmann model combines experience with population information.
