Radial tempered distribution

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Schwartz space Tempered distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 1
For n>1, a tempered distribution T on Rn is radial when T∘R=T for every R∈SO(n), where ⟨T∘R,φ⟩=⟨T,φ∘Rt⟩. This agrees with the usual radial function definition for regular smooth distributions, because the special orthogonal group acts transitively on spheres.
