Electromagnetic stress-energy divergence identity (∇b​Tab=Fac​∇b​Fcb/(4π))

For signature (+−−−) in Gaussian electromagnetic units, Tab=(4π)−1[−Fac​Fbc+gabFcd​Fcd/4]. The Electromagnetic Bianchi identity gives Fbc∇b​Fac​=∇a(Fcd​Fcd)/4, so differentiating the stress tensor proves the displayed identity. Stress conservation implies the source-free Maxwell equations if the matrix Fac​ is invertible; otherwise it only forces the field divergence into that matrix's kernel. Conversely source-free Maxwell equations always imply stress conservation.
