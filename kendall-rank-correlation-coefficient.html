Kendall rank correlation coefficient (τa​=(C−D)/(2n​))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Categorical variable Ordinal categorical variable
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 1
A family of ordinal association measures based on concordant pairs and discordant pairs. Without a tie adjustment, the coefficient is τa​=(C−D)/(2n​): the arithmetic mean, over unordered pairs, of the product of the two coordinate comparison signs. Reversing one ordering reverses the sign, and strictly increasing relabellings preserve it. Kendall's tau-b makes an explicit adjustment for tied pairs. The name commemorates Maurice Kendall.
