Massless Dirac equation (γm∂m​Ψ=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Dirac equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
The massless Dirac equation is γm∂m​Ψ=0. The Clifford algebra makes its square the massless wave equation. Quantization of a worldline spin variable as ψ​m=γm/2​ turns its fermionic constraint into this equation.
