OurBigBook.com Words: 29
In the geometric representation of a Coxeter system,
ℓ(wsi​)>ℓ(w)⟺w(ei​) is a positive root,
(32)
and
ℓ(si​w)>ℓ(w)⟺w−1(ei​) is a positive root.
(33)
Replacing “positive” by “negative” reverses either length inequality.
