Shock distance for a spherical simple wave launched at finite radius (Rs​=R0​exp[1/(R0​supf′)])

OurBigBook.com Words: 85
For area A(R)=R2 and smooth data q(θ,R0​)=f(θ) at R0​>0, the transformed distance is ζ=R0​log(R/R0​). If M=supf′>0, the first shock wave occurs at the displayed radius; if M≤0, there is no forward characteristic crossing. Nontrivial bounded data cannot instead be imposed at R=0 in this outgoing simple-wave model: along every characteristic, Rq is constant, so a bounded limit at the origin forces that constant to vanish. With distance measured from a finite emitting surface, use A(Z)=(R0​+Z)2 and subtract R0​ from the shock radius.
