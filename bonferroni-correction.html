Bonferroni correction

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical hypothesis test Multiple hypothesis testing
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 4
The Bonferroni correction tests each of m hypotheses at level α/m. The union bound then controls the familywise error rate by α under arbitrary dependence.
