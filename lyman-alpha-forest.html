Lyman-alpha forest

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Intergalactic medium
OurBigBook.com Words: 376 Articles: 5
The Lyman-alpha forest is the collection of foreground Lyman-alpha absorption features in a distant quasar spectrum. Each observed wavelength identifies a cosmological redshift. Weak forest lines usually sample diffuse intergalactic medium at modest density contrast; strong, self-shielding absorbers need a different density interpretation.
