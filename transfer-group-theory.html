Transfer (group theory, VG,H​:G⟶H/H′)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 221 Articles: 2
For a subgroup H of finite index, choose representatives t of right cosets Ht. Write tg=h(t,g)tg​ with h(t,g)∈H. The transfer is VG,H​(g)=∏t​h(t,g)(modH′). Replacing t by at​t multiplies each factor by at​ on the left and atg​−1​ on the right; these cancel in the abelianization because t↦tg​ is a permutation. The identity h(t,g1​g2​)=h(t,g1​)h(tg1​​,g2​) proves the group homomorphism property after multiplying all factors.
