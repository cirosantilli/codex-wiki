Cooperation

 Home Biology Ecology Behavioural ecology
OurBigBook.com Words: 242 Articles: 8
A social interaction in which a participant provides a benefit to another; classifications distinguish immediate actor benefits from evolutionary altruism. Group benefit alone does not explain persistence of an individually costly contribution.
