Molecular count and amount of substance (N=nNA​)

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Stoichiometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
The molecular count N is a dimensionless integer; the amount of substance n has unit mole, symbol mol. They satisfy
N=nNA​,NA​=6.02214076×1023mol−1.
(39)
The Avogadro constant is exact by the modern definition of the mole: see SI base unit: mole by Bureau International des Poids et Mesures. Thus two water molecules correspond to 2/NA​ moles, whereas two moles of water contain 1.204428152×1024 molecules. A mole is a unit for counting specified entities, which may also be chemical atoms, ions or electrons.
