Characteristic projection

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation Characteristic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For the first-order partial differential equation a(x,y,z)zx​+b(x,y,z)zy​=c(x,y,z), a lifted characteristic curve satisfies (x′,y′,z′)=(a,b,c). Its characteristic projection is the image (x(s),y(s)) in the plane of independent variables. The projection need not be a regular curve: if a=b=0, it can be stationary while z varies. In that situation the lifted curve cannot lie in the graph of a single-valued classical solution unless the required compatibility holds. Noncharacteristic Cauchy data require the tangent to the initial curve and the projected characteristic vector to be linearly independent.
