Hodge star operator (∗)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 37
On an oriented Riemannian n-manifold, the Hodge star is determined by
α∧∗β=⟨α,β⟩ωg​.
(11)
It maps p-forms to (n−p)-forms and satisfies ∗2=(−1)p(n−p) on p-forms.
