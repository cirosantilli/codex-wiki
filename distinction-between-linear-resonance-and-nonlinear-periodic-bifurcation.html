Distinction between linear resonance and nonlinear periodic bifurcation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology
OurBigBook.com Words: 101
Roots of unity among the linearization multipliers give periodic linearized perturbations but do not by themselves prove a branch of nonlinear periodic solutions. For the normalized delayed recurrence Fr​(x,y)=(y,r(1+y)/(1+(r−1)(1+x)2)−1), the multipliers at r=2 are e±iπ/3. Nevertheless, with Q=x2−xy+y2, its sixth iterate has leading displacement (r−2)Bv+QDv, where B=(1−1​12​) and D=(−1/21​−1−3/2​). The identity det(Bv,QDv)=Q2/2 obstructs cancellation for small nonzero v. Indeed any putative local six-cycle requires r−2=O(∣v∣2) by the norm equation, and then its determinant equation would read 0=Q2/2+O(∣v∣5), impossible. This is a concrete caution against promoting a linear resonance to a nonlinear bifurcation assertion.
