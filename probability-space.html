Probability space ((Ω,F,P))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 687 Articles: 18
A probability space is a measure space whose total measure is one. Its measurable subsets are events, and its measure P is their probability.
