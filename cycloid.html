Cycloid (x=R(θ−sinθ), y=R(1−cosθ))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Curve Parametric curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
A cycloid is the plane curve traced by a point on a circle rolling without slipping along a straight line. A parametrization with rolling radius R is x=R(θ−sinθ), y=R(1−cosθ); reversing the sign of y reflects the curve. Its cusps occur at integer multiples of 2π. A descending cycloidal arc solves the classical brachistochrone problem.
