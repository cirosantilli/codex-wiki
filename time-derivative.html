Time derivative (∂t​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Partial derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
A time derivative is the partial derivative of an evolving field with respect to time while holding its spatial coordinates fixed. For a quantity depending only on time, it is an ordinary derivative.
