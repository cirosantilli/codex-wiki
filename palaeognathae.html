Palaeognathae

 ... Diapsida Archosauria Dinosaur Theropod dinosaur Bird Aves
OurBigBook.com Words: 23 Articles: 2
The crown-bird lineage containing ostriches, emus, kiwis, tinamous and relatives. Its palaeognathous palate is an anatomical specialization, not evidence that its members are ancestors of neognaths.
