Quadratic growth of a sixfold product difference set (∣3SS−3SS∣≥21​min(p,∣S∣2))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Sum-product phenomenon
OurBigBook.com Words: 99
For S⊆Fp​ with ∣S∣≥2, put R=(S−S)/(S−S) using nonzero denominators. If R is proper, some r∈R+1 lies outside R, since invariance under addition of one would give all of the prime field. The map (x,y)↦x+ry on S2 is injective. Clearing the denominator in a representation of r−1 puts a dilate of its image inside 3SS−3SS. If R is the full field, averaging the collision count of S+rS gives some r with ∣S+rS∣≥21​min(p,∣S∣2). Clearing its denominator puts a dilate inside 2SS−2SS⊆3SS−3SS. Here SS is a product set, and the prefactors denote repeated sumsets, not scalar multiplication.
