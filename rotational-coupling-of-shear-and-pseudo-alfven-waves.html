Rotational coupling of shear and pseudo-Alfvén waves (ωI​/ωA​∼2Ω/(k⊥​vA​))

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation Rotating-frame magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 94
With aligned uniform magnetic field and rotation, pressure projection couples the two incompressible transverse wave polarization states at rate ωI​∼2Ω∣k∥​∣/k⊥​. Each nonrotating Alfvén wave has rate ωA​=∣k∥​∣vA​. Their ratio is independent of the anisotropy. Leading finite-timescale decoupling therefore needs k⊥​vA​≫2Ω, with rotational coupling also slow relative to any retained nonlinear evolution. At finite amplitude the usual anisotropic reduction still permits passive advection of pseudo-Alfvénic fluctuations by the Alfvénic subsystem; removing rotational mixing does not eliminate that one-way interaction. Because the two Alfvén polarizations are degenerate, weak coupling can still mix them over the longer time ωI−1​.
