Schramm–Loewner evolution (SLEκ​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 37
Chordal Schramm–Loewner evolution in the complex upper half-plane from 0 to ∞ is the random Loewner chain driven by Ut​=κ​Bt​, where B is standard Brownian motion and κ≥0.
