The real Airy function asymptotics used for the Airy turning-point connection formula are, for z,s→+∞,
Ai(z)Ai(−s)​∼2π​z−1/4​e−2z3/2/3,∼π​s−1/4​sin(2s3/2/3+π/4),​Bi(z)Bi(−s)​∼π​z−1/4​e2z3/2/3,∼π​s−1/4​cos(2s3/2/3+π/4).​
(28)
These real-axis asymptotics fix both normalization and phase.[ref] Near a, I∼2z3/2/3, Φ∼2(−z)3/2/3, and κ−1/2∼ℓ1/2z−1/4. Matching to the constants of c gives CA​=2π​ℓ1/2D and CB​=π​ℓ1/2G. Therefore
κ​De−I+GeI​⟷k1​​2Dcos(Φ−π/4)−Gsin(Φ−π/4)​.​
(29)
In particular, the decaying component matches the cosine with the requested −π/4 shift, with a factor two in its oscillatory coefficient. The growing component matches a sine with the indicated minus sign. Expanding into travelling waves gives
Ain​=D−2iG​,Ar​=D+2iG​.​
(30)
The factor two is essential when converting the real oscillatory components to incident and reflected travelling waves.
