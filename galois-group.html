Galois group (Gal(L/K))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Finite Galois extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The Galois group Gal(L/K) consists of the field automorphisms of L that fix K pointwise. For a finite Galois extension, its order is [L:K].
