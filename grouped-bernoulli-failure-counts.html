Grouped Bernoulli failure counts (p​=∑j​Xj​/(mq))

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution Binomial distribution Binomial proportion
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
With m groups of q trials and common marginal failure probability p, each count Xj​ has expectation qp, so the displayed estimate is unbiased without assuming independence within groups. Independent individual trials give variance p(1−p)/(mq). If only groups are independent, estimate the variance instead by sX2​/(mq2), retaining within-group dependence. Equal marginal probabilities alone do not justify a binomial confidence interval.
