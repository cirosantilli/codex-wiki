Heavy symmetric top

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics Symmetric top
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 2
A heavy symmetric top is a symmetric top with one point fixed in a uniform gravitational field. Its vertical and body-axis rotational symmetries give two cyclic Euler angles.
