For a splitting field L of f over K, define Gal(f)=AutK​(L). Each automorphism permutes the distinct roots, since it fixes the coefficients. An automorphism fixing every root fixes the field they generate, so this action is faithful. If there are r≤n distinct roots, it gives an embedding into Sr​, and hence into Sn​ by fixing n−r additional symbols.
