By Vieta's formulas, the elementary symmetric polynomials in the roots give
c1​=−(α1​+α2​+α3​),c2​=α1​α2​+α2​α3​+α3​α1​,c3​=−α1​α2​α3​.
(1)
For each root, αj3​+c1​αj2​+c2​αj​+c3​=0. Multiplying by αjk​ and summing gives the power sum recurrence
sk+3​+c1​sk+2​+c2​sk+1​+c3​sk​=0(k≥0).​
(2)
Its initial values are s0​=3, s1​=−c1​ and s2​=c12​−2c2​. They are polynomials with integer coefficients in c1​,c2​,c3​. The recurrence uses only addition and multiplication by those coefficients, so induction proves the same for every sk​. This is the cubic case of Newton's identities.
For c1​=0, put α1​=u, α2​=v, α3​=−u−v. Then c2​=−(u2+uv+v2) and c3​=uv(u+v). Expanding the product of root differences gives
Δ​=(u−v)2(u+2v)2(2u+v)2=4(u2+uv+v2)3−27u2v2(u+v)2=−4c23​−27c32​​.​
(3)
This is the discriminant of a depressed cubic in depressed form, and the displayed identity also proves its expression in integer-coefficient symmetric polynomials.
