Gauge dependence of helicity in an open magnetic flux tube (Hm​/L=πbCR02​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential Magnetic helicity
OurBigBook.com Words: 108
An open flux tube carries magnetic flux through its end caps. Its ordinary magnetic helicity can therefore change under a single-valued gauge transformation. For B=aReϕ​+bez​ inside R<R0​ and zero outside, a regular family of magnetic vector potentials is Aϕ​=bR/2, Az​=C−aR2/2 inside and Aϕ​=bR02​/(2R), Az​=C−aR02​/2 outside. Their tangential components agree at R0​. Inside, A⋅B=bC, so the helicity per unit length is πbCR02​. Changing C is the gauge transformation Λ=ΔCz, and changes this result whenever b=0. The convention Az​=0 outside gives C=aR02​/2 and helicity per unit length πabR04​/2, while C=0 gives zero. Without an end-cap or gauge convention the ordinary helicity is not unique.
