Hessian of a sum of functions of linear forms (∇2l(x)=ATdiag(yi​f′′(aiT​x))A)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Hessian matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For twice differentiable f:R→R, constant vectors ai​ and constant scalars yi​, let l(x)=∑i​yi​f(aiT​x), and let row i of A be aiT​. The chain rule gives
∇l(x)=∑i​yi​f′(aiT​x)ai​,∇2l(x)=∑i​yi​f′′(aiT​x)ai​aiT​.
(21)
The second formula is the displayed matrix product because entry (j,k) is the same weighted sum ∑i​yi​f′′(aiT​x)aij​aik​. Signed diagonal factors do not ensure a positive-definite Hessian matrix. The factorization also gives an efficient matrix-free Hessian-vector product.
