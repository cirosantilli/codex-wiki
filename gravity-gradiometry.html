Gravity gradiometry

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field Tidal tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 266 Articles: 1
Gravity gradiometry measures spatial differences of gravitational acceleration. Two simultaneous measurements along a unit vector e, separated by Le, give
Le⋅[g(R+Le)−g(R)]​≃eTTe.
(19)
This measures one directional component of the tidal tensor, not its whole matrix. Any identical platform acceleration cancels in the difference. Unequal instrument responses, changing geometry and rotating reference frames can spoil that cancellation. For a large baseline, the quotient is an average of the directional tidal tensor along the baseline, rather than its exact value at one point.
