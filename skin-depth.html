Skin depth (δ)

 ... Electric current Electrical resistance and conductance Electrical resistance Ohm's law Electrical conductivity Skin effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The skin depth is the distance over which a field amplitude falls by e−1 inside a conductor. In the local good-conductor limit, δ=2/(μσω)​. Intensity then falls by e−2 over one skin depth; amplitude and intensity penetration lengths differ by a factor two.
