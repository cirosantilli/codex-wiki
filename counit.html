Counit (ε)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Monoidal category Comonoid
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
The structure map ε:C→I of a comonoid, with (ε⊗1)Δ and (1⊗ε)Δ equal to the inverse unitors. This is distinct from the counit of an adjunction.
