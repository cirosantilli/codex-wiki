Morphism of finite type

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 1
A morphism f:X→Y is of finite type when every point of Y has an affine neighborhood V=SpecA for which f−1V has a finite affine cover SpecBi​ with each Bi​ a finitely generated A-algebra.
