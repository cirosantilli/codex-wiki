Biproduct-induced addition of morphisms (f+g=∇B​⟨f,g⟩)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Enriched category Semi-additive category Biproduct
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
In a pointed category with finite product in a category and coproducts in a category and invertible canonical maps c:A+B→A×B, the unique commutative-monoid enrichment is f+g=[1B​,1B​]cB,B−1​⟨f,g⟩. Associativity and commutativity follow by comparing fold maps on triple and swapped coproduct injections. Composition distributes by the universal properties. For uniqueness, bilinearity forces i1​p1​+i2​p2​=1 on each biproduct, and composing with the fold and the paired morphism forces the addition formula.
