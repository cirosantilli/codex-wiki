The Weil pairing is a bilinear, alternating, nondegenerate pairing
en​:E[n]×E[n]⟶μn​.
(28)
For an isogeny ϕ and its dual, it satisfies en​(ϕP,Q)=en​(P,ϕ​Q) whenever both sides are defined.
