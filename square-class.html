Square class ([a]∈K∗/K∗2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Kummer theory Square-class group of a field
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
The square class of a∈K∗ is its image in the square-class group of a field K∗/K∗2. Two nonzero elements have the same square class exactly when their quotient is a square. Every element of this quotient has order dividing 2. Over the rational numbers, a square class has a unique signed squarefree integer representative.
