Elastic-demand Wardrop equilibrium

In an elastic-demand Wardrop equilibrium, demand fs​ responds to the minimum route delay λs​ through fs​=Bs​(λs​). An inverse demand function supplies the utility term that must be subtracted from the Beckmann potential.
