Restriction probability of a Brownian half-plane excursion (P(B∩A=∅)=ψA′​(0))

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Planar Brownian motion Brownian excursion in the upper half-plane
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
For a compact H-hull avoiding zero, let ψA​ map its complement to the half-plane, fix zero, and have derivative one at infinity. The Brownian excursion in the upper half-plane avoids A with probability ψA′​(0). From an interior starting point z, the corresponding transformed process avoids A with probability ImgA​(z)/Imz; taking z=iε and ε↓0 gives the boundary derivative.
