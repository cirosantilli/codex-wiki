For constant Electron density, common path length L, and a solenoidal isotropic spectral tensor, the rotation-measure correlation contains the window ∣∫0L​eikz​zdz∣2. As L becomes large compared with the correlation length, this window acts as 2πLδ(kz​) within the integral. The transverse projector then has zz component one, yielding the displayed two-dimensional Fourier transform. For finite L, the windowed integral retains longitudinal modes and endpoint corrections. The method of using such rotation measure correlations to infer magnetic spectra is developed by Enßlin and Vogt.
