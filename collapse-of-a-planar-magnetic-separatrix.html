Collapse of a planar magnetic separatrix

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Magnetic relaxation
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For a planar magnetic field B=(ψy​,−ψx​,0), ideal induction advects the Cartesian magnetic flux function: Dt​ψ=0. Incompressible flow also preserves the area inside each material contour. During relaxation, the arms of a magnetic separatrix can approach each other while their field-line connectivity remains fixed. A transition of width ϵ with a finite change of tangential field has electric current density of order ϵ−1 and can approach a current sheet. This is a possible limiting mechanism, not a claim that every planar equilibrium is singular.
