Omega-spectrum (Ej​≃ΩEj+1​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Stable homotopy theory Spectrum (topology)
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
An Omega-spectrum has structure adjoints Ej​→ΩEj+1​ that are weak homotopy equivalences. For such a model the level spaces represent the degrees of its represented cohomology theory.
