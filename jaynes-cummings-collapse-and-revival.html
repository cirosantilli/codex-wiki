Jaynes-Cummings collapse and revival (trev​≃2πnˉ​/g)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Tensor product of quantum systems Jaynes-Cummings model
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
An initially excited atom interacting resonantly with a coherent state has inversion ∑n​Pn​cos(2gn+1​t), where Pn​ is its Poisson distribution of photon number. Different Rabi frequencies dephase on a time scale 1/g and approximately rephase near 2πnˉ​/g. The revival estimate follows from the adjacent-component frequency difference g/nˉ​. Large mean photon number makes the early oscillations nearly classical without removing the discrete spectrum responsible for revivals.
