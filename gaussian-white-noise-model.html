Gaussian white noise model (dY=fdt+n−1/2dW)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process Gaussian white noise
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
Observe Y(t)=∫0t​f(s)ds+σW(t) on [0,1], where f∈L2[0,1] is an unknown deterministic drift and W is standard Brownian motion. The observation against a deterministic test function is Y(h)=⟨f,h⟩+σW(h), with covariance σ2⟨h,g⟩. Gaussian white noise is represented by an isonormal Gaussian process on test functions rather than a pointwise noise function. Taking σ=n−1/2 is the usual statistical scaling.
