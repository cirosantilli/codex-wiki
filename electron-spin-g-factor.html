Electron spin g-factor (gs​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential Magnetic dipole moment Electron magnetic moment
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
The dimensionless coefficient in the spin part of the electron magnetic moment. It is distinct from a gyromagnetic ratio, which has units: in the angular convention, γe​=−gs​e/(2me​). It is also distinct from the Landé g-factor gJ​ of a combined atomic angular momentum and from a nuclear or hyperfine g-factor.
For spin one-half, the largest magnitude of a spin-component magnetic dipole moment is gs​μB​/2, not gs​μB​. Thus the ideal value gs​=2 gives one Bohr magneton. This follows directly from the spin angular momentum projections Sz​=±ℏ/2.
