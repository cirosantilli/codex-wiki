Stationary drift saturation (dZ=2λ​dW+gdt,EZt2​=1,Egt2​≤λ2)

For an adapted stationary process with the displayed bounds, the Itô formula and stationarity give ∫0t​(E[Zs​gs​]+λ)ds=0. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality makes each integrand nonnegative, so equality holds almost everywhere. Then E(gs​+λZs​)2≤0, proving g=−λZ for almost every time and outcome. Thus the process solves the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck stochastic differential equation and its initial value, rather than stationarity alone, determines its coupling with W.
