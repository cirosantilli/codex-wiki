Delta-one absoluteness (Δ1​)

 ... Foundations of mathematics Set theory Class (set theory) Transitive class Formula relativization to a class Set-theoretic absoluteness
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
Let M⊆N be transitive models of a theory proving both an existential-bounded and a universal-bounded equivalent of a formula. For parameters in M, the existential form gives upward absoluteness and the universal form gives downward absoluteness. Consequently the models agree on the formula. The equivalences must hold in both models.
