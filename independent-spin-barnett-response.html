Independent spin Barnett response (⟨Jz​⟩=(ℏ/2)tanh(ℏΩ/(2kB​T)))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential Magnetic dipole moment Barnett effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 333
Consider an explicitly stipulated dilute independent spin-one-half two-level system with zero applied magnetic field. Each constituent has Jz​=±ℏ/2 and mz​=γJz​, with constant signed gyromagnetic ratio γ. Ignore interactions and the specimen's own magnetic field. At positive temperature T, thermal equilibrium with the rotating lattice gives energy levels E±​=∓ℏΩ/2. Define the dimensionless variable x=ℏΩ/(2kB​T), where ℏ is the reduced Planck constant and kB​ is the Boltzmann constant. The partition function and Boltzmann distribution give
Z=ex+e−x=2coshx,p+​=Zex​,p−​=Ze−x​.
(68)
Taking the expected value gives
⟨Jz​⟩=2ℏ​tanhx,⟨mz​⟩=γ2ℏ​tanhx,Mz​=nγ2ℏ​tanhx,
(69)
where n is number density. This is the rotational version of the independent-moment magnetic response. The angular momentum aligns with positive rotation even when the magnetic moment points oppositely. The probability ratio p+​/p−​=e2x provides a direct population check of the sign.
For ∣x∣≪1, expand the hyperbolic tangent to obtain
Mz​≃4kB​Tnγℏ2​Ω=4kB​Tnγ2ℏ2​BΩ​.
(70)
The slope against BΩ​ is positive for either sign of γ, while the slope against Ω has the sign of γ. At fixed T>0, reversing Ω reverses both means, and Ω=0 gives zero. At fixed nonzero Ω, the formal T→0+ limit saturates ∣⟨Jz​⟩∣ at ℏ/2; the linear approximation must not be extrapolated there. At T→∞, the response vanishes. These limits describe the toy canonical ensemble, whose equilibration is assumed.
For a numerical example, stipulate n=1028m−3, T=300K, γ=−1.76×1011s−1T−1, and rotation frequency f=1000Hz. With Ω=2πf, ℏ=1.054571817×10−34Js, and kB​=1.380649×10−23J/K, the model gives
BΩ​=−35.7nT,x=8.00×10−11,Mz​=−7.42×10−6A/m.
(71)
These are teaching inputs and calculated outputs, not measured material values. The corresponding internal angular momentum density is Mz​/γ=+4.22×10−17Js/m3. Keeping a rotor at prescribed Ω while its internal angular momentum changes requires the drive to supply the appropriate angular impulse. In an isolated body the mechanical part instead changes oppositely, as in magnetomechanical angular momentum balance. There is no creation of net angular momentum by choosing a rotating frame.
