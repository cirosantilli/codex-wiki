Left Grassmann derivative (∂θL​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Exterior algebra Grassmann algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
Left differentiation with respect to an odd Grassmann variable is the odd linear operation ∂θL​θ=1, with zero derivative on every other generator. For a homogeneous element a of parity ∣a∣, it obeys the graded product rule
∂θL​(ab)=(∂θL​a)b+(−1)∣a∣a(∂θL​b).
(19)
For independent odd generators, ∂θˉL​(θθˉ)=−θ. This sign comes from moving the derivative through the first odd factor in the Grassmann algebra. It fixes chirality in the chiral-superfield component expansion; an ordinary commuting-variable product rule would give the wrong sign.
