Aggregate claims model (S=∑j=1N​Xj​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 34
An aggregate claims model separates the number N of claims from their sizes Xj​, with total S=∑j=1N​Xj​ and the empty sum equal to zero. Under independent and identically distributed random variables for claim sizes, independent of N, conditional expectation gives tractable moments and transforms.
