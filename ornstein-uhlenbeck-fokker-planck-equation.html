Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Fokker-Planck equation (∂t​f=Δv​f+∇v​⋅(vf))

OurBigBook.com Words: 140 Articles: 4
The probability-density equation for the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process with drift −v and diffusion coefficient 2​. The unit-covariance Gaussian density is stationary because its Fokker-Planck probability current vanishes. This normalization makes mean velocity decay at rate one and energy excess decay at rate two.
