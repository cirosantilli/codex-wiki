Surface gravity perturbation of a uniform-density sphere

For a uniform-density sphere with incompressible flow of radius R with one surface spherical harmonic displacement ξr​(R)=lARl−1Ylm​, the bulk density perturbation vanishes but the surface contributes δρ=ρξr​(R)δ(r−R). Continuity of the Newtonian gravitational potential and the Poisson equation give [∂r​δΦ]out−in​=4πGρξr​(R). The regular interior and decaying exterior solutions are
δΦin​=CrlYlm​,δΦout​=CR2l+1r−l−1Ylm​,C=−2l+14πGρlA​.

The coefficient follows because the derivative jump is −(2l+1)CRl−1Ylm​. This surface source is why neglecting the bulk density perturbation does not justify neglecting the perturbed gravity.
