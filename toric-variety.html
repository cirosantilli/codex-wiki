Toric variety

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Toric geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 38
A toric variety XΣ​ is obtained by gluing the affine toric varieties associated with the cones of a fan Σ. It contains an algebraic torus as a dense open orbit.
