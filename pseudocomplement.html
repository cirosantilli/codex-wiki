Pseudocomplement (¬a=a→0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The pseudocomplement of a is the greatest element disjoint from a. It need not satisfy a∨¬a=1. In a frame, double pseudocomplementation defines the Booleanization of a locale; its fixed elements are regular open elements.
