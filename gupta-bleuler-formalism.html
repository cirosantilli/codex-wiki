Gupta-Bleuler quantization keeps Lorentz covariance by first quantizing all four photon polarizations in an indefinite inner-product space. The condition (∂μ​Aμ)(+)∣phys⟩=0, followed by quotienting null states, leaves the two transverse physical polarizations.
