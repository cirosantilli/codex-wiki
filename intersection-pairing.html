Intersection pairing (Hk​(W)×Hn−k​(W,∂W)→F)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Intersection form
OurBigBook.com Words: 227 Articles: 3
An intersection pairing counts signed intersections of transverse representatives of complementary homology classes, using one relative representative when the manifold has boundary. With F2​ coefficients it is defined without orientation choices. Poincare-Lefschetz duality makes this a perfect pairing for compact manifolds.
