Fundamental group of a sphere with an attached chord (π1​(S2∪J)≅Z)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fundamental group Seifert-van Kampen theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
Attach a closed interval to a sphere S2 at two distinct endpoints, with its interior disjoint from the sphere. The fundamental group is infinite cyclic group. Remove a point of the sphere away from the endpoints: the remaining plane has a deformation retraction onto an arc between the endpoints, keeping the added closed interval fixed. This gives a circle. An open cap about the removed point is simply connected, and its punctured overlap has trivial map into the first fundamental group because it lies in the plane. The Seifert-van Kampen theorem therefore adds no further relator.
