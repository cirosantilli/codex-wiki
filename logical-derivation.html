Logical derivation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 68 Articles: 1
A logical derivation is a finite application of explicitly specified inference rules to premises or temporary assumptions. In natural deduction, which assumptions remain open is part of the logical derivation: a conclusion reached inside a temporary subproof cannot simply be used outside it.
