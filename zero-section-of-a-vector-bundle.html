Zero section of a vector bundle (0E​:X→E)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The zero section sends each base point to the zero vector in its fiber. It is a smooth section of a vector bundle and an embedded copy of the base. The Liouville one-form vanishes as an ambient covector precisely along the zero section of a cotangent bundle.
