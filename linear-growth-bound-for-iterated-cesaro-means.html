Linear-growth bound for iterated Cesàro means (∣an​∣≤Cn⟹∣Ckan​∣≤Cn)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Convergent sequence Cesàro mean
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
Let Can​=n−1∑j=1n​aj​ be the Cesàro mean of a sequence. If ∣an​∣≤Cn for every positive integer n, the triangle inequality gives ∣Can​∣≤C(n+1)/2≤Cn. Mathematical induction repeats this estimate with the same constant for every iterate. An eventual O(n) bound can be made global by increasing C to absorb the finitely many initial terms. Consequently iterated Cesàro means preserve linear growth in Big O notation; this bound alone does not assert convergence or summability.
