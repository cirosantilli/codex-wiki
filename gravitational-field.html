Gravitational field (g)

 Home Physics Gravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
In Newtonian gravity, the gravitational field g specifies the acceleration of a freely falling test particle at each position. A gravitational potential Φ gives g=−∇Φ, with the gradient taken in the spatial geometry of the model. In general relativity, the metric tensor describes gravitational effects, and tidal forces are encoded by spacetime curvature.
