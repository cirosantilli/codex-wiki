Advection-driven melting front in a porous matrix

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Stefan problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
For equal phase mass densities, equal sensible heat capacities per unit volume, and negligible thermal diffusion, a porous melting front driven by hot Darcy flux U has speed
V=1+SΔϕU​,S=cp​ΔTL​.
(26)
Here Δϕ is the newly melted volume fraction. Mass conservation preserves U on both sides: the liquid gained by melting fills the newly available pores. The enthalpy increase per unit bulk volume is ρcp​ΔT+ρLΔϕ, so its rate of production is balanced by incoming heat flux ρcp​UΔT.
