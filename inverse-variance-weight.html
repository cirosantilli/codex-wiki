Inverse-variance weight (wi​=vi−1​)

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Unbiased estimator Linear unbiased estimator Inverse-variance weighted mean
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A weight wi​=1/vi​ gives more weight to a more precise estimate. In random-effects meta-analysis the variance of the corresponding marginal distribution is vi​+τ2, giving weight (vi​+τ2)−1. A percentage weight divides the individual weight by the sum of all weights.
