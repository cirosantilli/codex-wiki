Bose-Einstein statistics

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 8k Articles: 141
Bose-Einstein statistics describes identical noninteracting bosons in equilibrium. Any occupation number is allowed in a one-particle state; the resulting Bose-Einstein distribution is n(E)=[eβ(E−μ)−1]−1.
