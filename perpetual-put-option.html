For risk-neutral stock dynamics dS=(r−δ)Sdt+σSdW, with r>0 and σ>0, let β−​<0 solve σ2β(β−1)/2+(r−δ)β−r=0. A fixed downward trigger L∈(0,X) gives continuation value (X−L)(S/L)β−​ for S>L. Optimizing over the trigger, or imposing smooth fit, gives the displayed boundary. The optimal value equals X−S below S∗ and the continuation expression above. A verification argument uses its payoff majorization and nonpositive discounted generator. At r=0 and δ≥0, the perpetual value is X for positive spot, approached by triggers tending to zero rather than attained at a positive finite trigger.
