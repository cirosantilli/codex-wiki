Rayleigh quotient (R[ψ])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Linear operator theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 251 Articles: 6
For a self-adjoint operator H, the Rayleigh quotient is
R[ψ]=⟨ψ∣ψ⟩⟨ψ∣H∣ψ⟩​.
(144)
For a symmetric matrix this reduces to xTAx/(xTx).
