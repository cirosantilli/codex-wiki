Rayleigh quotient (R[ψ])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 478 Articles: 11
For a self-adjoint operator H, the Rayleigh quotient is
R[ψ]=⟨ψ∣ψ⟩⟨ψ∣H∣ψ⟩​.
(41)
For a symmetric matrix this reduces to xTAx/(xTx).
