Infimum and supremum (inf, sup)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 203 Articles: 5
The infimum of a subset of an ordered set is its greatest lower bound; its supremum is its least upper bound. Neither bound must belong to the subset. For bounded nonempty subsets of the real numbers both bounds exist by completeness of the real numbers.
