on a positively oriented keyhole contour with inner radius ρ<1 and outer radius R>1. The upper bank travels from ρ to R, the large circle counterclockwise, the lower bank from R to ρ, and the small circle clockwise. One may first keep the banks at small nonzero angles and then take their limits.
On the upper bank, Logx=logx and x​ is positive. On the lower bank, the corresponding limits are logx+2πi and −x​. Reversing the lower-bank orientation therefore makes the sum of the bank integrals tend to
