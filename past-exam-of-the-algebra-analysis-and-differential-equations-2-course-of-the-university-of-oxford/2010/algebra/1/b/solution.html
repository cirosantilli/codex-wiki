Suppose Sb=0. Use the increasing chain of vector subspaces
0=kerS0⊆kerS⊆kerS2⊆⋯⊆kerSb=V.
(2)
Choose a basis successively, extending a basis of each kernel of a linear map to one of the next kernel of a linear map. If a basis vector first enters at level j, its image under S belongs to kerSj−1, spanned entirely by earlier basis vectors. Thus every matrix column has nonzero entries only above its diagonal: the matrix is strictly upper triangular.
Conversely, for such a matrix and its ordered basis e1​,…,en​, one has S⟨e1​,…,ej​⟩⊆⟨e1​,…,ej−1​⟩. Iterating n times kills every vector. Hence Sn=0​, establishing both directions without assuming a Jordan form.
