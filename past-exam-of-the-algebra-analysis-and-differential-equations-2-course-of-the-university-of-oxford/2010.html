Use a parameter t. The characteristic equations are
x˙=x+cy−bz,y˙​=y+az−cx,z˙=z+bx−ay.​

For a graph of a function z=f(x,y) satisfying the equation, solve the first two equations after substituting z=f(x,y). Thus the classical Dirichlet solution is unique. The usual continuity at the boundary is the interpretation of the stated boundary conditions; allowing singular boundary behavior would remove that hypothesis.
