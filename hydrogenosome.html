Hydrogenosome

 Home Biology Biological cell Organelle
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
A mitochondrion-related organelle class associated with anaerobic metabolism and, in typical examples, hydrogen and ATP production. Substrate-level phosphorylation can supply energy without a conventional oxygen-terminated respiratory chain.[ref]
