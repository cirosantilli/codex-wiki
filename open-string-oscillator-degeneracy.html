Open-string oscillator degeneracy (F(w)=∏n≥1​(1−wn)−24)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Open string
OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 1
For an ordinary critical open string with all Neumann boundary conditions, there are 24 independent transverse creation oscillators at each positive mode number n. Summing their nonnegative occupation numbers gives one factor (1−wn)−24. The coefficient dp​=[wp]F(w) counts oscillator polarizations at level p, excluding continuous center-of-mass momentum and additional endpoint multiplicities. Cauchy coefficient formula gives dp​=(2πi)−1∮F(w)w−p−1dw.
