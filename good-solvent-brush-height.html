Good-solvent brush height (h∼Na(σa2)1/3)

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymer physics Grafted polymer chain Polymer brush
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For an overlapping layer of long flexible grafted polymers, concentration blobs of width D∼σ−1/2 contain g∼(D/a)5/3 statistical units under the three-dimensional Flory approximation. A chain of N/g blobs has height h∼ND/g, giving the displayed good solvent scaling. Numerical prefactors are not supplied by this argument; extension must remain below the polymer contour length.
