Density-parameter divergence at a cosmological turnaround (Ω=ρ/ρc​,ρc​=3H2/(8πG))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Critical density Cosmological density parameter
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
At maximum expansion of a closed matter-dominated Friedmann solution, the Hubble parameter vanishes while the physical mass density stays positive and finite. Its critical density therefore vanishes and the cosmological density parameter diverges. This is a singularity of that ratio rather than a curvature singularity. In the cycloid variable θ, ΩM​=sec2(θ/2), diverging at θ=π and approaching one at the Big Bang and Big Crunch ends.
