Conjugacy class (ClG​(x))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group action Conjugation action
OurBigBook.com Words: 520 Articles: 9
The conjugacy class of x∈G is its orbit under the conjugation action:
ClG​(x)={gxg−1:g∈G}.
