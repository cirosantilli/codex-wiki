Subgame perfect equilibrium

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A strategy profile that is a Nash equilibrium in every subgame, including those outside the realized play path. In finite sequential games, backward induction constructs such profiles by solving continuation games before earlier decisions. When a stage is an all-pay contest, its solution can involve mixed strategies.
