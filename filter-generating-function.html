Filter generating function (A(z)=∑j∈Z​aj​zj)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Stationary process Weakly stationary process Linear filter of a stationary time series
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
For absolutely summable linear-filter coefficients, the generating function is the displayed Laurent series, absolutely convergent on the unit circle. If the filter is one-sided, it is a power series analytic inside the unit disk. A two-sided filter need not have a nonempty larger annulus of convergence. Substituting the backshift operator gives the formal notation Y=A(B)X.
