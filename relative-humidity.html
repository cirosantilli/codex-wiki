Relative humidity (ϕ)

 Home Biology Physiology Homeostasis Thermoregulation Humidity
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
Relative humidity is the ratio of the partial pressure of water vapor to its saturation vapor pressure at the same temperature, usually expressed as a percentage.
