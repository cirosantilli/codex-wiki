For the quadrant x,y>0, the displayed Green function satisfies ΔG=δ(ξ,η)​, zero value on y=0 and zero outward normal derivative on x=0. The method of images uses the logarithmic fundamental solution of the Laplace equation with coefficient 1/(2π): keep the source sign when reflecting across the Neumann side and reverse it when reflecting across the Dirichlet side. The four image signs are therefore +,+,−,− for sources (ξ,η),(−ξ,η),(ξ,−η),(−ξ,−η).
On y=0, equal numerator and denominator give zero. Evenness in x gives the Neumann condition at x=0. The outward normal derivative on the Dirichlet side is the positive kernel
∂n​G(x,0,ξ,η)=πη​((x−ξ)2+η21​+(x+ξ)2+η21​).
