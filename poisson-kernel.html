Poisson kernel (PΩ​(x,ξ)=−∂nξ​​GΩ​(x,ξ))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Poisson's equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 15
A Poisson kernel represents the harmonic extension of Dirichlet boundary data by an integral over the boundary. When the Dirichlet Green function is normalized by −ΔGΩ​=δ, its negative outward normal derivative gives the displayed kernel. The Poisson kernel for the upper half-space and the Poisson kernel for the upper half-plane give explicit examples with different boundary dimensions. Boundedness or other stated growth conditions are needed when selecting a solution in an unbounded domain.
