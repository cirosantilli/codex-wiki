Hook partition ((n−k,1k))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young diagram
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A hook partition has shape (n−k,1k), 0≤k≤n−1. Equivalently its Young diagram has no cell (2,2), so its only cell of content zero is (1,1). Its standard Young tableaux are counted by (kn−1​): choose the k entries below the initial 1, then the column and remaining row orders are forced.
