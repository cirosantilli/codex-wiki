Surjectivity of decomposition group reduction (Dq​↠Gal(kq​/kp​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 97
For a finite Galois extension of number fields, choose a nonzero residue field generator at q and lift it to an integral element a whose residues at the other primes above p are zero, using the Chinese remainder theorem. The orbit polynomial ∏σ∈G​(T−σa) has coefficients in the base ring of integers. Modulo q, its nonzero roots are precisely the orbit of a under the decomposition group. Its coefficients belong to kp​, so finite-field Frobenius automorphism permutes these roots. Consequently some decomposition-group element acts as Frobenius on the generator, and therefore on the entire residue field. Frobenius generates the finite-field Galois group, proving surjectivity.
