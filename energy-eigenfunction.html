Energy eigenfunction (H^ψ=Eψ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Energy eigenvalue Energy eigenstate
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
A nonzero position-space wavefunction representing an energy eigenstate. For a time-independent Quantum Hamiltonian, its time evolution is a global phase, so its position probability density is stationary.
