A finitely generated perfect group isomorphic to its alternating wreath product

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Semidirect product Wreath product Permutation wreath product
OurBigBook.com Words: 173
For every m≥5, let A=Am​ act by rooted permutations on the rooted tree of finite words over m letters. Define b(a) to have local permutation a∈A at the pairwise incomparable vertices 1r2 for r≥0, and identity elsewhere. Then b is an embedding of A and satisfies b(a)=(b(a),a,1,…,1) with trivial root permutation. The group H=⟨A,b(A)⟩ is finitely generated and perfect because it is generated by two finite perfect subgroups.
The root permutation and sections define an injective homomorphism H→HwrAm​. To prove surjectivity, conjugate b(c) by the rooted cycle (234) and take its commutator with b(a). In wreath coordinates this leaves only b([a,c]) in the first coordinate. Perfectness of Am​ therefore supplies the entire localized copy of b(A) there. Canceling it from the recursion for b(a) supplies a localized rooted a in the second coordinate. Transitivity moves both kinds of generators to every coordinate, yielding the full base Hm and hence the full wreath product. The group is infinite, since a nontrivial finite order h could not satisfy h=hm∣Am​∣.
