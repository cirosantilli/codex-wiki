Logarithmic stock payoff (f(S)=SlogS)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Black-Scholes model
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
Under the Black-Scholes model with interest rate ρ, volatility σ and remaining time Δ, risk-neutral pricing gives V(S)=S[logS+(ρ+σ2/2)Δ]. This follows by differentiating the lognormal exponential moment. Its option delta is logS+1+(ρ+σ2/2)Δ and its second stock derivative is 1/S.
