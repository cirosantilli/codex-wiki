Linear regression model (Y=Xβ+ε)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Normal linear model Linear regression
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A statistical model specifying a response as a linear combination of known regressor columns and an error. Common assumptions are E[ε]=0 and Cov(ε)=σ2I, although a normal distribution is an additional assumption, not part of the displayed equation alone. Full column rank of the design matrix makes the coefficients identifiable. Statistical association does not by itself give a causal effect.
