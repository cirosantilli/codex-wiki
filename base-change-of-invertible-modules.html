Base change of invertible modules (L↦B⊗A​L)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Projective module Invertible module
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For a ring homomorphism A→B, extension of scalars takes an invertible module to an invertible B-module. A direct-summand presentation in a finite free module remains a direct-summand presentation after tensoring. At a prime q⊂B contracting to p, localization gives Bq​⊗Ap​​Lp​≅Bq​. Compatibility with tensor products, identities and composition makes the Picard group of a ring a covariant functor.
