The standard-tableau right-ideal decomposition of CSn​, tensored over that algebra with V⊗n, gives this direct sum of general linear group modules. With et​=ht​/Hλ​, the map et​CSn​⊗T→et​T is an isomorphism. Individual summands generally need not be symmetric group submodules; instead each is a Schur module for the commuting action.
