Boltzmann distribution ratios and energy reference (pi​/pj​=e−β(Ei​−Ej​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Boltzmann distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 102
For two individual microstates, the partition function cancels:
pj​pi​​=e−β(Ei​−Ej​).
(65)
At positive temperature, a higher-energy individual microstate is less probable, and equal-energy individual microstates are equally probable. For example, a gap Ei​−Ej​=kB​Tln2, using the natural logarithm, gives pi​/pj​=1/2. This comparison concerns individual microstates; a whole macrostate can contain several of them.
Changing the reference for energy leaves every probability unchanged. If all energies become Ei′​=Ei​+C for the same constant C, then
Z′=e−βCZ,pi′​=e−βCZe−β(Ei​+C)​=pi​.
(66)
The partition function itself changes, but its common factor cancels in the Boltzmann distribution. Thus choosing a ground state energy of zero simplifies calculations without changing populations. The expected value of energy shifts by C, as it must when its reference changes.
