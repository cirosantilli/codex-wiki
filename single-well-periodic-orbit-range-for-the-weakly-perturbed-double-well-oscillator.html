Single-well periodic orbit range for the weakly perturbed double-well oscillator

 Home Physics Branch of physics Dynamical systems Energy balance method Energy balance for the weakly perturbed double-well oscillator
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
For the periodic energy levels −1/4<H0​<0 enclosing one well, the energy-balance value of α ranges from one at the center to 5/4 at the homoclinic loop. Thus for small positive ε, one expects one symmetric pair of single-well periodic orbits when 1<α<5/4.
