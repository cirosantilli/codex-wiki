AdS scalar-field boundary asymptotics

 ... Physics Branches of physics String theory Gauge-gravity duality AdS/CFT correspondence Holographic dictionary
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 2
A scalar field of mass m in AdSd+1​ behaves near the conformal boundary as ϕ=zd−ΔJ+zΔA+⋯, where m2=Δ(Δ−d). The leading coefficient is the source and the normalizable coefficient determines the operator expectation value.
