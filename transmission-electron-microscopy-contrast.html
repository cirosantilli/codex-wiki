Transmission electron microscopy contrast

 ... Branches of physics Optics Electron optics Electron microscope Electron microscopy Transmission electron microscopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 892 Articles: 10
Transmission electron microscopy contrast is a difference in recorded signal between specimen regions under specified illumination, angular acceptance and detector settings. For a positive reference signal Sref​, one possible signed contrast convention is
C=Sref​S−Sref​​.
(2)
Negative C means darker than the reference; it does not specify a surface height or chemical composition. The definition must accompany a numerical contrast value, since other conventions exist. A conspicuous feature need not establish fine spatial resolution: detecting a small signal difference and distinguishing two nearby features are different measurement questions.
