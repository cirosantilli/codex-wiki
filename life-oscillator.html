Life oscillator (Fp(A)=A)

 Home Computer science Conway Game of Life
OurBigBook.com Words: 263 Articles: 1
A Life oscillator is a nonconstant periodic orbit of the Conway's Game of Life update function F. Its period is the least positive integer p for which Fp(A)=A, comparing the entire live-cell set at the same coordinates. A configuration that never changes is instead a fixed point; a Life glider is not a Life oscillator on the infinite board because it returns at displaced coordinates. The Life blinker has period two.[ref]
