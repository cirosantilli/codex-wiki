Orthogonal completion of mixed neutral lepton kinetic terms (nL​=cα​νL​+sα​NL​,nL⊥​=−sα​νL​+cα​NL​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Weak interaction Charged current
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
If a chiral fermion triplet uses one normalized linear combination of two neutral leptons, adding the perpendicular combination as a gauge singlet completes their canonical kinetic terms. The two combinations are related to the original fields by a real orthogonal matrix. Hence their summed kinetic quadratic form is exactly that of the two original fields, with no mixed terms. The singlet carries the same chirality; no right-handed component is created for a purely left-handed massless neutrino.
