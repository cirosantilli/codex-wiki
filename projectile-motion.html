Projectile motion

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 465 Articles: 7
Motion after launch under a specified gravitational field, with negligible drag force and no thrust. In a constant downward gravitational acceleration g, horizontal velocity is constant and vertical position obeys y(t)=y0​+vy0​t−gt2/2. Eliminating time gives a parabola when horizontal velocity is nonzero. Over larger distances the changing direction and magnitude of gravity require an orbital model instead.
