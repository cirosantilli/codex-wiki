Optimization constraint (g(x)=0, h(x)≤0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Constrained optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 85 Articles: 1
An optimization constraint restricts the feasible points in constrained optimization, for example by an equality g(x)=0 or an inequality h(x)≤0. Extrema are considered on the resulting feasible set rather than on the whole domain of the objective.
