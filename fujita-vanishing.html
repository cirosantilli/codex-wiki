Fujita vanishing

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Sheaf cohomology Serre vanishing
OurBigBook.com Words: 190 Articles: 2
Given a coherent sheaf F and an ample Cartier divisor A on a projective scheme, one integer a0​ ensures Hi(X,F(aA+N))=0 for all i>0, all a≥a0​, and every nef divisor N. The uniformity in N is stronger than Serre vanishing. Fujino's note on Fujita vanishing states the theorem for arbitrary projective schemes over a field.
