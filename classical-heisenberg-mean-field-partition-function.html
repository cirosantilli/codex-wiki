For unit three-component classical spins and energy −J∑⟨ij⟩​Si​⋅Sj​ on a lattice of coordination zc​, mean-field decoupling gives EMF​=−zc​JS∑i​Siz​+Nzc​JS2/2. Integrating each spin over solid angle yields
ZMF​(S)=e−βNzc​JS2/2[4πsinh(βzc​JS)/(βzc​JS)]N.
(24)
The constant term corrects double counting. A normalized angular measure removes (4π)N but does not change the self-consistency equation. The lattice dimension and spin-space dimension are different data.
