Line search

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 529 Articles: 9
A line search chooses a positive step size α along a search vector s, updating x to x+αs. For a differentiable function f, a descent direction makes sufficiently short steps decrease the objective function; an Armijo condition quantifies the decrease, while the Wolfe conditions also prevent accepting an unnecessarily short step.
