Frobenius reciprocity

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Induced representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
For H≤G, an H-representation V, and a G-representation W,
HomG​(IndHG​V,W)≅HomH​(V,ResHG​W).
(65)
Equivalently, induction and restriction are adjoint for character inner products.
