Incidences between points and polynomial graphs

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Incidence geometry Szemerédi–Trotter theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For m distinct real univariate polynomials of degree at most d and n points with distinct first coordinates, the number of incidences between the points and the polynomial graphs is
O(m+n+d1/3m2/3n2/3).
(2)
Two polynomial graphs meet at most d times, so the graph formed from consecutive incidences has O(dm2) crossings; the Crossing lemma supplies the lower bound.
