Complex conjugation (z↦z)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Complex number Complex conjugate
OurBigBook.com Words: 89 Articles: 1
Complex conjugation sends a complex number z=a+ib to its complex conjugate z=a−ib. It is an involution and a field automorphism of C, with fixed field R. Applied componentwise to a complex vector, it produces the conjugated vector appearing in a Choi state for a rank-one Kraus operator.
