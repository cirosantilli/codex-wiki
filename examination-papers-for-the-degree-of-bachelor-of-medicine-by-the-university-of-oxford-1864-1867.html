Examination papers for the Degree of Bachelor of Medicine by the University of Oxford (1864–1867) (1864–1867)

 Home Biology Biology bibliography
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
A digitized volume of historical University of Oxford examinations. The First and Second Examination paper labels restart each year. Only the month and year are printed on the 1867 papers; page locators here refer to the PDF volume, not printed page numbers.
Original scan: upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3e/Examination_papers_for_the_Degree_of_Bachelor_of_medicine_-_1864-67_(IA_b24757810).pdf
Catalogue: wellcomecollection.org/works/d7b9zgnx
Wellcome Collection marks this digitized volume with the Public Domain Mark. The original was supplied by Royal College of Physicians, London. The original questions can therefore accompany the solutions here.[ref]
