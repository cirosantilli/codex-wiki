Canonical product construction for zeros escaping to the disk boundary (F(z)=zm∏n≥1​En​(z/an​))

 ... Analysis Complex analysis Holomorphic function Entire function Weierstrass factorization theorem Canonical product
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For nonzero prescribed zeros an​ with ∣an​∣→1, the Weierstrass elementary factors of increasing order give the displayed holomorphic function on the unit disc; m handles finitely many zeros at zero. On any compact disc, eventually ∣z/an​∣≤q<1, and ∣logEn​(z/an​)∣≤qn+1/[(n+1)(1−q)]. The infinite product convergence from logarithmic tails gives a nonvanishing tail. The finite initial factors supply exactly the prescribed zeros and multiplicities. No Blaschke condition is required unless boundedness is imposed.
