Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix (VCKM​)

The Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix V=UuL†​UdL​ compares the left-handed unitary matrices that diagonalize the up-type and down-type Yukawa matrices. Its rows label up quarks, charm quarks and top quarks; its columns label down quarks, strange quarks and bottom quarks. In the weak charged current, the coefficient of uˉLi​γμdLj​Wμ+​ is Vij​. A single CKM matrix therefore connects quark masses, flavour-changing decay amplitudes, and CP violation.
Write sij​=sinθij​ and cij​=cosθij​. An exactly unitary matrix with three mixing angles and one CP-violating phase is

This convention agrees with Makoto Kobayashi's account of three-generation quark mixing.[ref]
The Christenson-Cronin-Fitch-Turlay kaon CP violation experiment established the phenomenon that required an explanation. Makoto Kobayashi and Toshihide Maskawa showed that two-generation quark mixing cannot supply it, whereas adding a third quark generation permits an irreducible weak phase. Their 1973 paper also considered additional fields as alternative mechanisms; the three-generation conclusion concerns CP violation from the minimal unitary quark mixing mechanism.[ref]
A common weak phase makes CP violation across mesons testable through the BABAR time-dependent CP violation experiment. It does not explain the cosmic baryon asymmetry.[ref]
