Neo-Hookean solid

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Finite elasticity Hyperelastic material
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 1
For an incompressible neo-Hookean solid, a convenient strain energy density is W=μ(trC−3)/2, where C is the right Cauchy-Green deformation tensor. An additive constant in W does not affect the stress. The shear modulus μ fixes the small-strain stiffness.
