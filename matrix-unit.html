Matrix unit (Eij​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The matrix unit Eij​ has entry one in row i, column j, and zero in every other position. The matrix units form the standard basis of the vector space of matrices of a fixed size.
