Q-Q plot

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 61 Articles: 1
A quantile-quantile plot compares ordered observations with quantiles of a reference distribution, or with quantiles of another sample. Approximate linearity supports agreement up to location and scale. A regression normal Q-Q plot compares ordered standardized regression residuals with standard normal distribution quantiles.
