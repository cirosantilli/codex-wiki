Nugget effect (τ2)

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Geostatistics Intrinsically stationary random field Semivariogram
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
The nugget is the discontinuity of a distance semivariogram at zero. Independent location-specific noise of variance τ2 contributes covariance τ2 at zero displacement and zero covariance at nonzero displacement, so its semivariogram is τ21{h=0}​.
