Nonvanishing of the Jacobi theta function

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Theta function Jacobi theta function
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
The Jacobi triple product gives
θ(τ)=∏n≥1​(1−q2n)(1+q2n−1)2,q=eπiτ.
(39)
Every factor is nonzero for ∣q∣<1, and the product converges to a nonzero limit, so θ has no zero in the upper half-plane.
