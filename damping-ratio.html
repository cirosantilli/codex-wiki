Damping ratio (ζ=2mk​b​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Damping Viscous damping
OurBigBook.com Words: 214 Articles: 1
The damping ratio is the dimensionless ratio b/bcrit​. Substituting the exponential function x=ert into the viscous damping model gives the characteristic equation and its characteristic roots:
mr2+br+k=0,r±​=−ζω0​±ω0​ζ2−1​.
(26)
For 0<ζ<1, underdamping gives a pair of complex conjugate roots with negative real part. For ζ=1, critical damping gives the repeated negative real root. For ζ>1, overdamping gives two distinct negative real roots. At ζ=0 the roots are purely imaginary and the harmonic oscillator is undamped; this boundary case has no energy loss. With m,k>0, negative b would inject energy and lies outside the dissipative model.
The left panel locates r/ω0​ in the complex plane; the imaginary part gives the damped angular frequency, while the negative real part gives the exponential decay rate. The right panel shows the positive rates −Re(r±​)/ω0​. Beyond critical damping, one root moves toward zero, explaining slow late motion for generic initial conditions. The repeated-root solution also has a polynomial prefactor, so these rates alone do not describe its complete response.
