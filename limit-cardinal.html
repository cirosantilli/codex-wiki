Limit cardinal

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cardinal number
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
An infinite cardinal number is a limit cardinal if it is not a successor cardinal; equivalently it is an aleph number with zero or a limit ordinal as index. Uncountable limit cardinals are either singular cardinals or weakly inaccessible cardinals. This concerns succession among cardinals, rather than merely being a limit ordinal.
