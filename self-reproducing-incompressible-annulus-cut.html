Self-reproducing incompressible annulus cut

 ... Geometry and topology Topology Topological manifold 3-manifold Incompressible surface Three-manifold hierarchy
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
Let P be a once-punctured torus and let c be a nonseparating essential curve. The annulus c×[0,1] is an incompressible surface in P×[0,1]. Cutting gives a pair of pants times an interval. Both products are genus-two handlebodies, since a compact connected surface with boundary times an interval is a handlebody of genus 1−χ(P).
