Sequential quadratic programming

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Constrained optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 66 Articles: 1
Sequential quadratic programming computes steps from a quadratic model of the optimization Lagrangian with linearized constraints. Its constrained Newton system couples a primal step and multiplier estimates. A line search or trust region then controls the use of that local model.
