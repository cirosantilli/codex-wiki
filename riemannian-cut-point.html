Riemannian cut point (γ(c(v)),c(v)=sup{t:d(p,γ(t))=t})

 ... Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Arc length Riemannian distance Minimizing geodesic Cut locus
OurBigBook.com Words: 226 Articles: 2
For a unit-speed geodesic γ(t)=expp​(tv), a finite cut time c(v) is the last time to which its segment from p minimizes Riemannian distance. Its endpoint is the Riemannian cut point along that geodesic. It lies in the cut locus; it need not be a cut point in the topological sense. The segment up to the cut time still minimizes by continuity of distance.
