Velocity-anisotropy parameter (β)

 ... Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics Galactic distribution function Velocity dispersion
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
For spherical radial dispersion σr2​ and total two-component tangential dispersion σt2​, the velocity-anisotropy parameter is
β=1−2σr2​σt2​​.
(31)
Positive values are radially biased, zero is isotropic, and negative values are tangentially biased.
