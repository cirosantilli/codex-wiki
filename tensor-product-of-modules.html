Tensor product of modules (M⊗R​N)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 534 Articles: 11
The tensor product of an R-module M and an R-module N is an R-module M⊗R​N generated by pure tensors m⊗n, subject to additivity in both variables and the balancing relation
(rm)⊗n=m⊗(rn).
