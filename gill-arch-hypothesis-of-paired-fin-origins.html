Gill-arch hypothesis of paired fin origins

 Home Biology Anatomy Fin Paired fin
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The proposal that paired appendages evolved through modification of branchial structures. Evidence for a pharyngeal contribution to the shoulder is distinguishable from evidence that the whole fin arose by conversion of a gill arch.
