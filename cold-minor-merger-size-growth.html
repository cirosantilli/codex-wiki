Cold minor-merger size growth (Rg​∝M2)

 ... Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy evolution Galaxy merger Dry galaxy merger Minor galaxy merger
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For repeated minor galaxy mergers with negligible internal satellite binding energy and zero orbital energy at infinity, parabolic dry-merger energy accounting leaves total energy nearly constant. The virial theorem then gives gravitational radius Rg​∝M2 and mean-square speed proportional to M−1. Small satellite mass does not by itself imply negligible internal binding energy.
