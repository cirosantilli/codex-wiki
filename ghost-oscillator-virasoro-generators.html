Ghost oscillator Virasoro generators (Lmgh​=∑k​(m−k):bm+k​c−k​:)

OurBigBook.com Words: 40
Contracting an antighost with the cubic FP ghost term of the BRST operator produces the bilinear generators. They give [Lm​,bn​]=(m−n)bm+n​ and [Lm​,cn​]=−(2m+n)cm+n​. Their zero-mode additive constant depends on the vacuum and normal ordering convention; it must agree with the corresponding c0​ term in the charge.
