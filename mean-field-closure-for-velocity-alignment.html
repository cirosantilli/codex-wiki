Mean-field closure for velocity alignment (p2​(z,z⋆​)≃p1​(z)p1​(z⋆​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Kinetic theory Velocity alignment
OurBigBook.com Words: 175 Articles: 3
Neglect of pair correlations closes the first marginal equation of the dissipative Liouville equation. For unit-normalized marginal distributions, the acceleration is (N−1)L[p1​]. With number-normalized f=Np1​, it is (1−1/N)L[f]. The usual mean-field alignment equation neglects this relative 1/N correction. Independence at one time does not imply that interactions preserve independence.
