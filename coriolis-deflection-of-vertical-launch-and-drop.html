Coriolis deflection of vertical launch and drop (xlaunch​=−4Ωu3cosλ/(3g2),xdrop​=−xlaunch​/4)

 ... Mathematical physics Reference frame Rotating reference frame Coriolis force Coriolis acceleration Coriolis acceleration in local Earth coordinates
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
At first order in Earth's rotation, vertical velocity gives east acceleration −2Ωcosλz˙. A launch returning to its initial height lands westward, while a drop from the launch's maximum height lands eastward with one quarter the displacement magnitude. These results assume constant effective gravity, no air drag and no horizontal velocity in the Coriolis term. The northern component vanishes at this order.
