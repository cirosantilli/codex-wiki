Gravitino-induced torsion (Tμν​a=2κ2​ψˉ​μ​γaψν​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supergravity Minimal four-dimensional supergravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
With canonical Rarita-Schwinger field normalization, one convention gives the displayed torsion tensor. Its contorsion tensor is Kμab​=κ2(ψˉ​μ​γa​ψb​−ψˉ​μ​γb​ψa​+ψˉ​a​γμ​ψb​)/4. The algebraic spin connection equation determines ω=ω(e)+K; substituting it in the action produces four-fermion terms. This is an instance of Einstein-Cartan theory and is efficiently varied using the 1.5-order formalism. A rescaled gravitino changes the displayed coefficient.
