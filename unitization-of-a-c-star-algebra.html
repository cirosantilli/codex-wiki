Unitization of a C-star algebra (A=A⊕C1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra C-star algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 1
For a nonunital C-star algebra, adjoining an identity gives multiplication (a,λ)(b,μ)=(ab+λb+μa,λμ) and involution (a,λ)∗=(a∗,λ). Its standard C-star norm extends that of A, with the new identity of norm one. It can be constructed by adjoining the identity operator to a faithful nondegenerate operator representation and taking the operator norm. The original algebra is a closed ideal. The usual spectrum of an element in a nonunital algebra is taken in this unitization, permitting unital spectral arguments without requiring the original algebra to contain an identity.
