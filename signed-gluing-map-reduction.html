Signed gluing-map reduction (z=−xy,z′=μ+Asgn(z)∣z∣δ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Homoclinic orbit Symmetric homoclinic gluing bifurcation
OurBigBook.com Words: 206 Articles: 2
For x′=−μsgn(x)+Asgn(y)∣x∣δ, y′=sgn(x), the invariant sign sheet has y=±1 after one iterate. There z=−xy obeys the displayed one-dimensional map. A negative fixed z lifts to two symmetry-related fixed points, while a positive fixed z lifts to one period-two orbit. The origin of the return map represents the separatrix, where the flow return time diverges, and is not an ordinary finite-period orbit of a group action.
