Topographic sideband of an internal gravity wave

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave Reflection of an internal-wave ray
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
Reflection from periodic topography couples an incident horizontal wavenumber k to sidebands k+jkT​. Expanding the impermeability condition on a boundary of small amplitude h0​ produces the j=±1 sidebands at order h0​ and the j=0,±2 corrections at order h02​.
