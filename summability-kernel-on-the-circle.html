Summability kernel on the circle (∫kn​dm=1,supn​∥kn​∥1​<∞,∫∣t∣≥δ​∣kn​∣dm→0)

A sequence of continuous periodic functions satisfying the displayed conditions, with normalized Haar measure m and every δ>0, is a summability kernel. For f in a homogeneous Banach space on the circle, subtract f∫kn​dm from its convolution with kn​. Translation continuity controls the resulting integral near zero, while boundedness by 2∥f∥B​ and the vanishing tails control its complement. Thus ∥f∗kn​−f∥B​→0. The nonnegative Fejér kernel is an example with L1 norm one.
