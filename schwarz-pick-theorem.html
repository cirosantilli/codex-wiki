Schwarz-Pick theorem (ρ(f(z),f(w))≤ρ(z,w))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Schwarz lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 366 Articles: 4
A holomorphic self-map of the unit disc contracts its pseudohyperbolic distance. Conjugating by Möbius transformations that send one chosen source point and its image to zero reduces the statement to the Schwarz lemma. In the complex upper half-plane this gives
​f(z)−f(w)​f(z)−f(w)​​≤​z−wz−w​​.
(145)
If f(iY)=iY+o(Y) at infinity, multiply the equivalent inequality between one minus the squared distances by Y and let Y→∞. It yields Imf(z)≥Imz. Applied to the inverse mapping-out function of a compact H-hull, this proves the nonnegativity of its half-plane capacity.
