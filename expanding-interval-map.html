Expanding interval map (inf∣T′∣=λ>1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system
OurBigBook.com Words: 202 Articles: 2
A piecewise smooth interval map is uniformly expanding when every smooth branch has derivative magnitude at least λ>1. A finite branch partition gives a symbolic itinerary and cylinders with diameters at most a constant times λ−n. Expansion alone does not make every symbolic Gibbs measure absolutely continuous; the geometric potential and suitable branch regularity are essential.
