Cylinder-plane image charges (c=d2−a2​,C′=2πε0​/arcosh(d/a))

 Home Mathematics Method of images
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 1
An infinite conducting cylinder of radius a at centre height d>a above a grounded plane has the same exterior electric potential as opposite line charges ±λ at heights ±c. If R+​ and R−​ are distances from the positive and negative lines, V=λln(R−​/R+​)/(2πε0​). The plane has R−​=R+​; the cylinder has R−​/R+​=(d+c)/a. Its voltage is therefore λarcosh(d/a)/(2πε0​), giving the displayed capacitance per unit length. The Uniqueness of the Dirichlet problem fixes the exterior field. A Gaussian contour surrounding the cylinder encloses the positive image line, so λ is also its physical charge per unit length.
