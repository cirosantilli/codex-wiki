Bernstein growth dimension of a Weyl algebra module (d(M)=deg(dimC​Fj​An​M0​))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Gelfand–Kirillov dimension Gelfand–Kirillov dimension of a module
OurBigBook.com Words: 492 Articles: 7
For a nonzero finitely generated Weyl algebra module, choose a finite-dimensional generating subspace M0​. Under the Bernstein filtration, dim(Fj​An​M0​) is eventually polynomial because the graded module is finite over a polynomial ring. Its degree is independent of the generating subspace, since two generating filtrations bound each other after fixed shifts. It equals the Gelfand–Kirillov dimension of a module.
