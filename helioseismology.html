Helioseismology

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Stellar oscillation
OurBigBook.com Words: 220 Articles: 3
Helioseismology infers the Sun's structure and dynamics from its stellar oscillations. Spatial projection of resolved surface observations onto spherical harmonics identifies angular degree and azimuthal order; a temporal Fourier transform measures mode frequencies. Stellar acoustic modes constrain the adiabatic sound speed, while rotational splitting constrains stellar rotation. Their finite wavelength, surface reflection phases and finite observational coverage limit inversion resolution.
