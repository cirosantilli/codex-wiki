Bipartite binary quadratic optimization

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Quadratic optimization Binary quadratic optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
For a real rectangular matrix S, maximize pTSq over separate sign choices for p and q. With x=(p,q), the symmetric block matrix A=21​(0ST​S0​) satisfies xTAx=pTSq. Its zero diagonal blocks allow the Krivine rounding scheme to linearize the cross-block expected objective.
