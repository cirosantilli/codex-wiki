Picard functor of a curve (PicC/kd​)

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Locally free sheaf Line bundle Picard group
OurBigBook.com Words: 235 Articles: 2
For a smooth projective geometrically connected algebraic curve C/k, this functor assigns to a scheme T the line bundles on C×T having degree d on each geometric fibre, modulo bundles pulled back from T. One generally takes the associated sheaf for faithfully flat covers. A rational point of C permits normalization of every line bundle along that point; normalized bundles have no automorphisms preserving their normalization and satisfy effective descent. The resulting functor is represented by the degree-d component of the Picard scheme of a curve.
