Bolometer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Photodetector
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
A bolometer detects absorbed electromagnetic radiation through the resulting change in temperature, often read as a change in electrical resistance. Its response depends on absorbed power, so interpreting an optical spectrum requires accounting for the incident wavelength band and detector calibration.
