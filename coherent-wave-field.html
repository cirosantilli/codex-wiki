Coherent wave field (m=E[E])

 Home Physics Wave Wave scattering Random medium Wave propagation in a random medium
OurBigBook.com Words: 313 Articles: 3
In wave propagation in a random medium, the coherent field is the expectation of the complex wave amplitude, m(r)=E[E(r)]. The remaining field E−m has zero expectation. This is the coherent and diffuse wave fields decomposition. Statistical homogeneity of the medium does not make m spatially constant when the incident field is localized: translation symmetry applies to the response together with its incident data. The coherent component can attenuate while the total ensemble-averaged wave intensity remains substantial.
