Shear-horizontal wave (SH wave)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Elastic wave in an isotropic solid
OurBigBook.com Words: 437 Articles: 7
A shear-horizontal wave propagating in the x direction has displacement perpendicular to the sagittal xy plane, for example u=w(x,y,t)ez​. It is divergence-free and obeys the scalar shear-wave equation wtt​=cS2​(wxx​+wyy​).
