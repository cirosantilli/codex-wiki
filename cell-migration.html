Cell migration

 Home Biology Biological cell
OurBigBook.com Words: 297 Articles: 10
Cell migration is movement of a biological cell relative to its surroundings. In crawling cells, contact forces with a substrate can propel the cell, while chemotaxis can bias its direction in response to a chemical gradient.
