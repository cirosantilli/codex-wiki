Tests of special relativity

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 270 Articles: 2
Special relativity connects measurable clock rates, length contraction, light propagation, and four-momentum through the Lorentz transformation. Tests probe particular predictions rather than measuring a coordinate transformation in isolation. Examples include cosmic-ray muon time-dilation experiments, muon storage-ring time-dilation experiments, the Michelson-Morley experiment, the Fizeau experiment, and particle-collision energy and momentum balances.
