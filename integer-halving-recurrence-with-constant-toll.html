Integer-halving recurrence with constant toll (Sn​=a+m(1+⌊log2​n⌋),n≥1)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Sequence Recurrence relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
If S0​=a and Sn​=S⌊n/2⌋​+m for positive integers n, each halving removes one binary digit until zero is reached. There are 1+⌊log2​n⌋ such steps. The formula follows by strong induction, using ⌊log2​⌊n/2⌋⌋=⌊log2​n⌋−1 for n≥2 and checking n=1 separately. With fixed positive m, this is Θ(logn) as n→∞; the zero case retains its separately defined value.
