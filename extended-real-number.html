Extended real number (R)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Real number
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The extended real numbers adjoin −∞ and +∞ to R. They preserve the total order but leave some arithmetic expressions, such as +∞−∞, undefined.
