Tetracode length-isospectral lattice construction (L±​=(±3I+S)Z4)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Euclidean lattice
OurBigBook.com Words: 293 Articles: 3
For the skew matrix S=(0−1−1−1​101−1​1−101​11−10​), both Euclidean lattices reduce to the tetracode modulo 3. A reflection depending on the reduction coset matches their vectors coordinatewise in absolute value. Their weighted length spectra agree for all positive diagonal axis weights. Rationally independent weights force any possible linear isometry to be a coordinate sign change; the code then forces an overall sign, which does not identify the two lattices.
