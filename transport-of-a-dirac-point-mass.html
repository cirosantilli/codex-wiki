Transport of a Dirac point mass (u(x,t)=δ0​(x−ct))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation Linear transport equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For the constant-velocity linear transport equation ut​+c⋅∇x​u=0, a Dirac delta distribution initially at zero moves along x=ct. Its spacetime distribution pairs by ⟨u,φ⟩=∫φ(ct,t)dt, and its initial trace is obtained by weak convergence of distributions. The Fourier representation of the Dirac delta function writes it as an oscillatory integral with phase function (x−ct)⋅θ. Its singular support is precisely the trajectory; it has order-zero distribution regularity as a measure, without becoming a smooth function.
