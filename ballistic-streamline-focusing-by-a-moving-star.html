Ballistic streamline focusing by a moving star (b=d2v2/(2GM))

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical black hole Black-hole accretion Bondi accretion Bondi–Hoyle accretion
OurBigBook.com Words: 73 Articles: 1
In a cold pressureless stream of asymptotic speed v, conservation of specific angular momentum and the eccentricity vector makes a streamline at impact parameter d cross the downstream axis at b=d2v2/(2GM). Its axial velocity there remains v, while opposing transverse velocities collide in a shock.
