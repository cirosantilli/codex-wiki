Smooth algebraic surface

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 411 Articles: 9
A smooth algebraic surface is a two-dimensional smooth variety. Its local rings are regular, and its integral curves define Cartier divisors.
