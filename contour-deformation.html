Contour deformation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Cauchy's integral theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
A contour deformation changes the path of a contour integral through a region where its integrand is a holomorphic function. The Cauchy integral theorem preserves the integral when the endpoints are fixed and the connecting boundary terms vanish. Crossing a pole instead contributes the appropriately oriented residue; a branch cut limits which deformations are permitted on one analytic sheet.
