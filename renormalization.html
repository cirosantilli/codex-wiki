Renormalization

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 70
Renormalization rewrites a regulated quantum field theory in terms of finite parameters fixed by measurements or normalization conditions. Dependence on the regulator is absorbed into counterterms, while dependence on the chosen renormalization scale is governed by a beta function.
