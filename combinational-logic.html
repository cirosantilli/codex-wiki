Combinational logic

 Home Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Circuit complexity Boolean circuit
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 15
A Boolean circuit whose current output depends only on its current input, with no stored state or feedback dependency. Its delay is bounded by the longest input-to-output path of logic gates. This differs from a synchronous sequential circuit, whose output can depend on state stored at earlier clock edges.
