Rasiowa–Sikorski lemma

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Forcing Generic filter
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
If M is a countable transitive model, P∈M, and p∈P, enumerate the dense subsets of P belonging to M and recursively choose a decreasing sequence starting below p that meets each one. Its upward closure is a generic filter over M containing p.
