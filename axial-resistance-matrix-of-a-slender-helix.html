Axial resistance matrix of a slender helix ((Gz​Fz​​)=(AB​BD​)(ΩU​))

For radius b, arc length L, tangent angle ϕ to the horizontal plane, and local drag coefficient C, resistive-force theory gives
A=2CL​(1+cos2ϕ),B=−2CLb​sinϕcosϕ,D=2CLb2​(1+sin2ϕ).
(11)
The sign of B selects the handedness convention. With the force exerted on the fluid taken positive, AD−B2=C2L2b2/2>0. Translation-rotation coupling supplies propulsion for a rotating helical microswimmer with a spherical head.
