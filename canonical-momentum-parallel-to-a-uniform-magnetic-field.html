Canonical momentum parallel to a uniform magnetic field

 Home Physics Branch of physics Classical mechanics Lagrangian mechanics Charged-particle electromagnetic Lagrangian
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For a constant uniform magnetic field B, choose the symmetric gauge A=21​B×r. The coordinate parallel to B is cyclic, so its canonical momentum is conserved. Since A⋅B=0, this component is also the mechanical momentum mr˙⋅B.
