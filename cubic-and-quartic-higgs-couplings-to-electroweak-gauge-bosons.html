Cubic and quartic Higgs couplings to electroweak gauge bosons (HWW, HHWW, HZZ, HHZZ)

The gauge-covariant kinetic term of the Higgs doublet gives MW2​(1+H/v)2Wμ+​W−μ+MZ2​(1+H/v)2Zμ​Zμ/2. Differentiation with respect to the external fields yields Feynman rules 2iMV2​gμν​/v for one Higgs boson and two matching massive electroweak gauge bosons, and 2iMV2​gμν​/v2 for two Higgs bosons and the same pair. Identical neutral fields supply combinatorial factors. The neutral radial Higgs boson has no corresponding elementary coupling to photons at tree level.
