Subbundle slope bound from ambient Chern curvature (μ(F)≤Carea(X)/(2π))

 ... Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Holomorphic vector bundle Degree of a holomorphic vector bundle Slope of a holomorphic vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
Fix a Hermitian metric on a holomorphic vector bundle E over a compact Riemann surface and a positive area form ω. Write iFE​=Kω for its Chern curvature. If every eigenvalue of the Hermitian endomorphism K is at most C, the curvature formula for a holomorphic subbundle gives degF≤rank(F)C∫X​ω/(2π). The second-fundamental-form term is nonpositive. Compactness supplies a finite C independent of the subbundle. If FE​=0, every subbundle has nonpositive degree.
