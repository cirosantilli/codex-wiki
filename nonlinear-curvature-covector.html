Nonlinear curvature covector (ζi​=−∂i​loga+(H/Π)∂i​ϕ)

With negligible shear, a minimally coupled scalar field serving as a clock, and Π=0, the Hamiltonian constraint and momentum constraint imply H˙=−4πGNΠ2 and ∂i​H=−4πGΠ∂i​ϕ. Differentiating the local Friedmann relation yields Π˙=−N(3HΠ+V,ϕ​) and ∂i​Π=−(3H+V,ϕ​/Π)∂i​ϕ. Inserting these identities into ζ˙​i​ cancels all terms, including gradients of the lapse function. The conserved covector becomes the gradient of a uniform-density curvature perturbation at linear adiabatic order. Dropping the shear constraint is essential; this is not a proof of conservation in an arbitrary non-attractor phase.
