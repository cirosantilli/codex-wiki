Free dihedral action on a drilled torus (∣Dn​∣=2n,g=2n+1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Dihedral group
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
Let Dn​ act on Euclidean space by rotations through 2π/n about a vertical axis and half-turns about n equally spaced horizontal axes. A round solid torus about the vertical axis, drilled through radially along the 2n places where the horizontal axes cross its tube, has a smooth boundary of genus 2n+1 after equivariant rounding of its rims. All nonidentity rotation axes lie in the complement of this boundary, so the induced group action is a free action of a group. Each bore replaces two closed discs by an annulus, lowering Euler characteristic by two and increasing genus by one. The boundary orientation is preserved; its induced Riemann surface has an unramified quotient space of genus two by the Riemann-Hurwitz formula.
