Collision

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Particle dynamics Collision mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
A collision is an interaction between approaching bodies or particles that changes their motion. Momentum conservation and conservation of energy constrain the outcomes; inelastic collisions can transfer mechanical energy into internal degrees of freedom.
