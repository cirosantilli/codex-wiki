Spectral theorem (A=∫λdE(λ))

 ... Analysis Functional analysis Hilbert space Riesz representation theorem Adjoint operator Normal operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 848 Articles: 17
The spectral theorem represents a normal operator by multiplication by its spectral parameter. In a finite-dimensional inner product space, this is diagonalization in an orthonormal basis; for a self-adjoint operator on a Hilbert space, it uses a projection-valued measure. Unbounded operators additionally require their natural square-integrability domain.
