Trivial vector bundle

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A rank-d vector bundle is trivial when it is isomorphic over its base B to the product bundle B×Rd→B. Equivalently, it admits a global frame of d pointwise linearly independent sections.
