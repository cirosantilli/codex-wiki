Local isometries are determined by first-order data

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry First fundamental form Local isometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Two local isometries from a connected Riemannian manifold that have the same value and differential at one point agree everywhere. They preserve geodesics, so their equality follows on a normal neighborhood from f(expp​v)=expf(p)​(dfp​v). The set of points where both value and differential agree is consequently open, and continuity makes it closed. Connectedness completes the argument.
