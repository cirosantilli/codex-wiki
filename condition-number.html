Condition number (κ)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Quadratic form Definite matrix Positive-definite matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 56 Articles: 1
The condition number of a problem measures how strongly relative perturbations of its input can amplify relative changes in its output. For an invertible matrix and a chosen operator norm, κ(A)=∥A∥∥A−1∥.
