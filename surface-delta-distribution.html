Surface delta distribution (δS​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Dirac delta function
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 2
For a smooth hypersurface S, its surface delta is the distribution ⟨δS​,φ⟩=∫S​φdS. Its normalization uses geometric surface measure rather than a defining function. On the unit circle, δS1​ pairs by integrating φ(cost,sint) over 0≤t≤2π.
