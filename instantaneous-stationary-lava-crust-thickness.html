Instantaneous stationary lava-crust thickness (he​(τ)=1/[Bθ(τ)])

 ... Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth Magma Lava lake Radiogenically heated lava-lake crust model
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
In the radiogenically heated lava-lake crust model, the thickness derivative vanishes at he​=1/(Bθ) when the liquid superheat θ is positive. This moving curve balances thermal conduction and convective heat flux; it is not itself the evolving crust. Cooling raises the curve and keeps an initially absent crust below it. Warming lowers the curve, allowing lava-crust overshoot under warming. At zero superheat the stationary thickness is infinite, so its finite formula applies only after positive superheat develops.
