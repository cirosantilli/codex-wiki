D-brane

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 30
A D-brane is a hypersurface on which open-string endpoints obey Neumann boundary conditions tangentially and Dirichlet boundary conditions transversely. Its massless open-string vector mode is a worldvolume gauge field.
