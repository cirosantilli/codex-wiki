Seed

 Home Biology Organism Eukaryote Plant
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 14
A seed packages a plant embryo, a seed food reserve and a protective seed coat. It is a living starting point for a new plant, not merely stored food. In a mature pea seed, the large cotyledons hold reserves; other seeds retain substantial endosperm. These are alternative arrangements, not successive germination stages.[ref]
A useful reading exercise is to identify both the future plant and its initial food supply in each drawing. The large yellow area has the same role in both pictures, but it belongs to different plant tissues. Thus location and function alone do not establish tissue identity.
