Electroweak doublet gauge-boson mass matrix (mW2​=g2v2/4)

 ... Quantum field theory Standard Model Electroweak interaction Higgs field Higgs mechanism Electroweak symmetry breaking
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For a Higgs doublet with hypercharge 1/2 and vacuum expectation value (0,v/2​)T, its gauge-covariant kinetic term gives the neutral gauge-boson mass matrix
Mneutral2​=4v2​(g2−gg′​−gg′g′2​)
(5)
in the (W3,B) basis. Its eigenvalues are zero and (g2+g′2)v2/4, and the Weinberg angle rotation gives the photon and Z boson. The two charged electroweak gauge bosons have mW2​=g2v2/4. The zero eigenvalue expresses the unbroken electric-charge gauge symmetry.
