High-pass filter (H(s)=sτ/(1+sτ) for a first-order RC example)

 Home Electrical engineering Signal processing
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A high-pass filter attenuates low frequencies relative to a specified higher-frequency pass-band. A simple first-order RC high-pass has the displayed transfer function, with a zero at zero frequency and a pole at s=−1/τ. Combining a high-pass with a low-pass makes a band-pass, but its attainable selectivity depends on pole structure rather than gain alone.
