RNA-binding protein

 Home Biology Protein
OurBigBook.com Words: 511 Articles: 30
A protein that binds RNA. Binding specificity can depend on nucleotide sequence, RNA secondary structure, chemical modification and other partners; recovery in an RNA pull-down alone does not establish direct recognition.
