Haar approximation error for a function of bounded variation (∥Hj​(f)−f∥1​≤2−jTV(f))

OurBigBook.com Words: 79
Finite total variation of a function on R gives the following error bound for averaging on dyadic cells of length δ=2−j:
∥Hj​(f)−f∥1​≤δTV(f).
(7)
The cell error is at most its length times the oscillation on that cell, and the sum of the oscillations is at most the total variation. This proves that the difference is integrable even if f is not globally in L1. For an even function decreasing to zero on the positive half-line, TV(f)≤2f(0) and the bound becomes f(0)21−j.
