Brownian zero set

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The zero set {t≥0:Bt​=0} of one-dimensional Brownian motion is almost surely closed, uncountable, and has zero Lebesgue measure. The last fact follows from Tonelli theorem because P(Bt​=0)=0 for every t>0.
