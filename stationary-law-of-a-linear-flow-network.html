For the proportionally fair allocation on a linear flow network, independent Poisson flow arrivals at rates νr​ and exponential document sizes of rates μr​ give a continuous-time Markov chain with departures μr​nr​xr​(n). Put ρr​=νr​/μr​. Under ρ0​+ρi​<1 for each local route, the normalizing constant is Z=(1−ρ0​)I−1/∏i​(1−ρ0​−ρi​). The binomial coefficient weight has adjacent-state ratios equal to the aggregate service rates, proving detailed balance. For more general network topologies, proportional fairness need not give this reversible product form.
