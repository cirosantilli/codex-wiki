Adsorption isotherm

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Adsorption
OurBigBook.com Words: 962 Articles: 9
An adsorption isotherm relates equilibrium uptake to surrounding pressure or concentration at fixed temperature. For a gas, specify the partial pressure of the adsorbing chemical species. An uptake trace during incomplete equilibration need not be an adsorption isotherm.
