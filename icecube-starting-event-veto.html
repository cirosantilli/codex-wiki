The 2013 selection rejected early boundary light. Tagged muons checked leakage; air shower companion muons also vetoed downward atmospheric neutrinos.[ref]
A simple original conditional probability model explains why a veto changes the selected mixture. Let A mean that an atmospheric neutrino interaction would pass all other cuts, M that an accompanying muon reaches the veto, and V that the veto fires. Set a=P(M∣A) and ϵ=P(V∣M,A), and neglect unrelated veto hits. The law of total probability gives
P(passes veto∣A)=1−aϵ.
(17)
For chosen a=0.8 and ϵ=0.95, the passing fraction is 0.24. Neither this value nor either input is an IceCube measurement. With equal incident candidate populations, this mechanism alone reduces the atmospheric contribution relative to a hypothetical population having a=0. It does not make every surviving event astronomical: a=0 or ϵ=0 gives no suppression, while a=ϵ=1 removes the entire chosen atmospheric population. If unrelated light vetoes a surviving event independently with probability u, its passing fraction becomes (1−u)(1−aϵ); independence is an additional assumption.
