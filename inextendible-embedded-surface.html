Inextendible embedded surface

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Smooth surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
An embedded smooth surface S⊂R3 is inextendible when every connected smooth surface S⊂R3 containing S is equal to S.
