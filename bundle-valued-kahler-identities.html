Bundle-valued Kähler identities ([Λ,∂ˉE​]=−i(DE′​)∗)

On a Kähler manifold the Chern connection components satisfy [Λ,∂ˉE​]=−i(DE′​)∗ and [Λ,DE′​]=i∂ˉE∗​. Holomorphic normal coordinates and a Chern connection in a normal holomorphic frame reduce them to the scalar identities pointwise. Closedness of the Kähler form also gives [Λ,∂ˉE∗​]=0.
