Double-exchange mechanism

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetism Exchange interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
Double exchange couples localized magnetic moments through actual carrier hopping between sites of different valence. Strong local Hund's coupling makes hopping energetically easier when neighboring core spins align, lowering the carrier kinetic energy. In the classical strong-coupling limit, a common effective hopping is teff​=tcos(θ/2) for relative spin angle θ; aligned cores maximize its magnitude. Unlike insulating superexchange, this mechanism directly connects carrier motion and magnetic alignment.[ref]
