Sierpiński space (S)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological space Kolmogorov space
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
The Sierpiński space is {0,1} with open sets ∅, {1}, and {0,1}. Continuous maps X→S are in bijection with open subsets of X through g↦g−1(1).
