Integral closure in a finite extension of a complete discretely valued field

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Absolute value (algebra) Extension of an absolute value Unique extension of an absolute value to a finite extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
Let K be complete for a discrete valuation and let L/K be finite. The valuation ring OL​ is exactly the integral closure of OK​ in L. One direction follows from a monic integral equation; for the other, the coefficients of the minimal polynomial are elementary symmetric polynomials in conjugates of absolute value at most one.
