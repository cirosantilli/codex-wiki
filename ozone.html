Ozone (O3​)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound
OurBigBook.com Words: 350 Articles: 8
A triatomic form of oxygen, produced photochemically from molecular oxygen. The ozone layer absorbs ultraviolet radiation; ozone can trace atmospheric oxygen without being directly emitted by metabolism.
