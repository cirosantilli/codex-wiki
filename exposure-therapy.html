Exposure therapy

 Home Science Psychology Clinical psychology Cognitive behavioral therapy
OurBigBook.com Words: 279 Articles: 6
Planned contact with appropriate feared cues or situations to support new learning and reduce maladaptive avoidance. Its rationale requires informed, collaborative tasks and attention to the behaviors that could block learning; exposure does not mean deliberately creating genuine danger.
