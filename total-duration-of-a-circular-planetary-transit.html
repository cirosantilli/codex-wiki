Total duration of a circular planetary transit (T14​=πP​arcsin(a/R⋆​)2−b2(1+k)2−b2​​)

The first-to-fourth-contact duration of an exoplanet transit by a spherical opaque planet of radius ratio k=Rp​/R⋆​ on a circular orbit. The transit impact parameter is b=(a/R⋆​)cosi. The formula includes finite planet size and orbital curvature; it reduces to the transit duration of a small planet when k→0 and R⋆​/a≪1. It describes contact geometry, not a uniquely specified light-curve threshold in the presence of limb darkening.
