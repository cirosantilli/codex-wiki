First integral of the quadratic fold-Hopf amplitude flow (F=−u(λ2​+u2/3+v2))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory Codimension-two bifurcation Fold-Hopf bifurcation
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
The amplitude equations u′=−2uv, v′=λ2​+v2+u2 satisfy u′=Fv​, v′=−Fu​, hence preserve the displayed first integral. For λ2​=−a2<0 and u≥0, the centre equilibrium is (a,0) with F=2a3/3, while the two boundary saddle equilibria are (0,±a) with F=0. The level F=0 contains the boundary connection and the arc u2=3(a2−v2) joining the two saddle equilibria. Levels 0<F<2a3/3 form closed periodic orbits. Adding an independent rotating phase turns these closed amplitude trajectories into invariant tori.
