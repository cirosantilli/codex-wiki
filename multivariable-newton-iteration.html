Multivariable Newton iteration (DG(xk​)sk​=−G(xk​),xk+1​=xk​+sk​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Newton root-finding iteration Newton iteration in a Banach space
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 1
The finite-dimensional Newton root-finding iteration for a vector equation G(x)=0. At a point with invertible Jacobian matrix, solve the displayed linear system. For G=∇F, its Jacobian matrix is the Hessian matrix, giving Newton's method in optimization. The update solves the affine first-order approximation G(xk​)+DG(xk​)sk​=0; it can converge to any nondegenerate critical point, including a saddle point.
