Orthogonality of Sturm-Liouville eigenfunctions (∫ab​wym​​yn​dx=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Sturm-Liouville theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
For a regular self-adjoint Sturm-Liouville problem with positive weight w and self-adjoint boundary conditions, eigenfunctions with distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal in the weighted inner product ∫wuv. Integration by parts proves this by equating ∫uLv and ∫Luv.
