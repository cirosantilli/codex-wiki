Monotone circuit complexity

 Home Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Circuit complexity
OurBigBook.com Words: 194 Articles: 4
Monotone circuit complexity studies Boolean circuits built only from AND and OR gates, without negations. Such circuits compute monotone Boolean functions, but some monotone functions require much larger monotone circuits than unrestricted circuits.
