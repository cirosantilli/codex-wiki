Lebesgue decomposition theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Absolute continuity of measures
OurBigBook.com Words: 184 Articles: 3
For sigma-finite measures ν and μ, there is a unique decomposition ν=νac​+νs​ in which νac​≪μ and νs​⊥μ.
