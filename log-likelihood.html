Log-likelihood

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Maximum likelihood estimation Maximum-likelihood estimator
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
The log-likelihood is the logarithm of the likelihood as a function of the model parameter. Its maximizers are the same because the logarithm is strictly increasing.
