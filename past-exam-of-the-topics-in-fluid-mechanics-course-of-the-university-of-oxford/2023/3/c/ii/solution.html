The two external stream velocities impose
F′(+∞)=2Uc​ΔU​,F′(−∞)=−2Uc​ΔU​.​
(69)
Let r=ΔU/Uc​≪1 and seek F=O(r) over ξ=O(1). The nonlinear term FF′′ is then O(r2), so the leading ordinary differential equation is
F′′′+ξF′′=0.
(70)
For G=F′′, this is the first-order linear differential equation G′+ξG=0. Its integrating factor is eξ2/2, giving F′′=Me−ξ2/2. An integration using the error function gives
F′=M2π​​erf(ξ/2​)+E.
(71)
The two far-field boundary conditions require E=0 and M=r/2π​. A second integration uses
∫erf(ξ/2​)dξ=ξerf(ξ/2​)+π2​​e−ξ2/2.
(72)
Consequently
F=Aξerf(ξ/2​)+Be−ξ2/2+D,A=2Uc​ΔU​,B=Aπ2​​=Uc​2π​ΔU​.​
(73)
The relation between A and B cancels the extra ξe−ξ2/2 term when differentiating. It gives the familiar error function mean velocity profile
U=Uc​+2ΔU​erf(ξ/2​)
(74)
to first order in r. The additive constant D=O(r) does not affect this velocity profile at the retained order; its interpretation comes from the transverse velocity.
