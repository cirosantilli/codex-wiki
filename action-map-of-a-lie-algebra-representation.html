Action map of a Lie algebra representation (a:g⊗V→V,x⊗v↦xv)

 ... Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Lie algebra homomorphism Lie algebra representation Tensor product of Lie algebra representations
OurBigBook.com Words: 144 Articles: 1
Give g its Adjoint representation. The representation identity gives a(y⋅(x⊗v))=[y,x]v+xyv=y(xv), so the action map is a Lie algebra representation homomorphism. For a nontrivial simple module it is surjective. If the acting algebra is semisimple and the module is finite dimensional, Weyl complete reducibility theorem splits the surjection and makes V a direct summand.
