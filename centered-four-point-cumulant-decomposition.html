For a normalized Minkowski source functional Z=eiW with vanishing one-point function, Cn​=i1−nδnW/δJn and
G1234​=C1234​+C12​C34​+C13​C24​+C14​C23​.
(4)
Differentiating eiW assigns the four labels to connected blocks. Blocks of size one vanish, leaving one block of four or one of the three pair partitions. Thus G4​=iW4​−∑W2​W2​ in this source convention.
