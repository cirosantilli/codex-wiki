Entropy submodularity for three independent sums

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
For independent finitely supported random variables X,Y,Z in a finite additive group,
H(X+Y+Z)+H(Y)≤H(X+Y)+H(Y+Z).
(495)
This is the data processing inequality for mutual information applied to the Markov chain X→X+Y→X+Y+Z.
