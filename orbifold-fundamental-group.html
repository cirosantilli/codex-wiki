Orbifold fundamental group (π1orb​(B))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Orbifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 436 Articles: 5
The orbifold fundamental group records loops together with their local isotropy relations, so a loop around a cone point of order m has its mth power trivial rather than being trivial itself. For an oriented closed surface of genus g with cone orders m1​,…,mr​, its presentation is ⟨a1​,b1​,…,ag​,bg​,c1​,…,cr​∣cimi​​=1, ∏j​[aj​,bj​]c1​⋯cr​=1⟩. A torsion-free finite-index subgroup of a hyperbolic surface orbifold gives a smooth locally isometric surface cover. The absence of torsion means that the subgroup meets no nonidentity local cone stabilizer.
