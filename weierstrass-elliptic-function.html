Weierstrass elliptic function (℘)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Elliptic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 258 Articles: 7
For a period lattice Λ⊂C, the Weierstrass elliptic function is the normally convergent lattice sum
℘Λ​(z)=z21​+∑ω∈Λ∖{0}​((z−ω)21​−ω21​).
(296)
It is an even elliptic function with a double pole of principal part (z−ω)−2 and zero residue at every point ω of the period lattice.
