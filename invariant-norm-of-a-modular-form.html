Invariant norm of a modular form (yk/2∣f(τ)∣)

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Fourier expansion of a modular form Cusp form
OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 2
For a weight-k modular form on the full modular group, the quantity
(Imτ)k/2∣f(τ)∣
(8)
is modular invariant. For a cusp form it tends to zero at the cusp and is therefore bounded on a fundamental domain.
