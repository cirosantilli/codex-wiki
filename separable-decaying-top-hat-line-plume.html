Separable decaying top-hat line plume (b=αz/2,w=2z/(3τ),g′=2z/(3τ2))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulent plume Top-hat plume model Unsteady top-hat line-plume balances
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
In a homogeneous ambient fluid, the displayed nonsteady power-law ansatz solves the unsteady top-hat line-plume balances for τ=t+t∗​>0. It gives Q=Rαz2/(3τ), M=2Rαz3/(9τ2) and F=2Rαz3/(9τ3). The solution is singular at zero age and carries zero source flux at its unshifted ideal origin. It describes a separable branch away from a source; a finite-width boundary, virtual-origin shift or matching region is needed to supply nonzero physical source flux. It is distinct from the steady constant-flux line plume, whose top-hat half-width grows as αz.
