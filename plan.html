Plan

 Home Computer science Artificial intelligence Automated planning
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
In deterministic classical planning, a finite sequence of actions applicable successively from the initial state and ending in a state satisfying the goal. A STRIPS plan must respect every action's precondition set and both add and delete effects. A numerical bound on a possible plan's cost does not itself specify the action sequence.
