Particle-physics control region

 Home Physics Particle detection
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 1
An event sample designed to constrain a background contribution to a signal region, while having little expected signal contamination. Changed particle-identification, kinematic or tagging requirements can isolate a process. The model must account for its relation to the signal sample, shared uncertainties and any signal leakage. A separate validation sample can test extrapolation without tuning the discovery selection.
