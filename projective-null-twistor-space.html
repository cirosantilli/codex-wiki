Projective null twistor space (PN={[Z]∈CP3:h(Z,Z)=0})

 ... Branches of physics General relativity Twistor theory Twistor space Twistor Hermitian form Null twistor
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The five-real-dimensional space of projective null twistors. A projective line lies entirely in it precisely when its corresponding two-plane is totally isotropic for the twistor Hermitian form. Such lines represent real points of Minkowski conformal compactification. Two independent null twistors lie on one such line exactly when h(X,Y)=0.
