Alfvén wave

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Magnetohydrodynamic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 819 Articles: 15
An Alfvén wave is transverse and incompressible, with displacement perpendicular to both the background magnetic field and the wavevector. Its frequency is ω2=(k⋅vA​)2.
