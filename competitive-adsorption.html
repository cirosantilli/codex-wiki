Competitive adsorption

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Adsorption Adsorption isotherm Langmuir adsorption model
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
Competitive adsorption occurs when different chemical species seek the same adsorption sites. For intact molecules A and B, retain identical adsorption sites and no interactions, but replace the vacant fraction by 1−θA​−θB​. The same equilibrium balance gives
θA​=1+bA​PA​+bB​PB​bA​PA​​,θB​=1+bA​PA​+bB​PB​bB​PB​​.
(17)
Here PA​ and PB​ are partial pressures, not total pressure. Increasing PB​ can reduce θA​ at unchanged PA​: replacing PA​ by total pressure in a single-species Langmuir isotherm loses this competition.
