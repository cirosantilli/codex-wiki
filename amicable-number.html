Amicable number

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Arithmetic function Divisor function
OurBigBook.com Words: 55 Articles: 1
Two distinct positive integers a,b are amicable when the sum of the proper divisors of a is b and the sum of the proper divisors of b is a. The pair 220,284 is an example. Iterating the proper-divisor sum can also produce longer directed cycles.
