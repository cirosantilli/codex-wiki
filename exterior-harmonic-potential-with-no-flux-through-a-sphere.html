Exterior harmonic potential with no flux through a sphere (U=v0​(r+r03​/(2r2))cosθ)

An axisymmetric harmonic potential outside a sphere of radius r0​, with far field v0​rcosθ and zero radial derivative on the sphere, is
U=v0​(r+r03​/(2r2))cosθ.
(52)
The regular spherical expansion in Legendre polynomials fixes all coefficients. A spatial constant remains arbitrary if only the far-field gradient is specified, and is fixed by prescribing the potential difference at infinity.
