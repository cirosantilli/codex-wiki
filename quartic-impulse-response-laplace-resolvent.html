For the whole-line equation At​+a1​Axx​+a2​Axxxx​+a3​A=0 with Rea2​>0, the dispersion relation is s=a1​k2−a2​k4−a3​. The temporal Laplace transform of the impulse response at zero is the real-k integral of [s+a3​−a1​k2+a2​k4]−1/(2π). Factor the denominator as a2​(k2+κ12​)(k2+κ22​) with Reκj​>0 for large Res. Partial fractions give the displayed resolvent. Square-root sheets are chosen by continuation from this causal large-s region; choosing roots independently can introduce false spatial pinch points.
