Lawvere-Tierney topology

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Elementary topos
OurBigBook.com Words: 663 Articles: 15
A Lawvere-Tierney topology on a topos is a morphism j:Ω→Ω satisfying, internally,
j(⊤)=⊤,j(jp)=j(p),j(p∧q)=j(p)∧j(q).
(20)
It is also called a local operator.
