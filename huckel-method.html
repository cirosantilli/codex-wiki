Hückel method

 Home Chemistry Quantum chemistry Molecular orbital theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 523 Articles: 9
A model of conjugated π molecular orbitals with an orthonormal atomic basis, one-centre energies αi​, and resonance matrix elements βij​ between selected neighbouring sites. Solving Hc=Ec gives orbital energies and coefficients. Its simplicity makes symmetry and electron filling explicit, but quantitative charge predictions can depend strongly on the chosen parameters and omitted orbital relaxation.
