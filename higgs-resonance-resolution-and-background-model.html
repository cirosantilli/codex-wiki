Higgs resonance resolution and background model (mγγ2​=2E1​E2​(1−cosα))

The bridge from detector readout to a particle resonance is four-momentum conservation. In units c=1, sum the measured final-state four-momenta:
m2=(∑i​Ei​)2−∣∑i​pi​∣2.
(33)
For two photons separated by angle α, ∣pi​∣=Ei​ gives the displayed diphoton formula. Two back-to-back photons of 60GeV each have mγγ​=120GeV. A four-lepton candidate instead uses four four-momenta in the same sum. Collision debris outside the selected decay is not included. Since the partons carry unknown fractions of the incoming proton momenta, reconstructing the decay products is more informative than assigning the full beam energy to each candidate.
Suppose the intrinsic decay width is negligible compared with the stipulated detector spread. Let the reconstructed signal mass follow a normal distribution with mean m0​=120GeV and standard deviation σm​. For a total expected selected signal S=60 and flat background density b=8 events per 109 electronvolts, the expected count density is
dmdN​=b+2π​σm​S​exp[−2σm2​(m−m0​)2​].
(34)
This is a deliberately chosen response model, not a fit to either experiment. In the fixed window m0​−a<m<m0​+a with a=3GeV, direct integration gives
Bw​=2ab=48,Sw​=Serf(2​σm​a​).
(35)
The expected signal counts are 57.2700 for σm​=1.5GeV and 40.9614 for σm​=3GeV. Doubling the spread lowers the fraction in the same window without changing the total signal. Detector resolution can therefore change the visibility of a peak even with identical particle production.
As a separate counting example, stipulate an observed integer count n=70 and an exactly known background mean Bw​=48. The Poisson distribution gives a likelihood L(s)=e−(48+s)(48+s)70/70! for nonnegative signal mean s. Its maximum-likelihood estimator is s=22, and the likelihood ratio statistic against s=0 is
q0​=−2logL(22)L(0)​=2[70log4870​−22]≃8.82119.
(36)
This tests a specified window with a specified background, and is not either experiment's discovery significance. If the background mean is allowed to vary freely, background alone can choose 70 and fit that count exactly. Control samples, a defensible mass-shape model, nuisance parameters and corrections for searching many masses are therefore essential to the experimental inference.
