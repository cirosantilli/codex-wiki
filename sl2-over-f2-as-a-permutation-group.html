SL2 over F2 as a permutation group (SL2​(F2​)≅S3​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory General linear group over a finite field Special linear group over a finite field
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
A determinant-one matrix over F2​ permutes the three nonzero vectors of F22​. This group action is faithful because fixing two basis vectors forces the matrix to be the identity. There are (4−1)(4−2)=6 invertible matrices, all of determinant one since the only nonzero field element is one, so the action identifies the group with the symmetric group S3​. Reduction of integer determinant-one matrices modulo two is onto: (01​−10​) and (10​11​) induce two distinct transpositions, which generate S3​. Its kernel consists of matrices congruent to the identity modulo two, the level-two principal congruence subgroup.
