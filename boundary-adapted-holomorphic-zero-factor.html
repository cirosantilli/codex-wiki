Boundary-adapted holomorphic zero factor (Ea,w,K​(z))

OurBigBook.com Words: 98
For a proper plane domain D, choose a∈D and w∈/D attaining δ(a)=dist(a,C∖D). The holomorphic function
Ea,w,K​(z)=z−wz−a​exp(∑k=1K​k1​(z−wa−w​)k)
(12)
has exactly one zero in D, a simple zero at a. For ∣z−w∣>2δ(a) its zero-free holomorphic logarithm satisfies
∣logEa,w,K​(z)∣≤∑k>K​k2−k​≤K+12−K​.
(13)
Thus products of such factors can realize prescribed zeros approaching the boundary. If the zero has multiplicity m, choose the logarithmic error smaller by a factor m before taking the mth power. On a compact subset of D, eventual uniform summability of these logarithms gives a nonvanishing product tail and a holomorphic function with precisely the requested zeros.
