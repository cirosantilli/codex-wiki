Inclusion-exclusion principle for probabilities (P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Inclusion-exclusion principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
For a finite family of events, the probability of their union is the alternating sum of their intersection probabilities, with the same pattern as the inclusion-exclusion principle. Expand the indicator function of the union and apply linearity of expectation; this does not require independent events.
