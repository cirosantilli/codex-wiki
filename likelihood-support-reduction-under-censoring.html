Likelihood support reduction under censoring (L(w)=∏i​(∑j​Aij​wj​))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Nonparametric statistics Empirical likelihood Empirical likelihood with mixed censoring
OurBigBook.com Words: 116
Partition the time axis into cells on which membership in each observed exact-event or censoring set is constant. Let wj​ be a cell's probability mass and Aij​ indicate whether cell j is permitted by observation i. The empirical likelihood has the displayed form, with nonnegative masses summing to one. Columns of A that agree can be combined. If column j is componentwise dominated by another column k, moving all mass from j to k cannot reduce any factor, so an unconstrained nonparametric maximum-likelihood estimator can omit column j. The remaining log-likelihood is a concave function of the masses. Extra constraints can prevent a domination move; for a fixed survivor function value, split cells at the constrained time and retain the associated linear mass constraint.
