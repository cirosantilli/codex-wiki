Vertex splitting

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Flow network Maximum flow problem Maximum flow with vertex capacities
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
Replace a vertex v by vin​,vout​ and an arc between them carrying its vertex capacity. Incoming arcs enter vin​ and outgoing arcs leave vout​. A path must cross that internal arc to use the vertex. This converts node-capacitated maximum flow to an ordinary flow network. For undirected mixed-failure cuts, normalize the source side so vout​ inside implies vin​ inside; then each original link contributes at most one crossing orientation.
