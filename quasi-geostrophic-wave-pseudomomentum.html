Quasi-geostrophic wave pseudomomentum (A=q′2​/(2Qy​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Wave activity
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For small quasi-geostrophic approximation waves on a locally uniform basic zonal flow with nonzero meridional potential-vorticity gradient Qy​, this signed quadratic wave activity obeys At​+∇⋅F=q′D​/Qy​, where F is the Eliassen–Palm flux and D is a PV source. Multiply the linear PV equation by q′/Qy​ and use the Taylor identity for quasi-geostrophic flux to obtain it. In a locally uniform conservative wave packet, F=Acg​, so the flux follows the group velocity, with its sign determined by Qy​.
