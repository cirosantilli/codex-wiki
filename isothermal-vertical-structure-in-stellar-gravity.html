Isothermal vertical structure in stellar gravity (ρ(z)=ρ0​e−z2/(2H2),H=cs​/ΩK​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Thin disk Vertical structure of an astrophysical disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 228 Articles: 3
For a thin disk in the harmonic vertical gravitational potential ΩK2​z2/2, hydrostatic equilibrium and p=cs2​ρ with height-independent isothermal sound speed give the displayed Gaussian density. For perturbations with adiabatic index γ, the Brunt–Väisälä frequency is N2=(1−1/γ)ΩK2​z2/H2. An isothermal background can therefore support buoyancy for adiabatic perturbations; isothermal perturbations instead have γ=1 and zero buoyancy frequency.
