Totally geodesic submanifold

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Second fundamental form
OurBigBook.com Words: 73 Articles: 1
For a nondegenerate induced metric tensor, a totally geodesic submanifold has vanishing second fundamental form; equivalently, every ambient geodesic initially tangent to it remains in it. This extends the notion of a totally geodesic hypersurface to arbitrary codimension.
