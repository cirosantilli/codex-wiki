POVM on the occupied support (∑r​Mr†​Mr​=P)

OurBigBook.com Words: 63
If every allowed input density operator has support of a positive operator inside a subspace with orthogonal projector P, completeness need only equal P on that subspace. To define a measurement on the full Hilbert space, one can append M⊥​=I−P when the original operators act only on the subspace. The added outcome has zero probability on supported inputs. The effects Mr†​Mr​ form a POVM; the operators themselves additionally specify state updates.
