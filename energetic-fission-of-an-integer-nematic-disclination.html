Energetic fission of an integer nematic disclination

 ... Order parameter Liquid crystal Nematic liquid crystal Nematic director Nematic disclination Logarithmic energy of a planar nematic disclination
OurBigBook.com Words: 121
In a strictly planar nematic liquid crystal, a charge-one nematic disclination is a winding-two map into the circle RP1. Its nonzero fundamental group class prevents complete unwinding while the surrounding loop stays fixed, but permits splitting into two charge-1/2 defects. For separation r0​≪d≪L, the two near fields contribute πKλ02​(1/4+1/4)log(d/r0​) while their common charge-one far field contributes πKλ02​log(L/d). Compared with the unsplit defect, the change is
−2πKλ02​​log(d/r0​)+finite core-energy difference.
(4)
It is negative at sufficiently large scale separation. Thus integer-defect fission is energetically favorable despite conservation of total topological charge. This leading-log argument proves failure of global energetic stability, not absence of kinetic barriers or pinning. If escape into the third dimension is allowed, an integer winding can also become topologically trivial in RP2.
