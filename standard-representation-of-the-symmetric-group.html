Standard representation of the symmetric group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Permutation representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 123 Articles: 2
This representation is a distinguished example in the representation theory of the symmetric group.
The permutation representation of Sn​ on Cn decomposes as
C(1,…,1)⊕{(x1​,…,xn​):x1​+⋯+xn​=0}.
(56)
The first summand is the trivial representation and the second is the (n−1)-dimensional standard representation.
