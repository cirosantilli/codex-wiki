Boundedness criterion for a bilinear map (∥F(x,y)∥≤C∥x∥∥y∥)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Multilinear map Bilinear map
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A bilinear map between normed vector spaces is jointly continuous exactly when it satisfies the displayed product bound. Continuity at zero gives a bound on a product of balls; rescale each nonzero input separately to obtain the inequality. Conversely, expand the increment as F(x+h,y+k)−F(x,y)=F(h,y)+F(x,k)+F(h,k) and apply the product bound to every term. Thus continuity at zero already implies joint continuity everywhere for a bilinear map.
