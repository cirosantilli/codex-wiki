Sumset representation function (rA+B​(s))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Sumset
OurBigBook.com Words: 99
For finite sets A,B⊆G in an abelian group G, the sumset representation function is
rA+B​(s)=​{(a,b)∈A×B:a+b=s}​,s∈G.
(16)
It counts ordered pairs in the Cartesian product, so (a,b) and (b,a) are different when a=b, even though commutativity makes their sums equal. It is zero outside the sumset, and
∑s∈G​rA+B​(s)=∣A∣∣B∣.
(17)
For A={0,1,2,3}, its values at s=0,1,…,6 are 1,2,3,4,3,2,1. For B={0,1,4,10}, its values at the increasing elements of B+B are 1,2,1,2,2,1,2,2,2,1. Both lists total sixteen, but the first concentrates more ordered pairs on fewer sums. This distinction is what additive energy quantifies.
