Intrinsic permutation symmetry of nonlinear susceptibility

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Nonlinear optics Nonlinear optical susceptibility Nonlinear-susceptibility tensor symmetry
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
Commuting input field factors permit the response kernel to be symmetrized under interchange of input index-frequency pairs. For example χijk(2)​(ωs​;ω1​,ω2​)=χikj(2)​(ωs​;ω2​,ω1​). This does not authorize exchanging indices while keeping unequal frequency arguments in their old slots, nor exchanging the output index without further assumptions.
