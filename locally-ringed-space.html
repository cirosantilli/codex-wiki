Locally ringed space

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 71
A locally ringed space is a ringed space whose stalk OX,x​ is a local ring at every point. Morphisms of locally ringed spaces induce local homomorphisms on stalks.
