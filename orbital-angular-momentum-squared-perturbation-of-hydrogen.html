Orbital-angular-momentum-squared perturbation of hydrogen

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Hydrogen atom Hydrogen energy eigenbasis First excited hydrogen eigenspace
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A perturbation gL2 preserves the hydrogen eigenbasis and shifts ∣n,ℓ,m⟩ by
gℏ2ℓ(ℓ+1).
(10)
In the first excited eigenspace, the 2s state is unshifted and the three 2p states receive the common shift 2gℏ2.
