Strict increase of a Gaussian norm distribution

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process Gaussian measure Gaussian random variable in a Banach space
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
Suppose B is a nonzero separable Banach space, the Cameron-Martin space of a Gaussian random variable in a Banach space is a dense subset of B, and every centered ball of positive radius has positive probability. Then t↦P(∥X∥B​≤t) is strictly increasing on (0,∞). For 0<a<b, choose h in that Cameron-Martin space of a Gaussian random variable in a Banach space with ∥h∥B​=(a+b)/2 and 0<δ<(b−a)/2. The closed ball of radius δ centered at h lies in the annulus a<∥x∥B​<b and has positive probability by the symmetric Gaussian translation lower bound. The nonzero assumption is necessary: on B={0} the distribution function equals one everywhere.
