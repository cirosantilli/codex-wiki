Lorenz number (L=Ke​/(σT))

 ... Thermodynamics Heat Thermal conduction Fourier's law Thermal conductivity Wiedemann-Franz law
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The Lorenz number is the ratio L=Ke​/(σT). The Sommerfeld value L0​=(π2/3)(kB​/e)2≃2.44×10−8WΩK−2 applies under the assumptions of the Wiedemann-Franz law. The ratio must use electronic thermal conductivity if lattice heat flow is appreciable.
