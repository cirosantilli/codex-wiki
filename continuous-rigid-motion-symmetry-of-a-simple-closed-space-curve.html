Continuous rigid-motion symmetry of a simple closed space curve

 ... Differential geometry Frenet-Serret formulas Euclidean motion of Euclidean three-space Proper Euclidean motion of Euclidean three-space Infinitesimal proper Euclidean motion Orbit of a one-parameter proper Euclidean motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A simple closed curve in R3 preserved by a nonconstant smooth family of proper rigid motions is a circle. Differentiating the normalized family at the identity gives a nonzero infinitesimal proper Euclidean motion tangent to the curve. Compactness rules out its line and nonclosed helical orbits, leaving a circular rotation orbit.
