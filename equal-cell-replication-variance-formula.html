Equal-cell replication variance formula (Cov(μ​)=σ2I/m)

OurBigBook.com Words: 81
Suppose a normal linear model gives an unrestricted mean to each cell and has m independent observations per cell, with common error variance σ2. Each fitted cell mean is its sample mean, with variance σ2/m; different fitted cell means are independent. Estimate σ2 by the pooled within-cell residual sum of squares divided by n−q, where q is the number of cells. Thus a cell mean has standard error s/m​, a difference of two cell means has standard error s2/m​, and a two-by-two difference-of-differences has standard error 2s/m​.
