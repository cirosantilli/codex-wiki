Barotropic fluid

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 451 Articles: 9
A barotropic fluid has pressure determined by density alone, P=P(ρ), so pressure and density surfaces coincide in hydrostatic equilibrium.
