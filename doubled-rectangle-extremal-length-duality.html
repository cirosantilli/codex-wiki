Doubled rectangle extremal-length duality (λ(Γ)=2w/h,λ(Γ∗)=2h/w)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Conformal quadrilateral Conformal modulus of a quadrilateral
OurBigBook.com Words: 204
Glue two copies of [0,w]×[0,h] along their left, bottom and right sides. Their unglued top sides form the outer boundary of a disk; the two bottom corners are marked interior points. Let Γ be the loops surrounding both points, and Γ∗ the boundary-to-boundary crosscuts separating them. Then λ(Γ)λ(Γ∗)=4.
Each vertical seam joins a marked point to the outer boundary, so the Jordan curve theorem forces a loop surrounding both points to meet both seams. In the flat conformal metric, its total horizontal travel is therefore at least 2w. Doubled horizontal segments attain this length. Each separating crosscut meets the bottom seam and starts and ends at height h, so its total vertical travel is at least 2h; doubled vertical segments attain it. The total area is 2wh.
For an arbitrary density, integrate the lengths of the doubled horizontal segments over their height. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality yields Lρ​(Γ)2h≤2wAρ​. Doubled vertical segments similarly give Lρ​(Γ∗)2w≤2hAρ​. These upper bounds equal the flat-metric ratios, proving both displayed formulas. Mapping the upper and lower halves of a disc to these rectangles gives the corresponding duality for a simply connected domain with two marked interior points.
