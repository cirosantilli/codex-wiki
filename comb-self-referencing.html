Comb self-referencing

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Laser Optical frequency comb
OurBigBook.com Words: 342 Articles: 1
Comb self-referencing compares two parts of one optical frequency comb. Coherent broadening supplies an octave, a factor of two in frequency. Second-harmonic generation doubles a red tooth; a photodiode detects its beat with an original blue tooth.[ref] Applying the tooth formula gives
2fn​−f2n​=2(nfr​+f0​)−(2nfr​+f0​)=f0​.
(17)
The doubled branch carries 2f0​, while the original high-frequency branch carries f0​: their difference retains exactly one offset.
The displayed difference uses the order doubled tooth minus original tooth. An ordinary electronic frequency counter returns a nonnegative beat magnitude; spectral selection and known electronic shifts must identify whether it represents f0​, fr​−f0​, or a shifted version. Coherent broadening, overlapping photon polarization, spatial alignment and temporal overlap are required. Merely producing a broad patch of colored light does not establish a usable offset signal.
