Dennis-Moré condition (∥(Bk​−H∗​)sk​∥/∥sk​∥→0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Newton's method in optimization Quasi-Newton method
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
For a convergent quasi-Newton method sequence xk​→x∗​ with ∇f(x∗​)=0, nonsingular H∗​=∇2f(x∗​), and continuous Hessian nearby, the displayed condition implies q-superlinear convergence. Here Bk​sk​=−∇f(xk​) and sk​=xk+1​−xk​. The condition requires Hessian accuracy only along the actual steps, rather than convergence of the full matrix approximations. It is associated with John E. Dennis and Jorge Moré.
Indeed, the first-order expansion of the gradient gives ∇f(xk+1​)=−(Bk​−H∗​)sk​+o(∥sk​∥)=o(∥sk​∥). Nonsingularity of H∗​ gives ∥x−x∗​∥≤C∥∇f(x)∥ locally. Consequently ∥ek+1​∥≤εk​(∥ek​∥+∥ek+1​∥) with εk​→0. Rearrangement proves ∥ek+1​∥/∥ek​∥→0. Finite termination is understood separately from ratios with zero denominators.
