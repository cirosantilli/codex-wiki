Lindblad resonance (m(Ω−Ωp​)=±κ)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planetary system dynamics Planetary migration Type I migration
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 2
A rotating gravitational perturbation drives an epicyclic disk response where m(Ω−Ωp​)=±κ. The launched density waves carry angular momentum. For a planet, inner and outer waves exert torques of opposite signs, and their imbalance contributes to migration.
