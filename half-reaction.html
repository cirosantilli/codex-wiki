Half-reaction

 Home Chemistry Electrochemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A half-reaction is a balanced chemical equation for either the oxidation or the reduction part of a redox chemical reaction, explicitly including exchanged Electrons. Match the electron counts before adding the two half-reactions; the net equation then contains no transferred Electrons. A half-reaction is bookkeeping, not necessarily an elementary reaction. See Florida State University, lab background: separate electrode reactions and Daniell cell.
