Confined-plume momentum fold (b=R/2​,Ma​=M)

OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For the pressure-neglecting confined plume with compensating return flow, define specific plume momentum flux M=b2w2 and area fraction s=Q2/(R2M). Then J=M/(1−s) and
(1−2s)M′=MBs​Q​(1−s)2−R22Q​Q′.
(1)
The branch issuing from a pure plume reaches a fold at s=1/2, where plume and ambient have equal areas, opposite velocities and equal specific momentum fluxes. The numerator is nonzero on this branch, so M′ diverges although M,Q,w remain finite. This signals failure of the steady uniform-profile description, not infinite physical velocity.
