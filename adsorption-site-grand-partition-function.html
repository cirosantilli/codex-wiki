Adsorption-site grand partition function (Ξsite​=1+zs​eβ(μ+ϵ))

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Adsorption Adsorption isotherm Langmuir adsorption model
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
An independent site has one empty state and an occupied set with binding energy −ϵ and vibrational partition function zs​. Its occupied probability is θ=zs​eβ(μ+ϵ)/[1+zs​eβ(μ+ϵ)]. The denominator enforces the one-molecule-per-site constraint even though the vapour may obey classical statistics. Multiple occupancy or lateral interactions require a different model.
