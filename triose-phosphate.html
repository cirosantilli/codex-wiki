Triose phosphate

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Carbohydrate
OurBigBook.com Words: 27 Articles: 1
A triose phosphate is a three-carbon sugar carrying a phosphate group. Glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate is one example. The Calvin cycle supplies a net triose phosphate for further carbohydrate synthesis. [ref].
