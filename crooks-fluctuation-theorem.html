Crooks fluctuation theorem (PF​(W)/PR​(−W)=eβ(W−ΔF))

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Stochastic thermodynamics Entropy production Fluctuation theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 1
For a system coupled to a thermal reservoir at fixed temperature T, prepare the forward process in the initial canonical ensemble and the reverse process in the final control parameter's canonical ensemble. Reverse the protocol, assume microscopic reversibility and matching path probability support, and count W as work done on the system. With Helmholtz free energy difference ΔF=Ffinal​−Finitial​ and β=1/(kB​T),
PR​(−W)PF​(W)​=eβ(W−ΔF).
(62)
The final forward distribution need not already be in thermal equilibrium. Its reverse preparation is prescribed equilibrium preparation, rather than the actual forward final distribution used in finite trajectory entropy production. Thus the paired path logarithm β(W−ΔF) is dissipated work in thermal units, not automatically the instantaneous total stochastic entropy production at the end of an arbitrary driven protocol.
