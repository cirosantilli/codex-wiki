Pareto distribution (Pareto(α,β))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Continuous probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
In the shape–minimum parameterization, the density is αβαx−α−11{x>β}​, with α,β>0. The survival function is (β/x)α for x≥β. Large shape concentrates the distribution near its lower bound. This convention is useful for uniform-Pareto conjugacy.
