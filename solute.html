Solute

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry)
OurBigBook.com Words: 100
A solute is a component of a chemical solution distinguished from its solvent, typically present in a smaller proportion. There can be several solutes. A concentration must name both what is counted and the denominator: molar concentration uses amount of substance per final chemical solution volume; a solute-to-solvent mass ratio has another denominator.[ref]
“Added sodium chloride” and “dissolved sodium cations” describe different levels of the same inventory. Dissociation of an ionic compound changes the grouping of the particles; it preserves their chemical elements. A dissolved ion remains an ion, and dissolved sucrose remains molecular unless a separate chemical reaction occurs. Thus the name of the weighed solute need not be the name of every dissolved chemical species.
