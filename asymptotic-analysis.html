Asymptotic analysis

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 114 Articles: 4
Asymptotic analysis compares functions or sequences as their argument approaches a limit, using bounds such as Big O notation and relations such as asymptotic equivalence. It records the leading behavior and the size of errors.
