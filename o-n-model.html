O(N) model

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Renormalization group Wilson-Fisher fixed point
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The O(N) model has an N-component real field and an orthogonal group symmetry. Its standard Landau-Ginzburg interaction is g(ϕ⋅ϕ)2; below the upper critical dimension its continuous transition is governed by an O(N) Wilson-Fisher fixed point.
