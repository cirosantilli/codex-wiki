Negative resistance

 Home Electrical engineering
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A negative ratio of terminal voltage to entering electric current, or a negative differential slope in a more general terminal relation. A linear dependent electrical source can produce a negative Thévenin resistance without any of the network's ordinary resistors having a negative value.
