Cotangent fiber-dilation flow (Ds​(x,p)=(x,esp))

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Exact symplectic manifold Liouville vector field
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The vertical Liouville vector field on a cotangent bundle has complete flow Ds​(x,p)=(x,esp). Its pullbacks scale both the Liouville one-form and the canonical symplectic form by es. As s→−∞, each orbit tends to its base point on the zero section. This contraction proves rigidity of cotangent Liouville-form preservation.
