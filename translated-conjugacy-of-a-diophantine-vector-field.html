Translated conjugacy of a Diophantine vector field (Dχω=ω+w∘χ+a)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier series Cohomological equation on a Diophantine torus
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For a sufficiently small analytic periodic vector field w, a near-identity angular diffeomorphism χ and a constant vector a solve Dχω=ω+w∘χ+a. They satisfy χ−id=O(w), a=O(w); if ⟨w⟩=0, then a=O(w2). For the defect e=Dω​χ−ω−w∘χ−a, set A=Dχ, choose Δa=⟨A−1⟩−1⟨A−1e⟩, and solve Dω​v=A−1(Δa−e) with zero mean. Updating χ by Av leaves a quadratic defect (De)v−[w(χ+Av)−w(χ)−Dw(χ)Av]. The torus small-divisor estimate and geometrically decreasing strip losses yield a convergent analytic iteration.
