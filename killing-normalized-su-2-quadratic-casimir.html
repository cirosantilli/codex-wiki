Killing-normalized SU(2) quadratic Casimir (Cκ​=21​j(j+1)I)

OurBigBook.com Words: 53
With Hermitian angular-momentum generators [Ja​,Jb​]=iϵabc​Jc​, the Killing form is 2δab​ and its inverse contraction is Cκ​=21​∑a​Ja2​. Equivalently the anti-Hermitian basis Ta​=−iJa​ has Killing form −2δab​ and gives the same operator. In the complex highest-weight basis, Cκ​=H2/8+(E+E−+E−E+)/4. It acts on spin j by j(j+1)/2, so it differs in normalization from the usual angular-momentum quadratic Casimir operator.
