Marginal distribution

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Joint probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 64 Articles: 2
A marginal distribution is the distribution of a selected coordinate or subvector of a random vector, obtained from the joint distribution by integrating or summing over the remaining coordinates.
