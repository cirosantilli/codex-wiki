Definable power set (D(X))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 26
The definable power set D(X)=Def(X) consists of the subsets of X definable over the first-order structure (X,∈) by a first-order formula with parameters from X. Satisfaction for a set structure makes this a definable operation in ZF. Iterating it produces the constructible hierarchy and the relative constructible hierarchy; their unions are the constructible universe and the relative constructible universe.

Finite relation closure for set-theoretic coding

Words: 133
Definable power set OurBigBook.com
A useful finite conjunction of closure assertions requires empty set, pairing, set union, set difference, Cartesian products, all finite tuple domains An, and the following operations on their relations: relative complements, intersections, coordinate projections and pullbacks along finite coordinate maps, together with equality and membership restricted to A2. The arities and maps are quantified objects, so these requirements form finitely many first-order formulas, not an infinite schema. In a transitive set, individual finite tuples exist by pairing and union, making the tuple-domain assertions correct externally. Atomic truth relations, Boolean operations and projection then compute the truth table of each coded first-order formula correctly by finite induction. Thus satisfaction for a set structure and the relation B=Def(A) are absolute for shared arguments. Every nonzero limit level of the relative constructible hierarchy is closed under these operations, since each result can be defined at finitely many subsequent stages.

Constructible sets of low rank can appear at later stages (L∩Vω+1​=Lω+1​)

Words: 62
Constructible hierarchy OurBigBook.com
The equality L∩Vα​=Lα​ can fail even at α=ω+1. Inside the constructible universe, Lω​=Vω​ is countable, so its definable power set Lω+1​ is countable. The constructible power set of ω is uncountable there by the Cantor theorem. A real outside Lω+1​ remains a constructible subset of ω, hence belongs to Vω+1​. The cardinal comparison must be made inside L, since its reals can be externally countable.

Absoluteness of constructible levels (LαM​=Lα​)

Words: 106 Articles: 1
Constructible hierarchy OurBigBook.com
For a transitive model M of ZFC and α∈M∩Ord, the internally computed constructible hierarchy agrees with the ambient level: LαM​=Lα​. Satisfaction for a fixed set structure is computed from the same finite formulas and the same domain in both universes. Transfinite recursion then proves agreement at successors and limits. If θ=M∩Ord, it follows that Lθ​⊆M.

Constructible-level absoluteness over ZF (LαM​=Lα​)

Words: 52
Absoluteness of constructible levels OurBigBook.com
If M is a transitive model of ZF, its internally computed Lα​ is the actual Lα​ for each ordinal α∈M. At a successor stage use absoluteness of satisfaction for the same set structure, formula codes and parameters; at a limit stage take the union of the already identical earlier stages. Choice is not required.

Constructible universe (L)

Words: 1k Articles: 16
Constructible hierarchy OurBigBook.com
The constructible universe is the inner model L=⋃α∈Ord​Lα​ obtained by iterating the definable power set. It satisfies ZFC and the Generalized continuum hypothesis.

Shepherdson's wall

Words: 80
Constructible universe OurBigBook.com
The obstruction to proving the consistency of nonconstructibility by a uniform inner model construction is that, inside a universe satisfying V=L, every transitive inner model containing all ordinals has L as its own constructible universe and therefore is the entire ambient universe. The absoluteness of constructible levels proves this by induction over successor definability and limit unions. Thus a construction valid in every model of ZF cannot always produce such an inner model satisfying V=L, and in particular cannot always produce one violating axiom of choice.

Axiom of constructibility (V=L)

Words: 33
Constructible universe OurBigBook.com
Every set belongs to some level of the constructible hierarchy. The assertion is ∀x∃α(x∈Lα​), not that each set is definable without parameters. The constructible universe theorem supplies a relative-consistency model of this assertion from ZFC.

Separation proof in the constructible universe

Words: 57
Constructible universe OurBigBook.com
To separate a subset of a constructible set by a formula interpreted in the constructible universe, apply the reflection theorem for definable hierarchies to the formula and its subformulas. At a sufficiently large reflecting level containing the parameters, the desired subset is definable and belongs to the next constructible hierarchy level. The reflection proof uses least witness stages and ambient Axiom schema of replacement, so does not presuppose Axiom schema of separation within the constructible universe.

ZFC proves that the constructible universe is an inner model of ZFC and satisfies the Generalized continuum hypothesis. Relativizing to L yields the corresponding relative-consistency implication.

Countability of constructible omega-one (NC: ∣ω1L​∣=ℵ0​)

Words: 41
Constructible universe OurBigBook.com
This statement says that the first uncountable ordinal of an inner model L is a countable ordinal in the ambient universe. It can hold together with the Generalized continuum hypothesis: start with constructibility and use a finite-function collapse to countable size on ω1L​. The GCH preservation by a finite-function collapse calculation gives the relative consistency result already from the consistency of ZFC.

Hereditarily countable constructible sets appear below omega-one (Hω1​L​=Lω1​L​)

Words: 49
Constructible universe OurBigBook.com
Inside the constructible universe, every hereditarily countable set appears in a countable level of the constructible hierarchy. Take a countable elementary substructure of a sufficiently large Lη​ containing its transitive closure pointwise. The condensation lemma for the constructible universe identifies the collapse with Lβ​ for countable β, and the collapse fixes the original set. Conversely, a countable-indexed level is countable inside the constructible universe.

Relative constructible universe (L(A))

Words: 742 Articles: 7
Constructible universe OurBigBook.com
For a transitive set X, the relative constructible universe L(X) is the union of the relative constructible hierarchy, starting at L0​(X)=X, applying the definable power set at successors and taking unions at limit ordinals. It is the smallest inner model of ZF containing X and all its members. For an arbitrary set A, take the base X to be the transitive closure of {A}, so that A∈X. Axiom of choice need not hold for an arbitrary base; the construction uses initial parameters rather than a predicate relativization.
Inner models with all reals preserve omega-one (ω1W​=ω1​)
Words: 103
Relative constructible universe OurBigBook.com
An inner model W of ZF containing all ambient subsets of ω computes the same countable ordinals as the ambient universe. Every ambient countably infinite ordinal has a well-order code coded by a subset of ω, hence that code belongs to W; finite ordinals are already shared. The Mostowski collapse theorem inside W recovers the actual ordinal and its countability witness. Conversely any countability witness in W remains one outside. If W also contains the full power set of ω as a set, its Continuum hypothesis would give an ambient bijection from ω1​ onto that same power set. Thus an ambient failure of this well-orderable formulation of the Continuum hypothesis is preserved in such an inner model. No internal axiom of choice is required for this argument.
Relative constructible hierarchy (Lα​(X))
Words: 494 Articles: 4
Relative constructible universe OurBigBook.com
For a transitive set X, use L0​(X)=X, Lα+1​(X)=Def(Lα​(X)) and unions at nonzero limit ordinals. The successor operation is the definable power set. Each level is transitive and the levels increase; moreover X∈L1​(X). Their union is L(X), an inner model of ZF. The initial-set convention differs from a hierarchy using a predicate, or one starting at X∪{X}. Axiom of choice need not hold for an arbitrary base without an internally available well-order.
Relative condensation lemma
Words: 77
Relative constructible hierarchy OurBigBook.com
If α is a nonzero limit ordinal and Y≺Lα​(X) with X∪{X}⊆Y, the Mostowski collapse theorem sends Y to Lβ​(X) for some nonzero limit β≤α and fixes X pointwise. The base is fixed by transitivity and inclusion of all its members. Transfer the relative constructible level recognition formula first by elementarity and then through the collapse isomorphism to identify its image. If ρ=X∩Ord, ordinal heights are ρ+α and ρ+β, giving the bound. Having only X∈Y does not guarantee this base-fixing version.
Relative constructible level recognition (M⊨Φ(X) ⟺ M=Lα​(X))
Words: 103
Relative constructible hierarchy OurBigBook.com
For transitive sets M,X with X∈M, a single first-order formula Φ(X) recognises the nonzero limit levels Lα​(X). Require finite relation closure for set-theoretic coding, transitivity of X, that every element belongs to a coded relative constructible stage, and that every represented stage index has a larger represented index. Correct history codes have downward-closed indices with no maximum, hence form a nonzero limit ordinal α. The exhaustion assertion and transitivity then give M=⋃ξ<α​Lξ​(X). Conversely every limit level has the required closure, correct histories cofinal in its index, and exhaustion. This formulation does not assume ZF for an arbitrary input M and does not replace finite coding closure with an unjustified internal constructibility assertion.
Coded relative constructible stage (SX​(ξ,B))
Words: 247 Articles: 1
Relative constructible hierarchy OurBigBook.com
This first-order formula asserts the existence of a history function on ξ+1 starting at X, applying definable power set at successors and unions at nonzero limit ordinals, and ending at B. In a transitive set satisfying finite relation closure for set-theoretic coding, each history is correct by transfinite induction, so B=Lξ​(X). Relation operations produce restrictions to shorter histories. Since stage histories appear below every limit constructible level, every history of length ξ+1 for ξ<α belongs to Lα​(X) when α is a nonzero limit ordinal. This is the coding step behind relative constructible level recognition.
Stage histories appear below every limit constructible level (fξ​∈Lα​(X)(ξ<α, α a nonzero limit))
Words: 175
Coded relative constructible stage OurBigBook.com
For a transitive set X, let fξ​ be the history function η↦Lη​(X) on ξ+1 in the relative constructible hierarchy. By transfinite induction, fξ​∈Lξ+k​(X) for some finite k, possibly depending on ξ. The base and successor steps follow by forming finite ordered pairs and adjoining the next entry, using Lξ+1​(X)∈Lξ+2​(X). These operations require finitely many subsequent definable power sets.
At a nonzero limit ordinal λ, the inductive bounds put every earlier fη​ in Lλ​(X), since η+k<λ. This level satisfies finite relation closure for set-theoretic coding, independently of the availability of history functions. Consequently its coded relative constructible stage predicate computes the endpoints correctly. Finite pairing closure and the earlier histories show that
h={⟨η,B⟩∈Lλ​(X):(Lλ​(X),∈)⊨SX​(η,B)}
(3)
contains exactly the pairs ⟨η,Lη​(X)⟩ for η<λ. There are no extra indices: if η≥λ and B=Lη​(X)∈Lλ​(X), increasing levels would give B∈B, contradicting Axiom of foundation. Thus h=fλ​↾λ is a definable subset of Lλ​(X) and belongs to Lλ+1​(X). Extracting its domain λ and adjoining ⟨λ,Lλ​(X)⟩ uses finitely many further operations, proving the induction step. Every nonzero limit α therefore contains all fξ​ with ξ<α, as required by relative constructible level recognition.
Relative constructible universe can violate the continuum hypothesis
Words: 69
Relative constructible universe OurBigBook.com
Assume a transitive model M satisfies 2ℵ0​=ℵ2​. In M, encode a bijection e:ω2​→P(ω) by one set A⊆ω2​, using a fixed pairing of ω2​×ω with ω2​. Then L(A) decodes e and contains every real of M. The two models consequently have the same ω1​, and any bijection between ω1​ and the real numbers in L(A) would also be one in M. Thus L(A)⊨¬CH.

Condensation sentence for the constructible hierarchy

Words: 48
Constructible hierarchy OurBigBook.com
A condensation sentence is a fixed first-order sentence σ such that every transitive set satisfying σ is Lλ​ for a limit ordinal λ. It combines a sufficiently strong finite fragment of set theory, the assertion that every set is constructible, and the absence of a largest ordinal.

Well-order code

Words: 21
Constructible hierarchy OurBigBook.com
A well-order code is a binary relation R⊆ω×ω for which (ω,R) is a well-order. Its representation is the unique countable ordinal isomorphic to (ω,R).

Coding level of the constructible hierarchy

Words: 33
Constructible hierarchy OurBigBook.com
A level Lλ​ is a coding level when λ is a limit ordinal, the representation of every well-order code in Lλ​ is below λ, and every ordinal below λ has a well-order code in Lλ​.

Condensation lemma for the constructible universe

Words: 38
Constructible hierarchy OurBigBook.com
If X is an elementary substructure of a sufficiently large level Lθ​, then the transitive collapse of X is a level Lβ​. In particular, a countable X collapses to a level indexed by a countable ordinal.
