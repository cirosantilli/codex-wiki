Exponential martingale of a random walk (eθSn​−nκ(θ))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Random walk
OurBigBook.com Words: 291 Articles: 3
For a random walk with independent and identically distributed random variables as increments and finite moment-generating function at θ, put κ(θ)=logEeθX1​. Then eθSn​−nκ(θ) is a martingale by conditioning on the next independent increment. In particular, if r>0 and ErX1​=1, then rSn​ is a martingale. Stopping at a finite interval exit can make this process bounded, allowing the dominated convergence theorem to justify passage from bounded stopping times to the exit time.
