The dilution in the Bradford assay is 1000/5=200. Read the fitted line rather than assuming it passes through the origin: the two absorbances correspond approximately to 4.3 and 15μgmL−1 in the assay mixture. Therefore
cTEM1​cTEM52​​≃200(4.3)=860μgmL−1=0.86mgmL−1,≃200(15)=3000μgmL−1=3.0mgmL−1.​

Multiplying by the 0.500mL eluate volume gives about 0.43mg of TEM-1 and 1.5mg of TEM-52. Only TEM-52 meets the requirement.
Assuming the same recovery per culture volume, the required volumes are
Vculture,1​≃500.431​≃116mL,Vculture,52​≃501.51​≃33mL.
(6)
Use approximately 120mL or more for TEM-1; the existing 50mL is sufficient for TEM-52. Rounding the former up to 125mL is sensible given graphical precision. Reading the lower standard as 4–4.5μgmL−1 gives a corresponding requirement of roughly 110–125mL, not an exact integer volume. These estimates assume comparable dye response to bovine serum albumin, suitable buffer blanks and that recovered protein is the purified target; contaminants would inflate the inferred target yield.
