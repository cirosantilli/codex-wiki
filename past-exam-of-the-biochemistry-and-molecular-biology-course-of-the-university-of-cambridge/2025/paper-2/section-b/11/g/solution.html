The displayed separation between the two entrance landmarks increases from approximately 6.2 to 7.7A˚ in TEM-52, indicating a wider opening. The Gly238-to-Ser substitution can alter backbone packing and loop geometry; its effect is not simply the steric size of the new side chain. Increased accessibility or altered productive positioning could increase the effective association rate for penicillin G, producing a lower apparent Michaelis constant without changing the limiting catalytic step. In a simple Michaelis-Menten kinetics scheme, Km​=(k−1​+kcat​)/k1​, so increasing k1​ can lower Km​.
The structural change is consistent with improved access and the lower measured Km​, while similar Vmax​ suggests similar limiting turnover under these conditions. It does not uniquely establish the mechanism or prove tighter equilibrium binding. TEM-52 contains additional substitutions, so a matched single-substitution series would be needed to isolate the contribution of residue 238. Structural work on TEM-52 independently supports the importance of remodeling access to its active site.[ref]
