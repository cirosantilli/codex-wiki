Newtonian fluid

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 289 Articles: 10
A Newtonian fluid has viscous stress proportional to the local rate-of-strain tensor, with coefficients independent of that rate. For an isotropic fluid these coefficients are viscosities. Its Newtonian fluid stress tensor specifies the corresponding constitutive relation; the material and its stress tensor are distinct concepts.
