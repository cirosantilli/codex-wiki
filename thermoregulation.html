Thermoregulation

 Home Biology Physiology Homeostasis
OurBigBook.com Words: 345 Articles: 10
Thermoregulation regulates body temperature. Skin signals can anticipate a change of core body temperature, so a response need not wait for an internal error. [ref]
These responses have costs and environmental limits. Shivering consumes metabolic resources; cooling through sweating removes water that must eventually be replaced. High humidity restricts evaporation, so secreted sweat and successful cooling are not interchangeable. Maintaining core body temperature also depends on maintaining circulation: depleted blood volume can compromise skin blood flow and heat transfer. Seeking shade or reducing work changes the disturbance itself, whereas a larger regulatory command merely attempts to counter it. [ref]
