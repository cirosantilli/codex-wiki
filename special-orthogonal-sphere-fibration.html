Special orthogonal sphere fibration (SO(s)→SO(s+1)→Ss)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Orthogonal group Special orthogonal group
OurBigBook.com Words: 108 Articles: 1
The last-column map p(A)=Aes+1​ has fiber SO(s), included as diag(B,1). Local completion of a unit vector by the Gram-Schmidt process gives local fiber bundle charts. Consequently it has the homotopy lifting property and the long exact sequence of homotopy groups of a fibration.
