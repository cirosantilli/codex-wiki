Effective operator (Ci​Oi​/MΔi​−4)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Effective field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
An effective operator is a local interaction built from the retained fields in an effective field theory. Its coefficient records effects of heavier degrees of freedom, and allowed operators respect the relevant symmetries. In four dimensions an operator of mass dimension Δi​ has the displayed coefficient for dimensionless Ci​ and a heavy mass scale M. For example a dimension-six proton-decay operator has a coefficient proportional to M−2.
