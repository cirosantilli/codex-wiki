Category of coalgebras for a comonad (EG)

 ... Category theory Category Functor Endofunctor Comonad Coalgebra for a comonad
OurBigBook.com Words: 266 Articles: 4
The category EG consists of coalgebras for a comonad and their structure-preserving morphisms. Its forgetful functor has the cofree coalgebra as right adjoint. If E is a topos and G preserves finite limits, those limits are created by the forgetful functor, while exponentials in a coalgebra topos and the subobject classifier of a coalgebra topos can be constructed as equalizers inside cofree objects.
