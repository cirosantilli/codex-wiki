Radiative-zero disk surface

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Accretion disk Alpha disk Homologous vertical structure of a constant-opacity alpha disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
For a constant-opacity, gas-pressure-supported radiative disk surface at z=H, imposing p=T=0 does not impose F=0. If the outgoing surface flux is finite and positive, dividing hydrostatic balance by the temperature gradient gives dp/dT∼16σΩ2HT3/(3κFs​). Consequently p∝T4, ρ∝T3, and the diffusion equation gives T∝H−z. Thus the mass density also vanishes regularly while the surface radiates a finite flux. This ideal boundary replaces the finite-temperature photospheric transition; its accuracy is not controlled solely by the disk aspect ratio.
