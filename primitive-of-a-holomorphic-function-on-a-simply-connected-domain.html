Primitive of a holomorphic function on a simply connected domain

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Simply connected domain
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
Every holomorphic function f on a simply connected domain has a single-valued antiderivative. Fixing z0​ and setting
F(z)=∫z0​z​f(t)dt
(67)
gives a path-independent function with F′=f, because the Cauchy integral theorem makes the integral around every closed path zero.
