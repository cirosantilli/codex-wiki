Superconducting transition temperature (Tc​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Superconductivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 1
The superconducting transition temperature Tc​ is the temperature at which the material enters superconductivity on cooling in zero applied magnetic field and with negligible measuring electric current. A measured transition at nonzero magnetic field or appreciable electric current need not occur at this zero-field Tc​. If a resistance-versus-temperature experiment for superconductivity gives a transition spread over several temperatures, report whether its quoted value is the onset, midpoint, or disappearance of detectable electrical resistance, together with the experimental conditions. This operational distinction matters when comparing samples. [ref]
