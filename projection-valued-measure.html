Projection-valued measure (E)

 ... Riesz representation theorem Adjoint operator Normal operator Spectral theorem Spectral theorem for normal operators Spectral theorem for normal operators on a separable Hilbert space
OurBigBook.com Words: 287 Articles: 5
A projection-valued measure E assigns an orthogonal projection E(B) to each measurable set, with E(∅)=0, E(X)=I, E(B∩C)=E(B)E(C), and countable additivity in the strong operator topology on pairwise disjoint sets.
