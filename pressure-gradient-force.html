Pressure gradient force (f=−∇p)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Fluid pressure Pressure gradient
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
A spatially varying pressure exerts net force per unit volume −∇p on a small fluid element. Its contribution to acceleration is −∇p/ρ. Pressure increases in the direction opposite to this force.
