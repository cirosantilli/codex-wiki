Binomial thinning (Y∣N∼Bin(N,r))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Random variable Random sum
OurBigBook.com Words: 114 Articles: 1
Retain each of N objects independently with probability r, independently of the nonnegative integer-valued random variable N. The retained count Y has the displayed conditional binomial distribution. The probability generating function of an independent random sum gives GY​(s)=GN​(1−r+rs). If N has finite variance, the law of total expectation and law of total variance give
EY=rEN,Var(Y)=r(1−r)EN+r2Var(N).
(2)
The first term is the average conditional variance of the independent retention decisions; the second is the variance in their conditional expected value.
