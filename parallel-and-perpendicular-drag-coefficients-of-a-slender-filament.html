Parallel and perpendicular drag coefficients of a slender filament (ξ∥​, ξ⊥​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biological fluid dynamics Resistive-force theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
The local resistive-force theory coefficients ξ∥​ and ξ⊥​ describe drag along and across a filament tangent. For very slender bodies their leading logarithmic values have ratio ξ⊥​/ξ∥​≃2. Geometry, ends and nonlocal fluid interactions affect the corrections. Anisotropy enables propulsion by rotating helices.
