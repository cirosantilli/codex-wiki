Choose a cycle Γ surrounding σ(x) and a cycle Δ surrounding f(σ(x)), with f(Γ) inside the region of winding number one for Δ. The algebra-homomorphism property gives

Substitute this into the contour formula for g(f(x)), interchange integrals, and apply the Cauchy integral formula to the inner ζ integral. This proves composition as an equality of algebra elements, not merely an equality on characters, which need not separate a nonsemisimple algebra.
