Lapse rate (atmosphere, Γ)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The atmospheric lapse rate is the decrease of temperature with altitude, Γ=−dT/dz. In an ideal gas with constant gravity, a constant lapse rate produces a power-law relation between temperature and pressure.
