Good-solvent size calibration for polystyrene (Rg​/nm≃0.0123[M/(gmol−1)]0.593)

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polystyrene
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
The radius of gyration of a dilute linear polystyrene coil in a good solvent depends on its molar mass as Rg​=KMν, with a material- and solvent-dependent amplitude. A manufacturer-supplied light-scattering calibration for polystyrene in benzene or toluene near room temperature is the displayed relation, quoted over M≃2×105 to 5×107gmol−1. The exponent is close to the Flory approximation 3/5. Values outside the quoted mass range are extrapolations, not extra measured data. Using c∗=M/[NA​(4π/3)Rg3​] then gives the polymer overlap concentration scaling c∗∝M1−3ν. [ref]
