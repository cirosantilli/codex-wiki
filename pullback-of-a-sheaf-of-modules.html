Pullback of a sheaf of modules (f∗E)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Inverse image sheaf
OurBigBook.com Words: 185 Articles: 3
For a morphism of ringed spaces, the pullback of an OY​-module is
f∗E=OX​⊗f−1OY​​f−1E.
(2)
The inverse image functor supplies the sheaf f−1E; tensoring with the source structure sheaf supplies the module scalar extension.
