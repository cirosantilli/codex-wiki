Orientation averaging of an axisymmetric stresslet (⟨S(ee−I/3)⟩=−2S​(nn−I/3))

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Microswimmer Force-dipole flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 172 Articles: 2
The angular factor of an axial stresslet is linear in the symmetric traceless rank-two tensor ee−I/3. Hence averaging a collection of directors amounts to averaging this tensor. For uniformly distributed in-plane directions, ⟨ee⟩=(I−nn)/2, so ⟨S(ee−I/3)⟩=−(S/2)(nn−I/3). The averaged director is normal to the plane and its signed strength is reversed and halved.
