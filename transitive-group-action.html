Transitive group action

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group action
OurBigBook.com Words: 792 Articles: 14
A group action on a set X is transitive when every two points lie in the same orbit of a group action, equivalently when for all x,y∈X there is a group element g with gx=y.
