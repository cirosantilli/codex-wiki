Modified profile likelihood (ℓm​=ℓp​−21​log∣jλλ​∣−log∣∂λψ​/∂λ∣)

 ... Statistical model Statistical modelling Maximum likelihood estimation Maximum-likelihood estimator Likelihood function Profile likelihood
OurBigBook.com Words: 191 Articles: 2
For parameter of interest ψ, constrained nuisance fit λψ​, and suitable ancillary sample coordinates (ψ​,λ,a), this adjustment includes both nuisance observed information and a sample-space Jacobian determinant. The derivative holds ψ​,a fixed. Implicit differentiation of the constrained nuisance score function gives the equivalent expression ℓp​+21​log∣jλλ​∣−log∣ℓλ;λ​∣. The semicolon distinguishes data-coordinate differentiation from parameter differentiation. Omitting the Jacobian requires an additional justified simplification; the information adjustment alone is not universal.
