Intron

 Home Biology RNA splicing
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 4
A segment represented in a precursor RNA that is removed by RNA splicing; the corresponding segment of a gene is also called an intron. An intron can interrupt ribosomal RNA as well as messenger RNA.
