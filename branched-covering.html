Branched covering

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Covering space
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 1
A branched covering is a map that is a covering space away from a branch set and has a specified local covering model at that set. For a smooth two-fold covering branched over a codimension-two submanifold, the local model is (z,u)↦(z2,u). A two-fold branched cover of a knot is the basic three-dimensional example.
