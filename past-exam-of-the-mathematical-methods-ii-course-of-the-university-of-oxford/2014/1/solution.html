Choose the hemisphere's outward normal vector; its boundary circle is then traversed counterclockwise viewed from +z. Parametrize it by r=(2cost,2sint,0), 0≤t≤2π. On this curve, F=(8sint,2cost,0) and dr=(−2sint,2cost,0)dt. Therefore
