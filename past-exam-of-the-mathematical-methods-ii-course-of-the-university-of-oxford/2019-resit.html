A conservative vector field can be written as F=∇Φ for a single-valued scalar potential Φ. Its line integral between two points depends only on the endpoints, and its integral around every closed curve is zero. A necessary local test for a smooth planar field F=(P,Q) is Py​=Qx​. On a simply connected domain such as the whole plane, this is also sufficient.
Here
Py​=(3x2+x3y)exy=Qx​.
(14)
An explicit scalar potential is even more decisive:
Φ(x,y)=x2exy+y2,F=∇Φ.​
(15)
Indeed Φx​=2xexy+x2yexy and Φy​=x3exy+2y. Thus the field is conservative on R2.
An exact differential is a differential one-form equal to dΦ on its domain; an inexact differential is not the differential of a single-valued potential there. For example, d(xy)=ydx+xdy is exact. For the supplied form η=42ydx+xdy, the cross derivatives are
∂y∂(42y)​=42,∂x∂x​=1.
(16)
They disagree, so η is not even closed and hence cannot be exact. Equivalently, its counterclockwise line integral around the unit square is 1−42=−41=0. Therefore
42ydx+xdy is inexact.​
(17)
The connection is F⋅dr=dΦ: a vector field is conservative precisely when its work differential one-form is exact. Locally vanishing curl alone need not guarantee global exactness on a domain with holes.

For opposite propagation directions on a uniform string, write the equal-frequency transverse waves as
y1​=Acos(kx−ωt+α),y2​=Acos(kx+ωt+β).
(56)
The cosine addition identity gives
y1​+y2​=2Acos(kx+2α+β​)cos(ωt+2β−α​).​
(57)
This is a standing wave: the spatial pattern is fixed while its amplitude oscillates in time. Nodes occur at the zeros of the first cosine and are separated by π/k, half a wavelength; antinodes have displacement amplitude 2A. The two equal opposite travelling waves carry equal opposite average energy flux, so the net average transported power is zero.
For the same propagation direction, the same identity instead gives
Acos(kx−ωt+α)+Acos(kx−ωt+β)=2Acos2α−β​cos(kx−ωt+2α+β​).​
(58)
For a nonzero resultant this is another travelling wave, with fixed amplitude and moving constant-phase points, not a nontrivial standing wave. If the prefactor vanishes, the waves cancel identically; the zero disturbance is a trivial exception rather than a standing oscillation. The argument assumes propagating waves with k,ω>0 on the same uniform string.

Continuity checks the identity t=1+r. Setting M=0 recovers solution, while M→∞ at fixed nonzero frequency gives r→−1 and t→0, the fixed-boundary limit.
The signs of the imaginary terms depend on the time convention. With e−iωt and conjugate spatial conventions, both coefficients are complex conjugated; the source's displayed signs agree with the convention used here.
There is no damping in the point mass, so its cycle-averaged absorbed power is zero. Writing D=T2(k1​+k2​)2+M2ω4, the power fractions are
R=DT2(k1​−k2​)2+M2ω4​,T=D4T2k1​k2​​,R+T=1.
(65)
This provides an independent conservation check on both the numerator signs and the transmission prefactor.
