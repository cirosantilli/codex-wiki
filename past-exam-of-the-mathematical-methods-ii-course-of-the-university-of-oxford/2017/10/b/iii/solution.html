At the return encounter, transmission from light to heavy multiplies the amplitude by 2/3, giving a backward heavy-string pulse of amplitude −8Ai​/9. Its speed halves, so its width returns to w. Reflection within the light segment multiplies the incoming amplitude by −1/3, giving a right-going pulse of amplitude 4Ai​/9 and width 2w. The earlier Ai​/3 reflected pulse is already farther along the heavy string.
The sketches show these as separate pulses in the short-pulse regime w/20≪0.10s. For longer pulses the corresponding components overlap and must be superposed rather than interpreted as isolated visible humps.
