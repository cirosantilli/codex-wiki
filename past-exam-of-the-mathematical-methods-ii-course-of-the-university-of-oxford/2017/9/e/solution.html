For a central displacement in three freely ended coupled pendulums, the initial vector decomposes as (0,ϵ,0)=(ϵ/3)(1,1,1)−(ϵ/3)(1,−2,1). The antisymmetric second mode is absent. Hence
x1​=x3​=3ϵ​(cosω1​t−cosω3​t),x2​=3ϵ​(cosω1​t+2cosω3​t).​
(20)
Let ωˉ=(ω3​+ω1​)/2 and δ=(ω3​−ω1​)/2. The outer displacement is (2ϵ/3)sinωˉtsinδt, explicitly exhibiting an amplitude modulation. The middle displacement is ϵcosωˉtcosδt−(ϵ/3)sinωˉtsinδt, whose modulation envelope is
3∣ϵ∣​5+4cos[(ω3​−ω1​)t]​.
(21)
It ranges from ∣ϵ∣/3 to ∣ϵ∣, so it does not vanish completely. These are beats between the two excited frequencies. A slow envelope well separated from the carrier requires k/m≪g/l; in that limit ω3​−ω1​≃3k/(2mω1​). The exact two-mode formulas remain valid without that additional weak-coupling assumption.
