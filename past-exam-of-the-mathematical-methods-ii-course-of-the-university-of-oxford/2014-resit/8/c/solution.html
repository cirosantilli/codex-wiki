A symmetric quadratic form has twice its off-diagonal entry as the coefficient of the mixed product. Comparing with the preceding potential energy and kinetic energy expressions gives
V=mgl(40​01​),T=ml2(82​21​).​
(22)
Here V is the stiffness matrix and T the mass matrix in angular coordinates; their units differ accordingly. The dimensionless mass-matrix determinant is 8−4=4>0 and its first diagonal entry is positive, so the kinetic energy is positive for every nonzero angular velocity.
