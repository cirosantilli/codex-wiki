The directional error of a first-order Taylor approximation is determined by the omitted terms, not just the equal first partial derivatives. The Hessian matrix at the expansion point is e(42​22​), so
f−e−2​e(h+k)=e(2h2+2hk+k2)+O((h2+k2)3/2).

For equal small steps along the coordinate axes, the leading error is 2eh2 along x and ek2 along y. The error grows faster along the x axis, although the first-order change in the function is equal along the two axes.
