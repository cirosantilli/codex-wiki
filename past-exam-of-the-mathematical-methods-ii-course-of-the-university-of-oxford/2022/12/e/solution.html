Assume V0​=0. Linear independence of the Fourier sine basis makes a flat string equivalent to vanishing of all three temporal coefficients. The fundamental requires sin(ω1​t)=0, which automatically zeros the other two. Thus the earliest positive return is
tflat​=ω1​π​=cL​.​
(32)
The velocity need not have returned to its initial value at that time; full recurrence of the displacement-and-velocity data occurs at 2L/c.
A nonzero pure fundamental profile would require sin2ω1​t=sin3ω1​t=0. The common zeros have ω1​t an integer multiple of π, so the fundamental vanishes too. A pure second or third harmonic would already require the fundamental to vanish, again making the whole string flat. The string never has a nonzero pure sine profile. If V0​=0, it remains identically zero and there is no earliest positive isolated return.
