A normal mode of a stable undamped linear coupled oscillator has a single angular frequency and a fixed ratio of component displacements. All components share a sinusoidal time factor, with relative oscillation phases of zero or π for real mode shapes. A general free motion is a superposition of the normal modes. For this system the symmetric stiffness matrix gives real eigenvalues and orthogonal eigenvectors; positive spring constants make the squared angular frequencies positive.
