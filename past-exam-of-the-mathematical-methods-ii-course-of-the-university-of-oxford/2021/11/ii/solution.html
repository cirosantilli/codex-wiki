Take constant complex amplitude A and real wavenumber k and angular frequency ω. Substitution of the complex exponential gives
yt​=−iωy,yxx​=−k2y,im(−iω)y=k2y.
(51)
Thus every nonzero plane wave of the given form solves the equation precisely when
mω=k2.​
(52)
The wavenumber specifies spatial wave phase change per unit length, with wavelength λ=2π/∣k∣. The angular frequency specifies temporal wave phase change per unit time, with period 2π/∣ω∣ and ordinary frequency ∣ω∣/(2π). The constant m in this equation has dimensions time divided by length squared; without an additional dimensional coefficient, it is not an ordinary physical mass.
For propagating oscillatory solutions assume real m=0. If m=0, the equation becomes yxx​=0 and only k=0 among these spatial plane waves is possible; the usual dispersion relation branch and velocity discussion would degenerate.
