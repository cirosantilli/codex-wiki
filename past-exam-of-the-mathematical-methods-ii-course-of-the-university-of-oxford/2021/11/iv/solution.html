For equal complex amplitudes, the superposition principle and the complex exponential formula for the cosine give the exact identity
y​=Aei[(k0​+δk)x−(ω0​+δω)t]+Aei[(k0​−δk)x−(ω0​−δω)t]=2Acos(δkx−δωt)ei(k0​x−ω0​t)​.​
(54)
The cosine is a real periodic modulation of the rapidly varying carrier. It varies slowly when ∣δk∣≪∣k0​∣ and ∣δω∣≪∣ω0​∣. Its signed wave envelope travels with velocity δω/δk; its magnitude gives the visible amplitude envelope.
Equal magnitudes need not mean equal initial wave phases. If the two complex amplitudes are aeiϕ+​ and aeiϕ−​, write ϕˉ​=(ϕ+​+ϕ−​)/2 and δϕ=(ϕ+​−ϕ−​)/2. The same result becomes 2aeiϕˉ​cos(δkx−δωt+δϕ)ei(k0​x−ω0​t), still with a real slowly varying modulation.
