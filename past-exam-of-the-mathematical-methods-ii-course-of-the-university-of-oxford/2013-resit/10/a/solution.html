For a separated solution y=X(x)Q(t), the wave equation gives X′′/X=Q¨​/(v2Q). Choose the separation constant −k2. Then
X=Acoskx+Bsinkx,Q=Ccos(vkt)+Dsin(vkt).
(28)
These are the oscillatory separated building blocks. Since no endpoint conditions have yet been imposed, the general solution is a superposition over the allowed continuum of k, rather than one product with four constants. Equivalently, combining the left- and right-travelling separated components gives the D'Alembert's formula
y(x,t)=F(x−vt)+G(x+vt),​
(29)
with arbitrary twice differentiable functions F,G on the relevant characteristic intervals. Indeed, in characteristic coordinates ξ=x−vt, η=x+vt, the equation becomes yξη​=0, whose general solution is exactly this sum. This also includes zero-frequency and polynomial limits that would be omitted by restricting to one nonzero-k sine mode.
