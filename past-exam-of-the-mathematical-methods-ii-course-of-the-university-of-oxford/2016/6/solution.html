Construct a global vector-field potential rather than relying only on vanishing curl:
ϕ(x,y,z)=x2z+2y2​,∇ϕ=(2xz,y,x2)=F.
(10)
Thus F is a conservative vector field. The endpoints of the given parametric curve are r(0)=(3,0,0) and r(π/2)=(0,2,1). The fundamental theorem for line integrals gives
∫C​F⋅dr=ϕ(0,2,1)−ϕ(3,0,0)=2.​
(11)
The intervening turns of the curve have no effect because this line integral is path independent.
