Apply the product-to-sum formula 2cosucosv=cos(u+v)+cos(u−v) to the amplitude modulation:
A=acos(ωt−kx)+2b​cos((ω+ωm​)t−kx)+2b​cos((ω−ωm​)t−kx).​
(7)
The amplitude modulation sidebands have angular frequencies ω+ωm​ and ω−ωm​, each with amplitude b/2 in this real cosine convention. The carrier wave has angular frequency ω and amplitude a.
This identity is exact for the supplied space-time expression. A physical qualification is needed if every term is meant to be a freely propagating electromagnetic wave with speed of light c: the dispersion relation requires k±​=(ω±ωm​)/c, whereas the prescribed modulation at every position leaves all three wavenumbers equal to k=ω/c. A freely propagating modulated wave instead has envelope a+bcos(ωm​(t−x/c)); its analogous expansion has k±​. For example, at x=t=0 the prescribed expression has Att​−c2Axx​=−bωm2​, which is nonzero for nontrivial modulation. The given formula can describe modulation imposed throughout space or the temporal signal at one position.
