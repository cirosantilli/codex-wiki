The divergence theorem states that, for a sufficiently smooth vector field F on a volume Ω with piecewise smooth closed boundary ∂Ω,
∬​∂Ω​F⋅n^dS=∭Ω​∇⋅FdV​.
(16)
Here n^ is the outward unit normal. The left-hand side is the total outward flux; the right-hand side adds the local divergence throughout the volume. The orientation and the requirement to include the complete closed boundary are essential.
