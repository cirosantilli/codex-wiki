Use sinθcosθ=21​sin2θ with θ=πx/l. The initial shape becomes
y(x,0)=Asinlπx​+2A​sinl2πx​.
(51)
The sine functions are orthogonal on [0,l], so this decomposition directly identifies the two nonzero spatial normal mode coefficients. Since the string is released from rest, every time-dependent sine coefficient vanishes and the subsequent motion is
y(x,t)=Asinlπx​cosω1​t+2A​sinl2πx​cos2ω1​t​,ω1​=lπc​.
(52)
Thus the signed mode amplitudes are A1​=A, A2​=A/2 and An​=0 for n≥3; their magnitude amplitudes are ∣A∣ and ∣A∣/2. The factor of two in the second angular frequency is fixed by the same boundary conditions as its spatial wavelength.
