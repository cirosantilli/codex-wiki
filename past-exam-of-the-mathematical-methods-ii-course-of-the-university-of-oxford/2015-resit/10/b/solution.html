Write the fixed point as a=(a1​,a2​,a3​) and the variable point as r=(x1​,x2​,x3​). Then R=r−a and
R=[∑j=13​(xj​−aj​)2]1/2.
(42)
Since the fixed point is not on the surface, R>0 at every point being considered. Componentwise differentiation gives ∂R/∂xj​=(xj​−aj​)/R, hence
∇R=RR​,∣∇R∣=1​.
(43)
Thus the gradient points from the fixed point towards the variable point and is a unit vector. Keeping the fixed point fixed is important: differentiating a distance between two simultaneously moving points would include both endpoint derivatives.
