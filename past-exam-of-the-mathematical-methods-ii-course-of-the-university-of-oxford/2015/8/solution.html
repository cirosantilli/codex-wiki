Take the constant speed parameter v=0, so r=x+vt and s=x−vt are independent characteristic coordinates. The chain rule gives
∂x​=∂r​+∂s​,∂t​=v(∂r​−∂s​).
(17)
Applying these operators twice to a twice differentiable function yields
Fxx​=Frr​+2Frs​+Fss​,Ftt​=v2(Frr​−2Frs​+Fss​).
(18)
Consequently the wave equation reduces to 4Frs​=0. Integrate first with respect to s, obtaining Fr​=A(r), and then with respect to r, allowing a separate integration function of s. The general local solution is
F(x,t)=G(x+vt)+H(x−vt)​,
(19)
with arbitrary sufficiently differentiable functions G and H. For v>0, the first profile travels towards negative x and the second towards positive x. Substitution directly checks the equation. Initial or boundary conditions select the two profiles; none are specified here. This is D'Alembert's formula in characteristic form.
