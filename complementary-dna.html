Complementary DNA

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Reverse transcription
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
Complementary DNA is DNA made from an RNA template by reverse transcription. When its template is mature messenger RNA, its sequence reflects the processed transcript rather than every intervening intron in the corresponding gene.
