For each 0≤k≤2n, a propositional formula over n specified variables can have exactly k satisfying assignments and length O(n2). For 0<k<2n, interpret a Boolean valuation as the binary number x1​⋯xn​ and test whether it is less than k=b1​⋯bn​.
For every position i with bi​=1, use a conjunction asserting that all earlier bits equal bj​ and xi​=0, leaving later bits unrestricted. Take the disjunction of these terms. Each satisfying assignment has a unique first differing position, so the terms are disjoint, and their counts sum to ∑i:bi​=1​2n−i=k. Their combined length is O(1+2+⋯+n)=O(n2). Use logical falsity and logical truth at the two endpoints. The count is over all n variables, including those absent from an individual term or from the whole formula.
