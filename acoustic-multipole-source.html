Acoustic multipole source

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Linear acoustics
OurBigBook.com Words: 362 Articles: 8
Acoustic sources may be organized by the number of spatial derivatives in their effective wave equation forcing. A direct scalar source is an acoustic monopole, a divergence of a force density an acoustic dipole, and a double divergence of a stress tensor an acoustic quadrupole.
