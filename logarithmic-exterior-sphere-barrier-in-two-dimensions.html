Logarithmic exterior-sphere barrier in two dimensions

OurBigBook.com Words: 46
If B(y,R) is an exterior ball tangent to a planar domain at x0​, then
w(x)=logR∣x−y∣​
(508)
is a harmonic function in the domain, vanishes at x0​, and is positive at every other point of its closure. It is therefore a barrier for the Dirichlet problem and proves that x0​ is a regular boundary point.
