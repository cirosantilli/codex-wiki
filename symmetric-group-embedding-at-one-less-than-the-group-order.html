Symmetric-group embedding at one less than the group order

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group embedding Cayley theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For n>1, a cyclic group of prime-power order n cannot embed in Sn−1​, because a permutation of order pk requires a cycle of length at least pk. If n is not a prime power, every group of order n embeds in Sn−1​ by combining coset actions on subgroups of two distinct prime orders.
