Effective-potential stratification coefficient (N2=−ρ/(γp)−(dρ/dΨ)/ρ)

OurBigBook.com Words: 46
For barotropic equilibrium dp/dΨ=−ρ, this coefficient equals −ρ−1(dp/dΨ)[γ−1dlnp/dΨ−dlnρ/dΨ]. It weights displacement across effective-potential surfaces in the Cowling energy principle for a rotating barotropic star. Its sign distinguishes stabilizing from destabilizing buoyancy there. It is not itself a squared frequency: the physical local buoyancy-frequency square contains an additional factor ∣∇Ψ∣2.
