Finite-time extension of a quadratically thickened sheet (L∼∣k∣π​FμH​​(t∗−t)−1/2,t∗=4μH/F)

For initial thickness H(1+k2z02​) and constant positive force, the material-map integral is dominated by a shrinking region around the minimum thickness as Δ↑H. With k=0, the integral of 1/[H−Δ+Hk2z02​] gives the displayed inverse-square-root divergence, whose coefficient is independent of the initial half-length. If k=0, every material point thins equally and L=HL0​/(H−Δ), with the stronger inverse-time divergence L∼4μHL0​/[F(t∗−t)]. These are ideal slender-sheet predictions up to their singular limit.
