Quasi-isometry

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 822 Articles: 24
A map f:X→Y is a (λ,ε)-quasi-isometric embedding when
λ−1dX​(x,x′)−ε≤dY​(f(x),f(x′))≤λdX​(x,x′)+ε.
(591)
It is a quasi-isometry when every point of Y lies a uniformly bounded distance from its image.
