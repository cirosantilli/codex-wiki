Gravity-driven spreading of a planar viscous drop (ℓ(t)=[5gA3t/(36νI3)]1/5)

A wide, shallow drop with cross-sectional area A has the gravity-driven volume flux q=−gh3hx​/(3ν) when substrate no-slip and zero surface shear are imposed. Mass conservation yields a porous medium equation, ht​=[g/(3ν)](h3hx​)x​. Its symmetric source-type similarity solution has half-width ℓ in the display and height h=[9ν(ℓ2−x2)/(10gt)]+1/3​, where I=∫0π/2​cos5/3θdθ. Integrating the profile recovers A. The contact-edge slope diverges, so the bulk lubrication approximation requires an inner edge regularization if contact-line microphysics or surface tension is retained.
