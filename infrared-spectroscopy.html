Infrared spectroscopy

 Home Chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 83 Articles: 2
Infrared spectroscopy measures a sample's response to infrared electromagnetic radiation. Absorption associated with stretching or bending motions of chemical bonds helps identify groups and their environments. Peak positions are commonly reported as reciprocal wavelength, in cm−1; a lower value means lower frequency. [ref]
