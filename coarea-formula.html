For a scalar smooth map Φ:Rn→R, integration can be decomposed into its level surfaces with the gradient weight displayed in the title. Near a regular level, using Φ as the normal coordinate gives the volume element dx=dSds/∣∇Φ∣, which explains the formula by integration by substitution. In particular, ∫W(x)δ(Φ(x))dx=∫Φ=0​W/∣∇Φ∣dS when the zero level is regular and the integrals are well-defined. General versions allow Lipschitz functions and maps into higher-dimensional spaces.
