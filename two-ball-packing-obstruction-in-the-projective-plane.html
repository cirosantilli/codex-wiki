Two-ball packing obstruction in the projective plane (r12​+r22​≤1)

 ... Complex manifold Kähler manifold Fubini-Study metric Fubini-Study form Fubini-Study form from circle reduction Symplectic ball chart in complex projective space
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
Two disjoint symplectic balls of radii r1​,r2​ embedding into the unit four-ball must satisfy the displayed inequality. Extend their transported standard complex structures on smaller concentric balls to a compatible almost complex structure on the projective plane. A degree-one J-holomorphic curve through both centres has area π. The Monotonicity theorem for a J-holomorphic curve bounds its area in each smaller ball below by πρi2​. Disjointness allows these bounds to be added; letting ρi​ increase to ri​ proves the result.
