Transit-timing variation (TTV)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Exoplanet atmospheric observatory Exoplanet transit photometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 2
A departure of transit arrival times from a constant-period ephemeris can reveal gravitational interactions with other bodies. Dynamical fits may constrain an exoplanet mass independently of transit depth, providing a mass to combine with radius for exoplanet interior models.
