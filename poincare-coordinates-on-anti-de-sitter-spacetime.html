Poincare coordinates on anti-de Sitter spacetime (ds2=z−2(dz2+ηab​duadub))

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Anti-de Sitter spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 2
For Lorentzian boundary metric η and z>0, this Anti-de Sitter spacetime patch has unit curvature radius. Its orthonormal coframe has connection ωa3​=−θa and curvature Ωab​=−θa∧θb​, giving sectional curvature −1. The full local isometry algebra is so(d−1,2) in spacetime dimension d. A patch need not support every local Killing flow for all parameter values.
