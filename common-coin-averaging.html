In the copied-coin model, Xi​=Z for every i, so the sample mean is exactly Z, however large n is. Its expected value is 1/2 and its variance is 1/4, matching each observation's marginal variance. The off-diagonal covariances in the variance of a sum explain the difference:
Var(X)=n21​∑i,j=1n​Cov(Xi​,Xj​)=n21​∑i,j=1n​41​=41​.
(24)
For an IID sample, those off-diagonal terms are zero. Repeating the same random input therefore does not create the reduction in fluctuations obtained from fresh independent draws. The general common-plus-individual structure is described by compound-symmetry covariance.
