Meagre set meeting every Ramsey cone in both colours

 ... Area of mathematics Combinatorics Ramsey theory Space of infinite subsets of the natural numbers Ordinary topology on infinite subsets Gap-doubling closed family of infinite subsets
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
A closed nowhere dense family can meet every cone [M]ω in continuum many sets, as the gap-doubling closed family of infinite subsets does. Well-order the cones and choose two fresh candidates in each, permanently reserving one of each colour. The red family meets every cone and its complement contains every reserved blue candidate, so it is not Ramsey. As a subset of the closed nowhere dense family, it is still nowhere dense and has the Baire property in the ordinary infinite-subset topology. This recursion requires no regularity assumption on the continuum cardinal.
