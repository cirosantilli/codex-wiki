For linear gas drag with aerodynamic stopping times t1​,t2​, masses m1​,m2​, total mass m=m1​+m2​, and relative velocity v, define
κ=mm1​/t2​+m2​/t1​​,κb​=mm1​/t1​+m2​/t2​​,Δ=t2​1​−t1​1​.
(4)
If w is the gas velocity relative to the centre of mass, the relative drag acceleration is −κv+Δw. The first term dissipates internal specific orbital energy; the second is a differential headwind that vanishes for equal aerodynamic stopping times.
