The quantum capacitance per unit area is cq​=e2D(EF​). Differentiating the gate equation gives
cdiff​1​=cg​1​+cq​1​,cdiff​=edVg​dn​.

Thus the available electronic states act as a second contribution in series with geometric capacitance. At exact neutrality the ideal zero-temperature model has cq​=cdiff​=0; actual finite-temperature or spatially nonuniform samples need a broader description. A capacitance measurement can therefore probe the density of states, rather than geometry alone.
