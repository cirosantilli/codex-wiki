Monotonicity of a measure (A⊆B ⟹ μ(A)≤μ(B))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
For measurable sets A⊆B, the disjoint union B=A⊔(B∖A) and finite additivity give μ(B)=μ(A)+μ(B∖A)≥μ(A). This uses nonnegativity and is valid with infinite values; it does not subtract infinities. Monotonicity of a measure implies that increasing measurable sets have nondecreasing measures, but the equality of their limit with the measure of their union uses the stronger axiom of countable additivity.
