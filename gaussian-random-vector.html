Gaussian random vector (X∼N(μ,Σ))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Multivariate normal distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 2
A Gaussian random vector has a multivariate normal distribution; equivalently, every real linear combination of its coordinates is a normal random variable, allowing zero variance. Its expected value vector and covariance matrix determine its law. Subvectors are independent when their cross-covariances vanish, a feature particular to joint Gaussianity.
