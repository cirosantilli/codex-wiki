Milnor–Švarc lemma

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 1
If a group has a properly discontinuous group action by isometries that is also a cocompact group action on a nonempty proper metric space that is a geodesic metric space, then the group is finitely generated and every orbit map from the word metric for any finite generating set of a group is a quasi-isometry. Finite stabilizers are permitted.
