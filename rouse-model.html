Rouse model

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymer physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
An overdamped bead-spring model of polymer dynamics with local friction and no long-range hydrodynamic interaction. For an ideal unentangled chain of N identical frictional units, the centre-of-mass diffusion coefficient is proportional to N−1 and the longest internal relaxation time to N2. A tagged segment has an intermediate-time mean-square displacement proportional to t1/2 before ordinary whole-chain diffusion dominates. Entanglement adds constraints beyond this model.
