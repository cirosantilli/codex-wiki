Future-pointing timelike four-vector (P0>∣P∣)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Four-vector
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
In metric signature (+−−−), a future-pointing timelike four-vector has positive time component larger than its spatial magnitude. Two such vectors have strictly positive Minkowski metric scalar product, since P⋅Q=P0Q0−P⋅Q≥P0Q0−∣P∣∣Q∣>0. In the opposite signature the scalar-product sign reverses, while the causal condition is the same. The future timelike cone is closed under addition, which rules out a null photon momentum equalling a sum of two massive future four-momenta.
