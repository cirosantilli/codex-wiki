Gaussian blur (Gσ​∗g)

 Home Computer science Image processing Diffusion image processing Image smoothing
OurBigBook.com Words: 60 Articles: 1
Gaussian blur applies convolution with Gσ​(x)=(2πσ2)−n/2e−∣x∣2/(2σ2) to an image signal. It averages over a spatial scale σ, suppressing rapid variation while also blurring image edges. The heat-kernel convolution realizes this filter at time t=σ2/2.
