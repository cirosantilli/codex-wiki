Autophagy

 Home Biology Biological cell Organelle Lysosome
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 3
Autophagy delivers a biological cell's own material for digestion and recycling in lysosomes. Macroautophagy is the enclosing-carrier route illustrated here; other forms deliver cargo differently. The cargo's internal origin distinguishes this route from uptake by endocytosis.[ref]
