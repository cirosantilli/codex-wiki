Ambipolar damping

Ambipolar damping is attenuation of magnetic disturbances by friction between the magnetically coupled charged fluid and a neutral fluid. In a strongly collisional mixture, the fluids nearly move together, but their small relative velocity dissipates energy. With drag coefficient ρi​μin​=ρn​μni​, the frictional energy loss is −ρi​μin​∫∣ui​−un​∣2dV. Neglecting other dissipative processes does not remove this collisional loss.
