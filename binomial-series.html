Binomial series ((1+x)α)

OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 2
For ∣x∣<1, the binomial series is
(1+x)α=∑n=0∞​(nα​)xn,(nα​)=n!α(α−1)⋯(α−n+1)​.
(63)
For a complex number α, set (0α​)=1 and (nα​)=α(α−1)⋯(α−n+1)/n! for n≥1. The displayed power series represents exp(αLog(1+z)) on the unit disc, using the branch of the complex logarithm with value zero at 1. It terminates for a nonnegative integer α. Otherwise the ratio test gives absolute convergence for ∣z∣<1. Termwise differentiation gives (1+z)F′(z)=αF(z) and F(0)=1, which determine that holomorphic function. With α=−1/2 and argument −z, its coefficients are the normalized central binomial coefficients.
