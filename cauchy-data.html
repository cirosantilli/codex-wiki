Cauchy data

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Cauchy problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 83 Articles: 1
For a scalar equation of order k, Cauchy data consist of the first k normal jets u,∂N​u,…,∂Nk−1​u on the initial hypersurface.
These prescribed values define the initial conditions of a Cauchy problem.
