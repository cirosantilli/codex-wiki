Residue theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 3
If f is holomorphic on and inside a positively oriented closed contour except for finitely many isolated singularities aj​ inside it, then
∮f(z)dz=2πi∑j​Res(f,aj​).
