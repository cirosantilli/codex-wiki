Polymorphism (materials science)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics State of matter
OurBigBook.com Words: 127 Articles: 1
Crystal polymorphism is the existence of different crystal structures for the same chemical substance. Each crystal polymorph can have its own mass density, melting behavior and range of stability on a phase diagram. A different external shape of the same crystal structure alone does not establish a different crystal polymorph.[ref]
At fixed temperature and pressure, compare the chemical potentials of the possible crystal polymorphs. The lower value determines the stable bulk thermodynamic phase; equality permits phase coexistence when neither form is beaten by another thermodynamic phase. A higher-chemical potential form can persist through metastability, so finding it in a sample does not by itself locate an equilibrium boundary.
