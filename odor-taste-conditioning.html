Odor-taste conditioning

 Home Biology Animal behaviour Learning
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
A change in an odor's taste-like qualities or associations after pairing it with a taste. Pre/post odor-only judgments can test transfer beyond the original mixture, while controls are needed for expectation and use of response scales. Such transfer supports learned cross-sensory effects but does not by itself prove a distinct developmental synesthesia mechanism.[ref]
