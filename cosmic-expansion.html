Cosmic expansion

 Home Physics Branch of physics Cosmology Scale factor
OurBigBook.com Words: 174 Articles: 8
Cosmic expansion is the increase of physical distances between comoving observers as the scale factor grows. Its instantaneous fractional rate is the Hubble parameter.
