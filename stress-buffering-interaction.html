Stress-buffering interaction (∂E[D∣S,P]/∂S=β1​+β3​P)

 Home Biology Medicine Public health Epidemiology Protective factor
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A pattern in which a resource modifies the relationship between adversity and an adverse outcome. In the model E[D∣S,P]=β0​+β1​S+β2​P+β3​SP, a negative β3​ attenuates the adversity slope as the resource increases. Its interpretation is scale dependent, and an observational interaction is not automatically a causal buffering effect.
