Familywise error control for a laminar hypothesis family (pIadj​=maxJ⊇I​∣J∣m​pJ​)

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical hypothesis test Multiple hypothesis testing Familywise error rate
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For nonempty index sets forming a laminar family of sets, reject an intersection hypothesis when the displayed adjusted value is at most α. The inclusion-maximal true sets are disjoint and have total size at most m. Any false rejection forces some maximal true J to have pJ​≤α∣J∣/m. Validity and the union bound control the familywise error rate by α, without independence or a complete closure.
