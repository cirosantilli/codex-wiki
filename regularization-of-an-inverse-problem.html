Regularization of an inverse problem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 7k Articles: 128
A regularization is a family of continuous maps Rα​ such that Rα​f→A†f as α↓0 for every datum in the domain of the Moore–Penrose inverse of an operator. A linear regularization uses bounded linear operators; nonlinear variational regularization can use nonquadratic penalties. A parameter rule that also ensures stability as the data noise tends to zero makes it a convergent regularization of an inverse problem.
