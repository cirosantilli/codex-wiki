Ds meson (Ds+​=csˉ)

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron Meson D meson
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A charmed strange meson. Its ground-state charged particle has valence content csˉ and spin-parity 0−. Its antiparticle has the conjugate valence content. Nonleptonic decays are governed by weak charged currents and hadronic transition matrix elements.
