Stable homotopy theory

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 36
Stable homotopy theory studies topological spectra, which retain the information that persists under repeated reduced suspension. Its stable homotopy category admits invertible suspension of spectra and exact cofiber sequences of spectra.
