Cone over a diagram

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Diagram in a category

A cone over D:J→C with vertex A is a natural family γj​:A→D(j) satisfying D(u)γi​=γj​ for every u:i→j. Cone morphisms are maps of vertices commuting with every leg.
