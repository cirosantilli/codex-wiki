Truncated Euler-product lower bound

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Dirichlet series Euler product
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For a finite set of primes choose 0<g(p)<1, put k(p)=g(p)/(1−g(p)), and let W=∏p​(1+k(p)). Give each squarefree integer d on these primes probability k(d)/W. Inclusion of each prime is represented by a Bernoulli random variable with parameter g(p); these are independent random variables, so E[logd]=∑p​g(p)logp. If this is at most θlogL with 0<θ<1, the Markov inequality gives
∑d≤L​k(d)≥(1−θ)W.
(49)
This turns a full Euler product into a lower bound for the truncated normalizing sum of a Selberg sieve.
