Positive semidefinite bilinear form

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Quadratic form
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
A symmetric bilinear form is positive semidefinite when B(v,v)≥0 for every v, and positive definite when equality is possible only for v=0.
