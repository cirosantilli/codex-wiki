Rigid-backed shear-wave dispersion (tan(mh)=−μ1​m/(μ2​ℓ))

 ... Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Elastic wave in an isotropic solid Shear-horizontal wave Guided shear-horizontal mode
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 1
A layer with shear modulus μ1​ and wave speed c1​ lies between a rigid support and a half-space with shear modulus μ2​ and wave speed c2​>c1​. A trapped antiplane shear normal mode has layer amplitude Asin(my) and exterior amplitude Asin(mh)e−ℓ(y−h), where m=kc2/c12​−1​ and ℓ=k1−c2/c22​​. Continuity of displacement field and traction gives the displayed dispersion relation. It requires c1​<c<c2​ and differs from a Love wave with a free boundary.
