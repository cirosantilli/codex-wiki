Phase-group orthogonality for degree-zero dispersion (cp​⋅cg​=0)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Dispersion relation Phase velocity and group velocity
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
If a differentiable dispersion relation ω(k) is homogeneous of degree zero at nonzero k, the Euler homogeneous function theorem gives k⋅∇k​ω=0. Therefore phase velocity ωk/k2 is orthogonal to group velocity ∇k​ω. The result applies exactly to limiting inertial waves; finite compressibility can alter the dispersion.
