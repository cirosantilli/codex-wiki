Cutoff frequency (fc​)

 Home Electrical engineering Signal processing Linear filter
OurBigBook.com Words: 45 Articles: 1
A chosen boundary in a frequency response, commonly where the voltage amplitude gain falls to 1/2​ of its pass-region value. The convention must specify the reference gain.
