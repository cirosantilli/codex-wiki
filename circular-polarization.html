Circular polarization

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Plane electromagnetic wave Polarization of an electromagnetic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
An electromagnetic wave has circular polarization when its transverse electric field has constant magnitude and rotates once during each period. In complex-amplitude notation, two orthogonal components have equal magnitude and a relative phase of one quarter-cycle.
