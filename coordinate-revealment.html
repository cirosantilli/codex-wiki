Coordinate revealment (δi​(T,μ))

 ... Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Decision tree model Decision tree Decision-tree query cost
OurBigBook.com Words: 171
The coordinate revealment of coordinate i for a decision tree T under input probability distribution μ is
δi​(T,μ)=PrX∼μ​(T queries coordinate i).
(17)
It depends on the chosen decision tree and the probability distribution, not just on the Boolean function. Since no coordinate is queried twice on a graph path, indicator functions give
qT​(X)=∑i=1n​1{T queries i}​,Eμ​[qT​(X)]=∑i=1n​δi​(T,μ).
(18)
The second equality uses linearity of expectation; it does not require independence of random variables or a product measure. The cited paper defines these inspection probabilities and their sum for product measures; the displayed indicator function argument extends the identity to any input probability distribution.[ref]
For the three-bit majority decision tree under the discrete uniform distribution, (δ1​,δ2​,δ3​)=(1,1,1/2), whose sum is 2.5. Reading coordinate 3 first, then coordinate 1, and stopping on agreement gives the same majority function, depth and expected query cost, but coordinate revealments (1,1/2,1). The asymmetry belongs to the chosen query order. Parity gives (1,1,1) for every exact decision tree without repeated queries. If repeated queries were charged, inspection probabilities would count distinct coordinates; total query count would instead require expected multiplicities.
