Metric connection

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle)
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 24
A connection on a Riemannian vector bundle is metric-compatible when
d⟨s,t⟩=⟨∇s,t⟩+⟨s,∇t⟩.
(30)
Equivalently, its parallel transport maps are isometries.
