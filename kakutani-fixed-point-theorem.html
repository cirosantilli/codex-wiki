Kakutani fixed-point theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fixed-point theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
A nonempty compact convex set K in a finite-dimensional space and an upper hemicontinuous set-valued mapping F:K⇉K with nonempty closed convex values admit x∈F(x). A closed graph suffices when the whole output space is compact.
