Finite normal extension as a splitting field

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Splitting field
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
A finite extension L/K is normal exactly when it is the splitting field over K of some polynomial. For the forward direction, multiply the minimal polynomials of finitely many generators of L; normality puts all their roots in L. The reverse direction follows because a K-embedding permutes the roots of a polynomial and hence preserves its splitting field.
