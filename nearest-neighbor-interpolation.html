Nearest-neighbor interpolation

 Home Computer science Image processing
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
Nearest-neighbor interpolation assigns each requested location the value of its nearest available sample, with a specified convention for ties. In lossy block averaging, samples are placed at block centres and each is copied into its four original pixel positions. This enlarges the raster image without recovering discarded detail.
