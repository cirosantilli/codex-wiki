Immigration fraction among upward steps (2∑k≥0​πk​θ/(k+θ))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Birth-death chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
In a positive recurrent birth-death-immigration jump chain, let In​ and An​ count immigration events and upward steps before time n. The centered immigration indicators form bounded martingale differences; the strong law for martingales with bounded increments and the countable-state Markov chain ergodic theorem give In​/n→∑k​πk​θ/(k+θ) almost surely. The analogous argument for upward steps gives An​/n→∑k​πk​uk​. Summing detailed balance gives ∑k​πk​uk​=∑k​πk​dk​=1/2, since every step is upward or downward. Therefore In​/An​ converges to the displayed fraction. For this chain the explicit stationary distribution reduces the fraction to 1−p/(1−p).
