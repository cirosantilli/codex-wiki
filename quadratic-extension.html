Quadratic extension

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Field extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 142 Articles: 2
A quadratic extension is a field extension of degree two. It is normal because the other root of the minimal polynomial T2+bT+c of any non-base-field element is −b−α and lies in the same extension.
