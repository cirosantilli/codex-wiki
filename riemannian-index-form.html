Riemannian index form (I(V,W))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Energy of a curve Variation vector field
OurBigBook.com Words: 610 Articles: 9
Along a geodesic γ with tangent T, the index form on endpoint-vanishing vector fields is
I(V,W)=∫(⟨Dt​V,Dt​W⟩−⟨R(V,T)T,W⟩)dt.
