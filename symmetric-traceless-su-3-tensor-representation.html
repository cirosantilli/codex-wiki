Symmetric traceless SU(3) tensor representation (d(p,q)=21​(p+1)(q+1)(p+q+2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 255 Articles: 2
Symmetrize the p upper and q lower indices separately, and remove every upper-lower trace. The resulting representation has highest weight pω1​+qω2​. Its dimension is
(2p+2​)(2q+2​)−(2p+1​)(2q+1​)=2(p+1)(q+1)(p+q+2)​.
(6)
The subtraction is valid because contraction is onto: in the polynomial realization its adjoint, for the monomial-factorial inner product, is multiplication by the nonzero polynomial ∑i​xi​yi​, an injective map. The resulting kernel contains the highest-weight constituent and has precisely its Weyl dimension formula dimension, hence is irreducible. This highest-weight constituent is not always the largest-dimensional constituent of the full mixed tensor.
