Graph norm (∥x∥A​=∥x∥+∥Ax∥)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Graph of a linear operator Closed linear operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
The graph norm of A:D(A)⊆X→Y is ∥x∥A​=∥x∥X​+∥Ax∥Y​. If X and Y are Banach spaces, then A is a closed linear operator exactly when its domain is complete in the graph norm.
