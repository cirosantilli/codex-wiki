Quantile endpoint at probability one (F−1(1)=inf{x:F(x)=1})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Quantile function
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
The quantile function is finite at every probability strictly between zero and one, but need not be finite at one. For any unbounded-above normal distribution, F(x)<1 for every real x, so its generalized inverse at one is +∞. Inverse transform sampling still produces a real random variable almost surely because a continuous uniform distribution puts zero mass at that endpoint. A real-valued implementation may discard endpoint draws or assign an arbitrary finite value there without changing the sampled law.
