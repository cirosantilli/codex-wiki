Volume flux per unit width (Q)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Volumetric flow rate
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
For a flow invariant in one horizontal direction, the volume flux per unit width is the velocity integrated across the flow depth. It has dimensions of area per unit time.
