Tautological bundle

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 811 Articles: 15
The tautological bundle over a projective space has as its fiber over a line precisely that line. The real tautological line bundle over RPm has first Stiefel-Whitney class equal to the degree-one generator.
