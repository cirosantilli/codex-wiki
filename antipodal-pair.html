Antipodal pair ({x,−x})

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Sphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
On a sphere centred at the origin, an antipodal pair is the unordered pair {x,−x}. The antipodal map exchanges its two points.
