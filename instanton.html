Instanton

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Path integral Euclidean path integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 449 Articles: 7
An instanton is a localized finite-action classical solution in Euclidean spacetime, used in a semiclassical description of quantum tunnelling and other nonperturbative effects. In one-dimensional quantum mechanics it can connect distinct degenerate potential minima over infinite Euclidean time. A quartic double-well instanton is an elementary example; a Yang-Mills instanton is a gauge-field example with different geometric structure.
