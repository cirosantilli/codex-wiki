Just-infinite profinite group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Profinite group
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 1
An infinite profinite group is just infinite if every nontrivial closed normal subgroup has finite index, equivalently is open. Requiring closedness is part of the topological convention. This property says that every proper Hausdorff quotient is finite.
