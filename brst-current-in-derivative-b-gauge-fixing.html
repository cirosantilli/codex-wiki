Use gauge-fixing term (∂μb)⋅Aμ​, ghost term −(∂μcˉ)⋅Dμ​c, and the left-acting BRST differential with scˉ=b. Localizing the odd parameter on the left yields δS=−∫(∂μ​ϵ)jBμ​. Moving it through the antighost derivative determines the last sign. Changing the overall Noether convention reverses this current; rewriting gauge fixing by integration by parts changes its improvement term. Every summand has ghost number one.
