For a discrete image signal operator A, minimize α∥Au∥2​+21​∥u−g∥22​ with one Euclidean norm on the full gradient array. This differs from discrete isotropic total variation, which sums pixelwise Euclidean lengths. The global norm's dual ball is a single ball; its convex projection normalizes the whole vector array.
