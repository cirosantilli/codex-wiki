Superhorizon conservation of single-field comoving curvature (R˙≃0(k≪aH))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic inflation Slow-roll approximation Comoving curvature perturbation
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For an adiabatic single-field attractor, gradient terms are negligible well outside the Hubble radius and the growing comoving-curvature mode is constant. The separate-universe interpretation identifies it with the difference of local expansion histories. The conservation statement can fail for a non-attractor phase or an independent entropy mode.
