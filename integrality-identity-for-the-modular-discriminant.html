Integrality identity for the modular discriminant (Δ=(5S3​+7S5​)/12+100S32​+8000S33​−147S52​)

Put Sj​=∑n≥1​σj​(n)qn. Expanding E4​=1+240S3​ and E6​=1−504S5​ gives Δ=(5S3​+7S5​)/12+100S32​+8000S33​−147S52​. For every integer d, 5d3+7d5 is divisible by twelve, by separate congruences modulo three and four. Consequently every coefficient of the modular discriminant is integral, without using a product expansion.
