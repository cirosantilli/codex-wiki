Wasserstein distance (Wρ​(μ,ν))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Coupling
OurBigBook.com Words: 232 Articles: 7
For a metric ρ, the first Wasserstein distance is
Wρ​(μ,ν)=inf{Eρ(X,Y):(X,Y) couples μ,ν}.
