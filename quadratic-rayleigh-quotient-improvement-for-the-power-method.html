Quadratic Rayleigh-quotient improvement for the power method

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Linear operator theory Power method
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For a real symmetric matrix with ∣λ1​∣>∣λ2​∣ and a starting vector having nonzero component in the leading eigendirection, power-method direction errors are
O(​λ1​λ2​​​k),
(125)
whereas the Rayleigh quotient error is
O(​λ1​λ2​​​2k).
(126)
The improvement occurs because orthogonality cancels terms linear in the direction error.
