On a closed manifold with a Riemannian metric, a smooth solution of ∂t​u+Δu=0 for the positive Laplace-Beltrami operator satisfies the displayed identity by integration by parts. Zero initial data force the solution to vanish, proving uniqueness and the semigroup property for heat evolution.
