Normal variety

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Noetherian scheme Normal scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 3
An algebraic variety whose local rings are integrally closed domains. In particular an irreducible variety that is a normal variety has normal affine coordinate rings, enabling codimension-two extension of regular functions on a normal variety.
