Quantum XY chain (H=−J∑n​(Snx​Sn+1x​+Sny​Sn+1y​))

A one-dimensional quantum spin model with transverse XY exchange interactions; J>0 is the ferromagnetic sign in the displayed convention. Unlike the classical XY model, its site variables are noncommuting spin operators in a fixed irreducible spin representation. With periodic boundary conditions and L≥2, H=−2J​∑n​(Sn+​Sn+1−​+Sn−​Sn+1+​). It preserves total Sz and the associated global U(1) symmetry.
