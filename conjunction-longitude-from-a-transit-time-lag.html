Conjunction longitude from a transit time lag (Λ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planetary system dynamics Conjunction (astronomy)
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For two uniform angular motions with Pb​<Pc​, an inner exoplanet transit at zero and next outer exoplanet transit at τ, the next conjunction solves (nb​−nc​)t+nc​τ=2π. Hence its longitude modulo 2π is
Λ=2πPc​/Pb​−11−τ/Pb​​.
(2)
For an eccentric outer body this is only an approximation. Let fL​,ML​ be its line-of-sight anomalies and Δe​(t)=f(t)−M(t)−(fL​−ML​). Exact true alignment instead requires (nb​−nc​)t+nc​τ−Δe​(t)=2π. The correction follows directly from uniform mean anomaly and nonuniform true anomaly.
