Source (quantum field theory, J(x))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Generating functional
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
An external source is a prescribed field coupled to a dynamical field or operator in the action, for example SJ​=S0​+∫Ji​(x)ϕi​(x)ddx. Functional derivatives of the generating functional with respect to J insert the corresponding fields into correlation functions. The source is varied to generate these insertions, rather than integrated over as a dynamical degree of freedom. Transforming it in the appropriate representation maintains an invariant source coupling when deriving a Ward identity.
