Akaike information criterion

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Model selection
OurBigBook.com Words: 298 Articles: 6
For a model with k fitted parameters and maximized likelihood L(θ), the Akaike information criterion is
AIC=−2logL(θ)+2k.
