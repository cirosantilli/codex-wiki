Slower-speed degeneration of a large deviation principle (an​/bn​→∞:Ib​(x)=0 at μ, Ib​(x)=∞ elsewhere)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Convergence of random variables Large deviation principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
Suppose a large deviation principle at speed an​ has a good rate function with unique zero mu. At any slower speed bn​→∞ with an​/bn​→∞, its rate is zero at mu and infinity elsewhere. On a closed set excluding mu, the positive original rate gives decay faster than every exponential at speed bn​. Neighborhoods of mu have probability tending to one, giving the slower-speed open-set lower bound. The new rate is good because its finite sublevel set is the singleton mu.
