Image factorization in an abelian category (A↠imf↪B)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Subobject Image factorization
OurBigBook.com Words: 114 Articles: 2
For a morphism in an abelian category, its coimage coker(kerf) is canonically isomorphic to its image ker(cokerf). This yields an epimorphism followed by a monomorphism, unique up to unique compatible isomorphism. Every epi-mono factorization has this same middle object.
