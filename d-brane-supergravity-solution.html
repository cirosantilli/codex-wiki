D-brane supergravity solution (dss2​=H−1/2dx∥2​+H1/2dx⊥2​)

For the asymptotically flat extremal Dp solution with p<7, H=1+Q/r7−p is harmonic away from the source and eΦ=gs​H(3−p)/4. The electric potential can be chosen as C0⋯p​=gs−1​(H−1−1); magnetic descriptions use the dual flux, and a D3-brane has self-dual five-form flux. An equally oriented probe has DBI density −μp​gs−1​H−1 and Wess-Zumino density +μp​gs−1​(H−1−1), whose sum is position independent. This proves the corresponding parallel D-brane no-force identity in the classical background. The supergravity approximation requires controlled curvature and coupling; it does not resolve every brane core.
