Orientation of a smooth manifold

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Smooth manifold Orientable smooth manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
An orientation of a smooth manifold is a continuous choice of orientation of a vector space in every tangent space, equivalently an orientation of a vector bundle on its tangent bundle. It can be specified by an atlas with positive Jacobian determinants on all overlaps or by a nowhere-vanishing top-degree differential form. On a connected orientable manifold there are two choices, interchanged by reversing every tangent-space orientation.
