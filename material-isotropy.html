Material isotropy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Finite elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 3
Material isotropy makes the elastic response independent of a rotation of reference material directions: σ(FR)=σ(F) for proper rotations R. Combined with material frame indifference, it yields coaxiality of isotropic elastic stress.
