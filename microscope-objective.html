Microscope objective (mo​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Optical microscope
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The specimen-facing optical lens assembly of an optical microscope. A microscope objective collects light from the specimen and sets the object-side numerical aperture. In a finite-conjugate microscope it forms the intermediate microscope image directly; in an infinity-corrected microscope a tube lens completes that image formation. Its label alone does not calibrate a specimen length.
