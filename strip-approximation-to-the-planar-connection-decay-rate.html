Strip approximation to the planar connection decay rate

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Percolation theory Bond percolation Connection decay rate in a percolation strip
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
Let q(n) be the unrestricted planar two-point percolation two-point connection probability. Every finite connecting graph path is contained in some strip, so qk​(n)↑q(n) for each fixed n. Therefore
limk→∞​fk​(p)=infk≥1​infn≥1​n−logqk​(n)​=infn≥1​n−logq(n)​.
(2)
The equality uses commuting infima and monotonicity, not an unjustified interchange of two general limits. The right-hand side is the whole-plane connection decay rate by the Fekete lemma.
