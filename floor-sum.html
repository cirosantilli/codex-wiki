Floor sum

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Floor and ceiling functions Floor function
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 2
A floor sum adds values of a floor function, often in the form F(n,a,b,m)=∑i=0n−1​⌊(ai+b)/m⌋ for integers n≥0, m>0, a, and b. If a,b≥0, it counts lattice points (i,j) with 0≤i<n and 1≤j≤(ai+b)/m. Splitting a and b into quotients and remainders isolates an arithmetic progression and a constant contribution; swapping the remaining counting axes leads to an Euclidean algorithm reduction.
