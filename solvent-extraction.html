Solvent extraction

 Home Engineering Chemical engineering Separation process
OurBigBook.com Words: 36 Articles: 1
Transfer of a solute between immiscible liquid phases according to its relative chemical affinities. complexation and oxidation state can change the distribution; selective back-extraction is often called stripping. Extraction efficiency alone does not establish separation from a second solute.
