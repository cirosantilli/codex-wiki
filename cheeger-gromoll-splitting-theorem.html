Cheeger-Gromoll splitting theorem

OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 1
This is a Riemannian splitting theorem whose hypotheses include a globally minimizing geodesic line.
A complete connected Riemannian manifold with nonnegative Ricci curvature that contains a line splits isometrically as N×R.
