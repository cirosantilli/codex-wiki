Real-coefficient cubic-quintic amplitude equation (At​=μA+α∣A∣2A−∣A∣4A+Axx​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Nonlinear partial differential equation Complex Ginzburg–Landau equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 1
For real μ and α>0, this complex amplitude equation has a destabilizing cubic term and a saturating quintic term. Nonzero uniform intensities solve μ+αq−q2=0. The upper branch is radially stable and the lower branch radially unstable; constant phase is neutral because multiplication by eiϕ0​ preserves the equation. Stable conduction and the stable upper pattern coexist for −α2/4<μ<0. At the fold, a zero radial eigenvalue conceals one-sided nonlinear instability, so strict stability cannot be inferred from eigenvalues alone.
