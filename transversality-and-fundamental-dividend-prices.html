Iteration of the pricing Euler equation gives
ζt​St​=Et​[∑s=t+1N​ζs​ds​+ζN​SN​].
(11)
The displayed fundamental price follows when the future dividend series is integrable and Et​[ζN​SN​]→0. Without this dividend-price transversality condition, a deflated price martingale component can survive. For individual holdings, the analogous terminal term is E[ζN​SN​θN+1​]. Telescoping the discrete dividend budget equation gives the initial wealth cost of consumption minus this terminal term; its vanishing is needed for budget equality.
