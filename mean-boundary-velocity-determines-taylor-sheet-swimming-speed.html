Mean boundary velocity determines Taylor-sheet swimming speed

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Microswimmer Taylor swimming sheet
OurBigBook.com Words: 157 Articles: 2
A Taylor expansion of the no-slip boundary condition about the flat sheet makes the second-order mean tangential velocity ⟨u2​(0)⟩=−⟨y1​∂y​u1​(0)⟩. The mean mode of Stokes flow is linear in height when no pressure gradient is imposed. In an unbounded fluid, bounded velocity excludes mean shear; in a confined fluid, the force-free condition excludes it. The remaining mean velocity is uniform and equals the swimming speed in the sheet frame. Thus the leading speed can be found from the first-order field without solving the oscillatory second-order field.
