Binomial proportion (p​=D/n)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution Binomial distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 1
For D∼Bin(n,p) with n>0, the sample binomial proportion is D/n. Its expectation is p and its variance is p(1−p)/n. The central limit theorem gives a normal approximation when both expected outcome counts are sufficiently large. These moments determine a standard error and model-based control limits.
