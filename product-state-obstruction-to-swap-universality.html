Product-state obstruction to SWAP universality (SWAP(A⊗B)=(B⊗A)SWAP)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Controlled unitary gate Controlled-NOT gate SWAP gate
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
The SWAP gate and local unitary operators preserve product states. The displayed identity moves every exchange past a pair of local gates, reducing any such two-qubit quantum circuit to a local operation, optionally followed by SWAP gate. On more wires the same argument leaves a permutation of the tensor factors. By induction, a fully factored input remains factored. Since universal quantum computation includes operations that create quantum entanglement from a product state, these gates cannot form a universal quantum gate set.
