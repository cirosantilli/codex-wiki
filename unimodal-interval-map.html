Unimodal interval map

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Discrete dynamical system Interval map
OurBigBook.com Words: 440 Articles: 6
A unimodal interval map is a interval map that is increasing to one turning point and decreasing afterwards. For a differentiable map with a maximum at zero, a critical point of even order d means f(x)=f(0)+cxd+o(xd) with c<0. The critical order distinguishes universality classes of period-doubling cascades.
