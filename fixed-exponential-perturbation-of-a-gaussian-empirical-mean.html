Let Ai​ be independent random variables with normal distribution N(μ,σ2), σ2>0, and let B be an independent exponential random variable of rate λ>0. At speed L, (B+∑i=1L​Ai​)/L has the good rate function
K(x)={(x−μ)2/(2σ2),λ(x−μ)−λ2σ2/2,​x≤μ+λσ2,x≥μ+λσ2.​
(12)
The product large-deviation principle gives the joint cost λb+(z−μ)2/(2σ2) for b≥0, and the contraction principle for large deviations gives its infimal convolution under b+z=x. The minimum occurs at b=(x−μ−λσ2)+. The fixed exponential perturbation changes the far upper tail even though it vanishes in probability after division by L; a fixed Gaussian random variable divided by L instead has exponential equivalence with zero at speed L.
