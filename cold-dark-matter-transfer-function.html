Cold-dark-matter transfer function (CDM transfer function)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Linear cosmological perturbation theory Cosmological transfer function
OurBigBook.com Words: 660 Articles: 7
At fixed time after equality, the comoving cold-dark-matter density contrast per unit primordial curvature grows as k2 for k≪keq​ and only logarithmically for k≫keq​. Small-scale modes entered during radiation domination and could grow only logarithmically until equality.
