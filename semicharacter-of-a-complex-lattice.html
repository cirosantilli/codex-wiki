Semicharacter of a complex lattice (α:Λ→U(1))

 ... Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Complex torus Appell–Humbert theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For an integral alternating form E arising as the imaginary part of a Hermitian form, a semicharacter satisfies α(λ+μ)=eπiE(λ,μ)α(λ)α(μ). The Appell–Humbert theorem uses this datum together with the Hermitian form to specify a holomorphic line bundle on a complex torus. The ratio of two semicharacters is an ordinary unitary group homomorphism. On the square period lattice, with H(z,w)=zw, all semicharacters are α(m+ni)=(−1)mnumvn for u,v∈U(1).
