Compound Poisson transition law of a driftless square-root diffusion (N∼Poisson(2x/h),Yj​∼Exp(2/h))

The elapsed-time h>0 transition distribution of a driftless square-root diffusion starting at x is the compound Poisson distribution
Xh​ =d ∑j=1N​Yj​,N∼Poisson(2x/h),Yj​∼Exp(2/h),
(27)
where all variables on the right are independent and the empty sum is zero. Here the exponential distribution parameter is its rate. The Laplace transform of this sum is
exp[h2x​(2/h+u2/h​−1)]=exp(−1+uh/2xu​),
(28)
which is the Laplace transform of the driftless square-root diffusion; uniqueness of the Laplace transform proves the distributional identity. In particular, for 0≤v<2/h, the exponential moment is
Ex​[evXh​]=exp(1−vh/2xv​).
(29)
For x>0 this exponential moment is infinite when v≥2/h, since the event N=1 has positive probability and one exponential jump already has an infinite moment there. At x=0 the process remains zero and every such moment is one.
