Endoplasmic reticulum

 Home Biology Biological cell Organelle
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 10
The endoplasmic reticulum is a membrane-enclosed network of tubules and flattened sacs in a typical eukaryotic biological cell. Its continuous boundary encloses the ER lumen, separate from the cytosol, and connects with the outer nuclear envelope. Rough endoplasmic reticulum and smooth endoplasmic reticulum are regions of this network.[ref]
