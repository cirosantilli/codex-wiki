Relativistic collision mass threshold (W≥c2∑i​μi​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Four-momentum Four-momentum conservation
OurBigBook.com Words: 344 Articles: 4
If the total incoming four-momentum is timelike and has centre-of-momentum energy W, production of particles with rest masses μi​ requires W≥∑i​μi​c2. In the centre-of-momentum frame, total final energy is a sum of μi2​c4+c2∣pi​∣2​, each at least its rest energy. For massive products equality occurs with all at rest in that frame. A single product requires equality of the entire invariant mass, not just an inequality; a massless product cannot have nonzero energy and be at rest.
