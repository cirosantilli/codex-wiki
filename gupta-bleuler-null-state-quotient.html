Gupta-Bleuler null-state quotient (Hphys​=kerC/rad(kerC))

 ... Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing Covariant gauge Feynman gauge Gupta-Bleuler formalism
OurBigBook.com Words: 185 Articles: 1
Choose Cp​=ap0​−ap3​ with the standard temporal and longitudinal polarization vectors. The physical pre-space is the common kernel of all Cp​. In each regulated mode, a0 acts on creator polynomials as −∂a0†​ and a3 as ∂a3†​, so the constraint kernel consists of transverse creator polynomials and polynomials in C†=a0†−a3†. Since [C,C†]=0, these latter excitations remain constrained. They are orthogonal to every constrained state because ⟨Φ∣C†=⟨CΦ∣=0. Quotienting this radical of a Hermitian form leaves only the transverse bosonic Fock space with a positive inner product. The constraint alone gives a positive semidefinite Hermitian form; quotienting removes its null directions. If starting from finite-particle creator polynomials, take the Hilbert space completion of this positive quotient to obtain the physical Hilbert space.
