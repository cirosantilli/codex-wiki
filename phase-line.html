Phase line

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Autonomous system (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The phase line of a scalar autonomous differential equation y′=f(y) marks the zeros of f and the direction of motion between them. The sign of f gives the arrows. Under local uniqueness, a nonconstant trajectory cannot cross an equilibrium point; inward arrows indicate attraction and outward arrows repulsion.
