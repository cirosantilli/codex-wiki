Condensate fraction (f0​=N0​/N)

OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The condensate fraction is the population N0​ of a macroscopically occupied one-particle state divided by the total particle number N. For an interacting system, use a macroscopic eigenvalue of the one-particle density matrix, rather than assume that the relevant mode is an undeformed single-particle trap ground state. The condensate fraction is not generally the fraction measured as superfluid by a flow or rotation response.
