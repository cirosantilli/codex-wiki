Interchange process

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Continuous-time Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 91 Articles: 1
Place one distinct label at each vertex of a finite connected weighted graph. At rate cuv​=cvu​≥0, interchange the labels at u and v. This continuous-time Markov chain has a uniform distribution on a finite set as its stationary distribution. The position of one label is the random walk with the same rates.
