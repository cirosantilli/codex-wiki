Drag on a two-dimensional circular bubble from outer-flow dissipation (D=8πμU)

OurBigBook.com Words: 30
For the zero-circulation flow around a circle, e:e=8U2a4/r6. Integration over the exterior plane gives dissipation 8πμU2 per unit axial length and hence drag D=8πμU per unit length.
