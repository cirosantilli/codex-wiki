Rational divisible hull (G⊗Z​Q)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Abelian group Torsion-free abelian group
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
For a torsion-free abelian group G, its rational divisible hull is G⊗Z​Q. It consists of fractions g/n, with n>0 and g/n=h/m exactly when mg=nh. The natural map G→G⊗Q is injective. Every homomorphism from G into a torsion-free divisible Abelian group extends uniquely through this hull.
