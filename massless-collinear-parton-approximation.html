Massless collinear parton approximation (k=ξP,P2=k2=0)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Parton Parton model
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
The leading high-energy approximation neglects quark and target masses and transverse constituent momentum. A lightlike reference momentum is used for the hadron; this does not describe a massless particle at rest.
