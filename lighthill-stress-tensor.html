Lighthill stress tensor (Tij​=ρui​uj​+(p′−c02​ρ′)δij​−σij​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Linear acoustics Lighthill acoustic analogy
OurBigBook.com Words: 89 Articles: 1
This tensor contains convective momentum flux, departure from the reference linear pressure-density relation, and viscous stress. Its double divergence is an acoustic quadrupole distribution in Lighthill acoustic analogy.
