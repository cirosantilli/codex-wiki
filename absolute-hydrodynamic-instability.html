Absolute hydrodynamic instability

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Hydrodynamic stability
OurBigBook.com Words: 281 Articles: 5
An absolute hydrodynamic instability makes the response to a localized impulse grow at a fixed spatial point in a specified frame. This differs from convective hydrodynamic instability, which amplifies a moving wave packet while its response decays at fixed positions. For analytic dispersion relations, the relevant absolute frequency is associated with an accessible zero-group velocity saddle, with the spatial-branch selection checked rather than assumed for an arbitrary dispersion relation.
