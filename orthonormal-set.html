Orthonormal set

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Inner product
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 3
An orthonormal set consists of vectors that have norm one and are pairwise orthogonal. Its Gram matrix is the identity matrix.
