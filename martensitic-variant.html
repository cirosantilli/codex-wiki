Martensitic variant

 ... Chemical substance Metal Alloy Steel Steel microstructure Martensite
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 1
A martensitic variant is one of the lattice deformations corresponding to the low-temperature crystal phase. The parent crystal's discrete symmetries generate different variants. In a nonlinear elasticity model each representative Ui​ supplies a hyperelastic energy well SO(3)Ui​. A representative need not itself be a symmetric stretch tensor; multiplication by a proper rotation leaves the same well.
