In a flat universe containing pressureless matter and a positive cosmological constant, normalize the scale factor to one today. The Friedmann equation and dτ=dt/a give
τ0​=H0−1​∫01​Ωm​a+ΩΛ​a4​da​=H0−1​Ωm−1/3​ΩΛ−1/6​∫0(ΩΛ​/Ωm​)1/3​y(1+y3)​dy​.

The change of variable is a=(Ωm​/ΩΛ​)1/3y. Including radiation in cosmology makes this an approximation, with its omitted early contribution of order H0−1​Ωr​​/Ωm​. When the upper limit is large, the integral approaches I=Γ(1/6)Γ(1/3)/(3π​); this follows by setting z=y3 and using the beta function integral.
