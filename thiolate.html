Thiolate (RS−)

 Home Chemistry Ion
OurBigBook.com Words: 37 Articles: 1
A thiolate is RS−, the conjugate base of a thiol. Its polarizable sulfur is often effective in conjugate addition. protonation of the resulting carbon anion can regenerate thiolate from another thiol molecule.
