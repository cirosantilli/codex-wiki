Time independence of V makes the Green function depend on t,t′ through τ=t−t′. A spatially varying potential does not imply spatial translation invariance. Fix r′ and set x=r−r′; write the Fourier coefficient more fully as Gr′​(k;ω). The source position is an implicit parameter in the printed notation. Define
Vr′​(q)=∫d3xV(r′+x)e−iq⋅x=eiq⋅r′V(q).

(−ω2+k2+m2)Gr′​(k;ω)+2eω∫(2π)3d3k′​Vr′​(k−k′)Gr′​(k′;ω)=1.​
(18)
This is a source-position-dependent Fourier Green function. Dropping the explicit r′ subscripts recovers the requested notation with exactly the potential transform defined in the source. No assumption of a homogeneous potential is needed. The factor (2π)−3 and the source-position phase are necessary for the stated Fourier convention.
