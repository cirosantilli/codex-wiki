integration by parts in the action, with the variation vanishing on its boundary, yields δS=∫d4x(∂μ​Fμν)δAν​. Independent variations of the four components therefore give ∂μ​Fμν=0. The time component is ∇⋅E=0; the spatial components are ∇×B−∂t​E=0. These are the two source-free Maxwell equations obtained dynamically. The other two, ∇⋅B=0 and ∂t​B+∇×E=0, follow identically from F=dA, through the Electromagnetic Bianchi identity. They are not additional Euler-Lagrange field equations.[ref]
