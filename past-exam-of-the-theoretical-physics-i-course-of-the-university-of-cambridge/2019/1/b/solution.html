There is a sign inconsistency in the original PDF: varying its action gives a plus sign for the spatially projected magnetic term, whereas its displayed target equation has a minus sign. The following Lagrange multiplier for antisymmetry calculation derives the result of the action as printed.
Temporarily vary all sixteen entries Mμν independently and add
Lλ​=21​λμν​(Mμν+Mνμ),λμν​=λνμ​.
(4)
Variation of the symmetric Lagrange multiplier imposes Mμν+Mνμ=0. Define
wμ​=uρ​Mρμ​,Cμν​=hμρ​hνσ​Mρσ.
(5)
On this constraint surface, Cνμ​=−Cμν​. Differentiating the original contractions before imposing the constraint gives
∂Mμν∂​(uα​uβ​MλαMβλ​)​M=−MT​=uν​wμ​−uμ​wν​,
(6)
and
∂Mμν∂​(hαρ​hβσ​MαβMσρ)​M=−MT​=−2Cμν​.
(7)
In particular, the reversed order σρ in the last factor of the action matters. The unrestricted Euler-Lagrange field equation, evaluated on the antisymmetry constraint, is
0=21​Fμν​−2κ1​(uν​wμ​−uμ​wν​)+2χ1​Cμν​+λμν​.
(8)
Subtract its index-transposed equation to eliminate the symmetric multiplier:
0=Fμν​−κ1​(uν​uρ​Mρμ​−uμ​uρ​Mρν​)+χ1​hμρ​hνσ​Mρσ.​
(9)
A direct check is to restrict the action to a rest-frame magnetic component M12=m, M21=−m: its quadratic contribution is +m2/(2χ), whose derivative has a positive sign. To obtain the PDF's target equation instead, one must reverse the sign of that quadratic term in the action, or replace its last Mσρ by Mρσ. These are alternative repairs; changing conventions cannot make the printed action and printed equation agree.
