At ε=0 the algebraic poles are ω±(0)​=±1​; a retarded limiting prescription displaces them infinitesimally below the real axis. Set ωs​=s+δs​, with s=±1 and δs​=O(ε). The denominator becomes
1−(s+δs​)2−iε(s+δs​)3=−2sδs​−iεs3+O(ε2).
(50)
Consequently
ω±​=±1−2iε​+O(ε2)​.
(51)
For positive ε these two poles move below the real axis, giving slowly damped oscillations. For negative ε they move above it and the slow oscillations grow. This is only the slow part of the radiation-reaction oscillator pole structure; the third pole must still be examined.
