For the second symmetry, θ↦θ+ϵ and φ↦φ+ϵ, so the Noether theorem gives the sum of their conjugate momenta. Independent rotations are not symmetries of the spring coupling.
There are also discrete symmetries: simultaneous reflection (θ,φ)↦(−θ,−φ), and time reversal in classical mechanics, which reverses both angular velocities. These leave the dynamics invariant but provide no additional infinitesimal Noether charges. Combined with common rotations, the reflections give the full planar orthogonal symmetry of the angular geometry. If I1​=I2​, interchange of the two angular coordinates is an additional discrete symmetry of the reduced dynamics.
