Above the threshold, c2kj2​>1/(4τ2), the square root in part (d) is real. Both fields oscillate, but the first has envelope e−t/(2τ)​ and the second e+t/(2τ)​. Thus the first is damped and the second amplified.
Below the threshold, set σj​=1/(4τ2)−c2kj2​​. The temporal factors are
ϕ1​: e[−1/(2τ)±σ1​]t,ϕ2​: e[+1/(2τ)±σ2​]t.​
(57)
They are nonoscillatory. For nonzero kj​, 0<σj​<1/(2τ), so both first-field branches decay and both second-field branches grow. At kj​=0, one branch is constant; the other is e−t/τ for ϕ1​ or et/τ for ϕ2​.
At equality, the repeated temporal root gives (A+Bt)e−t/(2τ)​ for the first field and (C+Dt)et/(2τ)​ for the second. The full two-field system is generically unstable on both sides because of the amplified sector. This is compatible with its autonomous Lagrangian: the total conserved energy is an indefinite quadratic form, rather than a positive energy forcing every component to remain bounded.
