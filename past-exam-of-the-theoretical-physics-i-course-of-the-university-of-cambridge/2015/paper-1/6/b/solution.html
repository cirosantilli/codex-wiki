At S=0 take sinhx/x→1. A normalized solid-angle measure would remove the factor (4π)N without changing the self-consistency equation. These are three-component classical spins on a two-dimensional positional lattice, not spins constrained to a plane.
The PDF's hint is a correct angular-integration identity for a term with coefficient βJ/2. That coefficient is not the full single-site molecular field after summing all four neighbors and decoupling both entries of each bond. The net coefficient is βzc​J, as follows from the explicitly stated Hamiltonian; inserting the hint's coefficient as the total field would lose the coordination and double-counting factors.
