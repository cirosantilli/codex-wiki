Kerr metric

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
The Kerr metric is the stationary axisymmetric vacuum metric of a rotating mass. Parameters M and a determine whether its geometry has the horizons of a Kerr black hole; the metric also admits over-extremal parameters with no horizon.
