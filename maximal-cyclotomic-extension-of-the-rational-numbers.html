Maximal cyclotomic extension of the rational numbers

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Cyclotomic polynomial Cyclotomic field Maximal cyclotomic extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The maximal cyclotomic extension of Q is a proper subfield of Q​. Every finite subextension inside a cyclotomic field is Galois and abelian, whereas Q(32​)/Q is not normal.
