Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity (Hi(F(r−i))=0 (i>0))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Sheaf cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A coherent sheaf F on PN is r-regular if Hi(PN,F(r−i))=0 for every i>0. It is then (r+1)-regular and F(r) is globally generated. Persistence follows by induction with a hyperplane avoiding the associated points and its restriction exact sequence. A sheaf on an embedded projective scheme is tested after pushforward to projective space.
