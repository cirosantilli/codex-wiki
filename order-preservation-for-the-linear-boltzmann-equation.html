Order preservation for the linear Boltzmann equation (f1,0​≥f2,0​⟹f1,t​≥f2,t​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Boltzmann equation Linear Boltzmann equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
Nonnegative gain kernels and positive damping preserve pointwise order. The Volterra series for the linear Boltzmann equation has nonnegative terms when its initial datum is nonnegative. Applying this to the difference of two solutions proves comparison, with uniqueness identifying that difference with the positive series. This order property is distinct from positivity of a quadratic form on a Hilbert space.
