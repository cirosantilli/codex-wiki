OurBigBook.com Words: 83
Orient the planar material curve C(t) counterclockwise, and let t and n be its unit tangent and outward unit normal vector. The circulation and outward volume flux are
Γ(t)=∮C(t)​u⋅tds,M(t)=∮C(t)​u⋅nds.​
(17)
For u=(u,v) and this orientation, tds=(dx,dy) and nds=(dy,−dx), giving
Γ=∮C​(udx+vdy),M=∮C​(udy−vdx).
(18)
Here M is the instantaneous flux of the velocity field, rather than transport relative to the moving material curve; that relative transport is zero. If the enclosed region contains no source and the velocity field is smooth there, incompressibility and the planar divergence theorem imply M=0.
