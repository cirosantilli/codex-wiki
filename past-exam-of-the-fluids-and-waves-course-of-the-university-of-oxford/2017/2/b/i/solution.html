For planar incompressible flow and irrotational flow, combine a velocity potential and a streamfunction into the complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow:
w(z)=ϕ(x,y)+iψ(x,y),z=x+iy.​
(24)
The chosen conventions give
u=ϕx​=ψy​,v=ϕy​=−ψx​.
(25)
These are the Cauchy-Riemann equations, so w is a holomorphic function wherever the flow is regular and a consistent local potential is chosen. Its complex derivative is the complex velocity
dzdw​=ϕx​+iψx​=u−iv.​
(26)
A spatially uniform flow has w=(U−iV)z+ a constant. The following contour integral identities also apply to nonuniform potential flow, including the specified line vortex. A complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow may be multivalued around a source or vortex even though its derivative is single-valued.
