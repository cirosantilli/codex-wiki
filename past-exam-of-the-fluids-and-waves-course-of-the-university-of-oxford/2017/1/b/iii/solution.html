The streamlines are the level curves of the streamfunction, so θ is constant: radial rays directed outwards from the origin. Their arrows point outwards because Q>0. A complete straight line through the origin comprises two distinct rays separated by the excluded line source. Equivalently, rθ˙=uθ​=0 and r˙=Q/(2πr)>0 along each pathline; the steady flow makes these the same curves as the streamlines.
