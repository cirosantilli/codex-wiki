b∗​=2U1+1+4U2​​>1​.

The other root is negative, so the horizontal force points in the positive x direction for 1<b<b∗​, vanishes at b=b∗​, and points in the negative x direction for b>b∗​. In particular, the nearby line source pulls the cylinder towards itself as b↓1, while at large b the horizontal force is Fx​∼−2πρU/b2.
