The physical complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow is w(z)=W(g(z)). Applying the chain rule gives the complex velocity
u−iv=w′(z)=2πiΓ​(1−z21​)[z+z−1−c−c−11​−z+z−1−cˉ−cˉ−11​]​.
(31)
The physical velocity is (Rew′,−Imw′). A useful equivalent expression follows from
g(z)−g(c)=z(z−c)(z−c−1)​.
(32)
Factoring the image expression in the same way makes the −1/z terms cancel, leaving
u−iv=2πiΓ​[z−c1​+z−c−11​−z−cˉ1​−z−cˉ−11​]​.
(33)
This represents the physical line vortex at c, an image line vortex of the same strength at c−1, and image point vortices of opposite strength at cˉ and cˉ−1. All three image locations are outside the fluid region. Both boundary pieces inherit the constant streamfunction of the real axis in the mapped complex plane.
