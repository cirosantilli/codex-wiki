Orient C counterclockwise around the body. For an arc element ds, the body-outward unit normal vector obeys nds=(dy,−dx). The pressure acts normally into the body, so its force per unit length in the suppressed third direction is
dF=−pnds=(−pdy,pdx).
(18)
Integrating gives
Fx​=−∮C​pdy,Fy​=∮C​pdx.​
(19)
Taking positive torque to mean counterclockwise about the origin, the infinitesimal moment is dM=xdFy​−ydFx​=p(xdx+ydy). Hence
M=∮C​p(xdx+ydy).​
(20)
All three expressions are per unit span. They are the normal-pressure contributions assumed by the inviscid model; a viscous traction would require extra terms. A constant pressure contributes zero net force and torque on a closed body, since ∮dx=∮dy=∮d(x2+y2)=0.
