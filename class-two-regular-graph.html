Class two regular graph

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Degree (graph theory) Regular graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
An r-regular graph is class two when its edge chromatic number exceeds r. For a finite simple graph, Vizing's theorem makes that edge chromatic number exactly r+1. An r-colour proper edge colouring of an r-regular graph would partition its edges into r perfect matchings, since every vertex would see each colour exactly once.
