Nondegenerate bilinear form

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Bilinear form
OurBigBook.com Words: 477 Articles: 10
A bilinear form B on a finite-dimensional vector space is nondegenerate when
B(v,w)=0 for every v⟹w=0,
(2)
equivalently when w↦B(−,w) is an isomorphism from V to its dual space.
