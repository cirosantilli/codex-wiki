Nucleoid

 Home Biology Biological cell
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A nucleoid is the region occupied by chromosome DNA in a prokaryotic biological cell. It has no surrounding nuclear envelope, but its DNA is organized rather than freely spread at random. See OpenStax Biology 2e, 4.2: the nucleoid.
