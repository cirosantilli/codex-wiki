Neon-22 neutron excess from nitrogen-14 (X22​=1422​X14​,Ye​=21​−14X14​​)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Nucleosynthesis Stellar nucleosynthesis
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
Two alpha captures and an intervening beta-plus decay turn each nitrogen-14 nucleus into neon-22, incorporating eight baryons from helium and converting one proton to a neutron. With no loss or dilution and nuclear mass defects neglected, a nitrogen-14 mass fraction X14​ therefore yields neon-22 mass fraction (22/14)X14​, not X14​. In otherwise symmetric carbon-oxygen matter this gives the displayed electron fraction in stellar matter. A final neon mass fraction and a pre-helium-burning metallicity must not silently be treated as identical.
