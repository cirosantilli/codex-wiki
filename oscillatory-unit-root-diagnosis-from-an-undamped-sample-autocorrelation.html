Oscillatory unit-root diagnosis from an undamped sample autocorrelation (Φosc​(B)=1−2cosωB+B2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 57
A large, slowly damping oscillatory sample autocorrelation function suggests autoregressive zeros near a conjugate pair on the unit circle. For period s, the pair is near e±2πi/s, with minimal real filter 1−2cos(2π/s)B+B2. Such a plot is evidence, not proof: a finite sample cannot distinguish a near-unit stationary model or a random sinusoid solely from the shape.
