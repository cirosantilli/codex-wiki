Volume element (dV=∣detDΦ∣du1​⋯dun​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Integral Multiple integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
Under a smooth locally invertible coordinate map Φ into Euclidean space, the positive volume element in a multiple integral is multiplied by the absolute Jacobian determinant. In three-dimensional spherical coordinates, dV=r2sinθdrdθdφ; in cylindrical coordinates, dV=rdrdθdz. The absolute value distinguishes positive integration measure from a signed orientation convention.
