G protein

 Home Biology Protein
OurBigBook.com Words: 211 Articles: 17
A G protein is a protein switch whose activity depends on the bound guanine nucleotide. The three-subunit G proteins coupled to G protein-coupled receptors relay activation to other proteins; they are distinct from the receptors themselves. See the Alberts reference under G protein-coupled receptor.
