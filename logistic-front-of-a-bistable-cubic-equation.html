Logistic front of a bistable cubic equation (U=[1+e(x−ct−q0​)/2D​]−1)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Diffusion equation Reaction–diffusion system Bistable cubic reaction-diffusion equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
For the decreasing front connecting 1 to 0, the balanced stationary travelling-wave reduction of a reaction-diffusion system has first integral DUx2​/2−U2(1−U)2/4=0. Choosing the negative derivative gives Ux​=−U(1−U)/2D​ and hence U=[1+e(x−q)/2D​]−1. This logistic profile remains an exact travelling wave when the reaction parameter differs from 1/2: using z=(x−ct−q0​)/D​, its derivative is Uz​=−U(1−U)/2​, and the wave equation is satisfied for c=D/2​(1−2r). Thus the speed changes with the potential bias while the shape, apart from translation, does not.
