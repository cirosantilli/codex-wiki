Self-similar blast wave

Words: 306 Articles: 4
Compressible flow OurBigBook.com
A self-similar blast wave is an expanding shock wave whose post-shock profiles retain their shape in a rescaled radius or distance. A fixed explosion energy, ambient mass density and geometry set the front scaling through conservation of total energy. The dimensionless coefficient requires the profiles and an energy-normalization convention.

Thin-shell approximation for a spherical blast wave (α=1/2)

Words: 80
Self-similar blast wave OurBigBook.com
Let a strong shock wave of radius R and speed V sweep a uniform mass density ρ0​ into a shell of mass 4πρ0​R3/3 and speed 2V/(γ+1). If the cavity pressure is α times the immediate post-shock pressure, shell momentum balance gives RV˙=3(α−1)V2. Constant total energy gives R3V2=constant and hence α=1/2. Including shell internal and kinetic energy, and treating the cavity volume as 4πR3/3 to thin-shell accuracy, yields R=ξ(E/ρ0​)1/5t2/5 with ξ5=75(γ−1)(γ+1)2/[16π(5γ−3)]. This coefficient is approximate; resolving the shell's finite volume changes it.

Polytropic flow (p=Kργ)

Words: 40
Compressible flow OurBigBook.com
A polytropic flow uses a polytropic equation of state. When K and the adiabatic exponent are constant, a smooth streamline has specific enthalpy h=γKργ−1/(γ−1) and adiabatic sound speed cs2​=γKργ−1. Spatially varying entropy can make K depend on streamline; the constant-K spherical wind is a special case.

Isothermal shock (ρ2​/ρ1​=M12​)

Words: 81 Articles: 1
Compressible flow OurBigBook.com
An isothermal shock joins two states at the same temperature and isothermal sound speed. Mass and momentum conservation imply u1​u2​=cs2​ for a nontrivial planar jump, so the compression ratio is the upstream isothermal Mach number squared. Heat exchange replaces adiabatic energy conservation across the gas alone.

Cooling across an isothermal shock (LA​=j(u12​−u22​)/2)

Words: 40
Isothermal shock OurBigBook.com
The specific enthalpy is equal on both sides of an isothermal perfect-gas shock. The energy removed per unit area per unit time is j(u12​−u22​)/2, with j>0 the normal mass flux. Net cooling therefore selects compression shocks; an expansion jump requires heating.

Spherical polytropic flow with adiabatic exponent three halves

Words: 122
Compressible flow OurBigBook.com
For steady spherical isentropic flow with p=Kρ3/2 around a point mass, put j=r2ρv>0 and E=v2/2+2c2−GM/r>0. Eliminating the mass density gives c2=[j(3K/2)2]2/5r−4/5M−2/5.
