The compact quotient for the Gamma 0 congruence subgroup at level two has projective index three, two modular cusps of cusp widths one and two, one order-two elliptic orbit and no order-three elliptic orbit. It has genus zero. It differs from the level-two principal-congruence curve for Γ(2), which has three modular cusps.
