Class number one for Q of cube root six (hQ(36​)​=1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Ideal class group Minkowski's bound
OurBigBook.com Words: 99
For α3=6, the ring of integers is Z[α] and its field discriminant is −972. The Minkowski bound for ideal classes is 163​/π<9. Prime ideals of norm at most eight lie above 2,3,5,7. The primes above two, three and the degree-one prime above five are generated by α−2, 3+2α+α2 and α−1, of norm magnitudes 2,3,5. At seven the residue roots are 3,5,6; the primes at roots six and three are generated by α+1 and α2+α−5, both of norm seven. Their product with the remaining prime is (7), making that prime principal too. Every possible Minkowski representative is therefore principal.
