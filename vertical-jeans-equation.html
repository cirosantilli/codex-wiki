Vertical Jeans equation (∂z​(nσz2​)+R−1∂R​(RnvR​vz​​)=−n∂z​Φ)

 ... Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics Collisionless Boltzmann equation Jeans equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
For a steady axisymmetric stellar population with no mean vertical motion, the vertical Jeans equation balances the gradient of the vertical random-motion stress and the radial gradient of the tilted velocity ellipsoid against the vertical Newtonian gravitational field. Neglecting the mixed moment gives ∂z​(nσz2​)=−n∂z​Φ. A tracer density n need not equal the density sourcing the gravitational potential.
