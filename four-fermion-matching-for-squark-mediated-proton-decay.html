Four-fermion matching for squark-mediated proton decay (C6​=λ′λ′′∗/Md2​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 82
The R-parity violation vertices from LQD and UDD can both contain the same down-type squark. Write its linear couplings as −dA−d∗A† and its mass term as −M2∣d∣2. Eliminating it gives A†A/M2. The cross term is (λ′λ′′∗/M2)(νdL​−euL​)(uc†dc†)+h.c., with the color Levi-Civita symbol contracting the three quarks. It changes baryon number and lepton number together and supplies the dimension-six proton-decay operator for p→e+π0. The two down-flavor indices in UDD must differ, so strange- or bottom-squark exchange is allowed but identical down flavors vanish.
