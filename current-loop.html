Current loop (m=IA)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential Magnetic dipole moment
OurBigBook.com Words: 220 Articles: 1
A current loop is a closed path carrying electric current. For a thin wire following a closed curve C, the volume integral defining its magnetic dipole moment becomes a line integral:
m=2I​∮C​r×dr=IA,A=21​∮C​r×dr.
(84)
The vector area A is An for a planar current loop. For a nonplanar current loop, use the vector area, rather than the scalar area of an arbitrarily chosen spanning surface. Reversing the electric current reverses m without changing the geometry. [ref].
For N closely spaced identical turns carrying the same electric current, the magnetic dipole moments add: m=NIAn. Each turn contributes its own vector area; unequal areas or differently oriented turns require a vector sum. As a calculated example, twelve circular turns of radius R=3.0cm carrying I=0.40A give m=NIπR2=1.36×10−2Am2. This is an idealized example, not an experimental measurement.
