Parity-odd scalar interaction

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum field theory Discrete symmetry in quantum field theory Parity symmetry in quantum field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A parity-odd scalar interaction is rotationally invariant but changes sign under spatial reflection. It must contain a pseudoscalar contraction, such as a Levi-Civita symbol contracted with three spatial gradients.
