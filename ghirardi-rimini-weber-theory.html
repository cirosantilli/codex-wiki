Ghirardi-Rimini-Weber theory

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Foundations of quantum mechanics Objective-collapse theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 251 Articles: 5
The Ghirardi--Rimini--Weber theory modifies unitary time evolution by random Gaussian position localizations. Each constituent collapses at a small fixed rate, while the rate for an entangled macroscopic body grows with its number of constituents.
