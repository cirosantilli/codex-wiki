Meromorphic section of a holomorphic line bundle

 ... Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Holomorphic vector bundle Holomorphic line bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
In a holomorphic local frame, a meromorphic section has a meromorphic scalar coefficient; the coefficients transform by the bundle's transition functions of a vector bundle. A nonzero meromorphic section has a divisor on a complex manifold recording its zero and pole orders.
