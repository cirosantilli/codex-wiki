Galois cohomology (Hi(G,M))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 571 Articles: 9
Galois cohomology is the group cohomology of a Galois group acting on a module. For a discrete G-module M, the first group is the quotient of crossed homomorphisms c(στ)=c(σ)+σc(τ) by maps c(σ)=σm−m.
