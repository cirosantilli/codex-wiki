Impulsively kicked sinusoidal shear dispersion (κe​=κ+4TU12​​coth(κm2T/2))

When velocity consists of impulses U1​ spaced by T, the periodic corrector jumps by U1​ and decays between kicks. With a=κm2, g(t)=U1​e−at/(1−e−aT) for 0<t<T. Integrating g2 in the effective diffusivity of a periodic sinusoidal shear formula yields the displayed result. For aT≫1, independent transverse phases give longitudinal variance U12​/2 per kick and enhancement U12​/(4T). For aT≪1, the enhancement is U12​/(2κm2T2) to leading order, the steady-shear result for average speed U1​/T. The zero-diffusivity limit does not commute with the long-time limit: without transverse diffusion the displacement is ballistic.
