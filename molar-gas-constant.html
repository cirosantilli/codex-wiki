Molar gas constant (R=NA​kB​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Ideal gas
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The molar gas constant is the product of the Avogadro constant and the Boltzmann constant. It converts the particle-number form of the ideal gas law, pV=NkB​T, into the amount-of-substance form pV=nRT, using N=nNA​ with n measured in moles.
