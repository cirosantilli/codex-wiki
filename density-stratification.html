Density stratification

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 361 Articles: 8
Density stratification is variation of a fluid's mass density with height. Its stable or unstable character depends on whether the buoyancy force restores or amplifies a displaced parcel.
