Lambda definition of primitive recursion

 ... Foundations of mathematics Computability theory Lambda calculus Church encoding Church numeral Lambda-definable function
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
Using a fixed-point combinator, Church Boolean conditionals, a zero test, and a predecessor term, lambda-definable g and h give
Rx0=g(x),Rx(n+1)=h(x,n,Rxn).
(27)
Normal-order beta reduction evaluates only the selected conditional branch, so this term implements primitive recursion on Church numerals.
