Magnetic field intensity (H)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Bound charge and bound current Magnetization
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
The magnetic field intensity is H=B/μ0​−M and obeys ∇×H=Jfree​ in magnetostatics. In a linear isotropic medium, B=μH.
