Orient the square positively. Its enclosed poles at the integers −m,…,m have residues 1/(n2−b2). Since b is nonintegral, the additional poles b and −b are distinct from those integers and from one another. Their residues are g(b)/(2b) and g(−b)/(−2b), both equal to g(b)/(2b) because g is odd. Their combined contribution is therefore πcot(πb)/b.
