A subset B⊆C, with its subspace topology, is compact when every open cover of B has a finite subcover. The Heine-Borel theorem in the complex plane, identified with R2, says
B compact⟺B closed and bounded.​
(42)
Closedness is in the ambient complex plane. For a continuous real-valued function on a nonempty compact set, the supremum and infimum are finite and attained.
