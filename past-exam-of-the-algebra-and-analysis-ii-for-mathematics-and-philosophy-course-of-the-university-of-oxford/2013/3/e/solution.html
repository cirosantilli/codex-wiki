Factorization over Q gives
R1​≅Q×Q,R2​≅Q(i),R3​≅Q[ε]/(ε2),ε=x−1.
(27)
For R1​, the factors x−1 and x+1 are coprime. The nonzero elements (1,0) and (0,1) multiply to zero, so the ring is neither an integral domain nor a field. Squaring coordinatewise shows it has no nonzero square-zero element.
For R2​, the polynomial x2+1 has no rational root and is irreducible of degree two over Q. The quotient is consequently a field, hence an integral domain, and has no nonzero square-zero element.
For R3​, the class ε is nonzero by its degree, but ε2=0. Thus this ring is neither an integral domain nor a field, and does have a nonzero square-zero element.
