The Burnside lemma states that the number of orbits of a finite group action is
∣G∣−1∑g∈G​∣Fix(g)∣.
(4)
For m=2n−1≥3, the dihedral group consists of m rotations rj by 2πj/m, and m reflections rjs, each across the line through one vertex and the midpoint of the opposite edge. Thus ∣G∣=2m=4n−2.
For the pentagon, the identity fixes v5e5 colourings. Each of the four nonidentity rotations has a single cycle on vertices and a single cycle on edges, fixing ve colourings. Each of the five reflections has one fixed vertex and two vertex pairs, and one fixed edge and two edge pairs, fixing v3e3 colourings. Applying the Burnside lemma to simultaneous vertex and edge colourings gives
10v5e5+4ve+5v3e3​.​
(5)
Vertex colours and edge colours may be chosen independently; each symmetry must preserve both assignments.
