The two primal constraints are equalities, so let the two dual variables u,v be free. Primal feasibility restricts x to the nonnegative orthant, giving
minimize 300(u+v),⎩⎨⎧​2u+8v≥4,3u+v≥2,4u+v≥3,2u+5v≥5.​
(92)
Thus the feasible region lies above all four lines:
v≥max{1/2−u/4, 2−3u, 3−4u, 1−2u/5}.
(93)
Its lower boundary follows the third line up to (5/9,7/9), the fourth line from there to (10/3,−1/3), and then the first line. The second line never determines the envelope. Along these three pieces u+v respectively has slopes −3, 3/5, and 3/4, so its unique minimum is at the first corner:
u=95​,v=97​,300(u+v)=400.​
(94)
At this dual point the first two inequalities are strict, while the last two are equalities. complementary slackness therefore suggests x1​=x2​=0. The remaining primal equations become 4x3​+2x4​=300, x3​+5x4​=300, giving x3​=x4​=50. This is nonnegative and satisfies both equations. Its objective function is 3⋅50+5⋅50=400, equal to the feasible dual value. weak duality proves
x∗=(0,0,50,50),z∗=400.​
(95)
No nonnegativity restriction was imposed on the dual variables; the negative-v second corner is part of the feasible boundary, even though it is not optimal.
