The marginal probability density function is fY​(y)=∫R​fX,Y​(x,y)dx. For y with fY​(y)>0, the conditional density is
fX∣Y=y​(x)=fY​(y)fX,Y​(x,y)​.​
(47)
If the conditional first absolute moment is finite, the conditional expectation is
E[X∣Y=y]=∫R​xfY​(y)fX,Y​(x,y)​dx.​
(48)
Conditioning here uses a density, since the individual event Y=y normally has probability zero; a conditional law is specified only up to marginal-null values of y.
