A tangent vector to M at p is a velocity γ′(0) of a smooth curve γ:(−ε,ε)→M with γ(0)=p. The tangent space is the set of all such velocities:
Tp​M={γ′(0):γ(0)=p, γ a smooth curve in M}.​
(15)
It is a k-dimensional linear subspace of Rn; for a local parametrization ϕ, it is the image of the injective derivative Dϕ at the corresponding parameter point.
