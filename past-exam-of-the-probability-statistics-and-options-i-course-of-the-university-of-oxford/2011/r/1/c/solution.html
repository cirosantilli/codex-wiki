Within each connected component with ni​ vertices, grow a tree by starting at one vertex and repeatedly adding an edge to an unreached vertex. Connectedness guarantees such an edge until every vertex has been reached. Exactly ni​−1 edges are added, so the component contains at least that many edges. Summing over the k components gives
m≥i=1∑k​(ni​−1)=n−k.​

The selected trees form a spanning forest, and equality holds precisely when the original graph itself is a forest.
