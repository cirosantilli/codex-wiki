For sigma-finite measures, Tonelli's theorem states that the double integral of a nonnegative measurable function equals either iterated integral, allowing +∞. Fubini's theorem states that if a measurable function is absolutely integrable, its sections are integrable almost everywhere, both iterated integrals exist, and they equal the double integral.
