Use the convention that positive vortex strength is counterclockwise and complex velocity is u−iv. A complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow for the superposed uniform flow and two point vortices is
W(z)=Uz+2πiΓ​log(z−a)−2πiΓ​log(z+a).​
(32)
Local choices of logarithm branch change no velocity. Differentiation gives
W′(z)=U−π(z2−a2)iΓa​.
(33)
For nonzero vortex strength and U>0, its two zeros are
z±​=±a2+πUiΓa​​.​
(34)
The number under the square root is nonzero, so these are distinct stagnation points, neither at a vortex.
A free point vortex moves with the velocity induced by the other vortex and the background, omitting its own singular field. At either vortex, the other induces vertical velocity −Γ/(4πa), while the background contributes (U,0). Their velocities are therefore equal. Their separation stays (2a,0), making this induced velocity constant for all time. Both travel along parallel straight lines with
V=(U,−4πaΓ​),∣V∣=U2+16π2a2Γ2​​.​
(35)
