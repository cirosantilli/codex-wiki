Decode the Prüfer code by repeatedly attaching the smallest label absent from the remaining code to its first entry. This produces the successive edges 1−2, 3−6, 4−2, 5−2, followed by the final edge 2−6. Thus
E={{1,2},{3,6},{2,4},{2,5},{2,6}}.​
