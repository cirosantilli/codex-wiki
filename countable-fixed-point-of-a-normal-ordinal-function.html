Countable fixed point of a normal ordinal function (α=supn<ω​Φn(0))

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Ordinal Normal function on an ordinal Fixed point of a normal ordinal function
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
If a normal function on an ordinal begins at a countable value and preserves countability from Φ(ξ) to Φ(ξ+1), transfinite induction shows it maps all countable ordinals to countable ordinals. At countable limits this uses countable unions of countable sets, hence axiom of countable choice in the usual proof. Iteration from zero either stabilizes at a countable fixed point or has a countable limit fixed by continuity. Applying this to ωα gives the countable epsilon number ϵ0​.
