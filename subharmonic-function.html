Subharmonic function

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Harmonic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 699 Articles: 11
A twice differentiable function is strictly subharmonic where Δu>0. It cannot have an interior local maximum because the Hessian at such a maximum is negative semidefinite.
