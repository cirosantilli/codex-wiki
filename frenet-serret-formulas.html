Frenet-Serret formulas

For a unit-speed space curve with nonzero curvature, t=α˙, κ=∣t˙∣, n=t˙/κ, and b=t×n. With the torsion convention used here,
t˙=κn,n˙=−κt+τb,b˙=−τn.
