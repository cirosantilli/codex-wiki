Logical disjunction (∨)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Proposition Logical connective
OurBigBook.com Words: 41 Articles: 1
The disjunction A∨B holds when at least one of A and B holds. Constructively, a proof records which disjunct holds and provides its proof.
