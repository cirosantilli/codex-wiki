Integral-field spectrograph volume scaling

 ... Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Spectrometer Spectrograph Integral field spectrograph
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
With fixed spectral resolving power, wavelength interval, diffraction grating geometry, photodetector sampling and collimator focal ratio, a beam diameter scales as the spaxel width θ, but the optical camera focal length stays fixed. A simple on-axis beam-envelope model therefore gives collimator and disperser volumes proportional to θ3 and optical camera volume proportional to θ2. With a fixed number of spaxels per photodetector, replication multiplies these volumes by M∝θ−2. A fixed photodetector field adds transverse clearances and changes the small-θ limit; these exponents are not universal mechanical-volume laws.
