Holomorphic splitting of a twistor patching matrix (F=HH−1)

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Twistor theory Twistor correspondence Penrose-Ward correspondence
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
A bundle trivial on a twistor line has a global holomorphic bundle frame there. Comparing it with the given two patch bundle frames produces invertible matrices H,H, holomorphic on their respective affine patches, with F=HH−1. The obstruction to varying such a bundle frame locally with space-time is H1(CP1,O)n2=0, so the splitting factors can be chosen holomorphically in a neighbourhood. If two splittings differ by H↦Hg and H↦Hg, then g is holomorphic on the compact complex projective line and hence independent of its projective coordinate. It is the space-time gauge freedom.
