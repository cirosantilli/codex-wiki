Nearly Hamiltonian system (z˙=J∇H(z)+ϵR(z))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system
OurBigBook.com Words: 480 Articles: 7
A nearly Hamiltonian system is a weak perturbation of a Hamiltonian system. Along its paths, H˙=ϵ∇H⋅R. Averaging this drift over an unperturbed periodic orbit is the energy balance method; a simple zero of the averaged drift selects a persistent nearby periodic orbit for sufficiently small ϵ. A multiple zero requires higher-order analysis.
