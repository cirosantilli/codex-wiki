Galilean boost energy of an interacting gas (ΔE=Np02​/(2m))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Galilean transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
For a resting uniform continuum gas with pair interactions depending on relative positions, shifting every particle momentum by p0​ leaves interaction energy unchanged and changes the microscopic kinetic energy by p0​⋅P/m+Np02​/(2m). The resting ground state has total momentum P=0. The resulting boost energy uses the bare mass even in an interacting Fermi liquid; matching this with the Landau quasiparticle energy functional constrains its interaction kernel.
