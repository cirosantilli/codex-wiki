Volume derivative form of the heat-capacity difference (Cp​−CV​=[(∂U/∂V)T​+p](∂V/∂T)p​)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Enthalpy Heat capacity at constant pressure Difference between constant-pressure and constant-volume heat capacities
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For a fixed-composition simple compressible thermodynamic system with smooth equations of state, write the internal energy as U(T,V) and the enthalpy as H=U+pV. Differentiating H at constant pressure and applying the multivariable chain rule gives
Cp​=(∂T∂U​)p​+p(∂T∂V​)p​,(∂T∂U​)p​=(∂T∂U​)V​+(∂V∂U​)T​(∂T∂V​)p​.
(17)
Subtracting the heat capacity at constant volume proves the displayed identity. For an ideal gas, (∂U/∂V)T​=0 and (∂V/∂T)p​=NkB​/p, so Cp​−CV​=NkB​. For a nonideal material the internal energy change with volume contributes as well as expansion work.
