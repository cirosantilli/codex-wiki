Tail event

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Independent sigma-algebras Tail sigma-algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
A tail event is measurable with respect to a tail sigma-algebra, so changing finitely many coordinates does not affect whether it occurs.
