Superallowed beta decay

 ... Physics Nuclear physics Radioactive decay Beta decay Beta decay transition Allowed beta decay
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
An especially strong allowed beta decay, conventionally including pure Fermi transitions between 0+ isobaric analog states. Those states have closely related spatial and spin structure, so the isospin ladder operator has a large overlap. A 0+→0+ assignment alone fixes the allowed Fermi character, but does not establish analog-state overlap or a large rate; a small comparative half-life supplies additional evidence.
