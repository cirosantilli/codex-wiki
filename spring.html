Spring (F=kx)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Hooke's law
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A linear spring stores elastic energy kx2/2 when displaced by x from its relaxed position. The external force maintaining that displacement is kx; its restoring force on the moving attachment has the opposite sign. Its stiffness k has units force per length.
