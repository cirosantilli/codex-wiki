Lubin–Tate formal group (Ff​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field
OurBigBook.com Words: 466 Articles: 10
A Lubin–Tate formal group associated with a Lubin–Tate series f is the unique one-dimensional commutative formal group law Ff​ over the valuation ring OK​ for which f is an endomorphism with linear coefficient the chosen uniformizer π. The Lubin–Tate functional equation lemma constructs the law and the endomorphisms [a]f​ for every a∈OK​, with linear term aX. Their addition is given by the law, and their multiplication by composition. Thus the valuation ring acts on the group; [π]f​=f.
