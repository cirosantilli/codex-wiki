Lubin–Tate formal group (Ff​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field
OurBigBook.com Words: 205 Articles: 5
For a uniformizer π of a local field K with residue-field cardinality q, a Lubin–Tate series is a series f(X)∈OK​[[X]] satisfying f(X)≡πX(modX2) and f(X)≡Xq(modπ). It determines a unique one-dimensional commutative formal group law Ff​ on which every a∈OK​ acts through an endomorphism [a]f​ with linear term aX.
