Skeleton of a category

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Skeletal category
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
A skeleton of C is a full subcategory with exactly one object from each isomorphism class. The axiom of choice supplies skeletons of small categories. Conversely, if every small category has a skeletal equivalent, apply this to the groupoid on ∐i​Ai​ with exactly one morphism between objects in the same nonempty fibre. A quasi-inverse from the skeletal category selects one object per fibre, giving a choice function. This converse uses an equivalence of categories with specified quasi-inverse functors, not just the existence of a full essentially surjective functor in one direction.
