Lyman limit system (NHI​≳1017.2cm−2)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Hydrogen atom Lyman-alpha line Lyman-alpha absorption
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A Lyman limit system has approximately unit or greater optical depth at the neutral-hydrogen Lyman limit near 91.2nm. The threshold follows from τ912​=NHI​σ912​ with σ912​≃6.3×10−18cm2. Its strong continuum absorption distinguishes it from ordinary weak Lyman-alpha forest lines. Classification conventions often separate damped Lyman-alpha systems as the highest-column category.
