Schumacher compression

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 489 Articles: 7
Schumacher compression is quantum source coding into the typical subspace of ρ⊗n. Asymptotically reliable compression is possible at every qubit rate above the Von Neumann entropy S(ρ).
