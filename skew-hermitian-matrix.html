Skew-Hermitian matrix

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 74 Articles: 1
A complex matrix A is skew-Hermitian when A†=−A. Its matrix exponential etA is unitary for every real t.
