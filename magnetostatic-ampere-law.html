Magnetostatic Ampère law (∮C​B⋅dℓ=μ0​Ienc​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Maxwell equations Ampère's circuital law
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
In vacuum magnetostatics, the Ampère-Maxwell equation reduces to ∇×B=μ0​J. Applying Stokes theorem gives ∮C​B⋅dℓ=μ0​Ienc​, where Ienc​ is the electric current crossing an oriented spanning surface. Time-dependent electric fields require the additional displacement current term in the Ampère-Maxwell equation.
