Generating set of a group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group
OurBigBook.com Words: 463 Articles: 9
A subset S of a group G is a generating set when every element of G is a finite product of elements of S and their inverses, equivalently when the subgroup ⟨S⟩ generated by S equals G.
