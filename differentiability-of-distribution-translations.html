Differentiability of distribution translations (dhd​τh​u=−τh​u′)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Distribution (mathematical analysis) Translation of a distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
With translation of a distribution defined by ⟨τh​u,φ⟩=⟨u,φ(⋅+h)⟩, differentiation of the test function gives dhd​τh​u=−τh​u′ in weak convergence of distributions. The Taylor theorem shows convergence in the space of test functions, including a common compact support. Thus (τ−h​u−u)/h→u′; fixing the translation convention prevents a sign error.
