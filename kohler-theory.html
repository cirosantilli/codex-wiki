Köhler theory

 Home Chemistry Atmospheric chemistry Aerosol Cloud condensation nucleus
OurBigBook.com Words: 333 Articles: 5
The equilibrium water-vapor saturation ratio above a solution droplet combines a curvature effect, which raises vapor pressure, with dissolved-solute reduction of water activity. Its maximum defines a critical supersaturation for activation. In a dilute hygroscopicity approximation using droplet diameter D and dry diameter Dd​, s(D)≃AK​/D−κDd3​/D3, where AK​=4σMw​/(RTρw​). Then sc​≃4AK3​/(27κDd3​)​. These approximate forms assume the stated diameter convention and dilute solution; composition and surface tension must be consistent.[ref]
