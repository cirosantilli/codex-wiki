For a steady Rankine vortex in a fluid of constant density ρ with gravity −gk, the Euler equations give pr​=ρuθ2​/r and pz​=−ρg. Integrating on the two sides and matching pressure at r=a gives
p={B−ρgz−21​ρΩ2(2a2−r2),B−ρgz−ρΩ2a4/(2r2),​r≤a,r>a.​
(9)
Setting a constant surface pressure yields
z={C−Ω2(2a2−r2)/(2g),C−Ω2a4/(2gr2),​r≤a,r>a.​
(10)
Thus the free surface and its radial slope match at the core edge. The central depression is Ω2a2/g, twice the depression at r=a. Bernoulli's theorem applies throughout the exterior; the rotational core instead uses the radial momentum balance above.
