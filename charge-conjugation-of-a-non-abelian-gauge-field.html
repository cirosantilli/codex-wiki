Charge conjugation of a non-Abelian gauge field (Aμ​↦−AμT​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Discrete symmetry in quantum field theory Charge conjugation
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Write a matrix-valued gauge field Aμ​=Aμa​Ta in the fundamental representation. Charge conjugation sends it to −AμT​ because reordering the fermions transposes both the spinor and internal matrices. The identity [AT,BT]=−[A,B]T then sends the gauge field strength to −FμνT​. This distinction prevents treating all color components as individually C-odd.
