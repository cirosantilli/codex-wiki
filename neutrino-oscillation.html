Neutrino oscillation

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Lepton number Neutrino
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
Neutrino oscillation is the coherent conversion between neutrino flavors caused by different propagation phases of mixed mass eigenstates. It measures mass-squared differences and mixing parameters.
