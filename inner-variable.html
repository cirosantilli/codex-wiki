Inner variable (ξ)

An inner variable is a stretched coordinate such as ξ=(x−x0​)/δ(ϵ) that remains order one inside a thin region of width δ(ϵ). Its scale is chosen by a dominant balance, rather than by the size of a coefficient alone.
For the singular perturbation problem ϵy′′+y′=0 with y(0)=0, y(1)=1, set X=x/ϵ and Yϵ​(X)=yϵ​(ϵX). The chain rule gives
dxd​=ϵ−1dXd​,ϵyϵ′′​+yϵ′​=ϵ−1(Yϵ′′​+Yϵ′​).
(6)
Thus both derivative terms survive in the inner differential equation Yϵ′′​+Yϵ′​=0. The exact domain is 0≤X≤1/ϵ, with
Yϵ​(X)=1−e−1/ϵ1−e−X​,Y0​(X)=1−e−X.
(7)
For bounded X, Yϵ​→Y0​ as ϵ→0+: the inner expansion has a nonconstant limiting profile even though the outer solution is constant. The finite-ϵ profiles nearly collapse onto Y0​ after rescaling, with their remaining difference coming from the normalization at the distant right endpoint.
