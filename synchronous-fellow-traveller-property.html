Synchronous fellow-traveller property

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Combing of a group
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Two paths satisfy the synchronous fellow-traveller property with constant K if d(p(t),q(t))≤K for all equal times, with completed paths held at their endpoints. Checking integer times suffices up to increasing K by at most two for unit-speed edge paths. A synchronous combing of a group requires a uniform constant for all adjacent endpoints.
