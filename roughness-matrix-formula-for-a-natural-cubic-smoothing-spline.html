Roughness-matrix formula for a natural cubic smoothing spline (Aλ​=(I+λK)−1)

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical inference Nonparametric statistics Nonparametric regression Smoothing spline Cubic smoothing spline
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
For distinct ordered observations xi​, let su​ be their natural cubic spline interpolant with values ui​. Its roughness is a quadratic form, ∫(su′′​)2=uTKu, where K is positive semidefinite and its nullspace consists of vectors sampled from affine functions. Minimizing penalized least squares ∥y−u∥2+λuTKu gives u=(I+λK)−1y. Thus the smoothing matrix is Aλ​=(I+λK)−1 and its effective degrees of freedom are tr(Aλ​). As λ↓0 the fit approaches interpolation and the trace approaches n; as λ→∞ it approaches the least-squares affine line and the trace approaches 2.
