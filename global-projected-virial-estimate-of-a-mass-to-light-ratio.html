Global projected virial estimate of a mass-to-light ratio (GΥ=2∫0∞​rλLdr3∫0∞​Rμσp2​dR​)

For an isolated spherical stellar system in equilibrium with constant mass-to-light ratio, the virial theorem gives 2K+W=0. Spherical averaging gives K=3πΥ∫Rμσp2​dR, while W=−4πGΥ2∫rλLdr. Dividing yields the displayed estimator, independent of the radial velocity-anisotropy parameter. It requires the full aperture and negligible boundary stress. A radially varying mass-to-light ratio or an independent dark matter halo prevents interpreting this expression as a general reconstruction of the mass profile.
