Ramification index of a holomorphic map (ep​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Riemann-Hurwitz formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 575 Articles: 8
For a nonconstant holomorphic map of Riemann surfaces, choose local coordinates centred at p and f(p). The map has the form
z⟼zep​u(z),u(0)=0.
(58)
The positive integer ep​ is its ramification index. The point is ramified exactly when ep​>1 and contributes ep​−1 to the ramification divisor.
