Normal modes of a spring-mounted pendulum cart (mML2ω4−[kML2+(m+M)MgL]ω2+kMgL=0)

A cart of mass m moves horizontally on a spring of stiffness k, carrying a simple pendulum of bob mass M and length L. Let x be cart displacement and ϕ the small angle from downward vertical. The quadratic Lagrangian is

The kinetic cross term comes from the bob's horizontal velocity x˙+Lϕ˙​ to first order. The generalized eigenvalue problem has mass matrix (m+MML​MLML2​) and restoring matrix diag(k,MgL). Their characteristic determinant gives the displayed polynomial in ω2. For m=M=L=g=1,k=2, the squared angular frequencies are 2∓2​, with mode shapes (x,ϕ)=(1,±2​). The bob and cart move in phase in the lower normal mode and in opposite phases in the higher normal mode.
