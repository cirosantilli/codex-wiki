Subset sum dynamic programming (Di​(t))

 ... Computational complexity theory Polynomial-time reduction Polynomial-time many-one reduction NP-hardness NP-completeness Subset sum problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
For indexed occurrences with positive integer weights, let Di​(t) record whether a subset of the first i entries has sum t. The recurrence chooses whether the last occurrence is absent or present: Di​(t)=Di−1​(t)∨Di−1​(t−ai​), with an out-of-range term false. Initialize only sum zero true. Descending one-dimensional updates prevent reusing the current occurrence. Time is O(ns) for target s and space O(s); positivity permits immediate rejection above the total. With weights bounded by k, the total is at most nk, making the bound polynomial in n,k.
