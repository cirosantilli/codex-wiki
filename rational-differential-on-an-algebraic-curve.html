Rational differential on an algebraic curve

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Divisor on an algebraic curve Riemann-Roch theorem Canonical divisor
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A rational differential on a smooth projective curve is a rational section of its canonical bundle. In a local coordinate t it has the form f(t)dt for a rational function f.
