Lunar laser ranging

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Energy Potential energy Inverse-square potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 144 Articles: 1
Lunar laser ranging gives a more discriminating orbit readout. An observatory directs short laser pulses at a retroreflector array on the Moon, and a detector records returning photons. Round-trip time, statistically combined over several returns, constrains distance; pressure and temperature accompany the readout. The fitted model includes Newtonian gravity, terrestrial and lunar rotation, geophysical effects, and corrections from general relativity. Comparing orbit determination residuals across stations and model choices checks consistency. The inspected study reports 1.8cm weighted root mean square residuals for its 2005–2011 ranges, rather than for a bare isolated inverse-square potential. It tests the equivalence principle using the Earth and Moon's response to the Sun; it is not a direct escape velocity measurement. Uneven coverage near new and full Moon, radiation pressure, and thermal expansion of reflector mounts limit that inference.[ref]
