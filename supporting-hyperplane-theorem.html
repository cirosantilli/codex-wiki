Supporting hyperplane theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex set
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A convex set in a finite-dimensional space has a nonzero supporting linear functional at each boundary point. If zero lies outside a nonempty convex set C, weak separation gives p=0 with p⋅c≥0 for all c∈C, even when zero belongs to its closure. Strict separation needs stronger hypotheses.
