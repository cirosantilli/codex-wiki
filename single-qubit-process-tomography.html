Single-qubit process tomography (Rij​=21​Tr(σi​Vσj​V†))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Measurement in quantum mechanics Quantum state tomography
OurBigBook.com Words: 127
To identify the physical channel ρ↦VρV† of a single-qubit unitary gate, prepare each of the three positive Pauli matrix eigenstates and estimate each output Pauli expectation. Their nine means are
Rij​=21​Tr(σi​Vσj​V†),i,j∈{x,y,z}.
(4)
They determine the Bloch vector rotation r↦Rr, hence the channel on all states. If two gates have the same channel, their relative unitary commutes with all three Pauli matrices: commuting with Z forces it diagonal and commuting with X forces equal diagonal entries. Thus they differ by a scalar global phase, proving uniqueness up to that phase. This procedure needs no controlled gate or eigenstate of the unknown gate. Estimating its bounded measurement means to error ϵ with fixed confidence uses order ϵ−2 repetitions. It determines the relative eigenphases but cannot determine an overall eigenvalue phase from uncontrolled gate access alone.
