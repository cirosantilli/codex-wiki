Riemann sum convergence theorem (∥Pn​∥→0⟹∑(f;Pn​,ξ​n​)→∫ab​f)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Riemann integration Riemann sum
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For a bounded function on a fixed closed interval, Riemann integrability with Riemann integral I is equivalent to convergence of all tagged Riemann sums to I as the mesh of a partition tends to zero. Precisely, for every ε>0 there is δ>0 such that every partition of an interval of mesh less than δ, with any tags in the corresponding closed subintervals, has its Riemann sum within ε of I.
