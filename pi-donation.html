Pi donation

 Home Chemistry Chemical bond Covalent bond Coordinate covalent bond
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
Donation from an occupied ligand orbital into a metal orbital of pi symmetry relative to the metal–ligand axis. It differs from metal-to-ligand pi backbonding.
