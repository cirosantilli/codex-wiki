Lubell mass (λ(A))

 ... Combinatorics Extremal set theory Set family Boolean lattice Antichain Lubell-Yamamoto-Meshalkin inequality
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The Lubell mass of a set family A⊆P([n]) is
λ(A)=∑A∈A​(∣A∣n​)−1.
(15)
It is the expected value of the number of members of A on a Uniformly random maximal chain in a Boolean lattice.
