Cobraided bialgebra ((H,r))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Algebra over a commutative ring Bialgebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A bialgebra equipped with a coquasitriangular structure, namely a convolution-invertible scalar form r:H⊗H→k satisfying r(ab,c)=∑r(a,c(1)​)r(b,c(2)​), r(a,bc)=∑r(a(1)​,c)r(a(2)​,b) and ∑r(a(1)​,b(1)​)a(2)​b(2)​=∑b(1)​a(1)​r(a(2)​,b(2)​), with unit normalization. The induced right-comodule braiding is v⊗w↦∑w(0)​⊗v(0)​r(v(1)​,w(1)​).
