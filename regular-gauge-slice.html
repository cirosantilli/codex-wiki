Regular gauge slice

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
A gauge condition F(x)=0 is regular at a representative x0​ if its derivative along the gauge orbit is an invertible matrix. The inverse function theorem then makes the gauge-condition values valid local coordinates along that orbit. A unique zero without this regularity is insufficient: the translation action on the line with F(x)=x3 has a unique zero but a zero derivative there. Multiple regular intersections give a Gribov ambiguity rather than a globally unique slice.
