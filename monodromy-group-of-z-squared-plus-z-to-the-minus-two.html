Monodromy group of z squared plus z to the minus two (z↦z2+z−2)

 ... Analysis Complex analysis Holomorphic function Analytic continuation Monodromy theorem Monodromy group of a covering
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The degree-four rational map F(z)=z2+z−2 has branch values −2,2,∞. Away from their inverse images, the transformations
z↦−z,z↦z−1
(9)
are commuting deck involutions. They generate a Klein four-group acting transitively on each fibre
{z,−z,z−1,−z−1}.
(10)
Consequently the monodromy group is V4​, acting by the identity and the three double transpositions.
