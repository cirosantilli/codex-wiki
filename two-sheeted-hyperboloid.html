Two-sheeted hyperboloid (t2−x2−y2=1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Smooth surface Hyperboloid
OurBigBook.com Words: 72 Articles: 1
This regular surface has two connected components, t=±1+x2+y2​. The symmetric-matrix model of the two-sheeted hyperboloid realizes them as the positive-definite and negative-definite symmetric determinant-one 2×2 matrices. Each component is diffeomorphic to R2; this differs from the connected one-sheet hyperboloid.
