Tensor cube of the defining unitary representation (V⊗3=Sym3V⊕Λ3V⊕V(2,1)⊕2​)

For the unitary group U(n) with n≥3, the third tensor power of its defining representation decomposes as displayed. The first two summands have highest weights (3,0,…) and (1,1,1,0,…) and dimensions (3n+2​) and (3n​). The mixed summand has highest weight (2,1,0,…) and dimension n(n2−1)/3. In the three-dimensional weight space spanned by permutations of e1​⊗e1​⊗e2​, the vectors killed by all positive root vectors have coefficient sum zero, giving multiplicity two. For n=2, the exterior cube vanishes and V(2,1)​=det⊗C2.
