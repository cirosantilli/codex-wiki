Bi-invariant pseudo-Riemannian metric

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 209 Articles: 2
A nondegenerate invariant symmetric bilinear form on a Lie algebra extends by translation to a metric preserved by both left and right translation. It may have indefinite signature. Its Levi-Civita connection is ∇X​Y=21​[X,Y] for left-invariant vector fields, and its Ricci tensor is −K/4. Choosing the nondegenerate Killing form of a semisimple Lie algebra yields a canonical indefinite Einstein manifold; compact groups instead admit the positive metric −K.
