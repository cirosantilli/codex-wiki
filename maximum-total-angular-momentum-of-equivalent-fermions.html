Maximum total angular momentum of equivalent fermions (Jmax​=Nj−N(N−1)/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Addition of angular momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For N identical fermions restricted to one angular-momentum multiplet j, with all other one-particle labels fixed, the Pauli exclusion principle permits distinct angular momentum projections j,j−1,…,j−N+1. Their sum is Nj−N(N−1)/2, provided N≤2j+1. The resulting Slater determinant is killed by the total raising angular momentum ladder operator because raising any occupied state either exceeds j or duplicates an occupied state. It is therefore a highest-weight vector of precisely this total angular momentum. The formula is invariant under replacing particles by holes in the full shell.
