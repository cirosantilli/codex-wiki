Fixed-point-free self-maps homotopic to the identity on products of surfaces ((g1​−1)(g2​−1)=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Lefschetz fixed-point theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
Such a self-map of Σg1​​×Σg2​​ exists exactly when one factor is a torus. Necessity follows because its Lefschetz number equals the Euler characteristic 4(1−g1​)(1−g2​) and must vanish. Sufficiency is translation by a nonzero point in the torus factor, times the identity on the other factor. The condition does not determine the product g1​g2​, since the other genus can be arbitrary.
