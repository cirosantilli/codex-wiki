Isometry

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 964 Articles: 18
An isometry between metric spaces preserves every distance. A Riemannian isometry preserves the Riemannian metric and therefore distances, angles, geodesics, and intrinsic curvature.
