Polynomial evaluated at a matrix (p(A)=∑j=0d​aj​Aj)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Minimal polynomial Cayley-Hamilton theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
For a polynomial p(t)=∑j=0d​aj​tj, substitute a fixed square matrix A for t, taking A0=I. In particular, p(t)=t2−3t+1 becomes p(A)=A2−3A+I, not A2−3A with the scalar 1 added separately to each entry. Polynomials in the same matrix commute with each other, even though arbitrary matrix multiplication is noncommutative.
