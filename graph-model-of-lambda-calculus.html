Graph model of lambda calculus (Pω)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Computability theory Lambda calculus Lambda model Reflexive domain
OurBigBook.com Words: 263 Articles: 3
Let Pω=P(N) ordered by subset. Encode a finite subset u and output n by a natural number ⟨u,n⟩. Define a⋅b={n:∃u⊆b finite, ⟨u,n⟩∈a} and e(f)={⟨u,n⟩:n∈f(u)}. Every Scott continuous map satisfies f(b)=⋃u⊆b finite​f(u), proving that application is the projection of a retraction. Lambda abstraction has graph {⟨u,n⟩:n∈[[M]]ρ[x:=u]​}.
