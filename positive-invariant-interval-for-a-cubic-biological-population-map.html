Positive invariant interval for a cubic biological population map (F((0,3/2))⊂(0,3/2))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Forward-invariant set
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For F(N)=(N−1/2)(N−1)(2−N)+1/2, every positive initial value with F(N0​)>0 has positive subsequent iterates. Indeed F(N)<3/2 for every N>0, since F(N)−3/2=−N(2N2−7N+7)/2<0. On [0,3/2], the negative part of the cubic product has magnitude at most 3/8, so F(N)≥1/8. Thus the first positive image enters the forward-invariant interval (0,3/2).
