Projective embedding (X↪Pn)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic variety Projective space Projective variety
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
A projective embedding realizes a variety as a closed subvariety of projective space. A very ample line bundle gives such an embedding through its global sections.
