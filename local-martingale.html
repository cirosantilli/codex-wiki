Local martingale

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Continuous-time martingale
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 31
An adapted process M is a local martingale when there are stopping times Tn​↑∞ almost surely such that each stopped process Mt∧Tn​​ is a martingale. Every martingale is local, while a positive local martingale is a supermartingale.
