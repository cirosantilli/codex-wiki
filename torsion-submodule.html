Torsion submodule (T(M))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Module (mathematics) Torsion module
OurBigBook.com Words: 78 Articles: 1
For a module M over an integral domain R, its torsion submodule is
T(M)={m∈M:rm=0 for some 0=r∈R}.
(17)
The domain condition makes this a submodule: products of nonzero annihilators remain nonzero and annihilate sums.
