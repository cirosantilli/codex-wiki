Normal-valued lattice-ordered group

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Partially ordered group Lattice-ordered group
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 1
A lattice-ordered group is normal-valued when every value in a lattice-ordered group is a normal subgroup of its cover. Normality in the cover is weaker than normality in the entire group. Every linearly ordered group is normal-valued.
