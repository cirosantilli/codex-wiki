Quadrupolar tidal forcing

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field Tidal tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
For a point-mass companion at separation a, subtract the constant and linear terms of its Newtonian gravitational potential in a frame accelerating with the target's center. The leading residual is
Φtide​=−2a3GM2​​[3(n⋅r)2−r2].
(2)
It is degree two in spherical harmonics. In a circular orbit with angular frequency ωo​, its time-dependent m=±2 components force at 2ωo​ in a frame with no spin; there is also a static m=0 component. This is the leading approximation in r/a, and the tidal amplitude may be absorbed into a harmonic-dependent constant.
