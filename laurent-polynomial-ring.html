Laurent polynomial ring (k[X1±1​,…,Xr±1​])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Polynomial ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 320 Articles: 4
A Laurent polynomial ring is the localization of a ring k[X1​,…,Xr​] obtained by inverting all variables. Its elements are finite sums of Laurent polynomials with exponent vectors in Zr. It is a Noetherian ring by the Hilbert basis theorem and Localization of a Noetherian ring.
