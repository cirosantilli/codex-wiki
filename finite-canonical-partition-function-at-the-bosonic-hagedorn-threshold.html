Finite canonical partition function at the bosonic Hagedorn threshold (Z1​(βH​)<∞butZ1​ is nonanalytic at βH​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Hagedorn temperature
OurBigBook.com Words: 123
For a critical closed bosonic string in 25 noncompact spatial dimensions, the smoothed string oscillator rest-mass density behaves as ρ(M)∼CM−26eβH​M, with βH​=4πα′​. At large M, the centre-of-mass momentum integral of e−βM2+p2​ is asymptotic to (2πM/β)25/2e−βM, up to the convention for phase-space measure. Thus the high-mass tail of the one-string canonical partition function is proportional to ∫M0​∞​M−27/2e−(β−βH​)MdM. This converges at β=βH​ but fails for β<βH​. Expanding about the endpoint gives twelve finite derivatives and a nonanalytic term proportional to (β−βH​)25/2; the thirteenth derivative diverges. Consequently exponential state growth does not imply divergence of the partition function at the threshold itself. The discussion assumes the separate infrared instability of the bosonic tachyon has been regulated and does not claim a stable bosonic thermal vacuum.
