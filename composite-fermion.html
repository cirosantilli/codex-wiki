Composite fermion

 ... Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force Hall effect Quantum Hall effect Fractional quantum Hall effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 201 Articles: 3
An emergent description of correlated electrons in which an even number of statistical flux quanta is attached to each electron. The resulting effective field can turn selected fractional electronic fillings into integer effective fillings. Flux attachment encodes correlations; it is not attachment of literal external magnetic solenoids to individual electrons.
