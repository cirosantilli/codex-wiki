For a pure birth process with fixed initial state and finite statewise rates, the jump-count compensator gives dM=(eθ−1)M−​[dN−λ(X−​)dt], so M is a local martingale up to explosion. If λ≤C, a rate-C Poisson candidate clock with accepted jumps of probability λ(X−​)/C prevents explosion and dominates the jump count. Its finite exponential moments dominate every stopped M on each finite horizon, upgrading the local martingale to a true martingale. A random initial state requires EeθX0​<∞; uniformly bounded rates do not supply this missing initial integrability.
