Blood

 Home Biology Physiology Circulatory system
OurBigBook.com Words: 202 Articles: 11
Blood is a fluid containing biological cells that transports substances through a circulatory system. Its viscosity depends on its cellular content and deformation rate. Treating it as a Newtonian fluid in the Hagen-Poiseuille equation is an approximation that depends on the vessel size and flow conditions.
