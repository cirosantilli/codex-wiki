Random-intercept attenuation of marginal logistic slopes (dηd​logitM(η)=1−M(1−M)Var(pB​)​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 51
Put pB​=logit−1(η+B) and M(η)=E(pB​). Differentiation gives M′=E[pB​(1−pB​)]=M(1−M)−Var(pB​). Hence the displayed derivative lies between zero and one, strictly below one for a nondegenerate random intercept. A marginal logit is therefore generally nonlinear and its slope differs from the conditional slope even without confounding. This is a precise instance of noncollapsibility of the odds ratio.
