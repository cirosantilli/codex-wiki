Sinusoid (Acos(ωt+ϕ))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Trigonometric function
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A sinusoid is a function Acos(ωt+ϕ), where A is its amplitude, ω its angular frequency and ϕ its phase. For ω=0 its period is 2π/∣ω∣. A uniformly randomized phase can produce a strictly stationary process even though individual paths look periodic.
