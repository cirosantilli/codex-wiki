I-adic topology ((InM)n≥0​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Filtration of a module
OurBigBook.com Words: 233 Articles: 7
The I-adic topology on a module M has the powers InM as a neighbourhood basis of zero. It is generated by the I-adic filtration; separatedness means ⋂n​InM=0.
