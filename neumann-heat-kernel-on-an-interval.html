Neumann heat kernel on an interval (KN​(x,y,t))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Diffusion equation Heat equation Heat kernel
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
For L>0 and t>0, the Sturm-Liouville eigenfunction expansion for ut​=uxx​ on (0,L) with homogeneous Neumann boundary conditions gives
KN​(x,y,t)=L1​+L2​∑n=1∞​e−(nπ/L)2tcosLnπx​cosLnπy​.
(24)
The constant eigenfunction mode preserves the spatial integral. This is the β=0 limit of the weighted Neumann heat kernel with constant drift, and its boundary inputs have the signs in the Neumann boundary-forcing formula.
