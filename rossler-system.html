Rössler system (x˙=−y−z,y˙​=x+ay,z˙=b+z(x−c))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems
OurBigBook.com Words: 81 Articles: 1
An autonomous differential equation in three variables. Its linear planar rotation couples to a nonlinear vertical variable through xz. Depending on parameters, its solutions can approach an equilibrium point, a periodic orbit, or a more complicated attractor. The specialization b=a is used in the 2011 Oxford combined paper.
