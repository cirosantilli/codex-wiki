Adjacent-order spacing versus nearest-axis echelle interval (Scentres​=K/[m(m+1)])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Free spectral range Free spectral range of an echelle grating
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
At a fixed camera-axis direction, the grating equation gives mλm​=K=d(sinα+sinβ0​), so adjacent central wavelengths differ by K/[m(m+1)]. This differs from the exact wavelength cell where an order is nearest the optical camera axis. For β0​=0 and x=ftanβ, equal distances of orders m and m+1 mean opposite diffraction angles; adding their diffraction grating equations gives λ=K/(m+1/2). The full order-m cell is bounded by K/(m+1/2) and K/(m−1/2), hence has width K/(m2−1/4) for m≥2 and accessible neighbouring orders. Both widths approach K/m2 at high order.
