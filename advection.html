Advection

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 2
Advection transports a quantity with a velocity field. For a scalar field f, its local advective rate is u⋅∇f, the spatial contribution to the material derivative. The same term transports the components of a vector field.
