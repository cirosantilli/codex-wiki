Exact finite-grid high-frequency Jacobi smoothing optimum (ω∗​=2/(a+b),μ∗​=(b−a)/(a+b))

For the one-dimensional Dirichlet second-difference grid, high sine modes have eigenvalues 1−ωtk​, with tk​=1−cos(kπ/(n+1)) and k≥⌈(n+1)/2⌉. Let a and b be the smallest and largest of these tk​. Convexity gives attenuation max(∣1−ωa∣,∣1−ωb∣). Balancing its endpoint values with opposite signs gives the displayed exact minimax choice. For odd n, a=1; for even n, a=1+sin(π/[2(n+1)]), and always b=1+cos(π/(n+1)). Only in the large-grid or uniform-in-grid limit do these become ω∗​=2/3, μ∗​=1/3.
