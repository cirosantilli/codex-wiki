Standard normal distribution function (Φ)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Normal distribution Standard normal distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The standard normal distribution function is the cumulative distribution function of a standard normal random variable Z:
Φ(x)=P(Z≤x)=∫−∞x​ϕ(t)dt=21​(1+erf(2​x​)).
(82)
Here ϕ is the standard normal density and erf is the error function. The symmetric probability distribution gives Φ(−x)=1−Φ(x) and Φ(0)=1/2, while differentiation gives Φ′(x)=ϕ(x). For X∼N(μ,σ2) with σ>0, standardization of a normal random variable gives P(X≤x)=Φ((x−μ)/σ); subtracting two values calculates an interval probability.
