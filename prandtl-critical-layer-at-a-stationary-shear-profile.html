Prandtl critical layer at a stationary shear profile (y−yc​=O(α−1/4),c−c0​=O(α−1/2))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Hydrodynamic stability Critical layer in a shear flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
For the Prandtl normal-mode equation with viscosity scaled to one and a profile having a nondegenerate stationary point, U−c0​∼(y−yc​)2/2. A piecewise outer mode proportional to U−c0​ has a second-derivative jump. Balancing the advective and third-derivative terms smooths that jump over width α−1/4, with velocity amplitude α−1/2 and a phase-speed correction of the same order. Matching selects the eigenvalue through a third-order inner problem.
