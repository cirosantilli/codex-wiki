Two-mode tensorial squirmer flow

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Microswimmer Squirmer
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
A spherical squirmer with tangential surface slip velocity (I−nn)(A+Bn), where B is a symmetric second-rank tensor and a traceless second-rank tensor, translates with U=−2A/3 and does not rotate in an unbounded quiescent fluid. With S=x⋅Bx its exterior Stokes flow is
u=3a3​∇r3A⋅x​+2r53a2Sx​+2a4​∇r5S​,p−p∞​=r53μa2S​.
(41)
This follows from the Unscaled Papkovich–Neuber representation using harmonic functions as potentials Φ=−a2Bx/(2r3) and χ=a3A⋅x/(3r3)+a4S/(2r5). Matching the surface coefficients proves the formula. The two modes generate a potential dipole and a stresslet, respectively.
