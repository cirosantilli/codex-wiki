Nernst equation (E=E∘−(RT/nF)lnQ)

 Home Chemistry Electrochemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 17
For a cell reaction transferring n electrons, E=E∘−(RT/nF)lnQ, with dimensionless reaction quotient Q and consistently chosen standard states.
