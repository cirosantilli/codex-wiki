Subobject classifier of a coalgebra topos (ΩG​=Eq(1,GκδΩ​))

 ... Category Functor Endofunctor Comonad Coalgebra for a comonad Category of coalgebras for a comonad
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
Let κ:GΩ→Ω classify G⊤:1↪GΩ. In the cofree coalgebra RΩ, equalize the identity and GκδΩ​. A map χ:X→Ω classifies a subcoalgebra exactly when χ=κGχx, since this says the subobject equals the inverse image of its image under G. Transposition gives precisely the displayed equalizer condition.
