Charge-two cubic complex-scalar interaction (LI​=−g(ϕ2Φ†+ϕ†2Φ)/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Complex scalar field
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 1
This interaction preserves the global U(1) symmetry with relative charges qϕ​=1, qΦ​=2. The two conjugate interaction vertices have Feynman rule −ig, with either one charge-two arrow entering and two charge-one arrows leaving, or all arrows reversed. For ϕϕ→ϕϕ only the heavy s-channel exchange occurs at tree level. For ϕϕˉ​→ϕϕˉ​, with outgoing particle labelled 3 and outgoing antiparticle labelled 4, only the heavy u-channel exchange occurs. For small momenta and heavy mediator mass, the amplitudes coincide but the identical-particle total scattering cross-section is smaller by the identical final-state symmetry factor 1/2.
