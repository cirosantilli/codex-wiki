Fermi contact interaction (Hc​∝∣ψs​(0)∣2I⋅s)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Hyperfine structure Hyperfine interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
The isotropic magnetic interaction of a nuclear magnetic dipole moment with electron spin density at the atomic nucleus. A nonrelativistic penetrating s orbital can have ∣ψs​(0)∣2=0 and a substantial contact contribution to hyperfine structure. A filled paired shell cancels its net contact spin density in the independent-particle approximation; unpaired occupation and core polarization can restore it. Relativistic and finite nuclear-size effects modify the point-contact description.
