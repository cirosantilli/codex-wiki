Complex tautological bundle on a Grassmannian (γkN​={(W,v):v∈W})

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Tautological bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 267 Articles: 4
Over Grk​(CN) this rank-k complex vector bundle has fibre W at the point W. In a chart of planes that are graphs of maps from a fixed k-plane, (A,w)↦(graphA,w+Aw) gives a local trivialization. The direct-limit bundle over the infinite Grassmannian classifies rank-k bundles over compact Hausdorff spaces.
