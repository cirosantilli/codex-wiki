With constant lateral supply M, a discharge law of the displayed form gives equilibrium area [(m+1)(Qin​+Ms)/c]1/(m+1) and characteristic speed cAm. Under a Chezy law, a wetted-perimeter relation Pb​∝Aq gives m=(1−q)/2. Small area perturbations are transported downstream with amplitude inversely proportional to this characteristic speed; finite compressive disturbances can still form shocks because the flux is convex.
