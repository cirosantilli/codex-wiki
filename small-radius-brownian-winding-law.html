Small-radius Brownian winding law (θε​/logε⇒C/(2π))

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Planar Brownian motion Conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
For planar Brownian motion started at radius ε and stopped on first hitting radius one, the net winding count divided by logε converges to a standard Cauchy random variable divided by 2π. The complex exponential maps Brownian coordinates in the left half-plane to the punctured unit disk after the conformal Brownian clock. The unwrapped argument at exit is the vertical half-plane exit coordinate, with the Cauchy exit law from a Brownian half-plane. Counting integer turns introduces only a uniformly bounded error in winding units.
