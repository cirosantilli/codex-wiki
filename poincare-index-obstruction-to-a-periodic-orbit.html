Poincare-index obstruction to a periodic orbit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Poincaré index Index of a planar periodic orbit
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
A simply connected region containing no equilibrium, or containing equilibria whose total Poincaré index is not +1, cannot contain a periodic orbit that encloses exactly those equilibria.
