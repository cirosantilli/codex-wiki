Natal kick binding criterion (∣1±q∣<2f​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Binary star Supernova kick in a binary star
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For an initially circular binary losing mass instantaneously, let f be the retained total mass fraction and q the kick speed in units of the initial relative orbital speed. A kick exactly parallel or opposite to the exploding star's velocity leaves negative relative orbital energy precisely for the title inequality. Equality is marginal escape. For an opposite kick q=1, the relative velocity and angular momentum vanish: negative energy then produces a radial collision, rather than a persistent finite-pericentre binary.
