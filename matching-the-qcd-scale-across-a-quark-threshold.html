Matching the QCD scale across a quark threshold

 ... Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Renormalization Renormalization scale Running coupling One-loop running of the strong coupling
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
At a heavy-quark mass m, leading-order continuity gives β−​ln(m/Λ−​)=β+​ln(m/Λ+​). Therefore Λ+​=m(Λ−​/m)β−​/β+​​. This is a change of the strong-coupling scale parametrization across a change of active flavour number, not a discontinuity of the matched QCD coupling. For four to five active flavours in SU(3), β−​=25/3 and β+​=23/3, hence Λ5​=Λ4​(mb​/Λ4​)−2/23.
