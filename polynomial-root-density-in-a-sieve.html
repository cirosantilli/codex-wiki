Polynomial root density in a sieve

 ... Number theory Analytic number theory Sieve theory Upper-bound sieve Selberg sieve Selberg upper-bound sieve
OurBigBook.com Words: 283 Articles: 3
For an integer polynomial F and squarefree d, let ρF​(d) count the roots of F modulo d. The Chinese remainder theorem makes ρF​ multiplicative, and
#{m≤X:d∣F(m)}=dρF​(d)​X+O(ρF​(d)).
(37)
Thus g(d)=ρF​(d)/d and rd​=O(ρF​(d)) define a sieve distribution.
