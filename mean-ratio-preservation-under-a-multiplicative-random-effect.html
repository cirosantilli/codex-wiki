Mean-ratio preservation under a multiplicative random effect (EYj​/EYk​=eαj​−αk​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 65
If E(Yj​∣B,x)=Bexp(β0​+βTx+αj​) and the distribution of B is the same at each observation time with finite positive mean, integrating out B adds logEB to the marginal log-mean intercept. The conditional positive-B mean ratio and the marginal mean ratio both equal eαj​−αk​. This is a property of the multiplicative logarithmic link function; it need not hold for nonlinear links such as the logit link.
