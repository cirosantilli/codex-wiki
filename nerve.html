Nerve

 Home Biology Neuroscience Nervous system
OurBigBook.com Words: 36 Articles: 3
A bundle of axons in the peripheral nervous system, enclosed by supporting connective tissue. A nerve can carry sensory fibres, motor fibres, or both; it is distinct from an individual neuron.[ref]
