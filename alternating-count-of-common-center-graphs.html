Alternating count of common-center graphs (∑G​(−1)∣E(G)∣STARn​(G)=(n−1)(n−2)/2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 37
For n≥3, count the empty graph once, all one-edge graphs once, and each larger accepted graph under its unique center. The latter weighted contribution is n(n−2), giving 1−(2n​)+n(n−2). Its nonzero value proves evasiveness by the alternating-sum criterion for decision-tree evasiveness.
