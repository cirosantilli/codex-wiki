Pain

 Home Biology Neuroscience Sensory system Perception
OurBigBook.com Words: 53 Articles: 1
An unpleasant sensory and emotional experience. Pain should be distinguished from a stimulus applied by an experimenter and from an observer's inference that someone else is experiencing pain.
