Partial atomic charge

 Home Chemistry Chemical bond
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A partial atomic charge assigns an electric charge to a chemical atom by partitioning the actual electron density. The result depends on the partitioning convention and need not be an integer. It differs from formal charge, which divides bonding Electrons equally in a specified Lewis structure.
