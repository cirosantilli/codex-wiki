Placebo effect

 Home Science Psychology Psychological research methods
OurBigBook.com Words: 234 Articles: 5
A change associated with treatment context or expectation rather than the specific active component being tested. A comparison with no treatment and appropriate masking is needed to distinguish a placebo effect from spontaneous change or reporting differences.
