Fundamental form of a Hermitian manifold (ω)

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Hermitian manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
For the underlying Riemannian metric g and complex structure J, the fundamental form is ω(u,v)=g(Ju,v). It is a real (1, 1)-form. In complex dimension n, the compatible orientation has Riemannian volume form ωn/n!, and its Hodge star operator satisfies
∗ω=(n−1)!ωn−1​.
