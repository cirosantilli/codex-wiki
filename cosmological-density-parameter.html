Cosmological density parameter (Ω)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Critical density
OurBigBook.com Words: 502 Articles: 9
The density parameter is Ω=ρ/ρcrit​. In a Friedmann universe, its departure from one measures the spatial-curvature term relative to the density or expansion term.
The Friedmann equation relates this ratio to spatial curvature and the expansion rate; the parameter and the evolution equations are distinct concepts.
