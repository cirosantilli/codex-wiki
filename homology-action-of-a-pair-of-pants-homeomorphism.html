Homology action of a pair-of-pants homeomorphism

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological surface Surface with boundary Pair of pants (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Orient the three boundary classes c1​,c2​,c3​ so that c1​+c2​+c3​=0 in H1​(N;Z). A homeomorphism with orientation sign ϵ and boundary permutation σ sends ci​ to ϵcσ(i)​. Its trace on the rank-two quotient of Z3 by c1​+c2​+c3​ is therefore ϵ(#Fixσ−1).
