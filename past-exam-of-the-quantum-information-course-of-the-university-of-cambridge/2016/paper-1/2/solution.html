Omitting that conjugation would not reproduce the coefficient matrix. The squared Schmidt coefficients are the eigenvalues of a†a; equivalently d2=V†a†aV and d=U†aV. The Von Neumann entropy is S=−λ0​log2​λ0​−λ1​log2​λ1​. Ψ is more entangled: its Schmidt weights are closer to equal and its reduced-state entropy is larger. Neither state is a product state or maximally entangled on its two-dimensional support.
By Nielsen's pure-state conversion theorem, deterministic LOCC conversion requires the input vector of squared Schmidt coefficients to be majorized by the output vector. Here 0.699941<0.894238 and both sums are one, so λ(Ψ)≺λ(Φ). Therefore Ψ→Φ is possible deterministically; Φ→Ψ is not. Entanglement can be discarded by local operations but cannot increase deterministically for these pure inputs. Probabilistic concentration is a separate question and is allowed below.[ref]
There is a dimensional qualification in the proposed measurement. Write P=∣0A′​⟩⟨0A′​∣+∣1A′​⟩⟨1A′​∣. The two supplied measurement operators obey
M1†​M1​+M2†​M2​=P.
(9)
Thus their effects form a POVM on the occupied support, where P is the identity. Alice's full space is three-dimensional, so these two operators alone do not sum to I3​. A full-space completion is M3​=∣2A′​⟩⟨2A′​∣. It has zero probability on either input, so it changes none of the requested outcomes. Strictly, the POVM consists of the effects Er​=Mr†​Mr​; the specified Mr​ additionally fix a quantum instrument and therefore the conditional states.

The success probabilities belong to this particular binary Schmidt filter. They are not the optimum over every possible quantum instrument. The diagonal success operator diag(λ1​/λ0​​,1) instead gives the standard single-copy entanglement concentration probability 2λ1​. Postselection can increase entanglement in the retained branch, while the outcome-averaged entanglement cannot increase under LOCC.
