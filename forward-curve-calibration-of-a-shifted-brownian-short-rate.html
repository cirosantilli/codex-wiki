To match a prescribed initial instantaneous forward rate curve F0​(T) in the Gaussian short-rate model with a deterministic shift, choose
g(T)=F0​(T)+21​σ2T2.
(26)
Since W0Q​=0, its model forward rate is then f0​(T)=g(T)−σ2T2/2=F0​(T). Moreover,
P0​(T)=exp[−∫0T​g(s)ds+6σ2T3​]=exp[−∫0T​F0​(s)ds],

so the initial zero-coupon bond curve matches as well. A continuous initial forward curve gives pointwise calibration; a locally integrable curve gives the corresponding almost-everywhere interpretation of the instantaneous forward rate.
