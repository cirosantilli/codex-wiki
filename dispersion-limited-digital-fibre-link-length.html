Dispersion-limited digital-fibre link length (Lmax​∼Tr​/[∣∂f​(1/vg​)∣Δf])

A pulse's frequency components accumulate a differential group delay Δt=L∣∂f​(1/vg​)∣Δf. If that spread approaches the pulse repetition period Tr​, neighbouring symbols overlap. The resulting length estimate depends on bandwidth, initial pulse width, chirp and the receiver's allowable intersymbol interference. For n(f)=n0​+τ(f−f0​), the group index is n0​+τ(2f−f0​) and ∂f​(1/vg​)=2τ/c: differentiating the phase index alone loses a factor of two.
