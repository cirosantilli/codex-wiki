Dislocation

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Crystal structure Crystallographic defect
OurBigBook.com Words: 912 Articles: 30
A line defect in a crystal structure. Its Burgers vector measures the closure failure of a circuit around the line. Motion can produce permanent plasticity without simultaneously breaking every bond across a whole plane.
