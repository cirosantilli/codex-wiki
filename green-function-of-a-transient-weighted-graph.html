For the walk on a weighted graph, the Green function normalized by vertex weight is
g(x,y)=μy​1​Ex​∑n≥0​1{Xn​=y}​.
(396)
Symmetry of the conductances makes g(x,y)=g(y,x), and E(g(⋅,y),f)=f(y) for finitely supported f.
