Thermal efficiency (η=W/Qin​)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Thermodynamic cycle
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The thermal efficiency of a heat engine is the net work output divided by heat input. For a cycle absorbing Qin​ and rejecting the positive amount Qout​,
η=1−Qin​Qout​​.
