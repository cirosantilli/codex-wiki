Orthogonal splitting with bounded volume ratio (L≤32vr(E)2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Normed vector space Volume ratio
OurBigBook.com Words: 98
In a 2k-dimensional normed vector space in John position, two orthogonal k-dimensional subspaces admit ∥x∥E​≤∣x∣≤32vr(E)2∥x∥E​. To see this, take ε-nets in two orthogonal half-dimensional unit spheres, each of size at most (1+2/ε)k, and rotate them uniformly together. The volume ratio bound makes the probability that any net point has norm below 2ε at most 2[4vr(E)2(ε2+2ε)]k. With ε=(32vr(E)2)−1 this is less than 1. Since ∥⋅∥E​ is 1-Lipschitz for the Euclidean metric, the successful nets give the stated inequality everywhere on both spheres. A dimension-independent constant therefore requires a bound on the volume ratio; it does not hold for arbitrary normed vector spaces.
