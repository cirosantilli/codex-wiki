Factorial estimate for Picard iteration (∣yn+1​(x)−yn​(x)∣≤MLn∣x−a∣n+1/(n+1)!)

For Picard iteration yn+1​(x)=b+∫ax​f(s,yn​(s))ds, y0​=b, assume ∣f∣≤M and a Lipschitz constant L on a rectangle containing every iterate. The first difference is at most M∣x−a∣. Integrating the preceding bound multiplied by L proves the displayed estimate by mathematical induction. Its uniform bounds sum to a convergent exponential series for every finite Lh, so the Weierstrass M-test gives uniform convergence without requiring Lh<1.
For two arbitrary initial functions y,z in a function set preserved by the Picard iteration operator T, the same induction gives
∥Tny−Tnz∥∞​≤n!(Lh)n​∥y−z∥∞​.
(56)
Thus some iterate is a contraction mapping on any fixed finite interval for which every iterate stays in that set. The iterated contraction argument gives a unique fixed point of T on a nonempty complete metric space, without requiring Lh<1. For a rectangle with field bound M and vertical radius k, Mh≤k supplies the required self-mapping property.
