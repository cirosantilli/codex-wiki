For a,b>0, the convolution of the normalized interval indicator (2a)−11[−a,a]​ with the two-sided exponential (2b)−1e−∣x∣/b is
w(x)={[1−e−a/bcosh(x/b)]/(2a),sinh(a/b)e−∣x∣/b/(2a),​∣x∣≤a,∣x∣≥a.​
(74)
Split the defining integral at t=x in the interior case; outside the interval the absolute value has a fixed sign. These elementary integrals prove the formula and its continuity at the joining points. With Fourier transform convention w(k)=∫w(x)e−ikxdx, the convolution theorem gives w(k)=sin(ak)/[ak(1+b2k2)], using its continuous value at k=0. Parseval's identity can therefore turn the squared integral of this rational-trigonometric transform into a piecewise elementary real-space integral.
