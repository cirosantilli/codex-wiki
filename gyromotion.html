Gyromotion (Ωs​=qs​B/(ms​c))

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force
OurBigBook.com Words: 182 Articles: 3
Rapid circular particle motion perpendicular to a locally uniform magnetic field, superposed on parallel streaming when the parallel velocity is nonzero. In Gaussian units, its signed cyclotron frequency is given in the title and its gyroradius is v⊥​/∣Ωs​∣. Slowly varying fields permit a guiding center description.
