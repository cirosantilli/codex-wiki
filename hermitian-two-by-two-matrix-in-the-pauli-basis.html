Hermitian two-by-two matrix in the Pauli basis (M=dI+b⋅σ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Pauli matrices
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
Every Hermitian matrix of size two can be written uniquely as M=dI+bx​σx​+by​σy​+bz​σz​ with real coefficients. For M=(uw∗​wz​), take d=(u+z)/2, bz​=(u−z)/2, bx​=Rew and by​=−Imw. Alternatively, d=trM/2 and bi​=tr(Mσi​)/2 follow from the trace orthogonality of the Pauli matrices and the identity matrix.
The Pauli matrix multiplication law gives (b⋅σ)2=∣b∣2I, hence the two eigenvalues are d±∣b∣. For b=0, the corresponding pure-state Bloch vectors are ±b/∣b∣.
