Oil drop experiment

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Elementary charge
OurBigBook.com Words: 909 Articles: 2
The oil drop experiment infers an oil drop's electric charge from its motion under gravity, buoyancy, drag and a calibrated electric field. Repeated changes of a single drop's electric charge reveal discrete steps; see the model, experimental procedure and limitations in elementary charge.
