Diffusive shock acceleration

 ... Galaxy Milky Way Interstellar medium Cosmic ray Fermi acceleration First-order Fermi acceleration
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Diffusive shock acceleration repeatedly returns particles to a shock wave by scattering them in the upstream and downstream plasma. For fluid speeds u1​>u2​, particle speed v≫u1​, and shock compression ratio r=u1​/u2​, the cycle gain is ⟨Δp⟩/p=4(u1​−u2​)/(3v) and the cycle escape probability is 4u2​/v. Their ratio gives f(p)∝p−3r/(r−1) for the isotropic phase-space one-particle distribution function. For r=4 this is p−4, corresponding to dN/dE∝E−2 in the ultrarelativistic regime.
