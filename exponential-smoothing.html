Exponential smoothing (St​=KXt​+(1−K)St−1​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series
OurBigBook.com Words: 266 Articles: 4
With a fixed gain 0<K<1, exponential smoothing recursively combines the newest observation with the preceding estimate. Iteration assigns geometric weights K(1−K)j to older observations, together with a diminishing weight on the initial estimate. The steady-state local-level Kalman gain gives a probabilistic interpretation of this recursion.
