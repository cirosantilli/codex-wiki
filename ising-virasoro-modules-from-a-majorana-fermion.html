Ising Virasoro modules from a Majorana fermion (h=0,1/2,1/16)

 ... Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Worldsheet Two-dimensional conformal field theory Free chiral Majorana fermion conformal field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
A chiral Majorana fermion with modes {br​,bs​}=δr+s,0​ and br†​=b−r​ has T=−:ψ∂ψ:/2 and central charge 1/2. The even and odd fermion-number parts of its Neveu–Schwarz sector give unitary modules with lowest weights 0 and 1/2. In its Ramond sector, a choice of ground-state zero-mode eigenvalue gives the module of weight 1/16; the other choice gives an isomorphic Virasoro algebra module. Their cyclic highest-weight submodules are irreducible: a positive-energy invariant proper submodule would have a lowest vector orthogonal to every descendant of the original highest vector, which is impossible in a positive inner-product cyclic module.
