Odd-genus pencils from a four-divisible polarization (g=1+21​(m2H2+mKX​⋅H),4∣m)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Lefschetz pencil
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
On a smooth projective surface, fix an ample divisor H. For sufficiently large m, a general pencil in ∣mH∣ is a Lefschetz pencil. The adjunction formula gives the displayed genus. If m is divisible by four, both terms added to 1 are even integers, so the genus is odd.
