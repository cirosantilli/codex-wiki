Infinite-coordination Heisenberg antiferromagnet

 ... Physics Branch of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Spin angular momentum Heisenberg model
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
For a bipartite spin-one-half Heisenberg antiferromagnet with coordination z→∞, the two-sublattice mean-field state becomes exact. Antiparallel pure Bloch vectors give bond energy −J/4 for the convention JSi​⋅Sj​.
