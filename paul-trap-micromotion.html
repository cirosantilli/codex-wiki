Paul trap micromotion (ui​≃Xi​[1−(qi​/2)cosΩt])

 Home Physics Atomic physics Atomic measurement methods Ion trap Paul trap
OurBigBook.com Words: 324 Articles: 1
In the small-parameter regime, the slow coordinate Xi​ describing Paul trap secular motion approximately obeys a harmonic oscillator equation at angular frequency ωi​. Its superposed fast motion is Paul trap micromotion. Substituting the leading fast displacement from the RF-averaged trapping potential gives the displayed expression in our phase convention. A small positional ripple can have substantial velocity, because differentiating it brings down the large angular frequency Ω. Precision spectroscopy must therefore address both Paul trap secular motion and Paul trap micromotion; merely obtaining a bounded trajectory does not eliminate Doppler shifts.
