For 0<Q2<M2 there are two distinct positive roots:
r±​=M±M2−Q2​,F=r2(r−r+​)(r−r−​)​.​
(23)
Both roots are coordinate singularities of the static chart. In the standard extension, r+​ is the outer event horizon and r−​ is an inner Cauchy horizon. The curvature singularity is r=0. Between the horizons F<0, so the radial direction is timelike; for 0<r<r−​, F>0 again and the central curvature singularity is timelike. These are the three radial regions of the global structure of a charged spherical black hole.[ref]
For completeness, if Q=0 and M>0, the Schwarzschild spacetime limit has just the positive horizon r=2M and a spacelike curvature singularity at r=0; the formal root r−​=0 of r2F is not a regular inner horizon. If M=Q=0, the metric tensor is flat and the radial origin is regular in Cartesian coordinates.
