A Killing vector field K generates local isometries: its flow of a vector field Φs​ satisfies Φs∗​g=g. Equivalently, the Lie derivative of the metric tensor vanishes. For the Levi-Civita connection, metric compatibility converts the coordinate expression into the Killing equation:
LK​g=0,∇μ​Kν​+∇ν​Kμ​=0.​
(1)
The Levi-Civita connection is an implicit extra assumption in this question. The printed assumption of vanishing torsion tensor alone does not imply metric compatibility. For example, on Minkowski spacetime, take the affine connection whose only nonzero Christoffel symbol is Γ000​=1. The vector field K=∂0​ preserves the Minkowski metric, but K0​=−1 gives ∇0​K0​=1, contradicting the Killing equation. The following calculation, and the covariant formulas in part (b), use the Levi-Civita connection; this is the role of metric compatibility in Killing identities.
For the stated curvature sign convention, the Ricci identity on a covector has a minus sign:
[∇μ​,∇ν​]Kρ​=−Rσρμν​Kσ​.
(2)
Write Aμνρ​=∇μ​∇ν​Kρ​. Differentiating the Killing equation gives Aμνρ​=−Aμρν​. Alternately using this relation and the Ricci identity gives
Aμνρ​​=−Aμρν​=−Aρμν​+Rσνμρ​Kσ​=Aρνμ​+Rσνμρ​Kσ​=Aνρμ​−Rσμρν​Kσ​+Rσνμρ​Kσ​=−Aμνρ​−Rσρμν​Kσ​−Rσμρν​Kσ​+Rσνμρ​Kσ​.​
(3)
The first Bianchi identity and antisymmetry of the last two indices of the Riemann curvature tensor reduce the three curvature terms to 2Rσμνρ​Kσ​. Consequently the second covariant derivative of a Killing vector is
∇μ​∇ν​Kρ​=Rσμνρ​Kσ​.​
(4)
