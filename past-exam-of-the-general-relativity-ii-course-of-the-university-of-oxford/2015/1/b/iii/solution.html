In Cartesian coordinates on Minkowski spacetime, the Conformal Killing equation becomes
∂μ​Xν​+∂ν​Xμ​=2λημν​.
(9)
Differentiate the equations with index pairs (ν,ρ), (μ,ρ) and (μ,ν) by ∂μ​, ∂ν​ and ∂ρ​, respectively. Adding the first two and subtracting the third, Clairaut's theorem yields the flat conformal Killing integrability formula
∂μ​∂ν​Xρ​=ηνρ​∂μ​λ+ημρ​∂ν​λ−ημν​∂ρ​λ.
(10)
Apply ∂ρ and use ∂ρXρ​=4λ. With the d'Alembert operator □=ηαβ∂α​∂β​, this gives
4∂μ​∂ν​λ=2∂μ​∂ν​λ−ημν​□λ,2∂μ​∂ν​λ=−ημν​□λ.
(11)
A tensor contraction with ημν gives 2□λ=−4□λ, so □λ=0. Returning to the preceding equation proves the stronger statement
∂μ​∂ν​λ=0.​
(12)
Thus the conformal Killing factor is an affine function, not an arbitrary solution of the wave equation.
