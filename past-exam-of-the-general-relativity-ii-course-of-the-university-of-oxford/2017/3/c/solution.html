r˙2=E2+r2a2E2−L2​+r32M(L−aE)2​.​

An equivalent useful polynomial form is
r4r˙2=R(r),R(r)=[E(r2+a2)−aL]2−Δ(L−aE)2.​
(57)
These expressions use an affine parameter s; a common rescaling of E and L accompanies its rescaling. The inversion was performed in the exterior r>r+​, where Boyer-Lindquist coordinates are regular. The polynomial radial identity has a smooth continuation to a horizon, but the formulas for t˙ and ϕ˙​ there must be replaced by regular-coordinate expressions. In the limit a=0, the result reduces to the Schwarzschild spacetime equation r˙2=E2−(1−2M/r)L2/r2.
