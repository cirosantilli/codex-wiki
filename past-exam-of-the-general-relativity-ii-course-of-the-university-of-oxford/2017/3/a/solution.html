The event-horizon terminology in the paper needs qualification. In the usual asymptotically flat Kerr black hole extension, relative to a chosen exterior's future null infinity, the future branch of r=r+​ is the event horizon, the boundary ∂J−(I+). In the intervening black-hole region Δ<0, the coordinate r has timelike gradient and future-directed curves move toward smaller r; they cannot return across the outer boundary to that exterior. The inner surface r=r−​ is a Cauchy horizon, not a second event horizon for the same asymptotic end. Nullness is a local property; identifying an event horizon additionally requires this global causal structure.[ref]
