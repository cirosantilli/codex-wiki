The quadratic Christoffel symbol terms vanish here: any potentially contributing intermediate u index gives a factor Γuab​=0, and the remaining possible products have a lower v index and vanish. Since the symmetric matrix M gives F,ij​=2Mij​, lowering the first index gives
Ruiuj​=Rviuj​=−Mij​(u).​
(8)
All remaining nonzero components follow from the Riemann curvature tensor symmetries
Rabcd​=−Rbacd​=−Rabdc​=Rcdab​.
(9)
There are no other independent components: terms involving a v index vanish, while nonzero terms require one u in each antisymmetric pair.
The Ricci tensor is consequently
Ruu​=δijRiuju​=−trM,Rab​=0otherwise.
(10)
The Ricci scalar vanishes even when the trace is nonzero, since guu=0. With zero cosmological constant, the vacuum Einstein field equations reduce to Rab​=0. Therefore the necessary and sufficient condition is
trM(u)=0for every u.​
(11)
This is the Ricci tensor of a Brinkmann plane wave: a nonzero, trace-free profile can have nonzero Riemann curvature tensor despite vanishing Ricci tensor.
