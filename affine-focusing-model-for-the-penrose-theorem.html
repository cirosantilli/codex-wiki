The Null Raychaudhuri equation is the light-ray version of the Raychaudhuri equation associated with Amal Kumar Raychaudhuri. His original work concerned relativistic cosmology.[ref] For the normal null geodesic congruence before its first focal point, null twist vanishes. With an affine parameter λ, the four-dimensional equation reduces to the displayed ordinary differential equation. The null shear contraction is nonnegative on the transverse screen space, and the null convergence condition makes the other part of Q nonnegative. Consequently θ′≤−θ2/2.
