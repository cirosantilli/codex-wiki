Operator conjugation of a chiral field component (C^ψL​C^−1=PL​ψc=(ψR​)c)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Discrete symmetry in quantum field theory Charge conjugation Charge conjugation of a Dirac field
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
The unitary charge-conjugation operator acts on field operators and commutes with numerical chiral projectors, so C^(PL​ψ)C^−1=PL​ψc. This component is left-chiral. The algebraic conjugate of an already projected spinor instead satisfies (PL​ψ)c=PR​ψc, because its adjoint carries the opposite projector. Keeping the two operations separate avoids a false chirality conclusion.
