Interaction energy (W[μ]=21​∬W(x−y)dμ(x)dμ(y))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Optimal transport Interaction potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
The factor 1/2 counts unordered pairs once. For a symmetric interaction potential, this energy is unchanged by translating the entire probability measure. It therefore cannot have positive uniform displacement convexity on the unrestricted space containing translations. If D2W≥λI, integrate the pointwise strong convexity inequality along an optimal transport plan and its independent copy. The variance identity for independent copies then gives λ-displacement convexity on every class with fixed barycenter of a measure, whenever the integrals are finite.
