Chordal SLE in a specified scale (σ(γ))

OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A scale is a conformal bijection sending a marked starting boundary point to zero and target boundary point to infinity in the complex upper half-plane. A curve is SLE in that scale when its image is ordinary SLE using the half-plane-capacity parameterization of that image.
