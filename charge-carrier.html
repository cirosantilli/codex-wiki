Charge carrier

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Current density
OurBigBook.com Words: 390 Articles: 8
A charge carrier transports electric charge by moving: electrons are one example, and ions are another. For species s with number density ns​, electric charge qs​ and mean velocity vs​, the transport contributions add as J=∑s​ns​qs​vs​. A stationary charged background contributes to charge density but not to this current density. [ref].
