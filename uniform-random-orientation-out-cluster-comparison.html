Uniform random orientation out-cluster comparison (C→(o)=dC1/2​(o))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Percolation theory Bond percolation
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
If independent edges of a locally finite graph receive fair orientations, exploring outward from a root tests each fresh reached-to-unreached boundary edge with success probability 1/2. Deferred decisions give exactly the exploration law of the root cluster in bond percolation of density 1/2. Thus the two reachable graph vertex sets have the same distribution. An infinite out-cluster contains an infinite simple directed ray by the König infinity lemma. Geometrically biased orientations do not generally give the same uniform edge-success law.
