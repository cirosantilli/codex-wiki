Monotonicity of a convex subdifferential

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Subdifferential
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
Adding the two subgradient inequalities for p∈∂f(x) and q∈∂f(y) gives
⟨p−q,x−y⟩≥0.
(18)
Thus the subdifferential is a monotone operator. This inequality proves uniqueness of its resolvent: two solutions of z∈x+τ∂f(x) must coincide when τ>0.
