Monomial zero locus in an affine toric variety (V(χμ)=⋃⟨μ,τ⟩=0​V(τ))

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Toric geometry Toric variety Orbit-cone correspondence Orbit closure in a toric variety
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
On the orbit of a face τ⪯σ, the monomial χμ, with μ∈σ∨∩M, vanishes exactly when μ∈/τ⊥. Its reduced zero locus is consequently the union of those orbit closures. The principal subscheme need not be reduced: V(x2) and V(x) have the same support but different ideals.
