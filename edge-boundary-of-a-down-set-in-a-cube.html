Edge boundary of a down-set in a cube (be​(D)=n∣D∣−2∑A∈D​∣A∣)

 ... Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Set family Boolean lattice Down-set
OurBigBook.com Words: 107 Articles: 1
For a down-set D in the Boolean lattice, every member A has all its ∣A∣ immediate lower neighbors in D. Counting an internal edge at its upper endpoint gives e(D)=∑A∈D​∣A∣. The degree identity for the edge boundary of the hypercube graph then gives the displayed formula.
