Binary cyclic codes of length a power of two

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Coding theory Cyclic code Repeated-root cyclic code
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For N=2t, the binary cyclic codes are exactly the nested ideals generated by (X+1)j for 0≤j≤N. They have dimensions N−j and canonical check polynomials (X+1)N−j. The endpoint ideals are the entire word space and the zero code.
