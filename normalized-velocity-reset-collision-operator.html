Normalized velocity-reset collision operator (Bg=ρ(g)M−g)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Boltzmann equation Linear Boltzmann equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 251 Articles: 4
Let M≥0 have velocity integral one, and ρ(g)(x)=∫g(x,v)dv. On phase-space L1, the normalized relaxation collision operator is B=P−I, where Pg=ρ(g)M. It obeys ∥B∥≤2 because ∥Pg∥1​≤∥g∥1​. It preserves mass at each spatial position. The corresponding linear Boltzmann equation combines this bounded collision operator with the free-transport semigroup.
