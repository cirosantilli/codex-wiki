Second spherical moment integral (∫S2​ni​nj​dΩ=(4π/3)δij​)

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus Surface integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For the outward unit normal on a three-dimensional sphere, the off-diagonal integrals vanish by reflection symmetry. The diagonal integrals are equal by rotational symmetry, and their sum is ∫S2​∣n∣2dΩ=4π. This proves the displayed matrix identity, where δij​ is the Kronecker delta. Contracting it with a constant vector gives ∫S2​(U⋅n)ndΩ=(4π/3)U, useful for integrating far-field stress forces.
