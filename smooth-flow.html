Smooth flow (ϕt​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system
OurBigBook.com Words: 617 Articles: 11
A smooth flow is a smooth one-parameter family of diffeomorphisms satisfying ϕ0​=id and ϕt+s​=ϕt​∘ϕs​. Its generating vector field is F(x)=∂t​ϕt​(x)∣t=0​. On a closed manifold, every smooth vector field has a flow defined for all real times.
