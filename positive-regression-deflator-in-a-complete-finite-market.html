Positive regression deflator in a complete finite market (Zt​=Pt⊤​Vt−1​Pt−1​)

Let Vt​=E[Pt​Pt⊤​∣Ft−1​] be positive definite. Market completeness restricts each parent atom to at most n successors, while positive definiteness forces exactly n independent successor price vectors. The positive martingale deflator supplied by the fundamental theorem of asset pricing then has the unique one-step ratio Zt​=Pt⊤​Vt−1​Pt−1​>0. Its product deflates the prices into martingales.
