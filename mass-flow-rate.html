Mass flow rate (M˙=∫S​ρu⋅ndS)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Volumetric flow rate
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
The mass flow rate through an oriented cross-section is the integral of mass density times normal velocity. For constant density it is density times the volumetric flow rate. In a steady pipe without leaks, conservation of mass makes this rate independent of cross-section.
