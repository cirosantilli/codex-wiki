Schrödinger equation from a short-time path integral (i∂t​ψ=(−21​∂q2​+V)ψ)

OurBigBook.com Words: 40
Expand a smooth wavefunction and potential in the normalized short-time Schrödinger kernel. Its odd moment vanishes and its second moment is iΔt, so one step changes the wavefunction by iΔtψ′′/2−iΔtVψ+O(Δt2). Division by the time step gives the Time-dependent Schrödinger equation.
