Group delay (τg​=∂ϕ/∂ω)

 ... Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Wavefront Optical path length Group refractive index
OurBigBook.com Words: 70 Articles: 2
The group delay is the frequency derivative of an unwrapped transfer wave phase, using the e−iωt convention. It predicts the leading delay of a narrow wave envelope when the transfer wave amplitude and the delay vary little across its band; it is not generally a signal-front arrival time.
