Pressure measurement

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 13
A pressure measurement determines pressure relative to a specified reference using an instrument such as a manometer or pressure transducer. Gauge pressure, absolute pressure and differential pressure require different references; measurement calibration and measurement uncertainty determine how the reading can be interpreted.
