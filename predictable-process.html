Predictable process

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale
OurBigBook.com Words: 757 Articles: 19
In discrete time, a process An​ is predictable or previsible when An​ is Fn−1​-measurable for every n≥1. In continuous time, predictability means measurability with respect to the predictable sigma-algebra.
