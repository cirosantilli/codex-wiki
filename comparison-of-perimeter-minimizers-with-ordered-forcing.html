Comparison of perimeter minimizers with ordered forcing (g<h a.e.⟹∣Eg​∖Eh​∣=0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 42
Suppose Eg​ minimizes Per(E)−∫E​g and Eh​ minimizes Per(E)−∫E​h. Compare them with their intersection and union. Adding their optimality inequalities and using submodularity of relative perimeter gives ∫Eg​∖Eh​​(h−g)≤0. Strict ordering forces that set to be null. Applied to g=(f−t)/α, this yields nested ROF denoising superlevel sets.
