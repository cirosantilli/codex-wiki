Axisymmetric membrane deformation (R=R(z))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Lipid bilayer Lipid vesicle
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
An axisymmetric cylindrical membrane deformation is invariant under rotations about the cylinder axis, so its radius depends only on the axial coordinate. Its surface area is 2π∫R1+Rz2​​dz, and its enclosed volume is π∫R2dz. These scalar shape constraints are the basis of the fixed-volume capillary spectrum of a cylindrical membrane.
