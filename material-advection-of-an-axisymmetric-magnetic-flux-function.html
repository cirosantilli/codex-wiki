Material advection of an axisymmetric magnetic flux function (DΨ/Dt=0)

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Axisymmetric magnetohydrodynamic wind Poloidal magnetic flux function
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For an axisymmetric poloidal magnetic field Bp​=∇Ψ×∇ϕ, the ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation gives ∂t​Ψ+up​⋅∇Ψ=0, after fixing the time-dependent additive constant in Ψ. A regular axial reference makes 2πΨ the enclosed magnetic flux. This scalar statement of magnetic flux freezing holds even when rotation also produces a toroidal magnetic field.
