Michael criterion for axisymmetric toroidal-field interchange (D=κ2−μ0​ρr​drd​(rBϕ​​)2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Toroidal magnetic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 251 Articles: 2
For constant-density cylindrical rotation rΩ(r)eϕ​ and a purely toroidal magnetic field, an axisymmetric radial displacement gives bϕ​=(Bϕ​/r−Bϕ′​)ξr​. Combining its curvature force with conservation of angular momentum gives the restoring coefficient D above. A local incompressible radial-vertical mode has ω2=(kz2​/k2)D. Positive D gives stability in this ideal interchange setting; negative D permits growth. This is distinct from weak-field magnetorotational instability: a steeply increasing toroidal field can release current energy even without differential rotation.
