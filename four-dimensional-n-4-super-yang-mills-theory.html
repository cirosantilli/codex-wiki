Four-dimensional N=4 super Yang-Mills theory (N=4 SYM)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supersymmetric gauge theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 1
A four-dimensional N=4 supersymmetric gauge theory has one supersymmetric vector multiplet containing a gauge boson, four Weyl spinors, and six real scalar fields in the adjoint group representation. Its massless helicity multiplicities are 1,4,6,4,1 from helicity 1 to −1.
