Off-axis holography

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Holography
OurBigBook.com Words: 399
Off-axis holography tilts the holographic reference wave relative to the holographic object wave. Its interference fringes acquire a carrier spatial frequency; the two cross terms occupy different spatial frequency regions when the carrier is sufficiently large compared with the object's signal bandwidth. This permits filtering a single image term.
Gabor's lecture credits Emmett Leith and Juris Upatnieks with the later skew-reference method using helium–neon laser illumination, separating reconstructed images angularly.[ref]
For an original periodic calculation, let x run over a unit-length interval and choose
O(x)=0.2e2πi3x+0.1e−2πi5x,R(x)=e2πi20x.
(67)
All spatial frequencies here are cycles per unit length; the data are a chosen field at the recording plane, not measurements or a physical object profile. Direct expansion gives
I(x)=1.05+0.04cos(2π8x)+0.4cos(2π17x)+0.2cos(2π25x).
(68)
Consequently its Fourier series coefficients are 1.05 at zero, 0.02 at ±8, 0.2 at ±17 and 0.1 at ±25. The R∗O component lies at −17,−25; the RO∗ component lies at 17,25; the background lies at 0,±8. Filtering the negative-frequency cross term and multiplying by R recovers O, including its relative wave phase.
The figure samples N=1024 equally spaced positions with the endpoint excluded, computes a normalized discrete Fourier transform using a Fast Fourier transform, retains bins −30≤m≤−12, then applies the inverse discrete Fourier transform and multiplies by R. The recovered real and imaginary parts coincide with the chosen O to numerical precision. This exact periodic example avoids leakage by placing every frequency on an integer bin; finite nonperiodic records need allowance for broadening and an aperture.
For a continuous object field with Fourier transform confined to ∣f∣≤B, the background ∣O∣2 occupies ∣f∣≤2B. A carrier fc​>3B is a sufficient separation condition for the sideband centred at −fc​, and fc​+B must remain below the Nyquist spatial frequency. Here B=5, fc​=20 and the Nyquist spatial frequency is 512. This sufficient bound describes a whole bandwidth, rather than exploiting the gaps in this particular two-component field.
The same example also makes optical playback explicit. Choose t0​=0.3 and β=0.01. Since ∣O∣≤0.3, the bounds 0.49≤I≤1.69 imply 0.3049≤t≤0.3169, and ∣βI∣/t0​≤0.0564. The affine response is stipulated; its weak-modulation interpretation is approximate. In Rt, the desired field 0.01O has frequencies 3,−5; the conjugate term 0.01R2O∗ has frequencies 37,45; the background has frequencies 20,12,28. Thus the same carrier that separates the recorded cross terms separates the playback components. Reillumination restores a field through diffraction; computational filtering extracts the corresponding encoded field through Fourier analysis.
