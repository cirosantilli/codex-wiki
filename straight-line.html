Straight line (L)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 275 Articles: 7
A straight line in Euclidean space is the set L={x0​+tv:t∈R}, for a fixed point x0​ and nonzero direction vector v. Two distinct points determine one such line.
