Local formula for the Chern connection on a vector bundle (A=H−1∂H)

In a holomorphic local frame of a Hermitian holomorphic vector bundle, let H be the matrix of its Hermitian metric on a holomorphic vector bundle. With the convention ∇e=eA, the Chern connection has
A=H−1∂H.
