Maximal annihilator of a module element is prime

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Annihilator (ring theory) Annihilator of a module
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
Over a commutative unital ring, an ideal maximal among annihilators of nonzero elements of a module is a prime ideal. Each such ideal is the annihilator of a module given by the cyclic submodule generated by that element. If P=Ann(m) and ab∈P with b∈/P, then bm=0 and P⊆Ann(bm). Maximality makes these annihilators equal, so a∈P. A Noetherian ring ensures that a nonzero module has a maximal element annihilator by the ascending chain condition on ideals.
