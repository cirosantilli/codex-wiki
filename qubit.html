Qubit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state
OurBigBook.com Words: 24 Articles: 1
A qubit is a two-dimensional quantum system. Its pure state is a ray in a two-dimensional complex Hilbert space and may be written α∣0⟩+β∣1⟩ with ∣α∣2+∣β∣2=1.
