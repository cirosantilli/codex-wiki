Witt space with collared boundary

 ... Geometry and topology Topology Topological space Stratified space Stratified pseudomanifold Witt space
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A compact stratified space with boundary is a Witt space with collared boundary when its interior and boundary are Witt spaces and a stratified neighbourhood of the boundary is ∂X×[0,ε), with product stratification. Orientations are carried by the regular strata and induce the usual boundary orientation.
