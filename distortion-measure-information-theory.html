Distortion measure (information theory, d:X×X→[0,∞))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Rate-distortion theory Rate-distortion function
OurBigBook.com Words: 178 Articles: 3
A distortion measure assigns a nonnegative cost to replacing a source symbol x by a reconstruction x^. It need not be a metric: the two alphabets may differ, and neither symmetry nor the triangle inequality is required. Choosing the cost is part of the model. For numerical symbols, d(x,x^)=(x−x^)2 gives mean squared error after averaging; for exact symbol agreement, use Hamming distortion.
