The last term has absolute value O((xh2+h)(logx)k). Choose h=(logx)(β−k)/2 to obtain the displayed bound, with an additional O((logx)k) term harmless as x→∞ for fixed k. This elementary finite-difference argument converts a logarithmically smoothed Perron formula bound to cancellation in its original partial sum.
