Effective degrees of freedom (tr(H))

 ... Statistical model Statistical modelling Normal linear model Linear regression Fitted values Linear smoother
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
For a linear smoother Y=HY, its effective degrees of freedom are tr(H). This measures sensitivity of fitted values to responses even when H is not an orthogonal projection.
