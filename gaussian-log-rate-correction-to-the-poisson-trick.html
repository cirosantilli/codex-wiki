Gaussian log-rate correction to the Poisson trick (R(S)=EΓ(n,1)​e−(logV−logS)2/(2s2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 49
A mean-zero normal distribution prior of variance s2 on the log baseline multiplies the multinomial kernel by the displayed correction, up to a constant independent of coefficients. The factor is not constant in S, so the posterior equivalence is approximate. Dominated convergence makes it tend to one as s2 tends to infinity.
