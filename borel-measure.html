Borel measure

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 471 Articles: 7
A Borel measure on a topological space is a measure defined on the sigma-algebra generated by the open sets, equivalently on the Borel sets.
