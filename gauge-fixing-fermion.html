Gauge-fixing fermion (Ψ)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing BRST symmetry
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 3
A gauge-fixing fermion is a Grassmann-odd functional whose BRST transformation sΨ supplies the gauge-fixing and ghost terms. Nilpotence gives s(sΨ)=0 immediately.
