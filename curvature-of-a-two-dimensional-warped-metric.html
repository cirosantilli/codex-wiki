Curvature of a two-dimensional warped metric (ds2=dx2+f(x)2dy2,K=−f′′/f)

OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For positive twice differentiable f, the only nonzero Christoffel symbols are Γyyx​=−ff′ and Γxyy​=Γyxy​=f′/f. In the convention Rdabc​=∂b​Γacd​−∂c​Γabd​+Γace​Γebd​−Γabe​Γecd​, substitution gives Rxyxy​=−ff′′. Dividing Rxyxy​ by gxx​gyy​−gxy2​=f2 gives the Gaussian curvature K=−f′′/f. Thus f=x gives the flat metric in polar coordinates away from its axis, f=sinx gives unit positive curvature, and f=sinhx gives unit negative curvature.
