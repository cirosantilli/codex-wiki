Simplicial complex

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 36
An abstract simplicial complex is a pair K=(V,Σ), where V is a set of vertices of a simplicial complex and Σ is a collection of finite nonempty subsets, called simplexes. Every singleton {v} belongs to Σ, and every nonempty subset of a simplex is a simplex. Its dimension is the supremum of ∣σ∣−1 over σ∈Σ. Some conventions also include an empty simplex; it contributes no points to the geometric realization.
