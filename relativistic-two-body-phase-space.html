Relativistic two-body phase space (dΩdΦ2​​=16π2s​k​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering Scattering amplitude Relativistic scattering cross-section Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For two outgoing particles with centre-of-mass momentum magnitude k and total energy s​, integrating the four-momentum delta function gives dΦ2​/dΩ=k/(16π2s​). A factor 1/2! for identical outgoing particles is inserted when counting events over the full phase space, not into this labelled kinematic measure itself.
