Quark-hadron duality

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Quark-hadron duality is the correspondence, in suitable inclusive or averaged observables, between a quark-level description and a sum over hadronic states. Asymptotic freedom supports perturbative short-distance production, but sharp resonances and threshold regions can invalidate a pointwise partonic approximation. Inclusive predictions can have both radiative and nonperturbative corrections.
