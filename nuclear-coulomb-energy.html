Nuclear Coulomb energy (EC​≃aC​Z2/A1/3)

OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
Nuclear Coulomb energy is the positive electrostatic mass-energy contribution from proton repulsion in a nucleus. A uniform-charge model gives scaling Z2/R, and approximately constant nuclear density gives R∝A1/3. It reduces conventional positive nuclear binding energy.
