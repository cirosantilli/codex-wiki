Symmetric top with a quadratic alignment potential (κℓ2=mgh+AΩ2cosα−CnΩ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics Symmetric top
OurBigBook.com Words: 116
Consider a symmetric top with fixed vertex Q, axial unit vector e, principal moments of inertia A,A,C, and centre of mass at he. Let a force κℓ(k−e) act at ℓe, where k is the upward vertical unit vector. Together with gravity, it produces torque (κℓ2−mgh)e×k and potential energy mghk⋅e+κℓ2(1−k⋅e).
The axial angular velocity of a symmetric top n, vertical angular momentum k⋅L and total mechanical energy are conserved. For steady precession, e˙=Ωk×e and k⋅e=cosα. Substituting e×e¨=Ω2cosαe×k into Ae×e¨+Cne˙=(κℓ2−mgh)e×k gives the condition in the title for nonvertical inclination. If the total angular velocity itself is Ωk, then n=Ωcosα, and the condition becomes κℓ2=mgh+(A−C)Ω2cosα.
