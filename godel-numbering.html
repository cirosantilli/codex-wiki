Gödel numbering

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 18
A Gödel numbering assigns natural numbers effectively to symbols, formulas, and finite proofs. Syntactic predicates such as “p codes a proof in T of the formula with code q” can then be represented by arithmetical formulas.
