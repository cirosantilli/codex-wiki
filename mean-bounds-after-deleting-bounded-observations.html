Mean bounds after deleting bounded observations (yˉ​remaining​∈[(Nyˉ​−db)/(N−d),(Nyˉ​−da)/(N−d)])

OurBigBook.com Words: 89
Suppose N>d≥0 observations lie in [a,b] and have sample mean yˉ​. Delete any d observations. The remaining sample mean obeys
N−dNyˉ​−db​≤yˉ​remaining​≤N−dNyˉ​−da​.
(29)
Indeed, the removed sum is between da and db; subtract it from the original sum Nyˉ​ and divide by the remaining positive count. If only a rounded mean in [ℓ,u] is known, a valid enclosing interval is [(Nℓ−db)/(N−d),(Nu−da)/(N−d)], optionally intersected with [a,b]. These deterministic bounds do not require missing at random. They constrain a complete-case summary without claiming that complete-case selection is unbiased for a target population.
