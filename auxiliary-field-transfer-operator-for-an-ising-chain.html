Auxiliary-field transfer operator for an Ising chain (T(m′,m)=exp[−D(m′−m)2−{U(m′)+U(m)}/2])

For a nearest-neighbor auxiliary-field representation of an Ising model, sharing each local potential between adjacent bonds gives the displayed symmetric integral operator. Its periodic partition function is the trace of its Nth power, up to Gaussian normalization constants. In the slowly varying continuum limit, a slice of length ϵ has kernel [M/(2πϵ)]1/2exp[−M(m′−m)2/(2ϵ)−ϵ{U(m′)+U(m)}/2]. Expanding its action on a smooth function gives 1−ϵH+O(ϵ2), with H=−(2M)−1∂m2​+U. Thus fixed endpoints give an imaginary-time transition kernel and periodic endpoints give a Euclidean path integral trace. The finite-spacing integral operator and the continuum differential operator need not have identical high-energy spectra.
