Siegel zero

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Classical zero-free region for Dirichlet L-functions
OurBigBook.com Words: 231 Articles: 4
An exceptional zero is the possible real simple zero β omitted from the otherwise uniform classical zero-free region for Dirichlet L-functions. It belongs to a real nonprincipal primitive Dirichlet character and satisfies β<1.
