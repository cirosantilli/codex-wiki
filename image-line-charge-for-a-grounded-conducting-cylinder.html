Image line charge for a grounded conducting cylinder (rimage​=a2/b)

 Home Mathematics Method of images
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 1
A real infinite line charge λ lies at distance b>a from the axis of a grounded conducting cylinder of radius a. An image line −λ at radius a2/b lies inside the excluded conductor. On the circle, real and image distances obey r1​/r2​=b/a, so ϕ=(λ/(2πϵ0​))ln[(b/a)r2​/r1​] vanishes there. The image field solves the physical exterior problem with bounded far potential. Its additive constant matters; the interior image field is not a physical field inside the conductor.
