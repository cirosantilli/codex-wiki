Isospectral manifolds (Spec(M)=Spec(N))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Spectral geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 26
Two Riemannian manifolds are isospectral for a specified differential operator if their eigenvalues, counted with multiplicities and with the same boundary conditions, agree. Riemannian isometries preserve the Laplace-Beltrami operator spectrum, but the converse can fail. The transplantation theorem and the Sunada theorem give systematic constructions.
