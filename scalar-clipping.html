Scalar clipping (g(x)=min(b,max(a,x)))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological space Continuous map
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The continuous map projecting a real number onto the closed interval [a,b], with a≤b. It equals a below the interval, equals the input inside it, and equals b above it. It is nondecreasing and has Lipschitz constant one.
