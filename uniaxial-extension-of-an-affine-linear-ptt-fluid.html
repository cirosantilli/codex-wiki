For uniaxial extensional flow put q=τγ˙​>0, f=1+αtrA and T=f−2q. The diagonal components are a11​=2q/T, a22​=a33​=−q/(T+3q). The identity f−1=αtrA gives the cubic equation in the title. For α>0 its physical root has T>max(0,1−2q) and connects to T=1 at rest. Its extensional viscosity is ηE​=G0​τ[2/T+1/(T+3q)], tending to 3G0​τ at small q and 2G0​τ/α at large q. At α=0 the stable steady Maxwell extension instead ends at q=1/2; the finite high-rate limit must not be extended to that degenerate case.
