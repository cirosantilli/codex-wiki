This is the B1 paper for the MSc in mathematical modelling and scientific computing course of the University of Oxford. Questions 1–2 have original solutions below. The official revision page identifies public model solutions to questions 3–4, so those questions are skipped. The programme also advertises a student-only past-papers-and-solutions folder; its exact coverage of this paper has not been established.
