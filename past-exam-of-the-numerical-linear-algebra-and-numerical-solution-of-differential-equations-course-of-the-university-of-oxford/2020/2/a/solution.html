A constant-coefficient linear multistep method, normalized so that αk​=1, has the form
∑j=0k​αj​Yn+j​=h∑j=0k​βj​f(xn+j​,Yn+j​),xn​=x0​+nh.
(9)
Here Yn​ approximates the solution y(xn​) of the initial value problem, and Y0​,…,Yk−1​ are starting approximations. The coefficients are independent of n and h. If βk​=0, the update is explicit; otherwise it is an implicit time-stepping method, whose new value must satisfy the displayed equation.
