The assumed differentiability makes the remainders uniform on 0≤x≤1−h. Subtracting gives the consistency error
Tn​=h(21​−β)y′′(xn​)+O(h2).​

Because Kh​≤L(1+L) for h≤1, the prefactor is bounded independently of the mesh. When β=1/2, the consistency error is O(h2), hence the maximum global error is O(h2). This proves at least second-order numerical convergence of the explicit midpoint method.
