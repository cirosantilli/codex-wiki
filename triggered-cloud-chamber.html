Triggered cloud chamber

 Home Physics Particle detection Particle detector Cloud chamber Expansion cloud chamber
OurBigBook.com Words: 435 Articles: 1
A triggered cloud chamber expands in response to an event indicated by other detectors, rather than hoping that a particle arrives during a randomly chosen sensitive interval. The ionization trail is produced before expansion. The apparatus must respond while the ions remain sufficiently localized to make a useful photograph.
