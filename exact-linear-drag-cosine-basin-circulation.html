Exact linear-drag cosine basin circulation (rΨ′′+βΨ′−rm2Ψ=F0​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For streamfunction ψ=Ψ(x)cos(my) with impermeable walls at x=0,L, let A=−F0​/(rm2) and positive exponents satisfy λ−γ=β/r, λγ=m2. The exact profile is Ψ=A[1−CW​e−λx−CE​eγ(x−L)], where CW​=(1−e−γL)/(1−e−(λ+γ)L) and CE​=(1−e−λL)/(1−e−(λ+γ)L). Substitution proves the ordinary differential equation and both boundary conditions. The usual narrow western boundary current limit requires λL≫1 and γL≪1, with λ≃β/r and γ≃rm2/β; the opposite first inequality does not describe weak drag.
