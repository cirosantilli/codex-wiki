Gaussian orthogonal ensemble (GOE(n))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Random matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
In the unscaled variance-one convention, a Gaussian orthogonal ensemble matrix M is a real symmetric matrix whose upper-triangular entries are independent, with Mij​∼N(0,1) for i<j and Mii​∼N(0,2). Its joint probability density function relative to the upper-triangular coordinates is proportional to exp[−tr(M2)/4]. The scaled convention uses M/n​; the scaling must be stated. If X has independent standard normal entries, (X+XT)/2​ realizes the unscaled convention. Expanding in the entries of X gives the covariance identity E[Mij​Mkl​]=δik​δjl​+δil​δjk​, with Kronecker deltas.
