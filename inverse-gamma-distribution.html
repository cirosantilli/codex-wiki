Inverse-gamma distribution

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Continuous probability distribution Gamma distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 76 Articles: 1
An inverse-gamma random variable is the reciprocal of a gamma distribution random variable up to scale. In shape-scale parameters its density is proportional to x−a−1e−b/x on x>0.
