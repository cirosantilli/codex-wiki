Nucleotide

 Home Chemistry Molecule
OurBigBook.com Words: 717 Articles: 3
A nucleotide contains a nucleobase, a sugar, and one or more phosphate groups. In DNA and RNA, linked nucleotides form a polymer whose nucleotide sequence carries information. The sugar is deoxyribose in DNA and ribose in RNA. See NHGRI: nucleotide.
