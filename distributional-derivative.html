Distributional derivative

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 404 Articles: 8
For a distribution u and a multi-index α, its distributional derivative is defined by
⟨Dαu,φ⟩=(−1)∣α∣⟨u,Dαφ⟩.
