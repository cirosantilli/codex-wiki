Shallow-water quasi-geostrophic inversion ([∇h2​−LD−2​]ψ=q−βy)

OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The shallow-water quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity relation is a modified Helmholtz equation for the quasi-geostrophic streamfunction. Its inverse with specified boundary conditions recovers horizontal geostrophic flow and surface elevation η=f0​ψ/g. The Rossby deformation radius LD​=gH​/∣f0​∣ is the range of the balanced response: for a horizontal Fourier component, ψ​=−q​/(∣kh​∣2+LD−2​), after subtracting background potential vorticity.
