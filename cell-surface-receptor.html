Cell-surface receptor

 Home Biology Protein Receptor (biochemistry)
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 7
A cell-surface receptor detects an extracellular signal at the cell membrane. Its changed state affects the intracellular side, coupling reception to signal transduction or directly to an ion channel's opening.
