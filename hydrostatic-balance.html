Hydrostatic balance (pz​=−ρg)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Hydrostatic pressure Hydrostatic approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 86 Articles: 1
Vertical pressure gradient balances gravitational force per unit volume. Subtracting the resting hydrostatic state and using kinematic pressure gives pz′​=b′ for a Boussinesq approximation buoyancy anomaly. The approximation neglects vertical acceleration; this diagnostic relation is what couples buoyancy perturbation to the vertical pressure profile.
