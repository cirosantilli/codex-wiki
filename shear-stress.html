Shear stress (τ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Newtonian fluid Newtonian fluid stress tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 51 Articles: 1
Shear stress is the tangential component of traction on a surface. For leading unidirectional flow u(y) in a Newtonian fluid, τ=μdu/dy.
