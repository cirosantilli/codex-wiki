Correlation-length critical exponent (ν)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Critical exponent
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
The correlation-length critical exponent is defined by ξ∼∣t∣−ν. At a renormalization-group fixed point, its reciprocal is the positive eigenvalue of the temperature-like scaling direction.
