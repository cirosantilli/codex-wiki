Zero surgery on a connected sum as a splice

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Splice of knots
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
Let Y1′​=X1​(λ1​) and orient its filling core K1′​ so that its longitude is μ1​. Then zero surgery on K1​#K2​ is the splice of (Y1′​,K1′​) and (Y2​,K2​). Cutting the connected-sum exterior along its decomposing annulus and applying the annulus-quotient model of Dehn filling identifies λ1​ with λ2​ and μ1​ with μ2​, which is exactly the splice gluing.
