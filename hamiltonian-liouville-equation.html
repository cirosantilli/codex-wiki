Hamiltonian Liouville equation (∂t​f+∇v​H⋅∇x​f−∇x​H⋅∇v​f=h)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Kinetic theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 252 Articles: 5
Transport a phase-space density along Hamilton's equations. The Hamiltonian vector field is b=(∇v​H,−∇x​H) and the equation is ∂t​f+b⋅∇x,v​f=h. Its zero divergence gives volume-preserving transport. For h=0, the density is constant along characteristics; a source is integrated by the Duhamel formula for Hamiltonian transport. This is distinct from the elliptic Liouville equation.
