Extinction learning

 Home Biology Animal behaviour Learning Classical conditioning
OurBigBook.com Words: 189 Articles: 5
Learning during presentations of a previously predictive cue without the expected outcome. Reduced responding need not mean that the original association has been erased. Context, delay and subsequent experience can alter which association is retrieved.
