Porous medium equation (ut​=Δ(um))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Diffusion equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 585 Articles: 10
The porous medium equation ut​=Δ(um) with m>1 is a nonlinear partial differential equation whose diffusivity vanishes at u=0. Unlike the heat equation, it has compactly supported solutions with finite propagation speed.
