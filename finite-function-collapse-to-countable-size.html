Finite-function collapse to countable size (Col(ω,κ))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Forcing
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
For an infinite ground-model cardinal number κ, use finite partial functions from ω to κ, ordered by reverse inclusion. The union of a generic filter is a surjection ω→κ. The order has size κ, hence the κ+-chain condition, and preserves cardinals at least κ+.
