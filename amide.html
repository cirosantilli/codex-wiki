Amide (R−C(=O)−NR′R′′)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
Here an amide means a carbonyl group whose carbon is bonded directly to nitrogen; each of R, R′ and R′′ may be hydrogen or a group attached through carbon. A peptide bond contains this linkage. Chemical resonance distinguishes its nitrogen from that of an amine. [ref] and [ref]
