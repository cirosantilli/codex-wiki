For category counts y1​,…,yk​ with fixed total n and probabilities p1​,…,pk​, the multinomial likelihood is
L(p1​,…,pk​)=∏j​yj​!n!​∏j​pjyj​​,∑j​pj​=1.
(8)
Its maximum-likelihood fitted probabilities are p​j​=yj​/n.
