Positive compression α contributes negative quadratic potential energy −αϕx2​/2, while positive bending stiffness β contributes βϕxx2​/2. A plane wave has ω2=(βk4−αk2)/ρ, so sufficiently long waves grow exponentially. For displacement-fixed, moment-free endpoints at 0,l, the natural boundary conditions for a free endpoint on the slope give ϕxx​=0 as well as ϕ=0. The modes are sin(nπx/l), and the first Euler buckling of an elastic filament threshold is αc​=βπ2/l2.
