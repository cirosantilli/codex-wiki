Ultrametric space

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Absolute value on a field Non-Archimedean absolute value Ultrametric inequality
OurBigBook.com Words: 41 Articles: 1
An ultrametric space is a metric space satisfying the ultrametric inequality. Its open balls are clopen, and any two balls are either disjoint or one contains the other.
