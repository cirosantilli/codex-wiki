Characteristic variety of a Weyl algebra module (Supp(grM))

 ... Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Gelfand–Kirillov dimension Gelfand–Kirillov dimension of a module Bernstein growth dimension of a Weyl algebra module
OurBigBook.com Words: 128 Articles: 1
A good Bernstein filtration on a finitely generated module produces a finite graded module over grAn​≅C[x1​,…,xn​,ξ1​,…,ξn​]. Its support is the characteristic variety for this filtration. Its algebraic dimension is the Bernstein growth dimension of a Weyl algebra module, which is independent of the choice of good filtration.
