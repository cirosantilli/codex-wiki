Transmission through a sequence of linear polarizers (Iout​=(Iin​/2)∏j=2N​cos2(θj​−θj−1​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For ideal polarizers with successive transverse transmission directions θj​, the Jones matrix is the rank-one orthogonal projection Pj​=uj​ujT​. The identity ujT​uj−1​=cos(θj​−θj−1​) reduces the transmitted probability amplitude analogue, here the optical complex amplitude, to a product of projections. For unpolarized light, the first element transmits half the incident light intensity, giving the displayed product. For a specified incident linear polarization, replace the first half-factor with its actual projection.
