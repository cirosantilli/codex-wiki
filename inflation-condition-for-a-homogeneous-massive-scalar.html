Inflation condition for a homogeneous massive scalar (ϕ˙​2<m2ϕ2/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic inflation
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
In flat general relativity with no separate cosmological constant, a canonical massive scalar has ρ+3P=2ϕ˙​2−m2ϕ2. Accelerated expansion therefore requires ϕ˙​2<m2ϕ2/2. Initial potential dominance is an instantaneous condition and does not alone fix the duration.
