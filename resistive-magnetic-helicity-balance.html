Resistive magnetic-helicity balance (H˙=−2ηm​∫V​B⋅(∇×B)dV)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential Magnetic helicity
OurBigBook.com Words: 183 Articles: 1
For a magnetic vector potential A, a solenoidal magnetic field B=∇×A and constant magnetic diffusivity ηm​, write ∂t​A=u×B−ηm​∇×B−∇ϕ. In a fixed closed volume with zero helicity surface flux, integration by parts makes the two differentiated factors in H=∫V​A⋅BdV equal; the scalar-potential boundary term vanishes when B⋅n=0. One sufficient boundary gauge is n×A=0; another standard setting is integration over all space with sufficient decay. Orthogonality of u×B to B gives the displayed identity. Unlike the resistive magnetic energy loss, H˙ is not sign definite. The zero normal magnetic field also makes H gauge invariance under single-valued smooth gauge transformations.
