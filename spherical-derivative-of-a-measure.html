Spherical derivative of a measure (Ds​μ(x)=limr↓0​μ(B(x,r))/λd​(B(x,r)))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Measure density
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 1
The spherical derivative compares the mass of shrinking centered open balls with their Lebesgue measure, when the limit exists. This is a density of one measure relative to Euclidean volume, rather than the relative density of a set within one fixed measure. A singular finite Borel measure has spherical derivative zero Lebesgue almost everywhere.
