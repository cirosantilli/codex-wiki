Dynamic light scattering

 Home Biology Biophysical measurement
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Dynamic light scattering analyses fluctuations in scattered light to infer diffusion and hydrodynamic size under a suitable model. Large particles can dominate the intensity, making it sensitive to aggregation but limiting simple interpretation as a number distribution.
