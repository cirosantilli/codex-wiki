Coefficient of kinetic friction (μk​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newtonian mechanics Friction Kinetic friction
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
The coefficient of kinetic friction is the dimensionless quantity μk​=∣fk​∣/N for sliding contact with positive normal force N in the ideal kinetic friction model. It characterizes the pair of contacting surfaces and their condition. A constant μk​ means the model neglects dependence on contact slip speed; unlike the coefficient of static friction, it specifies a sliding force magnitude rather than a no-slip upper bound.
