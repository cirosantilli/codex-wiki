Percentage (x%=x/100)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Division (mathematics) Ratio
OurBigBook.com Words: 70 Articles: 1
A percentage expresses a ratio in hundredths: p as a fraction is 100p%. For example, 18/60=0.3=30%. Comparing two percentages requires specifying whether the change is an absolute difference in percentage points or a relative change divided by the initial value.
