Laboratory paths of an equal-mass binary

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Two-body problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
For equal masses and relative position r, the individual positions are rA​=R−r/2 and rB​=R+r/2, where the center of mass has uniform velocity in an isolated system. Bound inverse-square force motion makes r an ellipse; each mass follows a half-size ellipse in the center-of-mass frame. Adding a nonzero center-of-mass drift generally makes their laboratory trajectories nonclosed. The relative orbital period is not necessarily a period of either laboratory position.
