Frame dragging inside a slowly rotating spherical shell (ω=3R4GM​Ω)

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole Kerr black hole Lense-Thirring precession
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 1
For a weakly gravitating thin spherical shell of mass M, radius R and uniform angular velocity Ω, its interior linearized gravity metric has constant scalar potentials A=−GM/R, C=GM/R and g0i​=ω(z×x)i​. The scalar mass shell is a monopole. Its mass current is a dipole: regular interior and decaying exterior radial solutions are proportional to r and r−2, and their derivative jump fixes the factor 4/3. Local inertial axes co-rotate with the shell at ω relative to asymptotic nonrotating axes. This is the leading interior Lense-Thirring precession, neglecting quadratic rotation and higher gravitational orders.
