Adenosine

 Home Chemistry Molecule Nucleoside
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
Adenosine consists of adenine joined to ribose. Adding one, two, or three phosphate groups at the ribose's 5′ position gives AMP, ADP, or ATP, respectively. See Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell: the components of nucleotides.
