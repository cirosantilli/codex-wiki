Many-source queue scaling (L−1logP(QL​>Lb)→−infn≥1​In​(b+cn))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory Scaling limit of a queue
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
Scale independent source count, service and buffer by the same factor L. For lookback horizon n, the source-average cumulative input has rate function In​, and reaching buffer level b requires input above b+cn. Optimizing this horizon gives the displayed exponent under uniform control of the infinite stationary supremum. For independent slots, In​(x)=nIA​(x/n); discrete time keeps n an integer. Temporal dependence instead enters through the cumulative-input cumulant-generating function.
