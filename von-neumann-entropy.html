Von Neumann entropy (S(ρ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 52
The Von Neumann entropy of a density operator is
S(ρ)=−Tr(ρlogρ).
(1)
It vanishes for a pure state and measures mixedness and quantum entanglement in reduced states.

Entropy continuity from Jordan decomposition (∣S(ρ)−S(σ)∣≤δlog2​d+(1+δ)h2​(δ/(1+δ)))

Words: 60
Von Neumann entropy OurBigBook.com
For density operators in dimension d, let δ=21​∥ρ−σ∥1​. The positive and negative parts of their difference have the same trace δ. Normalize them as τ+​,τ−​ and write (ρ+δτ−​)/(1+δ)=(σ+δτ+​)/(1+δ). Applying the two entropy bounds for a quantum mixture to this common state proves the displayed bound. The function g(δ)=(1+δ)h2​(δ/(1+δ)) is increasing and concave, with g(0)=0, which also controls average entropy errors in pure-source compression.

For density operators supported on mutually orthogonal subspaces, the eigenvalues of their mixture are pi​λij​, where λij​ are the eigenvalues of ρi​. Substitution into the definition of Von Neumann entropy gives the displayed decomposition into Shannon entropy of the label and the mean entropy within each block. Zero eigenvalues use 0log0=0.

Entropy of a binary diagonal qubit mixture (S((1−u)∣0⟩⟨0∣+u∣1⟩⟨1∣)=h2​(u))

Words: 57
Von Neumann entropy OurBigBook.com
A mixture of two orthogonal pure qubit states has Von Neumann entropy equal to the binary entropy of its weights. Its entropy difference from the first pure state is exactly h2​(u) and its trace distance from that state is u. The elementary bound −(1−u)ln(1−u)≤u gives h2​(u)≤ulog2​(e/u); entropy also never exceeds one bit. For small u, the leading term is ulog2​(1/u).

If σ=∑i​Pi​ρPi​ is a nonselective projective measurement, then S(σ)≥S(ρ). Equality holds exactly when ρ is already block diagonal in the measurement decomposition, equivalently [ρ,Pi​]=0 for every i.

Relative entropy of a pinched state (D(ρ∥P(ρ))=S(P(ρ))−S(ρ))

Words: 62
Entropy increase under nonselective projective measurement OurBigBook.com
The pinching map is self-adjoint for the trace pairing, and logP(ρ) is block diagonal. Thus TrρlogP(ρ)=TrP(ρ)logP(ρ). The kernel of P(ρ) is contained in that of ρ, so the relative entropy is finite on its support. Expanding the quantum relative entropy gives the displayed identity. Its nonnegativity proves entropy increase, with equality exactly when the original state is already block diagonal.

Entanglement entropy (SA​=−Tr(ρA​logρA​))

Words: 559 Articles: 11
Von Neumann entropy OurBigBook.com
For a bipartite pure state, the entanglement entropy is the Von Neumann entropy of either reduced density matrix. The two reduced states have the same nonzero eigenvalues, so their entropies agree.

Average monotonicity of pure-state entanglement entropy (∑r​pr​S(ρA,r​)≤S(ρA​))

Words: 85
Entanglement entropy OurBigBook.com
For a bipartite pure state, entanglement entropy is the Von Neumann entropy of either reduced density matrix. When Alice makes a local measurement with pure conditional branches, Bob's reductions average to his original reduction. Concavity of Von Neumann entropy therefore gives ∑r​pr​S(ρB,r​)≤S(ρB​). When Bob measures, apply concavity to Alice's reductions. In each pure branch the two reduced entropies coincide by the Schmidt decomposition. Refining measurement outcomes to individual Kraus operators and iterating proves the inequality for arbitrary finite-round LOCC. Discarded local systems can be measured and their outcomes refined as well, so discarding does not evade the bound.

Schmidt decomposition

Every finite-dimensional bipartite pure state has a decomposition ∣ψ⟩=∑j=1r​sj​∣aj​⟩∣bj​⟩ with positive Schmidt coefficients sj​ and orthonormal families on each subsystem.

Operator Schmidt rank (OSR(K)=min{r:K=∑j=1r​Aj​⊗Bj​})

Words: 126 Articles: 1
Schmidt decomposition OurBigBook.com
The operator Schmidt rank of a bipartite linear operator is its shortest expansion as a sum of product linear operators. Expanding in local orthonormal operator bases gives a coefficient matrix; its matrix rank is the operator Schmidt rank, by a singular value decomposition. It is unchanged by invertible local operator-basis changes. A rank-one projector onto a bipartite pure state of Schmidt rank d has operator Schmidt rank d2: its expansion contains all d2 independent products of local matrix units.
Local Kraus rank bound from an entangled resource (OSR(Kμν​)≤R)
Words: 62
Operator Schmidt rank OurBigBook.com
If two parties share a pure resource of Schmidt rank R, a fully refined branch of their local operations has Kraus operator Kμν​=∑j=1R​pj​​Aμj​⊗Bνj​. Hence its operator Schmidt rank is at most R. Local ancillas, locally adaptive readouts and refined discarded environments are included in the branch operators. Shared classical randomness only mixes such branches. Classical comparison of records does not increase their operator Schmidt rank.

For the pure state λ0​∣00⟩+λ1​∣11⟩ with real normalized Schmidt coefficients, the two-body Pauli correlators form T=diag(s,−s,1), s=2λ0​λ1​. Thus arbitrary local axes give ⟨(a⋅σ)⊗(b⋅σ)⟩=s(ax​bx​−ay​by​)+az​bz​. The negative yy component follows from the phases in the Pauli operators.

Schmidt-rank contraction under product operators (rank(AMBT)≤rankM)
Words: 62
Schmidt rank OurBigBook.com
The coefficient matrix of a bipartite pure state has rank equal to its Schmidt rank, by singular value decomposition. A local product operation A⊗B sends it to AMBT, whose rank cannot increase. A complete classical transcript of an LOCC protocol still selects one product Kraus operator, so every nonzero branch obeys the bound. Discarding the transcript gives a mixed output with Schmidt number at most the initial rank.
Monotonicity of Schmidt rank under LOCC (rout​≤rin​)
Words: 66
Schmidt rank OurBigBook.com
The Schmidt rank of a bipartite pure state cannot increase under local operations, even in a nonzero selected branch. A coefficient matrix C changes under a local product operator to ACBT, whose rank is at most that of C. For deterministic exact conversions the same conclusion follows from Nielsen's pure-state conversion theorem: at the input rank r, the majorization inequality forces all output coefficients after r to vanish.
Maximally entangled overlap bound from Schmidt rank (F(∣Φd​⟩⟨Φd​∣,∣ζ⟩⟨ζ∣)≤r/d​)
Words: 48
Schmidt rank OurBigBook.com
A normalized bipartite pure state of Schmidt rank r has quantum fidelity at most r/d​ with a fixed maximally entangled state on d⊗d. Its coefficient matrix has matrix rank r and Hilbert-Schmidt norm one. Its overlap is the trace of that matrix divided by d​; trace duality and the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality bound this by r/d​. Uniform Schmidt coefficients on r matching basis pairs attain equality.

Every tripartite density operator satisfies S(AC)+S(BC)≥S(A)+S(B). Purify to ABCD and apply Strong subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy to ACD, conditioned on A. The Schmidt decomposition gives S(AD)=S(BC) and S(ACD)=S(B), proving the inequality. The original tripartite state need not be pure.

For density operators ρ1​,ρ2​ and 0≤p≤1, Von Neumann entropy is concave:
S(pρ1​+(1−p)ρ2​)≥pS(ρ1​)+(1−p)S(ρ2​).
(6)

Strict concavity of Von Neumann entropy

Words: 44
Concavity of Von Neumann entropy OurBigBook.com
For 0<b<1 and distinct density operators, the Von Neumann entropy of ρ=bρ1​+(1−b)ρ2​ is strictly larger than the corresponding average. The difference is bD(ρ1​∥ρ)+(1−b)D(ρ2​∥ρ), positive by nonnegativity of quantum relative entropy unless both states coincide. On the closed interval 0≤b≤1, equality holds exactly when b=0, b=1, or ρ1​=ρ2​.

Entropy bounds for a quantum mixture (∑i​pi​S(ρi​)≤S(∑i​pi​ρi​)≤H(p)+∑i​pi​S(ρi​))

Words: 77
Concavity of Von Neumann entropy OurBigBook.com
For a mixture of density operators, the lower bound follows from S(ρˉ​)−∑i​pi​S(ρi​)=∑i​pi​D(ρi​∥ρˉ​)≥0, with equality precisely when all positive-weight states coincide. For the upper bound, resolve each state into pure eigenvectors and form the Gram matrix of the resulting weighted vectors. Its nonzero eigenvalues are those of ρˉ​, while its diagonal has probabilities pi​λia​. Averaging diagonal unitary conjugations deletes its off-diagonal entries; Concavity of Von Neumann entropy therefore gives the bound. Equality holds exactly when positive-weight component supports are mutually orthogonal.

Pure-state negative conditional entropy criterion (S(B∣A)=−S(ρA​)<0⟺∣ψAB​⟩ is entangled)

Words: 56
Quantum conditional entropy OurBigBook.com
For a bipartite pure state with finite Von Neumann entropy, the joint Von Neumann entropy is zero, so its quantum conditional entropy is −S(ρA​). The Schmidt decomposition makes the nonzero eigenvalues of ρA​ the squared Schmidt coefficients. Their Shannon entropy vanishes exactly when there is one coefficient, equivalently the joint pure state is a product state. Thus negative quantum conditional entropy detects exactly the entangled bipartite pure states; it is only a sufficient criterion for entanglement of general mixed density operators.

Relative entropy of classically flagged states (D(⨁i​pi​ρi​∥⨁i​pi​σi​)=∑i​pi​D(ρi​∥σi​))

For common classical weights, the matrix logarithm on each positive-weight block is log(pi​ρi​)=(logpi​)I+logρi​ on its support. The two logarithms of pi​ cancel in the quantum relative entropy, giving the displayed identity. Zero-weight blocks are omitted, including if their unweighted relative entropy is infinite. For density operators, Klein's inequality gives D(ρ∥σ)≥0, with equality exactly when ρ=σ. If the support of a positive operator ρ is not contained in that of σ, the quantum relative entropy is +∞.

Eigenbasis proof of quantum relative entropy nonnegativity

Words: 58
Nonnegativity of quantum relative entropy OurBigBook.com
Diagonalize two density operators with eigenvalues ri​,sj​, and put qij​=∣⟨i∣j⟩∣2. This overlap matrix has row and column sums one. The scalar inequality uln(u/v)≥u−v yields D(ρ∥σ)≥∑ij​qij​(ri​−sj​)=0 in natural units; division by ln2 gives bits. Equality forces ri​=sj​ whenever qij​>0, so σ∣i⟩=ri​∣i⟩ and ρ=σ. Zero eigenvalues use limits, and failure of support inclusion gives infinite quantum relative entropy.

Donald's identity

Words: 110 Articles: 1
Quantum relative entropy OurBigBook.com
For an ensemble of density operators with average ω=∑j​pj​ωj​,
∑j​pj​D(ωj​∥ρ)=∑j​pj​D(ωj​∥ω)+D(ω∥ρ).
(12)
Expanding the trace definitions cancels the logωj​ terms, and the remaining sum combines to a trace against ω. Positive-weight ensemble states have their support contained in that of the average, so the first sum on the right is finite in finite dimension. If the reference-state support condition fails, both sides are +∞. The identity is associated with Donald's work on relative entropy.

The minimum is the Holevo quantity S(ω)−∑j​pj​S(ωj​), equivalently the quantum mutual information of the associated classical-quantum state.

Postselection can increase conditional quantum mutual information
Words: 90
Data processing for quantum mutual information OurBigBook.com
Let a uniformly random bit A be copied into a three-state classical register B with probability ε, and otherwise replaced in B by a flagged erasure independent of A. Then I(A:B)=ε bits. Projecting B onto its two nonerased states and conditioning on success gives a perfectly correlated bit pair with mutual information one. The postselection branch is completely positive and trace decreasing, but renormalization after conditioning is not a deterministic quantum channel. This does not contradict data processing for quantum mutual information, which applies to trace-preserving operations or an unconditioned instrument including its outcome flag.
Mutual-information loss as conditional mutual information (I(A:B)−I(A:B′)=I(A:E∣B′)≥0)
Words: 47
Data processing for quantum mutual information OurBigBook.com
For a channel on B, use a Stinespring dilation B→B′E. Isometry invariance gives I(A:B)=I(A:B′E), so the loss on discarding E is I(A:B)−I(A:B′)=I(A:E∣B′). This quantum conditional mutual information is nonnegative by Strong subadditivity of Von Neumann entropy. The identity holds for arbitrary mixed inputs and identifies the correlations lost to the discarded environment.
