Longitudinal study

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Causal inference Statistical association Observational study
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A longitudinal study observes the same individuals repeatedly. Within-person changes distinguish temporal development from persistent differences between people, although calendar-period changes, selective dropout and measurement error still require attention. A random-intercept linear mixed model can represent persistent individual differences and correlated repeated measurements.
