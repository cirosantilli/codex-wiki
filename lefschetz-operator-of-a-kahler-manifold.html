Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold (L)

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Kähler manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 25
The Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold is exterior multiplication by its Kähler form,
Lα=ω∧α.
(30)
Its formal adjoint is denoted by Λ=L∗ and lowers the bidegree of a differential form by (1,1).
This wedge operator participates in the Kähler identities; the theorem on integral (1,1) classes is a different result.
