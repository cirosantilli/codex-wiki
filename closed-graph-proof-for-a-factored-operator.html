Closed-graph proof for a factored operator

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Graph of a linear operator Closed graph theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
If bounded S:X→Y is injective and imT⊆imS, then R=S−1T has closed graph: xn​→x and Rxn​→z imply Sz=Tx=S(Rx) and hence z=Rx.
