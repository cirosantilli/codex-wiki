Band-gap extraction from intrinsic carrier activation

 Home Physics Semiconductor Intrinsic semiconductor Intrinsic carrier concentration
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For fixed band effective masses and band gap Eg​, the intrinsic carrier concentration obeys ni​=CT3/2e−Eg​/(2kB​T). Thus
d(1/T)dln(ni​/T3/2)​=−2kB​Eg​​.
(5)
A base-ten plot against x=1000K/T instead has slope −Eg​/(2000kB​Kln10) after the prefactor correction. The intrinsic branch must be used, rather than the extrinsic plateau or dopant freeze-out branch. A changing gap or effective mass makes this an activation-energy estimate, and a small gap uncertainty can produce a much larger multiplicative uncertainty in a room-temperature minority density.
