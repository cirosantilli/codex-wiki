Affleck--Kennedy--Lieb--Tasaki state (AKLT state)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Tensor network state Matrix product state Injective matrix product state
OurBigBook.com Words: 181 Articles: 4
The Affleck--Kennedy--Lieb--Tasaki state is an injective spin-one MPS whose virtual degrees of freedom are spin one-half. Its Z2​×Z2​ symmetry acts linearly on physical spins and projectively on the virtual spins.
