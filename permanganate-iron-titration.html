Permanganate-iron titration

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Redox Redox titration
OurBigBook.com Words: 860 Articles: 1
A proposed modern teaching measurement puts a pipette-measured portion of an iron Fe2+ chemical solution in a conical flask, acidifies with excess dilute sulfuric acid, and adds standardized potassium permanganate from a clamped burette while swirling. The documented endpoint principle is that added purple permanganate is consumed until the first small excess leaves a pale purple colour; no separate colour indicator is required. Insufficient acidity can produce brown manganese dioxide, invalidating the assumed five-electron half-reaction.[ref]
Record initial and final burette readings; their difference is the delivered volume V. The following controls are proposed additions to test the inference. Run a reagent blank with the same water, sulfuric acid and endpoint criterion but no sample, obtaining Vb​. Use a separately established potassium permanganate concentration c, clear the burette tip of air, and repeat sample portions. A known added amount of substance of Fe2+ should increase consumption by one fifth of that amount. A blank estimates reagent demand and the small endpoint excess; it cannot remove an interfering sample reactant.
Assuming complete, selective reaction according to the acidic permanganate-iron balance, the corrected inference is
n(Fe2+)=5c(V−Vb​).
(28)
With c in molL−1, use V and Vb​ in litres. As an original stipulated calculation, not an experimental result, take c=0.02000molL−1, V=12.60mL and Vb​=0.10mL. Then the permanganate consumption is 0.2500mmol and the inferred Fe2+ amount is 1.250mmol. In a 25.00mL sample portion this corresponds to 0.05000molL−1.
In this example the corrected volume is 12.50mL=0.01250L. The acidic permanganate-iron balance gives zA​=1 for Fe2+ and zT​=5 for permanganate. Thus 0.2500mmol of permanganate accepts 1.250mmol of formal electrons, exactly what 1.250mmol of Fe2+ supplies. The amount of substance ratio is five to one, despite equality of the exchanged electron amounts.
The denominator is the original 25.00mL aliquot, not its final volume after adding sulfuric acid, water and titrant. Such dilution changes concentrations in the flask without changing the initial sampled amount of substance of Fe2+. Omitting the 0.10mL titration blank would instead give 1.260mmol and 0.05040molL−1, an overestimate of 0.80% relative to the corrected constructed value. The displayed digits express the stipulated calculation, rather than establish its measurement uncertainty.
The endpoint detects residual permanganate, not the iron identity directly. Other sample reactants that consume permanganate raise the apparent Fe2+ amount; prior oxidation of sample Fe2+ to Fe3+ lowers it. A recovery check probes response to the added iron but does not prove absence of every interference. The readout determines the reacting Fe2+ inventory under the stated conditions, rather than total iron in an arbitrary mixture. No measured titres are asserted here.
