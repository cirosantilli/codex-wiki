Coherence of an affine module sheaf (M coherent⟺M finitely generated)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Quasi-coherent sheaf Affine module sheaf
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
For a Noetherian ring A, a finitely generated module M has a finite presentation of a module, and kernels of maps from finite free modules to M are finitely generated. Its affine module sheaf is therefore a coherent sheaf. Conversely, if M is coherent, choose a finite principal open subset cover on which its section modules are finitely generated. Lift their finitely many generators to numerators in M. The submodule generated by these numerators becomes all of M on every chart, and hence is all of M: its quotient has zero localization at every point. This proves finite generation without assuming that global sections preserve arbitrary sheaf surjections.
