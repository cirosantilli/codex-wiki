For two circular Kepler orbits with mutual inclination Im​, independently average the stellar quadrupole disturbing function over both mean anomalies. A circular ring with unit normal k has ⟨ri​rj​⟩=(δij​−ki​kj​)/2. Contracting the two ring tensors gives ⟨cos2ψ⟩=[1+(k⋅kp​)2]/4, proving the displayed exact angular factor. For small orbital inclinations relative to a common plane, Im2​=I2+Ip2​−2IIp​cos(Ωp​−Ω)+O(I4). Thus the secular angular factor is 1/4−3(I2+Ip2​)/8+3IIp​cos(Ωp​−Ω)/4. This potential generates the slow stellar contribution to satellite nodal precession.
