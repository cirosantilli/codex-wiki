Ergodic theorem for a positive Harris recurrent Markov chain

For a positive Harris recurrent Markov chain with invariant probability π and ∫∣h∣dπ<∞, its time average converges almost surely to the invariant expectation:

This is the consistency result for Markov chain Monte Carlo averages. Independent-observation variance formulas do not follow from this theorem.
