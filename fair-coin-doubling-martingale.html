Fair-coin doubling martingale

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
For fair independent and identically distributed random variables ξj​ with the Bernoulli distribution, Mn​=2n1{ξ1​=⋯=ξn​=1}​ is a nonnegative martingale. It has almost sure convergence to zero but EMn​=1, so it lacks convergence in L1.
