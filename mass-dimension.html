Mass dimension ([X])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Power counting in quantum field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
In units with ℏ=c=1, the mass dimension [X] is the exponent with which a quantity scales under a common rescaling of energies and momenta. In d spacetime dimensions a Lagrangian density has mass dimension d.
