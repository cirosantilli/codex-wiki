Turbulent dissipation anomaly (limν→0​ϵ>0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Energy cascade Zeroth law of turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
Although the local viscous dissipation is 2νSij​Sij​, increasingly intense velocity gradients can compensate for vanishing kinematic viscosity. A Burgers vortex illustrates this mechanism: its radius shrinks while its excess integrated viscous dissipation remains finite. Such a local model does not by itself prove the zeroth law of turbulence for a whole flow.
