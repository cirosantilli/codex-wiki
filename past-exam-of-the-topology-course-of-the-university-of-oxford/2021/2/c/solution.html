Past exam of the topology course of the University of Oxford / 2021 / 2 / c / Solution

 ... Year 2 of the mathematics course of the University of Oxford Topology course of the University of Oxford Past exam of the topology course of the University of Oxford 2021 2 c
OurBigBook.com Words: 117
Write Z=∐n∈N​Xn​, where each Xn​ is a labelled copy of the topological space X, with its topological disjoint union topology. Define the bijection
F:Z⟶N×X,F(x in Xn​)=(n,x).​
(20)
For a subset U⊆N×X, let Un​={x∈X:(n,x)∈U}. By definition of the topological disjoint union, F−1(U) is open in Z exactly when each Un​ is open in X.
The same condition characterizes open sets in the product topology on N×X. If U is open, the map x↦(n,x) is continuous, so each Un​ is open. Conversely, if each Un​ is open, then
U=⋃n∈N​({n}×Un​)
(21)
is open, since the discrete topology makes every {n} open in N. Hence F and its inverse function are continuous, proving the required homeomorphism. This also covers the case X=∅.
