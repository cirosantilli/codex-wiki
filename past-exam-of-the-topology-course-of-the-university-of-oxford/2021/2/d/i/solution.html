OurBigBook.com Words: 161
Use positive indices n≥1 and write p=(0,−1)∈S1. Set
A=N×{p},f(n,(u,v))=(nu​,nv+1​).​
(22)
For fixed n, this function is a homeomorphism from the unit circle to Cn​: it scales its radius to 1/n and translates its centre to (0,1/n). Thus it takes values in the Hawaiian earring X and is surjective onto X. It is continuous on each open slice {n}×S1; since these slices form an open cover of N×S1, f is continuous on the whole product topology.
Distinct circles Cn​,Cm​ meet only at o=(0,0). To see this directly, their equations are
x2+y2=n2y​,x2+y2=m2y​.
(23)
For m=n, subtraction gives y=0, then x=0. Each slice contains exactly one preimage of o, namely (n,p). Therefore the only nonsingleton fibre of f is A.
Let q:N×S1→(N×S1)/A be the quotient map collapsing A. The universal property of the quotient topology supplies a unique continuous map fˉ​ with f=fˉ​∘q. The fibre calculation proves injectivity, and surjectivity follows from that of f. Hence fˉ​ is the required continuous bijection.
