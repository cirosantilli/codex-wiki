The sphere S2 is path-connected. For u,v∈S2 which are not an antipodal pair, a continuous path is
γ(t)=∥(1−t)u+tv∥(1−t)u+tv​,0≤t≤1.
(18)
The denominator never vanishes: a zero would make u and v antipodal. If v=−u, choose a unit vector w perpendicular to u and concatenate the paths from u to w and from w to v of the same form. Thus S2 is connected. The quotient map qS​ is continuous and surjective, so the continuous image of a connected space theorem proves that RP2 is connected. In fact composing the paths above with qS​ also proves path connectedness.
