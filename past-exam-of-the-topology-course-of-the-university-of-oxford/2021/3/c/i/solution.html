OurBigBook.com Words: 63
A complete pairing of the six polygon edges leaves three edges and one face as CW cells, together with the vertex equivalence classes. If V denotes their number, then V≥1 and the polygonal-schema Euler count gives
χ=V−3+1=V−2≥−1.​
(37)
A closed orientable surface of genus two has Euler characteristic 2−2⋅2=−2. Since the Euler characteristic is a topological invariant, no complete pairing of a hexagon can give that surface. Equivalently, the polygonal-schema Euler count bounds the genus of an orientable result by 2g≤3, so g≤1.
