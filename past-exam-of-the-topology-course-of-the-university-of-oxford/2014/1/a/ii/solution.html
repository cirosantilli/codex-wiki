Suppose f is continuous and x∈A. Every open neighbourhood V of f(x) has an open preimage containing x, which meets A. Hence V meets f(A), proving f(x)∈f(A)​.
Conversely, suppose the indicated inclusion holds for every subset. To prove continuity, take any closed set C⊆Y and put A=f−1(C). Since f(A)⊆C and C is closed,
f(A)⊆f(A)​⊆C.
(2)
Thus A⊆f−1(C)=A, so A is closed. Every closed set has closed preimage, which is the closed-set characterization of continuity.
