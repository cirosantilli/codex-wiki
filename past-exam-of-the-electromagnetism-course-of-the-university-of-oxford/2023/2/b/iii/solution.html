The transmitted electromagnetic energy per unit time is the circuit's terminal power. For VI>0, the Poynting flux points in the direction of the inner electric current. The electromagnetic energy travels through the insulating gap and reaches a load at the end of the coaxial cable. The radii and material constants redistribute the electromagnetic energy density and the local Poynting vector, while their integrated Poynting flux depends only on the terminal electric potential difference and electric current.
