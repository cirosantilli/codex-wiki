The existence assertion is false under the hypotheses printed in the source. No measurability of θ is specified. Even requiring θ to be a Borel measurable function does not suffice: a coefficient can be positive at every point while approaching zero too rapidly near the starting point. This is the obstruction described by pointwise positivity does not ensure weak existence.

For completeness, the intended construction works if θ is a Borel measurable function and bounded away from zero on every compact set. In particular, it works for continuous strictly positive θ. Let B be standard d-dimensional Brownian motion, and define a clock and its inverse by
Au​=∫0u​2θ(Br​)dr​,τt​=inf{u≥0:Au​>t},Xt​=Bτt​​.

The Brownian path on each finite interval has compact range, so the added hypothesis makes Au​ finite. The clock is continuous and strictly increasing, and Au​≥u/(2Θ), so Au​→∞. Its inverse is continuous and satisfies τt​≤2Θt. Each τt​ is a stopping time for the Brownian filtration.
