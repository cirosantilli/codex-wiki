Phenylalanine (Phe, F)

 Home Biology Protein Protein structure Protein primary structure Amino acid composition
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
Phenylalanine is an amino acid, represented by Phe or F for an incorporated amino acid residue. It was identified at the other insulin N-terminus in Sanger's insulin sequencing experiment. See IUPAC Blue Book, P-103.1: amino-acid names and symbols.
