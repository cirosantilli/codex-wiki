Trivial representation in a tensor square (dim(V⊗V)G=dimHomG​(V∗,V))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation Tensor product of group representations
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For a finite-dimensional irreducible complex representation V, invariant tensors in V⊗V correspond to equivariant maps V∗→V. Schur's lemma makes their dimension one if V is self-dual and zero otherwise. In particular a real-character irreducible representation has precisely one trivial copy in its tensor square. For arbitrary complex irreducibles, the unconditional statement is that V⊗V∗ contains the trivial representation once.
