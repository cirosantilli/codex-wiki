Deviance information criterion (DIC=D+pD​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Model selection
OurBigBook.com Words: 188 Articles: 2
For Bayesian deviance D(θ)=−2logL(θ) under a common likelihood convention, define D=E[D(θ)∣y] and pD​=D−D(E[θ∣y]). Then DIC=D+pD​ trades average fit against effective complexity. Lower values suggest better penalized predictive fit in comparable models; differences are not Bayes factors or model posterior probabilities. The choice of stochastic parameters and latent-variable representation matters, especially in a hierarchical Bayesian model.
