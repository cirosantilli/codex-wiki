Single-channel current-voltage relation (i=g(V−Erev​))

 Home Biology Neuroscience Patch clamp Single-channel recording
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
For an ideal open ion channel with constant electrical conductance g, membrane potential V and reversal potential Erev​, define outward electric current as positive:
i=g(V−Erev​).
(72)
This is an Ohm's law model of the open state, not a universal channel law or a rule for opening probability. In the synthetic figure, g=10pS and Erev​=0mV. Because 1pS⋅1mV=10−3pA, the open-state electric currents at −80 and −40mV are −0.8 and −0.4pA. Zero electric current at the reversal potential does not imply a closed ion channel. Real ion channels can have nonlinear responses, so the straight line is an explicit idealization.
