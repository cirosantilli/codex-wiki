Adjoint Chevalley group of type A1 (A1​(K)≅SL2​(K)/{aI:a2=1})

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Linear algebraic group Adjoint Chevalley group
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
On the sl2 Lie algebra with basis e=E12​,h=E11​−E22​,f=E21​, the integral adjoint root polynomials are
x+​(t)ex−​(s)f​=e,=f,​x+​(t)hx−​(s)h​=h−2te,=h+2sf,​x+​(t)fx−​(s)e​=f+th−t2e,=e−sh−s2f.​
(1)
These are conjugation by the upper and lower elementary matrices of the special linear group. Those matrices generate SL2​(K), and a matrix commuting with both E12​ and E21​ is scalar. Thus the kernel is precisely {aI:a2=1} over every field, even in characteristic two, and the image is the displayed quotient.
