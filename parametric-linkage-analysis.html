Parametric linkage analysis

 Home Biology Genetics Genetic linkage
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
Parametric linkage analysis forms a pedigree likelihood from a specified disease model, including penetrance, allele frequencies and recessive inheritance or another transmission model. It estimates or tests the recombination fraction between a disease locus and a genetic marker.
