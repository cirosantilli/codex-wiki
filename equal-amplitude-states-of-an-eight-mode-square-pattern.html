Equal-amplitude states of an eight-mode square pattern

For the four wavevectors (2,1),(2,−1),(1,2),(1,−2) and their negatives, use invariant phases χ1​=2θ1​−2θ2​−θ3​+θ4​, χ2​=θ1​+θ2​−2θ3​−2θ4​. Add to the cubic normal form of a dynamical system the two quintic terms eA2​A32​A42​ and fA1​A22​A3​A4​ in the first equation, together with their square-symmetry partners; e,f are real. For equal positive magnitudes, steadiness forces sinχ1​=sinχ2​=0 unless e=f=0. Equality of radial equations further gives (e−f)(cosχ1​−cosχ2​)=0. Consequently the generic phase types are (0,0) and (π,π). When e=f=0, the mixed type (0,π), equivalent by square symmetry to (π,0), also exists. When both quintic coefficients vanish, every phase pair is allowed. Amplitude existence must still be checked from the remaining scalar equation.
