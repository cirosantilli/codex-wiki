Simple closed curve (γ:S1↪X)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Path-connected space Continuous path
OurBigBook.com Words: 93 Articles: 1
A topological embedding γ:S1→X of a circle into a topological space. On an oriented surface, an essential such curve is noncontractible; on a punctured surface, nonperipheral means that it does not surround only one puncture.
