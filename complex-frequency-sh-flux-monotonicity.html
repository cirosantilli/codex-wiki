For positive real mass density and shear modulus, real k and Imω>0, the displayed derivative of Sz​=−21​Re(V∗T) follows from the two first-order shear equations. Every nontrivial solution has strictly decreasing Sz​. Since Sz​=21​ReZ∣V∣2, positive-real impedance at a deeper point remains positive at all shallower points; negative-real impedance at a shallower point remains negative deeper down. A zero field is the necessary exception to strictness. At complex frequency this is a spectral flux diagnostic, reducing to a physical cycle average on the real axis.
