Hadamard-phase-Hadamard qubit rotation (HPα​H=eiα/2e−iασx​/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Hadamard transform Hadamard gate
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For Pα​=diag(1,eiα), conjugation by the Hadamard gate turns a relative quantum phase into an x-axis Bloch vector rotation. In the convention ρ=(I+P⋅σ)/2, the map is (Px​,Py​,Pz​)↦(Px​,Py​cosα−Pz​sinα,Py​sinα+Pz​cosα). An x-polarized input is unchanged, whereas a z-polarized input gives a sinusoidal population difference. A maximally mixed input remains maximally mixed under every unitary time evolution.
