Spreading coefficient (S=γSV​−γSL​−γLV​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Surface tension Wetting
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
The excess interfacial free energy released per newly wetted area when a liquid film replaces a solid-vapour interface is the displayed coefficient. Positive S favors macroscopic spreading in the ideal equilibrium surface-energy model, whereas negative S permits a finite contact angle obeying Young's equation. Surface preparation, chemical composition and adsorption can change all three interfacial energies.
