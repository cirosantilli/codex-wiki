Grand canonical ensemble

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 304 Articles: 7
At fixed temperature, volume, and chemical potential, the grand canonical ensemble permits both energy and particle number to fluctuate and weights a state by e−β(E−μN).
