Generalized estimating equation (∑i​DiT​Vi−1​(Yi​−mi​)=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Estimating equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 293 Articles: 4
For independent clusters with mean vector mi​(θ) and positive definite working covariance matrix Vi​, a generalized estimating equation solves
∑i​DiT​Vi−1​(Yi​−mi​)=0,Di​=∂mi​/∂θT.
(49)
Correct mean specification and regularity can give a consistent estimator of identifiable mean coefficients despite a misspecified working covariance. A sandwich covariance matrix accounts for actual cluster variation. Mean equations alone cannot identify two parameters that always enter through the same combination.
