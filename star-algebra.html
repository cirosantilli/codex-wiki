Star-algebra ((ab)∗=b∗a∗,(a∗)∗=a)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 107 Articles: 2
A complex associative algebra with a conjugate-linear involution satisfying the displayed product-reversal identities. An operator algebra closed under the Hilbert-space adjoint is a star-algebra. A C-star algebra additionally has a complete norm with ∥a∗a∥=∥a∥2.
