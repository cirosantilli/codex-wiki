The normalizer of a Lie subalgebra and the centralizer of a Lie subalgebra are
Ng​(a)={x:[x,a]⊆a},Cg​(a)={x:[x,a]=0}.
(33)
The second is always contained in the first. Conversely, let x∈Ng​(a) and a∈a. Then [a,x]∈a, and, because a is an abelian Lie algebra,
(ada)2x=[a,[a,x]]=0.
(34)
By the definition of a toral subalgebra, ada is diagonalizable. A diagonalizable linear map T satisfies kerT2=kerT: in an eigenbasis, both kernels consist of exactly the eigenvectors whose eigenvalue is zero. Thus [a,x]=0. This holds for every a∈a, so x∈Cg​(a) and
Ng​(a)=Cg​(a).​
(35)
This is the normalizer-centralizer equality for a toral abelian subalgebra; it does not require g to be a semisimple Lie algebra.
