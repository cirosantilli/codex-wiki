Organelle

 Home Biology Biological cell
OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 52
An organelle is a specialized subcellular structure. Usage varies over whether non-membrane-bounded structures such as ribosomes count as organelles, so explicitly say membrane-bounded organelles when comparing eukaryotic compartmentalization with prokaryotic organization. Mitochondria and chloroplasts are membrane-bounded examples. See OpenStax Microbiology, 3.4: cellular compartments.
