Razor-thin disk approximation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Thin disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 350 Articles: 6
The razor-thin disk approximation replaces a disk's vertical density profile by ρ(x,y,z)=Σ(x,y)δ(z). It is useful for horizontal dynamics on scales much longer than the disk thickness.
