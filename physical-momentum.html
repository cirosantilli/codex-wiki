Physical momentum (p⊥μ​=(δμν​+uμuν​)pν)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Four-momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
An observer with future-directed unit velocity uμ measures particle energy E=−uμ​pμ and spatial physical momentum given by the orthogonal projection of its four-momentum in the displayed formula. With signature (−+++) and pμ​pμ=−m2, its squared magnitude is p⊥μ​p⊥μ​=E2−m2. Components in the observer's orthonormal spatial frame agree with γmv. They differ in general from coordinate canonical momentum components. For a comoving observer in flat FRW, a conserved conventional spatial comoving covector has locally measured magnitude divided by the scale factor.
