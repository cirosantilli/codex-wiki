Stellar radius from transit duration and a linear mass-radius relation (R⋆​/R⊙​=[A⊙​/(a/R⋆​)]3/2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 32
Assume Kepler's third law, a negligible planet mass, and M⋆​/M⊙​=R⋆​/R⊙​. Define A⊙​=[(GM)⊙​P2/(4π2)]1/3/R⊙​. The total duration of a circular planetary transit determines a/R⋆​, giving the displayed stellar radius. Unknown orbital eccentricity, transit dilution or a different stellar mass-radius calibration introduces additional uncertainty.
