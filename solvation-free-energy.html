Solvation free energy (ΔGsolv∘​)

 ... Chemistry Chemical bond Ionic bonding Physical properties of salts Solubility Solvation
OurBigBook.com Words: 224 Articles: 1
For transfer of the same species X from a gaseous reference state into a solvent S, define the molar solvation free energy by
X(g)⟶X(S),ΔGsolv∘​(S)=μX∘​(S)−μX∘​(g).
(58)
Here μ∘ is a standard chemical potential. Negative solvation free energy means that transfer into the stated solvent lowers the Gibbs free energy relative to the stated gaseous reference. Include solute-solvent interactions, making room in the solvent, and its reorganization; counting only a favorable pair interaction omits part of the transfer.[ref]
State the temperature, gaseous reference pressure, dissolved reference concentration or other standard state convention, and species identity. Reference-state changes alter the quoted solvation free energy. For example, with an ideal dilute thermodynamic activity a=c/c∘, replacing c∘ by αc∘ while preserving the physical chemical potential requires μ∘ to increase by RTlnα. Thus the corresponding solvation free energy increases by RTlnα if the gaseous reference stays fixed. This follows directly from μ=μ∘+RTlna, with R the molar gas constant.
