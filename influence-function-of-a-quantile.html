Influence function of a quantile (IF(z;qp​,F))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Robust statistics Influence function
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
Let q be the unique pth quantile, with 0<p<1, and suppose the density f is continuous and positive at q. Define quantiles of contaminated distributions using the generalized inverse. For z=q, differentiating (1−ε)F(qε​)+ε1z≤qε​​=p gives
IF(z;qp​,F)=f(q)p−1z≤q​​.
(9)
At z=q, the generalized-inverse quantile stays exactly q under this contamination, so its derivative is zero. The step formula thus holds F-almost everywhere and determines the asymptotic variance p(1−p)/(nf(q)2). Its gross-error sensitivity is max(p,1−p)/f(q), but its local-shift sensitivity is infinite because of the jump.
