Big omega notation (f=Ω(g))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Big O notation
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For real-valued functions on the natural numbers, f(n)=Ω(g(n)) means that constants c>0 and N satisfy ∣f(n)∣≥c∣g(n)∣ for every n≥N. This is an eventual lower bound on magnitude. In algorithm analysis the functions are usually nonnegative, so the absolute values can be omitted. Together with the upper bound of big O notation, it gives big theta notation. This is the algorithmic lower-bound convention for Ω, rather than a convention expressing only a positive limit superior along a subsequence.
