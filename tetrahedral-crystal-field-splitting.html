Tetrahedral crystal field splitting (Δt​)

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry Ligand field Crystal field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
In the ideal tetrahedral point-charge model, the five metal d orbitals split into a lower twofold e set and an upper threefold t2​ set. Relative to their weighted barycentre their energies are −3Δt​/5 and +2Δt​/5, where Δt​>0 is the separation. For occupancies ene​t2nt​​, the crystal field stabilization energy is (−0.6ne​+0.4nt​)Δt​. Under weak-field high-spin filling it is −0.6Δt​, −1.2Δt​, −0.8Δt​ and zero for d6,d7,d8,d10 respectively. Shorter metal–ligand distances generally increase the splitting, so orbital filling can superimpose nonmonotonic bond-length changes on an ionic-size trend. The model alone does not determine an absolute bond length.
