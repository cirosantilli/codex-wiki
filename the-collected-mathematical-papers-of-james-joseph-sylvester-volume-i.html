Collected papers by James Joseph Sylvester, edited by Henry Frederick Baker, credited as H. F. Baker in the prefatory note dated April 1904. Cambridge University Press published Volume I in 1904. The collection date is distinct from the original dates of its individual papers. Internet Archive hosts the inspected digitized volume and OCR text.
