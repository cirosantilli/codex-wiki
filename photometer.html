Photometer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Photometry (optics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 490 Articles: 4
A photometer measures a visually weighted radiation quantity. A filtered photodiode approximates the photopic luminous efficiency function with its spectral responsivity; calibration establishes how its electric current maps to illuminance or collected luminous flux. NIST's detector-based realization traces the spectral responsivity to a cryogenic radiometer and the aperture area to length measurements. [ref]
