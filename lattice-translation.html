Lattice translation (t=n1​a1​+n2​a2​+n3​a3​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering Scattering amplitude Crystal scattering Bravais lattice
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A lattice translation moves every lattice point of a Bravais lattice onto another lattice point. Its vector is an integer combination of primitive lattice translation vectors. The Laue equations require the wave phase factor of every such translation to be one.
