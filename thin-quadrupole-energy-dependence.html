Thin-quadrupole energy dependence (f(E)=f0​[1+δ/β02​]+O(δ2))

 Home Physics Experimental apparatus Particle accelerator Beam optics Quadrupole magnet
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
At fixed gradient and magnet length, the signed thin-lens focal length is proportional to particle momentum. For total energy error δ=(E−E0​)/E0​, differentiating p2c2=E2−m2c4 gives dlnp/dlnE=1/β2. The ultrarelativistic result is f=f0​(1+δ) to first order. If energy means kinetic energy instead, the coefficient is (K+mc2)/(K+2mc2).
