Homotopy lifting property

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Serre spectral sequence Serre fibration
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A map p:E→B has the homotopy lifting property with respect to X when every homotopy X×I→B whose initial map lifts to E has a lift extending that initial lift.
