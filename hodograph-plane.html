Hodograph plane (q=u−iv)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Potential flow Complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow Complex velocity
OurBigBook.com Words: 351 Articles: 4
The hodograph plane records velocity rather than position. For a two-dimensional potential flow, the complex coordinate q=u−iv is the complex velocity. A straight impermeable wall fixes the velocity direction and hence maps to a radial line in this plane. A constant-speed free streamline maps to a circle centered at zero. Thus a free-boundary problem in physical space can become a fixed geometric domain in velocity space. Recovering the physical map then uses dz/dw=1/q(w), wherever q is nonzero.
