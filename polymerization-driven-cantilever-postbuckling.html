Polymerization-driven cantilever postbuckling (F≃π2B[1−3(ℓ−d)/(2d)]/(4d2))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biopolymer mechanics Euler buckling of an elastic filament
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
A clamped filament touching a laterally sliding, moment-free wall has fixed projected gap d. For an inextensible small-amplitude elastica, parameterize its tip angle by k=sin(θtip​/2). Then ℓ=B/F​K(k) and d=B/F​[2E(k)−K(k)], where K,E are complete elliptic integrals. Their small-k expansions give the displayed decrease of wall force with excess contour length. It concerns the weakly buckled branch, before overhang or additional wall contact changes the boundary conditions.
