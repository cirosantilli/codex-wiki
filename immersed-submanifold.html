Immersed submanifold

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Immersion
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 2
An immersed submanifold of M is a manifold N with an injective immersion ι:N→M; when N is treated as a subset, its manifold topology can be finer than its subspace topology.
