Barkhausen effect

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Bound charge and bound current Magnetization Ferromagnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 3
The Barkhausen effect consists of abrupt magnetization changes during a smooth applied magnetic field sweep. Its inductive signal is Barkhausen noise, often arising from collective magnetic domain wall motion.[ref]
The distinction is between a smooth drive and an intermittent material response. A smooth macroscopic hysteresis curve can hide smaller jumps. By Faraday's law of induction, a pickup coil responds to the rate of change of linked magnetic flux, so a rapid jump can be conspicuous even when the total change is small. A pulse is not one chemical atom flipping: neither its height nor the number of audible clicks counts individual electron spins. Qualitative crackling alone does not establish quantum spin; collective switching also admits a classical model with trapped interfaces. Compare the distinct mechanical readout of angular momentum transfer in the Einstein-de Haas effect.
