Slater's rules

 Home Chemistry Quantum chemistry Electron configuration Effective nuclear charge Shielding constant
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
An empirical orbital-screening prescription. Other electrons in an ns,np group count 0.35 each; electrons in shell n−1 count 0.85, deeper electrons 1.00, and outer electrons zero. For 1s, the partner contributes 0.30. d and f groups use separate rules.[ref]
