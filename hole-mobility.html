Hole mobility (vh​=μh​E)

 ... Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Current density Charge carrier Charge carrier mobility
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The positive hole mobility is the magnitude of an electron hole's weak-field drift velocity per unit electric field. In an isotropic relaxation-time model, μh​=eτh​/mh∗​, and its contribution to electrical conductivity is epμh​. The sign is opposite to an electron's drift because the electron hole has positive charge.
