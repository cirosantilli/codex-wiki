Diffeomorphism invariance of general relativity

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
Transforming a metric tensor and all matter fields together by the pullback of a diffeomorphism preserves the Einstein field equations and covariant matter equations. Passive coordinate changes only change the description of the fields; active pullbacks produce another representative of the geometry. The treatment of transformations acting on a boundary depends on the boundary conditions and possible charges. A diffeomorphism is not necessarily an isometry of a fixed metric.
