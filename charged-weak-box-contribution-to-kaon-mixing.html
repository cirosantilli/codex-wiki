Charged weak box contribution to kaon mixing (∑i,j​λi​λj​F(mi​,mj​))

 ... Physics Subatomic particle Hadron Meson Kaon Neutral kaon mixing
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 1
A Standard Model charged-current box diagram converts dsˉ into sdˉ. Internal quarks run over u,c,t, and the flavor sum contains λi​λj​ with λi​=Vis∗​Vid​. The loop function depends on internal masses; CKM matrix unitarity gives ∑i​λi​=0, producing the GIM mechanism cancellation of flavor-independent terms. The amplitude is of order g4 and second order in the weak interaction.
