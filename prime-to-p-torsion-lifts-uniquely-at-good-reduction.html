Prime-to-p torsion lifts uniquely at good reduction (E(K)[q∞]≅E(k)[q∞],q=p)

OurBigBook.com Words: 90
For good reduction of an elliptic curve over a complete local field of residue characteristic p, smoothness and the Hensel lemma make reduction surjective. Its kernel is a formal group on the maximal ideal, where multiplication by qm, q=p, is bijective by prime-to-residue-characteristic multiplication on a formal group. Thus there is no nonzero q-power torsion in the kernel. Given a lift P of a reduced point killed by qm, choose the unique kernel point R with qmR=qmP. Then P−R is a torsion lift. This proves both surjectivity and uniqueness of torsion lifting. For finite residue field these groups are finite.
