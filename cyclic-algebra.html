Cyclic algebra ((E/K,σ,a))

 ... Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Central simple algebra Brauer group Crossed-product algebra of a Galois extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 174 Articles: 2
For a cyclic extension E/K of degree m with generator σ and a∈K×, the cyclic algebra is generated by E and u with ub=σ(b)u, um=a. It is a central simple algebra of degree m. Its Brauer class can also be written (χ,a), where χ(σ)=1/m.
