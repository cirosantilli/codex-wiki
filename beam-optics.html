Beam optics

 Home Physics Experimental apparatus Particle accelerator
OurBigBook.com Words: 860 Articles: 22
The transverse transport of a charged-particle beam relative to a reference trajectory. Linear focusing gives betatron oscillations, described by Hill's equation and Courant-Snyder parameters; the beam envelope depends on its geometric emittance.
