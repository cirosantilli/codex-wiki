Longest increasing subsequence (L(x))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Increasing subsequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
A longest increasing subsequence is an increasing subsequence of maximum length. Changing or deleting one term changes its length by at most one.
