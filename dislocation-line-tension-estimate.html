Dislocation line-tension estimate (Tℓ​∼Gb2)

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Crystal structure Crystallographic defect Dislocation
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A curved dislocation can be approximated as a line with tension Tℓ​ of order Gb2. The coefficient depends on elastic constants, character and logarithmic length scales. The simple estimate Tℓ​≃Gb2/2 gives a pinning bypass stress Gb/ℓ for a semicircular segment of free span ℓ.
