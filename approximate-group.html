Approximate group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics
OurBigBook.com Words: 382 Articles: 8
A K-approximate group is a finite symmetric subset of a group A for which some set X with ∣X∣≤K satisfies A2⊆XA. In additive notation the covering condition is A+A⊆X+A.
