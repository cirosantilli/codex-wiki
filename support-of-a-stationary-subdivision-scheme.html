Support of a stationary subdivision scheme ([jmin​/(a−1),jmax​/(a−1)])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Computer aided geometric design Subdivision curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
Under Piℓ+1​=∑j​Mi−aj​Pjℓ​, the descendants of control index zero lie at aℓ−1j1​+⋯+jℓ​, where each jk​ is in the mask support. Dividing by the refined parameter scale aℓ gives a limiting influence enclosure with the displayed endpoints. For a mask supported on [−w,w] its width is 2w/(a−1) when the endpoints are active and a nonzero compactly supported basic limit exists. To prove exactness, the largest active mask term is the only surviving term near the right endpoint of the refinement equation, forcing β=(β+jmax​)/a; the analogous left-end argument completes the result. Mask padding should be removed, and a nonconvergent rule has no limit support.
