Primary quality

 Home Philosophy Philosophy of science Primary-secondary quality distinction
OurBigBook.com Words: 82 Articles: 1
In John Locke's account, a quality such as extension, solidity, shape, number or motion, attributed to bodies independently of whether they are perceived. This historical list should not be treated as an unrevisable inventory of fundamental physical properties.
