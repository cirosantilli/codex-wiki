Firehose instability (p∥​−p⊥​>B2/(4π))

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure Pressure tensor Pressure anisotropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
Parallel particle pressure can overcome magnetic tension. In the long-wavelength gyrotropic plasma limit, the Alfvén wave has ω2=k∥2​[B2/(4π)+p⊥​−p∥​]/ρ, so the condition in the title makes it unstable. The detailed kinetic threshold at shorter scales requires additional physics.
