S3-invariant Yukawa matrix for a doublet and a sign singlet (λ=diag(a,a,b))

OurBigBook.com Words: 95
Let the left and right fermions transform in D⊕ε, where D is the two-dimensional irreducible representation of the symmetric group S3​ and ε is the sign representation. Let the scalar field be a trivial representation. In matching orthonormal bases, invariance of ψˉ​L​λψR​ says that λ commutes with D⊕ε. The Schur lemma forces each diagonal irreducible block to be scalar and both mixed blocks to vanish, giving the displayed form. Thus after a vacuum expectation value h, the physical fermion mass matrix has singular values ∣ha∣,∣ha∣,∣hb∣: unbroken S3​ enforces a degenerate doublet. In a common nonorthonormal basis with invariant Gram matrix K, the doublet coefficient is aK; canonical normalization recovers aI2​.
