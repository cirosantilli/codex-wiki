Antiproton (pˉ​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Mode expansion of a Dirac field Antiparticle
OurBigBook.com Words: 792 Articles: 3
The antiproton is the antiparticle of the proton: it has the same rest mass and spin 1/2, with opposite electric charge and baryon number. A negative electric charge alone cannot identify an antiproton, because a negative pion also bends in that direction in a magnetic field. Combining magnetic rigidity with velocity determines a rest mass; agreement with the proton rest mass then makes the identification much more discriminating.
The mode expansion of a Dirac field explains how independent particle and antiparticle excitations enter a relativistic field description. It supplies a theoretical language for the antiproton, while the Chamberlain-Segrè antiproton identification supplies an experimental test. The proton is a composite baryon, so a free Dirac field is not a complete account of its internal dynamics or of antiproton production in a target.
