Circular topographic anticyclone (q=−fϵ1r<a​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Potential vorticity Shallow-water potential vorticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 161 Articles: 1
For an upstream irrotational current on an f-plane in a rigid layer of height h, a circular region of height h(1−ϵ) has the displayed relative vorticity if its fluid retains upstream potential vorticity f/h. To leading order in the height contrast, take u=−Ψy​, v=Ψx​ and Ψ=−Uy+ψ. Solving the Poisson equation for ψ gives ψ=−Cr2/2 inside and ψ=Ca2[log(a/r)−1/2] outside, where C=fϵ/2. The disturbance is clockwise for positive fϵ. Variable-depth transport and the transition across the rim introduce higher-order corrections to this leading columnar inversion.
