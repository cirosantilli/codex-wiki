Degree of an isogeny from its x-coordinate map (degϕ=max(degA,degB))

OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For an isogeny of elliptic curves ϕ:E1​→E2​, write x2​∘ϕ=A(x1​)/B(x1​) with coprime polynomials A,B. Each xi​:Ei​→P1 is a two-to-one finite morphism, and function field degrees multiply in a tower. Consequently 2degϕ=2max(degA,degB). This computes the total degree, including an inseparable factor. The zero homomorphism is assigned degree zero separately.
