Reduced norm (NrdA/K​:A→K)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Central simple algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
The reduced norm of an element of a central simple algebra of degree d is its determinant after any splitting isomorphism to Md​. Inner automorphisms of a matrix algebra preserve that determinant, while Galois invariance descends its polynomial coefficients to the center. It is a multiplicative homogeneous degree-d polynomial, vanishing exactly on noninvertible elements. On a central division algebra it is anisotropic.
