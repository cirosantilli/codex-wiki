Orthonormal translates and Fourier periodization (∑k​∣ϕ​(t+2πk)∣2=1)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Wavelet Multiresolution analysis Scaling function
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
With ϕ​(ξ)=∫ϕ(x)e−ixξdx in the Plancherel theorem sense, the integer translates of ϕ∈L2(R) are orthonormal exactly when
∑k∈Z​∣ϕ​(t+2πk)∣2=1almost everywhere.
(8)
The Fourier coefficients of the periodized energy are the translate inner products. Uniqueness of Fourier coefficients in L1 therefore proves both directions. No absolute integrability assumption on ϕ is required.
