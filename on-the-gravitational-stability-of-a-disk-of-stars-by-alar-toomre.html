On the Gravitational Stability of a Disk of Stars by Alar Toomre (1964, The Astrophysical Journal 139, 1217–1238)

 Home Physics Physics bibliography
OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by Alar Toomre Words: 42
The primary calculation treats a collisionless stellar disk and obtains the critical radial velocity dispersion 3.36GΣ/κ. A fluid disk has a related but numerically different criterion involving πGΣ/κ. The distinction matters when a fluid dispersion relation is used to approximate stellar random motion.
