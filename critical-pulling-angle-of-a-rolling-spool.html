Critical pulling angle of a rolling spool (cosθc​=r/R)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics Rolling without slipping
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
For a spool of outer radius R, axle radius r, mass M and axial moment of inertia I, a lower-tangent pull of tension T at upward angle θ produces horizontal acceleration a=TR(Rcosθ−r)/(I+MR2) while rolling without slipping. This follows by combining Ma=Tcosθ+f, Iα=Tr+Rf and a=−Rα, with rightward translation and counterclockwise rotation positive. The acceleration reverses at θc​; zero acceleration means equilibrium only if initially at rest. At that angle the static friction limit gives Tmax​=μMg/(cosθc​+μsinθc​).
