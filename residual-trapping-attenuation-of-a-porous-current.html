Residual-trapping attenuation of a porous current (h=h0​exp[−t/τ+ϕ(1−s)x/(uτ)])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Two-phase porous-media flow Capillary residual trapping
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 1
When a thinning porous gravity current leaves fraction s behind, receding storage changes by only ϕ(1−s)dh after accounting for immobile fluid. Thus its advective thinning equation is ϕ(1−s)ht​+uhx​=0. Advancing into previously unwetted pores still requires the full storage ϕh, so the nose moves at u/ϕ. With exponentially decreasing injection the mobile volume per unit well length is V=Qτ(e−st/τ−e−t/τ)/(1−s). It obeys V′=Qe−t/τ−sV/τ; the second term becomes immobile retained volume.
