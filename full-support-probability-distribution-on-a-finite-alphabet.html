Full-support probability distribution on a finite alphabet (supp(p)=X)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Support of a probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
A probability mass function p has full support on a specified finite alphabet if px​>0 for every symbol in that alphabet. This is relative to the chosen alphabet: deleting zero-probability symbols produces a smaller alphabet on which the same law has full support. In a weighted prefix code problem, full support makes every shortening of a retained codeword strictly reduce expected codeword length. Without it, a minimum-cost code tree may contain redundant zero-weight branches.
