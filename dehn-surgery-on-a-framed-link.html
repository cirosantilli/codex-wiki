Dehn surgery on a framed link

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Link Framed link
OurBigBook.com Words: 411 Articles: 10
Integral Dehn surgery removes a tubular neighborhood of every component and reglues a solid torus so that its meridian follows the slope specified by the framing.
The framing specifies the integer slope in this special case of Dehn surgery.
