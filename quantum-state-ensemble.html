Quantum state ensemble ({pi​,∣ψi​⟩},ρ=∑i​pi​∣ψi​⟩⟨ψi​∣)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 241 Articles: 2
A quantum state ensemble specifies a random preparation: normalized pure states ∣ψi​⟩ are selected with probabilities pi​≥0, ∑i​pi​=1. Without an accessible preparation label, its measurement statistics are those of the density operator ρ=∑i​pi​∣ψi​⟩⟨ψi​∣. Indeed every POVM effect E has probability ∑i​pi​⟨ψi​∣E∣ψi​⟩=Tr(Eρ). Different ensembles can therefore have identical operational statistics: equal mixtures of computational-basis qubits or Hadamard-basis qubits both have density operator I/2. The ensemble decomposition contains information about the preparation, not additional information accessible from the system alone.
