Centrifugal potential

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Reference frame Rotating reference frame
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
For uniform rotation about an axis, centrifugal acceleration is the negative gradient of the potential −Ω2s2/2, where s is distance from the rotation axis.
