Differential entropy of a uniform law (he​(U(a,a+L))=lnL)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Information entropy Differential entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 109
For a continuous uniform distribution on an interval of length L>0, the probability density is 1/L. Direct integration gives
he​(X)=−∫aa+L​L1​lnL1​dx=lnL.
(19)
Thus a uniform law on an interval of length 1/2 has differential entropy −ln2, while length two gives ln2. Both laws have total probability one. The shorter interval has larger probability density, making −lnf(X) negative; this is neither a negative probability nor a negative discrete information entropy.
