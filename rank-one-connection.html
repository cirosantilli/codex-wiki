Rank-one connection (A−B=a⊗N)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Deformation (mechanics) Finite strain theory Deformation gradient
OurBigBook.com Words: 237 Articles: 2
Two constant deformation gradients are rank-one connected when their difference is an outer product a⊗N. For a nonzero jump its matrix rank is one; equality A=B is the trivial compatible case. The vector N specifies a reference interface normal, and a is the jump amplitude. The Hadamard jump condition identifies this algebraic condition with continuity of an affine deformation across a planar interface. The normal can be normalized by rescaling a without changing the product.
