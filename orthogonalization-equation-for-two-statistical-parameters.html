Write the old nuisance parameter as λ=g(ψ,η) with gη​=0. The transformed score functions are uψnew​=uψ​+gψ​uλ​ and uη​=gη​uλ​. Their expected cross product is gη​(Iψλ​+gψ​Iλλ​). Setting it to zero gives the displayed ordinary differential equation along fixed-η curves, assuming positive nuisance Fisher information. Smooth solutions with distinct initial conditions give a local interest-respecting orthogonal coordinate system.
