Neutral meson mixing

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron Meson
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 3
Transitions between a neutral meson carrying flavour and its antiparticle. Decays are described on this two-state subspace by an effective Hamiltonian operator M−iΓ/2 with Hermitian matrices M,Γ.
