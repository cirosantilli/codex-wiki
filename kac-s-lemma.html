Kac's lemma

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
For an irreducible positive recurrent Markov chain with stationary distribution π, the expected return time to state i is 1/πi​. More generally, expected occupation rewards in a return cycle equal their stationary rate times the expected cycle length.
