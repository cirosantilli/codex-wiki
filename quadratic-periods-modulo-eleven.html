Quadratic periods modulo eleven

OurBigBook.com Words: 35
If Q and N are the nonzero square and nonsquare classes modulo eleven and ω=e2πi/11, then
∑q∈Q​ωq=2−1+i11​​,∑n∈N​ωn=2−1−i11​​.
(22)
Their sum is −1, and their difference is the Quadratic Gauss sum i11​.
