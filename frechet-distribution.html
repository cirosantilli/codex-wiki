Fréchet distribution (Φα​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Extreme value theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
For α>0, the standard Fréchet distribution has distribution function Φα​(x)=e−x−α for x>0 and zero for x≤0. Its maximum domain of attraction consists of distribution functions with an infinite right endpoint of a distribution and a survival function with regular variation of index −α.
