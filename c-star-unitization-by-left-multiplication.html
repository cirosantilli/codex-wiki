C-star unitization by left multiplication (∥a+λ1∥=∥La​+λI∥)

The left multiplication representation of a C-star algebra is isometric: test La​ on a∗/∥a∥ to get ∥La​∥=∥a∥. For a nonunital algebra, its closed image plus the identity operator is the algebraic unitization. For a formal z=a+λ1 and b∈A, ∥zb∥2=∥b∗z∗zb∥≤∥b∥∥Lz∗z​b∥. Thus ∥Lz​∥2≤∥Lz∗z​∥≤∥Lz∗​∥∥Lz​∥; applying the same estimate to z∗ proves the algebra involution is isometric and the C-star identity holds. This gives a unitization without first assuming an operator representation on a Hilbert space.
