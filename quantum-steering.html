Quantum steering

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Foundations of quantum mechanics Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen paradox
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
For a shared quantum entanglement resource, one party's choice of measurement can prepare different conditional ensembles for the distant party. Discarding the outcome leaves the same distant reduced density matrix, so steering does not itself permit signalling. Demonstrating steering against a local hidden-state model is stronger than merely observing a change in a postselected description.
