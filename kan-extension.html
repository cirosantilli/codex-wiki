Kan extension (Lanf​,Ranf​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Functor
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A left Kan extension is left adjoint to precomposition, when it exists; a Right Kan extension is right adjoint. For small indexing categories and set-valued functors, both exist by comma-category colimit and limit formulas. These two adjoints give the essential geometric morphism induced by a functor between presheaf indexing categories.
