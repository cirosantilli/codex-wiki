Survival function (S(t))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 686 Articles: 14
For an event time T, the survival function is S(t)=P(T>t)=1−F(t), up to the endpoint convention used for atoms.
