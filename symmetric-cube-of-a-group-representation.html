Symmetric cube of a group representation (Sym3V)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation Tensor product of group representations
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
The symmetric cube of a group representation V is the symmetric part of V⊗3. Over the complex numbers, its character is
χSym3V​(g)=6χV​(g)3+3χV​(g)χV​(g2)+2χV​(g3)​.
(9)
The projection onto symmetric tensors averages the six permutations of the three factors. The identity permutation contributes χ(g)3, the three transpositions each contribute χ(g)χ(g2), and the two three-cycles each contribute χ(g3). Cubic polynomial invariants are fixed vectors in Sym3(V∗), whose dimension follows by averaging this character for a finite group.
