Localization precision

 ... Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Fluorescence Fluorescence microscope Single-molecule fluorescence microscopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
Uncertainty in an estimated emitter position. It can be much smaller than the width of its point spread function, but it does not by itself guarantee that densely packed emitters or an entire labelled structure are resolved.
