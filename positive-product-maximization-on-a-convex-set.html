Positive-product maximization on a convex set

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Concave function Strictly concave function Strict concavity
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
A compact convex subset of R2 meeting the positive quadrant has a unique positive point maximizing xy. Existence follows by maximizing the continuous product on the intersection with the closed nonnegative quadrant; a positive attainable value excludes its axes. Uniqueness follows from strict concavity of logx+logy on the positive quadrant. This is a useful way to optimize a product even though the product itself is not concave.
