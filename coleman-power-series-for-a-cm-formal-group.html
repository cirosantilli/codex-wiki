Coleman power series for a CM formal group (fu​(tn​)=un​)

 ... Algebraic number theory Iwasawa theory Iwasawa module p-ramified Iwasawa module Class-field unit sequence Coleman power series
OurBigBook.com Words: 300 Articles: 3
For a height-one CM elliptic curve formal group and compatible pn-division parameters tn​, an integral Coleman series interpolates a norm-compatible sequence of local units un​. Its norm relation is multiplication of its translates by the p-torsion, with coefficient Frobenius where required by the unramified base. Coates–Wiles homomorphisms and the Frobenius-corrected logarithm turn this local series into critical values and measures.
