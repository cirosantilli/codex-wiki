Homomorphism of lattices

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Intuitionistic propositional logic Lattice
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
A homomorphism between lattices preserves binary joins and meets: f(x∨y)=f(x)∨f(y) and f(x∧y)=f(x)∧f(y). It is order preserving, since x≤y implies x=x∧y and hence f(x)=f(x)∧f(y). For a map into the two-element lattice, the inverse image of zero is a lattice ideal, and meet preservation says this ideal is prime: if a meet belongs to it, at least one factor does. Conversely the indicator of the complement of a prime lattice ideal preserves both operations. Preservation of distinguished top and bottom elements is an additional requirement for homomorphisms of bounded lattices.
