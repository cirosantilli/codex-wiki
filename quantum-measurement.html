Elitzur-Vaidman bomb tester

Words: 141 Articles: 1
Measurement in quantum mechanics OurBigBook.com
A photon in a quantum superposition of a safe path and an absorbing path can sometimes certify a live absorber without being absorbed. Recombination is chosen so that one output is impossible if the absorber is absent. A detection at that output is then an unambiguous successful test. The measurement back-action of a surviving live-absorber trial changes the interference pattern.

Repeated weak-rotation bomb-test efficiency (PN​=1+qq​(1−q2N),q=1−p)

Words: 84
Elitzur-Vaidman bomb tester OurBigBook.com
For an Elitzur-Vaidman bomb tester using a rotation and its inverse, let p be the probability of entering the absorbing path and q=1−p. Conditional on a live absorber, one trial has explosion probability p, successful safe identification probability pq, and inconclusive probability q2. The inconclusive projective measurement resets the input, so summing a geometric series gives the displayed success probability after at most N trials. Its limit is q/(1+q), approaching one half as p tends to zero through positive values. Exactly zero coupling produces no information.

Measurement backaction (ρ↦Km​ρKm†​/Tr(Km​ρKm†​))

Words: 48
Measurement in quantum mechanics OurBigBook.com
A measurement in quantum mechanics changes the conditional density operator, rather than merely revealing a pre-existing classical state. For an instrument with one operator Km​ per outcome, the displayed update occurs with probability Tr(Km​ρKm†​). Forgetting the outcome gives the corresponding quantum channel. Measurement-based quantum feedback must include this disturbance and the statistical information in the record.

Perfect discrimination of pure states requires orthogonality

Words: 65
Measurement in quantum mechanics OurBigBook.com
If a positive operator-valued measure distinguishes normalized pure states ∣u⟩,∣v⟩ with certainty, the effect E for the first answer satisfies ⟨u∣E∣u⟩=1 and ⟨v∣E∣v⟩=0. Positivity of E and I−E implies E∣v⟩=0 and E∣u⟩=∣u⟩, because a positive operator with zero expectation annihilates the vector. Consequently ⟨u∣v⟩=⟨u∣E∣v⟩=0. Conversely, orthogonal states can be measured with their rank-one linear projections. A common unitary operator preserves this inner product and cannot remove the obstruction.

Quantum nondemolition measurement

Words: 268 Articles: 3
Measurement in quantum mechanics OurBigBook.com
An ideal measurement preserving the measured eigenspaces. In the projective setting, the selective instrument ρ↦Pj​ρPj​ preserves every vector within outcome j, including coherence inside a degenerate eigenspace. Repeated measurements agree in the absence of intervening dynamics changing the measured quantity. Measuring a finer observable can destroy degeneracy coherence and need not realize the same Lüders rule instrument.

Entanglement-assisted statistical singlet verification (Ω=Ps​+(I−Ps​)/3)

Words: 63
Quantum nondemolition measurement OurBigBook.com
Choose a shared random axis uniformly from x,y,z, perform its entanglement-assisted nondemolition parity measurement with one Bell pair, and accept the anticorrelated parity. The spin singlet state always passes unchanged. The averaged acceptance operator is Ω=∑j​(I−σj​⊗σj​)/6=Ps​+(I−Ps​)/3, so an orthogonal triplet state passes with probability one third. This is a statistical verifier with a nonzero acceptance gap, rather than an exact single-shot singlet-versus-triplet decision. The Bell-pair cost of exact nondemolition singlet verification concerns the stronger zero-error task.

Bell-pair cost of exact nondemolition singlet verification (R≥4)

Words: 82
Quantum nondemolition measurement OurBigBook.com
Exact singlet verification accepts the spin singlet state with certainty, rejects every orthogonal state, and preserves the singlet on acceptance. Each nonzero accepting Kraus operator is then a scalar multiple of the singlet projector, whose operator Schmidt rank is four. The local Kraus rank bound from an entangled resource excludes a single shared Bell pair, which has Schmidt rank two. Two shared Bell pairs suffice by a Bell-state nondemolition measurement followed by reporting whether its label is the singlet. This protocol may disturb triplet coherence. The rank bound concerns the resource Schmidt rank, and is not an entropy lower bound of two bits for arbitrary resource states.

Entanglement-assisted nondemolition parity measurement (Kuv​=2−1/2Pu⊕v​)

Words: 65
Quantum nondemolition measurement OurBigBook.com
Share a Bell state meter pair, apply local system-to-meter CNOT gates, and measure each meter in the computational basis. The parity of the local meter records measures system ZA​ZB​ while preserving all coherence within either parity sector. The complete tuple of local records has Kraus operator Pu⊕v​/2​. Each local record is uniform, and the nonlocal result requires local operations and classical communication to compare records. A local Hadamard gate conjugation supplies the corresponding XA​XB​ measurement.

Antidistinguishable quantum states

A finite family of quantum states is antidistinguishable if a measurement in quantum mechanics has labelled outcomes such that each outcome has zero probability for its corresponding state, while the outcomes form a complete measurement in quantum mechanics. It can exclude a state without identifying which state was prepared. The PBR exclusion measurement for zero and plus supplies a four-state projective measurement example.

Postselection

Words: 219 Articles: 3
Measurement in quantum mechanics OurBigBook.com
Postselection conditions a measurement in quantum mechanics on a specified outcome. If its branch acts by a linear operator K on a normalized input ∣b⟩, its probability is ∥K∣b⟩∥2, and its normalized output is K∣b⟩/∥K∣b⟩∥. This conditional state is defined only when the probability is nonzero. Physically unsuccessful trials are discarded; postselection is not a deterministic implementation of the nonlinear normalization map.

Aharonov-Bergmann-Lebowitz rule (pj​=∣⟨b∣Pj​∣a⟩∣2/∑k​∣⟨b∣Pk​∣a⟩∣2)

Words: 161 Articles: 2
Postselection OurBigBook.com
For an ideal projective measurement with the Lüders rule, condition on both an initial quantum state preparation and a final successful postselection. Its outcome probabilities are proportional to ∣⟨b∣Pj​∣a⟩∣2, where a is the forward-evolved initial state and b the backward-evolved final state. The normalization must be nonzero. The expression is symmetric in these two boundary states, although its derivation uses the ordinary Born rule and conditional state update.

Context dependence of pre- and post-selected measurements

Words: 99 Articles: 1
Aharonov-Bergmann-Lebowitz rule OurBigBook.com
In the ABL rule, probabilities depend on the full intermediate measurement in quantum mechanics instrument. A binary Lüders rule measurement in quantum mechanics preserves coherence within its unresolved complement, whereas a fully resolved projective measurement removes it. Summing fine-grained probabilities after measurement in quantum mechanics does not generally reproduce the coarse-grained experiment. Different postselection success rates are part of the distinction.
N-box pre- and post-selection paradox (D=N2−N+1)
Words: 56
Context dependence of pre- and post-selected measurements OurBigBook.com
For a uniform prestate in N+1 dimensions and poststate proportional to ∑i=1N​∣i⟩−(N−1)∣N+1⟩, each binary question Pi​ versus I−Pi​ has conditional probability one for i≤N. A fully resolved measurement in quantum mechanics instead assigns 1/D to each of the first N outcomes and (N−1)2/D to the last. The context dependence of pre- and post-selected measurements explains why these alternative certainties do not imply simultaneous properties.

A generalized quantum measurement is specified by operators Mi​ satisfying ∑i​Mi†​Mi​=I. On input ρ, outcome i has probability Tr(Mi†​Mi​ρ) and conditional state Mi​ρMi†​/Tr(Mi†​Mi​ρ).

Nonselective quantum measurement (ρ′=∑i​Ai​ρAi†​)

Words: 56
Generalized measurement postulate OurBigBook.com
A nonselective measurement in quantum mechanics averages over outcomes that are not read or not communicated. Its quantum channel is ρ↦∑i​Ai​ρAi†​, with ∑i​Ai†​Ai​=I. The completeness condition makes the channel trace preserving. The average equals ∑i​pi​ρi​, where ρi​ are the conditional states from a selective quantum measurement. A nonselective projective measurement is the special case in which the operators are orthogonal projectors.

Selective quantum measurement (ρi​=Ai​ρAi†​/pi​)

Words: 52
Generalized measurement postulate OurBigBook.com
A selective measurement in quantum mechanics conditions on a known outcome. For Kraus operator Ai​, the Born rule gives pi​=Tr(Ai†​Ai​ρ) and the conditional density operator is Ai​ρAi†​/pi​ when pi​>0. Conditioning changes the state and can change the conditional reduced density matrix of a correlated remote system. That conditional state is available as such only to an observer with the outcome information.

Positive operator-valued measure (POVM)

Words: 374 Articles: 6
Generalized measurement postulate OurBigBook.com
A positive operator-valued measure is a family of positive operators Ei​ satisfying ∑i​Ei​=I. It determines measurement probabilities Pr(i)=Tr(Ei​ρ); choosing measurement operators with Ei​=Mi†​Mi​ additionally determines state updates.

POVM–ensemble duality for the maximally mixed state (Ei​=dpi​ρi​)

Words: 49
Positive operator-valued measure OurBigBook.com
A POVM {Ei​} in dimension d corresponds to a quantum state ensemble with pi​=Tr(Ei​)/d and ρi​=Ei​/Tr(Ei​), omitting zero effects. Its average is the maximally mixed state I/d. Conversely any ensemble with that average defines a POVM by Ei​=dpi​ρi​. These transformations are inverse. A pure POVM corresponds exactly to a pure-state ensemble decomposition of I/d.

Pure positive operator-valued measure

Words: 39
Positive operator-valued measure OurBigBook.com
A POVM whose nonzero effects have rank one, Ei​=wi​∣ψi​⟩⟨ψi​∣, with normalized pure states and wi​>0. Completeness is ∑i​wi​∣ψi​⟩⟨ψi​∣=I. This use of pure concerns effect rank, and does not mean an extremal point in the convex set of all POVMs.

Trine qubit POVM (Ej​=32​∣ϕj​⟩⟨ϕj​∣)

Words: 85
Positive operator-valued measure OurBigBook.com
Choose three pure qubit states whose unit Bloch vectors are coplanar, sum to zero and have pairwise angle 120 degrees. The three effects Ej​=32​∣ϕj​⟩⟨ϕj​∣ sum to the identity, giving a POVM. If an input is the state orthogonal to ϕ1​ or to ϕ2​, outcome one excludes the first input and outcome two excludes the second. Each conclusive outcome has probability one half on its corresponding input; the third outcome is inconclusive with probability one half on either input. This gives unambiguous quantum state discrimination, rather than identification on every trial.

Informationally complete POVM

Words: 123 Articles: 1
Positive operator-valued measure OurBigBook.com
A POVM is informationally complete if its outcome probabilities determine every input density operator uniquely. Its effects must span the real space of Hermitian operators. For a qubit, normalization leaves at most m−1 independent probabilities from m outcomes; an arbitrary Bloch vector has three real parameters, so one fixed informationally complete measurement requires at least four outcomes. Using several differently oriented binary measurements is a different experimental setting and can also perform quantum state tomography.
Tetrahedral qubit POVM (Ei​=41​(I+ni​⋅σ))
Words: 55
Informationally complete POVM OurBigBook.com
Choose the four unit Bloch vectors at the vertices of a regular tetrahedron, so ∑i​ni​=0 and ∑i​ni​niT​=4I/3. The displayed four positive rank-one effects sum to I. On input (I+r⋅σ)/2, their probabilities are pi​=(1+r⋅ni​)/4, giving the reconstruction r=3∑i​pi​ni​. This realizes the smallest fixed qubit informationally complete POVM.

Binary rank-one qubit POVM (E1​=(I+n⋅σ)/2,E2​=(I−n⋅σ)/2)

Words: 48
Positive operator-valued measure OurBigBook.com
Two positive rank-one effects summing to I in dimension two must be complementary orthogonal projections. Write the first as α∣ψ⟩⟨ψ∣ with normalized ψ. The second has eigenvalues 1−α and 1, so its rank-one condition forces α=1. The two unit Bloch vectors are antipodal, and this POVM is a projective measurement.

Equatorial qubit measurement (∣αs​⟩=(∣0⟩+(−1)se−iα∣1⟩)/2​)

Words: 114 Articles: 1
Projective measurement OurBigBook.com
The orthonormal quantum states ∣αs​⟩=(∣0⟩+(−1)se−iα∣1⟩)/2​, s=0,1, define an equatorial projective measurement. The measured observable is cosαX−sinαY, with eigenvalue (−1)s. Thus α=0 measures X, and α=π/2 measures −Y with these outcome labels. The sign convention matters when comparing different definitions of equatorial bases.

Equatorial measurement relabeling at Clifford angles (∣vs​(η+π)⟩=∣vs⊕1​(η)⟩)

Words: 72
Equatorial qubit measurement OurBigBook.com
For ∣vs​(η)⟩=(∣0⟩+(−1)seiη∣1⟩)/2​, adding π exchanges the two outcomes of the same projective measurement. At an angle ϕ=jπ/2, the choices ϕ and −ϕ therefore specify the same unordered pair of projectors: their labels differ by jmod2. An adaptive choice between these signs can be replaced by a fixed measurement and a classical label exchange. Together with Pauli-frame propagation along a measurement wire, this permits simultaneous measurements when all adaptive sign changes occur at these Clifford gate angles.

Pinching map (P(ρ)=∑i​Pi​ρPi​)

For mutually orthogonal projectors summing to the identity, a pinching map discards coherences between their ranges while preserving each diagonal block. Rank-one dephasing (Δ(ρ)=∑i​Pi​ρPi​,rankPi​=1)
Words: 86 Articles: 2
Nonselective projective measurement OurBigBook.com
In an orthonormal basis, rank-one dephasing is the quantum channel Δ(ρ)=∑i​∣ψi​⟩⟨ψi​∣ρ∣ψi​⟩⟨ψi​∣. It removes off-diagonal entries while preserving the basis probabilities. Its output Von Neumann entropy is their Shannon entropy, and the relative-entropy identity for rank-one dephasing quantifies the entropy increase.
Relative-entropy identity for rank-one dephasing (Drel​(ρ∥Δ(ρ))=S(Δ(ρ))−S(ρ))
Words: 26
Rank-one dephasing OurBigBook.com
Because logΔ(ρ) is diagonal, Tr(ρlogΔ(ρ))=Tr(Δ(ρ)logΔ(ρ)). Hence Drel​(ρ∥Δ(ρ))=S(Δ(ρ))−S(ρ). Support inclusion under rank-one dephasing handles zero probabilities, and Klein's inequality makes the difference nonnegative. Equality holds exactly when the state was already diagonal.
Support inclusion under rank-one dephasing (suppρ⊆suppΔ(ρ))

Rank-one dephasing OurBigBook.com
If pi​=⟨ψi​∣ρ∣ψi​⟩=0, positivity gives ρ​∣ψi​⟩=0, so the corresponding basis vector is in kerρ. Therefore kerΔ(ρ)⊆kerρ, and suppρ⊆suppΔ(ρ). This makes the quantum relative entropy of ρ relative to its rank-one dephasing finite.

A polarizing beam splitter converts a photon polarization qubit to two paths. Rotating one arm's photon polarization to match the other and recombining at a balanced beam splitter gives P1​(θ)=1/2+Re(α∗βeiθ). Measurements at phases zero and π/2 recover the transverse Bloch vector components; direct counts in the two separated arms recover the population difference. All three components, rather than a fringe alone, determine an arbitrary photon polarization density matrix.

Single-qubit process tomography (Rij​=21​Tr(σi​Vσj​V†))

Words: 127
Quantum state tomography OurBigBook.com
To identify the physical channel ρ↦VρV† of a single-qubit unitary gate, prepare each of the three positive Pauli matrix eigenstates and estimate each output Pauli expectation. Their nine means are
Rij​=21​Tr(σi​Vσj​V†),i,j∈{x,y,z}.
(4)
They determine the Bloch vector rotation r↦Rr, hence the channel on all states. If two gates have the same channel, their relative unitary commutes with all three Pauli matrices: commuting with Z forces it diagonal and commuting with X forces equal diagonal entries. Thus they differ by a scalar global phase, proving uniqueness up to that phase. This procedure needs no controlled gate or eigenstate of the unknown gate. Estimating its bounded measurement means to error ϵ with fixed confidence uses order ϵ−2 repetitions. It determines the relative eigenphases but cannot determine an overall eigenvalue phase from uncontrolled gate access alone.

No information without disturbance

Words: 298 Articles: 4
Measurement in quantum mechanics OurBigBook.com
If a quantum instrument leaves every input state unchanged even after conditioning on its classical output, then its output distribution is independent of the input state. Thus an ordinary quantum process cannot learn arbitrary state information while preserving every state.

A quantum instrument associates each classical outcome with a completely positive trace-nonincreasing map; summing the outcome maps gives a quantum channel. It specifies both outcome probabilities and conditional output states.

Unitary dilation of a measurement instrument

Words: 89
Quantum instrument OurBigBook.com
For Kraus operators satisfying ∑i​Ai†​Ai​=I, the map V∣ϕ⟩=∑i​Ai​∣ϕ⟩⊗∣i⟩ is an isometry. Implement it by a unitary operator on the system and a quantum ancilla, initially in an extra unused state ∣0⟩. Projecting the output quantum ancilla onto ∣i⟩ gives joint unnormalized state Ai​ρAi†​⊗∣i⟩⟨i∣. The outcome probability is Tr(Ai​ρAi†​), and the conditional system state is Ai​ρAi†​ divided by this probability. The unused level can be merged into the first orthogonal projection without changing any outcome on the prepared state, giving a complete projective measurement with exactly the original outcome labels.

POVM does not determine the post-measurement state

Words: 61
Quantum instrument OurBigBook.com
A POVM fixes outcome probabilities, but a quantum instrument additionally fixes the conditional output state. For computational-basis effects Ey​=∣y⟩⟨y∣, the operators My​=∣y⟩⟨y∣ leave the measured eigenstate as output, whereas Ny​=∣0⟩⟨y∣ reset every outcome to ∣0⟩. Both have My†​My​=Ny†​Ny​=Ey​. Thus a specification of effects alone cannot select a unique joint state that retains the measured quantum system.

Local quantum operation

Words: 81
Quantum instrument OurBigBook.com
A local quantum operation on one tensor-factor subsystem acts as ΦA​⊗idB​, with ΦA​ completely positive. An individual outcome branch can decrease trace, while the sum over outcomes of a normalized instrument preserves trace. The unselected operation consequently leaves the remote reduced state unchanged. In a local field algebra, Kraus operators inside one region and commuting with a distant observable give an analogous no-signalling statement. Locality of the operation must be established; an arbitrary globally defined projection is not automatically a physically local measurement.
