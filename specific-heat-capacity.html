Specific heat capacity (cp​)

 ... Physics Branch of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Heat capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 54 Articles: 1
Specific heat capacity is heat capacity per unit mass. The volumetric heat capacity is ρcp​, and k/(ρcp​) is the thermal diffusivity when k is the thermal conductivity.
