Projected surface mass density (Σ(ξ)=∫ρ(ξ,l)dl)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Density
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
Projected surface mass density is mass per unit projected area, obtained by integrating mass density along the line of sight. It determines the enclosed cylindrical mass used by a thin gravitational lens equation. It need not equal a physical disk's density, and its aperture mass differs from a spherical three-dimensional enclosed mass.
