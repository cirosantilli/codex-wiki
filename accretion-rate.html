Accretion rate (M˙)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Accretion disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The accretion rate is the mass crossing an inward-oriented cylindrical surface per unit time. A common convention takes M˙>0 for inward flow, so the outward radial mass flux is −M˙.
