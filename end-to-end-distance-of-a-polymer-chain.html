End-to-end distance of a polymer chain (Rrms​=⟨∣rN​−r0​∣2⟩​)

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymer physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 2
The end-to-end vector joins the two ends of a polymer chain. In an isotropic unforced polymer solution, its vector expected value is zero; a useful measure of chain size is its root-mean-square length. For an ideal chain with N independent isotropic steps of length b, the cross terms in the square of the step sum have zero expected value, so Rrms2​=Nb2. For a long Gaussian chain, its radius of gyration obeys Rg2​=Rrms2​/6. If N counts chemical repeat units instead of independent Kuhn length segments, the statistical length used in Rrms2​=Nb2 must be calibrated to that choice of N; it is not automatically the physical bond length or the Kuhn length itself.
