Gauge-invariant operator

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge invariance
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A local field combination is a gauge-invariant operator when its gauge representations and charges combine to a singlet. In an effective field theory, such operators may be multiplied by Wilson coefficients and inverse powers of a heavy scale. Multiplication by ϕ†ϕ preserves the invariance of an existing Higgs Yukawa operator.
