Weyl spinor

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field
OurBigBook.com Words: 964 Articles: 20
A left- or right-handed Weyl spinor transforms in the Lorentz representation (1/2,0) or (0,1/2). Parity exchanges the two chiralities.
