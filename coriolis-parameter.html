Coriolis parameter (f)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Rotating fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 131 Articles: 4
The Coriolis parameter is f=2Ωsinφ for planetary rotation rate Ω and latitude φ. It controls the vertical component of the Coriolis acceleration acting on horizontal flow.
