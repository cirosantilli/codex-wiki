Cauchy derivative convergence bound (sup∣z∣<r​∣fn(k)​−f(k)∣≤(R−r)k+1k!​Rsup∣z∣=R​∣fn​−f∣)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Locally uniform convergence of holomorphic functions
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
For 0<r<R, suppose fn​,f are holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed radius-R disc. Subtract their Cauchy integral formula expressions on that circle. Its length is 2πR, and the distance from an interior point with modulus at most r to the circle is at least R−r. The estimation lemma gives the displayed bound. Uniform convergence on that circle therefore implies uniform convergence of each fixed-order derivative on the smaller disc. The positive separation from the contour is essential for a uniform bound.
