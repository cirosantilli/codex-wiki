Semi-major axis (a)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Kepler orbit
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The semi-major axis is one half of an ellipse's longest diameter. For a Kepler ellipse with apsidal distances r1​,r2​, it is a=(r1​+r2​)/2.
This is one of the semi-major and semi-minor axes; the two-axis geometry is the broader concept.
