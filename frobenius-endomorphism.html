Frobenius endomorphism (x↦xp)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
In a field of characteristic p>0, the Frobenius map x↦xp is an injective field endomorphism because
(x+y)p=xp+yp.
(2)
Consequently every element of an extension field has at most one pth root.
