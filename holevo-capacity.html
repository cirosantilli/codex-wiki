Holevo capacity (χ∗(Λ))

 ... Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Positive linear map Completely positive map Quantum channel
OurBigBook.com Words: 257 Articles: 4
The Holevo capacity of a quantum channel is the maximum one-use output Holevo quantity,
χ∗(Λ)=max{px​,ρx​}​[S(∑x​px​Λ(ρx​))−∑x​px​S(Λ(ρx​))].
(11)
It is the unassisted classical communication limit for product state encodings with collective output measurements. Arbitrary entangled inputs require the regularized expression in the Holevo-Schumacher-Westmoreland theorem.
