Iron-responsive element

 Home Chemistry Polymer Nucleic acid Ribonucleic acid RNA secondary structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A structured RNA recognition element bound by iron regulatory proteins. In suitable 5′ contexts it controls biological translation, whereas a 3′ stability-control context can regulate RNA decay. It is not an autonomous iron-sensing riboswitch.
