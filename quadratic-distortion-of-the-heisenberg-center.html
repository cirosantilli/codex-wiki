Quadratic distortion of the Heisenberg center (∣cm∣H​≍∣m∣​)

The central infinite cyclic subgroup of the Integer Heisenberg group has quadratic subgroup distortion. The identity ck2=[ak,bk] gives ambient length at most 4k against intrinsic length k2. For general m≥0, take k=⌊m​⌋ and write m=k2+r, 0≤r≤2k, to obtain length at most 4k+r≤6m​. Conversely, a word of length n in a,b,c changes the first coordinate by at most n and its central coordinate by at most n2+n, so ∣cm∣ is bounded below by a positive multiple of ∣m∣​ for m=0. Negative exponents follow by inverses. Thus the ambient and intrinsic metrics have quadratic relative growth.
