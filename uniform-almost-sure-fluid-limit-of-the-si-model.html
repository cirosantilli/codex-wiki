The stochastic SI model with susceptible fraction Xn​, initial limit a, and total infection rate nλXn​(1−Xn​) has deterministic limit
x(t)=1−a+ae−λtae−λt​.
(24)
The Poisson time-change representation of a Markov chain writes Xn​ as its integrated drift minus a centered Poisson error. On [0,T] its clock is at most nλT/4. The Poisson maximal concentration bound makes every fixed error tolerance have summable probabilities in n, so the Borel-Cantelli lemma gives uniform error decay almost surely. The Gronwall inequality transfers that decay to Xn​−x, because the drift is Lipschitz continuous. The assertion is for fixed finite intervals and does not claim approximation over times growing with n.
