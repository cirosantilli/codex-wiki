Direct image sheaf (f∗​F)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules
OurBigBook.com Words: 529 Articles: 10
For a continuous map f:X→Y and a sheaf F on X, the direct image is defined by (f∗​F)(V)=F(f−1V) for every open V⊆Y.
The direct image sheaf is the result of the direct image functor: (f∗​F)(V)=F(f−1V).
