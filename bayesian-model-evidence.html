Bayesian model evidence (Z)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 308 Articles: 6
The Bayesian model evidence, also called the marginal likelihood, is
Z=∫L(θ)π(θ)dθ.
(42)
It normalizes the posterior and averages the likelihood over the prior.
