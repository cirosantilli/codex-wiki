Use the Berezin integral orientation ∫∏i​dθˉi​dθi​θ1​θˉ1​⋯θn​θˉn​=1. For an invertible matrix B, the normalized weight e−θˉBθ has ⟨θi​θˉj​⟩=(B−1)ij​. Differentiate its determinant with respect to Bji​ to obtain this formula; two derivatives give the displayed four-point formula. The second pairing carries a fermionic sign. These are ordered contractions, so exchanging two odd factors changes their sign.
