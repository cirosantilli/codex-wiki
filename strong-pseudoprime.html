Strong pseudoprime

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Fermat pseudoprime
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 1
Write the even number N−1 as 2sm with m odd. An odd composite number N is a strong pseudoprime to a coprime base b when
bm≡1(modN)
(6)
or b2jm≡−1(modN) for some 0≤j<s.
