Magnetic field line

 Home Physics Branch of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 107 Articles: 3
A magnetic field line is an integral curve tangent to the magnetic field. In coordinates xi, it obeys dxi/Bi=dxj/Bj wherever the relevant components are nonzero.
