Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra (GZn​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group
OurBigBook.com Words: 545 Articles: 8
For the chain S0​⊂⋯⊂Sn​, the Gelfand–Tsetlin algebra in CSn​ is generated by the centers of CSr​ for 0≤r≤n. Multiplicity-free restriction makes its joint eigenspaces one-dimensional in each irreducible representation. Products of the central primitive idempotents along restriction paths give its minimal projections. Thus it is a maximal commutative subalgebra and a semisimple algebra, isomorphic to a finite product of copies of C.
