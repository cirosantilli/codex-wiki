Splitting field of x cubed minus x minus one

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Galois group of an irreducible cubic
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The polynomial X3−X−1 is irreducible over Q and has discriminant −23, so its splitting field has Galois group S3​. Its unique quadratic subfield is the fixed field of A3​, namely Q(−23​).
