Catastrophic projectile diameter (Dcc​)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Debris disk Collisional cascade Catastrophic disruption threshold
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For equal-density spherical planetesimals, an impactor much smaller than target diameter D causes a catastrophic planetesimal collision when its specific impact energy reaches QD∗​. Neglecting gravitational focusing,
Dcc​=D(vrel2​2QD∗​​)1/3.
(22)
When encounter speed is much smaller than the target's escape velocity, impact speed is approximately vesc​=D2πGρ/3​. For QD∗​=Qb​Db, the focusing-dominated threshold is Dcc​=(3Qb​/πGρ)1/3D(1+b)/3. This assumes the disruption threshold uses impact energy per target mass and neglects energy-coupling efficiency variations.
