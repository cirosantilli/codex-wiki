Monotonicity of half-plane capacity

 ... Probability theory Stochastic process Schramm–Loewner evolution Compact H-hull Mapping-out function of a compact H-hull Half-plane capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 54 Articles: 1
If compact H-hulls satisfy A⊆C, then
hcap(A)≤hcap(C).
(19)
This follows from the Brownian representation of half-plane capacity or from composition of their mapping-out functions.
