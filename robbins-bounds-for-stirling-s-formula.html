Robbins bounds for Stirling's formula (e1/(12n+1)<n!/Sn​<e1/(12n))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Asymptotic analysis Asymptotic equivalence Stirling formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
For every positive integer n, Herbert Robbins's bounds give
Sn​e1/(12n+1)<n!<Sn​e1/(12n).​
(29)
Equivalently, the remainder in log(n!)=logSn​+rn​ satisfies 1/(12n+1)<rn​<1/(12n). This both proves that Sn​ underestimates n! and squeezes n!/Sn​ to one, establishing Stirling's formula. Robbins published the bounds in “A Remark on Stirling's Formula” in 1955. [ref]
For n=10, the relative error of S10​ lies strictly between 1−e−1/121 and 1−e−1/120, approximately 0.823% and 0.830%. A bound specifies the accuracy of an approximation before using it in another calculation.
