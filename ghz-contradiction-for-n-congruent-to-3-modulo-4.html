GHZ contradiction for N congruent to 3 modulo 4 (N≡3(mod4))

For ∣GN−​⟩=(∣0⟩⊗N−∣1⟩⊗N)/2​, let Oj​ have one Pauli X gate at site j and Pauli Y gates elsewhere, and let XN​=X⊗N. The relations Y∣0⟩=i∣1⟩, Y∣1⟩=−i∣0⟩ show that Oj​∣GN−​⟩ is proportional to ∣GN−​⟩ exactly when N is odd. In that case its eigenvalue is λN​=(−1)(N+1)/2, while XN​ always has eigenvalue −1.
Under the EPR criterion of reality and locality, each site's X and Y outcomes have predetermined values xj​,yj​∈{±1}. Multiplying the N certain equations xj​∏k=j​yk​=λN​ gives ∏j​xj​=λN​ when N is odd, since every yk​ appears an even number of times. This contradicts the certain quantum value −1 precisely when N≡3(mod4). Thus the displayed GHZ state gives an infinite family of perfect-correlation contradictions with local hidden-variable theory, without an inequality.
