The spin Hilbert space is a tensor product of two two-dimensional spaces and therefore has dimension four. Angular momentum addition decomposes it as
S=1: MS​=1,0,−1;S=0: MS​=0.​
(18)
These are three orthogonal triplet states and one singlet state. For identical electrons the complete wavefunction must also satisfy fermionic exchange symmetry: a symmetric triplet spin factor accompanies an antisymmetric spatial factor, whereas the antisymmetric singlet spin factor accompanies a symmetric spatial factor. If both electrons occupy the same spatial orbital, only the singlet spin factor is allowed. The four-dimensional spin space does not assert four allowed spin states for that particular spatial occupation.
