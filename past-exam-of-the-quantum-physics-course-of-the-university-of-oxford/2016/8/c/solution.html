There is no allowed spherical harmonic with m=l+1, so L+​Yll​=0. The equation −iℏ∂ϕ​Y=ℏlY first gives Y=f(θ)eilϕ. Combining the differential expressions for Lx​,Ly​ yields
L+​=ℏeiϕ(∂θ​+icotθ∂ϕ​).
(30)
Its annihilation of the highest-weight state becomes
f′(θ)−lcotθf(θ)=0.
(31)
Integrating f′/f=lcotθ gives f=Csinlθ, with the constant solution included for l=0. Hence
Yll​(θ,ϕ)=Csinlθeilϕ.​
(32)
The phase eilϕ is single valued for integral orbital quantum number l; normalization fixes only ∣C∣, leaving a conventional constant phase.
