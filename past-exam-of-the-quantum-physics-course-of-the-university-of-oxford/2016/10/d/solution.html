Their squared magnitudes sum to one. If both Ω and γ vanish, the initial amplitudes simply remain (1,0); the formulas have that continuous limit.
Source convention correction. For positive γ, the printed expression for c2​ lacks the factor −i required by the stated basis and initial vector. A concrete test is its derivative at t=0: the printed expression gives +γ, whereas the Schrödinger equation gives −iγ. Multiplying only c2​ by a phase changes the relative quantum phase. One may change the overall phase of the entire spinor, but must then transform c1​ and its initial amplitude as well. The printed modulus nevertheless gives the correct transition probability.
