The linear confinement correction to Coulomb bound states uses H′=br, and its first-order energy correction in a normalized Coulomb eigenstate is b⟨r⟩. Replace a0​ by aq​ in the hydrogen wavefunctions. The normalized spherical harmonics remove the angular integral, leaving
⟨r⟩nl​=∫0∞​r3∣Rnl​(r)∣2dr.

⟨r⟩2s​=2aq​​∫0∞​u3(1−u+4u2​)e−udu=2aq​​(3!−4!+45!​)=6aq​.

For 2p, the same calculation gives
⟨r⟩2p​=24aq​​∫0∞​u5e−udu=5aq​.

Consequently the first two Coulomb levels become, to first order,
E1s​E2s​E2p,m​​=−4ℏ2mq​a2​+23​baq​+O(b2),=−16ℏ2mq​a2​+6baq​+O(b2),=−16ℏ2mq​a2​+5baq​+O(b2),m=−1,0,1.​​

Thus the second level splits into a singlet orbital level and a triply degenerate orbital level, with E2s​−E2p​=baq​+O(b2). For the confining sign b>0, 2p lies lower than 2s: its smaller mean radius gives the smaller positive correction. The expansion assumes these corrections and the induced couplings to other principal shells are small compared with the relevant Coulomb energy gaps; baq2​/a≪1 is the governing small parameter for fixed low n.
