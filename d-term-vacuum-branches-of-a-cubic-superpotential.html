D-term vacuum branches of a cubic superpotential (W=λϕ0​ϕ+​ϕ−​)

For canonical Kähler potential, set a=∣ϕ0​∣2, b=∣ϕ+​∣2, c=∣ϕ−​∣2. The supplied supergravity F-term potential is ∣λ∣2eκ2(a+b+c)[ab+ac+bc+3κ2abc+κ4abc(a+b+c)]. Adding g(b−c−ζ)2 with g>0, λ=0 and ζ>0 gives a supersymmetric vacuum at a=c=0, b=ζ. The branch b=c=0 has positive energy gζ2, D-term breaking and a flat direction of a scalar potential in ϕ0​. Its charged squared masses are ∣λ∣2aeκ2a∓2gζ, so it is a local minimum valley only when both are positive. For negative ζ, exchange the charged fields. These are branches rather than two isolated minima; a conventional constant Fayet–Iliopoulos term additionally needs a consistent supergravity gauge completion.
