Radioactive activity (A=λN)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Radioactive decay
OurBigBook.com Words: 181 Articles: 3
With n parent atomic nuclei present, the conditional expected number of transformations in a short interval is approximately λnΔt. The corresponding radioactive activity is A=λn. Averaging over the finite-population radioactive decay model gives A(t)=λN(t). Its unit is the becquerel.[ref]
For a finite counting interval, depletion matters. Suppose each transformation is recorded independently with detection efficiency ε, with at most one count per transformation and no background. The expected value of the count between t and t+Δt is
E[C]=εN(t)(1−e−λΔt).
(19)
Only when λΔt≪1 does this become approximately εA(t)Δt. A count rate therefore needs a detector model before it can be interpreted as the source's radioactive activity.
