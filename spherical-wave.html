Spherical wave (e±ikr/r)

 Home Physics Wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For time dependence e−iωt, the three-dimensional radial solutions eikr/r and e−ikr/r describe outgoing and incoming spherical waves away from the origin. Their inverse-radius amplitudes make their flux through spheres independent of radius. They form the asymptotic radial ingredients of partial-wave scattering.
