Spin-one Cartesian basis

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Addition of angular momentum Total-spin sector
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
The spin-one Cartesian basis ∣x⟩,∣y⟩,∣z⟩ transforms as a three-dimensional vector under rotations. In its two-site tensor square, the scalar, antisymmetric, and symmetric traceless subspaces are respectively the total-spin 0, 1, and 2 sectors.
