Abel-Jacobi map of a genus-one curve (P↦[P−O])

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
Let E be a genus one curve over an algebraically closed field and choose O∈E. The map
E⟶Cl0(E),P⟼[P−O]
(241)
is an isomorphism. For a degree-zero divisor D, Riemann--Roch gives a nonzero section of D+O, so D∼P−O for some point P. If P−O∼P′−O with P=P′, a rational function with divisor P−P′ would give a degree-one map to the projective line, contradicting that E has genus one. The Abel--Jacobi map transports addition in the divisor class group to the elliptic-curve group law.
