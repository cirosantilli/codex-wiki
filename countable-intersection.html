Countable intersection (⋂n=1∞​An​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Set Set intersection
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
The countable intersection of a sequence of sets contains exactly the elements belonging to every set. De Morgan's laws identify its relative complement with the union of their complements.
