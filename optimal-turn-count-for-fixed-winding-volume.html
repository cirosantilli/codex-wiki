Optimal turn count for fixed winding volume (Rcoil​=Rs​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Galvanometer
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
For fixed mean turn length and fixed conductor volume, a coil of N turns has conductor length proportional to N and cross-sectional area proportional to 1/N. Its electrical resistance is therefore Rcoil​=aN2. With source electromotive force E and internal resistance Rs​, suppose the small-deflection driving torque is proportional to NI=NE/(Rs​+aN2). Its derivative has the sign of Rs​−aN2, so the maximum occurs at Rcoil​=Rs​. Geometry, damping and finite response time can alter the practical optimization; this is a conditional winding model, not a universal rule for all galvanometers.
