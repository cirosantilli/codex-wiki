Primitive central idempotent

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Center of an associative algebra Central idempotent
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A nonzero central idempotent is primitive central if it is not a sum of two nonzero orthogonal central idempotents. For an Artinian ring, it determines a block of an Artinian algebra. This differs from a primitive idempotent in the whole ring: for a matrix algebra of size greater than one over a field, 1 is primitive central but is a sum of diagonal idempotents.
