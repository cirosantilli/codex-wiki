Classical mixture of coherent states (ρ=∫P(α)∣α⟩⟨α∣d2α,P≥0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Coherent state
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
A normalized nonnegative probability measure over coherent states produces a classical mixture. The measure may contain Dirac delta distributions, so a pure coherent state is included. Conditioning on α gives Poisson distribution photon statistics; hence Varn=⟨n⟩+Var(∣α∣2)≥⟨n⟩. Each optical quadrature has variance at least its vacuum value because mixing adds nonnegative variance of conditional means. These inequalities provide simple nonclassicality witnesses.
