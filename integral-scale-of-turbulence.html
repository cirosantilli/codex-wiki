Integral scale of turbulence (ℓ=∫0∞​f(r)dr)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Homogeneous turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The integral scale of turbulence characterizes energy-containing velocity correlations; in isotropic turbulence one definition is ℓ=∫0∞​f(r)dr. Its turnover time is of order ℓ/u. Large-scale intensity fluctuations can change the energy supplied to the equilibrium range.
