Identical-particle factor in a final-state phase-space integral

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering Scattering amplitude Relativistic scattering cross-section Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
When the full Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure treats n identical final particles as labeled, each physical final configuration is counted n! times. The unlabelled relativistic scattering cross-section therefore includes 1/n!. For two identical scalars, this divides the full-angle result by two; equivalently, integrate over a region containing only one representative of each exchanged pair.
