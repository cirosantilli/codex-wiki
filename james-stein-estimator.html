For one observation X∼Np​(θ,Ip​) with p≥3, the James–Stein estimator is
δJS​(X)=(1−∥X∥2p−2​)X.
(69)
Under quadratic loss, Stein's risk identity gives
R(θ,δJS​)=p−(p−2)2Eθ​∥X∥21​<p,
(70)
so it dominates the usual estimator X.
