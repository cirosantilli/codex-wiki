F. Duncan M. Haldane's 1988 tight-binding model adds complex second-neighbour hopping and opposite sublattice offsets to a honeycomb lattice. Directed phases break time-reversal symmetry while each primitive cell has zero net magnetic flux. Smaller loops can enclose magnetic flux; ordinary Bloch states remain available.[ref]
