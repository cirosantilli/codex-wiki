Infant-directed speech

 Home Biology Animal behaviour Learning Language acquisition
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
Speech addressed to an infant, often differing in prosody from adult-directed speech. A listening preference is an experimentally measurable tendency whose magnitude can depend on the stimuli, the infant’s experience and the measurement method; it is not a universal individual trait.[ref]
