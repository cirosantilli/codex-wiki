Antiferromagnetic Ising ring (H=J∑j=1N​Sj​Sj+1​,J>0,SN+1​=S1​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Spin model Ising model Antiferromagnetic Ising model
OurBigBook.com Words: 355 Articles: 4
An antiferromagnetic Ising model on a closed one-dimensional ring, with Ising spins Sj​=±1 and the displayed periodic boundary conditions. Every opposite neighboring pair minimizes its bond energy. An even ring admits two completely alternating ground states; an odd ring must contain at least one parallel pair, an example of geometric frustration.
