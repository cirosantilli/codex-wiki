Mammillary body

 Home Biology Physiology Homeostasis Physiological integrator Hypothalamus
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
A paired hypothalamic structure with hippocampal and brainstem inputs and outputs to the anterior thalamic nuclei. It can contribute actively to memory processing rather than merely relay hippocampal output.
