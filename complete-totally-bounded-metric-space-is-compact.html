Complete totally bounded metric space is compact

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Topological analysis Metric space Compact metric space
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
A metric space is compact exactly when it is both complete and totally bounded. For the nontrivial direction, successively choose a point from a nested sequence of finite 2−n-nets; a diagonal construction gives a Cauchy subsequence of every sequence, and completeness gives its limit.
