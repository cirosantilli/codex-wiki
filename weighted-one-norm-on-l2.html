Weighted one-norm on l2 (Jw​(u)=∑k​wk​∣uk​∣)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Normed vector space L1 norm
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 1
For 0<c≤wk​<∞, interpret the nonnegative sum as an extended-valued functional on the l2 sequence space. Finiteness implies u∈ℓ1, but unbounded weights can make it infinite even there. It is proper and convex. Every finite partial sum is norm-continuous, so their supremum is sequentially lower semicontinuous and also weakly lower semicontinuous. This is useful as a sparsity penalty in variational regularization.
