Orthogonal decomposition by a closed subspace

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Hilbert space Hilbert projection theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 391 Articles: 8
For a closed subspace F of a Hilbert space,
H=F⊕F⊥.
(1)
The closest point PF​x gives the first component, and differentiating ∥x−PF​x−tu∥2 at zero shows that the remainder is orthogonal to every u∈F.
