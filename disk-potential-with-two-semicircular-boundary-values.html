Disk potential with two semicircular boundary values (u(r,θ)=2V1​+V2​​+πV1​−V2​​arctana2−r22arsinθ​)

On a disk of radius a, prescribe V1​ on the upper open semicircle and V2​ on the lower one. The bounded harmonic extension has the formula in the title for r<a. The boundary data have only a constant term and odd sine Fourier coefficients, giving u=(V1​+V2​)/2+[2(V1​−V2​)/π]∑m≥0​(r/a)2m+1sin((2m+1)θ)/(2m+1). The complex series is half of log[(1+w)/(1−w)], where w=(r/a)eiθ. Its quotient has positive real part, so taking the imaginary part yields the displayed principal arctangent without an argument ambiguity. At the two boundary jumps, the radial limit is the mean; the harmonic function has no continuous extension matching both adjacent constant arcs there.
