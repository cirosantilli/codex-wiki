Figurate number

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic
OurBigBook.com Words: 906 Articles: 15
A figurate number is an integer illustrated by a regular geometric arrangement of points. Triangular numbers and square numbers are basic examples: growing a prescribed shape turns a counting problem into a sequence of integers.
