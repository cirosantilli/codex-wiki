Bouwmeester three-photon GHZ experiment

 ... Branches of physics Quantum theory Controlled unitary gate Controlled-NOT gate Greenberger–Horne–Zeilinger state Photonic GHZ preparation by fourfold coincidence
OurBigBook.com Words: 139
The 1999 experiment used an approximately 200fs, 394nm pump pulse and a beta barium borate crystal. Two polarization-entangled photon pairs supplied four photons. A trigger and three other photodetectors selected a GHZ state through fourfold coincidence counting. Narrow interference filters lengthened the photon coherence time to approximately 500fs, exceeding the pump duration and suppressing timing information about which pair supplied which photon. The optical mode matching was essential to coherence, not merely to routing photons.[ref]
The observed state was selected by all four detections, which also measured the photons. A trigger alone did not demonstrate a guaranteed surviving three-photon output. This distinction connects postselection to what an apparatus actually supplies, rather than identifying a fourfold coincidence with an unconditional source.[ref]
