Triangle identities for an adjunction (εF​Fη=1F​,GεηG​=1G​)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Adjoint functors Unit and counit of an adjunction
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 2
For F⊣G with adjunction unit η and adjunction counit ε, the identities are εFC​FηC​=1FC​ and GεD​ηGD​=1GD​. They make the transpose maps f↦Gfη and g↦εFg inverse.
