Character inner product (⟨χ,ψ⟩G​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Character orthogonality
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
For complex class functions on a finite group G, the character inner product is
⟨χ,ψ⟩G​=∣G∣1​∑g∈G​χ(g)ψ(g)​.
(40)
If ψ is irreducible, this inner product is its multiplicity in the representation with character χ.
