Mass shell (p2=±m2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Four-momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
The mass shell is the locus of four-momenta satisfying the relativistic dispersion relation for a fixed mass m. With mostly-minus Minkowski metric, p2=m2; with mostly-plus Minkowski metric, p2=−m2. The positive-energy sheet represents particle four-momenta. For m=0 this is the light cone. The condition of being on shell refers more generally to satisfying the field equations.
