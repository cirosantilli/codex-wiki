Chiral-superfield fermion mass matrix (mij​=∂i​∂j​W)

OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For canonically normalized chiral superfields, the fermion bilinear is −21​Wij​ψi​ψj​+h.c. at the chosen vacuum. Its matrix is complex symmetric. The nonnegative physical Weyl spinor masses are its singular values, and their squared sum is tr(m†m). Using signed eigenvalues of a symmetric mass matrix instead can obscure the physical mass count.
