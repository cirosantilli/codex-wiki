Doubly linked list

 Home Computer science Data structure Linked list
OurBigBook.com Words: 89 Articles: 1
A linked list whose nodes store predecessor and successor references satisfying reciprocal-link consistency. A known node can be unlinked in constant pointer work without searching for its predecessor. The representation uses extra references and requires both directions to be updated on insertion and deletion.
