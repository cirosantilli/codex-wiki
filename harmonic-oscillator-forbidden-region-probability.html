Harmonic-oscillator forbidden-region probability (P0​=erfc(1))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum harmonic oscillator
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For the normalized ground state of a reduced quantum harmonic oscillator, ϕ0​(q)=π−1/4e−q2/2 and E0​=1/2. Its classical turning points are q=±1. The Born rule assigns probability P=2π−1/2∫1∞​e−q2dq=erfc(1)≃0.157299 to the classically forbidden region. This is a stationary probability of detection outside the classical interval. It is not a transmitted flux or an escape rate: the real stationary wavefunction has zero probability current.
