Settling rarefaction fan (ϕ=21​[1+(z−zm​)/(us​t)])

If lower initial concentration b is less than upper concentration a, the entropy solution expands into a fan with ϕ=[1+(z−zm​)/(us​t)]/2. Its characteristic speeds range from us​(2b−1) to us​(2a−1). The fan can be clipped by a clearing or deposition shock.
