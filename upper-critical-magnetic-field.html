Upper critical magnetic field (Bc2​=μ0​Hc2​=Φ0​/(2πξ2))

Linearize the stated model about ψ=0 in a uniform magnetic field. The kinetic operator has the Landau level ground energy ℏ∣q∣B/(2m∗​). Its cancellation with α<0 gives
Bc2​=ℏ∣q∣2m∗​∣α∣​=∣q∣ξ2ℏ​=2πξ2Φ0​​.
(17)
This is the bulk orbital field scale of this model; it excludes spin-polarization limits and uses the normal-state relation Bc2​=μ0​Hc2​. For the chosen ξ=10nm, it is about 3.29T. With λ=100nm, κ=10. Counting triangular cells at Bc2​ would give a/ξ=4π/3​​≃2.69, but it is the linearized field equation, rather than disks of an arbitrarily chosen core radius touching, that supplied the transition criterion.
