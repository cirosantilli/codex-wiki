If B⊨T and B⊆A∗≡A, every universal sentence of A holds in A∗ and is preserved by passage to the substructure B. Thus B witnesses satisfiability of the joint theory.
For universal theory and substructures of elementarily equivalent models, conversely let B satisfy T together with the universal theory of A. Every existential sentence true in B must be true in A: if an existential σ were false in A, its universal negation would belong to Th∀​(A) and hold in B, a contradiction. Hence Th∃​(B)⊆Th(A). In particular Th(A)∪Th∃​(B) is satisfiable, with model A. Apply the preceding embedding result to the theory Th(A) and structure B. It supplies a structure A∗⊨Th(A) containing B. Therefore A∗≡A and B⊆A∗, as required. Elementary equivalence here does not assert that A itself contains a copy of B.
