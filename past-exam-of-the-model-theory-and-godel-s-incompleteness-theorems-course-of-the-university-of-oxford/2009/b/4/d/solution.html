Existential sentences are preserved under extensions: witnesses remain present and quantifier-free truth is unchanged by an embedding. Therefore any existential axiomatization implies the specified extension-closure property.
For existential axiomatization from extension closure, let E be all existential consequences of T, and suppose A⊨E. We claim that T∪Th∀​(A) is satisfiable. Otherwise compactness supplies finitely many universal sentences θ1​,…,θm​ true in A such that T⊨¬(θ1​∧⋯∧θm​). The finite conjunction is universal after renaming bound variables, so its negation is existential. It belongs to E but is false in A, a contradiction.
The preceding universal-theory embedding result now yields B⊨T and A∗≡A with B⊆A∗. By the assumed extension closure, A∗⊨T; elementary equivalence implies A⊨T. Thus E axiomatizes exactly the models of T, proving existential axiomatizability is equivalent to closure under extensions. This includes the inconsistent case, whose empty model class is axiomatized by an existential contradiction.
