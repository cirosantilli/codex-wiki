OurBigBook.com Words: 60
A complete satisfiable theory has a model and decides every L-sentence: for each σ, exactly one of σ and ¬σ is a consequence. Under the closed convention, exactly one belongs to T. Satisfiability excludes the possibility of both. Equivalently all models of the theory are elementarily equivalent, even though they need not be isomorphic or have equal cardinality.
