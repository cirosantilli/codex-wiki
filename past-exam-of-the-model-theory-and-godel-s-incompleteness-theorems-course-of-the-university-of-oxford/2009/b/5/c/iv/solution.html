No. If a finite conjunction ϕ axiomatized the infinite spaces, it would follow from the vector-space axioms together with all the Dn​. Compactness would make it follow from finitely many of those axioms and dimension requirements. Let N be at least every dimension mentioned there. The finite-dimensional space KN satisfies all vector-space axioms and those finitely many Dn​, hence would satisfy ϕ. But it is a finite set and so cannot be a model of the purported axiomatization. This contradiction proves failure of finite axiomatizability, including finite axiomatizability relative to vector spaces.
