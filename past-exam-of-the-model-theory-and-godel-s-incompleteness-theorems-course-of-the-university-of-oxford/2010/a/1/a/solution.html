OurBigBook.com Words: 86
Write □A for the arithmetical provability predicate applied to the Gödel number of A. Use the Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions that belong to the course meaning of a provability predicate: external necessitation rule, internal modus ponens rule, and positive introspection. No incompleteness theorem is needed here.
If S⊢G, necessitation rule gives S⊢□G. The displayed Gödel sentence equivalence also gives S⊢¬□G. These two theorems contradict the assumption that S is a consistent first-order theory. Therefore
S consistent ⟹ S⊬G.​
(1)
For this part alone, the external necessitation rule condition suffices; the stronger internal conditions are used in the next part.
