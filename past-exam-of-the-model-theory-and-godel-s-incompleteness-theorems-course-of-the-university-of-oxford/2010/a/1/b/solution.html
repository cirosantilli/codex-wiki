Again abbreviate provability by □. The Gödel sentence equivalence gives S⊢G→¬□G. Internalizing this implication using the first two Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions gives
S⊢□G→□¬□G.
(2)
Positive introspection also gives S⊢□G→□□G. Internal modus ponens rule combines proofs of a sentence and its negation into a proof of a contradiction; a contradiction implies any sentence. Consequently
S⊢□G→□¬G.
(3)
These are syntactic manipulations of provability, not an appeal to soundness. If S⊢¬□¬G, taking the contrapositive of the last implication gives S⊢¬□G, and the diagonal equivalence gives S⊢G. Part (a) rules that out for a consistent theory. Hence
S⊬¬□¬Gwhen S is consistent.​
(4)
