Let θ(x) isolate the principal type. Consistency with T means T∪{∃xθ(x)} is satisfiable. Because T is a complete theory, it must imply ∃xθ(x); the alternative would make that extension inconsistent.
Therefore every model of T has a tuple a satisfying θ. Each implication T⊨θ→φ then makes a satisfy every member of the type. Hence
Every principal type is realized in every model of T.​
(20)
Completeness is what turns possible realization of the isolating formula into realization in every model.
