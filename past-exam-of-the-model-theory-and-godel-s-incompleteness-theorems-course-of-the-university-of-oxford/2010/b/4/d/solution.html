Add constants c1​,…,cn​ and form T∪{φ(c):φ∈p}. Every finite set has a model by consistency of the type. The compactness theorem gives a model M realizing the whole expanded theory, so cM realizes p.
Apply the downward Löwenheim-Skolem theorem to the countable expanded language, retaining these finitely many named elements. The resulting countable elementary substructure still satisfies every formula of p at the same tuple. Its reduct to the original language is a countable model of T realizing p. Since T has no finite models, this model is countably infinite.
Every finite-arity type has a realization in a countable model of T.​
