Abbreviate P(┌A┐) by □A. Since S⊢¬X, it proves X→G. The theoremhood and distribution conditions give
S⊢□X→□G.
(20)
The fixed-point equation gives S⊢G→(□G→X), because G implies ¬□G and a false antecedent implies any conclusion. Applying theoremhood and distribution yields
S⊢□G→□(□G→X).
(21)
Positive introspection gives □G→□□G; distribution then gives □G→□X. Thus S⊢□G↔□X. Combining with G↔¬□G proves
S⊢G↔¬□X.​
(22)
In particular, choosing a fixed logical contradiction for X identifies this Gödel sentence with the formal consistency statement of S.
