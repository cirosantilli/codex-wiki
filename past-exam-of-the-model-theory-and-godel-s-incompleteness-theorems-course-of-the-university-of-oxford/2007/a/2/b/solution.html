Write H(v) for the proposed stage-comparison logical formula and fix a standard tuple a. If a∈A, numeralwise enumeration in arithmetic supplies a standard m with S⊢F(aˉ,mˉ). Disjointness gives a∈/B, so S⊢¬G(aˉ,jˉ​) for every standard j, in particular j=0,…,m. The source's finite initial-segment axiom combines this finite list into
S⊢∀y≤mˉ¬G(aˉ,y).
(11)
Use mˉ as the existential witness to obtain S⊢H(aˉ).
If instead a∈B, choose n with S⊢G(aˉ,nˉ). Disjointness gives refutations of F(aˉ,jˉ​) for all j, so the finitely many j≤n give
S⊢∀x≤nˉ¬F(aˉ,x).
(12)
For an arbitrary x, the source's comparison axiom gives x≤nˉ or nˉ≤x. In the first case the first conjunct of the proposed witness condition is false. In the second case y=nˉ witnesses failure of its bounded universal conjunct, since G(aˉ,nˉ) is provable. Generalization over x therefore gives S⊢¬H(aˉ).
Thus the logical formula separates A from B. The formal proof uses the enumerators' numeralwise positive and negative formal proof properties and the two explicit order principles supplied in the source; it does not assume the first-order theory can decide arbitrary unbounded existential logical formulas.
