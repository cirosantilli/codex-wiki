Past exam of the model theory and Gödel's incompleteness theorems course of the University of Oxford / 2008 / A / 3 / a / Solution

 ... Model theory and Gödel's incompleteness theorems course of the University of Oxford Past exam of the model theory and Gödel's incompleteness theorems course of the University of Oxford 2008 A 3 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 173
Use the Hilbert-Bernays-Löb derivability conditions for an arithmetical provability predicate. Abbreviate PS​(┌A┐) by PA. The conditions needed below are
S⊢A⟹S⊢PA,S⊢P(A→B)→(PA→PB),S⊢PA→PPA.
(18)
These are the usual theoremhood, distribution and positive-introspection conditions intended by a standard arithmetical provability predicate; arbitrary predicate symbols merely agreeing with provability externally need not satisfy them.
Since S⊢¬Y, it proves Y→G. The first two conditions give S⊢PY→PG. For the converse, the fixed-point equivalence gives S⊢G→(PG→Y). Applying theoremhood and distribution gives
S⊢PG→P(PG→Y).
(19)
Positive introspection also gives PG→PPG, and distribution combines these to yield S⊢PG→PY. Thus S⊢PG↔PY. Combining with G↔¬PG proves
S⊢G↔¬PY.​
(20)
If S proved ¬PY, it would prove G. The theoremhood condition would then give PG, while the fixed-point equivalence gives ¬PG. Therefore first-order consistency implies
S⊬¬PY.​
(21)
