The source's system called K already contains positive introspection, so its displayed axioms are those usually called K4. Put
B=□(□X→X),A=(B→□X).
(31)
We first show that its axioms prove □A→A, the implication suggested in the hint. From B, positive introspection gives □B. Distribution applied to □A=□(B→□X) and □B gives □□X. Distribution applied to B=□(□X→X) and this last result gives □X. Thus the theorem-level implications combine by propositional reasoning into
K4⊢□A→(B→□X),
(32)
which is K4⊢□A→A.
Since S contains these axioms, it proves that implication. Its assumed Löb inference rule, applied to the first-order sentence A, yields S⊢A. Unpacking A proves
S⊢□(□X→X)→□X.​
(33)
This uses necessitation only as part of the ordinary modal theorem rules; no inference of a boxed temporary assumption has been made.
