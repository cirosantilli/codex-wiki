Set Y=□X→X. The Löb axiom at X is □Y→□X. Therefore Y and □Y together imply X, proving
GL⊢Y→(□Y→X).
(37)
For the converse, the tautology X→Y gives □X→□Y by necessitation and distribution. Consequently □Y→X together with □X gives X, so
GL⊢(□Y→X)→(□X→X),
(38)
whose consequent is Y. Combining both directions gives an unboxed equivalence as a theorem of GL. Necessitation then yields exactly
GL⊢□(Y↔(□Y→X)).​
(39)
