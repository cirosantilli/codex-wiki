Past exam of the model theory and Gödel's incompleteness theorems course of the University of Oxford / 2008 / B / 6 / iv / Solution

OurBigBook.com Words: 160
Add a predicate symbol Pθ​ for each L-formula θ(x), of arity equal to its displayed free-variable tuple. Let T+ consist of T together with all defining axioms
∀x (Pθ​(x)↔θ(x)).
(50)
Every first-order model of T has a unique such expansion, interpreting the new predicate symbols by their defining sets. Conversely the reduct of every first-order model of T+ satisfies T.
Given an arbitrary L+-formula ψ(x), replace each new atomic predicate symbol by its original L-definition, renaming bound variables to avoid capture. This produces an L-formula ψ−(x) with
T+⊨∀x (ψ(x)↔ψ−(x)).
(51)
The defining axiom for that particular logical formula now gives an atomic replacement:
T+⊨∀x (ψ(x)↔Pψ−​(x)).​
(52)
Thus the expanded first-order theory eliminates quantifiers. For a first-order sentence, the same translation is an L-sentence, decided by completeness of T; hence every expanded first-order sentence is decided and T+ is complete. Empty free-variable tuples can be handled by zero-ary predicate symbols, or by the usual logical truth and falsity constants. This definitional expansion is called Morleyization.
