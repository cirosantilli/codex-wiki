Tensor-product wavelet

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Wavelet Tensor-product wavelet basis
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A tensor-product wavelet is a product of one-dimensional wavelets and scaling functions, with at least one wavelet factor. In two dimensions, the three simultaneous-scale types are ψ⊗φ, φ⊗ψ and ψ⊗ψ. Their translates and normalized dilates form a tensor-product wavelet basis.
