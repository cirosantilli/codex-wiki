Use Fμνa​=∂μ​Aνa​−∂ν​Aμa​+gfabcAμb​Aνc​ and (Dμ​c)a=∂μ​ca+gfabcAμb​cc. The Faddeev-Popov gauge-orbit identity with Lorenz gauge function Ga=∂μAμa​, followed by Gaussian averaging of Ga=fa, supplies the quadratic gauge-fixing term. Represent the determinant of −∂μDμ​ using a Grassmann Gaussian integral over a Faddeev-Popov ghost field and antighost field to obtain the ghost term. The minus sign in this operator differs by a field-independent determinant factor from the direct gauge-condition Jacobian; it is a consistent antighost convention.
The ghost term equals (∂μcˉa)(Dμ​c)a up to a boundary term. Thus it contains a ghost-gluon vertex with the sign matching this kinetic convention. An equivalent auxiliary-field form replaces the gauge-fixing term by BaGa+ξBaBa/2. The Nakanishi-Lautrup field equation gives Ba=−Ga/ξ and recovers the displayed Lagrangian. With left-acting BRST transformations sA=Dc, sc=−g[c,c]/2, scˉ=B, and sB=0, the gauge-fixing and ghost terms together are s[cˉa(Ga+ξBa/2)].
