Linear algebraic group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 74
An affine algebraic group is an affine variety G whose multiplication and inversion are regular maps. Equivalently, its coordinate ring k[G] is a commutative Hopf algebra, with comultiplication induced by multiplication in G.
