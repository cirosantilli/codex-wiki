High-energy shift from a central delta barrier (En​−En(0)​→κ/a)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Delta potential Central delta barrier in a symmetric infinite well
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
Put c=mκ/ℏ2, kn​=(n+1/2)π/a, and δn​=a(λn​−kn​). The quantization equation gives δn​=arctan(c/λn​), so λn​δn​→c and δn​→0. Therefore λn2​−kn2​→2c/a, proving the displayed absolute energy shift. Its ratio to the unperturbed energy tends to zero. The normalized unperturbed even state has probability density 1/a at the origin, so its first-order delta-barrier expectation is also κ/a.
