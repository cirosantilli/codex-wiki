Internal energy of a van der Waals gas (U(V,T)=h(T)−a/V)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure Equation of state Van der Waals equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 102
For fixed amount of substance, thermodynamic consistency gives
(∂V∂U​)T​=T(∂T∂P​)V​−P.
(7)
Indeed, the first law of thermodynamics gives UV​∣T​=TSV​∣T​−P, and the Helmholtz free energy Maxwell relation gives SV​∣T​=PT​∣V​. For the fixed-amount convention P=RT/(V−b)−a/V2 with temperature-independent a,b, substitution gives UV​∣T​=a/V2. Integration yields U=h(T)−a/V. If the dilute-limit reference is U→cT as V→∞ for every T, then h(T)=cT.
The same formula may be interpreted molarly if V,U,a,b all use that convention. Mixing molar and total quantities changes the amount factors. The attraction controls the volume correction to internal energy, while excluded volume alone does not contribute to this derivative when a,b are independent of temperature.
