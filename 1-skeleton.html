1-skeleton (X(1))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology CW complex
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The one-skeleton X(1) of a CW complex is the union of its zero-cells and one-cells. It is a graph. The unit-edge path metric measures the infimum of lengths of paths in this graph; it is different from metrics that allow paths through two-cell interiors.
