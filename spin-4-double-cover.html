Spin(4) double cover (Spin(4)≅SU(2)×SU(2))

 ... Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Simple Lie algebra Special orthogonal Lie algebra Spin group
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
Two unit quaternions act on Euclidean four-space by q↦aqb−1. This norm-preserving action maps onto the SO(4) group. Its kernel is precisely (1,1) and (−1,−1): a kernel pair must have equal entries commuting with every quaternion. The two simply connected SU(2) factors therefore form the universal spin cover.
