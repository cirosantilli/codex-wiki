Inversion mapping torus of a torus (Mn​=(Tn−1×[0,1])/((x,0)∼(−x,1)))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Mapping torus
OurBigBook.com Words: 195 Articles: 3
This mapping torus fibers over the circle with fiber Tn−1 and inversion as monodromy. The Wang sequence computes its additive cohomology. Coordinate projections to the Klein bottle expose its mod-two cup products.
