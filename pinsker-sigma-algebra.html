Pinsker sigma-algebra

 ... Real analysis Measure theory Ergodic theory Entropy of a finite measurable partition Entropy rate of a measurable partition Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
The Pinsker sigma-algebra consists of the measurable sets A whose binary partition {A,X∖A} has entropy rate zero. It is a sigma-algebra and is the largest invariant factor with zero Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy.
