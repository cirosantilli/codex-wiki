Couette flow

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 3
Couette flow is viscous shear flow driven by relative tangential motion of parallel boundaries. In the steady no-pressure-gradient case its velocity profile is linear.
