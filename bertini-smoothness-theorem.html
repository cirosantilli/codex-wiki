Bertini smoothness theorem (Sing(Dgeneral​)⊆Bs∣D∣)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Weil divisor Linear system of divisors
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
On a smooth variety over a field of characteristic zero, a general member of a basepoint-free linear system of divisors is smooth. More generally it is smooth outside the base locus. Irreducibility is a separate assertion and requires suitable hypotheses on the associated morphism; a pencil composed with a map to a curve can have disconnected general divisors.
