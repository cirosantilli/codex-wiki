Three-body leptonic tau-decay kinematic bounds (mℓ​≤Eℓ∗​≤(mτ2​+mℓ2​)/(2mτ​))

Combine the two massless neutrinos in τ−→ℓ−νˉℓ​ντ​ into q. In the parent rest frame, four-momentum conservation gives
q2=mτ2​+mℓ2​−2mτ​Eℓ∗​,0≤q2≤(mτ​−mℓ​)2.
(44)
The lower bound follows from the nonnegative Minkowski norm of the sum of two future-directed null four-vectors; the upper bound follows from Eℓ∗​≥mℓ​. Substituting gives the displayed energy bounds. They are kinematic: they do not specify the weak interaction decay amplitude or the probability density function inside the interval.
If the charged daughter is also massless, put x=2Eℓ∗​/mτ​∈[0,1]. A parent with Lorentz factor γ and speed β in natural units produces
Eℓ​=γ2mτ​x​(1+βcosθ∗),0≤Eℓ​≤2Eτ​​(1+β).
(45)
Here θ∗ is the daughter angle relative to the boost direction in the parent rest frame. Two opposite parent momenta therefore need not produce two opposite visible daughter momenta: the invisible system can recoil differently in each particle decay.
