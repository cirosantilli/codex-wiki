Twistor theory

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 18
Twistor theory encodes the conformal geometry of four-dimensional spacetime using incidence geometry in a complex vector space. In the flat correspondence, a spacetime point is a two-plane in a four-dimensional twistor space, and hence a projective line in projective twistor space. Null separation becomes intersection of these lines. Choosing a spacetime signature is an additional reality condition on the complex geometry.
