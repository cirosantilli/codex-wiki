Light-emitting diode efficiency

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Diode Light-emitting diode
OurBigBook.com Words: 579 Articles: 4
Efficiency has several denominators. Specify a steady electric current I>0, positive terminal electric potential difference Vf​, and a simplified narrow emission spectrum with photon energy Eγ​. Assume at most one radiative photon per injected electron and electron hole pair. Let ηinj​ be the fraction of the electrical pair-supply rate I/q reaching the active layer, ηrad​ the fraction of those pairs recombining radiatively, and ηext​ the fraction of internally emitted photons that escape. Then counting the successive events gives
N˙active​=ηinj​qI​,N˙internal​=ηrad​N˙active​,N˙out​=ηext​N˙internal​.
(12)
Define internal quantum efficiency here conditionally on pairs reaching the active layer: ηIQE​=ηrad​. The internal photon yield per electrical pair is instead ηinj​ηIQE​. Stating this convention matters when another definition folds injection into internal quantum efficiency. The external quantum efficiency and wall-plug efficiency are
ηEQE​=I/qN˙out​​=ηinj​ηIQE​ηext​,ηWPE​=IVf​Popt​​=ηEQE​qVf​Eγ​​,Popt​=N˙out​Eγ​.
(13)
These are photon-count and energy-flow identities for the declared model. For a broad spectrum, replace Eγ​ by the mean escaping photon energy, weighted by photon number, when computing optical power.
A stipulated example chooses I=20mA, Vf​=3.20V, λ=450nm, ηinj​=0.90, ηIQE​=0.80 and ηext​=0.50. For every 100 electrical pairs, 90 reach the active layer, 72 emit internally, and 36 photons escape. Thus ηEQE​=0.36, not 0.80. Using q=1.602176634×10−19C gives
I/qN˙out​Popt​ηWPE​​=1.24830×1017s−1,=4.49389×1016s−1,=19.8375mW,IVf​=64.0mW,=0.309960.​
(14)
The dimensional analysis is transparent: A/C=s−1, J/s=W, and CV=J. The optical and electrical efficiencies differ because Eγ​/q=2.75520V differs from the chosen Vf​.
The factors suggest different improvements. Better confinement can reduce injection leakage; suppressing nonradiative recombination raises the radiative fraction; improving light extraction efficiency helps already-emitted photons escape. A photon trapped by total internal reflection has already passed the radiative step and can still be lost through absorption. Improving extraction therefore need not change the internal radiative fraction.
Shuji Nakamura's lecture cautions that why indium gallium nitride emits efficiently despite many defects was not completely settled in that account.[ref]
As an independent consistency check on the 1994 reported values, treating its broad spectrum as monochromatic at the 450nm peak gives
ηEQE​≈(0.020A)(hc/(450nm))(1.5×10−3W)q​=0.02722.
(15)
This agrees at the quoted precision with the reported 2.7%. Its optical/electrical power ratio is instead 1.5mW/(20mA×3.6V)=2.0833%. The first estimate uses a peak in place of a photon-number-weighted mean, so agreement is a consistency check rather than an exact reanalysis of the measured spectrum.
