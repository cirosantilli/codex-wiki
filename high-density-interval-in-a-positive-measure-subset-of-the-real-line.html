High-density interval in a positive-measure subset of the real line

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Measure density Lebesgue's density theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
If a measurable set E⊆R has positive measure, then for every ε>0 there is a bounded interval I such that
m(E∩I)>(1−ε)m(I).
(8)
Choose a density-one point of a finite positive-measure portion of E using the Lebesgue density theorem, and take a sufficiently small interval centred there.
