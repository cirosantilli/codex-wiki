Stimulated Raman adiabatic passage (STIRAP)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Control theory Quantum control Hamiltonian engineering Adiabatic quantum control
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For a resonant three-level chain with couplings Ω1​,Ω2​, the zero-energy dark state of a driven Hamiltonian is ∣D⟩=(Ω2​∣1⟩−Ω1​∣3⟩)/Ω12​+Ω22​​. Turning on the target-to-intermediate coupling first and sweeping Ω1​/Ω2​ from zero to infinity rotates this state from ∣1⟩ to −∣3⟩. The other eigenvalues are ±Ω12​+Ω22​​, so ℏ∣θ˙∣≪Ω12​+Ω22​​ controls nonadiabatic leakage. Ideally the intermediate state remains unoccupied, suppressing spontaneous-emission loss. The gap must not close while the dark state is rotating.
