Compact H-hull (A⊂H)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Schramm–Loewner evolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 27
A compact H-hull is a bounded relatively closed subset A of the complex upper half-plane H such that H∖A is a simply connected domain.
