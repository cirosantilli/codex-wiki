Geodesic congruence

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 35
A geodesic congruence is a family of nonintersecting geodesics filling an open spacetime region. Its derivative tensor describes the relative motion of neighboring geodesics.
Requiring each member to be a geodesic specializes a congruence of curves in general relativity.

Timelike geodesic vector field

Words: 268 Articles: 8
Geodesic congruence OurBigBook.com
A timelike geodesic vector field is a smooth unit timelike field ua whose integral curves obey ub∇b​ua=0. Its expansion scalar, shear tensor of a timelike congruence, and vorticity tensor of a timelike congruence evolve according to the timelike Raychaudhuri equation.

Spatial projector of a timelike congruence (hab​=gab​+ua​ub​)

Words: 29
Timelike geodesic vector field OurBigBook.com
For a unit timelike vector field uaua​=−1, the tensor hab​=gab​+ua​ub​ projects onto the three-dimensional subspace orthogonal to ua. In particular, hab​ua=hab​ub=0 and gabhab​=3 in four spacetime dimensions.

Deformation tensor of a timelike geodesic congruence (Bab​=∇b​ua​)

Words: 91 Articles: 2
Timelike geodesic vector field OurBigBook.com
For a unit geodesic vector field ua, differentiation of uaua​=−1 and the geodesic equation show that Bab​ua=Bab​ub=0. The tensor therefore acts entirely in the local rest space.

Kinematic decomposition of a timelike geodesic congruence

Deformation tensor of a timelike geodesic congruence OurBigBook.com
In four spacetime dimensions, the deformation tensor decomposes as
Bab​=31​θhab​+σab​+ωab​,
(1)
where θ=Babhab​ is the expansion scalar, σab​=B(ab)​−θhab​/3 is the symmetric trace-free shear tensor of a timelike congruence, and ωab​=B[ab]​ is the vorticity tensor of a timelike congruence.

Riccati equation for a timelike geodesic congruence

Words: 34
Deformation tensor of a timelike geodesic congruence OurBigBook.com
With the curvature convention [∇μ​,∇β​]Xα​=−Rραμβ​Xρ​, the deformation tensor along a unit timelike geodesic field satisfies
Xμ∇μ​Bαβ​=−Bμβ​Bαμ​+Rμβα​νXμXν​.
(2)
Taking its spatial trace and inserting the kinematic decomposition of a timelike geodesic congruence leads to the timelike Raychaudhuri equation.

Expansion scalar (θ)

Words: 27
Timelike geodesic vector field OurBigBook.com
The expansion scalar of a unit timelike congruence with tangent ua is θ=∇a​ua. It gives the fractional rate of change of an infinitesimal comoving spatial volume.

Shear tensor of a timelike congruence (σab​)

Words: 28
Timelike geodesic vector field OurBigBook.com
The shear tensor is the symmetric trace-free spatial part of ∇b​ua​. It changes the shape of an infinitesimal comoving volume without changing that volume to first order.

Vorticity tensor of a timelike congruence (ωab​)

Words: 22
Timelike geodesic vector field OurBigBook.com
The vorticity tensor is the antisymmetric spatial part of ∇b​ua​. It vanishes exactly when the timelike congruence is locally hypersurface orthogonal.

Focal point of a hypersurface

Words: 43
Timelike geodesic vector field OurBigBook.com
A focal point of a hypersurface along an orthogonal geodesic is a point where a nonzero normal Jacobi field arising from variations of the initial point vanishes. Beyond the first focal point, that geodesic no longer locally maximizes proper time from a spacelike initial hypersurface.

Timelike geodesic completeness

Words: 52 Articles: 1
Geodesic congruence OurBigBook.com
A spacetime is timelike-geodesically complete when every inextendible affinely parametrized timelike geodesic has proper time extending without a finite endpoint in both directions.

Timelike geodesic incompleteness

Words: 29
Timelike geodesic completeness OurBigBook.com
Timelike geodesic incompleteness means that at least one inextendible timelike geodesic reaches an endpoint after finite proper time. Singularity theorems commonly establish this conclusion without asserting that curvature diverges.

Null geodesic congruence

Geodesic congruence OurBigBook.com
A null geodesic congruence has an affinely parametrized null tangent ka satisfying kaka​=0 and kb∇b​ka=0.

Optical matrix of a null congruence (Bab​=Pa​cPb​d∇d​Uc​)

Words: 125 Articles: 1
Null geodesic congruence OurBigBook.com
For affine null tangent U, choose an auxiliary null vector N with U⋅N=−1 and parallel transport it along the rays. The screen-space projector is Pab​=gab​+Ua​Nb​+Na​Ub​. The optical matrix of a null congruence describes evolution of screen-projected deviation vectors. Its trace is the null expansion, its symmetric trace-free part is the null shear, and its antisymmetric part is the null twist. In four dimensions B=θP/2+σ+ω.

Sachs optical equations

Words: 61
Optical matrix of a null congruence OurBigBook.com
For a parallel-transported screen and affine null geodesic tangent U, the optical matrix satisfies
Uc∇c​Bab​=−Bac​Bcb​−Pa​ePb​fRcedf​UcUd.
(3)
Its trace gives the null Raychaudhuri equation. In a two-dimensional screen, symmetric trace-free matrices have scalar squares and anticommute with antisymmetric matrices. The trace-free symmetric and antisymmetric parts consequently yield the null shear and twist transport equations without additional quadratic trace-free terms.

Parallel null partner for ingoing Schwarzschild rays (U=−E∂r​,N=E−1(∂v​+21​f∂r​))

Words: 61
Null geodesic congruence OurBigBook.com
In the Ingoing Eddington-Finkelstein coordinates metric ds2=−f(r)dv2+2dvdr+r2dΩ2, where f=1−2M/r, the tangent U=−E∂r​ is affine when E>0 is constant along each ray. The displayed radial partner obeys N2=0 and U⋅N=−1. Since Γvrv​=Γarr​=0 and Γrrv​=−f′/2, its derivative ∂r​Nr=f′/(2E) cancels the connection term, giving ∇U​N=0. Its screen is the angular two-plane, with optical tensor BAB​=−ErγAB​, null expansion −2E/r, and zero null shear and null twist.

Affine rescaling of a null normal (∇K​K=κK,K(logh)=−κ)

Words: 49
Null geodesic congruence OurBigBook.com
If a null vector tangent satisfies ∇K​K=κK, then U=hK is affine when K(logh)=−κ. Choose h=1 on the initial transverse surface. The screen-space projector kills derivative terms proportional to K, so the initial optical tensor and its null expansion agree with those of K. In ingoing spherical coordinates, K=∂v​+(f/2)∂r​ has κ=f′/2.

Parallel auxiliary null vector (N2=0, U⋅N=−1, ∇U​N=0)

Words: 59
Null geodesic congruence OurBigBook.com
For an affine null geodesic congruence with future tangent U, choose a future unit timelike vector T on a transverse section, put E=−U⋅T, and set N=T/E−U/(2E2) there. It has N2=0 and U⋅N=−1. Parallel transport along each generator preserves these contractions by metric compatibility and ∇U​U=0. The construction is local up to caustics and supplies the auxiliary normal for a screen-space projector.

Null-gradient affine-geodesic identity (ka​=∇a​S, k2=0⇒∇k​k=0)

Null geodesic congruence OurBigBook.com
If ka​=∇a​S is a null gradient, symmetry of the Hessian gives ka∇a​kb​=ka∇b​ka​=21​∇b​(k2)=0. Its integral curves are therefore affinely parametrized null geodesics, rather than merely null curves with a nonaffinity term.

Screen-space projector (Pab​)

Null geodesic congruence OurBigBook.com
Given null vectors k and n with k⋅n=−1, the screen-space projector is
Pab​=δba​+kanb​+nakb​.
(4)
It annihilates k and n and, in D spacetime dimensions, projects onto the (D−2)-dimensional spacelike space transverse to them.

Spherical null expansion in ingoing coordinates (θK​=2K(r)/r)
Words: 53
Null expansion OurBigBook.com
For a four-dimensional spherical metric ds2=−fdv2+2dvdr+r2dΩ2, a radial null vector normal K to a round sphere has null expansion θK​=21​qABLK​qAB​=2K(r)/r, where qAB​=r2γAB​. The angular metric changes only by a common scale, so radial congruences have zero null shear; hypersurface orthogonality also gives zero null twist. The convention here is the trace, not the screen-averaged expansion.
Spherical null expansions in double-null coordinates (θU​=fr2r,U​​,θV​=fr2r,V​​)
Words: 121 Articles: 2
Null expansion OurBigBook.com
For a four-dimensional spherically symmetric metric ds2=−2fdUdV+r2dΩ2, the affine null gradients are f−1∂V​ and f−1∂U​. Their null expansions are 2r,V​/(fr) and 2r,U​/(fr). Their null shear and null twist vanish because the transverse metric changes only by a common scale and the covectors are exact gradients.
Double-null focusing identity (∂U​(f−1r,U​)=−f4πr​TUU​)

The Null Raychaudhuri equation and Einstein field equations give ∂U​(f−1r,U​)=−rRUU​/(2f)=−4πrTUU​/f, with an exchanged-coordinate identity in V. Under the null energy condition, each rescaled radial derivative is nonincreasing along its corresponding future null direction.
Persistence of spherical trapping
Words: 51
Double-null focusing identity OurBigBook.com
In a regular double-null region satisfying the null energy condition, an initially negative r,U​ on a Cauchy hypersurface remains negative in its future domain of dependence. If both spherical null expansions are negative at one sphere, the exchanged-coordinate focusing inequality preserves the other sign along its future outgoing null direction. Every later sphere that still exists there remains a trapped surface.

Null-shear propagation equation (U⋅∇σab​=−θσab​−Pa​ePb​fCcedf​UcUd)
Words: 59
Null shear OurBigBook.com
For an affine null congruence in four spacetime dimensions with a parallel-transported screen, the displayed equation follows by taking the symmetric trace-free part of the Sachs optical equations. The Ricci contribution to the optical tidal tensor is Pab​Rcd​UcUd/2, so it changes the expansion but contributes no screen-trace-free source. The projected Weyl tensor supplies the tidal source of shear.
Null twist (ωab​)
Words: 127 Articles: 2
Optical tensor OurBigBook.com
The null twist is ωab​=B[ab]​. It vanishes for null generators orthogonal to a hypersurface by the Frobenius theorem.
Null twist vanishes exactly for hypersurface-orthogonal geodesics (ωab​=0⟺U[a​∇b​Uc]​=0)
Words: 78
Null twist OurBigBook.com
For an affine null geodesic congruence, Bab​=∇b​Ua​ annihilates U in both slots. Relative to a null partner, its antisymmetric part is its screen projection plus a term of the form U[a​qb]​. Hence zero null twist implies U∧dU=0, and Frobenius theorem makes Ua​ locally proportional to a hypersurface gradient. Conversely, if Ua​=h∇a​S, its antisymmetric derivative has a factor of U, so the screen projection vanishes. Geodesicity is essential to the converse from vanishing screen twist to the full Frobenius condition.

Area-square-root optical focusing equation (A1/2(A1/2)′′​=−Rab​kakb/2−σ2)

Null Raychaudhuri equation OurBigBook.com
For an affinely parametrized twist-free null geodesic congruence, let A be local transported screen area and σ2=σAB​σAB/2. The Null Raychaudhuri equation gives θ′=−θ2/2−2σ2−Rab​kakb and A′/A=θ. Flat-space anisotropic beam shear focusing (A=1−λ2,σ2=(1−λ2)−2)

In flat spacetime take a twist-free beam's two screen Jacobi factors to be 1+λ and 1−λ. They solve the zero-curvature geodesic deviation equation. Their product is the displayed area, and subtracting the trace from their optical matrix gives the displayed nonnegative shear. Direct differentiation yields (A1/2)′′=−(1−λ2)−3/2=−σ2A1/2, an explicit check of the focusing sign.

Conjugate point to a spacelike surface
Words: 47
Null focusing theorem OurBigBook.com
A conjugate point to a spacelike surface occurs where normal geodesics develop a focal degeneracy: the transverse Jacobi field map from initial points on the surface ceases to be invertible. Collapse of the transverse area makes the null expansion diverge. Beyond a first such point, the normal null geodesic cannot continue as a generator of the achronal boundary of the surface's future.
