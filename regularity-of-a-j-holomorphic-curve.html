Regularity of a J-holomorphic curve (Du​ is surjective)

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Pseudoholomorphic curve Cauchy–Riemann operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 187 Articles: 2
A J-holomorphic curve is regular when the linearized Cauchy–Riemann operator is surjective. Regularity only for simple curves is weaker than regularity for all maps: multiple covers of an exceptional sphere can fail regularity even when that simple sphere is regular.
