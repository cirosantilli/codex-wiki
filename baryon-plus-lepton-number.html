Baryon plus lepton number (B+L)

 ... Physics Branch of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Baryon number
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Baryon plus lepton number is the sum of baryon number and lepton number. It is an accidental classical symmetry of the renormalizable Standard Model but has an SU(2)L​ chiral anomaly, so electroweak instantons and sphalerons violate it.
