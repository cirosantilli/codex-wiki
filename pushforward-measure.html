Pushforward measure (f∗​μ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 82 Articles: 1
For a measurable map f:X→Y, the pushforward of μ is the measure
(f∗​μ)(B)=μ(f−1(B)).
(40)
It is the distribution induced on Y by mapping points distributed according to μ through f.
