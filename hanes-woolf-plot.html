Hanes-Woolf plot

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Enzyme kinetics Michaelis-Menten kinetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A plot of [S]/v against [S] for a reaction modeled by Michaelis-Menten kinetics. The slope is 1/Vmax​, the vertical intercept Km​/Vmax​ and the horizontal intercept −Km​; graphical transformation changes how measurement errors are weighted.
