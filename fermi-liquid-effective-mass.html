Fermi-liquid effective mass (m∗=pF​/vF​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Fermi liquid Landau quasiparticle
OurBigBook.com Words: 170 Articles: 1
In a three-dimensional uniform isotropic continuum obeying the Principle of Galilean relativity, with one bare particle mass m, the Fermi-liquid effective mass satisfies
mm∗​=1+3F1s​​.
(13)
A band effective mass need not satisfy this relation.[ref]
For the stipulated m∗=2m in the occupation figure, this relation requires F1s​=3 if those assumptions apply. At fixed pF​, vF​ is half its free-particle value, so moving by 0.04pF​ from the Fermi surface costs approximately 0.02pF2​/m instead of 0.04pF2​/m. The factor of three belongs to the three-dimensional angular average, not to a universal definition of effective mass.
