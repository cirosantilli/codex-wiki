Two cube edges in every direction force many vertices (∣F∣≥⌈3n/2⌉+1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Coordinate shifts of a set family Downward coordinate compression
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
For a family in the n-dimensional Boolean lattice, the loss of trace cardinality on deleting coordinate i equals the number of pairs S,S∪{i} both in the family. If every direction has at least two such edges, downward coordinate compression preserves that property. The resulting down-set contains the empty set and all n singletons. Each coordinate must also occur in a two-element member. Those pairs cover all n coordinates and number at least ⌈n/2⌉, giving the bound. For n≥2, the empty set, all singletons and a minimum collection of pairs covering every coordinate attain it.
