Phosphine ligand

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry Ligand in coordination chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 131 Articles: 5
A phosphine ligand such as PR3​ donates a phosphorus lone pair. It can also accept pi backbonding into orbitals with substantial P–R antibonding character; phosphorus 3d orbitals need not be essential acceptors. Alkyl substitution generally increases sigma donation relative to electron-withdrawing substituents.
