Algebra over a commutative ring

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 173 Articles: 5
An algebra over a commutative ring R is a ring A with a specified unital ring homomorphism R→A whose image commutes with every element of A. Scalar multiplication is r⋅a=φ(r)a. For commutative A, the commutation requirement is automatic.
