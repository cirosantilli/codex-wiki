Nonseparating curve

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Path-connected space Continuous path Simple closed curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
A simple closed curve in a connected oriented surface is nonseparating when cutting along it leaves a connected surface. A separating curve bounds a subsurface and represents zero in first homology. For a nonseparating curve, an arc between the two new boundary components closes to a curve meeting it once. Its algebraic intersection number of curves on an oriented surface is ±1, proving that the original curve represents a primitive homology class.
