Wind Accretion and State Transitions in Cygnus X-1 by D. R. Gies and others (2003; preprint 2002)

 Home Physics Physics bibliography
OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by D. R. Gies Words: 44
Astrophysical Journal 583, 424–436. arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0206253
doi.org/10.1086/345345
The primary spectroscopic monitoring study compares H-alpha wind emission with X-ray state changes. The wind-to-accretion interpretation depends on wind velocity and ionization as well as mass loss; its abstract reports that a stronger H-alpha wind need not yield a brighter soft X-ray state.
