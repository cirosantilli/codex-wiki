Detection efficiency (ε)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Radioactive decay Radioactive activity
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The probability that an event of a specified kind produces a registered count in a specified arrangement. For a radiation detector, geometry, intervening material and the detector response can all affect detection efficiency.
