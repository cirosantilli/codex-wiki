Borel-Cantelli first lemma

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Borel-Cantelli lemmas
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
For arbitrary events An​, if ∑n​P(An​)<∞, then P(limsupn​An​)=0. The union bound gives P(limsupAn​)≤∑n≥m​P(An​)→0. No independence is required.
