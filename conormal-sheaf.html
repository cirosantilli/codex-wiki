Conormal sheaf (CW/V​=IW​/IW2​)

 ... Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Kähler differential Conormal exact sequence for Kähler differentials
OurBigBook.com Words: 248 Articles: 3
For a closed immersion, this quasi-coherent sheaf measures the first-order equations of the subvariety. Its local module is I/I2, and its map to the restricted Kähler differential sheaf sends the class of f to df. This is the definition in Stacks Project, Section 29.32.
