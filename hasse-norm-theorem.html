Hasse norm theorem (K×∩NL/K​JL​=NL/K​L×(L/K cyclic))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Class field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For a cyclic extension of number fields, an element of the base field is a global field norm exactly when it is a norm at every finite and infinite completion. The splitting criterion for a cyclic algebra encodes the norm obstruction as a Brauer group class. The Albert-Brauer-Hasse-Noether theorem makes a class that splits at every completion split globally, giving the result. Cyclicity is essential; arbitrary noncyclic extensions can have additional local-global norm obstructions.
