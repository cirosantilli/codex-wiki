 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Lagrangian mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 42 Articles: 1
The action of a path is the time integral of its Lagrangian. The principle of stationary action requires its first variation to vanish for every admissible fixed-endpoint variation.
