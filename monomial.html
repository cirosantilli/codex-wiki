Monomial

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
A monomial is a product of nonnegative integer powers of variables, usually multiplied by a coefficient. In variables x1​,…,xn​ it has the form cx1a1​​⋯xnan​​ with ai​∈Z≥0​.
