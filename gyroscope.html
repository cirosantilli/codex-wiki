Gyroscope

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Momentum Angular momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A rotating instrument whose nearly symmetric rotor provides a direction reference through its angular momentum. In an inertial frame the spin changes according to the applied torque, so negligible torque approximately preserves its direction. An ideal freely falling test gyroscope in general relativity has its spin undergo parallel transport along its timelike geodesic. Comparing that direction with distant reference axes measures Lense-Thirring precession; the direction need not remain fixed in arbitrary rotating coordinates.
