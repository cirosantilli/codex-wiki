Massive Pauli-Lubanski Casimir (W2=−m2s(s+1))

 ... Special relativity Lorentz transformation Lorentz group Poincaré group Poincare algebra Pauli-Lubanski pseudovector
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
For a massive positive-energy unitary irreducible representation of the Poincaré group with signature (+−−−), choose a rest frame. The Pauli-Lubanski pseudovector has zero time component and spatial components mJi​, up to a common sign convention. Its square is therefore −m2J2. The SU(2) little-group representation has spin s and J2=s(s+1), proving the invariant value. The mass and spin label the massive induced representation of the Poincare double cover.
