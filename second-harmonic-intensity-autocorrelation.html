Second-harmonic intensity autocorrelation (C(τ)∝∫I(t)I(t−τ)dt)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Light Optical pulse Intensity autocorrelation
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Overlap delayed replicas in a sufficiently thin phase-matched second-order crystal and collect their cross-generated sum-frequency signal. Its integrated energy gives the displayed autocorrelation under undepleted, broadband-response assumptions. Temporal resolution depends on gate overlap, group-velocity mismatch and spectral acceptance. Duration inference requires a pulse-shape assumption, and arbitrary temporal phase is not recovered.
