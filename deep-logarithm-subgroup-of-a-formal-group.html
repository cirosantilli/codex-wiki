Deep logarithm subgroup of a formal group (m>p−1e​,F(πmOK​)≅(OK​,+))

 ... Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Formal group law Formal logarithm
OurBigBook.com Words: 257 Articles: 2
Let F be a formal group law over the valuation ring of a finite extension K/Qp​, with vK​(π)=1 and e=vK​(p). If m>e/(p−1), its formal logarithm is an isomorphism F(πmOK​)→(πmOK​,+). Indeed, its scaled series is U+πR(U) with R integral and convergent on OK​; solving by contraction produces the formal group exponential. The subgroup has index qm−1 in F(πOK​), where q is the residue-field order.
