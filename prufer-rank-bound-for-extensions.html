Prüfer rank bound for extensions (rkP​(G)≤rkP​(N)+rkP​(G/N))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Prüfer rank
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For a normal subgroup N with Prüfer rank r and quotient of rank s, let H=⟨h1​,…,hk​⟩≤G. Choose at most s elements xj​∈H generating the image of H. Write hi​=wi​(x1​,…,xs​)ni​ with ni​∈N∩H. The finitely generated subgroup ⟨n1​,…,nk​⟩ needs at most r generators, so H needs at most r+s. The full intersection H∩N need not be finitely generated.
