Gravothermal stellar energy generation (ϵgrav​=−Du/Dt−PD(1/ρ)/Dt)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure Stellar energy balance equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
This term accounts for thermal storage and compression or expansion in the stellar energy balance equation. At fixed composition the first law of thermodynamics makes it −TDS/Dt. Kelvin-Helmholtz contraction can supply positive luminosity, while thermal adjustment may locally absorb energy. It is not simply the full change in gravitational potential energy: the stellar virial theorem also requires changes in internal energy.
