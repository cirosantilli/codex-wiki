Kernel for density estimation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Nonparametric statistics Density estimation
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 31
A kernel for density estimation is an integrable function K with unit integral, usually nonnegative and centered, whose rescaling Kh​(u)=h−1K(u/h) averages observations over bandwidth h.
