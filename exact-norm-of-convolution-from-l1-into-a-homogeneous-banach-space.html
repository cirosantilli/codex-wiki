Exact norm of convolution from L1 into a homogeneous Banach space (∥g↦f∗g∥L1→B​=∥f∥B​)

The convolution estimate gives the upper bound. Apply the operator to the Fejér kernel, whose L1 norm is one. The summability kernel on the circle property implies f∗Kn​→f in B, giving the reverse bound. This identifies the operator norm even when no single input attains it.
