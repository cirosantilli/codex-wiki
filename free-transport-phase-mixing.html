Free-transport phase mixing (f​t​(k,ξ)=f​0​(k,ξ+kt))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Kinetic theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
On the unit-period spatial circle, free streaming has solution ft​(x,v)=f0​(x−tv,v). With Fourier phase e−2πi(kx+ξv), its mixed Fourier transform is f​t​(k,ξ)=f​0​(k,ξ+kt). A nonzero spatial mode of the velocity-integrated density therefore samples increasingly large velocity frequencies. The conserved spatial zero mode remains, while extra velocity regularity supplies quantitative decay of the other modes.
