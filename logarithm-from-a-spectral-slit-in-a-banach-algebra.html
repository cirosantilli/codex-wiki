Logarithm from a spectral slit in a Banach algebra (x=exp(Θx​(log)))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra Spectrum of an element Holomorphic functional calculus
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
If a compact spectrum of an element avoids zero and zero belongs to its unbounded complementary component, choose a simple arc from zero to infinity avoiding the Banach algebra spectrum. Its complement is a simply connected neighborhood of the Banach algebra spectrum, admitting a holomorphic logarithm. Apply holomorphic functional calculus to that logarithm and the composition rule for holomorphic functional calculus to the exponential to obtain the displayed identity. An invertible matrix over the complex numbers always satisfies the hypothesis because its finite Banach algebra spectrum cannot separate zero from infinity.
