Kingman's coalescent

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Continuous-time Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 368 Articles: 7
A partition-valued Markov chain in which every unordered pair of current blocks merges at rate one. On n initial singleton blocks, the holding time with j blocks is exponential with rate (2j​). Restrictions to subsets of labels have the same law.
