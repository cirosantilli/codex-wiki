Localization of a ring (S−1R)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 23
For a multiplicatively closed set S, the localization S−1R formally makes every element of S invertible. Elements are represented by fractions r/s.
