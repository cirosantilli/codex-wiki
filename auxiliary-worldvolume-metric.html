Auxiliary worldvolume metric (γμν​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Brane Worldvolume
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 2
An auxiliary worldvolume metric is an independent metric introduced in a brane action, distinct from the induced worldvolume metric. Its algebraic equation can eliminate it. For the Polyakov action of a string, it is determined only up to a Weyl transformation.
