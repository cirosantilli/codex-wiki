Two-body relaxation

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 604 Articles: 9
Two-body relaxation is the cumulative randomization of stellar velocities by many weak gravitational encounters. Its timescale in a system of N comparable stars is of order N/logN crossing times.
