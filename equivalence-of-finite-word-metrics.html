Equivalence of finite word metrics

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Cayley graph Word metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For two finite symmetric choices of generating set of a group S,T, let K=max({1}∪{∣s∣T​:s∈S}) and L=max({1}∪{∣t∣S​:t∈T}). Replacing letters by their fixed words gives ∣g∣T​≤K∣g∣S​ and ∣g∣S​≤L∣g∣T​. Thus the identity is a bilipschitz equivalence between the word metrics. Finiteness is essential for these maxima; an infinite generating set can collapse whole subgroups to diameter one.
