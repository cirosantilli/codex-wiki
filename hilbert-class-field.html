Hilbert class field

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Class field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 718 Articles: 11
The Hilbert class field is the maximal unramified abelian extension of a number field. Its Galois group is canonically the ideal class group.
