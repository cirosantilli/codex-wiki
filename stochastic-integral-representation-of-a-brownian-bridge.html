Stochastic integral representation of a Brownian bridge (Xt​=(1−t)∫0t​(1−s)−1dBs​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 35
For t<1 this stochastic integral solves dXt​=dBt​−Xt​(1−t)−1dt. The Itô product rule also gives Xt​=Bt​−(1−t)∫0t​Bs​(1−s)−2ds. The process is centered Gaussian, with covariance min(s,t)−st, and has a continuous extension X1​=0. Time reversal of a Brownian bridge proves endpoint continuity without assuming it at the start.
