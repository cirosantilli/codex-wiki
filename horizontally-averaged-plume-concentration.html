Horizontally averaged plume concentration (C=c/w)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Scalar transport Passive scalar Horizontally integrated plume concentration
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For a plume of full width w(z), its horizontally averaged concentration is C(z,t)=c(z,t)/w(z), where c is horizontally integrated plume concentration. In a line plume with w=μz, division by z generally changes the location of maximum concentration. A gradient closure for c differs from one for C: w∂z​C=cz​−c/z, so integrated diffusive flux −Kw∂z​C equals −K(cz​−c/z).
