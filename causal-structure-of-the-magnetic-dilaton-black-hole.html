Causal structure of the magnetic dilaton black hole

OurBigBook.com Words: 102
For r−​<r+​, an ingoing chart with v=t+r∗​ has metric −Vdv2+2dvdr+R2dΩ22​. In its future interior, ∇r=∂v​+V∂r​ is future timelike, so every future causal curve decreases r and cannot reach future null infinity. The singularity r=r−​ is spacelike. A Kruskal–Szekeres coordinates extension has the same qualitative Penrose diagram as Schwarzschild spacetime, with two exterior regions, a future black-hole interior and a past white hole interior. The past interior has the same radial interval but is not a black hole region; specifying the radial interval alone does not identify the future component. For r−​>r+​, the physical exterior r>r−​ has V>0 everywhere and outgoing null rays escape from arbitrarily near a timelike naked singularity.
