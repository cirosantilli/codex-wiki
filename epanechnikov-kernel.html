Epanechnikov kernel (KE​(u)=43​(1−u2)1{∣u∣≤1}​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 123 Articles: 1
The Epanechnikov kernel for density estimation is a nonnegative symmetric truncated parabola with integral one, μ2​(KE​)=1/5, and R(KE​)=3/5. It has the smallest optimized asymptotic mean integrated squared error among nonnegative second-order density kernels, up to rescaling. Its canonical kernel has scale 151/5 and support [−151/5,151/5].
