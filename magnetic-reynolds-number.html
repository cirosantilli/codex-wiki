Magnetic Reynolds number (Rm=UL/ηm​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The magnetic Reynolds number compares field advection with resistive magnetic diffusion. With constant electrical conductivity and vacuum permeability, magnetic diffusivity is ηm​=1/(μ0​σ) and Rm=UL/ηm​. The ideal induction approximation applies when this ratio is large on the scales of interest.
