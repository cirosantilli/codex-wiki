Discriminant quintic of a cubic surface conic bundle (degΔ=5)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic surface Cubic surface Smooth cubic surface Conic bundle from a line on a smooth cubic surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
The matrix of the residual plane conic in coordinates (u,v,w) has coefficient degrees (112​112​223​) in the plane parameter (s,t). Every term of its determinant has degree five. The reduced singular fibres of a smooth conic bundle lemma rules out an identically zero determinant and makes each zero simple. Over an algebraically closed field, there are therefore exactly five singular fibres, each a pair of distinct coplanar projective lines. No residual conic contains the original line, because sA+tB≡0 would make A,B proportional and force a common zero. Pairs from different fibres are disjoint.
