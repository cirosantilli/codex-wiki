Holomorphic bundle map

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Vector bundle morphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A holomorphic bundle map is a holomorphic map between total spaces of holomorphic vector bundles that covers a holomorphic base map and is complex-linear on each fibre. Over a fixed base, its matrix in holomorphic local frames has holomorphic entries.
