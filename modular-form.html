Modular form

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 49
A modular form is a holomorphic function on the complex upper half-plane that transforms with a fixed weight under a congruence subgroup and is holomorphic at every cusp.
