Ventilation switching under wind and heating changes

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Natural ventilation Well-mixed ventilation temperature balance Opposing-wind ventilation bistability
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
Increasing wind or reducing heating both reduce the control parameter of opposing-wind ventilation bistability. However, wind moves the reversal threshold θw​=W/GT​, whereas heating does not. A sufficiently abrupt wind increase can place a hot upward-flow state in the cooler state's basin. Reducing positive heating at fixed wind cannot make an upward-flow state cross θw​, because its temperature time derivative there is positive. Slow variation follows a persistent stable branch; a fold destroys the cooler branch when wind is weakened or heat input increased.
