Dirichlet heat kernel on an interval (HL​(x,y,t))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Diffusion equation Heat equation Heat kernel
OurBigBook.com Words: 90 Articles: 1
For the heat equation with zero Dirichlet boundary conditions on (0,L),
HL​(x,y,t)=L2​∑n≥1​e−(nπ/L)2tsin(nπx/L)sin(nπy/L).
(22)
It is also obtained by the method of images, taking the difference between Gaussian sources at y+2jL and −y+2jL. The eigenfunction expansion is useful at long times; the image expansion is useful at short times.
