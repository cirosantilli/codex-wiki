Sharp-k smoothing filter (W(kR)=1k<1/R​)

 ... Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Dark matter halo Spherical-collapse model Press-Schechter formalism
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
A sharp-k filter includes every Fourier mode below a cutoff with unit weight and excludes all modes above it. For a Gaussian random field, increasing the cutoff adds independent Fourier shells, so the smoothed density contrast is a Brownian motion when parameterized by its variance. The real-space window is not a localized spherical top-hat, so its halo mass assignment needs a specified convention.
