Use transfinite induction. The assertion is immediate at stage zero, and at a limit stage it follows by taking the increasing union of the earlier stages.
Assume Lα​∩On=α. Any ordinal γ∈Lα+1​ is a subset of Lα​, since members of the definable power set are such subsets. Every element of γ is an ordinal, so
γ⊆Lα​∩On=α,
(18)
which implies γ≤α. This gives the upper bound on the ordinals appearing at the successor stage.
For the reverse inclusion, the smaller ordinals are already in Lα​ and hence in Lα+1​. The remaining ordinal α itself is the definable subset Lα​∩On. The fact used here is absoluteness of ordinalhood between a transitive set and the universe: ordinalhood has a bounded definition, and bounded formulas are absolute for transitive sets. Thus the formula asserting that a member is an ordinal, interpreted over Lα​, defines exactly α. Therefore α∈Def(Lα​)=Lα+1​.
This proves
Lα​∩On=αfor every ordinal α.​
(19)
In particular each ordinal α is a member of Lα+1​, so On⊆L​.
