For GCH arithmetic at aleph omega, let κ=ℵω​. Use monotonicity of cardinal exponentiation, the law (ab)c=abc, and κ⋅κ=κ for infinite cardinals in ZFC. Since 2≤κ≤2κ,
2κ≤κκ≤(2κ)κ=2κ⋅κ=2κ.
(16)
Thus κκ=2κ, and the generalized continuum hypothesis yields
ℵωℵω​​=ℵω+1​.​
(17)
The lower bound embeds binary-valued functions into κ-valued ones; the upper bound codes each value by a subset and combines the resulting pairs of indices. In particular the equality before applying the hypothesis is a ZFC equality.
