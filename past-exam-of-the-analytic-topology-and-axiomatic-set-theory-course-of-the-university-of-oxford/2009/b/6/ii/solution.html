A sufficient syntactic condition is that ϕ be a sigma-one formula in set theory, written Σ1​: an existential block ∃z1​⋯∃zm​θ with θ bounded, meaning that each of its quantifiers is of the form ∀t∈a or ∃t∈a. A bounded formula is also called Δ0​ and is absolute in both directions between transitive classes.
Prove the latter fact by structural induction. Atomic membership and equality have their actual meanings in both classes, and Boolean connectives preserve equivalence of truth. For a bounded quantifier with bound a∈U1​, transitivity ensures that every element of a belongs to U1​; thus its range is exactly the same in both classes, and induction applies to its matrix. This proves the bounded case. If the smaller class satisfies a Σ1​ formula, take its existential witnesses in U1​. They remain available in U2​, and bounded absoluteness preserves the matrix. Hence Σ1​ formulas are upward absolute. An arbitrary unbounded universal quantifier has no such witness-preservation argument.
