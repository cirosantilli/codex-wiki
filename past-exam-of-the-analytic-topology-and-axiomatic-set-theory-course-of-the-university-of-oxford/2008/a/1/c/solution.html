Since D is a discrete subset in the subspace topology, for each d∈D choose an open set Ud​⊆Z with Ud​∩D={d}. Closedness of D makes Z∖D open. The family
{Z∖D}∪{Ud​:d∈D}
(2)
is therefore an open cover of Z. A countable subcover exists by the Lindelöf property. Each point d can be covered only by its own Ud​ among this family: the complement misses all of D, and every other Ue​ meets D only at e. Hence the countable subcover must include Ud​ for every d∈D. This forces
D is countable.​
(3)
Both closedness and discreteness matter: they provide the open complement and the single-point traces, respectively.
