First prove the suggested density fact. If Z is dense in Y, V is open and y∈V, then every open neighborhood W of y has W∩V nonempty open. It meets Z, so W meets V∩Z. Hence
V⊆V∩ZY.
(15)
Identify X with its dense embedded image in the hausdorff compactification. For x∈X, local compactness supplies an open neighborhood U in X and a compact set K⊆X with x∈U⊆K. Choose an open set V in Y with V∩X=U. The inclusion into Y is continuous, so K is compact in Y and therefore closed, since Y is Hausdorff. The density fact now yields
x∈V⊆V∩XY=UY⊆K⊆X.
(16)
Thus every point of the embedded X has a Y-open neighborhood contained in X. Consequently
h[X] is open in Y.​
(17)
