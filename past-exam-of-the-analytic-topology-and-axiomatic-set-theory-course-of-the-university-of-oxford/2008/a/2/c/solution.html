Let B be a topological basis consisting of clopen sets. For U∈B its characteristic function χU​:X→{0,1} is continuous, since the preimages of both singleton values are open. Define
e:X→{0,1}B,e(x)=(χU​(x))U∈B​.
(11)
The coordinate criterion for product topology makes this map continuous. If x=y, the T0​ property gives an open set containing one and not the other. A topological basis element contained in that open set still distinguishes them, so their corresponding characteristic coordinates differ. Therefore e is injective.
Finally, for each topological basis element U,
U=e−1({z:zU​=1}).
(12)
The right-hand side is the preimage of an open cylinder in the product of discrete two-point topological spaces. These cylinders recover a topological basis for the topology of X, proving continuity of the inverse onto the image. Hence
X embeds homeomorphically into {0,1}B.​
