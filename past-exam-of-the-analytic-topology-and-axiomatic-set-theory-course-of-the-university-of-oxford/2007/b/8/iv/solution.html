Use the constructible universe theorem that L satisfies ZFC and GCH. In particular, internally it has a bijection
b:ω1L​⟶P(ω)L.
(29)
This bijection belongs to L and is also an actual bijection in the ambient universe, because its domain, range and graph are transitive-model sets with the same membership relation.
We must compare the ordinals rather than assume cardinality is absolute. Every ordinal below ω1L​ is countable in L, and its witnessing enumeration belongs to the ambient universe too. Thus every such ordinal is countable externally, proving ω1L​≤ω1​. Consequently the ambient cardinality of the constructible reals is at most ℵ1​.
If every subset of ω were constructible, the displayed bijection would enumerate the full ambient power set using at most ℵ1​ points. This contradicts the assumption 2ℵ0​=ℵ2​. Therefore
∃X⊆ωX∈/L.​
(30)
The argument permits ω1L​ to be strictly smaller than the ambient ω1​, exactly the cardinal-absoluteness issue highlighted in the source.
