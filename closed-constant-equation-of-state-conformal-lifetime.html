Closed constant-equation-of-state conformal lifetime (Δτ=2π/(1+3w))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric Conformal time
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For positive curvature and zero cosmological constant, the single-fluid Friedmann solution has a bang-to-crunch conformal time interval 2π/(1+3w) for w≥0. A radial null geodesic in FLRW spacetime travels that same great-circle comoving distance. Relative to a three-sphere circumference 2π, it is 1/(1+3w) circuits: one for dust and one half for radiation, with the final point reached only in the singular-endpoint limit.
