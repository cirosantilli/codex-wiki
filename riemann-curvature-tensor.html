Curvature sign convention (Rreversed​=−R)

Words: 54
Riemann curvature tensor OurBigBook.com
Definitions of the Riemann curvature tensor can differ by an overall sign, independent of the metric signature. Reversing that definition reverses the Ricci tensor, Ricci scalar and Einstein tensor, as well as the curvature side of the geodesic deviation identity and the Palatini identity. A derivation must choose the contraction and derivative ordering explicitly before combining formulas from different conventions. Changing a metric signature alone is a separate operation.

Double divergence of a contravariant tensor (Tab;ab​=Tab;ba​)

Words: 38
Riemann curvature tensor OurBigBook.com
For a Levi-Civita connection, a contravariant rank-two tensor satisfies Tab;ab​=Tab;ba​ without itself being symmetric. The Ricci identity gives their difference as Rpb​(Tpb−Tbp), which vanishes because the Ricci tensor is symmetric. Zero torsion alone for a general affine connection does not ensure this symmetry.

Pair symmetry from the normal-coordinate curvature formula (Rij,kl​=Rkl,ij​)

Words: 41
Riemann curvature tensor OurBigBook.com
With convention R(X,Y)=∇[X,Y]​−[∇X​,∇Y​], and Rij,kl​=g(R(∂i​,∂j​)∂k​,∂l​), normal coordinates give
Rij,kl​=21​(gjk,il​+gil,jk​−gjl,ik​−gik,jl​)
(1)
at their center. Symmetry of the metric and commutation of second derivatives prove pair exchange. The opposite curvature convention changes the formula's overall sign but preserves all these algebraic symmetries.

Bianchi identities from covariant derivative commutators

Words: 82
Riemann curvature tensor OurBigBook.com
The first Bianchi identity follows by applying cyclic curvature commutators to the gradient of an arbitrary scalar field: its symmetric Hessian makes the third-derivative terms cancel, leaving Rabc​d+Rbca​d+Rcab​d=0. For the second Bianchi identity, write the vector-bundle derivative as Da​=∂a​+Γa​, so [Db​,Dc​]=Rbc​. The operator Jacobi identity gives the cyclic sum of ∂a​Rbc​+[Γa​,Rbc​] equal to zero. Restoring the connection terms on the two curvature-form indices gives precisely ∇a​Rbcd​e+∇b​Rcad​e+∇c​Rabd​e=0: the additional terms cancel because the connection is torsion-free and curvature is antisymmetric in its first pair.

Curvature commutator is a tensor derivation (Δab​(Q⊗S)=(Δab​Q)⊗S+Q⊗Δab​S)

Words: 69
Riemann curvature tensor OurBigBook.com
For a torsion-free linear connection on a manifold, the commutator of two covariant derivatives obeys the tensor-product Leibniz rule: mixed first-derivative terms cancel after antisymmetrizing. It vanishes on scalar fields, so Δab​(fQ)=fΔab​Q; its action is therefore pointwise tensorial. Its vector action defines the Riemann curvature tensor. Applying the commutator to a scalar contraction proves that every covariant tensor index contributes minus the corresponding curvature action, while every contravariant index contributes plus. Torsion-free does not by itself imply metric compatibility.

Three-dimensional curvature from the Ricci tensor (Ric=0 ⟹ Riem=0(n=3))

Words: 24
Riemann curvature tensor OurBigBook.com
In dimension three the Riemann curvature tensor is Rijpq​=gip​Sjq​−giq​Sjp​−gjp​Siq​+gjq​Sip​, where S is the Schouten tensor. Consequently every three-dimensional Ricci-flat Riemannian manifold is a flat Riemannian manifold. The implication fails in higher dimensions.

Kretschmann scalar (K=Rabcd​Rabcd)

Words: 83
Riemann curvature tensor OurBigBook.com
The Kretschmann scalar is the scalar tensor contraction of two copies of the Riemann curvature tensor. In Schwarzschild spacetime, with metric coefficient 1−2M/r and G=c=1, it is 48M2/r6. Divergence of this scalar along a curve approaching a proposed regular point proves that there is no twice continuously differentiable, nondegenerate metric extension through that point: such an extension would have continuous finite curvature components and hence a finite scalar contraction. This gives a coordinate-independent test of a curvature singularity. Boundedness of this single scalar does not prove regularity.

Geodesic deviation (∇U​∇U​J=R(U,J)U)

Words: 692 Articles: 9
Riemann curvature tensor OurBigBook.com
For a family of affinely parametrized geodesics with tangent U and connecting field J, the Jacobi equation in the convention used here is
∇U​∇U​J=R(U,J)U.
(4)
It describes the relative acceleration produced by spacetime curvature.
Here J describes an infinitesimal separation and the covariant derivatives compare it in a frame transported along the reference geodesic. The Riemann curvature tensor thus becomes something measured with neighboring trajectories. The Newtonian version follows by differentiating gravitational acceleration with respect to position. In the exterior of a spherical mass, let ξr​ be radial separation and ξ⊥​ separation in either transverse direction. Newton's law of universal gravitation gives
ξ¨​r​=r32GM​ξr​,ξ¨​⊥​=−r3GM​ξ⊥​.
(5)
The upper particle falls less strongly than the lower one, stretching their radial separation; side-by-side particles accelerate toward converging radial directions, compressing their transverse separation. See Oregon State's derivation of tidal acceleration.
For a spherical Earth model with r=R⊕​=6.371×106m, g=9.81ms−2 and GM=gR⊕2​, a one-metre radial separation gives ξ¨​r​=2g/R⊕​=3.07958×10−6ms−2; a one-metre transverse separation gives −1.53979×10−6ms−2. Initially comoving particles therefore change separation after one second by approximately 21​ξ¨​t2: +1.54 micrometres radially or −0.770 micrometres transversely. This short-time calculation holds the reference radius fixed to leading order. Its beauty is that common falling motion drops out, leaving a measurable stretching pattern that reveals curvature.

Local tidal acceleration scaling (atidal​=O(RL))

Words: 76
Geodesic deviation OurBigBook.com
The geodesic deviation equation makes the relative gravitational acceleration proportional to the separation L and a typical Riemann curvature tensor component R. Thus tidal acceleration vanishes as L→0 at fixed bounded curvature. A uniform electric field can give different accelerations qE/m at the same point, independently of separation. Relative tidal changes accumulated over a fixed long time need not vanish as a fraction of the initial separation; a local spacetime experiment must control its duration as well as its spatial size.

Conserved deviation energy in constant curvature (K=∣DS∣2−κ∣S∣2)

Words: 110 Articles: 1
Geodesic deviation OurBigBook.com
In constant sectional curvature κ, a deviation vector orthogonal to a unit timelike geodesic obeys D2S=κS, where D=∇T​. Therefore K=g(DS,DS)−κg(S,S) is constant, since its derivative is 2g(DS,D2S)−2κg(S,DS)=0. Its squared length f=g(S,S) satisfies f¨​=2K+4κf. In four spacetime dimensions, κ=R/12.

Exponential and oscillatory geodesic deviation (D2S=κS)

Words: 68
Conserved deviation energy in constant curvature OurBigBook.com
In a parallel-propagated orthonormal frame, orthogonal geodesic deviation in constant sectional curvature reduces to three scalar equations S¨i=κSi. For κ>0, S=Aeκ​τ+Be−κ​τ: generic initial data grow exponentially, but the pure decaying mode is a counterexample to universal growth. Initially comoving neighbors have S=S(0)cosh(κ​τ). For κ<0, S=Acos(−κ​τ)+Bsin(−κ​τ), so the deviation is bounded and oscillatory. Simultaneous focusing requires compatible vector initial data and is not automatic for every oscillatory solution.

Orthogonality of proper-time deviation vectors (dτd​g(S,T)=0)

Words: 47
Geodesic deviation OurBigBook.com
For a geodesic variation of unit timelike geodesics, proper time normalization holds across the entire variation: g(T,T)=−1. Metric compatibility and ∇T​S=∇S​T then give dg(S,T)/dτ=21​S(g(T,T))=0. Consequently an initially orthogonal deviation vector stays orthogonal. The common normalization is essential: an arbitrary family of affinely parametrized curves with different tangent norms does not imply this conclusion.

A Jacobi field is the infinitesimal connecting vector between neighboring members of a one-parameter family of geodesics. It satisfies the geodesic-deviation equation along the reference geodesic.

Jacobi fields vanishing at their initial point (J(t)=(dexpp​)tV​(tDt​J(0)))

Words: 62
Jacobi field OurBigBook.com
For γ(t)=expp​(tV), every Jacobi field with J(0)=0 is uniquely of the form
J(t)=(dexpp​)tV​(tB),B=Dt​J(0)∈Tp​M.
(6)
Differentiate the geodesic variation expp​(t(V+sB)) in s to obtain the formula. Uniqueness of the Jacobi equation with prescribed initial value and derivative shows that all such fields are obtained. A nontrivial field vanishing at t=L>0 therefore corresponds to a nonzero vector in the kernel of (dexpp​)LV​, giving the conjugate point criterion.

Jacobi curvature operator (RX​(v)=R(v,X)X)

Words: 49
Jacobi field OurBigBook.com
For the Riemann curvature tensor convention R(X,Y)Z=∇X​∇Y​Z−∇Y​∇X​Z−∇[X,Y]​Z, define RX​(v)=R(v,X)X. Curvature pair interchange makes this operator self-adjoint. If v⊥X, then ⟨RX​v,v⟩=K(v,X)∣v∣2∣X∣2. The Jacobi field equation is Dt2​J+Rγ˙​​J=0. The operator v↦R(X,v)X is −RX​ and is likewise self-adjoint; this sign distinction matters when testing curvature inequalities.

Jacobi fields from conjugation at a central endpoint (JX​(t)=XetA−etAX)

Words: 40
Jacobi field OurBigBook.com
For a Lie group with a bi-invariant Riemannian metric, vary γ(t)=etA by conjugation: F(s,t)=esXetAe−sX. Its Jacobi field is JX​(t)=XetA−etAX. If eA is central, all these fields vanish at both endpoints. Their space has dimension dimg−dimz(A), since the kernel is the centralizer of an element of a Lie algebra.

Endpoint-vanishing normal Jacobi fields have dimension at most n-1 (dim{J⊥γ˙​:J(0)=J(1)=0}≤n−1)

Words: 69
Jacobi field OurBigBook.com
In dimension n≥2, along a nonconstant geodesic, such a Jacobi field is determined injectively by Dt​J(0), which lies in the (n−1)-dimensional normal space. On the round unit sphere the geodesic from a point to its antipode, parametrized with speed π on [0,1], has fields J(t)=sin(πt)E(t) for all parallel normal fields E, attaining the bound. For a constant geodesic the endpoint-vanishing Jacobi field space is zero.

Third covariant derivatives of a scalar

Words: 41
Symmetric Hessian of a scalar field OurBigBook.com
Use the sequential convention φ;lmn​=∇n​∇m​∇l​φ and define R by Xa;mn​−Xa;nm​=Rabmn​Xb. The symmetric Hessian of a scalar field gives symmetry in l,m, but the curvature commutator on a covariant tensor gives φ;l[mn]​=−Rplmn​φ;p​/2. Averaging the three distinct permutations yields φ;(lm)n​=φ;(lmn)​−(Rplmn​+Rpmln​)φ;p​/3. Thus a scalar's third covariant derivative is not generally totally symmetric.

Magnetic part of the Weyl tensor (Bab​=21​ϵacde​Cdebf​ncnf)

Words: 45
Weyl tensor OurBigBook.com
In four spacetime dimensions, relative to a timelike unit normal n, define Bab​=21​ϵacde​Cdebf​ncnf, with the chosen spacetime metric volume tensor. This is the dual tidal part of the Weyl tensor. Normal contractions vanish by curvature and volume-tensor antisymmetry. Its sign depends on the specified orientation and dualization convention.

Electric part of the Weyl tensor (Eab​=Cacbd​ncnd)

Words: 44
Weyl tensor OurBigBook.com
Relative to a timelike unit normal n, the electric part is Eab​=Cacbd​ncnd. It describes the tidal component of the Weyl tensor measured by that observer. Antisymmetry in each curvature pair makes it spatial in both slots; pair symmetry and vanishing traces make it symmetric and trace-free.

Trace-free property of the Weyl tensor

Words: 43
Weyl tensor OurBigBook.com
Every contraction of the Weyl tensor vanishes. Contracting its first and third indices directly cancels the Ricci and scalar-curvature terms; antisymmetry in each pair and symmetry under exchanging the two pairs reduce all other contractions to this one or make them vanish immediately.

Weyl tensor of a conformally flat metric

Every conformally flat metric in dimension at least four has identically zero Weyl tensor. The Weyl tensor is the obstruction to local conformal flatness in dimensions at least four.

Double divergence of the Riemann tensor (Rikmn;ik​=0)

Words: 72
Contracted Bianchi identity OurBigBook.com
For the Levi-Civita connection, the second Bianchi identity gives ∇i​Rikmn​=∇m​Rkn​−∇n​Rkm​. Applying another covariant derivative and the contracted Bianchi identity reduces its divergence to a commuting scalar Hessian plus curvature commutators on the Ricci tensor. The latter are the difference of symmetric tensors: a square of the Ricci tensor and RabRanbm​, which is symmetric in m,n by curvature pair exchange and Ricci symmetry. Both differences vanish. This identity does not require vacuum field equations and is independent of the overall curvature sign convention.

Words: 50

In a spatially flat universe dominated by a positive constant energy density ϵ, the Friedmann equations give H2=8πGϵ/(3c2).
