Overlap formula for the variance of a clique count (VarX/(EX)2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Binomial random graph Clique count in a binomial random graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 1
For the number X of k-cliques in a binomial random graph G(n,p), with 0<p<1 and μ=EX,
μ2VarX​=∑j=2k​(kn​)(jk​)(k−jn−k​)​(p−(2j​)−1).
(6)
Two k-sets overlapping in j vertices share (2j​) edges. Overlaps of zero or one vertex give independent indicators; j=k includes the diagonal terms. The identity is useful for the second moment method.
