Tight-coupling approximation

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Photon-baryon fluid

The tight-coupling approximation expands the photon-baryon Boltzmann equations in powers of k/Γ and H/Γ, where Γ is the Thomson scattering rate.
