Box norm (∥f∥□​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics
OurBigBook.com Words: 597 Articles: 8
For nonempty finite sets X,Y and a function f:X×Y→C on their Cartesian product, using uniform expectations, define
∥f∥□4​=Ex,x′,y,y′​f(x,y)f(x′,y)​f(x,y′)​f(x′,y′).
(4)
This equals Ey,y′​∣Ex​f(x,y)f(x,y′)​∣2. Hence it is nonnegative, and vanishing forces f=0 by taking y=y′. Absolute homogeneity and the box Cauchy-Schwarz inequality establish that it is a norm. For a real-valued function the complex conjugates can be omitted. The bilinear correlation bound for the box norm also holds for complex functions.
