Fano-limited X-ray CCD energy resolution (ΔEFWHM​=22ln2​FwE​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Photodetector Charge-coupled device
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
A photon of energy E fully absorbed in silicon produces on average E/w electron-hole pairs, with variance FE/w rather than necessarily a Poisson variance. Thus σE​=FwE​ and Gaussian FWHM is 22ln2​σE​. Representative silicon values w≃3.65 eV and F≃0.12 give about 156 eV FWHM at 10 keV. read noise in split-event pixels, trapping and gain errors broaden that limit; optical pixel size alone does not specify the energy resolution.[ref]
