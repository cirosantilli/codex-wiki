Primordial helium response to the neutron-proton mass difference (δlogYp​≃−δQ/[Tf​(1+e−Q/Tf​)])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Big Bang nucleosynthesis Primordial helium mass fraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
At fixed cosmological weak freeze-out temperature Tf​ and fixed neutron survival factor, neutron-proton chemical equilibrium gives rf​=e−Q/Tf​ and neutron-limited helium synthesis gives Yp​∝2rf​/(1+rf​). Differentiating yields the displayed sensitivity. A finite change Q↦(1+α)Q gives Yp′​/Yp​=(1+eQ/Tf​)/(1+e(1+α)Q/Tf​). Increasing the positive mass difference reduces helium production. Changes to the weak conversion rates, neutron lifetime or nuclear history must be supplied separately for a full abundance prediction.
