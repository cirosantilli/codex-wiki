Jones polynomial evaluation at one (VL​(1)=(−2)ℓ−1)

 ... Geometry and topology Knot theory Link Link diagram Kauffman bracket Jones polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For a nonempty link with ℓ components, the Jones polynomial skein relation at t=1 makes a crossing change preserve the value. Crossing changes turn the link into an unlink with the same number of components. Repeated Jones polynomial of a split union gives the formula. In particular the Jones polynomial is never the zero polynomial.
