Mathematical model parameter (μ)

 Home Mathematics Mathematical model
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A mathematical model parameter is a quantity held fixed while solving a particular instance of a model, but allowed to vary between instances. Changing a parameter can change equilibria, their stability and the qualitative behavior of trajectories. Parameter constancy does not mean probabilistic independence.
