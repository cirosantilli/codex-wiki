Growth function of a discrete metric space (βX,x0​​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 515 Articles: 9
For a locally finite discrete metric space X with basepoint x0​, its growth function is
βX,x0​​(r)=#{x∈X:d(x,x0​)≤r}.
(30)
Changing the basepoint changes the argument by at most an additive constant. In a vertex-transitive metric space, including every Cayley graph, the growth function is exactly independent of the basepoint.
