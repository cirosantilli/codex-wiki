Congruence class ([a]N​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Modular arithmetic
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
The congruence class of a modulo a positive integer N is {a+Nk:k∈Z}. Two integers have the same class exactly when their difference is divisible by N. Addition and multiplication of representatives descend to well-defined operations on Z/NZ, because replacing a representative by a multiple-of-N translate changes the result by a multiple of N.
