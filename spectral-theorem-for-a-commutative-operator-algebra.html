Spectral theorem for a commutative operator algebra

If A is a commutative C-star algebra of bounded operators on a Hilbert space containing its identity, there is a unique regular projection-valued measure E on the character space ΦA​ such that E(ΦA​)=I and a=∫ΦA​​adE for every a∈A, where a is the Gelfand transform.
