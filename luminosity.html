Luminosity (L)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 3
Luminosity is the total radiative energy emitted by an astronomical source per unit time. It differs from the received radiative flux, which also depends on distance and geometry.
