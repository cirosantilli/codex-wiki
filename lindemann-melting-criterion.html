Lindemann melting criterion (⟨x2⟩​=cL​d)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Phase transition Melting
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
Frederick Lindemann's heuristic associates melting with vibrational displacement reaching a specified fraction of the nearest-neighbor distance d. The displacement convention must be stated: one-coordinate variance and full three-dimensional variance differ by a factor of three. With the high-temperature Einstein mean-square displacement, the displayed convention gives Tm​=cL2​d2mkB​TE2​/ℏ2. Neither cL​ nor the independent-oscillator approximation is universal.
