Minimum-variance two-cue estimate (w1​=σ22​/(σ12​+σ22​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 70
Let unbiased independent estimates X1​,X2​ have variances a,b>0. The variance of wX1​+(1−w)X2​ is w2a+(1−w)2b. Its derivative is 2w(a+b)−2b and its second derivative is 2(a+b)>0, proving the unique optimum w=b/(a+b) and variance ab/(a+b). Independent Gaussian likelihoods give the same estimate by differentiating (x1​−x)2/a+(x2​−x)2/b. With covariance c, the variance becomes w2a+(1−w)2b+2w(1−w)c, and the unconstrained optimum is (b−c)/(a+b−2c) when the denominator is positive; it need not lie between zero and one.
