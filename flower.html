Flower

 Home Biology Organism Eukaryote Plant
OurBigBook.com Words: 514 Articles: 18
A flower is an angiosperm's reproductive structure. Stamens produce pollen; carpels enclose ovules. Petals and sepals surround these organs in a complete flower, but some flowers lack one or more groups, including either reproductive group.[ref]
Read the drawing from outside inward, then trace two routes: where pollen is released, and where an ovule is enclosed. This reveals why catching pollen and forming a seed require different structures. A detached petal cannot substitute for a stigma simply because both belong to the same flower.
