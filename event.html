Event

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability space
OurBigBook.com Words: 214 Articles: 3
An event is a measurable set in a probability space: a question with a yes-or-no answer for each outcome. On Ω={1,…,6}2, the event that the sum is at least nine consists of ten ordered pairs. Its probability is therefore 10/36=5/18 under independent uniform dice. Saying that an event occurred means the actual outcome belongs to that set.
