Polymer overlap concentration (c∗∼M/(NA​Vcoil​))

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry) Polymer solution
OurBigBook.com Words: 241 Articles: 2
The overlap concentration is the concentration at which distinct dilute polymer coils first substantially interpenetrate. It corresponds to roughly one chain per coil volume. If a chain contains N repeat units, has molar mass M and occupies a coil volume Vcoil​, then its chain number density is approximately 1/Vcoil​, its repeat-unit number density is N/Vcoil​, and its mass concentration is M/(NA​Vcoil​). A scaling convention uses Vcoil​∼R3; a commonly chosen geometric convention uses Vcoil​=4πRg3​/3. These give different order-one prefactors because a coil has no sharp surface. For an ideal chain, R∼bN1/2 and a site-volume estimate v0​∼b3 gives overlap volume fraction ϕ∗∼N−1/2. A good solvent chain with the Flory approximation R∼bN3/5 instead gives ϕ∗∼N−4/5.
