Angular fringe spacing of a transparent film

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Interference (wave propagation) Thin-film interference
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For a film of refractive index n and thickness d in air, its round-trip propagation phase is δ=(4πd/λ0​)n2−sin2θi​​. Two neighbouring same-type interference fringes differ by 2π. At small angular spacing in radians,
Δθi​≃2dsinθi​cosθi​λ0​n2−sin2θi​​​=2dsinθi​cosθi​λ0​ncosθ​.
(34)
The relation assumes slowly varying interface reflection coefficients and no rapidly varying reflection phase. At normal incidence the first derivative vanishes, so a local linear angular-spacing formula must be replaced by a quadratic expansion.
