Admissible sequence of Steenrod squares (ij​≥2ij+1​)

An index sequence obeying the displayed inequalities represents an admissible product Sqi1​⋯Sqir​. Its degree increment is the sum of the indices. The Adem relations rewrite every square monomial in terms of admissible ones, whose Steenrod excess controls universal instability.
