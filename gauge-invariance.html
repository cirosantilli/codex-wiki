Gauge invariance

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field
OurBigBook.com Words: 200 Articles: 4
Gauge invariance is invariance under local changes of gauge representative. Gauge-invariant observables depend only on the physical gauge orbit.
A gauge theory uses this redundancy to describe the same physical state by different field representatives.
