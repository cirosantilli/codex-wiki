Nuisance tangent space (N)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Semiparametric statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 272 Articles: 5
The nuisance tangent space is the closed linear span of score functions from statistical paths that vary the nuisance parameter while fixing the target statistical parameter. It is a closed subspace of a Hilbert space inside L02​(P). Removing its component from a parametric score function leaves the information that nuisance variation cannot imitate.
