A branching rule decomposes a representation into irreducible representations after restriction from a Lie group or Lie algebra to a subgroup or subalgebra.
A branching rule gives the irreducible representation decomposition after restricting a group representation to a subgroup, or a Lie algebra representation to a Lie subalgebra. For the standard block embedding SU(5)⊂SU(6), 6↓=5⊕1 and Λ26↓=10⊕5.
