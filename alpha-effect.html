Alpha effect (E=αB)

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Dynamo action Mean-field dynamo
OurBigBook.com Words: 456 Articles: 7
The alpha effect is the part of a mean-field electromotive force linear in the mean magnetic field itself, E=αB+⋯. The alpha tensor can be anisotropic; it need not be a scalar multiple of the identity. Its curl can couple transverse mean-field components and produce an alpha-squared dynamo.
