Torque-free rotation of a spherical squirmer (Ω)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Microswimmer Squirmer
OurBigBook.com Words: 136 Articles: 1
For a sphere of radius a with prescribed surface slip u′ and zero external torque, the Lorentz reciprocal theorem for Stokes flow gives
Ω=−8πa33​∫S​n×u′dS,
(40)
where n points from the sphere into the fluid. Use an auxiliary rotating sphere in Stokes flow with arbitrary angular velocity Ω and traction −3μΩ×n. The actual torque-free cross-work vanishes. Substituting the boundary velocity Ω×an+u′ into the other cross-work leaves −8πμa3Ω⋅Ω−3μΩ⋅∫n×u′=0. A possible translation contributes zero because the auxiliary net force is zero.
