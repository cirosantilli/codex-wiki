von Klitzing-Dorda-Pepper quantum Hall experiment

 ... Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force Hall effect Quantum Hall effect Integer quantum Hall effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 221 Articles: 2
Klaus von Klitzing, G. Dorda and M. Pepper reported a silicon MOSFET measurement in 1980. Its gate controlled an semiconductor inversion layer; transverse contacts measured the Hall voltage and separate longitudinal contacts measured dissipation. One sweep held B=18T, T=1.5K and I=1μA fixed while varying gate voltage. Hall plateaus accompanied small longitudinal voltages. Device-geometry comparisons and high-input-impedance voltage measurements helped test whether contact placement or electrical loading produced the result.[ref]
This original functional diagram separates transverse and longitudinal readouts; it does not reproduce the historical device's dimensions. The gate inset explains how a field-effect transistor provides an adjustable two-dimensional electron gas. A longitudinal voltage close to zero is a useful check because accidental longitudinal separation between Hall contacts can otherwise contaminate the transverse measurement.
