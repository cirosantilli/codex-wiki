Weinberg operator

OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 1
The Weinberg operator is the dimension-five Standard Model operator (LH)(LH)/Λ. After the Higgs field condenses, it gives a left-handed neutrino Majorana mass of order v2/Λ.
