HNN extension

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Bass-Serre theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 987 Articles: 16
Given isomorphic subgroups C,D≤A and an isomorphism ϕ:C→D, the HNN extension is
⟨A,t∣tct−1=ϕ(c) for every c∈C⟩.
(18)
Its stable letter t records the loop edge in the associated graph of groups.
