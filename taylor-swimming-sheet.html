Taylor swimming sheet

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Microswimmer
OurBigBook.com Words: 642 Articles: 13
The Taylor swimming sheet is an infinite two-dimensional microswimmer whose prescribed traveling deformation drives Stokes flow. A transverse wave ys​=ϵsin(x−t) of small amplitude swims opposite to its direction of propagation, with speed U=ϵ2/2+O(ϵ4) when lengths are scaled by inverse wavenumber and velocities by wave speed. Its biharmonic stream function for planar Stokes flow has first-order part ψ1​=(1+y)e−ysin(x−t).
