Liouville volume of a surface geodesic flow (μ=α∧θ∧ω)

 ... Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Sphere bundle Unit tangent bundle Canonical coframe of a surface unit tangent bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
The positive Liouville volume for the base-orientation/positive-fibre orientation is μ=π∗Ωa​∧ω. Its fibre integral is 2πΩa​, so its total volume is 2πArea(M). With the stated coframe convention, α∧dα=−μ; the signed contact volume and the positive measure should not be confused. The fields X,H,V preserve this volume by their structure equations.
