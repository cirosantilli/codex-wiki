Thin-disk potential-density inversion (Σ=−kΦ/(2πG))

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Thin disk Razor-thin disk approximation Razor-thin disk Poisson kernel
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For an isolated razor-thin disk, define the two-dimensional Fourier transform by g​(k)=∫e−ik⋅xg(x)d2x. At k>0, the razor-thin disk Poisson kernel gives the displayed inversion. Since the transform of 1/∣x∣ is 2π/k, inversion also gives Σ=(4π2G)−1(1/∣x∣)∗Δ2​Φ. The forward relation is Φ=−G(1/∣x∣)∗Σ. An additive potential constant is irrelevant; a uniform infinite sheet and scale-free divergent potentials require separate zero-mode or potential-difference treatment rather than an arbitrary decaying boundary condition.
