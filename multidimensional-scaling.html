Multidimensional scaling

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical learning Unsupervised learning
OurBigBook.com Words: 190 Articles: 2
Construct low-dimensional coordinates representing a dissimilarity matrix. Different methods preserve different features: classical multidimensional scaling approximates a centered Gram matrix, while other methods minimize discrepancies between fitted and supplied distances or preserve distance ordering. The method and dimension determine what geometric claims the resulting display supports.
