Superfluid

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Superfluidity
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 20
A superfluid exhibits superfluidity, with phase stiffness and a phase-gradient current. In an appropriate ordered or quasi-ordered regime it supports low-speed flow without viscous dissipation, and its long-wavelength Goldstone boson has a sound-like dispersion. True thermodynamic phase selection and Bose-Einstein condensation need separate infrared conditions.
