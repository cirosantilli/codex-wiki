Clique removal lemma

 ... Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Probabilistic combinatorics Edge density of a bipartite graph Regular pair of vertex sets Szemerédi regularity lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
For fixed r≥2 and every ε>0, some δ>0 has the following property for all sufficiently large n: a graph with fewer than δnr copies of Kr​ can be made Kr​-free by deleting at most εn2 edges. A Szemerédi regularity lemma partition and the regular clique counting lemma prove this. It converts sparse counts of a forbidden clique into a small edit distance from clique-freeness.
