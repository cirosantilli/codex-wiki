Use the unnormalized Choi matrix J(Φ)=(Φ⊗id)(∣Ψ⟩⟨Ψ∣), which has trace d for a channel. A positive such matrix has a spectral decomposition with vectors vk​=λk​​uk​. In paired bases define the Kraus operator Ak​ by (Ak​)ij​=(vk​)ij​, so vk​=(Ak​⊗I)∑j​∣j⟩∣j⟩. Partial contraction with a conjugate index vector gives Ak​∣ϕ⟩, hence Φ(∣ϕ⟩⟨ϕ∣)=∑k​Ak​∣ϕ⟩⟨ϕ∣Ak†​. Rank-one projectors span the operator space, proving the Kraus representation on all inputs. Trace preservation is equivalent to ∑k​Ak†​Ak​=I. This construction needs at most rankJ nonzero Kraus operators.
