For spatial scalar convention gij​=a2[(1−2Ψ)δij​−2E,ij​] and g0i​=a2B,i​, a passive gauge displacement gives E=E+λ and B=B−λ˙+Nˉ2ξ0/a2. Moving from synchronous gauge to Newtonian gauge therefore requires λ=−ES​ and ξ0=−a2E˙S​/Nˉ2. A scalar density transforms by δρ​=δρ−ρˉ​˙​ξ0, giving the displayed density contrast relation. Keeping the background lapse function explicit prevents cosmic-time and conformal-time factors from being confused.
