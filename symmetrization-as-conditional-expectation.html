Symmetrization as conditional expectation (E[Z∣I]=∣Γ∣1​∑g∈Γ​Z∘Tg​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Conditional expectation
OurBigBook.com Words: 175 Articles: 1
If a finite group Γ acts by measure-preserving maps on a probability space, let I be its invariant sigma-algebra. For an integrable random variable Z, the displayed group average is I-measurable. For A∈I, changing variables under each group element gives ∫A​Z∘Tg​dP=∫A​ZdP. The average therefore satisfies the defining integral identity for conditional expectation. For permutations of N exchangeable coordinates, averaging a product attached to d distinct indices yields the sum over all ordered distinct d-tuples divided by the falling factorial (N)d​=N!/(N−d)!.
