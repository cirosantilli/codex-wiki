Ringel form (⟨n,m⟩Q​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Quiver Representation of a quiver
OurBigBook.com Words: 199 Articles: 4
This bilinear form is ⟨n,m⟩Q​=∑i​ni​mi​−∑ρ:i→j​ni​mj​. The standard projective resolution of a quiver representation gives its value as dimHomQ​(X,Y)−dimExtQ1​(X,Y) for the dimension vectors of X,Y. Its diagonal is the Tits form of a quiver.
