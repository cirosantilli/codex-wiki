Largest-eigenvalue interlacing for a principal submatrix (λ1​≥μ1​≥λ2​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Eigenvalue interlacing
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
Let a real symmetric matrix M have ordered eigenvalues λ1​≥λ2​≥⋯≥λn​, with n≥2. If A is obtained by deleting one matching row and column, its largest eigenvalue μ1​ satisfies
λ1​≥μ1​≥λ2​.
(15)
The Rayleigh quotient of A is that of M restricted to vectors whose deleted coordinate is zero. This gives the upper bound. Some nonzero linear combination of the top two orthonormal eigenvectors has that coordinate zero; normalizing it gives a quotient at least λ2​. The argument does not require distinct eigenvalues.
