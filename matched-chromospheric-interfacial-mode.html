Matched chromospheric interfacial mode (ε−1≃2k2Ia​Ib​)

For a high-sound-speed, low-density atmosphere over a denser stellar envelope, set ω2=gk(1−ε) in the plane-parallel Cowling displacement-pressure equations. Define Ia​=∫−∞z0​​e2k(z−z0​)ρ−1dz and Ib​=∫z0​∞​e−2k(z−z0​)ρdz. The lower leading solution has Π/ξ=−2εgkIb​ at the match. Above, write ρ=ερ​, Π=εψ, ξ=μψ. The upward-decaying solution satisfies μ′+2kμ=−k/(gρ​), so μ(z0​)=−(k/g)εIa​. Writing f=Π/(εξ) below, continuity of displacement and Lagrangian pressure requires μ(z0​)f(z0​)=1, giving the displayed condition. The integrals must converge and the upper displacement must actually decay. For two constant-density incompressible layers it gives ε≃2ρa​/ρb​, the expansion of the exact interfacial gravity-wave dispersion relation.
