Sullivan minimal model ((ΛV,d))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Rational homotopy theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 646 Articles: 9
A free graded-commutative rational differential graded algebra with decomposable differentials and a quasi-isomorphism to the rational polynomial forms of a space. For simply connected finite-type spaces, Vi is dual to πi​(X)⊗Q. A proposed finite model must have its cohomology and representing quasi-isomorphism checked; matching a list of relations alone is insufficient.
