Multiplicative correction for summable adapted drift (Zn​=Xn​/∏j<n​(1+Yj​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 69
Suppose Xn​≥0 is an integrable adapted process, Yn​≥0 is finite and adapted, and E[Xn+1​∣Fn​]≤(1+Yn​)Xn​. The displayed correction is a nonnegative supermartingale: its next denominator is measurable at time n, and its reciprocal is at most one. If ∑n​Yn​<∞ almost surely, the denominators converge to a finite positive limit since log(1+y)≤y. The almost sure supermartingale convergence theorem then gives finite almost sure convergence of Xn​. No integrability of the infinite product or of ∑n​Yn​ is needed.
