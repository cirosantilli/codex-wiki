Genetic recombination

 Home Biology Genetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 172 Articles: 3
Genetic recombination produces a chromosome whose inherited segments come from different parental homologues. In a two-locus meiosis, the recombination fraction is the probability that the transmitted combination is recombinant relative to the parental phase.
