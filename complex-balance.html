Complex balance (∑y′→y​ky′→y​cy′=∑y→y′​ky→y′​cy)

OurBigBook.com Words: 56
At a positive concentration vector c, the total incoming deterministic reaction flux equals the total outgoing flux at every reaction complex. Here cy=∏i​ciyi​​ is the law of mass action monomial. This is stronger than balancing net production of each chemical species, but does not require pairwise detailed balance between reverse chemical reactions. A directed cycle can have nonzero circulation while satisfying complex balance.
