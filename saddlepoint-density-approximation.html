Saddlepoint density approximation (f​Sn​​(nx)=2πnK′′(t)​en[K(t)−tx]​)

For independent identically distributed continuous random variables, let K(t)=logEetY be finite near zero. At a mean value x reached by K′(t)=x in the interior of that domain, the displayed leading approximation describes the density of the sum Sn​. Exponential tilting by t puts the sum at its tilted mean; a local normal approximation gives the denominator, and undoing the tilt gives the exponential factor. Under suitable smooth nonlattice regularity, at fixed interior x the exact density is the displayed quantity times 1+O(n−1). This statistical approximation is an application of the saddle-point approximation to a transform inversion integral. It requires more than the existence of a few ordinary moments.
