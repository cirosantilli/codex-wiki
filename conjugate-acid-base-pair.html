Conjugate acid-base pair

 Home Chemistry Acid-base chemistry Base (chemistry) Conjugate base
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
A conjugate acid-base pair consists of two chemical species related by transfer of one proton. The conjugate acid has one more proton and one more positive unit of electric charge than the conjugate base.
