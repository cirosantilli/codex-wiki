Rarefied gas flow approximations

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Kinetic theory Knudsen number
OurBigBook.com Words: 287 Articles: 2
For Kn≪1, frequent gas–gas collisions can establish a local Maxwellian before macroscopic conditions change appreciably. Under this additional near-equilibrium assumption, the hydrodynamic limit of the Boltzmann equation motivates a continuum approximation with Navier-Stokes equations and heat conduction. A no-slip boundary condition is a leading wall approximation, not a statement about individual molecular velocities.
With a small but finite Knudsen number, a useful slip approximation retains continuum bulk equations and corrects wall velocity and temperature. A Navier slip boundary condition can represent the leading tangential velocity correction, but its slip length must come from the relevant gas–wall interaction model. A nearby Knudsen layer still requires kinetic treatment if its detailed structure is wanted. Slip corrections are perturbations; extending them into order-one Knudsen numbers has no general accuracy guarantee. At order-one Knudsen number, wall and intermolecular collisions both matter; this transitional situation calls for a kinetic theory model. At Kn≫1, free molecular flow becomes a useful limiting approximation. These are scale comparisons, with accuracy depending on geometry, flow conditions and the observable, rather than universal sharp numerical boundaries. [ref]
