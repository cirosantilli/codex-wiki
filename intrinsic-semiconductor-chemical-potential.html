Intrinsic-semiconductor chemical potential (μi​=(Ec​+Ev​)/2+(kB​T/2)ln(Nv​/Nc​))

For nondegenerate conduction bands and valence bands, charge neutrality gives Nc​e−(Ec​−μi​)/(kB​T)=Nv​e−(μi​−Ev​)/(kB​T). Taking logarithms yields
μi​=2Ec​+Ev​​+2kB​T​lnNc​Nv​​.
(3)
For single spin-degenerate parabolic extrema this becomes (Ec​+Ev​)/2+(3kB​T/4)ln(mh∗​/me∗​). Valley multiplicities or additional hole bands belong in Nc​,Nv​ and change the logarithm. The intrinsic chemical potential lies exactly at midgap only when the two effective density of states values agree.
