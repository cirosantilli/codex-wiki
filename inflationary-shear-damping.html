Inflationary shear damping (Kij​∝aloc−3​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmological perturbation theory Nonlinear cosmological perturbation theory Long-wavelength approximation in cosmology
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
The traceless mixed extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface can have leading solution Kij​=Cij​(x)aloc−3​. Cosmic inflation suppresses this anisotropic expansion. An anisotropic spatial shape can nevertheless freeze to a nonzero constant, so shear damping does not prove the absence of tensor cosmological perturbations.
