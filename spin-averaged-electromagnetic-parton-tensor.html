Spin-averaged electromagnetic parton tensor (Sμν=Q2Tμν+4kμkν)

For an on-shell massless quark with incoming momentum k and outgoing momentum k+q, average the initial spin and sum the final spin. On shell 2k⋅q=Q2=−q2, and defining Tμν=−gμν+qμqν/q2 and k=k−(k⋅q/q2)q yields the displayed transverse decomposition.
