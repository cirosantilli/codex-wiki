Dirichlet probability generating function (qX​(z)=E[X−z])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Probability generating function
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For a positive integer-valued random variable of finite support, this transform is a finite Dirichlet series. Its values encode ordinary moments, qX​(−r)=E[Xr], and its derivatives at zero encode logarithmic moments: (−1)rqX(r)​(0)=E[(logX)r]. Positive support is essential to these definitions; the transform is distinct from the ordinary probability generating function E[sX].
