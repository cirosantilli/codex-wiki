Exact differential form (α=dβ)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form Exterior derivative Closed and exact differential forms Closed differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 746 Articles: 15
A differential form is exact when it is the exterior derivative of a form of one lower degree. Since d2=0, every exact form is closed.
