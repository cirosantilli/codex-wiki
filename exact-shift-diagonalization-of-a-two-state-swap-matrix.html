Exact-shift diagonalization of a two-state swap matrix (μ=±1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis QR algorithm
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For A=(01​10​), one shifted QR algorithm step with real QR shift μ gives, with a consistent choice of factor signs,
A1​=1+μ21​(−2μμ2−1​μ2−12μ​).
(140)
To verify this, factor A−μI=QR with Q=(1+μ2)−1/2(−μ1​−1−μ​). Its columns are orthonormal, and R=QT(A−μI) is upper triangular. Then A1​=QTAQ. Thus A1​ is diagonal exactly when μ=±1, the two eigenvalues. The bottom-right-entry shift is zero and leaves the matrix unchanged under the positive-diagonal factor convention. This illustrates why a poorly chosen QR shift can stall even a two-by-two symmetric eigenvalue computation.
