Orbit-averaged drag work

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Kepler orbit Specific orbital energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A weak perturbing relative acceleration f changes specific orbital energy at rate ε˙=v⋅f. Since ε=−μ/(2a) for a Kepler orbit,
a˙=μ2a2​v⋅f.
(53)
For a circular Kepler orbit under f=−κv+g with constant g, the constant-force term averages to zero over an unperturbed orbit. Thus ⟨a˙⟩=−2κa. This is a leading-order orbital average; a strong constant force can distort the orbit instead.
