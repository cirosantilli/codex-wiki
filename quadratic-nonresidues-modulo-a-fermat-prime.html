Quadratic nonresidues modulo a Fermat prime

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Prime number Fermat prime
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
For a Fermat prime p=22k+1, the multiplicative group modulo p has order 22k. Its φ(p−1)=(p−1)/2 primitive roots are all quadratic nonresidues; since there are also (p−1)/2 nonresidues, the two sets coincide.
