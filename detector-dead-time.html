Detector dead time

 Home Physics Particle detection Particle detector
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 1
The interval during which a detector or readout cannot register another event while recovering from a response. A detector trigger may be fast while the camera, chamber or data acquisition is still busy.
In a simple nonextending model, every accepted event starts a fixed busy interval τd​; arrivals during it are lost and do not extend it. With Poisson process input rate R, the average cycle lasts τd​+1/R, giving observed rate
Robs​=1+Rτd​R​.
(26)
This is a specified mathematical model, not a universal correction. An instrument whose busy interval is extended by further arrivals behaves differently. Correcting the total rate also does not remove the multiplicity bias from a detector trigger.
