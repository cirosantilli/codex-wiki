Faraday's comparison of common and voltaic electricity

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrostatics Frictional electricity
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
In 1833 Michael Faraday compared electrical effects from different sources and then their quantitative delivery. His galvanometer test discharged a machine-charged jar battery through a wet thread and the detector, connecting frictionally produced charge to magnetic action.[ref]
