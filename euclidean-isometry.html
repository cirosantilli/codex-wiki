Euclidean isometry

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Isometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 497 Articles: 8
A Euclidean isometry has the form x↦a+Rx, where R is orthogonal. It preserves inner products of displacement vectors, distances, angles, and arc length.
