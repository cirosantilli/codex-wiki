Normal sheaf (NZ/X​)

 ... Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Kähler differential Conormal exact sequence for Kähler differentials Conormal sheaf
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 1
For a closed immersion i:Z↪X with ideal sheaf I, the normal sheaf is NZ/X​=HomOZ​​(I/I2,OZ​). It is the dual of the conormal sheaf. For a regular embedding it is a locally free sheaf; in the smooth case it agrees with the normal bundle. Without regularity it need not be locally free, but its global sections still classify first-order embedded deformations.
