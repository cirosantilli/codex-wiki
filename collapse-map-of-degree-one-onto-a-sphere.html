Collapse map of degree one onto a sphere (Mn⟶M/(M∖intDn)≅Sn)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Degree of a continuous mapping Degree of a map between oriented manifolds
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
Choose an oriented embedded closed disc in a closed oriented positive-dimensional manifold and collapse its complement to a point. A target point away from the collapsed value has one preimage with local degree +1, proving global degree one by the degree as a sum of local degrees.
