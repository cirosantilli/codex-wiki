Smith normal form

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 1
For every matrix A over a principal ideal domain, there are invertible matrices U,V such that
UAV=diag(d1​,…,dr​,0,…,0),d1​∣d2​∣⋯∣dr​.
(30)
The nonzero diagonal entries are unique up to multiplication by units. Over the integers, U and V are unimodular and the di​ may be chosen positive; their products d1​⋯dk​ are the greatest common divisors of the k-rowed minors.
