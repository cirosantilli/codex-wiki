Sampling rate (fs​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Wave speed Acoustic time-of-flight speed measurement
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The sampling rate is the number of recorded samples per unit time. A sample-index difference N represents a delay N/fs​ when the acquisition clock is correctly calibrated.
