Exponential map of a matrix Lie group (exp:g→G)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Matrix Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
For a Matrix Lie group G with Lie algebra g, its exponential map is the restriction of the matrix exponential, X↦eX. It maps g into G and is a local diffeomorphism at zero, but it need not be surjective globally.
