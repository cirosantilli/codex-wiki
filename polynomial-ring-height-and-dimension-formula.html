Polynomial-ring height and dimension formula (htP+dim(k[x1​,…,xn​]/P)=n)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Krull dimension Height of an ideal Height of a prime ideal
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For a prime ideal P of a polynomial ring over a field, the quotient's Krull dimension is the transcendence degree of its fraction field, and its height is n minus that degree. This dimension theorem identifies algebraic codimension with the length of prime chains. It must not be applied indiscriminately to every Noetherian ring without its polynomial-ring hypotheses.
