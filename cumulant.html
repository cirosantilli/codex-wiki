Cumulant (κn​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Moment-generating function Cumulant-generating function
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The nth cumulant is the coefficient of tn/n! in the cumulant-generating function. The first two cumulants are the expected value and variance; the third is the third central moment.
