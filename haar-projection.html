Haar projection (ΠVJ​​f=∑k​⟨f,ϕJ,k​⟩ϕJ,k​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Wavelet Orthonormal wavelet Haar wavelet
OurBigBook.com Words: 272 Articles: 4
The Haar projection at level J is the orthogonal projection onto the functions constant on level-J dyadic intervals. It replaces a function by its mean on each cell. It can be expressed using level-J Haar scaling functions, or using the constant function and Haar wavelets at levels below J.
