Permutation matrix (P)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 62 Articles: 1
A permutation matrix has exactly one entry equal to one in each row and column and zeros elsewhere. Left or right multiplication permutes coordinates, and P−1=PT.
