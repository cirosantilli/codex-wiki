Schoenfeld function (sj​(β)=zij​​−zˉ(β,tj​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
At a distinct event time, the Schoenfeld function is the event subject's covariate minus the hazard-weighted covariate mean over the risk set, as a function of a trial coefficient β. Its evaluation at the fitted coefficient is a Schoenfeld residual. The sum of these functions is the Cox partial likelihood score function, and each is conditionally centered at zero at the true proportional-hazards coefficient.
