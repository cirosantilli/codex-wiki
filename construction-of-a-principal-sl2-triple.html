Construction of a principal sl2 triple (e=∑i​ei​,h=2ρ∨,f=∑i​ci​fi​)

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Principal sl2 subalgebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
Choose simple root vectors with [ei​,fi​]=hi​=αi∨​, and let ρ∨ be the half-sum of positive coroots. It satisfies αi​(ρ∨)=1. Expand h=2ρ∨=∑i​ci​hi​. For i=j, [ei​,fj​]=0, since the difference of distinct simple roots is not a root of a root system. Thus [e,f]=h, while [h,e]=2e and [h,f]=−2f. These identities construct an embedded sl2 Lie algebra in any nonzero complex semisimple Lie algebra. Its raising vector is the principal nilpotent element with a nonzero component in each simple-root space.
