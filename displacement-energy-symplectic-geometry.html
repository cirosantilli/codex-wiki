Displacement energy (symplectic geometry, e(A)=infϕ(A)∩A=∅​dH​(id,ϕ))

The displacement energy of a subset of a symplectic manifold is the infimum of the energy norms of compactly supported Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms moving it off itself. The infimum is +∞ if no such map exists. One must use the same chosen norm convention in both this definition and any energy lower bound. A compact set can have zero displacement energy even when every nonidentity map has positive norm.
