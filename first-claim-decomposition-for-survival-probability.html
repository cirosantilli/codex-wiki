First-claim decomposition for survival probability

In a classical risk model, before the first claim at time t, available capital is u+ct. A claim of size x≤u+ct leaves future survival probability φ(u+ct−x) by the Markov property. Integrating over the independent first-arrival exponential distribution and claim density gives φ(u)=∫0∞​λe−λt∫0u+ct​φ(u+ct−x)f(x)dxdt.
