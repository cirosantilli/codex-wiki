Excess of an admissible Steenrod sequence (e(I)=i1​−i2​−⋯−ir​)

 ... Algebraic topology Cohomology Cohomology operation Stable cohomology operation Steenrod algebra Admissible sequence of Steenrod squares
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The excess measures the minimum degree on which an admissible Steenrod monomial can be nonzero. Strict excess below n picks out polynomial generators in the mod-two cohomology of K(Z/2,n); equality can correspond to a square, rather than a new generator.
