Stress-driven stagnation-point boundary layer (f′′′+ff′′+1−(f′)2=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Boundary layer Boundary-layer equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 176 Articles: 1
For a fluid occupying z<0, match an outer stagnation point flow u=x,w=−z to a flat impermeable interface with dimensionless shear stress βx. With boundary layer thickness δ=Re−1/2, inner variable ζ=z/δ and γ=δβ, the leading boundary-layer equation admits u=xf′(ζ) and w=−δf(ζ). Incompressibility is automatic, and tangential momentum conservation gives the displayed ordinary differential equation, with f(0)=0, f′′(0)=γ and f′→1 at ζ→−∞. The sign of this far-field endpoint is fixed by the fluid domain. This is a prescribed-traction interface; it does not impose a no-slip boundary condition.
