Geodesic circle (Sr​(p)={expp​(rv):∣v∣=1})

OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 1
For r smaller than the normal-coordinate radius, the geodesic circle on a Riemannian surface consists of points at distance r from p. The exponential map restricted to the radius-r circle in Tp​S gives its smooth embedded parameterization. Outside the injectivity range a distance sphere need not be smooth.
