Dual electromagnetic field tensor (Fμν)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The dual electromagnetic field tensor is obtained by applying the Hodge star operator to the electromagnetic field tensor. For metric signature (+−−−) and ϵ0123=+1, define Fμν=21​ϵμνρσFρσ​. With F0i=−Ei​/c and Fij=−ϵijk​Bk​, it has components F0i=−Bi​ and Fij=ϵijk​Ek​/c. The homogeneous Maxwell equations become ∂μ​Fμν=0.
