Beam divergence (θ)

Beam divergence describes the angular spread of a beam away from its optical axis; specify the radius convention and whether the quoted angle is a half-angle or full angle. For the fundamental Gaussian beam in vacuum, the far-field 1/e2 light intensity radius obeys
w(z)∼θ∣z∣,θ=limz→∞​zw(z)​=zR​w0​​=πw0​λ​.
(59)
Here θ is the small-angle half-angle in radians under the paraxial approximation; the full angle is 2θ. The geometric asymptote has angle arctan(w0​/zR​), approximated by w0​/zR​ in this regime. Other beam profiles need their own beam divergence definitions and propagation laws.
