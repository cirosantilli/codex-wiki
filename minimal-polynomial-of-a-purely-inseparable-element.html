Minimal polynomial of a purely inseparable element

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Purely inseparable field extension Purely inseparable algebraic element
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
An element is purely inseparable over K exactly when its minimal polynomial has the form
Tpn−a,a∈K.
(44)
To prove the nontrivial direction, repeatedly factor a zero formal derivative through T↦Tp until the remaining irreducible polynomial has nonzero derivative. Since Tpr−xpr has only the root x, that remaining separable polynomial must be linear.
