Sharp three-transitivity of Möbius transformations (∀(z1​,z2​,z3​),(w1​,w2​,w3​) ∃!M: M(zi​)=wi​)

For ordered triples of distinct points of the Riemann sphere, a unique Möbius transformation sends the first triple to the second. Normalize each triple to 0,1,∞ using Tz​(z)=(z−z1​)(z2​−z3​)/((z−z3​)(z2​−z1​)), with the usual limits at infinity, and compose Tw−1​∘Tz​. Any two choices differ by a Möbius transformation fixing those three standard points. Fixing infinity makes it affine; fixing zero and one then makes it the identity. Thus the Möbius group action is sharply three-transitive, a stronger property than mere transitivity on triples.
