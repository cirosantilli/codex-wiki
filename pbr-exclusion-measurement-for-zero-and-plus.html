PBR exclusion measurement for zero and plus

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Foundations of quantum mechanics Ontological model of a quantum system Pusey-Barrett-Rudolph theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The product preparations 00,0+,+0,++ are excluded respectively by the orthonormal vectors (01+10)/2​, (00−01+10+11)/2, (00+01−10+11)/2 and (00−11)/2​, written in the computational basis. Each vector has zero inner product with its labelled preparation. This projective measurement gives antidistinguishable quantum states and drives the two-copy PBR theorem contradiction.
