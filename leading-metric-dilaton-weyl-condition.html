Leading metric-dilaton Weyl condition (β​abg​=α′(Rab​+2∇a​∇b​φ))

For the conventional Euclidean dilaton coupling +(4π)−1∫h​Rφ and no antisymmetric background, the metric worldsheet Weyl anomaly vanishes at first order in α′ when Rab​+2∇a​∇b​φ=0. The one-loop Ricci term follows from the background field expansion of a string sigma model. Under a Weyl transformation, the dilaton coupling varies as −(2π)−1∫h​ω□h​φ; the embedding equations replace this by the target Hessian contribution. Full Weyl invariance also requires the dilaton curvature coefficient, including the central-charge deficit, to vanish. See the Lorentzian dilaton coupling sign convention when translating an action with a specified Lorentzian sign.
The conventional coefficient agrees with the background equations in Tong's string theory notes, Section 7.2.3.
