Reionization

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background
OurBigBook.com Words: 279 Articles: 5
Reionization returns some neutral cosmic gas to an ionized state after cosmological recombination. The resulting free electrons increase the cosmological optical depth through Thomson scattering and create a second contribution to the cosmological visibility function.
