Helix-coil transition model

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biopolymer mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 221 Articles: 1
A helix-coil transition model assigns local states of a biopolymer to an ordered helical conformation or a disordered coil conformation. Their competing free energies determine the tendency to form a helix, while the cost of boundaries between regions gives cooperativity: forming a new helical run can be less favorable than extending an existing one. A coil state is a class of disordered conformations, not a claim that the whole molecule is an ideal chain. The Zimm-Bragg model is a simple nearest-neighbour statistical model of this competition.
