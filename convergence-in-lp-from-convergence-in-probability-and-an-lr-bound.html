Convergence in Lp from convergence in probability and an Lr bound

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Convergence of random variables
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
If Xn​→X in probability and supn​∥Xn​∥r​<∞, then Xn​→X in Lp for every 1≤p<r. The Lr bound gives uniform integrability of the pth powers, while convergence in probability controls their bounded part.
