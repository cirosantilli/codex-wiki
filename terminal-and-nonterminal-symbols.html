Terminal and nonterminal symbols

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Formal language theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 47 Articles: 1
In a formal grammar, terminal symbols are symbols of completed words; a nonterminal symbol is an auxiliary symbol that may be replaced by a production. The two alphabets are disjoint.
