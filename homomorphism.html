Homomorphism (f:A→B)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A homomorphism is a map between algebraic structures preserving their specified operations. A group homomorphism preserves multiplication; a ring homomorphism preserves addition and multiplication, with the identity convention specified; a module homomorphism preserves addition and scalar multiplication.
