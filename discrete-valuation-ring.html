Discrete valuation ring

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Local ring Valuation ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 447 Articles: 8
A discrete valuation ring is a principal ideal domain with exactly one nonzero maximal ideal. Every nonzero element of its fraction field is a unit times a unique integer power of a uniformizer.
