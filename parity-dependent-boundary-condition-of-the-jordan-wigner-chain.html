Parity-dependent boundary condition of the Jordan-Wigner chain (cN+1​=−(−1)Nf​c1​)

For Sn+​=∏m<n​(1−2nm​)cn​ and periodic spins, the end exchange term carries the global fermion parity P=(−1)Nf​. Replacing the end bond by a uniform hopping expression requires cN+1​=−Pc1​: even parity gives antiperiodic fermions, odd parity periodic fermions. Each resulting quadratic spectrum must be restricted to states of the parity used in its boundary condition.
