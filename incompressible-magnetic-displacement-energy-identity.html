For a static ideal magnetohydrodynamics equilibrium in fixed gravity, let ∇⋅ξ=0 and Π=p+B2/(2μ0​). Frozen flux gives b=(B⋅∇)ξ−(ξ⋅∇)B and continuity gives ρ′=−ξ⋅∇ρ. Multiplying the linear momentum equation by the conjugate displacement and integrating by parts gives the displayed identity when surface terms vanish. The two magnetic cross terms cancel; the remaining magnetic equilibrium derivative combines with the mass density-gradient force to give the two Hessian matrices. Real symmetric Hessian matrices and the nonnegative magnetic tension term make σ2 real.
