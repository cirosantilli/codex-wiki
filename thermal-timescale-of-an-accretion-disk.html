Thermal timescale of an accretion disk (tth​)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Accretion disk Alpha disk Thermal stability of an accretion disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The thermal timescale compares the internal energy stored in a disc annulus with its local heating or cooling. In a gas-pressure-supported alpha disk, tth​∼(αΩK​)−1, whereas the viscous timescale is longer by approximately (R/H)2. A local thermal perturbation is therefore studied at fixed surface density of a disk, rather than fixed steady-state mass accretion rate.
