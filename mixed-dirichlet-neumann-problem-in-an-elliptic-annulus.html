Mixed Dirichlet-Neumann problem in an elliptic annulus

In elliptic coordinates x=coshμcosν, y=sinhμsinν, a harmonic function in a<μ<b, with 0<a<b and period 2π in ν, satisfies uμμ​+uνν​=0. Prescribe u(b,ν)=f(ν) and uμ​(a,ν)=g(ν). Expand both boundary functions in Fourier series, writing their constant terms as f0​,g0​. For either the sine or cosine coefficient of order n≥1, separation of variables gives
un​(μ)=fn​cosh[n(b−a)]cosh[n(μ−a)]​−gn​ncosh[n(b−a)]sinh[n(b−μ)]​.

The constant angular mode is u0​(μ)=f0​+g0​(μ−b). These expressions follow by solving un′′​−n2un​=0 and checking un​(b)=fn​, un′​(a)=gn​. Their Fourier series gives the solution for sufficiently regular boundary data.
The phrase normal derivative needs care: the scale factor is h=sinh2μ+sin2ν​, so the outward unit normal derivative on the inner ellipse is ∂n​u=−h−1uμ​. Prescribing a physical Neumann boundary condition therefore differs from prescribing uμ​.
For uniqueness, subtract two solutions to obtain v. Multiply vμμ​+vνν​=0 by v and use integration by parts over the periodic coordinate rectangle. The outer Dirichlet boundary condition, inner homogeneous derivative condition and periodic side cancellation give ∫ab​∫02π​(vμ2​+vν2​)dνdμ=0. Thus v is constant, and the outer condition makes it zero.
