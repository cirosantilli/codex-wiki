Perturbative interactions in scalar electrodynamics (Dμ​=∂μ​+ieAμ​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Quantum electrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 183 Articles: 3
Expanding the covariant kinetic term of scalar electrodynamics gives a one-photon two-scalar vertex and a two-photon two-scalar seagull vertex. Their tree amplitudes and Ward identities depend on a consistent choice of charge-flow and gauge covariant derivative convention.
