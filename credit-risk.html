Credit risk

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance
OurBigBook.com Words: 337 Articles: 5
Credit risk is the risk of loss through a counterparty's inability or failure to meet a financial obligation, or deterioration in its credit quality. A loss model distinguishes credit default frequency from loss given default; shared exposures or systematic factors may produce dependence beyond an independent Poisson model.
