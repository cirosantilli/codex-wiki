Using the total 336cm−3 would instead bound the mean mass, not the sum. This critical-density bound on the sum of relic neutrino masses is deliberately conservative: baryons, dark matter and dark energy leave less density available. It assumes the standard thermal abundance, negligible chemical potentials, no later dilution or extra populated states, and the idealized decoupling count. Small non-instantaneous-decoupling corrections do not matter for this estimate.
