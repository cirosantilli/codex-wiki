Positrons are a separate species with the same contribution, so (Se−​+Se+​)/Sγ​=7/4. This distinction is needed for the heating count in part d. At the exact supplied temperature the electron rest mass gives corrections to the ideal 7/8 ratio; it is the limiting relativistic value requested by the problem.
There is also a sign convention issue in the introductory entropy formula. With the chemical-potential convention used in the supplied distribution, the Euler relation is U=TS−PV+∑i​μi​Ni​, so S=(U+PV−∑i​μi​Ni​)/T. Its chemical-potential term has the opposite sign to the printed one. For a concrete counterexample, a dilute monatomic ideal gas with one internal state and nλT3​=e−1, where λT​ is the thermal de Broglie wavelength, has μ=−kB​T, U=3NkB​T/2 and PV=NkB​T. Its entropy is 7NkB​/2; the printed plus sign would instead give 3NkB​/2. All chemical potentials have been neglected here, so this source error does not change any of the above results.
