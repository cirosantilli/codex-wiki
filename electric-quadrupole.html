Electric quadrupole

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrostatics Electric multipole expansion
OurBigBook.com Words: 87 Articles: 1
An electric quadrupole is a charge distribution whose total charge and electric dipole moment vanish while its second charge moment does not. Its potential decays as r−3.
