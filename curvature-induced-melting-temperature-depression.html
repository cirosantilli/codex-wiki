Curvature-induced melting-temperature depression (TI​=Tm​−γTm​K/(ρL))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Surface tension Young–Laplace equation Gibbs--Thomson relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
Curvature depresses the equilibrium melting temperature of solid convex into liquid. Here K is the sum of principal curvatures, equal to twice a common convention for mean curvature, and Tm​ is absolute temperature. The coefficient uses solid-liquid surface energy, mass density and latent heat. This stabilizes fine corrugations of a solidification front.
