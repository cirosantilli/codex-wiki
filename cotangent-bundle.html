Cotangent bundle (T∗L)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Lagrangian submanifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 642 Articles: 16
The cotangent bundle has the canonical one-form λ(q,p)​(v)=p(dπ(v)) and the canonical symplectic form dλ up to a conventional sign. Its zero section is a Lagrangian submanifold.
