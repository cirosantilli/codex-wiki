Spirodienone

 Home Chemistry Molecule Molecular geometry Spiro compound
OurBigBook.com Words: 19
A spirocyclic framework containing a dienone ring. An acid-promoted dienone-phenol rearrangement can convert a suitably substituted spiro framework into fused-ring connectivity.
