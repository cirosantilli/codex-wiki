Knaster-Kuratowski-Mazurkiewicz lemma

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 3
For a simplex with vertices indexed by 0,…,n, let closed sets Fi​ have the property that every face spanned by an index set I lies in ⋃i∈I​Fi​. Then ⋂i=0n​Fi​ is nonempty. The triangle KKM lemma is a two-dimensional covering formulation of this simplex-intersection principle.
