Inner-horizon blueshift coordinate (V−​∝−e−κ−​v)

Near a nondegenerate outgoing inner Cauchy horizon, a regular null coordinate behaves as V−​∝−e−κ−​v, where κ−​>0 is the magnitude of the inner-horizon surface gravity. Transforming stress components gives TV−​V−​​∝e2κ−​vTvv​. Even a small power-law tail can therefore cause an unbounded locally measured flux, motivating mass inflation.
