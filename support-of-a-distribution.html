Support of a distribution (suppu)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 356 Articles: 8
The support of a distribution is the complement of the largest open set on which its pairing vanishes for every supported test function. A partition of unity shows that the union of such open sets still has this vanishing property. For example, the Dirac delta distribution and all its derivatives have support {0}.
