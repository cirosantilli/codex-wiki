Solid-body rotation (Ω=constant)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
In solid-body rotation the angular velocity is independent of distance from the rotation axis, so tangential speed is proportional to cylindrical radius. A galactic region with this galaxy rotation curve has radial epicyclic frequency κ=2Ω.
