Reaction mechanism

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Chemical kinetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 598 Articles: 8
A reaction mechanism proposes the sequence of elementary reactions that produces an overall chemical reaction. Its predicted rate law must agree with observations; matching one rate law need not uniquely identify a reaction mechanism. See Flowers et al., Chemistry 2e, §12.6: reaction mechanisms.
