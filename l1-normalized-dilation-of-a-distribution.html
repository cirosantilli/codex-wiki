L1-normalized dilation of a distribution (⟨uε​,ϕ⟩=⟨u,ϕ(ε⋅)⟩)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Dilation of a distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
For ε>0 and u∈D′(Rn), define uε​ by the displayed pairing. If u is a regular distribution, this agrees with uε​(x)=ε−nu(x/ε) and preserves its L1 space norm. With the convention δt​u(x)=u(tx) for dilation of a distribution, it equals ε−nδ1/ε​u.
If u∈L1(Rn), the dominated convergence theorem gives
⟨uε​,ϕ⟩=∫u(y)ϕ(εy)dy⟶ϕ(0)∫u,uε​→(∫u)δ0​.
(18)
The constant distribution u=1 instead gives uε​=ε−n and has no limit in weak convergence of distributions.
