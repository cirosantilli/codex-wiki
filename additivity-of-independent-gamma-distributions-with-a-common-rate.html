Additivity of independent gamma distributions with a common rate

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Continuous probability distribution Gamma distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
If independent random variables Xi​ have gamma distributions with shapes ai​ and one common rate r, then
∑i=1n​Xi​∼Gamma(∑i=1n​ai​,r).
(297)
Their Laplace transforms of nonnegative random variables multiply to (r/(r+s))∑i​ai​, which is the transform of the displayed gamma distribution; uniqueness of the Laplace transform proves the claim.
