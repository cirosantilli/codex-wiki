Genetic association

 Home Biology Genetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 646 Articles: 9
A genetic association is a statistical dependence between a genetic variant and a phenotype. An associated marker need not be causal: linkage disequilibrium can correlate it with a causal locus, and population stratification can produce an association without such a link.
