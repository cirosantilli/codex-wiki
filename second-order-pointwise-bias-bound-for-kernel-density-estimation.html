Let a nonnegative kernel for density estimation K have integral one, zero first moment and finite second moment μ2​(K). If a probability density function f has bounded second derivative, its kernel density estimator has pointwise bias at most h2μ2​(K)∥f′′∥∞​/2. Apply the Taylor theorem with Lagrange remainder to f(x−hu) and integrate: the first-order term vanishes and the remainder is bounded by h2u2∥f′′∥∞​/2. For K=1[−1/2,1/2]​, μ2​(K)=1/12.
