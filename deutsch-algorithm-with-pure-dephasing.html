Deutsch algorithm with pure dephasing (Pcorrect​=21​(1+vcosα))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Deutsch-Jozsa algorithm Deutsch algorithm
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
If the input qubit of the Deutsch algorithm acquires conditional environment states with overlap ⟨e0​∣e1​⟩=veiα between its two Hadamard gates, its fixed zero-for-constant, one-for-balanced rule succeeds with probability (1+vcosα)/2. A known phase can be compensated before the last Hadamard gate, raising the success probability to (1+v)/2. An orthogonal pair of environment states gives v=0 and destroys all information in the input qubit about the answer; unit overlap modulus preserves coherence up to a known phase.
