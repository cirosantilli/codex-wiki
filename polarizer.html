Polarizer

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Plane electromagnetic wave Polarization of an electromagnetic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
A linear polarizer transmits one linear polarization and suppresses the orthogonal one. Two ideal crossed polarizers block light unless an intervening medium changes its polarization of an electromagnetic wave. A twisted nematic field effect uses this selection to convert field-driven reorientation into brightness modulation.
