Lyddane-Sachs-Teller relation (ϵr​(0)/ϵ∞​=ωLO2​/ωTO2​)

For a single lossless polar optical oscillator with frequency-independent electronic background, ϵr​(ω)=ϵ∞​(ωLO2​−ω2)/(ωTO2​−ω2). Setting frequency to zero yields the displayed static-to-background ratio. Multiple infrared-active branches require the corresponding product relation, rather than selecting an arbitrary pair of modes.
