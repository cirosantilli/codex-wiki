Velocity gradient tensor (Aij​=∂j​ui​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Velocity field
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The velocity gradient tensor is Aij​=∂j​ui​. Its symmetric part is the rate-of-strain tensor S and its antisymmetric part describes local rotation. For incompressible flow, trA=trS=0. The sign convention for a cubic invariant must be specified explicitly.
