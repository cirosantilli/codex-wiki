For a strictly hyperbolic two-component system of conservation laws with ordered speeds λ−​<λ+​, a compressive family-j shock has its family characteristics entering from both sides: λj​(UR​)<s<λj​(UL​). The other family crosses the interface with the same sign of relative speed on both sides. These are the Lax shock admissibility conditions and give three incoming characteristic directions for two conservation laws.
In the present problem,
λ±​(UR​)=±1,λ±​(UL​)=u1​±h1​​.
(24)
Since u1​=s−s/h1​, we always have u1​−h1​​<s. Hence a positive-speed shock of this form cannot be a family-minus shock. For a family-plus shock the remaining strict inequalities are
1<s<u1​+h1​​.
(25)
The first is equivalent to h1​(h1​+1)>2, or h1​>1. The second is equivalent to s<h13/2​, whose square reduces to h1​+1<2h12​, again equivalent to h1​>1 for positive h1​. The minus speed on the right is −1<s automatically. Thus all causal inequalities hold exactly when
h1​>H,H=1​.
(26)
Depth increases behind the right-moving shock. For 0<h1​<1, the algebraic jump solution has the wrong characteristic orientation and is an inadmissible expansion shock; equality is the no-jump limit.
