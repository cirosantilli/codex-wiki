The upward jump at −1/2 creates a rarefaction wave with u=(x+1/2)/t, bounded by the characteristic lines x=−1/2 and x=t−1/2. The downward jump at zero joins the states 1,0, so part (a) gives the shock position s(t)=t/2. The intervening plateau exists exactly while t−1/2<t/2, or t<1. The fan satisfies the Inviscid Burgers equation classically and matches the constant branches continuously; the shock satisfies the Rankine-Hugoniot condition and is causal because 1>1/2>0. Its initial trace is the prescribed pulse.
