Pre-cutoff for Markov chains

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Mixing time of a Markov chain Cutoff for Markov chains
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A sequence of chains exhibits pre-cutoff if there are constants 0<A<B<∞ and times tn​ such that the worst-case distance tends to one at Atn​ and to zero at Btn​. For reversible chains, the product condition trel​=o(tmix​) is necessary for pre-cutoff.
