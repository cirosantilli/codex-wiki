Weak white noise (Cov(Ws​,Wt​)=σ21s=t​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Stationary process Weakly stationary process White noise
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
A white noise sequence has zero mean, a common finite variance and zero autocovariance at every nonzero lag. This requires uncorrelated random variables, rather than independent random variables.
