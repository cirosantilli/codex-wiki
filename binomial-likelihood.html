Binomial likelihood

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution Binomial distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
If x successes occur in n independent Bernoulli trials, the binomial likelihood for the success probability p is proportional to px(1−p)n−x.
