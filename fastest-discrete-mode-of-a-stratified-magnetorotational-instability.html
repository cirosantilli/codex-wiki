The growing root of the ideal magnetorotational dispersion relation is maximized continuously at a=vA2​k2/Ω2=15/16, with growth 3Ω/4. Discrete vertical modes instead have an​=2n(n+1)/β. The fastest one is found by comparing admissible integers on either side of the continuous maximum, rather than rounding a vertical wavenumber blindly. At β=24, n=3 gives s=Ω(17​−3)/2​.
