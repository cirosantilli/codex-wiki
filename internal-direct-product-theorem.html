Internal direct product theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Direct product of groups
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
If H,K⊴G, H∩K={1}, and HK=G, then multiplication defines an isomorphism H×K→G. Conversely, the two canonical factors of a direct product satisfy these conditions.
