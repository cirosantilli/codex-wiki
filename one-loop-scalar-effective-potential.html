For one canonically normalized real scalar field and V′′(φ)>0, the modified minimal subtraction scheme gives the displayed one-loop term. The Gaussian fluctuation approximation produces 21​∫ddkE​(2π)−dlog(kE2​+V′′(φ)). Its derivative in V′′ is half the Euclidean massive loop integral with n=1. Integrating and subtracting its pole yields the formula, up to a field-independent constant. Other finite counterterms alter the scheme-dependent nonlogarithmic part. Negative curvature requires an analytic continuation and can yield an imaginary part indicating instability of that background.
