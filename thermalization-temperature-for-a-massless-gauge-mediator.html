Thermalization temperature for a massless gauge mediator (Γ/H=αY2​mpl​/(1.66g∗​​T))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmological particle freeze-out
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
A rate proportional to T competes with radiation-era expansion proportional to T2. The interaction-to-expansion ratio therefore grows as the universe cools, crossing unity near Teq​=αY2​mpl​/(1.66g∗​​). If a phase transition occurs at Tc​≫Teq​, this high-temperature interaction never establishes thermal equilibrium before the transition. Subsequent faster number-changing processes may still establish a thermal distribution; the initial state is not automatically Fermi-Dirac just because the particles are relativistic.
