Radiative force multiplier (M)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure Mass-luminosity relation Eddington luminosity
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A radiative force multiplier is the ratio of total radiative acceleration to a reference electron-scattering acceleration. Spectral lines, bound-free absorption, and dust can make M≫1, whereas highly ionized gas approaches M=1.
