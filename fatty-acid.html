Fatty acid

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Organic acid Carboxylic acid
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 3
A fatty acid is a carboxylic acid with a hydrocarbon chain. Its chain supplies much of the hydrophobic region when incorporated into a triglyceride or typical glycerophospholipid. See Biology 2e: fatty acids.
