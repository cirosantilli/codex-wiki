Monomer

 Home Chemistry Polymer
OurBigBook.com Words: 432 Articles: 3
A monomer is a chemical substance whose molecules can undergo polymerization and contribute units to the essential structure of a polymer. A monomer molecule is the individual reactant; its incorporated monomeric unit is the portion identifiable as derived from that reactant. Incorporation can change chemical bonds and release small byproducts, so the unit in the chain need not have the complete molecular formula of the feed molecule. See IUPAC: monomer molecule and IUPAC, A brief guide to polymer terminology: monomeric units.
Three different counts answer three different questions. Monomer molecules describe the feed; monomeric units trace that feed's contributions to the product; a constitutional repeating unit describes the smallest chemical pattern repeating along a regular chain. In Nylon 6,10, two different monomers supply one interior constitutional repeating unit,
−NH−(CH2​)6​−NH−C(=O)−(CH2​)8​−C(=O)−.
(14)
Its molecular formula is C16​H30​N2​O2​. The diamine feed is C6​H16​N2​, and the diacid-chloride feed is C10​H16​Cl2​O2​; subtracting two HCl gives the interior-repeat budget. For a finite linear chain made from n of each reactant, there are 2n−1 joining connections, hence 2n−1 formal HCl eliminations, rather than 2n. One amine end and one acid-chloride end remain in that ideal unmodified chain. This is a stoichiometry check on the depicted reaction, not a claim that the washed experimental thread retains those ends. The reacting end-group chemistry is documented in Hammond, lecture 5, p. 4; the specified Nylon 6,10 reactant pair is reported in Polymer Day, nylon demonstration.
Ethene shows the reverse counting mismatch. One CH2​=CH2​ monomer molecule contributes a two-carbon −CH2​−CH2​− monomeric unit to ideal linear polyethylene, without a small byproduct in that growth step. But the smallest constitutional repeating unit of its uniform backbone is just −CH2​−: a chain containing m incorporated ethene monomeric units contains 2m such interior one-carbon repeats. End groups are additional bookkeeping. The frequently drawn [−CH2​−CH2​−]m​ is useful for tracing the feed, although it is not the smallest constitutional repeat. The minimum-pattern definition is given in IUPAC: constitutional repeating unit; the ethene growth chemistry is described by [ref].
A segment of a freely jointed chain or Gaussian chain instead belongs to a statistical model. It can represent many chemical bonds and several constitutional repeating units; its length and the segment count are chosen to describe chain conformation at the intended scale. The three-repeats-per-segment drawing in Figure 3. "From two liquid layers to a Nylon 6,10 thread: monomers, a constitutional repeating unit, and statistical segments" is a deliberately arbitrary coarse-graining example, not a measured Kuhn length. Always state the counting convention before substituting a segment count for a degree of polymerization. This separation lets chemistry track which chemical atoms entered the material while statistical models track how the material bends and extends.
