Dielectric strength (Ebd​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Permittivity Dielectric Dielectric breakdown
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
Dielectric strength characterizes the electric field at dielectric breakdown under specified test conditions. In an ideal uniform gap, it sets a voltage scale Vbd​≃Ebd​d.[ref] The parallel-plate capacitor model consequently gives the stored energy density scale
ubd​≃21​ϵEbd2​.
(12)
This is a model scale, not an operating rating: fringing field of a capacitor make local electric fields differ from V/d, while leakage and dielectric loss spend some supplied energy as heat. Comparing usable capacitor energy therefore requires insulation and breakdown performance as well as permittivity.
