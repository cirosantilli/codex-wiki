For a continuous Itô diffusion with diffusion generator L, let u solve Lu=λu with λ>0, be bounded on an open domain and its boundary, and equal g on the boundary. Stop at the first boundary hit τ. The Itô formula makes e−λ(t∧τ)u(Xt∧τ​) a bounded martingale. Its limit is zero on {τ=∞} and equals e−λτg(Xτ​) on {τ<∞}. Thus
u(x)=Ex​[e−λτg(Xτ​)1{τ<∞}​].
(36)
Boundedness justifies passage to the terminal expectation even if hitting is not almost surely finite.
