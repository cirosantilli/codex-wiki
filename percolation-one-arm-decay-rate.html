Percolation one-arm decay rate (γ(p)=limgn1/n​)

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Percolation theory Bond percolation Russo-Seymour-Welsh theorem One-arm probability
OurBigBook.com Words: 108 Articles: 1
For bond percolation on the square lattice, the one-arm probability gn​=Pp​(0↔∂[−n,n]2) has a root limit of a sequence γ(p)∈[p,1]. The BK boundary-splitting estimate implies that 32n2gn​ is submultiplicative for positive integers. Applying the Fekete lemma to its logarithm proves existence when p>0; at p=0 the rate is zero. The same rate is the root limit of the percolation two-point connection probability along a coordinate axis, by the reflection lower bound for two-point percolation.
