Injective object

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
An object I is injective when every morphism A→I extends across every monomorphism A↪B to a morphism B→I. This is the categorical extension property of an injective module or an injective sheaf; it is dual to the lifting property of a projective object in a category.
