Digital systems, linear algebra, introduction to formal proof examination paper by the University of Oxford (2014) (4224)

OurBigBook.com Words: 50
Primary source: Released examination archive, member 4224.pdf. The original nine-page cover identifies the Preliminary Examination in Computer Science, Trinity Term 2014, Friday 20 June. Questions 1–3 cover digital counters, the MIPS architecture and file systems; Questions 4–6 cover linear algebra; Questions 7–8 use the supplied natural deduction rules in Figures 1–3 on printed page 9.
