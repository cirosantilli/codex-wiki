Central simple algebra

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 25
A central simple algebra over a field k is a finite-dimensional associative k-algebra that is a simple ring and whose center is exactly k.
