Fracture toughness (Kc​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 408 Articles: 11
Fracture toughness measures a material's resistance to crack advance. In a thin elastic plate model, the threshold for advance can be represented by a prescribed front curvature κf​. Such a curvature parameter is not numerically interchangeable with a bulk fracture-toughness parameter without specifying the fracture model.
