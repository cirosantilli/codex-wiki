Variable-based grammar

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Formal language theory Formal grammar

A variable-based grammar has disjoint finite terminal and variable alphabets Σ,V, a start variable S∈V, and productions α→β in which α,β∈(Σ∪V)∗ and α contains at least one variable.
