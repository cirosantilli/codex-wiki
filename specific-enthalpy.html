Specific enthalpy (h)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Enthalpy
OurBigBook.com Words: 80 Articles: 1
Specific enthalpy is enthalpy per unit mass. For a simple compressible fluid of fixed composition, the first law of thermodynamics gives
dh=Tds+ρdp​,
(22)
where T is temperature, s is specific entropy, p is pressure, and ρ is mass density. Along an isentropic flow, dh=dp/ρ. For a perfect gas with constant specific-heat ratio γ, h=γp/[(γ−1)ρ].
