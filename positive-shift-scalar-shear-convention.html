Positive-shift scalar shear convention (κ=3(DΦ+HΨ)+Δχ+​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 42
For Ni​=a2B,i​, γij​=a2[(1−2Φ)δij​+2E,ij​] and Kij​=−(γ˙​ij​−Di​Nj​−Dj​Ni​)/(2N), put χ+​=a2(B−E˙)/Nˉ. Then Kij​=−Hδij​+(Φ˙/Nˉ+HΨ)δij​+∂i∂j​χ+​ and κ=3(Φ˙/Nˉ+HΨ)+Δχ+​. Defining χ=−χ+​ instead makes the trace-free tensor coefficient negative and the trace gradient term −Δχ. A sign change must affect both pieces.
