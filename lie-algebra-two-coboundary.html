Lie algebra two-coboundary (B2(g,k)={(x,y)↦t([x,y]):t∈g∗})

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Lie algebra cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
For trivial coefficients in a field k, a Lie algebra two-coboundary is a bilinear map β(x,y)=t([x,y]) for a linear functional t in the dual space g∗. Some conventions put a minus sign in this formula; the resulting vector subspace B2(g,k) is the same. Each such map is a Lie algebra two-cocycle: it is an alternating bilinear form, and its cyclic expression is t([[x,y],z]+[[y,z],x]+[[z,x],y])=0 by the Jacobi identity. Replacing a linear section of a central extension of a Lie algebra by s′(x)=s(x)+t(x)z changes its Lie algebra two-cocycle by −β. Thus H2(g,k)=Z2(g,k)/B2(g,k) in Lie algebra cohomology records exactly the obstruction to a section that is a Lie algebra homomorphism.
