Transient state

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
A state is transient when its probability of ever returning is less than one.
This is the alternative to a recurrent state for the return behavior of a Markov chain.
