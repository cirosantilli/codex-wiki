Coxeter length (ℓ(w))

 ... Cartan subalgebra Root-space decomposition Root system Weyl reflection Reflection group of a root system Weyl group
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 3
The Coxeter length ℓ(w) is the smallest number of simple reflections whose product is w. Its parity gives the sign of a Weyl-group element.
This is a particular length function, namely word length for the simple reflections.
