Nonnormal Fourier amplification matrix (supθ,nk≤T​∥G(θ)n∥≤CT​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Finite difference Finite difference method von Neumann stability analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For a system or a multilevel method, each Fourier mode evolves through an amplification matrix G(θ). Stability requires bounds on G(θ)n uniform in frequencies and mesh parameters. Eigenvalue moduli alone do not suffice: G=(10​11​) has unit eigenvalues but Gn has off-diagonal entry n. Uniformly bounded eigenvector matrices, or a uniformly positive energy symmetrizer, can supply the missing power bound.
