Spherical elastoplastic cavity (Pc​=4τY​/3,s=ae(P−Pc​)/(4τY​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Plasticity (physics) Perfect plasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
A spherical cavity of fixed reference radius a in an infinite solid with perfect plasticity, the Tresca yield criterion, and zero far-field stress first yields under internal pressure Pc​. In the plastic region a<r<s, radial force balance and τθθ​−τrr​=2τY​ give τrr​=−P+4τY​log(r/a). Matching to the outer linear elasticity solution, where τrr​(s)=−Pc​, gives the displayed plastic-front radius. These are small-strain reference-geometry formulae.
