OurBigBook.com Words: 90
Let q∤m be outside the excluded primes, let ζ have order m, and let ρ be a primitive qth root of unity. Galois equivariance identifies the conjugates with Φ(ρiζ) for 1≤i≤q−1. The distribution axiom includes the missing i=0 factor, so the field norm is Φ(ζq)/Φ(ζ). Arithmetic Frobenius sends ζ to ζq, giving the displayed factor. At higher powers of an already ramified prime all q translates occur instead. These are different norm relations; norm compatibility in just a p-power tower does not supply the auxiliary-prime relation by itself.
