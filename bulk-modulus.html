Bulk modulus (κ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 2
The bulk modulus relates hydrostatic compressive pressure to fractional volume compression. In isotropic three-dimensional linear elasticity, the spherical part of the stress tensor is κtr(ε)I, so uniform dilation has strain energy density κ(trε)2/2. Positive bulk modulus and positive shear modulus make the isotropic elastic stiffness tensor positive on nonzero symmetric strains.
