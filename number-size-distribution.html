Number size distribution (n(D)=dN/dlnD)

 Home Chemistry Atmospheric chemistry Aerosol
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A particle count per stated volume and logarithmic size interval. Its integral over dlnD gives a number density, unlike an aerosol mass distribution. In a cloud-droplet activation estimate, integrating the dry-size distribution above the size-dependent Köhler theory threshold gives activated number for a specified peak atmospheric supersaturation and composition.
