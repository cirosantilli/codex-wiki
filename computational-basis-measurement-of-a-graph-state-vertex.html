For a graph state ∣G⟩=∏ij∈E​CZij​∣+⟩⊗∣V∣, measuring vertex v in the computational basis with result r gives the normalized state
(∏u∈N(v)​Zur​)∣G−v⟩.
(8)
Each Controlled-Z gate from v to a neighbour acts as Zur​ after projecting v onto ∣r⟩; all other edges remain unchanged. The projection contributes 1/2​, so either result has probability 1/2. For a four-cycle, deleting one vertex leaves a path and adds byproducts on its two endpoints.
