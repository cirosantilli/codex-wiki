Column space (colA)

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix Row and column spaces
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
The column space of a matrix A is the linear span of its columns. It is the image of a linear map represented by A.
If an m×n matrix has columns c1​,…,cn​, then matrix multiplication gives Ax=x1​c1​+⋯+xn​cn​. Thus its column space consists of exactly the attainable output vectors in Fm, and Ax=b has a solution exactly when b belongs to this column space. Its dimension is the matrix rank; the kernel instead describes input vectors in Fn whose contributions cancel.[ref]
