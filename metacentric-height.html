Metacentric height (GM=Iwp​/Vd​−BG)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Buoyancy Archimedes' principle Floating equilibrium
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
For a small heel of a floating body in a uniform fluid, the metacentre is the intersection of the new vertical buoyancy line with the original vertical through the centre of buoyancy. With the centre of mass a signed distance BG above the original centre of buoyancy, the metacentric height is GM=Iwp​/Vd​−BG. Here Iwp​ is the waterplane second moment of area about the heel axis and Vd​ the displaced volume. Positive GM gives a restoring small-angle torque and negative GM gives instability. The formula describes local static stability, not all large-angle behaviour or effects of free liquid surfaces inside the body.
