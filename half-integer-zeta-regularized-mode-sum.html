For r=n+1/2, n≥0, the convergent frequency zeta function, expressed through the Riemann zeta function, is ∑r​r−s=(2s−1)ζR​(s) when Res>1. Its analytic continuation at s=−1 gives (1/2−1)(−1/12)=1/24. A common exponential frequency cutoff gives ∑r​re−εr=ε−2+1/24+O(ε2), so the same finite part follows by subtracting the leading divergence. In contrast, integer frequencies have regulated sum −1/12.
