Convex conjugate

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 54 Articles: 1
For a function f on a real vector space, its convex conjugate is
f∗(p)=supx​{p⋅x−f(x)}.
(8)
When f is differentiable and strictly convex, the maximizing point satisfies p=∇f(x).
