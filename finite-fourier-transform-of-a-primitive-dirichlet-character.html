Finite Fourier transform of a primitive Dirichlet character (Sχ​(r)=χ​(r)g(χ))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Dirichlet character Gauss sum of a Dirichlet character
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
For a primitive Dirichlet character, Sχ​(r)=χ​(r)g(χ) for every integer r, with zero on nonunits. For a unit r this follows by substitution. If a prime p divides both r and N, primitivity supplies a unit u≡1(modN/p) with χ(u)=1; substitution by u fixes the additive exponential and forces Sχ​(r)=0. For an imprimitive Dirichlet character this vanishing can fail.
