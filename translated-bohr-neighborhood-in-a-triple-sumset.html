Translated Bohr neighborhood in a triple sumset (t0​+B(Γ,α/4)⊆A+A+A,∣Γ∣≤64α−3)

For a subset A of density α in a finite cyclic group, put F=1A​∗1A​∗1A​ using normalized convolution on a finite group. Its average is α3, so choose t0​ with F(t0​)≥α3. Retain the large spectrum where ∣1A​​∣≥α2/8. The Parseval identity on a finite group gives at most 64α−3 frequencies. Their phases change by at most α/4 on the associated Bohr set, contributing at most α3/4 to the change of F. The discarded frequencies contribute at most another α3/4, since ∑∣1A​​∣2=α. Therefore F(t0​+b)≥α3/2>0 on the Bohr set, proving the containment.
