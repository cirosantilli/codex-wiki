Iwasawa algebra of a Zp-extension (Λ=Zp​[[Γ]]≅Zp​[[T]])

 ... Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Group algebra Completed group algebra Iwasawa algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 2
Choose a topological generator γ of the Galois group of a Zp-extension and set T=γ−1. The completed group algebra becomes the formal power series ring Zp​[[T]], a two-dimensional complete regular local integral domain with maximal ideal (p,T). Its finite-layer quotient is Λ/((1+T)pn−1)≅Zp​[Γ/Γpn].
