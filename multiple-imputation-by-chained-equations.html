Multiple imputation by chained equations

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Missing data Multiple imputation
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
Specify a conditional distribution for each incomplete variable given the others, cycle through parameter draws and missing-value draws, and retain multiple completed datasets after suitable convergence checks. Preserve observed entries. Adequate models, appropriate support and the intended analysis structure matter; arbitrary conditional models need not be compatible with one joint distribution. Standard applications assume missing at random given included predictors. Use Rubin's rules to pool estimates and uncertainty.
