Partition of a sample space (Ω=⨆j​Aj​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability space Sample space
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A family of disjoint events whose union is the sample space. For the countable law of total probability and law of total expectation, use a finite or countably infinite family. Conditioning formulas sum only positive-probability parts, or interpret zero-probability contributions as zero without assigning a conditional expectation there.
