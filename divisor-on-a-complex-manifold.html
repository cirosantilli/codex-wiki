A divisor on a complex manifold is a locally finite formal sum
D=∑Y​nY​Y,
(333)
where the Y are irreducible complex analytic hypersurfaces and nY​∈Z. Products of powers of local defining functions give local meromorphic equations fα​ for D whose ratios fα​/fβ​ are nowhere-vanishing holomorphic functions.
