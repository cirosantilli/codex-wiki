Kolmogorov-Arnold representation theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 335 Articles: 2
Every real continuous function on a compact square can be represented as ∑q=15​Gq​(aq​(x)+bq​(y)), with continuous one-variable functions and fixed inner functions aq​,bq​ independent of the represented function. Grid-separated additive coordinates give the inner functions. For a residual r of norm M, assign r at a sample point divided by three to each separated rectangle image, and continuously interpolate between images. Each point lies in at least three rectangles, so if k∈{3,4,5} summands are accurate, the error is at most [∣1−k/3∣+(5−k)/3]M+5η/3=2M/3+5η/3. Choose oscillation η≤M/10 to obtain contraction 5/6. Iteration gives outer functions by uniform convergence of corrections bounded by M(5/6)j/3. Whole-plane reduction for continuous superposition removes compactness of the original domain without assuming boundedness of the original function.
