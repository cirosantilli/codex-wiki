Critical level of an internal gravity wave (ω=ω−kU=0)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave Atmospheric internal gravity wave Intrinsic frequency
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 2
A critical level is a height at which the intrinsic frequency of an internal gravity wave vanishes. For finite positive buoyancy frequency, its local vertical wavenumber diverges as ω→0. The inviscid Taylor–Goldstein equation is singular there; viscosity, diffusion or nonlinear wave breaking can regularize the resulting small scales.
