Second Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor (S=JF−1σF−T=PF−T)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Constitutive equation Stress Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensors
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The second tensor has two material indices and is symmetric when the Cauchy stress tensor is symmetric. The displayed relation uses the material-first nominal stress tensor convention. In particular, PF is generally not this tensor. These conventions agree with A. F. Bower's definitions of stress measures.
