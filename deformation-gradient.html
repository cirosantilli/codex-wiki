Deformation gradient (FiI​=∂xi​/∂XI​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Deformation (mechanics) Finite strain theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 207 Articles: 5
The Jacobian matrix of a deformation map carries an infinitesimal reference line element to its current image. Its determinant J=detF is the local volume ratio. An orientation-preserving deformation has J>0, and incompressibility imposes J=1.
