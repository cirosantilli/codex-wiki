Elastodynamic Green tensor (Gik​(x,y,t))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Elastic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 261 Articles: 3
The causal Green function for isotropic linear elasticity is tensor-valued: column k is the displacement caused by a point impulsive body force in direction k. Its radiative terms are longitudinal P waves at t=R/α and transverse S waves at t=R/β, proportional to R−1. A term supported between the arrivals carries the intermediate/near-field response. Convolution in time and space constructs the displacement for a general source.
