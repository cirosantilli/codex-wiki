One-sided kinetic solidification dispersion (σ(1+S/p)=SV−Γα2−SV2/p)

OurBigBook.com Words: 275 Articles: 2
Use undercooling ΔT=Tm​−T∞​, solidification attachment mobility M, velocity scale U0​=MΔT, length ℓ=κ/U0​ and time κ/U02​. Let S=L/(cp​ΔT) be the latent-to-sensible heat ratio, θ=(T−T∞​)/ΔT, and Γ=γT​/(ΔTℓ). In the one-sided liquid-diffusion model, a travelling planar front has θ=Se−Vξ and V=1−S. For a corrugation ζeσt+iαy, the decaying thermal perturbation is A1​e−pξ, with p2−Vp=σ+α2 and the branch p=[V+V2+4(σ+α2)​]/2. The displaced kinetic condition gives A1​=(SV−σ−Γα2)ζ. The displaced Stefan condition gives Sσζ=pA1​−SV2ζ. Eliminating A1​ proves the displayed dispersion relation. Neglect of solid-side perturbation heat flux is part of this model.
