Rosseland mean opacity (κR​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Radiative transfer Radiative transfer equation Radiative diffusion
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The Rosseland mean opacity is the harmonic frequency average weighted by ∂Bν​/∂T. It controls radiative diffusion because flux preferentially travels through low-opacity frequencies.
It is a frequency-averaged opacity appropriate to the radiative-diffusion limit, not the general opacity concept itself.
