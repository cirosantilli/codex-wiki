Conservation of salt during freezing (S1​=S0​/(1−fi​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Sea ice thermodynamics Brine rejection
OurBigBook.com Words: 190 Articles: 2
If a fraction fi​ of initial liquid mass freezes into salt-free ice, all original salt remains in a liquid mass fraction 1−fi​. Writing salinity as salt concentration per mass gives S1​(1−fi​)=S0​, hence ΔS=S0​fi​/(1−fi​). For equal initial cooling and subsequent freezing heat extraction, fi​=cp,w​ΔT/Lf​. The liquid specific heat capacity, rather than the ice value, determines the initial heat removal. If ice retains salt, the mass balance instead reads S1​(1−fi​)+Si​fi​=S0​.
