Ahlswede–Daykin inequality

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Set family
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
For nonnegative functions on a finite Boolean lattice, the condition α(A)β(B)≤γ(A∪B)δ(A∩B) for all pairs implies (∑α)(∑β)≤(∑γ)(∑δ). Coordinate elimination and the two-point four-functions inequality prove it by induction. Indicators give inequalities for the union and intersection of set families. The Boolean-lattice statement appears in Theorem 3.1 of Nicholas Ruozzi's paper.
