For the Poynting–Robertson drag invariant C and A=βGM⋆​/c, the orbit-averaged time to the point-star limit is
tPR​=5A2C2​∫0e0​​(1−u2)3/2u3/5​du.
(28)
This tends to a02​/(4A) for a circular orbit and to 4Q01/2​q03/2​/(5A) for a highly eccentric orbit. Stellar radius, sublimation, collisions, and failure of orbital averaging can terminate the evolution earlier.
