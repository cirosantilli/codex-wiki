Nonuniqueness of geophysical inversion

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Different subsurface models can produce the same measured field. A gravity anomaly, for example, does not uniquely separate density, body shape and regional background. Geological constraints and complementary observations reduce this ambiguity; an inferred density should retain the model and reference-field assumptions used to obtain it.
