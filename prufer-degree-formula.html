Prüfer degree formula (degT​(v)=1+#{i:pi​=v})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Tree (graph theory) Cayley's formula Prüfer sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 208 Articles: 1
For a labelled tree with n≥2 vertices, the vertex degree of label v is one plus the number of occurrences of v in its Prüfer sequence:
degT​(v)=1+#{i:pi​=v}.
(14)
Each recording of v corresponds to an incident edge removed when a neighbouring leaf of a graph is deleted. Exactly one incident edge remains when v itself is deleted, or when it belongs to the final pair. In particular, v is a leaf of a graph exactly when it never occurs in the Prüfer sequence. The same reasoning applies to each remaining tree and its remaining code. [ref]
For p=(1,3,3,5), the occurrence counts in label order are (1,0,2,0,1,0), giving the degree sequence (2,1,3,1,2,1). Its sum is 10=2(6−1), as required by the degree-sum formula. More generally, summing the occurrence counts gives n−2, so summing the vertex degrees gives n+(n−2)=2(n−1).
