An open migration process is a continuous-time Markov chain of colony populations with external Poisson processes, state-dependent departure rates, and fixed probabilistic routing that eventually exits the system. Its traffic equations of an open migration process determine the total arrival rates, and its equilibrium has a product-form stationary distribution of an open migration process when the normalizing sums are finite.
