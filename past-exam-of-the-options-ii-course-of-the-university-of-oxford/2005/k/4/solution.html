Irrotational motion in this simply connected fluid region has a velocity potential, u=∇ϕ. Incompressibility gives ∇⋅u=0, hence Δϕ=0.
The moving surface is a material surface: differentiating y−η(x,t)=0 along a particle gives ηt​+ϕx​ηx​=ϕy​ at y=η. Linearizing about a flat stationary surface drops the product term and evaluates at zero, giving ηt​=ϕy​ there. The Unsteady Bernoulli equation, after dropping quadratic velocity terms and absorbing the time-only constant into ϕ, is ϕt​+p/ρ+gy=0. The linear capillary pressure at the surface is p=−Tηxx​ relative to atmosphere, in the sign convention of the source. Therefore
ϕy​=ηt​,ϕt​+gη−ρT​ηxx​=0(y=0).​
