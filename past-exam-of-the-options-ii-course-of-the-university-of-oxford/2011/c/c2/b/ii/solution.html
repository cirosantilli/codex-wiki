If gcd(b,c)>1, both Jacobi symbols vanish, so the formula holds. Otherwise write b=∏i​pi​ and c=∏j​qj​, retaining repeated factors. Multiplicativity of the Jacobi symbol and quadratic reciprocity give
(cb​)=∏i,j​(qj​pi​​)=∏i,j​(pi​qj​​)(−1)∑i,j​(pi​−1)(qj​−1)/4.
(17)
The exponent is
(∑i​2pi​−1​)(∑j​2qj​−1​)≡2b−1​2c−1​(mod2)
(18)
by the parity identity from part (i). The product of the reversed Legendre symbols is (c/b). Thus the Jacobi reciprocity law is
(cb​)=(bc​)(−1)(b−1)(c−1)/4.​
(19)
This also covers b=1 or c=1 by empty products.
