The cubic has no root in F2​, since its values at 0 and 1 are both 1. A cubic over a field is irreducible precisely when it has no root. Consequently
K=F2​[α],α3+α+1=0,
(1)
is a field extension of degree 3, with eight elements. The Frobenius endomorphism x↦x2 takes a root of a polynomial over F2​ to another root, so the roots are α,α2,α4. They are distinct: equality between any two would make the Frobenius endomorphism orbit have length less than 3, contradicting the minimal polynomial of α. They all lie in K, and one of them generates K. Hence
K≅F8​,[K:F2​]=3.​
