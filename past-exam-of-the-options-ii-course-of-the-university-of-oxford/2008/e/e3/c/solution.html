Intersect the ambient balls with E. All points on the vertical arm have distance at least 1/2 from the centre, so the open ball is
(0,1)×{0}​.
(18)
The closed ball also contains the endpoints of that segment and the lower half of the vertical arm:
([0,1]×{0})∪({0}×[0,1/2])​.
(19)
The closure of the open ball in E is just [0,1]×{0}. Both endpoints are limits of points in the open segment, whereas a vertical point of positive height has a small enough neighbourhood disjoint from it. Thus every point (0,y) with 0<y≤1/2 witnesses strict containment.
