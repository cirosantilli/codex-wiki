Every point other than a is isolated, so no such point can enter the closure unless it was already in B. The only possible extra point is a, and it lies in the closure precisely when every d-ball about a meets B. Therefore the three answers, in the printed order, are
B=B;B=B;B=B∪{a}​.
(26)
In the middle case, a ball of radius below d(a,B) misses B; in the last case the infimum zero makes every positive-radius ball meet B. If B is empty its closure is empty as usual, with the convention d(a,∅)=+∞.
