Options II, Honour School of Mathematics Part A, Trinity Term; Thursday 25 June 2009, 9.30am–12.30pm. The private editable transcription preserves the intact cover and all nineteen questions across seventeen pages. The source was supplied through the past-papers disclosure, in original Part A bundle pages 141–157. No exact worked-answer advertisement was found in the bounded check. The Classical Mechanics revision advertisement names other years, so it does not establish availability for this year's question.
