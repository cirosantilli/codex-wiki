The full twelve-page source, including the original identification diagram and all nineteen questions, is privately transcribed from original Part A bundle pages 20–31, supplied through the past-papers disclosure. No exact worked-answer advertisement was found in the bounded availability check. Printed formulas that are false as stated are retained in the transcription; the corresponding solutions explain their corrections.
