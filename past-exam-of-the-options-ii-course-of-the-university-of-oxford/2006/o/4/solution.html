Minimize ∥Y−Xθ∥2. Differentiation gives the normal equations XTXθ=XTY. Full column rank makes XTX positive definite, so
θ=(XTX)−1XTY,Eθ=θ,Cov(θ)=σ2(XTX)−1.​
(62)
The expectation uses the zero error mean; the covariance uses Cov(ϵ)=σ2I. With independent Gaussian errors of common variance, maximizing likelihood minimizes exactly the same residual sum of squares, so this is also the maximum likelihood estimator, whether the common variance is known or estimated.
For the quadratic design, decompose each group's residual sum of squares into its within-group constant plus the group size times the squared residual of its sample mean. The three parameter combinations α, α+β+γ and α−β+γ can fit all three group means exactly. Therefore
α=Y0​,β​=2Y1​−Y−1​​,γ​=2Y1​+Y−1​−2Y0​​.​
(63)
The group means are independent normal variables, with variances σ2/m,σ2/n,σ2/n. Hence their two stated linear combinations are jointly normal, and
Cov(β​,γ​)=41​(σ2/n−σ2/n)=0.
(64)
Joint normality therefore proves the two estimators are independent. Their variances are σ2/(2n) and σ2/(2n)+σ2/m, respectively. Equal replication at the two off-center temperatures is what cancels the covariance.
