For a nonzero real vector w, a Householder reflection is
H(w)=I−wTw2wwT​.
(79)
Writing P=wwT/(wTw) gives PT=P and P2=P, so HTH=(I−2P)2=I. Thus a Householder matrix is an orthogonal matrix, and it reflects across the hyperplane perpendicular to w.
For u=0, choose α=−sgn(u1​)∥u∥, taking the sign to be +1 when u1​=0, and put w=u−αe1​. This w is nonzero. Since α2=∥u∥2, direct expansion gives wTw=2(∥u∥2−αu1​) and wTu=∥u∥2−αu1​, so
H(w)u=αe1​​.
(80)
Apply this construction successively to the trailing part of each column of A, extending each reflection by an identity on earlier coordinates. Previously created zeros are preserved. If a trailing column vector is zero, use the identity there. The resulting product QT is orthogonal and QTA=R is upper triangular, yielding the QR factorization A=QR even when A is singular.
To solve Ax=b, multiply by QT and use back substitution on Rx=QTb when R is nonsingular. If A is singular, detR=det(QT)detA=0; an upper triangular determinant is its diagonal product, so at least one diagonal entry is zero. In that case consistency and free variables require separate treatment.
The unshifted QR algorithm starts from A0​=A, factors Ak​=Qk​Rk​, and forms
Ak+1​=Rk​Qk​=QkT​Ak​Qk​​.
(81)
Every iterate is related by an orthogonal similarity transformation to the previous one and hence to A, with the same eigenvalues. Symmetry is preserved. For symmetric matrices with appropriate spectral separation, the iterates approach diagonal form: the off-diagonal entries decrease and the diagonal exposes the eigenvalues. Practical shifted QR accelerates convergence and uses deflation when off-diagonal couplings become negligible. With a shift μk​, factor Ak​−μk​I=Qk​Rk​ and set Ak+1​=Rk​Qk​+μk​I; the same similarity identity still holds.
Universal diagonal convergence of unshifted QR would be too strong. For example A=(01​10​) admits the standard factorization Q=A, R=I, so the iteration remains at A rather than becoming diagonal, although its eigenvalues are ±1. Such equal-modulus cases require a convergence qualification or treatment of a retained block. Similarity is unconditional; convergence to a useful limiting form is an additional algorithmic property.
