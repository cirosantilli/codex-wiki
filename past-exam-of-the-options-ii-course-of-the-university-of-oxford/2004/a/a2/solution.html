The rotational tetrahedral group acts transitively on its four vertices. A rotation fixing a vertex must preserve its opposite face and rotate its three vertices cyclically; its stabilizer has order three. The orbit-stabilizer theorem gives ∣G∣=4⋅3=12. The rotations are the identity, two nontrivial rotations about each of the four vertex-to-opposite-face axes, and a half-turn about each of the three opposite-edge-midpoint axes. Therefore the counts by element order are

A rotation fixing all vertices fixes three linearly independent vertex vectors and is the identity. The vertex permutation action is therefore faithful. The listed rotations induce the identity, 3-cycles and double transpositions, all even permutations. Its injective image has order 12, the full order of A4​. Hence G≅A4​​.
