OurBigBook.com Words: 6k Articles: 175
Options II, Honour School of Mathematics Part A, Trinity Term; Thursday 30 June 2011, 9.30am–12.30pm. The complete sixteen-page source was supplied through the past-papers disclosure, in original Part A bundle pages 282–297. The private editable transcription retains the complete cover and every printed question.
New solutions cover the eighteen questions without an exact worked-answer advertisement found in the bounded availability check. H2 is recorded separately because Oxford explicitly advertises solutions for its students in the Classical Mechanics revision notes. No new solution is supplied for that question.
