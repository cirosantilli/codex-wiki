A material interface obeys D(y−η(x,t))/Dt=0. In each fluid this gives ϕjy​=ηt​+(Uj​+ϕjx​)ηx​ at y=η, where U1​=U and U2​=V. Discarding products of perturbations and evaluating at the undisturbed interface gives
ϕ1y​=ηt​+Uηx​,ϕ2y​=ηt​+Vηx​(y=0).​
(107)
For an inviscid irrotational fluid, unsteady Bernoulli is Φt​+21​∣∇Φ∣2+p/ρ+gy=C(t), with total potential Φ=Uj​x+ϕj​. Subtract the undisturbed relation and linearize at the moving interface. The pressure perturbation there is −ρj​(ϕjt​+Uj​ϕjx​+gη). With no surface tension the two pressures agree, giving
ρ1​(ϕ1t​+Uϕ1x​+gη)=ρ2​(ϕ2t​+Vϕ2x​+gη)(y=0).​
(108)
Use complex amplitudes and take real parts at the end. For k>0, solutions of Laplace's equation decaying away from the interface have the form
η=Aei(kx−ωt),ϕ1​=B1​ekyei(kx−ωt),ϕ2​=B2​e−kyei(kx−ωt).
(109)
Put Ω1​=ω−Uk and Ω2​=ω−Vk. The kinematic conditions imply B1​=−iΩ1​A/k and B2​=iΩ2​A/k. Substituting into the pressure condition gives
ρ1​(g−kΩ12​​)A=ρ2​(g+kΩ22​​)A.
(110)
A nonzero amplitude therefore requires
ρ1​(ω−Uk)2+ρ2​(ω−Vk)2=(ρ1​−ρ2​)gk.​
(111)
To determine stability, let R=ρ1​+ρ2​ and U=(ρ1​U+ρ2​V)/R. Completing the square yields
(ω−Uk)2=R(ρ1​−ρ2​)gk​−R2ρ1​ρ2​(U−V)2k2​.
(112)
For U=V, the right-hand side is negative exactly when
k>ρ1​ρ2​(U−V)2(ρ12​−ρ22​)g​.
(113)
Then the two frequencies have opposite nonzero imaginary parts. The root with positive imaginary part gives exponential growth through e−iωt. In terms of wavelength λ=2π/k, the unstable range is
λ<λc​=(ρ12​−ρ22​)g2πρ1​ρ2​(U−V)2​.​
(114)
This is the short-wavelength Kelvin-Helmholtz instability for a density-stable interface without surface tension. Equality is the neutral threshold; if U=V, the gravitational term is positive and this shear instability is absent.
