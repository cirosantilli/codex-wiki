The finite sum of truncated cubes is C2 by part (b) and is cubic on each knot interval. For x≤0, every term vanishes. For x≥4h, every truncated power equals its ordinary polynomial, so part (a) applied to P(x)=x3 gives
S(3)​(x)=∑k=04​(−1)k(k4​)(x−kh)3=Q(4)​(x)=0.
(166)
It is therefore zero on both exterior intervals. Its C2 regularity makes the endpoint second derivatives zero too, proving naturality.
Evaluation at the five knots gives
(S(3)​(0),S(3)​(h),S(3)​(2h),S(3)​(3h),S(3)​(4h))=h3(0,1,4,1,0).​
(167)
For example the three interior values in units of h3 are 1, 8−4=4, and 27−4⋅8+6=1. At 4h the cancellation is 64−4⋅27+6⋅8−4=0.
For h=1 an explicit graph description is
S(3)​(x)=⎩⎨⎧​0,x3,4−6(x−2)2+3∣x−2∣3,(4−x)3,​x≤0 or x≥4,0≤x≤1,1≤x≤3,3≤x≤4.​
(168)
It is symmetric about x=2, increases from zero to its maximum four at the centre, and decreases symmetrically back to zero. It joins the zero tails with zero first and second derivatives.
