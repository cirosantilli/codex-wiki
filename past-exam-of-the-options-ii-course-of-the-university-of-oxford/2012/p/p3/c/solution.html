The columns outside indices i,j are unchanged coordinate vectors. The remaining two columns have nonzero entries only in rows i,j, with the 2×2 block M(θ). They have squared norm cos2θ+sin2θ=1 and mutual inner product zero; they are also orthogonal to all the unchanged columns. Hence JTJ=I.

Every other coordinate is unchanged. If r=0, choose θ=0; the same conclusions hold. Therefore the procedure can be fixed with the nonnegative pivot convention
yi​=xi2​+xj2​​,yj​=0,yk​=xk​ (k=i,j).​
