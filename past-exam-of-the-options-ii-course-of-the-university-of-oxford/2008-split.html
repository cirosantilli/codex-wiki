Options II, Honour School of Mathematics Part A, Trinity Term; Thursday 19 June 2008, 9.30am–12.30pm. The full thirteen-page source, including its intact cover and all nineteen questions, is preserved in a private editable transcription. It was supplied through the past-papers disclosure, in original Part A bundle pages 80–92. Exact departmental worked answers are advertised for H2; that question is retained as an availability record rather than duplicated here. The bounded check found no exact answer advertisement for the other questions.
