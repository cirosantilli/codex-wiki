Ignoring a factor independent of the probabilities, the likelihood function is L(π)=∏i​πini​​, so its log is ℓ(π)=∑i​ni​logπi​ subject to ∑i​πi​=1. A Lagrange multiplier gives ni​/πi​=λ. Summing πi​=ni​/λ makes λ=n.
Thus
πi​=ni​/n.​
(88)
Since all counts are positive, a zero fitted cell probability would have zero likelihood. On the interior the log-likelihood is strictly concave, so this stationary point is its unique global maximum.
