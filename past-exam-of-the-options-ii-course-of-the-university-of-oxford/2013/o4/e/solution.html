At a true variance σ2>0, S/σ2∼χn2​. Therefore the power function is
w(σ2)=1−Fn​(σ2σ02​​Fn−1​(1−α)).​
(86)
The argument of Fn​ decreases as σ2 increases. Since the chi-squared density is positive on the positive half-line, the power is continuous and strictly increasing, with
w(0+)=0,w(σ02​)=α,w(∞)=1.​
(87)
These features determine the requested rough sketch. The curve passes through the size at the null value and approaches one for very large variance.
