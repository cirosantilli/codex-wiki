Local exponential chart (log:exp(U)⟶U⊆g)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Exponential map of a Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Since the differential of the Exponential map of a Lie group at zero is the identity, the inverse function theorem makes it a diffeomorphism on a sufficiently small neighborhood of zero. Its local inverse is a logarithm near the group identity. It need not extend to a global logarithm, even for the real general linear group.
