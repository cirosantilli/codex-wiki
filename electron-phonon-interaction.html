Electron-phonon interaction

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Bose-Einstein statistics Bose-Einstein distribution Phonon
OurBigBook.com Words: 929 Articles: 12
The electron-phonon interaction couples electronic states to the atomic displacements represented by phonons. A lattice vibration changes the potential seen by an electron; its quantized displacement permits phonon absorption and emission. This interaction affects electrical resistance, electronic thermal conductivity, lattice dynamics and pairing in conventional superconductors.
