Power-set axiom in the constructible universe (PL(a)=PV(a)∩L∈L)

OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For a∈L, ambient separation forms S=P(a)∩L. Ambient replacement bounds the first constructible stages of all members of S. At a stage containing a and all of S, the latter is definable by the bounded property of being a subset of a. Thus S belongs to the next constructible stage and is the internal power set in L.
