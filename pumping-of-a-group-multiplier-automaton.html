Pumping of a group multiplier automaton

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Automatic group Automatic structure for a group Group multiplier automaton
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
In an accepted padded convolution of words, a loop after one tape ends can be deleted or repeated while keeping that entire tape fixed. The altered other tape still represents the same required neighbouring group element. A shortest representative has a suffix no longer than the fixed number of automaton states; an accepted longer suffix gives infinitely many representatives of the same element.
