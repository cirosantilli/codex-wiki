Symmetric chain decomposition of a Boolean lattice

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 169 Articles: 3
A symmetric chain in the Boolean lattice P([n]) contains one set of each size from r through n−r. A symmetric chain decomposition partitions the Boolean lattice into such chains.
