Burgers material (J(t)=1/E0​+(1/E1​)(1−e−t/τ)+t/η0​)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity Linear viscoelasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 163
A four-element linear viscoelasticity model with a spring E0​, a Kelvin-Voigt model branch E1​,η1​, and a free dashpot η0​ in series. Adding their strains under common stress gives the displayed creep compliance, with τ=η1​/E1​. There is an instantaneous elastic response, a delayed recoverable response and steady viscous flow.[ref] Named after Johannes Martinus Burgers, it extends a Maxwell model with a delayed elastic branch, or a standard linear solid model with a free dashpot.
For a load σ0​ maintained for time th​, the two recoverable contributions behave as in recovery after a finite-duration stress load, while the free dashpot retains σ0​th​/η0​. Hence
ε(t)=E1​σ0​​(1−e−th​/τ)e−(t−th​)/τ+η0​σ0​th​​,t>th​,ε(∞)=η0​σ0​th​​​.
(4)
This residual response models viscous flow; it does not by itself establish a plastic yield threshold or a damage mechanism in a real specimen.
