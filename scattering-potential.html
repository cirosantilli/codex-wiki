Scattering potential (V)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Inverse scattering problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 177 Articles: 3
For a refractive index n(r) and background wavenumber k0​, one sign convention defines the scattering potential by V=k02​(1−n2). The total field then satisfies (Δ+k02​)ψ=Vψ.
The opposite convention V=k02​(n2−1) gives (Δ+k02​)ψ=−Vψ and reverses the sign attached to the corresponding Green-function integral. Either convention is valid when used consistently.
