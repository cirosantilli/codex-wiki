RSA private exponent reveals the factorization

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra RSA cryptosystem
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
Knowledge of a valid public and private exponent gives ed−1, a nonzero multiple of λ(N). Writing it as 2st with t odd and testing random bases produces a nontrivial square root of one modulo N with high probability; a greatest common divisor then factors N. Thus one customer's private key under a shared semiprime modulus compromises every exponent using that modulus.
