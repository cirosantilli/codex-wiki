Invariant volume criterion for a smooth flow (divΩ​F=Fu⟺LF​(e−uΩ)=0)

For a volume form Ω, define divergence by LF​Ω=(divΩ​F)Ω. The product rule for the Lie derivative gives divehΩ​F=divΩ​F+Fh. Consequently the flow preserves a smooth positive volume precisely when its divergence in any reference volume is a smooth flow coboundary. The displayed density has the minus sign dictated by this product rule.
