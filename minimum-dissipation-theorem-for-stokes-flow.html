Minimum-dissipation theorem for Stokes flow

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Viscous dissipation
OurBigBook.com Words: 414 Articles: 5
Among all incompressible velocity fields with the same prescribed boundary velocity and far-field behavior, the Stokes flow minimizes viscous dissipation. If v=u+w, where u is the Stokes solution and w has homogeneous boundary data, integration by parts and the Stokes equation eliminate the cross term, leaving
D[v]−D[u]=2μ∫e(w):e(w)dV≥0.
