Fibre of absolute Frobenius over a rational point of the affine line (Speck[t]/(t−a)p)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic variety Morphism of algebraic varieties Fiber of a morphism Scheme-theoretic fiber
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Over a perfect field k of characteristic p, the scheme-theoretic fiber of the Absolute Frobenius morphism of Ak1​ over a rational point a is
Spec(k[t]⊗k[t],t↦tp​k)≅Speck[t]/(t−a)p.
(209)
Its underlying space is one point, but its coordinate ring has a nonzero nilpotent element, so the fibre is a length-p nonreduced scheme.
