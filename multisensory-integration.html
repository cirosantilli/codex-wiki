Multisensory integration

 Home Biology Neuroscience Sensory system Perception Multisensory perception
OurBigBook.com Words: 488 Articles: 10
Interactions or combination of information across sensory modalities. Operational criteria must be specified: a neuron can show a changed paired response, a judgment can combine cues, or signals can be attributed to one event. These criteria constrain one another without being interchangeable.
