Dynamic boundary condition for the heat equation (bt​+αux​+βb=f)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Boundary condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 206 Articles: 3
A dynamic boundary condition includes a time derivative of the boundary trace. For a half-line heat equation, ut​(0,t)+αux​(0,t)+βu(0,t)=f(t) gives a separate boundary evolution coupled to the interior flux. Its Laplace transform contains the initial trace u0​(0). The sign of ux​ differs from that of the outward normal derivative at a left endpoint.
