Rigid-lid constant-shear baroclinic model (D(η)=1+η2/4−ηcothη)

OurBigBook.com Words: 82
For a uniform buoyancy frequency, constant Coriolis parameter and zonal shear U=Λz between rigid lids separated by H, a zero-interior-PV normal mode has Ψ′′−K2Ψ=0. Adiabatic no-normal-flow conditions give c=ΛH/2±ΛHD(η)​/η, η=KH. Growth requires D<0, or a negative squared phase speed relative to the mean flow, rather than a literal c2<0 in an arbitrary moving frame. The cutoff is ηc​≃2.39936; for zero meridional wavenumber maximum growth occurs at kLD​≃1.60612, with rate 0.30982Λf0​/N.
