Heisenberg coupling matrix for two spins (C=2S−I)

For two spin one-half systems, the dimensionless isotropic Heisenberg model coupling is C=X⊗X+Y⊗Y+Z⊗Z, where X,Y,Z are Pauli matrices. In the ordered computational basis (∣00⟩,∣01⟩,∣10⟩,∣11⟩),

where S is the SWAP gate. This follows by acting on each basis state: the XX+YY terms exchange the two opposite-spin states with coefficient two and annihilate the parallel-spin states. The symmetric three-dimensional sector has eigenvalue 1 and the antisymmetric sector has eigenvalue −3. Hence
e−iaC/2=eia/2(cosaI−isinaS).

This identity explains how isotropic exchange interaction generates a SWAP gate at a=π/2 up to a global phase.
