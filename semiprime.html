Semiprime (N=pq)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Composite number
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A semiprime is a composite number that is a product of exactly two prime numbers, counted with multiplicity. For example, 6=2⋅3 and 9=32 are semiprimes. Distinct factors give φ(pq)=(p−1)(q−1) for the Euler totient function, whereas equal factors give φ(p2)=p(p−1). This distinction matters when constructing a uniform coprime state for a semiprime.
