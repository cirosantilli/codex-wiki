Clifford gate

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum theory Quantum circuit Pauli group
OurBigBook.com Words: 261 Articles: 8
A Clifford gate is a unitary operation that normalizes the Pauli group: conjugating any Pauli operator by it produces another Pauli operator. Hadamard, phase, and controlled-Z gates generate the Clifford operations.
