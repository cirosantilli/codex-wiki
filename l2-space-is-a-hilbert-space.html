L2 space is a Hilbert space (L2(X,F,ν))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Lp space
OurBigBook.com Words: 215 Articles: 4
The space L2(X,F,ν) consists of almost-everywhere equivalence classes of measurable functions with ∫∣f∣2dν<∞. The inner product
⟨f,g⟩=∫fg​dν
(44)
induces its norm, and the Riesz-Fischer theorem makes it complete; hence it is a Hilbert space.
