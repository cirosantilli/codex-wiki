Poisson-gamma conjugacy (θ∣x∼Gamma(r+∑i​xi​,λ+n))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Bayesian posterior
OurBigBook.com Words: 192 Articles: 2
For conditionally independent Poisson observations of mean θ and a gamma distribution prior of shape r>0 and rate λ>0, the likelihood function contributes θ∑i​xi​e−nθ. Multiplying by the prior density gives a posterior distribution with shape r+∑i​xi​ and rate λ+n. The single-observation update is shape r+X and rate λ+1.
The posterior mean is the convex combination
λ+nr+∑i​xi​​=λ+nλ​λr​+λ+nn​xˉ.
(14)
Thus the prior mean r/λ and the sample maximum-likelihood estimate xˉ bracket it. Integrating the next conditional Poisson distribution against this posterior distribution gives the Poisson-gamma mixture, a negative binomial distribution; its spread includes uncertainty in θ as well as the conditional count variation.
