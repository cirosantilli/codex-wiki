Schmidt coefficient

 ... Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Von Neumann entropy Entanglement entropy Schmidt decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
The Schmidt coefficients of a normalized bipartite pure state are the nonnegative numbers sj​ in its Schmidt decomposition; they satisfy ∑j​sj2​=1 and their squares are the nonzero eigenvalues of either reduced density matrix.
