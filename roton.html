Roton (ϵ(p)≃Δ+(p−p0​)2/(2mr​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Superfluidity Superfluid Landau criterion
OurBigBook.com Words: 116
A roton is an excitation near a local minimum of a superfluid's dispersion relation at nonzero momentum p0​. The displayed local approximation defines a positive energy gap Δ and positive curvature effective mass mr​; it is not a free helium atom or a macroscopic quantum vortex.[ref] In the stipulated plot, these correspond to p0​=2p∗​, Δ=0.6E∗​ and mr​=p∗2​/(4E∗​). The plot illustrates how a low energy at appreciable momentum can lower the Landau criterion below the speed of sound, without assigning any measured parameter to real helium.
Landau's 1947 revision joined the low-momentum linear part to a spectrum with a finite-momentum minimum, retaining the macroscopic superfluid theory while changing its excitation model.[ref]
