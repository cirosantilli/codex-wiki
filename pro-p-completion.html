Pro-p completion (Gp​)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Profinite group Pro-p group
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The inverse limit of the quotients of an abstract group by its normal subgroups of finite p-power index. Maps to pro-p groups factor uniquely through the canonical dense image in this completion. Unlike profinite completion, only finite p-groups are used.
