Fuchsian linear differential system (F′=A(z)F,A(z)=R/(z−a)+H(z))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Linear differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 224 Articles: 2
In a fixed frame, a first-order linear differential equation is Fuchsian at a finite point when its coefficient matrix has at most a simple pole there. Its residue is R. The global object is the matrix-valued one-form A(z)dz: in the coordinate t=1/z its coefficient is −t−2A(1/t). A Fuchsian system has intrinsic regular singular points, but an intrinsically regular singular system may have higher-order coefficient poles in a different meromorphic frame.
