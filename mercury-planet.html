Mercury (planet)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planet
OurBigBook.com Words: 82 Articles: 5
The innermost planet has a large metallic core and a weak but global intrinsic magnetic field. Its observed field supports an active planetary dynamo in a fluid conducting part of that core. Slow rotation does not by itself exclude dynamo action.
