Percolation critical probability (pc​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Percolation theory Percolation probability
OurBigBook.com Words: 824 Articles: 7
For the rooted infinite model defining percolation probability, the percolation critical probability is
pc​=inf{p:θ(p)>0}.
(43)
Below pc​, the root has probability zero of belonging to an infinite percolation cluster. Above pc​, that probability is positive, by the definition and the monotone coupling of Bernoulli percolation. The definition alone does not determine behavior at pc​, or the numerical value of pc​: these require results specific to the graph and the model.
A finite graph with M edges has 2M open/closed configurations. The probability of any connectivity event is a finite sum of terms pk(1−p)M−k, hence a polynomial in p. The square's 2p2−p4 is an example. These finite probabilities vary smoothly, although an infinite-system limit can distinguish a whole interval with θ(p)=0 from one with θ(p)>0. A steep finite connection curve and a mathematically proved percolation critical probability are different kinds of information.
