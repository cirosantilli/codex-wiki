Intersection parameters from scalar triple products (s=[a,b,n]/[a,m,n],t=[a,b,m]/[a,m,n])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Scalar triple product
OurBigBook.com Words: 101
Consider lines r=a+sm and r=b+tn with nonzero direction vectors. If they intersect, then a−b=tn−sm, so the necessary coplanarity condition is [a−b,m,n]=0. For nonparallel directions it is also sufficient, since the orthogonal complement of m×n is the linear span of m,n. For parallel directions the condition alone is insufficient: distinct parallel lines also satisfy it.
Assume intersection and D=[a,m,n]=0. Take the scalar triple products [a,b,n] and [a,b,m] of b=a+sm−tn. The scalar triple product is an alternating multilinear map, giving respectively sD and tD, proving the title formulas. The nonzero denominator fixes a convenient determinant chart; intersections with D=0 can still exist and require a different auxiliary vector or another method.
