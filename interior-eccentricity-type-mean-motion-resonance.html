Interior eccentricity-type mean-motion resonance

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Orbital resonance Mean-motion resonance
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For an eccentric inner body and circular outer perturber near the reduced (m+n):m period ratio, the leading resonant argument is ϕ=(m+n)λout​−mλin​−nϖin​. Its disturbing function amplitude is of order einn​. At a mean conjunction, ϕ=n(Λ−ϖin​); at actual alignment the exact expression is (m+n)x−my with x=fin​ and y=Min​(x).
