Parametrize the data by (ξ,η,ζ)=(cosθ,sinθ,−1). The determinant testing Noncharacteristic Cauchy data is
det(−sinθcosθ​cosθsinθsin2θ​)=−sinθ.
(35)
Thus the open upper and lower semicircles are noncharacteristic, but the entire circle is not a valid noncharacteristic data curve: the determinant vanishes at (1,0) and (−1,0).
There is a stronger obstruction at those two points. On y=0, the differential equation forces 0=−z4/3, and hence z=0. This contradicts the prescribed value z=−1 at both endpoints. Therefore
No classical solution satisfies the data on the entire circle.​
(36)
The obstruction is already present if the equation is imposed next to the circle and the solution and its first partial derivatives extend continuously to it.
Away from the endpoints, the characteristic projections are radial half-lines:
(x,y)=r(cosθ,sinθ),r=1−ssinθ1​>0.
(37)
For sinθ>0, r increases with s; for sinθ<0, r decreases with s. Each projection meets its own open semicircle exactly once and never crosses y=0. The endpoint projections themselves are single stationary points, although their lifted characteristic curves have varying height.
