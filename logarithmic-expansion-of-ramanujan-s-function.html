Logarithmic expansion of Ramanujan's function (N(t)∼∑n≥0​cn​(logt)−n−1,∑n≥0​cn​zn/n!=1/Γ(1−z))

The Gamma reflection formula gives (sinπz/π)Γ(z)=1/Γ(1−z), analytic at zero with value one. A finite-parameter Watson's lemma representation of Ramanujan's function therefore gives the displayed coefficients. In particular c0​=1, c1​=−γ and c2​=γ2−π2/6, with γ Euler's constant. Obtaining this representation requires taking an imaginary part before sending its Mellin exponent to zero; the original complex integral is divergent at that boundary.
