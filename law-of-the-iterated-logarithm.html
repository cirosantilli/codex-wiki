Law of the iterated logarithm

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Convergence of random variables Almost sure convergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 3
For independent identically distributed centered variables of variance one under standard moment assumptions, the law of the iterated logarithm describes the almost-sure envelope of their partial sums on the scale 2nloglogn​.
