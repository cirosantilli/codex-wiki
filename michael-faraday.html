Michael Faraday (1791–1867)

 Home Chemistry Chemist List of chemists
OurBigBook.com Words: 48 Articles: 1
A chemist whose route into experimental research began with a bookbinding apprenticeship (1805–1812). He became a laboratory assistant at the Royal Institution in 1813, its laboratory director in 1825, and Fullerian Professor of chemistry from 1833 to 1867.[ref]
