For λ⊢n with n≥2,
⟨χλχλ,χ(n−1,1)⟩=∣λ−∣−1.
(222)
Indeed, adjoining the trivial character to the standard character gives the point-permutation character, and Frobenius reciprocity turns its multiplicity in the self-product into the norm of the multiplicity-free restriction.
