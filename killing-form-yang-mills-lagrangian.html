Killing-form Yang-Mills Lagrangian (LYM​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Yang-Mills theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For a real compact semisimple algebra, B=−κ is a positive internal metric. With signature (+−−−) and connection Dμ​=∂μ​+Aμ​, the healthy Lagrangian is L=−B(Fμν​,Fμν)/(4g2)=κ(Fμν​,Fμν)/(4g2). Gauge invariance follows from Killing-form invariance and δX​F=ϵ[X,F]. The physical Hamiltonian density is [B(Ei​,Ei​)+B(Bi​,Bi​)]/(2g2) after imposing the Gauss law constraint in gauge theory and treating the spatial boundary flux appropriately. An Abelian factor needs a separate positive invariant metric because its Killing form vanishes.
