Sachs-Wolfe plateau (ℓ(ℓ+1)Cℓ​=constant)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Cosmic microwave background power spectrum
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For a scale-invariant potential spectrum with AΦ​=k3PΦ​/(2π2) constant, the matter-era Sachs-Wolfe effect gives Cℓ​=(4πAΦ​/9)∫jℓ​(x)2dx/x. The logarithmic spherical Bessel square integral then gives ℓ(ℓ+1)Cℓ​=2πAΦ​/9. This plateau applies to large-angle temperature anisotropies, neglecting acoustic evolution and late evolving potentials; it is not a constant Cℓ​ spectrum.
