Reciprocal effective mass tensor ((M−1)ij​=ℏ−2∂ki​​∂kj​​E)

The curvature tensor of a nondegenerate energy band, determining the linear relation between force and acceleration in semiclassical band dynamics. From group velocity vi​=ℏ−1∂ki​​E and ℏk˙j​=Fj​, one obtains v˙i​=(M−1)ij​Fj​. Near a parabolic minimum, E−E0​=ℏ2δkTM−1δk/2. The principal values are reciprocal curvature effective masses; a cyclotron experiment measures a direction-dependent combination rather than every tensor entry separately.
