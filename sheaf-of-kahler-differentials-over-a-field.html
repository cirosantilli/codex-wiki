Sheaf of Kähler differentials over a field (ΩX/k1​)

 ... Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Kähler differential Sheaf of relative Kähler differentials
OurBigBook.com Words: 138 Articles: 2
For a variety over k, the module of Kähler differentials ΩA/k​ on each affine chart gives a quasi-coherent sheaf. Compatibility with localization glues these into ΩX/k1​. A finite presentation of an algebra gives a finite presentation of a module for its differentials, so this is a coherent sheaf on a variety.
