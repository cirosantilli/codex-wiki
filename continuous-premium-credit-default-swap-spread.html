Assume deterministic discount factors, deterministic risk-neutral default intensity λ, fixed fractional recovery R, and continuous premium until default or maturity. Survival is G(t)=exp(−∫0t​λ(u)du). Expected discounted protection is N(1−R)∫BλG, while premium is Ns∫BG. Equating them gives the par spread. Constant intensity gives s=(1−R)λ. Discrete premiums, default accrual, stochastic recovery and counterparty losses require changing the corresponding cash-flow expectations.
