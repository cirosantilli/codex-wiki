Scalar-field kink

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field-theory soliton
OurBigBook.com Words: 614 Articles: 10
A scalar-field kink is a one-dimensional finite-energy static solution approaching different vacua as x→−∞ and x→+∞. Its boundary values define a topological sector.
