Normalization of nonzero dynamo coupling coefficients (c1​=1,∣c3​∣=1)

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Dynamo action Equivariant three-mode dynamo normal form
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For nonzero coefficients of A∗V in B˙ and AB in V˙, a relative phase rotation makes c1​ real positive. Positive rescalings of A,V,B then normalize both magnitudes to one. The phase of c1​c3​ is invariant under these rescalings and remains as the phase of normalized c3​. Zero couplings cannot be normalized to one; they are degenerate parameter cases.
