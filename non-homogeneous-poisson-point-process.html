Non-homogeneous Poisson point process

OurBigBook.com Words: 50 Articles: 1
Let λ be a nonnegative measurable intensity function on a space with reference measure dx. A Non-homogeneous Poisson point process has independent counts on disjoint measurable sets and
N(A)∼Poisson(∫A​λ(x)dx)
(52)
whenever the integral is finite.
