Constant nonzero drift is impossible on a finite Markov chain (Pf−f≡−δ(δ>0))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Finite Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For a real function f on a finite state space, a state minimizing f satisfies Pf≥f, so strictly negative drift cannot hold at every state. Equivalently, telescoping the expected value would give Ex​f(Xn​)=f(x)−nδ, contradicting boundedness of f. Constant negative drift is useful before a target is reached; it must be restricted to the states outside that target.
