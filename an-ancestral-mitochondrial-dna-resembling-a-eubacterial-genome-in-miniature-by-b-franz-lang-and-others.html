Published in Nature.
Online text: www.nature.com/articles/387493a0
Full article paywalled. No detailed sequencing methods, contamination controls, raw sequence alignments or trees claimed inspected. Article text explicitly states this access limit; not presented as a paper review. Its 1997 closest-rickettsial-relative claim and obsolete host classification are not repeated. No modern species described as literal ancestor.
