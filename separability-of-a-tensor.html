Separability of a tensor (t=v⊗w)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor product
OurBigBook.com Words: 194
In a two-factor tensor product over a field, a pure tensor is also called a separable or decomposable tensor: it can be expressed as one product v⊗w. Every tensor is a finite sum of pure tensors, but a sum need not itself be a pure tensor.[ref]
For finite-dimensional vector spaces, the tensor-product coefficient matrix gives a decisive test: a nonzero tensor is a pure tensor exactly when its matrix rank is one. Indeed, every column of vwT is a scalar multiple of v. Conversely, if all columns of a nonzero matrix are multiples of one nonzero column v, collect their multipliers in w to obtain C=vwT. The zero tensor is also a pure tensor, for example 0⊗w, but its matrix rank is zero.
