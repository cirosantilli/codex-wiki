Boundary-intersection threshold for SLE (κ>4)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Schramm–Loewner evolution Phase classification of the SLE trace
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
Chordal SLEκ​ intersects the domain boundary away from its endpoints exactly when κ>4. The centered image of a boundary point is a Bessel process of dimension 1+4/κ, which hits zero exactly below dimension two.
