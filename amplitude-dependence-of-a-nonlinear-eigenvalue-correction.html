Amplitude dependence of a nonlinear eigenvalue correction (ν(C)=Cp−1ν(1))

Suppose a real simple leading eigenfunction is y0​=Cϕ and a first-order correction satisfies L0​v=−νwy0​−y0p​, where p≥2 is an integer, L0​ is self-adjoint with null space spanned by ϕ, the boundary conditions are homogeneous and independent of the parameter, and w>0. The Fredholm solvability condition gives
ν(C)=−Cp−1∫wϕ2dx∫ϕp+1dx​.
(21)
Indeed, orthogonality of the forcing to ϕ gives νC∫wϕ2+Cp∫ϕp+1=0, and C=0 permits division. Thus the numerical eigenvalue correction depends on the chosen leading amplitude; fixing an eigenfunction normalization is part of defining the nonlinear branch. This calculation assumes a perturbative branch and does not by itself establish that branch's existence.
