For Brownian motion and deterministic partitions of an interval [0,t] whose mesh ∣π∣ tends to zero, the displayed limit holds in L2 space. Split the error into Uπ​=∑j​Btj​​((ΔBj​)2−Δtj​) and the Riemann sum error Vπ​=∑j​Btj​​Δtj​−∫0t​Bs​ds. The summands of Uπ​ are martingale differences, hence orthogonal in L2 space, and the Gaussian fourth moment gives
EUπ2​=2∑j​tj​(Δtj​)2≤2t2∣π∣.

The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality and the Brownian increment variance give EVπ2​≤(t/2)∑j​(Δtj​)2≤t2∣π∣/2. Both vanish. This proves the limit directly without a stochastic change-of-variables formula.
