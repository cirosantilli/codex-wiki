Route congestion price (pr​=(ATμ)r​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The total price seen by a route is the sum of resource congestion prices on its links. With weighted logarithmic utility wr​logxr​, maximizing utility minus linear expenditure gives the demand xr​=wr​/pr​. This relation links user decisions to multiplicative resource-price dynamics.
