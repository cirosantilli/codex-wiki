Bounded distortion of continued-fraction cylinders (h(y)=(pn​+pn−1​y)/(qn​+qn−1​y))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Diophantine approximation Continued fraction Gauss continued-fraction map
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
The inverse branch of the nth iterate of the Gauss continued-fraction map on a fixed digit cylinder has the displayed form, with continued fraction convergents pj​/qj​. Its derivative has absolute value (qn​+qn−1​y)−2. Since qn−1​≤qn​, the maximum-to-minimum derivative ratio is at most four. The density of the Gauss measure changes this ratio by at most a further factor two. The cylinders shrink to irrational points, so this uniform distortion bound and the Lebesgue density theorem prove ergodicity of the map.
