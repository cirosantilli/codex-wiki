Extrapolation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Interpolation
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
Estimating values beyond the range of the supplied sample points. For two distinct points P,Q, the affine combination (1−t)P+tQ lies beyond an endpoint of their line segment when t<0 or t>1. The construction specifies an extension of the chosen model; it does not show that an unknown underlying function follows that extension.
