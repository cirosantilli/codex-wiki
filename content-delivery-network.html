Content delivery network

 Home Computer science Computer network Internet
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
A content delivery network uses delivery infrastructure, often at multiple locations, to supply content and direct requests to suitable delivery endpoints. It can retain objects through caching or retrieve them from an origin. Distribution across sites does not imply that those sites have independent administrative control.[ref]
