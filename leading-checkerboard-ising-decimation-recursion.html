Leading checkerboard Ising decimation recursion (K′=L+2K2,L′=K2)

 ... Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Renormalization group Real-space renormalization group Spin decimation Checkerboard decimation of the square-lattice Ising model
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
For dimensionless nearest-neighbour coupling K and diagonal coupling L, the second cumulant in checkerboard decimation of the square-lattice Ising model generates K2 for each pair of retained neighbours of an eliminated centre. A retained diagonal pair shares two centres, and an axial pair at distance 2a shares one. The displayed two-coupling recursion neglects K4, K2L, and L2, treating L as order K2. Its finite nontrivial renormalization-group fixed point is (K,L)=(1/3,1/9). Higher orders generate additional operators, so the closure is a property of the truncation.
