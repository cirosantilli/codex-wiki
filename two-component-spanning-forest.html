Two-component spanning forest (∣F∣=∣V∣−2)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Tree (graph theory) Forest
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
A two-component spanning forest of a finite connected graph is an acyclic spanning subgraph with exactly two connected components of a graph. It has ∣V∣−2 edges. For distinct terminals a,z, a separating two-component forest places them in different components. Deleting an edge of the a-to-z graph path of a spanning tree produces such a forest; adjoining any original edge across its cut reverses this operation. This is the counting correspondence behind the mean spanning-tree path current.
