Exoplanet transit photometry

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Exoplanet atmospheric observatory
OurBigBook.com Words: 531 Articles: 9
Exoplanet transit photometry measures the stellar flux decrement as an exoplanet crosses the visible stellar disc. After accounting for stellar intensity variation and transit geometry, its depth is approximately (Rp​/R⋆​)2, determining a radius ratio rather than an absolute planetary radius.
