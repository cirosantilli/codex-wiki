Conducting hemispherical protrusion in a uniform field (Φ=E0​z(1−a3/r3))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrostatics Conductor in electrostatic equilibrium Induced charge on a conducting sphere in a uniform electric field
OurBigBook.com Words: 108
A grounded hemispherical boss of radius a on the grounded plane z=0 in an otherwise uniform downward electric field has the displayed exterior electric potential, with r2=x2+y2+z2. It vanishes both on the hemisphere r=a and on the surrounding plane, and tends to E0​z at large distance. The outward normal field gives surface charge −3ε0​E0​cosθ on the hemisphere and −ε0​E0​(1−a3/s3) on the plane at cylindrical radius s>a. The apex field is 3E0​, so a field-limited breakdown voltage is reduced by a factor three. For a capacitor with finite spacing d≫a, this is a leading local approximation; it is not an exact solution on the distant second plate.
