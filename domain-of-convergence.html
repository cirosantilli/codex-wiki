Domain of convergence

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Power series
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The domain of convergence of a power series is the set of inputs where the series has a finite sum. Inside its radius of convergence the series has absolute convergence, and outside it diverges. At a finite positive radius the real domain may include either, both, or neither endpoint; the radius of convergence alone does not decide endpoint convergence.
