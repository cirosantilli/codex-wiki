Saturated multiplicative subset (ab∈S⟹a,b∈S)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Localization of a ring Multiplicatively closed set
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 2
A multiplicative subset is saturated if every factor of a product in the subset also lies in it. We include 1 in a multiplicative subset. A saturated subset containing 0 is the whole ring. The units form a saturated subset in a commutative ring.
