Chern-Simons 5-form (Q5​(A)=Tr(AF2−21​FA3+101​A5))

For a matrix-valued gauge field A, let F=dA+A∧A be its gauge field strength; products below combine matrix multiplication with the wedge product of differential forms. The five-form can be written Q5​=Tr(A(dA)2+23​A3dA+53​A5). Applying the exterior derivative, using its graded product rule and the graded cyclic property of the matrix trace, gives dQ5​=Tr(F3). The six-form is an invariant polynomial in the gauge curvature, while its local potential depends on the connection.
