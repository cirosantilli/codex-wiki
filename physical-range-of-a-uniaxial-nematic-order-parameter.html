Physical range of a uniaxial nematic order parameter (−21​≤Q≤1)

 ... Critical phenomenon Order parameter Liquid crystal Nematic liquid crystal Nematic order parameter Uniaxial nematic order
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For S=⟨3ssT−I⟩=Q(3nnT−I) and unit molecular orientations s, contraction with n gives 2Q=3⟨(s⋅n)2⟩−1. Since the squared projection is between zero and one, the displayed bounds follow. The eigenvalues in this normalization are 2Q,−Q,−Q. A differently normalized nematic order parameter has correspondingly rescaled bounds; its numerical coefficients cannot be transferred unchanged.
