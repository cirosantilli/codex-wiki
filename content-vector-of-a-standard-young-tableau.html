Content vector of a standard Young tableau (cT​)

 ... Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young tableau Standard Young tableau
OurBigBook.com Words: 123 Articles: 1
The content vector of a standard Young tableau T is (cT​(1),…,cT​(n)), where cT​(r) is the Content of a Young-diagram cell containing r. Such vectors are exactly the integer vectors satisfying: the first coordinate is zero; each subsequent coordinate has an earlier neighbor differing by one; between two occurrences of a both a−1 and a+1 occur. To reconstruct the tableau, insert each entry in the next available cell on its prescribed diagonal. The neighbor conditions supply its required predecessors, so each insertion is an addable node of a Young diagram.
