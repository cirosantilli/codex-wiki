Absolutely continuous representative along free characteristics (F(t)=C+∫0t​G(s)ds)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation Free transport equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A distributional weak solution of ∂t​f+v⋅∇x​f=g, with locally integrable f,g, becomes ∂t​F=G after the shear F(t,x,v)=f(t,x+tv,v). For almost every characteristic label, there is an absolutely continuous function representing F, and its increments are the integrals of G for every pair of times. Arbitrary modifications on time slices can destroy this every-time identity without changing the distributional solution.
