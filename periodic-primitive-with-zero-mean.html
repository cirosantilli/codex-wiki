For h∈L1(0,1) with integral zero, Ph is the unique absolutely continuous function with (Ph)′=−h, equal endpoint values, and integral zero. The first formula gives the derivative; zero integral of h gives equal endpoint values, while the added constant makes the mean zero. Uniqueness follows because the difference of two such primitives has zero weak derivative and therefore is constant, then its mean is zero. Its periodic extension has distributional derivative −h, with no endpoint Dirac delta distribution.
