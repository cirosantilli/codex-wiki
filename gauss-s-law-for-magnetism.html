Gauss's law for magnetism

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Maxwell equations
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 2
Gauss's law for magnetism is ∇⋅B=0. Its integral form says that the net magnetic flux through every closed surface vanishes, so the normal magnetic field is continuous across an ordinary interface.
