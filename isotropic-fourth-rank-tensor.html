Isotropic fourth-rank tensor (cijkl​=αδij​δkl​+βδik​δjl​+γδil​δjk​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor Isotropic tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 115
Every three-dimensional fourth-rank Cartesian tensor invariant under all proper rotations has the displayed form, without assuming index-interchange symmetry. Half-turns about the coordinate axes force every nonzero component to contain each coordinate label an even number of times. The surviving patterns have four equal labels or two equal pairs. Axis permutations identify the latter with c1122​=α, c1212​=β and c1221​=γ, while all four-equal components share a value A.
Evaluate the component with four copies of (e1​+e2​)/2​. Multilinearity gives (A+α+β+γ)/2, which must equal A by rotational invariance. Hence A=α+β+γ, proving the formula. Conversely each pairing of Kronecker deltas is invariant under any orthogonal transformation. For symmetric strain only α and β+γ matter, giving the two Lamé parameters of isotropic elasticity on tensor components.
