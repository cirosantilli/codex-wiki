Conjugate global relations for the modified Helmholtz equation (G​(λ)=G(λ)​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Boundary value problem Global relation for a linear boundary value problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 270 Articles: 4
The two modified Helmholtz adjoint plane waves vλ​ and vλ​=vλ​​ give boundary identities G(λ)=0 and G​(λ)=0. For real solution traces, G​(λ)=G(λ)​. Reality is needed for this conjugation argument; the parametrization separately yields the reciprocal identity G​(λ)=G(1/λ).
