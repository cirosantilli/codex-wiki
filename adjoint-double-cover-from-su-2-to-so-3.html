Adjoint double cover from SU(2) to SO(3) (SU(2)→SO(3))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Adjoint representation of a Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
Conjugation by SU(2) on its three-dimensional real Lie algebra of traceless skew-Hermitian matrices preserves −tr(AB) and defines a surjection
SU(2)⟶SO(3)
(8)
with kernel {±I2​}. It is the universal double covering of SO(3).
