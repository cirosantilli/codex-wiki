Integer division

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Division (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 355 Articles: 3
Division of integers that returns an integer quotient rather than an exact fraction. A rounding convention is required for negative operands. For n≥0 and d>0, the quotient is q=⌊n/d⌋ and the remainder is r=n−qd with 0≤r<d.
