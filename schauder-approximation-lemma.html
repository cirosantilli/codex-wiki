Schauder approximation lemma (supx∈K​∥T(x)−Tε​(x)∥≤ε)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fixed-point theorem Schauder fixed-point theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
Let K be a nonempty compact convex set in a Banach space and T:K→K a continuous map. For every ε>0, there is a finite-dimensional linear subspace Lε​ and a continuous map Tε​:K→K∩Lε​ that approximates T uniformly to within ε in norm.
Choose y1​,…,yN​∈T(K) whose open radius-ε balls cover the compact image T(K). Set wi​(x)=max{0,ε−∥Tx−yi​∥} and
Tε​(x)=∑i​wi​(x)∑i​wi​(x)yi​​.
(149)
The denominator is positive. This convex combination belongs to K∩span{y1​,…,yN​} and depends continuously on x. Every contributing yi​ has distance less than ε from Tx, so the triangle inequality gives ∥Tε​(x)−Tx∥<ε.
