Tex unit (1tex=10−6kg/m)

 Home Engineering Materials science Textile linear density
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
A unit of mass per length, equal to 1g/km. Force divided by a linear density stated in tex gives tensile tenacity in newtons per tex.
