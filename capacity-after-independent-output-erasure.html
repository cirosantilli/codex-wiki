Capacity after independent output erasure (Cerased​=(1−p)C)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Channel capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
Pass the output of a discrete memoryless channel of channel capacity C through an independent finite-alphabet erasure channel with erasure probability p. Its capacity becomes (1−p)C.
To prove it, let X be the original input, Y the original output, E the erasure indicator, and Z the final output. Since the erasure marker is distinguishable, E is determined by Z. It is independent of X,Y, so the chain rule for mutual information gives I(X;Z)=I(X;E,Z)=I(X;Z∣E)=(1−p)I(X;Y). Maximization over the same input distributions proves the formula. For p<1 the maximizing distributions are exactly the original channel's maximizers. For p=1, every input distribution attains the zero capacity. Input-dependent erasure need not satisfy this scaling identity.
