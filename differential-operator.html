Differential operator

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 578 Articles: 10
A differential operator maps a function to an expression depending on finitely many of its derivatives. A linear differential operator has the form P=∑∣α∣≤m​aα​(x)∂α; its order is the largest derivative order with nonzero coefficient. The Laplacian is a second-order example, and boundary conditions specify the domain when defining an operator on a function space.
