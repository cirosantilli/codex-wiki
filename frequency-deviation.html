Frequency deviation (Δf=maxt​∣fi​(t)−fc​∣)

 Home Electrical engineering Frequency modulation
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
The peak frequency deviation is the largest absolute value of the instantaneous frequency offset from the chosen carrier wave frequency fc​. For fi​=fc​+Kf​Mcos(2πfm​t), it is Δf=∣Kf​∣M. It is a frequency, measured in hertz, rather than a wave phase or an exact signal bandwidth.
