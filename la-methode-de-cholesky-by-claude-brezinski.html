La méthode de Cholesky by Claude Brezinski (2005 journal publication)

 Home Mathematics Mathematics bibliography
OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by Claude Brezinski Words: 143
Historical research article by Claude Brezinski, published in Revue d’histoire des mathématiques 11 (2005), pp. 205–238. Numdam hosts the original journal article. The printed received date, 4 November 2004, and revised date, 11 May 2005, are distinct from publication.
Original article: www.numdam.org/item/RHM_2005__11_2_205_0.pdf
Section 4 reproduces a primary manuscript authored by André-Louis Cholesky and dated Vincennes, 2 December 1910. Cholesky is the author of that manuscript; Brezinski is the author of the surrounding 2005 article. The manuscript date does not establish an exact invention date or a broad priority claim.
Cited passages: pp. 210–211: constrained geodetic corrections and the matrix MMT; p. 217: manuscript title, 2 December 1910 date and uncertainty about the exact invention date; §4, p. 229: the primary manuscript’s reported 56-equation altitude adjustment for Algerian triangulation and closing date. The primary manuscript transcription was inspected as text; no inspection of handwriting images is claimed.
