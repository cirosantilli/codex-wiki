Parent involvement in youth anxiety treatment

OurBigBook.com Words: 216 Articles: 3
Caregivers may facilitate access and practice, receive information, change accommodation, or receive treatment for their own difficulties. These roles imply different mechanisms and should be compared against the same adequate child treatment when testing augmentation. Parent-only treatment and adding parent sessions to child treatment answer different questions.[ref]
