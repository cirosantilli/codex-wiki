Two-dimensional potential doublet (W(z)=M/(z−b))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Potential flow Complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
A two-dimensional potential doublet is a localized potential flow singularity with complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow W=M/(z−b), where M=meiα specifies strength and orientation. It is the limiting field of an equal point source and sink as their separation tends to zero with strength times separation fixed, with the sign absorbed in M. Its complex velocity is −M/(z−b)2, so the velocity decays as inverse distance squared. The total volumetric flow rate around the singularity is zero. Its two-dimensional decay differs from that of the three-dimensional source dipole.
