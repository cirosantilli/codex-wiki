For bt​=db​bxx​−χb​∂x​[b(1+n)−2nx​]+b(1−b) and nt​=dn​nxx​+λb2/(b02​+b2)−μbn, the positive homogeneous equilibrium is (1,n∗​), where n∗​=λ/[μ(1+b02​)]. Set κ=χb​/(1+n∗​)2 and η=λ(b02​−1)/(1+b02​)2. The linear stability matrix at squared wavenumber q is (−1−db​qη​κq−μ−dn​q​). Its trace is negative and its determinant is db​dn​q2+(dn​+μdb​−κη)q+μ. Minimizing this quadratic polynomial over q>0 proves the displayed continuous-wavenumber growth criterion. It requires b0​>1. On a bounded domain an actual permitted nonzero Fourier mode must lie strictly inside the negative-determinant interval. This is a criterion for growth of infinitesimal perturbations, not a general exclusion of finite-amplitude patterns.
