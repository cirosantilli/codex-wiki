Gene regulatory network

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 21
A network describing how gene products affect the production of other gene products. Positive and negative regulatory interactions can generate multistability, oscillations, and noise-sensitive transitions.
