Orbit-cone correspondence

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Toric geometry Toric variety
OurBigBook.com Words: 67 Articles: 2
The orbit-cone correspondence assigns to every cone σ∈Σ a torus orbit of codimension dimσ. In particular, maximal cones correspond to torus-fixed points.
