Voter-model duality (ξt​(x)=ξ0​(Xtx​))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Interacting particle system Voter model
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 2
Independent Poisson process copying arrows construct the voter model. A backward ancestral line from a site follows a continuous-time random walk; lines that meet then coincide. Thus a finite collection of Ising spins at time t equals initial Ising spins at the positions of coalescing random walks. In particular Eη​ξt​(x)=∑z​pt​(x,z)η(z), and the probability of disagreement at two sites is bounded by the probability their ancestral walks have not yet coalesced.
