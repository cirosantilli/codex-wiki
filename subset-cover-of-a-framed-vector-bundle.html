Subset cover of a framed vector bundle (Xk​≅X×(k{1,…,n}​))

 ... Fiber bundle Vector bundle Sphere bundle Unit tangent bundle Stiefel manifold Unordered Stiefel bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A global ordered framing selects the displayed (kn​)-sheeted cover inside the unordered Stiefel bundle. It is a trivial cover because the frame labels are global. Selecting subsets of a direct-sum framing decomposes the cover as the disjoint union of fiber products of the two summands' subset covers.
