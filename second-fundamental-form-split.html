Second fundamental form (II)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 90
For an embedded submanifold M⊆N of a Riemannian manifold, the vector-valued second fundamental form is
A(X,Y)=(∇X​Y)⊥.
(1)
Here ∇ is the ambient Levi-Civita connection and the superscript denotes projection onto the normal bundle. For a hypersurface in Euclidean space with unit normal N, the scalar form is II(X,Y)=⟨A(X,Y),N⟩=⟨−dN(X),Y⟩. In a surface parametrization its coefficients are e=N⋅Xuu​, f=N⋅Xuv​, and g=N⋅Xvv​.
