Multiwire proportional chamber

 Home Physics Particle detection Particle detector
OurBigBook.com Words: 833 Articles: 4
A multiwire proportional chamber is a proportional counter: parallel anode wires between cathodes in a gas. Charged particle ionization frees electrons; an electric field drives them toward wires, triggering an electron avalanche read by electronic amplifiers.[ref]
Georges Charpak's electronic readout avoided film scanning and full spark chamber discharge.[ref]
Opposite polarities identify the avalanche wire. Positive ion motion dominates integrated signal; detection precedes complete collection. Calibrated electron travel times enable drift chambers.[ref]
