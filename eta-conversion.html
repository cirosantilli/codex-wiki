Eta conversion (λx.Mx≡η​M(x∈/FV(M)))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Computability theory Lambda calculus Untyped lambda calculus
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
Eta conversion expresses extensionality for a lambda abstraction whose fresh argument is only passed to a function. The side condition prevents removal of a genuine dependency. In a categorical model with D≅DD, the inverse laws for currying and evaluation validate this conversion as well as beta reduction.
