Isentropic temperature-volume derivative ((∂T/∂V)S​=−(T/CV​)(∂p/∂T)V​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Maxwell relations
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For a simple compressible system at fixed particle number, assume the internal energy has continuous second partial derivatives and T is a locally invertible function of entropy at fixed volume. Its exact differential dU=TdS−pdV gives the Maxwell relation TV​∣S​=−pS​∣V​. Meanwhile the constant-volume heat capacity is CV​=TST​∣V​. The chain rule therefore gives
(∂V∂T​)S​=−(∂T∂p​)V​(∂S∂T​)V​=−CV​T​(∂T∂p​)V​.
(49)
For one mole of an ideal gas with constant molar CV​>0, this becomes TV​∣S​=−RT/(CV​V), whose integral curves satisfy TVR/CV​=constant. It expresses cooling on reversible adiabatic expansion in terms of measurable thermal and mechanical derivatives.
