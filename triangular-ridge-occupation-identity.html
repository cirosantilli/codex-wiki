Triangular ridge occupation identity (g(h)=2cotδ∫h∞​n(H)dH)

 ... Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Sea ice Sea-ice pressure ridge Exponential ridge-draft model
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For nonoverlapping triangular sea-ice pressure ridges with a common along-track side slope tanδ, a ridge whose peak exceeds h contributes 2cotδdh of track length in a draft interval dh. Summing over peaks proves the identity. Above the cutoff of an exponential ridge-draft model, g(h)=Ae−bh with A=2B/(btanδ). This draft occupation is not the normalized peak probability density function; level ice and shallower parts supply the remaining probability.
