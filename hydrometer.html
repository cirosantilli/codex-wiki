Hydrometer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 72 Articles: 1
A floating density instrument. At equilibrium, Archimedes' principle gives ρVdisplaced​=m for fixed instrument mass m, so it displaces more volume in a less dense liquid. A graduated narrow stem converts this volume change into a visible immersion reading. Calibration specifies a temperature and a reading convention for the meniscus; a sample's other dissolved substances can affect a sugar-calibrated scale.
