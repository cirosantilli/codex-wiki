FRET spectral overlap quantifies overlap between the FRET donor's emission and the FRET acceptor's absorption as functions of wavelength λ. With normalized donor emission fD​ and acceptor molar absorption coefficient εA​, one convention is
∫0∞​fD​(λ)dλ=1,J=∫0∞​fD​(λ)εA​(λ)λ4dλ.
(5)
Matching two emission colors is insufficient: the receiving absorption matters.[ref]
