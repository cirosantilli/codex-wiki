Open dust distance-redshift relation (r(z)=a0​H0​Ω2(1+z)2[Ωz+(2−Ω)(1−1+Ωz​)]​)

For an open dust-only Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric with zero cosmological constant and 0<Ω<1, H(z)=H0​(1+z)1+Ωz​. The present radial cosmological proper distance is dS​=∫0z​dz′/H(z′), and the areal radial coordinate satisfies a0​r=sinh[H0​1−Ω​dS​]/[H0​1−Ω​]. Integrating with v=1+Ωz​ gives the displayed relation. It has r∼z/(a0​H0​) near the observer and r→2/(a0​H0​Ω) as z→∞. The luminosity distance is (1+z)a0​r, not the radial cosmological proper distance.
