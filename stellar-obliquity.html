Stellar obliquity (ψ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planetary system dynamics Planetary migration
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 1
The stellar obliquity is the angle between a star's spin and a planet's orbital angular momentum. The Rossiter-McLaughlin effect constrains the angle λ between the sky projections of those two axes, rather than a component of ψ. If the orbital and stellar-spin inclinations to the line of sight are iorb​ and i∗​, then cosψ=cosi∗​cosiorb​+sini∗​siniorb​cosλ; λ alone generally does not determine ψ. Large misalignments constrain formation and planetary migration, but can also reflect a tilted birth disc.
