For an isotropic self-gravitating collisionless stellar system with k<3 and no central point mass, M(r)=4πAr3−k/(3−k). Integrating the Spherical Jeans equation to an outer radius b gives the displayed pressure, with Pb​=ρ(b)σr2​(b). For k<1 the inner pressure is set by outer radii and σr2​∼Crk, with C depending on the boundary. An unbounded pure power law has divergent pressure in this case. For 1<k<3, inner radii dominate and σr2​∼2πGAr2−k/[(3−k)(k−1)]. At k=1 a logarithm appears. For k≥3 the enclosed central mass diverges unless the density law is modified at small radii.
