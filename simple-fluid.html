Simple fluid

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
A simple fluid has local stress determined by the history of deformation of a material element, rather than by spatial derivatives of that history. An isotropic incompressible simple fluid has an arbitrary isotropic pressure and an objective constitutive functional of the relative deformation gradients. Internal-variable equations can represent such a functional when the initial material state is fixed by the past history.
