Lévy measure

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Lévy process
OurBigBook.com Words: 116 Articles: 1
The Lévy measure ν of a Lévy process records the intensity of its jumps: for every measurable set A bounded away from zero, ν(A) is the expected number per unit time of jumps whose sizes lie in A. It satisfies
ν({0})=0,∫Rd​(1∧∣x∣2)ν(dx)<∞.
