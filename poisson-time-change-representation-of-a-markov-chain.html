A jump process with event types k, increments νk​, and rates ak​(X) can be represented as X(t)=X(0)+∑k​νk​Pk​(∫0t​ak​(X(s−))ds), with independent unit-rate Poisson processes. This represents the Markov jump-process generator through time-changed counts.
