One-period quadratic hedge (π∗=V−1Cov(S1​,ξ))

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fundamental theorem of asset pricing Contingent claim Claim replication
OurBigBook.com Words: 506 Articles: 6
A one-period quadratic hedge minimizes E[(ξ−ϕR−π⋅S1​)2] over deterministic holdings, where R=0 is the riskless gross return, μ=ES1​, and the covariance matrix V is invertible. For a square-integrable claim, the unique solution is π∗=V−1Cov(S1​,ξ) and ϕ∗=(Eξ−π∗⋅μ)/R. Centering separates the squared bias from the quadratic error. Completing the square gives the excess error as (π−π∗)TV(π−π∗) after the bias is minimized. Unlike claim replication, the residual need not vanish.
