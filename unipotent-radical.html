Unipotent radical (Ru​(G))

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Linear algebraic group Unipotent Unipotent algebraic group
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
It is the largest connected normal unipotent subgroup of a linear algebraic group. A connected reductive algebraic group has trivial unipotent radical. The subgroup 1+J(E) of a finite-dimensional algebra's unit group is closed, connected and unipotent.
