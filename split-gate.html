Split gate

 ... Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Fermi-Dirac distribution Fermi gas Two-dimensional electron gas Quantum point contact
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Two surface electrodes whose depletion regions define a narrow conducting opening in an underlying two-dimensional electron gas. Gate voltage changes the transverse confinement and channel transmission without physically etching the conducting channel to its electrical width.
