Trispectrum

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Bispectrum
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The trispectrum is the Fourier transform of a fourth-order cumulant, a fourth-order counterpart of the bispectrum. It describes connected four-point correlations; the primordial trispectrum applies it to cosmological random fields.
