Surface conductivity (Σ)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Current density Surface current density
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For a thin layer with essentially uniform tangential electric field, integrating electrical conductivity through its thickness gives the sheet conductivity tensor σs​. The two-dimensional form of Ohm's law is K=σs​E∥​, with surface current density K. Its SI unit is S=A/V: amperes per metre divided by volts per metre. When response is the same in every in-plane direction, the sheet conductivity reduces to a scalar Σ; for uniform bulk electrical conductivity σ and thickness t, Σ=σt.
