Exponential sampling stopped by a data-dependent coin (L(θ;x)∝θNe−θ∑Xi​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For independent exponential observations of rate θ, stopping after observation x with known probability α(x)∈(0,1) gives a complete-data likelihood proportional to θNe−θS. Its minimal sufficient statistic is (N,S) by the parameter-free likelihood-ratio criterion. If only the last value Y is recorded, its density instead is α(y)e−θy/∫0∞​α(x)e−θxdx. The stopping rule therefore changes the coarsened-data inference even though it adds no complete-data likelihood factor involving θ.
