Regular antipodal triangulation of a sphere

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Simplicial complex
OurBigBook.com Words: 116 Articles: 1
A triangulation of Sk is regular in this antipodal-combinatorics convention when the antipodal map preserves its simplices and its nested coordinate equators S0⊂S1⊂⋯⊂Sk−1 are subcomplexes. The two closed hemispheres at each stage are then triangulated balls. Barycentric subdivision preserves these conditions while making the mesh of a simplicial complex arbitrarily small. This convention is distinct from other meanings of regular triangulation in polyhedral geometry. Cambridge extremal-combinatorics notes use this convention.
