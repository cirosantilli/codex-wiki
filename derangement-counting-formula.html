Derangement counting formula (!n=n!∑k=0n​k!(−1)k​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group action Transitive group action Derangement in a transitive group action
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Inclusion-exclusion applied to the events that a permutation fixes a specified point gives the displayed formula. There are (kn​) choices of k fixed points and (n−k)! permutations fixing them. The probability that a uniformly chosen permutation is a derangement tends to e−1.
