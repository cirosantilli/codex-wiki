Free viscous decay between parallel plates (u(y,t)=∑n≥1​bn​sin(nπy/H)e−ν(nπ/H)2t)

For a unidirectional incompressible flow between stationary plates a distance H>0 apart, removing the driving pressure gradient leaves ut​=νuyy​ when the kinematic viscosity ν>0 is constant. The no-slip boundary condition gives u(0,t)=u(H,t)=0. For an initial profile g in L2 space, separation of variables and the Fourier sine series give the displayed solution, with
bn​=H2​∫0H​g(y)sinHnπy​dy.
(1)
Each normal mode decays at a rate proportional to n2. The lowest mode present dominates at late times; when b1​=0, its time constant is H2/(νπ2), consistent with the viscous diffusion time. The solution approaches the initial data in L2 space; compatible smooth profiles also give the familiar pointwise initial condition. This is a heat equation for momentum diffusion, rather than temperature.
