Photon-photon pair production (E1​E2​(1−cosα)≥2me2​c4)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Quantum electrodynamics Pair production
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 1
Two photons meeting at angle α can produce an electron and a Positron when their combined four-momentum has sufficient invariant mass. For a head-on collision the threshold is E1​E2​=me2​c4. Threshold means zero relative momentum in the centre-of-momentum frame, not necessarily in the laboratory.
