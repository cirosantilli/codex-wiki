Travel-time coordinate for a one-dimensional variable-speed wave equation

OurBigBook.com Words: 62 Articles: 1
For a one-dimensional wave equation whose principal symbol is ξt2​−c(y)2ξy2​, a travel-time coordinate is
s(y)=∫yc(r)dr​.
(755)
Its two families of characteristic curves are s(y)−t=constant and s(y)+t=constant wherever the wave speed c is positive.
