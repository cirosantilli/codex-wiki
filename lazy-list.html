Lazy list

 Home Computer science Data structure Linked list
OurBigBook.com Words: 433 Articles: 6
A linked list whose future structure is obtained by calling a delayed computation. In Standard ML, unit -> node delays a computation until demand. Filtering may need to force arbitrarily many rejected elements to produce its next retained element; filtering an infinite list need not produce a result. Delay alone does not imply memoization.
