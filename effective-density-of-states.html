Effective density of states (Nc​,Nv​)

 Home Physics Semiconductor
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
The effective density of states of a parabolic semiconductor band is its density of states integrated against a thermal Boltzmann factor measured from its edge. For one spin-degenerate valley,
Nc​=2(2πℏ2me∗​kB​T​)3/2,Nv​=2(2πℏ2mh∗​kB​T​)3/2.
(2)
These quantities have units of number per volume, unlike the energy-resolved density of states. In a nondegenerate semiconductor, n=Nc​e−(Ec​−μ)/(kB​T) and p=Nv​e−(μ−Ev​)/(kB​T); multiple equivalent valleys add their contributions.
