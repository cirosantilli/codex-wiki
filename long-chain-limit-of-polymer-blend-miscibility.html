Long-chain limit of polymer-blend miscibility (χc​=21​(NA−1/2​+NB−1/2​)2)

 ... Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry) Polymer solution Flory–Huggins solution theory Binary Flory-Huggins polymer blend
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
Connecting segments into chains reduces their independent translational mixing entropy per segment by factors 1/NA​ and 1/NB​. Consequently the tolerable positive contact penalty tends to zero as both chains become long. Equal chain lengths have χc​=2/N; favorable unlike interactions can still produce a miscible polymer pair.
