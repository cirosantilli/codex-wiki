Binary parenthesization

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Lattice path Dyck path Catalan number
OurBigBook.com Words: 132
A binary parenthesization specifies the order of binary operations on a fixed ordered list of operands, combining exactly two existing expressions at each step. It has a full binary tree as its expression structure: operands are leaves and operations are internal vertices. For four operands a,b,c,d, the five possibilities are ((ab)c)d, (a(bc))d, (ab)(cd), a((bc)d), and a(b(cd)).
A binary parenthesization of n+1 operands contains n binary operations. At the last operation, its left expression can contain k operations and its right expression n−1−k. The Catalan recurrence therefore gives Cn​ possible evaluation structures. The order of operands stays fixed; this is not a count of their permutations. Even for an associative operation whose result is unchanged, different structures can have different evaluation costs, as in the matrix-chain multiplication problem.[ref]
