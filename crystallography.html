 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Crystal structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 39
Crystallography studies crystal structures and their symmetries. A structure determination connects measurements such as crystal scattering patterns to a model of the unit cell and its atomic basis of a crystal.
X-ray crystallographic structure inference makes that connection explicit: compare proposed crystal structures through the positions and wave intensities of their predicted reflections. The main difficulty is that a diffraction pattern encodes wave interference, so it must be interpreted through a model rather than read as a direct picture of the chemical atoms.
