Lower critical dimension (dl​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon
OurBigBook.com Words: 226 Articles: 5
The lower critical dimension is the spatial dimension at or below which fluctuations prevent the ordered phase associated with a proposed finite-temperature transition. A short-range model with discrete symmetry normally has dl​=1, whereas continuous O(2) symmetry has dl​=2.
