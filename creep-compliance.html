Creep compliance (J(p)=[p2G(p)]−1)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity Linear viscoelastic fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A small step of shear stress s0​H(t) produces shear strain γ(t)=s0​J(t) from a relaxed state. This time-dependent deformation under held stress is creep. The causal constitutive convolution gives s=pGγ​, proving the displayed Laplace transform relation. For a Maxwell fluid, J(t)=1/G0​+t/(G0​τ): an elastic jump followed by viscous flow.
