Conformal Killing algebra of Minkowski spacetime (dimconf(R1,3)=15)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Conformal Killing vector field
OurBigBook.com Words: 98
In Cartesian coordinates on four-dimensional Minkowski spacetime, all Conformal Killing vector fields have the form
Xμ=aμ+ωμν​xν+cxμ+2(b⋅x)xμ−x2bμ,ωμν​=−ωνμ​,x2=ημν​xμxν.
(9)
Their conformal Killing factor is c+2b⋅x. There are four translation generators, six Lorentz transformation generators, one dilation generator, and four special conformal transformation generators. These form a fifteen-dimensional Lie algebra under the Lie bracket of vector fields.
For completeness, differentiate the Conformal Killing equation in three index orders to obtain
∂μ​∂ν​Xρ​=ηνρ​∂μ​λ+ημρ​∂ν​λ−ημν​∂ρ​λ.
(10)
Its divergence gives 2∂μ​∂ν​λ=−ημν​□λ; its tensor contraction gives □λ=0. Hence λ is an affine function. Subtracting the displayed quadratic and dilation terms leaves a Killing vector field whose second partial derivatives vanish, proving completeness of the list. The vector fields are globally smooth, but some special conformal transformations have incomplete flows on affine Minkowski spacetime; the dilation flow x↦esx is global.
