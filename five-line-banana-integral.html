Five-line banana integral (B5​(p))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram Feynman integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
The two-point function Feynman integral with two interaction vertices joined by five parallel scalar propagators has four independent loop momenta:
B5​(p)=∫∏j=14​(2π)dddkj​​∏j=14​(kj2​+m2)[(p−∑j​kj​)2+m2]1​.
(5)
This formula uses Euclidean momenta. In three dimensions the convolution theorem and G(x)=e−m∣x∣/(4π∣x∣) give
B5​(p)=256π41​∫0∞​ρ−3e−5mρ∣p∣ρsin(∣p∣ρ)​dρ,
(6)
with cutoff regularization required at ρ=0. With a lower radial cutoff a, the coefficient of p2 has ultraviolet divergence −log(1/(ma))/(1536π4). It therefore generates a divergent wave-function renormalization in three-dimensional sextic scalar field theory. By contrast the three-line sunset diagram has only a momentum-independent logarithmic ultraviolet divergence in three dimensions. This illustrates why a superficial degree of divergence of two allows both a mass counterterm and a kinetic counterterm, while degree zero allows only the former in a two-point function.
