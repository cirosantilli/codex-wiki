Relative risk aversion coefficient (α(W)=−WU′′(W)/U′(W))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Utility function Risk aversion
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 1
For positive wealth and increasing twice differentiable utility function, this dimensionless coefficient measures sensitivity to proportional wealth risk. If it is constant, integration gives U′(W)=cW−α with c>0, and hence U(W)=cW1−α/(1−α)+d for α=1 or clogW+d for α=1. Positive, zero and negative coefficients correspond respectively to risk aversion, risk neutrality and risk seeking. The usual CRRA utility subclass takes α>0.
