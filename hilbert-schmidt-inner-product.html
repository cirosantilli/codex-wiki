Hilbert-Schmidt inner product (⟨A,B⟩HS​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Compact operator Hilbert-Schmidt operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 72 Articles: 2
For Hilbert-Schmidt operators A and B, the Hilbert-Schmidt inner product is ⟨A,B⟩HS​=tr(A∗B). Its induced norm is the Hilbert-Schmidt norm.
