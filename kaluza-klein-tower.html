Kaluza-Klein tower (mn2​=M2+n2/R2)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Compactification (physics) Kaluza-Klein mode
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 2
For a field periodic on a circle of radius R, a Fourier series gives modes with quantized internal momentum n/R. Each mode is a lower-dimensional field with the displayed squared mass. The entire sequence is the tower; retaining only modes below a chosen energy gives an effective field theory. The tower is distinct from any individual Kaluza-Klein mode.
