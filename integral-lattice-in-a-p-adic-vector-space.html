Integral lattice in a p-adic vector space (Λ⊗Zp​​Qp​=V)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field p-adic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
An integral lattice in a finite-dimensional Qp​-vector space is a free Zp​-submodule of full rank. Any two such lattices are commensurable: their intersection has finite index in each, because a sufficiently large power of p multiplies either into the other. A finite group acting on the space admits invariant lattices by taking a sum of translates. This allows Herbrand quotient comparisons via finite quotient modules.
