Instanton size modulus (ρ>0)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field-theory soliton Gauge-theory soliton Yang-Mills instanton Instanton moduli space
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
The scale ρ>0 in a BPST instanton changes the width of its action density while leaving total action unchanged. This reflects conformal invariance of four-dimensional classical Yang-Mills action. As ρ→0, gauge curvature concentrates at the center and the smooth family approaches a bubbling boundary, not another smooth zero-size instanton.
