Operator-valued distribution (ϕ(f)=∫ϕ(x)f(x)ddx)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Quantized field
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
An operator-valued distribution assigns a linear operator on a common dense domain to each smooth test function of compact support f, continuously and linearly in the appropriate weak sense. A quantized field is generally of this type: ϕ(x) is formal point notation, while the smeared ϕ(f) has operator meaning. Equal-time canonical commutation relations and momentum oscillators are interpreted after smearing, which avoids treating a Dirac delta distribution or a coincident field as a finite ordinary operator. Products at coincident points can need further regularization even when individual smeared fields are defined.
