Matrix similarity

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 4
Square matrices A and B are similar when B=P−1AP for some invertible matrix P. They represent the same linear operator in different bases and therefore have the same Jordan normal form.
