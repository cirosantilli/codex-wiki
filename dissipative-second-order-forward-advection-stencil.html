Dissipative second-order forward advection stencil (D+,2​=(−3I+4S−S2)/(2h))

OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For the equation ut​=ux​, the second-order forward difference has Fourier symbol [−(1−cosθ)2+isinθ(2−cosθ)]/h. The nonpositive real part gives a contractive semidiscrete evolution in the discrete L2 norm. Equivalently, for the unitary lattice shift S, Re⟨u,D+,2​u⟩=−∥(S−I)2u∥2/(4h). A centred difference in another direction contributes only a skew-adjoint term, preserving this energy estimate.
