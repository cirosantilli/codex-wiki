Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow

 Home Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Hydrodynamic stability
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 3
For a parallel inviscid base flow U(z) and disturbance streamfunction ϕ(z)eiα(x−ct), the Rayleigh equation is
(U−c)(ϕ′′−α2ϕ)−U′′ϕ=0.
