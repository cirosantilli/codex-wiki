Eustachian tube

 Home Biology Neuroscience Sensory system Hearing Middle ear
OurBigBook.com Words: 16
The Eustachian tube connects the middle ear to the nasopharynx, enabling pressure equalization, mucosal clearance and protection from nasopharyngeal contents.
