Center of a Lie algebra (Z(g))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 71 Articles: 1
Like the center of a group, the Lie-algebra center consists of elements commuting with every element; here commutation means vanishing of the Lie bracket.
The center is Z(g)={z:[z,x]=0 for every x∈g}.
