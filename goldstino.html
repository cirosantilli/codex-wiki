Goldstino

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supersymmetry breaking
OurBigBook.com Words: 167 Articles: 2
A goldstino is the massless spin-one-half mode associated with spontaneously broken global supersymmetry. For F-term breaking, the fermion in a multiplet with nonzero auxiliary field transforms by an inhomogeneous term, δG⊃2​ϵF. In supergravity, the corresponding mode supplies the longitudinal degrees of freedom of a massive gravitino.
