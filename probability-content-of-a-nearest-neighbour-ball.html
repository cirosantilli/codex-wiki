Probability content of a nearest neighbour ball (px​(ρ(k)​(x)))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Order statistic Nearest neighbour distance
OurBigBook.com Words: 68 Articles: 1
For independent and identically distributed random variables with continuous distance distribution function px​, the probability integral transform makes the probability content of the order-k nearest ball a uniform order statistic, with the Beta distribution Beta(k,n+1−k).
