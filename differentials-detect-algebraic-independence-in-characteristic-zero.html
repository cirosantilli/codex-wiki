Differentials detect algebraic independence in characteristic zero

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Field extension Algebraic independence Transcendence basis Separating transcendence basis
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For a finitely generated field extension L/k in characteristic zero, a tuple ti​ is algebraically independent exactly when its differentials dti​ are linearly independent in the Kähler differentials. A relation of minimum total degree gives a nontrivial differential relation; an independent tuple extends to a transcendence basis. In positive characteristic, derivatives of nonconstant relations can vanish identically.
