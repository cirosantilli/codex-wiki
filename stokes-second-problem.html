Stokes' second problem (u(x,t)=U0​e−x/δcos(ωt−x/δ),δ=2ν/ω​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 141 Articles: 1
Stokes' second problem describes the oscillatory shear flow generated by a tangentially oscillating flat plate in a semi-infinite viscous fluid flow. The velocity satisfies ut​=νuxx​, with u(0,t)=U0​cosωt and decay away from the plate. For ω>0, the displayed time-periodic solution follows by taking the real part of U0​eiωt−(1+i)x/δ. The length δ is the penetration depth: both amplitude attenuation and phase lag grow with x/δ. Boundary data alone also allow homogeneous transients; the periodic solution describes the established oscillation after those transients decay.
