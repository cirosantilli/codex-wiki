Inverse marginal utility (I=(U′)−1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Utility function
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
For an increasing differentiable strictly concave utility function satisfying the Inada conditions, the inverse marginal utility maps each positive shadow price to the unique positive payoff with that marginal utility. For normalized CRRA utility, I(y)=y−1/R.
