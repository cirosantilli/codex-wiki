Photon brightness perturbation (Δ=∫q3f1​dq/∫q3f0​dq=4Θ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Free-streaming photon Boltzmann equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 197 Articles: 3
The photon brightness perturbation is the fractional frequency-integrated directional energy perturbation. For a small blackbody temperature shift Θ=δT/T, f1​=−qf0′​(q)Θ. Integration by parts gives Δ=4Θ. Its direction average is the photon density contrast. This temperature identification assumes a perturbation of blackbody form; the integrated brightness definition itself is more general.
