Galvanometer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 10
A galvanometer detects electric current through a mechanical response, such as the deflection of a pointer.[ref]
A sensitive galvanometer makes a small electric current visible; a calibrated deflection also estimates its magnitude. A center-zero scale distinguishes both signs of electric current. The moving-coil galvanometer rotates a coil in an imposed magnetic field, whereas a moving-magnet galvanometer rotates a magnetic needle in the coil's magnetic field. The spring-balanced model below describes the moving-coil galvanometer, not every instrument called a galvanometer.
