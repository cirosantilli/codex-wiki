Local-type primordial non-Gaussianity (fNL​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Field theory in cosmology Primordial non-Gaussianity
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 3
A local quadratic departure from a Gaussian random field is
φ(x)=φG​(x)+fNL​(φG​(x)2−⟨φG2​⟩).
(68)
The subtraction removes the homogeneous mean. At leading order, Wick theorem gives the primordial bispectrum Bφ​=2fNL​[Pφ​(k1​)Pφ​(k2​)+cyclic terms]. The numerical convention depends on which potential or comoving curvature perturbation is used.
