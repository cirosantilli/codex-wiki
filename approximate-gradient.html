Approximate gradient (∇u)

 ... Analysis Inverse problem Regularization of an inverse problem Variational regularization Total variation seminorm on a domain Function of bounded variation on a domain
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A BV space function is approximately differentiable at almost every point with respect to Lebesgue measure. Its approximate gradient is the density of the absolutely continuous part of its distributional derivative. This does not imply membership in a Sobolev space.
