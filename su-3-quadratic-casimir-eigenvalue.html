SU(3) quadratic Casimir eigenvalue (C2​(a,b)=(a2+ab+b2+3a+3b)/3)

OurBigBook.com Words: 85
For generators normalized by tr3​(ta​tb​)=δab​/2, the quadratic Casimir operator on the irreducible SU(3) representation of Dynkin labels (a,b) has the displayed scalar value. In SU(3) highest-weight coordinates (p,q) it is p2+q2+p+3​q.
To derive this, write the operator as H12​+H22​+∑i=13​(E+i​E−i​+E−i​E+i​). On a highest-weight vector, the raising operators vanish, and the three root commutators sum to H1​+3​H2​. Centrality and the Schur lemma extend this value to the representation. The singlet, fundamental, adjoint and decuplet values are 0,4/3,3,6, respectively. Tensor labels and multiplet dimensions are summarized in the Particle Data Group's SU(n) multiplet guide.
