Time-dependent neutral-B CP asymmetry (Atag​(Δt)=Ssin(ΔmΔt)−Ccos(ΔmΔt))

With CPT symmetry, write the propagating states of neutral meson mixing as p∣B0⟩±q∣Bˉ0⟩, defining the coefficient ratio q/p. Let Δm be their mass splitting and use units ℏ=1.
For rates f±​ tagged as B0,Bˉ0 and Δt=tf​−ttag​, neglect the decay width difference and take ∣q/p∣=1. Define
λf​=pq​Af​Aˉf​​,S=1+∣λf​∣22Imλf​​,C=1+∣λf​∣21−∣λf​∣2​.
(61)
Here Af​,Aˉf​ are decay amplitudes. The tagged asymmetry is (f+​−f−​)/(f+​+f−​)=Ssin(ΔmΔt)−Ccos(ΔmΔt) in this convention.[ref]
For a single weak phase in a charmonium-kaon CP eigenstate with eigenvalue ηf​, λf​≃ηf​e−2iβ gives S=−ηf​sin2β, C≃0, with
β=arg(−Vtd​Vtb∗​Vcd​Vcb∗​​).
(62)
This connects the CKM unitarity triangle to quantum interference between direct decay and decay after neutral meson mixing.[ref]
For an independent teaching example choose x=ΔmΔt, λf​=4i/3 and an arbitrary common positive envelope F(Δt). Then S=24/25, C=−7/25 and
f±​=F(Δt)[1±(2524​sinx+257​cosx)].
(63)
Because (24/25)2+(7/25)2=1, both rates remain nonnegative. At x=0, the asymmetry is 7/25; at x=π/2, it is 24/25. These chosen values illustrate the sign convention and are not experimental estimates. More generally S2+C2=1−[2Reλf​/(1+∣λf​∣2)]2≤1.
If a tag flips independently with probability w, exchanging f+​ and f−​ for that fraction of events gives Aobserved​=(1−2w)Atag​ by direct subtraction. The plotted toy choice w=1/5 reduces the amplitude to 3/5 of its ideal value; at w=1/2 the tag contains no flavour information. Finite time resolution additionally averages over nearby oscillation phases. Extracting a weak phase consequently requires calibrated readout as well as a CKM matrix prediction.
