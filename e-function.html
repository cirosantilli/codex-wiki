E-function (f(z)=∑n≥0​an​zn/n!)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Diophantine approximation Transcendence theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 216 Articles: 2
In the modern strict sense an E-function satisfies a nonzero linear differential equation over Q​(z), has algebraic coefficients an​ with all conjugates bounded by Cn+1, and has common positive denominators dn​≤Cn+1 making dn​a0​,…,dn​an​ algebraic integers. Siegel's original arithmetic growth condition permits, for each ϵ>0, bounds of size Oϵ​(nϵn) instead. The strict and original growth formulations should be distinguished rather than asserted equivalent. Exponentials with algebraic exponent satisfy the stronger conditions and a first-order differential equation.
