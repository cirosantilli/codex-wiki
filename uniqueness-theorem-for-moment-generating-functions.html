Uniqueness theorem for moment-generating functions

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Moment-generating function
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
If two random vectors have moment-generating functions that are finite and equal on a neighborhood of the origin, then they have the same probability distribution.
