Integral Hecke algebra of level-one cusp forms (T⊆End(Sk​(Γ(1))))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Hecke operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 81 Articles: 1
This is the subring generated by the Hecke operators Tn​ and the integer scalars acting on level-one cusp forms. It preserves the lattice of cusp forms with integral Fourier coefficients of a composite-index Hecke operator. It is distinct from the generic Hecke algebra attached to a Coxeter system.
