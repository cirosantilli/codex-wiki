Scalar curvature of an umbilic vacuum hypersurface ((3)R=6ελ2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Second fundamental form Totally umbilic hypersurface
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For a timelike unit normal with squared norm epsilon and vanishing ambient Ricci tensor, contraction of the Gauss equation for a nonnull hypersurface gives the displayed formula. In signature (+−−−) the timelike normal has epsilon plus one, so the scalar of the induced negative-definite metric is nonnegative. In signature (−+++) the positive-definite spatial metric instead has scalar −6λ2. Replacing the induced metric by its negative reverses the scalar, so the inequality must not be transferred between these conventions without adjustment.
