Homomorphism of abelian groups (f(a+b)=f(a)+f(b))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group homomorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
A group homomorphism between abelian groups, conventionally written additively. Its kernel and image are subgroups, and the additive formula implies preservation of zero, negation and every integer multiple. Maps on Mordell-Weil groups induced by isogenies of elliptic curves and by good reduction are examples.
