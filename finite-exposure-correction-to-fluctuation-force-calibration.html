For an equilibrium overdamped harmonic coordinate with covariance ⟨x(t)x(0)⟩=Ve−∣t∣/τ, a camera exposure Δ records the average over that interval. Integrating the covariance twice gives Var(xˉ)=2VΔ−2∫0Δ​(Δ−u)e−u/τdu. The displayed reduction follows with s=Δ/τ. In the tether calibration F=kB​Th/V, ignoring exposure averaging overestimates the inferred force. Independent localization-noise variance instead adds to the measured variance and biases it in the opposite direction. Other filters require their actual response functions.
