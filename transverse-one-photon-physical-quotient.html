Choose a temporal polarization vector, two transverse polarization vectors and a spatial longitudinal polarization vector. The Gupta-Bleuler quantization condition is (a0−a3)∣χ⟩=0. Applied to ∑λ​cλ​aλ†∣0⟩, it gives c3​=−c0​. The remaining temporal-longitudinal excitation a0†−a3† has zero norm and is orthogonal to every constrained state. Its field wavefunction is proportional to the null four-momentum, hence is pure gauge. Quotienting this direction leaves the two positive transverse photon states. Use wave packets or a box normalization to avoid treating continuum momentum eigenstates as normalizable vectors.
