Kirby color handle-slide identity

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Colored link Kirby color
OurBigBook.com Words: 137
The bracket of a framed link with every surgery component colored by the Kirby color is unchanged by a handle slide. For a fixed self-dual color a choose inclusion/projection maps ic​:c→b⊗a, pc​:b⊗a→c with pc​ic​=1c​ and ∑c​ic​pc​=1b⊗a​. Bend the a strand to form Ucb​:b→c⊗a and Vcb​:c⊗a→b. Closing the diagrams gives Vcb​Ucb​=(dc​/db​)1b​. Completeness of these dual fusion maps therefore gives ∑b​db​Ucb​Vcb​=dc​1c⊗a​. Resolve the strand meeting a slide band into these channels. Moving the band across the colored loop bends its maps into U and V, and this weighted identity replaces the old loop-color sum by precisely the new Kirby coefficient dc​. The exterior ribbon diagrams agree by isotopy. Summing the sliding color with its own Kirby weight proves the full identity, including its carried band framing.
