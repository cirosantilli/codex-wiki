Peierls distortion

 ... Quantum ideal-gas statistics Bose-Einstein statistics Bose-Einstein distribution Phonon Electron-phonon interaction Peierls transition
OurBigBook.com Words: 275 Articles: 2
A Peierls distortion modulates the atomic positions and chemical bonds of a one-dimensional chain with a partly filled energy band. In the half-filled example, alternating short and long bonds double the primitive unit cell. The distortion is the structural change; the associated Peierls transition is the change in electronic conduction.[ref]
