Metrology

 Home Physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 70
Metrology asks what a measurement means and how closely it can be reproduced. An instrument reading becomes useful when it is related to a reference through measurement calibration, with a stated measurement uncertainty. Measuring a changing object against a changing ruler makes the problem especially clear: an observed difference contains the responses of both.
