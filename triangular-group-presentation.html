Triangular group presentation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Group presentation
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
A triangular group presentation has relators of word length at most three. Every finitely presented group admits one. For a relator s1​⋯sℓ​ of length ℓ≥4, introduce generators y2​,…,yℓ−2​ and replace it by
s1​s2​y2−1​,yj−1​sj​yj−1​ (3≤j≤ℓ−2),yℓ−2​sℓ−1​sℓ​.
(4)
The defining relations say yj​=s1​⋯sj​; eliminating those generators by Tietze transformations recovers the original relation. Every new relator has length three. Geometrically these operations triangulate each relator disk in its presentation complex by inserting diagonals, so they preserve the fundamental group.
