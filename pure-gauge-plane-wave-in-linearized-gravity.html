Pure-gauge plane wave in linearized gravity

Under the convention δhμν​=∂μ​ξν​+∂ν​ξμ​, choose ξμ=bμsin(k⋅x) with constant bμ and null kμ. The trace-reversed metric perturbation has amplitude
ϵgaugeμν​=kμbν+kνbμ−ημν(k⋅b).
(7)
It satisfies kμ​ϵgaugeμν​=0 and □ξμ=0, so it is an allowed residual gauge symmetry of linearized gravity. Its linearized Riemann curvature operator is zero: the four wavevector factors in each second-derivative term cancel in the antisymmetric curvature combinations. Nonzero transverse-traceless curvature distinguishes the two physical gravitational wave polarizations from these coordinate modes.[ref]
