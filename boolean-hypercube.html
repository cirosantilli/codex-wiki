Boolean hypercube ({−1,1}n)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Analysis of Boolean functions
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 59
The Boolean hypercube is the set {−1,1}n, or equivalently {0,1}n. Its hypercube graph joins two vertices exactly when they differ in one coordinate.
