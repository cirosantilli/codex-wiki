Radian (rad)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Angle
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
An angle in radians is the ratio of its subtended arc length to the radius of a circle. A full turn is 2π radians and a right angle is π/2 radians.
