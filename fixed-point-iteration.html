Fixed-point iteration

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 258 Articles: 5
Fixed-point iteration seeks a fixed point of a map F by repeatedly setting xk+1​=F(xk​). Convergence requires additional hypotheses; for example, a contraction mapping on a complete metric space has a unique fixed point reached by this iteration.
