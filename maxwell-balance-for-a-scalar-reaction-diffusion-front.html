Maxwell balance for a scalar reaction-diffusion front

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Travelling wave Front solution
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A stationary front solution of ut​=uxx​+f(u) connecting equilibria u−​ and u+​ requires ∫u−​u+​​f(s)ds=0. Multiply the stationary equation by ux​ and integrate: the derivative term has zero endpoint contribution. This equal-potential condition is the Maxwell balance. For f(u)=μu+αu3−u5, a front from zero to a nonzero constant requires u+2​=3α/4 and μ=−3α2/16.
