Jacobson density theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Noncommutative algebra Primitive ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
If V is a simple left R-module and D=EndR​(V)op, then every prescribed map on a finite D-linearly independent subset of V is induced by an element of R.
