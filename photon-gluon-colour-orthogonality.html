Photon-gluon colour orthogonality

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Color charge Color average
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
In distinct-flavour quark annihilation, the one-photon exchange color charge tensor is δji​δkl​, whereas the one-gluon tensor is ∑A​TjiA​TklA​. Their inner product after the external color charge sum is ∑A​tr(TA)tr(TA)=0, since the fundamental gauge generators of SU(3) are traceless. Thus the averaged squared scattering amplitude has no leading electromagnetic–strong interference term for this process. Fixed external color charge components, or processes with different exchange pairings, need not share this cancellation.
