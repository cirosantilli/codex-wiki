At fixed measured count slope, NAsed​∝a−3, whereas at fixed measured diffusion coefficient, NAdiff​∝a−1. First-order propagation of uncertainty follows by taking logarithmic derivatives:
NAsed​dNAsed​​=TdT​+bdb​−ΔρdΔρ​−3ada​−gdg​,NAdiff​dNAdiff​​=TdT​−ηdη​−ada​−DdD​.
(16)
For independent small errors, the radius contributes 9(σa​/a)2 to the relative variance of NAsed​, compared with (σa​/a)2 for NAdiff​. Shared radius and temperature calibrations make the two estimates correlated; their agreement is therefore not two wholly independent determinations. As a finite-error example, overestimating a by 5% multiplies the respective answers by 1.05−3≃0.8638 and 1.05−1≃0.9524.
This explains the importance of the radius cross-checks in the 1909 memoir: testing the Stokes drag law through an independent radius estimate helps prevent circular inference.[ref] Convection must also be excluded: a residual horizontal speed u adds u2τ2 to an uncentred mean squared displacement, making a linear fit misleading.
