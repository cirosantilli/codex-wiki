Mechanical impedance (Z=F/v)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 191 Articles: 2
Mechanical impedance is the ratio of a driving force complex amplitude to the resulting velocity complex amplitude at a specified point and in a specified direction. Its units are Nsm−1. The direction of the force, the time convention and whether one is describing a travelling component or a total field must be stated. A positive real travelling-wave mechanical impedance transfers mean power ⟨P⟩=Z∣v∣2/2 when ∣v∣ is the peak velocity wave amplitude.
