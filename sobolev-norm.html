Sobolev norm (∥u∥Wk,p(D)​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For 1≤p<∞, an inhomogeneous Sobolev space norm is ∥u∥Wk,p(D)​=(∑∣α∣≤k​∥Dαu∥Lp(D)p​)1/p, using weak derivatives. In particular, ∥u∥H1(D)2​=∫D​(∣u∣2+∣∇u∣2).
