Right-handed Dehn twist

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological surface Mapping class group Dehn twist
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
In orientation-preserving annular coordinates (θ,r) with orientation dθ∧dr, the twist is (θ,r)↦(θ+2πf(r),r), where f increases from zero to one. The map is the identity outside this annulus and near its boundary. The orientation of the ambient topological surface, rather than the orientation assigned to the curve, fixes the handedness.
