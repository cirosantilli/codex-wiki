Data-processing inequality for quantum relative entropy

OurBigBook.com Words: 22
For every quantum channel T,
D(T(ρ)∥T(σ))≤D(ρ∥σ).
(18)
For a partial trace, this says that discarding a subsystem cannot make two density operators more distinguishable in quantum relative entropy.
