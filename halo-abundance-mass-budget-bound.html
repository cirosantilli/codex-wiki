Halo abundance mass-budget bound (∫Mnh​(M)dM≤ρˉ​m,0​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A population of distinct, nonoverlapping dark matter halos cannot contain more total mass per comoving volume than the cosmic mean matter density. A narrow mass population therefore obeys nc​Mh​≤ρˉ​m,0​. Do not sum subhalo masses and their inclusive host masses in the same bound, because that double-counts material. This check can reveal incompatible inferred absorption incidence, object size and halo-mass assumptions.
