Separation of a point and a compact set (x∈/K ⟹ x∈V, K⊆U, U∩V=∅)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Hausdorff space Compact subset of a Hausdorff space
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 1
In a Hausdorff space, a point x outside a compact subset K can be separated from all of K by disjoint open sets. Choose disjoint open neighbourhoods Uk​ of k and Vk​ of x for each k∈K. Finitely many Uk​ cover K by compactness. Their union and the matching finite intersection of the Vk​ give the displayed separation. If K is empty, choose U=∅, V=X. Thus X∖K is open, proving that K is closed.
