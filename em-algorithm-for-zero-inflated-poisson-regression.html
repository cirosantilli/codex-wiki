EM algorithm for zero-inflated Poisson regression (τi​=P(Zi​=1∣Yi​))

The expectation-maximization algorithm introduces Zi​=1 for a structural zero. Its E-step gives τi​=0 at positive counts and τi​=πi​/[πi​+(1−πi​)e−μi​] at zero. The M-step maximizes a logistic log-likelihood with fractional responses τi​ and a Poisson log-likelihood with weights 1−τi​. With only a binary predictor, write nj​ for group size, Sj​=∑xi​=j​τi​ and Cj​=∑xi​=j​Yi​; the explicit updates are πj​=Sj​/nj​ and μj​=Cj​/(nj​−Sj​). Log and logit group contrasts recover the regression coefficients, with boundary values interpreted through limits.
