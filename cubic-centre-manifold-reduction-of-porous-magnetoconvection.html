For three-mode porous magnetoconvection, set μ=r−1−q and assume 0<q<ζ/σ. The transverse eigenvalues are negative. Centre-manifold invariance gives
c=ζ23a2​+⋯,b=ζa​−ζ(ζ−σq)σμa​−ζ2(ζ−σq)3a3​+⋯.
(21)
Substitution into the first equation gives the displayed positive linear and cubic coefficients. Thus the bifurcation is a subcritical pitchfork bifurcation: the origin is stable for μ<0 and unstable for μ>0, while the small nonzero branches at a2=−ζ2μ/(3q) are unstable. Simply setting b˙=0 misses the time-derivative contribution responsible for the common factor (1−σq/ζ)−1.
