Preference relation (x⪰y)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Binary relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 174 Articles: 4
A weak preference relation records that one alternative is at least as desirable as another. Rational preferences are complete and transitive. Their strict preference and indifference relation are the asymmetric and symmetric parts. On a consumption space, continuity is expressed by a closed set of weakly ranked pairs.
