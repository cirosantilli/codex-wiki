The Cosmic Background Explorer connected two questions: does the cosmic microwave background follow a Planck spectrum, and how does its temperature vary across the sky? John C. Mather and George F. Smoot shared the 2006 Nobel Prize in Physics for these discoveries.[ref] The FIRAS and COBE DMR comparisons below explain why measuring spectral shape and measuring angular structure require different information.
