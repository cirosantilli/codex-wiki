Morphism of locally ringed spaces (fx#​:OY,f(x)​→OX,x​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A morphism is a continuous map f:X→Y together with a homomorphism OY​→f∗​OX​ of sheaves of rings whose induced map on every stalk is a local homomorphism. Applied to schemes, this is exactly a morphism of schemes.
