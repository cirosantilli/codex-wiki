For a primitive Dirichlet character modulo N>1 of parity a∈{0,1}, put Θχ​(τ)=∑n​naχ(n)eπin2τ, extending the character by zero on nonunits. The square root is analytic with positive value when −iτ is positive real. Apply Poisson summation separately in each residue class modulo N, and use the finite Fourier transform of a primitive Dirichlet character. The Fourier transforms of eπiτx2 and xeπiτx2 are (−iτ)−1/2e−πit2/τ and (t/τ)(−iτ)−1/2e−πit2/τ. These give the formula, including its odd-character phase.
