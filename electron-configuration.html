Electron configuration

 Home Chemistry Quantum chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 486 Articles: 11
An electron configuration records the occupation of electron shells and electron subshells by electrons. For neutral sodium in its ground state, 1s22s22p63s1 gives 2+2+6+1=11 electrons; adding the occupations within each electron shell gives (2,8,1). A superscript counts electrons, rather than multiplying the preceding labels. Removing the outer electron gives the sodium ion Na+ with 1s22s22p6, or (2,8). See Flowers et al., Chemistry 2e, electron configurations of atoms and ions.
