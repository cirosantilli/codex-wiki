Chemical master equation

OurBigBook.com Words: 23
For reactions with stoichiometric vectors νr​ and reaction propensity functions ar​, the probability mass function p(x,t) obeys
∂t​p(x,t)=∑r​[ar​(x−νr​)p(x−νr​,t)−ar​(x)p(x,t)].
(5)
It is the forward operator of a Markov jump process equation for the copy-number continuous-time Markov chain.
