Consistency of a negated-consistency extension (S⊢ConS​→ConS+¬ConS​​)

 ... Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order theory Consistent first-order theory Formal consistency statement
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
For adequate arithmetic provability predicates, the arithmetized deduction theorem identifies inconsistency of S+¬ConS​ with S-provability of ConS​. Formalized second incompleteness gives □S​ConS​→□S​⊥. Contraposition yields the displayed conditional. This is an internal theorem and does not license removal of its consistency premise.
