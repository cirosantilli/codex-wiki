Generic reducedness with no embedded components

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Reduced scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A Noetherian scheme with no embedded associated primes is reduced if it is reduced at all its generic points. Locally, a nonzero nilpotent ideal would have an associated prime, necessarily a minimal prime. Its localization there would be nonzero, contradicting generic reducedness. This is useful with the unmixedness of a complete intersection.
