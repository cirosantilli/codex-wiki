Lack-of-fit F-test (F=SSPE​/(n−g)(RSS−SSPE​)/(g−p)​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Normal linear model Lack of fit
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
For n independent normal responses of common variance at g distinct predictor settings, a rank-p normal linear model has residual degrees n−p. The saturated setting-mean model has g parameters and pure error degrees n−g. Splitting orthogonal residual spaces gives lack of fit degrees g−p. Under the fitted mean model, the two squared lengths divided by the common variance are independent chi-squared variables; their ratio of mean squares has the F-distribution shown. This test is unavailable without repeated settings or positive lack-of-fit degrees.
