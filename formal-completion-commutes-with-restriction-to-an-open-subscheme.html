Formal completion commutes with restriction to an open subscheme ((X/Y​)∣U∩Y​≅U/(U∩Y)​)

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Formal scheme Formal completion of a scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
Restricting the topological structure sheaf of a formal completion of a scheme to an open subscheme commutes with its inverse limit: both sheaves are lim​n​(OU​/(I∣U​)n+1) on ∣U∩Y∣. Equivalently, the identical infinitesimal neighbourhoods have identical restricted structure sheaves. On formal affine charts this uses completed localization of an adic ring.
