Countability of countably generated abelian groups

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Abelian group
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
A countably generated abelian group is a countable set. Given generators g1​,g2​,…, every element is ∑j=1m​aj​gj​ for some m and some aj​∈Z. For each fixed m, this is the image of a finite cartesian product of copies of the countable set Z; taking a countable union of countable sets proves the assertion. The same proof works for a module generated by a countable set over any countable ring.
