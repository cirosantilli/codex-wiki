Poisson data fidelity (∫[ylog(y/s)+s−y])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Regularization of an inverse problem Variational regularization
OurBigBook.com Words: 385 Articles: 4
For positive observations y and positive predicted intensities s, this is the generalized Kullback–Leibler divergence with observation in the first argument. Its scalar derivative in s is 1−y/s and its second derivative is y/s2>0. It differs from the divergence of a reconstructed density from a reference density by which argument is varied. A linear prediction s=Kx gives derivative K∗(1−y/(Kx)) through the adjoint operator, on suitable admissible function spaces. This fidelity arises from the negative log likelihood of a Poisson observation model, after removal of terms depending only on observations.
