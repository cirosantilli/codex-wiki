Retransmission (data networks)

 Home Computer science Computer network Layered network architecture Transport protocol
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
Data retransmission sends data again to recover from loss or detected corruption. Transport sequence numbers let a receiver recognize a duplicate if the original transmission arrived but its acknowledgement was lost.
