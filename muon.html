Muon (μ−)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Lepton
OurBigBook.com Words: 114 Articles: 3
The muon is the negatively charged second-generation lepton, with electric charge −1 and lepton number +1. Its antiparticle is the antimuon.
