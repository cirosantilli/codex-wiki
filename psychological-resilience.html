Psychological resilience

 Home Science Psychology Clinical psychology
OurBigBook.com Words: 415 Articles: 8
Successful adaptation in the context of significant adversity, specified by outcome domain, developmental context and time. It is not simply the absence of disorder in an unexposed person, a universally favorable personality profile or proof of invulnerability. Capacities and resources proposed to explain adaptation should be measured separately from the outcome they are meant to explain.[ref]
