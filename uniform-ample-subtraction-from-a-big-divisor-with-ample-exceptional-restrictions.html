Uniform ample subtraction from a big divisor with ample exceptional restrictions (D−ϵA is nef(0<ϵ≪1))

 ... Algebraic geometry Cartier divisor Positivity of divisors Big divisor Big real divisor Negative curves of a big real divisor lie in finitely many divisors
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
If a big class D=B+E has ample restriction to every component Ei​ of the effective support, then for any ample A a sufficiently small ϵ>0 makes D−ϵA nef. Use openness of the ample cone to keep B−ϵA and all (D−ϵA)∣Ei​​ ample with one finite minimum of bounds. Curves inside the support use these restrictions; curves outside use effectivity of E. Thus one does not need a uniform choice over infinitely many subvarieties.
