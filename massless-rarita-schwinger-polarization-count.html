Massless Rarita-Schwinger polarization count (h=±3/2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 75
Gauge equivalence ψμ​∼ψμ​+∂μ​ϵ permits gamma-trace gauge. The Rarita-Schwinger field equation then gives transversality and a massless Dirac equation for each component. For momentum along the third axis, residual gauge removes the temporal and longitudinal components. The remaining constraint is ψ2​=γ1γ2ψ1​, with ψ1​ in the two-dimensional kernel of p. Spinor helicity ±1/2 is paired with vector helicity of the same sign, leaving only ±3/2. A Majorana spinor reality condition identifies antiparticles; a Dirac vector-spinor has separate antiparticle modes.
