EM transition-count update on a tree (Kabnew​=Nab​/∑c​Nac​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Latent variable Expectation-maximization algorithm
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For a common unconstrained Markov kernel on the edges of a rooted tree, let Nab​ be the conditional expectation of the number of transitions a→b, given observed leaves and the old Markov kernel. The expectation-maximization algorithm maximizes ∑a,b​Nab​logKab​ over row probability distributions, giving Kabnew​=Nab​/∑c​Nac​. A row with zero total count is unrestricted by this objective. Belief propagation computes the counts exactly on a tree.
