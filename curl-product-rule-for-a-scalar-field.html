Curl product rule for a scalar field (∇×(ϕF)=ϕ∇×F+∇ϕ×F)

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus Curl
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For a continuously differentiable scalar field ϕ and vector field F, the curl satisfies
∇×(ϕF)=ϕ∇×F+∇ϕ×F.
(37)
Indeed, with fixed Cartesian basis vectors ei​, the Cartesian expression for the curl and the product rule give
∑i​ei​×∂i​(ϕF)=∑i​(∂i​ϕ)ei​×F+ϕ∑i​ei​×∂i​F.
(38)
The first sum is the cross product of the gradient of ϕ with F; the second is ϕ times the curl of F. The formula is useful when applying the Stokes theorem to a weighted vector field.
