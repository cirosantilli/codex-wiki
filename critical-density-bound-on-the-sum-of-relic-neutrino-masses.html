OurBigBook.com Words: 84
For three standard thermal relic mass eigenstates, each neutrino-antineutrino pair has number density 3nγ​/11 after pair annihilation. Their rest-mass density is this common per-species abundance times the mass sum. Requiring it not to exceed the near-critical total density gives the displayed conservative bound. Dividing instead by the total abundance over three species bounds the mean mass. With Tγ0​=2.725 K and H0​=72 km s−1 Mpc−1 the instantaneous-decoupling estimate is about 49 eV/c2 for the sum, conditional on standard abundances and nonnegative other energy components.
