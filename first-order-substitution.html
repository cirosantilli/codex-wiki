First-order substitution (φ[τ/z])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
Replacing every free occurrence of variable z in a first-order formula by a first-order term τ. Bound occurrences of z are left alone. The operation requires either that τ be free for substitution at those occurrences or that conflicting bound variables first be renamed, as in capture-avoiding first-order substitution.
