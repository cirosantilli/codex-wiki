Brownian paths are not uniformly continuous on the half-line

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
For every fixed mesh 1/k, independent increments with the normal distribution imply increments of absolute value greater than one occur infinitely often almost surely, by the second Borel-Cantelli lemma. A countable intersection over k contradicts uniform continuity on [0,∞).
