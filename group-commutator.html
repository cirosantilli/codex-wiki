Group commutator ([x,y]=x−1y−1xy)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Group operation
OurBigBook.com Words: 108 Articles: 2
The group commutator measures the failure of two elements of a group to commute. Under the convention [x,y]=x−1y−1xy, it is the identity exactly when xy=yx. The commutator subgroup is generated by these elements. The alternative convention xyx−1y−1 generates the same commutator subgroup; individual formulas must specify their convention.
