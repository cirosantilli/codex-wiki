Group commutator ([x,y]=x−1y−1xy)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Group operation
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 2
The group commutator measures the failure of two elements to commute. Under the convention [x,y]=x−1y−1xy, it is the identity exactly when xy=yx.
