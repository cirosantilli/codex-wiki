Finite presentation of an algebra (A≅k[x1​,…,xN​]/(f1​,…,fs​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Finitely generated algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A presentation of an algebra over a field with finitely many generators and relations. The Hilbert basis theorem ensures that every finitely generated algebra over a field admits such a presentation. Its module of Kähler differentials is presented by the Jacobian matrix of the relations.
