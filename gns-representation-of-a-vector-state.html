GNS representation of a vector state (U[a]=aη,Uπxη​​(a)U−1=a∣Aη​​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 120
For a vector state on a C-star algebra, N={a:aη=0} and ⟨[a],[b]⟩=⟨aη,bη⟩. Thus U0​[a]=aη is a well-defined linear isometry from the quotient pre-Hilbert space onto Aη. It extends to an isometry from the completed GNS space onto K=Aη​: its range is complete and therefore closed, and contains the dense subspace Aη. The space K is invariant under both A and its adjoints. On quotient vectors, Uπ(a)[b]=abη=aU[b], proving the displayed unitary equivalence after continuity. The cyclic vector maps to η.
If A=B(H), then Aη=H, since the rank-one map v↦⟨v,η⟩ζ sends η to any chosen ζ. In this case, for the rank-one orthogonal projection pη​, aη=0 is equivalent to apη​=0, by evaluating at η and using (apη​)v=⟨v,η⟩aη.
