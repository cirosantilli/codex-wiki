Generating function (A(x)=∑n≥0​an​xn)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Power series
OurBigBook.com Words: 108 Articles: 1
A generating function encodes a sequence as a series, whose coefficients or prescribed weighted coefficients recover the sequence. An ordinary generating function uses the power series A(x)=∑n≥0​an​xn; an exponential generating function uses ∑n≥0​an​xn/n!. A Dirichlet series ∑n≥1​an​n−s gives another generating-function encoding. The displayed power series is the ordinary specialization, rather than a restriction on all generating functions. These encodings turn combinatorial splitting and concatenation rules into algebraic identities. A counting generating function is not necessarily a probability generating function: its coefficients need not sum to one.
