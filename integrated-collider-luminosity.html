Integrated collider luminosity (Lint​=∫L(t)dt)

 ... Standard Model Standard Model fermion Lepton Tau (particle) SPEAR Mark I tau discovery experiment Collider luminosity
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
The time integral of the incident-beam overlap flux, with inverse-area units. For a fixed production cross section, the mean produced event count is N=Lint​σ. A selected count includes efficiency and acceptance, often integrated over final-state kinematics. This is distinct from astrophysical radiative luminosity, which has power units.
