= Paleophysiology and end-Permian mass extinction by Andrew H. Knoll and others
{parent=Ecology and evolution bibliography}
{c}
{title2=2007}

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012821X07000842

{parent=Ecology and evolution bibliography}
{c}
{title2=1994}

https://www.nature.com/articles/371065a0

{c}

{parent=Ecology and evolution bibliography}
{c}
{title2=1995}

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11536722/

{parent=Ecology and evolution bibliography}
{c}
{title2=1998}

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1689526/

= Essays upon Heredity and Kindred Biological Problems by August Weismann
{parent=Ecology and evolution bibliography}
{c}
{title2=1889 English collection}

https://www.gutenberg.org/files/48132/48132-h/48132-h.htm

Primary essays translated into English. The discussion of acquired characters and note 224 explicitly distinguishes old miniature-organism <preformationism> from a renewed material account, and contrasts these with <epigenesis>. The collection's preface warns that the essays preserve stages in the author's changing views.
