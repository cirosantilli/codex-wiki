For <steady fields in two leaky dielectric layers>, set $\mathcal D=\sigma_2d_1+\sigma_1d_2$. Then $E_1=\sigma_2V/\mathcal D$, $E_2=\sigma_1V/\mathcal D$, $J=\sigma_1\sigma_2V/\mathcal D$. The interfacial free and total charges are $(\epsilon_2\sigma_1-\epsilon_1\sigma_2)V/\mathcal D$ and $\epsilon_0(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)V/\mathcal D$, respectively.

Full derivation: <past-exam-of-the-physics-b-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2010/paper-2a/section-b/b6/solution>.

= B7
{c}
{parent=Section B}
{scope}

= Solution
{parent=B7}

During partial entry, resistive <magnetic braking> gives $v=g\tau(1-e^{-t/\tau})$ with $\tau=mR/(B_0a)^2$. The lossless inductive limit gives $x=g(1-\cos\omega t)/\omega^2$, $v=g\sin\omega t/\omega$, with $\omega=B_0a/\sqrt{mL}$. Complete immersion changes the equations; the solution below distinguishes reversal from full entry.

Full derivation: <past-exam-of-the-physics-b-course-of-the-university-of-cambridge/2010/paper-2a/section-b/b7/solution>.

The <parallel-plate transmission line> has per-length $C=\epsilon b/a$ and $L=\mu a/b$. The lossless <telegrapher's equations> imply wave speed $1/\sqrt{LC}=1/\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$, independent of the much slower <electron drift velocity>.

The <image charge for a grounded conducting sphere> is $-qa/d$ at radius $a^2/d$. With symmetric real charges, their exact force-balance equation has a solution close to $d/a=8$. Holding potential $V$ adds force $qaV/d^2$ to the grounded result, approximately $qV/(64a)$ at that separation.

<Bernoulli's theorem> and <conservation of mass> give $t=r_1^2/(2r)$. Ignoring weight, the stated stagnant-rear-pressure model gives zero resultant at $r_2=\sqrt2r_1$. Including the leading film-weight estimate gives a cancellation radius near $2gr_1^2/v^2$. The printed inequality does not establish negligible force at every larger radius; the shared derivation explains this source qualification.

The <torsional wave equation in a circular shaft> has speed $\sqrt{G/\rho}$. The given shaft has torque $100\,\mathrm{N\,m}$, twist $9.95\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{rad}$, strain energy $0.497\,\mathrm J$ and kinetic energy $181\,\mathrm J$. The respective transit-time estimates are $786\,\mathrm W$ and $286\,\mathrm{kW}$; these must not be confused with independent bounds on the actual $30\,\mathrm{kW}$ stress-work flux.

Stable circular power-law orbits require $nB>0$ and $n>-2$, excluding $n=0$. <Bertrand's theorem> selects $n=-1,2$ when every bound orbit must close. For $n=2+\epsilon$, the near-circular ellipse orientation precesses at $-\epsilon\Omega/8$ to first order.
