= Nobel Prize in Physics in 2020
{c}
{parent=Nobel Prize in Physics}

One half went to <Roger Penrose> for showing that <black hole> formation is a robust prediction of <general relativity>. One quarter each went to <Reinhard Genzel> and <Andrea Ghez> for discovering a supermassive compact object at the centre of the <Milky Way>.<The Nobel Prize in Physics 2020 by Nobel Prize Outreach>[Individual official summary: Penrose one half for robust relativistic black-hole formation; Genzel and Ghez one quarter each for the Galactic-centre compact object.]{ref}
