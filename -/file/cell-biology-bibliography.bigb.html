= The Science and Philosophy of the Organism by Hans Driesch
{parent=Cell biology bibliography}
{c}
{title2=1908}

https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/44388/pg44388-images.html

Primary participant retrospective, based on the 1907 lectures. Part I, section B.1, Experimental Morphology, recounts Roux's 1888 experiment and the author's work three years later. The account and its vitalist interpretation must be distinguished from contemporaneous reports of the original trials.

= The Cell by Oscar Hertwig
{parent=Cell biology bibliography}
{c}
{title2=1895}

https://embryology.med.unsw.edu.au/embryology/index.php?title=Book_-_The_Cell_-_outlines_of_general_anatomy_and_physiology_%281895%29
https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/item/59252

English edition of the primary textbook. Chapters I–II discuss investigation and organization; chapter VI discusses multiplication; chapter VII examines fertilization, including echinoderm eggs; chapter IX discusses the cell as the basis of heredity. The UNSW transcription has substantial OCR errors; the facsimile is authoritative for exact wording.
