A necessary compatibility condition is $\mathbf f\cdot\hat{\mathbf g}=0$, because a <cross product> with $\hat{\mathbf g}$ is perpendicular to it. If this condition fails, there is no solution.

If it holds, take $\hat{\mathbf g}\times$ the equation and use the <vector triple product identity>:

Conversely, $(\hat{\mathbf g}\times\mathbf f)\times\hat{\mathbf g}=\mathbf f$ under the compatibility condition, so every displayed <vector> solves the equation. The solution set is a <straight line>, including the line through the origin when $\mathbf f=0$.

A <stationary point> must satisfy both factored equations from part (a). If either coordinate is zero, the other equation forces the other coordinate to vanish. This gives $(0,0)$.

Otherwise $x^4=8y^2$ and $y^4=8x^2$. With $u=x^2>0$ and $v=y^2>0$, these become $u^2=8v$ and $v^2=8u$. Dividing gives $(u/v)^3=1$, so $u=v$. Then $u^2=8u$ forces $u=v=8$. Hence all five <stationary points> are

The <Hessian matrix> is
$$
H=\begin{pmatrix}30x^4-48y^2&-96xy\\-96xy&30y^4-48x^2\end{pmatrix}.
$$
At each nonzero <stationary point>, its diagonal entries are $1536$ and the off-diagonal entries are $\pm768$. Its <eigenvalues> are $768$ and $2304$, both positive. Thus the <Hessian matrix> is <positive-definite matrix>[positive definite], and the <second-derivative test> proves that
$$
\boxed{\text{all four nonzero stationary points are strict local minima}}.
$$
Their common value is $h=-512$. In fact they are <global minima>: with $u=|xy|$, the <arithmetic-geometric mean inequality> gives $x^6+y^6\geq2u^3$, so
$$
h\geq2u^3-24u^2\geq-512.
$$
The last one-variable <polynomial> attains its minimum at $u=8$, and equality in the first bound requires $|x|=|y|$, precisely the four points above.

Let $E$ be the finish-time offset from the start of its displayed minute. The elapsed time is $25+E-S$, so $T=E-S$. To obtain the stated density, use the intended model that $E$ and $S$ are <independent random variables>, both with the <uniform distribution> on $[0,1)$.

$$

The <integrand> is one exactly where $s\in[0,1]$ and $s+t\in[0,1]$. The overlap has length $1+t$ for $-1\leq t<0$, length $1-t$ for $0\leq t<1$, and zero otherwise. Therefore
$$

$$
\mathbb E[T]=\mathbb E[E]-\mathbb E[S]=\frac12-\frac12=0.

The <standing wave> oscillates about the constant level $1/2$, with time period $2\pi/|v|$. Suitable snapshots are
$$
\begin{aligned}
t=0:&\quad\psi=\tfrac12(1+\cos x),\\
