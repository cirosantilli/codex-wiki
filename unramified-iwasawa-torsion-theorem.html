Unramified Iwasawa torsion theorem (rankΛ​Y=0)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Iwasawa theory Iwasawa module Unramified Iwasawa module
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
The unramified Iwasawa module is finitely generated torsion over the Iwasawa algebra of a Zp-extension, for every Zp-extension of a number field. After a finite shift all ramified primes are totally ramified and their number s is constant. Class field theory bounds finite-layer coinvariant modules by rankZp​​YΓn​​≤s−1. The Compact Nakayama lemma proves finite generation, and a positive Iwasawa-module rank would force ranks at least rpn, a contradiction. No Leopoldt conjecture is required.
