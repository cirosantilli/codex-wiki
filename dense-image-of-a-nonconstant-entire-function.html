Dense image of a nonconstant entire function

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Liouville theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The image of every nonconstant entire function is dense in C. If a disc about w were omitted, then 1/(f−w) would be bounded and entire, so Liouville's theorem would make f constant.
