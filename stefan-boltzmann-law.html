Stefan–Boltzmann law (F=ϵσT4)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 3
The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives the thermal-radiation flux emitted by a surface as F=ϵσT4, where ϵ is its emissivity and σ is the Stefan–Boltzmann constant.
