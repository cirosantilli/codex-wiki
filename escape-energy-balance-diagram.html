Use the synthetic standard gravitational parameter μ=8m3/s2 and launch distance r0​=2m. Then escape speed is 22​m/s. Three outward radial launches give exact comparisons: v0​=2m/s has specific orbital energy −2J/kg and stops outward at r=4m; v0​=22​m/s has zero specific orbital energy; v0​=23​m/s has specific orbital energy +2J/kg and asymptotic speed 2m/s. The second case has speed 2m/s at r=4m. Doubling r0​ divides escape speed by 2​, whereas doubling M at fixed r0​ multiplies it by 2​. Changing the test particle's mass changes E but cancels from the escape speed.
