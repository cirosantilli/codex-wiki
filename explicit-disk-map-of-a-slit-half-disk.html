Explicit disk map of a slit half disk (u2=z2+164(z2+1)​,F(z)=u+u−1−iu+u−1+i​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Conformal map
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 1
For the upper half disk ∣z∣<2 slit along z=iy, 0<y≤1, squaring maps onto ∣w∣<4 minus [−1,4). After scaling the radius-four disc, the disk automorphism v=4(w+1)/(w+16) sends this slit to [0,1). Its square root with argument in (0,π) maps onto the upper half unit disc. There u+u−1 maps bijectively onto the lower half-plane: its imaginary part is Imu(1−∣u∣−2)<0, and the two roots of its inverse equation are reciprocal, with exactly one inside the unit circle. The final Möbius map gives the displayed conformal disk bijection.
