Binary symplectic space of Pauli labels (ω((a,b),(a′,b′))=a⋅b′+b⋅a′)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum circuit Pauli group Binary phase representation of a Pauli string
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 1
Modulo scalar phases, the n-qubit Pauli group is the additive space F22n​, with label (a,b) for XaZb. Multiplication adds labels, and commutation contributes the sign (−1)ω. The displayed alternating form is nondegenerate. An isotropic subspace L satisfies L⊆L⊥, hence dimL≤n; it labels commuting stabilizers.
