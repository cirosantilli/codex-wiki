Van den Berg-Kesten inequality

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Percolation theory Bond percolation
OurBigBook.com Words: 247 Articles: 3
For increasing events A,B in a product percolation measure, their disjoint occurrence satisfies P(A□B)≤P(A)P(B).
