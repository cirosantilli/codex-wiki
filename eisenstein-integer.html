Eisenstein integer

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Integral domain Principal ideal domain Euclidean domain
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 1
For a primitive cube root of unity ω, the Eisenstein integers form the triangular lattice
Z[ω]={a+bω:a,b∈Z}.
(210)
Their multiplicative norm is N(a+bω)=a2−ab+b2.
