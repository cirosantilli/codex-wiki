For a bottom-heavy spherical swimmer in axial flow uz​=W0​(−1+r2+w), the bottom-heavy spherical-cell orientation dynamics with aligning rate 1/B gives sinθ=−λ(2r+w′), λ=BW0​/(2R). On the upward-stable branch cosθ>0, the cells tilt inward in a downward Poiseuille flow. Zero radial cell flux with scalar diffusivity gives n′=χnsinθ, χ=Vc​R/D. For w=0, integration and the average normalization 2∫01​nrdr=1 give the displayed concentration. A steady deterministic orientation requires ∣λ(2r+w′)∣<1; beyond this shear threshold the stationary orientation closure fails.
