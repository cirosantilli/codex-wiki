Supersymmetry generator (QαA​,Qˉ​α˙A​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 144 Articles: 2
A supersymmetry generator is an odd Weyl spinor generator of the Super-Poincaré algebra. Acting on a physical state reverses fermion parity while preserving its four-momentum.
