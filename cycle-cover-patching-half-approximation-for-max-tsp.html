Cycle-cover patching half-approximation for Max-TSP (ALG≥21​OPT)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Travelling salesman problem Max-TSP
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
Solve the maximum-weight assignment problem forbidding fixed points, obtaining a cycle cover of a directed graph whose weight bounds every Hamiltonian cycle above. Remove a lightest edge from each graph cycle. Each graph cycle has at least two edges, so at least half its nonnegative weight survives. Connect the resulting disjoint graph paths cyclically; completeness supplies the connectors and their nonnegative weights cannot reduce the retained sum. The Hungarian algorithm and linear-time patching give a polynomial time approximation algorithm. Negative weights invalidate a universal multiplicative half guarantee: when every weight is −1, every tour has weight −n<(−n)/2.
