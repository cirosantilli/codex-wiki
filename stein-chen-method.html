Stein-Chen method

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution Poisson distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 220 Articles: 3
The Stein-Chen method compares an integer-valued count with a Poisson distribution by solving the Poisson Stein equation and estimating its expectation at the count. For sums of indicator random variables, dependency neighborhoods expose local overlap errors. This gives total variation distance bounds without having to compute the entire count distribution.
