Laplacian (Δ)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus
OurBigBook.com Words: 201 Articles: 6
The Laplacian of a twice differentiable scalar field is the divergence of its gradient, ∇2f=∇⋅∇f; it acts componentwise on vector fields.
