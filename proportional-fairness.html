Proportional fairness

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 261 Articles: 4
A rate allocation is proportionally fair when the sum of proportional changes toward any feasible competitor is nonpositive. If route r has nr​ identical active flows, this condition is ∑r​nr​(xr​−xr​)/xr​≤0 and is equivalent to maximizing weighted logarithmic utility subject to capacity constraints.
