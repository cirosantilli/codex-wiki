OurBigBook.com Words: 66
Acoustic directivity describes the angular dependence of radiation in the acoustic far field. For a time-harmonic source in d spatial dimensions, separating the geometric spreading and oscillatory phase leaves a complex amplitude pattern D. The angular intensity pattern is proportional to ∣D∣2. Directivity can be normalized by a reference direction or total radiated power; a stated convention is needed when comparing absolute directivity factors.
