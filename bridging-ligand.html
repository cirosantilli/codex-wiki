Bridging ligand

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry Ligand in coordination chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 206 Articles: 7
A bridging ligand attaches to two or more metal centers. The symbol μ records bridging; its electron donation must be apportioned consistently between the metals in electron counting.
