Small GTPase

 Home Biology Protein G protein
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 9
A small GTPase is a relatively small nucleotide-switch protein that cycles between GDP- and GTP-bound states. Ras family proteins illustrate signaling mechanisms distinct from the three-subunit organization of a heterotrimeric G protein.
