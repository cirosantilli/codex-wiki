Elliptic regularity

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Elliptic differential operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 630 Articles: 10
Elliptic regularity says that solutions of an elliptic equation gain derivatives wherever the coefficients and forcing are regular. For the Laplacian, local estimates typically control two derivatives of u by Δu together with a lower-order norm of u.
