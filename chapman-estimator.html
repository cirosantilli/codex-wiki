Chapman estimator (NCh​=R+1(M+1)(C+1)​−1)

The Chapman estimator is a correction intended to reduce the small-sample bias of the Lincoln-Petersen estimator. Under the fixed-size hypergeometric sampling assumptions it is exactly an unbiased estimator when M+C≥N; it can have nonzero bias otherwise. This qualification and the formula are stated in Barker, §1.1.1, equation (1.3), citing Chapman's work through Seber. For the toy observations,
NCh​=5+1(20+1)(25+1)​−1=6546​−1=90.
(9)
It is an alternative estimator, not a maximum-likelihood estimate for this capture-recapture model. The Chapman estimator remains finite when R=0, but a finite output does not supply the missing overlap information or repair unequal catchability, lost marks, or population change.
