Geometric Modelling examination paper by the University of Oxford (2015) (A12074W1, 2015-06-10)

 Home Computer science Computer science bibliography
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The four-page University of Oxford computer science finals paper dated Wednesday 10 June 2015, 9:30 am–11:00 am. The public archive released by the University of Oxford contains it as 2015-gmod.pdf. Printed pp. 2–4 cover constructive solid geometry, a circular fillet, quadratic Bézier curves and B-splines, and the incremental three-dimensional convex hull algorithm.
