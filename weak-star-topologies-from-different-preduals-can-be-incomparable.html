Weak-star topologies from different preduals can be incomparable (σ(ℓ1,c0​),σ(ℓ1,c))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Weak topology Weak-star topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
The same Banach space can acquire incomparable weak-star topologies from different isometric preduals. Pair ℓ1 with c0​ by ∑αn​βn​ and with the space c of convergent sequences by α1​γ∞​+∑n≥2​αn​γn−1​. The functional α↦α1​ is continuous only for the first topology: e1​−en​→0 for the second but its values remain one. Conversely α↦∑αn​ is continuous only for the second: en​→0 for the first but its values remain one. More generally, comparable weak-star topologies arising from isometric preduals agree on the common closed unit ball, since its identity map is a continuous bijection from a compact space to a Hausdorff space by the Banach-Alaoglu theorem.
