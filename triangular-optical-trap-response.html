Triangular optical-trap response (fp​/fc​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical tweezers Passage through a translating optical trap Repeated kicks from a circular optical trap
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
A triangular localized potential energy has forces +F behind and −F ahead of the trap centre. With vc​=F/ζ=Lfc​, α=2X0​/L and β=fT​/fc​, its circular response is fp​/fc​=αβ/(β2−1+α) for β>1. A trapped particle has fp​/fc​=β for 0≤β≤1, interpreting the discontinuous force at the cusp by a sticking or rounded-well limit.
