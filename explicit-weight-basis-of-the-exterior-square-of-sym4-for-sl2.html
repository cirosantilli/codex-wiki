Explicit weight basis of the exterior square of Sym4 for sl2 (Λ2(Sym4C2)=L(6)⊕L(2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 99
Let ei​=x4−iyi and wij​=ei​∧ej​ for i<j. For the sl2 Lie algebra, the raising and lowering operators are E=x∂y​, F=y∂x​, and wij​ has weight 8−2(i+j). A weight basis of the irreducible representation L(6) is
w01​, w02​, w03​+2w12​, w04​+8w13​, w14​+2w23​, w24​, w34​.
(5)
A weight basis of the complementary L(2) is
w03​−3w12​,w04​−2w13​,w14​−3w23​.
(6)
The first vector in each list is a highest-weight vector. Applying F along the first list gives successive coefficients 3,2,1,12,5,2; along the second it gives 1,2. The final vectors are killed by F. At the overlapping weights 2,0,−2, the two listed vectors are independent, proving the direct sum.
