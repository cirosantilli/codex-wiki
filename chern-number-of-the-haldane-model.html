Chern number of the Haldane model (C=21​[sgnm+​−sgnm−​])

For d=0, put d=d/∣d∣. The lower-band orthogonal projector is P−​=(I−d⋅σ)/2. Applying the Pauli matrix multiplication law to iTr(P−​[∂kx​​P−​,∂ky​​P−​]) gives
Ω−​=21​d⋅(∂kx​​d×∂ky​​d)=2∣d∣3d⋅(∂kx​​d×∂ky​​d)​.
(16)
Thus the Chern number is the signed number of times the Brillouin zone covers a two-sphere under d. The lower energy eigenstate's actual Bloch vector is −d. The Berry curvature has the displayed positive sign for this lower band and the stated Berry connection; switching to the upper band changes its sign.
A useful local calculation exposes what each valley contributes. Write p=ℏv∣q∣. The linear Dirac Hamiltonian gives
Ω−,τ​(q)=2[(ℏv∣q∣)2+mτ2​]3/2τmτ​(ℏv)2​,2π1​∫R2​Ω−,τ​d2q=2τ​∫0∞​(p2+mτ2​)3/2mτ​pdp​=2τ​sgnmτ​.
(17)
Each unbounded continuum cone gives a half-integer in this calculation. It is not a separately realizable half-integer Chern number of an isolated lattice energy band. The full honeycomb lattice supplies both valleys and the large-momentum completion. Starting with a trivial large-∣M∣ state, each single-valley mass crossing changes the lattice integer by the corresponding unit. There are no other band touchings. This establishes the exact lattice result
C=21​[sgn(M−μ)−sgn(M+μ)]={−sgn(sinϕ),0,​∣M∣<33​t2​∣sinϕ∣,∣M∣>33​t2​∣sinϕ∣,​
(18)
for t2​>0, excluding the boundaries. This sign belongs to our explicit bond vectors, orientation and occupied-band convention.
For an original worked example, choose t1​=1, t2​=0.08, ϕ=π/2 and M=0. Then μ≃0.415692, (m+​,m−​)=(−μ,+μ), both valley gaps are 2μ≃0.831384, and C=−1. Choosing instead t2​=0, M=μ gives exactly the same valley-gap magnitudes but C=0. The first state's two valley contributions reinforce; the second state's cancel.
An independent eigenvector-overlap calculation on periodic 31×31, 61×61 and 121×121 momentum grids gives C=−1 for the first example and C=0 for the second. Reversing ϕ in the first example gives C=+1. Direct integration of the analytic lattice Berry curvature agrees. For the overlap calculation one can remove the embedding phase from f, replacing it by 1+eik⋅a1​+eik⋅a2​; this makes the cell-basis Hamiltonian periodic and prevents a false boundary-seam contribution. The local Berry curvature can depend on this basis convention; the integrated Chern number agrees. All mass-zero parameters are excluded before evaluating any integer.
