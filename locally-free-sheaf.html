Locally free sheaf

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 44
A sheaf of OX​-modules is locally free of finite rank when every point has an open neighborhood U on which it is isomorphic to OU⊕r​ for some finite r. If X is connected, the rank is constant.
A sheaf of modules is locally free of rank r if each point has a neighborhood U on which it is isomorphic to OUr​. Finite-rank locally free sheaves correspond to vector bundles in the standard geometric settings.
