Amine (RNH2​, R2​NH, R3​N)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance
OurBigBook.com Words: 264 Articles: 18
A simple neutral amine has nitrogen bonded to one, two, or three groups attached through carbon, with hydrogen filling the remaining positions in the neutral three-bond form. These are the primary, secondary, and tertiary forms respectively. An amide has different connectivity to a carbonyl group. [ref]
