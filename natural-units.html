Natural units (ℏ=c=1)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Dimensional analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 2
Natural units choose units so that selected fundamental constants have numerical value one. In relativistic quantum field theory the standard choice is ℏ=c=1: energy, mass and inverse length then have the same units. This is a convention, not a physical change of the constants; other natural-unit systems may also normalize other constants.
