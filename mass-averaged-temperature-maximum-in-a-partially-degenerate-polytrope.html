Mass-averaged temperature maximum in a partially degenerate polytrope (TM,max​=49RA3μG2M4/3​)

For a homologous stellar polytrope of index 3/2, take P=Kρ5/3+RρT/μ and zero surface pressure. The stellar virial theorem, with Ω=−6GM2/(7R), gives
TM​=7RR2μGM​−3RR2μAM2/3​,A=3K∫01​(4πρˉ​3ρ​)2/3dq.
(3)
The normalized density profile fixes A independently of mass and radius at fixed composition. Writing TM​=α/R−δ/R2 shows that its maximum is α2/(4δ)=3μG2M4/3/(49RA), at R=7AM−1/3/(3G). Below this radius contraction increases the fraction of support provided by electron degeneracy pressure and lowers the thermal temperature. A fixed normalized profile also fixes the ratio of central to mass-averaged temperature, giving a hydrogen-burning minimum mass.
