Products of convex functions need not be convex

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Convex function
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
The affine functions x and −x are both convex functions on R, but their product is −x2. Its value at zero exceeds the average of its values at −1 and 1, contradicting convexity. Thus closure under nonnegative linear combinations does not imply closure under products.
