Longitudinal two-sphere mobility (M=(m1​h​hm2​​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Hydrodynamic interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
For well-separated spheres moving along their line of centres, the leading mobility matrix has diagonal entries mi​=(6πμai​)−1 and off-diagonal entries h=(4πμℓ)−1. This is the axial Stokeslet interaction. Finite-size and repeated-reflection corrections enter at higher order.
