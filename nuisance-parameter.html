Nuisance parameter

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical parameter
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
A nuisance parameter is a statistical parameter required to specify the distribution of observations but not itself the target of inference. A profile likelihood maximizes over it; Bayesian model evidence integrates over it using a proper prior distribution. These operations differ and need not give the same inference. A baseline hazard in a Cox proportional-hazards model is an infinite-dimensional example.
