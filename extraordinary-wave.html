Extraordinary wave (nex​(θ))

The extraordinary wave's electric displacement field lies in that plane and is perpendicular to the wavevector; its electric field need not be. For angle θ between the wavevector and optic axis,
nex​(θ)21​=no2​cos2θ​+ne2​sin2θ​.
(4)
Thus nex​(0)=no​ and nex​(π/2)=ne​.[ref]
A short derivation starts from the existing plane wave in an anisotropic dielectric equation. Put k=(kx​,0,kz​) and k0​=ω/c, with angular frequency ω and vacuum speed of light c. The y component gives the ordinary wave relation kx2​+kz2​=no2​k02​. The determinant of the x,z system gives
ne2​kx2​​+no2​kz2​​=k02​.
(5)
Substituting kx​=nex​k0​sinθ and kz​=nex​k0​cosθ yields the displayed directional refractive index.
