Singlet criterion for an unbroken subgroup (VH={0})

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Spontaneous symmetry breaking
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
An unbroken subgroup H must fix the vacuum expectation value v of a scalar field in a group representation V. Thus a nonzero such v exists exactly when VH={0}. For a compact group, complete reducibility of compact-group representations identifies this fixed vector subspace with the sum of the trivial representations in the restriction of a representation V∣H​. Every v∈VH has H⊆Stab(v); equality requires a separate stabilizer calculation. The criterion neither determines the full unbroken group nor guarantees that a given scalar potential has its minimum at that direction.
