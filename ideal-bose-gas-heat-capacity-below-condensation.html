Ideal Bose-gas heat capacity below condensation (CV​=415​NkB​ζ(3/2)ζ(5/2)​(T/Tc​)3/2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For quadratic three-dimensional dispersion below condensation, the excited-state thermal energy is proportional to T5/2. Differentiating at fixed particle number and volume gives the displayed heat capacity, with limiting value approximately 1.926NkB​ at Tc−​. The macroscopic ground occupation carries no excitation energy in the shifted reference. The exponent differs from the linear-phonon result of an interacting gas.
