Ring spectrum ((E,μ,η))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Stable homotopy theory Spectrum (topology)
OurBigBook.com Words: 74 Articles: 1
A ring spectrum is a unital associative monoid object of the stable homotopy category under the smash product of spectra. It has μ:E∧E→E and η:S→E, satisfying the associative and two unit identities. Structured ring spectra additionally specify coherent point-set or higher homotopies; a ring object alone does not assert those enhancements.
