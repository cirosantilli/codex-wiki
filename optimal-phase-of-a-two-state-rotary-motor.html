Optimal phase of a two-state rotary motor (smax​)

 ... Biology Biological cell Cell membrane Membrane transport Bacterial flagellar motor Driven two-state rotary motor
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For the driven two-state rotary motor with a triangular bound potential of height U0​, the torque scale is T0​=U0​/π. Differentiating the phase-averaged torque gives T′(s)=T0​[2PA​(s)−1]/π. The decreasing crossing of PA​=1/2 therefore gives smax​=ln(2α)/β and Tmax​=T0​lncosh(πβ/2)/(πβ). The slow-speed limit is T0​/2; the fast-speed limit vanishes as T0​πk0​/(8ω).
