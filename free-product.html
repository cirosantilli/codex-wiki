Free product (A∗B)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fundamental group Seifert-van Kampen theorem Amalgamated free product
OurBigBook.com Words: 430 Articles: 8
The free product A∗B is the amalgamated free product over the trivial group. Its nonidentity elements have unique reduced alternating normal forms.
