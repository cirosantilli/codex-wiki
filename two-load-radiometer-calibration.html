Two-load radiometer calibration (Ts​=Tc​+(Th​−Tc​)(Ss​−Sc​)/(Sh​−Sc​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Radiometry Radiometer
OurBigBook.com Words: 97
For a stable radiometer with affine output S=g(T+Tr​), let g>0 be the fixed response coefficient and Tr​ the receiver noise temperature. Two reference loads with known equivalent input noise temperatures Tc​<Th​ give outputs Sc​ and Sh​. Subtracting their outputs eliminates Tr​; dividing source and reference differences eliminates g:
Sh​−Sc​=g(Th​−Tc​),Ts​=Tc​+(Th​−Tc​)Sh​−Sc​Ss​−Sc​​.
(1)
This two-load radiometer calibration assumes unchanged response and receiver noise temperature between readings, with the loads and source referred to the same input. It also eliminates an additional constant output offset. Physical load temperatures must first be converted to the applicable equivalent input noise temperatures when the classical thermal approximation fails. The Penzias-Wilson microwave background experiment gives an original worked example.
