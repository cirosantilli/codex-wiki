Holographic reference wave (R)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Holography
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
A holographic reference wave is a known coherent comparison field at the recording surface. Its wave phase supplies the standard against which the holographic object wave is encoded. In in-line holography, the undiffracted part of the illumination can supply this field; in off-axis holography, a separate tilted beam supplies it.
