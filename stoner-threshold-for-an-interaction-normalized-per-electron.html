Stoner threshold for an interaction normalized per electron (Uc​=4EF​/3)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum magnetism Hubbard model Itinerant ferromagnetism Stoner criterion
OurBigBook.com Words: 108
For a three-dimensional zero-temperature parabolic Fermi gas, let ζ=(N↑​−N↓​)/N and EF​=ℏ2(3π2N/V)2/3/(2m). Suppose the interaction energy per electron is UN↑​N↓​/N2=U(1−ζ2)/4. The total energy per electron is
f(ζ)=103EF​​[(1+ζ)5/3+(1−ζ)5/3]+4U​(1−ζ2).
(90)
Its Taylor expansion has quadratic coefficient EF​/3−U/4 and positive quartic coefficient EF​/81. Hence the unpolarized state loses stability at Uc​=4EF​/3∝(N/V)2/3. This U has units of energy. It differs from a contact coupling g defined by interaction-energy density gn↑​n↓​, for which g=U/n and gc​=4EF​/(3n). Normalization must be fixed before comparing critical couplings. For U≤4EF​/3, convexity of x−1/3 implies f′′(ζ)=(EF​/3)[(1+ζ)−1/3+(1−ζ)−1/3]−U/2≥0, so the unpolarized minimum is a global minimum; a first-order polarized minimum does not pre-empt this threshold in the stated model.
