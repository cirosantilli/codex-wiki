Anchored variation norm (∥f∥V​=V01​(f),f(0)=0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Riemann integration Total variation of a function
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
On the vector space of continuous functions f:[0,1]→R of bounded variation with f(0)=0, the total variation of a function is a norm. Its triangle inequality and absolute homogeneity of a norm follow by applying the corresponding absolute value identities to every partition of an interval, then taking the supremum. The partition of an interval (0,t,1) gives ∣f(t)∣≤V01​(f), so zero total variation of a function forces f=0. The same inequality gives ∥f∥∞​≤∥f∥V​, proving that convergence for this norm implies uniform convergence. Without f(0)=0, every constant function would have zero total variation of a function.
