Coordinate criterion for weak convergence in a separable Hilbert space

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Hilbert space Weak convergence in a Hilbert space
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
For a Hilbertian basis (ei​), a sequence xn​ converges weakly if and only if (xn​) is norm bounded and every coordinate sequence ⟨xn​,ei​⟩ converges.
