Fan in toric geometry (Σ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Toric geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 29
A fan in toric geometry is a finite collection of rational strongly convex cones in toric geometry in a real vector space with a lattice, closed under taking faces of a polyhedral cone, such that the intersection of two cones is a face of each. A fan determines a toric variety.
A fan Σ in NR​ is a finite collection of strictly convex rational polyhedral cones closed under taking faces, such that the intersection of two cones is a face of each.
