AAPCS32

 Home Computer science Programming language Assembly language Calling convention
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The 32-bit calling convention for the ARM architecture family. Its base variant passes initial arguments in r0–r3, returns a word in r0, preserves r4–r8, r10, r11, and sp, and requires eight-byte stack pointer alignment at public calls.[ref]
