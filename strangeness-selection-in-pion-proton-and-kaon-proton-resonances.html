Strangeness selection in pion-proton and kaon-proton resonances

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Strong interaction Meson-baryon scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
The π+p channel has baryon number one, strangeness zero and I3​=3/2, matching the ordinary uuu Delta baryon. The K+p channel has strangeness plus one, which no three-light-quark baryon can have because each strange quark contributes minus one. A resonance in that channel would require exotic content such as four quarks and an antiquark; the ordinary low-energy qqq multiplets therefore explain the contrast.
