Hull-White model (drt​=(β(t)−art​)dt+ηdWtQ​)

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fixed-income security Interest rate Short rate
OurBigBook.com Words: 67 Articles: 1
The Gaussian Hull-White model has pricing dynamics drt​=(β(t)−art​)dt+ηdWtQ​. Its deterministic drift function can fit the initial yield curve exactly, while its Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process part controls stochastic variation. Like the Vasicek model, it permits negative rates.
