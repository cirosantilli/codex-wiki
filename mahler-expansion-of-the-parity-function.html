Mahler expansion of the parity function ((−1)x=∑n≥0​(−2)n(nx​) on Z2​)

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Continuous functions on the p-adic integers Mahler's theorem Mahler coefficient
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
The locally constant function equal to one on 2Z2​ and minus one on its other coset has Mahler coefficients an​=(−2)n: at nonnegative integers its values are (−1)j, so the forward difference operator multiplies it by −2. Since ∣an​∣2​=2−n and the binomial polynomials have absolute value at most one on the 2-adic integers, the series converges uniformly. The binomial theorem verifies its values on the nonnegative integers, and continuity extends the identity to the entire domain.
