Redistribution of polynomial factors for dice

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Probability generating function
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
Encode a fair die with face labels a1​,…,as​ by A(z)=∑i​zai​. Its probability generating function is A(z)/s. Two independent dice with polynomials A,B have the same sum probability distribution as another pair C,D if AB=CD and all four dice have s faces. Redistributing factors can construct unexpected examples, but each resulting polynomial must have nonnegative integer coefficients, no constant term if labels must be positive, and value s at z=1. For example, reallocating 1−z+z2 from one copy of z+⋯+z6 to the other gives face labels (1,2,2,3,3,4) and (1,3,4,5,6,8).
