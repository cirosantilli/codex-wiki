Trefoil knot (31​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Knot Torus knot
OurBigBook.com Words: 176
The trefoil is the torus knot T2,3​. It is the simplest nontrivial knot by crossing number of a knot: c(T2,3​)=3. A standard three-crossing alternating knot diagram is reduced, so the Tait crossing-number theorem establishes minimality. The four-crossing knot diagram shown below also represents the trefoil knot, illustrating that a particular projection need not achieve that minimum. Its Seifert genus is one.
Here is a small algebraic certificate that a trefoil knot cannot be deformed into an unknot. One Seifert matrix for a trefoil knot is
A=(1−1​01​),tA−AT=(t−1−t​1t−1​).
(5)
Taking the determinant gives the Alexander polynomial of a knot
det(tA−AT)=(t−1)2+t=t2−t+1≐t−1+t−1,
(6)
where ≐ allows multiplication by a Laurent unit ±tn in the Laurent polynomial ring. No such multiplication turns this three-term Alexander polynomial of a knot into the unknot's value 1. Since the Alexander polynomial of a knot is a knot invariant, this computation rules out every possible ambient isotopy between the two knots; it does not merely rule out the deformations we happened to try.
The knot group supplies another certificate. The presentation ⟨a,b∣a2=b3⟩ maps onto the symmetric group S3​ by a↦(12) and b↦(123), since both sides of the relation map to the identity. These images generate a non-abelian group, so the knot group cannot be the unknot's infinite cyclic group. However, a trefoil knot and its mirror of a link have isomorphic knot groups: the abstract knot group alone does not distinguish all knots.
