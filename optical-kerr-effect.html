Optical Kerr effect (n=n0​+n2​I)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Nonlinear optics Kerr effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 243 Articles: 4
An intensity-dependent refractive index produced by the optical field itself. In a simple instantaneous isotropic model, n=n0​+n2​I. A transversely nonuniform intensity produces self-focusing for positive n2​ and self-defocusing for negative n2​. Temporal intensity variation produces self-phase modulation. These are consequences of a third-order nonlinear optical susceptibility, distinct from a static applied-field electro-optic Kerr effect.
