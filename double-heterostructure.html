Double heterostructure

 Home Physics Semiconductor Semiconductor heterostructure
OurBigBook.com Words: 422 Articles: 2
A double heterostructure sandwiches one semiconductor between two layers of another, for example gallium arsenide between aluminium gallium arsenide layers. Suitable band offsets confine injected charge carriers centrally.[ref]
