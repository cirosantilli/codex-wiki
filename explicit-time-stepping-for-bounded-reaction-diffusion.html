Explicit time stepping for bounded reaction diffusion (∥(I+kDh​+kVh​)n∥≤eAnk)

OurBigBook.com Words: 78
Let Dh​ be the scaled Dirichlet discrete Laplacian and Vh​ a bounded diagonal reaction matrix. If 0≤k/hx2​≤1/2, the heat update Hh​=I+kDh​ is contractive in both the maximum norm and the mesh-weighted L2 norm. For ∥Vh​∥≤A,
∥(Hh​+kVh​)n∥≤(1+kA)n≤eAnk.
(18)
This proves finite-time mesh-uniform stability without requiring the full update to have nonnegative entries or to be contractive. Negative reaction terms can destroy nonnegativity at the endpoint k/hx2​=1/2, so it is the heat part alone that is treated as a nonnegative contraction.
