Polydisc (Pn)

 ... Geometry and topology Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A polydisc is a product of open discs in C. It is a contractible complex manifold, with vanishing positive-degree de Rham cohomology and vanishing Dolbeault cohomology in positive antiholomorphic degree.
