Scale function of a birth-death chain

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Birth-death chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A strictly increasing sequence si​ for which s(Xt​) is a martingale along the birth-death chain before hitting its boundary. With upward and downward probabilities pi​,qi​, harmonicity means pi​(si+1​−si​)=qi​(si​−si−1​). This coordinate is useful for hitting probabilities and optimal stopping.
