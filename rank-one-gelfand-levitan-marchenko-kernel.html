Rank-one Gelfand-Levitan-Marchenko kernel

 Home Physics Branch of physics Integrable systems Inverse scattering transform Gelfand-Levitan-Marchenko equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
If F(s)=ce−χs with χ>0, the Gelfand-Levitan-Marchenko equation has a separable kernel. Writing K(x,y)=−B(x)e−χy reduces the integral equation to one algebraic equation for B(x) and reconstructs a single soliton.
