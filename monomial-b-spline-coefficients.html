Monomial B-spline coefficients (ai(m)​=em​/(mk−1​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 2
For order k B-splines, comparison of coefficients in the Marsden identity gives
tm=∑i​(mk−1​)em​(ti+1​,…,ti+k−1​)​Ni​(t),0≤m≤k−1,
(17)
on the basic knot interval. Here em​ is the elementary symmetric polynomial, with e0​=1. The m=0 case is partition of unity; the m=1 coefficients are the Greville abscissae.
