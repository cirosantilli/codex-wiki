Kähler differential (ΩB/A​)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 22
For a ring map A→B, the module of Kähler differentials represents A-derivations: HomB​(ΩB/A​,M)≅DerA​(B,M).
For an algebra B over A, the module ΩB/A​ represents A-linear derivations: every derivation B→N factors uniquely through the universal map d:B→ΩB/A​.
