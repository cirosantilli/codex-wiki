Symmetric rational function in two variables

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Polynomial content Primitive polynomial Gauss lemma for polynomials
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
Every rational function in C(X,Y) fixed by exchanging X and Y is a quotient of coprime symmetric polynomials. In a coprime presentation p/q, symmetry gives
pq∗=p∗q.
(213)
Unique factorization implies p∗=λp and q∗=λq. The alternative λ=−1 would make both divisible by X−Y, contradicting coprimality, so λ=1.
