Unitary group as a regular level set (dimR​U(n)=n2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Unitary group
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
The smooth map F(A)=A∗A from complex matrices, regarded as a real vector space, to Hermitian matrices has derivative DFA​(K)=A∗K+K∗A. At a unitary A, taking K=AH/2 gives any prescribed Hermitian H. Thus I is a regular value and F−1(I)=U(n) is a smooth manifold of real dimension n2.
