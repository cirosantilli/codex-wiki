Ergodicity

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Ergodic theory Measure-preserving transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 23
A measure-preserving transformation is ergodic when every set in its invariant sigma-algebra has measure zero or full measure. Equivalently, every integrable invariant function is almost everywhere constant.
