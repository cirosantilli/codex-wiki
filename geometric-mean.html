Geometric mean (G=(x1​⋯xn​)1/n)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic
OurBigBook.com Words: 732 Articles: 6
The geometric mean replaces a list of nonnegative real numbers by one repeated value that preserves their product of numbers. For n≥1 inputs,
G=(x1​⋯xn​)1/n,Gn=x1​⋯xn​.
(7)
For two positive inputs, G=x1​x2​​ is their positive square root. The geometric mean of 1 and 9 is three: replacing 1⋅9 by 3⋅3 retains the same product. In contrast, their arithmetic mean five retains the sum 1+9=5+5. The geometric mean is zero if any input is zero; the logarithm formula below requires every input to be positive. [ref].
Its usefulness becomes immediate for successive growth factors. Suppose a hypothetical positive quantity starts at Q0​=1000, is multiplied by 3/2, then by 2/3. It goes 1000→1500→1000, so the geometric mean factor is (3/2)(2/3)​=1: a constant factor of one for both steps gives the same endpoint. The arithmetic mean factor is 13/12; repeating it would give 1000(13/12)2≈1173.61, a different endpoint. A mean of factors summarizes multiplicative change; subtracting one afterwards gives the equivalent change per step. Equal-duration steps are necessary to interpret this as change per common time interval.
For positive inputs, logarithms turn multiplication into addition, so
logG=n1​∑i​logxi​,G=exp(n1​∑i​logxi​).
(8)
Thus the geometric mean is an arithmetic mean in logarithmic coordinates, followed by the exponential function. For positive measurements with a common unit, one can use dimensionless ratios to a fixed reference x0​: G=x0​exp(n−1∑i​log(xi​/x0​)). Changing the common unit rescales G by the same factor as the inputs. As a check, doubling one input and halving another retains G while usually changing the arithmetic mean.
The arithmetic-geometric mean inequality gives G≤A. Apply that same inequality to the multiplicative inverses 1/xi​ to obtain H≤G, where H is the harmonic mean. Consequently
H≤G≤A​
(9)
for positive inputs, with equality throughout exactly when all inputs agree. For 30 and 60, the three values are H=40, G=302​≈42.4264, and A=45. They preserve, respectively, the sum of inverses, the product, and the sum. No one of these summaries preserves all three for unequal inputs.
