Aggregation of macroparasite burdens (Var(j)=m+m2/k)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Mathematical epidemiology Macroparasite burden model
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
If host-specific equilibrium mean burden has a gamma distribution, conditional Poisson distributions produce a Poisson-gamma mixture, hence a negative binomial distribution. With mean m and shape k, its zero-burden probability is (1+m/k)−k. Decreasing k increases aggregation: a smaller infected-host prevalence can accompany the same mean burden and more heavily infected individuals.
