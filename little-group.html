Little group

 ... Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz transformation Lorentz group Poincaré group
OurBigBook.com Words: 719 Articles: 15
The little group of a momentum p is its stabilizer in the Lorentz group. Its unitary irreducible representations label the internal degrees of freedom of one-particle states on the orbit of p.
These stabilizer representations are used in Wigner's classification of relativistic particle states.
