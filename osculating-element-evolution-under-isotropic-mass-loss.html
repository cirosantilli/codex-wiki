For isotropic stellar mass loss the eccentricity vector obeys e˙=−(μ˙​/μ)(e+r). With true anomaly f and e>0, this gives e˙=−(μ˙​/μ)(e+cosf) and ϖ˙=−(μ˙​/μ)sinf/e. The osculating pericentre satisfies q˙​/q=−(μ˙​/μ)(1−cosf)/(1+e), hence cannot decrease during mass loss and is instantaneously stationary at pericentre. The vector form is nonsingular at a circular orbit.
