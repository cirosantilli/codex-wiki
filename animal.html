Animal

 Home Biology Organism Eukaryote
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 271
Animals are multicellular organisms with eukaryotic cellular organization. A representative animal cell has a cell membrane, cell nucleus and mitochondria, but no cell wall or chloroplasts. A neuron illustrates how this common plan can acquire a specialized shape and signaling function. See OpenStax Biology 2e, 4.3: animal cell organization.
