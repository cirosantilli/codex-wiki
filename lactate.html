Lactate (CH3​CHOHCOO−)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Organic acid Carboxylic acid Lactic acid
OurBigBook.com Words: 12
Lactate is the conjugate base of lactic acid. Lactate dehydrogenase couples its interconversion with pyruvate to the NAD+/NADH redox pair.
