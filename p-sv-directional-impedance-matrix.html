P-SV directional impedance matrix (Z±​=(±A−C​C±B​))

For real frequency ω>0 and both vertical wavenumbers p=kα​>0, s=kβ​>0, let D=k2+ps, A=ρωp/D, B=ρωs/D, and C=k(2μD−ρω2)/(ωD). With traction t=(σxz​,σzz​) and particle velocity v=(vx​,vz​), each directional P wave/SV-wave field obeys t=−Z±​v. Direct displacement-potential substitution yields Z−​=−Z+†​. The Hermitian part of Z+​ is positive diagonal; thus forward/backward fluxes have opposite signs and mixed directional flux cross terms cancel.
