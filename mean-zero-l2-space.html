Mean-zero L2 space (L02​(P)={h∈L2(P):Ph=0})

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Lp space L2 space is a Hilbert space
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The mean-zero L2 space is the kernel of the bounded linear functional h↦Ph on L2 space for a probability measure P. It is a closed subspace of a Hilbert space, with inner product P(hk), and is the natural ambient space for score functions and canonical gradients.
