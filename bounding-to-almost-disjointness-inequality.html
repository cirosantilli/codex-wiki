Bounding-to-almost-disjointness inequality (b≤a)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Almost disjoint family on omega Maximal almost disjoint family on omega Almost disjointness number
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
For an infinite almost disjoint family on omega of size less than the bounding number, select countably many members and remove their intersections with earlier selected members to obtain infinite disjoint sets Cn​. Bound, eventually and simultaneously, the finite intersections of each member with Cn​. Choose one point of each Cn​ above its bound. The resulting infinite set is almost disjoint from every original member, proving the family is not maximal. A selected member has one exceptional infinite intersection, which is ignored in its bounding function.
