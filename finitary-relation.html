Finitary relation (R⊆X1​×⋯×Xn​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 43
A subset of a finite Cartesian product specifies which tuples of elements satisfy a relation. A first-order structure interprets a relation symbol with n argument places as such a subset of the n-fold Cartesian product of its domain of a first-order structure. For two argument places this is a binary relation; for three, the set of triples satisfying x+y=z is an example involving addition.
