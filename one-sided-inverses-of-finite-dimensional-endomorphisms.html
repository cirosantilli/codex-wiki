One-sided inverses of finite-dimensional endomorphisms

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For endomorphisms S,T of the same finite-dimensional vector space, ST=I makes S surjective and T injective. The rank-nullity theorem makes both invertible, hence TS=I. The finite-dimensional and equal-domain assumptions matter; unilateral shift operators on an infinite-dimensional sequence space give one-sided inverses that are not two-sided.
