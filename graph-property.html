Graph property

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 516 Articles: 10
A graph property is a class of finite graphs closed under isomorphism. Its labelled order-n slice consists of members on vertex set [n]. Closure under taking arbitrary subgraphs and closure under taking only induced subgraphs lead respectively to subgraph-closed graph properties and hereditary graph properties.
