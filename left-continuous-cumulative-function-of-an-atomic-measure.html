Left-continuous cumulative function of an atomic measure (F(x)=μ((−∞,x)))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Monotonic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For positive summable weights wj​ at points qj​, the strict cumulative function F(x)=∑qj​<x​wj​ is nondecreasing and left-continuous. A finite-head/tail argument proves left-continuity, and its right jump at an atom is the mass there. This strict convention differs at atoms from the usual right-continuous cumulative distribution function.
