Dust-burst accretion pulse (M˙p​=T[1−η4−α]md​(4−α)​(t/T)3−α)

Let the initial dust mass be md​, let its differential number distribution be KD−α with α<4 between Dmin​ and Dmax​, and put η=Dmin​/Dmax​. Under burst transport under inverse-size radial drag, complete absorption at a fixed radius occurs at t=TD/Dmax​, with T=tr​(Dmax​). The arriving mass is proportional to D3−αdD. Substitute D=Dmax​t/T and dD/dt=Dmax​/T and use mass normalization of a power-law size distribution to prove the displayed rate for ηT≤t≤T, with zero rate elsewhere. Its integral is exactly md​. Resonant delays, scattering losses, a finite release duration or grain destruction change this ideal pulse.
