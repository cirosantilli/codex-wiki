Four-thirds convective heat-transfer law (q∝(ΔT)4/3)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Navier-Stokes equation Rayleigh-Bénard convection
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
A high-Rayleigh number closure Nu=CRa1/3 gives q=CkT​[gβ/(νκ)]1/3(ΔT)4/3, independent of layer depth. It assumes boundary-layer-controlled heat transfer, a well-mixed interior, fixed material properties and a fixed Prandtl number regime. It is a model, not a universal asymptotic law for every turbulent convection regime or for an impulsive initial conductive transient.
