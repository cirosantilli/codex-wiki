Sequence space

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A sequence space is a vector space of scalar sequences, closed under coordinatewise addition and scalar multiplication. Examples include the l-p sequence space, the l-infinity sequence space and spaces of convergent sequences.
