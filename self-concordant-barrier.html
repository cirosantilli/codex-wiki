Self-concordant barrier (F,ν)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Conic optimization Interior-point method
OurBigBook.com Words: 298 Articles: 5
A convex three-times differentiable barrier F is self-concordant when
∣D3F(x)[h,h,h]∣≤2(D2F(x)[h,h])3/2.
(4)
A barrier of parameter ν additionally satisfies ∣DF(x)[h]∣≤νD2F(x)[h,h]​ and diverges at the domain boundary. Logarithmically homogeneous cone barriers satisfy F(tx)=F(x)−νlogt. The orthant barrier −∑i​logxi​ has parameter equal to the dimension, while the Lorentz-cone barrier −log(t2−∥z∥2) on t>∥z∥ has parameter two. Their Hessians define Dikin ellipsoids and control interior Newton steps.
