Barotropic equation of state

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 486 Articles: 11
A constant barotropic equation of state has P=wρ. The cases w=0 and w=1/3 describe pressureless matter and radiation respectively.
