Topological K-theory (K∗(X))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 39
Complex topological K-theory is the generalized cohomology theory built from stable equivalence classes of complex vector bundles. Its degree-zero group is the Grothendieck group K0(X), and its reduced theory is denoted K∗(X).
