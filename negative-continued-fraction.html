Negative continued fraction ([a1​,…,ar​]−)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Diophantine approximation Continued fraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
The finite negative continued fraction is
[a1​,…,ar​]−=a1​−a2​−⋯−1/ar​1​1​.
(88)
For ai​≥2, it describes a negative-framed chain of unknots: the diagonal framings are −ai​ and adjacent linking numbers are one. Repeated slam-dunk moves give rational surgery coefficient −[a1​,…,ar​]−. The corresponding chain matrix is negative definite. To check this, the successive determinants of its negative have recurrence dj​=aj​dj−1​−dj−2​, starting with d0​=1, d1​=a1​; they are positive and increasing, so Sylvester's criterion applies.
