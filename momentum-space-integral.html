Momentum-space integral (∫ddpf(p))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Momentum Momentum space
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
An integral over momentum space. For example, state counting in d spatial dimensions uses a measure proportional to ddp/(2πℏ)d, whereas a relativistic Feynman integral commonly uses ddp/(2π)d in natural units. The convention and any regulator must be specified. Independent loop momenta supply separate integration variables in a loop Feynman diagram; ultraviolet divergences and infrared divergences can require different regularizations.
