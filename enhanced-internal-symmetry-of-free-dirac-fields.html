Enhanced internal symmetry of free Dirac fields (m=0: U(2N);m=0: O(2N))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field theory Internal symmetry of a classical field theory Unitary flavour symmetry of degenerate Dirac fields
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
Without fixed gauge charges or interactions distinguishing particles from antiparticles, N free Dirac fields give 2N left-handed Weyl fields after charge conjugation of the right-handed fields. Their massless kinetic term has unitary group U(2N). Equal nonzero Dirac masses have equal positive singular values and can be written as 2N equal Majorana mass terms; their preserving group is the orthogonal group O(2N). Explicitly, in a basis with symmetric mass matrix m1, invariance requires V†V=VTV=1, hence real V. Restricting to transformations preserving a chosen common fermion number gives the usual U(N)L​×U(N)R​ at zero mass and U(N) at nonzero mass.
