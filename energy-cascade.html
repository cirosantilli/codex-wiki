Energy cascade

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 664 Articles: 16
An energy cascade transfers kinetic energy among scales through nonlinear motion. A forward cascade carries energy toward smaller spatial scales, where kinematic viscosity can dissipate it. Conservation and a mean scale-space flux do not uniquely prescribe the geometry or locality of the transfer mechanism.
