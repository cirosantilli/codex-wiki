This criterion holds even for reducible nonnegative matrices. If a fixed point p=0 exists, 1−e−z<z for z>0 gives (Λp)i​>pi​ on its support. Some α>1 therefore satisfies Λp≥αp, forcing the spectral radius above one by the Perron–Frobenius theorem. Conversely take v≥0, v=0, with Λv=ρv and ρ>1. For sufficiently small η>0, 1−e−ηρvi​≥ηvi​. Thus ηv is a subsolution; monotone iteration under T gives a positive fixed point below 1. Strictness of 1−e−z<z excludes survival at the critical value for this Poisson offspring law.
