Fold factorisation criterion

 Home Computer science Programming language Functional programming
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
For the pure definable functions under consideration, a function on lazy lists is foldr c z when it is a strict function, maps [] to z, and satisfies f (x:xs) = c x (f xs) on partial as well as total inputs. Necessity follows from the fold equations. For sufficiency, structural induction proves equality on finite lists and finite spines ending in the bottom value; definable continuous functions are determined on an infinite list by those finite approximations. The important information constraint is that a step receives the tail only through its folded result. Equal folded tails must yield equal extended results when the new head is the same.
