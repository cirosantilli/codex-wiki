Cosmological density parameter flow (dΩ/dloga)

For an expanding Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric with positive energy density, put Ω=8πGρ/(3H2) and w=P/ρ, in units c=1. The Friedmann equation, Friedmann acceleration equation and cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation give
H2H˙​=−1−21​(1+3w)Ω,dlogadΩ​=(1+3w)Ω(Ω−1).
(10)
For constant w, linearizing at Ω=1 gives Ω−1∝a1+3w. For variable w, replace the power by exp∫(1+3w(a))dloga. This describes the flatness problem for decelerating expansion, away from epochs at which H=0.
