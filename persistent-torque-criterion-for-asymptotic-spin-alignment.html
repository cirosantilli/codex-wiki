For nonzero Jh​,Jt​ and a noncollinear initial configuration, the alignment equation integrates to artanhc(t)=artanhc(0)+Jh​Jt​∫0t​K2​(s)ds. Hence the positive dissipative torque aligns the hole with the total vector if its integrated strength diverges. A fixed positive coefficient is sufficient. Pointwise positivity alone need not suffice for time-dependent coefficients: with Jh​=Jt​=1, K1​=0, K2​=e−t and c(0)=0, the limiting cosine is tanh1<1. Exactly antiparallel initial spin and total angular momentum form a stationary, nongeneric exception.
