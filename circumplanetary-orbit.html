Circumplanetary orbit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Kepler orbit
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 4
An orbit about a planet. Stellar tides, radiation pressure and other perturbations can limit its stability; a sufficiently small semi-major axis compared with the Hill sphere permits a local approximately planet-centred Kepler orbit.
