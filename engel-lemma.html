Engel lemma

 ... Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Lower central series of a Lie algebra Nilpotent Lie algebra Engel's theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 83 Articles: 1
A finite-dimensional Lie algebra representation in which every representing endomorphism is nilpotent has a nonzero vector annihilated by the entire Lie algebra. Applying this to the Adjoint representation is the key step in Engel theorem.
