Backbone hydrogen bond

 Home Biology Protein Protein structure Protein secondary structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A backbone hydrogen bond is a hydrogen bond involving groups of the protein backbone, typically a backbone oxygen chemical atom and a hydrogen chemical atom already joined by a covalent bond to backbone nitrogen. It can connect positions that are not adjacent along the polypeptide chain. The peptide bonds still determine which amino acid residues are consecutive. See the original structural argument cited under alpha helix.
