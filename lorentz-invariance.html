Lorentz invariance

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz transformation Lorentz covariance
OurBigBook.com Words: 72 Articles: 1
A physical quantity is Lorentz invariant when its value is unchanged under Lorentz transformations. Tensor equations can be Lorentz covariant while their components transform. Fixed-vector gauge choices need not make covariance manifest, although physical gauge-invariant predictions retain the symmetry.
