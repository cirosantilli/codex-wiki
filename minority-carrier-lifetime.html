Minority carrier lifetime (∂t​Δn=−Δn/τ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quasiparticle Electron hole Electron-hole recombination
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
In a weak-injection regime where the majority population is nearly fixed, a small excess minority charge carrier population often relaxes exponentially with time constant τ. This lifetime describes carrier removal by recombination. It differs from the momentum relaxation time governing charge carrier mobility: many momentum-scattering events can occur before a carrier recombines. Traps and spatial transport can make a single exponential inadequate.
