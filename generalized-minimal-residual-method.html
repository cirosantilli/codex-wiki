With zero initial guess, the generalized minimal residual method minimizes the Euclidean norm of the linear-system residual over each Krylov subspace. The Arnoldi decomposition reduces this to miny​∥Hk​y−∥b∥2​e1​∥2​, followed by xk​=Qk​y. Givens rotations solve the small problem in O(k2) operations once the decomposition is available. For an invertible matrix A, the same iterate minimizes the error in the weighted norm induced by ATA, because ∥Ax−b∥2​=∥x−x∗​∥ATA​.
