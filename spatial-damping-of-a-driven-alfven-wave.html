Spatial damping of a driven Alfvén wave (k=vA​ω​(1−iϵ)−1/2,ϵ=ηm​ω/vA2​)

 ... Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Magnetohydrodynamic wave Alfvén wave Resistive damping of an Alfvén wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
For fixed real positive angular frequency ω, a transverse Alfvén wave proportional to ei(kz−ωt) in a uniform fluid has k2=ω2/(vA2​−iηm​ω) when magnetic diffusivity ηm​ is retained and viscosity is neglected. Choose the branch with positive real and imaginary parts for propagation and attenuation along +z. For ϵ≪1, k=(ω/vA​)(1+iϵ/2+O(ϵ2)). The amplitude attenuation length is LA​=1/Imk, whereas the energy attenuation length is LE​=1/(2Imk). With wavelength λ=2π/Rek, LA​/λ∼1/(πϵ) and LE​/λ∼1/(2πϵ). Fixed-real-wavenumber temporal Resistive damping of an Alfvén wave is a different boundary-value prescription.
