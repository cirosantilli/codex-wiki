Fundamental and exterior-square weight systems of type A (εi​,εi​+εj​ (i<j))

OurBigBook.com Words: 84
For the special unitary group SU(N), put εi​=ei​−N−1∑j​ej​. Its defining representation has weight system {εi​}, and its exterior square has weight system {εi​+εj​:i<j}; each has multiplicity one. Their dual representations negate the weights of a representation. The Dynkin label coordinates are (εi​)r​=δir​−δi,r+1​. These follow by evaluating diagonal traceless generators on coordinate vectors and their exterior products.
Under the standard embedding SU(N−1)⊂SU(N), the last defining weight of a representation restricts to zero and the others become the defining weights of a representation of SU(N−1). Splitting pairs according to whether they contain the last index proves the branching of a representation VN​↓=VN−1​⊕1 and Λ2VN​↓=Λ2VN−1​⊕VN−1​, including multiplicities.
