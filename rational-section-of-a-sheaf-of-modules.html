Rational section of a sheaf of modules (Rat(F)=lim​U dense open​Γ(U,F))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules
OurBigBook.com Words: 347 Articles: 4
A rational section is a section on a dense open subset, with two representatives identified when they agree on a common dense open subset. Intersecting representatives' domains defines addition. Multiplication by a similarly represented rational function on an algebraic variety makes these classes a module over the ring Rat(X). For an irreducible variety, this ring is its function field.
