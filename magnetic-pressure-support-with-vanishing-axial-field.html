Magnetic pressure support with vanishing axial field

With no axial electric current, cylindrical magnetostatic pressure balance reduces to constant p+Bz2​/(8π). For p=p0​e−R2/a2 and Bz​(0)=0, its radial solutions are
Bz​=σ8πp0​​1−e−R2/a2​,jϕ​=−σ4πa2c8πp0​​​1−e−R2/a2​Re−R2/a2​,σ∈{−1,1}.
(12)
As R↓0, Bz​∼σ8πp0​​R/a and jϕ​→−σc8πp0​​/(4πa). These finite scalar limits do not give a smooth function of Cartesian position: R=x2+y2​ has a cusp at the axis, and the azimuthal unit vector has no unique limit there. Thus these data describe radial equilibrium away from the axis, but cannot satisfy smooth vector-field regularity on the axis without modifying the data.
