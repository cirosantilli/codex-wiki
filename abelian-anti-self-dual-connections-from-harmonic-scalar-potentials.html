Locally a U(1) connection whose gauge curvature has no (2,0) or (0,2) part can be written A=∂u+∂ˉv by the Dolbeault-Poincaré lemma. Unitarity permits v=−uˉ. Writing u=p+iq, a gauge transformation removes idq and leaves A=∂p−∂ˉp. Setting f=−2p gives the displayed unitary form, whose gauge curvature is ∂∂ˉf. Its remaining anti-self-duality of gauge curvature condition is the scalar Laplace equation. Alternatively a complex gauge transformation by e−u gives A=∂ˉ(v−u); this second gauge generally does not preserve unitarity.
