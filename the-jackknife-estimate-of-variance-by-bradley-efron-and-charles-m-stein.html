By Bradley Efron and Charles M. Stein, printed as B. Efron and C. Stein. Published in The Annals of Statistics 9(3), 586–596, 1981. The Institute of Mathematical Statistics is the publisher; Project Euclid hosts the article record.
Article record: projecteuclid.org/journals/annals-of-statistics/volume-9/issue-3/The-Jackknife-Estimate-of-Variance/10.1214/aos/1176345462.full
Bibliographic verification: Publications. The journal publication is distinct from the October 1978 Stanford technical report. Full text was unavailable during inspection; only the title and publication metadata are used.
