Product of a quotient map with the unit interval (q×id[0,1]​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Quotient topology Quotient map
OurBigBook.com Words: 110
If q:E→B is a quotient map, then q×id[0,1]​ is a quotient map. More generally the second factor may be any locally compact space that is Hausdorff. To prove the assertion, let W⊆B×[0,1] have open preimage O, and take (b,t)∈W. Choose e∈q−1(b) and a compact neighbourhood K of t with {e}×K⊆O. The set EK​={u:{u}×K⊆O} is open by compactness of K, and is saturated under q because membership in O depends only on q(u) and the second coordinate. Hence q(EK​) is open by the quotient topology. The rectangle q(EK​)×intK is a neighbourhood of (b,t) contained in W, proving that W is open. This justifies parameterized homotopies defined on quotient spaces.
