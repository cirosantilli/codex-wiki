Atomic mass (ma​)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Isotope
OurBigBook.com Words: 293 Articles: 4
The rest mass of a chemical atom in its ground state, a physical quantity expressed in SI mass units or the unified atomic mass unit. Here mi​ means the atomic mass of a neutral chemical atom of isotope i, not the mass of its bare atomic nucleus and not a population average.[ref]
Atomic mass includes Electrons and depends on nuclear binding energy; it is not just mass number times a universal nucleon mass. The atomic mass of neutral carbon-12 in its ground state is exactly 12u by definition of the unified atomic mass unit. That exactness establishes the reference scale, rather than making the atomic masses of all isotopes exact integers in the same unit.
