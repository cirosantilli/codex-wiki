Maximal ideal

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 5
A proper ideal m of a commutative ring R is maximal when no proper ideal lies strictly between m and R. Equivalently, R/m is a field.
