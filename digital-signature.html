Digital signature

 Home Computer science Cryptography
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 2
A public-verification message authentication scheme with key generation, signing using a private key, and verification using the corresponding public key. Signing an appropriate digest binds the signature to the message; encryption alone does not provide this authenticity.
