Gaussian random variable in a Banach space

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process Gaussian measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 1
A random variable X in a real separable Banach space B is Gaussian when ℓ(X) has a normal distribution for every continuous linear functional ℓ∈B∗. It is centered when all these expected values vanish. Its probability law is a Gaussian measure. Degenerate normal distributions are allowed.
