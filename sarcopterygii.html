Sarcopterygii

 ... Organism Eukaryote Animal Vertebrate Gnathostome Osteichthyes
OurBigBook.com Words: 86 Articles: 3
In crown-based usage, the clade comprising the most recent common ancestor of living coelacanths, lungfish and tetrapods, and all its descendants. Lobed, internally supported paired appendages provide a traditional diagnostic character; inherited organization can persist despite conversion of fins into limbs.
