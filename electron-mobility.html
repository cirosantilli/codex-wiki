Electron mobility (μe​)

 ... Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Current density Charge carrier Charge carrier mobility
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The positive electron mobility relates the drift velocity of conduction electrons to an electric field: vd​=−μe​E. In the isotropic Drude model, μe​=eτ/m∗, where τ is the momentum relaxation time and m∗ the band effective mass. The minus sign describes negative charge; the electron mobility itself is positive.
