Kusch-Foley magnetic moment experiment

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential Magnetic dipole moment Electron magnetic moment
OurBigBook.com Words: 243
Polykarp Kusch and Henry Michael Foley compared hyperfine structure resonance frequencies for different electronic states of gallium, indium and sodium. Their 1948 paper reports mutually consistent departures from the ratios expected with orbital factor 1 and spin factor 2. Comparing several states constrained the alternative explanation of perturbed atomic levels.[ref]
The Rabi molecular beam magnetic resonance experiment explains the readout principle: a resonant transition changes an atom's magnetic response, and hence its transmitted beam signal. Kusch describes rapidly interchangeable ovens, several radio frequency oscillators and careful field homogenization. Measurements taken during monotonic field drift were reduced to a common field.[ref]
Real hyperfine structure lines are not simply bare electronic gJ​ times field. Nuclear magnetic moments, known interaction constants and nonlinear field dependence enter the inference.[ref] Field homogeneity controls line broadening; alternating species addresses drift. Neither control removes an error in the atomic model. The beauty of the comparison is the separation of two problems: cancelling a shared calibration scale and testing whether several physical systems imply the same electron spin g-factor.
