Baryon decuplet (10=Sym33)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Flavor symmetry Eightfold way
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The completely symmetric three-quark representation of flavour symmetry has dimension ten. Its weight diagram has rows (Y,I)=(1,3/2),(0,1),(−1,1/2),(−2,0). It includes uuu at the Δ++ weight and sss at the Ω− weight. A spatially symmetric three-quark colour singlet realizes this multiplet with spin 3/2.
