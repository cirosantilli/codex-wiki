Equal-rate rocket burning and accretion (v(t)=u(1−e−αt/M0​))

 ... Branches of physics Classical mechanics Momentum Rocket equation Dust-collecting rocket Rocket equation with stationary mass accretion
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
If stationary mass arrives at the same constant time rate as fuel is ejected, total rocket mass stays fixed. The rocket equation with stationary mass accretion is then M0​v˙+αv=αu. For initial rest, speed approaches the relative exhaust speed exponentially with relaxation time M0​/α. This is the regular equal-rate limit of the unequal-rate mass-power formula.
