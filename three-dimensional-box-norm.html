Three-dimensional box norm (∥f∥□3​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Box norm
OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 1
For a complex function on X1​×X2​×X3​, its eighth power is the average of the conjugated product over the eight vertices of a coordinate cube. Three successive applications of the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality show that it bounds correlation with a product of three bounded functions, each omitting one coordinate. This is the analytic estimate behind the tetrahedron counting lemma.
