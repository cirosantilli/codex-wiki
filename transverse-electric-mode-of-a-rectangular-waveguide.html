Transverse electric mode of a rectangular waveguide (TEmn​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Rectangular electromagnetic waveguide
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For a transverse electric mode, Ez​=0 and Bz​=B0​(x,y)ei(kz−ωt). Put Δ=(ω/c)2−k2. Maxwell equations give Bx​=ik∂x​Bz​/Δ, By​=ik∂y​Bz​/Δ, Ex​=iω∂y​Bz​/Δ, and Ey​=−iω∂x​Bz​/Δ. The wall conditions become Neumann boundary conditions for B0​, whose separated solutions are cos(mπx/a)cos(nπy/b). The constant (0,0) candidate is excluded by Faraday's equation because its corresponding electric field vanishes.
