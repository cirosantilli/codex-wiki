Molecular weight cut-off

 Home Chemistry Biochemistry Dialysis (biochemistry)
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
The molecular weight cut-off rates retention of reference molecules. One manufacturer's definition uses the mass at which approximately 90% remains after seventeen-hour dialysis, tested with globular proteins. A 10kDa rating uses dalton units: a mass, not a length or an absolute passage threshold.[ref]
A semipermeable membrane has a distribution of passage sizes, and a molecule's shape affects passage as well as its mass. Near the nominal MWCO, slow passage can make substantial retention compatible with eventual leakage. The protein-retention experiment below makes retention an observable quantity rather than a universal yes-or-no property inferred from the label.
