Free-draining approximation

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Hydrodynamic interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
In the free-draining approximation, each separated component of a body experiences its isolated viscous drag, and hydrodynamic interactions between components are neglected. For widely separated spheres of radii ai​ in an unbounded Newtonian fluid of dynamic viscosity μ, Stokes law gives fi​=−ζi​vi​, with ζi​=6πμai​. This is a leading approximation in sphere size divided by separation. Any connecting rod must also have negligible drag; a small diameter alone does not guarantee this for an arbitrarily long rod.
