Vaidya metric (ds2=−(1−2M(v)/r)dv2+2dvdr+r2dΩ2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 265 Articles: 4
The ingoing Vaidya metric describes a spherically symmetric spacetime sourced by radial null matter, using advanced time v and areal radius r. In geometrized units it is ds2=−(1−2M(v)/r)dv2+2dvdr+r2dΩ2. A constant mass function reduces it to the Schwarzschild metric in ingoing null coordinates; a varying mass function changes the curvature and the radial null geodesics.
