Matching minimal covering nets of a compact group (F↔F′,f−1π(f)∈VV−1)

 ... Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Measure Haar measure Minimal left covering net of a compact group
OurBigBook.com Words: 102
Two minimal left covering nets for the same identity neighbourhood V can be matched bijectively so that paired translates intersect. Join f∈F to f′∈F′ when fV∩f′V=∅. For S⊆F, its neighbours N(S) cover every point covered by S, so (F∖S)∪N(S) still covers the group. Minimality forces ∣N(S)∣≥∣S∣. Hall's marriage theorem supplies the matching, and an intersection fv=f′v′ gives f−1f′=vv′−1∈VV−1. Averaging a continuous function over matched nets therefore gives values differing by at most its uniform oscillation on right displacements in VV−1. This is the finite combinatorial step in constructing Haar measure on a compact group.
