Planar viscous-sheet stretching equations (ht​+(hu)x​=0,(4μhux​)x​+γhhxxx​/2=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Viscous sheet
OurBigBook.com Words: 315 Articles: 3
A symmetric thin viscous sheet with shear-free faces has leading longitudinal velocity independent of thickness coordinate z, and incompressibility gives w=−zux​. Young–Laplace equation and the Newtonian fluid stress tensor give σzz​=−pa​+γhxx​/2 and σxx​=−pa​+γhxx​/2+4μux​. The effective longitudinal tension per unit transverse width is T=4μhux​+γhhxx​/2−γhx2​/4+2γ. The last two terms include the horizontal projections of the two surface tension pulls. Force balance gives Tx​=0, yielding the displayed longitudinal equation; the face kinematic conditions give the displayed conservation of mass equation.
