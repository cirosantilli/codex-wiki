Kinship coefficient (ϕXY​)

 Home Biology Genetics Identity by descent
OurBigBook.com Words: 217 Articles: 2
The kinship coefficient ϕXY​ is the probability that one randomly selected allele copy from each of X,Y is identical by descent. For a noninbred individual, ϕXX​=1/2, and more generally ϕXX​=(1+FX​)/2. For distinct individuals with parents MX​,PX​, Mendelian segregation gives ϕXY​=[ϕMX​Y​+ϕPX​Y​]/2. The offspring's inbreeding coefficient equals the parents' kinship coefficient.
