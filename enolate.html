Enolate

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance
OurBigBook.com Words: 256 Articles: 10
An enolate is the conjugate base formed by removing an alpha proton from a suitable carbonyl compound. Oxygen-centred and carbon-centred resonance contributors help explain its ability to react at either site.
