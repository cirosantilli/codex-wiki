Plane polar coordinates ((r,θ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Position
OurBigBook.com Words: 62 Articles: 1
Plane polar coordinates describe a point by its radial distance r and polar angle θ.
These are the planar polar coordinates; the general coordinate-system definition and transformations belong to that canonical concept.
