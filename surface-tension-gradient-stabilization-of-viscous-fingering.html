Suppose the apparent capillary pressure is p1​−p2​=γ(1+βx)(κ0​+∇⋅n), with normal towards the displaced fluid and positive apparent tension at the flat front x=X. Linearizing the Darcy law interface problem gives the local growth rate
σ(α;X)=ϕ(μ1​+μ2​)α​[(μ2​−μ1​)U−kγκ0​β−kγ(1+βX)α2].

The pressure increase experienced by a forward protrusion is stabilizing when κ0​β>0. It suppresses every positive wavenumber when U≤kγκ0​β/(μ2​−μ1​). For a fixed spatial gradient, X=Ut/ϕ makes the rate time dependent; constant exponential growth is a frozen-position approximation.
