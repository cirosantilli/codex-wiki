Cumulative distribution function (FX​(x))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 563 Articles: 12
The cumulative distribution function of a real random variable X is
FX​(x)=P(X≤x).
(48)
It is nondecreasing, right-continuous, tends to zero at −∞, and tends to one at +∞.
