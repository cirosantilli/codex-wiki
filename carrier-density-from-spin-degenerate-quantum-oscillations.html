Carrier density from spin-degenerate quantum oscillations

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Landau level Filling factor (quantum Hall effect)
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
One resolved spin branch of a two-dimensional Landau level has degeneracy eB/h per area. Thus the total carrier density is ns​=νeB/h, where the filling factor ν counts all occupied spin and other internal branches, including partial filling. If consecutive unsplit orbital peaks change filling by an unresolved degeneracy gd​,
ns​=hΔ(1/B)gd​e​.
(82)
For only spin degeneracy, gd​=2. Once spin splitting is resolved, adjacent individual branches differ by one rather than two; counting split subpeaks as unsplit orbital peaks gives a factor-two error.
