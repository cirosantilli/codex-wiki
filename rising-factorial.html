Rising factorial ((a)n​=a(a+1)⋯(a+n−1))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Factorial
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
For a nonnegative integer n, the rising factorial is the product of n consecutive increments starting at a, with (a)0​=1. Another notation is an; the falling factorial uses descending factors. It compactly expresses the coefficient recurrence of a confluent hypergeometric function of the first kind.
