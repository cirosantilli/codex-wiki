Radiative equilibrium of a single grey cloud layer (Tg4​=Q/[σ(1−e/2)])

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Irradiated planetary atmosphere Greenhouse effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A black surface receiving absorbed stellar irradiance Q, beneath a single non-solar-absorbing cloud with thermal emissivity e>0, satisfies Tc4​=Tg4​/2 when both bodies are in pure radiative equilibrium. The surface balance is σTg4​=Q+eσTc4​, giving the displayed result. If instead convection pins Tc​=qTg​, the whole-column balance gives Tg4​=Q/[σ(1−e+eq4)]; the nonradiative energy budget cannot then be discarded.
