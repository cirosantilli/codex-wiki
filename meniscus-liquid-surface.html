Meniscus (liquid surface)

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry) Solution preparation and volumetric calibration
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A liquid meniscus is the curved boundary where a liquid meets a vessel wall. In the transparent water examples above, the bottom of this curve is aligned with the calibration mark at eye level. Reading from above or below changes the apparent alignment without changing the contained volume.
