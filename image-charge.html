Image charge

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric field Electric potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 13
An image charge is a fictitious point charge placed outside the physical solution region so that its potential, combined with the real sources, enforces the required conductor boundary values. The Uniqueness of the Dirichlet problem then identifies the constructed potential with the physical one. The image need not describe the actual induced surface-charge distribution.
