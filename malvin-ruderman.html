Malvin Ruderman (1927-2024)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysicist
OurBigBook.com Tags: Physicist Words: 41 Articles: 1
With Charles Kittel, he calculated conduction-electron-mediated exchange between nuclear magnetic moments. His later research included neutron stars. Columbia's account of his career also recalls his enthusiasm for order-of-magnitude teaching.[ref]
