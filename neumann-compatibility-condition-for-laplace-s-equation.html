Neumann compatibility condition for Laplace's equation (∫∂Ω​gdS=0)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Boundary value problem Neumann boundary-value problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For a smooth bounded region Ω, prescribing the outward normal derivative ∂n​u=g of a harmonic function requires
∫∂Ω​gdS=0.
(1)
This follows from the divergence theorem applied to the gradient:
∫∂Ω​∂n​udS=∫Ω​∇⋅∇udV=∫Ω​ΔudV=0.
(2)
On the unit disk, the condition becomes ∫02π​g(θ)dθ=0. If g=∑n≥1​(an​cos(nθ)+bn​sin(nθ)) is sufficiently regular with zero mean, its regular harmonic expansion on a disk is
u(r,θ)=C+∑n≥1​nrn​(an​cos(nθ)+bn​sin(nθ)).
(3)
The factor 1/n compensates for the radial derivative. The arbitrary C reflects the uniqueness of the Neumann problem up to an additive constant on a connected region.
