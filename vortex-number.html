Vortex number (N)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field-theory soliton Gauge-theory soliton Abelian Higgs model Nielsen-Olesen vortex
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The vortex number is the total multiplicity of the zeros of the Higgs field, equivalently its phase winding at infinity or its magnetic flux divided by 2π in the standard normalization.
