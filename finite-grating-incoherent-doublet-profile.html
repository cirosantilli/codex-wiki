For narrow slits and equally bright mutually incoherent spectral lines, uj​=πpsinθ/λj​ and the intensities add as displayed. The Rayleigh criterion uses a main-peak-to-first-zero separation δλ≃λ/(∣m∣N). A slightly sub-Rayleigh doublet can still have two local maxima: the criterion is not a theorem of statistical non-identifiability. photon counts, background, unequal strengths and uncertainty in the instrumental profile govern actual inference.
