Greedy best-first search (f(n)=h(n))

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Graph Graph search Best-first search
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 1
Best-first search using only a heuristic function estimating remaining goal cost, without adding accumulated path cost. A comparator supplying the same total ordering of estimates suffices to choose expansions even if numeric estimates are unavailable. In general it need not be a complete search algorithm or an optimal search algorithm; particular stronger information or finite-graph policies can supply additional guarantees.
