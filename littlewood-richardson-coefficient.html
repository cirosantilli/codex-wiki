Littlewood–Richardson coefficient (cαβν​)

 ... Young tableau Tabloid Polytabloid Specht module Specht filtration Littlewood–Richardson rule
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
The nonnegative integer cαβν​ is the multiplicity of Sν in IndS∣α∣​×S∣β∣​S∣ν∣​​(Sα⊠Sβ) in characteristic zero. The Littlewood–Richardson rule counts it by Semistandard Young tableaux of skew shape ν/α, content β, and lattice word in right-to-left, top-to-bottom reading order. For example c(3,2,1),(3,2)(5,3,2,1)​=3: the two new top cells must be ones, and the remaining reading letters can be 122,212,221. This coefficient also multiplies Schur functions in the induction ring of symmetric-group characters.
