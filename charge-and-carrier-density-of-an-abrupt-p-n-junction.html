Charge and carrier density of an abrupt p-n junction

 Home Physics Semiconductor P-n junction Depletion region Depletion approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
For a zero-bias abrupt p-n junction, choose the p side at x<0 and the n side at x>0. Ionized acceptors and donors remain fixed while mobile electrons and electron holes diffuse and establish a built-in electric field. In the depletion approximation, mobile carriers are neglected on −xp​<x<xn​ and the net charge density is
ρ(x)=⎩⎨⎧​−eNA​,+eND​,0,​−xp​<x<0,0<x<xn​,outside the depletion region.​
(21)
Charge neutrality gives NA​xp​=ND​xn​. The quasi-neutral bulk can have a large mobile carrier density while its net charge density is nearly zero, because fixed dopant charge compensates it. With complete ionization and nondegenerate carriers, equilibrium also gives np=ni2​; real carrier profiles vary smoothly rather than forming exact step functions at depletion edges.
