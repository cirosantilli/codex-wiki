Neimark–Sacker bifurcation (λ1,2​=e±iθ,d=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 211 Articles: 2
A Neimark–Sacker bifurcation of a smooth planar map occurs when a simple complex-conjugate pair of fixed-point Floquet multipliers crosses the unit circle away from low-order resonances. If its radial normal form is r′=r(1+η+dr2)+⋯ with d=0, a small invariant closed curve has r2=−η/d+⋯. For d<0 it appears on η>0, where the fixed point has become unstable, and its radial Floquet multiplier is 1−2η+⋯, so the bifurcation is supercritical. For d>0 the curve lies on η<0 and is unstable, giving a subcritical bifurcation. Area-preserving maps have a vanishing cubic radial drift at an elliptic point and do not meet this dissipative nondegeneracy condition.
