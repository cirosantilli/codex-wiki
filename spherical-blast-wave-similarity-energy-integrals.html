Spherical blast-wave similarity energy integrals (Ekin​=2πE∫0ξ0​​ξ4fρ​fu2​dξ)

Use the fields of pressure similarity in a spherical blast wave, with front radius R=ξ0​ℓ. Since ρ0​ℓ5/t2=E, changing the radial integration variable to ξ gives
Ekin​=2πE∫0ξ0​​ξ4fρ​fu2​dξ,Eint​=γ−14πE​∫0ξ0​​ξ2fp​dξ.
(1)
Both are time independent for fixed integrable similarity profiles and constant front coordinate ξ0​. With explosion energy E and negligible external energy input or losses, their sum equals E. This normalization, together with shock matching and regularity, fixes the dimensionless blast coefficient rather than dimensional analysis alone.
