Heat semigroup ((Pt​))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Diffusion equation Heat equation Heat kernel
OurBigBook.com Words: 91 Articles: 1
The heat semigroup acts by convolution with the heat kernel: Pt​f=Kt​∗f. The identity Ks​∗Kt​=Ks+t​ gives Ps​Pt​=Ps+t​, and its infinitesimal generator is a constant multiple of the Laplacian.
