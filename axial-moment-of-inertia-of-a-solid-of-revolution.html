Axial moment of inertia of a solid of revolution (Iz​=2ρπ​∫r(z)4dz)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics Moment of inertia
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
A uniform solid with circular cross-sectional radius r(z) can be sliced into thin discs. A slice of thickness dz has mass dm=ρπr(z)2dz and axial moment of inertia dIz​=21​r(z)2dm. Thus
M=ρπ∫r(z)2dz,Iz​=2ρπ​∫r(z)4dz.
(20)
All slice axes coincide, so no parallel axis theorem correction is needed. For a linearly tapered section of length l with end radii r0​,r1​, direct integration gives M=ρπl(r02​+r0​r1​+r12​)/3 and Iz​=ρπl(r04​+r03​r1​+r02​r12​+r0​r13​+r14​)/10.
