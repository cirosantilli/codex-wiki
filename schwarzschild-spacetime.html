Non-traversability of the Schwarzschild bridge

Words: 56
Kruskal spacetime OurBigBook.com
In Kruskal–Szekeres coordinates, every future-directed radial causal curve has nondecreasing U and V. The right exterior has U<0,V>0, while the left has U>0,V<0. No future-directed causal curve can travel between the exteriors because it would have to decrease one null coordinate. The spatial bridge on a time-symmetric slice is therefore not a traversable passage.

Gravitational redshift measured by a computation counter (ΔN≃fop​τgΔh/c2)

Eddington experiment

Words: 96
Schwarzschild light deflection OurBigBook.com
The 1919 solar eclipse expeditions organized by Frank Watson Dyson and Arthur Eddington compared background-star positions near the obscured Sun with reference photographs.
