Ultraviolet fixed point (β(g∗​)=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Renormalization group
OurBigBook.com Words: 70 Articles: 1
A zero of the beta function approached by the running coupling as the energy scale increases. A simple one-coupling fixed point with β′(g∗​)<0 attracts nearby flows. Such a point can control two-point scaling at an ultraviolet fixed point.
