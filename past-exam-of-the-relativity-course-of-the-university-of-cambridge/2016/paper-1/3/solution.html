Use signature (+−−−), coordinates x0=ct and four-velocity uμ=dxμ/dτ normalized by uμ​uμ=c2. The derivative is the metric-compatible Levi-Civita connection. Fix the curvature sign by [∇α​,∇β​]Vμ=Rμναβ​Vν. Then the required contractions are
Rνβ​=Rμνμβ​,R=gνβRνβ​.​
(9)
With Gμν=Rμν−gμνR/2, the contracted Bianchi identity gives ∇μ​Gμν=0. The Einstein field equations Gμν+Λgμν=(8πG/c4)Tμν, with constant Λ, therefore require covariant conservation of stress-energy. This is a consistency relation between geometry and the total matter/field source. It does not mean ∂μ​Tμν=0 in arbitrary coordinates, nor guarantee a global conserved energy in an arbitrary curved spacetime. The geometric identity alone, without the field equations or matter dynamics, is not an equation of motion for a freely specified material source.
For relativistic dust, ρ is proper rest-mass density, measured in the fluid rest frame, and uμ is its timelike four-velocity with the c2 normalization above. In a local Cartesian inertial frame uμ=γ(c,v), so
T00=ργ2c2,T0i=ργ2cvi.​
(10)
Accordingly T0i/c is momentum density and cT0i is energy flux in direction i. The equality of momentum-density and energy-flux components after the appropriate factors of c reflects the symmetry of the stress-energy tensor. In the comoving frame the spatial stress is zero, as required for pressureless matter.
Expand the dust conservation equation by the product rule:
0=uν∇μ​(ρuμ)+ρuμ∇μ​uν.
(11)
Contract with uν​. metric compatibility and constant normalization give uν​∇μ​uν=∇μ​(uν​uν)/2=0, leaving c2∇μ​(ρuμ)=0. Substitute this rest-mass continuity law back and divide by nonzero ρ:
uμ∇μ​uν=0,dτ2d2xν​+Γαβν​dτdxα​dτdxβ​=0.​
(12)
This proves geodesic motion of uncharged dust along each fluid flow line. pressure forces or another exchange of momentum would change the argument; at zero density there is no dust worldline to constrain.
The electromagnetic field tensor is antisymmetric and has six independent components. In the stated local inertial coordinates choose F0i=−Ei​/c and Fij=−ϵijk​Bk​, giving
Fμν=​0Ex​/cEy​/cEz​/c​−Ex​/c0Bz​−By​​−Ey​/c−Bz​0Bx​​−Ez​/cBy​−Bx​0​​.
(13)
The fields are those measured by that local observer; changing observer mixes them through the Lorentz transformation of F. With ϵ0123=+1 and Fμν=ϵμναβFαβ​/2, two independent quadratic contractions are
Fμν​Fμν=2(B2−E2/c2),Fμν​Fμν=−c4​E⋅B.​
(14)
The second is a pseudoscalar: it is invariant under proper Lorentz transformations and changes sign under an orientation reversal. Opposite choices for the orientation tensor change its written sign. A nonzero null electromagnetic field can have both contractions zero, so the two numbers do not determine whether the tensor itself vanishes.
For charged relativistic dust, insert jα=(q/m)ρuα into the force-density equation:
uν∇μ​(ρuμ)+ρuμ∇μ​uν=mq​ρFναuα​.
(15)
Contraction with uν​ again gives the continuity law, because uν​Fναuα​=0 by antisymmetry. After removing the continuity term,
uμ∇μ​uν=mq​Fναuα​.​
(16)
In a local inertial frame the spatial components reduce to d(γmv)/dt=q(E+v×B) and the temporal component gives d(γmc2)/dt=qE⋅v. This is the relativistic Lorentz force, with gravity represented by the connection. The force is orthogonal to u and preserves the rest-mass normalization. Matter alone now exchanges energy and momentum with the electromagnetic field; adding the field tensor's stress-energy restores zero divergence of the combined source.
