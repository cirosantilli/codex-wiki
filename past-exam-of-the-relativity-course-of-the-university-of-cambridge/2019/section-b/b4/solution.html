Use signature (+−−−) and local inertial coordinates x0=ct. Write uμ=γ(c,v) and w=ρc2+p, where ρ is rest mass density. The perfect fluid in general relativity has components
T00=wγ2−p,T0i=Ti0=cwγ2vi​​,Tij=c2wγ2vi​vj​​+pδij​.
(5)
Here T00 is energy density, cTi0 is energy flux, T0i/c is momentum density, and Tij is the flux of the jth momentum component through a surface normal to i. In the local rest frame these reduce to energy density ρc2, zero energy and momentum flow, and isotropic pressure p. The equation ∇μ​Tμν=0 expresses local stress-energy conservation. Its temporal and spatial components give energy and momentum balance; in flat coordinates, for example, ∂t​T00+∂i​(cTi0)=0.
For relativistic dust, Tμν=ρuμuν. Contracting its divergence with uν​, and using metric compatibility together with uν​uν=c2, gives
0=uν​∇μ​(ρuμuν)=c2∇μ​(ρuμ)+2ρ​uμ∇μ​(uν​uν)=c2∇μ​(ρuμ).
(6)
Consequently ∇μ​(ρuμ)=0. Substituting this continuity equation back into the divergence leaves ρuμ∇μ​uν=0. Wherever matter is present,
uμ∇μ​uν=0,​
(7)
so the individual particles follow geodesics.
The electromagnetic four-force law fμ=qFμνuν​ must preserve the particle's rest mass. Thus uμ​fμ=0, and requiring this for arbitrary timelike four-velocities makes the electromagnetic field tensor antisymmetric. Its six independent components comprise the electric field and magnetic field. With the stated signature and x0=ct, one consistent convention is
Fi0=Ei​/c,F0i=−Ei​/c,Fij=−ϵijk​Bk​.
(8)
It gives the spatial force dp/dt=q(E+v×B) and energy transfer dE/dt=qE⋅v. Under a Lorentz transformation, Fμν transforms as a rank-two contravariant tensor; its electric and magnetic components consequently mix. A four-potential supplies Fμν​=∇μ​Aν​−∇ν​Aμ​, unchanged by Aμ​↦Aμ​+∇μ​χ. The Maxwell equations are
∇μ​Fμν=μ0​jν,∇α​Fβγ​+∇β​Fγα​+∇γ​Fαβ​=0.
(9)
These combine the source equations and the homogeneous equations into covariant tensor statements; jμ is the four-current.
For charged relativistic dust, the matter tensor exchanges four-momentum with the electromagnetic field: ∇μ​Tmμν​=Fναjα​, while their total stress-energy tensor is conserved. Identical particles of rest mass m and electric charge q have jα=(q/m)ρuα. Contraction with uν​ again gives mass continuity because uν​Fναuα​=0. Removing that term gives
uμ∇μ​uν=mq​Fναuα​.​
(10)
Using zero divergence for the matter tensor alone in this last step would omit the electromagnetic Lorentz force.
