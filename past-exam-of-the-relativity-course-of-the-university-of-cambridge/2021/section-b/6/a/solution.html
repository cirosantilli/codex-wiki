Take α>0 and work in a connected patch with x=0. Setting the line element to zero gives
dtdx​=±αx,x(t)=Ce±αt,C=0.​
(35)
These are the two families of Rindler null geodesics. They remain on the same side of x=0 and approach it only at infinite coordinate time. x=0 itself is not an additional geodesic within this chart, since the metric is degenerate there.
The paths can also be written t=±α−1log∣x/C∣. In two dimensions a null direction has only one independent orthogonal null direction, namely itself, so each smooth null curve is a geodesic after reparametrization. More explicitly, the transformation in part (d) makes these paths straight null lines. The coordinate t is generally not an affine parameter.
