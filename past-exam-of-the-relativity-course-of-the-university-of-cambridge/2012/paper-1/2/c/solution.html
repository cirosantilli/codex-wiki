With Cartesian Minkowski spacetime coordinates xμ=(ct,x,y,z), introduce the four-current Jμ=(cρ,J) and electromagnetic four-potential Aμ=(ϕ/c,A). The antisymmetric electromagnetic field tensor Fμν=∂μAν−∂νAμ has components F0i=−Ei​/c and Fij=−ϵijkBk​. Maxwell equations become
∂μ​Fμν=μ0​Jν,∂μ​Fμν=0,​Fμν=21​ϵμνρσFρσ​,ϵ0123=+1.
(8)
The time and space components reproduce Gauss's law and the Ampère-Maxwell equation; the dual equation gives absence of magnetic monopoles and Faraday's law of induction. Antisymmetry gives charge conservation ∂ν​Jν=0. The relativistic Lorentz force is dpμ/dτ=qFμνuν​. Every displayed equation transforms tensorially under a Lorentz transformation; the electric field and magnetic field are different components of the same object.
Two independent algebraic electromagnetic field invariants are
Fμν​Fμν=2(B2−E2/c2),Fμν​Fμν=−4E⋅B/c.​
(9)
The second is a pseudoscalar: it is invariant under proper Lorentz transformations but changes sign under spatial reflection. For perpendicular fields, the sign of the first distinguishes a frame with purely magnetic field from one with purely electric field. Both invariants vanish for a plane electromagnetic wave with E⊥B and E=cB, although the field itself is nonzero.
