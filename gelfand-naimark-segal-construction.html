Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction (Hx​=A/Nx​​,Nx​={a:x(a∗a)=0})

 ... Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra C-star algebra Positive functional on a C-star algebra State on a C-star algebra

For a state on a C-star algebra x on a unital algebra A, put ⟨[a],[b]⟩=x(b∗a) on A/Nx​, with the inner product linear in its first argument. The Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for positive C-star functionals makes the null vectors pair to zero with every vector. Moreover c∗c≤∥c∥21 gives x((ca)∗(ca))≤∥c∥2x(a∗a), so Nx​ is a left ideal and [a]↦[ca] is well-defined and bounded by ∥c∥. Completion gives a Hilbert space Hx​ and a star-representation πx​(c)[a]=[ca]. The adjoint identity follows from x(b∗ca)=⟨[a],πx​(c∗)[b]⟩. The unit vector ξx​=[1] is cyclic and recovers the state as x(a)=⟨πx​(a)ξx​,ξx​⟩.
