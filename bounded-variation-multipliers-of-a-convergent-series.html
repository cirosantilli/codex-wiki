If ∑an​ converges and bn​ is bounded with ∑∣bn+1​−bn​∣<∞, then ∑an​bn​ converges. For Tj​=∑k=mj​ak​, summation by parts gives
​∑j=mn​aj​bj​​≤supj≥m​∣Tj​∣(∣bn​∣+∑j=mn−1​∣bj​−bj+1​∣).

The first factor tends uniformly to zero by the Cauchy sequence criterion, and the second has a uniform bound. A bounded monotone sequence has finite total variation, so monotone multipliers are included. Eventual monotonicity also suffices, since finitely many initial terms do not affect convergence. Absolute convergence of ∑an​ is unnecessary.
