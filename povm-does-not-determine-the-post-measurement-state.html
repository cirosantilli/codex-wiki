POVM does not determine the post-measurement state

A POVM fixes outcome probabilities, but a quantum instrument additionally fixes the conditional output state. For computational-basis effects Ey​=∣y⟩⟨y∣, the operators My​=∣y⟩⟨y∣ leave the measured eigenstate as output, whereas Ny​=∣0⟩⟨y∣ reset every outcome to ∣0⟩. Both have My†​My​=Ny†​Ny​=Ey​.
