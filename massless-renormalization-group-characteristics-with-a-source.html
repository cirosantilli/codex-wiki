For a normalized connected functional obeying (μ∂μ​+β​∂λ​+γ​h​h∂h​)F=0, solve the displayed ordinary differential equations and keep F constant along their curves. Where β​=0, log(μ/μ∗​)=∫λ∗​λ(μ)​dg/β​(g) and h(μ)=h∗​exp∫λ∗​λ(μ)​γ​h​(g)dg/β​(g). For the dimensionless density f(λ,y) of a uniform source, with y=h/μ3, the equation is [β​∂λ​+(γ​h​−3)y∂y​+d]f=0. Its characteristic invariant is yexp[−∫(γ​h​−3)dg/β​], and its value acquires the factor exp[−∫ddg/β​]. A finite-volume total functional also retains the dimensionless volume argument.
