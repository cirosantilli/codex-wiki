Topological surface

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 59
A topological surface is a Hausdorff space with a countable basis in which every point has a neighborhood homeomorphic to an open subset of R2.
