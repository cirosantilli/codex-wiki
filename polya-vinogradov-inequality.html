Pólya–Vinogradov inequality (∣∑M<n≤M+N​χ(n)∣≪q​logq)

OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For a primitive nonprincipal Dirichlet character modulo q, finite Fourier inversion theorem and the primitive Gauss magnitude give this bound uniformly in the interval. Bound the exponential sums by C∥a/q∥−1 and sum the resulting harmonic series. The general Gauss identity follows from the prime-power identity by the Chinese remainder theorem.
