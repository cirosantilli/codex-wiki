Weakly interacting Bose gas (nex​/n≪1)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Bose-Einstein statistics Bose-Einstein distribution Bose-Einstein condensation
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 22
A dilute condensed Bose system whose interaction is sufficiently weak for a small-depletion expansion. Its low-energy excitations are described by a Bogoliubov transformation rather than free particles. The homogeneous quadratic theory is controlled only while noncondensate occupation remains small compared with the condensate and its excitation energies are stable.
