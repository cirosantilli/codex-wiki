Disappearance rate (vA​=−d[A]/dt)

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Chemical kinetics Reaction rate
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
The disappearance rate of a reactant A is its positive net loss of concentration per unit time, vA​=−d[A]/dt. For the fixed-volume, single-chemical reaction setting of reaction rate, consuming a units of A per conversion gives vA​=av. The factor belongs to the stoichiometry; stating it is essential when quoting a rate constant. A negative net disappearance rate means that A is accumulating.
