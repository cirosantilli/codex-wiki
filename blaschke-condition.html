Blaschke condition (∑n​(1−∣an​∣)<∞)

The Blaschke condition for a sequence in the unit disc is ∑n​(1−∣an​∣)<∞. It characterizes the zero sequences, counted with multiplicity, of nonzero bounded holomorphic functions. Sufficiency follows by constructing the Blaschke product. For necessity, Jensen's formula gives ∑∣an​∣<r​log(r/∣an​∣)≤log∥f∥∞​−log∣f(0)∣ when f(0)=0. Letting r↑1 and using −log∣a∣≥1−∣a∣ proves the condition. A finite zero at zero is factored out first. Equivalently, for the curvature-minus-one hyperbolic metric, ∑n​e−ρ(0,an​)<∞, since e−ρ(0,a)=(1−∣a∣)/(1+∣a∣).
