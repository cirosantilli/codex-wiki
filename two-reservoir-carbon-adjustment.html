Two-reservoir carbon adjustment (tp​≪tC​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Climate change Carbon cycle
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
A coupled atmosphere–ocean mass conservation model can have a fast atmospheric exponential relaxation time and a much slower ocean-carbon time. For atmospheric inventory coefficient I, exchange coefficient h, ocean water mass Mo​, removal coefficient b and linear surface-pressure relation ps​=C/K, atmospheric balance gives p≃C/K+(v−AL​W)/h. Substitution into the ocean equation cancels the weathering and exchange terms, giving (Mo​/b)C˙=v/(MCO2​​b)−C. Sustained extra emissions therefore first raise pressure at nearly fixed ocean concentration and then increase both reservoirs over the ocean removal time.
