Quasi-geostrophic approximation (QG approximation)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 266 Articles: 10
The quasi-geostrophic approximation describes slowly evolving, nearly geostrophic flow with small Rossby number. Its dynamics are governed by advection and forcing of potential vorticity.
