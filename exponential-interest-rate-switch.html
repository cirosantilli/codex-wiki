Exponential interest-rate switch (Rγ1​+λ(γ1​/γ0​)R=D1​)

 ... Mathematical finance Utility function Expected utility hypothesis Expected utility maximization Investment-consumption problem Merton consumption-investment problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 118
Consider an investment-consumption problem with an independent one-way rate change of intensity λ from r1​ to r0​. For a stock of constant drift μ and positive volatility σ, set Bj​=rj​+(μ−rj​)2/(2Rσ2), γ0​=[ρ−(1−R)B0​]/R, and D1​=ρ+λ−(1−R)B1​. In the finite-value regime γ0​,D1​>0, the prechange consumption coefficient is the unique positive root displayed above. The left side increases continuously from zero to infinity. Before the change, the value is γ1−R​w1−R/(1−R), the stock fraction is (μ−r1​)/(Rσ2), and consumption is γ1​w. At the change these become the ordinary Merton consumption-investment problem controls for r0​. The jump term in the Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation is λ(V0​−V1​); knowing only the marginal law of the change time does not justify that term without a conditional intensity assumption.
