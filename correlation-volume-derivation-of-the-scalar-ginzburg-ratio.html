Correlation-volume derivation of the scalar Ginzburg ratio (G≍uc−d/2∣r∣(d−4)/2)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Landau theory Landau-Ginzburg theory Ginzburg criterion
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
For the dimensionless scalar Landau-Ginzburg theory with stiffness c, quadratic coefficient r<0 and quartic term uϕ4/4, the mean-field approximation gives ϕ02​=∣r∣/u and correlation length ξ≍(c/∣r∣)1/2. Restricting the Gaussian Fourier transform covariance (cq2+2∣r∣)−1 to ∣q∣≲ξ−1 estimates the variance of the field averaged over a correlation volume as c−d/2∣r∣(d−2)/2, up to a dimension-dependent constant. Dividing by ϕ02​ proves the displayed Ginzburg criterion ratio. It diverges below four dimensions, vanishes above four and is marginal at four. Short-distance fluctuations renormalize microscopic coefficients and should not be confused with this long-distance critical test.
