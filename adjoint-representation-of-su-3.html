Adjoint representation of SU(3) (8=R(1,1))

OurBigBook.com Words: 156 Articles: 1
The complex Adjoint representation of SU(3) is the action by conjugation on traceless 3×3 matrices. With H1​=diag(1,−1,0) and H2​=diag(0,1,−1), its highest-weight vector is E13​ of Dynkin labels (1,1). The six off-diagonal matrix units have weights (2,−1),(−2,1),(−1,2),(1,−2),(1,1),(−1,−1), and the two-dimensional Cartan subalgebra has weight zero. In coordinates (h1​,(h1​+2h2​)/3​), the weight diagram is a regular hexagon together with a central point of weight multiplicity two. This representation describes both the gluon colour multiplet and the approximate flavour baryon octet, under different physical SU(3) symmetries.
