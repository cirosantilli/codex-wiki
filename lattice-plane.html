Lattice plane

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Crystal structure

A lattice plane is a plane containing a two-dimensional periodic array of lattice points. Parallel lattice planes form equally spaced geometrical sheets; an atomic basis can place chemical atoms between them.
With primitive lattice translation vectors ai​, write r=xa1​+ya2​+za3​. For integers h,k,l with greatest common divisor one, the plane equations
hx+ky+lz=m,m∈Z,
(6)
give a complete family labeled by Miller indices (hkl). In a primitive cubic lattice, (110) means x+y=m, parallel to a3​.[ref]
