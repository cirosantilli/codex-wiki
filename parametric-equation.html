Parametric equation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 66 Articles: 1
A parametric equation expresses coordinates as functions of one or more independent parameters. For example, x=cost, y=sint describes a circle; multiple parameters describe surfaces. A smooth curve is a differentiable single-parameter realization. A reparametrization changes the parameter without changing the geometric image.
