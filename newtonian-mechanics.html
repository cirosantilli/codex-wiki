Newtonian mechanics (F=ma)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 498 Articles: 8
Newtonian mechanics describes motion using forces, masses and acceleration in inertial frames with absolute time. It is the low-speed limit of special relativity; for constant proper acceleration with ∣at∣≪c, relativistic hyperbolic motion reduces to x≃at2/2.
