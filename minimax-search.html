Minimax search (V(s)=maxs′​V(s′) or mins′​V(s′))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Game tree
OurBigBook.com Words: 82 Articles: 2
Backward induction on a finite deterministic game tree of a two-player zero-sum game. Terminal payoffs are propagated by maximization at one player's decision nodes and minimization at the other's. With full information, the resulting choices give an optimal strategy against the opposing player's best response. A cyclic state graph requires a winning condition on infinite plays and a graph algorithm rather than unrestricted recursive unfolding.
