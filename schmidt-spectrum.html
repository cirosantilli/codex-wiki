The nonzero eigenvalues, including multiplicities, of either reduced density operator of a bipartite pure state. A Schmidt decomposition ∣ψ⟩=∑k​pk​​∣ak​bk​⟩ gives ρA​=∑k​pk​∣ak​⟩⟨ak​∣ and the corresponding expression for ρB​ by partial trace. Thus unequal marginal spectra rule out a pure joint state. The amplitudes pk​​ are often called Schmidt coefficients; the spectrum here uses the probabilities pk​.
