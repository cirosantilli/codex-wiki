OurBigBook.com Words: 51
In a binomial random graph with fixed edge probability below one, the displayed first-moment bound tends to zero for t=Clogn and sufficiently large fixed C. At the same time the edge density concentrates near p. Thus a positive fixed density cannot force balanced complete multipartite subgraphs whose part size grows faster than logarithmically.
