Discrete dynamical system

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 56
A one-dimensional discrete dynamical system iterates a map xn+1​=F(xn​). A fixed point satisfies F(x∗)=x∗, while a point of least period k satisfies Fk(x)=x but no corresponding equation for a smaller positive period.
