For the round-trip phase in a Michelson interferometer, take a lossless 50:50 beam splitter with t=1/2​ and r=i/2​, and mirror reflection amplitude −1. I(δ)=2∣A0​∣2​[1+cos(2kδ)]=∣A0​∣2cos2(kδ).​

The printed ∣A0​∣2(1+2coskδ) cannot be an intensity: it becomes negative, and does not match the depicted path difference. No choice of normalization fixes that negative minimum. For the half-ring sine-squared angular state, write C=8/(3π)​. The stated wavefunction is normalized because C2∫0π​sin4ϕdϕ=1. It and its first derivative match continuously to zero at the boundaries, so the integrations by parts have no boundary contributions. The angular momentum expectation and second moment are
⟨Lz​⟩=−iℏ∫ψψ′dϕ=0,⟨Lz2​⟩=ℏ2∫∣ψ′∣2dϕ=C2ℏ2∫0π​sin2(2ϕ)dϕ=34​ℏ2.
(49)
Hence Var(Lz​)=4ℏ2/3. The jumps in the second derivative do not invalidate this result: the periodic wavefunction has a square-integrable second derivative and a continuous first derivative.

c1​=2π​C​∫0π​sin2ϕe−iϕdϕ=3​π2​(−34i​)=−33​π8i​,P(Lz​=ℏ)=27π264​≃0.2402.​
