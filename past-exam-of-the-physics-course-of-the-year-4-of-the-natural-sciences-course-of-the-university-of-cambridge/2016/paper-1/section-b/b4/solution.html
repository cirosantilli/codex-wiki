For the normal modes of two spring-coupled equal pendulums, a normal mode has every coordinate oscillating at the same frequency with a fixed displacement ratio. The linearized equations here are
mx¨1​=−mω02​x1​−k(x1​−x2​),mx¨2​=−mω02​x2​−k(x2​−x1​).
(30)
Their normalized displacement patterns and angular frequencies are
e+​=2​(1,1)​,ω+​=ω0​;e−​=2​(1,−1)​,ω−​=ω02​+2k/m​=1.1ω0​.​
(31)
The spring is unstretched in the in-phase mode, and stretched or compressed in the opposite-phase mode. The numerical ratio follows from k/m=(21/200)ω02​.
The initial displacement is the equal sum of the two mode patterns, and the initial velocities vanish. Therefore
x1​(t)=2a​[cosω0​t+cos(1.1ω0​t)],x2​(t)=2a​[cosω0​t−cos(1.1ω0​t)]=asin(1.05ω0​t)sin(0.05ω0​t).​
(32)
With the printed ω0​=2πrads−1 this is x2​/a=sin(2.1πt)sin(0.1πt), with t in seconds. The fast oscillation has frequency 1.05Hz and the amplitude is bounded by a∣sin(πt/10)∣. The first complete displacement transfer is at t=5s, when x1​=0, x2​=a and both velocities vanish; the original state returns at 10s. Distinguishing absolute and signed beat envelopes, the absolute beat envelope repeats every 10s, although its signed sine factor has period 20s.
