Relativistic synchrotron radiation is concentrated within angle of order 1/γ around the instantaneous velocity, tangent to the circular orbit. The narrow instantaneous beam sweeps around with the particle; over a whole orbit the emission forms a thin fan about the orbital plane. With E≫me​c2, γ≃5000/0.5=104. Since νorb​≃c/(2πR), the supplied characteristic-frequency scaling gives
λchar​∼γ32πR​≃6.3×10−10m.​
(6)
This is subnanometer X-ray radiation, with a beaming angle of order 10−4rad. The conventional critical-angular-frequency normalization ωc​=3γ3c/(2R) gives 4.2×10−10m instead; the question's order-of-magnitude frequency prescription does not distinguish these order-one factors.
