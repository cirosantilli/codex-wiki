For nonequivalent p-s and p-p term counting, the closed 3s2 shell has L=S=0. For 3p4s, the open orbitals give L=1 and S=0,1, hence
3p4s:1P1​,3P0,1,2​.​
(8)
Hund's rules predict the order 3P0​<3P1​<3P2​<1P1​.
For 3p4p, the electrons are in nonequivalent orbitals. All L=0,1,2 and S=0,1 combinations are allowed:
3p4p:1S0​, 1P1​, 1D2​, 3S1​, 3P0,1,2​, 3D1,2,3​.​
(9)
Applying maximum spin first, maximum orbital angular momentum next, and smallest J first for these less-than-half-filled open subshells gives the qualitative ordering
3D1​<3D2​<3D3​<3P0​<3P1​<3P2​<3S1​<1D2​<1P1​<1S0​.​
(10)
The state counts are 12 for 3p4s and 36 for 3p4p. Imposing the equivalent-p2 restrictions would incorrectly discard allowed terms. These are Hund-rule predictions; exact interleavings in a real excited configuration require the actual Coulomb and spin-orbit matrix elements.
