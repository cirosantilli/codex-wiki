In the sudden approximation, the nuclear charge changes before the electronic wavefunction has time to evolve. Immediately afterward the state is still the original hydrogenic 1s wavefunction, now expanded in the eigenstates of the new Coulomb Hamiltonian. The final-ground-state probability is the squared overlap, rather than one minus an energy change.
