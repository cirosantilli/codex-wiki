Path (b) changes pressure before temperature. Its pressure integral is unchanged, but the thermal expansion occurs at P2​:
Wb​=21​V1​κT​(P22​−P12​)−V1​αP2​(T2​−T1​).​
(30)
Along the straight path (c), P=P1​+(P2​−P1​)(T−T1​)/(T2​−T1​). Thus ∫T1​T2​​PdT=(P1​+P2​)(T2​−T1​)/2, while ∫PdP depends only on the pressure endpoints. Consequently
Wc​=21​V1​κT​(P22​−P12​)−21​V1​α(P1​+P2​)(T2​−T1​)=2Wa​+Wb​​.​
(31)
This pressure-temperature path-work identity is a consequence of the linearized volume law and the straight interpolation. It need not hold for strongly varying compressibility or expansion coefficient.
