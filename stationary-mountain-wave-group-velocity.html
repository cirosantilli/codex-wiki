Stationary mountain-wave group velocity (cg​​=Uk(k,m)/(k2+m2))

Define intrinsic positive frequency ω and ground frequency ω^=Uk+ω for a nonrotating internal gravity wave. Stationarity with U>0 selects k<0 and k2+m2​=N/U. Differentiating the full dispersion before imposing that condition gives the displayed ground-frame group velocity. Upward energy propagation requires m<0 and ∣k∣<N/U. Its ray inclination is cos−1(U∣k∣/N). The ground-frame group velocity is opposite the normal intrinsic phase velocity; the intrinsic group velocity is perpendicular to that phase velocity. A strictly stationary ground-frame phase pattern has zero phase velocity, so mixing frames can produce a false angle claim.
