Weyl theorem for bounded compact perturbations (Σe​(L+K)=Σe​(L))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Lower norm of an operator Fredholm operator Essential spectrum of a closed operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
For bounded L and compact K, use the Fredholm convention Σe​(L)={z:L−zI is not Fredholm}. Applying compact perturbation invariance of Fredholm operators to every shift proves the equality. This general theorem needs no self-adjointness; other definitions of essential spectrum for nonnormal operators must be distinguished.
