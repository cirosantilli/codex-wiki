Quantum circuit gate-error telescoping bound (∥C′−C∥≤∑j​∥Uj′​−Uj​∥)

Words: 90
Quantum circuit OurBigBook.com
For two quantum circuits with corresponding unitary gates, their difference is a sum of terms containing one gate difference and exact or perturbed products on either side. Unitary invariance and the triangle inequality for the operator norm therefore bound the circuit difference by the sum of individual gate errors. Tensoring a gate with an identity leaves that norm unchanged. On a normalized input, the same bound controls the output vector distance with gate phases retained. This is a worst-case coherent bound; cancellation can make a particular circuit less sensitive, but cannot be assumed without further information.

Depth-first quantum circuit path summation

Words: 55
Quantum circuit OurBigBook.com
Expand a quantum circuit matrix element by inserting computational basis resolutions between gates, then enumerate the intermediate strings depth first. The active stack stores one basis label and accumulator per quantum circuit layer, using polynomial space for polynomially many qubits and gates. Recompute entries rather than storing an exponential matrix or state vector. The output probability requires the Born rule sum of squared amplitude moduli.

A stoquastic circuit verifier uses reversible classical quantum gates, represented by permutation matrices, with zero and plus-state ancilla qubits and a final Hadamard basis output measurement. Words: 38

If the output of a stoquastic circuit is ∣0⟩∣r0​⟩+∣1⟩∣r1​⟩ with nonnegative amplitudes, its plus-outcome probability is 1/2+Re⟨r0​∣r1​⟩≥1/2. Basis quantum witnesses with zero and plus ancilla qubits always have this property. A soundness threshold below one half cannot hold for every such input.

Feynman-Kitaev Hamiltonian (Hin​+Hprop​+Hout​)

Words: 146 Articles: 2
Computational history state OurBigBook.com
A Feynman-Kitaev Hamiltonian penalizes incorrect input initialization, disagreement between successive quantum circuit steps and clock labels, and optionally a rejecting output. Its propagation quadratic form is a sum of 21​∥ηt​−Ut​ηt−1​∥2. Without output penalty, its zero-energy space consists of correctly initialized computational history states. A nonlocal quantum clock makes the propagation formula simple but does not supply fixed qubit locality by itself.

Gap above a degenerate history ground space (Δ≥(T+1)−3)

Words: 51
Feynman-Kitaev Hamiltonian OurBigBook.com
After undoing quantum circuit propagation, the Hamiltonian without output penalty is Q⊗∣0⟩⟨0∣+I⊗E. The common kernel is the valid input space times the uniform clock vector. On its orthogonal complement, the smallest angle between two subspaces obeys cosϑ=T/(T+1)​. The Kitaev geometrical lemma and path gap 2/(T+1)2 give Δ≥(T+1)−3. Degenerate valid quantum witnesses must be removed together when computing this angle.

Input penalty of a history Hamiltonian (Hin​=Q⊗∣0⟩⟨0∣)

Words: 44
Feynman-Kitaev Hamiltonian OurBigBook.com
The input penalty of a history Hamiltonian tests the prescribed ancilla qubits at clock time zero and leaves the quantum witness unrestricted. Zero ancilla qubits are tested by ∣1⟩⟨1∣, and plus ancilla qubits by ∣−⟩⟨−∣. Their sum Q is positive and has positive integer eigenvalues. Gershgorin gap bound for an adiabatic history path (Δ(M(s))≥1−2s)
Words: 51
History-subspace propagation Hamiltonian OurBigBook.com
For M(s)=(1−s)D+sE in the unary history subspace, the first Gershgorin disc lies in [0,s] and every other disc has real part at least 1−s. For s<1/2, the first disc is isolated and contains the unique ground state energy. Thus the spectral gap is at least 1−2s, in particular at least 1/3 for s≤1/3.
Spectrum of a path propagation Hamiltonian (λk​=1−cos[πk/(T+1)])
Words: 50
History-subspace propagation Hamiltonian OurBigBook.com
For a path with T+1 vertices and edge weight 1/2, the eigenvalues are 1−cos[πk/(T+1)], k=0,…,T. The corresponding coefficients are proportional to cos[πk(t+1/2)/(T+1)]. The zero mode is uniform, and the spectral gap is at least 2/(T+1)2. The endpoint difference equations use reflecting ghost values, not periodic ones.

Two-qubit gate (U∈U(4))

Words: 193 Articles: 2
Quantum logic gate OurBigBook.com
A two-qubit gate is a unitary operator on two qubit lines, extended by the identity on the others. It may be entangling, as a CNOT gate is, or a product of local operations. In a Hamiltonian simulation model allowing arbitrary two-qubit gates, the exponential of a Hermitian term supported on two lines counts as one such gate. Compiling it into a fixed discrete universal quantum gate set introduces a separate approximation cost.

Diagonal two-qubit phase entanglement criterion (δ=ϕ00​−ϕ01​−ϕ10​+ϕ11​)

Words: 66
Two-qubit gate OurBigBook.com
A diagonal two-qubit gate with entries eiϕjk​ is a product of local diagonal gates, up to global phase, exactly when δ=0 modulo 2π. This follows by factoring the two-by-two array of phases; its determinant vanishes precisely in that case. If the determinant is nonzero, applying the gate to ∣+⟩⊗∣+⟩ produces an entangled state by the two-qubit product-state determinant criterion. In particular diag(−1,1,1,−1)=−Z⊗Z is local, whereas diag(1,1,1,−1) is the entangling Controlled-Z gate.

Every two-qubit gate has the displayed form with local elements of the special unitary group and real interaction parameters. Measurement-based quantum computation (MBQC)

Words: 570 Articles: 8
Quantum circuit OurBigBook.com
Measurement-based quantum computation prepares an entangled resource quantum state, then applies single-qubit measurement in quantum measurements, potentially adapting later bases to earlier outcomes. Pauli frame tracking and classical outcome processing convert random physical branches into a specified logical computation. Graph states and one-bit teleportation provide a concrete construction.

Measure vertex one in the equatorial qubit measurement basis ∣vr​(α)⟩=(∣0⟩+(−1)reiα∣1⟩)/2​, then vertex two at angle (−1)rβ, obtaining bits r,s. Measurement pattern

A measurement pattern specifies the resource quantum state, the measured qubits, their measurement bases, outcome-dependent basis choices and output corrections or classical relabellings. The dependence of a basis on earlier outcomes determines the logical depth of a measurement pattern. Preparing the resource and processing the final classical results are separate costs.

Logical depth of a measurement pattern

Words: 292 Articles: 3
Measurement pattern OurBigBook.com
Logical measurement depth is the number of sequential measurement layers required by outcome-dependent basis choices. Depth one means all bases are fixed in advance, so the measurement in quantum measurements on distinct qubits can be simultaneous. Classical output corrections may still depend on all outcomes. This does not assert depth-one graph-state preparation or constant-depth classical processing.
Two-layer measurement pattern for CNOT and x-axis rotations (measurement depth≤2)
Words: 86
Logical depth of a measurement pattern OurBigBook.com
An R(α)=J(α)J(0) gadget with input Pauli frame XpZq, angle-zero outcome s, and arbitrary-angle outcome t uses angle (−1)s⊕qα and produces frame Xp⊕tZq⊕s. The CNOT gate gadget also updates its Z frames without any dependence on incoming X frames. Therefore all adaptive angle signs depend solely on angle-zero outcomes. Measure all those vertices first, compute every sign, and measure all remaining vertices in a second layer. The result is the desired logical circuit in a known Pauli frame. Computational output measurements can share the second layer.

Nonadaptive Hadamard–CNOT measurement pattern (measurement depth≤1)

Uniform coprime state for a semiprime (∣ξN​⟩=φ(N)−1/2∑1≤k<N, gcd(k,N)=1​∣k⟩)

Words: 89
Quantum state preparation OurBigBook.com
For a semiprime N=pq with distinct known primes, this quantum state is uniform over the reduced residue classes. With n binary digits in N, its fraction among all 2n computational labels is a=(p−1)(q−1)/2n≥1/4. An ancilla qubit whose one probability is 1/(4a) dilutes the joint good probability to exactly 1/4. A reversible range-and-greatest common divisor predicate marks the good labels with ancilla qubit one, and a single exact amplitude amplification iteration produces ∣ξN​⟩∣1⟩. Known prime factors provide the cardinality; the membership test itself needs only N. Polynomial gate complexity presumes ideal one-qubit rotations at the specified amplitudes.

Pauli-string commutation parity (PQ=(−1)kQP)

Words: 58
Pauli group OurBigBook.com
For tensor products of Pauli factors from {I,X,Y,Z}, let k count the sites where both factors are nonidentity and different. Distinct nonidentity Pauli matrices anticommute, and equal or identity factors commute. Reversing the product site by site therefore gives PQ=(−1)kQP. Thus two Pauli strings commute exactly when k is even. The criterion applies to the perfect-correlation observables in the GHZ theorem.

Isotropic subspace of a binary symplectic space (ω∣L×L​=0)
Words: 64
Binary symplectic space of Pauli labels OurBigBook.com
A subspace L is isotropic when the symplectic pairing vanishes between every pair of its vectors. Its binary Pauli labels therefore commute. Nondegeneracy identifies the ambient space with its dual, so the map to L∗ is surjective and dimL⊥=2n−dimL. Since L⊆L⊥, it follows that dimL≤n. This explains the maximum number of independent generators of a stabilizer group.

Pauli operator OurBigBook.com

A nonidentity stabilizer contains at least one nonidentity local Pauli matrix and has zero trace, since the trace of a tensor product is the product of local traces. Its eigenvalues are therefore ±1 with equal multiplicities. This trace fact also gives dimXS​=2n/∣S∣ from the stabilizer-projector formula.
