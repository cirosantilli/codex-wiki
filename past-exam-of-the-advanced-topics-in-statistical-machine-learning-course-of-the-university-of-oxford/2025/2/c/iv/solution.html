An infinitely wide neural network in this regime follows the same training-prediction dynamics as kernel regression with its neural tangent kernel and matching initial predictions. For this squared-error loss function, the relevant algorithm is gradient flow in the Reproducing kernel Hilbert space. Matching predictions at arbitrary additional inputs also requires the corresponding cross-input neural tangent kernel to freeze and the initial predictions there to match. A finite-width neural network can change its feature map during training, so the equivalence depends on the assumptions rather than holding for every architecture and parameterization.
