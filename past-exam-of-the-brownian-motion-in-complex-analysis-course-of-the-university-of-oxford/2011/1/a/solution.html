The clock is finite at every finite time, since a continuous Brownian path has compact range there and f′ is continuous. To show its lifetime is infinite, choose a small disc on which ∣f′∣2≥c>0; such a disc exists because the entire function is nonconstant. A supporting recurrence result is that planar Brownian motion spends infinite total time in every nonempty open disc almost surely. One proof combines recurrent returns to a smaller disc with the strong Markov property: each visit has a uniformly positive chance of lasting a fixed positive time in the larger disc.
