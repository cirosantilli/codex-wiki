Vector field along a map (Y∈Γ(f∗TN))

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Pullback vector bundle Pullback tangent bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 174 Articles: 2
A vector field along a smooth map between manifolds f:M→N assigns Yp​∈Tf(p)​N smoothly to each p. It is a section of the pullback tangent bundle, not necessarily a vector field on N. Along a curve γ, the velocity γ˙​ is such a section.
