Under the same hypotheses, g(Tp) is reducible exactly when every coefficient of g is a pth power in K. If g=∑aip​Ti, then
g(Tp)=(∑ai​Ti)p.
(45)
Conversely, if g(Tp) is reducible, all its irreducible factors are separable. Its zero derivative forces every factor multiplicity to be divisible by p, so it is a pth power in K[T] and all its coefficients are pth powers.
