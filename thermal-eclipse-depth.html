For blackbody star and planet, define the planet-star contrast
rλ​=F∗,λ​Fp,λ​​=(R∗​Rp​​)2Bλ​(T∗​)Bλ​(Tp​)​.
(5)
A complete exoplanet secondary eclipse removes the planet's light from the combined out-of-eclipse light. Its exact normalized depth is dλ​=rλ​/(1+rλ​). The common approximation dλ​≃rλ​ requires rλ​≪1. This thermal model omits reflected light and spatial variations.
