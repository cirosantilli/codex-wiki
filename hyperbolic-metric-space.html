Hyperbolic metric space (δ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Quasi-isometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 469 Articles: 11
For δ≥0, a geodesic metric space is δ-hyperbolic when each side of every metric geodesic triangle lies in the closed δ-neighbourhood of the other two sides.
