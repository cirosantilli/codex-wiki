Viscous boundary layer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 420 Articles: 7
A viscous boundary layer is a boundary layer next to a surface in which viscosity remains important even when the outer flow has a large Reynolds number. It reconciles the outer inviscid velocity with the no-slip boundary condition.
