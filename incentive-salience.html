Incentive salience

 Home Science Psychology Motivation
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
The motivational attractiveness assigned to a represented outcome or its cue, often described as cue-triggered wanting. It can vary without a corresponding change in experienced pleasure or explicit belief about value.[ref]
