Four-velocity (Uμ=dXμ/dτ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 61 Articles: 1
A massive observer's four-velocity is the tangent Uμ=dXμ/dτ to its worldline, parametrized by proper time. Its invariant squared Minkowski norm is c2 in the +−−− metric signature, and −c2 in the opposite convention. Unlike the coordinate values Xμ, the tangent transforms as a vector under a general coordinate change.
