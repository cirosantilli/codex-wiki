Phase reduction (θ˙=ω+ϵZ(θ)⋅p)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Periodic orbit Limit cycle
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 2
Near a hyperbolic attracting limit cycle, a weak perturbation can be projected onto the neutral phase direction while transverse perturbations decay. Solving the periodic adjoint variational equation gives a phase response function. Averaging its inner product with weak forcing or coupling yields the first-order phase dynamics.
