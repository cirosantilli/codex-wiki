Quantum statistical mechanics

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Statistical mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
quantum statistical mechanics describes equilibrium and fluctuations with density matrices and traces over a quantum Hilbert space. The canonical equilibrium state is e−βH/Z, while particle exchange introduces H−μN in the grand canonical ensemble. Noncommutativity and particle exchange distinguish it from a classical phase-space distribution.
