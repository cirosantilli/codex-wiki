Effective conductivity (⟨a∇u⟩=a∗⟨∇u⟩)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Composite material
OurBigBook.com Words: 818 Articles: 16
Effective conductivity describes the averaged response of a microscopic conductivity field to a macroscopic temperature or electric-potential gradient. Here the positive conductivity flux is a∇u; physical heat flux has the opposite sign. For periodic media it is computed by cell correctors, and can be a tensor even for isotropic constituents.
