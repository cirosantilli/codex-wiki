Sequential response selection model (qj​(y)=P(A≥j∣A≥j−1,Y=y))

Suppose outcome collection stops at the first success or after m unsuccessful attempts, and let A count unsuccessful attempts. Conditional on the outcome y, put qj​(y)=P(A≥j∣A≥j−1,Y=y). For k<m, the joint density of A=k and an observed Y=y is
fβ​(y)(∏j=1k​qj​(y))(1−qk+1​(y)).
(20)
For A=m, the unobserved outcome is integrated out, giving ∫fβ​(y)∏j=1m​qj​(y)dy. These probabilities sum to one by telescoping and integration. Dependence of qj​ on y models missing not at random. If all qj​ are independent of y conditional on the regression predictors, the outcome density among responders remains fβ​, as required for complete-case analysis of that conditional model.
