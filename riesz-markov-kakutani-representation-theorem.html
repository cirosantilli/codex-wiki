Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 258 Articles: 4
Every positive linear functional φ on C(K) for a compact Hausdorff space K has a unique representation
φ(f)=∫K​fdμ
(31)
by a finite regular positive Borel measure μ. More generally, C(K)∗ is isometrically the space of finite regular complex Borel measures with the total-variation norm.
