Powerful pro-p group ([G,G]⊆Gpϵ)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Profinite group Pro-p group
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A pro-p group whose closed commutator subgroup lies in its closed power subgroup Gpϵ, with ϵ=1 for odd p and ϵ=2 for p=2. This is the profinite analogue of a powerful p-group. Finitely generated torsion-free examples are exactly the uniform pro-p groups. The additive group of a finite product of p-adic integers is an abelian example.
