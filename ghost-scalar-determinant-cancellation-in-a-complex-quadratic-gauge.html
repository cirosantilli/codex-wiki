Ghost-scalar determinant cancellation in a complex quadratic gauge

 ... Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing Nonlinear Abelian gauge fixing Complex quadratic Abelian gauge condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
A massless complex bosonic charged scalar field with Dμ​=∂μ​+iAμ​ contributes [det(−D2)]−1 to a Gaussian background-field integral. A Faddeev-Popov ghost field pair contributes detOA​. On the formal complex quadratic Abelian gauge condition, OA​=−D2, so the two determinants cancel and their effective-action contributions sum to zero. Use matching regulators, boundary conditions and removed zero modes. This is a formal cancellation of closed loops; the reality obstruction for a complex Euclidean gauge condition precludes deducing that physical scalar quantum electrodynamics has no quantum effects.
