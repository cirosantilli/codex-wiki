Operator conjugation by an exponential (eSHe−S=H+[S,H]+21​[S,[S,H]]+⋯)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Matrix exponential
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For finite matrices, or formally on a common invariant domain where the expansion is meaningful, exponential conjugation is generated by the commutator map. Differentiating F(s)=esSHe−sS gives F′=[S,F], and its Taylor series yields the nested-commutator expansion. An anti-Hermitian S makes this a unitary transformation. For perturbation theory this may be used order by order without asserting convergence for arbitrary unbounded operators.
