Closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution

 ... Physics Branch of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy evolution Galactic chemical evolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 167 Articles: 4
The closed-box model evolves a fixed total baryonic mass with no inflow or outflow. Under instantaneous recycling and constant yield it predicts Z=ylog(1/μ) for gas fraction μ.
