Jacobi identity ([x,[y,z]]+[y,[z,x]]+[z,[x,y]]=0)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Lie bracket
OurBigBook.com Words: 175
The Jacobi identity is the compatibility condition on a Lie bracket
[x,[y,z]]+[y,[z,x]]+[z,[x,y]]=0.
(22)
Together with an Alternating Lie bracket, it defines a Lie algebra. Unlike associativity, it relates three cyclic bracketings rather than equating two bracketings. Equivalently, using antisymmetry of a Lie bracket,
[x,[y,z]]=[[x,y],z]+[y,[x,z]].
(23)
Thus the linear map adx​:y↦[x,y] is a derivation of a Lie algebra: acting on a bracket is the sum of acting on each argument. This makes infinitesimal symmetries act consistently on each other's products. It also gives [adx​,ady​]=ad[x,y]​, the identity that makes the Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra a Lie algebra representation.
For the commutator in an associative algebra, the cancellation is directly visible:
[A,[B,C]][B,[C,A]][C,[A,B]]​=ABC−ACB−BCA+CBA,=BCA−BAC−CAB+ACB,=CAB−CBA−ABC+BAC.​
(24)
Each word occurs once with each sign. This proves the Jacobi identity without a restriction on the characteristic of a field.
For a computed check in the sl2 matrix commutator example,
[H,[E,F]]+[E,[F,H]]+[F,[H,E]]=[H,H]+[E,2F]+[F,2E]=0+2H−2H=0.
(25)
Here the last two terms are individually nonzero over characteristic zero; the identity records their cancellation. In the cross-product Lie algebra, the same cyclic cancellation is the vector triple product identity.
The historical connection is unusually concrete: Lie and Engel's volume I expands nested commutators of differential operators into twelve terms and cancels them cyclically, in Chapter 5, §26, equation (7). See Lie and Engel, §26, in Merker's translation, pp. 105–106.
