Helium-3 A phase (dA​=Δd(kx​+iky​))

Take a real unit vector in spin space d and choose orthogonal orbital axes. The model has
AA​=Δd(1,i,0),∣dA​∣=Δkx2​+ky2​​=Δ∣sinθ∣.
(29)
It vanishes at the two polar directions and is largest at the equator. The orbital factor changes sign when k is reversed, as required for the odd orbital part of spin-triplet p-wave pairing. Calling the phase A does not mean its order parameter is one real number.
