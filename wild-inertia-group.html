Wild inertia group (G1​)

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Ramification group Inertia group
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
The wild inertia group is the first ramification group G1​. It is the unique Sylow subgroup of the inertia group for the residue characteristic.
