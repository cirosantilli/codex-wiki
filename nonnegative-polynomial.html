Nonnegative polynomial

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 345 Articles: 4
A real polynomial is globally nonnegative if its value is nonnegative at every point of its real domain. A sum of squares polynomial is always nonnegative, but the Horn copositive matrix gives a nonnegative quartic polynomial that is not a sum of squares polynomial.
