Formal pseudodifferential composition rule (∂nf=∑r≥0​(rn​)f(r)∂n−r)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differential operator Formal pseudodifferential operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
The generalized Leibniz rule extends composition of differential operators to all integer n. Define (rn​)=n(n−1)⋯(n−r+1)/r!. For nonnegative n the sum stops at r=n. For n=−s<0, its coefficients are (−1)r(rs+r−1​); in particular ∂−1f=f∂−1−f′∂−2+f′′∂−3−⋯. Applying ∂ on the left cancels consecutive derivative terms and recovers multiplication by f, which explains the inverse expansion.
