Magnetic entropy deficit (Δsmag​=smax​−s(T,B))

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Temperature Adiabatic demagnetization Magnetic entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
The magnetic entropy deficit is the entropy increase still available to independent moments as their field polarization is removed. For a spin-J multiplet, smax​=kB​ln(2J+1) and the high-temperature deficit is kB​J(J+1)[gμB​B/(kB​T)]2/6. Isothermal magnetization creates this deficit while rejecting heat to a bath. Subsequent reversible demagnetization can absorb entropy from a cooling load. The deficit, not the initial absolute magnetic entropy, measures that capacity.
