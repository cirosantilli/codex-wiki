For two particles, let M=m1​+m2​ and Vcm​=(m1​v1​+m2​v2​)/M. The centre-of-mass frame velocities are vi∗​=vi​−Vcm​, so m1​v1∗​+m2​v2∗​=0. Thus before and after an isolated collision the two momentum vectors are equal and opposite. For an elastic collision their common magnitude is also unchanged: only their direction rotates.
The kinetic energy decomposition about the center of mass separates unchanged bulk translation from relative motion:
K=21​MVcm2​+21​μ∣v1​−v2​∣2,μ=Mm1​m2​​.
