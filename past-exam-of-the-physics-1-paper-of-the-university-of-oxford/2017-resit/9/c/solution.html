For masses m and 2m, the constant centre-of-mass velocity is (V/3)ex​. Initially the laboratory velocities are Vex​ and zero, while the centre-of-mass frame velocities are (2V/3)ex​ and −(V/3)ex​. After the elastic collision they are
v1∗​=32V​(cosχ,sinχ),v2∗​=−3V​(cosχ,sinχ).
(26)
Adding (V/3,0) produces the lower-left panel's laboratory velocity vectors. The given 30∘ laboratory angle implies
tan30∘=1+2cosχ2sinχ​,χ=30∘+arcsin(1/4)≃44.48∘.​
(27)
The centre-of-mass frame scattering angle is therefore different from the laboratory angle. Reflection across the x-axis gives the equally possible negative-angle configuration.
