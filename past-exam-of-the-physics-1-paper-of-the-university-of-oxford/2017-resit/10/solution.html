Einstein's postulates state that the laws of physics have the same form in every inertial frame, and that light in vacuum has the same speed of light c in every inertial frame, independently of the motion of its source. For a primed frame moving with velocity vex​ relative to an unprimed frame, with coincident origins at t=t′=0, the Lorentz transformations are
x′=γ(x−vt),t′=γ(t−vx/c2),y′=y,z′=z,γ=(1−v2/c2)−1/2.​
