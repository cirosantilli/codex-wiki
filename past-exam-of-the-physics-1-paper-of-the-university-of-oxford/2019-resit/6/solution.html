mf​=23​m+32​mc2v2​+O(v4/c4).​

The leading term is the classical sum of masses. The first correction is the internal energy retained after sticking, divided by c2: the initial classical kinetic energy is 3mv2/4, while the common-motion kinetic energy is 21​(3m/2)(v/3)2=mv2/12. Their difference is 2mv2/3, consistent with mass-energy equivalence.
