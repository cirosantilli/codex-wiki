A totally inelastic collision in the sticking sense has zero final relative velocity, so q=0. Both velocities are the conserved centre-of-momentum frame velocity:
v1′​=v2′​=M1​+M2​M1​u1​​x.​
(32)
Thus the bodies move together along the original incident direction; the moving projectile has θ1​=0. The common outgoing state is stationary in the centre-of-momentum frame. The lost translational kinetic energy is
ΔK=21​M1​+M2​M1​M2​​u12​,
(33)
which can become internal energy. As explained in part 8, setting only the normal coefficient of restitution to zero in a smooth oblique collision does not force sticking: tangential relative motion can remain.
