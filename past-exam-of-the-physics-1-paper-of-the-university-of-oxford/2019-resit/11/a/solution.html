About a fixed origin in an inertial frame, the angular momentum and torque are
L=r×mv,τ=r×F.​
(62)
For a specified fixed axis with unit vector n, use the projections Ln​=n⋅L and τn​=n⋅τ. The mass is constant. Differentiating and applying Newton's second law,
dtdL​=v×mv+r×mv˙=0+r×F=τ​.
(63)
Hence L˙n​=τn​ for the fixed axis. If the net torque vanishes, the corresponding angular momentum is conserved. Fixing the origin and axis is essential to this unmodified form of the law.
