Strictly, release from rest at a small angle θ0​ replaces 1−cosθ by cosθ0​−cosθ. The displayed result is the θ0​→0 limiting energy relation. Exactly upright and exactly at rest is an unstable equilibrium; without a perturbation it stays there. This distinction prevents the infinitesimal displacement convention from being mistaken for spontaneous motion.
