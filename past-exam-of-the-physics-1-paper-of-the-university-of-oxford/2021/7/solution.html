Inertia is resistance to a change of motion. Translational inertia is measured by mass in Newton's second law; rotational inertia about a specified fixed axis is measured by the moment of inertia

During the short elastic collision, neglect the impulse from gravity. The pivot can exert an external impulse, so total linear momentum of rod plus particle need not be conserved. Its torque about the pivot is zero, so conservation of angular momentum about that point applies. With the pivot as origin and z downward, its trajectory is x=(v0​/2)t, z=l+gt2/2, where t=0 at the collision. The rod instead swings about the fixed pivot in a vertical plane as a physical pendulum.
