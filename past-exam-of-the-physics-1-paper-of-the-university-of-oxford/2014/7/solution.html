Let θ measure the spoke's displacement from downward equilibrium. The total moment of inertia about the axle is I=MR22​+mR12​: the rim is a thin thin hoop, and the attached body is a point mass. The rim's weight has no axle torque, while the attached mass gives restoring torque −mgR1​sinθ. Hence
(MR22​+mR12​)θ¨+mgR1​sinθ=0,θ(0)=θ0​,θ˙(0)=0.​
(18)
Equivalently conservation of energy gives 21​Iθ˙2=mgR1​(cosθ−cosθ0​). For small angles, sinθ≃θ, so the motion is approximately θ=θ0​cos(ωt) with ω2=mgR1​/I. The small-amplitude period is
T=2πmgR1​MR22​+mR12​​​.​
(19)
At finite amplitude the nonlinear pendulum equation remains valid but this period approximation does not. The M→0 limit recovers a simple pendulum of length R1​.
