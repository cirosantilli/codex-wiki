For β=0.99, the Lorentz factor is γ=1/1−β2​=7.0888. In the tracks' rest frame the train undergoes length contraction to 100/γ=14.1067m, which fits in the 90m tunnel. There is a time interval during which it is entirely inside. In the train's rest frame the tunnel instead has length 90/γ=12.6961m, so it never simultaneously contains the whole 100m train:
Entirely inside at one track-frame time: yes. At one train-frame time: no.​
(15)
These refer to different sets of simultaneous events, as required by relativity of simultaneity. Quantitatively let A be the rear entering the near portal, with (xA​,tA​)=(0,0), and B the front reaching the far portal. In the track frame,
xB​=90m,ctB​=β90−100/γ​=76.6599m.
(16)
The train is wholly inside between these events. Transforming their time separation gives
c(tB′​−tA′​)=γ(ctB​−βxB​)=−88.1858m<0.
(17)
Thus the front leaves before the rear enters in the train frame. The two events are spacelike separated, so their time order may reverse without a causal contradiction. No doors or forced acceleration need be added to the problem.
