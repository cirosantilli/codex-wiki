Choose the positive axis from the approaching source towards the observer. The photon that reaches the observer is emitted forward, with source-frame four-momentum (E/c,E/c,0,0). Boosting to the observer frame gives Eobs​=γE(1+β), where β=v/c. Since photon energy is hP​ν,
ν=hP​E​γ(1+β)=hP​E​1−v/c1+v/c​​.​
(16)
This is the approaching-source relativistic Doppler effect. A photon emitted in a general source-frame direction would instead have the factor γ(1+βcosθ∗​); forward emission is the geometry of the photon directed to the observer here.
