For constant-area capture of stationary dust, let x=0 at cloud entry, M(0)=Mp​, v(0)=v∞​, and define k=ρA with fixed collecting area A. Every swept volume Adx adds stationary mass, so
dtdM​=kv,M=Mp​+kx.
(48)
There is no external longitudinal force, and the incoming dust has zero momentum in the cloud frame. The variable-mass system therefore satisfies d(Mv)/dt=0, rather than Mv˙=0. Using Mv=Mp​v∞​ gives
MM˙=kMp​v∞​,M2=Mp2​+2kMp​v∞​t.
(49)
Hence
M(t)=Mp​1+Mp​2ρAv∞​t​​,v(t)=1+2ρAv∞​t/Mp​​v∞​​,​
(50)
x(t)=ρAMp​​[1+Mp​2ρAv∞​t​​−1].​
(51)
The kinetic energy decreases during sticking: dK/dt=−21​M˙v2. The lost energy becomes internal energy. Radiation, gravity, sputtering and changes in area are excluded by the ideal complete-collection model. The zero-density limit recovers M=Mp​, v=v∞​ and x=v∞​t.
