n=−5,C=m8J2a2​,F(r)=−mr58J2a2​.​

The result applies to the regular arc where r>0 and J=0. The force is singular at the point where the geometrical circle meets the origin, so the curve is not a regular periodic orbit continued through that point. Indeed, U=−C/(4r4) and this trajectory has zero total energy, with kinetic and negative potential terms diverging as the centre is approached.
