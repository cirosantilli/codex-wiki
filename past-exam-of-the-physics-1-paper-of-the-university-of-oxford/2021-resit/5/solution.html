Interpret the percentage relative to the classical kinetic energy, so that Krel​=1.02Kcl​. Set β=v/c and use the Lorentz factor γ=(1−β2)−1/2. The relativistic kinetic energy and classical kinetic energy give
(γ−1)mc2=1.02(21​mv2),γ−1=0.51β2.
(17)
For the positive nonzero speed, insert β2=1−γ−2 and cancel γ−1:
γ+1γ2​=0.51,γ2−0.51γ−0.51=0,γ=20.51+2.3001​​≃1.01330403.
(18)
It follows that
v=c1−γ−2​≃0.161513c≃4.8420×107ms−1,​E=γmc2≃950.479MeV.​
(19)
Here mc2=938MeV is the proton's rest energy; the resulting relativistic kinetic energy is 12.479MeV. The zero-speed equality before cancellation has no meaningful percentage excess because both kinetic energies then vanish.
