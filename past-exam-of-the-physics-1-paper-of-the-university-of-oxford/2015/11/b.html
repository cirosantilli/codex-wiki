Let S′ be the runner's inertial frame, with origin at the entry event. The Lorentz transformation is
x′=γ(x−vt),t′=γ(t−c2vx​),γ=(1−β2)−1/2.
(59)
The emission event E=(L,0) has coordinates xE′​=γL, tE′​=−γvL/c2. It is earlier than entry in S′: entry and far-end emission are simultaneous only in the tunnel frame. This is relativity of simultaneity, not an extra delay of the light source.
