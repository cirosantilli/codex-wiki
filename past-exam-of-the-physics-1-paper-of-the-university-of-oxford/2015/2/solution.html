Consequently the grazing clearance of an impulsively struck rod occurs at
βcrit​=2π1​≃0.15915.​
(7)
For β>1/(2π) the whole stick has passed the pole by its first horizontal orientation, and the continually advancing centre makes all later passes safe. Equality is the limiting grazing contact of the endpoint; strict avoidance requires the strict inequality. If β<1/(2π), the pole lies within the stick at that first horizontal pass, so contact occurs before the freely moving trailing end can pass the pole. A uniform thin stick has β=1/12, below the threshold: sufficient mass near the ends is needed to increase its moment of inertia and reduce the rotation relative to translation.
