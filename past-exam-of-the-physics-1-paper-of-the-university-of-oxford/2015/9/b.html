Assume the cylinders are released from rest. In each case, “there is friction” is interpreted as sufficient static friction for rolling without slipping at the rough contacts. Without a coefficient or a no-slip assumption, sliding cases would not have a unique specified acceleration. On release the two lower cylinders separate; their mutual contact must not be retained as a rigid constraint.
Let the top cylinder's initial downward acceleration be w, and each lower cylinder's outward horizontal acceleration be u. The top cylinder initially has no horizontal acceleration. Differentiating the fixed upper-to-lower centre separation twice at the initial zero velocity gives
u=3​w.​
(38)
By reflection symmetry the top cylinder has zero angular acceleration, while the lower cylinders rotate in opposite senses.
