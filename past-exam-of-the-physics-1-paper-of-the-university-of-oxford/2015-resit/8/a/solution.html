For superterminal descent under quadratic drag, use v˙=vdv/dz in v˙=b(vt2​−v2). Equivalently,
dzd(v2)​=−2b(v2−vt2​),v2(z)=vt2​+(u2−vt2​)e−2bz.​
(23)
The speed approaches vt​ asymptotically from above. There is no finite exact distance at which it first equals the limiting speed. A practical estimate must specify an accuracy. Define “reached” as v≤(1+ϵ)vt​. Then
zϵ​=2b1​ln[(1+ϵ)2−1u2/vt2​−1​].​
(24)
For one-percent accuracy, ϵ=0.01 gives
z1%​≃0.797m≈0.8m.​
(25)
A five-percent criterion gives z5%​≃0.642m. The characteristic decay length of the excess squared speed is 1/(2b)≃0.0952m, but several such lengths are needed because the entry speed is so far above the limiting value. These estimates depend on the stated water-entry idealization; the source gives no finite tolerance or entry-loss model.
