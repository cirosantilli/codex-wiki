An independent invariant mass check is useful. In the rest frame of one daughter the other's total energy satisfies W2=2Q2+2QE′, so E′=178.8889GeV and the relative Lorentz factor is γrel​=E′/Q=161/81. Hence w/c=1−γrel−2​​, the same result.
The answer is unchanged if the parent is produced by a fixed-target collision. Its laboratory velocity then differs, but its rest mass and two-body decay kinematics do not. The scalar product of the daughters' four-momenta, equivalently their relative Lorentz factor, depends only on W and Q. A common boost cannot change the speed of one measured in the rest frame of the other.[ref]
