The endpoint mass cancels in the light-rod limit. A concrete comparison shows why the finite rod cannot silently be included: the same specified endpoint m with Mr​=m gives T=2π8l/(9g)​, distinct from the negligible-rod result. This is a physical pendulum when the rod contributes, and a simple pendulum in the light-rod limit. Both expressions require small oscillation amplitude; the full sine equation remains nonlinear.
