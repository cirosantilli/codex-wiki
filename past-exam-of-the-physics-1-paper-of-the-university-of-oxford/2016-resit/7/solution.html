The initial total momentum is zero, so the outgoing neutron recoils with momentum magnitude Eγ​/c. Put W=(mp​+mπ​)c2. Conservation of relativistic energy gives
W=Eγ​+mn2​c4+Eγ2​​,Eγ​=2WW2−mn2​c4​≃129.4MeV.​
(12)
For example the rest energies 938.2721, 139.5704 and 939.5654MeV for the proton, charged pion and neutron give this value.[ref] The result is smaller than the initial-to-final rest-energy difference of about 138.3MeV, because some released energy becomes neutron recoil kinetic energy. Atomic binding of a captured pion is neglected in the stated stationary-particle model.
