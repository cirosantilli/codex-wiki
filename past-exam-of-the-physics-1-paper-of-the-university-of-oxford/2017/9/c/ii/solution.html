At the threshold found in i, both products are stationary in the centre-of-momentum frame, so they have the same velocity in the laboratory frame. Take the energetic incident photon along +x. The total energy is E+ε and total momentum is (E−ε)/c, so the centre-of-momentum frame moves at
V=cE+εE−ε​x^.​
(49)
For the numerical threshold,
cV​=1−E+ε2ε​≃1−7.66×10−18,γCM​=2me​c2E+ε​≃2.56×108.
(50)
This is extremely close to, but strictly below, the speed of light. Each product has energy (E+ε)/2 in the laboratory frame. Above threshold the same ratio of total momentum to total energy determines the motion of the pair's centre-of-momentum frame, but the individual electron and Positron need not have a common velocity.
