Let the incident photon energies be E and ε, meeting at angle ψ in the laboratory frame. Add their four-momenta; because each is null, the squared total invariant energy is
S=(E+ε)2−c2∣p1​+p2​∣2=2Eε(1−cosψ).
(46)
For photon-photon pair production, its threshold value is (2me​c2)2: the electron and Positron have zero relative momentum in their centre-of-momentum frame. The minimum possible E occurs for a head-on collision, ψ=π, so
4Eε=4me2​c4,Emin​=ε(me​c2)2​.​
(47)
With me​c2≃0.511MeV and ε=10−3eV,
Emin​≃2.61×1014eV=261TeV≃41.8J.​
(48)
The quoted background photon energy is used directly, without substituting a different characteristic value of the cosmic microwave background. A less opposed collision has a higher threshold; parallel photons cannot produce this massive pair.
