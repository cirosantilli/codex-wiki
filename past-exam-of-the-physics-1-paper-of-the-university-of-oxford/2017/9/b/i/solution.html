Let S′ move at +v along x relative to S. The Lorentz transformation of energy and momentum, from primed to unprimed components, is
E=γ(E′+vpx′​),px​=γ(px′​+c2vE′​),py​=py′​,pz​=pz′​.
(40)
The inverse equations have v replaced by −v and the primes interchanged. A photon making angle θ′ with +x in S′ has px′​=(E′/c)cosθ′. Thus its general Doppler factor is
D=E′E​=γ(1+βcosθ′).
(41)
The requested blueshifted expression assumes propagation parallel to the boost, θ′=0:
D=γ(1+β)=1−β1+β​​.​
(42)
The propagation-direction assumption matters: for an oppositely travelling photon, D=γ(1−β), the reciprocal of the displayed factor. Relative frame speed alone does not select the longitudinal relativistic Doppler effect.
