Decay threshold (M≥∑i​mi​)

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Particle decay
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A parent of rest mass M can decay to on-shell daughters of rest masses mi​ only if M≥∑i​mi​. In the parent rest frame, each daughter energy is at least its rest energy, so four-momentum conservation proves necessity. The excess energy controls the available Lorentz-invariant phase space. A two-body decay threshold is the two-daughter special case; spin and other selection rules can still forbid an energetically allowed channel.
