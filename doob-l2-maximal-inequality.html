Doob L2 maximal inequality (Esups≤t​∣Ms​∣2≤4E∣Mt​∣2)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
For a square-integrable martingale starting at zero,
Esups≤t​∣Ms​∣2≤4E∣Mt​∣2.
(18)
For a continuous local martingale stopped so that its quadratic variation is integrable, the Itô isometry makes the right-hand side 4E[M]t​.
This is the p=2 specialization of Doob's martingale inequality.
