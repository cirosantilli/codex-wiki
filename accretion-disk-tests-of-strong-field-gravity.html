Accretion-disk tests of strong-field gravity

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Accretion disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 200 Articles: 2
Radiating material close to a compact object probes relativistic orbital motion, gravitational redshift and light propagation. A thin disk's inferred inner edge can be compared with the predicted innermost stable circular orbit, while iron-line transfer and thermal-continuum fitting probe complementary aspects of the same geometry. Agreement tests a combined spacetime-and-disk model. A black-hole spin measurement made after imposing a Kerr metric is not alone an independent verification of that metric tensor; geometry tests must vary gravitational predictions and astrophysical nuisance parameters together.
