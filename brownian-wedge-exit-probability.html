Brownian wedge-exit probability (pα​(r)=π4​arctan(r−π/α))

OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A planar Brownian motion starting at 1 exits the disk of radius r>1 before leaving a wedge of opening α∈(0,2π], centered on the positive axis, with probability (4/π)arctan(r−π/α). The power-map reduction for Brownian exit from a wedge, the reflection identity for Brownian exit from a half-disc and the Möbius calculation of circular Brownian exit give the formula. Its large-radius decay exponent is π/α.
