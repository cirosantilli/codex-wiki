For two weight-k level-one cusp forms, use the primitive real-analytic Eisenstein series and dμ=dxdy/y2. The Rankin–Selberg unfolding identity is
I(f,g,w)=(4π)w+k−1Γ(w+k−1)​∑n≥1​nw+k−1an​bn​​​.
(16)
Initially this follows by absolute convergence, unfolding to 0≤x<1, and integrating the Fourier series in x. Exponential cusp decay and polynomial growth of the primitive real-analytic Eisenstein series make the integral holomorphic for Rew>1, continuing the Dirichlet series to Res>k.
