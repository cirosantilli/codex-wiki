Right lifting property (i⋔p)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A map p has the right lifting property against i:A→B when every commutative square with i on the left and p on the right has a diagonal B→domp making both triangles commute. Lifting against a family of maps gives a class of right-injective maps.
