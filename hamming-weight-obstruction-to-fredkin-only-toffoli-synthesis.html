Hamming-weight obstruction to Fredkin-only Toffoli synthesis

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Controlled unitary gate Fredkin gate
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Every Fredkin gate preserves the Hamming weight of a computational-basis string, so any network of these gates does too. A Toffoli gate maps 110 to 111 and hence cannot equal such a network on the same three wires. Fixed ancillas returned unchanged do not remove the obstruction. Extra encoded outputs or input-dependent garbage change the resource model and do not by themselves implement the same clean coherent three-qubit gate.
