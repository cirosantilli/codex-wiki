Cut of a flow network

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Flow network
OurBigBook.com Words: 136 Articles: 3
An source-to-sink cut is a partition (A,V∖A) with the source in A and the sink outside A. Its capacity is the sum of the capacities of directed edges leaving A.
