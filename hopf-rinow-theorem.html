Hopf-Rinow theorem

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Geodesic Complete geodesic Geodesic completeness
OurBigBook.com Words: 235 Articles: 2
For a connected finite-dimensional Riemannian manifold, completeness of the Riemannian distance, geodesic completeness, and compactness of every closed bounded subset are equivalent. These conditions imply that any two points are joined by a minimizing geodesic. Pairwise existence of minimizing geodesics alone does not imply completeness: an open Euclidean ball is incomplete, yet the line segment between any two of its points stays in the ball and minimizes length.
