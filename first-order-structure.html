First-order structure

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 39
A structure supplies a nonempty domain and an interpretation of every constant, operation, and relation symbol in its language.
This is a structure for a first-order language, with finitary interpretations of its nonlogical symbols.
