Central processing unit

 Home Computer science
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 33
The processor executing a program's instructions. In polled input-output the CPU explicitly checks status; in interrupt-driven input-output it enters an interrupt handler; in DMA it delegates payload movement while retaining setup and completion work.
