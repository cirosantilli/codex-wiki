Grey body

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Stefan–Boltzmann law Emissivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
A grey body has spectral emissivity independent of wavelength over the range in which the approximation is used. Under Kirchhoff's law of thermal radiation, the corresponding radiometric absorptance is also wavelength-independent. This allows the same factor to weight emission at T and black-enclosure irradiation at a different Ts​ in net radiative heat exchange. The factor can still depend on temperature; treating it as constant over a cooling curve is an additional assumption. Wavelength independence alone does not imply direction independence.[ref]
