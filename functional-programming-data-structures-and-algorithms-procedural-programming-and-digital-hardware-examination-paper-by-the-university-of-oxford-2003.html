The eight-page Preliminary Examination in Computer Science, Paper 1, Long Vacation 2003. Original source: Released examination archive, member 4213-1.pdf. Printed pp. 2–3 cover functional programming, p. 4 covers algorithms, pp. 5–6 cover imperative programming, and pp. 7–8 cover digital electronics. The timing diagram on printed p. 8 permits a new clock cycle pulse immediately after a cycle that rearms the circuit.
