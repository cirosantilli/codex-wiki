Stern-Gerlach historical and modern interpretations

OurBigBook.com Words: 295
The 1922 interpretation concerned space quantization of atomic magnetic moments in the old quantum theory. The modern electron spin proposal came in 1925; the original observers did not identify intrinsic electron spin as the cause of their splitting. An orbital interpretation then seemed plausible, but the later spin explanation gives a different account of the same two-component evidence.[ref]
Modern atomic data assign neutral silver the ground state electron configuration [Kr]4d105s and electronic term 2S1/2​. The filled electron shells contribute zero net electronic angular momentum; the outer unpaired electron has orbital angular momentum L=0, spin S=1/2, and electronic total angular momentum J=1/2. The magnetic quantum number has two values mJ​=±1/2.[ref]
In the effective electronic description,
μz​=−gJ​μB​ℏJz​​,Jz​∣J,mJ​⟩=ℏmJ​∣J,mJ​⟩,μz​≃∓μB​(gJ​≃2).
(18)
The minus sign matters: for positive G, the component with mJ​=+1/2 has negative electronic magnetic moment and deflects toward negative z in our coordinates. Calling the upper spot “spin up” without specifying the magnetic field and channel calibration can therefore reverse the assignment. The total angular momentum operator here sums electronic orbital angular momentum and electronic spin; J is not a claim that the atomic nucleus has no spin. Hyperfine structure and nuclear magnetic moments can require additional internal levels, so a two-channel electronic approximation must be justified for the field and resolution being used.
This is a neutral atomic beam, not a beam of free electrons. The deflected mass in the flight equations is the entire silver chemical atom; its dominant electronic magnetic moment supplies the state-dependent force. Replacing that atom by a free electron would also introduce the charged particle's Lorentz force and a different preparation problem. The ideal two-state model is useful because it isolates the connection between discrete internal components and spatially resolved outputs; it does not retroactively turn the 1922 deposit into a direct contemporary identification of intrinsic electron spin.
