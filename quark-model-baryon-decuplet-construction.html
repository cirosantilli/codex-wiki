Quark-model baryon-decuplet construction (dimSym3C3=(35​)=10)

A particularly economical example is the baryon decuplet. Restrict to fully symmetric three-quark flavor states. The nonnegative occupation numbers satisfy nu​+nd​+ns​=3, giving (33+3−1​)=10 possibilities by stars and bars. Their additive labels are
Q=nu​−1,I3​=2nu​−nd​​,Y=1−ns​.
(24)
These formulas follow directly from the constituent table. With fixed ns​, there are 4−ns​ possible values of nu​, so the weight diagram has rows of four, three, two and one states. Its outer triangle is therefore a consequence of the constituent construction, rather than an arbitrary drawing convention.
The bottom corner has ns​=3, hence I3​=0, Y=−2 and Q=−1: the Omega baryon position. The top-right corner has uuu and Q=2, corresponding to the doubly charged Delta baryon. Every point satisfies the Gell-Mann--Nishijima formula Q=I3​+Y/2.
The flavor construction is only part of the state. The Pauli constraint on three-quark flavour multiplets also involves spin angular momentum, the spatial wavefunction and color charge. In particular, three identical fermions cannot be assigned a completely symmetric total state merely because the flavor diagram is symmetric. Nor does the same flavor diagram determine the hadron masses: an approximate flavor symmetry can organize a spectrum without making all its members degenerate.
