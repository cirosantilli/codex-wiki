For each nonnegative integer z, the events {X=z−y,Y=y}, 0≤y≤z, are pairwise disjoint and their union is {X+Y=z}. By countable additivity and then independence,
pZ​(z)​=y=0∑z​P(X=z−y,Y=y)=y=0∑z​pY​(y)pX​(z−y).​​
(53)
This is the discrete convolution of the two probability mass functions. If the convention for natural numbers excludes zero, set both probability mass functions equal to zero there; the formula remains valid. Also pZ​(z)=0 for negative z.
