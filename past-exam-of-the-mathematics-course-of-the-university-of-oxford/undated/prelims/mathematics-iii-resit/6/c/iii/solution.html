Indeed the slowest decaying component has failure probability 5/6, so P(T=t)≥(1/36)(5/6)t−1. At ∣s∣=6/5, the terms of the power series fail to tend to zero; outside that disk they grow. All six component series converge inside it. In particular, the probability generating function is defined on the usual real interval 0≤s≤1, with GT​(1)=1. The rational expression beyond the convergence disk is only an algebraic continuation, not the defining expected value.
