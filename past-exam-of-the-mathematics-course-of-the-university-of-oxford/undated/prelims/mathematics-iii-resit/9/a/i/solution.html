The independent uniform choices from the student list describe sampling with replacement. Under the stated independent normal distribution model for the heights, the population variance is unknown and the given 8.5 cm is the sample standard deviation s. The exact pivotal quantity is
S/100​X−μ​∼t99​.
(76)
Thus the exact 95% Student t confidence interval is
171.7±t99,0.975​108.5​ cm ≈ [170.01,173.39] cm,​
(77)
using t99,0.975​≈1.9842. The supplied approximation Φ(2)=0.975 instead gives a normal approximation for large sample size
171.7±2(0.85)=[170.0,173.4] cm.​
(78)
This latter confidence interval is approximate even under normality because it substitutes the sample standard deviation for the unknown population value. There is no finite population correction under the specified independent selections. Interpreting the finite student list literally as a discrete population makes normality an idealized model; it does not justify changing the stated sampling design.
