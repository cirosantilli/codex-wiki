An ordinary residual plot need not reveal this failure of independence. The errors have constant mean zero, constant variance one and zero pairwise covariances, so the usual trend and changing-variance clues need not appear. A visually patternless residual plot cannot validate independence; fitted regression residuals themselves also share the fitted coefficients even when the true errors are independent.
