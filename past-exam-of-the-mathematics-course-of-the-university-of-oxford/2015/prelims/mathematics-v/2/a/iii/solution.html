Choose F=∇ψ in the preceding product rule and apply the divergence theorem. Since ∇⋅∇ψ=Δψ, this gives the first of Green's identities:
∭V​(ϕΔψ+∇ϕ⋅∇ψ),dV=∬∂V​ϕ∂n​ψdS.​
(11)
Here ∂n​ψ=n⋅∇ψ is the outward normal derivative. Interchange ϕ and ψ, then subtract. The gradient dot products cancel, yielding the second of Green's identities:
∭V​(ϕΔψ−ψΔϕ)dV=∬∂V​(ϕ∂n​ψ−ψ∂n​ϕ)dS.​
(12)
For example, ϕ,ψ∈C2(V) with the needed extensions suffice for these applications of the divergence theorem.
