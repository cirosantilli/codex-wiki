The complete classification up to group isomorphism is
∣G∣123456​GC1​C2​C3​C4​, C2​×C2​C5​C6​, S3​​​​
(55)
Here C1​ is the trivial group. The prime number cases follow from i. For four elements, an element of order of a group element four generates C4​. If there is none, Lagrange's theorem makes every nonidentity element have order of a group element two. Then iv produces a four-element subgroup, which must be all of G, giving the Klein four-group.
For six elements, an element of order of a group element six gives C6​. Suppose there is none. If there were no element of order of a group element three either, all nonidentity elements would have order of a group element two. Part iv would then produce a subgroup of cardinality four, contradicting Lagrange's theorem. Thus choose a of order of a group element three. There is also an involution b: otherwise the five nonidentity elements could be paired into distinct pairs {g,g−1}, impossible for an odd number of elements.
The subgroup H=⟨a⟩ has index of a subgroup two, so it is a normal subgroup. Indeed, for g∈/H, both gH and Hg are the complement of H, and for g∈H both equal H. Consequently bab−1 is either a or a−1. The first possibility makes a,b commute, and ab then has order of a group element six: (ab)k=e forces ak=b−k∈⟨a⟩∩⟨b⟩={e}, so both 3 and 2 divide k. This contradicts our assumption. Therefore
bab−1=a−1.
(56)
Every element is uniquely ai or aib, with 0≤i<3, and the relation ba=a−1b determines all their products. Sending a to (123) and b to (12) gives a group isomorphism with the symmetric group S3​. This completes the classification of groups of order at most six.
Of these groups, exactly C1​,C2​,C3​,C4​,C2​×C2​,S3​ embed in S4​. Concrete subgroups are
{e},⟨(12)⟩,⟨(123)⟩,⟨(1234)⟩,{e,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)},{σ∈S4​:σ(4)=4}.
(57)
There is no C5​ subgroup, since 5 does not divide 24. There is no C6​ subgroup either: the order of a group element in a symmetric group is the least common multiple of the lengths in its disjoint cycle decomposition, and on four letters the only possibilities are 1,2,3,4.
