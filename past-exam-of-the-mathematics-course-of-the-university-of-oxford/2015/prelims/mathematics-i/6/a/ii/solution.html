OurBigBook.com Words: 46
If G=⟨g⟩, any two elements are ga,gb, and
gagb=ga+b=gb+a=gbga.
(51)
Thus every cyclic group is an abelian group.
For the converse, the Klein four-group C2​×C2​ is an abelian group, with componentwise multiplication. Each nonidentity element has order of a group element two, so none generates its four elements. It is therefore not a cyclic group.
