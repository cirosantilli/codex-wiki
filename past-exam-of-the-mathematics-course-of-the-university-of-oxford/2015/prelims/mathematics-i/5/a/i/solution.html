For a subgroup H of G, the left coset represented by g is
gH={gh:h∈H}.​
(44)
Suppose two left cosets intersect. There are h1​,h2​∈H such that g1​h1​=g2​h2​, so
g1​=g2​h2​h1−1​.
(45)
Because H is a subgroup, h2​h1−1​∈H. Thus, for every h∈H, g1​h=g2​(h2​h1−1​h)∈g2​H, proving g1​H⊆g2​H. Interchanging the two representatives gives the reverse inclusion. Therefore two left cosets are equal or disjoint.
