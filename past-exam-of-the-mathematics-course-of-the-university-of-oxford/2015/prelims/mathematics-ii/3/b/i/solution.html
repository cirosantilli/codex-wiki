The radius of convergence of a power series ∑n=0∞​an​zn is the number R∈[0,∞] such that the series has absolute convergence for ∣z∣<R and diverges for ∣z∣>R. Equivalently,
R=sup{r≥0: n=0∑∞​∣an​∣rn<∞}.​
(31)
Use r0=1, including at r=0. The supremum may be +∞. There is no general conclusion at ∣z∣=R: boundary convergence depends on the coefficients. If R=0, only the centre is guaranteed; if R=∞, every finite z lies inside the disk of convergence.
