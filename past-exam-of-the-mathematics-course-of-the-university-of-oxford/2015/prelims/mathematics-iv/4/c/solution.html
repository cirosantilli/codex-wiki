For steady precession at rate Ω about the fixed vertical unit vector k,
e˙=Ωk∧e,e¨=Ω2(cosαk−e).
(54)
Consequently e∧e¨=Ω2cosαe∧k and e˙=−Ωe∧k. Since the axial angular velocity of a symmetric top n is constant, substitution in the equation of motion gives
(AΩ2cosα−CnΩ)e∧k=(κℓ2−mgh)e∧k.
(55)
As 0<α<π/2, the cross product can be cancelled. For a specified axial spin, the required value is
κ=ℓ2mgh+AΩ2cosα−CnΩ​.​
(56)
The corresponding total angular velocity is
ω=Ωk+(n−Ωcosα)e.
(57)
If the stated angular velocity Ωk is the total angular velocity of the top, then n=Ωcosα and the requested value specializes to
κ=ℓ2mgh+(A−C)Ω2cosα​.​
(58)
This specialization permits a direct check: LQ​=AΩk+(C−A)Ωcosαe, whose time derivative is (A−C)Ω2cosαe∧k, exactly the required torque. The first formula keeps the separate axial spin explicit when Ω is understood as the rate of precession.
