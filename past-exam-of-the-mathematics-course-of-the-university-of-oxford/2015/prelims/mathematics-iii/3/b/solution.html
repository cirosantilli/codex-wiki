At a regular point of the optimization constraint, meaning ∇g=0, the necessary Lagrange multiplier condition is
∇f=λ∇gfor some λ∈R.​
(23)
Indeed, every allowed tangent vector t to the level set g=0 satisfies ∇g⋅t=0. At a constrained extremum, the directional derivative ∇f⋅t must vanish too. Thus both gradients are normal to the same tangent line and are parallel. This includes ∇f=0. At a singular point with ∇g=0, the displayed condition need not be necessary; those points require a separate examination.
