For a random variable with a continuous probability distribution and probability density function fX​, the mean and variance are
μ=E[X]=∫−∞∞​xfX​(x)dx,var(X)=E[(X−μ)2]=∫−∞∞​(x−μ)2fX​(x)dx.​
(51)
A finite mean requires ∫∣x∣fX​(x)dx<∞. A finite variance additionally requires the second moment to be finite; without that condition the nonnegative integral for the variance may be infinite.
