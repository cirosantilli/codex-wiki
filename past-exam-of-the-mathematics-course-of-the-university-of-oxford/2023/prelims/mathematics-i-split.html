Source: released examination PDF, pages 180–184.
Solutions cover all seven questions, including every optional question. The module label is I, with M1 used for Mathematics and Computer Science; the formal paper code is A10138W1. Throughout Question 4, diagonalisable matrices are understood over the real numbers.
