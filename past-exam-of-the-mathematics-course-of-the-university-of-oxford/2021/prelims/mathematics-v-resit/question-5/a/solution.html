The angular Fourier harmonics for a single-valued harmonic function in an annulus are cos(nθ) and sin(nθ), with integer n≥1. Their radial factors are independent combinations of rn and r−n; the zero angular mode has radial factor A0​+B0​logr. For arbitrary inner data, the sine and cosine modes need independent radial coefficients: forcing them to share one radial factor, as in the product form supplied in the paper, would impose an extra restriction. For integrable boundary data the radial factors give exponential decay on every closed subannulus strictly inside a<r<1, justifying the necessary termwise partial derivatives there.
There is a qualification at the two outer jump discontinuities. By pointwise convergence of a piecewise smooth Fourier series, the outer series gives 1/2 at θ=±π/2, whereas the printed data assign 1 at those two points. Thus the displayed solution attains the outer data at their continuity points, and has radial limit 1/2 at the jumps. One may separately assign the boundary value 1 at the two exceptional points without changing the interior harmonic function, but no continuous extension can match the discontinuous data there. For piecewise smooth inner data, the analogous boundary trace agrees with h at continuity points and with its midpoint at jumps. Some regularity of h is necessary for a classical interpretation; the problem imposes none explicitly.
