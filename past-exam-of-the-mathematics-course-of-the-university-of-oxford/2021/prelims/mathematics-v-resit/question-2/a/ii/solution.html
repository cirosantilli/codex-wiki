 ... 2021 Prelims Mathematics V resit Question 2 a ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For each of the three constant Cartesian unit vectors ej​, apply the divergence theorem to the vector field ϕej​. Its divergence is ∂j​ϕ, so
∭R​∂j​ϕdV=∬∂R​ϕnj​dS(j=1,2,3).
(10)
These are precisely the three components of
∭R​∇ϕdV=∬∂R​ϕndS=∬∂R​ϕdS​.
(11)
Thus the volume integral of the gradient is the boundary surface integral weighted by the vector surface element dS=ndS. The regularity needed here is that ϕ be continuously differentiable on a neighbourhood of the closure of R.
