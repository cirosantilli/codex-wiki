OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 124
Source: released examination PDF, bundle pages 25–32.
Solutions cover all seven questions: Section A, Questions 1–4, and Section B, Questions 5–7, including every optional question. The module label is I for Mathematics and Mathematics and Philosophy, and M1 for Mathematics and Computer Science; the formal paper code is A10138W1. In Question 2(b), n≥1 is understood, as required by Vn−1​.
