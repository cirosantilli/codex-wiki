so g maps the closed interval into itself. Also
∣g′(x)∣=75x4​≤755​=q<1.
(78)
The mean value theorem therefore gives ∣g(x)−g(y)∣≤q∣x−y∣ on the closed interval: g is a contraction mapping. For any starting value x0​∈[0,1/7], all iterates remain in that closed interval, and since g(ξ)=ξ,
∣xk​−ξ∣=∣g(xk−1​)−g(ξ)∣≤q∣xk−1​−ξ∣≤qk∣x0​−ξ∣⟶0.
(79)
Thus the fixed-point iteration converges to the unique real root of a polynomial from every x0​∈[0,1/7]. This explicit error estimate proves the needed convergence directly.
