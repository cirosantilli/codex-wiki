With t as in i, the foot of a perpendicular lies strictly between the endpoints precisely when 0<t<1. Since the denominator is positive, this gives
0<(c−a)⋅(b−a)<∥b−a∥2.​
(3)
Equivalently, both dot products
(c−a)⋅(b−a)>0,(c−b)⋅(a−b)>0
(4)
are positive: the angles of the triangle at A and B are both less than π/2. If “between” includes the endpoints, replace both strict inequalities by non-strict ones; equality gives P=A or P=B.
