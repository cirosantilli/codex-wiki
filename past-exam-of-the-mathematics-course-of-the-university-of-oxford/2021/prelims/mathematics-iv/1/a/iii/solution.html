 ... 2021 Prelims Mathematics IV 1 a iii
OurBigBook.com Words: 105
The altitude of a triangle through A has equation (a−d)⋅(b−c)=0, and the altitude of a triangle through B has equation (b−d)⋅(a−c)=0. Their normal vectors b−c and a−c are linearly independent because the triangle is nondegenerate. Consequently these two lines meet in exactly one point D.
Substitute its position vector d in the supplied identity. Its first term vanishes by the first altitude of a triangle equation, and its second term vanishes by the second altitude of a triangle equation. Hence
(a−b)⋅(c−d)=0.
(5)
This is exactly the condition that D lies on the altitude of a triangle through C. The three lines therefore meet in a unique point, the orthocenter. This argument includes right triangles and triangles whose orthocenter lies outside them.
