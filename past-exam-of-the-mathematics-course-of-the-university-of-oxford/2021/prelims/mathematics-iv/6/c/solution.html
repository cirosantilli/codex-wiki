To decide exactly when this is possible, view the equation as η=(c+e)/(1+ec) with c=cosϕ. Its derivative (1−e2)/(1+ec)2 is positive on [−1,1], and it takes endpoint values −1 and 1. Since g>0 implies η>0, the complete list is:
  • If 0<η<1, there are four distinct equilibria modulo 2π: 0, π, and ±arccos1−eηη−e​. For 0<η<e the additional positive angle lies in (π/2,π); at η=e the additional pair is ±π/2; for e<η<1 it lies in (0,π/2).
  • If η=1, the additional pair merges with ϕ=0, leaving two distinct equilibria, 0 and π.
  • If η>1, there are only the two equilibria 0 and π. In particular, η=1/e does not introduce a missing case: the undivided bracket is then the positive constant 1/e−e, so there is no extra root.
These are positions of the bead at rest relative to the rotating hoop. No stability classification of the particular ellipse is needed.
