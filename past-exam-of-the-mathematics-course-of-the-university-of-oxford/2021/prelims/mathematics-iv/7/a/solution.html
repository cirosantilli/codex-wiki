OurBigBook.com Words: 84
The greatest common divisor of 54 and 21 is 3, which divides 906. Divide the linear Diophantine equation by 3 to obtain
18x+7y=302.
(70)
Reducing modulo 7 gives 4x≡1(mod7), so x≡2(mod7). Substitution gives the complete family of integer solutions
x=2+7t,y=38−18t,t∈Z.​
(71)
Every such pair satisfies the equation, and every solution must have this residue for x, so none are omitted. Positivity requires 2+7t>0 and 38−18t>0. For integer t these are exactly 0≤t≤2. Thus the pairs with both entries positive are
(x,y)=(2,38), (9,20), (16,2).​
(72)
