Symmetry of the real inner product gives AT=A. Moreover, i with y=x shows that
xTAx=∥∑i=1n​xi​vi​∥2≥0(x∈Rn),
(37)
so the Gram matrix is a positive semidefinite matrix. To also check reality of every eigenvalue, allow a nonzero vector z=a+ib∈Cn with Az=λz. Since A is a real symmetric matrix,
z∗Az=aTAa+bTAb+i(aTAb−bTAa)=aTAa+bTAb≥0.
(38)
Here z∗ is the conjugate transpose, and aTAb=bTAa cancels the imaginary part. Thus
λ=z∗zz∗Az​∈[0,∞),​
(39)
because z∗z>0. This proves both that every eigenvalue is real and that it is nonnegative.
