 ... 2021 Prelims Mathematics I 6 b ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
Suppose an arbitrary binary operation ⋆ makes Z a group and makes ρ a left group action on G. For any group action, each acting element induces a bijection: the function induced by its inverse element is its inverse function.
In particular, the acting element whose underlying integer is 0 must induce a bijection G→G. But its power function is
x⟼ρ(0,x)=x0=e,
(58)
a constant function. This is injective only when all elements of G equal e. Therefore
G={e}.​
(59)
This proves the power-map group action obstruction for every possible ⋆; it never assumes that ⋆ is ordinary integer multiplication, or that the integer 0 is the acting identity element.
