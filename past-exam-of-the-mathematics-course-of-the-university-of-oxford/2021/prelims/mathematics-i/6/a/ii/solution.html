OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For W=aH and W′=bH, define
f:W→W′,f(w)=ba−1w.
(53)
If w=ah then f(w)=bh∈bH, so this function is well defined. Its inverse function is w′↦ab−1w′, since successive left translation in a group by ba−1 and ab−1 cancels. Thus
f:aH∼​bH is a bijection.​
(54)
In particular every left coset has the same cardinality as H.
