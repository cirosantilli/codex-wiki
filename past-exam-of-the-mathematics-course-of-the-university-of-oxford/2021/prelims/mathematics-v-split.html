OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 54
Source: released examination PDF, bundle pages 279–284.
Solutions to all six questions and all optional parts. The surface-volume formula in Question 2(b) requires an appropriate enclosing surface and orientation. The symmetry claim in Question 6(a), as printed, also needs the coefficient b(x,t) to be even in x; a counterexample to the unrestricted claim is given.
