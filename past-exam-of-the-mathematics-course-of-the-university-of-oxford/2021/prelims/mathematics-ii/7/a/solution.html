OurBigBook.com Words: 86
Let f:[a,b]→R be a bounded function, with a<b. A partition of an interval is P=(a=x0​<x1​<⋯<xN​=b); its mesh of a partition is ∥P∥=maxi​(xi​−xi−1​). Choose arbitrary tags ξi​∈[xi−1​,xi​]. The corresponding Riemann sum is
∑(f;P,ξ​)=∑i=1N​f(ξi​)(xi​−xi−1​).
(53)
The Riemann sum convergence theorem says that f is Riemann integrable with Riemann integral I if and only if, for every ε>0, some δ>0 satisfies
​∑(f;P,ξ​)−I​<ε​
(54)
for every partition of an interval with ∥P∥<δ and every permitted choice of tags. Equivalently, along every sequence of such tagged partitions of an interval with mesh of a partition tending to zero, the Riemann sums tend to I. Both the shrinking mesh of a partition and freedom of tags are essential.
