Back-substitute in the Euclidean algorithm to obtain an integer linear combination:
9​=45−36=45−(306−6⋅45)=7⋅45−306=7(657−2⋅306)−306=7⋅657−15⋅306.​
(73)
Multiplying by 3 gives the particular solution (x0​,y0​)=(−45,21) of the linear Diophantine equation. Subtracting it from any other solution and dividing by 9 gives
34(x+45)+73(y−21)=0.
(74)
The integers 34 and 73 are coprime, so 73 divides x+45. Writing x+45=73t forces y−21=−34t. Conversely these expressions satisfy the original equation for every integer t. Thus all solutions of a linear Diophantine equation here are
(x,y)=(−45+73t, 21−34t),t∈Z.​
(75)
