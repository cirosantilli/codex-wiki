All forces on the first particle lie in the plane spanned by er​ and ez​: this holds for gravity, tension, and the normal reaction. Hence the azimuthal component of Newton's second law vanishes:
m1​(r1​θ¨+2r˙1​θ˙)=0.

h˙=dtd​(r12​θ˙)=r1​(r1​θ¨+2r˙1​θ˙)=0.​

Strictly, h is the axial specific angular momentum; the actual axial angular momentum is m1​h, which is conserved as well.
Differentiate the stated total energy using the chain rule and then substitute the two Newton's second law equations:
E˙​=m1​r˙1​⋅r¨1​+m1​gz˙1​+m2​r˙2​⋅r¨2​+m2​gz˙2​=N⋅r˙1​+T1​⋅r˙1​+T2​⋅r˙2​.​

The gravitational potential energy terms have canceled the power of gravity. Also N is proportional to er​+ez​, whose dot product with r˙1​ is r˙1​+z˙1​=0. Thus the stationary smooth mechanical constraint does no work. The remaining tension powers cancel by part (b), proving E˙=0 and conservation of total energy while the massless inextensible string remains taut and contact is maintained.
