The free-body diagram shows a meridional section of the right circular cone. On the first particle, gravity acts vertically downwards, tension points along the straight line to the apex, and the normal reaction is perpendicular to the surface. The smooth mechanical constraint supplies no tangential friction. The second particle has only its downward gravitational weight and the upward tension.
