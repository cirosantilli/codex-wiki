A possible center c must be a fixed point, so (I−Rθ​)c=b. Let c=cosθ and s=sinθ. The determinant is
det(I−Rθ​)=(1−c)2+s2=2(1−c)=4sin2(θ/2)>0
(19)
when θ is not an integer multiple of 2π. The unique center is therefore
c=(I−Rθ​)−1b=2(1−cosθ)1​(1−cosθsinθ​−sinθ1−cosθ​)b.​
(20)
The fixed point equation implies b=c−Rθ​c, whence
T(v)=c+Rθ​(v−c).
(21)
Relative to c, this is precisely a planar rotation through θ. This also explains the excluded case: when θ∈2πZ, T is a translation, with no fixed point unless b=0.
