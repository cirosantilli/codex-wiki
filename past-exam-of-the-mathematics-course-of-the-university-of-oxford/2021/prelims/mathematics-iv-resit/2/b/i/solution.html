Write Tθ,b​(v)=Rθ​v+b. The sine addition formula and cosine addition formula give Rθ​Rψ​=Rθ+ψ​. Therefore function composition satisfies
Tθ,b​∘Tψ,c​=Tθ+ψ,Rθ​c+b​.
(17)
The resulting angle is real and the new translation vector lies in R2, so G is closed under function composition. Associativity holds because, for any functions S,T,U and any v, both (S∘T)∘U and S∘(T∘U) take v to S(T(U(v))).
The identity element is T0,0​, since R0​=I. Every element has the two-sided inverse element
Tθ,b−1​=T−θ,−R−θ​b​.​
(18)
Indeed Rθ​R−θ​=R−θ​Rθ​=I, and substituting into the composition formula makes the translation vector zero in either order. These verify all the group axioms. Thus G is a group under function composition, the special Euclidean group in two dimensions.
