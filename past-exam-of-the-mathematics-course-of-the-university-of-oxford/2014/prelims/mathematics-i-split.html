Solutions to all seven questions of the University of Oxford undergraduate Prelims Mathematics I examination, sat on 23 June 2014. The cover also identifies the shared mathematics and computer science paper as M1. Source: released examination archive, member FOI-20210708-1 - Data/4243.pdf, printed pages 1–4. Question statements are omitted. A bounded exact-paper availability check found no advertised full or partial answers; the published even-year policy explicitly establishes coverage back to at least 2016, rather than establishing this 2014 paper's availability.
