The preimage is
T−1(X)={u∈U:T(u)∈X}.
(24)
It contains 0, and if u,v belong to it and a,b∈R, then T(au+bv)=aT(u)+bT(v)∈X, since X is a vector subspace. Thus T−1(X) is a vector subspace. It contains the kernel of T because 0∈X.
To establish the dimension of the preimage of a vector subspace formula without assuming the theorem to be deduced, choose a basis k1​,…,knT​​ of kerT and a basis y1​,…,yd​ of X∩imT, where d=dim(X∩imT). For each j, choose uj​∈U with T(uj​)=yj​. These uj​ lie in T−1(X).
The family k1​,…,knT​​,u1​,…,ud​ is linearly independent: applying T to a vanishing linear combination forces all uj​ coefficients to vanish by linear independence of the yj​, and then all remaining coefficients vanish by linear independence of the ki​. It also spans T−1(X): for u in that preimage, write T(u)=∑j​bj​yj​. Then u−∑j​bj​uj​∈kerT, so it is a linear combination of the ki​. Consequently
dimT−1(X)=dim(X∩imT)+nT​.​
(25)
Set X=V. Then T−1(V)=U and V∩imT=imT, giving the rank-nullity theorem:
dimU=rT​+nT​.​
