Let v∈S. Choose a unit vector u perpendicular to v, and put w=v×u. The cross product identities show that (v,u,w) is an orthonormal basis with positive orientation: u×(v×u)=v, so
det(v​u​w​)=v⋅(u×w)=v⋅v=1.
(64)
Thus the matrix Av​ with these columns belongs to the special orthogonal group G and satisfies Av​N=v. For any v,z∈S, the element Az​Av−1​ sends v to z. Therefore the group action on the unit sphere is transitive.
