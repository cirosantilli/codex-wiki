OurBigBook.com Words: 175
Acting separately on disjoint sets of four and eight symbols embeds the direct product of groups S4​×S8​ into S12​. The action is injective since fixing all twelve symbols forces both factors to be identities.
For the lower bound, S4​ contains a cycle of order four and S8​ contains disjoint cycles of lengths five and three, whose product has order fifteen. The corresponding element of S4​×S8​ has order of a group element lcm(4,15)=60. Every isomorphic subgroup of Sn​ must therefore contain a permutation of order sixty. We can bound the minimum number of symbols for a permutation of prescribed order directly here.
In the disjoint cycle decomposition of such a permutation, all cycle lengths divide 60, and their least common multiple must contain the factors 4, 3, and 5. If these factors occur in three distinct cycles, those cycles use at least 4+3+5=12 symbols. If two occur in the same cycle, that cycle has length at least their product: combining 4,3 costs at least 12, combining 4,5 costs at least 20, and combining 3,5 costs at least 15. Each possibility already requires at least twelve symbols. Thus n≥12, and
nmin​=12.​
