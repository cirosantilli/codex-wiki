Every step function is bounded and Riemann integrable: away from its finitely many partition points it is constant, and those exceptional points can be covered by intervals of arbitrarily small total length. A partition of an interval containing their boundaries makes the difference of its upper Darboux sum and lower Darboux sum arbitrarily small.

U(f,P)≤U(ϕn​,P)+En​(b−a),L(f,P)≥L(ϕn​,P)−En​(b−a).
