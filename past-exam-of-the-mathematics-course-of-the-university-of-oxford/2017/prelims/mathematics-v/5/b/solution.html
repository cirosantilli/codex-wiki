This is wave reflection by odd extension. If a condition at infinity is desired, take the solution with finite wave energy; here the profile has bounded support at every finite time.
The initial profile is continuous and piecewise continuously differentiable, but its first derivative jumps at the support endpoints. Thus the formula gives a weak solution globally and a piecewise classical solution away from the characteristic lines for the one-dimensional wave equation issuing from those endpoints. The initial velocity is zero in the square-integrable function sense and pointwise wherever the initial profile is differentiable; at the kink points a pointwise right time derivative need not equal zero. This regularity qualification is needed for the given data.
