 ... 2017 Prelims Mathematics V resit Question 2 a iv
OurBigBook.com Words: 106
For α=3, the divergence vanishes at every point with r>0, so its ordinary volume integral, ignoring the undefined single point, is zero. Nevertheless, the outward flux integral through the unit sphere is still 4π:
∭0<r≤1​∇⋅udV=0,∬r=1​u⋅ndS=4π​.
(14)
The hypotheses of the divergence theorem fail at the origin. Indeed ∣u∣=r−2 there, so no continuous extension is possible. On the punctured region ε<r<1, the divergence theorem remains valid: the outer flux is 4π and the inner flux is −4π. Unlike iii, this inner contribution does not tend to zero. The discrepancy therefore comes from the persistent flux at the singular point, rather than a failure of the divergence theorem under its hypotheses.
