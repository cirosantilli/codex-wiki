On the unit circle, the positively oriented unit tangent vector is u=(−y,x,0), and ∣u∣=1. Hence dr=uds and
∮C​(ψu)⋅dr=∮C​ψds.
(27)
Since the scalar field ψ has no z dependence, the curl product rule for a scalar field gives
∇×(ψu)​=ψ(0,0,2)+(ψx​,ψy​,0)×(−y,x,0)=(0,0,2ψ+xψx​+yψy​).​
(28)
On the unit upper hemisphere S1​, the outward unit normal vector is (x,y,z), so its vertical component is z=cosθ. Applying the Stokes theorem to the vector field ψu therefore proves
∮C​ψds=∬S1​​(2ψ+x∂x∂ψ​+y∂y∂ψ​)cosθdS​.
(29)
The smoothness needed here is that ψ extend as a continuously differentiable scalar field to a neighbourhood of the projected unit disk. The left side is a scalar line integral with respect to arc length; the positive boundary orientation is used when identifying it with the vector line integral.
