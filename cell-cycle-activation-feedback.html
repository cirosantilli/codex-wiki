Cell-cycle activation feedback

 Home Biology Signal transduction Signaling feedback Positive feedback
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 13
Cell-cycle activation feedback is the reinforcement of Cdc2 activation through Cdc25 and suppression of Wee1. Its role is to help make transitions in the cell cycle decisive; the cell-cycle hysteresis experiment tests the resulting states rather than inferring them solely from circuit arrows.
