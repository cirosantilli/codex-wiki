Asymmetric Brownian interval-exit transform (E[e−λ2T/21{BT​=a}​]=sinh(λ(a+b))sinh(λb)​)

For standard Brownian motion started at zero and exit time T from (−b,a), a,b>0, stop the two exponential Brownian martingales with parameters λ and −λ. Their stopped values are bounded by e∣λ∣max(a,b). The optional stopping theorem and dominated convergence theorem give eλap+e−λbq=1 and e−λap+eλbq=1, where p,q are the discounted upper and lower exit probabilities. Solving gives the displayed formula and q=sinh(λa)/sinh(λ(a+b)). Thus
Ee−λ2T/2=cosh(λ(a+b)/2)cosh(λ(a−b)/2)​.
(28)
At λ=0, the first ratio has its removable value b/(a+b), and the second equals one. Finiteness of T follows from a uniformly positive one-unit-time chance to leave any fixed bounded interval.
