Causality root criterion for an autoregressive model (Φ(z)=0(∣z∣≤1))

A causal time series satisfying Φ(B)Xt​=εt​ with nondegenerate white noise has a square-summable present-and-past filter. The identity Φ(z)Ψ(z)=1 excludes roots inside the unit disk; a pole of 1/Φ on its boundary also prevents square summability. Thus all roots of Φ lie strictly outside the unit circle.
