Thermal trace of an interval image kernel (Z=∫0L​KD​(q,q;β)dq)

For a particle with mass m on (0,L), put K0​(x;β)=m/(2πℏ2β)​e−mx2/(2ℏ2β). The method of images gives KD​(qf​,qi​;β)=∑r​[K0​(qf​−qi​+2rL;β)−K0​(qf​+qi​+2rL;β)]. The diagonal integral is
Z=L2πℏ2βm​​∑r∈Z​e−2mL2r2/(ℏ2β)−21​.
(23)
The reflected intervals tile the real line, giving the subtraction 1/2. The Poisson summation formula converts this to ∑n≥1​e−βℏ2π2n2/(2mL2), proving equality between the image and energy eigenstate calculations.
