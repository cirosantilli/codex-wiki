For the centered second difference and centered first difference on a finite interval with zero Dirichlet boundary conditions, the diffusion matrix is symmetric negative definite and the drift matrix is skew-symmetric. Discrete summation by parts gives the displayed mesh-weighted identity. It proves contraction in the discrete L2 norm without needing periodic modes, positive stencil coefficients or simultaneous diagonalization.
