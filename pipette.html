A pipette transfers a measured portion of a chemical solution.
For an acid-base titration, a volumetric pipette selects an aliquot whose amount of substance is n=cV from a homogeneous sample. The delivered volume depends on the instrument's calibration and drainage convention; the residual liquid of a gravity-draining volumetric pipette is allowed for in that calibration. Its mark is read using the liquid meniscus. A volume calibration alone does not establish that the sampled chemical solution has the intended concentration.[ref]
