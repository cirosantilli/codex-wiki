Flat manifold

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Second fundamental form Gauss–Codazzi equations Gauss equation Sectional curvature
OurBigBook.com Words: 685 Articles: 12
A Riemannian manifold is flat when every sectional curvature is zero. A complete simply connected flat n-manifold is isometric to Euclidean space Rn.
