Collisionless preservation of a Planck spectrum

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Scale factor (cosmology) Cosmological redshift
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Let the scale factor grow by f from an initially thermal photon distribution. geodesic redshift gives νi​=fν, and conserving photons in corresponding comoving frequency bins gives nν​(t)=f−2nfν​(ti​). Inserting Planck's law leaves the same spectral form at temperature Ti​/f. Total photon-number conservation alone would not prove that the entire radiation spectrum stays Planckian; frequency redshift and collisionless propagation are also required. Absorption, injection or spectral mixing can invalidate this one-temperature argument.
