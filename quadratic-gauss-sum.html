Quadratic Gauss sum (G(χ))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Quadratic residue
OurBigBook.com Words: 66 Articles: 1
For ω=e2πi/p and the Legendre symbol χ, the quadratic Gauss sum is
G(χ)=∑a=1p−1​χ(a)ωa.
(21)
It equals p​ for p≡1(mod4) and ip​ for p≡3(mod4) with the standard choice of square root.
