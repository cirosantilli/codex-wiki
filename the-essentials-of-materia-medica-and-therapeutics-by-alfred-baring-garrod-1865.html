The Essentials of Materia Medica and Therapeutics by Alfred Baring Garrod (1865) (1865)

 Home Biology Biology bibliography Pharmacology and toxicology bibliography
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
American edition, William Wood, with British entries distinguished from bracketed United States additions. The preface dates the underlying British revision to May 1864. Edition and contents. Posological table and preparation strengths. Historical doses describe period practice, not current prescribing.
