Weierstrass gap (G(p))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Divisor on an algebraic curve Riemann-Roch theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 127 Articles: 2
At a point p of a smooth projective curve, a positive integer n is a Weierstrass gap when no rational function has a pole of order exactly n at p and no other poles. Equivalently,
ℓ(np)=ℓ((n−1)p).
