For positive gain and nonlinear-loss coefficients, a constant-density exciton-polariton condensate with physical velocity vx has ψ=γ/Γ​exp[i(mvx−μt)/ℏ]. Its chemical potential obeys the displayed relation. For prescribed real μ, a real velocity requires μ≥gγ/Γ. If chemical potential is determined by the solution, any real velocity is allowed. Linear density-phase perturbations in the comoving frame satisfy λ(λ+2γ/ℏ)+ϵk​(ϵk​+2gn∗​)/ℏ2=0, where ϵk​=ℏ2k2/(2m). Repulsive coupling has no growing modes in this simple model; attractive coupling has a long-wavelength instability even though the plane wave exists.
