Hecke character of a CM elliptic curve (ψE​:AF×​/F×→C×)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Hecke character
OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 1
Suppose F contains the CM field K and all CM endomorphisms are defined over F. CM reciprocity compares the torus with its idele-transformed lattice and gives a(s)∈K× for finite ideles. Then ψE​(s)=a(sf​)/NF/K​(s)∞​. On principal ideles, a(bf​)=NF/K​(b), so the character descends to the idèle class group. At a good prime its ideal value reduces to the Frobenius isogeny. If the original field omits K, construct the character over FK and induce the corresponding representation down to the original field.
