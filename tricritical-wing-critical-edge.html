Tricritical wing critical edge (Mc2​=−3u/(10v))

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Landau theory Tricritical point Tricritical wing
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
For f=rM2/2+uM4/4+vM6/6−hM with v>0, the ordinary critical edge of a tricritical wing satisfies f(1)=f(2)=f(3)=0. For u<0, Mc2​=−3u/(10v), rc​=9u2/(20v) and hc​=6u2Mc​/(25v). Its fourth derivative is −12u>0. The two signs of Mc​ give the two wings.
