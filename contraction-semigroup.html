Contraction semigroup (∥S(t)∥≤1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis C0-semigroup
OurBigBook.com Words: 669 Articles: 16
A contraction semigroup is a strongly continuous semigroup of bounded linear operators satisfying ∥S(t)∥≤1 for every t≥0. The Hille-Yosida theorem characterizes its infinitesimal generator of a semigroup by dense domain, closedness, and the resolvent estimates ∥(λ−A)−n∥≤λ−n for real λ>0 and integers n≥1.
