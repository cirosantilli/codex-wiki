Polynomial circle log-mean (Lp​(R)=log∣c∣+∑j​logmax(R,∣aj​∣))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Holomorphic function Jensen's formula Circle log-mean of a holomorphic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 212
For a polynomial p(z)=c∏j​(z−aj​) with c=0, no polynomial root at zero, and R>0 different from every ∣aj​∣, Jensen's formula gives the displayed expression. The polynomial roots are repeated according to their multiplicity of a zero: an exterior root contributes log∣aj​∣, whereas an interior root contributes logR.
For an explicit example, take the polynomial
f(z)=3(z−41​)(z+21​),f(0)=−83​.
(11)
Its two simple zeros are 1/4 and −1/2. The circle log-mean of a holomorphic function is therefore
Lf​(R)=⎩⎨⎧​log(3/8),log(3R/2),log(3R2),​0<R<1/4,1/4<R<1/2,R>1/2.​
(12)
At R=0.2 the open disc contains no zeros of a function, at R=0.4 it contains 1/4 only, and at R=0.75 it contains both. These three circles avoid the polynomial roots; the radii 1/4 and 1/2 are excluded from the displayed version.
