Write X=SpecB, S=SpecA with A,B integral domains, let α:A→B be the ring homomorphism, and put K=Frac(B). We do not assume that α is injective. Write A′=α(A)⊆B⊆K.
Let D⊆K be any valuation ring containing A′, with fraction field K. The canonical morphism of schemes SpecK→SpecB and the ring homomorphism A→A′↪D form a valuative problem for f. Since f is a proper morphism, there is an extension. On coordinate rings it is a ring homomorphism β:B→D such that
