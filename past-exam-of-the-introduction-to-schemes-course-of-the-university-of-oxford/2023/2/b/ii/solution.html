Let Xf​Yg​S be morphisms of schemes. Start with u:SpecK→X and v:SpecD→S satisfying gfu=v∣SpecK​.
For existence, first apply the extension condition for g to fu and v, obtaining w:SpecD→Y. Then apply the extension condition for f to u,w, obtaining ℓ:SpecD→X. This ℓ solves the problem for gf. If both f,g are universally closed morphisms, both are quasi-compact morphisms; their composite is a quasi-compact morphism by the allowed stability fact. Part (a)(i) therefore proves that gf is a universally closed morphism.
For uniqueness, suppose ℓ1​,ℓ2​ are extensions for gf. Uniqueness for g gives fℓ1​=fℓ2​=w, and uniqueness for f, now over this same w, gives ℓ1​=ℓ2​. If f,g are separated morphisms, their composite is a quasi-separated morphism by the allowed stability fact. Part (a)(ii) proves that gf is a separated morphism.
Finally, if f,g are proper morphisms, these arguments give both existence and uniqueness. Stability under composition of quasi-compact morphisms, quasi-separated morphisms and locally of finite type gives the other hypotheses of (a)(iii). Consequently
f,g universally closedf,g separatedf,g proper​⟹gf universally closed,⟹gf separated,⟹gf proper.​​
