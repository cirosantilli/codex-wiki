Let U=SpecA and V=SpecB be affine open subschemes of the separated scheme X. Its diagonal morphism is a closed immersion
Δ:X↪X×SpecZ​X.
(38)
Restricting this closed immersion to the open subscheme U×SpecZ​V identifies its inverse image with U∩V. Hence
U∩V↪U×SpecZ​V=Spec(A⊗Z​B)
(39)
is a closed immersion. A closed subscheme of an affine scheme is an affine scheme, so
U∩V is an affine open subscheme of X.​
(40)
The argument iterates to every finite intersection of affine open subschemes. The empty intersection of two disjoint charts causes no exception: the empty scheme is Spec(0).
