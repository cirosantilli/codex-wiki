Interpretation of the removed locus. Over Z, the notation [1:0:0] specifies a section SpecZ→PZ2​, with image V+​(x1​,x2​)≅SpecZ. We remove this entire closed subscheme. This is the interpretation forced by the asserted open cover X=U1​∪U2​. Removing one closed point in a particular fiber would not give that open cover. The native PDF uses the same notation as the transcription.
Put a=x0​/x1​, b=x2​/x1​ on U1​. On U2​ put c=x0​/x2​, d=x1​/x2​. On their intersection c=a/b and d=b−1, giving coordinate rings
Γ(U1​,OX​)=Z[a,b],Γ(U2​,OX​)=Z[a/b,b−1],Γ(U1​∩U2​,OX​)=L:=Z[a,b,b−1].
(43)
The Čech cochain complex for the structure sheaf, using increasing distinct indices, is
0⟶Z[a,b]⊕Z[a/b,b−1]δ​L⟶0,δ(f,g)=g−f.
(44)
Thus Hˇ0 is the intersection of the two subrings of L, while Hˇ1 is the quotient module of L by their additive sum.
The Laurent polynomial ring L is a free abelian group on the monomials aibj with i≥0, j∈Z. The first subring has the basis j≥0. The second has the basis
cidk=aib−i−k,i,k≥0,
(45)
so its allowed exponents satisfy j≤−i. A monomial belongs to both precisely when i=j=0. The monomials belonging to neither satisfy i≥2 and −i<j<0. Since the subgroups are spanned by subsets of the same basis, their sum introduces no other relations. Therefore
Hˇ0({U1​,U2​},OX​)Hˇ1({U1​,U2​},OX​)Hˇq({U1​,U2​},OX​)​=Z,=i≥2⨁​ k=1⨁i−1​Z[aib−k]=m,n≥1⨁​Z[x1m​x2n​x0m+n​​],=0(q≥2).​​
(46)
Here the square brackets denote cohomology classes, and the direct sums are as abelian groups. In particular, [a2b−1]=[x02​/(x1​x2​)] is a nonzero cohomology class. This scheme is a separated scheme, being an open subscheme of projective space over Z, and is a quasi-compact scheme by (d). Both charts and their intersection are affine schemes, so this Čech cohomology equals sheaf cohomology. The nonzero first sheaf cohomology of the quasi-coherent structure sheaf rules out an affine scheme:
X is not affine.​
(47)
