Past exam of the introduction to schemes course of the University of Oxford / 2017 / 1 / a / Solution

OurBigBook.com Words: 84
Apply the surjectivity of f(Y) to the identity morphism 1Y​. It gives a morphism of schemes g:Y→X satisfying f∘g=1Y​. To obtain the other identity, compare g∘f and 1X​ in X(X). Their images under f(X) agree, since
f∘(g∘f)=(f∘g)∘f=f=f∘1X​.
(1)
The injectivity of f(X) therefore gives g∘f=1X​. Thus
f is an isomorphism of schemes, with inverse g.​
(2)
Only surjectivity for the test scheme Y and injectivity for the test scheme X were needed. This is the isomorphism-detection argument behind the Yoneda lemma for the functor of points.
