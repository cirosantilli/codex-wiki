Disk of convergence ({z∈C:∣z−c∣<R})

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Power series Radius of convergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For a power series centred at c with radius of convergence R, its disk of convergence is the open disk ∣z−c∣<R, on which the series has absolute convergence. Boundary points may or may not belong to the larger domain of convergence. When R=0 this open disk is empty, although the power series still converges at its centre; when R=∞ it is the whole complex plane.
