Kähler class ([κ]∈H1,1(X,R))

 ... Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Kähler manifold Kähler cone
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The de Rham cohomology class of a Kähler form is a Kähler class. Such classes form the Kähler cone. They have positive integral on every complex curve, and their top self-intersection is positive.
