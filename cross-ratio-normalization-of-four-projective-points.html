Cross-ratio normalization of four projective points ((P0​P1​:P2​P3​)=T(P3​),(T(P0​),T(P1​),T(P2​))=(∞,0,1))

For four distinct projective points on the complex projective line, use the convention
(P0​P1​:P2​P3​)=[p0​,p3​][p1​,p2​][p0​,p2​][p1​,p3​]​,
(65)
where [pi​,pj​] is the determinant of chosen representative vectors. Rescaling any representative cancels, and applying an invertible matrix multiplies every bracket by its determinant, which also cancels. Thus the expression is a projective invariant. The projective general linear group action on the projective line has a unique element T taking the first three points to ∞,0,1. In those coordinates the displayed expression is exactly T(P3​). Consequently the cross-ratio completely determines the orbit of the ordered quadruple under the projective linear group. Its value lies in C∖{0,1}.
