Free-free absorption

Free-free absorption is the inverse of bremsstrahlung: a free electron absorbs a photon during an encounter with an ion, which permits conservation of momentum. In local thermodynamic equilibrium its absorption coefficient and the thermal free-free emissivity obey Kirchhoff's law of thermal radiation, jν​=αν​Bν​(T).
