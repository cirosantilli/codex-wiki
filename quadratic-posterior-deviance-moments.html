Quadratic posterior deviance moments (pD​=tr(JΣ))

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Model selection Deviance information criterion Effective parameter count in DIC
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
Suppose the Bayesian deviance is locally D(θ)=D(θ)+(θ−θ)TJ(θ−θ) with positive-definite J, and the posterior is normal with mean m and covariance Σ. Writing b=m−θ, expectation and variance of the quadratic form give
E[D(θ)∣y]=D(θ)+bTJb+tr(JΣ),
(9)
Var[D(θ)∣y]=2tr{(JΣ)2}+4bTJΣJb.
(10)
Consequently the usual effective parameter count in DIC is pD​=tr(JΣ), whereas half the deviance variance is tr{(JΣ)2}+2bTJΣJb. A locally flat prior gives m=θ and Σ=J−1, so both counts equal the parameter dimension. Informative priors generally destroy their equality. The expectation formula requires only the first two moments; normality is used for the variance formula.
