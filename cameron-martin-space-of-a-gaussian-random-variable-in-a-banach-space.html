Cameron-Martin space of a Gaussian random variable in a Banach space (H=SG,Sg=E[Xg])

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process Gaussian measure Cameron-Martin space of a Gaussian measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 94
Let G be the first Gaussian chaos of a centered Gaussian random variable in a Banach space. By Fernique's theorem, Sg=E[Xg] is a Bochner integral in B. The map S is injective, because Sg=0 implies E[gℓ(X)]=0 for every continuous linear functional ℓ, hence g=0. Its range becomes a Hilbert space under ⟨Sg,Sv⟩H​=E[gv]. The embedding in B is continuous by Cauchy-Schwarz inequality. Its reproducing property is ⟨h,Sℓ(X)⟩H​=ℓ(h). Thus this is the Reproducing kernel Hilbert space of the random variable, including degenerate laws. For h=Sg, the scalar coordinate h(X)=g has variance ∥h∥H2​; it is generally a measurable linear coordinate rather than a continuous functional on B.
