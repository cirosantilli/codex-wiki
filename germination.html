Germination

 Home Biology Organism Eukaryote Plant Seed
OurBigBook.com Words: 753 Articles: 5
Seed germination is renewed plant embryo activity and growth. Many germination assays score completion as radicle emergence, before seedling establishment. Seed imbibition precedes that endpoint; swelling alone is insufficient.[ref]
Water, molecular oxygen and suitable temperature support germination; light sensitivity depends on the species and seed state. Neither illumination nor one particular temperature is universally required. Seed dormancy connects germination timing to ecology by delaying seedling establishment.[ref]
The distinction matters when comparing propagation experiments: a treatment that increases radicle emergence need not improve later seedling survival. Define the endpoint before comparing treatments, then measure the later outcome separately.
