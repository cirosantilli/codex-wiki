Prismatic-channel shallow water equations (At​+(Au)x​=0,(Au)t​+(Au2+P)x​=0)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Shallow water equations
OurBigBook.com Words: 328 Articles: 4
In a channel whose transverse width b(z) does not depend on downstream position, the wetted area is A(h)=∫0h​b(z)dz and the integrated hydrostatic pressure force divided by density is P(h)=g∫0h​(h−z)b(z)dz. With cross-sectionally uniform velocity, volume conservation and momentum conservation give the displayed shallow water equations. Since P′(h)=gA(h), the two characteristic speeds are u±c with c2=gA/A′. For b(z)=Czp, c2=gh/(p+1) and the Riemann invariants are u±2(p+1)c.
