Monadic length

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Monad Eilenberg-Moore category Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
When each comparison functor has a left adjoint, iterating the Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor produces the monadic tower. Its monadic length is the least number of comparison steps needed to reach an equivalence; an equivalence has length zero and a non-equivalence that is already monadic has length one.
