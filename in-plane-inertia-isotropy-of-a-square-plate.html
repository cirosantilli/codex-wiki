In-plane inertia isotropy of a square plate (Iϕ​=Ml2/12)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics Moment of inertia Moment of inertia of a uniform square plate
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A uniform thin square of side l and mass M has Iz​=Ml2/6 about the perpendicular central axis. Any central in-plane axis and its perpendicular in-plane partner have equal moments by 90∘ rotational symmetry. The perpendicular axis theorem says their sum is Iz​, proving Iϕ​=Ml2/12 for every angle ϕ. A non-square rectangle generally lacks this angle independence.
