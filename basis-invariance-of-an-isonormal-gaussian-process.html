Basis invariance of an isonormal Gaussian process (W(h)=∑j​Zj​⟨h,ej​⟩)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process Isonormal Gaussian process
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
For any real separable Hilbert space H, any orthonormal basis (ej​) and independent standard normal random variables (Zj​), the displayed series converges almost surely for each fixed h∈H and in L2 space. Indeed, the Parseval identity for a Hilbertian basis gives ∑j​⟨h,ej​⟩2=∥h∥2, so the partial sums form an L2 space-bounded martingale. The martingale convergence theorem supplies both limits. Every finite linear combination has the normal distribution with variance ∥∑j​uj​hj​∥2. Thus its finite-dimensional characteristic function is exp(−∥∑j​uj​hj​∥2/2), independent of the chosen orthonormal basis. This proves equality of the finite-dimensional distributions of the resulting isonormal Gaussian processes; pathwise equality needs a coupling.
