Sobolev duality ((Hs(Rd))∗=H−s(Rd))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
The continuous dual space of Hs(Rd) is H−s(Rd) under the extension of the L2 inner product. The Fourier transform definition ∥f∥Hs2​=∫(1+∣ξ∣2)s∣f​(ξ)∣2dξ proves boundedness of the pairing by the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, and the Riesz representation theorem proves that every continuous functional has this form. Consequently a bounded linear operator K:Hr→Hr+a has an adjoint operator, relative to this pairing, from H−r−a to H−r. For an operator that smooths by two orders for every r, this yields K∗:H2→H4 by taking r=−4.
