Trace-adjoint identity for partial transpose

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Separable quantum state Partial transpose
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For bipartite operators X,Y in a fixed product basis,
Tr(XYTB​)=Tr(XTB​Y).
(20)
Expand both operators in products of matrix units. On the transposed factor, the identity is Tr(AT)=Tr(ATTT); bilinearity gives the bipartite result. It allows a negative partial transpose eigenvector to define an entanglement witness.
