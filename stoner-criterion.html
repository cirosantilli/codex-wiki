Stoner criterion (Uc​χ0​=1,χ0​=∫ν(−nF′​)dϵ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum magnetism Hubbard model Itinerant ferromagnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 377 Articles: 4
In the homogeneous spin-vector mean-field scheme with field cost M2/(2U) and spin-summed density of states per site, the paramagnetic curvature is proportional to U−1−∫ν(ϵ)[−nF′​(ϵ)]dϵ. It changes sign at Uχ0​=1. For a smooth low-temperature density of states, χ0​≃ν(μ), giving the familiar Fermi-level criterion. Interaction and spin-counting conventions must be specified before comparing coefficients.
