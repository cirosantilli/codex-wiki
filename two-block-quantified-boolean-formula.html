Two-block quantified Boolean formula (∃X∀Yψ(X,Y))

 Home Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Polynomial hierarchy Second level of the polynomial hierarchy
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
A propositional formula with two blocks of quantified Boolean variables. Determining the truth of an existential-then-universal instance is a canonical complete problem for the existential second level of the polynomial hierarchy, Σ2P​. For completeness one may restrict the matrix ψ to a disjunction of terms with at most three literals. The order of the quantifiers matters: the assignment to X must work for every assignment to Y.
