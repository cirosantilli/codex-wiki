Resistive induction equation (∂t​B=∇×(u×B)+η∇2B)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 239 Articles: 3
For a solenoidal vector field B and constant magnetic diffusivity η, the magnetic induction equation is
∂t​B=∇×(u×B)+η∇2B,∇⋅B=0.
(19)
It combines Faraday's law with the moving-conductor relation for electrical conductivity, neglecting displacement current. With incompressible flow, its advective form is ∂t​B+(u⋅∇)B=(B⋅∇)u+η∇2B.
