Susceptible-infective model with exponential density-dependent birth

The equations
S˙=S(be−aS−βI−d),I˙=I(βS−d−δ)
(16)
combine mass-action infection with an exponential density-dependent birth model. Susceptibles reproduce at per-capita rate be−aS, while infectives have no reproductive term and die at total per-capita rate d+δ.
