Twisting sheaf on projective space (O(d))

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Locally free sheaf Line bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 401 Articles: 6
The twisting sheaf O(d) on Projk[x0​,…,xn​] is associated with the shifted graded module k[x0​,…,xn​](d). For d≥0, its global sections are the homogeneous polynomials of degree d.
On projective space the twisting sheaf O(d) is the dth tensor power of O(1) for d≥0, and the corresponding dual power for d<0. Twisting another sheaf means tensoring it with this line bundle.
