Divergence and curl from orthogonal coordinate gradients (∇ui​=ei​/hi​)

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Change of variables formula Orthogonal coordinates
OurBigBook.com Words: 108
For a right-handed regular system of orthogonal coordinates, the gradient in orthogonal coordinates gives ∇ui​=ei​/hi​. Thus ϕe1​=(ϕh1​)∇u1​, and the curl product rule gives
∇×(ϕe1​)=∇(ϕh1​)×∇u1​=h1​h3​e2​​∂u3​​(ϕh1​)−h1​h2​e3​​∂u2​​(ϕh1​).
(90)
Also ∇u2​×∇u3​=e1​/(h2​h3​) and the divergence of this cross product is zero, by the divergence-of-a-cross-product identity and the vanishing curl of each gradient. Applying the divergence product rule to ϕe1​=(ϕh2​h3​)(∇u2​×∇u3​) gives
∇⋅(ϕe1​)=h1​h2​h3​∂u1​​(ϕh2​h3​)​.
(91)
Cyclic permutation and addition give the full component formulas for any vector field. This derivation accounts for the varying coordinate basis without differentiating its unit vectors explicitly. The factors hi​>0 are scale factors of orthogonal coordinates; the chart must avoid coordinate singularities.
