Introduction in Chem2O06 Laboratory Manual by McMaster University. The page heading says Experiment 2, while the manual index and directory identify Experiment 3. The footer is dated 17 September 1997; its initials do not establish a personal author.
Online text: www.chemistry.mcmaster.ca/~chem2o6/labmanual/expt3/exp3-int.html
Cited section: General Principles and boiling-point discussion; A Mixture of Ideal Liquids: first/later fractions; Liquids that Form Azeotropes: equal-composition constant-boiling mixtures and separation boundaries; Fractional Distillation: packed column and nonideal efficiency.
