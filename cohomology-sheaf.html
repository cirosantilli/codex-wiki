Cohomology sheaf (Hi(E))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Sheaf cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 1
For a cochain complex of sheaves E, its ith cohomology sheaf is Hi(E)=ker(di)/im(di−1), where the image and quotient are taken in sheaves. Its stalk at x is the cohomology of the stalk complex, because taking stalks is exact.
