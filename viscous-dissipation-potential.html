Viscous dissipation potential (S∈∂Ω(D))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 1
A convex scalar function Ω(D) on symmetric rate-of-strain tensors generates stress by S=∂Ω/∂D. For incompressible flow, its argument is trace free and a pressure reaction must be added to the stress. At nondifferentiable points the law is S∈∂Ω(D), where the subdifferential is defined by Ω(E)−Ω(D)≥S:(E−D) for every admissible E. This is a strain-rate potential, in contrast with potentials parameterized by thermodynamic forces.
