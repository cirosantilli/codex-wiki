Remote state preparation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 356 Articles: 5
Remote state preparation uses a shared entangled state, local measurement in quantum measurements and classical communication to prepare a state known to the sender at the receiver. The sender can tailor the measurement to the desired state, so restricted state families can require less communication than quantum teleportation. A real great-circle family of qubit states admits one-bit remote preparation of real qubit states.
