Hydrogen Lamb splitting (νL​=[E(2S1/2​)−E(2P1/2​)]/h)

 Home Physics Atomic physics Lamb shift
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
The zero-field energy levels here are averaged over hyperfine structure; h is the Planck constant. Electron self-energy and vacuum polarization produce radiative corrections. Self-energy describes the bound electron's radiative interaction with its own electromagnetic field. Precision predictions also include nuclear recoil and proton charge radius effects; assigning recoil terms to the reference or Lamb shift is convention-dependent. A finite proton charge radius changes binding independently of radiative effects.[ref]
A vacuum-electric field “jiggling” picture motivates one contribution, not every hydrogen atom energy correction.
