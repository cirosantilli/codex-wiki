Minor (linear algebra, detAI,J​)

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix Submatrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 86 Articles: 1
A matrix minor is the determinant of a square submatrix obtained by selecting rows and columns of a matrix. Over a field, the matrix rank is less than r exactly when every r-by-r matrix minor vanishes. In a matrix of regular functions, these equations define a closed rank-defect locus, explaining the determinantal variety construction.
