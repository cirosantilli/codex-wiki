Dirac field in two spacetime dimensions (γ0=σ1​,γ1=−iσ2​,γ5=σ3​)

For Minkowski metric diag(1,−1), the displayed gamma matrices satisfy the Clifford algebra and γ5=γ0γ1 has chirality eigenvalues +1,−1. Ordering the spinor as (ψR​,ψL​)T gives i(∂t​+∂x​)ψR​=mψL​ and i(∂t​−∂x​)ψL​=mψR​. A plane wave has the Dirac Hamiltonian pσ3​+mσ1​, whose square is (p2+m2)1, proving E2=p2+m2.
