Variance of a treatment contrast (Var(cTμ​)=cTCov(μ​)c)

 ... Statistical modelling Normal linear model One-way normal linear model Cell-means parametrization Linear contrast of cell means Treatment contrast
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
For a vector of estimated treatment means μ​, the variance of a treatment contrast with coefficient vector c is cTCov(μ​)c. Averaging subsamples from a common experimental unit does not make their shared variance component disappear.
