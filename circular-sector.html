Circular sector (A=θR2/2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 81
A circular sector is the portion of a disc between two radii and the connecting circle arc. If its radius is R and its central angle is θ radians, its area is
A=21​θR2.​

In a solid with horizontal sector cross-sections, radius R(z) and angle θ(z), its volume is 21​∫θ(z)R(z)2dz. Holding the angles and heights fixed while scaling every radius by a factor s scales that volume by s2.
