Jensen disk proof of the zeta zero-count bound (N(T)≪TlogT)

Put F(s)=(s−1)ζ(s). The fractional-part continuation formula for the Riemann zeta function gives ∣F(s)∣≪(∣s∣+1)2 for Res≥1/4. At 2+ij, the reciprocal Euler product bounds ∣ζ(2+ij)∣ below by 1/ζ(2). Applying the Jensen zero-count bound with outer radius 7/4 and inner radius 8/5 gives O(log(∣j∣+2)) zeros in the latter disk. Those disks cover the half-strip 1/2≤Res≤1. Reflecting zeros using the functional equation of the Riemann zeta function completes the O(TlogT) bound without requiring left-half-plane growth estimates.
