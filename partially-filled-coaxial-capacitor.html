Partially filled coaxial capacitor (C=2πϵ0​[L+(ϵr​−1)x]/ln(b/a))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrostatics Electrostatic energy Coaxial cylindrical capacitor
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 1
A long coaxial cylindrical capacitor of length L and radii a,b has a dielectric-filled overlap of length x and relative permittivity ϵr​. Neglecting end and interface fringing, its filled and vacuum portions are in parallel because both share the same two conductors. The electric potential difference V produces Er​=V/[rln(b/a)] in either portion; the electric displacement field and electrode charge differ by the local permittivity. Adding their capacitances gives the title formula. The dielectric boundary is axial while the interior electric field is radial and tangential to that interface, consistent with the electrostatic interface boundary conditions.
