Jump chain of a discrete-time Markov chain (Pij​=Pij​/(1−Pii​),i=j)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 1
For Pii​<1, observe a discrete-time Markov chain only when its state changes. The probability that its next different state is j is ∑r≥0​Piir​Pij​=Pij​/(1−Pii​). Its holding times are finite geometric waiting times. This construction deletes self-transitions and differs from the existing continuous-time jump-chain concept.
