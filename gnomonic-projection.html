Gnomonic projection ((ξ,η))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Sphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The gnomonic projection intersects rays from a sphere’s centre with a tangent plane. If b is its unit normal and e,n are tangent basis vectors, a direction s maps to f(s⋅e,s⋅n)/(s⋅b). It maps great circle arcs to straight lines and is singular at the tangent-plane horizon.
