Norm-compatible sequence of local units (U∞​=lim​n​Un​)

 ... Algebraic number theory Iwasawa theory Iwasawa module p-ramified Iwasawa module Class-field unit sequence Coleman power series
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
Such a sequence u=(un​) satisfies Nn/n−1​un​=un−1​ at every successive layer. The inverse limit uses the field norm, rather than inclusion of units from the lower field. In a p-power cyclotomic tower a Coleman power series interpolates the sequence, and its Coleman norm operator fixes the interpolating series.
