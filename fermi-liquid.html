Fermi liquid

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 437 Articles: 11
A Fermi liquid is an interacting fermion phase whose low-energy excitations are long-lived quasiparticles near a Fermi surface. Its effective interactions are classified by scaling only the frequency and momentum normal to that surface.
