Orthogonal transformation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Orthogonal group Orthogonal matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
An orthogonal transformation of a real inner-product space is a linear map Q satisfying QTQ=I. It preserves inner products, lengths, angles, and therefore Gaussian curvature when applied to an embedded surface in Euclidean space.
