Cluster hydrostatic mass estimator (M(<r)=−μmp​GkB​Tr​dlogrdlog(ρg​T)​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Galaxy cluster Intracluster medium
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For spherical thermal hydrostatic equilibrium, dP/dr=−ρg​GM(<r)/r2. Inserting the ideal gas law P=ρg​kB​T/(μmp​) with constant mean molecular weight gives the displayed estimator. The density ρg​ is gas density, while M includes every gravitating component. A varying μ adds −dlogμ/dlogr inside the logarithmic bracket. Nonthermal pressure requires differentiating the total supporting pressure rather than thermal pressure alone.
