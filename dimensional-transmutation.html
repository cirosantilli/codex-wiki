Dimensional transmutation

 ... Renormalization Renormalization scale Running coupling Beta function (physics) Asymptotic freedom Strong-coupling scale
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
Dimensional transmutation replaces a dimensionless coupling by a dimensionful renormalization-group invariant scale. In an asymptotically free theory, specifying the coupling at one scale is equivalent to specifying its strong-coupling scale.
