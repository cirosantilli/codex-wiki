Congelation ice

 ... Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Sea ice First-year sea ice
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
Congelation ice grows by freezing onto the underside of an existing sheet. Competitive growth selects grains whose fast basal-plane growth can advance downward, yielding columnar sea-ice crystal fabric with brine-bearing intergranular regions.
