Semilatus rectum (ℓ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Conic section
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
The distance from a focus to a conic section along a line perpendicular to its symmetry axis. In focus-centred polar coordinates, an ellipse may be written r(1−ecosθ)=ℓ, where e is its eccentricity of a conic.
The distance from a focus of a conic section to the curve measured perpendicular to its principal axis. In the focus-based polar coordinates equation r=p/(1+ecosϕ) it is the length p, where e is the eccentricity. For an ellipse, p=a(1−e2)=b2/a. For a Newtonian Kepler orbit, p=j2/(GM) with j the specific angular momentum.
