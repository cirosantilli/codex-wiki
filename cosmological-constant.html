Cosmological constant (Λ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Dark energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 2
The cosmological constant Λ is the constant term proportional to the metric in the Einstein field equations. Interpreted as vacuum energy, it has PΛ​=−ρΛ​, so its energy density is constant during expansion.
