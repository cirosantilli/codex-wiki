Dunning-Kruger effect

 Home Science Psychology Cognitive psychology Cognitive bias
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 1
A pattern in which low observed performers overestimate their performance, often accompanied by hypotheses about deficits in evaluating their own answers. Task skill, comparison-group beliefs, measurement noise and grouping can all affect the observed relation; the pattern alone does not identify one cause.[ref]
