Chandrasekhar number (Q=B02​d2/(μ0​ρ0​ην))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Magnetoconvection
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
For an imposed field B0​ in a layer of depth d, the Chandrasekhar number compares magnetic tension with viscous and magnetic-diffusive effects. Here η is magnetic diffusivity and ν is kinematic viscosity. In thermal-diffusion units the linear magnetic-force coefficient is Qζ, where ζ=η/κ and κ is thermal diffusivity.
