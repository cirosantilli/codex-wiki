Quantum rotating frame (Hr​=R†HR−iR†R˙)

OurBigBook.com Words: 71
A time-dependent unitary operator R(t) defines transformed quantum states by ∣ψr​⟩=R†∣ψ⟩. Substituting ∣ψ⟩=R∣ψr​⟩ into the Schrödinger equation gives the displayed transformed Hamiltonian operator, in units ℏ=1. If R=e−itH0​ for constant H0​, this becomes Hr​=R†HR−H0​. Removing a common nuclear Zeeman energy with H0​=ω(ZI+IZ)/2 leaves an isotropic Heisenberg model coupling unchanged, because it commutes with the generator of the common rotation. Removing two unequal nuclear frequencies instead makes their exchange terms oscillate at the difference frequency.
