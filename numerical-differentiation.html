Numerical differentiation (f′(x)≈hf(x+h)−f(x)​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 275 Articles: 2
Numerical differentiation estimates derivatives from finitely many function values, often by differentiating an interpolation polynomial or using a finite difference. Its error combines truncation error from the formula with errors already present in the sampled values and additional roundoff error. Decreasing the sample spacing can decrease truncation error while increasing the effect of uncertain values. This tradeoff is explained by the ill-posedness of numerical differentiation when only an absolute value-error bound is available.
