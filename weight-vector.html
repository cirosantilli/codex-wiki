Weight vector

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Highest-weight representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 986 Articles: 21
A vector v has weight μ∈t∗ when hv=μ(h)v for every h in the Cartan subalgebra.
A Weight is the eigenvalue functional; a weight vector is a nonzero vector carrying it.
