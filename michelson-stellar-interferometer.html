Michelson stellar interferometer

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Interferometer Astronomical interferometer
OurBigBook.com Words: 248 Articles: 3
The Michelson stellar interferometer, associated with Albert Abraham Michelson, uses separated collectors and beam-redirection optics to produce interference fringes. The collector spacing determines sensitivity to a stellar angular diameter, independently of the final beam spacing at the combiner. The first null for a uniform disk is D=1.21967λ/α, where α is the full angular diameter. Different surface-brightness laws, including limb darkening, change the null.
