Initial delay in Griffith growth under proportional loading (G(s,t)=t2G(s,1))

 ... Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Fracture toughness Griffith fracture criterion
OurBigBook.com Words: 108
For linear antiplane elasticity with body forces and Dirichlet data proportional to time, uniqueness gives u(s,t)=tu(s,1) at any fixed crack length s. The minimized elastic-plus-load potential and its energy release rate scale quadratically. The local Griffith fracture criterion laws are ℓ˙≥0, G(ℓ,t)≤Gc​ and ℓ˙(Gc​−G)=0, with positive toughness Gc​.
If G(s,1) is finite and continuous near the initial length, it is bounded there by M. Continuity of the crack path keeps it in that neighborhood for a positive interval. Choose the interval short enough that t2M<Gc​; complementarity then forces zero growth throughout it. This proves a positive initial waiting time without assuming the crack is stationary in advance.
