Aquifer drainage hydrograph (Q0​(t))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Aquifer
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
An aquifer drainage hydrograph plots the time-dependent discharge from an aquifer. In the shallow constant-recharge model, early discharge grows linearly according to the rain-fed aquifer similarity solution. Sustained recharge R on a finite length L gives eventual steady discharge RL when the other end is impermeable. After recharge stops, a separable aquifer drawdown has height proportional to 1/(t+t0​) and discharge proportional to 1/(t+t0​)2; this is generally a late-time description rather than an exact solution for arbitrary inherited height profiles.
