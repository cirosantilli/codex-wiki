Haploid (n)

 Home Biology Genetics Ploidy
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
A haploid cell nucleus has one complete set of chromosomes. For the stipulated n=3 example, both products after meiosis I and products after meiosis II are haploid; their Nuclear DNA contents differ because the former retain associated sister chromatids. Haploid therefore does not mean “unreplicated”.[ref]
