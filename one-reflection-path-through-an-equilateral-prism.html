One-reflection path through an equilateral prism (δ=180∘−2α+2θ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Geometrical optics Prism (optics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 98
For the lower-face-first ray path through an equilateral optical prism, take external entry angle θ, internal refraction angle r, base incidence α−r and one total internal reflection at the horizontal base. At the other sloped face the internal incidence equals r, so Snell's law gives exit angle θ again. Its counterclockwise, oriented direction change is the displayed expression with α=60∘. It is independent of refractive index for this route, but whether the base reflection is total is not. Under normal dispersion, a setting at the green critical threshold sends shorter wavelengths along the total-reflection route while longer wavelengths can leak through the base.
