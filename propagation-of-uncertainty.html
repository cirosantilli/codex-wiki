Propagation of uncertainty (σg2​≃∇gTΣ∇g)

 ... Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Wavenumber Wavelength Acoustic wavelength measurement
OurBigBook.com Words: 530 Articles: 9
Propagation of uncertainty estimates how measurement errors in inputs affect a calculated result. For small input errors with covariance matrix Σ, linearizing a differentiable function g gives the displayed approximation for its variance. For independent errors in a product g=2fd, the squared relative standard deviations add. Correlated errors need the covariance terms; systematic offsets require separate assessment.
