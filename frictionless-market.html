Frictionless market

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Financial market
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A financial market model that omits trading frictions such as transaction costs and a difference between buying and selling prices. The exact idealization must still specify permitted holdings, short selling, and borrowing and lending terms; the worked binomial market allows divisible holdings and unrestricted borrowing at the bank growth factor.
