The definition in this part uses a single Boolean valuation, so the relevant Nash equilibria are pure strategy equilibria of a Boolean game. Use its standard convention that the players' control sets form a set partition of the variables.
Membership follows from an existential choice of a strategy profile and a universal check of all unilateral deviations. Assign payoff one to satisfying a player's goal and zero otherwise. A strategy profile σ is a Nash equilibrium precisely when, for every player i and every alternative Boolean valuation τi​ of that player's variables,
γi​(σ) ∨ ¬γi​(τi​,σ−i​)
(17)
is true: either the goal is already satisfied or no deviation can satisfy it. Thus the requested property is
∃σ ∀(i,τi​)[ϕ(σ)∧(γi​(σ)∨¬γi​(τi​,σ−i​))].
(18)
The strings encoding σ, i and τi​ have polynomial length, and evaluating the bracketed propositional formulas takes polynomial time. Invalid encodings of a player index can simply be ignored by the test. This is the existential second level of the polynomial hierarchy, Σ2P​.
For hardness, reduce the canonical complete problem of deciding a two-block quantified Boolean formula
∃X ∀Y ψ(X,Y).
(19)
Make a two-player Boolean game: player 1 controls X and has goal γ1​=⊤; player 2 controls Y and has goal γ2​=¬ψ. Set the query formula to ϕ=ψ. The construction has size linear in the input formula.
If an assignment x satisfies ∀Yψ(x,Y), combine it with any assignment y. Player 1 already satisfies its goal, and player 2 cannot satisfy ¬ψ by any unilateral deviation. Thus (x,y) is a Nash equilibrium satisfying the query.
Conversely, let (x,y) be a Nash equilibrium satisfying ψ. Player 2 currently has payoff zero. If some alternative y′ made ψ(x,y′) false, that deviation would satisfy player 2's goal and increase its payoff to one. The Nash equilibrium condition therefore implies ∀Yψ(x,Y). This proves the required equivalence and the polynomial-time reduction.
Together, membership and hardness prove Σ2P​-completeness of existential equilibrium checking in a Boolean game. The reduction works with only two players and one constant goal.
