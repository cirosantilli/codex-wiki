No. The denominator test from part ii is satisfied here, so a stronger obstruction is needed. Put ai​=v({i}) and bij​=v({i,j}). In a three-player simple cooperative game, the Shapley value formula gives, for distinct i,j,k,
ϕi​=31​ai​+61​(bij​−aj​)+61​(bik​−ak​)+31​(1−bjk​).
(20)
Subtract the corresponding expression for ϕj​:
ϕi​−ϕj​=21​(ai​−aj​+bik​−bjk​).
(21)
Each coalition value is zero or one, so this difference must be an integer multiple of 1/2. This is the obstruction from pairwise Shapley differences in a three-player simple game. The proposed first two coordinates differ by 1/6, which is not an integer multiple of 1/2. Therefore the vector cannot occur, even though each coordinate separately passes the 1/6 denominator test.
