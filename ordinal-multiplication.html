Ordinal multiplication (αβ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Ordinal
OurBigBook.com Words: 181 Articles: 3
Ordinal multiplication is defined recursively by
α0=0,α(β+1)=αβ+α,αλ=supξ<λ​αξ
(37)
for nonzero limit ordinal λ. It is associative and left-distributive over ordinal addition, but generally neither commutative nor right-distributive.
