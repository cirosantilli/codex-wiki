On a bounded smooth domain, the Sobolev trace theorem can be strengthened to
∥Tu∥L2(∂U)2​≤C(∥u∥L2(U)2​+∥u∥L2(U)​∥Du∥L2(U)​)≤C′∥u∥L2(U)​∥u∥H1(U)​.
(3)
For smooth u, extend the outward unit normal to a smooth vector field X near the closure of U with X⋅ν=1 on its boundary. The divergence theorem gives ∫∂U​u2=∫U​(divX)u2+2uX⋅Du, and the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality gives the estimate. Density extends it to H1(U). A bound containing only the product ∥u∥2​∥Du∥2​ is false for nonzero constant functions. Equivalently, for every ε>0, the Young inequality gives ∥Tu∥22​≤ε∥Du∥22​+Cε​∥u∥22​.
