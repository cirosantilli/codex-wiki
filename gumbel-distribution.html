Gumbel distribution (Λ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Extreme value theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 157 Articles: 3
The standard Gumbel distribution for maxima has distribution function Λ(x)=e−e−x for every real x. The maximum domain of attraction is characterized by an exponential tail ratio on the scale of a Gumbel auxiliary function.
