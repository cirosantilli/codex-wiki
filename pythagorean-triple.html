Pythagorean triple (x2+y2=z2)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Pythagorean theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
A Pythagorean triple consists of three positive integers satisfying x2+y2=z2. It is primitive when their greatest common divisor is one. Reducing their square numbers modulo 3 shows that at least one leg is divisible by 3; reducing modulo 4 shows that both legs cannot be odd.
