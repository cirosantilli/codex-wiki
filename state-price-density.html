State-price density

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Risk-neutral measure Martingale deflator
OurBigBook.com Words: 729 Articles: 12
A state-price density is a positive random variable or process that converts future payoffs into present values by expectation. In a one-period zero-cost return model X, its normalization is commonly Eρ=1 and E[ρX]=0.
