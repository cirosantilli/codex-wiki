Dirac spinor

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 19
A Dirac spinor transforms as (1/2,0)⊕(0,1/2), so it combines the two Weyl chiralities into a parity-invariant representation.
