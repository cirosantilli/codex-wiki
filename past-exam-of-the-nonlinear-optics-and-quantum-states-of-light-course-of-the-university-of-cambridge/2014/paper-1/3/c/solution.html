For X=(a+a†)/2 and Y=(a−a†)/(2i), [X,Y]=i/2, so ΔXΔY≥1/4. Vacuum and coherent states have variance 1/4 in either optical quadrature. quadrature squeezing means that some rotated optical quadrature has variance below this reference, with compensating noise elsewhere. A reduced second moment about zero is not the correct test when the field's mean is displaced.
For S(ζ)=exp[(ζ∗a2−ζa†2)/2] and ζ=reiθ, the quiet principal axis of the optical squeezed vacuum is at angle θ/2 under the squeeze-operator convention used above, and
VarXθ/2​=41​e−2r,VarXθ/2+π/2​=41​e2r.​
(16)
The pure Gaussian quantum state saturates the uncertainty product. Displacement moves its centre without changing its covariance ellipse; squeezed thermal states instead have a larger uncertainty product. The optical squeezed vacuum has zero mean field, even photon-number parity and mean occupation sinh2r; it need not have sub-Poissonian photon statistics. number squeezing and quadrature squeezing are different conditions.
Degenerate parametric amplification generates single-mode squeezing; a two-mode squeezed vacuum instead has correlated occupations and joint quadrature squeezing. Either individual mode of an ideal two-mode state is thermal after the other is ignored, not individually squeezed. balanced homodyne detection measures the noise versus local oscillator phase. Reference phase drift admixes the anti-squeezed optical quadrature, while electronic noise and detector imbalance need separate checks.
For an optical loss channel with efficiency η and vacuum entering the unused port, optical squeezing degradation by loss gives
Vout​=ηVin​+(1−η)/4.​
(17)
The linear-loss limit follows directly by writing the output field as η​a+1−η​v with independent vacuum mode v. Loss therefore places a floor under measured squeezing. Squeezed input can improve phase-sensitive measurements when aligned with the measured optical quadrature; it does not reduce every noise source or both conjugate uncertainties at once.
