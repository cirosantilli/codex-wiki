Negative binomial distribution

On the failures-before-the-rth-success convention, a negative binomial variable with success probability p has probability mass function
P(X=x)=(xx+r−1​)(1−p)xpr,x=0,1,….
(5)
It has expected value r(1−p)/p and variance r(1−p)/p2.
