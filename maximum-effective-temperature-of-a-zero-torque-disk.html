Maximum effective temperature of a zero-torque disk (Tmax​≃0.488Tin​)

 ... Astrophysical disk Accretion disk Alpha disk Shakura--Sunyaev thin disk Standard thin-disk dissipation flux Effective-temperature profile of a zero-torque disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
For the effective-temperature profile of a zero-torque disk, differentiation of y−3(1−y−1/2) with y=r/rin​ gives ymax​=49/36. Thus Tmax​=(6/7)3/27−1/4Tin​≃0.488Tin​. This distinguishes a genuine maximum effective temperature from the normalization used in a power-law approximation.
