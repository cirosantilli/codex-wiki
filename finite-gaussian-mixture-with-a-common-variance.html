Finite Gaussian mixture with a common variance (f(y)=∑j​πj​ϕ(y;μj​,σ2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 242 Articles: 4
A finite Gaussian mixture with a common variance has density f(y)=∑j=1k​πj​ϕ(y;μj​,σ2), with positive common variance and weights summing to one. The means locate its latent components, which need not correspond to distinct modes. It has 2k free parameters: k−1 weights, k means and one variance. EM for Gaussian mixtures with a common variance supplies iterative likelihood updates.
