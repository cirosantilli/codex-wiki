Galilean covariance of Newton's second law (a′=Ra,F′=RF)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Galilean transformation Principle of Galilean relativity Galilean invariance
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For a Galilean transformation t′=t+b, x′=Rx−Ut+a, with constant rotation R and boost U, velocity transforms as v′=Rv−U and acceleration as a′=Ra. With invariant inertial mass, Newton's second law retains its form only if the actual interaction laws and their physical background data transform to give F′=RF. Acceleration invariance alone does not prove covariance of an arbitrary force law. For example, resistance depends on velocity relative to a medium, whose velocity must transform too.
