Recovering an analytic derivative from the boundary real part (f′(0)=(πr)−1∫02π​Ref(reiθ)e−iθdθ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Cauchy derivative formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
For a holomorphic function on a neighbourhood of a closed disc, parametrize the Cauchy derivative formula on its boundary. The conjugate Cauchy integral theorem gives ∫f(reiθ)​e−iθdθ=0. Replacing f in the derivative integral by f+fˉ​=2Ref therefore gives the displayed identity. The boundary real part determines every nonconstant analytic coefficient; only an imaginary additive constant is lost.
