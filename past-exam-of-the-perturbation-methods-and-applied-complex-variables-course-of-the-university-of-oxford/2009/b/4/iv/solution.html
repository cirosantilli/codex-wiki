(39)
Because cosscots=cscs−sins and sinscots=coss, these integrals are
x(θ)=−π2a​[log∣tan(θ/2)∣+cosθ],y(θ)=1−π2a​(1+sinθ).
(40)
As θ→0−, x→+∞ and y→1−2a/π. This must equal the outgoing stream depth a, so
a=π+2π​.​
(41)
The solution has a vertical tangent at D and approaches the horizontal line y=a downstream, agreeing with both solid-wall and free-streamline conditions.
