There are two distinct inconsistencies in the printed formulas. First the initial Cauchy integral has coefficient 1/(2π), whereas the stated boundary limits require 1/(2πi). Second the final integral is a solution of a difference jump problem, although the intervening equation for W is a sum jump problem. We derive the valid identities, show precisely where that final deduction fails, and give the corrected construction.
