The local fast modes are exponentials with rates ±1/ϵ​. A mode decaying into the interval can occur at either endpoint: e−x/ϵ​ at zero and e−(1−x)/ϵ​ at one. The zero fast exponent represents the matching outer component. The term xy is lower order on either fast scale, and even smaller near zero; it creates no further independent layer thickness.
