Newell–Whitehead–Segel equation (AT​=μA+(∂X​+i∂Y2​/2)2A−∣A∣2A)

The Newell–Whitehead–Segel equation is an amplitude equation for slowly modulated stationary rolls in an isotropic pattern-forming medium. With the displayed sign convention, a roll A=ReiqX has R2=μ−q2. A transverse phase modulation has linear growth rate qk2−k4/4, so long-wave transverse instability occurs for q>0. Reversing the carrier-wave convention reverses the sign attached to its detuning q; one must derive the stability sign from the operator actually used.
