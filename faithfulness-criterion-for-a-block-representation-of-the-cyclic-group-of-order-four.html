Faithfulness criterion for a block representation of the cyclic group of order four (T(a)=diag(1,(0c​b0​)))

 ... Algebra Group theory Group Cyclic group Finite cyclic group Cyclic group of order four
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
For real b,c, this matrix defines a group representation of C4​=⟨a:a4=e⟩ exactly when bc=±1. Indeed its lower block B satisfies B2=bcI2​, so T(a)4=I3​ exactly when (bc)2=1. If bc=−1, T(a)2=diag(1,−1,−1)=I3​, so its order is four and the representation is faithful. If bc=1, its order is exactly two and the kernel of a group homomorphism is {e,a2}, giving an unfaithful representation. Other values do not give representations at all, including bc=0, when the proposed matrix is singular.
