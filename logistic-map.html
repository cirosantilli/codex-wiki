Logistic map

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Discrete dynamical system Interval map
OurBigBook.com Words: 74 Articles: 1
The logistic map is the one-parameter family Fμ​(x)=μx(1−x) on the unit interval. As μ increases it exhibits a period-doubling cascade, periodic windows and chaotic parameter ranges.
