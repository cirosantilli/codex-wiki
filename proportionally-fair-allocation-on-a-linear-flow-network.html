On unit-capacity links 1,…,I, route zero uses every link and route i uses only link i. Let N=∑r=0I​nr​ and M=∑i=1I​ni​. Under proportional fairness, every active local route has n0​x0​+ni​xi​=1. Maximizing n0​logw0​+Mlog(1−w0​) for w0​=n0​x0​ gives w0​=n0​/N and the displayed per-flow rates on active routes. At the empty state departures vanish, and rates of absent flows may be set to zero.
