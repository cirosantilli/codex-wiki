Mendelian randomization

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Causal inference Instrumental variable
OurBigBook.com Words: 80 Articles: 2
Mendelian randomization uses inherited genetic variants associated with an exposure as instrumental variables. Its causal interpretation requires instrument relevance, instrumental-variable independence, and the exclusion restriction.
