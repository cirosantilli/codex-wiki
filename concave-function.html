Concave function

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 492 Articles: 9
A function f is concave exactly when −f is convex. Equivalently,
f(tx+(1−t)y)≥tf(x)+(1−t)f(y)
(54)
for 0≤t≤1.
