Bjorken scaling

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Parton Parton model
OurBigBook.com Words: 16
At leading order in the naive parton model, dimensionless deep-inelastic structure functions depend only on Bjorken x. Quantum chromodynamics evolution introduces scale dependence.
