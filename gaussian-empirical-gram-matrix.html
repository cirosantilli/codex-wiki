Gaussian empirical Gram matrix (Σ=GTG/n)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Inner product Gram matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
For a matrix G with independent standard normal entries, GTG/n is a Gaussian empirical Gram matrix and has expectation equal to the identity. With mean known to be zero it is the raw empirical second moment. Subtracting a sample mean would give a different estimator. On any fixed unit vector its quadratic form is a chi-squared variable divided by n.
