This continuous-time multi-state model permits progression 1→2, direct death 1→3, and death after progression 2→3, with no recovery and death an absorbing state. For constant positive rates a,b,c, write λ=a+b. Integrating over the time of the progression arrow gives
p12​(t)=∫0t​e−λuae−c(t−u)du=λ−ca(e−ct−e−λt)​.
(5)
Use ate−ct when λ=c. The other transition probabilities are p11​=e−λt, p13​=1−p11​−p12​, p22​=e−ct, p23​=1−e−ct and p33​=1. Unlike a purely sequential irreversible three-state disease model, the direct-death rate contributes to the first-state exit rate. A mixed panel and exact-death likelihood accounts for panel visits and exact death times with different kinds of factors.
