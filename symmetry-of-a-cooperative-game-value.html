Symmetry of a cooperative-game value

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Cooperative game theory Transferable utility game Symmetry of players in a cooperative game
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
A value has cooperative-game symmetry when it assigns equal payoffs to players satisfying symmetry of players in a cooperative game. Thus equality of their marginal contributions is reflected in the allocation, rather than merely being a property of the underlying game.
