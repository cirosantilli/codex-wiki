Height of an ideal (ht(I))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Krull dimension
OurBigBook.com Words: 612 Articles: 9
Height measures prime-chain length within Krull dimension theory.
The height of an ideal I is the infimum of the heights of prime ideals containing it:
ht(I)=infp⊇I​ht(p).
(13)
The height of a prime is the supremum of the lengths of strict prime chains ending at that prime.
