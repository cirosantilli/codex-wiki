Sphere rotations as special-unitary Möbius transformations

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Riemann sphere Stereographic projection
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
Under the stereographic projection w=(x+iy)/(1−z), rotations about the third axis have representatives diag(eiθ/2,e−iθ/2), and rotations about the second axis have representatives
(cos(θ/2)sin(θ/2)​−sin(θ/2)cos(θ/2)​).
(63)
These belong to the special unitary group SU(2). Euler-angle generation of the special orthogonal group SO(3) shows that every sphere rotation becomes a Möbius transformation represented in SU(2). Representatives U and −U induce the same transformation.
