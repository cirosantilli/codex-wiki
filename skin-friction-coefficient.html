Skin friction coefficient (cf​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Boundary layer Wall shear stress
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
The local skin friction coefficient normalizes wall shear stress by the outer dynamic pressure:
cf​(x)=21​ρU2τw​(x)​≃Rex​​0.664115​.
(19)
For the ideal Blasius boundary layer on one face of length L, averaging the local skin friction coefficient gives cf​≃1.328230/ReL​​. The factor of two follows from integrating x−1/2, not from counting both plate faces. Drag on two equally exposed faces doubles when the reference area remains that of one face.
