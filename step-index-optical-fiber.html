Step-index optical fiber

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Optical fiber
OurBigBook.com Words: 507 Articles: 3
A step-index optical fiber has a nearly uniform refractive index n1​ throughout its optical fiber core, with an abrupt decrease to n2​<n1​ in its optical fiber cladding. In a straight, ideal guide, meridional rays stay in a plane containing the optical axis and trace a zigzag through repeated total internal reflection. This is a geometrical optics approximation; it is particularly useful for a multimode optical fiber whose core diameter is large compared with the wavelength.
The upper panel shows an accepted meridional ray; the lower panel enlarges the limiting geometry. The angle of incidence i at the side wall is measured from its normal vector, whereas the internal ray angle ϕ and external launch angle θ are measured from the optical axis. Thus i+ϕ=90∘. The limiting ray is a boundary of the ideal model, rather than a complete normal mode solution.
