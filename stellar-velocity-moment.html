Stellar velocity moment (A=ν−1∫A(v)fd3v)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics Galactic distribution function
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For a galactic distribution function f(x,v) with tracer number density ν=∫fd3v, a velocity moment is the weighted integral of a velocity monomial. The first moment gives the mean velocity vi​; the second moment satisfies vi​vj​​=vi​vj​+σij2​, separating ordered motion from the random velocity dispersion tensor. Multiplying the Collisionless Boltzmann equation by vi​ and integrating yields the Jeans equation, with vanishing velocity-space boundary terms when the distribution decays sufficiently rapidly.
