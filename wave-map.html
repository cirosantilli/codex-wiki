Wave map (□ϕ=ϕ(∣ϕt​∣2−∣∇ϕ∣2))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Semilinear wave equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 331 Articles: 6
A wave map is a harmonic map with Lorentzian domain: a critical point of the action obtained by contracting the pullback target metric with the domain Lorentzian metric. For target sphere S2 in Euclidean space and □=−∂t2​+Δ, its extrinsic equation is □ϕ=ϕ(∣ϕt​∣2−∣∇ϕ∣2), subject to ∣ϕ∣=1. The derivative contractions are null forms for wave equations. Wave map Cauchy data must include a tangent initial velocity.
