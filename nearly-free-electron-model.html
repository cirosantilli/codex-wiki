Nearly-free electron model

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bloch's theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 509 Articles: 8
The nearly-free electron model treats a weak periodic potential by degenerate perturbation theory. At a Bragg crossing, a Fourier coefficient Vn​ couples the two degenerate plane waves and opens an energy gap of width 2∣Vn​∣.
