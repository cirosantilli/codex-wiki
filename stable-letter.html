Stable letter (t)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Bass-Serre theory HNN extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
A stable letter is the new generator of an HNN extension which implements an isomorphism between two specified subgroups by conjugation. In the convention tat−1=ϕ(a), it moves elements of the source subgroup A to the target subgroup B; using t−1at=ϕ(a) reverses the naming convention.
