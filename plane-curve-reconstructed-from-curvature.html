Plane curve reconstructed from curvature

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Curvature and torsion Curvature of a space curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
Given a smooth function κ(s), set
θ(s)=∫0s​κ(u)du,γ(s)=∫0s​(cosθ(u),sinθ(u))du.
(80)
Then γ is unit speed and has signed curvature κ. This gives the existence part of the fundamental theorem of plane curves.
