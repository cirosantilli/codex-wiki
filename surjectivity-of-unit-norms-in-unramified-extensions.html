Surjectivity of unit norms in unramified extensions (NL/K​(OL×​)=OK×​)

For a finite unramified extension of non-Archimedean local fields, the field norm on residue units is surjective: on a cyclic group of order qn−1 it is exponentiation by (qn−1)/(q−1) and has image of order q−1. For a common uniformizer π and r≥1, expansion of the conjugate product gives
NL/K​(1+πra)≡1+πrTrkL​/kK​​(aˉ)(modπr+1).
(47)
The field trace of a finite finite field extension is onto: its polynomial X+Xq+⋯+Xqn−1 is nonzero of degree below qn, so defines a nonzero linear map to the base field. Lift a residue norm and correct successively with elements of 1+πrOL​. The infinite product converges by completeness, and its norm is the prescribed unit. This argument also works in positive characteristic and when p divides n.
