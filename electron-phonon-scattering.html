Electron-phonon scattering

 ... Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Bose-Einstein statistics Bose-Einstein distribution Phonon Electron-phonon interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 266 Articles: 2
In electron-phonon scattering, an electron changes its energy by ±ℏωq​ while its crystal momentum changes by a phonon wavevector modulo a reciprocal lattice vector. Charge-transport relaxation weights a scattering angle θ by 1−cosθ. Small-angle events can therefore relax heat efficiently while relaxing electrical current weakly. At temperatures above the phonon excitation scale, scattering becomes approximately elastic in the electron thermal shell; residual elastic disorder often dominates at very low temperature.
