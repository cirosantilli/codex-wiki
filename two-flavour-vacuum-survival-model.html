Two-flavour vacuum survival model (Pμμ​)

For a deliberately simplified neutrino oscillation calculation, take two neutrino flavors related to two mass eigenstates by a real rotation matrix with angle θ. A quantum superposition acquires different phases during propagation; projecting back onto the initial neutrino flavor gives the probability
Pμμ​=1−sin2(2θ)sin2(1.27eV2Δm2​kmL​EGeV​),Δm2=m22​−m12​.
(10)
Here L is path length and E is neutrino energy; the numerical coefficient incorporates unit conversion.
The paper states the corresponding conversion formula.[ref]
The path dependence has a geometrical origin. In an original spherical-Earth model, let R be the radius and h a fixed production height, neglecting detector depth. Let Θ be the zenith distance of the direction in the sky from which the neutrino arrives, so c=cosΘ=+1 means downward propagation. The backward ray from detector to production obeys R2+L2+2RLc=(R+h)2. Its positive solution is
L(c)=−Rc+(R+h)2−R2(1−c2)​.
(11)
Thus L(+1)=h and L(−1)=2R+h. The calculation explains why opposite directions test very different baselines, rather than attributing every observed direction to the same travel distance. Actual production heights and the angle between the outgoing charged lepton and incoming neutrino smear that relationship.
For an original chosen example, set θ=π/4 and Δm2=2.5×10−3eV2. At E=1GeV, L=20km gives Pμμ​≃0.9960, while L=500km gives Pμμ​≃0.000279. The first minimum is at L/E≃494.7km/GeV. These are stipulated inputs, not a fit to the 1998 data. A broad distribution of true L/E, together with energy and directional uncertainty, smooths the sharp minima.
A direct amplitude check makes the mechanism explicit. Define
U=(cosθ−sinθ​sinθcosθ​),S=U(10​0e−2iφ​)UT,φ=1.27eV2Δm2​kmL​EGeV​.
(12)
Then S11​=cos2θ+sin2θe−2iφ. Taking its squared absolute value yields the displayed survival formula because 1−cos(2φ)=2sin2φ. Unitarity gives ∣S11​∣2+∣S21​∣2=1: disappearance from one channel becomes conversion to the other within this closed model.
This illustration does not describe all solar neutrino propagation. Coherent forward scattering in matter changes the effective propagation operator, and three active neutrino flavors require a larger mixing description. Even in the simplified model, neutrino oscillation measures a squared-mass difference; it does not determine the absolute neutrino mass.
