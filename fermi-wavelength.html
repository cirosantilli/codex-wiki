Fermi wavelength (λF​=2π/kF​)

 ... Quantum ideal-gas statistics Fermi-Dirac distribution Fermi gas Fermi energy Fermi momentum Fermi wavevector
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The wavelength associated with a specified Fermi wavevector. A local-density response normally requires variation on a scale large compared with kF−1​; numerical factors such as 2π must be kept explicit when comparing a variation scale with the full wavelength.
