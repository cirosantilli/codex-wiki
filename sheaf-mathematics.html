Sheaf (mathematics)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Presheaf of sets on a topological space
OurBigBook.com Words: 820 Articles: 16
A sheaf of sets is a presheaf in which compatible sections on any open cover glue to one unique section on the union.
A sheaf may additionally carry abelian group or module structure on its sections, with restriction and gluing preserving that structure. The underlying sheaf of sets retains the same local-to-global axiom.
