Capped claim moments (Emin(X,M)r=r∫0M​xr−1P(X>x)dx)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Reinsurance Excess of loss reinsurance
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For a positive claim and r>0, the tail integral formula for moments gives the displayed expression. In particular the first moment is ∫0M​F(x)dx and the raw second moment is 2∫0M​xF(x)dx. If the claim law has a density, capping adds an atom of a measure at M with mass P(X≥M).
