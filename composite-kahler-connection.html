Composite Kähler connection

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supergravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The scalar fields of chiral matter induce a composite phase connection proportional to i(Ki​∂μ​zi−Kiˉ​∂μ​zˉiˉ). For canonical Kähler potential it is proportional to the scalar phase current. The chiral matter fermions and gravitino have appropriate Kähler weights; expanding their connection terms produces scalar-current–fermion-bilinear couplings. Overall signs and coefficients depend on spinor and Planck-scale conventions.
