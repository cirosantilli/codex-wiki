Batchelor entrainment hypothesis

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulent plume
OurBigBook.com Words: 67 Articles: 1
The Batchelor entrainment hypothesis takes the inward mean edge velocity of a turbulent plume to be ve​=αw in the Boussinesq approximation. Its density-corrected non-Boussinesq form is ve​=αwρ/ρ0​​, where ρ and ρ0​ are plume and ambient mass densities and α is the entrainment coefficient.
