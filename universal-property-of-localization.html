Universal property of localization

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Localization of a ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 89 Articles: 1
The localization map ι:R→S−1R sends every element of S to a unit. If a ring homomorphism f:R→T also sends every element of S to a unit, there is a unique homomorphism f​:S−1R→T with f​(r/s)=f(r)f(s)−1 and f​ι=f.
