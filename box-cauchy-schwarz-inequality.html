Box Cauchy-Schwarz inequality

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Box norm
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For four real-valued functions on a finite Cartesian product, let Λ(f00​,f01​,f10​,f11​)=Ex0​,x1​,y0​,y1​​∏i,j=01​fij​(xi​,yj​). Repeated Cauchy-Schwarz inequalities give
∣Λ(f00​,f01​,f10​,f11​)∣≤∏i,j=01​∥fij​∥□​.
(8)
First separate the two y averages and apply Cauchy-Schwarz inequality in (x0​,x1​). Each resulting squared factor is Ey,y′​(Ex​f(x,y)f(x,y′))(Ex​g(x,y)g(x,y′)), bounded by ∥f∥□2​∥g∥□2​ by another Cauchy-Schwarz inequality. Expanding the four factors of f+g and applying this inequality to each of the sixteen terms gives the triangle inequality for the box norm.
