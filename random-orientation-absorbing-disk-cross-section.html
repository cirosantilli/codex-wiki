Random-orientation absorbing-disk cross-section (⟨σabs​⟩=πR2/2)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Hydrogen atom Lyman-alpha line Lyman-alpha absorption
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
A circular, opaque, geometrically thin disk has projected area πR2∣cosi∣. For isotropically oriented normals, ∣cosi∣ is uniform on [0,1], so the mean interception area is half the face-on area. A nonunit covering fraction multiplies this cross-section. Finite thickness, orientation-dependent absorption thresholds and incomplete coverage can change it; this is an average over randomly placed disks, not the inclination distribution conditional on an observed interception.
