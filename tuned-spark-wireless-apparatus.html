Tuned spark wireless apparatus

OurBigBook.com Words: 269 Articles: 1
Guglielmo Marconi's 1909 lecture describes antennas with earth connections and separate coupled receiving circuits. His 1900 system coupled a closed capacitor–inductor–spark gap loop to a transmitting antenna. Coil spacing adjusted coupling; inductance and capacitance adjusted tuning. All four circuits were tuned together. By 1909 his company's stations mainly used magnetic receivers, rather than coherers.[ref]
Those are a participant's retrospective descriptions, not a complete account of priority. An earth connection is part of this antenna arrangement; it is not a universal requirement for radio communication. Nor does tuning an isolated LC circuit ensure that its frequency remains unchanged when strongly coupled to another circuit. The coupled resonant circuit model quantifies that distinction.
