Exact null distribution of a rank sum (Pr(WX​=w)=(mm+n​)−1[zmtw]∏k=1m+n​(1+ztk))

Under the continuous common-distribution null of the Wilcoxon rank sum test, pooled labels are exchangeable. The product records every choice of ranks: choosing ztk assigns rank k to the first sample. Its indicated coefficient counts subsets of size m and rank sum w. Divide by (mm+n​) and sum the lower tail for the one-sided P-value. Equivalently the counts satisfy ak,h,w​=ak−1,h,w​+ak−1,h−1,w−k​ with a0,0,0​=1, allowing an exact integer computation.
