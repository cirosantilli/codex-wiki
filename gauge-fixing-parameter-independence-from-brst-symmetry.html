Gauge-fixing parameter independence from BRST symmetry (∂ξ​L=s(−baha/2))

 ... Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing BRST symmetry BRST charge BRST Ward identity
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 1
For auxiliary-field covariant gauge fixing, varying the parameter inserts a BRST-exact operator. Its correlations with separated physical BRST-closed operators vanish by the BRST Ward identity. Normalized physical correlators are therefore independent of the parameter under the invariant-measure and boundary assumptions.
