Thermal convection

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Convection
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 25
Thermal convection is fluid motion driven by temperature-dependent buoyancy. It transports heat by bulk motion in addition to thermal conduction.
