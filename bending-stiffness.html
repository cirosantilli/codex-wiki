Bending stiffness (B)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Elastic plate
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
Bending stiffness is the coefficient relating plate curvature to bending moment. In linear thin-plate theory a transverse displacement ζ produces a restoring load B∇4ζ.
