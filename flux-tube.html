Flux tube

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic field Magnetic field line
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
A flux tube is a bundle of magnetic field lines bounded by a surface everywhere tangent to the magnetic field. Gauss's law for magnetism makes the magnetic flux through any transverse cross-section constant along the tube. For a narrow tube with nearly uniform field magnitude B and transverse area A, this gives BA=constant.
