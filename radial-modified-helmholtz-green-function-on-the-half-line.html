Radial modified Helmholtz Green function on the half-line (G(x,ξ)=xξ​sinh(min(x,ξ))e−max(x,ξ))

For Ly=−x−2(x2y′)′+y on x>0, require boundedness at zero and at infinity. The substitution z=xy reduces the homogeneous equation to −z′′+z=0. The adapted solutions are sinhx/x at zero and e−x/x at infinity, with Wronskian −x−2. The displayed Green function is continuous and has first-derivative jump −1, so Lx​G=δ(x−ξ) with ordinary source measure dξ. It satisfies weighted reciprocity x2G(x,ξ)=ξ2G(ξ,x). For forcing equal to one on [0,R] and zero outside, integration gives y=1−(R+1)e−Rsinhx/x inside and y=(RcoshR−sinhR)e−x/x outside. Limits define the regular value at zero.
