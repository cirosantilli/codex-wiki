Ampere (A=C/s)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
The ampere is the SI unit of electric current. Since 20 May 2019 its definition fixes the elementary charge exactly at e=1.602176634×10−19C, with C=As. This gives electrical measurements a reference based on a fixed elementary charge. [ref]; [ref].
