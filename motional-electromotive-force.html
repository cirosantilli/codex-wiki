Motional electromotive force (Emot​=∮C​(v×B)⋅dl)

OurBigBook.com Words: 166 Articles: 2
Motional electromotive force is the contribution caused by a conductor moving through a magnetic field. The magnetic part of the Lorentz force separates charges within the conductor. For a bar of length L moving at velocity v perpendicular to a uniform field, its magnitude is BL∣v∣; the sign follows the chosen circuit orientation. Together with the electric-field contribution it enters Faraday's law for moving circuits.
