The comoving curvature perturbation is dimensionless. With spatial coordinates and time carrying inverse mass dimensions, the cubic interaction ∫dtd3xa3ϵ2ζζ˙​2 needs a mass-squared coefficient. Its standard gravitational normalization is MPl2​. Six De Sitter curvature mode functions supply MPl−6​; this vertex then supplies MPl2​, leaving the physical MPl−4​ curvature primordial bispectrum. The Wick-pairing multiplicity for a zeta zeta-prime-squared vertex is independently six: three choices of undifferentiated external leg times two differentiated-leg assignments.
