Dirichlet approximation theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Diophantine approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
For every irrational number α and positive integer N, there are integers p,q with 1≤q≤N such that
​α−qp​​≤qN1​.
(121)
The proof applies the pigeonhole principle to the N+1 fractional parts of 0,α,…,Nα.
