Isometry group (Isom(M,h))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Isometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 363 Articles: 5
The isometry group of a metric space consists of its bijective isometries, with composition as the group operation. For a Riemannian metric, these are the diffeomorphisms preserving the metric tensor. Right translations of a right-invariant Riemannian metric supply a faithful subgroup isomorphic to the underlying Lie group.
