Merton consumption constant (γM​=[ρ−(1−R)(r+κ2/(2R))]/R)

OurBigBook.com Words: 50
In the infinite-horizon Merton consumption-investment problem with constant relative risk aversion utility, this is the optimal ratio of consumption to wealth. A finite value requires γM​>0. The state-price budget constraint then fixes consumption's multiplier because E[ξt​(yeρtξt​)−1/R]=y−1/Re−γM​t, whose time integral is y−1/R/γM​. When this integral diverges, the value is +∞ for 0<R<1 and −∞ for R>1.
