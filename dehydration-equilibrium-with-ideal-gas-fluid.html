Dehydration equilibrium with ideal-gas fluid (Δr​G=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Chemical potential Chemical equilibrium
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
For a mineral reaction producing one mole of water, let H∘,S∘ refer to the P∘ standard state and let the solids’ volume change ΔVs​ be constant. If As​ is the product/reactant solid-activity ratio, the Gibbs energy of reaction is
Δr​G=H∘−TS∘+ΔVs​(P−P∘)+RTln(As​P∘fH2​O​​).
(18)
For temperature-independent specified activities and fugacity, equilibrium gives
T=S∘−Rln(As​fH2​O​/P∘)H∘+ΔVs​(P−P∘)​.
(19)
The gas pressure work is already represented by the fugacity term; adding a second constant water-volume term double counts it. For an ideal gas, f=P; a given fugacity coefficient gives f=ϕf​P. If these quantities depend on temperature, the equilibrium equation must instead be solved implicitly. Pressure units in the solid volume correction must match those used for ΔVs​.
