Tensor product of crystals

OurBigBook.com Words: 248 Articles: 2
The tensor product crystal uses the i-string data εi​ and φi​ to decide on which tensor factor a Kashiwara operator acts. Its connected components are highest-weight crystals and encode the decomposition of the tensor product representation.
This tensor-product operation combines one crystal basis with another, with factor selection governed by their string parameters.
