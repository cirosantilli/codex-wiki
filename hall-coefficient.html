Hall coefficient (RH​=Ey​/(Jx​Bz​))

 ... Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force Hall effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 323 Articles: 5
The Hall coefficient relates a transverse electric field to longitudinal current density and transverse magnetic field, with oriented axes as in the displayed formula. Its SI unit is m3/C. For one isotropic Drude model species it is 1/(nq); multiple charge carriers need not follow that formula.
