Observations on a certain theorem of Fermat and on others concerning prime numbers by Leonhard Euler (1738, Eneström E26)

OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by Jordan Bell, Work by Leonhard Euler Words: 109
By Leonhard Euler; English translation by Jordan Bell. Presented to the St Petersburg Academy on 26 September 1732; the original Latin paper, "Observationes de theoremate quodam Fermatiano aliisque ad numeros primos spectantibus", appeared in Commentarii academiae scientiarum Petropolitanae 6 (1738), pp. 103–107. The publication note on page one of the translation distinguishes presentation from publication. The linked translation is version three, dated 15 April 2008.
English translation: arxiv.org/pdf/math/0501118
Catalogue record: arxiv.org/abs/math/0501118
Cited passages: pp. 1–2, Euler's account of Fermat's proposed prime pattern and the factor 641 of 232+1; p. 1 publication and translator note. The short modular check in Fermat number is presented as a modern verification, not a historical reconstruction of Euler's search.
