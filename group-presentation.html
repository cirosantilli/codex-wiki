Group presentation (⟨S∣R⟩)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 42
A group presentation ⟨S∣R⟩ describes the quotient of the free group F(S) by the normal closure of the relators R. It is finite when both S and R are finite.
