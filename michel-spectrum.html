Michel spectrum (f(x)=2x2(3−2x))

OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For an unpolarized parent charged lepton undergoing a three-body leptonic V-A interaction decay, neglecting daughter masses and radiative corrections, the normalized charged-daughter energy distribution in the parent rest frame is f(x)=2x2(3−2x), where x=2Eℓ∗​/mparent​ lies in [0,1]. Direct integration gives ∫01​f(x)dx=1 and ∫01​xf(x)dx=7/10. A specified parent spin orientation also produces angular terms, so this one-variable law alone does not describe polarized samples.
