Helium metastability

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Helium Helium atom
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
The low 1s2s levels of a helium atom lack an allowed direct electric dipole decay to the ground 1s21S0​ level. The triplet is excluded by the spin selection rule, and the singlet-to-singlet S transition is excluded by parity and orbital selection. Weaker radiative channels and collisions can still de-excite these states; metastable does not mean infinitely lived.
