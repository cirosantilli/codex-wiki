Area-angle tradeoff (n12​A1​sin2θ1​=n22​A2​sin2θ2​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Geometrical optics Etendue
OurBigBook.com Words: 176
When both endpoint bundles have the filled circular-cone geometry of etendue of a circular aperture and cone, conservation of etendue gives
n12​A1​sin2θ1​=n22​A2​sin2θ2​.
(11)
For the same refractive index, quadrupling the area halves the sine of the cone half-angle. In the 10mm, 30∘ example, an ideal output of diameter 20mm must have sinθ2​=1/4, so θ2​≈14.4775∘. The exact result differs from simply halving 30∘. Conversely, reducing the diameter to 5mm requires a complete forward hemisphere, θ2​=90∘, as a limiting case.
Equal bounding area and solid angle do not alone prove equal etendue: an actual output can have unoccupied directions or position-direction correlations. The filled-cone equation is an exact model of the specified bundles and a capacity constraint on an optical instrument, not a guarantee of an aberration-free implementation.
