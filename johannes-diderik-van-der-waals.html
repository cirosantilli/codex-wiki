Johannes Diderik van der Waals (1837–1923)

 Home Physics Physicist List of physicists
OurBigBook.com Words: 45 Articles: 1
A physicist.[ref]
The Van der Waals equation connects a dilute gas model to the possibility of a liquid-gas critical point. Its usefulness includes showing how attractions and excluded volume can change an equation of state; its simple constants do not make it an exact model for every fluid.
