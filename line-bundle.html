Line bundle

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Locally free sheaf
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 38
A line bundle on a scheme is a locally free sheaf of rank one. Its global sections can define a morphism to projective space when they have no common zero.
