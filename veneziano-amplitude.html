Veneziano amplitude (A(s,t)=Γ(−2−α′(s+t))Γ(−1−α′s)Γ(−1−α′t)​)

The ordered tree-level four-tachyon open string amplitude is proportional to Γ(−1−α′s)Γ(−1−α′t)/Γ(−2−α′(s+t)). Its Mandelstam variables locate the mass levels, and pole residues reveal spin content.
