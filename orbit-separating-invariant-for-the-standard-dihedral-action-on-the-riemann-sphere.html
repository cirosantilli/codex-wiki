Orbit-separating invariant for the standard dihedral action on the Riemann sphere

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Riemann sphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
For r(z)=ζz and s(z)=1/z, where ζn=1, the rational function
zn+z−n
(96)
is invariant under D2n​. Equality of two values factors as (u−v)(uv−1)=0 for u=z1n​ and v=z2n​, so every fibre is exactly one dihedral orbit.
