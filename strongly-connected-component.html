Strongly connected component

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Directed graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 436 Articles: 6
A strongly connected component is a maximal set of vertices in a directed graph such that each can reach every other by a directed path.
