For the left shift, σp(s)=s precisely when the binary sequence is obtained by repeating its first p symbols. There are exactly 2p such sequences, although some have smaller least period. Hence the set of all periodic sequences is a countable union of finite sets and is at most countable. Passing from periodic points to periodic orbits cannot increase this cardinality.
To prove infinitude, for every integer p≥1 take the repeated word (0p−11)∞. Its least period is p, because its ones are separated by exactly p positions; a smaller positive period would carry a one to a zero. Distinct least periods give distinct periodic orbits. Thus there are countably infinitely many periodic orbits.
