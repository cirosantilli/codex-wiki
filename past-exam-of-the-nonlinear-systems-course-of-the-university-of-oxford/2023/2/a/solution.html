The fixed point equations give x=μy and
0=−μx+μy+y3=y(y2+μ−μ2).
(20)
Thus the origin exists for every real μ. The nonzero fixed points are
(x,y)=(±μμ(μ−1)​, ±μ(μ−1)​),μ<0 or μ>1,​
(21)
with the same sign in both positions. At μ=0 or μ=1 these expressions coalesce with the origin and do not give additional fixed points. There are no nonzero real fixed points for 0<μ<1.
