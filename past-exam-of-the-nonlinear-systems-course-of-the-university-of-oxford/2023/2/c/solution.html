Adjoin μn+1​=μn​. At the origin the stable direction is the x axis, with Floquet multiplier zero, while the y and parameter directions have Floquet multiplier one. By the centre manifold theorem for a discrete dynamical system, write the extended centre manifold as
x=h(y,μ),h(0,0)=0,Dh(0,0)=0.
(26)
On it the scalar map is Y=(1+μ)y−μh(y,μ)+y3. Its discrete centre-manifold invariance equation is
h(Y,μ)=μy.
(27)
Put h=h2​+O(ρ3) with ρ=∣y∣+∣μ∣. Since Y=y+O(ρ2), the quadratic equation is simply h2​(y,μ)=μy. Thus
x=μy+O(ρ3).​
(28)
In particular, setting the stable variable identically to zero would lose the leading parameter correction. The reduced map through total degree three is
yn+1​=yn​+μyn​−μ2yn​+yn3​+O(ρ4).​
(29)
In the natural pitchfork bifurcation scaling μ=O(y2) this has leading form yn+1​=yn​+μyn​+yn3​. Its nonzero fixed points satisfy y2∼−μ, and their reduced fixed-point multipliers satisfy 1−2μ+O(μ2)>1 for small μ<0. The transverse Floquet multiplier remains inside the unit circle. Therefore the bifurcation at μ=0 is a subcritical pitchfork bifurcation: the origin attracts for small negative μ, each nonzero branch is a saddle fixed point of a map, and the origin is a saddle fixed point of a map for small positive μ.
An exact check uses the full Jacobian matrix on either nonzero branch. Its trace is T∗​=1−2μ+3μ2 and its determinant is D∗​=μ2. Thus
p∗​(1)=1−T∗​+D∗​=2μ(1−μ)<0
(30)
whenever a nonzero branch exists. Also p∗​(0)=μ2>0, so one real Floquet multiplier lies in (0,1) and the other in (1,∞). This proves that each nonzero branch is a saddle fixed point of a map throughout μ<0 and μ>1, in particular near the requested bifurcation.
