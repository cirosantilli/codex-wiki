Eddy viscosity (νT​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Reynolds stress
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
An eddy viscosity parametrizes turbulent momentum transport by u′v′=−νT​Uy​. The mixing length model gives νT​=l2∣Uy​∣≥0. This coefficient depends on the mean flow and position; it is distinct from molecular kinematic viscosity. It describes momentum transport rather than molecular energy dissipation.
