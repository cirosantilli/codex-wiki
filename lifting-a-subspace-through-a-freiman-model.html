Lifting a subspace through a Freiman model (W⊆4L(B)⟹W⊆4B)

 ... Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Doubling constant Freiman-Ruzsa theorem Freiman-Ruzsa theorem over a finite field Injective linear modelling of a small sumset
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
Under injective linear modelling of a small sumset, every w∈W has a unique lift σ(w)∈4B, because differences of two lifts lie in 8B⊆12B. For w1​,w2​∈W, the discrepancy σ(w1​)+σ(w2​)+σ(w1​+w2​) lies in 12B∩kerL, so it is zero. Thus σ is a linear map and its image is a vector subspace of the same size as W. This verifies the additive lifting step rather than assuming that a preimage chosen from a sumset is automatically a subspace.
