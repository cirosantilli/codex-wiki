Radiation length (X0​)

 Home Physics Particle detection
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
The characteristic thickness over which a high-energy electron loses all but a fraction 1/e of its energy through bremsstrahlung. At fixed thickness x, smaller X0​ increases the radiative loss; the relevant dimensionless measure is x/X0​. Low-energy charged particles can instead be dominated by collisional stopping power.[ref]
