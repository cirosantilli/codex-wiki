Comoving coordinate

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Scale factor (cosmology)
OurBigBook.com Words: 325 Articles: 8
A comoving coordinate remains fixed for an observer following the homogeneous cosmological expansion.
A comoving coordinate provides labels for the comoving and proper distances comparison; proper separation additionally contains the cosmological scale factor.
