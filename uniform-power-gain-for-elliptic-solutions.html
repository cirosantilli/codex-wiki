For a nonnegative H1 weak solution of a homogeneous divergence-form elliptic equation on B1​⊂Rℓ, ℓ≥2, with symmetric measurable λI≤A≤ΛI, set R=Λ/λ and α=1+1/ℓ. There is Dℓ​≥1 independent of q≥2 such that
∥u∥Lqα(Br1​​)​≤(r2​−r1​Dℓ​R​)2/q∥u∥Lq(Br2​​)​,0<r1​<r2​≤1.

Test the equation with a cutoff times uq−1, first regularized or truncated, to get ∫ζ2∣∇uq/2∣2≤[q/(q−1)]2R2∫uq∣∇ζ∣2. Apply a fixed-domain Sobolev embedding theorem to ζuq/2 and take the 2/q power. Keeping the constant inside that power is crucial for Moser iteration.
