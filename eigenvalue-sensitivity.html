Eigenvalue sensitivity (s(λ))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Linear operator theory Eigenvalue Simple eigenvalue
OurBigBook.com Words: 48 Articles: 1
For unit left eigenvector u and right eigenvector v of a real matrix at a simple eigenvalue λ, its normwise sensitivity is
s(λ)=∣uTv∣1​.
(138)
It measures the first-order amplification of a matrix perturbation into an eigenvalue perturbation.
