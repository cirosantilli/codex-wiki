Symmetric Bregman distance (DJsym​(u,v)=⟨pu​−pv​,u−v⟩)

For pu​∈∂J(u) and pv​∈∂J(v), add the two Bregman distances to get DJpv​​(u,v)+DJpu​​(v,u)=⟨pu​−pv​,u−v⟩≥0. The choice of subgradients matters. This is generally not a metric: it need not separate points or satisfy a triangle inequality.
