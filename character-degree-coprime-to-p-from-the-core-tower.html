Character degree coprime to p from the core tower (p∤χλ(1))

OurBigBook.com Words: 76
Let p be a prime number, let n=∑r​αr​pr with 0≤αr​<p, and let cr​ be the sum of partition sizes at level r of the core tower of a partition λ⊢n. Then
p∤χλ(1)⟺cr​=αr​ for every r.​
(24)
Indeed n=∑r​cr​pr. Carrying p units from position r to position r+1 decreases ∑r​cr​ by p−1. Normalization to the base-p digits therefore gives ∑r​cr​≥∑r​αr​, with equality exactly when no carry is needed. The P-adic valuation of a symmetric-group character degree from the core tower is the difference divided by p−1.
