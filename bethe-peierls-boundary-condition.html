Bethe-Peierls boundary condition (limr→0​∂r​(rψ)/(rψ)=−1/a)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering Partial-wave S-matrix Scattering length from a partial-wave S-matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 2
This zero-range boundary condition replaces a short-range interaction by its S-wave scattering length. If ψ=A/r+B+O(r), it requires B=−A/a. An outgoing spherical wave also has a constant ik term; retaining that term gives the unitary scattering-length amplitude f0​=−a/(1+ika). The model neglects effective-range corrections and is applied outside the microscopic range, not literally inside a finite potential.
