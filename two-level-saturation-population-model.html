Two-level saturation population model (pe​=2(1+s+4Δ2/Γ2)s​)

 Home Physics Atomic physics Atomic measurement methods Laser spectroscopy Doppler-free saturation spectroscopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 442 Articles: 1
Choose a closed two-level system, equal stimulated absorption and emission strengths, a monochromatic pump, and no extra dephasing. Let pg​+pe​=1 be the lower and upper populations, and let spontaneous emission remove upper population at rate Γ=1/τe​. In this convention the natural linewidth is full width at half maximum Γ in angular frequency, its half width at half maximum is Γ/2, and its full width at half maximum in hertz is Γ/(2π). All Δ below are angular detunings.
Let s=I/Isat​ be the on-resonance saturation parameter. An effective stimulated transition rate that reproduces the monochromatic steady response is
R(Δ)=2(1+4Δ2/Γ2)Γs​,p˙​e​=Rpg​−(R+Γ)pe​=R(1−2pe​)−Γpe​.
(33)
Here R describes each stimulated direction, while Γ describes irreversible spontaneous emission. The rate equation gives the steady populations; it is not a description of transient Rabi oscillations. Setting p˙​e​=0 gives
pe​=Γ+2RR​=2(1+s+4Δ2/Γ2)s​,pg​=1−pe​,
(34)
and the population difference relevant to net weak-probe photon absorption is
d=pg​−pe​=1+s+4Δ2/Γ21+4Δ2/Γ2​,q=1−d=1+s+4Δ2/Γ2s​.
(35)
The pump depletes the lower population by pe​, but reduces the population difference by q=2pe​: stimulated emission contributes as well as missing ground-state absorbers. On resonance, s=1 gives pe​=1/4, pg​=3/4, d=1/2; s=9 gives pe​=0.45, pg​=0.55, d=0.10. As s→∞, the two populations approach 1/2, rather than producing a population inversion. Far off resonance, pe​→0 and d→1.
For moving atoms use Δ=δ−kv in these pump-prepared populations. A weak counterpropagating probe interrogates them near δ+kv=0. A simple population-only approximation to its absorption coefficient averages the weak-probe Lorentzian line shape times d(δ−kv) over the Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution. It neglects interference between the beams, changes of velocity during illumination, and multilevel optical pumping. Those restrictions matter for real alkali chemical atoms.
