The Stokes vibrational band has O, Q and S branches, with ΔN=−2,0,+2, respectively. Here N denotes end-over-end rotational angular momentum; treating electronic fine structure as unresolved gives the usual elementary rotor calculation. The absolute incident wavenumber is
νL​=1064107​=9398.496 cm−1,

and energy conservation for Stokes Raman scattering gives
νs​=νL​−1580−B{N′(N′+1)−N(N+1)}.
(4)
For the actual 16O2​ ground state, only odd N have nonzero nuclear-spin statistical weight. Thus the lowest initial levels are N=3 for O and N=1 for Q and S. Ignoring the small change in B upon vibration, the lines are:

The odd-N restriction follows from the zero nuclear spin and exchange symmetry of 16O2​; the electronic spin splits each such N into several J levels without changing this simplified branch-centre calculation.[ref]
If the phrase simplest approximation is instead taken to discard nuclear-spin statistics as well, a fictitious unrestricted rotor gives O 2→0 at 7826.9 cm−1 and S 0→2 at 7810.1 cm−1, with Q still at 7818.5 cm−1. Those even-N lines are not physical ground-state 16O2​ lines.
