(51)
The terms are electron kinetic energy, electron–nucleus attraction, and electron–electron repulsion. The nuclear kinetic operator is omitted in the Born-Oppenheimer approximation. The nuclear repulsion
VNN​=4πϵ0​∣R1​−R2​∣e2​
(52)
is a constant at fixed separation and is added to electronic energies to form the total potential curve. Some conventions include it in H^e​ as a constant; the electronic-energy convention used below excludes it so that the two one-electron energies can be compared without double-counting nuclear repulsion.
