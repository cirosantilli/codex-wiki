The measured decay is approximately consistent with the predicted 198 s−1 loss rate. Following rapid singlet-to-triplet population transfer, the unquenched phosphorescence intensity obeys
IP​(t)=IP​(0)e−(kP​+kD​)t,lnIP​=lnIP​(0)−198t.
(38)
A straight-line fit of lnIP​ to the four times, expressed in seconds, gives
kobs​=194.55 s−1,τobs​=5.14 ms,IP​(0)≃99.2.​
(39)
This differs from 198 s−1 by about 1.7%. For example, using the first point and the predicted rate gives subsequent intensities about 67.3,55.2,45.3, close to the supplied 66.3,56.2,45.3. The data support the expected millisecond exponential decay to this precision. With no measurement uncertainties supplied, a formal statistical acceptance or rejection is not justified.
