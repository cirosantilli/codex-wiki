IC4​​=4mB​R2(1−cosθ); the central atom makes no contribution. Let ρ be the radius of the square of outer atoms and hA​ the central atom's height above its plane. Then R2=ρ2+hA2​. Adjacent bond vectors have perpendicular in-plane components, so
cosθ=R2hA2​​,ρ2=R2(1−cosθ).
(24)
The centre of mass lies on the fourfold axis, even when mA​=mB​. Thus the central atom and the shift of origin along the axis contribute no perpendicular lever arm. The four outer atoms each contribute mB​ρ2, giving
IC4​​=mA​(0)2+4mB​ρ2=4mB​R2(1−cosθ).​
(25)
The result concerns the smallest adjacent bond angle; the angle between opposite outer atoms is a different angle in a pyramid.
