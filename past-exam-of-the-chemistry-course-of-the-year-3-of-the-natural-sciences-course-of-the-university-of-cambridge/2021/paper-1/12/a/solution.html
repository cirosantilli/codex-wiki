Past exam of the chemistry course of the year 3 of the natural sciences course of the University of Cambridge / 2021 / Paper 1 / 12 / a / Solution

 ... Chemistry course of the year 3 of the natural sciences course of the University of Cambridge Past exam of the chemistry course of the year 3 of the natural sciences course of the University of Cambridge 2021 Paper 1 12 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 301
Disconnect the N–cyclohexyl bond as a late reductive amination of a primary amine with cyclohexanone. Disconnect the exocyclic methylene as a Wittig methylenation of a substituted cyclohexanone. The useful ring precursor is 3-methyl-5-substituted cyclohex-2-en-1-one, accessible by a Robinson annulation. The pendant substituent throughout ring construction should be (CH2​)4​NHBoc.
Start with nucleophilic substitution of the supplied bromo ester by cyanide:
Br(CH2​)3​CO2​Me⟶NC(CH2​)3​CO2​Me.
(19)
Reduce the nitrile selectively to the primary amine while retaining the ester, for example by controlled catalytic hydrogenation with Raney nickel in the presence of ammonia, and trap the amine promptly as its Boc carbamate. Avoid heating or concentrating the free amino ester, which can cyclize to a lactam. lithium aluminium hydride would be unsuitable at this stage because it also reduces the ester.
Partial reduction of the protected ester with DIBAL at low temperature gives BocNH(CH2​)4​CHO. The carbon of the original nitrile is now the methylene nearest nitrogen; the original ester carbonyl is the aldehyde carbon. Condense this aldehyde with acetone and dehydrate to BocNH(CH2​)4​CH=CHCOMe.
Now add the enolate of a second acetone molecule in a Michael addition. With R=(CH2​)4​NHBoc, the resulting chain is
MeCOCH2​CH(R)CH2​COMe.
(20)
Intramolecular aldol condensation gives 3-Me-5-R-cyclohex-2-en-1-one. The beta methyl derives from the carbonyl end attacked in the ring closure; the pendant aminoalkyl chain remains at C5. Hydrogenate the ring C=C selectively, retaining the ketone, then methylenate the ketone with preformed Ph3​P=CH2​. Do the hydrogenation before installing the exocyclic methylene so that it is not hydrogenated away.
Remove Boc under brief mild conditions, checking for acid-induced exocyclic alkene isomerization. reductive amination of the resulting primary amine with cyclohexanone, followed by selective imine reduction, installs the secondary N-cyclohexyl group. Use controlled stoichiometry to suppress tertiary amine formation. The methyl and four-carbon aminoalkyl substituents occupy the required 1,3-related ring positions, with the exocyclic methylene at the former carbonyl. No ring stereochemistry is specified. The target contains eighteen carbons: six ring carbons, one ring methyl, one methylene, four in the linker, and six in the N-cyclohexyl group.
