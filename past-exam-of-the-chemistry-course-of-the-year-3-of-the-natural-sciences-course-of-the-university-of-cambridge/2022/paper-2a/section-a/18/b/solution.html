The Hückel orbital charge density and Hückel pi bond order give
ni​=∑k​fk​cik2​,pij​=∑k​fk​cik​cjk​.
(16)
Each site receives 1/2 electron from the lowest orbital and one from the occupied nonbonding subspace, so ni​=3/2 at every carbon, summing to six. Relative to one neutral pi electron per carbon this is a pi charge of −e/2 at each site.
For adjacent sites, only the lowest orbital contributes, giving p12​=p23​=p34​=p41​=1/2. For opposite sites the nonbonding contribution reverses the sign: p13​=p24​=−1/2. These non-neighbour negative density-matrix entries are not extra sigma bonds or extra resonance integrals in the Hamiltonian. If only skeletal nearest-neighbour pi bond orders are requested, the four values 1/2 are the relevant ones.
