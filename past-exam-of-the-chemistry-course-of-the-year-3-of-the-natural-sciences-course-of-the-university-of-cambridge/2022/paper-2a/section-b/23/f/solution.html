Endpoint energies, endpoint phase patterns and a conserved symmetry fix the qualitative connectivity. Each initial orbital must end in the appropriate product symmetry block, and the ordering within a generic block is preserved by avoided crossings. The two singleton blocks A2​ and B2​ have unique partners. No numerical calculation at each intermediate separation is required to draw those connections.
This conclusion is limited to a correlation diagram: it does not determine intermediate orbital energies, interaction strengths, the location or height of a reaction energy barrier, or the lowest route on a multidimensional potential energy surface. If the reaction breaks the assumed symmetry, previously distinct symmetry blocks can mix and the diagram must be reconsidered.
