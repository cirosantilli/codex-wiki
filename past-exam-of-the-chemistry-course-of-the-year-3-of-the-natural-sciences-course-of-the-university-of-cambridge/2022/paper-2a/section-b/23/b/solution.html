Each bonding oxygen orbital has equal-phase coefficients on its two O atoms; its antibonding partner reverses one coefficient. The two pi directions are z in the prospective ring plane and y perpendicular to it. carbon pi and pi-star use z-directed p orbitals with equal and opposite coefficient signs. In this separated-fragment comparison the occupations are those of neutral triplet O2​ plus neutral ethene.
