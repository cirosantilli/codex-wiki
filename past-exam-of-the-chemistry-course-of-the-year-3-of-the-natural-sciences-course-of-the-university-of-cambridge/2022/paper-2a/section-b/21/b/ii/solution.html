The subbranch spacing gives A−B=4.9 cm−1, hence A≃5.15 cm−1. Rotation about the C–I symmetry axis has moment

C and I lie on this axis, so only the three hydrogens contribute to Ia​. If θ is the I–C–H angle, their perpendicular distances are rCH​sinθ. Taking integer hydrogen mass gives
Ia​=3mH​rCH2​sin2θ,sin2θ≃3(1.66054×10−27)(1.1×10−10)25.44×10−47​≃0.902.

The molecular arrangement selects the obtuse solution, so θ≃108∘. The supplementary acute angle describes the opposite axial direction, not the bond angle to iodine. This is a rigid-geometry estimate based on an effective rotational constant and the stipulated bond length, rather than a precision equilibrium-structure measurement.
