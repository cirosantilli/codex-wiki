For [B12​H12​]2−, twelve BH vertices supply 12(2) skeletal electrons and the charge adds two:
Nskel​=26=2(12+1).
(8)
For the cobaltacarborane, nine BH vertices supply 18, two CH vertices supply six and CpCo supplies two by (a):
Nskel​=9(2)+2(3)+2=26.
(9)
Both are 12-vertex closo icosahedra with 13 skeletal electron pairs. In the cobalt compound, the Cp ring is external to the cage. cobalt caps the five-membered open face of the 11-vertex dicarbollide fragment and is a vertex of the resulting icosahedron, giving the usual adjacent-carbon cobaltacarborane framework. Counting all five Cp carbons as additional polyhedral vertices would be incorrect.
