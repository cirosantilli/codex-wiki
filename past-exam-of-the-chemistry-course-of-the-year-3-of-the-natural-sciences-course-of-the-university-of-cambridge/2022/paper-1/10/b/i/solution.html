Ln(s)+3H+(aq)⟶Ln3+(aq)+23​H2​(g).
(11)
A Hess cycle first atomizes the metal, then ionizes its gas-phase atoms, then hydrates the triply charged gas ions:
Ln(s)Ln(g)Ln3+(g)​⟶Ln(g)⟶Ln3+(g)+3e−(g)⟶Ln3+(aq)​​Δat​H∘>0,I1​+I2​+I3​>0,Δhyd​H∘<0.​
(12)
Close the reference leg by combining the three electrons with 3H+(aq) to make 23​H2​(g). Calling that element-independent contribution CH​ gives
ΔHacid∘​=Δat​H∘+I1​+I2​+I3​+Δhyd​H∘+CH​.​
(13)
A single-ion standard enthalpy of formation uses the conventional hydrogen-ion reference; it must not be treated as a balanced creation of net charge from neutral metal without an electron/reference term. The constant hydrogen leg does not affect comparisons along the lanthanide series.
