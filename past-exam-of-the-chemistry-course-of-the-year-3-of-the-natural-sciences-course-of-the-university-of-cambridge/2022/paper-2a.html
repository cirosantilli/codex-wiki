The doublet is resolvable in principle, but only marginally against the Doppler width. Use the D3h molecular point group for phosphorus pentafluoride, with z along the two axial P–F bonds. For each symmetry operation, only atoms left in place contribute to the character of the Cartesian displacement representation; each contributes the trace of the corresponding 3×3 spatial matrix. In the class order E,2C3​,3C2′​,σh​,2S3​,3σv​ this gives
χ3N​=(18,0,−2,4,−2,4).
(3)
For example a C2′​ fixes P and one equatorial F, and each fixed atom has Cartesian trace −1, giving −2. reflection in the equatorial plane fixes four atoms, each with trace 1, giving 4. Reduction with the character table gives
Γ3N​=2A1′​+A2′​+4E′+3A2′′​+2E′′.
(4)

Γvib​=2A1′​+3E′+2A2′′​+E′′.​
(5)

An alternant hydrocarbon has a pi-site graph that can be divided into two sets so that every retained neighbouring resonance interaction joins different sets. Equivalently, the graph is bipartite. A chain and an even ring satisfy this condition; an odd ring does not. The classification refers to the model's coupling graph, rather than simply to which bonds are drawn double in one Lewis structure.

Write H=αI+K with the same carbon diagonal parameter throughout, and define P to have entries +1 on one sublattice and −1 on the other. Bipartite connectivity implies
PKP=−K.
(17)
Thus Kc=ϵc implies K(Pc)=−ϵ(Pc). This proves energy pairing about α, E=α±ϵ, and equal site-probability magnitudes in each paired pair, since ∣(Pc)i​∣2=∣ci​∣2. This is the Coulson-Rushbrooke pairing property.[ref]
For the usual neutral half-filled closed-shell occupation, the lower member of each pair is doubly occupied. Combining paired coefficients with completeness of the orthonormal basis gives one pi electron per carbon, so the model predicts no net pi charge separation. The same argument makes off-diagonal pi bond orders between sites on the same sublattice zero. These statements assume a neutral half-filled density and do not apply unchanged to the charged six-electron four-site ion considered separately.
An imbalance of sublattice sizes also guarantees at least ∣NA​−NB​∣ nonbonding orbitals at energy α, because the rectangular coupling block has insufficient rank to remove all its null space. None of the spectral pairing, equal paired densities, neutral charge uniformity or sublattice bond-order restrictions is required for a non-alternant graph. Long-range same-sublattice couplings or unequal diagonal parameters can likewise destroy the simple proof.

In the Condon approximation the intensity from v′′=0 to v′ contains the Franck-Condon factor
∣⟨χv′A​∣χ0X​⟩∣2.
(29)
The lower ground-state vibrational wavefunction is localized near its equilibrium bond length. In the electronic state correlation diagram, the singly occupied reactant orbitals for triplet oxygen have symmetries B1​ and A2​, so their spatial product is B2​. The reactant state is therefore a triplet 3B2​, whereas the closed-shell product is 1A1​. A strictly spin-conserving path cannot correlate triplet reactants directly to the ground-state product. It correlates to an excited triplet product configuration or requires intersystem crossing; the symmetric direct reaction consequently has a substantial electronic obstacle.
Simply changing the oxygen spin does not repair the occupied carbon-orbital correlation. The lowest oxygen singlet manifold has spatial components of A1​ and B2​ symmetry in this subgroup. The A1​ singlet can have an avoided crossing with another A1​ configuration and reach the product singlet; the B2​ singlet cannot mix directly with the product 1A1​ while C2v​ is maintained. Following either closed-shell oxygen singlet component in the orbital diagram still leaves the carbon-pi electron pair in the rising σ+∗​ orbital. Its redistribution into the final occupied orbitals requires configuration mixing at an avoided crossing. A barrier is therefore expected even for the symmetry-constrained concerted singlet addition; singlet does not imply barrierless. The state curves in the original diagram are schematic, illustrating this avoided-crossing argument rather than computed energies.[ref]
The real singlet reaction can lower this obstacle through asymmetric or stepwise pathways that are absent from the stipulated direct approach. A primary multireference study explicitly distinguishes singlet and triplet addition through biradical intermediates, supporting that limitation of a one-coordinate symmetry argument.[ref] Neither correlation alone nor a spin label supplies a numerical activation energy.
