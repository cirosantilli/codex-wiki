For the character of a localized orbital basis, take the trace of the transformation matrix: both permutation of centres and signs of atomic orbitals matter. The two metal centres lie on the principal axis and remain fixed under rotation about z. At either metal the (px​,py​) basis has the matrix
R(θ)=(cosθsinθ​−sinθcosθ​).
