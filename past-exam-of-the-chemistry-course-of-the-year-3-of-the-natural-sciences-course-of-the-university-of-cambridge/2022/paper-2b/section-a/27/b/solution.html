For chromium the configuration is [Ar]3d54s1. The half-filled 3d subshell has Sd​=5/2 and Ld​=0; aligning the 4s spin gives S=3, while L=0. Therefore the ground-state term symbol is Cr:7S3​. For cobalt, [Ar]3d74s2 has a closed 4s2 pair. The d7 subshell has the same possible L,S values as three d holes; maximizing spin and then orbital angular momentum gives S=3/2, L=3. Because it is more than half full, J=3+3/2=9/2, giving Co:4F9/2​. For copper, [Ar]3d104s1 has a closed d subshell, leaving L=0, S=1/2 and Cu:2S1/2​. These are neutral-atom terms, rather than the terms of the common metal ions. The tabulated zero-energy levels independently confirm all three assignments.[ref][ref][ref]
