Treat the site atomic orbitals s1​,…,s4​ as orthonormal, as in the Hückel method. A normalized set of symmetry-adapted linear combinations is
ΦA1g​​ΦB1g​​ΦEu​,x​ΦEu​,y​​=21​(s1​+s2​+s3​+s4​),=21​(s1​−s2​+s3​−s4​),=2​1​(s3​−s1​),=2​1​(s2​−s4​).​​
(38)
The first combination is unchanged by every operation; the alternating combination has the transformation signs of x2−y2 for the printed axes. The last two transform together like (x,y). The squared coefficients sum to one in each case, and their pairwise inner products vanish. Any orthogonal rotation of the two Eu​ combinations is an equally valid basis for that degenerate subspace.
