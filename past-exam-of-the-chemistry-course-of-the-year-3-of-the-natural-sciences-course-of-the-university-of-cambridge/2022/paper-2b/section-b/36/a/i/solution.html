The drawn x,y axes pass through opposite sites, rather than along the square edges. In the D4h molecular point group class order (E,2C4​,C2​,2C2′​,2C2′′​,i,2S4​,σh​,2σv​,2σd​), an s orbital contributes +1 whenever its centre is fixed and zero otherwise. Thus
χΓ​=(4,0,0,2,0,0,0,4,2,0).
(36)
For example, a transverse twofold rotation about the printed x axis fixes sites 1 and 3, while its diagonal counterpart exchanges all four sites. Reflection in the square plane fixes every site. Taking character inner products, with class sizes (1,2,1,2,2,1,2,1,2,2), gives
Γ=A1g​⊕B1g​⊕Eu​.​
(37)
These have dimensions 1+1+2=4. The B1g​ label is tied to the printed diagonal-axis convention; rotating the transverse coordinate labels by 45∘ would interchange B1g​ and B2g​.
