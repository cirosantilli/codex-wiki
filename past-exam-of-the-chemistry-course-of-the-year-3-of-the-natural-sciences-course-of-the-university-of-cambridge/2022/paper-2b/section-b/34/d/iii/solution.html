The molecule with the larger inferred dissociation energy is assigned the stronger hydrogen–halogen bond: A=HBr and B=HI. Moving from bromine to the larger iodine atom generally lengthens the bond and reduces effective overlap with the compact hydrogen 1s orbital, so hydrogen iodide is more weakly bound. Independently, the tabulated ground-state harmonic constants near 2649 cm−1 for hydrogen bromide and 2309 cm−1 for hydrogen iodide agree with the inferred assignment.[ref][ref] The ordering is the identification criterion; the low-level Morse oscillator extrapolations above should not be presented as measured dissociation energies.
