Express the rotational constant as a spectroscopic wavenumber B=h/(8π2cI). The rotational absorption selection rule ΔJ=+1 gives
νJ→J+1​=2B(J+1).
(33)
Successive lines therefore have spacing 2B, regardless of the starting J. Both observed spacings are 20.88 cm−1, giving B=10.44 cm−1. The three lines are consequently the 5→6, 6→7 and 7→8 transitions within the stated precision; the first observed line is not the 0→1 line.
Using the given isotopic masses, the reduced mass is
μ=1.0078+34.9691.0078(34.969)​mu​=1.62661×10−27 kg.
(34)
With B=1044 m−1 and c in ms−1, the bond length follows from
r=8π2cμBh​​=1.28390×10−10 m≃1.284 A˚.​
(35)
The small common 0.01 cm−1 offset relative to integer multiples of the spacing is immaterial at the precision of this model. A real ground-vibrational-state rotational constant determines an effective vibrationally averaged bond length; it need not equal the exact equilibrium separation.
