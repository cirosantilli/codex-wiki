2.080=−0.560(1−ϕD​)+6.335ϕD​,ϕD​=0.3828,

where all scattering length densities are in 10−6A˚−2. The 100g of heavy water occupies about 90.99cm3. The required ordinary-water volume is
VH​=VD​ϕD​1−ϕD​​≃146.7cm3.
(48)
Thus add approximately 147g of H2​O. Volume fraction, rather than mass fraction, enters this contrast-matching equation. Isotope exchange to form HDO does not change the total nuclear scattering-length balance in the additive-volume approximation.
