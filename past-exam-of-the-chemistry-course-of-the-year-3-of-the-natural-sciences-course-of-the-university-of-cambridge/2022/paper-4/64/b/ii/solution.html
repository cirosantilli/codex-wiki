Use the water data to determine the common oxygen contribution rather than assume identical molar volumes. Ordinary water has molecular number density
nH​=18106​NA​=3.346×1028m−3,
(45)
so bO​=ρbH2​O​/nH​−2bH​≃5.81×10−15m. Therefore bD2​O​=2bD​+bO​≃1.915×10−14m and
nD​=bD2​O​6.335×1014​,ρD2​O​=NA​20nD​​≃1.10gcm−3​.
(46)
The assumptions are isotopically pure waters, additive nuclear scattering lengths, the same oxygen isotope contribution, and molar masses approximately 18 and 20gmol−1. Equal molar volumes would give the rougher estimate 20/18=1.11gcm−3.
