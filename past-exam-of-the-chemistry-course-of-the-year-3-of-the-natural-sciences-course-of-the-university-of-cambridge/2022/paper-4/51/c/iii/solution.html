A useful electrode-surface scheme for the proposed ECEC mechanism is
AQ+e−​AQ∙−+CO2​​AQCO2∙−​+e−​AQCO22−​+CO2​​AQ(CO2​)22−​.
(1)
Electron-transfer arrows occur at the electrode, whereas both capture steps occur in the adjacent solution layer. CO2​ is attacked by an oxygen-centered nucleophilic component of the reduced quinone, forming an O-carbonate linkage rather than simply physically adsorbing on neutral AQ.
At potentials more negative than −2.0V, direct second reduction of any unbound semiquinone is also accessible: AQ∙−→AQ2−, followed by carbonate formation. Thus the fully reduced unbound and mono-adduct species can feed the bis-adduct, and the drawing should include CO2​ transport into the layer and adduct transport away. ECEC is the proposed sequence; competing EECC transfer/capture ordering is possible at such negative potentials.
