Without significant anomalous scattering, the electron density is effectively real and Friedel's law gives F0​(−h)=F0​(h)∗, hence I(222)=13130.
With anomalous scattering the atomic imaginary term retains its sign when the reflection index reverses. Here ΔF(−222)=19.2i, so
I(−222)=∣F0∗​+19.2i∣2=13130+19.22−2(19.2)∣F0​∣sinϕ=11567.3 electrons2.

Thus the second expected intensity is approximately 1.157×104 electrons2. Conjugating the complete anomalous structure factor would incorrectly impose Friedel equality on the second experiment.
