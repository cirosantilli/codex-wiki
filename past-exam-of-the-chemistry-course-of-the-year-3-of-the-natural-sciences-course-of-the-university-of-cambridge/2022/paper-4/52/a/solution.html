OurBigBook.com Words: 124
Write Me=CH3​. The alternating-vertex cubane A is Li4​Me4​; replacing one or two methyl vertices gives B=Li4​Me3​X and C=Li4​Me2​X2​. Balanced redistributions are
A+LiX⇌B+MeLi,A+2LiX⇌C+2MeLi.
(3)
The smaller aggregate has composition D=Li2​MeX, since the conventional masses 2(7)+15+80=109 or 2(7)+15+127=156 match the supplied values. Thus A+4LiX⇌4D is another balanced redistribution.
For the structures, place Li on one tetrahedral set of cube corners and Me/X on the complementary set, joining adjacent unlike vertices. A contains four Me vertices, B three Me and one X, and C two of each. D is a four-membered alternating Li-Me-Li-X ring. The quoted relative masses use conventional elemental masses; a fully 6Li-enriched D would be approximately two mass units lighter, without changing its composition.
