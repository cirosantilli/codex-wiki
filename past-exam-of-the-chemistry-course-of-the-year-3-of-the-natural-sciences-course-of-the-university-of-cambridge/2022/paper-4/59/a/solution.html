Let H(0)∣n⟩=En(0)​∣n⟩ with a nondegenerate reference eigenvalue, and choose intermediate normalization ⟨n∣n(1)⟩=0. The first-order equation is
(H(0)−En(0)​)∣n(1)⟩=−(H(1)−En(1)​)∣n⟩.
(18)
Projection on ∣j⟩, j=n, gives ⟨j∣n(1)⟩=Hjn(1)​/(En(0)​−Ej(0)​). The second-order projected equation gives En(2)​=⟨n∣H(1)∣n(1)⟩, hence
En(2)​=−∑j=n​Ej(0)​−En(0)​∣Hnj(1)​∣2​.
(19)
The sum runs over a complete orthonormal reference basis except the reference state; continuum states require integration if present. Degenerate states with zero denominators require degenerate perturbation theory first. These are expansion coefficients in En​=En(0)​+λEn(1)​+λ2En(2)​+⋯.
