B contains both −CH2​−CH(Cl)− and −CH2​−CH(OCOCH3​)− monomeric units distributed along the same carbon backbone: a vinyl chloride-vinyl acetate copolymer. One possible local segment is
⋯−CH2​−CH(Cl)−CH2​−CH(OAc)−CH2​−CH(Cl)−⋯,OAc=OC(O)CH3​.
(44)
The ester is attached through oxygen to the backbone carbon. A random copolymer should not be drawn as two long homopolymer blocks. The stated 75:25 monomer feed is not enough to prove an incorporated 75:25 unit ratio: monomer reactivity ratios and conversion affect composition and sequence statistics. Under an explicitly ideal equal-incorporation assumption that ratio could be used as a schematic average, not a periodic repeat.
