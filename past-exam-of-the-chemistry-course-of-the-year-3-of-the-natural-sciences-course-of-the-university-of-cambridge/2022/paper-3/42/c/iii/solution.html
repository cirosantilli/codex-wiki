Using an isothermal atmosphere with the stated scale height gives p(30km)=105e−30/7Pa and [M]=p/(kB​T)=3.46×1017cm−3 at 288K. The water atmospheric mixing ratio then gives [H2​O]=6.92×1011cm−3. Applying steady state to the short-lived electronically excited oxygen atom,
[O(1D)]=k2​[M]+k3​[H2​O]J1​[O3​]​=21.7cm−3.
(10)
Its HOx family source is S=2k3​[O(1D)][H2​O]≃6.00×103cm−3s−1. Balancing this with termination gives 2k4​[OH][HO2​]=S. Combining with the rapid partition, or solving both stationary radical equations including termination, gives
[OH]≃8.53×104,[HO2​]≃3.52×108 cm−3​.
(11)
Consequently the main catalytic odd-oxygen loss is
LHOx​​≃1.27×106cm−3s−1​.
(12)
The Chapman cycle loss is LCh​=2k9​[O][O3​]=5.40×105cm−3s−1, so the given HOx​ cycle is about 2.35 times larger. Both rates count two odd-oxygen units per completed O-plus-O3​ destruction event. The omitted v4​≃3.00×103 correction is only about 0.24% of the main HOx​ loss. The family abundance divided by its source is about 5.87×104s, explaining why its absolute stationary state is a stronger assumption than rapid OH/HO2​ interconversion.
