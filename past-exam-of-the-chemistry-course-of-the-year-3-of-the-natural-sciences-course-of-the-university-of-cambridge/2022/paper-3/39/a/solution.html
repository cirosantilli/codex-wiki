The two substituents are −CH2​−O−C(O)−CH=CH−Ph, with the alkene E, and −C(OH)(CH3​)2​. The free bond at the left of each drawing attaches to the cyclohexene.
The A-D COSY correlation and 16.2Hz coupling identify the trans cinnamate double bond. Five aromatic protons and four phenyl-carbon environments identify its monosubstituted phenyl ring. F is a two-proton OCH2​ signal; its HMBC correlation to carbonyl 1 joins that methylene through oxygen to the ester. The two three-proton singlets N/O, their correlations to oxygenated quaternary carbon 10, and the missing OH resonance give the tertiary-alcohol substituent.
