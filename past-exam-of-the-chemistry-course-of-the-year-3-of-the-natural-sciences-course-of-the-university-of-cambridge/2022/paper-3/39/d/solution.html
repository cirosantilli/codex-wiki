Number ring positions r1​ through r6​ around the ring, with r1​=r2​ the double bond. These auxiliary ring-position labels are distinct from the printed carbon-signal numbers.
  • r1​: quaternary alkene carbon 4, attached to the OCH2​ side chain.
  • r2​: alkene CH 8, proton E.
  • r3​: allylic CH2​, one of carbon signals 14/15; its protons are K and one of G/H/I.
  • r4​: methine 12, proton L, attached to the tertiary-alcohol carbon 10.
  • r5​: CH2​ 17, protons J and M.
  • r6​: the other allylic CH2​ 15/14, carrying the remaining two of G/H/I.
The HSQC pairs J and M with 17 and places L on 12. It also places K and one unresolved member of G/H/I on one of the closely spaced allylic carbons, with the other two G/H/I protons on the other. COSY connects the vinylic E to the adjacent allylic part and connects the methine L to its neighboring methylenes; HMBC links F to the substituted alkene end and the gem-dimethyl group to the methine end.
The printed overlap does not justify a unique assignment of every G/H/I letter or a forced 14-versus-15 assignment to those two ring positions. The drawing retains those alternatives explicitly. In the half-chair, L is pseudoaxial; the resolved large/small couplings are consistent with pseudoaxial/pseudoequatorial methylene partners, but exact axial letter labels within the overlapped group remain uncertain.
