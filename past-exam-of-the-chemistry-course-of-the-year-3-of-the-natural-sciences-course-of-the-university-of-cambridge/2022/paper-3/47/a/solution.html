At fixed particle number and pressure, the joint isothermal-isobaric ensemble weight for a microstate of energy E and volume V is e−β(E+PV). Write its enthalpy as H=E+PV. Differentiating the isothermal-isobaric partition function with a temperature-independent volume measure gives
∂β​QP​=−∫0∞​dV∑s​Hs​(V)e−βHs​(V).
(19)
After division by QP​ this is the normalized ensemble average, so
⟨H⟩P​=−∂β​lnQP​​.
(20)
The differentiation assumes that the microscopic Hamiltonian and the integration measure have no additional explicit temperature dependence. An extra temperature-dependent normalization factor must be differentiated separately.
