The volume integral is a gamma function integral,

For the prescribed bridge relation, βPQP​=(βPΛ3)−N, so G=NkB​Tln(βPΛ3). Since Λ∝T−1/2, this gives the macroscopic ideal-gas result
H=G−T(∂T​G)P​=25​NkB​T,CP​=25​NkB​​,CP​−CV​=NkB​.
(25)
Heating at fixed pressure requires both an increase in internal energy and expansion work; at fixed volume the latter contribution is absent.
The finite-size normalization of the isothermal-isobaric ensemble matters here. Applying part (a) literally to the unprefactored QP​ instead gives ⟨H⟩P​=(5N/2+1)kB​T and CP​=(5N/2+1)kB​. The two answers differ because −∂β​ln(βPQP​)=−∂β​lnQP​−kB​T. The normalized continuous-volume distribution has ⟨PV⟩=(N+1)kB​T; its variance agrees with the unprefactored result in part (b). The bridge convention subtracts an order-one contribution and recovers the usual extensive thermodynamic answer. Both agree to leading order in the thermodynamic limit, but their exact finite-N derivatives should not be mixed.
