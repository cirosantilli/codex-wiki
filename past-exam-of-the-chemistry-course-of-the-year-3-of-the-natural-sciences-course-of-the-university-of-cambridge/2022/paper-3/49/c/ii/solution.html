Choose mass-weighted coordinates QA​, QE​=(QE1​,QE2​), and two triplets Q3​,Q4​ for the two T2​ copies. About a stationary equilibrium geometry, the allowed harmonic potential is
V(2)=21​kA​QA2​+21​kE​∣QE​∣2+21​k33​∣Q3​∣2+k34​Q3​⋅Q4​+21​k44​∣Q4​∣2.
(39)
The kinetic energy is one-half the sum of the squared conjugate momenta. Cross terms between inequivalent irreducible representations are forbidden, but the two equivalent T2​ triplets permit the displayed scalar-product term. This is equivalent-mode harmonic coupling. An orthogonal rotation between the two triplets diagonalizes their 2×2 force-constant matrix. In molecular normal coordinates the complete Hamiltonian becomes
H=E0​+21​(PA2​+ω12​QA2​)+21​j=1∑2​(PEj2​+ω22​QEj2​)+21​j=1∑3​(P3j2​+ω32​Q3j2​+P4j2​+ω42​Q4j2​)​.
(40)
Symmetry enforces degeneracies within each multiplet; it does not require equal frequencies for the two distinct T2​ normal modes.
