Lever arm (ℓ=rsinϕ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Momentum Angular momentum Torque
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
The lever arm of a nonzero force about an origin is the shortest distance from that origin to the line through the application point parallel to the force. Its length is ℓ=∣r×F∣, with unit vector F=F/∣F∣. Thus torque magnitude is Fℓ, maximal at a right angle for fixed r and F, and zero if the line passes through the origin. Sliding the application point along that line leaves torque unchanged, since (r+sF)×F=r×F. The same perpendicular-distance construction gives angular momentum magnitude pℓ for a line parallel to nonzero momentum through the particle.
