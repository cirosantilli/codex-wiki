Lower norm of an operator (ν(T))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 885 Articles: 14
For a densely defined operator,
ν(T)=inf{∥Tx∥:x∈D(T), ∥x∥=1}.
(37)
The symmetric lower norm μ(T)=min{ν(T),ν(T∗)} equals ∥T−1∥−1 when T is invertible and is zero otherwise.
