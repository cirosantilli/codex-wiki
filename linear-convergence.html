Linear convergence (∣ek+1​∣/∣ek​∣→ρ∈(0,1))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Fixed-point iteration Order of convergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
An iteration of a map has linear convergence to its limit when its nonzero errors ek​=xk​−α satisfy ∣ek+1​∣/∣ek​∣→ρ for some 0<ρ<1. The error eventually shrinks by approximately the same factor at each step. This is exact order of convergence one, in contrast to quadratic convergence.
