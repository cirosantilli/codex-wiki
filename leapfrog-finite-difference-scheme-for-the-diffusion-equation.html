Applying a centered leapfrog step in time and the centered second difference in space to ut​=uxx​ gives
umn+1​=umn−1​+2μ(um−1n​−2umn​+um+1n​).
(28)
For every μ>0, each nonconstant Fourier mode has an amplification root of modulus greater than one, so the scheme is unconditionally unstable.
