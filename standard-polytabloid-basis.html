Standard polytabloid basis

 ... Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young tableau Tabloid Polytabloid Specht module
OurBigBook.com Words: 181 Articles: 1
The polytabloids et​ indexed by standard Young tableaux of shape λ form a basis of Sλ over every field, and a basis of its integral lattice over Z. Column antisymmetry and Garnir relations straighten any polytabloid into this set. Ordering leading tabloids by the rows containing successive entries makes the corresponding coefficient matrix triangular with diagonal entries one, proving independence. In particular the dimension is the standard-tableau count in every characteristic.
