Skolemization

 ... Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order structure Elementary substructure Skolem hull Skolem function
OurBigBook.com Words: 72 Articles: 1
Replacing an existentially quantified variable by a fresh Skolem function of the preceding universally quantified variables. A witness independent of all universal variables is represented by a fresh constant. The operation preserves satisfiability after expanding the first-order signature; it is not generally logical equivalence for arbitrary interpretations of the new symbols.
