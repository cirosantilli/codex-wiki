Mass-to-light ratio of a power-law stellar population

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy stellar mass Mass-to-light ratio
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
Write x=m/M⊙​, the initial mass function as dN=Ax−αdx, and a main-sequence mass-luminosity relation as L/L⊙​=xβ. On a<x<b, the mass-to-light ratio in solar units is
M⊙​/L⊙​Υ​=∫ab​xβ−αdx∫ab​x1−αdx​.
(2)
For α=β=3 this is exactly 1/(ab). The result concerns the specified unevolved stellar population; stellar remnants, evolved luminous stars and cooling substellar objects require different accounting.
