Bipartite stability from few odd cycles (∣E(G)△E(H)∣≤11ϵ(2n​))

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Extremal graph theory Turán's theorem Edit-distance stability for clique-free graphs
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
Fix an odd cycle length. If the graph has edge density at least 1/2−ϵ and sufficiently few copies of that cycle, odd-cycle copies from positive triangle density forces few triangles in a graph. The triangle removal lemma deletes at most ϵ(2n​) edges, after which the clique-free edit bound applies with two parts. Absorbing its linear rounding error into the density margin gives the displayed bound for large order.
