Integral scheme

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Reduced scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 80 Articles: 1
An integral scheme is a nonempty scheme that is both reduced and irreducible. An affine scheme SpecA is integral exactly when A is an integral domain.
