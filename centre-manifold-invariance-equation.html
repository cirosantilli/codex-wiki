Centre-manifold invariance equation

 Home Physics Branch of physics Dynamical systems Centre manifold theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
If a centre manifold is the graph v=h(u) for u˙=f(u,v) and v˙=g(u,v), its coefficients satisfy Dh(u)f(u,h(u))=g(u,h(u)).
