For V=L(ω1​) with the G2 root system numbered short-first,
Λ2V=L(ω2​)⊕L(ω1​),S2V=L(2ω1​)⊕L(0).
(54)
In the chain basis of the crystal of the seven-dimensional G2 representation, normalize v1​=f1​v0​, v2​=f2​v1​, v3​=f1​v2​. Then v0​∧v1​ and v0​∧v3​−2v1​∧v2​ are nonzero highest-weight vectors of weights ω2​ and ω1​; the identities e1​v1​=v0​, e1​v3​=2v2​, e2​v2​=v1​ verify both raising conditions. The Weyl complete reducibility theorem and G2 dimension polynomial exhaust the twenty-one dimensions of the exterior square. In the symmetric square, v02​ generates the twenty-seven-dimensional summand. Self-duality supplies a Lie-invariant bilinear form, which is symmetric because a nondegenerate alternating bilinear form cannot have odd dimension; its inverse gives the remaining invariant line.
