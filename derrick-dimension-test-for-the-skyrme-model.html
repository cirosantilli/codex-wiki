Derrick dimension test for the Skyrme model (E(L)=Ld−2E2​+Ld−4E4​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field-theory soliton Skyrme model
OurBigBook.com Words: 156 Articles: 1
For a spatially dilated Skyrme model field UL​(x)=U(x/L), the quadratic and quartic derivative energies scale as shown. A static stationary field must satisfy (d−2)E2​+(d−4)E4​=0. In three spatial dimensions this permits E2​=E4​ and a positive second variation along scale. In two dimensions it requires E4​=0, and in four or more it forces a constant finite-energy smooth field. In one dimension the commutator term vanishes identically. Derrick scaling tests a necessary variational condition, not existence or stability under all deformations.
