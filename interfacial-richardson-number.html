Interfacial Richardson number (Rii​=g′δ/u2)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave Buoyancy frequency Richardson number
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
A local ratio of stabilizing buoyancy to shear across an interface of thickness δ, using reduced gravity jump g′ and characteristic velocity u. The dimensionless value is Rii​=g′δ/u2. It is distinct from a whole-layer bulk Richardson number based on the total mixed depth. Local entrainment laws use this ratio when the transition thickness and velocity characterize the turbulence-producing region.
