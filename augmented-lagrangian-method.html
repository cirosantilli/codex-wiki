For an inequality, the exact identity is min(gi​,μλi​)=μ(λi​−λi+​). Its unshifted negative part satisfies min(gi​,0)=μmin(λi​−λi+​,0). On a violated constraint, accurate new multipliers therefore give violation of the order of μ times the multiplier error. This can achieve high feasibility accuracy without a vanishingly small μ, reducing condition number difficulties. The negative-part qualification is essential if an iterate approaches a binding constraint from the feasible side.
