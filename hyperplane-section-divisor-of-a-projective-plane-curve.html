Hyperplane-section divisor of a projective plane curve

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Cartier divisor Hyperplane divisor Hyperplane section
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
If a projective line H does not contain the projective plane curve X, its hyperplane-section divisor is
[X∩H]=∑p∈X∩H​Ip​(X,H)p,
(194)
where Ip​(X,H) is the intersection multiplicity. The Bézout theorem gives deg[X∩H]=(degX)(degH)=degX.
