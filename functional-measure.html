Functional measure

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Path integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The functional measure is the formal integration measure over field configurations in a path integral. Its transformation under a symmetry can preserve the corresponding Ward identity or produce a quantum anomaly.
