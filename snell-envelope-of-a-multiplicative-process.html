Snell envelope of a multiplicative process (Ut​=Yt​ct​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Snell envelope
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
Let Yt​=Y0​∏j=1t​Zj​, where Y0​>0 is deterministic and the positive factors are independent and integrable. Use the natural filtration. Its finite-horizon Snell envelope is
Ut​=Yt​ct​,cT​=1,ct​=max{1,ct+1​EZt+1​}.
(46)
Indeed, backward induction and independence of random variables give E[Ut+1​∣Ft​]=Yt​ct+1​EZt+1​; take the maximum with immediate exercise Yt​. The constants are finite and at least one. Equivalently, ct​ is the maximum of 1 and the successive products ∏j=t+1u​EZj​ for t<u≤T. If the factors have a common mean λ>1, waiting until maturity is optimal and ct​=λT−t. Integrability and independence of random variables from the current information are essential to the finite deterministic coefficient conclusion.
