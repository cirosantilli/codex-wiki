Surfactant transport with exchange relaxation (Dt​C=−C∇s​⋅us​−Cun​κ+Ds​Δs​C−k(C−C0​))

The material derivative of surface concentration combines tangential and normal area change, surface diffusion, and relaxation towards a reservoir concentration C0​. The rate k represents exchange; setting k=0 recovers transport without exchange. The concentration is measured per interfacial area, so stretching dilutes it.
