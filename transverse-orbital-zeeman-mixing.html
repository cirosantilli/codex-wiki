Transverse orbital Zeeman mixing (E±​=(Et​+E0​)/2±(Et​−E0​)2/4+(μB​B)2​)

 Home Physics Atomic physics Zeeman splitting
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For an axially split p multiplet with pz​ energy E0​ and equal px​,py​ energies Et​, a transverse x-directed magnetic field leaves px​ unchanged and couples py​,pz​ through the orbital angular momentum operator Lx​. The matrix element has modulus μB​B, giving the two energy eigenvalues in the title. For μB​B≪∣Et​−E0​∣, the orbital shifts are quadratic despite a linear Zeeman energy operator. A full quadratic-field calculation also includes the diamagnetic term; electron spin gives its own linear contribution.
