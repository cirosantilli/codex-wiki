Hyperbolic three-manifold (M=H3/Γ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological manifold 3-manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 89 Articles: 1
A complete hyperbolic three-manifold is a complete Riemannian manifold of dimension three with constant sectional curvature −1. Its universal cover is hyperbolic space H3 and its deck group acts freely and discretely by Riemannian isometries. In the orientable case it is a torsion-free Kleinian group.
