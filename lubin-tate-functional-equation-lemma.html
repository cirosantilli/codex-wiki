Lubin–Tate functional equation lemma

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Lubin–Tate formal group Lubin–Tate change of series
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
Given Lubin–Tate series f,g for π and a linear form L=∑ai​Xi​, there is a unique power series F≡L modulo terms of degree two satisfying
f(F(X1​,…,Xn​))=F(g(X1​),…,g(Xn​)).
(38)
Comparison degree by degree determines each homogeneous term; the Lubin–Tate congruences provide the required divisibility by π.
