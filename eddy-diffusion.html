Eddy diffusion (∂t​σ⊃D∇2σ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 510 Articles: 9
Mixing by turbulence is represented approximately by a diffusive flux down a mean concentration gradient. Its coefficient is the eddy diffusivity. A constant-coefficient model gives a D∇2σ term and a smoothing rate Dk2 for a Fourier mode. Effective diffusion can suppress slow gravitational concentration even when microscopic diffusion is negligible.
