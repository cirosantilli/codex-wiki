Cayley-Bacharach theorem

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Degree of an algebraic variety Degree of a projective curve Bézout's theorem Projective plane curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 226 Articles: 2
In its classical nine-point cubic form, the Cayley-Bacharach theorem says that a cubic through eight of the nine distinct intersections of two plane cubics must contain the ninth. The eight-point cubic completion for two triples of lines is the elementary case needed for triangular-strip propagation in incidence geometry. The phenomenon explains why interpolation conditions at such a complete intersection are dependent.
