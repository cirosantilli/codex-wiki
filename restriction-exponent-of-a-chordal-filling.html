Restriction exponent of a chordal filling (P(K∩A=∅)=ΦA′​(0)α)

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Schramm–Loewner evolution Conformal invariance of SLE Chordal restriction property
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A conformally invariant random chordal filling has exponent α when its avoidance probability for every admissible hull A is the displayed power, where ΦA​ fixes zero and has derivative one at infinity. Composition of two hull-removing maps and the chain rule make conditional image avoidance probabilities the same as unconditional ones. The avoidance probabilities determine a chordal filling law principle therefore implies the chordal restriction property. Independent unions followed by filling add exponents, since avoidance probabilities multiply.
