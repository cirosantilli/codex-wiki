Coplanar comet collision time

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planetary system dynamics Planetary scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
The geometric collision cross-section in a coplanar calculation is a length 2Rp​, rather than an area. For one comet with local surface probability Σc​ and relative velocity vrel​, the rare-event rate is 2Rp​Σc​vrel​. Without gravitational focusing, the resulting mean collision time scales as Q3/2Mp−1/3​ap​ for deep planet-crossing orbits at fixed stellar mass and planetary mass density.
