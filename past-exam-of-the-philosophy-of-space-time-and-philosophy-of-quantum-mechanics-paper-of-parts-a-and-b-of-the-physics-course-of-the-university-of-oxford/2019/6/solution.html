An observer-independent global presentism conflicts with the absence of a distinguished simultaneity relation in bare special relativity. It is not a formal contradiction to add a preferred foliation. The issue is what the proposed present contains, and whether that extra structure is part of the physical theory or an additional metaphysical commitment. Presentism must be distinguished from the harmless indexical fact that an observer calls their own current event “now”.
Let two spacetime events have coordinate differences Δt=0, Δx=L=0 in an inertial frame. A relatively moving frame assigns
Δt′=−γvL/c2.
(13)
They are simultaneous in one frame and not in another. For a fixed event P, each inertial four-velocity u defines a different simultaneous hyperplane through P by η(u,Q−P)=0. No particular u is selected by the Minkowski metric alone. Thus an extended objective present cannot both be the equal-time slice of one ordinary inertial frame and be invariant under all Lorentz transformations.
An equivocation can make the incompatibility seem stronger than it is. If one defines “real together” using one observer's simultaneity, then changes observers in the next inference, transitivity across the mixed relations has not been justified. It is the failure of transitivity for mixed-frame simultaneity. The physics supplies the frame-indexed relations; it does not supply a single absolute co-existence relation formed by silently joining all of them. A sound argument against relativistic presentism challenges the physical basis of its preferred relation, rather than proving all events real by that equivocation.
There are several coherent responses, with different costs. A preferred foliation can be appended to Minkowski spacetime, declaring one of its slices the objectively existing present. If it affects no physical process, it has no support from the relativistic laws and breaks the symmetry of the total ontology without changing observable predictions. If it affects matter, new laws must explain why ordinary tests display Lorentz invariance. This is additional structure, not a logical impossibility. Eternalism, by contrast, treats the complete spacetime history as real and makes simultaneity relational, but the relativity of coordinate time by itself does not deductively establish that metaphysics.
A local present containing only P is invariant once P is given, yet it abandons a spatially extended world existing now. The past light cone is invariant too, but contains events of different ages and is not an instantaneous spatial present. An observer-relative present is meaningful, but relativizes the existence claim rather than defending a unique global present. The defensible conclusion is therefore an incompatibility among bare relativistic structure, an objective extended present, and full symmetry of existence claims; it is not an unconditional proof that every formulation of presentism is incoherent.
