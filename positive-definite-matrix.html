Positive-definite matrix

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Quadratic form Definite matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 13
A real symmetric matrix A is positive definite when
xTAx>0
(7)
for every nonzero real vector x. It therefore defines the inner product ⟨x,y⟩A​=xTAy and is invertible.
