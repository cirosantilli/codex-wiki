Dirichlet character

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 27
A Dirichlet character modulo m is a homomorphism (Z/mZ)×→C×, extended periodically to integers by zero on numbers not coprime to m.
