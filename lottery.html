Lottery

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Utility function Expected utility
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 2
A probability distribution over possible outcomes. A finite lottery assigning probabilities pj​ to outcomes ωj​ has expected utility ∑j​pj​u(ωj​). Mixing lotteries mixes their outcome probabilities; the independence axiom for lottery preferences describes how this operation preserves preference comparisons.
