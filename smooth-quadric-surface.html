A smooth quadric surface is a smooth two-dimensional quadric hypersurface. Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic other than two, every smooth quadric surface in P3 becomes
V(x0​x3​−x1​x2​),
(7)
after a projective linear transformation. The Segre embedding identifies it with P1×P1.
