Gas slip correction to Stokes drag (k=6πηa/C)

OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The dimensionless factor C compares a corrected linear drag resistance with the continuum Stokes's law: dividing by C>1 reduces the resistance. The oil drop charge readout and calibration article explains why this matters for electric charge inference.
