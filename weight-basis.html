Weight basis

 ... Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Highest-weight representation Weight vector Weight space Weight-space decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A weight basis of a Lie algebra representation is a basis consisting of weight vectors for a chosen Cartan subalgebra. When the Cartan action is diagonalizable, choose a basis separately in every weight space and combine them. This makes weight multiplicities directly visible and gives diagonal matrices for Cartan elements.
