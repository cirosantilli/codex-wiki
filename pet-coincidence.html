PET coincidence

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Tomography Positron emission tomography
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A pair of nearly simultaneous detector events treated as a candidate two-photon positron annihilation. True unscattered pairs define a coincidence line of response. For attenuation along one such line, the complementary survival path integrals add to the full chord and are independent of the annihilation depth.
