Coercivity transfers strong minimality to L1 minimality (f(x,u,v)≥c∣v∣p−C,p>1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Calculus of variations Strong local minimizer of a variational integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
With fixed endpoint values on a finite interval, the displayed derivative coercivity makes a finite-energy strong local minimizer of a variational integral also a local minimizer in L1. Otherwise choose better competitors converging in L1. The upper energy bound controls their derivatives in Lp, and a common endpoint controls their function norms. Compact Sobolev embedding gives a uniformly convergent subsequence; its limit is the L1 limit. This contradicts strong local minimality. Convexity of the integrand is unnecessary for this neighborhood-transfer argument.
