Resonant argument

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Orbital resonance Mean-motion resonance
OurBigBook.com Words: 632 Articles: 12
A resonant argument is a slow angular combination whose libration diagnoses resonance. For an exterior j:(j−1) eccentric resonance it can be ϕ=jλ−(j−1)λp​−ϖ.
A resonant argument is an integer combination of orbital angles whose slow variation can diagnose mean-motion resonance. Its libration, rather than circulation through every angle, characterizes trapping in the resonance.
