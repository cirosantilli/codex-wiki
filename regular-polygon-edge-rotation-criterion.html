Regular polygon edge rotation criterion (ej+1​=R±2π/n​ej​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Regular polygon
OurBigBook.com Words: 160
For n≥3 cyclically ordered planar vertices, let ej​=vj+1​−vj​, with indices modulo n. They describe a convex regular polygon exactly when e0​=0 and there is a single choice of sign for which every successive edge vector is obtained by a rotation through 2π/n in that orientation. Necessity follows from equal sides and equal exterior angles. Conversely, the edge vectors have the form Rje0​. Their sum is zero because the corresponding geometric series runs through the n distinct roots of unity. Summing its partial series shows that the vertices are a translated and scaled copy of those roots, so they form a convex regular polygon. A larger turn such as 4π/5 can instead trace a star, which is why merely checking a constant turning angle is insufficient.
