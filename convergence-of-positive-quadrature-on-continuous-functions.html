Convergence of positive quadrature on continuous functions

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Numerical integration Quadrature rule
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
On a compact interval, positive quadrature rule weights whose sum is the interval length define uniformly bounded functionals on continuous functions. If the rules eventually integrate every fixed polynomial exactly, the Weierstrass approximation theorem proves convergence for every continuous function.
