Turbulent magnetic pumping (Epump​=γ×B)

 ... Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Dynamo action Mean-field dynamo Mean-field electromotive force
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
Turbulent magnetic pumping is an effective transport of the mean magnetic field produced by correlations in inhomogeneous or anisotropic turbulence. Its contribution to the mean-field electromotive force is Epump​=γ×B. In the mean induction equation this enters as ∇×(γ×B), the same algebraic form as advection by a velocity γ. Thus it supplements the mean material velocity without requiring an equal mean mass flow. Downward pumping can help retain or couple fields in a solar dynamo.
