The logarithm from the Euler product of an Artin L-function has first-prime-power term ∑χ(gp​)(Np)−s. Higher powers are uniformly bounded as real s↓1: ∣χ(g)∣≤χ(1) and ∑p​∑m≥2​(Np)−ms/m≤2∑p​(Np)−2<∞. The finitely many ramified local factors also have bounded logarithms. The Artin L-function order at one therefore proves the displayed estimate. Expanding a conjugacy-class indicator by character orthogonality leaves only the trivial character's logarithmic term, with coefficient ∣C∣/∣G∣. This proves Dirichlet density directly; a natural-density assertion needs additional analytic arguments.
