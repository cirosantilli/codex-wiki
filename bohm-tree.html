Böhm tree (BT(M))

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Computability theory Lambda calculus Untyped lambda calculus Head normal form
OurBigBook.com Words: 273 Articles: 4
The Böhm tree of an unsolvable lambda term is ⊥. If M→β∗​λx1​⋯xn​.yM1​⋯Mr​ is a head normal form, its Böhm tree has root label λx1​⋯xn​.y and ordered children BT(M1​),…,BT(Mr​). The tree may be infinite. Church-Rosser theorem and alpha equivalence make this independent of the selected head reduct.
