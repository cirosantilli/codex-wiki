Electrical resistivity (ρel​=1/σ)

 ... Electric charge Electric current Electrical resistance and conductance Electrical resistance Ohm's law Electrical conductivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 376 Articles: 7
Electrical resistivity is the inverse of scalar electrical conductivity in an isotropic Ohmic medium. In its rest frame, E=ρel​j, so the electric field does work per unit volume j⋅E=ρel​∣j∣2. In Gaussian units, finite conductivity gives magnetic diffusivity η=c2/(4πσ); neglecting this diffusion is a distinct approximation from neglecting electron inertia in Hall magnetohydrodynamics.
