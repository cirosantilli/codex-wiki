Perfect adaptation (A∗​(S)=A0​)

 Home Biology Signal transduction Signaling adaptation
OurBigBook.com Words: 83 Articles: 1
A signalling output returns to its original steady state after a persistent change of input. This concerns the final output, not absence of an initial response or invariance of the internal state. Robust perfect adaptation additionally requires that the return property persist over a range of parameters.
