Integrated circuit interconnect

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Integrated circuit
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
An integrated circuit interconnect is a conducting path joining circuit regions. In Robert Noyce's patent, deposited metal strips cross retained oxide on silicon; selected openings permit contact to the semiconductor while oxide prevents unwanted contact across p-n junctions. Figs. 3–5 combine detection, filtering and electronic amplifier functions using a transistor in one structure. The key is both connectivity and insulation: a metal path must touch its intended terminals while crossing other regions without shorting them.[ref]
