Spectral tensor (Cij​(k))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Power spectrum
OurBigBook.com Words: 108 Articles: 1
For a statistically homogeneous vector random field, its spectral tensor is the Fourier transform of the componentwise two-point correlation Cij​(r)=E[Bi​(x+r)Bj​(x)]. It distributes cross-correlations between components over wavevectors. With B(k)=∫B(r)e−ik⋅rddr, homogeneity gives E[Bi​(k)Bj​(k′)]=(2π)dδ(k+k′)Cij​(k), understood as an identity of distributions for an infinite stationary field.
