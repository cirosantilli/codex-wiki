Borel functional calculus for a normal operator

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra C-star algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 224 Articles: 3
For a normal operator T with spectrum K, there is a contractive unital star-homomorphism
Ψ:L∞(K)⟶B(H),Ψ(z)=T,
(9)
where L∞(K) denotes the bounded Borel functions. It is obtained by integrating against the projection-valued spectral measure of T.
