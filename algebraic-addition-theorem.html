Algebraic addition theorem

 ... Analysis Complex analysis Isolated singularity Classification of isolated singularities Pole Meromorphic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 1
A meromorphic function f has an algebraic addition theorem if some nonzero polynomial P∈C[U,V,W] satisfies P(f(z),f(w),f(z+w))=0 wherever the three values are finite. For a polynomial f of degree d>0, take roots zi​ of f(z)−U and wj​ of f(w)−V; the product ∏i,j​(W−f(zi​+wj​)) has polynomial coefficients in U,V by the Fundamental theorem of symmetric polynomials, is monic in W, and supplies an addition relation. An elliptic function also has such a relation, by finite algebraic extension of its function field and the Weierstrass elliptic function addition formula.
