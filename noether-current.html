Noether current (jμ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 25
A continuous variational symmetry gives a current satisfying ∂μ​jμ=0 on shell. Integrating j0 over space gives a conserved charge when boundary flux vanishes.
