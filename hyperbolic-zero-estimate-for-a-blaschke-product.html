Hyperbolic zero estimate for a Blaschke product (∣B(w)∣≤exp[−2∑n​e−ρ(w,an​)])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Holomorphic function Blaschke product
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
Use curvature-minus-one Hyperbolic distance in the Poincare disc. Each Blaschke factor has modulus tanh(ρ(w,a)/2)=(1−t)/(1+t), with t=e−ρ(w,a). For 0≤t<1, differentiating shows log[(1+t)/(1−t)]≥2t. Sum this logarithmic inequality over the zero factors and pass to the Blaschke product by locally uniform convergence. At a prescribed zero, its modulus vanishes and the bound is immediate. All sums count multiplicities.
