After diffusion arrest of Gaussian packet compression, define the logarithmic area increase At​=21​log[detJ(t)/detJ(0)]. It is nonnegative. Assume At​/t has a large deviation principle with convex rate function I, minimized at λ>0. The exact amplitude relation f(t)=f(0)e−At​ and the Laplace principle for probability measures give the displayed moment decay rate. The rate function refers to area, so rare width fluctuations are included rather than discarded without justification. For differentiable strictly convex I, the interior minimizer satisfies I′(sμ​)=−μ. If μ∗​=−I′(0) is finite, then γμ​=I(0) for μ≥μ∗​: rare nearly unstretched packets dominate high moments. Below the threshold, d(γμ​/μ)/dμ=−I(sμ​)/μ2<0. These statements concern a positive Gaussian packet with flow-only averaging; a homogeneous signed random initial scalar requires a different averaging kernel. Arbitrary random C(t) alone does not imply this statistical model.
