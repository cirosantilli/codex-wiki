Magnetic domain

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Bound charge and bound current Magnetization Ferromagnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 245 Articles: 4
A magnetic domain is a region sharing an ordered magnetic orientation. Different magnetic domains can reduce the total magnetic moment without erasing the local magnetic order. A zero total magnetization therefore does not by itself establish antiferromagnetism.
