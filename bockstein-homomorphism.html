Bockstein homomorphism (β)

 ... Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Chain complex Short exact sequence of chain complexes Long exact sequence in homology Connecting homomorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 465 Articles: 11
The Bockstein homomorphism is the connecting homomorphism associated with a short exact sequence of coefficient groups. For 0→Z/n→Z/n2→Z/n→0, it has degree −1 on homology and degree +1 on cohomology.
