Simultaneous all-pay contests

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Contest theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 288 Articles: 3
Players choose efforts in several all-pay contests at the same time. With additive quasilinear utility and no shared effort budget, a player's expected payoff is the sum of its per-contest payoffs. An entry restriction couples the choices of contests even though the subsequent effort optimization is separable.
