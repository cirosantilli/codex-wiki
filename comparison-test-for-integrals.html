Comparison test for integrals

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
If measurable functions satisfy ∣f∣≤g almost everywhere and g is nonnegative and Lebesgue integrable, then f is Lebesgue integrable and ∫∣f∣≤∫g. This follows from monotonicity of the Lebesgue integral. Conversely, 0≤g≤∣f∣ with ∫g=∞ rules out Lebesgue integrability of f. For nonnegative improper integrals, the same comparison applies after taking increasing finite-interval limits.
