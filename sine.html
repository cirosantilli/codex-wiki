Sine (sin)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Trigonometric function
OurBigBook.com Words: 204 Articles: 2
For a real angle θ, sinθ is the vertical coordinate on the unit circle reached by a counterclockwise rotation through θ radians. For an acute angle in a right triangle, it is the opposite side divided by the hypotenuse. In particular,
sin0=0,sin(π/6)=21​,sin(π/2)=1,sinπ=0.
(67)
The first-quarter rise and second-quarter fall are visible in the circle projection; the lower semicircle gives negative values.
Reflecting the unit circle across the horizontal axis gives sin(−θ)=−sinθ, so sine is an odd function. A full turn gives sin(θ+2π)=sinθ, making it a periodic function. For real arguments, its range is [−1,1] and its zeros of a function occur at θ=kπ for integers k. The cosine describes the perpendicular coordinate of the same point, and the Pythagorean trigonometric identity keeps their two values tied together.
