Spin separation at a smooth quantum Hall edge (Δy≃EZ​/∣U′∣)

 ... Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force Hall effect Quantum Hall effect Integer quantum Hall effect Quantum Hall edge state
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For locally linear electron confinement potential energy U(y), two Zeeman splitting branches cross the same Fermi energy at positions differing by the energy splitting divided by ∣U′∣. The expression neglects self-consistent electrostatic screening and exchange interaction enhancement. If the quoted slope is an electrostatic voltage gradient instead of an electron-energy gradient, its magnitude must be multiplied by the elementary charge. A screened compressible quantum Hall edge strip is not a single sharp crossing with an imposed bare slope.
