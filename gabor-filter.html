Gabor filter

 Home Biology Neuroscience Computational neuroscience
OurBigBook.com Words: 126
A Gabor filter has a kernel formed by multiplying a Gaussian function by a sinusoid or a complex oscillation. In two-dimensional image processing, its orientation and spatial frequency select local image structure; evaluating the kernel's translated dot product with an image is spatial filtering. The real even example under signed spatial filter model of a simple cell uses a cosine.
There is experimental motivation for this family without making every simple cell an exact Gabor filter. Jones and Palmer fitted two-dimensional Gabor filters to measured spatial and spectral response profiles of simple cells in cat cortex and compared independently estimated parameters as a test of spatial linearity. Their reported residuals supported the approximation in that dataset. The chosen parameters and orientation tuning curve in this article are not extracted from their measurements. [ref]
