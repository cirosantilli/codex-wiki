Averaged operator (T=(1−α)I+αR)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Monotone operator Nonexpansive mapping
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 2
An operator is α-averaged for 0<α<1 when T=(1−α)I+αR for a nonexpansive mapping R. Averaging controls the update residual and enables fixed-point iteration. A firmly nonexpansive mapping is exactly a 1/2-averaged operator.
