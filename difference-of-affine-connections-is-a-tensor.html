Difference of affine connections is a tensor (Δ(X,Y)=∇X​Y−∇X​Y)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle) Affine connection
OurBigBook.com Words: 173 Articles: 2
The difference of two affine connections is a smooth tensor field of type (1,2). Linearity over smooth functions holds in the first slot by the connection axioms. In the second slot, the two extra terms X(f)Y cancel, so Δ(X,fY)=fΔ(X,Y). This tensoriality makes the value depend only on Xp​,Yp​. In coordinates its components are the differences of the Christoffel symbols, although either collection of connection coefficients individually is not a tensor.
