Drift velocity (vd​)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Current density Charge carrier
OurBigBook.com Words: 165 Articles: 2
The drift velocity is a charge carrier population's mean velocity relative to its material's rest frame. It describes the net transport superposed on microscopic motion. For a single species, current density is J=nqvd​; for electrons, q=−e reverses the direction. It is distinct from the propagation speed of an electrical disturbance. [ref].
