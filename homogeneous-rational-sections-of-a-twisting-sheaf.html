Homogeneous rational sections of a twisting sheaf (Γ(U,O(m))={r:degr=m, r regular on π−1(U)})

The local frame Xim​ on Xi​=0 identifies a section with a homogeneous rational function of degree m on the punctured affine cone. Homogenizing a local rational expression and cancelling common factors writes every nonzero section as a quotient of coprime homogeneous polynomials F/G with degF−degG=m. Conversely, such a rational function regular on the cone gives local coefficients r/Xim​, which glue with the twisting sheaf's transition functions of a vector bundle.
