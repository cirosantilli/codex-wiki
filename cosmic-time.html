Cosmic time

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
Cosmic time is the proper time measured by observers comoving with the homogeneous cosmological fluid. Constant-cosmic-time hypersurfaces are the homogeneous spatial slices of an FLRW spacetime.
