Unpolarized light

 Home Physics Wave Polarization (waves)
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Unpolarized light has no preferred transverse polarization direction in its time-averaged statistics. An ideal linear polarizer transmits half its incident light intensity: equal transverse components give the averaged projection factor ⟨cos2θ⟩=1/2. This statistical description differs from the fixed deterministic phase relation of a monochromatic wave in a pure polarization of waves state.
