Double-slit experiment

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Interference (wave propagation)
OurBigBook.com Words: 520 Articles: 9
The double-slit experiment compares propagation through two openings. Coherent illumination gives double-slit interference; independent incoherent source patches contribute light intensities whose displaced patterns can reduce the observed interferometric visibility.
