Casimir energy of a two-dimensional conformal field theory (E0​=−c/12)

 ... String theory Gauge-gravity duality AdS/CFT correspondence Holographic dictionary Holographic entanglement entropy Brown--Henneaux central charge
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
For a unit spatial circle, the vacuum energy of a two-dimensional conformal field theory is E0​=−c/12. It arises from the anomalous Schwarzian shift when the plane vacuum is mapped to the cylinder and is dual to the renormalized energy of global AdS3​.
