p-ramified Iwasawa module (X=Gal(M∞​/F∞​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Iwasawa theory Iwasawa module
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 17
Let M∞​ be the maximal abelian pro-p extension of F∞​ unramified away from primes over p. Its Galois group is the p-ramified Iwasawa module. In the cyclotomic tower of Q(μp​) for odd p, its Iwasawa-module rank is (p−1)/2. It is therefore quite different from the torsion unramified Iwasawa module.
