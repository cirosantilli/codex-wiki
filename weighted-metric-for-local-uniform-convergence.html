Weighted metric for local uniform convergence (σ(f,g)=∑n​wn​ϕ(∥f−g∥Kn​​))

 ... Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Uniform convergence Locally uniform convergence Compact-open topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For a compact exhaustion Kn​ and positive summable weights wn​, use the bounded metric transform ϕ(s)=s/(1+s) on each compact supremum norm. The resulting series defines a metric on continuous functions and realizes local uniform convergence on compact subsets. The triangle inequality is termwise. For convergence, control finitely many compact seminorms and then the uniformly bounded weight tail; conversely a vanishing metric controls each fixed seminorm.
