Transfer matrix (T)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A transfer matrix stores the weights of allowed transitions between finitely many boundary states. Its powers sum weights of compatible sequences of transitions. For a cyclic boundary condition, the matrix trace tr(Tn) counts weighted closed sequences of length n.
