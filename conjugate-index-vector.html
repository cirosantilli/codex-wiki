Conjugate index vector (∣ϕB∗​⟩=∑j​cj∗​∣j⟩B​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Pure state Relative state of a bipartite vector Index state
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
Fix paired orthonormal bases and let ∣ϕ⟩A​=∑j​cj​∣j⟩A​. Its conjugate index vector in the other Hilbert space uses the conjugated coefficients. For the unnormalized maximally entangled state ∣Ψ⟩=∑j​∣j⟩A​∣j⟩B​, the partial inner product is (I⊗⟨ϕB∗​∣)∣Ψ⟩=∣ϕ⟩A​. Both the conjugation and the lack of normalization of Ψ matter. Applying a linear operator on the first subsystem gives the corresponding relative state of the operated vector.
