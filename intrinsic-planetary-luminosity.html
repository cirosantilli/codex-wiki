Intrinsic planetary luminosity (Lint​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar evolution Kelvin-Helmholtz mechanism
OurBigBook.com Words: 201 Articles: 2
The intrinsic planetary luminosity Lint​ is the power supplied by interior cooling, contraction, nuclear reactions if present, or internal heating, excluding reflected or passively reradiated stellar light. For fixed mass and no accretion or nuclear source, global conservation of energy gives Lint​=Qdeep​−d(U+Ω)/dt, where Qdeep​ is any additional power deposited in the interior, such as tidal heating or Joule heating. For Qdeep​=0 and a gas-supported quasi-static sphere of fixed specific-heat ratio γ and negligible surface pressure, the stellar virial theorem gives Ω=−3(γ−1)U, hence
Lint​=−3(γ−1)3γ−4​dtdΩ​.
(5)
For a dynamically stable gas-supported model take γ>4/3. For γ=5/3 this is half the gravitational release. Degenerate giants require a different internal energy reservoir; the half-factor is not universal.
