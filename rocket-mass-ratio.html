Rocket mass ratio (R=Minitial​/Mfinal​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Momentum Rocket equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The rocket mass ratio compares the initial and final total rocket masses for a burn. It is dimensionless, and the ideal rocket equation becomes Δv=ulnR. For the same fixed rocket exhaust speed, two successive burns with rocket mass ratios R1​ and R2​ have combined rocket mass ratio R1​R2​ and total velocity gain ulnR1​+ulnR2​.
