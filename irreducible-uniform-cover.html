Irreducible uniform cover

 ... Area of mathematics Combinatorics Geometric combinatorics Euclidean body Coordinate projection of a Euclidean body Uniform cover
OurBigBook.com Words: 71 Articles: 1
A uniform cover is irreducible when it is not the disjoint union, as a multiset, of two nonempty uniform covers. There are only finitely many irreducible uniform covers of a fixed finite set: their multiplicity vectors form an antichain in N2n, and Dickson lemma forbids an infinite antichain there.
