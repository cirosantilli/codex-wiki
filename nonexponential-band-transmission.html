Nonexponential band transmission (−d2logT/dm2=−Varm​(κ))

OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A finite-band transmittance T=⟨e−mκν​⟩ averages different monochromatic attenuation rates. Its effective attenuation aeff​=−dlogT/dm obeys aeff′​=−Varm​(κ)≤0, where the weighting is proportional to the transmitted spectrum. Strongly absorbing parts saturate first, so surviving radiation samples weaker absorption. Jensen's inequality gives T≥e−m⟨κ⟩. Equality throughout requires a spectrally constant coefficient.
