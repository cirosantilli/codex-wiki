Ultrarelativistic magnetic bending radius

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
For motion perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field, the Lorentz force gives r=p/(∣q∣B). An ultrarelativistic massive particle has pc≃E, hence r≃E/(∣q∣Bc). Total energy and kinetic energy differ by the rest energy.
