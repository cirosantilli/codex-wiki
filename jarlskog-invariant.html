Jarlskog invariant (J)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 140
The Jarlskog invariant is a measure of CP violation that survives quantum field rephasings. Fix its sign by the quartet
J=Im(Vud​Vcs​Vus∗​Vcd∗​).
(58)
For the exact three-generation CKM matrix, direct multiplication gives
J=c12​c23​c132​s12​s23​s13​sinδ.
(59)
The CKM unitarity triangle's illustrative parameters give J=8999155​/3200000000≃2.085597×10−4. Setting s13​=0 or δ=0 makes J=0: the existence of three quark generations is necessary for this mechanism, but not sufficient for CP violation.
To see the invariance directly, the phases multiplying Vud​Vcs​Vus∗​Vcd∗​ sum to
(−αu​+βd​)+(−αc​+βs​)+(αu​−βs​)+(αc​−βd​)=0.
(60)
Under CP, the CKM matrix is complex conjugated and J changes sign. In an exactly unitary three-generation CKM matrix, quartets formed from any distinct pair of rows and columns have imaginary parts equal to J up to sign. This follows by multiplying a row orthogonality relation by a suitable pair of entries and taking its imaginary part: its diagonal product is real, forcing the two remaining imaginary parts to cancel. The same Jarlskog invariant therefore determines the areas of all six unscaled CKM unitarity triangles.
