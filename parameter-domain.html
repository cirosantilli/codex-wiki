Parameter domain (r:U→Rn)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Parametric equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The domain of a function U used by a parametrization r is its parameter domain. A parametrized surface may require several such domains, and duplicated images must be accounted for in a surface integral.
