Weak logarithmic zero-free region for the Riemann zeta function (∣1/ζ(σ+it)∣≪log7(∣t∣+2),σ≥1−c/log9(∣t∣+2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 57
The logarithm of the Euler product and 3+4cosθ+cos2θ≥0 give ζ(σ)3∣ζ(σ+it)∣4∣ζ(σ+2it)∣≥1. At σ=1+a/log9∣t∣, upper bounds ζ(σ)≪log9∣t∣/a and ∣ζ(σ+2it)∣≪log∣t∣ imply the lower bound ∣ζ∣≫a3/4log−7∣t∣. The Cauchy estimate for derivatives gives ∣ζ′∣≪log2∣t∣ nearby, allowing a sufficiently small leftward displacement of order log−9∣t∣. This elementary region is weaker than the classical logarithmic region but gives an explicit reciprocal bound by simple estimates.
