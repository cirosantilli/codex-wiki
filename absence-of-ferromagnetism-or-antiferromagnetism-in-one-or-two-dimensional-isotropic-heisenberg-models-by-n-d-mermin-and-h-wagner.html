Absence of Ferromagnetism or Antiferromagnetism in One- or Two-Dimensional Isotropic Heisenberg Models by N. D. Mermin and H. Wagner (1966)

 Home Physics Physics bibliography
OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by H. Wagner, Work by N. D. Mermin Words: 43
Physical Review Letters 17, 1133 (1966), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.17.1133. The abstract states the absence of ferromagnetic or antiferromagnetic order at nonzero temperature in finite-range one- or two-dimensional isotropic spin-S Heisenberg models. This is a primary source for the low-dimensional limitation of mean-field magnetic ordering.
doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.17.1133
