Two-pair component of polarization-entangled down-conversion (∣Ψ2​⟩=(K†)2∣0⟩/(23​))

For two collected spatial modes a,b, each with polarizations H,V, put K†=aH†​bV†​+aV†​bH†​. The one-pair state is a Bell state. The normalized two-pair component is
∣Ψ2​⟩=3​∣2Ha​,2Vb​⟩+∣Ha​Va​,Hb​Vb​⟩+∣2Va​,2Hb​⟩​.
(57)
This follows by expanding (K†)2 and using the bosonic creation operator identity (a†)n∣0⟩=n!​∣n⟩. The three orthogonal normalized Fock states have equal probability 1/3. A tensor product of independently labelled pairs is a different multimode description; identifying its labels without renormalization changes its norm.
