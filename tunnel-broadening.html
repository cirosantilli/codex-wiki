Tunnel broadening (ℏΓ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Potential barrier Quantum tunnelling
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
Finite lifetime of an island state due to tunnelling into leads gives an energy-width scale of order ℏΓ. The numerical factor depends on whether Γ denotes a population decay rate, an amplitude decay rate or a sum of partial rates.
