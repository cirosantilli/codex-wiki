Pressure similarity in a spherical blast wave (p=E2/5ρ03/5​t−6/5fp​(ξ))

OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For a spherical self-similar blast wave, put ℓ=(Et2/ρ0​)1/5, ξ=r/ℓ, ρ=ρ0​fρ​(ξ) and u=(r/t)fu​(ξ). The radial Euler equation then requires p=ρ0​(ℓ/t)2fp​(ξ), where
fp′​=−ξfρ​[fu2​−fu​+ξ(fu​−52​)fu′​].
(2)
This follows from ∂t​ξ=−2ξ/(5t) and ∂r​ξ=1/ℓ. The momentum equation alone leaves a spatially uniform additive pressure undetermined. The scale-free strong-shock boundary data fix that freedom consistently with the displayed similarity scaling; an independently prescribed ambient pressure introduces another scale.
