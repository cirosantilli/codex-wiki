Iterated integral of a path (∫s<u1​<⋯<uk​<t​dxu1​​⊗⋯⊗dxuk​​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Rough path Signature of a bounded variation path
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 1
For a continuous path of bounded variation, integrate successively over the ordered simplex using Riemann-Stieltjes integrals. These integrals form the levels of its path signature. Appending the final integration variable gives the differential recursion dSt(k)​=St(k−1)​⊗dxt​.
