Catenary minimizes the integral of height along a fixed-length curve (min∫yds)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Euler-Lagrange equation Catenary
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
Among curves of fixed arc length L>2a joining (−a,0) to (a,0), the hanging catenary globally minimizes ∫yds. Parametrize a competitor by arc length s∈[−L/2,L/2], so x′2+y′2=1. Integration by parts and the fixed horizontal endpoint separation give, for every b>0,
∫−L/2L/2​yds=−∫−L/2L/2​sy′ds=2ab−∫−L/2L/2​(bx′+sy′)ds≥2ab−∫−L/2L/2​b2+s2​ds.
(104)
The inequality is the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality. Equality requires x′=b/b2+s2​ and y′=s/b2+s2​. Integration gives the catenary
y=b[cosh(x/b)−cosh(a/b)],L=2bsinh(a/b).
(105)
The last equation has a unique positive b, since sinhq/q increases strictly for q>0. The equality curve is a single-valued graph below the chord, so the same global bound proves the result under those additional restrictions.
