Ten-dimensional super Yang-Mills theory (N=1 in 10D⟶N=4 in 4D)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supersymmetric gauge theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
Minimal ten-dimensional supersymmetric gauge theory contains a gauge field and an adjoint Majorana-Weyl spinor. They have eight propagating bosonic and eight propagating fermionic states per gauge generator and sixteen real supercharges. A toroidal zero-mode dimensional reduction to four dimensions gives one gauge field, six real adjoint scalar fields, and four adjoint Weyl spinors: a four-dimensional N=4 super Yang-Mills theory. The internal field strength Fmn​=−ig[Xm​,Xn​] gives the nonnegative scalar potential V=4g2​∑m,n​∥[Xm​,Xn​]∥2, where the norm uses a positive invariant inner product on the Lie algebra. Its minima are commuting tuples of adjoint scalar fields.
