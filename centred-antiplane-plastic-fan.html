Centred antiplane plastic fan

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Plasticity (physics) Perfect plasticity Antiplane perfect plasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
A centred fan consists of straight characteristic curves emanating from one point. Stress and antiplane velocity are constant on each ray and depend on the polar angle ϕ. The radial tangent condition is (cosϕ,sinϕ)∥(−fb​,fa​), so fa​cosϕ+fb​sinϕ=0. A boundary ray carrying a traction-free crack-face stress b=0 has slope tanϕb​=−fa​/fb​, interpreted as vertical when fb​=0. For the elliptic yield condition a2/A2+b2/B2=1, a forward fan has a=−A2sinϕ/H, b=B2cosϕ/H, H=(A2sin2ϕ+B2cos2ϕ)1/2. The boundary rays are vertical; the adjacent rear regions have constant stresses (∓A,0). Velocity is a function of angle whose magnitude requires further loading data.
