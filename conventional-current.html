Conventional current

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
Conventional current uses the direction in which positive electric charge flows. A moving negative electric charge contributes electric current in the opposite direction to its velocity. Thus electrons drifting left produce conventional current to the right; the convention describes signed electric charge transport, without requiring positive charge carriers. [ref].
