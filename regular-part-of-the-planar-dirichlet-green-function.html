For the canonical Dirichlet Green function with logarithmic singularity, its harmonic correction satisfies Gz​(z)=−logcradD​(z). If ϕ maps the unit disc to D with ϕ(0)=z, then
Gz​(ϕ(w))=−log​wϕ(w)−ϕ(0)​​.
(120)
The quotient extends at zero to ϕ′(0). On unbounded domains the canonical Green function, rather than boundary values alone, fixes the harmonic correction.
