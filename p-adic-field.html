p-adic field ([K:Qp​]<∞)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 1
A p-adic field is a finite extension of the P-adic number field Qp​, equipped with its extended p-adic absolute value. Conversely every characteristic-zero, nontrivially valued, non-Archimedean locally compact field is such an extension. A finite quotient O/pO supplies finitely many generators of O over Zp​ by repeated reduction and completeness.
