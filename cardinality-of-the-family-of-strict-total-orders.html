Cardinality of the family of strict total orders (∣LO(κ)∣=2κ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cardinal number Cardinal arithmetic
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For an infinite well-orderable cardinal number κ, each strict total order is a subset of κ2, giving at most 2κ possibilities by the square of an infinite cardinal. For a lower bound, order κ two-element blocks by the ordinal index and independently reverse each block according to a subset of κ. Transport along κ×2≅κ. Distinct subsets give distinct orders. The usual axiom of choice development supplies these product identities; for well-orderable cardinals they are already provable in ZF.
