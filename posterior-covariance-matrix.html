Posterior covariance matrix (Σy​=E[(θ−my​)(θ−my​)T∣y])

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Bayesian posterior Posterior variance
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For a vector parameter with finite second moments and posterior mean my​, the posterior covariance matrix records its uncertainty and linear dependence after conditioning on the observations. Its diagonal entries are posterior variances. In a regular locally flat-prior normal distribution approximation it is the inverse observed information. A quadratic form has posterior expectation equal to the form at the posterior mean plus the trace of its matrix times this covariance.
