Zeeman slower resonance tracking (δeff​=ωL​+kv−ω0​−ζB(z))

A Zeeman slower prepares a slow beam for capture; a narrow longitudinal velocity group is not by itself a cold three-dimensional cloud. The historical apparatus measured slowed-beam velocities with a separate fluorescence probe scanned in frequency, just after switching off the slowing beam. This avoided simultaneous-beam complications.[ref]
For an original resonance-tracking calculation, let atoms travel along +z with v>0 and the slowing beam along −z, so its atom-frame angular frequency is ωL​+kv. Approximate the selected cycling transition by ωeg​(B)=ω0​+ζB on one field branch; ζ is its signed angular frequency shift per unit magnetic field. A multilevel atom may require a nonlinear ωeg​(B) outside that branch. Maintaining a chosen effective laser detuning δs​ requires
B(z)=ζωL​−ω0​+kv(z)−δs​​.
(59)
For chosen constant deceleration magnitude a>0, vdv/dz=−a gives v(z)=vi2​−2az​ and length L=(vi2​−vf2​)/(2a). Substitution supplies a square-root magnetic field profile, plus a constant offset. The signs follow from the specified directions; reversing the selected transition's magnetic slope reverses the required field variation.
As a hypothetical design, take vi​=300 ms−1, vf​=30 ms−1 and a=5.0×104 ms−2. The required length is 0.891 m and flight time (vi​−vf​)/a=5.4 ms. For chosen λ=589 nm and m=3.82×10−26 kg, photon recoil velocity ℏk/m≃0.0294 ms−1 implies roughly 9.17×103 directed absorption events. The mean required scattering rate is about 1.70×106 s−1. This budget must lie below the available scattering rate, with margin for capture and intensity variation. Optical pumping into unwanted hyperfine structure or magnetic sublevels can interrupt the cycle. Longitudinal slowing, transverse recoil heating and final cloud temperature are separate quantities.
