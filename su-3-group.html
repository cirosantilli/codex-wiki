SU(3) group (SU(3))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Unitary group Special unitary group
OurBigBook.com Words: 486 Articles: 6
The SU(3) group consists of unitary complex three-by-three matrices of determinant one. Its defining action on C3 preserves the Hermitian norm. It contains block-diagonal copies of SU(2) and the real SO(3) group. Its Lie algebra consists of traceless anti-Hermitian matrices.
