Rayleigh scattering wavelength dependence (σ∝λ−4)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic radiation Rayleigh scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 142
For a fixed sphere and fixed refractive index, both the Rayleigh scattering cross-section and scattering efficiency are proportional to λ−4. Halving wavelength multiplies either by 16, as long as the shorter wavelength still satisfies ka≪1. If optical dispersion changes m, the material factor changes too; the pure fourth-power comparison then needs correction.
For equal incident light intensities at blue wavelength 450nm and red wavelength 650nm, the same toy sphere gives
σ(650nm)σ(450nm)​=(450650​)4=656128561​≃4.35315.
(27)
Blue light therefore produces about 4.35 times the scattered power. This compares the particle's response at two wavelengths; it does not assert that an actual sky has this ratio of observed blue and red light intensities.
