Hurewicz homomorphism (hn​:πn​(X)→Hn​(X;Z))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Homotopy group Hurewicz theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
A sphere map f:Sn→X is sent to f∗​[Sn]∈Hn​(X;Z). After tensoring with Q this gives the rational Hurewicz homomorphism. It is natural in X, sends Whitehead products to zero, and is an isomorphism in the first nonzero homotopy degree of a simply connected space by the Hurewicz theorem.
