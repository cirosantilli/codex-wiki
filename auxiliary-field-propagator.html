Auxiliary-field propagator (Dσ​(p)=−i/a)

 ... Supersymmetry Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry Superspace Superfield Chiral superfield Auxiliary field
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For a real auxiliary field with quadratic Lagrangian density −aσ2/2, a=0, the Feynman propagator is the inverse quadratic kernel multiplied by i: Dσ​(p)=−i/a. It is independent of momentum; in position space it is the contact distribution −iδ(d)(x−y)/a. Two interaction vertices ig joined by this auxiliary-field propagator give ig2/a. Completing the square in −aσ2/2+gσB produces g2B2/(2a), proving the equivalence to a local interaction by a Gaussian integral.
