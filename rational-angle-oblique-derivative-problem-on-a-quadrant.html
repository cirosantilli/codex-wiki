Rational-angle oblique derivative problem on a quadrant (β1​+β2​=nπ/2)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Boundary value problem Oblique derivative boundary condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 298 Articles: 3
For a complex-valued harmonic function in the first quadrant, write Φ=qz​ and Ψ(zˉ)=qzˉ​ as independent holomorphic functions. Prescribe i(eiβ1​Φ−e−iβ1​Ψ)=g1​ on the imaginary axis and e−iβ2​Φ+eiβ2​Ψ=g2​ on the real axis. If β1​+β2​=nπ/2, set ϵ=(−1)n. Reflection into four quadrants produces a scalar Riemann-Hilbert problem with parity H(−z)=−ϵH(z) and known jumps. Provided its corner and large-arc contributions vanish, the Cauchy integral formula gives
Φ(z)=2πi1​[eiβ2​∫0∞​g2​(s)(s−z1​−s+zϵ​)ds−e−iβ1​∫0∞​g1​(s)(is−z1​−is+zϵ​)ds].
(22)
Complex boundary data do not justify replacing qzˉ​ by the conjugate of qz​.
