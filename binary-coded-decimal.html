Binary-coded decimal

 Home Electrical engineering Digital electronics
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
Represent a decimal number by coding each decimal digit separately as four bits of weights 8,4,2,1. The codes 10–15 are unused for one digit; a Boolean circuit must specify whether these combinations produce a defined result or are don't-care conditions.
