Cluster baryon-fraction estimate of matter density (Ωm​=Ωb​/fb,cluster​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Critical density Cosmological density parameter Cosmic baryon fraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
If a measured cluster baryon fraction represents the cosmic baryon fraction Ωb​/Ωm​, combining it with the cosmological baryon density inferred from Big Bang nucleosynthesis gives the displayed estimate. Gas and stellar baryons must both be counted, and all distance or Hubble constant dependence must be consistent. Baryon depletion or feedback modifies the identification: with fb,cluster​=bΩb​/Ωm​, the corrected answer is Ωm​=bΩb​/fb,cluster​. The method measures total matter rather than vacuum or radiation density.
