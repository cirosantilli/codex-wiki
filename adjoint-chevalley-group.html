Adjoint Chevalley group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Linear algebraic group
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 1
Fix a Chevalley basis and its integral lattice. The adjoint Chevalley group over a field is generated by the root operators obtained from the integral divided-power polynomials xα​(t)=∑j​tj(adeα​)j/j! on that lattice. The coefficients are first computed over the integers and then reduced to the field; factorials need not be invertible there. This is the adjoint construction; other weight lattices give other isogeny forms of groups of Lie type.
