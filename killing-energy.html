Killing energy (E=−Ka​ua)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Killing vector field Geodesic conserved quantity from a Killing vector
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
For a geodesic tangent u and a stationary Killing vector field K, the Killing energy is E=−g(K,u). It is conserved along the geodesic. When K is future timelike and u is future causal, E>0 for a nonzero tangent; it need not be positive where K becomes spacelike.
