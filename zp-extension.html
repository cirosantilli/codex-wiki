Zp-extension (Gal(F∞​/F)≅Zp​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Iwasawa theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 237 Articles: 3
A Zp-extension is an infinite Galois extension of a number field whose Galois group is topologically isomorphic to the additive p-adic integers. There is a unique intermediate field of degree pn for every n≥0, corresponding to pnZp​. The finite layers form a tower with cyclic successive degree p extensions.
