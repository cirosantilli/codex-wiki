Minimum reachable label propagation

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Directed graph Strongly connected component Condensation graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
Assign each vertex of a finite directed graph a label in a total order. To find the least label reachable from each vertex, first compress its strongly connected components. Give each component its own least label, then process the condensation graph in reverse topological order, replacing each label by the minimum of itself and its successors' labels. Every reachable component is either the current one or reached through a first outgoing edge; induction over this order proves the recurrence. The graph-processing work is O(∣V∣+∣E∣) with constant-cost label comparisons. Store a representative vertex as well when a witness is required.
