Gaussian forward-rate field (df(t,T)=α(t,T)dt+⟨σ(t,T),dWt​⟩)

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fixed-income security Interest rate Heath-Jarrow-Morton model
OurBigBook.com Words: 462 Articles: 5
With deterministic Hilbert-space-valued volatility, a deterministic initial curve and the Heath-Jarrow-Morton model drift restriction, the instantaneous forward rate is a Gaussian random field indexed by observation time and maturity. Put Σ(t,T)=∫tT​σ(t,u)du. Absence of arbitrage requires α(t,T)=⟨σ(t,T),Σ(t,T)⟩. Indeed, Itô formula gives bond drift rt​−∫tT​α(t,u)du+∥Σ(t,T)∥2/2; equating it to the short rate and differentiating in maturity yields this restriction. The field covariance is
Cov(f(t,T),f(s,U))=∫0min(t,s)​⟨σ(v,T),σ(v,U)⟩dv.
(18)
Finite-dimensional volatility spaces give finite-factor models; an infinite-dimensional space permits much richer maturity correlations. Gaussian rates can be negative. Deterministic volatility gives explicit zero-coupon bond and bond-option formulas.
