Standard gravitational parameter (μ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field
OurBigBook.com Words: 118
The product μ=GM of the gravitational constant and a central mass sets the strength of the exterior Newtonian gravitational field. Its dimensions are length cubed divided by time squared. In the exact relative two-body problem, the parameter is G(M1​+M2​); using GM for one source assumes the test particle approximation.
The combination is useful because orbital and falling-body observations constrain μ directly. A gravitational circular orbit of radius a has oscillation period T=2πa3/μ​, so measuring a and T gives μ=4π2a3/T2. That measurement alone does not separately determine G and M. Similarly, radial free fall from rest has a natural time scale r03​/μ​.
Other interactions require a different model. Inverse-square radiation pressure can reduce the effective attraction for a dust Kepler orbit; calling that effective coefficient a gravitational parameter must not hide the added force.
