Continuum Gaussian free field

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process Gaussian random field Gaussian free field
OurBigBook.com Words: 517 Articles: 12
The continuum Gaussian free field on a planar domain D is a centered Gaussian process indexed by finite-energy measures or test functions, with covariance given by the Green function of the Laplacian on D.
