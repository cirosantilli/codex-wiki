Poisson summation formula

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 219 Articles: 3
For a sufficiently rapidly decreasing function f, the Poisson summation formula relates its samples to those of its Fourier transform:
∑n∈Z​f(n)=∑k∈Z​f​(2πk)
(2)
under the convention f​(ξ)=∫R​f(x)e−ixξdx.
