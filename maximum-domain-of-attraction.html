Maximum domain of attraction (D(G))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Extreme value theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 126 Articles: 2
A distribution function F lies in the maximum domain of attraction of G if F(an​x+bn​)n→G(x) at every continuity point of G for some an​>0 and real bn​. This expresses convergence in distribution of normalized sample maxima.
