Natural linewidth (Γ)

 Home Physics Atomic physics Spontaneous emission
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
For a closed two-level system with spontaneous emission population decay Pe​(t)=Pe​(0)e−Γt and no additional dephasing, the natural linewidth has full width at half maximum Γ in angular frequency, or Γ/(2π) in hertz. The excited-state lifetime is 1/Γ.
