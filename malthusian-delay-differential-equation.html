Malthusian delay differential equation

 Home Physics Branch of physics Mathematical biology
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 3
The scalar equation n′(t)=rn(t−τ) makes the present growth rate depend on the population one delay time earlier. A history function on [−τ,0] is required in place of a single initial value.
