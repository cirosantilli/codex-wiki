Totally isotropic subspace

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Quadratic form Isotropic quadratic form
OurBigBook.com Words: 280 Articles: 4
A subspace E is totally isotropic for B when B(u,v)=0 for all u,v∈E. For a real nondegenerate bilinear form of inertia (p,q), its dimension is at most min(p,q).
