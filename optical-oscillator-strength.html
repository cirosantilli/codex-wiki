Optical oscillator strength (fj​)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Permittivity Dielectric Electric susceptibility Lorentz oscillator model
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
An optical oscillator strength is a dimensionless weight assigned to an optical resonance. In an independent-resonance Lorentz oscillator model using a reference electron mass me​ and a specified participating number density n,
ϵr​(ω)=1+ϵ0​me​ne2​∑j​ωj2​−ω2−iγj​ωfj​​.
(7)
Its normalization must be stated: assigning one unit of total strength per participating electron means ∑j​fj​=1. A one-resonance approximation with f1​=1 represents the whole chosen electron population, rather than asserting that the first allowed interband transition carries all optical strength. The smallest band gap therefore need not equal the fitted resonance energy.
