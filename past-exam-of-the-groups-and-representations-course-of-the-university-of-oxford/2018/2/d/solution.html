The defining SU(3) representation restricts to the given matrix representation. All its restricted matrices are real, so the conjugate representation restricts identically. Hence the first two branchings are
3↓G​=χ−,+​⊕E,3↓G​=χ−,+​⊕E.​
(24)
For the adjoint representation of SU(3), use 3⊗3=1⊕8. This follows by splitting endomorphisms into scalar and traceless matrices. Subtracting one trivial constituent from c gives
8↓G​=χ+,+​⊕χ+,−​⊕χ−,+​⊕χ−,−​⊕2E.​
(25)
As an independent check, its restricted character is ∣χR​∣2−1=(8,0,0,0,0), the regular character of G. Thus this is precisely the regular representation, whose representation multiplicities equal the corresponding dimensions.
