The Clifford algebra condition for signature (−,+,+) is
{γμ​,γν​}=2ημν​I2​.
(39)
Each Pauli matrix squares to I2​, and distinct Pauli matrices anticommute. Therefore the given choice obeys
γ02​=(−iσ1​)2=−I2​,γ12​=γ22​=I2​,γμ​γν​+γν​γμ​=0(μ=ν).
(40)
These check all diagonal and off-diagonal anticommutators, proving
{γμ​,γν​}=2diag(−1,1,1)μν​I2​.​
(41)
Thus they are viable three-dimensional gamma matrices. Their Hermitian adjoints satisfy γ0†​=−γ0​ and γa†​=γa​ for a=1,2. Two components are minimal: one-dimensional nonzero scalar matrices cannot anticommute while all having nonzero squares.
