The spinor basis in c is compatible with the supplied gamma matrices. The rotation generator r(J) is anti-Hermitian and commutes with γ0​=−iσ1​. The two boost generators are Hermitian matrices and anticommute with γ0​. It follows separately for all three generators, and hence for every real T∈so(1,2), that
r(T)†γ0​+γ0​r(T)=0.​
(42)
For the infinitesimal Lorentz transformation, δψ=r(T)ψ and δψ†=ψ†r(T)†. Hence the Dirac adjoint transforms contragrediently:
δψ​=ψ†r(T)†γ0​=−ψ†γ0​r(T)=−ψ​r(T).
(43)
The two contributions to the scalar variation cancel:
δ(ψ​ψ)=(δψ​)ψ+ψ​δψ=0.​
(44)
This proves the spinor invariant in three spacetime dimensions has infinitesimal Lorentz invariance. Real transformation parameters matter because the Hermitian adjoint conjugates coefficients; this is not an invariance under arbitrary complexified parameters. For ordinary commuting spinor components, the bilinear is purely imaginary in this mostly-plus convention, and multiplying it by i gives a real invariant. Its reality is separate from its invariance.
