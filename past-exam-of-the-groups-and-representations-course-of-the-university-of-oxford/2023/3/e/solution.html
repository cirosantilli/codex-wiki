Number the simple roots of the A4​ root system consecutively along its Dynkin diagram. For a Young diagram with row lengths ri​, its highest weight has Dynkin labels ai​=ri​−ri+1​. Here i=1,…,5, vi↦Uij​vj, and Tij↦Uik​Ujl​Tkl. Contraction with the invariant alternating tensor identifies vi​=(1/4!)ϵii1​i2​i3​i4​​wi1​i2​i3​i4​, giving the dual representation of 5. The dimensions follow from (15​)=5, (45​)=5, (25​)=10.
Use the centralizer and circle group normalization of solution. The first three components have U(1) charge −2 and the last two have U(1) charge 3, so the branching of a representation is
5⟶(3,1)−2​⊕(1,2)3​,5⟶(3,1)2​⊕(1,2)−3​.​
(30)
For the dual representation, both U(1) charges reverse and the SU(3) representation is conjugated; the SU(2) doublet is equivalent to its conjugate. Taking the exterior square of the first decomposition gives three types of antisymmetric tensor components: Tab, Taα, and Tαβ, where a,b=1,2,3 and α,β=4,5. Their U(1) charges add, and Λ23=3, Λ22=1. Hence
10⟶(3,1)−4​⊕(3,2)1​⊕(1,1)6​.​
(31)
The dimensions are 3+6+1=10. These product labels describe genuine representations of the quotient group in solution: every element of its Z6​ kernel acts trivially on each summand.
