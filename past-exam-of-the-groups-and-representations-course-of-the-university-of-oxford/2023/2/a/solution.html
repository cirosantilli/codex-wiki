Conjugacy classes in the symmetric group are specified by cycle type, equivalently a partition of 4. If mj​ counts cycles of length j, the number of permutations of that cycle type is 4!/∏j​(jmj​mj​!): cyclic rotation of each cycle and interchange of equal-length cycles do not change the permutation.
