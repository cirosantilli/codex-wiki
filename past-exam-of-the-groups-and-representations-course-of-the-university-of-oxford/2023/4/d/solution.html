Choose the first SU(2) as SUw​(2) and embed SUc​(3) in the upper-left three-by-three block of SU(4). The fundamental representations then restrict as 4→3⊕1 and 4→3⊕1. Thus the representation in solution, forgetting hypercharge, restricts to
(2,3)⊕(2,1)⊕2(1,3)⊕2(1,1).
(41)
This contains every required SUw​(2)×SUc​(3) representation, with one extra singlet.
For the hypercharge embedding, let X=diag(1,1,1,−3) in the SU(4) fundamental representation, and T3R​=diag(1/2,−1/2) in the second SU(2). These commute with SUw​(2)×SUc​(3). The integer normalization in the paper is
Y=X+6T3R​.​
(42)
Explicitly, this circle group embeds in the covering group by
z⟼(I2​,(z30​0z−3​),diag(z,z,z,z−3)).
(43)
Both nontrivial blocks have determinant one, and the map is injective since the first three entries of the SU(4) block recover z.
On (2,1,4), T3R​=0, so the branching of a representation gives (2,3)1​⊕(2,1)−3​. On (1,2,4), the U(1) charges under X are −1 on 3 and 3 on 1, while 6T3R​ takes values ±3. Hence
(1,2,4)​⟶(1,3)−1+3​⊕(1,3)−1−3​⊕(1,1)3+3​⊕(1,1)3−3​.​
(44)
Combining the two summands gives
R⟶RF​⊕(1,1)0​.​
(45)
This is the Pati-Salam charge embedding, with X=3(B−L). In terms of the common Standard Model convention Qelectric​=T3L​+YSM​, the paper's integer hypercharge is Y=6YSM​.
The extra neutral singlet can be interpreted as the left-handed charge conjugate νRc​ of a right-handed neutrino. It is a sterile neutrino under the Standard Model gauge group, and completes the sixteen Weyl fermions of one family in this half-spin representation. Its neutrality permits a Majorana mass term without violating the remaining Standard Model gauge group.
