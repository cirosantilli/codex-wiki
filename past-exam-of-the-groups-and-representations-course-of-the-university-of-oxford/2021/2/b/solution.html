For permutations σ,τ, the prescribed linear maps satisfy
R(σ)R(τ)ek​=R(σ)eτ(k)​=eσ(τ(k))​=R(στ)ek​.
(14)
Since this holds on a basis, R(σ)R(τ)=R(στ). Also R(e)=I and R(σ)−1=R(σ−1). Hence R is a representation.
The trace of a permutation matrix counts its fixed coordinate basis vectors. There are respectively 3,1,0 fixed vectors on the three conjugacy classes, so χR​=(3,1,0)=χ1​+χD​. Consequently
R=1⊕D.​
(15)
In particular the sign representation is absent.
