The two invariant subspaces are
U0​=C(1,1,1)T,U2​={(z1​,z2​,z3​)T:z1​+z2​+z3​=0}.

Coordinate permutations preserve both equal coordinates and the coordinate sum. Their dimensions add to three and their intersection is zero, so C3=U0​⊕U2​. On U0​, the basis u0​=(1,1,1)T gives RU0​​(τ)=(1) for every transposition.
On U2​ choose the basis u=e1​−e3​, v=e2​−e3​. Directly permuting their entries gives
τ(12)(13)(23)​R(τ)uv−uu−v​R(τ)vuv−u−v​​
(17)
Thus, taking images as columns, the matrices of the matrix representation are
D(12)=(01​10​),D(13)=(−10​−11​),D(23)=(1−1​0−1​).​
(18)
Each has trace zero and squares to I. Their three-cycle products have trace −1, so this is exactly the two-dimensional irreducible representation of solution. These are all nonzero invariant subspaces affording individual irreducible representations, since each occurs once in solution.
