Order the simple roots of the G2 root system with α1​ long and α2​ short. This ordering is consistent with the supplied projection in solution; reversing the nodes would interchange the two fundamental Dynkin labels. Choose (α1​,α1​)=2, (α2​,α2​)=2/3, and (α1​,α2​)=−1. With the same row convention for the Cartan matrix as in solution,
AG2​​=(2−1​−32​),α1​=(2,−3),α2​=(−1,2).​
(27)

rootα2​α1​+α2​α1​+2α2​α1​α1​+3α2​2α1​+3α2​​Dynkin labels(−1,2)(1,−1)(0,1)(2,−3)(−1,3)(1,0)​length typeshortshortshortlonglonglong​​
(28)
For the highest weight (0,1), a lowering chain is
(0,1)−α2​​(1,−1)−α1​​(−1,2)−α2​​(0,0)−α2​​(1,−2)−α1​​(−1,1)−α2​​(0,−1).
(29)
The middle three terms form the length-three weight string for the short simple root. The Weyl dimension formula, with this long-first convention, is
dimV(a,b)=120(a+1)(b+1)(a+b+2)(2a+b+3)(3a+b+4)(3a+2b+5)​.
(30)
It gives seven for (0,1), so the chain exhausts the weight system, with every weight multiplicity equal to one:
7=(0,1):(0,0),±(0,1),±(1,−1),±(−1,2).​
(31)
For (1,0), observe that 2α1​+3α2​=(1,0) is the highest root. Thus this is the Adjoint representation; the twelve root spaces contribute the twelve nonzero weights once, and the rank-two Cartan subalgebra contributes two zero weights. Therefore
14=(1,0):±(2,−3), ±(−1,3), ±(1,0), ±(−1,2), ±(1,−1), ±(0,1), (0,0)[2].​
(32)
The same Weyl dimension formula gives fourteen, confirming the count.
