The highest root is θ=2α1​+3α2​. In the Extended Dynkin diagram, the extra node −θ is long and is joined by a single bond to the long node α1​; it is orthogonal to the short node α2​. Deleting the short node therefore leaves the A2 root system Dynkin diagram. Equivalently, the six long roots are a closed root subsystem, so their root spaces together with the Cartan subalgebra form an A2 root system Lie subalgebra. The corresponding compact subgroup is SU(3)⊂G2​.
For the particular projection in the paper, choose the A2 root system simple roots as β1​=α1​+3α2​ and β2​=α1​. Since these are long, β1∨​=α1∨​+α2∨​ and β2∨​=α1∨​. Consequently the Dynkin labels restrict as
(a,b)T⟼P(a,b)T=(a+b,a)T,P=(11​10​).​
(33)
Projecting the six short roots gives ±(1,0),±(0,1),±(1,−1). Group these into the three weights of 3 and the three of 3 listed in solution. The zero weight of 7 is left over, so
7↓SU(3)​=3⊕3⊕1.​
(34)
The three positive long roots project as (2,−3)↦(−1,2), (−1,3)↦(2,−1) and (1,0)↦(1,1). Together with their negatives and the two zero weights, these are exactly the Adjoint representation 8. The remaining six short roots again give 3⊕3. Hence the branching of a representation is
14↓SU(3)​=8⊕3⊕3.​
(35)
