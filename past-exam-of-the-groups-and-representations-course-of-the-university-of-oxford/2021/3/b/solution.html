For an A2 root system weight w=(w1​,w2​)T, the specified electric charge and flavour hypercharge are the linear functionals
q(w)=32w1​+w2​​,y(w)=3w1​+2w2​​.
(25)
Apply these to the meson octet weights in solution. The results, with electric charges measured in units of the positive elementary electric charge, are
w(2,−1)(−2,1)(1,1)(−1,2)(−1,−1)(1,−2)(0,0) twice​q1−110−100​y0011−1−10​pseudoscalar octet memberπ+π−K+K0K−Kˉ0π0,η8​​​​
(26)
The table illustrates the meson octet of pseudoscalar mesons; the same weight calculation applies to another meson species arranged in the same representation. Here flavour hypercharge is Y=B+S, with B the baryon number and S the strangeness, rather than weak hypercharge. If the full quark model tensor product 3⊗3 is retained, its additional trivial representation is a ninth meson with (q,y)=(0,0). This singlet is separate from the octet and need not share its mass.
