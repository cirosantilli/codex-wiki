since α3=1. Their traces are α+α−1=−1, whereas trR(e)=2. Therefore
χR​=(2,0,−1),⟨χR​,χR​⟩=64+3⋅0+2⋅1​=1.​
(11)
By character orthogonality, R is the irreducible representation 2 from c.
There is also a direct invariant subspace argument. The two distinct eigenvalues of R(τ1​τ2​) force any invariant line to be one of the two coordinate lines. But R(τ3​) exchanges those lines. Neither is invariant under the whole group, so this two-dimensional representation is irreducible.
