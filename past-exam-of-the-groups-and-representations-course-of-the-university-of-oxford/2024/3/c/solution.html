The change of variables in b diagonalizes the complexified action into z↦Uz and zˉ↦Uˉzˉ. Consequently the conventional complex branching rule is
6SO(6)​↓SU(3)=3⊕3ˉ.​
(38)
Precisely, this is a statement about the complexification of a real vector space carrying the real defining representation. Over the real numbers, the six-dimensional space is the realification of a complex representation 3; the conjugate summands above are not separate three-dimensional real invariant subspaces.
The block embedding SO(6)↪SO(7) fixes the seventh coordinate. It adds a trivial representation, giving
7SO(7)​↓SU(3)=3⊕3ˉ⊕1.​
(39)
The dimensions are complex dimensions after complexification of a real vector space, or equivalently 6+1 as real dimensions before it.
