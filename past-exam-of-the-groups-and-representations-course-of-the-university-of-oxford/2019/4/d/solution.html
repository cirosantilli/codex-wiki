Use the basis matrices Mab​=Eab​−Eba​ from solution. Define the Pauli matrices and the two-by-two identity by
σ1​=(01​10​),σ2​=(0i​−i0​),σ3​=(10​0−1​),I=I2​.
(49)
The following five Hermitian four-by-four matrices, written as Kronecker products, realize a Euclidean Clifford algebra:
γ1​=σ1​⊗I,γ2​=σ2​⊗I,γ3​=σ3​⊗σ1​,γ4​=σ3​⊗σ2​,γ5​=σ3​⊗σ3​,γa​γb​+γb​γa​=2δab​I4​.
(50)
Define the Lie algebra representation on C4 by
ρ(Mab​)=41​[γa​,γb​]=21​γa​γb​(a<b).​
(51)
These are anti-Hermitian matrices, appropriate to the real compact Lie algebra. For complete explicitness, all ten representation matrices are
M12​M14​M23​M25​M35​​2i​σ3​⊗I−2i​σ2​⊗σ2​2i​σ1​⊗σ1​2i​σ1​⊗σ3​−2i​I⊗σ2​​M13​M15​M24​M34​M45​​−2i​σ2​⊗σ1​−2i​σ2​⊗σ3​2i​σ1​⊗σ2​2i​I⊗σ3​2i​I⊗σ1​​​
(52)
Each table entry is the image under ρ of the basis matrix immediately to its left. Since the two-by-two factors are specified above, these Kronecker products specify every entry of each four-by-four matrix.
To verify the representation law, the Clifford algebra relations imply
[ρ(Mab​),γc​]=δbc​γa​−δac​γb​.
(53)
Apply this identity to ρ(Mcd​)=41​[γc​,γd​] and use the commutator product rule. The result is
[ρ(Mab​),ρ(Mcd​)]=δbc​ρ(Mad​)−δac​ρ(Mbd​)−δbd​ρ(Mac​)+δad​ρ(Mbc​),
(54)
exactly the Lie bracket relation in solution. Thus ρ is a Lie algebra representation.
It remains to identify its highest weight, rather than just its dimension. The commuting Hermitian Cartan subalgebra operators are
−iρ(M12​)=21​σ3​⊗I,−iρ(M34​)=21​I⊗σ3​.
(55)
Their simultaneous eigenvalues are (x,y)=(±21​,±21​). Converting with (a,b)=(x−y,2y) gives exactly the four weights of V(0,1) in solution. A state with (x,y)=(21​,21​) has highest weight (0,1), so this Clifford realization of the so5 spin representation is the requested r.
Finally, ρ(M12​)2=−I4​/4, so
e2πρ(M12​)=−I4​,e2πM12​=I5​.​
(56)
This explicitly exhibits the distinction between the spinor representation of so(5), or of Spin(5), and a single-valued group representation of SO(5).
