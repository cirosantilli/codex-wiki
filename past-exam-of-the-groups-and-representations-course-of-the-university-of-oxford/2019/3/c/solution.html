Here all indices run from one to six. The highest weight Dynkin labels follow from the differences of successive row lengths. In the same order the row lengths are (1), (1,1,1,1,1), (2), (1,1) and (2,1,1,1,1).
The defining vector transforms as va↦Uab​vb. For a unitary representation, the conjugate representation is equivalent to the dual representation, so 6 can be written as a covector with wa​↦wb​(U−1)ba​. Its five-box column is equivalently a totally antisymmetric tensor Aa1​⋯a5​, related by the invariant alternating tensor wa​=ϵaa1​⋯a5​​Aa1​⋯a5​/5!.
The symmetric tensor transforms as Sab↦Uac​Ubd​Scd, with 6(6+1)/2=21 independent components. The antisymmetric tensor has the same transformation law but obeys Aab=−Aba, giving 6(6−1)/2=15 independent components. For the Adjoint representation, T↦UTU−1; the trace constraint removes one component from the 36 entries of a mixed tensor, leaving 35. Its six-box Young diagram is obtained by converting the five-index column to a lower index and selecting the traceless mixed tensor.
Thus the requested dimensions, in the order above, are
