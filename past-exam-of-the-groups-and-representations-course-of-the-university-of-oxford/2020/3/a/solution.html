The componentwise product is closed in G1​×G2​. Associativity follows componentwise from associativity in each group:
((g1​,g2​)(h1​,h2​))(k1​,k2​)=((g1​h1​)k1​,(g2​h2​)k2​)=(g1​(h1​k1​),g2​(h2​k2​)).
(18)
The identity is (e1​,e2​), and (g1​,g2​)−1=(g1−1​,g2−1​). These verify all the group axioms, so this is the direct product of groups.
Write the paper's Kronecker product × as ⊗. Its mixed-product identity and the representation laws of R1​,R2​ give
R(g1​,g2​)R(h1​,h2​)​=(R1​(g1​)⊗R2​(g2​))(R1​(h1​)⊗R2​(h2​))=R1​(g1​)R1​(h1​)⊗R2​(g2​)R2​(h2​)=R1​(g1​h1​)⊗R2​(g2​h2​)=R((g1​,g2​)(h1​,h2​)).​
(19)
Also R(e1​,e2​)=In1​​⊗In2​​=In1​n2​​, and
R(g1​,g2​)−1=R1​(g1​)−1⊗R2​(g2​)−1.
(20)
Thus the images are invertible and this is a group representation of dimension n1​n2​, called the external tensor product of group representations. No irreducibility assumption is needed for this construction.
