Under the stated SU(2) embedding, the first two components of the defining SU(3) representation form a doublet, while the third is fixed. Hence 3↓SU(2)​=2⊕1. Its dual representation restricts in the same way because the SU(2) doublet is self-dual.
For the Adjoint representation, restrict 8=3⊗3−1. The SU(2) tensor product 2⊗2=3⊕1 gives
8↓SU(2)​=(2⊕1)⊗(2⊕1)−1=3⊕2⊕2⊕1.
(23)
Here subtraction denotes removal of the trivial representation generated by the identity matrix; it is not a negative-dimensional summand. The first SU(3) factor is unchanged, so the requested branching of a representation rules are
(3,3)(3,1)(1,3)(1,8)​↓H​=(3,2)⊕(3,1),↓H​=(3,1),↓H​=(1,2)⊕(1,1),↓H​=(1,3)⊕2(1,2)⊕(1,1).​​
(24)
The dimensions on the two sides are respectively 9=6+3, 3=3, 3=2+1 and 8=3+2+2+1.
