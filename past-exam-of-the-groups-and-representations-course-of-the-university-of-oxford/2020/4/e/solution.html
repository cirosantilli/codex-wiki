Compared with the complete three-flavour meson tensor product, the additional flavour states are exactly
3(ucˉ, dcˉ, scˉ)​​,3(cuˉ, cdˉ, csˉ)​​,1ccˉ​​.​
(39)
Thus there are seven new flavour states. For the pseudoscalar mesons, the six open-charm states are Dˉ0,D−,Ds−​ and D0,D+,Ds+​, respectively, using the D meson and Ds meson conventions. A pure ccˉ pseudoscalar meson is the idealized flavour state associated with the eta c meson. The quark model flavour calculation alone does not specify spin or spatial excitations, so the same flavour labels also organize other meson species.
If comparing the traceless SU(4) 15 with the traceless SU(3) meson octet, the extra seven states are instead the same two triplets and the normalized SU(3) singlet
η15​=12​uuˉ+ddˉ+ssˉ−3ccˉ​.​
(40)
Its coefficient matrix is traceless and has squared norm one. In particular, ccˉ by itself is not a vector in the traceless 15; its coefficient matrix has trace one.
To relate these two descriptions, let S4​=(uuˉ+ddˉ+ssˉ+ccˉ)/2 be the normalized SU(4) singlet and S3​=(uuˉ+ddˉ+ssˉ)/3​ the old SU(3) singlet. Then
S3​=23​​S4​+21​η15​,ccˉ=21​S4​−23​​η15​.​
(41)
This orthonormal change of basis shows explicitly why the complete tensor product contains two light-flavour singlets but adds only one state to the old light-flavour singlet sector.
