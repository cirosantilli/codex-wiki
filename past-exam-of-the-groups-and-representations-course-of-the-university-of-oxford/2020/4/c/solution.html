The projection deletes the third Dynkin label, so the four weights vi​ of solution restrict to (1,0),(−1,1),(0,−1),(0,0). The first three are the defining SU(3) weight system, and the fourth is a singlet. Negating them gives the dual representation. Hence
4↓SU(3)​=3⊕1,4↓SU(3)​=3⊕1.​
(34)
For the Adjoint representation, the projected nonzero weights consist of the six A2 root system roots, the three defining weights and their negatives. There are also three zero weights. Two zero weights belong to the SU(3) Adjoint representation, leaving one singlet. Therefore the SU4 adjoint branching to SU3 is
15↓SU(3)​=8⊕3⊕3⊕1.​
(35)
One can see all four invariant subspaces directly by writing a traceless matrix as
M=(Ac​bd​),trA+d=0.
(36)
The traceless part of A is 8, the column b is 3, the row c is 3, and the remaining diagonal matrix is proportional to diag(1,1,1,−3) and is fixed by SU(3). These have dimensions 8+3+3+1=15. Equivalently restrict 4⊗4−1 and use 3⊗3=8⊕1.
