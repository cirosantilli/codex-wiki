Take the quarks q=(u,d,s,c)T in the defining 4 of flavour SU(4) and the antiquarks in 4. The flavour part of a meson is a quark-antiquark pair Mij​=qiqˉ​j​. It transforms as M↦UMU−1, so the complete four-flavour meson multiplet is
4⊗4=15⊕1.​
(37)
Its traceless part M−41​(trM)I4​ is the Adjoint representation 15, the direct four-flavour analogue of the three-flavour meson octet. The trace supplies an additional trivial representation. Thus “the mesons” can refer to the traceless multiplet or to the complete quark model tensor product; keeping both explicit avoids dropping a state.
Using solution, the SU(3) representation contents are
15↓SU(3)​=8⊕3⊕3⊕1,(15⊕1)↓SU(3)​=8⊕3⊕3⊕21.​
(38)
Directly, the upper-left block qiqˉ​j​ with i,j≤3 is the old 3⊗3=8⊕1; the three entries qicˉ form 3, the three entries cqˉ​j​ form 3, and ccˉ is another singlet. The dimensions are 9+3+3+1=16. This is a classification by flavour symmetry, rather than colour or spin. The much larger charm quark mass breaks the flavour SU(4) approximation strongly; it does not change this representation counting.
