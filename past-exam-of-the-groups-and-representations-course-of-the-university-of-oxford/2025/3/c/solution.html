All gamma matrices in this basis are real, and the Lorentz transformation S(θ) is real. Therefore complex conjugation commutes with the connected Spinor representation of the Lorentz group. With a suitable phase convention, charge conjugation is simply ψc=ψ∗. The Majorana spinor condition ψc=ψ becomes
ψM​=(ab​),a∗=a,b∗=b.​
(34)
The reality constraint is preserved by Lorentz transformations because (SψM​)∗=SψM​. For fermion fields, real components mean self-conjugate Grassmann variables or self-adjoint field components, rather than ordinary commuting real numbers.
Moreover, Γ=σ3​ is real, so charge conjugation commutes with the chiral projectors. The Majorana spinor and Weyl spinor conditions can therefore be imposed simultaneously. The Majorana-Weyl spinors are
ψMR​=(a0​),ψML​=(0b​),a∗=a, b∗=b.​
(35)
