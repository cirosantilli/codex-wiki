A Weyl spinor is a Dirac spinor restricted to one eigenspace of Γ. The chiral projectors are PR​=(I+Γ)/2 and PL​=(I−Γ)/2. Consequently
ψR​=PR​ψ=(ψ+​0​),ψL​=PL​ψ=(0ψ−​​).​
(33)
The two independent Weyl spinors each contain one complex component. Since [S(θ),Γ]=0, each chirality is preserved by every connected Lorentz transformation, with ψR​↦eθ/2ψR​ and ψL​↦e−θ/2ψL​. A spatial reflection exchanges the two chiralities.
The left/right names depend on the sign convention for Γ. Choosing γ0​γ1​=−σ3​ as the chirality operator exchanges the labels L and R; the two invariant one-component representations are the same.
