A group representation on a vector space V is a group homomorphism R:G→GL(V), so R(gh)=R(g)R(h) and R(e)=I. A nonzero group representation is an irreducible representation if its only invariant subspaces are 0 and V. Here the representations and characters are finite-dimensional over the complex numbers.
The character of a representation is χR​(g)=trR(g). Three useful properties are:
  • It is a class function: χR​(hgh−1)=χR​(g), by the cyclic property of the trace.
  • It is additive on a direct sum and multiplicative on a tensor product of group representations: χR⊕S​=χR​+χS​ and χR⊗S​=χR​χS​.
  • The irreducible characters obey character orthogonality:
    ⟨χr​,χs​⟩=∣G∣1​∑g∈G​χr​(g)∗χs​(g)=δrs​.
    (1)
In particular, χR​(e)=dimV and χR​(g−1)=χR​(g)∗, the latter following from unitarization of a finite-group representation. The character inner product ⟨χr​,χR​⟩ counts the multiplicity of an irreducible representation r in R.
