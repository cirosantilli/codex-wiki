Put r=g1​ and s=g2​. Direct matrix multiplication gives
r3=s2=I,srs=r−1.
(4)
Moving every s to the right shows that every word in these generators of a group has the form rasb, with a∈{0,1,2} and b∈{0,1}. The three ra are distinct diagonal matrices, and the three ras are distinct off-diagonal matrices. Hence
G={I,r,r2,s,rs,r2s},∣G∣=6.​
(5)
Since srs=r2=r, this is a non-abelian group. Its group presentation identifies it with the dihedral group of an equilateral triangle, equivalently the symmetric group S3​.
The conjugacy classes are
{I},{r,r2},{s,rs,r2s}.​
(6)
Indeed s interchanges r and r2 by conjugation, while rasr−a=r2as runs through all three reflections. No reflection is conjugate to a rotation, since conjugation preserves order of a group element.
There are three inequivalent complex irreducible representations, one for each conjugacy class. The sum of squares of irreducible representation dimensions gives d12​+d22​+d32​=6. One representation is trivial, and the only possible positive integer dimensions are
d1​,d2​,d3​=1,1,2.​
(7)
