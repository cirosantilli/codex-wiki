Use the integer normalization c(3)=1 from solution. For two fundamental representations, the specified discrete gauge anomaly coefficient is A=q1​+q2​. Consequently all solutions in the finite cyclic group of charges are
(q1​,q2​)=(q,−q)(modn),q∈Zn​.​
(22)
There are n ordered charge pairs. The trivial pair is included: the problem does not require a faithful action. If a faithful action on these Weyl fermions were additionally required, one would retain precisely the q coprime to n.
This cancels the discrete gauge anomaly defined in the paper. Two left-handed fundamental representations by themselves have a nonzero pure cubic SU(3) gauge anomaly; a complete gauge theory would need additional fermions to cancel it. That separate condition does not alter the requested discrete congruence.
