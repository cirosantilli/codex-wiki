The Weyl dimension formula is increasing in each nonnegative Dynkin label. If a≥3 or b≥3, the dimension is at least d(3,0)=10. Among 0≤a,b≤2, the cases with both labels nonzero, other than (1,1), have dimension at least d(2,1)=15. Thus the following is the complete list of irreducible representations of dimension strictly less than 10:
