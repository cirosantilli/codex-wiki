Polynomial representation of the general linear group (ρij​∈C[gab​])

OurBigBook.com Words: 646 Articles: 12
In a polynomial representation, every matrix coefficient is a polynomial in the matrix entries. The action extends to the monoid of all matrices. The scalar action splits it into homogeneous polynomial degrees; each homogeneous degree-n category is the module category of the Schur algebra S(m,n). Its irreducibles are the Schur modules Dλ​(Cm) with λ⊢n and at most m rows.
