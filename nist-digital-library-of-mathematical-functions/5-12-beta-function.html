NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions / 5.12 Beta Function

 Home Mathematics Mathematics bibliography NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
Official NIST DLMF section 5.12. Equation 5.12.1 gives Euler's integral for the beta function and its expression as a ratio of gamma functions, with positive real parts of the two parameters. The integral identity supplies normalization and shifted-parameter moments for the beta distribution.
