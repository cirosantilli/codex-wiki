Expander mixing lemma (∣e(S,T)−d∣S∣∣T∣/n∣≤dρ∣S∣(1−∣S∣/n)∣T∣(1−∣T∣/n)​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For a d-regular undirected graph whose normalized adjacency eigenvalues other than the constant eigenvalue have absolute value at most ρ, center the indicator vectors of S,T. Their constant parts give the expected term d∣S∣∣T∣/n, and the operator norm on the mean-zero subspace bounds the remaining inner product. Here e(S,T)=1ST​A1T​ counts oriented incidences, so an edge within S∩T is counted twice.
