Aspect switching experiment

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Foundations of quantum mechanics Bell theorem Bell test
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
In 1982, Alain Aspect, Jean Dalibard and Gérard Roger used a calcium cascade and an acoustic standing wave in water to route each photon toward one of two fixed polarizers. Switching took about 10ns, against 40ns for light to cross between stations. Photomultiplier coincidence circuit rates, with delayed-background subtraction and polarizer-removal normalization, violated their Bell inequality. Supplementary polarizers checked each channel against Malus's law. The timing attacked the locality loophole, but the authors emphasized that switching was quasiperiodic, not random; incomplete switching and inefficient collection also limited the test. Its normalized statistic differs from the CHSH inequality convention used below.[ref]
