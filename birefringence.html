Birefringence (Δn=ne​−no​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Maxwell equations Maxwell equations in matter Linear anisotropic dielectric
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 27
Birefringence is the existence of different optical propagation constants for different eigenpolarizations in an anisotropic material. It provides the propagation-mode separation needed for adiabatic optical following in a twisted nematic.
Two different refractive indices can produce two displaced images, as in the calcite double-image experiment, or a phase retardation between overlapping components, as in a waveplate. Spatial separation concerns where energy flux travels; phase retardation concerns the relative wave phase. A specimen need not show two visibly separated images to have birefringence.
