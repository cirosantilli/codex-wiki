Dust-tail angular lag (Δθ=θd​−θp​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Interplanetary dust Dust tail
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 1
For unwrapped grain and parent azimuths, define a signed lag Δθ=θd​−θp​ and a positive trailing distance s=−Δθ. A grain released without a velocity kick from a circular parent orbit has Δθ=2β(sinθd​−θd​)+O(β2θd​). Keeping track of this sign avoids mistaking trailing motion for leading motion.
