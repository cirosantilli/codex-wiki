Protein targeting

 Home Biology Protein
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 18
Protein targeting directs a protein to its cellular destination. Synthesis in the cytosol does not imply that the protein works there.
For entry into the endoplasmic reticulum, distinguish three questions: does the nascent chain reach the membrane, does part of it cross into the ER lumen, and does a segment remain embedded as a transmembrane protein? Cotranslational protein translocation couples these events to biological translation. A signal peptide can initiate delivery without specifying the eventual destination of the mature protein.
This distinction matters experimentally. Finding a protein in a membrane-containing centrifugation pellet could reflect surface binding or protein aggregation. The microsomal protein import experiment instead asks whether an intact membrane prevents an external protease from reaching the product. That tests accessibility across a barrier, rather than proximity to it.
