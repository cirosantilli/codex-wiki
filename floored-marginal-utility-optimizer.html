Floored marginal utility optimizer (wT∗​=max{ξ,I(λζT​)})

OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For a nonnegative terminal wealth floor whose state-price density cost is strictly below the available budget, pointwise maximization of U(y)−λζT​y over y≥ξ gives the displayed payoff. The positive multiplier exhausts the state-price budget constraint. This requires the usual utility and integrability hypotheses ensuring that the multiplier exists and the payoff is replicable.
