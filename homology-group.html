Homology group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 60 Articles: 2
A homology group is one of the abelian groups produced by a homology theory. Its elements are homology classes, represented in singular homology by cycles modulo boundaries.
