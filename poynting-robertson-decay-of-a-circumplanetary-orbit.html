Poynting–Robertson decay of a circumplanetary orbit (⟨a˙⟩cp​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Interplanetary dust Poynting–Robertson drag
OurBigBook.com Words: 176 Articles: 1
Consider a circular circumplanetary orbit well inside the Hill sphere, with dust speed u2=GMp​/a and unit plane normal k. During one dust orbital period, take the stellar direction R and flux at the planet's stellar radius ap​ as constant. The velocity-dependent Poynting–Robertson drag has relative work
⟨ε˙p​⟩=−ap2​A​⟨u2+(u⋅R)2⟩,A=cβGM⋆​​.
(23)
The constant terms have zero work over the closed circular orbit. Since ⟨ui​uj​⟩=(u2/2)(δij​−ki​kj​),
⟨a˙⟩=−ap2​A​a[3−(k⋅R)2].
(24)
The coefficient is three for coplanar stellar and dust orbits; it is two for a dust plane normal to the instantaneous stellar direction. Averaging over a circular planetary year at fixed dust orbital inclination I gives 3−sin2I/2. Conservative perturbations must remain small enough for this circular-orbit average.
