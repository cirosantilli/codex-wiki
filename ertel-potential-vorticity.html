Ertel potential vorticity (Q=ωa​⋅∇b)

 ... Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Rotating fluid Potential vorticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Ertel potential vorticity is the scalar product of absolute vorticity with the gradient of a materially conserved stratifying scalar. In Boussinesq flow with buoyancy b,
Q=ωa​⋅∇b.
(158)
It is materially conserved by inviscid, adiabatic motion.
