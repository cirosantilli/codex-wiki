Mixture allocation indicator (Ij​=1{Z=j}​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Mixture model Finite mixture model
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
Generate a finite mixture model by drawing a categorical variable Z with P(Z=j)=πj​, then drawing X from the mixture component Pj​. The indicator random variables Ij​=1{Z=j}​ satisfy ∑j​Ij​=1: exactly one mixture component generates each draw. The joint probability distribution obeys P(Z=j,X∈A)=πj​Pj​(A), and the law of total probability gives the mixture distribution after summing out Z.[ref]
For independent draws from this statistical model, draw a fresh Zi​ for each Xi​. Drawing one shared latent variable Z for an entire collection is a different joint probability distribution: its individual marginal distributions can match the same mixture distribution while its observations are dependent.
