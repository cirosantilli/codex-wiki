Uniform pro-p group

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Profinite group Pro-p group
OurBigBook.com Words: 553 Articles: 7
A finitely generated powerful p-group in the category of pro-p groups is uniform if all successive indices in its lower p-series are equal. Equivalently it is torsion-free. If its generator number is d, the successive indices are pd, the power map carries Gpn bijectively onto Gpn+1, and an ordered minimal generating set gives unique coordinates g1λ1​​⋯gdλd​​ with λi​ in the p-adic integers. For p=2, powerfulness means [G,G]≤G4.
