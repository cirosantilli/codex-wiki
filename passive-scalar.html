Passive scalar

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Scalar transport
OurBigBook.com Words: 985 Articles: 17
A passive scalar is an advected and diffusing scalar field whose value has no dynamical effect on the transporting velocity field. Concentration of a dilute nonreacting dye is a common example. Its transport can depend on the flow while the flow equations remain independent of it.
