Saddle-node curves of a signed power gluing map (r=(Aδ)−1/(δ−1),μ=±δδ−1​r)

The fixed point relation is μ=z−Asgn(z)∣z∣δ. Setting the fixed-point multiplier Aδ∣z∣δ−1 equal to one gives the two saddle-node bifurcations at z=±r. For fixed 0<A<1, as δ↑1 from below their parameter width tends to zero as (1−δ)A1/(1−δ)/e, forming an exponentially narrow gluing-map cusp. Three scalar fixed points coexist inside this region: two stable outer branches and one unstable inner branch. For δ>1 near one the formal saddle-node bifurcations recede to infinity, outside the local gluing-map domain.
