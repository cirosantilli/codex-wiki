Distributive lattice

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Intuitionistic propositional logic Lattice
OurBigBook.com Words: 608 Articles: 15
A distributive lattice is a lattice in which meet distributes over join and, equivalently, join distributes over meet.
