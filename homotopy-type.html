Homotopy type

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Homotopy equivalence
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Two spaces have the same homotopy type when there is a homotopy equivalence between them. Their homology and homotopy groups are then isomorphic. In Morse theory, attaching a cell records a change in the homotopy type of a sublevel set.
