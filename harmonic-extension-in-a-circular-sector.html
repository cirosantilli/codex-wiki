Harmonic extension in a circular sector (Φ(r,θ)=∑n≥1​bn​(r/R)nπ/γsin(nπθ/γ))

For 0<θ<γ and 0<r<R, a bounded harmonic function that vanishes on the straight edges and has outer boundary data φ has coefficients
bn​=γ2​∫0γ​φ(θ)sinγnπθ​dθ.
(75)
This follows by separation of variables and Fourier orthogonality. Negative radial powers are excluded by boundedness at the vertex. Reflection-symmetric data φ(θ)=φ(γ−θ) have only odd Fourier modes. Knowing the resulting solution on the bisector determines all these odd coefficients by its radial power series. Without reflection symmetry, the even angular modes vanish on the bisector and cannot be recovered from that restriction alone.
