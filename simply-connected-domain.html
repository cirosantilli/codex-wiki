Simply connected domain

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 2
A domain is simply connected when every closed path in it can be continuously contracted to a point while remaining in the domain.
Such a domain is a simply connected space.
