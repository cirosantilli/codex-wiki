For E:y2=x(x2+ax+b), set b′=a2−4b and E′:Y2=X(X2−2aX+b′). The isogeny of elliptic curves with kernel {O,(0,0)} is ϕ(x,y)=(x+a+b/x, y(1−b/x2)). Its dual is ϕ​(X,Y)=((X−2a+b′/X)/4, Y(1−b′/X2)/8). The maps extend over their exceptional affine points and satisfy ϕ​ϕ=[2].
