Balance of linear momentum (ρDv/Dt=∇⋅σ+ρb)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
For a body occupying V, the rate of change of ∫V​ρvdV equals the body forces and surface tractions. With the Cauchy stress tensor, the local form is ρDv/Dt=∇⋅σ+ρb.
