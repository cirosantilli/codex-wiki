Left-right double cover of SO0(2,2) (SL(2,R)2/{(I,I),(−I,−I)}≅SO0​(2,2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 72
The action (A,B):M↦AMB−1 preserves the quadratic form −detM on real two-by-two matrices. Its kernel consists of the simultaneous scalar pairs (I,I) and (−I,−I). The differential (a,b):M↦aM−Mb is injective on the two traceless Lie algebras, and both source and target have dimension six. Its image is therefore open and, since the source is connected, equals the identity component of a Lie group SO0​(2,2). This gives a two-sheeted covering map onto the identity component, not onto the disconnected full special orthogonal group SO(2,2).
