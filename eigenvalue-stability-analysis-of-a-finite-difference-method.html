Eigenvalue stability analysis of a finite difference method

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Finite difference Finite difference method von Neumann stability analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 241 Articles: 3
For a normal amplification matrix Q, the bound ρ(Q)≤1 with semisimple unit-modulus eigenvalues controls every power Qn. For a nonnormal family, eigenvalues alone do not control transient growth or give a mesh-uniform bound; eigenvector conditioning, pseudospectra, or a direct energy estimate is then needed.
