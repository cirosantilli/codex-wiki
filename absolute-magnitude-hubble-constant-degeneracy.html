Absolute magnitude–Hubble constant degeneracy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Luminosity distance Type Ia supernova cosmology
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
In Type Ia supernova cosmology, the predicted apparent magnitude depends on the common absolute magnitude M0​ and the Hubble constant only through β=M0​−5log10​(H0​/Href​), after choosing a fixed reference Href​>0. A flat improper prior on M0​ makes the likelihood integrated over M0​ independent of H0​, by translation of the integration variable. If the other prior factors are independent and the posterior is proper, the marginal posterior of H0​ equals its prior. An external calibration of M0​ can break this lack of identifiability.
