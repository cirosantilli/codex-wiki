For a constant primordial bispectrum and large-angle transfer function Δℓ​(k)=jℓ​(kR)/5, the spherical Bessel product integral reduces the radial projection to ∫01​rL+2dr+∫1∞​r−L−1dr. For L>0 this equals 1/(L+3)+1/L, giving the displayed reduced CMB bispectrum. All powers of the distance R cancel. Under common large-multipole scaling it behaves as ℓ−4 at fixed shape; this is angular scale invariance, not a constant angular bispectrum. The all-monopole case L=0 has a logarithmically divergent radial tail and is excluded.
