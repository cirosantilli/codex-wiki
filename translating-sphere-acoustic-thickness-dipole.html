For a small rigid sphere translating with velocity V(t), the integrated thickness source ρ0​∫V⋅ns​dS vanishes. The first spatial moment is instead ρ0​∫yj​V⋅ns​dS=ρ0​VVj​, where V is the sphere volume. Expanding the retarded time across the compact source gives the displayed first nonzero thickness field. It is an acoustic dipole, at the same order as acoustic loading noise for a translating sphere. The added mass of a sphere supplies half this volume-mass coefficient through the loading term; their sum is 3ρ0​Vn⋅V¨/(8πc03​r).
