Quadratic variation obstruction to Hölder regularity (∑k=0N−1​(w((k+1)/N)−w(k/N))2≤C2N1−2α)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Quadratic variation
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
A function with finite Hölder seminorm of exponent α>1/2 has squared-increment sum on a uniform N-interval partition bounded by C2N1−2α, hence tending to zero. This conflicts with a nonzero quadratic-variation limit. In particular dyadic quadratic variation of Brownian motion prevents a Brownian coordinate law supported on such a Hölder space. This argument alone gives no conclusion at the critical exponent 1/2.
