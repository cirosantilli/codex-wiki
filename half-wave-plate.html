Half-wave plate (δ=π(mod2π))

 ... Electromagnetism Maxwell equations Maxwell equations in matter Linear anisotropic dielectric Birefringence Waveplate
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
A half-wave plate reverses the relative sign of the two components. For initial linear polarization at angle α to the fast axis, the outgoing line is at −α modulo π. The 45∘ input therefore rotates by 90∘.
