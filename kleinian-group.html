Kleinian group (Γ≤PSL2​(C))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Discrete subgroup
OurBigBook.com Words: 689 Articles: 12
A discrete subgroup of PSL2​(C), acting by Möbius transformations on the Riemann sphere or orientation-preserving isometries of hyperbolic space H3. A group may be Kleinian even when some elements fix points; freeness and absence of torsion are additional conditions.
