Substructure of a first-order structure (A⊆B)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 165 Articles: 2
An L-substructure of a first-order structure A⊆B has a nonempty domain containing all named constants and closed under the functions of B, and each relation of A is the restriction of the corresponding relation of B. Consequently every quantifier-free formula with parameters in A has the same truth value in A and B. This is weaker than being an elementary substructure, which preserves all first-order formulas.
