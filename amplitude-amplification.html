Amplitude amplification

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 377 Articles: 6
If a state has good amplitude sinθ, the product of the reflection in the bad axis and the reflection in the state line rotates its good-bad plane by 2θ. After k iterations the good probability is
sin2((2k+1)θ),
(5)
so O(1/sinθ) iterations raise a small good amplitude close to one.
