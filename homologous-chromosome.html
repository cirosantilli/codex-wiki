Homologous chromosome

 Home Biology Genetics Chromosome
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
Homologous chromosomes carry corresponding genetic loci, although their alleles may differ. In the diploid AB/ab example, the two homologous chromosomes carry the parental haplotypes AB and ab.
