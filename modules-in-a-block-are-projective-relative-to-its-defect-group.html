Modules in a block are projective relative to its defect group (M∣IndDG​ResDG​M)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Modular representation theory Block of a group algebra Defect group of a block
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
For a block of a group algebra with identity e and defect group of a block D, the trace criterion for defect groups writes e=TrDG​(a) with a∈(RG)D. On any module in the block, multiplication by a is D-linear and its relative trace is the identity. The D. Higman criterion therefore makes the module relatively D-projective.
