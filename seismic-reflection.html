Seismic reflection

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Seismology

The return of an elastic wave from a contrast in material properties. At normal incidence in an isotropic medium, the conventional stress-amplitude reflection coefficient is (Z2​−Z1​)/(Z2​+Z1​) for the relevant P wave or shear-wave impedance.
