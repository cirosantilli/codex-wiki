Källén–Lehmann spectral representation (Δ(p)=∫0∞​dσρ(σ)/(p2−σ+i0))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Real scalar field Scalar propagator
OurBigBook.com Words: 254 Articles: 2
For a Hermitian scalar field in a positive-norm Hilbert space with a Lorentz-invariant vacuum, the spectrum condition and completeness give the connected vacuum two-point function as a superposition of free mass-shell functions. Define the Källén–Lehmann spectral density by W(p)=2πθ(p0)ρ(p2), where W(x)=⟨0∣ϕ(x)ϕ(0)∣0⟩ and its vacuum expectation value has been subtracted if nonzero. Squared intermediate-state matrix elements make ρ a nonnegative measure supported on nonnegative mass squared. Applying time ordering to each free mass shell gives the displayed Feynman propagator representation, using iΔ=⟨Tϕϕ⟩ and signature (+−−−). Its positivity does not automatically extend to gauge-variant fields in an indefinite auxiliary space.
