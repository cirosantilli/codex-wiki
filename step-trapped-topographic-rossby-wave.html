Step-trapped topographic Rossby wave (ω=H+​κ+​+H−​κ−​fk(H+​−H−​)​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Rossby wave Topographic Rossby-wave dispersion relation

A depth discontinuity between two constant-depth rotating layers supports an exponentially trapped Rossby wave. Continuity of surface height and cross-step volume flux gives ω(H+​κ+​+H−​κ−​)=fk(H+​−H−​), where κ±2​=k2+(f2−ω2)/(gH±​) and positive roots give decay. For H±​=H0​±d with ∣d∣≪H0​, the low-frequency branch has ω≃(fd/H0​)k/k2+RD−2​​. In the Northern Hemisphere it propagates with the shallow side on its right.
