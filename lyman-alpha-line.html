OurBigBook.com Words: 583 Articles: 10
The Lyman-alpha line is the hydrogen 2p↔1s electric-dipole transition, with photon energy about 10.2eV and vacuum wavelength about 1216 angstroms. Its emission cools collisionally excited gas, while absorption traces neutral hydrogen along a background-source sightline.
