Random adjacent transposition shuffle

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 2
On the symmetric group Sn​, this Markov chain chooses uniformly from the identity and the n−1 adjacent transpositions, and multiplies on the right. Its stationary distribution is the uniform distribution on a finite set, because the generators are self-inverse. A fixed label's position is a lumped Markov chain on a path graph with off-diagonal transition probabilities 1/n to existing neighbours.
