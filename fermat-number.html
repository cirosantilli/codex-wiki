Fermat number (Fn​=22n+1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 1
A Fermat number is Fn​=22n+1 for n≥0. Distinct Fermat numbers are coprime integers. Their prime divisors are constrained by the multiplicative order of 2 modulo that prime; this remains useful whether or not the Fermat number itself is prime. The initial value F0​=3 must be distinguished when a statement restricts to positive indices.
