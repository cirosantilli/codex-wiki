Rigid inner pair in hierarchical secular inclination dynamics

If two nearby inner planets couple much more strongly to each other than to a distant third planet, their slow secular eigenmode has a common complex inclination. With L12​=L1​+L2​, the effective inner-to-outer coupling is
c=L12​L1​B13​+L2​B23​​.
(11)
Angular momentum balance makes the reverse coupling cL12​/L3​. The reduced matrix has eigenvalues 0 and −c(1+L12​/L3​). If L3​≫L12​, the nonzero frequency is approximately −c, while the fast inner differential mode has frequency −(B12​+B21​).
