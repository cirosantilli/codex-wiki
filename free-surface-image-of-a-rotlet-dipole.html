Free-surface image of a rotlet dipole

 ... Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Rotating sphere in Stokes flow Rotlet Rotlet dipole
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 1
At a flat shear-free impermeable plane, a tangential torque is reflected with opposite sign because torque is an axial vector. A parallel axial rotlet dipole therefore has an opposite signed moment at the reflected source. Its velocity vanishes at the point directly beneath the image, but its velocity gradient can turn the swimmer.
