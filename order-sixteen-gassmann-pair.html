Order-sixteen Gassmann pair (C4​×C4​, Q8​×C2​≤S16​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Permutation representation Gassmann equivalence
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
Embed C4​×C4​ and Q8​×C2​ in S16​ by their regular representations. Both have one element of order one, three of order two, and twelve of order four. An order-r element in a regular action has cycle type r16/r. Their intersections with every conjugacy class of S16​ consequently have the same size, so they are Gassmann equivalent. The first group is abelian and the second is not, so their underlying groups are not isomorphic. Finite-cover constructions from the Sunada theorem therefore give isospectral manifolds with different fundamental groups.
