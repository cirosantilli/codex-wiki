Šura-Bura lemma (C(x)=Q(x))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Connected space Connected component Quasi-component
OurBigBook.com Words: 184 Articles: 1
In a compact Hausdorff space, each connected component is its quasi-component and has a neighborhood basis of clopen sets. Let Q be the intersection of clopen neighborhoods of x. It is compact. If it splits into two nonempty compact sets, normality separates them by disjoint open neighborhoods. The finite intersection characterization of compactness gives a finite intersection of clopen neighborhoods lying in their union. Its intersection with the neighborhood containing x is then clopen and excludes part of Q, a contradiction. Thus Q is connected and equals the component. Applying the same finite-intersection argument to any neighborhood of Q proves the neighborhood-basis assertion. [ref].
