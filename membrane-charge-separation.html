Membrane charge separation (Qinside​=CVm​)

A membrane potential can coexist with near electroneutrality in the bulk cytosol and external chemical solution. The small imbalance producing the voltage lies near the cell membrane; most positive and negative electric charges balance in the bulk.[ref]
For a separate original capacitor model, choose a total membrane capacitance C=5pF and Vm​=−70mV. These are stipulated parameters, not measured properties of a particular biological cell, and this voltage is not asserted to equal the preceding EK​. This is a comparison with the chosen potassium inventory, not a fraction of all charged particles or a measurement of spatial charge density. No shape, surface area or thickness has been inferred from the chosen capacitance and volume. The calculation illustrates why substantial voltage does not require a strongly charged bulk cytosol.
