Hodge structure (HC​=⨁p+q=w​Hp,q)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Hodge theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 457 Articles: 9
A pure integral Hodge structure is a finite-rank free abelian group with a complex decomposition satisfying Hp,q=Hq,p and p+q=w. The integer w is its weight. Its equivalent Hodge filtration is Fp=⨁r≥p​Hr,w−r.
