Affine connection (∇)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle)
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 20
An affine connection on a smooth manifold is a connection on a vector bundle for its tangent bundle. It satisfies ∇fX​Y=f∇X​Y and ∇X​(fY)=X(f)Y+f∇X​Y. Its curvature is
R(X,Y)Z=∇X​∇Y​Z−∇Y​∇X​Z−∇[X,Y]​Z.
(16)
The Leibniz rule makes curvature tensorial in all three slots. An affine connection is also called a linear connection on the manifold; a Levi-Civita connection is the distinguished torsion-free metric-compatible example.
