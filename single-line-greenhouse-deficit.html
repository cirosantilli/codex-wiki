Single-line greenhouse deficit (D=π∫E(p,q)∂p​Bdp≥0)

For a black surface thermally coupled to a dry convective atmosphere, define E(p,q)=∫[1−Tν​(p)]dν, the line equivalent width between pressure p and space. If the line is sufficiently narrow to evaluate the Planck function at its centre, the outgoing flux is σTs4​−D. Integration by parts of the formal radiative transfer solution gives the displayed deficit, nonnegative because temperature and the Planck function increase downward. In the weak-line limit with constant integrated strength, E≃qSp/(gμd​) and the leading deficit is independent of normalized line shape.
