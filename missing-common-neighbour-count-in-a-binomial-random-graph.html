Missing common-neighbour count in a binomial random graph (EX=(2n​)(1−p2)n−2)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Binomial random graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 80 Articles: 1
Let X count unordered vertex pairs with no common neighbour in a binomial random graph. Each other vertex provides the required pair of edges independently, with success probability p2. A fixed pair has no common neighbour with probability (1−p2)n−2. Summing its indicators gives the displayed expectation.
