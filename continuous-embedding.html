Continuous embedding (X↪Y)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
For normed vector spaces of functions on the same domain, a continuous embedding is an injective inclusion X⊂Y satisfying ∥u∥Y​≤C∥u∥X​.
