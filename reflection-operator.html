Reflection operator

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 137 Articles: 2
For a unit vector ∣ψ⟩, the reflection I−2∣ψ⟩⟨ψ∣ negates the component parallel to ∣ψ⟩ and fixes its orthogonal complement.
