Hexagon

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Polygon
OurBigBook.com Words: 35 Articles: 1
A polygon with six sides and six vertices. A regular hexagon has equal sides and angles, with each interior angle equal to 2π/3. Its symmetry group consists of six rotations and six reflections, forming a dihedral group of order twelve.
