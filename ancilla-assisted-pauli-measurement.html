Ancilla-assisted Pauli measurement

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Measurement of a Pauli observable
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Prepare an ancilla in ∣+⟩, use it to control a Hermitian Pauli operator P, apply a Hadamard gate, and measure the ancilla. Outcome s applies the projector [I+(−1)sP]/2 to the data register.
