Weight multiplicities in the A2 tensor square of highest weight (2,0)

 ... Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Highest-weight representation A2 representation of highest weight (2,0) Tensor square of the A2 representation of highest weight (2,0)
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The tensor square has fifteen distinct weights: three outer corners of multiplicity one, six near-corner edge weights of multiplicity two, three edge midpoints of multiplicity three, and three interior weights of multiplicity four. In the irreducible components only R(2,1) is weight-degenerate: (1,0),(−1,1),(0,−1) each have multiplicity two. All weights of R(4,0) and R(0,2) have multiplicity one.
