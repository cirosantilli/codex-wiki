Real (p, p)-form (η​=η)

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Differential form of type (p, q)
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A differential form of type (p, q) with equal holomorphic and antiholomorphic degrees is real when it is fixed by complex conjugation. For such a form, ∂ˉη​=∂η, so ∂ˉη=0 implies dη=0. The converse follows from the distinct types of ∂η and ∂ˉη.
