Commutator identity for Lie derivatives ([LX​,LY​]=L[X,Y]​)

 ... Fiber bundle Vector bundle Tensor product of vector bundles Tensor field Tensor derivation Lie derivative of a tensor field
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The Lie derivative of a tensor field satisfies [LX​,LY​]=L[X,Y]​. On smooth functions this is the definition of the Lie bracket of vector fields; on vector fields it follows from the Jacobi identity. The Leibniz rule and contraction compatibility extend the equality to tensor fields. The cyclic double-commutator identity also follows directly from associativity of operator composition.
