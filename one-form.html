One-form

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 1
A differential 1-form is a smooth section of the cotangent bundle; it assigns a covector to every point of a smooth manifold. In planar coordinates, α=P(x,y)dx+Q(x,y)dy evaluates a tangent vector v as P(x,y)vx​+Q(x,y)vy​.
Along a parametric curve γ(t)=(x(t),y(t)), its line integral is
∫γ​α=∫ab​[P(x(t),y(t))x′(t)+Q(x(t),y(t))y′(t)]dt.
(5)
Reversing the direction reverses the integral. A force expressed as a one-form therefore accumulates work along a directed path. In Euclidean coordinates its components can be displayed as arrows, but identifying a one-form with a vector field uses an inner product; the covector field itself does not require a Riemannian metric.
