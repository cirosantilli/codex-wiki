Bend resistance (Rij,kl​=(Vk​−Vl​)/Ii→j​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Ballistic electron transport
OurBigBook.com Words: 239 Articles: 3
A four-terminal bend resistance uses adjacent current contacts and the other adjacent pair as voltage probes. It can be negative when a ballistic injector preferentially populates the opposite voltage probe. Its sign is a nonlocal routing property, not the sign of the total electrical dissipation.
