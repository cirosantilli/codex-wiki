Euler force (−mΩ˙×r)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Reference frame Rotating reference frame
OurBigBook.com Words: 40 Articles: 1
A mass m in a rotating reference frame with varying angular velocity experiences this apparent force. Dividing it by m gives the Euler acceleration.
