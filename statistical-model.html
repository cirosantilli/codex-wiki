Bayesian network structure score (p(G∣D))

Words: 90
Bayesian network OurBigBook.com
For observations D and graph G, Bayes' theorem gives p(G∣D)∝π(G)∫p(D∣θG​,G)π(θG​∣G)dθG​. Here π(G) is the graph prior distribution and θG​ the local distribution statistical parameters. The integral is Bayesian model evidence; it averages over nuisance parameters with a proper statistical parameter prior distribution. For independent complete observations, the node-factorized likelihood function and independence of local statistical parameter prior distributions make the evidence factor over nodes; suitable conjugate priors can make the local integrals analytic. With missing node observations, integrating out unobserved values can couple the local parameters, so independence of local prior distributions alone does not guarantee this factorization. Proper priors and coherent hyperparameters matter for comparing different graphs.

Under faithfulness of a directed acyclic graph, every conditioning set screening a vertex from all remaining vertices contains its graphical Markov blanket. A missing adjacent vertex leaves a one-edge active path; a missing other parent of a child leaves the active path k→h←u, since the child h is necessarily conditioned. Thus the graphical blanket is the unique smallest screening set.
