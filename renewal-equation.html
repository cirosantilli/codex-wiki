Renewal equation (un​=∑k=1n​fk​un−k​, u0​=1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Renewal process
OurBigBook.com Words: 173 Articles: 3
A renewal equation decomposes a process at its first renewal. For returns of a Markov chain to a fixed state, fk​ is the first-return probability and un​ the probability of being at that state at time n. Their probability generating functions satisfy U=1+FU.
