For a full-row-rank k×p matrix R and the null hypothesis Rθ=r, put
Wn,R​=n(Rθn​−r)T[RI(θn​)−1RT]−1(Rθn​−r).
(209)
Under the null, asymptotic normality and Slutsky theorem give Wn,R​d​χk2​.
