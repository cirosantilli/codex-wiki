Calling convention

 Home Computer science Programming language Assembly language
OurBigBook.com Words: 221 Articles: 5
The agreement between a caller and a subroutine on argument and result placement, processor register preservation, and call stack organization. Callee-saved registers retain their incoming values across a call; caller-saved registers must be saved by the caller if their values are still needed afterwards.
