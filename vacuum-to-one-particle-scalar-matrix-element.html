Vacuum-to-one-particle scalar matrix element (⟨Ω∣ϕ(x)∣p⟩=Fe−ip⋅x)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Quantized field Fock space One-particle state
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For a massive spin-zero one-particle state with covariant normalization and a Lorentz scalar field, Lorentz invariance makes F=⟨Ω∣ϕ(0)∣p⟩ independent of the on-shell four-momentum within a fixed species. A Lorentz boost to the rest frame, invariance of the vacuum state and scalar transformation at the origin prove this. Translation covariance of a quantum field then supplies the plane-wave factor. With noncovariant normalization, the boost law and the displayed momentum independence acquire energy factors.
