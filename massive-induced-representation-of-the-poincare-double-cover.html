Massive induced representation of the Poincare double cover (U(Λ,a)∣p,s,σ⟩=ei(Λp)⋅a∑τ​Dτσ(s)​(W(Λ,p))∣Λp,s,τ⟩)

Fix m>0, k=(m,0), and a rotationless Lorentz boost L(p) taking k to p on the positive-energy mass shell. The little group on the Lorentz spinor double cover is SU(2). Its spin-s irreducible representation induces a unitary representation on L2(d3p/(2p0))⊗C2s+1. Here W(Λ,p)=L(Λp)−1ΛL(p) is the Wigner rotation, lifted to SU(2) when necessary, and the translation convention is U(I,a)∣p⟩=eip⋅a∣p⟩. The little-group cocycle proves the group law, and Wigner's classification gives irreducibility. Every nonnegative integer or half-integer spin is allowed on the double cover; descent to the ordinary proper orthochronous Poincaré group requires integer spin, because its 2π rotation must act as the identity.
