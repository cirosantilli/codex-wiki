Coordination complex

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 6
A coordination complex contains a central atom, often a metal ion, bound to surrounding ligands in coordination chemistry. The metal oxidation state and the ligand donor types are separate pieces of its description.
