Planar rotation (Rθ​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Rotation matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
A planar rotation about the origin is the linear map represented by the rotation matrix Rθ​=(cosθsinθ​−sinθcosθ​). It preserves inner products, lengths, oriented angles and area, with positive angles measured anticlockwise. Its inverse is R−θ​ and its determinant is one.
