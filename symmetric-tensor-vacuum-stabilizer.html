Symmetric tensor vacuum stabilizer (H=S(∏i​O(mi​)))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Spontaneous symmetry breaking
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
A real symmetric tensor of the special orthogonal group transforms as Q↦OQOT. If its vacuum expectation value has distinct eigenvalues with multiplicities m1​,…,mp​, its stabilizer subgroup is S(O(m1​)×⋯×O(mp​)): each transformation preserves each eigenspace and the product of the block determinants is one. Its identity component is ∏i​SO(mi​). Thus a scalar multiple of the identity preserves the whole group, whereas a tensor with simple spectrum preserves only diagonal signs with total sign one. A tracelessness condition constrains the eigenvalues but does not change this stabilizer formula.
