Topological quantum order

Words: 114 Articles: 1
Topological quantum matter OurBigBook.com
The local-pair condition for topological quantum order asks for an orthogonal partner state with local indistinguishability on supports of diameter at most a fixed fraction of the system diameter, with a fixed error smaller than one. This criterion captures a form of macroscopic information hidden from local observables; it is not a complete definition of every topological phase.

Backward preservation of local indistinguishability

Words: 61
Topological quantum order OurBigBook.com
Undo a short evolution of two orthogonal locally indistinguishable states. Orthogonality is preserved exactly. A Lieb-Robinson bound localizes every backward-evolved observable within an expanded region and bounds its norm error. After normalizing the approximate observable, the initial expectation difference is at most ε(1+δ)+2δ. Polynomial volume growth in the system diameter controls the localization prefactor and yields preservation for sufficiently small linear-time coefficient.

Local indistinguishability

Words: 47
Topological quantum matter OurBigBook.com
Two quantum states are locally indistinguishable at a prescribed scale if all operators of norm at most one supported below that scale have nearly equal expectation values. This is a statement about restricted observables, not equality of the global states. An orthogonal pair can be locally indistinguishable on macroscopically large regions.

Abelian Chern--Simons theory

Words: 264 Articles: 7
Topological quantum matter OurBigBook.com
An Abelian Chern--Simons theory with integer symmetric invertible matrix K describes Abelian anyons whose braiding phases are determined by K−1.

Chern-Simons source stress convention (Tμν​=2cJ(μ​Aν)​−cgμν​Jρ​Aρ)

Words: 67
Abelian Chern--Simons theory OurBigBook.com
The pure Abelian Chern--Simons theory has zero metric stress. For a source written as −c∫⋆J∧A, holding the covariant components of the one-forms J,A fixed makes the source equal to −c∫−g​gμνJμ​Aν​d3x. Variation using δ−g​=−−g​gμν​δgμν/2 gives the displayed stress-energy tensor. If the independent source is instead the fixed two-form J=⋆J, the source action is metric-independent and its stress vanishes. The variational data must therefore be stated.

K-matrix (K)

Words: 122 Articles: 3
Abelian Chern--Simons theory OurBigBook.com
The K-matrix is the integral bilinear form multiplying the Abelian Chern--Simons gauge fields. Integer unimodular basis changes send K to WKWT without changing the phase.

Anyon lattice of an Abelian Chern--Simons theory

Words: 27
K-matrix OurBigBook.com
For an invertible integer K-matrix, Abelian anyons are integer vectors q modulo local particles KZN. Their mutual full-braiding phase is exp(2πiqTK−1q′).

Chern--Simons flux attachment

Words: 36
K-matrix OurBigBook.com
In an Abelian Chern--Simons theory, the equation of motion ties a quasiparticle's gauge charge q to gauge flux Φ=2πℏK−1q. A quasiparticle of charge q′ encircling it therefore acquires the Aharonov--Bohm phase 2πq′TK−1q.

Lagrangian subgroup of Abelian anyons

Words: 34
K-matrix OurBigBook.com
A Lagrangian subgroup is a maximal set of mutually bosonic anyons that can consistently condense at a gapped boundary. An anyon can join the condensate only if it braids trivially with every already-condensed anyon.

Torus ground-state degeneracy of an Abelian Chern--Simons theory

An Abelian K-matrix Chern--Simons theory has ∣detK∣ topologically distinct ground states on a torus.

Tx​(q′)Ty​(q)=e2πiq′TK−1qTy​(q)Tx​(q′).

The induced pairing on Zn/KZn is nondegenerate, so this algebra has a representation of dimension at least ∣detK∣.

An anyon is a two-dimensional quasiparticle whose exchange or braiding statistics can differ from bosonic and fermionic statistics.

Topological spin (θa​)

Words: 34
Anyon OurBigBook.com
The topological spin θa​ is the phase produced by a 2π rotation of anyon a, equivalently its self-exchange phase in an Abelian anyon theory. The two-dimensional spin-statistics relation is θa​=e2πisa​.

Charge-flux composite

Anyon OurBigBook.com
A charge-flux composite carries electric charge q and localized magnetic flux Φ. Mutual encircling of (q1​,Φ1​) and (q2​,Φ2​) produces the phase exp[i(q1​Φ2​+q2​Φ1​)/ℏ] from the Aharonov-Bohm and Aharonov-Casher effects.

Aharonov-Bohm effect

Words: 27
Charge-flux composite OurBigBook.com
The Aharonov-Bohm effect is the phase qΦ/ℏ acquired when a charge q encircles magnetic flux Φ, even when the magnetic field vanishes along the charge's path.

Aharonov-Casher effect

The Aharonov-Casher effect is the electromagnetic dual in which a magnetic moment or flux acquires a phase by encircling electric charge.

Mutual semion

Two particle types are mutual semions when taking either once around the other multiplies the state by −1.

An anyon condenses at a boundary when its string operator may terminate there without leaving an excitation, so the boundary can absorb or emit that anyon.

An electric boundary of the surface code condenses electric anyons, while a magnetic boundary condenses magnetic anyons. A local boundary Pauli can create a single condensed excitation because its string may end at that boundary.

A Majorana zero mode is a spatially localized Majorana operator that commutes with a gapped quadratic Hamiltonian, exactly or up to exponentially small finite-size corrections.
Majorana zero mode at a mass domain wall
Words: 89 Articles: 1
Majorana zero mode OurBigBook.com
Two counterpropagating Majorana fields coupled by a real mass m(x) bind a Majorana zero mode when m changes sign. Its envelope is proportional to exp[−∫xm(s)ds/(ℏv)] up to orientation conventions, so a constant asymptotic mass gives localization length ℏv/∣m∣.
Continuum p-wave Majorana interface mode
Words: 47
Majorana zero mode at a mass domain wall OurBigBook.com
For the long-wavelength Bogoliubov--de Gennes Hamiltonian H=−μ(x)τz​−iℏΔτx​∂x​, a sign change from μ>0 in a one-dimensional p-wave superconductor to μ<0 in the vacuum binds a zero mode of width ξ=ℏΔ/∣μ∣. Neglecting the quadratic kinetic term is valid when ∣μ∣≪mΔ2 up to an inessential numerical factor.
Dense encoding with Majorana zero modes (MZM dense encoding)
Words: 20
Majorana zero mode OurBigBook.com
2M Majorana zero modes define M complex fermionic modes. Fixing total fermion parity leaves a 2M−1-dimensional ground space, encoding M−1 qubits.

Majorana zero mode OurBigBook.com
When Majorana zero modes have disjoint exponentially localized supports, every local parity-even observable acts as a scalar on the fixed-parity ground space. Finite overlap changes this by terms exponentially small in separation divided by localization length.
Majorana braiding operator
Words: 107 Articles: 2
Majorana zero mode OurBigBook.com
The unitary Rab​=exp(πγa​γb​/4) exchanges two Majorana operators by conjugation, sending one to the other and the other to its negative, with signs fixed by braid orientation.
Measurement-only Majorana braiding
Words: 81 Articles: 1
Majorana braiding operator OurBigBook.com
Pairwise fermion-parity measurements involving an ancillary Majorana mode can reproduce a Majorana braiding operator without physically moving the zero modes. Projecting the ancilla back to its initial parity sector turns the product of projectors into the braid unitary up to normalization and known outcome-dependent signs.
Forced Majorana parity measurement
Words: 41
Measurement-only Majorana braiding OurBigBook.com
A forced Majorana parity measurement alternates an undesired pairwise measurement with a reference parity measurement until the desired outcome occurs. Anticommuting parity observables give either result with probability one half, so the protocol terminates almost surely with a geometric waiting time.

Fermion parity ((−1)F)

Words: 76 Articles: 1
Quadratic fermion Hamiltonian OurBigBook.com
Fermion parity is (−1)F. It acts as plus one on bosonic states and minus one on fermionic states. Even operators commute with it and odd operators, including supersymmetry generators, anticommute with it. Local observables are parity even because observable algebras of spacelike separated regions must commute.
Fermion-parity measurement
Words: 29
Fermion parity OurBigBook.com
A fermion-parity measurement projects onto an eigenspace of the parity of a chosen collection of fermionic modes. For two Majorana fermion operators γa​ and γb​, the projectors are (1±iγa​γb​)/2.

A chiral Majorana edge mode is a self-adjoint one-way boundary field with Hamiltonian proportional to −i∫c∂x​cdx. Its group velocity has one sign throughout the low-energy branch.

Boundary condition of a chiral Majorana edge mode

Words: 37
Chiral Majorana edge mode OurBigBook.com
On a closed boundary, a chiral Majorana field is antiperiodic in the sector with even enclosed vortex parity and periodic in the sector with odd enclosed vortex parity. Only the periodic sector contains a zero-momentum Majorana mode.

Kitaev chain

Topological superconductor OurBigBook.com
The Kitaev chain is a one-dimensional spinless p-wave superconductor. Its topological phase has a Majorana zero mode at each end of an open chain.

Topological equivalence of gapped Hamiltonians

Words: 93 Articles: 2
Topological quantum matter OurBigBook.com
Two local gapped Hamiltonians are topologically equivalent when they are connected by a continuous path of local Hamiltonians with a nonzero bulk gap, equivalently when their ground spaces are related by quasi-local evolution.

Quasi-adiabatic continuation

Words: 30
Topological equivalence of gapped Hamiltonians OurBigBook.com
Quasi-adiabatic continuation implements a gapped path of local Hamiltonians by a quasi-local unitary. It dresses local operators with exponentially decaying tails while preserving their action within the corresponding low-energy spaces.

For a two-component gapped BdG Hamiltonian constrained to a plane, the winding number counts how many times its coefficient vector encircles the origin as crystal momentum traverses the Brillouin zone.

A symmetry-protected topological phase is short-range entangled but cannot be deformed to a product state without breaking a protecting symmetry or closing the gap.

An on-site symmetry of an injective MPS acts on virtual indices by matrices defined up to phase. Their projective cohomology class is the one-dimensional SPT invariant.

On an open chain, a nontrivial virtual projective representation appears as edge degrees of freedom. Symmetry-preserving local perturbations cannot remove their projective class without a bulk phase transition or symmetry breaking.

Cluster state

Words: 22
Symmetry-protected topological phase OurBigBook.com
The one-dimensional cluster state is the common positive eigenstate of commuting stabilizers Zj−1​Xj​Zj+1​ and realizes a nontrivial Z2​×Z2​ SPT phase.

Intrinsic topological order

Words: 38
Topological quantum matter OurBigBook.com
Intrinsic topological order survives without a protecting symmetry and is characterized by features such as topology-dependent ground-state degeneracy and anyonic excitations. One-dimensional bosonic gapped systems have no intrinsic topological order, although they can have symmetry-protected topological order.

Kramers--Wannier duality

Words: 94 Articles: 2
Topological quantum matter OurBigBook.com
Kramers--Wannier duality exchanges order variables with domain-wall or disorder variables. In multiple dimensions it naturally appears as a tensor-network map with a nontrivial symmetry-sector kernel.

Kramers--Wannier intertwiner

Words: 39
Kramers--Wannier duality OurBigBook.com
For a one-dimensional periodic spin chain, a Kramers--Wannier intertwiner maps vertex spins in the X basis to edge domain walls. It obeys D(Xi​Xi+1​)=Zi+1/2​D and DZi​=(Xi−1/2​Xi+1/2​)D and identifies spin configurations related by the global Z2​ symmetry.

Kramers--Wannier projected entangled pair operator (Kramers--Wannier PEPO)

Words: 30
Kramers--Wannier duality OurBigBook.com
In two dimensions, vertex COPY tensors and edge XOR tensors form a PEPO that maps vertex spins to edge domain walls. Its image obeys a zero-flux constraint around every plaquette.

A one-form symmetry is generated on closed codimension-one manifolds and acts on line operators rather than local point operators.
