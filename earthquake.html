Earthquake

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth
OurBigBook.com Words: 349 Articles: 10
A transient release of stored mechanical energy in the solid planet, often associated with fault motion. Plate tectonics is a major terrestrial driver, but earthquakes can also occur without an active global plate system.
