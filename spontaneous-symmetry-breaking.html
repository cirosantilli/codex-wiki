Spontaneous symmetry breaking (SSB)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 33
Spontaneous symmetry breaking occurs when the action and equations have a symmetry but a chosen ground state is invariant only under a proper subgroup. Acting with the broken symmetry generates a degenerate vacuum manifold.
