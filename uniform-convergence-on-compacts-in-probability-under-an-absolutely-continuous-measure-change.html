Uniform convergence on compacts in probability under an absolutely continuous measure change

If Q≪P, uniform convergence on compacts in probability under P implies the same convergence under Q. Indeed, let Z=dQ/dP be the Radon-Nikodym derivative. For every event E,
Q(E)≤KP(E)+EP​[Z1{Z>K}​].
(44)
Integrability makes the second term arbitrarily small for large K. Thus P(En​)→0 implies Q(En​)→0; apply this to each event where the supremum on [0,T] exceeds a fixed tolerance. Only absolute continuity of measures is needed, not equivalence.
