Slip length (ℓs​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Navier slip boundary condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The slip length is the distance obtained by extrapolating a locally linear tangential velocity profile back to zero velocity beneath a wall. A Navier slip boundary condition has ub​=ℓs​∂n​u. Zero slip length gives a no-slip boundary condition; large slip permits approximately plug-like motion when the surrounding geometry and forces allow it.
