Cold-atom realization of the Haldane model

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Topological quantum matter Chern insulator Haldane model
OurBigBook.com Words: 115
Gregor Jotzu and collaborators realized an artificial Haldane model in 2014. They loaded spin-polarized potassium-40 fermions into a laser-made honeycomb lattice. Piezoelectric mirrors shook the optical lattice along two axes; their relative phase controlled time-reversal symmetry breaking. Optical phase control adjusted the sublattice offset.[ref]
A magnetic field gradient drove Bloch oscillations. Band mapping measured interband transfer to locate valley-gap closures. Opposite-force drift measurements distinguished cancelling from reinforcing Berry curvature contributions; reversing shaking handedness reversed the drift. Interband transfer and harmonic confinement affected this readout, including drift beyond ideal transition lines. This neutral-atom Floquet Hamiltonian realization did not measure electrical sheet conductivity. Magnetic fields were used for preparation and forcing.[ref]
