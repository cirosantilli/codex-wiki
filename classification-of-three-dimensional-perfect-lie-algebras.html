Classification of three-dimensional perfect Lie algebras (dimg=3, [g,g]=g ⟹ g≅sl2​(k))

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Perfect Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 118
Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, every three-dimensional perfect Lie algebra is isomorphic to the sl2 Lie algebra. Indeed the trace of a commutator gives tr(adx)=0 for all x, because each element is a sum of Lie brackets. If every adx were a nilpotent linear map, the Engel theorem would make the Lie algebra nilpotent, contradicting its constant nonzero lower central series of a Lie algebra. Some adx therefore has a nonzero eigenvalue λ. Its other eigenvalues are 0,−λ, since it kills x and has zero trace. They are distinct, so its eigenvectors give a basis h,e,f after scaling x to h=2x/λ, with [h,e]=2e and [h,f]=−2f. The Jacobi identity puts [e,f] in the zero eigenspace kh. Its coefficient cannot vanish, since otherwise the derived algebra would lie in ke⊕kf. Rescaling f gives [e,f]=h. Thus this basis is an sl2 triple, proving the claimed Lie algebra isomorphism.
