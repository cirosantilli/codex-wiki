Area-square-root optical focusing equation (A1/2(A1/2)′′​=−Rab​kakb/2−σ2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Geodesic congruence Null geodesic congruence Null Raychaudhuri equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 1
For an affinely parametrized twist-free null geodesic congruence, let A be local transported screen area and σ2=σAB​σAB/2. The Null Raychaudhuri equation gives θ′=−θ2/2−2σ2−Rab​kakb and A′/A=θ. Combining these gives the displayed equation. Positive shear focuses the beam and has a negative sign. For nonuniform expansion this is a local area identity, not the same differential equation for the entire integrated horizon area.
