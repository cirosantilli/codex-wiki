Linear-feedback shift register

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Coding theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 307 Articles: 8
A binary linear-feedback shift register of degree d updates a d-bit state by shifting and inserting a fixed linear combination over F2​. With recurrence
sn+d​=ad−1​sn+d−1​+⋯+a0​sn​,
(89)
its feedback polynomial is xd+ad−1​xd−1+⋯+a0​.
