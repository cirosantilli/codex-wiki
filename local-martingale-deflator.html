Local martingale deflator

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Risk-neutral measure Martingale deflator
OurBigBook.com Words: 339 Articles: 6
A local martingale deflator is a strictly positive process Y such that multiplying each traded asset price by Y produces a local martingale. It can support no-arbitrage arguments even when the deflated prices are not true martingales.
