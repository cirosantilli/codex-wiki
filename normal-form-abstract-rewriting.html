Normal form (abstract rewriting)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Computability theory Lambda calculus
OurBigBook.com Words: 52 Articles: 1
An expression in normal form admits no further reductions. A term is strongly normalizing when every reduction sequence from it terminates; weak normalization requires only one terminating sequence. Strong normalization theorem for simply typed lambda calculus establishes the stronger property for well-typed terms.
