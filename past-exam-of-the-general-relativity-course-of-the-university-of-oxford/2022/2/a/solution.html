Choose normal coordinates ξα centered at an event p. At p, the metric tensor in these coordinates is the constant Minkowski metric ηαβ​=diag(−1,1,1,1), with ξ0 measured in length units, and its first partial derivatives vanish. In arbitrary coordinates xμ, the chain rule for the line element gives
ds2​p​=ηαβ​dξαdξβ=gμν​(p)dxμdxν,
(18)
so
gμν​(p)=ηαβ​∂xμ∂ξα​∂xν∂ξβ​​p​.​
(19)
This construction is pointwise. At nearby events, the metric tensor in the chosen normal coordinates is gαβ(ξ)​(ξ)=ηαβ​+O(∣ξ∣2), with the quadratic corrections determined by the Riemann curvature tensor. Replacing g(ξ) by one constant Minkowski metric throughout an open region would make that region flat, so it cannot describe general curved spacetime exactly.
