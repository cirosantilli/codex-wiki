The Earth-Moon pair is a gravitationally bound system, so its separation does not obey r˙=H0​r. The Hubble flow describes the motion of the approximately homogeneous cosmological matter distribution; it is not a compulsory extra velocity assigned to the members of every local orbit. Thus the numerical proximity of the counterfactual rate to 3.8cmyr−1 is not evidence for that explanation.
The observed recession arises principally from tidal dissipation. The Earth rotates faster than the Moon orbits, so the displaced tidal response transfers angular momentum from the Earth's spin into the lunar orbit. For a nearly circular orbit, increasing orbital angular momentum increases its size. A model of this effect must include the appropriate tidal response, rather than add a universal Hubble flow recession.
