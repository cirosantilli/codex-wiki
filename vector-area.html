The vector area of an oriented surface is the integral of its unit normal vector times its area element. If its oriented boundary is a closed curve C, Stokes theorem gives
S=21​∮C​r×dr.
(37)
Consequently it depends only on the oriented boundary, not on the spanning surface. For a planar surface it is signed area times a unit normal vector. A current loop has magnetic dipole moment m=IS; the scalar area of a nonplanar spanning surface is not interchangeable with this vector.
