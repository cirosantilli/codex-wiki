If a subset A of a cyclic group has normalized additive energy at most α4+ϵ, every nonconstant Fourier coefficient on a finite abelian group of its indicator has modulus at most ϵ1/4. Expanding the count of a+c=2b gives the frequency product 1A​​(r)1C​​(r)1B​​(−2r). The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality and Parseval identity on a finite group bound its nonconstant part by the displayed quantity. Character doubling has multiplicity gcd(2,N), so no assumption that the modulus is odd is needed. The error is a normalized density error multiplied by N2.
