Comparison-adjunction unit criterion (λ(A,a)​ invertible⟺Gqa​ is a coequalizer)

 ... Foundations of mathematics Category theory Monad Eilenberg-Moore category Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor Left adjoint to the Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
For F⊣G inducing T=GF, the Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor has a left adjoint exactly when every algebra (A,a) has a coequalizer qa​:FA→L(A,a) of Fa,εFA​:FTA⇉FA. Transposition identifies maps equalizing this pair with monad algebra morphisms (A,a)→K(D), proving both directions of the criterion. The unit is λ=Gqa​ηA​ and satisfies Gqa​=λa. In the base category, a:TA→A is a split coequalizer of Ta,μA​:T2A⇉TA, split by ηA​ and ηTA​. Consequently λ is invertible exactly when Gqa​ is also a coequalizer. The unit is invertible everywhere precisely when G preserves all these specified coequalizers; preservation of arbitrary coequalizers is not required.
