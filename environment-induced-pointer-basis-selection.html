Environment-induced pointer-basis selection

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum decoherence Pointer basis
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
If record projectors commute with an apparatus-environment interaction, their populations are preserved while the corresponding environmental states can become distinguishable. Their conditional environment overlap then suppresses interference in the apparatus reduced density matrix. A nondegenerate record coupling can thereby select robust sectors; commutation alone does not select a unique basis in a degenerate or trivial interaction.[ref]
