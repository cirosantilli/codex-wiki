One-loop proper vertices of massless phi-fourth theory (τ4(1)​=2λ2​(fs​+ft​+fu​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Real scalar field Quartic interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
The interaction vertex −iλ and scalar propagator −i/(p2−iϵ) give one four-point bubble diagram for each of three momentum channels, each with Feynman-diagram symmetry factor 1/2. The two-point tadpole diagram is zero as a scaleless integral in dimensional regularization. If full proper vertices include the free quadratic kernel, τ2(0)​=−p2; the interaction self-energy convention instead starts at zero.
