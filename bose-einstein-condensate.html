A Bose-Einstein condensate is a state with macroscopic occupation of a one-particle mode. A phase-selected mean-field description can use ⟨ψ⟩=0, while a fixed-number finite-volume state need not have a nonzero field expectation. Bose-Einstein condensation and superfluidity are related but distinct notions; long-wavelength phase fluctuations can invalidate a uniform condensate saddle in low dimensions.
