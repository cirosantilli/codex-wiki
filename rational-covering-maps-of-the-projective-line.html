Rational covering maps of the projective line (dimC​Ratm​=2m+1)

 ... Complex analysis Isolated singularity Classification of isolated singularities Pole Meromorphic function Rational map (complex analysis)
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
A degree-m rational map of the complex projective line is represented by two degree-at-most-m polynomials without a common root, modulo common scalar multiplication. This is an open set of CP2m+1. Quotienting by domain Möbius transformations subtracts three complex dimensions, giving real dimension 4m−4 for degree m≥2. These are the local covering parameters of a multiple-covered J-holomorphic sphere.
