MICROSCOPE (satellite)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Equivalence principle Weak equivalence principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A satellite experiment testing the universality of free fall. Electrostatic control keeps test masses made from titanium and platinum alloys on matched orbital motion; the required differential forces constrain composition dependence. A reference sensor uses masses of the same composition. The experiment tests the weak equivalence principle, rather than the full strong equivalence principle.[ref]
