Rational interpolation linearization (q(xi​)=fi​r(xi​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Interpolation
OurBigBook.com Words: 100
For rational interpolation with degq≤m and a monic denominator r(x)=xℓ−∑j=0ℓ−1​bj​xj, multiplying through gives the linear system
∑j=0m​aj​xij​+fi​∑j=0ℓ−1​bj​xij​=fi​xiℓ​.
(1)
These equations are equivalent to the fraction conditions only when all r(xi​) are nonzero. If fi​=xim+1​ and m+ℓ=n, the coefficient matrix is the Vandermonde matrix with rows (1,xi​,…,xin​), so distinct interpolation nodes give a unique coefficient solution. More constructively, divide W(x)=∏i=0n​(x−xi​) by xm+1 as W=xm+1R+S, degS≤m. Then r=R, q=−S, since xm+1r−q is the unique monic degree-n+1 polynomial vanishing at all the interpolation nodes. The quotient may vanish at a node, so unique solvability of this linear system does not prove existence of a rational interpolant.
