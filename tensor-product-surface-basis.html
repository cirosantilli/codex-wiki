Tensor-product surface basis (bjk​(u,v)=ϕj​(u)ψk​(v))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Smooth surface Parametric surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 1
Products of two univariate bases form a surface basis. Nonnegativity, partition of unity and coordinate-wise linear precision pass to the products by multiplication of the respective univariate identities. If the factors are Cr and Cs, each mixed derivative through orders r,s is the product of the corresponding derivatives, giving continuous mixed derivatives and joint Cmin(r,s) regularity.
