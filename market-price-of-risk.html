Market price of risk (λt​=(μt​−rt​)/σt​)

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Risk-neutral measure Martingale deflator Local martingale deflator
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 2
In a one-factor diffusion market, the market price of risk is the excess drift per unit spot volatility. It is the coefficient in the Brownian motion part of a local martingale deflator, dYt​=−Yt​(rt​dt+λt​dWt​).
