Tricritical wing coexistence factorization (f−f(a)=6v​(M−a)2(M−b)2[(M+s)2+p])

Take two nonnegative coexisting minima a≤b, put s=a+b and p=ab, and fix v>0. For the sextic Landau free energy with quadratic, quartic, sextic and linear terms, equal stationary minimum values occur at
u=32v​(−2s2+3p),r=3v​(s4−s2p+3p2),h=3v​s3p.
(37)
At these coefficients,
f(M)−f(a)=6v​(M−a)2(M−b)2[(M+s)2+p]≥0.
(38)
Thus both are global minima. The case a=0 gives the zero-field three-phase line, including the negative minimum; the limit a=b gives the tricritical wing critical edge. Reflecting M,h gives the negative-field wing.
