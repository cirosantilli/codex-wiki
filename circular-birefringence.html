Circular birefringence

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Maxwell equations Maxwell equations in matter Linear anisotropic dielectric Birefringence
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 1
Circular birefringence means different refractive indices for opposite circular polarizations. In a transparent isotropic medium, their relative wave phase changes rotate the direction of transmitted linear polarization; optical activity gives the solution measurement and convention.
