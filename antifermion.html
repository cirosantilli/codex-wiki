Antifermion

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Mode expansion of a Dirac field Antiparticle
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The antiparticle of a fermion. In a Dirac field, its external wavefunctions are the negative-frequency v spinors, while its physical energy is positive. Fermion-number arrows on Feynman diagrams run opposite an antifermion's physical momentum.
