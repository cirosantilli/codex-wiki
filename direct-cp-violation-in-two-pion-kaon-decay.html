Direct CP violation concerns the decay transition itself. At leading order, the charged and neutral two-pion decay amplitude ratios separate a common ϵ from channel-dependent ϵ′.[ref]
For the corresponding double ratio of partial decay widths,

The expansion follows from (1−2x)/(1+x)=1−3x+O(x2). A single nonzero long-lived two-pion yield does not isolate x: it can already occur with ϵ′=0. Thus the 1964 evidence for CP violation must be distinguished from the later experimental establishment of direct CP violation through ϵ′.
