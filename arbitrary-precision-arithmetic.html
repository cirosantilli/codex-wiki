Arbitrary-precision arithmetic

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Integer arithmetic
OurBigBook.com Words: 291 Articles: 6
Integer arithmetic in which storage grows with the number of numerical digits needed, instead of imposing a fixed word size. It allows exact addition, multiplication, and exponentiation on large integers, subject to available memory; operation costs grow with operand length.
