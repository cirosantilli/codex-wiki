Metallicity distribution in a leaky box (p(Z)=ye−1​e−Z/ye​)

 ... Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy evolution Galactic chemical evolution Closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution Leaky-box model of galactic chemical evolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For initially pristine gas, zero bulk mass return, a constant stellar yield y and a well-mixed galactic outflow of mass-loading factor ν, gas conservation gives dMg​=−(1+ν)dMs​ and metal accounting gives Mg​dZ=ydMs​. Consequently Mg​=Mg,0​e−Z/ye​ with ye​=y/(1+ν). Since dMs​/dZ=Mg​/y, complete depletion gives a normalized mass-weighted metallicity distribution function p(Z)=e−Z/ye​/ye​ and mean stellar metallicity ye​. A time-independent initial mass function makes the distribution of surviving tracers proportional to this distribution; a sample selected by light need not have the same weights.
