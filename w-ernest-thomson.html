Author of the 1894 physical taper comparison in The True Position of Oxygen as a Restorative in Carbonic Acid Poisoning by W. Ernest Thomson. The author identity and career are corroborated by the British Journal of Ophthalmology obituary, 1938, p. 59, identifying the thesis and its publication. Exact-name portrait searches did not establish a securely identified portrait; no portrait or dates are invented.
