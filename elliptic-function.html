Elliptic function

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 315 Articles: 9
An elliptic function is a meromorphic function with two real-linearly independent periods ω1​,ω2​. A fundamental cell is a half-open parallelogram
z0​+{sω1​+tω2​:0≤s,t<1}.
(100)
Opposite boundary integrals cancel. Consequently a nonconstant elliptic function has equally many zeros and poles in a cell, counted with multiplicity, and the sum of its pole residues in a cell is zero.
