Triangular number recurrence (Tn​−Tn−1​=n)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Figurate number Triangular number
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
For a positive integer n, adding the row of length n to the previous arrangement gives Tn​=Tn−1​+n. Thus the first differences of the triangular numbers are 1,2,3,…. The triangular number recurrence provides incremental computation: after T6​=21, adding seven gives T7​=28.
