Differentiability almost everywhere of supercritical Sobolev functions

For p>n, the Hölder continuous function representative u∗ of u∈W1,p(Rn) has a classical Fréchet derivative at almost every point, equal to its weak derivative. At a point x where the p-mean oscillation of Du tends to zero, apply the Morrey inequality on a cube to u∗(y)−u∗(x)−Du(x)⋅(y−x). On a cube of side comparable to ∣y−x∣, its oscillation is at most
