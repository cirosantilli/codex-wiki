Koszul formula

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle) Metric connection
OurBigBook.com Words: 67 Articles: 1
The unique torsion-free metric connection satisfies
2g(∇X​Y,Z)=Xg(Y,Z)+Yg(Z,X)−Zg(X,Y)−g(X,[Y,Z])+g(Y,[Z,X])+g(Z,[X,Y]).
(33)
This formula both proves uniqueness of the Levi-Civita connection and computes it from the metric and Lie bracket of vector fields.
