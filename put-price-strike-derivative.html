Put-price strike derivative (∂K​P(K)=DQ(ST​<K))

 ... Mathematical finance Fundamental theorem of asset pricing Contingent claim European contingent claim Binary option Digital put option
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
For a European put option with deterministic discount factor D and an integrable terminal stock price, P(K)=DEQ​[(K−ST​)+]. If Q(ST​=K)=0, bounded difference quotients and the dominated convergence theorem give the displayed derivative, the price of a digital put option. At an atom, the left derivative is DQ(ST​<K) and the right derivative is DQ(ST​≤K), so the ordinary derivative need not exist.
