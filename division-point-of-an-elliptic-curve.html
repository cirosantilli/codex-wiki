Division point of an elliptic curve ([N]Q=P)

 ... Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Isogeny of elliptic curves
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
A division point above P is a point Q over an algebraic closure satisfying [N]Q=P, for a specified nonzero integer N. Two such points differ by an N-torsion point of an elliptic curve. If the characteristic of a field does not divide N, there are N2 distinct division points; in other characteristics the scheme fibre still has length N2, but its geometric points can be fewer. The unramified division torsors at good primes control their coordinate fields over local fields.
