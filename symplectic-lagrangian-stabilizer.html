Symplectic Lagrangian stabilizer (Pm​=Symm​(q)⋊GLm​(q))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic group Symplectic parabolic subgroup
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
With a Lagrangian subspace first in a paired symplectic basis, its stabilizer consists of (A0​ASA−T​) with S symmetric. The additive symmetric-matrix unipotent radical has order qm(m+1)/2; the Levi subgroup is GLm​(q), acting by S↦ASAT. Its index in Sp2m​(q) is ∏i=1m​(qi+1), the number of Lagrangian subspaces.
