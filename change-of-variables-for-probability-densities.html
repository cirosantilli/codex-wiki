Change of variables for probability densities (fY​(y)=fX​(h−1(y))​dydh−1(y)​​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 1
For a random variable X with probability density function fX​ on an interval, let Y=h(X), where h is a diffeomorphism onto another interval. The change of variables formula gives
fY​(y)=fX​(h−1(y))​dydh−1(y)​​.
(54)
Indeed, integrating the right-hand side over an outcome interval gives the probability that X lies in its inverse image. The absolute derivative preserves positive probability when h reverses orientation. A gravity deflection of an atomic beam is a physical example: its speed-to-height transformation changes the probability density and its mode.
