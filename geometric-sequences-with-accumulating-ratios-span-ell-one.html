Geometric sequences with accumulating ratios span ell-one (span{(1,βk​,βk2​,…)}​=ℓ1)

OurBigBook.com Words: 83
Suppose the distinct complex numbers βk​ satisfy ∣βk​∣≤δ<1. The geometric sequences in the displayed formula belong to the absolutely summable sequence space and have dense linear span. Otherwise the Hahn-Banach theorem gives a nonzero annihilating functional, represented by a bounded sequence bj​. Its power series F(z)=∑j≥1​bj​zj−1 is holomorphic in the unit disk and vanishes at every βk​. Compactness supplies an accumulation point inside that disk. The identity theorem forces F=0 and hence all its coefficients zero, a contradiction. This is a useful form of density by annihilators that converts a Banach-space problem into uniqueness of an analytic function.
