Quaternion algebra ((a,b)k​=k⟨i,j⟩/(i2−a,j2−b,ij+ji))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Central simple algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 160 Articles: 1
For a field of characteristic other than two and nonzero a,b, the quaternion algebra has basis 1,i,j,ij with the displayed relations. It is a four-dimensional central simple algebra. After adjoining a square root of a, diagonalizing i and taking j=(01​b0​) identifies it with M2​. Quaternion conjugation fixes scalars and negates i,j,ij, giving norm x02​−ax12​−bx22​+abx32​. An element is invertible exactly when its norm is nonzero. Thus the quaternion algebra is either a division algebra or a split matrix algebra. The familiar quaternion algebra is (−1,−1)R​.
