Symmetric Hermite integral obstruction (k+∑j​bj​eβj​=0)

Suppose nonzero algebraic numbers βj​ form a Galois-stable set, with Galois-invariant integer weights bj​, and k=0 is an integer. Choose P∈Z[X] vanishing at all βj​ with P(0)=0, and a positive integer c making all cβj​ algebraic integers. For a large prime p, put D=(1+degP)p−1 and fp​(X)=cDXp−1P(X)p/(p−1)!, and let Fp​ be the sum of its derivatives. The weighted sum kFp​(0)+∑bj​Fp​(βj​) is an integer, nonzero modulo p when p∤kcP(0). If the displayed exponential expression vanished, integrating (e−zFp​(z))′=−e−zfp​(z) along the finitely many segments to βj​ would bound that nonzero integer by Cp/(p−1)!→0. This contradiction proves the obstruction and supplies direct proofs for e and π.
