Infinitely many reciprocal-logarithmic gaps between zeta zeros (γn+1​−γn​>c/logn infinitely often for 0<c<2π)

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Riemann zeta function Nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function Riemann–von Mangoldt formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
List positive ordinates of Nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function in nondecreasing order, including multiplicity. If all sufficiently late gaps were at most c/logn, summing would give γn​≤(c+o(1))n/logn, since ∑j≤n​1/logj∼n/logn with the sum starting at two. The Riemann–von Mangoldt formula would then give n≤N(γn​)≤(c/(2π)+o(1))n, a contradiction when c<2π. The same argument applies when only distinct ordinates are listed, because n≤N(γn​) still holds.
