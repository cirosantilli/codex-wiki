Zero-mode correction to the spectral zeta value (ζL​(0)=at0​−dimkerL)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Spectral geometry Spectral zeta function
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For a Laplace-type operator on a closed manifold, let at0​ be the constant coefficient of its full heat trace expansion. In the Mellin transform defining the spectral zeta function, the reduced trace has constant coefficient at0​−dimkerL. Its contribution is (at0​−dimkerL)/s, and 1/Γ(s)=s+O(s2) proves the identity. For the scalar positive Laplace-Beltrami operator on a closed surface, at0​=∫RdV/(24π) and dimkerL is the number of connected components. In particular, a connected sphere has value −2/3 and a connected flat torus has value −1.
