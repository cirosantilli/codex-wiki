Sylow counts in a faithful degree-seven action with S4 point stabilizers

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Sylow theorems
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
Suppose a group acts faithfully and transitively on seven points, every point stabilizer is S4​, and every two-point stabilizer is a Klein four-group. Then the group has order 168 and
n2​=21,n3​=28,n7​=8.
(195)
Count pairs consisting of a point and a Sylow subgroup fixing it for p=2,3; the two-point stabilizer ensures uniqueness. For p=7, a normal Sylow subgroup would force the group into its order-42 normalizer in S7​.
