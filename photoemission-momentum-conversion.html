Photoemission momentum conversion (k∥​=2me​Ekin​​sinθ/ℏ)

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Photoemission spectroscopy Angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
For emission angle θ measured from the surface normal, a free outgoing photoelectron has parallel wavevector k∥​=2me​Ekin​​sinθ/ℏ. Translation symmetry parallel to a clean periodic surface conserves this component modulo a surface reciprocal lattice vector, after accounting for photon momentum when needed. With initial binding energy EB​ below the Fermi level, its kinetic energy immediately outside the sample is Ekin​=hν−ϕs​−EB​, where ϕs​ is the sample work function. This sample-vacuum energy is distinct from the analyzer-referenced energy when contact potentials are present. Perpendicular crystal momentum generally needs a final-state model, whereas a strictly two-dimensional band has no perpendicular dispersion to reconstruct.
