Multiplicativity of the nonarchimedean polynomial norm (∥fg∥G​=∥f∥G​∥g∥G​)

For a field with a Non-Archimedean absolute value, define the coefficient norm ∥f∥G​=maxi​∣ai​∣ for f(T)=∑i​ai​Ti. Then ∥fg∥G​=∥f∥G​∥g∥G​. For nonzero polynomials, scale each by one of its coefficients of maximum absolute value; the resulting polynomials have integral coefficients and at least one unit coefficient. Their reductions in the residue field are nonzero, so their product is nonzero. The product therefore also has coefficient norm 1, proving the formula after reversing the scaling. This compares binary-polynomial coefficient heights with the heights of their roots.
