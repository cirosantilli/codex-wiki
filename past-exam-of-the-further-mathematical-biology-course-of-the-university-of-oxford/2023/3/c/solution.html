J0​=(−1η​0−μ​),η=(1+b02​)22λb02​​−μn∗​=(1+b02​)2λ(b02​−1)​.

Its eigenvalues are −1, −μ​, both negative, so the positive equilibrium is asymptotically stable against spatially uniform perturbations. This remains true at μ=1, where a repeated eigenvalue may produce a decaying te−t contribution. The printed stability claim must be understood in this spatially uniform sense: sufficiently strong chemotaxis can destabilize nonuniform perturbations, as the next answer shows.
