The separable-profile closure for an age-structured population has a qualification: arbitrary age-dependent hazard functions do not give this constant-coefficient ordinary differential equation for arbitrary initial conditions. Without fixed fi​, the effective exit rates are ∫Γi​ni​dτ/Ni​ and can vary with time. Moreover, an exact positive separation of variables with both profiles fixed forces N2​/N1​ to be constant through the incoming fluxes, hence selects a common exponential mode. The displayed ordinary differential equation is the necessary integrated system for such profiles; it is also exact for arbitrary populations when both hazard functions are age independent.
