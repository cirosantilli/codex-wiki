Punctured complex plane

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Punctured Riemann surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 37 Articles: 1
The punctured plane C∗ is a connected Riemann surface homeomorphic to a cylinder.
It is the complex plane with a point removed, a special punctured Riemann surface.
