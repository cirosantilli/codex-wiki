Jacobian evolution of a smooth flow (∂t​J[φt​]=(∇⋅F)(φt​)J[φt​])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Flow map
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
For a smooth flow map φt​ generated by a vector field F, the chain rule and det(I+tDF)=1+ttr(DF)+o(t) give ∂t​J[φt​](x)=(∇⋅F)(φt​(x))J[φt​](x). With initial value one, its solution is the exponential of the time integral of this divergence. Thus a divergence-free generator preserves Euclidean volume and orientation. This connects the Jacobian determinant with a volume-preserving vector field.
