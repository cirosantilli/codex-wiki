Constituent quark mass (mqconst​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Quark Quark mass
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
A constituent quark mass is an effective mass parameter in a low-energy constituent-quark model. It accounts approximately for strong-interaction dressing and some spin-independent bound-state dynamics; it is not identical to a renormalized quark mass in the Standard Model Lagrangian.
