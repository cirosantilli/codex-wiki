Uniform unloaded space-elevator cable (η′(η′+1)=2η3)

 Home Engineering Aerospace engineering Space elevator
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
A radial co-rotating cable of constant linear mass density λ extends from planetary radius R to b=η′R, with zero applied endpoint tension. If the geostationary orbit has radius ηR, integration of the effective force gives η′(η′+1)=2η3 for a nonzero cable. At position r its tension is λ[GM(1/R−1/r)−ω2(r2−R2)/2]. Differentiation shows the maximum at the geostationary orbit. An anchor load or counterweight alters the integrated condition.
