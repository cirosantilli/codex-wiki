The physical-frequency convention uses θ=kh and W(k)=hW(kh) on [−π/h,π/h]. This is distinct from the discrete Fourier transform of a finite vector. Translation-invariant finite difference operators become Fourier multipliers, making Fourier stability analysis possible.
