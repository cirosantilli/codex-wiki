Nonuniqueness of spectral-norm low-rank approximation (∥A−(Ar​+tu1​v1T​)∥2​=σr+1​(∣t∣≤σr+1​))

Even distinct singular values do not make a best low-rank approximation unique in the spectral norm. If A=∑j=1n​σj​uj​vjT​ has σ1​>⋯>σn​>0 and 1≤r<n, its truncated singular value decomposition Ar​ can be changed to Ar​+tu1​v1T​ for any ∣t∣≤σr+1​. Its matrix rank stays r since σ1​+t>0, while the spectral norm error is max(∣t∣,σr+1​)=σr+1​, the optimum from the Eckart-Young-Mirsky theorem.
