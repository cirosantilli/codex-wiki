Repeated game

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 454 Articles: 7
A repeated game plays a stage game over several rounds, allowing actions to depend on earlier observations. Its payoff convention must specify a finite horizon, a discount factor or a long-time average; these need not have identical invasion properties.
