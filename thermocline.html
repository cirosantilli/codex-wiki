Thermocline

 Home Physics Branches of physics Physical oceanography
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A water layer across which temperature changes rapidly with depth. In a reduced gravity description of the upper ocean, its displacement is represented by the interface between warm surface water and colder deep water. Deepening the eastern tropical Pacific thermocline makes upwelling less effective at cooling the surface during El Niño.
