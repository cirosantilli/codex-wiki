Harmonic mean (H=n/(∑i​1/xi​))

 ... Arithmetic Addition Summation Sum Weighted arithmetic mean Arithmetic mean
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
The harmonic mean of positive numbers x1​,…,xn​ is H=n/(∑i​1/xi​). The Jensen inequality for 1/x gives H≤x, with equality exactly when all entries coincide.
