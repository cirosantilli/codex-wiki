Nested matching criterion for a regular subgraph

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Degree (graph theory) Regular graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
Let p be prime and set s=2p−2. If a bipartite graph has edge-disjoint perfect matchings M0​,…,Mσ−1​ on nested nonempty vertex sets, and ∣V(Mi+s​)∣>s∣V(Mi​)∣/(s+1), then the union of s+1 consecutive matchings in a graph has maximum degree at most 2p−1 and more than (p−1)∣V(Mi​)∣ edges. The prime-regular subgraph from Boolean polynomial constraints gives a p-regular subgraph. Its perfect matching decomposition gives a k-regular subgraph for every k≤p. If none exists, every s steps shrink the vertex set by factor at most s/(s+1), bounding σ by a constant times logn.
