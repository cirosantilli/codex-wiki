Starting from 0<a1​≤b1​, replace the lower endpoint by the geometric mean and the upper endpoint by the arithmetic mean. The arithmetic-geometric mean inequality gives an​≤an+1​≤bn+1​≤bn​. The bounded monotone sequence theorem gives limits, and the arithmetic recurrence forces them to coincide. The limiting value is the arithmetic-geometric mean of the initial pair.
