Response variable (Y)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A response variable is the outcome whose variation is described by a statistical model or whose change is compared across treatments. Its unit, measurement procedure and observation time must be specified. An outcome consisting of a feature count is one response per sketch, not one independent experimental response per feature.
