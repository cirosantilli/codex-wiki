Molar mass (M, Mn​)

 Home Chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
Molar mass is mass per amount of substance, commonly expressed in gmol−1. For chain counts ni​ and chain molar masses Mi​, the number average is Mn​=∑i​ni​Mi​/∑i​ni​. Relating it to a degree of polymerization requires a defined unit-mass convention and end-group accounting.
