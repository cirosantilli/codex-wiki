Transmission conditions for a delta coefficient ([y]c​=0,[y′]c​+αy(c)=J)

 ... Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Linear ordinary differential equation Second-order linear differential equation Point interaction in a second-order differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For a point interaction in a second-order differential equation, the equation y′′+αy(c)δc​=f+Jδc​, with nonsingular continuous f, requires
[y]c​=0,[y′]c​+αy(c)=J.
(9)
The distributional second derivative across a jump explains both conditions: there is no derivative-of-delta source to balance a value jump, and the coefficients of the delta source must agree. In particular, without a point source, [y′]c​=−αy(c). With a unit point source at the same location, the condition is [y′]c​=1−αy(c); one must combine the two contributions rather than impose two incompatible jump conditions.
