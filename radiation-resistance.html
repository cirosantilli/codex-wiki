Radiation resistance (Prad​=Irms2​Rrad​)

 Home Electrical engineering Antenna (radio)
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 1
Radiation resistance represents emitted electromagnetic power as a real input resistance of an antenna. It does not describe conversion to heat inside the conductor. If peak current amplitude is used, Prad​=∣I0​∣2Rrad​/2. Real ohmic loss adds a separate resistance and lowers radiation efficiency. For an ideal short uniform-current element of length d, the Hertzian dipole gives Rrad​=80π2(d/λ)2 in ohms, using the usual vacuum impedance approximation 120πΩ.
