If “steady temperature in 2050” means preventing the next annual increase relative to the climate at the delayed start, the relevant forcing increment is
ΔF2050​=5.4ln(502.5/500)=0.0269327 Wm−2,m2050,increment​=1.966×103 tons of SO2​.​
(21)
Its smaller value follows from dF/dC=5.4/C: the same additional ppm gives less incremental forcing at a higher baseline.
That interpretation must be separated from the earlier objective of holding temperature at the original reference climate. To cancel the accumulated increase from 415 to 500 ppm requires
ΔFaccumulated​=5.4ln(500/415)=1.00618 Wm−2,m2050,original reference​=7.344×104 tons of SO2​.​
(22)
This annual versus accumulated radiative forcing distinction is essential: the first box offsets just the further year's growth; the second is the equivalent one-year aerosol inventory needed for the accumulated forcing. In subsequent years that inventory must be replaced as particles disappear and increased as carbon dioxide rises. A delayed start does not erase the earlier warming merely because the annual incremental requirement is smaller.
