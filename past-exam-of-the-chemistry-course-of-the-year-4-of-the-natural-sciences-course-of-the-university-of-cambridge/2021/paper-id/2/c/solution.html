A linear optical-depth approximation would give 5.38%. A realistic calculation integrates the wavelength-dependent absorption cross section, quantum yield and actinic flux; clouds and multiple scattering modify the simple transmitted-beam model.
Ozone change can also alter the radiative temperature structure, water vapor and circulation, thereby changing the water reaction channel and the supply of ozone/precursors to the troposphere. If the held-fixed tropospheric ozone assumption is relaxed, altered ozone itself changes the photochemical source of oxygen singlet D atom. These are additional feedbacks, distinct from the isolated UV-transmission calculation above.
