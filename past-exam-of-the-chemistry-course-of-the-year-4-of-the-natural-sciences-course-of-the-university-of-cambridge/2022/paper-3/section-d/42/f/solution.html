The fluorine sites form a simple cubic sublattice of spacing ℓ=a/2=3.10 A˚. Adopt uncorrelated nearest-neighbour hopping, identify the field-correlation time with the total jump waiting time, and use homonuclear dipolar relaxation: fD​=c=1 and x∗​=0.616. Then
ω0​=(2.518×108)(0.25)=6.295×107 s−1,τc∗​=ω0​0.616​=9.79×10−9 s,
(11)
D(T∗)=6τc∗​(3.10×10−10)2​≃1.64×10−12 m2s−1​,D0​=D(T∗)exp[(8.61733×10−5)(926)1.52​]≃3.1×10−4 m2s−1​.
(12)
If the simplified single-frequency condition ω0​τc​=1 is used instead, the corresponding estimates are 1.01×10−12 m2s−1 and 1.9×10−4 m2s−1. These are explicit model-dependent estimates, not a uniquely measured tracer diffusivity: a different correlation-time-to-jump-time conversion or defect correlation factor multiplies them by fD​c. The lattice constant supplies a jump distance, not proof that all possible neighbouring sites are vacant.
