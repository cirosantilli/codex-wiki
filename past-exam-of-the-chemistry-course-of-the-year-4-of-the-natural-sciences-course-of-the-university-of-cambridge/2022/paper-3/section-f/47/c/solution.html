For K mutually exclusive classes, the output layer should provide K nonnegative probabilities summing to one, with labels aligned consistently to output coordinates. A softmax activation on logits ak​ provides
pk​=∑ℓ=1K​eaℓ​eak​​​,∑k​pk​=1.
(35)
Subtracting maxℓ​aℓ​ from all logits before exponentiation preserves these probabilities and prevents numerical overflow. Categorical cross-entropy −∑k​yk​logpk​ is appropriate for one-hot or probabilistic class targets. Independent sigmoid outputs fit a different setting in which multiple labels can be present simultaneously and their probabilities need not sum to one.
