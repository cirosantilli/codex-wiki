Put the dividing surface at x‡<0. The immediate positive flux includes all positive momenta and gives Cfs​(0+)=1/(2πℏβ), an overestimate. Trajectories with p2/(2m)<V0​ hit the barrier, return across this surface, and cease contributing to the later product-side indicator. After their recrossing transient, only p>2mV0​​ survives:
Cfsplateau​=2πℏ1​∫2mV0​​∞​mp​e−βp2/(2m)dp=2πℏβe−βV0​​​.
(32)
The exact rate is again unchanged; the transmission correction to the reactant-side transition-state estimate is e−βV0​. The return time depends on surface placement and velocity, so the plateau means a timescale-separated limiting flux, not the claim that every trajectory has one identical finite recrossing time.
