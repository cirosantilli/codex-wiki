 ... Past exam of the chemistry course of the year 4 of the natural sciences course of the University of Cambridge 2022 Paper 3 Section E 45 b
OurBigBook.com Words: 124
Each Pd(dppp) unit retains its chelating phosphine and offers two adjacent pyridyl binding sites at an approximately right angle. Three such units bridge pairs of metalloligands, giving [Fe2​Pd3​L2​(dppp)3​]10+, a trigonal bipyramid with two Fe vertices and three equatorial Pd vertices. All six outward pyridines are used: 2×3=3×2. The Pd/Fe ratio is 3/2, matching the reagent dose, and the cation charge is 2(2)+3(2)=10 because the organic ligands are neutral.
The two donor sites left by dppp are cis, so each equatorial Pd joins one arm from each Fe cap. A mere mass-spectral maximum charge is not an independent structure determination, but its consistency with donor counting and binding geometry strongly selects this model.
