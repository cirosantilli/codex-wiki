Past exam of the chemistry course of the year 4 of the natural sciences course of the University of Cambridge / 2022 / Paper 3 / Section G / 49 / d / Solution

 ... Past exam of the chemistry course of the year 4 of the natural sciences course of the University of Cambridge 2022 Paper 3 Section G 49 d
OurBigBook.com Words: 209
The secondary hydroxylamine condenses with formaldehyde. nitrogen attacks the carbonyl carbon, giving a carbinolamine; proton transfer and loss of water generate the tethered nitrone R−N+(O−)=CH2​, where R is the entire bicyclic substituent attached to nitrogen in D. Molecular sieves remove water, favouring this condensation.
The nitrone undergoes an intramolecular 1,3-dipolar cycloaddition with the retained cyclohexene double bond. To define E precisely, label the former oxime carbon CA​, the adjacent upper ring junction CB​, the other junction CC​, and the alkene carbons CD​ and CE​ such that the chain from CA​ to the more remote alkene terminus is CA​−CB​−CH2​−CD​=CE​−CH2​−CC​. Let CF​ be the new formaldehyde-derived nitrone methylene. The cycloaddition forms CF​−CD​ and O–CE​, retains N–O and N–CA​, and changes N=CF​ and CD​=CE​ into single bonds. The new five-membered heterocycle is N–CF​–CD​–CE​–O. These connections, together with the original bicycle, specify the bridged isoxazolidine E.
The inwardly tethered cycloaddition is suprafacial at the alkene and preserves the existing carbon stereocentres; the C–C and C–O additions occur on the same accessible alkene face. The pre-existing N-substituent orientation restricts which fold can bring both reacting ends together without crossing the bridge. This is a genuine two-new-bond cycloaddition, not a Mannich reaction followed by unrelated oxygenation.
