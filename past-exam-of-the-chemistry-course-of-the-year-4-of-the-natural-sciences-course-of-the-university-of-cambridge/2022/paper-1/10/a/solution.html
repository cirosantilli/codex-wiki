Past exam of the chemistry course of the year 4 of the natural sciences course of the University of Cambridge / 2022 / Paper 1 / 10 / a / Solution

OurBigBook.com Words: 255
A coherent mechanism is an allene cyclization–norbornene insertion–cyclopropane closure cascade. Label the propargyl chain N−CH2​−C≡CH as D−E−F. Base-assisted propargyl-to-allenyl isomerization gives N−CH=C=CH2​. The hydrogen relocation is important: the terminal carbon must become a methylene before the final C–H substitution.
Reduction of the palladium precursor gives a phosphine-ligated Pd(0) catalyst. Oxidative addition into the aryl C–I bond supplies an aryl–Pd(II) intermediate. Intramolecular carbopalladation of the allene forms the aryl–E bond and the six-membered lactam ring, retaining D=E and placing Pd on the terminal carbon F. This allylic organopalladium connectivity may be represented by its interconverting sigma and pi forms.
Norbornene approaches from its less hindered exo face and inserts into the F–Pd bond. Carbon F bonds to one former norbornene alkene carbon, while Pd bonds to the adjacent one. The resulting intermediate is geometrically unable to undergo an ordinary favorable beta-hydride elimination: the relevant syn arrangement is blocked, or would produce a bridgehead alkene.
Base-assisted activation of an F–H bond gives a cyclopropane-forming dialkylpalladium connectivity, represented as a four-membered Pd–C–C–F ring. Reductive elimination closes the bond between F and the Pd-bearing norbornene carbon, releases Pd(0), and gives the cyclopropane. The lactam alkene D=E remains intact. Carbonate removes the acid equivalent generated in the overall arylation.
This mapping accounts for the six-membered heterocycle, the new three-membered ring and exo addition to norbornene. The catalyst and substrates are achiral, so the drawn relative stereochemistry represents a racemic product. This is a proposed organopalladium pathway; the question's product alone does not uniquely establish the lifetime or order of every palladium intermediate.
