Include an intercept w0​ and let z be the chosen numerical feature vector. Logistic regression uses
h(z;w)=σ(w0​+wTz),σ(u)=1+e−u1​.​
(60)
The logistic function maps every real score into (0,1), allowing a Bernoulli distribution model for the label. Equivalently, the log odds are linear in the features:
log1−hh​=w0​+wTz.
(61)
This gives a smooth differentiable probability model with a linear decision boundary at any fixed probability threshold. The sigmoid output is a fitted conditional probability, not a categorical prediction until a threshold is chosen.
