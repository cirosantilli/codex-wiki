 ... Past exam of the chemistry course of the year 4 of the natural sciences course of the University of Cambridge 2022 Paper 1 9 a ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 113
The nonnucleophilic magnesium amide metalates the triazole carbon between the two ring nitrogen atoms, conventionally C5 of 1-propyl-1,2,4-triazole. That proton is more readily removed because the resulting heteroaryl anion is stabilized by the neighboring nitrogen atoms; the reagent therefore acts as a base rather than adding a tetramethylpiperidinyl group.
The heteroaryl organomagnesium intermediate attacks the allylic bromide, forming a new C–C bond. The product is 5-(cyclohex-2-en-1-yl)-1-propyl-1,2,4-triazole. Ordinary allylic substitution and an SN​2′ description give the same unlabelled cyclohexenyl connectivity for this unsubstituted allylic ring, so the structural assignment does not distinguish those trajectories.
The molecular formula checks the assignment: N-propyltriazole is C5​H9​N3​; replacing a ring H by C6​H9​ gives C11​H17​N3​, as printed.
