The deviations from 2 indicate spin-orbit coupling and incomplete quenching of orbital angular momentum. The partly filled e′′ manifold in Fe and e′ manifold in Ni permit orbital degeneracy in the ideal geometry. Co's high-spin orbital occupation is more orbitally balanced, but mixing with excited ligand-field states still produces a substantial effective g shift. These fitted scalar values summarize a thermally averaged response; they do not determine the individual components of an anisotropic g tensor.
