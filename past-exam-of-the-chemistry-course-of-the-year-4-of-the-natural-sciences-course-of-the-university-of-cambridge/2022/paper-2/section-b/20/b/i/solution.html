For two independent identical ions at sufficiently high temperature, Curie's law gives χT=2(NA​μB2​/3kB​)g2S(S+1). In the cgs units used here, NA​μB2​/3kB​≃0.125 cm3Kmol−1.
Fe(II), Co(II) and Ni(II) have S=2, 3/2 and 1, respectively. The corresponding high-spin occupancies, ordered as (e′′,e′,a1′​), are
FeII:d6=(3,2,1),CoII:d7=(4,2,1),NiII:d8=(4,3,1).​
(7)
Hund filling puts one electron in each orbital before additional pairing. Spin-only dimer values at g=2 would be 6.0, 3.75 and 2.0; the observed larger values anticipate orbital contributions. The spin assignments require the chemically appropriate high-spin states, rather than rounding the measured χT directly to a spin-only integer.
