F2​ is an eight-membered ring with sequence Cu−N−Li−Cipso​−Cu−N−Li−Cipso​, closed back to the first Cu. Each N belongs to TMP; each ipso carbon belongs to a phenyl group. Each Cu is approximately linear between one TMP nitrogen and one phenyl ipso carbon. Each Li contacts a TMP nitrogen and the phenyl group bound to the other Cu, so the dimer is assembled head-to-tail rather than from two independent separated ion pairs.
G has the eight-membered sequence Cu1​−N1​−Li2​−N2​−Cu3​−N3​−Li4​−Cipso​, closed to Cu1​. The three N atoms belong to three TMP groups and the carbon belongs to the single phenyl group. Both copper sites remain approximately two-coordinate. The Li next to phenyl can also interact with its aromatic π surface; this secondary contact need not have the same hapticity as in F2​.
