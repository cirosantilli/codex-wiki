2,8,18,20,34,40,58,68,90,92,106,112,138.​

For Al4​Cp4∗​ the delocalized count is 4(3)−4=8, matching 1S21P6. The tetrahedral aluminium core is consequently consistent with the jellium model. Counting the same eight electrons as though the tetrahedron were an ordinary closo Wade cluster would fail: a four-vertex closo framework would require 2n+2=10 skeletal electrons. The competing models use different physical assumptions and should not be interchanged just because both involve a polyhedron.
