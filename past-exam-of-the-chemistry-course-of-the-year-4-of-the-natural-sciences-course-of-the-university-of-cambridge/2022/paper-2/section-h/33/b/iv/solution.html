The revised question page explicitly gives the cluster as {Ga22​[N(SiMe3​)2​]10​}2−. Its charge supplies two electrons, so
Ne​=22(3)−10+2=58,​
(49)
a jellium shell closure, through 1G. The reported structure has a Ga atom inside an eleven-atom Ga shell, with ten further ligand-bearing Ga units outside that core.[ref]
The canonical exam copy omits the 2− charge. Taken literally as a neutral formula it gives 56 electrons, which is not a listed shell closure; assigning 58 without identifying this difference would be unjustified. The revised public source resolves the discrepancy rather than requiring an invented charge.[ref]
