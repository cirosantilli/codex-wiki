OurBigBook.com Words: 101
The product is 1,3,4-Oxadiazole. Protonation or phosphorylation activates one formyl carbonyl of the diacylhydrazine. The oxygen of the other carbonyl attacks intramolecularly, closing a five-membered ring containing the atom sequence O–C–N–N–C. Proton transfers and loss of water produce the conjugated ring; deprotonation restores the neutral aromatic heterocycle.
The overall transformation is C2​H4​N2​O2​→C2​H2​N2​O+H2​O. Polyphosphoric acid provides carbonyl activation and an acidic medium. P4​O10​ acts as a strong dehydrating agent, consuming water and maintaining the condensation-driving environment. Neither nitrogen is expelled, and the oxygen retained in the heterocycle originates from a carbonyl rather than an inserted reagent oxygen.
