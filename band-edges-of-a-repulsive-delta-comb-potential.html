Band edges of a repulsive delta-comb potential

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bloch's theorem Particle in a one-dimensional lattice Kronig-Penney model
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
For V(x)=α∑j​δ(x−ja) with α>0, put P=mαa/ℏ2 and x=ka. The Floquet discriminant of the delta-comb Kronig-Penney model is f(x)=cosx+Psinx/x. Extended Bloch states require ∣f(x)∣≤1. Since f(0)=1+P>1, there is a positive lower band edge; a repulsive potential does not support negative-energy states in this energy convention.
The jth positive-energy band ends at x=jπ. Its lower edge lies in ((j−1)π,jπ) and solves f(x)=(−1)j−1. A finite periodic ring selects only the discrete allowed values of crystal momentum within these bands. Nonzero band gaps do not by themselves imply an insulator: the band filling must also place the Fermi energy in a gap.
