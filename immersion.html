Immersion

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 652 Articles: 13
An immersion is a smooth map whose differential is injective at every point. A parametrized surface in R3 is immersed when its two coordinate tangent vectors are linearly independent.
