Discrete isotropic total variation (∥Du∥2,1​)

 ... Analysis Inverse problem Regularization of an inverse problem Variational regularization Total variation seminorm on a domain Total variation denoising
OurBigBook.com Words: 163 Articles: 2
With zero forward differences at the grid boundary, discrete isotropic total variation is the sum of the Euclidean lengths of the two-component forward gradients. Its dual constraint is the product of unit Euclidean balls, and minimizing 21​∥g−D∗p∥22​ over that product yields u∗​=g−D∗p∗​. The exact difference adjoint operator is essential. A dual proximal gradient method projects p+τD(g−D∗p) pointwise onto unit balls; 0<τ≤1/8 is safe on an unscaled square grid.
