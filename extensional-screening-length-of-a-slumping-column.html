Extensional screening length of a slumping column (z=h03​/(2λa0​)​)

For a uniform initial column, annular return flow around a slumping column competes with axial extensional stress over z=h03​/(2λa0​)​. With G=Δρg/(3μ), choose velocity w=Gz2 and time t=z/w. Equivalently z=R/λα0​​, where ϕ=a2/R2 and α(ϕ)=2ϕ​/(1−ϕ​)3 in the leading thin-annulus model. This length describes the vertical range of the basal constraint: a much taller column has a localized basal boundary layer, while a much shorter column responds predominantly through extension.
