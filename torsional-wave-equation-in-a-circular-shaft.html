Torsional wave equation in a circular shaft (θtt​=(G/ρ)θxx​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Elastic wave Torsional waves in a solid circular rod
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
For a slender homogeneous circular shaft in small-amplitude linear elasticity, torque is GJp​θx​ and axial angular inertia per length is ρJp​. Their common geometric factor cancels in the angular equation of motion, leaving the displayed wave equation. Here Jp​=∫r2dA is the polar second moment of area, rather than the mass moment of inertia itself.
