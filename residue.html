Residue (Res)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 33 Articles: 1
The residue of a meromorphic function f at an isolated singularity a is the coefficient of (z−a)−1 in its Laurent series. At a simple pole,
Res(f,a)=limz→a​(z−a)f(z).
