Dyadic occupancy approximation of a simple point measure (Mn​(J)=∑D∈Dn​, D⊂J​1{M(D)>0}​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Point process Simple locally finite point measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For a fixed bounded interval with no atoms at its endpoints, the occupation count of fully contained half-open dyadic intervals increases to the true count of a simple locally finite point measure. Refinement cannot reduce occupancy. Finitely many distinct atoms in a bounding interval have positive minimum separation; for fine enough cells each atom lies alone, and every interior atom's cell is contained in the target interval. Thus the counts eventually agree, path by path.
