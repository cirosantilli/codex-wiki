For 0≤r<1, this Poisson kernel for the unit disk has the uniformly convergent Fourier series
Pr​(θ)=1+2∑n=1∞​rncos(nθ).
(67)
The identity follows by taking the real part of the geometric series 1+2∑n≥1​(reiθ)n. For integrable 2π-periodic boundary data f, its Poisson extension is
u(r,θ)=2π1​∫−ππ​Pr​(θ−t)f(t)dt=2a0​​+∑n=1∞​rn[an​cos(nθ)+bn​sin(nθ)],
(68)
where an​,bn​ are the usual Fourier coefficients. Each term is a harmonic function in the open disk. For continuous boundary data, the extension solves the Dirichlet problem with those boundary values.
