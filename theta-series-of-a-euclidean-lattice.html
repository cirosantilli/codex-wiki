Theta series of a Euclidean lattice (ΘΛ​(t)=∑v∈Λ​e−πt∣v∣2)

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Theta function Gaussian theta sum
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The lattice theta series of a full-rank Euclidean lattice records the multiset of squared lengths of lattice vectors. The Gaussian form shown here converges for t>0. For a flat torus Rd/Λ, its heat trace is ΘΛ∗​(4πt), by the spectrum of a flat torus. Equal theta series do not in general imply orthogonal equivalence of lattices.
