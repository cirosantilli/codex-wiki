For a homologous black hole-dominated cluster, write E=−bGMH​Nm/(2R), and let a captured star's specific orbital energy be ϵcap​=ξE/(Nm). Removing negative orbital energy raises the remaining cluster energy, while growth M˙H​=mΓ deepens its potential. The energy budget gives the displayed radius response. Preferentially removing strongly bound stars can drive expansion; representative-energy removal instead gives the adiabatic contraction due to black-hole growth. The sign is not fixed by the loss cone angle alone.
