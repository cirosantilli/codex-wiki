Asymptotic-pair obstruction to an invariant metric

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Topological dynamics Proximality
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
If distinct points x,y of a dynamical system on a compact metric space satisfy d(Tnx,Tny)→0 in one compatible metric, no compatible metric can make T an isometry. On a compact space, all compatible metrics give the same asymptotic-pair property by uniform continuity, whereas an isometry preserves the strictly positive distance between distinct points. In a full shift, a constant sequence and a sequence differing at just one coordinate converge to each other under forward shifts, proving this obstruction directly in the product topology.
