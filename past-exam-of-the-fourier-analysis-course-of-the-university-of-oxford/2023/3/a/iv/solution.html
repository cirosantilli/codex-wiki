These are the smooth-window Fourier coefficients of a periodic distribution for period 2π. ⟨u,ϕ⟩​=⟨u,ϕ​⟩=⟨u,ΨPϕ​⟩=n∑​ϕ(−n)⟨u,Ψeinx⟩=2πn∑​cn​ϕ(n).​

The polynomial bound on cn​ and rapid decay of ϕ(n) make every displayed pairing absolutely convergent. In particular there are no derivatives of Dirac delta distributions in this formula.
The inverse Fourier transform of δn​ is (2π)−1einx. Applying continuous Fourier inversion on tempered distributions to the symmetric partial sums gives

The Fourier transform formula also proves that cn​ is independent of the chosen smooth periodic partition of unity.
