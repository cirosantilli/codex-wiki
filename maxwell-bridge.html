Maxwell bridge

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A null electrical circuit for measuring a lossy inductor against a standard capacitor. If opposite arms are Rx​+iωLx​ and (1/R1​+iωC)−1, and the remaining arms are R2​,R3​, balance requires their products to agree. Consequently Rx​=R2​R3​/R1​ and Lx​=CR2​R3​. Two independent real adjustments are necessary; changing the source frequency does not change the ideal balance conditions.
