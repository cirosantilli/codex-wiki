Spectrum of a commutative ring (SpecA)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 982 Articles: 20
The spectrum SpecA of a commutative ring A is the set of its prime ideals, with closed sets V(I)={p:I⊆p} and a structure sheaf whose sections locally look like fractions.
