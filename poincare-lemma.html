Poincaré lemma (dα=0⟹α=dβ locally,p>0)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form Exterior derivative Closed and exact differential forms Closed differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 443 Articles: 6
Every smooth closed differential form of positive degree is locally an exact differential form. On a coordinate ball centered at zero, put Ft​(x)=tx and let R=xi∂i​ be the radial vector field. Define the degree-lowering operator
Kα=∫01​t−1Ft∗​(ιR​α)dt.
(4)
For a p-form with p>0, the integrand has a factor tp−1 in its coordinate coefficients and is smooth at zero. Cartan's magic formula and differentiation of the pullback of a differential form give ∂t​(Ft∗​α)=t−1Ft∗​(dιR​α+ιR​dα). Integrating from zero to one gives dKα+Kdα=α, since F0∗​α=0 in positive degree. If dα=0, take β=Kα. The local potential of a closed differential one-form gives the explicit degree-one construction. Global exactness can fail through nonzero periods, measured by de Rham cohomology.
