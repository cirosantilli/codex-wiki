Young branching graph

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young's lattice
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
The Young branching graph is the graded cover graph of the Young lattice. Its level n consists of partitions of n, and its edges add one box. It is also the branching graph for complex Specht modules under the restriction branching rule for a symmetric group.
