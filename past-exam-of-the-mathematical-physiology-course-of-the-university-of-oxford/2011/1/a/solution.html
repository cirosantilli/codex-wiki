In the Hodgkin-Huxley model, V is the membrane potential, the voltage inside the axon relative to outside. The space clamp assumption makes it spatially uniform. The gating variables lie between zero and one: m describes sodium activation, h describes availability against sodium inactivation, and n describes potassium activation. Thus large h means channels remain available, not that inactivation is strong.
The left side Cm​V˙ is capacitive electric current per unit membrane area. The imposed I is inward-positive applied electric current. The other terms are outward-positive ionic electric currents: sodium with electrical conductance gNa​m3h, potassium with conductance gK​n4, and a passive leak with conductance gL​. Each current is its conductance times the difference between V and the corresponding reversal potential VNa​, VK​ or VL​. The coefficients gNa​,gK​ are maximal conductances; the gate factors represent independent permissive gates in an ion channel.
