Coronagraph throughput (T(θ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Coronagraph
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
For a specified photodetector measurement, coronagraph throughput T(θ) is the fraction of a source's light transmitted into that measurement at angular separation θ from the blocked source, relative to a stated reference configuration. Total transmitted power and power inside a small image opening can give different fractions; one must specify which is used. Throughput usually depends on wavelength and separation. A lower stellar residual is useful only in relation to the retained companion signal and noise. Guyon's worked designs plot transmitted off-axis light against separation to expose that tradeoff.
