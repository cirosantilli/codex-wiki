Constant polynomial (f(X)=c)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
A polynomial consisting only of a constant term. Over an integral domain, a nonzero constant polynomial has polynomial degree zero; it is a unit of the polynomial ring exactly when its value is a unit of the coefficient ring.
