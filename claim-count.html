Claim count (N∈{0,1,2,…})

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Aggregate claims model
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The number of insurance claims in a specified exposure period. Its probability generating function combines with the moment-generating function of an independent claim size through the random-sum transform identity. A Poisson distribution, geometric distribution, or negative binomial distribution provides a possible count model, with different dispersion and zero-claim probabilities.
