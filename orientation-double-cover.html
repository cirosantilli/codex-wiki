Orientation double cover (M={(p,o):o orients Tp​M})

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Covering space
OurBigBook.com Words: 147 Articles: 2
An oriented coordinate neighborhood of a smooth manifold gives two sheets distinguished by the sign of its orientation. On an overlap, the sheets agree where the two local orientation forms differ by a positive function. These open agreement sets define a two-sheeted smooth covering space, whose projection is locally a diffeomorphism.
