For a spherically symmetric speed with strictly increasing η(r)=r/v(r), a turning ray of parameter p has Δ(p)=2p∫pηa​​(dlogr/dη)(η2−p2)−1/2dη. Integrate this against (p2−μ2)−1/2 and interchange the triangular integration region. The inner kernel integral is π/2, giving the displayed radius and then v(r(μ))=r(μ)/μ. Complete turning-ray branch data are required.
