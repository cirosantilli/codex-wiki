Stationarity of ordinals of prescribed cofinality (Sθκ​={δ<κ:cf(δ)=θ})

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Ordinal Club set Stationary set
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For a regular infinite θ<κ, with κ a regular uncountable cardinal number, Sθκ​={δ<κ:cf(δ)=θ} is stationary. Build a strictly increasing continuous θ-sequence in any club set, and take its supremum. Closure places it in that club and the cofinality of an increasing ordinal supremum gives cofinality θ. In particular the two disjoint sets Sωω2​​ and Sω1​ω2​​ show that the club filter on ω2​ is not an ultrafilter.
