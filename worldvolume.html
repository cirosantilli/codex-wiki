Worldvolume (Σp+1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Brane
OurBigBook.com Words: 207 Articles: 4
The worldvolume is the history of a brane. An embedding X:Σ→M maps its local coordinates into target spacetime. A string worldvolume is a worldsheet.
