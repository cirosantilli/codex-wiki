Prefix-suffix sequence recognizer

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Formal language theory Finite-state machine Moore machine
OurBigBook.com Words: 134
For a fixed word w, store the length of the longest suffix of the received stream that is also a prefix of w. On a new input symbol, append it to the represented prefix and select the longest resulting suffix that is a prefix of w. The complete-word state signals recognition. Its outgoing transitions retain proper suffix matches, allowing overlapping occurrences rather than automatically restarting from the empty prefix. This is the finite-state form of the prefix information used by the Knuth-Morris-Pratt algorithm.
For example, recognizing 1101 needs suffix-prefix states of lengths zero through four. After a complete match followed by one, the last two symbols are 11, so the next state retains length two. This permits the second match in 1101101 without clearing the useful suffix.
