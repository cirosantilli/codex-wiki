Davis-Kahan curvature lemma (2γ​∥P−Q∥F2​≤tr(H(P−Q)))

If a symmetric matrix H has largest eigenvalue λ1​ separated from λ2​ by γ>0, and P=vv⊤ is its leading rank-one orthogonal projection matrix, then every unit vector u, with Q=uu⊤, satisfies the displayed inequality. Expanding u in an orthonormal eigenbasis gives λ1​−u⊤Hu≥γ(1−∣u⊤v∣2), while ∥P−Q∥F2​=2(1−∣u⊤v∣2). The analogous statement for a smallest eigenvalue follows by replacing H by −H.
