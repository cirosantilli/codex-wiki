Anyon vertex gauge transformation (vcab​↦ucab​vcab​)

 ... Quantum theory Topological quantum matter Anyon Anyon fusion rule Anyon fusion space Anyon fusion tree
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
A change of the chosen orthonormal basis of every anyon fusion space. In a multiplicity-free theory each basis change is an independent wave phase ucab​. Splitting vertices acquire ucab​ and adjoint fusion vertices acquire its inverse. A scalar diagram is invariant under these choices when its splitting and fusion vertex factors cancel. A closed diagram with independently specified unmatched vertex tensors need not be invariant.
