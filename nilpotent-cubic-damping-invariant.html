Nilpotent cubic damping invariant (b=g21​+3f30​)

 ... Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory Codimension-two bifurcation Bogdanov–Takens bifurcation Reflection-symmetric cubic double-zero normal form
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 1
For a reflection-symmetric vector field u˙=v+f30​u3+f21​u2v+⋯, v˙=g30​u3+g21​u2v+⋯, introduce x=u, y=u˙. Differentiating gives x˙=y and a cubic acceleration with x2y coefficient b=g21​+3f30​. The contribution 3f30​ comes from differentiating the first equation's cubic term. Further cubic near-identity transformations can remove nonresonant terms but cannot turn b=0 into a nonzero coefficient by a nonsingular scaling. This is a useful check before assigning a generic reflection-symmetric Bogdanov–Takens bifurcation to a calculated Taylor expansion.
