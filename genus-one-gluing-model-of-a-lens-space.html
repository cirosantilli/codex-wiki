Genus-one gluing model of a lens space (μ2​↦−qμ1​+pλ1​)

 ... Geometry and topology Knot theory Link Framed link Dehn surgery on a framed link Lens space
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
The quotient model of a lens space splits into two solid tori. In the boundary angle lattice Z2+Z(1/p,q/p), choose μ1​=(0,1) and λ1​=(1/p,q/p). The other meridian is (1,0)=−qμ1​+pλ1​. A second column completing this primitive slope to a determinant-one matrix specifies a gluing map after reversing one boundary-basis convention. Different completions differ by a meridional Dehn twist extending across the second solid torus, so they give the same three-manifold.
