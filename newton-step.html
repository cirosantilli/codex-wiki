Newton step (H(p)s=−∇f(p))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Newton's method in optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 1
A Newton step for Newton's method in optimization is the displacement s solving the displayed linear system, where H is the Hessian matrix and ∇f the gradient. The next iterate is p+s. This makes the first order Taylor expansion of the gradient at the new point vanish. An invertible Hessian matrix determines a unique step; it need not give a descent direction when the Hessian matrix is indefinite.
