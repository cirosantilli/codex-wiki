Inverse-square potential (V(r)=−k/r)

OurBigBook.com Words: 722 Articles: 4
Here inverse-square potential means the potential energy associated with an attractive inverse-square law for a central force: the potential energy itself varies as inverse distance. For k>0 and position r with r=∣r∣>0,
V(r)=−rk​,V(∞)=0,F=−∇V=−r2k​r=−r3kr​.
(117)
The gradient makes the distinction explicit: doubling r halves the magnitude of potential energy but quarters the magnitude of force. The neighboring repulsive inverse-square potential instead has potential energy proportional to r−2 and an inverse-cube force; the two naming conventions must be distinguished.
For Newton's law of universal gravitation, k=GMm, where G is the gravitational constant. Treat the attracting mass M as an isolated fixed point mass, or as a body with spherical symmetry and exterior test particle of constant mass m≪M. The spherical shell theorem gives the same exterior Newtonian gravitational potential energy U=−GMm/r without requiring uniform mass density. Here r is measured from the centre, and the formula applies outside the body; it does not describe its interior. The Newtonian gravitational potential per unit mass is Φ=U/m=−GM/r.[ref]
The negative sign records binding relative to infinite separation, rather than a negative kinetic energy. Moving from r1​ to r2​>r1​ increases potential energy by
ΔV=k(r1​1​−r2​1​)>0,Wgravity​=−ΔV.
(118)
Because the force is a conservative force, this work depends on the endpoints, including for a curved path. Integrating dV/dr=k/r2 with zero at infinity gives V=−k/r; adding a constant leaves the force unchanged but shifts the numerical escape energy threshold by that same constant. This is why the reference at infinity must accompany an escape velocity calculation.
For an exact synthetic comparison, stipulate the standard gravitational parameter μ=GM=8m3/s2 and a 1kg test particle. At r=2m, U=−4J and the inward force has magnitude 2N; at r=4m, U=−2J and the inward force has magnitude 0.5N. The increase is 2J of potential energy. These stipulated parameters are an arithmetic example, not observations of a particular body. The escape-energy balance diagram uses the same parameters.
