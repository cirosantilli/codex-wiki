Brightness temperature (Tb​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Exoplanet emission spectrum
OurBigBook.com Words: 80 Articles: 1
The brightness temperature at a frequency or wavelength is the temperature for which a blackbody has the measured specific intensity there: Iν​=Bν​(Tb​). It need not equal a physical temperature shared by the entire emitting object.
