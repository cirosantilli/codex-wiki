Test particle

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Particle dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A test particle is an idealized body whose own fields and effect on its environment are negligible. Its motion probes an externally prescribed field. A gravitational test particle follows a timelike geodesic when nongravitational forces are absent; appreciable self-gravity requires a more complete treatment.
