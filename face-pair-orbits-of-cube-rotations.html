Face-pair orbits of cube rotations (∣O∣=6,6,24)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group action Symmetry group of a cube Rotational symmetry group of a cube
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
The rotational symmetry group of a cube has three group orbits on ordered pairs of faces: equal faces, opposite faces, and adjacent faces. Their sizes are respectively 6, 6 and 24. Fixing one face leaves its cyclic group of four quarter-turns, which acts transitively on the four adjacent faces; this proves transitivity in the last orbit rather than merely counting its points.
