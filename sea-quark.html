Sea quark

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Quark
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A quark or antiquark in the nonvalence component of a hadron's parton distribution functions. Such pairs do not change the net valence flavor numbers. Approximately equal light-flavor sea distributions can dominate small-x deep inelastic scattering, but exact equality is not required by their definition.
