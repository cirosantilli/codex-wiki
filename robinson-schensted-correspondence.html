Robinson–Schensted correspondence (π⟷(P,Q))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young tableau
OurBigBook.com Words: 360 Articles: 4
Insert the successive letters of a permutation by row insertion to obtain an insertion tableau P, recording the insertion times in the new boxes of Q. This is a bijection between permutations and pairs of standard Young tableaux of a common shape. Reverse insertion removes the box marked by the latest recording time. The first row of P has length equal to the longest increasing subsequence.
