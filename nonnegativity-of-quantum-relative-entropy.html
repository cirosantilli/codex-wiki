Nonnegativity of quantum relative entropy (D(ρ∥σ)≥0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Von Neumann entropy Quantum relative entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
For density operators, Klein's inequality gives D(ρ∥σ)≥0, with equality exactly when ρ=σ. If the support of a positive operator ρ is not contained in that of σ, the quantum relative entropy is +∞.
