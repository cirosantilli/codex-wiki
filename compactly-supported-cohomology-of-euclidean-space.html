For a nonnegative integer d and an abelian group A,
Hcq​(Rd;A)≅{A,0,​q=d,q=d.​
(10)
The one-point compactification is the sphere Sd, and it is locally contractible at the added point. Apply the compact-support comparison with a one-point compactification and the reduced cohomology of a sphere. In dimension zero the compactification is S0, giving the same formula.
