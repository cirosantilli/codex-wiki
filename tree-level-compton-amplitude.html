Tree-level Compton amplitude

For a charged Dirac particle, incoming momenta p,k and outgoing momenta p′,k′ obey p+k=p′+k′. The two QED diagrams have internal fermion momenta p+k and p−k′, giving s and u channels. The amplitude is proportional to uˉ(p′)[ϵ′∗R(p+k)ϵ+ϵR(p−k′)ϵ′∗]u(p), where R(q)=(q+m)/(q2−m2+i0). It is related to particle-antiparticle two-photon annihilation by crossing symmetry, with appropriate changes to external wavefunctions and conjugations.
