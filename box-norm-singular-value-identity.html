Box norm singular-value identity (∥H∥□4​=∑j​σj4​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Box norm
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
For a real-valued function H on a nonempty finite Cartesian product, let TH​v(x)=Ey​H(x,y)v(y) with uniform inner products. The box norm obeys ∥H∥□4​=Tr((TH​TH∗​)2)=∑j​σj4​, where σj​ are its singular values. For a biregular graph this separates the constant component γ4 from the other fourth powers.
