For one transverse coordinate, the parabolic wave equation 2ikEx​+Ezz​=0 has the Fresnel propagator kernel
(Ux​f)(z)=2πixk​​∫R​eik(z−z′)2/(2x)f(z′)dz′,x>0.
(28)
The square root uses 1/i​=e−iπ/4. With f​(ν)=(2π)−1∫f(z)e−iνzdz, its Fourier transform multiplier is e−iν2x/(2k). This distinguishes the one-transverse-coordinate prefactor from the two-coordinate kernel. The operator is unitary on L2(R), although its spatial integral is interpreted as an oscillatory integral.
