Majorana-Weyl spinor (Γ∗​ψ=±ψ,ψc=ψ)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Weyl spinor Majorana spinor
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A Majorana-Weyl spinor satisfies both a Majorana spinor reality condition and a Weyl spinor chirality condition when dimension and signature permit them. In ten-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime it has sixteen real components off shell and eight physical massless spin-one-half polarizations.
