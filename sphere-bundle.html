Sphere bundle (S(E))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 666 Articles: 12
After choosing a fiber metric on a rank-d vector bundle E→B, its sphere bundle consists of the unit vectors and has fiber Sd−1. Different choices of metric give isomorphic sphere bundles.
