Amalgamated free product

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fundamental group Seifert-van Kampen theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 476 Articles: 9
Given homomorphisms iA​:C→A and iB​:C→B, the amalgamated free product A∗C​B comes with homomorphisms from A and B that agree on C. It is universal with this property: every compatible pair of homomorphisms A→H and B→H factors uniquely through A∗C​B.
