Gauge field (Aμ​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field
OurBigBook.com Words: 9k Articles: 200
A gauge field is a connection associated with a local symmetry in a gauge theory. Its gauge potential defines the internal-symmetry contribution to a covariant derivative. For an Abelian gauge field, the gauge field strength is Fμν​=∂μ​Aν​−∂ν​Aμ​.
