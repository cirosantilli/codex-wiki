Squared Sobolev gradient counterexample (w∈H1 ⟹ w∇w∈L2(n≥2))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space Sobolev algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 119
In dimensions at least two a function in the Sobolev space H1 can have finite norm while ∫w2∣∇w∣2=∞. Localize the following radial examples with a smooth cutoff function in an interior ball. For n≥3, set w(r)=r−α, α=(n−2)/3. Its gradient energy has radial exponent n−3−2α>−1, but its weighted gradient energy has exponent n−3−4α<−1. For n=2, take w(r)=(log(1/r))1/3 near zero. Substituting s=log(1/r) makes the two gradient integrals proportional to ∫∞s−4/3ds and ∫∞s−2/3ds, respectively. The first converges and the second diverges. In dimension one the Sobolev embedding theorem gives H1⊂L∞, preventing this failure. These examples show why a nonnegative coefficient w2 need not even define a bounded gradient form when only w∈H1 is assumed.
