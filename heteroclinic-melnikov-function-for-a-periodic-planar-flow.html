For q˙​=u0​(q)+ϵu1​(q,t) with planar Hamiltonian flow u0​=(−∂y​ψ0​,∂x​ψ0​), let q0​ be a heteroclinic orbit between two hyperbolic fixed points. Its first-order displacement satisfies q˙​1​=Du0​q1​+u1​. The covector w=∇ψ0​(q0​) satisfies w˙=−(Du0​)Tw, obtained by differentiating ∇ψ0​⋅u0​=0. Thus (w⋅q1​)′=w⋅u1​. Integrating the stable correction from the future and the unstable correction from the past gives the displayed function. At a regular section the first-order normal separation is ϵM/∣∇ψ0​∣. A simple zero yields a transverse intersection of the perturbed manifolds, by the implicit function theorem.
