Network simplex algorithm

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Minimum-cost flow problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 93 Articles: 1
The network simplex algorithm specializes the simplex algorithm to network flows. A basis is represented by a spanning tree with nonbasic flows at their bounds; potentials give reduced costs, and entering arcs produce cycle pivots. Degeneracy in linear programming is particularly common in assignment instances.
