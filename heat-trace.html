Heat trace (ZM​(t)=Tr(e−tΔM​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Spectral geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 1
For the positive Laplace-Beltrami operator on a closed compact Riemannian manifold, the heat trace is ZM​(t)=∑j​e−tλj​=∫M​KM​(t,x,x)dV(x). It records every eigenvalue with multiplicity. In a finite free isometric quotient it equals ∣U∣−1∑u∈U​∫N​KN​(t,x,ux)dV(x). The orbital integrals form a class function of the ambient finite isometry group, so Gassmann equivalence gives equal quotient heat traces and hence Sunada theorem.
