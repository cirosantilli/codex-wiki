Minimum degree algorithm

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Numerical linear algebra Gaussian elimination Sparse Gaussian elimination
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
At each step choose a remaining vertex of minimum degree in the current elimination graph. This heuristic reduces the size of the immediate Schur complement update and often reduces fill-in, but does not guarantee a globally optimal ordering. Its degrees must reflect earlier fill, rather than just the original graph.
