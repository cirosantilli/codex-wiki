Moment-generating function of a chi-squared distribution (Mχn2​​(t)=(1−2t)−n/2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Chi-squared distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A sum of the squares of n independent random variables with the standard normal distribution has the chi-squared distribution with n degrees of freedom. A scaled Gaussian integral and factorization by independence give its moment-generating function (1−2t)−n/2 for t<1/2. For positive n, this expectation diverges when t≥1/2.
