Clinical recovery

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Clinical trial Clinical outcome
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
Meeting an explicitly specified recovery criterion in a clinical context. The criterion can concern symptoms, diagnosis or functioning; published recovery percentages cannot be interpreted without knowing that definition, timing and denominator.
