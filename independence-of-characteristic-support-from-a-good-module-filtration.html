Independence of characteristic support from a good module filtration (AnngrF​M​=AnngrG​M​)

 ... Algebra over a field Associative algebra Gelfand–Kirillov dimension Gelfand–Kirillov dimension of a module Bernstein growth dimension of a Weyl algebra module Characteristic variety of a Weyl algebra module
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
Fix the Bernstein filtration on the Weyl algebra. Two good filtrations of a module are bounded shifts of one another: Fi​M⊆Gi+c​M and conversely. If an operator of degree d has symbol annihilating grF​M, its rth power lowers the expected filtration degree by r. For r>2c, comparison of filtrations forces the same power of its symbol to annihilate grG​M. Reversing the roles gives equality of the radical annihilator ideals and hence equality of support of a module. The algebra filtration is held fixed; changing between order and Bernstein filtrations need not preserve the same variety.
