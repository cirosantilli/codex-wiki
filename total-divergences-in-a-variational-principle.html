Total divergences in a variational principle

 ... Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus Divergence Total divergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
Adding dF(q,t)/dt to a mechanical Lagrangian changes its action by F(qf​,tf​)−F(qi​,ti​). Fixed endpoint variations therefore leave its bulk Euler-Lagrange equations unchanged. For fields, adding ∂μ​Kμ changes the action by a boundary flux. The same conclusion holds for compactly supported variations or appropriate fixed boundary data. If K depends on field derivatives, those boundary data must also control their variations. Such terms can still change canonical momenta, boundary observables, and representatives of Noether currents; equivalence of bulk equations does not mean that every boundary-value problem is identical.
