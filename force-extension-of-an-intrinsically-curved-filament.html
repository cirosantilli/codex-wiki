For a planar elastic filament under positive constant extension force F, the quadratic force-controlled energy is
H=21​∫[B(ζ′′−κ0​)2+F(ζ′)2]dx.
(20)
The higher-order Euler-Lagrange equation is Bζ′′′′−Fζ′′=Bκ0′′​. On a periodic interval or in a bulk Fourier transform treatment, every nonzero mode obeys the displayed transfer relation. The length deficit is 21​∫(ζ′)2dx, so its ensemble value is determined by the power spectrum of the quenched intrinsic curvature. Boundary conditions must be specified for a finite filament.
