Sign homomorphism (sgn)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Symmetric group Parity of a permutation
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The sign homomorphism sends an even permutation to 1 and an odd permutation to −1; its kernel is the alternating group.
A symmetric group is the group of all bijections from a set to itself, with function composition as its operation.
