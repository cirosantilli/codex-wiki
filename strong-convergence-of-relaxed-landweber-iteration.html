Strong convergence of relaxed Landweber iteration (xn​→x†)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Normal equation for a linear inverse problem Landweber iteration
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For nonzero compact operator A, step 0<τ<2/∥A∥2, and data admitting a minimum-norm least-squares solution x†, zero-initialized Landweber iteration satisfies
∥xn​−x†∥2=∑i​∣1−τσi2​∣2nσi2​∣⟨y,ui​⟩∣2​⟶0.
(32)
The Picard criterion makes the majorant summable, so the dominated convergence theorem proves this limit. No mesh-independent or singular-value-independent strict contraction factor is required. At τ=2/∥A∥2, the top singular mode has multiplier −1 and may not converge. Without solvable projected data, the iterates need not have a limit in the solution Hilbert space, even when their residuals approach the least-squares infimum.
