Andersonian faulting regime

 ... Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth Mantle convection Plate tectonics Geological fault
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 1
A local principal stress model associated with Ernest Masson Anderson in which one principal stress is vertical, as suggested by negligible shear stress on a flat free surface. With compression positive and σ1​≥σ2​≥σ3​, vertical σ1​ gives normal faulting, vertical σ2​ gives strike-slip faulting, and vertical σ3​ gives reverse faulting. The other two principal stresses are horizontal. Topography, mechanical layering and local fault geometry can violate the idealization.
