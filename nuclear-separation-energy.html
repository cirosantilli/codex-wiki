Nuclear separation energy

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Atomic nucleus Nuclear shell model
OurBigBook.com Words: 260 Articles: 5
The energy needed to remove specified nucleons from a bound atomic nucleus in its ground state. It compares neighboring nuclear masses, and so can reveal a nuclear shell closure more clearly than a smoothly varying total nuclear binding energy.
