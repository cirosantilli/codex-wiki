In two dimensions, the integer defects qx​ on dual faces are vortices of winding 2πqx​. Here G is the inverse positive lattice Laplacian with its boundary or zero-mode convention specified. At separations large compared with the lattice spacing, G(r)=−(2π)−1log(r/a)+constant, yielding the pair action −2πβ∑i<j​qi​qj​log(rij​/a) and core self-energies. Like charges repel and opposite charges attract. With periodic boundaries the total charge is zero and the inverse is taken on the mean-zero subspace; harmonic winding sectors require their own factors.
