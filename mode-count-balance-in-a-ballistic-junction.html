Mode-count balance in a ballistic junction

OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Let Tij​ count transmitted modes from injector j to collector i, excluding reflection Rii​. Scattering conservation gives ∑i=j​Tij​=Nj​−Rjj​; unitarity also gives the corresponding row balance. The conductance matrix has zero row sum, making equal lead potentials carry no current, and zero column sum, conserving total current.
