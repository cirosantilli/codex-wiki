Dyadic transport of material line elements (Dt​A=LA+ALT)

 ... Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity Conformation tensor Objective time derivative Upper-convected derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
For a material line tangent ℓ, Dt​ℓ=Lℓ with velocity gradient Lij​=∂j​ui​. The product rule gives Dt​(ℓℓT)=LℓℓT+ℓℓTLT. Consequently the upper-convected derivative vanishes for any transported sum of these outer products. Under the derivative-index-first convention G=LT the same formula is Dt​A=GTA+AG. The distinction prevents transposition errors in constitutive equations.
