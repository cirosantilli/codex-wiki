Electro-optic Kerr effect

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Nonlinear optics Kerr effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The electro-optic Kerr effect produces linear birefringence proportional to the square of a static applied electric field. Defining the Kerr effect coefficient K by Δn=λ0​KE2, a length L produces phase retardation δ=2πKLE2. A switchable quarter-wave plate requires ∣δ∣=π/2 modulo 2π, with fixed principal axes. This differs from the Faraday effect, whose eigenpolarizations are circular polarizations.
