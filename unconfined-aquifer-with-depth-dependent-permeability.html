Unconfined aquifer with depth-dependent permeability

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Aquifer Unconfined aquifer
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
With permeability of a porous medium k(z)=k0​(1+βz), the Dupuit approximation gives
q=−ub​(h+2β​h2)hx​,ϕht​+qx​=R,ub​=μk0​ρg​.
(3)
Here ϕ is porosity and R is recharge. The low-height limit has mobility proportional to h, whereas large heights have mobility proportional to h2. This changes both the forced filling similarity for power-law diffusion and the separable draining profile for power-law diffusion.
