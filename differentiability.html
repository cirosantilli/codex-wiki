Differentiability

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differentiable function
OurBigBook.com Words: 311 Articles: 5
Differentiability is the existence of a derivative at a point. For maps between finite-dimensional normed vector spaces, the existence of a Fréchet derivative requires a single linear map approximating the increment with error o(∥h∥). Merely having partial derivatives need not provide this approximation; continuous partial derivatives on a neighbourhood do suffice.
