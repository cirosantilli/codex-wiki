Hoeffding confidence interval (rn​=log(2/δ)/(2n)​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability inequality Concentration inequality Hoeffding inequality
OurBigBook.com Words: 133
For a fixed positive sample size n of independent and identically distributed Bernoulli random variables, let
rn​=2nlog(2/δ)​​,Cn​=[max{0,p​n​−rn​},min{1,p​n​+rn​}].
(29)
The Hoeffding inequality makes Cn​ a conservative confidence interval for p: Pp​(p∈Cn​)≥1−δ for every p∈[0,1]. Indeed, exclusion from the closed interval implies ∣p​n​−p∣>rn​, an event contained in the deviation event bounded by δ. Clipping to [0,1] retains coverage because p already lies there. For n=738, δ=0.05, and an observed frequency 369/738=1/2, rn​≈0.0499924, giving approximately [0.450008,0.549992].
Coverage concerns repeated independent samples: the unknown p is fixed and the confidence interval is random. This fixed-n guarantee does not justify repeatedly checking the same bound and stopping as soon as it looks favorable. The Hoeffding sample size for Bernoulli frequencies chooses n in advance; guarantees across repeated inspection times require separate control of the additional events, for example through a union bound with an allocated failure probability at each time.
