OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A two-index arrangement of vibronic transition wavenumbers. Differences along rows isolate lower-state spacings; differences down columns isolate upper-state spacings. Deviations from exact separability can expose measurement uncertainty, rotational structure or incorrect assignments.
