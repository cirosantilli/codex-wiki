Signed Young permutation module (Mλ)

OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Over the complex numbers, the signed Young permutation module is
Mλ=IndSλ​Sn​​sgn≅sgn⊗Mλ.
(26)
The isomorphism is the tensor identity for induced representations. The partition index is unchanged by this twist; conjugation instead changes the labels of its irreducible constituents. For the conjugate partition λ′, the character inner product of Mλ and Mλ′ is one: Mackey restriction formula reduces it to the unique zero-one matrix with row margins λ and column margins λ′.
