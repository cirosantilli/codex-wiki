Vanishing-discount limit of an elimination all-pay contest

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Contest theory Sequential elimination all-pay contest Backward-induction threshold in an elimination all-pay contest
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
As δ↑1, both active players' effective prizes in every nonfinal subgame tend to the marginal valuation vr+1​. The two-player complete-information all-pay equilibrium then gives each a winning probability 1/2. In the final stage, actual valuations determine the asymmetric winning probabilities. Taking this limit from discounted equilibria specifies the continuation selection instead of independently choosing an undiscounted game equilibrium.
