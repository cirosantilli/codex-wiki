OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A penguin diagram is a loop topology in which a neutral gauge boson is emitted during a flavour-changing transition. A representative electroweak top-quark diagram contains a charged vector and a down-type quark loop. Individual attachments need not be gauge invariant; the physical amplitude includes the required companion graphs and flavour sums.
