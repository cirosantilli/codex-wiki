Multivariate moving-average model (Zt​=∑j=0q​Θj​Wt−j​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 108 Articles: 1
A multivariate moving-average model filters vector white noise through fixed matrix coefficients. If the noise has zero mean and lag-zero covariance matrix D, with all nonzero-lag cross-covariances zero, then the filtered process is a weakly stationary process. With the convention Γ(h)=E[Zt​Zt+hT​], its covariance matrix is Γ(h)=∑j​Θj​DΘj+hT​, omitting terms outside the filter range. Positive and negative lags satisfy Γ(−h)=Γ(h)T.
