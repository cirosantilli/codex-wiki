Guiding-centre drift (vd​=F×B/(qB2))

 ... Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force Gyromotion
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 2
For a constant perpendicular force in a uniform magnetic field, write the particle velocity as v=vd​+u. Choosing qvd​×B=−F leaves the ordinary gyromotion equation for u. The gyroperiod-averaged transverse velocity is therefore the displayed drift. An electric force gives the same drift for both charge signs; a charge-independent applied force gives opposite drifts.
