Witt algebra ([ℓm​,ℓn​]=(m−n)ℓm+n​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 19
The Witt algebra is the Lie algebra of vector fields on a circle with Laurent-polynomial coefficients. Its nontrivial central extension of a Lie algebra is the Virasoro algebra.
