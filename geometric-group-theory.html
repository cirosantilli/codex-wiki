Geometric group theory

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 129
Geometric group theory studies finitely generated groups through their actions on metric spaces and through the large-scale geometry of spaces such as their Cayley graphs.
