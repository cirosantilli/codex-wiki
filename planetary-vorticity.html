Planetary vorticity (f=2Ωsinϕ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Vorticity Absolute vorticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
The planetary contribution to absolute vorticity is supplied by the rotating frame. Its local vertical component is the Coriolis parameter f=2Ωsinϕ. On a beta plane, motion with northward velocity v changes this contribution at the rate βv.
