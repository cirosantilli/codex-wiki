Symmetric Flory-Huggins polymer blend (χc​=2/N)

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry) Polymer solution Flory–Huggins solution theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 1
For two equal-site-volume polymers of chain length N, the mixing free energy per lattice site is f/(kB​T)=[ϕlnϕ+(1−ϕ)ln(1−ϕ)]/N+χϕ(1−ϕ). Each chain is one translating object, reducing the entropy of mixing per monomer by N relative to the regular solution model. Its curvature is f′′/(kB​T)=1/[Nϕ(1−ϕ)]−2χ. The symmetric thermodynamic critical point has ϕc​=1/2 and χc​=2/N. Long chains can therefore demix under a very small unfavorable Flory interaction parameter.
