Real-simple exception to uniqueness of the Killing form

 ... Lie algebra Lie algebra homomorphism Lie algebra representation Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra Killing form Uniqueness of an invariant bilinear form on a simple Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 97
The usual uniqueness theorem assumes a complex simple Lie algebra, or a real form with simple complexification. For a complex simple algebra regarded as real, both ReκC​ and ImκC​ define a real symmetric invariant bilinear form on a Lie algebra, and they are linearly independent. The real Killing form is 2ReκC​. For sl2​(C) and H=diag(1,−1), κC​(H,H)=8 and κC​(H,iH)=8i, proving the independence. This real algebra is simple: its real complexification is two complex simple ideals exchanged by conjugation, and a real ideal would complexify to a conjugation-stable sum of these ideals, hence either zero or the whole algebra.
