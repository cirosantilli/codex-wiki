Square-integrable martingale (E∣Mt​∣2<∞ for every finite t)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A martingale is square integrable when its value at every time belongs to L2 space. This implies ordinary integrability by Cauchy-Schwarz inequality. The condition is separate from a uniform bound on these second moments over an infinite horizon. An Itô integral is square integrable on a finite interval when its predictable integrand has finite expected integrated square, by Itô isometry.
