Riemann tensor Clifford contraction (Rabcd​γaγbγcγd=−2R1)

OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The four-gamma product decomposes into fully antisymmetric, two-gamma and scalar parts. The first part vanishes on contraction by the first Bianchi identity, and the two-gamma parts vanish because they contract symmetric Ricci curvature against antisymmetric gamma matrices. The scalar contractions are −gacgbdRabcd​+gadgbcRabcd​=−2R. Here R=gacgbdRabcd​ fixes the curvature-slot convention.
