Natural cubic spline interpolant (Nv,(Nv)(xi​)=vi​)

 ... Analysis Uniform approximation Spline (mathematics) Spline approximation Cubic spline Natural cubic spline
OurBigBook.com Words: 263 Articles: 3
For at least two distinct knots x1​<⋯<xn​ and values vi​, the unique natural cubic spline matching those values and linear beyond x1​,xn​. Its interpolation map is a linear map by uniqueness. Over a larger domain [a,b], the two exterior pieces remain linear. With only one knot, arbitrary slopes of affine functions make uniqueness false.
