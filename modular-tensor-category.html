Modular tensor category

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Monoidal category Braided monoidal category
OurBigBook.com Words: 155 Articles: 4
A finite semisimple ribbon braided monoidal category with nondegenerate modular S-matrix. For a unitary anyon theory, this means that the only transparent anyon is the vacuum anyon. Such data define a three-dimensional topological quantum field theory.
