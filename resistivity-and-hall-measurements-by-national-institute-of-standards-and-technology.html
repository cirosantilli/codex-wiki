By National Institute of Standards and Technology.
Official text: www.nist.gov/pml/nanoscale-device-characterization-division/popular-links/hall-effect/resistivity-and-hall
Institutional measurement procedures covering contact geometry, reversal of electric current and magnetic field, and extraction of sheet carrier density and Hall mobility. Bulk density requires the conducting thickness.
