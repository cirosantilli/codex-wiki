Radical polymerization

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymerization Chain-growth polymerization
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 2
A chain-growth polymerization whose propagating active centres are free radicals. Initiation creates active chains, propagation adds monomers while regenerating an active centre, and termination or transfer changes the population of growing chains.[ref]
