Light intensity (I=⟨S⟩⋅n)

 Home Physics Wave Intensity (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
Here light intensity means the time-averaged energy flux per perpendicular area for light. It is the propagation-direction component of the averaged Poynting vector. For plane electromagnetic waves in the same medium it is proportional to squared electric field wave amplitude, giving the projection-squared factor in Malus's law.
