Bendixson-Dulac theorem

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
If D is simply connected and a C1 function B makes ∇⋅(BF) have one sign and not vanish identically on any open subset of D, then the planar system x˙=F(x) has no periodic orbit lying in D.
