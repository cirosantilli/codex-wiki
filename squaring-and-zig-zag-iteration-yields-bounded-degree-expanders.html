Squaring and zig-zag iteration yields bounded-degree expanders

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Metric embedding Expander graph Zig-zag product
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
Choose D=d2 and a fixed d-regular graph H on D2 vertices with absolute nonconstant eigenvalues at most 1/10. Start with the loop-inclusive complete graph G0​ on D vertices and iterate Gt+1​=Gt2​zigzagH. The degree stays D, vertex counts are D2t+1, and ρt+1​≤ρt2​+21/100. Since ρ0​=0, all ρt​≤1/2. The spectral cut bound gives multiplicity-counted edge expansion at least D/4. Removing loops and merging parallel edges loses at most a factor D, producing simple expander graphs with maximum degree D and edge expansion at least 1/4.
