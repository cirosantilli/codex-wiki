Etendue of a circular aperture and cone (G=n2Aπsin2θmax​)

Consider a planar circular aperture of diameter D in constant refractive index n. Suppose every position in its area A=πD2/4 is filled with directions inside the same rotationally symmetric cone about the unit normal, with half-angle θmax​. Using spherical coordinates, the exact etendue is

where NA=nsinθmax​ is the appropriate side's numerical aperture. The ordinary cone solid angle is 2π(1−cosθmax​); its cosine-weighted value is πsin2θmax​. A full forward hemisphere therefore gives G=n2Aπ, rather than 2πn2A. [ref]
