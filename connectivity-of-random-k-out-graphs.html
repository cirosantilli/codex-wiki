Connectivity of random k-out graphs (Pr(Gk-out​ connected)→1(k≥2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For k=2, a disconnected graph has a component of size 3≤s≤n/2. The probability that a fixed s-set has no crossing edge is at most (s/n)2s(1−s/n)2(n−s). The union bound and (sn​)≤(n/s)s[n/(n−s)]n−s bound the failure probability by ∑s=3⌊n/2⌋​(s/n)s=o(1). Split this sum at n​. The random k-out graph coupling extends the result to every fixed k≥2.
