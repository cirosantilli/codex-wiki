For the Gaussian Brownian drift filter Yt​=αt+Wt​, with independent prior α∼N(m0​,τ0−1​), the observation law has density
Lt​(y)=τ0​+tτ0​​​exp(2(τ0​+t)(τ0​m0​+y)2​−2τ0​m02​​)
(2)
relative to driftless Wiener measure. The law having fixed observation drift k has density ekYt​−k2t/2 relative to the same reference. Their ratio is therefore a positive mean-one density on every finite horizon. In the observation filtration it satisfies dDt​=−Dt​(mt​−k)dWt​, where mt​ is the posterior mean and W the innovation process. This density-ratio argument establishes a true martingale without an unsupported global Novikov condition for the unbounded posterior mean.
