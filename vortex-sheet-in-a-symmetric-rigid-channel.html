Vortex sheet in a symmetric rigid channel (ω=k(U1​+U2​)/2±i∣k(U1​−U2​)∣/2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Shear layer Vortex sheet Kelvin-Helmholtz instability
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
Consider an equal-density vortex sheet halfway between rigid walls, with equal layer depths h, base velocities U1​,U2​ and no restoring interfacial force. The irrotational perturbations have vertical factors cosh(k(y−h)) above and cosh(k(y+h)) below. Their kinematic boundary conditions and pressure continuity give
coth(∣k∣h)[(ω−kU1​)2+(ω−kU2​)2]=0.
(113)
The common depth factor cancels, yielding the displayed dispersion relation roots and Kelvin-Helmholtz instability for nonzero shear. The real phase velocity of the pattern is (U1​+U2​)/2 and the growing branch has temporal growth rate ∣k(U1​−U2​)∣/2. This exact depth cancellation depends on equal depths and equal mass densities; a single nearby wall or unequal layers changes the inertia factors.
