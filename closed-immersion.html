Closed immersion

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes
OurBigBook.com Words: 869 Articles: 17
A morphism i:Z→X is a closed immersion when it identifies Z homeomorphically with a closed subset of X and the morphism OX​→i∗​OZ​ is surjective. Affine-locally it has the form Spec(A/I)→SpecA.
