Elliptic curve group law from Riemann-Roch (E(k)≅Pic0(E))

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
Fix O∈E(k). On a genus-one smooth projective curve, every degree-one divisor class has a unique effective point representative by the Riemann-Roch theorem: its section space has dimension one, since the complementary canonical-divisor degree is negative. Thus P↦[P−O] is a bijection onto the degree-zero Picard group of a curve. Transporting divisor-class addition proves the group axioms, with identity O, and characterizes P+Q by P+Q∼R+O.
