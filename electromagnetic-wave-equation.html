Electromagnetic wave equation (∇2E−c−2∂t2​E=0)

In source-free vacuum the electric field and magnetic field obey
∇2E−c−2∂t2​E=0,∇2B−c−2∂t2​B=0.

A brief derivation takes the curl of Faraday's law and substitutes the Ampère-Maxwell equation. The vector calculus identity ∇×(∇×E)=∇(∇⋅E)−∇2E, together with source-free Gauss's law, leaves ∇2E=μ0​ϵ0​∂t2​E. The same argument gives the magnetic field equation. The operator ∇2 is the Laplacian, applied componentwise.
