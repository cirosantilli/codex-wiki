Ergodic component (μy​)

 ... Real analysis Measure theory Ergodic theory Measure-preserving transformation Ergodicity Ergodic decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
A conditional probability of an invariant probability measure given its invariant sigma-algebra. In a standard Borel probability system, almost every such probability is invariant and ergodic. Their integral recovers the original measure. The countable-test proof of ergodicity of conditional components explains why invariance alone is not the whole conclusion.
