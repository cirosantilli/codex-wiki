Multiplicity bounds the length of a holonomic Weyl module (length(M)≤m(M))

 ... Algebra over a field Associative algebra Gelfand–Kirillov dimension Gelfand–Kirillov dimension of a module Bernstein growth dimension of a Weyl algebra module Holonomic Weyl algebra module
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
Every nonzero subquotient of a holonomic Weyl algebra module over a characteristic-zero field has dimension n, by Bernstein inequality for Weyl algebra modules and dimension and multiplicity in a filtered exact sequence. Each strict step in a submodule chain consumes positive multiplicity of a filtered module. With a standard graded algebra normalization these multiplicities are integral. There are therefore at most m(M) strict steps. The module is Artinian and Noetherian, and its module length is bounded by that multiplicity.
