Nonlinear-susceptibility tensor symmetry

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Nonlinear optics Nonlinear optical susceptibility
OurBigBook.com Words: 258 Articles: 4
The order-n response has one output vector index and n input vector indices. Material point-group invariance, permutation of input index-frequency pairs, and reality of the electric polarization impose different constraints. They must not be conflated with the stronger dispersion-neglecting Kleinman symmetry.
