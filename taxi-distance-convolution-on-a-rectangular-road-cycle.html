Taxi distance convolution on a rectangular road cycle (D=A+B)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Joint probability distribution Convolution of independent random variables
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For a taxi starting at any vertex of a rectangle with side lengths s,t, a uniform pickup vertex gives approach-distance law uniform on 0,s,t,s+t. Conditional on that pickup, a uniform distinct destination gives passenger-distance law uniform on s,t,s+t, independent of the pickup vertex. Hence the two distance variables are independent despite sharing that vertex. Their convolution has twelve equally likely pairs. Its mean is 7(s+t)/6 and its variance is (s2+t2)/4+2(s2+t2−st)/9. Repeated numerical sums combine their multiplicities when some distances coincide.
