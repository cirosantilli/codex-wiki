Householder reflection sending a vector to a coordinate axis (Hx=αe1​,∣α∣=∥x∥2​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Householder transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 140
For a nonzero real vector x∈Rn, choose α with ∣α∣=∥x∥2​ and put v=x−αe1​. When v=0, the Householder reflection
H=I−vTv2vvT​
(63)
satisfies Hx=αe1​: every component except the first becomes zero. Here e1​ is the first Cartesian basis vector. The calculation is short:
vTv=2(∥x∥22​−αx1​),vTx=∥x∥22​−αx1​,Hx=x−v=αe1​.
(64)
If v=0, the vector is already at the selected target, so the identity matrix suffices.
In floating-point arithmetic, a customary choice is
α={−∥x∥2​,∥x∥2​,​x1​≥0,x1​<0.​
(65)
Then ∣v1​∣=∣x1​∣+∥x∥2​, avoiding subtraction of nearly equal positive quantities in the first component. Either sign can be valid in exact arithmetic; the numerical choice protects the construction from catastrophic cancellation. [ref]
For x=(3,4)T, this choice gives α=−5 and v=(8,4)T. Dividing v by its Euclidean norm gives u=(2,1)T/5​, exactly the normal in the geometric example.
