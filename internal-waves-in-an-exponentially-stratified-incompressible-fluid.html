Internal waves in an exponentially stratified incompressible fluid (∂t2​(∇2w−αwz​)+αgwxx​=0)

For a quiescent two-dimensional incompressible inviscid fluid with mass density ρ0​(z)=re−αz and α>0, hydrostatic balance and density advection give the linear perturbation equations
ux​+wz​=0,ρ0​ut​=−px​,ρ0​wt​=−pz​−gρ′,ρt′​=αρ0​w.
(2)
Eliminating pressure and density perturbations gives the title partial differential equation. It retains the variation of inertial density rather than invoking the Boussinesq approximation. The transformation w=eαz/2W gives ∂t2​(∇2W−α2W/4)+αgWxx​=0. Between rigid boundaries at z=0,h, the normal modes have
W∝sin(nπz/h)ei(kx−ωt),ω2=k2+(nπ/h)2+α2/4αgk2​,n≥1.
(3)
The vertical velocity is eαz/2W; the exponential factor compensates for the decreasing background mass density in the mode structure.
