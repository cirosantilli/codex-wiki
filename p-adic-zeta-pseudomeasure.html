p-adic zeta pseudomeasure (∫xkdζp​=(1−pk−1)ζ(1−k)(k≥2 even))

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory L-function p-adic L-function Kubota-Leopoldt p-adic L-function
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
On G=Zp×​/{±1}, this element of the total quotient ring of the Iwasawa algebra becomes an integral measure after multiplication by every difference [a]−[1]. Its even moments interpolate the displayed Euler-corrected zeta values. Its trivial-character component has a pole, so the pseudomeasure itself must not be treated as an element of the integral group algebra. Multiplication by the augmentation ideal removes the pole.
