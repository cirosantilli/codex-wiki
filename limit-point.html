Limit point

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Closed set
OurBigBook.com Words: 188 Articles: 1
A limit point of a subset A of a topological space is a point x whose every neighbourhood meets A∖{x}. Thus 1 is a limit point of [0,1) in the real line, even though 1∈/[0,1). In contrast, an isolated point of A belongs to its closure but is not a limit point of A. This distinction separates limit points from the more inclusive notion of an adherent point.
