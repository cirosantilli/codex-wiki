Overdamped harmonic-trap power spectrum (Sx(1)​(f)=π2γ(f2+fc2​)kB​T​,fc​=k/(2πγ))

In the classical thermal model, a coordinate in a harmonic potential obeys γx˙+kx=ξ, where γ is the drag coefficient and ⟨ξ(t)ξ(t′)⟩=2γkB​Tδ(t−t′). Its stationary one-sided spectral density per hertz is the displayed Lorentzian line shape. The integral over positive frequencies equals kB​T/k, consistent with the equipartition theorem. On a log-log plot the spectrum is flat for f≪fc​ and has slope −2 for f≫fc​. The crossover is determined by the restoring stiffness and drag; an added detector response multiplies the mechanical spectrum by the squared magnitude of its transfer function.
