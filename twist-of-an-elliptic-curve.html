Twist of an elliptic curve

OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A twist of an elliptic curve E/K is an elliptic curve over K that becomes isomorphic to E over an algebraic closure of K. Twists are classified by the Galois-cohomology set H1(GK​,Aut(EK​)).
