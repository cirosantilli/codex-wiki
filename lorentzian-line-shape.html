Lorentzian line shape (L(ν)=γ2/[(ν−ν0​)2+γ2])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar atmosphere Spectral line
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
A spectral line profile with the shape of a Cauchy distribution. In the displayed peak-normalized convention, ν is frequency, ν0​ is the line centre, and γ>0 is the half width at half maximum: L(ν0​±γ)=1/2, so the full width is 2γ. The normalization used for a probability density function differs from this unit-peak convention.
