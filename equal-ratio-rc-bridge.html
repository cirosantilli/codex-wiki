Equal-ratio RC bridge (ω0−2​=Rx2​Cx​(C−Cx​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A bridge driven between top and bottom nodes has equal resistors on its left arms, upper-right electrical impedance Z=Rx​/(1+iωRx​Cx​) and lower-right electrical impedance W=Rv​+1/(iωC). A zero detector electric current requires Z=W. Equating real parts and imaginary parts gives Rv​=Rx​(1−Cx​/C) and ω0−2​=Rx2​Cx​(C−Cx​), requiring 0<Cx​<C. Its open-circuit right-minus-left junction voltage is V0​(W−Z)/[2(W+Z)], whereas a connected ideal ammeter has zero terminal voltage at every angular frequency. This distinction separates a null-current measurement from an unloaded voltage measurement.
