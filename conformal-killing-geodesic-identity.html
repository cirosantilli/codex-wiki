Conformal Killing geodesic identity (d[g(X,U)]/ds=λg(U,U))

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Conformal Killing vector field
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
Let X be a Conformal Killing vector field with conformal Killing factor λ, and let U be the tangent vector of a geodesic with affine parameter s. The product rule, metric compatibility and geodesic equation give
dsd​g(X,U)=g(∇U​X,U)+g(X,∇U​U)=UaUb∇(a​Xb)​=λg(U,U).
(11)
Every null geodesic therefore has the conserved quantity g(X,U). On a timelike geodesic with proper time as parameter, the derivative is −λ in metric signature (−,+,+,+). With a nonaffine parameter satisfying ∇U​U=fU, an extra term fg(X,U) appears; the conserved expression for a null geodesic is tied to its affine parameter normalization.
