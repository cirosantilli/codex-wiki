Centralizer of a subalgebra (CA​(B))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 413 Articles: 5
For a subalgebra B of an associative algebra A, its centralizer is
CA​(B)={a∈A:ab=ba for every b∈B}.
(1)
It is a subalgebra of A. When A is a group algebra and B is the group algebra of a subgroup H, its elements are exactly the linear combinations with coefficients constant on the conjugation orbits of H.
