Arzela-Ascoli theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Topological analysis Equicontinuous family of functions
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 2
On a compact metric space, a uniformly bounded equicontinuous sequence of scalar continuous functions has a uniformly convergent subsequence. Equivalently, uniform boundedness and equicontinuity characterize relative compactness in C(K).
