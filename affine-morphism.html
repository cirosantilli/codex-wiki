Affine morphism

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 1
A morphism f:X→Y is affine when the inverse image of every affine open subscheme of Y is affine. For a quasi-coherent sheaf F on X, one has Rqf∗​F=0 for q>0.
