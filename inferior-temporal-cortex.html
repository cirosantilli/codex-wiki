Inferior temporal cortex

 Home Biology Neuroscience Brain Cerebral cortex
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A visual cortical region in primates with responses selective for objects and faces. Experience can change its tolerance to transformations, and face-selective patches differ in viewpoint and identity coding.
