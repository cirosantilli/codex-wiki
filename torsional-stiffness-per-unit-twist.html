Torsional stiffness per unit twist (Kτ​=dN/dτ)

 ... Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Hooke's law Spring Torsion spring Torsional stiffness
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For a uniform rod of length L and twist per unit length τ, the end-rotation angle is φ=Lτ. The chain rule gives Kτ​=LKφ​, where Kφ​ is the torsional stiffness. If a source defines a normalized torque M=N/(2π), its derivative is dM/dτ=Kτ​/(2π).
