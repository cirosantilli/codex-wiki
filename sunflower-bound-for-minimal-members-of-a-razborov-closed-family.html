Sunflower bound for minimal members of a Razborov-closed family (as​≤s!(r−1)s)

 ... Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Circuit complexity Monotone circuit complexity Razborov approximation method Razborov closure
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
An inclusion-minimal s-set in an upward-closed family cannot belong to an r-member delta-system of such minimal sets: its proper core would be adjoined by Razborov closure, violating minimality. The Erdős–Rado sunflower lemma therefore bounds the number as​ of minimal s-sets. This weaker factorial bound is sufficient for the superpolynomial monotone clique lower bound.
