Local ring of a smooth algebraic curve

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic variety Projective space Projective variety Smooth projective curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 19
At every point of a smooth algebraic curve, the local ring is a discrete valuation ring. Its valuation measures the order of vanishing of a rational function at that point.
