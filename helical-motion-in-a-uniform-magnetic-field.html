Helical motion in a uniform magnetic field

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Charged particle in a uniform magnetic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
In a uniform magnetic field, the Lorentz force leaves the component of velocity parallel to the field constant and rotates the perpendicular component at the nonrelativistic cyclotron frequency ∣q∣B/m. The resulting path is a helix around a field line.
