Elementary row operation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Numerical linear algebra Gaussian elimination
OurBigBook.com Words: 203 Articles: 3
An elementary row operation interchanges two rows of a matrix, multiplies one row by a nonzero scalar, or adds a scalar multiple of another row to it. These invertible operations are used in Gaussian elimination. For a square matrix, interchange changes the sign of its determinant, multiplication scales its determinant by the same scalar, and adding another row leaves its determinant unchanged, by multilinearity and alternation.
