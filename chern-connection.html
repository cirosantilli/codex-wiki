Chern connection

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold First Chern class
OurBigBook.com Words: 189 Articles: 4
The Chern connection is the unique connection on a Hermitian holomorphic bundle compatible with both its metric and holomorphic structure. Its curvature has type (1,1).
