Logical connective

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Proposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 776 Articles: 14
A logical connective forms a compound proposition from component propositions; logical truth and logical falsity are the zero-input cases. Logical negation has one input, while logical conjunction, logical disjunction, and logical implication each have two. In classical propositional logic, these logical connectives are truth-functional: their output value is determined by their input values, as listed in a truth table. This does not characterize every kind of logical connective in every logic. See forall x: Calgary, section 10.1.
