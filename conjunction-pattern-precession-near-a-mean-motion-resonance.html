Conjunction-pattern precession near a mean-motion resonance (Λ˙)

 ... Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Orbital resonance Mean-motion resonance Resonant argument Resonant conjunction geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 116 Articles: 1
With constant mean motions and Pc​/Pb​=(1+δ)(p+q)/p, mean conjunctions advance by ΔΛ=2π/(Pc​/Pb​−1) each synodic period. Tracking one branch every q conjunctions and subtracting 2πp gives
Λ˙=qPsyn​qΔΛ−2πp​=−qPb​(1+δ)2πpδ​.
(58)
The usual formula −2πpδ/(qPb​) keeps only first order in δ. A labelled branch takes qPb​(1+δ)/(p∣δ∣) to turn once, whereas the full set of q interchangeable directions repeats after Pb​(1+δ)/(p∣δ∣). Finite orbital eccentricity introduces true-conjunction corrections.
