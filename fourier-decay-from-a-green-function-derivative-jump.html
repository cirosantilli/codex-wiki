Fourier decay from a Green-function derivative jump (∫H(s)e−iqsds∼−[H′]e−iqy/q2)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Green's function One-dimensional modified Helmholtz Green function
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
If H is continuous, piecewise twice differentiable, and has derivative jump [H′]=H′(y+)−H′(y−) at y, then its distributional second derivative is Hreg′′​+[H′]δy​. If the regular part is integrable and the boundary terms vanish, two integrations by parts give the displayed asymptotic expansion by the Riemann-Lebesgue lemma. A jump [H] in H itself instead produces [H]e−iqy/(iq) at leading order. This distinguishes the different decay rates of the streamfunction and streamwise velocity in linear inviscid damping in unbounded Couette flow.
