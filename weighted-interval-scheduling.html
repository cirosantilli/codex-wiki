Weighted interval scheduling (max∑i∈S​wi​)

 Home Computer science Algorithm Greedy algorithm Interval scheduling
OurBigBook.com Words: 97
Weighted interval scheduling assigns each job a reward wi​ and maximizes their sum over compatible jobs. Earliest finish can fail for this different objective function; dynamic programming can compare alternatives.[ref]
For a separate two-job example, let X=[0,2) have reward one and Y=[1,3) reward ten. Earliest finish accepts X and rejects Y, earning one; the optimum accepts Y, earning ten. Replacing Y by X preserves job count but loses reward. Thus the interval scheduling exchange argument proves the unit-value theorem only: its preserved quantity must match the intended objective function.
