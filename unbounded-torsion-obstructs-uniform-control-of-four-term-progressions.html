Unbounded torsion obstructs uniform control of four-term progressions (∥f∥U3​→0 ⇒ Λ4​→0 uniformly in G)

 ... Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Gowers uniformity norm Gowers U3 norm Generalized von Neumann inequality for four-term progressions Four-term progression bound with torsion factors
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
A bound tending to zero with one factor's Gowers U3 norm, uniformly over all finite abelian groups, is false. On G=F2m​, choose a random independent sign at each point. A cube with eight distinct vertices has sign product of expectation zero. The probability of a collision among its vertices is at most (28​)/∣G∣, since each equality of distinct vertices imposes a nonzero linear equation in the increments. Thus some sign function f has ∥f∥U38​≤28/∣G∣. But the four-term arithmetic progression is (x,x+d,x,x+d), so choosing factors (f,1,f,1) gives correlation identically one. As m→∞, the norm tends to zero. Bounded torsion, as in cyclic groups, avoids this obstruction.
