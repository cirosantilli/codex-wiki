Normed algebra ((A,∥⋅∥))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 224 Articles: 3
A normed algebra is an algebra over a field with a norm satisfying ∥ab∥≤∥a∥∥b∥. Its completion is a Banach algebra, since multiplication extends continuously. Completeness is additional structure, rather than part of the definition of a normed algebra.
