Coordination number (CN)

 Home Chemistry Chemical bond Ionic bonding Ionic crystal
OurBigBook.com Words: 47 Articles: 2
In this structural use, the coordination number counts nearest neighbouring constituents. State which neighbours are counted and which ionic crystal structure is being described; it is not an ion's electric charge number.
