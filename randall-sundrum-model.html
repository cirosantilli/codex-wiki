Randall–Sundrum model (ds2=e−2k∣y∣ημν​dxμdxν+dy2)

A warped five-dimensional geometry redshifts masses of fields localized on the distant brane: mIR​=e−kLm0​. For the usual doubled orbifold interval, integration of the Einstein-Hilbert action gives MPl2​=M53​(1−e−2kL)/k. Exponential redshift can generate a large separation of observable scales with a moderately sized dimensionless distance kL. Stabilizing the radion remains a dynamical requirement.
