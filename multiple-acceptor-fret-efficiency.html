Multiple-acceptor FRET efficiency (En​=nE/[1+(n−1)E])

 Home Biology Biophysical measurement Förster resonance energy transfer FRET efficiency
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
For n identical independent available FRET acceptors, transfer rates add. With single-pair FRET efficiency E, the donor exit probability is En​=nE/[1+(n−1)E]. It is not nE; competition with the donor's other decay routes must be retained.
