Vacuum energy density (ρvac​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram Vacuum Feynman diagram Vacuum energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
The vacuum energy density is the bulk limit ρvac​=limV→∞​E0​(V)/V, with boundary effects removed and a fixed convention for the Hamiltonian. In an effective field theory at a homogeneous vacuum it is the value of the renormalized scalar potential. A global supersymmetry algebra expresses the regulated vacuum energy as a sum of supercharge norms, giving nonnegative density; the scalar potential of supergravity instead includes a negative gravitational term.
