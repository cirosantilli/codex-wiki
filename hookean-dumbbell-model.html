Hookean dumbbell model (U(R)=H∣R∣2/2)

 Home Chemistry Polymer
OurBigBook.com Words: 603 Articles: 7
Represent a dilute polymer by two beads connected by a spring obeying Hooke's law, with stiffness H>0 and bead drag coefficient ζ>0. Neglect bead inertia and hydrodynamic interactions. For component-first velocity gradient Kij​=∂j​ui​, the configuration Fokker-Planck probability current is
J=KRψ−ζ2​(HRψ+kB​T∇R​ψ),∂t​ψ=−∇R​⋅J.
(1)
The factor two comes from relative motion of both beads. At rest its normalized probability density is ψeq​=(H/(2πkB​T))3/2e−HR2/(2kB​T). The conformation tensor C=⟨RRT⟩ has dimensions of length squared; (H/(kB​T))C is its dimensionless version. The Hookean dumbbell in planar extension is a particular imposed flow in this model.
