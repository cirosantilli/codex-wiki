Filtration on a group

 ... Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Group algebra Completed group algebra Iwasawa algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 4
A filtration on a group G is a function ω:G→R∪{∞} satisfying
ω(xy−1)≥min{ω(x),ω(y)},ω([x,y])≥ω(x)+ω(y).
(8)
It is separated when ω(x)=∞ only for x=1.
