A sphere cannot fibre over a lower-dimensional odd sphere

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Serre spectral sequence Cohomological Serre spectral sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 109
There is no fiber bundle F→Sm→Sq when m>q≥3 and q is odd. A local trivialization makes F a retract of an open subset of Sm, so Hj(F;Q)=0 for j>m; the long exact sequence of homotopy groups of a fibration makes F connected. In the multiplicative rational Serre spectral sequence, only base columns zero and q occur. Since Hq(Sm)=0, a fibre class a∈Hq−1(F;Q) must satisfy dq​a=u, the base generator. Its degree is even. If as=0 is its first vanishing power, the derivation law gives 0=dq​(as)=suas−1=0 on the Eq​ page, a contradiction. Bounded fibre cohomology ensures such a vanishing power exists. In particular S4 cannot be the total space of a bundle over S3.
