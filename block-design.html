Block design

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Design of experiments Blocks in experimental design
OurBigBook.com Words: 337 Articles: 7
A block design assigns treatments to experimental units partitioned into blocks. Its incidence counts nij​ record how often treatment i occurs in block j. Balance and connectedness determine which treatment contrasts are estimable and how much adjustment for blocks costs.
