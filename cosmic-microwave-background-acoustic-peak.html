Cosmic microwave background acoustic peak (CMB acoustic peak)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 154 Articles: 2
Cosmic microwave background acoustic peaks arise from photon-baryon oscillations caught at successive extrema at recombination. Their approximate phase is set by krs​=nπ and their angular positions measure the sound horizon relative to the distance to last scattering.
