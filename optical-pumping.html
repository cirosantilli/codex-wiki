Optical pumping

 Home Physics Atomic physics Atomic measurement methods
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 4
Optical pumping redistributes populations among internal quantum states by selective photon absorption followed by spontaneous emission. It can prepare atoms for measurement and participate in sub-Doppler cooling.
The practical aim is to make otherwise almost equally populated magnetic sublevels behave differently. Repeated absorption and emission can store a population imbalance in the long-lived ground state; a change in photon absorption or fluorescence then reveals a perturbation of that prepared distribution. This combines preparation and measurement: a weak radio wave can reorganize the populations, while abundant optical photons supply the readout.
An imbalance among ground state magnetic sublevels need not be a population inversion between optical energy levels. Optical pumping also supplies excitation in some lasers, where an appropriate population inversion matters for gain. The preparation problem here concerns the distribution within the ground state. A two-level system with only one lower quantum state cannot describe this redistribution.
