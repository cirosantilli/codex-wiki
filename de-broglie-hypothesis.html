de Broglie hypothesis

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Wave function Matter wave de Broglie wavelength
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
A material particle carries a quantum phase whose wave vector and angular frequency obey p=ℏk and E=ℏω. Interference and diffraction test this relation; a localized particle requires a wave packet.
