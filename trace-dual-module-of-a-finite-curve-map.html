Trace-dual module of a finite curve map (π∗​ΩC1​≅Hom(π∗​OC​,ΩD1​))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic variety Morphism of algebraic varieties Finite morphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
For a finite generically separable morphism between smooth projective curves, the field trace on rational differentials identifies the differential sheaf with the trace-dual module. Locally, for A=k[[s]]⊂B=k[[u]] with s=f(u), the trace-dual fractional ideal is f′(u)−1B. Multiplying it by ds=f′(u)du gives Bdu. For a monogenic finite extension with monic polynomial P, the identity Tr(g(u)/P′(u))=[Te−1](g(T)modP(T)) proves the dual-module formula: the top-coefficient pairing on the basis 1,u,…,ue−1 has invertible antitriangular matrix. Tensoring locally with an invertible sheaf yields π∗​(M∨⊗ΩC1​)≅Hom(π∗​M,ΩD1​).
