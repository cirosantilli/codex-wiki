Development of the method of types

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Type (information theory) Method of types
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
In his 1998 retrospective, Imre Csiszár describes the method of types as a 1970s refinement of the typical set approach, with older counting ingredients. He credits his research group with turning those ingredients into a general tool, while explicitly declining to give a complete discovery history.[ref] The useful shift is from an approximately typical collection to exact frequency classes: the latter retain enough structure to study how fast error probabilities decay.
