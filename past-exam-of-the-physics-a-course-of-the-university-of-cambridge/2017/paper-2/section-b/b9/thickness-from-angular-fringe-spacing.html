Eliminate the internal angle using Snell's law:
δ(θi​)=λ4πd​n2−sin2θi​​,dθi​dδ​=−λ4πd​n2−sin2θi​​sinθi​cosθi​​.
(44)
Neighbouring bright fringes differ in phase by 2π. The local angular fringe spacing of a transparent film therefore gives
d≃2sinθi​cosθi​Δθi​λn2−sin2θi​​​.
(45)
With Δθi​=0.4π/180=6.981×10−3rad, θi​≃10∘, n=1.2 and λ=500nm,
d≃2.49×10−4m≃0.25mm​.
(46)
The data specify a local spacing near 10∘, so differentiating is appropriate; choosing particular neighbouring fringe angles slightly shifts this approximate result. The phase decreases as incidence angle increases.
