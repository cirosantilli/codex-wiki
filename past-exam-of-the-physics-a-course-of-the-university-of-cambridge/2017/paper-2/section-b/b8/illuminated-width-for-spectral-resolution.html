Near order m, the first angular zero from a principal maximum is δθ=λ/(NDcosθ). Two wavelengths have peak separation Δθ=mΔλ/(Dcosθ). Equating them applies the Rayleigh criterion and gives the resolving power of a diffraction grating,
Δλλ​=mN​.
(34)
Here the required resolving power is 400/0.2=2000, so in second order N≥1000. The illuminated width is therefore
W≃ND=2.00mm​.
(35)
Whether one counts the full illuminated window or the first-to-last slit-centre distance changes the width by one pitch and is immaterial at this precision. The result assumes negligible aberrations and adequate slit illumination and coherence.
