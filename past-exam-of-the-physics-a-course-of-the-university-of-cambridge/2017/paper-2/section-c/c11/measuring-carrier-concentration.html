Use a Hall effect measurement. Pass a known current I through a thin rectangular specimen, apply a perpendicular magnetic field B, and measure the transverse Hall voltage VH​. For a uniform single-carrier sample of thickness t,
RH​=IBVH​t​,n=e∣RH​∣1​=et∣VH​∣IB​​.
(62)
The Hall-voltage sign identifies the carrier sign. Reverse B and the current to separate the field-odd Hall signal from voltage-probe misalignment and offsets, and measure the specimen thickness accurately. The extraction assumes a one-carrier Hall factor near one. In intrinsic or mixed-carrier material, the two-carrier Hall coefficient is mobility weighted, so Hall voltage and conductivity need a two-carrier interpretation rather than this simple inverse formula.
