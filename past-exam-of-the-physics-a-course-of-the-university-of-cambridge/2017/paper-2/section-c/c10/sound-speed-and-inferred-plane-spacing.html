Reading near the origin, an acoustic point is approximately (q,ω)=(1.9×109m−1,0.9×1013s−1). The initial slope therefore estimates
vs​=dqdω−​​​q=0​≃4.7×103ms−1​.
(55)
Combining the slope with the inferred spring constant gives
a=vs​2αmA​+mB​​​≃2.0×10−10m=0.20nm​.
(56)
This a is the model's adjacent Na-plane to Cl-plane spacing; its cell length is 2a. The slope is a graphical estimate, so values around 4.5–5.0kms−1 and spacings near 0.2nm are consistent with the reading precision. A real ionic crystal has interactions beyond the scalar nearest-neighbour model; an intercept-and-slope fit need not reproduce the entire measured dispersion or independently measured structural spacing exactly.
