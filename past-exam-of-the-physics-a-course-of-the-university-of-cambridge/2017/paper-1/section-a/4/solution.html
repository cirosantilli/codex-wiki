Use an inverting operational amplifier. Ground the non-inverting input, connect the source to the inverting input through Rin​, and connect Rf​ from the output back to that same input.
In linear operation the ideal operational amplifier draws no input electric current, and feedback holds the inverting input at the same electric potential as the grounded non-inverting input. This node is a virtual ground. Kirchhoff's current law there gives
Rin​Vi​​+Rf​Vo​​=0,Vi​Vo​​=−Rin​Rf​​.
(9)
The source supplies current Ii​=Vi​/Rin​, so its input impedance is Rin​. The required design is therefore
Rin​=10kΩ,Rf​=100kΩ,Vo​=−10Vi​.​
(10)
Only these two resistors and the single ideal operational amplifier are needed in the signal circuit; its power supplies are implicit. The result assumes an input small enough to keep the output within its linear operating range.
