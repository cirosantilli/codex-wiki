Apply the radial momentum operator to a smooth radial test function. More generally [f(r),pr​]=iℏf′(r), giving the first two labelled identities. Expanding the square gives the third:
(i) [r,pr​]=iℏ,(ii) [r−1,pr​]=−iℏr−2,(iii) pr2​=−ℏ2(∂r2​+r2​∂r​).
(6)
The kinetic-energy decomposition is p2=pr2​+L2/r2. A central potential commutes with L2 and Lz​, so choose simultaneous angular eigenfunctions Ylm​, with L2=l(l+1)ℏ2 and l=0,1,…. The radial Hamiltonian consequently has the stated centrifugal term. The radial inner product uses measure r2dr; adjoints below are taken on compatible regular bound-state domains. The formal radial momentum is not thereby asserted to be a self-adjoint observable on a half-line.
Set q=l+1, W=−q/r+1/(qa) and D=ipr​/ℏ. Since D†=−D formally and [D,W]=W′=q/r2,
Al†​Al​=2a2​[ℏ2pr2​​+r2l(l+1)​−ar2​+q2a21​].
(7)
The hydrogen radial Hamiltonian factorization follows by matching the Coulomb coefficient, which fixes the Bohr radius
a=me24πϵ0​ℏ2​​,Hl​=ma2ℏ2​(Al†​Al​−2(l+1)21​).
(8)
For the second pair of labelled relations, the opposite product changes the sign of W′, hence
(i)[Al​,Al†​]=a2W′=r2a2(l+1)​=ℏ2ma2​(Hl+1​−Hl​).
(9)
Using [A,A†A]=[A,A†]A in the factorization then gives
(ii)[Al​,Hl​]=(Hl+1​−Hl​)Al​,Hl+1​Al​=Al​Hl​.
(10)
This Coulomb angular-momentum intertwining means applying Al​ to an energy eigenstate either gives zero or an eigenstate of Hl+1​ with the same energy. For a normalized negative-energy state,
∥Al​ψ∥2=ℏ2ma2E​+2(l+1)21​≥0.
(11)
At fixed E<0 this would become negative if raising l continued indefinitely. The bound-state ladder termination by norm positivity occurs at some L with AL​ψ=0, giving
En​=−2ma2n2ℏ2​=−2(4πϵ0​)2ℏ2n2me4​,n=L+1=1,2,…​.
(12)
The terminating radial function is proportional to rLe−r/[(L+1)a], regular and normalizable; adjoint intertwiners generate lower l values at fixed n. Positive-energy scattering states are not subject to this terminating negative-energy argument. Here m is the mass in the supplied Hamiltonian; including nuclear motion replaces it by the reduced mass.
