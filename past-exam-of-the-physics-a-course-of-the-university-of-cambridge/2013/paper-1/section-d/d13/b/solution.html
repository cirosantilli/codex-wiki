This explicitly combines data and prior precision, approaching the weighted mean when prior information is weak. Unknown calibration parameters should be jointly inferred and marginalized, not silently fixed. posterior intervals are conditional on the assumed model and prior; posterior predictive checks and sensitivity to plausible priors help expose poor fits and weakly identified parameters. The maximum-likelihood estimate and a Bayesian posterior answer different inferential questions even when their numerical centres agree.
