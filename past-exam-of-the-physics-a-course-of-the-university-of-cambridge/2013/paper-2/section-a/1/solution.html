The wavelength is λ=c/f≃0.020 m. The longest interferometric baseline measures the finest angular structure and the shortest-baseline angular scale describes the coarsest:
θres​∼Bmax​λ​=2.0×10−4 rad≃41′′,θlarge​∼Bmin​λ​=4.0×10−3 rad≃14′​.

These are order-of-magnitude scales, not sharp cutoffs. A commonly used maximum-recoverable-scale convention is 0.6λ/Bmin​, giving about 8.2′; reconstruction also depends on baseline coverage and source structure. The individual antenna's primary beam is a separate field-of-view limit.[ref]
