Packet and group index. A wave packet superposes nearby wavevectors to form a localized envelope. The phase velocity is vp​=ω/k; for a narrow packet with weak distortion the envelope velocity is vg​=dω/dk. Since k=2πfn(f)/c,

ng​(f0​)=1.56,vg​(f0​)=1.56c​≃1.92×108 ms−1​.

(19)
Higher-frequency components have the lower group velocity. For a band symmetric about f0​, the endpoint velocities are approximately vg​(f0​)±370 ms−1.
dispersion-limited digital-fibre link length. Differential group delay accumulates as
Δtdisp​=LΔ(1/vg​)=c2τLΔf​≃(2.0×10−14 sm−1)L.
(20)
The pulse spacing is tr​=10−9 s. Pulse overlap and intersymbol interference become serious when the dispersive spread approaches that spacing, giving
Lmax​∼2τΔfctr​​≃50 km​.
(21)
A conservative full-width estimate tp​+Δtdisp​≲tr​ gives about 45 km; this refinement is not a universal sharp boundary because pulse shape, chirp and detection tolerance are unspecified. Attenuation and modal or waveguide dispersion could impose further limits, but are not quantified by the supplied index law.
digital optical pulse regeneration. Each stage must recover and regenerate the data with fresh, short pulses and correct timing, as well as restore signal level. Amplifying the already broadened waveform alone does not remove accumulated dispersion; explicit dispersion compensation is an alternative to full regeneration.
