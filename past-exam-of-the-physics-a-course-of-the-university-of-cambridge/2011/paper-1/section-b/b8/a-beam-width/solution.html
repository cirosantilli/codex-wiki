 ... Past exam of the physics A course of the University of Cambridge 2011 Paper 1 Section B B8 a (beam width)
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
A 5.0mm beam illuminates N=5.0mm/p=1000 slits. The resolving power of a diffraction grating in order m=3 is mN=3000, corresponding to a Rayleigh criterion separation δλR​≃460/3000=0.153nm. The 0.5nm separation therefore gives two well separated main peaks. The finite-grating incoherent doublet profile adds the mutually incoherent lamp intensities, not their amplitudes.
