 ... Past exam of the physics A course of the University of Cambridge 2011 Paper 1 Section B B8 b (beam width)
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
A 1.5mm beam illuminates only N=300 slits, giving mN=900 and δλR​≃0.511nm. The two main lobes broaden by 1000/300 compared with the wider beam. Their 0.5nm separation is slightly below the stated Rayleigh threshold: marginally unresolved by that criterion. This is not an assertion that the profile has only one maximum: the ideal equal-line sum still has two maxima and a modest central dip, as plotted. Inference from real data also requires adequate signal-to-noise ratio and a known instrumental profile.
