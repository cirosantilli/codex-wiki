Use periodic boundary conditions temporarily to count the allowed wavevectors. Each state occupies (2π)3/V in reciprocal space. For one acoustic polarization, the spherical shell between k and k+dk contains
dN=(2π)3V​4πk2dk.
(8)
Both positive and negative wavevector directions are already included in that sphere; no further factor of two is needed. With the linear dispersion relation k=ω/vs​,
g1​(ω)=dωdN​=2π2vs3​Vω2​.
(9)
A three-dimensional monatomic crystal has three acoustic branches. Under the stated common-speed approximation,
g(ω)=2π2vs3​3Vω2​.​
(10)
This is a phonon density of states per unit angular frequency, not per unit ordinary frequency. If longitudinal and transverse speeds differ, the isotropic expression becomes g=Vω2(vL−3​+2vT−3​)/(2π2). In the Debye model, imposing ∫0ωD​​g(ω)dω=3N sets the cutoff; the linear approximation is not continued to arbitrarily high frequency in a finite lattice.
