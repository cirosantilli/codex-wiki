The reciprocal lattice consists of all wavevectors G whose plane-wave phase is unchanged under every direct-lattice translation R:
eiG⋅R=1,G⋅R∈2πZ.
(43)
It expresses periodicity in wavevector space, with dimensions of inverse length.
Choose incident field convention eiki​⋅r−iωt. The wave at the second atom acquires incident phase ki​⋅rn​ relative to the atom at the origin. Its path to a distant detector is shorter by approximately k^f​⋅rn​, contributing outgoing phase −kf​⋅rn​. Therefore
Δϕ=(ki​−kf​)⋅rn​=ks​⋅rn​.
(44)
Neglecting variation of detector distance across the specimen, identical atoms have a common scattering amplitude factor and
S∝n∑​eiks​⋅rn​.​
(45)
The original geometry diagram shows the incident direction, outgoing direction and direct-space displacement separately; ks​ is a wavevector difference, not an extra ray path.
The primitive reciprocal vectors obey
A⋅a=2π,A⋅b=A⋅c=0,​
(46)
with cyclic versions for B,C. If ks​=hA+kB+lC, every lattice term has phase 2π times an integer, so all terms add constructively. Conversely, full translational reinforcement requires ks​⋅a, ks​⋅b and ks​⋅c to be integer multiples of 2π, hence ks​ is reciprocal. These are the Laue equations. A finite crystal gives finite-width maxima rather than ideal reciprocal-space delta peaks; elastic scattering must also satisfy ∣ki​∣=∣kf​∣.
For the two-dimensional Bravais lattice interpretation, with one atom per primitive cell, take
a=a(1,0),b=a(1/2,3​/2).
(47)
Solving the duality conditions gives
A=a2π​(1,−1/3​),B=a2π​(0,2/3​),
(48)
so
Ghk​=a2π​(h,3​2k−h​),∣Ghk​∣2=3a216π2​(h2−hk+k2).​
(49)
The reciprocal primitive vectors have equal length 4π/(3​a) and angle 120∘; the array itself is triangular. A different primitive basis can use a 60∘ angle without changing the lattice. The direct primitive-cell area is 3​a2/2 and its reciprocal-cell area is (2π)2 divided by that area.
For nonzero integer pairs, h2−hk+k2 has minimum one, attained for six shortest vectors. Therefore the first nonforward maximum has ∣ks​∣=4π/(3​a). If the forward transmitted maximum is included literally, the smallest value is ∣ks​∣=0; the nonzero value is the first diffraction shell. Reaching that shell in elastic scattering additionally requires 4π/(3​a)≤2∣ki​∣.
