For the mathematical induction step, let D^=[A^,B^] and use [A^,D^]=0. The case n=1 is immediate. If [A^m,B^]=mD^A^m−1, then

For position and momentum, [x^,p^​]=iℏI^. Hence [p^​2,x^]=−2iℏp^​, [V(x^),x^]=0 and [V(x^),p^​]=iℏV′(x^). Substitution gives Ehrenfest theorem:

The mean momentum changes by the mean force. It does not follow for a general nonlinear Potential energy that ⟨V′(x^)⟩=V′(⟨x^⟩): a closed classical trajectory for the mean position requires an additional condition, such as a quadratic Potential energy or a sufficiently localized wave packet.
