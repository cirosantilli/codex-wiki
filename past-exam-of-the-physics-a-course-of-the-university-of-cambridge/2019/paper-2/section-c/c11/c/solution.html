Between delta barriers, V=0 and the Schrödinger equation gives ψ′′+k2ψ=0, with k2=2mE/ℏ2 for positive energy. Write the state and derivative as a two-component vector v=(ψ,ψ′)T. Free propagation across one interval of length a is
v(a−)=Pv(0+),P=(coska−ksinka​sinka/kcoska​).
(51)
The wavefunction is continuous at a delta barrier, while its derivative jumps by gψ, with g=2mα/ℏ2. Thus the barrier matrix is
D=(1g​01​),v(a+)=Mv(0+),M=DP.
(52)
Bloch's theorem requires v(a+)=eiϕav(0+). Both D and P have determinant one, so the two eigenvalues of M are eiϕa and e−iϕa. Taking its trace gives
2cos(ϕa)=trM=2coska+kg​sinka.
(53)
Therefore the Floquet discriminant of the delta-comb Kronig-Penney model yields
cos(ϕa)=cos(ka)+ℏ2kmα​sin(ka).​
(54)
This trace argument is the one-cell transfer method for the Kronig-Penney model. The loop imposes the additional discrete values of ϕ derived in part (b). Real ϕ requires the right-hand side to lie in [−1,1], giving allowed energy bands; values outside this range are band gaps. The strength α has units of energy times length, because a Dirac delta distribution has units of inverse length.
