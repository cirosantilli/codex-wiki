For the nth transverse mode, substitute qn​=nπ/a into the membrane waveguide dispersion relation:
k2=Tσω2​−(anπ​)2.
(42)
Longitudinal propagation requires real nonzero k, hence
ω>ωc,n​=anπ​σT​​.​
(43)
Below the cutoff frequency of a membrane waveguide, k=iκ, with κ=(nπ/a)2−σω2/T​, and a bounded excitation decays as e−κx rather than carrying a travelling wave along an infinite guide. At equality k=0, the displacement has no longitudinal phase advance and the group velocity is zero; it is a cutoff oscillation, not a propagating mode carrying finite longitudinal power.
