Write CR​=4R/(1−R)2, keeping this coefficient distinct from the finesse F. The transmission has maxima at δ=2πm. At a displacement ϵ from a maximum, its ratio to peak transmission is [1+CR​sin2(ϵ/2)]−1. Half maximum therefore requires
sin2(δ1/2​/2)=4R(1−R)2​,δ1/2​=2arcsin(2R​1−R​).​
(14)
This is the half-width in wave phase; the full width at half maximum is 2δ1/2​. Successive resonances are 2π apart, so the exact ideal-model result is
F=2arcsin[(1−R)/(2R​)]π​.​
(15)
For narrow peaks, (1−R)/(2R​)≪1, the small-angle approximation to the inverse sine gives
δ1/2​≃R​1−R​,F≃1−RπR​​.​
(16)
Thus the formula requested in the paper is the high-reflectivity approximation, not an exact identity at arbitrary R. Ordinary half-maximum peaks exist only when (1−R)/(2R​)≤1, or R≥3−22​; below that threshold the transmission minimum never reaches half the peak. The prefactor T2/(1−R)2 cancels from this relative-width calculation.
