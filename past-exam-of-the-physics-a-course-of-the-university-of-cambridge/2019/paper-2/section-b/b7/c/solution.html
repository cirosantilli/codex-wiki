All curves start at the origin with the same positive slope v0​. Underdamping gives a decaying sequence of positive and negative lobes. Critical damping gives one positive lobe followed by return to zero. Overdamping also remains positive for these initial data, but has a longer tail. The initial impulse changes velocity abruptly, not displacement.
