Compare a directly transmitted ray emerging at A with a ray that makes one extra round trip inside the Fabry–Pérot interferometer and emerges at C. Both emerge parallel at angle θ to the plate normal. The extra internal distance is 2d/cosθ, and their emergence points differ laterally by AC=2dtanθ. The first ray has already travelled ACsinθ towards a common outgoing wavefront when the second reaches C. The air-gap optical path difference in a Fabry–Pérot interferometer follows because optical path length equals geometric path length in the air-gap approximation:
Δ=cosθ2d​−2dtanθsinθ=cosθ2d(1−sin2θ)​=2dcosθ​.
(3)
The subtraction is essential: comparing distances only inside the plates would compare different outgoing wavefronts. The corresponding propagation phase difference is 2πΔ/λ. Coating reflection phases can add a separate phase offset; they do not change this geometric optical path difference.
