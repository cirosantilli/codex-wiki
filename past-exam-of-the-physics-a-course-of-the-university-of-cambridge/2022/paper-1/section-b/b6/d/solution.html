Set B^=a^x†​a^y​−a^y†​a^x​. Taking the Hermitian adjoint reverses the order of products, so B^†=−B^. Complex conjugation also reverses the sign of −i, and therefore
Q^​†=iℏB^†=−iℏB^=Q^​.
(23)
Thus Q^​ is a self-adjoint operator on the usual oscillator states.
Using the coefficients in part (a), products containing two position operators or two momentum operators cancel in the difference. Cross-coordinate operators commute, leaving
B^=2iαβ(x^p^​y​−y^​p^​x​)=ℏi​(x^p^​y​−y^​p^​x​).
(24)
Hence
Q^​=x^p^​y​−y^​p^​x​=L^z​.​
(25)
It is the component of orbital angular momentum perpendicular to the plane, with units kgm2s−1 (equivalently Js). The rotational symmetry of the two-dimensional isotropic harmonic oscillator makes this a conserved observable; directly, each term in B^ preserves Nx​+Ny​, so [H^,Q^​]=0.
