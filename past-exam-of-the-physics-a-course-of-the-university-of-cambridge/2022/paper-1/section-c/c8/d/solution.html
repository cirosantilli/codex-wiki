Thus the model with a fitted intercept provides the better description, rather than merely lowering the residual by adding a parameter.

Hence d=(13.11±2.49)mrad, about 5.26 standard deviations above zero. A positive constant systematic error in the angular zero, or an additional nearly constant torque, could account for it. Its presence is evidence against the ideal zero-intercept model; four points alone do not identify the physical cause uniquely. A calibration error in κ only rescales the inferred gravitational constant and cannot remove this residual pattern.
