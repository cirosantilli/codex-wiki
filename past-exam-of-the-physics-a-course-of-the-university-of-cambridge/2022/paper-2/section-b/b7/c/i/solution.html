The quotient is interpreted by its limit where both numerator and denominator vanish. At small angles on a screen at R=1m, sinθ≃X/R and β=πX/(10mm). Principal interference fringes occur at
Xm​≃mdλR​=m(10.0mm),I(Xm​)=9I1​.​

The pattern is symmetric about X=0. The positions quoted in millimetres use the paraxial approximation; exactly dsinθm​=mλ and Xm​=Rtanθm​.
