More accurate Fresnel integrals, C(1)=0.7798934 and S(1)=0.4382591, give IP​/I0​=2.562. The central light intensity is increased to about 2.6 times the unobscured value, an increase of roughly 160%.
There is no violation of conservation of energy: the aperture reduces the total transmitted power, while removing contributions that partly cancel the central field redistributes the transmitted power across the observation plane. In particular, the square aperture does not give the same central field as a circular aperture of a superficially similar size.
