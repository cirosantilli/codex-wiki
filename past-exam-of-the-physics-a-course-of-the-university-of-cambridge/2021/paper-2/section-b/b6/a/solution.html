Use transverse displacement y(x,t), small slopes and constant tension T. For a short string element of length dx, Newton's second law gives ρL​dxytt​=T[yx​(x+dx)−yx​(x)]. Hence the wave equation on a string is
ytt​=cL2​yxx​,cL​=ρL​T​​,
(12)
where ρL​ is the linear mass density, in kgm−1.
The mechanical impedance of a rightgoing travelling wave is the transverse driving force divided by the transverse velocity at the driven section. Choose the force exerted by the material to the left on the material to the right, Fy​=−Tyx​. For y=f(x−cL​t), the transverse velocity is vy​=yt​=−cL​yx​. Thus the positive characteristic impedance of a stretched string is
ZL​=vy​Fy​​=cL​T​=ρL​cL​=TρL​​,[ZL​]=Nsm−1=kgs−1.​
(13)
A leftgoing component has Fy​/vy​=−ZL​ with this fixed orientation of force. Specifying the orientation avoids confusing that propagation sign with a negative material mechanical impedance.
