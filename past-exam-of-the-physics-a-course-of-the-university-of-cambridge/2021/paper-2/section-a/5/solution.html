Let N=L/a be the integer number of primitive unit cells. A Bloch state can be written as ψk​(x)=eikxuk​(x), with uk​(x+a)=uk​(x). The periodic boundary condition ψk​(x+L)=ψk​(x) therefore requires
eikL=1,kj​=L2πj​,j∈Z.
(10)
The spacing of allowed wavevectors is Δk=2π/L. The first Brillouin zone has width 2π/a, so
Nk​=2π/L2π/a​=aL​=N.​
(11)
For an exact endpoint convention take a half-open Brillouin zone, such as [−π/a,π/a). Its two endpoints differ by a reciprocal lattice vector and must not both be counted as distinct values of crystal momentum. There are exactly N inequivalent integers j modulo N, irrespective of whether N is even or odd. This is state counting in a Bloch band: one allowed wavevector per primitive unit cell per energy band. A separate twofold spin multiplicity gives 2N electronic states per energy band, without doubling the number of wavevectors.
