For the isotropic three-dimensional quantum harmonic oscillator, V(r)=mω2r2/2, hence r−1dV/dr=mω2 is constant. No radial integral remains, so the spin-orbit energy shift in a central potential reduces to
ΔEl,j​=4mc2ℏ2ω2​[j(j+1)−l(l+1)−3/4].
(18)
For l=0, the only possibility is j=1/2, and the bracket vanishes. For l=1, coupling to spin one-half permits j=1/2 and j=3/2, whose brackets are −2 and +1. Thus
ΔE0,1/2​ΔE1,1/2​ΔE1,3/2​​=0,=−2mc2ℏ2ω2​,=+4mc2ℏ2ω2​.​​
(19)
These spin-orbit shifts of an isotropic harmonic oscillator are a zero line, a downward parabola, and an upward parabola of half the downward magnitude. They are independent of the radial excitation number, and their magnetic multiplicities are respectively two, two and four. Their weighted mean within the l=1 manifold is zero: 2ΔE1,1/2​+4ΔE1,3/2​=0.
