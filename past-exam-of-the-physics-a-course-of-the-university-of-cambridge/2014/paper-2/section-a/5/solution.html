Translating a scattering centre by any lattice translation R changes the scattered amplitude by eiG⋅R, up to the immaterial overall sign convention. constructive interference requires G⋅R=2π times an integer. Applying this to ax^,ay^​,az^ yields
G=a2π​(h,k,l),h,k,l∈Z​.
(3)
Thus G lies in the reciprocal lattice. elastic scattering additionally requires ∣ki​+G∣=∣ki​∣=2π/λ; not every reciprocal vector is accessible for a fixed incident beam.
