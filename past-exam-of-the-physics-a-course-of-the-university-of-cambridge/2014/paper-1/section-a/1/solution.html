Write the one-electron Coulomb energies as En​=−C/n2, C>0. The Pauli exclusion principle allows 2n2 electrons in shell n, including spin. The ground configuration therefore has two electrons in n=1 and seven in n=2, giving Eg​=−C(2+7/4)=−15C/4. The least energy-increasing promotion is from n=2 to n=3:
Ee​=−C(2+46​+91​)=−1865C​,Eg​Ee​​=2726​.​
(1)
Rearranging the partly occupied degenerate n=2 shell does not produce a higher energy. Both total energies are negative, so this ratio is below one even though Ee​>Eg​.
