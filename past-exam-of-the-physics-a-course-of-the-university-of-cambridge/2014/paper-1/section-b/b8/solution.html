The angular momentum commutation relations are [Lx​,Ly​]=iℏLz​ and cyclic permutations. The product rule yields [Lz​,Lx2​]=iℏ(Ly​Lx​+Lx​Ly​) and [Lz​,Ly2​]=−iℏ(Lx​Ly​+Ly​Lx​), so [Lz​,L2]=0. Simultaneous eigenstates have eigenvalues ℏ2l(l+1) and ℏm; orbital angular momentum has integer l≥0 and m=−l,…,l.
By cyclic symmetry, L2 commutes with every component and hence every squared component. Therefore [H,L2]=0: an energy eigenbasis can be chosen with definite l. For unequal transverse coefficients, Lz​ need not commute with H, so definite m is not generally available.
Expanding the ladder operators gives L+2​+L−2​=2(Lx2​−Ly2​) and L2−Lz2​=Lx2​+Ly2​. Comparing coefficients gives

ℏ2(β+γ2α​2αβ+γ​).

Its symmetric and antisymmetric combinations have energies ℏ2(ax​+az​) and ℏ2(ay​+az​), respectively. Consequently
{El=1​}=ℏ2{ax​+ay​, ay​+az​, az​+ax​}.​

The energies are related by permutation of the principal axes, not generally equal. Two coincide when the corresponding coefficients coincide; all three become 2aℏ2 in the isotropic limit.
