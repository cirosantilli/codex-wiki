Small sound disturbances in a gas couple density, pressure and longitudinal velocity. Linearizing mass conservation and the pressure-gradient force about a uniform state gives
∂t​ρ′+ρ0​∇⋅u=0,ρ0​∂t​u=−∇p′,p′=cs2​ρ′.
(35)
Eliminating velocity yields ∂t2​ρ′=cs2​∇2ρ′. When local heat exchange is negligible over an oscillation, cs2​=(∂p/∂ρ)s​. For an ideal gas,
cs​=γp0​/ρ0​​=γkB​T/mmolecule​​.​
(36)
The adiabatic index is γ=Cp​/CV​; an isothermal disturbance instead has cT2​=p0​/ρ0​. Pressure and velocity in a progressive plane wave are in phase and obey p′=ρ0​cs​u, with average energy flux ⟨p′u⟩=ppeak′2​/(2ρ0​cs​). Particle oscillation speed is not the sound propagation speed. Viscosity, heat conduction and molecular relaxation dissipate sound; changes in active internal degrees of freedom can cause dispersion. The continuum acoustic model requires wavelength large compared with the molecular mean free path and small fractional perturbations.
