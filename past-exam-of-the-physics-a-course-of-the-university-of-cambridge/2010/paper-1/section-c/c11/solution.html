The donor-state overlap estimate invalidates an independent isolated-level ionization model at this concentration. Shallow binding supports abundant mobile carriers, but the full ionization versus isolated donor occupation distinction means full ionization cannot be justified solely by comparing binding with temperature while simultaneously retaining isolated levels and a degenerate chemical potential.
