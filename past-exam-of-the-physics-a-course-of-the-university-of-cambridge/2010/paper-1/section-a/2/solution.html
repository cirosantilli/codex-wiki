For the adjacent reflected minima of an air-film-air layer in thin-film interference, reflection at the front surface acquires a phase reversal, while reflection at the rear does not. Near normal incidence the reflected minima therefore obey 2nt=mλ for integral m. A single intermediate maximum means that the two stated minima are consecutive orders:
500nm2nt​−600nm2nt​=1.
(2)
Thus 2nt=3000nm, with orders6 and5, and
t=2(1.3)3000​nm=1.15μm.​
(3)
The intermediate reflected maximum is at λ=3000/5.5≃545nm. This assumes negligible dispersion of the supplied refractive index and an air environment on both sides. [ref]
