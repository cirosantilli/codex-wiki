In Fraunhofer diffraction, the phase difference from aperture coordinate y to observation angle theta is qy, with q=2πsinθ/λ. Coherent superposition of the transmitted field elements therefore gives
ψ(θ)∝∫T(y)eiqydy.​
(18)
The common propagation factor is omitted. This is the Fourier transform of the complex amplitude transmission, not of its intensity.
For a uniform slit of total width W=0.2mm, integration gives ψ0​=Wsinc(qW/2), where sincs=sins/s. Its normalized intensity is sinc2(πWsinθ/λ), with a central maximum and first zeros at sinθ=±λ/W=±0.0025.
For a half-covered slit choose T=+1 on the left half and T=−1 on the right. Put h=W/2 and s=qh/2. The two equal rectangles have centres separated by h, so
ψπ​=hsincs(e−is−eis)=−2ihsincssins,I0,peak​Iπ​​=sinc2ssin2s=s2sin4s​.​
(19)
This half-slit phase-step diffraction factorization is the phase-reversed two-slit interference factor multiplied by the diffraction envelope of each half-slit. Here the half-slit width and centre separation both equal the earlier0.1mm separation. The central intensity is zero; the largest off-axis pair satisfies tans=2s, giving ∣sinθ∣≃0.00186. Other zeros are at s=mπ. The plot includes the requested uniform-slit and half-covered patterns.
