Low-weight coefficients of a binary Hamming code (A3​=6n(n−1)​,A4​=24n(n−1)(n−3)​,A5​=120n(n−1)(n−3)(n−7)​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 94
For n=2ℓ−1, a codeword in a Hamming code is a subset of the nonzero vectors of F2ℓ​ whose sum is zero. Ordered weight-three supports of a vector are determined by two distinct nonzero vectors, giving n(n−1) choices. For weight four, the third vector must avoid the three nonzero vectors in the linear span of the first two, giving n(n−1)(n−3) choices. For weight five, the first three must be linearly independent, and the fourth must avoid all seven nonzero vectors in their linear span, giving n(n−1)(n−3)(n−7) choices. The final vector is their sum. Dividing by 3!, 4!, and 5! counts unordered supports of a vector and proves the displayed weight enumerator coefficients.
