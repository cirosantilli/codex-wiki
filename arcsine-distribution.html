Arcsine distribution (Beta(21​,21​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Continuous probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 989 Articles: 4
The standard arcsine distribution is a continuous probability distribution on [0,1] with probability density function
f(x)=πx(1−x)​1​,0<x<1.
(4)
It is the beta distribution with both parameters equal to 1/2. The density grows without bound toward both endpoints, yet neither endpoint has any probability mass. Its name comes from the inverse sine in its cumulative distribution function.[ref]
A useful way to see the shape is to choose a point uniformly around a circle and project it onto a diameter. Equal angular intervals do not give equal horizontal distances: the projection moves slowly near either end. An ideal harmonic oscillator sampled at a uniformly chosen time in one complete period has the same endpoint concentration after rescaling its position. The distribution translates a simple geometric slowdown into a precise probability calculation.
For an original comparison, take equal-length regions: the middle half [1/4,3/4], and the two outside quarters together. Then
P(1/4≤X≤3/4)=31​,P(X<1/4 or X>3/4)=32​.​
(5)
A uniform distribution on [0,1] instead assigns each region probability 1/2. Both distributions have mean 1/2; the mean alone hides their different concentration.
