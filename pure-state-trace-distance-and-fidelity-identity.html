Pure-state trace distance and fidelity identity (D=1−F2​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Fidelity of quantum states
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
For pure states ρ=∣ψ⟩⟨ψ∣ and σ=∣φ⟩⟨φ∣, the unsquared quantum fidelity is F=∣⟨ψ∣φ⟩∣. In their two-dimensional span, ρ−σ has trace zero and determinant −(1−F2), hence eigenvalues ±1−F2​. Its trace norm is twice the positive eigenvalue, giving trace distance D=1−F2​.
