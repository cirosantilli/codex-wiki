Theta temperature (Θ)

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry) Polymer solution
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
The theta temperature is the temperature at which the leading effective two-segment interaction of a dilute long polymer vanishes, so its large-scale size is approximately that of an ideal chain. In the simplest Flory–Huggins solution theory this is the condition χ=1/2. It is the infinite-chain limit of the upper critical temperature in the usual energetic model, not the critical temperature of every finite chain.
