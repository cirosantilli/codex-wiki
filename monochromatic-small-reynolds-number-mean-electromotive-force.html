Monochromatic small-Reynolds-number mean electromotive force (E=Rm​E(1)+Rm2​E(2)+⋯)

 ... Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Dynamo action Mean-field dynamo Mean-field electromotive force
OurBigBook.com Words: 182 Articles: 2
Suppose a steady solenoidal velocity satisfies Δu=−u, and spatial averaging permits integration by parts without boundary terms. For a uniform test magnetic field B, the small-magnetic Reynolds number expansion gives b1​=(B⋅∇)u and Δb2​=−∇×(u×b1​). Using the self-adjointness of the Laplacian inside the average,
E(1)=⟨u×(B⋅∇)u⟩,E(2)=⟨u×∇×[u×(B⋅∇)u]⟩.
(12)
The second equality concerns the averaged mean-field electromotive force; it does not assert that the quadratic forcing itself is monochromatic.
