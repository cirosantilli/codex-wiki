Surjective isometry of normed vector spaces

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Normed vector space
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 3
A surjective isometry u:V→W between normed vector spaces preserves every distance:
∥u(v)−u(w)∥=∥v−w∥.
