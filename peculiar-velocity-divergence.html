Peculiar-velocity divergence (θ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Scale factor (cosmology) Comoving coordinate Peculiar velocity
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
The peculiar-velocity divergence is θ=∇⋅v. For irrotational linear perturbations it captures the scalar part of the peculiar velocity field.
