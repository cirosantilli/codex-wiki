Pulsar dedispersion (tcorrected​=t−Δt(f))

 ... Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar evolution Neutron star Pulsar Pulsar dispersion delay
OurBigBook.com Words: 143
Pulsar dedispersion aligns radio wave arrival times in separate frequency channels before combining them. For an original illustrative model choose
Δt(f)=D[(100MHzf​)−2−(100MHz150MHz​)−2],D=0.24s.
(30)
Both bracketed ratios are dimensionless, so D supplies all the time units. The reference is 150MHz: delays are 0 there, 0.13333s at 100MHz, and 0.26833s at 80MHz. These are chosen model values, not measurements of the first pulsar. Subtracting the delay aligns the peaks; summing channels first spreads a short pulse over a longer interval and reduces its peak. Finite channel width and incorrect delay parameters leave residual smearing.
