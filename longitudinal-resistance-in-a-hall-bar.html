Longitudinal resistance in a Hall bar

 ... Lorentz force Hall effect Quantum Hall effect Integer quantum Hall effect von Klitzing-Dorda-Pepper quantum Hall experiment Hall bar
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
Voltage contacts separated along the current direction measure longitudinal resistance, even when the device is called a Hall bar. For an approximately uniform rectangular strip, Rxx​≃ρxx​L/W. Transverse contacts measure Rxy​ instead. Shubnikov-de Haas effect oscillations in Rxx​ need not be Hall-resistance plateaus; in the integer quantum Hall effect, longitudinal minima accompany quantized transverse plateaus.
