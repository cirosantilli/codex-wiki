The Hénon-Heiles system is a Hamiltonian system with two degrees of freedom and Hamiltonian function
H=21​(px2​+py2​)+V(x,y),V(x,y)=21​(x2+y2)+x2y−31​y3.

Its Hamilton's equations give x¨=−x−2xy and y¨​=−y−x2+y2. Its potential energy combines an isotropic quadratic minimum with a cubic interaction.
