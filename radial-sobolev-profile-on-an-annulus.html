Radial Sobolev profile on an annulus (∥uˉ∥W1,p(a,b)​≤C∥u∥W1,p({a<∣x∣<b})​)

Let 0<a<b<∞ and 1≤p<∞. A radial function in W1,p({a<∣x∣<b}) has a radial profile in W1,p(a,b); its first weak derivative is the profile of the radial derivative. To see the identity, sum the weak derivative tests against the components of ϕ(r)x/r to obtain ∫gϕrn−1=−∫uˉ(ϕrn−1)′. Substitute ϕ=ζ/rn−1 for arbitrary ζ∈Cc∞​(a,b). The weighted Lp norms and a>0 give the displayed bound. Radiality of the radial derivative follows by applying a rotation to the weak derivative identity: if u(Qx)=u(x), then ∇u(Qx)=Q∇u(x) almost everywhere, so the radial scalar product is invariant.
For p>1, the one-dimensional Sobolev representative obeys ∣uˉ(r)−uˉ(s)∣≤∣r−s∣1−1/p∥uˉ′∥p​. The reverse triangle inequality transfers this to u, giving a continuous embedding of this radial Sobolev space into C0,1−1/p on the annulus. The two Hölder seminorms are equal: one inequality follows from ​∣x∣−∣y∣​≤∣x−y∣, and the reverse follows by putting both points on the same ray. The restriction a>0 is essential for the unweighted profile bound.
