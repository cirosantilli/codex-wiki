Donor-wind Roche-lobe response (ζL​=(1−f)ζ0​+fζ1​)

 ... Stellar astrophysics Binary star Roche lobe Roche-lobe overflow Nonconservative binary mass transfer Donor-wind angular-momentum loss
OurBigBook.com Words: 239 Articles: 3
Under donor-wind angular-momentum loss and the Roche lobe approximation RL​=0.46a(Md​/M)1/3, the Roche-lobe radius response exponent is affine in retained fraction: ζL​=(1−f)ζ0​+fζ1​, where q=Md​/Ma​, ζ0​=1/3−4q/[3(1+q)] and ζ1​=2q−5/3. Differentiate logRL​=loga+(logMd​−logM)/3+constant and the circular-binary orbital angular momentum to obtain this expression.
