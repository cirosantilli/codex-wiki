Phase quadrature (Δϕ=π/2)

 Home Physics Wave Phase (waves)
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
Two sinusoidal signals are in phase quadrature when their phase difference is a quarter cycle, for example cosϕ and sinϕ. Their time averages have zero correlation over a cycle. In a plane wave, this separates fields such as displacement and its time derivative, velocity.
