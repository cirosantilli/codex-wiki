Internal-wave attractor

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave Reflection of an internal-wave ray Internal-wave ray tracing
OurBigBook.com Words: 93 Articles: 1
A periodic spatial internal-wave ray toward which neighbouring ray paths converge after reflection in a confined stratified basin. A fixed point of an internal-wave ray return map is attracting when its derivative has magnitude below one. Viscous attenuation of an internal-wave beam and internal-wave breaking regularize the ideal concentration of energy. A spatial ray construction does not remove turning-level limitations of the WKB approximation.
