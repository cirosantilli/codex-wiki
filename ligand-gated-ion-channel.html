Ligand-gated ion channel

 Home Biology Biological cell Cell membrane Ion channel
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A ligand-gated ion channel couples ligand occupancy to the probability of pore opening. The ligand-binding site and ion pathway can be distinct, and sustained occupancy can lead to a desensitized nonconducting state.
