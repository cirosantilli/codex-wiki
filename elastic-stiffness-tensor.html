Elastic stiffness tensor (σij​=cijpq​∂p​uq​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 2
An elastic stiffness tensor maps a small displacement gradient tensor to the stress tensor. Minor symmetries in each index pair ensure symmetric stress and dependence only on symmetric strain; major symmetry cijpq​=cpqij​ follows from an elastic energy and underlies reciprocity. Stability requires positive elastic energy on nonzero strains. In an isotropic material, cijpq​=λδij​δpq​+μ(δip​δjq​+δiq​δjp​), with Lamé parameters λ,μ.
