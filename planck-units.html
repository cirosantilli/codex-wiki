Planck units (ℏ=c=G=1)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Dimensional analysis Natural units
OurBigBook.com Words: 66 Articles: 1
Planck units use fundamental constants to set natural units of mass, length and time. The displayed convention sets the ordinary Planck mass to one. The related reduced Planck units convention instead normalizes the reduced Planck mass.
