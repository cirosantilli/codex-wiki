For unit Euclidean norm columns, cumulative coherence measures the largest sum of k correlations with one other column. Its domain is 0≤k≤N−1, with μ1​(0)=0 by the empty-sum convention. It is nondecreasing, μ1​(1)=μ for N≥2, and μ1​(k)≤kμ, where μ is the mutual coherence. Keeping a sum of actual correlations can be substantially sharper than replacing each summand by the largest one.
