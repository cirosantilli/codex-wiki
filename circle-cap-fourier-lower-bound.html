Circle cap Fourier lower bound (∣ψdσ​∣≳δ on a δ−2×δ−1 rectangle)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Fourier restriction theory Fourier extension operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
For normalized circle measure and a nonnegative cutoff near (1,0), equal to one on ∣θ∣≤δ/C and supported on ∣θ∣≤2δ/C, remove the constant phase e−iξ1​. On ∣ξ1​∣≤δ−2, ∣ξ2​∣≤δ−1, the remaining phase has magnitude at most 2/C2+2/C. Taking C sufficiently large keeps its real part positive and comparable to one, so the Fourier transform has magnitude at least a constant times the cap mass, which is at least δ/(πC). No upper bound on the cutoff is required for this lower bound.
