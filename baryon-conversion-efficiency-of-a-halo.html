Baryon conversion efficiency of a halo (f∗​=M∗​/(fb​Mh​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Cosmological structure formation Hierarchical galaxy formation
OurBigBook.com Words: 198 Articles: 3
The baryon conversion efficiency of a halo is the fraction of its baryonic mass incorporated into stars. If the halo retains the cosmic baryon fraction fb​, its stellar-to-total-mass ratio is fb​f∗​. This efficiency depends on halo mass, epoch, gas supply and feedback and must not be confused with a star-formation efficiency per free-fall time.
