Urysohn's lemma

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Normal space
OurBigBook.com Words: 124 Articles: 2
If A,B are disjoint closed subsets of a normal space, there is a continuous function f:X→[0,1] with f∣A​=0 and f∣B​=1.
