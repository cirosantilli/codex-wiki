Symbolic Model Checking without BDDs by Armin Biere and others (1999)

 Home Computer science Computer science bibliography
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
By Armin Biere, Alessandro Cimatti, Edmund Clarke and Yunshan Zhu. Published in Tools and Algorithms for the Construction and Analysis of Systems, volume 1579, pp. 193–207.
Author-hosted text: cca.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/papers/BiereCimattiClarkeZhu-TACAS99.pdf
Introduces bounded model checking by translating a search for a bounded counterexample into a Boolean satisfiability problem. Section 2 explains unrolling and loop closure through a worked example; §3 states infinite-path semantics; §4 gives the encoding; §5 discusses bounds.
