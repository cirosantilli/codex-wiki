Infrared limitations of one-loop QCD running (αs​(μ2)=1/[β0​ln(μ2/Λ2)],μ>Λ)

OurBigBook.com Words: 97
For positive β0​, the one-loop running coupling tends to zero at high energy and grows as energy decreases. Near the strong-coupling scale, omitted perturbative terms cease to be small; the apparent infrared pole is not a controlled prediction of a physical infinity. Continuing the algebraic formula below Λ gives a negative coupling and is not a valid physical continuation. Confinement and hadron formation are nonperturbative phenomena, not a theorem proved by this one-loop pole. The integration constant obeys Λ=μ0​exp[−1/(2β0​α0​)], so an order-one coefficient alone cannot determine an absolute mass scale; a reference coupling or empirical input is also necessary.
