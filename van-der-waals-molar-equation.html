Van der Waals molar equation (p=RT/(vm​−bm​)−am​/vm2​)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure Equation of state Van der Waals equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 538 Articles: 1
The molar volume vm​=V/nmol​ is the volume per amount of substance, not the total container volume or the volume per particle. In the molar convention of the Van der Waals equation, take constant positive parameters
[am​]=Pam6mol−2,[bm​]=m3mol−1,[R]=Pam3mol−1K−1.
(8)
Then
p=vm​−bm​RT​−vm2​am​​,vm​>bm​,
(9)
or, for any fixed amount of substance,
(p+V2am​nmol2​​)(V−nmol​bm​)=nmol​RT.
(10)
Both terms in the first equation are pressures. The total excluded volume is nmol​bm​: using V−bm​ would subtract quantities with different units. Changing the sample size at fixed molar volume and temperature changes V and nmol​ proportionally, while leaving pressure unchanged. This is a useful unit and extensivity check before fitting a real gas.
For the molar Van der Waals critical point, differentiate with respect to molar volume at fixed temperature:
(∂vm​∂p​)T​=−(vm​−bm​)2RT​+vm3​2am​​,(∂vm2​∂2p​)T​=(vm​−bm​)32RT​−vm4​6am​​.
(11)
Setting both to zero gives RTc​=2am​(vm,c​−bm​)2/vm,c3​ and RTc​=3am​(vm,c​−bm​)3/vm,c4​. Equating them and dividing by the nonzero factors yields 2vm,c​=3(vm,c​−bm​), so
vm,c​=3bm​,Tc​=27Rbm​8am​​,pc​=27bm2​am​​.
(12)
Here vm,c​ has units m3mol−1, Tc​ has units K, and pc​ has units Pa. This is the molar version of the particle derivation below: vm,c​=NA​vc​, with the same physical Tc​ and pc​. If one writes vc​=3b, Tc​=8a/(27Rb) and pc​=a/(27b2) without subscripts, then all of vc​,b,a in those formulas must be molar quantities.
Now define the dimensionless reduced variables explicitly as x=vm​/vm,c​, t=T/Tc​ and q=p/pc​. Substituting the three critical values cancels am​,bm​,R and gives
q=3x−18t​−x23​.
(13)
Because vm​/vm,c​=v/vc​, these are exactly the reduced variables in the Van der Waals isotherm discussion. The Van der Waals law of corresponding states is therefore independent of whether one starts with particle or molar units. It predicts a critical ratio pc​vm,c​/(RTc​)=3/8, providing a third check on any proposed parameter fit.
