Representation theory of the symmetric group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 64
The representation theory of Sn​ is organized by a partition of an integer. Over the complex numbers, partitions label the irreducible Specht modules; over positive-characteristic fields, the simple modules are labelled by regular partitions.
