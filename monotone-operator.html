Monotone operator

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 905 Articles: 21
An operator F on an inner product space is monotone when
⟨F(v)−F(w),v−w⟩≥0
(83)
for every v,w. The gradient of every differentiable convex function is monotone.
