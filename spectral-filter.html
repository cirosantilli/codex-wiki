Spectral filter

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Regularization of an inverse problem Tikhonov regularization

A spectral filter replaces unstable inversion of a singular value σ by a bounded scalar factor gα​(σ). For Tikhonov regularization, gα​(σ)=σ/(σ2+α).
