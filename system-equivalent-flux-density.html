System-equivalent flux density (SEFD=2kB​Tsys​/Ae​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Observational astronomy Radio astronomy System temperature
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The system-equivalent flux density is the unpolarized spectral flux density that would produce an antenna temperature equal to Tsys​. With effective collecting area Ae​, SEFD=2kB​Tsys​/Ae​. The factor two follows from one polarization of waves measurement of an unpolarized source. State effective area and polarization of waves conventions when comparing sensitivities.[ref]
