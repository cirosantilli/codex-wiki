Harmonic Hardy space of the upper half-space (h1(Hd+1))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Harmonic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 1
For the upper half-space Hd+1=Rd×(0,∞), this is the space of harmonic functions with finite norm ∥u∥h1​=supt>0​∫Rd​∣u(x,t)∣dx. It differs from an analytic Hardy space: no holomorphic function condition is imposed, and its boundary object can be a singular finite Borel measure.
