Restricted likelihood from orthogonal error contrasts (ℓR​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 38
For Y∼N(Xβ,Vθ​) and a full-column-rank design matrix X∈Rn×p, take A∈Rn×(n−p) with ATA=I and ATX=0. Then ATY∼N(0,ATVθ​A), so restricted maximum likelihood maximizes
ℓR​(θ)=−2n−p​log(2π)−21​logdet(ATVθ​A)−21​YTA(ATVθ​A)−1ATY.
(40)
Changing the orthonormal basis by an orthogonal matrix leaves this expression unchanged.
