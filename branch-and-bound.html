Branch and bound

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Integer programming
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 2
A branch-and-bound search partitions a feasible set, maintains a feasible incumbent, and prunes subproblems using bounds that cannot improve that incumbent. For a quadratic knapsack problem, fixing a selected set S adds its exact quadratic value as a constant and replaces remaining profits by vi​+2∑j∈S​pij​ under the ordered-pair convention. Residual fractional row bounds for quadratic knapsack and a Lagrangian knapsack bound can then be recomputed. Worst-case search remains exponential.
