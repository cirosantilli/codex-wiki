Consecutive integers divisible by prescribed prime powers (qiai​​∣n+i(1≤i≤r))

 Home Mathematics Chinese remainder theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
Let q1​,…,qr​ be distinct prime numbers and a1​,…,ar​ positive integers. There are infinitely many positive integers n with qiai​​∣n+i for every i. Indeed the moduli qiai​​ are pairwise coprime, so the Chinese remainder theorem solves n≡−i(modqiai​​) simultaneously. Adding multiples of their product gives arbitrarily large positive solutions. This allows long runs in which every integer has a large prescribed prime power divisor, even though neighboring integers are coprime.
