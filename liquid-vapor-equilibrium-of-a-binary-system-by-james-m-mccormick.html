Adapted by James M. McCormick, credited as J. M. McCormick, from an exercise used at the University of Kansas. This credit identifies the adaptation author of this standalone handout. Issued by Truman State University ChemLab and hosted by Truman State University. Last Update: 19 December 2012; this is a revision date.
Online text: chemlab.truman.edu/physical-chemistry/physical-chemistry-laboratory/liquid-vapor-equilibrium-of-a-binary-system/
Cited passages: Introduction, acetone–chloroform system; Procedure, Figure 1 caption and apparatus prose, Table 1, thermometer calibration, corrected pressure, stable-temperature reflux and paired samples; Abbé refractometer readout, pure-solvent and condensate agreement control and evaporation limitation; Table 2 caption and composition conversion; repeated additions paragraph and Results and Analysis. The apparatus caption and prose were inspected, but the apparatus photograph pixels were not. The handout specifies a procedure rather than a measured student dataset; its changing-temperature series is not a direct test of a fixed-temperature activity-coefficient relation.
