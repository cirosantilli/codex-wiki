2001 attosecond pulse-train experiment

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Laser Attosecond physics Reconstruction of attosecond beating by interference of two-photon transitions
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
Pierre Agostini's experiment illuminated an argon jet with an annular 800nm beam. A pinhole removed the annulus; a tungsten-coated mirror relayed harmonics and weak central reference into another argon jet. A magnetic-bottle electron spectrometer used time of flight and microchannel plate detectors. Glass plates varied delay. Reference-on sidebands contrasted with reference-off peaks. Atomic-phase-corrected reconstruction yielded 250-attosecond bursts.[ref]
The two-path attosecond phase reconstruction explains the control's purpose: adding the reference opens an interference channel. Its fitted oscillation tests phase relationships under the weak-field and atomic-response model. It does not independently reveal every target phase or an arbitrary nonperiodic optical pulse.
