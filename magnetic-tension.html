Magnetic tension

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
Magnetic tension is the restoring stress associated with bending magnetic field lines. In a divergence-free field, the Lorentz-force density splits into a magnetic-pressure gradient and the tension term (B⋅∇)B/μ0​ in SI units.
