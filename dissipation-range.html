Dissipation range

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Energy cascade
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 2
The dissipation range contains scales at which kinematic viscosity appreciably affects velocity gradients and converts kinetic energy into heat. In a high-Reynolds-number three-dimensional cascade its characteristic length and time are the Kolmogorov microscales. Intense vortex cores can be intermittent within this range.
