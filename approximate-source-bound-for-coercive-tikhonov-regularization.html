Let K be injective, u†=K†f, z=β−2B∗Bu†, and ηr​=inf∥w∥≤r​∥z−K∗w∥. Since R(K∗)​=N(K)⊥=U, ηr​→0. For e=Rα​f−u† the normal equation for coercive Tikhonov regularization gives ∥Ke∥2+α∥Be∥2=−αβ2⟨z,e⟩. Insert an approximate K∗w and complete the square in ∥Ke∥ to obtain ∥e∥2≤∥z−K∗w∥∥e∥+αβ2r2/4. This implies the displayed bound, even with coefficient 1/2 on its last term, and proves consistency without a fixed exact source condition for quadratic regularization.
