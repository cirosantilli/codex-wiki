Maximum clique (∣C∣=ω(G))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Complete graph Clique (graph theory)
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A maximum clique is a clique with the greatest possible number of vertices in the graph. Its size is the clique number. Every maximum clique is a maximal clique, since adding a vertex would produce a larger clique. A graph can have several maximum cliques; the word maximum specifies their common size, rather than uniqueness.
