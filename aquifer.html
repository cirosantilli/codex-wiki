Aquifer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 571 Articles: 12
An aquifer is a permeable geological formation containing and transmitting groundwater. A confined aquifer lies beneath a confining layer; an unconfined aquifer instead has a free water table.
