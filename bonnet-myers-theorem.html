Bonnet-Myers theorem

 ... Differential geometry Gauss map Second fundamental form Gauss equation Sectional curvature Ricci curvature
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
If a complete connected n-dimensional Riemannian manifold satisfies Ric≥(n−1)kg for some k>0, then its diameter is at most π/k​. It is therefore compact and has finite fundamental group.
