Good-solvent force-extension scaling (R∼Na(fa/kB​T)2/3)

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymer physics Tensile blob
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For a long flexible polymer in a good solvent, a tensile blob of size ξt​∼kB​T/f contains g∼(ξt​/a)1/ν statistical segments. Aligning N/g blobs gives R∼(N/g)ξt​=Na(fa/kB​T)1/ν−1. The three-dimensional Flory approximation ν=3/5 gives the displayed power. The regime requires a≪ξt​≪aNν: below its force range the coil has ordinary linear response, and above it finite link extension matters. Numerical amplitudes depend on solvent quality and segment conventions.[ref]
