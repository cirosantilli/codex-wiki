Structure factor (F(Q)=∑j​fj​(Q)eiQ⋅rj​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering Scattering amplitude Crystal scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
The coherent scattering amplitude associated with a specified spatial arrangement, obtained by summing the site amplitudes with their relative wave phases. Distinguish the crystal lattice structure factor, which sums over lattice sites, from the crystal basis structure factor, which sums the chemical atoms inside a unit cell. In a periodic crystal the two factors multiply. A basis factor can vanish even at an allowed reciprocal lattice vector, causing an extinct reflection.
