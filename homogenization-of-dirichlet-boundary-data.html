Homogenization of Dirichlet boundary data

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space Affine Sobolev space
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
To solve a linear Dirichlet problem with boundary value g, choose a sufficiently regular extension G whose trace is g and write v=w+G. Then w has zero trace, while moving the known term LG to the right-hand side turns the equation Lv=f into the homogeneous-boundary problem Lw=f−LG.
