Perturbative gauge-coupling independence of the Wilsonian superpotential (∂τ​Wpert​=0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 63
The holomorphic coupling is τ=ϑ/(2π)+4πi/g2. Perturbative coefficients are independent of the topological angle ϑ. A holomorphic function invariant under continuous shifts of Reτ is constant by the Cauchy-Riemann equations, hence a perturbative local superpotential coefficient cannot depend on τ. Terms in e2πiτ can evade this argument nonperturbatively. This does not forbid renormalization of the separate gauge kinetic F-term, or wave-function renormalization from the Kähler potential.
