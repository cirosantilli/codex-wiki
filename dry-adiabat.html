Dry adiabat (T/T0​=(p/p0​)Ra​/cp​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics First law of thermodynamics Adiabatic process
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
For an ideal gas of constant specific heat, an isentrope without phase changes satisfies T=T0​(p/p0​)Ra​/cp​. A dilute saturated atmosphere approximates this path only when both vapour sensible heat and latent-heating corrections are small.
