Power rule (d(xp)/dx=pxp−1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For a real exponent p and x>0, write xp=exp(plnx). The chain rule and the derivative of the natural logarithm give d(xp)/dx=pxp−1. For a nonnegative integer exponent this agrees with polynomial differentiation and extends to all real x. Reversing differentiation gives ∫xpdx=xp+1/(p+1)+C for p=−1 on a suitable real branch. The exceptional exponent p=−1 gives ln∣x∣+C on either half-line.
