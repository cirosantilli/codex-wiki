Line element on a sphere (ds2=R2(dθ2+sin2θdϕ2))

In spherical coordinates on a sphere of radius R, the position is X=R(sinθcosϕ,sinθsinϕ,cosθ). Its coordinate tangent vectors satisfy Xθ​⋅Xϕ​=0, ∣Xθ​∣2=R2, and ∣Xϕ​∣2=R2sin2θ. Expanding ∣dX∣2 proves the displayed line element, the first fundamental form of the sphere. Along a parametrized path it gives the arc length by integrating Rθ˙2+sin2θϕ˙​2​. The vanishing azimuthal coefficient at a pole is a coordinate singularity, not a singularity of the sphere.
