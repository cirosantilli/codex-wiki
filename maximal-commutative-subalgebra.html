Maximal commutative subalgebra

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A commutative unital subalgebra D of an associative algebra A is maximal commutative if no larger commutative subalgebra contains it. Equivalently CA​(D)=D: any element of its centralizer of a subalgebra generates a commutative algebra together with D. The full diagonal matrix algebra inside Mn​(C) is an example.
