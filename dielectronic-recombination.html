Dielectronic recombination

 Home Physics Atomic physics Ionization
OurBigBook.com Words: 229 Articles: 3
A free Electron is resonantly captured while exciting the ionic core, producing a doubly excited state above the parent-ion threshold. Capture becomes permanent recombination only if the state undergoes radiative stabilization rather than autoionization. For resonance d, entrance ion i, and Maxwellian electrons, αd​=(gd​/(2gi​))(h2/(2πmkT))3/2e−Ed​/kTAdia​Astabr​/(Atota​+Atotr​). Detailed balance determines the capture rate; a steady population balance determines the survival factor. The rate coefficient has dimensions volume per time.
