Mod-two intersection pairing of the Klein bottle (Q=(11​10​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological surface Klein bottle
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A one-sided section loop and a two-sided fiber loop in the reflection mapping torus have mod-two self-intersections one and zero and meet once. Their intersection form is Q. In the evaluation-dual cohomology basis, the Poincare duality pairing has matrix Q−1, giving t2=0 and x2=tx=0.
