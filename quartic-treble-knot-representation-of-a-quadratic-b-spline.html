Quartic treble-knot representation of a quadratic B-spline

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation Spline (mathematics) Spline approximation B-spline
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
For a uniform quadratic B-spline with interior control points Pi​, label its ith span [i,i+1]. A quartic B-spline with interior spline knot sequence τ3i​=τ3i+1​=τ3i+2​=i represents the identical curve when
Q3i−1​=4Pi−1​+3Pi​​,Q3i​=12Pi−1​+10Pi​+Pi+1​​,Q3i+1​=43Pi​+Pi+1​​.
(13)
To verify this, raise each quadratic Bézier curve span twice by degree elevation of Bernstein coefficients. The three interior quartic controls are the displayed points. One additional knot insertion at each interior knot supplies the shared endpoint as the mean of its two neighboring controls, recovering all five quartic Bézier curve controls. Thus the knot multiplicity is three, giving C1 joins rather than imposing unwanted extra smoothness.
