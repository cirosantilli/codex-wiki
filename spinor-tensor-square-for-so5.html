Spinor tensor square for so5 (4⊗4=1⊕5⊕10)

OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For the B2 root system with long simple root first, the four-dimensional spinor representation has highest weight (0,1). Its symmetric square has highest weight (0,2) and dimension ten, so it is the Adjoint representation. The exterior square has the four nonzero defining weights and two zero weights; it splits into the five-dimensional vector representation of highest weight (1,0) and a trivial representation. Thus the tensor square decomposes as displayed. The invariant alternating bilinear form reflects the pseudoreal representation structure. The spinor representation is a group representation of the Spin group Spin(5), while its tensor square descends to SO(5).
