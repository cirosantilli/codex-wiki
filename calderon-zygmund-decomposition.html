Calderón–Zygmund decomposition (f=g+∑Q​bQ​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Harmonic analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
For f∈L1(Rd) and a positive threshold λ, take maximal dyadic cubes with mean of ∣f∣ exceeding λ. They are disjoint, have total volume at most ∥f∥1​/λ, and their means are at most 2dλ. Replace f inside each selected cube by its mean to obtain g; write bQ​=(f−fQ​)1Q​. Then ∥g∥∞​≤2dλ, ∥g∥1​≤∥f∥1​, ∫bQ​=0, and ∑Q​∥bQ​∥1​≤2∥f∥1​. The outside bound follows from the Lebesgue differentiation theorem. This separates a bounded part from localized cancellation errors.
