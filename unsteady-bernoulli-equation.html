Unsteady Bernoulli equation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Euler equations for an inviscid fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 246 Articles: 3
For time-dependent irrotational flow with u=∇ϕ, constant mass density, and no body force, the Euler equations for an inviscid fluid integrate to
∂t​ϕ+21​∣∇ϕ∣2+ρp​=C(t).
(24)
A time-dependent change of gauge in ϕ may set C(t)=0.
This is the unsteady potential-flow version of Bernoulli's principle, with a time-dependent potential gauge.
