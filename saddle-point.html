Saddle point

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 399 Articles: 6
A saddle point of a differentiable real-valued function is a critical point which is neither a local maximum nor a local minimum. For example f(x,y)=x2−y2 has a saddle at the origin. For a nonconstant holomorphic function, critical points of its real part supply the complex-phase application used by the method of steepest descent.
