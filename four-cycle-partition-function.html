For offered loads on consecutive routes a,b,c,d of the reversible proportionally fair allocation on a four-cycle, the normalizing sum of the stationary distribution is
B=(1−a−b)(1−a−d)(1−c−b)(1−c−d)(1−a)(1−b)(1−c)(1−d)−abcd​.
