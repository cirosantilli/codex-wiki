Four-dimensional N=8 supergravity (N=8 SUGRA)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supergravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
Four-dimensional N=8 supergravity has eight independent Weyl spinor supercharges. Its supergravity multiplet has helicities from 2 to −2, with multiplicities 1,8,28,56,70,56,28,8,1.
