Boolean formula

 ... Computational complexity theory Polynomial-time reduction Polynomial-time many-one reduction NP-hardness NP-completeness Boolean satisfiability problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 287 Articles: 6
A Boolean formula is a finite expression built from Boolean variables and Boolean operations. A choice of truth values determines its value. The Boolean satisfiability problem asks whether some choice makes it true.
