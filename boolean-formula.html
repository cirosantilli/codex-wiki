Boolean formula

 ... Computational complexity theory Polynomial-time reduction Polynomial-time many-one reduction NP-hardness NP-completeness Boolean satisfiability problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 525 Articles: 9
A Boolean formula is a finite expression built from Boolean variables and Boolean operations, equivalently a propositional formula with two-valued semantics. A Boolean valuation determines its truth value. In (x∨y)∧¬x, logical negation acts only on x, and the outer logical conjunction combines x∨y with ¬x. Occurrences of the same Boolean variable share one truth value. The Boolean satisfiability problem asks whether some Boolean valuation makes the Boolean formula true.
