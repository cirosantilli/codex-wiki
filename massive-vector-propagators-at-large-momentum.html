Massive-vector propagators at large momentum (DL(ξ)​=ξ/(kE2​+ξm2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 228
Consider one massive gauge field with m>0 after Higgs mechanism. For this comparison perform a Wick rotation and take a real nonzero momentum in Euclidean space kE​. The transverse and longitudinal orthogonal projectors are
PμνL​=kE2​kEμ​kEν​​,PμνT​=δμν​−PμνL​.
(16)
Here “longitudinal” means parallel to the off-shell four-momentum, not the physical longitudinal polarization of an on-shell massive gauge boson. The R-xi gauge quadratic vector operator and its inverse are
Kξ​=(kE2​+m2)PT+(kE2​/ξ+m2)PL,Dξ​=kE2​+m2PT​+kE2​+ξm2ξPL​,ξ>0.
(17)
Multiplication verifies Kξ​Dξ​=PT+PL=I, since PTPL=0 and (PT,L)2=PT,L. The unitary gauge vector operator lacks the gauge fixing derivative term in the longitudinal sector, giving
KU​=(kE2​+m2)PT+m2PL,DU​=kE2​+m2PT​+m2PL​=kE2​+m2δμν​+kEμ​kEν​/m2​.
(18)
Thus every eigenvalue of Dξ​ falls as 1/kE2​ at fixed finite positive ξ, whereas DU​ has the constant longitudinal eigenvalue 1/m2. Unitary gauge consequently obscures ordinary power counting; this is not a failure of the physical theory.
For a worked comparison choose ∣kE​∣=10m. The dimensionless longitudinal eigenvalue m2DL​ is 1 in unitary gauge, 1/101 at ξ=1, and 1/11 at ξ=10. Its crossover is ∣kE​∣∼ξ​m: taking ξ→∞ first gives a constant, whereas taking ∣kE​∣→∞ first gives zero. The two limits do not commute. Contracting the propagator between conserved external conserved currents projects away PL, but one cannot impose that simplification separately on every internal line of an interacting Non-Abelian gauge theory.
