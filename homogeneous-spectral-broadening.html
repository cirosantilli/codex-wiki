Homogeneous spectral broadening (ΔωFWHM​=2/T2​)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar atmosphere Spectral line Spectral line broadening
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
Broadening shared by otherwise identical emitters, often described by a common optical coherence time T2​. Exponential field-coherence decay produces a Lorentzian line shape with full angular-frequency width 2/T2​. Population decay, dephasing and collisions can all contribute, with their time constants distinguished.
