Density of the smooth locus

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Zariski tangent space Smooth locus of a variety
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 1
If X⊆An is irreducible of dimension d, choose a nonzero (n−d)-rowed minor of a Jacobian matrix at one point of minimum tangent dimension. Its nonvanishing locus is a nonempty Zariski-open set on which the Jacobian has rank n−d, so every point there is smooth. Irreducibility makes every nonempty Zariski-open set dense.
