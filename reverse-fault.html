Reverse fault

 ... Solid Earth Mantle convection Plate tectonics Geological fault Dip-slip faulting Reverse faulting
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
A fault on which the hanging wall moves up relative to the footwall, accommodating shortening. A low-angle reverse fault is a thrust fault. The fault dip is a geometric property and should be distinguished from the direction of fault slip.
