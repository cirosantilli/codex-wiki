An initial segment of the binary order on the discrete cube contains the vertices whose integer encodings are 0,…,m−1. It is a down-set, so its internal edges and two-dimensional cube faces can be counted at their unique upper vertices by ∑j<m​wt(j) and ∑j<m​(2wt(j)​).
