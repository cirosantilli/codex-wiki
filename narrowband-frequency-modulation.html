Narrowband frequency modulation

OurBigBook.com Words: 97
When the FM modulation index satisfies ∣β∣≪1, a Taylor expansion gives a useful first-order approximation. With ωc​=2πfc​ and ωm​=2πfm​,
As(t)​=cos(ωc​t)−βsin(ωm​t)sin(ωc​t)+O(β2)=cos(ωc​t)+2β​cos((ωc​+ωm​)t)−2β​cos((ωc​−ωm​)t)+O(β2).
(19)
The product-to-sum formula for sines exposes a carrier and one pair of sidebands, with opposite signs for this wave phase convention. Higher sidebands are neglected, not identically absent. For β=0.1, the predicted normalized first-sideband magnitude 0.05 is close to the original exact evaluation J1​(0.1)≈0.0499375. The neglected waveform term is bounded in absolute value by β2/2=0.005, using the second-derivative bound for the cosine. Small frequency deviation relative to fc​ alone does not justify this approximation: it is Δf/fm​ that must be small.
