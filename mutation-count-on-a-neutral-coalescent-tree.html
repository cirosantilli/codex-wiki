Mutation count on a neutral coalescent tree (S=∑j=2n​Yj​)

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Continuous-time Markov chain Kingman's coalescent
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 3
In Kingman's coalescent, the epoch with j ancestral lineages lasts Tj​∼Exp((2j​)), independently across epochs. If each lineage carries an independent Poisson process of mutations with rate θ/2, its epoch mutation count satisfies Yj​∣Tj​∼Poisson(jθTj​/2). The total mutation count is S=∑j=2n​Yj​. Under the infinite sites mutation model, this also counts segregating sites; without that assumption it still counts mutation events, which need not correspond one-to-one to observed polymorphisms.
