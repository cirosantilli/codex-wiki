Random matrix

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 435 Articles: 8
A random matrix is a matrix whose entries are random variables. Its eigenvalues form a random configuration, and its empirical spectral measure records their distribution with equal mass on each eigenvalue. Entry assumptions and normalization determine the spectral regime.
