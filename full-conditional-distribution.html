Full conditional distribution

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Conditional probability Conditional distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A full conditional distribution is the conditional law of one component of a joint model given all the other components and the observed data. A Gibbs sampler updates components by drawing from these laws.
