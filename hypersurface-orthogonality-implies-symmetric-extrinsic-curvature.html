Hypersurface orthogonality implies symmetric extrinsic curvature (Kμν​=Kνμ​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Numerical relativity 3+1 decomposition of spacetime Extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
Unit normalization gives nν∇μ​nν​=0. Hence Kμν​=−Pαμ​Pβν​∇α​nβ​=−Pαμ​∇α​nν​. Set Fμν​=∇μ​nν​−∇ν​nμ​. Frobenius theorem for a hypersurface normal gives nα​Fβγ​+nβ​Fγα​+nγ​Fαβ​=0. Contracting with nαPβμ​Pγν​ leaves −Pβμ​Pγν​Fβγ​=0, precisely the vanishing antisymmetric part of the extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface.
