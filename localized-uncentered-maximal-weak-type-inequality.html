Localized uncentered maximal weak-type inequality (∣Eα​∣≤3dν(Eα​)/α)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Hardy-Littlewood maximal function Uncentered maximal function of a finite measure Uncentered maximal weak-type inequality
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For a positive Borel measure on Rd, let Eα​={Mu​ν>α}, using open balls in the uncentered maximal function of a finite measure. Every ball witnessing the inequality lies entirely in Eα​. Cover a compact subset by finitely many witnessing balls and select disjoint balls greedily by decreasing radius. Their threefold dilations cover the compact set. Their total measure is at most ν(Eα​), giving the bound by inner regularity of Lebesgue measure. For a signed or complex measure, apply this to its total variation measure.
