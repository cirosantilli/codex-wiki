Longitudinal-vector annihilation through a scalar contact interaction (σLL​∼g4s/(32πmA4​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Proca equation Proca field
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For a vertex 2ig2ημν​ and two identical outgoing real scalar particles, the longitudinal-pair amplitude is g2(s−2mA2​)/mA2​. The high-energy cross section is the displayed expression, including the final-state factor 1/2!. An unpolarized incoming pair averages over nine spin states and has leading cross section g4s/(288πmA4​). A written interaction g2Aμ​Aμh2 instead gives vertex 4ig2ημν​ and four times these cross sections. The distinction separates a vertex normalization from polarization averaging and identical-particle phase-space counting.
