ARM architecture family

 Home Computer science Instruction set architecture
OurBigBook.com Words: 79 Articles: 1
A family of processor architectures with several execution states and instruction encodings. The 32-bit instruction sets include the compact Thumb instruction set; the 32-bit AAPCS32 calling convention specifies interoperation at subroutine boundaries.
