Q=−6μαρg​h4hx​,A=αh2​.
(3)
Conservation of volume is (h2)t​=C(h4hx​)x​, C=ρg/(6μ). Here α is the wall slope to the horizontal, not the opening half-angle measured from the vertical in a general V-section. Vertical shear dominates transverse shear by the shallow-slope approximation.
