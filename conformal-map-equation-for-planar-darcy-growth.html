Conformal map equation for planar Darcy growth (Re(ζFζ​Ft​​)=Q(∣ζ∣=1))

OurBigBook.com Words: 97
Let F(ζ,t) map the unit disk conformally to a planar Darcy flow region, with F(0,t)=0 at a point source of flux 2πQ. With zero velocity potential on the boundary, the logarithmic source potential becomes ϕ=Qlog∣ζ∣. Differentiating the inverse map at fixed physical position gives ϕt​=−QRe(Ft​/(ζFζ​)), while ∣∇ϕ∣2=Q2/∣ζFζ​∣2. The boundary law ϕt​+∣∇ϕ∣2=0 therefore gives the displayed equation, for nonzero Q.
The vector ζFζ​dθ is outward normal times arclength on ζ=eiθ. Consequently the equation gives the area growth law d∣D∣/dt=2πQ. For a circular map F=R(t)ζ, it reduces to RR˙=Q, and hence R(t)2=R(0)2+2Qt.
