Curvature difference formula (F∇+a​=F∇​+d∇​a+a∧a)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Curvature form
OurBigBook.com Words: 124 Articles: 2
If two objects ∇1​ and ∇2​ are each a connection on a vector bundle and satisfy ∇1​=∇2​+a for an End(E)-valued one-form a, then
F∇1​​=F∇2​​+d∇2​​a+a∧a.
(61)
This follows by expanding (∇2​+a)2 with the covariant exterior derivative on the endomorphism bundle connection.
