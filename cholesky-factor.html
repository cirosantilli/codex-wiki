Cholesky factor (A=CCT)

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Quadratic form Definite matrix Positive-definite matrix Cholesky decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For a real symmetric positive-definite matrix, the lower triangular matrix C with positive diagonal in its Cholesky decomposition is its lower Cholesky factor; CT is the corresponding upper factor. If a full-column-rank matrix B has a reduced QR decomposition B=QR with positive diagonal of R, then BTB=RTR, so R is the upper Cholesky factor of BTB.
