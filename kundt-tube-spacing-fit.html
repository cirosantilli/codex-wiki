Kundt tube spacing fit (xj​=a+jd,c=2fd)

Write the measured positions of consecutive maxima of sound pressure amplitude as xj​=a+jd+εj​. The arbitrary origin a is fitted along with the half-wavelength d by linear regression; forcing the line through zero would turn a ruler-origin offset into an erroneous slope. For independent equal-variance position errors of standard deviation σx​,
d=∑j​(j−jˉ​)2∑j​(j−jˉ​)(xj​−xˉ)​,σd​=∑j​(j−jˉ​)2​σx​​.
(4)
As an original example, choose f=1000Hz, a=24mm and d=171.5mm. Add stipulated position errors (1,−1,0,0,−1,1)mm at j=0,…,5. Their sum and index-weighted sum vanish, so the fitted values remain a=24mm and d=171.5mm, giving c=343.0m/s. These are synthetic inputs, not measurements of a particular air sample.
With stipulated σx​=1mm and independent drive-frequency uncertainty σf​=1Hz, first-order uncertainty propagation gives
∑j=05​(j−jˉ​)2=17.5,σd​≃0.2390mm,σc2​=(2fσd​)2+(2dσf​)2,σc​≃0.5884m/s​.
(5)
This statistical calculation omits common scale-calibration error, thermal drift and mode misidentification. Counting more positions reduces independent localization error; it cannot correct a wrong family of extrema or an incorrectly calibrated ruler.
