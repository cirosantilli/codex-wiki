Special values of the Dirichlet beta function

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Dirichlet beta function
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
Hankel residue extraction gives β(0)=1/2, β(−2)=−1/2, and values at other nonpositive integers from the Taylor coefficients of 1/(2cosht).
