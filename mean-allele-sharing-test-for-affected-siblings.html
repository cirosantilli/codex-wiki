Mean allele-sharing test for affected siblings (Z=(Jˉ−1)/1/(2n)​)

 Home Biology Genetics Genetic linkage Nonparametric linkage analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For independent pairs of affected full siblings with unrelated, noninbred parents and a fully informative genetic marker, the no-linkage null gives shared identity by descent count J∼Bin(2,1/2). Therefore EJ=1 and VarJ=1/2. An excess-sharing test uses the displayed approximately standard normal statistic and an upper-tail critical value. Pairs sharing family members are dependent, so their variance needs separate treatment. This targeted test differs from an omnibus test of all three sharing probabilities.
