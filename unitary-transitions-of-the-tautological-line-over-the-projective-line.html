Unitary transitions of the tautological line over the projective line (g01​(ζ)=∣ζ∣/ζ)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Tautological bundle Complex tautological line bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For ζ=z2​/z1​ and η=1/ζ, the tautological frames (1,ζ) and (η,1) differ by 1/ζ. Normalize their ambient Hermitian form norms; the resulting frames differ by the displayed phase. The unit circle total space is S3⊂C2 and its projection to the complex projective line is the Hopf fibration. The transition of the dual line is the reciprocal phase.
