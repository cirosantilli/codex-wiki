Complete negative filtered-graded transfer of Noetherianity (grR right Noetherian⟹R right Noetherian)

OurBigBook.com Words: 81
Lift finite homogeneous generators of the associated graded module of a right ideal I to xi​∈I of degrees di​. Successive leading-symbol cancellations write an element x∈Fd​I as finite partial sums ∑i​xi​ai(q)​ with residual in Fd−q​R. The coefficient increments lie in Fd−q−di​​R and therefore converge in the complete negative filtration. Multiplication is continuous, so their limits express x as a finite sum ∑i​xi​ai​. This generates the actual right ideal and does not presuppose that it is closed. The left-sided statement is analogous.
