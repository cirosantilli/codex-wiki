Travel coordinate for a conservative transport perturbation (a(s,t)=ϕ(ξ(s)−t)/V(s),ξ(s)=∫0s​V(r)−1dr)

For at​+(V(s)a)s​=0 with V>0, the flux perturbation b=Va solves bt​+Vbs​=0. The travel coordinate transforms it into unit-speed transport. Thus the flux perturbation is constant on characteristics while area amplitude changes as 1/V. In a supplied power-law river, Aeq′​=M/V, giving travel time [Aeq​(l)−Aeq​(0)]/M. At a zero-speed head, this time can remain integrable even though the amplitude formula is not uniform.
