Spin coherent-state Berry phase

 Home Physics Branch of physics Statistical physics Quantum magnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 160 Articles: 4
The spin coherent-state path integral contains a Berry phase equal, modulo gauge choices, to the spin S times the solid angle swept on the Bloch sphere. Single-valued path-integral amplitudes require 2S∈Z.
