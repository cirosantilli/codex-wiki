Periodic point of an interval map (Fn(x)=x)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Dynamical systems Discrete dynamical system Interval map
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
A point x is periodic for an interval map F when Fn(x)=x for some positive integer n. Its least such n is its period, and the finite set
{x,F(x),…,Fn−1(x)}
(2)
is its periodic orbit.
