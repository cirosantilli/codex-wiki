Torsion point of an elliptic curve (E[n])

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 598 Articles: 11
The n-torsion subgroup E[n] is the kernel of multiplication by n. When the characteristic does not divide n, it is isomorphic over an algebraic closure to (Z/nZ)2.
