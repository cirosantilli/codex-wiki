Even-function spectral gap on a hypercube (2Var(f)≤−E[fLf](f(−ω)=f(ω)))

OurBigBook.com Words: 39
Global sign-even functions have zero coefficients on odd-size Walsh characters. Every nonconstant remaining character has generator eigenvalue at most −2, yielding the displayed improvement from gap one to gap two. Combining this with the convexity of a norm proves the sharp Rademacher second-moment inequality.
