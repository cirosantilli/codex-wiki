Optical phonon (ωq​→ω0​>0 as q→0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 187 Articles: 1
An optical phonon is a quantum of vibration on an optical phonon branch, involving relative motion of atoms within a primitive cell. Its zone-center frequency can remain nonzero. Some such modes couple strongly to light when the relative displacement changes electric polarization; the name does not imply that every optical phonon is optically active. Their finite energies produce thresholds for electron emission and thermally suppressed absorption at low temperature.
