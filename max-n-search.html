Max-n search (maxn)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Game tree
OurBigBook.com Words: 207 Articles: 1
Backward induction with an n-component utility vector. At a node controlled by player i, evaluate each child, choose a child maximizing coordinate i and copy its entire vector. A fixed tie rule makes the returned vector well defined. By mathematical induction from terminal positions, the selected continuation is a best response at every node of a finite perfect-information game, giving a pure strategy subgame perfect equilibrium. The vector must be copied intact; separate coordinatewise maxima need not describe any attainable outcome.
