Photon polarization (∣ψ⟩=α∣H⟩+β∣V⟩)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Photon
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
For a photon in a fixed spatial mode, the two transverse photon polarizations span a qubit space. A pure photon polarization state is α∣H⟩+β∣V⟩ with ∣α∣2+∣β∣2=1, and a general state is a two-dimensional density matrix. Its Bloch vector gives the normalized Stokes parameters. Polarization-dependent path separation can correlate this internal degree of freedom with a path qubit.
