Bufferless queue output (Y=min(A,c),W=(A−c)+)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory Queue (queueing theory) Bufferless queue
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 2
The work served in one slot is capped at the service rate of a queue. The remainder is lost, so output is input minus its positive part above capacity. Every downstream threshold below the cap has exactly the same overflow event for input and output; thresholds at or above the cap cannot be exceeded by the output.
