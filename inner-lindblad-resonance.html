Inner Lindblad resonance (m(Ωφ​−Ωp​)=ΩR​)

 ... Astrophysics Planetary science Planetary system dynamics Planetary migration Type I migration Lindblad resonance
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The kR​=−1 orbital resonance of an m-fold perturbation of a galactic disk. For near-circular stellar orbits, ΩR​ is the radial epicyclic frequency. The harmonic changes radial action and azimuthal angular momentum in the ratio ΔJR​/ΔJφ​=−1/m, so an orbit losing angular momentum gains random radial motion.
