Hydrodynamic attraction of a plane wall to a point source (F=ρQ2/(16πa2))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Potential flow Point source
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
An incompressible point source of volume flux Q at height a above an impermeable plane has velocity potential ϕ=−Q(1/r+​+1/r−​)/(4π), with an equal image source below the plane. On the wall, us​=Qs/[2π(s2+a2)3/2]. The Bernoulli equation gives p−p∞​=−ρQ2s2/[8π2(s2+a2)3]. Integrating the pressure deficit over the plane gives the displayed attraction force relative to ambient pressure. If source strength instead denotes the coefficient Q/(4π) of radial inverse-square velocity, the force is πρm2/a2.
