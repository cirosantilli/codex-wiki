Galactic angular-momentum catastrophe

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Cosmological structure formation Hierarchical galaxy formation
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
The galactic angular-momentum catastrophe is the excessive loss or redistribution of baryonic specific angular momentum in early simulated galaxy formation, producing overly compact disks or large central concentrations. Premature cooling, gravitational torques, interactions, and resolution contributed. Improved stellar feedback and gas accretion histories can mitigate it; it is a historical simulation problem rather than a theorem against cold dark matter.
