Dominant eigenvalue

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Eigenvalue Diagonalizable matrix Spectral decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
An eigenvalue is dominant when its modulus is greater than or equal to that of every other eigenvalue. It is uniquely dominant when the inequality is strict.
