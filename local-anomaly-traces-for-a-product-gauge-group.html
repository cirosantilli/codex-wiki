For left-handed Weyl fermions in representations (Ri​,Qi​) of a simple semisimple factor times U(1), the displayed traces cancel the local cubic non-Abelian, mixed non-Abelian–Abelian, and cubic Abelian gauge anomalies. Include spectator multiplicities in every trace. A triangle with one non-Abelian insertion and two Abelian insertions vanishes by tracelessness. Coupling to gravity also requires ∑i​dim(Ri​)Qi​=0, and global anomalies must be checked separately. For a conjugate representation the cubic coefficient changes sign but the Dynkin index T(R) does not.
