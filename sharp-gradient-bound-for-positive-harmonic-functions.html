Sharp gradient bound for positive harmonic functions (∣∇u(z)∣≤2u(z)/(1−∣z∣2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For a positive harmonic function on the unit disc, choose a global harmonic conjugate and apply the Carathéodory derivative bound for the right half-plane to u+iv. This gives ∣∇u(0)∣≤2u(0) by the Cauchy-Riemann equations. A disk automorphism sending zero to z has derivative modulus 1−∣z∣2, so the chain rule gives the displayed bound. Composing a boundary-pole Poisson kernel with a disk automorphism attains equality, proving the coefficient is optimal at every point.
