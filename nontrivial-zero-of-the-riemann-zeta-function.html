Nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Riemann zeta function
OurBigBook.com Words: 829 Articles: 19
A nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function lies in the critical strip 0<ℜs<1. The functional equation of the Riemann zeta function reflects such zeros across the critical line ℜs=1/2.
