Past light cone

 ... Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Minkowski spacetime Minkowski metric Light cone
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The past light cone of an event consists of earlier events connected to it by past-directed null curves. A retarded field at the event depends on sources where their worldlines intersect this cone.
