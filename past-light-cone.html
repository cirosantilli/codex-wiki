Past light cone

 ... Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Minkowski spacetime Minkowski metric Light cone
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
The past light cone of a spacetime event O is the past-directed part of its light cone. In Minkowski spacetime, its spacetime events satisfy ∣Δx∣=−cΔt with Δt<0. They are the possible emission events of light rays arriving at O. The interior also contains events that can send slower signals to O. The retarded potential of a moving point electric charge, given by the Liénard–Wiechert potential, uses the emission event where the source's world line intersects this boundary.
