Interior contraction of the Riemannian volume form (ιX​ωg​=∑i​(−1)i−1fi​θi​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian metric Riemannian volume form
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 2
In a positive Riemannian orthonormal frame X=∑i​fi​Ei​ with dual coframe ωi​, the Riemannian volume form is ω1​∧⋯∧ωn​. Inserting Ei​ in its first slot omits ωi​ and produces sign (−1)i−1, giving the displayed interior product formula.
