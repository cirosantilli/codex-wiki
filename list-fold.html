List fold (foldr :: (a -> b -> b) -> b -> [a] -> b)

 Home Computer science Programming language Functional programming
OurBigBook.com Words: 326 Articles: 4
The structural recursion that replaces an empty list by a chosen value and a cons constructor by a combining function applied to the head and folded tail. A direct list fold is strict in the list spine at its outermost constructor, even when the combining function does not demand the folded tail. Its algebraic recognition rule is the fold factorisation criterion.
