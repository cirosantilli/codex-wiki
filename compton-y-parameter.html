Compton y parameter (y=me​c2σT​​∫ne​kB​Te​dℓ)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Compton wavelength Compton scattering Kompaneets equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The Compton y parameter is the Thomson scattering optical depth weighted by the dimensionless Electron temperature. It measures the integrated electron pressure along a line of sight. When hot, nonrelativistic electrons scatter much colder photons, the first-order radiation energy gain is 4y times the incident energy. The spectrum is redistributed while the total photon number density is conserved locally by scattering.
