Noether current of scalar electrodynamics (Jμ=iq[ϕ∗Dμϕ−ϕ(Dμϕ)∗])

For Dμ​=∂μ​+iqAμ​, the constant phase transformation δϕ=−iqϵϕ has the displayed Noether current. Varying the gauge field gives ∂μ​Fμν=Jν. In this convention Jμ=iq(ϕ∗∂μϕ−ϕ∂μϕ∗)−2q2Aμ∣ϕ∣2. The matter equations imply ∂μ​Jμ=0; the integrated electric charge additionally requires zero boundary flux. Local phase invariance gives a Noether second theorem identity, rather than independent physical charges for arbitrary functions.[ref]
