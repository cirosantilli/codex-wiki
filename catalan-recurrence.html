Catalan recurrence (Cn​=∑k=0n−1​Ck​Cn−1−k​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 1
The first-return decomposition of a Dyck path and the rule of product give
C0​=1,Cn​=k=0∑n−1​Ck​Cn−1−k​(n≥1).​
(37)
For a given k, choose the inside path in Ck​ ways and its suffix in Cn−1−k​ ways. Different first-return locations give disjoint groups, so the rule of sum adds their counts. For n=4, the terms are 1⋅5+1⋅2+2⋅1+5⋅1=14. Iterating the recurrence relation computes the counts without enumerating every full path.
