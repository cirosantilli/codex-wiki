Fixed-angular-momentum energy test for principal-axis rotation (ΔT=21​J12​(I1−1​−I3−1​)+21​J22​(I2−1​−I3−1​))

For torque-free rotation, total angular momentum magnitude J and rotational kinetic energy are conserved. Near rotation about principal axis three, eliminate J32​=J2−J12​−J22​ from T=∑i​Ji2​/(2Ii​) to obtain the displayed energy difference from J2/(2I3​). If I3​ is largest, it is a constrained energy minimum; if smallest, a maximum. Both extrema are stable in the conservative problem. An intermediate moment gives opposite signs in the quadratic form, a saddle and instability. Dissipation changes the fixed-energy argument and must not be silently added to torque-free motion.
