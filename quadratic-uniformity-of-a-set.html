Quadratic uniformity of a set (∥1A​−∣A∣/∣G∣∥U3​≤α)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Gowers uniformity norm Gowers U3 norm
OurBigBook.com Words: 280 Articles: 2
A subset A of a finite abelian group G with subset density η=∣A∣/∣G∣ is quadratically α-uniform, in the norm convention, if its balanced subset indicator g=1A​−η has Gowers U3 norm at most α. This controls correlations on additive three-dimensional cubes after the subset density contribution is removed. If a convention instead bounds the eighth power of the norm by α, its parameter corresponds to the norm parameter α1/8. The convention must therefore be stated when giving quantitative thresholds.
