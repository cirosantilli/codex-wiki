Effective action (Γ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 19
The quantum effective action is the Legendre transform of the generating functional of connected correlation functions. Its functional derivatives are the one-particle-irreducible vertex functions.
