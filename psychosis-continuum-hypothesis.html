Psychosis continuum hypothesis

 Home Science Psychology Clinical psychology Psychopathology Psychotic experience
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
The hypothesis that some psychosis-related experiences vary dimensionally across clinical and nonclinical populations. Overlap in a dimension does not imply that all people share the same cause, prognosis or need for care.
