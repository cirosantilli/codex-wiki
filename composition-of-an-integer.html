Composition of an integer (x1​+⋯+xk​=N,xi​≥1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Stars and bars (combinatorics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 87 Articles: 1
A composition of an integer N>0 is an ordered tuple of positive integers with sum N. Its length k counts the parts. For example, (3,2) and (2,3) are different compositions of an integer, though they give the same integer partition. Stars and bars counts compositions of an integer with fixed length. [ref]
