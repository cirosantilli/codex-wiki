Tangent addition functional equation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
A differentiable real function satisfying
f(x+y)=1−f(x)f(y)f(x)+f(y)​
(286)
on an interval has f(0)=0 and f′=C(1+f2), hence f(x)=tan(Cx) wherever the tangent remains finite.
