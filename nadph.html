NADPH (NADPH)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
NADPH is the reduced form of nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate. It carries electrons used in synthesis; in the Calvin cycle it becomes the oxidized form NADP+, available for reuse by light-dependent reactions. NADPH and ATP serve different roles even though both connect light capture to carbon fixation.
