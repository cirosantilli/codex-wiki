Lefschetz fixed-point theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 262 Articles: 6
If L(f)=0, then f has a fixed point. For a fixed-point-free map, sufficiently fine simplicial approximation has zero diagonal chain coefficients, hence zero Lefschetz number.
