Walk count from powers of an adjacency matrix

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Adjacency matrix of a graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
For every nonnegative integer k, the entry (Ak)uv​ equals the number of length-k walks from u to v. This follows by induction, since matrix multiplication appends one adjacent vertex to each walk.
