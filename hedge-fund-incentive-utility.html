Hedge fund incentive utility (F(y)=U(εy+α(y−w0​)+​))

An incentive fee above a hurdle changes a manager’s utility function into F(y)=U(εy+α(y−w0​)+​). For constant absolute risk aversion utility, each branch is strictly concave, but the marginal reward jumps upward at the hurdle. Thus the effective terminal-wealth utility is not concave, and concavification of incentive utility can reveal optimal risk-taking lotteries.
