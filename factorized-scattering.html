Factorized scattering

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Integrable quantum field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 483 Articles: 8
Multiparticle quantum scattering is factorized when it decomposes into two-body scatterings without particle production or a new irreducible many-body amplitude. The independence of the answer from the order of exchanges imposes the Yang-Baxter equation. Classical pairwise additivity of soliton shifts is a corresponding property of integrable soliton collisions.
