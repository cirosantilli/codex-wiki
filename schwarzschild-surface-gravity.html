Schwarzschild surface gravity (κ=1/(4M))

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Null hypersurface Killing horizon Surface gravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
For a Schwarzschild black hole of mass M in geometrized units, f=1−2M/r gives κ=f′(2M)/2=1/(4M). The Killing vector field is normalized at infinity; changing that normalization changes κ.
