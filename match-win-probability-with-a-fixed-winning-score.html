Match win probability with a fixed winning score

OurBigBook.com Words: 107
Suppose independent Bernoulli trials give a player a win with probability p, and the match ends when either player reaches r wins. The player's match-win probability is
∑n=0r−1​(r−1r+n−1​)pr(1−p)n.
(42)
For a win with n losses, the final trial must be a win. The preceding r+n−1 trials contain exactly r−1 wins and n losses; the binomial coefficient counts their arrangements. Because n<r, the opponent cannot already have won, and because the player has only r−1 previous wins, the match has not ended earlier. Summing the disjoint terminal events gives the formula, a truncated negative binomial distribution sum. The constant game-win probability must describe independent games, or equivalently remain the same conditional on every previous history.
