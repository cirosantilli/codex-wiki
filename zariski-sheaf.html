Zariski sheaf

 ... Category Functor Functor category Presheaf category Grothendieck topology Sheaf on a site
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
A contravariant set-valued functor on schemes is a Zariski sheaf if compatible local elements on any Zariski cover glue uniquely. Explicitly, F(T) is the equalizer of ∏i​F(Ui​)⇉∏i,j​F(Ui​×T​Uj​). The functor of points of every scheme is a Zariski sheaf because morphisms of schemes glue over open covers.
