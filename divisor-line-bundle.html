Divisor line bundle (OX​(D))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Cartier divisor
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
On an integral scheme, for a Cartier divisor locally represented by ai​∈K∗, its divisor line bundle is the subsheaf of rational functions locally equal to ai−1​OX​. Unit ratios glue these free rank-one modules. Given a nonzero rational section of a line bundle s=ai​ei​, the maps ei​↦ai−1​ identify that invertible sheaf with OX​(D). Replacing s by a nonzero rational multiple changes D by a principal Cartier divisor.
