Topological ring

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 417 Articles: 6
A topological ring is a ring equipped with a topology making addition, additive inversion and multiplication continuous functions. An adic topology is obtained from powers of an ideal.
