Minimal polynomial

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Linear operator theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 2
The minimal polynomial mA​ is the unique monic polynomial of least degree satisfying mA​(A)=0. It divides every polynomial that annihilates A.
