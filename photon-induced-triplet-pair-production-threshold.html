Photon-induced triplet pair-production threshold (Eγ,thr​=4me​c2)

 ... Special relativity Four-momentum Four-momentum conservation Relativistic collision mass threshold Fixed-target production threshold Photon fixed-target production threshold
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For a photon incident on a stationary Electron, producing an additional electron and positron leaves three massive particles in the final state. Four-momentum conservation gives W2=me2​c4+2me​c2Eγ​ for the centre-of-momentum energy. Its minimum is 3me​c2, so the incident threshold is 4me​c2. At threshold all final particles are at rest in their centre-of-momentum frame, which moves at 4c/5 relative to the target laboratory frame. This differs from a heavy-target threshold because the electron target's recoil cannot be neglected.
