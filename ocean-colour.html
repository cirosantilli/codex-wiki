Ocean colour

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Radiometry Ocean optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 179 Articles: 6
The spectral distribution of light emerging from a water body, determined by absorption and scattering by water, dissolved material, particles and sometimes the bottom. It supports remote inference of biological and optical properties.
