Lithosphere

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth
OurBigBook.com Words: 696 Articles: 14
The mechanically strong outer shell of a rocky planet, including crust and the colder upper mantle. It can form mobile plates in plate tectonics or a relatively stagnant lid. Its temperature, water content and damage affect its ability to yield.
