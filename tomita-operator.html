Tomita operator (S0​(aΩ)=a∗Ω)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Operator algebra Cyclic vector for an operator algebra Separating vector for an operator algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 2
For a Von Neumann algebra with a cyclic separating vector, this is a densely defined antilinear operator. It is closable: the commutant orbit lies in the domain of its antilinear adjoint and is dense. Write S for its closure and S=JΔ1/2 for its polar decomposition. In finite dimension it is everywhere defined and invertible. For a tracial vector, S0​ is isometric and extends to an antiunitary involution.
