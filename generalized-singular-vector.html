Generalized singular vector (∥Kuλ​∥=1,λK∗Kuλ​∈∂J(uλ​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Absolutely one-homogeneous functional
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
Let K:U→V be a bounded linear operator from a real Banach space to a real Hilbert space, and let J be a proper lower-semicontinuous convex absolutely one-homogeneous functional on U. The normalization and source relation imply λ=J(uλ​) by the Euler identity for a convex one-homogeneous functional. For the norm penalty J(u)=∥u∥ on a Hilbert space, its nonzero-point subgradient is u/∥u∥. Hence K∗Kuλ​=uλ​/∥uλ​∥2: v=uλ​/∥uλ​∥ is a right singular vector with singular value σ=1/∥uλ​∥, and Kuλ​ is the corresponding left singular vector. Thus the definition recovers the ordinary operator singular-vector relation using the one-homogeneous norm penalty. Uniqueness of the vector from its data requires extra hypotheses on K and J.
