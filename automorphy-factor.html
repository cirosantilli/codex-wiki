Automorphy factor (j(γ,τ)=cτ+d)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Slash operator for modular forms
OurBigBook.com Words: 15
For γ=(ac​bd​), the automorphy factor is j(γ,τ)=cτ+d. It obeys the cocycle identity
j(γδ,τ)=j(γ,δτ)j(δ,τ).
