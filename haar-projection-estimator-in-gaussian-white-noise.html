Haar projection estimator in Gaussian white noise (ΠVJ​​f=∑k​(∫ϕJ,k​dY)ϕJ,k​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 1
In the Gaussian white noise model, estimate a Haar scaling function coefficient by ∫ϕJ,k​dY. The resulting Haar projection estimator is an unbiased estimator with independent coefficient errors of variance 1/n. On each cell it is the observed path increment divided by the cell length. This gives a finite-dimensional estimator without imposing smoothness on the drift.
