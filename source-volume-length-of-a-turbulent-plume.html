Source-volume length of a turbulent plume (LQjet​=Q0​/(cM0​​),LQplume​=(Q0​/CQ​)3/5)

OurBigBook.com Words: 57
Source volume flux is negligible only when entrained volume greatly exceeds it. In the jet limit of the top-hat plume model, this requires z≫Q0​/(cM0​​). In a pure plume with Q=CQ​z5/3, it requires z≫(Q0​/CQ​)3/5, where CQ​=(3c/5)(9cF0​/20)1/3 for full-cross-section fluxes and c=2αe​π​. These scales differ from the jet length which measures imposed momentum relative to buoyancy. Neglecting source volume does not automatically permit neglecting source momentum.
