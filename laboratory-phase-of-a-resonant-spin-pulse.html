Laboratory phase of a resonant spin pulse (φend​=φ+ω0​τ)

With H0​=−ℏω0​σz​/2 and a co-rotating transverse magnetic drive of phase φ, an initial spin-up state becomes, up to common phase, cos(Ωτ/2)∣↑⟩+ie−i(φ+ω0​τ)sin(Ωτ/2)∣↓⟩. A quarter-turn pulse therefore prepares laboratory +x when φend​=π/2 and +y when φend​=0. Dropping the free-precession phase instead specifies targets in rotating axes. Transverse laboratory targets subsequently precess if the static field is retained.
