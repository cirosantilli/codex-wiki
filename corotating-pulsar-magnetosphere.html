Corotating pulsar magnetosphere

 ... Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar evolution Neutron star Pulsar Pulsar magnetosphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 52 Articles: 1
In an idealized corotating pulsar magnetosphere, plasma moves with u=Ω×r. In Gaussian units, ideal magnetohydrodynamics then gives E=−u×B/c.
