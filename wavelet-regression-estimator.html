At design sites i/n, let Yn​ be the step function taking response Yi​ on ((i−1)/n,i/n]. Project it onto an interval-adapted wavelet basis through resolution J. Each coefficient is cλ​=∑i​Yi​∫Ii​​bλ​(t)dt. Its expectation is the corresponding coefficient of the step function of sampled regression means. For a Haar wavelet basis, dyadic sample size and resolution at most the sampling scale, this is exactly n−1∑i​Yi​bλ​(i/n) with right-closed pointwise versions. Independent errors of variance σ2 give coefficient variance at most σ2/n: Cauchy-Schwarz inequality on each sampling cell yields ∑i​(∫Ii​​bλ​)2≤∥bλ​∥22​/n.
