Characteristic impedance of a stretched string (Z=Tμ​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Wave equation on a string
OurBigBook.com Words: 496 Articles: 6
For the lossless small-slope wave equation on a string, tension T and linear mass density μ give wave speed c=T/μ​. With transverse force Fy​=−Tyx​ exerted across a cut toward increasing x, a rightgoing displacement y=f(x−ct) has velocity vy​=−cyx​. Its positive mechanical impedance is therefore Z=Fy​/vy​=T/c=Tμ​. A leftgoing component has Fy​/vy​=−Z under the same fixed orientation. A rightgoing time-harmonic wave with peak displacement wave amplitude ∣A∣ carries mean power Zω2∣A∣2/2.
