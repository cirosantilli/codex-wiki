Conserved quantity

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Hamiltonian mechanics Integral of motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
A conserved quantity is a quantity that remains constant along the time evolution of a system. In Hamiltonian mechanics, a possibly time-dependent phase-space function F is conserved exactly when ∂t​F+{F,H}=0.
