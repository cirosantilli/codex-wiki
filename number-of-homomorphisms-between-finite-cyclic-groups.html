Number of homomorphisms between finite cyclic groups (∣Hom(Zm​,Zn​)∣=gcd(m,n))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group homomorphism Homomorphism between cyclic groups
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
A group homomorphism from Zm​ to the additive cyclic group Zn​ is determined by the image a of 1, subject to ma≡0(modn). Conversely every such a gives a well-defined map k↦ka. If d=gcd(m,n), the condition is equivalent to n/d dividing a. There are exactly d such residues modulo n, proving the formula. More generally, homomorphisms from Zm​ to any group correspond to target elements whose order of a group element divides m.
