Hopping integral (tij​=⟨i∣H∣j⟩)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bloch's theorem Tight binding
OurBigBook.com Words: 184 Articles: 2
A hopping integral is an off-diagonal Hamiltonian matrix element between localized orbitals on different lattice sites. It has units of energy and controls the dispersion in a tight-binding model. Its sign and phase depend on the orbital phase convention; observable band energies do not. The sign of a real nearest-neighbour t in E(k)=E0​+2tcos(ka) determines the location of the minimum in that convention.
