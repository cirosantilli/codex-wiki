End-to-end distance of a worm-like chain (⟨R2⟩=2ℓp​L−2ℓp2​(1−e−L/ℓp​))

For a free three-dimensional worm-like chain with contour length L and persistence length ℓp​, the unit-tangent correlation is ⟨t(s)⋅t(s′)⟩=e−∣s−s′∣/ℓp​. Since R=∫0L​t(s)ds, integrating this correlation gives
⟨R2⟩=2∫0L​(L−u)e−u/ℓp​du=2ℓp​L−2ℓp2​(1−e−L/ℓp​).
(68)
For L≫ℓp​, the leading term is 2ℓp​L, the ideal chain law with Kuhn length 2ℓp​. For L≪ℓp​, a Taylor series gives ⟨R2⟩=L2−L3/(3ℓp​)+O(L4/ℓp2​), approaching a rigid rod. This ideal semiflexible-chain model omits long-range excluded volume; sufficiently long dilute chains in a good solvent can cross over to swollen-chain statistics.
