Shifted integral representation of a p-series tail (kp​=N−[(p−1)∑n=N∞​n−p]−1/(p−1))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series P-series
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
Fix an integer N>1 and a real exponent p>1, and put Sp​=∑n=N∞​n−p. There is a unique shift kp​<N such that
∫N∞​(x−kp​)−pdx=Sp​.
(102)
Integration evaluates the left side as (N−kp​)1−p/(p−1), yielding the displayed formula. Comparing the decreasing function x−p with unit rectangles proves
p−1N1−p​<Sp​<p−1(N−1)1−p​.
(103)
The integral is a strictly increasing function of its shift, so 0<kp​<1. This is an exact reparametrization of a tail, not a claim that its shift is independent of p; interpolating shifts for different exponents is an additional approximation.
