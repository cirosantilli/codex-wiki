Potential scale reduction factor (R)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Markov chain Monte Carlo Markov chain Monte Carlo convergence diagnostics
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
The potential scale reduction factor compares variation within simulated chains to a variance estimate also incorporating differences between chains. In its classical form, R=V/W​, where W is the average within-chain sample variance. Values appreciably above one indicate disagreement; values close to one do not prove convergence. Splitting chains helps expose changes over time, and modern rank-based variants improve robustness.[ref]
