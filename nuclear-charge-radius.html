Nuclear charge radius (rch​=⟨r2⟩ch​​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 98
The root mean square radius of a nuclear charge distribution, referred to its center. Define its charge-weighted second moment by
⟨r2⟩ch​=Ze1​∫r2ρch​(r)d3r.
(5)
For a spherical charge density with finite fourth moment, insert the Taylor series sinu/u=1−u2/6+O(u4) into the nuclear charge form factor:
F(q)=1−6q2​⟨r2⟩ch​+O(q4),⟨r2⟩ch​=−6d(q2)dF​​q=0​.
(6)
Thus a radius is a low-momentum transfer slope, not necessarily the position of a sharp edge. In this real static model,
∣F(q)∣2=1−3q2​⟨r2⟩ch​+O(q4).
(7)
The factor of two between the slopes of F and ∣F∣2 matters when interpreting a differential scattering cross-section. A proton charge radius is the corresponding size concept for the proton; the spatial arrangement of many nucleons introduces an additional scale in a heavier atomic nucleus.
