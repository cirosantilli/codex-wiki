Affine bundle

Words: 33
Fiber bundle OurBigBook.com
A fiber bundle whose fibers are affine spaces and whose transition functions are affine. Differences of points in one fiber lie in a corresponding model vector bundle, but there need not be a preferred zero in each fiber.

Section (fiber bundle, π∘s=idB​)

Words: 99 Articles: 1
Fiber bundle OurBigBook.com
A section of a fiber bundle π:E→B is a continuous map s:B→E with π∘s=idB​. It chooses a point of each fibre continuously. A section of a vector bundle is the special case for vector fibres; a projective bundle section chooses a line in each fibre of the underlying vector bundle.

Cohomological obstruction to a section of the twistor bundle (S2⟶CP3⟶S4)

Words: 55
Section (fiber bundle) OurBigBook.com
If the displayed fiber bundle had a section s, its projection p would satisfy s∗p∗=1. The cohomology ring of complex projective space is Z[h]/(h4) with ∣h∣=2. For a generator u∈H4(S4;Z), write p∗u=mh2. But s∗h=0 since H2(S4;Z)=0, giving u=s∗p∗u=0. This contradiction proves that no section exists, without needing the value of m.

Leray-Hirsch theorem (H∗(E;R)≅⨁j​H∗−∣ej​∣(B;R)ej​)

Words: 246 Articles: 2
Fiber bundle OurBigBook.com
For a fiber bundle π:E→B admitting a finite trivializing open cover, whose fiber has finite free cohomology over R, suppose classes ej​∈H∗(E;R) restrict to a basis on every fiber. Then multiplication a⊗ej​↦π∗a⌣ej​ gives the indicated module isomorphism. A finite trivializing open cover suffices, by the local Künneth theorem and the Mayer–Vietoris sequence. This determines an additive module structure; multiplicative relations must still be computed. Powers of the hyperplane class on a complex projective bundle provide an important application.

Finite-cover proof of the Leray-Hirsch theorem

Words: 83
Leray-Hirsch theorem OurBigBook.com
For global cohomology classes satisfying the local basis criterion for Leray-Hirsch classes, multiplication commutes with the Mayer–Vietoris sequence, including its connecting homomorphism: multiply cochains on the right by global cocycle representatives. Induct over a finite trivializing open cover. If U is a union of r−1 patches and V is the last patch, U∩V has a cover by at most r−1 trivialized intersections. The induction hypothesis on U, V and U∩V, followed by the Five lemma, proves the theorem on U∪V. A compact base supplies the finite cover.

Local basis criterion for Leray-Hirsch classes

Words: 88
Leray-Hirsch theorem OurBigBook.com
On a trivialized fiber bundle over U, assume the fiber has finite-dimensional total cohomology over a field. The Künneth theorem identifies the total-space cohomology with a finite free graded module over H∗(U). Classes restricting to a basis on every fiber give an invertible change of generators: its degree-zero coefficient blocks are invertible on each path component, while its positive-base-degree terms strictly decrease fiber degree and are therefore nilpotent. Inverting the diagonal blocks and then using a finite geometric series proves that multiplication by the chosen classes is an isomorphism over U.

Local trivialization

Words: 137 Articles: 2
Fiber bundle OurBigBook.com
A local trivialization of a fiber bundle p:E→B over an open set U⊆B is a homeomorphism p−1(U)≅U×F commuting with the projections to U. For a vector bundle, it must be linear on each fiber. Compatible local trivializations specify the bundle structure and give its transition functions on overlaps.

Transition function of a vector bundle (gij​:Ui​∩Uj​→GLr​)

Words: 88 Articles: 1
Local trivialization OurBigBook.com
Two local frames of a vector bundle differ on their overlap by an invertible matrix-valued function. These functions satisfy the Čech cocycle condition. For a holomorphic vector bundle they are holomorphic, and for a holomorphic line bundle they are scalar holomorphic units.

Structure group of a vector bundle (gij​:Ui​∩Uj​⟶G⊆GL(k,C))

Words: 56
Transition function of a vector bundle OurBigBook.com
A complex vector bundle has structure group G when it admits fiber-linear local trivializations whose transition matrices take values in G. Replacing frames by hi​ changes transitions to hi−1​gij​hj​ in the frame convention. Reducing the structure group means choosing new trivializations with values in a specified subgroup. The same transitions glue Ui​×G by left multiplication to a right principal bundle.

Ehresmann fibration theorem

Words: 20
Fiber bundle OurBigBook.com
Every proper surjective submersion is a locally trivial smooth fiber bundle. In particular, the fibers in a proper smooth family are diffeomorphic.

Principal bundle

Words: 2k Articles: 36
Fiber bundle OurBigBook.com
A principal G-bundle is a fiber bundle π:P→B with a free right action of a Lie group G that is locally equivariantly isomorphic to U×G→U. Each fiber is a single G-orbit.

Principal circle bundle on real projective three-space (RP3⟶CP1)

Words: 109
Principal bundle OurBigBook.com
Write a point of Real projective space as a nonzero vector (w1​,w2​)∈C2 modulo real nonzero scaling. The map to Complex projective space sends its class to the complex line through that vector. A free right action of the circle group is [w]⋅u=[λw] where λ2=u; the two possible roots differ by a real sign and therefore give the same projective point. Local smooth square-root branches prove smoothness. Over [z:1], the fibre coordinate is (w2​/∣w2​∣)2; over [1:ζ], it is (w1​/∣w1​∣)2. On the overlap ζ=1/z, the transition function of a principal bundle is u↦(z/z)u. Its phase winds twice. Ordinary scalar multiplication by u instead has a kernel of order two and is not a free action of a group.

Transition function of a principal bundle (Φj​Φi−1​(x,g)=(x,ψji​(x)g))

Words: 57
Principal bundle OurBigBook.com
For equivariant local trivializations of a right principal bundle, the change of coordinates is left multiplication in the group coordinate. Indeed an equivariant change H satisfies H(x,gh)=H(x,g)h, so evaluating at the identity gives H(x,g)=(x,ψ(x)g). Its inverse shows that ψ takes values in the Lie group, and restriction to (x,e) makes it smooth. Composition on triple overlaps gives ψki​=ψkj​ψji​.

Frame bundle (Fr(TM))

Words: 45
Principal bundle OurBigBook.com
The frame bundle of an n-dimensional smooth manifold has fiber over x consisting of linear isomorphisms u:Rn→Tx​M. Right composition by GL(n,R) makes it a principal bundle. A global frame of a vector bundle is a global section of a fiber bundle and trivializes the tangent bundle. A metric restricts the allowed frames to the orthonormal frame bundle.

Orthonormal frame bundle (O(M,g))

Words: 335 Articles: 4
Principal bundle OurBigBook.com
For a metric of signature (r,s), the orthonormal frame bundle consists of all linear isometries u:Rr,s→Tx​M. Right composition by O(r,s) makes it a principal bundle. Its standard associated vector bundle is the tangent bundle, via [u,v]↦u(v). A global orthonormal frame is a global section and therefore trivializes this principal bundle. A Lie group with a left-invariant metric has such a frame by left translating an orthonormal basis at the identity.

Oriented orthonormal frame bundle of anti-de Sitter spacetime (SO(3,2)⟶AdS4​)

Words: 74
Orthonormal frame bundle OurBigBook.com
Represent the Anti-de Sitter spacetime quadric by x⋅x=−ℓ2 in R3,2. Appending its timelike unit normal x/ℓ to an oriented tangent pseudo-orthonormal frame identifies the frame bundle with SO(3,2). The stabilizer of that normal is SO(3,1), so projection realizes SO(3,2)/SO(3,1) as the base of a principal bundle. For the commonly used universal cover of the anti-de Sitter quadric, pull this bundle back along the covering map; the quadric itself has a periodic timelike coordinate.

Oriented orthonormal frame bundle of Minkowski spacetime (R3,1⋊SO(3,1)⟶R3,1)

Words: 70
Orthonormal frame bundle OurBigBook.com
An oriented pseudo-orthonormal frame in Minkowski spacetime consists of its base point a and a Lorentz matrix L acting on a fixed reference frame. The pairs (a,L) form the Poincaré group, and right multiplication by (0,h) sends the frame to (a,Lh) while fixing its base point. Thus the displayed projection is a principal bundle with group SO(3,1). The full frame bundle uses O(3,1); a specified time orientation restricts to the corresponding connected component.

Oriented frame bundle of de Sitter spacetime (SO(4,1)⟶dS4​)

Words: 122 Articles: 1
Orthonormal frame bundle OurBigBook.com
Represent unit-radius de Sitter spacetime by the spacelike unit hyperboloid in five-dimensional Minkowski spacetime. Appending its position vector as the last column of an oriented pseudo-orthonormal frame produces a matrix in SO(4,1). Projection to that last column identifies SO(4,1) with the oriented orthonormal frame bundle, a principal bundle with group SO(3,1). The full bundle allowing both orientations is O(4,1) with group O(3,1). Requiring a time orientation as well selects the corresponding identity components.
Global frame on four-dimensional de Sitter spacetime (E0​=∂τ​,Ei​=(coshτ)−1Yi​)
Words: 57
Oriented frame bundle of de Sitter spacetime OurBigBook.com
In global coordinates the de Sitter spacetime metric is −dτ2+cosh2τgS3​. A global frame of a vector bundle orthonormal for the round metric Y1​,Y2​,Y3​ on S3 gives the displayed global pseudo-orthonormal frame. For example, identify S3 with the unit quaternions and use qi,qj,qk. Choosing the spatial orientation and the future direction consistently gives a section of a fiber bundle for the oriented, time-oriented frame bundle. The principal bundle trivialization by a global section then proves that bundle is trivial.

Associated bundle (P×G​F)

Words: 46
Principal bundle OurBigBook.com
For a principal bundle P→B and a left action of its structure group on a manifold F, the associated bundle is (P×F)/∼, where (pg,f)∼(p,gf). Local sections identify it with U×F; principal transition functions act on F. Linear actions give associated vector bundles.

Principal bundle trivialization by a global section (B×G⟶P,(x,g)↦s(x)g)

Words: 50
Principal bundle OurBigBook.com
A principal bundle is trivial exactly when it has a global section of a fiber bundle. Given such a section, the displayed map is a smooth equivariant isomorphism: the free transitive action on each fiber gives the unique inverse group coordinate, and local trivializations make it smooth. Conversely, a product trivialization has the section x↦(x,e).

Associated vector bundle (E=P×ρ​V)

Words: 71 Articles: 1

A linear representation ρ:G→GL(V) of a principal bundle's structure group defines the quotient of P×V by (p,v)∼(pg,ρ(g)−1v). It is a vector bundle over the same base. A principal connection induces parallel transport and a local covariant derivative d+ρ∗​(A) on its sections.

Adjoint bundle (adP=P×Ad​g)

Words: 36
Associated vector bundle OurBigBook.com
The adjoint bundle is the associated vector bundle for the Adjoint representation of a Lie group on its Lie algebra. The gauge curvature of a principal connection is globally a two-form with values in this bundle. Trace of vector-bundle curvature (α=trΘ)

The trace of the curvature form of a connection is a global closed differential form of degree two.
