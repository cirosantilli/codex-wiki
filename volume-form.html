Volume form

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 306 Articles: 5
A volume form is a nowhere-vanishing differential form of top degree on an oriented manifold. A metric supplies the canonical local density ∣detg∣​ and hence a metric volume form after choosing orientation.
