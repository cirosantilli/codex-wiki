Finite-variation process

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Semimartingale
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 2
A process A has finite variation on compact intervals when every sample path has finite total variation of a function there. Such a process can be integrated pathwise by the Lebesgue-Stieltjes integral.
