Molecular rotation

 Home Chemistry Molecule
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 9
Molecular rotation changes a molecule's orientation. Its rotational kinetic energy depends on the arrangement and masses of its chemical atoms. A rigid rotor freezes internal distances to isolate this motion; rotational spectroscopy tests the resulting energy eigenvalues through transitions between quantum states. The molecular beam electric resonance experiment provides a complementary probe of the coupling to an electric field.
