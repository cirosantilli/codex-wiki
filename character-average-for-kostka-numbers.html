Character average for Kostka numbers (Kμλ​=∣Sλ​∣−1∑g∈Sλ​​χμ​(g))

OurBigBook.com Words: 70
Over the complex numbers, the multiplicity of Sμ in the Young permutation module Mλ equals Kμλ​. Its character is IndSλ​Sn​​1, so Frobenius reciprocity expresses this multiplicity as the average of the restricted irreducible character over the Young subgroup. For λ=(2,1n−2) that subgroup contains only the identity and one transposition, giving (χμ​(1)+χμ​((12)))/2. For λ=(1n) it is trivial, giving the character degree. This supplies a calculation from ordinary character values without enumerating semistandard tableaux. An indexing convention writing the multiplicity as mλ,μ​ reverses the conventional Kostka indices.
