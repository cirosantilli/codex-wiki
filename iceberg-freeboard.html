Iceberg freeboard (F=H−ρw−1​∫0H​ρi​(z)dz)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Iceberg
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For a vertical-sided floating iceberg of height H, Archimedes' principle equates displaced water mass to ice mass. Its draft is ρw−1​∫0H​ρi​(z)dz and its freeboard is the remaining height above water. A depth-dependent mass density must be integrated; using the densest layer throughout overestimates the draft.
