Schwarzschild tidal force

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Schwarzschild spacetime Radial Weyl curvature of Schwarzschild spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The orthonormal-frame components of Schwarzschild curvature scale as M/r3. The geodesic deviation equation therefore gives radial stretching and transverse compression whose magnitude diverges as r→0.
