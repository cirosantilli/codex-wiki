Derjaguin approximation (F(h)=2πReff​W(h))

 Home Chemistry Mixture Colloid
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
For gently curved interacting surfaces whose range and closest gap are small compared with their radii, integrate the local planar interaction over nearly parallel surface elements. With W(s) the planar interaction free energy per area and Π(s)=−W′(s), the local gap for two spheres is s≃h+r2/(2Reff​), where Reff−1​=a1−1​+a2−1​. Thus F=2π∫0∞​rΠ(s)dr=2πReff​∫h∞​Π(s)ds=2πReff​W(h) when W(∞)=0. Equal spheres have Reff​=a/2; a sphere and plane have Reff​=a. Use consistent surface boundary conditions when supplying the planar interaction.
