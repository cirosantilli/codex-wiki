Dispersion relation of an equal-diffusivity interface dynamo (16η2q4=ikVα)

 ... Star Sun Solar magnetic field Solar dynamo Interface solar dynamo Equal-diffusivity interface dynamo
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
Let p=s−iω and q2=k2+p/η, with Req>0. The upper poloidal amplitude is (A0​+Cz)e−qz and the lower toroidal amplitude is (B0​+cz)eqz. Their forcing equations give 2ηqC=αB0​ and −2ηqc=ikVA0​; interface matching gives C=2qA0​ and c=−2qB0​. Eliminating amplitudes yields the dispersion relation. Its potentially growing branch has s=ηk2(D/32​−1) and ω=−ηk2D/32​ with the convention eikx−iωt. The opposite branch decays.
