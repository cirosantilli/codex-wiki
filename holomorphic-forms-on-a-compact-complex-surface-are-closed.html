Holomorphic forms on a compact complex surface are closed

 ... Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Canonical bundle Holomorphic differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 127 Articles: 1
On a compact complex surface, the claim also holds without a Kähler metric. For a holomorphic one-form α, let β=dα. It is a closed holomorphic two-form, and the Stokes theorem gives ∫β∧βˉ​=∫d(α∧βˉ​)=0. Positivity of β∧βˉ​ forces β=0. Functions are constant on each connected component, and holomorphic two-forms are closed by dimension.
