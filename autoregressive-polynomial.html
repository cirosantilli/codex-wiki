Autoregressive polynomial (Φ(z)=1−∑j=1p​ϕj​zj)

The autoregressive polynomial is Φ(z)=1−∑j=1p​ϕj​zj. Substituting the backshift operator gives the autoregressive filter. Zeros outside the unit disk give the causality root criterion for an autoregressive model; zeros on the unit circle obstruct a nondegenerate stationary innovation-driven solution.
