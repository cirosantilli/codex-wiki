Positive trace index for a compact simple Lie algebra (Tr(d(X)d(Y))=μκ(X,Y),μ>0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For a nontrivial finite-dimensional anti-Hermitian Lie algebra representation of a compact Lie algebra that is simple, its Trace form of a Lie algebra representation is a positive multiple of the Killing form. Define T by H(X,Y)=κ(TX,Y). Invariance makes T commute with the Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra. Symmetry makes T self-adjoint for −κ, so its eigenspaces are ideals. Simplicity implies T=μI. The kernel of the representation is an ideal and hence vanishes; therefore H(X,X)=−Tr(d(X)†d(X))<0 for nonzero X, proving μ>0.
