Radiocarbon dating uses the loss of carbon-14 relative to stable carbon after carbon exchange stops. It can measure either an isotope ratio by accelerator mass spectrometry or radioactive activity per fixed carbon content. Interpreting an age requires radiocarbon calibration and control of contamination and reservoir differences.
