Arithmetic right shift (ASRk​(a)=⌊a/2k⌋)

 Home Computer science Programming language Bit shift
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
An arithmetic right shift propagates the sign bit into the vacated high positions. For a signed two's complement integer, its result is division by 2k rounded down. This differs from C integer division on negative nonmultiples of 2k, because that quotient truncates towards zero. Shifting an n-bit word arithmetically by n−1 produces zero for a nonnegative value and the all-one word, representing −1, for a negative value.
