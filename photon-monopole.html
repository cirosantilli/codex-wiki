Photon monopole (Θ0​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Photon temperature multipole
OurBigBook.com Words: 263 Articles: 6
The photon monopole is the direction-averaged fractional temperature perturbation. In Newtonian gauge its gravitationally observable effective temperature is the Sachs-Wolfe combination Θ0​+Ψ.
