Fractional part ({x})

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Floor and ceiling functions Floor function
OurBigBook.com Words: 130
The fractional part of a real number is
{x}=x−⌊x⌋∈[0,1).
(6)
It is the amount left after removing the lower enclosing integer, even for negative inputs. Thus {−2.7}=−2.7−(−3)=0.3. It is not the signed string of digits obtained by truncation toward zero.
Translation by an integer leaves the fractional part unchanged: {x+m}={x}. It is therefore a periodic function with period one, increasing from zero toward one on each unit interval, then returning to zero at the next integer. In Euclidean division N=kq+r with 0≤r<k, {N/k}=r/k; for 102/20, this is 2/20=0.1.
The notation is useful for following repeated motion modulo an integer. In normality and equidistribution under integer multiplication, the values {Knx} shift the digits of a positional notation; the fractional part discards the leading integer portion without losing the remaining digits. Its braces must be distinguished by context from the braces denoting a set.
