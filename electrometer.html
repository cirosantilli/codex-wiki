Electrometer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge
OurBigBook.com Words: 34 Articles: 1
An electrometer measures small electric potential differences or electric charges while drawing very little electric current. The conducting-pail experiment in electric charge uses a voltage response to infer electric charge with a fixed effective capacitance.
