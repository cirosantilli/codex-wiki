Principal cube root of a Banach-algebra element (x1/3=exp(31​Logx))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra Spectrum of an element Holomorphic functional calculus
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
If the spectrum of an element x avoids (−∞,0], its holomorphic functional calculus value for the principal cube root is the unique y with y3=x and spectrum in {z=0:∣argz∣<π/3}. For uniqueness, any other such v defines a continuous unital algebra homomorphism f↦Θv​(f∘(z↦z3)) from the slit-plane holomorphic functions to the algebra. It sends the coordinate to x, so continuity and uniqueness of holomorphic functional calculus identify it with Θx​. Applying it to the principal cube root gives v=y. This argument does not assume commutativity of the ambient algebra or separation by algebra characters.
