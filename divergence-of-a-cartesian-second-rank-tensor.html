Divergence of a Cartesian second-rank tensor (Ui​=∂j​Tij​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor Cartesian second-rank tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For a constant orthogonal Cartesian coordinate change x′=Rx, a Cartesian second-rank tensor obeys Tij′​=Rik​Rjl​Tkl​. The chain rule gives ∂j′​=Rjm​∂m​. Consequently ∂j′​Tij′​=Rik​Rjl​Rjm​∂m​Tkl​=Rik​∂l​Tkl​, the transformation rule for a vector. Under non-Cartesian position-dependent coordinate changes, partial derivatives must be replaced by the appropriate covariant derivative.
