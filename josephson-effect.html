Josephson effect (Is​=Ic​sinϕ,ϕ˙​=2eV/ℏ)

In the ideal sinusoidal Josephson junction, the pair electric current and quantum phase obey
Is​=Ic​sinϕ,dtdϕ​=ℏ2e​V.
(18)
Here e>0 is the magnitude of the electron electric charge, ℏ=h/(2π) uses the Planck constant, and Ic​ is the junction's superconducting critical current under the specified conditions. Define the signs as follows: positive conventional electric current flows from terminal 2 to terminal 1, and V is the voltage of terminal 2 relative to terminal 1. The Gauge-invariant Josephson phase below fixes the matching orientation of ϕ. The DC Josephson effect and AC Josephson effect follow from these relations. [ref]
