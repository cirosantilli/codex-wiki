Two successive hydraulic controls

 ... Fluid mechanics Reduced gravity Gravity current Froude number Hydraulic control Hydraulic flow in an inverted channel
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
For two same-height throats carrying the same light-layer volume flux and reduced gravity, the narrower throat has the larger critical minimum energy. A narrower downstream throat can back up and submerge an upstream throat. If the first throat is narrower, it can control discharge and feed a supercritical current through the second; a hydraulic jump followed by a second control is also possible when the available energy drop and intermediate geometry permit it. Equal throat widths are a marginal lossless case. Relative width alone does not fix jump position or mixing in the intervening reservoir.
