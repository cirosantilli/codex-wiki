Classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations

 ... Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Simple Lie algebra Special linear Lie algebra sl2 Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 344 Articles: 6
For every n≥0 there is one irreducible sl2​(C)-module V(n) of dimension n+1, with weights n,n−2,…,−n, each of multiplicity one. Every finite-dimensional representation is a direct sum of these modules.
