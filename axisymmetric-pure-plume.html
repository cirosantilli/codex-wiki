Axisymmetric pure plume

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulent plume Top-hat plume model Pure plume
OurBigBook.com Words: 160 Articles: 3
For a top-hat axisymmetric pure plume of radius r, speed U, and reduced gravity g′, the conserved fluxes are Q=πr2U, M=πr2U2, and B=πr2Ug′. The Batchelor entrainment hypothesis gives Q∝B1/3z5/3.
