Isogeny of elliptic curves

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 33
An isogeny between elliptic curves is a nonconstant morphism preserving their group identities; it is finite and surjective with finite kernel.
An isogeny is a nonconstant morphism of elliptic curves preserving their identity points. It is a finite surjective group homomorphism.
