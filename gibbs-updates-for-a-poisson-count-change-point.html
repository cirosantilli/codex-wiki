Gibbs updates for a Poisson count change point

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Change-point detection Poisson count change-point model with gamma priors
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The full conditional distributions are λ∣m,x∼Gamma(α+Sm​,β+m) and ϕ∣m,x∼Gamma(γ+SN​−Sm​,δ+N−m), in shape-rate notation. Conditional on the two rates, m has weights wj​=exp[j(ϕ−λ)+Sj​log(λ/ϕ)]. A Gibbs sampler cycles through these three draws. Each update preserves the joint posterior distribution; subtracting the largest log weight before exponentiation stabilizes the categorical draw. Averages of 1{m=N}​ estimate the posterior probability of no observed change.
