Phasor (A)

 Home Physics Wave Wave amplitude Complex amplitude
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A phasor is a complex amplitude representing a sinusoid at one specified angular frequency: a(t)=Re(Aeiωt). Its modulus gives the peak amplitude and its complex argument gives the wave phase in this convention. Both may instead use root mean square magnitudes if that normalization is stated.
