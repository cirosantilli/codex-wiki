Gibbs free energy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 28
The Gibbs free energy is G=E−TS+pV. For a simple system of fixed composition,
dG=−SdT+Vdp.
