Stellar equation-of-state regime diagram

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 286 Articles: 4
A temperature versus density diagram separates classical, degenerate, nonrelativistic and relativistic stellar-matter limits. The Electron degeneracy boundary is T∼TF​(ρ), with slope 2/3 at low density and 1/3 at high density. Thermal relativity is controlled by kB​T∼me​c2, while relativity of degenerate Electrons is controlled by pF​∼me​c. Photon pressure and thermal electron-positron pairs add further crossovers; all depend on composition and are gradual, not thermodynamic phase boundaries.
