Quasi-likelihood

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Generalized linear model
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
Quasi-likelihood specifies a mean and a variance function without necessarily specifying a full response distribution. For independent responses, the estimating equation is ∑i​(∂μi​/∂β)(yi​−μi​)/(ϕV(μi​))=0. A common scalar dispersion parameter rescales coefficient covariance while leaving the roots of the quasi-score equation unchanged.
