Concave Legendre dual (g(z)=infs​(zs−f(s)))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Convex conjugate
OurBigBook.com Words: 224 Articles: 3
For a differentiable strictly concave function f whose derivative is a bijection of R, define
g(z)=infs∈R​{zs−f(s)}=zh(z)−f(h(z)),h=(f′)−1.
(14)
The unique minimizing point is h(z), and g′=h follows by comparing minimizers at adjacent arguments, even when h is not differentiable. This dual is concave and equals −(−f)∗(−z) in terms of the convex conjugate. If f′′<0, then g′′=1/f′′(h). For f(s)=cs−s2/2, g(z)=−(z−c)2/2.
