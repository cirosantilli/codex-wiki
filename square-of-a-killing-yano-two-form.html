Square of a Killing-Yano two-form (Kab​=Yac​Yb​c)

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Riemannian manifold Killing-Yano tensor Killing-Yano two-form
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
The square is a rank-two Killing tensor. It is symmetric because it pairs the covectors Ya⋅​ and Yb⋅​ using the metric. Along every affinely parametrized geodesic, its contraction K(v,v) is the squared norm of the carried by parallel transport covector Y(v,⋅). Metric compatibility makes that norm constant, proving the Killing tensor equation. On a Riemannian manifold this tensor is positive semidefinite.
