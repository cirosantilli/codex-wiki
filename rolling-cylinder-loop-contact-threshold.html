Rolling-cylinder loop contact threshold (hmin​=11(R−a)/4)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics Rolling without slipping
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 1
A solid cylinder rolling inside a circular loop has centre-path radius ℓ=R−a, kinetic energy 3mv2/4, and speed v2=4g[h−ℓ(1−cosθ)]/3 at angle θ from the bottom. The normal contact force is smallest at the top. Requiring vt2​≥gℓ gives the displayed necessary contact threshold under imposed no-slip rolling. The centre's maximum acceleration on the circle is at the bottom, and at the threshold it is 11g/3 upward. This is a limiting contact condition, not a guarantee for arbitrary finite static friction or an unspecified approach curvature.
