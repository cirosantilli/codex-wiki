Unitary flavour symmetry of degenerate Dirac fields (U(N))

A nondegenerate fermion mass matrix restricts the symmetry to unitary matrices commuting with it. If the theory is entirely free and rotations into charge-conjugate fields are allowed, its enhanced internal symmetry of free Dirac fields can be larger than this subgroup.
