Elastic strain Green operator (ε=E−Γτ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 223 Articles: 4
For a homogeneous comparison elastic stiffness tensor C0, the elastic strain Green operator maps an elastic polarization to minus the induced infinitesimal strain tensor. In a bounded domain the induced displacement field has zero boundary data; in an infinite medium it is the decaying perturbation. The defining static elastic equilibrium equation is ∇⋅(C0sym∇u+τ)=0. The bounded-domain operator is self-adjoint, annihilates constant elastic polarizations, has zero mean output, and satisfies ΓC0Γ=Γ.
