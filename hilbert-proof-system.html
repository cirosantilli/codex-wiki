Hilbert proof system

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Proof theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 155 Articles: 2
A Hilbert proof system specifies axiom schemes and inference rules, commonly including modus ponens. A formal proof is a finite sequence of axiom instances, permitted assumptions and conclusions obtained from preceding lines by those rules. Soundness and completeness compare this syntactic derivability with semantic validity or semantic entailment. These properties concern the particular chosen axioms and rules.
