Nagata theorem for divisor class groups

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Weil divisor Divisor class group Localization sequence for the divisor class group
OurBigBook.com Words: 71 Articles: 1
If A is a Noetherian normal domain and S is a multiplicative set, then Cl(S−1A) is the quotient of Cl(A) by the classes of height-one primes meeting S. Tracking the units of S−1A determines the relations among those prime divisors.
