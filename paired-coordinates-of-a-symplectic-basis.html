Paired coordinates of a symplectic basis (ω(v,w)=qTP−pTQ)

Write vectors in a symplectic basis as v=∑i​(qi​ei​+pi​fi​) and w=∑i​(Qi​ei​+Pi​fi​). Their coordinate vectors and the matrix of a bilinear form are
[v]B​=(qp​),J=(0−In​​In​0​),ω(v,w)=[v]BT​J[w]B​=∑i=1n​(qi​Pi​−pi​Qi​).
(31)
Here In​ is the identity matrix, and T denotes transpose. The ordering matters: interleaving the basis as (e1​,f1​,…,en​,fn​) instead gives a block diagonal matrix with 2×2 blocks (0−1​10​). Some conventions reverse every fi​, giving −J instead. Always check the displayed pairing convention.[ref]
Over the real numbers, each summand is the signed area of a parallelogram in the (qi​,pi​) coordinate plane. For a=(2,1,1,1)T and b=(1,1,0,2)T, the two signed areas are −1 and 1, so ω(a,b)=0 although the vectors are linearly independent. The cancellation concerns the symplectic form; it does not assert that their dot product vanishes. Over a general field, the same determinant formula is algebraic, without a signed geometric interpretation.
