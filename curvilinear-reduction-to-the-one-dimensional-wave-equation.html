In x,y>0, the change of variables in the title reduces
−xyuxx​+uyy​=21​(yux​+yuy​​)
(17)
to the one-dimensional wave equation Uss​−Urr​=0. Indeed uxx​=Urr​/(4x)−Ur​/(4x3/2) and uyy​=yUss​/4+Us​/(4y​); the first-derivative terms cancel exactly against the right-hand side. The independent characteristic coordinates ξ=r+s, η=r−s then give Vξη​=0 and u=g(ξ)+h(η). Two characteristic families alone generally reduce only the principal part; the cancellation of lower-order terms is the extra feature that gives this simple exact solution.
