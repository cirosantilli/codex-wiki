Signal-to-noise ratio (SNR)

 Home Electrical engineering Signal processing Signal
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
A signal-to-noise ratio compares a chosen measure of a signal with the corresponding noise measure. For a constant measured electric potential difference V and a root mean square noise voltage vn​, an amplitude ratio is ∣V∣/vn​; the corresponding power ratio under equal electrical resistance is its square. State the convention and measurement signal bandwidth, since changing either changes the numerical ratio.
