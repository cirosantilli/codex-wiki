Prüfer multiplicities for a full rooted binary tree

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Tree (graph theory) Cayley's formula Prüfer sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
A tree on 2m+1 labels has one root of degree two, m−1 other interior vertices of degree three, and m+1 leaves precisely when its Prüfer code contains one label once, m−1 labels twice, and all other labels zero times. The Prüfer degree formula gives both directions, and identifies the root as the uniquely once-occurring label. Children are unordered unless a plane embedding is additional data.
