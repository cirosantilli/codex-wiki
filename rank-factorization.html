Rank factorization (A=BC,B∈Rm×r,C∈Rr×n)

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix Matrix rank
OurBigBook.com Words: 102
For a matrix of matrix rank r, choose an ordered basis of its column space as the columns of B. Express every column of A in that basis and put its coordinates in C. Then A=BC, B has full column rank and C has full row rank. Choosing independent original columns makes the corresponding columns of C the standard basis vectors, directly proving its rank.
The basis choice is not unique: for any invertible r×r matrix S, the factors BS and S−1C have the same product. Once B is fixed, its coordinate matrix is unique. For rank zero, the empty factors of the displayed shapes represent the zero matrix.
