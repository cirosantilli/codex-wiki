Quasi-Newton method

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Newton's method in optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 568 Articles: 5
A Quasi-Newton method replaces the Hessian matrix in Newton's method in optimization by a matrix Bk​ learned from successive gradients. Solving Bk​sk​=−∇f(xk​) gives a descent direction whenever Bk​ is a positive-definite symmetric matrix and the gradient is nonzero. After choosing a step size, one updates Bk​ to satisfy the Quasi-Newton secant equation, rather than calculating every second derivative again.
