Multiply equilibrium by the displacement and integrate. Integration by parts and the traction condition give
0=∫B​u⋅(∇⋅T+ρg)dV=∫∂B​u⋅σdS−∫B​T:∇udV+∫B​ρu⋅gdV.
(4)
The stress is symmetric, so it contracts only with the symmetric part of ∇u, namely e. Hooke's law then gives T:e=λ(tre)2+2μe:e=2W. Hence
∫B​ρu⋅gdV=∫B​2WdV−∫∂B​u⋅σdS.​
(5)
The identity is the linear-elastic work balance: twice the stored quadratic energy equals the body-force and boundary-traction work paired with the final displacement.
