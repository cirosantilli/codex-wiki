Statistical degrees of freedom (ν)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The number of free dimensions remaining after specified constraints often determines a reference sampling distribution. In a full-rank normal linear model with n observations and p mean coefficients, the residual space has dimension n−p. A regular nested likelihood-ratio test instead uses the difference in free statistical parameter dimensions. Boundary constraints may invalidate this regular calibration.
