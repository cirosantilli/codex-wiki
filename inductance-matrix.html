Inductance matrix (λ=Li)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Inductance Mutual inductance Coupled inductors
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
An inductance matrix relates magnetic flux linkages to electric currents in a linear model of coupled inductors. For reciprocal two-coil coupling, L=(L1​M​ML2​​). Its stored magnetic energy is iTLi/2. With L1​,L2​>0, it is a positive-definite matrix exactly when M2<L1​L2​. Equality is a singular limiting model requiring separate treatment.
