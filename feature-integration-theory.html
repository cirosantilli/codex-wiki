Feature integration theory

 Home Science Psychology Cognitive psychology Attention
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
A theory proposing early registration of separable visual features and a role for focused attention in correctly binding their conjunctions into objects. Visual-search slopes and binding errors are complementary tests, not interchangeable proofs of one serial architecture. The original model already recognized possible top-down constraints on unattended feature combinations.[ref]
