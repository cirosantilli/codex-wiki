Equipotential curve (ϕ(x,y)=C)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Energy Potential energy Equipotential surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
An equipotential curve is a planar level curve of a scalar potential. Where the gradient is nonzero, its tangent is perpendicular to that gradient. In potential flow, it is therefore perpendicular to the velocity field; this distinguishes equipotential curves from streamlines.
