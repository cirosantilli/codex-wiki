Beta integral for strict ordering of coupled Bessel lifetimes (P(σx​<σy​)=I1−x/y​(4a−1,1−2a))

Couple dX=dB+aX−1dt and dY=dB+aY−1dt from 0<x<y, with 1/4<a<1/2. Suppose their finite lifetimes end at zero and ∫0σy​​Yt−2​dt=∞. Their difference is positive and has derivative −a(Y−X)/(XY), so σx​≤σy​. For Θ=(Y−X)/Y, the Itô formula gives
dΘ=−ΘY−1dB+ΘY−2(1−a−1−Θa​)dt.
(15)
The normalized scale function of a one-dimensional diffusion IΘ​(4a−1,1−2a) is a bounded local martingale. Its finite quadratic variation and the divergent Y−2 clock force Θ→0 on simultaneous extinction; on strict extinction Θ→1. Its terminal value is therefore the strict-extinction indicator, proving the formula by optional sampling theorem. For SLE boundary images, a=2/κ; this compares strict and simultaneous boundary swallowing times for 4<κ<8.
