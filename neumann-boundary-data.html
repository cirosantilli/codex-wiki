Neumann boundary data (gN​=∂n​u)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Boundary condition Neumann boundary condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
Neumann boundary data are prescribed values of the outward normal derivative of a function on the boundary of a boundary value problem. They enter a Neumann boundary condition as ∂n​u=gN​. Directional derivatives defined using a fixed coordinate direction must be converted to the outward-normal convention: for example, on the bottom of an upper domain, ∂n​u=−uy​.
