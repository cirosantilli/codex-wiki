Plant

 Home Biology Organism Eukaryote
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 73
Plants are eukaryotes. A typical photosynthetic leaf cell has a cell nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplasts, and a cell wall outside its cell membrane. A root cell need not have chloroplasts; the plant's parts specialize for different functions. Plants perform cellular respiration as well as photosynthesis. See OpenStax Biology 2e, 4.3: plant cell organization.
