Color

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Color vision
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The perceived color of a transmitting sample depends on the illuminating spectrum and the wavelength-dependent transmission. Removing a blue band can make a sample appear yellow; absorption maximum alone does not determine a unique visual shade.
