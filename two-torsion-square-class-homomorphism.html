Two-torsion square-class homomorphism (αT​(P)=[x(P)−x(T)])

OurBigBook.com Words: 68
Let E:y2=f(x) with f monic and separable, and let T=(θ,0). Over K containing θ, the map αT​(P)=[x(P)−θ] for P=O,T, completed by αT​(O)=1 and αT​(T)=[f′(θ)], is a group homomorphism to the square-class group of a field. A nonvertical chord has the product of its three x−θ values a square; a chord through T instead has the product of the other two values equal to f′(θ). These identities include tangencies.
