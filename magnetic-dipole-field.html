Magnetic dipole field

Outside a localized magnetic dipole μ, the vacuum field in Gaussian conventions is
B(r)=r53(μ⋅r)r​−r3μ​.
(15)
For a dipole along the polar axis, Br​=2μcosθ/r3 and Bθ​=μsinθ/r3.
