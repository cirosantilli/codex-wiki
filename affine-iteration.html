Affine iteration (xk+1​=Mxk​+c)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Fixed-point iteration
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
Iterating an affine map gives xk​=Mk−1x1​+∑j=0k−2​Mjc. A fixed point exists exactly when c is in the image of a linear map I−M. If the spectral radius of M is below one, its matrix powers tend to zero and every orbit converges to the unique fixed point (I−M)−1c.
