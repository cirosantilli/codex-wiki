Monodromy group of a covering

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Holomorphic function Analytic continuation Monodromy theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 251 Articles: 2
For a covering map p:X→Y and base point y, the path lifting theorem lifts each based loop from each point of the fibre p−1(y). Its endpoint defines a permutation of that fibre; path reversal gives the inverse. The homotopy lifting theorem for a covering map makes the permutation depend only on the based homotopy class. These permutations form the monodromy group, the image of the fundamental group action
π1​(Y,y)⟶Sym(p−1(y)).
(28)
The image is independent of the convention for composing routes; one may reverse the loop classes to obtain a left group action when route multiplication means following the first route and then the second.
For p(w)=w2 on the punctured plane, the fibre over 1 is {1,−1}, and a once-around based loop exchanges its two points. The monodromy group has two elements: only the parity of the winding number matters. For the covering map p(w)=ew from the complex plane to the punctured plane, the fibre over 1 is 2πiZ, and a positive turn acts by 2πik↦2πi(k+1). Its monodromy group is an infinite cyclic group. These are the geometric versions of logarithm and square-root continuation along different paths: the base point returns, while its lifted endpoint can move to another sheet.
