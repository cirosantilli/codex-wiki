Centralizer lower bound for a root vector

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Centralizer of an element of a Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
In a complex semisimple Lie algebra whose rank of a semisimple Lie algebra is ℓ, a root vector x satisfies dimZg​(x)≥3ℓ−2. To see this, select ℓ−1 roots whose images form a basis of the real root span modulo Rα, where x∈gα​. Take the highest endpoints of the root strings through both signs of each selected root. Their 2(ℓ−1) distinct root spaces commute with x. Together with kerα⊂t and Cx, these give 2(ℓ−1)+(ℓ−1)+1 independent vectors in the Lie algebra centralizer.
