Correlation time (τc​)

 ... Probability and statistics Time series Stationary process Weakly stationary process Autocovariance Autocorrelation
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A correlation time measures how rapidly a stationary stochastic process loses temporal dependence. Specify whether it means an exponential decay time, an integral of the normalized autocorrelation, or another width. For R(t)=R(0)e−∣t∣/τ, the exponential time and the one-sided integral ∫0∞​R(t)dt/R(0) both equal τ.
