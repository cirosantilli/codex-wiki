Uniform convergence of Bernstein polynomial derivatives (∥Bn(r)​f−f(r)∥∞​→0)

 ... Analysis Functional analysis Stone-Weierstrass theorem Weierstrass approximation theorem Bernstein polynomial Derivatives of Bernstein polynomials
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For fixed r and f∈Cr[0,1], uniform convergence of normalized finite differences to f(r), uniform continuity of f(r), and (n)r​/nr→1 imply gn,r​→f(r) uniformly. Positivity and partition of unity make Bernstein polynomials contractions in the supremum norm. Consequently ∥Bn−r​gn,r​−f(r)∥∞​≤∥gn,r​−f(r)∥∞​+∥Bn−r​f(r)−f(r)∥∞​→0. The differentiability assumption is essential; continuity alone does not define the target derivative.
