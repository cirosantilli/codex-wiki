Scale-free potential and bulk-flow variance (σΦ​(R)∝R(1−n)/2,σv​(R)∝R−(n+1)/2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 101
For a matter power spectrum P(k)=Akn at a fixed epoch, the Poisson equation gives potential Fourier amplitudes proportional to δk​/k2, and the growing-mode peculiar velocity is proportional to δk​k/k2. With a spherical top-hat window function, their variances are proportional to ∫kn−2W(kR)2dk and ∫knW(kR)2dk, respectively. Rescaling kR gives the displayed powers when the integrals converge: 1<n<5 for the averaged potential and −1<n<3 for the bulk velocity. Absolute averaged potential has infrared sensitivity; band-limited fluctuations or potential differences can have different convergence requirements. The density variance converges only for −3<n<1, so these three absolute averages cannot all converge for one unbroken power law at every scale.
