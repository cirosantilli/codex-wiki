Weak geometric p-rough path (X:[0,T]→G⌊p⌋(Rd))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Rough path
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
A weak geometric p-rough path is a continuous path in the free step-N nilpotent Lie group, with N=⌊p⌋, whose increments have finite p-variation for the Carnot-Carathéodory distance. Its multiplicative increments are Xs,t​=Xs−1​⊗Xt​. They obey the Chen identity and the algebraic symmetries of path signatures.
