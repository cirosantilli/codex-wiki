Island of a meromorphic function

 ... Isolated singularity Classification of isolated singularities Pole Meromorphic function Nevanlinna theory Ahlfors covering surface theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
An island over a Jordan domain D is a relatively compact component U of its inverse image on which the meromorphic function is a proper map onto D. A component reaching the boundary of the source is instead a peninsula. The island has an integer mapping degree; degree one means that the map is a biholomorphism. A simply connected island can have larger degree and need not be a simple island.
