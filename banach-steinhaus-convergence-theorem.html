Banach-Steinhaus convergence theorem

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Normed vector space Banach space Uniform boundedness principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
Let X be a normed space, Y a Banach space, and An​:X→Y be bounded linear operators with C=supn​∥An​∥<∞. Convergence of An​d for every d in a dense subset D implies convergence of An​x for every x∈X to a bounded linear operator A, with ∥A∥≤liminfn​∥An​∥. Indeed, ∥An​x−Am​x∥≤2C∥x−d∥+∥An​d−Am​d∥ makes (An​x) a Cauchy sequence; completeness gives its limit, and linearity and the bound pass to that limit. If X is a Banach space, pointwise convergence conversely implies the uniform bound by the Uniform boundedness principle. If the limit bounded linear operator is specified in advance, completeness of Y is unnecessary.
