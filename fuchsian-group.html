Fuchsian group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 91 Articles: 2
A Fuchsian group is a discrete subgroup of PSL2​(R), acting by orientation-preserving isometries of the hyperbolic plane. It is non-elementary when it has no finite orbit in the hyperbolic plane or its ideal boundary.
