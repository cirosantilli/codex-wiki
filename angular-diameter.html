Angular diameter

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Angle
OurBigBook.com Words: 81 Articles: 1
The angle subtended by an object at an observer. For a circular object of diameter D viewed face-on at a distance d much larger than D, its angular diameter is approximately D/d in radians.
