Periodic utility indifference payment for Gaussian income (y​=a−μρb/σ−γb2(1−ρ2)/2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For Gaussian income of mean a, standard deviation b and tradable correlation ρ, exponential utility makes the maximum acceptable per-period fee equal to the mean minus the correlated-income hedge cost and the residual variance penalty. Compare optimized utilities with and without the contract; a fee above this level makes the contract strictly inferior. At perfect correlation the residual penalty vanishes, leaving only the mean and hedge-cost terms.
