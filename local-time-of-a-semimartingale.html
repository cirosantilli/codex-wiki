Local time of a semimartingale (Lta​(X))

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Itô formula Tanaka formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
Local time measures how intensely a semimartingale visits a level. For a continuous semimartingale it appears as the increasing correction term in the Tanaka formula.
