Stellar mass function with a square-root exponential cutoff (n∗​(M∗​)∝M∗−7/4​e−M∗​/M∗,ch​​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy stellar mass Galaxy stellar mass function
OurBigBook.com Words: 90 Articles: 1
A generalized mass function with low-mass slope −7/4 and cutoff e−M∗​/M∗,ch​​ differs from the ordinary exponential Schechter cutoff. Its total stellar-mass density is finite, even though its untruncated total galaxy count diverges. This form arises from a scale-free Press-Schechter mass function with a constant stellar-to-halo mass mapping.
