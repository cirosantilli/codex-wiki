The escape-rate Green function is
Gf​(z)=limn→∞​d−nlog+∣fn(z)∣.
(3)
It vanishes on the filled Julia set, is positive and harmonic on the basin of infinity, satisfies Gf​∘f=dGf​, and equals log∣ϕf​∣ near infinity and by continuation throughout the basin.
