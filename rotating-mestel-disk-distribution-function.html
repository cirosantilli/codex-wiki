Rotating Mestel disk distribution function (f=ALzq​e−E/s,q=vc2​/s−1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Mestel disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
In the midplane Mestel disk potential Φ=vc2​log(R/R0​), take specific energy E=(vR2​+vϕ2​)/2+Φ and Lz​=Rvϕ​. A prograde galactic distribution function f=ALzq​e−E/s for Lz​>0, with s>0, has surface density proportional to Rq−vc2​/s. Matching Σ=Σ0​R0​/R gives q=vc2​/s−1>−1 and
A=π​2q/2s(q+2)/2Γ((q+1)/2)Σ0​R0−q​​.
(19)
The denominator is the product of the full radial Gaussian integral and the positive-azimuthal half-line moment. Here s is a squared-velocity scale, not a velocity dispersion itself. Changing the potential's additive constant changes A. With positive exponent eε/s, ε must instead be the negative of the specific total energy.
