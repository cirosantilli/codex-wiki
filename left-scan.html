Left scan (scanl :: (b -> a -> b) -> b -> [a] -> [b])

 Home Computer science Programming language Functional programming Left fold
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
Return the successive accumulator values of a left fold, including the initial accumulator: scanl f e [] = [e] and scanl f e (x:xs) = e : scanl f (f e x) xs. The kth result is the fold of the first k elements, proved by the fold equation. Thus the scan equals map (foldl f e) . inits, but computes n updates instead of n(n+1)/2 on a finite input of length n. Accumulator strictness and the cost of an update remain separate from this operation count.
