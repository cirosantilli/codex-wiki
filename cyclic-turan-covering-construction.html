Cyclic Turán covering construction (((ℓ−1)/r)ℓ−1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Hypergraph Extremal hypergraph theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
With r balanced cyclically ordered vertex classes and 2≤ℓ≤r+1, include an ℓ-set if some start has at least k+1 vertices in its first k classes for every 1≤k≤ℓ−1. The cycle lemma implies every (r+1)-set contains an edge. Its asymptotic density is ((ℓ−1)/r)ℓ−1; taking the hypergraph complement gives a lower bound for the Turán density of a complete uniform hypergraph.
