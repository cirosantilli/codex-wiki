Mean-field critical exponent

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Landau theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
For an ordinary quartic Landau transition, the heat-capacity and order-parameter exponents are α=0 and β=1/2. At a tricritical sextic point they are α=1/2 and β=1/4.
