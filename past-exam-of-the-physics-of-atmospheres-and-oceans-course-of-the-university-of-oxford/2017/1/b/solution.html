For the Gaussian profile, with τ0​=mS/(π​ΔD​)≫1, absorption extends to ∣δν∣≃ΔD​logτ0​​, so E∼2ΔD​logτ0​​. For a strongly saturated pure Lorentz profile the wings instead give E∼2mSΔL​​.
