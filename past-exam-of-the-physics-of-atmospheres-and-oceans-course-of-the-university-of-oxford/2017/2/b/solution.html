Using the adiabatic cloud droplet moments, define N=∫n(r)dr and the mean volume radius by rv3​=N−1∫r3n(r)dr. Summing the liquid mass of spherical droplets gives
w=34πρw​​∫r3n(r)dr=34πρw​​Nrv3​.
(18)
Therefore, with the shallow-cloud liquid-water content w=cw​h,
rv​(h)=(4πρw​N3cw​h​)1/3.​
(19)
The growth is h1/3 at fixed number concentration, and the radius scales as N−1/3 at fixed water content. The dimensions are consistent: cw​ has units kgm−4, so cw​h/(ρw​N) has units m3. The formula is a third-moment radius; a monodisperse distribution is not required. Fixed N per volume is the shallow-cloud approximation, not exact conservation of number per parcel volume during an arbitrarily deep expansion.
