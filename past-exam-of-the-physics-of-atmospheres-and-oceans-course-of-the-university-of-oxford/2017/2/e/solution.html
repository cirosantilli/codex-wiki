The two transmissions account approximately for the atmosphere above the cloud and may be absorbed into an effective illuminating flux. In the simplest global transparent-overburden estimate Fsw​=L⊛​/4. Positive sa​ gives increased reflection and negative shortwave forcing, the Twomey effect. Numerical forcing cannot be determined from the given susceptibility alone: it needs baseline albedo, cloud fraction, illumination and an empirically constrained sa​. Humidity swelling, cloud contamination of aerosol retrievals and common meteorological influences can create spurious correlations. Changes in cloud amount, water path or lifetime are additional adjustments, not the fixed-water Twomey term derived here.
