Third body and steady partition. The third molecule removes excess association energy and momentum, stabilizing newly formed ozone rather than being consumed in the net reaction. Its decreasing abundance with altitude suppresses three-body ozone formation strongly. Write x=[O], y=[O3​], a=k1​n2​nM​, j=J3​ and b=k3​, with n2​,nM​ held as reservoirs. The kinetic equations are
x˙=2J2​n2​−ax+jy−bxy,y˙​=ax−jy−bxy,dtd(x+y)​=2J2​n2​−2bxy.
(20)
The fast association/photolysis subsystem conserves odd oxygen, x+y, and has eigenvalues 0,−(a+j). Its partition-adjustment time is therefore
τF​≃a+j1​=k1​n2​nM​+J3​1​​.
(21)
This neglects the slower production and mutual-destruction terms while finding that fast mode. On its equilibrium partition, ax≃jy, and total odd-oxygen equilibrium gives bxy=J2​n2​. Hence
x≃(bk1​nM​J2​j​)1/2,y≃n2​(bjJ2​k1​nM​​)1/2,yx​≃k1​n2​nM​j​.
(22)
With fixed oxygen mole fraction and temperature, the atomic-oxygen mixing ratio scales as x/nM​∝nM−3/2​ in this approximation, and thus increases upward as pressure decreases.
Lifetime versus relaxation. The distinction is formalized by Chapman family lifetime versus relaxation time. In the ozone-dominated regime y≫x, the family abundance is approximately y, and its gross chemical lifetime is
τOx​​≃2bxyy​=2bx1​=21​(J2​J3​k3​k1​nM​​)1/2​.
(23)
This recovers the printed τS​ as a family turnover time under its additional partition assumption. It is not generally an e-folding relaxation time. With rapid partitioning, the family equation is X˙=P−kX2. Linearizing about equilibrium gives relaxation rate 2kXe​ but turnover rate kXe​, a factor of two difference. More generally the approximate family lifetime is (x+y)/(2bxy), and its relaxation time is half that quantity. Neither can be replaced by 1/(2bx) when atomic oxygen dominates.
For a direct check using the complete two-species equations, the equilibrium Jacobian matrix is
J=(−a−bya−by​j−bx−j−bx​).
(24)
The numerical high-altitude example below gives eigenmode times about 321 s and 4.0×105 s, whereas the printed τS​ is only 5.15×103 s. Thus interpreting both printed expressions as universal eigenmode time constants would be false, even though the fast partition and the ozone-dominated turnover approximations are useful.
Setting x=y in the fast-partition approximation gives a=j, hence
nM​≃0.2k1​J3​​​,p≃kB​T0.2k1​J3​​​=12.3 Pa=0.123 hPa​.
(25)
Retaining mutual destruction gives a=j+bx and changes this estimate by less than one percent. The formal high-altitude limit of τF​ is 1/J3​≃333 s: ozone photolysis, unlike three-body association, does not slow merely because collider density decreases. This is a partition timescale, not the lifetime of all odd oxygen. Extrapolating the fixed-reservoir approximation indefinitely upward would eventually cease to be self-consistent.
Day and night. At p=0.01 hPa=1 Pa and 230 K, nM​=p/(kB​T)=3.15×1020 m−3 and n2​=0.2nM​. The exact equilibrium is conveniently obtained from
k1​nM​x2−J2​x−k3​J2​J3​​=0,y=k3​xJ2​n2​​.
(26)
The physical root gives
x=9.97×1016 m−3,y=6.38×1014 m−3,χO​=3.17×10−4,χO3​​=2.03×10−6​.
(27)
Their ratio is 156, consistent with the requested approximate factor 150; the simpler fast-partition estimate gives approximately 151. The printed fast time is 331 s at this pressure.
For nighttime Chapman ozone, after sunset set both photolysis rates to zero. Ozone initially grows by converting atomic oxygen, but simply putting every initial oxygen atom into one ozone molecule neglects the simultaneous O+O3​ sink. The night equations are
x˙=−x(a+by),y˙​=x(a−by).
(28)
While sufficient atomic oxygen remains, the ozone balance tends toward
y∼a/b,χO3​​∼k3​k1​n2​​=6.36×10−5​.
(29)
This is a useful long-night sunrise estimate, roughly thirty times the sunset ozone abundance, not an exact result for an unspecified night duration. The precise phase-plane relation is
x−x0​=y−y0​+b2a​ln(a−by0​a−by​)(y<a/b).
(30)
Together with the night equations it supplies y(tnight​) for any supplied duration. For illustration only, a twelve-hour dark interval gives χO3​​≃5.8×10−5; no twelve-hour duration is stated by the paper. Even the infinite-dark-time endpoint is slightly below a/b, because the finite initial atomic reservoir is consumed. This conditional estimate avoids inventing a unique sunset-to-sunrise duration.
