Put K=(k2+m2)1/2 and P=p′/ρ0​. Acting on the Fourier mode, D0​=−i(ω^−Uk)=−iω. Continuity and horizontal momentum give u=−mw/k and P=ωu/k. The buoyancy equation gives gρ′/ρ0​=iN2w/ω. Substitution into vertical momentum yields
ω2=k2+m2N2k2​,ω^=Uk±KNk​.​
(43)
The two signs describe the two frequency branches; when the intrinsic frequency is required positive, ω=N∣k∣/K. For a stationary disturbance ω^=0, so ω=−Uk>0. With U>0 this requires k<0 and selects the minus sign, ω^=Uk−Nk/K. The stationary dispersion condition is
K=UN​,m2=U2N2​−k2.​
(44)
Differentiate the unconstrained dispersion before imposing stationarity. The stationary mountain-wave group velocity is obtained from the ground-frame dispersion; before imposing stationarity it is
c^gx​=U−K3Nm2​,c^gz​=K3Nkm​.
(45)
On the stationary locus this becomes
cg​​=K2Uk​(k,m),c^gx​=K2Uk2​,c^gz​=K2Ukm​.​
(46)
Differentiating after substituting K=N/U as though all neighbouring modes were stationary would incorrectly erase the frequency gradients.
For upward energy propagation from the lower boundary, choose m<0, since k<0 and c^gz​>0. It requires real nonzero m, hence
0<∣k∣<N/U⟺λx​>2πU/N.​
(47)
The downstream ray inclination above horizontal is
θ=tan−1∣k∣∣m∣​=cos−1NU∣k∣​=cos−1Nω​.​
(48)
It is fixed for a given monochromatic forcing, with the two signs of m giving the upward/downward rays.
Frame qualification for the requested phase angle: the normal intrinsic phase velocity is cp​=ω(k,m)/K2. Because ω=−Uk, the boxed stationary group velocity is exactly −cp​: its angle to intrinsic phase propagation is π, and it is perpendicular to the stationary phase lines. By contrast the intrinsic group velocity cg​=cg​​−Ux^ satisfies cg​⋅(k,m)=0, so its angle to intrinsic phase propagation is π/2. In the ground frame the phase velocity of a strictly stationary pattern is zero and has no nonzero propagation direction. For example k=−0.6N/U, m=−0.8N/U gives cg​​=(0.36U,0.48U) and cp​=(−0.36U,−0.48U). These distinctions prevent applying the usual zero-flow perpendicularity rule to two velocities expressed in different frames.
