The incident stellar power is intercepted over a disk and distributed over the spherical surface. With a black shortwave surface, no clouds and no shortwave atmospheric absorption, the global mean absorbed flux is F∗​=L⊛​/4. Define Te​=(F∗​/σ)1/4.
To relate a radiating-level temperature to the ground, assume the intervening well-mixed gas follows a dry adiabat with constant Rg​,cpg​:
T(p)=Ts​(ps​p​)κg​,κg​=cpg​Rg​​.
(13)
Emission from prad​ balances absorbed sunlight when σT(prad​)4=F∗​. Hence
Ts​=(4σL⊛​​)1/4(prad​ps​​)Rg​/cpg​≃Te​τ∞Rg​/cpg​​.​
(14)
The final form uses the optically thick result in part (a). Since prad​<ps​, the surface is warmer than the effective emission temperature. Efficient horizontal redistribution alone does not mathematically fix the vertical lapse rate: the adiabatic/convective assumption is needed to obtain the requested dependence on Rg​/cpg​. A different imposed vertical profile would give a different relation.
