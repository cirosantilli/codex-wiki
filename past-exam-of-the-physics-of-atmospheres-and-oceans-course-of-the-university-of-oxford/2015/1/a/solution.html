Let b denote buoyancy in a stably stratified Boussinesq approximation. Geostrophic balance and hydrostatic balance imply the thermal wind relation f0​Uz​=−bˉy​. Consequently the specified vertical shear requires
bˉy​=−f0​Λ,bˉz​=N2>0​.
(1)
For northern-hemisphere f0​>0 and Λ>0, the mean potential temperature decreases northward and increases upward: Θˉy​=−(Θ0​/g)f0​Λ, Θˉz​=(Θ0​/g)N2. The corresponding mean isentropes slope upward toward the north, with dz/dy=f0​Λ/N2. Stable potential-temperature stratification does not require the actual temperature to increase upward; the dry adiabatic lapse also enters that comparison.
In the rigid-lid constant-shear baroclinic model, perturbation quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity is
q′=∇h2​ψ′+N2f02​​ψzz′​.
(2)
The mean interior potential-vorticity gradient vanishes on this constant-f0​, constant-N, linear-shear basic state. Linear advection of that perturbation therefore becomes (∂t​+Λz∂x​)q′=0, the supplied interior equation. It retains slow balanced motion and omits friction, planetary-vorticity gradients and nonlinear disturbance advection.
Substitution of ψ′=Ψ(z)cos(ly)eik(x−ct) gives
ik(Λz−c)[N2f02​​Ψ′′−(k2+l2)Ψ]=0.
(3)
A possible branch has zero interior perturbation potential vorticity, so
Ψ′′−K2Ψ=0,Ψ=Acosh(Kz)+Bsinh(Kz),K=∣f0​∣Nk2+l2​​​.
(4)
Both supplied applications have f0​>0, so this equals the source's expression. For an unstable mode the nonzero imaginary part of the phase speed prevents a real critical level Λz=c; for real speeds the displayed solution remains a valid zero-interior-PV branch away from, and smoothly through, any critical level. Taking the real part gives the physical disturbance.
The rigid lids enforce w′=0. Linearized adiabatic buoyancy conservation is
(∂t​+Λz∂x​)b′+v′bˉy​+N2w′=0.
(5)
Use the geostrophic conventions v′=ψx′​ and b′=f0​ψz′​. At a lid this gives (∂t​+Λz∂x​)ψz′​−Λψx′​=0, hence
−cΨ′(0)−ΛΨ(0)=0,(ΛH−c)Ψ′(H)−ΛΨ(H)=0​.
(6)
These are the no-normal-flow condition expressed through boundary buoyancy advection, not conditions of zero horizontal velocity. Boundary temperature anomalies can interact even though this solution has no interior perturbation PV, allowing baroclinic instability to extract mean available potential energy.
