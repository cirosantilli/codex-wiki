The cloud absorbs eσTg4​ from below and emits eσTc4​ from each face. It absorbs no stellar radiation. Its net radiative heating per unit area is therefore
Rc​=eσ(Tg4​−2Tc4​)=eσTg4​(1−2q4).​
(16)
For e>0, a cloud with q4<1/2 warms in the absence of other energy transfers; one with q4>1/2 cools. For the thin pressure thickness δp, its initial temperature tendency is approximately gRc​/(cp​δp) if its sensible heat capacity dominates. Latent heat and changing condensate modify that conversion from heating to temperature change, but not the sign of the radiative input.
With Tg​ fixed, pure radiative equilibrium requires
Tc,rad​=2−1/4Tg​.​
(17)
This equals the ambient dry-adiabatic process temperature precisely when
q4=21​,ps​pc​​=2−1/(4κ)​.
(18)
For ordinary dry air, κ≃2/7, so the ratio is about 0.545. If e=0, the cloud exchanges no thermal radiation and its radiation budget does not select any equilibrium temperature.
