Write u=U+u′, v=v′, and ξ=ξ′, with U=uˉ constant. Discarding products of perturbations in the vorticity equation gives
(∂t​+U∂x​)ξ′+βv′=0.

Introduce the streamfunction convention

(47)
It enforces incompressibility identically. The linear partial differential equation is
(∂t​+U∂x​)∇h2​ψ′+β∂x​ψ′=0.
(48)
For the plane wave ψ′=Re{ψ​exp[i(kx+ly−ωt)]}, put K2=k2+l2>0. Substitution yields
i(ω−Uk)K2ψ​+iβkψ​=0,ω=Uk−k2+l2βk​.​
(49)
The first term is the Doppler shift by the background flow; the second is the intrinsic Rossby wave frequency. The source's convention ω≥0 selects representatives of real waves, rather than making this expression nonnegative for arbitrary k,l. For U=0 and β>0, nonzero positive frequency requires k<0; the conjugate mode has opposite signs of k,l,ω and describes the same real field.
