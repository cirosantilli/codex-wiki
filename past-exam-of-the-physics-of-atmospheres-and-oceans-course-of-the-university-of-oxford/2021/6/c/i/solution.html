The streamlines are contours of ψ. Since A<0, the exact streamfunction is negative inside the rectangle and zero on its perimeter. It has one interior minimum, with closed anticlockwise streamlines: a broad northward interior flow returns southward along the west wall; flow is westward near the north edge and eastward near the south edge. The western parts of the streamlines are closely packed because the western boundary current is much faster.
The figure uses the exact ordinary differential equation solution, so it also remains consistent with both wall conditions when the asymptotic scale separation is only moderate. It depicts the stipulated dynamical model, not observed winds or a reconstruction of the source illustration.
