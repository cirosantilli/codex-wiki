The first equation expresses horizontal incompressibility. The other two express steady balance of the Coriolis force, a geopotential gradient, linear drag and a prescribed zonal acceleration Fu​(y); nonlinear advection has been neglected. The parameter r>0 is an inverse drag time, so the frictional acceleration is −r(u,v). On the equatorial beta plane, f=βy.
Use the streamfunction convention
u=−ψy​,v=ψx​,ζ=vx​−uy​=∇h2​ψ.
(58)
Take ∂x​ of the meridional momentum equation minus ∂y​ of the zonal one. The mixed geopotential derivatives cancel, and
f(ux​+vy​)+βv=−r(vx​−uy​)−dydFu​​.
(59)
Horizontal incompressibility removes the first term, leaving
βψx​+r∇h2​ψ=Fζ​(y),Fζ​=−dydFu​​.​
(60)
The right side is the vertical curl of the imposed acceleration, with units s−2. The left side balances planetary vorticity advection and frictional removal of relative vorticity. The streamfunction has units m2s−1, since it describes horizontal velocity, rather than depth-integrated volume flux.
