Both curves fall from +∞ as T→0+, pass through Y=0 at the triple point, and are convex. At the triple point there is a corner because the latent heat changes from Ls​ to L∗​. Their right-hand slopes there agree. Above the triple point,
Yvariable′​=−KT2Tc​−T​,Yfixed′​=−Rwv​T2L∗​​,Yvariable′′​=KT32Tc​−T​>0.
(7)
The variable-latent heat curve lies above the fixed-latent heat curve: its downward slope has smaller magnitude at every T>T∗​. It reaches a horizontal tangent at Tc​, whereas the fixed-latent heat curve is still descending. Both limiting ordinates are finite; this model gives Yvariable​(Tc​)≃−7.30 and Yfixed​(Tc​)≃−11.40. These are extrapolations of the stipulated model, not accurate water liquid-gas critical point data. Extending the variable branch beyond Tc​ would give negative latent heat and is physically inadmissible.
