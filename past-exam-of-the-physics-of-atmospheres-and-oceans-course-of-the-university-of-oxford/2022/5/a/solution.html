The constants Ra​,Rb​,Rd​ are effective reservoir capacities: stored carbon dioxide mass anomaly per unit effective concentration anomaly, in GtCO2​ per ppmv. Only Ca​ is a literal atmospheric mole fraction; Cb​,Cd​ are scaled reservoir variables chosen to share its equilibrium value. The exchange coefficients r,s are conductances, in GtCO2​ per ppmv per year: r(Ca​−Cb​) is transfer from the atmosphere to the fast reservoir and s(Cb​−Cd​) transfer from that reservoir to the deep ocean. They are not by themselves inverse times; division by a reservoir capacity gives an inverse-time rate.
Hydrostatic balance estimates total atmospheric mass as Mair​=4πRE2​pˉ​/g. One ppmv of a dilute ideal gas is a mole fraction 10−6, so, with dry-air molar mass Mmol,air​≃28.97 g/mol and carbon dioxide molar mass 44.01 g/mol,
