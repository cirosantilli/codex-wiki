Geodesic flow

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Geodesic Exponential map (Riemannian geometry)
OurBigBook.com Words: 462 Articles: 8
The geodesic flow sends a tangent vector v to the velocity at time t of the unique geodesic with initial velocity v. Smooth dependence of solutions of the geodesic equation makes it a smooth flow on the tangent bundle wherever defined.
