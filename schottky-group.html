Schottky group

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Discrete subgroup Kleinian group
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 1
A Schottky group is a free, discrete group of Möbius transformations constructed by pairing disjoint Jordan curves on the Riemann sphere, taking the exterior of one bounded disk to the interior of its partner. For round circles it is classical. Strictly separated defining disks give a Cantor set as the limit set of a Kleinian group. The tangent-disk extension permits parabolic elements and is often called a noded or T-Schottky group; its Kleinian limit set need not be a Cantor set.
