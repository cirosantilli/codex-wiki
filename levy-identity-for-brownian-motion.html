Lévy identity for Brownian motion

OurBigBook.com Words: 35
For a standard Brownian motion B, its Brownian running maximum M, and its local time of a semimartingale L0 at zero, the process pairs satisfy
(Mt​−Bt​,Mt​)t≥0​=d(∣Bt​∣,Lt0​)t≥0​.
(40)
In particular, M−B and ∣B∣ are both Reflected Brownian motion and have the same law as processes.
