Like-charge attraction between conductors

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electrostatic induction
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
Conductors with net charges of the same sign can attract because their internal charge distributions respond to one another. A point-charge calculation misses this electrostatic induction contribution. Exact conducting-sphere results are given in Electrostatics of Two Charged Conducting Spheres by John Lekner.[ref]
