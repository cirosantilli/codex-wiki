Spectrum of an element (σA​(a))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 30
The spectrum of a in a unital complex algebra A is
σA​(a)={λ∈C:a−λ1 is not invertible}.
(2)
For a nonunital algebra it is defined in the unitization of an algebra.
