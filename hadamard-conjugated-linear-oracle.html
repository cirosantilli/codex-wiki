Let Ua​∣x⟩∣b⟩=∣x⟩∣b⊕(a⋅x)⟩ be the Boolean quantum oracle for a linear Boolean function, and put W=H⊗(n+1). The CNOT gates Cj→yaj​​ implement Ua​, where the data wires control the ancilla qubit. CNOT control reversal in the Hadamard basis and W2=I imply the exact unitary operator identity
WUa​W=∏j=1n​Cy→jaj​​,WUa​W∣x⟩∣b⟩=∣x⊕ba⟩∣b⟩.

Here ⊕ on strings is bitwise exclusive or. To conjugate a product, insert W2=I between its factors; each factor reverses its control and target, and the unused Hadamard gates cancel. The global phase is exactly 1. In particular, the identity holds on arbitrary quantum superpositions, not only on the initialized inputs of the Bernstein-Vazirani algorithm.
