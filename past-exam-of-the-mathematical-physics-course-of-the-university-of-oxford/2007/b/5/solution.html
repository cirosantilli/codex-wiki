Use metric signature (+−−−) and the curvature sign fixed by the stated Newtonian limit. In flat spacetime the source-free Maxwell equations are ∂a​Fab=0 and ∂[a​Fbc]​=0. The stress-energy tensor in mixed form is Tab​=FacFcb​+δba​Fcd​Fcd/4. Its divergence is
∂a​Tab​=(∂a​Fac)Fcb​+Fac∂a​Fcb​+41​∂b​(Fcd​Fcd).
(17)
Contracting the cyclic Maxwell equation with Fac gives Fac∂a​Fcb​=−∂b​(Fcd​Fcd)/4. The first term also vanishes, proving ∂a​Tab=0​. The component b=0 expresses local electromagnetic energy conservation, and the spatial components express momentum conservation. Integrating converts them into the usual energy and momentum flux balances.
In curved spacetime the tensor equations are
∇a​Fab=0,∇[a​Fbc]​=0.​
(18)
They agree with the flat equations in coordinates of a local inertial frame at a point, where the connection vanishes, and covariance then extends them to arbitrary coordinates. Antisymmetry makes the homogeneous equation equivalent to dF=0. Metric compatibility permits the same contraction proof, now giving ∇a​Tab=0​. In general,
∂a​Tab=−Γaac​Tcb−Γbac​Tac,
(19)
which need not vanish. Covariant local balance remains valid; ordinary coordinate components do not provide a universal conserved global energy-momentum vector in a curved geometry. Additional spacetime symmetries can furnish conserved currents. A locally inertial frame removes the connection at a point, not the curvature or the global obstruction.
The alternating array with fixed component ϵ0123​=1 is appropriate in oriented inertial special-relativistic coordinates, whose proper Lorentz transformations have determinant one. It is not itself a covariant tensor under general coordinate changes. For an oriented Lorentzian metric, replace it by the metric metric volume tensor εabcd​=−detg​[abcd]. The genuine dual is
(∗F)ab​=21​εabcd​Fcd.​
(20)
This is the Hodge star; the determinant factor, orientation and raising conventions are essential. Even in flat spacetime arbitrary curvilinear coordinates require the metric volume tensor, so the distinction is about coordinate covariance rather than curvature alone.
The isolated equation for R00​ singles out a coordinate component and cannot be a generally covariant law. Replacing it by a proportionality Rab​∝Tab​ still fails: the contracted Bianchi identity gives ∇aRab​=∇b​R/2, whereas stress-energy conservation requires ∇aTab​=0. Such a law would force R constant and hence impose an unphysical restriction on a varying relativistic dust density. Missing units or a coupling constant in the proposed proportionality would not repair this structural problem.
The Einstein tensor Gab​=Rab​−Rgab​/2 is divergence-free. Therefore
Gab​=−8πGTab​​
(21)
is consistent with covariant stress-energy conservation in the source's curvature convention. Its trace gives R=8πGT and hence Rab​=−8πG(Tab​−Tgab​/2). For slowly moving relativistic dust with VaVa​=1, T00​≃ρ, T≃ρ, g00​≃1, so R00​≃−4πGρ, exactly matching −Δϕ and Poisson's equation.
Finally, electromagnetic stress-energy is trace-free: Taa​=FacFca​+Fcd​Fcd=0 by antisymmetry. The traced field equation then gives R=0​ for a purely electromagnetic source. This does not say Rab​=0 or that spacetime is flat; its trace-free Ricci curvature can still be nonzero.
