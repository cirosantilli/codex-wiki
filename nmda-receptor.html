NMDA receptor

 Home Biology Neuroscience Neuron Synaptic integration
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
An NMDA receptor is an ionotropic receptor gating an ion channel with voltage-dependent magnesium block. This voltage dependence permits cooperative amplification when inputs produce sufficient local depolarization; calcium can also pass through the open channel.[ref]
