Finite-horizon extension of a boundary transform (G(k,t)⟼G(k,T),T>t)

 ... Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Boundary value problem Global relation for a linear boundary value problem Finite-time spectral boundary transform
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
In a Fokas method boundary integral over ∂D+​, where Reω<0 inside, the contribution of replacing G(k,t) by G(k,T) is an integral of eikx∫tT​eω(k)(s−t)g(s)ds times an holomorphic coefficient. For x>0 it vanishes by the Cauchy integral theorem and the exponential decay in the upper domain. The transformed amplitude is then independent of the observation time, which simplifies differentiation of the partial differential equation. Temporal inversion at 0<t<T recovers the full boundary value rather than the half-value of a truncated transform at its endpoint.
