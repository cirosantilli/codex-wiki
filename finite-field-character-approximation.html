Finite-field character approximation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Almost period of a function
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
Sampling the normalized Fourier expansion of f:Fpn​→C gives an average of O(q/ϵ2) phase-adjusted characters whose Lq error is at most ϵ∥f​∥1​. The common kernel of those characters is therefore a low-codimension subspace of almost periods of f.
