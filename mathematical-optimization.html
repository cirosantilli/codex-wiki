Lagrange sufficiency theorem

Words: 53
Mathematical optimization OurBigBook.com
For maximizing f subject to gi​(x)≥0 and hj​(x)=0, suppose a feasible x∗ and multipliers λi​≥0,μj​ satisfy complementary slackness and x∗ globally maximizes
L(x)=f(x)+∑i​λi​gi​(x)+∑j​μj​hj​(x).
(1)
Then x∗ globally maximizes f on the feasible set. Convexity or concavity hypotheses are commonly used to prove the required global extremum of the Lagrangian from first-order conditions.
