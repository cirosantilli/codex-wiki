Let 0<a<b and impose zero Dirichlet boundary conditions at a,b. With the convention Lx​G(x,s)=δ(x−s) for the Dirac delta distribution, the Green's function is continuous and its first derivative has jump one. For n=0 choose ℓ(x)=x−n−a−n and r(x)=x−n−b−n. Their Wronskian is n(a−n−b−n)s−n−1, so
G(x,s)=n(a−n−b−n)sn+1(x<−n​−a−n)(x>−n​−b−n)​,x<​=min(x,s),x>​=max(x,s).

For n=0, take the continuous limit or use ℓ=log(x/a) and r=log(x/b) to obtain
G(x,s)=log(b/a)slog(x<​/a)log(x>​/b)​.
(26)
The solution is u(x)=∫ab​G(x,s)f(s)ds. These kernels use ordinary measure ds. The factor sn+1 comes from the nonconstant Wronskian and must not be discarded by confusing the original operator with its multiplied divergence form (xn+1u′)′.
