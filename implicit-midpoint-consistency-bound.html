Implicit midpoint consistency bound (∥τn​∥≤(M3​/24+LM2​/8)h2)

 ... Analysis Numerical analysis One-step method Runge-Kutta method Implicit Runge-Kutta method Implicit midpoint rule
OurBigBook.com Words: 105
For an exact solution y′=f(t,y), define the normalized local truncation error of the implicit midpoint rule by
τn​=hy(tn​+h)−y(tn​)​−f(tn​+2h​,2y(tn​+h)+y(tn​)​).
(8)
If ∥y′′∥≤M2​, ∥y′′′∥≤M3​ and the state Jacobian matrix of f has operator norm at most L on the intervening state segments, then the displayed bound holds uniformly on a fixed finite interval. To prove it, put t=tn​+h/2. Symmetric Taylor expansions give
​hy(t+h/2)−y(t−h/2)​−y′(t)​≤24M3​h2​,​2y(t+h/2)+y(t−h/2)​−y(t)​≤8M2​h2​.
(9)
For the first bound, integrate the second-order remainder of y′(t+s) over the symmetric step; for the second, average the second-order remainders of y at the endpoints. The odd terms cancel. The Lipschitz condition for f and y′(t)=f(t,y(t)) finish the proof by the triangle inequality. The unnormalized step residual is hτn​=O(h3).
