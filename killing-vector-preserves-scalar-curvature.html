Killing vector preserves scalar curvature

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Killing vector field Killing equation Second covariant derivative of a Killing vector
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
Every Killing vector field K preserves the scalar curvature: LK​R=Kα∇α​R=0. One index proof rewrites this derivative as a doubly contracted curvature commutator acting on the antisymmetric tensor ∇[μ​Kλ]​; both terms vanish by symmetry of the Ricci tensor.
