Conditional regression coefficients from a Gaussian precision matrix (θjk​=−Ωjk​/Ωkk​)

 ... Probability theory Expected value Variance Covariance Covariance matrix Precision matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
In a Gaussian graphical model, regressing component k on the others gives the displayed coefficients, residual variance 1/Ωkk​ and intercept μk​−μ−kT​θ−k,k​. Opposite directions have identical zero patterns and signs because Ω is symmetric with positive diagonal, but generally unequal coefficient magnitudes.
