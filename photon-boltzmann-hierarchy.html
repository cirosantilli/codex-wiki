Photon Boltzmann hierarchy (Θℓ​ hierarchy)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Free-streaming photon Boltzmann equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 134 Articles: 3
Expanding the direction-dependent photon temperature in Legendre polynomials turns its transport equation into an infinite hierarchy. Free streaming couples each multipole Θℓ​ only to its neighbours Θℓ−1​ and Θℓ+1​.
