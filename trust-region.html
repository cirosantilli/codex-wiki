Trust region (∥s∥≤Δ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 372 Articles: 6
A trust region restricts a local approximation to a neighborhood where it is expected to model the objective function accurately. A quadratic model is m(s)=f(x)+gTs+21​sTHs, where g=∇f(x) and H is a symmetric matrix approximating the Hessian matrix. Minimizing this quadratic function over a Euclidean ball of radius Δ>0 is the trust-region subproblem. The trust-region acceptance ratio then checks the model against the actual objective function.
