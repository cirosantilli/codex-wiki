Source-to-far-field operator at fixed frequency (Tk​:L2(A)→L2(S1​))

 Home Physics Acoustics Acoustic inverse source problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 2
For (Δ+k2)ψ=Q, the outgoing Green function for the three-dimensional Helmholtz equation yields the far-field pattern
(Tk​Q)(θ)=−4π1​∫A​e−ikθ⋅rQ(r)d3r,∣θ∣=1.
(2)
The sign corresponds to the Green function satisfying (Δ+k2)G=−δ. This Hilbert-Schmidt operator samples the source Fourier transform on the sphere ∣ξ∣=k; for a ball of radius r0​, its squared Hilbert-Schmidt norm is r03​/3.
