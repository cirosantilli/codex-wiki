Buscher dilaton shift (φ′=φ−21​loggyy​)

 ... Two-dimensional conformal field theory Free boson conformal field theory Compact boson Momentum and winding modes T-duality Buscher procedure
OurBigBook.com Words: 126 Articles: 1
The quantum measure in abelian T-duality changes the conventional dilaton by φ′=φ−21​loggyy​ in alpha-prime-one units for a spacelike isometry. The regulated Gaussian integration over the auxiliary gauge field produces this curvature coupling. It is consistent with invariance of the string-frame density e−2φdetg​ under the local Buscher rules.
