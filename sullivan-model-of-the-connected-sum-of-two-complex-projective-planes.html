Sullivan model of the connected sum of two complex projective planes ((Λ(a2​,b2​,r3​,s3​),dr=ab, ds=a2−b2))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Rational homotopy theory Sullivan minimal model
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The two quadratic relations form a regular sequence in Q[a,b], so their Koszul complex has only the quotient-ring cohomology. Represent the two degree-two classes by rational polynomial forms and choose primitives for the exact relations to obtain a quasi-isomorphism. There are no further generators.
