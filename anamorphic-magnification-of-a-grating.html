Anamorphic magnification of a grating (∣dβ/dα∣=cosα/cosβ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Diffraction grating Grating equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 1
At fixed wavelength, the grating equation gives dβ/dα=−cosα/cosβ. Thus an incident angular width is magnified by cosα/cosβ in the dispersion direction. Including the ratio of camera and collimator focal lengths gives the projected slit width. The ESO B&C operating manual, Appendix A explicitly includes this anamorphic factor.
