Magnetic pressure (pB​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 2
Magnetic pressure is the isotropic part of magnetic stress. It is pB​=B2/(2μ0​) in SI units and B2/(8π) in Gaussian units.
