Finite difference

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 103
A finite difference compares the values of a function at finitely separated arguments. For example, the forward finite difference with step h is
Δh​f(x)=f(x+h)−f(x).
(1)
It is the discrete analogue of a derivative and lowers the degree of a nonconstant polynomial by one.
