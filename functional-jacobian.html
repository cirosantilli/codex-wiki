Functional Jacobian (J[ϕ]=det[δF(x)/δϕ(y)])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Quantized field Gaussian functional integral Functional determinant
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For a change of field variables ϕ↦F[ϕ], the functional Jacobian is the functional determinant of the linearized change. At a finite regulator it is an ordinary Jacobian determinant on the finitely many field coordinates; the continuum expression denotes the regulated limit. It changes the functional integral measure and can depend on the background field. The Faddeev-Popov determinant is the example obtained by replacing the integration coordinate along a gauge orbit by a chosen gauge fixing condition.
