Exponential tightness upgrades a weak large deviation principle

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Convergence of random variables Large deviation principle Weak large deviation principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
Suppose a weak large deviation principle with rate function I holds on a Hausdorff space, at speed an​→∞, and the random elements are exponentially tight. For a finite r, choose a compact set K whose complement has upper exponential rate strictly below −M, with M>r. The open-set lower bound on Kc implies infKc​I>M, so {I≤r}⊂K. Lower semicontinuity makes this sublevel set closed, hence compact. For any closed set F, split its probability between the compact F∩K and Kc. The upper exponential rate is at most max{−infF​I,−M}. Letting M→∞ proves the full large deviation principle with a good rate function.
