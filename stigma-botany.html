Stigma (botany)

 Home Biology Organism Eukaryote Plant Flower
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
The stigma is the carpel's pollen-receiving region. Compatible pollen may form a pollen tube that grows toward an ovule; arrival alone does not establish fertilization.[ref]
A useful diagnostic question is what was actually observed: pollen on a stigma, a growing pollen tube, or a new plant embryo? Each observation tests a different stage. A pollen tube germination assay isolates the middle process for direct viewing.
