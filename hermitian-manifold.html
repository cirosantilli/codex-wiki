Hermitian manifold

 ... Geometry and topology Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 297 Articles: 7
A Hermitian manifold is a complex manifold whose tangent bundle carries a smoothly varying positive-definite Hermitian form. Its real part is a Riemannian metric invariant under the complex structure J.
