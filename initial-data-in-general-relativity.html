Initial data in general relativity

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Causal hierarchy Globally hyperbolic spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 2
Gravitational initial data on a spatial manifold consist of a Riemannian metric h and an extrinsic curvature K satisfying the Hamiltonian constraint and momentum constraint. Matter theories require their own data and constraints as well. The data determine a maximal Cauchy development.
