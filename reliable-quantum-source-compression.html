Reliable quantum source compression

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Schumacher compression
OurBigBook.com Words: 259 Articles: 3
A quantum source-compression scheme has encoding and decoding quantum channels with compressed space Kn​ and rate limsupn​n−1log2​dimKn​. For a pure-state source ensemble, average reliability means that the mean squared quantum fidelity after decoding tends to one. A stronger coherent criterion is Fe​(π⊗n,Dn​∘En​)→1, where entanglement fidelity also tests a purification reference. Encoding the quantum typical subspace achieves both criteria above S(π). Schumacher's original quantum coding paper establishes quantum source coding.
