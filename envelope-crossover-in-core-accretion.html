Envelope crossover in core accretion

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planet formation Core accretion Runaway gas accretion
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The epoch when the gaseous envelope mass becomes comparable with the solid core mass. Envelope self-gravity then changes structure and can accelerate gas capture through cooling and contraction. Crossover is a useful stage in classical models, rather than a universal core mass independent of opacity and accretion history.
