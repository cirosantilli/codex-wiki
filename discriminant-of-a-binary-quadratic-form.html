Discriminant of a binary quadratic form

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Binary quadratic form
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
The discriminant of [a,b,c] is D=b2−4ac. It is invariant under proper equivalence, and a real form with a>0 is positive definite exactly when D<0.
