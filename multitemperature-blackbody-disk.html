Multitemperature blackbody disk

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Accretion disk Alpha disk Shakura--Sunyaev thin disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 124 Articles: 2
A multitemperature blackbody disk is an optically thick disk approximated as concentric blackbody annuli of different effective temperatures. If T∝R−3/4, its intermediate-frequency spectrum satisfies Sν​∝ν1/3.
