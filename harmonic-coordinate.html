Harmonic coordinate

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Numerical relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 183 Articles: 6
A harmonic coordinate satisfies Δg​xλ=0 for a Riemannian metric, or □g​xλ=0 for a Lorentzian metric. Equivalently gμνΓμνλ​=0 or ∂μ​(∣detg∣​gλμ)=0. This common coordinate condition applies in both Riemannian geometry and general relativity.
