Haar scaling function (ϕJ,k​=2J/21IJ,k​​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Wavelet Orthonormal wavelet Haar wavelet
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
At level J, a Haar scaling function is the normalized indicator function ϕJ,k​=2J/21IJ,k​​ of the dyadic interval IJ,k​=[k2−J,(k+1)2−J). Its L2 norm is one because its squared height is 2J and its support length is 2−J.
For a real square-integrable function f, its coefficient is
⟨f,ϕJ,k​⟩=2J/2∫IJ,k​​f(x)dx=2−J/2f​IJ,k​​.
(50)
Multiplying the coefficient by the Haar scaling function restores the cell average on that interval. The disjoint supports make the functions at one level an orthonormal basis of the cellwise-constant subspace. This normalization is also what turns coefficient variances in the Gaussian white noise model into 1/n and cellwise variances into 2J/n in the Haar projection estimator in Gaussian white noise.
