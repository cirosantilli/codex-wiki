Cartan's magic formula (LX​=dιX​+ιX​d)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form Lie derivative of a differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
The Lie derivative of a differential form satisfies LX​=dιX​+ιX​d. Both sides agree on functions. On a differential 1-form η, evaluation at a vector field Y gives X(η(Y))−η([X,Y]) on both sides, by the definition of the exterior derivative and the Lie bracket of vector fields. Both operators are degree-zero derivations of an algebra on the exterior algebra. Agreement on functions and coordinate differential 1-forms therefore proves agreement on every differential form. Since d2=0, the formula also gives LX​d=dLX​, so the Lie derivative of a differential form commutes with the exterior derivative.
