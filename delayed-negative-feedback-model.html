Delayed negative feedback model (d)

 Home Biology Signal transduction Signaling feedback Negative feedback
OurBigBook.com Words: 278 Articles: 1
A feedback delay is a time interval between an event and its returning influence. Multiple biochemical steps can introduce an effective lag; a fixed delay is an idealization, not a universal description of signal transduction. To isolate timing, let x be a signed displacement from an equilibrium point and use normalized dimensionless variables:
dtdx​=−x(t)−2x(t−d),d≥0.
(7)
The local removal is −x and the returning influence is negative at every delay. At d=0 the ordinary differential equation reduces to the decay already illustrated under signaling feedback. For d>0 a delay differential equation requires a history on [−d,0], not just an initial condition at one instant. Negative values of x are displacements, not negative chemical concentrations.
An exponential function x=eλt gives the characteristic equation of a delay differential equation
λ+1+2e−λd=0.
(8)
Putting λ=iω and equating real and imaginary parts gives cos(ωd)=−1/2 and ω=2sin(ωd). The first crossing with ω>0 therefore occurs at
ω=3​,dc​=33​2π​≃1.20920.
(9)
For this particular mathematical model, the equilibrium point is asymptotically stable for 0≤d<dc​. No nonnegative real delayed feedback characteristic root can satisfy the equation; the first loss of asymptotic stability is the indicated pair of imaginary delayed feedback characteristic roots. Differentiating the characteristic equation of a delay differential equation along a root yields
dddλ​=−1+d(λ+1)λ(λ+1)​,Re(dddλ​)d=dc​​=(1+dc​)2+3dc2​3​>0.
(10)
Thus the pair moves into growth as the delay increases through dc​. The neutral mode at dc​ is a periodic function, but its amplitude depends on history: this linear mathematical model does not select an attracting limit cycle.
The figure plots x(t)=eatcos(bt) using a delayed feedback characteristic root λ=a+ib and that same formula on the history interval. At d=0.7 the displayed mode decays; at d=1.5 it grows. These are exact modes up to numerical root accuracy, not step responses with identical histories. Molecular bounds and a changed return law would be needed to interpret what happens after an unstable biological response becomes large.
