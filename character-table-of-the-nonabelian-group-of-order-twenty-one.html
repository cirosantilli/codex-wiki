For the nontrivial action of C3​ on C7​, the conjugacy class sizes are 1,7,7,3,3. Three linear characters factor through the quotient C3​, and the remaining two irreducible characters have degree three. Put ω=e2πi/3 and let α,β=(−1±i7​)/2. With suitable class labels the table is

One can derive the last two rows by inducing nontrivial characters of C7​ along the two orbits of the order-three automorphism on their exponents. Their degree-three orbit sums vanish off C7​. On a generator they give the two sums of seventh roots over quadratic residues and nonresidues. Their sum is −1 and their product is 2: the nine pairwise exponent sums between the two triples contain zero three times and every nonzero residue once. Thus they are the roots of X2+X+2. Alternatively column orthogonality forces the zeros on the centralizers of order three, and orthogonality with the identity column interchanges α and β in the last row. This finite table illustrates how centralizer sizes and a small number of given values determine missing character values.
