Capillary depression

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Surface tension Capillary action Jurin's law
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A negative signed capillary rise for a nonwetting liquid: the equilibrium contact angle exceeds a right angle, giving cosθ<0 in Jurin's law. Correcting a mercury-column measurement requires a consistent meniscus and surface condition.
