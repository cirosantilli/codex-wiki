Open-loop gain (A(s))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Electronic amplifier Operational amplifier
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 1
The open-loop gain of an operational amplifier is the ratio of its output voltage to its differential input voltage before an external feedback control connection is applied. A common small-signal model is A(s)=A0​/(1+s/ωa​), with a large direct-current gain A0​ and a dominant pole at −ωa​. Above ωa​ its magnitude falls approximately inversely with angular frequency.
