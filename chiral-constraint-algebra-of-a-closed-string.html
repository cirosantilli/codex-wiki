Chiral constraint algebra of a closed string (Diff1​⊕Diff1​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For a closed string, the currents J±​=P±TX′ have vanishing mixed Poisson brackets. The densities H±​=J±2​/(4T) therefore generate commuting constraint algebras. With opposite Fourier orientations their modes satisfy {Lm​,Ln​}=−i(m−n)Lm+n​ and the identical relation for Ln​. This is a direct sum of Lie algebras consisting of two Witt algebras.
