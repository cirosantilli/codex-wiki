Binomial random hypergraph (H(r)(n,p))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Hypergraph Uniform hypergraph
OurBigBook.com Words: 169 Articles: 1
A binomial random r-uniform hypergraph on n labelled vertices includes each of the (rn​) possible hyperedges independently with probability p. Thus indicators of different possible edges are independent Bernoulli random variables. A fixed set of k vertices spans a complete r-uniform hypergraph with probability p(rk​), and the expected number of such complete sets is (kn​)p(rk​). Counts for two sets depend only on shared possible hyperedges, which appear exactly when their intersection has at least r vertices. For r=3,p=1/2, balancing the logarithm of this expectation gives the largest-clique scale k of order logn​; expectation alone proves the upper obstruction, not the corresponding existence assertion.
