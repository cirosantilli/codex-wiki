Residual-preserving clipping of an ROF minimizer (w=TM​u,g=f−u+w,w∈argminv​{αTV(v)+21​∥v−g∥22​})

If u minimizes scalar total variation denoising with f∈BV∩L2, set w=TM​u, r=u−w and g=f−r. The scalar total variation splitting under clipping gives TV(u)=TV(w)+TV(r). Compare u with v+r and use TV(v+r)≤TV(v)+TV(r); cancelling the tail shows that w minimizes the same model for g. It is bounded while g may be unbounded, and w−g=u−f exactly. This reduction concerns the actual minimizer, rather than convergence of minimizers for truncated data.
