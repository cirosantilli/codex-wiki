Adjoint Dirac equation (i(∂μ​Ψˉ)γμ+mΨˉ=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Dirac equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
Varying the Dirac action with respect to the spinor, rather than its Dirac adjoint, gives the left-acting field equation after integration by parts. Together with the Dirac equation it proves conservation of the vector current.
