Classical left ring of quotients (Qcll​(R)={c−1r:c regular, r∈R})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Noncommutative algebra Ore condition Ore localization
OurBigBook.com Words: 169 Articles: 1
This is the localization of a ring at all its regular elements of a ring, with left fractions. It exists precisely when those denominators satisfy the left Ore condition. If R embeds in a ring Q, all regular elements become units in Q, and every element of Q is such a fraction, the Ore condition follows by writing rc−1=s−1b, which gives sr=bc.
