Folded conductance-model nullcline

 Home Biology Neuroscience Computational neuroscience Hodgkin-Huxley model Planar reduction of the Hodgkin-Huxley model
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
For g(v,n)=γK​(v+vK​)n4−(1−v)(H−n)M(v), with rapidly increasing saturating activation M, the zero-current nullcline satisfies n4/(H−n)=(1−v)M(v)/[γK​(v+vK​)] in the physical strip. The left side increases strictly, so it defines one height for each voltage. Since gn​>0, its slope is −gv​/gn​. Rapid activation can make gv​ negative between two positive-slope current regimes, giving two descending attracting branches and an ascending repelling branch in the voltage–recovery phase plane. Sufficient activation steepness is a parameter condition; the shape is not forced by monotonicity alone.
