For a prime number p, the ring of p-adic integers is the inverse limit
Zp​=lim​n​Z/pnZ.
(85)
Every p-adic integer has a unique convergent expansion ∑j≥0​bj​pj with 0≤bj​<p. It is a complete discrete valuation ring with uniformizer p and residue field Fp​.
