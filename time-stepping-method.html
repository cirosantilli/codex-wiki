Time-stepping method

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 81 Articles: 1
A time-stepping method approximates the evolution of an ordinary differential equation by successive updates at discrete times. It can be applied after a spatial finite element method has converted a time-dependent partial differential equation into an ordinary differential equation system. The implicit time-stepping method is one class of such methods.
