Nuclear mass defect (Δm=Zmp​+Nmn​−Mnuc​)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Isotope Atomic mass
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
The mass deficit of a bound atomic nucleus relative to its separated protons and neutrons. If their rest masses are mp​ and mn​, the atomic nucleus has rest mass Mnuc​, and the positive nuclear binding energy is B, then
Δm=Zmp​+Nmn​−Mnuc​=c2B​,
(17)
with c the speed of light. Releasing energy when the nucleons bind reduces the rest mass of the bound system without changing the exact count A=Z+N. A neutral atomic mass also includes Z electron rest masses, reduced by the energy required to separate those bound electrons. Consequently A−ma​/u is not the definition of the nuclear mass defect: the reference unit and constituent rest masses must also be accounted for.
