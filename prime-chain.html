Prime chain (P0​⊊⋯⊊Ps​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Krull dimension
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A strictly increasing finite sequence of prime ideals. Its length is the number of strict inclusions, so the displayed chain has length s. The Krull dimension of a ring is the supremum of such lengths; the height of a prime ideal restricts to chains ending at that prime.
