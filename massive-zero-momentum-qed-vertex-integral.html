Massive zero-momentum QED vertex integral (α=(D−2)2/D, β=2−D)

OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For the QED proper Feynman vertex with both external four-momenta zero, a massive internal fermion and a massless internal photon in Feynman gauge, the even numerator reduces under Lorentz covariance to γμ[αk2+βm2]. The gamma matrices identity γνγμγν​=(2−D)γμ and the integrated replacement kρ​kσ​↦gρσ​k2/D prove the coefficients. The term m2/[k2(k2−m2+i0)2] has neither an infrared divergence nor an ultraviolet divergence for m>0; its integral in four dimensions is −i/(16π2). Only the remaining squared massive denominator supplies the ultraviolet pole.
