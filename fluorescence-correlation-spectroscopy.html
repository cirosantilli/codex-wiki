Fluorescence correlation spectroscopy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Fluorescence
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
fluorescence correlation spectroscopy analyzes fluctuations of detected fluorescence to infer quantities such as molecular number and movement through an observation volume. Photophysical fluctuations and background must be distinguished from transport.
