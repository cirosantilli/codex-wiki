Ricker population map (Nt+1​=Nt​er(1−Nt​/K))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
The Ricker population map models density-dependent reproduction with a declining per-capita growth factor. Its nonzero equilibrium is K, with multiplier 1−r. It loses stability through a period-doubling bifurcation at r=2. Every population after the first update is bounded by Ker−1/r, the maximum of the update function on nonnegative populations.
