Canonical scroll of a trigonal curve (C∈∣3M+(4−g)L∣)

The rank-two bundle of fibre spans is globally generated and splits as O(a1​)⊕O(a2​) with a1​+a2​=g−2. On its rational normal scroll, L∣C​ is the degree-three pencil and M∣C​=KC​. The adjunction formula then forces the displayed divisor class. Writing a=a2​−a1​ and M=B+a2​L on the Hirzebruch surface gives C∈∣3B+(a+a2​+2)L∣.
