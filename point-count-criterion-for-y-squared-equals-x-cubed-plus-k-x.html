For odd p and k≡0(modp), take E:y2=x3+kx. If p≡3(mod4), pairing x with −x cancels the Legendre symbols. If p≡1(mod4), the coefficient formula for trace of Frobenius modulo p gives
ap​≡((p−1)/4(p−1)/2​)k(p−1)/4≡0(modp).

The binomial coefficient is nonzero because every factorial involved is shorter than p. Thus the Trace of Frobenius is not zero in the second case.
