Uniaxial magnetic anisotropy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetism Magnetic anisotropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A magnetic energy preference for an axis, with the two opposite directions equivalent in a purely uniaxial model. An easy-axis energy can be written Ksin2θ for K>0. A unidirectional term distinguishes the two directions and can instead shift a hysteresis.
