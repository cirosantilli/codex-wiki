Weierstrass approximation does not preserve derivatives

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Stone-Weierstrass theorem Weierstrass approximation theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
On [0,1], the polynomials pn​(x)=xn/n converge in supremum norm to the zero function, since ∥pn​∥∞​=1/n. But pn′​(1)=1 for every n, whereas the zero function has left derivative zero at that endpoint. Thus uniform convergence of function values does not imply uniform convergence of derivatives. This does not contradict uniform convergence of Bernstein polynomial derivatives, which has a particular approximation construction and additional differentiability hypotheses.
