Amount of substance (n)

 Home Physics Physical quantity
OurBigBook.com Words: 154 Articles: 2
Amount of substance measures a count of specified entities, such as chemical atoms, molecules, ions, or Electrons, using the mole as its SI base unit. It differs from mass: equal amounts of substance of different chemical substances contain equal entity counts but generally have different masses. If N entities are present, n=N/NA​, where NA​ is the Avogadro constant. The specified entity matters: one mole of water molecules contains two moles of hydrogen chemical atoms. [ref]
