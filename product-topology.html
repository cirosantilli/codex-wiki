Product topology

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 28
The product topology turns separate observations into a notion of closeness: a basic test checks finitely many coordinates, with a separate tolerance in each. For an index set I and topological spaces Xi​, the underlying Cartesian product and its coordinate projection maps are
X=∏i∈I​Xi​={(xi​)i∈I​:xi​∈Xi​},πi​:X→Xi​,πi​(x)=xi​.
(57)
The product topology is the coarsest topology making every πi​ a continuous map.[ref]
The Cartesian product specifies which points exist; the product topology specifies their neighborhoods. Finite observations motivate its finite-coordinate basis for the product topology, while convergence in a product space and continuity into a product space explain why it is useful. The compactness result for this construction is Tychonoff's theorem.
