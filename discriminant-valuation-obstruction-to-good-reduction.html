Discriminant valuation obstruction to good reduction (vp​(Δ)≡0(mod12)⟹bad reduction at p)

OurBigBook.com Words: 83
Discriminants of isomorphic Weierstrass equations of an elliptic curve differ by a twelfth power of the scale parameter, so their valuations differ by a multiple of twelve. An integral model with good reduction has discriminant valuation zero. Thus a displayed integral model with discriminant valuation not divisible by twelve proves intrinsic bad reduction. More generally, a displayed discriminant valuation between zero and eleven is already minimal, since any smaller integral-model valuation would be nonnegative and differ by twelve. Positive valuations divisible by twelve alone do not establish bad reduction; rescaling may remove them.
