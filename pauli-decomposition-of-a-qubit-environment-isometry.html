Every linear map from a qubit into the tensor product of that qubit and an environment can be expanded in the Pauli matrix orthonormal basis of operators. If V∣0⟩=∣0⟩∣e00​⟩+∣1⟩∣e01​⟩ and V∣1⟩=∣0⟩∣e10​⟩+∣1⟩∣e11​⟩, then the four coefficient vectors are
∣e0​⟩=2∣e00​⟩+∣e11​⟩​,∣e1​⟩=2∣e01​⟩+∣e10​⟩​,∣e2​⟩=2i(∣e10​⟩−∣e01​⟩)​,∣e3​⟩=2∣e00​⟩−∣e11​⟩​.
(5)
These environment vectors need not be orthogonal, so this is an operator expansion rather than a claim that the quantum channel is a classical Pauli channel. Physical isometries additionally preserve the inner products of the two input basis states.
