Infinite-horizon singularity of Brownian motion with constant drift (Pμ​⊥P0​ (μ=0))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Brownian motion with drift
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
The laws on continuous paths of a standard Brownian motion and a Brownian motion with drift μ=0 are mutually singular measures on the whole half-line. The event limt→∞​ω(t)/t=0 has full measure for the former and zero measure for the latter by the strong law for Brownian motion. On every fixed finite horizon, their laws are equivalent by the Girsanov theorem. The finite-horizon densities eμBt​−μ2t/2 tend to zero almost surely while retaining expectation one, so they lack uniform integrability over all time.
