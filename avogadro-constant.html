Avogadro constant (NA​=6.02214076×1023mol−1)

 Home Physics Physical quantity Amount of substance
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The Avogadro constant converts amount of substance into a dimensionless entity count: N=nNA​. Its value is exact under the SI definition of the mole. [ref]
