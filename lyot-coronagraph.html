Lyot coronagraph

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Coronagraph
OurBigBook.com Words: 940 Articles: 4
A classical Lyot coronagraph first forms an image of a bright source on the optical axis. A coronagraphic focal plane mask hides its core. Relay optics then form an image of the entrance pupil, where a Lyot stop blocks the outer rim and, when needed, borders around internal obstructions. Finally an optical camera forms the science image. The focal plane sorts incoming directions into image positions; the optical pupil sorts contributions across the aperture. This change of plane makes it possible to reject additional stellar light that survives the first mask. [ref].
