Chiral projector (PL,R​=(1∓γ5)/2)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Weyl spinor Chirality (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 81 Articles: 1
The projectors PL​=(1−γ5)/2 and PR​=(1+γ5)/2 select the two chirality eigenspaces of a Dirac field. They satisfy PL​+PR​=I, PL​PR​=0, and PL2​=PL​, PR2​=PR​.
