Hour angle (h)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Equatorial coordinate system
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The hour angle of a direction is the westward angle from the upper celestial meridian to its hour circle. In a consistent equinox convention, h=LST−α modulo one turn, where α is right ascension and LST is local sidereal time.
