Thom class (uE​)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Orientation of a vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 21
A Thom class of an R-oriented rank-d vector bundle is a class uE​∈Hd(D(E),S(E);R) restricting to the chosen generator on every fiber pair (Dd,Sd−1).
