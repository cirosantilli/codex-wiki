Cross-dispersed echelle spectrograph

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Spectrometer Spectrograph
OurBigBook.com Words: 59 Articles: 2
A cross-dispersed echelle spectrograph uses an echelle grating for strong dispersion and a cross-disperser in the perpendicular direction to separate overlapping diffraction orders. It records many orders simultaneously in an echellogram.
