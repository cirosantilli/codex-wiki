Randomized rounding

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 236 Articles: 4
A method turning a solution of a continuous optimization relaxation into a random feasible solution of a discrete problem. An expected objective bound can give an approximation guarantee. Gaussian hyperplane rounding and Rademacher rounding for a semidefinite relaxation are different examples.
