Commutative monoid

 ... Algebra Algebraic operation Binary operation Associative operation Semigroup Monoid
OurBigBook.com Words: 386 Articles: 7
A commutative monoid is a monoid whose operation is commutative. The natural numbers with addition form a commutative monoid that is not an abelian group, because positive elements have no additive inverses.
