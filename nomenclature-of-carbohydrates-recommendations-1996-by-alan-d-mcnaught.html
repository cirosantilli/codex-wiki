Document prepared by Alan D. McNaught for the IUPAC-IUBMB Joint Commission on Biochemical Nomenclature. The inspected landing page distinguishes the printed document’s preparation from the World Wide Web version prepared by G. P. Moss. Queen Mary University of London hosts the web version. Pure and Applied Chemistry 68, 1919–2008, is the 1996 publication; the listed 1997 reprints are distinct.
