Electron-phonon Umklapp threshold

OurBigBook.com Words: 102
For a spherical Fermi surface of radius kF​ lying inside a zone with shortest reciprocal lattice vector magnitude G>2kF​, a translated neighbouring Fermi sphere is separated from it by qU​=G−2kF​. An electron-phonon Umklapp scattering process satisfying k+q=k′+G therefore needs a phonon wavevector of at least qU​. For an approximately linear acoustic dispersion its activation scale is
TU​∼kB​ℏvs​(G−2kF​)​.
(47)
At T≪TU​ these phonons are sparsely occupied; once they become thermally available, momentum-relaxing scattering can grow rapidly. Normal small-angle scattering and residual disorder need not vanish below this scale, so it is a crossover estimate rather than a sharp universal conductivity threshold.
