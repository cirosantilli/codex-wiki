Thus a “ten times the lower resistor” rule does not guarantee one-percent agreement with the unloaded target.

For equal 10kΩ arms, Rth​=5kΩ, so one-percent loading error requires RL​≥495kΩ. Reducing both divider electrical resistances reduces loading but increases unloaded supply electric current and Joule heating: P=Vs2​/(R1​+R2​). The stipulated equal-arm example dissipates 5mW in total when unloaded. This tradeoff explains why the ratio alone does not determine a useful divider.
