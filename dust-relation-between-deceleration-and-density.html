Dust relation between deceleration and density (q=Ωm​/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Deceleration parameter
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
For pressureless matter with zero cosmological constant, the Friedmann acceleration equation gives q=4πGρ/(3H2)=Ωm​/2. Spatial curvature need not vanish. Consequently kc2/a2=(2q−1)H2; this relation does not hold for a general fluid mixture.
