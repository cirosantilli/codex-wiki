Harmonic measure

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Planar Brownian motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 698 Articles: 12
The harmonic measure of a boundary set A⊆∂D viewed from x∈D is the probability that Brownian motion started at x first exits D through A. As a function of x, it is harmonic in D.
