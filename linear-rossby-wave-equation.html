Linear Rossby-wave equation

 Home Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Rossby wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
For a mode with streamfunction ϕ, deformation radius LD​, and constant planetary-vorticity gradient β, the linear Rossby-wave equation is
∂t​(∇h2​ϕ−LD−2​ϕ)+βϕx​=F.
(166)
In the long-wave limit it becomes a first-order westward transport equation.
