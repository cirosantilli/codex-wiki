Chromosomal crossover

 Home Biology Genetics Genetic recombination
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 5
A chromosomal crossover is a reciprocal exchange of segments between nonsister chromatids of homologous chromosomes during meiosis. It can change the haplotypes inherited at selected genetic loci; whether it does so depends on the transmitted chromatid and interval.
