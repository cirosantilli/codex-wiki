Unitary matrix

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 56 Articles: 1
A complex matrix is unitary when U†U=UU†=I. Its columns form an orthonormal basis, and it preserves inner products and norms.
