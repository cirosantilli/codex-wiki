Fusion closure of transparent anyons

OurBigBook.com Words: 49
For transparent anyons a,b, the hexagon identities for braiding for braiding express double exchange of a⊗b with any charge x as the successive double exchanges of a and b with x. Both are identities. Naturality restricts the resulting identity to every simple anyon fusion summand c of a⊗b, proving that c is transparent.
