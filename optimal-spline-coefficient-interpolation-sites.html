The equality holds when an alternating unit-norm spline s∗​ has as many ordered extremal sites as the dimension, and those sites satisfy the Schoenberg–Whitney theorem. For every admissible set, sampling has operator norm at most one, giving κ(S)≤∥Ax−1​∥∞​. At the alternating sites the checkerboard inverse of a totally nonnegative matrix identifies each inverse absolute row sum with the magnitude of the corresponding coefficient of s∗​. The Chebyshev spline coefficient and dual norm equality therefore gives equality. This minimizes amplification into B-spline coefficients; it does not by itself prove optimality for the distinct Lebesgue constant of interpolation.
