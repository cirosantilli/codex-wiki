Symmetric powers of the defining sln representation (SymmCn=V(mω1​))

For n≥2 and m≥0, the representation SymmCn of sln​(C) is irreducible with highest weight mω1​. Its monomial basis supplies weights by adding m defining-representation weights, with repeated weights counted separately.
