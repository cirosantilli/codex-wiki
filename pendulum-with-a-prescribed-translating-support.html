Pendulum with a prescribed translating support (lθ¨+a¨x​cosθ+(g+a¨y​)sinθ=0)

For support displacement a(t) and angle measured from the downward vertical, the mass position is (ax​+lsinθ,ay​−lcosθ). Its Euler-Lagrange equation is lθ¨+a¨x​cosθ+(g+a¨y​)sinθ=0. In the support frame the effective acceleration is g−a¨; taking its torque about the hinge gives l2θ¨z^=l×(g−a¨). The prescribed support can do work, so pendulum energy need not be conserved.
