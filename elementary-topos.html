Elementary topos

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 274 Articles: 8
An elementary topos is a category with finite limits, a cartesian-closed structure, and a subobject classifier. These axioms support an internal intuitionistic higher-order logic.
