Identity tensor (idTM​=δμν​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Covariant derivative Tensor type
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The identity tensor is the canonical type-(1,1) tensor that maps each tangent vector to itself. It pairs a covector η and vector V as η(V), and its components are the Kronecker delta δμν​ in every coordinate system.
