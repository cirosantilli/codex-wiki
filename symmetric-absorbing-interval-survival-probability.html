For diffusion starting at the centre of (−L,L) with absorbing endpoints, only even-parity spatial eigenfunctions contribute. Integrating their cosine expansion gives the displayed series. The leading late-time term has prefactor 4/π and decay rate π2D/(4L2); its first relative correction is −31​e−2π2Dt/L2.
