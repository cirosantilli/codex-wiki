Gaussian–exponential hierarchical colour model

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Hierarchical Bayesian model
OurBigBook.com Words: 109
An observed astronomical colour is the sum of a normal intrinsic colour, a nonnegative dust contribution with an exponential distribution, and normal measurement noise. With latent Cs​∼N(μ,v), Es​∼Exp(mean τ) and Os​∣Cs​,Es​∼N(Cs​+Es​,rs​), the Gibbs sampler conditionals for Cs​ are normal and those for Es​ are truncated normal distributions. A flat prior on μ and inverse-gamma priors on v,τ give inverse-gamma conditional updates for the two scales. Log-flat priors on both scales instead make the joint posterior improper when all rs​>0, despite formally proper full conditionals at generic latent states: the marginal observed likelihood has a positive limit at either zero-scale boundary. Proper positive-scale priors restore a genuine joint posterior.
