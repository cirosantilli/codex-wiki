Volume of a spherical cap (V=πh2(R−h/3))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Spherical cap
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
For a solid spherical cap of height 0≤h≤2R cut from a sphere of radius R,
V=πh2(R−3h​).​
(41)
Centre the sphere at the origin with the cap on the side z≥R−h. Its cross-section at height z is a circle of squared radius R2−z2. A volume integral in cylindrical coordinates gives
V=∫R−hR​∫02π​∫0R2−z2​​rdrdϕdz=π∫R−hR​(R2−z2)dz=πh2(R−h/3).
(42)
The formula includes caps larger than a hemisphere, when h>R, and the whole solid sphere, when h=2R. For the retained region z≥−d with 0<d<R, set h=R+d to obtain V=π(2R3/3+R2d−d3/3). Multiplying by uniform mass density gives its mass.
