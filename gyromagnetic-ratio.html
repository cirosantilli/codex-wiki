Gyromagnetic ratio (γ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential Magnetic dipole moment
OurBigBook.com Words: 87 Articles: 1
The proportionality coefficient between a system's magnetic dipole moment and angular momentum when μ=γJ applies. In the angular convention, γ is expressed in radians per second per tesla; γ/(2π) is the corresponding coefficient in cycles per second per tesla. Its sign determines the relative orientations of the magnetic dipole moment and angular momentum.
