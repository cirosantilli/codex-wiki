Group ring of a weight lattice (Z[P])

 ... Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Cartan subalgebra Root-space decomposition Root system Weight lattice
OurBigBook.com Words: 172 Articles: 1
The group ring of a weight lattice P consists of finite sums ∑λ​aλ​e(λ) with integer coefficients, where e(λ)e(μ)=e(λ+μ). A finite-dimensional Lie algebra representation has formal character ∑λ​(dimVλ​)e(λ) in this ring. The Weyl group acts by e(λ)↦e(wλ). The ambient ring need not consist of genuine characters; the representation ring of a semisimple Lie algebra of finite-dimensional modules for a complex semisimple Lie algebra identifies with the invariant subring Z[P]W.
