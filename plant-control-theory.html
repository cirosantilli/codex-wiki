Plant (control theory)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Control theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 209 Articles: 5
The controlled plant is the part of a control theory model that receives the control input and produces the plant output, including the mechanism that applies the command. The controller is specified separately. In the illustrative ordinary differential equation x˙=u+w, the controlled plant accumulates the control input and control disturbance; it does not itself choose u.
