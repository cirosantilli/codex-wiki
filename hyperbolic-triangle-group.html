Hyperbolic triangle group (Δ(p,q,r))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Fuchsian group
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
When 1/p+1/q+1/r<1, the orientation-preserving hyperbolic triangle group has presentation
Δ(p,q,r)=⟨a,b,c∣ap=bq=cr=abc=1⟩
(21)
and is a non-elementary Fuchsian group.
This is the orientation-preserving hyperbolic member of the broader triangle group construction.
