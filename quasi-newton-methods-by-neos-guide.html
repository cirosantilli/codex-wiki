neos-guide.org/guide/algorithms/quasi-newton/
The NEOS Guide's mathematical account of quasi-Newton methods, deriving the secant condition from an integrated Hessian matrix and describing low-rank updating, including BFGS. The related nonlinear-equation account is neos-guide.org/guide/algorithms/broyden/ and discusses Broyden's method.
