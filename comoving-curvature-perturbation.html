Comoving curvature perturbation (R)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic inflation Slow-roll approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 457 Articles: 10
The comoving curvature perturbation is the scalar curvature perturbation on hypersurfaces orthogonal to the total matter flow. For single-field adiabatic inflation it is conserved on superhorizon scales.
