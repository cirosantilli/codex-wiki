Phase angle (astronomy, α)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Natural satellite Moon Lunar phase
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The astronomical phase angle is the angle at an illuminated body between the directions to its light source and to its observer. For a lunar phase it is the Sun–Moon–observer angle at the Moon, rather than the elongation measured at Earth. A small phase angle gives a nearly full apparent disk in the spherical model.
