Velocity Verlet method (xn+1​=xn​+hvn​+21​h2an​)

For an ordinary differential equation x¨=f(x,t) with acceleration independent of velocity, the velocity Verlet method advances position and velocity by
an​=f(xn​,tn​),xn+1​=xn​+hvn​+2h2​an​,vn+1​=vn​+2h​(an​+f(xn+1​,tn​+h)).
(1)
Here h is the time step and tn+1​=tn​+h. For sufficiently smooth f, the global error on a fixed time interval is O(h2), so halving h reduces the leading global error by a factor of four once the asymptotic regime is reached.
