Mixture responsibility (τij​=P(Zi​=j∣yi​))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Mixture model Finite mixture model
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A mixture responsibility is the posterior probability of a component label given an observation and current parameters: τij​=πj​fj​(yi​)/(∑l​πl​fl​(yi​)). Each observation's responsibilities sum to one. The expectation-maximization algorithm uses these soft assignments rather than fixed hard cluster labels.
