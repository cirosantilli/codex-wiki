Half-level Neveu–Schwarz vector state (∣A;p⟩=A⋅b−1/2​∣0;p⟩)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Spinning string Neveu–Schwarz sector
OurBigBook.com Words: 134 Articles: 1
The lowest fermionic excitation has level 1/2. Its old-covariant physical conditions are α′p2+1/2−a=0 and p⋅A=0. For nonzero real momentum, timelike p gives a positive polarization space, null p gives a nonnegative space with a longitudinal null direction, and spacelike p permits a negative-norm time polarization. The limiting a=1/2 state is a massless gauge vector after quotienting null polarizations. This level-specific test does not prove positivity of every higher NS level.
