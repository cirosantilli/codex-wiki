Nonpositive modes in a Dirichlet-Robin interval expansion (y(0)=0,y′(1)=y(1)/α,α>0)

For −y′′=λy, positive eigenvalues have eigenfunctions sin(qn​x), where tanqn​=αqn​ and qn​>0. Their squared norms are Nn​=1/2−sin(2qn​)/(4qn​). A zero eigenvalue occurs precisely at α=1, with eigenfunction x. A negative eigenvalue occurs precisely at 0<α<1: it is −κ2 with eigenfunction sinh(κx), where tanhκ=ακ. There is exactly one such κ, since tanhκ/κ decreases strictly from 1 to 0. The integration by parts identity
⟨y,−y′′⟩=∫01​∣y′∣2dx−α∣y(1)∣2​
(10)
and the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality show positivity for α>1 and nonnegativity at equality. The complete expansion of a square-integrable function therefore includes the extra term 3x∫01​tf(t)dt when α=1, or sinh(κx)∫01​f(t)sinh(κt)dt/[sinh(2κ)/(4κ)−1/2] when α<1, in addition to ∑n​[∫01​f(t)sin(qn​t)dt/Nn​]sin(qn​x). Omitting that extra mode makes the system incomplete. In particular, f=x at α=1 is orthogonal to every positive sine mode and is an immediate counterexample to a sine-only expansion.
