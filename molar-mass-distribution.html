Molar mass distribution

 Home Chemistry Polymer
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
The distribution of molar masses among the chains in a polymer sample. Number-weighted and mass-weighted descriptions assign different relative weights to long chains: for populations Ni​ of chains with molar mass Mi​, the number-average molar mass is Mn​=∑i​Ni​Mi​/∑i​Ni​, while the weight-average molar mass is Mw​=∑i​Ni​Mi2​/∑i​Ni​Mi​. A single average does not determine the full distribution.
