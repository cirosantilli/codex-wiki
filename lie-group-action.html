Lie group action

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 364 Articles: 8
A Lie group action is a smooth group action of a Lie group on a smooth manifold. Differentiating the action gives an infinitesimal action of its Lie algebra by vector fields.
