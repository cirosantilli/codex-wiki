Pencil of plane curves

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Projective hypersurface
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 2
Given homogeneous polynomials F,G of the same degree, their pencil is the one-parameter family
V(sF+tG)⊆P2,[s:t]∈P1.
(42)
Its total space is the projective hypersurface V(sF+tG)⊆P2×P1, and projection to [s:t] has these plane curves as its fibres.
