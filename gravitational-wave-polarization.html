Gravitational wave polarization

 ... General relativity Linearized gravity Trace-reversed metric perturbation Lorenz gauge in linearized gravity Plane gravitational wave in linearized gravity Transverse-traceless gauge
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 5
A gravitational wave in four-dimensional general relativity has two independent linear polarizations, conventionally called plus and cross. A rotation by angle ϑ in the transverse plane rotates their pair by 2ϑ.
