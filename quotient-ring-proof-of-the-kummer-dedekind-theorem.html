Quotient-ring proof of the Kummer-Dedekind theorem (OK​/pOK​≃Fp​[X]/(m))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Kummer-Dedekind theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
For an integral generator α of a number field, let A=Z[α] and suppose p is coprime to [OK​:A]. Localizing at p makes A and the ring of integers equal, so reduction gives the displayed quotient ring. If m=∏giei​​ with distinct monic irreducible polynomials, the Chinese remainder theorem gives local factors Fp​[X]/(giei​​). Their residue fields have degrees deggi​ and their maximal ideals have nilpotence indices of an ideal ei​. Localizing the Dedekind domain OK​ at the corresponding prime ideals gives discrete valuation rings, where this same nilpotence index of an ideal is the ramification index of a prime ideal. Thus (p)=∏(p,gi​(α))ei​ and each residue degree is deggi​.
