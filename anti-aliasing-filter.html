Anti-aliasing filter

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem Aliasing
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
An anti-aliasing filter suppresses signal components outside the intended acquisition band before sampling. For voltage recording it is an analogue filter ahead of the analog-to-digital converter, with a transition band allowed below half the sampling rate. Once two frequencies have become indistinguishable through aliasing, a digital filter generally cannot recover the lost distinction. The Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem concerns an already bandlimited function; a physical filter approximates that prerequisite.
