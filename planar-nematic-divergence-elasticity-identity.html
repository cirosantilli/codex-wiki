Planar nematic divergence-elasticity identity (∣∇⋅Q∣2=λ02​∣∇χ∣2)

For a planar nematic director n=(cosχ,sinχ) and constant amplitude λ0​, the nematic order parameter is
Q=2λ0​​(cos2χsin2χ​sin2χ−cos2χ​).
(6)
Differentiation gives ∇⋅Q=λ0​(−sin2χχx​+cos2χχy​,cos2χχx​+sin2χχy​). The coefficient matrix is orthogonal, proving the identity. Hence K∣∇⋅Q∣2/2 is the angle-gradient energy with effective modulus Kλ02​. The full tensor-gradient norm instead obeys ∑ijk​(∂k​Qij​)2=2λ02​∣∇χ∣2 in this normalization; the two local densities must not be confused.
