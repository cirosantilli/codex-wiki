Equicontinuity

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Topological analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 328 Articles: 7
A family F of maps between metric spaces is equicontinuous when, near each point, one input tolerance controls the output variation uniformly for every f∈F. Uniform equicontinuity uses one input tolerance over the whole domain.
A family F of maps between metric spaces is equicontinuous at x when, for every ε>0, one δ>0 works for all f∈F: d(x,y)<δ implies d(f(x),f(y))<ε.
