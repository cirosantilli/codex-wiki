Parity preservation by polynomial least-squares projection

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation Polynomial approximation Polynomial least-squares approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
On a symmetric interval with an even positive weight, the best least-squares polynomial approximation to an odd function is odd, and the approximation to an even function is even. Indeed, the even and odd polynomial subspaces are orthogonal. For odd u, write a candidate as p=po​+pe​. Then
∥u−p∥2=∥u−po​∥2+∥pe​∥2.
(1)
Deleting a nonzero even component strictly reduces the error, so the minimizer has pe​=0. The same argument exchanges the parities for even u. This reduces the normal equations to the matching parity block and avoids computing coefficients known in advance to vanish.
