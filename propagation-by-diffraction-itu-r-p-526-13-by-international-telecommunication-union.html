Propagation by diffraction (ITU-R P.526-13) by International Telecommunication Union (November 2013)

www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/p/R-REC-P.526-13-201311-S%21%21PDF-E.pdf
Institutional recommendation titled “Propagation by diffraction”; Annex 1, Section 2.7 defines the Fresnel integrals and Section 4.1 treats a single knife edge. These normalize diffraction relative to the unobstructed field at the same receiver position.
