Compact Hausdorff reflection (R:Top→KHaus)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Reflective subcategory
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
The inclusion of compact Hausdorff spaces in topological spaces has a left adjoint. Its unit X→RX is universal among continuous functions from X to compact Hausdorff spaces and has dense image. Completeness follows from products in a category and closed equalizers. For any map X→K, its closed image has a dense subset of cardinality at most ∣X∣ and hence cardinality at most 22∣X∣. Representatives of all such compact Hausdorff spaces and maps establish the solution-set condition, so the general adjoint functor theorem gives the reflection. The unit need not be injective or a topological embedding for an arbitrary X.
