Diamagnetism (χ<0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 280 Articles: 4
Diamagnetism is a magnetic response in which the induced magnetization opposes the applied magnetic field intensity. In a linear isotropic description, M=χH with negative magnetic susceptibility χ.
