Finitely generated algebra

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field
OurBigBook.com Words: 319 Articles: 5
A k-algebra is finitely generated when finitely many elements generate it under addition, multiplication, and scalar multiplication. Equivalently, it is a quotient of a polynomial ring k[x1​,…,xn​].
