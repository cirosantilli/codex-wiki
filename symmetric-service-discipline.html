Symmetric service discipline (δi​(n)=γi​(n))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory Service discipline
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
In an ordered queue, let γi​(n) be the fraction of service capacity assigned to position i when n customers are present. A symmetric discipline inserts an arrival into position i with probability γi​(n+1). The same position rule for service and insertion makes transitions satisfy detailed balance for the word weights ∏i​αci​​/μci​​. It accommodates class-dependent exponential service rates; processor sharing is an example.
