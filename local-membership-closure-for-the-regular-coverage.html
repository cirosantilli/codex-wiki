Local membership closure for the regular coverage (F′(A)={x:∃α:B↠A, F(α)x∈F′(B)})

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Regular category Regular coverage
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For a subfunctor F′⊆F of a regular-coverage sheaf, its closure consists of sections whose restriction belongs to F′ along one covering regular epimorphism. Pullbacks prove that local membership is a subfunctor; composition of witnessing covers proves the sheaf condition. It is the smallest subsheaf containing F′.
