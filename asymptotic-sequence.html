Functions (ϕn​) form an asymptotic sequence at x0​ when ϕn+1​=o(ϕn​). The notation
f∼∑n≥0​an​ϕn​
(221)
means that for every N≥0,
f−∑n=0N​an​ϕn​=o(ϕN​).
(222)
Successive division of the remainder by ϕN​ uniquely recovers every coefficient.
