Cyclotomic Euler system

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Cyclotomic polynomial Cyclotomic field Cyclotomic unit
OurBigBook.com Words: 379 Articles: 4
A cyclotomic Euler system consists of units in auxiliary cyclotomic extensions satisfying compatible norm relations. Reducing at carefully chosen auxiliary primes and descending these relations bounds class-group modules. Combined with global unit-index formulas, this supplies an alternative route to the Iwasawa main conjecture.
