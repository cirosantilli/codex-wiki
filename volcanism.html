Volcanism

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 7
Transport and eruption or intrusion of molten rock and associated gases. Subduction, spreading and mantle upwellings can supply melt; volcanism does not require globally active plate tectonics. Outgassing couples interior evolution to atmospheric inventories.
