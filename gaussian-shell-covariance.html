Gaussian shell covariance (G>​(x−y))

For a centered shell Gaussian measure of a real scalar field with positive shell kernel K(p)=κp2+r, its Gaussian functional integral gives ⟨ϕ​(p)ϕ​(q)⟩=(2π)Dδ(D)(p+q)/K(p) on the shell. Inverse Fourier transforms therefore yield G>​(x−y)=∫Λ/b<∣p∣≤Λ​dDp(2π)−Deip⋅(x−y)/K(p). The kernel must be positive throughout the shell. Translation invariance makes the separation essential; a numerator involving x alone is valid only with y=0 or if x is redefined as the separation.
