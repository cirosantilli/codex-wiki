Massless longitudinal Pauli-Lubanski eigenvalues ((W0​,W3​)=(kj3​,−kj3​))

 ... Special relativity Lorentz transformation Lorentz group Poincaré group Poincare algebra Pauli-Lubanski pseudovector
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
With signature (+−−−) and ϵ0123​=+1, a momentum (k,0,0,k) and helicity j3​ give lower components W0​=kj3​ and W3​=−kj3​. They obey Wμ​Pμ=k(W0​+W3​)=0. These longitudinal identities require only the momentum and rotation-projection eigenvalues, not a statement about transverse little-group translations.
