Melnikov energy-balance method

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Nearly Hamiltonian system
OurBigBook.com Words: 433 Articles: 6
For a weak perturbation of a planar Hamiltonian system, integrate the first-order orbital derivative of the Hamiltonian along an unperturbed homoclinic orbit. This measures the first-order separation of stable and unstable manifolds on a transverse section. A simple zero as a parameter varies permits a nearby homoclinic connection by the implicit function theorem. The resulting leading balance is not a claim that the unperturbed separatrix remains an exact orbit after perturbation.
