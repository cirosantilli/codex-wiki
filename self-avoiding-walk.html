Self-avoiding walk

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Lattice path
OurBigBook.com Words: 453 Articles: 9
An n-step self-avoiding walk on a graph is a sequence v0​,…,vn​ of distinct graph vertices with an edge between successive graph vertices. It is a graph path with n edges and n+1 graph vertices. On a transitive graph, the exponential growth rate of rooted self-avoiding walk counts is the connective constant.
