Kummer cohomology divisibility criterion (Hn+1(L,μp​)=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Kummer theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
When the characteristic differs from p, the Kummer sequence gives 0→Hn(L,Ks×​)/p→Hn+1(L,μp​)→Hn+1(L,Ks×​)[p]→0. For n≥1, the positive-degree multiplicative groups are torsion. Thus the middle group is zero exactly when the first multiplicative group is p-divisible and the second has zero p-primary torsion.
