Skeleton of a directed graph

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Directed graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The skeleton of a directed graph is the undirected graph obtained by replacing every directed edge with an undirected edge and forgetting its orientation. In causal structure learning, the PC algorithm first recovers this adjacency structure before learning orientations.
