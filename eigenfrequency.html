Eigenfrequency (ω)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Normal mode
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
An eigenfrequency is the natural frequency associated with a free normal mode. Always specify whether it means the angular frequency ω or the frequency f=ω/(2π) in hertz. For a uniform fixed-end wave equation on a string, ωn​=nπτ/μ​/L and fn​=nτ/μ​/(2L); the amplitude does not change either within the linear model. In a generalized eigenvalue problem for small oscillations, the eigenvalue is ω2, rather than ω itself.
For a first-order system z˙=iAz, a real eigenvalue λ of A instead gives a signed eigenfrequency in eiλt. A positive imaginary part of λ gives exponential decay with this sign convention. Thus a complex damped eigenfrequency encodes both oscillation and decay; it is not just the number of cycles per second.
