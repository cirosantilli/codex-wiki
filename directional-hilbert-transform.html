Directional Hilbert transform (Hv​g(x)=π−1pv∫R​g(x−sv)ds/s)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier transform Hilbert transform
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For a unit vector v, the directional Hilbert transform integrates along lines parallel to v. Its Fourier multiplier is −isgn(ξ⋅v) under the usual transform convention. Applied to a rectangle whose long axis is v, it reduces to the one-dimensional logarithmic tail of an interval indicator function, separately on each transverse coordinate.
