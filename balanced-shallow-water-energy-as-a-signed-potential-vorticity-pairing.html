where q=ζ−fη/H is the signed linearized shallow-water potential-vorticity anomaly. The stationary height equation and integration by parts prove the identity. Replacing q by ∣q∣ destroys it: an odd height paired with symmetric opposite-sign sheets would then misleadingly give zero.
