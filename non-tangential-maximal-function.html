Non-tangential maximal function (Fa∗​(x)=supy>0, ∣x−x′∣≤ay​∣F(x′,y)∣)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Harmonic analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 1
This supremum function takes a supremum over a cone approaching the boundary point x; the positive number a is its aperture. For an analytic upper-half-plane H1 function, it belongs to L1. To prove this without first assuming boundary absolute continuity, shift the function upwards, apply subharmonic Poisson comparison to its square root, and use the strong L2 bound for the Hardy-Littlewood maximal function. Fatou lemma removes the shift.
