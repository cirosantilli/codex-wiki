Cyclic adenosine monophosphate

 Home Biology Signal transduction Second messenger
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
Cyclic adenosine monophosphate is a cyclic nucleotide made from ATP. It can serve as an intracellular second messenger, changing the activity of target proteins after a signal has been received. Different biological cells can respond differently because they contain different targets. [ref]
