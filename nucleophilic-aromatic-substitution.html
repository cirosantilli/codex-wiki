Nucleophilic aromatic substitution

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Organic reaction Nucleophilic substitution
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 6
In an addition–elimination pathway, a nucleophile attacks an aromatic carbon and forms a Meisenheimer complex; leaving-group loss restores aromaticity. Electron-withdrawing groups ortho or para to the attacked site can stabilize the addition intermediate.
