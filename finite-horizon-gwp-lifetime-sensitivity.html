Finite-horizon GWP lifetime sensitivity (τ(1−e−H/τ))

 ... Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Irradiated planetary atmosphere Greenhouse effect Greenhouse gas Global warming potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For a gas pulse with unchanged greenhouse gas radiative efficiency and decay e−t/τ, its integrated forcing through H is proportional to τ(1−e−H/τ). Comparing two such lifetimes at fixed carbon-dioxide reference gives the ratio of these two factors. A fractional lifetime increase is not generally the same fractional GWP increase at finite H.
