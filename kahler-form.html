Kähler form (ω(u,v)=g(Ju,v))

For a Kähler metric g with complex structure J, the associated real two-form is ω(u,v)=g(Ju,v). It has type (1,1), is positive in the sense ω(v,Jv)=g(v,v)>0 for v=0, and is closed. Conversely a real closed positive (1,1) form determines a Kähler metric. A local geometric Kähler potential writes it as i∂∂ˉφ.
