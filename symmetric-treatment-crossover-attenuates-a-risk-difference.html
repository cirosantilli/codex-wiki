Symmetric treatment crossover attenuates a risk difference (Δassignment​=(1−2c)Δreceipt​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Causal inference Randomized controlled trial Intention-to-treat analysis Treatment contamination
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
Assume treatment receipt gives event risk p1​, nonreceipt gives risk p0​, and a fraction c in each equally sized randomized arm crosses to the opposite treatment. The two assignment risks are (1−c)p1​+cp0​ and cp1​+(1−c)p0​. Subtracting yields the displayed attenuation, with Δreceipt​=p0​−p1​. It depends on the stated equal crossover fractions and unchanged receipt-specific risks; it is not a universal formula for every form of treatment contamination.
