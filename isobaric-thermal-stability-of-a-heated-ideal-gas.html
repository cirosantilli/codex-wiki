Isobaric thermal stability of a heated ideal gas (s=(1−α)H/(cp​T0​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 254 Articles: 2
Let the net cooling per unit mass of an ideal gas be L=AρTα−H, with positive constants A,H. At fixed pressure and composition, ρT is constant, so L=CTα−1−H. At a thermal equilibrium T0​, CT0α−1​=H. The specific heat capacity at constant pressure gives cp​d(δT)/dt=−(∂L/∂T)p​δT. Thus δT∝est with the displayed growth rate. The equilibrium is stable for α>1, unstable for α<1, and marginal at linear order for α=1. In particular, optically thin Bremsstrahlung with α=1/2 is unstable under this heating law and the isobaric constraint. Fixed-density perturbations have a different criterion.
