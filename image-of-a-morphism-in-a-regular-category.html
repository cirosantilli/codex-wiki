Image of a morphism in a regular category

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Regular category
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
In a regular category, the image of f:A→B is the monomorphism in its essentially unique factorization
A↠imf↪B
(13)
as a regular epimorphism followed by a monomorphism. Images are stable under pullback.
