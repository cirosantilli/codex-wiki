A dynamical system consists of a state space together with a rule that determines how its state evolves in time.

State space

Words: 29
Dynamical system OurBigBook.com
The state space of a dynamical system is the set of all states that the system may occupy. An evolution rule traces an orbit through this space from each admissible initial state.

Energy balance method

Words: 184 Articles: 4
Dynamical systems OurBigBook.com
For a weak perturbation of a planar Hamiltonian system, integrate the first-order change of the unperturbed Hamiltonian around each unperturbed periodic orbit. Zeros of this averaged energy drift select candidate perturbed periodic orbits, and a change from positive to negative drift indicates stability.

Index of a planar periodic orbit

Words: 72 Articles: 1

A planar periodic orbit, oriented by the flow, has Poincare index +1. The index theorem equates this with the sum of the indices of the isolated equilibria enclosed by the orbit. A hyperbolic equilibrium has index +1 when its Jacobian matrix has positive determinant and index −1 when it is a saddle equilibrium.

Sharkovsky theorem

Words: 104 Articles: 1
