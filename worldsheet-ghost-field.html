Worldsheet ghost field (bαβ​,cα)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Polyakov action
OurBigBook.com Words: 665 Articles: 14
Gauge fixing worldsheet diffeomorphism and Weyl symmetry introduces an anticommuting vector ghost cα and a symmetric traceless antighost bαβ​. In complex coordinates they form holomorphic and antiholomorphic bc systems.
