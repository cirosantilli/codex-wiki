Let Rt​ be the horizontal rotation matrix through angle t. Define the screw motion
Φt​(x,y,z)=(xcost−ysint, xsint+ycost, z+t).​
(26)
Its linear part is an orthogonal matrix with determinant one, and its remaining part is a translation. Thus it is a proper Euclidean motion of Euclidean three-space, with Φs​Φt​=Φs+t​.
In horizontal-vector notation the given parametric surface satisfies r(u,v)=(Rv​(1+cosu,sinu),v). Therefore
Φt​(r(u,v))=r(u,v+t).
(27)
The defining circle in each horizontal plane is entirely parametrized by u, so r covers X. The displayed equality and Φ−t​=Φt−1​ prove Φt​(X)=X. This one-parameter subgroup preserves the dot products and cross products defining I, II and the chosen unit normal. Consequently their coordinate coefficients, and the Gaussian curvature, depend only on u.
