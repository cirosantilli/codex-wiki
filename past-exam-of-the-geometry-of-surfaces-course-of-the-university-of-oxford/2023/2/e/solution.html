The dihedral group here has order 2n. Substituting h=2 into part (d) gives
g=2n+1.​
(21)
In particular, n=3 requires genus seven. A geometric construction is to start with a round solid torus about the vertical axis, then drill 2n thin radial tunnels through its tube in the horizontal midplane, equally spaced at azimuths kπ/n, 0≤k<2n. The tunnels run from the central opening to the exterior; their directions are horizontal and radial. Round all their rims in the same way, preserving the dihedral symmetry, and let X be the boundary of the resulting solid.
Here is why the construction has exactly the required properties. Every nonidentity power of β is a rotation in three dimensions fixing the vertical axis, which lies in the central opening and is disjoint from X. Each element αβj is a half-turn about a horizontal line at azimuth jπ/n, modulo π. The n such lines meet the original solid torus in 2n short radial segments. Each segment lies strictly inside one drilled tunnel, so these axes are also disjoint from X. A nonidentity rotation in three dimensions fixes only its axis. Thus no nonidentity element fixes a point of X, proving a free action of a group.
Rotation by 2π/n permutes the tunnels, while α:(x,y,z)↦(x,−y,−z) preserves their arrangement and the original solid torus. Hence X is preserved by the two generators and therefore by G. The tunnels can be chosen sufficiently thin to be pairwise disjoint, and the rounding can be made group-invariant in small disjoint neighbourhoods of their rims. It neither changes the genus nor introduces an intersection with any axis.
Each tunnel removes two discs from the existing boundary and inserts the cylindrical wall of the bore. Its contribution to Euler characteristic is −2: the two removed discs contribute −2, and the inserted annulus and its boundary circles have zero Euler characteristic. The boundary remains connected and orientable, so each tunnel increases genus by one. Starting from a torus and drilling 2n tunnels therefore gives g=1+2n. The construction also supplies a Riemann surface: use the complex structure associated with the induced oriented Riemannian metric. The ambient generators are isometries preserving the solid and ambient orientation of a vector space, so they preserve the induced boundary orientation and act by biholomorphisms. Part (d) then confirms that the quotient space has genus two. This is a free dihedral action on a drilled torus.
