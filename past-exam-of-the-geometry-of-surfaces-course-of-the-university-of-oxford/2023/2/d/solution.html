For a free action of a group, every stabilizer subgroup is trivial. Hence every ramification index of a holomorphic map is one, and the projection is an unramified covering map of degree of a holomorphic map ∣G∣. The Riemann-Hurwitz formula reduces to
2g−2=∣G∣(2h−2),g−1=∣G∣(h−1).​

This is also the multiplicativity of Euler characteristic under a finite covering.
