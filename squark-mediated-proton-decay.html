Squark-mediated proton decay (τp​∼mq​4​/(∣λ′λ′′∣2mp5​))

Simultaneous trilinear baryon number and lepton number violation permits a squark to connect the two interactions and induce a dimension-six proton-decay operator. Its coefficient is of order λ′λ′′/mq​2​. A dimensional decay-width estimate then gives Γp​∼∣λ′λ′′∣2mp5​/mq​4​, up to phase space and hadronic matrix elements. The flavor antisymmetry of UcDcDc matters: the two down-type flavor indices must differ. For example, λ112′′​ and λ112′​ allow strange-squark exchange in p→e+π0.
