Duality of Lp spaces ((Lp)∗=Lq)

OurBigBook.com Words: 389 Articles: 6
For 1≤p<∞ and conjugate exponent q, every bounded linear functional on Lp over a sigma-finite measure space has the form

for a unique ω∈Lq, and its operator norm is ∥ω∥q​. At the endpoint p=1, this says (L1)∗=L∞.
This identifies the continuous dual space of an Lp space; it is not an alternative definition of the entire Lp-space topic.
