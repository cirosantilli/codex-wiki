Semivariogram (γ(h))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Geostatistics Intrinsically stationary random field
OurBigBook.com Words: 310 Articles: 7
For an intrinsically stationary random field, the semivariogram is γ(h)=21​Var[Z(s+h)−Z(s)]. It is even and vanishes at zero. With constant mean, a second-order stationary field has γ(h)=C(0)−C(h). An isotropic model writes it as a function of nonnegative distance.
