Ample curve on an abelian surface generates the surface

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic surface Exceptional curve of the first kind Minimal algebraic surface Abelian surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
Let C be a smooth connected ample curve in an abelian surface A. Translate its chosen basepoint to zero and apply the universal property of the Jacobian variety. The image of J(C)→A is a closed connected algebraic subgroup containing the translated curve. If it had dimension one, that curve would be the subgroup itself; a distinct coset is disjoint and numerically equivalent, forcing C2=0. This contradicts ampleness, so the homomorphism is surjective.
