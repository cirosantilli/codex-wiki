Kuhn's theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Perfect recall
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
In a finite extensive-form game with perfect recall, each mixed strategy has a realization-equivalent behavioural strategy, and every behavioural strategy has a realization-equivalent mixed strategy. The latter direction samples all contingent actions independently in advance. For the former, condition the random complete plan on the player's own action and information history leading to an information set; perfect recall makes that own history common to its nodes. Conditional action probabilities then reproduce the terminal path factors. At an information set reached with zero probability under that player's own plan distribution, choose any action distribution. The result does not assert equivalence without perfect recall.[ref]
