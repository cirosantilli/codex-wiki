Constructible universe theorem (L⊨ZFC+GCH)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Definable power set Constructible hierarchy Constructible universe
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
ZFC proves that the constructible universe is an inner model of ZFC and satisfies the Generalized continuum hypothesis. Relativizing to L yields the corresponding relative-consistency implication.
