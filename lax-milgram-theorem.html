Lax-Milgram theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 2
A bounded coercive bilinear form a on a Hilbert space H represents every bounded linear functional uniquely: for each ℓ∈H′, there is a unique u∈H with a(u,v)=ℓ(v) for every v∈H.
