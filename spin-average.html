Spin average

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Spin sum
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
For an unpolarized initial state, average the squared scattering amplitude over its equally populated spin states. With two incoming spin-one-half particles this gives a factor 1/(2⋅2)=1/4. Unobserved final spins are summed rather than averaged.
