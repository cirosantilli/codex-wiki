Integer shear group (Bn​=(10​n1​),Bn​Bm​=Bn+m​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Cyclic group Infinite cyclic group
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
These invertible matrices, for n∈Z, form an infinite cyclic group. The displayed multiplication rule, B0​=I, and Bn−1​=B−n​ give a group isomorphism from the additive group of integers via n↦Bn​. Its generator is B1​. The subgroups of an infinite cyclic group become {Bmk​:k∈Z} for integers m≥0, and their subgroup indices are m when m>0. The case m=0 is the trivial subgroup and has infinite index. As transformations, these matrices send (x,y) to (x+ny,y).
