Reflection length of a Euclidean orthogonal map (ℓ(Q)=rank(I−Q))

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Isometry Euclidean isometry Finite reflection decomposition of a Euclidean isometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
A product of m reflections has rank(I−Q)≤m, by the rank inequality applied successively to I−AB=(I−A)+A(I−B). Conversely an orthogonal transformation fixes the orthogonal complement of its moved space; successive Householder reflections fix one new basis direction at each step in that moved space. At most its dimension rank(I−Q) are required. Thus central inversion in three dimensions needs exactly three reflections.
