Heat invariants (Tre−tΔ∼(4πt)−d/2∑j≥0​aj​tj)

 ... Analysis Partial differential equation Diffusion equation Heat equation Heat kernel Heat kernel expansion
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 2
For the positive scalar Laplace-Beltrami operator on a compact manifold without boundary, the heat invariants are the integrated coefficients aj​=∫M​uj​(x,x)dVg​ in the short-time heat kernel expansion. In particular a0​=Vol(M) and a1​=61​∫M​Rg​dVg​, with Rg​ the scalar curvature. They are determined by the heat trace, hence by the spectrum with multiplicities. The leading positive coefficient recovers the dimension from limt↓0​logTr(e−tΔ)/log(1/t)=d/2.
