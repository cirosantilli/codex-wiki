Vicarious nucleophilic substitution

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Organic reaction Nucleophilic substitution Nucleophilic aromatic substitution
OurBigBook.com Words: 61 Articles: 1
Vicarious nucleophilic substitution replaces aromatic hydrogen using a nucleophile that carries its own leaving group. In nitroarenes, reversible carbanion addition gives a sigma-H adduct; base-induced elimination of the hydrogen and the nucleophile-borne leaving group, followed by protonation, gives substitution without expelling hydride.
