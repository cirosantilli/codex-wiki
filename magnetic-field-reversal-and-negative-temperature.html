Magnetic-field reversal and negative temperature

 Home Physics Branch of physics Statistical physics Canonical ensemble Negative temperature
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
Instantaneously reversing a Hamiltonian H(B) satisfying H(−B)=−H(B) leaves populations unchanged but rewrites them as a canonical distribution with β′=−β. When the partition function is unchanged, the free energy changes sign and the heat capacity is unchanged.
