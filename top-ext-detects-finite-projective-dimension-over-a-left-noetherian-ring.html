Top Ext detects finite projective dimension over a left Noetherian ring (pdR​M=n<∞⟹ExtRn​(M,R)=0)

 ... Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Projective module Projective dimension Top Ext detects finite projective dimension
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
For a nonzero left finitely generated module over a left Noetherian ring, take a finite projective resolution with finite projective modules. Vanishing of its top Ext functor would make the dual of its final injection surjective. These duals are right finite projective modules, so the surjection splits. Dualizing back and using finite projective double dual modules splits the original injection and shortens the resolution, contradicting the definition of projective dimension. When the dimension is zero, a coordinate map of a finite free module containing the nonzero projective summand supplies a nonzero map to R. The left/right distinction in the duals is essential for a noncommutative ring.
