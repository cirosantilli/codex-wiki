Countable rank-initial segment cannot model ZFC

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Von Neumann hierarchy
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
If α is a countable ordinal and Vα​⊨ZFC, then α must be a limit ordinal. Choose a countable cofinal sequence (αn​) in α. The internal axiom of choice gives, for every n, a bijection between Vαn​​ and some ordinal below α; that ordinal is externally countable, so every Vαn​​ is countable. Hence Vα​=⋃n​Vαn​​ is countable. But Vα​ contains the full power set P(ω), which is uncountable by Cantor theorem, a contradiction.
