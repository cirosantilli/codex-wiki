Covariant Yoneda embedding (Y:Cop→[C,Set],Y(A)=C(A,−))

 ... Category Functor Functor category Representable functor Yoneda lemma Yoneda embedding
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The covariant version of the Yoneda embedding sends an object to its outgoing representable functor. Precomposition reverses arrows. The Yoneda lemma gives Nat(Y(A),Y(B))≅C(B,A), so it is full and faithful. It carries existing colimits in C to pointwise categorical limits.
