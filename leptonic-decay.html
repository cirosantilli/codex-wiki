Leptonic decay

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Particle decay
OurBigBook.com Words: 93 Articles: 1
A leptonic decay has only leptons in its non-radiative final state. For example, a charged pion can decay as π+→μ+νμ​. A semileptonic decay instead retains at least one hadron alongside the leptons. This classification concerns the final particles, not whether the initial particle is a hadron or a lepton.
