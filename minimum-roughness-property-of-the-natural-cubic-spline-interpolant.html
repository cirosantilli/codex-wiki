Minimum roughness property of the natural cubic spline interpolant (J(g​)=J(g)+J(g​−g))

 ... Uniform approximation Spline (mathematics) Spline approximation Cubic spline Natural cubic spline Natural cubic spline interpolant
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 1
For g=Nv and any g​∈C2[a,b] taking the same values at at least two distinct knots, J(g​)=J(g)+J(g​−g). To prove it, set r=g​−g, integrate g′′r′′ by parts on each polynomial interval and use g(4)=0, r(xi​)=0, continuous g′′ and linear exterior pieces. Boundary terms cancel. Expanding the square proves the identity. Equality forces r′′=0, so r is an affine function and its two prescribed zeros make it identically zero.
