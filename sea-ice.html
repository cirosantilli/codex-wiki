Sea ice

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 45
Sea ice is frozen ocean water. A local thermodynamic description uses thermal conduction, a surface energy balance and the Stefan condition; salinity, mechanical transport and fractional ice coverage add further physics.
