Riemannian manifold

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 34
A Riemannian manifold is a smooth manifold equipped with a smoothly varying positive-definite inner product on each tangent space.
