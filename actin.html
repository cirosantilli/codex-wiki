Actin

 Home Biology Protein
OurBigBook.com Words: 387 Articles: 16
Actin is a protein whose monomers assemble into filaments. An idealized actin filament has a bending modulus and persistence length; inferring its effective elastic modulus from those measurements additionally requires a cross-sectional model.
