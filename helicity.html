Helicity (λ)

 ... Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz transformation Lorentz group Poincaré group Little group
OurBigBook.com Words: 86 Articles: 1
Helicity is the projection of angular momentum onto the momentum direction. For a finite-helicity massless particle, the Pauli-Lubanski vector satisfies Wμ=λPμ.
