Enclosed mass does not determine a disc rotation curve

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy rotation curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The spherical shell theorem yields vc2​=GM(<r)/r only in spherical geometry. A razor-thin axisymmetric disc instead requires vc2​=R∂R​Φ(R,0), including forces from exterior annuli. The interior gravitational force of an exterior thin ring gives a concrete counterexample with identical enclosed mass but different circular speeds.
