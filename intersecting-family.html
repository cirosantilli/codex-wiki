Intersecting family

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Set family
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 28
A family of sets is intersecting when every two of its members have nonempty intersection.
This is a property of a set family, distinct from extremal theorems bounding its size.
