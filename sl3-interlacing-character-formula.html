sl3 interlacing character formula (χa,b​=∑b≤p≤a+b​∑0≤q≤b​∑q≤r≤p​x1r​x2p+q−r​x3a+2b−p−q​)

For the special linear Lie algebra sl3​, the irreducible highest-weight representation with Dynkin labels (a,b) has the displayed formal character on diagonal matrices with x1​x2​x3​=1. The interlacing rows (a+b,b,0), (p,q) and (r) enumerate a Gelfand–Tsetlin basis; each triple contributes the exponent differences (r−(p+q−r),(p+q−r)−(a+2b−p−q)) as its weight. This gives exact integer weight multiplicities, including repeated weights, without floating-point character evaluation.
