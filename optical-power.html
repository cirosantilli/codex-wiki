Optical power (Φ=1/f)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Geometrical optics Lens (optics) Focal length
OurBigBook.com Words: 165 Articles: 1
The optical power is reciprocal signed focal length, Φ=1/f. With f expressed in metres, optical power is expressed in dioptres: f=1m gives Φ=+1D, while f=−0.5m gives Φ=−2D. The sign distinguishes converging and diverging optical lens elements; the magnitude measures how strongly they change the direction of a light ray at a given height. [ref].
For two ideal thin lens elements in contact in air, optical powers add: Φtotal​=Φ1​+Φ2​. Thus +2D and −1D combine to give +1D, or ftotal​=1m. Separation changes the rule: for two ideal thin lens elements separated by d in air, their equivalent optical power is Φ1​+Φ2​−dΦ1​Φ2​. The contact rule is the d=0 case.[ref]
