The inequality follows immediately because the denominator belongs to (0,1]. Equality holds precisely at c=0, where hydrostatic equilibrium gives ρgη=−p1​cos(kx). If only strictly positive c is allowed, p1​/(ρg) is the infimum, approached as c↓0, and is not an attained minimum. This bound applies to the travelling forced component, rather than an arbitrary superposition with free surface gravity waves.
