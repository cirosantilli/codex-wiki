Critical-point criterion for equal conformal connections (∇p​=∇p​⟺(dσ)p​=0)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian metric Conformal rescaling of a Riemannian metric Levi-Civita connection under conformal rescaling
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
On a positive-dimensional Riemannian manifold, equality means equality of the two covariant derivatives at p for every pair of vector fields. A critical point of σ annihilates the conformal correction. Conversely, writing that correction as A, the identity g(A(v,v),v)=g(v,v)dσ(v) for all tangent vectors forces dσ=0 if A=0. A smooth function on a nonempty compact boundaryless manifold has a critical point, so conformally related connections coincide somewhere. Compact manifolds with boundary need not have one.
