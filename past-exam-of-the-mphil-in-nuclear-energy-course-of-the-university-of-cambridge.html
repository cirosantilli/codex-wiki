The 2019 and 2021 NE2 papers match Engineering 4I10 papers with public worked cribs: 2019 crib and 2021 crib. The 2020 NE1 cover explicitly crosslists Engineering 4M16; Engineering advertises student access to examination cribs for all years in its library. These three papers are skipped for new solutions. Student-answer availability for the other held papers remains unverified.
