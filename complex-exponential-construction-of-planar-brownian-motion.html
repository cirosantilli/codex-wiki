For independent one-dimensional Brownian motions β,θ, the real and imaginary parts of Z are continuous local martingales with equal bracket A and zero cross bracket. The Divergence of a driftless Brownian exponential clock makes A∞​=∞, and the common-clock time change of orthogonal local martingales makes ZA−1(r)​ planar Brownian motion started at 1. The exponential never vanishes, proving that this Brownian motion never hits zero. Recurrent visits of β to arbitrarily negative levels prove visits arbitrarily near zero at arbitrarily large Brownian times.
