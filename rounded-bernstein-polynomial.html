Rounded Bernstein polynomial (Bn∗​(f,x))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Stone-Weierstrass theorem Weierstrass approximation theorem Bernstein polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
The rounded Bernstein polynomial replaces each coefficient (kn​)f(k/n) in the unnormalized basis xk(1−x)n−k by its floor function. It therefore has integer coefficients in the ordinary monomial basis. If f(0),f(1)∈Z, only interior rounding errors remain and
∥Bn∗​f−Bn​f∥∞​≤max{3/(2n),(n−1)(3/4)n}⟶0(n≥2).
(32)
To prove this, bound each interior rounding error by one. On [0,1/4], sum the resulting powers as a geometric series of ratio at most 1/3 and maximize x(1−x)n−1; reflect for [3/4,1]. On the middle interval each degree-n product is at most (3/4)n. integer endpoints are necessary for this convergence: a noninteger endpoint produces a fixed nonzero rounding error there.
