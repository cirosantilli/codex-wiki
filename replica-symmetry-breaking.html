Giorgio Parisi's 1979 work replaced a single replica order parameter by an infinite hierarchy in the mean-field theory of spin glasses. The claim concerns a thermodynamic model, rather than inequivalence of the labels attached to two finite laboratory copies.[ref]
For equilibrium states with weights wα​ and local means miα​, define Qαγ​=N−1∑i​miα​miγ​. The distribution PJ​(q)=∑α,γ​wα​wγ​δ(q−Qαγ​) records their similarities. This state-based overlap distribution is distinct from the finite instantaneous spin overlap used in the triangle example.[ref]
