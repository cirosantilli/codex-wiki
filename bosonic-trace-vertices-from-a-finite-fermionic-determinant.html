Bosonic trace vertices from a finite fermionic determinant (Veff​(x)=−Trlog[I−gB−1∑k​xk​C(k)])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Quantized field Grassmann Gaussian integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
Integrating finite Grassmann variables out of a quadratic action gives det[B−gC(x)]. If B is invertible, divide by detB and expand its logarithm to obtain Veff​=∑m≥1​gmTr[B−1C(x)]m/m. Since the bosonic variables commute, an m-leg effective Feynman rule uses the fully symmetrized trace tensor, with weight −gm(m−1)!SymTr[B−1C(k1​)⋯B−1C(km​)]. The minus sign comes from e−Veff​. This organizes closed fermion loops into bosonic vertices. It is a formal perturbative identity; a singular B prevents the normalized expansion, even though the unfactored determinant polynomial still exists.
