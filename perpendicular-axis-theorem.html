Perpendicular axis theorem

 Home Physics Branch of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics Moment of inertia
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
For a planar lamina in the xy-plane, the moments about three mutually perpendicular axes through one point satisfy
Iz​=Ix​+Iy​.
(29)
This follows pointwise from x2+y2=y2+x2 in the defining mass integrals.
