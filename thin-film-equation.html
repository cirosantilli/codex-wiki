Thin-film equation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Lubrication theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 18
A thin-film equation combines a lubrication flux with mass conservation to evolve a free-surface thickness. Gravity, capillary pressure, wall motion, and intermolecular forces may contribute to the flux.
