Spatial root splitting for the dispersive Stokes resolvent (r3+r+p=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Integrable systems Lax pair Airy equation Linear dispersive Stokes equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 231 Articles: 2
For a time Laplace transform parameter with Rep>0, the spatial characteristic roots satisfy r3+r+p=0. Exactly one root r−​ has negative real part and two have positive real part. Indeed an imaginary root would force p=−i(k−k3) to be imaginary. The count is therefore constant on the connected right half-plane; for real p>0, the cubic has one negative real root and a conjugate pair with positive real parts. Repeated roots would satisfy 3r2+1=0, which again puts p on the imaginary axis. Thus r−​(p) is a nonzero holomorphic function in the right half-plane. Only er−​x decays at positive spatial infinity.
