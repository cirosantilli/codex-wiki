Paraboloid sector surface measures

OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For h,R>0 and 0<α≤2π, the curved sector z=h(1−r2/R2), 0≤r≤R, 0≤θ≤α has volume αhR2/4 beneath it. Its surface area is
A=12h2αR4​[(1+R24h2​)3/2−1].
(6)
To derive this, parametrize the surface by (rcosθ,rsinθ,h(1−r2/R2)). The upward vector area element is (2hr2cosθ/R2,2hr2sinθ/R2,r)drdθ; its magnitude integrates to the stated surface area. Its integral gives the vector area
S=(32hR​sinα,32hR​(1−cosα),2αR2​).
(7)
In the shallow limit h/R→0, the surface area is αR2/2+αh2/2+O(h4/R2), approaching the area of its planar sector projection.
