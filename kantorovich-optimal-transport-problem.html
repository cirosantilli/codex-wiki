Kantorovich optimal transport problem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Optimal transport
OurBigBook.com Words: 243 Articles: 5
The Kantorovich problem minimizes ∫c(x,y)dπ(x,y) over transport plans π with marginal distributions μ,ν. It relaxes the Monge optimal transport problem by allowing source mass to split among destinations.
