Quadrupole potential of a circular binary

 ... Astrophysics Planetary science Planetary system dynamics Disturbing function Secular perturbation Quadrupole approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 1
A binary star of total mass M, reduced mass μ and separation vector a has far-field potential function
Φ=−xGM​−2x3Gμa2​(3cos2θ−1)+O(GMa3/x4),
(12)
where θ is the angle between x and a. The dipole term vanishes in centre of mass coordinates. Averaging a circular coplanar binary over its fast phase gives Φ=−GM/x−Gμa2/(4x3).
