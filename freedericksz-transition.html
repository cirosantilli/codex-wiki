Fréedericksz transition

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Order parameter Liquid crystal Nematic liquid crystal
OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 1
The Fréedericksz transition is a field-driven orientational instability of a confined nematic liquid crystal. Elasticity and surface anchoring resist the electric field or magnetic torque; a lowest allowed distortion mode becomes unstable at a finite threshold. Positive dielectric anisotropy of a nematic permits a normal electric field to tilt a planar anchored director.
