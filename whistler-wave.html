Whistler wave (ω±​=±vA​di​k∥​k)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Electron magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
In the inertia-free, constant-density electron magnetohydrodynamics model with uniform guide field B0​z^, a transverse magnetic plane wave obeys ωb=ivA​di​k∥​k×b. The helicity decomposition of a transverse Fourier mode gives the displayed dispersive branches with opposite circular polarizations. This electron-current mode is called a whistler wave; see Lyutikov's analysis of electron magnetohydrodynamics. Its pressure-free induction equation should not be identified with a general kinetic Alfvén model.
