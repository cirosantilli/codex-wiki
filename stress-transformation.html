Stress transformation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Constitutive equation Stress
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
Resolution of a stress tensor onto differently oriented planes. For two compression-positive principal stresses, the resolved normal stress and shear stress trace a Mohr circle as the plane normal rotates, with double the physical rotation angle.
