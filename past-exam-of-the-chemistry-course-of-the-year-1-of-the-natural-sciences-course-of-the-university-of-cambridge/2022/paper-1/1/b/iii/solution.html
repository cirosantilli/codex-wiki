The two methyl groups give one six-proton NMR doublet centred at (1.319+1.296)/2=1.3075 ppm. The methine gives a one-proton NMR septet centred near 2.976 ppm. The latter is split by six equivalent methyl protons, producing the first-order 1:6:15:20:15:6:1 intensity pattern.

There is no methylene triplet or quartet: the six-proton integral and septet establish the branched skeleton.
