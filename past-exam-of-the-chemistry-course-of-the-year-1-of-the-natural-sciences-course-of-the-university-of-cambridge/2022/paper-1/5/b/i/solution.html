The forward and reverse pathways pass through the same transition state. Measured from the cis minimum, the forward barrier is Ea,1​; measured from the trans minimum, the reverse barrier is Ea,−1​. Since Ea,−1​=Ea,1​+2.23kJmol−1, the trans minimum lies 2.23kJmol−1 below the cis minimum in the elementary energy-profile model:
Etrans​−Ecis​=Ea,1​−Ea,−1​=−2.23kJmol−1.
(29)
The absolute barrier heights cannot be deduced from their difference. The following original profile uses an arbitrary transition-state height and labels that distinction explicitly.
