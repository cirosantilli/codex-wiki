Past exam of the chemistry course of the year 1 of the natural sciences course of the University of Cambridge / 2021 / Paper 1 / 1 / c / iii / Solution

 ... Past exam of the chemistry course of the year 1 of the natural sciences course of the University of Cambridge 2021 Paper 1 1 c iii
OurBigBook.com Words: 152
A: ethoxy CH2​ ↔ CH3​, J≃7Hz; vinyl CH ↔ each alkene methyl, small 4J≃1–1.5Hz.
C: ethoxy CH2​ ↔ CH3​, about 7Hz; the two OCH2​ proton types have a geminal coupling of about 9–10Hz; acetal CH ↔ C≡CH has 4J≃2Hz.
B: internal vinyl CH ↔ trans terminal H, about 17Hz; ↔ cis terminal H, about 10Hz; terminal CH2​ geminal coupling about 1–2Hz. Vinyl CH ↔ OCH2​ is about 6Hz. Both vicinal links along the butanoate alkyl chain are about 7–8Hz.
Use J=300Δδ when a separation is measured in ppm. Thus 0.057ppm corresponds to about 17Hz, whereas 0.023ppm corresponds to about 7Hz. These are approximate readings, not a fitted spin simulation.
The small four-bond splittings illustrate allylic scalar coupling and acetal-alkyne long-range coupling.
