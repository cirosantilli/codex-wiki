Suppress the filled 1s core pair, which contributes zero net bond order. For N2​, use the valence ordering
σ2s​<σ2s∗​<π2p​<σ2pz​​<π2p∗​<σ2pz​∗​
(1)
and occupy it as (σ2s​)2(σ2s∗​)2(π2p​)4(σ2pz​​)2.
For O2​, the ordering of the bonding 2p levels is reversed: σ2pz​​<π2p​. Occupancy is (σ2s​)2(σ2s∗​)2(σ2pz​​)2(π2p​)4(π2px​∗​)1(π2py​∗​)1, with parallel spins in the two degenerate antibonding pi orbitals.
Li2​ has (σ2s​)2; Be2​ has (σ2s​)2(σ2s∗​)2 in the elementary valence description. Empty 2p-derived levels must also be included to discuss mixing and Be2​'s weak bonding; their precise energies are not set by this qualitative diagram.
