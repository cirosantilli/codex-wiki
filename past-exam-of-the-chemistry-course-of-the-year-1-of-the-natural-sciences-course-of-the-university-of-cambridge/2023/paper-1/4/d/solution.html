For a reversible temperature change at constant pressure and fixed composition, dSm​=δqrev,m​/T and δqrev,m​=Cp,m​dT. Hence
dSm∘​=TCp,m∘​(T)​dT.
(23)
With T0​=298K, termwise integration yields
Sm∘​(T)−Sm∘​(T0​)=​alnT0​T​+b(T−T0​)+2c​(T2−T02​)+3d​(T3−T03​)−2e​(T−2−T0−2​).​​
(24)
The last sign follows from integrating eT−3, not eT−2. Every term has units JK−1mol−1; the logarithm has a dimensionless argument. Inserting 800 K gives an entropy increase of 41.733JK−1mol−1, and therefore
Sm∘​(NH3​,800K)=231.59JK−1mol−1.​
(25)
The standard pressure remains fixed during this integration; the 200-bar equilibrium mixture is not the standard state. Differentiating the integrated expression recovers Cp​/T, providing a check on all five terms. Using the ammonia heat-capacity polynomial here does not retroactively replace the explicitly requested constant-reaction-enthalpy approximation in the preceding equilibrium calculation.
