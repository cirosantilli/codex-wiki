OurBigBook.com Words: 178
Build the phenethyl grignard reagent, use one portion to make a phenethyl-substituted ester, and add two further equivalents to that ester. Write R=PhCH2​CH2​; the target is R3​COH, so all three carbon substituents must be phenethyl, not benzyl.
hydroboration followed by oxidation of styrene gives PhCH2​CH2​OH: use BH3​ in a suitable ether, followed by alkaline hydrogen peroxide. The Anti-Markovnikov addition regiochemistry is essential; acid-catalysed Markovnikov addition of water would give the wrong alcohol. Convert the primary alcohol to PhCH2​CH2​Br with phosphorus tribromide, then form RMgBr with magnesium in dry ether. The organomagnesium reagent must be prepared and used without a protic solvent.
carboxylate one portion with carbon dioxide and acidify to obtain RCO2​H, then esterify with ethanol under acid catalysis to give RCO2​Et. Treat the isolated ester with at least two equivalents of another portion of RMgBr and then perform an aqueous acidic work-up:
RCO2​Et2RMgBrthen aqueous acid​R3​COH.
(14)
The first addition to the ester gives a ketone after ethoxide elimination; the second addition gives the tertiary alkoxide. Work-up supplies its OH proton. The extra central carbon comes from carbon dioxide, and each original styrene contributes one intact eight-carbon phenethyl unit. The resulting carbon count is 1+3×8=25, matching the target skeleton.
