The integrals sum to eight hydrogens. Multiplet centres give the chemical shifts; a peak label at one edge of a multiplet is not its centre. The terminal vinyl hydrogens are chemically nonequivalent because one is cis and the other trans to the O-bound vinyl hydrogen. Each crotonyl CH couples to the other and to the methyl group; the resolved long-range interaction makes the methyl a doublet of doublets rather than a simple doublet.
