Here c,t mean central and terminal. Each listed occupied spatial orbital contains two electrons of opposite spin. The central N's two p atomic orbitals are perpendicular, so they can support the two perpendicular pi bonds. No central-N lone pair is left over: its positive formal charge accompanies four bonding pairs in this Lewis structure.
Allowing resonance mixes a terminal-N lone pair into the C–N–N π orbitals. In the alternative terminal-sp basis, the same electron count is four occupied σ orbitals (8 electrons), one terminal-sp lone pair (2), one perpendicular N–N π orbital (2), and two occupied orbitals of a three-centre C–N–N π system (4). The 16-electron count and the paired ground state survive this change of description; the localized picture does not assert two perfectly isolated double bonds.
