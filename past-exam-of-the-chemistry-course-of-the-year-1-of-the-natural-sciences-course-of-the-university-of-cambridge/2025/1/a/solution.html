The diagnostic infrared spectroscopy absorptions are the conjugated ester carbonyl near 1720cm−1 and the alkene stretch near 1650cm−1. Reasonable approximate ranges are 1715–1730 and 1620–1660cm−1, respectively. Conjugated system lowers the carbonyl stretching spectroscopic wavenumber relative to an unconjugated ester.
The ester C–O stretches and strong C–F stretches lie mainly in the 1000–1300cm−1 region. Vinylic C–H stretching is just above 3000cm−1, and saturated C–H stretching is about 2850–3000cm−1. There is no hydroxy group to give a broad O–H band.
