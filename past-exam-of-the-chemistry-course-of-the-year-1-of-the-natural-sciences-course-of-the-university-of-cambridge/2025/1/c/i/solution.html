The supplied carbon-13 spectrum is interpreted as proton-decoupled NMR, as indicated by its unsplit ethoxy signals. Proton decoupling removes C–H splitting but leaves C–F scalar spin-spin coupling. Three equivalent spin-21​ fluorine nuclei give each resolved quartet its 1:3:3:1 intensity pattern. The carbonyl's much smaller four-bond C–F interaction is unresolved in the plotted spectrum; “singlet” describes what is observed, rather than proving an exactly zero interaction.
The wide quartet is centred at (127.48+116.74)/2=122.11 ppm. The other two centres are (132.04+130.63)/2=131.335 and (129.09+128.85)/2=128.97 ppm. The signal near 77 ppm is the deuterated chloroform solvent, not another carbon of the analyte. The structure labels skip Ci​: the ethoxy methylene is Cj​.
