Bulk electroneutrality gives [NO3−​]=[H+]+2[Zn2+]​. Each dissolved zinc ion contributes two positive elementary charges, whereas each nitrate contributes one negative charge.
This assumes complete nitric acid dissociation, only H+ and Zn2+ as significant cations, and only nitrate as a significant anion. It neglects OH− at acidic pH, other dissolved salts, zinc complexes and additional ionic nitrogen products. It is a bulk charge balance; a thin charged electrode interface does not invalidate bulk electroneutrality.
