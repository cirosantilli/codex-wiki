The equilibrium molecular-acid concentration is [H2​N2​O2​]=0.162molL−1​. From the supplied pH convention,
[H+]=c∘10−4=10−4M.
(17)
With only these monovalent ions appreciably present, electroneutrality requires [HN2​O2−​]=[H+]. Substitution in the acid dissociation constant gives
[H2​N2​O2​]=(6.1690×10−8)(1M)(10−4M)2​=0.16210M.
(18)
This calculation assumes ideal thermodynamic activities for the acid and its conjugate base as well as for H+, negligible water autoionization, negligible second dissociation, and no additional electrolyte. The acid's analytical concentration would include its dissociated fraction and be approximately 0.16220M, rather than exactly the molecular-acid concentration requested.
