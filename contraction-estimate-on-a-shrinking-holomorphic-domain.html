Contraction estimate on a shrinking holomorphic domain (​∫0t​∂x​u(x,z)dz​α​≤R4α​∥u∥α​)

Write N=∥u∥α​, r=∣t∣ and A=α(1−s)>r. Along the straight integration segment, choose σ(τ)=s+(A−τ)/(2α). A Cauchy estimate on a disc of radius R(σ−s) bounds the integrand by 4αNτ/[R(A−τ)2]. Therefore
rA−r​​∫0t​∂x​u(x,z)dz​≤R4αN​[1+rA−r​log(1−r/A)]≤R4αN​.
(5)
The integral is taken at fixed x, and the disc and segment lie inside the shrinking domain. Consequently u↦∫0t​(iux​+f)dz is a contraction mapping when 0<α<R/4 and the holomorphic source f is bounded on the relevant closed polydisc. The source term has weighted norm at most αsup∣f∣, so the Banach fixed-point theorem gives a holomorphic solution with zero initial data.
