Two-interval approximation by one interval (infh interval​errD​(h)≤31​)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Statistical learning theory Shattering coefficient VC dimension VC dimension of interval indicators
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
For a target positive set consisting of two disjoint intervals of total probability p, choosing its heavier component has misclassification risk at most p/2. Choosing the spanning interval has misclassification risk at most 1−p. The smaller bound is at most 1/3. The constant is sharp: on three ordered atoms with equal masses and labels 1,0,1, every single interval makes a mistake on at least one atom. Together with the VC uniform convergence theorem, this gives weak PAC learning using an interval hypothesis class.
