Van der Waals force

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Force
OurBigBook.com Words: 323 Articles: 6
The Van der Waals force is a distance-dependent nonbonded interaction between atoms or molecules, including dispersion and dipolar contributions. In the attractive nonretarded pair model, an interaction energy U(r)=−β/r6 gives force magnitude 6β/r7. Macroscopic integration introduces a Hamaker constant; it does not replace the sign change and molecular cutoff needed at very short distances.
