For basic shear U(z)=Λz with a resting lower boundary, the rigid-boundary buoyancy condition for quasi-geostrophic waves and the decaying vertical exponential give
c=mΛ​,ω=kc,m=NK/∣f0​∣.
(7)
This is real. The edge wave propagates in the same laboratory direction as the basic flow above its resting lower wall. If the basic buoyancy is M2y+N2z, then Λ=−M2/f0​.
