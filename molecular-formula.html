Molecular formula

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 42 Articles: 1
A molecular formula gives the number of chemical atoms of each chemical element in a molecule. For urea, CH4​N2​O counts one carbon, four hydrogen, two nitrogen and one oxygen chemical atom. It records composition rather than the arrangement of chemical bonds.
