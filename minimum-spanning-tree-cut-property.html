Minimum spanning tree cut property

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Tree (graph theory) Spanning tree Minimum spanning tree
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The unique lightest edge across a cut of a graph belongs to every minimum spanning tree. If a spanning tree omits it, inserting it creates a graph cycle with another edge across that cut of a graph. Removing that heavier edge gives a lighter spanning tree, a contradiction. This proves the correctness of Prim algorithm and Kruskal algorithm for distinct weights.
