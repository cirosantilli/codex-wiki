Maclaurin series (∑n=0∞​f(n)(0)xn/n!)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Taylor's theorem Taylor series
OurBigBook.com Words: 14
A Taylor series centred at zero. Its coefficients are the derivatives there divided by the corresponding factorials.
