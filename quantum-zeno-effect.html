Quantum Zeno effect

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum measurement
OurBigBook.com Words: 358 Articles: 3
Inhibition of a transition by increasingly frequent ideal projective measurements that check whether a quantum system remains in its initial quantum state. For a normalized vector ψ, a fixed Hamiltonian operator H and the orthogonal projection P=∣ψ⟩⟨ψ∣, the probability of passing every check at N equally spaced times up to fixed time T is
PN​(T)=​⟨ψ,e−iHT/(Nℏ)ψ⟩​2N.
(4)
For a finite-dimensional Hilbert space, the quadratic short-time quantum survival probability implies PN​(T)→1. This is an ideal limit with instantaneous measurements and unitary time evolution between them.
