Torsion contractions of a left-parallel Lie-group connection (Ta​ac​=0,Ta​bc​Tb​ad​=Kcd​)

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Left and right translation on a Lie group Flat translation connections on a Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
Declare a global frame of left-invariant vector fields parallel on a semisimple Lie group. This affine connection is flat and preserves a left-invariant metric tensor because its frame components are constant. If [ea​,ec​]=Ca​bc​eb​, its torsion tensor is Ta​bc​=−Ca​bc​. For fixed c, the matrix with entries Ta​bc​ is adec​. Its trace vanishes because a semisimple Lie algebra is perfect and adjoints of brackets are commutators. The trace of the product for c,d is the Killing form Kcd​, proving both contractions. This flat metric connection differs from the curved torsion-free Levi-Civita connection of the Killing metric.
