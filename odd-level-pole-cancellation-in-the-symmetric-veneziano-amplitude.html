For identical open-string tachyons, s+t+u=−4/α′. At α′s=n−1, the ordered Veneziano amplitude residues in the s,t and s,u terms differ by (−1)n, so their symmetric sum cancels odd-level s-channel poles at generic t. In particular the massless vector does not couple to this identical-scalar singlet amplitude. It remains a state of the open-string spectrum and is visible in suitable ordered or color-dependent amplitudes. Pole absence due to an external-state selection rule must not be interpreted as absence of the exchanged state from the theory.
