Plane of incidence

 ... Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Plane electromagnetic wave Interface conditions for electromagnetic fields Dielectric-interface boundary conditions Fresnel equations
OurBigBook.com Words: 142 Articles: 2
For a ray meeting an interface at nonzero incidence angle, the plane spanned by the incoming wavevector and the surface normal. transverse electric polarization has electric field perpendicular to this plane, while transverse magnetic polarization has electric field in it. At exact normal incidence, the surface normal and ray alone do not select a unique plane.
