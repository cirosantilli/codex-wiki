Degree of a map between oriented manifolds (degf)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Degree of a continuous mapping
OurBigBook.com Words: 627 Articles: 12
For a map f:M→N between closed connected oriented d-manifolds, its degree is the integer determined by f∗​[M]=(degf)[N] in top-dimensional homology.
