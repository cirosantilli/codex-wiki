Spin wave

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Goldstone boson Goldstone-mode effective free energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 1
A spin wave is a smooth long-wavelength fluctuation of the orientation of an ordered continuous-symmetry field. In an O(2) phase it is a fluctuation of the angle θ governed at leading order by the Gaussian gradient energy.
