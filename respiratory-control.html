Respiratory control

 Home Chemistry Biochemistry Energy metabolism Electron transport chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 455 Articles: 2
Respiratory control links mitochondrial oxygen consumption to demand for ATP. When ADP permits faster ATP synthase turnover, proton return lowers the opposing electrochemical gradient, permitting faster electron transport.[ref]
The mitochondrial oxygen electrode assay tests this connection by changing ADP availability while observing oxygen. A slowed reaction rate alone cannot locate the cause: depleted substrate, scarce oxygen, impaired enzymes and a strong opposing electrochemical gradient can all restrict turnover. A renewed response to ADP helps distinguish these possibilities.
