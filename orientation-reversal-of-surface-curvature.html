Orientation reversal of surface curvature

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Gauss map Second fundamental form Mean curvature
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
Replacing the chosen unit normal N by −N replaces the shape operator S by −S. On a surface, Gaussian curvature K=detS is unchanged, while mean curvature H=tr(S)/2 changes sign.
