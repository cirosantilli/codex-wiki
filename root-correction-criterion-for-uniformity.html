Root correction criterion for uniformity (u↦up:Uj​/Uj+1​⟶Uj+1​/Uj+2​)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Profinite group Pro-p group Uniform pro-p group
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
For odd p and a finitely generated powerful pro-p group, put Uj​=Gpj. The displayed group homomorphisms are surjective. If one has a nonzero kernel, choose x∈Uj​∖Uj+1​ with xp∈Uj+2​. Successive corrections x↦xur​, with ur​∈Ur​, cancel its p-th power one layer at a time, using (xur​)p≡xpurp​(modUr+2​). Compactness and the separated power filtration give a nonidentity limit of order p. Thus a torsion-free group has constant layer indices and is a uniform pro-p group. Conversely constant indices make every displayed map injective; comparison at the first differing power layer proves that x↦xp is injective, so there is no nontrivial torsion.
