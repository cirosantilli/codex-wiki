q:(X×I)⊔Y⟶M(f)
(9)
for the quotient map defining the mapping cylinder, and put j(x)=q(x,0). Since the equivalence relation only attaches points at height 1 to Y, distinct points at height 0 remain distinct. Thus j is an injective continuous map.
Define the homotopy
H(x,s)=q(x,1−s).
(10)
It is a continuous map, with H(x,0)=q(x,1)=i(f(x)) and H(x,1)=j(x). Hence
i∘f≃j,j injective.​
(11)
Even if f identifies many points, the free end of the mapping cylinder retains a separate copy of every point of X.
