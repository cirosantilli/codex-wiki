The normal closure ⟨⟨B⟩⟩G​ is the smallest normal subgroup of G containing B, equivalently the intersection of all normal subgroups containing B. Any such normal subgroup contains every gb±1g−1 and hence every finite product of these elements. Thus N⊆⟨⟨B⟩⟩G​.
Include the empty product so that 1∈N, in particular when B=∅. Products of elements of N are obtained by concatenating their factor lists. Inversion reverses the list and replaces ϵi​ by −ϵi​, so N is closed under inverses. For any k∈G,

Therefore N is a normal subgroup and it contains B. Minimality of the normal closure gives the reverse inclusion, proving
N=⟨⟨B⟩⟩G​.​

The notation n∈N in a finite-product description is understood to allow the empty product; otherwise the empty generating subset would require a separate identity convention.
In a group presentation, X is a set of formal generators and R is a subset of the free group F(X), consisting of relators which are required to become the identity. The presented group is
⟨X∣R⟩=F(X)/⟨⟨R⟩⟩F(X)​.​
(5)
The universal property of a group presentation says that a function X→H extends to a group homomorphism from the presented group precisely when every relator evaluates to 1 in H: first extend freely to F(X), then factor through the quotient because its kernel contains the normal closure of R.
A rotation α by 2π/n and a reflection β of a regular n-gon satisfy
αn=1,β2=1,βαβ=α−1.
(6)
The last identity holds because reflection reverses the sense of rotation, and it implies αβαβ=1. Hence all defining relators vanish, giving a homomorphism θ:D2n​→Mn​ with θ(σ)=α and θ(τ)=β.
To prove it is an isomorphism without presupposing the order of the presented group, use στ=τσ−1. Move every occurrence of τ to the left, cancel pairs by τ2=1, and reduce exponents modulo n. Every element has one of the forms
σjorτσj,0≤j<n.
(7)
Thus ∣D2n​∣≤2n. For the usual regular polygon with n≥3, the n rotations αj are distinct and the n reflections βαj are distinct; no rotation is a reflection, since their planar orientation behavior differs. The supplied description says these exhaust Mn​, so ∣Mn​∣=2n, and every one lies in the image of θ. Surjectivity and the order bound force ∣D2n​∣=2n and trivial kernel. Thus θ is an isomorphism.
