Kerr effect

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Nonlinear optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 320 Articles: 6
The Kerr effect is a change in a material's refractive index quadratic in an applied electric field. A static applied electric field can produce linear birefringence; the optical field itself can also produce an intensity-dependent refractive index in nonlinear optics.
