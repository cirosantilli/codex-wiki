The Kármán-Howarth equation evolves two-point correlations in homogeneous isotropic turbulence. In trace form it is ∂t​C=r−2∂r​[r−1∂r​(r4u3K)]+2νr−2∂r​(r2C′). Integrating with radial volume weight reduces conservation of the Saffman integral to vanishing nonlinear and viscous boundary fluxes.
