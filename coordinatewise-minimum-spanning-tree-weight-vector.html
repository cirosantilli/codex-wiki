Coordinatewise minimum spanning-tree weight vector

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Tree (graph theory) Spanning tree Minimum spanning tree
OurBigBook.com Words: 110
Sort the edge weights of a minimum spanning tree T and of any spanning tree S in nondecreasing order. Then wi​(T)≤wi​(S) for every position. To prove it, fix a threshold t and retain the graph's edges of weight at most t. If this graph has c connected components, T contains a spanning forest of each component using those edges: otherwise a light edge joining two of its pieces could replace an edge heavier than t on its tree path, decreasing total weight. Hence exactly ∣V∣−c edges of T have weight at most t. No other spanning tree can have more, since its retained edges form a forest inside those components. These threshold counts imply the coordinatewise inequality for sorted weights.
