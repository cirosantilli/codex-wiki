Limited-memory BFGS

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Newton's method in optimization Quasi-Newton method BFGS method
OurBigBook.com Words: 128
Limited-memory BFGS is a quasi-Newton method that applies an inverse-Hessian matrix approximation without storing a dense square matrix. It retains only a fixed number ℓ of recent secant pairs sk​=xk+1​−xk​ and yk​=∇f(xk+1​)−∇f(xk​). The standard two-loop recursion computes the search direction using O(ℓd) storage and arithmetic in dimension d.
With a positive-definite initial metric and safeguarded curvature updates, the step is xk+1​=xk​−αk​Hk​∇f(xk​), with αk​ chosen by a line search. It is useful when full-memory BFGS would require too much storage. Fixed-memory convergence results typically give linear convergence under strong convexity, bounded metric and suitable line search assumptions; one must not automatically transfer the full-memory superlinear theorem. In a nonconvex problem, a local method does not by itself certify a global minimum.[ref]
