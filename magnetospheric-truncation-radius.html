Magnetospheric truncation radius (rm​)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Accretion disk Accretion flow Magnetically channelled accretion
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The inner radius at which a stellar magnetic dipole field disrupts a surrounding accretion disk. In a free fall pressure-balance estimate with fixed aspect ratio ϵ=H/r, rm​∼(ϵ2μ4/(GMM˙2))1/7. Geometry and inflow structure change its numerical coefficient.
