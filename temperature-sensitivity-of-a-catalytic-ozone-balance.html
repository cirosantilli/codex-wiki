Temperature sensitivity of a catalytic ozone balance (dln[O3​]/d(T−1))

 Home Chemistry Atmospheric chemistry Odd oxygen Chapman cycle
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
Suppose a reduced steady balance is P=AX2+BX, where X=[O3​], and hold P and all specified background concentrations fixed. With u=1/T, dlnA/du=−a and dlnB/du=−b, implicit differentiation gives dlnX/du=(aAX2+bBX)/(2AX2+BX). The quadratic loss term includes temperature-dependent O/O3 partitioning as well as the direct reaction coefficient. Holding the loss ratio fixed over a finite change is an additional approximation; solving the new quadratic provides a check.
