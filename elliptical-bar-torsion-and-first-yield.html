Elliptical-bar torsion and first yield (R=a2+b2πμa3b3​,∣Ω∣Y​=2μa2b(a2+b2)τY​​(a>b))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Torsion stress function for a simply connected bar
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
On x2/a2+y2/b2<1, the torsion stress function for a simply connected bar is ϕ=μΩa2b2(1−x2/a2−y2/b2)/(a2+b2). Its Laplacian is −2μΩ and its boundary value is zero, proving the formula by uniqueness of the Poisson equation with Dirichlet data. Integration gives M=πμΩa3b3/(a2+b2), hence the displayed stiffness R=M/Ω.
The three principal stresses are 0,±∣∇ϕ∣. With τY​ defined as the shear yield stress, the Tresca yield criterion is ∣∇ϕ∣≤τY​. For a>b its maximum is 2∣C∣/b at (0,±b), where C=μΩa2b2/(a2+b2). Equating that maximum to τY​ gives the displayed first-yield twist. The ends of the minor axis yield first; geometric distance from the bar axis alone does not determine stress in a noncircular section.
