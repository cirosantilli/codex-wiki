Poisson arrivals see time averages

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The Poisson arrivals see time averages property says that an external Poisson process independent of a stationary system sees the system-state distribution at its arrival times. Thus the fraction of arrivals that accept a state-dependent admission rule p(n) is ∑n​πn​p(n).
