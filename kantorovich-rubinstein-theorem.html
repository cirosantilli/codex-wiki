On a Polish space (X,ρ), probability measures with finite first moments satisfy
W1​(μ,ν)=supLip(f)≤1​(∫fdμ−∫fdν).
(46)
The supremum runs over functions with Lipschitz constant at most one. Fixing f(x0​)=0 ensures integrability from the moment assumption.
