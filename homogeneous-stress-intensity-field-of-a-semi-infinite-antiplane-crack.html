Homogeneous stress-intensity field of a semi-infinite antiplane crack (Fh′​(z)=iC/z​)

 ... Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Antiplane shear Complex stress potential for antiplane shear Hilbert problem for an antiplane crack Cauchy solution for a steadily moving semi-infinite antiplane crack
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
For real C, this analytic homogeneous field has real boundary values on both faces of the negative-axis crack, so it adds no face shear traction. Ahead of the tip it adds stress −μC/x​. Its stress tends to zero at infinity, but its primitive grows like z​, producing unbounded remote displacement and infinite total elastic energy. Thus merely requiring vanishing far stress does not uniquely fix the face-loaded crack solution. Excluding an added remote stress-intensity field, for example through the localized-load bounded-displacement far condition, removes this homogeneous ambiguity. Ordinary inverse-square-root tip stress remains allowed and has finite energy in any bounded neighbourhood of the tip.
