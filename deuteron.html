Deuteron (A=2,J=1,I=0)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Atomic nucleus
OurBigBook.com Words: 65 Articles: 1
The bound atomic nucleus containing one proton and one neutron. Its ground-state spin is one and its isospin is zero. The appropriately quantized toroidal two-Skyrmion models these quantum numbers.
