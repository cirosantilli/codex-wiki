Scalar graph power-counting identity (D=d−(d/2−1)E−∑n​[n−d(n/2−1)]Vn​)

For a connected scalar field theory graph without derivative vertices, each independent loop momentum supplies d powers and each internal scalar propagator removes two. Thus D=dL−2I. The graph identities L=I−∑n​Vn​+1 and 2I+E=∑n​nVn​ give the displayed formula. The mass dimension of the field is (d−2)/2 and that of gn​ is n−d(n/2−1). Negative-dimensional couplings allow the superficial degree to grow with vertex count. This is overall power counting in quantum field theory; subgraphs can diverge even when the whole graph has negative degree.
