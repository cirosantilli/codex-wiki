Chlorophyll

 Home Biology Metabolism Photosynthesis Light-dependent reactions
OurBigBook.com Words: 60 Articles: 1
Chlorophyll is a family of molecules that absorb light for photosynthesis. In chloroplasts, chlorophyll works with proteins in thylakoid membranes; absorbing light initiates energy capture rather than directly constructing a sugar. See OpenStax Biology 2e, 8.2: photosynthetic pigments.
