Rational model of a collapsed complex projective subspace

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Rational homotopy theory Sullivan minimal model
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
The quotient CP5/CP2 is five-connected and has cohomology only in degrees 0,6,8,10, with positive products zero. Its minimal model therefore starts with closed a6​,b8​,c10​; no decomposable differential can occur below degree twelve. Sending these three generators to the cohomology basis and all later generators to zero is a chain map and a quasi-isomorphism. Thus its full minimal model is the same as that of S6∨S8∨S10. It starts with generators u11​,v13​,w15​,t15​ satisfying du=a2,dv=ab,dw=ac,dt=b2, followed by generators killing remaining products and syzygies.
