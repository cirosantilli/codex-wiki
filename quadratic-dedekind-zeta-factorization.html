Quadratic Dedekind zeta factorization

OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For a quadratic field K of discriminant D,
ζK​(s)=ζQ​(s)L(χD​,s),
(35)
where χD​(p) is 1, −1, or 0 according as (p) splits, remains prime, or ramifies in OK​. Comparing the three possible local Euler factors proves the formal identity.
