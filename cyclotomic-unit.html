Cyclotomic unit (cn​(a))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Cyclotomic polynomial Cyclotomic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 584 Articles: 8
Cyclotomic units include roots of unity and suitable ratios (1−ζa)/(1−ζ) in cyclotomic fields. Compatible roots ζ make these ratios norm-compatible along cyclotomic towers. Their closed norm-limit module is a concrete submodule of global units, and its local images generate the power series in the Iwasawa main conjecture.
