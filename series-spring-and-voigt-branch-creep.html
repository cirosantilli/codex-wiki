Series spring and Voigt-branch creep (J(t)=1/E0​+(1/E1​)(1−e−E1​t/η))

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity Standard linear solid model
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
A spring E0​ in series with a parallel spring-dashpot branch E1​,η has creep compliance J(t)=1/E0​+(1/E1​)(1−e−E1​t/η). It includes immediate and delayed recoverable strain, but no unbounded viscous flow. Large strain, damage or anisotropy require additional constitutive structure.
