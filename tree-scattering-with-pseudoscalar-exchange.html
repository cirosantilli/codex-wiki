Tree scattering with pseudoscalar exchange (V=−igγ5)

The pseudoscalar Yukawa interaction with coefficient −g has the displayed Feynman vertex. Its two-fermion tree-level Feynman diagrams are the t and u exchanges; antisymmetry of the final identical fermions requires their relative minus. Fermion-antifermion scattering has t exchange and s annihilation, again with a relative minus.
