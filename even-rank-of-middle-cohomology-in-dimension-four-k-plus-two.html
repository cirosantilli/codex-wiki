Even rank of middle cohomology in dimension four k plus two (b2k+1​(M4k+2) is even)

 ... Algebraic topology Cohomology Cap product Fundamental class Poincare duality Poincare duality pairing
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
For a closed oriented manifold, the rational middle-dimensional cup-product pairing is nondegenerate by Poincare duality. Its degree is odd, so graded commutativity makes it skew-symmetric. A nondegenerate skew-symmetric form has even dimension. In particular a closed oriented six-manifold cannot have a rank-one third cohomology group.
