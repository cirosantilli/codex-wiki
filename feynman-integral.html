Feynman integral

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
A Feynman integral is the momentum-space integral associated with a Feynman diagram: each independent loop momentum is integrated with measure ddk/(2π)d, and propagators, vertex numerators and momentum conservation determine the integrand. Its definition also requires a pole prescription and, when divergent, a regularization in quantum field theory. Feynman parameters combine propagator denominators and permit shifts of loop momenta. A massless two-propagator example is ∫ddk/[(2π)dk2(p−k)2], with its causal prescriptions understood. Subtracting local counterterms turns a regulated diagram into a finite contribution to a renormalized amplitude.
