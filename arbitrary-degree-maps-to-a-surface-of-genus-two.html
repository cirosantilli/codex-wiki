Arbitrary-degree maps to a surface of genus two (Σ∣k∣+1​⟶Σ2​,deg=k)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Degree of a continuous mapping Degree of a map between oriented manifolds
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
For k>0, an epimorphism π1​(Σ2​)→Z/k gives a connected k-sheeted cover. Its Euler characteristic is −2k, hence its genus is k+1, and the lifted orientation gives degree k. For negative k compose the ∣k∣-sheeted cover with an orientation-reversing homeomorphism of the target. For k=0 take a constant map from the torus.
