Circuit family

 Home Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Circuit complexity
OurBigBook.com Words: 222 Articles: 5
A circuit family (Cn​) contains one circuit for each input length n. The circuit for one length may be chosen independently of those for other lengths, so a family is a nonuniform model of computation.
