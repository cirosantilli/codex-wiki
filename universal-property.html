Universal property

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A universal property characterizes an object by a prescribed family of morphisms and a unique factorization of every competing family. It determines that object up to a unique compatible isomorphism. Examples include the pushout in a category and the universal property of the tensor product of modules.
