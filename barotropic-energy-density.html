Barotropic energy density (ρU′−U=P)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Barotropic fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For a barotropic fluid with pressure P=P(ρ), choose an energy density U satisfying ρU′(ρ)−U=P. The continuity equation then gives ∂t​U+∇⋅(Uu)=−P∇⋅u. For an isothermal equation of state P=cs2​ρ, one choice is U=cs2​ρln(ρ/ρref​). A fixed reference density changes U only by a conserved mass term. The same construction holds with surface density and vertically integrated pressure. This closure energy need not equal the microscopic internal energy of a gas maintained at fixed temperature.
