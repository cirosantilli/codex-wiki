Spinor Lorentz generator commutator ([σμν,γρ]=2i(gνργμ−gμργν))

Define σμν=2i​[γμ,γν] using the gamma matrix Clifford algebra. Moving γρ through the two factors with {γμ,γν}=2gμν proves the displayed operator commutator. For real antisymmetric ωμν​ and S=1−4i​σμνωμν​, it gives S−1γρS=γρ+ωρν​γν+O(ω2). This is the infinitesimal Lorentz covariance identity required by the Dirac equation.
