Lorentz force (F=q(E+v×B))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 8k Articles: 70
The Lorentz force is the force exerted by an electric field E and a magnetic field B on a body with electric charge q and velocity v. In SI units its form in nonrelativistic classical mechanics, using mass m and Newton's second law, is
F=q(E+v×B),mdtdv​=q(E+v×B).
(13)
Both fields are evaluated at the body's position at the current time. The electric field contributes qE even at rest, whereas the magnetic force depends on velocity through a cross product. [ref]; [ref].
For a concrete charged particle in a uniform magnetic field, use nondimensionalization to stipulate m=q=B=1, magnetic field B=(0,0,1), zero electric field, initial position (0,0,0), and initial velocity (1,0,0). These are rescaled quantities, not an assertion that the mass, electric charge, and magnetic field have the same physical units. At the start, (1,0,0)×(0,0,1)=(0,−1,0), so the magnetic acceleration points toward −y. The resulting circular motion is
r(t)=(sint,cost−1,0),v(t)=(cost,−sint,0),a(t)=(−sint,−cost,0).
(14)
At t=π/2 the position is (1,−1,0) and the velocity is (0,−1,0); at t=π the position is (0,−2,0) and the velocity is (−1,0,0). Thus the circle has center (0,−1,0) and radius one. Viewed from +z toward the xy plane, the motion is clockwise. Changing only the electric charge to q=−1 gives position (sint,1−cost,0) and reverses the bending to counterclockwise.
Adding initial velocity vz​=0.5 leaves the transverse circular motion unchanged and gives
r(t)=(sint,cost−1,0.5t),v(t)=(cost,−sint,0.5).
(15)
This is a helix of transverse radius one, cyclotron frequency ωc​=1, and transverse period T=2π; the advance along z in one turn is π. The parallel component of velocity stays constant because the magnetic force has no parallel component: there is no magnetic acceleration along the magnetic field. If the initial velocity were entirely parallel to the magnetic field, the magnetic force would vanish and the path would be straight. [ref].
More generally, with zero electric field, no other forces, and a uniform constant magnetic field of magnitude B>0, decompose velocity into perpendicular and parallel components. For nonzero electric charge q, the perpendicular speed v⊥​ determines the gyroradius, while the cyclotron frequency is an angular frequency:
r=∣q∣Bmv⊥​​,ωc​=m∣q∣B​,T=ωc​2π​=∣q∣B2πm​.
(16)
These are nonrelativistic formulas; see the relativistic cyclotron orbit for the correction. The beauty is that increasing perpendicular speed increases the radius in exactly the proportion needed to leave the period unchanged. Conversely, known magnetic field, perpendicular speed, and measured radius determine the magnitude of the electric charge divided by mass. This makes the same geometry useful for comparing bodies with different electric charge and mass.
Two short checks explain the result. First, the cross product gives v×B=(−sint,−cost,0) for both stipulated positive-electric charge paths, exactly their acceleration. Second, the transverse magnetic force supplies the centripetal acceleration, so mv⊥2​/r=∣q∣v⊥​B; for v⊥​>0 this gives the gyroradius, and ωc​=v⊥​/r gives the cyclotron frequency.
Magnetic forces do no work: the instantaneous power of the magnetic part of the Lorentz force vanishes because a cross product is perpendicular to its first argument. The work-energy theorem therefore gives
dtdK​=F⋅v=qE⋅v+qv⋅(v×B)=qE⋅v,K=21​m∣v∣2.
(17)
The dot product v⋅(v×B) is zero at every instant, rather than only after a full turn. With zero electric field and no other forces, the kinetic energy and speed remain constant; the electric field can increase or decrease kinetic energy according to the sign of qE⋅v. In the two stipulated examples the constant kinetic energy is respectively K=1/2 and K=5/8 in rescaled units. The extra parallel velocity raises kinetic energy without changing the gyroradius or the cyclotron frequency.
For an electric example in rescaled units, stipulate m=q=1, zero magnetic field, constant electric field E=(2,0,0), no other forces, and rest at t=0. The Lorentz force gives acceleration (2,0,0) and velocity (2t,0,0), hence kinetic energy K=2t2 and power dK/dt=4t=qE⋅v. At t=1, the kinetic energy has risen from zero to two: the electric field has done positive work.
The existing electromagnetic field tensor formulation expresses the same interaction covariantly. For four-momentum pμ and four-velocity uμ, using proper time τ, the Lorentz force law is
dτdpμ​=qFμνuν​.
(18)
Its spatial and temporal components are
dtdp​=q(E+v×B),dtdE​=qE⋅v.
(19)
