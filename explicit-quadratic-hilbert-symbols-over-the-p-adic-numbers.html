Explicit quadratic Hilbert symbols over the p-adic numbers ((pαu,pβv)p​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For odd p and p-adic units u,v, the quadratic Hilbert symbol is
(pαu,pβv)p​=(−1)αβ(p−1)/2(pu​)β(pv​)α.
(45)
The Legendre symbols record the two unit square classes. For p=2, put ϵ(u)=(u−1)/2mod2 and ω(u)=(u2−1)/8mod2. Then
(2αu,2βv)2​=(−1)ϵ(u)ϵ(v)+αω(v)+βω(u).
(46)
The basis −1,2,5 of the square-class group of the 2-adic numbers gives this formula: its only negative basis pairings are (−1,−1)2​ and (2,5)2​=(5,2)2​. The first is obstructed by sums of squares modulo four, the second by x2−2y2 modulo eight; the remaining pairings have explicit field norm representatives.
