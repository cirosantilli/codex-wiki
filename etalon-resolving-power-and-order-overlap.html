Etalon resolving power and order overlap (dF≥Mc/(2Δν),d<c/(2Δν))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Fabry–Pérot interferometer Finesse
OurBigBook.com Words: 136
For an ideal air-gap Fabry–Pérot etalon at normal incidence, the free spectral range is νFSR​=c/(2d) and the peak full width at half maximum is δν=νFSR​/F. To separate a pair of lines spaced by Δν by at least M peak widths, require dF≥Mc/(2Δν). Putting both lines inside one unambiguous free spectral range additionally requires d<c/(2Δν), so F>M. In terms of their vacuum wavelengths, c/Δν=λ1​λ2​/∣λ2​−λ1​∣.
The strict order condition needs a margin if tails from adjacent periodic peaks are to be negligible. If every neighbouring peak in a scanned two-line periodic spectrum must be separated by M widths, the stronger condition is min(Δν,νFSR​−Δν)≥MνFSR​/F. Ordinary resolving power alone does not prevent ambiguous repeated orders. A fixed etalon's transmission as a function of wavelength is a periodic-in-frequency filter; it is not automatically a plot of the incident source's spectral lines.
