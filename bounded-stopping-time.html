Bounded stopping time (T≤N)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Stopping time
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A bounded stopping time is a stopping time bounded almost surely by a deterministic finite constant. In discrete time, a bound T≤N makes MT​ integrable whenever M0​,…,MN​ are integrable random variables, and the optional stopping theorem applies to a martingale without additional limiting hypotheses. Almost-sure finiteness alone is weaker than boundedness.
