Mushy layer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Stefan problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 801 Articles: 11
A mushy layer contains solid crystals and liquid in local phase equilibrium, rather than a single sharp phase boundary. Its solid fraction changes as heat and solute are transported. A concentration-dependent liquidus couples temperature to liquid composition; a connected crystal framework makes the residual liquid behave as a porous-media flow.
