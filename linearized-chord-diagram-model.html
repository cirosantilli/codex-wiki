Linearized chord diagram model (Gn(1)​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Random graph Preferential attachment
OurBigBook.com Words: 259 Articles: 2
Start with one self-loop, and at step t add one vertex and one edge. An old vertex i is chosen with probability di​(t−1)/(2t−1), and the new vertex is chosen with probability 1/(2t−1), producing a self-loop. Self-loops contribute two to the degree of a vertex. There are n edges and total vertex degree 2n at time n. This exact normalization is distinct from treating the total vertex degree as the number of edges.
