Free category on independent arrows (L(S)=∐s∈S​[1])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Category of small categories
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
Take two distinct objects and one nonidentity arrow for each element of S, without arrows between different copies. A functor from this category to C is exactly a choice of one arbitrary arrow of C for each element of S. Consequently this construction is left adjoint to the functor taking the set of all arrows of a small category. Its free object has three arrows per generator, including the two identity arrows.
