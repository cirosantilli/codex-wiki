Isotropic inverse-Compton drag (P=(4/3)σT​cuph​β2γ2)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Compton wavelength Compton scattering Inverse Compton scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
An isotropic photon bath with total radiation energy density uph​ exerts this drag on an electron in the Thomson scattering regime. Its stress-energy tensor has spatial diagonal entries uph​/3. A boost to the electron frame gives radiation energy flux S∥′​=−(4/3)cγ2βuph​; multiplying its radiation force σT​S∥′​/c by the laboratory speed gives the electron's energy loss. This is net energy transferred, not all scattered photon energy. If only the magnetic wave-energy density is used, it is half of uph​ and the numerical coefficient doubles.
