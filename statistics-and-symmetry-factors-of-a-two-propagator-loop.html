Statistics and symmetry factors of a two-propagator loop

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram Feynman rule Feynman-diagram symmetry factor
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
A closed Dirac loop gives a minus sign and a spinor trace. Replacing it by two distinguishable real scalar species removes both, with no extra interchange factor. One identical real scalar species instead has Feynman-diagram symmetry factor two. A cubic interaction gϕχ2/2 has vertex ig, whereas gϕχ2 has vertex 2ig; those choices give different numerical coefficients even though their drawn loop topology is the same.
