Conjugacy class splitting in a prime-index normal subgroup

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group action Conjugation action Conjugacy class
OurBigBook.com Words: 195 Articles: 2
If N⊴G has prime index p, a conjugacy class of G lying in N splits into one or p conjugacy classes of N. Conjugation by G acts transitively on these smaller classes, while N fixes every one. This factors through G/N, and the orbit-stabilizer theorem makes the number of classes a divisor of p.
