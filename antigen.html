Antigen

 Home Biology Protein Antibody
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 2
In antibody binding, an antigen is material bearing a recognized epitope. An antigen need not be a protein; polysaccharides can also carry epitopes. One antigen may present several different epitopes, so recognizing one feature does not imply recognizing every part of the target. [ref]
