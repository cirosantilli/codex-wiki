Larmor theorem (ω=−eB/(2m))

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
For particles with the same charge-to-mass ratio, rotationally invariant mutual central forces, and a weak uniform magnetic field, a frame with the displayed angular velocity cancels the velocity-dependent magnetic Lorentz force against the Coriolis force. The remaining rotational and magnetic terms are quadratic in B. Thus, to first order in the field, the rotating-frame equations coincide with the zero-field equations. This is the classical dynamical basis of Larmor precession; equality is an approximation rather than an exact removal of the magnetic field.
