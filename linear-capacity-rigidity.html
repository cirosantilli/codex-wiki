Linear capacity rigidity (c(TE)=c(E) for all ellipsoids⟹T∗ω=±ω)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
An invertible real linear map preserving symplectic capacities of all solid ellipsoids is symplectic or anti-symplectic. For a pair with ω(u,v)=1, if 0<∣ω(TTu,TTv)∣<1, complete this pair and its normalized transformed pair to symplectic bases. The resulting transpose matrix sends a unit ball into a symplectic cylinder of radius less than one, contradicting the capacity axioms after iterating the resulting linear map: its first coordinate pair contracts geometrically, while the image ellipsoid retains positive capacity. Perturbation handles a zero pairing; using T−1 handles absolute value greater than one. Thus the absolute bilinear pairings agree. Their squares agree as polynomials, and the polynomial ring being an integral domain forces one global sign.
