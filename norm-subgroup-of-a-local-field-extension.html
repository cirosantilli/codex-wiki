Norm subgroup of a local field extension (NL/K​(L×))

 ... Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Maximal abelian extension Local class field theory Local Artin reciprocity
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 3
For a finite extension of local fields L/K, its norm subgroup is the image of the field norm NL/K​:L×→K×. For an abelian extension, it is the kernel of the induced local Artin map to Gal(L/K).
