Mass-weighted elastic normal modes (H^uα​=ρωα2​uα​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Normal mode
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 1
For a conservative elastic body, the stiffness operator is a self-adjoint operator under compatible boundary conditions. The dynamical operator ρ−1H^ is self-adjoint in the mass inner product ⟨u,v⟩ρ​=∫ρu∗⋅vdV. Choosing mass-normalized normal modes converts the field equation into independent forced oscillators. If the normal modes have mass norm Nα​ instead, each projected force must be divided by Nα​.
