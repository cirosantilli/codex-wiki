Center of symmetry (S=2c−S)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Rigid transformation Point reflection
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
A point c is a center of symmetry of a set S in Euclidean space if point reflection x↦2c−x maps S onto itself. Every ellipse has its center of an ellipse as a center of symmetry. More generally, a nonempty bounded set has at most one: two such reflections compose to translation by twice the difference of their centers, and iterating a nonzero translation contradicts boundedness.
