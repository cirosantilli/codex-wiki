Fibonacci multiple-index divisibility

OurBigBook.com Words: 29
For positive integers m,n, the Fibonacci numbers satisfy Fm​∣Fmn​. The Fibonacci addition formula gives Fmn+m​=Fm−1​Fmn​+Fm​Fmn+1​, so mathematical induction on n proves the assertion. This explains why every fifth Fibonacci number is divisible by F5​=5.
