Mixed log-likelihood derivative obstruction to parameter separation (∂ψ​∂λ​logf)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical parameter Nuisance parameter
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
On a common positive support, a factorization f=ϕ0​(x)ϕC​(x;ψ)ϕS​(x;λ) implies ∂ψ​∂λ​logf=0. Thus any nonzero mixed derivative prevents this exact separation. For n independent gamma distributed observations with shape a and rate b, this mixed derivative is n/b, so exact separation of shape and rate into such factors is impossible.
