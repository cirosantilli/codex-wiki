Bilinear map

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Multilinear map
OurBigBook.com Words: 401 Articles: 5
A bilinear map is a map B:V×W→U between vector spaces that is linear in each argument when the other is fixed. The nonlinear temperature advection can be written as the diagonal of a bilinear map, N(ϕ,χ)=−u[ϕ]⋅∇χ, when velocity depends linearly on temperature. Unlike a bilinear form, its output need not be a scalar.
