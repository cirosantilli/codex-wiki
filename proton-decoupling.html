Proton decoupling

 Home Chemistry Nuclear magnetic resonance
OurBigBook.com Words: 21 Articles: 1
Irradiation that removes resolved scalar spin-spin coupling to protons from another nucleus’s spectrum. Other couplings, such as carbon–fluorine coupling in a proton-decoupled carbon-13 NMR spectrum, can remain.
