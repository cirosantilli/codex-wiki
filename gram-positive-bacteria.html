Gram-positive bacteria

 Home Biology Organism Prokaryote Bacteria
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
Typical Gram-positive bacteria have a relatively thick peptidoglycan layer outside the cell membrane, without the bacterial outer membrane of typical Gram-negative bacteria. This is a representative cell envelope pattern, not a complete description of every member of bacteria.
