Cutoff independence of an oscillatory integral (RN−r+k→0,r>N+k)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Oscillatory integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For an amplitude of symbol order N in k frequency variables, applying a phase integration operator r>N+k times makes the pairing with a test function absolutely integrable. Derivatives of a large-frequency cutoff are supported where ∣θ∣≍R and give an error bounded by CRN−r+k times finitely many test derivatives. That error tends to zero, making the resulting distribution independent of the cutoff and of the admissible number of integrations.
