Stochastic-approximation step-size conditions (∑n​αn​=∞,∑n​αn2​<∞)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical learning Stochastic approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A common condition along each repeatedly sampled component is ∑n​αn​=∞ and ∑n​αn2​<∞. The first permits continuing correction; the second limits accumulated noise under the relevant stochastic-approximation hypotheses. The example αn​=1/n has a divergent harmonic sum and a convergent sum of squares. These conditions are not sufficient without suitable assumptions about the update dynamics and sampling.
