Hadronic tensor (Wμν)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Deep inelastic scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 248 Articles: 5
The inclusive product of hadronic-current matrix elements, summed over final states with a Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure. Electromagnetic current conservation makes both indices transverse to the exchanged four-momentum.
