Membrane capacitance (Cm​)

 Home Biology Neuroscience Patch clamp Whole-cell recording
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
Membrane capacitance is the capacitance associated with separated electric charge across cell membrane. A local ideal capacitor has charging electric current IC​=Cm​dV/dt. If a known actual membrane voltage change is ΔV, integrating the charging transient after subtracting sustained ionic and leak components gives Cm​=ΔV−1∫IC​dt. Pipette capacitance, finite patch-clamp series resistance and imperfect transient subtraction can bias the estimate. A capacitive transient is not by itself an ion channel opening.
