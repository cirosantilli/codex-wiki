Complex projective line (CP1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Complex projective space
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
The complex projective line is the space of complex lines in C2. It is diffeomorphic to the two-sphere and is the quotient S3/S1 in the Hopf fibration.
