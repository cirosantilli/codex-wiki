Local time (mathematics)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus
OurBigBook.com Words: 334 Articles: 5
Local time records how much a stochastic process visits a level. Its normalization depends on the process: when the state space is a discrete space, it is occupation time at a state, while local time of a semimartingale is an occupation density weighted by quadratic variation. Brownian local time is a special case.
