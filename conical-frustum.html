Conical frustum (Alateral​=π(a+c)ℓ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Surface of revolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
The lateral surface obtained by rotating a straight segment in a plane containing the rotation axis has endpoint radii a,c and slant length ℓ. Its surface area is π(a+c)ℓ: the radius is linear in the segment's arc length, so the surface area of a surface of revolution is 2π∫0ℓ​ρ(s)ds=2πℓ(a+c)/2. This formula excludes the end disks and assumes that the segment's radial direction is fixed. A straight segment whose endpoint horizontal directions differ generally sweeps a different surface of revolution.
