Charged-current W branching-fraction counting (D=3+3∑i=u,c​∑j=d,s,b​∣Vij​∣2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Neglect final-state masses and QCD corrections. Each of the three leptonic W boson decay channels contributes one common unit of width. Each open quark channel contributes 3∣Vij​∣2, with the factor three counting final colours. The top quark is kinematically inaccessible in an on-shell W decay because mt​>mW​. Thus B(W→ℓν)=1/D≃1/9 and B(W−→buˉ,bcˉ)=3(∣Vub​∣2+∣Vcb​∣2)/D. The large Vtb​ does not open a forbidden top-quark channel.
