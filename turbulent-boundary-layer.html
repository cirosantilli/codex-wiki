Turbulent boundary layer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Boundary layer
OurBigBook.com Words: 426 Articles: 8
A boundary layer in which turbulent velocity fluctuations transport momentum and scalars. A near-wall constant-stress region supports the law of the wall; buoyancy modifies it through Monin-Obukhov similarity theory. A viscous or roughness sublayer and an outer-layer scale bound the range of either approximation.
