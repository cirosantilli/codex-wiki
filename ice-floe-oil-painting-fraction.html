Ice-floe oil painting fraction (min{1,Q/(dvh)})

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Oil spill under sea ice
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
If a buoyant plume of diameter d supplies oil at volume rate Q to an ice floe moving at speed v, the plume sweeps area dv per unit time. Retaining a layer of depth h over that area would require volume rate dvh, so conservation of mass gives the painted fraction min{1,Q/(dvh)}. Discontinuous painting starts when v>Q/(dh). This estimate assumes all arriving oil is retained and a constant track width equal to the plume diameter; the plume's circular cross-sectional area does not enter the swept-area rate.
