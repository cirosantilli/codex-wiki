Born series (ψs​=∑j≥1​Tjψi​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Inverse scattering problem Scattering potential Born approximation for scalar wave scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The Born series is the Neumann series expansion of the Lippmann-Schwinger equation. Its terms describe successive orders of wave scattering; convergence is ensured, for example, by a scattering operator norm smaller than one.
