Solvability of the radical of the Killing form

 ... Lie theory Lie algebra Lie algebra homomorphism Lie algebra representation Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra Killing form
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The radical g⊥={x:κ(x,g)=0} of the Killing form is a solvable ideal. Invariance makes it an ideal, and the Cartan solvability criterion applied to its adjoint image proves solvability.
