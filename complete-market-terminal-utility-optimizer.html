Complete-market terminal utility optimizer (X∗=I(yζ))

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Utility function Expected utility hypothesis Expected utility maximization
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 1
For an increasing strictly concave utility function satisfying the Inada conditions, put I=(U′)−1 and let ζ be the positive terminal state-price density. In a finite complete market, select y>0 so that E[ζI(yζ)] equals initial wealth. The displayed payoff is optimal under that budget: the supporting-line inequality for utility has marginal slope yζ, and its expected linear error vanishes on the budget constraint. Market completeness supplies its replicating strategy.
