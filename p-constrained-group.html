p-constrained group

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Finite group p-core of a finite group
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
Put G=G/Op′​(G) using the p-prime core of a finite group. A finite group is p-constrained if CG​(Op​(G))≤Op​(G). If Op′​(G)=1, the p-core of a finite group has its centralizer contained in itself. Then block idempotents centralize a normal p-subgroup places every block idempotent in kOp​(G), a local ring, so kG has only the principal block.
