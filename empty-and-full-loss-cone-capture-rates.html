Empty and full loss-cone capture rates (Γempty​∼trel​log(1/Rlc​)N​,Γfull​∼PNRlc​​)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics Loss cone
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
Here Rlc​=Jlc2​/Jc2​ is the orbital loss cone fraction, P an orbital period, and trel​ a stellar relaxation time. When angular diffusion per orbit is small relative to the cone, a steady two-dimensional diffusion solution is logarithmic and gives the empty-cone flux. Rapid refilling maintains an approximately isotropic full cone and gives the second rate. Numerical total rates need a stellar density and orbital-energy distribution, not merely a star count.
