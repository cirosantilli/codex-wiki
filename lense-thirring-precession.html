Lense-Thirring precession (L˙=ΩLT​×L)

OurBigBook.com Words: 482 Articles: 5
Lense-Thirring precession is the rotation of orbital angular momentum caused by the gravitational field of a spinning mass. In the secular weak-field picture, the precession vector is parallel to the central spin. Thus an annulus has torque proportional to Jh​×δJd​, perpendicular to Jh​. Summing such torques preserves this orthogonality even if their radial coefficients differ. Dissipation and the internal response of a warped accretion disk can supplement precession with an alignment torque.
