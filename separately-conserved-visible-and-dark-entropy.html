Separately conserved visible and dark entropy (ξ=sSM​/sϕ​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmological entropy conservation
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
After thermal decoupling in cosmology, two homogeneous sectors that evolve adiabatically without entropy injection satisfy sSM​a3=constant and sϕ​a3=constant, hence their entropy density ratio ξ is fixed. At a common relativistic temperature before decoupling, ξ=g∗sSM​/g∗sϕ​. Internal annihilations can change each sector's temperature and effective entropy degrees of freedom without changing its total comoving entropy.
