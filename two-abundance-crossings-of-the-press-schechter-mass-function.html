Two abundance crossings of the Press-Schechter mass function (NlnM​∝νe−ν2/2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 83
At fixed mass, the Press-Schechter halo mass function has abundance per logarithmic interval proportional to νe−ν2/2. This increases as ν falls from infinity to one and then decreases as ν falls toward zero, reflecting incorporation of small dark-matter haloes into larger ones. A target below the maximum therefore has two mathematical crossings if the linear growth factor can increase without limit. The large-ν crossing describes first appearance; the small-ν crossing is late depletion. Late vacuum domination limits growth, so a formal future crossing need not be reached physically.
