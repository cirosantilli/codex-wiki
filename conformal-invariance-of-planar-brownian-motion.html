Conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Planar Brownian motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 424 Articles: 7
If B is planar Brownian motion in a domain D and f:D→D′ is a nonconstant holomorphic function, then f(B), run with the clock ∫0t​∣f′(Bs​)∣2ds, is planar Brownian motion in D′ until the relevant exit time. In particular, conformal maps transport harmonic measure between planar domains.
