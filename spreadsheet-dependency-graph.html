Spreadsheet dependency graph (G=(V,E))

 Home Computer science Spreadsheet Spreadsheet dependency evaluation
OurBigBook.com Words: 378 Articles: 2
The directed graph whose vertices are the spreadsheet cells and whose directed edges u→v mean that the spreadsheet formula in v references u. Input spreadsheet cells have no prerequisites in this model. Retain both incoming references and outgoing consumers: depth-first search for evaluation follows incoming references backwards, whereas downstream invalidation follows outgoing consumers forwards. A directed path then expresses indirect dependence, and a directed cycle expresses a spreadsheet circular reference.
