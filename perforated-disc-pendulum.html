Perforated-disc pendulum (ω2=g∣∑i​mi​xi​∣/IO​)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Physical pendulum
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For a plane lamina suspended at O, treat removed pieces as negative masses and attached pieces as positive ones. Add their moments of inertia about O using the parallel axis theorem, and add their first moments to locate the centre of mass. If the first moments lie along one axis, the gravitational restoring coefficient is g∣∑i​mi​xi​∣. Dividing it by the total inertia gives the small-oscillation angular frequency squared. Both the restoring moment and inertia change when material is moved.
