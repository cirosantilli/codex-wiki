Primary-secondary quality distinction

 Home Philosophy Philosophy of science
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 3
In an account of perception, distinguish an object's physical constitution from its powers to produce experiences in perceivers. The distinction between primary qualities and secondary qualities does not by itself prove an external world or imply that dispositions are unreal.[ref]
