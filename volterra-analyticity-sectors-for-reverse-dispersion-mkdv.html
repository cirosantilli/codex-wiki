Volterra analyticity sectors for reverse-dispersion mKdV (Dj​: (Imk,Imk3))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Integrable systems Lax pair
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
For the modified Korteweg-De Vries equation with linear part qt​−qxxx​, the Volterra integral equation kernel is ei[k(x−x′)−4k3(t−t′)]σ3​. In column one its off-diagonal modulus is e2Imk(x−x′)−8Imk3(t−t′); column two has the reciprocal modulus. Define D1​ by signs (+,+), D2​ by (+,−), D3​ by (−,+) and D4​ by (−,−). The normalization points (0,T), (0,0) and (∞,t) give column domains (D4​,D1​), (D3​,D2​) and (C+​,C−​), respectively. Finite-path eigenfunctions can be entire at fixed coordinates while failing to be bounded outside these spectral sectors.
