Bernoulli trial (X∼Bernoulli(θ))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution Bernoulli distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A random experiment with two outcomes, success and failure, whose success indicator has a Bernoulli distribution. Repeated trials sharing success probability θ give a binomial distribution for their success count if they are independent conditional on θ; integrating over an uncertain common θ generally makes the trials dependent marginally.
