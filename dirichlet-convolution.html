Dirichlet convolution (∗)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Arithmetic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 10
The Dirichlet convolution of arithmetic functions f,g is the arithmetic function
(f∗g)(n)=∑d∣n​f(d)g(n/d)=∑a,b>0ab=n​​f(a)g(b),n>0.
(24)
In the first sum, d runs over the positive integer divisors of n; the second uses ordered pairs of positive integers whose product is n. Both sums are finite. Each pair contributes one weight from f and one from g, so Dirichlet convolution combines information attached to complementary factors. It turns a divisor sum into an operation on arithmetic functions. [ref]
For n=12 the six ordered pairs are (1,12),(2,6),(3,4),(4,3),(6,2),(12,1). The constant-one arithmetic function gives one per pair, while the identity arithmetic function weights the first factor by its size:
τσ1​​=1∗1,=id∗1,​τ(12)σ1​(12)​=1+1+1+1+1+1=6,=1+2+3+4+6+12=28.​
(25)
Here τ is the divisor function and σ1​ is the sum-of-divisors function. By comparison, the pointwise product of arithmetic functions gives (1⋅1)(12)=1 and (id⋅1)(12)=12.
Commutativity follows by swapping the two entries of every ordered pair. Associativity follows by reindexing either grouping over the same ordered tuples of three positive integers:
((f∗g)∗h)(n)=∑a,b,c>0abc=n​​f(a)g(b)h(c)=(f∗(g∗h))(n).
(26)
For example, ((1∗1)∗1)(12) counts ordered tuples of three positive integers with product 12. Distribute the two occurrences of the prime factor 2 among three entries in (22+2​)=6 ways, and the one occurrence of the prime factor 3 in (21+2​)=3 ways. These binomial coefficients are the stars and bars counts of exponent choices in the nonnegative integers; prime factorization therefore gives 6⋅3=18 such tuples. Directly, the same Dirichlet convolution is ∑d∣12​τ(d)=1+2+2+3+4+6=18.
The Identity for Dirichlet convolution is ε, and the Möbius function reverses a divisor sum because μ∗1=ε. This makes Dirichlet convolution useful both for counting factorizations and for recovering an arithmetic function from its divisor sum.
