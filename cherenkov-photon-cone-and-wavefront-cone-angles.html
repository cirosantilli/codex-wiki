Cherenkov photon-cone and wavefront-cone angles

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Cherenkov radiation
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For a charge moving at v=βc, the Cherenkov radiation photon direction makes angle θC​ to the trajectory with cosθC​=1/(nβ). The full cone of photon directions has opening 2θC​. The corresponding conical wavefront is perpendicular to those directions and has half-angle μ=π/2−θC​, satisfying sinμ=1/(nβ). Therefore a full opening angle α gives n=1/[βcos(α/2)] for the photon-direction cone, but n=1/[βsin(α/2)] for the wavefront cone. These are different geometrical definitions, not competing approximations to the same angle.
