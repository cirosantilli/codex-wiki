Tube lens (ft​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Optical microscope Infinity-corrected microscope
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
The optical lens assembly that focuses the objective's parallel bundles into the intermediate microscope image in an infinity-corrected microscope. Its focal length participates in the objective-system transverse magnification −ft​/fo​.
