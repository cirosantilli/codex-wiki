For a closed manifold and a Morse function, the displayed strong inequalities hold for every j, with equality at the top dimension. Indeed the Morse chain complex rank formula makes the alternating sum equal to rj+1​≥0. Adding the inequalities at j and j−1 gives cj​≥bj​. The weak inequalities, even together with equality of Euler characteristics, are less restrictive: b=(1,0,0,1) and c=(2,0,0,2) satisfy those numerical conditions but violate the strong inequality at j=1.
