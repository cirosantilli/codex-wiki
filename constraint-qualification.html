Constraint qualification

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
A constraint qualification is a regularity hypothesis on optimization constraints that guarantees a specified optimality or duality conclusion. For the necessity of Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions, an appropriate constraint qualification ensures that a local minimum admits suitable Lagrange multipliers; linear independence of the gradients of equality optimization constraints and active constraints is one sufficient hypothesis in KKT regularity at a local minimum.[ref] Slater's condition provides a different constraint qualification for convex optimization, yielding strong duality and dual attainment.[ref]
