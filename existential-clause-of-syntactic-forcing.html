Existential clause of syntactic forcing

 ... Set theory Forcing Generic filter Generic extension Forcing theorem Syntactic forcing relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For the usual recursive syntactic forcing relation, p⊩∗∃xφ(x) means that conditions q≤p forcing φ(σ) for some forcing name σ are dense below a forcing condition p. This density clause proves the existential step of the forcing theorem from the truth lemma for each named instance. A generic filter containing p meets that dense set after it is augmented by conditions incompatible with p.
