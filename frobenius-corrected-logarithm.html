Assume Kp​=Qp​, p>2, f(t)∈Zp​[[t]]×, and [π]t≡tp(modp). Then the displayed ratio lies in 1+pZp​[[t]], so its logarithm divided by p is integral. A formal-group isomorphism over the completed unramified coefficient ring transfers it to an integral Amice transform. A norm relation makes the corresponding p-adic measure supported on units. Its kth moment has the Euler correction 1−πk/p.
