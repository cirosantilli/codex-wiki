If a set partition refines a rectangle U×V of a graph into rectangles Ua​×Vb​, write d=d(U,V) for its edge density of a bipartite graph. The weighted variance identity is
∑a,b​∣Ua​∣∣Vb​∣d(Ua​,Vb​)2−∣U∣∣V∣d2=∑a,b​∣Ua​∣∣Vb​∣(d(Ua​,Vb​)−d)2.
(14)
It follows by expanding the square and using ∑a,b​∣Ua​∣∣Vb​∣d(Ua​,Vb​)=∣U∣∣V∣d. In particular, equitable regularity energy cannot decrease under refinement. If a witness rectangle has relative sizes at least ε and edge density of a bipartite graph discrepancy greater than ε, its contribution, after division by the squared total vertex count, exceeds ε4∣U∣∣V∣/N2.
