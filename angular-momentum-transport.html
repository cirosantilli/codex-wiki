Angular momentum transport

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Momentum Angular momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
Angular momentum transport moves angular momentum between locations through matter, stresses, waves, or fields. An accretion disk must transport angular momentum outward for most of its mass to move inward.
