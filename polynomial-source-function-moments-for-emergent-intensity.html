Polynomial source function moments for emergent intensity (Aj​=j!aj​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Radiative transfer Radiative transfer equation Formal solution of the radiative transfer equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For a semi-infinite plane-parallel atmosphere with polynomial source function S(τ)=∑aj​τj, the outward specific intensity is ∫0∞​S(τ)e−τ/μdτ/μ. Substitution u=τ/μ gives coefficients Aj​=j!aj​ in the surface polynomial in μ. The deep boundary term must vanish. A local truncated Taylor series instead gives this contribution plus a remainder, so its surface polynomial is not automatically exact.
