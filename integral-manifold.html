Integral manifold (Tp​N=Dp​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Distribution (differential geometry) Integrable distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 180 Articles: 2
An integral manifold of a rank-r smooth distribution D is an immersed r-dimensional submanifold with Tp​N=Dp​ at every point. For an integrable distribution, maximal connected integral manifolds form its leaves.
