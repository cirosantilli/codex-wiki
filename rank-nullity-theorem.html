Rank-nullity theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Kernel of a linear map
OurBigBook.com Words: 179 Articles: 2
For a linear map T:V→W with finite-dimensional domain,
dimV=dimkerT+dimimT.
(5)
If a basis of kerT is extended to a basis of V, the images of the added basis vectors form a basis of imT. Counting the two parts proves the formula.
