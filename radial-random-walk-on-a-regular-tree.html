For a simple random walk on an infinite regular tree of degree z≥2, every vertex other than the chosen root has one neighbour closer to the root and z−1 farther away. The graph distance from the root is therefore a nearest-neighbour Markov chain on the nonnegative integers, with the displayed bulk transition probabilities and a certain outward step from zero.
