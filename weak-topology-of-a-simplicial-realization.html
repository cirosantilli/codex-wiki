Weak topology of a simplicial realization

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Simplicial complex Geometric realization of a simplicial complex
OurBigBook.com Words: 279 Articles: 1
Give the geometric realization of an abstract simplicial complex the quotient topology obtained by gluing the disjoint union of its standard simplexes along their labelled faces. A subset U is open exactly when U∩∣σ∣ is open in every closed geometric simplex ∣σ∣. This is the weak realization topology. It is the topology of its CW complex structure; the word weak here refers to the simplexwise test, rather than to the weak topology of a topological vector space. A map out of the realization is continuous exactly when its restriction to every simplex is continuous.
