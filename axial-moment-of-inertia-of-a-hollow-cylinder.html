Axial moment of inertia of a hollow cylinder (I=21​M(Ro2​+Ri2​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics Moment of inertia
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For a cylinder of uniform mass density ρ, length L, inner radius Ri​ and outer radius Ro​, an annular element has dm=2πρLrdr. Thus the axial moment of inertia is I=∫r2dm=πρL(Ro4​−Ri4​)/2. Dividing by M=πρL(Ro2​−Ri2​) yields the displayed result. At fixed mass, the solid-cylinder limit is MRo2​/2 and the thin-shell limit is MR2.
