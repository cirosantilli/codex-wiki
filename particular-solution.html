Particular solution

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Linear ordinary differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A particular solution is any one solution of a forced linear differential equation. Every solution is the sum of that particular solution and an arbitrary homogeneous solution.
