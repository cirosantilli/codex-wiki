Efficient score in independent-error regression (ℓ=(h−Ev​h)ρ+(Ev​h)ε/τ2)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Semiparametric statistics Nuisance tangent space Efficient score
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
With unknown independent covariate and centered error distributions, take the regular mean-preserving error tangent space, let h=∂θ​gθ​, ρ=−f′/f and τ2=Ef​ε2>0. Under Ef​ρ=0, Ef​(ερ)=1 and finite second moments, orthogonal projection removes (Ev​h)(ρ−ε/τ2) from hρ. Thus the efficient score and efficient information are ℓ=(h−Ev​h)ρ+(Ev​h)ε/τ2 and I=Varv​(h)Ef​ρ2+(Ev​h)2/τ2. The reduction to hε/τ2 is valid for a normal distribution of errors or for constant h, but need not hold otherwise. Centered covariates and logistic distribution errors give the counterexample ℓ=Xtanh(ε/2).
