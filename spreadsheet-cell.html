Spreadsheet cell

 Home Computer science Spreadsheet
OurBigBook.com Words: 710 Articles: 5
An addressed entry of a spreadsheet, such as A1. Here it stores either a literal numerical input or a spreadsheet formula; its current value is distinct from the stored formula. A cached cell value records a previously computed value, not an instruction to compute it again.
In a spreadsheet tabular model, D2 can store =C2*(1+$F$1) while displaying 9. The spreadsheet cell reference D2 identifies the location, the spreadsheet formula describes the calculation, and 9 is the value obtained from the present inputs. Keeping those three ideas distinct makes copying and checking a model much easier.
