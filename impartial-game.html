Impartial game

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 367 Articles: 13
An impartial game is a game in which both players have the same legal moves from any given position. Under the normal-play convention, a player with no legal move loses. For finite games without draws, Grundy numbers encode positions for the Sprague-Grundy theorem.
