Complex Lie group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
A complex Lie group is a complex manifold whose multiplication and inversion are holomorphic maps between complex manifolds. Its tangent space at the identity is a complex Lie algebra; the Adjoint representation of a Lie group and the Lie exponential map are holomorphic. Compatibility with a complex manifold structure is essential: an arbitrary even-dimensional real Lie group need not have holomorphic multiplication.
