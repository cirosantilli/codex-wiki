Category of relations (Rel)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Regular category
OurBigBook.com Words: 176 Articles: 3
For a regular category C, the category Rel(C) has the objects of C and subobjects of A×B as relations A⇝B. Composition takes the image of the pullback expressing existential quantification over the middle object.
For C=Set this is the category of relations (sets); the general construction also applies to other regular categories.
