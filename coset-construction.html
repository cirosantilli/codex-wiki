Coset construction (Lng/h​=Lng​−Lnh​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For a current subalgebra h⊂g, its currents have conformal weight one under both Sugawara construction stress tensors. Their difference therefore commutes with all h currents and with the h stress tensor. It obeys the Virasoro algebra with central charge cg​−ch​. For a positive-level compact unitary vacuum representation, ∥L−2g/h​∣0⟩∥2=(cg​−ch​)/2≥0. A Cartan subalgebra has ch​=rankg, giving the lower bound on cg​ under these hypotheses.
