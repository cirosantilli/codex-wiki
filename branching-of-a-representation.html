Branching of a representation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation Restriction of a representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 171 Articles: 2
The branching of a group representation V to a subgroup H is its decomposition into irreducible representations of H, when that restriction of a representation is a semisimple representation: V∣H​=⨁λ​mλ​Wλ​. The nonnegative integers mλ​ are the branching multiplicities. For finite groups over the complex numbers, they are the character inner products of the restricted character with the irreducible characters of H. Restriction to an arbitrary subgroup need not be completely reducible.
