Mean free path (ℓ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Ideal gas
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 2
The mean free path is the average distance travelled between collisions. For dilute targets with number density n and effective collision cross-section σc​, ℓ∼1/(nσc​); identical-particle conventions introduce factors such as 2​.
