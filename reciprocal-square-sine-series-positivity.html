Reciprocal-square sine series positivity (S(ξ)=∑l≥1​sin(lξ)/l2)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier series
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
The absolutely convergent sine series S(ξ)=∑l≥1​sin(lξ)/l2 has the integral representation S(ξ)=sinξ∫0∞​te−t/(1−2e−tcosξ+e−2t)dt. The integrand is positive for 0<ξ<π, proving S>0 there without incorrectly maximizing individual Fourier terms.
