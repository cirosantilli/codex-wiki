Four-flavour meson multiplet (4⊗4=15⊕1)

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron Quark model
OurBigBook.com Words: 97
Four quark flavours in the defining representation of flavour SU(4) and their antiquarks give sixteen meson flavour states. The trace of Mij​=qiqˉ​j​ is the trivial representation; its traceless part is the Adjoint representation. Under light-flavour SU(3), the full content is 8⊕3⊕3⊕21. Relative to the light-flavour nonet, the new states are ucˉ,dcˉ,scˉ,cuˉ,cdˉ,csˉ,ccˉ. Inside the traceless fifteen-dimensional multiplet the additional singlet is instead (uuˉ+ddˉ+ssˉ−3ccˉ)/12​. The pure ccˉ state is a linear combination of this state and the full SU(4) singlet. The classification concerns flavour; it does not imply degenerate physical masses or fix spin.
