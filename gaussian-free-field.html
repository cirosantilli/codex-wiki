Gaussian free field

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process Gaussian random field
OurBigBook.com Words: 732 Articles: 19
The Gaussian free field is a centered Gaussian random field whose covariance is the Green kernel of a Laplace operator. It has discrete versions on graphs and continuum versions as a random generalized function on a domain.
