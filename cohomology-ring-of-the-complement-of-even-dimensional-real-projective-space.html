Cohomology ring of the complement of even-dimensional real projective space (H∗(U;F2​)≅F2​[x,y]/(xk,y2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 111
For k≥1, put U=CP2k∖RP2k. Its cohomology ring is
H∗(U;F2​)≅F2​[x,y]/(xk,y2),∣x∣=2,∣y∣=2k.
(67)
Here x is the restricted hyperplane class. The cohomological Gysin map of an embedding sends a2j to hk+j and every odd-degree class to zero, by Poincare duality and mod-two restriction from complex to real projective space. Its exact sequence gives one-dimensional groups in even degrees 0,2,…,4k−2, and zero otherwise. Choose y with δy=a. The connecting-map module identity gives δ(xjy)=a2j+1 for 0≤j<k, so these are the upper-half generators. The lower-half generators are xj. The relations xk=0 and y2=0 follow respectively from i!​1=hk and H4k(U;F2​)=0. The result is a ring isomorphism with Complex projective space CPk−1 times S2k; it does not by itself assert a homotopy equivalence of the spaces.
