Natural satellite

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science

A natural satellite is a naturally occurring body orbiting a planet or another nonstellar body. The Moon is Earth's natural satellite; its synchronous rotation provides a familiar example of tidal locking.
