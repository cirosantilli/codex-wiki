Open-set frame (O(X))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Complete Heyting algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The open subsets of a topological space form a frame. Arbitrary joins are unions, finite meets are intersections, and an arbitrary meet is the interior of an intersection. A continuous map induces a frame homomorphism in the opposite direction by inverse image.
