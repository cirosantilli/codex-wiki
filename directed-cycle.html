Directed cycle

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Directed graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 179 Articles: 2
A closed directed path whose vertices are distinct except for the common start and endpoint is a directed cycle. In a residual network, augmenting around a directed cycle preserves flow balance. A negative total cost on such a cycle gives an improving displacement for a minimum-cost flow.
