Itô product rule

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Itô's lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 19
For continuous semimartingales,
d(Xt​Yt​)=Xt​dYt​+Yt​dXt​+d[X,Y]t​.
(16)
It is the stochastic counterpart of the ordinary product rule; the quadratic covariation supplies the additional second-order term.
