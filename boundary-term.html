Boundary term

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Integration by parts
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
A boundary term is an endpoint or boundary integral left when derivatives are transferred by integration by parts. It vanishes under some boundary conditions and otherwise supplies natural boundary conditions, conserved charges, or physical surface forces.
