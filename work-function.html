Work function (Φ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Potential barrier Quantum tunnelling Tunnel junction
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The work function is the minimum energy needed to remove an electron from a solid to the nearby vacuum level. A junction's effective barrier need not equal either electrode's isolated work function.
