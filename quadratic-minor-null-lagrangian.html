Quadratic minor null Lagrangian (Kkγ​ϵαβγ​ϵijk​ui,α​uj,β​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Calculus of variations
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For constant K, this quadratic combination of two-by-two gradient minors is a divergence. Indeed,
Kkγ​ϵαβγ​ϵijk​ui,α​​uj,β​=∂α​(Kkγ​ϵαβγ​ϵijk​ui​​uj,β​),
(1)
because the term containing uj,βα​ vanishes by antisymmetry of the Levi-Civita symbol. Its volume integral vanishes for homogeneous displacement boundary data. It also vanishes pointwise on rank-one matrices, since να​νβ​ is symmetric. Consequently one can strengthen an elastic energy density by such a term without changing fixed-boundary incremental energies or acoustic tensors.
