Tangency to a plane along a curve forces zero Gaussian curvature

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Gauss map Second fundamental form Fundamental forms of a graph surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
After a rigid motion, write the plane as z=0 and the surface locally as z=h(x,y). Along the curve of tangency, h=0 and ∇h=0. Differentiating ∇h(γ(s))=0 shows that the Hessian annihilates the nonzero tangent γ′(s), so its determinant and therefore the Gaussian curvature vanish.
