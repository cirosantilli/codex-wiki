Quintic real Ginzburg-Landau equation (AT​=μA+α∣A∣2A−∣A∣4A+AXX​,α>0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory Amplitude equation Real Ginzburg–Landau equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 155 Articles: 1
A cubic destabilizing term and quintic saturation describe a subcritical stationary pattern bifurcation. A plane wave A=ReiQX has squared amplitude x=R2 satisfying μ−Q2+αx−x2=0. The nonzero branches meet at x=α/2, μ=Q2−α2/4. The lower branch is unstable to amplitude perturbations; the upper branch can also undergo an Eckhaus instability of its phase.
