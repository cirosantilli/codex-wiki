Spring tide

 ... Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field Tidal tensor Tidal force Tide
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A spring tide has an enhanced high-to-low tidal range because the Moon and Sun are approximately aligned as seen from the Earth, so their principal tidal contributions reinforce. Such alignments occur twice in a lunar phase cycle.
