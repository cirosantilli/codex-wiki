Repulsive inverse-square potential (V(r)=c/r2,c>0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Energy Potential energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 90 Articles: 1
This potential energy generates a repulsive central force Fr​=−V′(r)=2c/r3. It is an inverse-square potential but an inverse-cube force; the powers must not be confused with the Coulomb or gravitational potential proportional to 1/r.
