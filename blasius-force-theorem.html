Blasius force theorem (X−iY=(iρ/2)∮C​(W′)2dz)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Potential flow Complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 507 Articles: 5
For a steady two-dimensional incompressible flow and irrotational flow around an impermeable body, the displayed contour formula gives the force on the body per unit span. Here W′=u−iv is the complex velocity, ρ is the constant mass density, and C runs counterclockwise around the body, excluding other fluid singularities. To verify the sign, Bernoulli's principle gives p=p0​−ρ∣q∣2/2. On a counterclockwise boundary, the complex pressure force is d(X−iY)=−ipdzˉ. Tangency gives qdz=qˉ​dzˉ, and therefore ∣q∣2dzˉ=q2dz. Integrating removes the constant p0​ term and gives the formula. The theorem is named after Heinrich Blasius.
