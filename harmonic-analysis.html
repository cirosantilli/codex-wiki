Harmonic analysis

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 19
Harmonic analysis decomposes functions into elementary oscillations, generalizing Fourier series and the Fourier transform. On a locally compact Abelian group the elementary oscillations are continuous unitary characters, and Haar measure supplies translation-invariant integration.
