Mismatched asymptotic equipartition property (n−1log2​qn​(Xn)→−H(p,q))

For independent and identically distributed random variables with probability mass function p on a finite alphabet, evaluate their realized strings under a fixed product model qn​(xn)=∏i​q(xi​). If q is positive on the probability support of p, the strong law of large numbers gives
n1​log2​qn​(Xn)=n1​∑i​log2​q(Xi​)⟶−H(p,q)=−H(p)−DKL​(p∥q)
(13)
almost surely, using cross-entropy equals entropy plus relative entropy. If q vanishes at an outcome of positive p probability, that outcome eventually appears almost surely, making all subsequent product likelihoods zero and the limit −∞. This matches infinite cross-entropy.
