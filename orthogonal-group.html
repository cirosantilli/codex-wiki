Orthogonal group (O(n))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 27
The real orthogonal group is O(n)={R:RTR=I}. Its tangent space at the identity is the vector space of skew-symmetric matrices.
