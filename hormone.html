Hormone

 Home Biology Signal transduction
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 14
A hormone is a chemical messenger released by biological cells that regulates target biological cells bearing suitable receptors. In endocrine signaling, hormones travel to distant targets through the bloodstream. The extract-transfer experiment under second messenger isolates part of this signal transduction from intact biological cells. [ref]
