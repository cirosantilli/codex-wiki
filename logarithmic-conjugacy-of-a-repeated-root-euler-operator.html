Logarithmic conjugacy of a repeated-root Euler operator (x2D2−xD+1=x(d2/dt2)x−1,t=logx)

For x>0, write t=logx and y=xv(t). The chain rule gives y′=v+vt​, y′′=x−1(vt​+vtt​), and therefore
(x2D2−xD+1)y=xvtt​.
(32)
On 1<x<eℓ with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions, the eigenfunctions are xsin(nπlogx/ℓ) and the eigenvalues are −(nπ/ℓ)2. The weighted inner product with weight x−3 becomes ∫0ℓ​vwdt, so their squared norms are ℓ/2 and their orthogonality is exactly that of the Fourier sine series. The weight is the factor that makes this conjugacy preserve the inner product.
