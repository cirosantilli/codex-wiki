Six-point matching geometry

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics
OurBigBook.com Words: 402 Articles: 6
The complete graph on six points has fifteen edges and fifteen perfect matchings. Its six one-factorizations organize a dual incidence geometry exchanging points with factorizations and edges with matchings. The classical names are duads, synthemes and totals of synthemes. This geometry supports duad-syntheme duality on six points and a construction of the small Witt design.
