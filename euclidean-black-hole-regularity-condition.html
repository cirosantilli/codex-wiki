Euclidean black-hole regularity condition

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole Black-hole thermodynamics Hawking temperature
OurBigBook.com Words: 220 Articles: 3
Near a nonextremal Euclidean horizon, the radial--time plane is smooth only when Euclidean time has period βH​=4π/f′(rH​). Any other period produces a conical singularity at the horizon.
