Strong ellipticity in elasticity (cαiβj​mi​να​mj​νβ​>0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 51
Strong ellipticity requires the displayed inequality for every nonzero displacement vector m and material direction ν. Equivalently, every acoustic tensor is positive-definite. It is weaker than positivity of the moduli on all matrices. A quadratic minor null Lagrangian vanishes on rank-one matrices, so adding such a term does not change the acoustic tensor or this condition.
