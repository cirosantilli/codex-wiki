Logical biconditional (P↔Q)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Proposition Logical connective
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A binary logical connective true exactly when its two arguments have the same truth value. It is logically equivalent to (P→Q)∧(Q→P), and hence to (¬P∨Q)∧(¬Q∨P). Its truth table has value one on input pairs (0,0) and (1,1), and zero on (0,1) and (1,0).
