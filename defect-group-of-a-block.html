Defect group of a block

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Modular representation theory Block of a group algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 30
A defect group of a block idempotent e is a maximal p-subgroup D for which the Brauer image BrD​(e) is nonzero. Equivalently, the vertices of the block algebra as an R[G×G]-module are the diagonal subgroups ΔD for the defect groups D. All defect groups of a block are conjugate.
