A regular outer measure has, for every E⊆X, a measurable hull H⊇E with μ(H)=μ(E).
Suppose C is an algebra containing Mμ∗​ and μ is finitely additive on C. Fix A∈C and an arbitrary E of finite outer measure. Take a measurable hull H of E. Both H∩A and H∖A belong to C, so
μ(E)=μ(H)=μ(H∩A)+μ(H∖A)≥μ(E∩A)+μ(E∖A).
(19)
Outer subadditivity gives equality. If μ(E)=∞, outer subadditivity already forces the sum on the right to be infinite, again giving equality. Therefore A satisfies the Caratheodory criterion. We have proved C⊆Mμ∗​, so
C=Mμ∗​.​
(20)
This proves the maximal additivity algebra of a regular outer measure without any sigma-finiteness assumption.
