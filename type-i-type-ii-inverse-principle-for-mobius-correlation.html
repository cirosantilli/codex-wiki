Type I–type II inverse principle for Möbius correlation

For ∣f∣≤1, if ∣∑n≤X​μ(n)f(n)∣≥δX and U+V≤δX/2, the Vaughan identity for the Möbius function gives a type I sum or a type II sum of modulus at least δX/4. The first is ∑d≤UV​cd​∑k≤X/d​f(dk), with cd​=∑bc=d,b≤U,c≤V​μ(b)μ(c). The second is ∑d>V,w>U,dw≤X​ad​μ(w)f(dw), with ad​=∑c∣d,c>V​μ(c). Both coefficient sequences are bounded by the divisor function. Thus correlation forces either detection by small-modulus periodic indicators or a large multiplicatively organized bilinear sum, without claiming that f itself is literally periodic or multiplicative.
