Prime-power modular intersection bound (∣F∣≤(min(r,m−1)n​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Frankl-Wilson theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
Let m=pa and let an r-uniform set family have no distinct pair whose intersection is congruent to r modulo m. Its size is bounded as displayed; the customary nontrivial form (m−1n​) requires m−1≤r. The Lucas theorem makes (m−1z−r+m−1​) a residue detector modulo p, equal to one exactly when z≡r(modm). Its Boolean evaluation functions have degree at most m−1 and a diagonal evaluation matrix on the family. The low-degree evaluation rank on a uniform layer supplies the sharp dimension bound. Without the size qualification, three singletons with n=3,r=1,m=4 contradict the customary printed form, since 3>(33​).
