Decimal digit-sum divisibility test (a≡∑i​ai​(modd)(d∣9))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Divisibility Divisibility rule
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
For a nonnegative integer with decimal expansion a=∑i​ai​10i, the congruence 10≡1(modd) gives a≡∑i​ai​(modd) whenever d∣9. Thus divisibility by three or nine is equivalent to divisibility of the sum of its decimal digits by the same number. More generally a base-b expansion has the analogous rule for every divisor of b−1. This follows by repeated addition and multiplication in modular arithmetic; it does not require an empirical rule about decimal notation.
