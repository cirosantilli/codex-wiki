Small baryon abundance delays hydrogen recombination (B/T≃log[η−1(me​/T)3/2])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Recombination (cosmology) Recombination temperature
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For hydrogen ionization fraction Xe​ of order one, the hydrogen-only Saha equation gives 1∼nb​(2π/(me​T))3/2eB/T. The baryon-to-photon ratio and photon number density imply nb​∼ηT3. Therefore 1∼η(T/me​)3/2eB/T and the displayed logarithmic balance follows. A small η supplies many ionizing photons per baryon, so neutral hydrogen forms at a temperature much smaller than its Hydrogen binding energy. The numerical temperature depends on the ionization-fraction convention and on whether equilibrium remains valid.
