Tait graph

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Link Link diagram
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
Choose a checkerboard coloring of the complementary regions of a link diagram. The Tait graph has one vertex for each black region and one edge through each crossing joining its adjacent black regions. The other coloring gives its planar dual. For a connected alternating link diagram, all-A and all-B smoothing circles correspond to the regions of its two checkerboard colors; one-circle smoothing states correspond to spanning trees.
