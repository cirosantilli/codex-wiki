Top quark (t)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Quark
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 2
The top quark is the up-type member of the third quark generation. Its mass exceeds the W boson mass, so a real W boson cannot decay into an on-shell top quark. A massless approximation for accessible decay products must not silently remove this channel threshold.
