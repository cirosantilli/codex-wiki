Mean-square derivative of a Gaussian process (Y(t)=L2-limh→0​(X(t+h)−X(t))/h)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process

A Gaussian process has a mean-square derivative at t when its difference quotients converge in L2 space. The mean-square convergence need not give differentiable sample paths. For a centered stationary process with twice continuously differentiable covariance function K(t−s), the difference quotients are Cauchy in L2 space, since their covariance is a rectangular average of −K′′. Their limits form a Gaussian process with covariance function E[Y(s)Y(t)]=−K′′(s−t).
