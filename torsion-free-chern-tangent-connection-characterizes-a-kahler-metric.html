Torsion-free Chern tangent connection characterizes a Kähler metric (T∇C​=0⟺dω=0)

For a Hermitian metric on a complex manifold, the Chern connection on its holomorphic tangent bundle corresponds to a real metric-compatible connection preserving the almost complex structure. Its mixed torsion vanishes, and in holomorphic coordinates its remaining coefficients are Γijk​=hklˉ∂i​hjlˉ​. Symmetry in i,j is equivalent to ∂ω=0; its conjugate gives dω=0. Thus zero torsion is exactly the Kähler metric condition, and then uniqueness identifies this connection with the Levi-Civita connection.
