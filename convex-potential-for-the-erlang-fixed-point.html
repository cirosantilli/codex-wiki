The function U is the carried load of an Erlang loss resource evaluated at its inverse blocking parametrization. It is continuous and strictly increasing from zero to C. Hence F is a coercive function and a strictly convex function on the nonnegative orthant. Its first-order conditions are the fixed-routing Erlang fixed point equations, including zero prices at unused links.
