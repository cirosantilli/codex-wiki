Reed-Solomon key equation (Λ(z)S(z)≡Ω(z)(modzn−k))

With syndromes Sℓ​=∑i​Ei​Xib+ℓ​ and distinct error-location values Xi​, form S(z)=∑ℓ=0n−k−1​Sℓ​zℓ and the error locator polynomial Λ(z)=∏i​(1−Xi​z). There is an error evaluator polynomial Ω of degree less than the number of errors satisfying the key equation. When the number of errors is at most ⌊(n−k)/2⌋, the Berlekamp-Massey algorithm or extended Euclidean algorithm recovers these polynomials, normalized by Λ(0)=1.
