Debye heat capacity (CV​=9NkB​(T/ΘD​)3∫0ΘD​/T​x4ex/(ex−1)2dx)

The three-dimensional Debye model normalizes its phonon density of states to 3N modes: g(ω)=9Nω2/ωD3​ below the Debye frequency ωD​. The thermal energy is U=∫0ωD​​g(ω)ℏω/[eℏω/(kB​T)−1]dω. Differentiate at fixed volume and substitute x=ℏω/(kB​T) to obtain the displayed heat capacity at constant volume.
At T≪ΘD​, integration by parts gives ∫0∞​x4ex/(ex−1)2dx=4∫0∞​x3/(ex−1)dx=4π4/15. Therefore CV​∼(12π4/5)NkB​(T/ΘD​)3. At T≫ΘD​, each mode approaches the equipartition theorem value kB​ of heat capacity, so CV​→3NkB​, the Dulong-Petit law. Fixed harmonic zero-point energy does not contribute to either limit.
