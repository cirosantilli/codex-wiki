Einstein equivalence principle (EEP)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Equivalence principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 2
The Einstein equivalence principle combines the weak equivalence principle with local Lorentz invariance and local position invariance of nongravitational experiments. In a sufficiently small freely falling laboratory, their results agree with special relativity independently of its location and velocity. The strong equivalence principle extends this claim to local gravitational experiments.
