Albert A. Michelson (1852–1931)

OurBigBook.com Tags: Physicist Words: 68 Articles: 1
A physicist.[ref]
The Michelson interferometer turns a change in an optical path into a shift of interference fringes. Controlling the returning beams and counting the shifts can connect very small distances to a wavelength.
