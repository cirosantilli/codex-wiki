Law of reflection

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Geometrical optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
The incident ray, reflected ray and surface normal lie in one plane; incidence and reflection angles measured from the normal are equal. For a unit incident direction d and unit normal n, reflection gives d−2(d⋅n)n.
