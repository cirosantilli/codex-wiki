Uniform pro-p group and powerful Lie lattice correspondence

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Profinite group Pro-p group Uniform pro-p group
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The Lie algebra of a uniform pro-p group construction and BCH convergence on a powerful Lie lattice are mutually inverse on objects and morphisms. On the group side morphisms are continuous group homomorphisms; on the lattice side they are linear Lie algebra homomorphisms over the p-adic integers. A group homomorphism respects powers, roots and limits, and a Lie homomorphism respects every convergent Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula term.
