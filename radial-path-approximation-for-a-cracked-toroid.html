In this magnetic circuit approximation, each circular path has air-gap length d, core length 2πr−d, and the same normal magnetic flux density in air and core. Ampère's law then gives B(r)≃μ0​μr​NI/[2πr+(μr​−1)d]; integrating across the section gives the displayed mutual inductance. Even d≪a can strongly reduce inductance when μr​d/a is appreciable. This is a path estimate, not an exact local field solution in the slit geometry. It neglects magnetic fringing and redistribution between paths. In particular, a literal field proportional to ϕ​/(r+q) everywhere in a current-free core is not curl-free; transverse corrections are required. Narrow-gap circuit approximations and their limitations are discussed in [ref].
