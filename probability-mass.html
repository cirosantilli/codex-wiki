Probability mass (P(X=x))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The probability mass at an outcome x is the probability of its singleton, P(X=x). It equals the jump FX​(x)−FX​(x−) of the cumulative distribution function. For a discrete probability distribution these masses form the probability mass function; a continuous probability distribution gives every individual outcome mass zero. Positive probability mass at a point is distinct from the height of a probability density function.
