Simultaneous wavelet coefficient noise bound (τλ​=σqλ​2log(2N/δ)​)

For coefficient weights wλi​ and independent mean-zero sub-Gaussian random variables of parameter σ, put qλ2​=∑i​wλi2​. Multiplying moment-generating-function bounds shows that the weighted noise has parameter σqλ​. The exponential Markov inequality, optimized in its parameter, gives tail bound 2exp(−t2/(2σ2qλ2​)). At the displayed thresholds, a union bound over N coefficients gives simultaneous control with probability at least 1−δ. No coefficient independence is required. Integrated wavelet weights satisfy qλ​≤n−1/2. Finite variance alone only supports the weaker Chebyshev choice τλ​=σqλ​N/δ​.
