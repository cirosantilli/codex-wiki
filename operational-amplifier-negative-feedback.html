Operational-amplifier negative feedback (Acl​=A/(1+Aβ))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Electronic amplifier Operational amplifier
OurBigBook.com Words: 187 Articles: 3
Return a fraction of the output to the inverting input so that it opposes the input difference. Large stable loop gain makes the closed-loop gain depend mainly on the external feedback ratio rather than the uncertain open-loop gain. Frequency-dependent phase lag can destabilize the loop; saturation and finite slew rate also limit the approximation.[ref]
