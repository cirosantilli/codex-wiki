STRIPS

 Home Computer science Artificial intelligence
OurBigBook.com Words: 447 Articles: 6
A representation for automated planning in which a state S is a set of true ground facts and an action has a positive precondition set P, add set A and delete set D. Under the closed-world assumption, facts absent from S are false. The action applies when P⊆S and produces
S′=(S∖D)∪A.
(1)
Negative effects are delete-list entries; they do not require negative preconditions. A movement action must delete an object's old location as well as add its destination. Capacity and safety restrictions require explicit action conditions or auxiliary planning fluents; they do not follow automatically from an intended physical interpretation.
