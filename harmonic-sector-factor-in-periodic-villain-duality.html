On a rectangular torus, an integer height for the integer-current representation of the Villain model may have twists wx​,wy​∈Z. Subtract wx​x/Lx​+wy​y/Ly​ to get a periodic real height. Its Gaussian energy splits into the ordinary gradient energy and (Ly​wx2​/Lx​+Lx​wy2​/Ly​)/(2β). Height Poisson summation supplies a phase with dipole moments Px​=∑qx​x and Py​=∑qx​y. A further Poisson summation of the twists gives, up to a charge-independent factor,
W(q)=∑Wx​,Wy​∈Z​exp[−2π2β{Ly​Lx​​(Wx​−Lx​Px​​)2+Lx​Ly​​(Wy​−Ly​Py​​)2}].

Here G is the inverse lattice Laplacian on the mean-zero subspace. This keeps the smooth spin wave determinant separate, while correctly retaining the coupling between vortex dipole moments and global winding sectors at finite volume. Dropping those sectors without stating boundary conditions is not an exact torus duality.
