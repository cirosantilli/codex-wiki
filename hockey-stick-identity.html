Hockey-stick identity (∑r=jN​(jr​)=(j+1N+1​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Binomial coefficient
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
For nonnegative integers n,m, the binomial coefficients satisfy
∑k=0m​(kn+k​)=(mn+m+1​).
(25)
The identity follows by mathematical induction: adding the next term combines two adjacent binomial coefficients through Pascal's identity. An equivalent form is ∑r=jN​(jr​)=(j+1N+1​), a diagonal sum in Pascal's triangle. It provides closed forms for sums of binomial coefficients without expanding individual factorials.
