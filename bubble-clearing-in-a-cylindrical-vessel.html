Bubble clearing in a cylindrical vessel (Φ(t)=ϕ0​[1−Vs​(1−ϕ0​)t/H]+​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Void fraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
For initially uniform bubbles with upward speed Vs​(1−ϕ), no new gas production and free escape at the top, gas conservation gives flux Vs​ϕ(1−ϕ). A clearing shock rises from the impermeable bottom with speed Vs​(1−ϕ0​) by the Rankine-Hugoniot condition. The displayed mean void fraction averages the remaining uniform region over the whole liquid depth H. This assumes the dilute regime and no retained foam layer.
