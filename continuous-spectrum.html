Continuous spectrum

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Spectrum (functional analysis)
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For a self-adjoint operator T, a real number λ lies in the continuous spectrum when T−λI is injective with dense range but is not surjective. Such spectral values need not be eigenvalues of normalizable states. The free-particle Hamiltonian on the real line has a continuous energy spectrum.
