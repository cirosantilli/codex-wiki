Hydrogen iodide (HI)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Hydrogen halide
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
The diatomic molecule containing one hydrogen atom and one iodine atom. Its measured spectroscopic wavenumbers include anharmonic corrections beyond the two-term Morse oscillator model.[ref]
