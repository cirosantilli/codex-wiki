Boussinesq equation for an unconfined aquifer

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Aquifer Unconfined aquifer
OurBigBook.com Words: 236 Articles: 4
For pore fraction n, permeability k, liquid density ρ, viscosity μ, rainfall recharge R, and a shallow groundwater thickness h,
nht​=μkρg​(hhx​)x​+R.
(1)
This is a nonlinear diffusion equation with diffusivity proportional to the local thickness.
