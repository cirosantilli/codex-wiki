Quantum state exclusion

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Quantum state discrimination
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 1
A task in which a quantum measurement outputs a label of a preparation that can be ruled out. If preparations are ρj​ and the outcome labelled j has effect Ej​, error-free exclusion requires Tr(Ej​ρj​)=0 for every j, together with Ej​≥0 and ∑j​Ej​=I. A family admitting a complete error-free exclusion measurement consists of antidistinguishable quantum states. Exclusion need not identify the actual preparation.
