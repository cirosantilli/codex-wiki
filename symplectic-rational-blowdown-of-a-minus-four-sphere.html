Symplectic rational blowdown of a minus-four sphere

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Symplectic submanifold Symplectic sum
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A symplectic sphere of self-intersection −4 can be rationally blown down by taking the symplectic sum with CP2 along the sphere and a smooth conic of self-intersection +4. Equivalently, its disk-bundle neighborhood is replaced by the rational ball CP2∖ν(Q) whose boundary is the lens space L(4,1).
