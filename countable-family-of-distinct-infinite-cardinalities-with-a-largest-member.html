Countable family of distinct infinite cardinalities with a largest member

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cardinal number Cardinal arithmetic Infinite cardinal arithmetic
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
Pairwise distinct cardinalities do not prevent a countable family from having a largest member. For example, take X1​=ℵω​ and Xn+2​=ℵn​ for n<ω, viewing cardinals as initial ordinals. Every later set is a subset of X1​, so
⋃n≥1​Xn​=X1​
(31)
although all the cardinalities are different.
