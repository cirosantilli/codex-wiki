Irreducible characters of degree coprime to p (Irrp′​(G))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Character table Irreducible character degree
OurBigBook.com Words: 17
For a finite group G and prime number p, the notation
Irrp′​(G)={χ∈Irr(G):p∤χ(1)}
(45)
denotes the irreducible characters whose irreducible character degree is coprime to p.
