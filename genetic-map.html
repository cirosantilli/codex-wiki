Genetic map

 Home Biology Genetics Genetic linkage
OurBigBook.com Words: 539 Articles: 3
A genetic map turns observations of inherited haplotypes into an ordered account of genetic loci along a chromosome. Its distances describe genetic recombination, which makes the map useful for locating a genetic locus relative to known genetic markers. A genetic map measures inheritance separation; separation measured in base pairs describes positions along DNA. Recombination hotspots help explain why equal numbers of base pairs need not give equal genetic map distances. See T. A. Brown, Genomes, 2nd edition (2002), chapter 5: Mapping Genomes.
Write d for the expected value of the number of chromosomal crossovers encountered along one transmitted chromatid between the chosen genetic loci, measured in morgans; 100d is the distance in centimorgans. This counts exchanges along that transmitted chromatid, rather than all exchanges in the four-chromatid meiotic bundle. For a short interval with very few multiple exchanges, the recombination fraction satisfies r≈d: about 1% recombinant gametes then corresponds to about one centimorgan.
For a longer interval, hidden even numbers of chromosomal crossovers make that approximation inaccurate. Under the Haldane mapping function, d=0.2 morgans gives r≈0.164840; conversely r=0.2 gives d≈0.255413 morgans, or 25.5413 centimorgans. Applying the inverse formula to an already specified genetic map distance would confuse a count with a probability.
Genetic map distances add along ordered adjacent intervals because the total exchange count is the sum of its interval counts. For independent random variables describing counts in disjoint intervals of the Haldane mapping function model, the endpoint recombination fraction instead obeys
r12​=r1​(1−r2​)+(1−r1​)r2​=r1​+r2​−2r1​r2​.

Exactly one interval must change parental origin. Thus r1​=r2​=0.2 gives r12​=0.32, although the two genetic map distances add to approximately 0.510826 morgans. The simple composition formula requires the stated independence; crossover interference can invalidate it.
