Noncausal stationary autoregression (Xt​=−∑j≥1​ϕ−jεt+j​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Autoregressive moving-average model Autoregressive model
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 1
A weakly stationary process satisfying an autoregressive model can depend on future driving white noise. For Xt​=ϕXt−1​+εt​ with ∣ϕ∣>1, the convergent in the sense of mean-square convergence solution is Xt​=−∑j≥1​ϕ−jεt+j​. Its autocovariance is σ2ϕ−∣h∣/(ϕ2−1). This is different from a causal time series.
