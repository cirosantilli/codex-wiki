Unique readability theorem

 Home Computer science Programming language Abstract syntax tree Syntax tree of a formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 126 Articles: 1
The unique readability theorem asserts uniqueness of the outer formation rule and its immediate components in a logical formula. For the fully parenthesized language with negation and implication, every formula is uniquely a variable, a negation of one formula, or an implication between an ordered pair of formulas. It gives a well-defined syntax tree and licenses recursive definitions and structural induction on formulas.
