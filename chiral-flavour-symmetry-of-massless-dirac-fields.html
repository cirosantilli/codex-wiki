Chiral flavour symmetry of massless Dirac fields (U(N)L​×U(N)R​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field theory Internal symmetry of a classical field theory Unitary flavour symmetry of degenerate Dirac fields
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
The free kinetic terms of left- and right-handed Weyl fields are separately invariant under unitary group rotations in flavour space. For N massless Dirac fields, the subgroup preserving common fermion number is U(N)L​×U(N)R​. A common nonzero Dirac mass term couples the two chirality sectors and leaves its diagonal U(N) subgroup. Gauge interactions can obstruct some of the classical axial currents through a chiral anomaly.
