Nested transitive models from a worldly cardinal

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cardinal number Large cardinal Worldly cardinal
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A worldly cardinal permits a countable transitive model M of ZFC and a transitive model N of ZFC with M∈N, ∣N∣=ℵ1​, and (ω1​)N=ω1​. First collapse a countable elementary substructure of Vκ​. Then collapse an ℵ1​-sized Skolem hull containing every countable ordinal and every member of M, as well as M itself. The transitive collapse fixes transitive subsets lemma preserves M and those ordinals.
