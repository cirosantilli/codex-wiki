Boundary variation of the Yang-Mills theta term (δ∫M​tr(F∧F)=2∫∂M​tr(δA∧F))

For F=dA+A∧A, the gauge-theory Bianchi identity is DF=0 and the variation is δF=DδA. The graded product rule for the covariant exterior derivative gives the displayed boundary integral. Thus a constant-coefficient Yang-Mills theta term leaves the bulk Yang-Mills equations unchanged, but can change the boundary variational condition. Its four-form density is exactly gauge-invariant by conjugation of F and cyclicity of the matrix trace, even if the gauge parameter is nonzero on the boundary. Gauge changes of a local Chern-Simons 3-form must not be confused with gauge changes of this globally defined curvature density.
