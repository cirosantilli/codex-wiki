D-wave superconductivity

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Superconductivity Unconventional superconductivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 351 Articles: 6
Pairing with d-wave orbital structure, such as a crystal gap proportional to kx2​−ky2​. Its sign changes under a quarter-turn rotation, but a compensating overall phase change can leave gauge-invariant observables fourfold symmetric. Gap nodes and phase signs therefore do not by themselves establish nematicity or time-reversal breaking. A phase-sensitive test of d-wave pairing probes relative signs through interference.
