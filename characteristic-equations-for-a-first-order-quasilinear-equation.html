Characteristic equations for a first-order quasilinear equation (xs​=P(x,y,z),ys​=Q(x,y,z),zs​=R(x,y,z))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation Characteristic curve Method of characteristics
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For the first-order quasilinear partial differential equation P(x,y,z)zx​+Q(x,y,z)zy​=R(x,y,z), the displayed ordinary differential equations trace curves tangent to every classical solution surface. Indeed, if z=u(x,y) solves the equation and xs​=P, ys​=Q, then the chain rule gives zs​=ux​P+uy​Q=R. Conversely a surface swept out by these curves solves the equation wherever its projection to (x,y) is invertible and hence represents a differentiable graph. The coefficients can depend on the unknown z, which distinguishes these equations from characteristic equations for a prescribed linear transport equation.
