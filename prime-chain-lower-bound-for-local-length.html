Prime-chain lower bound for local length (HR​(t)≥cts)

 ... Commutative algebra Ring Graded ring Associated graded ring Hilbert-Samuel polynomial Hilbert–Samuel growth dimension
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
A prime chain of length s in a Noetherian local ring forces its maximal-ideal length function to be at least cts for large t. Quotient by the initial prime to reduce to a domain, choose a nonzero a in the next prime, and use the Artin-Rees lemma to obtain HR​(t)≥HR​(t−c0​)+HR/(a)​(t). Induction on chain length and summing about t/(2c0​) terms prove the bound. This yields dimR≤d(R) without assuming the Krull height theorem.
