Edge weight (w(e))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The numerical label assigned to an edge e of a graph. An edge weight can be negative when the model allows it. It is distinct from the vertex degree, which counts incident edges.
