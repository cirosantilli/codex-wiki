Patched conic approximation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 1
The patched conic approximation models different portions of a spacecraft trajectory by separate two-body Kepler orbits, matching position and velocity where one attracting body gives way to another. For a surface departure from a nonrotating planet, conservation of energy gives u2=ve2​+w2, where u is surface launch speed, ve​ is the planet's surface escape speed, and w is the planet-relative hyperbolic excess speed. Adding that excess velocity vector to the planet's orbital velocity supplies the heliocentric initial condition. It neglects the simultaneous full gravitational action of both bodies near the matching region.
