Power-law gravitational potential (Φ=−Ar−β)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field Newtonian gravitational potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A radial Newtonian gravitational potential Φ=−Ar−β produces inward acceleration Aβr−β−1. The point-mass case has β=1 and A=GM. Treating the power-law gravitational potential as prescribed does not require it to be sourced by the flowing gas.
