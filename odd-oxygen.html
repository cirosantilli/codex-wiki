Odd oxygen (Ox​=O+O3​)

 Home Chemistry Atmospheric chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 268 Articles: 4
A chemical-family bookkeeping variable in a simple stratospheric model. Interconversion of atomic oxygen and ozone preserves this count, while O+O3​→2O2​ removes two units. The definition must be extended consistently when including additional reservoirs or excited atomic states.
