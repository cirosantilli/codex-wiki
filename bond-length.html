Bond length (r)

 Home Chemistry Chemical bond
OurBigBook.com Words: 70 Articles: 1
A bond length describes the separation of the atomic nuclei of two bonded chemical atoms. Distinguish an equilibrium geometry from an instantaneous or experimentally averaged separation. Methods of determining molecular geometry can use different averaging conventions, so reported bond lengths need their measurement context. See the IUPAC Gold Book: bond length. Changing a bond length is one possible reaction coordinate.
