Orbital decay timescale (τP​=P/∣P˙∣)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Binary star Gravitational-wave emission from a binary system Circular gravitational-wave inspiral
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
An instantaneous orbital decay timescale is positive even when the orbital period decreases. For a constant-mass circular binary with A˙=−C/A3 and P∝A3/2, τP​=2A4/(3C). Integrating to formal zero separation instead gives tmerge​=A4/(4C)=3τP​/8. A local logarithmic decay time is not generally the remaining lifetime.
