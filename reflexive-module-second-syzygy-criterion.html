Reflexive-module second-syzygy criterion

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Torsion-free module Reflexive module
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Let A be a Noetherian ring that is an integral domain. If
0⟶M⟶N⟶P⟶0
(18)
is exact, N is a finitely generated free module, and P is a torsion-free module, then M is a reflexive module. In particular, the dual of every finitely generated A-module is reflexive.
