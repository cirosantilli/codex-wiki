Bernoulli equation with exponential forcing (y′=ay+qeλxyp)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Bernoulli differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For p=1 on a positive real branch, put v=y1−p and κ=(1−p)a. The Bernoulli power substitution gives v′−κv=(1−p)qeλx. Its integrating factor is e−κx, yielding
v=⎩⎨⎧​Ceκx+λ−κ(1−p)q​eλx,eκx[C+(1−p)qx],​λ=κ,λ=κ.​
(96)
The resonant case needs an extra factor x because the forcing has the same exponential dependence as the homogeneous solution. Recover y=v1/(1−p) only on an appropriate real branch. The case p=1 is already a first-order linear differential equation; a zero solution or zero-crossing behavior must be checked in the original equation separately.
