Radiation reaction

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic radiation
OurBigBook.com Words: 308 Articles: 3
Radiation reaction is the influence on a source's motion of its own electromagnetic field, including the balance of radiated energy and momentum with changes of the near field. For sufficiently weak radiation loss, an averaged power balance can give the leading damping law without solving the full self-field dynamics. Replacing a constant-frequency radiation formula by its instantaneous slowly varying value requires an additional time-scale assumption; it is not an exact treatment of arbitrarily rapid motion changes.
