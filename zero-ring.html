Zero ring (0=1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The ring with a single element, which is both its additive and multiplicative identity. If a unital ring has 0=1, then every element r equals r1=r0=0, so it is this ring. Under the usual definition it is excluded from integral domains and fields. The quotient ring Z/(1) is an example.
