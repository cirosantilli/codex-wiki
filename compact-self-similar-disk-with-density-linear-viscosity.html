Compact self-similar disk with density-linear viscosity (νˉ=Ar2Σ,Σ=σ[(R/r)a−1]+​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Accretion disk Viscous evolution of an accretion disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 2
The Keplerian viscous diffusion equation with the displayed kinematic viscosity has two such nontrivial finite-support self-similar solutions: a=1, R=Ct2/5, and a=5/4, R=Ct1/2. Both have σ=(15At)−1 for A,C,t>0. Matching powers of radius eliminates the third power only when (5/2−2a)(2−2a)=0. The first solution conserves disk angular momentum; the second conserves disk mass but requires an inner torque.
