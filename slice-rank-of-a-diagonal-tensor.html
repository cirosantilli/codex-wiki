Slice rank of a diagonal tensor

(22)
on a finite set X, and every cx​ is nonzero, then the slice rank of T is ∣X∣. The upper bound uses one x-slice for each diagonal entry. For the lower bound, restrict any shorter slice decomposition to a common kernel of the coefficient functions in two slice directions; the remaining diagonal matrix has rank larger than the number of slices available in the third direction, a contradiction.
