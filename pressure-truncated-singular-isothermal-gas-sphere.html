Pressure-truncated singular isothermal gas sphere

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Singular isothermal sphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
For p=aT2​ρ, spherical hydrostatic equilibrium admits ρ=aT2​/(2πGr2) with M(r)=2aT2​r/G. Matching an external pressure p0​ fixes rs​=aT2​/2πGp0​​. The central density is singular although the enclosed mass tends to zero; this is one branch, not every finite-centred isothermal cloud.
