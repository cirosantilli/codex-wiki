Jordan–Hölder factor

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Composition series
OurBigBook.com Words: 50 Articles: 1
A Jordan–Hölder factor is a simple quotient in a composition series. The Jordan–Hölder theorem says that the multiset of such factors is independent of the chosen composition series.
