Supersymmetric relation between quartic and Yukawa couplings

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry Wess–Zumino model
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 1
In a canonical Wess–Zumino model with cubic superpotential gΦ3/3, eliminating the auxiliary field gives V⊃∣g∣2∣φ∣4 and L⊃−gφψψ+h.c.. Thus the coefficient of the quartic scalar field interaction is the squared modulus of this convention for the Yukawa coupling.
