Local field

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 8k Articles: 161
A local field is a nondiscrete locally compact topological field. A non-Archimedean local field is a finite extension of Qp​ or of Fp​((t)).
