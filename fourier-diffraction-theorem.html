Fourier diffraction theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Inverse scattering problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 89 Articles: 1
The Born approximation for scalar wave scattering relates an incident plane wave and the transverse Fourier transform of its scattered field to samples of the three-dimensional Fourier transform of a scattering potential on a shifted Ewald sphere. One incident direction at one frequency samples a surface, rather than an entire three-dimensional frequency region. Additional illuminations, frequencies or structural constraints are needed for a full reconstruction.
