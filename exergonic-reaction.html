Exergonic reaction (ΔG<0)

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
An exergonic reaction has negative molar reaction change in Gibbs free energy under the specified conditions. At fixed temperature and pressure, its forward direction is thermodynamically favourable; its reaction rate remains a separate question. See OpenStax Biology 2e, 6.2: exergonic reactions.
