Sample variance (sn2​)

For observations X1​,…,Xn​, the unbiased sample variance is
sn2​=n−11​∑i=1n​(Xi​−Xn​)2.
(48)
For independent identically distributed observations with finite variance σ2, it converges in probability to σ2.
