Maximal inequality for conditional information functions

 ... Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Measurable partition Entropy of a countable measurable partition Conditional information function
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For increasing sigma-algebras Gn​, put fn​=Iμ​(ξ∣Gn​) and F=supn​fn​. For each atom A∈ξ and t≥0,
μ(A∩{F>t})≤e−t.
(2)
Together with the bound by μ(A) and the tail integral formula for moments, this yields ∫Fdμ≤Hμ​(ξ)+1. The inequality follows by stopping the conditional-expectation martingale E[1A​∣Gn​] the first time it falls below e−t.
