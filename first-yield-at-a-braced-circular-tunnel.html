First yield at a braced circular tunnel (Pcrit​=p∞​(1−sinϕ))

OurBigBook.com Words: 94
For a circular tunnel of radius a in a plane-strain elastic medium, isotropic far compression p∞​ and wall bracing pressure P give τrr​=−p∞​−(P−p∞​)a2/r2 and τθθ​=−p∞​+(P−p∞​)a2/r2. The Mohr circle center is −p∞​ and its radius is ∣P−p∞​∣a2/r2. Its maximum is at the wall, proving that first yield occurs there. Cohesionless frictional tangency requires this radius to equal p∞​sinϕ. Lowering P from hydrostatic confinement yields the displayed threshold and stress ratio τθθ​/τrr​=(1+sinϕ)/(1−sinϕ). Increasing bracing instead reaches P=p∞​(1+sinϕ) with the reciprocal ratio. The two cases must not be confused when identifying the first loading threshold.
