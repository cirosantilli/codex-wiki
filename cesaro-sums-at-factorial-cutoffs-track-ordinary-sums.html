Cesàro sums at factorial cutoffs track ordinary sums (NJ​=(J+1)!−1,σNJ​​−sNJ​​=O(J−1))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Convergent sequence Cesàro mean
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For a formal cosine series ∑j​aj​cos(j!t) with uniformly bounded coefficients, the Fourier Cesàro mean at NJ​ differs from its ordinary partial sum by −(NJ​+1)−1∑j≤J​j!aj​cos(j!t). Since ∑j≤J​j!≤2J!, the difference is O(1/J) uniformly in t. Thus any divergent behavior of these ordinary cutoff sums persists along the same Cesàro subsequence. Averaging by the sparse coefficient index is a different summation operation from averaging the actual Fourier frequency cutoffs.
