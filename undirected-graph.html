Undirected graph

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 73 Articles: 1
An undirected graph has edges that are unordered pairs of vertices. In a simple undirected graph there are no loops or multiple edges. The skeleton of a directed graph forgets the directions of its edges and is an undirected graph.
