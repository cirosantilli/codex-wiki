Stagnation point

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A stagnation point of a fluid flow is a point at which the velocity field vanishes. For a two-dimensional streamfunction, it is a critical point satisfying both first spatial derivatives of the streamfunction equal to zero.
