Coordinate vector ([v]B​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Basis Ordered basis
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 1
For an ordered basis B=(b1​,…,bn​), the coordinate vector [v]B​=(a1​,…,an​)T records the unique coefficients satisfying v=∑i​ai​bi​. Change of basis changes this representation while preserving the underlying vector.
For b1​=(1,0)T, b2​=(1,1)T, the vector v=(3,1)T has coordinate vector (2,1)T, since 2b1​+b2​=v. These are the same coefficients extracted by the dual basis in its worked example. The vector is independent of the chosen basis; its list of coordinates is not. In an orthonormal eigenbasis, the coordinates additionally separate the action of a self-adjoint operator into independent scalar multiplications.
