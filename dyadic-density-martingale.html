Dyadic density martingale (Xn​=∑D∈Dn​​μ(D)λ(D)−11D​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Absolute continuity of measures Lebesgue decomposition theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 1
For a finite positive measure on [0,1), average its mass over each half-open dyadic interval. The resulting nonnegative martingale has constant expectation μ([0,1)): a parent interval's mass is the sum of its children's masses. Its almost-sure limit is integrable but the martingale need not be uniformly integrable. The limit recovers the density of the absolutely continuous component in the Lebesgue decomposition theorem.
