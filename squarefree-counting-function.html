Squarefree counting function (Q(x))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Integer Square-free integer
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
The squarefree counting function is Q(x)=#{n≤x:n is squarefree}. The Möbius function gives Q(x)=∑d≤x​​μ(d)⌊x/d2⌋, because ∑d2∣n​μ(d) is the indicator of a square-free integer.
