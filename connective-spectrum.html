Connective spectrum

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Stable homotopy theory Spectrum (topology)
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 2
A connective spectrum has πq​E=0 for q<0. This makes the homological Atiyah-Hirzebruch spectral sequence first quadrant for a based space, and prevents negative coefficient degrees from affecting its first nonzero represented homology theory.
