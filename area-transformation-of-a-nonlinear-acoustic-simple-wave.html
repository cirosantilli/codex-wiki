Area transformation of a nonlinear acoustic simple wave (q=gQ,g=A(Z0​)/A(Z)​,ζ=∫Z0​Z​g(s)ds)

For positive smooth tube area A, the equation qZ​−qqθ​+q(logA)′/2=0 becomes Qζ​−QQθ​=0 under the displayed transformation. Indeed, g′/g=−A′/(2A) cancels geometrical spreading and ζ′=g makes the remaining nonlinear and transport coefficients equal. Starting with q(θ,Z0​)=f(θ) gives Q=f(ξ) and θ=ξ−f(ξ)ζ; characteristic crossing first occurs when ζsupf′=1, if that supremum is positive.
