Spectrum

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Linear operator theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
The spectrum of a linear operator consists of the scalars λ for which A−λI is not invertible. In finite dimensions it is the set of eigenvalues.
