Hecke operator on marked lattices (Tp​F(L,t)=p−1∑L′​F(L′,tmodL′))

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Lattice model of a modular form Gamma 1 level structure on a complex lattice
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 1
Sum over index-p overlattices in which the marked point retains exact order N. There are p+1 summands at good primes and p at bad primes. With homogeneity F(uL,ut)=u−kF(L,t), the coefficient 1/p is the normalization giving the standard Hecke operator Fourier action. Cusp holomorphy under rational slash operators proves preservation of modular forms.
