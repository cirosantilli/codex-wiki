Carbon-14 (14C)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Carbon
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
Carbon-14 has six protons and eight neutrons. Atmospheric production is dominated by 14N(n,p)14C using secondary cosmic ray neutrons. It undergoes beta-minus decay to nitrogen-14, with a physical half-life of approximately 5730 years.
