Methanolysis of an anhydro acetal

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Hydrolysis Acetal hydrolysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
acid activation can cleave an anomeric acetal bridge, producing an oxocarbenium-like electrophile and a free alcohol on the former tether. methanol capture produces a methyl acetal; anomeric stereochemistry and chair preference must be distinguished.
