Classical solution of an abstract Cauchy problem (u′=Lu+f)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis C0-semigroup Abstract Cauchy problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
For a closed operator L on a Banach space H, a positive-time classical solution belongs to C([0,T],H)∩C1((0,T],H), takes values in D(L) for positive time, and has Lu∈C((0,T],H). It satisfies the differential equation and the initial condition in H. Classical regularity also at time zero requires the corresponding regularity of the initial datum, in particular u0​∈D(L). For an analytic semigroup and Hölder continuous forcing, the variation-of-constants formula gives such a solution: subtracting the forcing at the current time turns the singular estimate ∥LS(r)∥≤C/r into the integrable bound Crθ−1.
