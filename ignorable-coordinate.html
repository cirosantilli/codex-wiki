Ignorable coordinate

 Home Physics Branch of physics Classical mechanics Lagrangian mechanics Generalized momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A coordinate qi​ is ignorable, or cyclic, when ∂L/∂qi​=0. The Euler--Lagrange equation then gives p˙​i​=0, so its generalized momentum is conserved.
