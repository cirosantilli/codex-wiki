Pressure-gradient parameter of a protoplanetary disk (η=−21​(cs​/vK​)2dlnPg​/dlnr)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Sub-Keplerian rotation
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A convention for the small fractional reduction of azimuthal gas speed due to radial pressure support. With cs2​=Pg​/ρg​, radial balance gives vgϕ2​=vK2​(1−2η) and hence vgϕ​≃vK​(1−η). Other authors absorb the factor of two into the definition; the convention must accompany a drift formula.
