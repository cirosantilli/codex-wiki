Mészáros equation (δyy​+2y(1+y)2+3y​δy​−2y(1+y)3​δ=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Linear cosmological density perturbation Radiation domination Mészáros effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 108 Articles: 1
In a flat universe containing pressureless matter and smooth radiation in cosmology, put y=a/aeq​ and C=H02​Ωm,02​/Ωr,0​. The Friedmann equation gives H2=C(1+y)/y2, so y′2=C(1+y) and y′′=C/2. The subhorizon cosmological Poisson equation gives δ′′+Hδ′=3Cδ/(2y). The chain rule produces the displayed equation. Smoothing radiation neglects its rapid acoustic forcing after horizon entry; it is not an exact treatment of all radiation perturbations.
