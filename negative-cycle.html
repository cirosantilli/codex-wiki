Negative cycle (∑e∈C​w(e)<0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Directed graph Directed cycle
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A directed cycle in an edge-weighted graph whose total weight is negative. Repeating it makes walk costs arbitrarily small. For a particular shortest-path distance d(s,t) to be affected, the cycle must be reachable from s and must have a route to t; an unrelated component does not change that distance.
