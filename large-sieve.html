Large sieve

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Sieve theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 407 Articles: 8
The large sieve bounds how much an exponential sum can concentrate at separated points of the circle group. Its variance form of the large sieve also bounds simultaneous concentration in residue classes modulo many primes.
