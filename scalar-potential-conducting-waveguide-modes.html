Scalar-potential conducting waveguide modes (γ2=ω2/c2−k2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Electromagnetic waveguide
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For dependence ei(kz−ωt), a Helmholtz equation (Δ⊥​+γ2)ψ=0 generates transverse-magnetic fields e⊥​=∇⊥​ψ, ez​=−iγ2ψ/k, b⊥​=ωz×∇⊥​ψ/(kc2), bz​=0, for k=0. The conducting wall requires a Dirichlet boundary condition on ψ. A transverse-electric representation is e⊥​=z×∇⊥​ψ, ez​=0, b⊥​=−k∇⊥​ψ/ω, bz​=iγ2ψ/ω, with a Neumann boundary condition. These are families of modes, not a completeness assertion for multiply connected guides and their possible TEM fields.
