Pressure-balanced plasma perturbation (δp⊥​+B0​δB/(4π)=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Kinetic magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A low-frequency, strongly oblique plasma perturbation in which perpendicular magnetic pressure equilibrates rapidly compared with the retained dynamics. In Gaussian units, its leading total transverse pressure perturbation vanishes as shown. Thus δB/B0​=−(β/2)δp⊥​/p0​, where β is plasma beta. A small fractional magnetic field perturbation can still exert a leading force.
