Heisenberg group

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Heisenberg Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 480 Articles: 11
The real Heisenberg group consists of upper-unitriangular 3×3 real matrices. In coordinates (q,r,s) its multiplication is (q,r,s)(q′,r′,s′)=(q+q′,r+r′,s+s′+qr′).
