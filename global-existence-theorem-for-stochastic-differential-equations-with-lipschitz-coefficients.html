Global existence theorem for stochastic differential equations with Lipschitz coefficients

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Stochastic differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
Globally Lipschitz drift and diffusion coefficients have linear growth and give a nonexplosive strong stochastic solution for a prescribed initial state and Brownian motion. The solution satisfies pathwise uniqueness. Local versions apply after stopping inside compact regions.
