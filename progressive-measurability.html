Progressive measurability

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Adapted process
OurBigBook.com Words: 221 Articles: 2
A process H is progressively measurable when, for every T, its restriction to [0,T]×Ω is measurable for B([0,T])⊗FT​. Continuous adapted processes are progressively measurable, and a Borel function of such a process remains so. A progressively measurable square-integrable Brownian integrand has an equivalent predictable integrand for the Itô integral, up to equality for dtdP.
