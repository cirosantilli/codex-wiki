Symplectic reduction (μ−1(c)/G)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Hamiltonian group action
OurBigBook.com Words: 276 Articles: 4
For a Hamiltonian group action, a regular value level preserved by a free proper group action has a smooth quotient. The restricted symplectic form descends to a symplectic form on that quotient when the kernel is exactly the orbit directions; this is symplectic reduction.
