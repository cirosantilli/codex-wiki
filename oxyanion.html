Oxyanion

 Home Chemistry Ion
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
An oxyanion carries negative charge on oxygen, often with delocalization onto neighboring atoms. Stabilization of a developing oxyanion is important in many hydrolytic enzyme pathways.
