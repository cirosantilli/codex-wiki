Lorentzian connection-form antisymmetry (ωab​=−ωba​,ωab​=ηac​ωcb​)

Words: 46
Connection 1-form OurBigBook.com
In an orthonormal coframe in spacetime, antisymmetry applies after lowering the first index with the Lorentzian frame metric. Thus mixed time–space connection 1-forms are equal under index interchange, while mixed spatial forms are opposite. The same rule holds for curvature 2-forms. Confusing lowered antisymmetry with ordinary mixed-index antisymmetry changes the curvature signs.

Spin connection (ωμAB​)

Words: 914 Articles: 17
Connection 1-form OurBigBook.com
In an orthonormal frame, the spin connection is the Lorentz connection acting on spinors. With γAB=21​[γA,γB], its covariant derivative is ∇μ​ψ=∂μ​ψ+41​ωμAB​γABψ. The metric connection determines ω when torsion conditions are specified. A zweibein gives its two-dimensional form; omitting it on a curved worldsheet generally destroys covariance.

Spin connection of a conformally flat metric (ωaμν​=ηaμ​∂ν​logΩ−ηaν​∂μ​logΩ)

Words: 35
Spin connection OurBigBook.com
For gab​=Ω2ηab​, use the vierbein eμa​=Ωδμa​ and constant flat gamma matrices. The tetrad postulate and Γcab​=δac​∂b​logΩ+δbc​∂a​logΩ−ηab​ηcd∂d​logΩ give the displayed lowered spin connection. With L=γ​c∂c​logΩ, its spinor action is ∇a​=∂a​+[γ​a​,L]/4. The Lorentz index lowering matters in indefinite signature.

Tetrad postulate (∂a​eμb​+ωa​μν​eνb​−Γcab​eμc​=0)

Words: 40
Spin connection OurBigBook.com
The tetrad postulate expresses compatibility of the spacetime and Lorentz-frame connections on a vierbein. For the inverse coframe eν​b, solving gives ωa​μν​=eν​b(Γcab​eμc​−∂a​eμb​). Metric compatibility makes the lowered connection antisymmetric in its Lorentz indices. Lifting this connection through the Spinor representation of the Lorentz group defines the spinor covariant derivative.

Spinor covariant derivative (∇μ​ψ=∂μ​ψ+41​ωμAB​γABψ)

Words: 794 Articles: 14
Spin connection OurBigBook.com
Lift the Levi-Civita connection to the spinor bundle using the spin representation of the Lorentz algebra. Under a local Lorentz transformation, its matrix Ωμ​=41​ωμAB​γAB obeys Ωμ′​=SΩμ​S−1−(∂μ​S)S−1, so ∇μ′​ψ′=S∇μ​ψ. The Clifford algebra identity [γC,γAB]=2(ηCAγB−ηCBγA) fixes the coefficient. The tetrad postulate then gives ∂μ​γν+Γμρν​γρ+[Ωμ​,γν]=0, establishing compatibility with curved Clifford multiplication.

Twistor spinor (∇a​ϵ=n1​γa​γb∇b​ϵ)

Words: 75 Articles: 1
Spinor covariant derivative OurBigBook.com
A twistor spinor has vanishing gamma-trace-free part of its spinor covariant derivative. In n dimensions the displayed equation uses gamma matrices satisfying {γa​,γb​}=2gab​. It is weaker than being a parallel spinor. In four dimensions it implies [γa​∇b​+γb​∇a​−gab​γc∇c​/2]ϵ=0.
Conformal rescaling of a parallel spinor to a twistor spinor (ϵ=Ω1/2ϵ0​)
Words: 43
Twistor spinor OurBigBook.com
In the conformal frame for g=Ω2η, identify the spinor fibres using the chosen vierbein. If ϵ0​ is a flat constant spinor field, the spin connection of a conformally flat metric gives ∇a​ϵ=γ​a​γ​c(∂c​logΩ)ϵ/2. Contracting with curved γa=Ω−1γ​a yields γa∇a​ϵ=nΩ−1γ​c(∂c​logΩ)ϵ/2. Thus ∇a​ϵ=γa​γb∇b​ϵ/n: the rescaled parallel spinor is a twistor spinor.

Two-component spinor calculus (va=vAA′,gab​=ϵAB​ϵA′B′​)

Words: 358 Articles: 5
Spinor covariant derivative OurBigBook.com
The complexified tangent space of a four-dimensional Lorentzian manifold factors locally into an unprimed and a primed two-dimensional spinor representation. Their alternating forms ϵAB​ and ϵA′B′​ encode the metric tensor and raise or lower spinor indices. A convention must specify the order of contraction with these alternating forms. An antisymmetric spacetime tensor decomposes into symmetric unprimed and primed spinors, FAA′BB′​=ϕAB​ϵA′B′​+ϕ​A′B′​ϵAB​. This splits the six-dimensional space of differential two-forms into the two three-dimensional eigenspaces of the complexified Hodge star operator.
Two-component spinor (πA′​∈C2)
Words: 55
Two-component spinor calculus OurBigBook.com
A two-component two-component spinor is an element of a two-dimensional complex spin space with invariant antisymmetric form ϵAB​. In complexified four-dimensional geometry there are independent unprimed and primed spin spaces, and vectors are represented by xAA′. Raising an index uses the antisymmetric form, so πA′πA′​=0. This identity makes the incidence-plane directions πA′∂AA′​ totally null.
Weyl curvature spinor (ΨABCD​=Ψ(ABCD)​)
Words: 43
Two-component spinor calculus OurBigBook.com
The totally symmetric unprimed four-index spinor represents one chiral part of the Weyl tensor. The other part is represented by a primed spinor, conjugate to the unprimed one on a Lorentzian real slice. The trace-free symmetry separates conformal curvature from the Ricci tensor and scalar-curvature terms.
Chiral spinor curvature operator ([∇AA′​,∇BB′​]=ϵA′B′​ΔAB​+ϵAB​ΔA′B′​)
Words: 189 Articles: 2
Two-component spinor calculus OurBigBook.com
The spinor covariant derivative commutator is a differential two-form in its derivative indices, so the two-component spinor calculus decomposition defines symmetric curvature operators ΔAB​ and ΔA′B′​. With ϵABϵAB​=2, the unprimed projection is ΔAB​=ϵA′B′[∇AA′​,∇BB′​]/2, and the primed projection is analogous. For a torsion-free connection these are algebraic curvature derivations: they annihilate scalar functions and obey the Leibniz rule on tensor products.
Chiral Riemann curvature spinor decomposition (XABCD​=ΨABCD​−ΛϵD(A​ϵB)C​)
Words: 76
Chiral spinor curvature operator OurBigBook.com
Metric compatibility makes the curvature action on a two-component spinor symplectic, so its lowered curvature spinor is symmetric in each pair. The pair-exchange symmetry of the Riemann curvature tensor makes XABCD​=XCDAB​. This six-component tensor decomposes into the five-component totally symmetric Weyl curvature spinor ΨABCD​=X(ABCD)​ and one scalar term. In the displayed normalization, with alternating forms satisfying ϵABϵAB​=2, its scalar is Λ=XABCD​ϵACϵBD/3. This coefficient follows by contracting the displayed scalar term; different normalizations of that term give different scalar conventions.
Contracted chiral curvature identity (ΔAB​ϕAB=0(ϕAB=ϕ(AB)))
Words: 62
Chiral spinor curvature operator OurBigBook.com
For the Levi-Civita connection, the action of the curvature commutator on a bivector wab obeys [∇a​,∇b​]wab=0: both resulting curvature contractions pair the symmetric Ricci tensor with an antisymmetric tensor. For a bivector of one chirality, wAA′BB′=ϕABϵA′B′, this is 2ΔAB​ϕAB=0. In particular it holds for ϕAB=αAαB; polarization gives ΔAB​(α(AβB))=0. The polarization step avoids incorrectly calling αA​βB​ϵA′B′​ a simple bivector for independent spinors.

Parallel spinor (∇ϵ=0)

Words: 116 Articles: 1
Spinor covariant derivative OurBigBook.com
A parallel spinor field is annihilated by the spinor covariant derivative. Its value at one point determines it on a connected domain by parallel transport. The spinor curvature identity implies RμνAB​γABϵ=0, but this local condition alone need not settle global existence: the spin structure and holonomy also matter. Lorentzian parallel spinors need not force the whole Ricci tensor to vanish, unlike the positive-definite case.
Polar-frame parallel spinor (ϵ(ϕ)=eϕγ1γ2/2ϵ0​)
Words: 63
Parallel spinor OurBigBook.com
In flat three-dimensional spacetime with polar spatial orthonormal coframe (dT,dρ,ρdϕ), the spin connection has ω12​=−dϕ. Thus every parallel spinor has the displayed components. Since (γ1γ2)2=−1, these components change sign under ϕ↦ϕ+2π. This is the lift of a full rotation to the spin structure extending over the disk; the polar frame itself does not extend over the origin. In a Cartesian frame the same parallel spinors have constant components.

Spinor curvature identity ([∇μ​,∇ν​]ψ=41​RμνAB​γABψ)

Words: 199 Articles: 3
Spinor covariant derivative OurBigBook.com
The spinor covariant derivative has curvature dΩ+Ω∧Ω=41​RAB​γAB, since the spin representation preserves the Lorentz algebra bracket. In a coordinate basis with the torsion-free connection, antisymmetric second covariant derivatives therefore give the displayed commutator. With unit-weight antisymmetrization, ∇[μ​∇ν]​ψ=81​RμνAB​γABψ=161​RμνAB​[γA,γB]ψ. A noncoordinate frame requires its frame-commutator correction when using the operator commutator; the full second covariant derivative includes that correction automatically.
Killing-spinor integrability with rescaled gamma matrices ([Da​,Db​]=Rabcd​γcd/(4s)+2c2γab​)
Words: 107 Articles: 1
Spinor curvature identity OurBigBook.com
For {γa​,γb​}=2sgab​, the compatible spinor covariant derivative has curvature Rabcd​γcd/(4s). The torsion-free connection preserves Clifford multiplication, so the mixed derivative terms cancel for Da​=∇a​+cγa​. Its integrability is therefore (Rabcd​γcd+8sc2γab​)ϵ=0. Changing antisymmetrization weight cannot change the relative coefficient. The common normalization uses s=1; s=2 gives the coefficient 16c2.
Maximal Killing spinors force constant negative curvature (Rabcd​=−4sc2(gac​gbd​−gad​gbc​))
Words: 56
Killing-spinor integrability with rescaled gamma matrices OurBigBook.com
Four independent complex Dirac solutions in four dimensions span each spinor fibre, because a connection-parallel solution is determined by its value at one point. Their integrability operator must therefore vanish on the whole fibre. Independence of the six Clifford bivectors then gives the displayed constant-curvature tensor and Rab​=−12sc2gab​. When c>0, the Ricci tensor is automatically nondegenerate.
Einstein tensor contraction of spin curvature (γμνρ[∇ν​,∇ρ​]=Gμλ​γλ)
Words: 34
Spinor curvature identity OurBigBook.com
For the torsion-free spin connection with [∇ν​,∇ρ​]=Rνρab​γab/4, Clifford expansion and the first Bianchi identity give γμνρRνρab​γab=4Rμλ​γλ−2Rγμ. Thus the antisymmetric double derivative equals Gμλ​γλ/2. This is the shared algebra behind quadratic-order minimal four-dimensional supergravity invariance and the Nester two-form divergence.

Cartan curvature forms for a static spherical metric (g=−Vdt2+V−1dr2+r2dΩ2)

Words: 63
Curvature 2-form OurBigBook.com
In the exterior orthonormal coframe E0=V​dt, E1=dr/V​, E2=rdθ, E3=rsinθdϕ, use Ωab​=21​Rabcd​Ec∧Ed. The independent mixed-index curvature 2-forms are Ω01​=−V′′E0∧E1/2, Ω0A​=−V′E0∧EA/(2r), Ω1A​=−V′E1∧EA/(2r) for A=2,3, and Ω23​=(1−V)E2∧E3/r2. The orthonormal Ricci tensor is diagonal with entries V′′/2+V′/r, −V′′/2−V′/r, and twice (1−V−rV′)/r2. The Einstein field equations with negative cosmological constant then give V=1−2M/r+r2/l2.

Diagonal curvature operator

Words: 73 Articles: 1
Curvature 2-form OurBigBook.com
The curvature operator is diagonal in an orthonormal coframe {ea} when every curvature 2-form satisfies Rab=Kab​ea∧eb. Its matrix on the corresponding basis of 2-forms is then diagonal.

Diagonal curvature operator implies diagonal Ricci tensor

Words: 46
Diagonal curvature operator OurBigBook.com
If Rab is proportional to ea∧eb for every pair, then a curvature component can be nonzero only when its two index pairs describe the same coordinate two-plane. The contraction Rbd​=Rabad​ therefore vanishes for b=d, so the Ricci tensor is diagonal in the same frame.
