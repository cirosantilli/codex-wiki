Complex cobordism (MU∗(X)=ΩU∗​(X))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Cobordism
OurBigBook.com Words: 382 Articles: 7
Complex cobordism is the generalized cohomology theory represented by the stable Thom spaces of universal complex vector bundles. Its geometric cycles use maps with chosen stable complex normal data. Its complex Thom classes give Chern classes in complex cobordism, and proper oriented maps have a Gysin map in complex cobordism.
