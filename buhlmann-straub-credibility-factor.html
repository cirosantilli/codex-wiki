Bühlmann–Straub credibility factor (Z=Wa+vWa​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Bühlmann model Bühlmann–Straub model Bühlmann–Straub credibility estimate
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
The experience weight is Z=Wa/(Wa+v), where W is total observed exposure, a the variance of hypothetical means, and v the expected process variance. With positive a, it is W/(W+v/a). More exposure raises the weight; larger process noise lowers it. If a=0, the unknown conditional mean is constant and one can set Z=0.
