Pressure coordinate (p)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 72 Articles: 1
An atmospheric vertical coordinate using pressure instead of geometric height. Under hydrostatic balance, pressure decreases upward and geopotential obeys ∂p​Φ=−1/ρ. With the ideal gas law, ∂lnp​Φ=−RT. Horizontal gradients taken at fixed p lie on an isobaric surface. A log-pressure coordinate is a useful related coordinate with the opposite upward sign.
