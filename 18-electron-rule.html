18-electron rule

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry Electron counting
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The 18-electron rule is a useful closed-shell guideline for many low-valent transition-metal complexes, filling nine valence orbitals. It is not universal: many early-metal d0 complexes have fewer electrons, while stable square-planar d8 complexes are often 16-electron.
