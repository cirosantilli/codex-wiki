Euclidean path integral (Z=∫Dϕe−SE​[ϕ])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Path integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 466 Articles: 8
A path integral with weight e−SE​ uses the Euclidean action obtained by Wick rotation. Bosonic convergence requires appropriate positivity and boundary conditions.
