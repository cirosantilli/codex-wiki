OurBigBook.com Words: 89
For an expanding pressureless matter universe with zero cosmological constant, fixed current Hubble parameter H0​ and current matter cosmological density parameter Ω>0, the age of a dust universe without a cosmological constant is H0​t0​=∫01​x​/Ω+(1−Ω)x​dx. For every 0<x<1 the integrand decreases strictly as Ω increases. Thus open models with 0<Ω<1 are older than the flat model, and closed expanding models with Ω>1 are younger. The flat value is 2/3 and the empty Milne universe limit is 1. Specifying what is held fixed matters: at fixed present scale factor and energy density, the integral t0​=∫0a0​​da/C/a−k​ instead increases with k, provided all three models reach a0​ on their expanding branches.
