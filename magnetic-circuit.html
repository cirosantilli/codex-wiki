Magnetic circuit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetostatics
OurBigBook.com Words: 366 Articles: 6
A magnetic circuit approximates a narrow core and its gaps by sections carrying a common magnetic flux Φ. With negligible leakage and approximately uniform magnetic flux density across each section, Gauss's law for magnetism gives B=Φ/A. The magnetostatic Ampère law then gives NI=∫Hdl=Φ∫dl/(μ0​μr​A). This requires an appropriate material relation; constant magnetic permeability is an approximation for an actual magnetic core.
