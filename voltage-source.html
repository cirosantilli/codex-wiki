Voltage source

 Home Electrical engineering
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
An ideal voltage source prescribes an electric potential difference; its electric current is determined by the connected electrical circuit. A dependent electrical source can prescribe a voltage controlled by another circuit variable instead.
