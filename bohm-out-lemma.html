Böhm-out lemma

 ... Computability theory Lambda calculus Untyped lambda calculus Head normal form Böhm tree Böhm transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 136 Articles: 1
For a finite path in a Böhm tree, choose q at least the head arity at every preceding node. Replace relevant variables by Uq​=λz1​⋯zq​w.wz1​⋯zq​. After exposing each node, supply its initial bound variables with Uq​, pad its m arguments to q, and apply the projection λz1​⋯zq​.zi​ selecting the next child. Each step returns that child with accumulated substitutions, so iteration reaches a substitution instance of the chosen subtree. Tupling preserves all children until selection, even when the same variable occurs at several nodes.
