Axiom of determinacy (AD)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Descriptive set theory Infinite game of perfect information Determined infinite game
OurBigBook.com Words: 35 Articles: 1
The axiom of determinacy states that every game G(A) with A⊆ωω is determined. Its version for a move set M is denoted ADM​.
