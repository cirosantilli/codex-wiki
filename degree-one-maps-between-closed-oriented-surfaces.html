Degree-one maps between closed oriented surfaces (Σg​→Σh​ of degree 1⟺g≥h)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Degree of a continuous mapping Degree of a map between oriented manifolds
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A degree of a map between oriented manifolds equal to one makes the pullback on degree-one cohomology injective by the Poincare duality pairing. Thus a degree-one map of closed oriented surfaces requires 2h≤2g. Conversely, collapse the extra handles in Σh​#Σg−h​ onto a point. The retained oriented disc shows that this map has degree one.
