One-particle distribution function (f(x,p))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Boltzmann equation Relativistic kinetic theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
A scalar function on the future mass shell describing the expected particle count in a phase-space distribution cell. In a local orthonormal tetrad, the number crossing a spacelike surface element is fpμdΣμ​d3p/E. On a constant-time slice it becomes fd3xd3p. Including degeneracy and phase-space normalization in f gives the stress-energy tensor Tμν=∫(d3p/E)fpμpν. Other conventions may put the degeneracy or (2π)−3 explicitly in the measure.
