Spin magnetization (M=n−1∑i=1n​Si​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Spin model
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The spin magnetization is the average signed spin of a discrete spin system. For Si​∈{−1,1}, k positive spins give M=−1+2k/n. Multiplying by the magnetic moment per spin and the spin density relates this dimensionless average to physical magnetization.
