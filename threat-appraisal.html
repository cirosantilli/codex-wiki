Threat appraisal

 Home Science Psychology Clinical psychology
OurBigBook.com Words: 123 Articles: 3
Interpretation of an event or sensation in terms of its danger, likelihood, consequences and available coping. An appraisal becomes a proposed maintaining process when it predicts continuing fear and changes in it alter the relevant response.
