Dirichlet eigensystem of a rectangle (λmn​=(mπ/Lx​)2+(nπ/Ly​)2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Laplace operator Dirichlet Laplacian
OurBigBook.com Words: 108
On 0<x<Lx​, 0<y<Ly​ with positive side lengths and zero Dirichlet boundary conditions, separation of variables for −Δu=λu gives
umn​(x,y)=sin(mπx/Lx​)sin(nπy/Ly​),m,n≥1,
(65)
with the displayed eigenvalues. The one-dimensional endpoint conditions exclude constant and hyperbolic factors and force the positive integer sine frequencies. The product Fourier sine series basis is complete in the square-integrable functions on the rectangle. Consequently an eigenfunction with prescribed λ is a linear combination of exactly those modes with λmn​=λ; if none match, only the zero solution exists. Distinct pairs with the same sum give an eigenvalue with geometric multiplicity greater than one. On a square of side π, for instance, λ=25 has precisely the pairs (3,4) and (4,3), so its eigenspace has dimension two.
