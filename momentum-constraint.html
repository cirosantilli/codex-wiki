Momentum constraint (Hi​=−2Dj​(πji​/h​)=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Numerical relativity 3+1 decomposition of spacetime Hamiltonian formulation of general relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 79 Articles: 1
The momentum constraint is the equation obtained by varying the shift vector in the Hamiltonian formulation of general relativity. It expresses the tangential-normal projection of the Einstein field equations and generates spatial coordinate transformations. Writing pij=πij/h​ avoids ambiguities about covariant derivatives of tensor densities.
