Hill constant (K)

 Home Biology Protein Allostery Cooperative binding Hill equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
The positive concentration giving half of the maximal response in a Hill equation. This effective threshold need not equal an individual binding site's microscopic dissociation constant.
