Hamiltonian group action

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 819 Articles: 14
A Hamiltonian group action is a Lie group action on a symplectic manifold with an equivariant moment map μ:M→g∗. In the sign convention ιXH​​ω=dH, each component satisfies d⟨μ,X⟩=ιXM​​ω, where XM​ is the fundamental vector field. The opposite Hamiltonian vector field convention reverses the sign.
