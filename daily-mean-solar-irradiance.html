Daily mean solar irradiance (S=(S0​/π)(H0​sinϕsinδ+cosϕcosδsinH0​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 51
At latitude ϕ and solar declination δ, integrate S0​max(0,sinϕsinδ+cosϕcosδcosH) over hour angle H. Sunset occurs at H0​=arccos(−tanϕtanδ) when this lies in [0,π], with clipping for polar day or night. The displayed daily mean is at the top of the atmosphere; local absorbed heat flux also includes transmission and surface albedo.
