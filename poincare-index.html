Poincaré index

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems
OurBigBook.com Words: 182 Articles: 3
The Poincare index of a planar vector field around a simple closed curve containing no zero on the curve is the winding number of the vector-field direction along that curve.
The index is therefore a winding number for the normalized vector-field direction.
