Spherical inclusion area discrepancy (∣A(V)A(D)−A(S)A(V∩D)∣≤κL(∂V)2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Gauss-Bonnet theorem Spherical isoperimetric inequality
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
On the unit sphere, write x=A(V) and s=4π. The expression equals ∣xA(D∖V)−(s−x)A(D∩V)∣, at most x(s−x). A constant-version spherical isoperimetric inequality therefore gives the displayed bound, uniformly in nonempty open D. A smooth positive area density θ gives a second metric; integrating this bound over Dt​={θ>t} by the layer cake representation bounds the discrepancy between the two normalized area fractions by κ(maxθ−minθ)L2/(sA(S)).
