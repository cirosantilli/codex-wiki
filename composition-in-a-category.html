Composition in a category (g∘f)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
Given morphisms f:A→B and g:B→C, their composite is gf:A→C. Composition is associative and has the identity morphisms as units.
