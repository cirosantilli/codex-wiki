Rational implicit advection stencil with exact shift exceptions (μ=Δt/Δx)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Finite difference Finite difference method
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
Consider a one-step stencil with new-level coefficients a=μ(1+μ)/2, b=(1+μ)(2−μ) and c=(1−μ)(2−μ)/2 at offsets −1,0,1, and old-level coefficients d=2−μ, e=1+μ at offsets 0,1. For ut​=ux​, its exact-solution residual starts at fourth degree with coefficient μ(μ−2)(μ−1)(μ+1)/24. Thus its normalized local truncation error is third order at fixed nonzero Courant number, except at the exact-shift values −1,1,2; zero gives the zero-step identity. For its Fourier symbol, ∣D∣2−∣N∣2=μ(μ−2)(μ−1)(μ+1)(1−cosθ)2. Uniformly invertible stable steps occur for μ≤−1, 0≤μ≤1 or μ≥2.
