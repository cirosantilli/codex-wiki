Tension (T)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Force
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
Tension is a pulling force transmitted along a taut string, rope, or similar body. For an ideal uniform string with linear mass density μ, constant tension T sets the small transverse wave speed c=T/μ​.
