Flasque sheaf

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Sheaf cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 384 Articles: 4
A sheaf is flasque when every restriction map is surjective. Flasque sheaves are acyclic for global sections, so Hi(X,F)=0 for i>0.
