Cube symmetry action on edges (H↪S12​,∣H∣=48)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group action Symmetry group of a cube
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
The full symmetry group of a cube acts transitively and faithfully on its twelve edges. For vertices (±1,±1,±1), its signed permutation matrices act transitively on the edges. The stabilizer subgroup of {(1,1,z):−1≤z≤1} consists of independently swapping x,y and reversing z, and is a Klein four-group. The orbit-stabilizer theorem gives order 12⋅4=48. Fixing every edge fixes every vertex, as each vertex is the unique intersection of its three incident edges, so the group action is faithful. The resulting subgroup of S12​ has index 12!/48 and is not normal: central inversion has cycle type 26, whose conjugacy class in S12​ has 12!/(266!)=10395>48 elements.
