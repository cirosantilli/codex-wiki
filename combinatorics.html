(A+B)n=∑k=0n​(kn​)An−kBk.

Overlap structure of digit spalindromes

Words: 41
Combinatorics OurBigBook.com
Two length-2k palindromes with distinct first-half digits cannot begin fewer than k positions apart: the later first half would include the repeated middle pair of the earlier palindrome. At separation k, their union has form AArevA.

Bounded-displacement permutation of the natural numbers

Words: 38
Permutation OurBigBook.com
A permutation σ of N has displacement at most one when ∣σ(j)−j∣≤1. Such a permutation is a disjoint collection of fixed points and adjacent transpositions, so independently swapping the pairs (2n−1,2n) already gives uncountably many examples.
