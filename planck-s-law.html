Planck's law

Planck's law gives the equilibrium spectral radiance of a blackbody at temperature T. The Planck function per unit frequency and per unit wavelength is
Bν​(T)=c22hν3​ehν/(kB​T)−11​,Bλ​(T)=λ52hc2​ehc/(λkB​T)−11​.
(41)
The relation Bν​dν=−Bλ​dλ accounts for the change from frequency to wavelength. Dividing the isotropic spectral energy density 4πBν​/c by the photon energy hν gives the Planck photon distribution.
