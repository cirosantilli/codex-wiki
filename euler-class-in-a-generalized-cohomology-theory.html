Euler class in a generalized cohomology theory (eh​(ξ)=z∗uξ​)

 ... Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Orientation of a vector bundle Thom class Euler class of a vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
For an h-oriented real bundle ξ of rank r, pull its Thom class back along the zero section to obtain its Euler class in hr(X). It depends on the orientation and the actual bundle, not merely its stable isomorphism class. In complex cobordism the orientation may be supplied by a stable complex structure on a real vector bundle.
