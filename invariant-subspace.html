Invariant subspace

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 87 Articles: 2
An invariant subspace of a linear map A:V→V is a vector subspace W⊆V satisfying A(W)⊆W. For a group representation, invariance means ρ(g)W⊆W for every represented group element g.
