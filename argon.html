Argon (Ar, Z=18)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Noble gas
OurBigBook.com Words: 637 Articles: 3
The chemical element with atomic number eighteen. Argon is a colourless noble gas. It can provide a protective atmosphere around hot, reactive materials, for example in welding, where excluding oxygen limits oxidation. Commercial production separates it by distillation of liquid air.[ref]
Its discovery shows why a small disagreement can matter more than a reassuringly repeatable result. Two preparations can each give consistent measurements while producing different mixtures. The Rayleigh-Ramsay argon density experiment investigated just such a discrepancy. Gas density links the weighing to a mean molar mass; a separate separation process tests which component accounts for it.
