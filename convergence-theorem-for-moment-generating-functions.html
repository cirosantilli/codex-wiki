Convergence theorem for moment-generating functions

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Moment-generating function
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
Suppose the moment-generating functions Mn​ are finite on a common open interval (−a,a) containing zero and converge there pointwise to the moment-generating function MX​ of a random variable X, with MX​ finite there. Then Xn​ tends to X in distribution. Equivalently, a finite pointwise limit on such an interval is the moment-generating function of a unique limiting probability distribution. This theorem requires control on a neighborhood of zero; convergence of isolated moments is a different hypothesis.
