Stabilization of images of powers of a linear map (imhm⊇imhm+1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Morphism Endomorphism Linear endomorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
For a linear endomorphism h of an n-dimensional vector space, the images of a linear map formed by its powers decrease and stabilize by exponent n. Indeed, hm+kv=hm(hkv) proves inclusion. If equality occurs at one step, applying h to both sides proves equality at the next step, and induction gives equality forever after. Before stabilization, every inclusion is strict and lowers dimension by at least one. Starting at dimension n allows at most n such decreases.
By the rank-nullity theorem, the increasing null spaces of the same powers stabilize at the same time. This gives a short proof of the finite-dimensional Fitting decomposition.
