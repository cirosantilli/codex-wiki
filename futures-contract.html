Futures contract

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fundamental theorem of asset pricing Contingent claim
OurBigBook.com Words: 147 Articles: 3
A standardized maturity-and-quantity agreement whose quote changes generate margin payments as gains and losses are marked to market. A newly settled contract has zero value, while its quoted delivery price is generally nonzero. In the continuous-settlement idealization, futures pricing uses a pricing-measure martingale quote.
