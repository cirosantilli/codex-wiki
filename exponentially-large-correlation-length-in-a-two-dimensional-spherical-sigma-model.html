Exponentially large correlation length in a two-dimensional spherical sigma model (ξ∼Λ−1exp[2π/((n−2)g0​)])

At D=2 and n>2, the weak-coupling renormalization-group flow obeys dg/dℓ=(n−2)g2/(2π). Integrating gives 1/g(ℓ)=1/g0​−(n−2)ℓ/(2π). The flow reaches order-one coupling at a length whose logarithm is 2π/[(n−2)g0​]+O(1) at this order. Restoring the microscopic scale Λ−1 gives the displayed leading exponential correlation length. Higher loops affect its prefactor, and this conclusion does not apply to the compact n=2 model with its Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition.
