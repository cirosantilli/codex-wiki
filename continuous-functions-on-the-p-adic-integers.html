Continuous functions on the p-adic integers (C(Zp​,Qp​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 567 Articles: 7
The continuous functions from the p-adic integers to the P-adic numbers form a Banach space over Qp​ with supremum norm ∥f∥∞​=supx∈Zp​​∣f(x)∣p​. Compactness of the domain bounds the norm, and uniform convergence in the complete codomain proves completeness. Translation and the forward difference operator Δf(x)=f(x+1)−f(x) are bounded operators, with ∥Δf∥∞​≤∥f∥∞​.
