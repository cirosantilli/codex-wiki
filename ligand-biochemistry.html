Ligand (biochemistry)

 Home Biology Protein
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A biochemical ligand is an ion or molecule that binds a protein. For a receptor, a ligand may activate signaling, prevent activation, or have another effect. This usage concerns protein binding; it is distinct from the coordination-chemistry use of the word. See the protein-binding reference under receptor.
