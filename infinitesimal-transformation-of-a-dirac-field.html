Infinitesimal transformation of a Dirac field (δΨ=−ωμν​xν∂μ​Ψ−4i​ωμν​σμνΨ)

For Λ=1+ω with antisymmetric lowered parameters, the spin matrix is S=1−(i/4)ωμν​σμν, where σμν=(i/2)[γμ,γν]. At transformed coordinates this is the full component transformation. At fixed coordinates an additional orbital term −ωμν​xν∂μ​Ψ appears.
