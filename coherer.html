Coherer

 Home Electrical engineering Telecommunication Radio communication Wireless telegraphy
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
A filings coherer changes from high to low electrical resistance following electrical excitation. A local battery supplies the readout electric current; a tap resets the detector. It records events rather than the incident radio wave waveform.
Marconi described nickel and silver filings between silver plugs, with a cell and sensitive telegraph relay controlling a tapper and recorder.[ref]
