Hörmander integral kernel condition (supy=0​∫∣x∣>2∣y∣​∣K(x−y)−K(x)∣dx<∞)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Convolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 333 Articles: 4
This integral smoothness condition on a convolution kernel controls how translations change the kernel away from the translation scale. It need not imply differentiability. Coupled with an L2 operator bound, it yields a weak type (1,1) bound through the Calderón–Zygmund decomposition. It is distinct from the bracket-generating condition for differential operators also named after Hörmander.
