Alpha-spending function (α(u))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Clinical trial Group sequential design
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
An alpha-spending function assigns the cumulative Type I error budget to successive information fractions in a group sequential design. Its total at the final analysis is the overall significance level. The rejection boundaries must be calibrated using the joint distribution of the sequential test statistics; independent fixed-level tests do not automatically spend the assigned budget correctly.
