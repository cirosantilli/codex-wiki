Index notation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
Index notation describes vectors, matrices and tensors by their components relative to a chosen basis. For example, matrix multiplication reads (AB)ij​=∑k​Aik​Bkj​. The free indices i,j identify the resulting component, while k is summed. An explicit summation sign avoids depending on an implicit Einstein summation convention.
