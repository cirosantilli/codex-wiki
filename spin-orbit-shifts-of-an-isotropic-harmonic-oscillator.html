Spin-orbit shifts of an isotropic harmonic oscillator (ΔE=ℏ2ω2[j(j+1)−l(l+1)−3/4]/(4mc2))

 Home Physics Atomic physics Fine structure Spin-orbit interaction Spin-orbit energy shift in a central potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For an isotropic three-dimensional quantum harmonic oscillator, V(r)=mω2r2/2 makes r−1V′(r)=mω2. The spin-orbit energy shift in a central potential is therefore independent of the radial excitation. For spin one-half, the l=0,j=1/2 shift is zero, while the two l=1 shifts are −ℏ2ω2/(2mc2) for j=1/2 and +ℏ2ω2/(4mc2) for j=3/2.
These shifts are quadratic in trap frequency. Their degeneracy-weighted sum in the l=1 manifold is zero, since the multiplicities are two and four. They are perturbative corrections rather than full oscillator energies; a nonrelativistic treatment requires them to be small compared with the orbital spacings.
