Image criterion for a matrix factorization (AX=B⟺imB⊆imA)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra System of linear equations
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
For matrices representing linear maps on finite-dimensional vector spaces, AX=B has a solution precisely when the image of a linear map represented by B is contained in that represented by A. Necessity follows by applying the equation to vectors. For sufficiency choose an A-preimage of each column of B and assemble those vectors as the columns of X. All solutions are X0​+N with AN=0, equivalently every column of N lies in the kernel of a linear map represented by A. When B has at least one column, a solution is unique precisely when A has trivial kernel.
