Noether normalization by weighted substitutions

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Krull dimension Noether normalization lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 138 Articles: 1
To normalize k[a1​,…,an​] over an arbitrary field, choose a nonzero polynomial relation and a base M larger than all its exponents. The substitutions bi​=ai​−anMi​ give distinct top weights to its monomials, so its highest power of an​ has a nonzero scalar coefficient. It becomes a monic polynomial equation, making the algebra finite over k[b1​,…,bn−1​]. Induction proves the Noether normalization lemma, including over finite fields.
