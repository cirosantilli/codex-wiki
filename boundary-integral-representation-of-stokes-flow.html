Boundary integral representation of Stokes flow (c(y)u(y)=∫J(y−x)σndS+∫uK(y−x)ndS)

For homogeneous Stokes flow in a smooth bounded volume, use Jij​(r)=(δij​/∣r∣+ri​rj​/∣r∣3)/(8πμ) and Kijk​(r)=−3ri​rj​rk​/(4π∣r∣5). The Lorentz reciprocal theorem with a point-force Stokeslet gives the displayed representation, with c=1 inside, 0 outside and 1/2 on a smooth boundary. The double-layer integral at the boundary has its limiting principal-value meaning. In an exterior problem the body's outward normal is opposite to the fluid-domain normal, reversing both boundary terms; a nonzero ambient flow adds its far-boundary contribution.
