Turbulent mixing-layer similarity equation (F′′′+(ξ+F)F′′=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Turbulent mixing Planar turbulent mixing layer
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For a planar turbulent mixing layer, let Uc​=(Ua​+Ub​)/2, ΔU=Ua​−Ub​, νT​=CΔUδ, δ=SΔUx/Uc​, and ξ=S/C​y/δ. The stream function ψ=Uc​[y+δC/S​F(ξ)] enforces mean incompressibility. Substitution into UUx​+VUy​=νT​Uyy​ gives the displayed ordinary differential equation, with F′(±∞)=±ΔU/(2Uc​). When ΔU/Uc​ is small, its linear approximation is F′′′+ξF′′=0, so F′′ is a Gaussian function and F′ an error function.
