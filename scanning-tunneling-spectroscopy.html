Scanning tunneling spectroscopy (STS)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Potential barrier Quantum tunnelling Tunnel junction Scanning tunneling microscopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 3
A conducting tip probes the energy-dependent local density of states by recording tunnel current versus applied bias, often measuring dI/dV with a small bias modulation and lock-in detection. At low temperature and for slowly varying tip density and matrix element, dI/dV is proportional to sample local density of states at the corresponding bias energy. Thermal broadening, surface structure and energy-dependent tunnelling selectivity limit a direct interpretation as bulk band structure.
