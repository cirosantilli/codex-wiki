Integral-scale intermittency

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Internal intermittency
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
Integral-scale intermittency is modulation of the energy-containing motions and the energy supply on large lengths or long turnover times. Averaging velocity statistics over regions or realizations with differing viscous dissipation rates can invalidate universal coefficients based only on the global mean viscous dissipation.
