Einstein mean-square displacement (⟨x2⟩=ℏcoth(TE​/(2T))/(2mωE​))

 ... Thermodynamics Heat Heat capacity Heat capacity at constant volume Dulong-Petit law Einstein solid
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For one Cartesian direction in an Einstein solid, ⟨x2⟩=ℏ[2⟨n⟩+1]/(2mωE​). Substituting the thermal oscillator occupation proves the displayed result. At T≫TE​, equipartition gives ⟨x2⟩=kB​T/(mωE2​)=ℏ2T/(mkB​TE2​). The total displacement variance of an isotropic atom is three times the one-coordinate result, a distinction that matters in applying a melting threshold.
