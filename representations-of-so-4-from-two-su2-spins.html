Representations of SO(4) from two SU2 spins ((jL​,jR​),jL​+jR​∈Z)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Orthogonal group Special orthogonal group SO(4) group
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
Tensor products VjL​​⊗VjR​​ give the irreducible representations of the Spin(4) double cover. The simultaneous central sign acts by (−1)2jL​+2jR​, so exactly the integer-sum pairs descend to SO(4). Their dimensions are (2jL​+1)(2jR​+1). Under the diagonal spatial rotation subgroup, the Clebsch-Gordan decomposition for SU2 gives spins from ∣jL​−jR​∣ through jL​+jR​ in steps of one.
