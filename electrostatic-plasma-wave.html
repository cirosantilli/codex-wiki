Electrostatic plasma wave (δE=−ikϕ)

 Home Physics Plasma (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 189 Articles: 3
A plasma wave dominated by a longitudinal electric field. The leading dispersion relation is k⋅ϵ⋅k=0. In a cold magnetized plasma, this becomes k⊥2​S+k∥2​P=0. An exactly longitudinal finite-wavenumber solution must also satisfy the full Maxwell equations; the oblique electrostatic condition is usually an asymptotic large-refractive index approximation.
