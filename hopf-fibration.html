Hopf fibration

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Complex projective space
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 2
The complex Hopf fibration is the principal circle bundle
S1⟶S2n−1⟶CPn−1.
(63)
It is the unit-sphere bundle of the tautological complex line bundle.
