Baryon number (B)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 3
Baryon number assigns B=1/3 to each quark, B=−1/3 to each antiquark and zero to leptons. A proton has baryon number one.
