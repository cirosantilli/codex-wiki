Distance extrema on a central plane section of an ellipsoid (dmin2​=λmax​(UTAU)−1,dmax2​=λmin​(UTAU)−1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Quadric surface Distance extrema on a real quadratic surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
Let A be a real positive-definite matrix and let the columns of U be an orthonormal basis of a two-dimensional linear subspace in three-dimensional Euclidean space. Points in that central plane are x=Uq, with ∣x∣2=∣q∣2. Restricting the ellipsoid xTAx=1 gives qT(UTAU)q=1. The compressed matrix UTAU is a positive-definite matrix, and its extreme eigenvalues give the distance formulas in the title by the Rayleigh quotient. The two extremal axes are the images under U of the corresponding eigenvectors. This restriction reduces a problem with two Lagrange multipliers to a two-dimensional quadratic form.
