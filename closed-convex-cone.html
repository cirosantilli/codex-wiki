Closed convex cone

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex set Convex cone
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 1
A convex cone that is a closed set in its ambient topology. In a finite-dimensional normed vector space, this means it contains every limit of its convergent sequences. For example, the positive semidefinite cone is closed because each test X↦xTXx is continuous.
