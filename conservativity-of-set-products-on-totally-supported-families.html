Conservativity of set products on totally supported families (∏i​fi​ bijective,Ai​,Bi​=∅⟹fi​ bijective)

 ... Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Regular category Capital regular category Power of sets as a capital regular category
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
If some fi​ is not injective, extend two distinct equal-image elements to tuples using common choices at all other coordinates; the product is not injective. If some fi​ is not surjective, extend a missing target element to a tuple; the product is not surjective. The axiom of choice supplies these extensions. Thus the set-product functor reflects isomorphisms between families whose coordinates are all nonempty.
