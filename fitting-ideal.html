Fitting ideal (Fitt0​(M))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Module (mathematics) Finitely presented module
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
For a module over a commutative ring presented by a matrix with n generators, its zeroth Fitting ideal is generated by all n-by-n minors of the relation matrix. It is independent of the chosen finite presentation. Over a unique factorization domain, the greatest common divisor of these minors defines the module order up to a unit. This does not require a Smith normal form over the ring. The square Seifert-matrix presentation of the Alexander module makes the order its determinant.
