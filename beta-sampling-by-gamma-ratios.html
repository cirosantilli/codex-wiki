Beta sampling by gamma ratios (S/(S+T))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Beta distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Independent gamma distributions S∼Γ(α,r) and T∼Γ(β,r) with a common rate yield a Beta distribution S/(S+T)∼Beta(α,β). The change of variables S=wu, T=w(1−u) has Jacobian determinant w, and factors the joint probability density function into a Beta distribution density in u and a gamma distribution density in w. In particular, independent chi-squared distributions with 2α and 2β degrees of freedom give the required ratio.
