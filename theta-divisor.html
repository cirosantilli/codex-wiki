Theta divisor (Θ⊂Picg−1(C))

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic variety Algebraic group Abelian variety Jacobian variety
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 14
The theta divisor consists of degree-g−1 line bundles with a nonzero global section. It is the irreducible image of the symmetric product of a curve C(g−1), with its reduced Cartier divisor structure. The determinant description of the theta divisor supplies equations, multiplicities and pullbacks in families.
