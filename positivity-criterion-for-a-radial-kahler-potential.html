For a potential already smooth on all of C, the associated form is a Kähler form exactly when the displayed two positivity conditions hold. The first gives a positive metric coefficient off the origin; the second is positivity of fzzˉ​(0). The associated real metric is 2fzzˉ​(dx2+dy2). Positivity away from the origin alone permits a degenerate metric at the origin.
