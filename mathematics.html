For pairwise coprime positive integers m1​,…,mr​, reduction induces a ring isomorphism
Z/(m1​⋯mr​)Z≅∏j=1r​Z/mj​Z.
(1)
Equivalently, every list of residue classes modulo the mj​ has a unique simultaneous residue class modulo their product.

Lagrange theorem for polynomial congruences

Words: 46
Chinese remainder theorem OurBigBook.com
A nonzero polynomial of degree d over a field has at most d roots. In particular, if a polynomial congruence f(x)≡0(modp) has degree d and not all coefficients are divisible by the prime p, it has at most d incongruent solutions modulo p.

Multiplicative order under the Chinese remainder theorem

Words: 22
Chinese remainder theorem for unit groups OurBigBook.com
The order of a unit modulo a product of coprime moduli is the least common multiple of the orders of its components.
