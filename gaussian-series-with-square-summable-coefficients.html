Gaussian series with square-summable coefficients (∑j​αj​Xj​∼N(0,∑j​αj2​))

For independent standard Gaussian Xj​ and ∑αj2​<∞, the partial sums are Cauchy in L2 space, since a tail's squared norm is the corresponding sum of coefficient squares. Completeness gives an L2 limit. The finite-sum characteristic functions converge to exp[−t2∑αj2​/2], identifying the limit as Gaussian, including the zero-variance case. The identification needs convergence of distributions, not just agreement of the first two moments.
