Dalitz plot

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Decay width Lorentz-invariant phase space
OurBigBook.com Words: 140 Articles: 2
A display of three-body final states in two independent pairwise squared invariant masses. For a parent of mass M decaying into daughters of masses m1​,m2​,m3​, four-momentum conservation gives s12​+s13​+s23​=M2+m12​+m22​+m32​. A narrow intermediate two-body resonance fixes one pairwise invariant mass and appears as a band.
