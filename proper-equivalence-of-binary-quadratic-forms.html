Proper equivalence of binary quadratic forms

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Binary quadratic form
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
Integral forms f and g are properly equivalent when
g(x,y)=f(αx+βy,γx+δy)
(53)
for some (αγ​βδ​)∈SL2​(Z). Proper equivalence preserves both represented integers and orientation.
