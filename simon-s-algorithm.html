Simon's algorithm

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum Fourier transform Hidden subgroup problem Simon's problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 309 Articles: 5
Simon's algorithm prepares hidden-subgroup coset states, applies the quantum Fourier transform over (Z2​)n, and samples vectors y satisfying y⋅p=0 over F2​. Repeated samples and Gaussian elimination recover p with O(n) oracle queries and polynomial computation.
To solve Simon's problem, prepare a uniform quantum superposition, evaluate the function coherently into a second quantum register, and perform Simon Fourier sampling on the first. Each run produces a uniform binary string z with z⋅s=0 over the two-element field. Once the observed rows have matrix rank n−1, Gaussian elimination recovers s as the unique nonzero element of their kernel. The random spanning of a binary vector space bounds the required number of runs.
