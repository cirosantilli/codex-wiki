Equalizer presentation by powers of a coseparator (B⟶SI⇉SJ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Coseparator
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
In a complete category that is a locally small category with a coseparator S, suppose every monomorphism is a regular monomorphism. The evaluation embedding B→SI, I=C(B,S), is an equalizer of some pair into Q. Composing that pair with the evaluation embedding Q→SJ leaves the equalizer unchanged. Here powers mean products in a category indexed by sets, without assuming an exponential object exists.
