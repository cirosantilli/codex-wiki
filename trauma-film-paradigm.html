Trauma-film paradigm

 Home Biology Neuroscience Memory in animals Memory retrieval Intrusive memory
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
An experimental analogue using distressing film material to study later involuntary memories. It permits controlled manipulations and prospective diaries but is not equivalent to clinical trauma exposure or a diagnosis of PTSD.[ref]
