Shallow-water quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity (Q=∇2ψ−ψ/LD2​+βy)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Potential vorticity Shallow-water potential vorticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 434 Articles: 7
For small Rossby number and small free-surface displacement, shallow-water potential vorticity reduces, up to a constant factor and additive constant, to relative vorticity plus background PV gradients and the stretching term −ψ/LD2​.
