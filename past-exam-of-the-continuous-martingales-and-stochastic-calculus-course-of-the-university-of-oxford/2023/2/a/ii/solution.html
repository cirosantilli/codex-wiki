Here ⟨M⟩ is the predictable quadratic variation, equal to [M] for a continuous martingale. On the whole half-line, the terminal form is
E∣(H⋅M)∞​∣2=E∫0∞​Hs2​d⟨M⟩s​.

For completeness, cross terms of the finite increment sum have expectation zero by conditioning on the earlier endpoint of the later increment. Also M2−⟨M⟩ is a martingale, so the remaining terms satisfy
E[ξj2​(Mv​−Mu​)2]=E[ξj2​(⟨M⟩v​−⟨M⟩u​)](u=tj​<t, v=tj+1​∧t).
