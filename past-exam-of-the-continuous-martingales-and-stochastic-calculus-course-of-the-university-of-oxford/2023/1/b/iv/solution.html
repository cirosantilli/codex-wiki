Use the same finite truncation Tm​(h)=∑j≤m​⟨h,ej​⟩Aj​ for f and g. Since E[Ai​Aj​]=δij​ by independence, zero means and unit variances,
E[Tm​(f)Tm​(g)]=∑j≤m​⟨f,ej​⟩⟨g,ej​⟩.
(14)
Here δij​ is the Kronecker delta. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality and convergence in L2 justify passing to the limit in the product's expectation. The Parseval identity for a Hilbertian basis now gives
E[(Wf)(Wg)]=⟨f,g⟩H​=∫01​f(t)g(t)dt.​
(15)
Because each Wh has mean zero, this is also its covariance identity. Thus W is an isonormal Gaussian process over H.
