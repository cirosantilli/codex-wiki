Rank of an elliptic curve (r=dimQ​(E(K)⊗Z​Q))

 ... Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Mordell-Weil group
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
For an elliptic curve over a number field K, the Mordell-Weil theorem writes E(K) as a finite torsion subgroup plus a free abelian group of finite rank of an abelian group r. This integer is the rank of the elliptic curve over K. It can increase under field extension. A two-isogeny descent supplies rank bounds from rational square-class images; the canonical height pairing can certify independence of rational points.
