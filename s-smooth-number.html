S-smooth number (S-smooth number)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Smooth number
OurBigBook.com Words: 87 Articles: 1
For a finite set S of primes, an integer is S-smooth when every prime dividing it belongs to S. Bounding the largest prime factor is equivalent to restricting the integer to be S-smooth for a suitable finite S.
