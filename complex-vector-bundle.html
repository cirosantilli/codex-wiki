Complex vector bundle (E→X)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 272 Articles: 4
A complex vector bundle is a vector bundle with complex-linear local trivializations and transition maps. A rank-r complex bundle has an underlying oriented real bundle of rank 2r, using the orientation of complex bases. Rank-one bundles are complex line bundles, and their higher-rank counterparts have integral Chern classes.
