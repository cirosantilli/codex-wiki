For a=−1−α′s/4, b=−1−α′t/4 and a+b+c=1, the amplitude has generic simple poles at a=−n. The gamma function residue and recurrence give

The residue polynomial has degree 2n, consistent with closed-string exchange up to spin 2n. When a=−n+δa​ and b=−m+δb​, the reciprocal factor 1/Γ(a+b) removes a putative double pole: the leading ratio is (nn+m​)2(1/δa​+1/δb​). Thus channel singularities must be counted using the kinematic constraint, not by multiplying independent numerator poles.
