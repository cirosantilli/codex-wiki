Fundamental principle of Riemannian holonomy

 ... Principal bundle Connection (principal bundle) Horizontal distribution of a principal connection Holonomy Riemannian holonomy group Holonomy representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
Evaluation at a point identifies parallel tensor fields with tensors fixed by the full Riemannian holonomy group. A parallel field is fixed after transport around every loop. Conversely transport a fixed tensor along paths from the basepoint; loop invariance makes the result independent of the path, and local parallel transport shows smoothness and parallelness. Connectedness gives uniqueness.
