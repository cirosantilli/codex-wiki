John contact decomposition (∑j​cj​uj​⊗uj​=I)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Ellipsoid John ellipsoid
OurBigBook.com Words: 140
For a centrally symmetric convex body in John position, there are contact points uj​∈∂K∩Sd−1 and positive weights cj​ such that ∑j​cj​uj​⊗uj​=I. Taking the trace gives ∑j​cj​=d. A supporting hyperplane at uj​ shows ∣⟨uj​,x⟩∣≤∥x∥K​.
Here is the maximum-volume argument for the decomposition. If I were outside the convex cone generated by the contact matrices, a separating hyperplane would give a symmetric matrix A with trA>0 and ⟨Au,u⟩≤0 at every contact. Subtract a small positive multiple of I so these inequalities become strict while the trace remains positive. The support function of (I+tA)B2d​ is ∣(I+tA)v∣. It decreases below 1 near all contact directions for small positive t; away from them, the strict gap hK​(v)>1 preserves containment. Its volume increases, since det(I+tA)=1+ttrA+O(t2), a contradiction. The cone is closed because its generators have trace 1 and form a compact set; finite-dimensional convexity gives a finite decomposition.
