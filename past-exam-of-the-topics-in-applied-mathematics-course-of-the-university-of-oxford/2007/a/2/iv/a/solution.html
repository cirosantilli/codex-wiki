At the origin the Jacobian has trace η−1−K/ϵ<0 and determinant (λ−ηK)/ϵ>0. Its off-diagonal entries are positive, making the eigenvalues real, so this is an attracting node. At the positive equilibrium the slow reduced derivative is η−λK/(ue​+K)2=ηve​>0, while the fast derivative is −(ue​+K)/ϵ<0; equivalently the full Jacobian determinant is negative. Thus the positive point is a saddle equilibrium. Its stable manifold separates extinction from unbounded substrate growth.
