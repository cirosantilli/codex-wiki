A positive fixed point requires β+u∗γ​=α, so it exists exactly when α>β​ and is u∗​=(α−β)1/γ. Differentiating the map at it gives
F′(u∗​)=1−γ(1−αβ​).​
(1)
For 1<γ≤2 and 0<β/α<1, this fixed-point multiplier is strictly between −1 and 1. Hence the positive fixed point is linearly asymptotically stable, including γ=2; positivity of β makes the lower bound strict.
