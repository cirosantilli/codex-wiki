A fixed point X∗​ satisfies F(X∗​)=X∗​. The Jacobian matrix is DF=(fx​gx​​fy​gy​​); its eigenvalues are the linear fixed-point multipliers used in (i)–(iii).
For the Lozi map, y=bx and x=1+bx−a∣x∣. At a positive point this gives (1+a−b)x=1, and at a negative point (1−a−b)x=1. Therefore
X+​=1+a−b(1,b)​when b<1+a,X−​=1−a−b(1,b)​when b>1−a.​
(35)
There is no fixed point at x=0, because then y=0 and the first component would be one. On an equality boundary the corresponding scalar equation is 0=1, so that branch is absent, not a line of fixed points.
For x=0, DF=(−asgnxb​10​). If a=0, there is exactly one fixed point (1,b)/(1−b) unless b=1, when there is none. Its eigenvalues satisfy λ2=b. It is a sink fixed point of a map for ∣b∣<1 and a source fixed point of a map for ∣b∣>1. At b=−1, the affine map about its fixed point is a quarter turn, DF2=−I and F4=I, so it is neutral, not attracting. At b=1 no fixed point exists. The positive/negative branch labels follow the sign of 1−b.
If b=0, X+​=(1/(1+a),0) exists for a>−1 with fixed-point multipliers −a,0. It is a sink fixed point of a map for −1<a<1 and a forward saddle fixed point of a map for a>1. At a=1 the fixed-point multiplier −1 is neutral; on the invariant axis the interval 0≤x≤1 is mapped by x↦1−x, giving two-cycles and the fixed midpoint. The negative point X−​=(1/(1−a),0) exists only for a>1 and has fixed-point multipliers a,0, again a forward saddle fixed point of a map. For a≤−1 there is no fixed point. At a=b=0, both fixed-point multipliers vanish and two iterates send all initial points to the fixed point.
For a genuine two-cycle let the first coordinates be x1​,x2​ and put h=1−b. The two equations are hx2​=1−a∣x1​∣, hx1​=1−a∣x2​∣. If both first coordinates had the same sign s, subtracting would give (h−as)(x2​−x1​)=0. Since distinct cycle points must have different first coordinates, this contradicts a2=h2. If one coordinate were zero, the remaining equations would instead force a=±h, also excluded. Hence x1​x2​<0​.
Choose x1​>0>x2​. Solving ax1​+hx2​=1, hx1​−ax2​=1 gives
x1​=a2+h2a+h​,x2​=a2+h2h−a​,X1​=(x1​,bx2​),X2​=(x2​,bx1​).​
(36)
The required signs hold exactly when a>∣1−b∣​. This is the unique two-cycle up to interchanging its two points, under the stipulated nondegeneracy. Borderline parameter lines can carry different degenerate families and were explicitly excluded. These are Lozi fixed points and the alternating-sign two-cycle.
