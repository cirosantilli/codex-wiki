For ν=1,κ=0, the first integral is
E=21​y2−21​x2+41​x4.​
(33)
Its minimum −1/4 occurs at the two centres. Levels between −1/4 and 0 give a periodic orbit around each centre equilibrium; positive levels give larger periodic orbits surrounding both wells. The zero level is the figure-eight separatrix through the saddle, with two homoclinic loops.
On E=0, y2=x2(1−x2/2). The printed parametrization x=±2​sechz, y=±2​sechztanhz, taking matching signs, satisfies this identity because 1−sech2z=tanh2z. It also has dx/dz=−y and dy/dz=−(x−x3), so its parameter is physical reversed time, z=−t+constant, rather than z=t. It is nonetheless exactly a parametrization of the requested trajectory. In physical forward time one can use x(t)=2​secht, y(t)=−2​sechttanht on the right loop.
Approach to the origin requires z→±∞, hence infinite physical time. Equivalently ∣y∣∼x near the right-loop endpoint, so the elapsed time contains the divergent integral ∫0δ​dx/x. The point at x=2​ is reached in finite time from any interior point; it is the asymptotic saddle endpoints, not that turning point, that cause the infinite total traversal time.
