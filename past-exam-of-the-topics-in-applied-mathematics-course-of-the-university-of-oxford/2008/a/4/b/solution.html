For spatially homogeneous perturbations write q=1+u and c=η+v. The linearized reaction equations are
(u˙v˙​)=J(uv​),J=(−1κη​0−κ​).​
(41)
This triangular matrix has eigenvalues −1 and −κ, both negative. Hence the homogeneous steady state is linearly asymptotically stable against homogeneous perturbations. Even when κ=1, where the two eigenvalues coincide and a polynomial factor may occur in the matrix exponential, all such perturbations still decay.
