Free pro-p group (Fp​(X))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Profinite group Pro-p group
OurBigBook.com Words: 260 Articles: 3
For finite X, this is the pro-p completion of the free group on X. Every function from X to a pro-p group extends uniquely to a continuous group homomorphism. More generally one can require a basis tending to the identity and impose the universal property for maps with that convergence condition; alternatively the pro-p completion of the abstract free group represents unrestricted maps from an arbitrary set. These infinite-rank conventions should not be conflated when interpreting generator cardinalities.
