Transformation model (gY∼Pgθ​when Y∼Pθ​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model
OurBigBook.com Words: 309 Articles: 4
A group action on the sample space induces a compatible action on the parameter space, preserving the family of probability laws. In a transitive model every law arises by transforming a fixed reference law. A location-scale model is generated by y↦a+by, b>0, with parameter action (μ,σ)↦(a+bμ,bσ).
