Enol

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 5
An enol has an OH group bonded to a carbon of a C=C bond. Protonation and pi-bond rearrangement can interconvert it with a carbonyl compound through keto-enol tautomerism.
