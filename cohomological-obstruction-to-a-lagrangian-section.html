Cohomological obstruction to a Lagrangian section ([σ]=0⟹no global Lagrangian section)

 ... Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Lagrangian submanifold Cotangent bundle Twisted cotangent symplectic form Twisted Lagrangian graph criterion
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
If [σ]=0 in de Rham cohomology, a twisted cotangent symplectic form has no Lagrangian submanifold whose projection is a diffeomorphism onto the entire base. Such a submanifold would be a global one-form graph, and the twisted Lagrangian graph criterion would make σ exact.
