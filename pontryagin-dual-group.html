Pontryagin dual group (G)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Abelian group Pontryagin duality
OurBigBook.com Words: 191 Articles: 2
For a locally compact Hausdorff Abelian group G, its Pontryagin dual is the group of continuous unitary characters, with pointwise multiplication and the compact-open topology. Its topology agrees with the Gelfand topology of the L1 convolution algebra. It is again a locally compact Hausdorff Abelian group.
