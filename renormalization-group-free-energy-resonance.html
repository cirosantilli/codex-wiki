An analytic source monomial gmn​tmhn in an additive renormalization-group transformation of free-energy density is resonant when its scaling degree equals the spatial dimension. The analytic background coefficient would require division by 1−bmλt​+nλh​−D=0. On a branch with t=0 and λt​>0, a particular nonanalytic background is −gmn​tmhnlog∣t∣/(λt​logb): substituting t′=bλt​t, h′=bλh​h verifies the additive recursion exactly. Thus logarithms can be required even when the eliminated-mode source is analytic. This is distinct from marginal interaction running, which can create additional logarithmic corrections.
