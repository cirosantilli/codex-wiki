A one-particle state belongs to the one-excitation sector of a Fock space. In a momentum basis with [ap​,aq†​]=(2π)3δ3(p−q), the wavepacket ∫d3pf(p)ap†​∣0⟩/(2π)3 has norm squared ∫d3p∣f(p)∣2/(2π)3. Sharp-momentum states have distributional rather than unit normalization; factors of 2Ep​​ convert to relativistic normalization of a one-particle state.
