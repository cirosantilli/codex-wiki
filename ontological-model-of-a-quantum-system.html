Ontological model of a quantum system (μψ​(λ),ξj​(λ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Foundations of quantum mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 334 Articles: 6
An ontological model associates each quantum state preparation with a probability distribution over underlying physical states λ. A measurement in quantum mechanics's response probabilities depend on its specification and λ, sum to one, and reproduce the Born rule when averaged over the preparation distribution. The model need not assign deterministic outcomes. Overlap or disjointness of preparation measures defines the psi-epistemic model versus psi-ontic model distinction.
