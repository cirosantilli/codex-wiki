LOD score (Z(θ)=log10​[L(θ)/L(1/2)])

 Home Biology Genetics Genetic linkage
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
The LOD score compares the likelihood at recombination fraction θ with independent assortment:
Z(θ)=log10​L(1/2)L(θ)​.
(4)
When phenotype likelihood is independent of θ, it can be computed from marker probabilities conditional on phenotype and ascertainment. Positive values support linkage; the observed marker pattern must be specified precisely because affected-only and affected-plus-unaffected observations give different likelihoods.
