Antenna effective area (Pav​=Aeff​S)

 Home Electrical engineering Antenna (radio) Antenna aperture
OurBigBook.com Words: 167 Articles: 2
The antenna effective area converts incident energy flux into available received power for a specified direction and matched polarization of an electromagnetic wave. In a lossless free-space link, a transmitter of antenna gain G at range r and a receiver of effective area Aeff​ have Pr​/Pt​=GAeff​/(4πr2). Impedance or polarization mismatch reduces the delivered power.
