Henkin omission extension lemma

 ... Model theory Elementary extension Complete type Type space Omitted type Omitting types theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
Let p(x) be a nonprincipal partial type over a consistent first-order theory T. Suppose T∪{σ(c)} is consistent, with σ a finite condition in finitely many new constants, and let t(c) be a tuple of closed terms. Some ψ∈p leaves T∪{σ(c),¬ψ(t(c))} consistent. Otherwise the original-language first-order formula
θ(x)=∃z(σ(z)∧x=t(z))
(70)
is consistent with T and entails every member of p, contradicting nonprincipality. Replace all new constants occurring in the condition or terms by variables. This lemma makes the omission requirements dense in a countable Henkin construction.
