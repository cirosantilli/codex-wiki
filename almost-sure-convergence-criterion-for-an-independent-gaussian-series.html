Almost sure convergence criterion for an independent Gaussian series (∑k​ak​Zk​ converges a.s.⟺∑k​ak2​<∞)

For independent standard Gaussian random variables, the partial sums have characteristic functions exp(−u2vn​/2), where vn​=∑k≤n​ak2​. A finite almost-sure limit implies convergence of these characteristic functions to one continuous at zero; vn​→∞ would instead give zero at every nonzero u. Conversely, bounded vn​ makes the partial sums an martingale bounded in L2 space, which converges almost surely and in L2 space.
