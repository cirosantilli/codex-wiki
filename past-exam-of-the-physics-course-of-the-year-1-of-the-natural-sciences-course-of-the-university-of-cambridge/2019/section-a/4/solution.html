Use base dimensions M,L,T for mass, length and time. The proposed dimensional analysis gives
[v]=LT−1,[ω]=T−1,[ρ]=ML−3,[g]=LT−2,
(14)
so
[ωaρbgc]=MbL−3b+cT−a−2c.
(15)
Equating the independent dimensional powers requires
b=0,−3b+c=1,−a−2c=−1.
(16)
Consequently
a=−1,b=0,c=1,v∝ωg​.​
(17)
The mass density drops out because there is no other mass-bearing quantity with which to combine it under the stated assumptions.
Dimensional analysis fixes the powers but leaves a dimensionless coefficient undetermined. For a linear deep-water gravity wave, the dispersion relation is ω2=gk, giving phase velocity ω/k=g/ω and group velocity dω/dk=g/(2ω). Both obey the inferred scaling. The phrase wave speed alone does not distinguish these two velocities or fix the coefficient.
