The dimensionless factor C cancels when the same confinement geometry and state are scaled. Hence
Kp​=Ke​mp​me​​(Rp​Re​​)2,Kp​≃8.3MeV.​
(11)
Using mp​/me​≃1836 gives this value. It remains small compared with the proton rest energy, so the nonrelativistic mass-radius scaling is self-consistent.
