For a fixed set of bodies in an inertial frame, total linear momentum is constant if the resultant external force vanishes. Summing Newton's second law cancels internal force pairs by Newton's third law. For an open subsystem, momentum carried across its boundary must also be included.
For an equal-intake-fuel mass-loading rocket, enclose the original craft and the stationary material collected during dt. Its initial momentum is mu. At the end, the craft has mass m−μdt and the exhaust has mass 2μdt. To first order,
