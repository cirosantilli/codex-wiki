The threshold must conserve both energy and momentum. For head-on photons, the total energy is E1​+E2​, while the total momentum has magnitude ∣E2​−E1​∣/c. Their combined invariant mass M satisfies
M2c4=(E1​+E2​)2−c2(cE2​−E1​​)2=4E1​E2​.
(12)
At the threshold for photon-photon pair production, the electron and Positron have no relative momentum in their centre-of-momentum frame, so M=2me​. Therefore
E2,min​=E1​(me​c2)2​=10−3 eV(5.110×105 eV)2​≃2.61×1014 eV=261 TeV.​
(13)
Simply equating the laboratory energy to 2me​c2 would miss the very large laboratory momentum: the newly produced pair must carry it away.
