The upper relation is an asymptotic design condition, because “approximately” specifies no numerical tolerance. For example, retaining at least 99% of the low-frequency amplitude would sharpen the upper bound to approximately 68.7 μF.
A practical illustrative choice is C=22 μF. Its voltage gain is approximately 0.99896 at 1 Hz and 0.001096 at 20 kHz: the latter amplitude is reduced by a factor of about 912. Its cutoff frequency is 1/(2πRC)≃21.9 Hz.
