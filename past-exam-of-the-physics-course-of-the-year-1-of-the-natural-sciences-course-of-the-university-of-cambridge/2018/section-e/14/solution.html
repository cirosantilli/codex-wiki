Infinite circular cylindrical rod. Let b be the radius of the circular cross-section, so A=πb2, and write λ=ρA for the linear mass density. Choose a coaxial cylindrical Gaussian surface with radius r>b and length L. Translation and rotation symmetry make g=gr​(r)r.
