Use dimensions M,L,T for mass, length and time. The reduced Planck constant, gravitational constant and speed of light have dimensions
[ℏ]=ML2T−1,[G]=L3M−1T−2,[c]=LT−1.
(7)
For lp​=ℏαGβcγ to have the dimensions of a length, dimensional analysis requires
α−β=0,2α+3β+γ=1,−α−2β−γ=0.
(8)
The first equation gives β=α; the third gives γ=−3α. Substitution in the second gives 2α=1. Hence
α=21​,β=21​,γ=−23​,lp​=c3ℏG​​.​
(9)
With ℏ=1.054571817×10−34Js, G=6.67430×10−11m3kg−1s−2 and c=2.99792458×108ms−1, the Planck length is
lp​≃1.6163×10−35m.​
(10)
The proposed monomial fixes the dimensionless prefactor to one; dimensional analysis on its own cannot select an arbitrary dimensionless multiplier.
