Frame definitions and relativistic effects. Let S be the inertial frame of Earth and the stationary asteroid, with +x pointing toward the asteroid. Bob's inertial frame S′ moves at +v relative to S. For two events at Bob's fixed position, Δx′=0, the inverse Lorentz transformation gives
Δt=γΔt′,γ=(1−v2/c2)−1/2.
(30)
The elapsed proper time on Bob's clock is Δτ=Δt′, so time dilation means Δt=γΔτ: the separation of his clock ticks is larger in Earth coordinates. For a rod at rest in S′ with proper length L0​, a length measurement in S uses simultaneous endpoints, Δt=0. The direct spatial Lorentz transformation gives L0​=Δx′=γΔx, hence length contraction:
L=L0​/γ.​
(31)
The simultaneity condition is essential: the endpoint events used to measure length are different from the two clock-tick events used to demonstrate time dilation.
Bob's speed. Food consumption measures Bob's own proper time, τB​=4 months=1/3yr. The Earth-frame distance is D=1/4ly and the Earth travel time is D/v. Consequently
τB​=γvD​,γcv​=cτB​D​=43​.
(32)
Putting β=v/c, β2/(1−β2)=9/16, so
v=53​c=0.600c,γ=45​.​
(33)
The Earth travel time is 5/12yr=5 months. Equivalently Bob measures the contracted Earth-asteroid separation D/γ=0.20ly and sees the asteroid approach at 0.60c, taking 1/3yr to reach him.
Events A and B. Set both coordinate origins at Event A. Alice reaches the asteroid at Event B, travelling at u=2c/3 in S, so
Δx=41​ly,Δt=uD​=83​yr=4.5 months.​
(34)
The Lorentz transformations into Bob's frame give
Δt′Δx′​=45​(83​−53​41​)yr=329​yr=3.375 months​,=45​(41​−53​83​)ly=321​ly=0.03125ly​.​
(35)
Here c(1yr)=1ly has been used consistently. With the time-positive sign convention, the Minkowski interval is
Δs2Δs′2​=c2Δt2−Δx2=(649​−644​)ly2=645​ly2​,=c2Δt′2−Δx′2=(102481​−10241​)ly2=645​ly2​.​
(36)
Thus the Minkowski interval is invariant. It is timelike, as expected for two events on Alice's subluminal worldline. Using the opposite metric sign convention would change both signs together, without changing invariance.
Relative velocity and spacetime diagram. Divide the differential spatial and temporal Lorentz transformations for the same trajectory:
ux′​=dt′dx′​=γ(dt−vdx/c2)γ(dx−vdt)​=1−vux​/c2ux​−v​​.
(37)
For Alice,
uAlice′​=1−(2/3)(3/5)(2/3−3/5)c​=91​c.​
(38)
The spacetime diagram uses horizontal x′ and vertical ct′. In these coordinates Bob's worldline is x′=0, Alice's is x′=ct′/9, and the asteroid's is
xasteroid′​=γD​−vt′=51​ly−53​ct′.
(39)
Thus Alice has slope d(ct′)/dx′=9, while the asteroid has slope −5/3. Their intersection is exactly B, (x′,ct′)=(1/32,9/32)ly. The asteroid later reaches Bob at (0,1/3)ly. These intercepts and gradients show the relativistic velocity addition geometrically.
