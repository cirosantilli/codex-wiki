The force is conservative, with F=−∇U and potential energy zero at the origin:
U(t)=2k​[x(t)2+y(t)2]=2k​[x02​cos2(ωt+ϕ1​)+y02​cos2(ωt+ϕ2​)].​
(43)
Released from rest at (c,c), the trajectory has x=y=ccosωt, giving
U(t)=kc2cos2ωt=2kc2​(1+cos2ωt).​
(44)
The right panel of the figure shows this nonnegative oscillation: it starts at a maximum kc2, reaches zero at t=π/(2ω), and repeats after π/ω. Its frequency is twice the coordinate frequency. Conservation of mechanical energy gives K(t)=kc2sin2ωt, so the missing potential energy is kinetic energy, not an energy loss.
