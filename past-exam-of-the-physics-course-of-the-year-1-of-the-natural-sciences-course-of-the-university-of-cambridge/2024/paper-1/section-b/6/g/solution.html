For actual complete bounce distances D1​=4m and Dn+1​=rDn​, the geometric series gives
Dtotal​=1−r4​m,0≤r<1.​
(19)
A natural intended estimate compares the observed first round trip with the loss-free round trip from the initially compressed position,
Dideal​=2(Hs​+x)=5.13764m.
(20)
If the reduction factor is taken to be r=D1​/Dideal​=0.77857, the answer is Dtotal​≃18.1m. The first term is the actual 4 m, not the hypothetical loss-free distance.
This numerical inference needs the stated comparison assumption. Strictly, knowing the first actual bounce distance and saying subsequent distances share a ratio does not independently specify that ratio. A change in spring compression also means that an energy-loss fraction is not automatically the same as a vertical-distance ratio.
