Let CT​=C+C1​ be the total parallel capacitance, since capacitors in a parallel circuit have additive capacitances. Put ω∗​=2π(50Hz) into the lossy parallel coil resonance condition:
CT​=R2+ω∗2​L2L​=102+[2π(50)(0.150)]20.150​F=64.6368μF.

The additional capacitance is therefore
C1​=CT​−C=64.6368−10.0000=54.6368μF≃54.6μF.​
(57)
This is the added capacitance, rather than the new total, and the conversion from frequency to angular frequency is essential.
