The approach track's curvature is unspecified, so a global bound for an arbitrary approach shape cannot be proved from the sketch alone; the result above concerns the specified circular part and the usual smooth approach interpretation.
The finite-friction qualification of a marginal rolling loop also matters: the contact threshold presumes the stated no-slip idealization. At equality, near the top, required tangential friction vanishes linearly with angular distance from the top but N vanishes quadratically. Thus any finite coefficient of static friction requires a release height above this limiting bound to maintain rolling everywhere; no coefficient is provided. This does not change the requested ideal contact criterion.
