For two equal lenses with a virtual intermediate object, use positive distances for real objects on a lens's incident side and real images on its outgoing side. The first lens gives 1/v1​=1/f−1/u1​=1/30−1/60, hence v1​=60cm. At the second lens these rays are already converging toward a point 50cm to its right: its object distance is therefore u2​=−50cm.
v2​1​=301​−−501​=754​cm−1,v2​=18.75cm to the right of lens 2.​
(1)
Thus the final image is real, 28.75cm to the right of lens 1. Its transverse magnification is (−60/60)(−18.75/[−50])=−3/8: it is inverted. The intermediate focus is not a real object placed before lens 2.
