Loop Feynman diagram (L=I−V+1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram
OurBigBook.com Words: 79 Articles: 1
A connected loop Feynman diagram has at least one independent internal loop momentum. With I internal lines and V vertices, the number of independent loops is L=I−V+1. A tree-level Feynman diagram has L=0. A multi-leg amplitude generated by a loop is not an extra fundamental interaction vertex of the original Lagrangian.
