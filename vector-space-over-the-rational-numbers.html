Vector space over the rational numbers (V over Q)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A vector space with scalar field Q is equivalently a torsion-free divisible Abelian group. An embedding of such groups automatically respects rational scalar multiplication because solutions to nb=ma are unique.
