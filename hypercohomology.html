Hypercohomology (Hk(X,C∙))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Sheaf cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
Hypercohomology applies the derived global-section functor to a complex of sheaves. One may resolve the complex by an injective double complex, take global sections, and take the cohomology of its total complex. For a complex concentrated in degree zero it is ordinary sheaf cohomology. The relative de Rham complex uses this construction to compute fibre cohomology and its Hodge filtration.
