Darwin term (HD​=ℏ2∇2V/(8m2c2))

 Home Physics Atomic physics Fine structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
The spin-one-half low-energy expansion of the Dirac equation includes this local correction in a static potential energy V. For V=−Ze2/(4πϵ0​r), ∇2V=Ze2δ(3)(r)/ϵ0​, so the correction is positive and probes the wavefunction at the origin. In point-Coulomb hydrogen atom states it contributes only to S waves at leading nonrelativistic order. It is part of fine structure, distinct from the radiative Lamb shift. [ref]
