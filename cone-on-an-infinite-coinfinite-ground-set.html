Cone on an infinite coinfinite ground set (EA​=[A]ω)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Ramsey theory Space of infinite subsets of the natural numbers Ellentuck topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 1
For an infinite coinfinite set A⊆N, let EA​=[A]ω in the space of infinite subsets of the natural numbers. This cone is a nonempty clopen set in the Ellentuck topology, because it is the basic neighbourhood [∅,A], and any X∈/EA​ has a finite initial segment containing a point outside A and hence a basic neighbourhood disjoint from EA​.
The cone is a closed set with empty interior in the ordinary topology on infinite subsets: membership of a point outside A witnesses its complement as an open set, while every ordinary finite-stem neighbourhood can be extended by an arbitrarily large point outside A. Thus EA​ is an ordinary nowhere dense set, but it is neither ordinary open nor Ellentuck nowhere dense. It provides both distinctions between these two topologies.
