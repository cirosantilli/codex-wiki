For Lk​=k∂z​+k−1∂zˉ​, k=0, ∣k∣=1, this kernel satisfies Lk​Gk​=δ0​. A real-linear change of complex coordinate reduces its derivation to the Cauchy-Green operator; the determinant changes sign at the unit circle. For ∣k∣=1 the operator becomes a real directional transport equation, whose characteristic singularity requires limiting prescriptions. The kernel has Gk​∼k/(πz) at zero and Gk​∼1/(πkzˉ) at infinity.
