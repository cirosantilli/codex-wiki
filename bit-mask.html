Bit mask

 Home Computer science Programming language
OurBigBook.com Words: 137 Articles: 3
A word selecting specified binary positions under a bitwise operation. To extract a stored bit at position b, logically shift by b and combine with the mask 1. Grouping thirty-two cells into one Java integer permits a compact representation of a Conway Game of Life board.
