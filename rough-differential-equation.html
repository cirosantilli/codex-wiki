Rough differential equation (dY=V(Y)dX)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Rough path
OurBigBook.com Words: 255 Articles: 3
A rough differential equation interprets a differential equation using the higher-order data of its driving rough path. For 2<p<3, its local expansion includes Vi​(Ys​)Xs,ti​+DVj​(Ys​)Vi​(Ys​)Xs,tij​, with a remainder controlled at order greater than one. For a smooth driver it is the usual controlled ordinary differential equation; for enhanced Brownian motion it is the corresponding Stratonovich equation.
