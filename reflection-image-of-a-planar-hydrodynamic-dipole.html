For a complex potential w(z)=m/(z−a), reflection in the straight wall through zero at angle θ is Rz=e2iθzˉ. The image construction W(z)=w(z)+w(Rz)​ gives
W(z)=z−am​+z−e2iθaˉe2iθmˉ​.
(75)
On the wall Rz=z, this is real, so its stream function is constant there. This proves the impermeability condition. Thus the image position is a′=e2iθaˉ and its moment is m′=e2iθmˉ. If the point dipole is advected by the regular image flow, the complex velocity convention W′=u−iv gives
dtdaˉ​=−(a−a′)2m′​=4d2mˉ​,
(76)
where d=Im(e−iθa) is its signed distance from the wall. The last equality follows from a−a′=2ieiθd. It applies when d=0.
