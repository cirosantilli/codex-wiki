Groups of order seventy (C70​, D70​, D14​×C5​, D10​×C7​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 114
These are all the groups of group order 70 up to group isomorphism, with Dn​ denoting a dihedral group of order n. The Sylow theorems make the Sylow subgroups of orders five and seven unique: the possible counts divide fourteen and ten, respectively, and only one has the required residue. These normal subgroups of coprime order commute, giving a normal subgroup N≅C35​. Cauchy's theorem for finite groups provides an element of order two outside N, so G=N⋊C2​.
The automorphism of a finite cyclic group defining this semidirect product is a unit a modulo 35 with a2≡1. The Chinese remainder theorem makes its restrictions modulo five and seven independently +1 or −1. The resulting exponents are 1,34,6,29, giving the four displayed groups in order. Their center of a group orders are 70,1,5,7, so no two are isomorphic. Every case exists as the specified semidirect product.
