Period-doubling renormalization operator (T(f)(x)=f(f(ax))/a)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Discrete dynamical system Interval map Unimodal interval map
OurBigBook.com Words: 393 Articles: 5
For a normalized even unimodal interval map with f(0)=1 and a=f(1)∈(−1,0), define
T(f)(x)=a−1f(f(ax)).
(15)
This returns the second iterate near the critical point to the original spatial and height normalization. Then T(f)(0)=f(1)/a=1. A restrictive-interval condition is also needed for the composition to remain unimodal and map [−1,1] into itself; normalization alone is not sufficient.
