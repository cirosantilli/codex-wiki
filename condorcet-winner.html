Condorcet winner

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Mechanism design Social choice theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
A Condorcet winner defeats every other alternative by a strict majority in pairwise voting. It is unique when it exists. For an odd electorate with single-peaked preferences, the median peak is a Condorcet winner: alternatives on either side lose to the majority whose peaks lie at or beyond the median in the other direction. With an even electorate ties may prevent a strict winner.
