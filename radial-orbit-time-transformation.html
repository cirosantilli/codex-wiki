Radial orbit time transformation (dτ/dt=(q/r)dq/dr)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 223 Articles: 4
For a central-force radial energy equation, choose a monotone radius q=q(r) and clock τ such that dτ/dt=(q/r)dq/dr. Then q′=(r/q)r˙, and multiplying the old energy equation by r2/q2 preserves the centrifugal form L2/(2q2). A split of the remaining terms into constant energy and a new Newtonian gravitational potential produces a transformed radial orbit.
