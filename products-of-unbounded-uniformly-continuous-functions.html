Products of unbounded uniformly continuous functions

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Topological analysis Metric space Uniform continuity
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
A product of two uniformly continuous functions need not have uniform continuity on an unbounded domain. The functions f(x)=g(x)=x give the counterexample x2, since inputs n and n+1/n approach each other while their squared values remain separated. For bounded factors the estimate ∣f(x)g(x)−f(y)g(y)∣≤∥f∥∞​∣g(x)−g(y)∣+∥g∥∞​∣f(x)−f(y)∣ restores uniform continuity.
