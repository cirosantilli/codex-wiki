Zero-framed Hopf-link handle decomposition of the sphere product (S2×S2=h0∪h2∪h2∪h4)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological manifold Handle attachment Handle decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
Write each sphere as two disks, S2=D−2​∪D+2​. In their product, D−2​×D−2​ is a zero-handle; D+2​×D−2​ and D−2​×D+2​ are two-handles. Their attaching circles are the core circles of the complementary solid tori in ∂(D2×D2), hence a Hopf link. Constant normal directions from the disk factors give both components the zero Seifert framing. The remaining D+2​×D+2​ is a four-handle. This product construction establishes the actual diffeomorphism type, rather than relying on its intersection form alone. The resulting intersection matrix is (01​10​).
