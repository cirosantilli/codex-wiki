Transvection (T=I+vf,f(v)=0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map
OurBigBook.com Words: 178 Articles: 3
For a nonzero vector v and nonzero linear functional f vanishing on v, this invertible map fixes the hyperplane kerf pointwise and has determinant one. Its inverse is I−vf, since (vf)2=0. A symplectic transvection is obtained by taking f=cB(−,v) for a nondegenerate alternating bilinear form.
