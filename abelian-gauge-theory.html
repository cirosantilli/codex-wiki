Abelian gauge theory

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field
OurBigBook.com Words: 166 Articles: 4
An Abelian gauge theory has a commutative gauge group. Its gauge field strength has no commutator term, so the pure gauge Lagrangian has no cubic or quartic gauge-boson self-interactions.
