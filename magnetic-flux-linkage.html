Magnetic flux linkage (Λ=∑k=1N​Φk​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Inductance
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
Magnetic flux linkage adds the signed magnetic fluxes through the turns of a coil:
Λ=∑k=1N​Φk​,Φk​=∫Sk​​B⋅dS.
(152)
Choose each oriented surface Sk​ consistently with positive electric current around its turn using the right-hand rule. If all N turns link approximately the same magnetic flux Φ, then Λ≈NΦ; equality holds when every turn links exactly the same magnetic flux. Uniform magnetic field perpendicular to identical flat turns of area A gives Φ=BA. For a stipulated 100-turn coil with magnetic flux 0.015Wb per turn, magnetic flux linkage is 1.5Wb, not 0.015Wb. The turn count is dimensionless. [ref].
