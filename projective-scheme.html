Projective scheme

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Proper morphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 519 Articles: 12
A projective scheme over a base S is an S-scheme admitting a closed immersion into some projective space PSn​. Every projective morphism is proper.
Projectivity is a property of the scheme over its specified base, expressed by its structural projective morphism.
