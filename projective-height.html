Projective height (Hp​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Height function Absolute multiplicative Weil height
OurBigBook.com Words: 74 Articles: 1
For a nonzero vector a=(a0​,…,an​) over a number field K, its absolute projective height is
Hp​(a)=∏v∈MK​​maxi​∣ai​∣vdv​/[K:Q]​.
(57)
The product formula makes this unchanged by multiplying all coordinates by the same nonzero scalar. The projective height of a linear form is the projective height of its coefficient vector.
