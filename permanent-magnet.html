Permanent magnet

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetism Magnet
OurBigBook.com Words: 17
A permanent magnet retains magnetization after the applied magnetizing magnetic field is removed. Its working magnetic field persists without a driving electric current in a winding.
