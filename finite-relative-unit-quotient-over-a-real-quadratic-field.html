Finite relative unit quotient over a real quadratic field

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Dirichlet unit theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
If K is real quadratic and L/K is a number-field extension with OL×​/OK×​ finite, then the unit ranks agree. If d=[L:K] and L has signature (r1​,r2​), this forces
r1​+r2​=2,r1​+2r2​=2d.
(18)
For a proper extension the only possibility is d=2 and signature (0,2). For K=Q(5​), the example L=K(i) has this signature and a nontrivial finite quotient because i is a new root of unity.
