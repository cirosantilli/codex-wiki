For q=u,c and a neutral photon or Z boson, the loop runs over down-type quarks. CKM unitarity cancels a common mass-independent loop contribution. The large bottom mass relative to down and strange masses normally makes the surviving bottom contribution largest, and ∣Vcb​∣>∣Vub​∣ favours final charm. Adding a resolved photon to a qZ final state introduces another electromagnetic coupling and three-body phase space without removing the flavour cancellation. Some diagrams use the quartic gauge vertex, so the number of vertices need not increase.
