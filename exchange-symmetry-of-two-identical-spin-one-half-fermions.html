Exchange symmetry of two identical spin-one-half fermions

 ... Quantum mechanics Tensor product of quantum systems Multiparticle quantum state Particle exchange operator Indistinguishable particles Fermion
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
For two identical spin-one-half fermions with no additional antisymmetric internal factor, the antisymmetric spin-one-half singlet state combines with an even-ℓ symmetric orbital state, while the symmetric spin-one-half triplet state combines with an odd-ℓ antisymmetric orbital state.
