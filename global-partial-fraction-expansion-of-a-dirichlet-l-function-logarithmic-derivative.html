For a primitive nonprincipal Dirichlet character of conductor q and parity a, the completed Dirichlet L-function is entire of order one. Its genus-one Hadamard factorization gives the displayed logarithmic derivative, with Bχ​ the logarithmic derivative of the completion at zero and zeros counted with multiplicity. Combined summands converge locally normally away from zeros. For an imprimitive character induced by χ∗, add ∑p∣Q, p∤q​χ∗(p)logp/(ps−χ∗(p)) for the removed Euler factors. The primitive principal case is the global partial-fraction expansion of the zeta logarithmic derivative.
