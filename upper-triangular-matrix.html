Upper triangular matrix

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Triangular matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 73 Articles: 1
An upper triangular matrix has zero entries below its main diagonal. Its determinant is the product of its diagonal entries, and an invertible upper triangular linear system is solved by backward substitution.
