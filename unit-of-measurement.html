Unit of measurement

 Home Physics Metrology
OurBigBook.com Words: 124 Articles: 14
A unit of measurement is a chosen reference for comparing magnitudes of a physical quantity. A value written as a number times a measurement unit changes its numerical part when the measurement unit changes, while representing the same physical quantity.
