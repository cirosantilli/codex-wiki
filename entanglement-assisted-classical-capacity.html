Entanglement-assisted classical capacity (CE​(N)=maxρ​I(R;B))

 ... Quantum theory Quantum information theory Positive linear map Completely positive map Quantum channel Memoryless quantum channel
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
This is the supremum of reliable classical bits per use of a memoryless quantum channel when sender and receiver share unlimited prior quantum entanglement. If ∣ψρ​⟩RA​ purifies the input, let ωRB​=(idR​⊗N)(∣ψρ​⟩⟨ψρ​∣). The single-letter capacity formula is maxρ​[S(ρ)+S(N(ρ))−S(ωRB​)], the maximum quantum mutual information between reference and output. The preshared resource is independent of the message.
