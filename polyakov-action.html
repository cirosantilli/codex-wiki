Polyakov action

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory

The Polyakov action introduces an independent worldsheet metric gαβ​:
SP​=−2T​∫Σ​d2σ−g​gαβ∂α​X⋅∂β​X.
(2)
Its metric equation makes gαβ​ conformal to the induced worldsheet metric, reducing it classically to the Nambu–Goto action.
