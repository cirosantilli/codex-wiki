Tidal force

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field Tidal tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 1
Differential gravitational acceleration, multiplied by the affected mass, across a spatially extended body. In the linear approximation the acceleration difference is TΔr, where T is the tidal tensor. Stellar tidal forces limit the domain of a circumplanetary orbit; sufficiently strong ones can produce tidal disruption.
