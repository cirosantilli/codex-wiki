Brownian endpoint sufficiency for a constant drift

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Innovation process Gaussian Brownian drift filter
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
For Ys​=λs+Ws​ and fixed t>0, subtracting (s/t)Yt​ leaves the Brownian bridge Ws​−(s/t)Wt​. It is independent of (λ,Yt​), since it is independent of λ and has zero covariance with Wt​ in their jointly Gaussian law. The observed path is determined by this bridge and its endpoint, so the bridge gives no further information about λ. Hence conditioning on the full observation filtration at t gives the same posterior as conditioning on Yt​ alone.
