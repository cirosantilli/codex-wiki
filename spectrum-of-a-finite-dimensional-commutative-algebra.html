Spectrum of a finite-dimensional commutative algebra (SpecC=MaxSpecC,∣SpecC∣≤dimK​C)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Noetherian ring Artinian ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 99
If C is a commutative algebra of finite dimension over a field K, every prime ideal is maximal and ∣SpecC∣≤dimK​C. Indeed, the quotient by a prime is a finite-dimensional integral domain. Multiplication by any nonzero element is an injective linear endomorphism, hence surjective, so the quotient is a field. Distinct maximal ideals are pairwise comaximal. For any finite list m1​,…,mr​, construct ei​≡1(modmi​) and ei​≡0(modmj​) for j=i by multiplying elements in mj​ congruent to one modulo mi​. These give a surjection C→∏i​C/mi​. Each factor has positive K-dimension, so r≤dimK​C, excluding an infinite list.
