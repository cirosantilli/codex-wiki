Smooth locus of a variety

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Zariski tangent space
OurBigBook.com Words: 156 Articles: 3
The smooth locus consists of points P for which dimTX,P​=dimX. For an irreducible variety over a perfect field it is a nonempty Zariski-open subset and therefore dense.
