Graphical Stirling number ({nk​}G​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Set partition Stirling numbers of the second kind
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The graphical Stirling number {nk​}G​ counts partitions of the vertices of a graph G into k nonempty independent sets. Equivalently, it counts proper colourings with k unlabeled nonempty colour classes.
