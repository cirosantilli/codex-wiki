Right Ore condition (au=sb)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Noncommutative algebra Ore condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 221 Articles: 3
A multiplicative subset S⊆A with 1∈S and 0∈/S of a ring A satisfies the right Ore condition if for every a∈A and s∈S there are u∈S and b∈A such that au=sb. For a noncommutative domain, taking S=A∖{0} gives a ring of right fractions as−1; every nonzero fraction is invertible. The usual additional denominator reversibility condition is automatic when the elements of S are nonzero in a noncommutative domain.
