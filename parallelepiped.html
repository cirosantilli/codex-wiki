Parallelepiped

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Polyhedron
OurBigBook.com Words: 62 Articles: 1
A parallelepiped is a polyhedron with six parallelogram faces in three opposite pairs. Three generating vectors determine its edges; the absolute value of their determinant gives its volume in orthonormal Cartesian coordinates.
