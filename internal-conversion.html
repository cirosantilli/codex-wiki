Internal conversion

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Atomic nucleus Nuclear structure Nuclear de-excitation
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
Internal conversion transfers nuclear excitation energy directly to a bound atomic electron, which is emitted, rather than to a real gamma ray. Photon counts alone then underestimate the number of nuclear transitions unless the conversion contribution is included.
