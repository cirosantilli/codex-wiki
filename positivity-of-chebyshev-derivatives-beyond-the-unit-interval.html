Positivity of Chebyshev derivatives beyond the unit interval

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Chebyshev polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
Every root of Tn(k)​ lies in (−1,1) and its leading coefficient is positive. Hence
Tn(k)​(x)>0
(178)
for x≥1 and 0≤k≤n.
