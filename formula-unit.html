Formula unit

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
A formula unit is a specified compositional accounting entity for a chemical substance that need not consist of discrete molecules. For ordinary crystalline NaCl, one formula unit counts one sodium ion Na+ and one chlorine ion Cl−; it does not identify an isolated pair within the crystal. Cohen et al., IUPAC Green Book, 3rd ed., §2.10.1(iii)(b), p. 51 defines a formula unit as a specified group of chemical atoms, rather than a unit of measurement. An amount of substance of one mole of NaCl formula units therefore corresponds to one mole of each kind of ion.
