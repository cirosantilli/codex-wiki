Fermi velocity (vF​)

 ... Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Fermi-Dirac distribution Fermi gas Fermi energy Fermi momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
The group velocity of an excitation evaluated on the Fermi surface. For an isotropic dispersion relation ϵ(p), vF​=(dϵ/dp)pF​​, where pF​ is the Fermi momentum.
