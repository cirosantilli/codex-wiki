Finite fundamental group from a uniform positive Ricci bound (Ric≥(n−1)κg ⟹ ∣π1​(M)∣<∞)

OurBigBook.com Words: 72
If a connected complete Riemannian manifold has Ric≥(n−1)κg with κ>0, its universal cover with the lifted metric is complete and satisfies the same bound. The Bonnet-Myers theorem makes that cover compact. A covering fiber is closed and discrete and hence finite; the number of its elements equals the order of the fundamental group. Applying the diameter theorem only to the base would not prove this conclusion: the positive lower bound must also be used on the cover.
