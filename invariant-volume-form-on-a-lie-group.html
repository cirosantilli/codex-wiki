Invariant volume form on a Lie group (ωg​=(Lg−1​)∗ωe​)

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Left and right translation on a Lie group Left-invariant differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
Choose a nonzero element of ΛnTe∗​G for an n-dimensional Lie group and transport it by inverse left translations. This gives a smooth nowhere-zero left-invariant differential form of top degree, hence an orientation. The identity L(hg)−1​Lh​=Lg−1​ proves invariance. The space of invariant top forms is one-dimensional, since evaluation at the identity identifies it with ΛnTe∗​G.
