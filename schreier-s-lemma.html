Schreier's lemma

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Quasi-isometry Finite-index subgroup quasi-isometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 93 Articles: 1
If a group G is generated by S, a subgroup H has a set T of right-coset representatives, and ts denotes the representative of Hts, then H is generated by the Schreier generators
tsts−1(t∈T,s∈S).
(592)
In particular, every finite-index subgroup of a finitely generated group is finitely generated.
