Negative hydrogen ion opacity (κ∝ρ1/2T9)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure Radiative stellar structure Opacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 1
The negative hydrogen ion supplies important continuum opacity in cool hydrogen-rich stellar atmospheres. A useful local approximate law is κ=κ0​ρ1/2T9; it is not valid over arbitrary temperatures and compositions. In an ideal gas power-law opacity radiative envelope matched to ∇phot​=1/8, it gives ∇rad​=−1/3+(11/24)(T/Teff​)9/2, rising rapidly toward convective instability.
