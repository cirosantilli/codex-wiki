Symplectic quotient of the binary subset module (1⊥/⟨1⟩)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory General linear group over a finite field Symplectic group over a finite field
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For even n, the even-weight subspace of F2n​ has radical ⟨1⟩ for the dot product. Its quotient is a nondegenerate alternating bilinear form space of dimension n−2. The symmetric group acts by coordinate permutations. At n=6 this action is faithful and gives S6​≅Sp4​(2).
