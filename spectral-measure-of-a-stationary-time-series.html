Spectral measure of a stationary time series (γ(k)=∫−ππ​eikωdF(ω))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Stationary process Weakly stationary process
OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 2
The spectral measure of a weakly stationary process is the finite nonnegative measure whose Fourier coefficients are its autocovariances. Its total mass is the process variance. A time-series spectral density exists exactly when this measure is absolutely continuous with respect to Lebesgue measure.
