Flat galaxy rotation curve

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy rotation curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
A flat galaxy rotation curve has approximately constant circular speed vc​ over a radial interval. Under spherical symmetry it implies M(<r)=vc2​r/G and mass density ρ(r)=vc2​/(4πGr2).
