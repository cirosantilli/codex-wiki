Forced Airy boundary-value problem (−u′′+xu=f,u(0)=u(1)=0)

 ... Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Linear ordinary differential equation Second-order linear differential equation Airy ordinary differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
On the unit interval, the weak form is a(u,v)=∫01​fv, with a(u,v)=∫01​(u′v′+xuv) on the zero-boundary Sobolev space. The nonnegative potential makes this form coercive in the derivative norm, and the Poincaré inequality makes that norm equivalent to the Sobolev norm. The Lax-Milgram theorem gives a unique solution. A conforming Ritz method minimizes the corresponding strictly convex quadratic energy and produces a symmetric positive-definite tridiagonal matrix for piecewise-linear hats.
