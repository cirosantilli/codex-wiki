Clopen up-set

 ... Propositional logic Intuitionistic propositional logic Lattice Distributive lattice Priestley duality Priestley dual space
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
A clopen up-set is both clopen in the topology and upward closed in the order. The clopen up-sets of a Priestley space form a distributive lattice under intersection and union.
