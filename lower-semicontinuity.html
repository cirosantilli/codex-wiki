Lower semicontinuity

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Limit of a function Continuous function
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A function is lower semicontinuous when its strict superlevel sets are open, equivalently when its sublevel sets are closed. In Euclidean spaces this is the sequential lower semicontinuity condition f(x)≤liminfk​f(xk​) whenever xk​→x. This closedness property is essential to existence of proximal minimizers.
