Relativistic dust (Tμν=ρuμuν)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Stress-energy tensor Perfect fluid in general relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 2
Relativistic dust is a continuum of noninteracting massive particles with one local four-velocity uμ and negligible pressure. With signature (+−−−), uμuμ​=c2 and rest mass density ρ, its stress-energy tensor has the displayed form. Contracting stress-energy conservation with uν​ and using metric compatibility gives c2∇μ​(ρuμ)=0. Substituting back yields uμ∇μ​uν=0 wherever ρ>0: each particle follows a geodesic. The cosmological pressureless matter model is a homogeneous application.
