Divisor class

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Divisor on an algebraic curve Linear equivalence of divisors
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A divisor class is an equivalence class of divisors under linear equivalence. Degree descends to divisor classes because every principal divisor has degree zero.
