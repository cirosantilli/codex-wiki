Isotropic fourth-rank susceptibility (χijkl​=aδij​δkl​+bδik​δjl​+cδil​δjk​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Nonlinear optics Nonlinear optical susceptibility Nonlinear-susceptibility tensor symmetry
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
An isotropic fourth-rank polar tensor has the displayed form. If its three input slots are fully interchangeable at the same relevant frequency arguments, then a=b=c, giving χxxxx​=3χxxyy​. For mixed-sign or unequal frequencies, intrinsic permutation exchanges the arguments too; it need not make all coefficients at one fixed ordering equal.
