Coherent information upper bound by input entropy (Ic​(Λ,ρ)=S(ρ)−I(R:E)≤S(ρ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Coherent information
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
In a purified Stinespring dilation, coherent information satisfies Ic​=S(RE)−S(E)=S(ρ)−I(R:E). Nonnegativity of quantum mutual information implies Ic​≤S(ρ). Equality means that the reference and environment are uncorrelated, so the environment learns nothing about the purified input reference.
