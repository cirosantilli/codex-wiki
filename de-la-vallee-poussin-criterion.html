De la Vallée-Poussin criterion (supj​∫Ψ(∣vj​∣)<∞,Ψ(s)/s→∞)

On a finite measure space, a family is uniformly integrable exactly when it admits the displayed bound for some nonnegative increasing convex superlinear function Ψ. By the Dunford-Pettis theorem this ensures relative weak compactness in L1. This proves the criterion in both directions.
