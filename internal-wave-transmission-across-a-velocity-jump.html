Internal-wave transmission across a velocity jump

Across a horizontal material interface with continuous background mass density but a velocity jump, an internal gravity wave preserves laboratory frequency and horizontal wavenumber. The matching conditions are continuity of displacement w/ω and pressure, not continuity of w. For wave amplitudes and upward phase-line angles measured from the vertical θ1​,θ2​, define r=ω2​/ω1​. Then the transmission coefficient is
Ai​At​​=r−1cosθ2​/cosθ1​+rsinθ2​/sinθ1​2​.
(8)
The displacement condition is Ai​+Ar​=At​cosθ2​/(rcosθ1​), while pressure continuity gives Ai​−Ar​=rAt​sinθ2​/sinθ1​. Adding eliminates the reflected amplitude and gives the coefficient. It assumes nonzero intrinsic frequencies and propagating outgoing branches.
