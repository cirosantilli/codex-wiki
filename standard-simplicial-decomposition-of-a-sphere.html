Standard simplicial decomposition of a sphere (Fn=∂conv{±e1​,…,±en+1​})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Simplicial complex Regular antipodal triangulation of a sphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The boundary simplicial complex of the cross-polytope has vertices ±ei​, and its faces choose at most one vertex from each opposite pair. Radial projection identifies its geometric realization with the round sphere Sn. It is a regular antipodal triangulation of a sphere and is useful as a target for signed vertex labels: a vertex map is a simplicial map precisely when no edge receives opposite labels.
