RNA replication

 Home Chemistry Polymer Nucleic acid Ribonucleic acid
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
Copying an RNA sequence into RNA using complementary base pairing. It is distinct from transcription of a DNA template and from reverse transcription into DNA.
