Critical manifold (C0​={(x,y):F(x,y)=0})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Singular perturbation
OurBigBook.com Words: 211 Articles: 2
For a system εx˙=F(x,y), y˙​=G(x,y) with fast variable x and slow variable y, the critical manifold is the set of equilibria of the fast problem dx/ds=F(x,y) with y frozen. It is attracting where the fast Jacobian matrix has eigenvalues with negative real parts and repelling where those real parts are positive. A smooth graph can cease to be attracting at a fold point of a critical manifold.
