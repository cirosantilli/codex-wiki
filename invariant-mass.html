Invariant mass (m)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Four-momentum Energy–momentum relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 241 Articles: 4
Rest mass is the invariant mass m in the relativistic energy-momentum relation. It equals the energy measured in the particle's rest frame divided by c2.
