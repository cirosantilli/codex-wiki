Sine-Gordon theory (L=21​(∂φ)2−β2m2​(1−cosβφ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Real scalar field
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 19
A relativistic real scalar field theory with a periodic cosine potential. In physical coordinates yμ, one normalization is L=21​∂μ​φ∂μφ−m2(1−cosβφ)/β2. With ϕ=βφ and xμ=myμ, its action is S=β−2∫d2x[21​(∂ϕ)2+cosϕ−1]. Its Euler-Lagrange field equation is the Sine-Gordon equation. Distinct scalar-field vacua differ by 2π in ϕ, permitting a Sine-Gordon kink.
