Chandra X-ray Observatory

 Home Physics Experimental apparatus Scientific instrument Telescope
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
An X-ray observatory used for high-resolution imaging and spectroscopy of astronomical sources. Its observations of the Perseus cluster reveal structure in the emitting intracluster gas around radio bubbles.[ref]
