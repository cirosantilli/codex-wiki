Multisensory response enhancement

 ... Biology Neuroscience Sensory system Perception Multisensory perception Multisensory integration
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 2
A combined response exceeding a specified unisensory benchmark. For baseline-corrected mean spike counts, RAV​>max(RA​,RV​) defines enhancement over the strongest component. This differs from exceeding the sum, and depends on the response measure, analysis window and statistical comparison.
