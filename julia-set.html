Julia set (J(f))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 407 Articles: 6
The Julia set is the complement of the Fatou set. It is nonempty, closed, completely invariant, and perfect for every rational map of degree at least two.
