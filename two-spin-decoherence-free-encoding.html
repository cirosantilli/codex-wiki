Two-spin decoherence-free encoding (∣0L​⟩=∣01⟩,∣1L​⟩=∣10⟩)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum error correction Decoherence-free subspace
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
The zero-total-Z eigenspace of two spin one-half systems stores one logical qubit. Both basis states satisfy (ZI+IZ)∣ψ⟩=0, so every collective dephasing rotation acts as the identity operator on their span. Also ZZ∣01⟩=−∣01⟩ and ZZ∣10⟩=−∣10⟩, so simultaneous Pauli Z gates preserve every encoded density matrix. For independent rotations, the logical amplitudes instead acquire phases e−i(ϕ1​−ϕ2​)/2 and ei(ϕ1​−ϕ2​)/2, which generally alter their relative phase. The code is not a two-dimensional decoherence-free subspace for arbitrary collective rotations about all axes; the invariant two-spin singlet alone is only one-dimensional.
