Normal dispersion

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Geometrical optics Dispersion (optics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
In normal dispersion, the refractive index increases with frequency, dn/dω>0, equivalently dn/dλ<0 for vacuum wavelength λ. Transparent regions away from isolated absorption resonances often have this behaviour. Near a resonance the slope can reverse, producing anomalous dispersion accompanied by absorption.
