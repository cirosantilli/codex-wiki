Odd filling obstructs a spin-degenerate band insulator

 ... Quantum theory Bloch's theorem Electronic band structure Energy band Band filling Band insulator
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
In an independent-electron crystal with two spin states and no spin splitting, each completely filled energy band contains two electrons per primitive cell. An odd electron count per primitive cell therefore cannot fill an integer number of such bands. Enlarging the actual primitive cell, breaking the relevant degeneracy or including correlated-electron physics changes the premises. Even filling is necessary in this model but not sufficient: bands can overlap.
