Brownian exit-time skeleton (σak+1​=inf{t≥σak​:∣Bt​−Bσak​​∣=a})

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Brownian exit time Brownian exit from an interval
OurBigBook.com Words: 142 Articles: 2
Successive exits from intervals of radius a around the current position embed a simple symmetric random walk into Brownian motion. The Strong Markov property makes the durations independent with common law a2σ1​; the displacements divided by a are independent symmetric signs. The displacement sequence is independent across exits; no independence between a given displacement and its duration is needed for these conclusions.
