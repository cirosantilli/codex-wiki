First-order sentence

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 59 Articles: 1
A first-order sentence is a formula with no free variables, so its truth in a structure is independent of a variable assignment.
A sentence has no free variables; a first-order sentence is its first-order instance.
