Cartesian second moment of a solid ellipsoid (∫D​x2dV=4πa3bc/15)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Ellipsoid Solid ellipsoid
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For the solid ellipsoid D={x2/a2+y2/b2+z2/c2≤1} with positive semi-axes, set x=au, y=bv, z=cw. The Jacobian determinant of this inverse coordinate map is abc, and D becomes the unit ball. Symmetry gives ∫B1​​u2dV=31​∫B1​​r2dV=4π/15, proving the displayed moment. The other two moments follow by interchanging the semi-axes; mixed moments vanish by reflection symmetry.
