Spacetime

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 208 Articles: 4
Spacetime combines spatial position and time into one geometric setting for physical events. In special relativity it is modeled by Minkowski spacetime; in general relativity its geometry is dynamical.
