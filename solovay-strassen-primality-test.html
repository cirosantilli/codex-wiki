Solovay-Strassen primality test

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Euler pseudoprime Euler witness
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The Solovay-Strassen primality test samples unit bases and rejects an odd candidate as composite when it finds an Euler witness. Every prime passes every base by Euler's criterion, while an odd composite passes a random base with probability at most one half.
