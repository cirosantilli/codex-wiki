Root-moment bound for open self-avoiding walks (E(κn1/n​)≤pcn1/n​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Lattice path Self-avoiding walk
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
In independent bond percolation on a graph, let cn​ be the number of n-step self-avoiding walks from a fixed graph vertex and κn​ the number that are open. Then Eκn​=cn​pn. The Jensen inequality for the concave function t1/n gives E(κn1/n​)≤pcn1/n​. No independence between different self-avoiding walk indicators is needed. When cn1/n​→μ, the limit superior is at most pμ.
