Pathline (x˙=u(x,t))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A pathline is a fluid particle's material trajectory through a time-dependent velocity field. Unlike a streamline, which follows the velocity field at one frozen time, a pathline solves the displayed ODE. The two curves coincide in a steady flow but generally differ in an unsteady one.
