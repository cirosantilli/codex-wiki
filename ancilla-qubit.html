Ancilla qubit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Quantum ancilla
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
An ancilla qubit is a two-dimensional quantum ancilla. It is the quantum version of an ancilla bit; an auxiliary quantum register can contain several such qubits.
