Five-point Dirichlet Laplacian as a Kronecker sum

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Finite difference method Dirichlet discrete Laplacian
OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 3
If T is the one-dimensional Dirichlet second-difference matrix on m interior points, the two directional parts of the two-dimensional five-point Laplacian are
Ax​=T⊗Im​,Ay​=Im​⊗T.
(170)
They commute, and Ax​+Ay​ is the Kronecker-sum discretization of the Laplacian.
