Independent quaternion classes over rational numbers (⨁p≡3 (4)​Z/2↪Br(Q))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Central simple algebra Brauer group
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
Associate to each prime p≡3(mod4) the class of (−1,p)Q​. These classes have order two, since quaternion conjugation identifies the algebra with its opposite. The product rule [(−1,a)][(−1,b)]=[(−1,ab)] follows by factoring their tensor product into the commuting subalgebras generated by i1​,j1​j2​ and by i1​i2​,j2​; the second is the split algebra (1,b). Every nonempty finite product of distinct such primes has an odd valuation at one of them. The quaternion norm obstruction at primes congruent to three modulo four therefore makes its class nonzero, proving independence. Euclid's construction 4p1​⋯pm​−1 proves there are infinitely many such primes.
