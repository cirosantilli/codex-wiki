Supersonic flow

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Compressible flow Mach number
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
A supersonic flow has Mach number greater than one, so the flow speed exceeds the local sound speed.
For uniform gas moving at Uex​ past a resting body, even an acoustic wave directed against the fluid flow has body-frame velocity (U−c)ex​ downstream. More generally, an acoustic wave travelling along a unit vector n has body-frame velocity Uex​+cn; its x component is at least U−c>0. All such weak signals move downstream and their possible directions fit inside a Mach cone.
