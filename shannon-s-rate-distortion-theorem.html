Shannon's rate-distortion theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Rate-distortion theory Rate-distortion function
OurBigBook.com Words: 149
For a finite alphabet source with independent and identically distributed symbols, a finite reconstruction alphabet, and a bounded nonnegative separable distortion, the rate-distortion function is the asymptotic limit for fixed-length lossy codes. In particular, for a distortion target in the interior of the feasible range and every ε>0, sufficiently long block codes exist with rate at most R(D)+ε and expected distortion at most D+ε. Every such n-symbol code obeys Rn​≥R(Dn​), where Dn​ is its actual expected distortion.[ref]
The outline is instructive. The data processing inequality bounds the mutual information in a reconstruction by the information entropy of the transmitted message, which is at most log2​Mn​. Independence and the convexity of the rate-distortion function then give the per-symbol lower bound. For achievability, choose many reconstruction blocks using a suitable probability distribution, so that the source's typical blocks are likely to have an acceptable nearby reconstruction. This is a covering argument; it does not require recovering the original block exactly.
