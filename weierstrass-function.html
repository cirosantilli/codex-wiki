A standard Weierstrass function is a uniformly convergent lacunary cosine series, here parameterized by a>1 and an integer frequency multiplier b>1. Its continuity follows from the Weierstrass M-test. In the range 1<a<b, its modulus of continuity has the fractional-power scale δloga/logb for the positive odd-frequency model; the critical case a=b can have the larger scale δlog(1/δ). These examples distinguish small approximation error from ordinary Lipschitz continuity.
