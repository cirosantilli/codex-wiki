The Global Schmidt Law in Star Forming Galaxies by Robert C. Kennicutt Jr. (1998, The Astrophysical Journal 498, 541)

 Home Physics Physics bibliography
OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by Robert C. Kennicutt Jr. Words: 35
This primary observational study combines disk-averaged star-formation and gas tracers for normal spirals and infrared-selected starbursts. Its fitted power-law index is 1.4±0.15; the tracer selection, dust correction and gas conversion factors matter to interpretation.
arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9712213
doi.org/10.1086/305588
