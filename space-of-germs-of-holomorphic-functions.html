Space of germs of holomorphic functions

The space G of germs over a domain D has basic open sheets
{[f]z​:z∈U}
(88)
for holomorphic f on open U⊆D. The projection π([f]z​)=z restricts to a homeomorphism on each sheet, and these projections form holomorphic coordinate charts.
