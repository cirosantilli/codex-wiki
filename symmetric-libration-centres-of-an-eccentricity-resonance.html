Symmetric libration centres of an eccentricity resonance

 ... Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Orbital resonance Mean-motion resonance Resonant argument Resonant-argument libration
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 1
In the weak, non-crossing, leading-eccentricity planar model, exterior orders one to three have positive resonant disturbing function coefficients Bq​. Interior eccentricity-type coefficients of these orders have sign (−1)q. A local resonant Hamiltonian H=−AJ2/2−Beqcosϕ, with A>0, gives ϕ¨​=ABeqsinϕ. Linear stability analysis therefore selects ϕ=π for B>0 and ϕ=0 for B<0. Exterior symmetric centres are π; interior centres are 0,π,0 for orders one, two and three. These are conditional leading-harmonic predictions, not universal centres at arbitrary orbital eccentricity; asymmetric resonant-argument libration changes the picture.
