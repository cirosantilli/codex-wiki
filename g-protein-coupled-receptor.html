G protein-coupled receptor

 Home Biology Protein Receptor (biochemistry) Metabotropic receptor
OurBigBook.com Words: 198 Articles: 13
A G protein-coupled receptor is a cell-surface receptor that relays activation through a G protein on the intracellular side of the cell membrane. The activated G protein can regulate an enzyme or ion channel, so a GPCR supplies a concrete metabotropic receptor example: ligand recognition and the eventual response are connected through distinct components. Different G proteins couple to different targets. See Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell, 4th edition: signaling through G-protein-linked cell-surface receptors.
