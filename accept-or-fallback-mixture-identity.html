Let a discrete random variable Y have probability mass function py​. Given Y=y, return y with probability ay​∈[0,1]; otherwise draw from a conditional distribution with probability mass function K(y,x). The law of total probability gives the displayed identity. Its total mass is ∑y​py​[ay​+(1−ay​)∑x​K(y,x)]=1. If the fallback is a discrete uniform distribution on 0,…,y, then K(y,x)=1{0≤x≤y}​/(y+1), so the fallback sum includes y=x.
