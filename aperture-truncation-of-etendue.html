Aperture truncation of etendue (Gtransmitted​/Gincident​=(d/D)2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Geometrical optics Etendue
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
Insert a centred opaque circular stop of diameter d=6mm directly in the plane of the D=10mm bundle. It transmits the same 30∘ directions at every surviving position, but discards the surrounding annulus. Therefore
Gincident​Gtransmitted​​=D2d2​=0.36,Gtransmitted​=49π2​mm2sr≈22.207mm2sr.
(119)
With the uniform radiance of etendue of a circular aperture and cone, the transmitted radiant flux is 2.2207mW and the blocked fraction is 64%. Surviving light rays retain their radiance; the stop has not concentrated them. Lossless propagation after the stop preserves the etendue of this surviving subset, not that of the original incident bundle. A stop placed elsewhere can select positions and directions together, so its physical area ratio alone need not give the transmitted radiant flux fraction.
