Unsteady Boussinesq triangular-profile line-plume balances

In the Boussinesq approximation, density-weighted fluxes are Q=ρ0​bW, M=(2/3)ρ0​bW2 and F=(2/3)gbW(ρ0​−ρ). With two-sided Batchelor entrainment e=αW, the integral balances are
34​(Q2/M)t​+Qz​=3αρ0​M/Q,Qt​+Mz​=FQ/M,(FQ/M)t​+Fz​=0.
(5)
The factors 4/3 and 3 depend on the triangular profile and the definition of the entrainment coefficient.
