Impact parameter from total transit duration (b2=[(1+k)2−A2s2]/(1−s2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 90 Articles: 1
For a circular orbit, define k=Rp​/R⋆​, A=a/R⋆​ and s=sin(πT14​/P). Inverting contact geometry gives b2=[(1+k)2−A2s2]/(1−s2). The inferred value is conditional on the stellar scale, the total-duration convention and the orbital model; it must satisfy 0≤b<1+k. A non-grazing complete crossing additionally has b<1−k.
