Turbulent energy spectrum (E(k))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Homogeneous turbulence Isotropic turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The turbulent energy spectrum E(k) distributes kinetic energy over wavenumber shells, normalized by ∫0∞​E(k)dk=⟨∣u∣2⟩/2. For isotropic incompressible flow its tensor form is Φij​=E(k)(δij​−ki​kj​/k2)/(4πk2). Its low-wavenumber behavior records large-eddy momentum statistics.
