Lepton-number test of a decay channel

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Lepton number
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
Sum the lepton numbers of every final particle and compare with the initial sum. For example, e−νe​e−e+ has Le​=2, whereas e−νˉe​e−e+ has Le​=0. The two differ physically; changing a neutrino bar is not a harmless typographic simplification. Overall lepton number is conserved by the usual charged-current Standard Model amplitudes with Dirac or massless neutrinos.
