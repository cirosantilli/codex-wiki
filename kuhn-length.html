Kuhn length (bK​=2lp​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biopolymer mechanics Worm-like chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The Kuhn length is the link length of an equivalent long ideal chain with the same contour length and mean squared end-to-end distance. A three-dimensional worm-like chain satisfies ⟨R2⟩∼2lp​L for L≫lp​, hence bK​=2lp​.
