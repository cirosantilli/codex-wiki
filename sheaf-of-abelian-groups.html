Sheaf of abelian groups

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Presheaf of sets on a topological space Sheaf (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 359 Articles: 6
A sheaf of abelian groups is a sheaf of sets whose sections on each open set form an abelian group and whose restriction maps are group homomorphisms. Its stalk of a sheaf is the corresponding group of germs. Kernels are computed on sections, whereas cokernels are obtained by sheafification of the sectionwise cokernel. An exact sequence can therefore be checked on stalks.
