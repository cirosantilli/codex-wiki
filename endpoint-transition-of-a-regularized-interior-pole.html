Endpoint transition of a regularized interior pole (I∼[π/2+arctan(d/(ηL0​))]/(aηL0​))

Suppose a,b,L0​>0, L(x) is smooth and positive near zero with L(0)=L0​, and d=O(η) as η→0+. The leading contribution to
I=∫0b​(ax−d)2+η2L(x)2dx​
(36)
is obtained by scaling x=ηs and replacing L by L0​. Integration of the resulting reciprocal quadratic gives the displayed formula. For constant L the exact expression is
I=aηL0​1​[arctanηL0​ab−d​+arctanηL0​d​].
(37)
This proves the half-peak value at d=0 and the transition between an interior peak and a pole outside the interval. If L varies, smoothness and positivity on the contributing neighborhood justify its leading replacement; other zeros or singular contributions elsewhere must be excluded.
