M-G-1 queue

OurBigBook.com Words: 50
An M/G/1 queue has Poisson arrivals, independent identically distributed service times, and one server. If service has mean m and λm<1, its mean busy period is
1−λmm​.
(7)
This follows by decomposing the busy period into the first service and the busy-period descendants of arrivals during that service.
