Electroweak symmetry breaking

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Electroweak interaction Higgs field Higgs mechanism
OurBigBook.com Words: 229 Articles: 4
Electroweak symmetry breaking is the Higgs-field breaking SU(2)L​×U(1)Y​→U(1)Q​. It gives masses to the W and Z bosons while leaving the photon massless.
