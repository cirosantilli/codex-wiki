Chernoff bound

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability inequality Concentration inequality
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
For every λ>0, Markov inequality applied to eλX gives
P(X≥t)≤e−λtE[eλX].
(17)
Optimizing over λ gives the Chernoff bound in terms of the moment-generating function of X.
