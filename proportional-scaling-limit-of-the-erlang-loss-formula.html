Proportional scaling limit of the Erlang loss formula (E(Nν,NC)→max{0,1−C/ν})

Scale the offered load and capacity together. Reversing the denominator of the Erlang loss formula gives
E(Nν,NC)−1=∑j=0NC​∏ℓ=0j−1​NνNC−ℓ​.
(18)
For each fixed j, the summand tends to (C/ν)j. If ν>C, the dominated convergence theorem applies with the summable geometric series bound (C/ν)j. Otherwise each fixed finite partial sum has limiting value at least its number of terms, so the reciprocal diverges. Taking reciprocals proves the formula, including the critical case ν=C.
