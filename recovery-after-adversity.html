Recovery after adversity

 ... Science Psychology Clinical psychology Psychological resilience Measurement of psychological resilience Psychological resilience trajectory
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A return toward earlier or adequate functioning after disruption. Recovery after adversity differs from maintaining low disturbance throughout, and its evaluation depends on the baseline and observation window.
