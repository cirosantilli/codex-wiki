On a one-dimensional representation, si​ acts by a nonzero scalar εi​. Its square is one. The braid relation becomes εi2​εi+1​=εi+12​εi​, and hence εi+1​=εi​. Thus all adjacent transpositions act by one common scalar ε with ε2=1.
In characteristic different from two, ε=1 or −1. Since adjacent transpositions generate the group, these choices give exactly the trivial representation and sign representation. For n≤1 they coincide, leaving only the trivial representation.
