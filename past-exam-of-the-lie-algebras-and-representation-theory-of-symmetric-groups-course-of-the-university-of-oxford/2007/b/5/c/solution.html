For a cycle type μ=(m,ν), the Murnaghan–Nakayama rule is
χλ(m,ν)=∑λ/ρ a rim hook of size m​(−1)ht(λ/ρ)χρ(ν).
(18)
The sum is over partitions ρ whose diagrams remain after removing the specified border strip; ν is the remaining cycle type, and the recursion ends with the empty character equal to one. At the identity, χρ(1∣ρ∣)=fρ. Incompatible total sizes contribute zero.
For shape (2,2) and a transposition, a horizontal strip in the bottom row leaves (2) with positive sign; a vertical strip in the right column leaves (1,1) with negative sign. Both remaining dimensions are one. Thus
χ(2,2)(2,1,1)=1−1=0.​
(19)
