The flag makes every composition factor one-dimensional, described by a character λ:H→F. The Jordan–Hölder theorem for modules for finite-length modules says that the multiset of simple composition factors is independent of the chosen composition series.
Here is a construction valid over every F. For h∈H, every generalized eigenspace of ρ(h) is an H-submodule. Indeed ad(h) is nilpotent on H, say of exponent c, and

If v is killed by (ρ(h)−aI)N0​, take N≥N0​+c. Terms with j≥c vanish by adjoint nilpotence, and all other terms kill v. This proves invariance of the generalized eigenspace.
The flag also makes the characteristic polynomial of every ρ(h) split over F. Whenever two composition characters differ, choose an h on which they differ and split the module into the distinct generalized eigenspaces of ρ(h). Repeat within each summand until all its composition factors have the same character. Every nontrivial split lowers dimensions, so the process terminates. Finally combine summands carrying the same character. Jordan–Hölder ensures that the resulting distinct blocks have distinct factor types, giving
V=λ⨁​Wλ​,all composition factors of Wλ​ have character λ.​
(12)
This recursive method does not assume that a single element separates every character, which could fail over a finite field.
