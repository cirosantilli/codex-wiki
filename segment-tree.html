Segment tree

 Home Computer science Data structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 2
A binary tree organizing a fixed sequence into recursively subdivided contiguous intervals. Each node stores an aggregate for its interval. Range queries and point updates visit logarithmically many nodes; range-update variants store additional information such as a coverage count to maintain the length of a union of active intervals.
