Discrete integrating factor (Un−1​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 36
For xn+1​=an​xn​+bn​, put U0​=1 and Un​=∏j=1n​aj​, with aj​=0. Dividing by this accumulated homogeneous growth gives zn​=xn​/Un−1​ and zn+1​−zn​=bn​/Un​. A telescoping series then proves
xn+1​=Un​(x1​+∑j=1n​Uj​bj​​).
(14)
This is the discrete counterpart of the integrating factor for a first-order linear differential equation.
