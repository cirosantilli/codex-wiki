Kaluza-Klein compactification scale (mKK​∼R−1)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Compactification (physics) Kaluza-Klein mode Kaluza-Klein tower
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
The compactification scale is the first nonzero internal-mode mass. On a circle it equals 1/R for a higher-dimensional massless field. A zero-mode dimensional reduction applies at energies well below it. Warping, nontrivial geometry and boundary conditions can change the relation between the first mass and a geometric radius.
