Majorana zero mode (MZM)

 ... Branch of physics Quantum theory Topological quantum matter Topological superconductor Quadratic fermion Hamiltonian Majorana fermion operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 277 Articles: 7
A Majorana zero mode is a spatially localized Majorana operator that commutes with a gapped quadratic Hamiltonian, exactly or up to exponentially small finite-size corrections.
