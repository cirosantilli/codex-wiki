Symplectic form (ω)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 41 Articles: 1
A symplectic form is a closed differential form of degree two whose value on every tangent space is a nondegenerate alternating bilinear form. In canonical position-momentum coordinates it is ∑i​dqi​∧dpi​.
