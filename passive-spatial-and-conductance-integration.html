Passive spatial and conductance integration

The spatial panel uses two compartments with membrane potentials Vs​,Vd​, one representing the soma and one a dendrite. Set vs​=Vs​−EL​, vd​=Vd​−EL​, with leak reversal potential EL​=−65mV, each membrane capacitance C=100pF, each leak electrical conductance gL​=5nS and axial electrical conductance gc​=10nS. Electric charge conservation gives
Cv˙s​=−(gL​+gc​)vs​+gc​vd​+Is​,Cv˙d​=gc​vs​−(gL​+gc​)vd​+Id​.
(11)
This chosen passive model has two decay time constants, C/gL​=20ms for the equal-voltage mode and C/(gL​+2gc​)=4ms for the voltage-difference mode. Starting from rest, a 100pA step into the soma gives, with u the time since onset,
vs​(u)=10(1−e−u/20)+2(1−e−u/4)mV,
(12)
while the same step into the dendrite gives
vs​(u)=10(1−e−u/20)−2(1−e−u/4)mV.
(13)
u is expressed in ms. Their long-time rises are 12 and 8mV respectively. Applying both inputs gives 20(1−e−u/20)mV exactly: spatial attenuation coexists with linear summation of prescribed electric currents. After the steps end, both modes decay with their respective time constants. This deliberately coarse model is not a reconstructed dendrite or a fit to the experiment above.
The conductance panels use the existing one-compartment synaptic potential equation, with C=200pF, gL​=10nS, EL​=−65mV and excitatory reversal potential Ee​=0mV. For a constant excitatory synaptic conductance ge​, the steady state voltage rise and membrane time constant are
ΔV∞​=gL​+ge​ge​(Ee​−EL​)​,τeff​=gL​+ge​C​.
(14)
Each isolated 2nS input has a limiting rise 130/12≈10.833mV. Adding those isolated rises predicts 21.667mV, but opening both together gives 260/14≈18.571mV. The two conductances share a changing driving force. This is sublinear input summation even though the voltage ordinary differential equation is linear for each prescribed conductance waveform.
Add gi​=20nS with reversal potential Ei​=EL​ to the joint 4nS excitation. The limiting rise becomes 260/34≈7.647mV and the membrane time constant becomes 200/34≈5.882ms. The shunt alone leaves rest unchanged. At the joint-excitation-only steady state V≈−46.429mV, however, its inward electric current would be 20(−65+46.429)≈−371.429pA: it opposes that excursion. The finite 50ms traces in the diagram approach, but need not reach, these limiting voltages. See shunting inhibition for the constant-electric current comparison.
