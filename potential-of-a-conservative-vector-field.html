Potential of a conservative vector field (X=∇Φ)

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus Conservative vector field
OurBigBook.com Words: 126 Articles: 1
A potential for a conservative vector field X is a function with scalar values Φ whose gradient is X. Potentials differ by a constant on each connected component. The fundamental theorem for line integrals gives ∫γ​X⋅dx=Φ(γ(1))−Φ(γ(0)). In mechanics a conservative force is conventionally written −∇V, so its potential energy is V=−Φ. This spatial potential differs from the scalar potential V(ϕ) that denotes the derivative-free energy density of a scalar field.
