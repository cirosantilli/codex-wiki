Product-form stationary distribution of a multiclass queueing network (π((xj​)j​)=∏j​(1−ρj​)∏i​λj,cji​​/μj,cji​​)

Connect multiclass single-server queues by Markovian routing. Each node must either have common exponential service parameters across its classes or use a symmetric service discipline. If the traffic equations for a multiclass queueing network give loads ρj​=∑r​λjr​/μjr​<1, its equilibrium word law is the product of the individual queue laws. Queue populations are independent at a fixed equilibrium time. A proof reverses routing with pba∗​=λa​pab​/λb​, external arrivals with νa∗​=λa​pa0​, and exits with pa0∗​=νa​/λa​, and interchanges the insertion and service position rules. Weight ratios verify each reversed transition, and the traffic equations match total rates. This is a quasireversibility construction. A standard ordered-queue formulation is described in Walton's paper on multiclass queueing networks, Section 2.
