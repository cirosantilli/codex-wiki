Tricritical point

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Landau theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 504 Articles: 8
A tricritical point is where a line of continuous transitions meets a line of first-order transitions; in a Z2​ Landau expansion both quadratic and quartic coefficients vanish and a positive sextic term stabilizes the free energy.
