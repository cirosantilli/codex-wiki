Normalization of generalized Laguerre polynomials (Ln(m)​(0)=(nn+m​),∥Ln(m)​∥xme−x2​=n!(n+m)!​)

For integers m,n≥0, the standard Rodrigues' formula Ln(m)​=exx−mDn(xn+me−x)/n! has the endpoint value and squared norm displayed above. The lowest-power term after n differentiations is (n+m)!xm/m!, giving the endpoint value after division by n!xm. For the norm, integrate by parts n times against the same polynomial; its leading coefficient is (−1)n/n!, so the final derivative is (−1)n. All boundary terms vanish and the remaining Gamma function integral is (n+m)!/n!. Normalizing the value at zero to one divides the polynomial by (nn+m​) and its squared norm by the square of that factor. Only when m=0 or n=0 do these normalizations already coincide.
