Cloud

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Meteorology
OurBigBook.com Words: 433 Articles: 10
A suspension of liquid-water droplets, ice particles or both in an atmosphere. Clouds interact with solar and thermal radiation; their net radiative effect depends on height, water content, particle size distribution and coverage.
