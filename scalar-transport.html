Scalar transport

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 22
Scalar transport follows a material quantity through advection and diffusion equation terms, for example Θt​+U⋅∇Θ=κΔΘ. A passive scalar does not affect the velocity field; an active scalar feeds back through forces such as buoyancy. With incompressible flow bounded by impermeable walls and homogeneous Neumann boundary conditions, the spatial mean is conserved.
