Brownian scaling

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
For every c>0, the process (c−1/2Bct​)t≥0​ is again Brownian motion.
This is a rescaling invariance of the Brownian motion law.
