BRST symmetry

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 27
BRST symmetry is a nilpotent fermionic symmetry of a gauge-fixed action. Its differential s replaces an infinitesimal gauge parameter by the ghost field and satisfies s2=0.
