Double-root criterion for steady precession of a symmetric top

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics Symmetric top Heavy symmetric top
OurBigBook.com Words: 113
After fixing the two cyclic angular momenta of a heavy symmetric top, its inclination has an effective potential U(u) with u=cosθ. Write the reduced energy equation in the form P(u)=2A(E−U(u))(1−u2). A steady nonvertical inclination has E=U(u0​) and U′(u0​)=0, the latter coming from the reduced second-order Euler-Lagrange equation. Therefore P(u0​)=P′(u0​)=0.
This proves the double-root condition without dividing by a velocity that vanishes in the very solution under study. A simple turning point has P(u0​)=0 but nonzero derivative, and is not a steady inclination. Exactly vertical spinning corresponds to u=±1, where the inclination coordinate and its reduced denominator are singular; these endpoint configurations require a separate treatment and do not supply an interior double root.
