p-subgroup (P≤G,∣P∣=pa)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Finite p-group
OurBigBook.com Words: 53 Articles: 1
A p-subgroup is a subgroup which is a finite p-group, for a specified prime p. Every p-subgroup lies in a Sylow subgroup.
