For positive real numbers xi​ and nonnegative weights with ∑i​wi​=1, the weighted harmonic mean is
Hw​=(∑i​xi​wi​​)−1.
(31)
Equal weights give the harmonic mean, while a zero weight excludes that input. For travel at positive constant speeds vi​ over positive leg lengths di​, the weights are fractions of total distance:
wi​=∑j​dj​di​​,vavg​=∑i​di​/vi​∑i​di​​=(∑i​vi​wi​​)−1.
(32)
For 30km at 30km/h and 90km at 60km/h, the weights are 1/4,3/4, elapsed time is 5/2 hours, and the average speed is 48km/h. Weighting the speeds by their time fractions instead gives the same result as a weighted arithmetic mean: (2/5)30+(3/5)60=48. The two weight systems describe the same journey in different coordinates and must not be interchanged. [ref].
