Greenhouse gas radiative efficiency (ai​)

 ... Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Irradiated planetary atmosphere Greenhouse effect Greenhouse gas
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
The incremental radiative forcing per increment of a specified atmospheric gas burden. State whether the abundance unit is emitted mass, mole fraction or ppb, and specify the radiation-adjustment convention. For equal emitted masses in a fixed atmospheric air inventory, a per-ppb coefficient must be divided by the gas molar mass before comparing efficiencies. Spectral overlap with existing absorption makes efficiency depend on the background state.
