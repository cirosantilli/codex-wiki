Saddle-point optical transition contour (kx2​−ky2​=(ℏω−Es​)/A)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Density of states Joint density of states
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
For a local transition-energy model Ec​−Ev​=Es​+A(kx2​−ky2​) with A>0, positive detuning has two hyperbola branches opening along x. Negative detuning opens along y; the zero-detuning limiting contour is a pair of intersecting lines. The coarea integrand on one positive-detuning branch reduces to dky​/[2Aky2​+δ/A​]. The gradient vanishes at the saddle, so this is a limiting description as nonzero detuning approaches zero.
