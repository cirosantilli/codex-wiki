Coherence length from a finite spectral band (ℓc​∼[1/λmin​−1/λmax​]−1)

 ... Physical optics Optical coherence First-order optical coherence Temporal optical coherence Optical coherence time Optical coherence length
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
For vacuum wavelength endpoints λmin​<λmax​, the corresponding frequency span is Δν=c(1/λmin​−1/λmax​). The reciprocal-bandwidth estimate of optical coherence time is τc​∼1/Δν, giving ℓc​∼c/Δν. This endpoint conversion is valid even for a broad band, whereas ℓc​∼λ02​/Δλ requires a relatively narrow optical spectrum.
For constant spectral density per unit frequency in the band, the normalized first-order optical coherence is g(1)(τ)=e−i2πν0​τsin(πΔντ)/(πΔντ). Its first zero occurs at τ=1/Δν. A different spectral shape or a different contrast criterion changes the numerical optical coherence length; endpoints alone do not define an exact universal value.
