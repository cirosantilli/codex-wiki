Endpoint-false disjunctive normal form

 ... Polynomial-time many-one reduction NP-hardness NP-completeness Boolean satisfiability problem Boolean formula Disjunctive normal form
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A propositional formula false on the all-true and all-false Boolean valuations has a disjunctive normal form in which every minterm contains a positive literal and a negative literal. Grouping the positive propositional variables into C and the negated propositional variables into the negation of a logical disjunction D writes each minterm as ¬(C→D). De Morgan's laws therefore give the negation of a logical conjunction of such logical implications. For a contradiction use ¬(p→p).
