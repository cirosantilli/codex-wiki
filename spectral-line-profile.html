Spectral line profile (φ(ν))

 Home Physics Atomic physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 182 Articles: 3
A normalized spectral line profile distributes a transition's emission or absorption over frequency, with ∫φ(ν)dν=1 and units Hz−1. Thermal motion, finite excited-state lifetime and collisions can give different contributions to the shape. Normalization lets an optically thin integrated line measure the relevant column density without requiring the detailed profile.
