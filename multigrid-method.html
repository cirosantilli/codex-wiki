Multigrid method

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 435 Articles: 6
A multigrid method combines relaxation of high-frequency fine-grid errors with coarse-grid correction of smooth errors. Residual restriction, an approximate coarse error solve, error prolongation and post-smoothing form a two-grid cycle; recursive coarse solves give multilevel cycles.
