Disease-ascertained transmission probability (P(g∣gf​,gm​,D)=∑h​qh​Rh​qg​Rg​​)

 Home Biology Genetics Genetic association Genotype relative risk
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
Let qg​=P(g∣gf​,gm​) be the Mendelian segregation probability, and assume the child's disease status depends on the parental genotypes only through its own genotype, with penetrance proportional to Rg​. Bayes' theorem gives the displayed conditional law. For a diallelic parent pair there are four equally likely labelled transmission outcomes. If an unordered genotype occurs in mg​ of them, qg​=mg​/4, so its probability is mg​Rg​/∑h​mh​Rh​. Dropping multiplicities would, for example, misweight a heterozygous child of two heterozygotes.
