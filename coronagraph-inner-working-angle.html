Coronagraph inner working angle (θIWA​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Coronagraph
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
The coronagraph inner working angle describes how close to the blocked source a specified coronagraph can usefully transmit or detect a faint neighbor. It requires a criterion, such as a chosen fraction of the far off-axis coronagraph throughput, and is often expressed in units of λ/D. It is not automatically the coronagraphic focal plane mask radius or a guarantee of detection. Small separations make useful source transmission and accurate control of wavefront errors particularly demanding. [ref].
