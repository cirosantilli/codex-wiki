Meromorphic function

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Isolated singularity Classification of isolated singularities Pole
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 3
A meromorphic function is holomorphic except at isolated poles. Equivalently, it is locally a quotient of two holomorphic functions whose denominator is not identically zero.
