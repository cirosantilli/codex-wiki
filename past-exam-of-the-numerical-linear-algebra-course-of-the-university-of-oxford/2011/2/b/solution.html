The induced matrix norm associated with the given energy norm is a supremum of length ratios. The invertible change of variables y=A1/2x gives
∥B∥A​=supx=0​∥x∥A​∥Bx∥A​​=supy=0​∥y∥2​∥A1/2BA−1/2y∥2​​=∥A1/2BA−1/2∥2​​.
(12)
The last expression is the Euclidean operator norm. This is the matrix norm induced by an energy norm: transforming to coordinates with Euclidean lengths transforms the operator by the corresponding matrix similarity.
