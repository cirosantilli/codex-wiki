Multiplying the Arnoldi decomposition by QkT​ shows that the leading square block is
Hk​=QkT​AQk​.
(47)
It is an upper Hessenberg matrix by the Arnoldi iteration. Because A=AT, it is also a symmetric matrix. Entries below the first subdiagonal vanish by the Hessenberg structure, and their reflected entries above the first superdiagonal vanish by symmetry. Consequently
Tk​=QkT​AQk​ is symmetric tridiagonal​,AQk​=Qk+1​Tk​​.
(48)
The extra last row has only its final entry possibly nonzero. With the usual choice of positive normalization factors, the individual column relations are the three-term Lanczos algorithm recurrence
Aqj​=βj−1​qj−1​+αj​qj​+βj​qj+1​,αj​=qjT​Aqj​,βj​>0,
(49)
where the first term is omitted for j=1. This reduction is an exact-arithmetic statement; round-off error can affect the orthogonality in an implementation.
