The thin QR decomposition gives AX=QR. Its columns already belong to the column space of Q, so the orthogonal projection QQT leaves them fixed:
AX=QQTAX=QQTQR=QR=AX​.
(28)
This identity uses only AX=QR and QTQ=Ir​; it remains valid if R is singular, provided such a factorization with orthonormal Q is used.
