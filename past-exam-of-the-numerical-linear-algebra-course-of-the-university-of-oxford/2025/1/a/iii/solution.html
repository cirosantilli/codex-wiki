The columns of A1​V are mutually orthogonal, since (A1​vi​)T(A1​vj​)=λj​δij​. For each λj​>0, set u1j​=A1​vj​/λj​​. These columns are orthonormal. At a zero λj​, one has A1​vj​=0; choose u1j​ by completing the already prescribed columns to an orthonormal set of n columns in Rk. Such a completion exists because k≥n. This gives A1​V=U1​Σ1​, including the zero columns.
Likewise, the Gram matrix of A2​V is diag(1−λj​). Normalize its nonzero columns and complete at its zero columns in Rm−k, using m−k≥n. The resulting U2​ has orthonormal columns and A2​V=U2​Σ2​. Combining the identities gives
A=(U1​0​0U2​​)(Σ1​Σ2​​)VT​.
(8)
This is a form of the cosine-sine decomposition. Completing the zero columns is essential: neither block was assumed to have full column rank.
