Depletion approximation

 Home Physics Semiconductor P-n junction Depletion region
OurBigBook.com Words: 245 Articles: 2
The depletion approximation replaces the junction's transition region by a region without mobile carriers, leaving only fixed ionized dopants. For a uniformly doped abrupt p-n junction, ρ=−eNA​ on the p side, ρ=+eND​ on the n side and ρ≃0 outside. Charge neutrality gives NA​xp​=ND​xn​. Integrating Poisson equation gives the electric field and the electrostatic band bending.
