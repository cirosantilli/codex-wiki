Vapor pressure (psat​(T))

 ... Statistical physics Thermodynamics Thermodynamic potential Thermodynamic free energy Gibbs free energy Phase coexistence curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The equilibrium pressure of a vapor in contact with its liquid or solid at a specified temperature. For pure liquid–gas phase coexistence with negligible interface effects, it is the pressure on their phase coexistence curve. In a gas mixture, distinguish the component's partial pressure from the total pressure; the pure-substance Integrated Clausius-Clapeyron relation does not describe the total pressure of arbitrary humid air.
