P-value

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical hypothesis test
OurBigBook.com Words: 48 Articles: 1
A p-value is the probability, computed under a null hypothesis, of obtaining a test statistic at least as unfavorable to that hypothesis as the observed value.
