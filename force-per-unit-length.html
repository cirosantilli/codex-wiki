Force per unit length (f=dF/dℓ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Force
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
A force per unit length describes a distributed force on a filament and has units Nm−1. For a charged filament with linear charge density λ and longitudinal current I, the external Lorentz force is f=λE+It×B. Length, density and force must all refer to the same measurement frame. An infinite uniform filament generally has a finite force density but no finite total force.
