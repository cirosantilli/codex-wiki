Dynamic electric polarizability (dω​=α(ω)Eω​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrostatics Electric dipole moment Electric polarizability
OurBigBook.com Words: 203 Articles: 1
The dynamic electric polarizability relates the induced electric dipole moment at a driving frequency to the applied electric field at that frequency in linear response theory. A complex coefficient can encode both a dispersive phase and absorption. For a charged one-dimensional quantum harmonic oscillator with charge magnitude e, mass m, and natural frequency ω0​, the undamped response is
α(ω)=m(ω02​−ω2)e2​.
(28)
One derivation uses the oscillator electric-dipole selection rule and first-order time-dependent perturbation theory. The same result follows from the classical Lorentz oscillator model: mz¨+mω02​z=−eE and d=−ez imply d¨+ω02​d=e2E/m. At exact resonance there is no bounded periodic response without damping; a finite switch-on produces secular growth rather than a finite steady-state value of the pole expression.
