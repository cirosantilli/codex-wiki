Modulus of continuity (ω(f,δ))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Topological analysis Metric space Uniform continuity
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
The modulus of continuity of a function f is
ω(f,δ)=sup{∣f(x)−f(y)∣:d(x,y)≤δ}.
(9)
It quantifies uniform continuity by recording the largest oscillation of f across pairs of points at distance at most δ.
