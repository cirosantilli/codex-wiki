Phosphoenolpyruvate (PEP)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Organic acid Carboxylic acid Phosphoenolpyruvic acid
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
Phosphoenolpyruvate supplies a phosphate group to ADP in the final glycolysis step catalyzed by pyruvate kinase. The free ionic form used in the worked balance has charge −3.
