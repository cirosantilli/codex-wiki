Instrumental variable (Z)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Causal inference
OurBigBook.com Words: 508 Articles: 16
An instrumental variable changes an exposure or treatment but affects the outcome only through that exposure. Together, instrument relevance, instrumental-variable independence, and the exclusion restriction identify causal effects under suitable structural assumptions.
