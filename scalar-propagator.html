Scalar propagator (ΔE​(p))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Real scalar field
OurBigBook.com Words: 353 Articles: 5
For a free massive scalar in a Euclidean path integral, inversion of the quadratic kernel gives ΔE​(p)=1/(p2+m2). The Minkowski version follows by Wick rotation.
