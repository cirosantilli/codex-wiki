For source-free Keplerian viscous diffusion equation, the variables y=r​ and g=r3/2Σ give gt​=(3ν0​/4)gyy​ when νˉ=ν0​r. Rescaling y gives the displayed heat equation. Initial and radial boundary conditions must also be transformed; the reduction does not specify them.
