Lorentzian quasiparticle spectral peak (A(k,ω)=Γk​/[π((ω−Ωk​)2+Γk2​)])

OurBigBook.com Words: 94
A normalized retarded amplitude GR(k,t)=−iΘ(t)e−iΩk​t−Γk​t, with constant Γk​>0, has Fourier transform GR(k,ω)=(ω−Ωk​+iΓk​)−1. Consequently A=−ImGR/π is the displayed positive Lorentzian line shape, of unit frequency integral. Its maximum is at Ωk​ and its half width at half maximum is Γk​. The energy half-width is ℏΓk​. Amplitude decays on time 1/Γk​ whereas probability decays on time 1/(2Γk​). A general interacting electron has quasiparticle residue Z and incoherent spectral weight in addition to this normalized local pole model; frequency-dependent width or self-energy can invalidate a global Lorentzian.
