Proximality

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Topological dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 246 Articles: 2
In a compact metric space with a continuous map T, points x,y are proximal if
infn≥0​d(Tnx,Tny)=0.
(1)
This property is independent of the compatible metric. If T is injective and x=y, arbitrarily small proximal distances must occur at arbitrarily large times, since each finite collection of distances is strictly positive. Proximal points need not be equal or have dense orbits.
