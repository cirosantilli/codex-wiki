Autonomic emotion classification

 Home Science Psychology Psychophysiology
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 1
Statistical classification of emotion categories using measurements of the autonomic nervous system. It requires an independently justified target label, appropriate cross-validation, and evaluation in the population and context where the classifier will be used. Predictive success does not imply that every instance of a category has one invariant physiological pattern.
