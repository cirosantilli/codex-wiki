Hydrostatic equilibrium in optical depth

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure Stellar surface boundary condition Photosphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 299 Articles: 5
In a plane-parallel atmosphere with dτ=−κρdr and dP/dr=−ρg, one has dP/dτ=g/κ. If gravity and opacity vary slowly, the pressure at optical depth τ is approximately P≃gτ/κ.
