Antipodal alternating-simplex parity lemma (pk​≡1(mod2))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Simplicial complex Simplicial map Positive alternating simplex
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
An antipodal simplicial map from a regular antipodal triangulation of a sphere Sk to Fn has an odd number of positive alternating k-simplices. For each k-simplex, the number of positive alternating facets is odd exactly when the simplex is positive or negative alternating; otherwise it is even, including collapsed images. Count these incidences in one hemisphere: interior facets contribute twice, boundary facets once. Antipodal pairing then gives pk​≡pk−1​, starting with one positive vertex on S0. In particular, no such map exists when k>n.
