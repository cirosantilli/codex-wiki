Holomorphic functional calculus

For x in a unital Banach algebra and f holomorphic near σ(x), the holomorphic functional calculus defines
f(x)=2πi1​∫Γ​f(z)(z1−x)−1dz,

where Γ winds once around the spectrum. It is a continuous unital algebra homomorphism and satisfies the spectral mapping theorem σ(f(x))=f(σ(x)).
