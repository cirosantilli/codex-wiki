Rabi detuning (δ=ω0​−ω)

 ... Quantum state Observable Hamiltonian (quantum mechanics) Unitary time evolution Rabi cycle Driven two-level Rabi model
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The Rabi detuning is the unperturbed transition angular frequency minus the drive angular frequency in this convention. Its sign controls the longitudinal rotation of the Bloch vector; the single-pulse population formula is even in δ. Reversing the definition of Rabi detuning requires reversing its sign in the rotating-frame Hamiltonian operator as well.
