Running coupling

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Renormalization Renormalization scale
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 24
A running coupling is a renormalized coupling regarded as a function of the renormalization scale. Its scale derivative is its beta function.
