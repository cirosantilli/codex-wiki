Official publication: link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-014-2730-9
Section 3 gives the Standard Model branching rules of the SU(6) representations 6 and 15. Their cubic gauge anomaly coefficients are −1 and 2, respectively; one 15 with two 6 therefore cancels this gauge anomaly. Additional charged fermions can form a vectorlike gauge spectrum with invariant mass terms.
