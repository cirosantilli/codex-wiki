Incremental elastic moduli (cαiβj​=W,Aiα​Ajβ​​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Finite elasticity Hyperelastic material
OurBigBook.com Words: 211 Articles: 3
For unconstrained hyperelastic material, the Hessian of the strain energy density gives δNαi​=cαiβj​δAjβ​. Commuting the second derivatives gives major symmetry cαiβj​=cβjαi​. These material-coordinate moduli linearize about a finite base deformation; they need not have the minor symmetries of a small-strain elastic stiffness tensor. Material constraints require their additional reaction increments and admissibility equations.
