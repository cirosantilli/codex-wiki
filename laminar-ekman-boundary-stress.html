Laminar Ekman boundary stress (Tb​=ρfν/2​(1+i)Wg​)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Ocean circulation Wind stress Ekman layer
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
For f>0, a stationary lower no-slip boundary condition below uniform exterior geostrophic flow Wg​ produces WE​=−Wg​e−(1+i)z/δE​. The upward shear component at the bottom is Tb​=ρνWE′​(0)=ρfν/2​(1+i)Wg​; the bottom traction on the fluid is −Tb​. This law cannot be assigned to an arbitrary prescribed surface wind stress without an additional boundary condition.
