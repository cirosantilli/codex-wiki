Coordinate seminorms (px​(x,y)=∣x∣,py​(x,y)=∣y∣)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Topological vector space Locally convex space Seminorm
OurBigBook.com Words: 129
On R2, the coordinate seminorms px​ and py​ measure horizontal and vertical components separately. Their kernels of seminorms are the vertical and horizontal axes. Neither is a norm, but together they form a separating family of seminorms. A basic zero neighbourhood is
{px​<a}∩{py​<b}=(−a,a)×(−b,b)(a,b>0).
(7)
Each condition alone is an unbounded strip; their intersection is an open rectangle. For a=b=1, this is the unit open ball of the maximum norm ∥(x,y)∥∞​=max{∣x∣,∣y∣}. For unequal tolerances it is the unit open ball of the weighted norm max{∣x∣/a,∣y∣/b}. Thus the two coordinate measurements generate the usual topology of the plane.
For a numerical check, take a=1, b=1/2: (0.8,0.4) passes both tests, (0.8,0.7) passes only the horizontal test, and (1.2,0.4) passes only the vertical test. A direction missed by one seminorm is detected by the other.
