Fractional distillation

 Home Chemistry Distillation
OurBigBook.com Words: 237 Articles: 3
Fractional distillation brings rising vapor into contact with descending liquid in a fractionating column. Packing supplies contact area; it does not act as a sieve for individual molecules. Repeated exchange can enrich the rising vapor in the more volatile component. Some condensed liquid returns downward as reflux, rather than all being withdrawn as distillate.[ref]
An equilibrium stage is an idealization of this contact. A real fractionating column has finite contact time and imperfect wetting and mixing; its length is not automatically a count of equilibrium stages. Product withdrawal and reflux affect the separation together. Thus the repeated ideal-stage enrichment example illustrates the composition advantage of repeated equilibration, but supplies neither a column design nor a specified reflux requirement.[ref]
