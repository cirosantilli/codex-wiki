Fermionic creation operator

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Mode expansion of a Dirac field
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
A fermionic creation operator is the adjoint of a fermionic annihilation operator and adds one fermion to a mode. Its square vanishes, implementing the Pauli exclusion principle.
