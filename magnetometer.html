Magnetometer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 34 Articles: 1
A magnetometer measures a magnetic field or a specimen's magnetic dipole moment. A vibrating-sample magnetometer determines the latter from the electromotive force induced in pickup coils by specimen motion.
