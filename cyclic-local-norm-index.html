Cyclic local norm index ([K×:NL/K​L×]=[L:K])

 ... Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Maximal abelian extension Local class field theory Local Artin reciprocity Norm subgroup of a local field extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For a cyclic extension of p-adic fields, the Herbrand quotient of the local multiplicative group together with Hilbert theorem 90 gives this index. If the ramification index and residue degree are e,f, the norm valuation formula vK​(Nx)=fvL​(x) then gives the unit norm index e. In particular all units are norms in an unramified extension.
