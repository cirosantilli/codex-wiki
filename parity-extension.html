Parity extension

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Coding theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
Appending a parity bit makes every word even-weight. A binary [n,m,d] code becomes an [n+1,m,d+] code, where
d+={d,d+1,​d even,d odd.​
