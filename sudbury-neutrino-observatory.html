The SNO Collaboration used 1000 tonnes of heavy water in a 12m acrylic sphere, surrounded by ordinary water shielding and 9456 photomultipliers. Weak charged current (CC) breakup of a deuteron selects electron neutrinos; weak neutral current (NC) breakup measures all active neutrino flavors; electron elastic scattering (ES) has reduced non-electron sensitivity.[ref]
