Massless inextensible string

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Force Tension
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
An ideal string of negligible mass and fixed length, transmitting tension when taut. In an ideal guide with no friction, the tension magnitude is the same on both sides: otherwise the force balance on an arbitrarily short massless segment would fail. Its fixed length gives a mechanical constraint relating the endpoint velocities. The two endpoint tension powers sum to zero when the guide is stationary; the string can transfer energy between connected particles without adding net energy.
