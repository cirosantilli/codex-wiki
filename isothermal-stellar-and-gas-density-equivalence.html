Isothermal stellar and gas density equivalence (σ2ρ′=−ρΦ′,σ2=kB​T/m0​)

 ... Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics Collisionless Boltzmann equation Jeans equation Spherical Jeans equation Isothermal collisionless tracer
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
Without an escape-energy cutoff, a galactic distribution function proportional to exp[(Ψ−v2/2)/σ2] gives ρ∝eΨ/σ2 and a constant one-dimensional velocity dispersion. Since Ψ=Φ0​−Φ, differentiation gives the displayed hydrostatic relation. An ideal gas at constant temperature has pressure P=ρkB​T/m0​, so its hydrostatic equilibrium equation is identical at the displayed dispersion-temperature identification. Coupling either relation to the same Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity, with matched central density and boundary data, therefore gives the same density structure. Truncating the stellar velocities at escape changes this equivalence.
