Kernel in a category (kerf)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Pointed category
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
The kernel of f:A→B is the universal morphism k:K→A satisfying fk=0. It is the equalizer of f and the zero morphism.
