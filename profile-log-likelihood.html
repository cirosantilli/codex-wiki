Profile log-likelihood (ℓp​(ψ)=ℓ(ψ,λψ​))

 ... Statistical model Statistical modelling Maximum likelihood estimation Maximum-likelihood estimator Likelihood function Profile likelihood
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
The logarithm of the profile likelihood is the full log-likelihood evaluated at the nuisance fit constrained to the specified parameter of interest. It preserves likelihood ratios and maximizers, but is not generally a normalized sampling log-density. Conditioning and integrating out nuisance parameters are different operations.
