Transpose (AT)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 231 Articles: 4
The matrix transpose interchanges rows and columns: (AT)ij​=Aji​. On a fixed matrix space, A↦AT is the transposition map, a linear map that preserves the trace norm but is not completely positive in dimensions greater than one.
