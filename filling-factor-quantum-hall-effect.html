Filling factor (quantum Hall effect, νf​=ns​h/(eB))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Landau level
OurBigBook.com Words: 131 Articles: 1
For a two-dimensional electron gas in a perpendicular magnetic field of magnitude B>0, the filling factor is the electron number per unit area ns​ divided by the orbital degeneracy per unit area eB/h of one spin branch of a Landau level. Here e is the positive elementary charge and h the Planck constant. The nominal filling factor varies continuously with ns​/B; it need not be an integer.
