Electron density (n(r))

 Home Chemistry Quantum chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The electron density describes the expected number of electrons per unit volume at position r. For a system with a fixed number N of electrons, ∫n(r)d3r=N. A Lewis structure allocates electron counts without specifying this spatial density.
