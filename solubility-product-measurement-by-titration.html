Solubility product measurement by titration

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Precipitation (chemistry) Solubility product
OurBigBook.com Words: 351
How can an invisible dissolved inventory be measured? The unsigned laboratory handout Solubility Product Constant, Ksp, for Calcium Hydroxide by Acid-Base Titration, Procedure, starts with saturated calcium hydroxide, supplied by the teacher or prepared from calcium and water. Its apparatus includes filter paper, a funnel, stoppered flasks, a volumetric pipette, a volumetric flask and a burette. Two successive rounds of filtration remove particles. The filtrate is stoppered to reduce carbon dioxide exposure. A 10.00mL aliquot is diluted with deionized water and titrated with standardized hydrochloric acid; bromothymol blue gives a pale-green titration endpoint. The readout is the difference between final and initial burette readings; four consistent trials are required.[ref]
Unlike AB, calcium hydroxide has one-to-two dissolution stoichiometry:
Ca(OH)2​(s)⇌Ca2+(aq)+2OH−(aq),Ksp,c​=[Ca2+][OH−]2.
(14)
For molar solubility s in the ideal model with no background calcium or hydroxide, no additional chemical reactions, and negligible self-ionization of water, [Ca2+]=s and [OH−]=2s. Hence Ksp,c​=4s3, with units M3; it is not the AB constant used in the figures. The neutralization chemical equation is H++OH−⟶H2​O. Let CH​ be the known titrant molar concentration, v its delivered volume at the equivalence point, and Va​ the original aliquot volume. Then the inventory calculation is
h=[OH−]=Va​CH​v​,s=2h​,Ksp,c​=2h3​.
(15)
Dilution of the aliquot changes its concentration during the acid-base titration, but preserves its initial amount of substance of hydroxide. Therefore Va​, rather than the diluted flask volume, belongs in the denominator.
The handout identifies two biases: carried-over calcium hydroxide solid dissolves during acid-base titration, consuming extra titrant; carbon dioxide consumes hydroxide and forms calcium carbonate. Filtration and stoppering address these effects, while repeat trials test consistency. Its review questions also ask about dissolved ion pairs, which filtration cannot remove.[ref]
Further controls would compare a water blank, independently verify titrant standardization, and record the saturation temperature; these are proposed checks, not reported measurements. A titration endpoint estimates the equivalence point and may introduce a systematic offset. Even perfect inventory measurement does not determine activity coefficients. Recovering the dimensionless thermodynamic solubility product additionally requires
Ksp​=γCa​γOH2​(c∘)3Ksp,c​​.
(16)
This explains both the power and the limit of the experiment: acid-base titration measures a dissolved inventory, while the link from that inventory to a thermodynamic equilibrium constant still depends on a chemical model.
