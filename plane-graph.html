Plane graph

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Planar graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
A plane graph is a planar graph together with a specified crossing-free embedding in the plane. Its faces are the connected complementary regions, including the unbounded region. Face counts and facial boundary walks refer to this embedding. For a finite connected plane graph, the Euler formula for a connected planar graph is v−e+f=2: deleting a cycle edge lowers both edge and face counts by one until a tree remains, where e=v−1 and f=1. The distinction between an abstract planar graph and its chosen embedding matters when discussing which edges bound a face.
