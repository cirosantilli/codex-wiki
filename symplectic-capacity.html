Symplectic capacity (c(X,ω))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 292 Articles: 4
A symplectic capacity is monotone under symplectic embeddings, scales by c(X,aω)=ac(X,ω) for a>0, and has positive unit-ball capacity and finite unit-cylinder capacity. It is invariant under symplectomorphisms. A normalized symplectic capacity additionally assigns π to both unit domains.
