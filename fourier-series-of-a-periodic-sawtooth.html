The Fourier series converges to the ramp away from its jumps and to zero at every jump, the average of the one-sided limits. For K=0, Termwise differentiation of a Fourier series fails here as an ordinary convergent series: the differentiated summands have nondecaying amplitude 2K/L. The classical derivative equals K/L between jumps, whereas the periodic distributional derivative includes negative point masses of weight K at the jumps.
