Hermitian metric on a smooth complex vector bundle (hb​:Eb​×Eb​→C)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Complex vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
This is a smoothly varying positive-definite Hermitian form on each fiber, without requiring a holomorphic base or bundle. A partition of unity glues local positive metrics into a global one. Applying smooth Gram-Schmidt to local frames then makes the transitions unitary, reducing the structure group of a vector bundle to U(k). For a line bundle its unit vectors form a principal bundle for the circle group.
