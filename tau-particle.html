Tau (particle, τ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Lepton
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 12
The tau is the charged lepton of the third fermion generation. In the massless approximation its charged-current W boson partial decay width equals those of the electron and muon, reflecting lepton universality.
The tau lepton has electric charge −1 in units of the positive elementary electric charge; τ+ is its antiparticle. Its weak charged current decays make the tau lepton useful for testing lepton universality. They also illustrate how particle detection can establish a short-lived parent through visible daughters and a consistent invisible recoil.
