Coding sequence

 Home Biology Genetics Gene
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A coding sequence is the part of a nucleotide sequence that specifies a polypeptide through the genetic code. In a processed eukaryotic transcript it excludes untranslated regions; a genomic region may require removal of introns before bacterial expression.
