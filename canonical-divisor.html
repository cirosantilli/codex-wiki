Canonical divisor (KX​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Divisor on an algebraic curve Riemann-Roch theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 615 Articles: 16
A canonical divisor is the divisor of any nonzero rational differential. Its divisor class is independent of the differential and has degree 2g−2.
