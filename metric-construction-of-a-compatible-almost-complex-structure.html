Metric construction of a compatible almost complex structure

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Compatible almost complex structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
Choose an auxiliary Riemannian metric h and define the skew-adjoint bundle map A by ω(u,v)=h(Au,v). Then
J=A(−A2)−1/2
(2)
is a compatible almost complex structure. The positive square root is defined fiberwise by the spectral theorem for normal operators, and its smooth dependence on A makes J smooth.
