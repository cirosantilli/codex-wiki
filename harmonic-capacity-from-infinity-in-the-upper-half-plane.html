Harmonic capacity from infinity in the upper half-plane (cap(K)=limy→∞​πyPiy​(BT(H∖K)​∈K))

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Planar Brownian motion Harmonic measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 242 Articles: 3
This capacity measures the asymptotic probability that planar Brownian motion hits a compact H-hull before the real axis. It scales linearly with length and is distinct from half-plane capacity. A vertical slit of height h has capacity 2h; a half-disc of radius r has capacity 4r. Monotonicity of Brownian hitting probabilities gives cap(K)≤4rad(K), where the radius is that of the least real-centred enclosing half-disc. For finite slit hulls the capacity is the length of the image of the two-sided absorbing boundary under the mapping-out function.
