Coherent-field condensate variational state (∣ψ⟩=e−Nˉ/2exp(∫ψΨ†)∣0⟩)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Coherent state
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
A normalized bosonic field coherent state with Nˉ=∫∣ψ∣2 has ⟨Ψ(r)⟩=ψ(r). For a normal-ordered contact-interacting Hamiltonian, its energy expectation is ∫[ℏ2∣∇ψ∣2/(2m)+V∣ψ∣2+g∣ψ∣4/2]d3r. Minimizing at fixed mean particle number gives the stationary Gross–Pitaevskii equation. This mean-field state has number fluctuations and does not include the pair correlations responsible for quantum depletion.
