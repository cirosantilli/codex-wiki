Anxiety disorder

 Home Biology Medicine Psychiatry Mental disorder
OurBigBook.com Words: 496 Articles: 14
A diagnostic grouping characterized by persistent or excessive fear or anxiety and associated impairment. Specific disorders differ in their feared outcomes and behavioral patterns, so a shared maintaining process is a hypothesis to assess rather than a universal explanation.
