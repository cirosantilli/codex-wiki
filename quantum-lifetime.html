Quantum lifetime (τq​)

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Electron Electron scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The quantum lifetime determines the broadening of a single-particle spectral level by scattering. It need not equal the momentum relaxation time: small-angle scattering broadens levels while relaxing current weakly. A common Lorentzian broadening convention is a half-width Γ=ℏ/(2τq​).
