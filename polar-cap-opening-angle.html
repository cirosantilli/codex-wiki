Polar-cap opening angle (θ∗​)

 ... Stellar astrophysics Stellar evolution Neutron star Pulsar Pulsar magnetosphere Pulsar polar cap
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
For an aligned dipole whose last relevant field line reaches equatorial radius Re​, the polar-cap opening angle obeys sin2θ∗​=R∗​/Re​. It is approximately R∗​/Re​​ for a small cap.
