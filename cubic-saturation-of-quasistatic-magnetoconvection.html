Cubic saturation of quasistatic magnetoconvection (a2=s2+Qπ28(R−Rc​)​+O((R−Rc​)2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 88
The primary roll and temperature modes force only a horizontally uniform temperature mode proportional to sin(2πz) at quadratic order. Its amplitude is c=−αab/(8π). Its feedback on the primary temperature mode saturates magnetoconvection, giving the displayed positive squared amplitude above threshold. Thus a simple roll onset is a supercritical pitchfork bifurcation for every Q≥0. The three-mode Fourier truncation and full perturbation calculation agree through the cubic solvability condition because all forced quadratic modes are retained; this is not an assertion that the truncation gives an exact finite-amplitude PDE solution.
