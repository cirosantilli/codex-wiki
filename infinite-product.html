Infinite product (∏n=1∞​an​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Series (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 335 Articles: 5
An infinite product is the limit of the finite partial products ∏n=1N​an​. If an​=1+un​, every factor is nonzero, and ∑n​∣un​∣<∞, then the product converges to a nonzero value.
