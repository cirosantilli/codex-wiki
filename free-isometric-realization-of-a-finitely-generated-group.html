Free isometric realization of a finitely generated group (T≅Fr​/kerπ)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Covering space Deck transformation Deck transformation group
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
Every finitely generated group acts freely and properly discontinuously by Riemannian isometries on a connected manifold in any dimension d≥3. Choose a surjection Fr​→T and the regular covering of #r​(S1×Sd−1) associated with its kernel. Pulling back a metric makes the deck transformation group isometric. Uniqueness of lifts makes its action free. The cover can be noncompact and need not be simply connected; no claim about realizing every group as a closed three-manifold fundamental group is involved.
