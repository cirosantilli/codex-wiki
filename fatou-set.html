Fatou set (F(f))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 625 Articles: 10
The Fatou set of a rational map is the largest open subset of the Riemann sphere on which its iterates form a normal family. Its complement is the Julia set.
