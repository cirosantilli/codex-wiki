Semidirect product of a Lie algebra and a module (g⋉V)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 54 Articles: 1
For a Lie algebra representation of g on V, the semidirect product g⋉V is the vector space g⊕V with bracket
[(x,v),(y,w)]=([x,y],xw−yv).
(11)
The subspace V is an abelian ideal of a Lie algebra.
