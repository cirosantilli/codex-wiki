Magnetic phase mixing under differential rotation (exp[−ηm2f′(r)2t3/3])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Magnetic diffusion
OurBigBook.com Words: 155 Articles: 1
A nonuniform angular velocity f(r) winds an azimuthal magnetic mode into phase m(ϕ−f(r)t). Its radial phase gradient grows as −mtf′(r), creating small scales even when its ideal amplitude does not decay. The corresponding leading resistive damping rate is ηm2t2f′(r)2, so a local short-wavelength estimate gives damping factor exp[−ηm2f′(r)2t3/3]. Hence magnetic diffusion can become important at time [ηm2f′2]−1/3 although it is initially small. This is a local phase-mixing estimate, not a globally exact solution in a finite radial domain: boundary forcing, field stretching and spatial variation of f′ must also be retained there.
