Network modularity (Q=(2m)−1∑ij​(Aij​−ki​kj​/(2m))1{ci​=cj​})

Network modularity measures excess within-community edge weight relative to a network null model based on degrees of vertices. For an undirected unweighted graph, its standard form is Q=(2m)−1∑ij​(Aij​−ki​kj​/(2m))1{ci​=cj​}. A single community containing every vertex has Q=0; a partition with more internal connectivity than expected can have positive Q.
