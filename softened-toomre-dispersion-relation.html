Softened Toomre dispersion relation (ω2=κ2−2πGΣ0​ke−kϵ+cs2​k2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Gravitational instability of an astrophysical disk Toomre's stability criterion
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 2
Linear axisymmetric compressive modes of a barotropic closure of a razor-thin disk combine radial epicyclic restoration, softened self-gravity and pressure. The off-plane razor-thin Poisson kernel supplies the exponential gravity reduction. Here k is the nonnegative radial wavenumber magnitude. The full linear system also has an axisymmetric geostrophic mode.
