Cyclotomic Zp-extension

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Iwasawa theory Zp-extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For odd p, remove the finite torsion subgroup μp−1​ from the Galois group of Q(μp∞​)/Q by taking its fixed field. For p=2, take the fixed field of {±1} instead. The remaining Galois group is Zp​, using the p-adic logarithm on 1+pZp​ or 1+4Z2​. For a number field K, the cyclotomic Zp-extension is the compositum with this rational tower.
