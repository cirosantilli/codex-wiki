For independent errors of common unknown variance in Yi​=βxi​+εi​, the ordinary least squares estimate is β​=∑i​xi​Yi​/∑i​xi2​. Fitting one parameter leaves n−1 residual degrees of freedom. Therefore s2=∑i​(Yi​−β​xi​)2/(n−1) estimates the common error variance, and s2/∑i​xi2​ estimates the slope variance. For errors with a Gaussian distribution, the slope's standardized deviation follows a Student's t-distribution with n−1 degrees of freedom. A common calibration bias does not appear as independent residual scatter.
