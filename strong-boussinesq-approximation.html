Strong Boussinesq approximation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Boussinesq approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
In the constant-property convection convention, the strong Boussinesq approximation supplements small relative mass density changes with constant dynamic viscosity, thermal diffusivity, specific heat capacity and thermal expansion coefficient throughout the modeled temperature interval. A uniform reference density replaces density in inertia and mass conservation, while a linear density anomaly is retained in gravitational buoyancy. The temperature obeys an advection-diffusion equation with constant coefficients. The term “strong” is not uniform across all fluid-mechanics literature: some stratified-flow treatments instead emphasize the use of a globally constant reference density. The constant-property convection usage is explained in courses.physics.ucsd.edu/2021/Spring/physics218c/Spiegel%20Convection.pdf .
