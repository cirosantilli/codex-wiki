Maximum-subarray segment summary ((S,P,Q,B))

 Home Computer science Algorithm Maximum subarray problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 107
For a segment, store its total sum S, maximum possibly-empty prefix sum P, maximum possibly-empty suffix sum Q and maximum possibly-empty internal contiguous sum B. Adjacent segment summaries combine by
(S,P,Q,B)=(SL​+SR​, max(PL​,SL​+PR​), max(QR​,SR​+QL​), max(BL​,BR​,QL​+PR​)).
(5)
A prefix either stays on the left or includes the whole left segment and a right prefix. A suffix has the symmetric decomposition. An internal segment either stays in one child or crosses the boundary as a left suffix followed by a right prefix. These cases prove the formulas, including empty candidates. Store the corresponding endpoints to recover a winning segment. This constant-size summary gives a linear time divide-and-conquer algorithm for the empty-allowed maximum subarray problem.
