Past exam of the introduction to quantum information course of the University of Oxford / 2021 / 3 / d / Solution

OurBigBook.com Words: 174
Write the unknown common eigenbasis as an unknown unit Bloch vector n. Then ρA​ has vector 0.8n, while ρB​ has vector −0.4n. A single deterministic channel working for every possible basis must therefore map 0.8n to −0.4n for every unit n.
By the basis-independent conversion of qubit Bloch radii, linearity forces that channel to be E−1/2​. Explicitly, add the conditions for n and −n to get E(I)=I, then use the three coordinate directions to fix its action on every Pauli matrix. But −1/2<−1/3, so part iv excludes complete positivity. Thus the forward conversion cannot be implemented reliably without knowing the basis.
The reverse conversion would require E−2​, which is not even positive by part ii. Therefore the reverse conversion is also impossible. Unitary operations interspersed with physically allowed depolarizing maps cannot evade these restrictions: their composition remains a physical quantum channel. The question concerns one basis-independent operation on the supplied qubit, not an additional resource of many copies for learning its basis.
