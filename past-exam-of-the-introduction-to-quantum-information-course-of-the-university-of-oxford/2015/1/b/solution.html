Write ⊕ for bitwise exclusive or. Both quantum registers begin in ∣0n⟩. After the first Hadamard transform their joint quantum state is
2−n/2∑x∈{0,1}n​∣x⟩∣0n⟩.
(4)
The quantum oracle Uf​ maps each component ∣x⟩∣0n⟩ to ∣x⟩∣f(x)⟩. By linearity, the required quantum state is therefore
∣Ψ⟩=2−n/2x∈{0,1}n∑​∣x⟩∣f(x)⟩.​
(5)
The different first-register computational-basis states are orthogonal, so there are 2n orthogonal joint components of squared probability amplitude 2−n, even though some second-register values coincide. Thus the displayed quantum state is normalized.
