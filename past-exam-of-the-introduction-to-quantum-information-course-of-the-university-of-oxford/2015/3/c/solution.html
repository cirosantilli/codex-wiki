Normalize the second qubit as ∣−⟩=(∣0⟩−∣1⟩)/2​. Since a Pauli X gate changes ∣−⟩ to −∣−⟩, the Boolean quantum oracle acts as
Uf​∣x⟩∣−⟩=(−1)f(x)∣x⟩∣−⟩.
(37)
This quantum phase kickback leaves the second qubit uncorrelated with the first. Put b=f(0)⊕f(1): b=0 for a constant function and b=1 for a balanced Boolean function. Apart from the irrelevant global phase (−1)f(0), the first qubit and environment reach
2​∣0⟩∣e0​⟩+(−1)b∣1⟩∣e1​⟩​.
(38)
The population-preserving interaction commutes with the quantum oracle on this answer-register input, so its placement before or after the oracle, between the two Hadamard gates, gives the same result. Substitute φ=πb into b:
P(0∣b)=21+(−1)bvcosα​,P(1∣b)=21−(−1)bvcosα​.​
(39)
The printed Deutsch algorithm uses the rule zero means constant function; one means balanced Boolean function. With that rule, the Deutsch algorithm with pure dephasing succeeds for either class with
Pcorrectprinted​=21+vcosα​.​
(40)
For real nonnegative environment overlap this is (1+v)/2, ranging from certainty at v=1 to random guessing at v=0. For general complex overlap, a coherent shift can invert the printed answer: for example v=1,α=π makes the printed rule always wrong. If the overlap is known but the printed quantum measurement is retained, choose the better labeling of its outcomes; this gives
Pcorrectrelabeled​=21+∣vcosα∣​.​
(41)
There is a further improvement if a known α can be compensated by the phase gate P−α​ before the final Hadamard gate. It removes the coherent shift without reversing any actual quantum decoherence, and yields
Pcorrectcompensated​=21+v​.​
(42)
This is optimal when only the first qubit is accessible, with equal prior probabilities for the two classes. Before the final Hadamard gate, their reduced density matrices are
ρb​=21​(1(−1)bc​(−1)bc1​).
(43)
The difference ρ0​−ρ1​ has eigenvalues ±v, giving trace distance v. The Holevo–Helstrom theorem therefore bounds the success probability by (1+v)/2, exactly attained by the compensated procedure. A known phase shift and loss of coherence have different effects: at v=0 the two density matrices coincide and no measurement on the available qubit can reveal the answer.
