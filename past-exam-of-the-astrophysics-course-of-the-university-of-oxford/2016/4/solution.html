Before and through cosmological recombination, photons diffuse between hotter and cooler parts of the tightly coupled photon–baryon fluid. Radiative viscosity and heat transport erase short-wavelength acoustic perturbations. As free electrons disappear, the photon mean free path grows, enhancing this diffusion before decoupling. Silk damping can be represented by a factor approximately e−k2/kD2​ in the linear acoustic amplitude. The comoving Silk wavelength may be defined as λD​=2π/kD​; the associated diffusion-length convention is rD​=1/kD​.
