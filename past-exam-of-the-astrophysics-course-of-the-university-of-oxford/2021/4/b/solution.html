With rG​=0.015 Mpc, n0​=0.01 Mpc−3, and c/H0​=4282.75 Mpc, set C=cπrG2​n0​/H0​=0.030273. Consequently
τ(1)=0.0369013.​
(49)
For random uncorrelated intersections, the zero-intersection probability is e−τ from the Poisson distribution, so the fraction of the sky covered by foreground galaxies out to z=1 is
f=1−e−τ(1)=0.03623≃3.6%.​
(50)
Ignoring overlaps gives f≃τ≃3.7%. A literal shell of zero thickness exactly at z=1 has zero coverage; the integrated foreground interpretation is needed. Clustering changes the exact overlap probability, although the mean optical depth above still follows from the mean number density.
Finally,
τ=1⟹zs​=(1+2C3​)2/3−1=12.67.​
(51)
At this optical depth, the Poisson sky coverage is 1−e−1≃63%, not 100%. The large inferred cosmological redshift extrapolates conserved galaxy counts and fixed radii far beyond their plausible physical range; it is the answer within the prescribed toy model.
