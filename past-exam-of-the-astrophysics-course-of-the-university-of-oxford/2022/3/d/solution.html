The finite angular resolution of COBE DMR suppresses small angular scales, so only low spherical harmonic multipoles can be recovered usefully. The approximate relation θ∼π/l associates l≃17 with scales of order ten degrees; this is an effective sensitivity and beam-window limit, not a mathematical hard truncation.

The stated amplitude uncertainty gives approximately 18% relative uncertainty in A, or A≃(1.38±0.25)×106 Mpc4, keeping H0​ fixed. The ideal matter-era Sachs-Wolfe plateau with precisely the c spectrum convention has coefficient 2π/9=0.698, rather than 0.732; the supplied numerical coefficient is about 5% larger and has been treated as the paper's prescribed approximation. A real beam-convolved analysis retains its window function and additional cosmological transfer effects.
