21​I¨xx​=2Kxx​−Gi<k∑​rik3​mi​mk​(xi​−xk​)2​.​

A stationary or time-averaged bounded system has ⟨I¨xx​⟩=0, yielding 2⟨Kxx​⟩+⟨Wxx​⟩=0. If ordered and random motions are separated, 2Kxx​=2Txx​+Πxx​, where Txx​=21​∫ρux2​dV and Πxx​=∫ρσxx2​dV. Summing the three diagonal equations recovers the scalar virial theorem, with the pair gravitational potential energy counted once.
