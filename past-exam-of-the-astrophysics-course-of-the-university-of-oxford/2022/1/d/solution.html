For the unresolved Lyman-alpha line, the lower 1s and combined upper 2p state degeneracies are g1​=2 and g2​=6, so the Einstein coefficients give
B12​=38πhν123​A21​c3​=2.03×1020 m3J−1s−2.
(6)
The measured equivalent width W=7.0×10−12 m then implies
NHI​=3A21​c38πWν124​​=1.29×1017 m−2=1.29×1013 cm−2.
(7)
Assume a central transverse line of sight through a uniform filament, so ℓ=d=0.5 Mpc=1.54×1022 m. Nearly all neutral hydrogen is in its ground state at this temperature, hence
nHI​=NHI​/d=8.3×10−6 m−3=8.3×10−12 cm−3.​
(8)
The cylinder's length L determines its total volume but does not enter a transverse column density. Geometry must be specified: an axial line of sight has ℓ=L and gives nHI​=4.2×10−7 m−3 for the same measured equivalent width. Intermediate chords give intermediate path-dependent estimates. The numbers above treat W as a rest-frame equivalent width; an observed value at redshift z must first be divided by 1+z.
