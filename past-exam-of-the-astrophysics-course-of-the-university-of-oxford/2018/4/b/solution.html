Use the complete supplied three-level model, including collisions between levels 2 and 3. Put g1​=4, g2​=4, g3​=6, and denote a collisional rate coefficient by qij​. Detailed balance gives
qij​=gi​gj​​e−(Ej​−Ei​)/(kB​Te​)qji​(Ej​>Ei​).
(31)
At Te​=104K, the supplied collision strengths give, in m3s−1,
​q21​5.999×10−14​q31​6.006×10−14​q32​1.089×10−13​​,
(32)
q12​=7.075×10−15,q13​=1.057×10−14,q23​=1.626×10−13.
(33)
The rounded optical wavelengths set the excitation energies; treating the fine splitting as very small compared with kB​Te​ changes none of the main conclusions.
Let D2​=A21​+ne​(q21​+q23​), D3​=A31​+A32​+ne​(q31​+q32​), and R32​=A32​+ne​q32​. Statistical equilibrium for the upper levels is
n2​D2​−n3​R32​=n1​ne​q12​,n3​D3​−n2​ne​q23​=n1​ne​q13​.
(34)
Eliminating n1​ yields the line-ratio formula
j21​j31​​=A21​ν21​A31​ν31​​q12​D3​+q13​(A32​+ne​q32​)q13​D2​+ne​q23​q12​​.​
(35)
The same ratio holds for integrated intensities when the two lines sample the same optically thin volume and differential extinction is negligible. Inserting the data gives the particularly simple numerical form
R(ne​)=1+x1.49325+0.442056x​,x=7.8607×108m−3ne​​.​
(36)
The low- and high-density limits are approximately 1.49 and 0.442. The collisional loss channels give
ncrit,3​=q31​+q32​A31​+A32​​=1.54×109m−3,ncrit,2​=q21​+q23​A21​​=3.95×109m−3.
(37)
Thus the useful density range is around 109–1010m−3. Maximizing ∣dlogR/dlogne​∣ for the rational expression gives its strongest fractional sensitivity at ne​=7.8607×1081.49325/0.442056​=1.44×109m−3. Omitting the supplied 3–2 collisions would change the critical densities and inferred density appreciably.
For the observed ratio 0.50, inversion gives ne​=1.35×1010m−3. This is on the high-density side of the transition, but is not the infinite-density limit. Solving the statistical-equilibrium equations and imposing f1​+f2​+f3​=1 gives the level fractions
(f1​,f2​,f3​)≃(0.8453, 0.05754, 0.09716).​
(38)
A rough high-density estimate would instead use fi​∝gi​e−Ei​/(kB​Te​); it is useful as a limiting check, not an exact solution for R=0.50.
For the separate abundance calculation, use the expressly prescribed ne​=1012m−3. The same model now gives (f1​,f2​,f3​)=(0.77426,0.09045,0.13528), nearly the collisional Boltzmann limit. The measured angle-integrated volume emissivity gives
n3​=A31​hc/λ31​j31​​=1.300×106m−3,n(S+)=f3​n3​​=9.61×106m−3.
(39)
Therefore
ne​n(S+)​=9.61×10−6.​
(40)
This is an abundance per free electron; it is approximately n(S+)/n(H+) for predominantly ionized hydrogen with ne​≃n(H+). No 4π factor is inserted because the supplied emissivity is power per volume, not power per volume per steradian. The imposed 1012m−3 predicts a ratio about 0.443, rather than exactly 0.50; the two numerical instructions cannot both describe one uniform zone exactly within this model.
The source's 2p3 configuration label is a typographical error for the sulfur valence configuration 3s23p3. This does not alter the stated J values or the calculation above.[ref]
