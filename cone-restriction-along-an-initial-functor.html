Cone restriction along an initial functor

 ... Category theory Category Diagram in a category Cone over a diagram Categorical limit Initial functor
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
For an initial functor F:I→J, restriction gives an isomorphism between the category of cones over D:J→C and that over DF. Given a cone over DF, choose (i,u:Fi→j) and define its j-leg as D(u)γi​; connectedness of (F↓j) makes the result independent of the choice.
