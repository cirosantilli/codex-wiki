Determinant-n matrix representatives for Hecke operators (Πn​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Hecke operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
Left SL2​(Z)-orbits of integral matrices of positive determinant n have unique representatives (a0​bd​) with a,d>0, ad=n and 0≤b<d. Bezout's identity reduces the first column to (a,0); a row shear reduces b modulo d.
