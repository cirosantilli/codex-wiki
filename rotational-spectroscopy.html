Rotational spectroscopy

 Home Chemistry Molecule Molecular rotation
OurBigBook.com Words: 363 Articles: 2
Rotational spectroscopy probes transitions between molecular rotation quantum states. In the simple linear rigid rotor, line spacing determines a moment of inertia, while an isotope shift of a linear rotor tests the mass dependence. More general molecular geometry requires several principal moments of inertia and more complicated assignments. The combination makes spectra sensitive fingerprints of both composition and shape.
