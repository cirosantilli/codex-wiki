Einstein manifold (Ric=Λg)

 ... Differential geometry Second fundamental form Gauss–Codazzi equations Gauss equation Sectional curvature Ricci curvature
OurBigBook.com Words: 148 Articles: 2
An Einstein manifold has Ricci curvature equal to a constant multiple of its metric. In the Riemannian setting, the round unit sphere has Λ=n−1. This condition is weaker than constant sectional curvature in higher dimensions. The definition can also be applied to pseudo-Riemannian metrics, but positivity is assumed in the completeness theorems discussed here.
