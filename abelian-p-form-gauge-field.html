Abelian p-form gauge field (Fp+1​=dAp​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field
OurBigBook.com Words: 458 Articles: 8
An Abelian p-form gauge potential Ap​ is an antisymmetric tensor field with gauge equivalence Ap​↦Ap​+dΛp−1​ and field strength Fp+1​=dAp​. Its field strength obeys the Bianchi identity for an Abelian p-form. The potential can have global gauge data even when its local field strength vanishes.
