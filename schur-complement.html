Schur complement (S=D−CA−1B)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 439 Articles: 3
For a block matrix M=(AC​BD​) with A an invertible matrix, the Schur complement of A in M is S=D−CA−1B. It is the effective coefficient matrix for the remaining variables after block elimination. The definition requires invertibility of A, but not of S or M. Its size is that of D, even when B and C are rectangular matrices.
In the original example under block matrix inverse, D=5 becomes S=19/6 because changing the remaining variable also changes the two variables solved through A. This is what makes a Schur complement more informative than simply deleting rows and columns. It retains their mediated effect, and its matrix inverse is the corresponding block of M−1 when the block matrix inverse exists.
The same elimination appears when minimizing a quadratic form. The Schur complement as a minimized quadratic form gives a geometric example; the Schur complement determinant identity gives an exact test for an invertible matrix.
