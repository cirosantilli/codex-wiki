Finite-height polynomial root avoidance

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Fundamental solution of a linear differential operator Malgrange–Ehrenpreis theorem Hörmander staircase
OurBigBook.com Words: 115
Suppose P(ξ′,z) has degree m in z and a nonzero constant leading coefficient a. Among the m+1 heights hℓ​=3ℓ, at least one satisfies
∣P(ξ′,s+ihℓ​)∣≥∣a∣for all s∈R
(18)
at each real ξ′. Factor into m linear factors with multiplicity. Each root's imaginary part can be within distance less than one of at most one candidate height. The pigeonhole principle leaves a height whose distance from every root is at least one, proving the product bound. The height can be chosen measurably: the set where a candidate succeeds is the closed intersection of ∣P(ξ′,q+ihℓ​)∣≥∣a∣ over rational q, and selecting the first successful candidate gives a Borel set partition. This avoids any need for continuous global root labels.
