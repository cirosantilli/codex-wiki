Non-Abelian Ward identity for fermion annihilation (k2ν​Mabμν∝k1μ​)

 ... Gauge field Gauge fixing BRST symmetry BRST charge BRST Ward identity Slavnov-Taylor identity
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For a Dirac field annihilating with its antiparticle into two massless gauge bosons, the two ordered fermion-exchange diagrams leave a colour matrix commutator when one outgoing momentum contracts the scattering amplitude. The three-boson exchange diagram cancels this current term but leaves a term proportional to the other outgoing momentum. With D=∂−igA, Jcλ​=vˉγλtcu, q=k1​+k2​, and the source's positive-sign three-boson vertex, the tree result is k2ν​Mabμν=ig2fabc(Jc​⋅k2​)k1μ​/q2. It vanishes after contraction with a physical polarization vector on leg 1, although the open-index tensor is generally nonzero.
