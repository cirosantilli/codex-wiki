Voltage clamp

 Home Biology Neuroscience
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
A voltage clamp uses electrical feedback to hold a preparation's membrane potential near a commanded value while measuring the electric current required to maintain it. Holding voltage separates changes in electrical conductance from changes in electrical driving force. Imperfect voltage control and finite recording bandwidth limit the inference. The muscle experiment under ionotropic receptor used separate intracellular electrodes for voltage control and a sealed patch pipette for recording local electric current.[ref]
