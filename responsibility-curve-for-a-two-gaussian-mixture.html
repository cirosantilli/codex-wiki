It is the conditional expectation of the mixture allocation indicator. This posterior probability concerns the hidden label, with θ held fixed; it does not require a prior distribution over θ. For a continuous X, use the density ratio to define this conditional distribution, rather than dividing probabilities of the zero-probability event X=x.[ref]

At x=0 the two unweighted component densities are equal, so the mixture responsibilities equal the mixture weights: r1​(0)=1/3, r2​(0)=2/3. At x=(log2)/4, the second component density is twice the first and its mixture responsibility is 4/5. Equal mixture responsibilities instead occur at x∗​=−(log2)/4≈−0.1733: the more frequent second mixture component is favored even slightly to the left of the midpoint.
The mixture weights describe membership before learning x; mixture responsibilities describe membership after learning x. The law of total expectation gives E[rj​(X;θ)]=πj​ under this statistical model, although rj​(x;θ) varies across individual values. In fitting by the expectation-maximization algorithm, evaluate mixture responsibilities at the current statistical parameters and recompute them after an update.
