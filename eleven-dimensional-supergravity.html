Eleven-dimensional supergravity (GMN​, AMNP​, ΨM​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supergravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 359 Articles: 5
Eleven-dimensional supergravity contains a metric, a three-form potential and a gravitino. Its bosonic on-shell counts are 11(11−3)/2=44 for the metric and (39​)=84 for the three-form, totaling 128. Flat toroidal zero-mode reduction gives type IIA supergravity in ten dimensions and four-dimensional N=8 supergravity in four dimensions without losing these bosonic polarizations.
