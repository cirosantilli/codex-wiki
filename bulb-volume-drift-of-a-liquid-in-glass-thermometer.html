Bulb-volume drift of a liquid-in-glass thermometer

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Temperature Thermometer Liquid-in-glass thermometer
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A bulb-volume change shifts the indicated temperature. Contraction raises the liquid column at a fixed temperature; expansion lowers it. Permanent and temporary changes require distinction when interpreting an ice-point check.[ref]
