Twomey effect

 Home Chemistry Atmospheric chemistry Aerosol Aerosol-cloud interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 1
Increasing cloud droplet number at fixed liquid-water content and cloud thickness reduces mean droplet size and increases total extinction area, raising shortwave optical depth and cloud albedo. This idealized response produces negative shortwave radiative forcing. Changes in cloud amount, water content and dynamics are separate adjustments.[ref]
