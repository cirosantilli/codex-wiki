Photon gas

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
A photon gas consists of photons in thermal equilibrium. Since photon number is not conserved when photons can be emitted and absorbed, the equilibrium photon chemical potential is zero. The photon energy density is aR​T4, where aR​=4σ/c, and its pressure is one third of this energy density.
