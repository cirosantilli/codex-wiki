Stationary internal-wave WKB solution (w∼m−1/2ei(kx+∫mdz))

 ... Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave Atmospheric internal gravity wave Taylor–Goldstein equation Scorer parameter
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For a stationary internal gravity wave, the Taylor–Goldstein equation gives m2=N2/U2−U′′/U−k2. In a propagating interval with slowly varying m, the WKB approximation has amplitude proportional to m−1/2 and phase derivative m. The conditions ∣m′∣≪m2 and ∣m′′∣≪∣m∣3 fail at a regular turning level. With U>0, the upward branch has m>0 and negative intrinsic frequency; laboratory group velocity is U(k2,km)/(k2+m2) when the curvature term is negligible.
