Zero matrix (0m×n​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
A matrix all of whose entries are zero. It is the additive identity among matrices of the same dimensions and represents the zero linear map in any choice of bases.
