Evaluation embedding into cogenerator products

For a small cogenerating family (Qi​) in a locally small category, the evaluation arrow C→∏i,q∈C(C,Qi​)​Qi​ is a monomorphism whenever that small product in a category exists. Equality after all its projections is equality after every map to a cogenerator, hence equality of the original parallel morphisms.
