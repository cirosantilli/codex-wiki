Associative algebra

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field
OurBigBook.com Words: 6k Articles: 122
An associative algebra is an algebra over a field whose multiplication satisfies (ab)c=a(bc). Unless stated otherwise, the algebras considered here have a multiplicative identity.
