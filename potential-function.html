Potential function

 Home Physics Branch of physics Classical mechanics Energy Potential energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
A potential function V assigns a scalar to each point of a configuration or spatial domain. In mechanics it represents potential energy, and its negative gradient gives the corresponding conservative force.
