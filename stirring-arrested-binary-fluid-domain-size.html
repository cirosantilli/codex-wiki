OurBigBook.com Words: 29
If stirring imposes a time scale τ, replacing time derivatives by 1/τ predicts a steady domain size. Viscous-capillary balance gives Λ∼στ/η, while inertial-capillary balance gives Λ∼(στ2/ρ)1/3.
