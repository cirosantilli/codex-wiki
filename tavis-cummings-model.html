The Tavis-Cummings model extends the Jaynes-Cummings model to several two-level systems exchanging excitations with a common quantized normal mode under the rotating-wave approximation. It conserves total excitation number. Equal coupling preserves permutation-symmetric atomic sectors, allowing finite-dimensional reductions for a fixed initial excitation number.
