Kerr black hole

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 24
The Kerr black hole is the asymptotically flat stationary axisymmetric vacuum black hole with mass M and angular momentum J=Ma.
A Kerr black hole is the Kerr metric with mass M>0 and spin parameter satisfying ∣a∣≤M in geometrized units; its outer horizon is r+​=M+M2−a2​.
