Deflated wealth equation with consumption (d(YX)=H⋅d(YP)−Ycdt)

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Risk-neutral measure Martingale deflator State-price density
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
A self-financing investor with consumption satisfies X=H⋅P and dX=H⋅dP−cdt. The Itô product rule and d[Y,X]=H⋅d[Y,P] give the displayed equation. For a positive local martingale deflator Y, the first term is a stochastic integral against the vector of deflated asset-price local martingales, not their current levels.
