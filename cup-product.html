Cup product (⌣)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 711 Articles: 15
The cup product is a natural bilinear operation Hp(X;R)×Hq(X;R)→Hp+q(X;R). It is graded-commutative: a⌣b=(−1)pqb⌣a.
