Two-sided stationary inverse of an autoregressive polynomial (A(B)Xt​=ϵt​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Autoregressive moving-average model Autoregressive model Noncausal stationary autoregression
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
If an autoregressive model has white noise of positive variance, a unique weakly stationary process solving its equation on all integer times exists precisely when its autoregressive polynomial has no root on the unit circle. In that case 1/A(z) has an absolutely summable Laurent series on an annulus containing the unit circle; its coefficients define a bilateral linear filter of a stationary time series. Applying that inverse also proves uniqueness among bounded second-moment solutions. A root on the unit circle forces the candidate spectral density of a stationary process to have a nonintegrable singularity. The stronger causality root criterion for an autoregressive model requires every root outside the closed unit disk.
