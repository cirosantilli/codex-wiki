An observable on a spin removed by decimation is inserted before its local sum. For τ2, replace Vst​=∑τ​eβτ(J1​s+J2​t) by Qst​=∑τ​τ2eβτ(J1​s+J2​t)=2cosh[β(J1​s+J2​t)]. Contracting the unchanged ring gives the displayed thermal expectation value. In the infinite chain it becomes v+T​Qv+​/λ+​ for normalized dominant eigenvector v+​.
