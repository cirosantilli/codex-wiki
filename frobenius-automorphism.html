Frobenius automorphism (Frob)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Unramified extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 184 Articles: 2
For a finite residue field k of cardinality q, arithmetic Frobenius is x↦xq. Its lift generates the Galois group of a finite unramified extension; geometric Frobenius is its inverse.
