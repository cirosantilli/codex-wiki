Canonical Riemann-Roch space (L(KX​))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Divisor on an algebraic curve Riemann-Roch theorem Canonical divisor
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
If KX​=(ω) is represented by a nonzero rational differential, multiplication by ω identifies the Riemann-Roch space L(KX​) with the vector space of holomorphic differential forms. The Riemann-Roch theorem gives ℓ(KX​)=g for a smooth projective curve of genus g.
