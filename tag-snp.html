Tag SNP

 Home Biology Genetics Genetic variant Single-nucleotide polymorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A tag SNP represents other nearby single-nucleotide polymorphisms whose alleles are strongly correlated through linkage disequilibrium. Tagging reduces the number of markers needed for association mapping; it does not establish that the tag itself causes the phenotype.
