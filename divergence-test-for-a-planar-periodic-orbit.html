Divergence test for a planar periodic orbit

 Home Physics Branch of physics Dynamical systems Floquet multiplier
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
For a planar periodic orbit γ of period T, its nontrivial Floquet multiplier is
exp(∫0T​∇⋅F(γ(t))dt).
(25)
The orbit is asymptotically stable when the integral is negative and unstable when it is positive.
