Symmetric equal-mass relativistic elastic scattering

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Particle dynamics Collision mechanics Elastic collision
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
A particle of total incident energy E and rest energy E0​ strikes an identical stationary particle. If the two outgoing paths make equal and opposite nonzero angles θ to the incoming direction in the laboratory frame, transverse conservation of momentum makes their outgoing energies equal to (E+E0​)/2. Longitudinal conservation of momentum and the mass shell relation imply tan2θ=2E0​/(E+E0​). The nonrelativistic limit is θ=45∘; at large incident energy both paths become forward directed.
