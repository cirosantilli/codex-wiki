For the additive first-order structure, let

It defines nZ. These sets are pairwise distinct: if m>n>0, the integer n belongs to nZ but not to mZ. Thus there are infinitely many inequivalent one-variable first-order formulas modulo the complete theory of the additive integers. The Ryll-Nardzewski theorem implies that first-order theory is not aleph-zero-categorical.
