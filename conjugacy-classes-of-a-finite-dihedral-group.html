Conjugacy classes of a finite dihedral group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Dihedral group
OurBigBook.com Words: 134
Use Dn​ for the group of order 2n with rotation R and reflection m. Every element is Rj or Rjm, 0≤j<n. The rotational conjugacy classes are the sets {Rj,R−j}, reduced to singletons for central rotations. For odd n, all reflections form one class, giving (n+3)/2 classes in total. For even n, the identity and Rn/2 are central singletons, the other rotations form pairs, and the reflections split into even and odd powers of R, giving n/2+3 classes. Indeed conjugation by Rs changes a reflection exponent by 2s, while conjugation by m negates it. For odd prime n, a normal subgroup containing a reflection must contain all reflections and hence the whole group; the proper normal subgroups are therefore only the identity subgroup and the rotation subgroup.
