Monte Carlo integration (IS​=S−1∑s=1S​h(Xs​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Monte Carlo method
OurBigBook.com Words: 283 Articles: 4
To calculate I=∫h(x)p(x)dx, generate independent Xs​ with density p and average h(Xs​). Integrability gives convergence by the strong law of large numbers; finite variance gives an unbiased estimate with variance Var(h(X))/S. Applying this to indicators estimates probabilities. Correlated Markov chain Monte Carlo samples can also approximate the integral, but their error is determined by the chain's dependence rather than the independent-sample variance formula.
