Underdamped Langevin dynamics

OurBigBook.com Words: 37
Underdamped Langevin dynamics augments position by momentum and combines Hamiltonian transport with friction and matching Gaussian noise. For potential U, momentum variance η, and friction γ, its invariant density is proportional to exp[−U(x)−∣p∣2/(2η)].
