Subformula

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Propositional formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 219 Articles: 4
A subformula is a constituent propositional formula in a formula's recursive construction, including the whole formula itself. For an atomic variable the only subformula is itself; for ¬A, add ¬A to the subformulas of A; for a binary connective, add the combined formula to the union of the two operands' subformulas. The set of distinct subformulas can have smaller cardinality than the number of occurrences in the abstract syntax tree when the same expression appears more than once.
