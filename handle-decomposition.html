Handle decomposition

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological manifold Handle attachment
OurBigBook.com Words: 482 Articles: 7
A handle decomposition builds an n-dimensional manifold by successively adjoining Dh×Dn−h along Sh−1×Dn−h. The attaching data comprise an attaching sphere and its framing of an embedded sphere. Reading a decomposition backwards replaces index h by index n−h and interchanges its attaching spheres and belt spheres.
