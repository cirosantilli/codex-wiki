Finite-input-resistance inverting amplifier (G=−1+(1+R2​/R1​+R2​/Rin​)/AR2​/R1​​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 52
With the noninverting input grounded, a differential input impedance Rin​ draws current from the summing node. For zero output impedance, Vo​=−Av and Kirchhoff's current law gives (v−Vi​)/R1​+(v−Vo​)/R2​+v/Rin​=0. Eliminating v yields the displayed voltage gain. The resistor-ratio result requires ∣A∣≫1+R2​/R1​+R2​/Rin​ as well as stable, unsaturated operation. The same expression applies to small-signal phasors with complex A(f).
