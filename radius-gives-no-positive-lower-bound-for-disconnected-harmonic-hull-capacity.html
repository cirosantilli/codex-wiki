Radius gives no positive lower bound for disconnected harmonic hull capacity

 ... Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Planar Brownian motion Harmonic measure Harmonic capacity from infinity in the upper half-plane
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
For c=1−ε2​, three vertical slits of height ε rooted at −c,0,c form a compact H-hull of enclosing radius one. Subadditivity of harmonic hull capacity gives capacity at most 6ε, which can tend to zero. Thus a uniform lower bound requires additional geometric hypotheses.
