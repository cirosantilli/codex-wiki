On Teflon, the water drop has an obtuse contact angle and beads up, whereas the oil drop has an acute contact angle and spreads into a shallower patch. For small drops with negligible Bond number, both can be drawn as spherical caps.
To compare shape at equal volume V, let R denote the generating sphere radius. The cap height and footprint radius are
h=R(1−cosθ),s=Rsinθ,V=3π​R3(2−3cosθ+cos3θ).
(25)
For a fixed V, these relations give a taller, narrower drop for the larger contact angle. The angles are measured inside the liquid, including the obtuse angle of the water drop.
