Here T is the absolute temperature and kB​ the Boltzmann constant. An adsorption blob contains g∼(ξ/b)2 monomers, so there are N/g∼N(b/ξ)2 such blobs. Confining a blob removes an amount of conformational entropy of order kB​; thus the positive confinement contribution to the Helmholtz free energy is
Fconf​≃kB​TgN​=kB​TN(ξb​)2.
(14)
The statement about one blob's entropy is used as an entropy loss relative to the free chain, rather than as an absolute entropy.
In the assumed slab model, contacts occur within a monomer-scale distance b of the substrate. The contact fraction is approximately b/ξ, giving Nc​≃Nb/ξ. Multiplying this by the negative contact energy yields
Fcontact​=−ϵkB​TξNb​,F(ξ)=NkB​T[(ξb​)2−ϵξb​].​
(15)
The two contributions express the competition between loss of conformational entropy and gain of adsorption energy. The numerical coefficients are the ones stipulated by this blob approximation.
