Scalar field

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 770 Articles: 17
A scalar field assigns one scalar to each point of a specified space, such as Euclidean space or spacetime. For example, F(x,y)=x2+2y2 is a real-valued function on a plane, and its change along a moving observation is given by the derivative along a curve. Under a change of coordinates, a scalar field's value at the same physical point is unchanged.
