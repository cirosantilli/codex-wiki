Construction of finite colimits from coproducts and reflexive coequalizers

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Colimit
OurBigBook.com Words: 123
For a finite diagram in a category D:J→C, set X=∐j​D(j) and Y=∐a:i→j​D(i). The two maps s,t:Y⇉X on the a-summand are the injection of D(i) and the injection of D(j) after D(a). Their coequalizer is the colimit of D. The pair [s,1X​],[t,1X​]:Y⨿X⇉X is a reflexive pair with the same coequalizer. Thus finite coproduct in a category constructions and coequalizers of reflexive pairs suffice, including the empty coproduct for the empty diagram. Finite products cannot replace coproducts here: the poset with elements 0,a,b,u0​,u1​,…, order 0<a,b<un+1​<un​, and incomparable a,b has finite meets and top u0​, hence finite categorical products. Every reflexive parallel pair is an equal pair and has its identity as coequalizer, but a,b have no least upper bound and therefore no coproduct.
