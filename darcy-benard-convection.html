Darcy-Bénard convection

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Darcy law
OurBigBook.com Words: 536 Articles: 8
Darcy-Bénard convection is thermal convection in a porous layer heated from below, with velocity governed by Darcy's law. With fixed temperatures and impermeable boundaries, a normalized two-dimensional model uses streamfunction ψ and temperature θ:
∇2ψ=−Rθx​,θt​+ψz​θx​−ψx​θz​=∇2θ.
(7)
The control parameter is a porous-medium Rayleigh number. On 0<z<1 with temperature values 0,−1, the conductive state is ψ=0, θ=−z.
