Rankine half-body (b=Q/(2U))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Potential flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
The region bounded by the dividing streamlines of a two-dimensional point source superposed on uniform flow. Its stagnation point is −Q/(2πU) upstream and its far downstream width is Q/U. The boundary encloses fluid originating at the source rather than a rigid obstacle; the upstream stagnation point is reached only asymptotically by the upstream source trajectory.
