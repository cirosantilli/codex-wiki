Gravitino (ψμ​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supergravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The gravitino is the spin-3/2 gauge field of local supersymmetry and the partner of the graviton. A massless four-dimensional gravitino has two physical helicities, ±3/2; a massive one has four, adding ±1/2. The super-Higgs mechanism supplies these extra states from the goldstino.
