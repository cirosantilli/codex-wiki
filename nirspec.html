NIRSpec

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Exoplanet atmospheric observatory James Webb Space Telescope
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
NIRSpec provides multiple spectroscopic modes within 0.6–5.3μm. Its time-series modes can measure exoplanet transmission spectra and exoplanet secondary eclipses; individual settings have narrower coverage. See NASA's instrument overview.
