Translation-covariant phase expansion (DY​=∂Y​+ϕY​∂ξ​)

Let p=ϕY​, r=ϕYY​ and w2​=C1​(ξ)r+C2​(ξ)p2. Two applications of the chain rule give DY2​w2​=C1​ϕYYYY​+2(C1′​+C2​)pϕYYY​+(C1′​+2C2​)r2+(C1′′​+5C2′​)p2r+C2′′​p4. In a Fredholm solvability condition, the fourth term generally contributes to the coefficient of ϕY2​ϕYY​. It cannot be omitted merely because the fast-coordinate correction functions have been denoted by Cj​(x).
