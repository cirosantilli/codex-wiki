Annual stellar aberration amplitude (δ≃v⊕​/c)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Relativistic aberration
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
At a sightline perpendicular to Earth's instantaneous orbital velocity, a heliocentrically transverse ray acquires an apparent angular displacement towards the observer's motion. A telescope of length L moves transversely by v⊕​L/c during the light crossing, giving the first-order result. The exact transverse Lorentz boost gives sinδ=v⊕​/c. For a circular orbital radius R and year Y, v⊕​=2πR/Y. The amplitude is half the full opposite-season excursion for a suitable sightline; it is not annual parallax, which depends on stellar distance.
