On closed Riemann surfaces of genus at least two, if a Teichmüller map f0​:X→Y has unit-area source differential q and dilatation K0​, any quasiconformal map f:X→Y in the same homotopy class satisfies K0​≤∫X​∣q∣∣1+μf​q/∣q∣∣2/(1−∣μf​∣2). The plus sign corresponds to a Teichmüller map with μf0​​=k0​∣q∣/q and K0​=(1+k0​)/(1−k0​). This fundamental inequality yields Teichmüller's uniqueness theorem by the pointwise triangle inequality and its equality case. See Gardiner and Hu, §5, equation (11).
