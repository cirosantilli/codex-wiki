Divisor restriction exact sequence (0→OX​(D−E)→OX​(D)→OE​(D)→0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Cartier divisor Effective Cartier divisor
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
For an effective Cartier divisor E and any Cartier divisor D, multiplication by a defining section of E gives the displayed short exact sequence of sheaves. Its long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology compares sections and cohomology on X with their restrictions to E.
