Tensor product (⊗)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 730 Articles: 16
The tensor product V⊗W of vector spaces is generated by symbols v⊗w subject to bilinearity. It is characterized by the property that every bilinear map from V×W factors uniquely through a linear map from V⊗W.
