Phosphorus-31 nuclear magnetic resonance (31P NMR)

 Home Chemistry Nuclear magnetic resonance
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
Phosphorus-31 nuclear magnetic resonance observes spin-one-half phosphorus-31. An A2​X system can show a two-phosphorus doublet and a one-phosphorus triplet; three inequivalent mutually coupled phosphorus sites can each give a doublet of doublets in the first-order limit.
