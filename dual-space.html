Dual space

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 782 Articles: 19
The dual space of a vector space V over F is V∗=HomF​(V,F). If V is finite-dimensional, then dimV∗=dimV.
