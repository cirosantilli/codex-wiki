Nonfaithful coset action of the symmetric group on three letters (kerρ=A3​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group action Faithful group action
OurBigBook.com Words: 181
Let G=S3​, the symmetric group on three letters, and take the normal subgroup N=A3​={e,(123),(132)}, the alternating group of even permutations. Its two left cosets are
E=N={e,(123),(132)},O=(12)N={(12),(13),(23)}.
(44)
Products of permutations apply the rightmost factor first. Left multiplication by an even permutation preserves both cosets, while left multiplication by an odd permutation exchanges them. For example,
(123)⋅E=E,(123)⋅O=O,(12)⋅E=O,(12)⋅O=E.
(45)
Consequently all six group elements give only two distinct permutations of {E,O}. Both stabilizer subgroups equal A3​, so their intersection, the group kernel, is A3​. This is a transitive group action because (12) reaches the other coset, but it is not a faithful group action because (123) fixes both.
The orbit-stabilizer theorem still works perfectly: 6=2⋅3. It counts reachable labels, without asserting that the induced group homomorphism is injective. The quotient group S3​/A3​≅C2​ captures exactly the two visible operations, identity and exchange. This is the same group homomorphism as the sign of a permutation, with +1 represented by fixing both labels and −1 by exchanging them. Contrast the three-label example in faithful coset action of the symmetric group on three letters.
