Winning coalition (v(S)=1)

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Cooperative game theory Transferable utility game Simple cooperative game
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
A coalition of value 1 in a simple cooperative game. Monotonicity makes every superset of a winning coalition winning. An inclusion-minimal winning coalition has no winning proper subset.
