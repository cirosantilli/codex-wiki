Depletion force (Udep​=−Πp​Vov​)

 Home Chemistry Mixture Colloid
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 1
A depletion force is an effective interaction caused by exclusion of smaller particles or macromolecules from regions around larger objects. Overlap of the excluded regions increases the accessible volume for the smaller species. At fixed reservoir osmotic pressure Πp​, the associated pair free energy is −Πp​ times the overlap volume in the ideal dilute-depletant approximation. It is an entropic attraction for two large hard particles. Surface adsorption can introduce different interactions, so adding an arbitrary polymer does not by itself guarantee depletion attraction.
