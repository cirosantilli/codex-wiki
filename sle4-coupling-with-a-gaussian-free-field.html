SLE4 coupling with a Gaussian free field (λ=π/2when GH​(z,w)=log∣(z−w)/(z−w)∣)

For a Gaussian free field with boundary heights −λ and +λ on the two sides of the starting point, its distinguished zero-height interface has chordal SLE4​ law. Conditional on an initial curve segment, the field is a zero-boundary field in the slit domain plus mt​(z)=λ−(2λ/π)arg(gt​(z)−Wt​). Its mean bracket equals minus the variation of its conditional Green covariance. Subtracting the initial harmonic function gives the corresponding coupling to a zero-boundary field.
