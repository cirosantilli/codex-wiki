Uniform-current toroidal-field curl reduction ((ω2−m2J2)∇×b=−2mkJ2b)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Toroidal magnetic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 151 Articles: 1
For constant-density incompressible ideal magnetohydrodynamics about B=Jreϕ​, the cylindrical basis contributions make the linearized Lorentz force equal to imJb+2Jez​×b, apart from a magnetic pressure gradient. The induction equation reduces to iωb=imJu. Taking the curl after eliminating velocity gives the displayed relation, with J=J/μ0​ρ​. A second curl gives a vector Helmholtz equation and squared frequencies ω2=J2[m2±2∣mk∣/(α2+k2)1/2]. The lower ∣m∣=1 branch grows when 3k2>α2.
