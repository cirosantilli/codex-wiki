MOS inversion threshold (ψs​≃2ϕF​)

 Home Physics Semiconductor Surface state Band bending Semiconductor inversion layer
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For an ideal MOSFET with a nondegenerate p-type semiconductor of acceptor density NA​, define ϕF​=(kB​T/e)ln(NA​/ni​)>0, where ni​ is the intrinsic carrier concentration. A positive surface electric potential ψs​ lowers the local conduction band and valence band by eψs​. The surface electron density is ns​=(ni2​/NA​)exp(eψs​/kB​T). Equality ns​=NA​ gives the conventional onset of strong inversion at ψs​=2ϕF​. The earlier crossover ns​=ps​=ni​ occurs at ψs​=ϕF​. Neither condition requires the Fermi level to enter the conduction band.
