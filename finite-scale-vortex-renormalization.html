Finite-scale vortex renormalization (ℓmax​=log(L/a))

OurBigBook.com Words: 196
An experiment of size L stops the renormalization-group flow at a finite ℓmax​. In the leading scaled model take the original initial conditions Y0​=0.06 and X0​=−0.10,−0.06,−0.03. The invariant is respectively 0.0064,0,−0.0027. The first approaches X=−0.08, hence K=(2.08)/π; the second follows the critical separatrix; the third eventually leaves the small-vortex fugacity regime. These are chosen model trajectories, with no fitted experimental parameters.
On the critical separatrix, X=−Y implies Y′=−Y2, hence
Y(ℓ)=1+Y0​ℓY0​​,K(ℓ)=π2​+π(1+Y0​ℓ)Y0​​.
(5)
Even a critical finite sample therefore has a phase stiffness above its asymptotic jump value, with a slow logarithmic correction in L. Slightly above the transition, a sample with L≪ξ can resemble the algebraic phase before the flow reaches unbound vortices. A trap additionally varies the local density and phase stiffness; neither its whole-cloud coherence nor a finite observation window automatically measures the homogeneous thermodynamic limit.
