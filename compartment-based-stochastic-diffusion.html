Compartment-based stochastic diffusion

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Chemical reaction network Stochastic chemical kinetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 72 Articles: 1
Compartment-based stochastic diffusion represents molecular motion by nearest-neighbour jumps on a spatial lattice. Jump rates of order D/h2±v/(2h) converge to an advection--diffusion equation as compartment width h tends to zero.
