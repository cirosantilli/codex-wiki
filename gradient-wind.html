Gradient wind

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Shallow water equations Geostrophic balance
OurBigBook.com Words: 85 Articles: 1
The gradient wind retains the centripetal acceleration of a curved flow as well as the Coriolis force and horizontal pressure gradient force. For cyclonic circular flow in the northern hemisphere, v2/r+fv=ρ−1dp/dr, with r increasing away from the low-pressure centre.
