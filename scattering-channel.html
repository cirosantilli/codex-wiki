Scattering channel

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
A specified asymptotic configuration of the particles and their internal quantum states in quantum scattering. An incoming channel can scatter into several outgoing channels; S-matrix elements give their probability amplitudes with a stated probability current normalization. A channel can be energetically inaccessible even when other channels are open.
