Bhattacharyya coefficient (B(p,q)=∑i​pi​qi​​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 1
For probability distributions p,q on the same finite alphabet, the Bhattacharyya coefficient measures their square-root overlap:
B(p,q)=∑i​pi​qi​​.
(2)
The vectors (pi​​)i​ and (qi​​)i​ have unit Euclidean norm, so the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality gives 0≤B≤1. Equality B=1 holds exactly when the distributions agree; B=0 holds exactly when they assign positive probability to disjoint outcomes. For p=(0.9,0.1) and q=(0.6,0.4), B=0.54​+0.2≈0.934847. This overlap is not the probability that two independent draws agree, which here is 0.58.
The fidelity of commuting mixed states reduces to this coefficient. More generally, the measurement characterization of quantum fidelity minimizes it over outcome distributions of a common measurement in quantum mechanics, connecting classical overlap to the unsquared quantum fidelity.
