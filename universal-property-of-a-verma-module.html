Universal property of a Verma module

 ... Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Highest-weight representation Verma module
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
If a g-module contains a vector v of weight λ annihilated by n+, there is a unique homomorphism Mλ​→V sending the canonical highest-weight vector to v. Every highest-weight module of weight λ is therefore a quotient of Mλ​.
