Axisymmetric magnetic winding (∂t​Bϕ​=RBp​⋅∇Ω)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 2
For prescribed axisymmetric differential rotation u=RΩeϕ​, the ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation and Gauss's law for magnetism give
∂t​Bp​=0,∂t​Bϕ​=RBp​⋅∇Ω.
(42)
The poloidal field is unchanged while rotational shear generates a toroidal magnetic field. For time-independent Ω, the toroidal component grows linearly until neglected dynamical feedback becomes relevant.
