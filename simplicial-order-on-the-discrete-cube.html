Simplicial order on the discrete cube (≺simp​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Analysis of Boolean functions Boolean hypercube
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 2
Order subsets first by increasing cardinality and, within each level, by lexicographic order, with the smallest differing coordinate belonging to the earlier set. This convention makes simplicial initial segments the extremizers in Harper theorem.
