Discrete valuation from nontrivial local compactness (m=πOK​,∣OK​/m∣<∞)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Local compactness criterion for a complete non-Archimedean field
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
For a locally compact field with a nontrivial Non-Archimedean absolute value, scaling a compact neighborhood makes its valuation ring compact. Its open maximal ideal gives a finite discrete residue field, so that ideal is also closed and compact. A nonzero element of maximal absolute value in the ideal generates it. Successive division by this uniformizer makes the value group discrete.
