Cauchy stress tensor (t=σn)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Constitutive equation Stress
OurBigBook.com Words: 314 Articles: 6
The stress tensor maps a surface normal to the traction exerted across that surface. Its divergence is the force density in local momentum balance. For an incompressible Newtonian fluid it combines pressure with twice the viscosity times the symmetric strain-rate tensor.
