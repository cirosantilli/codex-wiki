Connection (principal bundle)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Principal bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 18
A principal connection can be specified by a G-equivariant horizontal distribution H⊂TP complementary to the vertical bundle, or equivalently by a Lie-algebra-valued connection form A that reproduces infinitesimal generators and has H=kerA.
