Character group of a finite abelian group (G)

 ... Algebra Group theory Group Abelian group Pontryagin duality Character group
OurBigBook.com Words: 459 Articles: 8
The character group G consists of the group homomorphisms γ:G→C×. Pointwise multiplication makes it a finite abelian group isomorphic to G.
