The initial bottom-right entry is zero, so the chosen QR shift is μ0​=0. One valid QR factorization is Q0​=A, R0​=I, using explicitly that ATA=I. The update is
A1​=R0​Q0​=A.
(11)
It repeats forever and never approaches a diagonal matrix. With the standard positive-diagonal convention for R, this factorization is unique, so the Rayleigh-shift iteration is stationary and fails to diagonalize A.
The failure is independent of harmless sign conventions. The columns of A are already orthonormal, so another QR factorization can only replace Q0​ by AD and R0​ by D, where D is diagonal with entries ±1. Then A1​=DAD still has zero diagonal and off-diagonal magnitudes one. Subsequent bottom-right shifts remain zero and the off-diagonal magnitude never decreases. The eigenvalues are +1,−1, with equal absolute values, explaining the lack of separation in the unshifted QR algorithm.
