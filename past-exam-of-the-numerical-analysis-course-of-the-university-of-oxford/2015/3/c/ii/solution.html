Let r=xi2​+xj2​​. If r>0, choose θ so that
cosθ=rxi​​,sinθ=rxj​​.
(41)
Such an angle exists because the two ratios have squared sum one. Then the changed coordinates are
yi​=rxi2​+xj2​​=r,yj​=−rxj​xi​​+rxi​xj​​=0.
(42)
All other coordinates satisfy yk​=xk​. If r=0, both input coordinates are zero; take θ=0, giving the identity matrix on this pair. In all cases,
yi​=xi2​+xj2​​,yj​=0,yk​=xk​ (k=i,j).​
(43)
This fixes a nonnegative retained coordinate and handles division by zero explicitly. For floating-point computation, the stable construction of a Givens rotation uses scaled Euclidean norm evaluation to avoid unnecessarily overflowing or underflowing the squares.
