Induced current in a tilted resistive loop (I(t)=πr2B0​ωcosθsin(ωt)/R)

OurBigBook.com Words: 90
Choose the positive loop normal and its positive circulation by the right-hand rule. A spatially uniform external magnetic field of fixed direction and signed magnitude B0​cosωt, at angle θ to that normal, gives magnetic flux Φ=πr2B0​cosθcosωt. Neglecting the loop's self-inductance, Faraday's law of induction and Ohm's law give the displayed electric current. At a quarter-cycle the external field is zero but its normal component is decreasing most rapidly, so the current produces a field in the direction of the initial normal flux, as required by Lenz's law.
