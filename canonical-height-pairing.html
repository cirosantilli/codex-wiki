OurBigBook.com Words: 134 Articles: 1
The canonical height pairing is ⟨P,Q⟩=(h(P+Q)−h(P)−h(Q))/2. The height parallelogram identity makes it bilinear. It descends through torsion and is positive definite on the free part of the Mordell-Weil group. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality gives h(P−Q)≤2h(P)+2h(Q), useful for height descent.
