Proca time-ordering contact term (Δabcov​−ΔabT​=iδa0​δb0​δ(4)/m2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Proca equation Proca field Proca propagator
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
For a free massive Proca field in the mostly-plus Minkowski metric, the covariant inverse of the quadratic action and the canonical time-ordered product of vector potentials differ locally. With Fourier transform eiq⋅(x−y), the covariant Proca propagator is −i(ηab​+qa​qb​/m2)/(q2+m2−i0), while the literal canonical two-point correlation function subtracts iδa0​δb0​/m2 in momentum space. The time component is nondynamical; its physical-mode numerator is ∣q∣2/m2, rather than −1+(q0)2/m2. The difference is an instantaneous Dirac delta function, not another propagating state. Both expressions agree away from coincidence. This distinction matters when identifying a covariant Green function with a canonical correlator in a constrained field theory.
