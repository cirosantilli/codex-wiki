Oversampling (fs​>2B)

 Home Electrical engineering Analog-to-digital converter
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
oversampling takes samples faster than the minimum rate required by the desired signal bandwidth. With suitable uncorrelated conversion noise, digital filtering removes noise outside that bandwidth before decimation. Independent-error averaging improves standard deviation as N−1/2; fourfold averaging ideally gains one bit of amplitude resolution. Repetition of an unchanged biased code does not produce the same benefit.
