A narrow fringe makes the center easier to resolve statistically; it does not certify an unbiased frequency. For example, with ideal π/2 pulses and a second-pulse oscillation phase offset φc​, the finite-pulse Ramsey transition probability approaches
Pg→e​=cos2(2δT−φc​​),νcenter​−ν0​=−2πTφc​​.
(18)
This sign uses exactly the Rabi detuning convention and pulse Hamiltonian operator above, with the second pulse replacing σx​ by cosφc​σx​+sinφc​σy​. The factor 2π converts angular frequency to ordinary frequency. For the original example, φc​=0.010 rad and T=5ms give a shift of about −0.318Hz. For finite pulses or distributed flight times, use the full signal and its atomic clock frequency lock rather than inserting an unweighted mean T.

The connection to special relativity is quantitative. A freely moving emitter's proper time advances more slowly than laboratory time by 1−v2/c2​. With first-order propagation effects removed in the ideal comparison, its internal laboratory frequency is therefore
ν0​νlab​−ν0​​≃−2c2v2​.
(19)
Correcting an ensemble signal requires the effective velocity weights of preparation, interrogation and detection, not simply the oven's mean speed. Additional shifts from Zeeman splitting, unwanted light, collisions and cavity fields likewise require separate evaluation; feedback control locks to the observed line, including any uncorrected bias. Comparing clocks at different gravitational potentials additionally requires gravitational time dilation. The moving-clock experiments in twin paradox clock experiments compare calibrated clock readings along different world lines; the beam atoms' internal motion is part of the clock mechanism's own correction.
