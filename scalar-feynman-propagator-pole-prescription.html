Scalar Feynman propagator pole prescription (ΔF​(p)=i/(p2−m2+i0))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Quantized field Propagator Feynman propagator
OurBigBook.com Words: 128 Articles: 1
With ΔF​=⟨0∣T{ϕ(x)ϕ(y)}∣0⟩, for a mode of positive energy E>0 the Feynman i-epsilon prescription puts poles +E−i0 and −E+i0 in opposite half-planes. The energy contour integral closes clockwise below for positive time separation and counterclockwise above for negative separation. The residue theorem gives e−iE∣t∣/(2E) in either case. Thus the four-dimensional Fourier integral has numerator i and denominator p2−m2+i0. The integral is understood as a distribution limit, not an ordinary absolutely convergent integral; changing the definition to iΔF​ would change the displayed normalization.
