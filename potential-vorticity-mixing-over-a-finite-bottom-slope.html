Potential-vorticity mixing over a finite bottom slope (ΔQ=−Sy1{∣y∣<a}​,S=fϵ/H)

Homogenizing the potential vorticity over a finite constant bottom slope creates the odd anomaly displayed here while leaving the exterior unchanged. Inverting (∂y2​−LR−2​)ψ=ΔQ with decay at both infinities and matching ψ,ψy​ gives a central return current and flanking jets. The deformation radius sets the smoothing length. For LR​≪a, the central surface follows the bottom slope to leading order, but edge corrections and decaying exterior flow are essential.
