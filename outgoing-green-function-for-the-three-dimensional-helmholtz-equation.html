Outgoing Green function for the three-dimensional Helmholtz equation (Gk​(r,r′)=eik∣r−r′∣/(4π∣r−r′∣))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Helmholtz equation

The outgoing Green function satisfies (Δ+k2)Gk​=−δr′​ and the Sommerfeld radiation condition. For time dependence e−iωt, its radial phase travels outward. Away from its source it solves the homogeneous Helmholtz equation. At large r, Gk​=eikre−ikr⋅r′/(4πr)+O(r−2).
