Schmidt projection entanglement concentration (dk​=(kn​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 203 Articles: 1
For n copies of a normalized pure state α∣00⟩+β∣11⟩, regroup Alice's and Bob's tensor factors. Alice performs a projective measurement of the total number k of zeroes, preserving all coherence among strings with that count. The outcome has binomial distribution
pk​=(kn​)∣α∣2k∣β∣2(n−k).
(4)
Each retained string has the same complex amplitude αkβn−k, so, up to a common phase, the conditional quantum state is
∣Tk​⟩=(kn​)−1/2∑z(x)=k​∣x⟩A​∣x⟩B​.
(5)
Its equal Schmidt coefficients make it a maximally entangled state of Schmidt rank (kn​). Alice sends the count to Bob. They can relabel the corresponding local computational basis strings with local unitary operations, and convert the resulting resource to Bell pairs. Measuring the individual bits instead would reveal the entire string and leave a product state.
