Main-sequence lifetime (tMS​∼fMc2ϵ/L)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Main sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
The time a star spends with core hydrogen nuclear fusion as its principal energy source. If the usable fuel fraction f, conversion efficiency ϵ, and composition are comparable, the main-sequence lifetime scales as M/L. With a local mass-luminosity relation L∝Mα, the lifetime scales as M1−α. This is a scaling estimate: convective core size and changing luminosity affect detailed ages.
