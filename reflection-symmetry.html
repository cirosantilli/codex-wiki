Reflection symmetry

 Home Physics Symmetry (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 58 Articles: 1
Invariance under a spatial reflection. A one-dimensional Quantum Hamiltonian invariant under x↦a−x admits energy eigenfunctions of definite even or odd midpoint parity. Even and odd functions are orthogonal on a reflection-symmetric interval.
