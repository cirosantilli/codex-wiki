Differentiability in quadratic mean (∥pt​​−p​−2t​gp​∥2​=o(∣t∣))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical path
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 2
A dominated statistical path is differentiable in quadratic mean at P if its square-root probability density function has an L2 space derivative (1/2)gp​, with g∈L2(P). The score function is g. This formulation controls mass near zeros of p as well as the derivative on the support of P; a pointwise log-density derivative is insufficient by itself.
