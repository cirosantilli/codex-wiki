Fejér-kernel proof of the analytic large sieve

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Sieve theory Large sieve Exponential-sum large sieve
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
A triangular taper majorizes a fixed fraction of the summation interval. Its Fourier kernel is the Fejér kernel, bounded by Cmin(m,(m∥θ∥2)−1). For separated sample points, split each row sum at distance 1/m: the plateau and square-decay tail each contribute O(δ−1), while the diagonal contributes O(m). Bounding the resulting quadratic form proves the dual sieve with constant C(N+δ−1). Operator norm duality gives the primal sieve.
