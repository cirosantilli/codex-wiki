Finite pullback of an ample line bundle

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Locally free sheaf Line bundle Ample line bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For a finite morphism f:X→Y, the pullback of an ample line bundle is ample. If f is also surjective, ampleness is equivalent in both directions. These results hold for proper schemes over a field, including nonreduced schemes. Cohomological ampleness and the exact finite pushforward prove the forward direction; coherent-sheaf devissage gives finite-surjective descent. In particular, a finite map to projective space makes the pulled-back hyperplane bundle ample.
