Differential rotation (Ω(R,z))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
Differential rotation means that different parts of a rotating body or fluid have different angular velocities. An axisymmetric rotational velocity field can be written u=RΩ(R,z)eϕ​.
