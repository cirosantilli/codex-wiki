Steady uniaxial extension of the Giesekus model (bs12​+(1−2W)s1​−2W=0)

For uniaxial extensional flow with G=diag(γ,−γ/2,−γ/2), write W=λγ, b=αpT​, and the stress as pT​diag(s1​,s2​,s2​). The displayed longitudinal equation is supplemented by bs22​+(1+W)s2​+W=0. Choose the roots tending to zero at W=0. For 0<b<1 these roots have 1+si​>0 at every nonnegative rate. The stretching stress is finite at W=1/2 and the extensional viscosity (σ11​−σ22​)/γ tends to 2λ/α as γ→∞. In the zero-α Upper-convected Maxwell model, the physical steady longitudinal branch terminates at W=1/2. Arbitrary positive b is insufficient: b=2,W=1 gives the impossible real transverse equation 2s22​+2s2​+1=0.
