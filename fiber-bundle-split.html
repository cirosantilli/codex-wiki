Fiber bundle

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 16k Articles: 322
A fiber bundle is a map π:E→B locally isomorphic over each open set U⊆B to the projection U×F→U for a fixed fiber F.
