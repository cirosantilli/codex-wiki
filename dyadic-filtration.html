Dyadic filtration (Fn​=σ{dyadic cubes of side 2−n})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Filtration (probability theory)
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
On (0,1]d, the dyadic filtration is generated by the partitions into half-open cubes of side 2−n. The conditional expectation of an integrable function is its average on each atom. The increasing partitions generate the Borel sigma-algebra. Step functions on these partitions are dense in L1; approximation and the Doob maximal inequality show that their conditional averages converge in L1 and almost everywhere to the original function.
