Here is the fully specified squeeze rejection sampling procedure. Draw Y with probability density function q(y)=e−∣y∣/2, independently draw U∼Unif(0,1), and compute H=max(0,1−Y2/2) and B=e​e−∣Y∣. If U≤H/B, return Y immediately. Otherwise evaluate P=e−Y2/2, independently draw V∼Unif(0,1), and return Y if V≤(P−H)/(B−H). On rejection restart with fresh draws. In this example B>H everywhere, so the residual denominator is positive.
