P(Y=k)​=∫0∞​k!e−xxk​λe−λxdx=k!λ​(1+λ)k+1Γ(k+1)​=1+λλ​(1+λ1​)k.​

Here the gamma function identity Γ(k+1)=k! evaluates the integral. Hence
Y∼Geom(1+λλ​)​
(18)
on {0,1,2,…}. Put r=1/(1+λ) and ρ=1−r. The geometric series gives F(k)=1−rk+1. Its generalized inverse, the quantile function, is
F−1(u)=⌈logrlog(1−u)​⌉−1,0<u<1.​
(19)
This is the smallest nonnegative integer k for which 1−rk+1≥u; dividing by logr<0 reverses the inequality. Draw U from the uniform distribution on (0,1) and use this formula. The commonly used expression ⌊log(1−U)/logr⌋ has the same probability distribution, but differs from the exact generalized inverse when U is precisely a jump value of the cumulative distribution function. Such values have probability zero for an ideal continuous uniform draw.
