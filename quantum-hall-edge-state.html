Quantum Hall edge state

 ... Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force Hall effect Quantum Hall effect Integer quantum Hall effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 536 Articles: 11
A quantum Hall edge state is confined across a boundary but can extend along it and carry electric current. B. I. Halperin related these current-carrying boundary states to quantized Hall transport in a disordered two-dimensional system.[ref]
