Pseudohyperbolic distance (δ(z,w)=∣(z−w)/(1−wz)∣)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Hyperbolic geometry Hyperbolic metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
On the unit disc, the pseudohyperbolic distance is
δ(z,w)=​1−wzz−w​​.
(24)
It is invariant under disc Möbius transformations. For the curvature-minus-one hyperbolic metric with length element 2∣dz∣/(1−∣z∣2), δ(z,w)=tanh(ρ(z,w)/2) and e−ρ(z,w)=(1−δ)/(1+δ). The Schwarz-Pick theorem says that holomorphic functions from the disc into itself contract this distance. A Blaschke factor has modulus δ(z,a), so the distance also controls the size of a product with prescribed zeros.
