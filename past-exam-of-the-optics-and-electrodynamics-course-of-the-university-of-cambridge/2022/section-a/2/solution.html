This is the transmitter-induced equivalent noise temperature of the receiver port. It does not determine the actual physical temperature of its internal electrical resistance, or any additional receiver and background noise. The calculation assumes the usual far-field link, negligible path loss beyond geometric spreading, matched polarization of an electromagnetic wave at the two antennas and the stated effective collecting area. No additional one-half polarization factor is needed for the matched polarization of radiation from one transmitting antenna.
