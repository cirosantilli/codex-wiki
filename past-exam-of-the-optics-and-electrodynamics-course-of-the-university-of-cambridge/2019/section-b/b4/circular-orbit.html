Time averaging removes the azimuthal dependence. The axial intensity is twice the equatorial intensity, and no direction has zero mean intensity: the instantaneous dipole axis continually rotates. This is also the sum of the two orthogonal linear-oscillation patterns with their relative phase quadrature.
