Use retarded time tr​ and reserve r for the large observation distance. The source velocity is nonrelativistic, ∣u∣/c≪1. There are two errors in the original PDF that must be corrected before the radiation zone calculation: the vector-potential prefactor contains an extraneous 1/r, and the displayed magnetic radiation field has the wrong sign for the stated outward unit vector n. The extra distance factor is dimensionally wrong; the magnetic sign would make the Poynting vector point inward. The following derivation uses dimensionally consistent retarded potentials and outgoing radiation.
