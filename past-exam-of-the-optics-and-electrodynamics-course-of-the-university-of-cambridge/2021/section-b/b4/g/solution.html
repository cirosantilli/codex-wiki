The circular eigenmode orthogonal projection matrices obey P±2​=P±​, P+​P−​=0 and P+​+P−​=I. Define the uniform-medium generator
K=γ+P+​+γ−P−​=(γ−iδ​iδγ​).​
(24)
Expanding its matrix exponential by its power series and using these projection identities gives
e−Kz=e−γ+zP+​+e−γ−zP−​=J(z).​
(25)
Equivalently, with Q=(0−i​i0​), one has Q2=I, K=γI+δQ and e−Kz=e−γz[Icosh(δz)−Qsinh(δz)]. This is exactly the corrected matrix of part (d). The entries of K have units of inverse length, and the map satisfies dJ/dz=−KJ, J(0)=I and the homogeneous propagation law J(z1​+z2​)=J(z1​)J(z2​).
The extra sech2 factor in the printed version violates this last law for nonzero δ, so it cannot be generated by one constant K with the stipulated eigenvalues. The constant-K description assumes uniform coefficients and fixed circular eigenmodes.
