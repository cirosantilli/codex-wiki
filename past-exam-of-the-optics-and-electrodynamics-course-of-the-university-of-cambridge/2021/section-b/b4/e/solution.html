When γ±=α± are real, γ and δ are real. The two opposite circular polarizations retain their wave phases but are attenuated at different wave amplitude rates. This circular dichroism changes the ellipticity without rotating the principal axis of the ellipse.
For input E0​x, the corrected Jones matrix gives
Eout​=E0​e−γz(cosh(δz)isinh(δz)​).
(21)
The common factor e−γz describes the mea wave amplitude attenuation. The cosh(δz) factor reflects the sum of the two transmitted modal wave amplitudes; tanh(δz) fixes their imbalance and hence the signed ellipticity. The real field has components proportional to cosh(δz)cosωt and sinh(δz)sinωt, so
major semiaxisminor semiaxis​=∣tanh(δz)∣,I(0)I(z)​=e−2γzcosh(2δz)=2e−2α+z+e−2α−z​.​
(22)
The ellipse's principal axis stays along the original input direction. For large differential optical depth it approaches the less attenuated circular eigenmode. A passive medium has α±≥0, or γ≥∣δ∣, so the growing hyperbolic function factor does not imply net optical gain. Using the printed sech prefactor would give a spurious extra attenuation inconsistent with the modal laws.
