In S, adding the stationary opposite line cancels charge density and electric field but contributes no current. Hence
λtot​=0,Itot​=λu,Etot​=0,Btot​=2πrμ0​λu​φ​.
(29)
The neutral current filament in a boosted frame is not neutral. Transform its line-integrated four-current:
λtot′​=−c2γuItot​​=−γλc2u2​,Itot′​=γItot​=γλu.​
(30)
The same result follows by adding the original resting line (λ′,0) to the new negative line, whose primed density and current are (−γλ,+γλu). In particular λ′−γλ=−γλu2/c2 because λ=γλ′.
Transforming the total fields gives
Etot′​=−2πϵ0​c2rγλu2​r,Btot′​=2πrμ0​γλu​φ​.​
(31)
For positive λ, u×B points inward, explaining the electric-field sign. The primed Gauss and Ampère fields calculated from λtot′​ and Itot′​ reproduce these expressions. Both a moving charge distribution and a moving current must be transformed; boosting just one constituent would give an inconsistent total.
