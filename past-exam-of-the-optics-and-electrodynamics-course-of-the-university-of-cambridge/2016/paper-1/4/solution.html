For the incident z polarization, the dipole is along z: intensity is proportional to sin2χ, where χ is the angle from z. There is no radiation on the dipole axis and a maximum in the xy plane. Continuous homogeneous dielectric. One must sum amplitudes coherently and include their spatial phases, rather than retain the preceding sum of intensities. Random orientations give ⟨uuT⟩=I/3, so the averaged response is isotropic and its induced polarization is along z. Its coherent radiation toward y, when allowed by the sample geometry, is therefore purely z polarized, with Ix​=0. The independent-needle prefactor is no longer a quantitative dense-medium model; local-field and collective effects determine the effective permittivity.
There is also coherent continuum phase matching: a uniform bulk driven by a plane wave has no arbitrary outgoing 90∘ plane-wave component. In an ideal infinite homogeneous medium there is no diffuse side scattering at all; propagation, refraction and reflection are determined by the medium and its interfaces. A finite sample's coherent side signal depends on its shape and boundaries. Thus the old 1:3 diffuse ratio is replaced by a coherent isotropic-medium response, not by a universal nonzero N2 side-scattering intensity.
