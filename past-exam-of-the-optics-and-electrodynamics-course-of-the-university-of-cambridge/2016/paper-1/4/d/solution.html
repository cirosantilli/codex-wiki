Unpolarized incident light has equal, mutually incoherent y- and z-polarized intensities. Both components scatter into the same direction v, so their coherency matrices add to
Js​=2KI0​​(uy2​+uz2​)(ux2​ux​uz​​ux​uz​uz2​​).
(31)
This matrix has rank one when the signal is nonzero. The light is therefore fully linearly polarized along v, with
Iunpol​=2Ia​+Ib​​=IR​.​
(32)
A fixed highly anisotropic needle can act as a polarizing scatterer even for unpolarized input. The equality of total intensities with (c) does not mean the incident states were identical. These conclusions use the leading longitudinal-polarizability approximation; finite transverse response can change the polarization purity.
