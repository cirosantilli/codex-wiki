Cohomological Gysin map of an embedding (i!​:Hj(N;R)→Hj+r(M;R))

OurBigBook.com Words: 108
For a closed smooth embedding i:N↪M of codimension r whose normal bundle is oriented over a coefficient ring R, a tubular neighborhood, excision and the Thom isomorphism theorem identify
Hq−r(N;R)≅Hq(M,M∖N;R).
(40)
The relative-to-absolute map defines i!​:Hj(N;R)→Hj+r(M;R). For closed oriented M,N, it is characterized by
⟨i!​b⌣c,[M]⟩=⟨b⌣i∗c,[N]⟩
(41)
with compatible orientations. It obeys i!​(b⌣i∗c)=i!​b⌣c. If j:M∖N↪M and δ:Hq(M∖N;R)→Hq+1−r(N;R) is the connecting map followed by the inverse Thom isomorphism, then, over F2​,
δ(j∗c⌣u)=i∗c⌣δu.
(42)
These identities follow from naturality of the relative cup product and show how the exact sequence determines multiplication in complements. Over F2​ every real normal bundle has the required orientation.
