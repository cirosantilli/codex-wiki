Finite full-support measure-preserving map is bijective

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Ergodic theory Measure-preserving transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Let X be a finite set, let every singleton have positive probability, and let T:X→X preserve that probability measure. For every x∈X,
μ(T−1{x})=μ({x})>0,
(21)
so every x has a preimage. Thus T is a surjection, and every surjection from a finite set to itself is a bijection.
