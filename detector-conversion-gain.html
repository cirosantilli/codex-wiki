Detector conversion gain (g)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Photodetector
OurBigBook.com Words: 161 Articles: 2
Detector conversion gain is the number of collected Electrons per analogue-to-digital unit. With independent Poisson distribution photoelectron counts, the shot-noise variance in count units is the mean count divided by the gain. Detected-photon and Electron conventions agree only when each detected photon contributes one Electron.
