Contingent claim

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fundamental theorem of asset pricing
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 56
A contingent claim is a future payoff whose value depends on uncertain market outcomes. A replicable claim has the unique no-arbitrage price of its replicating portfolio.
