Verification by a nonnegative control supermartingale

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Dynamic programming Bellman equation Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For a nonnegative classical solution V of a finite-horizon Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation with nonnegative running reward r, the process V(Xt​,t)+∫0t​r(Xs​,as​)ds is a local supermartingale under every admissible control. The Itô formula gives nonpositive drift because the chosen control cannot exceed the supremum in the equation. Stop where the stochastic integral becomes a true martingale, take expectations and apply the Fatou lemma. Nonnegativity prevents loss of a lower bound when removing the stops, giving the verification upper bound. A maximizing control attains the bound when its stopped reward processes are uniformly integrable.
