For spherical fragments of common mass density ρ, with n(D)=KD−α between specified diameter cutoffs, mass normalization of a power-law size distribution and integration of the geometric cross-section give
σtot​=2ρ3m​α−34−α​Dmax4−α​−Dmin4−α​Dmin3−α​−Dmax3−α​​,3<α<4.
(17)
Indeed m=(πρK/6)∫D3−αdD and σtot​=(πK/4)∫D2−αdD; eliminating K proves the formula. If both cutoff ratios raised to α−3 and 4−α are small, respectively, this reduces to 3m(4−α)Dmin3−α​Dmaxα−4​/[2ρ(α−3)]. Approaching either endpoint requires the corresponding logarithmic integral.
