Random relabelling is not independent right censoring

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis Censoring (statistics) Independent censoring
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
Suppose the recorded durations retain an exponential distribution of rate θ, but an independent fraction q are relabelled as failures and the rest as right-censored. Applying the ordinary survival likelihood gives θ=d/∑i​yi​→qθ, rather than θ. A censoring flag chosen independently of the recorded duration does not establish independent censoring of a latent failure time. Genuine simulation instead draws an event time and an independent censoring time and records their minimum.
