Integrating factor depending on one coordinate (μ′/μ=(Py​−Qx​)/Q)

 ... Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Linear ordinary differential equation Integrating factor Integrating factor for a differential one-form
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For a differential one-form Pdx+Qdy, seek a nonzero integrating factor μ(x). Expanding (μP)y​=(μQ)x​ gives the displayed equation where Q=0. Such a factor exists locally when (Py​−Qx​)/Q depends only on x, and is μ=exp∫[(Py​−Qx​)/Q]dx. Likewise, a factor depending only on y obeys μ′/μ=(Qx​−Py​)/P where P=0. The resulting product is an exact differential form on a sufficiently small regular domain; singular lines of the factor need separate treatment.
