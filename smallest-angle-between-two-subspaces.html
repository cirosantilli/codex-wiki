Smallest angle between two subspaces (cosϑ=∥PQ∥)

OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
For two finite-dimensional subspaces with projections P,Q, define cosϑ=sup∥u∥=∥v∥=1​∣⟨u,v⟩∣=∥PQ∥, with vectors drawn from the respective subspaces. The angle is zero when they intersect nontrivially. To measure separation after removing a common intersection, restrict both subspaces to its orthogonal complement. The resulting angle controls sums of positive operators in the Kitaev geometrical lemma.
