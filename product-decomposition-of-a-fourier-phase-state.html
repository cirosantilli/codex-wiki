Product decomposition of a Fourier phase state (N−1/2∑l​eiϕl∣l⟩=⨂j=1n​2​∣0⟩+eiϕ2n−j∣1⟩​)

Write the integer label in most-significant-bit-first order as l=∑j​bj​2n−j. The phase factors as eiϕl=∏j​eiϕbj​2n−j, giving the displayed product state. For dyadic Fourier phases this explains why the Fourier basis states are unentangled even though a general quantum Fourier transform can create entanglement.
