Calcium intake

 Home Biology Calcium in biology
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
Calcium intake is the amount of calcium consumed, including food and supplements. It is an exposure that can be changed by an intervention, and should not be identified automatically with serum calcium in a causal inference analysis.
