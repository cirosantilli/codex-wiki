Local-to-global gap in isogeny descent

 ... Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Mordell-Weil group Kummer map of an elliptic curve Isogeny Selmer group
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
Coverings surviving all local solubility tests need not have rational points. The quotient of an isogeny Selmer group by the rational-point image is a kernel in the Tate–Shafarevich group. A nonzero class therefore passes every local test but contributes no rational point to a search. This explains why two-isogeny descent can leave a gap between a certified upper bound and found lower bound for the rank of an elliptic curve. Higher descents address finer obstructions; see Tom Fisher's paper on higher descents with rational two-torsion.
