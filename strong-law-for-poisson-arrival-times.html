Strong law for Poisson arrival times (Yn​/n→1/λ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Poisson process
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
For a homogeneous Poisson process of rate λ>0, N(t)/t→λ almost surely: unit-time increments are independent Poisson variables and the strong law of large numbers applies, then monotonicity fills the intervals between integers. At its nth point Yn​, simplicity gives N(Yn​)=n. Consequently Yn​/n→1/λ. Rate and mean interarrival time are reciprocals.
