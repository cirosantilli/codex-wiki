Use the question's indexing convention: mλ,μ​ is the multiplicity of the irreducible Specht module SCμ​ in the permutation module MCλ​. In notation for semistandard-tableau Kostka numbers this is often written Kμλ​, with the indices reversed.
Let Sλ​=Sλ1​​×Sλ2​​×⋯ be the Young subgroup. The permutation character is
ξλ=IndSλ​Sn​​1,
(4)
and ξλ(g) counts the λ-tabloids fixed by g. Equivalently it counts ordered partitions into labelled rows of the prescribed sizes, each row invariant under g. Character inner products and Frobenius reciprocity give the purely character-theoretic computation
mλ,μ​=⟨ξλ,χμ​⟩Sn​​=∣Sλ​∣1​h∈Sλ​∑​χμ​(h).​
(5)
The characters of symmetric groups are real-valued, so no conjugation is needed in the displayed subgroup average.
For λ=(2,13), the Young subgroup has two elements, the identity and one transposition. Thus m(2,13),μ​=(χμ​(1)+χμ​((12)))/2, and part (a) gives
μm(2,13),μ​​(5)1​(4,1)3​(3,2)3​(3,1,1)3​(2,2,1)2​(2,13)1​(15)0​​​
(6)
The resulting dimension is 1+3⋅4+3⋅5+3⋅6+2⋅5+4=60=5!/2, the dimension of the permutation module.
This is the character average for Kostka numbers.
