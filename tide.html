Tide

 ... Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field Tidal tensor Tidal force
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 4
A tide is a periodic change in ocean level driven mainly by the differential gravitational forces of the Moon and Sun, together with the Earth's rotation. Their tidal forces act across the extended planet; a uniform acceleration shared by the whole Earth does not by itself generate a tide. Ocean-basin resonance and coastal geometry modify the local response.
