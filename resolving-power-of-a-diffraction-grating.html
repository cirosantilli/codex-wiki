Resolving power of a diffraction grating (R=λ/δλ=∣m∣N)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Diffraction grating
OurBigBook.com Words: 182 Articles: 2
For N coherently illuminated grating lines used in diffraction order m, the Rayleigh criterion yields the displayed ideal resolving power of a diffraction grating. The angular maximum-to-first-zero separation is λ/(NDcosθm​), while nearby wavelengths are separated by ∣m∣δλ/(Dcosθm​). The order must exist, carry appreciable light intensity and remain free from dominant instrumental broadening. Line count alone therefore does not determine whether a particular instrument resolves a specified doublet.
