Strict positive covariance with an increasing payoff (Cov(g(Z),Z)>0)

Conditional covariance (Cov(X,Z∣G))

This observation underlies recovery of a martingale-transform integrand by conditional covariance.

Uncorrelated random variables

Bernoulli independence from zero covariance (Cov(X,Y)=ad−bc)

Words: 65
Uncorrelated random variables OurBigBook.com
For two Bernoulli random variables with joint probabilities a,b,c,d in the order (0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1), their covariance is d−(c+d)(b+d)=ad−bc. Zero covariance fixes the (1,1) probability to the product of its marginals; subtracting this from each marginal fixes all three remaining joint probabilities to their products. Thus uncorrelated random variables taking only zero and one are independent, including degenerate marginals. The implication is special to this two-point setting and fails for general distributions.

Correlation matrix (C=D−1/2ΣD−1/2)

Words: 62
Covariance matrix OurBigBook.com
For a random vector with covariance Σ and positive coordinate variances, let D=diag(Σ). Its correlation matrix is the covariance of D−1/2(X−EX), so it is positive semidefinite with unit diagonal. Each entry is the correlation of the corresponding pair of variables. In particular tr(C)=p, explaining why an eigenvalue in correlation-based principal component analysis represents that eigenvalue divided by p of the total variance. The empirical version is the sample correlation matrix.

Words: 37
Covariance matrix OurBigBook.com
This unit-variance covariance matrix has eigenvalue 1+(p−1)r on the constant-vector direction and eigenvalue 1−r on its orthogonal complement. It is positive semidefinite exactly when −1/(p−1)≤r≤1. For positive r, the first population principal component is the normalized sum of the centered variables.

Mahalanobis distance (dM​(x,y)=(x−y)TΣ−1(x−y)​)

Words: 52
Covariance matrix OurBigBook.com
For a positive-definite covariance matrix, this is Euclidean distance after whitening. It accounts for both scales and correlations and is invariant under consistent nonsingular linear changes of coordinates. Estimating the covariance can be unstable with small samples, and a singular covariance requires restricting to a nonsingular subspace rather than claiming an ordinary metric on the full space.

Compound-symmetry covariance (Σ=aI+cJ)

Words: 69
Covariance matrix OurBigBook.com
A compound-symmetry covariance matrix has a common diagonal entry and a common off-diagonal entry: Σ=aI+cJ. For a group of size k, its within-group eigenvalue is a and its constant-direction eigenvalue is a+kc. Thus a≥0 and a+kc≥0 characterize positive semidefiniteness. When c≥0, an independent shared random intercept plus individual noise realizes this covariance. A group mean has variance c+a/k; a difference of two disjoint subgroup means within the same group cancels c.

Terminal proportionality identifies bounded stock volatility
Words: 54
Perfect positive correlation OurBigBook.com
For two strictly positive zero-rate stock martingales with bounded volatilities and the same initial value, proportional terminal prices must be equal because their expectations coincide. Conditional expectation then makes the whole paths equal. Comparing their stochastic integrals by the Itô isometry gives equality of the volatility coefficients almost everywhere in time and probability. Perfect positive correlation alone does not provide the required proportionality.
