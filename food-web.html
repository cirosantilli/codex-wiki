Food web

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology
OurBigBook.com Words: 219 Articles: 3
A food web records resource-consumer interactions among species. A linear food chain is a special case. Positive interaction coefficients alone do not imply persistence: the per-capita growth balances must admit feasible positive long-term mean abundances.
