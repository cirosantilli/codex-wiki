Cyclotron resonance

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Landau level Cyclotron frequency
OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 1
Cyclotron resonance is resonant absorption by charged particles in a magnetic field when an applied oscillating electric field drives their cyclotron motion. For a parabolic band, the resonance frequency is ωc​=∣q∣B/m∗, so measuring it determines the cyclotron effective mass. In the quantum description, transverse electric dipole transitions connect adjacent Landau levels, whose separation is ℏωc​. For an electron and a field along z, only the circular polarization rotating in the electron's orbital sense drives the positive-frequency resonance. Scattering broadens the absorption peak; resolving it requires an orbital period short compared with the relaxation time.
