Santiago Ramón y Cajal (1852–1934)

 Home Biology Neuroscience Neuroscientist Neuroanatomist List of neuroanatomists
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
His drawings of neurons made their branching structure visible with extraordinary clarity. The historical illustration under Purkinje cell shows how different neurons can occupy the same part of the cerebellum while having strikingly different shapes.
