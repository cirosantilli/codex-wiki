Diffraction envelope (sinc2(πbsinθ/λ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Fraunhofer diffraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
A diffraction envelope is the angular factor imposed by the shape and width of each opening in an interference pattern. For two equally illuminated rectangular slits of width b and separation s, Fraunhofer diffraction gives I(θ)=4Is​sinc2(πbsinθ/λ)cos2(πssinθ/λ), where sincu=sinu/u and Is​ is one slit's on-axis light intensity. The first factor limits the observable angular range even when the rapid interference fringes remain perfectly coherent.
