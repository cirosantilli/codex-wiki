Finite-buffer server utilization (1−P0​=λμ(1−Pn​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory Server utilization
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For a work-conserving single-server queue with independent Poisson arrivals, finite buffer and independent identically distributed admitted service times of mean μ, Poisson arrivals see time averages give admitted rate λ(1−Pn​). Little law for the server alone then gives the displayed busy fraction. The time parameter is the mean service duration, and need not be exponentially distributed.
