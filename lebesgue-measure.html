Lebesgue measure

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Sigma-finite uniqueness theorem for measures
OurBigBook.com Words: 45 Articles: 1
Lebesgue measure is the unique translation-invariant σ-finite Borel measure on R normalized by λ((0,1])=1. Translation invariance follows by comparing translated measure with λ on half-open intervals and applying uniqueness.
