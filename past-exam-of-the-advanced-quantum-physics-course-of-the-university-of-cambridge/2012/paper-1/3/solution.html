For a spinless particle, minimal coupling gives H=(p−qA)2/(2m)+qϕ. Choose the vector potential A=(0,αx2/2,0) and ϕ=0. Its curl is B=αxz^ and [py​,Ay​]=0, so
H=2m1​[px2​+(py​−2qαx2​)2].​
(13)
The translation separation in a magnetic field gradient follows from [H,py​]=0: a simultaneous energy eigenstate can be written Ψ=Ly−1/2​eikyΦk​(x). The periodic boundary condition requires eikLy​=1, hence k=2πn/Ly​, n∈Z. The remaining Schrödinger equation is
[−2mℏ2​dx2d2​+Vk​(x)]Φk​=Ek​Φk​,Vk​(x)=2m1​(ℏk−2gx2​)2,g=qα.​
(14)
This magnetic-gradient quartic confinement has a positive quartic coefficient for g=0. If k/g<0, it is a single well at zero, with Vk​(0)=ℏ2k2/(2m). Its harmonic curvature gives
ωs​=m−ℏgk​​,Φ0​∝e−x2/(2as2​),Φ1​∝xe−x2/(2as2​),as​=mωs​ℏ​​.
(15)
Thus the two lowest states are respectively even and odd, with one central peak or two opposite-sign lobes, and approximately Ej​=Vk​(0)+(j+1/2)ℏωs​. They are not a nearly degenerate pair.
If k/g>0, the two minima are x=±x0​, x0​=2ℏk/g​, with zero potential there and a central barrier ℏ2k2/(2m). For well-separated minima the lowest parity states of a symmetric double well are
Φ0​≃2(1+s)​ϕL​+ϕR​​,Φ1​≃2(1−s)​ϕL​−ϕR​​,s=⟨ϕL​∣ϕR​⟩≪1.​
(16)
Here ϕL​,ϕR​ are the localized ground-state Gaussians. Their even combination is the lower state; the odd combination has a node at the origin. Weak quantum tunnelling gives an exponentially small double-well tunneling splitting.
To obtain their common leading energy, put x=x0​+δx. Since ℏk=gx02​/2,
Vk​(x0​+δx)=2mg2x02​​(δx)2+O((δx)3),ωd​=m∣g∣x0​​=m2ℏgk​​.
(17)
The double-well oscillator scale in a linear magnetic field therefore gives
Ek,0​≃Ek,1​≃2ℏωd​​=m1​2ℏ3qαk​​.​
(18)
The asymptotic condition is x0​≫ℏ/(mωd​)​, so the barrier separates narrow local oscillator states. It is this scale comparison that makes the source's “large” ratio meaningful.
The signed group velocity of a magnetic-gradient doublet follows by differentiating the band energy with respect to the conserved wavevector:
vy​=ℏ1​dkdEk​​≃2ℏkEk​​=2m2ℏqαk​ℏqα​.​
(19)
For qα>0 the double-well branch has k>0 and moves along +y; reversing both signs reverses its velocity. To this order the two parity states have the same velocity. Corrections include local anharmonicity and the exponentially small splitting. The spinless Hamiltonian specified here has no extra Zeeman effect term.
