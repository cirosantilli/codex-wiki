Starting instead in ψ1​, which is odd about the centre, the pulse can transfer amplitude to the ground state and to even-labelled excited states ψ2​,ψ4​,…. Other odd-labelled states have zero first-order dipole amplitude. Both downward and upward transitions are possible; their relative probabilities depend on dipole matrix elements and the pulse's spectral weight at the corresponding energy gaps. Most population remains in ψ1​, with a small depletion equal to the summed leading transition probabilities. The common diagonal dipole phase in an infinite well is −eE0​wτ/(2ℏ) and has no population effect. After the pulse, the remaining coherent superposition evolves under the unperturbed Hamiltonian; no spontaneous relaxation is implied by this closed-system model.
