By addition of angular momentum, combining the first two spin one-half particles gives 0⊕1. Adding the third gives the three spin-one-half angular-momentum decomposition
(21​)⊗3=(0⊕1)⊗21​=21​⊕21​⊕23​.
(1)
Thus there is one S=3/2 total-spin sector and two independent S=1/2 sectors. Since the particles are distinguishable, no fermionic exchange symmetry restriction removes either copy. In a simultaneous angular momentum eigenstate, S^2 and S^(z) have eigenvalues ℏ2S(S+1) and ℏM, so
ES,M​=ϵt​S(S+1)+ϵz​M.​
(2)
The energy levels and their generic multiplicities are
S3/21/2​M−3/2,−1/2,1/2,3/2−1/2,1/2​ES,M​15ϵt​/4+ϵz​M3ϵt​/4+ϵz​M​multiplicity1 for each M2 for each M​​
(3)
The count 4+2×2=8 agrees with the dimension of the three-particle tensor product. These multiplicities must be added whenever numerical energy eigenvalues coincide. In particular, for ϵz​=0 and ϵt​=0, there are two fourfold degenerate energy levels, 15ϵt​/4 and 3ϵt​/4. For ϵt​=0 and ϵz​=0, the multiplicities at M=−3/2,−1/2,1/2,3/2 are 1,3,3,1. If both coefficients vanish, the single energy level has multiplicity eight. Other coincidences between the two total-spin sectors occur when 3ϵt​+(M−m)ϵz​=0, with M from the quartet and m=±1/2 from a doublet.
