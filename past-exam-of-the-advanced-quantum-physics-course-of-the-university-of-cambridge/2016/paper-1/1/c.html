Using a spherical Bohr-radius volume for the atom, the calculations below give a cutoff electromagnetic vacuum energy density of about 6.2×1015Jm−3, compared with a hydrogen binding-energy density of about 3.5×1012Jm−3. The cutoff vacuum estimate is about 1.8×103 times larger. Alternative reasonable definitions of atomic volume change this ratio by a factor of a few, not the main order-of-magnitude conclusion.
The cutoff result is a bare zero-point mode sum. It is not a classical radiation bath incident on the atom, nor a calculation of an observable absolute vacuum energy without a subtraction or renormalization prescription.
