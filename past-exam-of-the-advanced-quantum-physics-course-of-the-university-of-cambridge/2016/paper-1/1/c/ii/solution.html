Take the hydrogen ground-state binding-energy magnitude as EB​≃13.6eV=2.18×10−18J, and use a0​≃5.29×10−11m as its characteristic radius. A definite volume convention is
VH​=34πa03​​≃6.21×10−31m3.
(8)
It gives
uH​=VH​EB​​≃3.51×1012Jm−3,uH​uvac​​≃1.78×103.​
(9)
A quantum atom has no sharp outer surface. If instead its characteristic volume is taken as a03​, the estimate is 1.47×1013Jm−3 and the ratio is about 4.2×102. Both conventions place the two densities roughly three orders of magnitude apart at order-of-magnitude accuracy. The comparison uses the magnitude of the binding energy, not its negative sign relative to ionization.
