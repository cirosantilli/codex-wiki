This dispersionless chiral interface wavepacket therefore translates rigidly with speed c in that direction, without dispersion in this exactly linear model. It remains confined in y; there is no free transverse group velocity, because the bound-state family is not labelled by a transverse travelling-wave momentum.
The continuity equation gives the current components jx​=cΨ†σx​Ψ=−c∣Ψ∣2 and jy​=cΨ†σy​Ψ=0. This explicitly confirms absence of probability transport away from the interface. For the step-mass case the profile is normalizable because its tails have positive decay constants and c>0. A general sign-changing profile also requires F∈L2(R); nonzero asymptotic masses suffice, whereas signs alone do not guarantee confinement if their integrated magnitudes grow too slowly. If c<0, the supplied exponential instead grows at infinity; a normalizable state requires the opposite spinor and envelope sign. Reversing the wall orientation also reverses the propagation chirality.
