For two equivalent p electrons, antisymmetry permits
3P0,1,2​,1D2​,1S0​.​
(3)
Their 9+5+1=15 states exhaust (26​). Hund's rules first maximize spin, then orbital angular momentum; the less-than-half-filled p shell puts the smallest J lowest. Hence the ground level is 3P0​. This is the allowed LS terms of a p-squared configuration.[ref]
