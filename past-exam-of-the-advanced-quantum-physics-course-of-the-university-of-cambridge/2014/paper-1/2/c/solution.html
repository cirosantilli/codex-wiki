A laser combines pumping, an amplifying medium and optical feedback. Stimulated emission adds photons to an occupied optical mode; net amplification requires population inversion between the relevant levels after accounting for their degeneracies. Pumping through additional levels and rapid lower-level relaxation can sustain inversion; a closed two-level system under steady incoherent resonant pumping cannot sustain it.
