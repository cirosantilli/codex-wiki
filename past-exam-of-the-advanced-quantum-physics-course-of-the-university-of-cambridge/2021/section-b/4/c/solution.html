With Ω=γBx​/2, the Born rule applied to the supplied down-spin probability amplitude gives
P↓​(t)=δ2+Ω2Ω2​sin2(2δ2+Ω2​t​).

For nonzero transverse field, the maxima occur at
tj​=ωR​(2j+1)π​,j=0,1,…,Pmax​(ω)=(ω−ωz​)2+Ω2Ω2​.​

The first maximum is reached at π/ωR​; on resonance this is a Rabi pi pulse. The resonance envelope of a Rabi transition is a Lorentzian line shape, centred on ωres​=ωz​ under the orientation convention above. It has half its peak value at ∣ω−ωz​∣=∣Ω∣, so its full width at half maximum is
Δω=2∣Ω∣=∣γBx​∣,Δωωres​​=∣Bx​∣∣Bz​∣​.​
(18)
If resonance width is instead defined as the half-width, the ratio differs by a factor of two but has the same requested proportionality. The quality factor is large precisely in the regime where the rotating-wave approximation is justified. The envelope is obtained by choosing the best time separately at each drive frequency; a measurement at one fixed pulse duration has a different line profile.
