Parallelization of a Lie group by left translations (Xa​(g)=(dLg​)e​Ta​)

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Left and right translation on a Lie group Left-invariant vector field
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
A basis Ta​ of the Lie algebra of a Lie group gives a global frame of a vector bundle of its tangent bundle by Xa​(g)=(dLg​)e​Ta​. Left translation is a diffeomorphism, so the left-invariant vector fields are independent everywhere. Every Lie group is therefore a parallelizable manifold.
