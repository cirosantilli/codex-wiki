Hubble constant (H0​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Scale factor (cosmology) Expansion of the universe Hubble parameter
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
The Hubble constant is the present value H0​ of the Hubble parameter. It sets the local proportionality between recession velocity and distance in the Hubble law.
