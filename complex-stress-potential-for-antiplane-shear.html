Complex stress potential for antiplane shear (σ23​+iσ13​=μF′(z))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Antiplane shear
OurBigBook.com Words: 434 Articles: 5
A harmonic conjugate ϕ satisfying ϕ,1​=w,2​ and ϕ,2​=−w,1​ makes F=ϕ+iw a holomorphic function. Its derivative packages the two antiplane shear stresses into the displayed complex field. On multiply connected domains a global potential additionally requires the appropriate periods to vanish; prescribed displacement jumps can instead be represented by branches of the potential.
