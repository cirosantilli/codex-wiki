Representation of an associative algebra (ρ:A→Endk​(V))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 375 Articles: 6
An algebra representation is a unital algebra homomorphism into the endomorphisms of a vector space. It is equivalent to making that vector space a left module over the algebra, with av=ρ(a)v.
