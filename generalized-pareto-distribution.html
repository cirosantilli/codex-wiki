Generalized Pareto distribution (Gξ,σ​(y))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Extreme value theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 237 Articles: 4
For scale σ>0 and shape ξ, the generalized Pareto distribution on nonnegative excesses has cumulative distribution function
Gξ,σ​(y)=1−(1+ξy/σ)−1/ξ,y≥0,1+ξy/σ>0.
(1)
The case ξ=0 is the continuous limit 1−e−y/σ. Positive ξ gives a power-law tail, zero gives an exponential distribution, and negative ξ gives a finite upper endpoint −σ/ξ. Its role in modelling high-threshold excesses is explained by the Pickands-Balkema-de Haan theorem.
