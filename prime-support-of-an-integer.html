Prime support of an integer (P(n)={p prime:p∣n})

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Arithmetic function Radical of an integer
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The prime support of an integer n>0 is the finite set of its distinct prime factors. For example, P(72)=P(36)={2,3}, P(30)={2,3,5} and P(1) is the empty set. Its cardinality is the prime omega function ω(n), and the product of its elements is the radical of an integer rad(n). These are two different ways to summarize the same finite set: a count and a product.
