Fixed-threshold value of a perpetual call (CB​(S)=(B−K)(S/B)p,0≤S≤B)

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance American option American call option Perpetual American call option
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
For the perpetual American call option model and a fixed exercise threshold B>K, this is the discounted expectation of B−K at its first hit, with zero payoff if the hit never occurs. The stationary ordinary differential equation and boundary conditions CB​(0)=0, CB​(B)=B−K prove the formula. At fixed S<B, differentiation gives ∂B​CB​=SpB−p−1[pK−(p−1)B], so the unique maximizing threshold is pK/(p−1). If a larger threshold is imposed, CB′​(B−)>1 and its value lies below immediate exercise payoff just below B. This is allowed for a committed policy, but violates the exercise lower bound if quoted for an unrestricted American call option.
