An ideal complete Bell-basis measurement, followed by outcome-dependent Pauli gates, produces a chosen Bell state with white-noise visibility v1​v2​: either white-noise input makes the remote pair maximally mixed. If the reported label is reliable with probability q and otherwise uniformly random, averaging the erroneous corrections makes that branch maximally mixed too, so vout​=v1​v2​q. A second independent detector of the same quality during quantum teleportation adds another factor q to the teleported Bloch vector.
