Student's t-test

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical hypothesis test
OurBigBook.com Words: 215 Articles: 3
Student's t-test divides a normally distributed estimated effect by its estimated standard error. Under its null hypothesis the statistic has a Student's t-distribution, with degrees of freedom determined by the variance estimate.
