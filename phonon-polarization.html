Phonon polarization

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Bose-Einstein statistics Bose-Einstein distribution Phonon
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The displacement eigenvector of a lattice normal mode, including relative amplitudes and phases for the atoms in a primitive cell. Longitudinal and transverse describe its orientation relative to the phonon wavevector in simple isotropic cases.
