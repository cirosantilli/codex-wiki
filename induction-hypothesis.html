Induction hypothesis

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical proof Mathematical induction
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
The assertion assumed for earlier cases during an induction proof. It is a conditional assumption inside the induction step, not an assumption that all cases already hold.
