Radial volume transform of a homogeneous Poisson point process

For a homogeneous Poisson point process of intensity λ in Rd, mapping x to vd​∣x∣d, where vd​ is the unit-ball volume, gives a rate-λ Poisson process on [0,∞). Mapping to ∣x∣d instead gives rate λvd​.
