Diffuse-layer differential capacitance (CD​=εκD​cosh[eψ0​/(2kB​T)])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrostatics Poisson-Boltzmann equation Grahame equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
Differentiate the Grahame equation for a planar symmetric monovalent electrolyte with respect to surface potential at fixed reservoir concentration and temperature. The result is the displayed capacitance per unit area. It approaches εκD​ in the Debye–Hückel approximation. A compact surface layer can contribute a separate series capacitance; point-ion packing assumptions also fail at sufficiently high near-surface concentration.[ref]
