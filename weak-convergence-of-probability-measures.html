Weak convergence of probability measures

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Convergence of random variables Convergence in distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 421 Articles: 8
Probability measures μn​ on a metric space converge weakly to μ when ∫fdμn​→∫fdμ for every bounded continuous real function f.
