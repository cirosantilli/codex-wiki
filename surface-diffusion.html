Surface diffusion (Ds​Δs​C)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Surface tension Surfactant Insoluble surfactant
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
Surface diffusion transports particles along an interface down a concentration gradient. On an isotropic smooth interface with constant diffusivity Ds​, its flux is −Ds​∇s​C and its concentration source is Ds​Δs​C, using the surface gradient and surface Laplacian.
