Cognitive psychology

 Home Science Psychology
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 130
The study of how information is acquired, represented and used in tasks such as remembering, understanding and reasoning. Behavioral experiments constrain proposed processes; a plausible explanation must predict performance and errors, not merely name an internal faculty.
