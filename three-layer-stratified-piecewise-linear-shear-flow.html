Three-layer stratified piecewise-linear shear flow (Ui​=±ΔU/2, J=gΔρh/(ρ0​ΔU2))

This three-layer shear flow has U=ΔUz/h and density ρ0​ between z=±h/2. Above and below, the velocity is the corresponding constant ±ΔU/2, and the mass density is ρ0​∓Δρ/2. The density drops and vorticity jumps coincide at both interfaces. Its hydrodynamic stability involves coupled gravity-vorticity interface waves, unlike a profile with uniform shear extending through all three layers.
