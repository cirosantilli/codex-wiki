X-ray binary

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Binary star
OurBigBook.com Words: 216 Articles: 4
An X-ray binary has a compact mass-gaining star or black hole accreting from a companion, so that much of its emitted power appears as X-rays. Gravitational binding energy supplies an accretion luminosity of order GMM˙/R when there is a surface or an appropriate inner disk radius. The donor star may feed an accretion disk through Roche-lobe overflow or a stellar wind.
