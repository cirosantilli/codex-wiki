Bounded-degree bipartite Ramsey bound

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Probabilistic combinatorics Dependent random choice
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
If a bipartite graph H has k vertices and maximum degree d, then
R(H)=O(d2dk).
(2)
A dependent random choice argument finds, in one colour, enough vertices whose every subset of at most d vertices has a large common neighbourhood; a greedy embedding then places H.
