Evolution family (U(t,s))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis C0-semigroup Abstract Cauchy problem Nonautonomous abstract Cauchy problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 58 Articles: 1
An evolution family consists of bounded operators U(t,s) for s≤t satisfying U(s,s)=I and U(t,r)U(r,s)=U(t,s). For sufficiently regular data it solves ∂t​U(t,s)x=A(t)U(t,s)x and ∂s​U(t,s)x=−U(t,s)A(s)x.
