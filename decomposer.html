Decomposer

 Home Biology Ecology Ecosystem Consumer (food chain)
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A decomposer breaks down dead organic material and absorbs products of that breakdown; many bacteria and fungi perform this role. Decomposers connect detritus from different trophic levels to material recycling, so placing all decomposers at one final step of every food chain is misleading. See Clark, Douglas and Choi, Biology 2e, 46.1: grazing and detrital food webs.
