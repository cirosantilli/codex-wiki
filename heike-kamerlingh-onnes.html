Heike Kamerlingh Onnes (1853–1926)

 Home Physics Physicist List of physicists
OurBigBook.com Words: 30 Articles: 1
A physicist.[ref]
Helium liquefaction supplied a route to very low temperatures. The resistance-versus-temperature experiment for superconductivity and measurements of superconducting persistent current show how reaching those conditions opened new questions about electrical behavior.
