Scalar surface integral (∫S​fdS)

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus Surface integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 204 Articles: 2
A scalar surface integral integrates a scalar field against the unsigned surface area element. For a regular parametrization r(u,v), it is ∫f(r(u,v))∥ru​×rv​∥dudv. Reversing orientation does not change it. For example, the scalar surface integral of the constant one is the surface area; a signed flux instead integrates the normal component of a vector field.
