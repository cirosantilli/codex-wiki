For a periodic thin mucus gap h(x) translating as a backward wave relative to a force-free foot, put Ij​=∫0L​h−jdx. In the wave frame, the plane and foot have horizontal velocities V−U and V. A Couette-Poiseuille flow with periodic pressure has flux q=(V−U/2)I2​/I3​. Zero net plane force gives the displayed crawling speed. The leading viscous dissipation per wavelength is Φ=2μUVI1​.
