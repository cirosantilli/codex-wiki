Liquid-gas critical point ((Tc​,pc​,vc​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon
OurBigBook.com Words: 38 Articles: 1
The endpoint of a gas-liquid phase coexistence curve, where the distinct liquid and gas states merge. For a smooth fluid equation of state, the critical isotherm has pv​=pvv​=0 at this point; this thermodynamic meaning differs from a stationary critical point of an arbitrary function.
