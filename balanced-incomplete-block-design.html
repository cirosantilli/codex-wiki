Balanced incomplete block design ((t,b,r,k,λ))

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Design of experiments Blocks in experimental design Block design
OurBigBook.com Words: 184 Articles: 3
A binary block design has t treatments, b blocks of size k<t, every treatment in r blocks, and every distinct pair in λ blocks. Counting occurrences and pairs gives tr=bk and r(k−1)=λ(t−1). Its incidence matrix of a set system N satisfies NNT=(r−λ)I+λ11T. For statistical comparison of all treatment effects one also needs connectedness; λ>0 guarantees it, whereas singleton blocks with λ=0 do not.
