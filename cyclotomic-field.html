Cyclotomic field

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Cyclotomic polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 27
A cyclotomic field is generated by a root of unity. If K⊆C and ζn​ is primitive, every K-conjugate of ζn​ is a power ζna​ and already lies in K(ζn​). Thus K(ζn​)/K is Galois, and its Galois group embeds in (Z/nZ)×, so it is abelian.
