Pullback of a differential form (ϕ∗)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A smooth map ϕ:M→N pulls a differential form on N back to one on M by applying dϕ to each vector argument. Pullback commutes with wedge products and the exterior derivative.
It is the alternating-tensor case of pullback.
