Bayes risk

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Statistical decision theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 144 Articles: 3
For a prior π, the integrated risk is r(π,δ)=∫R(θ,δ)π(dθ). Its infimum over decision rules is the Bayes risk, attained by a Bayes rule when one exists.
