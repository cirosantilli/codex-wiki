Symplectic leaf

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Poisson manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
A symplectic leaf is a maximal connected immersed submanifold on which the Poisson bivector has constant full rank and therefore inverts to a symplectic form.
