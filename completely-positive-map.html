Completely positive map (CP map)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Positive linear map
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 64
A linear map Φ between matrix algebras is completely positive when Φ⊗idr​ is positive for every ancillary dimension r. In finite dimensions this is equivalent to positivity of its Choi matrix and to the existence of a Kraus representation.
