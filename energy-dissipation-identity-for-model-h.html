Energy dissipation identity for Model H (E˙=−∫[M∣∇μ∣2+η∣∇v∣2])

For constant positive order-parameter mobility M, dynamic viscosity η and mass density ρ, deterministic Model H dynamics dissipates the sum of composition free energy and kinetic energy:
dtd​[F+2ρ​∫∣v∣2]=−∫[M∣∇μ∣2+η∣∇v∣2]≤0.
(23)
Assume smooth fields and periodic or closed, no-work boundaries. Using the chemical potential of a composition field, integration by parts and incompressible flow gives F˙=∫ϕv⋅∇μ−M∫∣∇μ∣2. Dotting the momentum equation with velocity gives E˙kin​=−∫ϕv⋅∇μ−η∫∣∇v∣2. Pressure and convective work vanish under the boundary assumptions; the two capillary transfer terms cancel. This proves both the sign of the Korteweg force and its reversible exchange of energy with the composition field.
