Extremal Reissner-Nordstrom causal crossing (r˙2=E2−(1−M/r)2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole Reissner-Nordstrom spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
A neutral radial timelike geodesic of an extremal black hole has conserved specific energy E=(1−M/r)2t˙, giving the displayed radial equation. An infalling geodesic reaches r=M with finite nonzero r˙=−E and finite proper time. In ingoing coordinates, outgoing radial null curves have dr/dv=(1−M/r)2/2; those starting inside approach r=M only at infinite v and cannot return to the same exterior future null infinity. This distinguishes causal access from length on a static slice, and supplies an operational event horizon test even without strictly trapped round spheres.
