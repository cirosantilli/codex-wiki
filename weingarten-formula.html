Weingarten formula (DX​ξ=−Aξ​X+∇X⊥​ξ)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Second fundamental form Shape operator Shape operator in a normal direction
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The Weingarten formula decomposes the ambient derivative of a normal field into a tangential shape operator term and the normal connection. The minus sign corresponds to ⟨Aξ​X,Y⟩=⟨II(X,Y),ξ⟩. For the outward unit normal of a unit sphere, Aξ​=−I with this convention.
