Stokes–Einstein relation (D=kB​T/ζ)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Surface traction in translating-sphere Stokes flow Stokes's law
OurBigBook.com Words: 191 Articles: 3
The Stokes–Einstein relation connects translational diffusion and linear drag through D=kB​T/ζ. For an unbounded spherical particle, ζ=6πμa and D=kB​T/(6πμa).
