Thermodynamic state

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Thermodynamic system
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
An equilibrium thermodynamic state specifies sufficient independent macroscopic properties, together with material identity and constraints, to determine the other equilibrium properties. For a uniform fixed-composition ideal gas, its amount of substance, temperature and volume determine its pressure through the ideal gas law. These properties specify a state, without specifying a thermodynamic process that prepared it.[ref]
