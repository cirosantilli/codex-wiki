Three-level discount equilibrium with geometric annual counts (π=1−p2q(q2(1+p),pq,p2)​,p+q=1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics No claims discount system
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
Suppose annual claim counts are independent with geometric distribution mass pqj, j≥0. In a no claims discount system with discounts 0,α,β, a claim returns either of the first two levels to zero; at the top, one claim sends the level to α and two or more send it to zero. The transition matrix is (qqq2​p0pq​0pp​). Solving πP=π gives the displayed stationary distribution. With full premium c, the stationary expected value of the premium is c[1−(αpq+βp2)/(1−p2q)]. Independent annual sampling is essential; a persistent random intensity does not justify this marginal transition matrix.
