Bragg's law (2dsinθ=mλ)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering Scattering amplitude Crystal scattering Elastic Bragg scattering condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
Constructive interference between waves reflected by successive equally spaced lattice planes requires their path difference 2dsinθ to be an integer multiple of the wavelength λ. Here d is plane spacing, θ is the glancing angle and m is the diffraction order.
