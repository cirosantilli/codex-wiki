Maximum-weight matching

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Matching (graph theory)
OurBigBook.com Words: 118
Assign each edge e a real benefit we​. A maximum-weight matching maximizes ∑e∈M​we​ over all matchings in a graph M; the empty matching in a graph is allowed. Setting every we​=1 recovers a maximum cardinality matching, but otherwise the objectives can disagree.
On the same path graph P4​, give the middle edge benefit 3 and each outer edge benefit 1. The matching in a graph {(2,3)} has total benefit 3, while the two-edge matching in a graph {(1,2),(3,4)} has total benefit 2. Thus the smaller choice is the unique maximum-weight matching. If instead the requirement is to find a maximum cardinality matching and then maximize benefit among those, the outer edges must be chosen. Requiring a perfect matching before optimizing cost is another distinct problem, covered by minimum-weight perfect matching. With negative benefits a maximum-weight matching can even be empty, so it need not be a maximal matching.
