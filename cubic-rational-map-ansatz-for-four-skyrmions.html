Cubic rational-map ansatz for four Skyrmions (R(z)=z4−23​iz2+1z4+23​iz2+1​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 49
This degree-four rational map obeys R(iz)=1/R(z) and R((iz+1)/(1−iz))=e2πi/3R(z). These pair the generators of the rotational symmetry group of a cube with target rotations, giving combined spatial-isospin symmetry in the Skyrme model. Its spatial half-turn kernel is a Klein four-group. The map gives a cubic four-Skyrmion approximation; preserving only its Wronskian zero set does not guarantee the same rotational symmetry.
