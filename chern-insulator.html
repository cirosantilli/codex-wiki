Chern insulator (C=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Topological quantum matter
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 4
A Chern insulator is a two-dimensional band insulator whose occupied energy bands have a nonzero total Chern number. It has a bulk band gap, yet an interface with a topologically trivial band insulator carries a net excess of quantum Hall edge states propagating in one direction. Time-reversal symmetry must be broken. The Haldane model shows that a nonzero average magnetic flux is unnecessary.
Why distinguish two solids that both have a band gap? The energy eigenvalues tell us whether an excitation costs energy; the energy eigenstates also describe how the state changes around the Brillouin zone. Their Berry curvature can integrate to a nonzero integer. A small deformation changes the Berry curvature continuously, but cannot continuously change an integer. A Chern insulator and a trivial band insulator therefore cannot be connected through a path of isolated occupied energy bands with a common bulk band gap. The distinction survives sufficiently weak local perturbations. It does not make every microscopic quantity or every boundary state immune to every disturbance.
