Variable-mass system (mv˙=F+(u−v)m˙)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 210 Articles: 2
A body exchanging mass with material of inertial velocity u satisfies the displayed momentum conservation law for one stream, with signed m˙ positive for entry and negative for exit. The force F excludes the incoming or outgoing momentum flux. A short time interval gives d(mv)/dt=F+um˙, proving the formula. For stationary incoming matter, d(mv)/dt=F; for matter leaving at the body's own velocity, mv˙=F. The rocket equation and accreting-drop motion are applications.
