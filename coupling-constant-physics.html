OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A coefficient multiplying an interaction term controls its strength relative to the chosen field normalization. Its engineering dimension determines whether it is dimensionless, relevant or irrelevant. A renormalized coupling constant constant is specified at a renormalization scale; its change with that scale is described by the renormalization-group beta function.
