Global phase symmetry (φj​↦e−iqj​αφj​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field theory Global symmetry in field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 81 Articles: 1
An internal circle group action with spacetime-independent parameter α. For canonical complex scalar fields, Noether's theorem gives jμ=i∑j​qj​φj†​∂μφj​. The Noether charge of the particle annihilated by φj​ is qj​, and its antiparticle has charge −qj​.
