Phosphoinositide

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Lipid Phospholipid
OurBigBook.com Words: 90 Articles: 4
A phosphoinositide is a phosphorylated derivative of phosphatidylinositol. Phosphate position changes the recognition surface of the headgroup, allowing distinct species to recruit different membrane-associated proteins.
