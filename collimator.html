Collimator

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument
OurBigBook.com Words: 49 Articles: 1
A collimator turns light from a source near its focus into a nearly parallel beam. A slit of width x produces an angular width approximately x/f, where f is the focal length.
