Band offset (ΔEc​,ΔEv​)

 Home Physics Semiconductor Semiconductor heterostructure Heterojunction
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
A band offset is an interface discontinuity in a conduction band or valence band edge.[ref]
For a chosen two-material example, let the central material A have Ec,A​=1 and Ev,A​=0, and the surrounding material B have Ec,B​=1.6 and Ev,B​=−0.4, in stipulated energy units. Define positive confinement barriers by ΔEc​=Ec,B​−Ec,A​ and ΔEv​=Ev,A​−Ev,B​. Then ΔEc​=0.6, ΔEv​=0.4, and the band gaps obey
Eg,B​−Eg,A​=ΔEc​+ΔEv​=1.
(29)
This identity determines the sum of the band offsets; a band gap difference alone cannot determine their partition. The chosen numbers illustrate the definition and do not specify a measured material pair.
