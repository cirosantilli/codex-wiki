Differential scanning calorimetry

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Calorimetry
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
differential scanning calorimetry measures differences in heat flow between sample and reference during a controlled temperature scan. Transition peaks can reveal cooperative lipid-chain melting, with scan rate, composition and reversibility affecting interpretation.
