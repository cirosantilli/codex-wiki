Matrix-unit extraction lemma for special linear ideals

 ... Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Simple Lie algebra Special linear Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
Over a field of characteristic different from two, any Lie algebra ideal in sln​ containing a nonscalar matrix is the whole special linear Lie algebra. Indeed, if Aji​=0 for i=j, then [Eij​,[Eij​,A]]=−2Aji​Eij​ extracts a matrix unit. For a nonscalar diagonal matrix, [Eij​,A]=(Ajj​−Aii​)Eij​ extracts one instead. Its bracket with Eji​ produces H=Eii​−Ejj​, and [H,Eji​]=−2Eji​ extracts the reverse unit. Brackets [Eij​,Ejk​]=Eik​ and [Eki​,Eij​]=Ekj​ spread these units to every row and column; opposite pairs span all trace-zero diagonal matrices. Thus only ideals consisting of scalar matrices can be proper. This proof works even when restrictions of distinct roots of a root system to a Cartan subalgebra coincide in positive characteristic.
