Tensor component transformation law

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Covariant derivative Tensor type
OurBigBook.com Words: 116 Articles: 1
Under a coordinate change x↦x′, the components of a type-(r,s) tensor acquire one forward Jacobian for each contravariant index and one inverse Jacobian for each covariant index. For a type-(1,1) tensor,
Tμ′ν′​=∂xα∂xμ′​∂xν′∂xβ​Tαβ​.
