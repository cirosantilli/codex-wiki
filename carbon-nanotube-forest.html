Carbon nanotube forest

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Carbon Carbon nanotube
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A dense population of approximately aligned carbon nanotubes growing from a support. Collective crowding can favor alignment even when the support does not epitaxially select every tube orientation.
