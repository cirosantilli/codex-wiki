Rubber elasticity

 Home Chemistry Polymer Elastomer
OurBigBook.com Words: 287 Articles: 3
In a flexible crosslinked polymer network, deformation restricts the configurations of chain segments between cross-links. The resulting decrease of entropy produces a restoring entropic force. For an ideal affine Gaussian network, the shear modulus is G=νs​kB​T, where νs​ is the number density of elastically active strands. A nearly incompressible isotropic rubber has Young's modulus E≃3G. At fixed network density within the rubbery regime, both increase approximately linearly with absolute temperature. This statement does not describe crossing the glass transition or chemical degradation.
