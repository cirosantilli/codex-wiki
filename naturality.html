Naturality

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Functor Natural transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
For a natural transformation θ:F⇒G, naturality is the equation G(f)θA​=θB​F(f) for each morphism f:A→B. Thus transforming and then applying a functor gives the same result as applying that functor and then transforming. It makes the component maps a coherent transformation of functors.
