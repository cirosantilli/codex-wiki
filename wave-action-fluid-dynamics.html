Wave action (fluid dynamics, A=E/ω)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Wave activity
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 1
For a small-amplitude conservative wave packet in a slowly varying fluid flow, its intrinsic energy divided by intrinsic frequency is transported along its group velocity, with the basic advection added in the laboratory frame. A stationary background conserves laboratory angular frequency, but a changing mean velocity changes the intrinsic one through the Doppler effect; intrinsic energy alone need not be conserved. Geometrical spreading and dissipation enter the action-flux balance.
