Sublattice pseudospin in graphene (⟨σ⟩=(cosϕ,sinϕ,0))

 ... Chemical substance Chemical element Carbon Graphene Nearest-neighbour model of graphene Dirac valleys in graphene
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
At K+​ and positive energy, a normalized eigenvector for q=∣q∣(cosϕ,sinϕ) is (1,eiϕ)/2​. Its Pauli matrix expectation values give the displayed direction. This pseudospin records the relative amplitudes and phase on the two sublattices. Real spin supplies another label; confusing these two labels would double-count states and misinterpret the two-band Hamiltonian.
