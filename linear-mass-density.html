Linear mass density (μ=dm/dℓ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Density
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
Linear mass density is mass per unit length, measured in kgm−1. A uniform string of mass m and length L has μ=m/L; with constant tension T, its transverse wave speed is T/μ​. Mass density instead measures mass per unit volume.
