Particle Reynolds number (Rep​=2rρf​Ws​/μ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Suspension (chemistry) Settling velocity
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A Reynolds number based on particle diameter and relative speed in the carrier fluid. Small values justify Stokes drag law; a high-Reynolds-number regime with nearly constant drag coefficient instead gives quadratic drag. The drag law must be evaluated in the appropriate regime for every species.
