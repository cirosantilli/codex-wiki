Cross-link

 Home Chemistry Polymer
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A cross-link connects different segments of a polymer network and restricts their independent motion. Permanent chemical cross-links commonly use covalent bonds; physical associations can instead have finite lifetimes. A connected permanent network can sustain long-time shear stress, whereas an uncrosslinked melt eventually flows. Cross-link density and network structure influence elastomer stiffness.
