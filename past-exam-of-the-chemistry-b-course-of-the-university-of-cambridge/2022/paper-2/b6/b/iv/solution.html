Treat each semiquinonate ligand as a symmetric O,O chelate, with the free radical delocalized over the dioxolene framework. The ideal octahedral donor-connectivity structures are one trans form and two mirror-related cis forms, giving three stereoisomers. Trans refers to the two pyridine N donors, not to the two O atoms of a chelate, which must remain adjacent.
In the diagram, coloured curves join the two O donors belonging to one ligand. For an unambiguous coordinate specification, the trans form has Py at +z,−z, with chelate pairs (+x,+y) and (−x,−y). A cis pair has Py at +z,+x, with chelates (−z,+y) and (−x,−y); reflecting y↦−y gives the other cis form. Proper rotations cannot superpose those two cis arrangements. Ring conformations and pyridine rotations are not extra donor-connectivity isomers.
