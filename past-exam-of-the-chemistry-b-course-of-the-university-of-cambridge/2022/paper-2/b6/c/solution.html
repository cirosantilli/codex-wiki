Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge / 2022 / Paper 2 / B6 / c / Solution

 ... Chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge 2022 Paper 2 B6 c
OurBigBook.com Words: 231
Four equivalents of base remove the four carboxylic-acid protons from two ligand molecules, giving two dianionic pyridine-2,5-dicarboxylate linkers per Pt/Mn pair. The ring nitrogen and neighbouring 2-carboxylate supply a five-membered N,O chelate. A consistent chain has Pt(II), N2​O2​, with square planar molecular geometry, and Mn(II), O6​, with octahedral molecular geometry.
At each Pt, two ligands bind through their ring N and one O of the adjacent 2-carboxylate; their remote 5-carboxylates point toward successive Mn sites. At each Mn, a remote carboxylate from each of two neighbouring Pt-containing units binds through both O atoms. Two coordinated water molecules complete Mn's six-coordinate sphere. The repeating connectivity, with each bracket denoting a single organic linker, is
⋯Ptone linker[κ2(N,O):L2−:κ2(O,O)]​​Mn(H2​O)2​[κ2(O,O):L2−:κ2(N,O)]Pt⋯.
(7)
Each Pt has a second N,O chelate extending in the opposite chain direction; each Mn similarly receives two carboxylate chelates. The original donor-connectivity diagram makes those counts explicit.
Pt(II) is 5d8 and strongly favours square planar molecular geometry. Its affinity for the pyridyl N donor and the chelate effect select the mixed N,O pocket. Mn(II) is high-spin 3d5, is harder, and favours the carboxylate and water O donors; six-coordinate octahedral molecular geometry is natural in water. Charge balance per repeat is +2+2+2(−2)=0. A suitable repeat formulation is [PtMn(L)2​(H2​O)2​]n​. This is a justified proposed coordination chain, not a unique crystal structure established from stoichiometry alone; water content, carboxylate bridging modes and actual porosity require structural characterization.
