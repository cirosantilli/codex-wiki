A reasonable product is sodium facial trimetaphosphate–molybdenum tricarbonyl, with the anion
[Mo(CO)3​(κ3O,O′,O′′-P3​O9​)]3−,gas=3CO.​
(19)
One terminal O from each phosphorus binds Mo; the three O donors occupy one octahedral face, while the three carbonyls occupy the opposite face. The ring bridges remain P–O–P bridges and are not replaced by Mo–P bonds. Mo remains Mo(0), d6, and 6+3(2)+3(2)=18 electrons. Overall,
Na3​P3​O9​+Mo(CO)6​⟶Na3​[Mo(CO)3​(P3​O9​)]+3CO.
(20)
acetonitrile can transiently replace CO during heating, but it is not required in the final six-coordinate proposal. The same anion has been structurally characterized with a different counterion: that primary study independently supports the facial O3​/CO3​ connectivity, without establishing identical solubility or yield for the sodium salt.[ref]
