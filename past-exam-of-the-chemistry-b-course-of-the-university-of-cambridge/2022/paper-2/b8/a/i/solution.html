 ... Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge 2022 Paper 2 B8 a i
OurBigBook.com Words: 147
Choose z along M–N–N. The occupied dinitrogen 3σg​ orbital, with amplitude on the terminal N atoms, overlaps an empty metal orbital of sigma symmetry to give N2​-to-metal sigma donation. Filled metal dxz​ and dyz​ orbitals separately overlap the two empty N2​ πg∗​ orbitals, giving metal-to-N2​ pi backbonding. The lower-energy combination of each interacting pair is bonding and the higher is antibonding; each donation channel starts with two electrons in the occupied fragment orbital. The lower panel of the original orbital diagram shows these three channels.
As for a terminal carbonyl ligand, the donor and acceptor interactions reinforce one another: sigma donation helps make electron density available at the metal, while pi backbonding strengthens M–N and weakens N–N. For conventional electron counting an intact end-on neutral N2​ ligand is an L-type two-electron donor; counting the backbonded electrons a second time as extra donated ligand electrons would double count them.
