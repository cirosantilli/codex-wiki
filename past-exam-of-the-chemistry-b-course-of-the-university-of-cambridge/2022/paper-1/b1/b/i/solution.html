Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge / 2022 / Paper 1 / B1 / b / i / Solution

 ... Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge 2022 Paper 1 B1 b i
OurBigBook.com Words: 216
Write t=C(CH3​)3​. D is the sodium enolate t−C(O−)=CH2​ Na+. Its two resonances in the alkene-proton region belong to the two hydrogens of the terminal methylene group. One is cis to oxygen and the other trans to oxygen, so they are not related by a symmetry operation even though both belong to the same carbon. Replacing one or the other gives different alkene geometries, so they are diastereotopic. Their weak or unresolved geminal coupling is consistent with the reported singlet descriptions. Without an additional spatial assignment, it is not justified to decide uniquely which numerical shift belongs to which of those two orientations. The nine-proton singlet is the tert-butyl group. The formula C6​H11​ONa follows from removing one alpha hydrogen from pinacolone.
E is the enol-dominated 1,3-diketone tCO−CH2​−COt, observed as tC(OH)=CH−COt. The very downfield broad signal belongs to the enol OH in a six-membered intramolecular O–H···O chelate. The one-proton signal in the vinyl region is its central enol CH; a predominantly diketo structure would instead require a two-proton central CH2 signal and no enol OH. The eighteen-proton singlet accounts for both tert-butyl groups. Rapid exchange between the two degenerate enol orientations can average their different local environments; accidental unresolved equality cannot be excluded by the supplied spectrum alone. Thus one should not infer a permanently symmetric single enol Lewis structure from that singlet. The total integration is 1+1+18=20, agreeing with C11​H20​O2​.
