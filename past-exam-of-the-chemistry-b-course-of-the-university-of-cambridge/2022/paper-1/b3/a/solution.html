 ... Chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge 2022 Paper 1 B3 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 168
Number the OH-bearing carbon C1 and the adjacent brominated carbon C2. In the trans bromohydrin, base generates an alkoxide. A chair conformation can place O at C1 and Br at C2 trans-diaxial, aligning the oxygen nucleophile for backside attack at C2. Intramolecular SN2 closure expels bromide and inverts C2: the retained C1–O bond and the newly formed C2–O bond are both on the original OH face, giving the epoxide shown. ring inversion can provide the reactive chair even if it is not the dominant ground-state conformer.
The cis bromohydrin cannot place OH and Br trans-diaxial: one is axial when the other is equatorial. In the chair with Br axial, the hydrogen on C1 can instead be antiperiplanar to C2–Br. An alkoxide-assisted semipinacol pathway moves the C1–H bonding pair to C2 while the oxygen lone pair forms C1=O and bromide departs. hydride migration gives the ketone without changing the carbon skeleton. The hydrogen is transferred to C2 rather than expelled as a free hydride. The original phenyl-bearing stereocentre is not involved in either bond-reorganization sequence. Configuration controls which properly aligned displacement or migration can occur; a flat ring drawing alone conceals that distinction.
