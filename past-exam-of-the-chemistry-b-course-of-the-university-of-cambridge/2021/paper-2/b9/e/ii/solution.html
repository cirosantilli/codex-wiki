Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge / 2021 / Paper 2 / B9 / e / ii / Solution

 ... Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge 2021 Paper 2 B9 e ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
Each trisilanol cage loses its three hydroxyl protons and supplies a facial tridentate silanolate ligand of charge 3−. Two such coordination ligands replace all six chlorides of tungsten(VI), giving
W{Cy7​Si7​O9​(O)3​}2​​
(9)
with approximately octahedral WO6​ coordination. tungsten remains +6, is d0, and is bound to six oxygen donors, three from each cage. The six liberated protons combine with the six chlorides to give six HCl; no W=O bond or tungsten reduction is needed. The molecular formula is C84​H154​Si14​O24​W. This bis(cage) tungsten structure was directly prepared and characterized in the primary literature.[ref]
