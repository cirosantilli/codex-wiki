Let Ar denote the 2,5-dimethoxyphenyl group. A is the doubly C-alkylated beta-ketoester
EtO−C(=O)−C(CH2​CH2​Ar)(CH2​CH2​CH=CH2​)−C(=O)−CH2​−O−tBu​.

The central carbon has no hydrogen after the second step. The alkene, ester and tert-butyl ether remain intact.
carbonate reversibly removes a proton from the methylene group between the two carbonyl groups. The resulting resonance-stabilized enolate attacks the primary iodoethyl carbon of the aryl-containing reagent by an SN​2 displacement, releasing iodide. The monoalkylated intermediate still has one proton at the same activated carbon. A second deprotonation followed by SN​2 attack on the primary bromide introduces the but-3-en-1-yl group.
The beta-dicarbonyl position is much more acidic than the methylene group next to the ketone and ether at the other end, explaining the alkylation site. Counting the retained carbon skeleton gives C24​H36​O6​, including the two carbonyl groups and one terminal alkene. The second panel of the original product drawing specifies this connectivity.
