The product is 1,4-dimethoxy-2,5-bis(2-methylbutan-2-yl)benzene. Each new substituent is −C(CH3​)2​CH2​CH3​, a tert-amyl group, rather than the unrearranged secondary alkyl group of the alcohol. The two substituents are related by the symmetry of the ring.

The unusually shielded terminal ethyl methyl signal is compatible with an aryl-containing environment; its NMR quartet–NMR triplet partner and integral are the decisive assignment evidence. The molecular formula and degree of unsaturation, DBE=4, fit a benzene ring with saturated side chains.
