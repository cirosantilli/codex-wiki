In A the two chlorides of the precursor are removed and neutral solvent donors occupy the reduced centre. In C the bromide is removed while the aryl ligand remains and the neutral alkyne coordinates; the drawn neutral intermediate has Ni(I). In F an anionic alkoxide-derived product is released and the neutral solvated Ni(0) complex is regenerated. zinc supplies electrons by oxidation to Zn(II); halide and alkoxide ions can be taken up in zinc-containing products.
A is precatalyst activation. The two one-electron reductions C and F together require two electrons per catalytic turnover, corresponding to one Zn atom if its oxidation ends at +2. The diagram does not establish whether each formal reduction is a single elementary electron-transfer event or a sequence of surface reactions and ligand transfers.
