First hydrolyse both Si–Cl groups to give a silanol of monomer functionality two, then condense Si–OH groups to form the Si–O–Si backbone:
R2​SiCl2​+2H2​O⟶R2​Si(OH)2​+2HCl,
(18)
≡Si−OH+HO−Si≡⟶≡Si−O−Si≡+H2​O.
(19)
monomer functionality two favours chains, with cyclic oligomers also possible. Removal of condensation products or equilibration under appropriate conditions favours high molar mass. The hydrolysis and condensation can overlap in a one-pot process; the equations distinguish their chemical functions rather than requiring isolation of every silanediol.
