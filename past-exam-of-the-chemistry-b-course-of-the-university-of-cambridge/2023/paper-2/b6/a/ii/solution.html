cisplatin has square planar molecular geometry and a 5d8 metal centre. Choose x and y along the four Pt–donor directions. The strongly antibonding dx2−y2​ orbital is highest and remains empty; the other four d orbitals are doubly occupied. The square-planar splitting is represented by the conventional qualitative order
(dxz​,dyz​)4(dz2​)2(dxy​)2(dx2−y2​)0.
(2)
The ordering among the lower occupied levels can depend on the ligand set, and the cis arrangement removes exact fourfold symmetry. The robust result is the filled lower four levels and empty highest level.
satraplatin has approximately octahedral molecular geometry around Pt(IV), 5d6. Its large octahedral splitting favours a low-spin complex:
t2g6​eg0​.​
(3)
All three t2g​ orbitals are paired. Mixed ligands remove exact degeneracies, but do not change this qualitative occupancy.
