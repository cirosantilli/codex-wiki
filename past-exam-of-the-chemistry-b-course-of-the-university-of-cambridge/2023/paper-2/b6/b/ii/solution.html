cyclohexylamine is expected highest in the spectrochemical series for comparable metal environments. Its alkyl substituent increases the electron-donating ability of nitrogen relative to ammonia, strengthening the sigma interaction and raising the metal–ligand sigma-antibonding levels. The anionic pi donors tend to raise occupied metal dπ​ levels and give smaller splittings. Steric effects and the metal can modify the exact ordering, so the comparison is a ligand field trend rather than a universal basicity-to-splitting identity.
