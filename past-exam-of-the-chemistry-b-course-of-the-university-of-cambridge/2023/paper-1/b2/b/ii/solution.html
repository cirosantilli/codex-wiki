Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge / 2023 / Paper 1 / B2 / b / ii / Solution

 ... Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge 2023 Paper 1 B2 b ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 163
Number the cyclopentanone ring p1​ at its carbonyl, then p2​ at the methyl-bearing carbon, p3​ at the mesylate-bearing carbon, p4​ at the carbon joined to cyclohexane h2​, and p5​ at the remaining methylene. B has a p2​=p3​ enone and the h1​=h6​ enamine. triethylamine removes the hydrogen at p2​ while the p3​–O bond breaks to release mesylate. An E2 elimination, with approximately antiperiplanar geometry in the puckered ring, gives the conjugated cyclopentenone. Elimination towards p4​ instead would give a nonconjugated alkene.
The enamine carbon h6​ now attacks enone beta carbon p3​ intramolecularly. nitrogen donates into h1​ to give the iminium ion, while the enone electrons move onto the ketone oxygen. protonation of the ketone enolate gives C. The second new bond is h6​−p3​; the first was h2​−p4​. These two connections account for the bridged skeleton without a concerted cycloaddition or an aldol step. The formula of B is C16​H23​NO: relative to A, elimination removes CH4​O3​S overall, namely methanesulfonic acid after proton transfers.
