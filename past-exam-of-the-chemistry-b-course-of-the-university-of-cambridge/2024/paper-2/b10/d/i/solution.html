OurBigBook.com Words: 144
His238 contributes strongly to turnover, with a plausible acid/base or electrostatic role near the attacking water. Replacing its imidazole by the methyl side chain of alanine removes both proton-transfer capacity and a positioned polar group, so the large fall in turnover number fits impaired chemical catalysis. A candidate role is acceptance of a proton from zinc-bound water, or stabilization and subsequent proton shuttling around the attacking oxygen.
Little change in the Michaelis constant suggests that the major kinetic defect is not a gross loss of substrate recognition, but KM​ is not generally a binding dissociation constant: KM​=(k−1​+kcat​)/k1​ for the elementary scheme. Nor does reduced kcat​ uniquely prove that His238 is the initial general base. Purified-protein controls should check fold, zinc occupancy and active concentration. Importantly, primary mouse-enzyme experiments found that His238 mutants could still hydrate a substrate analogue, directly illustrating this mechanistic limitation.[ref]
