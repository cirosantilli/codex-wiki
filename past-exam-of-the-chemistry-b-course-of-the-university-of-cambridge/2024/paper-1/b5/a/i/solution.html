Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge / 2024 / Paper 1 / B5 / a / i / Solution

 ... Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge 2024 Paper 1 B5 a i
OurBigBook.com Words: 147
A: aldehyde oxygen is the best acceptor; there is no conventional strong O–H or N–H donor. The N-methylpyrrole nitrogen contributes its lone pair to aromatic bonding and is not an ordinary amine acceptor. A formyl C–H or ring C–H can participate in weak contacts, but is not comparable to the donors in B and C.
B: pyridine nitrogen is the best acceptor and the phenolic OH is the best donor. Its oxygen can accept more weakly, but nitrogen provides the primary basic site in the depicted hydroxypyridine form.
C: lactam carbonyl oxygen is the best acceptor and the amide N–H is the donor. amide nitrogen is not a useful acceptor because its lone pair is delocalized toward the carbonyl.
D: the secondary amine nitrogen is the best acceptor, and its N–H is the only conventional donor, but a weak one here. Its ketone oxygen is also an acceptor, although weaker than the amine site in this comparison. These assignments distinguish functional-group identity and lone-pair availability, rather than counting heteroatoms.
