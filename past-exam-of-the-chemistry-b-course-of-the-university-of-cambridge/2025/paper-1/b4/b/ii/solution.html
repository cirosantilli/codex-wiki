The diagnostic feature is the large vicinal coupling characteristic of an axial–axial relationship in a chair. The td patterns for Ha​ and Hc​ contain two large couplings to neighbouring axial hydrogens, so both are axial. The ddd patterns for Hb​ and Hd​ also include a large ring vicinal coupling, with the smaller values reflecting the other ring hydrogen and the side chain. The assignments are
Ha​, Hb​, Hc​, Hd​ all axial.​
(2)
The chemical shifts alone do not determine this assignment. In particular, three unequal splittings of a methine are not a geminal-coupling pattern.
