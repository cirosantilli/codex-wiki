Apply the Felkin-Anh model to the stereocentre alpha to the ketone. Its large group is the chain continuing through the phenyl-bearing carbon, its medium group is methyl, and its small group is hydrogen. In the preferred transition-state arrangement, put the large group approximately perpendicular to C=O and approach from the opposite face, passing the small hydrogen along a Bürgi-Dunitz trajectory. The remote ether cannot form the usual small alpha-alkoxy chelate, and the coordinating solvent further supports using an open-addition prediction.[ref]
The predicted new stereocentre is S. In the question's zigzag projection, this corresponds to the new OH bond directed behind the page and the newly introduced methyl toward the reader, with the original stereocentres unchanged. The new-centre priority order is OH > phenyl > the substituted alpha-carbon chain > methyl. This is a model-based major-product prediction, rather than a quantitative diastereomeric ratio.
