Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge / 2025 / Paper 2 / B8 / b / i / Solution

 ... Past exam of the chemistry B course of the University of Cambridge 2025 Paper 2 B8 b i
OurBigBook.com Words: 140
Write L− for the cyclopentadienyl ligand bearing its CH2​–2-pyridyl arm; η5:κN means that its ring and pyridyl nitrogen both bind the same metal. D=[Mo(η5:κN-L)(CO)2​(NCMe)]+. The base removes the pyridinium N–H proton in C, restoring a pyridine lone pair. Intramolecular nitrogen coordination then replaces one acetonitrile ligand and closes a chelate ring. Both CO ligands are retained; the molybdenum remains Mo(II), d4.
This is an proton transfer followed by chelation, with metal electron count 4+6+2N​+4CO​+2NCMe​=18. The chelate effect, favorable formation of the Mo–N bond, and release of a solvent molecule explain the driving force. Merely invoking a 16-to-18-electron change would be misleading because C is already 18-electron. In the preceding acid treatment, protonation of the η3 allyl ligand allows release of propene; the other proton resides on the pendant pyridine, accounting for C's overall 2+ charge. One proton is removed here; the cyclopentadienyl tether is not deprotonated or detached.
