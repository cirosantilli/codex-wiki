Conformal cylinder of two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime (ds2=sec2T(dT2−dχ2),−π/2<T<π/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity de Sitter spacetime Two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
The conformal time T=2arctan(et)−π/2 satisfies sinT=tanht and cosT=secht. Multiplying the physical metric by cos2T gives a finite conformal compactification with periodic spatial coordinate. Its past and future conformal boundaries are spacelike circles. Null geodesics have slopes dχ/dT=±1.
