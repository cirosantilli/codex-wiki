Block of S3 in characteristic three (chark=3)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Modular representation theory Block of a group algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 124 Articles: 1
Over a field of characteristic three, the group algebra A=kS3​ has a single block of an Artinian algebra. Put r=(123), s=(12) and a=r−1. Then a3=0, sas=−a+a2, and J(A)=aA: the latter is a nilpotent ideal, with semisimple ring quotient kC2​. The center of an associative algebra is k1⊕ka2⊕ka2s, whose last two summands form a square-zero ideal. A central idempotent d1+n satisfies d2=d and (2d−1)n=0, hence is zero or one. Thus no nontrivial central block decomposition exists.
