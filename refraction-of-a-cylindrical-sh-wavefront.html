Refraction of a cylindrical SH wavefront (aT​=CTSH​/r1​+β1​q12​(t−t1​)/q22​​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 59
Place a line source a distance h above a plane welded interface and put qj​=(βj−2​−p2)1/2, t1​=h/(β12​q1​) and r1​=h/(β1​q1​). The transmitted elastic ray is x=hp/q1​+β22​p(t−t1​), z=β22​q2​(t−t1​). Elastic-interface boundary conditions give TSH​=2μ1​q1​/(μ1​q1​+μ2​q2​). Differentiating the ray positions across neighboring values of p and applying SH ray-tube amplitude transport gives the displayed leading amplitude. Both qj​ must be positive; critical and evanescent incidence require different asymptotics.
