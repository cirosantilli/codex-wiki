Sparse Gaussian elimination

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Numerical linear algebra Gaussian elimination
OurBigBook.com Words: 555 Articles: 9
A sparse direct solver performs Gaussian elimination while storing and updating only the predicted nonzero entries. A symbolic phase predicts the factor pattern from an elimination ordering, and a numerical phase evaluates the factors. The ordering can greatly alter fill-in and arithmetic work. For symmetric positive-definite systems, Cholesky decomposition avoids numerical pivoting; general systems require a balance between sparsity and stable pivot selection.
