Schur functor (Dλ​:V⟼ht​V⊗n)

OurBigBook.com Words: 140 Articles: 2
For a partition of an integer λ⊢n, applying a Young symmetrizer of that shape to the tensor power of each vector space gives a Schur functor. The construction respects linear maps because their tensor powers commute with place permutations. For single rows it gives symmetric powers, and for single columns exterior powers.
