Subgradient optimality condition (0∈∂f(x))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Convex function Subgradient
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
A proper convex function f is minimized at x exactly when 0∈∂f(x). For a differentiable convex term g and another convex term h, the subdifferential sum rule gives the condition 0∈∇g(x)+∂h(x).
