Foster's theorem (∑e​ce​Reff​(e)=∣V∣−1)

OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For a finite connected unweighted loopless graph with n graph vertices,
∑e∈E​Reff​(e)=n−1.
(33)
The edge-inclusion formula for a uniform spanning tree gives the left-hand side as E∣T∣, and every spanning tree has n−1 edges. On the same finite connected graph with positive edge conductances ce​>0, the corresponding identity is ∑e​ce​Reff​(e)=n−1.
