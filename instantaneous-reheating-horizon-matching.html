Instantaneous-reheating horizon matching (eN≳(Hi​/H0​)(areh​/a0​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic inflation Number of e-folds
OurBigBook.com Words: 111
Start with an inflationary patch of physical radius Hi−1​ in units c=1. After N e-folds and subsequent expansion, its present radius is Hi−1​eNa0​/areh​. Requiring it to cover the present Hubble radius gives the displayed inequality. With instantaneous conversion to radiation, fixed relativistic degrees of freedom and a fixed ratio Hi​/Hend​, energy conservation gives Hi​∝Treh2​, whereas adiabatic subsequent expansion gives a0​/areh​∝Treh​. Thus the required N increases approximately as logTreh​. Holding Hi​ fixed while varying the end energy independently is a different comparison and reverses that simple temperature-only trend. The inflationary energy history and post-inflationary entropy history must be specified.[ref]
