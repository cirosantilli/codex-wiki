Instruction pipelining

 Home Computer science Central processing unit
OurBigBook.com Words: 175 Articles: 6
Instruction pipelining divides execution into stages with stored intermediate state, allowing different instructions to occupy different stages simultaneously. The processor clock period is constrained by stage timing; moving a barrel shifter and arithmetic logic unit into the same stage can lengthen that stage's combinational critical path.
