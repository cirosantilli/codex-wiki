Spin-flavour symmetry

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron Quark model
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For three light flavours and two spin states, an approximate SU(6) spin-flavour symmetry acts on the six-dimensional single-quark spin-flavour space. A spatial ground-state three-quark colour singlet requires the symmetric representation of dimension 56. Under flavour symmetry and ordinary spin rotations, it decomposes as (10,4)⊕(8,2): the baryon decuplet has spin 3/2 and the baryon octet spin 1/2. The product of spin and flavour factors can require a correlated sum, rather than a single unentangled product.
