Monotone sequence

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 9
A real sequence is increasing when an+1​≥an​ and decreasing when an+1​≤an​.
A monotone sequence has one of these directions for every index. Here increasing means nondecreasing and decreasing means nonincreasing: equal successive terms are allowed. Strict increase would require an+1​>an​ at every step, and strict decrease would require an+1​<an​. A constant sequence is both increasing and decreasing. These conventions are those of Basic Analysis: Introduction to Real Analysis, Volume I by Jiří Lebl (§2.1.1, Definition 2.1.9).
For n≥1, the sequence 1−1/n increases toward one, while 1+1/n decreases toward one. Repeating each term gives the still increasing sequence 0,0,1/2,1/2,2/3,2/3,…. Plateaus do not obstruct the bounded monotone sequence theorem. Checking the sign of an+1​−an​ is often the simplest way to verify a direction; finding an upper bound or lower bound is a separate task.
