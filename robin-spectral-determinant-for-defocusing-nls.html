Robin spectral determinant for defocusing NLS (D(k)=(2k+ic)a(k)a♯(−k)+(2k−ic)b(k)b♯(−k))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Integrable systems Defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation Linearizable Robin scattering for defocusing NLS
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The effective boundary coefficient is Γ=−(2k−ic)b♯(−k)/(aD). All its ingredients come from initial scattering and the Robin boundary condition. Genuine zeros produce pole data; common zeros in numerator and denominator can instead be removable. It is incorrect to assert absence of boundary poles for every real Robin parameter.
