Electromagnetic charge matrix of electroweak gauge fields (Qgauge​=​0i00​−i000​0000​0000​​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Electroweak interaction Electroweak gauge boson
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
In the (A1,A2,A3,B) basis with gauge transformations U=eiαQ and Q=T3​+Y, the non-Abelian connection transforms by conjugation. The Lie brackets [T3​,T1​]=iT2​ and [T3​,T2​]=−iT1​ give δA1=αA2, δA2=−αA1, with neutral A3,B. Thus δA=iαQgauge​A gives the displayed Hermitian matrix. The fields W±=(A1∓iA2)/2​ transform as e±iαW±, while both neutral combinations are uncharged. This separates the Adjoint representation on the gauge fields from the doublet charge matrix diag(1,0) on the Higgs field.
