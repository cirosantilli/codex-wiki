Current-voltage characteristic

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
A current-voltage characteristic describes the relation between terminal electric potential difference and electric current for specified physical conditions. A resistor obeying Ohm's law gives a straight line; a diode has an asymmetric relation.
