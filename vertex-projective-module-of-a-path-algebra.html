Vertex projective module of a path algebra (P(i)=kQei​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Quiver Path algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 1
The vertex projective module of a path algebra P(i)=kQei​ has at vertex j the span of paths from i to j. Each arrow appends itself to such paths. It is a projective module because it is a summand of the free module kQ. Its endomorphism ring is (ei​kQei​)op, acting by right multiplication. The closed-path corner of a path algebra is a domain, so this ring has no nontrivial idempotents and P(i) is indecomposable. Directed cycles may make these projectives infinite-dimensional.
