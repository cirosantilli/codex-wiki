Alpha-amino acid

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Organic acid Carboxylic acid Amino acid
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 10
An alpha-amino acid has amino functionality on the carbon next to its carboxyl carbon. The skeleton in amino acid shows this adjacency explicitly. Numbering the carboxyl carbon as C1 makes the adjacent position C2, also called the alpha position. [ref]
