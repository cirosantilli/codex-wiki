Universal cover of two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime (ds2=sec2η(−dη2+dξ2),ξ∈R)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity de Sitter spacetime Two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
The unrestricted spatial coordinate unwraps the spatial circle of two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime. The conformal time η=arcsin(tanht) lies in (−π/2,π/2), and null geodesics have slopes dξ/dη=±1. Constant-η lines are Cauchy hypersurfaces: along any causal curve ∣dξ/dη∣≤1, so a curve cannot escape to spatial infinity in a bounded conformal-time interval. The covering spacetime is therefore globally hyperbolic, despite its observer-dependent cosmological horizons. Identifying ξ modulo 2π gives the de Sitter cylinder, also globally hyperbolic.
