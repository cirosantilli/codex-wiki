Different-wavenumber cancellation in sheet swimming (U2​=21​(k⊥2​b2−k∥2​a2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 38
When longitudinal and transverse sheet waves share unit phase speed but have different wavenumbers k∥​,k⊥​, their second-order swimming coefficient is (k⊥2​b2−k∥2​a2)/2. Thus their leading propulsion cancels at b/a=k∥​/k⊥​. This statement concerns the small-amplitude leading order.
