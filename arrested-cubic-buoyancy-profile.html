Arrested cubic buoyancy profile (G=Q2(z+H)3/(27d8))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Eddy diffusion Buoyancy-gradient mixing-length closure
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
An upward volume flux Q and a downward turbulent buoyancy transport d4(Gz​)3/2 balance in a steady reactor. Matching G=Gz​=0 below −H gives QG=d4(Gz​)3/2. Integrating yields the cubic profile above the front. If downward source buoyancy flux is Bs​, then H=3d8/3Bs1/3​/Q. The corresponding eddy diffusivity is K=Q(z+H)/(3d2). All these prefactors use the same area and mixing-length normalization; an actual cross-sectional area S replaces the denominator in K by 3S.
