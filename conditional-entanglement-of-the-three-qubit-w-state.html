Conditional entanglement of the three-qubit W state

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state Dicke state W state
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
Every rank-one projective measurement of one qubit of the three-qubit W state has at least one nonzero-probability outcome whose other two qubits remain entangled. To see this, contract the measured factor against ⟨u∣, where ∣u⟩=a∣0⟩+b∣1⟩. The unnormalized tensor-product coefficient matrix of the other pair is
Cu​=3​1​(ba​a0​),detCu​=−3a2​.
(12)
At least one vector in a complete measurement basis has a=0, giving nonzero determinant and an entangled conditional branch. Its probability (2∣a∣2+∣b∣2)/3 is positive. This property survives local unitary changes of basis. The GHZ state lacks it because computational-basis measurement of one qubit leaves a product state on each branch, so the property also separates the states under local-unitary equivalence.
