Tarski monster groups are two-generated and simple

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Infinite group Tarski monster group
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For a Tarski monster group, any nonidentity element generates a proper subgroup of order p. Adjoining an element outside that subgroup generates the whole group. A proper nontrivial normal subgroup would have order p; conjugation into its finite automorphism group forces it to be central. Combining it with another order-p cyclic subgroup would then give a proper subgroup of order p2, a contradiction.
