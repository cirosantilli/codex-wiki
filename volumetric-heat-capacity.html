Volumetric heat capacity (Cvol​=C/V=ρc)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Heat capacity Specific heat capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
Volumetric heat capacity is heat capacity per unit volume, with units Jm−3K−1. For homogeneous material of mass density ρ=m/V, it equals ρc, using specific heat capacity c for the same constraint. It measures the heat required per small temperature increase for a chosen occupied volume. In a material model using specific heat capacity at constant pressure, thermal diffusivity is α=k/(ρcp​), where k is thermal conductivity.
