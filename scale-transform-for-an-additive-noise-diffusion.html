For dX=b(X)dt+dW with continuous drift, integrate the positive derivative displayed above. The resulting scale function of a one-dimensional diffusion solves g′′=−2bg′. The Itô formula removes the drift of g(X), giving dY=h(Y)dW, where h=g′∘g−1 on the interval g(R). If the drift is bounded, h′=−2b∘g−1 is bounded, so h is Lipschitz on that interval.
