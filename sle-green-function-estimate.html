For 0<κ<8, the probability that chordal SLEκ​ approaches an interior point z to conformal radius at most ϵ is, on compact subsets of H, comparable to
Gκ​(z)ϵ1−κ/8,
(31)
where Gκ​ is the SLE Green function. The positive exponent implies that the trace has zero planar Lebesgue measure.
