Charge conjugation of the Dirac adjoint (ψc​=−ψTC−1)

For ψc=Cψˉ​T, use a unitary charge-conjugation matrix with C−1γ0C=−(γ0)T and Hermitian γ0 of square one. The Dirac adjoint then satisfies
ψc​=ψT(γ0)∗C−1γ0=−ψTC−1.
(6)
If the unitary charge conjugation operator sends ψ to ηC​ψc, it sends ψˉ​ to −ηC∗​ψTC−1. Antisymmetry CT=−C also gives (ψc)c=ψ. The unitary operator acting on fields is not an antiunitary operator acting on numerical coefficients.
