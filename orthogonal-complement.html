Orthogonal complement (F⊥)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Hilbert space Hilbert projection theorem Orthogonal decomposition by a closed subspace

The orthogonal complement of a subset F of an inner-product space is
F⊥={x:⟨x,y⟩=0 for every y∈F}.
