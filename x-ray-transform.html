X-ray transform (Ih(γ)=∫γ​h)

OurBigBook.com Words: 491 Articles: 8
The X-ray transform records integrals of a function or tensor evaluation over a specified family of curves. The curve family, parametrization and any weights are part of the definition. Straight lines, geodesics and magnetic flows give different versions; their injectivity and unavoidable potential kernels depend on that geometry.
