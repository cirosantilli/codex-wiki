Sum of squares in ANOVA (SS=YTPY)

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Normal linear model Linear regression Analysis of variance
OurBigBook.com Words: 65 Articles: 1
For an orthogonal projection matrix P, a sum of squares in ANOVA is YTPY=∥PY∥2. Mutually orthogonal projections yield an additive decomposition of total squared response magnitude. Removing the grand mean gives the corrected total.
