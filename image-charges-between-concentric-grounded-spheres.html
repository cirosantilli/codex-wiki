Image charges between concentric grounded spheres (k=b/a>1)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric field Electric potential Image charge
OurBigBook.com Words: 155
For radii a<b and a point charge q at r0​ez​, a<r0​<b, set k=b/a. The method of images gives the two families (qkn,r0​k2nez​) and (−qakn/r0​,a2k2nez​/r0​) for n∈Z. The first family's n=0 term is the physical point charge. All other image charges lie outside the gap. Sum their Coulomb's law electric potentials with factor 1/(4πϵ0​).
For a short proof, reflection of (Q,z) in a grounded sphere of radius R gives (−RQ/z,R2/z). On r=a this pairs first-family index n with second-family index −n; on r=b it pairs n with 1−n. Each pair has zero boundary electric potential. The negative-index electric potential tails are O(kn) and the positive-index tails are O(k−n), uniformly on the closed gap after excluding the physical singularity. These geometric series establish absolute convergence, boundary cancellation, and termwise differentiation away from the physical point charge. Uniqueness of Poisson equation identifies the solution. A spherical Gaussian surface just outside a encloses the first family with n≤−1 and the second with n≤0, so Gauss's law and the geometric series give Qa​=−qa(b−r0​)/(r0​(b−a)).
