For odd n=2k+1, a symmetric quantum teleportation resource can encode an unknown qubit into E(α∣0⟩+β∣1⟩)=α∣Dkn​⟩+β∣Dk+1n​⟩. Begin with ∣Dk+1n+1​⟩, apply a controlled-NOT gate from the input to the sender's resource qubit, apply a Pauli X gate to that resource qubit and a Hadamard gate to the input, and measure those two qubits. The four unnormalized remaining vectors are E∣ψ⟩/2, EX∣ψ⟩/2, EZ∣ψ⟩/2 and EXZ∣ψ⟩/2. Each outcome has probability 1/4. Since X⊗n interchanges ∣Dkn​⟩ and ∣Dk+1n​⟩, and Z⊗n acts as logical Z up to the common phase (−1)k, local Pauli corrections make the output independent of the result.
