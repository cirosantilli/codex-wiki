Arrhenius plot (ln(k/kref​) against 1/T)

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Chemical kinetics Activation energy Arrhenius equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
An Arrhenius plot places ln(k/kref​) against 1/T, using a fixed reference rate constant with the same units as k. Under the constant-parameter Arrhenius equation,
lnkref​k(T)​=lnkref​A​−REa​​T1​.
(10)
The slope is −Ea​/R and the intercept is ln(A/kref​). Changing the reference units shifts the intercept but leaves the slope unchanged. If the horizontal coordinate is displayed in 10−3K−1, its numerical slope must be rescaled before multiplying by −R. A temperature-dependent pre-exponential factor or activation energy can make the plot curved.
