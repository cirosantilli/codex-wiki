No-penetration boundary condition

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stress boundary condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
The no-penetration boundary condition sets the relative normal velocity of a fluid and an impermeable boundary to zero. Tangential slip may still be permitted.
