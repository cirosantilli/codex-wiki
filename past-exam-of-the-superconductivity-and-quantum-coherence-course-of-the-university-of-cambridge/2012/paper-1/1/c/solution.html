Write the condensate order parameter as Ψ=∣Ψ∣eiθ. Away from vortex cores, single-valuedness around a closed loop gives ∮∇θ⋅dl=2πn. For neutral condensed objects of mass M, vs​=ℏ∇θ/M, hence the quantized circulation
∮vs​⋅dl=nMh​.​
(5)
Here M is the atomic mass for a simple bosonic atomic condensate, or the pair mass for a paired-fermion condensate. A nonzero winding can surround a vortex core where the order-parameter amplitude falls to zero. The result concerns collective condensate phase winding, not separate orbit quantization of every constituent particle.
For charge q per condensed object, the gauge-invariant relation is Mvs​=ℏ∇θ−qA. Integrating gives
M∮vs​⋅dl+qΦ=nh,Φ+qM​∮vs​⋅dl=qnh​.
(6)
This is fluxoid and flux quantization in a superconductor. With Cooper-pair charge magnitude 2e, the flux quantum's magnitude is
Φ0​=2eh​.​
(7)
The orientation fixes its sign. A loop taken deep in a thick superconductor has negligible superflow and therefore encloses an integer flux quantum. In a thin loop or one with appreciable screening current, it is the fluxoid, including the circulation term, that is quantized; the magnetic flux alone need not be exactly an integer multiple.
