Fixed-voltage capacitor energy balance (dUfield​=dWmech​+VdQ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Capacitor Capacitor energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 1
For a quasistatic change of capacitance at fixed terminal electric potential difference, the capacitor energy is U=CV2/2 and plate electric charge is Q=CV. Electrical work supplied to the electric field by the battery is VdQ, so mechanical work done on the capacitor is dWmech​=dU−VdQ=−V2dC/2. Increasing the separation of a parallel-plate capacitor makes dC<0: mechanical work is positive even though electrostatic energy decreases, because electrical energy returns to the source. Conversely, when dC>0, the battery supplies V2dC: half increases capacitor energy and half is available as mechanical work done by the electric field. Differentiating capacitor energy alone at fixed terminal electric potential difference omits this exchange with the battery.
