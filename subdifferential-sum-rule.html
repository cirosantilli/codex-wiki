Subdifferential sum rule

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Subdifferential
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
For proper convex functions under a standard relative-interior qualification,
∂(f+g)(x)=∂f(x)+∂g(x).
(11)
In particular, if f is differentiable at x, then ∂(f+g)(x)=∇f(x)+∂g(x).
