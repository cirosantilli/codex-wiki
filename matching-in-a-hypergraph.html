Matching in a hypergraph

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Hypergraph
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
A matching in a hypergraph is a family of pairwise vertex-disjoint hypergraph edges. A perfect matching in a hypergraph covers every vertex; an almost-perfect matching in a hypergraph leaves a small proportion uncovered. In a proper edge colouring, each colour class is a matching in a hypergraph.
