Gaussian-integer disc group with an ideal-square Dirichlet domain (D={2(∣x∣+∣y∣)≤1+x2+y2}∩D)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Properly discontinuous group action Dirichlet domain
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
Consider determinant-one matrices (uv​vu​) over Gaussian integers with u+v≡1(mod2Z[i]), modulo their common sign. They act on the Poincare disc model by fractional linear maps. Their origin-centered Dirichlet domain is
D={x+iy:x2+y2<1, 2(∣x∣+∣y∣)≤1+x2+y2}.
(27)
For a nonidentity matrix set N=∣v∣2≥1 and uv=m+in. The group orbit point is (m+in)/(N+1) and its bisector inequality is 2(mx+ny)≤N(1+x2+y2). Since m2+n2=N(N+1), integrality gives ∣m∣,∣n∣≤N, so the four inequalities defining D imply all of them. They are necessary because the matrices with u=1±i, v=±i realize the four combinations m,n=±1. The four sides are arcs of the unit-radius circles centered at ±1±i, and the ideal vertices are 1,i,−1,−i.
