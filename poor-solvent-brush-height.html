Poor-solvent brush height (h∼Nv0​σ/ϕ)

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymer physics Grafted polymer chain Polymer brush
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
In a laterally uniform dense polymer brush with approximately fixed polymer volume fraction ϕ, conservation of occupied volume gives ϕh=Nv0​σ, with repeat-unit volume v0​. At fixed solvent quality this gives h∝Nσ, unlike the good-solvent brush height. Isolated collapsed polymer globules and laterally segregated grafted layers need not obey this uniform-layer model.
