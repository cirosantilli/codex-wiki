Complex special linear group in dimension two (SL(2,C))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Special linear group
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
Complex two-by-two matrices of determinant one form a complex three-dimensional Lie group, or a real six-dimensional Lie group. Their congruence action on Hermitian two-by-two matrices gives the Lorentz spinor double cover with kernel {I,−I}. The two Weyl spinor representations are A and (A†)−1. Treating the group as real is essential for the second, antiholomorphic representation.
