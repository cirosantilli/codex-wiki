Fourier sine transform (S(k)=∫0∞​sin(kx)f(x)dx)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier transform
OurBigBook.com Words: 73 Articles: 1
The Fourier sine transform is a half-line transform adapted to a homogeneous Dirichlet boundary condition. With the displayed normalization, inversion is f(x)=(2/π)∫0∞​sin(kx)S(k)dk. It is equivalent to the Fourier transform of an odd extension. If f(0)=0 and boundary terms vanish, two integrations by parts give the transform of f′′ as −k2S(k).
