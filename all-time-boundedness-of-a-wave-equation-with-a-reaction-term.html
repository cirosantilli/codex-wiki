All-time boundedness of a wave equation with a reaction term (α<λ1​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For utt​=Δu+αu with a homogeneous Dirichlet boundary condition, let λ1​>0 be the first Dirichlet Laplacian eigenvalue. On a bounded one-dimensional interval, finite-energy displacement remains bounded for all time and all initial data exactly when α<λ1​. The conserved energy 21​(∥ut​∥22​+∥∇u∥22​−α∥u∥22​) controls the gradient and, by the Poincaré inequality and one-dimensional integration, the maximum norm. At equality a mode can grow linearly; above it a mode can grow exponentially. Bounded oscillations need not have a long-time limit.
