Flux-tube axial field virial identity (⟨Bz2​⟩=Bp2​−2μ0​⟨p⟩)

For a regular cylindrical flux tube with B=(0,Bϕ​(s),Bz​(s)), vanishing field beyond radius s0​, and exterior pressure pe​, define ⟨h⟩=2s0−2​∫0s0​​sh(s)ds and Bp2​=2μ0​pe​. Integrating cylindrical magnetostatic pressure balance against s2 gives the displayed identity. The toroidal magnetic pressure and hoop magnetic tension cancel in this cross-sectional axial-field average. Nonnegative gas pressure gives ⟨Bz2​⟩≤Bp2​, with strict inequality precisely when ⟨p⟩>0.
