Normalization of the electromagnetic dual tensor (Fab=21​ϵabcdFcd​)

In oriented Minkowski coordinates with (+−−−) signature and ϵ0123=+1, the conventional dual is Fab=21​ϵabcdFcd​. For F0i​=Ei​ and Fij​=−ϵijk​Bk​ in c=1 units, FabFab​=−4E⋅B. A definition without the half produces twice this dual and contraction −8E⋅B. The factor accounts for summing both orders of each antisymmetric index pair.
