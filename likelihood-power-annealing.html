Likelihood-power annealing (πT​(θ)∝L(θ)1/T)

OurBigBook.com Words: 286 Articles: 2
To maximize a log-likelihood, use energy −ℓ and Boltzmann distribution proportional to eℓ/T=L1/T at temperature T>0. Reversing this sign favors likelihood minima and can make the probability density function nonnormalizable. For Poisson observations with total s and sample size m, with respect to dλ the normalized probability density function is
λ∼Gamma(s/T+1,m/T).
(2)
Its mean is (s+T)/m and variance (sT+T2)/m2, so it concentrates at the maximum-likelihood estimate s/m as T↓0. Cooling of equilibrium laws and convergence of a particular changing-temperature chain are different statements; the latter requires mixing or a direct chain argument.
