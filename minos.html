A long-baseline neutrino oscillation experiment using a Fermilab beam, near and far detectors, and magnetized iron/scintillator sampling calorimeters. A charged current muon neutrino event gives a negative muon and a hadronic shower; their reconstructed energies estimate the incident neutrino energy.
