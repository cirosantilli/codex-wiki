Activation barriers of the Adler phase equation (ΔV−​−ΔV+​=2πω)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Phase oscillator Adler phase equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
For 0<ω<ϵ, set α=arcsin(ω/ϵ) and κ=ϵ2−ω2​. The neighboring saddles of the tilted washboard potential have barriers ΔV+​=2κ−ω(π−2α) and ΔV−​=2κ+ω(π+2α) above the minimum at α. Their difference is 2πω; both have curvature −κ, so forward and backward Kramers escape rates have equal leading prefactors.
