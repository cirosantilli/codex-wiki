Normal subgroups of a compact group with irreducible adjoint action

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Adjoint representation of a Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
Let G be connected and compact with nonzero irreducible real Adjoint representation. Its Lie algebra has no proper invariant Lie algebra ideal. If it is Abelian, irreducibility forces dimension one, so G is the circle group. Otherwise its centre has zero-dimensional Lie algebra and is finite. A proper closed normal subgroup likewise has zero-dimensional Lie algebra, hence is finite; connectedness of G makes conjugation on that finite subgroup trivial, so the subgroup is central. A Lie group homomorphism onto a nontrivial connected target with surjective differential consequently has finite central group kernel. The qualifications “proper” and “nontrivial” are essential.
