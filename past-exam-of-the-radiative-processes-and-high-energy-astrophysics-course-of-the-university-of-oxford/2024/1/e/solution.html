For the stipulated Strong-shock Rankine-Hugoniot conditions, ρd​=4ρu​ and vd​=vu​/4. The ideal gas law is Pd​=ρd​kB​Td​/μ, with μ a dimensional mean particle mass. The specific enthalpy of a nonrelativistic monatomic gas is 5kB​T/(2μ). Dividing the energy flux equation in d by the common mass flux gives
2vu2​​=32vu2​​+2μ5kB​Td​​.
(11)
Therefore
2μ5kB​Td​​=3215vu2​​,kB​Td​=163​μvu2​.​
(12)
As an independent check, conservation of momentum gives Pd​=ρu​vu2​−4ρu​(vu​/4)2=3ρu​vu2​/4, and division by ρd​=4ρu​ gives exactly the same temperature. The numerical coefficient relies on γ=5/3 and the absence of dynamically important radiative or magnetic stresses.
