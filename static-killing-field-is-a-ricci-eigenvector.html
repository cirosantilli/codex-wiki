Static Killing field is a Ricci eigenvector (Rab​kb ∥ ka​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Stationary spacetime Static spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
A timelike Killing vector with hypersurface orthogonality admits local coordinates with k=∂t​ and ds2=N(x)2dt2−hij​(x)dxidxj. The only connection coefficients with time indices that can be nonzero are Γ0i0​=∂i​logN and Γ00i​=Nhij∂j​N. Substitution into the Ricci tensor gives R0i​=0. Thus Rab​kb has only its time component and is proportional to ka​. Equivalently kd​Rd[a​kb]​=0. This local statement does not require the Vacuum Einstein equations and is independent of the overall curvature-sign convention.
