Circular light ray in a linear speed profile ((x−x0​)2+(y+c0​)2=R2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Geometrical optics Light ray
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
For speed c(y)=y+c0​>0, Fermat principle and the Beltrami identity yield circular light rays whose centres lie on y=−c0​. The ray between (±a,0) in the upper half-plane is y=a2+c02​−x2​−c0​.
