Matched-load scattering of a Hertzian dipole (Psca​=Pload​)

 Home Electrical engineering Antenna (radio) Dipole antenna Hertzian dipole
OurBigBook.com Words: 138
For a lossless ideal Hertzian dipole driven by an incident wave, use its induced open-circuit voltage and its feed impedance Rrad​+iX. A conjugately matched load has impedance Rrad​−iX. The same induced current therefore delivers equal power to the load and to radiation resistance: Psca​=Pload​. The scattered wave has the usual dipole angular pattern. In direction of gain G, its scattering cross-section is GAeff​, since the load receives Sinc​Aeff​.
This ideal single-mode scattering is not the same as a signal that travels through an already matched antenna port, reflects from a mismatched terminating load, and returns. A zero port reflection coefficient removes that latter echo but does not make the physical dipole invisible to the incident field. Additional structural scattering, ohmic loss or a different load changes the result.
