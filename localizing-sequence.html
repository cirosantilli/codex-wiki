Localizing sequence

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Continuous-time martingale Local martingale
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A localizing sequence for a local martingale M is an increasing sequence of stopping times Tn​↑∞ almost surely such that every stopped process MTn​ is a true martingale. One can often choose the times so that the stopped processes are bounded.
