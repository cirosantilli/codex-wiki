High-speed passage expansion of a localized trap (Δx=∑m≥1​vT−m​∫(F/ζ)mdy)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical tweezers Passage through a translating optical trap
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
If the maximum force-induced speed is smaller in magnitude than vT​, expand the passage denominator as a uniformly convergent geometric series. For a localized conservative well with equal outside potential levels, the first force moment vanishes and displacement starts at order vT−2​. An odd force has no cubic moment, giving the next correction at order vT−4​. Compact force support alone does not guarantee a vanishing first moment or positive displacement.
