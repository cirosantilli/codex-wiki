Risk-free asset (R=1/B)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Financial asset
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For a fixed horizon, a risk-free asset has a deterministic terminal payoff. If the price of a unit terminal payoff is B>0, its gross return is R=1/B. In a finite state market, the sum of the Arrow state prices is B. Risk-free does not mean that a longer-maturity bond has a deterministic resale price before its maturity.
