Tate module (Tℓ​(A)=lim​n​A[ℓn])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation Galois representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 173 Articles: 2
For an abelian variety A and a prime number ℓ different from the characteristic, the inverse limit of its geometric ℓn-torsion, with transition multiplication by ℓ, is its Tate module. For an elliptic curve, it is free of rank two over the p-adic integers and satisfies Tℓ​(E)/ℓnTℓ​(E)≃E[ℓn]. The absolute Galois group acts continuously and commutes with all base-defined endomorphisms.
