Lattice Eisenstein sum (Gk​(Λ)=∑ω∈Λ∖{0}​ω−k)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Eisenstein series
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
For a period lattice Λ⊂C and an integer k>2, this sum converges absolutely. Pairing ω and −ω makes it zero when k is odd. For even weights it supplies the Laurent coefficients of the Weierstrass elliptic function. Scaling the period lattice gives Gk​(aΛ)=a−kGk​(Λ).
