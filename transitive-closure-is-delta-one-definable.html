Transitive closure is Delta-one definable (y=TC(x) is Δ1ZF​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cumulative hierarchy Transitive closure
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
The transitive closure is obtained by x0​=x, xn+1​=⋃xn​, t=⋃n<ω​xn​. This is transitive, contains x as a subset, and is contained in any other transitive set containing x, by induction. Its graph has a Sigma-one formula in set theory saying there exist ω, a sequence with this recursion, and its range. The bounded definition of omega, function and union predicates keep the matrix bounded. Existence and uniqueness in ZF express failure of y=TC(x) by the existential assertion that the construction produces some z=y. Thus the graph is a Delta-one formula modulo ZF, and Delta-one absoluteness makes transitive closure absolute between nested transitive models of ZF.
