Graded module (M=⨁d​Md​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Ring Graded ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 216 Articles: 3
A graded module over a graded ring R=⨁i​Ri​ is an R-module with a direct sum decomposition M=⨁d​Md​ satisfying Ri​Md​⊆Mi+d​. A graded homomorphism preserves these degrees. Over a polynomial ring with positive variable degrees, a finitely generated module of this kind is bounded below and has finite-dimensional graded components over the coefficient field.
