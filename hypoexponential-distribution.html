Hypoexponential distribution

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Continuous probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 206 Articles: 2
A hypoexponential distribution is the distribution of a sum of independent variables with exponential distributions. For two positive unequal rates λ,μ, convolution of independent random variables gives the probability density function
f(s)=μ−λλμ​(e−λs−e−μs),s≥0.
(1)
It is zero for negative s. Its equal-rate limit is the shape-two gamma distribution with rate λ.
