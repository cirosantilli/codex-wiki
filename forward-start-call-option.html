Forward-start call option ((ST2​​−λST1​​)+)

 ... Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fundamental theorem of asset pricing Contingent claim European contingent claim Forward start option
OurBigBook.com Words: 130
A forward start option whose call option strike is fixed at time T1​ as λST1​​, with expiry T2​>T1​ and λ>0. Its terminal financial payoff depends on the intermediate stock price as well as the final price. Two paths ending at the same price can therefore have different payoffs, so a recombining stock tree must retain enough history to value the contract.
In a homogeneous dividend-free Black-Scholes model, let c(1,λ,T2​−T1​) be the European call option price with current stock one and strike λ over the remaining duration. Homogeneity gives value ST1​​c(1,λ,T2​−T1​) when the strike is fixed. The discounted stock is a martingale, so risk-neutral pricing before that date gives
Vt​=St​c(1,λ,T2​−T1​),t<T1​.
(20)
The same argument holds in a homogeneous binomial options pricing model with the corresponding remaining-step call value. It depends on the normalized future pricing rule being independent of the stock level at the fixing date.
