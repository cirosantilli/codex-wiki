Scalar-tensor theory

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Einstein field equations Modified gravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 273 Articles: 4
A scalar-tensor theory uses a metric tensor and an additional scalar field to mediate gravity. A scalar multiplier of the Ricci scalar makes the curvature coupling depend on that field. The Brans-Dicke theory is a standard example; the choice of scalar potential and kinetic coupling distinguishes different members of the class.
