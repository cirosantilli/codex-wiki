For a bounded linear functional on a unital C*-algebra, the state criterion is
x(1A​)=∥x∥=1.​
(18)
Equivalently it is positive and takes the unit to one.
In the Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction, set Nx​={b:x(b∗b)=0}. This is a left ideal. Give A/Nx​ the inner product ⟨[b],[c]⟩=x(c∗b), linear in the first variable, and complete it to a Hilbert space Hx​. Left multiplication gives the bounded *-representation πx​(a)[b]=[ab]. The vector ξ=[1A​] is cyclic and satisfies x(a)=⟨πx​(a)ξ,ξ⟩.
The Hermitian matrix ρ has eigenvalues λ±​=1/2±∣α∣ and trace one. In an orthonormal eigenbasis v+​,v−​,
xρ​(a)=λ+​⟨av+​,v+​⟩+λ−​⟨av−​,v−​⟩.
(19)
If ∣α∣≤1/2, both weights are nonnegative and sum to one, so this functional is positive and normalized, hence a state. If ∣α∣>1/2, evaluating on the positive rank-one projection onto the negative-eigenvalue eigenspace gives a negative value, so it is not a state. Therefore
xρ​ is a state⟺∣α∣≤1/2.​
(20)
To obtain the representation dimension, cyclicity of trace gives
xρ​(b∗b)=Tr(ρb∗b)=∥bρ1/2∥HS2​.
(21)
Thus A/Nxρ​​ identifies isometrically with the matrix space {bρ1/2:b∈M2​(C)}, with left multiplication as the representation. If r=rankρ, this space consists, after a unitary change of basis, of arbitrary first r columns and zero remaining columns. Its complex dimension is 2r. It is already complete because it is finite-dimensional. Rank is one at ∣α∣=1/2 and two at ∣α∣<1/2, proving
dimHxρ​​=2 at ∣α∣=1/2,dimHxρ​​=4 at ∣α∣<1/2.​
(22)
