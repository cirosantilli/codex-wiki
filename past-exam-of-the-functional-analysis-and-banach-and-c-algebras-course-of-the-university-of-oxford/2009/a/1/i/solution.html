The quotient norm is
∥x+Y∥X/Y​=y∈Yinf​∥x−y∥.​
(1)
Changing the representative does not change the infimum. The triangle inequality and absolute homogeneity follow from those of the original norm. Vanishing means x belongs to the closure of Y, hence to Y because the subspace is closed. Thus this is a norm.
Use the absolutely convergent series criterion for completeness. If ∑∥ξn​∥X/Y​<∞, choose representatives zn​ of ξn​ satisfying ∥zn​∥≤∥ξn​∥X/Y​+2−n. The series ∑zn​ converges in the Banach space X to some z. Since the quotient map is contractive, ∑ξn​ converges to z+Y. Consequently the quotient is a Banach space.
