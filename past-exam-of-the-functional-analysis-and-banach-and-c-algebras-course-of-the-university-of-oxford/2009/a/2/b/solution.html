The weak topology on X is the coarsest topology making every f∈X∗ continuous. The weak-star topology on X∗ is the coarsest making every evaluation f↦f(x), x∈X, continuous. In each case basic neighborhoods impose finitely many evaluation inequalities. The Banach-Alaoglu theorem states that the closed unit ball of a dual space is compact for its weak-star topology. The separation theorem needed here says: if K is a nonempty weak-star compact convex subset of a dual space E∗ and z∈/K, then some e∈E strictly separates them,
