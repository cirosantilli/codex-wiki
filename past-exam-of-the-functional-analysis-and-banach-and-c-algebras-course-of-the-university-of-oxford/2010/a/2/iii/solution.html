The compact set S(Bc0​​) is bounded, so S is a bounded linear operator. For each g∈Y∗, the continuous linear functional g attains its maximum on that compact set. Symmetry of S(Bc0​​) implies that maximum is ∥S∗g∥, so S∗g attains its norm on Bc0​​. By norm attainment on the vanishing sequence space,
S∗(Y∗)⊆c00​⊂ℓ1​.
(14)
Regard the Banach-space adjoint S∗ as a bounded linear operator from the Banach space Y∗ into c00​ with its inherited ℓ1​ norm. The completeness of the dual space holds even if Y is incomplete. The preceding Baire category theorem argument shows that S∗ has finite rank.
If S∗(Y∗)=span{f1​,…,fr​}, set N=⋂j=1r​kerfj​. For x∈N and every g∈Y∗, g(Sx)=(S∗g)(x)=0. The Hahn-Banach theorem implies that continuous linear functionals separate points of Y, so Sx=0. Thus N⊆kerS, and
rankS≤codimN≤r<∞.​
(15)
This proves compact closed-ball image forces finite rank on c0.
