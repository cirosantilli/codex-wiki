Continuously differentiable functions on a compact interval form a Banach algebra (C1[0,1],∥f∥=∥f∥∞​+∥f′∥∞​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 1
With complex scalars and pointwise multiplication, the product rule gives a submultiplicative norm. For a Cauchy sequence, uniform limits f of the functions and g of their derivatives satisfy f(t)−f(0)=∫0t​g(s)ds, proving f′=g and completeness. Polynomials are dense in this norm: approximate f′ uniformly by polynomials and integrate, fixing the initial value. This also identifies the character space of C1 on a compact interval.
