Linear attenuation coefficient (μ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic radiation X-ray X-ray attenuation
OurBigBook.com Words: 172 Articles: 3
The linear attenuation coefficient is the infinitesimal probability of removal from the uncollided beam per unit path length. Its units are inverse length. In the homogeneous monoenergetic model, μ=−dlnI/dx; 1/μ is the thickness leaving fraction e−1 uncollided. The mass attenuation coefficient is μ/ρ for material mass density ρ. Removing a photon by scattering of light does not imply deposition of all its energy at that point; attenuation and energy absorption are different quantities.[ref]
