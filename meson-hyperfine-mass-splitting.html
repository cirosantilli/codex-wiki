Meson hyperfine mass splitting (M1​−M0​=A/(m1​m2​))

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron Meson
OurBigBook.com Words: 109
In a simple constituent quark model, a spin-dependent contribution AS1​⋅S2​/(m1​m2​) splits a spin-zero and spin-one meson having the same spatial configuration. For two spin-half constituents, addition of angular momentum gives S1​⋅S2​=[S(S+1)−3/2]/2, equal to −3/4 for S=0 and +1/4 for S=1. Thus
M0​=Mbase​−4m1​m2​3A​,M1​=Mbase​+4m1​m2​A​,M1​−M0​=m1​m2​A​.
(26)
The spin-averaged mass is (M0​+3M1​)/4=Mbase​. Positive A places the vector state above the pseudoscalar. Determining A from just M0​ needs an additional assumption about Mbase​; setting it equal to m1​+m2​ neglects all other spin-independent binding terms. A splitting below the pion mass closes a one-pion strong decay to the lower meson, often making the allowed electromagnetic transition with photon emission dominant.
