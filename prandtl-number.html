Prandtl number (Pr=ν/κT​)

 ... Thermodynamics Heat Thermal conduction Fourier's law Thermal conductivity Thermal diffusivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The Prandtl number is the ratio of kinematic viscosity to thermal diffusivity. It compares the rates at which momentum and temperature variations diffuse. In thermal-diffusion units the Boussinesq equations have momentum inertia multiplied by Pr−1; large, rather than small, Prandtl number supplies the usual instantaneous Stokes flow limit.
