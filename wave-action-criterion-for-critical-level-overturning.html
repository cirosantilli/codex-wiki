Wave-action criterion for critical-level overturning (cot4θ<Bk3ω/(N3sin3θ))

For upward internal gravity waves, let B=cgz​N2Aζ2​/ω be constant. Combining cgz​=(N/k)sinθcos2θ with the monochromatic internal-wave overturning criterion gives cot4θ<Bk3ω/(N3sin3θ). Near a critical level the onset balance is cotθ∼(Bk3ω/N3)1/4. This is compatible with ω=Ncosθ; it fixes the overturning point rather than redefining the dispersion relation.
