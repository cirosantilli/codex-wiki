Helix

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Curve Space curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 2
A helix is a space curve that winds around an axis while advancing along it at a constant rate. A circular helix can be parametrized by (Rcost,Rsint,at).
