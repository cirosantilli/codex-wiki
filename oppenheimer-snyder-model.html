Oppenheimer-Snyder model

The Oppenheimer-Snyder model describes a homogeneous ball of pressureless matter collapsing from rest, with a closed FLRW metric inside and a Schwarzschild metric outside. In geometric units its interior is ds2=−dτ2+a2(dχ2+sin2χdΩ2), with a=amax​(1+cosη)/2 and τ=amax​(η+sinη)/2. The boundary is a fixed comoving angle. Density conservation gives ρa3 constant, and the Friedmann equation at the initial turning point gives 8πρ0​amax2​/3=1.
