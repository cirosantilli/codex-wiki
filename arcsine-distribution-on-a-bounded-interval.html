Arcsine distribution on a bounded interval (Y=a+(b−a)X,a<b)

OurBigBook.com Words: 128
Specify whether two parameters mean endpoints or location and width. With endpoints a<b, define Y=a+(b−a)X for a standard arcsine distribution variable X. Then
fY​(y)=π(y−a)(b−y)​1​,a<y<b,FY​(y)=π2​arcsinb−ay−a​​.
(14)
This is a location-scale family with location a and width b−a. Its mean is (a+b)/2 and its variance is (b−a)2/8. A convention using parameters a,w for location and width instead has right endpoint a+w, so it must not be read as having endpoints a,w.[ref]
For example, a chosen interval [−2,6] gives mean 2 and variance 8, while its density still concentrates toward both endpoints. It is a bounded probability distribution; its endpoint singularities do not make its support infinite.
