Since D2=I, the processing is a unitary transformation. Direct multiplication gives DUj​∣s⟩=(5∣j⟩−∑k=j​∣k⟩)/(33​), establishing success probability 25/27 for each label. This calculation establishes the achieved probability, without requiring an optimality claim.
