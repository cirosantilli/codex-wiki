Compact closed-ball image forces finite rank on c0 (S(Bc0​​) compact⟹rankS<∞)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Compact operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 106
Suppose a linear map S:c0​→Y has compact image of the closed unit ball. It is bounded, and each g∈Y∗ attains its maximum on this compact image. Thus S∗g attains its norm, so norm attainment on the vanishing sequence space implies S∗(Y∗)⊆c00​. The Baire category theorem applied to the inverse images of the first n coordinate spans makes this adjoint finite rank: these are closed linear subspaces covering the complete continuous dual space Y∗, so one has interior and is all of Y∗. Finally the Hahn-Banach theorem identifies kerS as the common kernel of S∗(Y∗), proving finite rank of S. Ordinary compact operators require only relative compactness of the ball image, so they need not have finite rank.
