Spectral kernel expansion of a reversible chain (Pt(x,y)/π(y))

For a finite irreducible Markov chain satisfying detailed balance with π, its transition matrix is a self-adjoint operator for ⟨f,g⟩π​=∑x​π(x)f(x)g(x). For a real orthonormal eigenbasis (fj​) with eigenvalues (λj​),
π(y)Pt(x,y)​=∑j​λjt​fj​(x)fj​(y).
(11)
Indeed, apply the spectral decomposition of Pt to the function 1{y}​/π(y), whose coefficient against fj​ is fj​(y). For t=0, every eigenvalue multiplier is interpreted as one.
