Failure of first-order Sobolev embedding into Linfinity in two dimensions (H1(U)↪L∞(U))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space Sobolev embedding theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
On a two-dimensional domain containing the origin, a logarithmic singularity such as u(r)=(log(1/r))α with 0<α<1/2 belongs locally to H1 but is unbounded. Thus the critical first-order Sobolev space does not embed into L∞.
