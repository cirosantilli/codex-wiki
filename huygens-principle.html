Huygens' principle

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Huygens-Fresnel principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Each point on a wavefront acts as a source of secondary wavelets, whose envelope gives the next wavefront. The geometric construction gives refraction from secondary wavelets. The Huygens-Fresnel principle additionally keeps the amplitudes and phases needed to calculate interference and diffraction.
