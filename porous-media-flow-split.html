Porous-media flow

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 93
Porous-media flow is fluid motion through an interconnected pore space. At small pore-scale Reynolds number, its volume-averaged velocity is commonly governed by Darcy's law.
