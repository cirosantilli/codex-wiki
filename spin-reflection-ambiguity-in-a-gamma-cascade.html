Spin-reflection ambiguity in a gamma cascade

 ... Atomic nucleus Nuclear structure Nuclear de-excitation Gamma decay Nuclear gamma-ray multipole selection rules Missing gamma lines and matrix-element hindrance
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
If a cascade determines only absolute spin differences relative to a known reference spin, reflection of all inferred spins around that reference can preserve the same multipole pattern. Nonnegative-spin constraints, independently known spins, angular correlations or polarization measurements may break the ambiguity. A set of line energies and lowest-multipole labels need not do so.
