K3 surface

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Complex surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 643 Articles: 12
A complex K3 surface is a compact, simply connected complex surface with trivial canonical bundle. For projective algebraic surfaces, one can equivalently require a trivial canonical bundle and H1(X,OX​)=0. Nonprojective complex K3 surfaces are also included, which is essential for the full moduli space of Ricci-flat K3 metrics.
