Center-of-mass velocity (VCM​=∑i​mi​vi​/∑i​mi​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Center of mass
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For fixed classical particle masses, differentiating the center of mass position gives the displayed mass-weighted velocity. The total momentum is MVCM​. The relativistic zero-momentum frame instead has velocity c2P/E; treating rest mass as the only weight there generally fails.
