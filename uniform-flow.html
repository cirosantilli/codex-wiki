Uniform flow (u(x)=U)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A uniform flow has the same velocity at every position. A constant velocity field has zero divergence and zero vorticity; a velocity potential is ϕ(x)=U⋅x. It describes the remote ambient flow in idealized obstacle and line vortex problems.
