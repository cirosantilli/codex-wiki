Rarita-Schwinger field (γμνρ∂ν​ψρ​=0)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Dirac spinor Spinor representation of the Lorentz group
OurBigBook.com Words: 256 Articles: 3
A vector-spinor describes a spin-three-halves field after its lower-spin components are removed by constraints and, in the massless theory, gauge equivalence. The massless equation is invariant under ψμ​↦ψμ​+∂μ​η. Gamma-trace gauge and the field equation give γ⋅ψ=∂⋅ψ=∂ψμ​=0. Only helicities ±3/2 remain. A four-dimensional Majorana field has two physical polarizations; a complex field also has independent antiparticle modes. The gravitino is the supergravity realization of this field.
