Predictable rectangle (B×(s,t],B∈Fs​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Predictable process Predictable sigma-algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A predictable rectangle has the displayed form for 0≤s<t. Its indicator is a left-continuous adapted process, and these rectangles generate the predictable sigma-algebra on positive times. If the time-zero slice is included, also use B×{0} with B∈F0​.
