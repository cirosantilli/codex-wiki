Element distinctness problem

 Home Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
Decide whether all input elements are distinct. In the comparison model, this needs Ω(nlogn) comparisons in the worst case. An accepting decision-tree leaf for distinct keys must force a total order: incomparable keys could otherwise be made equal consistently with its strict comparison outcomes. Thus the n! possible orders require different accepting leaves, giving a decision-tree depth of at least log3​(n!)=Ω(nlogn) even for three-way comparisons.
