Linear cosmological transfer maps the primordial bispectrum to the reduced CMB bispectrum. A Fourier representation of the momentum delta function and three Rayleigh plane-wave expansions separate the radial transfer integrals from a Gaunt integral. The six angular factors and three Fourier measures give (4π)6/(2π)9=(2/π)3. The observer-position phase cancels by momentum conservation; the remaining radial position is an auxiliary integration variable.
