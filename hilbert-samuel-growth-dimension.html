Hilbert–Samuel growth dimension (d(R)=degHR​(t))

 ... Algebra Commutative algebra Ring Graded ring Associated graded ring Hilbert-Samuel polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 116 Articles: 1
For a Noetherian local ring (R,m), let HR​(t)=length(R/mt). Its eventual Hilbert-Samuel polynomial has degree d(R), equivalently the pole order at 1 of the Hilbert series of grm​R. This is the local length-growth invariant, not the embedding dimension. The prime-chain lower bound for local length proves dimR≤d(R); the full local dimension theorem gives equality.
