Flory interaction parameter (χ)

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry) Polymer solution Flory–Huggins solution theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
The Flory interaction parameter measures the excess free-energy cost of unlike contacts in Flory–Huggins solution theory. For a simple lattice of coordination number zc​ and contact energies ϵps​, ϵpp​ and ϵss​,
χ=kB​Tzc​​[ϵps​−2ϵpp​+ϵss​​].
(2)
Real effective parameters may also contain entropic contributions and composition dependence. With site volume v0​ and composition-independent χ, the effective two-segment excluded volume is veff​=v0​(1−2χ). Positive, zero and negative veff​ correspond respectively to a good solvent, the theta temperature condition and a poor solvent. In particular, χ=1/2 means cancellation of leading repulsive and attractive contributions; it does not mean that every molecular interaction vanishes.
