Ladder operator

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Highest-weight representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 76 Articles: 2
A ladder operator maps weight vectors to weight vectors whose weights differ by one root. Positive-root vectors raise weights and negative-root vectors lower them.
