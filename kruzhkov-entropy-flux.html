For ηk​(z)=∣z−k∣, the displayed flux is continuous at z=k and has derivative sgn(z−k)f′(z) almost everywhere. On a bounded state range with ∣f′∣≤M, its symmetric two-state version Q(a,b) obeys ∣Q(a,b)∣≤M∣a−b∣ and is Lipschitz in both arguments. The vanishing factor at a=b removes any sign-function jump contribution.
