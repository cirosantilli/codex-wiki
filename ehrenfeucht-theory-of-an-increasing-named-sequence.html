Ehrenfeucht theory of an increasing named sequence

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Model theory Quantifier elimination Dense linear order without endpoints
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
Add constants c0​<c1​<⋯ to the theory of dense linear orders without endpoints. The three countable models have respectively a least upper bound for the named sequence, no upper bound, or upper bounds with no least one. The unbounded model is an atomic model; the bounded model with no least upper bound is an omega-saturated model. The remaining model omits the type ci​<x<d over its least-upper-bound parameter d.
