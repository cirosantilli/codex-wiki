Bidirectional reflectance distribution function (fr​(λ,ωi​,ωo​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Radiometry Reflectance
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A function relating reflected radiance in one direction to incident irradiance from another direction at a surface location. A local nonemissive, nonfluorescent optical reflection model integrates fr​Li​cosθi​ over incident solid angle to obtain outgoing radiance. Transmission, wavelength conversion and nonlocal transport need additional models.
