Finite approximation of a lazy list

 Home Computer science Data structure Linked list Lazy list Partial list
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
A finite list prefix followed by bottom records only the constructors known so far. The chain ⊥, x1​:⊥, x1​:x2​:⊥,… approximates an infinite lazy list. A Scott continuous map is determined on the limit by its values on these approximations. This justifies extending an identity proved on finite partial lists to definable operations on infinite lists; equality merely on finite terminating lists does not provide the same premise.
