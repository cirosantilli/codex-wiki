Distance (d(x,y))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A measure of separation between locations or objects. In a metric space, distance is specified by a metric satisfying positivity, symmetry and the triangle inequality. The familiar straight-line separation in Euclidean space is Euclidean distance; other metrics can assign different distances to the same pair of points.
