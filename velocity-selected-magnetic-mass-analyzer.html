Velocity-selected magnetic mass analyzer

 Home Chemistry Mass spectrometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 192
Reuse the velocity selector analysis in the Thomson charge-to-mass experiment, but now send positive ions with q=ze into a separate magnetic analyzer. The selector fields Es​ and Bs​ give v0​=Es​/Bs​. If the analyzer field Ba​ is perpendicular to the selected velocity, its gyroradius gives
qm​=v0​Ba​r​=Es​Ba​Bs​r​,D=2r
(30)
for a semicircular return to the entrance plane. The displayed impact separation D is a diameter. This formula is linear in r at fixed selected speed; the fixed-electric potential difference model above instead gives m/q∝r2. They impose different experimental conditions.
For a chosen example, take Es​=2.00×104V/m, Bs​=0.200T, and Ba​=0.500T. Thus v0​=1.00×105m/s. Approximating an ion mass by 24u and setting q=e gives r≈49.75mm and D≈99.50mm. Here u denotes the unified atomic mass unit. These are stipulated teaching inputs, not a reported Aston measurement or an exact magnesium atomic mass.
A finite accepted speed range broadens the radius distribution. At fixed m/q and Ba​, δr/r=δv/v. At fixed electric charge and mass, K=mv2/2 gives δv/v≃δK/(2K) for a small kinetic energy spread. A one-percent kinetic energy spread therefore produces roughly a half-percent radius spread before other broadening is included. Its width convention must be specified before converting that statement into a mass resolving power.
