Vanishing of a finitely generated module near a prime (MP​=0⟺AnnR​(M)⊆P)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Localization of a ring Localization of a module
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For a finitely generated module M=(m1​,…,mt​) and a prime ideal P, if MP​=0, choose si​∈/P annihilating mi​. Their product f∈/P annihilates all of M. Conversely such an f becomes a unit in RP​, forcing MP​=0. Thus
{P:MP​=0}=⋃f∈AnnR​(M)​D(f)=SpecR∖V(AnnR​(M)).
(2)
This identifies the support of a module M with the Zariski-closed set V(AnnR​(M)). For M=0, use f=1. No Noetherian assumption on R is required.
