Frobenius inner product (⟨A,B⟩F​=tr(A∗B))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Inner product
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 2
For complex matrices, the inner product ⟨A,B⟩F​=tr(A∗B) is conjugate-linear in its first argument. On real symmetric matrices it becomes tr(AB). In particular ⟨A,xxT⟩F​=xTAx, identifying quadratic forms with linear tests on matrices.
