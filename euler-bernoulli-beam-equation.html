Euler-Bernoulli beam equation (utt​+cb2​uxxxx​=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 624 Articles: 9
The Euler-Bernoulli beam equation models transverse linear bending waves in a slender beam. The normalized equation has cb​=1 and may be reduced by the Schrodinger factorization of the elastic beam equation. Prescribing displacement and curvature at an endpoint supplies two boundary traces for its fourth spatial derivative.
