Meridian of a knot (μ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Knot Knot exterior
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A meridian is an oriented simple closed curve on the boundary of a knot exterior that bounds a disk in the removed tubular neighborhood and has linking number one with the knot.
Viewed inside the removed tubular neighborhood, this is a meridian of a solid torus.
