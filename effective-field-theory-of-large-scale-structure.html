Effective field theory of large-scale structure (EFTofLSS)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe
OurBigBook.com Words: 284 Articles: 8
The effective field theory of large-scale structure represents unresolved short-scale dynamics by an effective stress tensor. Its leading deterministic correction is proportional to k2δ(1) and renormalizes ultraviolet-sensitive loop contributions.
