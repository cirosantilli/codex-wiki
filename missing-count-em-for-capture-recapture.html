Missing-count EM for capture-recapture (E[nij​∣m,ϕ,P]=Ni​wij​/Ci​)

Let Ni​=Ri​−∑s=i+1J​mis​ count individuals never recaptured after release i. Partition them into death-interval counts nij​ and terminal survivors niJ​. Their unnormalized weights are wij​=(1−ϕj​)∏ℓ=ij−1​ϕℓ​(1−Pℓ+1​) for j<J, and wiJ​=∏ℓ=iJ−1​ϕℓ​(1−Pℓ+1​). Conditional counts are multinomial with normalizer Ci​=∑j=iJ​wij​. The EM algorithm replaces all missing counts by the displayed conditional expectations, then maximizes the resulting complete-data log likelihood. Survival and detection updates are expected successes divided by expected opportunities. A flat observed-likelihood ridge remains nonidentifiable even if each conditional complete-data maximization is explicit.
