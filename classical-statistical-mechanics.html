Classical statistical mechanics

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Statistical mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
classical statistical mechanics assigns probability distributions to classical states. For a Hamiltonian particle system the canonical weight is e−βH on phase space. The weak-exchange, high-temperature limit of a quantum gas supplies the measure ddxddp/(2πℏ)d per particle and the indistinguishable-particle factor 1/N!.
