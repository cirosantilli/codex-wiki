Exponential tail extension of Varadhan's lemma (limM→∞​limsupL​L−1logE[eLF(XL​);F(XL​)>M]=−∞)

For a continuous F and a family satisfying a large deviation principle at speed L, the displayed exponential tail condition extends Varadhan's lemma to F unbounded above. Apply the bounded-above result to min(F,M) and split the full integral into F≤M and its tail; the tail is negligible and the truncated variational suprema increase to supx​(F(x)−I(x)). A sufficient condition is limsupL​L−1logEeγLF(XL​)<∞ for some γ>1, since the tail is at most e−(γ−1)LMEeγLF(XL​).
