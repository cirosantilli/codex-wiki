Cyclically reduced sequence in an HNN extension

 ... Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Bass-Serre theory HNN extension Normal form theorem for an HNN extension Reduced sequence in an HNN extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A cyclically reduced sequence has no pinch in an HNN extension either internally or across its cyclic junction. If its stable-letter length is positive, every positive power remains reduced, so Britton's lemma gives infinite order. Repeated cyclic conjugation and pinch removal shows that any finite-order element of an HNN extension is conjugate into its base group.
