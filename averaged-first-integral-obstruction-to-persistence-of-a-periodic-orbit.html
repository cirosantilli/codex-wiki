Averaged first-integral obstruction to persistence of a periodic orbit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Energy balance method
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 1
Suppose V is a first integral of an unperturbed planar system and V˙=εG+O(ε2) after perturbation. A periodic orbit converging to an unperturbed orbit γ can persist only if
∫γ​Gdt=0.
(7)
Indeed, the net change of V over every period is zero. A nonzero averaged drift therefore obstructs persistence.
