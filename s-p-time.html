S–P time (Δt=tS​−tP​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Seismology Earthquake location by arrival times Earthquake location from interphase delays
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The delay between corresponding S wave and P wave arrivals. For a common uniform path of length r, Δt=r(1/Vs​−1/Vp​), cancelling earthquake origin time. Matched phase identification and an appropriate velocity model are needed to infer distance.
