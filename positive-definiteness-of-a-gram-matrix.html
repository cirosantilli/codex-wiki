Positive definiteness of a Gram matrix (cTGc=∥∑i​ci​xi​∥2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Inner product Gram matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For real vectors xi​ in a Euclidean inner product space, their Gram matrix Gij​=xiT​xj​ satisfies the displayed identity by expanding the squared norm. Thus every Gram matrix is a positive semidefinite matrix, and it is a positive-definite matrix exactly when the vector family is linearly independent. The complex version replaces transposes by conjugate transposes and uses c∗Gc=∥∑i​ci​xi​∥2.
