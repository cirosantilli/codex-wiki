Recovery after a finite-duration stress load (ε(t)=E1​σ0​​(1−e−th​/τ)e−(t−th​)/τ,t>th​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 65
Apply constant stress σ0​ at t=0 and remove it at t=th​. For series spring and Voigt-branch creep, the series spring recovers its strain σ0​/E0​ immediately on unloading. The Kelvin-Voigt model branch has retardation time τ=η/E1​ and must keep continuous strain because an instantaneous dashpot displacement would require an impulse. Its post-unloading equation is ηε˙+E1​ε=0. Solving with the branch's strain at th​ gives the title formula. Thus all remaining strain eventually recovers: this standard linear solid model has no permanent set.
