Covariance of a field equation does not fix homogeneous solutions

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz transformation Lorentz covariance
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
If a differential operator L is a linear map and has nonzero kernel of a linear map, the equation LA=J determines A only up to a homogeneous solution. Lorentz covariance of L and J therefore does not force independently selected solutions in different inertial frames to obey a particular tensor transformation law. A covariant Green function and consistently transformed homogeneous data do provide such a choice. For the four-potential in Lorenz gauge, □Aμ=−μ0​Jμ still permits Aμ↦Aμ+∂μχ with □χ=0, a residual Lorenz gauge symmetry. Constant spatial potentials for zero sources are a concrete example.
