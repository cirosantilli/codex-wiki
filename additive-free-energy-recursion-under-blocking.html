For dimensionless free energy per site F(u,C)=f(u)+βC, an exact blocking kernel preserves the partition function and gives F(u,C)=b−DF(u′,C′). Thus f(u)=b−Df(u′)+g(u), where g(u)=β[b−DC′−C] is the additive contribution from the eliminated degrees of freedom. Repeating this equality gives the displayed sum. After subtracting analytic backgrounds, the singular free-energy density obeys the homogeneous scaling hypothesis for critical phenomena, subject to marginal or dangerous-variable qualifications.
