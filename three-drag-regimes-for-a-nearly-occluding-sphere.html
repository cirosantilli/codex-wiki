Three drag regimes for a nearly occluding sphere (F∗∼L∗+3πϵ−5/23πL∗ϵ−5/2+4π2ϵ−3​)

Use F∗=F/(6πμaU) and L∗=4L/(3a). The control-volume drag formula for a sphere in a tube gives the displayed uniform leading drag. If L∗≪ϵ−1/2, F∗∼(4π/3)ϵ−1/2 and local annular shear dominates. If ϵ−1/2≪L∗≪ϵ−5/2, F∗∼L∗ and the Hagen-Poiseuille flow resistance of the long tube dominates. If L∗≫ϵ−5/2, F∗∼3πϵ−5/2 and pressure-driven bypass through the narrow gap dominates. The laboratory flow is piston-like in the first two limits and negligible in the third.
