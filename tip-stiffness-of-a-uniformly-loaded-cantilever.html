Tip stiffness of a uniformly loaded cantilever (keff​=8B/L3)

 ... Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biopolymer mechanics Stokesian dynamics of an elastic filament Small-slope elastohydrodynamic filament equation Uniform-load bending of a clamped filament
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
For a uniform-load bending of a clamped filament, total load F=wL and tip displacement δ=wL4/(8B) obey Hooke's law in the form F=keff​δ, with keff​=8B/L3. This stiffness depends on the specified distributed loading. A concentrated tip force instead gives 3B/L3, so equal total forces with different distributions are not interchangeable.
