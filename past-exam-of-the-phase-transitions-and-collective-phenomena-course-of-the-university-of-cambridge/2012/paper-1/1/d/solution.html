Hyperscaling adds the physical assumption that the singular free energy associated with one correlation volume is of order kB​Tc​:
fs​ξd∼kB​Tc​,fs​∼ξ−d.
(7)
At fixed scaled field x=h/sΔ, this predicts fs​∼sdν, whereas the given thermodynamic form has power 2−α. Hence
dν=2−α.​
(8)
The product gf​(x)[gξ​(x)]d can be a finite dimensionless function of fixed x; it need not be constant. The homogeneity of ξ alone is insufficient: the correlation-volume free-energy assumption supplies the connection. Above an upper critical dimension, a dangerously irrelevant coupling can invalidate this argument; at a marginal dimension logarithms may qualify pure powers.
