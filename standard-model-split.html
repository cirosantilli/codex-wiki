Standard Model (SM)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 9k Articles: 225
The Standard Model is a gauge theory with gauge group SU(3)c​×SU(2)L​×U(1)Y​. Its Higgs field breaks the electroweak subgroup to the electromagnetic U(1)Q​.
