Thom isomorphism in complex cobordism (MUq(X)≅MUq+r(Th(ξ)))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Cobordism Complex cobordism
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For a rank-r real bundle with chosen stable complex normal orientation, the complex-cobordism Thom class gives the displayed isomorphism by a↦π∗auξ​. Stabilizing by a trivial complex bundle suspends the Thom space and its class compatibly. The class of an actual complex rank-n bundle has degree 2n.
