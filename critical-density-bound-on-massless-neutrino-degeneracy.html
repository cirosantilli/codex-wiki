Combining the degenerate neutrino and antineutrino energy density with a critical density bound constrains the neutrino degeneracy parameter through the fourth root of the available energy budget. The numerical bound depends on Tν,0​/Tγ,0​ and the number of degenerate flavors. The standard ratio (4/11)1/3 assumes neutrino decoupling before electron-positron annihilation in cosmology; it should be stated as a thermal-history assumption if the degeneracy is very large. The massless formula is inappropriate for a species that is nonrelativistic today.
