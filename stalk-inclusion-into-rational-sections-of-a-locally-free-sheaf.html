Stalk inclusion into rational sections of a locally free sheaf (FP​↪Rat(F))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Rational section of a sheaf of modules
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
On an irreducible variety, every nonempty open subset is dense, so a germ determines a rational section. For a locally free sheaf, this map is injective: a local section which vanishes on a dense open has all its regular coefficient functions zero, hence is zero. Local freeness is important; a section of a torsion sheaf can vanish generically without having zero germ.
