For the point interaction in a second-order differential equation Lα​=∂x2​+αδc​ on (0,ℓ), use homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions. The unperturbed Green function is
G0​(x,ξ)=−ℓmin(x,ξ)(ℓ−max(x,ξ))​,
(20)
with a positive unit derivative jump at x=ξ. Whenever 1+αG0​(c,c)=0,
Gα​(x,ξ)=G0​(x,ξ)−1+αG0​(c,c)αG0​(x,c)G0​(c,ξ)​.
(21)
To derive this rank-one operator correction, move the delta coefficient to the right-hand side and apply the unperturbed inverse: Gα​(x,ξ)=G0​(x,ξ)−αG0​(x,c)Gα​(c,ξ). Setting x=c determines Gα​(c,ξ) and gives the formula. Its denominator vanishes exactly at Dirichlet resonance for a point interaction.
The kernel is symmetric. It is continuous at x=c and x=ξ, satisfies [∂x​Gα​]ξ​=1 when ξ=c, and satisfies [∂x​Gα​]c​=−αGα​(c,ξ) away from the source. When ξ=c, the single combined condition is [∂x​Gα​]c​=1−αGα​(c,c). These are the transmission conditions for a delta coefficient, including a source at the interaction.
