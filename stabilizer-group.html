Stabilizer group

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum theory Quantum circuit Pauli group
OurBigBook.com Words: 226 Articles: 9
A stabilizer group is an abelian subgroup of the Pauli group that does not contain −I. Its simultaneous positive eigenspace is its stabilizer subspace.
