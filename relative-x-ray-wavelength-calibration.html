Relative X-ray wavelength calibration (λ1​/λ2​=n2​sinθ1​/(n1​sinθ2​))

For two lines measured with the same interplanar spacing at the same temperature, division of Bragg's law gives
λ2​λ1​​=n1​sinθ2​n2​sinθ1​​.
(15)
The common d cancels. Consequently a precise wavelength ratio can coexist with an uncertain conversion to SI length. In contrast, angular calibration errors generally survive: a shared small zero offset ϵ changes the inferred ratio fractionally by approximately (cotθ1​−cotθ2​)ϵ. Nearby lines partially cancel this effect, but widely separated angles need not. Different crystals, temperatures or refraction corrections also prevent unqualified cancellation.
