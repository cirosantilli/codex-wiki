Enriched category

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 40
For a monoidal category (V,⊗,I), a V-enriched category has hom-objects in V, composition morphisms C(B,C)⊗C(A,B)→C(A,C), and unit morphisms I→C(A,A) satisfying associativity and unit laws.
