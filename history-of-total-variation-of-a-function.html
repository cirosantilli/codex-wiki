Camille Jordan developed bounded variation as part of his work in real analysis; his treatment of functions and arc length was part of the influence of his analysis textbooks.[ref] The Jordan decomposition of a function of bounded variation expresses the underlying idea especially well: complicated changes of direction can be separated into two functions that only increase.
