Force of infection (λ(t))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Compartmental models (epidemiology) SEIR model
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
The force of infection is the instantaneous infection hazard experienced by a susceptible individual. Under homogeneous frequency-dependent mixing it is βI(t)/N.
