OurBigBook.com Words: 51
An ideal PID controller combines proportional control, integral action, and a term proportional to the derivative of the tracking error. With kd​=0, it gives proportional-integral control. The integral stores past error, while the derivative responds to its current rate of change. Practical implementations must account for measurement noise, which an ideal differentiator can amplify.
