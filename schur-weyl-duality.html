Schur–Weyl duality (V⊗n=⨁λ⊢n​Sλ⊗Dλ​(V))

The commuting actions of the symmetric group and the general linear group on a tensor power are mutual commutants. The displayed sum ranges over partitions with at most dimV rows. The Specht modules and Schur modules are the simple factors for the two actions. In particular a primitive Young symmetrizer selects one copy of the matching Schur module.
