Elastic beam

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
An elastic beam is a slender body that supports bending moments and transverse loads. In the small-slope Euler-Bernoulli approximation its bending moment is M=EIyxx​, and transverse momentum balance gives ρAytt​+EIyxxxx​=q for a uniform beam under distributed force per length q. Here I is the second moment of area; boundary conditions specify the end constraints and applied moments and forces.
