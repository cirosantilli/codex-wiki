Exponential map (expp​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Geodesic
OurBigBook.com Words: 170 Articles: 4
For p in a Riemannian manifold and v sufficiently close to zero in Tp​M, let γv​ be the geodesic with γv​(0)=p and γ˙​v​(0)=v. The exponential map is
expp​(v)=γv​(1).
(97)
Equivalently, expp​(tv)=γv​(t) wherever both sides are defined.
