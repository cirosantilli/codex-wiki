Contraction and admissible fixed spaces

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation Smooth representation of a locally profinite group Admissible representation of a locally profinite group
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
For the upper-triangular subgroup of GLn​(F), conjugation by diag(ϖ−(n−1),…,1) expands the upper-unipotent coordinates. For a decomposed compact-open subgroup K=T0​U0​, its conjugates eventually contain K, while their fixed-vector spaces are isomorphic. Finite dimension therefore forces those spaces to be equal. Inverse conjugation contracts any fixed unipotent element into U0​, proving that the unipotent radical acts trivially in every admissible representation of this triangular group.
