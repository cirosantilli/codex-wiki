To isolate conversion losses, suppose a converter receives 100 blue photons at λb​=450nm, absorbs a fraction a=0.60, and has fluorescence quantum yield Φ=0.90: 0.90 converted photons per absorbed blue photon. Stipulate that every converted photon has λc​=560nm, that unabsorbed blue is transmitted, and that both outputs escape without further losses. The output is then 40 blue and 54 converted photons. The total photon yield is 0.94, while the retained optical energy fraction is
Pbefore​Pafter​​=(1−a)+aΦλc​λb​​=0.40+0.60×0.90×560450​=0.833929.
(16)
The lower energy of each converted photon matters even if Φ=1. Combining this stipulated converter with the preceding chosen light-emitting diode gives Pafter​=16.5430mW and an overall optical/electrical power ratio 0.258485. These chosen narrow lines and photon counts illustrate conversion accounting; they do not establish a white appearance, color quality or luminous efficacy. Those require the full spectrum and a model of human visual sensitivity.
