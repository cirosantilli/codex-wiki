Perseus cluster

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Galaxy cluster
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A galaxy cluster whose X-ray-emitting core is a well-studied example of interaction between radio bubbles and cooling gas. density fronts and ripples provide diagnostics of the mechanical energy released by the central active galaxy.[ref]
