One-cocycle

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group cohomology Group cocycle
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 3
For a G-module M, a one-cocycle is a map f:G→M satisfying f(gh)=f(g)+g⋅f(h).
