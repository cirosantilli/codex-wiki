Exponential form of Lévy characteristic functions (φXt​​(u)=etη(u))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Lévy process Characteristic exponent of a Lévy process
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
Independent increments and stationary increments make a Lévy process's characteristic functions multiplicative in time. Together with continuity of Lévy characteristic functions and the value one at time zero, this forces an exponential. A continuous local complex logarithm is additive: its failure of additivity would be a continuous integer multiple of 2πi, hence zero. The Cauchy functional equation and subdivision then give the exponential at every time. This construction of the characteristic exponent of a Lévy process precedes the Lévy–Khintchine formula describing the exponent's possible form.
