For centered unit-variance independent and identically distributed increments with m4​=Eξ4<∞, an interpolated increment is N−1/2∑j​cj​ξj​. Here cj​ is the length of the overlap of (Ns,Nt) with the jth unit cell, so 0≤cj​≤1 and ∑cj​=N(t−s). Independence gives its fourth moment as N−2[3(∑cj2​)2+(m4​−3)∑cj4​]. This is at most max(3,m4​)N−2(∑cj2​)2, and ∑cj2​≤N(t−s) gives the bound, including intervals crossing a mesh point. A uniform fourth-moment continuity criterion yields tightness of probability measures on C0​[0,1].
