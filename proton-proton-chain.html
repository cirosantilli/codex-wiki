Proton–proton chain

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar nuclear fusion
OurBigBook.com Words: 483 Articles: 8
The proton–proton chain converts hydrogen to helium and dominates hydrogen burning in stars near and below the Sun's mass. Near 2×107K, its specific energy generation is roughly proportional to ρT4.
