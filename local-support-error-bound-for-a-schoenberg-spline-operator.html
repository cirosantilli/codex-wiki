Local-support error bound for a Schoenberg spline operator (∥Vf−f∥∞​≤ω(f,k∣Δ∣))

 ... Analysis Uniform approximation Spline (mathematics) Spline approximation B-spline Schoenberg spline operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
For nonnegative partition-normalized order-k B-splines, let Vf(t)=∑i​f(τi​)Ni​(t) with each sample point inside [ti​,ti+k​]. If Ni​(t) is nonzero, both t and τi​ lie in an interval of length at most k∣Δ∣. Their function values differ by at most the modulus of continuity at that length. Summing with nonnegative weights of total one proves the displayed bound. With fixed order and vanishing maximum knot gap, uniform continuity gives uniform convergence. The same argument works for bounded piecewise-continuous outputs if full interior knot multiplicities are allowed; their individual continuity should not then be asserted.
