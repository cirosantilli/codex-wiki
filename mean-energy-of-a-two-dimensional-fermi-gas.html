Mean energy of a two-dimensional Fermi gas (U/N=EF​/2)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Fermi-Dirac distribution Fermi gas Two-dimensional free-electron density of states
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A quadratic-dispersion Fermi gas in two dimensions has constant density of states g. At zero temperature, N=gEF​ and U=gEF2​/2. Hence the mean energy is half the Fermi energy, independent of a common spin-degeneracy factor.
