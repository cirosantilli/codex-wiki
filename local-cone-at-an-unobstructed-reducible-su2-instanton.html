Local cone at an unobstructed reducible SU2 instanton (C4k−1/S1)

 ... Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle) Metric connection Unitary connection Anti-self-dual connection ASD deformation complex
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
On a simply connected negative-definite four-manifold, an ASD connection of full circle holonomy has hA0​=1. If hA2​=0, the index formula gives hA1​=8k−2. The neutral diagonal deformation space vanishes because b1​=0, leaving a complex vector space of dimension 4k−1 with stabilizer action z↦λ2z. Kuranishi reduction and the Coulomb slice give the local quotient C4k−1/S1, equivalently the cone on CP4k−2.
