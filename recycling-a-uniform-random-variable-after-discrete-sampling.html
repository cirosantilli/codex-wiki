Recycling a uniform random variable after discrete sampling (V=(U−CJ−1​)/wJ​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Quantile function Inverse transform sampling
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For positive mixture weights wj​ summing to one, set Cj​=∑i≤j​wi​ and choose J=j when Cj−1​≤U<Cj​, where U has a uniform distribution. Then V=(U−CJ−1​)/wJ​ has a uniform distribution independently of J: for 0≤v≤1, Pr(J=j,V≤v)=wj​v. This permits exact finite mixture model sampling with a recycled continuous random variable.
