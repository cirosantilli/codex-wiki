Phosphotyrosine residue (pY)

 Home Biology Protein Polypeptide Amino acid residue Tyrosine residue
OurBigBook.com Words: 16
A phosphotyrosine residue is a tyrosine residue modified by phosphorylation. Its location and surrounding protein primary structure help determine which SH2 domains can recognize it.
