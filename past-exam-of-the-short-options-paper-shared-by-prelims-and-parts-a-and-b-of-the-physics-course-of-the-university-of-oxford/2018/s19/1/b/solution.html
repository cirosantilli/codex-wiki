If the quoted coupling is k=εy​/εx​, then εy​=kεx​. Combining the horizontal factor of eight with k falling from 0.1 to 0.02 gives
εy,old​εy,new​​≃0.10.02​81​=401​.​
(141)
This is the usual leading estimate treating the horizontal emittance in part a as unchanged by small coupling.
If one instead accounts for exact redistribution of a fixed natural-emittance sum, εx​=ε0​/(1+k) and εy​=kε0​/(1+k). Then the horizontal ratio is (1/8)(1.1/1.02)≃0.1348 and the vertical ratio is 0.02696, an improvement by about 37. These are consistent refinements of the stated approximate factor forty, not a second independent reduction to apply afterward.
