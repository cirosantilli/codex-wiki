The target collision energy requires 175 GeV per lepton beam. Its initial energy after the half-linac is 62.5 GeV, so each lepton must gain 112.5 GeV. Define the transformer ratio as accelerating field experienced by the witness divided by decelerating field experienced by the driver. If the useful stage length exhausts the driver's 62.5 GeV while delivering the required gain, then
kT​=62.5112.5​=1.8.​
(147)
This matches depletion to acceleration and avoids leaving unused driver energy at the witness target. It is an ideal energy-transfer estimate, assuming suitable loading and longitudinal field profiles; a field ratio alone does not specify a practical wake or its efficiency.
