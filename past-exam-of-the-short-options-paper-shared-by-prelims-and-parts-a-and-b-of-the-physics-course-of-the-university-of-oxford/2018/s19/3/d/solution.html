The mean witness gradient must be 112.5GeV/1.1m≃102GVm−1. To turn this into a density estimate, assume an accelerating wake amplitude of order the cold plasma wave-breaking scale,
Ew​∼eme​cωp​​,ωp2​=ε0​me​n0​e2​.
(150)
Eliminating ωp​ gives
n0​∼me​c2ε0​Ew2​​≃1.1×1024m−3≃1.1×1018cm−3.​
(151)
The associated plasma wavelength is about 31 micrometres, illustrating why a short driver structure is required. The given length and energy gain do not uniquely fix density without an assumption about wake amplitude relative to the plasma scale. Driver shape, beam loading, relativistic wave-breaking corrections and matching to a Positron witness are substantive additional issues; the estimate is a scale calculation, not a completed accelerator design.
