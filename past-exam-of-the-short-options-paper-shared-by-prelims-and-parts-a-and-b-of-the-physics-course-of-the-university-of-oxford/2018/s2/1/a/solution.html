The distance modulus at 100pc is five magnitudes. Thus the two approximate main-sequence endpoints read from the original figure, mV​≃0,20, correspond to MV​≃−5,15. With the given solar reference,

Use the figure's approximate endpoint effective temperatures 25,000 K and 2500 K. If V-band luminosity is used as a rough bolometric proxy, the Stefan–Boltzmann law gives
R⊙​R∗​​≃(L⊙​L​)1/2(Teff​T⊙​​)2.
(13)
Taking T⊙​≃5800 K gives
Rsmall​≃0.054R⊙​,Rlarge​≃5.4R⊙​.​
(14)
These are schematic estimates, not precision radii. The diagram supplies V magnitudes rather than bolometric luminosities, and hot and cool endpoints need different bolometric corrections. The exact range cannot be recovered without those corrections; the quoted values state the intended approximation explicitly.
