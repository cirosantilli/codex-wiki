for dust. Thus the proper horizon at this epoch is about 0.25 Mpc and its present comoving size about 250 Mpc. The factor three accounts for expansion during light propagation. These scales describe the limited causal domain in the simple decelerating model. The near-uniform CMB temperature across much more widely separated last-scattering regions motivates the horizon problem; the question's rough ct estimate and the exact dust horizon should not be conflated.
