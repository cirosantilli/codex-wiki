At this small redshift, D≃cz/H0​≃20.0 Mpc. The outer angular radius gives Rout​≃Dθ≃21.3 kpc. Approximating the dynamical mass by a spherical equivalent,
Mdyn​(<Rout​)≃GVrot2​Rout​​≃4.9×1011M⊙​.​
(18)
The supplied Tully-Fisher relation gives MV​≃−22.49, and hence LV​≃9.9×1010LV,⊙​. Treating the population as solar-like gives the estimate ΥV​≃1M⊙​/LV,⊙​, so
Mluminous​≃1.0×1011M⊙​.​
(19)
The dynamical estimate exceeds that by about a factor of five; a massive extended dark-matter component is the natural interpretation. Solar-like spectra do not exactly fix the population mass-to-light ratio, and a disk is not spherical, but order-unity corrections do not remove this large outer mass excess under the stated estimate.
