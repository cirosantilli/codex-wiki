Excited neutral states have an energy gap of at least 3R/4 above the ground state. At the temperatures relevant to the plotted maximum their Boltzmann factors are very small despite the finite degeneracy increase. Highly excited levels also require a physical plasma cutoff; the untruncated infinite isolated-atom partition sum is not a valid finite-density model. Ground-state dominance is an approximation in this regime, not a theorem at all temperatures.
