A thin spherical shell of thickness dr has mass 4πr2ρdr. Balancing its inward gravitational force with the pressure difference across its faces gives
drdP​=−r2GMr​ρ​,drdMr​​=4πr2ρ.​
(154)
For a transparent estimate take constant density ρ=3M⊙​/(4πR⊙3​) and negligible surface pressure. Then Mr​=M⊙​r3/R⊙3​, so integration gives
Pc​=8πR⊙4​3GM⊙2​​≃1.3×1014Pa.​
(155)
Combining with the ideal gas law and μ≃0.6 yields Tc​≃μmH​GM⊙​/(2kB​R⊙​)≃7×106 K. The less specific virial scaling gives Tc​∼μmH​GM⊙​/(kB​R⊙​)∼107 K.
The real Sun is strongly centrally concentrated: a uniform-density model substantially underestimates central pressure and is not a detailed solar model. Its value is a justified scale estimate, while more realistic structure produces central temperatures of order 1.5×107 K and central pressures of order 1016 Pa. Hydrostatic equilibrium alone does not uniquely determine both profiles without an equation of state and a density/thermal model.
