Factorizing the projector found in part b gives the normalized eigenfunctions
φn​(x)=a2​​sinanπ(x+a/2)​,−a/2<x<a/2,n≥1,​
(116)
with zero continuation outside. Their squared integrals are one and different positive integers give orthogonal functions. These are the familiar infinite-square-well states shifted so that the well centre is at zero. Each eigenfunction has an arbitrary overall constant phase, which cancels from the projector.
