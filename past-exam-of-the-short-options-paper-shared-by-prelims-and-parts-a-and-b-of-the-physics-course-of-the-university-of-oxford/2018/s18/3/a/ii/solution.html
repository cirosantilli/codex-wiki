If Av=λv for A=αi​ or β, then A2=I implies λ2=1. The polynomial x2−1 has distinct roots, so such a matrix is diagonalizable and
λ=+1 or −1.​
(124)
Anticommutation with another invertible Dirac matrix maps a +1 eigenspace to a −1 eigenspace, so both signs occur with equal multiplicities in a nontrivial representation. A Hermitian operator is appropriate for the physical Hamiltonian and additionally guarantees an orthonormal eigenbasis.
