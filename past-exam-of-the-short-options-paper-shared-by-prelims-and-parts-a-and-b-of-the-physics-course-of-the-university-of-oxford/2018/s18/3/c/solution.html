Use p=−iℏ∇ and regard e as the signed charge in the printed minimal coupling. Multiplying the first Dirac equation by cγ0 gives H=cα⋅π+βmc2+eΦ, with π=p−eA/c. Removing the rest-energy phase yields
iℏ∂t​φ~​iℏ∂t​χ~​​=eΦφ~​+cσ⋅πχ~​,=cσ⋅πφ~​+(eΦ−2mc2)χ~​.​​
(133)
For the alternative equation use its specified field conventions literally. They give
Fμν​γμγν=c2​α⋅E+2iΣ⋅B,Σ=diag(σ,σ).
(134)
Consequently its Hamiltonian is cα⋅p+βmc2−κβΣ⋅B+(iκ/c)βα⋅E. The two shifted equations are
iℏ∂t​φ~​iℏ∂t​χ~​​=−κσ⋅Bφ~​+(cσ⋅p+ciκ​σ⋅E)χ~​,=(cσ⋅p−ciκ​σ⋅E)φ~​+(−2mc2+κσ⋅B)χ~​.​​
(135)
These electric-field factors depend on the source's convention Ei​=cF0i​; substituting a different Gaussian or SI definition without changing the equation would give incorrect powers of c.
