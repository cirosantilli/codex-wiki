With the apsidal origin chosen as specified, G=0. For pϕ​=0, rearrangement gives
pr​=(rpϕ​​−pϕ​mk​)tanϕ.
(79)
Let u=1/r. Since du/dϕ=−pr​/pϕ​, the equation is u′=−(u−mk/pϕ2​)tanϕ. Its solution, continued through points where the intermediate division fails, is
r1​=pϕ2​mk​+ecosϕ.​
(80)
As printed, e has units of inverse length and is not the usual dimensionless eccentricity. Writing ℓ=pϕ2​/(mk) gives the standard form r=ℓ/(1+εcosϕ) with ε=eℓ. The orbit is an ellipse for ∣ε∣<1, parabolic for unit magnitude and hyperbolic for magnitude greater than one on its allowed angular interval. The purely radial case pϕ​=0 must be handled separately and is excluded by these divisions.
