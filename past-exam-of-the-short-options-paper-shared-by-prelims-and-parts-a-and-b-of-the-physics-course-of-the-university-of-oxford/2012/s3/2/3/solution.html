rμv2​=4πϵ0​r2Ze2​,μvr=nℏ.
(35)

rn​=μZe24πϵ0​ℏ2​n2=a0​μme​​Zn2​.​
(36)
The total energy is E=μv2/2−Ze2/(4πϵ0​r)=−Ze2/(8πϵ0​r), hence
En​=−2(4πϵ0​)2ℏ2n2μZ2e4​=−hcRM​n2Z2​,RM​=R∞​me​μ​.​

Here a0​ is the ordinary Bohr radius and R∞​ the infinite-nuclear-mass Rydberg constant. The electron's radius about the centre of mass is Mrn​/(M+me​); for a heavy nucleus this distinction and the reduced-mass correction are small, but the supplied nuclear mass permits them to be stated precisely.
