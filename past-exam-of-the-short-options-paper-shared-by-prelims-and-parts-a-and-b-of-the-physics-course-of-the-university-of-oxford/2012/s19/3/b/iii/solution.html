 ... Past exam of the short options paper shared by Prelims and Parts A and B of the physics course of the University of Oxford 2012 S19 3 b iii
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
Use a calibrated, nonintercepting DC beam current transformer to measure the circulating current repeatedly during a fill, with top-up injection absent or separately recorded. At fixed revolution frequency, I=eNfrev​, so a fit to logI versus time gives slope −1/τ for exponential decay. The transformer's zero offset and long-term drift must be controlled, especially at low current. An AC current transformer or a calibrated bunch pickup can additionally resolve bunch charge, whereas a DC transformer provides the total circulating intensity needed here.
