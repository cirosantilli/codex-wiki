For a 5 GeV electron, γe​=E/(me​c2)≃9785. With ρ=10 m, the stated synchrotron critical frequency formula gives
ϵc,e​≃2.77×104eV=27.7keV.​
(174)
At the same total beam energy and bend radius, a proton has γp​=5/0.9383≃5.329, and the same supplied formula gives
ϵc,p​≃4.48×10−6eV.​
(175)
The ratio is (γp​/γe​)3≃(me​/mp​)3, explaining the strong suppression for protons. The critical-energy formula is an ultrarelativistic approximation, excellent for the electron and only approximate for this moderately relativistic proton. If “5 GeV proton beam” instead denotes kinetic energy, then γp​=1+5/0.9383 and the estimate is about 7.50×10−6 eV; the two beam-energy conventions should not be mixed.
