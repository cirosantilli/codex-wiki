Apart from the finite forward limit, amplitude zeros obey sinx−xcosx=0, or tanx=x, where x=qr0​. The first positive root is x1​≃4.49341. Since the accessible range is 0≤x≤2kr0​,
kc​=2r0​x1​​≃r0​2.247​.​
(154)
Thus there are no zeros for k<kc​; the first enters at the largest momentum transfer, θ=π, when k=kc​.
For larger roots, xn​ lies just below an​=π(n+21​), n=1,2,…. Expanding tan(an​−ε)≃1/ε gives xn​=an​−1/an​+O(an−3​). Keeping its leading term yields the requested diffraction estimate
θn​≃2arcsin[2kr0​π​(n+21​)],π(n+21​)≤2kr0​.​
(155)
Exactly, replace the numerator π(n+21​) by the corresponding root xn​. The displayed approximation is a large-qr0​ root approximation: taking k large at fixed low order does not make x1​ equal to 3π/2. For example, the exact first angle at high k is approximately 4.49341/(kr0​), not 4.71239/(kr0​).
