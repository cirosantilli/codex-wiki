For dependence on z alone and constant V0​, remove the common phase by ψ=e−iV0​tψ​. Then ∂t​ψ​=−σz​∂z​ψ​. The normalized eigenvectors
u+​=(10​),u−​=(01​)​
(158)
obey σz​u±​=±u±​. The corresponding first-order transport equations have characteristics t−z and t+z, respectively, giving
ψ(z,t)=e−iV0​t[η(t−z)u+​+ρ(t+z)u−​].​
(159)
Here η propagates toward +z and ρ toward −z, both at unit speed.
For a fixed unit vector n^, replace z by n^⋅x and choose orthonormal spinors satisfying (σ⋅n^)u±​(n^)=±u±​(n^). If n^=(sinϑcosφ,sinϑsinφ,cosϑ), one choice is
u+​(n^)=(cos(ϑ/2)eiφsin(ϑ/2)​),u−​(n^)=(−e−iφsin(ϑ/2)cos(ϑ/2)​).
(160)
Independent constant phases on the spinors can be absorbed into the two arbitrary envelope functions.
