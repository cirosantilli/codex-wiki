Massive young stars emit ionizing ultraviolet radiation that creates H II regions. Recombination and radiative cascades produce H-alpha emission, including the n=3→2 transition. Shocks or other ionizing sources can also produce it, but disk star-forming gas is the natural interpretation for an extended spiral-galaxy rotation measurement.
