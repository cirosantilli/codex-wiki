For translation in a fixed direction n^, take K=n^ and the preceding Fn​. The triple-product identity gives Fn​=−(q/2)n^⋅(B×x). Thus
Qn​=p⋅n^−Fn​=n^⋅(mx˙+qB×x).
(87)
Choosing the three Cartesian directions gives the three constants
Kmag​=mx˙+qB×x=constant vector.​
(88)
These are magnetic pseudomomentum components, not the three components of kinetic momentum mx˙. A direct check is dKmag​/dt=qx˙×B+qB×x˙=0. In the stated gauge, Kmag​=p+(q/2)B×x, consistent with the Noether boundary correction.
