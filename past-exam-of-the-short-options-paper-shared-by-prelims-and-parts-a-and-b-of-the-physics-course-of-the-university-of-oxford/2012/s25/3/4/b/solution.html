Holding emissions at E00​=8.069GtCyr−1 gives equilibrium concentration
C∞​=C0​+b0​b1​E00​​≃682.43ppmv.
(210)
For time t since 2000,
C(t)=C∞​+(C00​−C∞​)e−t/(200yr).​
(211)
It increases monotonically on the same 200-year timescale and reaches about 654.07ppmv in 2500. The limiting, and hence peak asymptotic, warming is
ΔTpeak​⟶3log2​(682.43/279)≃3.87K.​
(212)
In 2500 the equilibrium warming is about 3.69K, still below that asymptote. A stable thermal adjustment much faster than the carbon timescale follows this slow approach with a short lag and has the same limiting warming. These are predictions of the specified linear reservoir model, not a claim that constant real emissions produce a finite universal atmospheric equilibrium.
