Low excited states lie many thermal energies above the ground state at the inferred temperature, so their contributions to the atomic internal partition function are exponentially suppressed. For hydrogen, E2​−E1​=10.2 eV and the level degeneracy ratio is g2​/g1​=4. Hence
Nn=1​Nn=2​​=4e−10.2eV/(kB​T)≃6.8×10−15.​
(140)
The ground-state approximation is therefore excellent for this level under the assumed thermal-equilibrium conditions. A formal sum over infinitely many isolated-atom Rydberg states needs a physical high-level cutoff in a plasma; it should not be used to overturn the finite-level Boltzmann estimate. Radiation-driven nonequilibrium populations would require a different model.
