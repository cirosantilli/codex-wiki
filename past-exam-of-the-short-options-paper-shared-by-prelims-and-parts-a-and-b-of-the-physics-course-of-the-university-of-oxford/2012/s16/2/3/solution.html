For a nonrelativistic, three-dimensional ideal electron gas with spin degeneracy two,
EF​=2me​ℏ2​(3π2ne​)2/3,ωpe​=me​ϵ0​ne​e2​​.
(130)
Dividing gives
EF​ℏωpe​​=ℏ2ϵ0​4e2me​​​(3π2)−2/3ne−1/6​.​
(131)
Write the density-independent coefficient as K. From ℏωpe​/EF​=1.384, ne​=(K/1.384)6. In this free-electron model,
ne​≃1.59×1029m−3,EF​≃10.69eV,ℏωpe​≃14.79eV.​
(132)
The result uses the stipulated energy ratio, rather than substituting a separately tabulated aluminium density or plasmon energy.
