The first Born approximation substitutes the incident wave for the exact wave inside the scattering integral. Thus
RB​=k2ℏ4m2​​∫e2ikxU(x)dx​2.​
(47)
For the attractive delta this is β2/k2, the leading small-β/k expansion of the exact result. Physically one neglects the distortion of the incident state by repeated interaction with the potential. At low k the Born result can exceed one, while the exact reflection remains bounded and approaches one; it is not valid in that limit.
