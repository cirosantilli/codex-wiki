For ultrarelativistic particles with fixed magnet settings, A(δ)=A0​(1−δ) and B(δ)=B0​(1−δ). Hence the half-trace is
C(δ)=C0​+δ[d(A0​+B0​)−d2A0​B0​]+O(δ2),C0​=1−d(A0​+B0​)+21​d2A0​B0​.​
(113)
Off-energy motion is stable when ∣C(δ)∣<1, separately in each transverse plane. Where sinμ0​=0, its first-order phase shift is
Δμ=−sinμ0​d(A0​+B0​)−d2A0​B0​​δ.
(114)
For equal opposite strengths,
μ(δ)=μ0​−2tan(μ0​/2)δ+O(δ2).​
(115)
Thus positive momentum error reduces the focusing and phase advance; negative error moves toward the overfocused unstable boundary. The unexpanded symmetric stability condition is ∣f0​∣(1+δ)>d/2, with 1+δ>0, within the ultrarelativistic energy scaling. Its natural chromaticity per cell is d(μ/2π)/dδ=−tan(μ0​/2)/π. Away from the ultrarelativistic limit replace the displayed first-order energy error by δ/β02​. Linearizing at a stability boundary is singular and needs separate treatment.
