Below the critical photon energy, radiation is emitted over a wider angular cone. The usual low-frequency characteristic-angle estimate is
σθ​(ε)∼γ1​(εεc​​)1/3,
(124)
up to an order-one angular-width convention. At ε=0.012eV this gives
σθ​∼1.49×10−2rad,σy​∼0.149m.​
(125)
The aperture should therefore be enlarged vertically. A full width of order 0.30m covers ±1 characteristic width; approximately 0.90m covers ±3 and gives containment comparable with the preceding X-ray estimate in the same Gaussian model. The original 10mm width would pass only roughly erf[0.005/(2​0.149)]≃2.7% in that model. Equivalent optics could instead reduce the spot at a relocated aperture, at the expense of angular acceptance. These estimates concern geometric acceptance; THz coherence and bunch structure require further beam data.
