On the incident side, ϕ is the superposition of incoming and reflected waves, producing a standing-wave pattern whose travelling-wave de Broglie wavelength is λ=2π/k. On the forbidden side, it decays exponentially and remains nonzero near the boundary. A sketch should therefore show oscillations for x<0, a continuous function and slope at zero, and the evanescent tail for x>0 beside the potential step.
The quantum distinction is finite probability density inside the classically forbidden region, despite complete reflection. There is no transmitted flux at arbitrarily large positive x for this semi-infinite step. A finite-width barrier would allow quantum tunnelling, a different boundary-value problem.
