The supplied confinement criterion alone fixes a product, not a unique current or machine size. Choose a representative ion density ni​=1020m−3. Then
τ≳ni​1020​s≃1s.​
(100)
For ni​=1019 to 1021m−3, the same criterion gives τ≳10 to 0.1s. These are illustrative density choices, not additional source data.
For a current estimate, assume a large-aspect-ratio circular tokamak with major radius R0​=3m, minor radius a=1m, toroidal field Bϕ​=5T and edge tokamak safety factor qs​≃3. The geometrical winding relation and Ampère's law give
qs​≃R0​Bθ​aBϕ​​,Bθ​(a)≃2πaμ0​Ip​​,Ip​≃μ0​R0​qs​2πa2Bϕ​​.
(101)
For the stated assumptions,
Ip​≃2.8×106A,​
(102)
so toroidal plasma currents of order megaamperes are a natural scale. As a consistency check at T∼108K, quasineutral singly charged heavy hydrogen has total thermal pressure p≃2ni​kB​T≃2.8×105Pa. With 5T, 2μ0​p/B2≃0.028, so the assumed magnetic pressure exceeds the thermal pressure. The particular current depends on geometry and field-line pitch; it cannot be inferred from the confinement product alone.
