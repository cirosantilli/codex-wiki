Seek a constant transverse guiding-centre drift vd​ whose magnetic Lorentz force balances the applied force. Define
vd​=qB2F×B​.
(98)
Since F⋅B=0, the vector triple-product identity gives qvd​×B=−F. Writing v=vd​+u therefore reduces the full equation to mu˙=qu×B, the ordinary gyromotion equation. Its transverse orbit is circular in the drifting frame, so
vgc​=vd​=q1​B2F×B​.​
(99)
Averaging over a gyroperiod removes the transverse oscillatory velocity and leaves this drift, together with any constant parallel velocity. For an electric force F=qE, this becomes the charge-independent E×B/B2 drift; a charge-independent force instead gives opposite drift directions for opposite charge signs.
