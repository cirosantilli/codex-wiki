Use collisional inverse bremsstrahlung in a weakly coupled, unmagnetized plasma. The specified density is ne​=1026m−3, and kB​T=1000eV. The electron Debye length is 2.35×10−8m, the plasma frequency is 5.64×1014s−1, and the laser angular frequency is ω=2πc/λ=1.79×1015s−1.
