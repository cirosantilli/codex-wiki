Define the plasma parameter here as the number of electrons in an electron Debye sphere,
ND​=34π​ne​λD3​.
(90)
Some texts instead use ne​λD3​, or its inverse as a coupling parameter. A weakly coupled collective plasma has ND​≫1; fluctuations from individual nearby particles are small compared with the collective description.
A Coulomb collision means the cumulative deflection or relaxation produced by many small-angle encounters, not necessarily a hard contact between two particles. The Coulomb logarithm lnΛC​≃ln(bmax​/bmin​) summarizes the impact-parameter range, with bmax​ of order λD​. A process is collisionless when its timescale is much shorter than the appropriate collisional relaxation time, or its length scale is much smaller than a corresponding mean free path. It still contains collective interactions.
For fully ionized ions of charge Ze, a thermal electron–ion momentum-relaxation rate has the scaling
νei​∼ϵ02​me1/2​(kB​T)3/2Zne​e4lnΛC​​,tcol​∼ZlnΛC​ωp​ND​​​,
(91)
up to numerical factors depending on the Debye-sphere and collision-time conventions. More explicitly, the Maxwellian rate used below gives νei​/ωp​≃0.089ZlnΛC​/ND​, or tcol​ωp​≃11.3ND​/(ZlnΛC​). Specifying this factor prevents mixing a small coupling parameter with a large Debye-sphere population.[ref]
