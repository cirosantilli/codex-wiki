Kepler's third law is P2=4π2a3/[G(MS​+MP​)], with a the semimajor axis of the relative orbit. For a circular edge-on orbit, the star's speed is VS​=2πaS​/P, and the centre of mass relation gives aS​=aMP​/(MS​+MP​). Cubing the speed expression and eliminating a yields
(MS​+MP​)2MP3​​=2πGPVS3​​.
(15)
For MP​≪MS​, this reduces to
MP3​≃2πGVS3​PMS2​​.​
(16)
Without the edge-on assumption, a factor sin3i multiplies the numerator, explaining the minimum-mass degeneracy of the radial-velocity method.
