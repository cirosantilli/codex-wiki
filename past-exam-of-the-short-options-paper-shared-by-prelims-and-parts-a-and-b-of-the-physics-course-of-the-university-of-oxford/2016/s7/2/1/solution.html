A constant of motion has zero total derivative on every solution, and may explicitly depend on time. For an infinitesimal transformation δqi​=ϵξi​ with δL=ϵdF/dt, the on-shell first variation is δL=ϵd(∑i​pi​ξi​)/dt. Therefore I=i∑​pi​ξi​−F​ is conserved. Including a time shift δt=ϵτ gives the corresponding I=∑i​pi​ξi​−Hτ−F. This proves Noether's theorem in its usual point-transformation form. A symmetry of the action up to a boundary term is sufficient; exact invariance of the displayed Lagrangian is stronger than necessary. Discrete transformations by themselves do not give this continuous-charge construction.
