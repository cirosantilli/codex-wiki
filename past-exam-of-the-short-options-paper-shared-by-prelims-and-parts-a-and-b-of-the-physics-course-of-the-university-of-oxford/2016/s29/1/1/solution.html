Radiative equilibrium balances absorbed solar power against emitted thermal power, with no net energy accumulation. A spherical body's projected area absorbs πr2FS​(1−A)/RAU2​; its full area emits 4πr2ϵσT4. Hence
Teq​=[4ϵσRAU2​FS​(1−A)​]1/4.​
(44)
Here A is bolometric Bond albedo, not a geometric albedo measured at one phase or wavelength. The radius cancels because the absorption and emission areas scale together.
