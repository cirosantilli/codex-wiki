At D, MV​=20.3−15.09=5.21. Ignoring differential bolometric corrections, L/L⊙​≃10−0.4(5.21−4.74)≃0.65, and L∝M4 gives MD​≃0.90M⊙​​. If accessible fuel fraction is similar, lifetime scales as M/L∝M−3. Normalizing to a solar main-sequence lifetime about 10 Gyr gives an age near 14 Gyr​.
This is much older than the Sun's current approximately 4.6 Gyr and close to the cosmological age scale. The approximate estimate is not evidence for a cluster older than the Universe: bolometric corrections, abundance, accessible hydrogen fraction and the crude mass-luminosity law limit precision. The stated 26.7 MeV conversion and an assumed core fuel fraction can supply the order-of-magnitude solar lifetime; they do not by themselves specify the burned fraction or composition precisely.
