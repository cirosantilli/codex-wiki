Internal-wave ray tracing

 Home Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Internal gravity wave Reflection of an internal-wave ray
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
Internal-wave ray tracing follows the group velocity while preserving the wave frequency. In a uniformly stratified fluid, each straight ray keeps the angle sin−1(ω/N) to the horizontal until reflection or a change in the medium.
