Distribution annihilated by a function with simple zeros

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Multiplication of a distribution by a smooth function
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 1
If a smooth function a on R has finitely many zeros rj​, all simple, then aw=0 exactly when w=∑j​cj​δrj​​. Away from the zeros, divide a test function by the nonvanishing a to show w vanishes. Near rj​, write a(x)=(x−rj​)bj​(x) with smooth nonzero bj​. The kernel of multiplication by a coordinate, after translation and multiplication by bj​, says that the local distribution is a multiple of δrj​​. A partition of unity gives the global sum. Simplicity of the zeros excludes derivatives of deltas.
