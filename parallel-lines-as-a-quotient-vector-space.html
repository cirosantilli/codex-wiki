Parallel lines as a quotient vector space (R3/span(u))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Quotient vector space
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For fixed u=0, the parallel affine lines lx​=x+span(u) are precisely the cosets of the indicated quotient vector space. Changing representatives adds a multiple of u, so lx​+ly​=lx+y​ and alx​=lax​ are well-defined. The zero is the whole line l0​, not a single point. If (u,b1​,b2​) is a basis, the two line classes lb1​​,lb2​​ form a basis of the quotient by spanning and independence modulo span(u).
