Guiding center

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Charged particle in a uniform magnetic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 166 Articles: 2
The guiding center is the center of a charged particle's circular cyclotron orbit. In a nonuniform field it drifts slowly compared with the rapid cyclotron frequency.
