Local principal connection form (A=s∗A,A′=g−1Ag+g−1dg)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Principal bundle Connection (principal bundle)
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 1
Pulling a principal connection back along a local section s gives a Lie-algebra-valued one-form on the base. If the section changes to sg, the form has the displayed inhomogeneous transformation. Its gauge curvature F=dA+A∧A transforms homogeneously as F′=g−1Fg. The collection of such local forms encodes a global principal connection even when no single section exists globally.
