Clearing denominators relative to an independent module subset (0=r∈R,rM⊆⟨T⟩)

Let a finitely generated module over an integral domain have a finite generating set S and a maximal independent subset T. For each s∈/T, dependence of T∪{s} gives a nonzero coefficient as​ with as​s∈⟨T⟩. The product of those coefficients is nonzero and carries the whole module into ⟨T⟩.
