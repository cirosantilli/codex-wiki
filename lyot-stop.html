Lyot stop

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Coronagraph Lyot coronagraph
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
A Lyot stop is a mask in the reimaged optical pupil after a coronagraphic focal plane mask. For an unobstructed circular entrance pupil, it usually transmits a smaller concentric disk. In a classical Lyot coronagraph, much of the residual stellar field lies near the optical pupil boundary; the Lyot stop rejects that field before the final image is formed. It also rejects useful off-axis light, so choosing its opening involves a coronagraph throughput tradeoff. [ref].
