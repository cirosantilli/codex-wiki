Decay width (Γ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field
OurBigBook.com Words: 465 Articles: 8
In units with ℏ=1, the inverse lifetime of an unstable particle. A neutral meson mixing effective Hamiltonian operator incorporates decay through its anti-Hermitian part −iΓ/2.
