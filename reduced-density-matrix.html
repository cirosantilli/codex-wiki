Reduced density matrix (ρA​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bell state
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 20
For a bipartite density operator ρAB​, the reduced state of subsystem A is the partial trace ρA​=TrB​ρAB​. Every measurement performed only on A has outcome probabilities determined entirely by ρA​.
