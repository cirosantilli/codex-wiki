Tail sigma-algebra (T)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Independent sigma-algebras
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 3
For a sequence of random variables (Xn​), the tail sigma-algebra is
T=⋂n≥1​σ(Xn​,Xn+1​,…).
(9)
Its events are unaffected by changing any finite initial segment.
