Spatial projection tensor (⊥μν​=δμν​+nμnν​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Numerical relativity 3+1 decomposition of spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 225 Articles: 5
For a unit timelike normal nμnμ​=−1, the spatial projection tensor annihilates nμ and projects every spacetime tensor onto a spatial hypersurface.
