Magnetic coenergy (WB′​=∑i​Ii​λi​−WB​)

For reversible quasistatic magnetic circuits with flux linkages λi​, currents Ii​ and mechanical coordinate q, write dWB​=∑i​Ii​dλi​−Fq​dq. The Legendre transform WB′​=∑i​Ii​λi​−WB​ obeys dWB′​=∑i​λi​dIi​+Fq​dq. Thus Fq​=(∂WB′​/∂q)Ii​​, while Fq​=−(∂WB​/∂q)λi​​. In a linear reciprocal system WB′​=WB​=21​ITL(q)I numerically, but the variables held fixed in their force derivatives differ. The coenergy formulation includes the effect of electrical sources maintaining the currents; it avoids reversing the sign of the magnetic force by differentiating stored energy under the wrong constraint.
