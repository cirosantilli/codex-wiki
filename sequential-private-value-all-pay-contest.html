Sequential private-value all-pay contest

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Contest theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 307 Articles: 4
Two players pay their invested efforts regardless of outcome. A leader invests first, and a follower observes that effort before deciding. With ties favoring the follower and unit costs, a follower of value u matches leader effort b when u>b, and otherwise declines. For a continuous follower cumulative distribution function F, the leader wins with probability F(b) and has payoff vF(b)−b.
