Rotating-frame reduction of circular viscoelastic shear (Leff​=(0v′−v/r​00​))

 ... Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity Conformation tensor Objective time derivative Upper-convected derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For azimuthal velocity v(r)eθ​, the velocity gradient in the rotating polar basis is (0v′​−v/r0​). The basis rotates along a particle at angular speed v/r. Accounting for that rotation in the material derivative replaces L by L−(v/r)(01​−10​) in the upper-convected derivative. Its remaining shear rate is v′−v/r, so the local viscoelastic normal-stress difference follows the same constitutive algebra as simple shear flow.
