Transition probability (Pij​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Stochastic matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
The transition probability Pij​ is the conditional probability that a Markov chain moves to state j on its next step given that its current state is i.
