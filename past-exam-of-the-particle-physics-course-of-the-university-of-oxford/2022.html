An incoming fermion contributes a Dirac spinor us​(p) and an outgoing fermion contributes its Dirac adjoint uˉs​(p)=us†​(p)γ0. The label s specifies a spin state; p is the on-shell four-momentum. Incoming and outgoing antifermions instead contribute vˉs​(p) and vs​(p).
The exchanged boson is a virtual photon, represented in the Feynman rules by the photon propagator −igμν​/(q2+iϵ) in Feynman gauge. Its momentum transfer is q=p1​−p3​=p4​−p2​; unlike an external real photon, it need not satisfy q2=0. Each Feynman vertex contracts a gamma matrix with this propagator and contributes −ieγμ in a conventional QED sign convention. The coupling constant e sets the interaction strength, with fine-structure constant α=e2/(4π). One may reverse the sign of the photon field and both vertex signs without changing the result. The gamma matrices obey {γμ,γν}=2gμνI, with Minkowski metric g=diag(1,−1,−1,−1).

There is one tree-level Feynman diagram: the distinguishable electron and muon exchange a photon in the t channel. There is no identical-particle exchange diagram.
Multiplying the external Dirac spinors, the two Feynman vertices and the photon propagator gives
iM=[uˉ3​(−ieγμ)u1​]q2+iϵ−igμν​​[uˉ4​(−ieγν)u2​],M=q2e2​[uˉ3​γμu1​][uˉ4​γμ​u2​].​
(1)
The overall phase of this scattering amplitude is convention dependent. Four external spin labels with two choices each give 24=16 possible configurations before taking a limit. For E≫m,M, the vector QED interaction preserves fermion chirality, which becomes helicity for each external particle. Thus the surviving configurations, written as (he​,hμ​)in​→(he​,hμ​)out​, are
LL→LL,LR→LR,RL→RL,RR→RR.​
(2)
Here L,R specify helicity along each particle's own momentum. The other configurations require at least one mass-suppressed flip and vanish in the strictly massless limit.

The Δ++ Delta baryon has electric charge +2 and valence quark content uuu. In its ground state the spatial wavefunction has orbital angular momentum L=0 and is symmetric. Its spin is 3/2; the maximal projection state u↑​u↑​u↑​ is symmetric in spin, and its quark flavour wavefunction is symmetric as well. Three identical fermions require an antisymmetric total wavefunction by the Pauli exclusion principle. A further internal degree of freedom must therefore supply the antisymmetry.
With three color charges, that factor is the normalized colour singlet
∣C⟩=6​∣rgb⟩+∣gbr⟩+∣brg⟩−∣rbg⟩−∣grb⟩−∣bgr⟩​.​
(17)
It changes sign under exchange of any two quarks. The remaining symmetric factors then give the required antisymmetric total state. This argument motivates color charge independently of the already present quark flavour and spin labels.

For quarks in an Nc​-dimensional internal space V, complete antisymmetrization of three labels corresponds to a Young diagram with one column of three boxes, the partition (1,1,1). Its representation is the exterior power ⋀3V and has dimension
dim⋀3V=(3Nc​​).
(18)
For Nc​=2, the column is too tall: ⋀3C2=0. With only two labels at least two of the three coincide, and the Young tableau column antisymmetrizer annihilates the state. For Nc​=3, the filled column r,g,b spans a one-dimensional determinant representation. It is a colour singlet of SU(3) because every group element has determinant one.
Equivalently, the tensor product of group representations decomposes as
3⊗3⊗3=10[3]​⊕8[2,1]​⊕8[2,1]​⊕1[1,1,1]​.​
(19)
The two mixed-symmetry terms account for 16 dimensions, so the total is 10+8+8+1=27. Antisymmetry alone establishes at least three internal states. Exactly three follows in the usual special unitary group model when the observed three-quark baryon must be a colour singlet: for Nc​>3 the antisymmetric third exterior power has dimension greater than one and is not a singlet. This extra assumption should be made explicit.

Away from narrow resonances and quark thresholds, the QED annihilation cross section into each accessible quark flavour is its squared fractional electric charge times the muon-pair cross section, multiplied by the number Nc​ of distinguishable color charges. Thus the inclusive hadronic ratio is
Rhad​=σ(e+e−→μ+μ−)σ(e+e−→hadrons)​≃Nc​∑f​Qf2​.
(20)
With Nc​=3, the continuum predictions are Ruds​=2, Rudsc​=10/3 and Rudscb​=11/3 as successive charm quark and bottom quark channels become accessible. The measured normalization and steps support three color charges. At leading order these hadronic events form two back-to-back jets from the quark and antiquark; quantitative comparisons include QCD corrections and avoid the nonperturbative threshold region.
The extra resolved jet is explained by e+e−→γ∗→qqˉ​g, with gluon radiation from either the quark or antiquark. One possible color charge routing is initial qr​qˉ​rˉ​ followed by qr​→qb​+grbˉ​; the final qb​, qˉ​rˉ​ and grbˉ​ combine to a net colour singlet.
The three-jet cross section contains one additional strong interaction vertex in the amplitude, so its ratio to the two-jet rate is of order αs​=gs2​/(4π) times a resolution-dependent kinematic coefficient. A resolved fraction of order 15% is consistent with perturbative QCD and a strong coupling constant much larger than the fine-structure constant. It is not an equality αs​=0.15: the jet definition and cuts are needed. Photon radiation has a much smaller electromagnetic rate. The large hadronic third-jet rate supports gluon radiation; identifying the radiated object specifically as a colour-octet gluon also uses the QCD vertex and colour-flow description, not merely the existence of three visible jets.

Represent the color charges by r=(1,0,0)T, g=(0,1,0)T and b=(0,0,1)T. The Gell-Mann matrices satisfy
λ4r=b,λ5r=ib,2λ4−iλ5​=∣b⟩⟨r∣.
(21)
The physical quark-gluon Feynman vertex contains gs​Ta with Ta=λa/2; the off-diagonal combination therefore allows r→b. Color charge conservation assigns the emitted gluon the colour-flow label rbˉ.
The claimed impossibility of r→r is false. A direct counterexample from the printed matrices is
λ3r=r,λ8r=3​r​.​
(22)
Thus both diagonal gluons have nonzero r→r vertex elements, (T3)rr​=1/2 and (T8)rr​=1/(23​). They correspond to the traceless combinations
2​rrˉ−ggˉ​​,6​rrˉ+ggˉ​−2bbˉ​.
(23)
A Gell-Mann matrix is a generator rather than a finite rotation, but exponentiating a diagonal generator also leaves the red basis direction fixed up to a phase. The actual excluded object is the identity generator, proportional to the colour singlet (rrˉ+ggˉ​+bbˉ)/3​. Adding it would extend SU(3) to unitary group and introduce a ninth singlet vector boson. A colour-preserving vertex among the existing eight gluons does not require that extra boson and does not make the ordinary diagonal gluons colour singlets.

In gluon-fusion Higgs production, two gluons couple to a closed top quark loop, and the Higgs boson couples to that massive quark through its Yukawa interaction. The right panel of the diagram in c shows one explicit colour-flow component: internal tr​→tb​ at one gluon insertion, then tb​→tr​ at the other; the colour-neutral Higgs boson insertion preserves the internal color charge. Include both loop orderings. The color charge factor is
tr(TaTb)=21​δab,
(24)
so the two incoming gluons are projected onto a colour singlet. The Yukawa coupling is yt​=2​mt​/v, and the htˉt vertex has strength mt​/v. No direct tree-level hgg mass coupling is needed. Integrating out a sufficiently heavy top quark generates an effective interaction proportional to (h/v)Gμνa​Gaμν, which is allowed by gauge invariance even though the gluon is massless.
There is also no kinematic obstacle: two massless particles with nonparallel momenta can have nonzero total invariant mass. In particular, head-on gluons have s^=4E1​E2​, providing the energy needed for the Higgs boson at the LHC.

The Lorentz force is dp/dt=qv×B. Because it is perpendicular to the velocity, it changes the direction of momentum without changing its magnitude. For a charged particle in a uniform magnetic field, the radius of curvature is R=p/(∣q∣B). Over the short track, the bending angle is ϕ≃L/R. Equivalently, integrate the force for a time L/v while neglecting the small rotation in its direction. The magnitude of the acquired momentum component perpendicular to the incident direction is
∣Δp⊥​∣=psinϕ≃pϕ=∣q∣BL.​
(25)
Its direction follows qv×B; a signed component can therefore be written qBL after fixing an oriented bending axis. Chord length and arc length agree at this order in L/R.

In vacuum the electron-flavour survival probability depends only on ∣Uei​∣2 and the mass-squared differences. Complex conjugation of the lepton mixing matrix therefore leaves it unchanged:
P(νe​→νe​)=P(νˉe​→νˉe​),∂δ∂Pee​​=0.​
