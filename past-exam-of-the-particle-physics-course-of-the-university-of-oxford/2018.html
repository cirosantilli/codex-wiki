The flavour states K0=dsˉ and Kˉ0=sdˉ have definite Strangeness and are distinguished by flavour-sensitive production or decay. The propagation states diagonalize the effective Hamiltonian Heff​=M−iΓ/2; their complex eigenvalues give a pole mass and a decay width. The common flavour combinations specified in the source assume the usual CPT-symmetric two-state description and are normalized with ∣p∣2+∣q∣2=1.
Choose the phase convention CP∣K0⟩=∣Kˉ0⟩. If mixing conserves CP, p=q can be chosen, and the symmetric and antisymmetric combinations are CP even and odd. More generally the equality must be understood with the corresponding flavour-state phase convention; ∣p∣=∣q∣ alone is not a statement fixing an arbitrary written phase.
The almost CP-even state decays rapidly to two pions, whereas the almost CP-odd state is largely restricted to slower channels such as three pions. Their very different lifetimes are easier to distinguish than their tiny mass splitting, motivating the names KS0​ and KL0​. In the source's first notation the subscript L means light, not long-lived: the light state is identified with the short-lived kaon, and the heavy state with the long-lived kaon. Below, ℓ,h label light/heavy to avoid that collision.

The two Weyl spinors transform in the inequivalent left- and right-chiral representations of the proper Lorentz group. They are selected by PL,R​=(1∓γ5)/2 and have different boost transformation laws; parity exchanges the two. A massive Dirac equation couples them, whereas in the massless limit they can propagate independently.

a OurBigBook.com
A fast charged particle loses energy by ionizing and exciting silicon, creating electron–hole pairs along its path. In a reverse-biased microstrip diode, the electric field separates and drifts the electrons and holes toward opposite electrodes. Their motion induces a current on the strips, which is integrated by the readout electronics to locate the hit.
The bias depletes the bulk of mobile equilibrium carriers, reducing conduction and capacitance, and supplies rapid directed charge collection before recombination or trapping. Without a collecting field, diffusion is slow and charge spreads or recombines, degrading signal and position measurement. The signal is generated by the traversing particle's energy deposition, not by the bias creating the ionization charge.
