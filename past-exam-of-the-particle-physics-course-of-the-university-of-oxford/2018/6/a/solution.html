For negligible fermion masses, the neutral-current vertex connects a fermion and antifermion of opposite helicity. The two incoming choices are eL−​eR+​ and eR−​eL+​; the two outgoing choices are fL​fˉ​R​ and fR​fˉ​L​. Their combinations give four channels, denoted LL,LR,RL,RR, where each letter refers to the fermion rather than antifermion.
Take the incoming electron momentum along +z and Positron momentum along −z. The initial L pair has both spins along −z, giving total projection −1; the R pair has both along +z, giving projection +1. If the outgoing fermion moves along n^, an outgoing L pair has both spins along −n^ and an R pair both along +n^. The figure shows momentum and spin arrows separately for all four channels.
