The charged-current process is
e−+μ+⟶νe​+νˉμ​,​
(41)
with a t-channel W− emitted at the electron-to-neutrino vertex and absorbed at the antimuon-to-antineutrino vertex. It is not an s-channel Annihilation of the unlike-flavour charged leptons into one W.
At high momentum transfer the incident electron's left-chiral fraction tends to 4/5. In the collision centre-of-mass frame, the incoming antimuon moves along −z and its charged-current allowed right helicity has spin along −z. A rest-frame spin at Angle θ to +z projects onto that spin direction with probability sin2(θ/2). Therefore, if σ0​ denotes the cross section for a pure left-helicity electron and fully selected antimuon spin,
σ(θ)≃54​σ0​sin22θ​=52​σ0​(1−cosθ).​
(42)
It vanishes at θ=0 in the ultrarelativistic selection limit and is maximal at θ=π. Finite-mass corrections can fill the suppressed orientation. The absolute normalization depends on beam energy and the exchanged-boson propagator; the spin-angle dependence is what is fixed here.
