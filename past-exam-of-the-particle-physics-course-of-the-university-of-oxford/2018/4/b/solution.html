The two Weyl spinors transform in the inequivalent left- and right-chiral representations of the proper Lorentz group. They are selected by PL,R​=(1∓γ5)/2 and have different boost transformation laws; parity exchanges the two. A massive Dirac equation couples them, whereas in the massless limit they can propagate independently.
For the particular u printed in this paper, transforming its supplied Dirac γ5 gives
γW5​=u(0I​I0​)u−1=(I0​0−I​).​
(37)
Thus the upper block is right chiral and the lower block left chiral in this convention. Many references use the opposite block order; neither convention changes physics, but they must not be mixed. Chirality is not generally the same as helicity for a massive electron. The weak charged current selects the left-chiral electron component, reducing to negative helicity at high energy.
