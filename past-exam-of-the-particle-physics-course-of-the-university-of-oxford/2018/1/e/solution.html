Assume only Standard Model decay channels, negligible final-state masses, three lepton families, three quark colours and a unitary CKM matrix. The top-quark channels are kinematically closed. Summing the allowed quark channels gives 3∑i=u,c​∑j=d,s,b​∣Vij​∣2≃6 times one leptonic width, while the leptons contribute three. Neglecting QCD and electroweak radiative corrections,
ΓW​≃9Γℓ​=48π9g2mW​​.
(8)
The source's rounded CKM entries give almost the same factor; their tiny nonunitarity is not physically significant. Consequently
g2=9mW​48πΓW​​=0.4356±0.0083,g=0.6600±0.0063.​
(9)
The propagated uncertainty uses δg/g=21​δΓW​/ΓW​, taking mW​ as fixed. It is the measurement uncertainty within the stated tree-level approximation; omitted radiative corrections are an additional modelling limitation. In particular QCD increases the hadronic width and lowers the inferred coupling when included.
The printed electromagnetic comparison 4π/137 is e2, not the probability amplitude coupling e. Compare like quantities:
e2g2​≃4.75,e=4π/137​≃0.303,g/e≃2.18.​
(10)
Equivalently αW​=g2/(4π)≃0.0347 exceeds αEM​≃1/137. The weak interaction is weak at low energy primarily because of the massive propagator, rather than an exceptionally small fundamental coupling.
