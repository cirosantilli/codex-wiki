The three structures are the radiative cascades
ψ(2S)→γl​χcJ​,χcJ​→γh​J/ψ,J=0,1,2.
(6)
In the quark model, the J/psi meson and ψ(2S) are respectively 13S1​ and 23S1​ charmonium states. Thus L=0, S=1, J=1, and meson parity and charge conjugation gives JPC=1−−. The intermediate chi c mesons are 13PJ​: L=1, S=1, and addition of angular momentum permits J=0,1,2. Consequently
χc0​:0++,χc1​:1++,χc2​:2++.​
(7)
These are electric dipole transitions, changing L by one and preserving S to leading order. A singlet 11P1​ state has JPC=1+− and is not another member of these spin-triplet cascades.
The primary photon energy is (M2S2​−MχJ​2​)/(2M2S​), about 261, 171 and 128 MeV for J=0,1,2. The secondary photon remains more energetic even after its boost into the parent rest frame. Hence m2(J/ψγh​)=MχJ​2​: with this quantity on the vertical axis, each resonance gives a horizontal band. A finite decay width and detector measurement resolution broaden each band; photon ordering removes the mirrored duplication of an unordered Dalitz plot.
The faint band beside the negative-slope diagonal boundary is ψ(2S)→J/ψπ0, followed by π0→γγ. The neutral pion is predominantly (uuˉ−ddˉ)/2​, a 1S0​ pseudoscalar meson with JPC=0−+. This production transition violates isospin, accounting for its small rate. To locate the band, set X=m2(J/ψγl​) and Y=m2(J/ψγh​). Four-momentum conservation gives
X+Y=M2S2​+MJ/ψ2​−mγγ2​.​
(8)
For the neutral pion, mγγ2​=mπ02​ is small, putting the line just inside the mγγ2​=0 boundary. The allowed J/ψη channel, with the eta meson also 0−+, produces a parallel line further inside, at mγγ2​=mη2​. The positive-slope edge X=Y instead comes from ordering the two photon energies; proximity to that edge alone would not identify the two-photon state.
