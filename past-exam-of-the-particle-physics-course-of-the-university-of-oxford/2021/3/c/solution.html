For solar neutrinos propagating in the same vacuum model, the vastly longer baseline makes the oscillations extremely fine in energy, with the same bounds 1/2 and 1. The figure uses a deliberately schematic enlargement of the oscillation spacing to show those bounds. A 1 MeV detector energy resolution averages very many periods, so ⟨cos2φ⟩=1/2 and
⟨Pee​⟩vacuum​=1−21​sin22θ=43​.​

The detector averages event rates weighted by source spectrum, interaction cross section and response; the displayed constant follows when these vary slowly across each tiny oscillation period. Reactor production physically gives electron antineutrinos. In this two-flavor vacuum approximation their survival probability is identical; the positive matter-potential discussion in d concerns solar neutrinos.
