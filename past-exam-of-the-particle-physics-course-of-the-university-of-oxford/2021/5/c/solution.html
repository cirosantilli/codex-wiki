Use a clean Z boson sample with final-state radiation, Z→e+e−γ, and independently calibrated electron four-momenta. For measured P=pe+​+pe−​ and measured photon direction n^γ​, write pγ​=Eγ​(1,n^γ​). The known Z boson mass supplies
MZ2​=P2+2Eγ​(P0−P⋅n^γ​),Eγpred​=2(P0−P⋅n^γ​)MZ2​−mee2​​.​
(36)
Comparing the reconstructed photon energy with this prediction constrains its scale versus energy, detector position and conversion status. In practice fit the e+e−γ invariant mass distribution, allowing for the physical Z boson decay width, detector response and backgrounds, instead of assigning every event exactly MZ​. Select final-state-radiation topologies: an initial-state-radiation photon is not part of the Z boson decay and generally does not satisfy the same mass constraint. The calibration applies over the populated photon energy range; extrapolation to still higher energies needs a validated response model.
