The right-sign denominator has its own small mixing contribution. In the same CP symmetry convention its decay amplitude is ARS​=g+​+re−iδg−​. After cancelling e−τ, the exact numerator and denominator are
ND​=21​r2(coshyτ+cosxτ)+r(cosδsinhyτ−sinδsinxτ)+21​(coshyτ−cosxτ),=21​(coshyτ+cosxτ)+r(cosδsinhyτ+sinδsinxτ)+21​r2(coshyτ−cosxτ),R=N/D.​
(24)
Define u=ycosδ and v=xsinδ. Their Taylor expansions are N=r2+r(u−v)τ+{x2+y2+r2(y2−x2)}τ2/4+O(τ3) and D=1+r(u+v)τ+{y2−x2+r2(x2+y2)}τ2/4+O(τ3). Dividing the series gives the strict second-order answer
RC1​C2​​=r2+C1​τ+C2​τ2+O(τ3),=r[(1−r2)u−(1+r2)v],=4(1−r4)(x2+y2)​−r2(u2−v2)+r4(u+v)2.​​
(25)
The usually quoted approximation additionally drops terms suppressed by powers of r in the right-sign denominator:
R≃r2+r(ycosδ−xsinδ)τ+4x2+y2​τ2.
(26)
For r=0.1 and x=y=0.01, this is 0.01+0.001τ+0.00005τ2 for δ=0, and 0.01−0.001τ+0.00005τ2 for δ=π/2. The first increases; the second initially decreases, reaches approximately 0.005 at τ=10, then rises. The strict series instead has (C1​,C2​)=(0.00099,0.000049005) and (−0.00101,0.000051005). At τ=20 the exact ratios are approximately 0.04913 and 0.009934; the leading approximations give 0.05 and 0.01.
With no neutral meson mixing, x=y=0 and g−​=0, so R=r2=0.01​ at all decay times. The wrong-sign signal then remains through direct doubly Cabibbo suppressed decay.
