The local Fermi interaction gives a three-body leptonic decay width of the form
Γ(l→aνˉa​νl​)=192π3GF2​ml5​​f(z),z=ml2​ma2​​,f(z)=1−8z+8z3−z4−12z2logz,
(27)
at tree level with negligible neutrino masses and universal weak coupling physics values. The fifth power follows from the dimension of GF​ and the three-body phase space; f results from integrating the V-A interaction matrix element over the reduced phase space when the charged daughter has nonzero mass. It is more than a purely geometric volume correction because the spin-dependent matrix element is included.
As z→0, f(z)→1; increasing daughter mass reduces the available energy and suppresses the rate. The largest ratio is mμ​/mτ​, so the τ→μ correction is visibly below one. The electron is extremely light relative to either parent. Using the lepton masses gives
fτμ​≃0.9725601,fμe​≃0.99981295,fτe​≃0.99999934​.
(28)
These reproduce the supplied factors to their stated accuracy. Radiative and finite-W boson corrections are separate small corrections when a precision lepton universality test is performed.
