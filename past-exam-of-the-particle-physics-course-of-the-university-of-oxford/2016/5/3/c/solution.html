Assuming that the two listed leptonic decay categories exhaust the leptonic modes, the inclusive hadronic branching fraction is 100−17.83−17.41=64.76%. Thus
Rτ​=Γ(τ→eνˉe​ντ​)Γ(τ→hadronsν)​=17.8364.76​=3.632.
(34)
At the simplest massless quark level, the W boson couples to udˉ and usˉ in three color charge states, so equal quark and lepton weak coupling physics values predict
Rτ(0)​=Nc​(∣Vud​∣2+∣Vus​∣2)≃3.0024≃3​.
(35)
This is a rough check of color multiplicity in a decay width and universal weak-current normalization. It is much less precise than the two-lepton ratio because hadronic Quantum chromodynamics and electroweak interaction corrections are sizeable: the data require a net correction factor near 3.632/3.0024≃1.21. They must be included before extracting a quark-to-lepton coupling physics ratio. It would be incorrect to interpret the excess over three directly as a failure of weak universality. The displayed exclusive hadronic modes alone would underestimate the inclusive sum.
