The Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix is the unitary change of basis between down-type quark mass eigenstates and the combinations appearing in the weak charged current. In particular, udˉ and usˉ production carry Vud​ and Vus​. Exclusive tau lepton decays into a charged pseudoscalar meson give
B(τ→πν)B(τ→Kν)​=fπ2​∣Vud​∣2fK2​∣Vus​∣2​(1−mπ2​/mτ2​)2(1−mK2​/mτ2​)2​,
(32)
up to small radiative corrections. The pseudoscalar decay constants specify the vacuum-to-meson axial-current matrix elements, ⟨0∣qˉ​γμγ5u∣P(p)⟩=ifP​pμ.
The observed branching fraction ratio is 0.70/10.83=0.06464, and the kinematic factor is approximately 0.8622. Hence the directly determined combination is
fπ​∣Vud​∣fK​∣Vus​∣​≃0.2738​.
(33)
With an independent, representative lattice input fK​/fπ​≃1.193, this becomes ∣Vus​/Vud​∣≃0.2295, close to the source's rounded expectation 0.224/0.975=0.2297. Conversely that expectation and the hadronic input predict a branching fraction ratio near 0.0648. Without the decay-constant input the table does not isolate a pure Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix ratio. Setting fK​=fπ​ would give approximately 0.0455, demonstrating the importance of flavor symmetry breaking.[ref]
