The neutral vector mesons with isospin zero have the same conserved quantum numbers, so an off-diagonal mass matrix can mix the singlet V1​=(uuˉ+ddˉ+ssˉ)/3​ and octet V8​=(uuˉ+ddˉ−2ssˉ)/6​. Choose phases such that
ω=cosθV1​+sinθV8​,ϕ=sinθV1​−cosθV8​.
(19)
At the ideal mixing angle tanθ=1/2​, or θ≃35.3∘, these become
ω≃(uuˉ+ddˉ)/2​,ϕ≃ssˉ​.
(20)
The overall sign of ϕ is unobservable. The omega meson near 782 MeV decays predominantly to three pions, whereas the phi meson near 1020 MeV strongly favours kaon-antikaon channels when they are open. Suppression of its nonstrange channels agrees with the OZI rule and a predominantly strange state. The omega meson is below the two-kaon threshold, so its absence of that channel alone would not establish the mixing angle; masses and the broader decay pattern provide the evidence together.[ref]
