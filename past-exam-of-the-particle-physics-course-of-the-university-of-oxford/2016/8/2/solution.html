Therefore
L≃8.18×1037 m−2s−1=8.18×1033 cm−2s−1​.
(51)
The factor 4π accounts for the convolution of two beams, rather than the 2π normalization of just one beam. The result assumes all stated bunch pairs collide at the chosen interaction point, speeds approximately c, zero crossing angle and negligible hourglass reduction. Additional interaction points do not multiply the local collider luminosity at a single detector.
