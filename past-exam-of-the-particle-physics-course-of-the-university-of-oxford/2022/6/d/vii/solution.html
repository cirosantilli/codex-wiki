Thus a reactor disappearance measurement cannot search for the intrinsic CP violation phase in the standard three-neutrino model. Sensitivity to that phase calls for suitable appearance channels and, usually, comparison of neutrino and antineutrino beams. Matter can produce differences between propagation of neutrinos and antineutrinos; these are not by themselves intrinsic CP violation, and are small for the proposed short reactor baseline.
