Using c and omitting ∂μ​Φ, the covariant derivative is
Dμ​Φ=(−igfWμ+​/2​iGfZμ​/2​),G=g2+g′2​.
(44)
The two components are orthogonal, so their squared magnitudes add; (Wμ+​)†=Wμ−​. Thus the Higgs field kinetic contribution to the Lagrangian density is
L=(v2+2vh+h2)(4g2​Wμ+​W−μ+8G2​Zμ​Zμ).​
(45)
The two v2 terms are gauge boson mass terms. Comparing with mW2​W+W−+21​mZ2​Z2 gives mW​=gv/2, mZ​=Gv/2​ and mW​/mZ​=cosθW​. The factor 1/2 for the real Z boson field is essential.
The two 2vh terms are the cubic Higgs boson coupling to W bosons and Higgs boson coupling to Z bosons. They produce the three-leg hW+W− and hZZ Feynman vertices, allowing Higgs decay to two W bosons, Higgs decay to two Z bosons when kinematically available, and the corresponding production mechanisms with virtual gauge bosons. The two h2 terms are quartic hhW+W− and hhZZ interactions, including vector-boson scattering into a Higgs boson pair. Written in terms of the masses,
L⊃mW2​W+W−+21​mZ2​Z2+v2mW2​​hW+W−+vmZ2​​hZ2+v2mW2​​h2W+W−+2v2mZ2​​h2Z2.
(46)
The corresponding Feynman vertex factors are 2imV2​gμν​/v for one h and 2imV2​gμν​/v2 for two h, for V=W,Z, after differentiating with respect to identical fields. These six contributions contain no photon mass or direct hAA interaction because QΦ=0.
