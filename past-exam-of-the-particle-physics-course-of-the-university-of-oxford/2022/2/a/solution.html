The favoured weak charged current transition is cˉ→sˉW− followed by W−→duˉ. The spectator quark u combines with uˉ to form the uuˉ/2​ component of the neutral pion, while dsˉ forms K0. The product of CKM matrix factors is Vcs​Vud∗​. The doubly Cabibbo suppressed transition is cˉ→dˉW− followed by W−→suˉ, with factors Vcd​Vus∗​; the final sdˉ is the neutral antikaon. Both are colour-suppressed internal-emission Feynman diagrams, with the same spectator routing.
