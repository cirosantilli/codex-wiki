For a valence-dominated isoscalar target, uvN​(x)=dvN​(x)=qv​(x). A left-chiral incident neutrino has unsuppressed scattering on a left-chiral quark and the (1−y)2 weight for a right-chiral quark. Summing the up and down contributions yields
dxdyd2σNCνN​​=πGF2​s​xqv​(x)[GL2​+GR2​(1−y)2],​
(45)
where GL2​=(gLu​)2+(gLd​)2 and GR2​=(gRu​)2+(gRd​)2. The common normalization here uses the conventional Fermi coupling and PDFs in a; the requested y shape is independent of an overall normalization convention.
Put w=sin2θW​. With I3u​=+1/2, I3d​=−1/2, Qu​=2/3 and Qd​=−1/3,
gLu​=21​−32​w,gLd​=−21​+31​w,gRu​=−32​w,gRd​=31​w,
(46)
so
GL2​=21​−w+95​w2,GR2​=95​w2,dydσNC​​∝(21​−w+95​w2)+95​w2(1−y)2.​
(47)
Fit the relative constant and (1−y)2 components to determine their coupling ratio, or use the NC-to-CC cross-section ratio to constrain the absolute combination. In this valence limit, integrating over 0<y<1 gives
Rν​=σCCνN​σNCνN​​=GL2​+31​GR2​=21​−w+2720​w2.​
(48)
The same valence momentum integral cancels between numerator and denominator. Experimentally, NC events have hadronic activity without the charged-current outgoing muon; known fluxes or CC normalization, muon identification and acceptance corrections are needed. Sea-quark contributions, charm thresholds, QCD corrections and departures from isoscalarity must be included for a precision weak-mixing-angle measurement.
