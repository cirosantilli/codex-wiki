Use B0=dbˉ and Bˉ0=dˉb. A box Feynman diagram with two W bosons and internal up-type quarks changes one neutral flavour into the other. The top quark contribution dominates the dispersive mixing amplitude and carries the squared CKM combination involving Vtd​,Vtb​. With a consistent meson phase convention,
pq​≃Vtd∗​Vtb​Vtd​Vtb∗​​.
(27)
The illustration in b shows this box and the dominant decay topology. A suitable common final state is f=J/ψKS​, reconstructed for example through J/ψ→μ+μ− and KS​→π+π−. The leading bˉ→cˉcsˉ tree transition gives B0→J/ψK0, while the conjugate gives Bˉ0→J/ψKˉ0. Neutral kaon mixing projects both kaon flavours onto the observed KS​.
The final state is approximately a CP eigenstate with ηf​=−1: J/ψ and KS​ each have CP approximately +1, and their relative orbital angular momentum is L=1 to couple the vector charmonium to the spin-zero parent. Neglecting the small kaon CP impurity and subleading decay amplitudes,
λf​=pq​Af​Aˉf​​≃−e−2iβ,Imλf​=sin2β.​
(28)
There are two indistinguishable paths, direct decay and mixing followed by decay, so their amplitudes interfere. For ΔΓd​≃0 and negligible direct CP violation, the time-dependent neutral-B CP asymmetry is
Γ(Bˉ0(t)→f)+Γ(B0(t)→f)Γ(Bˉ0(t)→f)−Γ(B0(t)→f)​=sin2βsin(Δmd​t).​
(29)
Measure the production flavour and proper decay time, fit the oscillation coefficient, and extract sin2β. This determines β up to the usual trigonometric ambiguities unless further information is used. The cancellation of the dominant decay phase is why this channel isolates the angle involving Vtd​ without needing Vub​.
