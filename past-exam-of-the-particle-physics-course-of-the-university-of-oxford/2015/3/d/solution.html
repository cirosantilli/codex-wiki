For a neutral ccˉ bound state, meson parity and charge conjugation give P=(−1)L+1 and C=(−1)L+S. In spectroscopic notation n2S+1LJ​, the integer n labels radial excitation, L is orbital angular momentum, S the total constituent spin, and J their total angular momentum. The surviving level diagram contains eight states:
  • ηc​: 11S0​, (L,S,J)=(0,0,0), 0−+.
  • J/ψ: 13S1​, (0,1,1), 1−−.
  • hc​: 11P1​, (1,0,1), 1+−.
  • χc0​: 13P0​, (1,1,0), 0++.
  • χc1​: 13P1​, (1,1,1), 1++.
  • χc2​: 13P2​, (1,1,2), 2++.
  • ηc​(2S): 21S0​, (0,0,0), 0−+.
  • ψ(2S): 23S1​, (0,1,1), 1−−.
The spin-dependent splittings distinguish the singlet and triplet levels and the three triplet P levels. The radial excitation changes neither parity nor charge conjugation.
