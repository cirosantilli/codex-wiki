The narrow scattering resonance indicates suppressed strong interaction decay. In the quark model, ccˉ is below the open-charm quark DDˉ threshold, so its constituents cannot simply emerge in separate charmed mesons. The OZI rule suppresses annihilation into light hadrons; for a 1−− color-singlet state the leading purely gluonic channel needs three gluons. The observed leptonic channels remain competitive. Its mass and I=0 fit charmonium; the small width alone would not uniquely identify the new quark flavour without the wider spectrum and threshold evidence.
