The basic Feynman diagrams have τ−→ντ​W−∗ followed by W−∗→e−νˉe​, μ−νˉμ​, or duˉ,suˉ, with the latter pairs undergoing hadronization. See the charged-current diagrams in a. Neglect all daughter masses, QCD corrections and inaccessible charm final states, and assume lepton universality. Each leptonic mode then has one unit of decay width; the two accessible quark combinations have three color charge states and total CKM weight ∣Vud​∣2+∣Vus​∣2≃1. Therefore
Γe​:Γμ​:Γhad​=1:1:3,Bhad​=53​=0.60.
(29)
The result is an inclusive parton model estimate. Perturbative QCD corrections increase the hadronic contribution, while muon mass, nonperturbative dynamics and radiative corrections also matter; the physical fraction is roughly 0.65.
Every ordinary hadronic mode already contains a tau neutrino, so it contains a lepton as well as hadrons if invisible particles are counted. For an additional visible charged lepton, a virtual photon can internally convert in a hadronic mode, for example τ−→ντ​π−γ∗→ντ​π−e+e−. The extra electromagnetic vertices suppress such modes; they are not a large fraction of the inclusive width. Alternatively a daughter pion or kaon can decay semileptonically or leptonically in flight, for example τ−→ντ​π−K0 followed by a semileptonic neutral-kaon decay. Those are secondary decay chains, not independent primary tau lepton branching fractions. Lepton-flavour-violating neutrinoless modes such as τ−→μ−π+π− do not contribute appreciably in the Standard Model.
