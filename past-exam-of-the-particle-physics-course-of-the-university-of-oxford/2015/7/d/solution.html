Neglect radiation, take Eτ​=MZ​/2≃45.6GeV, mτ​≃1.777GeV, and treat a fixed hadronic invariant mass mh​=1.5GeV as a single recoil system in τ−→h−ντ​. With negligible neutrino mass,
Eh∗​=2mτ​mτ2​+mh2​​,ph∗​=2mτ​mτ2​−mh2​​,Eh​=γ(Eh∗​+βph∗​cosϑ),γ=mτ​Eτ​​.
(32)
Thus Ehmin,max​=γ(Eh∗​∓βph∗​), numerically approximately 32.50 to 45.60GeV. In the ultrarelativistic limit the range is Eτ​mh2​/mτ2​≤Eh​≤Eτ​. A spread of hadronic invariant masses broadens these endpoints; an average mass alone is not a strict endpoint specification for the whole sample.
Let Pτ​=+1 denote a positive-helicity τ−, and measure ϑ from its laboratory flight direction in its rest frame. For a spin-zero hadronic recoil, the left-helicity tau neutrino and angular momentum conservation give
Γ1​dcosϑdΓ​=21​(1+Pτ​cosϑ).
(33)
For example a tau lepton with spin along +z can emit a left-helicity neutrino along −z, carrying spin along +z, with the spin-zero recoil along +z. A positive-helicity tau lepton then gives a harder visible recoil. If xh​=Eh​/Eτ​, r=mh2​/mτ2​ and β≃1, the more general tau hadronic polarimetry formula is
Γ1​dxh​dΓ​=1−r1​[1+αh​Pτ​1−r2xh​−1−r​],r<xh​<1,⟨Eh​⟩=γEh∗​+3γβph∗​​αh​Pτ​.
(34)
For spin-zero recoil αh​=1. For a spin-one system produced by the V-A interaction, the longitudinal and transverse weights are mτ2​:2mh2​, with opposite angular slopes; summing gives αh​=(mτ2​−2mh2​)/(mτ2​+2mh2​). At mh​=1.5GeV this is about −0.175, so the negative-helicity tau lepton has the higher mean total hadronic energy, about 39.43 versus 38.67GeV, within that spin-one model. [ref]
The supplied mean invariant mass does not specify the recoil's spin composition or its helicity amplitudes. Consequently the last sign cannot be universal: positive helicity is harder for a spin-zero recoil, negative helicity is harder for the specified 1.5GeV spin-one recoil, and a mixture requires its measured spectral weights. Polarization of an intermediate vector or axial-vector meson also changes the division of energy among its daughter hadrons; that information is not fixed by the total jet invariant mass alone.
