The bottom quark has charge −1/3 and left-handed weak isospin −1/2, requiring a charge-+2/3 partner in a complete Standard Model weak doublet. Completing the third quark generation also maintains the quark/lepton cancellation of gauge anomalies. A three-generation CKM matrix permits an irreducible CP violation phase. Flavour constraints and loop corrections, including their strong top-mass dependence in electroweak precision observables, provided indirect evidence before direct production. These arguments identify the required partner and its interactions; they do not by themselves fix its mass.
The Tevatron collided protons and antiprotons at s​=1.8TeV. Quantum chromodynamics produces ttˉ, mainly through qqˉ​ annihilation there. Each top quark decays predominantly to Wb before hadronization, giving W+W−bbˉ. The dilepton channel has two isolated oppositely charged leptons, missing transverse momentum from neutrinos, and bottom jets. The single-lepton channel has an isolated electron or muon, missing transverse momentum, and four leading jets, two consistent with bottom decay. The all-hadronic channel has more background. Secondary-vertex and soft-lepton tags identify bottom hadrons.
CDF and D0 reported independent excesses in 1995, with event kinematics and reconstructed masses consistent with a common very heavy quark. Fits assign jets to the two decay chains and compare with expected W+jets, multijet and instrumental backgrounds. Agreement of counts, bottom content and reconstruction across channels is stronger evidence than an excess in one event class alone. [ref] [ref]
