Nuclear fusion in the Sun produces predominantly electron neutrinos, through the pp chain and subsidiary reactions. The historical solar-neutrino deficit could have reflected either a source-model error or flavour transformation. In a two-flavour approximation, mixing gives the vacuum survival law derived in a. In matter the additional electron neutrino charged current potential is Ve​=2​GF​Ne​. Dropping a common diagonal term, the flavour Hamiltonian is
Hf​=4EΔm2​(−cos2θsin2θ​sin2θcos2θ​)+(Ve​0​00​).
(35)
The MSW effect enhances mixing near 2EVe​=Δm2cos2θ. An initially electron-like high-density state follows its instantaneous mass eigenstate approximately adiabatically as solar density falls. After phase averaging, high-energy solar survival approaches sin2θ12​, while low-energy vacuum-averaged survival approaches 1−21​sin22θ12​, neglecting the small third-angle correction.
The Sudbury Neutrino Observatory used about a kilotonne of heavy water viewed by photomultipliers. Its three channels separate flavour from total active flux:
νe​+d→p+p+e−(CC),νx​+d→p+n+νx​(NC),νx​+e−→νx​+e−(ES).
(36)
The charged current channel measures electron flavour, the neutral current channel measures all active flavours nearly equally, and elastic scattering is more sensitive to electron flavour but also detects the others. Cherenkov radiation gives the charged-particle energy and direction. Neutrons were detected through capture gamma rays, with later salt loading and dedicated counters improving the neutral-current separation. The different angular patterns, light isotropy and energy spectra permit a combined channel fit, with radioactive backgrounds and calibrations controlled.
The measured electron flux was lower than the total active flux, while the latter agreed with solar fusion expectations. This directly established a non-electron component and removed a simple overall source-flux error as the explanation. [ref] Super-Kamiokande supplied high-statistics directional elastic-scattering data; radiochemical chlorine/gallium experiments supplied complementary thresholds, and reactor antineutrino disappearance independently confirmed the solar mass-squared difference. Together these support large-angle mixing at Δm212​∼7.5×10−5eV2 rather than the atmospheric splitting in c.
