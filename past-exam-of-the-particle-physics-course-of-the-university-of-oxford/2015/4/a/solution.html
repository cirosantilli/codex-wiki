Assume coherent production and detection, relativistic neutrinos, two active neutrino flavours, propagation in vacuum, negligible packet separation and no absorption. Invert the given real lepton mixing matrix to obtain ∣νμ​⟩=c∣ν2​⟩−s∣ν3​⟩ and ∣ντ​⟩=s∣ν2​⟩+c∣ν3​⟩, where c=cosθ, s=sinθ. With phases ϕa​=Ea​t−pa​⋅x in natural units,
∣ν(L)⟩=ce−iϕ2​∣ν2​⟩−se−iϕ3​∣ν3​⟩,⟨ντ​∣ν(L)⟩=cs(e−iϕ2​−e−iϕ3​).
(15)
Using the paper's opposite sign convention conjugates this probability amplitude and leaves the probability unchanged. For equal momentum, Ea​≃p+ma2​/(2p) and t≃L, so Δϕ=Δm2L/(2E). The equal-energy convention gives the same leading result from the momentum difference; evaluating each component at a different arrival time would incorrectly double the phase.
Pμτ​=4c2s2sin22Δϕ​=sin2(2θ)sin24EΔm2L​,Pμμ​=1−Pμτ​.
(16)
Since ℏc=1.97327×10−7eVm,
4EℏcΔm2L​=1.26693E[GeV]Δm2[eV2]L[km]​.
(17)
This gives the stated factor 1.27. Neutrino oscillation in this approximation measures ∣Δm2∣, not the absolute neutrino mass or its ordering.
