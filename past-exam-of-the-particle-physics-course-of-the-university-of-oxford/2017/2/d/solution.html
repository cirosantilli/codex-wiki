Use the explicit charged channels D+→π+π+π− and K+→π+π+π−, labeling the two positive pions as 1,2 and the negative one as 3. With mπ​=0.139GeV, the opposite-charge pair mass squares have the ranges
4mπ2​≤s13​,s23​≤(M0​−mπ​)2.​
(20)
Their exact correlated boundaries follow by taking the pair rest-frame angle to its endpoints. For general daughter masses, define Λ(a,b,c)=a2+b2+c2−2ab−2ac−2bc. At fixed s=s13​,
s23±​=m22​+m32​+2s(M02​−m22​−s)(s+m32​−m12​)±Λ(M02​,s,m22​)Λ(s,m12​,m32​)​​.​
(21)
The charged-pion thresholds give the following numerical comparison:
Parent | Q=M0​−3mπ​ | Allowed single-axis interval, GeV2 | Dalitz area, GeV4 \D+ | 1.453GeV | 0.07728 to 2.99636 | approximately 5.074 \K+ | 0.077GeV | 0.07728 to 0.12603 | approximately 0.001611
The area is about 3150 times smaller for the kaon. Including the M0−2​ phase-space prefactor, its integrated labelled three-body phase space is about 220 times smaller; neither comparison predicts a ratio of weak decay widths without the matrix elements.
A ρ0→π+π− intermediate state produces horizontal or vertical bands near s13​ or s23​=mρ2​≃(0.775GeV)2 in the charm decay. Scalar pion-pair amplitudes, including broad low-mass S-wave contributions and accessible f0​(980), can also populate the plot. The kaon pair mass cannot exceed 0.355GeV, so it cannot contain an on-shell ρ0 or f0​(980) band; smooth nonresonant and rescattering effects remain possible. Channels containing neutral pions use their supplied 0.135GeV mass and shift these boundaries slightly without changing the conclusion. The figure shows kinematic domains and representative accessible bands, not an assumed measured intensity.
