For a nucleon at rest and beam energy well above its mass scales, s≃2mN​E. If the leading parton distributions are approximately independent of beam energy, integration over x,y therefore makes both total cross sections approximately proportional to E. The plotted plateaus are evidence for scattering on approximately pointlike constituents in this range, with the departures expected from QCD evolution and threshold effects.

Read the original high-energy plateaus as approximately 0.67 and 0.33 in the plotted 10−38cm2/GeV units. With R=σνˉ​/σν​≃1/2 and r=Qˉ​/Q,

Using the literal readings 0.33/0.67 gives r=0.1905, so twenty percent is an appropriate graphical estimate, not a precision fit. A purely valence target would have R=1/3, while a fully symmetric sea would give R=1.
The neutrino-antineutrino DIS sea momentum ratio is a ratio of momentum fractions in the effective flavour combinations, not an unweighted count of antiquarks. The cross-section integral contains xq(x): it cannot determine ∫qˉ​/∫q without additional assumptions about their shapes. A distribution growing at small x can have a large number integral while its momentum integral remains finite.
