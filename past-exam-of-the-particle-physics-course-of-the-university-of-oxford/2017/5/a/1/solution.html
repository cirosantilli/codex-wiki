Assume first an ultrarelativistic charged-current interaction with negligible electron mass in the centre-of-mass spin analysis. Choose the incident antineutrino along +z. Its right helicity gives spin +z; the interacting left-helicity electron moves along −z and also has spin +z. The total initial projection is +1. The final antineutrino and selected electron likewise have total spin +1 along the outgoing antineutrino direction. The spin-one rotation therefore gives

The full Standard Model also has neutral-current Z exchange. In the contact limit it supplies a right-electron contribution as well as interference in the left-electron channel. With gL​=1/2+sin2θW​, gR​=sin2θW​, the full high-energy shape is proportional to gL2​(1+cosθ⋆)2/4+gR2​. Thus the pure squared angular form is an explicitly stated charged-current approximation, not an exact prediction for all Standard Model contributions.
