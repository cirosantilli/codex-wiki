Top decay occurs before a colour-bound hadron can form. Using representative scales Γt​∼1–1.5GeV and ΛQCD​∼0.2GeV,
τt​∼Γt​ℏ​∼5×10−25s<τhad​∼ΛQCD​ℏ​∼3×10−24s.​
(42)
Thus top quarks do not live long enough to form ordinary top-containing mesons. The decay products still participate in QCD radiation and hadronization, so “free decay” here means decay before binding, not a stable observable free coloured asymptotic particle.
