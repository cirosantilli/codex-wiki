The weak charged current couples an up-type quark to the rotated down field diL′​=∑j​Vij​djL​. A neutral current is flavour universal within equal-charge quarks. Rotating it into the mass basis therefore gives
∑i​dˉiL′​γμdiL′​=∑j,k​dˉjL​γμ(V†V)jk​dkL​=∑j​dˉjL​γμdjL​.
(43)
The off-diagonal terms vanish by CKM unitarity, so no tree-level flavour-changing neutral current is generated. In the two-generation description, the orthogonal strange-charm partner completes the rotation whose single down-strange combination alone would have produced an off-diagonal neutral coupling. The complete multiplet supplies the cancellation.
At loop level, a flavour-changing amplitude has the structure
Aa→b​∝∑i​Vai​Vbi∗​F(mi2​/mW2​),a=b.
(44)
If all internal masses were equal, F would be common and ∑i​Vai​Vbi∗​=0. Subtracting an arbitrary common value makes the suppression explicit:
Aa→b​∝i∑​Vai​Vbi∗​[F(mi2​/mW2​)−F(mref2​/mW2​)].​
(45)
This is the Glashow-Iliopoulos-Maiani mechanism. Unequal masses leave small loop-level flavour-changing neutral currents, so the statement that they are never observed would be too strong: the mechanism suppresses them and forbids them at tree level. The cancellation requires complex CKM products; the rounded table of absolute values alone must not be summed as if every phase were positive.
