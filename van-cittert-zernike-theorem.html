Van Cittert-Zernike theorem (g(b)=I(b/λ)/I(0))

 ... Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Optical coherence First-order optical coherence Spatial optical coherence
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For a distant spatially incoherent quasimonochromatic source in the small-angle regime, the equal-delay mutual coherence across projected baseline b is the Fourier transform of its angular intensity. With matched collector response, the normalized degree is g(b)=∫I(θ)e−i2πb⋅θ/λd2θ/∫I(θ)d2θ. Equal collector intensities and matched polarizations make fringe visibility ∣g∣. Finite bandwidth and unmatched optical paths introduce additional temporal-coherence averaging.
