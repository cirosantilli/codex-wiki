Separated scheme

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Separated morphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 1
A scheme over a base S is separated when its structure morphism to S is a separated morphism. An absolute scheme is usually called separated when it is separated over SpecZ.
