Transverse electromagnetic mode (Ez​=Bz​=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
A transverse electromagnetic mode has both electric field and magnetic field transverse to its propagation axis. An ideal two-conductor guide can support such a mode without a geometric waveguide cutoff frequency; an empty simply connected perfect conductor pipe cannot.
