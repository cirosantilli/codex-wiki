Moving-coil galvanometer

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Galvanometer
OurBigBook.com Words: 915 Articles: 6
A moving-coil galvanometer has a winding that rotates about an axis, a restoring spring, and a pointer or optical readout. In the radial-field construction, a cylindrical iron core and shaped magnetic poles keep the magnetic field approximately in the coil's plane as it turns. The resulting moving-coil galvanometer torque is nearly independent of the deflection angle; the spring then converts electric current into a proportional settled deflection. [ref]
