Center of a group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 67 Articles: 1
The centre of a group is Z(G)={z∈G:zg=gz for every g∈G}. It is a normal subgroup. A nontrivial finite p-group has nontrivial centre: in the class equation, each noncentral conjugacy class has size divisible by p, so ∣Z(G)∣≡∣G∣≡0(modp).
