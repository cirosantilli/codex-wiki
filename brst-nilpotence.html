BRST nilpotence (Q2=0)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing BRST symmetry
OurBigBook.com Words: 74 Articles: 1
A BRST charge is nilpotent when Q2=0, making BRST cohomology well-defined. For the critical bosonic string theory, the matter and ghost central charges cancel, D−26=0, and the intercept is one. In a ghost convention with a zero-mode linear shift, the shifted total constraint generators, rather than the unshifted ones, obey the centerless Witt algebra.
