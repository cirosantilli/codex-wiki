Conjugation Jacobian for a compact Lie group (J(t)=∏α>0​∣1−α(t)∣2)

For a connected compact Lie group with maximal torus T, the map q:G/T×T→G, q(xT,t)=xtx−1, has the displayed real Jacobian determinant. Choose an invariant inner product and identify the tangent space of G/T with t⊥. Translation of the derivative at (T,t) to the identity gives (X,Y)↦(Ad(t−1)−I)X+Y. Each pair of opposite roots of a root system corresponds to a real two-plane on which the determinant is ∣1−α(t)∣2. The torus direction has determinant one. Normalized quotient and Haar measures have matching volume constants because the Riemannian submersion gives volG=vol(G/T)volT. The map covers regular elements ∣W∣ times, giving the Weyl integration formula with conjugacy averaging for general functions.
