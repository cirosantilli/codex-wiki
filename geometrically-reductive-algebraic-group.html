Geometrically reductive algebraic group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Linear algebraic group
OurBigBook.com Words: 167 Articles: 1
For every finite-dimensional rational representation V and every nonzero v∈VG, a geometrically reductive algebraic group admits a homogeneous invariant polynomial F∈k[V]G of positive degree with F(v)=0. A linearly reductive group can take degree one. In positive characteristic, higher degree is essential: a power pr can separate a fixed vector even when an invariant linear functional cannot.
