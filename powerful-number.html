Powerful number

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Integer
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 3
A positive integer is squarefull when every prime dividing it also divides it to at least the second power. Thus n is not squarefull as soon as some prime p satisfies p∣n but p2∤n.
