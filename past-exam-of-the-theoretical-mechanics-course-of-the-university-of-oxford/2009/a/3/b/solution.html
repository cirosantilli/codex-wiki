It is conservation of the incompressible liquid volume, with no liquid entering or leaving through the plates or material rim.
When the plates approach, h˙<0 and p>0 inside: the liquid is squeezed outward. Separation requires p<0 relative to the surrounding air and inward motion of the edge. Negative gauge pressure is not intrinsically impossible, but substantial suction can cause cavitation or air invasion, and an advancing air/liquid interface can develop fingering. Thus the same mathematical lubrication solution exists for either sign, whereas its opening-plate physical validity needs these additional failure mechanisms to be absent; the closing case is the robust regime of the stated idealized model.
