Impermeability imposes homogeneous Neumann data: ϕx​=0 at x=0,a and ϕy​=0 at y=0,b. Separation of variables therefore gives
ϕ=Acos(nπx/a)cos(mπy/b)e−iωt,ω2=π2c02​(n2/a2+m2/b2).​
(16)
One may take n,m≥0 without duplication. For a nontrivial oscillation they are not both zero. A time-independent spatially constant potential is a gauge mode at zero frequency, rather than an additional sound oscillation.
