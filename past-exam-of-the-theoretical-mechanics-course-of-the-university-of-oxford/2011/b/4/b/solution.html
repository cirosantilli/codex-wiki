Linearize about rest, with the equilibrium forces already absorbed in the reduced pressure. The quadratic convective acceleration drops out. Since k×(ui+vj+wk)=−vi+uj, the component equations are
ut​−2Ωv=−Px​/ρ,vt​+2Ωu=−Py​/ρ,wt​=−Pz​/ρ,ux​+vy​+wz​=0.​
(43)
The scan prints vw​ in the continuity equation; the derivative required by incompressibility is vy​.
To eliminate pressure, put ζ=vx​−uy​ and ∇h2​=∂x2​+∂y2​. Taking the vertical component of the curl of momentum gives
ζt​+2Ω(ux​+vy​)=0,soζt​=2Ωwz​.
(44)
Taking horizontal divergence of momentum and substituting continuity gives
ρ−1∇h2​P=wzt​+2Ωζ.
(45)
Differentiate this in z and use Pz​/ρ=−wt​:
∂t​(∇2w)+2Ωζz​=0.
(46)
One more time derivative, followed by the vorticity relation, yields the Poincaré equation for inertial waves
∂t2​(∇2w)+4Ω2wzz​=0.​
(47)
This derivation retains all three velocity components, including the transverse component coupled by rotation.
