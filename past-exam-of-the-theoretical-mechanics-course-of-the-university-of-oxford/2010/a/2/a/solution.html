Balance streamwise convection of order one with transverse viscous diffusion Re−1∂y2​u. This requires a thickness Re−1/2. Continuity then makes the normal velocity of that same small order. Set
Y=Re1/2y,V=Re1/2v.​
(12)
The normal momentum equation makes the leading pressure independent of Y. It equals the outer pressure, whose inviscid tangential momentum balance gives −px​=Us​Us′​. The leading tangential equation and continuity are consequently
uux​+VuY​=Us​Us′​+uYY​,ux​+VY​=0.​
(13)
The divergence-free field (u,V) has a streamfunction with u=ΨY​, V=−Ψx​. Substituting yields
ΨY​ΨxY​−Ψx​ΨYY​=Us​Us′​+ΨYYY​.​
(14)
The stationary impermeable wall has u=V=0, so ΨY​(x,0)=0 and Ψ(x,0) is constant, chosen to be zero. Matching to the outer slip velocity gives ΨY​(x,∞)=Us​(x).
