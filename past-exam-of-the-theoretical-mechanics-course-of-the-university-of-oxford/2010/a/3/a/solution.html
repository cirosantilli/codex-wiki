Introduce R=ρUL/μ. Balancing the streamwise pressure gradient Π/L with cross-layer viscosity μU/(δ2L2) gives
Π=δ2LμU​.​
(20)
After division by that viscous scale, the streamwise momentum equation is
δ2R(ut​+uux​+wuz​)=−p​x​+uzz​+δ2uxx​+S,S=μUδ2ρgL2​.
(21)
The transverse equation, divided by its pressure-gradient scale, is
δ4R(wt​+uwx​+wwz​)=−p​z​+δ2wzz​+δ4wxx​.
(22)
Continuity scales exactly to ux​+wz​=0. With δ≪1, δ2R≪1 and S=O(1), the leading equations are
0=−p​x​+uzz​+S,p​z​=0,ux​+wz​=0.​
(23)
