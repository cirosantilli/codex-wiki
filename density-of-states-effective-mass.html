Density-of-states effective mass (Nc​=2[mDOS​kB​T/(2πℏ2)]3/2)

 Home Physics Semiconductor Effective density of states
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
A scalar effective mass chosen to reproduce carrier state counting near a band edge. For gv​ equivalent ellipsoidal conduction valleys, mDOS3/2​=gv​(ml​mt2​)1/2 when the displayed factor two accounts for spin degeneracy. The valley degeneracy is then already included and must not be multiplied in again. A conductivity mass weights inverse curvatures and is generally different; for an isotropically averaged set of ellipsoids, 3/mcond​=1/ml​+2/mt​.
