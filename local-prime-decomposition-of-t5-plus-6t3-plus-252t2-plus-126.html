Local prime decomposition of T5 plus 6T3 plus 252T2 plus 126 (T5+6T3+252T2+126)

This polynomial is Eisenstein at 2. Over Q3​, its Newton polygon has a single slope −2/5, so it is irreducible and a root generates a totally ramified extension of degree five. Over Q7​, its factor degrees are 1,1,3: the simple residue roots 1,−1 lift, and the remaining roots have valuation 1/3. Thus the degree-five number field generated by a root has one prime above 3 and three above 7.
