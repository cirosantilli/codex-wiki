Orbital radius (r=∣r∣)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Orbit (dynamical system)
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
The instantaneous distance from an attracting centre to an orbiting body. It is constant on a circular orbit but varies along an elliptic orbit. For Kepler's third law the relevant size is the semi-major axis, which equals the orbital radius only for a circular orbit.
