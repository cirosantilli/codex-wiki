Local coordinate

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A local coordinate at a point p of a Riemann surface is a biholomorphism from a neighbourhood of p to an open subset of C. After translating the image, one may require that the coordinate sends p to zero.
