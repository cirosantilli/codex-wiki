Low-pass filter

 Home Electrical engineering Signal processing Linear filter
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 10
A low-pass filter preferentially transmits low frequencies and attenuates higher frequencies. It can smooth rapid noise in a slowly varying signal, but it also suppresses wanted variations outside the useful signal bandwidth. An RC low-pass filter gives a concrete continuous-time example; a moving average implements related smoothing on a discrete-time signal.
The useful question is how much change the measurement can tolerate. Preserving the low frequencies of a temperature record while reducing a fast unwanted oscillation can improve readability. Preserving the sharp onset of a pulse requires higher frequencies, so excessive smoothing changes its timing and shape. The RC low-pass frequency response and RC low-pass step response make this signal bandwidth versus settling time tradeoff quantitative.
