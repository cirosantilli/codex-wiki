Let G be a connected compact Lie group, T a maximal torus, and W its Weyl group. With normalized Haar measures and D(t)=∏α>0​(1−e−α(t)), every continuous class function satisfies ∫G​f(g)dg=∣W∣−1∫T​f(t)∣D(t)∣2dt. For a general continuous function, replace f(t) by its average ∫G/T​f(gtg−1)d(gT). Conjugation from G/T×T covers the regular elements ∣W∣ times, and its real Jacobian determinant is ∣D(t)∣2.
