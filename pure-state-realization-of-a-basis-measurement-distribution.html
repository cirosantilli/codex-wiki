Pure-state realization of a basis measurement distribution (∣ψ⟩=∑j​pj​​eiθj​∣ej​⟩)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Wave function Probability amplitude Born rule
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For an orthonormal basis {∣ej​⟩} and nonnegative numbers pj​ summing to one, the displayed vector is a unit vector and gives measurement probabilities pj​ by the Born rule. Conversely, every pure state with those probabilities has coefficients of these magnitudes, with arbitrary phases on the nonzero coefficients. A common change of all phases is a global phase; differences between them are relative phases that can affect measurements in another basis. Zero-probability coefficients vanish, so their phases have no effect.
