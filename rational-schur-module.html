Rational Schur module (Dλ​=det−k⊗Dλ+k1​)

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Linear algebraic group Rational representation Rational representation of the general linear group
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
For a dominant integer tuple λ, choose k=max(0,−λm​) and define Dλ​=det−k⊗Dλ+k(1,…,1)​, using the polynomial Schur module on the right. The alternant character formula for the general linear group shows that a larger admissible shift yields the same irreducible representation.
