Vector potential

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus Curl
OurBigBook.com Words: 79 Articles: 1
A vector potential for a divergence-free vector field F is a vector field A satisfying F=∇×A. It is not unique because adding a gradient leaves its curl unchanged.
