Computer bus

 Home Computer science
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 21
A computer bus connects components through agreed communication paths and transfer rules. On a shared computer bus, a CPU and a DMA engine can both want the same path to computer memory: attaching another initiator does not create another independent path. Bus arbitration chooses who may start; bus ownership states who may use each resource; bus throughput measures the useful data delivered. This makes sharing a performance question as well as a correctness question.
