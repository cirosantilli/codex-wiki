Combing of a group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 337 Articles: 4
Choose a finite symmetric generating alphabet Σ and one word σ(g) representing each group element, with σ(1) the empty word. The associated paths in the Cayley graph start at the identity and traverse one edge per unit time, remaining at their endpoint after completion. A synchronous combing has a constant K such that paths to adjacent endpoints stay within K at equal times. An asynchronous version permits increasing reparametrizations. The formal language need not be a regular language; regular synchronous representative languages define automatic groups.
