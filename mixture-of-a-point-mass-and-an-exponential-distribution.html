Mixture of a point mass and an exponential distribution (L(Y)=pδ−c​+(1−p)Exp(λ))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Mixed probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
Let c>0, 0≤p≤1 and λ>0. With probability p, take Y=−c; otherwise draw an exponential distribution of rate λ. This mixed probability distribution has a negative atom and a continuous nonnegative component. Its moments are E[Y]=−pc+(1−p)/λ and E[Y2]=pc2+2(1−p)/λ2. The law of total variance equivalently gives Var(Y)=(1−p)/λ2+p(1−p)(c+1/λ)2.
When p<1, conditioning on Y≥0 selects exactly the exponential component, so P(Y≥t∣Y≥0)=e−λt for t≥0. An ordinary probability density function for the continuous component integrates to 1−p, not one; the missing mass is the atom, not a normalization error.
