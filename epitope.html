Epitope

 Home Biology Protein Antibody
OurBigBook.com Words: 62 Articles: 1
An epitope is the molecular feature recognized by an antibody. Protein denaturation can expose an epitope or destroy the spatial arrangement required for its recognition; an antibody that works on a folded protein need not work in SDS-PAGE-based Western blot antibody detection.
