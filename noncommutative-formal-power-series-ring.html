Noncommutative formal power series ring (k⟨⟨x1​,…,xr​⟩⟩)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Free associative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 182 Articles: 2
For a field k and finitely many generators, this ring consists of arbitrary coefficient families indexed by finite words in the generators. Addition is coefficientwise and multiplication uses concatenation: the coefficient of w in FG is ∑uv=w​Fu​Gv​, a finite sum. Finite series form the free associative algebra. If F,G=0, their lowest nonzero homogeneous parts multiply to a nonzero polynomial, since a word of fixed length has a unique splitting at a specified position. Thus the ring is a noncommutative domain.
