Group (periodic table)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Periodic table
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A periodic group is a vertical column of the periodic table. For example, fluorine and chlorine are in periodic group 17, with seven valence electrons in each neutral ground state chemical atom. A shared outer electron configuration supports similarities in chemical reactions, rather than equality of every property.
