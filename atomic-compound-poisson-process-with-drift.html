Atomic compound Poisson process with drift (Xt​=γt+∑j​zj​Ntj​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Lévy process
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
With finitely many nonzero marks zj​ and independent Poisson processes Nj of rates λj​, the displayed process is a Lévy process with no Gaussian component. Its Lévy measure is ∑j​λj​δzj​​, its mean is t(γ+∑j​λj​zj​), and its variance is t∑j​λj​zj2​. Its paths have finitely many jumps on every bounded interval and deterministic slope γ between jumps. Coincident mark sizes combine their rates; zero marks have no effect. The drift here uses the uncompensated finite-sum convention, which differs from the drift parameter in a compensated Lévy–Khintchine formula.
