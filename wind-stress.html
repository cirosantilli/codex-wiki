Wind stress (τ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Ocean circulation
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 28
Wind stress is the tangential traction exerted by the atmosphere on the ocean surface. Its curl injects vorticity into the ocean.
