With insertion convention iΠ, ghost propagator i/p2, and oriented ghost-gluon Feynman rule gfabcpμ with p pointing away along the ghost arrow, the ghost loop is iΠgh​=−g2CA​δab∫dDl(2π)−D(l+k)μlν/[l2(l+k)2]. In dimensional regularization with D=4−ϵ, a Feynman parameter and a shift give the displayed ultraviolet pole. Its contraction is g2CA​δabk2kν/(32π2ϵ); it cancels the nontransverse part of the two-three-gluon-vertex contribution. A different insertion or ghost-vertex convention must be translated before comparing signs.
