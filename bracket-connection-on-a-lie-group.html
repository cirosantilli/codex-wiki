Bracket connection on a Lie group (∇X​Y=λ[X,Y]for left-invariant X,Y)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle) Affine connection
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
Specify the displayed rule on left-invariant vector fields and extend by the Leibniz rule to an affine connection on the Lie group. In a left-invariant vector field frame Li​, the extension is ∇Li​​(zjLj​)=Li​(zj)Lj​+λzj[Li​,Lj​]. Applying the bare bracket formula to arbitrary vector fields instead would violate the Leibniz rule unless λ=1. With R(X,Y)Z=∇X​∇Y​Z−∇Y​∇X​Z−∇[X,Y]​Z, the Jacobi identity gives
T(X,Y)=(2λ−1)[X,Y],R(X,Y)Z=λ(λ−1)[[X,Y],Z],Ric(X,Y)=λ(λ−1)B(X,Y),
(17)
for left-invariant arguments, where B is the Killing form and Ric(X,Y)=tr(Z↦R(Z,X)Y). These identities determine the tensor fields everywhere. The endpoints λ=0,1 have zero curvature, with respectively left- and right-invariant parallel frames, and opposite torsion tensors. The midpoint λ=1/2 is torsion-free but need not have zero curvature.
