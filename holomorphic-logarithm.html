Holomorphic logarithm

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
A holomorphic logarithm of a nonvanishing holomorphic function f is a holomorphic function g with eg=f. On a simply connected domain, every nonvanishing holomorphic function has one.
