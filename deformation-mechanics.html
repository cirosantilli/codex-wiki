Deformation (mechanics)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 36
Deformation changes a body's shape or size. Strain quantifies relative changes in lengths and angles, whereas a rigid translation or rotation alone produces no strain. The continuum approximation describes these changes using displacement fields and their spatial variation.
