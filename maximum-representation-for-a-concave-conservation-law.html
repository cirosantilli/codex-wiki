Let f be smooth and strictly concave, with f(0)=0 and f′ bijective, and let u0​ be smooth with compact support. For 0<t<t∗​ in the smooth concave-flux characteristic lifespan, the primitive Ux​=u solves the Hamilton-Jacobi equation Ut​+f(Ux​)=0 and obeys
U(t,x)=maxy​{U(0,y)+tg(tx−y​)},
(17)
where g is the concave Legendre dual. The tangent inequality for a concave function bounds every candidate by U(t,x), and the backward characteristic curve attains equality. Its foot x0​ is the unique maximizer, and u(t,x)=h((x−x0​)/t) for h=(f′)−1. This statement concerns the smooth solution, without asserting a post-crossing continuation.
