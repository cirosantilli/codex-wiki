Global section (s∈Γ(X,F))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Global section functor
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
A global section of a sheaf of modules F is an element of Γ(X,F), meaning a section defined over the whole space X.
