Zero power sums force a finite complex multiset to vanish (∑j=1n​λjk​=0 (1≤k≤n) ⟹ λj​=0)

Collect the distinct nonzero values among the λj​ as μ1​,…,μr​ with positive multiplicities mj​. The first r power sums imply ∑j​mj​μjk​=0 for 1≤k≤r. The coefficient matrix is an invertible weighted Vandermonde matrix, so all mj​ vanish, a contradiction unless r=0. Its invertibility also follows because a polynomial ∑k=1r​ak​zk vanishing at zero and all the μj​ has r+1 distinct roots and degree at most r.
