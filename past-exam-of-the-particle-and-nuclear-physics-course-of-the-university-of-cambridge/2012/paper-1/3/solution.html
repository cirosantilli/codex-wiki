A Feynman diagram specifies a term in the perturbative scattering amplitude, not a measured microscopic trajectory. Assign external spinors or polarization vectors, an interaction factor to each Feynman vertex and a propagator to each internal line; impose four-momentum conservation and integrate over independent loop momenta. Sum amplitudes for indistinguishable alternatives, then square, sum final spins and average unpolarized initial spins.
