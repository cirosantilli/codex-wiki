In neutron-proton radiative capture, the initial pair at rest has total energy Mc2, where M=mp​+mn​. The emitted photon carries momentum, requiring equal and opposite nuclear recoil. Some binding energy becomes deuteron kinetic energy.
Exact two-body kinematics in natural units gives
M=Eγ​+md2​+Eγ2​​,Eγ​=2MM2−md2​​.
(46)
Since md​=M−B,
Eγ​=B−2MB2​,B−Eγ​=2MB2​.​
(47)
With the stated rounded masses,
B−Eγ​=2(938.272+939.565)(2.224)2​MeV=0.00131699MeV=1.317keV​.
(48)
The photon energy is 2.222683MeV. At leading order the difference equals the nonrelativistic recoil estimate pγ2​/(2md​)≃B2/(2md​); exact kinematics fixes the small distinction between using md​ and M in that approximation.
