The negative muon emits a virtual W− and becomes a muon neutrino; the boson creates an electron and an electron antineutrino:
μ−→νμ​+W−∗→νμ​+e−+νˉe​.
(22)
Both vertices are weak charged currents.
Typical transfers satisfy ∣q2∣≲mμ2​≪MW2​, so the propagator factor (MW2​−q2)−1 expands as MW−2​ plus small corrections. Contracting this short-distance exchange produces the local Fermi interaction, with strength contained in GF​. This effective description does not assume that the real W mass vanishes. The same treatment applies to leptonic tau decays with the relevant masses changed.[ref]

Γμ​=192π3(1.166×10−5)2(0.106)5​GeV=3.0562×10−19GeV,
(23)

(24)
