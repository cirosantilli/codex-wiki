Use an inclusive spectator model: one heavy constituent decays through the Fermi interaction, and sum the open charged-current channels. In weak-decay channel counting, each massless final lepton pair gives basic width
Γ0​(m)=192π3GF2​m5​,τ=CΓ0​(m)ℏ​.
(25)
A quark pair contributes three colors multiplied by its squared Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix element. Do not multiply by three just because the initial quark has three colors: its lifetime is the width of one initial state, averaged over initial color when appropriate. Define
Su​=∣Vud​∣2+∣Vus​∣2=0.9893,Sc​=∣Vcd​∣2+∣Vcs​∣2=0.9893.
(26)
For the tau lepton, two leptonic channels contain an electron or a muon; light hadronic channels have duˉ or suˉ. Therefore
Cτ​=2+3Su​=4.9679,Γτ​=2.0103×10−12GeV,ττ​≃3.27×10−13s.​
(27)
No charmed hadron can be produced: the lightest charmed meson exceeds the tau mass. [ref] The supplied charm-quark parameter is not a free asymptotic final-particle mass. The leading count neglects electron and muon masses and light hadronic threshold effects.
For the charm quark, c→sW+∗ and c→dW+∗ dominate. The emitted current has two light leptonic channels and the same light hadronic channels as above; a tau cannot be produced:
Cc​=Sc​(2+3Su​)=4.91474347,Γc​=8.5232×10−13GeV,τcspectator​≃7.72×10−13s.​
(28)
For the bottom quark, retain b→cW−∗ with the supplied Vcb​ and neglect the much smaller b→u channel. The emitted current can produce three lepton flavors and two up-type quark families, with three colors for each family. In particular b→ccˉs is open at the given masses, although suppressed by phase space. Counting each open channel as massless gives
Cb​=∣Vcb​∣2[3+3Su​+3Sc​]=0.01429728,
(29)
Γb​=1.0204×10−12GeV,τbspectator​≃6.45×10−13s.​
(30)
Setting Cabibbo sums to one recovers the familiar counts 5, 5 and 9∣Vcb​∣2. Despite its larger mass, the bottom quark is slowed by its small interfamily mixing factor.
These are leading estimates rather than precise lifetimes of free quarks. confinement makes charm and bottom observable in hadrons, whose lifetimes involve spectator interactions, Quantum chromodynamics corrections and bound-state effects. mass corrections are significant for bottom: even the single-charm channel with massless leptons has the three-body weak-decay mass correction
f(r)=1−8r+8r3−r4−12r2lnr,r=(mc​/mb​)2=0.09,f(r)≃0.520.
(31)
Tau and double-charm channels have different suppressions, so multiplying the entire width by this one factor is inconsistent. Accurate hadron lifetimes require those channel-dependent factors and hadronic dynamics; the exercise supports the explicitly stated leading counts.
