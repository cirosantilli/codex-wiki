The dominant interaction is strong. At hadron level, panel (iii) is an effective hadronic vertex for Δpπ. The initial uuu constituents rearrange into uud and udˉ after creation of a ddˉ pair:
uuu⟶(uud)+(udˉ).
(16)
One original up quark enters the charged pion; the other two join the new down quark in the proton. Gluon-induced pair creation supplies the quark-flow diagram interpretation. This is nonperturbative hadronization: the effective hadronic vertex does not claim that one elementary perturbative quark graph calculates its width. Charge, baryon number and net quark flavor are conserved.
