Treat the quoted maximum as the resonant Z boson contribution described by the Breit-Wigner resonance cross section. The electron and Positron each have spin 1/2, while the Z boson has spin one, so
g=[2(1/2)+1]22(1)+1​=43​.
(1)
Neglect the electron mass at this centre-of-mass energy. Each incident momentum magnitude is then pi​=E0​/2 in natural units. Write Be​=Γee​/Γ. At the resonance peak the denominator is Γ2/4, and the same partial decay width appears for production and decay:
σpeak​=pi2​πg​Γ2/4Γee2​​=E02​12π​Be2​.
(2)
The peak resonance cross section and branching fractions relation contains Be2​, because the electronic channel occurs at both ends of the resonant process.
For an area in ordinary units, restore (ℏc)2. The original PDF prints ℏc=197.5MeV, which is dimensionally incomplete: the intended standard conversion is 197.5MeVfm=0.1975GeVfm. Also,
2.0nb=2.0×10−37m2=2.0×10−7fm2.
(3)
Consequently
Be​=12π(ℏc)2σpeak​E02​​​=12π(0.1975)2(2.0×10−7)(91.2)2​​,B(Z0→e+e−)=0.03363≃3.4%.​
(4)
This estimate uses the supplied resonance model. The full physical elastic electron-Positron cross section also contains nonresonant photon exchange and interference; an experimental use of the formula must isolate the resonant contribution.
