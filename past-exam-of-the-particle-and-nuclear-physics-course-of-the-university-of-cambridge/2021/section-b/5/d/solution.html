The charged pion has valence content π−=duˉ. Its constituents annihilate into a virtual W boson, followed by the lepton pair:
d+uˉ⟶W−∗⟶ℓ−+νˉℓ​,ℓ=e,μ.
(23)
The quark vertex carries the u--d Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix element, and the leptonic vertex is the weak charged current. The pion's nonperturbative spatial structure is encoded in the pion decay constant when evaluating the decay amplitude.
