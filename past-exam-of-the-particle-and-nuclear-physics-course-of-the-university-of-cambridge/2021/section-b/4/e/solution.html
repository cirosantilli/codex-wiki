The quoted measurement, about 500±10, is larger by a factor 500/19.75≃25.3. If that whole difference were attributed to a ratio of other amplitudes, it would require a factor 25.3​≃5.03.
This does not invalidate quark mixing. The supplied Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix is only a schematic hierarchy in powers of sinθC​, particularly for Vub​; its omitted coefficients matter greatly after squaring. Equal phase space also does not imply equal hadronic decay amplitudes: the two transitions have different hadronic form factors and strong-interaction dynamics, and loop contributions can interfere with the charmless tree amplitude. Thus the calculation correctly predicts enhancement of the charmed channel, but the supplied CKM hierarchy alone does not quantitatively predict the measured ratio. It would be unjustified to treat 19.75 as a precision prediction with only the quoted experimental error.
