For (i), CP symmetry relates the negative- and positive-pion radiative channels to excellent accuracy. The original positive-pion table therefore predicts
B(π−→μ−νˉμ​γ)≃(2.00±0.25)×10−4.​
(49)
The comparison requires the same photon-energy and event-selection definition. The table attaches an unexplained footnote to this radiative channel; it does not specify those cuts in the supplied excerpt. An arbitrarily soft photon is not a separately infrared-finite exclusive observable, so the numerical value must retain the table's definition.
Part (ii) contains an apparent notation error in the original PDF as well as the TeX: it prints e−νe​e−e+, rather than e−νˉe​e−e+. Taken literally, the charged leptons carry net electron-family lepton number 2−1=+1, and the printed νe​ adds another +1. The initial pion has zero lepton number. This is a ΔLe​=2 channel, not the charge conjugate of the listed positive-pion decay. In the ordinary lepton-number-conserving Standard Model approximation,
B(π−→e−νe​e−e+)=0for the channel as printed.​
(50)
Allowing lepton-number-violating masses for Majorana neutrinos would require additional physical assumptions and does not justify assigning the tabulated number to that channel.
For the likely intended charge-conjugate process, the prediction is instead
B(π−→e−νˉe​e−e+)≃(3.2±0.5)×10−9.​
(51)
This distinction follows from a lepton-number test of a decay channel; replacing the printed neutrino by an antineutrino must be stated explicitly.
