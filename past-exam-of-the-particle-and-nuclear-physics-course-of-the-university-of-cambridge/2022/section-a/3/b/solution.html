There are three vertex types, with all legs labeled in the following original drawings:
ggg,W+W−γ,W+W−Z.​
(9)
The gluon cubic interaction vertex follows from the non-Abelian gauge field strength of Quantum chromodynamics. The electroweak Yang-Mills theory Lagrangian, after expressing the fields in the physical charged and neutral basis, produces the two charged-W boson vertices. In particular there are no fundamental γγγ, ZZZ, ZZγ, or Zγγ vertices, and the separate colour and electroweak gauge factors do not generate a mixed ggγ or ggZ vertex. The count is by the boson labels requested, not by separate assignments of gluon colour indices.
