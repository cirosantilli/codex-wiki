Use the usual Feynman rule convention in which the diagonal quadratic terms of the free Lagrangian, including the physical W boson and Z boson masses, are incorporated into their propagators. Then there are
0 fundamental two-gauge-boson interaction vertices.​
(8)
A line joining two ends is a propagator, not an interaction vertex. Gauge boson self-interactions begin with cubic terms in the Yang-Mills theory Lagrangian. The question's g,W,Z,γ name four families: they include eight gluon colour components and the two charged W bosons W±.
There is a convention to distinguish here: if a calculation expands around massless free fields, quadratic mW2​Wμ+​W−μ and mZ2​Zμ​Zμ/2 terms may be drawn as two-leg mass insertions. Those two insertion types belong to that bookkeeping convention. Similarly, two-point self-energy corrections occur in loop diagrams. Neither is an additional fundamental interaction in the standard physical-mass, tree-level rules used for the count above.
