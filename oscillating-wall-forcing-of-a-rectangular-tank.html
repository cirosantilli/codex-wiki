Oscillating-wall forcing of a rectangular tank (an​=kn​sinh(kn​h)−(ω2/g)cosh(kn​h)Hn​​)

For a tank 0<x<L, −h<z<0, let the left wall move by Re(ce−iωt) with small amplitude, while the right wall and bottom remain fixed. A periodic velocity potential Re(fe−iωt) has fx​(0,z)=−iωc. Add the harmonic function
Q(x,z)=2Liωc​[(z+h)2−(L−x)2]
(94)
to obtain G=f+Q with homogeneous wall and bottom Neumann boundary conditions. Thus G=a0​/2+∑n≥1​an​cos(kn​x)cosh(kn​(z+h)), where kn​=nπ/L. The linearized free-surface boundary conditions give Gz​−λG=H at z=0, with λ=ω2/g and
H(x)=Liωc​[h−2λ​(h2−(L−x)2)].
(95)
Its Fourier cosine coefficients Hn​=(2/L)∫0L​H(x)cos(kn​x)dx give the title expression for n≥1 and a0​=−H0​/λ. In particular Hn​=2iωcλ/(Lkn2​) for n≥1. The denominator vanishes at the corresponding normal modes of surface gravity waves in a rectangular tank, giving resonance. Exact undamped resonance produces secular growth instead of a bounded periodic response. Free transients do not generally decay in this inviscid model.
