Sigma-centered forcing (σ-centered forcing)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Forcing Centered subset of a forcing order
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
A forcing order is sigma-centered when it is the union of countably many centered subsets. Every sigma-centered forcing has the countable chain condition for forcing.
