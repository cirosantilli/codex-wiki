Extensive games and the problem of information by Harold W. Kuhn (1953, 10.1515/9781400881970-012)

Publisher record for the reprint in Classics in Game Theory, pp. 46–68: www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/9781400829156-011/html
The paper develops extensive-form games and Kuhn's theorem relating mixed strategies, behavioural strategies and perfect recall. The finite two-round construction is proved directly in the examination solution.
