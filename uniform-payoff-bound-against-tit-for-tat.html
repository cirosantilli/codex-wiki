Uniform payoff bound against Tit for tat (∑t<T​ut​≤RT+(Ttempt​−R))

In a symmetric two-action stage game write R,P,Ttempt​,S for mutual cooperation, mutual defection, sole defection and sole cooperation payoffs. Suppose Ttempt​≥R, P≤R and Ttempt​+S≤2R. Against an initially cooperative Tit for tat, every action sequence has limiting upper average payoff at most R.
For a deviator's actions at​, put a−1​=C, h(C)=0 and h(D)=Ttempt​−R. The opponent at time t plays at−1​. Check the four transitions: C to C and C to D satisfy
u(at​,at−1​)≤R+h(at​)−h(at−1​)
(7)
with equality; D to C uses S≤2R−Ttempt​, and D to D uses P≤R. A telescoping sum gives the displayed finite-horizon bound. Divide by the horizon and let it increase. Mutual Tit for tat yields R every round, so it is a Nash equilibrium under the long-run average payoff criterion. The argument is pathwise and therefore covers arbitrary deviations representable by finite-state machines, as well as action sequences outside that class.
