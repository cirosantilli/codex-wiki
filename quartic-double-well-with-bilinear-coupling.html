Quartic double well with bilinear coupling (g(x,y)=x4+y4−4a2xy,a>0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Critical point
OurBigBook.com Words: 111
The stationary points of this polynomial are the origin and (a,a), (−a,−a). Its Hessian matrix at the origin has eigenvalues ±4a2, so the origin is a saddle point. At each of the other points the eigenvalues are 8a2 and 16a2, giving strict local minima. The identity
g=(x2−y2)2+2(xy−a2)2−2a4
(60)
shows that they are also the only global minima, with value −2a4.
For −2a4<c<0, the level set g=c has two closed components, one surrounding each minimum. The zero level set has two lobes meeting at the origin with the coordinate axes as tangent directions. For c>0, there is one closed outer component. These statements also follow in polar coordinates from the quadratic equation in r2, together with the absence of other stationary points.
