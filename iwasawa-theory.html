Iwasawa theory

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 37
Iwasawa theory studies arithmetic objects in infinite towers of number fields, often Zp-extensions. Passing to inverse limits turns growing ideal class groups, unit groups and Galois groups into modules over an Iwasawa algebra. Their algebraic ranks and characteristic ideals organize the growth and relate it to p-adic L-functions.
