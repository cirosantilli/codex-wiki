Formal group law

OurBigBook.com Words: 403 Articles: 12
A one-dimensional commutative formal group law over a ring R is a series F(X,Y)∈R[[X,Y]] satisfying the identity, commutativity, and associativity axioms. A homomorphism from F to G is a series θ(T)∈TR[[T]] satisfying θ(F(X,Y))=G(θ(X),θ(Y)).
