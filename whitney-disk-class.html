Whitney disk class (π2​(x,y))

 ... Topology Topological manifold 3-manifold Heegaard splitting Heegaard Floer homology Heegaard Floer chain complex
OurBigBook.com Words: 122
For intersection generators x,y, a Whitney disk is a map of a disk into the symmetric product, with its two boundary semicircles mapped to the two product tori and its endpoints mapped to x,y. Its homotopy class belongs to π2​(x,y). Projecting it to the Heegaard surface gives an integral domain. With the surface oriented, the convention is ∂α​D=y−x. The obstruction to a disk is the class ϵ(x,y)∈H1​(Y) obtained by joining the points along alpha arcs and returning along beta arcs. In the Heegaard Floer setup, its vanishing is equivalent to equality of the generators' Spin-c structures; for the rational homology sphere diagrams this is equivalent to π2​(x,y)=∅.
