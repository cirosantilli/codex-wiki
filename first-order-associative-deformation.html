First-order associative deformation (a∗b=ab+εf(a,b),ε2=0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Formal associative deformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
The order-ε part of associativity is af(b,c)−f(ab,c)+f(a,bc)−f(a,b)c=0, so f is a Hochschild cocycle. A change of coordinates T=1+εg changes f to f+δg. Therefore equivalence classes of first-order associative deformations of a fixed underlying algebra are its second Hochschild cohomology. Unital deformations use normalized cochains.
