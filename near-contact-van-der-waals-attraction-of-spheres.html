Near-contact van der Waals attraction of spheres (U(h)≃−AH​a/(12h))

 Home Chemistry Mixture Colloid
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For two equal spheres of radius a separated by a surface gap h≪a, a positive Hamaker constant AH​ gives the nonretarded Van der Waals force approximation U=−AH​a/(12h) and F=−dU/dh=−AH​a/(12h2). The Derjaguin approximation derives the geometry factor from the planar interaction energy per area W=−AH​/(12πh2) and effective radius a/2. A sphere and plane instead have effective radius a, doubling the force at the same gap. Molecular repulsion, roughness and retardation limit these continuum formulas.
