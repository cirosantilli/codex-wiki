Bracketing method

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 5
A bracketing method finds a zero of a function by maintaining a root bracket and shrinking it as new values of the function become available. The bisection method always tries the midpoint; its special attraction is a predictable bisection error bound without any derivative evaluations.
