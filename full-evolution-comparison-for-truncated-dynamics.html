OurBigBook.com Words: 50
A Duhamel comparison can bound truncation error using commutators evolved under the full Hamiltonian. This avoids assuming that a black-box Lieb-Robinson bound for H also holds for every truncation. Polynomial shell weights and exponential distance suppression make the sum over omitted shells convergent. Comparing two truncations through the full evolution bounds their difference.
