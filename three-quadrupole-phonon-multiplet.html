Three-quadrupole-phonon multiplet (S3(V2​)=V0​⊕V2​⊕V3​⊕V4​⊕V6​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Three identical quadrupole quanta occupy the third symmetric power of the five-dimensional spin-two space. Its dimension is (37​)=35, and its irreducible representations have J=0,2,3,4,6, each once. All have positive parity. This counts allowed angular states in the ideal harmonic nuclear quadrupole vibration; anharmonic interactions can split their energies.
