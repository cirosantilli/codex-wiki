Geothermal gradient (dT/dz)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
The geothermal gradient is the rate at which temperature varies with depth. For steady one-dimensional thermal conduction with constant thermal conductivity kT​, the magnitude of upward heat flux is q=kT​dT/dz.
