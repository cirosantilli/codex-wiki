Labelled hypergraph copy count

For hypergraphs F,H, a labelled copy is an injective function ϕ:V(F)→V(H) taking every hypergraph edge of F to an hypergraph edge of H. Specified vertex of a hypergraph classes may additionally require ϕ(i)∈Vi​. The count is the sum, over these injective maps, of the product of the host hypergraph edge indicators. Without injectivity the same expression counts hypergraph homomorphisms. On a common host of size n, their counts differ by at most (2∣V(F)∣​)n∣V(F)∣−1: every noninjective map identifies at least one pair of abstract vertices of a hypergraph, each prescribed equality permits at most n∣V(F)∣−1 maps, and the union bound completes the estimate. An induced copy additionally preserves nonedges, so its indicator product includes nonedge factors. The counting lemma for octahedrally quasirandom three-uniform hypergraphs estimates these labelled products when the requisite centered triple indicators have small box norm.
