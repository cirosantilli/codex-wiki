Steadily moving antiplane shear (y=βx2​,β=1−V2/c2​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Antiplane shear
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
For subsonic motion ∣V∣<c, an antiplane shear displacement w(x1​−Vt,x2​) satisfies β2wxx​+wx2​x2​​=0. The displayed rescaling makes it a harmonic function of (x,y), so locally w=ReH(x+iy) for a holomorphic function H. Set F=βH. The Cauchy-Riemann equations then give μF′=βσ13​−iσ23​. Keeping this potential normalization explicit avoids losing a factor of β in the dynamic stress field. At sonic or supersonic speeds the elliptic harmonic reduction does not apply.
