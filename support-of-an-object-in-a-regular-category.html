Support of an object in a regular category (σ(A)=im(A→1))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Regular category
OurBigBook.com Words: 300 Articles: 3
The support of an object A in a regular category is the subterminal object appearing as the image of a morphism in a regular category of A→1. A regular functor preserves this construction. In the Category of sets, the support is the empty set if A is empty and a singleton otherwise.
