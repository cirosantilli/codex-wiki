Least Herbrand model (HF​=⋂{H:H is a Herbrand model of F})

 ... Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic Herbrand universe Herbrand structure Herbrand model
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
For a satisfiable equality-free universal logical conjunction of Horn clauses, intersecting the sets of true ground atoms of its Herbrand models gives another Herbrand model, contained in every one of them. A ground atom is true in this least Herbrand model exactly when it holds in every Herbrand model. Positive existential sentences are true in the least Herbrand model exactly when they follow from the Horn clauses; allowing negated atoms makes this equivalence fail. For example, the least Herbrand model of ∀x(P(x)→Q(x)) over a single constant a makes both predicates empty and satisfies ∃x¬P(x), whereas the first-order structure making both predicates universal is a countermodel to that consequence.
