Barotropic deformation radius (LD​=gH​/f)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Potential vorticity Shallow-water potential vorticity Shallow-water quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The barotropic deformation radius is the horizontal scale at which free-surface gravity and rotation balance. It contributes LD−2​ to the denominator of the one-layer Rossby-wave dispersion relation.
