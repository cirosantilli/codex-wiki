Rapid-rotation mean gyrotactic orientation (⟨p⟩=−2e1​/(3BΩ))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biological fluid dynamics Gyrotaxis Gyrotactic orientation Fokker-Planck equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 1
For rigid fluid rotation with vorticity −2Ωe2​, BΩ≫1, and BDR​ of order one, the normalized steady density is f=(4π)−1[1−2p1​/(BΩ)]+O((BΩ)−2). The result follows by acting with the rotation generator on p1​ and using ∫pi​pj​dΩ=4πδij​/3. Reversing the imposed rotation reverses the mean horizontal swimming direction.
