Hadley cell

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Meteorology
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
A tropical atmospheric overturning circulation with rising air near the equator, poleward flow aloft, subtropical descent and an equatorward lower return. In its ideal upper branch, conservation of angular momentum gives u=Ωasin2ϕ/cosϕ for air initially at rest relative to the equatorial surface. Eddies and friction modify this idealization.
