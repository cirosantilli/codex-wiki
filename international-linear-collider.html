International Linear Collider

 Home Physics Experimental apparatus Particle accelerator Linear particle accelerator Linear collider
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A proposed electron–positron linear collider based on superconducting RF acceleration. The 2007 reference design relevant to an exam-era comparison targeted 500 GeV centre-of-mass energy and peak collider luminosity 2×1034cm−2s−1, with approximately 31 km total footprint. These are historical design parameters, not achieved machine performance. [ref]
