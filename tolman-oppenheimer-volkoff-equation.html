Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equation (TOV equation)

 Home Physics Branch of physics General relativity Stress-energy tensor Perfect fluid in general relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 3
The Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff equation governs a static, spherically symmetric perfect fluid in general relativity. In geometrized units, if m(r) is the mass inside areal radius r, then
drdm​=4πr2ρ,drdp​=−r(r−2m)(ρ+p)(m+4πr3p)​.
