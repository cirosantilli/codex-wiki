Characters of a C-star algebra respect the involution (ϕ(x∗)=ϕ(x)​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra Character of an algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For a self-adjoint element h, the exponentials exp(ith) are unitary. A continuous algebra character therefore satisfies ∣exp(itϕ(h))∣≤1 for every real t, forcing ϕ(h) to be real. Decomposing an arbitrary element into real and imaginary self-adjoint parts proves the displayed identity. Thus every algebra character is a star-preserving scalar homomorphism; commutativity of the whole algebra is not needed for this fact.
