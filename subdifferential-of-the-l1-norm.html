Subdifferential of the L1 norm (∂∥β∥1​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Subdifferential
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
The subdifferential of the L1 norm consists of vectors z with zj​=sgn(βj​) when βj​=0 and zj​∈[−1,1] when βj​=0. These coordinate conditions give the Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions for Lasso and Square-root Lasso.
