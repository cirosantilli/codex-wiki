Vasicek model (drt​=a(b−rt​)dt+ηdWt​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A Gaussian short rate model with Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process dynamics under the chosen pricing measure. For a>0, its unit zero-coupon bond value is P(t,T)=A(τ)e−B(τ)rt​, where τ=T−t, B(τ)=(1−e−aτ)/a and
logA(τ)=(b−2a2η2​)(B(τ)−τ)−4aη2​B(τ)2.
(23)
Solve the linear stochastic differential equation, integrate over future time, and use the exponential moment of the resulting Gaussian integral to obtain this price. Its Gaussian law permits negative short rates.
