The algebraic set
V(x2+y2−1,x2−z2−1)⊆AC3​
(25)
has the two irreducible components
V(y−iz,x2−z2−1)andV(y+iz,x2−z2−1).
(26)
Each is irreducible because the linear change u=x−z, v=x+z identifies its coordinate ring with
C[u,v]/(uv−1)≅C[u,u−1],
(27)
an integral domain.
