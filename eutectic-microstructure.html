Eutectic microstructure

 ... Thermodynamic potential Thermodynamic free energy Gibbs free energy Phase coexistence curve Phase diagram Eutectic system
OurBigBook.com Words: 85 Articles: 1
A two-solid microstructure formed by the coupled transformation of liquid at a eutectic point. Lamellae and rods are common morphologies. A hypoeutectic alloy can contain both primary crystals and a eutectic microstructure; those microconstituents are not the same accounting categories as its total phase fractions.
