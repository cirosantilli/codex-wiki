T gate (T=diag(1,eiπ/4))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Phase gate
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The phase gate with angle π/4. It rotates the Bloch vector by π/4 around z, and T2=S. It is not a Clifford gate: it takes the positive X eigenstate to a state with vector (1/2​,1/2​,0), outside the stabilizer octahedron.
