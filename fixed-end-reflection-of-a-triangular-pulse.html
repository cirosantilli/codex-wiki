Fixed-end reflection of a triangular pulse (y/H=T(x/L−ct/L)−T(−x/L−ct/L))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Wave equation on a string
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For a mechanical string on x≤0 with fixed endpoint y(0,t)=0, take an initially incident symmetric triangular pulse HT(x/L), where T(s)=max(0,1−∣2s+1∣). The superposition principle gives the title formula. Both terms satisfy the wave equation away from the corners and satisfy it weakly everywhere; their values cancel at the endpoint. At ct=L/2 their displacements cancel on the whole half-line, but the velocity is nonzero on −L/2<x<0. Hence a flat instantaneous shape need not mean zero wave energy.
