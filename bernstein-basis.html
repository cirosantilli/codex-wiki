These n+1 nonnegative polynomials form a basis of polynomials of degree at most n, and sum to one on [0,1]. For 0≤j≤n,
xj=∑k=0n​(n)j​(k)j​​bk,n​(x),

where ratios for j=0 equal one and terms with k<j vanish. The identity follows from (kn​)(k)j​/(n)j​=(k−jn−j​) and the binomial theorem. It gives explicit basis coefficients using the falling factorial.
