Fundamental group of a closed orientable surface (π1​(Σg​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 31
For the closed orientable surface of genus g,
π1​(Σg​)=⟨a1​,b1​,…,ag​,bg​​∏i=1g​[ai​,bi​]=1⟩.
(17)
In particular, the sphere has trivial fundamental group and the torus has fundamental group Z2.
