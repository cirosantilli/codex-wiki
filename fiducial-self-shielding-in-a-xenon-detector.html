Fiducial self-shielding in a xenon detector

 Home Physics Particle detection Time projection chamber Dual-phase xenon detector
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
Selecting an inner fiducial volume reduces externally produced radiation because outer xenon attenuates it before it reaches the selected target. This trades target mass for reduced background. It does not remove uniformly distributed internal radioactivity or every neutron recoil, so purification, veto information and calibration remain important.
