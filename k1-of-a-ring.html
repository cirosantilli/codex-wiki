K1 of a ring (K1​(A)=GL∞​(A)/E∞​(A))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic K-theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 2
Stable invertible matrices, with stabilization g↦diag(g,1), form the stable general linear group. Its elementary subgroup is its commutator subgroup, and the displayed quotient is its abelianization. Elementary matrices lift along any surjective ring map, even when arbitrary invertible matrices do not lift. Over a field, determinant identifies K1​ with its multiplicative unit group.
