The two outcomes are not independent. Conditional on the selected die, the rolls are independent, but sharing the unknown die creates statistical dependence. Each nonfavored face has probability (1−1/2)/5=1/10.
The unconditional marginal distribution assigns the following probability to a 1 on either roll:
P(X=1)=P(Y=1)=61​(21​+5⋅101​)=61​.

P(X=1,Y=1)=61​[(21​)2+5(101​)2]=201​=361​.

Thus the joint probability does not factor into the two marginals. Conditional independence given a shared latent choice does not imply unconditional independence.
