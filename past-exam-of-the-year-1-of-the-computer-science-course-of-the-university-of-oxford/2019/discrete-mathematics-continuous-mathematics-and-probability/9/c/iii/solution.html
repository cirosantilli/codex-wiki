Represent the removed half as the first m=500 cards in a uniformly random ordering. Every unordered half is then equally likely. Let X1​,…,X500​ be their values. Each has mean zero and variance one, and every distinct pair has covariance −1/999 by the same ordered-pair argument as in (ii).
The general covariance expansion from (b), applied without imposing independence, gives
E[Z]=0,Var(Z)=500−999500⋅499​=999250000​.​
(79)
This is the finite population correction for a half-sample; treating the draws as independent would give the wrong variance.
Finally {Z≥50}⊆{∣Z∣≥50}, so Chebyshev's inequality yields
P(Z≥50)≤502Var(Z)​=999⋅2500250000​=999100​.​
(80)
The cards returned before this sampling step restore the original population; no conditioning on the earlier two observed values is imposed.
