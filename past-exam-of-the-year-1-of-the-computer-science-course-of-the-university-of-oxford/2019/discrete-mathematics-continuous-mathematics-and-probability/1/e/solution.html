There are six functions: the four power maps x↦xk for k=1,2,3,4, and the two constant maps 0,1. All calculations in this part are in the finite field Z5​.
Put e=f(1). Multiplicativity gives e2=e, so the absence of zero divisors forces e=0 or e=1. If e=0, then f(x)=f(x)f(1)=0 for every x, giving only the constant-zero map.
Suppose e=1. For nonzero x, the equality f(x)f(x−1)=1 shows f(x)=0. The nonzero elements form a cyclic group generated by 2, in the order 1,2,4,3. Choosing a=f(2) determines every value on this group by f(2j)=aj. Each a∈{1,2,3,4} satisfies a4=1, so all four choices give consistent group homomorphisms.
Finally, z=f(0) satisfies z2=z and z=zf(x) for every x. The choice z=0 works for all four group maps. The choice z=1 forces every nonzero value to be 1, giving one extra map, the constant-one map. Thus the count is 1+4+1=6. The four zero-preserving possibilities are exactly the four displayed power maps. This is the special case with five elements of multiplicative self-maps of a finite field.
