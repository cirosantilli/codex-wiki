Let m≥1 be the number of real contacts, and suppose the requested name is at position j, counting from one. The implementation above makes exactly j name-equality comparisons, including the successful comparison; null checks are not string comparisons. Thus the minimum is one, when the first real contact is requested, and the maximum is m, when the last is requested. The precondition excludes unsuccessful recalls and recalls on an empty book. This counts calls to string equality, not individual character inspections within each call.
