Solutions to all nine questions of the University of Oxford computer science preliminary examination, Long Vacation 2019, held on 4 September 2019. The printed groups are Discrete Mathematics (1–3), Continuous Maths (4–6), and Probability (7–9). All options are treated.
Source: Public examination-paper archive, member 2018/L/a10100w1.pdf, nine scanned pages, paper A10100W1. The dated cover establishes 2019 despite the archive member’s directory name.
