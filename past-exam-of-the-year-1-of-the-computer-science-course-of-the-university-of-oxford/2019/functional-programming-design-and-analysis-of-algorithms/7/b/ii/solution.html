Past exam of the year 1 of the computer science course of the University of Oxford / 2019 / Functional programming, design and analysis of algorithms / 7 / b / ii / Solution

 ... Past exam of the year 1 of the computer science course of the University of Oxford 2019 Functional programming, design and analysis of algorithms 7 b ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 102
During the computation in part i, record an index last[t] attaining the minimum at every positive amount. Starting with t=v, print xlast[t]​ and replace t by t−xlast[t]​, continuing until t=0.
Every step decreases the amount and reduces the table entry by exactly one. The printed denominations therefore sum to v and their number is exactly M[v], proving optimality. The total running time is O(nv+M[v]), including the output; M[v]≤v because denomination 1 is available. The predecessor array adds O(v) to the space complexity. This is reconstruction from the dynamic programming certificate, not the largest-denomination greedy algorithm, which need not be optimal for arbitrary denominations.
