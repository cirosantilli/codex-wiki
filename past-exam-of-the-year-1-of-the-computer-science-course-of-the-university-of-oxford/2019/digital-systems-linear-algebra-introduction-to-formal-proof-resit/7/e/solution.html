Write H for (ϕ→ψ)→ϕ. The following derives Peirce's law directly from the printed primitive rules, including double-negation elimination:
LineFormulaJustification
1HAssume for the final implication
2¬ϕAssume locally
3ϕAssume inside the scope of 2
4⊥Negation elimination rule, 2 and 3
5ψLogical falsity elimination rule, 4
6ϕ→ψImplication introduction rule, discharging 3–5
7ϕImplication elimination rule, 1 and 6
8⊥Negation elimination rule, 2 and 7
9¬¬ϕNegation introduction rule, discharging 2–8
10ϕDouble-negation elimination, 9
11((ϕ→ψ)→ϕ)→ϕImplication introduction rule, discharging 1–10
Every assumption is discharged, so the last formula is a theorem. In particular, no unproved appeal to the derived RAA rule is hidden in the argument.
