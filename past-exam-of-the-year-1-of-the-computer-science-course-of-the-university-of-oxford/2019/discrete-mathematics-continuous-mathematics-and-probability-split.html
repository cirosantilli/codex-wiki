OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 146
Source paper: released examination archive, member 2018/T/a10100w1.pdf. The cover identifies the regular Preliminary Examination in Computer Science, Trinity Term 2019, Thursday 27 June, 14:30–17:30. The archive directory does not determine the sitting. All nine questions are covered, including every printed Roman subpart.
Questions 1–3 form Discrete Mathematics, 4–6 Continuous Maths, and 7–9 Probability. The printed relation in 3(c)(ii) fails to be a partial order; that issue is treated explicitly rather than silently changing the source.
