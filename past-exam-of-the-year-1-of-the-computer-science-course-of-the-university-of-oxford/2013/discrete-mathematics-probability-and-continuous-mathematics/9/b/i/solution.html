Assume a<b. An increasing affine map sends the two endpoints to zero and one when ca+d=0 and cb+d=1. Solving gives
c=b−a1​,d=−b−aa​.​
(123)
Indeed, for 0≤t≤1, the cumulative distribution function of the transformed random variable is
P(b−aV−a​≤t)=P(V≤a+(b−a)t)=b−aa+(b−a)t−a​=t,
(124)
which is the cumulative distribution function of the continuous uniform distribution on [0,1].
A decreasing affine map works too, by swapping the endpoints:
c=−b−a1​,d=b−ab​.​
(125)
These are the two possible choices, since the image interval must have endpoints zero and one. The case a=b cannot be transformed into a nondegenerate continuous uniform distribution by deterministic constants.
