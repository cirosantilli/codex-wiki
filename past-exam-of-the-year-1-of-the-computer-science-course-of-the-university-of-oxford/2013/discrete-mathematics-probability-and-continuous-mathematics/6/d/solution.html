r2+2r+2=0,r=−1±i.
(85)

2a+2ax+2b=8x+4,
(86)
so a=4 and b=−2. Adding the homogeneous and particular solutions yields
y(x)=e−x(C1​cosx+C2​sinx)+4x−2.​
(87)
The two arbitrary real constants account for the second order of the ordinary differential equation.
