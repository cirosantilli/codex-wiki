In a symmetric relation, each of the n diagonal pairs (a,a) can be chosen independently. For each of the (2n​) unordered pairs of distinct elements, either both ordered pairs (a,b),(b,a) occur or neither occurs. Every such choice is free and produces a distinct symmetric relation. Hence
2n2(2n​)=2n(n+1)/2.​
