Keep the two history D flip-flops and add an output D flip-flop with previous value q=zn−1​. Its next value q+=zn​ is one if all three inputs are one, or if the output was one and at least one of the three inputs is one. In the latter case the samples are not all zero, so the previous one must be retained. This gives
q+=abc+aq+bq+cq,qinitial​=0.​
(5)
If abc=111, the first product sets the output. If abc=000, every product vanishes and the output clears. For every mixed input triple, at least one of aq,bq,cq equals q, so the output is retained. Feed this sum of products to the output D input, use b+=a,c+=b for the history, and use asynchronous clear or an appropriate initialization to establish q=0.
Minimality can be seen directly from the truth table. At q=0, the only one-entry is abc=111, forcing the product abc or a narrower term. At q=1, the triples 100, 010 and 001 must all produce one; combining any two in one product would also admit 000, which must produce zero. Thus three more products are needed. The displayed four products achieve the minimum; removing any Boolean literal from abc,aq,bq,cq would include a zero-entry.
