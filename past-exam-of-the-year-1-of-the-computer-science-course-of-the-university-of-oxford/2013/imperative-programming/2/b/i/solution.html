Define F to be the sequence of values obtained by starting at first and repeatedly following next until null. The representation invariant says that size is nonnegative; size == 0 holds exactly when both endpoints are null; otherwise there are exactly size distinct reachable linked list nodes, the final one is last, first.prev == null, last.next == null, and all neighboring links are reciprocal.
