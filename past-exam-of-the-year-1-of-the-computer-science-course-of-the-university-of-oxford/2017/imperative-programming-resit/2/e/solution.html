Past exam of the year 1 of the computer science course of the University of Oxford / 2017 / Imperative programming (resit) / 2 / e / Solution

 ... Year 1 of the computer science course of the University of Oxford Past exam of the year 1 of the computer science course of the University of Oxford 2017 Imperative programming (resit) 2 e
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
Maintain that all positive h<lo fail rh2≥i2, while hi satisfies that inequality; its least satisfying integer lies in [lo,hi]. Monotonicity of rk2 in positive k validates both updates. At exit the endpoints agree and identify exactly that least integer.
The loop variant hi-lo decreases on every iteration and is at least halved up to rounding. Starting with at most i candidates therefore needs O(log(i+1)) arithmetic operations, conventionally O(logi) for i≥2. For i=1 the loop is empty. The final upper-bound check proves the returned Boolean is the required membership answer.
