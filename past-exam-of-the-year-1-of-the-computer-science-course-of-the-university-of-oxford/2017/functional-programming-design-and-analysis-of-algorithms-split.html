This is the 13 June 2017 preliminary examination. Questions 1–4 concern functional programming; Questions 5–8 concern algorithm design and analysis of algorithms. All 35 printed subparts are answered. Source paper: released examination archive, member a10097w1-1.pdf.
The Haskell definitions use separate question namespaces. For Question 1, hide the locally defined names with import Prelude hiding ((.), zipWith, uncurry); for Question 4 use import Prelude hiding (scanl). Alternative solve definitions are alternatives, not simultaneous duplicate declarations. Assume finite total puzzle inputs; infinite streams and partial values are treated explicitly where relevant. Operation counts separate numeric bit costs from list and comparison steps.[ref]
