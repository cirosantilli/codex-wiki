Substitute the QR decomposition into the normal equations:
RTRy=RTQTb.
(19)
The diagonal of R is nonzero, so RT is invertible. Cancel it algebraically to obtain
Ry=QTb.​
(20)
Compute the three inner products in QTb, then use back substitution in this upper triangular matrix. There is no need to form an inverse or explicitly construct ATA. The orthogonal projection of b onto the column space is QQTb; its orthogonal residual proves that this solution minimizes ∥Ay−b∥2​.
