Apply the two-pass strongly connected component algorithm:
1. Run depth-first search over all vertices of G, recording their finish order.
2. Construct the graph transpose by reversing every edge.
3. Mark every vertex unvisited again. In decreasing first-pass finish order, start a transpose search whenever the next vertex is unvisited. The vertices newly reached by each search form one strongly connected component.
The finish order prevents a second-pass search from entering a different still-unassigned component, while mutual reachability makes it visit the entire current component. With adjacency lists, each traversal and the transposition scan takes O(∣V∣+∣E∣) time. Thus the total worst-case time is Θ(∣V∣+∣E∣), including isolated vertices.
