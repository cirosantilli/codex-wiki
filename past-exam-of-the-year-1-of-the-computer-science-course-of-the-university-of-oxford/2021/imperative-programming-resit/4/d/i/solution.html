def display: Unit = {
  print("[")
  var current = first
  var separator = ""
  while (current != null) {
    print(separator)
    print(current.datum)
    separator = ", "
    current = current.next
  }
  println("]")
}
Its precondition is a valid receiver, including an empty one. Its postcondition leaves q=q0​ and prints the entries of q0​ from left to right in the displayed bracket format, followed by a newline. The loop invariant says that the printed entries are precisely the chain before current; the not-yet-printed entries are the chain starting there. Advancing along the acyclic chain terminates after its length. Printing changes the output stream, but it changes neither links nor payloads.
