A general-purpose high-dimensional choice is Monte Carlo integration. Draw independent vectors Wr​ uniformly from the unit cube and evaluate H(Wr​)=∑i=1100​e∥Wr​−ai​∥2. The cube has volume 1, so its integral is the expected value of H(W1​). The iterative estimator is

The integrand is continuous on a compact set, hence bounded with finite variance. The strong law of large numbers gives almost sure convergence, and the square root of the mean squared error is exactly σ/N​, where σ2=Var(H(W1​)). Thus the standard stochastic accuracy rate is O(N−1/2); the dimension affects the variance and evaluation cost, not this exponent.
