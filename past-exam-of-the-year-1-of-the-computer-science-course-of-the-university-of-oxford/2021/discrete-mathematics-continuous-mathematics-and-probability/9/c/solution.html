Treat one visit to a level as a whole gambler's ruin excursion, starting at its chamber 1 and ending at local chamber 0 or 4. The room choices are fair, so the chance of reaching 4 before 0 is 1/4, and the chance of reaching 0 first is 3/4. The one-way hatches reset the next excursion to chamber 1 of the adjacent level. Thus the embedded Markov chain at level exits moves upward with probability 1/4 and downward with probability 3/4, even though individual room choices are unbiased.
Let u be the food probability from the lower starting chamber, and v that from the upper chamber 1. The lower level either exits unsuccessfully or reaches the upper level, while the upper level either finds food or returns to the lower start. First-step analysis at these level exits gives

Absorption occurs almost surely, since from each active level a fixed positive chance of terminal absorption occurs within two excursions; the original chamber excursions are also almost surely finite.
