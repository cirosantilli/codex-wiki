The minority Boolean function is one at input assignments 000,001,010,100. Put a on the rows and bc in Gray code order 00,01,11,10 on the columns. The Karnaugh map has rows 1101 and 1000. Pair the cell 000 with each of its three one-cell neighbours; the horizontal pair across the two map edges is valid because those columns are adjacent.

De Morgan laws show that the final NAND gate computes the required disjunction of the three products. This uses three two-input NAND gates and one three-input NAND gate, besides the input inverters. If only two-input NAND gates are available, replace the final three-input gate by t=NAND(n1​,n2​), u=NAND(t,t), m=NAND(u,n3​).
The three pairs are essential: the one-input assignments 001,010,100 each have only 000 as an adjacent one-cell, so no pair can be omitted from this disjunctive normal form.
