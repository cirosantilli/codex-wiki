Write the inputs as a=2a1​+a0​ and b=2b1​+b0​. Each row compares the represented value of a with all four values of b. In Gray code order 00,01,11,10 along both axes, the Karnaugh map is
a1​a0​\b1​b0​00011110​001111​010111​110010​100011​​

The equality cases lie on the diagonal and are all one. A complete row of ones at a=3 and a complete column at b=0 provide useful checks. The outlined groups are used in the next part.
