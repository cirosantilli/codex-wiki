Connect the fourth JK flip-flop with J3​=K3​=1 and clock it from Q2​, retaining the source's positive-edge convention. Read the four-bit state as Q3​Q2​Q1​Q0​. Its state-transition diagram is a single cycle
0000→1111→1110→⋯→0010→0001→0000,
(2)
with one input rising clock edge per arrow; the figure displays every state explicitly.
Each stage halves the preceding triggering frequency. Q3​ is an ideal square wave with a 50-percent duty cycle at 1GHz/16=62.5MHz, with wave period 16ns and high and low intervals of 8ns. Its initial phase depends on the starting state. The figure chooses initial state 0000 and a first input rising clock edge at 1ns. Actual ripple propagation shifts edge times and creates intermediate transient codes; no device delays were supplied, so the waveform describes settled ideal logic.
