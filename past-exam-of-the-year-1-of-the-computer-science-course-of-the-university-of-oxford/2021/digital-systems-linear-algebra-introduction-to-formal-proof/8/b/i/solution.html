 ... Past exam of the year 1 of the computer science course of the University of Oxford 2021 Digital systems, linear algebra, introduction to formal proof 8 b i
OurBigBook.com Words: 134
Choose arbitrary fresh variables y,x,z and assume H=((x=y∨y=z)∧P(y)) under label a. The conjunction elimination rule gives x=y∨y=z and P(y). Apply the disjunction elimination rule to the equality disjunction.
In the case x=y, the supplied symmetry rule gives y=x, and the equality elimination rule applied to P(y) gives P(x). Disjunction introduction rule gives P(x)∨P(z). In the case y=z, equality elimination rule applied directly to P(y) gives P(z); the other disjunction introduction rule gives the same conclusion P(x)∨P(z). Discharge the two equality case assumptions by disjunction elimination rule.
Discharge a with the implication introduction rule. There are now no undischarged assumptions. Use the universal quantifier introduction rule successively for z, then x, then y, yielding
∀y∀x∀z(((x=y∨y=z)∧P(y))→(P(x)∨P(z))).
(27)
Freshness holds at every generalization because the sole temporary assumption has already been discharged; the arbitrary variables are not tied to any premise.
