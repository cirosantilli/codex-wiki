 ... Past exam of the year 1 of the computer science course of the University of Oxford 2021 Digital systems, linear algebra, introduction to formal proof 8 b ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 128
Let u be fresh, not free in any of the three universal premises. Universal quantifier elimination rule gives ¬P(u)∨R(c,u), Q(u)→R(c,u) and P(u)∨Q(u). Apply disjunction elimination rule to the last formula.
In the P(u) branch, apply disjunction elimination rule to ¬P(u)∨R(c,u). Its ¬P(u) case contradicts P(u) by negation elimination rule, and logical falsity elimination rule then gives R(c,u). Its R(c,u) case already gives that conclusion. Discharge those two inner case assumptions to obtain R(c,u) under the outer P(u) assumption. In the outer Q(u) branch, the modus ponens rule applied to Q(u)→R(c,u) gives R(c,u) directly.
Discharge the two outer case assumptions by disjunction elimination rule. The conclusion R(c,u) now depends only on the universal premises, in which u is not free. Universal quantifier introduction rule therefore gives ∀xR(c,x). All case assumptions have been discharged, and the argument requires no classical reductio ad absurdum.
