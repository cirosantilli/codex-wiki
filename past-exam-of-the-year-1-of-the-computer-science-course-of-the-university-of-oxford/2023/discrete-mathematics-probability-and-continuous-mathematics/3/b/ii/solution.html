If p∣a, there is nothing to prove. Otherwise the only possible positive common divisors of the prime p and a show that gcd(p,a)=1. By Bézout's identity, up+va=1 for some integers u,v. Multiplying by b gives b=upb+vab. Both terms on the right are divisible by p, the second because p∣ab. Hence p∣b. This proves Euclid's lemma, including when either factor is zero or negative.
