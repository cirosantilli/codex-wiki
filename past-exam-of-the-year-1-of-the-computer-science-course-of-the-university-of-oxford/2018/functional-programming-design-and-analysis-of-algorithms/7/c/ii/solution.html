Replace breadth-first search by Dijkstra's algorithm from s, using the positive edge weights to compute D(v). The strongly connected components, the condensation graph and the reverse-topological order propagation are unchanged, because reachability ignores weights. Only the label assigned to a reachable vertex changes.
With a binary heap, the total time is O((∣V∣+∣E∣)log∣V∣), including the linear component-processing stages. More specialized priority queues give their corresponding shortest-path bounds. The proof still compares the precomputed distances from s; it must not add the weight of a path from u to the candidate, which would optimize a different quantity.
