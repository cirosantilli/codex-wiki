False. Let the directed edges be r→u, u→r, and r→v. Start depth-first search at r and explore its neighbor u before v. Then
d[r]=1,d[u]=2,f[u]=3,d[v]=4,f[v]=5,f[r]=6.
(5)
The directed path u→r→v exists and d[u]<d[v], but u and v are siblings in the discovery tree. An existing path may leave the current recursive branch via a gray ancestor and only later enter a different branch. The usual white-path condition would need all vertices of a suitable path to be white when u is discovered; that condition is absent here.
