A fixed-size array cannot implement unbounded successful insertion. Either specify a bounded double-ended queue with an extra not-full insertion precondition, or retain the unbounded interface and specify a possible full-capacity failure. Silently overwriting an endpoint would change the stated behavior. Here use the bounded contract, with a fixed positive capacity MAX and abstract state ∣s∣≤MAX. Both additions require ∣s∣<MAX; the other postconditions remain unchanged. The class declaration in part (c) is interpreted as implementing this explicitly revised contract, rather than as a behavioral subtype of the unrestricted contract in part (a).
The concrete state is an array a of length MAX, a first-position index head, and an occupied-entry count size. Its representation invariant is
MAX>0,a.length=MAX,0≤head<MAX,0≤size≤MAX.
(4)
The abstraction function is s(k)=a((head+k)modMAX) for 0≤k<size. Unoccupied slots are unconstrained. Storing size distinguishes empty and full states even though their two circular endpoint positions coincide. MAX=1 is valid. Using a circular buffer avoids shifting entries and makes every endpoint operation constant time.
