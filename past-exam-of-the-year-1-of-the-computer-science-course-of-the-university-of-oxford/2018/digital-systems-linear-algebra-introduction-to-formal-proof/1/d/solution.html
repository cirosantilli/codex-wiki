Use four-variable Karnaugh maps with rows AB=00,01,11,10 and columns Cx=00,01,11,10, both in Gray code order. A dash denotes an unused present state. The maps for the three next-state bits are

For A+, join its sole one with the dash directly below it. For B+, use the four-cell groups Ax and BCˉ, and the two-cell group BˉCx; the last group wraps between the top and bottom rows. For C+, join the one at row 01, column 00 to the dash below it; the one at row 00, column 01 is isolated. These prime implicants give
DA​=BCx,DB​=Ax+BCˉ+BˉCx,DC​=BCˉxˉ+AˉBˉCˉx.​
(4)
Here products mean Boolean conjunction and sums mean Boolean disjunction. The D inputs are the desired next-state bits of the three D flip-flops. The equations are independent of the unused values except for the explicitly chosen Karnaugh map groups.
