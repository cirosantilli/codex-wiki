States 101 and 110 enter a valid state after one edge. State 111 does so after one edge for zero, or moves to 110 and enters a valid state on the following edge for either next input. Thus every unused state recovers within at most two clock edges, independently of the incoming bits. The machine cannot become trapped there. This self-starting finite-state machine property does not guarantee meaningful recognizer output during recovery; initialization to S0​ still establishes the intended stream history.
