False, even for two equivalence relations. On A={1,2,3}, let R1​ have equivalence classes {1,2} and {3}, and let R2​ have equivalence classes {1} and {2,3}. Both binary relations are transitive. For S=R2​∘R1​, we have 3S2, using the intermediate element 3, and 2S1, using the intermediate element 1. However, 3S1 fails: the only R1​-successor of 3 is 3, and 3R2​1 is false. Thus the composite fails transitivity. The order of composition here means first R1​, then R2​.
