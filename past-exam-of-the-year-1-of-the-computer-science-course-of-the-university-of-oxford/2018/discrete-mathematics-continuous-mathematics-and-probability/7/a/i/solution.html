The probability measure P maps the sigma-algebra F of events into [0,1]. Its probability axioms are nonnegativity, normalization P(Ω)=1, and countable additivity: for every pairwise disjoint sequence of events A1​,A2​,…,
P(⋃n=1∞​An​)=∑n=1∞​P(An​).
(34)
The sample space is Ω, and F specifies which subsets are events. Requiring countable additivity, rather than only its finite version, is part of the probability space definition.
