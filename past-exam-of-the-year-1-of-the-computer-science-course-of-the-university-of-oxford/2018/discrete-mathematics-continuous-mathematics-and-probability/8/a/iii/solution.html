Let Ic​ be the indicator random variable that colour c appears at least once. Then X=IR​+IB​+IG​. For a colour of single-roll probability pc​, independence of rolls gives P(Ic​=0)=(1−pc​)n. By linearity of expectation,
EX=c∑​(1−(1−pc​)n)=3−(21​)n−(32​)n−(65​)n.​
(43)
The three indicators are not independent, but linearity of expectation does not require them to be. This is the expected number of observed categories.
