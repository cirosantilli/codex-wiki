Condition first on X. A Poisson distribution with parameter k has conditional probability generating function ek(s−1), including the degenerate zero case when k=0. The law of total expectation gives
GZ​(s)=E[eX(s−1)]=GX​(es−1)=exp(μ(es−1−1))​.
(50)
For 0≤s≤1 the inner argument is in (0,1], so this use of the generating function is justified directly. The result is a compound Poisson distribution with a Poisson distribution of unit parameter for each jump, also called a Neyman Type A distribution in this case. It is generally not an ordinary Poisson distribution.
