For n=2, take
A=(00​10​),B=I2​.
(13)
They are commuting operators because every matrix commutes with the identity matrix. However A has only the eigenvalue zero, and kerA=span{(1,0)T} has dimension one. It has no eigenbasis, so it cannot appear in any simultaneous diagonalization. Equivalently, a diagonal matrix similar to this nonzero nilpotent matrix would have to be zero, an impossibility. For any prescribed n≥2, use A=E12​ and B=In​; its zero eigenspace has dimension n−1. For n=1 no such example exists.
