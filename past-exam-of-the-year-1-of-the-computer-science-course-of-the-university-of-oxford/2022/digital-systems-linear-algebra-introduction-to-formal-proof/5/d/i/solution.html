Let fj​ be the determinant of the leading j×j principal submatrix. Expansion along the last row gives an aj​fj−1​ term and a cj−1​ term with negative cofactor sign. In the latter minor, the final column has only bj−1​ nonzero, and deleting that row and column leaves the leading (j−2)×(j−2) principal submatrix. Thus the tridiagonal determinant recurrence is
f0​=1,f1​=a1​,fj​=aj​fj−1​−cj−1​bj−1​fj−2​(j≥2).​
(21)
Here f0​=1 is the empty determinant convention, needed to include f2​=a1​a2​−b1​c1​. The derivation uses cofactor expansion, so the recurrence relation remains valid when coefficients or intermediate determinants vanish; it never divides by a pivot element.
