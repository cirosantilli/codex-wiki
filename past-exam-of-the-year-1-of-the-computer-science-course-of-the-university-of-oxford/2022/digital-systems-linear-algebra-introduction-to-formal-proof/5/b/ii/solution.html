The same entrywise computation gives (D−1AD)ii​=ai​ and (D−1AD)i,i+1​=(D−1AD)i+1,i​=bi​ci​​, with every other entry zero. Thus the result is a symmetric matrix with a tridiagonal pattern. Positivity of the diagonal entries ai​ is not needed for this positive diagonal symmetrization of a tridiagonal matrix; positivity of bi​,ci​ ensures the displayed positive, invertible D. In particular, the assertion is not an arbitrary-real-coefficient symmetrization claim when the off-diagonal signs differ.
