The Hessian criterion for convexity, in the usual twice-differentiable multivariate setting, is
g is convex on Rd⟺vTH(g)(x)v≥0 for all x,v∈Rd.​
(14)
Thus the Hessian matrix must be a positive semidefinite matrix everywhere. The criterion follows by applying the second-derivative criterion for convexity to each line restriction t↦g(x+tv), whose second derivative is vTH(g)(x+tv)v. Continuous second partials are a sufficient regularity hypothesis for this line-restriction argument.
