Minimize the square of the Euclidean distance, using minimizers under strictly increasing transformations. For s=x+y>0,
x2+y2+ln2(x+y)=2s2​+2(x−y)2​+ln2s.
(17)
For fixed s the unique minimum has x=y=s/2. Put x=y=α>0 and J(α)=2α2+ln2(2α). Its derivative is
J′(α)=α2​(2α2+ln(2α)).
