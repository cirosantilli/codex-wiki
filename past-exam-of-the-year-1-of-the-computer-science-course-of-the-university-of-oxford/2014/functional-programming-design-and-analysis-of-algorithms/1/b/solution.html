Recurse on the first argument for addition and multiplication, and on the exponent for exponentiation:
add :: Natural -> Natural -> Natural
add Zero     n = n
add (Succ m) n = Succ (add m n)

mul :: Natural -> Natural -> Natural
mul Zero     n = Zero
mul (Succ m) n = add n (mul m n)

power :: Natural -> Natural -> Natural
power m Zero     = Succ Zero
power m (Succ n) = mul m (power m n)
The defining arithmetic identities are
0+n=n,(m+1)+n=1+(m+n),0n=0,(m+1)n=n+mn,m0=1,mn+1=mmn.​
(1)
Each recursive call consumes one constructor. The base of exponentiation makes power Zero Zero equal to one, the empty-product convention. Applying convert and using structural induction verifies all three operations.
