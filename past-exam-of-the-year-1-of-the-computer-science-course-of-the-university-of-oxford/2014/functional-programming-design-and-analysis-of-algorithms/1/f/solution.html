Let T(n)=(Fn​,Fn+1​). The equations derived in part (e) become T(0)=(0,1) and T(n+1)=s(T(n)), where s(a,b)=(b,a+b). Replacing zero and successor by that seed and step is exactly a natural-number fold:
twoFibsNat :: Natural -> (Integer, Integer)
twoFibsNat = foldNat step (0, 1)
  where
    step :: (Integer, Integer) -> (Integer, Integer)
    step (a, b) = (b, a + b)

fibNat :: Natural -> Integer
fibNat = fst . twoFibsNat
Consequently
fibNat=fst∘foldNat((a,b)↦(b,a+b))(0,1).​
(3)
Structural induction follows the constructors of Natural: the seed gives the first two Fibonacci numbers, and the successor step uses the Fibonacci recurrence. The extra component carries exactly the information needed for the next update.
