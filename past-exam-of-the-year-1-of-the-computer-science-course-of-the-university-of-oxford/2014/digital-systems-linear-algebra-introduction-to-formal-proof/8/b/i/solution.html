The existential premise is ∃x(Q(x)→R(x)), with the logical implication inside the existential quantifier. Eliminate it using a fresh witness a, specialize ∀xQ(x) to that same witness, and introduce the required existential quantifier before closing the witness subproof:

The eigenvariable a is absent from the premises and from ∃xR(x). Consequently,
∃x(Q(x)→R(x)), ∀xQ(x)⊢∃xR(x).​
