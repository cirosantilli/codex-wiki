Use three D flip-flops whose stored bits A,B,C represent 4A+2B+C. Initialize them to 000. The Moore machine's output is this stored binary representation, and its state transition table is:
Present ABCNext A+B+C+
000001
001010
010011
011100
100101
101000
Treat the two unused state encodings 110,111 as don't-care conditions when minimizing the sum of products. With juxtaposition for AND, + for OR and a bar for NOT, one minimal choice is
DA​=AC+BC,DB​=BC+ABC,DC​=C.​
(18)
For DA​, the required ones at 011 and 100 cannot share one valid implicant: their differing bits would force inclusion of required zeros. Pair 011 with the unused 111 to get BC, and 100 with unused 110 to get AC. Neither term can lose a Boolean literal without covering a required zero.
For DB​, state 001 is isolated from every other required one and from the unused states by required zeros, so it forces the three-Boolean literal term ABC. State 010 pairs with unused 110 to give BC. Finally DC​=C is a single Boolean literal. Thus the three expressions are minimal sum of products expressions under the stated unused-state convention. In fact these choices also recover from 110→111→100, so initialization into an unused state eventually joins the intended modulo-six counter cycle.
