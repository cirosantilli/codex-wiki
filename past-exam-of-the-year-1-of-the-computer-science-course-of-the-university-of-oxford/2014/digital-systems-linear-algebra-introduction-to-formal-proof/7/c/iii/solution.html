The premise turns an assumed ¬P into a contradiction. Use the negation introduction rule to obtain ¬¬P, then the supplied classical double-negation elimination rule:
LineContextFormulaJustification
1{1}¬P→⊥Premise
2{1,2}¬PTemporary assumption
3{1,2}⊥implication elimination rule →-elim on 1, 2
4{1}¬¬Pnegation introduction rule ¬-intro on 3; discharge 2
5{1}Pdouble-negation elimination ¬¬-elim on 4
Hence (¬P→⊥)⊢P​. The final classical rule is essential: deriving ¬¬P alone is not yet a proof of P in Intuitionistic propositional logic.
