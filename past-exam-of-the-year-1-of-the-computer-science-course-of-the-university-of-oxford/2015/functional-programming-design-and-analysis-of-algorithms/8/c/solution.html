No. For a path P with k edges, adding C to every edge changes its cost to
w′(P)=w(P)+Ck.
(11)
Different paths can have different numbers of edges, so this transformation need not preserve their order.
For a concrete Directed acyclic graph, use edges s→t of weight 1, s→u of weight −2, and u→t of weight 2. Originally the route through u has cost 0 and beats the direct edge of cost 1. Adding C=3 makes every edge positive, but the direct route now costs 4, while the route through u costs 1+5=6. Dijkstra's algorithm on the modified graph therefore returns a path that is not shortest in the original graph.
The two-edge path receives the added constant twice; the direct path receives it once. This is the failure of uniform edge shifts to preserve shortest paths.
