Let τ be the absorption time. Its expectation is finite: from every interior state, a string of at most n successive left moves reaches 0, and the probability of such a string is at least p=3−n. Hence the Markov property gives Pk​(τ>jn)≤(1−p)j, a geometric tail bound, and Ek​[τ]<∞.
For 1≤k≤n−1, one step is always spent before absorption or the remaining walk. Apply first-step analysis and the law of total expectation to the three possible first moves. The conditional expectations of the remaining absorption time are ek+1​,ek​,ek−1​, respectively; independence of future moves gives the same law as a fresh random walk from the new position. Thus
ek​=1+31​ek+1​+31​ek​+31​ek−1​,

31​ek+1​−32​ek​+31​ek−1​=−1.​
(93)
