The equality introduction rule is reflexivity, with no premise. The equality elimination rule is substitution of equal first-order terms into a first-order formula:
Γ⊢t=t​ =I,Γ⊢ϕ(t)Γ⊢s=tΓ⊢ϕ(s)​ =E.​
(67)
The indicated substitution must be capture-avoiding substitution in first-order logic: the first-order terms must be free for the replaced variable. One can designate a particular argument occurrence by choosing ϕ(v) appropriately; the rule does not demand replacing every occurrence of an identical-looking term in a formula.
