For a binary relation R⊆A×A, reflexivity means aRa for every a∈A; a symmetric relation satisfies aRb⇒bRa for every a,b∈A; an antisymmetric relation satisfies (aRb and bRa)⇒a=b for every a,b∈A; transitivity means (aRb and bRc)⇒aRc for every a,b,c∈A; an irreflexive relation satisfies ¬(aRa) for every a∈A. In particular, failure of reflexivity means at least one missing diagonal pair, whereas irreflexivity requires every diagonal pair to be missing.
