In proof by contradiction, assume the negation of the desired conclusion and derive logical falsity; discharge that assumption and infer the desired conclusion. Its sequent rule is
Γ⊢ϕΓ,¬ϕ⊢⊥​.​
(62)
This is a rule of classical propositional logic. It follows from the paper's negation introduction rule, which first yields ¬¬ϕ, and double-negation elimination, which yields ϕ. This is stronger than merely deriving ¬ϕ by showing that ϕ leads to logical falsity.
