The premise limits every possible existential witness to the objects named by c0​,c1​; it does not assert that these constants name distinct objects. Instantiate it at a fresh witness v, then apply disjunction elimination. In the first case equality elimination rule transfers R(v) to R(c0​); in the second it transfers R(v) to R(c1​), contradicting the assumed negation. The full natural deduction derivation is
