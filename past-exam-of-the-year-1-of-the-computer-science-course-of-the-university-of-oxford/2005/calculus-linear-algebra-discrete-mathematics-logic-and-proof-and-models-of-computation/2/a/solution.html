The printed word “eigenvalues” must read “eigenvectors”: eigenvalues are real numbers, whereas a basis of Rn consists of vectors. The intended assertion is
A is diagonalizable over R⟺Rn has a basis of eigenvectors of A.​
(8)
This wording error is present in the original scan.[ref]
If X−1AX=D=diag(λ1​,…,λn​) for an invertible matrix X, write xi​ for its columns. The matrix identity AX=XD says Axi​=λi​xi​. The columns of an invertible matrix are a basis, hence they give the required basis of eigenvectors.
Conversely, put a basis of real eigenvectors x1​,…,xn​ into the columns of X, with corresponding eigenvalues λi​. The basis property makes X an invertible matrix. The equations Axi​=λi​xi​ give AX=XD, so X−1AX=D. This is precisely diagonalizability over the real numbers.
