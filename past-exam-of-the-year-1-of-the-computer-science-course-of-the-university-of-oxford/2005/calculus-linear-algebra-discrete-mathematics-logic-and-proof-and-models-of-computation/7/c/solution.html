Totality of the binary relation is expressed by
τ=∀x∀y(R(x,y)∨R(y,x)).​
(51)
When R is already an order, this adds comparability and makes it a total order. In fact this first-order sentence alone entails ρ=∀xR(x,x).
By the definition of semantic entailment, τ⊨ρ means that every first-order structure satisfying τ also satisfies ρ. Let M be any such first-order structure, and let a be any element of its domain of a first-order structure. The instance of τ with x=y=a gives R(a,a)∨R(a,a), which is true only if R(a,a) is true. Since a was arbitrary, M⊨∀xR(x,x). Therefore
τ⊨ρ.​
(52)
There is no exception for x=y in the displayed totality axiom; that case is exactly what forces the reflexive relation property.
