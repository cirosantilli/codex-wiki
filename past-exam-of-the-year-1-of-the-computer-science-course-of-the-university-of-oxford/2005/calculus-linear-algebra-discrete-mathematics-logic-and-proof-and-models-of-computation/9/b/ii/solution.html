These strings are the even-length palindromes uuR. Suppose this were a regular language, and let p be its length from the pumping lemma for regular languages. The string
w=0p110p
(60)
belongs to the formal language, with u=0p1. In any allowed decomposition w=abc, where ∣ab∣≤p and ∣b∣>0, the pumped block is b=0k with 1≤k≤p. Pumping it away gives
ac=0p−k110p.
(61)
This is not a palindrome: its initial and final runs of zeros have different lengths. Hence it cannot have the form uuR, contradicting the pumping lemma for regular languages. Therefore
{uuR:u∈{0,1}∗} is not regular.​
(62)
