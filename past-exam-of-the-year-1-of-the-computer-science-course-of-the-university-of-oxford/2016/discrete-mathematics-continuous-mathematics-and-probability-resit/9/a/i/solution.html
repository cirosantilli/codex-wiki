The cumulative distribution function is
FX​(x)=P(X≤x)​.
(81)
For an absolutely continuous probability distribution, the probability density function is a nonnegative integrable function fX​ with
FX​(x)=∫−∞x​fX​(u)du,∫−∞∞​fX​(u)du=1​.
(82)
Thus interval probabilities are integrals of fX​, and FX′​=fX​ almost everywhere, in particular at continuity points of fX​.
The word “continuous” in the source is understood in this density sense. A merely continuous cumulative distribution function need not possess a probability density function; it can instead describe a singular continuous distribution.
