The critical points satisfy both first partial derivatives equal to zero:
hx​=2x+y=0,hy​=x+3y2=0.
(51)
Substitute x=−y/2 into the second equation to get y(3y−1/2)=0. Thus the candidates are (0,0) and (−1/12,1/6).
The Hessian matrix and its determinant are
H=(21​16y​),detH=12y−1.
(52)
At (0,0) the determinant is −1, so the Hessian matrix is indefinite and the point is a saddle point. At (−1/12,1/6) the determinant is 1 and hxx​=2>0, so the Hessian matrix is positive definite and the point is a strict local minimum, with value
h(−1/12,1/6)=−4321​​.
(53)
There is no local maximum. Along x=0, h(0,y)=y3 is unbounded above and below, so there are no global extrema either.
