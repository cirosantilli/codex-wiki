We prove the stronger explicit bound n!≤nn/2n−1 by mathematical induction. The case n=1 is equality. The binomial theorem gives (1+1/n)n≥1+n/n=2, so (n+1)n≥2nn. Consequently the induction hypothesis yields
(n+1)!≤2n−1(n+1)nn​≤2n(n+1)n+1​.
(21)
Therefore n!=O(nn/2n), with constant 2 and threshold 1.
The second bound is asymptotically tighter. Using n≤2n,
2n2nn/2n​=(2n+1n​)n≤2−n⟶0.
(22)
Thus its bounding function is negligible relative to the first in little o notation; this compares the bounds without needing Stirling's formula.
