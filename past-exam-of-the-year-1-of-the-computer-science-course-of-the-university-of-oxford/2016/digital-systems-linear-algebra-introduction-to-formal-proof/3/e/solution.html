Keep both the limit and current argument in callee-saved registers, so a call to foo preserves them. Under the same no-delay-slot convention, use
li   $s0, n
li   $s1, 1
slti $t0, $s0, 1
bne  $t0, $0, testEnd
testLoop:
move $a0, $s1
jal  foo
break 1
beq  $s1, $s0, testEnd
addi $s1, $s1, 1
b    testLoop
testEnd:
break 0
b    testEnd
At every break 1, $s1 is the tested value and $v0 is its result. Resuming after the breakpoint continues to the next value; after n the program reaches its separate final stop. The initial guard handles n<1 with no calls. Testing equality before incrementing also avoids overflowing the loop variable after the final call when n is the largest positive machine integer. As with the supplied procedures, evaluating all calls assumes enough stack space and representable arithmetic results.
