Generate successive matrix powers and take cumulative sums using matrix addition:
seriesMat :: Num a => Matrix a -> [Matrix a]
seriesMat m = scanl1 addMat (iterate (multMat m) m)
For the stipulated square matrix, iterate (multMat m) m produces m, m*m, m*(m*m), and so on. Associativity of exact matrix multiplication identifies these as M,M2,M3,…. The left scan scanl1 addMat then returns
[M, M+M2, M+M2+M3,…]​.
(5)
All dimensions remain compatible. The infinite outer list is available a finite prefix at a time under lazy evaluation; no convergence of the matrix geometric series is needed. With floating-point entries, the expression specifies this particular multiplication and addition order, since machine arithmetic need not obey the exact algebraic laws.
