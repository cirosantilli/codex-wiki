The proposed proof is invalid: its existential-elimination step exports Q(x0​) while the witness name x0​ still occurs free in that conclusion. Introducing an existential quantifier later does not retrospectively repair that scope error.
Introduce the existential quantifier before discharging the witness box:
1  ∀x (P(x) → Q(x))   premise
2  ∃x P(x)            premise
3  | P(a)             assumption, a fresh
4  | P(a) → Q(a)      ∀-elim 1
5  | Q(a)             →-elim 4,3
6  | ∃x Q(x)          ∃-intro 5
7  ∃x Q(x)            ∃-elim 2,3–6
The conclusion of existential elimination now has no free occurrence of a, and the name occurs in neither original premise, satisfying the eigenvariable condition.
