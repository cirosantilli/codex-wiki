In a finite domain, an existential quantifier says that at least one candidate satisfies ϕ. Reason with a fresh name v for such an unspecified witness. If the same conclusion κ follows using only ϕ(v) and the original premises, without depending on which candidate v names, then κ follows from the existential premise itself.
This is exactly existential elimination in boxed form: the sequent Γ,ϕ(v)⊢κ is the branch inside the box. The eigenvariable v must not occur free in the original undischarged context or in the exported conclusion κ. Otherwise the proof could smuggle in information about a specially chosen individual or export a fact about a witness whose name has just left scope. Freshness does not mean that the domain contains an additional object; it means that its temporary name carries no other assumptions.
