OurBigBook.com Words: 231
Use a four-state Moore machine. In state A wait for an outer-sensor activation; B combines the outer-only and both-sensors phases; C records that the visitor has reached the inner-only phase; I emits the one-cycle count pulse. Only I has INC = 1.
The complete state transition table, with input order x1​x0​, is
state    00    01    11    10    INC
 A        A     B     B     A      0
 B        A     B     B     C      0
 C        I     B     B     C      0
 I        A     B     B     A      1
For a normal entry the states are A→B→B→C→I; retreat after the first sensor instead gives A→B→A with no pulse. Repeated samples at one sensor remain in the corresponding phase. State I always exits on the next edge, so its output lasts exactly one clock period; an outer activation on that next edge goes directly to B, allowing the next visitor to start immediately.
This follows the question's single-visitor entry model and merges the two outer-active phases as its hint requests. Inputs are sampled synchronously; the table also specifies deterministic recovery for other sampled combinations. It is not a specification of a bidirectional occupancy counter for arbitrary overlapping entry and exit traffic.
