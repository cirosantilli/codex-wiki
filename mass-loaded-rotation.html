Mass-loaded rotation (Iω˙=L˙in​−ωI˙+τext​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
When material sticks to a rotor, the incoming angular momentum flux L˙in​ and external torque τext​ obey d(Iω)/dt=L˙in​+τext​. With zero incoming axial angular momentum and zero external axial torque, Iω is constant. For deposition at fixed radius a at mass flow rate α, I˙=αa2 and the rotational kinetic energy satisfies K˙=−αa2ω2/2. This loss is the irreversible cost of bringing the incoming material up to tangential speed; it does not violate conservation of angular momentum.
