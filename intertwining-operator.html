Intertwining operator

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 137 Articles: 2
An intertwining operator between representations ρ and σ is a linear equivariant map T satisfying Tρ(g)=σ(g)T for every group element g.
