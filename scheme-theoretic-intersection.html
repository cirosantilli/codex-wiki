Scheme-theoretic intersection (Y∩X​Z=Y×X​Z)

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Fiber product of schemes
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
For two closed subschemes of a scheme X, their intersection is their fiber product of schemes over X. On an affine chart, ideals I,J give the quotient by I+J. This retains nonreduced structure and intersection multiplicities which a set-theoretic intersection would lose.
