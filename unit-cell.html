Unit cell

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering Scattering amplitude Crystal scattering Bravais lattice
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 2
A region whose translates tile a lattice's ambient space, together with the lattice points represented in it. A primitive unit cell represents one lattice point; a conventional unit cell may represent several to display symmetry.
