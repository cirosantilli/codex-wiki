Seismic lithosphere-asthenosphere boundary

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth Lithosphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
A seismically inferred transition associated with the base of the lithosphere, often identified by a decrease in shear-wave velocity. Its depth and sharpness constrain combinations of temperature, composition, hydration, melt fraction and seismic anisotropy. A sharp discontinuity inside a craton can instead lie within its thick lithospheric mantle.[ref]
