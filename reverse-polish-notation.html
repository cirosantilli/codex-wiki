Reverse Polish notation

 Home Mathematics Mathematical notation Expression notation
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Place each operator after its operands. A binary application encodes its first subtree, then its second subtree, then the operator, a postorder traversal. A stack machine evaluates the tokens in one pass, popping the right operand before the left one. Reversing a Polish notation token list also reverses operand order: prefix subtraction - a b would become b a - rather than the required a b -.
