Composition of relations (R∘S={(x,z):∃y, xRy, ySz})

OurBigBook.com Words: 184 Articles: 2
This convention composes paths in their written order; some sources instead reverse that convention. Composition of relations is associative. Taking inverse relations reverses the order: (R∘S)−1=S−1∘R−1.
