Proper fraction (0≤m<n)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Rational number Fraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A nonnegative fraction with positive denominator and numerator smaller than its denominator, so its value lies in [0,1). The zero value has the reduced fraction representation 0/1. The term is sometimes extended to negative fractions by comparing absolute values; the nonnegative convention is the one used here.
