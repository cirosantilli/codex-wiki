Barotropic spherical accretion equation ((u−cs2​/u)u′=2cs2​/r−Φ′)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Barotropic fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 2
Steady spherical continuity equation gives r2ρu=constant. Combining this with Euler equations for an inviscid fluid and cs2​=dp/dρ gives the displayed equation. Smooth transonic passage requires u2=cs2​ and Φ′=2cs2​/r simultaneously. The barotropic enthalpy function gives the integral u2/2+h+Φ=constant.
