Collar lemma (sinhwsinh(ℓ/2)=1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Hyperbolic geometry Hyperbolic surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A simple closed curve that is a geodesic of length ℓ on a hyperbolic surface has an embedded collar of half-width w with sinhwsinh(ℓ/2)=1. In circumference-one coordinates its metric is dr2+ℓ2cosh2rdt2, and its conformal modulus of an annulus is (2/ℓ)arctan(csch(ℓ/2))∼π/ℓ.
