Surgery trace (W(L))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Link Framed link Dehn surgery on a framed link
OurBigBook.com Words: 224 Articles: 5
The surgery trace W(L) is obtained from B4 by attaching one two-handle along every component of a framed link L⊂S3. Its boundary is the three-manifold produced by surgery on L.
