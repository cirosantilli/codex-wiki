Gumbel auxiliary function (a(t))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Extreme value theory Gumbel distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A positive Gumbel auxiliary function makes F(t+a(t)x)/F(t)→e−x as t approaches the right endpoint of a distribution, for every real x. Under the Von Mises conditions for extreme values, the reciprocal hazard function is a valid choice.
