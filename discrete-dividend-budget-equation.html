Discrete dividend budget equation (Ct​+St​θt+1​=(St​+dt​)θt​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Budget constraint
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
When a productive stock pays dt​ before trading at ex-dividend price St​, an investor entering with θt​ shares receives dt​θt​ and sells or buys shares to leave with θt+1​. The displayed budget constraint accounts for the available consumption and the cost of the new holding. Here θt​ is known before date t, while Ct​ and θt+1​ may depend on the information revealed at that date.
