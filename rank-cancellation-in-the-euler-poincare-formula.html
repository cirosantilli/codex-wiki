Rank cancellation in the Euler-Poincare formula (dimCi​=bi​+ri​+ri+1​)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Simplicial homology Euler characteristic Euler-Poincare formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
Use the cellular chain complex or simplicial chain complex over Q, rather than the infinite singular chain complex. Put ri​=rank∂i​, with r0​=rd+1​=0. The rank-nullity theorem gives dimker∂i​=dimCi​−ri​; quotienting the chain cycles by the chain boundaries then gives bi​=dimCi​−ri​−ri+1​. Each boundary operator's rank occurs in adjacent degrees with opposite signs, so
∑i=0d​(−1)idimCi​=∑i=0d​(−1)ibi​.
(31)
Finiteness makes this cancellation an ordinary finite sum. This is the algebra behind the cell-count formula for Euler characteristic.
