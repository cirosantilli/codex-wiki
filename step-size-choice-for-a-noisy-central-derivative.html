Step-size choice for a noisy central derivative (h∗​=(3δ/M)1/3)

OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Suppose ∣f′′′∣≤M and each sampled value of f has absolute error at most δ. For a central finite difference with h>0, the triangle inequality and the sharp central-secant derivative error give
∣d−f′∣≤6Mh2​+hδ​.
(8)
For M,δ>0, the derivative of this bound is Mh/3−δ/h2. It changes sign at h∗​=(3δ/M)1/3, proving the unique minimum, of value (3δ)2/3M1/3/2. This balance explains why reducing the step indefinitely amplifies sample absolute error even as truncation error decreases.
