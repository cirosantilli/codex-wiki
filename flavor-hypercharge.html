Flavor hypercharge (Y=B+S)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Flavor symmetry
OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 3
The additive flavor symmetry quantum number Y=B+S combines baryon number with strangeness. For the (u,d,s) quark triplet its values are (1/3,1/3,−2/3). The Gell-Mann--Nishijima formula is Q=I3​+Y/2. This is distinct from the normalization of electroweak hypercharge.
