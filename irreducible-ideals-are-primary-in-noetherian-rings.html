Irreducible ideals are primary in Noetherian rings

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Ring Ideal Irreducible ideal
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
In a Noetherian ring, an irreducible ideal is a primary ideal. In its quotient, if xy=0 and x=0, choose r after the annihilators of powers of y stabilize. Then (x)∩(yr)=0, so irreducibility forces yr=0. Combined with finite decomposition into irreducible ideals, this proves the Lasker–Noether theorem.
