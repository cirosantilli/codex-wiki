Vortex-pair renormalization flow (dK−1/dℓ=4π3y2,dy/dℓ=(2−πK)y)

In the dilute-vortex description of the two-dimensional XY model, K=ρs​/(kB​T) is dimensionless stiffness and y is phase vortex fugacity. A phase vortex has logarithmic energy πρs​log(L/a), while its placement entropy is 2kB​log(L/a). Small phase vortex pairs screen the logarithmic interaction, lowering the stiffness. Near K=2/π, the rescaled variables X=2−πK, Y=4πy obey X′=Y2, Y′=XY to leading order. The invariant X2−Y2 leads to an essential correlation-length singularity ξ∼aexp(C/T−Tc​​), not a finite power-law exponent.
Here ℓ=log(r/a) increases toward larger observation distances, and the vortex fugacity y is a dimensionless weight per unit core, conventionally initialized as y0​≃e−Ec​/(kB​T) with the microscopic measure fixed. Numerical coefficients depend on this normalization. In the displayed convention, differentiating X=2−πK using the original flow gives
X′=4π4K2y2=4(2−X)2​Y2,Y′=XY.
(3)
Only replacing the prefactor (2−X)2/4 by one near X=0 gives the scaled leading model. Its first integral follows directly:
dℓd​(X2−Y2)=2XY2−2Y(XY)=0.
(4)
Thus this first integral belongs to the stated scaled model, not exactly to the original K,y equations. For Y≥0, the critical separatrix is X=−Y. The bound side has X<−Y and flows to Y=0, X<0; the unbinding side ultimately runs to large vortex fugacity, where the dilute approximation ceases to be quantitative.
To derive the unusual correlation length scaling without fitting an exponent, write the invariant on a nearby unbinding trajectory as X2−Y2=−c2. Then X′=X2+c2 and the time to cross fixed small limits −b to b is 2arctan(b/c)/c∼π/c. If a physical one-parameter path crosses the separatrix transversely, its c2 is proportional to the positive reduced temperature. Since distance grows as eℓ, this gives ξ∼aexp(b0​/t​), with a nonuniversal b0​>0. This is the dimensionless-temperature form of the singularity above.
