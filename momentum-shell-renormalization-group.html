Momentum-shell renormalization group

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Renormalization group
OurBigBook.com Words: 377 Articles: 6
The momentum-shell renormalization group splits a field into slow modes below Λ/ζ and fast modes in Λ/ζ<∣k∣<Λ, integrates out the fast modes, then rescales momenta and fields to restore the cutoff and kinetic normalization.
