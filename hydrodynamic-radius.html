Hydrodynamic radius (Rh​)

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry) Hydrodynamic size
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 1
The hydrodynamic radius is the radius of a hypothetical no-slip sphere with the same translational diffusion coefficient as the dissolved species. In the dilute continuum regime the Stokes–Einstein relation gives
Rh​=6πηDkB​T​,
(4)
where kB​ is the Boltzmann constant, T the absolute temperature, η the dynamic viscosity of the solvent, and D the translational diffusion coefficient. This effective radius need not equal the radius of gyration or a geometric molecular radius. It is a useful descriptor for SEC molar mass calibration, rather than a complete specification of how every shape enters every pore.
