Gaussian-noise posterior for an autologistic image (pvpost​=logistic(α+βzv​+yv​−21​))

Independent unit-variance Gaussian observations with mean xv​ add yv​−1/2 to the local binary field. The posterior distribution conditional probability is logistic(α+βzv​+yv​−1/2). The neighbor interaction is unchanged, so checkerboard conditional independence and the same Gibbs schemes remain valid.
