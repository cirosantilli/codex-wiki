Flat F-term breaking with a linear superpotential (W=α+κΦ,V=∣κ∣2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supersymmetry breaking
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
With one canonical ungauged chiral superfield and κ=0, the auxiliary expectation is nonzero at every scalar value. The potential is flat and positive, so supersymmetry is broken without an isolated minimum. The fermion is a massless Goldstino and the scalar is massless at tree level. Equality of these zero masses does not imply an unbroken supersymmetric vacuum.
