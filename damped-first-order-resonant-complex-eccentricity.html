Damped first-order resonant complex eccentricity (z˙=−iC−(A+iBx)z)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Interplanetary dust Resonant trapping of dust
OurBigBook.com Words: 203 Articles: 1
For an exterior (p+1):p mean-motion resonance, use mean longitude in ϕ=(p+1)λ−pλp​−ϖ, z=eexp(iϕ), x=(a−ar​)/ar​ and B=3pnp​/2. Given e˙=−Csinϕ−Ae and ϖ˙=(C/e)cosϕ, the leading Keplerian detuning gives ϕ˙​=−Bx−(C/e)cosϕ. Differentiating z and using eiϕ(sinϕ+icosϕ)=i proves the displayed equation. It retains resonant forcing and eccentricity damping without the coordinate singularity at e=0. True longitude adds a fast orbital ripple and does not yield this autonomous averaged equation directly.
