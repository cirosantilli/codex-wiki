Triangle removal lemma (#K3​<ρN3⟹at most ηN2 edge deletions)

 ... Graph theory Probabilistic combinatorics Edge density of a bipartite graph Regular pair of vertex sets Szemerédi regularity lemma Clique removal lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For every η>0, some ρ>0 and N0​ have this property: a graph on N≥N0​ vertices with fewer than ρN3 unordered triangles in a graph can be made triangle-free by deleting at most ηN2 edges. A Szemerédi regularity lemma partition deletes exceptional, intraclass, irregular and sparse-pair edges cheaply. A surviving triangle in a graph would force a positive cubic count by the regular triangle counting lemma.
