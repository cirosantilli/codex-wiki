Homotopy sphere (Md≃Sd)

 ... Algebraic topology Cohomology Cap product Fundamental class Poincare duality Homology sphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
A homotopy sphere is a closed manifold with the homotopy type of a sphere of the same dimension. In dimensions at least two, a closed simply connected integral homology sphere is a homotopy sphere: successive applications of the Hurewicz theorem produce a map from the sphere inducing an isomorphism on integral homology, and the homological Whitehead theorem makes it a homotopy equivalence. The topological generalized Poincare theorem identifies its topological type, while prescribed smooth structures require a separate question.
