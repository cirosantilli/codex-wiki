Gauge group

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 2
The gauge group is the group of local internal symmetries. Its Lie algebra labels the gauge-boson species, while a matter field carries a representation of the group.
