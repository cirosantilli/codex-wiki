Isolated vertices in the Erdős-Rényi model

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Random graph Erdős-Rényi model
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 1
Let N be the number of isolated vertices in G(n,p). Indicator variables give
EN=n(1−p)n−1
(61)
and, because a specified pair is simultaneously isolated precisely when the 2n−3 incident edges are absent,
E(N2)=n(1−p)n−1+n(n−1)(1−p)2n−3.
(62)
