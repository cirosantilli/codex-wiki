Alternative routing

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory Stochastic network Loss network
OurBigBook.com Words: 213 Articles: 3
Alternative routing lets a call try another resource path when its preferred route is blocked. Overflow onto longer paths can create feedback and multiple solutions of the Erlang fixed point approximation, even though an exact finite irreducible occupancy Markov chain has a unique stationary distribution.
