Real linear equivalence of divisors (D∼R​E)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Cartier divisor Real Cartier divisor
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
Real linear equivalence means that D−E is a finite real combination of principal Cartier divisors. Rational linear equivalence uses rational coefficients instead. Both imply numerical equivalence of divisors.
