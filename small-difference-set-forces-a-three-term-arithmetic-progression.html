Small difference set forces a three-term arithmetic progression

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Doubling constant Freiman-Ruzsa theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
For every C there is N(C) such that a set A⊆Z with ∣A∣≥N(C) and ∣A−A∣≤C∣A∣ contains a nontrivial three-term arithmetic progression. The Freiman-Ruzsa theorem places A densely in a bounded-rank coset progression, where the Szemerédi theorem in a bounded-rank coset progression applies.
