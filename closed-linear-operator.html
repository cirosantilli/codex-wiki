Closed linear operator

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Graph of a linear operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 394 Articles: 9
A linear operator A:D(A)⊆X→Y is closed when its graph is closed in X×Y. Equivalently, if xn​→x and Axn​→y, then x∈D(A) and Ax=y.
