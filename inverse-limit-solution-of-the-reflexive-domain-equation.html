For Dn+1​=[Dn​→Dn​] with recursively lifted embedding-projection pairs, form coherent sequences xn​=pn​xn+1​, ordered componentwise. Stage projections Pn​ and embeddings En​ satisfy Pn​En​=id, En​Pn​↑id. A coherent family fn​∈[Dn​→Dn​] induces f=supn​En​fn​Pn​; conversely fn​=Pn​fEn​. These constructions are inverse. Removing the initial coordinate of the coherent sequence identifies this function-space limit with the original domain limit.
