Spin model

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 6k Articles: 80
A spin model assigns a discrete or continuous spin variable to each site of a lattice or vertex of a graph and gives interacting configurations a Boltzmann factor. Spin models describe collective order and phase transitions in magnetic and other many-body systems.
