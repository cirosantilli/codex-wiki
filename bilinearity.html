Bilinearity

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Multilinear map Bilinear map
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A map B:V×W→Z between vector spaces is bilinear when it is linear in either argument with the other held fixed. A scalar-valued bilinear form is the case V=W and Z is the scalar field. Checking bilinearity is one of the algebraic steps in verifying a real inner product; being a symmetric bilinear form and having positive squared norm impose further conditions.
