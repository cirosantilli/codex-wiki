Source of an indecomposable module

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Modular representation theory Relative projective module Vertex of an indecomposable module
OurBigBook.com Words: 223 Articles: 3
For an indecomposable module M with vertex of an indecomposable module D, a source is an indecomposable RD-module S which is a direct summand of ResDG​M and for which M is a direct summand of IndDG​S. It has vertex D as a D-module. Sources at a fixed vertex are conjugate by its normalizer.
