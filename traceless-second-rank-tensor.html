Traceless second-rank tensor (TrT=0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A second-rank Cartesian tensor is traceless when the contraction Tii​ vanishes. For a symmetric second-rank tensor, this is equivalent to its eigenvalues summing to zero. The orientational nematic order parameter is both symmetric and traceless, excluding an isotropic scalar contribution.
