Eigenvalue (λ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Linear operator theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 418 Articles: 20
A scalar λ is an eigenvalue of a linear operator A when Av=λv for some nonzero vector v.
