Surface current density (K)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Current density
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 4
A surface current density is electric current per unit transverse length confined to a surface. Integrating a thin layer's current density through its thickness gives K.
