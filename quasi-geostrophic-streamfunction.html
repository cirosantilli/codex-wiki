Quasi-geostrophic streamfunction (ψ)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Quasi-geostrophic approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
The quasi-geostrophic streamfunction defines horizontal velocity by u=−ψy​ and v=ψx​. For a one-layer free-surface model, geostrophic balance gives ψ=gη/f0​.
