Dual feasibility (ATy≤c)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Linear programming Linear programming duality
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
For minimization with constraints Ax=b, x≥0, a dual-feasible vector y obeys ATy≤c, so bTy is a lower bound on every primal-feasible objective value. For a simplex dictionary with basis matrix B, the associated vector is yT=cBT​B−1. Its basic reduced costs are zero and its nonbasic reduced costs are cNT​−cBT​B−1AN​. Thus nonnegative reduced costs are exactly dual feasibility in this minimization convention; basic primal values may still be negative, as in the dual simplex algorithm.
