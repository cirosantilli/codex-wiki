Growth rate of two-column sticky deposition (Hn​/n⟶2/3almost surely)

Suppose each new unit block independently selects one of two columns with equal probability. On the taller column it increases the maximum height; on the shorter column it sticks level with the other column, leaving the maximum unchanged. If the columns have equal height, the next block increases the maximum. Their absolute height difference Dn​ is the reset-or-increment Markov chain with p=1/2.
For the maximum height Hn​, each increment obeys Hn​−Hn−1​=1{Dn​>0}​. The stationary distribution has π0​=1/3. Applying the countable-state Markov chain ergodic theorem to the bounded indicator function of the positive states gives
nHn​​=nH0​​+n1​∑m=1n​1{Dm​>0}​⟶1−π0​=32​

almost surely, from any fixed finite starting configuration. The reward average depends only on whether a reset occurred, even though the difference chain has infinitely many states.
