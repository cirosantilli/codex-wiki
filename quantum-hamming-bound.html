Quantum Hamming bound (∑j=0t​3j(jn​)≤2n−k)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum error correction Nondegenerate quantum error-correcting code
OurBigBook.com Words: 107 Articles: 1
For unknown-location Pauli operators of weight at most t, there are N(t)=∑j=0t​3j(jn​) physically distinct errors modulo phase, including the identity. A nondegenerate quantum code assigns them mutually orthogonal error-image subspaces, each of dimension 2k, within the 2n-dimensional physical Hilbert space. Hence N(t)2k≤2n. The hypothesis of nondegeneracy is essential to this dimension count.
