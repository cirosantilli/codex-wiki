Einstein-frame metric (gab(E)​=e−4φ/(D−2)gab(s)​)

 ... Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Polyakov action String nonlinear sigma model String-frame metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
For D>2, rescale the string-frame metric by g(E)=e−4φ/(D−2)g(s). The Weyl transformation of the Ricci scalar makes the coefficient of the Einstein-frame Ricci term independent of the dilaton. Indeed g(s)=e4φ/(D−2)g(E) contributes factors e2Dφ/(D−2) from the volume, e−2φ from the original coupling and e−4φ/(D−2) from the Ricci term; their product is one. Derivative terms supply the transformed dilaton kinetic term. The two-dimensional case is excluded from this particular rescaling.
