SN 1987A neutrino burst

OurBigBook.com Words: 137
The original Kamiokande paper reported a low-energy event cluster on 23 February 1987 above surrounding background. Inverse beta decay, νˉe​+p⟶e++n, provides an electron antineutrino channel through Positron light. Neutrino-electron elastic scattering is more directional. The burst supported core-collapse supernova neutrino emission, with limited statistics and channel ambiguity.[ref]
An original counting illustration shows why timing matters. Suppose an independently measured background is a Poisson process of rate b. In a prespecified window Δt, the probability of at least two background events is
P(N≥2)=1−e−bΔt(1+bΔt).
(7)
For the synthetic choice bΔt=0.1, this is about 0.004679, obtained by subtracting the zero- and one-event probabilities of the Poisson distribution. The Homestake experiment's delayed extraction cannot supply such capture-time clustering. This calculation is not a significance estimate for SN 1987A: searching many possible windows, varying acceptance and correlated backgrounds change the inference.
