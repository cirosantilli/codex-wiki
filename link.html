Link

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 67
A link is a smooth embedding of a finite disjoint union of circles in S3, considered up to ambient isotopy. A one-component link is a knot.
