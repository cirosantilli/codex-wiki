Lamb shift

 Home Physics Atomic physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 4
A quantum electrodynamics correction separates hydrogen atom levels such as 2s1/2​ and 2p1/2​ that coincide in the simplest model.
The Lamb shift measures an energy level displacement beyond a specified Dirac equation reference. In the point-proton, infinite-nuclear-mass Coulomb potential model, 2S1/2​ and 2P1/2​ share principal quantum number n=2 and total electronic angular momentum j=1/2, hence energy, despite differing orbital angular momentum values.[ref]
The Lamb-Retherford experiment converts a small energy difference into a large detector change: prepare a long-lived quantum state, transfer population into a short-lived one, and read the surviving beam.
