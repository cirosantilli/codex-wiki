Mertens first theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Mertens' theorems
OurBigBook.com Words: 230 Articles: 4
The prime sum ∑p≤y​(logp)/p=logy+O(1) is the first Mertens theorem. It controls the mean logarithmic size in a truncated Euler-product lower bound.
