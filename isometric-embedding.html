Isometric embedding

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Isometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 55 Articles: 1
An isometric embedding f:X→Y between metric spaces is an injective function satisfying dY​(f(x),f(x′))=dX​(x,x′) for all x,x′∈X.
