Exactness of localization

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Localization of a ring Localization of a module
OurBigBook.com Words: 165 Articles: 1
For a multiplicative subset S of a commutative ring A, localization preserves short exact sequences of A-modules. Surjectivity follows by lifting a numerator. If a fraction maps to zero, some element of S annihilates its image, so multiplying its numerator by that element puts it into the original kernel. For an injective original map, the same criterion shows its localized kernel is zero. Thus S−1A is a flat module over A.
