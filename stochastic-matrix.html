Stochastic matrix (P)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 260 Articles: 2
A row-stochastic matrix has nonnegative entries and each row sums to one. For a time-homogeneous Discrete-time Markov chain, its transition matrix has entries
Pij​=P(Xn+1​=j∣Xn​=i).
(27)
If a state has zero probability at time n, this row is specified by the model's Markov kernel, rather than recovered by conditioning on that zero-probability event. Rows index departure states and columns arrival states. Writing the current probability distribution as a row vector μn​ gives
μn+1​=μn​P,μn​=μ0​Pn.
(28)
The row-sum condition ensures that matrix multiplication preserves total probability. Under a column convention the evolution uses the transpose; the convention must accompany the formula.
For the sunny/rainy transition matrix, direct matrix multiplication gives
P2=(0.720.56​0.280.44​).
(29)
In particular, (P2)SS​=0.8×0.8+0.2×0.4=0.72. The two terms sum the disjoint paths S→S→S and S→R→S. Requiring both subsequent days to be sunny has probability 0.64; requiring only the second day to be sunny permits the extra path through rain.
Generally (Pn)ij​ is the n-step transition probability. The identity Pm+n=PmPn is the discrete-time Chapman-Kolmogorov equation: sum over the intermediate state. This makes matrix powers useful for forecasting many steps without enumerating every path individually.
