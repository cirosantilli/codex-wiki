Normal operator

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Hilbert space Riesz representation theorem Adjoint operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 910 Articles: 19
An operator is normal when TT∗=T∗T. Equivalently,
∥Tx∥=∥T∗x∥
(11)
for every x; the converse follows by polarizing the quadratic form of the self-adjoint commutator T∗T−TT∗.
