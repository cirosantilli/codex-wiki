Homotopy colimit of spectra (hocolimTj​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Stable homotopy theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 1
A homotopy colimit of spectra is the derived colimit of a diagram of topological spectra. For a sequential diagram T0​→T1​→⋯, it is the cofiber of spectra of 1−s:⋁j​Tj​→⋁j​Tj​, where s uses the bonding maps into the next summands.
