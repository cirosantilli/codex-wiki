Hückel orbital charge density (ni​=∑k​fk​∣cik​∣2)

 Home Chemistry Quantum chemistry Molecular orbital theory Hückel method
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
In an orthonormal Hückel method basis, an occupied molecular orbital with occupancy fk​ contributes fk​∣cik​∣2 electrons to site i. Adding one electron to a fixed orbital changes the site population by ∣ci​∣2. A coefficient sign indicates orbital phase, not charge sign. A net atomic charge also needs a reference population and treatment of the electrons outside the model.
