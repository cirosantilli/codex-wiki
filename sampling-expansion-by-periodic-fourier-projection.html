Sampling expansion by periodic Fourier projection (f(t)=∑n∈Z​f(n)sinc(t−n))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
When the continuous Fourier transform F of an integrable function vanishes outside [−π,π], its periodic Fourier coefficients are the integer samples of f, with the sign of the index reversed. Projecting F in L2[−π,π] and then applying the Fourier inversion theorem gives the displayed sinc function reconstruction. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality controls the reconstruction error uniformly in t. Bessel's inequality bounds the shifted sinc coefficient vector, giving absolute uniform tails when the sample sequence belongs to ℓ2.
