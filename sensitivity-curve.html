Sensitivity curve (SCn​(z)=(n+1)[T(Fn+1,z​)−T(Fn​)])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Robust statistics Influence function
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Add one observation z to the empirical sample, so Fn+1,z​=n+1n​Fn​+n+11​δz​. The displayed finite difference is a finite-sample counterpart of the influence function. Its supremum magnitude measures worst-case one-observation sensitivity; unlike an infinitesimal derivative it records the actual response at contamination fraction 1/(n+1).
