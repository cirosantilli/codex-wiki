Gravitational-lensing critical curve (detA(θ)=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Gravitational lensing Strong gravitational lensing
OurBigBook.com Words: 191 Articles: 3
A critical curve is a locus in the image plane where the Jacobian matrix of the thin gravitational lens equation becomes singular. Its formal point-source lensing magnification diverges, while finite source size regularizes the measured flux. Its mapped locus in the source plane is a gravitational-lensing caustic.
