Cosmic baryon fraction (fb​=Ωb​/Ωm​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Critical density Cosmological density parameter
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 1
The cosmic baryon fraction is the ratio of mean baryonic matter density to total matter density. Assigning this fraction to a halo is a simplifying assumption; cooling, ejection and other baryonic processes can change the halo's retained gas and stellar fractions. A baryon conversion efficiency becomes a stellar-to-total-halo mass ratio only after multiplying by the applicable baryon fraction.
