Removed compartment (r=R/N)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Compartmental models (epidemiology) SIR model
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
The removed compartment consists of people permanently excluded from both acquiring and passing on infection in the SIR model. Its count is R(t) and its fraction is r(t)=R(t)/N. Removal can represent regained health with lasting immunity, or another permanent exclusion from acquiring or passing on infection; it need not mean being healthy again. Here there are no deaths. In a variant that counts deaths within R, conservation of population would concern the original cohort, rather than the number currently alive.[ref]
