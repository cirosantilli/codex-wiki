Erdős-Rado theorem for finite arities (ℶr​(λ)+⟶(λ+)λr+1​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Partition relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 331 Articles: 2
For an infinite cardinal λ and finite r≥0, put ℶ0​(λ)=λ and ℶr+1​(λ)=2ℶr​(λ). Every λ-coloring of (r+1)-element subsets of ℶr​(λ)+ has a homogeneous subset of size λ+. The case r=1 is the Erdős-Rado theorem for pairs. For the induction, the end-homogeneous routing tree yields a sequence of length ℶr−1​(λ)+ on which the color of an (r+1)-tuple depends only on its first r entries. The previous-arity theorem homogenizes those r entries. The case r=0 is the infinite pigeonhole principle at a successor cardinal.
