Ahlfors second fundamental theorem (∑j​nj​≥(q−2)S−hL)

 ... Isolated singularity Classification of isolated singularities Pole Meromorphic function Nevanlinna theory Ahlfors covering surface theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 1
For a finite simply connected covering surface over the Riemann sphere and q≥3 fixed separated Jordan domains, let nj​ count its simply connected islands over the jth domain once each, regardless of mapping degree. Let S be its average sheet number and L its spherical boundary length. The displayed inequality holds with h depending only on the target domains. For regular bordered surfaces f:X→Y, a general formulation is max{−χ(X),0}≥−Sχ(Y)−hL, where L counts only the boundary mapped into the interior of Y and χ is the modern Euler characteristic. The boundary error disappears for an actual branched covering. The covering-surface formulation is explained in www.math.purdue.edu/~eremenko/dvi/ahl.pdf ; the simply connected island estimate is also recorded in Lemma 2 of pdfs.semanticscholar.org/d247/562607e237af7c8fc6e683b77be6b94829fa.pdf .
