LQ decomposition (A=LQ,QQT=I)

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix Matrix decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
An LQ decomposition factors a real m×n matrix into a lower trapezoidal m×n matrix L and an orthogonal n×n matrix Q. Transposing a QR decomposition of AT proves existence. Equivalently, right Householder transformations clear the part of each row beyond its diagonal while preserving earlier zeros. This factorization is useful for underdetermined systems, complementing the role of QR decomposition in overdetermined systems.
