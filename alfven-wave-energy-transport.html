Alfvén wave energy transport (∂t​E+∂z​Fz​=0)

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Magnetohydrodynamic wave Alfvén wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 120
Dot the two Linear Alfvén wave equations with u⊥​ and b⊥​/μ0​, respectively, and add. The result is conservation of energy with
E=2ρ∣u⊥​∣2​+2μ0​∣b⊥​∣2​,Fz​=−μ0​B0​​u⊥​⋅b⊥​.
(14)
A single traveling Alfvén wave has equal kinetic energy and perturbation magnetic energy, and Fz​=±vA​E. This equality need not hold instantaneously at each point of a superposed standing wave.
As an original ideal-model example, stipulate B0​=1.00T, ρ=104kgm−3 and a uniform path L=0.20m. Using μ0​≃4π×10−7Hm−1, the Alfvén speed is 8.92ms−1 and the transit time L/vA​ is 22.4ms. Doubling the magnetic field halves that time. A sinusoidal velocity with peak amplitude 0.010ms−1 has magnetic peak amplitude 1.12mT and mean energy flux ⟨Fz​⟩=ρvA​U2/2=4.46Wm−2 in the positive direction. These are stipulated ideal parameters, not historical measurements or a prediction including vessel walls.
