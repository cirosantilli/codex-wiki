Hölder class (H(β,L))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space Sobolev embedding theorem Hölder space
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
A Hölder class bounds derivatives through order ⌈β⌉−1 and requires the highest derivative to be Hölder continuous with exponent β−⌈β⌉+1 and constant L.
