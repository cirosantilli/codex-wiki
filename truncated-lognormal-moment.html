Truncated lognormal moment (E[Sp1{S>K}​])

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Normal distribution Log-normal distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
If logS=m+sZ with s>0, K>0 and Z having the standard normal distribution, then for every real p,
E[Sp1{S>K}​]E[Sp1{S<K}​]​=epm+p2s2/2Φ(sm+ps2−logK​),=epm+p2s2/2Φ(slogK−m−ps2​).​
(78)
Here Φ is the standard normal distribution function. Multiplying the normal distribution density by epsZ and completing the square shifts its mean by ps and supplies the factor ep2s2/2; this is a Gaussian exponential tilt. Integrating above or below the shifted threshold proves both formulas. They apply to noninteger and negative powers, including the inverse-price moment p=−1. The lower-tail formula gives the European power put formula after a binomial theorem expansion.
