Duane-Hunt law (λmin​=hc/(eV))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic radiation Bremsstrahlung
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
For an electron accelerated through a electric potential difference V and stopped at a massive target, the largest single photon energy is approximately eV. Conservation of energy and Eγ​=hc/λ give the short-wavelength cutoff λmin​=hc/(eV). Target recoil is neglected in this limiting relation.
