Quasi-compact morphism

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A morphism of schemes is quasi-compact if the inverse image of every quasi-compact open subset is quasi-compact. It suffices to test affine open subschemes of the target. This is a relative condition: the source need not be a quasi-compact scheme when the target is not one.
