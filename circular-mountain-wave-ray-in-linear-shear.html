Circular mountain-wave ray in linear shear ((x−xt​)2+(z+H)2=(H/q0​)2)

For a stationary internal gravity wave in constant buoyancy frequency N and wind U=U0​(1+z/H), set q0​=kU0​/N<1. Its upward group velocity has ratio dx/dz=k/m=q/1−q2​, where q=q0​(1+z/H). Integrating from the origin gives a circular ray with center (H1−q02​​/q0​,−H) and radius H/q0​. The ascending ray becomes horizontal at the turning level of a stationary internal wave; its reflected branch continues downwind along the same circle.
