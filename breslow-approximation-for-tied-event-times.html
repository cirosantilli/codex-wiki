Breslow approximation for tied event times ((∑i∈Rj​​exiT​β)dj​exp(∑i∈Dj​​xiT​β)​)

When dj​ events occur at the same recorded time, the Breslow approximation to Cox partial likelihood uses the factor exp(∑i∈Dj​​xiT​β)/(∑i∈Rj​​exiT​β)dj​, where Rj​ is the risk set and Dj​ the event set. It approximates the ordering of tied events rather than treating their within-tie order as known.
