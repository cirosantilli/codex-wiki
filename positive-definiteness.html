Positive definiteness (q(v)>0(v=0))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Bilinear form Positive-definite bilinear form
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The strict positivity of a quadratic form on every nonzero vector. A real symmetric matrix has positive definiteness exactly when its associated quadratic form does, making it a positive-definite matrix. A Hermitian form is positive definite when its value on (v,v) is real and strictly positive for every nonzero vector.
