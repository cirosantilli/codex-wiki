Trace of a raising-lowering product in an irreducible sl2 module (tr(XY)=k(k+1)(k+2)/6)

 ... Semisimple Lie algebra Simple Lie algebra Special linear Lie algebra sl2 Lie algebra Classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations sl2 highest-weight lowering formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
For a highest-weight vector v of weight k in an irreducible finite-dimensional sl2 Lie algebra module, the basis v,Yv,…,Ykv diagonalizes XY, with eigenvalue (j+1)(k−j) on Yjv. Summing those eigenvalues gives tr(XY)=k(k+1)(k+2)/6. The formula XYnv=n(k−n+1)Yn−1v requires v to be a highest-weight vector, not an arbitrary vector of the module.
