Rademacher excess-risk bound for empirical risk minimization

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Statistical learning theory Rademacher complexity
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For a zero-one-valued loss class F, the excess misclassification risk of an empirical risk minimizer h over a population minimizer h∗ satisfies
R(h)−R(h∗)≤2Rn​(F)+n2log(2/δ)​​
(63)
with probability at least 1−δ. The proof combines Rademacher symmetrization with the Bounded differences inequality for the supremum of the empirical excess-loss process.
The observable version is
R(h)−R(h∗)≤2R(F(Z1:n​))+2n2log(3/δ)​​.
(64)
It follows by another application of the Bounded differences inequality to the Empirical Rademacher complexity.
