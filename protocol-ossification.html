Protocol ossification

 Home Computer science Computer network Communication protocol
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Protocol ossification occurs when deployed implementations or intermediaries make established behaviour difficult to change, for example by rejecting an unfamiliar transport format or assuming fields will never evolve. Endpoint support alone may then be insufficient for deployment over real paths.[ref]
