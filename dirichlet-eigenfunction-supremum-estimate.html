Dirichlet eigenfunction supremum estimate (∥u∥∞​≤C(n)λn/4∥u∥2​)

For n≥3 and a bounded smooth domain, a Dirichlet Laplacian eigenfunction with eigenvalue λ satisfies the displayed estimate. A signed power test for a Laplacian eigenfunction and the zero-boundary Sobolev inequality give a Moser iteration with exponents pj​=2[n/(n−2)]j. The geometric series ∑j​1/pj​=n/4 gives the exact power of λ, while ∑j​(logpj​)/pj​<∞ controls the remaining constant. The constant depends only on dimension, because the zero-boundary Sobolev inequality follows by extension by zero to the whole space.
