For background shear Ωyx and uniform field B0​x, first-order smoothing gives the magnetic shearing-wave amplitude equation
c˙=Ωcy​x+ikx​B0​v−ηK2c.

If magnetic relaxation is fast compared with shear, cy​,cz​≃ikx​B0​(vy​,vz​)/(ηK2) after free transients decay. The spatially averaged mean-field electromotive force of real waves has an extra factor 1/2.
