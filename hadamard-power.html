Hadamard power (X∘k)

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix Hadamard product
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For integer k≥1, the entrywise power has entries Xijk​ and equals the repeated Hadamard product. The zero power is the all-ones matrix, even at zero entries, because it represents the constant function one. Every nonnegative integer Hadamard power of a positive semidefinite matrix is positive semidefinite by the Schur product theorem. It is not an ordinary matrix power.
