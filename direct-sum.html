Direct sum (⊕)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space
OurBigBook.com Words: 300 Articles: 6
The direct sum U⊕W of two vector spaces consists of pairs (u,w) with componentwise operations. A vector space is an internal direct sum of subspaces U and W exactly when every vector has a unique expression u+w with u∈U and w∈W.
