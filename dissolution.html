Dissolution

 ... Chemistry Chemical bond Ionic bonding Physical properties of salts Solubility Solvation
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 2
Dissolution transfers material into a chemical solution. It can include separation from an ionic crystal and solvation, or dispersal of neutral molecules. For sucrose and sodium chloride, respectively,
C12​H22​O11​(s)⟶C12​H22​O11​(aq),NaCl(s)⟶Na+(aq)+Cl−(aq).
(12)
Here (aq) specifies water as the solvent; it does not specify a fixed number of attached water molecules. The neutral sucrose remains molecular. Dissociation of an ionic compound separates pre-existing ions in the second example; solvation stabilizes their new surroundings.[ref][ref]
Ionization instead creates ions from neutral species. Hydrogen chloride reacting with water illustrates protonation of water rather than mere rearrangement of a hydration shell:
HCl+H2​O⟶H3​O++Cl−.
(13)
The hydronium and negative ion together preserve total electric charge. Solvation can influence this chemical reaction, but an ion's solvation does not itself change its net electric charge.[ref]
