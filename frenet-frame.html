Frenet frame ((T,N,B))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Frenet-Serret formulas
OurBigBook.com Words: 134 Articles: 3
For a sufficiently smooth unit-speed curve with κ>0, the Frenet frame is the positively oriented orthonormal frame
T=γ′,N=κT′​,B=T×N.
