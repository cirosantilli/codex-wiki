Conservation of relativistic energy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Four-momentum Energy–momentum relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
In an isolated relativistic collision, total four-momentum is conserved; its time component states conservation of total relativistic energy.
This is the relativistic form of conservation of energy, expressed through the conserved four-momentum.
