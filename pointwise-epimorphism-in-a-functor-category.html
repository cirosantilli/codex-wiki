Pointwise epimorphism in a functor category

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Functor Functor category
OurBigBook.com Words: 123 Articles: 1
A natural transformation in a set-valued functor category is an epimorphism exactly when every component is a surjective function. More generally, limits and colimits in a functor category are computed pointwise whenever they exist in the codomain.
