With AD​=SD​Λϵ/(2π)D, the one-loop renormalization-group beta function is βg​=−ϵg+(n−2)AD​g2. For n>2 and ϵ>0, its two nonnegative renormalization-group fixed points are zero and g∗​=ϵ/[(n−2)AD​]. Differentiating the beta function gives the displayed thermal eigenvalues, so the nonzero point has correlation-length critical exponent ν=1/ϵ to leading order. At ϵ=0 the zero eigenvalue requires nonlinear analysis: g is marginally relevant for n>2, but the n=2 perturbative spin-wave theory has a line of fixed points and omits vortices.
