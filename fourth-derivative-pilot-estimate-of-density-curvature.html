Twice integrating by parts gives R(f′′)=∫ff(4). Estimate this by averaging a fourth-derivative kernel density estimator at the observations. Its expectation contains diagonal term K(4)(0)/(ng5) and leading smoothing bias −g2μ2​(K)R(f′′′)/2. Balancing them yields pilot scale g≍R(f′′′)−1/7n−1/7. With suitable derivatives and tails, the estimation error is bounded by Op​(g2+(ng5)−1+n−1/2+n−1g−9/2), giving an Op​(n−2/7) relative-bandwidth bound after substitution. More carefully calibrated bias cancellation can improve that bound.
