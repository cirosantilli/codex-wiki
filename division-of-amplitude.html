Division of amplitude

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Interferometer
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
In division of amplitude, a partially reflecting interface sends parts of the same incident wavefront into separate paths that are later recombined. A Michelson interferometer is an example. The resulting interference visibility depends on relative optical path length, polarization of waves, intensity balance and optical coherence.
