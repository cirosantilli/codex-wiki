Radiative-equilibrium obstruction in a rotating barotropic star (∇⋅(f∇ϕ)=f′∣∇ϕ∣2+f∇2ϕ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure Uniformly rotating stellar hydrostatic equilibrium
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
On a regular equipotential surface, f,f′,ρ and ∇2ϕ are constant, but ∣∇ϕ∣2 generally varies. Consequently a flux F=f(ϕ)∇ϕ cannot ordinarily satisfy local radiative equilibrium ∇⋅F=ρϵ at all latitudes. Special cases such as f′=0 or spherical symmetry evade this obstruction. Stellar meridional circulation supplies heat advection in the leading hydrostatic treatment.
