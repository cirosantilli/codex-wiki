Phantom map of spectra

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Stable homotopy theory Spectrum (topology) Map of topological spectra
OurBigBook.com Words: 197 Articles: 3
A phantom map of spectra is a stable map p:D→E with pu=0 for every map u:F→D from a finite spectrum. Such a map can be nonzero even though it induces zero on all represented homology theories on based spaces.
