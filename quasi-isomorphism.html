Quasi-isomorphism (Hn​(f) is an isomorphism for every n)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Chain complex Chain map
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
A chain map inducing isomorphisms on all homology groups. The same definition applies to a map of cochain complexes using cohomology groups. A quasi-isomorphism need not be an isomorphism in each degree.
