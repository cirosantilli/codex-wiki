Invariance of reunion ages under clock resynchronization (s=t+κx/c)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz factor Time dilation Twin paradox
OurBigBook.com Words: 101
Consider a change of distant-time convention s=t+κx/c, where κ is constant and ∣κ∣<1. This changes time labels and simultaneity slices while leaving the world lines and the metric tensor as geometric objects unchanged. In one spatial dimension,
dsspacetime2​=−c2ds2+2κcdsdx+(1−κ2)dx2.
(8)
Substitute dt=ds−κdx/c into the Minkowski interval to obtain the formula. For a timelike curve, the proper time calculated from this transformed metric tensor equals the old value. At shared departure and reunion events with x=0, the labels also obey s=t. Thus a distant synchronization convention cannot alter the twin paradox's reunited clock readings. It would be an error to change time labels while retaining the old diagonal metric components.
