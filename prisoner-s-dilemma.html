Prisoner's dilemma (T>R>P>S)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 427 Articles: 9
The prisoner's dilemma has cooperation and defection actions, with mutual cooperation reward R, mutual defection payoff P, unilateral temptation T and exploited-cooperator payoff S. Defection dominates in the one-shot game. The extra inequality 2R≥T+S expresses cooperative efficiency against alternating asymmetric outcomes; it is a further assumption, not implied by the basic ordering.
