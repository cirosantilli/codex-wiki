Edge boundary in a graph (∂e​A)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Analysis of Boolean functions Boolean hypercube
OurBigBook.com Words: 86 Articles: 2
The edge boundary of a vertex set A consists of graph edges with exactly one endpoint in A. In a d-regular graph,
∣∂e​A∣=d∣A∣−2e(A),
(102)
where e(A) counts edges with both endpoints in A.
