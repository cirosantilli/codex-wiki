Landé g-factor (gJ​)

 Home Physics Atomic physics Zeeman splitting
OurBigBook.com Words: 157 Articles: 1
The Landé g-factor is the dimensionless electronic magnetic splitting factor relating a level's magnetic dipole moment to its total angular momentum. In the approximation where total orbital angular momentum L and total spin S couple to J=L+S, take the orbital factor as one and the electron spin factor as gs​≃2. For J>0,
gJ​=1+(gs​−1)2J(J+1)J(J+1)+S(S+1)−L(L+1)​.
(9)
This follows by projecting L+gs​S=J+(gs​−1)S onto J and using 2S⋅J=J2+S2−L2. At S=0, it gives gJ​=1; at L=0 and J=S, it gives gJ​=gs​. A J=0 level instead has zero first-order electronic shift; the formula's zero denominator must not be used. This gJ​ is distinct from the statistical weight of an atomic level 2J+1, sometimes also written gJ​.
