Two-stage rocket with proportional fuel and body masses (v2​=ulogM+(1−k)m0​M+m0​​+ulogMM+m0​​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For zero initial velocity, body masses kM,(1−k)M and fuel masses km0​,(1−k)m0​, the rocket equation gives the displayed final second-stage speed after burning the first-stage fuel, discarding its body without impulse, and burning the second-stage fuel. It assumes M>0 and 0≤k<1. The speed exceeds the single-stage value when 0<k<1 and fuel is positive. Its limit as k→1− concerns a surviving second stage of vanishing mass; at k=1 no such stage exists.
