Reduced expression in a Coxeter group

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Coxeter group Coxeter system
OurBigBook.com Words: 185 Articles: 5
A reduced expression for w∈W is a product of the fewest possible simple generators representing w. The number of factors is its Coxeter length ℓ(w).
