Biholomorphism

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Holomorphic map
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
A biholomorphism is a bijective holomorphic map whose inverse is holomorphic. A degree-one nonconstant holomorphic map between compact connected Riemann surfaces is a biholomorphism.
