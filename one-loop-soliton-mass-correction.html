One-loop soliton mass correction (ΔMosc​=21​∑n​(Ωn​−Ωn(0)​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field-theory soliton Semiclassical soliton mass
OurBigBook.com Words: 196 Articles: 2
In a common finite-volume regularization in quantum field theory, the oscillator contribution is ΔMosc​=21​∑n​(Ωn​−Ωn(0)​), including every discrete and continuum-derived mode and treating translation as a collective coordinate. Physical frequencies are Ωn​=mωn​ when coordinates are measured in units of m−1. A mass counterterm is added only afterwards. The vacuum subtraction removes the extensive vacuum energy but can leave an ultraviolet divergence.
