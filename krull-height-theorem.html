Krull height theorem

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Krull dimension Height of an ideal Krull principal ideal theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 1
If an ideal of a Noetherian ring is generated by n elements, every prime ideal minimal over it has height at most n. The proof inducts on n, using the Krull principal ideal theorem in a quotient and prime avoidance to choose a prime chain compatible with the induction.
