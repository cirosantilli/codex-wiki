Four-momentum

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 28
A massive particle has four-momentum
Pμ=(E/c,p)=mγ(c,v),P2=m2c2.
(11)
For a massless particle, Pμ=(E/c,p) with E=c∣p∣ and P2=0.
