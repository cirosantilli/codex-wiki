Antiunitary operator (⟨Au,Av⟩=⟨u,v⟩​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Antilinear map
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
An antiunitary operator between complex Hilbert spaces is a surjective antilinear map satisfying the displayed inner product identity. It preserves norms but conjugates scalars. In a chosen orthonormal basis it is UK, with U a unitary operator and K coefficientwise complex conjugation. Conversely any such UK is antiunitary. Antilinearity by itself does not preserve norms.
