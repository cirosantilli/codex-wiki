Chordal restriction property

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Schramm–Loewner evolution Conformal invariance of SLE
OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 1
A random curve γ from 0 to infinity in the complex upper half-plane has the chordal restriction property when, for every admissible compact H-hull A avoiding zero, the conditional law of ψA​(γ) given γ∩A=∅ equals the original law of γ. Here ψA​:H∖A→H fixes zero and has derivative one at infinity.
