Hartogs theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 2
For every set X there is a least ordinal h(X) that does not inject into X.
The least ordinal supplied by the theorem is the Hartogs number.
