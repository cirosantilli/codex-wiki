Ramification in a Zp-extension

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Iwasawa theory Zp-extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
At least one finite prime ramifies in a Zp-extension; otherwise the entire tower would lie in a finite Hilbert class field. Ramification can occur only over p: local class field theory makes inertia away from p an image of a unit group with finite maximal pro-p quotient, whereas Zp​ has no nontrivial finite subgroup. Nonzero inertia is open in Zp​, so it becomes total after passing to a sufficiently high finite layer.
