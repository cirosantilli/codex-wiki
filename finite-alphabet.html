Finite alphabet (∣X∣<∞)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Alphabet
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
A finite alphabet is an alphabet whose symbols form a finite set. The binary alphabet {0,1} and a finite vocabulary of possible messages are examples.
