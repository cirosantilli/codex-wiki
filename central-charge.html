Central charge (c)

 ... Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Worldsheet Two-dimensional conformal field theory Holomorphic stress-energy tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 182 Articles: 4
The central charge is the coefficient of the fourth-order pole in the stress-tensor operator-product expansion, T(z)T(w)∼c/[2(z−w)4]+⋯. A free real boson contributes one and a free real chiral fermion contributes one half.
