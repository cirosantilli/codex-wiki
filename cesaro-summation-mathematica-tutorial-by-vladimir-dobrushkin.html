By Vladimir Dobrushkin. An authored tutorial page for the second course, Part V, hosted at Brown University. No dated publication edition is specified here.
Online text: www.cfm.brown.edu/people/dobrush/am34/Mathematica/ch5/cesaro.html
Cited passages: the Cesàro-summation definition gives both zero-based and one-based partial-sum conventions; Example 1 discusses Grandi's series; Example 2 obtains the weighted geometric-series formula. Our finite examples and images are independently computed.
