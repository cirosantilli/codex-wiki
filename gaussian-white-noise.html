Gaussian white noise

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process
OurBigBook.com Words: 387 Articles: 6
Gaussian white noise is a generalized Gaussian process with zero mean and covariance proportional to a Dirac delta function in time, and possibly in space. It is the formal time derivative of Brownian motion and has independent fluctuations on disjoint time intervals.
