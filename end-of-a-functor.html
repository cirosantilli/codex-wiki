End of a functor (∫C​T(C,C))

 ... Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Diagram (category theory) Cone over a diagram Categorical limit
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For T:Cop×C→D, an end is a universal object E with maps eC​:E→T(C,C) satisfying T(1C​,f)eC​=T(f,1D​)eD​ for every f:C→D. Every other such family factors uniquely through E. For small C in a complete category, it is the equalizer of the two maps ∏C​T(C,C)⇉∏f:C→D​T(C,D).
