Boltzmann encoder demonstration

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical learning Neural network Boltzmann machine
OurBigBook.com Words: 102
David H. Ackley, Geoffrey Hinton and Terrence J. Sejnowski tested their 1985 algorithm on a simulated 4–2–4 encoder. Two four-unit visible groups communicated through two hidden units, with no direct connections between the groups. Preparation set weights to zero and supplied corresponding one-active-unit patterns. Noisy clamping and annealed settling generated pair-coactivation readouts, compared with freely running readouts to choose weight changes. Learned hidden codes were the outcome. Equal sampling effort in clamped and free conditions supplied the comparison; noise limited weight growth. Finite settling was assumed to reach equilibrium, limiting the evidence for exact likelihood learning.[ref]
