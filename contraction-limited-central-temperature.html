Contraction-limited central temperature (Tmax​∝μi​M4/3)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Brown dwarf Hydrogen-burning minimum mass
OurBigBook.com Words: 157 Articles: 1
A fixed stellar homology profile with P=ρRT/μi​+Knr​ρ5/3 gives
Tc​=Rμi​​(AGM/R−BKnr​M2/3/R2),
(2)
where A,B>0. As a function of 1/R this is a concave quadratic; its maximum is A2G2μi​M4/3/(4BKnr​R), at R=2BKnr​M−1/3/(AG). At the maximum, the central electron degeneracy pressure and thermal pressure are equal. If this temperature is insufficient for sustained hydrogen burning, the object becomes a brown dwarf. Only the positive-temperature segment represents a physical sequence.
