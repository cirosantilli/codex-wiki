Ultraviolet pole (1/ϵUV​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Renormalization Regularization in quantum field theory Ultraviolet divergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
An ultraviolet divergence appears as a pole of a dimensionally continued Feynman integral. In d=4−2ϵ, a logarithmically divergent one-loop integral typically has a 1/ϵ term. Dimensional regularization can also produce infrared divergences with the same formal regulator, so the origin must be identified before interpreting a pole as an ultraviolet counterterm.
