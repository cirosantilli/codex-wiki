Radical of a primary ideal

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Primary ideal
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The radical I​={x:xn∈I for some n≥1} of a primary ideal is prime. In a number ring this forces a nonzero primary ideal to have only one prime in its unique factorization.
