Fivefold-twinned silver nanowire

 Home Engineering Nanotechnology Nanowire Silver nanowire
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A silver wire composed of five twinned fcc domains around its axis, commonly with a pentagonal cross section. Sidewall passivation and atom addition at ends can favour one-dimensional growth from a multiply twinned seed. Cross-sectional microscopy and diffraction are stronger evidence for this structure than the outer wire outline alone.[ref]
