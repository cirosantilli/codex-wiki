Low-dimensional Frechet embedding into linfinity (X↪ℓ∞k​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Metric embedding Bourgain embedding theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 85 Articles: 1
For every integer q≥2, every n-point metric space embeds into ℓ∞k​ with distortion at most 2q−1 and
k≤Cqn1/qlogn.
(8)
The coordinates are distance-to-subset functions. Randomly sampled subsets at q density scales separate every pair at one scale; a union bound leaves the stated number of coordinates.
