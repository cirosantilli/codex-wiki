Potential vorticity (q)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 28
Potential vorticity combines vorticity with vortex stretching and density stratification. It also applies to nonrotating stratified flow through Ertel potential vorticity. In the inviscid shallow water equations,
q=hf+ζ​,
(5)
where ζ is relative vorticity and h is layer thickness.
