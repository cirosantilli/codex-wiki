Potential vorticity (q)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Rotating fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 142 Articles: 4
Potential vorticity combines fluid rotation with stretching and stratification. For an inviscid barotropic shallow-water layer,
q=hf+ζ​,
(157)
where ζ is relative vorticity and h is layer thickness.
