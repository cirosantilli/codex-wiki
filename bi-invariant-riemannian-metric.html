Bi-invariant Riemannian metric

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Left-invariant metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 230 Articles: 4
A Riemannian metric on a Lie group is bi-invariant if both left and right translations are isometries. Its identity inner product is invariant under the Adjoint representation of a Lie group; infinitesimally,
⟨[X,Y],Z⟩+⟨Y,[X,Z]⟩=0.
(10)
This skew-adjointness is the key additional property beyond left invariance.
