Affine invariance of kurtosis (κ(aX+b)=κ(X)(a=0))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Kurtosis
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
Changing units or shifting the origin preserves kurtosis. For real constants a=0 and b, the mean of Y=aX+b is aμ+b, its variance is a2σ2, and its fourth central moment is a4E[(X−μ)4]. The factors a4 cancel in the ratio, even for negative a. Hence excess kurtosis is also unchanged. For example, 100X+7 and X have the same kurtosis, although their means and variances differ. The case a=0 produces zero variance and is excluded.
