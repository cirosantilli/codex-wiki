Adjoint reciprocity for a weighted Helmholtz operator (LTu=∇⋅[f∇(u/f)]+k2u)

For smooth positive f, let L=f−1∇⋅(f∇)+k2. Its unweighted bilinear transpose is the displayed operator. If v satisfies αv+β∂n​v=0, its transpose boundary condition is αu+β(∂n​u−u∂n​logf)=0. The resulting Green kernels satisfy GL​(r2​,r1​)=GLT​(r1​,r2​). Equivalently GLT​(r,r2​)=f(r)GL​(r,r2​)/f(r2​), exhibiting weighted acoustic Green-function reciprocity.
