Mean-square relative displacement in EXAFS (σ2=⟨[(uj​−u0​)⋅R]2⟩)

OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The bond-direction variance of relative atomic displacement. A Gaussian distance distribution damps the coherent round-trip phase by e−2k2σ2. Correlated atomic motion and static bond disorder contribute, so this is not generally a single atom's mean-square displacement about its lattice site.
