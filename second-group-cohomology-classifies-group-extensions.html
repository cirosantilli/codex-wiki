Second group cohomology classifies group extensions

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group cohomology Group extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 54 Articles: 1
Equivalence classes of group extensions of G by an abelian G-module A correspond to H2(G,A). A section s:G→E produces the extension cocycle
c(g,h)=s(g)s(h)s(gh)−1,
(425)
and changing the section changes c by a group coboundary.
