Silicon (Si, Z=14)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element
OurBigBook.com Words: 39 Articles: 1
The chemical element with atomic number 14. Oxygen burning produces silicon-group nuclei; subsequent silicon burning is a reaction network approaching an iron-group mixture.
