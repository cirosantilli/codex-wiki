Linear viscoelastic fluid

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 240 Articles: 4
A linear viscoelastic fluid has causal stress response linear in the deformation history, often expressed as σd(t)=2∫0∞​G(s)E(t−s)ds. The approximation requires small deformation and rotation over the relevant memory times. High frequency is allowed at sufficiently small amplitude. An integrable decaying relaxation modulus gives a finite zero-shear viscosity; an instantaneous solvent term may be written separately.
