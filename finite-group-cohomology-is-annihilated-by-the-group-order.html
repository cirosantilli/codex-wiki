Finite-group cohomology is annihilated by the group order

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 70 Articles: 1
If G is finite and n>0, multiplication by ∣G∣ annihilates Hn(G,M). Indeed, restriction to the trivial subgroup is zero in positive degree, while the restriction-corestriction composite is multiplication by ∣G∣.
