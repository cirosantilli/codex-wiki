Mass-anisotropy enhancement of a weak-coupling BCS gap (Δ≃2εc​e−1/(λ0​γ1/3))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Fermi liquid BCS theory BCS gap equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For a fixed-density balanced two-spin Fermi gas with masses (m,m,γm), the Fermi energy scales as γ−1/3 while the single-spin Fermi-level density of states scales as γ1/3. A constant attractive interaction g within a narrow symmetric energy window εc​≪εF​ therefore gives 1=λ0​γ1/3arsinh(εc​/Δ). Solving gives Δ=εc​/sinh[1/(λ0​γ1/3)], whose weak-coupling limit is displayed. Extrapolating to arbitrarily large anisotropy violates the assumptions that the Fermi energy dominates the window and that the pairing coupling is small.
