Simultaneous conditional entropic Ruzsa distance (dR​(X;Y∥U))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Entropic Ruzsa distance Conditional entropic Ruzsa distance
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
The simultaneous conditional distance samples one value of the conditioning variable for both arguments:
dR​(X;Y∥U)=∑u​P(U=u)dR​(X∣U=u,Y∣U=u).
(16)
It differs in general from dR​(X∣U;Y∣U), which uses two independently sampled conditioning values.
