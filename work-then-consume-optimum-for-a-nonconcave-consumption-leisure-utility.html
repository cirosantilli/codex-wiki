Work-then-consume optimum for a nonconcave consumption-leisure utility

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Control theory Optimal control
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
For wealth dynamics x˙=rx+ℓ−c and utility cα(1−ℓ)β, with 0<α,β<1 and α+β>1, the unconstrained interior control equations do not give the maximum. Put s=1−ℓ. At positive shadow price p, maximization over c leaves a function of s proportional to sβ/(1−α)−ps, up to constants. It is convex and hence maximal at s=0 or s=1. The optimal controls work with zero consumption first, then cease work and consume at c(t)=Cert/(1−α). The switch occurs where this consumption reaches α/(1−α); the full discounted budget fixes C and the switch time. The decreasing-consumption interior stationary candidate violates the Hamiltonian maximization condition.
