Radiative cooling

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Heat transfer
OurBigBook.com Words: 671 Articles: 12
A body loses internal energy by emitting thermal radiation faster than it absorbs radiation or receives other heating. In a gravitating gas this can remove pressure support and allow further contraction.
