Lp norm (∥⋅∥p​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 205 Articles: 5
For a measurable function f, its Lp norm is
∥f∥p​=(∫∣f∣p)1/p
(5)
when 1≤p<∞, while ∥f∥∞​ is its essential supremum.
