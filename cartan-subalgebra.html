Cartan subalgebra

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 59
For a complex semisimple Lie algebra, a Cartan subalgebra is a maximal abelian subalgebra consisting of semisimple elements. It is also called a maximal torus in this setting.
