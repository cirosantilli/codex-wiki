Extension of a bounded linear operator from a dense subspace

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Topological vector space Continuous linear operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 105 Articles: 1
Let D be a dense linear subspace of a normed space X, let Y be a Banach space, and let T:D→Y be linear with ∥Tx∥≤C∥x∥. Then T has a unique bounded extension T:X→Y, defined by Tx=limn​Txn​ for any sequence xn​∈D converging to x, and ∥T∥≤C.
