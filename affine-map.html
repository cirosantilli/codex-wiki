Affine map (x↦Ax+b)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Affine geometry Affine space
OurBigBook.com Words: 141 Articles: 2
A map between affine spaces whose difference map is linear. After choosing origins it is x↦Ax+b, where A is its linear part of an affine map and b its translation. This definition allows noninvertible maps; invertibility requires A invertible.
