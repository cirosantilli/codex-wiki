Inverse-clock proof of the Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem (Bu​=MTu​​,Mt​=BVt​​)

 ... Probability theory Martingale Continuous-time martingale Local martingale Continuous local martingale Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For a continuous strictly increasing unbounded bracket V, the inverse times Tu​=inf{t:Vt​>u} satisfy VTu​​=u and TVt​​=t. Optional sampling of the original martingale stopped at bounded bracket levels makes MTu​​ a continuous local martingale in FTu​​. Time-changing the square-minus-bracket martingale gives its bracket u; the Lévy characterization of Brownian motion identifies it as Brownian.
