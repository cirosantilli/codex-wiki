Darcy flow

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Darcy law
OurBigBook.com Words: 266 Articles: 4
Darcy flow is slow flow through a porous medium whose averaged flux obeys the Darcy law. The Darcy velocity is the fluid volume flux per total cross-sectional area; it differs from the velocity averaged only over pore space.
