Scaled Lie derivative defect identity (fLX​−LfX​=DX⊗df​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For a smooth function f and vector field X,
fLX​−LfX​=DX⊗df​
(14)
on every tensor field, with X⊗df interpreted as the endomorphism Y↦Y(f)X. Both sides vanish on functions; on vector fields this follows from [fX,Y]=f[X,Y]−Y(f)X. Both are contraction-compatible tensor derivations, so agreement on functions and vector fields proves equality on all tensor types. In particular LfX​ω=fLX​ω+ω(X)df.
