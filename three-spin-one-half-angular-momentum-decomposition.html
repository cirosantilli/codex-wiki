Three spin-one-half angular-momentum decomposition ((1/2)⊗3=3/2⊕1/2⊕1/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Addition of angular momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For three distinguishable spin one-half systems, the Clebsch-Gordan decomposition first combines two spins into 0⊕1, then couples the third: 0⊗1/2=1/2 and 1⊗1/2=1/2⊕3/2. Thus the tensor product has one quartet and two doublets. A Hamiltonian operator depending only on the total S2 and Sz​ acts identically on the two doublet copies. In particular H=ϵt​S2/ℏ2+ϵz​Sz​/ℏ has energy eigenvalues ϵt​S(S+1)+ϵz​M, generically once for each quartet projection and twice for each doublet projection. Equal numerical energies have their multiplicities added.
