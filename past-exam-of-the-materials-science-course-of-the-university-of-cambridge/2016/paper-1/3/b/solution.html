The exact angles inherit the lattice-parameter uncertainty. Five reflection families are kinematically possible with this longer wavelength: h2+k2+l2≤(2a/λFe​)2≃12.9. The next FCC family, 400, cannot satisfy Bragg's law.
A cylindrical Debye-Scherrer camera records paired curved lines where each diffraction cone intersects the film. Both forward and backward cones can be intercepted. The last three families are back reflections here, unlike the rings recorded on the original forward flat plate. Film position is proportional to camera angle along the equatorial circumference, rather than to Ltan2θ; the incident and transmitted beam holes must also be distinguished from reflections. A sufficiently resolved exposure may separate the characteristic doublet, but the schematic does not claim to predict line intensities.
