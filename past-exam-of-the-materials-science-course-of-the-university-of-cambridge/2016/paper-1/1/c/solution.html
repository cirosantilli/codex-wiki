At fixed temperature, a phase's Gibbs free energy satisfies dG=Vdp. For the metallic-minus-molecular difference,
(∂p∂ΔG​)T​=ΔV<0.
(6)
Increasing pressure favors the smaller-volume metallic phase. With a pressure-independent volume approximation, ΔG(p)≃ΔG(p0​)+(p−p0​)ΔV, so pressure can overcome an initially positive free-energy difference. The volume comparison explains the direction of stabilization; it does not by itself determine the transition pressure or the electronic origin of metallic bonding.
