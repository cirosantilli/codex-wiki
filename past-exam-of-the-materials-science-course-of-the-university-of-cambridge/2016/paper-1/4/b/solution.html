The horizontal axis is copper mass percentage. The two liquidus branches meet at (28%,779∘C) and connect to pure silver at 960∘C and pure copper at 1083∘C. The terminal solid-solution boundaries at the eutectic temperature are 9% Cu for α and 92% Cu for β. Label the single-phase fields L,α,β, the two liquid-plus-solid fields L+α,L+β, and the lower two-solid field α+β.
The solidus boundaries link the pure-component melting points to their terminal solubility limits; the eutectic isotherm spans the three-phase tie line. Below it, the solvus branches approach pure Ag and pure Cu at room temperature. The supplied data determine this topology and the marked points, not the exact shapes of every boundary. The drawing therefore does not invent a numerical liquidus temperature for an intermediate composition.
