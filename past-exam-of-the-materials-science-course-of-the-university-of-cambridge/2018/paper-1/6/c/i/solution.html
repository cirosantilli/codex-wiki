It rises linearly from zero, bends downward and approaches the plateau k. Its half-height occurs at s=τ−2.
For ω2≪τ−2, the dashpot contributes the larger strain and dominates the compliant response. For ω2≫τ−2, dashpot deformation during a cycle is small relative to spring strain, and the response is spring-dominated. The crossover separates these regimes rather than a discontinuous transition.
