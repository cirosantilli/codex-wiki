For high-strength steel, use
tmax,high​=π1​(Yσy,high​KIC,high​​)2,tyield,high​≃σy,high​7MPam​.​
(35)
At fixed toughness, raising yield stress reduces tmax​ quadratically but reduces the required pressure thickness only linearly. Thus a higher-strength grade can have a worse leak-before-break screening margin. The relevant screening combination is KIC2​/σy​, since simultaneous satisfaction requires
σy​KIC2​​>πY2Pr≃22.0Y2MPam.​
(36)
Use measured low-temperature toughness and appropriate joining/environmental performance; strength alone does not establish suitability. The comparison also has to remain within the thin-wall approximation.
