At two-phase equilibrium, the endpoints of a common tangent to the two molar free-energy curves give the coexisting compositions. They are not the separate curve minima. Reading the graph gives approximately xB,α​≃0.02 and xB,m​≃0.64, with a few atomic-percent graphical uncertainty.
Let fα​ be the fraction of atoms in the precipitate thermodynamic phase. Conservation of the overall B fraction gives
0.55=fα​xB,α​+(1−fα​)xB,m​,
(43)
and hence
fα​=xB,m​−xB,α​xB,m​−0.55​≃0.64−0.020.64−0.55​≃0.145≈15%.​
(44)
About85percent remains in the B-richer matrix. Smooth readings of the original curves put the result near14–15percent; the graph does not justify many significant figures. The result is an atomic or molar thermodynamic phase fraction. Its identification with a volume fraction in the next estimate requires comparable atomic volumes of the two thermodynamic phases.
