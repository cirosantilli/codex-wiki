Bond-centred inversion in diamond cubic gives one inversion centre at the midpoint of a nearest-neighbor bond:
c=(81​,81​,81​).​
(3)
Mark its projected point (1/8,1/8) and label its height z/a=1/8. Inversion about this point sends r to (1/4,1/4,1/4)−r. It exchanges the two identical FCC sublattices and therefore preserves the crystal. An individual atom at the chosen origin is not an inversion centre.
