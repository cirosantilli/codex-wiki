 ... Past exam of the materials science course of the University of Cambridge 2015 Paper 1 1 a i
OurBigBook.com Words: 125
The vertical mirrors of the diamond cubic structure have the required orientation: their normals lie in the (001) plane. In the chosen atomic-origin convention, their traces satisfy
x−y=2n​,x+y=2n​,n∈Z,​
(2)
with x,y expressed as fractions of a. These are the two orientations of vertical {110} mirrors and their translated copies. For example, the plane x=y interchanges the first two coordinates and leaves every diamond site in an equivalent site. Reflection in x+y=1/2 sends (x,y,z) to (1/2−y,1/2−x,z), which also preserves the full three-dimensional motif.
The projected traces alone do not test a mirror: the atom heights must also agree under reflection. In particular, a vertical {100} trace can appear plausible in an unlabelled projection but does not preserve all heights as a pure mirror; glide operations are different symmetry elements.
