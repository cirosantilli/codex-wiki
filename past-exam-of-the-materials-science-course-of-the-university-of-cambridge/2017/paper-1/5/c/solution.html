Let Vc​ be the carbon-fibre volume fraction of the whole composite. Then Vm​=0.4 and Vg​=0.6−Vc​. Under the same longitudinal rule of mixtures,
E=3(0.4)+75(0.6−Vc​)+220Vc​=46.2+145Vc​GPa.
(21)
Setting E=71.94GPa gives
Vc​=0.1775,Vg​=0.4225,Vm​=0.4000.​
(22)
Thus carbon occupies 17.8% of the complete composite, or about 29.6% of its total fibre volume. The resulting 60% total fibre fraction is much more plausible than the glass-only estimate. This remains an ideal stiffness calculation requiring aligned, effectively loaded fibres; strength and fatigue checks are separate.
