Use mass density ρ=ZM/(NA​a3), with M=74.55gmol−1. The rock salt structure has Z=4 formula units per conventional cell; the CsCl structure has Z=1. In pressure order the densities are approximately
2099, 2172, 2208, 2527, 2571, 2614 kgm−3​.
(21)
The increase across the transition is approximately 14.5%, much greater than the smooth compression within either branch. Comparing a3 directly without correcting the different Z would create a fictitious volume discontinuity.
