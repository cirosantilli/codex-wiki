Fourier sine series

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier series
OurBigBook.com Words: 677 Articles: 9
A Fourier sine series expands a function on an interval in the eigenfunctions sin(nπx/L) of the Dirichlet Laplacian. It is the odd Fourier-series extension across the interval endpoints.
