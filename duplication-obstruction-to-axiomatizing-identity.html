Duplication obstruction to axiomatizing identity

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic Logical equality
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
When an equality-like symbol is treated as an ordinary binary predicate, no first-order theory consisting of assertions valid for actual identity can force it to be identity. Given a genuine-equality structure on A, replace the domain by A×{0,1} and pull all relations back along the projection π. Lift functions so that π commutes with them. Induction on first-order formulas preserves satisfaction because π is surjective. Yet the two copies of each point satisfy the equality-like predicate. Thus actual identity must be part of the semantics, or the resulting equivalence classes must be quotiented out.
