Linear independence of distinct field embeddings

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Field homomorphism Field embedding
OurBigBook.com Words: 160 Articles: 2
Distinct field embeddings σ1​,…,σn​:L→Ω are linearly independent over Ω as functions on L. Thus a nontrivial linear combination ∑i​ai​σi​ cannot vanish on every element of L.
For the proof, choose a vanishing relation with the fewest nonzero coefficients and normalize one coefficient to one. If σ1​=σm​, choose y with σ1​(y)=σm​(y). Evaluating at xy and subtracting σm​(y) times the relation evaluated at x eliminates its mth term but leaves a nonzero first coefficient, contradicting minimality.
