Convergence in L1 (E∣Xn​−X∣→0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Convergence of random variables Convergence in Lp
OurBigBook.com Words: 60 Articles: 1
Convergence in L1 means that the expected value of the absolute error tends to zero. It implies convergence in probability and convergence of expected values.
