Higgs self-coupling Landau-pole bound (μL​=μ0​exp[1/(2bαH​(μ0​))])

OurBigBook.com Words: 84
In the self-coupling-dominated approximation dαH​/dlnμ2=bαH2​, b>0, the running coupling reaches a perturbative Landau pole at the displayed scale. With the conventional Higgs potential, mH2​=2λH​v2 and one can normalize αH​=λH​/(4π). A larger Higgs boson mass therefore decreases the logarithmic interval before the self-coupling becomes strong. Requiring perturbative validity up to a fixed high cutoff gives an upper Higgs-mass bound in this approximation. The full Standard Model beta function also contains gauge and Yukawa couplings; its sign is not universally positive. A perturbative pole is not a theorem excluding every nonperturbative ultraviolet completion.
