Lipschitz continuity

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 405 Articles: 10
A function f is Lipschitz continuous when there is a constant L≥0 such that
∣f(x)−f(y)∣≤L∣x−y∣
(3)
for all points in its domain.
