Square-integrable function (f∈L2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Lp space
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
A measurable function is square-integrable when the integral of its squared absolute value is finite. Its equivalence class belongs to the L2 space; on a probability space, this means a finite second moment.
