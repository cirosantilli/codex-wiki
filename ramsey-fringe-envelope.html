The exact finite-pulse Ramsey transition probability separates a slowly varying pulse response from rapid quantum interference. Define the single-zone transfer probability p and a pulse-induced quantum phase θ by
p=q2Ω2​sp2​,b=qδ​sp​,θ=atan2(b,cp​).
(60)
Since cp2​+b2=1−p, factorizing the real bracket in the original formula gives
Pg→e​=4p(1−p)cos2(2δT​+θ).
(61)
Where cp​=b=0, the probability is zero and θ need not be defined. Elsewhere this identity proves the envelope bound without a weak-drive assumption. The envelope 4p(1−p) is not the single-zone response p: two equal coherent interactions have different transfer weights. A contiguous resonant Rabi pi pulse of duration 2τ provides another useful comparison with the same total drive duration, but no dark interval. Its line is broad because it has no long flight phase.
