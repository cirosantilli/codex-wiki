Well-formed weighted projective space

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Toric geometry Toric variety Weighted projective space
OurBigBook.com Words: 188 Articles: 2
A weighted projective space with positive integer weights q0​,…,qn​ is well-formed when the greatest common divisor of the weights with any one weight omitted is one. The quotient toric lattice N=Zn+1/Z(q0​,…,qn​) is free and the images of the coordinate vectors are primitive toric rays. Its fan contains the cones on all proper subsets of these rays.
