The magnetic reluctance of a linear section of magnetic circuit is the ratio of the winding's integrated magnetic field intensity to the magnetic flux, with units AWb−1. For uniform length ℓ, cross-sectional area A, and relative magnetic permeability μr​, R=ℓ/(μ0​μr​A). Series sections carrying the same magnetic flux have additive magnetic reluctances, and NI=Φ∑i​Ri​.
