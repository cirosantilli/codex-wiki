For a locally uniform mean magnetic field, first-order smoothing solves ∂t​b−ηΔb=(B⋅∇)u. Inserting its causal diffusion propagator into the mean-field electromotive force and taking the trace of the isotropic response tensor gives the displayed relation to kinetic helicity. When diffusion during a correlation time is negligible, it reduces to α=−τH​⟨u⋅∇×u⟩/3. At small magnetic Reynolds number, a quasistatic monochromatic velocity instead gives α=−⟨u⋅∇×u⟩/(3ηk02​). The effective correlation time and diffusion weighting should not be silently discarded.
