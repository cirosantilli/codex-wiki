Finite image construction for a planar wedge (argz∈(0,π/N),zk+​=z0​e2πik/N,zk−​=z0​​e2πik/N)

For a positive integer N, a point source in a grounded planar wedge of opening π/N can be supplemented by 2N−1 images. Keep positive sources at z0​e2πik/N and negative sources at z0​​e2πik/N, k=0,…,N−1, counting the actual source as the first positive one. Reflection in either boundary pairs equal distances with opposite signs, so the logarithmic electrostatic potential vanishes on both rays. Every image lies outside the open physical wedge.
The identity ∏k​(z−z0​e2πik/N)=zN−z0N​ compresses the image sum to
Φ(z)=−2πε0​e​log​zN−z0​​NzN−z0N​​​.
(3)
Here the normalization is that of a two-dimensional point source, equivalently a charge per unit length in a translationally invariant three-dimensional problem. For N=3, a source on the bisector with unit radius has z03​=i, giving the ratio (z3−i)/(z3+i). The method of images works finitely here because successive boundary reflections generate a finite rotation group; arbitrary wedge angles need not have a finite image construction.
