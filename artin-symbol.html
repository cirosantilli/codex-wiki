Artin symbol ((pL/K​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Class field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 269 Articles: 4
For an unramified prime p in an abelian extension L/K, the Artin symbol is the unique Galois element acting on residue fields by x↦xNp.
