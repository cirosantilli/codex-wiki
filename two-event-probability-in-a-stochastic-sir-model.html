Two-event probability in a stochastic SIR model

 ... Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Compartmental models (epidemiology) SIR model Stochastic SIR model
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
With one initial infectious individual, two events require an infection as the first event. If it occurs at time s, its density is βNe−(βN+γ)s and the next total event rate is 2β(N−1)+2γ. Integrating this density times the chance of another event before a fixed horizon gives the two-event probability.
