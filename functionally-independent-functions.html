Functionally independent functions (dF1​∧⋯∧dFr​=0)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Differentiable map
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Functions whose differentials are linearly independent at the point in question. For F=(F1​,…,Fr​) this means the derivative has rank r, so F is a submersion there. The condition is open. It is distinct from global algebraic independence of functions.
