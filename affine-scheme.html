Affine scheme

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Spectrum of a commutative ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 835 Articles: 17
An affine scheme is a scheme isomorphic to SpecA for some commutative ring A. Homomorphisms A→B correspond contravariantly to morphisms SpecB→SpecA.
