Supersymmetric quantum mechanics (SUSY QM)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 30
Supersymmetric quantum mechanics factors partner Hamiltonians through first-order operators. Its odd supercharges anticommute to the Hamiltonian; on a Riemannian manifold, the Hilbert space can be realized by square-integrable differential forms with supercharges given by a differential and its adjoint.

Supersymmetric factorization and zero-mode normalizability (A=∂x​+w(x),H−​=A†A/2,H+​=AA†/2)

Words: 73
Supersymmetric quantum mechanics OurBigBook.com
The candidate zero modes are e−∫xw in the minus sector and e+∫xw in the plus sector; this follows from the positive squared-norm expressions for their energy. Their membership in the Hilbert space determines whether supersymmetry is unbroken. For w=x only the first is normalizable. For w=x2+a, a>0, neither is normalizable, while both partner potentials grow quartically and have discrete spectra. Thus their strictly positive lowest energy is paired and supersymmetry is broken, with zero Witten index.

Witten index (I(β)=Tr((−1)Fe−βH))

Words: 62
Supersymmetric quantum mechanics OurBigBook.com
With a well-defined regulated supertrace, positive-energy states of supersymmetric quantum mechanics are paired by an odd invertible charge. Their opposite fermion parities cancel, leaving I=nB0​−nF0​, independent of β. A nonzero index proves the existence of a supersymmetric vacuum; zero does not prove its absence. Discrete-spectrum or suitable infrared conditions matter, because continuum thresholds and states escaping to infinity can invalidate naive trace manipulations or deformation invariance.

Gradient superpotential Hamiltonian in supersymmetric quantum mechanics (H={q,q†})

For a real smooth function χ on Euclidean space, put Aa​=∂a​−∂a​χ and q=∑a​ψa†​Aa​. The adjoint operator for the usual L2 inner product is Aa†​=−∂a​−∂a​χ. Since [Aa​,Ab​]=0, the canonical anticommutation relations give q2=0. Expanding H={q,q†} and using [Aa​,Ab†​]=−2∂a​∂b​χ gives
H=(−Δ+∣∇χ∣2+Δχ)I−2∑a,b​(∂a​∂b​χ)ψa†​ψb​.
(1)
In the empty fermionic Fock space sector the last term vanishes; in the filled sector ψa†​ψb​=δab​. Consequently the two scalar Hamiltonian operators are H0​=−Δ+∣∇χ∣2+Δχ and Hd​=−Δ+∣∇χ∣2−Δχ. On the compatible self-adjoint operator domain the Hamiltonian operator is nonnegative because ⟨v,Hv⟩=∥qv∥2+∥q†v∥2.

For the gradient superpotential Hamiltonian in supersymmetric quantum mechanics, H0​=∑a​Aa†​Aa​. A zero-energy normalizable wavefunction therefore satisfies Aa​f=0 for every a, because its energy is ∑a​∥Aa​f∥2. Equivalently ∂a​(e−χf)=0, so on a connected configuration space f=Ceχ. It exists in the Hilbert space precisely when eχ is square-integrable. In three dimensions with radial χ, this requires 4π∫0∞​r2e2χ(r)dr<∞. The sign depends on the convention A=∂−∂χ; the filled sector instead has the possible zero mode e−χ.

Fermion-number sectors in supersymmetric quantum mechanics (Hn​=L2(Rd)⊗ΛnCd)

Words: 229 Articles: 1
Supersymmetric quantum mechanics OurBigBook.com
For d fermionic creation operators with the canonical anticommutation relations, the Hilbert space L2(Rd)⊗Λ∙Cd splits into Hn​=L2(Rd)⊗ΛnCd, 0≤n≤d. The fermion number operator N=∑a​ψa†​ψa​ has value n on Hn​; its internal multiplicity is (nd​). An odd supercharge Q=q+q†, with q raising n and q2=0, gives H={q,q†} and preserves these sectors.
On an eigenspace with E>0, Q/E​ is an odd involution pairing bosonic and fermionic states. More precisely,
v=E−1(qq†+q†q)v
(2)
gives an orthogonal decomposition of each state into the images of q and q†: their inner product vanishes because (q†)2=0. If u∈imq†, then q†u=0, q†qu=Eu and ∥qu∥2=E∥u∥2. Thus q/E​ pairs such a state in degree n with one in degree n+1, and q†/E​ is the inverse. States with zero energy are killed by both operators and may remain unpaired. The pairing applies to positive spectral subspaces as well; discreteness is not implied.

Qf(r)∣0⟩=(f′−χ′f)ψr†​∣0⟩,ψr†​=∑a​rxa​​ψa†​.
(3)
The normalized partner is E−1/2(f′−χ′f)ψr†​∣0⟩. Indeed [H,Q]=0, and ∥Qf∣0⟩∥2=E∥f∣0⟩∥2; acting with Q again returns E​ times the original state. Polarized fermionic Fock space (FP​=Λ(PH)⊗​Λ((I−P)H​))

Words: 302 Articles: 4
Fermionic Fock space OurBigBook.com
An orthogonal projection P splits a one-particle Hilbert space into empty and filled modes. Take completed exterior Fock spaces of empty particles and of holes in the filled modes, with the graded tensor product enforcing the canonical anticommutation relations between both factors. The vacuum is killed by particle annihilation in PH and by particle creation in (I−P)H. Only finite particle-hole excitations are needed for a dense basis. Normal ordering subtracts the filled-vacuum expectation from bilinear observables.

In charge q, let Ωq​ fill all integer modes below q. Its rotation energy is q(q−1)/2. The negative Heisenberg current algebra of a fermion on the circle modes create mutually commuting oscillators bn∗​=J−n​/n​ with [bn​,bm∗​]=δnm​. Their normalized occupation monomials applied to Ωq​ are orthonormal. The Jacobi triple product identifies the fermionic charge-energy generating function with ∑q​uqtq(q−1)/2∏n>0​(1−tn)−1, proving completeness in each finite-dimensional energy space. Thus the rotation Hamiltonian is Q(Q−1)/2+∑n>0​nbn∗​bn​.

A Fourier mode of the normal-ordered fermion density is a fermionic current. On the finite-energy domain its defining sum has finitely many nonzero actions on each occupation vector. The modes satisfy Jn∗​=J−n​ and [Jn​,Jm​]=nδn+m,0​I. Segal quantization criterion for fermions (u implementable⟺[P,u]∈S2​)

Words: 60
Polarized fermionic Fock space OurBigBook.com
For the irreducible representation of the canonical anticommutation relations determined by P, a unitary one-particle map u is unitarily implementable precisely when P−uPu∗ is Hilbert-Schmidt, equivalently when [P,u] is Hilbert-Schmidt. The implementer is unique up to a scalar phase. The equivalent representation criterion for two polarizations is that their projections differ by a Hilbert-Schmidt operator. This yields a continuous projective unitary representation of the restricted unitary group.

Fermionic operator (PAP=−A)

Words: 43
Fermionic Fock space OurBigBook.com
A fermionic operator is odd under fermion parity: if P=(−1)N is the parity operator, then PAP=−A. A fermionic creation operator or fermionic annihilation operator is an example. Interchanging odd insertions in a time-ordered product contributes the corresponding fermionic sign. Odd parity alone does not imply that every pair of such operators has zero anticommutator.

Fermionic bilinear charge algebra ([QA​,QB​]=Q[A,B]​,QA​=∫d3x:ψ†Aψ:)

Words: 207 Articles: 2
Canonical anticommutation relations OurBigBook.com
For constant internal matrices acting on Dirac fields, the equal-time canonical anticommutation relations give
[ψi†​ψj​,ψk†​ψl​]=δjk​ψi†​ψl​−δil​ψk†​ψj​
(4)
with the corresponding spatial delta functions for fields. The quartic terms cancel after fermion reordering. Integrating proves the displayed algebra for well-defined global Noether charges, with normal ordering and a regulator that preserves the nonanomalous internal symmetry. Orthogonal chiral projectors make the left/right mixed commutators zero. This reduces current-charge closure to a finite matrix calculation.

(5)

Triplet charged-current closure on electromagnetic charge ([T+,T−]=4Qℓ​/e)

For normalized triplets (E+,n,e)L​ and (E+,N,e)R​, let the raising matrix have ones in entries (1,2) and (2,3). Its commutator with its adjoint is diag(1,0,−1). Neutral mixing in n=cα​ν+sα​N leaves this result unchanged because cα2​+sα2​=1. Grassmann change-of-variables formula (Dη=(detS)−1Dξ)

Words: 63

For independent Grassmann variables and an invertible ordinary matrix S, the linear change η=Sξ transforms the Berezin integral measure by the inverse Jacobian determinant. Indeed, the highest-degree monomial transforms by detS, and coefficient extraction must undo that factor. For paired variables η=Sξ and ηˉ​=ξˉ​S−1 the two factors cancel. The order of odd variables and differentials fixes the overall sign and must be kept consistent.
