Stuart's tau-c (τc​=2m(C−D)/[n2(m−1)])

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Categorical variable Ordinal categorical variable
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For an r×c contingency table of two ordinal categorical variables, write m=min(r,c)>1, n for the sample size and C,D for concordant pairs and discordant pairs. The coefficient is
τc​=n2(m−1)2m(C−D)​.
(25)
It provides an ordinal association measure whose normalization accounts for the table dimensions. It generally differs from Kendall's tau-b and Goodman and Kruskal's gamma on a table with tied pairs. An association sign presupposes the chosen category ordering and is not a causal direction. The eponym is associated with Alan Stuart.[ref]
