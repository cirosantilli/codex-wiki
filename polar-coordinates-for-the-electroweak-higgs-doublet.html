Polar coordinates for the electroweak Higgs doublet (ϕ=2​v+H​U(10​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Electroweak interaction Higgs field
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
A nonzero Higgs doublet has a real radial magnitude and three real angular coordinates. For a normalized vector z=(z1​,z2​)T, the SU(2) matrix U=(z2∗​−z1∗​​z1​z2​​) satisfies U(0,1)T=z. Near the vacuum, the three broken generators can equivalently be parameterized by e−in⋅σ+in3​. A local gauge transformation removes these angular fields in unitary gauge. This coordinate choice is local about a nonzero vacuum and need not extend through zeros or topological defects.
