Small subgroups of tetrahedral symmetry (25 subgroups with ∣H∣≤6)

The full symmetry group of a regular tetrahedron is the symmetric group S4​ acting on its vertices. Its subgroups with at most six elements have eight types up to conjugation, with the following representative subgroups and counts:

A transposition is a reflection through the plane containing the two fixed vertices and the midpoint of the exchanged edge. A double transposition is a half-turn about the axis joining the midpoints of the two exchanged edges. A three-cycle is a one-third-turn about a vertex-to-opposite-face axis. A four-cycle is a quarter-turn composed with a reflection perpendicular to the opposite-edge midpoint axis; it is an improper orthogonal transformation.
The unique Klein four-group containing only double transpositions consists of the three opposite-edge half-turns and the identity element. The other three Klein four-groups consist of two perpendicular mirror reflections, their half-turn product, and the identity element. Each S3​ is a stabilizer subgroup of a vertex, including the three rotations and three mirror reflections preserving that vertex.
For completeness, a Klein four-group containing (12) must contain the commuting involution (34) or (12)(34), yielding the displayed subgroup; one containing no transpositions must contain all three double transpositions. An S3​ subgroup has a unique cyclic subgroup of cardinality three, whose unique fixed vertex must therefore be preserved by the entire S3​. These observations and the disjoint cycle decomposition exhaust the types allowed by groups of order at most six. The counts sum to 25.
