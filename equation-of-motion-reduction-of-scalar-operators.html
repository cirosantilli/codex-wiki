Equation-of-motion reduction of scalar operators (□ϕ≐−m2ϕ−λϕ3/6)

At first order in the suppression of an effective operator, a factor of the leading Euler-Lagrange field equation defines a redundant operator removable by a local field redefinition. For L0​=(∂ϕ)2/2−m2ϕ2/2−λϕ4/24, one obtains ϕ3□ϕ≐−m2ϕ4−λϕ6/6 and (□ϕ)2≐m4ϕ2+m2λϕ4/3+λ2ϕ6/36. Lower-dimensional parameter shifts must accompany this reduction when m=0; it is not an identity for untransformed off shell correlation functions.
