Burrows-Wheeler transform (BWT(S))

 Home Computer science Algorithm String-searching algorithm
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
The Burrows-Wheeler transform appends a unique smallest terminator to a string, sorts its cyclic rotations in lexicographic order, and records the last column. It is reversible because the first and last columns contain the same characters and the last-to-first mapping preserves the occurrence order of each character. The resulting letter arrangement supports both compression and backward search.[ref]
