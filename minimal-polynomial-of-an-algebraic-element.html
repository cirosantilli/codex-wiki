Minimal polynomial of an algebraic element (mα,K​(X))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Algebraic element
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The minimal polynomial of an algebraic element α over K is the unique monic polynomial of least positive degree in K[X] that vanishes at α. It is irreducible, and its degree equals [K(α):K].
