Lagrangian submanifold

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 42
A Lagrangian submanifold of a 2n-dimensional symplectic manifold is an n-dimensional submanifold L satisfying ω∣L​=0. The symplectic form identifies its normal bundle with its cotangent bundle.
