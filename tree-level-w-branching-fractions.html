Tree-level W branching fractions (Γtot​=9Γℓ​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Electroweak interaction Electroweak gauge boson W boson
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
With three massless lepton families, two accessible up-type quarks, three colors, and no Quantum chromodynamics radiative correction, a W boson has total decay width 9Γℓ​. With an identity CKM matrix, each leptonic channel has branching fraction 1/9 and each of the two allowed quark-flavour channels has 1/3. With mixing, each accessible quark channel is weighted by ∣Vij​∣2, while row unitarity preserves the total at this order if all down-type channels are accessible.
