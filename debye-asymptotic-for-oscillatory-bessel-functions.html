Debye asymptotic for oscillatory Bessel functions (Jn​(nsecα)≈[2/(πntanα)]1/2cos[n(tanα−α)−π/4])

OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For fixed 0<α<π/2, the Bessel function of the first kind has an interior nondegenerate stationary point in its cosine integral. The stationary phase method gives the displayed oscillatory leading term and an additive O(n−3/2) remainder. The approximation loses uniformity near α=0, where the stationary point reaches the endpoint and the curvature vanishes. For negative integer-order arguments, parity supplies a factor (−1)n.
