Mantle thermal budget (Cm​T˙=Hm​+Qcmb​−Qm​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth Mantle convection
OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 1
An energy balance for heat stored in the mantle. Here Cm​ is its effective heat capacity, Hm​ internally generated radioactive power, Qcmb​ power entering across the core-mantle boundary and Qm​ power leaving the mantle. The sign of the right-hand side determines warming or secular cooling in this reduced model. Different reservoirs and latent heat may require additional terms. Surface heat loss includes crustal contributions and should not be substituted for Qm​ without specifying the partition.
