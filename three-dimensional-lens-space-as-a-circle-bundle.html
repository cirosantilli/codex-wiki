The diagonal quotient L(p)=S3/(Z/p) is the unit circle bundle of a complex line bundle over S2 whose Euler class is p times a generator. Its Gysin sequence gives
Hj(L(p);Z)≅⎩⎨⎧​Z,Z/p,0,​j=0,3,j=2,otherwise,​
(49)
while Hj(L(p);Fp​)≅Fp​ for 0≤j≤3.
