Vertex-disjoint paths

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Menger theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
Graph paths are vertex-disjoint if no vertex belongs to more than one of them, including their endpoints. In contrast, internally vertex-disjoint paths between common endpoints are allowed to share exactly those endpoints. The Set version of Menger theorem uses vertex-disjointness, while the two-terminal form uses internal vertex-disjointness.
