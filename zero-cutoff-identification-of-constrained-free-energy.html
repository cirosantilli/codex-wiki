Zero-cutoff identification of constrained free energy (Fc​(M)=limΛ→0​UΛ​(M))

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Landau theory Landau free energy Constrained order-parameter free energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
In a finite box, retain the constant mode M while integrating all nonzero field modes using the Wilsonian coarse-grained statistical Hamiltonian. Below the smallest nonzero momentum, the remaining effective energy is VUΛ​(M) and its Boltzmann weight is precisely the constrained order-parameter free energy weight. Thus the displayed equality holds with consistent field-independent normalization. It is not an identity between a bare statistical Hamiltonian and an unconstrained equilibrium free energy. An analytic polynomial Landau free energy further assumes a homogeneous branch and regular effective coefficients; long-distance fluctuations near and below an upper critical dimension can invalidate that analytic approximation.
