Circular cylinder

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Surface of revolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 443 Articles: 5
A circular cylinder of radius R has principal curvatures 0 and ±1/R, according to orientation. Hence K=0 and ∣H∣=1/(2R).
This is the circular generating-curve case of a Cylinder; the curvature formulas here refer to its lateral surface.
