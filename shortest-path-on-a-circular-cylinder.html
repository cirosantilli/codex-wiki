Shortest path on a circular cylinder (d=mink∈Z​R2(ϕ1​−ϕ0​+2πk)2+(z1​−z0​)2​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 100
Unroll a circular cylinder of radius R>0 using the coordinate u=Rϕ. Its line element becomes ds2=du2+dz2, so a geodesic lifts to a straight line in the plane. An endpoint has infinitely many lifts differing by 2πRk. Minimize the ordinary Euclidean distance over those lifts to obtain the unrestricted shortest path. A fixed lift instead specifies a winding class and need not give the global shortest path.
For angular difference 0≤δ≤2π, put m=min(δ,2π−δ). Then d=R2m2+(z1​−z0​)2​. At δ=π, two different geodesics have the same shortest length. This is the source of the corner in the distance-versus-angle graph at the opposite generator of the circular cylinder.
