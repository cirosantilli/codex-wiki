Character extension (χN​=θ)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Character theory Character of a representation Restriction of a character
OurBigBook.com Words: 255 Articles: 3
An extension of θ∈Irr(N) from N◃G is a character χ of G whose restriction is exactly θ. It is necessarily irreducible, because a nontrivial invariant subspace for G would also be one for N. An ordinary extension can fail to exist even when θ is invariant under G.
