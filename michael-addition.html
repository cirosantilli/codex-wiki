Michael addition

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Organic reaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 4
A Michael addition makes a bond at the beta carbon of a conjugated electrophile through conjugate addition. An enamine can supply a carbon nucleophile; subsequent hydrolysis restores its original carbonyl group.
