Lorentz line shape (κν​=πS​(ν−ν0​)2+Δ2Δ​)

 Home Physics Atomic physics Spectral line profile
OurBigBook.com Words: 85 Articles: 1
A normalized collisional line model κν​=(S/π)Δ/[(ν−ν0​)2+Δ2], with integrated strength S and half-width Δ. The centre scales as S/Δ and far wings as SΔ/(ν−ν0​)2. In a dilute pressure-broadened gas Δ scales approximately with pressure.
