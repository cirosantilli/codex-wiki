With independent standard-normal priors on the two coefficients, the conditional AR(2) likelihood gives precision Λ=I+∑t​vt​vtT​, where vt​=(xt+1​,xt​)T, and mean Λ−1∑t​vt​xt+2​. Positive definiteness holds even for a deficient design. If Λ=(AC​CD​) and the linear term is (r,s), the conditional means are (r−Cb)/A and (s−Ca)/D, with variances 1/A and 1/D.
