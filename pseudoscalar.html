Pseudoscalar

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Parity Spatial reflection
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
A pseudoscalar is invariant under proper rotations but changes sign under a spatial reflection. In three dimensions the scalar triple product is a pseudoscalar.
