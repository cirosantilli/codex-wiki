Power object (PA=ΩA)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Elementary topos
OurBigBook.com Words: 362 Articles: 6
A power object PA represents parameterized subobjects of A: E(X,PA)≅Sub(X×A). In an elementary topos, it is ΩA, with the membership subobject supplied by evaluation at the subobject classifier.
