Electrophilic aromatic substitution

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Organic reaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 170 Articles: 9
An electrophile replaces a substituent, commonly hydrogen, on an aromatic system. Initial bond formation can make an arenium ion; loss of a proton then restores aromaticity.
