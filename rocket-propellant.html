Rocket propellant

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Momentum Rocket equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 2
Rocket propellant is the onboard material expelled to produce rocket thrust. A chemical rocket normally carries both fuel and oxidizer; both contribute to its mass and to the expelled material. In the ideal rocket equation, M(t)=Mdry​+Mpropellant​(t), with fixed rocket dry mass and decreasing remaining rocket propellant. [ref]
