Number operator

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum harmonic oscillator Creation and annihilation operators
OurBigBook.com Words: 70 Articles: 2
The number operator N=A†A satisfies
[N,A]=−A,[N,A†]=A†.
(100)
It is self-adjoint and positive semidefinite.
