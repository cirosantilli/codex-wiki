One-loop diagram classes for Compton scattering

OurBigBook.com Words: 79
At order e4, the open electron line can carry an internal-electron self-energy, a correction at either photon vertex, or a virtual photon spanning both vertices. Include the crossed external-photon order for each class. External-electron self-energies and external-photon vacuum polarization insertions appear in unamputated functions and are treated by LSZ reduction and field residues in physical amplitudes. A closed loop with three photon vertices cancels between orientations by Furry's theorem. Renormalized calculations also include the mass, field and vertex counterterms of the same perturbative order.
