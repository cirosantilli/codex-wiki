Dolbeault operator (∂ˉ)

 ... Geometry and topology Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 938 Articles: 24
The complexified cotangent bundle decomposes into (1,0) and (0,1) parts. The Dolbeault operator is the type-(0,1) component of the exterior derivative, ∂ˉ:Ωp,q→Ωp,q+1, and integrability gives ∂ˉ2=0.
Together with its (1,0) counterpart, the Dolbeault operator decomposes the exterior derivative on complex differential forms.
