Two-particle distribution function (f2​(z,z⋆​,t))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Phase-space distribution function
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
For N identical particles with symmetric, unit-normalized phase-space distribution function DN​, the number-normalized two-particle marginal is f2​(z1​,z2​)=N(N−1)∫DN​dz3​⋯dzN​. Its integral over z2​ is (N−1)f(z1​) and its full integral is N(N−1). The factorial convention counts ordered distinct pairs and absorbs the combinatorial coefficient in the first BBGKY hierarchy equation. It does not imply statistical independence of the pair.
