Volume ratio (vr(E))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Normed vector space
OurBigBook.com Words: 137 Articles: 1
The volume ratio of a d-dimensional normed vector space is vr(E)=(vol(BE​)/vol(E))1/d, where E is its John ellipsoid. It is unchanged by invertible linear changes of coordinates. In John position, polar integration gives vr(E)d=∫Sd−1​∥u∥E−d​dσ(u), with normalized spherical measure. Consequently σ{∥u∥E​≤t}≤(vr(E)t)d.
