Standard normal distribution (N(0,1))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Normal distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 324 Articles: 6
The standard normal distribution is N(0,1): the normal distribution with mean zero, variance one and standard deviation one. Its standard normal density ϕ integrates to one; its standard normal distribution function Φ records the area accumulated from −∞ to a threshold. The distinction matters: ϕ(0)=1/2π​≈0.398942, Φ(0)=1/2, and P(Z=0)=0 for a standard normal random variable Z.
For a toy measurement X∼N(100,152), leave the measurement unit unspecified. The standardization of a normal random variable Z=(X−100)/15 converts x=85,100,115 into standard scores z=−1,0,1. Using the standard normal distribution function,
P(85≤X≤115)P(100≤X≤115)P(X=100)​=Φ(1)−Φ(−1)≈0.6826894921,=Φ(1)−Φ(0)≈0.3413447461,=0.​
