Single-walled carbon nanotube

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Carbon Carbon nanotube
OurBigBook.com Words: 298 Articles: 7
A carbon nanotube comprising one rolled graphene wall. The wrapping indices determine its allowed circumferential electronic wave vectors and therefore its metallic or semiconducting band structure.
