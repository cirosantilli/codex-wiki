Geodesic quadrilateral in a hyperbolic metric space

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Quasi-isometry Hyperbolic metric space
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Every side of a geodesic quadrilateral in a δ-hyperbolic metric space lies in the closed 2δ-neighbourhood of the other three sides. Indeed, a diagonal divides the quadrilateral into two δ-thin triangles.
