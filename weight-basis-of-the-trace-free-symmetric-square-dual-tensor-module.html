Weight basis of the trace-free symmetric-square dual tensor module (ker(S2W⊗W∗→W))

Let tij​=(ei​⊗ej​+ej​⊗ei​)/2, and let fi​ be the dual basis. The equivariant contraction sends tij​⊗fk​ to δik​ej​+δjk​ei​. Its kernel has a weight basis consisting of tii​⊗fk​ for i=k, tij​⊗fk​ for three distinct indices with i<j, and tij​⊗fj​−21​tii​⊗fi​ for i=j. The three families have sizes six, three and six. This is the irreducible fifteen-dimensional highest-weight representation with highest weight 2L1​−L3​.
