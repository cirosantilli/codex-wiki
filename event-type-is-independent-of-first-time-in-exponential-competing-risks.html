Event type is independent of first time in exponential competing risks (f(t,J=A)=(λ/s)se−st)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis Competing risks Competing risks model
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
For independent exponential competing times with rates λ,γ, the observed first time has rate s=λ+γ, while death-type probability is λ/s. The joint density factors as λe−st=(λ/s)se−st. Thus the conditional observed time given type still has rate s. Independence of type and time generally fails for time-varying hazard ratios.
