Field with four elements (F4​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Finite field
OurBigBook.com Words: 344
Build this finite field by taking a quotient ring of a polynomial ring:
F4​=F2​[t]/(t2+t+1),α=[t].
(16)
The polynomial f(t)=t2+t+1 has f(0)=f(1)=1 in the prime field F2​. A polynomial of polynomial degree two is an irreducible polynomial exactly when it has no polynomial root in the coefficient field. Thus the quotient by an irreducible polynomial is a field construction applies. Its elements have unique representatives a+bα, with a,b∈F2​: precisely 0,1,α,α+1.
The defining relation is α2+α+1=0. Since this finite field has characteristic two, every element equals its additive inverse, and the relation becomes α2=α+1. Addition acts separately on the two coefficients:
(a+bα)+(c+dα)=(a+c)+(b+d)α.
(17)
All coefficient calculations use modular arithmetic modulo two. For example, (α+1)+α=1 and (α+1)+(α+1)=0. Encoding a+bα as the pair of bits (b,a) gives 0↔00, 1↔01, α↔10, and α+1↔11; addition is bitwise exclusive or. These are coordinates in the basis 1,α, so the label 10 does not make α the element 1+1.
For multiplication, expand using the distributive property and replace every α2 by α+1:
(a+bα)(c+dα)=(ac+bd)+(ad+bc+bd)α.
(18)
For example, α(α+1)=α2+α=1, while (α+1)2=α2+1=α. The following complete tables are computed from the coefficient rules. Colors identify results, rather than their numerical sizes. Every row of the addition table lists all four elements, and every nonzero row of the multiplication table does too: adding an element, or multiplying by a nonzero element, can be undone.
The nonzero multiplicative inverses are 1−1=1, α−1=α+1, and (α+1)−1=α. Consequently the equation αx=α+1 has the unique solution x=(α+1)2=α. Repeated multiplication gives α0=1, α1=α, α2=α+1, and α3=1. They cycle through every element of the multiplicative group of a finite field.
Compare the ring Z/4Z, also with four elements. Its addition satisfies 1+1=2=0, and its multiplication satisfies 22=0. Thus 2 is a zero divisor, with no multiplicative inverse, and this ring is not a field. Multiplying by 2 collapses different inputs to the same output: both 0 and 2 give 0, while both 1 and 3 give 2. In the field with four elements, multiplying by α instead rotates the three nonzero elements, preserving their distinctness.
