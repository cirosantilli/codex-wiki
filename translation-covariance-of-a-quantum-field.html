Translation covariance of a quantum field (Φ(x)=eiP⋅xΦ(0)e−iP⋅x)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Quantum field
OurBigBook.com Words: 86 Articles: 1
For a quantum field with four-momentum operator Pμ, spacetime dependence is implemented by the displayed conjugation. Taking a matrix element between momentum eigenstates gives ⟨q∣Φ(x)∣p⟩=ei(q−p)⋅x⟨q∣Φ(0)∣p⟩. Differentiating gives i∂t​Φ=[Φ,H] and −i∇Φ=[Φ,P], connecting the Heisenberg equation of motion to spatial translation covariance.
