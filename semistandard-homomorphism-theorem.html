Semistandard homomorphism theorem

 ... Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young tableau Tabloid Polytabloid Specht module
OurBigBook.com Words: 108
A filling T of shape λ and content α defines a module homomorphism ΘT​:Mλ→Mα: given a numbered tableau, sum over distinct assignments obtained by permuting T within its rows, and put each numbered entry into output row j when its assigned label is j. Restricting to Sλ gives ΘT​. For Semistandard Young tableaux T, these restrictions are linearly independent over every field. They form a basis of HomFSn​​(Sλ,Mα) if the characteristic of a field is not two, or if λ is a two-regular partition of an integer. For two-singular shapes in characteristic two, they need not span. Column antisymmetrization and triangular leading tabloids establish independence; straightening the images of a cyclic polytabloid establishes spanning under the stated hypotheses.
