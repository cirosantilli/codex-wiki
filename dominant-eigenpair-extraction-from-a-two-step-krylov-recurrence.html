Dominant eigenpair extraction from a two-step Krylov recurrence (yk+2​=syk+1​−pyk​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Power method
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
When two independent iterates span an invariant two-dimensional subspace, their recurrence gives the characteristic polynomial λ2−sλ+p. For distinct roots λ1​,λ2​, corresponding eigenvectors are yk+1​−λ2​yk​ and yk+1​−λ1​yk​. For a merely approximately dominant subspace, the same computation gives approximate eigenpairs. A single orbit cannot separate a repeated eigenvalue's entire eigenspace when its projection is only one direction.
