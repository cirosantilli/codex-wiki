Unitary connection

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle) Metric connection
OurBigBook.com Words: 806 Articles: 14
A connection on a complex vector bundle with Hermitian metric h is unitary when
dh(s,t)=h(∇s,t)+h(s,∇t).
(34)
In a unitary local frame its connection matrix is skew-Hermitian, and so is its curvature matrix.
