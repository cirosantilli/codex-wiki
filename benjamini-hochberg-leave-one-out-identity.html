Benjamini-Hochberg leave-one-out identity

Let R be the number of rejections by the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure at level α, and let Ri​ be the rejection count after replacing pi​ by zero. Then Ri​≥1 and
R∨11{i rejected}​=Ri​1{pi​≤αRi​/m}​.
(82)
If i was rejected, lowering its p-value does not change any ordered p-value beyond rank R, so Ri​=R. Conversely, if pi​≤αRi​/m, the other Ri​−1 rejected values together with pi​ satisfy the original threshold; monotonicity gives R=Ri​. For a valid true-null p-value independent of the others, conditioning on Ri​ bounds the expectation of the right side by α/m. Summing over true nulls proves false discovery rate at most m0​α/m.
