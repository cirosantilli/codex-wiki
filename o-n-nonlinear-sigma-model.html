O(N) nonlinear sigma model

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Spontaneous symmetry breaking Nonlinear sigma model
OurBigBook.com Words: 941 Articles: 16
The O(N) nonlinear sigma model has an N-component field constrained to a sphere, commonly n2=N or 1. An auxiliary Lagrange-multiplier field turns the constraint into a self-consistent mass gap in the Large-N expansion.
