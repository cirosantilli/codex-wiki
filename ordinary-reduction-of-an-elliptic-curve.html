Ordinary reduction of an elliptic curve

OurBigBook.com Words: 51
A good reduction in characteristic p is ordinary when the geometric p-torsion has p points, equivalently its formal group of an elliptic curve has height one. For a CM elliptic curve, a good prime split in its CM field is ordinary. One of the two roots of X2−ap​X+p is a p-adic unit, enabling the bounded ordinary p-adic L-function.
