Universal NOT gate (N(A)=tr(A)I−A)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Positive linear map
OurBigBook.com Words: 157 Articles: 1
The ideal universal NOT map sends every qubit Bloch vector s to −s, taking a pure state to its orthogonal pure state. It is a trace-preserving positive map but not a quantum channel: applied to one half of a Bell state, it produces an operator with a negative eigenvalue. A physical approximation can reverse all directions with factor −1/3 using the equal mixture of conjugations by the three Pauli matrices. The isotropic qubit map complete-positivity bound proves that a larger uniform reversal is impossible among such isotropic maps.
