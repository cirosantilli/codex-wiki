Polytropic atmosphere

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Polytrope
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 4
A polytropic atmosphere obeys p=Kρ1+1/m for polytropic index m. Under uniform vertical gravity and a free surface, its density and pressure are powers of depth below that surface.
