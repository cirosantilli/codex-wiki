NADP+ (NADP+)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
NADP+ is the oxidized form of nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate. It can accept electrons to form NADPH; the light-dependent reactions supply this conversion during oxygenic photosynthesis.
