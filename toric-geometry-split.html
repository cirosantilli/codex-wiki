Toric geometry

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 81
Toric geometry studies algebraic varieties containing an algebraic torus as a dense open subset, with the torus action extended to the whole variety. Rational polyhedral fans translate their geometry into lattice combinatorics.
