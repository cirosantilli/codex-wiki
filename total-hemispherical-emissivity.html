Total hemispherical emissivity (ϵ(T)=M(T)/(σT4))

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Stefan–Boltzmann law Emissivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
Total hemispherical emissivity integrates over all emitted wavelengths and the outward hemisphere. If ϵλ,Ω​ is spectral emissivity resolved by direction, and Bλ​(T) is blackbody spectral radiance from Planck's law, then
ϵ(T)=σT4∫0∞​∫Ωoutward​​ϵλ,Ω​(T)Bλ​(T)cosθdΩdλ​.
(36)
It is a weighted average, not an unweighted mean of surface properties. Even if the wavelength-resolved material response stays unchanged, the Planck's law weighting changes with temperature, so the total ratio can change. Likewise, total radiometric absorptance averaged over radiation from hotter surroundings generally differs from this emission-weighted ratio. Kirchhoff's law of thermal radiation equates matched spectral and directional quantities; it does not equate arbitrary broadband averages.
