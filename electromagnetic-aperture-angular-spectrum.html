Electromagnetic aperture angular spectrum (q=(κ,ν,η),κ=k2−ν2−η2​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Electromagnetic waveguide
OurBigBook.com Words: 107
For an aperture normal to ex​, let Fy​,Fz​ be its tangential electric field traces extended by zero outside the aperture. With the outgoing branch of κ, the Weyl plane-wave representation and the curl aperture integral give a spectrum proportional to (−(νFy​+ηFz​)/κ,Fy​,Fz​). Each wave is transverse to q. The longitudinal electric component is regenerated by oblique diffraction even when it vanishes at the input. For G=eikr/(4πr) and a unit prefactor on the stated curl integral, the spectrum has an overall factor 1/2; a doubled half-space Green kernel gives factor one. The normalization must follow the chosen Green kernel and equivalent-current convention.
