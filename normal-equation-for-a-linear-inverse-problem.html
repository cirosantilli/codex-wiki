Normal equation for a linear inverse problem (A∗Au=A∗f)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 794 Articles: 18
The normal equation for Au=f is A∗Au=A∗f. Its solutions minimize ∥Au−f∥; when they exist, the unique solution orthogonal to kerA is the Moore--Penrose solution A†f.
