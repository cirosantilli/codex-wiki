Binomial-market probability density (Ln​=(q/p)Nn​((1−q)/(1−p))n−Nn​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Binomial options pricing model Discrete-time binomial market
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
With independent up moves of physical probability p and risk-neutral probability q, the Radon-Nikodym derivative on the first n periods is the displayed likelihood ratio, where Nn​ counts up moves. Its conditional one-step multiplier has physical expectation one, so Lk​ is a positive mean-one martingale. The terminal state-price density is Ln​/Bn​ for deterministic bank-account value Bn​.
