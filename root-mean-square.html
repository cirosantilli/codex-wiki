Root mean square (xrms​=⟨x2⟩​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
The root mean square of a real function or data set is the square root of its mean square. A sinusoid of amplitude a has root mean square ∣a∣/2​. For orthogonal or uncorrelated zero-mean components, the squared root mean square of their sum is the sum of their mean squares; this follows by expanding the square and observing that all cross terms average to zero.
