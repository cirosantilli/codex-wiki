Confidence as posterior correctness probability (P(chosen answer correct∣E))

 Home Biology Animal behaviour Metacognition Subjective confidence Retrospective confidence
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
A normative Bayesian inference model of retrospective confidence. For two exhaustive alternatives with equal prior probabilities, let L=log[p(E∣H1​)/p(E∣H0​)] be the log-likelihood ratio. Bayes' theorem gives posterior odds eL. Choosing the alternative with the larger posterior yields P(correct∣E)=(1+e−∣L∣)−1. Unequal priors add prior log odds; choosing a different action changes which posterior probability is appropriate. This calculation does not establish that an observer knows the true likelihoods.
