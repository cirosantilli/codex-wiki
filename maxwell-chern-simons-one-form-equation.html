Maxwell–Chern–Simons one-form equation (d∗F+kF=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For a differential one-form A on an oriented three-dimensional spacetime, varying 21​∫(F∧∗F+kA∧F), with F=dA, gives d∗F+kF=0. With signature (−,+,+), ∗2=−1 on one- and two-forms; writing B=∗F gives dB−k∗B=0 and the identity d∗B=0. An Abelian gauge transformation changes the action by 2k​∫∂M​ΛF. Thus the bulk field equation is invariant, while invariance of the integrated action depends on boundary conditions.
