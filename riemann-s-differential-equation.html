Riemann's differential equation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Fuchsian differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 4
Riemann's differential equation is a second-order ordinary differential equation with three regular singular points, normalized to zero, one and infinity by a Möbius transformation. Its local characteristic exponents are encoded by the Papperitz symbol and constrained by the Fuchs relation.
