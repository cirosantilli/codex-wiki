Product affine connection (p1∗​∇1⊕p2∗​∇2)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle) Affine connection
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 1
Connections ∇1,∇2 on TM1​,TM2​ give a connection on
T(M1​×M2​)≅p1∗​TM1​⊕p2∗​TM2​
(18)
by taking the direct sum of their pullback connections. In product coordinates its Christoffel symbols have the two factor blocks and zero mixed blocks. On arbitrary fields, the ordinary derivatives of their coefficients are still taken in both factors. On fields lifted separately from the factors it satisfies ∇Y1​+Y2​​(X1​+X2​)=∇Y1​1​X1​+∇Y2​2​X2​.
