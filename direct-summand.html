Direct summand

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Direct sum
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
A submodule N of a module M is a direct summand if some submodule L gives M=N⊕L. Equivalently, the inclusion has a left inverse R-module homomorphism, or N is the image of an idempotent endomorphism of M.
