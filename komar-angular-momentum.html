Komar angular momentum (J=(16π)−1∫S​⋆dm)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Killing vector field Komar charge
OurBigBook.com Words: 174 Articles: 2
Komar angular momentum is the Komar charge associated with an axial Killing vector field in four-dimensional general relativity. In geometrized units its normalization is (16π)−1∫S​⋆dm, where m is the metric-dual differential one-form. With zero cosmological constant, vacuum Einstein field equations make d⋆dm=0, so homologous surrounding surfaces in the vacuum region give the same charge.
