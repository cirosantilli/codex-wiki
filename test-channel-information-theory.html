Test channel (information theory, pX∣X​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Rate-distortion theory Rate-distortion function
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
A test channel is a proposed conditional distribution of a reconstruction given the source symbol. It describes a candidate joint probability distribution for evaluating mutual information and expected distortion. It need not be a physical transmission channel, or an implementable one-symbol encoder; the Shannon rate-distortion theorem relates this optimization to long block codes.
