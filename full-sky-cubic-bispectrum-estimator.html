Full-sky cubic bispectrum estimator (f^​NL​=(6F)−1∑b1GΘ1​Θ2​Θ3​/(C1​C2​C3​))

For an isotropic, full-sky temperature map with a fixed bispectrum template b=fNL​b1, the inverse-variance-weighted cubic statistic has normalization F=61​∑(b1)2G2/(C1​C2​C3​) over ordered triples. It is unbiased under the linear template relation and has Gaussian cosmic variance 1/F. The factor 1/6 counts the six cross-triple Wick contractions. A removed monopole cancels the nine internal-pairing terms; incomplete sky coverage or anisotropic noise generally require a linear correction.
