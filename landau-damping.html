Landau damping

 Home Physics Plasma (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 348 Articles: 4
Collisionless damping of a collective plasma perturbation by phase mixing and resonant exchange with particles near its phase velocity. In linear electrostatic response, the imaginary part of the dielectric function at real frequency is proportional to the derivative of the equilibrium phase-space distribution function at resonance. A velocity plateau removes this contribution on an interval; compact velocity support also permits undamped waves whose phase velocities lie outside that support.
