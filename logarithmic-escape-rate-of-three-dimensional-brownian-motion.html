Logarithmic escape rate of three-dimensional Brownian motion (limt→∞​logtlog∣Wt​∣​=21​almost surely)

OurBigBook.com Words: 70
For W started at a point of radius one, its radial process has the law of the inverse-clock process eBJt​​+Jt​/2, where J inverts Hs​=∫0s​e2Br​+rdr. The logarithmic growth of an exponential Brownian clock with positive drift gives logHs​/s→1, whereas log(eBs​+s/2)/s→1/2. Substituting s=Jt​ proves the displayed almost sure escape rate and divergence of the radius. Independently, Brownian scaling gives the same rate in probability; if an almost sure logarithmic rate is already known to exist, that identifies its value.
