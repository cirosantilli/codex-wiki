Trace pairing

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Field trace
OurBigBook.com Words: 307 Articles: 6
For a finite field extension L/K, the trace pairing is the bilinear form
(x,y)⟼TrL/K​(xy).
(51)
It is nondegenerate exactly when L/K is separable.
