Weierstrass functions

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Elliptic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 23
Associated with a period lattice are the Weierstrass sigma, zeta, eta and elliptic functions. They satisfy ζ=σ′/σ and ℘=−ζ′; the eta constants record quasi-period increments of the Weierstrass zeta function.
