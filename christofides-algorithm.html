Christofides algorithm (ALG≤23​OPT)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Travelling salesman problem Metric travelling salesman problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
Compute a minimum spanning tree, then a minimum-weight perfect matching on its odd-degree vertices. Their multiset union is connected with even degrees, so it has an Euler circuit. Shortcut repeated vertices to obtain a metric tour. The tree costs at most the optimal tour, and shortcutting the optimal tour to its odd-degree subset gives a cyclic order whose alternating matchings show that the added matching costs at most half the optimum. The result is a polynomial-time 3/2-approximation algorithm for symmetric metric instances.
