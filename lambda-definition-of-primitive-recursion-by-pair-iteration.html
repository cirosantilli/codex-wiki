If total functions g and h are represented by lambda terms G,H, define
Stepx​=λp.Pair(Succ(Fstp))(Hx(Fstp)(Sndp)),
(28)
and
R=λxn.Snd(nStepx​(Pairc0​(Gx))).
(29)
After j iterations the Church pair holds (cj​,cf(x,j)​), proved by mathematical induction using the defining recursion equations. Consequently R represents the total primitive recursive function f. This statement concerns total inputs and total recursion constituents; it is not an unguarded composition theorem for arbitrary partial functions.
