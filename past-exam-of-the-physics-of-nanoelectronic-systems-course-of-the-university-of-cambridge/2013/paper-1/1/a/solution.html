Use e>0, B>0 along +z, and U(y) for the electron's confinement potential energy. The spin-resolved Landau level branches in the smooth-edge approximation are
En,±​(y)=ℏωc​(n+21​)±21​EZ​+U(y),ωc​=eB/m∗.​
(1)
The spin labels denote upper and lower energies; the sign of a material's effective g factor is not inferred from the stated positive splitting. In units of ℏωc​, the four occupied bulk energies are 1/3,2/3,4/3,5/3. A chemical potential in (5/3,7/3) therefore gives filling factor four. The branches rise at both confining edges and cross the common Fermi energy once at each edge. Each crossing is one spin-resolved quantum Hall edge state; there are four on either side. The two spin branches within an orbital level are separated by ℏωc​/3, while the intervening gap to the next orbital pair is 2ℏωc​/3.
