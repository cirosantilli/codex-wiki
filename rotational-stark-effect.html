Rotational Stark effect

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Stark effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 195 Articles: 5
An electric-field shift or splitting of molecular rotational levels. The observed line shift is the difference of upper- and lower-level shifts, subject to polarization-dependent selection rules; shifting only one endpoint gives an incorrect spectral splitting.
