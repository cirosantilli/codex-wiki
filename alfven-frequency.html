Alfvén frequency (ωa​=k⋅B/μ0​ρ​)

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Magnetohydrodynamic wave Alfvén wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The Alfvén frequency is the frequency of an Alfvén wave with wavevector k in a uniform magnetic field B and mass density ρ. For a vertical field and vertical wavenumber k, its square is ωa2​=k2Bz2​/(μ0​ρ). Its competition with orbital shear determines the ideal magnetorotational dispersion relation.
