Transition intensity matrix (Q=(qrs​))

For a finite-state continuous-time Markov chain, a transition intensity matrix has nonnegative off-diagonal rates qrs​ and qrr​=−∑s=r​qrs​. Every row sums to zero. In the time-homogeneous case its transition probability matrix is the matrix exponential P(t)=etQ. An absorbing state has a zero row.
