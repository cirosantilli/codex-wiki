Duality of primitive cyclic Reed-Solomon codes (Ck,b⊥​=Cn−k,1−b​)

For n=q−1 and aj​=ωj, the two evaluation forms are aj1−b​f(aj​) and ajb​h(aj​), with degf<k and degh<n−k. Their dot product is ∑j​aj​f(aj​)h(aj​)=0, since every appearing exponent is between one and n−1 and the sums of those powers of aj​ vanish. Comparing dimensions proves equality with the dual code. In particular, the dual of the unweighted evaluation code has multipliers aj​; it need not be an unweighted evaluation code on the same ordered points.
