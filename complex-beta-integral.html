Complex beta integral (∫C​∣z∣2a−2∣1−z∣2b−2dxdy)

OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For c=1−a−b with positive real parts Rea,Reb,Rec, the integral converges at its two finite singularities and at infinity, and equals
πΓ(1−a)Γ(1−b)Γ(1−c)Γ(a)Γ(b)Γ(c)​.
(43)
To prove it, use Schwinger parameterization with exponents 1−a and 1−b. Integrating the resulting planar Gaussian integral gives π/(λ+ρ) times exp[−λρ/(λ+ρ)]. Set q=λ+ρ and x=λ/q; the q integral is a gamma function and the x integral is B(a,b), giving the stated ratio. This domain justifies the interchanges; elsewhere the answer is interpreted by analytic continuation. The string measure d2z=2dxdy multiplies the answer by two.
