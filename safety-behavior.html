Safety behavior

 Home Biology Animal behaviour Learning Operant conditioning Avoidance learning
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 2
A precaution intended to prevent a feared outcome or conceal anxiety. It can maintain fear when safety is attributed to the precaution, when it blocks informative experience, or when it produces misleading symptoms. Whether a behavior has this function depends on context; objectively useful precautions are not inherently pathological.
