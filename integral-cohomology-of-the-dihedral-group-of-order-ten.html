Integral cohomology of the dihedral group of order ten

OurBigBook.com Words: 37
For the dihedral group D10​=C5​⋊C2​,
Hn(D10​,Z)≅⎩⎨⎧​Z,0,Z/2,Z/10,​n=0,n odd,n≡2(mod4),n>0 and n≡0(mod4).​
(38)
The Lyndon–Hochschild–Serre spectral sequence proves this from the inversion action of C2​ on H2(C5​,Z).
