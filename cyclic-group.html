Cyclic group (Cn​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 2
A cyclic group is generated by one element. A cyclic group of order n has exactly d solutions of xd=1 for every positive integer divisor d of n.
