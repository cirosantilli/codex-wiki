Davis-Kahan theorem

OurBigBook.com Words: 148 Articles: 2
The Davis-Kahan theorem bounds the change of an invariant eigenspace of a symmetric matrix in terms of a matrix perturbation and an appropriate spectral gap. The rank-one eigenprojector perturbation bound below depends only on the unperturbed gap. Yu, Wang and Samworth's formulation gives population-gap bounds in greater generality.
