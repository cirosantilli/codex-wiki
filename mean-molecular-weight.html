Mean molecular weight (μ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Ideal gas
OurBigBook.com Words: 193 Articles: 3
The mean molecular weight is the average mass per freely moving gas particle in units of the atomic mass unit. A fully ionized hydrogen-helium gas with mass fractions X,Y has μ−1=2X+3Y/4.
