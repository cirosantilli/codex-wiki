Fermion number of a Bogoliubov excitation (Δ⟨N⟩=ϵ/E)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quasiparticle Bogoliubov quasiparticle
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For a normalized spinless paired ground state u∣0⟩+ivcp†​c−p†​∣0⟩, its mean fermion number is 2v2. The canonical Bogoliubov quasiparticle creation operator produces cp†​∣0⟩, containing exactly one fermion. With BCS coherence factors v2=(1−ϵ/E)/2, the change in mean fermion number is 1−2v2=ϵ/E. This need not be an integer because the pairing Hamiltonian preserves fermion parity while failing to conserve the original fermion number operator. Every measurement of that number operator still has integer outcomes.
