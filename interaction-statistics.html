Interaction (statistics)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Regression model
OurBigBook.com Words: 306 Articles: 5
A statistical interaction means that the effect of one predictor depends on another on the chosen response scale. In a linear regression, an interaction term β3​XZ changes the slope in X to β1​+β3​Z. Whether interaction is present depends on the comparison scale; additive effects and multiplicative effects use different null contrasts. Comparing subgroup effects requires an interaction contrast, rather than contrasting their separate significance labels.
