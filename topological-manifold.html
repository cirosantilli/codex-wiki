Topological manifold

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 103
A topological n-manifold is a Hausdorff second-countable space in which every point has a neighborhood homeomorphic to an open subset of Rn.
