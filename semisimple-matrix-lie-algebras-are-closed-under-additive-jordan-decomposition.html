Semisimple matrix Lie algebras are closed under additive Jordan decomposition

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Jordan normal form Jordan–Chevalley decomposition Adjoint compatibility of additive Jordan decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
For a complex semisimple Lie algebra g⊆End(V) and X∈g, both parts of its Additive Jordan decomposition lie in g. The polynomial semisimple part of adX shows Xs​ normalizes g. Split End(V)=g⊕M by the Weyl complete reducibility theorem; the M-component of Xs​ centralizes g. On each irreducible summand of V it is scalar by the Schur lemma. Its trace is zero because g is a perfect Lie algebra and the nilpotent part has zero trace, so those scalars vanish. Nonsemisimple subalgebras may fail this property: C(I+N) with N=0 nilpotent contains neither Jordan part of I+N.
