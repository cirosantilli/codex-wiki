Focal length (f)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Geometrical optics Lens (optics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 254 Articles: 2
The focal length f specifies the signed distance from an ideal thin lens to its optical focus for incident light rays parallel to the optical axis. A converging thin lens has f>0, with a rear optical focus beyond the thin lens; a diverging thin lens has f<0, with a virtual optical focus on the incoming side. The focal length is a property of the optical lens in its specified surroundings, not the image distance for every object. For example, a converging thin lens with f=1m images an object 3m away at 1.5m, as computed below. [ref].
