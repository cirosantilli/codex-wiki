Category of adjunctions inducing a fixed monad

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Monad Monad induced by an adjunction
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
Fix a monad on C. Objects are adjunctions with left-hand category C whose induced monads are identified with the fixed monad. A morphism between two such adjunctions is a functor between their right-hand categories commuting with both adjoints and respecting the specified units and counits. Strict identifications give a category; coherent identifications give the analogous universal property up to compatible natural isomorphism.
