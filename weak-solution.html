Weak solution

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Solution of a partial differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 586 Articles: 10
A weak solution satisfies an integrated identity in which derivatives are transferred to test functions. It can therefore exist with fewer classical derivatives.
