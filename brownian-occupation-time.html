Brownian occupation time

The occupation time of a measurable set A through time t is ∫0t​1{Bs​∈A}​ds. The occupation-times formula expresses such integrals through local time of a semimartingale. Recurrence and the Strong Markov property imply that one-dimensional Brownian motion spends an unbounded total time in every nonempty open interval.
