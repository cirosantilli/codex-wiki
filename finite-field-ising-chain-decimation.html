Use the symmetric transfer matrix Tss′​=exp[Kss′+h(s+s′)/2] with dimensionless field h. For blocking length two set W=T2, then write Wss′​=Aexp[K′ss′+h′(s+s′)/2]. Matching its three independent entries proves the title relations and gives A=(W++​W−−​W+−2​)1/4. Explicitly W±±​=e2K±2h+e−2K and W+−​=2coshh. At zero field the exact chain recursion is recovered; at K=0 the independent-spin field stays unchanged. Splitting the field equally between neighboring bonds is essential for this normalization.
