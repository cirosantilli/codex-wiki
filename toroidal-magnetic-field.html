Toroidal magnetic field (Bϕ​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 621 Articles: 8
A toroidal magnetic field points in the azimuthal direction around a chosen symmetry axis. Its curved field lines exert inward magnetic tension −Bϕ2​/(μ0​R) in cylindrical geometry.
