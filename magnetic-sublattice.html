Magnetic sublattice

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetism Magnetic order
OurBigBook.com Words: 177
A magnetic sublattice groups sites with a common ordered magnetic moment in a description of magnetic order. In the simplest two-sublattice description, assign magnetizations MA​ and MB​ per total specimen volume, so that
M=MA​+MB​,L=MA​−MB​.
(116)
Here L is one normalization of the staggered magnetization; conventions may include a factor 1/2. Using the same total volume for both contributions is essential. Equal magnetic moment per site does not imply equal sublattice contributions if the site populations differ.
In this six-site example, three A sites and three B sites give total magnetic moments 6m0​, 0, and 3m0​, respectively, along the chosen axis. The antiferromagnetic case has M=0 but L=6m0​z/V. Cancellation of the sum leaves the alternating information intact.
