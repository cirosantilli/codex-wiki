Multiplicative diallelic penetrance (qG​=pθG,G∼Bin(2,ϕ))

 Home Biology Genetics Phenotype Penetrance
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
With Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, let G count copies of the risk allele. The penetrance pθG must lie in [0,1] for every possible genotype. Writing α=1−ϕ+ϕθ and β=1−ϕ+ϕθ2 gives E(qG​)=pα2 and E(qG2​)=p2β2.
