Onset of convection in a horizontal Darcy layer (Rc​=4π2)

A perturbation proportional to sin(nπz)est+ikx has growth rate
s=k2+n2π2Rk2​−(k2+n2π2).
(8)
Minimizing the neutral curve (k2+n2π2)2/k2 gives n=1, k=π and Rc​=4π2. Thus the first convection rolls have horizontal wavelength twice the layer depth.
