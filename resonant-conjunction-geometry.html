Resonant conjunction geometry

 ... Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Orbital resonance Mean-motion resonance Resonant argument
OurBigBook.com Words: 226 Articles: 3
For an eccentric outer body and circular inner perturber, the exterior resonant argument is ϕ=(p+q)λout​−pλin​−qϖ. At equal mean longitudes Λ, conjunction phases are
Λj​=ϖ+qϕ+2πj​,j=0,…,q−1.
(57)
The synodic period and coprime p,q determine the visitation order, with advance 2πp/q. For actual true alignment, write x=f and y=M(f) for the eccentric body's true anomaly and mean anomaly. Then ϕ=(p+q)y−px instead of qx. The evenly spaced directions are a leading-small-eccentricity approximation.
