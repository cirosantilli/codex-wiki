Complete omega-consistent arithmetic theories are sound (S⊢X⟺N⊨X)

 ... Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order theory Soundness of an arithmetic theory Omega-consistency of an arithmetic theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A bounded-complete complete omega-consistent arithmetic first-order theory is correct on every arithmetic first-order sentence. Completeness and first-order consistency make its theoremhood obey the Boolean truth tables. Omega-consistency ensures that every proved existential sentence has a provable standard numeral instance, since otherwise completeness refutes all such instances. Induction on logical formulas now identifies theoremhood with standard truth.
