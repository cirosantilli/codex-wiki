Covariance ellipse (xTΣ−1x=1)

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Expected value Variance Covariance Covariance matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For a two-dimensional positive-definite covariance matrix Σ, the centered covariance ellipse is the image of the unit circle under its positive definite square root of a matrix. Equivalently, it satisfies xTΣ−1x=1. Its semi-major and semi-minor axes have lengths given by the square roots of the larger and smaller eigenvalues, along the corresponding eigenvectors. The ellipse encodes a second-moment geometry; a probability-coverage interpretation needs additional information about the probability distribution and the chosen scale.
