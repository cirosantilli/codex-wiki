The flow is steady and irrotational, so the Bernoulli equation has the same constant throughout the connected exterior. Far from the sphere it equals v2/2+p∞​/ρ. On the surface the speed is 3v∣sinθ∣/2, giving
p(R,θ)=p∞​+2ρv2​(1−49​sin2θ).​
(10)
The maximum stagnation pressure is p∞​+ρv2/2 at both poles. At the equator, where the flow is fastest, the pressure is p∞​−5ρv2/8. The fore-aft symmetry gives zero integrated pressure drag in this inviscid model, the content of D'Alembert's paradox; it does not predict zero drag for a real viscous separated flow.
