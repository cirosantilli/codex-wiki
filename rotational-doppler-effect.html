Rotational Doppler effect

 Home Physics Wave Doppler effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 1
A frequency change associated with rotation and exchange of angular momentum. If an optical element rotates at angular velocity Ω about a chosen axis and gains angular momentum ΔL from a photon, its mechanical energy gain is ΩΔL in the prescribed slow-rotation model. Energy conservation then gives photon frequency change Δω=−ΩΔL/ℏ. The rotating half-wave plate frequency shift is a spin-polarization example.
