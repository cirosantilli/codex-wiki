Gradient ascent pulse engineering (GRAPE)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Control theory Optimal control Quantum optimal control
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
Gradient ascent pulse engineering parametrizes each control by time-slice amplitudes and optimizes a differentiable quantum-control objective. Forward products of slice unitary operators and backward adjoint propagation give derivatives with respect to every amplitude. For Uj​=e−iHj​Δt, the exact derivative is ∂fm​​Uj​=−i∫0Δt​e−iHj​(Δt−τ)Hm​e−iHj​τdτ. Updating all slice amplitudes along these derivatives is gradient ascent for a fidelity, or gradient descent for its negative.
