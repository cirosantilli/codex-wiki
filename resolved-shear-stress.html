Resolved shear stress (τ=σcosϕcosλ)

 ... Physical chemistry Crystal structure Crystallographic defect Dislocation Dislocation glide Slip system
OurBigBook.com Words: 200 Articles: 5
For uniaxial stress σ, projection onto a slip-plane normal and a slip direction gives the displayed shear stress. The angles ϕ and λ are measured from the tensile axis to that normal and direction respectively.
