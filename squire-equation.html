Squire equation ([(−iω+iαU)−Re−1(D2−κ2)]η​=−iβU′v)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Hydrodynamic stability Orr-Sommerfeld equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 160 Articles: 2
The Squire equation governs wall-normal vorticity η=∂z​u−∂x​w in the same normal mode convention as the Orr-Sommerfeld equation. It follows by taking ∂z​ of the streamwise momentum equation minus ∂x​ of the spanwise momentum equation. At a rigid no-slip wall, η​=0.
