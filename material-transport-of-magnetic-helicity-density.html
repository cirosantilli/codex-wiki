Material transport of magnetic helicity density (Dt​(A⋅B/ρ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential Magnetic helicity
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
For smooth ideal magnetohydrodynamics, write B=∇×A and ∂t​A=u×B−∇φ. The continuity equation and induction equation give Dt​(B/ρ)=(B/ρ)⋅∇u. The vector-potential equation becomes Dt​A=∇(u⋅A−φ)−(∇u)TA. Contracting these two equations cancels their velocity-gradient terms and proves
Dt​(ρA⋅B​)=ρB​⋅∇(u⋅A−φ).
(51)
Consequently the magnetic helicity in a material volume satisfies dHm​/dt=∫∂V​(u⋅A−φ)B⋅ndS. It is conserved when the magnetic field is tangent to the boundary. A single-valued gauge transformation changes Hm​ by ∫∂V​ΛB⋅ndS, which also vanishes under that condition.
