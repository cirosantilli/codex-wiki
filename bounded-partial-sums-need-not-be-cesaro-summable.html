Bounded partial sums need not be Cesàro summable

 ... Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Series (mathematics) Summation method Cesàro summation
OurBigBook.com Words: 166
Construct partial sums sn​ from alternating blocks of zero and one, with lengths 1,3,9,27,…. Block j, beginning with j=0, has length 3j and value zero when j is even, one when j is odd. Define a series by a0​=s0​ and an​=sn​−sn−1​ for n≥1; the telescoping series identity recovers these chosen partial sums. They remain in [0,1].
At the end of odd block 2m+1, the Cesàro mean is exactly 3/4. At the end of even block 2m, it is
4(3⋅9m−1)3(9m−1)​⟶41​.
(26)
These two subsequences have different subsequential limits, so the series has no first-order Cesàro sum. A later block remains a substantial fraction of everything averaged so far. Boundedness alone therefore does not ensure a stable average.
