Free electron model (ϵk​=ℏ2∣k∣2/(2m))

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Electron Electron gas
OurBigBook.com Words: 192 Articles: 2
The free electron model approximates conduction electrons by noninteracting particles with the displayed parabolic dispersion relation, filling one-particle states according to Fermi-Dirac distribution. A uniform positive background supplies charge neutrality. Periodic boundary conditions give one spatial state per k-space volume (2π)d/Vd​ in d dimensions; the physical spin degeneracy is two. The model captures basic state counting and degeneracy but omits the periodic lattice potential, interaction corrections and detailed electronic band structure.
