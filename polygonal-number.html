Polygonal number (Ps,n​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Figurate number
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 5
For integers s≥3 and n≥1, the nth s-gonal number is Ps,n​=((s−2)n2−(s−4)n)/2. Its successive differences form an arithmetic progression. The cases s=3,4,5,6 give triangular numbers, square numbers, pentagonal numbers, and hexagonal numbers.
