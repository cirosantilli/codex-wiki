Functor represented by a scheme (hX​(R)=Hom(SpecR,X))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
The functor of points describes a scheme through its morphisms from test schemes. On commutative rings, it is covariant because a ring homomorphism reverses the corresponding affine-scheme map. A functor on rings is represented by X in the algebraic-geometric sense when it is naturally isomorphic to hX​. The representing scheme need not be affine; this is different from demanding a representable functor in the category of rings itself.
