Impedance matching

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Electrical impedance
OurBigBook.com Words: 285 Articles: 3
For a source with internal electrical impedance Zs​, conjugate matching ZL​=Zs∗​ maximizes delivered mean power when the source is fixed. For a purely resistive source and equal load electrical resistance R, the load sees half the open-circuit voltage. A Johnson-Nyquist noise source with open-circuit mean-square voltage 4kB​TRdν therefore delivers kB​Tdν to the matched load, rather than 4kB​Tdν.
