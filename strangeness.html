Strangeness (S)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Flavor symmetry
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
An additive flavor symmetry quantum number: a strange quark has S=−1, its antiquark has S=+1, and up and down quarks have S=0. The strong interaction conserves net strangeness. Together with baryon number, it determines flavor hypercharge Y=B+S.
