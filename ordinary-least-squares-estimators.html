Ordinary least squares estimators (β​=(XTX)−1XTY)

For a linear regression design matrix X with full column rank, the ordinary least squares coefficient vector is β​=(XTX)−1XTY. In a normal linear model with error covariance matrix σ2I, it is an unbiased estimator with covariance matrix σ2(XTX)−1. Replacing σ2 by the residual variance estimate gives the reported coefficient standard errors.
