Major-axis relaxation theorem (T≥L2/(2Imax​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
At fixed angular momentum, rotational energy is minimized by rotation about the largest principal moment of inertia. Indeed, T=∑i​Li2​/(2Ii​)≥∑i​Li2​/(2Imax​). Weak internal dissipation can therefore relax a nearly rigid isolated body towards that axis. A perfectly rigid torque-free body conserves energy and does not settle; both extreme-inertia axes are stable in its ideal dynamics. The maximum-inertia axis need not be the geometrically longest axis.
