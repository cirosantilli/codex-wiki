Rotating point-vortex relative equilibrium (d(H+ΩL)=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Relative equilibrium
OurBigBook.com Words: 109
For identical point vortices on the plane, use ω=∑a​dxa​∧dya​, ιXH​​ω=dH, and L=21​∑a​(xa2​+ya2​). The generator Y of simultaneous counterclockwise rotations is −XL​, and L is its moment map with the convention ιY​ω=−dL. In coordinates rotating with angular velocity Ω, the Hamiltonian vector field is XH​−ΩY=XH+ΩL​. Hence a collision-free configuration rigidly rotating at that velocity is exactly a critical point of the augmented Hamiltonian function H+ΩL. For H=−∑a<b​log∣ra​−rb​∣2, scaling all positions shows ∑a​ra​⋅∇a​H=−N(N−1); a rotating relative equilibrium therefore obeys 2ΩL=N(N−1). For N≥2 this implies positive Ω in the stated circulation convention. Reversing the contraction convention requires consistent changes to the Hamiltonian and rotation signs.
