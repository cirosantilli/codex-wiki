Measurement noise (n(t))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Measurement error
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
Measurement noise is a fluctuating contribution to measurement error. An additive model for a measured output is ym​(t)=y(t)+n(t), where y is the actual plant output. A controller using feedback control can react to n as though it were a change in the plant output. This effect is absent from the ideal noiseless examples under control theory; an errors-in-variables model represents a related distinction between latent quantities and their noisy observations.
