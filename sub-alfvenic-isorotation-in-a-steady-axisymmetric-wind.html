Sub-Alfvénic isorotation in a steady axisymmetric wind (Ω≃ΩF​(MAp2​≪1))

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Axisymmetric magnetohydrodynamic wind
OurBigBook.com Words: 102
Assume zero toroidal loop voltage in smooth steady ideal magnetohydrodynamics, so the poloidal flow aligns with the poloidal magnetic field. Write ρup​=ηBp​. The continuity equation makes η constant along a magnetic line. The induction equation gives the conserved field-line angular velocity ΩF​=Ω−ηBϕ​/(ρR). The azimuthal momentum equation is ρup​⋅∇(R2Ω)=Bp​⋅∇(RBϕ​)/μ0​; hence L=R2Ω−RBϕ​/(μ0​η) is also constant along the line.
With MAp2​=μ0​η2/ρ=∣up​∣2/vAp2​, eliminating Bϕ​ gives
Ω=1−MAp2​ΩF​−MAp2​L/R2​.
(49)
For MAp2​≪1 and nonsingular invariants, Ω=ΩF​+O(MAp2​) and matter approximately corotates along a field line. With exactly zero poloidal flow, induction directly gives Ferraro's law of isorotation. The approximation requires the correction MAp2​(ΩF​−L/R2) to be small on the segment in question.
