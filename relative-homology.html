Relative homology (H∗​(X,A))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 551 Articles: 12
The relative chain complex is C∗​(X,A)=C∗​(X)/C∗​(A), and its homology is the relative homology H∗​(X,A). A pair A⊆U⊆X produces a long exact sequence for the triple.
