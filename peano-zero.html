Peano zero (f(x0​+t)=o(∣t∣r))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Taylor theorem Taylor expansion
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A Peano zero through order r means that the function admits the zero Taylor polynomial with remainder o(∣t∣r) at x0​. This does not assert the existence of ordinary iterated derivatives on a neighborhood. For example, multiplying tr+1 by a bounded continuous function with dense points of nondifferentiability preserves this estimate while destroying neighborhood differentiability.
