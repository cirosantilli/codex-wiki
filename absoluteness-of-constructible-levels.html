Absoluteness of constructible levels (LαM​=Lα​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Definable power set Constructible hierarchy
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
For a transitive model M of ZFC and α∈M∩Ord, the internally computed constructible hierarchy agrees with the ambient level: LαM​=Lα​. Satisfaction for a fixed set structure is computed from the same finite formulas and the same domain in both universes. Transfinite recursion then proves agreement at successors and limits. If θ=M∩Ord, it follows that Lθ​⊆M.
