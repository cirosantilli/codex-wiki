Diffusive establishment time of a wound toroidal field (tη​∼ℓ2/η)

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Toroidal magnetic field Toroidal magnetic-field winding equation Steady toroidal induction by spherical differential rotation
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
With a fixed axial seed field and prescribed localized differential rotation, the winding source is time independent. Early induction gives Bϕ​∼B0​Ωt, while the steady balance has Bϕ​∼B0​Ωℓ2/η. Their comparison gives the magnetic diffusion time, not merely the rotation period. It diverges as conductivity becomes infinite. In an unbounded domain, this is a local equilibration estimate on scale ℓ, not finite-time exact convergence everywhere. Lorentz force feedback may invalidate the prescribed-flow approximation sooner.
