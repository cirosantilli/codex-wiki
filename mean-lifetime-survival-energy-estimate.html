Mean-lifetime survival energy estimate (p=exp[−H/(γvτ0​)])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz factor Time dilation Cosmic-ray muon time-dilation experiment
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
An unstable particle of rest lifetime τ0​ traversing distance H at constant speed v has survival probability p=exp[−H/(γvτ0​)]. Requiring a flight time no greater than one dilated mean lifetime selects p≥e−1. In the ultrarelativistic approximation v≃c, this gives γ≳H/(cτ0​) and E≳mc2H/(cτ0​). It is a specified survival-probability criterion, not a sharp cutoff for individual random decays. Continuous energy loss requires integrating the decay probability along the path.
