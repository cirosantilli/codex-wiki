Standard Model active neutrinos decouple while relativistic and retain a thermal momentum distribution. They therefore constitute hot dark matter for the light masses compatible with the standard cosmological picture. Their collisionless free streaming erases linear cosmological density perturbations below a characteristic scale. Making them the dominant gravitating matter would delay small-scale structure formation and suppress the observed matter power spectrum.

Increasing the mass sum enough to provide all the stipulated dark matter conflicts with the successful cold dark matter description of cosmological structure. Independently, the Tremaine-Gunn bound on the fermionic phase-space occupation limits the density light neutrinos can supply in small galactic haloes.[ref] Oscillations establish nonzero neutrino mass differences, but do not make the relic neutrinos cold. Active neutrinos can contribute some matter; they cannot supply the dominant cold component. A heavier sterile neutrino candidate would be a different hypothesis, not an ordinary Standard Model relic.
