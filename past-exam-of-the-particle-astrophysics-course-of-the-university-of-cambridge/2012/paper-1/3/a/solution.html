Use consistent mass-energy units. The factor 1/2 assumes identical self-conjugate particles; distinct particle-antiparticle populations require their separate number densities. A gamma-ray continuum with an endpoint near the particle mass is generic for many channels, whereas a narrow line from χχ→γγ or γZ would be especially distinctive but often loop suppressed. dwarf spheroidal galaxies offer high dark-matter content with limited conventional emission; the Galactic centre has a potentially larger signal and much more complicated foregrounds.
Charged Positrons and antiprotons are redistributed by magnetic fields and lose energy, so their radiation spectrum depend on propagation and cannot usually point directly to the source. annihilation electrons can also produce synchrotron and inverse-Compton radiation. dark matter captured in the Sun could produce escaping high-energy neutrinos, with the signal depending on capture, annihilation equilibrium and particle interactions. Early annihilation energy injection can alter recombination and cosmic microwave background anisotropies.
A persuasive interpretation joins particle-like spectral structure with a consistent density-dependent morphology and other channels. Pulsars, cosmic-ray interactions and unresolved sources can imitate continuum excesses. Density-profile uncertainties, substructure and velocity-dependent annihilation modify the predicted signal; a lack of a detectable line does not exclude all annihilating dark matter.
