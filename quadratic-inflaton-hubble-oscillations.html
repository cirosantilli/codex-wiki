For a homogeneous canonical inflaton oscillating near V=m2ϕ2/2, use reduced-Planck units and require m≫H. The slowly varying ansatz ϕ=A(t)cosmt gives Hm2​≃m2A2/6. The Nonincreasing Hubble rate for a canonical inflaton becomes
H˙≃−23​Hm2​(1−cos2mt).
(33)
Cycle averaging gives H˙m​=−3Hm2​/2, so Hm​=2/[3(t−t∗​)]. Integrating the rapidly varying term while holding its coefficient approximately constant gives the displayed correction. It is smaller than the mean by O(Hm​/m). Although the correction oscillates, the total Hubble parameter remains nonincreasing. Equal average kinetic and potential energies give pressureless-matter-like expansion, with amplitude A∝a−3/2.
