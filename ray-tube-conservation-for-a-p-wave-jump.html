Ray-tube conservation for a P-wave jump (ρα(a[k])2J=constant)

 ... Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Elastic wave P wave P-wavefront discontinuity transport
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
A tube of neighboring elastic rays follows the wavefront normal. If J is its infinitesimal transverse area, its expansion is dlogJ/ds=∇⋅n along arclength. The P-wavefront discontinuity transport law therefore gives the displayed conserved flux. Geometrical spreading, mass density and wave speed determine the leading jump wave amplitude together; the rule is valid before the ray tube reaches a caustic.
