Two-carrier Hall coefficient (RH​=e(pμh​+nμe​)2pμh2​−nμe2​​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For isotropic electrons and electron holes with positive mobilities, adding their weak-field electrical conductivity tensors and imposing zero transverse total current gives
RH​=e(pμh​+nμe​)2pμh2​−nμe2​​,σ=e(nμe​+pμh​).
(34)
The Hall coefficient changes sign at pμh2​=nμe2​. Carrier abundance alone does not fix the sign; mobilities enter quadratically. The formula assumes μe​B≪1, μh​B≪1, independent semiclassical carriers, and no anomalous Hall contribution.
