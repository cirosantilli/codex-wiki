Clifford algebra

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field
OurBigBook.com Words: 703 Articles: 16
For a vector space with quadratic form Q, its Clifford algebra is generated by vectors subject to v2=Q(v)1. In a basis this is equivalently γμγν+γνγμ=2ημν1.
