Dirichlet energy dissipation for the heat equation

OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For a real smooth solution wt​=Δw on a bounded regular domain with wt​=0 on its boundary, differentiation and Green's first identity yield
dtd​∫V​∣∇w∣2dV=−2∫V​(Δw)2dV≤0.
(13)
The boundary term is zero because the boundary values are fixed in time, not because the normal derivative vanishes. Equality at a particular time is equivalent to Δw=0 throughout the domain at that time.
