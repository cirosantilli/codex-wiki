Survival probability of the contact process (θ(λ,μ))

OurBigBook.com Words: 372 Articles: 4
The survival probability is the probability that the contact process begun from one infected vertex never reaches its absorbing empty state. Its critical infection rate is λc​(μ)=inf{λ:θ(λ,μ)>0}.
