Tresca yield criterion (f=21​maxi,j​∣σi​−σj​∣≤τY​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Plasticity (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The Tresca yield criterion bounds the largest resolved shear stress, expressed through the principal stresses. In spherical symmetry, the two tangential principal stresses coincide, so f=∣τrr​−τθθ​∣/2. Here τY​ is a shear yield stress; the corresponding uniaxial yield strength is 2τY​.
