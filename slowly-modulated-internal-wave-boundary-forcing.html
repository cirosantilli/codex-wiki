Slowly modulated internal-wave boundary forcing (AT​+cgz​AZ​=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave Boundary-forced internal gravity wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 1
For a plane internal gravity wave in uniform buoyancy frequency, substitute ζ=A(μt,μz)ei(kx+mz−ωt) into the displacement equation. The leading order gives ω2=N2k2/(k2+m2); the next order gives the displayed transport equation with cgz​=−ωm/(k2+m2). A boundary amplitude a(T) radiates as A=a(T−Z/cgz​), choosing the sign of m so the group velocity points into the fluid. The wavelength-averaged energy is N2∣A∣2/2 and its vertical flux is cgz​ times that energy. A causal ramp therefore creates a translating front, not an instantaneous monochromatic field throughout the half-space.
