Quadratic-drag turning angle (tanθ=μ∣f∣/(ρw​Cw​U))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Drag (physics) Quadratic drag
OurBigBook.com Words: 155 Articles: 1
For an ice mass in still water, approximate the wind stress by a fixed vector aex​ while retaining quadratic drag in the water. With mass per water-drag area μ=m/Aw​, water coefficient b=ρw​Cw​ and f=2Ωsinϕ, the steady balance is aex​=bUu+μfz×u. Resolving along the drift and perpendicular to it gives acosθ=bU2, asinθ=μ∣f∣U. Thus b2U4+(μf)2U2=a2 and tanθ=μ∣f∣/(bU). The direction is right of the wind in the northern hemisphere and left in the southern hemisphere. This angle neglects intrinsic boundary-layer turning, ocean currents and contact forces; it is not a universal observed sea-ice turning angle.
