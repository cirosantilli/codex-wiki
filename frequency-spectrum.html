Frequency spectrum

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
A frequency spectrum describes the frequency components of a function. A Fourier series supplies discrete Fourier coefficients; the Fourier transform of an integrable function supplies a continuous, generally complex-valued description. An amplitude plot shows magnitudes and omits wave phases, so it need not determine the original function. In the example with three Fourier harmonics above, the one-sided amplitude plot has heights 1, 1/2, and 1/4. The corresponding complex Fourier coefficients at positive and negative frequencies each have half those heights: cos(2πνt)=(e2πiνt+e−2πiνt)/2.
