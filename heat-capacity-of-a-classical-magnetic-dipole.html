Heat capacity of a classical magnetic dipole (CB​=kB​[1−x2/sinh2x])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Magnetic susceptibility Independent-moment magnetic response
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
A freely orientable classical magnetic dipole moment of fixed magnitude m in a uniform magnetic field B has angular canonical partition function Z=4πsinhx/x, where x=mB/(kB​T). Its internal energy is U=−mBcothx+kB​T, and its fixed-field heat capacity is CB​=kB​[1−x2/sinh2x]. At low temperature, two locally Cartesian tangent coordinates on the orientation sphere each contribute kB​T/2 by the equipartition theorem, giving CB​→kB​. A discrete quantum spin in a nonzero magnetic field instead has a spectral gap and its heat capacity vanishes exponentially at sufficiently low temperature.
