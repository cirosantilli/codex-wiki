Heat capacity at constant volume (CV​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Heat capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 2
For a fixed-composition closed system, the heat capacity at fixed volume is CV​=(∂U/∂T)V​, with internal energy U. Under the quasistatic process assumptions in heat capacity, only pressure-volume work is permitted, so dV=0 gives δQ=dU=CV​dT. A rigid vessel therefore needs Q=ΔU, or Q=CV​ΔT when CV​ is constant. Its per-unit-mass counterpart is specific heat capacity at constant volume.
