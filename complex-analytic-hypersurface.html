Complex analytic hypersurface

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Complex submanifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 730 Articles: 13
A complex analytic hypersurface in an n-dimensional complex manifold is a closed analytic subset of pure complex codimension one. It is irreducible when it is not the union of two proper closed analytic subsets.
