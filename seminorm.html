Seminorm (p)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Topological vector space Locally convex space
OurBigBook.com Words: 66 Articles: 1
A seminorm on a vector space is a nonnegative function p satisfying p(λx)=∣λ∣p(x) and p(x+y)≤p(x)+p(y). Unlike a norm, it may vanish at nonzero vectors.
