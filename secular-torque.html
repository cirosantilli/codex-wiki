Secular torque

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planetary system dynamics Disturbing function Secular perturbation
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
A secular torque is the orbit-averaged gravitational torque that changes orbital orientation or shape after fast orbital phases have been averaged out.
