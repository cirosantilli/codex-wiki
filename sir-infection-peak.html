Thus about 30.38% of the modelled population belongs to the infectious compartment simultaneously at the SIR infection peak. This is a peak of the infectious compartment, not of incidence, whose flow is βsi. If s0​≤γ/β, the maximum is already i0​ at the start and the interior SIR infection peak formula does not apply.
The numerical solution of a differential equation puts the SIR infection peak at about day 26.63258 with i≈0.303812682386, agreeing with the SIR first integral to within 10−11. The accompanying generator checks nonnegative fractions and conservation of population without clipping or rescaling. For steps 0.4, 0.2 and 0.1 day, the largest differences between successive numerical trajectories at shared times are approximately 1.086×10−7 and 6.985×10−9. Their ratio is 15.55, close to the factor 16 expected when a fourth-order Runge-Kutta method's step is halved.
