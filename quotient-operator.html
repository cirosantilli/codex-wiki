Quotient operator (BY​(0,c)⊆T(BX​(0,1)))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Topological vector space Continuous linear operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 1
A quotient operator is a surjective bounded linear operator that is an open map. It is equivalent to requiring a ball about zero in the image of the open unit ball. Translation and scaling then prove being an open map at every point. For a Banach space domain, a bounded linear operator is a quotient operator exactly when its Banach-space adjoint is an isomorphic embedding: a lower bound on the adjoint gives approximate ball-surjectivity by Hahn-Banach separation, and successive approximation for quotient operators gives exact preimages.
