Risk-neutral probability (Q(A))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Risk-neutral measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
The probability of a market event under an equivalent martingale measure for a specified numéraire. It can differ from the physical probability. For a random numéraire, impose the martingale condition on asset-price ratios rather than treating the numéraire return as deterministic.
