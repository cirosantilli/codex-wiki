Canonical one-form on a cotangent bundle (λ)

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Lagrangian submanifold Cotangent bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 2
For the cotangent bundle projection π:T∗X→X, the canonical differential one-form is λ(x,ξ)​(v)=ξ(dπ(v)). In coordinates it is ∑j​ξj​dxj​. The canonical symplectic form is −dλ in the position-first convention ∑j​dxj​∧dξj​.
