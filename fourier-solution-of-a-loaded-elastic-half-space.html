Fourier solution of a loaded elastic half-space (A=(1+∣k∣y)Pe−∣k∣y/k2)

For plane strain in y>0 under surface normal traction −P(x) and zero surface shear, use the forward Fourier transform phase eikx. At k=0, biharmonicity gives (∂y2​−k2)2A=0, whose decaying part is (C+Dy)e−∣k∣y. Boundary traction fixes C=P/k2, D=∣k∣C, proving the displayed formula. Constitutive inversion and a decaying displacement mode give 2μv=[y+2(1−ν)/∣k∣]Pe−∣k∣y. Multiplication by −ik produces an unambiguous displacement-gradient convolution kernel.
The zero mode is separate: a nonzero resultant load P(0) conflicts with an ordinary fully decaying displacement normalization in two dimensions and leads to a logarithmic reference issue. Balanced loading or a distributional/reference-displacement treatment is required before extending the transformed formulas globally. Dividing by k2 and assigning a finite value at k=0 is invalid.
