Hook graph of a partition

 ... Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young diagram Hook of a Young diagram Hook length
OurBigBook.com Words: 72 Articles: 1
The hook graph is the Young diagram with the hook length written in each box. It displays the hook product and thus the hook-length formula for the corresponding Specht module. This use of the name is recorded in Frame, Robinson and Thrall's original hook-graph paper; it refers to a labeled array, not a directed hook-walk graph.
