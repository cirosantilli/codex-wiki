Coincidence circuit

 Home Physics Particle detection Particle detector Trigger (particle physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 284 Articles: 1
A coincidence circuit responds when detector signals occur within a specified time window. Two counters placed on opposite sides of a chamber can preferentially select a charged particle that crosses both. Simultaneity within the electronics' window alone does not establish that two signals have the same physical cause: unrelated detections produce accidental coincidences.
