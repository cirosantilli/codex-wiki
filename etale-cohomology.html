Étale cohomology (Heti​(X,F))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
Étale cohomology is sheaf cohomology on the site whose objects are étale morphisms Y→X and whose coverings are jointly surjective families of such morphisms. It provides cohomological invariants for algebraic varieties over fields of positive characteristic. In Deligne-Lusztig theory, compactly supported groups with ℓ-adic coefficients, for ℓ different from the field characteristic, give finite-dimensional representations of the finite groups acting on the varieties.
