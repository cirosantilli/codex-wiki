Taylor identity for quasi-geostrophic flux (v′Q′​=Fy​+Gz​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Transformed Eulerian mean Eliassen–Palm flux
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
For zonal averages, u′=−ψy′​, v′=ψx′​, and Q′=∇H2​ψ′+∂z​(Sψz′​), with S=f02​/N2 depending only on height, define F=−u′v′ and G=Sψx′​ψz′​​. Integrating zonal derivatives by parts gives v′Q′​=∂y​ψx′​ψy′​​+∂z​(Sψx′​ψz′​​). This identity is exact within quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity, without assuming small disturbance amplitudes. Periodicity or appropriate vanishing zonal boundary terms is essential.
