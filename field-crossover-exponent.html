Field crossover exponent (Δ=βδ=β+γ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Critical exponent
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
The field crossover exponent describes the field scale h∼∣t∣Δ at which the zero-field critical regime changes. In the two-variable scaling hypothesis for critical phenomena, its value is the ratio yh​/yt​ of magnetic and thermal scaling dimensions. It is distinct from either yh​ or the scaling dimension of the order parameter. The Widom scaling relation and differentiation of the homogeneous free-energy density give the displayed identities when ordinary scaling applies.
