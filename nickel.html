Nickel (Ni, Z=28)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element
OurBigBook.com Words: 64 Articles: 1
The chemical element with atomic number 28. Radioactive nickel-56 is produced in explosive burning; the nickel-56 decay chain contributes energy to supernova emission.
