Logical right shift (LSRk​(a)=⌊a/2k⌋)

 Home Computer science Programming language Bit shift
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
A logical right shift moves bits toward less significant positions, discards those shifted out, and fills the high positions with zero. On the unsigned interpretation of a machine word, it computes the quotient in integer division by 2k. Indeed, splitting the binary representation after its lowest k bits gives a=2kq+r with 0≤r<2k; the retained upper bits represent q. A fixed-width left bit shift by n−k followed by a logical right shift by n−k extracts precisely the low k bits, giving the remainder.
