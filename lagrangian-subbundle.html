Lagrangian subbundle

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Symplectic vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
A rank-n vector subbundle of a rank-2n symplectic vector bundle is Lagrangian when each fibre is a Lagrangian subspace. If it exists, a compatible almost complex structure supplies a complementary Lagrangian subbundle by applying J. Existence is a global condition: the area-oriented tangent bundle of the two-sphere has no real line subbundle, by triviality of real line bundles over the sphere and the Hairy ball theorem.
