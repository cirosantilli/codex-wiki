Positive Laplace-Beltrami operator (Δ+​=−divg​gradg​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Laplace-Beltrami operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 1
The positive convention for the Laplace-Beltrami operator is Δ+​f=δdf, where δ is the codifferential. It is the restriction of the Hodge Laplacian to functions. In coordinates,
Δ+​f=−detg​1​∂i​(detg​gij∂j​f).
(12)
On a compact boundaryless Riemannian manifold, or for compactly supported functions, ⟨f,Δ+​f⟩=∥df∥2≥0. The opposite divgrad convention is also standard; formulas involving the sign must specify which is used.
