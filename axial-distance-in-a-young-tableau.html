Axial distance in a Young tableau (dT​(i,j)=cT​(i)−cT​(j))

OurBigBook.com Words: 45
The axial distance from label j to label i in a standard Young tableau is the difference of their Young-diagram cell contents. This sign convention makes cT​(r+1)−cT​(r) equal to +1 for consecutive entries in a row and −1 in a column. The reciprocal distance supplies the diagonal coefficient of Young orthogonal form.
