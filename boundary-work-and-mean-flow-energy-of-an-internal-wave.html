Boundary work and mean-flow energy of an internal wave (W=cp​ρ0​u′w′)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave Internal-wave momentum deposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For a traveling sinusoidal boundary forcing with horizontal phase speed cp​, the pressure and horizontal velocity polarization gives the displayed relation between upward energy input and vertical wave momentum flux. A slowly established outgoing wave envelope deposits horizontal mean momentum as its front advances. In the frame moving at cp​, the order-amplitude-squared change of mean kinetic energy is −ρ0​cp​∫udz. Its loss rate equals the boundary work in the laboratory frame. The comparison uses a finite perturbation of the infinite uniform background energy.
