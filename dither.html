Dither

 Home Electrical engineering Analog-to-digital converter
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 1
dither is an intentionally introduced fluctuation that makes a quantizer's output vary between codes, reducing signal-correlated quantization artifacts. A suitable distribution can make the averaged code carry information about a constant voltage smaller than one conversion step. The improvement requires filtering or averaging; extra noise does not improve an individual conversion or repair arbitrary calibration errors.
