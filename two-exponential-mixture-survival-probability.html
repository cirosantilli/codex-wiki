In the classical risk model with density 3e−4x+e−2x/2 and relative safety loading 3/5, the claim mean is 5/16 and λ/c=2. The convolution terms Aa​(u)=∫0u​ϕ(u−x)e−axdx satisfy Aa′​=ϕ−aAa​. Applying (D+4)(D+2) to ϕ′=2ϕ−6A4​−A2​ yields ϕ(3)+4ϕ′′+3ϕ′=0. Its solution is fixed by ϕ(∞)=1, ϕ(0)=3/8 and ϕ′(0)=3/4, giving the displayed probability. The derivative condition comes from the original integral equation and excludes spurious solutions of the higher-order equation.
