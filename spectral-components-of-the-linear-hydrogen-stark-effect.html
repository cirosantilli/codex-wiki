The outer pair originates in the p0​ parts of ∣ψ±​⟩ and has transition electric dipole moments along the applied electric field, conventionally called π components. The central pair originates in p±1​ and has transverse rotating transition electric dipole moments, conventionally called σ± components. Because electromagnetic radiation is transverse, the π components disappear when viewed along the electric field. Viewed from the side, the π and σ components have polarization of an electromagnetic wave respectively parallel and perpendicular to the applied electric field. Line intensities additionally require the initial quantum state populations and the collection geometry.
