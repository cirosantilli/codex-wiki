Crosslinking and immunoprecipitation

Mapping close RNA–protein contacts by crosslinking, immunoprecipitation of a selected protein and analysis of attached RNA fragments. Input abundance, antibody specificity and crosslinking bias constrain the interpretation of an apparent binding peak.
