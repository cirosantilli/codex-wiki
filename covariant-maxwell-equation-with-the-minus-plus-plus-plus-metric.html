Covariant Maxwell equation with the minus-plus-plus-plus metric

With metric signature (−,+,+,+), A0​=−Φ/c, and jμ=(cρ,J), the field tensor satisfies
∂ν​Fμν=μ0​jμ,∂[λ​Fμν]​=0.
(25)
Its components are F0i​=−Ei​/c and Fij​=ϵijk​Bk​, so these tensor equations reproduce all four Maxwell equations.
