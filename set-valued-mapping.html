Set-valued mapping (S:X⇉Y)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Variational analysis Set-valued analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 224 Articles: 5
A mapping S:X⇉Y assigns a subset S(u)⊂Y to each u. Empty and multiple values are allowed. The graph of a set-valued mapping records the relation as a subset of X×Y.
