Beth number (ℶα​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cardinal number Cardinal arithmetic
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The cardinal numbers defined by ℶ0​=ℵ0​, ℶα+1​=2ℶα​, and ℶλ​=supα<λ​ℶα​ at limit ordinals are the beth numbers. In particular ℶ1​=∣P(ω)∣, the cardinality of the continuum. This notation distinguishes iterated power sets from the enumeration by Aleph numbers of well-orderable infinite cardinal numbers.
