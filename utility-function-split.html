Utility function

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance
OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 87
In expected-utility portfolio choice, an increasing utility function prefers greater wealth, while concavity models aversion to risk.
A utility function assigns numerical values to outcomes consistently with preference comparisons. It represents utility; the representation and the preference concept are distinct.
