Spin orbital (uaσ​(r,s)=ϕa​(r)χσ​(s))

 ... Multiparticle quantum state Particle exchange operator Indistinguishable particles Fermion Pauli exclusion principle Spatial orbital
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
When spatial and spin factors separate, a spin orbital combines a spatial orbital ϕa​ with a normalized spin state χσ​. For an electron, choose spin one-half along an axis with σ=↑,↓. The two spin orbitals ua↑​ and ua↓​ are orthogonal even though their spatial orbital is identical, since their inner product contains ⟨χ↑​∣χ↓​⟩=0. When spatial and spin variables do not separate, the complete one-particle state is instead a multicomponent wavefunction u(r,s); exclusion still applies to that complete quantum state.
