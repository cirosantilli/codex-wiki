Phase transition

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 51
A phase transition is a nonanalytic change in equilibrium behavior as a thermodynamic control parameter varies. A continuous phase transition has a diverging correlation length and no latent heat.
