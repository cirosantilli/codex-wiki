Tau antineutrino (νˉτ​)

 ... Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Lepton number Neutrino Tau neutrino
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
The antiparticle associated with the tau-flavour neutrino. A tau lepton of positive charge emits it in an ordinary charged current decay; a negative tau lepton instead emits a tau neutrino.
