Einstein-frame representation of metric f(R) gravity (g​μν​=−f′gμν​,ϕ=3/2​log(−f′))

In the mostly-minus convention with vacuum gravitational action proportional to ∫−g​f(R) and f′<0, the displayed conformal transformation turns its vacuum equation into Gμν​=Tμν​(ϕ), with potential V=(Rf′−f)/(2f′2) and the stress-energy tensor of a canonical scalar field. The conformal Ricci formula cancels the second derivatives of f′ against the original field equation. Its remaining derivative tensor is ∂μ​ϕ∂ν​ϕ−g​μν​(∇ϕ)2/2. Expressing V as a function of ϕ requires a locally invertible R↦f′(R). When f′′=0, this scalar-tensor parametrization degenerates and introduces no new propagating scalar degree of freedom.
