Twin prime

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Prime number
OurBigBook.com Words: 246 Articles: 5
Two primes differing by two form a twin-prime pair, for example 3,5 or 11,13. The twin-prime upper bound using Bombieri–Vinogradov limits their number without proving there are infinitely many.
