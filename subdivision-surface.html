Subdivision surface

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Computer aided geometric design
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 20
A subdivision surface is the limit of repeated mesh refinement and vertex averaging. Exact interrogation evaluates this limit and, where defined, its tangent vectors and normal vectors; a finite refined control mesh is generally only an approximation to the limit.
