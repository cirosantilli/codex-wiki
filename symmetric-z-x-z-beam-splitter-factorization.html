Since HZH=X, unitary conjugation gives He−iβZ/2H=e−iβX/2. Multiplying by the two outer Rz​(α) rotations gives
U=(e−iαcos(β/2)−isin(β/2)​−isin(β/2)eiαcos(β/2)​).
(16)
The case α=β=π/2 equals −iH. Equal output probabilities for one input do not by themselves identify an optical component with the Hadamard gate; relative wave phases also matter.
