Kelvin-Voigt cantilever creep (τδ˙+δ=Fℓ3/(3EI))

OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For a quasistatic cantilever of Kelvin-Voigt material, the bending moment is M=EIyxx​+ηs​Iyxxt​. A tip load gives M=F(t)(ℓ−x), so the free-tip displacement obeys the displayed equation with τ=ηs​/E. A step load gives a finite asymptotic deflection. If inertia is retained, the interior equation is ρAytt​+EIyxxxx​+ηs​Iyxxxxt​=0, with the corresponding moment and shear conditions at the tip.
