Secant domination for expected utility derivatives

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Utility function Expected utility hypothesis Expected utility maximization
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
If a negative differentiable concave utility function satisfies EU(θX)>−∞ on an open interval, outer secants at four points bound the sample derivative on a smaller compact interval. Negativity makes the endpoint values absolutely integrable. The dominated convergence theorem then gives dEU(θX)/dθ=E[XU′(θX)] and continuous derivative when U′ is continuous. This is a justification of differentiation, rather than an assumption of integrability of marginal utility.
