Vibroseis correlation

 ... Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Seismology Seismic source Vibroseis
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
Let a linear Earth response h be recorded using a known source sweep s, giving d=s∗h+n. Cross-correlating the record with the sweep yields
c(τ)=∫d(t)s(t−τ)dt=(h∗Rss​)(τ)+Rns​(τ).
(4)
Thus a compact sweep autocorrelation Rss​ produces pulse-compressed arrival wavelets, while unrelated noise has a smaller coherent response. The Fourier transform of the result is H(ω)∣S(ω)∣2+N(ω)S(ω)​. Correlation is a matched-filter operation, not an exact inversion of missing source frequencies. Spectral shaping or stabilized deconvolution can further adjust the wavelet within the measured bandwidth.[ref]
