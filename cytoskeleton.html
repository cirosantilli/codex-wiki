 Home Biology Biological cell
OurBigBook.com Words: 499 Articles: 12
A network of protein filaments within a biological cell that contributes to shape, mechanical support and motion. Its coupling to the lipid bilayer adds mechanical responses, including shear elasticity, absent from an isolated fluid bilayer.
