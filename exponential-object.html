Exponential object (BA)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Adjoint functors Cartesian closed category
OurBigBook.com Words: 408 Articles: 7
An exponential object BA represents maps out of a product with A: there are natural bijections
C(X×A,B)≅C(X,BA).
(18)
The corresponding operations are currying and uncurrying, and the identity of BA corresponds to the evaluation morphism BA×A→B.
