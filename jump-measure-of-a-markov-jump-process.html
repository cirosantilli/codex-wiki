For a Markov jump process with off-diagonal rate kernel q(x,dy), the jump measure records the jump time and the destination state. Relative to a filtration for which the process has the Markov jump property, its predictable compensator is the displayed rate measure, up to explosion. The corresponding generator is Qf(x)=∫[f(y)−f(x)]q(x,dy). An enlarged filtration revealing future jumps need not preserve this compensator, so the filtration condition is essential.
