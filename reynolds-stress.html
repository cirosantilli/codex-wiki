Reynolds stress (Rij​=ρui′​uj′​​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 291 Articles: 5
Velocity-fluctuation covariances transport mean momentum. For positive wall shear, −u′w′ is the downward momentum-transport magnitude and contributes to the total shear stress. Some stress conventions include the minus sign in the definition of the tensor itself; the momentum equation must use the same convention.
