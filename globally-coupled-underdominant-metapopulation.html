Globally coupled underdominant metapopulation (u˙i​=sui​(1−ui​)(2ui​−1)+σ(uˉ−ui​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Metapopulation
OurBigBook.com Words: 195 Articles: 2
This model couples deterministic underdominant allele-frequency dynamics in equal-sized habitats by global migration. Starting with m pure habitats of one allele and N−m of the other preserves two within-class frequencies x,y, reducing the system to x˙=f(x)−σ(1−q)(x−y), y˙​=f(y)+σq(x−y) with q=m/N. Migration is conservative in the mean, but selection need not conserve global allele frequency.
