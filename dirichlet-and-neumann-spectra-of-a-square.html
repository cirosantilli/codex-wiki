Dirichlet and Neumann spectra of a square (λjk​=j2+k2on [0,π]2)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Helmholtz equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
For −ΔΦ=λΦ on the square, separation of variables gives Dirichlet eigenfunctions sin(jx)sin(ky) with j,k≥1, and Neumann eigenfunctions cos(jx)cos(ky) with j,k≥0. The eigenvalues in both cases are j2+k2, with multiplicity equal to the number of ordered admissible pairs. In particular, an arbitrary expression λ=m2+p2 with integers m,p need not be a Dirichlet eigenvalue when one index is zero. The Neumann zero eigenvalue has the constant eigenfunction, whereas the Dirichlet zero problem has only the zero solution by Dirichlet uniqueness for the Poisson equation.
