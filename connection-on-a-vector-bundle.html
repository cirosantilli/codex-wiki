Connection on a vector bundle (∇)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 640 Articles: 24
A connection is a linear map ∇:Γ(E)→Ω1(B;E) satisfying ∇(fs)=df⊗s+f∇s. In a local frame it has the form d+A for a matrix-valued one-form A.
