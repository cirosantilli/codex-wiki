Ethanol (C2​H5​OH)

 Home Biology Metabolism
OurBigBook.com Tags: Chemical compound Words: 35
Ethanol is the chemical compound produced by the alcoholic fermentation described under Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Its two carbon chemical atoms explain the product bookkeeping there. See NIST Chemistry WebBook: ethanol formula and identity; the Biology 2e section cited under Saccharomyces cerevisiae identifies ethanol as the alcohol product.
