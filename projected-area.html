Projected area (A⊥​=Acosθ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Radiometry Radiance
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
The projected area of a planar patch viewed from a direction is the area of its perpendicular shadow onto a plane normal to that direction. If n and s are its unit normal and viewing unit vector, then A⊥​=A∣n⋅s∣ by the dot product. On the outward side, this is Acosθ. For a rectangle tilted around one edge, the width perpendicular to that edge contracts by cosθ and the edge length stays fixed, proving the formula. At grazing view A⊥​=0; interpret the radiance definition by a limit rather than dividing by zero.
