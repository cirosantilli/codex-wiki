Joint distribution of least-squares and variance estimators (β​⊥σ2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Normal linear model
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
With a full-column-rank n×p design and isotropic normal errors, ordinary least squares gives β​∼Np​(β,σ2(XTX)−1), independent of the residual squared length. Orthogonal design and residual projections of the multivariate normal distribution are independent, and RSS/σ2∼χn−p2​. The maximum likelihood variance estimate is RSS/n, not the unbiased estimate RSS/(n−p).
