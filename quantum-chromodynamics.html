Quantum chromodynamics (QCD)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 79
Quantum chromodynamics is the SU(3)c​ gauge theory of quarks and gluons. At low energies it confines coloured fields into gauge-singlet hadrons.
