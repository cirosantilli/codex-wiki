Radiative envelope

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure Stellar convective stability
OurBigBook.com Words: 368 Articles: 5
A radiative envelope transports most of its energy by radiative diffusion. Its stratified entropy profile gives a different rapid mass-loss response from a convective envelope.
