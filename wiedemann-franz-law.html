Wiedemann-Franz law (Ke​/(σT)=L0​)

 ... Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Thermal conduction Fourier's law Thermal conductivity
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 2
The Wiedemann-Franz law relates the electronic thermal conductivity of a normal degenerate metal to its electrical conductivity:
Ke​=L0​σT,L0​=3e2π2kB2​​.
(11)
An isotropic kinetic theory estimate gives Ke​=ce​vF2​τQ​/3. With electronic heat capacity density ce​=π2nkB2​T/(2EF​), vF2​=2EF​/m∗ and σ=ne2τσ​/m∗, the ratio is L0​τQ​/τσ​. Approximately elastic scattering with a smooth energy dependence gives equal heat and charge relaxation times. Inelastic electron-phonon scattering can make them different; the law also concerns the electronic contribution, not additional lattice heat conduction.[ref]
