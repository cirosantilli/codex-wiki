Singular point of an algebraic variety

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Zariski tangent space Smooth locus of a variety
OurBigBook.com Words: 212 Articles: 4
A point p of an irreducible variety X is singular when dimTp​X>dimX. Equivalently, its local ring is not regular. The singular locus is the complement of the smooth locus.
