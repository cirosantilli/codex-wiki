Huygens-Fresnel principle

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 2
The Huygens-Fresnel principle describes propagation by coherent addition of secondary-wave contributions from a wavefront or aperture. Their complex amplitudes, including relative wave phases, must be summed before calculating light intensity. The Fresnel propagator follows by retaining quadratic propagation wave phase in the paraxial approximation.
