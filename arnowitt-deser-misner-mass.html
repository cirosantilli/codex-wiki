Arnowitt-Deser-Misner mass (MADM​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For an asymptotically flat stationary solution in its asymptotic rest frame, the ADM mass is the gravitational mass measured by the Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy. It is fixed by the asymptotic metric falloff, not by a coordinate parameter's name. If spatial ADM momentum is present, distinguish the asymptotic energy component from the invariant mass obtained from the energy-momentum norm. For a static Schwarzschild-Tangherlini metric in D dimensions with f=1−μ/rD−3, the mass is (D−2)ΩD−2​μ/(16πGD​); the dimensional normalization is also reproduced by the Komar mass normalization in higher dimensions.
