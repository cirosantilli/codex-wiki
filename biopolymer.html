Biopolymer

 Home Chemistry Polymer
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A biopolymer is a polymer produced by a living system, including proteins, nucleic acids and polysaccharides. Local chemical structure, sequence and interactions can give it stiffness or preferred conformations beyond those of an ideal chain. Biopolymer mechanics uses models such as the worm-like chain and the helix-coil transition model to represent those features.
