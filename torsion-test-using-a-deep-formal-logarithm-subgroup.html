Torsion test using a deep formal logarithm subgroup (logF​([N]P)∈NpmZp​)

 ... Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Formal group law Formal logarithm Deep logarithm subgroup of a formal group
OurBigBook.com Words: 121
Let E/Qp​ have good reduction, and choose an integer m>1/(p−1). Its filtration of elliptic-curve points over a local field has a torsion-free group Em​ identified by the formal logarithm with pmZp​. The quotient group G=E(Qp​)/Em​ is finite. For g∈G of order of a group element N, choose a lift P. There is a torsion point of an elliptic curve in this coset exactly when the displayed condition holds. Indeed, it is equivalent to the existence of Q∈Em​ with [N]Q=[N]P. Then P−Q is killed by N; conversely every torsion representative has order N, since Em​ is torsion-free. The logarithmic division and the formal group exponential give the unique such Q. Changing the lift changes the logarithm by an element of NpmZp​, so the criterion is well-defined. This finite-coset test includes residue-characteristic torsion; only the prime-to-p torsion lifts uniquely at good reduction are automatic.
