Cartesian powers of the Cantor set (dimH​Cd=dlog2/log3)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Fractal geometry Self-similarity Cantor set
OurBigBook.com Words: 115
For the middle-third Cantor set C and positive integer d, Cd is compact and totally disconnected. A connected subset projects to a point in each coordinate and is therefore itself a point. At level n, the product has 2dn cubes of side 3−n. These covers give dimension at most dlog2/log3. The product digit probability assigns each cube mass 2−dn; a ball of radius comparable to 3−n meets only a bounded number of cubes, so its mass is at most a constant times rdlog2/log3. The cover-cost argument for Hausdorff dimension proves the matching lower bound. Thus compact totally disconnected Euclidean subsets can have arbitrarily large Hausdorff dimension when the ambient dimension is allowed to increase.
