Polychromatic coloring of integer translates

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Hypergraph Polychromatic coloring of a hypergraph
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For finite S⊂Z, use vertices Z and hyperedges S+t. Independent uniform k-coloring gives missing-color probability at most ke−∣S∣/k, and the dependency graph of events has degree at most ∣S∣(∣S∣−1). For k≥20 and ∣S∣≥6klogk, the symmetric Lovász local lemma condition holds. The compactness extension of the Lovász local lemma gives a coloring in which every translate contains every color.
