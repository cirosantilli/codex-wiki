 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 16
A cubic polynomial in one variable has polynomial degree three. Over a splitting field, its factorization of polynomials contains three polynomial factors of polynomial degree one when multiplicity of a root is counted.
