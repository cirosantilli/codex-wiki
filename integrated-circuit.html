Integrated circuit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 9
An integrated circuit combines interconnected electrical circuit elements in a fabricated structure. A monolithic integrated circuit forms its elements in or on one piece of semiconductor, rather than merely putting separately manufactured components into one enclosure. Transistors supply control in electronic amplifiers; resistors and capacitors can supply bias and timing. Jack St. Clair Kilby's patent describes making such functions from a common semiconductor body with compatible fabrication steps.[ref]
The useful change is to fabricate connections and functions together. Shrinking an enclosure still leaves individual parts to position and join. Integration makes their relative arrangement part of a repeatable fabrication pattern. The distinction is functional as well as geometric: one small piece can implement a whole electrical circuit, while its external terminals expose only the connections needed by its surroundings.
