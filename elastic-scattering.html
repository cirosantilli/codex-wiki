Elastic scattering

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering Scattering amplitude
OurBigBook.com Words: 90 Articles: 2
Elastic scattering preserves the total kinetic energy. For one particle scattered by a fixed target, the incoming and outgoing wavevectors therefore have equal magnitudes.
