Quadratic-mean to L1 density derivative (∥pt​−p−tpg∥1​=o(∣t∣))

Let δt​=pt​​−p​=(t/2)gp​+rt​, with ∥rt​∥2​=o(∣t∣). Since pt​−p=2p​δt​+δt2​, the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality gives ∥pt​−p−tpg∥1​≤2∥rt​∥2​+∥δt​∥22​=o(∣t∣). Thus differentiability in quadratic mean suffices to differentiate bounded density integrals, even when the density itself has no useful pointwise derivative.
