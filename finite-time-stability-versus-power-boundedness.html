Finite-time stability versus power boundedness (supnτ≤T​∥Gh,τn​∥≤CT​)

 ... Analysis Numerical analysis Finite difference Finite difference method von Neumann stability analysis Eigenvalue stability analysis of a finite difference method
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
Stability of the partial differential equation requires a bound uniform over refining meshes and time steps on every fixed physical time interval. This is distinct from demanding an all-time bound for one fixed matrix. A fixed matrix is power bounded exactly when all eigenvalues are in the closed unit disk and its unit-modulus Jordan blocks have size one. A time-step family can nevertheless have harmless polynomial physical-time growth: Gτ​=I+τN with N2=0 gives Gτn​=I+nτN, uniformly bounded for nτ≤T despite its nontrivial unit-modulus Jordan block. Mesh dependence and the scaling of the step must therefore be kept in an eigenvalue argument.
