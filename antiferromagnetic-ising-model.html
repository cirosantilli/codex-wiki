Antiferromagnetic Ising model (J>0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Spin model Ising model
OurBigBook.com Words: 220 Articles: 3
With the convention H=J∑⟨ij⟩​σi​σj​−h∑i​σi​, positive J favors opposite neighboring Ising spins. On a bipartite graph, the Néel order parameter measures opposite sublattice magnetizations; it is distinct from the uniform magnetization. Changing the signs of spins on one sublattice maps the zero-field model to the ferromagnetic Ising model.
