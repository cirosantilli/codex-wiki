For radiative, gas-pressure-supported homologous stars with constant opacity, write ρ=Mb/(4πR3), P=GM2p/(4πR4) and T=μGMp/(RRb). The radiative diffusion in a star equation contains the dimensionless coefficient D=3κ0​R4L/(64π2acμ4G4M3). A common fixed profile fixes D, so L∝M3 and the luminosity of a fixed-mass member is constant. Gravitational energy generation in a homologously contracting ideal-gas star gives R˙=−2ELR2/(3GM2) for a positive profile constant E. Thus R−1=R0−1​+2ELt/(3GM2). For CNO cycle heating ϵ∝ρT16, the stellar nuclear fusion luminosity scales as M18R−19. Equating this with M3 gives RMS​∝M15/19. Neglecting R0−1​, the arrival time is tMS​∝M−34/19, hence a coeval cluster's newly arriving mass scales as t−19/34. All comparisons hold stellar composition, opacity normalization and dimensionless profiles fixed.
