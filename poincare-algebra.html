Poincare algebra

 ... Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz transformation Lorentz group Poincaré group
OurBigBook.com Words: 234 Articles: 5
The Poincare algebra is generated by translations Pμ and Lorentz transformations Mμν. Its mixed commutator is [Mμν,Pρ]=i(ηνρPμ−ημρPν).
