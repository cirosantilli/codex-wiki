Weighted sine transform for half-line drift diffusion (u=eαx/2q)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Diffusion equation Advection-diffusion equation Dirichlet gauge transform for constant drift
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
For qt​=qxx​+αqx​, the exponential change of unknown gives ut​=uxx​−α2u/4. Its Fourier sine transform satisfies a closed scalar evolution with a boundary forcing term. The initial transform uses eαx/2q0​(x); ordinary integrable-transform arguments require sufficient decay of this weighted datum. For q0​=e−ax this requires a>α/2. In contrast, the unweighted sine transform of q couples to its cosine transform through the drift term, so it does not directly diagonalize this half-line problem.
