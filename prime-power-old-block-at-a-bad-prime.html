The normalized Fricke involution acts by ej​↦εer−j​, where WM​f=εf. These two operators act irreducibly: a nonzero invariant subspace contains an eigenvector of Up​; its eigenvalue is ap​ or zero. Either it contains e0​ immediately or applying the reversal and a power of Up​ produces e0​, using ap2​=pk. Reversal then produces er​, and its successive images produce every basis vector.
