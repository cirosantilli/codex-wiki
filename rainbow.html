Rainbow

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Atmospheric optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 72 Articles: 1
A rainbow is an atmospheric optical arc produced by refraction, internal reflection and wave dispersion of light in water droplets. Wavelength-dependent refractive index separates the colours. The primary arc involves one internal reflection, and the secondary arc involves two.
