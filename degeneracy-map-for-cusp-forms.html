Degeneracy map for cusp forms (f(τ)↦f(Dτ))

 ... Number theory Modular function Modular form Fourier expansion of a modular form Cusp form Old subspace of cusp forms
OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 1
If M∣N and D∣N/M, conjugating Γ0​(N) by diag(D,1) gives matrices (ac/D​Dbd​) in Γ0​(M). This proves the weight transformation of f(Dτ). At any rational modular cusp, factor the rational change of variable into an integral cusp representative followed by an upper-triangular rational matrix of positive slope. The original cusp expansion then stays holomorphic and has zero constant term. Thus this map takes cusp forms of level M to those of level N.
