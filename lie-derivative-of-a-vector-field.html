Lie derivative of a vector field (LX​Y=[X,Y])

 ... Fiber bundle Vector bundle Tensor product of vector bundles Tensor field Tensor derivation Lie derivative of a tensor field
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
For vector fields X,Y, the Lie derivative is the Lie bracket of vector fields LX​Y=[X,Y]. Pulling Y back by the local flow of X and differentiating gives dY(X)−dX(Y). No affine connection is needed.
