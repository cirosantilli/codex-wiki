Proper period of a circular Schwarzschild geodesic

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Timelike geodesic effective potential Effective potential for timelike Schwarzschild geodesics Angular velocity of a circular Schwarzschild geodesic
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
Over one circular orbit at radius r>3M, the orbiting clock measures
Δτ=2πMr2(r−3M)​​.
(79)
The factor r−3M combines gravitational and orbital kinematic time dilation.
