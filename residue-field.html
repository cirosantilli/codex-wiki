Residue field (κ(m)=R/m)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Maximal ideal
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 2
The residue field at a maximal ideal m is the quotient field κ(m)=R/m. At a prime ideal p, the residue field is the fraction field of R/p.
