Bell-basis measurement

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bell state
OurBigBook.com Words: 69 Articles: 1
A Bell-basis measurement is the four-outcome projective measurement whose projectors are onto the four Bell states. It jointly measures two qubits and is the sender's measurement in quantum teleportation.
