Stress relaxation

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Stress relaxation is decay of stored stress after a deformation is imposed and then held fixed. It probes a material's memory through its relaxation modulus. An instantaneous generalized Newtonian fluid has no stored shear stress once the shear rate is zero and cannot describe a nontrivial relaxation tail.
