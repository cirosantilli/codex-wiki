Psychoactive drug

 Home Biology Medicine
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 3
A psychoactive drug is a substance that changes mental activity, mood or perception. In population causal inference, its per-user adverse-event risk and its total population harm are distinct: the latter also depends on how many people use it, for how long, and on substitution between substances.
