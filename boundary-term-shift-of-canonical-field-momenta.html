Boundary-term shift of canonical field momenta

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Hamiltonian mechanics Canonical momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
If the spatially integrated Lagrangian changes by dB[Φ]/dt, where B depends on fields and their spatial derivatives but not velocities, the canonical momentum components change by πa​↦πa​+δB/δΦa​. This follows by varying dB/dt and using spatial integration by parts. The canonical one-form changes by the exact variation δB, so the symplectic form and canonical commutation relations are preserved. For the two Feynman gauge Maxwell densities, B=∫A0​∂i​Ai​d3x gives shifts Δπ0=∂i​Ai​ and Δπi=−∂i​A0​. Boundary flux must vanish for this spatial functional calculation.
