Lyapunov exponent (λ=limt→∞​t−1log∥DΦt​v∥)

OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 1
A Lyapunov exponent measures the asymptotic exponential rate of an infinitesimal tangent displacement under a dynamical system, when the displayed limit exists for a nonzero tangent vector v. A positive exponent represents exponential separation along that direction. In a smooth velocity field, the tangent displacement obeys l˙=(∇u)l. Finite-separation growth eventually leaves this linear regime; moments of random separation need not grow at the same rate as typical separation.
