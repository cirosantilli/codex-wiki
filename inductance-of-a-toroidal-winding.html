Inductance of a toroidal winding (L≃μN2A/ℓ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Inductance
OurBigBook.com Words: 347 Articles: 3
For a slender winding around a toroid with N turns, core area A, mean magnetic path length ℓ and approximately constant magnetic permeability μ, Ampère's law gives Hℓ=NI. The linked magnetic flux is NΦ=NμHA, so L=NΦ/I=μN2A/ℓ. This approximation neglects flux leakage and variation across the core and assumes operation below magnetic saturation. With a nonlinear magnetic core, an incremental inductance instead depends on differential permeability at the operating point.
