Quantum bit-flip channel (Ep​(ρ)=(1−p)ρ+pXρX)

 ... Quantum information theory Positive linear map Completely positive map Quantum channel Random unitary channel Pauli channel
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
A bit-flip channel applies the Pauli X gate with probability p and does nothing otherwise. If the classical error outcome is not recorded, its output is the convex mixture Ep​(ρ)=(1−p)ρ+pXρX, not a coherent superposition of the two alternatives.
