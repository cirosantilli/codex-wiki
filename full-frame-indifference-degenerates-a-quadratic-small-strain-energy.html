Full frame indifference degenerates a quadratic small-strain energy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
Write a quadratic small-strain energy as W(F)=Q(sym(F−I)) with Q homogeneous of degree two. If it were fully frame indifferent, the rotation R=diag(1,−1,−1) would have zero energy. Quadratic homogeneity would then make every Fs​=I+s(R−I) have zero energy. At s=1/4, Fs​=diag(1,1/2,1/2) has determinant 1/4, yet zero energy. Thus a nondegenerate linear elasticity energy cannot also be used as a fully objective finite-deformation energy. Its invariance concerns infinitesimal rotations around the reference state.
