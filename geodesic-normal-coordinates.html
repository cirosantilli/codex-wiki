Geodesic normal coordinates

OurBigBook.com Words: 172 Articles: 3
Choose an orthonormal basis of Tp​M and use its linear coordinates on a neighborhood of zero on which expp​ is a diffeomorphism. Transporting these coordinates through expp​ gives geodesic normal coordinates centered at p.
