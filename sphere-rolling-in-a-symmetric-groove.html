Sphere rolling in a symmetric groove (acm​=gsinθ/[1+2/(5sin2(ϕ/2))])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rolling acceleration with rotational inertia
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 1
A sphere of radius a touching both faces of a symmetric groove of opening angle ϕ rotates instantaneously about the line joining its contacts. The centre is a distance h=asin(ϕ/2) from this line, so rolling without slipping means v=hω. For a homogeneous sphere, K=mv2/2+Iω2/2=mv2[1+2/(5sin2(ϕ/2))]/2. Differentiating this kinetic energy with respect to distance down an incline gives the displayed acceleration. Both contact forces do no work for fixed faces and no slip.
