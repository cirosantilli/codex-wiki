Prime ideal factorization

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Algebraic integer Ring of integers
OurBigBook.com Words: 126 Articles: 2
Every nonzero ideal of the ring of integers of a number field factors uniquely as a finite product of nonzero prime ideals. Thus each x∈K× has integer valuations ordp​(x), almost all zero, determined by the factorization of its principal ideal.
