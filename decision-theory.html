Decision theory

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 257 Articles: 5
The study of choices under uncertainty and consistency conditions on preferences. In a quantum application, recovering Born rule weights from preferences requires specifying how physically equivalent games and irrelevant branch refinements affect value; it is not a consequence of decoherence alone.
