Lévy characterization of Brownian motion

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
A continuous local martingale M with M0​=0 is standard Brownian motion exactly when its quadratic variation satisfies [M]t​=t.
The characterization asserts that a continuous local martingale starting at zero with quadratic variation t is Brownian motion.
