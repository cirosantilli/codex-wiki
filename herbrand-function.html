Herbrand function (φL/K​(s)=∫0s​[G0​:Gt​]−1dt)

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Ramification group Lower ramification numbering
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 2
The Herbrand function is a continuous strictly increasing piecewise linear function on the nonnegative reals. Its inverse is denoted ψL/K​. It reparametrizes the lower ramification numbering to the upper ramification numbering.
