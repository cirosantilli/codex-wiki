Countability of elastic eigenfrequencies

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Normal mode
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
Distinct squared traction-free eigenfrequencies in a bounded lossless elastic body have eigenfunctions orthogonal in L2(D,ρdV)3, by the Betti identity for elastodynamic fields. Normalize one field from each eigenspace. This is an orthonormal set in a separable Hilbert space, so it is countable: disjoint balls of radius less than 1/2​ around its unit vectors must contain distinct points of a countable dense set. Positive and negative frequency partners add at most a factor of two. This argument proves countability without requiring a separate compact-resolvent proof.
