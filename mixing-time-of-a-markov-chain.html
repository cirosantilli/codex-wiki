Mixing time of a Markov chain (tmix​(ε))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 557 Articles: 14
The ε-mixing time is the least t for which every initial state has total variation distance at most ε from the invariant distribution after t steps.
