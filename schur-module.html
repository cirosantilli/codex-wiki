Schur module (Dλ​(V))

 ... Lie theory Linear algebraic group Rational representation Rational representation of the general linear group Polynomial representation of the general linear group Schur functor
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 1
A Schur module is the value of a Schur functor on a vector space. Over the complex numbers it is either zero or an irreducible polynomial representation of the general linear group. Its character is the Schur polynomial sλ​ in the eigenvalues, and its highest weight is λ padded with zeros.
