Involutive Metropolis proposal

OurBigBook.com Words: 45
An involutive Metropolis proposal uses a deterministic bijection S satisfying S2=I. For a volume-preserving S and target probability density function ρ, accept S(z) with probability min(1,ρ(S(z))/ρ(z)). The identity ρ(z)α(z)=min(ρ(z),ρ(S(z))) and a change of variables under S prove detailed balance. A non-unit Jacobian determinant must be included when volume is not preserved.
