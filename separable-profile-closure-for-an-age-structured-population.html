For ni​(a,t)=Ni​(t)fi​(a) with ∫fi​=1, integrating the age-structured population equation replaces its exit integral by Λi​Ni​. For one-to-one transition from stage one to stage two and two-to-one reproduction from stage two to stage one, the population matrix is (−Λ1​Λ1​​2Λ2​−Λ2​​). This follows by integrating the age derivative and substituting the incoming boundary conditions. Exact time-independent positive profiles also force a constant stage ratio and common exponential growth through those boundary conditions. Thus the constant-coefficient closure is not an exact transient description for arbitrary age-dependent hazard functions and arbitrary initial conditions; constant hazard functions are an important exception for the integrated populations.
