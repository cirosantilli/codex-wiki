Gyroscope vector geometry (τO​=dLO​/dt)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Momentum Angular momentum Gyroscope
OurBigBook.com Words: 290
Choose a fixed support point O and nonrotating axes in an inertial frame. All angular momenta, torques, and moments of inertia below refer to O. Let e point along the axle from O toward the centre of mass, and let z point vertically upward. Write θ for their angle. For a rapidly spinning symmetric top, the dominant angular momentum is LO​≃Ls​e, where Ls​=I3​ω3​ is signed and I3​ is the axial principal moment of inertia. The sign of the axial angular velocity ω3​ follows the right-hand rule.
With mass M, centre of mass distance d, and constant gravitational acceleration g, the gravitational force and its torque are
r=de,Fg​=−Mgz,τO​=r×Fg​=Mgdz×e.
(15)
An ideal support applies a force at O, so its lever arm and torque about O vanish. At the instant the axle lies in the xz plane with a positive x component, the gravitational torque points along +y. It adds dLO​=τO​dt toward +y, rather than along the downward force. The dot product identity d∣LO​∣2/dt=2LO​⋅τO​ shows why perpendicular torque changes direction without instantly changing magnitude. This vector balance and the slow-turning relation τO​=Ω×LO​ are developed in Chapter 20: Rotation in space (sections 20-2 and 20-3, equations 20.13–20.15).
For steady precession about the vertical, Ω=Ωz and de/dt=Ωz×e. Equating the two cross products gives
Ω≃Ls​Mgd​=I3​ω3​Mgd​,∣τO​∣=Mgdsinθ.
(16)
The angular momentum tip traces a circle of radius ∣Ls​∣sinθ: its sideways speed is ∣ΩLs​∣sinθ. Both that radius and the gravitational lever arm contain sinθ, explaining its cancellation for 0<θ<π. Exactly vertical alignment has zero gravitational torque and no distinguishable azimuthal precession to infer by this cancellation. Positive Ls​ gives positive precession, counterclockwise viewed from above; reversing the spin reverses the slow precession. [ref]
