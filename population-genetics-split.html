Population genetics

 Home Biology Genetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 60
Population genetics studies the frequencies of alleles and genotypes in populations, and their changes through inheritance, mutation, selection, migration and random sampling. Kingman's coalescent describes a neutral sample's ancestry backward in time.
