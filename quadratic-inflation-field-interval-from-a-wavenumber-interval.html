Quadratic-inflation field interval from a wavenumber interval (ΔΦ/MPl​≃2Δlogk/4N+2​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic inflation Slow-roll approximation Quadratic-potential slow-roll solution
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For a quadratic inflaton potential ending at the leading estimate ϵV​=1, Φ/MPl​=4N+2​. Cosmological horizon exit gives dlogk/dN=−1+ϵH​. A narrow logarithmic interval therefore probes a field span 2Δlogk/(4N+2​(1−ϵH​)), to the accuracy of the slow-roll approximation. At sixty e-folds, five logarithmic wavenumber units probe about 0.64MPl​ around a background field near 15.6MPl​.
