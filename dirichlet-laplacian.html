Dirichlet Laplacian (−ΔD​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Laplace operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 163 Articles: 2
The nonnegative Dirichlet Laplacian is the self-adjoint realization of −divgrad with zero boundary values. On a bounded domain, its weak form is the Dirichlet energy on H01​. For polygonal domains the weak operator domain is preferable to a requirement of smoothness at corners.
