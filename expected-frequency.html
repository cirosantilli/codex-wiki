Expected frequency (Ej​=npj​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Descriptive statistics Frequency table
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
The expected value of the number of observations in a category under a specified statistical model. For n observations with common category probability pj​, linearity of expectation gives Ej​=npj​; the calculation does not itself require independence. In a contingency table, fitting independence gives Eij​=ni+​n+j​/n. These are model-based counts, which may be fractional, rather than an additional set of observed responses. Pearson's chi-squared test compares observed counts with their corresponding expected frequencies.
