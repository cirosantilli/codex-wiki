A uniform rigid body disk of mass m and radius a moves in a frictionless plane. Let N≥3 identical springs attach equally spaced rim points to fixed anchors in their radial directions. The equilibrium spring length is l, natural length l0​<l, and spring constant k. Write the small centre displacement as q and the rotation as θ. For a spring with radial and tangential unit vectors n,t, its length is
s=l−n⋅q+2a​θ2+2l(t⋅q+aθ)2​+O((∣q∣+∣θ∣)3).

The equally spaced directions satisfy ∑n=∑t=0 and ∑nnT=∑ttT=(N/2)I. Summing the spring energies gives
V2​=4Nk​(1+τ/l)∣q∣2+2lNkτa(a+l)​θ2.

The translational normal modes are degenerate; rotation is independent. At zero prestretch the rotational frequency vanishes at quadratic order, and finite-angle spring stretching first contributes at higher order.
