Open neighbourhood (x∈U∈T)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Open set Neighbourhood (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
An open neighbourhood of a point x in a topological space (X,T) is an open set U containing x. A general neighbourhood need only contain an open neighbourhood; it need not itself be open. A finite intersection of open neighbourhoods of the same point is again an open neighbourhood, by the topology axioms.
