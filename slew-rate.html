Slew rate (max∣dvo​/dt∣)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Electronic amplifier Operational amplifier
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
The slew rate bounds the large-signal output-voltage change per unit time. It is distinct from a small-signal gain-bandwidth product. A sinusoidal output of amplitude Vp​ and angular frequency ω needs ωVp​ below the available slew rate to avoid this source of distortion.
