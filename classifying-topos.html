Classifying topos (Geom(F,ET​)≃T-Mod(F))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Elementary topos Grothendieck topos
OurBigBook.com Words: 817 Articles: 13
A topos classifies a geometric theory when geometric morphisms into it from any Grothendieck topos correspond naturally to internal models of that theory. Pulling back one generic model gives the corresponding model. For a finitary algebraic theory, the classifying topos is the covariant functor category on finitely presented models.
