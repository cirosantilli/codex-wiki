Exponential concentration of terminal stochastic attrition survivors (P(∣XN−1−α2​∣>δ)≤2e−(1−α2)δ2N/256)

OurBigBook.com Words: 108
Start the symmetric stochastic attrition process at (N,⌊αN⌋), with 0<α<1, and let XN be the final first-population size divided by N. The bound holds for all sufficiently large N, depending on α,δ>0. At absorption, one coordinate is zero. If the first survives, the terminal squared difference is N2(XN)2; if it is extinguished, that difference is nonpositive. In either case the indicated deviation forces an error of at least 1−α2​δN2 from the limiting squared invariant. The initial rounding error and total conditional drift are only O(N). Apply Azuma-Hoeffding inequality to the centered squared-population near-invariant for stochastic attrition, using its 2N increments bounded by 4N, to obtain the displayed exponent.
