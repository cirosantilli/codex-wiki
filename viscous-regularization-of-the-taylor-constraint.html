Viscous regularization of the Taylor constraint (TL​(s)+Tν​(s)=0)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Magnetostrophic balance Taylor constraint
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
With small viscosity, the azimuthal cylindrical balance permits Lorentz force torque to be cancelled by viscous stress. For ordinary no-slip rotating boundaries an Ekman layer gives stress of order ρΩLUE​, larger than the order-E bulk viscous correction at fixed velocity. Thus bounded small-E flows approach a magnetostrophic Taylor state but need not satisfy its constraint exactly at finite viscosity. An order-one uncompensated magnetic torque would require a divergent flow as E→0, invalidating the assumed small-inertia balance. Stress-free boundaries remove the leading no-slip friction and require their own torque/regularity conditions.
