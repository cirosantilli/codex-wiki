Radial epicyclic frequency (κr​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 426 Articles: 10
The radial epicyclic frequency describes small radial oscillations about a circular orbit. For specific angular momentum h(r), κr2​=r−3d(h2)/dr.
