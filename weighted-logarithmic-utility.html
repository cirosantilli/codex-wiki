Weighted logarithmic utility (∑r​nr​logxr​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Proportional fairness
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The utility ∑r​nr​logxr​ sums logarithmic utilities over individual flows. Its first-order condition gives proportional fairness. Its negative is a strictly convex function on active flow rates, giving uniqueness of those rates on a convex set of feasible allocations.
