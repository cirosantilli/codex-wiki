Muon storage-ring time-dilation experiment

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz factor Time dilation
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Accelerator-produced muons circulating in a magnetic storage ring allow a direct comparison between their measured laboratory mean lifetime and γ times the rest lifetime. The 1977 experiment at γ=29.33 measured positive- and negative-muon lifetimes near 64.4μs, consistent with time dilation. This is a controlled complement to atmospheric survival measurements, with different systematic effects.[ref]
