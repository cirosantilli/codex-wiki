Uniform ball sampling by Brownian stopping (WτR​​∼Unif(B(0,r)))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Brownian exit time
OurBigBook.com Words: 247 Articles: 2
For standard Brownian motion started at zero, choose an independent radius R with P(R≤s)=(s/r)n, 0≤s≤r, and stop on first reaching that radius. Rotational invariance of Brownian motion makes the conditional exit position uniform on each sphere. Mixing with radius density nsn−1/rn gives the uniform probability distribution on the ball. In dimension one, the conditional sign has probabilities 1/2,1/2. The stopped point samples volume measure even though each conditional exit law is supported on a boundary.
