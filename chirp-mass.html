Chirp mass (M=(m1​m2​)3/5/(m1​+m2​)1/5)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Gravitational wave Newtonian binary chirp
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
The chirp mass is the combination of two component masses controlling the leading Newtonian binary chirp. Inverting its frequency equation gives
M=Gc3​[965​π−8/3f−11/3f˙​]3/5.
(9)
Use f in hertz and f˙​ in hertz per second to obtain kilograms. For equal component masses m, M=m/21/5. A stipulated 20+20 solar mass system therefore has M≈17.411 solar masses. In a cosmological observation the time and frequency measured at the detector instead yield (1+z)Msource​, where z is redshift; phase evolution alone does not independently determine the source mass and redshift.
