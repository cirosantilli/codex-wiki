Supermultiplet

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry
OurBigBook.com Words: 810 Articles: 18
A supermultiplet is an irreducible representation of the Super-Poincaré algebra. Conserved supercharges pair its bosonic and fermionic states at equal four-momentum.
