Bernoulli saturated log-likelihood (ℓsat​=0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Saturated statistical model
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For individual Bernoulli observations, the statistical saturated model assigns one independent probability to each response. Each likelihood factor is at most one and is exactly one at πi​=yi​. Hence the maximum log-likelihood is zero, with 0log0=0. This boundary maximum is attained when probabilities zero and one are allowed; restricting them to the open interval gives the same supremum.
