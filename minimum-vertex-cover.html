Minimum vertex cover

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Vertex cover
OurBigBook.com Words: 315 Articles: 1
A minimum vertex cover is a vertex cover of smallest cardinality among all vertex covers of the same finite simple graph. The optimum size is the vertex cover number. By the complement of a set correspondence, a minimum vertex cover leaves a largest possible independent set unselected.
Minimum size is stronger than merely being unable to remove a selected vertex. In the star graph K1,3​, the three leaves form a vertex cover from which no vertex can be removed: each leaf covers its own edge. Nevertheless, the centre alone is a minimum vertex cover of size one. Such an inclusion-minimal vertex cover can therefore be much larger than a minimum vertex cover.
