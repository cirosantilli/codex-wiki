Dirac valleys in graphene (Kτ​=(τ4π/(33​b),0),τ=±1)

 ... Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Carbon Graphene Nearest-neighbour model of graphene
OurBigBook.com Words: 293 Articles: 3
At either displayed wave vector, the three complex hopping amplitudes add to zero: 1+2cos(2π/3)=0. Therefore the energy bands touch. There are two inequivalent electronic valleys; other equivalent corners are related by reciprocal-lattice translations. Setting k=Kτ​+q and retaining first-order terms gives
Hτ​(q)≃ℏvF​(τqx​σx​+qy​σy​),vF​=2ℏ3tb​,E±​≃±ℏvF​∣q∣,
(23)
where σx​,σy​ are Pauli matrices. For example, at K+​, f≃−3b(qx​−iqy​)/2. The matrix has the form of a massless two-dimensional Dirac Hamiltonian. Here vF​ is a material-model velocity, rather than the vacuum speed of light; this description does not change an electron's vacuum rest mass. It describes the dispersion relation of electronic excitations near a band crossing.
