This implicit family includes y=0 and the branch y=−cotx when C=0. Where the coefficient of y′ is nonzero, its differentiated form directly recovers the original equation. A useful equivalent relation is ycosx+y2sinx=Ccosx. At cosx=0, the original equation requires y=0; continuation there must be checked in that original equation rather than justified by the singular integrating factor. In particular the zero solution extends everywhere, and y=−cotx extends smoothly across the cosine zeros where it is defined.
