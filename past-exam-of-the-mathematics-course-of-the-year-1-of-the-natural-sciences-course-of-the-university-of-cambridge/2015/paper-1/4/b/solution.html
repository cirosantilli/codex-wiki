Within the requested interval, the function is symmetric about x=π/2 because sin(π−x)=sinx. Its derivative cotx is positive before π/2, zero there, and negative afterwards. The negative second derivative shows that this is a strictly concave function. Consequently its unique maximum is (π/2,0). It tends to −∞ at both ends, giving vertical asymptotes x=0 and x=π.
