Writing r2=x2+y2+z2, the gradient is v=2er2(x,y,z). Its divergence, equivalently the Laplacian of ϕ, is
∇⋅v=(6+4r2)er2.
(18)
On the unit sphere this scalar is the constant 10e. The lower hemisphere has surface area 2π, so
∫S​(∇⋅v)dS=20πe​.
(19)
This is a scalar surface integral of the divergence, not the electric- or vector-field flux integral. There is no normal component to insert.
