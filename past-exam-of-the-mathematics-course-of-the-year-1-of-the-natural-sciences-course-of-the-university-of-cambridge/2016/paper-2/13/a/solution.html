Take a>0 for the sketch; changing its sign rotates the picture by π. The parametric curve passes through (0,0) at t=0 and through (3a/2,3a/2) at t=1. As t→±∞ it returns towards the origin, since x∼3a/t2 and y∼3a/t. The positive-t branch makes a loop in the first quadrant, leaving the origin tangent to the positive x axis and returning tangent to the positive y axis.
For a more precise sketch, differentiation gives
x′(t)=(1+t3)23a(1−2t3)​,y′(t)=(1+t3)23at(2−t3)​.
(38)
The loop has its largest x at t=2−1/3 and largest y at t=21/3. Their coordinates are a(22/3,21/3) and its reflection in y=x.
The parameter value t=−1 is excluded. Cancelling the common factor in the sum gives
x+y=t2−t+13at​⟶−a,x−y∼−t+12a​.
(39)
Thus the asymptote is x+y+a=0​. As t→−1−, x→+∞ and y→−∞; as t→−1+, x→−∞ and y→+∞. The branch t<−1 approaches the origin from the fourth quadrant as t→−∞, while −1<t<0 connects the other infinite branch to the origin through the second quadrant.
This is the folium of Descartes. If a=0, the parametrization degenerates to the origin for every defined parameter value and there is no nontrivial loop.
