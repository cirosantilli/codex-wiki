The first partial derivatives factor as
gx​=4x(2+x2−3y2),gy​=4y(2+y2−3x2).
(29)
If either coordinate vanishes, the other equation forces the other coordinate to vanish as well. If both are nonzero, subtracting the two bracket equations gives x2=y2, and then x2=y2=1. Thus the complete list of stationary points is (0,0) and the four points (±1,±1).
The Hessian matrix is
Hg​=(8+12x2−12y2−24xy​−24xy8+12y2−12x2​).
(30)
At the origin it is 8I, which is a positive-definite matrix. At the four other points its eigenvalues are 32 and −16, independently of the signs. The second-derivative test therefore gives
(0,0): strict local minimum, g=0;(±1,±1): saddle points, g=4.​
(31)
There are no local maxima. The minimum at the origin is not global, since g(t,t)=8t2−4t4→−∞ as ∣t∣→∞.
