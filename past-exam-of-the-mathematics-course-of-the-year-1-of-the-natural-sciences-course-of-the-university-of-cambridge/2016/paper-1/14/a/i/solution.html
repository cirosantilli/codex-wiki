By the spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices, choose the normalized eigenvectors to form an orthonormal basis. With Pn​=en​enT​, orthonormality gives Pn​Pm​=δnm​Pn​ and ∑n​Pn​=I. For every vector x, expand x=∑n​(enT​x)en​ and apply A:
Ax=∑n​λn​en​(enT​x).
(41)
Since this equality holds for every x, the spectral decomposition is
A=n=1∑N​λn​en​enT​.​
(42)
Each Pn​ is the rank-one orthogonal projection onto its eigenvector direction. In a degenerate eigenspace, normalized eigenvectors must be chosen mutually orthogonal; normalization alone does not guarantee this resolution of the identity.
