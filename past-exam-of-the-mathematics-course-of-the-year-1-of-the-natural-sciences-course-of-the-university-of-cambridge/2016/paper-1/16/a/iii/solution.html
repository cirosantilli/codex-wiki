The graph of the positive-delay probability density function is a horizontal segment at height 1/R from s=0 to r, followed by zero from r to R. Values at individual endpoints do not affect probabilities. The separate atom 1−r/R at zero is not a finite-height part of this density graph.
The companion cumulative distribution function in the figure is FS​(s)=0 for s<0, FS​(s)=1−r/R+s/R for 0≤s<r, and FS​(s)=1 for s≥r. Its jump at zero displays the discrete part without confusing density height with probability mass.
