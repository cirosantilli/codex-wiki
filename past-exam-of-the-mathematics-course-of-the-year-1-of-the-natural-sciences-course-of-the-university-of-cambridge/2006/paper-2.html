The commuting plane reflections criterion follows: the operator vanishes if the normals are orthogonal, or if they are parallel. Conversely, if c=a⋅b=0 and the operator vanishes, applying baT−abT to a gives b−ca=0, proving parallelism.

Thus the level sets of a linear numerator over a radial quadratic are circles centred on y=x, real only for 0<∣h∣≤1/2​. The extreme levels shrink to points. The zero contour is the straight line x+y=0, separating positive and negative heights; near it, small nonzero levels are large circles. Heights tend to zero at infinity.
