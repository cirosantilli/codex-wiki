Under a uniform shuffle, every unordered five-card hand is equally likely, giving (552​) possible hands. Choose the triple's rank in 13 ways and its suits in (34​) ways; then choose a different pair rank in 12 ways and its suits in (24​) ways. Each full house determines these choices uniquely, so there is no further ordering factor. Therefore
N=13(34​)12(24​)=3744,P=(552​)3744​.​
(51)
The count uses binomial coefficients because the order of cards within a hand is irrelevant.
