For x>−1, this bounds its absolute value by ∣x∣tmin(x,0), which is integrable on (0,1). It also supplies the domination used in part (i).
For x=−1, the integrand is negative and behaves as −1/[t∣lnt∣] near zero; its improper integral diverges.
