There are ten balls of each colour. Under uniform sampling without replacement, all (1220​) subsets of twelve labelled balls are equally likely. A favourable subset chooses four of the ten white balls and eight of the ten black balls, giving (410​)(810​) choices. The hypergeometric distribution therefore gives
P(four white)=(1220​)(410​)(810​)​.​
(74)
Thus the requested integers are N1​=N2​=10, m1​=4, m2​=8​, with N=20 and k=12.
