Compare the positive terms with those of the harmonic series:
1/n(n2+2n)/(n3+3n2+1)​=n3+3n2+1n3+2n2​⟶1.
(97)
By the limit comparison test, the given series has the same convergence behaviour as ∑1/n, which diverges. Thus the series diverges to +∞.
