The cobweb plot uses the curve v=2+u​ and the diagonal v=u. From a point on the diagonal, a vertical step to the curve computes the next iterate; a horizontal step returns its value to the diagonal.
For 0≤u<2, (2+u)−u2=(2−u)(1+u)>0, so u<2+u​<2. Thus a start below 2 gives a strictly increasing sequence bounded above by 2. For u>2 the inequalities reverse to 2<2+u​<u, giving a strictly decreasing sequence bounded below by 2. Both types of staircase approach the same intersection.
