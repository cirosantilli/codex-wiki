The curve has x=0 and can be written y=1/x−x. It is a hyperbola with vertical asymptote x=0 and oblique asymptote y=−x, crossing the horizontal axis at (±1,0). It is symmetric under (x,y)↦(−x,−y).
On each branch dy/dx=−1/x2−1<0. For x>0, the branch descends from +∞ as x↓0, passes through (1,0), and approaches y=−x from above as x→+∞. The negative branch follows by the central symmetry.
