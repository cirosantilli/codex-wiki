A constant real part Relnz=L means ln∣z∣=L, so its level sets are the concentric circles
x2+y2=e2L,L∈R.​
(28)
Their radii are strictly positive. The real part is independent of the logarithm's branch, so there is no branch ambiguity in these circles.
For Re(1/z)=K, the equation is x=K(x2+y2). If K=0, complete the square to obtain
(x−2K1​)2+y2=4K21​,(x,y)=(0,0).​
(29)
These circles have centres on the real axis and are tangent to the imaginary axis at the excluded origin. Positive K puts the circle to the right, negative K to the left. The separate case K=0 gives the punctured imaginary axis x=0, y=0​. The sketch uses an open marker at the origin to emphasize that 1/z is never defined there.
