Because the exponential function is strictly increasing, the level sets f=L>0 are exactly those of G=lnL. For a=1 they satisfy
y2=2lnL−x2−2x4​.
(57)
There are no contours for L<1, the level L=1 is the origin, and L>1 gives nested closed ovals symmetric about both axes. Their stronger fourth-power growth in x makes them relatively narrower horizontally as the level rises.
For a=−1, the contour equation becomes
y2=2lnL−x2+2x4​.
(58)
The central local minimum is surrounded by closed ovals for 1<L<e1/4, accompanied by outer open branches. At L=e1/4, the level passes through the two saddle points (±1,0) and changes its connectivity. Levels L<1 have only outer branches, while levels above the saddle value have an upper and a lower branch. The sketch is restricted to the requested square; labels give G=lnf, so they identify the corresponding contours of f without exponentially large labels.
