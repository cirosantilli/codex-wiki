A probability density function must be nonnegative. The printed condition s=0 is therefore too weak: for s<0 the given prefactor makes the function negative everywhere. The location-scale form of the logistic distribution requires s>0, which is assumed in the remaining parts.
Set t=(x−μ)/(2s), so dx=2sdt. The normalization of a probability distribution follows from the antiderivative of the squared hyperbolic secant:
∫−∞∞​f(x)dx=21​∫−∞∞​sech2tdt=21​[tanht]−∞∞​=1​.

For completeness, the same expression with s<0 integrates to −1, since the transformed limits reverse. If a signed scale were desired, replacing its prefactor by 1/(4∣s∣) would give a valid density, but that is a correction to the printed formula.
