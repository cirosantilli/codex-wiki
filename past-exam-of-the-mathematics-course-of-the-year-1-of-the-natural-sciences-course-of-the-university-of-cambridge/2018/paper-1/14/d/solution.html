Evaluate the Hessian matrix at the five stationary points. At the origin it is
H(0,0)=(−2−4​−4−2​),

whose eigenvalues are −6 and 2, so the origin is a saddle point. At the two points with x=y=±3​/2,
H=e−3/2(102​210​),
(51)
whose eigenvalues are 12e−3/2 and 8e−3/2. Both are positive, so these are strict local minima. At the two points with x=−y=±1/2,
H=e−1/2(−6−2​−2−6​),
(52)
whose eigenvalues are −8e−1/2 and −4e−1/2. Both are negative, so these are strict local maxima. In summary,
(0,0): saddle;x=y=±3​/2: minima;x=−y=±1/2: maxima.​
