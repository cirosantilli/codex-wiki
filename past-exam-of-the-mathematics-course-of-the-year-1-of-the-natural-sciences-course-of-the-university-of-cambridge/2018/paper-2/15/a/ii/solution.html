The characteristic polynomial factors as (r+1)(r+2), so the homogeneous solution is Ae−x+Be−2x. The forcing e−x already occurs in that solution, so a constant multiple of e−x cannot be a particular solution. Use yp​=Cxe−x instead. Its derivatives are C(1−x)e−x and C(x−2)e−x, so substitution gives Ce−x and fixes C=1. Thus
y=Ae−x+Be−2x+xe−x.​
(52)
The extra factor of x accounts for the repeated forcing exponent in the method of undetermined coefficients.
