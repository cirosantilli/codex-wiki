At μ=−1, c2​=−c1​, while c1​ and c3​ are independent. Their span is a plane. A solution requires b to lie in that plane, so every scalar triple product formed from two columns and b must vanish. The product with c1​,c2​ vanishes identically; the other two give the plane condition.
Explicitly, c1​×c3​=(−7,1,3), so
[c1​,c3​,b]=(−7,1,3)⋅(1,λ,0)=λ−7.
(68)
Thus λ=7 is necessary and, since the two columns span the plane, sufficient. The equations then give x−y=1 and z=2, so
λ=7,r=(1,0,2)T+t(1,1,0)T,t∈R.​
(69)
The solution is an affine line parallel to the homogeneous null space from part (c), obtained by intersecting the compatible constraint planes. Every point on it is nonzero because z=2.
