Differentiate the Airy ordinary differential equation y′′=sy to obtain y(3)=y+sy′. Combining part (a)'s derivatives yields
ut​+uxxx​=[ata−1+b3at4a][y(s)+sy′(s)].
(61)
To make this vanish for an arbitrary Airy profile, the time powers must agree: a−1=4a, giving a=−1/3. Their common coefficient must then vanish, giving −1/3+b−1=0, hence b=3. Therefore
a=−31​,b=3,u(x,t)=t−1/3y((3t)1/3x​).​
(62)
For t>0 the two terms cancel exactly:
ut​=−31​t−4/3(y+sy′),uxxx​=31​t−4/3(y+sy′).
(63)
This is an Airy similarity solution of the linear dispersive equation. The Airy equation here is the linear partial differential equation ut​+uxxx​=0, the reduced equation specified in the question; it is distinct from the profile's Airy ordinary differential equation. The construction works for every linear combination of its two independent profiles, including zero. No condition at t=0 is supplied, and this singular time is excluded. With real cube roots the final formula can also be used separately on t<0.
