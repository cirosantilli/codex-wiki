Orient the three faces outward from the tetrahedron. The two coordinate-plane faces have normals parallel to −ex​ or −ey​, so their flux of the curl is zero. On the inclined face z=1−x−y, its upward vector area is (1,1,1)dxdy, with x,y≥0, x+y≤1. Thus the direct surface integral is
