Each additional pair of independent shots resolves the tie with probability r=2p(1−p), since exactly one team must score. It preserves the tie with probability q=p2+(1−p)2=1−r. Finishing after 10+2k+2 shots means an initial tie, then k tied pairs, then a decisive pair. Independence and geometric continuation after an initial tie give
Pr(N=12+2k)=α[p2+(1−p)2]k2p(1−p),k=0,1,….​

(1−α)+∑k=0∞​αqkr=1−α+1−qαr​=1.
