The original PDF requires B itself to be an orthogonal skew-symmetric matrix; it does not require C to be diagonal. A real two-dimensional skew-symmetric matrix has the form B=(0−u​u0​). The orthogonal matrix condition gives u2=1, so choose u=1. Then C=BTAB is forced:
B=(0−1​10​),C=(−4−3​−34​).​
(82)
Direct multiplication verifies BT=−B, BTB=I and BCBT=A. Choosing −B gives the same C. Replacing B by a matrix of eigenvectors to make C diagonal would generally fail the printed antisymmetry requirement.
