The three components of the curl cancel pairwise:
∇×F=(1−1,1−1,1−1)=0.
(97)
The domain is all of R3, which is simply connected, so this is consistent with a conservative vector field. To construct its scalar potential, integrate Φx​=y+z to obtain Φ=x(y+z)+h(y,z). Matching Φy​=x+z gives hy​=z, hence h=yz+k(z). Finally Φz​=x+y forces k′(z)=0. Consequently
Φ(x,y,z)=xy+xz+yz+C,F=∇Φ.​
(98)
Every scalar potential differs by a constant on this connected domain. The endpoint values differ by one, agreeing with the line integral in part (a).
