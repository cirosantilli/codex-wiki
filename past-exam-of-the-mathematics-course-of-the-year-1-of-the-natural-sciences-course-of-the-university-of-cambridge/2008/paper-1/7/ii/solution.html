The domain is x>0, and xlogx→0 from below as x↓0. Its derivative is 1+logx, so it decreases up to x=e−1 and then increases, with its unique global minimum at (e−1,−e−1). It crosses the horizontal axis at (1,0) and tends to +∞ as x→∞. Its second derivative is 1/x>0, so it is strictly convex.
