For 0<c<1, the contours are ellipses centred at (0,1/c), shrinking to the maximum as c↑1. The zero contour is the parabola from part (c). For −1<c<0, the contours are vertical-opening hyperbolas. At c=−1 they degenerate into the intersecting straight lines, and for −2<c<−1 they are horizontal-opening hyperbolas. These descriptions specify the remaining contour families and their symmetry about the y axis. The lower bound −2 is approached as ∣x∣→∞ at fixed y but is never attained.
