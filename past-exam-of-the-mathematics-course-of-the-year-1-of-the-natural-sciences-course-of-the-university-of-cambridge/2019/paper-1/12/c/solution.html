In the x,z plane, the embankment lies above the parabola z=Hx2/L2 and below z=H. The bore has circular cross-section
D={(x,z):x2+(z−D/2)2≤D2/4}.
(33)
It touches the valley floor at the origin. To check that this entire disk lies inside the projected embankment, its equation gives x2≤Dz−z2≤Dz. Since HD/L2<1, it follows that Hx2/L2≤z, with strict inequality away from the bottom contact. Also z≤D<H. Thus no circular segment needs to be subtracted from the tunnel section.
For each (x,z) in the disk, the length of bore inside the material is the local width 2b(2−z/H). The required volume integral is therefore
Vcut​=∬D​2b(2−Hz​)dxdz.
(34)
The disk has area A=πD2/4 and centroid height D/2, so ∬D​zdxdz=AD/2. Applying the volume of a solid with affine thickness formula gives
Vcut​=2bA(2−2HD​)=πbD2(1−4HD​).​
(35)
The width varies along the circular section; multiplying its area by the top width alone would miss this variation.
