Standard concentration (c∘)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Chemical potential Chemical equilibrium Standard state
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The reference concentration c∘ used to make concentration activities dimensionless. Changing this reference changes the numerical dimensionless equilibrium constant when the reaction changes the total number of solute or gas molecules.
