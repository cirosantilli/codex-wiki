Nobel Lecture by Alan Hodgkin, delivered 11 December 1963. The lecture delivery date is distinct from a publication date. The University of Wyoming hosts a copy of the primary lecture text.
Lecture text: www.uwyo.edu/neuron/hodgkin-lecture.pdf
Official copy: www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/hodgkin-lecture.pdf
Cited passages: pp. 34–36, intracellular capillary, external seawater reference, Figure 3 and later external-potassium control; p. 42, voltage clamp.
