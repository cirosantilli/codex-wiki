Jacobi coordinates

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Center of mass Center-of-mass motion Center-of-mass reduction
OurBigBook.com Words: 105 Articles: 2
For particles with masses mj​ and positions rj​, Jacobi coordinates consist of the total center of mass and successive relative coordinates ρj​=rj+1​−(∑l=1j​ml​rl​)/(∑l=1j​ml​). They separate translation from internal motion and diagonalize the kinetic energy with the corresponding reduced masses.
