Principal unit

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field p-adic unit
OurBigBook.com Words: 227 Articles: 5
For a non-Archimedean local field K with maximal ideal mK​, a principal unit is an element of 1+mK​. The higher principal-unit groups are 1+mKn​.
