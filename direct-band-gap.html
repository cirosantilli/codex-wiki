Direct band gap

 ... Branches of physics Quantum theory Bloch's theorem Electronic band structure Energy band Band gap
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
A band gap whose conduction band minimum and valence band maximum occur at the same crystal momentum, allowing an optical transition without a momentum-balancing phonon.
