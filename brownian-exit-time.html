Brownian exit time (TD​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 843 Articles: 15
For a domain D, the Brownian exit time is TD​=inf{t≥0:Bt​∈/D}. It is a stopping time, and it has finite expectation when D is bounded.
