Poisson binomial distribution (K=∑i=1n​Xi​,Xi​∼Bernoulli(pi​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
The sum of independent Bernoulli random variables with possibly unequal success probabilities pi​ has a Poisson binomial distribution. Its probability generating function is GK​(z)=∏i​(1−pi​+pi​z): expand the product to read off the probability mass function. Linearity of expectation and additivity of variance for independent random variables give EK=∑i​pi​ and Var(K)=∑i​pi​(1−pi​). Equal pi​=p recover the binomial distribution.
For three trials with success probabilities 1/10,3/10,4/5, expanding (9/10+z/10)(7/10+3z/10)(1/5+4z/5) gives masses 0.126,0.572,0.278,0.024 at 0,1,2,3. Their expected value is 1.2 and variance is 0.46. Replacing all three success probabilities by their average 0.4 preserves the expected value but gives binomial distribution variance 0.72, so it does not preserve the law.
