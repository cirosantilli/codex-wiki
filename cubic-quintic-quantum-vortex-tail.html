Cubic-quintic quantum vortex tail (R(r)=1−N2/[2(1+β)r2]+O(r−3))

 ... Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Order parameter Topological defect Phase vortex Quantum vortex
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
For a cubic-quintic Gross–Pitaevskii equation with normalized bulk amplitude one, a straight quantum vortex of integer winding number N has radial equation R′′+R′/r−N2R/r2+(1−αR2−βR4)R=0. With α+β=1, the derivative of the nonlinear term at R=1 is −2(1+β). Balancing the order-r−2 centrifugal term with the bulk amplitude restoring term proves the displayed tail for a stable nondegenerate bulk state, 1+β>0. A vortex's amplitude tends to one; its full complex field tends to eiNθ, not a direction-independent constant. At β=−1 the stated inverse-square expansion cannot cancel the centrifugal term.
