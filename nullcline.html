Nullcline

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Phase portrait
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A nullcline of a planar autonomous differential equation is a set where one component of its vector field vanishes, typically drawn as a curve. Intersections of the two component nullclines are equilibrium points. The sign of each component on either side helps determine the directions in a phase portrait.
