On the tensor product of spins j1​ and j2​, the combined angular momentum operator is J=S(1)⊗I+I⊗S(2). The Clebsch-Gordan decomposition is
Hj1​​⊗Hj2​​≅J=∣j1​−j2​∣⨁j1​+j2​​HJ​,​
(2)
where J increases by one and each irreducible spin representation occurs once. The dimensions agree: ∑J​(2J+1)=(2j1​+1)(2j2​+1).
The uncoupled orthonormal basis diagonalizes S3(1)​,S3(2)​; the coupled orthonormal basis diagonalizes J2,J3​. The Clebsch-Gordan coefficients are their change-of-basis entries, with convention
∣J,M⟩=∑m1​,m2​​Cj1​,j2​​(J,M;m1​,m2​)∣j1​,m1​⟩⊗∣j2​,m2​⟩.
(3)
They vanish unless m1​+m2​=M. Since both bases are orthonormal, this change-of-basis matrix obeys unitarity. Overall phases of the coupled basis vectors are fixed by a convention and do not change the energy eigenvalues.
