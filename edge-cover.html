Edge cover (F⊆E,⋃e∈F​e=V)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 81 Articles: 1
An edge cover of a finite graph is a subset of its edges incident to every vertex. It exists if and only if there is no isolated vertex, with the empty graph covered by the empty set. A minimum-cardinality edge cover uses as few edges as possible; a minimum-weight edge cover minimizes their total weight. These objectives are related to maximum cardinality matching and maximum-weight matching, respectively, by the edge cover and matching identity and minimum-weight edge cover by matching savings.
