Carbon burning

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar nuclear fusion
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
Carbon burning fuses carbon nuclei at temperatures of order 109K. In intermediate-mass stars it can ignite off-centre under partial electron degeneracy and propagate inward as a burning flame.
