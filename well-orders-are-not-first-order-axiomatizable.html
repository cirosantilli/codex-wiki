Well-orders are not first-order axiomatizable

If a theory axiomatized the well-orders, adjoin constants c0​,c1​,… and the sentences cn+1​<cn​. Every finite fragment has a finite well-ordered model, but compactness would produce a model with an infinite descending chain.
