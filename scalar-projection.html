Scalar projection (compc​a=a⋅c/∥c∥)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Inner product Dot product
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
The scalar projection of a real Euclidean vector a onto a nonzero Euclidean vector c is its signed component along the unit vector c/∥c∥:
compc​a=a⋅∥c∥c​.
(10)
For a=(3,4), the scalar projection onto (2,0) is 3, while that onto (−2,0) is −3. Reversing the measuring direction reverses the sign; the scalar projection is not necessarily a nonnegative length. For nonzero a, it also equals ∥a∥cosθ, using the angle with c and its cosine. For the zero vector it is zero, even though that angle is undefined.
