Hodge splitting of Euclidean two-forms (Λ2=Λ+2​⊕Λ−2​,P±​=(1±∗)/2)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form Hodge star operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 93 Articles: 1
On an oriented Euclidean metric four-space, the Hodge star operator on two-forms is an orthogonal involution. Its two eigenspaces each have dimension three. A self-dual two-form has eigenvalue +1, while an anti-self-dual two-form has eigenvalue −1. The splitting uses the metric-normalized Riemannian volume form; an arbitrarily rescaled volume in the defining wedge identity would rescale the operator itself.
