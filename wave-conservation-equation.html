Wave conservation equation (kt​+ωx​=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Geometrical optics Eikonal equation for a variable-speed wave Phase compatibility in geometrical optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
For a smooth phase Θ, define local wavenumber k=Θx​ and local angular frequency ω=−Θt​. Equality of mixed derivatives gives kt​+ωx​=0. Dividing by 2π expresses conservation of signed crest count with density k/(2π) and flux ω/(2π). When a homogeneous local dispersion relation gives ω=ω(k), the equation becomes kt​+vg​(k)kx​=0; its characteristic curves move at the group velocity. This phase compatibility is distinct from energy or wave-action conservation.
