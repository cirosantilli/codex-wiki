Geostrophic wind (ug​=(ρf)−1z×∇h​p)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Shallow water equations Geostrophic balance
OurBigBook.com Words: 223 Articles: 3
The geostrophic wind is the horizontal flow for which the pressure gradient force balances the Coriolis force. It runs along atmospheric isobars and has speed proportional to their pressure gradient, at fixed density and nonzero Coriolis parameter. Curvature, friction and small spatial scales can invalidate that approximation.
