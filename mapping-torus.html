Mapping torus

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 676 Articles: 13
The mapping torus of f:F→F is
Tf​=(F×[0,1])/((x,1)∼(f(x),0)).
(14)
If f is a homeomorphism, the projection to the circle S1 is a fiber bundle with fiber F. For a general continuous map, the mapping torus need not be a fiber bundle, but its homology still has the Wang sequence.
