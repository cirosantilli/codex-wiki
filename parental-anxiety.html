Parental anxiety

 ... Science Psychology Clinical psychology Child and adolescent clinical psychology Youth anxiety treatment Parent involvement in youth anxiety treatment
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
Anxiety experienced by a parent, distinguished from the child's symptoms and from parental accommodation. It may affect burden and participation, but association with child difficulty does not establish that treating it necessarily augments child treatment.[ref]
