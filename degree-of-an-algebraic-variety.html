Degree of an algebraic variety (degX)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 34
The degree of a projective algebraic variety of dimension r is the length, with intersection multiplicities, of its intersection with a sufficiently general linear subspace of complementary codimension. Equivalently, its Hilbert polynomial has leading coefficient degX/r!. For a projective curve, a general hyperplane section measures this degree.
