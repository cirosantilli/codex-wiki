Poisson moderate deviation principle (L(1+β)/2SL​−Lλ​,aL​=Lβ,I(x)=x2/(2λ))

For a Poisson random variable SL​ of mean Lλ>0 and 0<β<1, the scaled cumulant-generating function is λL1−β(eθL(β−1)/2−1−θL(β−1)/2). Its Taylor expansion tends to λθ2/2 for every real θ. The Gärtner–Ellis theorem applies because the limit is finite and differentiable everywhere. Its Legendre-Fenchel transform is the displayed quadratic good rate function.
