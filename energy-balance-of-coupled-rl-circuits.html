The magnetic energy of this constant-inductance model is
W=21​L1​i12​+Mi1​i2​+21​L2​i22​=21​iTLi.
(19)
The positive-definite matrix condition makes W≥0. Multiplying the two circuit equations by i1​ and i2​, then adding, gives the displayed conservation of energy statement: input power Vi1​ supplies stored magnetic energy and Joule heating. With zero drive, W˙≤0. After the drive becomes zero, the stored magnetic energy supplies the remaining Joule heating.
