Galaxy two-point correlation function (dP12​=nˉ2[1+ξ(r)]dV1​dV2​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Correlation function (astronomy)
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 1
For a homogeneous isotropic point process of galaxies with mean number density nˉ, the galaxy two-point correlation function gives the excess pair probability at distinct positions. Dividing the factorial pair density by the probability of a central galaxy gives conditional neighbouring-galaxy density nˉ[1+ξ(r)]. Choosing a central location uniformly in volume does not perform this conditioning.
