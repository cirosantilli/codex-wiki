Post-translational modification

 Home Biology Protein
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 10
A post-translational modification is a chemical change to a protein after its synthesis, such as phosphorylation or protein lipidation. It can change activity, stability, localization or partner binding without changing the encoded sequence.
