CM-field

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Number field Totally imaginary number field
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 1
A CM-field is a totally imaginary number field that is a quadratic extension of a totally real number field. Its nontrivial automorphism over that real subfield acts as complex conjugation in every complex embedding. Imaginary quadratic number fields are examples. An arbitrary totally imaginary number field need not have such a real subfield of index two.
