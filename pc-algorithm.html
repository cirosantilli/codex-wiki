PC algorithm

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Causal inference Causal directed acyclic graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 190 Articles: 2
The PC algorithm starts with a candidate undirected graph, removes edges using conditional independence tests over increasing conditioning-set sizes, records separating sets, then orients unshielded colliders and propagates orientations. Under oracle tests and faithfulness of a directed acyclic graph, it estimates the graph's Markov equivalence class.
