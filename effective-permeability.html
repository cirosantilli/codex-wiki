Effective permeability (keff​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Darcy law Permeability of a porous medium
OurBigBook.com Words: 648 Articles: 3
Effective permeability is a bulk permeability of a porous medium that reproduces the total Darcy flux through a heterogeneous sample under specified boundary conditions. For a horizontal sample with length L, dynamic viscosity μ, and positive inlet-to-outlet fluid pressure drop Δp, define keff​ by Q/A=keff​Δp/(μL). The description is useful when individual layers or passages are smaller than the scale of the calculation. It depends on direction, geometry and boundary conditions; it is not generally the arithmetic mean of local intrinsic permeabilities.
Layered porous permeability illustrates the mechanism: when every path must cross a resistant layer, successive hydraulic resistances add; when more permeable paths span the whole sample alongside less permeable ones, their discharges add. This is the same organizing idea as series and parallel resistors. For fixed fluid properties the corresponding hydraulic conductivities average in the same way.
