Lines through a point of a smooth cubic surface (#{L⊂S:P∈L}≤3)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic surface Cubic surface Smooth cubic surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
Every projective line through P on a smooth cubic surface lies in its projective tangent plane. Each is a distinct linear factor of the cubic plane section. There can therefore be at most three. Equality occurs when the tangent section consists of three concurrent lines.
