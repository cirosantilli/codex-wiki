Conservative force

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Energy Potential energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 52 Articles: 1
A force is conservative when its work depends only on the endpoints. On a simply connected domain this is equivalent to F=−∇V for a potential energy V.
