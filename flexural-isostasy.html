Flexural isostasy

 Home Physics Branch of physics Geophysics Postglacial rebound Glacial isostatic adjustment
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
Flexural isostasy models the lithosphere as an elastic plate that bends over a buoyant substrate. For a sinusoidal deflection of wavenumber k, bending contributes a restoring stress proportional to Bk4, where B is the plate's bending stiffness.
