Anticanonical strict transform under a point blowup ([−Y]≅KX​⟺dimC​X=2)

 ... Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Blowup of a complex manifold at a point Canonical bundle formula for a point blowup
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
Let Y be a smooth complex analytic hypersurface through the blowup centre with [−Y]≅KX​. Its multiplicity at that point is one, so σ∗Y=Y+E. Combining this with the canonical bundle formula for a point blowup gives [−Y]≅KX​⊗[E]⊗(2−n). The nontrivial powers of the exceptional line bundle of a point blowup prove the equivalence for n≥2. On a compact complex curve the premise cannot hold for an irreducible hypersurface: it is one point, so its negative divisor has degree −1, whereas the canonical bundle has even degree 2g−2.
