Time-ordered exponential (U(t,t0​)=Texp[−i∫t0​t​H(s)ds/ℏ])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Wick's theorem Time ordering
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 2
The time-ordered exponential solves iℏ∂t​U=H(t)U with initial condition U(t0​,t0​)=I. Its expansion is the Dyson series. For bounded continuous Hamiltonian operators the series converges, as in differentiation of the Dyson time-ordered exponential. If the generators commute at all times, time ordering is unnecessary and an ordinary operator exponential suffices.
