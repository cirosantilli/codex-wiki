Early surface-reconstruction measurements with scanning tunneling microscopy

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Potential barrier Quantum tunnelling Tunnel junction Scanning tunneling microscopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 109
For gold (110), the 1982 paper compared an untreated surface with one prepared by argon sputtering and annealing. Repeated cleaning reproduced the corrugation; the gap-modulation signal checked work function variation. This supported a structural interpretation while exposing the danger of confusing work function contrast with geometric relief.[ref]
The authored lecture describes resolving the 1×2 surface reconstruction on gold (110), alongside ribbons of other periodicities. It also reports gold transfer to the tip on contact and fluctuating resolution, attributed tentatively to migrating tip chemical atoms. The probe could change during observation: an image was evidence requiring controls, not an unambiguous map of atomic positions.[ref]
