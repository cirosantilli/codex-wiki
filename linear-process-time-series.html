Linear process (time series, Xt​=μ+∑j​ψj​εt−j​)

A linear process is an L2-convergent white-noise filter Xt​=μ+∑j∈Z​ψj​εt−j​, with square-summable coefficients. It is weakly stationary, and its autocovariance is σε2​∑j​ψj​ψj+h​. A causal representation restricts the coefficients to j≥0; it is then an infinite moving-average representation.
