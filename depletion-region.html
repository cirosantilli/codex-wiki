Depletion region

 Home Physics Semiconductor P-n junction
OurBigBook.com Words: 555 Articles: 6
The region around a p-n junction with reduced mobile charge carrier density.
An illustrative abrupt-junction depletion approximation assumes uniform, fully ionized donor impurities and acceptor impurities, negligible mobile space charge in the depleted layer, and permittivity εs​. For area A, doping number densities NA​,ND​ and built-in electric potential difference Vbi​, it gives
W≃q2εs​​(NA​1​+ND​1​)(Vbi​−V)​,Cj​≃Wεs​A​.
(20)
Here q>0 is the elementary charge and V is positive for forward bias. Increasing both doping densities reduces W in this estimate. That explains the preparation strategy for a tunnel diode; it does not make this sharp-depletion approximation a precise microscopic model of a degenerate semiconductor.
