Orientation of a vector bundle

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 24
This is a fiberwise orientation compatible with local trivializations of a vector bundle. An R-orientation of a rank-d vector bundle is a coherent choice of generator of Hd(Eb​,Eb​∖{0};R) for every fiber, equivalently a Thom class with the corresponding fiberwise restriction.
