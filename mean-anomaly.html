Mean anomaly (M)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Kepler orbit Orbital element

Mean anomaly is the uniformly increasing phase M=n(t−τ) of a Kepler orbit, measured from periapsis time τ.
