Irreducible character

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Character theory Character of a representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 241 Articles: 4
An irreducible character is the character of an irreducible representation. Over the complex numbers, every finite-group character decomposes uniquely as a nonnegative integer combination of irreducible characters.
