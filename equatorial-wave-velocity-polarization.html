Equatorial wave velocity polarization (u=ω2−k2i(ωyv−kv′)​,η=ω2−k2i(kyv−ωv′)​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Equatorial wave Equatorial shallow-water dispersion relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
In dimensionless equatorial wave units with gravity wave speed and beta plane coefficient one, a Fourier mode ei(kx−ωt) obeys ωu−kη=iyv and ωη−ku=−iv′. Inverting this two-by-two linear system gives the displayed wave polarization, provided ω2=k2. Inserting it in the meridional momentum equation gives the Hermite differential equation after a Gaussian factor is removed. At singular values the original equations, rather than these quotient formulae, determine whether a trapped mode exists.
