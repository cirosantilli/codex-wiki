For time dependence eiωt and a segment of length ℓ, let β=ωℓ/c. Adding the forward and backward arterial pressure waves gives the displayed upstream-to-downstream matrix. The flow orientation is downstream at both ends. At a junction, pressure is continuous and incoming volumetric flow rates balance outgoing ones.
