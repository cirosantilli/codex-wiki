Ice-albedo feedback

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Climate change Climate feedback
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
Replacing bright ice or snow with darker water or ground decreases surface albedo, increases absorbed sunlight and can cause further melting. Locally the extra absorbed heat flux is (αice​−αwater​)S↓​ on the newly exposed surface. The effect depends on season, cloud cover and incident sunlight.
