Eddy current

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
An eddy current is a circulating electric current induced within an electrical conductor by changing magnetic flux, as described by Faraday's law of induction. Its magnetic field opposes the inducing change according to Lenz's law; electrical resistance converts electrical energy into heat.
