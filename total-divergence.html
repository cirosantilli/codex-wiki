Total divergence

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus Divergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A total divergence has the form ∇⋅F. The divergence theorem converts its volume integral into a boundary flux, which vanishes when the field has suitable decay or boundary conditions.
