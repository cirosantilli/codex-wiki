Ricci curvature of a bi-invariant Riemannian metric (Ric(X,X)=41​∑i​∥[X,ei​]∥2)

For a bi-invariant Riemannian metric on a Lie group, the induced inner product on its Lie algebra makes every adjoint operator skew-adjoint. The Levi-Civita connection of a bi-invariant metric gives R(X,Y)Z=−41​[[X,Y],Z]. Thus K(X,Y)=41​∥[X,Y]∥2 for orthonormal X,Y, and
Ric(X,X)=41​∑i​∥[X,ei​]∥2.
(11)
The nullspace of this quadratic form is exactly the center of a Lie algebra. A zero center therefore gives positive Ricci curvature, uniformly bounded below by a positive constant times the metric through left invariance and compactness of the unit sphere in the Lie algebra.
