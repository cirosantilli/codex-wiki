Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Doubling constant
OurBigBook.com Words: 181 Articles: 5
If finite sets satisfy ∣A+B∣≤K∣A∣, then the Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality bounds iterated sumsets and difference sets by
∣ℓB−mB∣≤Kℓ+m∣A∣.
