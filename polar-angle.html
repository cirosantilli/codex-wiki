Polar angle (θ)

 ... Calculus Multivariable calculus Change of variables formula Orthogonal coordinates Hyperspherical coordinates Spherical coordinate system
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
In the usual physics convention for spherical coordinates, the polar angle is the angle between the positive z axis and the position vector. For r=x2+y2+z2​>0, it satisfies cosθ=z/r with 0≤θ≤π. The north and south axes have θ=0 and θ=π, respectively; the plane z=0 has θ=π/2. The angle is undefined at the origin. In plane polar coordinates, the same phrase usually denotes the oriented in-plane angle instead, so the reference axis and convention should always be stated. The azimuth supplies the other angular coordinate in three dimensions.
