Bn Dynkin diagram and affine extension

 ... Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Simple Lie algebra Special orthogonal Lie algebra Bn root system
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
For n≥2, the Bn root system has simple roots αi​=εi​−εi+1​ for i<n and αn​=εn​. Its Dynkin diagram is a chain with a double terminal bond pointing toward short αn​. The highest root is θ=ε1​+ε2​. In the Extended Dynkin diagram, α0​=−θ attaches by a single bond to α2​ for n≥3. For n=2, both long nodes 0,1 attach by double bonds to short node 2. Rank one is the rank-one root system; its affine diagram has two equal-length nodes joined by a double bond without a short-root arrow.
