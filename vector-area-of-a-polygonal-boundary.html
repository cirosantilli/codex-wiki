Vector area of a polygonal boundary (A=21​∑i​Bi​×Bi+1​)

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry First fundamental form Area element of a surface Vector area element Vector area
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For an oriented closed polygon, triangulate with a common vertex P. Its total vector area is 21​∑i​(Bi​−P)×(Bi+1​−P); the terms involving P telescope, giving the displayed boundary-only formula. Its signed projected area along a unit vector V is V⋅A. The maximum is ∣A∣, in direction A/∣A∣ if nonzero, and zero projections have directions perpendicular to A. If A=0, every direction has zero signed projection despite possible nonzero unsigned surface area.
