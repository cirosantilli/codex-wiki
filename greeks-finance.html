Greeks (finance)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance
OurBigBook.com Words: 361 Articles: 5
The Greeks are sensitivities of a financial claim value to model inputs, expressed through partial derivatives. The option delta measures sensitivity to the underlying price; a delta hedge uses that sensitivity to cancel first-order price exposure.
