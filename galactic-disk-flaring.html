Galactic disk flaring

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galactic disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
Galactic disk flaring is an outward increase of vertical thickness. A constant vertical velocity dispersion in an approximately thin-sheet stellar Newtonian gravitational field gives h∝1/Σ∗​. An exponential galactic disk then predicts h∝eR/Rd​ for a gas tracer. Inside a thick stellar layer the potential is instead approximately harmonic, and the tracer width is σz​/νz​ with vertical epicyclic frequency νz​.
