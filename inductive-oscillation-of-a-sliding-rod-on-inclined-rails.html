Inductive oscillation of a sliding rod on inclined rails (ωB2​=B2l2cos2θ/(mL))

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Electromotive force Motional electromotive force
OurBigBook.com Words: 105 Articles: 1
A vertical field linking a conducting loop on an incline contributes flux Blcosθs. For zero resistance and zero initial current, flux conservation gives LI=Blcosθs. The uphill magnetic force component then makes the downhill coordinate oscillate about se​=mgLsinθ/(B2l2cos2θ). Release from s=0 first stops at 2se​, not at the force-free point se​. The energy is stored in the inductor, not dissipated.
