Schwarzschild static observer

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Schwarzschild spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 190 Articles: 4
An observer at fixed exterior Schwarzschild spatial coordinates. Its proper time is dT=f​dt, with f=1−2GM/(rc2)>0. It is accelerated, not freely falling; no such static timelike worldline exists on or inside the event horizon.
