Constant of integration (∫f(x)dx=F(x)+C)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Integral Antiderivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
If F′=f on a connected interval, every other antiderivative is F+C for a constant C. Indeed (G−F)′=0, so the mean value theorem makes G−F constant. A boundary condition fixes C. For example T′=−B/r2 integrates to T=B/r+C, and T(Rs​)=Ts​ gives C=Ts​−B/Rs​. Discarding an integration constant therefore needs an actual boundary condition or an approximation showing that its effect is small.
