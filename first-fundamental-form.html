First fundamental form (I)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 835 Articles: 14
The first fundamental form of a smooth surface S⊆R3 is the restriction of the ambient inner product to each tangent space Tp​S. For a regular parametric surface X(u,v), it is
I=Edu2+2Fdudv+Gdv2,E=Xu2​,F=Xu​⋅Xv​,G=Xv2​.
(54)
Here Xu2​ means Xu​⋅Xu​, not a componentwise square. The coefficients form the Gram matrix of the two coordinate tangent vectors. Regularity makes this a positive-definite matrix, so E>0 and EG−F2>0.
To calculate the length of a curve γ(t)=X(u(t),v(t)), apply the chain rule and integrate its speed:
L(γ)=∫Eu˙2+2Fu˙v˙+Gv˙2​dt.
(55)
The same inner product gives the angle α between nonzero tangent vectors V,W through cosα=I(V,W)/I(V,V)I(W,W)​, and gives the area element dA=EG−F2​dudv as a positive density. One compact expression therefore supplies rulers, protractors, and surface area measurements.
For the lateral surface of a circular cylinder, take the circumferential arc length u rather than the angle:
X(u,v)=(RcosRu​,RsinRu​,v),Xu​=(−sinRu​,cosRu​,0),Xv​=(0,0,1).
(56)
Direct inner products give E=G=1,F=0, hence I=du2+dv2. This is exactly the Euclidean metric on the unrolled plane. For instance, a path with u=3t,v=4t, 0≤t≤1, has length 5 on either surface. Every corresponding path retains its length, not merely this example, so sufficiently small neighborhoods retain their intrinsic distances. The local isometry preserves angles and surface areas too. Globally u is periodic with period 2πR: unrolling requires a seam, and the whole circular cylinder is not a single planar manifold chart.
