First fundamental form (I)

The first fundamental form of a surface S⊆R3 is the restriction of the ambient inner product to each tangent space Tp​S. For a parametrized surface X(u,v), it is
I=Edu2+2Fdudv+Gdv2,E=Xu2​,F=Xu​⋅Xv​,G=Xv2​.
