For Lint​=g2(ψˉ​ψ)2/2 with one species of Dirac field, the tree-level Feynman diagrams for f(p1​)fˉ​(p2​)→f(p3​)fˉ​(p4​) give
M=g2[(uˉ3​u1​)(vˉ2​v4​)−(vˉ2​u1​)(uˉ3​v4​)].
(5)
The fermionic sign is the antisymmetry of the two external contractions. The initial spin average is one quarter of the spin sum. The two diagonal contributions are g4(t−4m2)2 and g4(s−4m2)2; the interference Dirac trace is R=(s−4m2)2+(t−4m2)2−(u−4m2)2, giving the displayed result after subtracting g4R/2. Here the Mandelstam variables satisfy s+t+u=4m2. At threshold this gives 16g4m4; for zero mass it gives g4(s2+t2+u2)/2. The formula describes the leading scattering amplitude of an effective interaction and is not a statement of perturbative renormalizability.
