Closed-channel cell-flux constraint (∫−aa​Jz​dx=0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 92
A genuinely closed steady channel with impermeable sidewalls and ends must have zero integrated axial cell flux through every section, not only zero fluid flux. In the bulk phototactic ansatz n′=βn, ωy​=−w′, and ⟨pz​⟩=−K1​w′/(2Dr​), the axial flux is [1+Vs​βK1​/(2Dr​)]∫nwdx. Meanwhile the momentum equation, zero fluid flux and integration by parts give g′∫nwdx=−μ∫(w′)2dx. Thus a nontrivial bulk profile is generally incompatible with exact closed-end cell conservation unless the prefactor vanishes. Additional axial concentration variation is needed; neglecting end regions does not itself remove this global constraint.
