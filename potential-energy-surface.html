Potential energy surface (V(R1​,…,RN​))

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Chemical kinetics Reaction mechanism Reaction coordinate
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 2
A potential energy surface assigns potential energy to the configurations of the reacting chemical atoms, for a specified electronic description. A reaction coordinate selects a path through this many-dimensional configuration space; a potential-energy profile then records the potential energy along that path. Different paths can share endpoints but cross different barriers. See the IUPAC Gold Book: potential-energy reaction surface.
