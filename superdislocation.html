Superdislocation

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Crystal structure Crystallographic defect Dislocation
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
An order-preserving total dislocation in an ordered crystal, often dissociated into smaller dislocations separated by an antiphase boundary. Two matching a⟨110⟩/2 dislocations can restore order in the gamma prime phase.
