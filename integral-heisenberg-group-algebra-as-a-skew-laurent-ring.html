Integral Heisenberg group algebra as a skew Laurent ring (kH≅k[y±1,z±1][x±1;σ],σ(y)=zy)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Polynomial ring Skew polynomial ring of an automorphism Skew Laurent polynomial ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For the Integer Heisenberg group, choose x=I+E12​, y=I+E23​ and central z=I+E13​. Every element has a unique form yczbxa, and xy=zyx. The group algebra is therefore the displayed skew Laurent polynomial ring, with σ(z)=z. The base is a commutative Laurent polynomial ring and an integral domain; the skew Hilbert basis theorem gives both-sided Noetherianity and the leading coefficient of a product proves that there are no zero divisors.
