Translation-invariant linear forms on p-adic continuous functions vanish (T=0)

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Continuous functions on the p-adic integers Mahler's theorem Discrete antidifferentiation on the p-adic integers
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Let T:C(Zp​,Qp​)→Qp​ be linear and invariant under translation by one. For any f, choose its continuous discrete antiderivative Jf. Then T(f)=T(ΔJf)=T(Jf(⋅+1))−T(Jf)=0. No continuity or boundedness of T is assumed. In particular this applies to a form invariant under every translation by a p-adic integer.
