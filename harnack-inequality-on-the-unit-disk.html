For a nonnegative harmonic function on the unit disc, the displayed inequalities are sharp. Apply the Poisson integral on the unit disk on radius R>∣z∣, bound its positive kernel between (R−∣z∣)/(R+∣z∣) and its reciprocal, and use the mean value property for harmonic functions. Letting R↑1 proves the assertion. Positive Poisson kernels with one boundary pole give equality in the appropriate radial directions.
