Standard normal random variable (Z∼N(0,1))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Normal distribution Gaussian random variable
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A standard normal random variable has the standard normal distribution, mean zero and variance one. If X∼N(μ,σ2) with σ>0, the standardization of a normal random variable (X−μ)/σ is a standard normal random variable. Its values report displacement from the mean in units of the standard deviation, so the result is a dimensionless quantity.
