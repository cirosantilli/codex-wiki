Dielectric breakdown

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Permittivity Dielectric
OurBigBook.com Words: 180 Articles: 2
Dielectric breakdown is failure of an insulating regime under a sufficiently strong electric field, allowing substantial electric current through the material. For example, an electron freed from a chemical atom or molecule can become a mobile charge carrier. The small-field linear electric susceptibility model does not describe this conducting state.[ref]
