Phase modulation (w(x,y,t)=w0​(x+ϕ(y,t))+⋯)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Pattern formation
OurBigBook.com Words: 197 Articles: 3
A phase modulation changes the local position of a periodic pattern. Its local wavenumber is shifted by derivatives of the phase. In a method of multiple scales, a correction profile depending on the translated coordinate ξ=x+ϕ(Y) must be differentiated with DY​=∂Y​+ϕY​∂ξ​. Omitting this chain rule can change the nonlinear coefficient of a reduced amplitude equation even when its linear coefficients are unchanged.
