One-dimensional entropic chain force-extension relation (F=(kB​T/a)artanh(L/(Na)))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biopolymer mechanics Ideal chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 176 Articles: 1
For N independent links pointing left or right with length a, fixed signed extension L gives NR​=(N+L/a)/2 and NL​=(N−L/a)/2. The multiplicity is (NL​N​) when these counts are nonnegative integers. Leading Stirling's approximation gives F=(kB​T/(2a))ln(NR​/NL​) as the externally applied tension conjugate to L. Its small-extension limit is kB​TL/(Na2). At fixed tension, the exact one-link partition function is 2cosh(βFa) and the mean extension is Natanh(βFa), confirming the same inverse relation without using Stirling at the full-extension endpoint.
