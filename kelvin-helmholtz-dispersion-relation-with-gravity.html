Kelvin-Helmholtz dispersion relation with gravity (ρ1​(U1​−c)2+ρ2​(U2​−c)2=g(ρ2​−ρ1​)/k)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Shear layer Vortex sheet Kelvin-Helmholtz instability
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
Two deep layers of incompressible flow with upper mass density ρ1​, lower mass density ρ2​, and tangential velocities U1​,U2​ have this dispersion relation when surface tension is absent. Decaying velocity potentials and the linearized kinematic boundary condition determine their amplitudes; the dynamic boundary condition for an inviscid interface gives the displayed relation. For k>0 the squared departure of the phase velocity c from the density-weighted mean velocity is g(ρ2​−ρ1​)/[k(ρ1​+ρ2​)]−ρ1​ρ2​(U1​−U2​)2/(ρ1​+ρ2​)2. A negative value gives Kelvin-Helmholtz instability despite stable density stratification.
