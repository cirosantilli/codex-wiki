Controllability matrix (C=[B​AB​⋯​An−1B​])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Control theory Controllability
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For the linear time-invariant system x˙=Ax+Bu, with A∈Rn×n and B∈Rn×m, the controllability matrix has n rows and nm columns. The columns of B are directions influenced directly by the control inputs; AB,A2B,… account for how the dynamics carry their influence into further directions. The column space of the controllability matrix is the zero-state reachable set for any T>0 when inputs are unrestricted. [ref]
