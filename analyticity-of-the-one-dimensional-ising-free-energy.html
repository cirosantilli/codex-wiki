Analyticity of the one-dimensional Ising free energy

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Spin model Ising model Transfer matrix for the one-dimensional Ising model
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
At real field and nonzero temperature, every entry of the finite-range Ising transfer matrix is positive. Its largest eigenvalue is simple by the Perron–Frobenius theorem and depends smoothly on field and temperature. The thermodynamic free energy per site is its negative logarithm times kB​T. In the nearest-neighbour chain it is analytic and the zero-field magnetization vanishes, despite exponentially long low-temperature correlations. Finite-volume spin-inversion symmetry alone would not suffice to exclude spontaneous thermodynamic order.
