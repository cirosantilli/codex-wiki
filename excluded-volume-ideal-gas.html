Excluded-volume ideal gas (p(V−b)=RT)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Pair potential Hard-sphere gas Excluded volume
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
For one mole, a simple repulsive-volume model uses p(V−b)=RT with b>0 and domain V>b. The Maxwell relation and first law of thermodynamics give (∂U/∂V)T​=T(∂p/∂T)V​−p=0, so U=U(T). If its molar heat capacity at constant volume CV​ is temperature independent, it is constant, U=CV​T+U∗​ and Cp​=CV​+R. Reversible adiabatic processes obey T(V−b)R/CV​=constant or p(V−b)1+R/CV​=constant. All positive-temperature isotherms are strictly decreasing and convex; this single-phase model contains no attractive liquid-gas coexistence mechanism. The parameter b is an effective unavailable molar volume, not automatically the literal geometric volume of the molecules.
