Eliminating central density from the Lane-Emden scalings gives
R∝Kn/(3−n)M(1−n)/(3−n)
(11)
for n=3. In particular, R∝KM−1/3 for n=3/2.
With Yn​=−ξ12​θ′(ξ1​), the exact rearranged relation is
M=An​Kn/(n−1)R(3−n)/(1−n),
(12)
where
An​=4πYn​(4πGn+1​)n/(n−1)ξ1(3−n)/(n−1)​.
(13)
For n=3, the radius dependence disappears and the mass is proportional to K3/2.
