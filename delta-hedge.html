Delta hedge (Δ=∂s​V)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Greeks (finance) Option delta
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A delta hedge holds Δt​=∂s​V(t,St​) units of the risky asset. In the Black-Scholes model, this choice cancels the claim's Brownian exposure.
The option delta is one of the Greeks (finance); hedging is the trading procedure that uses this sensitivity.
