Heegaard Floer homology (HF∘(Y,s))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological manifold 3-manifold Heegaard splitting
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 16
Heegaard Floer homology assigns groups HF∘(Y,s) to a closed oriented three-manifold with a Spin-c structure. A pointed Heegaard diagram of genus g gives product Lagrangian tori Tα​,Tβ​⊂Symg(Σ); the chain complex is generated by their intersection points and its differential counts Whitney disk classes represented by holomorphic disks. The minus, infinity, plus and hat versions use different powers or quotients of the variable U. The Heegaard Floer cobordism maps and the Mixed Heegaard Floer invariant relate these groups to smooth four-dimensional topology.
