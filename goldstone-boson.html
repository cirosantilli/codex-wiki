Goldstone boson

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon
OurBigBook.com Words: 388 Articles: 8
Each broken continuous internal-symmetry generator normally produces a massless Goldstone mode; for a symmetry breaking pattern G→H, their number is dimG−dimH in the standard relativistic setting.
