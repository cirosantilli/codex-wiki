An unresolved source has an unknown beam filling factor, so its observed brightness need not equal its electron temperature. Angular size and an independent distance give a geometric depth L; a uniform model then yields ne​=EM/L​. Clumping instead measures a density-squared average, not the mean density.[ref]
A free-free spectrum alone does not uniquely determine density and size. The density-depth degeneracy of free-free radiation is explicit: for a resolved uniform column, ne​↦qne​, L↦L/q2 leaves both emissivity-integrated brightness and optical depth unchanged at fixed temperature.
