for that worst projection. A strictly east-west offset in a sides-aligned square instead samples at most 18m, giving a 5.73GHz first null and a 1.43GHz ten-percent-loss bandwidth. The original two-antenna geometry supplies an additional cosθ projection; these examples use its maximum magnitude. Thus a bandwidth of order 1GHz is a reasonable conservative estimate with a stated small-loss criterion, rather than a unique geometry-free limit.
To use a much wider total band, channelize the signals before correlation or before irreversible frequency averaging. Correlate sufficiently narrow channels, track the reference delay and phase at each frequency, and combine frequency-aware visibilities or images. This preserves sensitivity while avoiding field-dependent averaging losses in radio interferometry. Merely adding a phase correction after a broad channel has decorrelated cannot recover the lost signal; nor can one tracking delay cancel every sky direction simultaneously.[ref]
