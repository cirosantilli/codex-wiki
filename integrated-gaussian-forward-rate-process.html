For a centered Gaussian forward-rate field with covariance Cov(X(s,u),X(r,w))=c(s∧r,u,w), integrate the short-rate past and the current forward curve together using the displayed mean-square integral. Its variance is
v(s,T)=∫0T​∫0T​c(s∧u∧w,u,w)dudw.
(19)
For fixed T, its increments are independent of the full earlier forward-rate filtration: their covariance with X(z,w) at z≤r≤s is the integral of c(s∧u∧z,u,w)−c(r∧u∧z,u,w)=0. Use the uncorrelated jointly Gaussian variables are independent principle first for finite collections and then for their generated sigma-algebra. With c(0,u,w)=0, this centered process starts at zero.
