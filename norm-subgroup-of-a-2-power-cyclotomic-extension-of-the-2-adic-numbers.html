For every positive integer n,
NQ2​(ζ2n​)/Q2​​(Q2​(ζ2n​)×)=2Z(1+2nZ2​).
(49)
Under Local Artin reciprocity, the cyclotomic character identifies the quotient with (Z/2nZ)×: a uniformizer acts trivially on the totally ramified extension, while a 2-adic unit acts through its residue class modulo 2n.
