Dirac-Born-Infeld action (−Tp​∫e−Φ−det(P[G]+P[B]+2πα′F)​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory D-brane
OurBigBook.com Words: 513 Articles: 8
The Dirac-Born-Infeld action describes the leading derivative dynamics of one D-brane, including nonlinear dependence on slowly varying worldvolume field strengths. Its induced tensors are D-brane worldvolume pullbacks. The combination P[B]+2πα′F is invariant under the combined two-form and boundary gauge transformation. The dilaton factor reflects a disk's Euler characteristic. Expanding about flat space with small field strengths and embedding gradients gives the worldvolume gauge and scalar kinetic terms. A coincident stack has a non-Abelian low-energy Yang-Mills action; this does not imply that a naive traced determinant is exact at every order.
