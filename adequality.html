Adequality

 Home Mathematics History of mathematics
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A procedure associated with Pierre de Fermat for extrema and tangents. Compare expressions at a and a+e, remove common terms, divide by a remaining power of e, and suppress terms still containing e. Its interpretation must be distinguished from ordinary equality at finite e and from a modern limit definition.[ref]
