Abc-hit

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Diophantine equation abc conjecture
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A triple of positive integers (a,b,c) with a<b, a+b=c, gcd(a,b)=1, and rad(abc)<c, where rad is the radical of an integer. The example (1,8,9) has rad(72)=6<9.
