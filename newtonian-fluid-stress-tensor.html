Newtonian fluid stress tensor (σ=−pI+μ[∇u+(∇u)T])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Newtonian fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 252 Articles: 9
For isotropic incompressible flow of a Newtonian fluid, the stress tensor is the displayed expression, with pressure p, dynamic viscosity μ, velocity u, and identity matrix I. In Couette flow, its off-diagonal component is the shear stress.
