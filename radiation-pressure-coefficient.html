Radiation-pressure coefficient (β)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Interplanetary dust
OurBigBook.com Words: 185 Articles: 4
The radiation-pressure coefficient is the ratio of outward stellar radiation pressure to inward stellar gravity on a dust grain. The grain therefore feels effective gravitational parameter (1−β)GM∗​.
