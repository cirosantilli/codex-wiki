Canonical Coleman decomposition of power-series units (R×≃W×(1+pR))

 ... Iwasawa module p-ramified Iwasawa module Class-field unit sequence Coleman power series Coleman norm operator Coleman norm-fixed lift
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
Let W be the subgroup fixed by the Coleman norm operator. The Coleman norm-fixed lift gives the unique factorization f=w(f​)v, with v∈1+pR. Multiplication consequently gives the displayed direct product of groups, while reduction identifies W with Fp​[[T]]×. This is a decomposition of unit groups, not an identification of a power-series ring with a product of groups.
