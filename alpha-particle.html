Alpha particle (A=4,Z=2,J=I=0)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Atomic nucleus
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
The helium-four atomic nucleus, containing two protons and two neutrons. Its ground-state spin and isospin are zero. A quantized cubic four-Skyrmion supplies a corresponding nuclear state in the soliton description.
