Fourier inversion on a locally compact abelian group (f(x)=∫G​f​(χ)χ(x)dmG​(χ))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier transform Fourier transform on a locally compact abelian group
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
For the dual Haar measure, the formula first follows for continuous compactly supported convolution squares from the Bochner theorem. More generally, if f∈L1(G) and f​∈L1(G), the right-hand side is a continuous representative of f; equality holds everywhere if f is continuous, and otherwise at every point where the chosen representative is continuous. Convolution with normalized small-support positive convolution squares proves the extension: the spatial convolutions approach f in L1, while their transforms approach f​ in L1 by equicontinuity of compact families of characters and an integrable-tail split.
