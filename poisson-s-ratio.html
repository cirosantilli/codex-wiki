Poisson's ratio (ν)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 17
Poisson's ratio is minus the transverse strain divided by longitudinal strain under uniaxial loading in isotropic linear elasticity.
