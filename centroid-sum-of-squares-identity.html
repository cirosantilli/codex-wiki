Centroid sum-of-squares identity (∑i​∥xi​−z∥2=∑i​∥xi​−xˉ∥2+n∥z−xˉ∥2)

For a nonempty finite collection of Euclidean vectors, its sample mean xˉ=n−1∑i​xi​ uniquely minimizes the sum of squared Euclidean distances to a proposed centroid z. Expand each squared Euclidean norm around xˉ and use ∑i​(xi​−xˉ)=0 to cancel the cross term. The displayed identity leaves a nonnegative term n∥z−xˉ∥2, equal to zero exactly at z=xˉ.
