Kaluza-Klein theory (ds2=gμν​dxμdxν+R2(dy+Aμ​dxμ)2)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Compactification (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 413 Articles: 5
Kaluza-Klein theory obtains lower-dimensional fields from a higher-dimensional metric and other fields on compact dimensions. In the displayed fixed-radius circle ansatz, the off-diagonal metric supplies a vector. The coordinate change y↦y+λ(x) is compensated by A↦A−dλ, producing an Abelian gauge symmetry. Allowing the radius to vary adds a scalar modulus. Kaluza-Klein modes also include nonzero internal momenta, giving a tower of massive fields.
