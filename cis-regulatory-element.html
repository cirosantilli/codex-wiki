Cis-regulatory element

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Gene expression
OurBigBook.com Words: 52 Articles: 1
A sequence element acting on expression or processing of the molecule or locus carrying it. A binding motif in an intron can regulate nearby alternative splicing, with its effect depending on position and the available RNA-binding proteins.
