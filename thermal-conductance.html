Thermal conductance (G=1/Rth​)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Thermal conduction Thermal resistance
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Thermal conductance is the heat transfer rate divided by the driving temperature difference in a linear contact model. It is the reciprocal of thermal resistance, with units WK−1; thus Q˙​=G(T1​−T2​) for heat transferred from body 1 to body 2.
