OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For a nonnegative martingale, the displayed maximal probability bound follows by stopping at the first crossing of a. The stopped expectation is at most its initial expectation, and on the crossing event its value is at least a. Bound the stopping time first and pass to the limit using nonnegativity. This form is useful for the Poisson maximal concentration bound.
