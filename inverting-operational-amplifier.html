Inverting operational amplifier (Vo​/Vi​=−Rf​/Rin​)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Electronic amplifier Operational amplifier Ideal operational amplifier
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
Connect a resistor Rin​ from the input source to the inverting input, a feedback resistor Rf​ from output to that same input, and ground the non-inverting input. For ideal linear operation the inverting node is a virtual ground. Kirchhoff's current law gives Vi​/Rin​+Vo​/Rf​=0, yielding the displayed voltage gain. The source sees input impedance Rin​, even though the amplifier input itself has infinite input impedance.
