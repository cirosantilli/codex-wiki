Vertex renormalization (Z1​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Renormalization
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Multiplicative adjustment of an interaction vertex to cancel its ultraviolet divergence. In scalar electrodynamics, write the one-photon two-scalar field coefficient as Z1​e and the scalar kinetic coefficient as Z2​. The scalar QED Ward identity gives Z1​=Z2​ in a symmetry-preserving renormalization scheme.
