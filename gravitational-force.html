Gravitational force (F=−m∇Φ)

 Home Physics Gravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
The force exerted through gravity. In a Newtonian gravitational potential Φ per unit mass, a point mass m experiences the displayed force, while a continuous mass density ρ experiences force density −ρ∇Φ.
