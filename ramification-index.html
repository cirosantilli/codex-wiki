Ramification index (e(L/K))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field
OurBigBook.com Words: 322 Articles: 6
For a finite extension L/K of non-Archimedean local fields, the ramification index e(L/K) is the index of the value group of K in that of L. Normalized valuations satisfy vL​∣K​=e(L/K)vK​.
