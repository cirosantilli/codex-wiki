Kolmogorov backward equation

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Stochastic differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 636 Articles: 10
For a diffusion with infinitesimal generator L, the Kolmogorov backward equation is ∂t​u=Lu. Its solutions propagate terminal observables backward through the transition semigroup.
