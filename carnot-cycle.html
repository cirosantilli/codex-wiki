Carnot cycle

 ... Physics Branch of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Second law of thermodynamics Carnot theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 58 Articles: 1
A Carnot cycle consists of two reversible isothermal changes, one at each reservoir temperature, joined by two reversible adiabatic processes. It attains the maximum thermal efficiency permitted between those reservoirs, 1−Tc​/Th​.
