Monoidal tensor product (⊗)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Monoidal category
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
The bifunctor ⊗:C×C→C in a monoidal category. Its associativity is expressed by the associator, rather than equality in general.
