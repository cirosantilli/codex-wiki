Globally generated line bundle

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Locally free sheaf Line bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 3
A line bundle is globally generated if at each point some global section has a germ generating its stalk as a module over the local ring. Equivalently its evaluation map from global sections tensored with the structure sheaf is surjective.
