Linkage disequilibrium (D=P(AB)−P(A)P(B))

 Home Biology Genetics Population genetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 198 Articles: 3
Linkage disequilibrium is nonindependence of allelic states at different loci in population haplotypes. For alleles A,B, D=P(AB)−P(A)P(B). It is a population-frequency property, distinct from genetic linkage, which concerns transmission within meioses.
