Third homotopy group of a Hopf attachment with a sphere summand (π3​(Xd,f​)≅Z⊕Z/gcd(d,f))

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Homotopy group Hopf invariant Cohomology ring of a Hopf attachment with a sphere summand
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
Map Xd,f​ to K(Z,2)=CP∞ using its degree-two generator. The homotopy fibre is the total space of the corresponding circle bundle, is 2-connected, and has π3​ equal to that of Xd,f​. Over S2∨S3 its total space is P=S3∪S1​(S1×S3), with H3​(P)=ZA⊕ZB. The four-cell bundle is trivial; its relative degree-four generator has boundary dA+fB, the lifted attaching map. The long exact sequence in relative homology and Hurewicz theorem therefore give π3​(Xd,f​)=Z2/⟨(d,f)⟩. Smith normal form gives the displayed expression when d>0, including f=0.
