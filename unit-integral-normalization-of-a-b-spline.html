Unit-integral normalization of a B-spline (∫Mi​(t)dt=1)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation Spline (mathematics) Spline approximation B-spline
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
For strictly increasing spline knots, Mi​(t)=k[ti​,…,ti+k​](⋅−t)+k−1​ is a nonnegative integral-normalized B-spline. Integrating the truncated power function over its support replaces its spline knot value by (u−ti​)k/k. The order-k divided difference of this degree-k polynomial is its leading coefficient 1/k. Multiplying by the prefactor k in Mi​ gives integral one. The corresponding partition-normalized B-spline is Ni​=(ti+k​−ti​)Mi​/k.
