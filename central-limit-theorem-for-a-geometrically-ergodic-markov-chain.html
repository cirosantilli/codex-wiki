Central limit theorem for a geometrically ergodic Markov chain (n​(hn​−πh)⇒N(0,vh​))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Markov chain Monte Carlo Geometric ergodicity
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
For an aperiodic positive Harris recurrent Markov chain with geometric ergodicity, an observable with stationary moment π(∣h∣2+δ)<∞ for some δ>0 satisfies a central limit theorem. Its asymptotic variance is γh​(0)+2∑k≥1​γh​(k), with stationary covariances. Ergodicity alone does not guarantee this theorem.
