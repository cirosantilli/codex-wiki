Periodic domain

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A periodic domain identifies opposite faces of a fundamental cell, producing a flat torus. On an interval [0,L], smooth periodic boundary conditions require u(0,t)=u(L,t) and matching spatial derivatives. These endpoints are one location, rather than two independently controlled walls. Periodic integration by parts has no boundary term, and zero-mean functions have no zero Fourier mode.
