Faddeev-Popov ghost

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing Faddeev-Popov determinant
OurBigBook.com Words: 469 Articles: 8
A Faddeev-Popov ghost field is a Grassmann-valued scalar field whose Gaussian functional integral represents the Faddeev-Popov determinant. Ghosts occur only on internal lines and cancel unphysical gauge-field contributions.
