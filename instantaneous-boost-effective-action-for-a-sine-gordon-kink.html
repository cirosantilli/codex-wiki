Substitute the instantaneous Sine-Gordon kink profile θK​(γ(x−X)) and prescribed velocity −γX˙θK′​ with γ=(1−X˙2)−1/2 into the field density. For A1​=0, A0​=xf(t), the integrals ∫(θK′​)2=8 and ∫θK′​=2π give the displayed collective-coordinate effective Lagrangian. Its equation is d(8γX˙)/dt=−2πf. This is relativistic particle motion with mass 8 and topological coupling charge 2π. When γ varies, the prescribed velocity omits a width-change contribution; acceleration correction for an instantaneous boosted kink describes the exact chain-rule distinction.
