Higman's embedding theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Group presentation Recursive presentation of a group
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
A finitely generated group embeds in a finitely presented group if and only if it has a recursive presentation of a group. The necessity follows by enumerating proofs that substituted generator words are trivial in the larger finite group presentation. The sufficiency is the embedding theorem; it also applies to effectively countably generated recursive presentations after an effective two-generator embedding. This is the theorem of Subgroups of finitely presented groups.
