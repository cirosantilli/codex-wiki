Protein phosphatase

 Home Biology Enzyme
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 4
A protein phosphatase is an enzyme that removes a phosphate group from a modified protein through hydrolysis. This restores a modifiable site rather than regenerating ATP by simply running protein kinase chemistry backward.
