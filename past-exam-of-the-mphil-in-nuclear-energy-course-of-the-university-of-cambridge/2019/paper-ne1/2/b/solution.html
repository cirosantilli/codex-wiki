The one-group particle balance with a prescribed external volume source S can be written
v1​∂t∂ϕ​=−∇⋅J−Σa​ϕ+νΣf​ϕ+S,J=−D∇ϕ.
(12)
Here ϕ is scalar neutron flux, D is the neutron diffusion coefficient, and Σa​,Σf​ are macroscopic cross sections. The terms describe accumulation, current divergence, absorption, nuclear fission production and external input.
For a steady state, set ∂t​ϕ=0; source-free means S=0. In a homogeneous medium D is spatially constant, so substitution gives
D∇2ϕ+(νΣf​−Σa​)ϕ=0.
(13)
The supplied notation defines η as the effective production per absorption, η=νΣf​/Σa​. Therefore
∇2ϕ+D(η−1)Σa​​ϕ=0.​
(14)
This identification uses the absorption accounting for the homogeneous model; a fuel-only reproduction factor with other absorbers excluded would not automatically equal this η. The derivation assumes one prescribed energy group, constant effective coefficients and the diffusion approximation; all neutron production included here is represented by the steady nuclear fission term.
