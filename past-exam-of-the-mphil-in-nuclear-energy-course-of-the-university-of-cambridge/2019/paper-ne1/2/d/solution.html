R2a2​+H2π2​=Bm2​,R2=Bm2​a2+π2/x2​.
(21)
The cylinder volume is consequently
V(x)=Bm3​πx​(a2+x2π2​)3/2,x>0.
(22)
Its logarithmic derivative is
dxdlnV​=x1​−x(a2x2+π2)3π2​=x(a2x2+π2)a2x2−2π2​.
(23)
The denominator is positive. The derivative changes from negative to positive exactly once, and V diverges as x→0 or x→∞. Hence this stationary point is the global minimum:
RH​=x∗​=2.4052​π​=1.84735.​
(24)
Equivalently H/(2R)≃0.924. This is an aspect ratio in the supplied idealized boundary value problem; it does not determine an absolute size without a specified material parameter.
