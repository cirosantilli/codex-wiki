The cobalt-60 mass is approximately 5.73×10−8 kg. The nuclear decay constant follows from the half-life, using a 365.25-day year:
λ=5.27(365.25)(86400)ln2​=4.16784×10−9 s−1.
(1)
Since radioactive activity is A=λN, the parent population at certification is
N=4.16784×10−92.4×109​=5.75838×1017.
(2)
Convert the supplied atomic mass into molar mass, M=0.05993382 kgmol−1, and use the Avogadro constant:
m=NA​NM​=6.02214076×1023(5.75838×1017)(0.05993382)​=5.73088×10−8 kg.​
(3)
This is about 57.3 μg of the specified isotope. It is not the total mass of the pellets or capsule, which can also contain other material.
