Use the thermal power when applying nuclear fuel burnup. No capacity factor is supplied, so take a full-power year of 365 days; if the operating fraction were u, the annual quantities in this part would be multiplied by u.
Pth​=0.371650​=4459.459 MW,Eth​=4459.459(365)=1.627703×106 MWdyr−1.

F=P+W,Fxf​=Pxp​+Wxw​,

so
PF​=xf​−xw​xp​−xw​​=0.007−0.0030.050−0.003​=11.75.

F=11.75M=347.7365 tUyr−1​,W=318.1419 tUyr−1.
