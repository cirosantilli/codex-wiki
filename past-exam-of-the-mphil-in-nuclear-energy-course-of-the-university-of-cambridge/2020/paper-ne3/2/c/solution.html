Neglect absorption, other constituents and changes in neutron energy within the thin sheet. For any other assumed composition, use the displayed weighted formula; mass fractions must first be converted to atomic fractions. Since the supplied elemental cross-sections span 2.8–4.1 barns, any mixture of these three elements at the stated number density gives approximately 2.39–3.48%. This is a bound within the idealized mixture model, not a claim that every such composition is stable austenite.
