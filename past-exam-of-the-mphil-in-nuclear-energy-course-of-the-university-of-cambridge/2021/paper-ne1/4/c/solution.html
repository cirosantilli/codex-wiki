Use neutron fluence Φ as the independent variable and abbreviate a=σaTh​, b=σaU​ and c=σcTh​. Substituting the parent solution into the second linear differential equation gives
dΦdNU​​+bNU​=cNTh0​e−aΦ.
(34)
The integrating factor is ebΦ. Integrating from the initial state yields
NU​(Φ)=NU0​e−bΦ+b−acNTh0​​(e−aΦ−e−bΦ),b=a.​
(35)
The first term is survival of the initial population; the second accumulates production at earlier exposures, weighted by subsequent survival. For b=a, the continuous limit is (NU0​+cNTh0​Φ)e−aΦ. The same formula applies to a time-varying prescribed flux through Φ(t); intervals of zero flux leave both populations unchanged.
