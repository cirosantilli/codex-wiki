For the defining n-dimensional special linear Lie algebra module, with n≥2, write Ti​(p)=ei​⊗p(y1​,…,yn​). The equivariant contraction is C(Ti​(p))=∂yi​​p. The map ι(f)=∑i​ei​⊗yi​f is also equivariant, since ∑i​ei​⊗yi​ is an invariant identity tensor. Direct differentiation gives Cι=(n+1)I, so the displayed direct sum follows. Its trace-free part is an irreducible representation of highest weight ω1​+2ωn−1​. Its highest-weight vector is e1​⊗yn2​; the Weyl dimension formula gives dimension n(n−1)(n+2)/2, exactly dimkerC.
