Upper-bound sieve

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Sieve theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 21
An upper-bound sieve bounds the size of a sifted set from above. In a dimension-one problem with one forbidden class modulo each relevant prime p, its main density factor is comparable to ∏p​(1−1/p).
