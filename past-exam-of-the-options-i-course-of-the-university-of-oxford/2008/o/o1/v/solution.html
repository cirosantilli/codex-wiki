The recessive alternative distinguishes CC from the other two types while assigning CT and TT the same disease-risk category. Pooling those two types therefore removes a distinction that this alternative does not use. The resulting counts are 83,1856 for cases and 50,1885 for controls, arranged as CC versus non-CC.
The resulting two-by-two table has (2−1)(2−1)=1​ reference degree of freedom. Since 8.41>3.84, reject no association at the five-percent level using the supplied chi-squared test. The observed CC proportion is higher among cases, 83/1939, than among controls, 50/1935, so the direction agrees with the proposed increased-risk category.
