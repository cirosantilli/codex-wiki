The convective acceleration is
uux​+vuy​=sinxcosx,uvx​+vvy​=−sinycosy.
(30)
Steady Euler equations therefore give px​=−ρsinxcosx and py​=ρsinycosy. Integrating these compatible equations yields
p(x,y)=p0​−2ρ​sin2x+2ρ​sin2y​,
(31)
with arbitrary additive constant p0​. As a check, p/ρ+(u2+v2)/2=p0​/ρ+1/2−ψ2, indeed constant on each streamline. This is a Steady Euler flow with vorticity proportional to its stream function.
