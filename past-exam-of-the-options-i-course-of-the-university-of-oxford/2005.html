Orb(ω)={ωg:g∈G},H=Stab(ω)={g∈G:ωg=ω}.

The rotational action on the eight vertices of a cube is transitive. Model the vertices as (±1,±1,±1). For any desired sign triple, choose a permutation whose parity makes the corresponding signed permutation matrix have determinant one; it maps (1,1,1) to that vertex and preserves the cube. A rotation fixing a vertex fixes the axis through that vertex, the centre and the opposite vertex. It cyclically permutes the three incident edges, giving precisely rotations by 0,2π/3,4π/3. Thus the stabilizer is cyclic of order three.

An element is algebraic over F when some nonzero polynomial in F[x] vanishes at it. Its minimal polynomial is the monic polynomial of least positive degree with this property; scaling a least-degree nonzero vanishing polynomial makes it monic.
If m1​,m2​ are two such polynomials, they have the same least degree. Their difference has smaller degree because both are monic, and it also vanishes at a. Minimality forces the difference to be zero. Hence the monic minimal polynomial is unique.

A set E⊆[0,1] is null when for every ε>0 it can be covered by countably many open intervals whose lengths sum to less than ε. Equivalently its Lebesgue outer measure is zero. This definition does not presuppose measurability of E.

A topology on X contains ∅,X, is closed under arbitrary unions and under finite intersections. The cofinite family has these properties: a nonempty union contains one cofinite member and therefore has finite complement; the complement of a finite intersection is a finite union of finite sets. The empty union and the sets X,∅ are included explicitly.
Any two nonempty open sets intersect, because their intersection has finite complement in the infinite set X. Thus no two disjoint nonempty open sets can separate X, proving connectedness. For compactness, take any open cover and choose a nonempty member U. Only finitely many points lie outside U. Choose one covering member for each of those points. Together with U these give a finite subcover. Thus X is compact.
