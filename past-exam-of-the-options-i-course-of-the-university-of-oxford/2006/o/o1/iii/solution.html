OurBigBook.com Words: 76
The expected counts in the final two separate classes are too small for a reliable chi-squared asymptotic approximation. Pooling the last three classes gives the tail class ≥7, with observed frequency 8+3+1=12 and expected frequency 5.464+1.473+0.447=7.384. This exceeds the usual expected-count guideline of 5. Pooling only the last two would leave an expected count of 1.920, still too small. Pooling requires recomputing the contribution from the pooled totals, rather than adding the old separate contributions.
