After pooling, there are eight categories: 0,1,…,6,≥7. Their frequencies have one constraint because they sum to N, and one model parameter was fitted. Thus the reference chi-squared distribution has
ν=8−1−1=6

degrees of freedom. The reported statistic satisfies 25.914>22.46, the stated upper 0.001 cutoff for six degrees of freedom. Therefore reject the Poisson model at the 0.1% significance level under the independence and common-mean assumptions. The discrepancy is especially pronounced in the zero-count class, but the test assesses the whole fitted distribution. The conclusion uses the approximate Pearson chi-squared goodness-of-fit test, not an exact finite-sample tail probability.
