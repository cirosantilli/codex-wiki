There are six pooled classes, one normalization constraint, and one fitted Poisson distribution parameter. Thus the reference degrees of freedom are 6−1−1=4​. Under the null model the Pearson statistic has approximately the Chi-squared distribution with four degrees of freedom. Its supplied 5% upper critical value is 9.49.
Because 2.07<9.49​, do not reject the Poisson count distribution at the 5% level. This is consistency with that distribution, not proof that it is exactly true. A test of the marginal count frequencies also does not by itself verify independence of successive time windows.
