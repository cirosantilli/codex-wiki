In characteristic p, the binomial theorem gives (α+1)p=αp+1. Hence
(α+1)p−(α+1)−a=αp−α−a=0.
(1)
Iterating shows that all α+j, with j∈Fp​, are roots. They are distinct, since the prime-field elements j are distinct. There are p of them, equal to the polynomial's degree. Thus if α∈F,
Xp−X−a=j∈Fp​∏​(X−α−j)​
(2)
splits into distinct linear factors in F[X]. Equivalently, its derivative is −1, so no repeated roots are possible.

Interpret the displayed factorization as a proper factorization, with 1≤r≤p−1; that restriction is necessary for the conclusion. In a splitting field, the monic degree-r factor has r of the distinct roots of the original polynomial. Comparing its coefficient of Xr−1 gives
−a1​=∑j∈J​(α+j)=rα+∑j∈J​j,
(3)
for an r-element subset J⊆Fp​. Both a1​ and the final sum lie in F. Since 0<r<p, the element r is invertible in F, so
α=r−1​−a1​−j∈J∑​j​∈F.​
(4)
Part (1) then gives complete splitting into distinct linear factors. Thus an Artin–Schreier polynomial of this form either is irreducible or splits completely; a trivial factorization with r=0 or r=p would not imply a root in F.

A topology on X contains ∅ and X, is closed under arbitrary unions, and is closed under finite intersections. Its members are the open sets. A subset is closed precisely when its complement in X is open.

For each continuous map to a Hausdorff target, equality of its values is reflexive, symmetric and transitive. The relation R requires all these equalities simultaneously. Explicitly, f(x)=f(x) gives reflexivity; f(x)=f(y) implies f(y)=f(x); and f(x)=f(y)=f(z) gives f(x)=f(z). A QR decomposition is a factorization A=QR with QTQ=I and R upper triangular. An orthogonal transformation preserves the Euclidean norm because
∥Qx∥2=xTQTQx=xTx=∥x∥2.​
(76)
For the basic QR algorithm, start with A0​=A and repeatedly form
Ak​=Qk​Rk​,Ak+1​=Rk​Qk​.
(77)
In a convergent diagonal form, the diagonal entries give the eigenvalues; practical symmetric-matrix implementations use shifts and deflation to obtain and isolate such convergence. The basic unshifted iteration alone need not converge diagonally for every symmetric input.

Ak+1​=Rk​Qk​=QkT​Ak​Qk​.​
(78)
