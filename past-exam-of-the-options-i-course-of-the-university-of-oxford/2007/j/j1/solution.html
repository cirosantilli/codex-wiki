∮Γ​B⋅dℓ=μ0​IS​,IS​=∫S​J⋅ndS,

with boundary and surface orientations related by the right-hand rule. Stokes' theorem gives the left side as ∫S​(∇×B)⋅ndS, proving the integral law from the stated curl equation.
For a circular contour of radius r about the axis, the left side is 2πrBθ​. For r≤a the enclosed current is Ir2/a2; for r≥a it is I. Therefore
B(r)=⎩⎨⎧​2πa2μ0​Ir​eθ​,2πrμ0​I​eθ​,​0<r≤a,r≥a.​​
(27)
Both expressions agree at the boundary. At the axis the field is zero, interpreted by continuity even though the angular unit vector is undefined there.
