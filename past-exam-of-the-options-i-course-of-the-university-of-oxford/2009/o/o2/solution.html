With immediate release and ideal independent uniform sampling of fish, each catch is a Bernoulli trial with tagged-success probability p=k/N. Stopping at the rth success makes the count of untagged catches a negative binomial random variable. A total of x failures requires r−1 successes among the first x+r−1 trials and a success on the last trial, so the probability is (r−1x+r−1​)pr(1−p)x. Separate days use disjoint independent sequences of trials, hence their counts form an independent and identically distributed sample. Equal catch probabilities alone do not mathematically imply independence; this is the usual repeated independent sampling assumption in the stated model.
