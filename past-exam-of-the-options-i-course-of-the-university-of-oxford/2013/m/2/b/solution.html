Put Yn​=n(1−Mn​)≥0. For fixed x≥0 and sufficiently large n>x,
P(Yn​>x)=P(Mn​<1−x/n)=(1−x/n)n⟶e−x.
(68)
For x<0 its distribution function is zero. The limiting distribution function is therefore F(x)=0 for x<0, F(x)=1−e−x for x≥0, the exponential distribution of rate one. Since this distribution function is continuous everywhere,
n(1−Mn​) d​ Exp(1).​
