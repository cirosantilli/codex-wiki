Logical formula (ϕ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 93 Articles: 1
A logical formula is a finite expression constructed by the formation rules of a formal language. Propositional formulas combine propositional variables by logical connectives. First-order formulas additionally contain terms, atomic relations and quantifiers. A logical formula is a syntactic object; its truth is determined only after specifying a suitable interpretation and values for its free variables.
