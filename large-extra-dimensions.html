Large extra dimensions (MPl2​=M∗n+2​Vn​)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Compactification (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
Large-extra-dimension models lower the fundamental higher-dimensional gravitational scale M∗​ while obtaining a large four-dimensional reduced Planck mass from compact volume Vn​. Ordinary matter can be localized on a brane while gravity propagates in the bulk. The Planck mass from compactification volume relation explains weak effective four-dimensional gravity but leaves volume stabilization and the origin of a large internal volume as separate problems.
