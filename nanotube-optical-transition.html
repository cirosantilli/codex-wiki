Nanotube optical transition (Eii​)

 ... Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Carbon Carbon nanotube Single-walled carbon nanotube
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
An optical excitation between nanotube electronic subbands, often labelled Eii​ for prominent axial-polarization transitions. One-dimensional Van Hove singularities enhance the joint density of states, but selection rules, oscillator strengths and strong excitons interactions determine actual absorption peaks. Dielectric environment, bundling, doping and defects can change those peaks.
