To first order in α′, the conventional Euclidean dilaton coupling gives β​μνG​=α′[Rμν​−Hμρσ​Hν​ρσ/4+2∇μ​∇ν​Φ] and β​μνB​=α′[−∇ρHρμν​/2+(∇ρΦ)Hρμν​]. The scalar coefficient is (d−26)/6+α′[(∇Φ)2−∇2Φ/2−H2/24]. Here H is the Kalb-Ramond field strength. Vanishing of these coefficients, including higher-order corrections, is the background-field condition for quantum Weyl transformations to remain a gauge symmetry. The scalar coefficient includes the central charge deficit and is not implied by the metric equation alone.
