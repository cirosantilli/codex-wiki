For an integrable discrete-time martingale, stop at the first time ∣fn​∣≥N. For any deterministic m, conditioning the terminal value fm​ on each event {τ=j} gives E∣fτ∧m​∣≤E∣fm​∣. Monotone convergence theorem therefore proves the displayed overshoot bound. The maximum of the stopped martingale is at most N∨∣fτ​∣1{τ<∞}​, an integrable variable. This permits a convergence proof without assuming bounded overshoots.
