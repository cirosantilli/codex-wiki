OurBigBook.com Words: 91
Fix the value w(∞)=0 for positive-degree sigma-model lumps. Their holomorphic rational maps can be written p/q, where q is monic of degree k, p has degree at most k−1, and the polynomials have no common zero. The coefficients of p and the nonleading coefficients of q give 2k complex parameters; coprimality is an open condition. The topological degree is k, obtained by counting generic preimages with multiplicity. Allowing a common factor lowers the degree after cancellation and can describe a degeneration rather than another smooth point of this degree-k parameter space.
