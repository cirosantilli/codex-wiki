Residue degree (f(L/K)=[kL​:kK​])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
The degree of the residue field extension induced by a finite extension of complete discretely valued fields. Along with the ramification index, it satisfies [L:K]=ef. An unramified extension additionally has separable residue extension; a totally ramified extension has residue degree one.
