Riemannian parallel-spinor Ricci-flatness (∇ψ=0, ψ=0⟹Ric=0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 58
A parallel spinor has zero spin curvature. Contracting the spinorial Ricci identity with a gamma matrix gives Rμν​γνψ=0. For each fixed μ, squaring Clifford multiplication by that Ricci row gives ∣Rμ⋅​∣2ψ=0. Riemannian positivity and a nonzero spinor force every row to vanish. This implication is local and does not itself require compactness; the compact hypothesis is used to obtain parallelism from harmonicity.
