Nonlinear ODE bounds from interior extrema (f′′=f+(f′)2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Topological analysis Equicontinuity Arzelà-Ascoli theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
If a thrice differentiable solution has both endpoint values and both endpoint first derivatives bounded in modulus by N, it obeys ∣f∣≤N and ∣f′∣≤N throughout the interval. A positive interior maximum of f would give f′′=f>0, and a negative interior minimum would give f′′=f<0, both impossible. For w=f′, differentiation gives w′′=w+2ww′, so the same argument applies at an extremum of w. The resulting uniform bound and Lipschitz constant imply total boundedness of the solution family by the Arzelà-Ascoli theorem.
