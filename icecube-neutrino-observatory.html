IceCube Neutrino Observatory

OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 6
IceCube turns Antarctic ice into a Cherenkov detector: 5160 digital optical modules on 86 strings span depths of 1450–2450m. Hot-water drilling allowed deployment before refreezing. Neutrino interactions produce charged particles; their Cherenkov radiation, rather than the incoming neutrino, supplies the optical readout.[ref]
Neutrino astronomy asks where the incoming neutrino originated. A Cherenkov detector asks how its reaction products make measurable light. Connecting these questions requires track reconstruction, detection efficiency and background tests. An astronomical messenger and the light carrying information inside the apparatus are different stages of one inference.
