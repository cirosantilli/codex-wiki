Tensile blob (ξt​∼kB​T/f)

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymer physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 1
A tensile blob is the scale below which thermal conformations of a stretched polymer are weakly disturbed by tension f. Balancing stretching work fξt​ with kB​T gives ξt​∼kB​T/f. For free-chain size law R∼bgν, each blob contains gt​∼(ξt​/b)1/ν monomers. A stretched string of blobs has extension z∼(n/gt​)ξt​ and stretching free energy of order kB​Tn/gt​. This description requires blobs larger than individual statistical segments and smaller than the unperturbed coil.
