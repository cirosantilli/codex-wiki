Sigma c baryon (Σc++,+,0​,Σc∗++,∗+,∗0​)

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron Baryon Charmed baryon
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The symmetric light pairs uu,{ud},dd with one charm quark give isospin one and charges 2,1,0. Their light spin is one; coupling the charm spin gives the J=1/2 Sigma c baryon triplet and J=3/2 excited-spin triplet conventionally marked by a star.
