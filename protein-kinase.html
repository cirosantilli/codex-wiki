Protein kinase

 Home Biology Enzyme
OurBigBook.com Words: 483 Articles: 28
A protein kinase is an enzyme that catalyzes phosphorylation of a protein. In receptor tyrosine kinase signaling, ATP supplies the transferred phosphate group.
