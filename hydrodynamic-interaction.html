Hydrodynamic interaction

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 351 Articles: 6
Bodies immersed in one fluid affect each other through the flow that each produces. At large separation, a force-driven sphere generates a Stokeslet that modifies the other sphere's velocity. These interactions produce off-diagonal entries in the hydrodynamic mobility matrix.
