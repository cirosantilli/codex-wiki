Put γ=mω/ℏ. Substitution of ψ=F(y)e−γy2/2 in the one-dimensional Schrödinger equation with energy (n+1/2)ℏω gives
F′′−2γyF′+2γnF=0.
(45)
For degrees zero, one and two, coefficient comparison yields, up to independent nonzero constants,
F0​=1,F1​=y,F2​=y2−2γ1​.​
(46)
For the shifted coordinates yi​=xi​−(r0​)i​, let G(y)=e−γ∣y∣2/2. A basis of wave functions for the first three energy levels is:
  • N=0: G​.
  • N=1: y1​G, y2​G, y3​G​.
  • N=2: (yi2​−1/(2γ))G (i=1,2,3),y1​y2​G, y1​y3​G, y2​y3​G​.
These are products of the one-dimensional polynomial solutions. Arbitrary linear combinations within each listed eigenspace are also stationary states. Their counts 1,3,6 agree with part (a), so the degeneracies have all been included.
