Count the original infected person first. Conditional on their producing X=k new infections, each of the k new people initiates an independent descendant family with the same law as the original total. If Ti​ denotes that family size, the branching property gives
T=d1+i=1∑X​Ti​.​
(53)
The family variables are mutually independent and independent of X. At X=0 the sum is empty and equals zero, so the total is one. This is the total progeny decomposition of a Galton-Watson process.
