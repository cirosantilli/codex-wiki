A metric space is complete when every Cauchy sequence in it converges to a point of that space. A sequence is Cauchy when, for each ϵ>0, there is N such that d(xm​,xn​)<ϵ whenever m,n≥N. The requirement that the limit lie in the space distinguishes completeness from having limits only in a larger ambient space.
