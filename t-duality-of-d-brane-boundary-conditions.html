T-duality of D-brane boundary conditions (N↔D)

 ... Worldsheet Two-dimensional conformal field theory Free boson conformal field theory Compact boson Momentum and winding modes T-duality
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 1
For a compact string embedding map coordinate, T-duality preserves its left-moving part and reverses its right-moving part. Consequently ∂τ​X=∂σ​X and ∂σ​X=∂τ​X. A Neumann boundary condition becomes a Dirichlet boundary condition and conversely. A D-brane wrapping the circle loses one spatial worldvolume dimension; a D-brane transverse to it gains one in the dual description.
