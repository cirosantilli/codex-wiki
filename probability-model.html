Probability model ((Ω,F,P))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A mathematical account of random outcomes using a probability space and specified random variables. It determines probabilities for events or observed data. A statistical model commonly supplies a family of such probability distributions with unknown parameters; choosing a model and estimating its parameters are distinct tasks.
