Ohm's law (V=IR)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Electrical resistance and conductance Electrical resistance
OurBigBook.com Words: 593 Articles: 11
Ohm's law states that the potential difference across an ohmic component equals its current times its resistance: V=IR.
