Duffin-Schaeffer polynomial derivative inequality (∥p(k)∥∞​≤Tn(k)​(1))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation Bernstein inequality for algebraic polynomials Markov inequality for polynomial derivatives
OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 1
If a real degree-at-most-n polynomial has absolute value at most one at every extremum cos(jπ/n) of the Chebyshev polynomial Tn​, its kth derivative on [−1,1] is bounded by Tn(k)​(1), for 1≤k≤n. This strengthens the Markov inequality for polynomial derivatives by replacing a bound on the whole interval with these finitely many samples. The node set is rigid, as quantified by uniqueness of Chebyshev derivative-norming nodes.
