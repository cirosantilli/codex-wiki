Control bus

 Home Computer science Computer bus Shared computer bus
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The computer bus paths specifying operations and coordinating progress, including requests and acknowledgments. Some control travels from initiator to target and some returns. In the diagram, bus arbitration requests and grants are shown separately from transfer control to distinguish choosing an initiator from executing its bus transaction.[ref]
