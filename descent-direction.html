Descent direction (∇f(x)Ts<0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Line search
OurBigBook.com Words: 89 Articles: 1
A vector s is a descent direction for a differentiable function f at x if its directional derivative ∇f(x)Ts is negative. The first-order expansion f(x+αs)=f(x)+α∇f(x)Ts+o(α) then gives f(x+αs)<f(x) for sufficiently small positive α. The negative gradient is a descent direction whenever the gradient is nonzero.
