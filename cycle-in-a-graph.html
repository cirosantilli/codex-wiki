Cycle in a graph

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Path in a graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 14
A cycle is a closed path: its final vertex is joined back to its initial vertex, and no other vertex is repeated.
A closed walk can revisit other vertices and need not contain a simple cycle with every property of the whole closed walk, such as using both of two edge colours.
