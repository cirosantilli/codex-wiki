Choose a directed path ηxy​ of positive-probability transitions for each ordered pair of states in a finite reversible Markov chain. Write Q(e)=π(u)P(u,v) for a directed edge e=(u,v), and let Ne​(η) count its occurrences. Define
ρ=maxe:Q(e)>0​Q(e)1​∑x,y​π(x)π(y)∣ηxy​∣Ne​(ηxy​).
(15)
The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality bounds (f(x)−f(y))2 by ∣ηxy​∣ times the sum of squared differences along the path. Multiply by π(x)π(y)/2, sum and interchange finite sums. The identity Varπ​(f)=21​∑x,y​π(x)π(y)(f(x)−f(y))2 proves the bound. This convention counts directed edges and retains the factor 1/2 in the Dirichlet form of a Markov chain.
