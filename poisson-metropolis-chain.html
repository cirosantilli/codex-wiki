Poisson Metropolis chain (un​=21​min(1,λ/(n+1)), dn​=21​min(1,n/λ))

A Poisson Metropolis chain samples a Poisson distribution by proposing an upward or downward unit step with equal probability and reflecting a downward proposal at zero into a self-proposal. Its upward and downward transition probabilities are the displayed un​,dn​, with d0​=0, and the diagonal entry is 1−un​−dn​. For πn​=e−λλn/n!, adjacent stationary fluxes equal 21​min(πn​,πn+1​). Hence detailed balance holds. Positive adjacent probabilities imply an irreducible Markov chain; P(0,0)≥1/2 implies aperiodicity. The normalized invariant Poisson distribution implies positive recurrence, so the countable-state Markov chain convergence theorem applies. The boundary proposal probability is essential: forcing every proposal at zero to go upward would require a different acceptance ratio.
