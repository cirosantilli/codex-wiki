Killing-form Einstein metric (g=B,Ric=−41​B)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Bi-invariant pseudo-Riemannian metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
On a real semisimple Lie group, the nondegenerate invariant Killing form gives a bi-invariant pseudo-Riemannian metric. For left-invariant fields the Koszul formula gives ∇X​Y=[X,Y]/2 and R(X,Y)Z=−[[X,Y],Z]/4. Taking the trace gives Ric(Y,Z)=−B(Y,Z)/4, proving the Einstein property. On a compact semisimple group, −B is positive definite and has Einstein constant 1/4. The indefinite general construction is not a proof of existence of a positive-definite bi-invariant metric on a noncompact group.
