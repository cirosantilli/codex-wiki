Covered by the official notes: definition of a pure force via constant rest mass (PDF p. 13; handwritten p. 8).[ref]
The page labels dpμ/dτ as a proper force; that is the four-force. The requested proper force is the three-force measured in the instantaneous rest frame of the object. For a pure force its magnitude is m0​ times the proper acceleration, and in that frame the four-force has components (0,f0​).
