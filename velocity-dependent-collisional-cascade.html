Velocity-dependent collisional cascade

For catastrophic disruption threshold QD∗​=Qb​Db, encounter speed vrel​=vp​Dp, fixed belt volume, and small impactors, the catastrophic projectile diameter scales as Dcc​∝D1+(b−2p)/3. If α>1, small destructive impactors dominate the catastrophic planetesimal collision rate, giving
Rcc​(D)∝D2+pDcc1−α​.
(20)
Mass per logarithmic bin scales as D4−α. Constant mass flux in a collisional cascade then gives
α=6+b−2p21+b+p​.
(21)
Size-independent speed and strength recover the Dohnanyi collisional cascade slope 7/2. A size-dependent belt thickness changes this derivation. The broader coupled size-and-velocity problem is studied by Pan and Schlichting.
