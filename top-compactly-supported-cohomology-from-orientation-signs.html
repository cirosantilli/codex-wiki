Top compactly supported cohomology from orientation signs (Hcn​(M;Z))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Cohomology Compactly supported cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
For a connected n-manifold without boundary, a finite good cover presents its top compactly supported cohomology by one generator per disc and relations ui​=±uj​ on overlaps. This follows from the compact-support Mayer–Vietoris sequence and vanishing above dimension n. The overlap graph is connected, so the group is cyclic. Coherent local orientations give Z; an orientation-reversing loop gives Z/2.
