Frustrated bond in an antiferromagnetic Ising ring (Sj​Sj+1​=+1)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Spin model Ising model Antiferromagnetic Ising model Antiferromagnetic Ising ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
A neighboring parallel pair in an antiferromagnetic Ising ring. It costs 2J relative to a satisfied antiparallel pair. If there are m such pairs, the energy is −JN+2Jm, and the periodic boundary conditions force N−m to be even because ∏j​Sj​Sj+1​=1. Thus an odd ring has 2N ground states, obtained by locating one such pair and choosing one overall Ising spin orientation. The even ring has two ground states with no such pair. This definition uses the local preferred ordering; a parallel pair is a defect in staggered order rather than a wall between uniformly magnetized domains.
