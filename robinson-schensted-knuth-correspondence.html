Robinson-Schensted-Knuth correspondence (A⟷(P,Q))

 ... Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young tableau Robinson–Schensted correspondence
OurBigBook.com Words: 232 Articles: 2
List each pair (i,j) with multiplicity aij​, sorted first by i and then by j. Apply row insertion to the lower letters j, and record the upper letters i in the new cells. The row insertion comparison is strictly greater than the carried entry. The result is a pair of Semistandard Young tableaux of the same shape. Reverse insertion removes the rightmost cell containing the largest recording entry, then replaces the rightmost strictly smaller entry in each preceding row. It reconstructs the ordered list, proving the bijection. The insertion and recording contents are respectively the column sums and row sums of A.
