Oblique projection (Q(v+w)=v(H=V⊕W))

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Linear operator Projection (linear algebra)
OurBigBook.com Words: 194 Articles: 1
If two closed subspaces of a Hilbert space give H=V⊕W, the projection onto V along W fixes V and vanishes on W. It need not be an orthogonal projection. The bounded inverse theorem applied to the addition map (v,w)↦v+w on the complete component spaces proves boundedness of the component maps. With both complementary subspaces nonzero, its operator norm and that of I−Q equal secθV,W⊥​, with the directed subspace angle convention. Indeed, for fixed v∈V, the smallest possible norm of v+w over w∈W is ∥PW⊥​v∥, which proves the formula for ∥Q∥. Equality with ∥I−Q∥ follows from the block representation Q=(I0​B0​) on V⊕V⊥: both squared operator norms are 1+∥B∥2. If a summand is zero, Q is zero or the identity operator, and one complementary operator norm is zero; those cases must be treated directly.
