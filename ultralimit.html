Ultralimit

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Filter (set theory) Ultrafilter
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 1
A point x is the limit of a map f:I→X along an ultrafilter U on I when f−1(V)∈U for every neighbourhood V of x. Every ultrafilter on a compact space has a limit, and that limit is unique in a Hausdorff space.
