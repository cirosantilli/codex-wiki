Graph total variation denoising is a penalized least-squares estimator for noisy vertex signals on a graph. The squared loss is strictly convex, so the fitted signal is unique. Its basic inequality for a penalized least-squares estimator separates a noise inner product from the change in penalty. The incidence pseudoinverse decomposition controls the nonconstant noise through the geometry of the graph.
