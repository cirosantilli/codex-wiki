Zero-point energy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum harmonic oscillator
OurBigBook.com Words: 80 Articles: 1
Zero-point energy is the nonzero ground-state energy left by quantum fluctuations. One harmonic oscillator contributes ω/2 before any regularization or normal-ordering subtraction.
