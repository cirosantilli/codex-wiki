Mechanism design

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 54
Mechanism design chooses allocation and payment rules while accounting for how agents' information and incentives affect their reports. Social choice theory studies collective choice from preferences, while auction design also uses monetary transfers.
