Loss cone (J<Jlc​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 195 Articles: 2
A loss cone consists of low-angular momentum stellar trajectories whose next pericentre enters a central absorbing region. For capture radius rc​ in a black hole-dominated potential, the Newtonian near-parabolic estimate is Jlc2​≃2GMH​rc​. At radius r and speed v, its angular half-width is Jlc​/(rv). Two-body relaxation can refill this depleted part of phase space.
