Retarded potential

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 365 Articles: 9
A retarded potential evaluates each source at the event on the source's past light cone. This accounts for propagation at the speed of light and enforces causality.
