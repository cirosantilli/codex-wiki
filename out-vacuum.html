Out-vacuum (bi​∣0out​⟩=0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The state annihilated by every annihilation operator of the positive-frequency mode basis chosen in the asymptotic future. It need not coincide with the in-vacuum on a time-dependent spacetime.
