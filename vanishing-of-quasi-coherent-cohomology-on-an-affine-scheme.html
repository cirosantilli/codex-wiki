Vanishing of quasi-coherent cohomology on an affine scheme (Hi>0(SpecA,M)=0)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Quasi-coherent sheaf
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Every quasi-coherent sheaf on an affine scheme has zero higher sheaf cohomology. Together with the equivalence between modules and quasi-coherent sheaves on an affine scheme, this makes affine varieties the basic acyclic pieces for computing cohomology. An affine open cover of a separated scheme has affine finite intersections, so the acyclic cover theorem identifies its Čech cohomology with sheaf cohomology.
