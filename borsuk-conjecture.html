Borsuk conjecture

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 314 Articles: 5
The Borsuk conjecture asserted that every bounded subset of Rd with positive diameter can be partitioned into d+1 subsets of strictly smaller diameter. It is false in sufficiently high dimension.
