Atmospheric turbulence

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Atmospheric optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 174 Articles: 6
Atmospheric turbulence produces changing refractive index fluctuations. These alter optical path lengths, distorting wavefronts and causing astronomical seeing and atmospheric scintillation.
