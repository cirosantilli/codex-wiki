Poincare group

 Home Physics Branch of physics Special relativity Lorentz transformation Lorentz group
OurBigBook.com Words: 172 Articles: 6
The Poincare group is the semidirect product of spacetime translations by the Lorentz group. It is the isometry group of Minkowski spacetime.
