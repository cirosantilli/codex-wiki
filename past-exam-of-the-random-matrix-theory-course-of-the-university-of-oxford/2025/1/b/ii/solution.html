Write the four cyclic factors as A=Mij​, B=Mjk​, C=Mkl​ and D=Mli​. The Gaussian moment pairing theorem gives three terms, corresponding to (AB)(CD), (AC)(BD) and (AD)(BC). We sum each term over i,j,k,l.
For (AB)(CD), the entry covariance gives
i,j,k,l∑​E[AB]E[CD]​=i,j,k,l∑​(δik​+δij​δjk​)(δik​+δkl​δli​)=n3+2n2+n.​
(8)
Indeed the four products leave respectively three, two, two and one free indices. A cyclic relabelling gives exactly the same sum for (AD)(BC).
For the crossing pairing (AC)(BD),
i,j,k,l∑​E[AC]E[BD]​=i,j,k,l∑​(δik​δjl​+δil​δjk​)(δjl​δki​+δji​δkl​)=n2+3n.​
(9)
The first product allows i=k and j=l freely, whereas each other product forces all four indices to coincide. Combining the three pairings and the trace normalization,
E[m4​(n)]=n32(n3+2n2+n)+(n2+3n)​=2+n5​+n25​ ⟶ 2.​
(10)
