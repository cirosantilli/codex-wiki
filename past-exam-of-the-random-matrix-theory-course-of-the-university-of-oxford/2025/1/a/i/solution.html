Taking the transpose gives MT=(XT+X)/2​=M, so M is a symmetric matrix. For i<j, Mij​=(Xij​+Xji​)/2​ has the standard normal distribution, whereas Mii​=2​Xii​ has the normal distribution N(0,2). Distinct upper-triangular entries depend on disjoint sets of the original independent random variables, so they remain independent.
Consequently the joint probability density function, relative to ∏i≤j​dmij​, is
f((mij​)i≤j​)=(4π)−n/2(2π)−n(n−1)/4exp(−41​i∑​mii2​−21​i<j∑​mij2​)=(4π)n/2(2π)n(n−1)/4exp[−tr(m2)/4]​.​
(1)
Here tr(m2)=∑i​mii2​+2∑i<j​mij2​ because m is a symmetric matrix. This is the Gaussian orthogonal ensemble, with off-diagonal variance one and diagonal variance two; the rescaled matrix M/n​ has the corresponding 1/n off-diagonal variance.
We will also use its entry covariance. Expanding in the independent entries of X, for which E[Xij​Xkl​]=δik​δjl​, gives
E[Mij​Mkl​]=δik​δjl​+δil​δjk​.​
(2)
The symbols δab​ are Kronecker deltas. This formula includes the diagonal case, where the two terms both contribute.
