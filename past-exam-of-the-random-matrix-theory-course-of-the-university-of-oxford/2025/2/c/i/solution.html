The correlation functions of a point process use factorial normalization:
R3(k)​(x1​,…,xk​)=(3−k)!3!​∫R3−k​ρ3​(x1​,x2​,x3​)dxk+1​⋯dx3​.
(34)
Start with ρ3​=D3​/6. By Gaudin's lemma, integrating D3​ once gives D2​, and integrating D2​ once gives 2D1​. The factorial factors cancel, so the eigenvalues form a determinantal point process with
R3(1)​(x)=K3​(x,x),​
(35)
and
R3(2)​(x,y)=det(K3​(x,x)K3​(y,x)​K3​(x,y)K3​(y,y)​)=K3​(x,x)K3​(y,y)−K3​(x,y)2.​
(36)
The last equality uses the real symmetry of this kernel. In particular, ∫R3(1)​=3 and ∬R3(2)​=6, as required for three points and six ordered pairs of distinct points.
