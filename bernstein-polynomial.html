Bernstein polynomial

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Stone-Weierstrass theorem Weierstrass approximation theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 680 Articles: 11
For f∈C([0,1]d), its tensor-product Bernstein polynomial is the expectation of f(X1​/n,…,Xd​/n) for independent binomial variables Xj​∼Bin(n,xj​). Uniform continuity and concentration of the binomial variables prove uniform convergence to f.
