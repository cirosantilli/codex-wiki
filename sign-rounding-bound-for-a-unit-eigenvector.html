Sign rounding bound for a unit eigenvector (Δ(S,S)≤n∥vv⊤−vv⊤∥F2​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 65
Suppose vi​=±1/n​ encodes the true partition and v is a unit estimated eigenvector. Every coordinate of the wrong sign contributes at least 1/n to minσ=±1​∥v−σv∥22​=2(1−∣v⊤v∣). Since this is at most 2(1−∣v⊤v∣2), which is the squared Frobenius distance between the rank-one orthogonal projection matrices, the bound follows. Coordinates estimated as zero may be assigned consistently to either group.
