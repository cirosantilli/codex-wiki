Scale invariance of decimal significands

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution Benford law
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
A decimal significand lies in [1,10), obtained by removing the integer power of ten from a positive number. Multiplication by a constant rotates the fractional logarithm modulo one. Invariance under every such rotation makes the fractional logarithm uniform and hence implies Benford law. This is an invariant significand distribution, not a proper scale-invariant distribution for the entire positive number.
