Recurrence relation

OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 1
A recurrence relation specifies members of a sequence in terms of previous members, together with enough initial values to determine the sequence. A three-term recurrence for orthogonal polynomials relates consecutive orders. For example, the physicists' Hermite polynomials satisfy Hn+1​(x)=2xHn​(x)−2nHn−1​(x), starting from H0​=1 and H1​=2x.
