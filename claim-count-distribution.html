Claim count distribution (pn​=P(N=n))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Aggregate claims model
OurBigBook.com Words: 214 Articles: 3
The probability distribution of the nonnegative integer number of claims over a specified accounting period. Its probability generating function determines the count probabilities. A Poisson distribution gives equal count expected value and variance; a Poisson mixture allows heterogeneity in the underlying risk intensity.
