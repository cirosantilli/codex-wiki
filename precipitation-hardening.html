Precipitation hardening

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Metal Alloy
OurBigBook.com Words: 260 Articles: 7
Heat treatment that develops a fine dispersion of a new solid phase in an alloy, impeding dislocation motion. It is distinct from precipitation from a liquid chemical solution. A solid solution may first be formed at high temperature, quenched to retain supersaturation, and then aged to promote solid-state precipitation.
