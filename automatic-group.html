Automatic group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 306 Articles: 5
An automatic group admits an automatic structure for a group: a regular language of representatives and synchronous group multiplier automata recognizing right multiplication by each generator and equality of representatives. Representatives need not be unique. This finite-state description yields effective algorithms for the word problem for a group.
