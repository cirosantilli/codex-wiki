Internal symmetry of a classical field theory

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 408 Articles: 5
An internal symmetry changes field values while leaving spacetime coordinates fixed, preserving the action. A constant phase rotation of a complex scalar field is an example. This distinguishes internal charges from the spacetime translation charges in the canonical stress-energy tensor. Localizing the parameter generally requires gauge fields; a global internal symmetry is not automatically a gauge symmetry.
