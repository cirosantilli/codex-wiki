Lindley recursion (Wk+1​=(Wk​+Zk+1​)+)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory Queue (queueing theory) Workload of a queue
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 1
For arrivals added before the slot's offered service, Zk​ is the arriving work minus that service. With independent identically distributed increments of negative mean, the stationary queue workload has the law supm≥0​∑j=1m​Zj​. Iterating the recursion from the past gives this supremum; the strong law makes it finite. A different arrival/service ordering changes slot-boundary workload but requires its convention to be stated.
