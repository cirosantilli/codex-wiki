Characteristic curves for speed one minus y squared (dy/dx=±(1−y2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 37
For c(y)=1−y2 in the strip ∣y∣<1, the travel-time coordinate is s(y)=artanhy. The characteristic curves are
artanhy±x=constant,
(11)
equivalently y=tanh(C∓x). Outside the strip the same separated ordinary differential equation gives hyperbolic cotangent branches, and the equilibrium curves y=±1 are also characteristic.
