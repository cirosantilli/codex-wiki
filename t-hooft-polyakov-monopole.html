't Hooft-Polyakov monopole

OurBigBook.com Words: 59
A smooth finite-energy monopole in an SU(2) Yang-Mills theory with an adjoint Higgs field uses the normalized Higgs direction at infinity to define a map S∞2​→S2. Its nonzero degree supplies the magnetic topological sector. Unlike a singular Abelian Dirac monopole, the non-Abelian gauge and Higgs fields can have a regular core. In the zero-potential limit the Bogomolny-Prasad-Sommerfield monopole equations are Bi​=±Di​Φ.
