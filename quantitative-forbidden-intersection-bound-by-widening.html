Quantitative forbidden-intersection bound by widening (ρ≤max{e−gℓ+D2n/4,e−gn+(2g+h)ℓ})

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Forbidden-intersection density increment
OurBigBook.com Words: 101
Let two set families on n coordinates forbid cross-intersection size ℓ, and let ρ be their density product. For 0<δ≤1/10, put g=log(1+δ), h=−log(1−δ−2δ2), and D=g+h. Repeated forbidden-intersection density increments terminate when one endpoint of the forbidden interval reaches zero or the remaining dimension. In the first case, if v steps widened the interval, the cross-intersection bound from cube separation gives logρ≤−gℓ+Dv−v2/n≤−gℓ+D2n/4. In the second case, at least n−ℓ steps occurred and at most ℓ widened, giving logρ≤−gn+(2g+h)ℓ. For a single set family take the square of its density. Choosing δ=1/50 proves ∣A∣≤1.999n for forbidden intersections n/4 and ⌊n/8⌋, with small dimensions handled directly.
