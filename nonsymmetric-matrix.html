Nonsymmetric matrix (AT=A)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
A nonsymmetric matrix is a square matrix unequal to its transpose. Its right eigenvectors need not be orthogonal, and it need not be a diagonalizable matrix. For instance, A=(10​12​) has eigenvectors (1,0)T and (1,1)T for its distinct eigenvalues. A starting vector (1,0)T has nonzero inner product with (1,1)T but zero coefficient in that direction in the eigenbasis. This is why power iteration and inverse iteration require a nonzero spectral component rather than an ordinary nonorthogonality test in the general case.
