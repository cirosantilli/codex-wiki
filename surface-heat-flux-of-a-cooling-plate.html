Surface heat flux of a cooling plate (qs​(t)=kΔT[1+2∑n≥1​e−n2t/τ0​]/a)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth Lithosphere Oceanic plate cooling model
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For a hot initial plate cooled from above, Fourier's law gives the displayed upward heat flux. At old ages only the steady value kΔT/a remains. At young ages the sum approaches a Gaussian integral, giving qs​∼kΔT/πκt​. This divergence is an idealized sudden surface cooling, not an infinite observed ridge heat flux.
