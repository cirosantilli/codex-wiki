Coproduct-to-product comparison in a finitary abelian category (j:∐i​Ai​→∏i​Ai​)

 ... Category theory Enriched category Semi-additive category Additive category Abelian category Finitary abelian category
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
In a complete finitary abelian category, the comparison j with diagonal matrix entries the identity and off-diagonal entries zero is monic. Express its source as the directed colimit of finite biproducts. Their maps into the product have finite-coordinate retractions and are therefore split monomorphisms; the finitary axiom applies to this cocone. If the category is also cofinitary abelian category, duality makes j epic and hence invertible. For countably many copies of any object, the shift-and-fold argument in countable biproducts in an additive category force triviality then forces the identity to be zero, so every object is a zero object.
