Newtonian gravitational stress tensor (Tij​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field
OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 1
For a Newtonian gravitational potential obeying the Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity, define Tij​=[∂i​Φ∂j​Φ−δij​∣∇Φ∣2/2]/(4πG). Commutation of mixed partial derivatives gives ∂j​Tij​=ρ∂i​Φ. Thus the gravitational force density can be put in conservative form as −∂j​Tij​. The sign is chosen so that Tij​ is added to the momentum flux; reversing the stress convention reverses the divergence sign.
