Stellar halo

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy
OurBigBook.com Words: 126 Articles: 2
A stellar halo is the extended, approximately spheroidal stellar population surrounding a galaxy. It differs from the total dark matter halo. If its outer luminosity density is r−p, its projected light follows the projection of a spherical power-law emissivity and scales as R1−p away from breaks and outer truncation.
