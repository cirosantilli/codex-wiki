For f,g∈E, define their convolution by
(f∗g)(λ)=∑μ+ν=λ​f(μ)g(ν).
(21)
The support restrictions make each sum finite: for fixed upper-cone representatives, the simple root coefficients in the two downward displacements are bounded nonnegative integers. This also proves closure in E and associativity by finite reordering at each coefficient. The unit is δ0​, and δμ​∗δν​=δμ+ν​.
