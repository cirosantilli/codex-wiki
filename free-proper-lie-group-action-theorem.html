Free proper Lie-group action theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Principal bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
If a Lie group acts smoothly, freely, and properly on a smooth manifold P, then the quotient P/G has a unique smooth structure for which P→P/G is a principal bundle. An action by a compact Lie group is automatically proper.
