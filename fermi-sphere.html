Fermi sphere (∣k∣≤kF​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Fermi liquid Fermi surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For an isotropic three-dimensional free electron gas at zero temperature, occupied wavevectors fill a sphere of radius kF​. Including two spin states, n=kF3​/(3π2). The volume in reciprocal space counts occupied states; folding an extended-zone sphere into a first Brillouin zone does not remove states outside that first zone.
