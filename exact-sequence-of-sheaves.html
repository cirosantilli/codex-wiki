Exact sequence of sheaves

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Presheaf of sets on a topological space Sheaf (mathematics) Sheaf of abelian groups
OurBigBook.com Words: 72 Articles: 1
An exact sequence of sheaves of abelian groups is a sequence of sheaf homomorphisms for which the image of each map equals the kernel of the next. Exactness can be checked on every stalk of a sheaf. Exactness of the associated sequences of sections on every open set is a stronger condition: a surjective sheaf homomorphism need only admit lifts locally.
