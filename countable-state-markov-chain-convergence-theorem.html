Countable-state Markov chain convergence theorem (Pn(i,j)→πj​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Convergence to an invariant distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
For an irreducible Markov chain on a finite or countable state space, a positive recurrent state implies a unique stationary distribution π. If the chain is also an aperiodic Markov chain, its transition probabilities satisfy the displayed limit for every pair of states. The same limit holds from any initial probability distribution by bounded convergence. Positive recurrence cannot be omitted in the countably infinite case, and aperiodicity is required for ordinary, rather than averaged, convergence. This is the standard discrete-time convergence theorem; the finite-state statement alone does not cover unbounded renewal countdowns.
