Empirical adequacy

 Home Philosophy Philosophy of science Constructive empiricism
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A theory is empirically adequate when it correctly represents the observable phenomena, including those not yet observed. Agreement with the data already collected is therefore insufficient to establish empirical adequacy.
