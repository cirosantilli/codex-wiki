FRET efficiency (E)

FRET efficiency is the probability that an excitation of the FRET donor ends in transfer to the FRET acceptor. Write kD​=kr​+knr​ for the total decay rate without a FRET acceptor, including radiative fluorescence and other decay, and kT​ for the transfer rate. For constant competing rates,
kT​=kD​(rR0​​)6,E=kD​+kT​kT​​=∫0∞​kT​e−(kD​+kT​)tdt.
(8)
The integral counts transfer events among all excitations in a population with exponential decay. Substitution gives the separation law above. This short rate argument assumes one FRET donor, one FRET acceptor, weak coupling, an available FRET acceptor, and fixed geometry during the decay; it is not a model for arbitrary interacting labels.[ref]
