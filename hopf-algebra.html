Hopf algebra

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field
OurBigBook.com Words: 633 Articles: 15
A Hopf algebra is a compatible algebra and coalgebra equipped with a counit and antipode. Coordinate rings of affine algebraic groups are commutative Hopf algebras.
