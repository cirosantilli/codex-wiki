Polyaddition

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymerization Step-growth polymerization
OurBigBook.com Words: 320 Articles: 1
Polyaddition is step-growth polymerization by joining reactions without elimination of small molecules. Compatible functional groups on different chain lengths remain eligible to join; the word addition alone does not imply chain-growth polymerization.[ref]
