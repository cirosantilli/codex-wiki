Ring of rational functions on a reduced variety (Rat(X))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic variety
OurBigBook.com Words: 151 Articles: 1
For a possibly reducible algebraic variety X, take pairs (U,f) with U a dense open subset and f a regular function on U. Identify pairs agreeing on a common dense open subset. Addition and multiplication on intersections make these classes a ring. For an affine variety with reduced coordinate ring A, this ring is its total quotient ring. For an irreducible variety, it is the function field. An isomorphism of dense open subsets induces an isomorphism of these rings.
