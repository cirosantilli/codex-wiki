Commutative ring spectrum

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Stable homotopy theory Spectrum (topology) Ring spectrum
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A commutative ring spectrum, in the homotopy-category convention, satisfies μτ=μ, where τ interchanges the factors of the smash product of spectra. This condition alone does not assert an E∞​ refinement.
