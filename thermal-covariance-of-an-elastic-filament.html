Thermal covariance of an elastic filament (C(x,y)=kB​TK−1(x,y))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Elastic filament
OurBigBook.com Words: 311 Articles: 4
For a positive quadratic bending operator K with a real orthonormal basis of eigenfunctions, the equipartition theorem gives C(x,y)=kB​T∑n​Wn​(x)Wn​(y)/μn​, where KWn​=μn​Wn​. This is the inverse-operator Green function multiplied by Boltzmann constant and temperature. Unconstrained zero-energy filament modes prevent a normalizable canonical ensemble, and negative modes signal an unstable quadratic model.
For both ends clamped and K=A∂x4​, the diagonal is C(x,x)=kB​Tx3(L−x)3/(3AL3). This differs from a clamped-free tip variance. The center variance is kB​TL3/(192A).
