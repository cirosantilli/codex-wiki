Paley–Wiener–Schwartz theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Paley–Wiener theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 428 Articles: 4
For a compact convex set K⊂Rn, the Fourier transform gives a bijection between distributions supported in K and entire functions satisfying
∣F(ζ)∣≤C(1+∣ζ∣)NeHK​(Imζ),HK​(η)=supx∈K​x⋅η,
(26)
for some C,N. Here HK​ is the support function. In the closed-ball case K=BR​, the exponential factor is eR∣Imζ∣. Convexity is essential to infer support in K from this bound: the support function cannot distinguish a nonconvex set from its convex hull.
