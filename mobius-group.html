Möbius group (M≅PSL2​(C))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Projective linear group
OurBigBook.com Words: 354 Articles: 4
The Möbius group is the group of all Möbius transformations of the Riemann sphere, under function composition. An invertible 2×2 complex matrix represents z↦(az+b)/(cz+d), and nonzero scalar multiples represent the same transformation. Rescaling a representative to determinant one gives a surjective group homomorphism SL2​(C)→M with kernel of a group homomorphism {I,−I}. Thus it is also the projective linear group PGL2​(C) and the quotient PSL2​(C)=SL2​(C)/{±I}.
