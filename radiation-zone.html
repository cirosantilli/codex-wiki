Radiation zone

 Home Physics Branch of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 163 Articles: 4
In the radiation zone of a localized source, the leading electromagnetic fields decay as 1/R, are transverse to the observation direction, and satisfy B=R×E/c. Terms decaying faster than 1/R do not contribute to the limiting radiated power.
