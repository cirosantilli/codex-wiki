Kinetic theory (f(t,x,v))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 23
Describe many-particle systems through distributions on phase space. Transport moves particles along trajectories, while collision or diffusion terms modify their velocity distributions. The Boltzmann equation, Hamiltonian Liouville equation and Fokker-Planck equation are central models.
