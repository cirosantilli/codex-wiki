Moving the currents past each other gives [Lm​,Jna​]=−nJm+na​; the additional Q in the denominator is the adjoint-Casimir reordering term. The resulting Virasoro algebra has central charge c=kdimg/(k+Q/2). On a compact unitary positive-energy module, Lm†​=L−m​.
