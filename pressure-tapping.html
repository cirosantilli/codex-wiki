Pressure tapping

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Lift (force) Pressure coefficient
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A small flush opening communicating the local surface static pressure through tubing to a manometer or pressure transducer. An upstream-facing Pitot tube instead brings incident flow to rest to measure stagnation pressure.
