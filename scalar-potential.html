Scalar potential (V(ϕ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field theory Scalar field
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
A scalar potential is the derivative-free energy density V(ϕ) of a scalar field. Its derivative supplies the force term in the field equation, and its local minima describe candidate vacuum field values.
