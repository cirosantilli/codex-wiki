Number-average molar mass (Mn​)

 Home Chemistry Molar mass
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
The number-average molar mass weights each molecule equally. For counts ni​ with molar masses Mi​,
Mn​=∑i​ni​∑i​ni​Mi​​.
(11)
A mass-proportional SEC detector response requires conversion to counts before determining this average.
