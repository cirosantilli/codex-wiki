Semiclassical cubic mass law for Schwarzschild evaporation (M(t)3=M03​−3αt)

 ... General relativity Black hole Black-hole thermodynamics Hawking temperature Hawking radiation Black-hole evaporation
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
For a large isolated Schwarzschild black hole with a fixed effective set of radiated species, Hawking radiation has T∝M−1 and luminosity P=α/M2 in natural units. Integrating dM/dt=−α/M2 gives the displayed law. A horizon-area blackbody radiation estimate gives α=1/(15360π); greybody factors and species change it. With mass-dependent α, integrate t=∫M(t)M0​​m2dm/α(m) instead. The semiclassical gravity approximation fails near Planck mass, so a formal zero of the cubic law does not settle the final state.
