Cogenerator bound for comma-category solution sets (C(C0​,Qi​)↪D(B,GQi​))

 ... Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Adjoint functors Special adjoint functor theorem Limit form of the special adjoint functor theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 112
Under the hypotheses of the limit form of the special adjoint functor theorem, intersect all subobjects of C supporting x:B→GC. Limit preservation gives a smallest supporting (C0​,x0​). If G(u)x0​=G(v)x0​ for maps u,v:C0​→Qi​, their equalizer supports x0​, so minimality makes it invertible and u=v. Thus the indicated map of hom-sets is injective. The evaluation embedding into cogenerator products embeds C0​ in a product of cogenerators indexed by the realized subsets of the fixed sets D(B,GQi​). There are only a set of such products, a set of their subobjects, and a set of maps from B into their images under G. These data give a weakly initial set in (B↓G). Using realized subsets avoids assuming maps into every cogenerator exist.
