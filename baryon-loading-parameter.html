Baryon loading parameter (R=3ρˉ​b​/(4ρˉ​γ​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Photon-baryon fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
The inertia ratio of the photon-baryon fluid is R=3ρˉ​b​/(4ρˉ​γ​). Photon momentum is weighted by the radiation enthalpy 4ρˉ​γ​/3, so this ratio controls momentum exchange, the common sound speed and the offset of the acoustic oscillator. Background conservation gives R∝a and R˙=HR.
