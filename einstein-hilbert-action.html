Einstein-Hilbert action

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Einstein field equations
OurBigBook.com Words: 718 Articles: 12
The Einstein-Hilbert action is the integral of the Ricci scalar against the metric volume form. Varying it with respect to the metric gives the vacuum Einstein field equations, up to a boundary term.
