Reciprocal transformation of a generalized circle ((a,b,c)↦(c,bˉ,a))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Projective linear group Möbius transformation Generalized circle under a Möbius transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
Write a generalized circle equation as a∣z∣2+bzˉ+bˉz+c=0, where a,c are real. Under the reciprocal Möbius transformation z=1/w, with w=0, multiply by ∣w∣2 to obtain
c∣w∣2+bˉwˉ+bw+a=0.
(66)
Thus the coefficient triple transforms as (a,b,c)↦(c,bˉ,a). A nondegenerate circle through zero has c=0 and becomes a straight line; a straight line missing zero becomes a circle through zero on the Riemann sphere. When working only with finite nonzero coordinates, retain the excluded point w=0 even if it satisfies the multiplied equation.
This reciprocal transformation is different from inversion in a circle, whose unit-circle formula is z↦1/zˉ. The latter reverses orientation; the reciprocal transformation is holomorphic away from its pole.
