Official University of Illinois semiconductor-device lecture slides. Slide 26 distinguishes carrier-counting, conductivity and cyclotron effective masses, and gives the multiple-valley density-of-states effective mass for silicon. This is useful when interpreting a supplied scalar mass in an elementary semiconductor model.
