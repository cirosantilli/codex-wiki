For the distinct-knot linear B-spline hats, let hi​=ti+1​−ti​>0. The mixed-normalization spline Gram matrix has entries
gii​=32​,gi,i−1​=3(hi​+hi+1​)hi​​,gi,i+1​=3(hi​+hi+1​)hi+1​​,gij​=0(∣i−j∣≥2).
(16)
Only indices in the finite basis are retained. This matrix has a row strict diagonal dominance margin at least 1/3, so the inverse infinity-norm bound from diagonal dominance gives ∥G−1∥ℓ∞​≤3. Equidistant spline knots give off-diagonal entries 1/6. Both pieces of every hat are retained; repeated endpoint spline knots require different boundary formulas.
