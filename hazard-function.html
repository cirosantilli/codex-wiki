Hazard function (h(t))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis Survival function
OurBigBook.com Words: 568 Articles: 11
The hazard function is the instantaneous event rate conditional on survival to time t. For an absolutely continuous distribution, h(t)=f(t)/S(t).
