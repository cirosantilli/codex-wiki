Quantum Hall antidot

 ... Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force Hall effect Quantum Hall effect Integer quantum Hall effect Quantum Hall edge state
OurBigBook.com Words: 325 Articles: 5
A depleted potential hill inside a two-dimensional electron gas. In a strong perpendicular magnetic field, closed quantum Hall edge states circulate around it. Coupling these states to propagating edge channels can produce resonant backscattering; Coulomb blockade can substantially alter the single-particle resonance pattern.
