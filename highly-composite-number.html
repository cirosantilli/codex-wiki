Highly composite number (τ(n)>max1≤m<n​τ(m))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Arithmetic function Divisor function
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
A positive integer whose divisor count strictly exceeds that of every smaller positive integer. A record scan maintains the largest earlier value of the divisor function and emits exactly candidates exceeding it. Equality does not create a new record; the first candidate is handled by a zero initial record.
