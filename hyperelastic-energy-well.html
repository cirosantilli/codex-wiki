Hyperelastic energy well

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Finite elasticity Hyperelastic material
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A hyperelastic energy well is a set of deformation gradients minimizing the stored-energy function at a fixed temperature. Material frame indifference makes each well invariant under left multiplication by proper rotations. A phase-changing crystal can have a single austenitic rotational well at high temperature and several martensitic wells below a transition. Continuous deformations cannot freely join arbitrary wells: planar interfaces require a rank-one connection between the selected gradients.
