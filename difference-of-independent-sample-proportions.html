Difference of independent sample proportions (δ=p​1​−p​0​)

For two independent samples of Bernoulli random variables, the difference of their sample proportions estimates the difference of population probabilities. Since the samples are independent,
Var(p​1​−p​0​)=n1​p1​(1−p1​)​+n0​p0​(1−p0​)​.
(48)
Substitution of the sample proportions gives a standard error for a large-sample Wald confidence interval. This interval is a descriptive comparison of the specified groups; a causal effect needs further assumptions. Sparse counts and probabilities near zero or one can make the normal approximation poor. Testing equality can instead use a null-pooled estimate of the common probability for the test's standard error.
