Stefan problem

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 73
A Stefan problem is a moving-boundary problem in which a phase boundary moves as latent heat is released or absorbed. Temperature satisfies a heat equation in each phase and the Stefan condition supplies the interfacial energy balance.
