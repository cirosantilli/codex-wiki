Electromagnetic vacuum energy with a wavelength cutoff (u=2π2ℏc/λmin4​)

Counting two transverse polarizations and zero-point energy ℏck/2 per mode gives u=ℏckmax4​/(8π2)=2π2ℏc/λmin4​. This is a bare cutoff-dependent mode sum, not a classical incident radiation bath or a renormalization-independent absolute observable.
