Origin-regular spherical Poisson flux law (F(x)=∣x∣3x​∫0∣x∣​f(s)s2ds)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Spherically symmetric solution of Poisson equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 1
For a spherically symmetric gradient field regular at the origin and satisfying Poisson equation, the divergence theorem on a ball determines its radial magnitude from the enclosed source integral. It depends only on the source at smaller radii. Regularity excludes the source-free but origin-singular addition Cx/∣x∣3; without that condition, solving the local equation on a punctured domain leaves this extra term undetermined.
