An industrial platinum resistance thermometer is a platinum resistance thermometer built for practical measurement conditions. The label Pt100 specifies a nominal reference electrical resistance, not an SPRT, a calibration certificate, or a universal accuracy. Calibrate the probe together with its readout when the desired result is the performance of the complete indicating system.
