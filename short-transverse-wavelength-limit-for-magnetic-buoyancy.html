Short transverse wavelength limit for magnetic buoyancy (ikx​ξx​=γp+Cρgξz​+Ciky​ξy​​−iky​ξy​−ξz′​)

For a horizontal field, set C=B2/μ0​. Static force balance gives δΠ=ρgξz​−(γp+C)∇⋅ξ+Ciky​ξy​. Given smooth compactly supported vertical and along-field displacements, the displayed choice makes total-pressure perturbation zero while ξx​=O(kx−1​). The nonnegative pressure and transverse-tension terms vanish as ∣kx​∣→∞. This constructs the infimum needed for the magnetohydrodynamic energy principle; a strictly negative limit is already negative at some finite wavenumber.
