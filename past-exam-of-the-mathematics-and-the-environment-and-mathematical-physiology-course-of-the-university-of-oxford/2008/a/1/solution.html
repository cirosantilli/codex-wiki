The left side is atmospheric heat storage per unit surface area. Incoming solar flux Q is averaged over the spherical surface by the factor 1/4; albedo a reflects a fraction of it. The outgoing term is a reduced Stefan-Boltzmann flux, with effective emissivity γ representing atmospheric greenhouse trapping. Ice-albedo feedback usually makes a decrease as temperature rises through an ice-melting range; greater carbon-dioxide pressure increases infrared opacity and therefore decreases this effective γ. The simple estimates below hold albedo fixed.
Radiative equilibrium gives
γ0​=4σT04​(1−a)Q​≃0.5978.​
(1)
Differentiating γT4=constant gives Δγ≃−4γ0​ΔT/T0​≃−0.02474. A doubling from 36 Pa changes pressure by 36 Pa, hence
γ1​≃290⋅364γ0​⋅3​=6.87×10−4 Pa−1.​
(2)
Reference weathering balance requires AL​W0​=v0​, so W0​=2.0×10−3 kgm−2yr−1​.
For the requested approximate system, take (T0​,p0​) as the reference radiative/weathering equilibrium, set θ=(T−T0​)/ΔTc​, P=p/p0​, and t^=t/tCO2​​, where
tCO2​​=Ma​gv0​MCO2​​Ap0​​,tT​=4σγ0​T03​ρa​cp​d​,ϵ=tCO2​​tT​​,Γ=4γ0​ΔTc​γ1​p0​T0​​.​
(3)
Linearize the radiative loss, retaining terms linear in T−T0​ and p−p0​. Its two terms are 4σγ0​T03​(T−T0​) and −σγ1​T04​(p−p0​). The carbon balance scales exactly with v=vCO2​​/v0​. Therefore
ϵθt^​=−θ+Γ(P−1),Pt^​=v−Pμeθ.​
(4)
Numerically tCO2​​≃7.41×103 yr,tT​≃0.0958 yr,ϵ≃1.29×10−5,Γ≃0.1795​. The time conversion uses seconds for the radiative constants and years for the supplied carbon production.
Because ϵ≪1, temperature rapidly approaches θ=Γ(P−1), while carbon changes slowly. A sustained v≃40 has equilibrium determined by
μlogP∗​+Γ(P∗​−1)=log40.
(5)
Its left side is strictly increasing on positive pressure, giving P∗​≃16.83,ΔTeventual​=ΔTc​Γ(P∗​−1)≃36.9 K​ within this extrapolated toy model. Counting volcanic production as well gives v=41 and 37.2 K instead. An inventory estimate for the rise is tCO2​​(P∗​−1)/(v−1)≃3.0×103 years. Linearization near the final equilibrium gives the e-folding time
trelax​=v(Γ+μ/P∗​)tCO2​​​≃9.4×102 yr.​
(6)
Thus the carbon/weathering response is millennial, despite the short thermal adjustment time.
There are two numerical-reference inconsistencies in the printed setup. The requested expansion treats (T0​,p0​) as its reference equilibrium although earlier labels pair T0​ with present-day 36 Pa rather than p0​=28 Pa. Also, the calibrated Γ=0.1795 gives 130.3e13Γ≃22.3, not the stated hint. That hint instead corresponds to Γ≃3/13, obtained by calibrating a doubling of 28 Pa. Such a convention gives a pressure around 13–14 and a similar rough warming of several tens of kelvin. The displayed results above consistently use the stated 36 Pa doubling calibration and explicitly declared reference-model approximation. A large extrapolation of a linearized radiative law is a property of this exercise, not a quantitative climate projection.
This is a weathering feedback with fast thermal adjustment.
