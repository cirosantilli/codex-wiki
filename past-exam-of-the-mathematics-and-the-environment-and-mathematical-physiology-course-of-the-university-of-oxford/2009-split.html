This University of Oxford Part C paper combines Mathematics and the Environment in section A with Mathematical Physiology in section B. [ref] The archive preserves the complete editable seven-page paper. Bounded searches of official sources found no advertised answers for this exact paper. The solutions distinguish linear and asymptotic conclusions from exact nonlinear claims, and identify the carrier-total typo and small-parameter respiratory approximations.
