The flux is outward when the substrate gradient is reversed and inward when se​>si​. Saturation occurs because there is a finite carrier population and finite binding-release/conformational turnover: arbitrarily abundant exterior substrate cannot make carriers cycle arbitrarily quickly. This is passive facilitated transport, so no maintained flux remains when the two free concentrations agree. Varying se​ at fixed si​ compares reservoir-controlled steady states or systems with different total substrate; it is not a trajectory preserving one fixed s0​.
