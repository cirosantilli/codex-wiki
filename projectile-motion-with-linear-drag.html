For constant acceleration vector g and γ>0, a projectile initially at r0​ with velocity v0​ has
v(t)=γg​+(v0​−γg​)e−γt,r(t)=r0​+γg​t+γv0​−g/γ​(1−e−γt).
(47)
The integrating factor eγt solves the first-order linear ordinary differential equation for the velocity; one more integration proves the position formula. The terminal velocity is g/γ, and 1/γ is the relaxation time. For g=(0,−g) and r0​=0, eliminating time when V0x​=0 gives
z(x)=(V0x​V0z​​+γV0x​g​)x+γ2g​ln(1−V0x​γx​).
(48)
For finite t≥0, the physical domain is 0≤γx/V0x​<1. If V0x​=0, the path is vertical and cannot generally be represented as a single-valued graph z(x). Expanding at fixed x as γ→0 recovers z=(V0z​/V0x​)x−gx2/(2V0x2​).
