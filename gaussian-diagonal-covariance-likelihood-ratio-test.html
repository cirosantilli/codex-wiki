Gaussian diagonal-covariance likelihood-ratio test (Λ=∣R∣n/2)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical hypothesis test Likelihood-ratio test
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For independent multivariate normal observations with unrestricted mean and positive-definite sample covariance matrix S=n−1∑i​(xi​−xˉ)(xi​−xˉ)T, the unrestricted maximum-likelihood estimates are xˉ,S. Requiring a diagonal population covariance matrix gives the same mean and covariance estimate D=diag(S11​,…,Spp​). Both fitted quadratic terms equal np, so their likelihood quotient is (∣S∣/∣D∣)n/2=∣R∣n/2, where R=D−1/2SD−1/2 is the sample correlation matrix. Small determinants give evidence against diagonality. For two variables this is a two-sided test of their sample correlation.
