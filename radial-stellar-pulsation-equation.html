Radial stellar pulsation equation (ξr​=rη)

For a radial stellar oscillation, put ξr​=rη. The self-gravitating adiabatic equation is (Γ1​Pr4η′)′+r3[(3Γ1​−4)P]′η+ρr4ω2η=0. It is a Sturm-Liouville problem whose Rayleigh quotient tests radial stability. The familiar constant-exponent threshold is Γ1​=4/3; a variable exponent requires the full integral test.
