Massive two-form at closed-string level two (M2=8πT)

 ... Physics Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory String oscillator Bosonic string mass spectrum
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
Each level-two chiral sector of the critical closed string is the symmetric traceless square S02​V of the massive little group vector space. The tensor product of its two sectors contains the exterior square Λ2V: the equivariant map (S,T)↦ST−TS is onto the antisymmetric matrices. Compact-group complete reducibility realizes this quotient as a subrepresentation. It describes a massive two-form field with M2=8πT=4/α′.
