Virtual-reality delusion experiment

 Home Science Psychology Clinical psychology Virtual reality therapy
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
Freeman and colleagues compared graded virtual social exposure with threat testing that included safety behavior reduction, assessing belief conviction and later real-world distress. The brief small-sample experiment probes a combined causal manipulation rather than establishing durable effects of every component.[ref]
