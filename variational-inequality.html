Variational inequality (F(x∗)⋅(x−x∗)≥0(x∈K))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For a feasible set K and a vector field F, a variational inequality asks for x∗∈K satisfying the displayed inequality for every feasible x. If K is convex and F is the gradient of a differentiable convex function, it is the necessary and sufficient first-order condition for minimizing that function. It also describes Wardrop equilibria when no scalar objective has the route-cost vector as its gradient.
