Essential supremum (esssupf)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Lp inclusion on a finite measure space
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
The essential infimum and essential supremum are the two almost-everywhere order bounds; this section defines the upper bound.
The essential supremum of a measurable function is the least number M such that f≤M almost everywhere. It equals the L∞ norm for a nonnegative function and, on a finite measure space, ∥f∥p​→∥f∥∞​ as p→∞.
