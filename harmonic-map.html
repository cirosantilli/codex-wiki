Harmonic map (τ(Q)=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A harmonic map between Riemannian manifolds is a critical point of the Dirichlet energy 21​∫∣dQ∣2. For a Euclidean domain and target unit sphere, the equation is ΔQ+∣∇Q∣2Q=0. Stationary wave maps are exactly harmonic maps of their spatial domain.
