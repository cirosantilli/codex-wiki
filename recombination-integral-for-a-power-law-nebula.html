Recombination integral for a power-law nebula

For fully ionized pure hydrogen of density n(r)=n0​(r/r0​)−β and constant Case B recombination coefficient α, the total recombination rate is
R(R;rin​)=4παn02​r02β​∫rin​R​r2−2βdr.
(10)
The central integral converges only for β<3/2; an envelope extending to infinity has finite recombination capacity only for β>3/2. At β=3/2, each logarithmic radius interval contributes equally. A cored power-law density profile removes the central singularity.
