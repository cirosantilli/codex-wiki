Erlang fixed point approximation

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory Stochastic network Loss network Erlang loss formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 195 Articles: 3
The Erlang fixed point approximation treats resource blocking as independent and applies the Erlang loss formula to traffic screened by other resources. Its self-consistency equations have a unique fixed point under fixed routing, but can have several under alternative routing.
