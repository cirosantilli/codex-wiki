Universal property of a quotient group (φ=φˉ​∘π)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Quotient group
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
Let N be a normal subgroup of G, and let π:G→G/N be the quotient map of groups. If a group homomorphism φ:G→H has group kernel containing N, there is exactly one group homomorphism φˉ​:G/N→H with φ=φˉ​∘π. Its value is forced: φˉ​(gN)=φ(g). Every measurement by a group homomorphism that ignores N can thus be performed on the quotient group directly. This is the universal property of the construction.
