Acid (chemistry)

 Home Chemistry Acid-base chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 37
A chemical acid can donate a proton, or accept an electron pair to form a covalent bond. The pH and buffer solution examples below use the former meaning. See IUPAC Gold Book, acid.
