Quantum-mechanical propagator (K(qf​,tf​;qi​,ti​))

 ... Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state Observable Hamiltonian (quantum mechanics) Unitary time evolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 338 Articles: 7
A quantum-mechanical propagator is the position-space kernel K(qf​,tf​;qi​,ti​)=⟨qf​∣U(tf​,ti​)∣qi​⟩ of the time-evolution operator. It is the amplitude to evolve between the specified endpoint configurations.
