Boolean literal (u or ¬u)

 ... Polynomial-time reduction Polynomial-time many-one reduction NP-hardness NP-completeness Boolean satisfiability problem Boolean formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
A Boolean literal is a Boolean variable u or its logical negation ¬u. The two Boolean literals have opposite truth values: a Boolean valuation v gives v(¬u)=1−v(u) when false and true are encoded as zero and one. Thus u=0 makes the positive Boolean literal u false and the negative Boolean literal ¬u true. A Boolean clause combines Boolean literals, and a Boolean-pair colouring gadget represents a Boolean variable and its logical negation by two vertices with opposite Boolean colours.
