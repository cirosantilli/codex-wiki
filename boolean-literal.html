Boolean literal (u or ¬u)

 ... Polynomial-time reduction Polynomial-time many-one reduction NP-hardness NP-completeness Boolean satisfiability problem Boolean formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A Boolean literal is a Boolean variable u or its negation ¬u. The two literals have opposite truth values. A Boolean clause combines literals, and a Boolean-pair colouring gadget represents a variable and its negation by two vertices with opposite Boolean colours.
