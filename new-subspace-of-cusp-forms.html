New subspace of cusp forms (Sk​(Γ1​(N))new)

OurBigBook.com Words: 173 Articles: 3
The new subspace is the orthogonal complement of the old subspace of cusp forms for the Petersson inner product. A form belongs to it precisely when the adjoints of all lower-level oldform by argument dilation maps annihilate it.
