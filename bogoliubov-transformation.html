Bogoliubov transformation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Canonical transformation of mode operators
OurBigBook.com Words: 675 Articles: 14
A Bogoliubov transformation is a canonical transformation of mode operators mixing creation operators and annihilation operators. It diagonalizes quadratic Hamiltonians or relates different vacuum descriptions. Bosonic and fermionic canonical bracket constraints differ. A nonzero creation-mixing coefficient makes its vacuum contain particles relative to the original mode decomposition.
