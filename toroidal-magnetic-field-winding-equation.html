Toroidal magnetic-field winding equation (D(Bϕ​/(ρR))/Dt=(Bp​/ρ)⋅∇Ω)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Toroidal magnetic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 193 Articles: 2
In axisymmetric ideal magnetohydrodynamics, Ω=uϕ​/R is the material angular velocity. Cylindrical basis-vector derivatives and mass conservation give the displayed winding equation. Thus Bϕ​/(ρR) is an advected scalar only when Bp​⋅∇Ω=0. Differential rotation along a field line otherwise produces toroidal field. This does not violate magnetic flux freezing, which concerns flux through a material surface rather than a cylindrical vector component.
