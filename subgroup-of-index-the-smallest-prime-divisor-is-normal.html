Subgroup of index the smallest prime divisor is normal ([G:H]=p=min{q:q prime, q∣∣G∣} ⟹ H⊴G)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group Subgroup Index of a subgroup
OurBigBook.com Words: 119
Let G be finite and let H have index p, the smallest prime divisor of ∣G∣. The group action on left cosets gives Φ:G→Sp​, with kerΦ⊆H. The image order divides both ∣G∣ and p!. By the choice of p, the only possible nontrivial prime factor common to these orders is p, and p! contains only one factor of p. Thus the image order is 1 or p. The action is transitive on p>1 cosets, so its image is nontrivial and has order p. The first isomorphism theorem gives ∣kerΦ∣=∣G∣/p=∣H∣, hence kerΦ=H. A kernel of a group homomorphism is a normal subgroup, proving the claim. For example, every subgroup of order 25 in a group of order 75 is normal.
