Liouville theorem for integrable nonnegative elliptic subsolutions

For n≥3, a nonnegative entire weak subsolution of a bounded measurable uniformly elliptic divergence-form equation that belongs to Lp(Rn) for some p>1 is zero almost everywhere. Rescaling the local Moser iteration estimate gives ∥u∥L∞(BR/2​)​≤CR−n/p∥u∥Lp(BR​)​. Let R→∞.
