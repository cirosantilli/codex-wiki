Lexicographic order

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
For equal-size subsets A and B of an ordered ground set, A precedes B in lexicographic order when the smallest element of their symmetric difference belongs to A.
For finite sequences over a total order, lexicographic order compares the first differing sequence terms; the smaller term determines the smaller sequence. If one sequence is a proper prefix of the other, the shorter precedes its extension. For fixed-length tuples, this is the first-difference rule used by radix sort key encoding.
