Look-elsewhere effect

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical hypothesis test Statistical significance
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
Searching many possible signal locations increases the chance of finding an apparently unusual fluctuation somewhere. A local p-value for a specified location differs from the global p-value for the search over locations.[ref]
