Poloidal magnetic field (Bp​=BR​eR​+Bz​ez​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
A poloidal magnetic field lies in meridional planes through the symmetry axis, in contrast to a toroidal magnetic field. For an axisymmetric vector field, it is represented by a poloidal magnetic flux function as Bp​=R−1∇Ψ×eϕ​.
