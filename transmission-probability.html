Transmission probability

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The transmission probability is the ratio of transmitted to incident probability current. When the asymptotic wavenumbers agree, it is the squared modulus of the transmission amplitude.
