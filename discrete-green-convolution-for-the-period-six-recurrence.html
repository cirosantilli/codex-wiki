Discrete Green convolution for the period-six recurrence (Pk​=∑j=1k​ak−j+1​fj​,an​=3​2​sin(nπ/3))

OurBigBook.com Words: 60
The unit-response sequence a1​=a2​=1, an+2​−an+1​+an​=0 is six-periodic. In the displayed convolution, all older terms cancel under Pk+2​−Pk+1​+Pk​ by this recurrence; the two new boundary terms reduce to fk+2​. Thus it gives a particular solution of the inhomogeneous linear recurrence with forcing fk+2​. Add the two-dimensional homogeneous span of cos(kπ/3) and sin(kπ/3) for the general solution. This is a discrete Green function construction.
