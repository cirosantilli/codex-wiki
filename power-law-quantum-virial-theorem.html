Power-law quantum virial theorem (2⟨T⟩=n⟨V⟩)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Ground state Ground-state energy Quantum variational principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
For a bound stationary state in a homogeneous potential V(λx)=λnV(x), scaling a normalized wavefunction gives stationarity of λ2⟨T⟩+λ−n⟨V⟩ at λ=1. Hence 2⟨T⟩=n⟨V⟩.
