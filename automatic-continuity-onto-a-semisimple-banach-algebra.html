Automatic continuity onto a semisimple Banach algebra (T:A↠B⟹T is continuous)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra Semisimple Banach algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
An algebra homomorphism onto a semisimple Banach algebra is continuous. To see the spectral mechanism, suppose an​→0 and T(an​)→b. For any c∈B, choose u,v∈A with T(u)=b and T(v)=c, and set d=cb, dn​=T(van​). Apply the spectral-radius three-circle inequality to T((1−z)van​+zvu). On the outer circle its norm tends uniformly to ∥d∥; on the circle of radius 1/R its spectral radius is bounded by (1+1/R)∥van​∥+∥vu∥/R. Hence r(d)2≤∥d∥∥vu∥/R for every R>1, so r(cb)=0. The unit criterion for the Jacobson radical gives b∈J(B)=0, and the closed graph theorem finishes the proof.
