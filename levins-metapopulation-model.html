Levins metapopulation model (p˙​=cp(1−p)−mp)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Metapopulation
OurBigBook.com Words: 116 Articles: 1
Colonization of empty habitat at rate c and extinction of occupied habitat at rate m give the displayed patch-occupancy equation. The positive equilibrium is 1−m/c when c>m. Competition can be incorporated through replacement fluxes between patches occupied by different species.
