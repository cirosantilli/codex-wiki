Density bound on a relativistically decoupled massive relic (mx​≲Ωγ​Ωx,max​​nx​/nγ​⟨Eγ​⟩​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmological entropy conservation Thermal decoupling in cosmology Conserved cosmological relic abundance
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
A species freezing out while relativistic retains its thermal comoving number. If it is nonrelativistic today, ρx​≃mx​nx​. Comparing with photons gives Ωx​/Ωγ​=mx​(nx​/nγ​)/⟨Eγ​⟩. An upper density fraction therefore bounds its mass inversely with its relic number density. The relation does not apply unchanged to a species whose abundance is depleted by nonrelativistic annihilation.
