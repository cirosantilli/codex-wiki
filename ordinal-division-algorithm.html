Ordinal division algorithm (α=δq+r,r<δ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Ordinal Ordinal multiplication
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For ordinals α and δ>0, there are unique ordinals q,r with the displayed relation. The function q↦δq is strictly increasing, continuous at limit ordinals and unbounded. Its first value above α occurs at a successor, giving the largest q with δq≤α. The ordered tail of α after that initial segment gives r, and maximality gives r<δ.
