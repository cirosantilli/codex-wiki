Rabi vector (W)

 ... Quantum state Observable Hamiltonian (quantum mechanics) Unitary time evolution Rabi cycle Driven two-level Rabi model
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For a rotating-frame two-state Hamiltonian operator H=ℏW⋅σ/2, the Bloch vector obeys R˙=W×R. Thus W specifies a rotation axis and angular speed. With detuning defined as drive frequency minus transition frequency and the lower state at positive Rz​, a phase-zero transverse drive can give W=(Ω,0,δ). Changing basis or detuning convention requires changing the corresponding signs consistently.
