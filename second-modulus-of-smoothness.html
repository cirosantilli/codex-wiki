Second modulus of smoothness (ω2​(f,h))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 64 Articles: 1
For a continuous periodic function set
ω2​(f,h)=sup∣u∣≤h​∥f(⋅+u)−2f+f(⋅−u)∥∞​.
(3)
It measures smoothness through second differences without requiring a derivative. For h>0, factoring the translation difference gives ω2​(f,t)≤(1+t/h)2ω2​(f,h). The second-difference integral formula gives ω2​(f,h)≤h2∥f′′∥∞​ for twice continuously differentiable functions.
