Trivial Lie algebra representation

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Lie algebra homomorphism Lie algebra representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The trivial representation sends every element of the Lie algebra to the zero linear map. For a complex semisimple Lie algebra, the irreducible representation of highest weight zero is the one-dimensional trivial representation.
