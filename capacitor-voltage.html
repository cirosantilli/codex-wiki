Capacitor voltage (VC​=q/C)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Capacitor
OurBigBook.com Words: 62 Articles: 1
The voltage across an ideal linear capacitor of constant capacitance C is its signed plate charge divided by C. With the passive current convention, I=CV˙C​.
