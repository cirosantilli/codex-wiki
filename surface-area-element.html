Surface area element (dS=∣Xu​×Xv​∣dudv)

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus Surface integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 175 Articles: 2
For a regular parametrization X(u,v) of a surface in three-dimensional Euclidean space, the scalar surface area element is the area of the infinitesimal parameter parallelogram after mapping. Its value is the displayed cross product magnitude. The vector surface area element additionally records the chosen orientation.
