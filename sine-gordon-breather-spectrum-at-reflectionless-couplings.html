Sine-Gordon breather spectrum at reflectionless couplings (mk​=2Msin[kπ/(2n)],k=1,…,n−1)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Real scalar field Sine-Gordon theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 189 Articles: 3
At renormalized coupling γ=8π/n, the kink-antikink transmission poles lie at ϑ=iπ(1−k/n). The relativistic bound-state mass from a rapidity pole gives the displayed increasing breather masses. The kink mass is M=mn/π, and k=n is an excluded threshold state. No breathers occur at n=1; scattering involving a physical second breather requires n≥3.
