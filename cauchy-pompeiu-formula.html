For a continuously differentiable function on a bounded planar domain, the Cauchy-Pompeiu formula supplements the Cauchy integral formula with an area integral of its Wirtinger derivative. Apply the Generalized Stokes theorem to f(ζ)dζ/(ζ−z) on a punctured domain; the small-circle integral supplies 2πif(z). If the boundary term vanishes at infinity, it gives the Cauchy-Green operator representation f(z)=π−1∫fζˉ​​(ζ)/(z−ζ)dA.
