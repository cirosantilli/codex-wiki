Continuum hypothesis (2ℵ0​=ℵ1​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cardinal number Cardinal arithmetic
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 3
The continuum hypothesis asserts that every infinite subset of the real numbers is either countable or equinumerous with the real numbers, equivalently 2ℵ0​=ℵ1​.
