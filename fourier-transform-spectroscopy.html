Fourier transform spectroscopy

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Spectrometer
OurBigBook.com Words: 252 Articles: 3
Fourier transform spectroscopy measures an interferogram as a function of optical path length difference. Its Fourier transform recovers the spectrum. An imaging implementation obtains an interferogram at every detector pixel, but a scanned cube is not simultaneous at all wavelengths.
