Morrey's inequality (∣u(x)−u(y)∣≤Cd,p​∣x−y∣1−d/p∥∇u∥Lp​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space Sobolev embedding theorem

For d<p<∞, every Sobolev space element u∈W1,p(Rd) has a Hölder continuous function representative of exponent 1−d/p, with the displayed bound. It also satisfies ∥u∥∞​≤Cd,p​(∥u∥p​+∥∇u∥p​). Averaging the fundamental theorem of calculus along a line segment over a ball gives
∣u(x)−uB(x,r)​∣≤Cd​∫B(x,r)​∣x−z∣d−1∣∇u(z)∣​dz.
(30)
The Holder inequality bounds this by Cd,p​r1−d/p∥∇u∥p​, because (d−1)p′<d. To compare two ball averages, translate a ball along the segment between their centres and use the same fundamental theorem of calculus along a line segment. These bounds give the displayed estimate. Density of smooth functions in a Sobolev space supplies the representative for nonsmooth u. For p=∞, the corresponding conclusion is Lipschitz continuity.
