Rod excess dissipation in shear (D′=CL3γ2sin2(2θ)/12)

OurBigBook.com Words: 43
A force-free, torque-free straight rod contributes a particle stresslet tensor (CL3/3)(p⋅Ep)pp. Its extra viscous dissipation is (CL3/3)(p⋅Ep)2. In a simple shear flow, θ˙=−γsin2θ; the dissipation is greatest along the extensional or compressional axes and vanishes at flow and gradient alignment.
