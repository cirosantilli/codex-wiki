BOOMERANG acoustic-peak measurement

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Physical cosmology
OurBigBook.com Words: 101
BOOMERANG's 1998 Antarctic balloon flight scanned a low-dust region with a microwave telescope near 38km and bolometers at 0.3K. Ground beam measurements and microwave angular dipole calibration connected signals to sky temperature. Frequency-map and time-split differences tested contamination. The 2000 paper fitted the Cosmic microwave background power spectrum, finding ℓpeak​=197±6 at one standard deviation. Its near-flat-geometry inference used a chosen model family and parameter priors; secondary peaks were not yet established.[ref]
