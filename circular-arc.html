Circular arc (s=Rθ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological space Circle
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
A circular arc is a connected portion of a circle. For radius R>0 and subtended central angle 0<θ<2π measured in radians, its arc length is Rθ. Two distinct endpoints determine two circular arcs: when they are not opposite, the minor arc has θ<π and the major arc has θ>π. Opposite endpoints determine two semicircles. The straight line segment joining the endpoints is a chord, rather than the curved circular arc itself.
