One-variable main conjecture for CM elliptic curves (charΛ​Xχ​=(Lχ​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Iwasawa theory Main conjecture of Iwasawa theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
At a good split prime, the one-prime CM division tower has a pro-p Galois group isomorphic to Zp​. Its prime-ramified Iwasawa module is torsion on each finite-character component. The main conjecture identifies its characteristic ideal with the corresponding elliptic-unit p-adic L-function. The class-field unit sequence and equality of characteristic ideals of global-units modulo elliptic-units and of class groups prove the identification. The cyclotomic Selmer formulation requires the appropriate CM twist and two-variable specialization; the one-prime tower is not the cyclotomic tower.
