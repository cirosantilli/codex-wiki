Quadratic polynomial (ax2+bx+c,a=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
A quadratic polynomial in one variable over a field has polynomial degree two and the displayed form. Its roots of a polynomial satisfy a quadratic equation. The polynomial and its associated polynomial function are distinct objects: over a finite field, different polynomials can define the same function.
