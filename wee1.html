Wee1

 Home Biology Signal transduction Signaling feedback Positive feedback Cell-cycle activation feedback
OurBigBook.com Words: 125
Wee1 is a protein kinase that inhibits CDK1 by adding a phosphate group at an inhibitory site. Purified human Wee1 phosphorylated Cdc2 at Tyr15 in the reported in vitro assay, with no Thr14 modification detected. A catalytic-site mutation abolished both the measured protein kinase activity and the ability to inhibit division. This identifies a catalytic brake rather than merely a correlated protein amount.[ref]
Do not attribute all inhibitory CDK1 phosphorylation to Wee1: the Xenopus laevis Myt1 experiment separately identified activity toward both Thr14 and Tyr15. Nor does every phosphorylation inhibit its target: activating Thr161 modification in the studied Xenopus laevis Cdc2 has the opposite role. In cell-cycle activation feedback, suppression of Wee1 removes a brake on CDK1, reinforcing the transition without implying that the total amount of either protein instantly disappears.
