Kaluza-Klein monopole

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Compactification (physics) Kaluza-Klein theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A gravitational magnetic monopole for a compact circle, whose fibre is nontrivially twisted over a surrounding two-sphere. With flat spectator directions its nonsingular local geometry is a Taub–NUT space. Reducing along the M-circle yields the D6-brane. The magnetic dual of the IIB circle vector is instead a five-brane in nine noncompact dimensions.
