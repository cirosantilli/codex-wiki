Rayleigh number (Ra)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth Mantle convection
OurBigBook.com Words: 207 Articles: 4
The Rayleigh number compares buoyancy driving with viscous and thermal diffusion. For a layer of thickness h, temperature drop ΔT, density ρ, thermal expansion coefficient αT​, gravitational acceleration g, thermal diffusivity κ, and dynamic viscosity μ,
Ra=μκρgαT​ΔTh3​.
(6)
Thermal convection begins when this exceeds a geometry-dependent critical value.
