Finite-depth vortex-sheet dispersion relation

 ... Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Shear layer Vortex sheet Kelvin-Helmholtz instability
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For an equal-density vortex sheet a distance h above a rigid wall, with lower and upper base velocities U and zero, a mode ei(kx−ωt) satisfies
(ω−kU)2coth(∣k∣h)+ω2=0.
(112)
The wall changes the inertia of the lower perturbation through the factor coth(∣k∣h).
