Proteinogenic amino acid

 ... Chemistry Chemical substance Organic acid Carboxylic acid Amino acid Alpha-amino acid
OurBigBook.com Words: 66 Articles: 1
A proteinogenic amino acid is incorporated into proteins during translation. The conventional twenty include glycine, alanine, methionine and proline; they are a subset of amino acids, not a definition of the whole family. [ref]
