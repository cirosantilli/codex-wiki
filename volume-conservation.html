Volume conservation

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Conservation law
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
Volume conservation states that the volume of incompressible material can change only through boundary fluxes or specified sources and sinks. In a one-dimensional shallow layer of depth h and depth-averaged speed u, it has the form ht​+(hu)x​=s.
