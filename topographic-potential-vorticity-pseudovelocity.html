Topographic potential-vorticity pseudovelocity (u=z×∇(f/H))

 ... Geophysical fluid dynamics Ocean circulation Wind stress Ocean gyre Sverdrup balance Topographic Sverdrup balance
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
In the steady small-Rossby number transport equation, this coefficient field advects the ocean transport streamfunction along contours of background shallow-water potential vorticity f/H. It is not a physical velocity: when ψ has units of volume transport, its units are inverse length squared per time. For uniform depth on a beta plane it points westward, u=(−β/H,0). With no forcing or drag, ψ is constant on each connected characteristic; locally ψ=Φ(f/H) at regular points.
