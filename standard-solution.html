Standard solution

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry)
OurBigBook.com Tags: Titration Words: 34
A solution whose concentration has been established for use as a measurement reference, for example in titration. It can be prepared from a primary standard or established by titrant standardization. The word “standard” does not imply one universal concentration.
