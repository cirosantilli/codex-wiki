Supremum bound for a complete function-algebra norm (∥f∥∞​≤∥f∥)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For an algebra of complex-valued functions with a complete algebra norm, every point value f(t) belongs to the spectrum of an element of f in the artificial unitization. Indeed (h,λ)↦h(t)+λ is an algebraic unital character of an algebra, and it vanishes on f−f(t)1. The spectrum bound gives ∣f(t)∣≤∥f∥ without first assuming that evaluation is continuous.
