Parallel current sheets with a resistive termination (B=μ0​I/w,E=IR/d,vselected​=Rw/(μ0​d))

OurBigBook.com Words: 83
Two broad perfect conductors of width w, separated by gap d≪w, carry opposite steady electric currents I and connect through a terminating resistor R. Away from edges, Ampère's circuital law and the superposition principle give gap magnetic field magnitude μ0​I/w. Each conductor is an equipotential, and Ohm's law sets their electric potential difference to IR. Planar symmetry and charge-free Gauss's law then give uniform gap electric field magnitude IR/d. Injection parallel to the upper current makes the electric and magnetic Lorentz forces opposite. Their cancellation selects speed Rw/(μ0​d) independent of nonzero electric charge and of operating current; nonrelativistic kinetic energy is m[Rw/(μ0​d)]2/2.
