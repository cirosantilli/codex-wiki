For a second-order kernel density estimator, write R(q)=∫q2 and μ2​(K)=∫u2K(u)du. Under the usual smoothness and shrinking-bandwidth assumptions, the leading mean integrated squared error is the displayed sum of integrated variance and squared bias of an estimator. Its minimizer is h=[R(K)/(nμ2​(K)2R(f′′))]1/5, giving order n−4/5. An added O(n−1) integrated covariance term is lower order at this bandwidth and does not change its leading constant.
