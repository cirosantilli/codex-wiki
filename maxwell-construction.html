Maxwell construction

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Gibbs free energy Phase coexistence curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
At fixed temperature, choose a coexistence pressure p∗​ such that the gas and liquid have equal chemical potentials. Because dμ=vdp, this gives ∫vl​vg​​vdp=0, or equivalently ∫vl​vg​​[p(v,T)−p∗​]dv=0. The equal areas replace the unstable part of an isotherm by a horizontal coexistence segment.
