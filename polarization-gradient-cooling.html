Polarization-gradient cooling

 Home Physics Atomic physics Atomic measurement methods Laser cooling Sub-Doppler cooling
OurBigBook.com Words: 619 Articles: 2
Polarization-gradient cooling uses spatial variation in electromagnetic polarization together with optical pumping between internal quantum states to cool moving chemical atoms.
Jean Dalibard and Claude Cohen-Tannoudji distinguish cooling by changing elliptical polarization from cooling by rotating linear polarization. The hill picture below concerns the former.[ref]
