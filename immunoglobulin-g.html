Immunoglobulin G (H2​L2​)

 Home Biology Protein Antibody
OurBigBook.com Words: 390 Articles: 2
Immunoglobulin G is an antibody class whose conventional four-chain architecture supplies the example above.
An ordinary IgG has two matching immunoglobulin heavy chains and two matching immunoglobulin light chains, abbreviated H2​L2​. Its gamma-type immunoglobulin heavy chains contribute both to recognition and to interactions with immune partners.[ref]
A useful way to read this picture is to follow a single polypeptide chain continuously from its N-terminus to its C-terminus, then examine the crosslinks separately. The same immunoglobulin heavy chain contributes to a Fab fragment and to the Fc fragment; these named fragments are not pre-existing separate chains. Conversely, one Fab fragment contains material from two different chains. The protein primary structure and the fragment inventory therefore answer different questions about the same protein.
