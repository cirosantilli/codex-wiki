The generators in the Spinor representation of the Lorentz group satisfy M†γ0+γ0M=0, so the finite transformation preserves the indefinite form S†γ0S=γ0. This makes ψˉ​ψ a Lorentz scalar through the Dirac adjoint. Boost generators are Hermitian rather than anti-Hermitian: a boost has eigenvalues e±η/2 and is not unitary in the ordinary positive-definite component norm. Rotations are unitary. This component representation is distinct from the unitary action on the physical state space.
