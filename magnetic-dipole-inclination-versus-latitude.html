Magnetic dipole inclination versus latitude (tanι=2tanλ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic field Magnetic field line Magnetic dipole field
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For a centred magnetic dipole moment pointing toward geographic south, the field at northern latitude λ has northward component BN​=Beq​cosλ and downward component BD​=2Beq​sinλ. These follow by resolving the magnetic dipole field into local radial and meridional directions. The downward inclination is therefore tanι=BD​/BN​=2tanλ, while the magnitude is Beq​1+3sin2λ​. This ideal aligned-dipole model has no eastward component and does not capture actual geographic departures of the geomagnetic field.
