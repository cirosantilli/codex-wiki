Bar product of binary linear codes (C1​∣C2​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Coding theory Linear code Reed-Muller code
OurBigBook.com Words: 36 Articles: 1
For length-n binary linear codes C2​⊆C1​, their bar product is
C1​∣C2​={(u,u+v):u∈C1​, v∈C2​}.
(95)
It has length 2n and dimension dimC1​+dimC2​.
