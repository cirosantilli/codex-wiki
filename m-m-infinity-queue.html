M-M-infinity queue

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov chain Continuous-time Markov chain Birth-death process
OurBigBook.com Words: 144 Articles: 4
An M/M/∞ queue has Poisson arrivals of rate λ, independent exponential service times of rate μ, and infinitely many servers. Its occupancy is a birth-death process with birth rate λ and death rate nμ in state n.
