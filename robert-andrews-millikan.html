Robert Andrews Millikan (1868-1953)

 Home Physics Physicist List of physicists
OurBigBook.com Words: 61 Articles: 1
An experimental physicist known for measurements of electron charge and the photoelectric effect. The Millikan photoelectric experiment describes the apparatus and stopping-voltage measurements behind his 1916 test.[ref]
