Electrochemical potential (μ​m​=μm​+zFFar​ψ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Chemical potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 250 Articles: 4
The molar electrochemical potential combines the molar chemical potential μm​ with the electrical contribution zFFar​ψ, where z is the signed charge number of an ion, FFar​=NA​e is the Faraday constant and ψ is electric potential. Both terms have units Jmol−1. In the particle convention the corresponding expression is μ​particle​=μparticle​+zeψ, with elementary charge e; multiplying it by the Avogadro constant gives the molar form. Transfer between locations at the same temperature and pressure is driven by differences in electrochemical potential. The membrane transport examples use it to distinguish neutral solute transfer from ion transfer.
