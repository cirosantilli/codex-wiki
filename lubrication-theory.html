Lubrication theory

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 6k Articles: 106
Lubrication theory describes viscous flow through a gap whose thickness is much smaller than its streamwise length; pressure is nearly uniform across the gap and controls a locally parabolic velocity profile.
