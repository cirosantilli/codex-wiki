Closed term

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order language First-order term
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A closed term contains no variables, so its value in a first-order structure does not depend on an assignment. In a language containing only relations and the constant zero, zero is the only closed term. A term-generated model has every element denoted by such a term.
