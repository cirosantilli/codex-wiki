Adjustment coefficient (R>0: λ(MX​(R)−1)=cR)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Classical risk model
OurBigBook.com Words: 788 Articles: 13
The adjustment coefficient is a positive root of λ(MX​(r)−1)=cr in the classical risk model. When the moment-generating function is finite at R, the process exp(R[∑j=1Nt​​Xj​−ct]) is a continuous-time martingale. It yields the Lundberg inequality and, under the relevant tilted integrability, the Cramér–Lundberg ruin asymptotic.
