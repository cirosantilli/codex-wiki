Injective linear modelling of a small sumset (kerL∩12B={0},∣G′∣<4∣12B∣)

 ... Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Doubling constant Freiman-Ruzsa theorem Freiman-Ruzsa theorem over a finite field
OurBigBook.com Words: 134 Articles: 1
For a finite subset B⊆F2N​ containing zero, choose m=⌈log2​(2∣12B∣)⌉ and a uniformly random linear map L:F2N​→F2m​. Each fixed nonzero vector has zero image with probability 2−m. The union bound gives a map whose kernel meets 12B only at zero. It is injective on 6B, and 2m<4∣12B∣. The Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality makes the image of B have density at least 1/(4C12) if ∣B+B∣≤C∣B∣.
