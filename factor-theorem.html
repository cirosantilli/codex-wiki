Factor theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial Zero of a function Root of a polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 19
For a polynomial f over a field, f(a)=0 exactly when X−a divides f(X). Polynomial division gives f(X)=(X−a)q(X)+f(a).
