Virasoro generators are the contour modes of the holomorphic stress tensor in a two-dimensional conformal field theory. Their commutators are [Lm​,Ln​]=(m−n)Lm+n​+c(m3−m)δm+n,0​/12. A contour around an operator insertion extracts the corresponding coefficient of its stress-tensor operator product expansion. L−1​ generates translations, L0​ measures holomorphic weight, and all positive generators annihilate a primary state. An independent antiholomorphic copy acts on the other weight.
