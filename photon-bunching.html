Photon bunching

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum optics Photon statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
Photon bunching is enhanced near-coincident detection relative to an appropriate independent-count reference. It need not require nonclassical light. In a classical intensity model with nonnegative instantaneous light intensity I, ideal detection with a conditional Poisson distribution gives
g(2)(0)=⟨I⟩2⟨I2⟩​=1+⟨I⟩2VarI​≥1.
(9)
A fluctuating intensity therefore bunches counts. For a stationary classical intensity with finite second moment, the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality further gives ⟨I(t)I(t+τ)⟩≤⟨I2⟩. A zero-delay correlation smaller than one at a later delay cannot be explained by this model.
