Wishart distribution (Wp​(ν,V))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Random matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 1
For integer ν, the sum ∑j=1ν​Xj​XjT​ of independent Np​(0,V) vectors has a Wishart distribution. Its positive-definite density extends to real ν>p−1 and is proportional to ∣W∣(ν−p−1)/2exp[−tr(V−1W)/2]. It is a conjugate prior for the precision matrix of a multivariate normal distribution. Its mean is νV.
The WinBUGS declaration dwish(R, nu) uses R=V−1; the density convention is documented in the NIMBLE BUGS-language manual. For ν=p=3 it is proper, while the expected inverse matrix does not exist: that expectation requires ν>p+1. Specifying a Wishart prior on precision is therefore a substantive covariance assumption.
