Stationary points of a cosine-modulated Gaussian (z(x,y)=e−x2−y2cosx)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Calculus of variations Stationary point
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
The gradient vanishes only when y=0 and tanx=−2x. On each tangent branch, tanx+2x increases strictly from negative to positive infinity, so it has exactly one root xs​. With zs​=e−xs2​cosxs​, the Hessian matrix is diagonal with entries −(4xs2​+3)zs​ and −2zs​. Its determinant is positive, so every stationary point is a strict maximum when zs​>0 or a strict minimum when zs​<0. Signs alternate between successive tangent branches. This gives infinitely many nondegenerate extrema under a decaying Gaussian function envelope.
