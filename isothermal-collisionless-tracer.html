Isothermal collisionless tracer (ρ=ρ0​eψ/σ02​)

 ... Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics Collisionless Boltzmann equation Jeans equation Spherical Jeans equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 182 Articles: 2
A steady galactic distribution function depending only on relative energy E=ψ−v2/2 is isotropic and has zero mean velocity. If its one-dimensional velocity dispersion is constant, the Spherical Jeans equation reduces to σ02​ρ′=ρψ′, yielding the displayed density. This is a tracer relation in a specified gravitational potential, not a requirement that the tracer generate that potential. With no escape-energy cutoff its local velocity distribution is Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution.
