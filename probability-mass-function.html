Probability mass function (pX​(x))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
For a discrete random variable X, its probability mass function is
pX​(x)=P(X=x).
(55)
It is nonnegative and sums to one over the possible values of X.
