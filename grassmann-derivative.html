Grassmann derivative (∂θL​,∂θR​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Exterior algebra Grassmann algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
A Grassmann derivative is an odd differentiation operation on a Grassmann algebra. A left Grassmann derivative obeys ∂θL​(ab)=(∂θL​a)b+(−1)∣a∣a(∂θL​b) for homogeneous a. Right derivatives use the corresponding right-sided product rule. Their placement must be fixed when differentiating fermionic sources in a generating functional, since commuting ordinary derivatives would lose fermionic signs.
