Use the source's +−−− metric signature and x0=η, with conformal time derivatives denoted by primes. For this vector cosmological perturbation, the metric tensor and inverse metric through first order are
g00​=a2,g0i​=0,gij​=−a2(δij​−Jij​),g00=a−2,g0i=0,gij=−a−2(δij​+Jij​).

Γβγα​=21​gασ(∂β​gσγ​+∂γ​gσβ​−∂σ​gβγ​).

Because a=a(η) only, it gives
Γ000​=21​a−2(2aa′)=H,Γ0i0​=21​a−2∂i​(a2)=0.​
(30)
For the other requested Christoffel symbols, only the time derivative of the spatial metric tensor contributes:
Γij0​=−21​a−2∂η​gij​=21​a−2[2aa′(δij​−Jij​)−a2Jij′​].
(31)
Thus
Γij0​=H(δij​−Jij​)−21​Jij′​.​
(32)
Finally contract the first upper and first lower spatial indices, summing over i:
γiji​=21​(∂i​Jij​+∂j​Jii​−∂i​Jij​)=21​∂j​Jii​.
(33)
Since Jii​=2∂i​Fi​=0 by the transverse vector field condition on F,
γiji​=0.​
(34)
The useful Christoffel symbol traces are consequently Γμ0μ​=4H and Γμjμ​=0 to first order: the first uses Jii′​=0 and the second uses the contraction just proved.
