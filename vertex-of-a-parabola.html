Vertex of a parabola ((h,k))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Conic section Parabola
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
For a parabola described by the function graph y=a(x−h)2+k, with nonzero a in the real numbers, its vertex of a parabola is the point (h,k). This point gives its minimum or maximum value of y in these coordinates.
