Polar gamma-plane Rossby wave

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Rossby wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
Near a north pole, f=2Ω−γr2/2 with γ=2Ω/a2. If a quasi-geostrophic streamfunction satisfies ∇h2​ψ−ψ/Ld2​=−α2ψ, then Q=f−α2ψ. Its nonlinear self-advection cancels because J(ψ,ψ)=0, leaving −α2ψt​+γψϕ​=0. An azimuthal mode cos(ωt−nϕ) therefore has ω=−nγ/α2 at any amplitude consistent with the underlying balanced model.
