Multiplicative group of a non-Archimedean local field (K×)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 2
If a non-Archimedean local field K has uniformizer π, valuation ring OK​, maximal ideal mK​ and residue field Fq​, then
K×≅πZ×μq−1​×(1+mK​).
(13)
The first factor records the discrete valuation, the Teichmuller representatives split reduction OK×​→Fq×​, and 1+mK​ is the group of principal units.
