Let Ω⊂Rn be an open set and (Uα​)α∈A​ an open cover of Ω. There exist nonnegative smooth functions (χj​)j∈J​ and choices α(j) such that
suppΩ​χj​⊂Uα(j)​,j∈J∑​χj​(x)=1(x∈Ω),​
(45)
and the family of supports is locally finite: every point has a neighborhood meeting only finitely many of them. This is a smooth partition of unity subordinate to the cover, allowing a refinement of its indexing. In Euclidean open sets, one can choose J countable and the individual supports compact in their assigned cover members. Local finiteness ensures that the sum is smooth and that only finitely many terms meet any given compact set.
