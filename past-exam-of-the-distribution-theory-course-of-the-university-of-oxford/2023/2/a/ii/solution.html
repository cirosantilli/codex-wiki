The weak convergence of distributions uj​→u means
⟨uj​,ϕ⟩⟶⟨u,ϕ⟩for every ϕ∈D(R).
(22)
For θ∈D(R), its convolution with a distribution is the smooth function
(θ∗u)(x)=⟨uy​,θ(x−y)⟩.
(23)
The subscript records the variable on which the distribution acts. With θˇ(x)=θ(−x), its regular distribution satisfies the useful pairing identity
⟨θ∗u,ϕ⟩=⟨u,θˇ∗ϕ⟩.
(24)
Indeed integrating θ(x−y)ϕ(x) in x gives (θˇ∗ϕ)(y); continuity of the distribution justifies moving the pairing through that integral, by approximation with sums of test functions in one common compact support.
Part (i), applied to the reflected mollifier, gives ρˇ​ε​∗ϕ→ϕ in the space of test functions. Continuity of u therefore proves
⟨ρε​∗u,ϕ⟩=⟨u,ρˇ​ε​∗ϕ⟩⟶⟨u,ϕ⟩,ρε​∗u→u in D′.​
(25)
Reflection is harmless even if the chosen standard mollifier is not even.
For the mollifiers themselves, a change of variable gives
⟨ρε​,ϕ⟩=∫ρ(y)ϕ(εy)dy⟶ϕ(0)∫ρ(y)dy=ϕ(0).
(26)
Thus ρε​→δ0​ in D′(R)​, with limit the Dirac delta distribution, despite the failure of convergence in the L1 space.
