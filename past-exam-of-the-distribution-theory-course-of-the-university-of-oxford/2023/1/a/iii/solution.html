Assign arbitrary values at the endpoints. For a test function ϕ, split −∫dϕ′ at these corners and apply integration by parts on each piece. The adjacent boundary terms cancel because d is continuous, and only finitely many pieces meet the compact support of ϕ. Hence
⟨d′,ϕ⟩=∫R​s(x)ϕ(x)dx,d′=s.​
(7)
The distributional derivative is exactly the regular distribution of the ordinary derivative defined almost everywhere. There are no Dirac delta distributions in this first distributional derivative: corners change slope, while a jump of the function itself would create a point mass.
