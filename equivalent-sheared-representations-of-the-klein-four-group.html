Equivalent sheared representations of the Klein four-group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Klein four-group
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
Let the Klein four-group have generators X,Y and Z=XY, with X2=Y2=I and XY=YX. For every real p, the matrices
D(X)=−I2​,D(Y)=(−10​−p1​),D(Z)=−D(Y)
(16)
define a group representation. Indeed, D(Y)2=I2​, and D(X) commutes with every matrix, so the group relations hold.
Set S=(10​−p/21​). A direct change of basis gives
S−1D(Y)S=diag(−1,1),S−1D(Z)S=diag(1,−1).
(17)
Consequently all these representations are isomorphic representations, with character values (2,−2,0,0) on (I,X,Y,Z). Each is a direct sum of the two one-dimensional representations whose values on X are −1 and whose values on Y are +1 and −1. A group representation need not be orthogonal; nonzero p changes the basis rather than the represented group.
