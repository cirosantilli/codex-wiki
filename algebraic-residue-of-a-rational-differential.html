Algebraic residue of a rational differential (resP​(η)=[u−1]η/du)

OurBigBook.com Words: 70
At a smooth point with local parameter u, expand a rational differential as ∑j​aj​ujdu in the completed local field; its residue is a−1​. Formal substitution shows this coefficient is independent of the parameter, in arbitrary characteristic. For a finite separable extension of these local fields, residues commute with the differential trace, with a sum over branches. The monogenic trace formula in the trace-dual module of a finite curve map proves this compatibility by coefficient extraction.
