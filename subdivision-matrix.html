Subdivision matrix (S)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Computer aided geometric design Subdivision surface Subdivision mask
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A local subdivision matrix maps the control points in a refinement-invariant neighborhood to the refined neighborhood. Row sums one express constant reproduction. Its constant eigenvector has eigenvalue one; remaining eigenvalues govern decay of shape modes. Near an extraordinary subdivision vertex, tangent modes and a characteristic map of a subdivision surface are needed to test geometric smoothness, beyond simple convergence.
