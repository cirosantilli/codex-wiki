Semilinear action (σ(av)=σ(a)σ(v))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Galois cohomology Galois descent of vector spaces
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
For an extension L/K with Galois group G, a semilinear action on an L-space is an additive group action satisfying σ(av)=σ(a)σ(v). Scalar coefficients are transformed along with vectors. Invariants form a K-space, and Galois descent of vector spaces reconstructs the original space from it.
