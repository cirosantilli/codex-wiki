Quadrupole magnet

 Home Physics Experimental apparatus Particle accelerator Beam optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 2
A magnet whose transverse field varies linearly with displacement. Its Lorentz force focuses a charged-particle beam in one transverse plane and defocuses it in the other. Oppositely oriented elements provide alternating-gradient focusing.
