Doubling constant (∣A+A∣/∣A∣)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics
OurBigBook.com Words: 339 Articles: 10
The doubling constant of a nonempty finite set A is ∣A+A∣/∣A∣. A small doubling constant indicates that A behaves approximately like a coset of a subgroup.
