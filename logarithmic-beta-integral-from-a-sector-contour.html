Logarithmic beta integral from a sector contour (∫0∞​1+xβlogx​dx=−β2sin2(π/β)π2cos(π/β)​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Residue theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 116
For real β>1, choose a branch of the complex logarithm whose argument interval contains [0,2π/β] strictly, and integrate Logz/(1+zβ) over an annular sector of angle α=2π/β. The one enclosed simple pole is eiπ/β, with residue −iπeiπ/β/β2. The circular arcs tend to zero, with bounds O(ϵ∣logϵ∣) and O(R1−βlogR).
Writing I for the title integral and J=∫0∞​(1+xβ)−1dx, the two radial segments and the residue theorem give
(1−eiα)I−iαeiαJ=β22π2​eiπ/β.
(141)
Divide by eiπ/β and equate real and imaginary parts. This gives J=π/[βsin(π/β)] and the title formula. When 1<β<2, the sector extends below the negative real axis; the principal complex logarithm therefore cannot be used throughout that sector. A cut along a ray just below the positive axis avoids this issue for every β>1.
