Ramsey ensemble coherence (χ(T)=⟨eiηT⟩)

OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For an ensemble with static detunings δ+η, ideal same-phase quarter-turn pulses give p​2​(T)=[1+Re(eiδTχ(T))]/2. Here χ is the characteristic function of the offset distribution. A mean-zero Gaussian of RMS width σ gives χ=e−σ2T2/2; a symmetric uniform interval gives a sinc function. A width alone does not uniquely specify the envelope.
