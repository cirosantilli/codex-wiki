Fixed-sign null space condition

 ... Analysis Numerical analysis Sparse optimization Compressed sensing Basis pursuit Nullspace property
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For a real vector x0​ with support S, uniqueness in basis pursuit is equivalent to ∣⟨sgn(x0,S​),hS​⟩∣<∥hSc​∥1​ for every nonzero h∈kerA. The supporting-line inequality for the absolute value proves sufficiency. Taking small positive and negative multiples of h before any active sign changes proves necessity.
