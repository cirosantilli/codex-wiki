Liénard boundedness function (W=V(x)+z2/2−xz)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Lyapunov function
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For the Liénard system x˙=z−F(x), z˙=−g(x) with F=x3/3−2x, g=x3−x, and V′=g, the displayed function is coercive up to a constant. Differentiation gives W˙=−gF−z2+Fz+xg≤−z2/2−5x6/18+8x4/3−x2. Its derivative is negative outside a compact set, providing bounded forward dynamics even though the original oscillator has negative damping near the origin. The coordinate z=y+F(x) exposes this restoring and damping balance.
