Regular matrix summability method

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Space of sequences converging to zero Convergent sequence space Matrix summability method
OurBigBook.com Words: 311 Articles: 4
A matrix summability method is regular if it preserves the ordinary limit of every convergent sequence. The Silverman-Toeplitz theorem characterizes regularity by uniformly bounded absolute row sums, entrywise convergence to zero down every fixed column, and convergence of the row sums to one.
