Holomorphic gauge coupling (f=gh−2​−iϑ/(8π2))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supersymmetric action Gauge kinetic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
A holomorphic gauge coupling is the coefficient of the gauge kinetic F-term in a local Wilsonian effective action written in holomorphic variables. In a common normalization its real part is 1/gh2​. The continuous perturbative shift of its topological angle, together with holomorphy, restricts its beta function to one-loop order. Wave-function renormalization and anomalous canonical field rescalings distinguish it from the physical gauge coupling.
