Piezoelectric coefficient (dij​)

 ... Quantum mechanics Potential barrier Quantum tunnelling Current-distance test of vacuum tunnelling Piezoelectricity Direct piezoelectric effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A tensor coefficient relating an increment of electrical displacement to stress, or the corresponding converse strain response to electric field, under stated constitutive conventions. Charge-per-force units CN−1 are equivalent to displacement-per-voltage units mV−1 for the usual d coefficients.
