A Pt100 has nominal electrical resistance 100Ω at 0∘C.[ref] Here 0∘C is the reference for the nominal element designation; it is distinct from the 0.01∘C water triple point used in SPRT calibration. The following Pt100 local linear model deliberately stipulates one approximate response for calculations.
