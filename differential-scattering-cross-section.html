Differential scattering cross-section (dσ/dΩ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 2
The differential scattering cross-section is the effective incident area scattered per unit solid angle. It is defined by the ratio of the outgoing particle rate through dΩ to the incident flux.
