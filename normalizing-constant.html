Normalizing constant (Z=∫h(x)dx)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Continuous probability distribution Probability density function
OurBigBook.com Words: 17
A normalizing constant makes a nonnegative integrable function into a probability density function: if 0<Z=∫h<∞, then h/Z is a probability density function.
