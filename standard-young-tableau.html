Standard Young tableau (SYT(λ))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young tableau
OurBigBook.com Words: 338 Articles: 5
A standard Young tableau fills a Young diagram bijectively with 1,…,n, increasing along rows and down columns. The cells occupied by the first r entries form a diagram, so a tableau is equivalently a path formed by adjoining one addable node of a Young diagram at each step. The number of standard tableaux of shape λ is the dimension of the complex Specht module Sλ.
