Standard Young tableau (SYT(λ))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young tableau
OurBigBook.com Words: 591 Articles: 5
A standard Young tableau of shape λ⊢n is a Young tableau that uses 1,…,n exactly once and strictly increases left to right along every row and top to bottom down every column. Formally, a bijection T:Y(λ)→{1,…,n} is standard when
T(i,j)<T(i,j+1),T(i,j)<T(i+1,j)
(32)
whenever the indicated cells belong to the Young diagram. We write SYT(λ) for the set of such fillings and fλ=∣SYT(λ)∣ for its cardinality.
For (3,2), the five standard Young tableaux are listed below, with each pair giving the top row followed by the bottom row:
T1​T3​T5​​=((1,2,3),(4,5)),=((1,2,5),(3,4)),=((1,3,5),(2,4)).​T2​T4​​=((1,2,4),(3,5)),=((1,3,4),(2,5)),​
(33)
There are no others: the upper-left entry must be 1, and choosing the two bottom-row entries fixes both increasing row orders. Of the six choices of two entries from {2,3,4,5}, only bottom row (2,3) fails, because its 3 would lie below 4. Hence f(3,2)=5 by direct enumeration, independently of the hook-length formula.
The cells occupied by 1,…,r form a Young diagram: every cell above or to the left of an occupied cell has a smaller label. Consequently a standard Young tableau is equivalently a path from the empty shape to λ in the Young branching graph, adjoining one addable node of a Young diagram at each step. For T2​, the successive shapes are
∅, (1), (2), (2,1), (3,1), (3,2).
(34)
Conversely, number the cell added at step r by r to recover the standard Young tableau. This path interpretation also makes a standard Young tableau a linear extension of a partially ordered set defined by the row and column requirements; see Stanley's Algebraic Combinatorics, Chapter 8, on tableaux and paths in Young's lattice.
The usefulness goes beyond counting: the standard polytabloid basis of a Specht module Sλ over the complex numbers is indexed by SYT(λ), so dimC​Sλ=fλ. Each of the five pictured standard Young tableaux therefore labels one basis element of the irreducible representation S(3,2) of the symmetric group S5​, whose dimension over the complex numbers is five. The filled diagrams index the basis; the actual vectors are polytabloids. The hook-length formula computes this dimension without listing all the standard Young tableaux.
