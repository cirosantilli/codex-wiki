At the centre of geodesic normal coordinates, expand the square of the Twisted Dirac operator. The diagonal terms give the rough Laplacian; the off-diagonal terms are ∑i<j​c(ei)c(ej)[∇i​,∇j​]. The tensor-product vector-bundle curvature is the sum of spin curvature and FW. Substituting spin curvature 41​∑a,b​⟨R(ei​,ej​)ea​,eb​⟩c(ea)c(eb) and using the Clifford algebra relations yields the scalar term Scal/4: the four-generator contribution vanishes by the algebraic Bianchi identity and the contracted terms are the scalar curvature. This proves the displayed identity at every point.
