Sodium D-line Zeeman pattern

 Home Physics Atomic physics Zeeman splitting Anomalous Zeeman effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 197 Articles: 1
Neglect hyperfine structure and use a magnetic field weak compared with the spin-orbit interaction. The sodium D doublet has transitions 3p2P1/2​→3s2S1/2​ and 3p2P3/2​→3s2S1/2​. Their Landé g-factors are 2/3, 4/3 and 2 respectively. With mu​,ml​ denoting upper and lower magnetic labels, the photon shift is
δEγ​=μB​B(gu​mu​−2ml​),mu​−ml​=0,±1.
(10)
The first transition has four allowed shifts μB​B{±2/3,±4/3}; the second has six, μB​B{±1/3,±1,±5/3}. The most blue-shifted component of the second is mu​=1/2→ml​=−1/2, with shift 5μB​B/3. The apparently larger shift mu​=3/2→ml​=−1/2 is excluded by the electric-dipole selection rule. The 3p2P3/2​ level lies above 3p2P1/2​, so in this weak-field model the 5/3 component also has the shortest wavelength of the whole doublet. [ref]
