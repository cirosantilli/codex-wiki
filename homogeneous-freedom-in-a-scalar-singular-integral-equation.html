Homogeneous freedom in a scalar singular integral equation (w=w(Cρ+H))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Boundary value problem Riemann-Hilbert problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For the scalar jump (a+b)w+​+(b−a)w−​=c, divide by a nonvanishing canonical homogeneous solution w to obtain an additive jump ρ=c/((a+b)w+​). Its Cauchy integral gives a particular quotient, while every additional quotient H with no jump is homogeneous freedom. The density f=w+​−w−​ therefore differs from the normalized particular density by −2bw+​H/(a−b). Normalization, index and endpoint conditions determine the allowable H; dropping it without such conditions does not give the general solution.
