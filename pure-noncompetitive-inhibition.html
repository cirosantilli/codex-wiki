Set Ki​=Ki′​, so that α=α′. The reversible enzyme inhibition equation reduces to
v=α1​KM​+sVmax​s​,Vmax,app​=αVmax​​,KM,app​=KM​.
(16)
At the chosen I=30μM, every reaction rate is one quarter of its uninhibited value. The plateau falls to 25μMmin−1, but half that plateau still occurs at s=20μM. Increasing substrate cannot restore the uninhibited plateau. An unchanged apparent Michaelis constant here expresses equality of the two binding effects, rather than an absence of inhibitor binding to the enzyme-substrate complex.
