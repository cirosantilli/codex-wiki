Uniform extension of a one-dimensional elastic body

 ... Physics Branch of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity Linear elasticity Navier-Cauchy equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
For a one-dimensional body with endpoints displaced by zero and Δ, the static Navier-Cauchy equation reduces to u′′=0 and gives the uniform strain solution u(x)=Δx/L.
