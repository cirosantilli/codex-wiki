Recursion tree

 Home Computer science Algorithm Recursion (computer science)
OurBigBook.com Words: 148 Articles: 1
A recursion tree records recursive calls as nodes and their direct recursive subcalls as children. Its node labels can record the work done outside those subcalls. Summing the work over levels or charging it to nodes proves a total running time bound. For T(n)=aT(n/b)+f(n), level j has approximately aj instances of size n/bj, so its nonrecursive work is ajf(n/bj). Summing those quantities explains the cases of the master theorem.
