Anosov flow

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Smooth flow

A nonsingular smooth flow on a compact smooth manifold is Anosov if there is a continuous invariant splitting TN=Es⊕RF⊕Eu and constants C,λ>0 with ∥Dϕt​vs​∥≤Ce−λt∥vs​∥ and ∥Dϕ−t​vu​∥≤Ce−λt∥vu​∥ for t≥0. Uniform contraction and expansion are transverse to the neutral flow direction, which distinguishes the splitting from that of an Anosov diffeomorphism.
