Collinear divergence

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Effective field theory Infrared divergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A singularity of a massless scattering or radiation integral when momenta of two particles become parallel. In Quantum chromodynamics, unresolved quark-gluon splittings cause such divergences. A mass regulator or dimensional regularization exposes them; they cancel between suitable inclusive real and virtual contributions or are absorbed into factorized distributions where appropriate.
