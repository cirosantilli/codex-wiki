Thin-film interference

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Interference (wave propagation)
OurBigBook.com Words: 395 Articles: 6
Thin-film interference combines coherent light contributions reflected or transmitted at the two surfaces of a layer. For a film of refractive index n, thickness d and internal angle θt​, the round-trip optical path difference is 2ndcosθt​. The relative reflection phase shift must also be included. For a nonabsorbing higher-index film surrounded by air, one of the two leading reflected contributions has a π phase shift and the other does not. Reflection maxima therefore satisfy 2ndcosθt​=(m+1/2)λ0​. A smooth reflection spectrum filters many visible wavelengths at once; varying thickness and angle produce spatially varying mixed colours.
