Fourth-moment deficit and bracket variance identity (EXt4​=3t2−3Var(⟨X⟩t​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 46
The polynomial X4−6X2⟨X⟩+3⟨X⟩2 has zero Itô drift. Finite fourth maximal and second bracket moments make it a true martingale. If EXt2​=t and Cov(Xt2​,⟨X⟩t​)=0, then EXt4​=3t2−3Var(⟨X⟩t​). Equality at every time forces a deterministic clock and hence Brownian motion by the Lévy characterization of Brownian motion.
