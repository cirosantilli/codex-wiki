Punctured ball (Br​(p)∖{p})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Open set Open ball
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
A punctured ball is an open ball with its centre removed. In the real line it has two connected components. In Euclidean space of dimension n≥2 it is path-connected: for any two points, choose a third point inside the ball outside the two lines through the centre and those points. Such a point exists since two lines do not cover an open ball in dimension at least two. The two line segments joining through that point stay inside the ball, which is a convex set, and avoid its centre. They form a continuous path.
