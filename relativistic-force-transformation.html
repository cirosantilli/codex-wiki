Relativistic force transformation (F⊥​=F⊥′​/γ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For a particle instantaneously at rest in a primed frame moving at vx relative to the laboratory, the ordinary three-force transforms as F∥​=F∥′​ and F⊥​=F⊥′​/γ. The spatial components of the four-force are γu​F, whereas its time component is γu​F⋅u/c. For a co-moving material segment, length contraction also changes its laboratory length, so transforming total force is not the same as transforming force per unit length.
