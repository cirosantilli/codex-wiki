Ertel's theorem (D(ρ−1ωa​⋅∇α)/Dt=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Potential vorticity Ertel potential vorticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
An inviscid fluid with conservative body force, a materially conserved stratifying scalar α, and an equation of state making ∇ρ,∇p,∇α coplanar conserves its Ertel potential vorticity. The stretching of absolute vorticity per unit density cancels the deformation of the scalar gradient; the remaining baroclinic triple product vanishes. In an incompressible density-stratified ideal fluid, choosing the advected density as α gives that cancellation directly.
