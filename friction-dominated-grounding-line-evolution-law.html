Friction-dominated grounding-line evolution law ([αρw​/ρ−Hx​]x˙G​)

 ... Branches of physics Geophysics Glaciology Ice sheet dynamics Ice shelf Grounding line
OurBigBook.com Words: 192 Articles: 1
For a bed depth b=αx, a thin subglacial till layer of thickness l is idealized by Newtonian till lubrication, with dynamic viscosity λμ and basal drag λμu/l. Neglecting longitudinal stress divergence in the bulk yields u=−(l/λ)(g/ν)Hhx​. Retaining the unbuttressed Newtonian grounding-line stress condition as boundary data and differentiating ice-sheet flotation along the moving grounding line gives
(αρρw​​−Hx​)x˙G​=λl​νg​Hhx​(hx​+α)−8νg′H2​.
(34)
Indeed, mass conservation gives Ht​=−uHx​−Hux​, Hx​=hx​+α, and ice-sheet flotation gives Ht​=(αρw​/ρ−Hx​)x˙G​. This is a formal local friction closure, not a uniformly valid removal of membrane stress near every grounding line. If the coefficient of x˙G​ vanishes, it is an implicit compatibility condition and cannot be divided to obtain a finite speed.
