Rolling one-period bond account (βt​=βt−1​/Pt−1​(t))

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fixed-income security Interest rate Bank account
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
Reinvest each maturity payment in the next unit-principal zero-coupon bond. Positive bond prices make the resulting self-financing portfolio strictly positive, so it can be used as a numéraire. Its one-step growth is the reciprocal of the current one-period bond price.
