Weak isospin (T,T3)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Flavor symmetry Isospin
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
Weak isospin labels representations of the SU(2) factor in the electroweak gauge group. A left-handed lepton doublet has T=1/2 with third-component eigenvalues +1/2,−1/2; a right-handed charged-lepton singlet has T=0. Along with electric charge, the third component determines the weak neutral current through T3−Qsin2θW​.
