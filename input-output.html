Input-output

 Home Computer science
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
Transfers of data between a computer and its devices or external environment. An operating-system process can need to wait for an input-output operation to complete, leaving the CPU available for other runnable work. Polled input-output, interrupt-driven input-output and DMA describe different aspects of arranging and observing such transfers.
