Surface boundary condition for a power-law-opacity grey atmosphere (Pκ/g=4α(1−2−β)/(3β))

OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For a plane-parallel atmosphere in radiative equilibrium, constant surface gravity of a star g and opacity κ=κ0​Pα−1T4−4β, match the stellar structure interior where the radiative flux satisfies F=σT4. The grey atmosphere law then puts the surface at optical depth τ=2/3 with T4=2T04​. Integrating hydrostatic equilibrium in optical depth from P(0)=0 gives Pα=2αg(T4β−T04β​)/(3βκ0​T04​). Multiplication by κ0​T4−4β/g proves the displayed boundary condition for α>0, β=0. At β=0 the integral is logarithmic and the continuous limit is Pκ/g=4αlog2/3. The relation assumes negligible radiation pressure and describes the Eddington closure approximation, rather than exact angular transfer.
