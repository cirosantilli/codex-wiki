Knight theorem for orthogonal martingales (Mj​(t)=Bj​(⟨Mj​⟩t​))

Let M1​,…,MN​ be continuous local martingales starting at zero, with ⟨Mi​,Mj​⟩=0 for i=j and ⟨Mj​⟩∞​=∞ almost surely. The individual Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem time changes produce independent standard Brownian motions Bj​ with Mj​(t)=Bj​(⟨Mj​⟩t​). Independence is between the Brownian motions; each may still depend on its own clock.
