Complex amplitude (a^)

 Home Physics Wave Wave amplitude
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 2
A complex number can encode both the wave amplitude and phase of a harmonic disturbance: a(x,t)=Re{a^ei(kx−ωt)}. Its modulus is the real oscillation amplitude and its complex argument fixes the wave phase.
