Sheaf of meromorphic functions on a complex manifold (KX​)

 ... Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Complex submanifold Complex analytic hypersurface Divisor on a complex manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 165 Articles: 4
The sheaf KX​ assigns to an open subset of a complex manifold its meromorphic functions. Its subsheaf KX∗​ consists of the nonzero meromorphic functions, while OX∗​ consists of the nowhere-zero holomorphic functions.
