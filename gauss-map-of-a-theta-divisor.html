Gauss map of a theta divisor (Γ:Θsm​→P(T0​J∗))

 ... Algebraic geometry Algebraic variety Algebraic group Abelian variety Jacobian variety Theta divisor
OurBigBook.com Words: 142 Articles: 1
Translate the tangent hyperplane at a smooth point of the theta divisor to the identity of the Jacobian variety. Its annihilator defines the Gauss map. At L=OX​(D) with h0(L)=1, the derivative of the Abelian sum map identifies this annihilator with the unique line H0(KX​(−D)). Thus Γ(L)=[ω] implies D≤div(ω). This connects the intrinsic geometry of the theta divisor to the canonical map of the curve.
