Nottingham group (N(Fp​))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Profinite group Pro-p group
OurBigBook.com Words: 289 Articles: 3
Over k=Fp​, the Nottingham group is N(k)={f(t)=t+∑i≥2​ai​ti:ai​∈k} with composition and the t-adic topology. Equivalently it is the group of continuous k-automorphisms of the Laurent series field k((t)) that act trivially on (t)/(t2). With ordinary series composition, the isomorphism sends f to the automorphism h(t)↦h(f−1(t)); this inverse avoids reversing the group product. It is a pro-p group, recovered from its finite truncations, and contains elements of arbitrarily large finite p-power order.
