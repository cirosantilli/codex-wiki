Host–guest complex

 Home Chemistry Supramolecular chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 5
A molecular association in which a host provides a binding site or cavity for a guest. Its equilibrium constant includes solvent and entropy contributions, not only direct host–guest attraction.
