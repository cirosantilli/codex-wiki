Downstream similarity of a shear-driven gravity current (yN​=(3μΔρgQx/τ2)1/3)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Reduced gravity Gravity current Shear-driven viscous gravity current
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For a steady point-source shear-driven viscous gravity current far downstream, h≪yN​≪x permits dropping the downstream gravity flux. With w=h2, the equation is wx​=D(w2)yy​/(4A), a quadratic porous medium equation. Conserving A∫wdy=Q yields yN​=(3μΔρgQx/τ2)1/3 and h2=τ(yN2​−y2)/(2Δρgx) inside the current. The central height decays as x−1/6. Near the source, balancing shear and gravity gives an upstream reach of order μΔρgQ​/τ, whose numerical coefficient is not determined by scaling.
