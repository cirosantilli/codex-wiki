Centered convection-diffusion semidiscretization

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Finite difference Finite difference method
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
With homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions, centered differences turn one-dimensional diffusion into a symmetric negative-definite matrix and constant advection into a skew-symmetric matrix. Their sum dissipates the discrete Euclidean norm, giving mesh-uniform energy stability for every fixed advection coefficient.
