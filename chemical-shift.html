Chemical shift (δ=(ν−νref​)/νref​)

 Home Chemistry Nuclear magnetic resonance
OurBigBook.com Words: 91 Articles: 3
A chemical shift measures an NMR resonance position relative to a reference. Electronic environments alter local magnetic shielding, so shifts can report a changed molecular environment without uniquely specifying a structure.
