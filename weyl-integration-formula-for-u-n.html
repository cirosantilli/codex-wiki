Weyl integration formula for U(n) (n!1​∏i<j​∣zi​−zj​∣2)

For the unitary group, the diagonal maximal torus has coordinates zi​=eiθi​ and Weyl group Sn​. The Weyl integration formula weights independent uniform angles by n!−1∏i<j​∣zi​−zj​∣2. The Vandermonde determinant det(zin−j​) has squared torus integral n!, by Fourier orthogonality. Multiplying an irreducible character of highest weight λ by this determinant gives an alternating Laurent polynomial. Its leading alternant has coefficient one; character orthogonality for compact groups forces every other alternant coefficient to vanish. This yields the quotient det(ziλj​+n−j​)/det(zin−j​).
