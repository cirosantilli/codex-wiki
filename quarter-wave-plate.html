Quarter-wave plate (δ=±π/2(mod2π))

 ... Electromagnetism Maxwell equations Maxwell equations in matter Linear anisotropic dielectric Birefringence Waveplate
OurBigBook.com Words: 495 Articles: 5
A quarter-wave plate gives a quarter-cycle phase retardation. Equal transmitted wave amplitudes initially in linear polarization at 45∘ become circular polarization; unequal wave amplitudes generally give elliptical polarization. Reversing the sign reverses the traversal of the same circle.
