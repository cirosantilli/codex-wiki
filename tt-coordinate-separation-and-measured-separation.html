In transverse-traceless gauge, particles initially at rest have fixed spatial coordinates because Γμ00​=0. Their separation measured in a parallel-propagated orthonormal frame changes: to first order SA^=SA+hAB​SB/2. Hence the physical geodesic deviation is ξ¨​A^=h¨AB​LB/2, whereas the coordinate displacement obeys ξ¨​coordA​=0. The extra ∂0​ΓA0B​LB term in the coordinate expansion of the second covariant derivative reconciles the two results. This applies to infinitesimal separations and linear metric perturbations.
