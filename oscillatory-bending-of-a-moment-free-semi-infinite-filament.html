Oscillatory bending of a moment-free semi-infinite filament

 ... Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biopolymer mechanics Stokesian dynamics of an elastic filament Small-slope elastohydrodynamic filament equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
In units of elastohydrodynamic penetration length and inverse forcing frequency, yt​=−yxxxx​. Prescribing y(0,t)=y0​cost, zero bending moment yxx​(0,t)=0, and decay at infinity gives
y(x,t)=2y0​​[e−Cxcos(t+Sx)+e−Sxcos(t−Cx)],C=cos(π/8),S=sin(π/8).
(47)
The two components have phase velocities −1/S and 1/C. Their unequal spatial attenuation produces a nonreciprocal shape cycle even though the endpoint actuation is reciprocal.
