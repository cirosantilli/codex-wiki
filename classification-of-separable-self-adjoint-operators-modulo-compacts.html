OurBigBook.com Words: 72
Bounded self-adjoint operators on separable infinite-dimensional complex Hilbert spaces are unitarily equivalent modulo compact operators exactly when they have the same essential spectrum in the Calkin algebra. The Weyl-von Neumann theorem diagonalizes each modulo a compact error. Their diagonal cluster sets are the essential spectra; cluster-set matching of bounded sequences pairs the two diagonal lists with differences tending to zero, giving a compact diagonal difference. The separability assumption is essential: projections with countable and uncountable range dimensions can have the same essential spectrum but cannot differ by a compact operator after unitary conjugation.
