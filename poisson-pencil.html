Poisson pencil (Πλ​=Π1​+λΠ2​)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Poisson manifold Poisson structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 230 Articles: 3
Two Poisson structures are compatible when every linear combination Π1​+λΠ2​ is a Poisson structure. This family is a Poisson pencil. In terms of the Schouten-Nijenhuis bracket, compatibility means [Π1​,Π2​]=0, in addition to the two individual Jacobi identities. Constant Poisson bivectors are compatible. Over a real manifold, complex parameter values refer to the complexification of its function algebra.
