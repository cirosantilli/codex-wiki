Timelike separation (s2<0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Minkowski spacetime Minkowski metric Minkowski interval
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
Two distinct spacetime events have timelike separation when their displacement has negative Minkowski interval in metric signature (−,+,+,+), equivalently c∣Δt∣>∣Δx∣. A massive observer can connect them along a straight timelike curve. If the later spacetime event has Δt>0, every Lorentz transformation in the orthochronous Lorentz group keeps it later. The elapsed proper time on the straight inertial segment is −s2​/c.
In the light-second units of the Minkowski spacetime example, O to A has s2=(−42+32)ls2=−7ls2. Its straight moving clock therefore records 7​s, approximately 2.646s, while the coordinate time difference is 4s. Coordinate time and proper time answer different questions.
