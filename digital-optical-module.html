A glass-enclosed photomultiplier with local digitization: pulse times and charges enter event reconstruction. Reciprocal electrical pulses synchronize module clocks to the surface time base.[ref]
Weak charged current interactions of muon neutrinos usually give extended muon tracks. Compact particle showers occur in other channels, including weak neutral current interactions of every neutrino flavor. Tracks generally constrain direction better; escaping particles complicate energy inference.[ref]
