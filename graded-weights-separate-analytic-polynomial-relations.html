Graded weights separate analytic polynomial relations

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Dimension of level-one modular forms Polynomial ring of level-one modular forms
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
Let Pw​(E4​,E6​) be weighted homogeneous of modular weight w, and suppose ∑w​Pw​(z)=0 as a holomorphic function. Apply the modular group matrices (1n​01​). At a fixed point z in the complex upper half-plane, this gives ∑w​(nz+1)wPw​(z)=0 for every integer n. A polynomial with infinitely many roots vanishes identically, so every Pw​(z)=0. Within one modular weight, the monomials reduce to powers of E43​/E62​, after taking out a common factor. This ratio is nonconstant, as its Fourier expansion begins 1+1728q+⋯, so its powers are linearly independent. This proves algebraic independence even when the generators are regarded as analytic functions rather than elements of a formal graded ring.
