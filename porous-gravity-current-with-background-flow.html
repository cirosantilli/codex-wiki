Porous gravity current with background flow (ϕht​+(Uh−Kb​hhx​)x​=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Porous gravity current
OurBigBook.com Words: 206 Articles: 3
A shallow buoyant porous gravity current in a deep aquifer with uniform imposed Darcy velocity U obeys ϕht​+(Uh−Kb​hhx​)x​=0 away from sources, where Kb​=kΔρg/μ. Here k is permeability of a porous medium, ϕ is porosity, and μ is dynamic viscosity. The pore-fluid drift speed is u=U/ϕ, and the gravity diffusion coefficient multiplying (hhx​)x​ is a=Kb​/ϕ. The sharp-interface model neglects capillary trapping and dissolution.
