P-star approximation (p∗(θ∣a;θ)=c(θ,a)∣j(θ)∣1/2eℓ(θ)−ℓ(θ))

For a regular model with a suitable ancillary statistic a, the displayed expression approximates the conditional density of the maximum-likelihood estimator. The normalizing factor includes (2π)−p/2 for a p-dimensional estimator, depending on convention. The observed information is evaluated at the sample's own fitted value. In a pure scale model the factor is proportional to 1/θ; this Jacobian determinant can make the normalized approximation coincide with the exact conditional density.
