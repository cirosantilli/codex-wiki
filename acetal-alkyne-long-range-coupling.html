Acetal-alkyne long-range coupling

 Home Chemistry Nuclear magnetic resonance Scalar spin-spin coupling
OurBigBook.com Words: 19
Proton coupling across H-C-C≡C-H can resolve a small four-bond splitting. In a propargyl acetal this connects the acetal methine and terminal alkyne proton.
