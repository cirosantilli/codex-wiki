Stopping-time sigma-algebra (FT​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Stopping time
OurBigBook.com Words: 82 Articles: 1
The information available at a stopping time T is the sigma-algebra
FT​={A∈F:A∩{T≤t}∈Ft​ for every t≥0}.
(28)
In discrete time, A∩{T=k}∈Fk​ for A∈FT​. A bounded stopping time permits conditional forms of the optional stopping theorem with respect to this sigma-algebra.
