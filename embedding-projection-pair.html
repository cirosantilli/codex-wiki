Embedding-projection pair (pe=id,ep≤id)

 ... Set theory Set Partially ordered set Complete partial order Directed-complete partial order Pointed complete partial order
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
For Scott continuous maps e:D→E, p:E→D, these two identities say that D is embedded in E and that projection followed by embedding approximates from below. Such pairs lift to endomorphism spaces by f↦efp and g↦pge. Their composite on the smaller space is the identity, and the other composite is g↦(ep)g(ep)≤g.
