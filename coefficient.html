Coefficient (ak​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear combination
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A scalar multiplying a specified term in a linear combination, polynomial or power series. When the term family has linear independence, the coefficients are unique; uniqueness of finite Laurent polynomial coefficients follows after multiplying by a sufficiently large power to obtain an ordinary polynomial.
