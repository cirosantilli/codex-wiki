Chebotarev density theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Dirichlet density
OurBigBook.com Words: 128 Articles: 1
For a finite Galois extension L/K and a conjugacy class C in G=Gal(L/K), the unramified primes with Frobenius conjugacy class C have Dirichlet density ∣C∣/∣G∣. In particular,
∑p:Frobp​∈C​(Np)−s=∣G∣∣C∣​logs−11​+O(1)(s↓1).
(50)
One proof uses Brauer induction, the Artin L-function order at one, and character orthogonality. The trivial Artin L-function is the Dedekind zeta function of K. The full theorem also gives the natural density, but this logarithmic estimate alone establishes the Dirichlet density assertion.
