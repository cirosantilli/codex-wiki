Complete lottery preferences (L⪰M or M⪰L)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Finitary relation Binary relation Preference relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A preference relation on lotteries is complete when every pair is comparable, allowing an indifference relation. Completeness does not require strict ranking of distinct lotteries. Together with a transitive relation, it gives the ordering assumption in the Von Neumann-Morgenstern utility theorem.
