Bed shear response

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Sediment transport Bedform
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
For a small sinusoidal bedform, a fluid-dynamical closure can be written τ^=τ0​k(A+iB)η^​ for k>0. The real coefficient A is in phase with the bed height and B describes an upstream phase lead. Neither coefficient follows from the Exner equation or sediment relaxation alone; they require a flow calculation or measurement.
