Analog-to-digital converter

 Home Electrical engineering
OurBigBook.com Words: 369 Articles: 6
An analog-to-digital converter produces a discrete numerical code from an input voltage. Its specifications include input range, resolution, conversion rate, input impedance, offset, gain error and nonlinearity. A uniform b-bit converter covering voltage span Vspan​ has nominal spacing q≃Vspan​/2b. The uniform-quantization error bound applies to rounding inside the range, while clipping is a different error. Sampling and quantization are separate operations: neither sufficient sampling rate nor a large bit count alone guarantees accurate reconstruction.
