Gowers uniformity norm (Uk norm)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics
OurBigBook.com Words: 300 Articles: 10
The Gowers uniformity norm measures the average multiplicative derivative of a function around affine cubes. The Uk norm detects polynomial phases of degree below k.
