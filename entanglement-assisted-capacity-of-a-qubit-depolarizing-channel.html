Entanglement-assisted capacity of a qubit depolarizing channel (CE​=2−H4​(1−p,p/3,p/3,p/3))

For the quantum depolarizing channel applying the identity with probability 1−p and each nonidentity Pauli operator with probability p/3, the entanglement-assisted classical capacity is the displayed number of bits per use. Here H4​ is Shannon entropy of the four listed probabilities. Its unassisted product-state capacity is 1−h2​(2p/3). Both vanish at p=3/4. If q instead denotes replacement by I/2, then p=3q/4 and complete depolarization is at q=1. The original entanglement-assisted capacity paper gives the depolarizing-channel formula.
