Nevanlinna theory

 ... Analysis Complex analysis Isolated singularity Classification of isolated singularities Pole Meromorphic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 23
Nevanlinna theory studies the growth and value distribution of meromorphic functions through their integrated pole counts and logarithmic proximity to target values. The Nevanlinna characteristic combines both quantities, while the Nevanlinna first main theorem compares it with the corresponding counts and proximity for any fixed target.
