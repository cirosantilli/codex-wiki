Linear polarization

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Plane electromagnetic wave Polarization of an electromagnetic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A wave is linearly polarized when its oscillating electric field always lies along one fixed line. For a transverse plane wave, that line is perpendicular to the wavevector. Unlike circular polarization, the field oscillates along the line rather than rotating through directions.
