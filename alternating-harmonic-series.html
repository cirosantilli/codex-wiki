Alternating harmonic series

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Conditional convergence Alternating series test
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
The alternating harmonic series ∑n≥1​(−1)n+1/n converges. Its even partial sums increase, its odd partial sums decrease, and the two limits agree because their difference is the next term. In particular, every even partial sum is a lower bound and every odd partial sum is an upper bound for the limit.
