Orbital plane

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Kepler orbit Orbital element
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
The plane containing a central-force Kepler orbit, perpendicular to its conserved specific angular momentum. Its orientation can be specified by an orbital inclination and an orbital node. Perturbing forces can change that orientation.
