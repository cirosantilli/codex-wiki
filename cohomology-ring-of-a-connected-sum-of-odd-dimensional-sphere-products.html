Cohomology ring of a connected sum of odd-dimensional sphere products (H∗(Wg​;Z))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Smooth manifold Closed manifold Connected sum of oriented manifolds
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
For odd r≥1 and Wg​=#i=1g​(Sr×Sr), the integral cohomology has one copy of Z in degrees zero and 2r, Z2g in degree r, and zero elsewhere. Choose the top orientation class ω and degree-r classes αi​,βi​ from the two factors of summand i. Their nonzero positive-degree basis products are
αi​⌣βj​=δij​ω,βj​⌣αi​=−δij​ω.
(16)
All αi​αj​ and βi​βj​ vanish, and ω annihilates positive-degree classes. The Künneth theorem computes each sphere product; the Mayer–Vietoris sequence computes the connected sum groups, and its degree-one pinch maps determine the products. Thus its middle-degree Poincare duality pairing is a symplectic vector space after changing coefficients to a field of characteristic different from two. The case r=1 is the usual cohomology ring of a closed oriented surface.
