Zweibein (eAμ​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Orthonormal frame in spacetime Orthonormal coframe in spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A zweibein is a two-dimensional orthonormal coframe in spacetime: its components obey hμν​=ηAB​eAμ​eBν​. It converts worldsheet coordinate indices into local Lorentz-frame indices and defines curved gamma matrices γμ=eA​μγA. The associated spin connection supplies the covariant derivative of worldsheet spinors. In flat conformal gauge the coframe can be constant.
