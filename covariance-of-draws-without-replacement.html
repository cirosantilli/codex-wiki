Covariance of draws without replacement (Cov(Z1​,Z2​)=−σN2​/(N−1))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Probability sampling Sampling without replacement
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
Draw two distinct positions uniformly from a fixed population z1​,…,zN​ with N>1. Let μ=N−1∑i​zi​ and σN2​=N−1∑i​(zi​−μ)2. Each draw has expected value μ, while
E[Z1​Z2​]=N(N−1)(∑i​zi​)2−∑i​zi2​​=μ2−N−1σN2​​.
(14)
Subtracting the product of the expected values proves the covariance formula and explains the finite population correction. If the sampled values are coin biases and the actual tosses are conditionally independent given those biases, the law of total covariance gives the same covariance for their head indicator random variables.
