Gnathostome

 Home Biology Organism Eukaryote Animal Vertebrate
OurBigBook.com Words: 409 Articles: 23
A member of the jawed-vertebrate lineage. Crown usage includes the ancestor of living cartilaginous and bony vertebrates and all its descendants; a total-group usage additionally includes jawless stem relatives. The usage must be stated when discussing early fossils.
