Character table from a cyclic quotient and two elementary-abelian orbits (C32​⋊C4​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Character table
OurBigBook.com Words: 111
A group of order thirty-six with a normal C32​ and a cyclic quotient of order four acting freely on its eight nonzero elements has six conjugacy classes of sizes 1,4,4,9,9,9. Its four linear characters are lifted from the cyclic quotient. The remaining irreducible degrees are four and four, by the sum of squared degrees. Column orthogonality forces the last three values of both degree-four rows to vanish. The square of the order-four action is inversion on C32​, making the first two nontrivial class values real. Orthogonality then gives sum minus one and product minus two, so the rows are (4,1,−2,0,0,0) and (4,−2,1,0,0,0). This illustrates why reality must be justified when reconstructing a character table from orthogonality.
