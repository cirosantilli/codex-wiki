Irreducible closed subset

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Irreducible topological space
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
A nonempty closed subset which, with its induced topology, is an irreducible topological space. In an affine algebraic set these subsets correspond, with reversed inclusion, to prime ideals of the coordinate ring.
