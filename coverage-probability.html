Coverage probability (Pθ​(θ∈C(Y)))

The coverage probability is the repeated-sampling probability that a random confidence interval contains the fixed true statistical parameter. A finite-sample level-1−α procedure has coverage probability at least 1−α at every admissible statistical parameter; exact nominal coverage means equality. An asymptotically calibrated procedure approaches the nominal value as sample size grows, under its stated assumptions. Higher-order Edgeworth expansions can quantify the discrepancy from nominal coverage probability when their regularity conditions hold.
Observed coverage in finitely many repetitions is itself random. For m independent repetitions of the exact normal mean confidence interval with known variance, the count K of covering confidence intervals has binomial distribution Binomial(m,0.95). At m=40, its expectation is 38, but
