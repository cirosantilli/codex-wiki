Sobolev inequality

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space Sobolev embedding theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 147 Articles: 4
A Sobolev inequality bounds a function in one Lq norm by its weak derivatives in another Lp norm. In three dimensions, the first-order case ∥u∥L6​≤C∥∇u∥L2​ is the endpoint estimate behind the continuous embedding H1↪L6.
