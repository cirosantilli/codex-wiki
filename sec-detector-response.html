SEC detector response

 Home Chemistry Chromatography Liquid chromatography Size-exclusion chromatography SEC chromatogram
OurBigBook.com Words: 118
A SEC detector response must be specified before interpreting a peak area. Ultraviolet absorbance depends on a species' response factor and concentration, as described by the Beer-Lambert law; differential refractive index detection depends on how the dissolved species changes refractive index. Equal amounts need not produce equal signal areas across unlike species.
Even a calibrated mass-proportional trace is not a chain-count probability distribution. In an original two-population example, equal recovered masses of chains with molar masses 20,000 and 80,000gmol−1 contain four times as many chains in the lighter population. Thus equal signal areas, assuming equal response per unit mass, correspond to number fractions 4/5 and 1/5: their number-average molar mass is 32,000gmol−1, while their weight-average molar mass is 50,000gmol−1. Converting a continuous trace additionally requires response correction and the appropriate change of variables between elution and molar mass bins.
