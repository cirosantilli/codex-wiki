Visual contrast (C=(L−L0​)/L0​)

 Home Biology Neuroscience Computational neuroscience Neural coding Receptive field
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For the toy models here, visual contrast means brightness relative to a positive uniform background L0​: C(x,y)=(L(x,y)−L0​)/L0​. Positive values mean brighter than background, negative values darker, and zero unchanged. This convention separates spatial structure from the background level. A sinusoid C=acos(⋯) with 0≤a≤1 keeps L=L0​(1+C) nonnegative. Different experimental conventions for visual contrast require their own definitions; the toy values below do not specify a historical stimulus intensity.
