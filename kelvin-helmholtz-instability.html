Kelvin-Helmholtz instability

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Shear layer Vortex sheet
OurBigBook.com Words: 390 Articles: 8
For a planar vortex sheet separating equal-density streams of velocities U1​ and U2​, a mode of nonzero wavenumber k has complex growth exponent
σ=−ik2U1​+U2​​±2∣k∣∣U1​−U2​∣​.
(52)
One sign has positive growth rate, so every nonzero wavenumber is unstable in the inviscid zero-thickness model.
