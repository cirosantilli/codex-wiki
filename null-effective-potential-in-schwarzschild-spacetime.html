For an equatorial null geodesic in Schwarzschild spacetime, let k=(1−2μ/r)dt/dλ and h=r2dϕ/dλ be the conserved quantities for an affine parameter λ. The null metric condition gives r˙2/2+Veff​=c2k2/2. For h=0, the effective potential is zero at the Schwarzschild event horizon, positive outside, and tends to zero at infinity. Its unique exterior maximum is at r=3μ, with value h2/(54μ2) and second derivative −h2/(81μ4). This maximum gives the unstable photon sphere.
The impact parameter of an incoming ray is b=∣h∣/(ck) for k>0. Capture requires its radial energy to exceed the maximum, namely b<33​μ. At equality the incoming ray approaches the circular orbit asymptotically; it does not cross the horizon. For h=0, the potential is identically zero and the ingoing radial ray is captured.
