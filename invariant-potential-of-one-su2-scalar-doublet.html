Invariant potential of one SU2 scalar doublet (V(ϕ)=F(ϕ†ϕ))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field theory Scalar field Scalar potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
The special unitary group SU(2) acts transitively on every sphere in its defining C2: for ∣u∣2+∣v∣2=1, the matrix with columns (u,v)T and (−vˉ,uˉ)T is in SU(2). Thus any invariant real polynomial in a single commuting doublet and its complex conjugate depends only on ϕ†ϕ. Through total degree four it is V0​+m2ϕ†ϕ+λ(ϕ†ϕ)2. A holomorphic polynomial invariant of positive degree does not exist for one doublet: Symd2 is a nontrivial irreducible representation for every d>0.
