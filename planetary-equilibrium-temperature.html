Planetary equilibrium temperature (Teq​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Irradiated planetary atmosphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For a planet of Bond albedo AB​ orbiting a blackbody star of radius R∗​ and temperature T∗​, complete global redistribution and negligible internal heat give
Teq4​=(1−AB​)T∗4​4a2R∗2​​.
(4)
Uniform dayside-only reradiation replaces 4a2 by 2a2. The planetary equilibrium temperature describes absorbed stellar power; including an intrinsic cooling flux gives a total effective temperature Teff4​=Tint4​+Teq4​, distinct from Teq​. Neither quantity is necessarily the greenhouse-warmed surface temperature.
