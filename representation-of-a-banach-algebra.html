Representation of a Banach algebra (π:A→EndC​(X))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Representation of an associative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 350 Articles: 5
A representation of a complex Banach algebra is a complex-linear multiplicative action on a vector space. For a unital action require π(1)=I; a nonunital algebra can be handled by extending the action to its unitization. A nonzero action is algebraically irreducible when it has no nonzero proper invariant vector subspace, equivalently when it defines a simple module.
