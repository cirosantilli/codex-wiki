Moduli space of Ricci-flat K3 metrics (SO(3,19)/(SO(3)×SO(19))×R>0​)

 ... Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Complex surface K3 surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
Locally, a unit-volume Ricci flat metric on a K3 surface determines a positive three-plane in its real second cohomology, with the K3 intersection lattice fixed. The corresponding Grassmannian has dimension 3⋅19=57. Allowing the volume gives one further positive real parameter; large diffeomorphisms identify lattice-equivalent descriptions. This is the same continuous moduli count as the three-torus Narain moduli space plus its coupling.
