Shearing-coordinate magnetic flux equation (∂t​a=η[(∂s​−imtΩ′)2+s−1(∂s​−imtΩ′)−m2/s2]a)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Resistive induction equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
For a planar magnetic field written as B=∇×(Az) and a circular velocity u=sΩ(s)ϕ​, the resistive induction equation reduces, after choosing the additive gauge transformation, to ∂t​A+Ω∂ϕ​A=η∇2A. A Fourier mode A=a(s,t)eim[ϕ−Ω(s)t] therefore obeys the equation in the title. Indeed, each radial derivative acting on the exponential replaces ∂s​ by ∂s​−imtΩ′, while the azimuthal Laplacian contributes −m2/s2. At zero magnetic diffusivity, a is constant in time, but the physical magnetic field can still be amplified algebraically by differential rotation.
