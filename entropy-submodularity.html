Entropy submodularity

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Mutual information
OurBigBook.com Words: 285 Articles: 5
For discrete random variables X,Y,Z,
H(X,Y)+H(Y,Z)≥H(Y)+H(X,Y,Z).
(7)
The difference between the left- and right-hand sides is the nonnegative conditional mutual information I(X;Z∣Y).
