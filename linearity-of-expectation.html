Linearity of expectation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Expected value
OurBigBook.com Words: 11
Expectation is linear without requiring independence: E[aX+bY]=aE[X]+bE[Y] whenever the expectations exist.
