Particle-conserving displacement of a Fermi circle (ν(θ)=p0​cosθ+pF2​−p02​sin2θ​−pF​)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Fermi liquid Fermi surface Fermi contour Fermi circle
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
Translating a Fermi circle of radius pF​ by p0​x^ preserves its area. For p0​<pF​, its radial displacement from the original centre is the displayed expression. Its Taylor expansion is ν=p0​cosθ−p02​sin2θ/(2pF​)+O(p04​/pF3​). The second-order term cancels the apparent area increase from the first-order shape: ∫02π​(pF​ν+ν2/2)dθ=0. This distinction matters when converting a grand-potential variation into a fixed-particle number energy change.
