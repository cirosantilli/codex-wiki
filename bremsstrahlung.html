Bremsstrahlung

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic radiation
OurBigBook.com Words: 170 Articles: 3
Bremsstrahlung is electromagnetic radiation emitted when a charged particle accelerates during interaction with another charged particle or field. The microscopic process e−+Z→e−+Z+γ creates a photon, while its inverse absorbs one. Thus it contributes to a photon-number collision source in the photon number current, unlike ordinary Compton scattering. Thermal bremsstrahlung averages this emission over a thermal distribution of charged particles.
