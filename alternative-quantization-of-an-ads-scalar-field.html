Alternative quantization of an AdS scalar field (Alternative quantization of an AdS scalar field)

When both scalar falloffs in anti-de Sitter spacetime are normalizable, alternative quantization exchanges which near-boundary coefficient is treated as the source and which as the expectation value. In AdS4, a field with m2=−2 may be dual to a scalar operator of dimension one.
