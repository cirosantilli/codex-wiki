Witness acceptance operator (F=J†U†ΠUJ)

 Home Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Quantum complexity theory QMA
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
For quantum witness embedding J, verifier unitary U and accepting projector Π, the witness acceptance operator is the positive contraction F=J†U†ΠUJ. The maximum acceptance over normalized quantum witnesses is λmax​(F) by the Rayleigh quotient. Restricting to basis quantum witnesses only tests its diagonal entries and need not find that maximum.
