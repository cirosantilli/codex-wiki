Chiral Z-boson partial width (Γf​∝Nc​(gL2​+gR2​))

At leading order, for final fermions much lighter than the Z boson, the partial decay width is proportional to Nc​(∣gL​∣2+∣gR​∣2). Here Nc​ is the final-state color multiplicity in a decay width, and gL​=T3L​−Qsin2θW​, gR​=T3R​−Qsin2θW​ are the dimensionless left- and right-chirality couplings. The common normalization cancels in ratios.
For an electron, gL​=−1/2+sin2θW​, gR​=sin2θW​. For one active neutrino flavour, gL​=1/2, gR​=0. Therefore
Γ(Z→e−e+)Γ(Z→ννˉ)​=(−1/2+sin2θW​)2+sin4θW​1/4​.
(40)
There is no extra flavour factor for a single specified neutrino species. Summing the three active species produces three times that invisible partial width, within the same massless approximation.
