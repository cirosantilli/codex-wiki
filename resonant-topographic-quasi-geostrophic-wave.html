Resonant topographic quasi-geostrophic wave

OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 1
A stationary boundary corrugation can resonate with a Rossby wave whose intrinsic propagation cancels the background current. For constant N, rigid upper height H, and m=π/H, define κ2=k2+ℓ2+f02​m2/N2. When U=β/κ2, a lower corrugation proportional to eikxsin(ℓy) admits a disturbance proportional to eikxsin(ℓy)[Btcos(mz)+C(z−H)sin(mz)], with B=2ikf0​U2/β and C=N2/(f0​m). Its wave activity grows quadratically with time while the upward Eliassen–Palm flux grows linearly. The linear prediction applies only while the disturbance remains small.
