Canonical arithmetic progression dichotomy

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Ramsey theory Gallai theorem for an integer lattice
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
Every colouring of the positive integers, even with infinitely many colours, has an arithmetic progression of any prescribed finite length on which the colouring is constant or injective. Encode a progression by the equivalence relation of equality of its colours. This gives a finite colouring of its starting point and step. A Gallai theorem for an integer lattice copy of a suitable two-dimensional pattern makes either all indices inequivalent, or one equal-colour pair range independently over two whole progressions. In the second case, one of those progressions is monochromatic.
