Lagrange theorem for polynomial congruences

 Home Mathematics Chinese remainder theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A nonzero polynomial of degree d over a field has at most d roots. In particular, if a polynomial congruence f(x)≡0(modp) has degree d and not all coefficients are divisible by the prime p, it has at most d incongruent solutions modulo p.
