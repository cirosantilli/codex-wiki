Extent of reaction (ξ)

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Stoichiometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 111
For a closed system with one specified chemical reaction, the extent of reaction ξ records progress from ξ=0 through ni​=ni,0​+νi​ξ. Here ni​ is the amount of substance of species i, and the signed stoichiometric vector has negative entries for reactants and positive entries for reaction products. For the water chemical equation in the order H2​,O2​,H2​O, it is (−2,−1,2). The normalization of ξ depends on the chosen coefficients. See IUPAC Gold Book, extent of reaction.
With particle numbers instead of molar amounts, a molecular extent ξN​ obeys dNi​=νi​dξN​, and the chemical potentials are measured per particle. At fixed temperature and volume, the Helmholtz free energy then satisfies dF=(∑i​νi​μi​)dξN​. An interior chemical equilibrium requires ∑i​νi​μi​=0; the molar convention gives the same relation with chemical potentials per mole.
