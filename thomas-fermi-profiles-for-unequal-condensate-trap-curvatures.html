For a miscible two-component Bose-Einstein condensate with Vi​=Ai​r2, D=U11​U22​−U122​>0, invert the local interaction matrix. The displayed result and n2​=[U11​(μ2​−A2​r2)−U12​(μ1​−A1​r2)]/D hold in the overlap region. Factoring by Ri2​=μi​/Ai​ puts the ratio λ=A2​/A1​ in the first profile and λ−1 in the second. Outside overlap, a negative formal density must be replaced by the appropriate single-component solution, not retained as a physical density.
