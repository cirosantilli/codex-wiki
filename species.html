Species

 Home Biology Taxonomy (biology)
OurBigBook.com Words: 778 Articles: 6
A species is a named unit of biological classification. The useful criteria vary among groups: reproductive compatibility can inform classification in many animals, while microbial comparisons also use genomes and other characters. Escherichia coli is a species; K-12 MG1655 identifies a particular strain within it. See OpenStax Biology 2e, 18.2: species concepts.
