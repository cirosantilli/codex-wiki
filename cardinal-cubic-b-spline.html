Cardinal cubic B-spline

 ... Analysis Uniform approximation Spline (mathematics) Spline approximation B-spline Cardinal B-spline
OurBigBook.com Words: 218 Articles: 3
A cardinal cubic B-spline is a degree-three Cardinal B-spline. At the three interior integer knots, its nonzero normalized values are 1/6, 4/6, and 1/6.
