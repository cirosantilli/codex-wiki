Phase difference (Δϕ=ϕ2​−ϕ1​)

 Home Physics Wave Phase (waves)
OurBigBook.com Words: 60 Articles: 1
The difference of two wave phases at the same place and time, considered modulo 2π. It determines whether two same-frequency oscillations reinforce, cancel, or are in phase quadrature.
