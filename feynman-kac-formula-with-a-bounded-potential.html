Feynman-Kac formula with a bounded potential (ut​=21​uxx​+Vu)

For bounded V and bounded initial data f, a sufficiently regular classical solution bounded on every finite time slab satisfies
u(t,x)=Ex​[f(Bt​)exp(∫0t​V(Bs​)ds)].
(37)
To prove this, the Itô formula and Itô product rule make u(T−t,Bt​)e∫0t​V(Bs​)ds a local martingale. Its deterministic bound on [0,T] makes it a true martingale; taking expectations at the two endpoints gives the formula. This also gives uniqueness in the finite-horizon bounded class. The exponent has the same sign as the potential in the equation.
