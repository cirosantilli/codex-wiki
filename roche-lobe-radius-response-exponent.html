Roche-lobe radius response exponent (ζL​=dlogRL​/dlogMd​)

 ... Stellar astrophysics Binary star Roche lobe Roche-lobe overflow Conservative binary mass transfer Dynamical stability of binary mass transfer
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 1
For conservative binary mass transfer and RL​∝a(Md​/M)1/3,
ζL​=2q−35​,q=Ma​Md​​.
(15)
The conservation of angular momentum gives dloga/dlogMd​=−2(1−q). Since a mass loss has dlogMd​<0, the donor moves inside its lobe when ζad​>ζL​. An ideal fully convective donor with ζad​=−1/3 is therefore stable for q<2/3 and unstable for q>2/3 under these assumptions.
