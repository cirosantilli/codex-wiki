Vibronic coupling

 Home Chemistry Molecule Molecular vibration
OurBigBook.com Words: 60 Articles: 1
Coupling between electronic and nuclear vibrational motion. Dependence of an electronic electric dipole transition moment on a normal mode coordinate can give intensity to a transition whose purely electronic matrix element vanishes at the equilibrium geometry.
