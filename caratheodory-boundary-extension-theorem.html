Caratheodory boundary extension theorem

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Uniformization theorem Riemann mapping theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For a bounded simply connected domain D and a conformal bijection f:D→D, f extends continuously to the closed disc if and only if ∂D is a locally connected space. A Jordan boundary gives a homeomorphism of closed discs, but local connectedness alone need not give injectivity on the boundary: a slit has two boundary approaches. The same statement applies on the sphere to unbounded domains after a suitable change of coordinates. This boundary theorem is different from the Caratheodory extension theorem for measures.
