Data processing inequality for mutual information

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Mutual information
OurBigBook.com Words: 60 Articles: 1
If X→Y→Z is a Markov chain, then
I(X;Z)≤I(X;Y),I(X;Z)≤I(Y;Z).
(476)
Thus deterministic or randomized processing cannot increase the information shared with an unprocessed variable.
