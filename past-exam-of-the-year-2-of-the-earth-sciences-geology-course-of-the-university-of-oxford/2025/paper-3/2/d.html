The sketches assume the car can be approximated by a compact magnetic dipole whose induced magnetic moment is parallel to the local geomagnetic field. The host contributes negligible local contrast. Real steel also has remanent magnetization and shape-dependent magnetization, so the car's actual profile need not follow this idealization. A proton-precession magnetometer observes

ΔT≃4πr3μ0​m​[3(B0​⋅r)2−1].
