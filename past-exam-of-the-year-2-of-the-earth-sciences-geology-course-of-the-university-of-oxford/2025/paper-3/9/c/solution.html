The compositions alone do not specify a unique thermodynamic activity model. Adopt the usual ideal, independent mixing on crystallographic sites approximation: three Mg–Fe positions per formula unit, with the other sites fixed at their stated endmember compositions. Then the mineral endmember activities are
aphlogopite​=0.853,apyrope​=0.233.
(50)
The exponent counts the three mixing cations; treating each whole mineral formula as a single mixing site would be a different model. In particular, the magnesian biotite and garnet components here are phlogopite and pyrope.
For the written reaction, the reaction quotient is
Q=aphlogopite​apyrope​​P0​P​,
(51)
with the other solid activities equal to one. The Gibbs energy of reaction balance therefore gives
T=170.7−8.3145ln[4500(0.23/0.85)3]130862.28​=981.235K≃708.085∘C.​
(52)
The smaller product-endmember activity lowers the reaction chemical potential and lowers the equilibrium temperature in this model. Nonideal activity coefficients or unequal occupancies of crystallographic sites would require further information rather than a revised universal numerical answer.[ref]
