OurBigBook.com Words: 102
Assuming pure dip-slip faulting, use an east–west striking, 65∘ north-dipping normal fault, with the northern hanging wall moving down dip. In a right-hand-rule strike convention this is strike 270∘, dip 65∘, rake −90∘. The auxiliary nodal plane strikes east–west and dips 25∘ south. The focal mechanism has a predominantly vertical P axis and a predominantly north–south horizontal T axis; the steep central direction is dilatational.
The sketch is a double-couple seismic moment tensor calculation, not a claim that first motions uniquely identify which nodal plane slipped. The mapped north-dipping surface supplies that additional geological choice.
