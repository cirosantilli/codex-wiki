Choose shot and receiver intervals to sample the shortest relevant spatial wavelength and steepest event moveout without spatial aliasing. A simple bound is
Δx≲2fmax​vapp,min​​,
(26)
using the smallest apparent velocity of the events one needs to retain or separate. Using 1500ms−1 and 100 Hz alone suggests 7.5 m, but slower surface waves or dipping events can demand finer spacing. It is not a universal final design interval.
Also consider target depth and lateral resolution, reflector dip, near-surface heterogeneity, source strength and coupling, maximum offset and aperture, common-midpoint bin size and seismic acquisition fold, refraction crossover distances, access and budget. Receiver spacing and source spacing jointly determine coverage and cannot be selected from temporal sampling alone.
