Specify the amplitude convention. For normal-stress/pressure amplitudes, the seismic reflection coefficient and seismic transmission coefficient are
Rσ​=Z2​+Z1​Z2​−Z1​​=3719​=0.51351,Tσ​=Z1​+Z2​2Z2​​=3756​=1.51351.​
(22)
For particle displacement or velocity resolved in one fixed normal coordinate, the reflected amplitude changes sign and the transmitted ratio is instead
Rv​=−3719​,Tv​=Z1​+Z2​2Z1​​=3718​=0.48649.
(23)
These are alternative conventions, not conflicting energy predictions. The reflected and transmitted energy flux fractions are
R=1369361​=0.26370,T=13691008​=0.73630,
(24)
which sum to one for the ideal lossless interface. Thus an amplitude transmission coefficient greater than one does not violate conservation of energy.
