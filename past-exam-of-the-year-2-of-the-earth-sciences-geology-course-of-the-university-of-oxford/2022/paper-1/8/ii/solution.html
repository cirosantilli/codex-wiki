Subtract reaction (b) from reaction (a) and divide by two. In the direction chosen here, the resulting balanced conversion is
NaAlSiO4​+2H4​SiO40​=NaAlSi3​O8​+4H2​O.
(37)
Thus nepheline plus dissolved silica converts to albite. Additivity of the Gibbs energy of reaction, equivalently Hess's law, gives
log10​Kc​=21​(logKa​−logKb​)=21​(6.88+15.18)=11.03.​
(38)
For the reverse reaction the answer is −11.03; the reaction direction must accompany the equilibrium constant.
