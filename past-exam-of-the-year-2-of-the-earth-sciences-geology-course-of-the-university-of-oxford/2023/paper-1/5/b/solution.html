For a laterally extensive Bouguer slab of thickness h and geophysical density contrast Δρ,
Δg=2πGΔρh,ρgs​=2670+2πGhΔg​.
(9)
The greenstone belt reaches from roughly +1.0 to −0.5 km near its deepest central part, so use h≃1500 m. The gravity maximum is approximately −100 mGal; the exposed host-rock background in the south is approximately −115 mGal. Taking this as a constant regional reference gives a local excess Δg≃15 mGal=1.5×10−4 ms−2. Thus
ρgs​≃2670+2π(6.6743×10−11)(1500)1.5×10−4​≃2.91×103 kgm−3​.
(10)
This is an approximate density under a constant-background, infinite-slab model. The finite width, variable thickness and neighboring pluton require a fuller gravity inversion for a precise value. In particular, the absolute value −100 mGal must not be inserted as the local excess.
