Assume the greenstone belt is more magnetically susceptible than the host rocks and granite, its magnetization is mainly induced magnetization, and the body is roughly east–west elongated so that the south–north line crosses it. At southern magnetic latitudes the main field has negative magnetic inclination in a north-and-down coordinate convention. The total-field magnetic anomaly is then generally asymmetric: the principal negative lobe is on the southern side and the positive lobe is displaced northward for the simple induced source illustrated here. The exact lobe sizes and peak positions depend on body geometry and the actual magnetic inclination.[ref]
An explicit sketching model is an induced magnetic dipole at depth d. With northward coordinate x, r=(x,−d) and unit field direction F=(cosI,sinI), its normalized total-field magnetic anomaly is
