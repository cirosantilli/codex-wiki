For a heterogeneous fault slip distribution, seismic moment is
M0​=G∫A​DdA=GAD,
(2)
where G=30×109 Pa is the shear modulus, A the fault plane area, and D the area-averaged fault slip. The peak colour cannot be used as D: most of the illustrated fault plane has much smaller fault slip.
One reproducible graphical estimate divides the approximately 190 km long and 40 km down-dip panel into seven 25 km strips and a final 15 km strip. Reading area averages from the colour scale, from southwest to northeast, gives roughly 1.7,2.7,3.1,1.8,1.9,1.7,1.0,0.3 m. This includes the pale, nearly unslipped lower part of each strip. Hence
∫A​DdA≃40{25(1.7+2.7+3.1+1.8+1.9+1.7+1.0)+15(0.3)} km2m≃1.4×1010 m3,
(3)
and M0​≃4.2×1020 Nm. A reasonable graphical range is approximately 3–6×1020 Nm, dominated by colour averaging and the down-dip extent of small fault slip. The corresponding moment magnitude is about Mw​=7.7, using Mw​=32​(log10​(M0​/Nm)−9.1). This is an estimate from the supplied distribution, rather than a substitution of a catalogued earthquake magnitude.
