Subtract the chlorite–gibbsite reaction from the chlorite–kaolinite reaction. The common chlorite, protons and magnesium cancel, leaving
2Al(OH)3​+2H4​SiO4​=Al2​Si2​O5​(OH)4​+5H2​O.
(37)
This converts gibbsite and dissolved silica into kaolinite. Gibbs energies of reaction add with reaction stoichiometry, so decimal logarithms of equilibrium constants also subtract:
log10​K=61.14−52.65=8.49​.
(38)
With unit water and solid thermodynamic activities, K=aH4​SiO4​−2​; thus x=−8.49/2=−4.245, a vertical boundary. Reversing the reaction would give log10​K=−8.49 and the same boundary. Its intersection with either sloping line is (x,y)=(−4.245,13.077).
