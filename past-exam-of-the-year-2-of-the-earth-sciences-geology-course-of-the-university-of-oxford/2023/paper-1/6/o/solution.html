Apply the same stress-amplitude convention to S-wave seismic impedance. At limestone–sandstone,
RS​=2.4+3.32.4−3.3​=−193​=−0.1579,TσS​=1916​=0.8421​.

At sandstone–salt,
RS​=3.0+2.43.0−2.4​=91​=0.1111,TσS​=910​=1.1111​.
(26)
A stress-amplitude transmission greater than one does not violate conservation of energy: the transmitted energy fraction includes the seismic impedance ratio. For particle velocity, the corresponding (Rv​,Tv​) pairs are (3/19,22/19) and (−1/9,8/9). At normal incidence in isotropic layers, pure P and pure S waves do not undergo seismic mode conversion.
