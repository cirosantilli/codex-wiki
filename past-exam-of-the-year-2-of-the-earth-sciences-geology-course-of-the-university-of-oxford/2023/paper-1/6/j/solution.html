A transmitted P-wave seismic head wave occurs when its refraction angle reaches 90∘, requiring sinic​=VP,incident​/VP,transmitted​≤1. At the limestone–sandstone boundary the ratio is 2500/2000>1: there is no P-wave seismic critical angle for incidence from limestone. A slower lower layer cannot carry that transmitted head wave.
At the sandstone–salt boundary,
sinic​=40002000​=21​,ic​=30.00∘​.
(20)
The head wave travels along the faster salt boundary and radiates energy back into the slower overlying medium. Reversing the direction of incidence would change which interfaces admit such a head wave.
