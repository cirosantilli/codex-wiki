Under ideal independent mixing on crystallographic sites, a mineral endmember's thermodynamic activity is the product of its required site fractions, one factor per mixed site. Muscovite has one K and three Al per formula unit, sanidine has one K and one Al, and corundum has two Al. Interpreting the given Al fractions as the fractions on every relevant Al/Fe site gives
amus​=0.95(0.9)3=0.69255,asan​=0.75(0.9)=0.675,acor​=(0.8)2=0.64.

The cubic power for muscovite includes its tetrahedral Al site and two octahedral Al sites; if site-specific fractions differed, bulk Al/(Al+Fe) alone would not determine this activity. The calculation uses the uniform-site ideal-mixing interpretation intended by the given data.
