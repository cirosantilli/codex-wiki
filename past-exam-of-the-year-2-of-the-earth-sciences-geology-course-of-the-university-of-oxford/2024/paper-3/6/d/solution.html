The null space consists of concentration patterns on which transport produces no change. Here it is the family

Every box has the same concentration. Ocean circulation may continue, but inflow and outflow of the chemical tracer balance in each box. With b=0 these are exactly the steady states; physically admissible members have α≥0. With nonzero b, adding α1 to any particular steady state leaves its transport balance unchanged, but a null space vector alone need not balance the sources and sinks.
