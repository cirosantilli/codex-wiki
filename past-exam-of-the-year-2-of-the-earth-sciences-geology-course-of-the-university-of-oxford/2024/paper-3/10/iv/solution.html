A stability boundary in a mineral activity diagram must also be stable against the third mineral. Use the common-potential test for a three-mineral activity diagram. For the two corundum-producing directions,
2RTln10Δr​Ga​​=y−(3.44−x),2RTln10Δr​Gb​​=y−(−7.59−3x).
(90)
Negative values favour corundum. For the albite-to-nepheline conversion, Δr​G/(RTln10)=2x+11.03, so low silica activity favours nepheline and high silica activity favours albite. These comparisons give:
  • Nepheline–corundum: stable for x<−5.515, metastable on the opposite extension.
  • Albite–corundum: stable for x>−5.515, metastable on the opposite extension.
  • Nepheline–albite: stable for y>8.955 along x=−5.515, metastable below the intersection.
All three coexist at the intersection. Pairwise mass-action equations alone do not distinguish the stable segments from their metastable extensions.
