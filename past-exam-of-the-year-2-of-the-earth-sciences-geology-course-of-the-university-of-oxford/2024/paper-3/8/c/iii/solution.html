At x=10δY​, the annual attenuation is e−10 and the daily attenuation is e−10365​, approximately 10−83. Thus
Θ−Θ0​=​Ae−10cos(ωY​t−10)+Be−10365​cos(ωD​t−10365​).​​
(79)
On the same vertical scale as the surface plot this is almost the constant Θ0​. With a sufficiently enlarged scale, a residual annual oscillation of amplitude 4.54×10−5A remains. Its phase lag is ten radians, interpreted modulo 2π when comparing periodic maxima.
