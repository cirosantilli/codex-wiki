The source text and its figure are inconsistent here. The plotted low-frequency maximum in W is near 0.0055day−1, rather than the printed 0.055day−1. The original PDF shows that this discrepancy is already present in the examination, rather than introduced by transcription.
Machine 2 records only about half a year. Its first nonzero discrete Fourier transform bin is therefore at
Δf≃180–183days1​≃0.0055day−1.
(72)
The annual component near 1/365day−1 is below that first bin. The finite window function spreads it through spectral leakage, with a prominent first-bin contribution. This explains the plotted peak without invoking a new physical period.
A literal peak at 0.055day−1 would instead have a period of about 18.2 days. Neither the two stated model frequencies nor the given sampling schedule identifies such a separate component. The interpretation above treats the printed decimal as an apparent typo; it does not silently change the stated number.
