Vertex of a polyhedron

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Polyhedron
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A corner of a polyhedron, stored with its spatial coordinates and incidences with boundary edges and faces. In a closed manifold boundary representation, the incident faces form a single cyclic fan. The vertices of a polyhedron and edges define a graph, but geometric embedding and valid face incidence require additional information.
