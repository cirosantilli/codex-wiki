Acetylcholine (ACh)

 Home Biology Neuroscience Neuron Synapse Neurotransmitter
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
Acetylcholine is a neurotransmitter that acts through nicotinic ionotropic receptors and muscarinic metabotropic receptors. The receptor and the target cell's machinery determine the response; acetylcholine itself does not specify whether coupling is direct or mediated by intracellular signal transduction. [ref]
