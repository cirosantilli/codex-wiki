For an interface perturbation ηeikx+st with instantaneous interface kinetics, take the liquid concentration perturbation proportional to e−qz. Here Γ>0 is the coefficient of curvature-induced melting-temperature depression. The advection-diffusion equation and interfacial mass conservation give
s=Dq2−Vq−Dk2,sCI​=(Dq−V)[DVCI​​−mG+Γk2​].
(22)
For a growing normal mode the unique decaying liquid branch has q=(V+V2+4D(s+Dk2)​)/(2D). Writing b=(G+Γk2)/(mCI​), its rate is s=(D/2)(V/D−b)(b2+4k2​−b). Instability requires G+Γk2<mVCI​/D. The Gibbs-Thomson effect suppresses sufficiently short waves; a quasistatic solute diffusion to solute diffusion changes the rate and must be stated separately.
