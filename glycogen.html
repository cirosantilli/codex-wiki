Glycogen

 Home Biology Metabolism
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
Glycogen is a highly branched polysaccharide of glucose units, serving as a storage form of glucose in animals. Liver and muscle provide familiar examples. The classical second messenger experiments measured regulation of an enzyme involved in glycogen breakdown, glycogen phosphorylase. [ref]
