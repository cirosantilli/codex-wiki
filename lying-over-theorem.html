Lying-over theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Integral element Integral extension
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
If A⊆B is an integral extension, then every prime ideal p of A is the contraction of a prime ideal q of B. Thus the contraction map SpecB→SpecA is surjective.
