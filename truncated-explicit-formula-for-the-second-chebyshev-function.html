OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For 2≤T≤x, the truncated Perron formula and a contour shift give the displayed form of the Riemann–von Mangoldt explicit formula. The pole at one contributes x and the Nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function contribute the finite sum. One can choose a separated height in [T,T+1] and then restore T using the local zero count for the Riemann zeta function. The error absorbs the half-weight discrepancy at a prime power. Outside this range of T, one needs the fuller error with the near-integer terms retained.
