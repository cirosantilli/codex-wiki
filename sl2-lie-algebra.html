sl2 Lie algebra (sl2​)

 ... Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Simple Lie algebra Special linear Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 799 Articles: 16
The Lie algebra sl2​ has generators e,h,f with [h,e]=2e, [h,f]=−2f, and [e,f]=h.
This is the n=2 instance of the special linear Lie algebra. Classifying its modules is a separate representation-theory problem.
