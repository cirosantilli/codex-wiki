Turbulence

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 107
Turbulence is irregular fluid motion with interacting velocity fluctuations over a range of scales. It can mix a particle suspension and promote fluid entrainment into a turbulent plume.
