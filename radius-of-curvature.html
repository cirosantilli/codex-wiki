Radius of curvature (ρ=1/κ)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Curvature and torsion Curvature of a space curve Curvature of a plane curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Where a regular curve has positive curvature κ, its radius of curvature is the radius ρ=1/κ of its osculating circle. A sharper bend has a smaller radius. At zero curvature there is no finite radius; writing ρ=∞ is a limiting convention rather than an ordinary real reciprocal of zero.
