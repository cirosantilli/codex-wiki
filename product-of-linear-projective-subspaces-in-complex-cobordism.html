Product of linear projective subspaces in complex cobordism ([CPk,νk​][CPm,νm​]=[CPk+m−n,νk+m−n​])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Cobordism Complex cobordism
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
The displayed product applies when k+m≥n. Move one projective subspace by a unitary isotopy into transverse position. The intersection is a linear projective subspace of dimension k+m−n, with normal complex bundle the sum of the restricted input normal bundles. If k+m<n, representatives can be made disjoint and the product is zero.
