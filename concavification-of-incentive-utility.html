Concavification of incentive utility (Fˉ=least concave majorant of F)

 ... Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Utility function Expected utility hypothesis Expected utility maximization Hedge fund incentive utility
OurBigBook.com Words: 147 Articles: 2
Concavification replaces a nonconcave incentive utility function by its least concave majorant. In a complete market with an atomless state-price density, the optimizing payoff may avoid all intervals where the majorant strictly exceeds the original utility, making the relaxed optimum exactly attainable. At a pricing atom corresponding to a linear segment, a lottery between contact points can attain the same value at unchanged cost.
