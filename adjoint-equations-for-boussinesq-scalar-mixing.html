Adjoint equations for Boussinesq scalar mixing

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Scalar transport
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
For a smooth incompressible direct trajectory with total velocity field U and active scalar Θ, the negative-constraint Lagrangian function in constrained optimization convention gives
∂t​u†+U⋅∇u†−(∇U)Tu†+∇p†+νΔu†−θ†∇Θ=0,∇⋅u†=0,
(1)
∂t​θ†+U⋅∇θ†+κΔθ†−RiB​uy†​=0.
(2)
The transpose term is the formal adjoint of δu⋅∇U; the two couplings transpose scalar transport by advection and buoyancy. For terminal cost ∫Θ(T)2, the terminal data are u†(T)=0, θ†(T)=2Θ(T). They are integrated backward along the stored direct trajectory. No-slip boundary conditions and Dirichlet boundary conditions for the adjoint velocity and homogeneous Neumann boundary conditions for the adjoint scalar remove the spatial boundary terms.
