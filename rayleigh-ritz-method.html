Rayleigh-Ritz method

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Finite element method
OurBigBook.com Words: 417 Articles: 6
The Ritz method minimizes a quadratic energy over a finite-dimensional trial space. For basis functions ϕj​ and bilinear form a, its linear system is Ka=F with Kjk​=a(ϕk​,ϕj​) and Fj​=⟨f,ϕj​⟩.
