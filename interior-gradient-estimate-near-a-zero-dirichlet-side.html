Interior gradient estimate near a zero Dirichlet side

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Elliptic differential operator Elliptic regularity
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For a smooth uniformly elliptic homogeneous equation away from other boundaries, an interior gradient estimate on a ball of radius comparable to the distance s from a flat side gives ∣∇u∣≤Cs−1supBcs​​∣u∣. If u extends continuously with value zero on that side, s∣∇u∣→0 locally along it. This justifies vanishing boundary terms such as ux​sin(nx) in a distributional Fourier sine projection.
