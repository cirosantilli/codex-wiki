Worldsheet integration count after conformal gauge fixing (dg​+n−kg​=3g−3+n)

 ... Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Polyakov action Polyakov path integral Closed-string scattering amplitude
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For a stable n-point closed-string scattering amplitude on a genus-g surface, let dg​ count complex unmarked worldsheet moduli and kg​ count complex dimensions of the worldsheet conformal Killing group. Integrating the n vertex positions and moduli, then fixing kg​ positions, leaves dg​+n−kg​ complex integrations. The punctured Riemann surface moduli dimension identifies this as 3g−3+n. Faddeev-Popov ghost fields and antighost insertions implement the removed zero modes and the surviving modulus measure; they do not add independently integrated worldsheet coordinates.
