Direct product of rings (∏λ​Rλ​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 426 Articles: 5
The direct product of a family of rings consists of all tuples of their elements, with coordinatewise addition and multiplication. Unlike a direct sum, tuples may have infinitely many nonzero coordinates.
