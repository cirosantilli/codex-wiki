Electronic heat capacity coefficient (γ=limT→0​Cel​/T)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Heat capacity Electronic heat capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 2
For a Fermi liquid, Cel​=γT+o(T) at low temperature. In a simple quasiparticle description, γ=(π2/3)kB2​g(EF​), where the density of states includes both spin states and uses the same per-volume or per-mole normalization as γ. A fit C/T=γ+βT2 separates this contribution from a Debye lattice term over an appropriate low-temperature range. Magnetic or other low-energy excitations can require extra fit terms.
