Omega-consistency of an arithmetic theory (ω-consistency)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order theory Soundness of an arithmetic theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 76 Articles: 1
A first-order theory is omega-consistent when it never proves ∃xϕ(x) while proving ¬ϕ(nˉ) for every standard natural number n. Restricting the matrix to arithmetic bounded arithmetic formulas gives one-consistency. This condition concerns standard instances and is stronger tha first-order consistency.
