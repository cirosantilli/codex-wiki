Combinatorial line

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Ramsey theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 3
Let X be a finite alphabet. A combinatorial line in Xn is obtained by fixing some coordinates and replacing every coordinate in one nonempty active set by the same variable letter. It therefore contains one word for each letter of X.
