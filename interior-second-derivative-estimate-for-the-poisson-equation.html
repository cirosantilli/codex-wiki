Interior second-derivative estimate for the Poisson equation (∥u∥H2(U)​≤C(∥u∥H1(V)​+∥Δu∥L2(V)​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 85
If u∈H1(V) and Δu=f∈L2(V) weakly, then u∈Hloc2​(V). Test the weak equation with −δ−h​(η2δh​u), move the first difference quotient onto Du, and bound the remaining quotient on the forcing side by the first derivative of η2δh​u. Young inequality absorbs the localized second derivatives. Uniform bounds in h, together with the Sobolev characterization by bounded difference quotients, give the assertion without differentiating f. The constant depends only on dimension and the positive distance from U to the boundary of V.
