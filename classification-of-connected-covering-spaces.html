Classification of connected covering spaces

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Covering space
OurBigBook.com Words: 331 Articles: 8
Under the usual local hypotheses, based connected coverings of X correspond to subgroups of π1​(X); changing the point above the base point conjugates the subgroup. Normal subgroups correspond to regular coverings.
