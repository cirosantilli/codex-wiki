Metal ion catalysis

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Chemical kinetics Catalysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
Metal ion catalysis uses a coordinated metal ion to activate a reagent, orient reactants or stabilize charge. Zinc can lower the acidity constant of coordinated water and support attack by the resulting oxygen nucleophile without a metal redox step.
