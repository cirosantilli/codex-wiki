Gauge-boson mass matrix (Lmass​=21​Aμa​(M2)ab​Abμ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge boson
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 1
With canonically normalized gauge field kinetic terms, a gauge-boson mass matrix is the real symmetric matrix of coefficients in the displayed quadratic mass term. Its eigenvalues are squared physical gauge boson masses. For a complex scalar field vacuum Φ0​ in the Higgs mechanism, (M2)ab​=2Re[(ga​Ta​Φ0​)†(gb​Tb​Φ0​)]. Thus for real xa​, xTM2x=2∥∑a​xa​ga​Ta​Φ0​∥2≥0: it is a Gram matrix and a positive semidefinite matrix. An unbroken generator annihilates the vacuum expectation value and lies in its null space. Diagonalizing the matrix identifies the massive and massless field combinations.
