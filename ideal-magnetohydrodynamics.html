Ideal magnetohydrodynamics (ideal MHD)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 6k Articles: 117
Ideal magnetohydrodynamics assumes vanishing resistivity, so magnetic flux is frozen into the fluid and the electric field in the fluid rest frame vanishes.
