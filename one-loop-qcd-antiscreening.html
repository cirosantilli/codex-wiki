One-loop QCD antiscreening (β0​=11−2nf​/3)

OurBigBook.com Words: 176
The +11 contribution to β0​ is the net Non-Abelian gauge theory contribution; the −2nf​/3 term comes from quark loops. Thus quarks screen color charge, opposing asymptotic freedom. It is the net gluon contribution that produces antiscreening: the inferred QCD coupling decreases toward shorter distances. Unlike the electrically neutral photon, a gluon carries color charge and has self-interactions. Describing this as vacuum polarization must not imply that all vacuum contributions have the screening sign of quantum electrodynamics.[ref]
Individual gauge-dependent Feynman diagrams do not separately define observable screening clouds. A proof of the coefficient sums the gauge fixing, Faddeev-Popov ghost and matter contributions and extracts the charge renormalization; the final one-loop order coefficient is independent of the gauge choice. The beta function is negative near zero for integer 0≤nf​≤16. This is a statement about weak coupling, not a determination of the low-energy particle spectrum.[ref]
