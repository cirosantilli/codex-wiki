Parameter-dependent boundary data in a regular perturbation hierarchy (L0​un​=fn​−L1​un−1​,Bun​=gn​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Asymptotic expansion Regular perturbation
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
For (L0​+ϵL1​)u=f(ϵ) and Bu=g(ϵ), expand all three quantities in powers of ϵ. The order-n problem has L0​un​=fn​−L1​un−1​ and Bun​=gn​, with u−1​=0. In particular, Taylor series boundary coefficients are generally nonzero even when the differential operator depends linearly on the parameter. If the homogeneous realization of L0​ is invertible and boundary data admit smooth lifts, the same inverse solves each successive inhomogeneous boundary value problem. Induction establishes unique coefficients at every order. This formal conclusion does not alone assert convergence of the full power series; convergence needs additional bounds on the inverse and the perturbation.
