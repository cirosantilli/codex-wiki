Amplitude of an oscillatory integral (a(x,θ))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Oscillatory integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 317 Articles: 4
An oscillatory integral amplitude multiplies eiΦ in an oscillatory integral with phase function Φ. A finite symbol class order controls its frequency growth and the order of a distribution obtained by integration by parts.
