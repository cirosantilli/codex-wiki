Dirac bracket

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Hamiltonian mechanics Poisson bracket
OurBigBook.com Words: 60 Articles: 1
Second-class constraints χa​ with invertible bracket matrix can be eliminated with a modified bracket. For an ordinary even constraint set it is {F,G}D​={F,G}−{F,χa​}(C−1)ab{χb​,G}, Cab​={χa​,χb​}. Graded constraints require the matching graded sign convention.
