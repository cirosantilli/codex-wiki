Pressure compatibility of nonlinear torsional Alfvén profiles (∂z​(fg)=0)

 ... Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Magnetohydrodynamic wave Alfvén wave Torsional Alfvén wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For uniform-density incompressible flow with uϕ​=f(R,z+vt)+g(R,z−vt), Bϕ​/μ0​ρ​=f−g and Bz​/μ0​ρ​=v(R), the azimuthal magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation and ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation hold automatically. Let Π=p/ρ+Φ+[(f−g)2+v2]/2. The remaining equations require Πz​=0 and ΠR​=4fg/R, so ∂z​(fg)=0 is necessary and locally sufficient. A single traveling profile is unrestricted; two nonzero profiles on an unrestricted axial domain must be opposite exponentials or independent of their traveling arguments. Bounded profiles of both signs therefore reduce to steady solutions.
