Degree of a continuous mapping (degf)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 33
For a map f:Sm→Sm, the degree is the integer by which f∗​ multiplies a chosen generator of Hm​(Sm;Z). It is invariant under homotopy.
