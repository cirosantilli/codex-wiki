Infinite sites mutation model

 Home Biology Genetics Population genetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 131 Articles: 1
The infinite-sites model assumes that every mutation occurs at a previously unmutated site, so each mutation on the sample genealogy produces a distinct segregating site. If mutation rate per lineage is θ/2 in standard coalescent time units, a tree of total length L has a Poisson distribution of mutation count with mean θL/2.
