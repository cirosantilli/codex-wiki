Consider a nonlinear diffusion equation with uniform supply, ϕht​=Dm​(hmhx​)x​+R, on x>0, with an absorbing boundary h(0,t)=0 and initially h=0. Far from the boundary, h=Rt/ϕ. Balancing the time derivative, supply, and diffusion gives
h=ϕRt​F(η),η=ℓ(t)x​,ℓ(t)=[ϕm+1Dm​Rmtm+1​]1/2.
(1)
The similarity solution satisfies
(FmF′)′+1−F+2m+1​ηF′=0,F(0)=0,F(∞)=1.
(2)
The outward boundary volume flux per unit width is Q=Rℓcm​, where cm​=limη↓0​FmF′. Integrating the equation gives
cm​=2m+3​∫0∞​(1−F)dη.
(3)
Thus the growing region of depleted storage fixes the discharge prefactor, and Q∝t(m+1)/2.
