If the galactic distribution function is not restricted to positive relative energy, isotropic velocity integration gives ρ(ψ)=4π2​∫−∞ψ​f(E)ψ−E​dE. The same Abel transform argument as Eddington inversion yields the displayed formula, with lower limit −∞. For ρ=ρ0​eψ/σ02​, substitution ψ=E−σ02​s2 gives f(E)=ρ0​(2πσ02​)−3/2eE/σ02​. An escape-energy truncation changes both the density law and the velocity tails.
