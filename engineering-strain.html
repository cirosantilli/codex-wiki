Engineering strain (ε=ΔL/L0​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Constitutive equation Strain
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
Engineering strain is the change in length divided by that segment's initial length. For an extensometer, use its own initial gauge length and extension.
