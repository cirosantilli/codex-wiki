Rng (algebra)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 248 Articles: 3
A rng has an abelian group under addition, an associative multiplication, and the distributive property; a multiplicative identity element is not required. Every ring under this wiki's unital convention is a rng. A nonunital ring may happen to have an identity: the terminology removes an axiom rather than imposing the absence of an identity. For example, the even integers with their usual operations have no multiplicative identity.
