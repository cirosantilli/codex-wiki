Clock

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Time in physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 2
A device that supplies a reproducible measure of elapsed time. An ideal relativistic clock measures proper time along its world line; the clock hypothesis separates this geometric ideal from disturbances of a particular physical mechanism.
