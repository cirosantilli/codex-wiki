Option delta (Δ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Greeks (finance)
OurBigBook.com Words: 239 Articles: 3
The option delta is the derivative of an option value with respect to the underlying asset price. In a smooth complete diffusion model, it is also the number of underlying units in the replicating delta hedge.
