Monomorphism

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category
OurBigBook.com Words: 667 Articles: 13
A monomorphism m:A→B is left-cancellative: mu=mv implies u=v. It represents a subobject of B.
