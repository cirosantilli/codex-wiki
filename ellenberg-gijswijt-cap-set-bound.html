Ellenberg–Gijswijt cap-set bound

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Polynomial method in combinatorics Cap set
OurBigBook.com Words: 134
There is a constant C<3 such that every cap set in F3n​ has cardinality less than Cn.
For the polynomial method in combinatorics, use
T(x,y,z)=∏i=1n​(1−(xi​+yi​+zi​)2).
(77)
Over F3​, this is the indicator function of x+y+z=0. On A3 it is therefore a diagonal tensor with ∣A∣ nonzero diagonal entries. Every monomial in its expansion has individual exponents at most two and total degree at most 2n, so one of its three variable blocks has degree at most 2n/3. Grouping terms according to such a block and using the slice rank of a diagonal tensor gives
∣A∣=slice rank(T∣A3​)≤3mn​,
(78)
where mn​ is the number of α∈{0,1,2}n with ∑i​αi​≤2n/3. If Xi​=1−αi​ are independent random variables uniform on {−1,0,1}, then
3nmn​​=P(∑i​Xi​≥3n​).
(79)
Any exponential upper bound for this tail probability gives mn​≤(3−ϵ)n and hence the result after absorbing the factor three and finitely many small dimensions into C<3.
