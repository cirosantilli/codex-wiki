Add independent rate-μ resets to zero to a linear birth process with immigration. The resulting irreducible continuous-time Markov chain is nonexplosive by domination of births and finiteness of Poisson reset counts. When μ=λ, an excursion from one reaches n with probability 2/(n+1), so its jump chain is recurrent with infinite expected jump count. The elapsed return-cycle time instead has mean
λ−1+∑n≥1​λ(n+1)(n+2)2​=2/λ.
(73)
Thus the continuous-time chain is positive recurrent while its jump chain is null recurrent. Its stationary probabilities are π0​=1/2 and πn​=1/[(n+1)(n+2)] for n≥1.
