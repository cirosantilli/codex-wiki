An ideal electric dipole at the origin with smooth electric dipole moment p(t) has Lorenz gauge electric potential and magnetic vector potential, for r>0,
ϕ=4πϵ0​1​(r2n⋅p(t0​)​+crn⋅p′(t0​)​),A=4πrμ0​​p′(t0​),n=rr​.

For a short proof, place electric charges sq at sp/(2q), s=±1, and let q→∞ with p fixed. The corresponding retarded times are t0​+sn⋅p(t0​)/(2qc)+O(q−2). Each Liénard–Wiechert potential has denominator r−s(n⋅p+rn⋅p′/c)/(2q)+O(q−2). The opposite q/r terms cancel, leaving the displayed electric potential. The products of signed electric charge and velocity add, giving the displayed magnetic vector potential. Bounded time derivatives on the relevant interval justify the Taylor expansions.
