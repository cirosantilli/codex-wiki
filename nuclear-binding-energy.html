Nuclear binding energy (Ebind​=(Zmp​+Nmn​−MA​)c2)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Atomic nucleus
OurBigBook.com Words: 420 Articles: 8
The positive energy needed to separate a bound atomic nucleus into its constituent protons and neutrons. It is the mass defect times c2. In a Skyrmion model of a nucleus, both separated nucleon masses and the multi-soliton state require a consistent quantization and calibration.
