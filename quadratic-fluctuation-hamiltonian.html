Expanding the Hamiltonian about a static classical solution removes the linear term by its Euler-Lagrange field equation. For a canonical real scalar field with potential U, the quadratic fluctuation operator is H=−∂X2​+U′′(φcl​). Positive-frequency normal modes become quantum harmonic oscillators under canonical quantization, yielding the Gaussian contribution to a one-loop soliton mass correction. A negative eigenvalue signals an instability, while a physical zero mode in field theory must be replaced by a collective coordinate.
