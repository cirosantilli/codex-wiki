Jeans-length ionization correction for the Lyman-alpha forest (NH​∝NHI1/3​Γ1/3[fg​T/α(T)]1/3)

Assume optically thin photoionization equilibrium and an absorber depth of order its local Jeans length, L∝(fg​T/nH​)1/2, where fg​ is the local gas fraction. Then NHI​≃α(T)nH2​L/Γ∝[α(T)/Γ](fg​T)1/2nH3/2​. Solving for nH​ and substituting in NH​=nH​L gives the displayed correction. Thus the ionized mass integral weights the neutral-hydrogen column-density distribution by NHI1/3​ rather than NHI​. This model does not apply without modification to optically thick systems or arbitrary unconfined gas.
