Atom in a topos

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Elementary topos
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
An atom is a noninitial object with only the empty and whole subobject. In the atomic finite-surjection site, the sheaf component generated by one primitive class is an atom: membership of one descendant in a subobject descends to its primitive ancestor and then propagates to all descendants. A decomposition into atoms makes subobjects selections of components.
