Bloch ball ({s∈R3:∣s∣≤1})

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Bloch vector
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
The qubit density operators ρ=(I+s⋅σ)/2 correspond exactly to the closed unit ball: their eigenvalues are (1±∣s∣)/2, so positivity is equivalent to ∣s∣≤1. The boundary is the Bloch sphere of pure states, and the centre is I/2. A depolarizing channel scales this ball isotropically, possibly with a sign reversal in the larger completely positive parameter interval.
