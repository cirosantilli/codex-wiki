Conormal flux of a divergence-form operator (∑i,j​aij​Di​uνj​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Solution of a partial differential equation Weak solution Weak formulation
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
For sufficiently regular coefficients and a function u, this boundary quantity is the outward flux appearing when a divergence-form principal operator is integrated by parts. Its zero condition is the natural Neumann boundary condition for the corresponding bilinear form. With only bounded coefficients and a Sobolev solution, the weak formulation can encode that natural condition without asserting that a pointwise boundary derivative exists.
