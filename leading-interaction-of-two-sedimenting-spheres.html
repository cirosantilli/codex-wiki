Leading interaction of two sedimenting spheres (r˙=−δz−3D(I+nn)z/(4r))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Method of reflections for Stokes flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 266 Articles: 2
For two well-separated spheres with isolated downward speeds Vi​ and radii ai​, let r=x1​−x2​, r=∣r∣, n=r/r, and let z point upwards. Their leading Stokeslet interactions give
r˙=−δz−4r3D​(I+nn)z,δ=V1​−V2​,D=a2​V2​−a1​V1​.
(35)
Each sphere's Stokes drag law supplies force −6πμai​Vi​z on the fluid. Evaluate the other's Stokeslet at its centre and subtract the two velocities to obtain the equation. Corrections from finite size and potential dipoles are higher order in radius divided by separation. The approximation must remain well separated throughout the segment of trajectory to which it is applied.
