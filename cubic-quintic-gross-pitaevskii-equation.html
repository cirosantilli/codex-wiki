A local two-body and three-body mean-field energy density V0​n2/2+W0​n3/3 generates cubic and quintic terms in the Gross–Pitaevskii equation. Variation with respect to ψ∗ gives (V0​n+W0​n2)ψ, with n=∣ψ∣2. A uniform stationary state's chemical potential is μ=V0​n0​+W0​n02​. Its positive-compressibility condition is V0​+2W0​n0​>0. If μ>0, scaling amplitude by n0​​ and distance by ℏ/2mμ​ gives coefficients α=V0​n0​/μ, β=W0​n02​/μ and α+β=1. Negative chemical potential requires a different sign in the dimensionless linear term.
