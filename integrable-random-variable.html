Integrable random variable

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Expected value
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
A real or complex random variable X is integrable when E∣X∣<∞. Its expected value is then finite, and conditional expectation preserves integrability.
