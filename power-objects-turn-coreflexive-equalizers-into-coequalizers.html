Power objects turn coreflexive equalizers into coequalizers (PA⇉PBPe​PE)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Elementary topos Power object Contravariant power-object functor
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For a coreflexive pair f,g:B⇉A with equalizer e:E↪B, direct image along the mono f satisfies Pf∃f​=1 and Pg∃f​=∃e​Pe. Thus any map h:PB→Z equalizing Pf,Pg obeys h=h∃e​Pe. Since Pe∃e​=1, the map Pe is their coequalizer. These identities hold for parameterized subobjects in any elementary topos.
