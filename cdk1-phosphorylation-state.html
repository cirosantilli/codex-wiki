CDK1 phosphorylation state

 ... Biology Signal transduction Signaling feedback Positive feedback Cell-cycle activation feedback Cyclin-dependent kinase 1
OurBigBook.com Words: 316 Articles: 2
A CDK1 phosphorylation state identifies modified amino acid residues. Different sites have opposite effects: in studied Xenopus laevis Cdc2, Thr14/Tyr15 are inhibitory, while Thr161 phosphorylation is activating. T denotes a threonine residue, Y a tyrosine residue, and numbers are protein primary structure positions. Do not assume this numbering for every species.[ref]
The 1992 study supplies a useful separation of mechanisms: replacing Thr14, Tyr15 and Thr161 still permitted cyclin B binding, but replacing Thr161 prevented activation of histone H1 phosphorylation. Binding therefore did not suffice for catalytic activity in that preparation. Activating phosphorylation also occurred with catalytically inactive Cdc2, supporting a separate CDK-activating kinase rather than a requirement for intramolecular autophosphorylation.[ref]
In this deliberately coarse classification, bound cyclin B, activating phosphorylation, and cleared inhibitory sites must coincide for the activity-permissive state. Removing inhibitory phosphate groups does not supply missing cyclin B or activating phosphorylation. Conversely, adding activating phosphorylation does not erase inhibitory modification. Actual preparations contain mixtures of states and additional regulators; this classification gives no numerical reaction rate.
