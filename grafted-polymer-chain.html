Grafted polymer chain

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymer physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 630 Articles: 12
A polymer chain tethered by an end to a surface. Its surface number density σg​ sets a typical lateral separation D∼σg−1/2​. Comparing D with the isolated coil size determines whether grafted chains behave almost independently or must stretch cooperatively away from the surface.
