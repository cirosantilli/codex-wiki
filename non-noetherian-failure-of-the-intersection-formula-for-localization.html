Non-Noetherian failure of the intersection formula for localization

OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Let
R=k[tq:q∈Q≥0​]
(468)
be the semigroup algebra inside k[tQ], and let I be generated by the tq with q>0. This integral domain is not Noetherian, since
(t)⊊(t1/2)⊊(t1/4)⊊⋯.
(469)
Moreover I2=I, because tq=(tq/2)2. Thus ⋂j≥1​Ij=I=0, whereas the map R→(1+I)−1R is injective because R is a domain.
