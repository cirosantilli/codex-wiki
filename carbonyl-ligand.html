Carbonyl ligand

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry Ligand in coordination chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 80 Articles: 2
A carbonyl ligand is coordinated carbon monoxide, usually attached through carbon. Sigma donation and pi backbonding cooperate in the metal–carbon bond. Terminal and bridging forms have different electron-counting descriptions.
