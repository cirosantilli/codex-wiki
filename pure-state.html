Pure state

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 180 Articles: 3
A pure quantum state is represented by a unit vector ∣ψ⟩ up to global phase, or equivalently by the rank-one density operator ∣ψ⟩⟨ψ∣.
