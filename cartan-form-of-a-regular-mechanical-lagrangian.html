Cartan form of a regular mechanical Lagrangian (θL​=Lvi​dqi,ωL​=−dθL​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Lagrangian mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
A regular Lagrangian L:TQ→R defines a fibre derivative FL:TQ→T∗Q and the Cartan one-form θL​=(FL)∗θ. Its two-form ωL​=−dθL​ is nondegenerate when the velocity Hessian is invertible. The energy is EL​=viLvi​−L. The equation ιΓL​​ωL​=dEL​ determines the Euler–Lagrange vector field. A hyperregular Lagrangian makes the fibre derivative a global diffeomorphism and transports this dynamics to Hamiltonian phase space.
