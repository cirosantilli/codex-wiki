Skolem hull (HullN(A))

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order structure Elementary substructure
OurBigBook.com Words: 170 Articles: 2
After choosing a Skolem function for each existential formula, the Skolem hull of A⊆N is the smallest subset containing A and closed under those functions. The Tarski-Vaught test makes it an elementary substructure of N, and in a countable language an infinite hull has cardinality at most ∣A∣+ℵ0​.
