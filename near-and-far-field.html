Near and far field

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 339 Articles: 9
The near and far field distinguish source-dependent electromagnetic field regions. Close to a localized source, reactive fields and induction terms contribute; sufficiently far away, the leading transverse fields decrease as the inverse distance and carry outgoing power. The radiation zone describes this far-field limit.
