Boltzmann distribution (Pi​=Z−1e−βEi​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
At thermal equilibrium, the Boltzmann distribution assigns a state of energy Ei​ probability Pi​=Z−1e−βEi​, where β=(kB​T)−1 and the partition function Z normalizes the probabilities.
