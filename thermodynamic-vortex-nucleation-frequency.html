Thermodynamic vortex-nucleation frequency (Ωc​=ΔE/ΔLz​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A quantum vortex becomes energetically favourable in a rotating reference frame when ΔE−ΩΔLz​<0. For a singly quantized vortex in a long radially Thomas–Fermi condensate, the cutoff-shell estimate is
Ωc​≃mRr2​2ℏ​[loga0​Rr​​−21​].
(3)
Integrating the trapped number density against uθ2​ produces the logarithm and constant; integrating it against angular momentum per particle gives ΔLz​≃πHn0​ℏRr2​/2. Resolved-core corrections can alter the nonlogarithmic constant. This criterion compares equilibrium rotating-frame energies and does not remove a dynamical vortex-entry barrier.
