Supergravity auxiliary field (Fi=−eK/2Kijˉ​Dj​W​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 37
After eliminating the chiral auxiliary fields in Planck-unit supergravity, one convention gives Fi=−eK/2Kijˉ​Dj​W​. The upper-index inverse satisfies Kijˉ​​Kkjˉ​=δik​. Nonzero auxiliary expectation values diagnose supersymmetry breaking; their common phase convention does not affect the vanishing condition.
