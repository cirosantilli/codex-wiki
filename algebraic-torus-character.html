Algebraic torus character (χm)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Toric geometry Algebraic torus Character lattice of an algebraic torus
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
An algebraic torus character is a morphism of algebraic groups T→Gm​. For a split torus with character lattice M, these are precisely the Laurent monomials χm, with χm+m′=χmχm′.
