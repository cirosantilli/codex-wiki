Dual Haar measure (dmG​)

 ... Algebra Group theory Group Abelian group Pontryagin duality Pontryagin dual group
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
The dual Haar measure is the scale of Haar measure on the Pontryagin dual group compatible with Fourier inversion on a locally compact abelian group. It can be constructed without first assuming inversion: for a compactly supported test function ψ, choose a finite sum of convolution squares p with p​>0 on its support and define J(ψ)=∫ψ/p​dμp​. The Bochner consistency identity for convolution squares makes this independent of the choice. Modulation by a character shows J is invariant under dual translation; the Riesz representation on compactly supported continuous functions gives the measure. It satisfies dμp​=p​dmG​ and scales reciprocally when dmG​ is rescaled.
