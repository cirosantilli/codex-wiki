Decreasing population hazard under constant individual hazards (h′(t)=−Var(Λ∣T>t)≤0)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis Survival function Hazard function Population hazard of a survival mixture
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For a nonnegative random constant individual hazard Λ, write Mk​(t)=E[Λke−Λt]. The population survivor function is M0​ and its hazard is M1​/M0​. Under the integrability needed to differentiate, Mk′​=−Mk+1​, so
h′=M02​−M2​M0​+M12​​=−Var(Λ∣T>t).
(2)
The conditional distribution is weighted by e−Λt. This proves decreasing population hazard without any decrease in individual hazards and explains survival selection in a heterogeneous population.
