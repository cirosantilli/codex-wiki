Let R=Zp​[[T]], p odd, and ϕ(T)=(1+T)p−1. An element g∈R is the Frobenius-corrected logarithm of a unit f with f(0)∈1+pZp​ if and only if g′(0)=0. Necessity follows by differentiating at zero, using ϕ′(0)=p. For sufficiency put H=exp(pg)∈1+pR and solve fp=Hϕ(f). Choose f0​=exp(pg0​/(p−1)). The linear equation reduces to H1​=0, so f1​ is free. In degree n≥2 the unknown fn​ has coefficient pf0p−1​(1−pn−1). The remaining terms are divisible by p because reduction of any integral truncated f satisfies fp≡ϕ(f)(modp). Division gives a unique integral fn​. Two lifts differ by (1+T)c, c∈Zp​, as follows from the logarithmic equation in the coordinate z=log(1+T). If also Sg=0, the Coleman trace operator gives log(Nf/f)=pSg=0. As Nf/f∈1+pR, injectivity of the p-adic logarithm implies Nf=f.
