Exon

 Home Biology RNA splicing
OurBigBook.com Words: 56 Articles: 1
A segment retained in the mature RNA after RNA splicing, or the corresponding segment of a gene. An exon need not encode a protein: the ribosomal RNA exons in the Tetrahymena ribozyme experiment are an example.
