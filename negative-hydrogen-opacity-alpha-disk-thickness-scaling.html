Let R=kB​/(μm​mp​) for constant mean molecular weight μm​ in proton-mass units, and let σ be the Stefan–Boltzmann constant in the Stefan–Boltzmann law. The gas sound speed used here satisfies cs2​=RT, and Ω is the local orbital frequency. For the local opacity approximation κ=κ0​ρ1/3T10, a gas-supported optically thick alpha disk has ρ∼Σ/H and RT∼Ω2H2. Integrated viscous heating is F+​∼αΣΩ3H2. Radiative diffusion gives F−​∼(σ/κ0​)T−6Σ−4/3H1/3∼(σ/κ0​)R6Ω−12Σ−4/3H−35/3. Equating the fluxes proves the thickness scaling. At fixed composition and α, it gives H/r∝r53/82Σ−7/41 and cs​∝r6/41Σ−7/41 in Keplerian rotation. Radial powers therefore require a surface-density profile; they are not determined by opacity alone. The formula is local to the stated opacity regime and small disk aspect ratio.
