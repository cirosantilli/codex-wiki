Let β=1/(kB​T) and use the two spin values +1,−1 as row and column labels. Define the spin-chain transfer matrix
Wst​=e−βE0​(s,t),s,t∈{+1,−1}.
(21)
It is a real symmetric matrix because E0​(s,t)=E0​(t,s); every entry is strictly positive for finite bond energies at T>0. The Boltzmann factor of a spin configuration factorizes into the product Wσ1​σ2​​⋯WσL​σ1​​. Summing the intermediate spin labels is exactly matrix multiplication, and the final closure imposes periodic boundary conditions. Thus
ZL​=σ1​,…,σL​∑​j=1∏L​Wσj​σj+1​​=TrWL.​
(22)
Writing W=(ab​bd​), its eigenvalues are
λ±​=2a+d±(a−d)2+4b2​​,ZL​=λ+L​+λ−L​.​
(23)
A positive-entry matrix need not have two positive eigenvalues; λ−​ can be negative. The Perron–Frobenius theorem ensures λ+​>∣λ−​∣ here. Therefore the Helmholtz free energy is FL​=−kB​TlnZL​, and its value per spin in the thermodynamic limit is −kB​Tlnλ+​.
