OurBigBook.com Words: 47
For finite subsets Ai​ of a finite ambient set X, the indicator function of their complement is ∏i​(1−1Ai​​). Expanding this finite product and summing it against any weight f:X→R proves the weighted formula. The empty intersection is X. Negative weights are allowed; finiteness removes every convergence issue.
