Cataclysmic-variable period minimum (Pmin​)

For a Roche lobe-filling donor with local radius response R2​∝M2ζ​, the Roche-lobe-filling period-density relation gives P∝M2(3ζ−1)/2​. As mass is lost, the period falls for ζ>1/3 and rises for ζ<1/3, with a minimum at the transition. The reversal is called a period bounce. Slow evolution near the turning point produces an accumulation in the period distribution: for a steady flow of systems along one evolutionary branch, N(P)∝1/∣P˙∣.
