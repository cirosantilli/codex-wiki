Almost sure submartingale convergence theorem (supn​EYn+​<∞⟹Yn​→Y∞​ a.s.)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Doob upcrossing inequality Martingale convergence theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
A submartingale with supn​EYn+​<∞ converges almost surely to a finite integrable random variable. The positive-part bound also bounds negative parts because EYn​≥EY0​. The result specializes to an L1-bounded martingale, and hence to every nonnegative martingale. It does not by itself give convergence in L1; uniform integrability supplies that stronger conclusion.
