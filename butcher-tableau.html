A Butcher tableau records a Runge-Kutta method's stage matrix A, time nodes c, and output weights b in the block array
c​AbT​​.
(3)
Read row i above the line as the recipe for Runge-Kutta stage i: the left entry gives tn​+ci​h, and the right entries give the coefficients in Yi​=yn​+h∑j​aij​kj​. Below the line, bi​ weights slope ki​ in the final update. Zeros on and above the diagonal identify an Explicit Runge-Kutta method. For the internally consistent convention, row sums of A equal c; first-order consistency of a numerical method also requires ∑i​bi​=1. An embedded Runge-Kutta pair adds another weight row while retaining A,c.
