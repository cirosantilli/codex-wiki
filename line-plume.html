Line plume

 ... Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulent plume Top-hat plume model Pure plume
OurBigBook.com Words: 149 Articles: 3
A line plume is invariant along one horizontal direction, so its integral fluxes are measured per unit span. A wall line plume entrains through one exposed edge and has width proportional to distance from its plume virtual origin.
