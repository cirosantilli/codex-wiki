Finite-energy flow criterion for transience

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Flow network Flow Energy of a flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
An infinite locally finite connected graph is a transient graph exactly when it supports a unit flow from a vertex to infinity with finite energy of a flow.
