Diffie-Hellman key exchange

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Coding theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 1
Alice publishes ga and Bob publishes gb in a public cyclic group; each can then compute the shared secret gab. Its security relies on the difficulty of recovering the exponents or otherwise solving the computational Diffie--Hellman problem.
