Finite volume method

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 534 Articles: 9
A finite volume method evolves cell integrals or cell averages by differences of shared interface numerical fluxes. For a scalar conservation law, Ujn+1​=Ujn​−(k/d)(Fj+1/2​−Fj−1/2​). Sharing the same flux between neighboring cells makes conservation follow by telescoping.
