Polytropic equation of state

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Polytrope
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A polytropic equation of state relates pressure and density by p=KρΓ with constant K and exponent Γ. Writing Γ=1+1/n defines the polytropic index n.
