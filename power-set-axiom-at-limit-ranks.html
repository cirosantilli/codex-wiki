Power-set axiom at limit ranks ((Vδ​,∈)⊨PowerSet)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cumulative hierarchy
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
For a nonzero limit ordinal δ, the structure (Vδ​,∈) satisfies the Axiom of power set. For x∈Vβ​ with β<δ, transitivity gives x⊆Vβ​, so P(x)⊆Vβ+1​ and P(x)∈Vβ+2​⊆Vδ​. Every external subset is therefore present and the external power set is also the internal one.
