Helmholtz equation (Δu+k2u=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 472 Articles: 15
The Helmholtz equation is the constant-coefficient elliptic equation
Δu+k2u=0.
(775)
On a bounded domain, nonzero Dirichlet solutions occur when k2 is an eigenvalue of the negative Laplacian.
