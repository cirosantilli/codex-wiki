Central ideal

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Center of a Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
A central ideal is a linear subspace of the center of a Lie algebra. It is a Lie algebra ideal because its Lie bracket with every element is zero. Passing to the quotient Lie algebra removes this central subspace. For slp​ in characteristic p, the identity matrix has zero trace and spans a central ideal.
