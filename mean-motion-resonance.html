Mean-motion resonance

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Orbital resonance
OurBigBook.com Words: 965 Articles: 21
A mean-motion resonance has jn2​−kn1​≃0 for small integers j,k. Its slow resonant angle combines mean longitudes with apsidal or nodal longitudes.
