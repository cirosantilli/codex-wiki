Skew product (F(x,y)=(R(x),Sx​(y)))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system
OurBigBook.com Words: 250 Articles: 3
A skew product evolves a base coordinate by a dynamical system R and a fibre coordinate by a map Sx​ depending on the current base coordinate: F(x,y)=(R(x),Sx​(y)). The base evolution is independent of the fibre, while the fibre may depend on the base. Continuity or measurability is imposed according to the setting. An irrational skew shift is a useful affine example on a torus.
