Nearest neighbour density estimation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Nonparametric statistics Density estimation
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
In one dimension, a nearest neighbour density estimator is f​(k)​(x)=k/[2(n+1)ρ(k)​(x)]. It uses the nearest neighbour distance to select an interval containing a fixed number of observations. The factor n+1 corresponds to the exact expected value of the associated uniform order statistic.
