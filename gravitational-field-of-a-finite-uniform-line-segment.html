Gravitational field of a finite uniform line segment (gr​=−GM/[rr2+a2​])

OurBigBook.com Words: 79
Spread mass M uniformly along −a<z<a on an axis, with linear mass density λ=M/(2a). In its perpendicular bisector plane, axial field components cancel, and the radial component is
gr​=−Gλr∫−aa​(r2+z2)3/2dz​=−rr2+a2​GM​.
(11)
This is an exact line mass model and a thin-rod approximation for a physical cylinder. A centred circular orbit in this symmetry plane has v2=GM/R2+a2​. Thus v→GM/a​=2Gλ​ for R/a→0, and v∼GM/R​ for R/a→∞. For a finite-width rod, the first limit is useful only while its transverse size remains much smaller than R.
