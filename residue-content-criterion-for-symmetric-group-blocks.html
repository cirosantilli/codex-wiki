Residue-content criterion for symmetric-group blocks (cr​(λ)=#{(i,j)∈λ:j−i≡r(modp)})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Modular representation theory Block of a group algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 1
For shapes of the same size, equal residue-content multiplicities are equivalent to equal cores of a partition at modulus p. Removing a rim p-hook subtracts one of each residue. With the number of beta numbers divisible by p, the packed-runner charges are qr​=cr​−cr+1​, so the multiplicities recover the core. Symmetric polynomials in Young–Jucys–Murphy elements act by their evaluations on these residues, and generate the modular center. Equality of its characters is precisely the block-equivalence criterion for simple modules.
