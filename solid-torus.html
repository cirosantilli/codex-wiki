Solid torus (S1×D2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological surface Torus
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 2
A solid torus is the product of a circle with a closed disk. Its boundary is the torus S1×S1. It deformation retracts onto its central circle, so its degree-one de Rham cohomology is one dimensional and injects into that of its boundary.
