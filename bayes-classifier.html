Bayes classifier

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Statistical decision theory

Under zero-one loss, a Bayes classifier assigns an observation to a class of greatest posterior probability. For two classes with prior probabilities π0​,π1​ and densities f0​,f1​, it chooses class one exactly when π1​f1​(x)≥π0​f0​(x).
