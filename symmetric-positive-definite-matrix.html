Symmetric positive-definite matrix (A=AT,xTAx>0 (x=0))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Symmetric matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A real symmetric matrix is positive definite when its quadratic form is positive on every nonzero vector. By the spectral theorem, this is equivalent to all its eigenvalues being positive. It has a unique symmetric positive-definite square root.
