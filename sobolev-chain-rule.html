Sobolev chain rule (D(Φ(u))=Φ′(u)Du)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Distributional derivative Weak derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 93 Articles: 1
If u∈Wloc1,2​ and Φ is continuously differentiable with bounded derivative on the range in use, then D(Φ(u))=Φ′(u)Du almost everywhere. One proves this first for smooth functions and then uses density of smooth functions in a Sobolev space and boundedness of Φ′. On compact sets one may truncate Φ away from the range of a bounded u.
