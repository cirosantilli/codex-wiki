Adaptedness of a stopped right-continuous process (XtT​=Xt∧T​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Adapted process
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For an adapted process with right-continuous paths and a stopping time T, XtT​=Xt∧T​ is adapted. At fixed t, round t∧T upward on finite partitions of [0,t]. Every approximating value is Ft​-measurable, and right continuity gives the limit. Completeness handles a null exceptional set if right continuity holds only almost surely. A martingale assumption and boundedness of T are unnecessary.
