Column orthogonality (∑χ∈Irr(G)​χ(g)χ(h)​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Character orthogonality
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For a finite group, the sum of products of irreducible character values is zero when g,h are not conjugate, and equals ∣CG​(g)∣ when g,h are conjugate. To prove this from row orthogonality relations for a character table, choose representatives gj​ of its conjugacy classes and form Uij​=∣gjG​∣/∣G∣​χi​(gj​). Row orthogonality says UU∗=I. There are equally many irreducible characters and conjugacy classes, so this is a square unitary matrix and U∗U=I as well. Its column equations give the result, using ∣G∣/∣gjG​∣=∣CG​(gj​)∣.
