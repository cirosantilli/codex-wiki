Straightening of a symmetric-group character indexed by a composition

 ... Partition of an integer Young tableau Tabloid Polytabloid Specht module Murnaghan–Nakayama rule
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For the alternating expression ψλ indexed by an integer composition, swapping two adjacent entries of λ−id negates ψλ. If two entries coincide the expression vanishes; otherwise sorting produces, up to sign, the irreducible character indexed by the resulting partition.
