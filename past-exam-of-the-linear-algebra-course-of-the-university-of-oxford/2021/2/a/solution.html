In the monomial basis B=(1,x,x2,x3) of the complex vector space V=C[x]≤3​, the binomial theorem gives
[T]B​=​1000​1100​1210​1331​​.
(16)
The characteristic polynomial of this upper triangular matrix is the product of t minus its diagonal entries, so χT​(t)=(t−1)4.
For the minimal polynomial, set Δ=T−I, the forward difference with step one. For a nonconstant polynomial of polynomial degree d, the binomial theorem shows that f(x+1)−f(x) has polynomial degree d−1. Constant polynomials have zero forward difference. Consequently Δ4=0 on V. To prove that the nilpotency index is exactly four, compute
Δx3=3x2+3x+1,Δ2x3=6x+6,Δ3x3=6=0.
(17)
It follows that
χT​(t)=(t−1)4,mT​(t)=(t−1)4.​
(18)
If T were diagonalizable, its only eigenvalue would be 1, and its diagonal matrix in an eigenbasis would be I. This would make T=I, whereas T(x)=x+1=x. Thus T is not diagonalizable. Equivalently, its minimal polynomial has a repeated root.
