Solutions to all three questions of the University of Oxford Part A linear algebra paper, sat on 20 June 2023. Source: released examination PDF, parent PDF pages 258–260; paper pages 1–3. Question labels follow the paper; the solutions supply their own reasoning. Part 2(c) includes the exceptional case of characteristic of a field three because that part does not specify its field separately.
