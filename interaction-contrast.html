Interaction contrast ((μ1a​−μ1b​)−(μ0a​−μ0b​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
An interaction contrast compares a treatment difference across levels of another factor. A nonzero difference of differences shows that an additive representation of those cell means is inadequate. Its standard error must use the error stratum in which that interaction term is randomized.
