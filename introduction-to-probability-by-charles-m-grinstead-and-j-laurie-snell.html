The linked CHANCE Project version is dated 4 July 2006. Its cover identifies the underlying second-edition book as copyright 2003 by the two authors, and this version as copyright 2006 by Peter G. Doyle. The version credit does not identify Doyle as an original-book coauthor or formal editor. These dates describe copyrights and a version, rather than a claim about first publication.
Cited passages: §3.1, Example 3.3 and Table 3.1, printed pp. 77–78: uniform-calendar assumptions, distinct-label product and threshold.
