Weighted graph

A weighted graph assigns a symmetric nonnegative conductance μxy​ to each edge. With killing weights κx​, the associated discrete-time walk moves from x to y with probability μxy​/μx​ and is killed with probability κx​/μx​, where μx​=κx​+∑y​μxy​.
