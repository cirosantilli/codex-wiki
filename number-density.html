Number density (n)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Phase-space distribution function
OurBigBook.com Words: 126 Articles: 3
The number density is the number of particles per unit volume. For a homogeneous species with g internal states and phase-space distribution f(p) in natural units,
n=g∫(2π)3d3p​f(p).
