Steady dust-tail continuity equation (n(s)s˙=N˙P(t(s)))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Interplanetary dust Dust tail
OurBigBook.com Words: 83 Articles: 1
For a single-stream dust tail with injection rate N˙, monotone angular distance s(t), and survival probability P(t), the number per unit angle is n(s)=N˙P(t(s))/s˙. A vanishing angular drift creates an idealized density enhancement; velocity dispersion rounds it.
