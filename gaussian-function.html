Gaussian function

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Exponential function
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 2
A Gaussian function has the form Aexp[−a(x−b)2] with a>0. Its integral and moments reduce by translation and scaling to the Gaussian integral.
