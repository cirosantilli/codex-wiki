Take the indicator function f=1(0,1)​. Direct integration gives
Hf(x)={1,1/x,​0<x≤1,x>1.​
(3)
Although the function vanishes beyond one, its average remembers the unit area accumulated before one. Squaring and integrating both portions gives
∥f∥22​=1,∥Hf∥22​=∫01​1dx+∫1∞​x−2dx=1+1=2≤4∥f∥22​=4.

Thus the tail accounts for half of the squared L2 norm. Averaging does not always decrease the L2 norm with this Lebesgue measure on the half-line. The same indicator function also explains the restriction p>1: ∫1∞​x−1dx=∞, so its average is not in L1 space.
