Optical fiber acceptance angle (θmax​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Optical fiber Step-index optical fiber
OurBigBook.com Words: 260
The optical fiber acceptance angle is the largest launch half-angle admitted by the specified guide model. For a flat entrance face perpendicular to the optical axis, external refractive index n0​, and a straight step-index optical fiber, Snell's law at the entrance gives
n0​sinθ=n1​sinϕ.
(32)
At the core–cladding interface, the critical angle for total internal reflection ic​ satisfies
sinic​=n1​n2​​,i=2π​−ϕ.
(33)
For a meridional ray, strict total internal reflection requires i>ic​. At the limiting geometry i=ic​, the refracted light ray is tangent to the interface, so
n0​sinθmax​=n1​cosic​=n12​−n22​​≡NA.
(34)
This identifies the numerical aperture with an entrance cone under these assumptions. With n0​=1 for air, the conventional inclusive boundary condition is
sinθ≤n12​−n22​​,
(35)
for a nonnegative launch angle θ; a practical launch stays inside the boundary. If NA>n0​, this ideal model imposes no further restriction within the forward hemisphere, and arcsin(NA/n0​) must not be used. The light ray must also intersect the optical fiber core: a suitable angle alone does not correct a misplaced beam.
For an original example, take n1​=1.50, n2​=1.48 and air at the entrance. Then
NA=2.25−2.1904​=0.0596​≃0.244131,θmax​=arcsin(0.244131)≃14.13∘.
(36)
The internal limiting angle is only ϕmax​≃9.37∘, while the critical angle for total internal reflection is ic​≃80.63∘. A 10∘ launch has ϕ≃6.65∘ and i≃83.35∘, so it satisfies total internal reflection. A 20∘ launch has ϕ≃13.18∘ and i≃76.82∘, so it fails this test.
This cone is a geometrical optics result for meridional rays. A single-mode optical fiber requires the spatial overlap of the incident electromagnetic wave with its guided normal mode; its numerical aperture does not impose an exact angular cutoff on arbitrary beams. Diffraction, finite cladding, and bends require a physical optics description.[ref]
