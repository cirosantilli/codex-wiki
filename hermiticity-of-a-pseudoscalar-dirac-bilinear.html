Hermiticity of a pseudoscalar Dirac bilinear ((ψˉ​γ5ψ)†=−ψˉ​γ5ψ)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Yukawa interaction Pseudoscalar Yukawa interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
With the usual Dirac adjoint, Hermitian γ5 and {γ0,γ5}=0, taking the adjoint gives ψ†γ5γ0ψ=−ψ†γ0γ5ψ. Consequently a real pseudoscalar with coupling −gϕψˉ​γ5ψ has a Hermitian interaction only if g∗=−g. Writing g=igP​ with real gP​ gives the familiar interaction −igP​ϕψˉ​γ5ψ. Formal Euler-Lagrange field equations obtained with independent spinor and adjoint variables can still be computed for the original symbolic coupling; it must not silently be identified with a real physical coupling.
