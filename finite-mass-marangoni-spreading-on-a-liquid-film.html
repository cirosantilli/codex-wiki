A localized amount M=∫Cdx of insoluble surfactant spreads over a planar film of depth scale h0​. Neglect gravity, capillarity and diffusion. Then C∼M/ℓ and Marangoni stress drives us​∼AMh0​/(μℓ2), giving ℓ∼(AMh0​t/μ)1/3. The thin-film equations with insoluble surfactant admit a similarity solution in which the film height and concentration depend on x/ℓ, conserving total surfactant and fluid volume relative to the undisturbed layer.
