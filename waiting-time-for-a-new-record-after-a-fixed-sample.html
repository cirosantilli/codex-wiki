Waiting time for a new record after a fixed sample (P(τ=k)=(n+k−1)(n+k)n​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Order statistic
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
For independent identically distributed observations with continuous cumulative distribution function F, fix the maximum Yn​ of the first n≥1 observations and let τ be the first subsequent observation exceeding it. The probability integral transform gives U=F(Yn​) density nun−1 on (0,1). Conditional on U=u, τ has a geometric distribution of parameter 1−u. Therefore
P(τ=k)=n∫01​un+k−2(1−u)du=(n+k−1)(n+k)n​,P(τ>k)=n+kn​.
(44)
The masses sum to one, so the waiting time is finite almost surely, but the tail-sum formula for expectation gives Eτ=∞. These conclusions require only continuity of F, not a density or a strictly increasing distribution function.
