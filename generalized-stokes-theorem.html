Generalized Stokes theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form Exterior derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
If X is an oriented n-manifold with boundary, F:∂X↪X is the inclusion, and ω is a compactly supported (n−1)-form, then
∫X​dω=∫∂X​F∗ω,
(516)
where the boundary has the outward-normal-first boundary orientation. A subordinate partition of unity reduces the theorem to the fundamental theorem of calculus in oriented coordinate half-spaces; local finiteness and compact support ensure that only finitely many terms contribute.
