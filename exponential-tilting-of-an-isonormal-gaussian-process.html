Exponential tilting of an isonormal Gaussian process (dQ/dP=eX(k)−∥k∥2/2)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process Isonormal Gaussian process
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For an isonormal Gaussian process X on a real Hilbert space H and k∈H, the positive density exp(X(k)−∥k∥2/2) has expectation one and defines an equivalent probability measure. Under this measure, X(h) has mean ⟨h,k⟩ and the same covariance as before. The shifted family X(h)−⟨h,k⟩ is again an isonormal Gaussian process. This follows by evaluating the joint Gaussian exponential formula for any finite collection of arguments. It is the Hilbert-space version of exponential tilting.
