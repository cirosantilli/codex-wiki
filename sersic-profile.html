Sérsic profile (I(R)=Ie​e−bn​[(R/Re​)1/n−1])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Observational astronomy Surface brightness
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
A Sérsic profile models projected galactic light, with index n controlling curvature and bn​ chosen so that Re​ encloses half the luminosity. Its logarithmic slope is dlnI/dlnR=−(bn​/n)(R/Re​)1/n. The n=1 case is exponential; n=4 gives the traditional de Vaucouleurs form. Unlike a fixed power law, its slope steepens outward.
