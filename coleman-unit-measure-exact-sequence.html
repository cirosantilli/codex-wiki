Let V∞​ be the norm limit of local principal units in the p-power cyclotomic field tower, for odd p. The Coleman power series fu​ gives the Frobenius-corrected logarithm p−1log(fup​/ϕfu​), whose inverse Amice transform is supported on Zp×​. This is the middle map in the displayed exact sequence; the last map is the first moment μ↦∫xdμ. Its kernel consists of norm-compatible p-power roots of unity. The logarithmic difference equation has one resonant degree, namely degree one in log(1+T), accounting for the last term. Complex conjugation acts by −1 on both copies of Zp​(1). Taking its even part therefore identifies the local principal-unit limit in the real tower with Zp​[[Zp×​/{±1}]].
