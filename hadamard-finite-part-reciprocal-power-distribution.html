Hadamard finite-part reciprocal-power distribution (Λm​=Fp(x−m))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Distribution (mathematical analysis) Hadamard finite-part integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
For m≥2, the symmetric Hadamard finite-part integral defining Λm​ subtracts the Taylor polynomial through degree m−2 from a test function before division by xm. It has finite order of a distribution and satisfies
Λm​=(m−1)!(−1)m−1​(dxd​)mlog∣x∣.
(1)
The lower-order subtraction terms are integrable at infinity, while the remaining odd 1/x singularity cancels under symmetric truncation.
