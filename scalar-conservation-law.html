Scalar conservation law (ut​+∂x​f(u)=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 29
A scalar conservation law in one space dimension has the form ut​+∂x​f(u)=0. While a classical solution exists, it obeys the quasilinear transport equation ut​+f′(u)ux​=0.
