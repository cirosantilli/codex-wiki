Quantum relative entropy (D(ρ∥σ))

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum theory Density operator Von Neumann entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 151 Articles: 7
The quantum relative entropy is D(ρ∥σ)=Tr[ρ(logρ−logσ)] when the support of ρ lies in that of σ.
