Linear dilaton conformal field theory

 ... Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Worldsheet Two-dimensional conformal field theory Free boson conformal field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
A linear dilaton conformal field theory improves the free-boson stress tensor by a second derivative, T=−α′−1:∂X∂X:−q∂2X. The improvement shifts the central charge to c=1+6α′q2 and corresponds to a dilaton linear in the target coordinate X.
