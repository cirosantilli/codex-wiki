Hankel function (Hν(1)​,Hν(2)​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Bessel function
OurBigBook.com Words: 60 Articles: 1
The two Hankel functions Hν(1)​=Jν​+iYν​ and Hν(2)​=Jν​−iYν​ solve the Bessel differential equation. Their large-argument forms distinguish outgoing and incoming oscillations and have sector-dependent asymptotic expansions.
