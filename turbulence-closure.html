Turbulence closure

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 206 Articles: 3
A turbulence closure approximates the unclosed higher-order moments in evolution equations for lower-order turbulent statistics. For example, an equation for the velocity correlation tensor involves triple correlations, whose evolution involves fourth-order correlations and pressure. A closure must be checked against conservation laws, realizability and boundary conditions; matching an energy spectrum alone does not establish all these properties.
