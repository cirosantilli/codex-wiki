Coordinatewise median

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Median Sample median
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
The coordinatewise median of vectors applies the univariate sample median separately to each coordinate. It is easy to compute but depends on the chosen coordinate axes.
