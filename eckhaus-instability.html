Eckhaus instability

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory Amplitude equation Real Ginzburg–Landau equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 207 Articles: 2
For r,ξ,g>0, longitudinal phase modulation of a detuned convection roll yields diffusion coefficient Dϕ​=ξ(r−3ξQ2)/(r−ξQ2). The expression follows by eliminating the fast amplitude correction from the coupled linear amplitude-phase equations at small modulation wavenumber. Thus convection rolls become unstable for Q2>r/(3ξ) although they exist for Q2<r/ξ. At the boundary the leading phase diffusion vanishes and higher spatial orders matter.
