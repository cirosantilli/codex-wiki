Gauge-theory Bianchi identity (D[μ​Fνρ]​=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge field strength
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
The Jacobi identity for gauge covariant derivatives gives D[μ​Fνρ]​=0. In four dimensions this is Dμ​Fμν=0, with Fμν=21​εμνρσFρσ​. It holds independently of the Yang-Mills equations.
