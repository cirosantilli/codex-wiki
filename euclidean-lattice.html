Euclidean lattice

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 718 Articles: 12
A Euclidean lattice is a discrete subgroup Λ⊆Rn spanning the ambient vector space. Its covolume m(Λ) is the volume of a fundamental parallelepiped.
