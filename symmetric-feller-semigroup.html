Symmetric Feller semigroup (⟨Pt​f,g⟩L2(μ)​=⟨f,Pt​g⟩L2(μ)​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis C0-semigroup Contraction semigroup Feller semigroup
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 2
A Feller semigroup symmetric in the space associated with an invariant measure has a self-adjoint contraction realization in L2(μ). Its two-point kernel measure μ(dx)Pt​(x,dy) is symmetric. This reversibility expresses its joint energy as an integral of products of increments.
