Cognitive behavioral therapy

 Home Science Psychology Clinical psychology
OurBigBook.com Words: 811 Articles: 20
A family of psychological treatments using explicit formulations and structured cognitive and behavioral procedures. Treatment tests and modifies relevant learning, interpretations and behavior; its content depends on the problem and the evidence rather than on one universal technique.
