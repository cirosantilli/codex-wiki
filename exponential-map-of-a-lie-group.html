Exponential map of a Lie group (exp:g→G)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 254 Articles: 4
For a matrix Lie group, the exponential map is the matrix exponential expX=∑n≥0​Xn/n! and sends one-parameter additive subgroups of the Lie algebra to one-parameter subgroups of the group.
