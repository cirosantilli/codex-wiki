Vertically averaged alpha closure for a mixed-pressure layer (νˉ=αΩH2/9)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Accretion disk Alpha disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 91 Articles: 1
For the constant gas-radiation ratio vertical disc structure, the integrated alpha prescription νˉΣ=∫μdz=(α/Ω)∫pdz gives the displayed mean kinematic viscosity and λ/(1+λ)=αHΩκΣ/(8c). Uniform dynamic viscosity and nonuniform pressure are compatible because this prescription is an integrated equality. Imposing the corresponding pointwise equality would not preserve the exact vertical solution.
