Solar dynamo (∂t​B=∇×(U×B+E−η∇×B))

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Star Sun Solar magnetic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 589 Articles: 7
The solar dynamo is the induction and regeneration of the solar magnetic field by conducting flows powered ultimately by solar convection and rotation. In a mean-field dynamo, differential rotation supplies the Omega effect and helical correlations or the Babcock-Leighton mechanism regenerate a poloidal magnetic field. The mean induction equation is
∂t​B=∇×(U×B+E−η∇×B),
(1)
where E=⟨u×b⟩ is the mean-field electromotive force. A model must close this correlation and include feedback to predict a saturated magnetic cycle; prescribing a shear alone does not close the regeneration loop.
