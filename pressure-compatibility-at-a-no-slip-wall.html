Pressure compatibility at a no-slip wall

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow No-slip boundary condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
For a smooth incompressible Navier-Stokes equation trajectory with zero velocity at a fixed flat wall, the normal momentum equation determines the normal pressure gradient. In a perturbation model with buoyancy term RiB​θy^​, it gives ∂n​p=νn⋅Δu−RiB​θn⋅y^​. An extra homogeneous pressure Neumann boundary condition is valid only when the right side vanishes; it does not follow from the no-slip boundary condition.
