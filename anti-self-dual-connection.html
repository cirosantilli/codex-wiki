Anti-self-dual connection (FA+​=0)

A unitary connection A on an oriented Riemannian four-manifold is anti-self-dual when FA+​=(FA​+∗FA​)/2=0. Its curvature is covariantly closed by the Bianchi identity and solves the Yang-Mills equations. With anti-Hermitian matrices and the fundamental trace, k=c2​(E)[X]=(8π2)−1∫X​Tr(FA​∧FA​)=∥FA​∥22​/(8π2) for an SU(2) bundle. Thus its charge is nonnegative.
