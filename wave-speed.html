Wave speed (c)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 527 Articles: 5
For the homogeneous one-dimensional wave equation utt​=c2uxx​ with constant c>0, wave speed means the speed at which a travelling wave profile translates. In F(x−ct), a feature initially at x=s follows x=s+ct, giving signed velocity +c; in G(x+ct) it follows x=s−ct, giving signed velocity −c. The characteristic lines for the one-dimensional wave equation therefore have slopes dx/dt=±c. The speed of either profile is c, even though the displacement at a fixed point can change sign or pass through zero.[ref]
The same c bounds the causal cone for the constant-speed wave equation. For a wave equation on a string, c=T/μ​ depends on tension and linear mass density; for small acoustic waves in stationary uniform air it is the speed of sound. This interpretation assumes the stated ideal wave equation. In media with wave dispersion, phase velocity, group velocity and the earliest signal arrival have different meanings: a measured wave crest speed need not be the speed of a wave envelope, and neither alone establishes a causal cone for the constant-speed wave equation.
