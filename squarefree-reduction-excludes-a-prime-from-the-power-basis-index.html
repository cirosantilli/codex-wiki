Squarefree reduction excludes a prime from the power-basis index (f​ squarefree⟹p∤[OK​:Z[α]])

For an algebraic integer α generating a number field, the polynomial discriminant of its minimal polynomial f equals the discriminant of elements of a number field for its power basis. A squarefree reduction modulo p has nonzero resultant with its derivative, so p∤disc(f). The discriminant-index formula for an integral lattice, disc(f)=[OK​:Z[α]]2dK​, proves the assertion.
