Interface conditions for electromagnetic fields

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Plane electromagnetic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 22
Across an interface with unit normal n from medium one to medium two, Maxwell equations imply n×(E2​−E1​)=0, n⋅(B2​−B1​)=0, n⋅(D2​−D1​)=σf​, and n×(H2​−H1​)=Kf​. Here σf​ and Kf​ are the free surface electric charge and electric current densities.
