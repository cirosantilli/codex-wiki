3-j symbol ((j1​m1​​j2​m2​​j3​m3​​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of SU(2) Clebsch-Gordan coefficients
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A symmetrized form of the Clebsch-Gordan coefficients used for coupling three angular momenta. The relation is ⟨j1​m1​,j2​m2​∣j3​m3​⟩=(−1)j1​−j2​+m3​2j3​+1​(j1​m1​​j2​m2​​j3​−m3​​). The symbol vanishes unless the magnetic indices sum to zero and the angular momenta obey the triangle and integrality conditions. Products of two such symbols express the Gaunt integral; the zero-magnetic-index symbol enforces even total multipole for integer spherical harmonics.
