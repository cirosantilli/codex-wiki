Slit-limited resolving power of a grating (R=mλfcoll​/(xdcosα))

 ... Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Diffraction grating Grating equation Anamorphic magnification of a grating
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
A slit of width x at the focus of a collimator subtends x/fcoll​. Its projected width is xfcam​cosα/(fcoll​cosβ). Dividing this width by the grating dispersion gives Δλ=xdcosα/(mfcoll​). Thus the spectral resolving power is R=mλfcoll​/(xdcosα). Omitting the anamorphic factor gives a denominator cosβ instead; these agree in the Littrow configuration.
