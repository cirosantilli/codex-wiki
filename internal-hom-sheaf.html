Internal Hom sheaf (HomOX​​(F,G))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For sheaves of modules F,G on a ringed space, the internal Hom sheaf assigns to an open U the module of sheaf morphisms F∣U​→G∣U​ respecting OU​. Compatible morphisms glue, so this is a sheaf. Its global sections are precisely the global module-sheaf morphisms. For a locally free sheaf F of finite rank it is F∨⊗G.
