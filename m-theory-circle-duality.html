M-theory circle duality (R11​=gs​ℓs​,ℓp3​=gs​ℓs3​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory M-theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
Compactifying M-theory on a circle gives type IIA superstring theory. The circle radius sets its coupling. Multiplying M2-brane and M5-brane tensions by the circle circumference gives the fundamental string and D4-brane tensions, while unwrapped objects give D2-branes and NS5-branes. Momentum and a Kaluza-Klein monopole supply D0-brane and D6-brane charges. The circle dictionary and worldvolume reductions are developed in Four Lectures on M-theory.
