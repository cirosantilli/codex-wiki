Two hinged rods sliding on a floor (θ˙2=2l3g​1+3sin2θsinα−sinθ​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Rigid body dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
Two equal uniform rods of length 2l and mass M, one pivoted to the floor and the other endpoint sliding on it, have arch coordinates B=(2lcosθ,2lsinθ) and C=(4lcosθ,0). The kinetic energy decomposition about the center of mass gives T=34​Ml2(1+3sin2θ)θ˙2, while gravitational potential energy is 2Mglsinθ. Conservation of energy from rest at angle α gives the displayed equation and hinge impact speed 6glsinα​, provided contact is maintained. The endpoint's normal reaction becomes negative during this constrained fall if sinα>35/54; a unilateral floor then permits detachment and the constrained formula no longer describes the entire motion.
