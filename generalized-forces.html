Generalized forces (δW=∑i​Qi​δqi​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Virtual work
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
Generalized forces are the coefficients of virtual work in a set of generalized coordinates. A translational coordinate has a force as its coefficient, while an angular coordinate has a torque. A generalized force conjugate to qi​ has units of energy divided by the units of qi​. Forces not already included in the Lagrangian enter the Euler-Lagrange equations as d(∂L/∂q˙​i​)/dt−∂L/∂qi​=Qi​.
