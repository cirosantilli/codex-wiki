Connection decay rate in a percolation strip (fk​(p))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Percolation theory Bond percolation
OurBigBook.com Words: 178 Articles: 2
For nearest-neighbour bond percolation with 0<p<1 on the strip Tk​=Z×{−k,…,k}, put qk​(n)=Pp​((0,0)↔(n,0) in Tk​). The Harris-FKG inequality and translation invariance imply qk​(n+m)≥qk​(n)qk​(m). Thus −logqk​(n) is a subadditive sequence, and the Fekete lemma gives
fk​(p)=infn≥1​−n1​logqk​(n),qk​(n)≤e−nfk​(p).
(1)
The direct horizontal graph path gives 0≤fk​(p)≤−logp. Enlarging the strip increases every percolation two-point connection probability, so fk​(p) is nonincreasing in k and has a nonnegative limit of a sequence.
