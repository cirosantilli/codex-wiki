Zonotope

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex set Convex polytope
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A zonotope is a Minkowski sum of finitely many line segments, equivalently an affine map image of a cube. For vectors ξj​, the set ∑j​[0,1]ξj​ is the support of the associated box spline. A centred version uses [−1/2,1/2]ξj​ instead. In two dimensions, sums of differently directed segments give centrally symmetric polygons; repeated directions lengthen their corresponding edges without adding new edge directions.
