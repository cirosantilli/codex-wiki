Hurewicz theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Homotopy group
OurBigBook.com Words: 216 Articles: 4
If a path-connected space is (n−1)-connected for n≥2, then its first potentially nonzero homotopy group maps isomorphically to homology:
πn​(X)∼​Hn​(X;Z).
