Likelihood-ratio criterion for minimal sufficiency

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Sufficient statistic Minimal sufficient statistic
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
For a dominated family with positive densities pθ​, a statistic T is minimal sufficient if
T(x)=T(y)⟺pθ​(y)pθ​(x)​ is independent of θ.
