Extensional equations for a slender Newtonian column ((a2)t​+(a2w)z​=0)

 ... Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity Extensional viscosity Uniaxial extensional flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 309 Articles: 4
A slender axisymmetric column of a Newtonian fluid, with radius a(z,t), axial velocity w, external pressure pe​ and axial body-force density f, obeys (a2)t​+(a2w)z​=0 and 3μ(a2wz​)z​/a2=pe,z​−f when inertia, surface tension and leading external shear are negligible. The factor three is the Trouton ratio. Radial mass conservation gives ur​=−rwz​/2; the Newtonian fluid stress tensor gives p=pe​−μwz​ and axial excess stress 3μwz​. This reduces a three-dimensional Stokes flow to one-dimensional evolving geometry.
