Quantum effective potential (Γ[φ=constant]=∫ddxVeff​(φ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Effective action
OurBigBook.com Words: 193 Articles: 2
The quantum effective potential is the derivative-free part of the quantum effective action evaluated on a spatially constant background. The displayed sign uses Γ=−W+∫Jφ for Z=eiW and the source term +Jϕ; the opposite effective-action convention gives −∫Veff​. Its derivatives give zero-momentum one-particle-irreducible vertices. The value depends on a renormalization scheme and scale; an arbitrary additive vacuum-energy constant may be fixed separately.
