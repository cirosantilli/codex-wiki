Heating curve

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Heat capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A heating curve records temperature against accumulated supplied heat under a specified constraint. Within one thermodynamic phase, its slope is dT/dQ=1/CX​, where CX​ is the constrained heat capacity. A latent heat plateau instead records changing phase fraction at the phase coexistence temperature. The sensible heat example combines two sloping intervals and a melting plateau in one heating budget.
