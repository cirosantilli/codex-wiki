Equal-range search ([lower_bound(x),upper_bound(x)))

 Home Computer science Algorithm Binary search
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
Find both the lower-bound search boundary i and the upper-bound search boundary j of a key x in a sorted array. Every entry in [i,j) is at least and at most x, so all are equal to x. Values immediately outside this interval, when present, are strictly smaller or strictly larger; the interval is consequently maximal. An absent key gives i=j at its insertion position. Two binary searches give total time complexity O(log(N+1)) under constant-cost comparison and indexed access.
