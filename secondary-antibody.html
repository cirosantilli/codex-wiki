Secondary antibody

 Home Biology Protein Antibody
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A secondary antibody recognizes another antibody, rather than the proposed target protein directly. In Western blot antibody detection, its attached label makes retained primary antibodies visible. A secondary antibody must recognize the kind of primary antibody used.
