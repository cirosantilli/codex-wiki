Stark effect of the hydrogen ground state

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Hydrogen atom Hydrogen energy eigenbasis Electric-dipole selection rules for hydrogen
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
The even, spherically symmetric hydrogen ground state has no permanent electric dipole, so a uniform electric field gives no first-order energy shift. Odd-parity excited states mix in at second order, yielding a negative shift quadratic in the field.
