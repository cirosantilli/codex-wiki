Discretely valued field

 ... Algebra Commutative algebra Local ring Valuation ring Discrete valuation ring Discrete valuation
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
A discretely valued field is a field equipped with a discrete valuation. It is complete when every Cauchy sequence for the induced metric converges.
