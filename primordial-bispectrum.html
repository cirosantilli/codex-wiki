Statistical homogeneity defines the primordial bispectrum by
⟨ϕk1​​ϕk2​​ϕk3​​⟩=(2π)3δ(3)(k1​+k2​+k3​)B(k1​,k2​,k3​).

A bispectrum is a Fourier-space third-order statistic; primordial perturbations supply the cosmological field in this instance.
