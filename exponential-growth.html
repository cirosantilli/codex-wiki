Exponential growth (y(t)=y0​ert,r>0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Exponential function
OurBigBook.com Words: 74 Articles: 1
A positive quantity undergoes exponential growth at constant rate r>0 when y′=ry. For y0​>0, the solution of a differential equation is y(t)=y0​ert. Every interval of duration Δt multiplies the value by the same factor erΔt, independently of its starting value.
