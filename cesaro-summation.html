Cesàro summation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Series (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A series is Cesàro summable to L when the Cesaro means of its partial sums converge to L. This is a summation method for series, distinct from a mean of the original terms. The Cesaro theorem for convergent sequences ensures that every ordinarily convergent series has the same Cesàro sum. The alternating series 1−1+1−⋯ has Cesàro sum 1/2 without an ordinary sum.
