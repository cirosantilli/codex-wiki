Tree on a Polish space

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Topological analysis Metric space Complete metric space Polish space
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
A tree on P is a subset of ⋃n≥0​Pn closed under initial segments. It is closed when each finite level is closed in the corresponding product topology, and well-founded when it has no infinite branch. A countable-basis approximation tree, with nested sets of shrinking diameter, reduces a closed well-founded tree on a Polish space to a countable well-founded tree and bounds its height by a countable ordinal.
