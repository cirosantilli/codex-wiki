Stereographic projection (σN​(X,Y,Z)=(X+iY)/(1−Z))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Riemann sphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 926 Articles: 7
Stereographic projection identifies a sphere minus one pole with the plane and supplies the standard charts of the Riemann sphere.
In the equatorial-plane convention of the Riemann sphere, draw the straight line through N=(0,0,1) and a point P=(X,Y,Z)=N on the unit sphere. Its intersection with the plane Z=0 has complex coordinate
z=σN​(P)=1−ZX+iY​.
(23)
The inverse function, for z=x+iy and r2=x2+y2, is
P(z)=(1+r22x​,1+r22y​,1+r2r2−1​).
(24)
Here positive x gives positive X, and positive y gives positive Y. Thus 1 and i lie on the positive X and Y ends of the equator. The omitted north pole is assigned z=∞.[ref]
For original worked points, the real numbers 1/2 and 2 lift to points with the same X coordinate but opposite Z coordinates:
P(1/2)=(4/5,0,−3/5),P(2)=(4/5,0,3/5).
(25)
Projecting back gives (4/5)/(1+3/5)=1/2 and (4/5)/(1−3/5)=2. A point off the real axis checks the other sign:
P(2+i)=(2/3,1/3,2/3),σN​(2/3,1/3,2/3)=2+i.
(26)
The coordinates satisfy 4/9+1/9+4/9=1, as a point on the unit sphere must. Also P(0)=S, P(−1)=(−1,0,0) and P(i)=(0,1,0).
A short derivation starts with the line N+t(P−N)=(tX,tY,1+t(Z−1)). Setting its last coordinate to zero gives t=1/(1−Z), hence the projection formula. Conversely the line from N to (x,y,0) is (tx,ty,1−t). Substitution in the unit sphere equation gives
t2r2+(1−t)2=1,t(t(1+r2)−2)=0.
(27)
The root t=0 is N; the other root t=2/(1+r2) gives P(z). These line intersections establish both the formula and its bijection property without guessing the inverse.
The same algebra explains how large planar distances become small spherical ones:
1−Z=1+∣z∣22​,∥P(z)−N∥=1+∣z∣2​2​.
(28)
Consequently ∣z∣→∞ brings P(z) to N independently of direction. The last expression is twice the chordal metric distance χ(z,∞). A whole unbounded exterior in the complex plane therefore becomes a small cap on the unit sphere.
