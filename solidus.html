Solidus (TS​(C))

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Gibbs free energy Phase coexistence curve Phase diagram
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
The solidus bounds the entirely solid equilibrium region of a phase diagram. For a linear liquidus TL​(Cl​)=−mCl​ and a constant segregation coefficient Cs​=kD​Cl​, the corresponding solidus is TS​(Cs​)=−mCs​/kD​. The concentration argument is the solid concentration, not the neighboring liquid concentration.
