Chirp requirement for longitudinal bunch compression (σzf2​=σz2​+2R56​Cov(z,δ)+R562​σδ2​)

 Home Physics Experimental apparatus Particle accelerator Beam optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
A magnetic chicane's momentum-dependent path length does not specify a compression factor from initial length and marginal energy spread alone. A suitable longitudinal position-energy correlation is required. With zero correlation the variance above is minimized at R56​=0, so such a chicane cannot shorten that ideal uncorrelated bunch. An imposed chirp and its uncorrelated residual determine the achievable minimum.
