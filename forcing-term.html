Forcing term (Ly=f)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Linear differential equation Inhomogeneous linear differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
For a linear differential equation written Ly=f, the prescribed right-hand side f is the forcing term. It may represent an external input or source, and can be constant or time dependent. Setting f=0 gives the associated homogeneous linear differential equation.
