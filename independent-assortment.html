Independent assortment (θ=21​)

 Home Biology Genetics Mendelian segregation
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Independent assortment between two diallelic genetic loci means that the transmitted allele at one genetic locus is independent of that at the other, conditional on the parental states. In the ordinary two-locus linkage model this has recombination fraction 1/2, with each of four combinations equally likely for a parent heterozygous at both genetic loci.
