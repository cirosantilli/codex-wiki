For the isotropic harmonic oscillator flow, Hamilton's equations are X˙=V, V˙=−ω2X. In dimension three these are three identical uncoupled pairs. For ω=0, put ct​=cos(ωt), st​=sin(ωt). The solution from (x,v) at time zero is
X(t)=ct​x+ωst​​v,V(t)=−ωst​x+ct​v.​
(15)
The inverse flow is obtained by replacing t by −t:
S−t​(x,v)=(ct​x−ωst​​v, ωst​x+ct​v).
(16)
Integrating the source along the backward characteristic gives the Duhamel formula for Hamiltonian transport:
f(t,x,v)=f0​(ct​x−ωst​​v, ωst​x+ct​v)+∫0t​h(s, ct−s​x−ωst−s​​v, ωst−s​x+ct−s​v)ds.​
(17)
Indeed f(t,St​z)=f0​(z)+∫0t​h(s,Ss​z)ds, and setting z=S−t​(x,v) gives the formula. It has the prescribed initial value and differentiation along the characteristic gives the source.
The zero-frequency limit has st​/ω→t, ωst​→0, so St​(x,v)=(x+tv,v) and
f(t,x,v)=f0​(x−tv,v)+∫0t​h(s,x−v(t−s),v)ds(ω=0).​
(18)
