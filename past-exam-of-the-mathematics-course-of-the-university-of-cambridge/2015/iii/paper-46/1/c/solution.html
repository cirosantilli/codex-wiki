After integrating out the shell, let a chosen operator's coefficient be κ+Δκ and write the two-derivative quadratic term as 21​(1+ΔZ)(∂ϕ)2. Here Δκ is the shell-induced change before rescaling, and ΔZ is the shell correction to the wavefunction renormalization. Work in an operator basis in which that quadratic term has this form; the other generated operators remain in the Wilsonian effective action.

This is Wilsonian rescaling of a scalar coupling. The exponent is minus the coupling's engineering mass dimension, [κ]=4−n−m. The rescaling also returns the low-momentum cutoff to Λ, because p′=p/b.
