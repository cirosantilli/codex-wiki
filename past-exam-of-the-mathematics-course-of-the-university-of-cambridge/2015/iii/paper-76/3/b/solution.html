For n=0 the sum is empty, giving zero. The expression is well defined without an inverse of A∗A, which can have a kernel of a linear map and an unbounded inverse on its range. Each iterate lies in the closure of RanA∗, so initialization at zero eliminates the arbitrary null-space component of a solution.
