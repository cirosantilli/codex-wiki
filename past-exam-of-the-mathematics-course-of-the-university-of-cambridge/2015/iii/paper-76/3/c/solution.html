Use the convention required by the printed expansion:
Avi​=σi​ui​,A∗ui​=σi​vi​,σi​>0,
(43)
where ui​∈Y and vi​∈X form the positive-singular-value parts of the singular system of a compact operator. Consequently A∗Avi​=σi2​vi​. Apply part (b)'s polynomial to each such eigenvector, using noisy data:
xn(δ)​=∑i​τσi​∑j=0n−1​(1−τσi2​)j⟨y(δ),ui​⟩vi​.
(44)
The geometric series then gives the full inverse coefficient, or Landweber full inverse filter,
gα​(σ)=σ1−(1−τσ2)1/α​,α=1/n,n∈N.​
(45)
In particular, for the unrelaxed iteration τ=1, gα​(σ)=[1−(1−σ2)1/α]/σ. The dimensionless Landweber spectral filter is instead σgα​(σ); the printed expression uses the full coefficient and therefore needs the denominator σ.
For each fixed finite n, the filter extends continuously at zero by g1/n​(0)=0, since g1/n​(σ)=nτσ+O(σ3) as σ→0. Small singular values are suppressed rather than immediately divided into noisy data. A component of y(δ) in kerA∗ contributes nothing. The parameter here is discrete: 1/α is an integer. This matters when 1−τσ2<0; arbitrary real powers are not intended. A family for every α>0 can use n=⌊1/α⌋ instead.
