Vary Fi​ and Fi∗​ independently. The auxiliary fields have algebraic, rather than differential, Euler-Lagrange equations:
Fi∗​+Wi​=0,Fi​+Wi​​=0.
(24)
Alternatively complete the square:
LF​=∑i​∣Fi​+Wi​​∣2−∑i​∣Wi​∣2.
(25)
Substitution therefore yields the on-shell auxiliary fields and nonnegative scalar potential
Fi​=−Wi​​,LFon shell​=−VF​,VF​=i∑​∣Wi​∣2.​
(26)
The sign in the Lagrangian is minus the F-term scalar potential; changing the metric convention does not change this algebraic result. A supersymmetric vacuum requires all Wi​=0, namely F-flatness. For a noncanonical positive Kähler metric Kijˉ​​, the purely bosonic elimination instead gives VF​=Kijˉ​Wi​Wj​​; fermionic connection terms must also be included in a complete nonlinear component action.
