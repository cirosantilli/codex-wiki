Use the minimal four-dimensional Super-Poincaré algebra, with {Qα​,Qβ​}=0, {Qα​,Qˉ​β˙​​}=2σαβ˙​ν​Pν​ and commuting translations. Lorentz covariance and closure on the existing supercharges permit only
[Pμ,Qα​]=A(σμ)αγ˙​​Qˉ​γ˙​.
(1)
The graded Jacobi identity for Pμ,Qα​,Qβ​ therefore gives
0={[Pμ,Qα​],Qβ​}+{Qα​,[Pμ,Qβ​]}=2A(σαγ˙​μ​ϵγ˙​δ˙σβδ˙ν​+σβγ˙​μ​ϵγ˙​δ˙σαδ˙ν​)Pν​.
(2)
For example, with σ0=I, μ=0 and α=β=1, the coefficient is a nonzero multiple of A(P1​−iP2​). Independence of the translation generators forces A=0. Its adjoint gives the barred result. Thus every supercharge commutes with four-momentum:
[Qα​,Pμ]=0.​
(3)
Consequently [Qα​,P2]=0. If ∣u⟩ has a particular mass-shell condition, the nonzero state Qα​∣u⟩ has the same one. This gives supersymmetric mass degeneracy within an unbroken physical supermultiplet. The qualification matters: after spontaneous supersymmetry breaking, the chosen vacuum is not annihilated by the supercharges, and its one-particle excitations need not constitute degenerate supermultiplets.
For the O'Raifeartaigh model, write X=ϕ1​, Y=ϕ2​, Z=ϕ3​. Field phases allow g,M,m to be positive. The canonical Kähler potential and elimination of the auxiliary fields give the F-term scalar potential
V=g2∣Z2−m2∣2+M2∣Z∣2+∣2gXZ+MY∣2.
(4)
A supersymmetric vacuum would require WY​=MZ=0, hence Z=0, while WX​=g(Z2−m2)=−gm2 could not vanish. Thus F-flatness is impossible. More precisely, with Z=u+iv,
V=g2m4+(M2−2g2m2)u2+(M2+2g2m2)v2+g2(u2+v2)2+∣2gXZ+MY∣2.
(5)
The stated strict inequality makes every nonconstant term nonnegative. The entire classical vacuum family is
Z=Y=0,X=x arbitrary,V0​=g2m4>0.​
(6)
Here FX​=−WX​​=gm2, while FY​=FZ​=0. The Weyl spinor ψX​ is the massless goldstino, and the complex scalar field X is a pseudomodulus with two zero tree-level squared masses. The hierarchy M≫m separates the massive fields from the breaking scale, but it does not itself select x=0 along this flat direction.
At the representative vacuum x=0, the chiral-superfield fermion mass matrix is
mF​=​000​00M​0M0​​.
(7)
Its physical masses are its singular values: 0,M,M. The two massive Weyl spinors can be combined into one massive Dirac spinor. For Y=(yR​+iyI​)/2​, Z=(zR​+izI​)/2​, the real scalar squared masses are
mXR​2​=mXI​2​=0,myR​2​=myI​2​=M2,mzR​2​=M2−2g2m2,mzI​2​=M2+2g2m2.​
(8)
In particular, there is no tachyon under the stated inequality. The splitting of the Z masses displays supersymmetry breaking directly.
For completeness, the mass spectrum can be given throughout the classical vacuum family, rather than silently fixing the pseudomodulus. A phase rotation makes x real and nonnegative without changing physical masses. The massive fermion squared masses are
f±​=M2+2g2∣x∣2±2g∣x∣M2+g2∣x∣2​.
(9)
The real and imaginary scalar sectors have two-by-two scalar squared-mass matrices
Bϵ​=(M22gM∣x∣​2gM∣x∣M2+4g2∣x∣2+2ϵg2m2​),ϵ=−1,+1,
(10)
whose eigenvalues are
bϵ,±​=M2+2g2∣x∣2+ϵg2m2±(2g2∣x∣2+ϵg2m2)2+4g2M2∣x∣2​.
(11)
Both determinants are M2(M2+2ϵg2m2)>0 and their traces are positive, so stability holds at every finite x. Quantum corrections can lift a pseudomodulus; these formulas describe the classical, tree-level spectrum requested here.
Define the physical mass supertrace by
STrM2=∑s​(−1)2s(2s+1)trMs2​.
(12)
Each real scalar contributes once and each Weyl spinor twice with a minus sign. At x=0 this gives 4M2−2(2M2)=0. At general x, ∑ϵ,±​bϵ,±​=4M2+8g2∣x∣2=2(f+​+f−​). Hence the tree-level mass supertrace vanishes despite broken supersymmetry:
STrM2=0.​
(13)
This is the tree-level supertrace mass sum rule, not a claim that the individual masses are equal.
For direct breaking in the Minimal supersymmetric Standard Model, the difficulty is both field content and the tree-level spectrum. A linear gauge-invariant superpotential term needs a gauge-singlet chiral superfield, which the minimal model does not contain. Moreover, with canonical kinetic terms and purely neutral F-term breaking, without D-term mass shifts, the MSSM tree-level sfermion mass constraint applies separately to conserved charge sectors. For one electron pair it gives me~1​2​+me~2​2​=2me2​: both scalar partners cannot be heavy. This is an illustrative charge-sector consequence under those assumptions, not an unrestricted inference from the total supertrace alone. A separate hidden supersymmetry-breaking sector communicating effective soft supersymmetry breaking through loops or suppressed operators avoids the direct canonical tree-level obstruction.
