Let Y be the affine variety with coordinate ring A. Its principal opens D(fi​) cover Y because ∑i​gi​fi​=1. On Xfi​​, the localization of global sections on a principal open identifies its coordinate ring with Afi​​, giving an isomorphism
ϕi​:Xfi​​∼​D(fi​)⊆Y.
(13)
On an overlap the two maps are induced by the same elements of A, or equivalently by the same identification with Afi​fj​​, so they agree. Glue them to ϕ:X→Y. The inverses agree on the overlaps as well and glue to its inverse. At a closed point P, this is the map corresponding to the evaluation ring homomorphism A→k, a↦a(P). This proves the cohomological criterion for affineness by an explicit global isomorphism.
