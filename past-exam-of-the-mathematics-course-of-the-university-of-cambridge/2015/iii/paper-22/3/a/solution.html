Use the orientation F:C→D and G:D→C. An adjunction F⊣G is equivalently specified by the natural transformations
η:1C​⇒GF,ε:FG⇒1D​,
(19)
called the unit and counit of an adjunction, satisfying the triangle identities for an adjunction
εFC​∘F(ηC​)=1FC​,G(εD​)∘ηGD​=1GD​.​
(20)
The corresponding natural bijection is D(FC,D)≅C(C,GD), with f↦G(f)ηC​ and inverse g↦εD​F(g). The two triangular equations are the required compatibility conditions. No proof of equivalence of the formulations is needed here.
