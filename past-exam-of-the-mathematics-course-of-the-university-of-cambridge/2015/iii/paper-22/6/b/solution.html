Write F:X→A and G:A→X, with adjunction counit ε. The induced monad has T=GF and μ=GεF. Define the Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor by
K(A)=(GA,GεA​),K(f)=Gf.​
(48)
The action aA​=GεA​:TGA→GA satisfies aA​ηGA​=1GA​ by a triangular equation. Naturality of ε at εA​:FGA→A gives
εA​FGεA​=εA​εFGA​.
(49)
Applying G yields aA​T(aA​)=aA​μGA​, the other algebra identity. For f:A→A′, naturality gives
GfGεA​=GεA′​GF(Gf),
(50)
so Gf is a monad algebra morphism. Identities and compositions are respected because G is a functor. Finally,
GTK(A)=GA,KF(X)=(GFX,GεFX​)=(TX,μX​)=FTX,
(51)
and the same equalities hold on morphisms. Thus both comparison identities hold, strictly when T is the specified induced monad:
GTK=G,KF=FT.​
(52)
