Let J:C↪B exhibit a reflective subcategory, with reflector K, and let E:I→C be a small diagram in a category. Completeness of B gives a categorical limit L of JE. For every B′∈B, the universal property of this categorical limit and the reflection adjunction give
B(JKB′,L)​≅ilim​B(JKB′,JEi​)≅ilim​B(B′,JEi​)≅B(B′,L).​
(41)
The composite is precomposition with ρB′​, so L satisfies the hom-set condition from the preceding part. Its proof of invertibility of ρL​ did not require repleteness. Thus L≅JKL whether or not the chosen full reflective subcategory is replete.
Transport the ambient limit cone along ρL−1​:JKL→L. Its legs lie in the full subcategory, and their ambient universal property, restricted to objects of C, is exactly the internal categorical limit property. Hence every small diagram in the reflective subcategory has a limit:
C is complete, with a chosen limit KL.​
(42)
For a replete subcategory, the ambient limit object L itself belongs to C. This argument includes the empty diagram and requires no limit-preservation hypothesis on the reflector.
