Assume the hom-set condition. Its surjectivity at B′=B gives u:KB→B with uρB​=1B​. We show that this splitting is two-sided.
Because J is fully faithful, εKB​ is invertible. Both triangle identities for an adjunction give, after suppressing J,
KρB​=ρKB​=εKB−1​.
(38)
Applying K to uρB​=1B​ yields KuρKB​=1KB​. Naturality of the adjunction unit at u gives ρB​u=KuρKB​, hence ρB​u=1KB​. Consequently the hom-set condition forces the unit to be invertible:
ρB−1​=u.​
(39)
Only surjectivity at B′=B was needed for this direction; the given family of bijections certainly supplies it.
