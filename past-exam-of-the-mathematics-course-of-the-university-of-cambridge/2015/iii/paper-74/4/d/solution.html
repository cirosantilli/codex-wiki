Seek a nonzero-buoyancy, separable similarity solution of the unsteady Boussinesq triangular-profile line-plume balances, using powers of distance and time. Let τ=t+t∗​>0, allowing a time origin, and initially take distance z from the virtual origin. Balancing the powers in the momentum and buoyancy equations and the entrainment balance gives the form
Q=Aτz2​,M=Bτ2z3​,F=Cτ3z3​.
(53)
One way to recover the exponents is to let Q∝zaτp and M∝zbτq. Matching the spatial powers in ∂t​(Q2/M), Qz​ and M/Q gives a=2,b=3; matching the time powers gives p=−1,q=−2. The momentum equation then gives the powers of F. In the resulting solution, Q2/M is time-independent, so the storage term in the first equation vanishes exactly.
Direct substitution yields the coefficient equations
2A=3αρ0​AB​,−A+3B=BCA​,−2BCA​+3C=0.
(54)
For a rising buoyant solution C>0, the last equation gives B=2A/3. The first then gives A=αρ0​, and the second gives C=B. The separable decaying line-plume similarity is therefore
Q=αρ0​τz2​,M=32​αρ0​τ2z3​,F=32​αρ0​τ3z3​.​
(55)
Reconstructing the physical fields confirms
W=τz​,b=αz,ρ0​g(ρ0​−ρ)​=τ2z​.​
(56)
For example, ∂t​(FQ/M)=−2αρ0​z2/τ3 and Fz​=2αρ0​z2/τ3 cancel; the momentum balance similarly gives −αρ0​z2/τ2+2αρ0​z2/τ2=FQ/M.
The unshifted form uses t∗​=0 and is defined for t>0, with a singular zero-time limit. To give a finite solution for all t≥0, choose t∗​>0. The equations also permit replacing z by z+z∗​>0, a plume virtual origin; at a finite physical source distance this family has flux histories proportional to τ−1,τ−2,τ−3. The Boussinesq approximation requires (z+z∗​)/(gτ2)≪1 in the region modelled. This is a decaying similarity family; its time and virtual origins require source or initial data. The PDF supplies neither, so a unique forced-startup history cannot be selected from it. In particular, the singular unshifted field should not be presented as a regular solution at t=0.
