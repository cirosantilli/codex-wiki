First suppose A<∞. For an interior ball BR​, with R≤1, write MR​=esssupBR​​u, mR​=essinfBR​​u, and ω(R)=MR​−mR​. Constant shifts of a divergence-form elliptic operator change its forcing. The two nonnegative weak supersolutions v=MR​−u and w=u−mR​ satisfy, respectively,
Lv=div(MR​b−F)+(MR​d−g),Lw=div(F−mR​b)+(g−mR​d).
(5)
Their Weak Harnack inequalities on BR/2​ therefore have forcing errors bounded by CNRα. Indeed ∣MR​∣,∣mR​∣≤A, the new divergence forcing is bounded by ∥F∥q​+A∥b∥q​, and the scalar forcing by ∥g∥q/2​+A∥d∥q/2​; also R2α≤Rα.
On BR/2​ we have v+w=ω(R). Even if p<1, the elementary inequality (s+t)p≤Cp​(sp+tp) implies
ω(R)≤Cp​((∣BR/2​∣1​∫BR/2​​vp)1/p+(∣BR/2​∣1​∫BR/2​​wp)1/p).
(6)
The sum of the two essential infima is ω(R)−ω(R/2). Enlarging a uniform constant C0​>1 gives
ω(R)≤C0​(ω(R)−ω(R/2)+CNRα),ω(R/2)≤θω(R)+CNRα,
(7)
where θ=1−C0−1​∈(0,1). This is an oscillation decay estimate; inhomogeneous oscillation decay implies Hölder continuity. Choose
0<μ<min{α,−log2​θ,1}.
(8)
Iterating on concentric balls, starting at a fixed admissible radius R∗​≤1, yields
ω(2−kR∗​)≤θkω(R∗​)+CNR∗α​∑j=0k−1​θj2−α(k−1−j)≤CN2−kμ.
(9)
The strict choice of μ bounds the geometric series, including the case in which the two original decay rates coincide. Monotonicity of oscillation extends this bound to intermediate radii.
Take R∗​ uniformly smaller than the distance of Ω′ from the boundary of Ω. The vanishing essential oscillation produces a continuous representative of u. A ball centred at either of two sufficiently close points of Ω′ contains both, so its oscillation bounds their difference by CN∣x−y∣μ. For separated points use ∣u(x)−u(y)∣≤2A. Thus the Hölder seminorm satisfies
[u]μ;Ω′​≤C(Ωesssup​∣u∣+∥F∥Lq(Ω)​+∥g∥Lq/2(Ω)​).​
(10)
The constants depend only on the indicated domain separation, dimension, coefficient norms, uniform ellipticity bounds and q. If the global essential supremum is infinite, the displayed global estimate is vacuous; local boundedness of weak elliptic subsolutions on slightly larger compact subsets still makes the same argument prove interior Hölder continuity.
