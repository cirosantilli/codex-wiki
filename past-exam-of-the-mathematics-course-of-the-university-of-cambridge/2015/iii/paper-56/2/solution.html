For a Matrix Lie group G, the left Maurer-Cartan form identifies tangent vectors with the Lie algebra by translating them to the identity:
ρg​(v)=(dLg−1​)g​v=g−1v,ρ=g−1dg.​
(8)
It is a Lie algebra-valued differential form of degree one. For a fixed h∈G, replacing g by hg gives (hg)−1d(hg)=g−1dg, so it is a left-invariant differential form.
Differentiate g−1g=I to get d(g−1)=−g−1(dg)g−1. The exterior derivative then gives
dρ=d(g−1)∧dg+g−1d2g=−g−1dg∧g−1dg=−ρ∧ρ.
(9)
Here the exterior product of matrix-valued differential forms includes matrix multiplication; the order of the matrices matters. Hence the Maurer-Cartan equation is
dρ+ρ∧ρ=0.​
(10)
Write ρ=σαTα​. Antisymmetry of the exterior product implies
ρ∧ρ=21​∑α,β​σα∧σβ[Tα​,Tβ​]=21​∑α,β,γ​cγαβ​σα∧σβTγ​.
(11)
Comparison of the Lie algebra components yields the Maurer-Cartan equation in a Lie-algebra basis:
dσγ=−21​α,β∑​cγαβ​σα∧σβ.​
(12)
Thus the canonical antisymmetric choice is fγαβ​=−21​cγαβ​. The factor one half is required because the sum includes both ordered pairs (α,β) and (β,α). If only α<β is summed, its coefficient is −cγαβ​. Strictly, the equality of differential forms determines only the antisymmetric part of f: one may add any tensor symmetric in α,β without changing it. This is the symmetric-part ambiguity in Maurer-Cartan coefficients.
A faithful matrix representation of the orientation-preserving real affine group is
g(a,b)=(a0​b1​),a>0, b∈R.​
(13)
Its action on (x,1)T has first component ax+b. The group operation and inverse are
g(a,b)g(a′,b′)=g(aa′,b+ab′),g(a,b)−1=g(a−1,−b/a).
(14)
Different transformations have different matrix entries, proving faithfulness. With the Lie algebra basis
D=(10​00​),T=(00​10​),[D,T]=T,
(15)
the left Maurer-Cartan form is
g−1dg=(da/a0​db/a0​)=σDD+σTT.
(16)
The requested left-invariant differential forms therefore form the Maurer-Cartan coframe of the real affine group:
σD=ada​,σT=adb​,dσD=0,dσT=−σD∧σT.​
(17)
For a direct invariance check, left translation by (a0​,b0​) sends (a,b) to (a0​a,b0​+a0​b), and the pullbacks of the two displayed differential forms are unchanged. Their dual left-invariant vector fields are a∂a​ and a∂b​, whose bracket is a∂b​, in agreement with [D,T]=T.
