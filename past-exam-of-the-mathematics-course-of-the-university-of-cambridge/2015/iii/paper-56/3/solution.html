The product of the magnetic field with the velocity in this question is the three-dimensional cross product. Fix ϵ123​=1 and write Kij​=ϵikj​Bk, so Kv=B×v. In coordinates y0=t, yi=xi on R3×R, prescribe an affine connection by the following Christoffel symbols:
Γi00​=−Ei,Γi0j​=Γij0​=−Kij​=ϵijk​Bk,​
(18)
with all other Christoffel symbols zero. These are smooth globally in the given Cartesian coordinates. The lower-index symmetry makes this a torsion-free connection. It is the geometrization of the Lorentz force by an affine connection; no condition on the curl of E or the divergence of B is required for this construction.
An affinely parametrized geodesic, with primes denoting differentiation with respect to λ, satisfies
t′′=0,x′′−E(t′)2−2t′B×x′=0.
(19)
On the branch t′=c=0, use t itself as an affine parameter. Dividing the spatial equation by c2 gives
dt2d2x​=E+2B×dtdx​.​
(20)
Conversely, each solution of this equation makes λ=t, y(t)=(t,x(t)) an affinely parametrized geodesic of the constructed affine connection. Therefore its image is also an unparametrized geodesic. A general change of parameter adds a term proportional to the tangent in the geodesic equation, leaving the curve unchanged. The branch t′=0 is not a trajectory with time as parameter.
For the metric realization, assume E=−∇U and ∇⋅B=0. Introduce the differential form
F=ιB​(dx1∧dx2∧dx3)=21​Fij​dxi∧dxj,Fij​=ϵijk​Bk.
(21)
Then dF=(∇⋅B)dx1∧dx2∧dx3=0. The global Poincaré lemma on the contractible space R3 supplies a one-form A=Ai​dxi with dA=F, equivalently a magnetic vector potential with ∇×A=B. An explicit radial-gauge potential for a divergence-free magnetic field is
Ai​(x)=∫01​sxjFji​(sx)ds,A(x)=∫01​sB(sx)×xds.​
(22)
This is the radial homotopy formula for a closed two-form and is smooth even at the origin. For a constant magnetic field it gives A=21​B×x.
Consider the Eisenhart-Duval lift with the Lorentzian metric
g=dx2+2dt(du−2Ai​dxi−Udt).
(23)
The independent one-forms dx1,dx2,dx3,dt,du−2Ai​dxi−Udt exhibit three positive directions and a two-dimensional block with one positive and one negative direction. Thus the metric is nondegenerate, of signature (4,1). Its coefficients do not depend on u, and guu​=0, so ξ=∂u​ is a null Killing vector field. Indeed gAu​ are constant, so ΓABu​=0 for the Levi-Civita connection and ξ is parallel.
The geodesic Lagrangian for an affine parameter λ is
L=21​∣x′∣2−U(t′)2−2Ai​x′it′+t′u′.
(24)
The cyclic coordinate u gives the conserved quantity pu​=∂L/∂u′=t′. Work at a nonzero value of pu​ and rescale the affine parameter to set t′=1. This is the essential step in the null Kaluza-Klein reduction of a stationary force: one fixes the momentum along the null isometry and projects its geodesics, rather than dividing by guu​.
The spatial Euler-Lagrange equations, before setting t′=1, are
xi′′​+∂i​U(t′)2+2(∂i​Aj​−∂j​Ai​)x′jt′−2Ai​t′′=0.
(25)
Since t′′=0 and Fij​vj=−(B×v)i​, their reduction is
x¨=−∇U+2B×x˙.​
(26)
Equivalently, after taking t as the affine parameter, the term u˙ is a total derivative and the reduced Lagrangian is Lred​=21​∣x˙∣2−2A⋅x˙−U. Its Euler-Lagrange equations give the same sign and factor two.
There is also an explicit converse using null geodesics. For any physical trajectory, set
u˙=U+2A⋅x˙−21​∣x˙∣2.​
(27)
This makes its five-dimensional tangent null. The conserved momentum of the cyclic coordinate t becomes
pt​=−2U−2A⋅x˙+u˙=−(U+21​∣x˙∣2).
(28)
The quantity in parentheses is conserved, because its derivative is x˙⋅(2B×x˙)=0. Hence the t equation, as well as the spatial and u Euler-Lagrange equations, is satisfied. Every trajectory admits a null geodesic lift with pu​=1, and every such lift projects to the required trajectory.
Finally, a time-independent gauge transformation A↦A+∇χ is absorbed by u↦u+2χ. The one-form du−2Ai​dxi−Udt and the five-dimensional metric are unchanged. These gauge transformations of an Eisenhart-Duval lift alter the reduced Lagrangian only by the total derivative −2dχ/dt.
