There is a useful explicit reconstruction of a shorter trace from a longer one. Suppose
ρβ​(α)=⟨S0​,…,Sn−1​⟩,Si​=Cβiα​​∩α,
(18)
and fix ξ<α. Let j<n be the first index with Sj​∖ξ=∅, if it exists. Before that index, the walk to ξ follows the walk to α. At index j it moves to δ=min(Sj​∖ξ)<α. Thus
ρβ​(ξ)=⟨Si​∩ξ:i≤j⟩⌢ρδ​(ξ).
(19)
When δ=ξ, the appended trace is empty. If no such j exists, the walk first reaches α and then continues toward ξ, giving
ρβ​(ξ)=⟨Si​∩ξ:i<n⟩⌢ρα​(ξ).
(20)
Both formulas depend only on the displayed sequence, α,ξ, and the fixed club sequence. Equal traces ρβ​(α)=ργ​(α) consequently reconstruct the same trace at every ξ<α. This proves the trace coherence lemma for minimal walks:
ρβ​↾α=ργ​↾α.​
(21)
