Fix forcing names τ,τ1​,…,τn​∈M for the set w and the parameters. Define a name
σ={(η,p):∃q ((η,q)∈τ, p≤q, p⊩φ(η,τ,τ1​,…,τn​))}.
(54)
Only η appearing in τ and p∈P are needed, so this is a subset of a ground-model set. The forcing definability lemma makes its defining predicate a formula of M. Ground-model axiom schema of separation therefore gives σ∈M.
If (η,p)∈σ with p∈G, the accompanying q≥p belongs to G, so ηG​∈τG​. The forcing theorem gives φ(ηG​,τG​,(τ1​)G​,…,(τn​)G​).
Conversely, if u∈τG​ satisfies this formula, choose (η,q)∈τ with q∈G and ηG​=u. The forcing truth lemma supplies r∈G forcing the formula for these names. Directedness gives p∈G with p≤q,r. Then (η,p)∈σ, and u∈σG​. Thus
σG​={u∈w:φ(u,w,v1​,…,vn​)}.​
(55)
This proves the instance of the axiom schema of separation in the generic extension, without assuming that instance there in order to construct the name.
