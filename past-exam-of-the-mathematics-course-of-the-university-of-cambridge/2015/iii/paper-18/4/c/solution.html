A positive holomorphic line bundle F admits a Hermitian metric whose Chern connection curvature satisfies that iFF​ is a positive real (1, 1)-form. In a local holomorphic local frame with squared length e−φ, the local formula for the Chern connection on a line bundle gives FF​=∂∂ˉφ, so positivity means i∂∂ˉφ is positive definite. Its closedness makes ω=iFF​ a Kähler form; use this form to define the operators below.
Let n=dimC​X>0, choose a Hermitian metric on E, and equip Gm​=E⊗F−m with the tensor-product metric. The curvature of a tensor product connection gives
iFGm​​=iFE​−mωidE​.
(32)
On Gm​-valued zero-forms, the Lefschetz commutator is [L,Λ]=−nid. Thus the Bochner-Kodaira-Nakano identity gives
ΔGm​′′​=ΔGm​′​+RE​+mnid,RE​=[iFE​,Λ]​A0,0(E)​.
(33)
This last operator is a fixed smooth self-adjoint bundle endomorphism. Compactness supplies a finite C such that ⟨RE​v,v⟩≥−C∥v∥2 at every point. For a holomorphic section s of Gm​, D′′s=0 and D′′∗s=0 by degree, so integrating the identity gives
0=∥D′s∥2+⟨RE​s,s⟩+mn∥s∥2≥(mn−C)∥s∥2.
(34)
Choose an integer m0​ with m0​n>C. Then H0(X,E⊗F−m)=0(m≥m0​)​. The threshold depends on the fixed bundle E, as the curvature bound makes explicit. Positive complex dimension is necessary: on a zero-dimensional manifold positivity is vacuous and a nonzero fibre has nonzero sections for every twist.
