For a holomorphic vector bundle E, the sheaf of vector-bundle-valued differential forms is
Ap,q(E)(U)=Γ(U,⋀p(T1,0X)∗⊗⋀q(T0,1X)∗⊗E),
(36)
where sections are smooth and restrictions are the usual ones. In a holomorphic local frame of E, the Dolbeault operator ∂ˉE​ acts coefficientwise and satisfies ∂ˉE2​=0. Its Dolbeault cohomology with values in a holomorphic vector bundle is the cohomology of the global section complex Ap,∙(X,E).
The bundle-valued Dolbeault theorem identifies this with sheaf cohomology:
Hq(X,ΩXp​⊗O(E))≅H∂ˉp,q​(X,E).​
(37)
Here ΩXp​ is the sheaf of holomorphic differential forms, and O(E) is the sheaf of holomorphic sections of a vector bundle. Equivalently, the complex
0⟶ΩXp​⊗O(E)⟶Ap,0(E)∂ˉE​​Ap,1(E)∂ˉE​​⋯
(38)
is a fine sheaf resolution, by the local Dolbeault-Poincaré lemma and smooth partitions of unity. In particular p=0 computes Hq(X,O(E)); for p>0 the holomorphic differential-form factor must be retained.
