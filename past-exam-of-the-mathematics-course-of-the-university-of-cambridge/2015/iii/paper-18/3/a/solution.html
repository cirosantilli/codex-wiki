Cp(U,F)=∏i0​<⋯<ip​​F(Ui0​​∩⋯∩Uip​​).
(17)
It is a product, not a finite-support sum. The Čech coboundary is
(δc)i0​…ip+1​​=∑j=0p+1​(−1)jci0​…ij​​…ip+1​​​Ui0​​∩⋯∩Uip+1​​​.

Every term in δ2c occurs twice with opposite signs, so δ2=0. The Čech cohomology of the cover is Hˇp(U,F)=kerδ/imδ. Refinements induce maps on these groups; different choices of refinement indices give maps related by a cochain homotopy, hence the same map on cohomology. Taking the direct limit over covers gives
Hˇp(X,F)=Ulim​​Hˇp(U,F).​
(19)
One may use locally finite covers, since a complex manifold is a paracompact space and these covers are cofinal. In degree zero this recovers the group of global sections by the sheaf gluing axiom.
