With mixed orientations, use the general pair shift established above and determine growing spectators by the sign of κj​(v1​−vj​); the same multiplication of pair coefficients proves additivity.
There is no independent three-body contribution to the asymptotic shift. The pairwise additivity of soliton shifts is a classical manifestation of factorized scattering in an integrable partial differential equation. The collision preserves the individual asymptotic rapidities and profiles, and the net shift is independent of the sequence of separated pair collisions. In the quantum theory, consistency of the corresponding species-changing S-matrices becomes the Yang-Baxter equation; the classical scalar shift identity is its physical precursor, rather than a derivation of all quantum matrix identities.
