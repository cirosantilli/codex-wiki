Y(t)=∫0t​f(s)ds+n−1/2W(t),0≤t≤1,​

where W is standard Brownian motion and the deterministic drift belongs to L2 space on [0,1]. Equivalently, dY(t)=f(t)dt+n−1/2dW(t). For every deterministic h∈L2[0,1], the observed stochastic integral satisfies
Y(h):=∫01​h(t)dY(t)=⟨h,f⟩+n−1/2W(h),Cov(W(h),W(g))=⟨h,g⟩.
