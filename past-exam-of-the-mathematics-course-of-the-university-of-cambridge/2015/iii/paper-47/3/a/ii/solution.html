Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2015 / iii / Paper 47 / 3 / a / ii / Solution

 ... 2015 iii Paper 47 3 a ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
There are two diagrams: the t-channel Z boson exchange from the weak neutral current, and a crossed u-channel W boson exchange from the weak charged current. The charged-current vertices pair the incoming neutrino k with the outgoing Electron p′ and the incoming Electron p with the outgoing neutrino k′. The exchanged momentum has square u=(k−p′)2. With charge flow from the first vertex to the second, the exchanged boson is W+. The corresponding panels show both topologies; no photon or Higgs diagram survives the stated neutrino assumptions.
