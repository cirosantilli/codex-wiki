Flavour issue in the printed effective interaction. The displayed charged-current factor uses an Electron field even for the muon-neutrino term. Taken literally, this introduces an extra νμ​e charged-current interaction, contradicting the Standard Model diagrams in part (a). In the Standard Model that factor uses the charged lepton of the same flavour; for Electron scattering the charged-current contribution is therefore present only for νe​. The weak flavour selection in neutrino-electron scattering fixes the physically consistent assignment used below. The literal printed consequence is given afterwards, so the discrepancy is explicit.
At leading order in the Electron mass and in the four-fermion regime, define ΓLα​=γα(1−γ5). The weak neutral current yields the scattering amplitude
Mμ​=−2​GF​​[uˉν​(k′)ΓLα​uν​(k)][uˉe​(p′)γα​(cV​−cA​γ5)ue​(p)].​
(18)
An overall amplitude phase has no observable effect. The Fermi interaction is valid when the exchanged invariants are small compared with the squared weak-boson masses.
Average over the two initial Electron spin states, and sum over final spins. There is no averaging over a sterile right-handed incoming neutrino. Using the fermion spin sum, the averaged square is
∣Mμ​∣2​=4GF2​​Tr[k′ΓLα​kΓLβ​]Tr[p′γα​(cV​−cA​γ5)pγβ​(cV​−cA​γ5)].
(19)
To display the trace contraction, define
Sαβ(a,b)=aαbβ+aβbα−gαβ(a⋅b),Eαβ(a,b)=ϵαβρσaρ​bσ​.
(20)
The supplied gamma matrix trace identities give the two tensors 8(S(k′,k)+iE(k′,k)) and 4[(cV2​+cA2​)S(p′,p)+2icV​cA​E(p′,p)]. Symmetric-antisymmetric mixed contractions vanish. Set X=(k′⋅p′)(k⋅p) and Y=(k′⋅p)(k⋅p′). The remaining contractions are S(k′,k)S(p′,p)=2(X+Y) and E(k′,k)E(p′,p)=−2(X−Y), using the stated negative epsilon-contraction sign. Their sum gives
∣Mμ​∣2​=16GF2​[(cV​+cA​)2(k⋅p)(k′⋅p′)+(cV​−cA​)2(k⋅p′)(k′⋅p)].
(21)
The massless Mandelstam variables satisfy s+t+u=0, and the two products are s2/4 and u2/4, respectively. Therefore
∣Mμ​∣2​=4GF2​[(cV​+cA​)2s2+(cV​−cA​)2u2].​
(22)
In the centre-of-momentum frame, momentum conservation gives E=s​/2. If z=cosθ is the angle cosine between p and p′, then u=−s(1+z)/2. The massless two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space is dρ2​=dΩ/(32π2), while the flux is 2s. Thus
dzdσ​=32πs∣M∣2​​=8πGF2​s​[(cV​+cA​)2+41​(cV​−cA​)2(1+z)2].
(23)
Integrate ∫−11​dz=2 and ∫−11​(1+z)2dz=8/3:
σμ​=4πGF2​s​[(cV​+cA​)2+31​(cV​−cA​)2]=3πGF2​s​(cV2​+cV​cA​+cA2​).​
(24)
Consequently the intended coefficients are
F(s)=3πs​,A=1,B=1,C=0.​
(25)
This is the massless neutrino-electron contact cross-section.
For completeness, the literal printed muon-neutrino charged-current term would instead shift both couplings by one, exactly as in part (c):
σμ,literal​=3πGF2​s​[cV2​+cV​cA​+cA2​+3(cV​+cA​)+3].
(26)
For arbitrary real cV​,cA​, its linear terms cannot be represented by the requested quadratic form with coupling-independent constants. The Standard Model result and the literal printed interaction are not the same problem.
