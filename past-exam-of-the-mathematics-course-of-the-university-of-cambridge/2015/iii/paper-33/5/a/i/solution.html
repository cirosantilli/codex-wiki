For a nonnegative random variable, T=∫0∞​1{T>t}dt. Tonelli theorem permits interchanging expectation and this nonnegative integral, giving the tail integral formula for moments:
ET=∫0∞​P(T>t)dt=∫0∞​S(t)dt.​
(25)
The equality also holds when the mean is infinite. Thus mean completion time is the total area under the survival function, while a restricted mean survival time integrates only to a specified follow-up horizon.
