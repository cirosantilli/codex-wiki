The strong law for Brownian motion says Ws​/s→0 almost surely. For sufficiently large s, therefore 2Ws​−s≤−s/2, giving
V∞​:=∫0∞​e2Ws​−sds<∞almost surely.

Thus Jt​=∫0t​eWs​−s/2dBs​ has finite terminal quadratic variation. Stopping when its bracket reaches each integer makes it an L2-bounded martingale, which converges by the martingale convergence theorem. Patching these limits shows that
J∞​=∫0∞​eWs​−s/2dBs​​

is a well-defined finite almost sure limit. This construction uses local square integrability; a global L2 bound is not required.
Conditional on the entire W path, the independent Brownian motion B still supplies a centered Gaussian stochastic integral, with variance V∞​∈(0,∞). The conditionally Gaussian stochastic integral with an independent integrator therefore has no atoms, even after averaging over W.
