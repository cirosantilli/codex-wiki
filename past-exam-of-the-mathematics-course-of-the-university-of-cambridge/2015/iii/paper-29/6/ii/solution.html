Construct a rate-λ Poisson process, for λ>0, from independent waiting times Ej​ with exponential distribution Exp(λ). Put Sk​=E1​+⋯+Ek​, S0​=0, and Nt​=max{k:Sk​≤t}. The strong law of large numbers gives Sk​/k→1/λ, so there are only finitely many arrivals on each finite interval. Consequently the counting paths are càdlàg, and N0​=0 almost surely.
At any deterministic time s, the memorylessness of the exponential distribution says that the residual waiting time has again exponential distribution Exp(λ) and is independent of the observed history. Subsequent waiting times are fresh independent copies. Thus the Poisson process restarts independently at s, proving independent increments and stationary increments. Integrating the joint waiting-time densities over 0<s1​<⋯<sk​≤t<sk+1​ gives
P(Nt​=k)=e−λtk!(λt)k​,k≥0,
(31)
so its time values have the expected Poisson distribution.
For h↓0,
P(Nh​=0)=1−e−λh⟶0.
(32)
Together with stationary increments, this gives stochastic continuity at every time, from either side where applicable. All the Lévy process requirements hold, and
EeiθNt​=exp{λt(eiθ−1)},ψ(θ)=λ(eiθ−1).​
(33)
For λ=0, the identically zero process gives the degenerate case.
