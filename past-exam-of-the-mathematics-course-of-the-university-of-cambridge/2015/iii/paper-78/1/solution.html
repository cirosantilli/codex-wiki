As permeability of a porous medium decreases, Λ increases: the interface pressure rises toward the finite value Δ/γ, while the interface temperature falls toward T∞​. Hydraulic resistance suppresses freezing through pressure-induced freezing-point depression. In the limit Π→0, aa˙∼ΠΔ/(γμβ); a zero-seed radius grows as Πt​ and tends to zero at fixed time. At exactly zero permeability an incompressible rigid model cannot expel the expansion volume, so sustained growth stops; deformation or compressibility would require a different model.
