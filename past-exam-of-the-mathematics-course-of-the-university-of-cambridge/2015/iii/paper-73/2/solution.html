An axial control volume balance then gives the displayed equation. The approximation requires a slender column and slowly varying radius.

The bulk falls at approximately −w, where excess weight balances annular hydraulic resistance. Extensional stresses adjust this velocity to zero in a basal boundary layer of thickness z; almost all thickening occurs there.

Thus z is the vertical distance over which extensional stress communicates the basal constraint before annular resistance screens it.
