Use the sl2 Lie algebra relations [H,X]=2X, [H,Y]=−2Y and [X,Y]=H. A highest-weight vector satisfies Xv=0 and Hv=mv, and HYjv=(m−2j)Yjv. For k=0, XYv=YXv+Hv=mv. Assuming the formula at k−1, one gets
XYk+1v=(YX+H)Ykv=[k(m−k+1)+m−2k]Ykv=(k+1)(m−k)Ykv.
(4)
Thus the sl2 highest-weight lowering formula is
XYk+1v=(k+1)(m−k)Ykv(k≥0).​
(5)
In particular a finite-dimensional Irreducible Lie algebra representation has highest weight m∈Z≥0​ and weight vectors v,Yv,…,Ymv, as in the classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations.
Let Vλ​ be a finite-dimensional Irreducible Lie algebra representation of a complex semisimple Lie algebra, with highest weight λ. Write mλ​(μ)=dim(Vλ​)μ​, taking it to be zero when μ is not a weight, and let ρ be the half-sum of positive roots. The Killing form induces an inner product on the real span of weights. Freudenthal multiplicity formula states
((λ+ρ,λ+ρ)−(μ+ρ,μ+ρ))mλ​(μ)=2α>0∑​j≥1∑​(μ+jα,α)mλ​(μ+jα).​
(6)
The sums are finite. For a weight μ=λ, the coefficient on the left is positive: move μ into the dominant Weyl chamber, use that a weight is below λ in dominance order, and note that (μ,ρ) does not increase on moving back out of that chamber. The resulting recursion starts from mλ​(λ)=1.
Here is a proof using the allowed Casimir operator. Choose root vectors Eα​,Fα​ for positive and negative roots, normalized by B(Eα​,Fα​)=1, and let tα​∈h satisfy B(tα​,h)=α(h). Invariance of a bilinear form on a Lie algebra gives [Eα​,Fα​]=tα​. If hi​,hi are dual bases of the Cartan subalgebra under B, the Casimir operator is
C=∑i​hi​hi+∑α>0​(Eα​Fα​+Fα​Eα​).
(7)
On the weight space of μ, the first sum acts by (μ,μ). Replacing Eα​Fα​ with Fα​Eα​+tα​ gives
C​(Vλ​)μ​​=(μ,μ+2ρ)I+2∑α>0​Fα​Eα​​(Vλ​)μ​​.
(8)
Define Tα​(μ)=tr(Fα​Eα​​(Vλ​)μ​​). The cyclic trace identity between adjacent weight spaces and the commutator relation yield
Tα​(μ)=tr(Eα​Fα​​(Vλ​)μ+α​​)=Tα​(μ+α)+(μ+α,α)mλ​(μ+α).
(9)
Iterate upwards until the weight spaces vanish to obtain Tα​(μ)=∑j≥1​(μ+jα,α)mλ​(μ+jα). Finally take the trace of the displayed restriction of C. Its Casimir eigenvalue is (λ,λ+2ρ), so subtracting (μ,μ+2ρ)mλ​(μ) proves the formula. This trace argument handles weight multiplicities greater than one without choosing a separate sl2 Lie algebra string through each vector.
