We use the finite-horizon fundamental theorem of asset pricing in deflator form: an arbitrage-free finite market admits a strictly positive adapted process D, with D0​=1, such that DP is a martingale. Equivalently, on every step,
E[Dt​Pt​∣Ft−1​]=Dt−1​Pt−1​.

∑i​qi​zi​pi​=(∑i​qi​pi​pi⊤​)V−1p0​=p0​.

This is the positive regression deflator in a complete finite market. It also proves the suggested conclusion: with Y0​=1 and Yt​=∏u=1t​Zu​,

Hence Y is a strictly positive martingale deflator. Finite-state structure makes these expectations integrable on each finite horizon. Positive definiteness alone would not ensure positivity of the regression factor; market completeness and the positive deflator are essential.
