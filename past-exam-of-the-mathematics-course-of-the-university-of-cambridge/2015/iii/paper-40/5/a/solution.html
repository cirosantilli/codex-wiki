Use a telescoping replication of a stock-price sum. Hold T−t+1 shares during interval (t−1,t]; at time t, sell one share and keep its proceeds in the bond. Start with T shares and no cash, costing TS0​.
After the time-t rebalance, the stock holdings are T−t and the cash holdings are ∑u=1t​Su​, so wealth is
Vt​=∑u=1t​Su​+(T−t)St​.
(36)
The sale of one share exactly funds the cash increase, making the strategy self-financing. Equivalently,
TS0​+t=1∑T​(T−t+1)(St​−St−1​)=u=1∑T​Su​.​
(37)
At time T there are no remaining shares, and the cash equals the claim. All stock positions over trading intervals are predictable.
