Increasing ∣β∣ at fixed W reduces the shear and the growth rate, without creating a finite-β wavelength cutoff for these purely meridional modes. The limit β→0 at fixed W is not a valid finite Sverdrup balance; its divergent velocity violates the weak-flow assumptions. Growth draws on the interfacial displacement and vertical shear, rather than on a time-dependent wind forcing, whose perturbation was set to zero.
