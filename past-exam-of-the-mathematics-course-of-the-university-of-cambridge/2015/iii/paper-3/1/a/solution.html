A representation of a quiver assigns a k-vector space Xi​ to each vertex i∈Q0​ and a linear map fρ​:Xs(ρ)​→Xt(ρ)​ to each arrow ρ∈Q1​. Here s(ρ) and t(ρ) are its source and target. A quiver representation morphism θ:X→Y is a family of linear maps θi​:Xi​→Yi​ such that every arrow square commutes:
θt(ρ)​fρ​=gρ​θs(ρ)​(ρ∈Q1​).​
(1)
Composition and identities are defined vertex by vertex. A quiver representation morphism is an isomorphism precisely when every θi​ is invertible.
