Number the source vertices 1,…,r and the sink c=r+1, and write ai​:i→c for the arrows. The only basis paths are e1​,…,er​,ec​,a1​,…,ar​. Using standard matrix units, define
ei​⟼Eii​,ec​⟼Ecc​,ai​⟼Eci​.

The relations ec​ai​=ai​=ai​ei​, orthogonality of the vertex idempotents, and ai​aj​=0 agree exactly with multiplication of these matrix units. The map is therefore an algebra homomorphism, and its images form a basis of the algebra
kSr​≅{(diag(λ1​,…,λr​)v​0μ​):λi​,μ∈k, v∈k1×r}.​
(11)
This is an explicit isomorphism of the path algebra of the subspace quiver with a (2r+1)-dimensional triangular matrix algebra.
