Use the connection convention ∇α​Yρ=∂α​Yρ+Γρμα​Yμ, with the derivative index last. This is consistent with the printed curvature formula and the final formula in part (iv). The paper's Tμν​ρ=Γρμν​−Γρνμ​ is then the negative of the geometric torsion tensor defined by T(X,Y)=∇X​Y−∇Y​X−[X,Y]. Keep the paper's component convention throughout this question.
Set Aμνρ​=gρλ​(Γλμν​−Sλμν​). The difference of affine connections is a tensor. Since the Levi-Civita connection S is symmetric and metric compatible, expanding the nonmetricity tensor gives
Tμνρ​=Aμνρ​−Aνμρ​,Nμνρ​=−Aμρν​−Aνρμ​.
(34)
Here Tμνρ​=gρλ​Tμν​λ is antisymmetric in its first two slots, while Nμνρ​ is symmetric in its first two slots. Cyclically permuting these identities and eliminating the other components gives
Aμνρ​=21​(Tμνρ​+Tνρμ​−Tρμν​+Nμνρ​−Nνρμ​−Nρμν​).
(35)
Raise the last slot to obtain the connection decomposition
Γρμν​=Sρμν​−Kμν​ρ+Wμν​ρ,​
(36)
where
Kμν​ρ=−21​(Tμν​ρ+Tν​ρμ​−Tρμν​),Wμν​ρ=21​(Nμν​ρ−Nν​ρμ​−Nρμν​).
(37)
The PDF is missing the factor 1/2 in its definition of K. With the printed K, the decomposition would instead contain −K/2. In these formulas Tρμν​ raises the first slot of the fully lowered tensor; it must not be confused with Tμν​ρ, which raises the last slot.
