Let γX​(s) be the affinely parametrized geodesic of the affine connection with γX​(0)=p and γ˙​X​(0)=X. For fixed t, the curve η(u)=γX​(tu) has initial velocity tX. The geodesic equation is homogeneous of degree two in the velocity, so its left-hand side for η is t2 times the left-hand side for γX​, and is zero. Uniqueness of the geodesic initial value problem gives η=γtX​.
By the definition of the affine exponential map, rescaling the initial velocity rescales affine parameter:
expp​(tX)=γtX​(1)=γX​(t).​
(38)
At t=0 both sides equal p. The statement is made where the geodesics exist; without geodesic completeness, the affine exponential map is defined only on a suitable neighbourhood of zero in Tp​M, not necessarily all of Tp​M. An affine parameter distance need not be a metric length.
