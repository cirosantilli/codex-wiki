Here is the existential common-substructure test for quantifier elimination. Use a first-order language with a constant symbol, as in the applications below, or the usual conventions for empty generated substructures and truth constants. Let T be a first-order theory. Suppose that whenever M,N⊨T have a common substructure A, then, for every quantifier-free formula θ(x,yˉ​) and every finite tuple aˉ∈A,
M⊨∃xθ(x,aˉ)⟹N⊨∃xθ(x,aˉ).
(8)
The condition is symmetric because it applies also with M and N exchanged. The conclusion is quantifier elimination for T: for every first-order formula φ(yˉ​) there is a quantifier-free formula ψ(yˉ​) such that
T⊨∀yˉ​(φ(yˉ​)↔ψ(yˉ​)).
(9)
The common substructure need not itself satisfy T, and witnesses are sought in the whole models, not necessarily in A. This test is the QET1 version of the common-substructure test for quantifier elimination.
