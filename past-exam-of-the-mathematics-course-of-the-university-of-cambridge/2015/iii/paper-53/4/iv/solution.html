The horizon-entry and equality matching fixes order-one coefficients and additive constants inside the logarithm. The displayed branches capture the requested leading spectrum and share its schematic normalization; their asymptotic forms must not be joined literally at kτeq​=1, where the high-k logarithm alone vanishes.
