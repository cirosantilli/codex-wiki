Let D(z) be the linear growth factor normalized to D(0)=1. The present-extrapolated spherical-collapse barrier is δc​(z)=δsc​/D(z), where δsc​≃1.686 is the linearly evolved overdensity required for spherical collapse at that epoch. The smoothed matter density variance σ(M,z) is the root-mean-square linear density contrast after smoothing on a Lagrangian region containing mass M; σ(M,z)=D(z)σ(M,0). Thus the barrier-to-variance ratio is equivalently δc​(z)/σ(M,0)=δsc​/σ(M,z).
