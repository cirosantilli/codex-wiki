If unit vectors fn​ satisfy (L−λI)fn​→0, a bounded inverse would give 1≤∥(L−λI)−1∥∥(L−λI)fn​∥→0. Hence λ belongs to the spectrum of a bounded operator.
Conversely a spectral point is real by the preceding argument. Put A=L−λI, again a self-adjoint operator. If inf∥f∥=1​∥Af∥>0, then A is injective with closed range. Its range is dense by image-kernel orthogonality for an adjoint, so it is bijective with a bounded inverse, a contradiction. Thus this infimum is zero; choose unit fn​ with ∥Afn​∥<1/n. We have proved
λ∈Σ(L)⟺∃(fn​): ∥fn​∥=1,(L−λI)fn​→0.​

Such a sequence is a spectral Weyl sequence. No weak convergence is required here; nonreal λ are ruled out by the lower bound in the preceding solution.
