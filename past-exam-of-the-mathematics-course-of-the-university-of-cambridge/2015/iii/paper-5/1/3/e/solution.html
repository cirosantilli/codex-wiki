In fact the maximizing foot is unique. The concave Legendre dual can be written g(z)=infs​(zs−f(s)) and satisfies g′(z)=h(z): compare the minimizing values at z and z+ε and use continuity of h. This avoids assuming differentiability of h, which strict concavity by itself does not guarantee. Differentiating the maximizing expression with respect to y gives u0​(y)−h((x−y)/t)=0, hence Xt​(y)=x and y=x0​. The maximum representation for a concave conservation law therefore reproduces the characteristic solution of a scalar conservation law throughout the smooth lifespan.
