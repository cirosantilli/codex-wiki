A characteristic curve has x−at constant. Backtracking reaches the initial line if x≥at and the inflow boundary otherwise. Hence
u(t,x)={u0​(x−at),f(t−x/a),​x≥at,0≤x<at.​​
(5)
Values on the dividing characteristic curve are immaterial for bounded weak solutions.
For a classical solution on the closed quadrant, choose u0​,f∈C1([0,∞)) and impose the corner compatibility for constant-speed transport
f(0)=u0​(0),f′(0)=−au0′​(0).​
(6)
These make the values and both first derivatives agree across x=at; each branch solves the transport equation. They are also necessary for a C1 solution up to the initial and inflow boundaries.
Spatial C∞ regularity for every t≥0, up to x=0, holds precisely for smooth data with all matching jets:
f(n)(0)=(−a)nu0(n)​(0)(n=0,1,2,…).​
(7)
Indeed the spatial derivatives of order n at x=at are u0(n)​(0) and (−1/a)nf(n)(0). Necessity of smooth u0​ follows at t=0; smoothness of f on any finite interval follows by reading the boundary branch in a spatial slice at a larger time.
