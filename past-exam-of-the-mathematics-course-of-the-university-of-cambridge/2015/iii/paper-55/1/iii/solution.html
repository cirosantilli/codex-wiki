For W=ρˉ​+Pˉ=0, the conserved uniform-density curvature in the spatial-metric convention 1−2Φ is
ζud​=−Φ+3Wδρ​.​
(13)
The source's plus sign in front of both terms is not gauge invariant and cannot yield the claimed conservation. Choosing the opposite overall sign, Φ−δρ/(3W), is equally valid, but changing only one term is necessary. A passive time displacement t↦t+T gives Φ=Φ+NˉHT and δρ​=δρ+3NˉHWT, so their combination above is unchanged. This is the uniform-density curvature sign test.
The lapse perturbation in the perturbed energy-conservation source is Ψ, not the spatial-curvature perturbation Φ. Indeed the perturbation of the proper-time density derivative is Dδρ−ΨDρˉ​, while the normal expansion changes by −κ. The consistent equation is
Dδρ=−3H(δρ+δP)+W(κ−3HΨ)−Δu.
(14)
For a constant barotropic equation of state, δP=wδρ and DW=−3H(1+w)W. Differentiate ζud​ and cancel the density terms:
Dζud​=−DΦ+3κ​−HΨ−3WΔu​=31​Δχ+​−3WΔu​.
(15)
More generally, δPnad​=δP−(DPˉ/Dρˉ​)δρ gives
Dζud​=−WH​δPnad​+31​Δ(χ+​−Wu​).​
(16)
Regular scalar modes have gradient terms suppressed by (k/aH)2. Thus adiabatic uniform-density curvature is conserved to leading order on superhorizon scales, ζ˙​ud​≃0. This is a long-wavelength limit, not exact finite-wavenumber conservation; it assumes a regular gradient expansion and excludes W=0, where this variable is undefined. In contrast the literal printed variable obeys Dζprinted​=2DΦ+O(k2) even after repairing the energy equation, and generally evolves across changes of the background equation of state.
There are three practical benefits. First, uniform-density curvature is gauge invariant, so matching it does not depend on coordinate slicing. Second, superhorizon conservation of uniform-density curvature transports primordial amplitudes through changes in the expansion history without solving each short-scale dynamical equation. Third, when a mode re-enters the horizon it supplies initial conditions for density growth and Cosmic microwave background anisotropy; non-adiabatic pressure is also an explicit diagnostic of when that simple transport fails.
