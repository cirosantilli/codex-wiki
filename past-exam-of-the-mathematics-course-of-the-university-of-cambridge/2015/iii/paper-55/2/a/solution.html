In the Spatially flat FLRW metric, neglect metric perturbations and write ϕ=ϕˉ​+δϕ. The kinetic invariant is then exactly quadratic in field derivatives:
X=21​(ϕˉ​˙​+δϕ˙​)2−2a21​(∂i​δϕ)2.
(17)
Subtracting Xˉ=ϕˉ​˙​2/2 gives the second-order expansion
δX=ϕˉ​˙​δϕ˙​+21​δϕ˙​2−2a21​(∂i​δϕ)2.​
(18)
In the derivative-dominated approximation, δϕ=−ϕˉ​˙​ζ/H with ϕˉ​˙​/H treated as slowly varying. Thus δϕ˙​≃−ϕˉ​˙​ζ˙​/H and
δX1​=−H2Xˉ​ζ˙​,δX2​=H2Xˉ​[ζ˙​2−a2(∂i​ζ)2​].
(19)
This uses the linear flat-to-comoving slicing relation. Differentiating its slowly varying coefficient would add terms involving undifferentiated ζ; it is not an exact nonlinear gauge transformation, and those neglected terms do not alter the leading pure ζ˙​3 coefficient computed here.
Only P,XX​δX1​δX2​ and P,XXX​δX13​/6 contribute to the requested three-time-derivative term. Therefore the directly evaluated cubic coefficient is
C=−H3a3​(2Xˉ2P,XX​+34​Xˉ3P,XXX​)=H32a3Xˉ2P,XX​​A,A=−1−3P,XX​2XˉP,XXX​​.​
(20)
This is the derivative-cubic coefficient of a noncanonical scalar. For a physical P(X,ϕ) scalar field theory, differentiating ρ=2XP,X​−P at fixed ϕ gives ρ,X​=P,X​+2XP,XX​. Its sound speed is consequently
cs2​=P,X​+2XˉP,XX​P,X​​,C=Ha3ϵ​cs2​1−cs2​​A,ϵ=H2XˉP,X​​.​
(21)
The minus sign printed in the denominator of cs2​ reverses the asserted coefficient identity; it must be a plus. The printed background equation also lacks a power: it is 3H2=ρ in the stated reduced-Planck units. A stable propagating scalar has positive time-kinetic coefficient P,X​+2XP,XX​ and positive gradient coefficient P,X​, as summarized by noncanonical scalar kinetic stability.
The unfactored expression for C stays meaningful if P,XX​=0, although A separately then need not exist. For a canonical kinetic term with no higher kinetic derivatives, C=0. The cubic curvature action for a P(X, phi) scalar field can include other vertices and gravitational terms; they are outside this requested derivative coefficient.
