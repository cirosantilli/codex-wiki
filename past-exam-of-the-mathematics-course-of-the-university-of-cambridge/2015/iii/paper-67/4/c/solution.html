No limiting interchange is needed: the sum has finitely many shells. Each Gs(Z)​ is supported within the union of supports appearing in Hs​ together with Z, because its unitary time evolution acts trivially outside that neighbourhood. The individual shell terms need not commute with the full ground state projector; the full filtered sum does.
