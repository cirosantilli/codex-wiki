Applying death-only rate correction under exponential censoring to the death fraction and combined-rate estimate, the corrected plug-in death rate is
λplug-in​=32​×0.3=0.2 year−1.​
(63)
The corresponding censoring-rate estimate is 0.1 year−1. This correction is consistent under the independent exponential competition model: the death-only conditional rate estimates s and the observed fraction estimates λ/s.
There is an important distinction from the full-data maximum-likelihood estimate. If TD​ and TC​ are total observed follow-up for the 100 deaths and 50 censored subjects, the full likelihood for both rates is
L(λ,γ)∝λ100γ50e−(λ+γ)(TD​+TC​),λfull​=TD​+TC​100​.​
(64)
The reported death-only rate determines TD​=100/0.3, but supplies no TC​, so the exact full-data estimate cannot be reconstructed from the numerical summaries alone. The value 0.2 is the requested corrected rate using those summaries; it need not equal the realised full-data maximum-likelihood estimate. The full analysis should use the actual censored follow-up whenever available.
