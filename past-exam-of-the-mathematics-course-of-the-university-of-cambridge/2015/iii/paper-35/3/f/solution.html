Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2015 / iii / Paper 35 / 3 / f / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2015 iii Paper 35 3 f
OurBigBook.com Words: 234
Use one transition per time step in the printed discrete-time multi-state diagnosis model. Put a=δE​, b=δN​, and r=1−b. Let Ik​=S1k​∼Poisson(hk​(θ)) be the new infection count. Let Uk​ be the undiagnosed non-early count present in state 3 at step k, including those retained by its self-loop. Start with I0​=U0​=0.
For the realised random variables, define conditionally
Ek​∣Ik−1​∼Binomial(Ik−1​,a),Dk​∣Uk−1​∼Binomial(Uk−1​,b),
(25)
using independent individual transition choices, and set
S2k​=Ek​,Uk​=Ik−1​−Ek​+Uk−1​−Dk​,S4k​=Dk​.​
(26)
The early split's two complementary counts are not independent conditional on Ik−1​, nor are the late split's two counts conditional on Uk−1​. Random state incidences must not be equated to their expectations.
Explicitly, the first four realised vectors are
S1​S2​S3​S4​​=(I1​,0,0,0),=(I2​,E2​,I1​−E2​,0),=(I3​,E3​,I2​−E3​+I1​−E2​−D3​,D3​),=(I4​,E4​,I3​−E4​+I2​−E3​+I1​−E2​−D3​−D4​,D4​).​
(27)
Here Ek​ and Dk​ have the conditional laws just given; D1​=D2​=0. The corresponding expressions solely in terms of the parameters are the mean flow vectors. Writing hj​=hj​(θ) and interpreting state 3 with its self-loop as S3k​=Uk​, they are
ES1​ES2​ES3​ES4​​=(h1​,0,0,0),=(h2​,ah1​,(1−a)h1​,0),=(h3​,ah2​,(1−a)(h2​+rh1​),b(1−a)h1​),=(h4​,ah3​,(1−a)(h3​+rh2​+r2h1​),b(1−a)(h2​+rh1​)).​
(28)
These follow from EUk​=(1−a)hk−1​+rEUk−1​. They make the minimum one-step early delay and two-step late delay explicit. If “entering state 3” is reserved for first entry only, its incidence is Ik−1​−Ek​ with mean (1−a)hk−1​; Uk​ is then a separate occupancy variable. The late-diagnosis expressions are unchanged. This distinction resolves the wording's use of incidence alongside a state-3 self-loop.
