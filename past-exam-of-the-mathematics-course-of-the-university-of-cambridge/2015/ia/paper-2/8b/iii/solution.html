Suppose v=αe1​+βe2​. Applying M−λ2​I would give e2​=α(λ1​−λ2​)e1​, contradicting linear independence of the two eigenvectors. Therefore the generalized eigenvector v lies outside their span and (e1​,e2​,v) is a basis. Write x=ae1​+be2​+dv. Since Mv=λ2​v+e2​, the linear system of ordinary differential equations gives
a˙=λ1​a,d˙=λ2​d,b˙=λ2​b+d.
(21)
Solving these equations gives a=A1​eλ1​t, d=A3​eλ2​t and b=(A2​+A3​t)eλ2​t. Thus
x(t)=A1​eλ1​te1​+eλ2​t[A2​e2​+A3​(v+te2​)].​
(22)
The length-two Jordan chain solution for the Jordan chain (e2​,v) produces the teλ2​t mode. The three arbitrary constants span all initial data, so this is the full general solution.
