The chain rule gives ∂x​=∂ξ​+β∂η​ and ∂y​=α∂ξ​+∂η​. The coefficients of uξξ​ and uηη​ are 2+3α2−7α and 2β2+3−7β. Their integer roots are α=2 and β=3. Thus choose characteristic coordinates ξ=x+2y, η=3x+y, whose Jacobian determinant is −5=0. The coefficient of uξη​ is 4β+6α−7(1+αβ)=−25, so the equation becomes −25uξη​=0, equivalently uξη​=0.
Integrating the transformed hyperbolic partial differential equation gives u=F(ξ)+G(η). Along x=−2y the coordinates are (0,−5y), so the zero boundary condition gives G(η)=−F(0) for every real η. Along x=0 they are (2y,y), so F(2y)−F(0)=4y2, hence F(ξ)=ξ2+F(0). The constants cancel and the unique resulting solution is
u(x,y)=(x+2y)2.​
