The integrating factor is e−2x, so (e−2xy)′=e(λ−2)x. Integrating gives the general solution
y=Ce2x+λ−2eλx​(λ=2).​
(1)
The particular solution is only defined up to a homogeneous solution. Subtract e2x/(λ−2) from it, absorbing that multiple into the arbitrary constant. The same family can then be written
y=Ce2x+λ−2eλx−e2x​.
(2)
Holding C fixed, the quotient tends to xe2x, the derivative of eλx with respect to λ at 2. Thus the resonant family is y=e2x(C+x). Direct differentiation gives y′−2y=e2x, so it is the full general solution at resonance. This resonant exponential forcing in a first-order equation limit concerns a reparametrized family, not a fixed value of the original divergent constant C. The initial condition gives C=2e−2−1, hence
y(x)=e2x(x−1+2e−2).​
(3)
