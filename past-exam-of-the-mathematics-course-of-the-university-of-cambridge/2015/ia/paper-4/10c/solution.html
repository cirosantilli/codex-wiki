The Lorentz force in the uniform magnetic field gives
L˙=r×mv˙=qr×(v×B)=q[(r⋅B)v−(r⋅v)B],
(30)
where the last step is the vector triple product identity. Hence
dtd​(L⋅B)=q[(r⋅B)(v⋅B)−B2(r⋅v)].
(31)
On the other hand, ∣r×B∣2=B2r2−(r⋅B)2, so
dtd​(2q​∣r×B∣2)=q[B2(r⋅v)−(r⋅B)(v⋅B)].
(32)
The derivatives cancel, proving the magnetic angular-momentum invariant
L⋅B+2q​∣r×B∣2=constant.​
(33)
The magnetic Lorentz force is perpendicular to velocity, so its power is zero:
T˙=mv⋅v˙=qv⋅(v×B)=0.
(34)
Thus the kinetic energy is constant.
For the alternative kinetic energy expression, work on an interval where r>0 so that the radial unit vector u=r/r is defined. Differentiating u⋅u=1 once and twice gives
u⋅u˙=0,u⋅u¨=−∣u˙∣2.
(35)
Since v=r˙u+ru˙, it follows that u⋅v=r˙ and v2=r˙2+r2∣u˙∣2. Therefore the unit-direction kinetic-energy identity is
T=2m​u⋅[(u⋅v)v−r2u¨]=2m​v2.​
(36)
This identity holds for any twice differentiable trajectory away from the origin, not only for motion in a magnetic field.
