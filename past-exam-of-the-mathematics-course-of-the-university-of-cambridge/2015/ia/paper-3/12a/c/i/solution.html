True. In all four tests, an array is understood to have components specified in each rotated orthonormal basis; being a scalar means that the contraction is invariant under those changes. Write the Frobenius inner product as A:B=∑ij​Aij​Bij​. Every test Cartesian second-rank tensor obeys B′=RBRT. The assumption says
A′:(RBRT)=A:Bfor every B,hence(RTA′R−A):B=0for every B.
(69)
Choosing the elementary matrices as B, or choosing B=RTA′R−A, gives RTA′R=A. Therefore
A′=RART,​
(70)
which is the required Cartesian second-rank tensor transformation law. This is the scalar contraction test for a Cartesian tensor.
