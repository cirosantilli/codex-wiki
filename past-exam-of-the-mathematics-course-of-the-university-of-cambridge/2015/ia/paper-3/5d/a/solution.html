The identity permutation fixes every multivariate polynomial. With permutation composition (στ)(i)=σ(τ(i)), substitution gives
[σ⋅(τ⋅f)](x1​,…,xn​)=f(xσ(τ(1))​,…,xσ(τ(n))​)=[(στ)⋅f](x1​,…,xn​).
(16)
Thus this is a left variable-permutation action on polynomials. The substitution acts on variable names; using the displayed definition avoids reversing the composition order.
For the given multivariate polynomial, the group orbit corresponds to the three partitions of four indices into two unordered pairs:
Orb(f)={x1​x2​+x3​x4​, x1​x3​+x2​x4​, x1​x4​+x2​x3​}.​
(17)
These three polynomials are distinct, and every pairing occurs under a permutation. The orbit-stabilizer theorem gives
∣(S4​)f​∣=24/3=8.​
(18)
The eight elements of the stabiliser subgroup independently interchange the members of each pair, and may also interchange the two pairs.
