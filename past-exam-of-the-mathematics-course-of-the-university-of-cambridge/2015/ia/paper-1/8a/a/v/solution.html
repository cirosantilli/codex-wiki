 ... 2015 ia Paper 1 8A a v
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
Start with the assumed basis of eigenvectors, grouping them by eigenvalue. In each eigenspace, apply the Gram-Schmidt process, which replaces a linearly independent list by an orthonormal list with the same span. Every new vector stays within that eigenspace, since an eigenspace is a vector subspace.
By part (iv), different eigenspaces are orthogonal. The combined lists therefore form an orthonormal basis of the entire space, still consisting of eigenvectors. Let U have these vectors as columns. Then U is a unitary matrix, so
U−1=U†,U†AU=diag(λ1​,…,λn​).​
(39)
This gives the required unitary diagonalization of a normal matrix, including repeated eigenvalues.
