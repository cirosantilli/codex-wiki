Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2015 / ii / Paper 2 / 17F / iii / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2015 ii Paper 2 17F iii
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
Write K=Q(μm​), L=Q(μn​). For coprime m,n, Bézout's identity and powers of a primitive mn-th root give KL=Q(μmn​). The degrees of these cyclotomic fields are φ(m), φ(n) and φ(mn)=φ(m)φ(n). The natural restriction map
Gal(KL/Q)⟶Gal(K/Q)×Gal(L/Q)
(59)
is injective and, by these orders, bijective. Under this product description the subgroups fixing K and L are the two complementary factors. Together they generate the whole group, so their common fixed field is Q. By the Fundamental theorem of Galois theory, K∩L=Q. This also includes m=1 or n=1.
