The recursive definition of ordinal addition is α+0=α, α+(β+1)=(α+β)+1, and α+λ=supβ<λ​(α+β) for a nonzero limit ordinal λ. The synthetic definition is the order type of the ordered disjoint union of a copy of α followed by a copy of β.
For the synthetic operation, adding the empty order does nothing, adding a successor appends one final point, and concatenation with a limit order is the increasing union of the initial concatenations with all its proper initial segments. Their order types have supremum supβ<λ​(α+β). Thus the synthetic operation satisfies the recursive equations. Transfinite induction gives uniqueness of the recursively defined operation, proving equivalence.
