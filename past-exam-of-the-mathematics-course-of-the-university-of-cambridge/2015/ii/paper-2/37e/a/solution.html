 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2015 ii Paper 2 37E a
OurBigBook.com Words: 142
Order the interior grid values into a vector. The coefficient matrix has diagonal 4+ch2 and off-diagonal entries −1 at horizontal and vertical neighbours, with boundary values already zero. Every row is strictly diagonally dominant because c>0. The standard convergence theorem for strict diagonal dominance gives convergence of both the Jacobi method and the Gauss-Seidel method. The matrix is also symmetric positive definite.
For D=(4+ch2)I, the weighted Jacobi method is u(r+1)=u(r)+ωD−1(b−Au(r)), with iteration matrix Hω​=I−ωD−1A. The sine-product grid eigenvectors give
λk,l​=1−ω4+ch24{sin2(πkh/2)+sin2(πlh/2)}+ch2​,1≤k,l≤m.​
(131)
These weighted Jacobi eigenvalues for a square Dirichlet grid determine convergence. All eigenvalues of A are positive. Thus for fixed h convergence requires 0<ω<2(4+ch2)/amax​, where amax​=8cos2(πh/2)+ch2. As h↓0, this upper bound tends to 1 from above. Any fixed ω>1 therefore fails on sufficiently fine grids; ω≤0 also fails. Consequently 0<ω≤1 is necessary for all mesh sizes, and it is sufficient, since amax​<2(4+ch2) for every finite grid.
