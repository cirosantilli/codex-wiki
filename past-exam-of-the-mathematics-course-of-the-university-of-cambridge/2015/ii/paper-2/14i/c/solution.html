The binomial upper bound for a Ramsey number gives R(3,t)≤(2t+1​)=O(t2). For the triangle-free Ramsey lower bound by alteration, a binomial random graph G(n,p) has expected triangle count (3n​)p3 and expected independent t-set count (tn​)(1−p)(2t​). Choose a realization whose sum of these counts is at most its expectation. Delete one vertex from each surviving forbidden set, iterating through the originally listed sets. The remaining graph has neither a triangle nor an independent t-set. This random alteration method proves
R(3,t)>n−(3n​)p3−(tn​)(1−p)(2t​).
(48)

log(en/t)−2p(t−1)​≤21​logt−23​loglogt+O(1)−2logt+o(1),

which tends to −∞. That term is eventually below 1. Thus R(3,t)>5n/6−1 and
R(3,t)=Ω((t/logt)3/2).​
