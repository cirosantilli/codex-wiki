Put s=−14​. Then OK​=Z[s] and the field discriminant is −56. Reduction of X2+14 modulo 2 and 3 gives
(2)=p22​, p2​=(2,s);(3)=p3​p​3​, p3​=(3,s−1), p​3​=(3,s+1).​
(56)
Their ideal norms are 2,3,3, respectively. The Minkowski bound for ideal classes is (2/π)56​<5, so every ideal class has an integral representative of norm at most 4. The only possible representatives are the unit ideal, p2​, p3​, p​3​, and the norm-4 ideal p22​=(2). Thus there are at most four classes.
The class of p2​ is nontrivial, since a principal ideal of norm 2 would require x2+14y2=2. Also p32​ is not principal: norm 9 forces x+ys=±3, whose ideal is p3​p​3​, not p32​.
Now N(2+s)=18. It is divisible by p2​ and p3​, but not by p​3​, as substitution of s=−1 modulo 3 gives 1. Unique ideal factorization and norms therefore give (2+s)=p2​p32​. Consequently [p3​]2=[p2​]−1=[p2​], a nontrivial class of order 2. The class of p3​ has order 4, and the four-class bound is attained:
Cl(Q(−14​))≅C4​, generated by [(3,−14​−1)].​
(57)
