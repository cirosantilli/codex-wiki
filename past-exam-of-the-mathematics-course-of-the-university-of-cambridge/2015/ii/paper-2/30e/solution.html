A Schwartz function is bounded and uniformly continuous. Given a tolerance, first choose R so the tail integral of ∣ϕ∣ is small. On ∣y∣≤R, uniform continuity makes the difference uniformly small for every x when εR is small. On the tail it is bounded by 2∥f∥∞​. This proves uniform convergence, without assuming ϕ≥0, and is the approximate identity argument.
