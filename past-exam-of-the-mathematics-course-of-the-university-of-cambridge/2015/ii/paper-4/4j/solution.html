Colour is a categorical variable, not a numerical trend. Width has the same slope for every colour, since no interaction term is fitted. The parameters are estimated by maximum likelihood estimation.
The approximate Wald confidence interval for the width coefficient is 0.46±1.96(0.106)​, or approximately (0.2522,0.6678). This uses the approximate normal distribution of the maximum-likelihood estimator, rather than a confidence interval for an individual binary response.
