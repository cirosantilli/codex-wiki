Regard complex matrices as a real vector space of dimension 2n2 and Hermitian matrices as a real vector space of dimension n2. Set F(A)=AA∗. Its derivative is DFA​(H)=HA∗+AH∗. At a unitary A, any Hermitian S is reached by H=SA/2. Hence I is a regular value, and the regular level set theorem makes U(n)=F−1(I) a smooth real manifold of dimension n2​.
The tangent space is the kernel of this derivative. Equivalently,
H∈TA​U(n)⟺A∗H+H∗A=0⟺H=AK with K∗=−K.​
(42)
At I, therefore, H is skew-Hermitian. The explicit geodesic with initial point I and velocity H is γ(t)=etH. The matrix exponential is unitary because etH∗etH=I, and γ˙​(0)=H. Its acceleration is γH2. Since H2 is Hermitian and every tangent vector at γ is γK with K skew-Hermitian, the induced real inner product satisfies
⟨γH2,γK⟩=Retr(H2K∗)=0.
(43)
Indeed the trace of a Hermitian matrix times a skew-Hermitian matrix is purely imaginary. Its acceleration is therefore always normal to the manifold, which is the geodesic equation for an induced Euclidean metric. Diagonalizing H=Wdiag(iλj​)W∗ gives the equivalent explicit construction Wdiag(eitλj​)W∗.
