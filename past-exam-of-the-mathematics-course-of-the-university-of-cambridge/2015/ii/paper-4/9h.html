The Chinese remainder theorem says that for pairwise coprime positive moduli ni​, arbitrary residue classes modulo each ni​ specify one residue class modulo ∏i​ni​. For an odd positive integer N=∏piei​​, the Jacobi symbol is (a/N)=∏i​(a/pi​)ei​, where each factor is a Legendre symbol; for N=1 the empty product is one. The Jacobi symbol is multiplicative in a, and is zero exactly when a and N have a common prime factor.
