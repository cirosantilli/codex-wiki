Linearizing the shallow water equations about depth h at rest gives wave speed c=gh​. Replacing h by c in the nonlinear equations yields ct​+ucx​+(c/2)ux​=0 and ut​+uux​+2ccx​=0. Direct addition and subtraction give
[∂t​+(u±c)∂x​][u±2(c−c0​)]=0.
(88)
Thus the Riemann invariants J±​=u±2(c−c0​) are constant on the characteristics C±​ with speeds u±c. In this problem c0​=gh0​​.
