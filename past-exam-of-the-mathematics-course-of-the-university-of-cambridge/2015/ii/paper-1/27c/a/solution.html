For Rez>0, the gamma function is
Γ(z)=∫0∞​tz−1e−tdt.
(59)
Rotate the integration ray through π/2, or insert a damping factor and then let it vanish. With the principal power, contour rotation gives
∫0∞​tγ−1eitdt=eiπγ/2Γ(γ),0<γ<1​.
(60)
For the damping argument, ∫tγ−1e−(ϵ−i)tdt=Γ(γ)(ϵ−i)−γ, and Dirichlet tail estimates justify the limit. The condition γ>0 ensures integrability at zero; γ<1 makes the amplitude tend to zero and permits conditional convergence at infinity. At γ=1, the upper-end oscillation has no limit; larger γ also fails ordinary improper convergence. These restrictions concern this improper integral, not its analytic regularizations.
