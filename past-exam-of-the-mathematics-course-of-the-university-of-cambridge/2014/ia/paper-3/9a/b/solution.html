 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2014 ia Paper 3 9A b
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
At every regular point of the double circular cone, its normal is perpendicular to F by part (a). Therefore the integrand of the flux integral vanishes pointwise:
∫S​F⋅dS=0.​
(35)
The vertex, if included, is a single point of zero surface area and does not alter the surface integral. The answer is independent of both the subset and its orientation. Tangency, rather than a cancellation between different parts of the surface, explains the zero flux.
