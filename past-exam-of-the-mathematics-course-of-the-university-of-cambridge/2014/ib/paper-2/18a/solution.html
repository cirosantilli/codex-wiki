For a conductor in electrostatic equilibrium, the interior field is zero and the surface is equipotential. The tangential exterior field is zero. A thin Gaussian pillbox gives the normal jump Eout​⋅n−Ein​⋅n=σ/ϵ0​. Thus, in vacuum and with n pointing out of the conductor,
Eout​=ϵ0​σ​n.​

These are the electrostatic boundary conditions at a conductor.
