True. Boundedness follows from ∥x+y∥≤∥x∥+∥y∥. To prove closedness of the Minkowski sum, let zj​=xj​+yj​∈X+Y converge to z. By the Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem, the bounded sequence xj​ has a subsequence converging to x. Closedness gives x∈X. Along that subsequence, yj​=zj​−xj​→z−x, which belongs to Y by closedness. Thus z=x+(z−x)∈X+Y. This proves that the sum is bounded and closed.
