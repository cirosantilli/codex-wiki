Use the ideal long-solenoid approximation: negligible pitch and end effects, vacuum permeability μ0​, and negligible displacement current. By cylindrical symmetry the magnetic field is axial. An Ampère's law rectangular loop with one axial side inside and the other outside gives Bin​−Bout​=μ0​NI. A loop wholly outside shows the outside axial field is constant with radius; requiring it to vanish far away gives
Bin​=μ0​NIz,Bout​=0.​
(17)
The axial sign is determined by the winding direction and the right-hand rule. The small axial component of current from the spiral pitch is neglected in this idealization.
There are Nl turns, and the magnetic flux through each is πa2μ0​NI. The flux linkage is LI, so the self-inductance is
L=μ0​N2πa2l.​
(18)
The induced electromotive force opposes increasing current by Faraday's law. Together with Ohm's law, the circuit equation is LI˙+RI=E0​. Solving this first-order ordinary differential equation with I(0)=0 gives
I(t)=RE0​​(1−e−Rt/L),t≥0.​
(19)
The time constant is L/R, and the limiting current is E0​/R.
