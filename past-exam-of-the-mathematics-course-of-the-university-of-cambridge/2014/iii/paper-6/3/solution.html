The Riesz-Markov-Kakutani representation theorem says that a positive linear functional on C(K) has the form f↦∫K​fdν for a unique finite positive regular Borel measure ν, with norm ν(K). Every finite Borel measure on [0,1] is regular, so the given μ belongs to the dual unit ball of C[0,1]. Apply Banach-Alaoglu theorem and Krein-Milman theorem to that ball.
