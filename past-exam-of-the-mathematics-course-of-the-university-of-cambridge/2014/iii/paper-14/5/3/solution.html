Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2014 / iii / Paper 14 / 5 / 3 / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2014 iii Paper 14 5 3
OurBigBook.com Words: 267
The dual Thurston polytope is the polar of the Thurston norm unit ball. More intrinsically, in the real dual of H2​(Y,∂Y) it is
BT​(Y)={α:∣α(u)∣≤∥u∥T​ for every u}.
(21)
The pairing can be regarded as evaluation of H2(Y,∂Y;R) on relative homology. Its definition remains valid when the Thurston norm has a kernel: the polytope then lies in the annihilator of that kernel.
Identify Y with the pair of pants product P×S1. A regular Seifert fiber has homology class h=μ1​+μ2​+μ3​. Let ei​ be the relative homology class corresponding by Poincare-Lefschetz duality to the homomorphism taking the ith meridian to one and the other two to zero. A spanning disk for L1​ punctured once by each of L2​,L3​ is an embedded pair of pants F representing e1​, with c(F)=1.
Take two arcs in P, one joining boundary one to boundary two, the other joining boundary one to boundary three. Their products with S1 are embedded vertical surfaces in a Seifert fibered space, namely annuli A12​,A13​. Orient them so that their relative classes are e1​−e2​ and e1​−e3​. They cost zero. Thus the three required inequalities, with αi​=α(ei​), are
∣α1​∣≤1,∣α1​−α2​∣≤0,∣α1​−α3​∣≤0.
(22)
For completeness they give the whole polytope. Oriented cut-and-paste of copies of F,A12​,A13​ gives the upper bound ∥ae1​+be2​+ce3​∥T​≤∣a+b+c∣. For the reverse bound, compress a minimizing surface and use the classification of incompressible surfaces in Seifert fibered spaces. Its horizontal components cover P and have negative Euler characteristic equal to their unsigned covering degree; its vertical components have zero cost and zero intersection with a regular Seifert fiber. The total signed horizontal degree is a+b+c, so its cost is at least ∣a+b+c∣. Hence
∥ae1​+be2​+ce3​∥T​=∣a+b+c∣,BT​(Y)={(s,s,s):−1≤s≤1}.​
(23)
It is a line segment, because this Thurston norm has a two-dimensional kernel.
