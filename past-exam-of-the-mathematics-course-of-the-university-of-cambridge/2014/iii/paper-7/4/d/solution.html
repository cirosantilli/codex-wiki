Put u=FN​/γN​, where the Gaussian density is strictly positive. Extend ulogu continuously at zero by 0log0=0. Both densities have integral one, so the relative entropy can be written as
HN​(FN​)​=∫RN​γN​ulogudv=∫RN​γN​(ulogu−u+1)dv.​
(42)
The bracket is nonnegative and vanishes only at u=1: its derivative for u>0 is logu, with a unique minimum at one. Hence
HN​(FN​)≥0,HN​(FN​)=0 ⟺ FN​=γN​ a.e.​
(43)
The inequality holds also for infinite entropy. Its negative integrand part is integrable, since ulogu≥−1/e and γN​ has integral one, so the extended-value integral is well defined. This is relative entropy in Kac's model; no differentiation is needed for nonnegativity.
