Use the correct Gaussian entropy decomposition
HN​(F)=∫FlogFdv+2N​log(2π)+21​∫∣v∣2Fdv.

The last coefficient is 1/2, as follows from −logγN​=Nlog(2π)/2+∣v∣2/2; the printed hint omits it. Under the allowed differentiability and integrability assumptions, the supplied collision invariants conserve mass and energy. Differentiating therefore gives
dtd​HN​(F)=N∫(Q−I)FlogFdv,
(45)
where the extra derivative term ∫∂t​F vanishes by mass conservation. Thus the Kac entropy production is
DN​(F)=2πCN​N​∑i<j​∫RN​∫02π​(F−F∘Rij,θ​)logFdθdv.
(46)
For one pair, call the double integral Aij​. The measure-preserving substitution (v,θ)↦(Rij,θ​v,−θ) interchanges F and F∘Rij,θ​, with angles taken modulo 2π. Averaging the original and substituted expressions gives
Aij​=21​∫RN​∫02π​(F∘Rij,θ​−F)(log(F∘Rij,θ​)−logF)dθdv.
(47)
Since N/(4πCN​)=1/[2π(N−1)], the desired Kac entropy dissipation formula is
DN​(F)=2π(N−1)1​i<j∑​∫RN​∫02π​(F(Rij,θ​v)−F(v))logF(v)F(Rij,θ​v)​dθdv≥0.​
(48)
For positive values a,b, (a−b)(loga−logb)≥0 because the logarithm is increasing. At two zeros use value zero; at one zero and one positive value use the nonnegative extended value +∞. One may first use positive densities and then regularize by (F+εγN​)/(1+ε); rotation invariance of γN​ preserves the formula and permits the usual limit at zeros under the stated assumptions.
Thus relative entropy is nonincreasing along the evolution. When the dissipation is finite, zero dissipation means pairwise rotation invariance and hence radiality, by the preceding kernel argument. Radial normalized densities other than γN​ can be stationary with positive relative entropy: vanishing dissipation is not a claim that the unique stationary density is Gaussian.
