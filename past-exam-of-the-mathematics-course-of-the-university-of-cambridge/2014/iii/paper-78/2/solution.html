Choose U and Q as the speed and source strength in the same kinematic velocity convention. With r=(x2+y2)1/2 and ϑ=arg(x+iy), superposition of uniform potential flow and a two-dimensional point source gives
Φ=Ux+2πQ​logr,ψ=Uy+2πQ​ϑ,​
(8)
where (u,v)=∇Φ=(ψy​,−ψx​). The angular branch cut records the volume flux from the source; the logarithm's arbitrary reference length only changes an additive constant in the velocity potential.
The stagnation point lies on the negative axis, where u=U+Q/(2πx)=0. Thus the limiting upstream reach of source fluid is
xs​=−2πUQ​=−πb​,b=2UQ​.​
(9)
The stagnation point is approached asymptotically, rather than reached in finite time by a parcel on the upstream axis. The dividing streamlines through it have ψ=±Q/2 when the angle is taken in (−π,π). Far downstream ϑ→0, so these streamlines approach y=±Q/(2U). Consequently the source fluid occupies the Rankine half-body between them, with downstream width 2b=Q/U. In the upper half-plane its boundary can also be parametrized by y=b(1−ϑ/π), x=ycotϑ, 0<ϑ<π.
For arrival at the downstream axis, the direct streamline y=0, x>0 has speed U+Q/(2πx). Its transit time from the ideal point source is
Ts​(x)=∫0x​U+Q/(2πX)dX​=Ux​−2πU2Q​log(1+Q2πUx​).
(10)
The first source fluid at a downstream cross-section lies on this symmetry axis: for positive x, the horizontal source contribution Qx/[2π(x2+y2)] is maximal at y=0, and off-axis paths cannot advance through positive x more quickly. A far-away parcel passing the inlet line at the same release time has transit time x/U. The time advance is therefore
τ=Ux​−Ts​(x)=πUb​log(1+bπx​).​
(11)
This is an advance, not the source parcel's absolute transit time. If a source parcel is released at time t0​ while the distant comparison parcel crosses x=0 at time zero, the advance relative to that parcel is τ−t0​; the printed formula uses simultaneous releases. If U,Q instead denote Darcy velocity and bulk-area source flux in a medium of uniform porosity ϕ, their ratio still gives the same b, but both parcel times acquire a factor ϕ. No porosity is specified here, so the displayed time formula fixes the kinematic convention.
