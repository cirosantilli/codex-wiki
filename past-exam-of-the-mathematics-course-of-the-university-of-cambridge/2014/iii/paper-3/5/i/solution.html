For a finitely generated associative algebra A, the representation variety of an associative algebra consists of generator matrices satisfying all defining polynomial relations. With fixed orthogonal idempotents 1=∑i=1m​ei​, require a representation on ⨁i​kni​ to send ei​ to the standard vertex projector. This is the meaning of RepA​(n); without specified idempotents, use the usual single dimension r and RepA​(r)=Homk-alg​(A,Mr​(k)). Polynomial relations cut out a closed affine variety in the space of generator matrices.
The group G=∏i​GLni​​(k) acts by conjugation, preserving the prescribed projectors. Its stabilizer is AutA​(X), a nonempty open subset of EndA​(X). The orbit dimension formula consequently gives
dimG−dimOX​=dimStabG​(x)=dimAutA​(X)=dimEndA​(X)​.

A degeneration of a module X to Y means that OY​⊆OX​​, equivalently that one representative of Y lies in this closure.
For 0→X′→X→X′′→0, choose a vector-space splitting, so every generator has block matrix xg​=(xg′​0​bg​xg′′​​). Conjugation by diag(tI,I), t=0, gives
xg​(t)=(xg′​0​tbg​xg′′​​).
(12)
This polynomial family extends to t=0, retains all algebra relations, and at zero represents X′⊕X′′. Thus splitting an extension gives a module degeneration. Iterating along a composition series gives X⇝grX=⨁j​Xj​/Xj−1​. Degenerations are transitive because an orbit closure is closed and invariant under base change.

The action is A↦g2​Ag1−1​. Its rank orbits of a matrix under left-right multiplication are indexed by r=0,…,min(n1​,n2​), with a representative containing an r×r identity block and zeros elsewhere. Their dimensions are r(n1​+n2​−r); their closures contain exactly matrices of rank at most r.
