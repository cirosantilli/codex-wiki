Set z=z1−α​. Under the null hypothesis rejection requires both W1​≥f and W2​>z. Since W2​ has the standard normal distribution,
P0​(reject)=α−P0​(W1​<f, W2​>z).
(8)
The bivariate normal distribution in the preceding calculation has a nonsingular covariance matrix and strictly positive statistical probability density everywhere. For every finite futility boundary f and 0<α<1, the open rectangle {W1​<f,W2​>z} has positive probability. Therefore
0<P0​(reject)<α.​
(9)
For an explicit expression, conditional on W1​=w the full-sample statistic is N(w/2​,1/2) under the null hypothesis, yielding
P0​(reject)=∫f∞​ϕ(w)[1−Φ(2​z−w)]dw.
(10)
The Type I error is reduced because some otherwise rejecting paths stop for futility. Equality is approached as f→−∞, but does not hold at a finite futility boundary.
