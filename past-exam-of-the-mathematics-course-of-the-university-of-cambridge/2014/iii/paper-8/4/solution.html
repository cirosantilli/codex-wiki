Use the standard setting of a compact metrizable convex set K in a Hausdorff locally convex real topological vector space, with its metrizable topology. Write A(K) for the continuous affine real functions on K. The affine upper envelope of a bounded real function is

For the real L∞ example, give its closed unit ball K the weak-star topology σ(L∞,L1), not the norm topology. The Banach-Alaoglu theorem makes it compact, and separability of L1[0,1] makes this ball metrizable. Its extreme points are exactly the classes h satisfying ∣h∣=1 almost everywhere. Indeed, if ∣h∣≤1−ε on a positive-measure set, adding and subtracting ε times its indicator decomposes h nontrivially inside the ball. Conversely, if ∣h∣=1 almost everywhere and h=(u+v)/2 with ∣u∣,∣v∣≤1, pointwise equality at the endpoints of [−1,1] forces u=v=h almost everywhere. This is the extreme-point criterion for the L-infinity unit ball.
