ρνˉ​≃2π2e−μν​/Tν​​∫0∞​p3e−p/Tν​dp=π23Tν4​​e−μν​/Tν​.

It is exponentially negligible. The neutrino degeneracy parameter ξν​=μν​/Tν​ is conserved in the assumed adiabatic massless evolution: redshifting preserves the distribution with both Tν​ and μν​ proportional to a−1.
There is a sign qualification in the printed absolute-value formula. If μν​<0, the distribution of neutrinos themselves is exponentially suppressed, and the antineutrinos, with positive chemical potential, form the degenerate sea. In either case the degenerate neutrino and antineutrino energy density is
ρν​+ρνˉ​≃8π2∣μν​∣4​​.
(12)
Thus the formula with ∣μν​∣ describes the dominant member of the pair, or their leading total, rather than the named neutrino distribution for both signs. No extra factor of two is present: only one member is densely occupied. Additional populated internal states multiply the result by their degeneracy.
