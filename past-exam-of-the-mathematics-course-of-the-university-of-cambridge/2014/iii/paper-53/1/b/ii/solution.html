Here the two derivative factors supply τ2, cancelling a2=1/(H2τ2). The vacuum prescription for inflationary in-in integrals can be implemented by a factor eδτ on the real negative axis, with δ>0, followed by δ↓0. In this notation
∫−∞0​e(δ−iK)τdτ=δ−iK1​⟶Ki​,∫−∞0​τe(δ−iK)τdτ=−(δ−iK)21​⟶K21​.

Therefore
∫C−​​(1+iki​τ)e−iKτdτ=i(K1​+K2ki​​).
(16)
An undamped boundary evaluation on the real axis at −∞ is not valid. On the upper contour the complex-conjugate integrand instead yields the conjugate value. Conjugating the modes without conjugating the contour would produce exponential growth.
Using all six connected Wick contractions from the preceding part and U=Ak1​​Ak2​​Ak3​​ gives the literal-Hamiltonian result
BC​(k1​,k2​,k3​)=16ϵMPl6​CH4​(k1​k2​k3​)31​i=1∑3​kj2​kl2​(K1​+K2ki​​).​
(17)
The connected correlator is (2π)3δ(3)(∑ki​)BC​. The three assignments constitute the curvature bispectrum from a zeta zeta-prime-squared interaction. The numerator contains six powers of H from the six modes, two of which are cancelled by a2; the six powers of MPl​ remain unless the vertex supplies two.
Consequently the printed final expression, with MPl−4​, is obtained for C=MPl2​​. With the literal printed Hamiltonian C=1, the answer instead has MPl−6​​. The Planck normalization of a cubic curvature interaction requires a factor MPl2​ in the Hamiltonian if its final bispectrum is intended. In addition, integrating the intermediate expression literally with only three assignments gives half the fully contracted amplitude. These are normalization defects, rather than changes in the momentum shape. For dimensionless comoving curvature perturbations, the usual interaction normalization is also required by the mass dimension of the action.
For the standard normalization, Pζ​(k)=H2/(4ϵMPl2​k3), so B/Pζ2​ is of order ϵ. This slow-roll suppression means that the primordial non-Gaussianity from this interaction alone is not expected to be detectably large. For example, in the squeezed bispectrum configuration k1​≪k2​≃k3​, matching this contribution to the local convention gives fNL​=5ϵ/24. This is a single-vertex contribution, not the complete single-field slow-roll inflation prediction; other vertices and field redefinitions contribute at the same slow-roll order. A claim of detectability for enhanced interactions requires a model beyond this approximation.
