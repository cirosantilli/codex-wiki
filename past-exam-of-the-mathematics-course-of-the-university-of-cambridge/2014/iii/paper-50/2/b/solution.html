Power is energy per time. In geometrized units, energy has the dimension of length and time is converted to length using c, so the dimensionless geometrized luminosity is
L⊙,geom​=c5GL⊙​​≃1.06×10−26.​
(14)
Equivalently, c5/G is the corresponding SI power unit. The lifetime for emitting the entire rest energy at the given constant luminosity is
tall​=L⊙​M⊙​c2​≃4.65×1020s=1.47×1013yr​.
(15)
The same result follows from M⊙(time)​/L⊙,geom​. The stipulated full-mass radiation time is of order 1013 years; this is the constant-luminosity energy budget asked for, not a stellar-evolution calculation.
