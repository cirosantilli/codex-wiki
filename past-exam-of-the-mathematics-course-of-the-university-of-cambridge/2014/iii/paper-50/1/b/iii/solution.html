The pushforward of a contravariant tensor applies dϕp​ to each of its r vector slots. Choose a basis ei​ of Tp​M and expand
T=Ti1​⋯ir​ei1​​⊗⋯⊗eir​​.
(6)
Its pushforward is Ti1​⋯ir​(dϕei1​​)⊗⋯⊗(dϕeir​​). Each factor is tangent to the hypersurface and fixed by P. Consequently
P⊗r(ϕ∗​T)=ϕ∗​T.​
(7)
The basis expansion proves the result for arbitrary tensors, rather than only a single decomposable tensor product.
