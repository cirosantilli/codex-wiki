Define the diffusion generator L=b∂x​+σ2∂xx​/2. Fix a horizon t and start the strong solution from the deterministic state x. The Itô formula applied to the time-reversed test function gives
du(t−s,Xs​)=[−ut​+Lu](t−s,Xs​)ds+σ(Xs​)ux​(t−s,Xs​)dWs​.
(43)
The drift vanishes by the Kolmogorov backward equation. This is initially a local martingale; localization on compact state/time sets justifies the stochastic integral without a global derivative bound. Since u itself is bounded, this local martingale is a true martingale on [0,t]. Its two endpoint expectations give
u(t,x)=Ex​[f(Xt​)].​
(44)
This is the bounded backward-equation stochastic representation. Starting the strong solution at deterministic x is the precise meaning of the conditional notation at X0​=x. Also f=u(0,⋅) is bounded, even though boundedness was not separately imposed on f.
