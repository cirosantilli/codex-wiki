Work over a ground model of ZFC+Generalized continuum hypothesis and let λ=ℵ3​. Force with finite binary partial functions on λ×ω, with extensions stronger. The Delta-system lemma thins any uncountable family of finite domains to an uncountable family with one common root. Only finitely many binary assignments on that root occur, so two conditions agree there and their union is a common extension. Hence the forcing has the countable chain condition for forcing and preserves cardinals and cofinalities.
The generic union yields λ distinct reals. Totality at each coordinate is dense, and for two different coordinates it is dense to assign different values at some unused natural-number position. Thus the extension satisfies 2ℵ0​≥λ.
A nice forcing name for a subset of ω1​ uses one countable forcing antichain at each ordinal below ω1​. Since the forcing has size λ, there are at most (λℵ0​)ℵ1​=λℵ1​ such forcing names. Ground Generalized continuum hypothesis gives λℵ1​=λ; for instance apply the Hausdorff formula at λ=ℵ2+​ and the Generalized continuum hypothesis arithmetic below it. Therefore 2ℵ1​≤λ in the extension. Combining the bounds gives
2ℵ0​=2ℵ1​=ℵ3​.​
(34)
The ordinal and cardinal ℵ3​ is the same in both models by the chain in a partial order condition. The forcing theorem formalizes this construction as the requested relative-consistency implication. A countable transitive ground model is a convenient presentation, not an additional consequence silently derived from mere consistency. This is the Cohen forcing two-level continuum plateau.
