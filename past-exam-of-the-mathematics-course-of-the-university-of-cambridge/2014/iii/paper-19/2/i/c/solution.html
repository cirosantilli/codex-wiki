The predicate is defined by the constructible hierarchy:
L0​=∅,Lα+1​=Def(Lα​),Lδ​=α<δ⋃​Lα​(δ limit).​
(10)
Here Def(A) consists of subsets of A definable in the set structure (A,∈) by a first-order formula with finitely many parameters from A. To express x∈Lα​ in the language of set theory, assert that α is an ordinal and there is a hierarchy history of length α+1 satisfying this recursion whose last stage contains x. Formulas are coded by natural numbers and truth is the definable satisfaction for a set structure. Transfinite recursion gives a unique history. The standard coding makes this predicate Δ1​ over ZF; thus the constructible-level absoluteness over ZF applies to transitive models of ZF.
