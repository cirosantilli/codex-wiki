König theorem for cardinal numbers states that if κi​<λi​ for each i∈I, then ∑i​κi​<∏i​λi​. Its cofinality formulation gives, for every infinite cardinal κ,
κcf(κ)>κ.​
(8)
Taking a cofinal sequence of smaller cardinals in κ and comparing their sum with the product of their successors gives this inequality. Another standard consequence, for infinite λ and κ≥2, is
cf(κλ)>λ,cf(2λ)>λ.​
(9)
Indeed, if μ=cf(κλ)≤λ, the preceding inequality at κλ would contradict (κλ)μ=κλμ=κλ.
