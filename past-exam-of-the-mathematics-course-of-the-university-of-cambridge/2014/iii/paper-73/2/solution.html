For positive Q,J, the phase plane nullclines are H=3QΓ/(2J) for ΓX​=0 and H=2QΓ/J for HX​=0. Above both lines, trajectories go left and upward; between them they go left and downward; below both they go right and downward. There is no positive-quadrant equilibrium. The axes are singular boundaries of this positive-flux reduction, not regular equilibria.
