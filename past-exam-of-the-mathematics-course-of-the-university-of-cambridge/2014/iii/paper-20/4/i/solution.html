Write C=[Cop,Set] and similarly for D. The geometric morphism induced by a functor has
f∗P=P∘Fop,f∗​Q=RanFop​Q.​
(28)
Precomposition preserves all pointwise limits and colimits, so in particular it preserves finite limits. The Right Kan extension exists because the categories are small and sets have all small limits; its universal property gives f∗⊣f∗​. Thus these functors define a geometric morphism C→D.
There is also f!​=LanFop​, a left Kan extension, with f!​⊣f∗. The Yoneda lemma identifies f!​(yC)≅y(FC), since for every P,
Hom(f!​yC,P)≅Hom(yC,f∗P)≅P(FC)≅Hom(y(FC),P).
(29)
