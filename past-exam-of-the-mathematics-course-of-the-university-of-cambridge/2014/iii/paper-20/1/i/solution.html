Write the comonad as (G,ϵ,δ) and its category of coalgebras for a comonad as EG. A coalgebra for a comonad is a map a:A→GA with ϵA​a=1A​ and δA​a=Gaa. A morphism f:(A,a)→(B,b) satisfies bf=Gfa. Let U:EG→E be the forgetful functor and let R(X)=(GX,δX​) be the cofree coalgebra. The adjunction U⊣R has the explicit correspondence
f:UA→X⟷Gfa:A→R(X).
(1)
We construct the three pieces of the elementary topos structure.
Because G preserves finite limits, each underlying finite limiting cone has a unique coalgebra structure induced by the structures on its vertices. The counit and coassociativity equations can be checked after its jointly monic projections. Thus U creates finite limits and reflects isomorphisms. A morphism of coalgebras is monic exactly when its underlying morphism is monic, by the diagonal criterion using the created pullback.
For exponentials in a coalgebra topos, fix coalgebras (A,a) and (B,b) and put E=BA in E. On the cofree coalgebra R(E) there is an underlying evaluation
e:GE×A⟶B,e=ev(ϵE​×1A​).
(2)
The two maps
be,Ge(δE​×a):GE×A⟶GB
(3)
use G(GE×A)≅GGE×GA in the second expression. Transpose them in E to maps r,s:GE⇉(GB)A, and then transpose across U⊣R to coalgebra morphisms r,s:R(E)⇉R((GB)A). Take their equalizer C in EG.
An underlying map UX×A→B corresponds to a coalgebra map h:X→R(E). The equation saying that the original map is a coalgebra morphism is precisely rh=sh, since δE​h=Ghx for the structure x:X→GX. By the cofree adjunction, this is equivalent to rh=sh, hence to unique factorization through C. Therefore
EG(X,C)≅EG(X×A,B),
(4)
naturally in X. This constructs the required exponential object.
For the subobject classifier of a coalgebra topos, let ⊤:1↪Ω be the underlying subobject classifier, and let κ:GΩ→Ω classify the mono G⊤:G1≅1↪GΩ. Its cofree transpose is the coalgebra endomorphism
k=GκδΩ​:RΩ⟶RΩ.
(5)
Define ΩG​ as the equalizer of k and 1RΩ​. The transpose of ⊤ factors through this equalizer and gives ⊤G​:1→ΩG​.
Indeed, for a subobject S↪UX classified by χ:UX→Ω, the pullback x−1(GS) has characteristic map κGχx. It is always contained in S, by naturality of the counit. Equality holds exactly when x restricts to a coalgebra structure on S; its axioms then follow by composing with the monomorphisms GS↪GX and GGS↪GGX. Under U⊣R, the classifying map becomes h=Gχx:X→RΩ. The equality of subobjects is ϵΩ​h=κh. Transposing this equality gives h=kh, so exactly the coalgebra subobjects correspond to maps X→ΩG​. Their pullback of ⊤G​ is the desired subcoalgebra, and uniqueness follows from uniqueness of χ.
Thus we have finite limits, exponentials and a subobject classifier:
EG is an elementary topos.​
(6)
The construction does not assume that G preserves the underlying exponentials or underlying subobject classifier.
