Rearranging the last two terms is exactly the requested −21​∑Ak​+∑Ek​(αk†​αk​+1/2) form. Each momentum appears in the sums, so opposite-momentum pairs are not counted again separately.
The Bogoliubov quasiparticle dispersion is linear at low momentum when Vk​→V0​>0:
Ek​≃ℏcs​∣k∣,cs​=nV0​/m​.​

It is a gapless phonon of the condensate, unlike the free-particle quadratic spectrum. For a contact repulsion Vk​=g>0, the healing length ξ=ℏ/2mng​ controls crossover to the particle regime Ek​≃ϵk​+ng. A momentum-dependent potential can modify the higher-energy curve; the sketch uses the contact case and does not assume every interaction has that entire shape.
