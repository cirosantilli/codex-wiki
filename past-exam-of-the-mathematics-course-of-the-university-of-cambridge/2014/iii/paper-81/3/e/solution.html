For smooth phase differences, −Jcos(ϕn+1​−ϕn​)=−J+J(ϕn+1​−ϕn​)2/2+⋯. After integrating the angular momenta in the same inverse-energy time coordinate τ, the coupled quantum rotor chain has the fluctuation action
S=∫0β​dτ∫dx[2ℏ2I​(∂τ​ϕ)2+2J​(∂x​ϕ)2].​
(23)
Unit spacing replaces the lattice sum and difference by their continuum limits. The constant energy −J per bond contributes an overall factor eβJN, suppressed when describing fluctuations. The phase is compact; the Gaussian action describes its smooth sector.
There is an imaginary-time normalization for a coupled rotor chain issue in the printed formula. Factoring out 1/ℏ2 from the action above places ℏ2J, not J, in its spatial bracket. The printed bracket is therefore correct in ℏ=1 units, or with its spatial coefficient redefined as Jbracket​=ℏ2JHamiltonian​. With the same dimensional J as in the Hamiltonian, it lacks that factor.
In physical real time the wave equation is I∂t2​ϕ=J∂x2​ϕ. The harmonic phase mode of a quantum rotor chain consequently has
ω(q)=J/I​∣q∣,E(q)=ℏJ/I​∣q∣.​
(24)
The lattice expression is ω(q)=2J/I​∣sin(q/2)∣. The mode is a gapless phase wave analogous to sound in a superfluid, with rotor angular momentum conjugate to its phase. In one dimension this harmonic regime has strong long-distance phase fluctuations rather than true continuous-symmetry long-range order; compact phase slips can change the infrared phase outside the smooth-phase approximation.
