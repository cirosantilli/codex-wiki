In the homogeneous spin-field sector, choose M=Me^z​. The fermionic levels become ϵk​−μ−σM, where σ=±1 is the Pauli eigenvalue. The fermionic one-level thermal determinant can be applied independently to every momentum and spin, while the field cost is βNM2/(2U). Hence the homogeneous spin-field effective action is
Seff​[M]=−k,σ∑​ln[1+e−β(ϵk​−μ−σM)]+2UβNM2​.​

The remaining homogeneous-field integral is Z≃∫DMe−Seff​ in this mean-field restriction. Orientation and fluctuation-independent measure factors do not alter the displayed extensive action or its stationary equation. The two bands are exchange split by 2M; M has energy units and is proportional to the spin polarization, rather than being an electron number itself.
