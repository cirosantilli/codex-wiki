Fix the sign convention by taking the proper two-point insertion to be iΣ(p). This convention matches the loop expression and mass conversion printed later. Dyson resummation of successive fermion self-energy insertions gives
iG=p−mi​+p−mi​(iΣ)p−mi​+⋯=p−m+ΣR​(p)i​​.
(15)
The subscript denotes the renormalized self-energy. If the insertion is instead named −iΣusual​, then Σusual​=−ΣR​ and the denominator is written p−m−Σusual​. These are the same physical convention.
Lorentz covariance allows ΣR​=A(p2)p+B(p2)m. The physical mass-shell condition is
[1+A(mphys2​)]mphys​−[1−B(mphys2​)]m=0.​
(16)
It locates the mass-shell singularity of the Dirac propagator. In infrared-regulated perturbation theory this is the pole mass; near a simple pole the quantum field theory propagator has the form iZpole​(p+mphys​)/(p2−mphys2​+i0). The mass and pole residue are different quantities: the former fixes the singularity's location, the latter the field normalization. The calculation below uses this standard perturbative pole-mass definition.
