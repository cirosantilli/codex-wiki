subject to the affine cone constraints
(t,u−g)∈Qn+1​,(2qi​+1​,2qi​−1​,ai​)∈Q3​,ai​≥0,ai​−(Gu)i​≥0(i=1,…,n),

where Qd​={(r,z):r≥∥z∥2​} is the second-order cone. All coordinates displayed inside the cone memberships are affine in the optimization variables, so stacking them has precisely the form Ax−b∈K. The first cone enforces t≥∥u−g∥2​, and each three-dimensional cone enforces qi​≥ai2​. The two scalar inequalities give ai​≥((Gu)i​)+​.
Every feasible lift therefore has objective at least the original objective. Conversely, for any u, choose ai​=((Gu)i​)+​, qi​=ai2​ and t=∥u−g∥2​ to attain equality. Thus the reformulation is exact. It is a second-order cone program over the product K=Qn+1​×Q3n​×R+2n​, a proper closed self-dual cone. It preserves the asymmetric derivative penalty; replacing it by ∥Gu∥2 would change the problem.
The canonical Lorentz-cone barrier is −log(r2−∥z∥2) on r>∥z∥, and each orthant coordinate contributes −logs. Their sum, composed with the affine slack map, is
B(u,a,q,t)=−log(t2−∥u−g∥2)−i∑​log(qi​−ai2​)−i∑​logai​−i∑​log(ai​−(Gu)i​).​
(36)
Its domain explicitly requires t>∥u−g∥, qi​>ai2​, ai​>0 and ai​>(Gu)i​; the positive Lorentz branch must not be inferred merely from positivity of a squared expression. The barrier parameter of the product-cone barrier is ν=4n+2​, with two per Lorentz block and one per scalar orthant slack. A strict feasible lift can always be obtained by choosing a above both bounds, q above a2, and t above the fidelity norm.
