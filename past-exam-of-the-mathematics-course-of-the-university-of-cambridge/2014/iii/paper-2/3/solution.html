(r−(p+q−r), (p+q−r)−(4−p−q)).

Starting with (1,2,4,3,7), subtracting Γ4,1​ leaves (0,1,2,2,5); subtracting Γ2,2​ leaves (0,0,1,1,3); then the two ten-dimensional modules leave a single copy of the dominant weight (1,1).
