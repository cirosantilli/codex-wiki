Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2018 / ii / Paper 4 / 25I / c / i / Solution

OurBigBook.com Words: 145
Suppose that a connected smooth surface S⊂R3 properly contains S. Since S and S have the same dimension, S is open in S. Choose a point of its relative boundary. Inside a sufficiently small compact normal neighbourhood in S, choose q∈S and a closest point p∈S∖S. A minimizing S-geodesic from q to p stays in S before its endpoint, since encountering the complement earlier would contradict the choice of p.
The restriction of this curve is consequently a geodesic of S with a finite maximal endpoint at p. On the other hand, the Gaussian curvature of S is the restriction of the smooth Gaussian curvature of S, so it remains bounded near p. This contradicts condition (∗). Hence
S=S​,
(224)
so S is an inextendible embedded surface. This is the curvature-blowup criterion for inextendibility of a surface.
