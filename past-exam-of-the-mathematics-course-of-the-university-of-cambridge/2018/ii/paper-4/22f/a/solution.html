OurBigBook.com Words: 133
Choose a countable dense subset {x1​,x2​,…} of the normed vector space X. For each fixed j, the scalar sequence (fn​(xj​)) is bounded because
∣fn​(xj​)∣≤∥fn​∥∥xj​∥≤∥xj​∥.
(169)
Repeated use of the Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem followed by a diagonal subsequence argument produces a subsequence (fn​)n∈Λ​ for which fn​(xj​) converges for every j.
Let D be the linear span of the xj​. On every x∈D, define
f(x)=limn∈Λn→∞​​fn​(x).
(170)
Finite linear combinations show that f:D→R is linear, and passage to the limit in ∣fn​(x)∣≤∥x∥ gives ∣f(x)∣≤∥x∥. Thus f is a bounded linear functional on the dense linear subspace D and extends uniquely to an element of the dual space X∗ with ∥f∥≤1.
For arbitrary x∈X and y∈D,
∣fn​(x)−f(x)∣≤∣fn​(x−y)∣+∣fn​(y)−f(y)∣+∣f(y−x)∣≤2∥x−y∥+∣fn​(y)−f(y)∣.
(171)
First choose y close to x, then let n∈Λ tend to infinity. This proves
fn​(x)⟶f(x)for every x∈X​.
(172)
This is the Sequential Banach-Alaoglu theorem for a separable predual.
