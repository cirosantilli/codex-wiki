Fix z0​∈S1, and let δz0​​∈C(S1)∗ be the point evaluation functional δz0​​(f)=f(z0​). Define orbit averages in the dual space by
μn​=n1​∑j=0n−1​(T∗)jδz0​​.
(180)
Each μn​ has norm at most one and satisfies μn​(1S1​)=1. The space C(S1) is separable, so part (a) gives a subsequence converging pointwise to some μ∈C(S1)∗. In particular,
μ(1S1​)=1.
(181)
For every f∈C(S1), the Cesaro average telescopes at the endpoints:
μn​(Tf)−μn​(f)=nδz0​​(Tnf)−δz0​​(f)​,
(182)
whose absolute value is at most 2∥f∥∞​/n. Passing to the convergent subsequence proves
μ(Tf)=μ(f)for every f∈C(S1)​.
(183)
Thus μ is an invariant functional of a composition operator.
It need not be unique when T is not the identity operator. For the nonidentity homeomorphism τ(z)=z, both 1 and −1 are fixed. Hence the two distinct point evaluations
δ1​(f)=f(1),δ−1​(f)=f(−1)
(184)
have value one on 1S1​ and are invariant under T. Therefore nonidentity does not imply uniqueness.
