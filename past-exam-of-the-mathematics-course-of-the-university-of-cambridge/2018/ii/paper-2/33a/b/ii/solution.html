(244)
Expanding it and differentiating L with respect to t gives
[L,A]ψ=−6ipx​ψx​+[−3ipxx​+qxxx​−4(q2)x​]ψ,

Lt​ψ=−2iqt​ψx​+(pt​−iqtx​)ψ.
(246)
Equating the coefficient of ψx​ in Lax's equation Lt​=[L,A] yields
qt​=3px​.
(247)
The imaginary scalar terms then cancel because qtx​=3pxx​, leaving
pt​=qxxx​−4(q2)x​.
(248)
Differentiate the first equation in t and the second in x to eliminate p. The third-order Boussinesq Lax pair therefore produces
qtt​=3(pt​)x​=3qxxxx​−12(q2)xx​.
(249)
Thus the requested Boussinesq equation has
a=3,b=−12.​
