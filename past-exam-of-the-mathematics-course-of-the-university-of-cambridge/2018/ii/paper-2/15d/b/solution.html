Quantum no-signalling says that, without communicating a measurement outcome, any local trace-preserving operation performed on subsystem A leaves all outcome probabilities for measurements on subsystem B unchanged; equivalently, it leaves the reduced density matrix ρB​ unchanged.
The two Bell states are orthogonal. Applying the inverse Bell-state preparation circuit, namely a Controlled-NOT gate from A to B followed by a Hadamard gate on A, gives
∣ϕ+⟩⟼∣00⟩,∣ϕ−⟩⟼∣10⟩.
