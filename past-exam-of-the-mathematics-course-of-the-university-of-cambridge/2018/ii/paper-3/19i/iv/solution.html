Let ∣G∣=pq with primes p>q and G nonabelian. The Sylow theorems give np​∣q and np​≡1(modp), so np​=1 and the Sylow p-subgroup P is normal. Also nq​∣p and nq​≡1(modq). If nq​=1, both Sylow subgroups are normal and G is their abelian direct product, a contradiction. Hence nq​=p, and
q∣p−1.​
(135)
Because G/P is abelian, the commutator subgroup G′ lies in P. It is nontrivial because G is nonabelian, and ∣P∣=p is prime, so G′=P. Therefore
∣G/G′∣=q,
(136)
and G has exactly q linear characters.
Every nonlinear irreducible degree divides pq. Its square is at most pq, so the only possibility is degree q. If there are b such characters, the sum of squares of irreducible degrees gives
pq=q+bq2,b=qp−1​.
(137)
The number of irreducible characters equals the number of conjugacy classes, so
k(G)=q+qp−1​.​
(138)
These are the irreducible characters of a nonabelian group of order p q.
