Subtracting eliminates the first term, so minimality forces h(cj​)=cj​ for every h and j. Thus every cj​∈F. The row for the identity automorphism then contradicts the F-linear independence of the xj​.
Therefore m≤n. Taking an F-basis of L gives [L:F]≤n, and hence
[L:LG]=∣G∣.​
