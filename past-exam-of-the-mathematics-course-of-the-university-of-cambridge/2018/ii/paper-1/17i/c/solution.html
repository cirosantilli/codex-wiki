First consider a Kt,t​-free bipartite graph with parts X,Y, each of size at most n, and m edges. Count pairs (S,y) in which S is a t-element subset of the graph neighbourhood of y∈Y. Any t vertices of X have at most t−1 common neighbours, since t common neighbours would form the complete bipartite graph Kt,t​. Consequently
∑y∈Y​(td(y)​)≤(t−1)(t∣X∣​).
(115)
If the average degree of a vertex is bounded in terms of t, then m=O(n) and the required result is immediate. Otherwise, convexity of x↦(tx​) and the preceding inequality give
∣Y∣(tm/∣Y∣​)=O(nt),
(116)
and hence m=O(n2−1/t). Every graph has a bipartite spanning subgraph containing at least half its edges, obtained by choosing a random bipartition. Applying the bipartite estimate to that subgraph proves the Kővári–Sós–Turán theorem in the required form:
 ex(n,Kt,t​)=O(n2−1/t). ​
(117)
The stated "nice" subsets of the finite cyclic group Zn​ are Sidon sets. If

then a+d=c+b. Niceness forces (a,b)=(c,d); the alternative pairing would force a=b. Thus the ∣A∣(∣A∣−1) ordered nonzero differences are distinct elements of the n−1 nonzero residue classes. Therefore
∣A∣(∣A∣−1)≤n−1,
(119)
so
 f(n)≤21+4n−3​​=O(n​). ​
