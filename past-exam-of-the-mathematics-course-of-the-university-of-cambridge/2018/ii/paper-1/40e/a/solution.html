Since Sw=ce(1) and Aw=λ1​w,
A(ce(1))=SAS−1Sw=SAw=λ1​ce(1).
(364)
Thus the first column of A is (λ1​,0,…,0)T, and A is a block upper triangular matrix:
A=(λ1​0​∗B​),
(365)
where B is its bottom-right (n−1)×(n−1) submatrix. Therefore
det(zI−A)=(z−λ1​)det(zI−B).
(366)
A similarity transformation preserves the characteristic polynomial, so
spec(A)={λ1​}∪spec(B),​
(367)
with algebraic multiplicities included.
To construct S, use a Householder transformation. With α=∥w∥2​, choose the sign of α to avoid cancellation and set
v=w−αe(1),S=I−2vTvvvT​.
(368)
Then S is an orthogonal matrix and maps w to αe(1) up to the chosen sign. If w already lies on the first coordinate axis, take a suitable diagonal sign matrix. This is the one-vector case of orthogonal coordinate reduction of a subspace.
