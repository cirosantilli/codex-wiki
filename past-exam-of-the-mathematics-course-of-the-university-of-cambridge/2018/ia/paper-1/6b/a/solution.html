The characteristic polynomial of the displayed rotation matrix is (1−λ)(λ2−2λcosθ+1), so its eigenvalues are 1,eiθ,e−iθ, all of modulus one. The real eigenvector (0,0,1)T spans the rotation axis, while the arguments ±θ of the conjugate eigenvalues record the rotation angle on its perpendicular plane.
