Put θ=T−Tr​ and u=U−Tr​. The term −aθ models oven cooling to the room, Q is heater input, and −bu=bθ−bu makes the pizza relax toward the oven temperature. Both protocols supply unit total heat because ∫δ(t)dt=1 and the rectangular pulse has height 1/τ and width τ.
For the impulse, θ jumps by one at zero while u remains continuous:
θ1​(t)=e−at,u1​(t)=b−ab​(e−at−e−bt)​
(20)
for a=b; when a=b, u1​=bte−bt.
Define causal functions
Ga​(t)=H(t)a1−e−at​,K(t)=H(t)b−ab​(a1−e−at​−b1−e−bt​).
(21)
The rectangular-pulse solutions are
θ2​(t)=τGa​(t)−Ga​(t−τ)​,u2​(t)=τK(t)−K(t−τ)​.​
(22)
Finally Ti​=Tr​+θi​, Ui​=Tr​+ui​. These formulas make T2​,U2​ continuous at 0,τ; only T1​ jumps at the delta impulse. As τ→0, the difference quotients tend to Ga′​=θ1​ and K′=u1​, because the rectangular pulse is an approximate identity.
