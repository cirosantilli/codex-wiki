View X as the matrix of a linear map T:Fm→Fn of matrix rank r. Choose vectors u1​,…,ur​ whose images form a basis of imT, extend them by a basis of kerT to a basis of Fm, and extend Tu1​,…,Tur​ to a basis of Fn. In these two bases the matrix of T is the rank normal form
(Ir​0​00​).
(29)
The two changes of basis give invertible P,Q with the displayed matrix equal to Q−1XP.
For the block upper triangular matrix A=(B0​DC​), every nonzero term in the Leibniz formula for determinants sends the rows belonging to C into the columns belonging to C; bijectivity then sends the remaining rows into the B columns. The permutation sum consequently factors into the determinant sums for B and C, proving
detA=detBdetC.​
(30)
Finally suppose L(X)=0, so AX=XB: the map X:Cm→Cn is an intertwining operator. If v lies in the generalized eigenspace of B for λ, then for some k,
(A−λI)kXv=X(B−λI)kv=0.
(31)
Because A and B have no common eigenvalue, A−λI is invertible, hence Xv=0. The generalized eigenspaces of B span Cm, so X=0. Thus the Sylvester equation operator L is injective.
