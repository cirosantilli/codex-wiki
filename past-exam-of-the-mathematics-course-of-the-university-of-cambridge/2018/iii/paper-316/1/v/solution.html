A coplanar target sweeps a strip of width 2Rp​ through the comet's local position probability. Hence the relevant geometric collision cross-section is a length, and the rare collision rate per comet is Γcol​=2Rp​Σc​(ap​)vrel​.
Using the corrected phase-mixed comet surface density and the notation s=q/ap​, γ=3−22s​​, the coplanar comet collision time is
tcol​≃2Rp​γGM⋆​​π2Q3/2ap​1−s​​.​
(12)
For q≪ap​, this becomes π2Q3/2ap​/(23​Rp​GM⋆​​), so tcol​∝Q3/2Mp−1/3​ap​ at fixed stellar mass and planetary mass density. This is the mean waiting time for one orbiting comet; the mean interval between impacts from Nc​ such independent comets is tcol​/Nc​. It assumes rare encounters and orbital phase mixing, not a Poisson process derived from a single deterministic trajectory.
