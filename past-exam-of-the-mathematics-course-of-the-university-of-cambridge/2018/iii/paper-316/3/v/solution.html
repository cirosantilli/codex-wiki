To first order in orbital eccentricity, the semi-minor axis is a+O(e2), while the ellipse's center is displaced from the star by −ae. Thus each orbit is a circle of radius a about −az, in complex spatial coordinates.
Write z=zf​+ep​eiϖp​ at one secular epoch. The centers of the individual circles lie on a circle of radius aep​ about Cf​=−azf​. Their union is the proper-eccentricity annulus
a(1−ep​)≤∣r−Cf​∣≤a(1+ep​),Cf​=−aef​.​
(41)
Its full radial width is 2aep​ and its center is displaced by aef​ toward the forced apoapsis direction. Relative to the star, if the forced longitude of periapsis is zero,
rinner,outer​(θ)≃a[1−ef​cosθ∓ep​].
(42)

For fully sampled orbital phases and proper phases, the annulus is occupied throughout, but its density need not be uniform; turning points in the proper radial excursion give integrable edge enhancements. The construction requires ef​,ep​≪1 and is an instantaneous secular geometry.
