Choose the common initial longitude of the particle and G as zero. Let the fixed inertial longitude of periapsis be ϖ, positive ahead of that ray. At release f0​=−ϖ, but the mean anomaly is
M0​=f0​−2esinf0​+O(e2)=−ϖ+2esinϖ+O(e2).
(55)
Thereafter M(t)=nt+M0​, f(t)=M(t)+2esinM(t)+O(e2), and the particle's longitude is λ(t)=ϖ+f(t). The reference ray has longitude λG​=ng​t.
In the rotating reference frame, the radial x axis points outward along the star-G ray and the y axis points forward. Define δλ=λ−λG​. The exact positional relations are
x=rcosδλ−ag​,y=rsinδλ.​
(56)
The diagram shows a later instant; initially δλ=0, so y(0)=0. Retaining the O(e) correction in M0​ is what will preserve that initial condition in the approximate trajectory.
