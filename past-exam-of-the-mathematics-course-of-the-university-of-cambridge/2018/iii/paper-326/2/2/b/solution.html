Let η=fδ−f, so ∥η∥≤δ. By linearity, uδ(k)​−u(k)=R1/k​η. The preceding operator norm estimate gives
∥uδ(k)​−u(k)∥≤kτ​δ.​
(33)
Moreover, KR1/k​ has factors 1−(1+τσj2​)−k on the data singular vectors, and is zero on R(K)⊥. Every factor is in [0,1], hence
∥Kuδ(k)​−Ku(k)∥≤δ.​
(34)
Thus, for any fixed k, one can take C=kτ​ and γ=1.
The first constant cannot generally be chosen independently of the iteration count. On the l2 sequence space, take (Ku)n​=un​/n, τ=1, and noise η=δen​. At k=n2 the solution perturbation has norm
δn[1−(1+n21​)−n2]∼(1−e−1)δn.
(35)
This disproves a uniform-in-k interpretation of the stated first estimate. For a closed operator range, boundedness of the Moore–Penrose inverse of an operator instead supplies the uniform constant ∥K†∥.
