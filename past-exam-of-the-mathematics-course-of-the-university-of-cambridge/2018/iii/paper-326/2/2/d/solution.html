Write the residual of an inverse problem as rk​=Kuδ(k)​−fδ. The iteration can be rearranged as
uδ(k+1)​−uδ(k)​=−τK∗rk+1​.
(40)
Applying K gives (I+τKK∗)rk+1​=rk​. Since KK∗ is a positive operator, its resolvent operator (I+τKK∗)−1 has operator norm at most one. Hence
∥rk+1​∥≤∥rk​∥.​
(41)
More explicitly, taking the inner product of the residual equation with rk+1​ gives
∥rk+1​∥2+τ∥K∗rk+1​∥2=Re⟨rk​,rk+1​⟩≤∥rk​∥∥rk+1​∥.
(42)
This directly proves the same monotonicity by the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, including the case rk+1​=0. An unresolvable data component in R(K)⊥ remains unchanged.
