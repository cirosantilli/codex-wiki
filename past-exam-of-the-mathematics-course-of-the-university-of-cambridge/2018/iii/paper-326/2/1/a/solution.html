Fix a singular system of a compact operator, with Kvj​=σj​wj​ and K∗wj​=σj​vj​, σj​>0. A spectral regularization method has the form
Rα​f=j∑​σj​gα​(σj2​)⟨f,wj​⟩vj​.​
(20)
It annihilates R(K)⊥. Standard sufficient spectral filter conditions are boundedness of sup0<σ≤∥K∥​∣σgα​(σ2)∣ for each α, pointwise λgα​(λ)→1 as α↓0 for λ>0, and a uniform bound on ∣λgα​(λ)∣. These give bounded linear operators and convergence on the domain of the Moore–Penrose inverse of an operator by the Picard criterion and dominated convergence theorem.
For Tikhonov regularization,
gα​(λ)=λ+α1​,Rα​=(K∗K+αI)−1K∗.​
(21)
This can be computed by solving the Tikhonov normal equation, without knowing the singular value decomposition.
For spectral cutoff regularization, take
gα​(λ)=λ1[α,∞)​(λ)​,​
(22)
with value zero at λ=0. Only singular values σj​≥α​ are inverted. Here gα​ multiplies σ; conventions calling the full coefficient σgα​(σ2) the spectral filter are equivalent.
