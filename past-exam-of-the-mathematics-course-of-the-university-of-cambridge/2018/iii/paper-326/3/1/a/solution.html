The listed existence hypotheses alone do not imply uniqueness or convergence of an arbitrary selection. For example, on R2, set K(x,y)=x, f=1, and
J(x,y)=x2+(max{∣y∣−1,0})2.

This is nonnegative, convex, continuous and has J(0)=0; every Φα,f​ has coercivity. Its minimizers are (1/(1+2α),y) for all ∣y∣≤1. Along αn​=α+1/n, selecting yn​=(−1)n prevents convergence. Without uniqueness, the valid conclusion is that every cluster point minimizes the limiting objective.
