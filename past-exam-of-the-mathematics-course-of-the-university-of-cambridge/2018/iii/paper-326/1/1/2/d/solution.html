The direct method in the calculus of variations gives the following existence theorem. Suppose bounded sequences in the Banach space U have τ-convergent subsequences, and E is a proper extended-real function with coercivity and is τ-sequentially lower semicontinuous. Then E has a finite-valued global minimizer.
To prove this, properness makes m=infE<+∞. First rule out m=−∞: a sequence with E(un​)→−∞ eventually lies in a fixed sublevel set, which is bounded by coercivity. A τ-convergent subsequence would then have a limit u with E(u)≤−∞, contradicting the codomain. Hence m is finite.
