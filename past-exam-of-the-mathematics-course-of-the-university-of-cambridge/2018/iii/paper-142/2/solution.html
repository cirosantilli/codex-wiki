To prove the Whitney product formula for Stiefel–Whitney classes, apply the splitting principle for real vector bundles simultaneously to E and E′. Its injective pullback on mod-two cohomology follows by iterating the Projective bundle definition of Stiefel–Whitney classes. It suffices to compute for a split vector bundle E=⨁i=1r​Li​. On P(E), the coordinate projections of the tautological inclusion λ↪p∗E give sections of the real line bundles λ∗⊗p∗Li​ with no common zero. The mod-two Euler class of a real line bundle is its first Stiefel–Whitney class, and the First Stiefel–Whitney class of a tensor product of real line bundles gives u+p∗xi​. Multiplication of Euler classes under a direct sum, and vanishing of the Euler class of a vector bundle with a nowhere-zero section, therefore give
∏i=1r​(u+p∗xi​)=0.
