An integral form of a group representation V is a G-stable finite free O-submodule W⊂V with K⊗O​W≅V. Such a form exists: take a basis lattice L and replace it by ∑g∈G​gL, which is finite and torsion-free and therefore free over the discrete valuation ring O.
Choose ordinary simple modules Vi​ with forms Wi​, and modular simple modules Sj​ with projective covers Pj​. With π a uniformizer, put Wi​=Wi​/πWi​. The decomposition matrix and Cartan matrix of a group algebra have entries
dij​=[Wi​:Sj​],cℓj​=[Pj​:Sℓ​].
(17)
The first numbers do not depend on the integral form: the Brauer character of its reduction is the ordinary character restricted to p-regular elements, and part 2(a) determines all composition multiplicities from this restriction.
For the assertion in (i), set I=HomOG​(W,W′). It is a submodule of the finite free module HomO​(W,W′), hence is finite free over O. There is a natural injective map
K⊗O​I⟶HomKG​(M,M′).
(18)
For any homomorphism f in the target, clearing the finitely many denominators of its values on an O-basis of W gives πNf(W)⊂W′. Thus πNf∈I, and the map is surjective. Therefore I is an O-form of HomKG​(M,M′)​, regarding this Hom space as a vector space. The original PDF supplies part (i), which is missing from the supplied TeX.
