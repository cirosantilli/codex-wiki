Completeness permits idempotent lifting from kG to OG. In particular, each projective cover Pj​ lifts to a finite projective OG-lattice Pj​; one can lift an idempotent presenting it as a summand of a finite free module. Set Qj​=K⊗O​Pj​.
For a simple Sℓ​, every map Pj​→Sℓ​ factors through its simple head Sj​. Splitting and Schur lemma give dimk​HomkG​(Pj​,Sℓ​)=δjℓ​. Exactness of this Hom functor along a composition series therefore gives
dimk​HomkG​(Pj​,Wi​)=dij​.
(23)
Part (iii) identifies this with dimK​HomKG​(Qj​,Vi​). By Maschke's theorem and splitting, KG is split semisimple, so
Qj​≅⨁i​Vi⊕dij​​.
(24)
The reductions of any two integral forms of the same ordinary module have identical Brauer characters, hence identical composition multiplicities. We may thus reduce a direct-sum form for the displayed decomposition instead of Pj​. In the modular representation ring this gives
[Pj​]=∑i​dij​[Wi​]=∑ℓ​(∑i​diℓ​dij​)[Sℓ​].
(25)
Comparing the simple basis coefficients proves cℓj​=∑i​diℓ​dij​, or C=DTD​.
