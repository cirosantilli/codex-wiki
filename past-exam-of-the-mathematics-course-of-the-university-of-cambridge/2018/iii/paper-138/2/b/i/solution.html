Let g be a p-regular element, with eigenvalues λi​ on V. On both g−1 acting on V and g acting on the dual representation V∗, the eigenvalues are λi−1​. Multiplicative lifts of roots of unity satisfy λi−1​​=λi−1​=λi​​. Hence
χV​(g−1)=χV​(g)​=χV∗​(g)​.
(8)
All these identities concern the domain of a Brauer character, namely the p-regular elements. If necessary, extend the p-modular system to contain the relevant roots of unity and use one consistent choice of lifts.
