Fix the maturity horizon. Since YB is a nonnegative local martingale and r is bounded, EYT​<∞: BT​≥B0​e−∥r∥∞​T and E(YT​BT​)≤B0​. The bounded nonnegative payoff thus makes YT​ξT​ integrable. Set
Nt​=E[YT​ξT​∣Ft​],Xt​=Yt​Nt​​.
(45)
The Brownian martingale representation theorem yields Nt​=N0​+∫0t​ζs​dWs​, using its locally square-integrable version for an integrable terminal random variable. The Itô formula for N/Y gives
dXt​=(rt​Xt​+λt​(ζt​/Yt​+λt​Xt​))dt+(ζt​/Yt​+λt​Xt​)dWt​.
(46)
Choose the replicating strategy
θt​=St​σt​ζt​/Yt​+λt​Xt​​,ηt​=Bt​Xt​−θt​St​​.​
(47)
Its self-financing portfolio equation has exactly the displayed drift and diffusion, because μ−r=σλ. All coefficients are locally integrable after stopping; the holdings are predictable in the augmented natural Brownian filtration. This construction has Xt​≥0 and XT​=ξT​, so it is an admissible replicating strategy under the question's nonnegative-wealth convention.
For any other nonnegative self-financing portfolio X replicating the same payoff, part (b) implies X0​≥E[YT​ξT​]. The constructed strategy attains equality, since Y0​=1 and N0​=E[YT​ξT​] in the augmented natural Brownian filtration. Hence
minimal initial cost=E[YT​ξT​].​
(48)
This is deflator-based claim replication; it does not require upgrading the local martingale deflator to a true martingale density.
