Keeping the powers and exponentials of v and τ in the joint kernel gives the two inverse-gamma distributions, using the printed shape–scale convention:
v∣rest∼IG(2N​,21​s∑​(Cs​−μC​)2),τ∣rest∼IG(N,s∑​Es​)​.
(30)
Indeed their kernels are v−(N/2+1)exp[−∑s​(Cs​−μC​)2/(2v)] and τ−(N+1)exp[−∑s​Es​/τ]. With positive measurement variances and generic continuously drawn latent values, the two scale parameters are positive almost surely. Together with (a),(b), these are all 2N+3 formal full conditional updates.
There is, however, no joint posterior probability distribution to sample under the stated priors. Integrate out the intrinsic Cs​ first. For fixed μC​,v>0, the observational marginal density is
L(μC​,v,τ)=∏s​∫0∞​τ−1e−E/τϕv+rs​​(os​−μC​−E)dE,
(31)
where ϕV​ is the centred normal density of variance V. Substitute E=τu. Dominated convergence gives
limτ↓0​L(μC​,v,τ)=∏s​ϕv+rs​​(os​−μC​)>0.
(32)
The convergence is uniform on any compact μC​ interval and any compact positive v interval, since the normal densities are uniformly bounded and continuous there. Hence some such region has L≥c>0 for all sufficiently small τ, and
∫0ε​L(μC​,v,τ)τdτ​=∞.
(33)
Integrating over those positive-measure intervals proves that the full normalizing constant is infinite. If all rs​>0, the v↓0 boundary also gives a positive marginal likelihood and a divergent dv/v integral at fixed τ>0. Thus the stated joint posterior is improper​, even though its generic full conditionals are proper. Apparent stability of a simulated chain cannot establish a nonexistent stationary probability law.
A valid repair that preserves direct sampling is to replace the two log-flat priors by independent proper v∼IG(av​,bv​) and τ∼IG(aτ​,bτ​), with all four constants positive. The normal and truncated-normal updates remain as above, while
v∣rest∼IG(av​+2N​,bv​+21​∑s​(Cs​−μC​)2),τ∣rest∼IG(aτ​+N,bτ​+∑s​Es​).
(34)
With positive rs​, the flat μC​ prior is then harmless: after integrating it out, the Gaussian measurement likelihood is bounded uniformly over the latent shifts and the scales, and the remaining proper priors integrate to one. A proper prior on μC​ is also possible.
For this repaired model, initialize v,τ>0, Es​≥0 and finite Cs​,μC​. Repeatedly choose one of the 2N+3 coordinates with fixed positive probabilities and redraw from its full conditional using the latest values. This Random-scan Gibbs sampler preserves the proper target and satisfies detailed balance: for each coordinate, both forward and reverse flows equal the marginal density of the remaining coordinates times the product of the two conditional densities. A systematic full sweep is another invariant scheme, though it need not itself be reversible.
Run several dispersed chains; inspect traces of both scales, μC​, representative latent values and the log posterior. Assess rank-normalized split R, autocorrelation, and the effective sample size of a Markov chain, and extend runs until relevant Monte Carlo errors are sufficiently small. Scale–latent dependence can cause slow mixing, so diagnostics must include boundary exploration and alternative initializations. Keep post-warmup draws without automatic thinning. These are convergence checks for the repaired posterior; the printed model admits only the formal update algorithm, not a valid posterior-sampling interpretation.
