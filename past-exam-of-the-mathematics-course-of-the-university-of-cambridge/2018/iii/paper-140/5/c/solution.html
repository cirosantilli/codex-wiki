Use C2 with the standard symplectic form ω0​=dx1​∧dy1​+dx2​∧dy2​ and the compatible almost complex structure given by multiplication by i. Consider the embedded submanifold
L={(s+it,t):s,t∈R}.​
(34)
In coordinates (x1​,y1​,x2​,y2​), its tangent space is W={(a,b,b,0):a,b∈R}. Applying J gives J(a,b,b,0)=(−b,a,0,b). If this is again in W, its fourth coordinate forces b=0, and its second and third coordinates then force a=0. Thus W∩JW=0 and dimR​L=2: L is a totally real submanifold.
However, the pullback of a differential form under the parametrization is
ω0​∣L​=ds∧dt=0.​
(35)
Thus this totally real submanifold is not a Lagrangian submanifold.
