Let σ(z)=z, componentwise complex conjugation. Interpret the printed ∣z2∣ in the potential as ∣z∣2=∑j​∣zj​∣2. With D=1+∣z∣2, the local Fubini-Study form is
ωFS​=2i​∑j,k​(Dδjk​​−D2zj​zk​​)dzj​∧dzk​.
(12)
Its pullback of a differential form under σ is
σ∗ωFS​=2i​∑j,k​(Dδjk​​−D2zj​zk​​)dzj​∧dzk​=−ωFS​,
(13)
where the last equality swaps j,k and uses antisymmetry of the wedge product of differential forms. Since σ2=I, it is an anti-symplectic involution, with fixed-point set Rn. Part (c) gives
Rn⊂(Cn,ωFS​) is Lagrangian.​
(14)
