Suppose D≡A′+E′ as in (c), and set P=D−E′. Its numerical class is ample, so P is an ample real divisor. Fix an ample Cartier divisor A. The ample cone is open by Kleiman's criterion, so choose ε>0 with P−εA still ample. Express this ample real divisor as a positive real combination ∑bi​Bi​ of ample Cartier divisors. Some positive multiple of each Bi​ has an effective representative; dividing by that multiple gives Bi​∼Q​Gi​ with Gi​ effective. Hence
D∼R​εA+(E′+∑i​bi​Gi​),
(21)
which is (b). The equivalence of numerical ampleness with a positive combination of ample Cartier representatives follows by rational approximation inside the open ample cone; the rational approximation of an ample-plus-effective real divisor proof also accounts for principal-divisor directions.
Finally, if D′≡D, the same expression D′≡A′+E′ satisfies (c). By the equivalences just proved, D′ satisfies all three conditions exactly when D does. Thus bigness is invariant under numerical equivalence, for real as well as Cartier divisors.
