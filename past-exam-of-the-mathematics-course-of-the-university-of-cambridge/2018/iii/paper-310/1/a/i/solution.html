Use units c=1 throughout. Set χ=∣x∣, so the spatial Euclidean metric is dx2=dχ2+χ2dΩ2. On either hemisphere of the embedded three-sphere, the constraint gives u2=R2−χ2 and hence
du=−uχ​dχ,dℓ2=1−χ2/R2dχ2​+χ2dΩ2.
(1)
Introduce the dimensionless comoving coordinate r=χ/R. Then
ds2=−dt2+a(t)2{1−Kr2dr2​+r2dΩ2},a(t)=Rb(t),K=+1.​
(2)
This is the positively curved Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker metric, with a(t) the physical curvature radius. An equally valid convention retains r=χ, takes a=b, and sets K=R−2; only the coordinate normalization differs. The chart with r≤1 describes a hemisphere, not the entire closed slice. Writing r=sinψ gives dℓ2=R2(dψ2+sin2ψdΩ2), with 0≤ψ≤π, displaying the full spherical spatial slices of a closed FLRW universe.
