Suppose first that x=0, and let E be the real span of the root system, of dimension equal to the rank of a semisimple Lie algebra ℓ. The images of the roots span E/Rα, so select roots β1​,…,βℓ−1​ whose images form a basis of this quotient.
For each i, take the highest endpoint γi+​ of the α root string through βi​, and the highest endpoint γi−​ of the root string through −βi​. By construction, γi±​+α is not a root. Moreover γi±​=−α, since their images in E/Rα are nonzero. The root-space decomposition and its bracket rule therefore give
[gγi±​​,x]=0.
(16)
These 2(ℓ−1) roots are distinct: their quotient images are the two signs of a basis, which are distinct. Their one-dimensional root spaces consequently contribute 2(ℓ−1) independent vectors to the Lie algebra centralizer.
In addition, the ℓ−1 dimensional subspace kerα⊂t commutes with x, because [h,x]=α(h)x. The line Cx⊂gα​ also commutes with x. These contributions are independent by the root-space decomposition; in particular none of the selected endpoint roots is α. Thus the centralizer lower bound for a root vector is
dimZg​(x)≥2(ℓ−1)+(ℓ−1)+1=3ℓ−2.​
(17)
If x=0, then Zg​(x)=g. The root system contains the 2ℓ distinct roots ±αi​, so dimg=ℓ+∣Φ∣≥3ℓ, which also proves the desired inequality. The argument includes rank one, where the list of βi​ is empty.
