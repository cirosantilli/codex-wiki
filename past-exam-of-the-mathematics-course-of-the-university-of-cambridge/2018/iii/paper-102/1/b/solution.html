The derived series gives a direct test, with no structural theorem needed. By bilinearity and antisymmetry of the Lie bracket, every bracket in h is a multiple of z, and z=[x,y] actually occurs. Therefore
h(1)=[h,h]=Cz,h(2)=[Cz,Cz]=0.
(3)
Consequently this Heisenberg Lie algebra is a nonzero solvable Lie algebra. It is an ideal of a Lie algebra in itself, since [h,h]⊆h. Its solvable radical is therefore all of h, contradicting the first criterion in part (a). Hence
h is not semisimple.​
