Let CC∞​ be the additive sheaf of complex-valued smooth functions, and CC∞,∗​ its multiplicative sheaf of nowhere-zero functions. The smooth exponential sequence is
0⟶Z​⟶CC∞​f↦e2πif​CC∞,∗​⟶1.
(12)
Its kernel is the integer-valued locally constant functions, namely the integer constant sheaf. It is surjective on stalks because a nowhere-zero smooth function has a smooth logarithm on a sufficiently small neighbourhood.
The additive smooth-function sheaf is a fine sheaf, using a partition of unity, and the smooth manifold is a paracompact space. Its positive-degree sheaf cohomology vanishes. The long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology consequently gives an isomorphism
δ:H1(X,CC∞,∗​)∼​H2(X,Z).
(13)
To connect this with bundles, trivialize a smooth complex line bundle over an open cover. Its transition functions gij​ form a multiplicative Čech cocycle, satisfying gij​gjk​=gik​. Changing trivializations changes it by a Čech coboundary. Conversely, any such cocycle glues trivial line bundles, and cohomologous cocycles give isomorphic bundles. Thus the isomorphism classes are H1(X,CC∞,∗​).
The connecting isomorphism is the First Chern class. Locally choose logarithms gij​=e2πifij​; on triple intersections
nijk​=fij​+fjk​−fik​∈Z
(14)
represents that class. Combining the gluing classification with δ gives the requested bijection:
{smooth complex line bundles on X}/≅  c1​ ​ H2(X,Z).​
(15)
