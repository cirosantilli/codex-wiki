Let ω be the Kähler form associated with the metric. The Lefschetz operator of a Kähler manifold and adjoint Lefschetz operator are
Lα=ω∧α,Λ=L∗.​
(34)
Here the adjoint is taken with respect to the pointwise Hermitian inner product on complexified forms, or equivalently the global L2 inner product. It lowers degree by two and bidegree by (1,1). In terms of the Hodge star operator, Λ=∗−1L∗.
Since dω=0, dL=Ld, so L takes closed differential forms to closed differential forms and exact differential forms to exact differential forms. This already gives its action on de Rham cohomology. For both operators together, use the Kähler identities: L commutes with the Hodge Laplacian, and taking adjoints shows that Λ does too. Thus both preserve harmonic differential forms.
When M is compact, the Hodge decomposition theorem identifies Hk(M,C) with its space of harmonic k-forms. If hα​ is the unique harmonic differential form representing [α], define
L[α]=[Lhα​],Λ[α]=[Λhα​].​
(35)
The resulting forms are harmonic and therefore closed, and uniqueness of hα​ makes these definitions independent of the initial representative. The cohomological definition of Λ uses harmonic differential forms as representatives: Λ need not take an arbitrary closed form to a closed form.
