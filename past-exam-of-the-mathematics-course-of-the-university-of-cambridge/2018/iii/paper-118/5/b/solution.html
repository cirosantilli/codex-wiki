The holomorphic de Rham complex is exact locally, which is the criterion for an exact sequence of sheaves. Work in a small star-shaped holomorphic coordinate neighbourhood in C2, centred at zero. A holomorphic function has ∂f=0 exactly when it is constant on this neighbourhood, giving exactness at OM​ and the injection of the constant sheaf.
