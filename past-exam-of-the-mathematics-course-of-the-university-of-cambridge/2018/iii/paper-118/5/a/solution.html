A holomorphic differential form α of type (p,0) satisfies ∂ˉα=0. The adjoint ∂ˉ∗ lowers antiholomorphic degree, so ∂ˉ∗α=0 as well. Therefore Δ∂ˉ​α=0.
The Kähler Laplacian identity gives Δd​α=2Δ∂ˉ​α=0. Compactness lets us use the L2 identity for the Hodge Laplacian:
0=⟨Δd​α,α⟩=∥dα∥2+∥d∗α∥2.
(42)
Both terms are nonnegative, so
dα=0.​
(43)
This proves that holomorphic forms on a compact Kähler manifold are closed. The original PDF uses ΩMp​ and AM,Cp+1​; the extra symbols in the local TeX superscripts are transcription errors.
