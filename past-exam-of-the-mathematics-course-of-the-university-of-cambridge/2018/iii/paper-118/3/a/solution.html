Use the integral normalization of the Chern connection curvature. For a holomorphic local frame e, put he​=h(e,e)>0. The fundamental form of a Hermitian holomorphic line bundle is
ω(L,h)​=2πi​Fh​=−2πi​∂∂ˉloghe​.​
(23)
This follows from the local formula for the Chern connection on a line bundle, Fh​=∂ˉ∂loghe​. It is a closed real (1, 1)-form representing the First Chern class. If he​=e−φ, the formula becomes (i/(2π))∂∂ˉφ.
The Hermitian metric is positive when this is a positive real (1, 1)-form: for every nonzero v∈T1,0M,
−iω(L,h)​(v,vˉ)>0.​
(24)
Equivalently, the matrix (∂2φ/∂zj​∂zˉk​) is a positive-definite matrix in every frame. Such a metric makes L a positive holomorphic line bundle. Omitting the normalization factor 2π changes none of these positivity conditions.
