Fix A, and let S(B)=B⨿A be the functor obtained from the chosen binary coproduct in a category construction. Its arrow map is S(v)=v⨿1A​. For hA​=C(A,−), the binary coproduct property gives hB​×hA​≅hB⨿A​. The Yoneda lemma and part (i) therefore give, naturally in B,F,
(FhA​)(B)=Nat(hB​×hA​,F)≅Nat(hB⨿A​,F)≅F(B⨿A).
(45)
Thus exponentiation by hA​ is precomposition S∗:F↦F∘S.
This precomposition has a right adjoint given by Right Kan extension. Explicitly, for K:C→Set put
(RK)(B)=lim(C,u:B→SC)∈(B↓S)​K(C).
(46)
The comma category is small, so this categorical limit exists in the Category of sets. A map v:B→B′ gives (RK)(v) by taking a compatible family indexed by u:B→SC and selecting its entries at u′v for the indices u′:B′→SC.
A natural transformation α:F∘S→K determines F→RK by sending x∈F(B) to the family αC​(F(u)x) indexed by (C,u). Conversely, a map F→RK determines F(SC)→K(C) by projecting to the index (C,1SC​). Naturality and the limit's compatibility relations make these constructions inverse. Hence S∗⊣R, proving the tiny covariant representable functor on a category with binary coproducts result:
C(A,−) is tiny.​
(47)
