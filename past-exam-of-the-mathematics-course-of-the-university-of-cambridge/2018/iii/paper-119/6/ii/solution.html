Use countably many copies of A, and let c:S=∐i≥1​A→P=∏i≥1​A be the assumed isomorphism. Write pi​=πi​c:S→A, let δ:A→P be the diagonal with every component 1A​, and put q=c−1δ. Thus pi​q=1A​ for every i. Let s:S→S shift the injections, sνi​=νi+1​, and let σ:S→A be the fold map, σνi​=1A​.
The coordinate relations give p1​s=0 and pi+1​s=pi​, while p1​ν1​=1A​ and pi+1​ν1​=0. Therefore q and ν1​+sq have identical pi​-coordinates. Since c is invertible, these coordinates jointly distinguish arrows into S, so
q=ν1​+sq.
(53)
The coproduct property gives σs=σ, because both have value 1A​ on each injection. Consequently z=σq satisfies
z=σν1​+σsq=1A​+z.
(54)
This proves the absorbing-endomorphism assertion without assuming an infinite addition operation on hom-sets.
If the category is an additive category, each hom-set is an abelian group, so cancellation gives 1A​=0. Any arrow f:A→B is then f1A​=f0=0. Every hom-set has exactly one arrow, and every object is isomorphic to the zero object. This is countable biproducts in an additive category force triviality:
z+1A​=z;C additive ⟹ C≃1.​
