Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2018 / iii / Paper 119 / 2 / ii / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2018 iii Paper 119 2 ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 244
Use the square's notation HF=KG. For E∈E, choose (C,u:E→GC) in the nonempty connected category (E↓G). Lift Ku:KE→HFC through the discrete fibration H, obtaining the uniquely determined arrow
u:Du​⟶FC,Hu=Ku.
(11)
Define LE=Du​. If a:C→C′ satisfies Gau=u′, then Fau is a lift of Ku′ with codomain FC′. Uniqueness makes it exactly u′, including equality of its domain. Connectedness of the comma category therefore makes LE independent of the chosen object, with strict equality rather than merely an isomorphism.
For v:E→E′, choose (C,u:E′→GC) and lift Kv:KE→KE′=HLE′ with codomain LE′. Write this lift as w:D→LE′. Its composite with u is the lift of K(uv) to FC, so its domain is LE. Set Lv=w. The lift of an identity is an identity, and the composite of two lifts is the lift of the composite with the specified codomain. Thus L is a functor, and HL=K.
At E=GC, choose (C,1GC​); its lift is 1FC​, giving LGC=FC. For a:C→C′, the unique lift of KGa=HFa with codomain FC′ is Fa, giving LG=F on arrows as well. Finally, any other filler L′ must send u to the same lift of Ku with codomain FC, so L′E=LE; it must also send v to the unique lift of Kv with codomain LE′. This proves orthogonality of final functors and discrete fibrations:
∃!L:E→D with HL=K,LG=F.​
(12)
