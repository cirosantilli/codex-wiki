Absorb kB​T into the free energy, so the statistical weight is e−F. Under the Gaussian fixed point rescaling, invariance of the gradient energy gives the engineering dimension
[ϕ]=2d−2​=21​(d=3)​.
(9)
The engineering dimensions of ϕ2,ϕ4,ϕ6 are respectively 1,2,3. Their coupling eigenvalues are d−[ϕ2m], hence 2,1,0. The quadratic and quartic interactions are relevant operators; the sextic interaction is a marginal operator at this order.
The three steps of the momentum-shell renormalization group are:
  • Split ϕ=φ+η into slow modes ∣k∣<Λ/b and fast modes Λ/b<∣k∣<Λ, and integrate over η.
  • Rescale x′=x/b to restore the ultraviolet cutoff to Λ.
  • Rescale the field as ϕ′(x′)=b(d−2)/2φ(bx′) to normalize the gradient energy.
Here b=es>1, so increasing s means flowing toward longer distances. At leading order, the fast field has a Gaussian distribution with covariance
Cb​(x−y)=∫Λ/b<∣k∣<Λ​(2π)3d3k​k2+μ02​eik⋅(x−y)​,Ib​=Cb​(0).
(10)
This perturbative integration requires k2+μ02​>0 on the shell. By the Wick theorem, odd fast-field moments vanish and ⟨η4⟩=3Ib2​, ⟨η6⟩=15Ib3​. Therefore the first term of the cumulant expansion of a coarse-grained free energy is
λ0​⟨(φ+η)6⟩=λ0​(φ6+15Ib​φ4+45Ib2​φ2+15Ib3​).
(11)
This is the quartic interaction generated by a sextic interaction. Including the rescaling, the requested flow is
g(b)=15bλ0​∫Λ/b<∣k∣<Λ​(2π)3d3k​k2+μ02​1​+O(λ02​)​.
(12)
In spherical coordinates, Ib​=(2π2)−1∫Λ/bΛ​k2/(k2+μ02​)dk. In particular, for μ02​=0,
g(b)=2π215λ0​Λ​(b−1)+O(λ02​),dsdg​=g+2π215Λ​λ+⋯.
(13)
The same integration shifts the quadratic coefficient by 90λ0​Ib2​ before rescaling and adds an irrelevant constant to the free energy. Thus setting the bare quartic coupling to zero does not place the theory on the tricritical critical surface.
To calculate the sextic renormalization-group beta function to second order, let V=λ0​∫d3x(φ+η)6. The required cumulant expansion is
Feff​=F0​+⟨V⟩−21​(⟨V2⟩−⟨V⟩2)+O(λ03​).
(14)
The subtraction removes disconnected contributions. The term 20λ0​φ3η3 at each vertex supplies the primitive sextic correction. The Wick contractions give
⟨η(x)3η(y)3⟩=6Cb​(x−y)3+9Ib2​Cb​(x−y).
(15)
The first term contains three propagators connecting the two vertices; its factor 6 is the number of bijections between the three fields at each vertex. The second contains a local tadpole diagram at each vertex and one connecting propagator. The primitive contribution to the free energy is therefore
ΔF6​=−1200λ02​∫d3xd3yφ(x)3φ(y)3Cb​(x−y)3.
(16)
Expanding the slow fields about a common point gives a negative local sextic correction proportional to
Jb​=∫d3zCb​(z)3=∫(2π)6d3pd3q​(p2+μ02​)(q2+μ02​)((p+q)2+μ02​)χb​(p)χb​(q)χb​(p+q)​≥0,
(17)
where χb​ restricts a momentum to the shell. This connected Feynman diagram has two independent loop momenta. Other Wick contractions also generate lower interactions and tadpole diagrams; they must be included or subtracted consistently when fixing the tricritical critical surface.
To extract the logarithmic tricritical sextic beta function, use normal ordering to remove local tadpoles and tune the quadratic and quartic relevant operators. The massless three-dimensional propagator is C(z)=1/(4π∣z∣). The same three-line contraction over short separations a<∣z∣<ba gives
∫a<∣z∣<ba​d3zC(z)3=16π21​logb.
(18)
Its positive coefficient and the negative sign of the second cumulant expansion term establish the sign of the flow. Matching this logarithm to a renormalized coupling, or performing consistent iterated shell integration, gives
dsdλ​=−Cλ2+O(λ3),C>0​,λ(s)=1+Cλ(0)sλ(0)​+⋯.
(19)
The precise normalization of C is not needed. In a sharp momentum cutoff scheme, simply forcing all three internal momenta into one infinitesimal shell does not extract this two-loop logarithm: the shell restrictions remove its linear-in-ds phase space. The finite-shell cumulant and the logarithmic renormalized calculation above are distinct stages of the computation. Field normalization corrections at order λ2 enter this renormalization-group beta function only at order λ3.
A positive, stabilizing sextic coupling is marginally irrelevant in three dimensions on the tricritical critical surface. Reversing the convention for s reverses the sign of the renormalization-group beta function. A negative sextic coupling without higher stabilizing powers makes the potential unbounded; moreover, the untuned bare choice g=0 generally flows away through its generated relevant operator.
