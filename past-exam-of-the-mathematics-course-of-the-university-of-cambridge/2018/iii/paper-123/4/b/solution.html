The polynomial is Eisenstein at 3, so a root α generates a totally ramified cubic field F=Q3​(α) and is a uniformizer of F. Its cubic discriminant is
Δ=−4(−3)3−27⋅32=−135=−5⋅33.
(36)
Its 3-adic valuation is 3, so it is not a square. The cubic discriminant criterion therefore gives
Gal(M/Q3​)≅S3​,[M:Q3​]=6.​
(37)
The unique quadratic subfield is generated by Δ​. Since the permitted square root −5​ is in Q3​, this subfield is
E=Q3​(3​).
(38)
It is totally ramified of degree two, by the Eisenstein polynomial T2−3. The ramification index of M/Q3​ is divisible by both the index 3 of F and the index 2 of E. Since the whole degree is 6, it follows that
e(M/Q3​)=6,kM​=F3​.​
(39)
In particular G0​=G.
We use the following standard facts about totally ramified extensions: every uniformizer generates the valuation ring over the base valuation ring, and for a totally ramified Galois extension
Gi​={σ:vM​(σ(π)−π)≥i+1}(i≥0).
(40)
Thus we can use the uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups. Put s=3​. Since vM​(α)=2 and vM​(s)=3,
π=sα2​,vM​(π)=4−3=1
(41)
is a uniformizer of M.
Let α1​,α2​,α3​ be the three roots. The Galois group acts transitively on their unordered pairs and preserves the valuation, so all differences between distinct roots have the same valuation d. The discriminant identity gives
18=vM​(Δ)=2∑i<j​vM​(αi​−αj​)=6d,
(42)
hence d=3. Thus, for any σ, either σ(α)=α or σ(α)−α has valuation 3.
A nontrivial three-cycle fixes s, since it fixes the quadratic subfield, and sends α to a different root. Also vM​(σ(α)+α)=2: write the sum as 2α+(σ(α)−α), whose two terms have unequal valuations 2,3. Therefore
vM​(σ(π)−π)=3+2−3=2for either nontrivial three-cycle.
(43)
A transposition changes the sign of s. Consequently
σ(π)−π=−sσ(α)2+α2​.
(44)
The numerator has valuation 4: it is 2α2 plus terms of strictly higher valuation if the root changes, and exactly 2α2 if the root is fixed. Thus every transposition has vM​(σ(π)−π)=1. We conclude that the ramification groups of the splitting field of T3 minus 3T plus 3 over Q3 are
G0​=S3​,G1​=A3​≅C3​,Gi​={1}(i≥2).​
(45)
As an independent check, the cubic field has OF​=Z3​[α] and different exponent vF​(g′(α))=vF​(3(α2−1))=3. The extension M/F is tamely totally ramified of degree two, so its different exponent is 1. The different in a tower gives d(M/Q3​)=1+2⋅3=7, agreeing with the ramification sum (6−1)+(3−1)=7.
