Put I={x:∣C(x)∣=∞}, the union of all infinite percolation clusters. Since p>pc​, the percolation probability θ(p) is positive. The translation ergodicity of Bernoulli percolation makes {I=∅} a zero-one event; it has positive probability because Pp​(0∈I)=θ(p)>0, hence it occurs almost surely.
Let Dm​={B(m)∩I=∅}. These events increase to {I=∅}, so continuity from below of a measure gives Pp​(Dm​)→1. On Dm​, some vertex of B(m) has an infinite open path in a graph. For every N≥m, the segment up to its first visit to ∂B(N) lies entirely in B(N). Thus even with this restriction on the connecting path in a graph,
n≥1inf​Pp​(B(m)⟷∂B(m+n) within B(m+n))≥Pp​(Dm​)⟶1.​
