Each edge cutset Πi​ separates a from b. Adjacent vertices have graph distances from a differing by at most one. Hence an edge can cross at most one of these level boundaries, so the edge cutsets are pairwise edge-disjoint. Write ki​=∣Πi​∣≥1; then ∑i​ki​≤∣E∣.
The Nash-Williams inequality for unit conductances gives

where the middle inequality is the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality. One can also derive the first inequality directly: every unit flow has signed net flux one through each Πi​, so its energy of a flow on that cut is at least 1/ki​; sum over the disjoint cuts and use the Thomson principle.
