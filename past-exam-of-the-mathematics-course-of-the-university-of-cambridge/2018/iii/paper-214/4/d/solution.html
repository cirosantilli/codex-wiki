On a tree, every finite connected induced subgraph is itself a tree, and its only spanning tree contains all its edges. Thus the free uniform spanning forest is deterministic:
FSF=δE​.

Suppose for a contradiction that G is a transient graph. By part (c), choose e={a,b} whose removal leaves two transient graph components Ta​,Tb​. Let Ra​,Rb​<∞ be their effective resistances to infinity measured from their respective endpoints.
In a wired finite exhaustion, the edge e of resistance one is in parallel with the route from a through Ta​ to the wired boundary and back through Tb​ to b. The latter route has resistance Ra,k​+Rb,k​, converging to Ra​+Rb​. Consequently the limiting wired effective resistance is
Reffw​(a,b)=1+Ra​+Rb​Ra​+Rb​​<1.
