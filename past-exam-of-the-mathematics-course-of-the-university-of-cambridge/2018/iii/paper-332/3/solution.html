Nor does this fixed-edge-curvature propagation law prescribe how an irreversible fracture closes under negative inflow. The final-size answer is conditional on reaching the advancing late branch. In particular the reservoir is generally not emptied, and β controls the approach rate rather than the equilibrium size.
