Take x in the injection direction and y along the flat interface. The specified U must be a Darcy velocity, so the pore-fluid velocity and unperturbed interface speed are U/ϕ. If x is measured from the unperturbed front and γ denotes its local apparent tension, set X=0 to obtain the usual fixed-coefficient expression. For a physically fixed spatial gradient, X=Ut/ϕ and the displayed rate is an instantaneous, generally time-dependent growth rate; the amplitude solves a˙=σ(α;X(t))a, rather than one constant exponential law for the entire displacement. The parameter β has dimensions of inverse length; the absolute tension gradient is γβ.
