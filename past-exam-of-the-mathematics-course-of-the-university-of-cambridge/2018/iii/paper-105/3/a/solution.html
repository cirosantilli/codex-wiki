On the zero-boundary Sobolev space H01​(U), the associated bilinear form is
B[u,v]=∫U​(i,j∑​aijuxi​​vxj​​+i∑​biuxi​​v+cuv)dx.​
(26)
The homogeneous Dirichlet boundary condition removes the boundary term, so (Lu,v)L2​=B[u,v] for sufficiently regular u and v∈H01​.
Formal self-adjointness means that L equals its formal adjoint, or equivalently (Lϕ,χ)=(ϕ,Lχ) for compactly supported smooth functions. Here
L∗v=−∑i,j​(aijvxi​​)xj​​−∑i​bivxi​​+(c−divb)v.
(27)
With the symmetric real principal coefficients in the question, L=L∗ is equivalent to b=0. It makes B a symmetric bilinear form.
For the assertions in part (b), positivity must mean strict Dirichlet positivity:
B[u,u]>0for every nonzero u∈H01​(U).​
(28)
This is the positive-definite operator convention. The weaker positive semidefinite operator convention B[u,u]≥0 does not suffice: L=−Δ−λ1​ has zero energy on a first Dirichlet eigenfunction. Under strict positivity, the compactness argument in part (b)(i) gives a uniform lower bound as well.
