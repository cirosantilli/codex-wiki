The tree-level Feynman diagrams contain an unchanged spectator quark, of up quark flavour and the two possible weak charged current transitions of the charm antiquark:
For Dˉ0→K+π−, cˉ→sˉW− and W−→duˉ. The spectator quark combines with sˉ into the kaon K+=usˉ, while duˉ forms the pion π−. The Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix factor is Vcs​Vud∗​.
For Dˉ0→K−π+, cˉ→dˉW− and W−→suˉ. The spectator quark combines with dˉ into the pion π+=udˉ, while suˉ forms the kaon K−. The Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix factor is Vcd​Vus∗​.
Neglecting neutral D-meson mixing and assuming comparable strong interaction matrix elements, the relative direct decay widths are
Γ(Dˉ0→K+π−)Γ(Dˉ0→K−π+)​≈​Vcs​Vud∗​Vcd​Vus∗​​​2≈tan4θC​.​
(30)
Here θC​ is the Cabibbo angle. The second process is doubly Cabibbo suppressed: it contains two small Cabibbo suppression factors in its amplitude. The Cabibbo angle estimate assumes similar Quantum chromodynamics matrix elements; it is not an exact rate equality.
