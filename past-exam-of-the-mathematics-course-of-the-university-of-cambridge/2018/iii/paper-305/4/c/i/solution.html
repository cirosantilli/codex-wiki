Use the massless collinear parton approximation in a high-energy frame: P2=0, k=ξP, and k′=ξP+q with k′2=0. This neglects target-mass corrections to the parton model; it does not literally set a stationary massive target to a massless particle in the earlier flux formula.
For a quark of dimensionless charge Qf​, the electromagnetic vector current matrix element is Qf​uˉ(k′)γμu(k). The spin average and gamma-matrix trace give
21​∑spins​JμJν∗=2Qf2​(kμk′ν+kνk′μ−gμνk⋅k′).
(43)

Wμν=2ξEk′​δ(q0+ξEP​−Ek′​)​(kμk′ν+kνk′μ−gμνk⋅k′).
(44)
Since k⋅k′=ξν, this is
Wμν=Ek′​δ(q0+ξEP​−Ek′​)​[ξPμPν+21​(Pμqν+qμPν)−2ν​gμν].
(45)
For the massless Electron momenta, p2=p′2=0 and q=p−p′. Substitution into the leptonic tensor gives
qμLμν​=4[−(p⋅p′)pν′​+(p⋅p′)pν​−(p⋅p′)(pν​−pν′​)]=0,
(46)
and likewise qνLμν​=0. These Ward identities eliminate every term with an exposed q index in the contraction. Therefore
Wμν≐Ek′​δ(q0+ξEP​−Ek′​)​[ξPμPν−2ν​gμν].​

Here ≐ means equality after contraction with the leptonic tensor. The shortened tensor is not itself conserved; the omitted terms restore current conservation in the full hadronic tensor.
