Write JVμ​=ψˉ​γμψ for the Dirac current and JAμ​=ψˉ​γμγ5ψ for the axial current. Under parity symmetry in quantum field theory, the Dirac spinor phases cancel and
P^JVμ​(x)P^−1=Pμν​JVν​(xP​),P^JAμ​(x)P^−1=−Pμν​JAν​(xP​).
(13)
The extra sign for the axial current follows from γ0γ5γ0=−γ5. Combining these transformations with part (c), both currents transform under CP symmetry as JV,Aμ​(x)↦−Pμν​JV,Aν​(xP​). The vector relativistic quantum field transforms as Vμ(x)↦ηC​Pμν​Vν†(xP​). The two parity matrices cancel in the tensor contraction, giving
(CP)[ψˉ​γμ(gv​−ga​γ5)ψVμ​](x)(CP)−1=−ηC​[ψˉ​γμ(gv​−ga​γ5)ψ](xP​)Vμ†​(xP​).​
(14)
For a real vector relativistic quantum field with ηC​=−1, the interaction is invariant under CP symmetry. A complex vector relativistic quantum field instead maps to the conjugate-field interaction, which must be compared with its Hermitian conjugation partner.
