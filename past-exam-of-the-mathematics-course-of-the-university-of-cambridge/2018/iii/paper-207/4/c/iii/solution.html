Let dj​ be the number of events at potential time aj​ and let rj​ be the size of the risk set immediately before that time, including observations censored at aj​. Define the discrete conditional event probability
qj​=P(T=aj​∣T≥aj​).
(27)
Under independent censoring, and ignoring the separate censoring distribution, the survival likelihood factorizes as
L({qj​})∝∏j​qjdj​​(1−qj​)rj​−dj​.
(28)
This follows by multiplying a survival factor for each individual at risk who does not fail at aj​, and an event factor for each failure. The log factor dj​logqj​+(rj​−dj​)log(1−qj​) is maximized at q​j​=dj​/rj​, with the corresponding boundary values when dj​=0 or dj​=rj​.
The survival function is the product of successive conditional survival probabilities. Therefore
F(t)=aj​≤t∏​(1−rj​dj​​).​
(29)
This is the Kaplan–Meier estimator. A product over no event times is 1. If everyone remaining experiences an event, the product becomes zero and stays zero; subsequent empty risk sets supply no additional factor.
