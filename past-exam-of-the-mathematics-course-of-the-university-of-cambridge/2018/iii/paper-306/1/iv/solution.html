Let q=σ/L, with L>0, and let Z=X1+iX2. Direct differentiation gives Z¨=Z′′=−Z/L2, while the time coordinate also satisfies the wave equation. Using the Minkowski metric,
X˙⋅X′=0,X˙2=−cos2q,X′2=cos2q.
(12)
Thus both Virasoro constraints hold, and the induced worldsheet metric is gμν​=cos2qdiag(−1,1). The configuration solves the Nambu–Goto action equations in its interior.
Place the fixed end at σ=0. The first free endpoint has Z′=eit/Lcosq=0 at q=π/2. For the usual single unfolded rotating Nambu–Goto string,
0≤σ≤2πL​,ℓproper​=∫0πL/2​∣Z′∣dσ=L.​
(13)
This parameter interval need not equal the earlier interval [0,π], since that interval was a coordinate convention. The proper length is measured on a constant-X0 slice; the local motion is perpendicular to the string, so there is no longitudinal Lorentz contraction.
All points have the same phase t/L, hence the segment rotates rigidly with angular velocity Ω=1/L. At radius r=Lsinq, its speed is v=r/L. Therefore the free tip moves at the speed of light. This is consistent with its Neumann boundary condition: X′=0 and the Virasoro constraints imply X˙2=0 at the free tip. The null endpoint is a boundary limit, not a nondegenerate interior point.
In this conformal gauge, Pm​=TX˙m​, so the energy density per σ is −P0​=T. Equivalently, the energy density per proper length is T/1−r2/L2​. Thus
E=T∫0πL/2​dσ=2πTL​,Erest​=TL,
(14)
and the rotational kinetic energy is
Erot​=(2π​−1)TL=(1−π2​)E.​
(15)
The angular momentum about the fixed point is
J=∫dσ(X1P2​−X2P1​)=TL∫0πL/2​sin2(σ/L)dσ=4πTL2​.
(16)
Therefore the Regge trajectory is
E=2πTL​,J=πTE2​,β=πT1​.​
(17)
The endpoint conditions alone also admit folded extensions with parameter length (n+21​)πL, n≥0. Their proper length, energy and angular momentum are (2n+1) times the values above, and β=1/[(2n+1)πT]. The displayed answer uses the implicit unfolded-segment convention.
