The original PDF has a sign error in the stated multiplier transformation; the TeX transcription has the consistent plus sign. Keeping the PDF's displayed minus sign mechanically would instead give i∫dtbi​(c˙i−cjλˉkfjk​i). That expression exponentiates the determinant of the printed transformation, but that transformation does not preserve the stated action with the canonical convention above. It cannot be used as the invariant result without changing another convention consistently.
