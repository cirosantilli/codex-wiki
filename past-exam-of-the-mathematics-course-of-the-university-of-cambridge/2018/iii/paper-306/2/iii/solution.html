For covariant quantization of the bosonic string,
[xm,pn​]=iδnm​,[αjm​,αkn​]=jηmnδj+k,0​,(αkm​)†=α−km​.
(28)
All other commutators between independent canonical variables vanish. The covariant Fock vacuum obeys αkm​∣0;p⟩=0 for every k>0 and every m. Normal ordering moves these annihilating string oscillators to the right and gives
Lj​=21​∑k∈Z​:αj−k​⋅αk​:,L0​=2πTp2​+Ncov​,Ncov​=∑k>0​α−k​⋅αk​.
(29)
Then Ncov​∣0;p⟩=0. Its commutator [Ncov​,α−km​]=kα−km​ grades a Fock state basis by N=∑knkm​, although its inner product is indefinite because of the timelike oscillator.
The quantum Virasoro algebra has central charge D:
[Lj​,Lk​]=(j−k)Lj+k​+12D​j(j2−1)δj+k,0​.​
(30)
The Virasoro central extension comes from commutators needed to order infinite oscillator sums; it is a quantum effect absent from the classical Poisson brackets. Replacing a classical Poisson bracket by a commutator cannot recover that term without a regularized ordering calculation.
If a state were annihilated by every nonzero Lj​, the j=1,k=−1 commutator would imply L0​∣ψ⟩=0. The j=2,k=−2 commutator would then imply (D/2)∣ψ⟩=0. For D>0, only the zero vector is annihilated by all nonzero Virasoro constraints. This explains why only positive modes annihilate a physical string state.
The vacuum is a physical string state when p2=2πTa. At level one all states have the form ∣A;p⟩=Am​α−1m​∣0;p⟩. Since [Lj​,αkm​]=−kαj+km​, the positive-mode Virasoro constraints give
L1​∣A;p⟩=πT​A⋅p​∣0;p⟩,Lj​∣A;p⟩=0(j≥2).
(31)
Thus the complete level-one conditions and norm are
p2=2πT(a−1),A⋅p=0,⟨A;p∣A;p⟩=Am∗​ηmnAn​,​
(32)
with the common vacuum normalization suppressed.
For a>1, choose spacelike momentum pm=(0,k,0,…), k2=2πT(a−1). A purely timelike polarization has A⋅p=0 and norm −1. Hence a negative-norm physical string state exists when a>1.
For a<1, the mass-shell condition gives M2=2πT(1−a)>0. In a rest frame, A⋅p=0 forces A0​=0, leaving D−1 positive-norm vector-particle polarizations, those of a massive vector.
For a=1 and nonzero null momentum, A⋅p=0 leaves a null direction Am​∝pm​. The corresponding null string state is p⋅α−1​∣0;p⟩=πT​L−1​∣0;p⟩. It is orthogonal to every physical string state because L1​ annihilates them. Quotienting by this gauge redundancy, Am​∼Am​+ζpm​, leaves D−2 positive vector-particle polarizations. Thus the level-one spectrum agrees with light-cone gauge in string theory at a=1. This level-one argument alone does not establish consistency or absence of negative norms at all higher levels.
