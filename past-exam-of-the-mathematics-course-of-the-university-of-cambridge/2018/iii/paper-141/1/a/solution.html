Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2018 / iii / Paper 141 / 1 / a / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2018 iii Paper 141 1 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 135
Use the meridian of a knot μ and Seifert longitude λ of the unknot on its boundary torus. The unknot's knot exterior is a solid torus whose disk-bounding curve is λ. A genus-one Heegaard diagram is therefore
Σ=R2/Z2,α=λ=(0,1),β=5μ+3λ=(5,3).​
(1)
Here the horizontal coordinate is μ and the vertical coordinate is λ. The drawing uses a small translate of α to avoid intersections on the edge of the square; opposite edges are identified. The algebraic intersection number of curves on an oriented surface has absolute value five.
Starting with Σ×[0,1], attach a three-dimensional two-handle along α×{0}, using its surface framing, and cap the resulting sphere with a three-handle. This gives the solid torus on that side. Attach a two-handle along β×{1}, again using the surface framing, and cap its sphere with a three-handle. The second solid torus has its disk-bounding curve identified with 5μ+3λ, so the resulting oriented lens space is precisely the specified 5/3 Dehn filling.
