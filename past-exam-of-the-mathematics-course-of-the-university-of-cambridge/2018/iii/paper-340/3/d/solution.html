For the multiresolution analysis Vj​ and its detail spaces Wj​, take the completed tensor product Vj​=Vj​⊗Vj​. Since Vj+1​=Vj​⊕Wj​,
Vj+1​=Vj​⊕(Wj​⊗Vj​)⊕(Vj​⊗Wj​)⊕(Wj​⊗Wj​).
(31)
The three tensor-product wavelets are
Ψ1(x,y)=ψ(x)φ(y),Ψ2(x,y)=φ(x)ψ(y),Ψ3(x,y)=ψ(x)ψ(y).
(32)
Taking products of members of the two one-dimensional families gives an orthonormal basis of each respective completed tensor product. Density and the coarse-scale trivial intersection yield
{2jΨa(2jx−k1​,2jy−k2​):a=1,2,3, j,k1​,k2​∈Z}​
(33)
as an orthonormal basis of L2(R2). The normalization is 2j in two dimensions. On the square, tensorize the interval-adapted wavelet basis and include the finite coarse scaling function part.
