No. For example, take R=k, any field. It is an Artinian ring, since its only ideals are zero and itself. In the formal power series ring k[[x]], however,
(x)⊋(x2)⊋(x3)⊋⋯
(45)
is a strictly descending chain of ideals. The inclusion is strict because xn has a nonzero coefficient of xn, whereas every element of (xn+1) has that coefficient zero. Thus k[[x]] fails the descending chain condition and is not Artinian.
The same argument works for any nonzero coefficient ring R: the coefficient 1 in xn cannot be produced by a multiple of xn+1. Therefore R[[x]] is never Artinian when R=0. The zero ring is the harmless exception.
