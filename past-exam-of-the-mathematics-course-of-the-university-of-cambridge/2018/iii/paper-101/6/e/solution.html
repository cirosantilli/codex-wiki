Z[i](2+i)​ is not integral over Z(5)​.​
(72)
This exhibits why localization of an integral extension at a prime need not be integral. Localizing both rings by the same multiplicative subset preserves an integral extension, but Z[i]P​ inverts additional elements, including 2−i, whose image at the other prime ideal (2−i) is zero. The original extension is integral because i satisfies the monic equation T2+1=0: the subalgebra generated by finitely many integral elements property applies to Z[i].
