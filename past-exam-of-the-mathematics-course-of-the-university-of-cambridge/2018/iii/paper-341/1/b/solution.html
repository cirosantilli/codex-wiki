Factor the first characteristic polynomial as
ρ(w)=(w−1)(w2−2αw+1).
(4)
The other two roots of a polynomial have product one. If −1<α<1, they are α±i1−α2​: distinct unit-modulus roots, both different from one. At α=−1, the root −1 is double; at α=1, the root one is triple. Outside [−1,1], one of the reciprocal real roots has modulus greater than one. Thus the root condition for a multistep method holds exactly for −1<α<1.

the method is convergent exactly when −1<α<1.​

The exceptional third-order formula is not zero-stable. At α=1, σ≡0 and ρ=(w−1)3: the recurrence contains no vector-field evaluations. It satisfies the displayed Taylor identities only formally and is a degenerate, nonconvergent formula, not a usable second-order ODE solver. This distinction avoids interpreting formal cancellation as convergence.
