Let T be the M×M tridiagonal matrix with diagonal two and adjacent entries minus one, incorporating zero Dirichlet boundary conditions. The actual PDF scheme is
Un+1=Gμ​Un,Gμ​=(I+μT)−1.
(19)
An orthonormal basis of eigenvectors of T has components 2/(M+1)​sin(jmπ/(M+1)), and substitution gives
λj​=2−2cosM+1jπ​=4sin22(M+1)jπ​>0,1≤j≤M.
(20)
They form an orthonormal basis by the spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices. Hence I+μT is invertible for every μ>0, and the amplification eigenvalues are gj​=(1+μλj​)−1∈(0,1). For the discrete L2 norm ∥U∥h2​=h∑m​∣Um​∣2,
∥Gμn​U∥h​≤∥U∥h​for all n≥0, μ>0.​
(21)
This bound has constant one independent of time step and mesh, which is the required stability of a numerical method. Therefore every positive Courant number is stable. If a forcing or local-defect sequence is added, the discrete variation-of-constants formula gives a bound by the initial norm plus the sum of those perturbation norms; this also justifies the global error conclusion in part (a).
