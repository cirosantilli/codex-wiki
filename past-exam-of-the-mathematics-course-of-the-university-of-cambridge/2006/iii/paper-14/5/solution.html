The general, or asymmetric Lovász local lemma, is as follows. Let E1​,…,Em​ be events, with a dependency graph of events having neighbour sets N(i). Each Ei​ must be independent of the sigma-algebra generated by all the events indexed by its nonneighbours. If numbers 0≤xi​<1 satisfy
P(Ei​)≤xi​∏j∈N(i)​(1−xj​),
(29)
then
P(⋂i=1m​Eic​)≥∏i=1m​(1−xi​)>0.
(30)
For completeness, the key inductive bound is P(Ei​∣⋂j∈S​Ejc​)≤xi​ whenever i∈/S. Split S into neighbours S1​ of i and nonneighbours S2​. If S1​ is empty, the dependency condition gives the bound directly. Otherwise,
P(Ei​∣⋂j∈S​Ejc​)≤P(⋂j∈S1​​Ejc​∣⋂j∈S2​​Ejc​)P(Ei​∣⋂j∈S2​​Ejc​)​≤∏j∈S1​​(1−xj​)P(Ei​)​≤xi​.
(31)
The denominator bound follows by exposing the exclusions in S1​ one at a time and using the induction hypothesis with strictly fewer conditioning events. The same induction ensures that all conditioning events have positive probability. Finally multiply the successive conditional probabilities of avoiding E1​,…,Em​ to obtain the stated lower bound.
For the symmetric form, suppose each event has probability at most q and the dependency graph has maximum degree D≥1. Choose xi​=1/(D+1). Then
xi​∏j∈N(i)​(1−xj​)≥D+11​(D+1D​)D≥e(D+1)1​.
(32)
The final inequality follows from (1+1/D)D≤e. Thus eq(D+1)≤1 suffices for positive probability that no bad event occurs. If D=0, the dependency condition gives joint independence and the same criterion implies q<1, so the conclusion follows directly.
Apply this to a fair independent two-colouring of the hypergraph vertices, with EW​ the event that hypergraph edge W is monochromatic. Its probability is q=21−r. Events on disjoint hypergraph edges use disjoint independent variables, giving a dependency graph by joining intersecting hypergraph edges. Each hypergraph edge meets at most
D≤∑v∈W​(dH​(v)−1)≤r(Δ−1)
(33)
other hypergraph edges; repeated counting only enlarges this upper bound. The slightly looser bound D+1≤r(Δ+1) is enough for the printed condition. Indeed,
Δ<2re2r​−1⟹e,21−r(D+1)≤e,21−rr(Δ+1)<1.
(34)
The symmetric Lovász local lemma therefore gives a proper two-colouring. A hypergraph with no hypergraph edges is trivially colourable and needs no dependency calculation.
