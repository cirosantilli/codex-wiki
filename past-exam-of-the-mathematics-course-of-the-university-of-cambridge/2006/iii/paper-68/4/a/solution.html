The boundary term is zero at both ends. The same calculation with two different functions proves that the associated form is a symmetric bilinear form. Positivity here concerns the homogeneous domain; the condition u(0)=1 instead defines an affine set of admissible solutions.

Compactly supported test functions first yield u′′=xu in the sense of distributional derivatives. Since xu∈L2, the solution is in H2. Integration by parts then leaves u′(1)w(1)=0 for every w∈V, hence u′(1)=0. The other boundary condition is built into 1+V. Conversely a solution of this boundary value problem satisfies the first variation condition. Finally,
