Use the truncated tensor algebra T(N)(Rd)=⨁k=0N​(Rd)⊗k, with truncated concatenation product. The step-N path signature is
SN​(x)s,t​=1+∑k=1N​∫s<u1​<⋯<uk​<t​dxu1​​⊗⋯⊗dxuk​​.
(1)
These iterated integrals of a path exist as Riemann-Stieltjes integrals, since a Lipschitz continuous path on a compact interval has bounded variation. The degree-zero coordinate is 1, and the degree-one coordinate is xt​−xs​.
Let St​=SN​(x)0,t​ and write St(k)​ for its degree-k coordinate. Integrating first over all variables except the last gives
St(k)​=∫0t​Su(k−1)​⊗dxu​,St(0)​=1.
(2)
Thus dSt(k)​=St(k−1)​⊗dxt​ for 1≤k≤N. If ei​ is a standard basis vector, define the linear vector field Wi​(g)=g⊗ei​, with degrees above N discarded. The component identities are precisely the controlled ordinary differential equation
dSt​=i=1∑d​Wi​(St​)dxti​=St​⊗dxt​,S0​=1.​
(3)
For a Lipschitz driver this may also be written S˙t​=St​⊗x˙t​ almost everywhere. Recursive integration of its levels proves uniqueness and recovers the signature formula. The Chen identity follows by splitting each integration simplex at an intermediate time.
