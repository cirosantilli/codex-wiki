The direct image sheaf is defined by
(ϕ∗​F)(U)=F(ϕ−1U)​.
(6)
Its restrictions are the restrictions of F. Inverse images preserve covers and intersections, so the sheaf gluing axiom holds immediately. The morphism of ringed spaces supplies
ϕ#:OX​(U)⟶OY​(ϕ−1U).
(7)
An element of OX​(U) acts on the displayed sections through this ring map and the original OY​-module action. This makes the direct image sheaf an OX​-module; no additional sheafification is required.
