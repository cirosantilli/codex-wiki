The order-k divided difference defining Mi​ is a finite linear combination of values at its knots. Hence integration can be interchanged with it directly. For a knot value u∈[a,b],
∫ab​(u−t)+k−1​dt=k(u−a)k​.
(45)
It follows that
∫ab​Mi​(t)dt​=k[ti​,…,ti+k​](u↦∫ab​(u−t)+k−1​dt)=[ti​,…,ti+k​](u−a)k=1.​
(46)
The final equality holds because the order-k divided difference of a degree-k monic polynomial is its leading coefficient, one. Thus
∫ab​Mi​(t)dt=1.​
(47)
This proves the unit-integral normalization of a B-spline. Its support is contained in [ti​,ti+k​]: below all its knots, the sampled truncated power agrees with a degree-k−1 polynomial, annihilated by the order-k divided difference; above all its knots, every sample is zero. No mass is omitted by integration over [a,b].
Since Ni​=(ti+k​−ti​)Mi​/k, the corresponding partition-normalized B-spline has integral (ti+k​−ti​)/k. Its normalization is different from that of Mi​.
