Its constant particular solution is 3; the two characteristic roots of the homogeneous recurrence are ρ=2−3​ and ρ−1=2+3​. Imposing the two boundary values gives

This can also be checked by direct substitution, since ρ+ρ−1=4. The numerator is smallest at the central index or central pair of indices: its successive difference is (1−ρ)(ρn−i−ρi). Thus, with j=⌊(n+1)/2⌋, the exact inverse norm of midpoint cubic spline collocation is
∥An−1​∥ℓ∞​=uj​=3[1−1+ρn+1ρj+ρn+1−j​]<3.​
(39)
Equivalently, if n=2m−1 the fraction is 2ρm/(1+ρ2m), and if n=2m it is (ρm+ρm+1)/(1+ρ2m+1). The finite norm is not exactly three: it equals 3/2 for n=1, two for n=2, and tends to three as n grows. Hence three is the sharp dimension-independent bound for these inverse matrix norms.
