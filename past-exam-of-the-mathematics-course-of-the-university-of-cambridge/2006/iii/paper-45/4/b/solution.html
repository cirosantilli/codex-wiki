In the infinite sites mutation model, mutations occur as independent Poisson processes at rate θ/2 per lineage per unit of the coalescent time used in part (a). Conditional on the genealogy, superposition over branches gives a Poisson distribution with mean θL/2. Since this depends only on L, conditioning on total length alone gives
S∣L,θ∼Poisson(θL/2).​
(33)
Each mutation occurs at a new site and below the common ancestor, so it contributes exactly one segregating site. The law of iterated expectation therefore gives, with an−1​=∑i=1n−1​1/i,
E[S∣θ]=2θ​EL=θan−1​.​
(34)
Here θ is fixed when taking the expectation. If it is also random under a proper prior with finite mean, the unconditional expectation is an−1​Eθ.
