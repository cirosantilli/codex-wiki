Xe2​1−Xe​​=π22ζ(3)​η(me​2πT​)3/2eI/T.​

If the positive right side is denoted by A(T), the physical root is
Xe​(T)=1+1+4A(T)​2​.​

This form avoids subtracting nearly equal numbers when A is small, and makes the limits transparent: Xe​≃1−A for A≪1, whereas Xe​≃A−1/2 for A≫1. It is an equilibrium prediction; a kinetic cosmological recombination treatment is needed after reactions cease to keep up with expansion.
