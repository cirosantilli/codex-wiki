 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2006 iii Paper 30 3 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 254
Write A2​(N,δ) for the largest number of codewords in a binary length-N code with minimum Hamming distance at least δ. The volume of a Hamming ball does not depend on its centre and is
vN​(d)=∑j=0d​(jN​).
(19)
For the Hamming bound, put r=⌊(δ−1)/2⌋. The radius-r Hamming balls around distinct codewords are disjoint: a point belonging to two such balls would, by the triangle inequality for Hamming distance, put their centres at distance at most 2r<δ. Counting points in the binary cube therefore gives
∣C∣vN​(r)≤2N.
(20)
For the Gilbert–Varshamov bound, choose a code maximal under inclusion among those with minimum Hamming distance at least δ, for example by successively adding any allowable word. Its radius-(δ−1) Hamming balls cover the cube. Otherwise an uncovered word would be at distance at least δ from every codeword and could be added, contradicting maximality. Thus
2N≤∣C∣vN​(δ−1).
(21)
The lower bound is an existence assertion about a suitable code, while the upper bound holds for every such code. Together they give
⌈vN​(δ−1)2N​⌉≤A2​(N,δ)≤⌊vN​(⌊(δ−1)/2⌋)2N​⌋.​
(22)
Let h2​(x)=−xlog2​x−(1−x)log2​(1−x) be the binary entropy. For δ=⌊λN⌋,
Nδ−1​⟶λ,N⌊(δ−1)/2⌋​⟶2λ​.
(23)
The assumed Hamming ball volume exponent applies also to any integer radii dN​ with dN​/N→θ∈(0,1/2). Indeed, for each small ε>0 these radii eventually lie between ⌊(θ−ε)N⌋ and ⌊(θ+ε)N⌋. monotonicity of vN​ sandwiches its normalized logarithm between limits h2​(θ−ε) and h2​(θ+ε); continuity then lets ε tend to zero. Consequently the asymptotic Gilbert–Varshamov bound and asymptotic Hamming bound have respective exponential scales
vN​(δ−1)2N​=2N(1−h2​(λ))+o(N),vN​(⌊(δ−1)/2⌋)2N​=2N(1−h2​(λ/2))+o(N).
(24)
Equivalently, the optimal code rate satisfies
1−h2​(λ)≤N→∞liminf​Nlog2​A2​(N,⌊λN⌋)​≤N→∞limsup​Nlog2​A2​(N,⌊λN⌋)​≤1−h2​(λ/2).​
(25)
These are bounds on the rate, not a claim that the optimal rate has a known limit or equals either endpoint.
