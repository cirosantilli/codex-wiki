For the specified flat matter-vacuum model,
F(z)=s−0.3s3+0.7​.
(22)
Its small-redshift behaviour is F(z)=0.55z+O(z2), since q0​=Ωm,0​/2−ΩΛ,0​=−0.55. At high redshift, matter dominates and F∼−0.3​s3/2, so the initially positive redshift drift must turn negative. To locate the crossing, square s=0.3s3+0.7​; both sides are positive, so squaring introduces no positive-s spurious root. Factorization gives
0.3s3−s2+0.7=(s−1)(0.3s2−0.7s−0.7).
(23)
Besides z=0, the physical nonzero redshift-drift root in flat matter-Lambda cosmology is
z∗​=61+133​​≃2.089.​
(24)
The redshift drift is positive for 0<z<z∗​ and negative for z>z∗​. Its maximum satisfies 1=0.45s2/0.3s3+0.7​ and lies near z=0.949, where F≃0.240. This zero is distinct from the acceleration transition at zacc​=(2ΩΛ,0​/Ωm,0​)1/3−1≃0.671: redshift drift compares expansion at two epochs, while acceleration is local to one epoch.
