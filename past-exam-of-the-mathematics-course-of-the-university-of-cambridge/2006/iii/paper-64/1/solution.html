An orthonormal coframe writes a metric as g=ηab​θa⊗θb with constant diagonal signature matrix η. In Cartan's first structure equation, solve
dθa+ωab​∧θb=0,ωab​=−ωba​,ωab​=ηac​ωcb​.
(1)
These are the torsion-free and metric-compatibility conditions and determine the Levi-Civita connection. Then Cartan's second structure equation gives
Ωab​=dωab​+ωac​∧ωcb​=21​Rabcd​θc∧θd.
(2)
Contract Rabad​ to obtain the Ricci tensor and then contract again for the scalar curvature. This fixes the curvature convention; reversing the definition of the Riemann curvature tensor reverses the resulting curvature signs.
On z>0, choose θ0=dt/z, θ1=dx/z, θ2=dy/z, θ3=dz/z, with η=diag(−1,1,1,1). For a=0,1,2,
dθa=θa∧θ3,dθ3=0.
(3)
A torsion-free metric connection therefore has
ωa3​=−θa,ω3a​=ηaa​θa,ωab​=0(a,b<3).
(4)
For example, Ωa3​=−dθa=−θa∧θ3, while for a,b<3,
Ωab​=ωa3​∧ω3b​=−ηbb​θa∧θb.
(5)
Metric antisymmetry supplies the remaining components, giving uniformly
Ωab​=−θa∧θb​,Rabcd​=−(ηac​ηbd​−ηad​ηbc​).​
(6)
Thus the metric has constant sectional curvature −1. In four dimensions,
Ricμν​=−3gμν​,R=−12.​
(7)
It is an Einstein manifold, with Einstein constant −3 in the stated curvature convention.
These are Poincare coordinates on anti-de Sitter spacetime of unit radius. Constant curvature gives the maximal ten-dimensional local isometry algebra so(3,2). This can also be checked directly: set u=(t,x,y), ηab​=diag(−1,1,1) and ua​=ηab​ub. The ten independent Killing vector fields are
Pa​=∂a​,Mab​=ua​∂b​−ub​∂a​,D=ua∂a​+z∂z​,Ka​=2ua​D−(ubub​+z2)∂a​.
(8)
Translations and Lorentz transformations leave the numerator and z unchanged. A dilation rescales numerator and denominator equally. For Ka​, direct differentiation gives LKa​​η(4)=4ua​η(4) and Ka​(z)=2ua​z, so the conformal factor cancels and LKa​​g=0.
Hence the maximally extended Anti-de Sitter spacetime has connected isometry group locally SO0​(3,2), with the appropriate covering group if one unwraps its time coordinate. The displayed coordinates cover only a patch. The ten local generators do not all give globally complete flows preserving that patch; for example special conformal flows can cross its horizon. The manifest complete patch symmetries include the boundary Poincare transformations and positive dilations. This distinguishes local maximal symmetry from the global isometries of a chosen coordinate domain.
