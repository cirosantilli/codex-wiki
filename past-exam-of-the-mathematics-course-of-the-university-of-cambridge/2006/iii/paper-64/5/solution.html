The two unheaded requests are addressed here; the individual group-cover calculations are in the scoped parts below. A quaternion q=a+bi+cj+dk has norm ∣q∣2=a2+b2+c2+d2. Thus the unit quaternions form exactly the sphere S3⊂R4, with quaternion multiplication giving its smooth group structure. The explicit identification with the special unitary group SU(2) is
q⟼(a+ib−c+id​c+ida−ib​).
(39)
The matrices representing i,j,k satisfy the quaternion multiplication relations. The displayed matrix obeys U†U=∣q∣2I and detU=∣q∣2; conversely every SU(2) matrix has that form with ∣q∣=1. Hence S3 is the manifold of unit quaternions, and its group is SU(2).
For the exterior-square double cover of SO(3,3), fix a basis e1​,…,e4​ and volume element v=e1​∧e2​∧e3​∧e4​. Define the symmetric bilinear form Q on the six-dimensional exterior square by
α∧β=Q(α,β)v.
(40)
It is symmetric because two-forms commute under the wedge product. Put
a1​=e1​∧e2​,a2​=e1​∧e3​,a3​=e1​∧e4​,b1​=e3​∧e4​,b2​=−e2​∧e4​,b3​=e2​∧e3​.
(41)
Then Q(ai​,aj​)=Q(bi​,bj​)=0 and Q(ai​,bj​)=δij​. The Gram matrix is (0I3​​I3​0​): the vectors (ai​+bi​)/2​ have norm +1, while (ai​−bi​)/2​ have norm −1. Therefore the quadratic form ω↦ω∧ω has signature (3,3).
For A∈SL(4,R),
(Λ2Aα)∧(Λ2Aβ)=Λ4A(α∧β)=α∧β.
(42)
Thus ρ(A)=Λ2A preserves Q. The special linear group is connected: polar decomposition of an invertible real matrix writes A=OP with O∈SO(4) and P positive definite of determinant one, and both factors can be joined to the identity. The image consequently lies in SO0​(3,3).
If Λ2A=I, then Au∧Aw=u∧w for every pair, so A preserves every two-plane. A line is an intersection of two such planes, hence every line is preserved. A linear map preserving every line is scalar: apply it to basis vectors and then to their pairwise sums. Thus A=λI, and Λ2A=I forces λ2=1. The kernel is exactly {±I}. It is discrete, so the derivative of ρ is injective. Both Lie algebras have dimension 15, making the image open; an open subgroup of a connected group is the whole group. Hence
SO0​(3,3)≅SL(4,R)/{±I}.​
(43)
The identity-component restriction is essential. The same principle of an explicit form-preserving representation, kernel calculation and dimension argument supplies the five remaining covers.
