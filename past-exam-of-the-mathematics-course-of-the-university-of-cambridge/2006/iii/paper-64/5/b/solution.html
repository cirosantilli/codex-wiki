Identify a Minkowski vector (t,x,y,z) with the Hermitian matrix representation of Minkowski four-vectors
X=(t+zx+iy​x−iyt−z​),detX=t2−x2−y2−z2.
(46)
For A∈SL(2,C), the real-linear action X↦AXA† preserves Hermiticity and the determinant, so it preserves the Lorentz quadratic form. It also preserves the cone of positive-definite Hermitian matrices, hence the future timelike cone. The group SL(2,C) is connected, by polar decomposition of an invertible complex matrix into SU(2) and positive determinant-one Hermitian matrices. Thus its image lies in the Proper orthochronous Lorentz group.
If AXA†=X for every Hermitian X, taking X=I makes A unitary; the remaining equations then say it commutes with every Hermitian matrix. Their real span is all Hermitian matrices and their complex span is all complex matrices, so A is scalar. The determinant condition leaves precisely A=±I. A discrete kernel gives an injective differential, and the real dimensions of both groups are six. The image is therefore open and equals the connected Lorentz group:
SO0​(3,1)≅SL(2,C)/{±I}.​
(47)
The quotient does not include the disconnected time-reversing component of SO(3,1).
