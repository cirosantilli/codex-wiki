The Poincare duality pairing on Hm(X;Q) is B(α,γ)=⟨α⌣γ,[X]⟩. It is nondegenerate, and graded commutativity of the cup product gives B(α,γ)=−B(γ,α) because m is odd. In particular B(α,α)=0 over Q. Part (b) therefore makes bm​ even, so
χ(X)≡0(mod2).​

For the failure without orientability, RP2 is a closed nonorientable surface with one cell in each dimension zero, one and two; hence χ=1−1+1=1. More generally, RP4r+2 is nonorientable and has Euler characteristic one. Thus the orientation hypothesis in the Euler characteristic parity in dimensions congruent to two modulo four is essential.
