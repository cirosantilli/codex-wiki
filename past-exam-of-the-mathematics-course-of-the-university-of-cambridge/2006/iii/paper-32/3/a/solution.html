Fix x>0 and let A={M0​<x}∈F0​. On A, continuity of the paths implies 0≤Mt∧Tx​​≤x, and MTx​​=x on {Tx​<∞}. Bounded-time optional stopping gives, for every t,
E[1A​Mt∧Tx​​∣F0​]=1A​M0​.
(21)
On A∩{Tx​=∞}, the stopped process is Mt​→0; on A∩{Tx​<∞} it eventually equals x. The bound by x permits conditional dominated convergence, yielding
x1A​P(Tx​<∞∣F0​)=1A​M0​.
(22)
On Ac, Tx​=0, so the conditional hitting probability is one. Finally, a continuous nonnegative path tending to zero attains any positive supremum: after a sufficiently late time it is below half that supremum, and its maximum on the earlier compact interval is attained. Thus {M∗≥x}={Tx​<∞}, including equality at the level. We have proved the conditional form of the maximal identity for a continuous nonnegative local martingale tending to zero:
P(M∗≥x∣F0​)=1∧xM0​​.​
(23)
The restriction to A before taking the limit is important: M0​ need not be bounded, and the unstopped martingale need not have uniform integrability.
