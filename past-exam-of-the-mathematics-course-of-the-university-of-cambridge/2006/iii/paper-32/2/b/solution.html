ϕ′(λ)=∫xeλxμ(dx),EλX1​=ϕ(λ)ϕ′(λ)​(λ>0).​

The mean under one-sided exponential tilting needs an endpoint qualification. At λ=0, finiteness of positive exponential moments makes X1+​ integrable, but gives no integrability of X1−​. The formula holds with the right derivative and the extended mean ϕ+′​(0)=EX1​∈[−∞,∞). To see this, apply monotone convergence to (1−e−hX1−​)/h and dominated convergence to (ehX1+​−1)/h as h↓0.
