The PDF writes only k≥0, without explicitly declaring integer levels. If it is read as allowing real k, then τk​=τ⌈k⌉​ and the displayed factor must be e−λ⌈k⌉; the hitting probability is likewise e−λ0​⌈k⌉. For example, increments +1 and −1 with probabilities 1/3 and 2/3 have λ0​=log2: hitting level 1/2 has probability 1/2, not 2−1/2. The integer interpretation supplies the intended formula.
