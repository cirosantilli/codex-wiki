Only overlapping innovations contribute to the autocovariance function of the moving-average process. Set c0​=1,c1​=θ1​,c2​=θ2​. The overlap formula is γk​=σ2∑j​cj​cj+∣k∣​, with coefficients outside 0,1,2 set to zero. Thus
γk​=⎩⎨⎧​σ2(1+θ12​+θ22​),σ2θ1​(1+θ2​),σ2θ2​,0,​k=0,∣k∣=1,∣k∣=2,∣k∣>2.​​
(11)
The time-series spectral density is the Fourier sum of this finite covariance sequence:
f(λ)=2πσ2​[1+θ12​+θ22​+2θ1​(1+θ2​)cosλ+2θ2​cos2λ].​
(12)
It also equals σ2∣1+θ1​e−iλ+θ2​e−2iλ∣2/(2π), which directly verifies nonnegativity. Integrating from −π gives the requested time-series spectral distribution:
F(λ)=2πσ2​[(1+θ12​+θ22​)(λ+π)+2θ1​(1+θ2​)sinλ+θ2​sin2λ],−π≤λ≤π.​
(13)
Extend it by zero below −π and by γ0​ above π. In particular its total mass is the process variance, not necessarily one.
