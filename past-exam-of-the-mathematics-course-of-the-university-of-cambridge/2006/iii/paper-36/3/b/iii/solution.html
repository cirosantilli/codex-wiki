Two distinct triangles in a graph with at most one common vertex use disjoint edges, so their indicator random variables are independent and have zero covariance. A pair sharing an edge has covariance p5−p6. The count from part (i) gives
Var(X)=(3n​)p3(1−p3)+3(3n​)(n−3)p5(1−p).
(16)
With μ=(3n​)p3, the triangle variance in a binomial random graph therefore satisfies
μ2Var(X)​≤(3n​)p31​+(3n​)p3(n−3)​=O((np)−3)+O((n2p)−1).
(17)
Both terms tend to zero when np→∞, because n2p=n(np)→∞. Applying part (ii) proves
P(X>0)⟶1​,
(18)
completing the triangle-existence threshold in a binomial random graph.
