The original Figure 1 has the allowed chain 1↔2↔3↔4. Its positive resonance frequencies are ω1​=E2​−E1​, ω2​=E2​−E3​ and ω3​=E4​−E3​ in units ℏ=1; level 2 lies above level 3. Use a pulse at ω1​ to transfer level population from 1 to 2, then one at ω2​ to transfer it from 2 to 3, and finally one at ω3​ to transfer it from 3 to 4.
For a selected pair, choose the optical phase so the effective Quantum Hamiltonian has a fixed off-diagonal rotation axis. If its coupling coefficient is gjk​(t)=Ajk​(t)∣djk​∣/2 in this normalization, the active two-dimensional propagator is
Ujk​=cosβIjk​−isinβKjk​,β=∫gjk​(t)dt,
(14)
where Kjk2​=Ijk​. A full level population transfer has β=π/2. Equivalently, in the Rabi-frequency convention ΩR​=2gjk​, the required area is ∫ΩR​dt=π. On spectator levels the embedded two-level quantum rotation is the identity.
Thus three selective π pulses at ω1​,ω2​,ω3​, in that order, transfer ∣1⟩ to ∣4⟩ up to global phase. A strongly regular quantum Hamiltonian separates the resonances; envelopes long enough that their bandwidth and coupling strength are small relative to unwanted frequency gaps make off-resonant excitation arbitrarily small within the selective-pulse limit. The phases accumulated along this path do not affect the final level population.
