The dimension assumption supplies a nonzero h∈S2​(Γ0​(11)). The projective index of the Gamma 0 congruence subgroup is twelve: reduction modulo eleven identifies its cosets with the twelve points of P1(F11​). There are two modular cusps, by the preceding classification. There are no effective elliptic fixed points. An order-two fixed point would require a root of x2+1 modulo eleven, which does not exist; an order-three fixed point would require a root of x2+x+1, equivalent to a nontrivial cube root of unity in a group of order ten, which also does not exist.
The regular-cusp valence formula on a torsion-free modular curve therefore gives
∑P∈X0​(11)​ordP​h=122⋅12​=2.
(15)
All orders are nonnegative and both cusp orders are at least one. Thus h has order exactly one at both modular cusps and has no zeros in the half-plane. One way to justify the degree count here is the genus formula for a modular curve: the same index, cusp and elliptic data give genus one. The invariant differential h(τ)dτ has cusp order ordh−1, because dτ is a nonzero constant times dqc​/qc​. Its total canonical degree 2g−2=0 therefore makes the sum of the two cusp-adjusted orders and the interior orders zero, giving precisely the count above.
Let H(τ)=Δ(τ)Δ(11τ). By the product formula it has no zeros in the half-plane; by the cusp-order table it has order twelve at each cusp. Its weight is twenty-four. Therefore H/h12 is a weight-zero modular function, with neither zeros nor poles anywhere on the compactified modular curve. It is constant by the maximum modulus principle on a compact surface. Normalize h so its leading Fourier coefficient at infinity is one. Since H=q12+O(q13), that constant becomes one:
h12=Δ(τ)Δ(11τ),h(τ)=qn≥1∏​(1−qn)2(1−q11n)2.​
(16)
This fixes the holomorphic twelfth root by its leading term and proves the weight-two discriminant root at level eleven is a nonzero member, hence a generator, of the given space. Taking an arbitrary pointwise twelfth root without this argument would not establish modularity: it could carry an unwanted character.
