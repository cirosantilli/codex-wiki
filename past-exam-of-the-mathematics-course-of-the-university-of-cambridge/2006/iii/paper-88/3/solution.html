It is holomorphic in the complex upper half-plane; at each modular cusp, the rational change of variable preserves cusp decay by the upper-triangular factorization used in 2(i). Fourier coefficient extraction on the line Imτ=1/n gives an​=O(nk/2), so Res>1+k/2 suffices for the interchange.
Split the integral at one and change y to 1/y in its lower part. The phase-normalized Fricke functional equation becomes transparent:
ΛN​(f,s)=∫1∞​F(y)(ys−1+εyk−s−1)dy.​

The integral defines an entire function: exponential decay dominates every power and every logarithmic derivative uniformly on compact s sets. Replacing s by k−s and using ε2=1 now proves

This gives the full analytic continuation of the completed L-function of a cusp form, not merely a formal identity in its initial convergence region.
