Construct the tensor-product weight diagram by placing one octet diagram at each triplet representation weight u,d,s and adding multiplicities where the shifted diagrams overlap. Its highest weight is
u+α3​=(1,3​2​)=2ω1​+ω2​,
(26)
so remove the (2,1) diagram by highest-weight character subtraction. Its unequal-sided hexagon has nine boundary representation weights of multiplicity one and three interior representation weights of multiplicity two, giving dimension 15.
To see the remainder explicitly, measure diagram coordinates by (a,b)=(2h1​,23​h2​). After subtracting this 15, the residual representation weights are
(a,b)multiplicity​(0,4)1​(−2,−2)1​(2,−2)1​(1,1)2​(−1,1)2​(0,−2)2​​
(27)
The next highest weight is (h1​,h2​)=(0,2/3​)=2ω2​, with Dynkin labels (0,2). The conjugate sextet 6 is a triangular weight diagram containing each of these six representation weights once. Subtracting it leaves (1,1),(−1,1),(0,−2) once each in the integer coordinates: precisely the triplet. The SU(3) triplet-octet decomposition is therefore
3⊗8=15(2,1)​⊕6(0,2)​⊕3(1,0)​.​
(28)
