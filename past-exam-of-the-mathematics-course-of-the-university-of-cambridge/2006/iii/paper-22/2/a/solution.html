Use the convention ∇=d+A with anti-Hermitian connection one-forms. For a unitary complex line bundle, A is locally an iR-valued one-form and F∇​=dA, since the Lie algebra is abelian. Under a change of unitary frame, the vector-bundle curvature transforms by conjugation, which is trivial for U(1). Hence F∇​ is a globally defined imaginary-valued two-form, and iF∇​ is an ordinary real two-form. Equivalently, End(L) has its canonical scalar trivialization, so no nontrivial coefficient bundle remains.
Locally dF∇​=d2A=0, and these equalities patch globally. If another unitary connection is ∇′=∇+iβ for a global real one-form β, then
F∇′​=F∇​+idβ,iF∇′​=iF∇​−dβ.​
(5)
Thus the real vector-bundle curvature is closed and its de Rham cohomology class is independent of the unitary connection.
With the First Chern class normalization c1​(L)R​=[iF∇​/(2π)], the transverse section also identifies this class as PD[Σ]. Indeed, the complex orientation of each normal disk makes the section wind positively once around a transverse zero. The Euler class of the underlying oriented plane bundle counts these zeros with their induced orientation, and equals c1​(L). This records the role of the section and orientation hypotheses; connection independence itself did not require choosing a section.
