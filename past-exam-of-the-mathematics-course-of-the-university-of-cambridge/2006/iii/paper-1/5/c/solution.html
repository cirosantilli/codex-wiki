For the usual positive roots, the chamber condition is that the coefficients of L1​,L2​,L3​ be weakly decreasing, since their consecutive differences are the simple-coroot pairings. Adding the same constant to all three coefficients changes no weight, because L1​+L2​+L3​=0.
The given coefficients are (−2,3,0). Let s1​ exchange L1​,L2​, and s2​ exchange L2​,L3​. With the rightmost reflection acting first,
3L2​−2L1​s1​​3L1​−2L2​s2​​3L1​−2L3​.
(45)

w=s2​s1​=(1 3 2),w(3L2​−2L1​)=3L1​−2L3​=3ω1​+2ω2​.​
(46)
Its coefficients (3,0,−2) are strictly decreasing, so it lies even in the open Weyl chamber; its Dynkin labels are (3,2).
