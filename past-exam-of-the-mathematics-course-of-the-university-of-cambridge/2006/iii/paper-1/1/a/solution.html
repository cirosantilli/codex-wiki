Use the standard sl2 triple H=x∂x​−y∂y​, X=x∂y​, Y=y∂x​. In the tensor product of Lie algebra representations, each operator acts on both factors. Put
uij​=x3−iyi⊗x2−jyj,0≤i≤3,0≤j≤2.
(1)
These twelve vectors form a weight basis, and Huij​=(5−2(i+j))uij​. Thus the bases of the weight spaces are
weight531−1−3−5​basisu00​u10​,u01​u20​,u11​,u02​u30​,u21​,u12​u31​,u22​u32​​​
(2)
There are no other weights. The weight diagram shows their multiplicities, with the decomposition also displayed for comparison.
The raising operator acts by
Xuij​=iui−1,j​+jui,j−1​,
(3)
with out-of-range terms omitted. At weights five, three and one its kernels are respectively spanned by
h5​=u00​,h3​=u10​−u01​,h1​=u20​−2u11​+u02​.​
(4)
At weight one the kernel equations are 2a+b=0 and b+2c=0 for au20​+bu11​+cu02​. At weight minus one they are 3a+b=0, 2b+2c=0, c=0, which force zero; the maps on the two remaining negative-weight spaces are injective as well. Thus these three lines give all nonzero highest-weight vectors up to scalar, at their respective weights. A linear combination of different lines is killed by X but is not a weight vector, so is not a highest-weight vector.
The lowering operator is
Yuij​=(3−i)ui+1,j​+(2−j)ui,j+1​.
(5)
In particular,
h1​=u20​−2u11​+u02​,Yh1​=u30​−2u21​+u12​​
(6)
is an explicit basis for the submodule Γ1​. Indeed Y2h1​=0, Xh1​=0, X(Yh1​)=h1​, and the two vectors have weights 1,−1. The analogous strings from h5​,h3​ have dimensions six and four. Their distinct irreducible highest weights and total dimension twelve give the sl2 tensor product of cubic and quadratic symmetric powers
U≅Γ5​⊕Γ3​⊕Γ1​.​
(7)
