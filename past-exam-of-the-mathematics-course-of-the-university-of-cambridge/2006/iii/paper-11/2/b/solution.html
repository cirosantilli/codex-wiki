For the construction, use the standard basic-sequence selection procedure. At stage n, choose finitely many functionals which almost norm the unit sphere of the finite-dimensional span of the vectors already chosen. Put the next vector in their common kernels, and in the analogous kernels retained from earlier stages. These impose only finite codimension. Choosing the norming tolerances with a finite product gives a uniformly bounded basis constant, which may be fixed in advance, for example below two. The hypothesis then supplies a unit vector in this finite-codimensional subspace with image smaller than the prescribed εn​. This yields the requested normalized basic sequence.
