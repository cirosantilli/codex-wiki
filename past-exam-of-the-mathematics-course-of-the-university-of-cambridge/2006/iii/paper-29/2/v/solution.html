We can use reduction instead of expanding a very large rational addition formula. Modulo 2, both P1​ and P2​ reduce to (0,0), which generates the three-element reduced group. The reduction homomorphism therefore gives
P1​+5P2​​=6(0,0)=O.
(16)
The rational point P2​ is not any of the three torsion points just found, so it has infinite order. In particular R=P1​+5P2​=O: otherwise multiplication by three would give 15P2​=O.
If R had integral coordinates, its affine integral representative would reduce to an affine point modulo 2, not to the point at infinity. This contradicts its reduction to O. Thus
P1​+5P2​ does not have integral coordinates.​
(17)
Indeed, it is not even integral at 2. This is a reduction certificate for a nonintegral elliptic point, with the crucial nonidentity condition verified by the torsion calculation.
