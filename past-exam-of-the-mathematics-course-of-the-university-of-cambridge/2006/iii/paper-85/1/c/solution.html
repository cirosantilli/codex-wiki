At (σ,μ)=(1,0) the origin's matrix is (02​00​): it has a double zero eigenvalue and one eigenvector. Trace and determinant vary independently with μ and σ there, so two parameters are needed. The symmetry of an odd function removes quadratic terms, giving a reflection-symmetric cubic double-zero normal form. The quadratic nonlinearity required for a generic Bogdanov–Takens bifurcation vanishes.
