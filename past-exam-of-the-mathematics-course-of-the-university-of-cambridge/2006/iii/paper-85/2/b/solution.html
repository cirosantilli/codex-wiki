The origin's Jacobian matrix is triangular, J=(a+10​1b−1​), so its Floquet multipliers are a+1, b−1. It is attracting for −2<a<0, 0<b<2. The candidate codimension-one boundaries are a=0, a=−2, b=2, b=0: respectively a +1 or −1 Floquet multiplier. No complex-pair crossing is possible at the origin.
The map is odd. To locate the nearby steady branches, write S=x2>0. The first fixed-point equation gives y=x(S−a); substituting into the second gives
S[1−(S−a)3]=a(2−b).​
(20)
For a symmetric two-cycle solve F(x,y)=−(x,y). Then y=x(S−a−2) and
S[1+(S−a−2)3]=b(a+2).​
(21)
These exact orbit equations for a triangular cubic map determine the amplitude and parameter side without confusing the original cubic coefficients with their centre manifold values.
On a=0, with b=2, steady states have x2=a(2−b)+o(a) and y=−ax+x3. The pitchfork bifurcation is supercritical for b<2 and subcritical for b>2. Its centre manifold cubic coefficient is −1/(2−b). In the stable-origin boundary segment 0<b<2, an attracting pair appears on a>0.
On a=−2, with b=0, the two-cycle has x2=b(a+2)+o(a+2). The centre manifold map has cubic coefficient −1/b and Floquet multiplier −1+(a+2). Since its unstable side is a+2<0, the period-doubling bifurcation is supercritical for b<0 and subcritical for b>0. In particular the boundary segment 0<b<2 is subcritical: the nearby two-cycle lies on a>−2, where the origin is still attracting, and is unstable in the critical direction.
On b=2, with a=0, the steady branch has
y2∼−1+a3a3​(b−2).
(22)
Its scalar centre manifold map is y′=(1+b−2)y+(1+a−3)y3+⋯. It is supercritical for −1<a<0, and subcritical for a<−1 or a>0. At a=−1 the cubic vanishes: the exact equation gives b−2=−3S2+O(S3), so this is a degenerate pitchfork bifurcation with destabilizing quintic coefficient and subcritical quartic amplitude scaling. It is not a generic point of the codimension-one curve.
On b=0, with a=−2, put A=a+2. The two-cycle satisfies
y2∼1−A3A3​b.
(23)
The centre manifold map's cubic coefficient is 1−A−3. Thus the period-doubling bifurcation is subcritical for −2<a<−1, and supercritical for a<−2 or a>−1. In the stable-origin edge −1<a<0, a stable small two-cycle appears for b<0. At a=−1 the cubic vanishes, requiring the calculation below. All criticality labels concern the centre manifold direction; a bifurcating orbit is fully attracting only if the remaining Floquet multiplier is also stable. The sketch marks the small branches on the appropriate sides of each boundary of the stable-origin rectangle.
For clarity, these stability labels follow directly from one-dimensional derivatives. At a pitchfork bifurcation z′=(1+η)z+cz3, the nonzero state has z2=−η/c and Floquet multiplier 1−2η to first order. At a period-doubling bifurcation z′=−(1+η)z+cz3, the symmetric cycle has z2=η/c and one-step derivative −1+2η; its two-step Floquet multiplier is 1−4η+o(η). A supercritical branch lies on η>0 and is attracting in the centre manifold direction; a subcritical branch lies on η<0 and is repelling there.
At (a,b)=(−1,0) the Floquet multipliers are 0,−1, so the degeneracy is not two simultaneous unit Floquet multipliers. On its centre manifold, x=−y−y3+O(y7) and direct substitution gives y′=−y−3y5+O(y7). With α=a+1 small, the unfolding to leading weighted order is
y′=(−1+b)y+3αy3−3y5+⋯.​
(24)
This is a generalized flip bifurcation: one parameter crosses −1, and the other changes the cubic sign. Writing S≃y2, its two-cycles obey b+3αS−3S2=0. For α>0 a stable smaller two-cycle and an unstable larger two-cycle collide at
b=−43​α2+o(α2),S=21​α+o(α).​
(25)
The intervening range −3α2/4<b<0 contains both. For α<0 the period-doubling bifurcation is subcritical. At α=0, the exact orbit equation is 3S2−3S3+S4=b, so the unstable two-cycle exists for b>0 with ∣y∣∼(b/3)1/4. This proves both the codimension and the required quintic effect.
