Uniform convergence of fn​ to f on an interval I means that for every ϵ>0 there is N such that ∣fn​(x)−f(x)∣<ϵ for all n≥N and every x∈I. Equivalently supx∈I​∣fn​(x)−f(x)∣→0. The same N must work throughout the interval, unlike pointwise convergence.
For completeness, continuity is preserved: choose N with uniform error below ϵ/3, and use continuity of fN​ at a given x to make ∣fN​(y)−fN​(x)∣<ϵ/3. The triangle inequality then gives ∣f(y)−f(x)∣<ϵ. Thus each function involved in the integral is continuous on the closed interval and Riemann integrable. The estimate
