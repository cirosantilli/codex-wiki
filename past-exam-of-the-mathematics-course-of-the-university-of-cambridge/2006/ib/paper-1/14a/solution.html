In orthonormal Cartesian coordinates, a second-rank tensor has components transforming under a change of axes Q as Mij′​=Qik​Qjℓ​Mkℓ​, where QQT=I. Its contraction is invariant:
Mii′​=Qik​Qiℓ​Mkℓ​=δkℓ​Mkℓ​=Mkk​.

Thus Mii​ is a scalar. This is tensor contraction with the Euclidean metric. In general nonorthonormal coordinates the invariant expression is gijMij​, not an unweighted sum of diagonal covariant components.
