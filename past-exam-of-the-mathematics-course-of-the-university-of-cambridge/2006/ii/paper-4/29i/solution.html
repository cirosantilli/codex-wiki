is the unique positive multiplier with zero terminal balance.
For the constrained problem with no terminal reward, x(T)≥0 is exactly the weighted spending budget ∫er(T−t)u(t)dt≤W. The candidate u∗(⋅;μ∗) spends that budget exactly. Concavity gives logu−logu∗≤(u−u∗)/u∗. Since e−βt/u∗=μ∗e−βTer(T−t), integrating this inequality makes the utility function difference nonpositive for every feasible control. This proves the requested optimality of the logarithmic spending with a terminal multiplier solution directly, including uniqueness up to changes on measure-zero sets.
