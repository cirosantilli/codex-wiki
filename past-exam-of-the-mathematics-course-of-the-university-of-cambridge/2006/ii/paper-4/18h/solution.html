The multiquadratic field F is the splitting field of ∏i​(t2−ai​), a product of separable quadratic polynomials: each has distinct roots because ai​=0 and the characteristic is not two. Even if factors repeat, this splitting field is a separable normal extension, and therefore Galois. Each automorphism sends xi​ to ±xi​, and these signs determine it, embedding its Galois group into (Z/2)n. Every subgroup of this elementary abelian group is (Z/2)m for some m≤n.
For a quadratic intermediate field, its fixing subgroup H has index two and is the kernel of a nontrivial one-dimensional character χ:G→{±1}. The coordinate sign characters span the dual of G: a linear functional on the subspace G⊆F2n​ extends to the ambient space by extending a basis. Hence χ=∏i∈I​χi​ for some subset I. Set y=∏i∈I​xi​.
