With c=cos(θ/2) and s=sin(θ/2), the matrix of Sz​cosθ+Sx​sinθ is (ℏ/2)(cosθsinθ​sinθ−cosθ​). Multiplication by (c,s)T and (−s,c)T gives eigenvalues +ℏ/2 and −ℏ/2, using the double-angle identities.
The one-particle transformation is the matrix R=(cs​−sc​), with determinant one. The antisymmetric tensor ∣↑↓⟩−∣↓↑⟩ transforms by this determinant under R⊗R, so the singlet is unchanged. The total Sz​ kills it. Its invariance under these rotations implies that total Sz​cosθ+Sx​sinθ also kills it for every θ, so total Sx​ kills it. The spin commutator then gives total Sy​ acting as zero. Therefore S2∣χ⟩=0 and it has total spin zero.

Each arrow represents the corresponding value ±ℏ/2. The sequential measurements commute because they act on different particles. The two parallel outcomes leave triplet states with total spin one and S2=2ℏ2. Each antiparallel product state is a superposition of the spin-zero singlet and the spin-one zero-projection triplet, and is not an S2 eigenstate. Outcomes of zero probability at a special angle do not actually occur.
