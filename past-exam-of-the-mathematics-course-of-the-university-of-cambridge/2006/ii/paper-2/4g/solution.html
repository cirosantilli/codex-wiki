Add 00,01,10 to the four specified binary words. None of the resulting seven words is a proper suffix of another: the longer words end in 11, and their shorter terminal strings never equal another of the specified longer words. Thus this is a suffix code. Reading from the right determines the last codeword uniquely; deleting it and repeating proves unique decipherability. Its length sum is 3/4+1/8+1/16+2/32=1, in agreement with the bound.
