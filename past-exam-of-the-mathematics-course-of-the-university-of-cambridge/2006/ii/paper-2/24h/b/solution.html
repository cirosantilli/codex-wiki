For a parametrization X(u1,u2), put gij​=Xi​⋅Xj​. Decompose the second derivatives into tangent and normal components:
Xij​=Γijk​Xk​+bij​ν.
(47)
These tangent coefficients are the Christoffel symbols. Differentiate gij​ and combine the resulting three identities to obtain
Xij​⋅Xl​=21​(∂i​gjl​+∂j​gil​−∂l​gij​),Γijk​=21​gkl(∂i​gjl​+∂j​gil​−∂l​gij​).​
(48)
Here (gkl) is the inverse matrix. The formula proves that these symbols depend only on the first fundamental form and its first derivatives, not on the second fundamental form.
