For any equivalent pricing measure, the call expectation is positive and strictly less than EQ​S1​/2=1/2, since (s−1)+<s/2 for 0<s<2. Both endpoints can be approached. Symmetric densities concentrated in [1−δ,1+δ] give prices tending to zero; symmetric densities concentrated near zero and two give prices tending to one half. Mix either density with an arbitrarily small amount of the uniform law to keep it everywhere positive; symmetry keeps its mean one. The set of positive mean-one densities is convex and the call price is linear, so every intermediate value is attained. Thus the exact price range is (0,1/2), with neither endpoint attained.
