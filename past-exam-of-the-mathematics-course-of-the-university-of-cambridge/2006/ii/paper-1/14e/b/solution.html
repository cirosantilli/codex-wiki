Write y=x˙ and E=(x2+y2)/2. Along a trajectory,
E˙=−ϵ(αx2+βy2−γ)y2.
(35)
Use the unperturbed orbit x=Rsint,y=Rcost to compute the leading energy change over one period:
ΔE=−ϵπ[4α+3β​R4−2γR2]+O(ϵ2).
(36)
Zero averaged energy change gives the nonzero amplitude
R=2α+3βγ​​+O(ϵ).​
(37)
The supplied sign condition makes this real and nonzero. The averaged amplitude equation is R˙=ϵ[γR/2−(α+3β)R3/8]+O(ϵ2), whose derivative at that root is −ϵγ+O(ϵ2). Its nonzero derivative also makes the root persist as an isolated periodic orbit for sufficiently small positive ϵ.
Directly, the planar divergence is −ϵ(αx2+3βy2−γ). On the leading orbit its integral over 2π is −ϵ[πR2(α+3β)−2πγ]=−2πϵγ. Thus
μ=exp[−2πϵγ+O(ϵ2)].​
(38)
The cycle is attracting for γ>0 and repelling for γ<0. Both sign possibilities are allowed by (α+3β)γ>0.
