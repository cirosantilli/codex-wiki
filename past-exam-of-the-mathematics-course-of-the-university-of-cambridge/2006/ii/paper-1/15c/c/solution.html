Differentiation gives dI/dE=2bm/2mE​=2b/v, exactly the out-and-back period. Under slow wall motion the action I is the adiabatic invariant; a convention using I/(2π) is equivalent. Hence
E(b)=E(b0​)(bb0​​)2.​
