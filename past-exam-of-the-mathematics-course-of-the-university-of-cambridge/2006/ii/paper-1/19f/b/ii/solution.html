Write g=(a,b) for the affine map z↦az+b. Conjugation by (a,0) sends translation by b to translation by ab, so it permutes transitively the nontrivial characters of U. Since U is normal, the induced character is zero outside U. On a translation by b it is the sum of all conjugates of ψ, hence
χρ​(a,b)=⎩⎨⎧​p−1,−1,0,​a=1,b=0,a=1,b=0,a=1.​​
(52)
For b=0 the sum of the nontrivial additive characters is minus one, since the sum including the trivial character is zero. Its character norm is
⟨χρ​,χρ​⟩G​=p(p−1)(p−1)2+(p−1)​=1.
(53)
By the irreducibility criterion for characters, ρ=IndUG​ψ is irreducible of dimension p−1. All choices of nontrivial ψ give the same representation.
