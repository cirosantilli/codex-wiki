Let the universe be the n! permutations of {1,…,n}, and let Aj​ be the set of permutations fixing j. A derangement belongs to none of the Aj​. If k specified points must be fixed, the remaining n−k points can be permuted in (n−k)! ways, so every such k-fold intersection has that size. There are (kn​) choices of the specified fixed points.
