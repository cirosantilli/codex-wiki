For the final unheaded conductivity identity, let P=nnT, the orthogonal projection onto the unit vector n. Then P2=P and the uniaxial conductivity tensor is σ=αI+γP. The antisymmetric tensor D sends a vector v to v×n. Applying it twice and using the vector triple product gives
D2v=(v×n)×n=n(n⋅v)−v,D2=P−I.
(59)
Consequently
σD2=(αI+γP)(P−I)=αP−αI.
(60)
This equals −σ=−αI−γP exactly when (α+γ)P=0. Since P has rank one and is nonzero, the requested parameter is
γ=−α​.
(61)
At this value conductivity vanishes along n; it is precisely the singular case excluded in part (ii), so there is no conflict with that part's conclusion.
