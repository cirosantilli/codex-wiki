A real function ϕ on an interval is a convex function if
ϕ(θa+(1−θ)b)≤θϕ(a)+(1−θ)ϕ(b)
(6)
for all a,b in its domain and 0≤θ≤1. For a finite-valued random variable X with values xj​ and probabilities pj​, Jensen's inequality states
ϕ(∑j​pj​xj​)≤∑j​pj​ϕ(xj​),equivalently ϕ(EX)≤Eϕ(X).
(7)
Apply this with equal probabilities to a,b and the convex function ϕ(t)=tp on the nonnegative axis:
(2a+b​)p≤2ap+bp​.
(8)
Multiplying by 2p proves the sharp two-term convex power bound. Conversely taking a=b>0 forces 2p≤2cp​. Thus
cp​=2p−1.​
(9)
For p=1 the inequality is equality for every pair; for p>1 equality occurs when a=b.
