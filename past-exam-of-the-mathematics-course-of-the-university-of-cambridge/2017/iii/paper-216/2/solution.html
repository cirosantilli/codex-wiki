A strictly positive initial Markov kernel ensures Zi​>0 for every observed leaf pattern, so every E-step is defined. At a later boundary iterate, require positive likelihood for the actual data and restrict the conditional distribution to its support. EM likelihood monotonicity proves that the update cannot decrease observed-data likelihood function. It is a maximum-likelihood iteration, not a guarantee of finding the global maximum-likelihood estimate; different initial kernels may lead to different stationary points.
