A zero-mean autoregressive process of order one satisfies
Yt​=αYt−1​+εt​,
(53)
where εt​ is white noise with variance σε2​>0. A causal time series uses present and past innovations only. For ∣α∣<1 the series
Yt​=∑j=0∞​αjεt−j​
(54)
has mean-square convergence because ∑j​α2j<∞, and it supplies the causal weakly stationary process. Its variance is σε2​/(1−α2). For h≥0, only the common innovations contribute to the autocovariance:
γ(h)=σε2​∑j=0∞​αh+jαj=1−α2σε2​αh​.
(55)
Evenness then gives
γ(h)=1−α2σε2​α∣h∣​,ρ(h)=α∣h∣.​
(56)
When α<0, the signs alternate; when α=0, the process is white noise. With nondegenerate innovations, ∣α∣≥1 does not give a causal finite-variance stationary solution. These covariance calculations need uncorrelated innovations, not Gaussianity; Gaussian innovations additionally yield a Gaussian process.
