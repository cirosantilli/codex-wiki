Conversely, suppose p∈G and p⊩∗σ∈τ. The syntactic forcing relation says that
D={s≤p:∃(ρ,r)∈τ (s≤r∧s⊩∗σ=ρ)}
(23)
is dense below a forcing condition p. This is a set in M by definability of forcing and axiom schema of separation. The dense-below generic meeting lemma gives s∈G∩D: augment D by all incompatible forcing conditions with p to obtain a globally dense ground-model set, and use p∈G to exclude the incompatible part.
Choose the witnessing (ρ,r)∈τ. Since s≤r and s∈G, upward closure gives r∈G. The assumed equality truth lemma gives σG=ρG, while r∈G gives ρG∈τG. Combining both directions,
σG∈τG ⟺ ∃p∈G (p⊩∗σ∈τ).​
(24)
This completes the atomic membership truth lemma for forcing; no separate assumption of the membership truth lemma was made.
