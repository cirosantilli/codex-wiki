The Picard criterion with the input/output convention above states
f∈R(K)⟺f∈R(K)​ and j∑​σj2​∣⟨f,vj​⟩∣2​<∞.​
(13)
Necessity follows from ⟨f,vj​⟩=σj​⟨u,uj​⟩ and Bessel inequality. Conversely the summability constructs u=∑j​σj−1​⟨f,vj​⟩uj​∈U; the closure condition ensures that its image is all of f. The closure condition must not be omitted: a component orthogonal to the range cannot be reconstructed.
For the diagonal example, fj​=j−p is itself in ℓ2 only for p>1/2. Its preimage would be uj​=j1−p, so the range criterion is the convergence of ∑j​j2−2p. The P-series gives
f∈R(K)⟺p>3/2.​
(14)
At p=3/2 the reconstruction norm diverges harmonically. For 1/2<p≤3/2 the data are legitimate but have no preimage; for 0<p≤1/2 they are not even in the specified data space.
