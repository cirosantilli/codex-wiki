Let ft​ be the optical telescope focal length, x=ft​θ the physical optical slit width, fcoll​ the collimator focal length, and Acoll​=Dfcoll​/ft​ the incident collimated beam diameter. For the grating equation mλ=d(sinα+sinβ), differentiation at fixed wavelength gives ∣dβ/dα∣=cosα/cosβ. Thus the monochromatic optical slit image width is
p=xfcoll​fcam​​cosβcosα​.
(14)
The incident and emergent optical pupil widths are Acoll​=Wcosα and Acam​=Wcosβ. Multiplying cancels the anamorphic magnification of a grating:
fcam​pAcam​​=fcoll​xAcoll​​=θD,L=RθD=fcam​RpAcam​​.​
(15)
This is conservation of the Lagrange optical invariant in the dispersion plane. It uses paraxial optical slit angles, an unvignetted optical pupil and matching full-width conventions for θ,p,D,Acam​.
