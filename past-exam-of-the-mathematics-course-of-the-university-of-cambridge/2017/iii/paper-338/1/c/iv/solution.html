Vd​ is the Abbe number, a dimensionless inverse measure of optical dispersion. Large Vd​ means weak dispersion. The Abbe diagram below spans a representative visible-light optical glass range, roughly 1.45≲nd​≲2.0 and 20≲Vd​≲95. The shaded families are schematic, not exact catalogue boundaries; modern compositions overlap and include high-index crown glasses.
For a usual positive objective, choose component 1 as a higher-Vd​ crown glass and component 2 as a lower-Vd​ flint glass. Then f1d​>0, f2d​<0, and ∣f2d​∣/f1d​=V1d​/V2d​>1: the crown glass is converging, the flint glass diverging, and their dispersive powers cancel while leaving positive net power. The Abbe numbers alone do not set their absolute focal lengths; the specified total focal length does. As a representative pair, V1d​=64.17, V2d​=36.37 gives f1d​≃0.4332fd​, f2d​≃−0.7644fd​. Representative index and dispersion data are given in SCHOTT's optical-glass data.
