Although the PDF permits assuming even multiplicity, there is a short proof of even multiplicity of unit-circle roots of a nonnegative trigonometric polynomial. The real analytic function p(eiθ)≥0 cannot have a zero of odd order. Near ζ=eiθ0​, eiθ−ζ has a simple zero, and the nonzero factor e−idθ leaves the zero order of P(eiθ) unchanged. Hence the multiplicity of a root must be even.
