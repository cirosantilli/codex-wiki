The symmetric group has n! elements, not the printed order n. For n≥2 the identity and the n−1 adjacent transpositions have probabilities 1/n, so the specified transition matrix is normalized. Its stationary distribution is uniform and it satisfies detailed balance, since each transposition is its own inverse. Adjacent transpositions generate the symmetric group, making this an irreducible Markov chain; the positive holding probability makes it an aperiodic Markov chain.
