The logical depth of a measurement pattern counts sequential layers of measurement in quantum measurements forced by dependence of measurement bases on earlier outcomes. Depth one means every basis can be fixed before measurements start, allowing all measurement in quantum measurements on distinct qubits to be performed in parallel. This does not require depth-one resource-state preparation or constant-depth classical parity processing.
Suppose the circuit contains m gates J(α1​),…,J(αm​) in time order, with each αj​∈{0,π/2}. Prepare an (m+1)-vertex path graph state from ∣+⟩⊗(m+1) and Controlled-Z gates between neighbours. Its first qubit supplies the specified input ∣+⟩. Measure vertex j−1 in the fixed equatorial qubit measurement basis at angle αj​ for j=1,…,m, obtaining sj​. Measure the final vertex in the computational basis, obtaining d.
Track the Pauli frame as Xaj​Zbj​, starting with a0​=b0​=0. A hypothetical sequential reading of the same measurement in quantum measurements gives the step Xsj​J(αj​)Xaj−1​Zbj−1​. The supplied commutation rules imply, up to global phase,
Xsj​J(αj​)XaZb=Xsj​⊕bZaJ((−1)aαj​).
(31)
For αj​=0, the sign is irrelevant. For αj​=π/2, the identity J(−π/2)=XJ(π/2) absorbs the possible sign change into the Pauli frame. If tj​=0 for αj​=0 and tj​=1 for αj​=π/2, the update is
aj​=sj​⊕bj−1​⊕tj​aj−1​,bj​=aj−1​,k=d⊕am​.​
(32)
Inductively the final unmeasured quantum state would be Xam​Zbm​J(αm​)⋯J(α1​)∣+⟩. The final computational basis outcome is corrected by am​ and is unaffected by bm​. The induction on a sequential interpretation proves the joint statistics; projectors on distinct vertices commute, so the identical fixed-basis pattern can actually be measured simultaneously, including its final vertex.
The first printed commutation formula has an incorrect exact scalar phase: with the printed matrix definition, J(α)X=eiαZJ(−α), rather than the negative-exponent prefactor. For example, at α=π/4 the two versions differ by a factor i. They agree up to global phase, so this error does not affect the Pauli frame recurrence or any measurement in quantum mechanics probability.
Consequently a fixed-basis path-state pattern of logical depth one simulates every such circuit. Only Pauli measurements occur: the angle-zero basis measures X, and the angle-π/2 basis measures −Y with the printed eigenvector labels. Classical exclusive or processing suffices for all output corrections. These gates are Clifford gates, which explains why angle adaptation can be eliminated. For m=0, simply measure the initial ∣+⟩ in the computational basis; it is already a depth-one pattern.
