Put h=1/(N+1) and xi​=ih. The interior piecewise-linear hat functions are

They are a basis of the continuous piecewise-affine functions vanishing at both endpoints. The support of a function for each hat only overlaps those of neighbouring hats, so the stiffness matrix is a tridiagonal matrix. Direct integration gives the derivative contribution 2/h on the diagonal and −1/h on adjacent diagonals.

At the endpoints the coefficient multiplying the zero boundary value is simply omitted. Since f∈L2, its point values are not even well defined; replacing bi​ by hf(xi​) would require an additional quadrature rule convention and regularity hypothesis, not present here. The exact Ritz method uses the displayed integrals. The finite element interpolation estimate and Céa lemma, using y∈H2, give an O(h) energy norm error; the Aubin–Nitsche duality argument gives O(h2) in the L2 norm on this interval.
