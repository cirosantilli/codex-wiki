For 0≤θ≤π/2, define the Gaussian rotation of independent copies
Uθ​=Xsinθ+Ycosθ,Vθ​=Xcosθ−Ysinθ.

Writing Σ=Cov(X), both rotated covariance matrices are Σ and their cross-covariance is zero. The pair has a centered multivariate normal distribution, so its components are independent and (Uθ​,Vθ​) has the same probability law as (X,Y). This calculation uses no inverse of Σ and therefore also proves the assertion for degenerate multivariate normal distributions.
