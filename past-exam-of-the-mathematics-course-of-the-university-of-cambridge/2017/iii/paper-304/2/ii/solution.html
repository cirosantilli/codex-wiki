A formal BRST symmetry construction uses sAμ​=∂μ​c, sc=0, scˉ=ih, sh=0 and the gauge-fixing fermion Ψ=∫cˉ(F−iξh/2)d4x. Acting with the odd differential gives

For a real admissible gauge functional, integration over h gives F2/(2ξ) in the Euclidean action, and ξ→0 imposes the gauge condition. Overall constant phases in the Faddeev-Popov determinant can be absorbed in the measure convention. Here nonlinear Abelian gauge fixing makes OA​ depend on A. The action for the Faddeev-Popov ghost fields contains the interaction −2icˉAμ​∂μ​c, so the Faddeev-Popov ghost fields cannot be discarded as in a linear Abelian gauge, even though the U(1) Adjoint representation is trivial.
There is a genuine reality obstruction for a complex Euclidean gauge condition in the source. For a Hermitian U(1) gauge field, each Euclidean component Aμ​ is real. The real and imaginary parts of F=0 separately require ∂⋅A=0 and ∑μ​Aμ2​=0, hence A=0. For example A2​=Bx1​, with other components zero and B=0, has curvature F12​=B; no gauge transformation can put it on this slice because curvature is gauge invariant. Thus the stated condition is not a gauge fixing of general real Euclidean configurations. The boxed action is the intended formal complex-gauge construction. A legitimate complexified contour prescription would be additional data, not an ordinary real delta-functional enforcing this printed condition.
