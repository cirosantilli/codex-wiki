The kinetic term fixes the mass dimension of a canonically normalized real scalar field in d dimensions to (d−2)/2. In six dimensions [ϕ]=2, and every term in the Lagrangian density has dimension six. Therefore [gn​]=6−2n, and
n<3: relevant;n=3: marginal;n>3: irrelevant.​
(16)
For positive integral n, these mean n=1,2, n=3 and n≥4, respectively. If n=0 is included, its vacuum-energy constant is also relevant. These are classical power counting in quantum field theory classifications of relevant couplings, marginal couplings and irrelevant couplings, not assertions that loop corrections preserve exact marginality.
