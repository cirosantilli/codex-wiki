Use a local orthonormal tetrad and units in which the speed of light is one. A particle has four-momentum pa^=E(1,e) and moves with velocity e. Thus its energy density contributes E, its momentum density contributes Eei, and its momentum flux contributes Eeiej. Integrating the phase-space distribution function gives the kinetic stress-energy tensor. The measure d3p/E is the future mass-shell Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure, so the same expression transforms as a spacetime stress-energy tensor; fixed state-counting or polarization factors are understood to be included in f.
