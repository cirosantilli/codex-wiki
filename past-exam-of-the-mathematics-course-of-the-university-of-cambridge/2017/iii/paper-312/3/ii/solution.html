For a nonzero Fourier mode k=kz^, the divergence-free condition implies Bz​=0. The complex vectors m(±)=(x^±iy^​)/2​ form a Hermitian orthonormal basis of this transverse plane. Their opposite rotation phases give the helicity decomposition of a transverse Fourier mode; the factor i/2​ in the amplitudes is a convention. The baryon velocity in this vector sector has the analogous decomposition.
For helicity σ=±1, the angular source is
e⋅B(σ)=2i​B(σ)sinθeiσφ=−σi32π​​B(σ)Y1σ​.
(40)
The same formula holds for vb(σ)​. Thus these sources have only angular order m=σ. Streaming multiplies by ikcosθ and the stated Thomson scattering operator is diagonal in m, so neither mixes this sector with other angular orders. Assuming the initial anisotropy is in the same vector-helicity sector, or considering the anisotropy generated from zero initial data, the solution therefore contains only Yℓσ​ with ℓ≥1. Arbitrary independently imposed anisotropies of other m would instead evolve in their own homogeneous sectors.
There is a genuine normalization inconsistency in the PDF. To retain its printed temperature expansion, write
Θ(σ)=∑ℓ≥1​Cℓ​qℓ(σ)​Yℓσ​,Cℓ​=(−i)ℓ8π2ℓ+1​​,
(41)
where qℓ​ denotes exactly the multipoles called Θℓ​ in that expansion. The spherical-harmonic streaming recurrence gives the coefficient of Yℓσ​ in ikcosθΘ, divided by Cℓ​, as
ik[Cℓ​Cℓ+1​​(2ℓ+3)(2ℓ+1)(ℓ+1)2−1​​qℓ+1​+Cℓ​Cℓ−1​​(2ℓ+1)(2ℓ−1)ℓ2−1​​qℓ−1​].
(42)
Here Cℓ+1​/Cℓ​=−i(2ℓ+3)/(2ℓ+1)​ and Cℓ−1​/Cℓ​=i(2ℓ−1)/(2ℓ+1)​. Both streaming denominators are therefore 2ℓ+1, rather than the PDF's 2ℓ+3 and 2ℓ−1. The dipole source also changes: C1​=−i3/(8π)​, so −(e⋅B˙+τ˙e⋅vb​)/C1​ is −σ(4π/3)(B˙(σ)+τ˙vb(σ)​)Y1σ​. The quadrupole collision correction is still −τ˙q2​δℓ2​/10.
Thus the vector photon Boltzmann hierarchy consistent with the literal printed expansion is
q˙​ℓ(σ)​+2ℓ+1k​[(ℓ+1)2−1​qℓ+1(σ)​−ℓ2−1​qℓ−1(σ)​]=τ˙(1−10δℓ2​​)qℓ(σ)​−σ34π​(B˙(σ)+τ˙vb(σ)​)δℓ1​.​
(43)
A concrete counterexample to the printed hierarchy with this expansion is a collisionless instant with q2​=1 and every other multipole zero. Direct streaming gives q˙​1​=−k3​/3, whereas the printed hierarchy would give −k3​/5.
Alternatively, preserve the PDF's desired hierarchy by making the photon multipole normalization change
Θℓ(σ)​=4π2ℓ+1​qℓ(σ)​,Θ(σ)=∑ℓ≥1​(−i)ℓ2ℓ+12π​​Θℓ(σ)​Yℓσ​.
(44)
This is the reciprocal angular normalization, not the printed expansion. Multiplying the corrected hierarchy by (2ℓ+1)/(4π) gives precisely
Θ˙ℓ(σ)​+k[2ℓ+3(ℓ+1)2−1​​Θℓ+1(σ)​−2ℓ−1ℓ2−1​​Θℓ−1(σ)​]=τ˙(1−10δℓ2​​)Θℓ(σ)​−σ(B˙(σ)+τ˙vb(σ)​)δℓ1​.​
(45)
This supplies both consistent conventions explicitly. The lower coupling vanishes at ℓ=1, so no vector monopole is introduced. Cosmological optical depth to the observer has τ˙<0, giving damping of the dipole and the factor 9/10 quadrupole damping in this temperature-only collision model; no polarization collision term has been added to the supplied equation.
