Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2017 / iii / Paper 130 / 1 / i / Solution

OurBigBook.com Words: 134
Write N={1,2,…} and let χ be the given finite coloring of the edges of the complete graph on the positive integers. Construct an increasing sequence (vi​) and nested infinite sets A0​⊃A1​⊃⋯ as follows. Set A0​=N. Having chosen Ai−1​, take vi​=minAi−1​, partition Ai−1​∖{vi​} by the color of {vi​,w}, and use the infinite pigeonhole principle to choose an infinite set Ai​ on which this color is constant, say ri​. Every element of Ai​ exceeds vi​.
The infinite pigeonhole principle applied again to (ri​) gives an infinite set of indices I and a color r such that ri​=r for all i∈I. For i<j in I, nesting gives vj​∈Ai​, so χ({vi​,vj​})=r. Thus the infinite set X={vi​:i∈I} is a monochromatic complete graph:
χ​X(2)​≡r.​
(1)
This proves the required infinite case of Ramsey's theorem; finiteness of the color set is used at both applications of the infinite pigeonhole principle.
