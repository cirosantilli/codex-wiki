For the spherical coordinates in the question, the coordinate tangent vectors are mutually orthogonal, with lengths 1,r,rsinθ. Their metric tensor is therefore diag(1,r2,r2sin2θ) and the Jacobian determinant is r2sinθ. Applying the divergence formula to the gradient gives
Δu=r21​∂r​(r2ur​)+r2sinθ1​∂θ​(sinθuθ​)+r2sin2θ1​uϕϕ​,dx=r2sinθdrdθdϕ.​
(26)
For example, the formula follows directly from Δu=∣deth∣−1/2∂i​(∣deth∣1/2hij∂j​u) for this diagonal metric. These coordinates are valid away from the polar axis and angular seam; the Laplacian is a smooth geometric operator there as well, described by other charts. The coordinate volume form integrates over 1/2<r<2, 0<θ<π, 0≤ϕ<2π, with the usual periodic identification.
