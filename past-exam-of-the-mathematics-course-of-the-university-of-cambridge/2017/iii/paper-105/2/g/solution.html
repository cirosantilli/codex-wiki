The coefficients in the spherical Laplacian are independent of ϕ, so [Δ,∂ϕ​]=0. Also ∂ϕ​ is tangent to both boundary spheres; differentiating the zero boundary trace gives another zero trace. Therefore w=∂ϕ​u satisfies Δw=∂ϕ​f and the same homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions.
In Cartesian coordinates ∂ϕ​=x1​∂2​−x2​∂1​, a globally smooth rotation field even where angular coordinates are singular. Permuting coordinate axes gives R12​, R23​ and R31​. For each, the rotational commutator for the Laplacian follows directly from Δ(xi​∂j​u)=xi​∂j​Δu+2∂i​∂j​u; antisymmetrizing cancels the extra terms. Thus
Δ(Rij​u)=Rij​f,Rij​u∣∂Ω​=0.​
(33)
If f is radial, all these right-hand sides vanish. Uniqueness from the energy estimate makes every Rij​u zero. At each point of a sphere the rotation fields span its tangent plane, so all tangential derivatives of u vanish. Each sphere is connected, giving u(x)=U(∣x∣)​. This proves radiality without assuming it as a separation-of-variables ansatz.
