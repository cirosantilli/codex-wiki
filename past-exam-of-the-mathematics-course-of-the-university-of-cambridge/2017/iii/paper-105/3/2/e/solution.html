For the stated mollifier, (∂t​+∂s​)χε​=(∂x​+∂y​)χε​=0. Thus the interior doubled integral is
∫χε​(t−s,x−y)[H(u(t,x),v(s,y))φt​(t,x)+Q(u(t,x),v(s,y))φx​(t,x)].
(52)
On a bounded state range, H is Lipschitz in each argument, and so is Q: away from the diagonal each partial derivative has modulus at most sup∣f′∣, and the function is continuous across the diagonal. Local L1 translation continuity therefore makes this converge to the corresponding single-time, single-space integral. The compact time support is chosen below T, with a margin for s≤t+2ε.
The initial terms need a separate argument. Since the time kernel is supported at t−s∈[−2ε,−ε], Bv​ is identically zero for t≥0, whereas Bu​ samples v(s,⋅) at ε≤s≤2ε.
The averaged initial trace of an entropy solution follows directly from its inequalities. Test the constant-level entropy for v with (1−s/δ)+​ρ(y), using smooth approximations of that temporal cutoff. Boundedness of v and its entropy flux gives
δ1​∫0δ​∫ρ∣v(s)−k∣≤∫ρ∣v0​−k∣+O(δ).
(53)
Approximate v0​ locally in L1 by finitely many constants with a smooth nonnegative partition of unity. Applying this estimate on each partition member and using the triangle inequality makes the limsup of δ−1∫0δ​∥v(s)−v0​∥L1(K)​ds arbitrarily small. This proves its convergence to zero for each compact K.
The time kernel has size O(ε−1) and the spatial kernel is a normalized approximate identity. The averaged trace, followed by spatial L1 translation continuity of v0​, therefore gives Bu​→∫∣u0​−v0​∣φ(0). Its time mass is one, not one-half, because its whole support lies on the positive s side. Consequently
∫0T​∫[∣u−v∣φt​+Q(u,v)φx​]+∫∣u0​−v0​∣φ(0)≥0.​
(54)
This is the Kato inequality for scalar conservation laws with its initial contribution. Assuming an initial trace solely from interior translation continuity would leave a gap; the entropy argument above supplies it.
