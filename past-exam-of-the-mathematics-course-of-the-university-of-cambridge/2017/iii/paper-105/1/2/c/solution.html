The valid complete boundary-jet condition for formal adjoints is that, at each boundary point, either all derivatives of u through order m−1 vanish or all such derivatives of v vanish. On a bounded piecewise smooth domain with smooth coefficients up to its closure, repeated integration by parts produces boundary terms of the form (∂γu)∂δ(aα​v), multiplied by a normal component, with ∣γ∣,∣δ∣≤m−1. Expanding the second factor shows that every summand vanishes under this stronger whole-jet alternative. Hence the desired identity holds under that condition. The later cap argument satisfies this stronger condition face by face, so it remains valid despite the literal error in this subpart. Boundary regularity and finite integrals are also needed for a general noncompact-support integration formula; the caps used below have them.
