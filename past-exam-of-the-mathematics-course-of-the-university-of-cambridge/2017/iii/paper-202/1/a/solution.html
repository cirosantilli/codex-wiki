Work on a filtered probability space with the usual conditions for a filtration. A continuous martingale belongs to the class of L2-bounded continuous martingales when
t≥0sup​E∣Mt​∣2<∞.​
(1)
This means a uniform bound over the entire time interval, not merely being square-integrable at each individual time. The L2 martingale convergence theorem gives a terminal variable M∞​ and almost sure convergence and convergence in the Lebesgue space L2; moreover Mt​=E[M∞​∣Ft​] by conditional expectation.
The predictable sigma-algebra P on Ω×[0,∞) is the smallest sigma-algebra making every left-continuous adapted process measurable. Equivalently, it is generated by
A×{0},A∈F0​,A×(s,t],0≤s<t, A∈Fs​,
(2)
where the sample coordinate comes first. A previsible process is precisely a process measurable for this predictable sigma-algebra. Values at time zero matter for its definition, although they make no contribution to integration against a continuous quadratic variation starting at zero.
