Under the corrected hypothesis, Nt​=Xt​−1 is a continuous local martingale with N0​=0, [N]t​=At​=∫0t​e2Bs​ds, and A∞​=∞ almost surely. The Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem states that, for such a process,
τu​=inf{t≥0:[N]t​>u},Wu​=Nτu​​
(30)
defines a standard Brownian motion relative to the filtration Fτu​​, and Nt​=W[N]t​​. The divergence of the quadratic variation ensures every τu​ is finite, so no extension of the probability space is needed. Here A is continuous and strictly increasing because its derivative is e2Bt​>0, so the inverse is particularly straightforward. Consequently,
Xt​=1+W∫0t​e2Bs​ds​.​
(31)
The constructed Brownian motion need not be independent of its random clock. Under the literal PDF assumptions X need not be a local martingale, as the counterexample in part (a) shows, so this Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem conclusion cannot be asserted without the correction.
