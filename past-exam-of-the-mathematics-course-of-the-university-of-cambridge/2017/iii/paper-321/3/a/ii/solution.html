For a normal mode proportional to eikx−iωt, the linear equations give
−iωUx​=2ΩUy​−ikΣ0​cs2​​σ^,−iωUy​=−2Ω​Ux​,−iωσ^=−ikΣ0​Ux​.
(19)
For ω=0, eliminate Uy​=−iΩUx​/(2ω) and σ^=kΣ0​Ux​/ω. The density-wave dispersion relation in a non-self-gravitating disk is
ω2=Ω2+cs2​k2.​
(20)
The Ω2 term is the radial epicyclic frequency squared for Keplerian rotation: Coriolis force and background shear restore an orbital displacement. The cs2​k2 term is acoustic restoration by a pressure gradient. Long wavelengths are mainly epicyclic motion; short wavelengths are mainly acoustic waves. The given model contains no disk self-gravity term.
For completeness, the full three-variable determinant is proportional to ω(ω2−Ω2−cs2​k2). Its additional ω=0 branch has Ux​=0 and 2ΩUy​=ikcs2​σ^/Σ0​. This is a stationary pressure/Coriolis balance, the zero-frequency balanced mode of an axisymmetric disk, rather than a propagating density wave. At k=0 it reduces to a uniform surface-density change. Dividing by ω is legitimate only for the wave branches just derived.
