Keep ℓ=e0−e3 and set P=p′+p2=A′′/A, Q=q′+q2=B′′/B. Differentiation gives d(pe1)=Pℓ∧e1 and d(qe2)=Qℓ∧e2. In Cartan's second structure equation, the quadratic terms for these components vanish. The apparently possible transverse component cancels:
ω10​∧ω02​+ω13​∧ω32​=pqe1∧e2−pqe1∧e2=0.
(30)
Thus the potentially nonzero mixed-index curvature 2-forms are
Θ01​=Θ10​=Θ31​Θ02​=Θ20​=Θ32​​=Pℓ∧e1,=Qℓ∧e2,​Θ13​Θ23​​=−Pℓ∧e1,=−Qℓ∧e2.​​
(31)
All other components vanish. Equivalently, the independent lower-index forms are Θ01​=Θ13​=−Pℓ∧e1 and Θ02​=Θ23​=−Qℓ∧e2, with the rest supplied by lower-index antisymmetry. These signs use Θab​=21​Rabcd​ec∧ed and the curvature convention in question 2(c). This is the curvature of a diagonal plane wave in Rosen coordinates.
