 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2017 iii Paper 309 4 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
Choose signature (−,+,+,+), with e0 timelike. The orthonormal coframe in spacetime reconstructs the Lorentzian metric as
g=−(e0)2+(e1)2+(e2)2+(e3)2=−dt2+A(t−z)2dx2+B(t−z)2dy2+dz2.​
(28)
Because A and B are nowhere zero on the domain, the coframe is invertible and the metric nondegenerate; either sign of each function is allowed. In retarded and advanced null coordinates u=t−z, v=t+z the metric is −dudv+A(u)2dx2+B(u)2dy2, a diagonal example of Rosen coordinates for a plane gravitational wave. Multiplication by −1 would give the opposite overall signature convention; all subsequent signs here use the displayed one.
