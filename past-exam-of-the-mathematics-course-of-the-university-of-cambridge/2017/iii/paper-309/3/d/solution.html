An electromagnetic field tensor obeys the homogeneous Maxwell equations dF=0. Together with its invariance under the Killing vector field V, Cartan's magic formula gives d(ιV​F)=LV​F−ιV​dF=0. The local potential of a closed differential one-form construction, the one-form Poincaré lemma, therefore supplies a scalar field Φ on a sufficiently small contractible neighborhood with
ιV​F=dΦ.​
(25)
For an explicit local proof, write ιV​F=αi​(x)dxi on a coordinate ball centered at zero and take Φ(x)=∫01​xiαi​(sx)ds. Since dα=0, differentiating under the integral gives ∂j​Φ=∫01​[αj​(sx)+sxi∂i​αj​(sx)]ds=αj​(x). There need not be a global potential when this closed one-form has nonzero periods. The homogeneous Maxwell equation is essential; an arbitrary invariant two-form would not suffice.
Use proper time for the massive particle, let u be its tangent, and assume m=0. The Killing equation makes uaub∇a​Vb​=0. The Lorentz force equation then implies
dτd​(Va​ua)=mq​Va​Fab​ub=mq​VaFab​ub=mq​dτdΦ​.
(26)
Consequently the local conserved quantity is
mVa​ua−qΦ=constant.​
(27)
The sign follows from contracting F in its first argument, exactly as in the source. Adding a constant to Φ merely shifts the conserved quantity. This is a charged-particle conserved quantity from a Killing symmetry.
