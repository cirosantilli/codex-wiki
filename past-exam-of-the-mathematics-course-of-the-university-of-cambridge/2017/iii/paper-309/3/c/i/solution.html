Use Cartan's magic formula. The exterior derivative and interior product of a differential form give
dα=−2dx∧dy,ιX​α=−x2−y2+z2,ιX​dα=2xdx+2ydy.

Thus d(ιX​α)=−2xdx−2ydy+2zdz, and the first two components cancel:
LX​α=2zdz.​
(20)
The rotation in the xy-plane leaves ydx−xdy invariant, while the dilation in z doubles the infinitesimal weight of zdz, providing a geometric check of this Lie derivative.
