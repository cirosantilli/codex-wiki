Use spatial indices i,j=1,2,3, set ϕi​=∂i​ϕ, and assume ϕ is smooth. The inverse metric has gtt=−c−2e−2ϕ/c2 and gij=δij. The Christoffel symbols of the Levi-Civita connection follow from Γαβγ​=gαδ(∂β​gγδ​+∂γ​gβδ​−∂δ​gβγ​)/2:
Γtti​=Γtit​=c2ϕi​​,Γitt​=e2ϕ/c2ϕi​.​
(11)
All other components vanish. Although gtt​=−c2−2ϕ+O(c−2) diverges, its affine connection has a smooth limit on compact subsets:
Γ(∞)itt​=ϕi​,all other limiting components=0.​
(12)
This is the Newtonian connection from an exponential lapse. It is torsion-free, and its geodesic equation, using t as an affine parameter when t˙=0, is d2xi/dt2=−∂i​ϕ. Thus ϕ has the role of a Newtonian gravitational potential. There is no finite limiting nondegenerate Lorentzian metric in these fixed coordinates; −g(c)/c2 tends to dt2, a rank-one tensor. The limiting connection preserves dt and the contravariant spatial tensor with components hij=δij, htμ=0, which describe the degenerate temporal/spatial structures of this limit.
