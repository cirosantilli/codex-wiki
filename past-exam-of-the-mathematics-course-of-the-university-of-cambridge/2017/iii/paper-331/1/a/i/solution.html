OurBigBook.com Words: 93
Separate the uniform stream and an arbitrary time-dependent gauge from each velocity potential: ϕj​=Uj​x+fj​(t)+ϕj′​. Only gradients of the velocity potential determine the velocity field. The remote fluid must remain in its unperturbed uniform stream, so
∇ϕ1​→(U1​,0)(z→+∞),∇ϕ2​→(U2​,0)(z→−∞).​
(1)
After fixing the harmless gauge, the nonzero-wavenumber perturbation velocity potentials themselves vanish at the corresponding infinities. Incompressible flow and irrotational flow give the Laplace equation for each perturbation. For a normal mode with k>0, the vertical amplitude solves Φj′′​−k2Φj​=0, hence
ϕ1′​=A1​e−kzeik(x−ct),ϕ2′​=A2​ekzeik(x−ct).
(2)
The opposite exponentials are excluded because they make the perturbation grow at infinity. It is the perturbation, rather than the total potential Uj​x+fj​(t), that decays.
