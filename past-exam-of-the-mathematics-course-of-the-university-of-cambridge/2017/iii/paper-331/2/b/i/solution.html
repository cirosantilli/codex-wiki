The qualifier “solution” needs care at the critical level of an internal gravity wave z=0. For 0<k<1, w∼z1−k and w′∼(1−k)z−k: it is a solution separately on either side, not a classical smooth eigenfunction across the critical level of an internal gravity wave. A branch for negative tanhz is also needed. For example, a limit from ci​>0 assigns (tanhz)1−k=∣tanhz∣1−ke−iπ(1−k) on z<0.
The critical-layer regularity of a neutral Hazel mode further gives local finite horizontal kinetic energy only for 0<k<1/2, since incompressible flow gives u=iw′/k and ∫0ϵ​z−2kdz then converges. At k=1, J=0 and w=sechz is smooth after the removable U′′/U quotient is continued. At k=0, J=0 and the formal tanhz profile does not decay at infinity, so it fails the remote endpoint condition. These qualifications prevent interpreting the whole closed parameter interval as a family of classical decaying modes.
