Assume n≥2. The place-permutation action on Cn preserves the coefficient-sum kernel
W={z:∑j​zj​=0}.
(28)
It is the augmentation subrepresentation of a permutation representation. The point action of Sn​ is two-transitive, so the irreducible augmentation criterion for a transitive group action makes W irreducible. The two-row Young permutation module decomposition of M(n−1,1)≅Cn identifies its nontrivial summand as V(n−1,1). Hence W≅V(n−1,1).
All standard Young tableaux of shape (n−1,1) are Tj​, 2≤j≤n, with j below the first cell and the remaining entries increasing along the first row. Their content vectors of standard Young tableaux are
cTj​​(r)=⎩⎨⎧​r−1−1r−2​r<j,r=j,r>j.​

wj​=j(j−1)​e1​+⋯+ej−1​−(j−1)ej​​.
