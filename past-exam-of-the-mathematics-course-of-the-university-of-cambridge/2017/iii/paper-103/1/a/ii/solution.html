Consequently the map sending a diagonal element to its scalar on each Gelfand–Tsetlin basis vector is a ring isomorphism
GZn​≅T∏​C.​
(5)
Each factor is a simple module over this algebra, and the algebra is their direct sum as a module over itself. This proves that GZn​ is a semisimple algebra. The argument uses its explicit diagonal structure; being a subalgebra of a semisimple algebra by itself would not suffice.
