There is an index error in the displayed identity of the original PDF. With its definition of the signed Young permutation module, the tensor identity for induced representations gives
Mα=IndSα​Sn​​sgn≅sgn⊗Mα.​
(59)
Both superscripts must be α. In particular Mλ′≅sgn⊗Mλ′, rather than the printed sign twist of Mλ. For λ=(n), n≥2, the printed left side has dimension n!, while the printed right side has dimension one. Thus that identity cannot hold as written.
The requested character inner product between Mλ and Mλ′ nevertheless has a well-defined answer. Let R be the row Young subgroup of a tableau of shape λ, and let C be its column subgroup, conjugate to Sλ′​. By Frobenius reciprocity and the Mackey restriction formula, the inner product is a sum over R\Sn​/C of
dimHomR∩gCg−1​(1,sgn).
(60)
These double cosets are encoded by nonnegative integer matrices A=(aij​) with row sums λi​ and column sums λj′​, recording intersection sizes of row and column blocks. The intersection subgroup is a product of Saij​​. Its sign representation is trivial exactly when every aij​≤1. Each zero-one matrix contributes one, and every other matrix contributes zero.
There is exactly one zero-one matrix with these margins. Its first row has length λ1​, equal to the number of nonzero column sums, so that row must consist entirely of ones. Delete it, subtract one from every column margin and discard zero columns. The remaining margins are those of (λ2​,λ3​,…) and its conjugate partition; induction forces the rest. The unique matrix is aij​=1 precisely for j≤λi​. Therefore
⟨χMλ​,χMλ′​⟩Sn​​=1.​
(61)
This calculation uses the actual two modules requested in the PDF and does not depend on its erroneous displayed isomorphism.
