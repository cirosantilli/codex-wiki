Put m=n−k and take the subgroup Sm​×Sk​ permuting the first m and last k letters. Define the skew representation of a symmetric group as the multiplicity space
Vλ/μ=HomSm​​(Vμ,ResSm​Sn​​Vλ).​
(39)
The last-letter copy of Sk​ commutes with Sm​, so it acts on a map f by g⋅f=ρλ​(g)∘f. This gives a genuine CSk​-module, without claiming that the whole restricted module is itself the skew representation. Equivalently,
ResSm​×Sk​Sn​​Vλ≅⨁ν⊢m​Vν⊗Vλ/ν.
(40)
Fix a prefix tableau of shape μ and complete it to shape λ. Iterated restriction branching rule for a symmetric group identifies the multiplicity-space orthonormal basis with standard skew Young tableaux, using labels 1,…,k for the last k cells. The Young orthogonal form restricts to this basis. When R=si​T is standard and d=cT​(i+1)−cT​(i),
si​wT​=d−1wT​+1−d−2​wR​.
(41)
An admissible interchange has ∣d∣≥2, so its off-diagonal coefficient is nonzero. Consequently wR​ belongs to the group algebra span of wT​. The preceding reduced-path argument reaches every standard skew tableau, so this span is the whole module. Every wT​ is a cyclic vector for a group representation. The inherited invariant inner product makes this a unitary representation.
