Assume a genuinely inclined plane, 0<α<π/2. With Z=z/h and U=ρgh2sinα/μ, the zero-flux velocity profile is u/U=Z/3−Z2/2. It is positive for 0<Z<2/3, zero at Z=2/3, and negative above that level. Its maximum is U/18 at Z=1/3, and its surface value is −U/6. Thus equal integrated downslope and upslope transports cancel, rather than the fluid being motionless.
