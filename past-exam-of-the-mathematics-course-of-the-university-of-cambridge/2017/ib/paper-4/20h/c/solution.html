  • s→b→e→t: 5.
  • s→c→e→t: 5.
  • s→b→c→d→t: 2.
  • s→b→c→e→t: 1.

The flows on c→a and e→d are zero. Capacity constraints and flow conservation hold at each interior vertex; net source outflow is 6+8+5=19.
