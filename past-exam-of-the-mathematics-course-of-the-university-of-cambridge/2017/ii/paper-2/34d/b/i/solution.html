For noninteracting fermions, each single-particle level has occupation ni​∈{0,1} by the Pauli exclusion principle. A microstate has E=∑i​ϵi​ni​, N=∑i​ni​, so its grand-canonical weight is the product of its independent level weights. The grand potential is Ω=−kTlogZ; the particle number, entropy and pressure follow from N=−∂μ​Ω, S=−∂T​Ω and P=−∂V​Ω. This occupation description is the basis for the product, continuum and adiabatic calculations.
Summing separately over the two allowed occupations of every level gives
Z=∏i​∑ni​=01​e−ni​(ϵi​−μ)/(kT)=∏i​(1+e−(ϵi​−μ)/(kT)).
(118)
Thus
logZ=i∑​log(1+e−(ϵi​−μ)/(kT)).​
(119)
The factorization uses the absence of interactions; each level, including any degeneracy labels, is counted separately.
