Set β=1/(1−α)=1/r​. For fixed t>0 and large n,
P(n1/r(θn​−θ)>t)=(1−ntr​)n⟶e−tr​.
(88)
This endpoint maximum likelihood for a singular location density calculation gives a Weibull distribution with shape r. The estimator is a consistent estimator, and its error has order n−1/r, faster than the n−1/2 error of θn​. Its bias is asymptotic to Γ(1+1/r)n−1/r and its mean squared error to Γ(1+2/r)n−2/r, versus a positive constant times n−1 for the sample-mean estimator. Thus the minimum estimator is preferable for its much faster convergence, despite its positive finite-sample bias. This is a nonregular support-dependent model; ordinary regular-model efficiency bounds do not apply.
