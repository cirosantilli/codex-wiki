If p∣d and p≡3(mod4), a unit of norm −1 would have the form (a+bd​)/2 with integers a,b and satisfy a2−db2=−4. Reducing modulo the odd prime p gives (a/2)2≡−1(modp), impossible because −1 is not a quadratic residue for such a prime. Hence every unit has norm +1 in this case.
