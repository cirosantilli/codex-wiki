For the stated Sine-Gordon equation, explicitly repair the first prefactor by taking V=Vprint​/2, that is 1/(4iλ). Its zero-curvature condition becomes uxt​=sinu. For this corrected pair, tr(w(t,λ)k) is conserved for every λ=0. Since U is entire in λ, so are W and these traces. Their Taylor coefficients in λ supply an infinite family of first integrals, by the identity theorem extending conservation through λ=0. At a fixed spectral parameter the higher traces of a 2×2 matrix are related by the Cayley-Hamilton theorem; no independence of the resulting first integrals is claimed.
