For this chemical reaction network, the law of mass action gives the reaction rates are k1​s2e, k2​c and k3​c. Their stoichiometry gives
s˙=−2k1​s2e+2k2​c,e˙=−k1​s2e+(k2​+k3​)c,c˙=k1​s2e−(k2​+k3​)c,p˙​=2k3​c.
(14)
Here the convention for k1​ absorbs any combinatorial factor for two identical reactants. Direct differentiation gives the two conservation laws
e+c=e0​,s+2c+p=s0​.​
(15)
Eliminating e and p leaves
s˙=−2k1​s2(e0​−c)+2k2​c,c˙=k1​s2(e0​−c)−(k2​+k3​)c.
(16)

u′=−2u2(1−v)+2(μ−λ)v,ϵv′=u2(1−v)−μv,​
(17)

ϵ=s0​e0​​,μ=k1​s02​k2​+k3​​,λ=k1​s02​k3​​,u(0)=1,v(0)=0.​
