The Clebsch-Gordan decomposition for SU2 states
Vm​⊗Vn​≅r=0⨁min(m,n)​Vm+n−2r​.​
(62)
Realize the tensor product as polynomials of bidegree (m,n) in (x1​,y1​) and (x2​,y2​). The determinant D=x1​y2​−y1​x2​ is invariant under the simultaneous SU2 action. For each r, the vector
vr​=Drx1m−r​x2n−r​
(63)
has torus weight q=m+n−2r and is killed by E=x1​∂y1​​+x2​∂y2​​. Applying F=y1​∂x1​​+y2​∂x2​​ produces q+1 nonzero vectors of distinct weights, with Fq+1vr​=0. The relations [E,F]=H and Hvr​=qvr​ give EFjvr​=j(q−j+1)Fj−1vr​, so their span is an irreducible copy of Vq​.
These irreducible submodules have distinct highest weights and form a direct sum: a sum of earlier ones cannot contain an irreducible module inequivalent to all its summands. Equivalently use complete reducibility, obtained by averaging an inner product over the compact group. Assuming m≤n, their total dimension is
∑r=0m​(m+n−2r+1)=(m+1)(n+1),
(64)
which equals the dimension of the tensor product. Hence they exhaust it, proving the theorem.
