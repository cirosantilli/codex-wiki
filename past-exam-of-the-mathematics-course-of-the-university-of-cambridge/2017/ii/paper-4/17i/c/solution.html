The cyclotomic polynomial Φ7​=1+X+⋯+X6 is irreducible: replacing X by X+1 gives an Eisenstein polynomial at seven. Hence the extension has degree six, and
G=(Z/7Z)×≅C6​,σa​(η)=ηa.​
(59)
There is one subgroup of each order 1,2,3,6, and therefore precisely four intermediate fields. The trivial subgroup fixes Q(η); the whole group fixes Q. The order-two subgroup {1,6} is complex conjugation and fixes Q(η+η−1), of degree three: η satisfies X2−(η+η−1)X+1 and is not real.
The order-three subgroup {1,2,4} fixes u=η+η2+η4. Its other conjugate is v=η3+η5+η6. Expanding products and using ∑j=16​ηj=−1 gives u+v=−1 and uv=2. Thus u2+u+2=0, and this fixed field is Q(u)=Q(−7​), of degree two. These generators have the required degrees and are fixed by the indicated subgroups, so the Galois correspondence proves they are the full fixed fields.
