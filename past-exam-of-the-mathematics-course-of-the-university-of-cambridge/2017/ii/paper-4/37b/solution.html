Assume incompressible inviscid layers, gravity −gey​, no surface tension, and irrotational perturbations of the uniform base flows. The full bulk equations are ∇⋅uj​=0 and ∂t​uj​+uj​⋅∇uj​=−ρj−1​∇pj​−gey​. At the rigid walls u1y​(h)=u2y​(−h)=0. At the material interface y=η(x,t) each layer obeys ujy​=ηt​+ujx​ηx​, and p1​=p2​. There is no tangential-velocity matching condition across this vortex sheet.
Write uj​=(Uj​,0)+∇ϕj​, with U1​=U, U2​=−U. Then ∇2ϕj​=0, and the wall conditions are ∂y​ϕ1​(h)=∂y​ϕ2​(−h)=0. Linearizing the interface conditions at zero gives
∂y​ϕj​(0)=(∂t​+Uj​∂x​)η,
(161)
ρ1​(∂t​+U∂x​)ϕ1​−ρ2​(∂t​−U∂x​)ϕ2​=(ρ2​−ρ1​)gη.
(162)
The second follows from linear Bernoulli pressure pj′​=−ρj​(∂t​+Uj​∂x​)ϕj​ and the hydrostatic pressure shift −ρj​gη.
For k>0, use amplitudes ϕ1​=B1​coshk(y−h)eikx+σt and ϕ2​=B2​coshk(y+h)eikx+σt. Kinematics gives B1​=−(σ+ikU)A/[ksinhkh] and B2​=(σ−ikU)A/[ksinhkh]. The dynamic condition becomes
ρ1​(σ+ikU)2+ρ2​(σ−ikU)2=−(ρ2​−ρ1​)gktanhkh.
(163)
Completing the square yields
σ=ikUρ1​+ρ2​ρ2​−ρ1​​±(ρ1​+ρ2​)24ρ1​ρ2​​k2U2−ρ1​+ρ2​ρ2​−ρ1​​gktanhkh​.
(164)
A positive real part occurs precisely when
U2>4ρ1​ρ2​ρ22​−ρ12​​kg​tanhkh.​
(165)
The function tanh(kh)/k decreases strictly from h at k=0 to zero at infinity. Indeed its derivative has numerator khsech2(kh)−tanh(kh)<0, since sinh(2kh)>2kh. Therefore the minimum speed producing instability at every finite nonzero wavenumber is
Ucrit​=4ρ1​ρ2​(ρ22​−ρ12​)gh​​.​
(166)
At equality every k>0 still satisfies the strict growth inequality, since tanh(kh)<kh; the limiting infinite-wavelength mode is marginal. A strict uniform long-wave margin requires U>Ucrit​.
