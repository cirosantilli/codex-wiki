For the M-M-1 queue with Bernoulli feedback, assume 0<δ≤1. The number of service visits before departure is geometric on 1,2,…, with probabilities δ(1−δ)j−1, independent of the independent exponential visit lengths. The transform of the total time actually being served is

This excludes the waiting between visits. A feedback completion changes the customer's position but not the number in the system. By exponential memoryless property, the latter process has births of rate λ and true deaths of rate δμ while nonempty; unsuccessful completions are self-events. It is therefore an M/M/1 number process with effective service rate δμ, giving
λ<δμ,πj​=(1−δμλ​)(δμλ​)j.​
(104)
For positive λ equality is null recurrent, larger arrival rate is unstable, and δ=0 admits no departure-based equilibrium.
To prove the output claim, state the relevant time reversal of a continuous-time Markov chain theorem: an equilibrium continuous-time chain satisfying detailed balance has the same reversed path law. In this reversible birth-death process, final departures are precisely downward jumps; time reversal turns them into upward jumps. Upward jumps are supplied by a state-independent Poisson clock of rate λ, so their process is Poisson. Hence the stationary final-departure process is Poisson of rate λ. This is the output conclusion of Burke theorem for the effective number process; it does not say the process of all service completions has that rate.
