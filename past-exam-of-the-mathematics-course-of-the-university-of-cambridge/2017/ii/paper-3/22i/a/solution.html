OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For algebraic varieties that are irreducible topological spaces, a rational map X⇢Y is an equivalence class of morphisms U→Y on nonempty open subsets of X, with two representatives equivalent if they agree on a nonempty open intersection. Such subsets are dense. A birational map has a rational inverse; equivalently it restricts to an isomorphism between dense open subsets. In function field language it induces an isomorphism k(Y)≅k(X) over the ground field.
