In the birth-death master equation, The upward jump has rate λ and the downward jump rate βx, so the first two jump moments per unit time are A(x)=λ−βx and B(x)=λ+βx. The second-order Kramers-Moyal expansion gives the diffusion approximation of a birth-death process
∂t​P=−∂x​[(λ−βx)P]+21​∂x2​[(λ+βx)P].​
(8)
For the associated Fokker-Planck equation, integrating by parts with vanishing moment boundary terms gives d⟨f⟩/dt=⟨Af′+21​Bf′′⟩. Taking f=x,x2 yields
dtd⟨x⟩​=λ−β⟨x⟩,dtd⟨x2⟩​=−2β⟨x2⟩+(2λ+β)⟨x⟩+λ.​
(9)
For example, if m0​=⟨x(0)⟩, then m(t)=λ/β+(m0​−λ/β)e−βt, while the variance satisfies v′=−2βv+λ+βm. The two boxed moment equations also follow exactly from the discrete Markov jump-process generator, since quadratic functions have no higher jump terms. A reflecting diffusion confined to x≥0 can have extra boundary contributions; the formal approximation near extinction is not an exact replacement for the discrete process.
