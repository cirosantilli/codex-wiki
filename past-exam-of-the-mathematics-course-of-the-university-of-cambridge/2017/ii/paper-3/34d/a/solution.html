A clockwise engine Carnot cycle has four reversible steps. At hot temperature Th​, isothermal expansion absorbs Qh​=Th​ΔS>0 and does positive work by the gas. Adiabatic expansion then cools it to Tc​, with zero heat and positive work by the gas. Isothermal compression at Tc​ emits Qc​=−Tc​ΔS and requires work on the gas. Finally adiabatic compression returns it to Th​, with zero heat and work on the gas.
For an ideal gas, the p,V isotherms obey pV=constant and the adiabats pVγ=constant, the latter being steeper. In the T,S plane, the isotherms are horizontal and the adiabats vertical, forming a clockwise rectangle. Heat occurs only on the horizontal legs, while work occurs on all four p,V legs.
W=(Th​−Tc​)ΔS,ηCarnot​=1−Tc​/Th​.​

Reversing the arrows gives a refrigerator, reversing the heat and work signs.
