The Chebyshev polynomial bound on [0,1] gives ∣P(λ)∣≤1/8, and the actual endpoints 0,1 attain it. At the dominant eigenvalue, P(1+ϵ)=1/8+ϵ+ϵ2. Thus
ρ2=1+8ϵ+8ϵ21​,ρ=1−4ϵ+O(ϵ2).​
(106)
For a small prescribed error δ relative to a fixed initial-component factor C, the number of matrix-vector iterations is approximately log(C/δ)/(−logρ). The three leading denominators are ϵ,2ϵ,4ϵ. Therefore the iteration counts are in the ratio 1:1/2:1/4: the single shift halves and the alternating double shift quarters the leading count. A pair of double-shift steps costs two matrix-vector products, already accounted for by using ρ per iteration rather than ρ2.
