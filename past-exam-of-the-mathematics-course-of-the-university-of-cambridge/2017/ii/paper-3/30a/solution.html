The centre manifold theorem gives, near an equilibrium of a sufficiently smooth system, a local invariant manifold tangent to the generalized eigenspaces with zero-real-part eigenvalues, expressible as a graph of a function over them. Its reduced dynamics govern local equilibria and their stability in the presence of strictly stable transverse directions. The manifold need not be unique or analytic; finite Taylor jets can nevertheless be determined from invariance. Treating a parameter as a variable with μ˙​=0 gives the extended centre manifold for a parameter.
At r=1 the linearized (x,y) block has eigenvalues 0,−2, and the z direction has eigenvalue −1. Thus the origin is a nonhyperbolic equilibrium, with stable dimension two and non-extended centre dimension one. The extended centre dimension is two. The transformed equations are

Z=u2+Ow​(4),V=−41​μu+4a+1​u3+Ow​(5),u˙=21​μu+2a−1​u3+Ow​(5).​

Here Ow​(j) means terms of weighted order at least j under the stated scaling. If a<1, the pitchfork bifurcation is supercritical: stable branches u∼±μ/(1−a)​ appear for μ>0 as the origin loses stability. If a>1, it is a subcritical pitchfork bifurcation: unstable branches occur for μ<0, while the origin is stable there.
For a=1, assign μ weight four. The leading cubic terms in u˙ cancel. The invariance equations now give V=u3/2+Ow​(5) and Z=u2+0u4+Ow​(6): Zu​u˙ first contributes at weight six and v2 also first contributes there. Substitution then gives
u˙=21​μu+u5+Ow​(7),u∼±(−μ/2)1/4(μ<0).​

The derivative along the nonzero branches is −2μ>0, so they are unstable. The origin is stable for μ<0, unstable for μ>0, and nonlinearly unstable at μ=0 because the leading term is +u5. This is a degenerate subcritical pitchfork bifurcation.
