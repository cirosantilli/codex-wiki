Write a=μ/λ<1. The embedded birth-death chain moves upward with probability λ/(λ+μ) and downward with probability μ/(λ+μ) away from zero. The gambler's ruin formula, obtained by solving its two-point difference equation on {0,…,N} and then letting N→∞, gives Pk​(hit 0)=ak. Translation gives return probability a from r+1 to r. From r−1 the chain hits r almost surely: before that hit it remains in a finite reflecting interval, from every state of which a uniformly positive finite-step chance of hitting r is available. This also proves that starting at zero it hits each r≥1 almost surely.
After departure from r≥1 the return probability is therefore
rr​=λ+μλ​a+λ+μμ​=λ+μ2μ​.
(101)
The holding rate is qr​=λ+μ, so the exponential occupation-time parameter is qr​(1−rr​)=λ−μ. Applying the Strong Markov property at the almost-sure first hit gives
Tr​∼Exp(λ−μ),r≥1​.
(102)
At zero the holding rate is λ and return probability after departure is a, again giving parameter λ−μ. From initial state k, the expected occupation time at zero is ak/(λ−μ), since the time is zero unless zero is reached. Averaging over the initial law with probability generating function G yields
ET0​=λ−μG(μ/λ)​​.
(103)
For μ=0, the interpretation is a0=1, ak=0 for k>0, and G(0)=P(X(0)=0); the same formulas hold.
