The identity is one conjugacy class. In N∖{1}, conjugation by c pairs each element with its inverse, giving four classes represented by a,b,ab,ab2, each of size two. All nine elements of Nc are conjugate, since conjugation by n∈N changes the reflection component by n2 and squaring is a bijection of N.
There are two linear characters: the trivial character and the character equal to one on N and minus one on Nc. Write ω=e2πi/3. The remaining four irreducible characters of a representation are induced from the nontrivial characters aibj↦ωri+sj of N, pairing (r,s) with (−r,−s). They are exactly the two-dimensional representations already proved irreducible. Taking representatives (1,0),(0,1),(1,1),(1,2) yields the character table

Indeed the induced value on aibj is ωri+sj+ω−(ri+sj), equal to two or minus one, and is zero off N. The squared dimensions sum to 1+1+4⋅4=18, so this is the complete table.
An element of N centralizes c only if it equals its inverse, hence only if it is the identity. No reflection centralizes a. Thus Z(E)={1} is cyclic, but the two linear representations kill N and each two-dimensional irreducible has a nontrivial kernel of a group homomorphism of order three. Therefore a cyclic centre does not imply a faithful irreducible representation.
