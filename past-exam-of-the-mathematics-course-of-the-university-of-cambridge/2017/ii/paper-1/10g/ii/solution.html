For any vector y∈F2n​, use its Hamming weight w(y)=#{j:yj​=1}. The stated product identity also follows directly by summing each coordinate independently:
∑y​uw(y)(−1)x⋅y=∏j=1n​(1+(−1)xj​u)=(1−u)w(x)(1+u)n−w(x).
(26)
For t=0, interchange the two finite sums with u=s/t. Character orthogonality evaluates the inner sum over codewords, whereas the product identity evaluates the sum over all binary vectors. Thus
2k∑y∈C⊥​(s/t)w(y)=∑x∈C​(1−s/t)w(x)(1+s/t)n−w(x).
(27)
Multiply by tn to obtain the MacWilliams identity in the paper's weight-enumerator convention:
WC⊥​(s,t)=2−kWC​(t−s,t+s)​.
(28)
Both sides are polynomials, so equality for t=0 extends to t=0 as well; the intermediate quotient is not a restriction on the final identity.
