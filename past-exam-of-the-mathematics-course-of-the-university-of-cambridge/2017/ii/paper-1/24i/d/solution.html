The semicubical parabola has coordinate ring
A=k[x,y]/(x2−y3)≅k[t3,t2]⊂k[t],
(88)
under x↦t3, y↦t2. The isomorphism is valid in every characteristic: after division by the monic polynomial in x, a remainder P(y)+xQ(y) maps to a sum of distinct even powers and odd powers at least three, and can vanish only when both polynomials are zero.
Its fraction field contains t=t3/t2, which is an integral element over A because it satisfies z2−y=0. However t∈/A, since a polynomial in t2,t3 has no term of degree one. Thus A is not an integrally closed domain. The ring k[t] of the affine line is a principal ideal domain and therefore an integrally closed domain. This property is preserved by ring isomorphism. By coordinate-ring functoriality,
Z(x2−y3)≅A1​.
(89)
The parametrization is a bijection on points over an algebraically closed field, but that does not make its inverse a morphism at the cusp.
