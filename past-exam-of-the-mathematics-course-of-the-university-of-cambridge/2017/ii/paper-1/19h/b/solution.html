By the preceding argument take a nonzero integer m∈a. The ideal property gives mOL​⊆a. If d=[L:Q], an integral basis identifies the additive group of OL​ with Zd, so
OL​/mOL​≅(Z/∣m∣Z)d.
(57)
The natural projection onto OL​/a is surjective. Thus the latter is an abelian group of order at most ∣m∣d, proving
OL​/a is finite abelian​.
(58)
In particular the ideal is a full-rank integer lattice; this permits the integral-basis argument in the next part.
