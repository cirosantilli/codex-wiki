Let F=∣F∣>0 and choose an orientation of a regular curve following the vector field. Its unit tangent vector is t=σF/F, where σ=±1 is constant along a connected regular segment. Arc length differentiation along that curve is d/ds=(σF/F)⋅∇. Therefore
dsdt​=F1​(F⋅∇)(FF​)=F2(F⋅∇)F​−F3F(F⋅∇)F​.
(45)
Taking the cross product with t removes the term parallel to F:
t×dsdt​=σF3F×(F⋅∇)F​.
(46)
Where the Frenet frame exists, t×t′=κ(t×n)=κb. Thus
F3F×(F⋅∇)F​=σκb.​
(47)
The printed sign corresponds to orientation along or against the field. Taking magnitudes gives the orientation-independent curvature of an integral curve of a vector field, κ=∣F×(F⋅∇)F∣/F3. This scalar formula also applies at zero curvature, where b itself may be undefined.
