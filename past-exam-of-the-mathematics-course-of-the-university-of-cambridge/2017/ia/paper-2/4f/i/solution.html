Integrate the joint probability density over y to obtain the marginal density of X:
fX​(x)=∫0∞​xe−x(y+1)dy=e−x,x>0.
(10)
Thus X has an exponential distribution of rate one. Dividing the joint probability density by this marginal density gives the conditional density
fY∣X=x​(y)=xe−xy1{y≥0}​,x>0.​
(11)
Equivalently, Y∣X=x has an exponential distribution of rate x. The value at x=0 can be assigned arbitrarily: it is a probability-zero conditioning value and the density ratio there is not meaningful.
