If f is continuous on [a,b], the Heine-Cantor theorem makes it uniformly continuous. Given ε>0, choose δ so that intervals of length below δ have oscillation below ε/(b−a). A partition of mesh below δ then satisfies
SD​(f)−sD​(f)<ε,
(96)
proving that Continuous functions are Riemann integrable.
For the function defined using g, choose K with ∣f∣≤K. Given ε>0, choose δ>0 so small that 2Kδ<ε/2.
