In odd dimension,
detA=detAT=det(−A)=−detA,
(54)
so detA=0. Since A is real, its kernel contains a nonzero real vector a.
The plane a⊥ is invariant under A, because a⋅Ab=−(Aa)⋅b=0. On this two-dimensional plane, Ab is perpendicular to b. Put ∣Ab∣=θ∣b∣ with θ>0. Then A2b is parallel to b, and
b⋅A2b=−(Ab)⋅(Ab)=−θ2∣b∣2,
(55)
so
A2b=−θ2b​.
(56)
The matrix exponential fixes the kernel vector:
eAa=a​.
(57)
Separating even and odd powers in its series and using A2jb=(−1)jθ2jb gives
eAb=cosθb+θsinθ​Ab​.
(58)
Thus eA acts as a rotation through angle θ on a⊥ and fixes its axis Ra.
