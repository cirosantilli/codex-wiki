The dimensions are [m]=M, [α]=MT−1, [u0​]=LT−1, and [g]=LT−2. Under the stated model, m,α,u0​,g are the only parameters determining the ascent time. If mpαqu0r​gs is dimensionless, then p+q=0, r+s=0, and −q−r−2s=0, leaving one independent dimensionless variable. One convenient choice is
λ=mgαu0​​,T=αm​f(λ).​
(9)
Thus dimensional analysis fixes the form, but Newton's second law must determine the function f.
Take upward velocity as positive. Throughout ascent, Newton's second law with linear drag gives mv˙=−mg−αv. An integrating factor or direct solution of this linear ordinary differential equation gives
v(t)=(u0​+αmg​)e−αt/m−αmg​.
(10)
The first zero of this velocity is the highest point, so
f(λ)=log(1+λ),T=αm​log(1+mgαu0​​).​
(11)
This vertical ascent under linear drag has T<u0​/g for u0​>0, since log(1+λ)<λ. The limit α→0 recovers the no-drag ascent time u0​/g; u0​=0 gives T=0.
