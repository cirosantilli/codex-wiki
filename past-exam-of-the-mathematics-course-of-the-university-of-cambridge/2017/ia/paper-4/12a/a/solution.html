Write the total remaining rocket mass as M=M+m(t), with m˙=−α. Over an interval dt, an exhaust mass dme​=αdt is expelled backwards with inertial velocity v−u to first order. Applying momentum conservation to the rocket and this exhaust gives
Mv=(M−dme​)(v+dv)+dme​(v−u)+o(dt).
(58)
Cancellation and division by dt give the rocket equation
(M+m)dtdv​=αu.​
(59)
With M>0, m(t)=m0​−αt, and 0≤t≤m0​/α, integration gives
v(t)−v0​=ulogM+m(t)M+m0​​.
(60)
At burnout,
vfinal​=v0​+ulog(1+Mm0​​).​
(61)
The logarithm depends on the mass ratio, not on the burn rate. This follows from momentum conservation of the complete rocket–exhaust system; applying a constant-mass equation directly to the rocket alone would omit the momentum carried away.
