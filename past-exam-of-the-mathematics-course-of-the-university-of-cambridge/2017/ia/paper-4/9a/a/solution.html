 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2017 ia Paper 4 9A a
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
Use momentum conservation and conservation of energy in the laboratory inertial frame. The photon has momentum magnitude E/c, while the outgoing Electron satisfies the relativistic energy-momentum relation. Its total energy and momentum obey
Ee​=mc2+E1​−E2​,c2∣pe​∣2=E12​+E22​−2E1​E2​cosθ.
(37)
Substitute these into Ee2​=m2c4+c2∣pe​∣2. Cancelling m2c4 and (E1​−E2​)2 gives mc2(E1​−E2​)=E1​E2​(1−cosθ). Both photon energies are positive, so division yields
E2​1​−E1​1​=mc21−cosθ​,E2​=1+(E1​/mc2)(1−cosθ)E1​​.​
(38)
This is Compton scattering. The energies include the Electron's rest energy; using only its kinetic energy in the mass-shell equation would give the wrong result. The outgoing photon has unchanged energy at θ=0 and smaller energy for a nonzero scattering angle.
