The original PDF measures θ at the rim point O, from the positive x′-axis to OP; it is not the angle at the centre of the circle. The converted TeX omits this entire subpart. In rotating Cartesian coordinate system, the centre is (a,0), and the nonzero intersection of the ray r=ρ(cosθ,sinθ) with (x′−a)2+y′2=a2 has ρ=2acosθ. Therefore
r=2acosθ(cosθ,sinθ,0)=a(1+cos2θ,sin2θ,0).​
(52)
The second form stays regular even at the rim point O, where the polar radius is zero. Define the outward unit normal from the centre and the increasing-angle unit tangent vector by
n=(cos2θ,sin2θ,0),τ=(−sin2θ,cos2θ,0).
(53)
The first two derivatives in the rotating reference frame are v′=2aθ˙τ and a′=2aθ¨τ−4aθ˙2n. These describe a bead on a horizontal hoop rotating about its rim.
