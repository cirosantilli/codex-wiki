Because the contact is frictionless, the horizontal normal force is Fh​=Nn, with N a signed outward component. Any vertical contact component balances Newtonian gravity and does not enter the horizontal equations. With ω=ωz, the rotating reference frame equation gives
a′=Nn−2ωz×(2aθ˙τ)+ω2r.
(54)
Since z×τ=−n, r⋅n=2acos2θ, and r⋅τ=−asin2θ, its normal and tangential components are
−4aθ˙2=N+4aωθ˙+2aω2cos2θ,2aθ¨=−aω2sin2θ.
(55)
Hence the requested horizontal force vector is
Fh​=−2a(2θ˙2+2ωθ˙+ω2cos2θ)(cos2θ,sin2θ,0).​
(56)
Its magnitude is the absolute value of the scalar coefficient. That coefficient need not always be negative: the constraint can exert either sign of normal force, depending on the bead's relative velocity. The tangential equation, which does not contain the reaction, is θ¨=−(ω2/2)sin2θ​.
