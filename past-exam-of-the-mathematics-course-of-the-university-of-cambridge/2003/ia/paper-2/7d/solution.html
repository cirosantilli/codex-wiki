For a real two-by-two matrix, the characteristic polynomial is λ2−(trA)λ+detA. Under the stated distinct-eigenvalue assumption, all nonzero homogeneous modes are purely oscillatory exactly when the eigenvalues are ±iω with ω>0. Their sum and product imply trA=0, detA=ω2>0. Conversely these trace and determinant conditions force those eigenvalues, so there is no exponential growth, decay or Jordan-block secular growth.
