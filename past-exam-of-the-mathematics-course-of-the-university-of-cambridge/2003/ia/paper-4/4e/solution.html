About a fixed origin, the total momentum and angular momentum are
P=∑i​mi​vi​,L=∑i​xi​×mi​vi​.
(11)
A time-independent translation xi′​=xi​−b leaves every velocity unchanged. Therefore
P′=P,L′=L−b×P​.
(12)
The cross product b×P is perpendicular to P, giving the invariant
L′⋅P′=L⋅P​.
(13)
The other requested transformation follows from the vector triple-product identity:
L′×P′=L×P+∣P∣2b−(b⋅P)P​.
(14)
The actual PDF asks about L×P; the TeX's occurrence of F is a transcription error.
If P=0, the translation term vanishes and angular momentum is independent of origin. If P=0, choose
b=∣P∣2P×L​+λP,λ∈R​.
(15)
Then b×P=L−(L⋅P)P/∣P∣2, and so
L′=∣P∣2L⋅P​P.
(16)
Thus the new angular momentum is parallel to the total momentum. The freedom along P gives the central axis of a momentum system. If the invariant dot product is zero, the new angular momentum is the zero vector. All expressions assume a translated fixed origin, not a moving reference frame.
