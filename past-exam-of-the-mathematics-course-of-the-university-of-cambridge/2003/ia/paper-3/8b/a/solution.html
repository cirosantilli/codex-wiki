For the eigenvalue 1, the equations (A−I)v=0 give v2​=v1​ and v3​=0. Thus every corresponding nonzero eigenvector is a nonzero multiple of (1,1,0)T. These eigenspaces together have dimension 2, so they cannot provide an eigenvector basis of R3. The matrix is not diagonalizable; the eigenvalue 1 has algebraic multiplicity 2 but geometric multiplicity 1.
