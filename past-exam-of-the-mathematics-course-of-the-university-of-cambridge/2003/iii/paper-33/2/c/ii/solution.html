Thus Y is a continuous local martingale, but it is not Brownian motion. Brownian motion from any fixed starting point has quadratic variation t. If the displayed bracket were identically t, differentiating its continuous density would force e2Wt​=1 for every t, hence Wt​=0 identically, an event of probability zero. This is the cosine-exponential Brownian local martingale. Its initial value Y0​=1 is retained in the next part.
