An index-λ handle in dimension d is Dλ×Dd−λ. A handle attachment glues its attaching region Sλ−1×Dd−λ to a corresponding neighborhood in the boundary of the preceding manifold with boundary. Besides the embedding of the attaching sphere, one specifies a framing of an embedded sphere, namely the normal-disk directions used in this gluing. Corners are then smoothed.
In dimension four, a two-handle is D2×D2 and attaches along S1×D2. Its attaching circle is a knot in the boundary of the zero-handle D4. Identify that boundary with S3 and remove a point away from the circles to draw them in R3. Disjoint attaching circles give a framed link. Each integer records the twist of its normal framing relative to the zero Seifert framing; the integer is also the linking number of the circle and its framed push-off. The resulting boundary change is Dehn surgery on a framed link. This is the two-handle part of a Kirby diagram.
For S2×S2, use the following explicit handle decomposition. Write each factor as two disks, S2=D−2​∪D+2​, glued along their boundary circles. The four product pieces give
h0=D−2​×D−2​,h12​=D+2​×D−2​,h22​=D−2​×D+2​,h4=D+2​×D+2​.
(26)
The first is a four-dimensional zero-handle after rounding its corners. Its boundary is the union of the solid tori S1×D2 and D2×S1. The two middle product pieces attach along these tori. Their attaching circles S1×{0} and {0}×S1 form the standard Hopf link, with linking number one after choosing orientations.
Both framings are zero. For example, the normal disk of S1×{0} is the second disk factor. Its constant normal push-off has linking number zero with the original circle: the circle bounds D2×{0} in the four-ball, and a parallel push-off bounds a disjoint translated disk. Thus the framed self-intersection is zero; the same argument applies with the factors interchanged. These product framings are the zero Seifert framings.
After these two two-handles, the remaining product piece D+2​×D+2​ is a four-ball. Its boundary is exactly the boundary of the assembled first three pieces, so it attaches as a four-handle. Equivalently, zero surgery on the Hopf link gives S3, which this handle caps. We have therefore constructed the actual product, proving
S2×S2=one zero-handle+two zero-framed Hopf-linked two-handles+one four-handle.​
(27)
There are no one- or three-handles. As a check, the two factor spheres have self-intersection zero and meet once, giving the intersection form (01​10​). The explicit product construction, rather than the intersection form alone, identifies the smooth four-manifold. This is the zero-framed Hopf-link handle decomposition of the sphere product.
