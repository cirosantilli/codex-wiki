In a convenient cardinal parameter normalization, take the unit m-cube and project by the sum of its coordinates. Its pushforward density is the unit-direction univariate box spline
Bm​(x)=∫[0,1]m​δ(x−∑r=1m​ur​)du1​⋯dum​.
(17)
Equivalently, with B1​=1[0,1)​ at its discontinuity points,
Bm​=B1​∗⋯∗B1​,Bm​(x)=∫01​Bm−1​(x−u)du.​
(18)
Here there are m convolution factors; some conventions instead index the spline by its degree m−1. The half-open convention for m=1 assigns knot values consistently and does not change the pushforward measure.
Support and positivity. A sum of m numbers in [0,1] lies in [0,m], so the closed support is finite. For 0<x<m, the interval of u∈(0,1) with 0<x−u<m−1 has positive length. Induction in the convolution recurrence therefore gives Bm​(x)>0 in the interior. For m≥2, Bm​(0)=Bm​(m)=0: strict positivity includes the open support interval, not its endpoints.
Partition of unity. The translates of B1​ cover the line exactly once under the half-open convention. Nonnegativity permits interchange of sum and integral, and induction gives
∑j∈Z​Bm​(x−j)=∫01​∑j∈Z​Bm−1​(x−u−j)du=1.
(19)
Thus j∑​Bm​(x−j)=1​, a locally finite sum. Each Bm​ also has integral one, although partition of unity does not mean its maximum value is one.
For m≥2, repeated integration gives the explicit truncated-power form
Bm​(x)=(m−1)!1​∑j=0m​(−1)j(jm​)(x−j)+m−1​.
(20)
On each unit knot interval this is a polynomial of degree at most m−1, and on (0,1) it is xm−1/(m−1)!, so the degree is attained. Each truncated power has m−2 continuous derivatives; the derivative of order m−1 jumps at the first knot. Therefore the dependence on the projected box dimension is
degree m−1,continuity Cm−2,support width m in unit knot intervals.​
(21)
For m=1 the pieces are constants and the function is discontinuous at the knots. If geometric width is measured under orthogonal projection onto the unit diagonal instead, the projected coordinate is x/m​, so the physical support width is m​; its unit-mass density is m​Bm​(m​x). These are different scalings of the same cardinal spline, not different continuity or degree claims.
For a bivariate box spline, choose a spanning direction matrix Ξ=(ξ1​,…,ξm​) in the plane and project the unit cube by u↦Ξu. Define its normalization by
∫R2​MΞ​(x)φ(x)dx=∫[0,1]m​φ(Ξu)du.
(22)
The support is the zonotope ∑r​[0,1]ξr​, and the polynomial pieces generally have total degree m−2. The grid comes from the chosen direction families and their lattice translates. Two independent directions, with repetitions, give tensor-product B-splines on a parallelogram grid. Three families such as e1​,e2​,e1​+e2​ give a three-direction triangular grid; an affine change of basis can make it the usual equilateral triangular grid. The four families e1​,e2​,e1​+e2​,e1​−e2​ give the criss-cross square grid and the quadratic four-direction box spline. Changing the direction matrix and repeating directions therefore changes the grid, support and smoothness while retaining the box-projection construction.
