The local film formula diverges right at the rim θ0​. Accordingly the thin-film calculation is an outer approximation away from a small matching fringe, not a claim of a uniformly thin film at its contact with bulk water. Both integrated stress densities vanish at that rim; in the thin-film limit the fringe contributes only a higher-order correction. Surface-tension curvature, altered thermal gradients or appreciable density-change fluxes would require a different balance.
