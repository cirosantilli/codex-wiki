For the requested connection to Besicovitch sets, let δ be small and dilate the unit segments of a bounded planar Kakeya set by δ−2. Their δ-neighborhoods become direction-separated rectangles of length δ−2 and width δ−1. A smooth circle cap of angular width cδ has a Fourier extension operator of magnitude at least c′δ on such a rectangle: after removing the constant phase, the tangential phase variation is O(c) and the normal variation is O(c2). A frequency modulation translates the rectangle to any desired location. Choose separated caps for the rectangle directions, and multiply their modulated densities by independent Rademacher random variables. Because the caps are disjoint, the input remains bounded by one.
