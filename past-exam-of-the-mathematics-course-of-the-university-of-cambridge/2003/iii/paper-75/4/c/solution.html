If d≤0, the stated leading positive-gap branch does not exist. Without the absolute-pressure relation, the problem gives a family parameterized by Q/U, rather than a unique Δp(U). Errors from finite L, higher powers of λ and higher powers of C have been omitted consistently in this leading approximation. Resolving the displayed O(C) correction to b−2 requires the omitted finite-domain and higher-λ corrections to be smaller than C; otherwise only its leading value is justified.
