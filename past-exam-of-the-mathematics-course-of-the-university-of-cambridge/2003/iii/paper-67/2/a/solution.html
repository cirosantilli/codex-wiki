For every admissible real α, ρ(1)=0 and ρ′(1)=2α=σ(1), giving consistency of a numerical method. Factorization gives
ρ(ζ)=(ζ−1)((2+α)ζ−(2−α)).

The second root is r=(2−α)/(2+α). The root condition for a multistep method requires ∣r∣≤1 and forbids a repeated root at one. For real α=−2,
∣r∣≤1⟺(2−α)2≤(2+α)2⟺α≥0.
(11)
At α=0 both roots equal one, violating zero-stability. For α>0 the second root lies strictly inside the unit disk. The Dahlquist equivalence theorem therefore gives
convergence exactly for α>0.​
(12)
