There is a genuine indexing defect in the printed PDF: its new value is at m+1, while every old-time coefficient is centered at m. On a fixed grid the literal residual contains
ku(x+δ,t+k)−u(x,t)​−Lδ​u(x,t)=kδ​ux​+O(1)+O(k)+O(δ2),k=μδ2.
(18)
For a general solution the leading term is ux​/(μδ), so this is not a consistent diffusion discretization. It also leaves one new interior value unspecified while imposing a generally incompatible update on a prescribed boundary value. The calculation that follows is for the intended same-node update Umn+1​=Umn​+kLδ​Umn​; that correction is necessary, not an unnoticed transcription change.
For the midpoint flux stencil for one-dimensional diffusion, define
Lδ​u(x)=δ2a(x−δ/2)[u(x−δ)−u(x)]+a(x+δ/2)[u(x+δ)−u(x)]​.
(19)
Assume sufficient smoothness of a and the solution for the displayed remainders. Multiplying the centered Taylor expansions of a and u gives
Lδ​u=(aux​)x​+δ2(12auxxxx​​+6a′uxxx​​+8a′′uxx​​+24a(3)ux​​)+O(δ4).
(20)
The first time difference is (u(t+k)−u(t))/k=ut​+kutt​/2+O(k2). After using the diffusion equation, the normalized local truncation error is
τmn​=2k​utt​−δ2(12auxxxx​​+6a′uxxx​​+8a′′uxx​​+24a(3)ux​​)+O(k2+δ4).
(21)
Therefore, for the corrected update,
τ=O(k+δ2),d=kτ=O(k2+kδ2).​
(22)
The first is the residual divided by the time step; the second is the unnormalized one-step defect. At fixed μ=k/δ2 they are respectively O(δ2) and O(δ4). A rate based on derivatives is not justified for a merely bounded nonsmooth a.
