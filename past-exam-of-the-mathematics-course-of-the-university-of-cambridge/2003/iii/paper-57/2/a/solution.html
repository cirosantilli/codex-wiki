Choose a basis Ta​ of the Lie algebra, with [Tb​,Tc​]=cabc​Ta​. On the Matrix Lie group set
λ=g−1dg=Ta​λa,ρ=dgg−1=Ta​ρa.
(8)
Left multiplication by a constant matrix preserves λ, while right multiplication preserves ρ. Differentiating g−1g=I gives d(g−1)=−g−1(dg)g−1. Apply the graded Leibniz rule to obtain
dλ=−λ∧λ,dρ=+ρ∧ρ.
(9)
Because the wedge product antisymmetrizes the matrix products, these Maurer-Cartan equations become
dλa=−21​cabc​λb∧λc,dρa=+21​cabc​ρb∧ρc.​
(10)
Follow the vector-field naming used in the question: La​(g)=Ta​g is right-invariant and generates left multiplication, whereas Ra​(g)=gTa​ is left-invariant and generates right multiplication. They are dual to ρa and λa, respectively. For any one-form η, dη(X,Y)=X(η(Y))−Y(η(X))−η([X,Y]). Evaluating the two Maurer-Cartan equations on the corresponding invariant fields yields
[La​,Lb​]=−ccab​Lc​,[Ra​,Rb​]=ccab​Rc​,[La​,Rb​]=0.​
(11)
For the last identity, the left and right multiplication flows commute. The opposite sign for the right-invariant vector fields is therefore intrinsic; the letters attached to the two families are only a convention.
