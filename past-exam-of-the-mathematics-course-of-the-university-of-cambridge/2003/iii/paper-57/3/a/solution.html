A Poisson manifold is a smooth manifold P with a bilinear bracket on C∞(P) that is antisymmetric, satisfies the Jacobi identity and obeys {f,gh}={f,g}h+g{f,h}. Equivalently it has a bivector Π defining {f,g}=Π(df,dg) with vanishing Schouten bracket [Π,Π]; nondegeneracy is not required.

For completeness, evaluate dω=0 on Xf​,Xg​,Xh​. Its three derivative terms sum to minus the cyclic sum of {f,{g,h}}, and its three bracket terms sum to minus the same cyclic sum, using the displayed identity. Hence that sum is zero. This proves the Jacobi identity for the Poisson bracket, not merely the Jacobi identity modulo constants. The inverse of a closed nondegenerate two-form therefore defines a Poisson manifold.
