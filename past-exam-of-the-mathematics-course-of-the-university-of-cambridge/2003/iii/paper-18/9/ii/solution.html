For T=GF, define the Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor
K(D)=(GD,aD​),aD​=GεD​,K(h)=Gh.​
(25)
The algebra unit law is a triangle identity. Naturality of the counit at εD​ gives its algebra associativity law; naturality at h:D→E gives GhaD​=aE​T(Gh), making K(h) an algebra morphism.
Suppose first that G reflects G-split coequalizers. For every D, consider the counit presentation
FGFGDFGεD​⇉εFGD​​​FGDεD​​D.
(26)
It equalizes the pair by counit naturality. After applying G it becomes T2GD⇉TGDaD​​GD, with parallel maps μGD​,T(aD​). This is split: take s=ηGD​ and t=ηTGD​. The identities aD​s=1, μGD​t=1 and T(aD​)t=ηGD​aD​ follow from the algebra law, a monad unit law, and naturality of η. Reflection therefore makes εD​ a coequalizer in D.
For faithfulness, if Gh=Gk then naturality gives hεD​=εE​FGh=εE​FGk=kεD​. Coequalizers are epic, so h=k.
For fullness, let α:GD→GE be an algebra morphism, so αaD​=aE​Tα. Put h0​=εE​Fα:FGD→E. Its two composites with the counit-presentation pair are equal: by counit naturality they are εE​F(aE​Tα) and εE​F(αaD​). Hence there is a unique h:D→E with hεD​=h0​. Applying G gives GhaD​=aE​Tα=αaD​. Since aD​ is split epic, Gh=α. This proves full faithfulness directly by descent.
Conversely suppose K is full and faithful. We need the following explicit algebra lifting fact. For algebra morphisms f,g:(A,a)⇉(B,b) with a split underlying coequalizer q:B→Q, the arrow qb:TB→Q equalizes Tf,Tg. The split diagram is preserved by T, so Tq is their coequalizer and there is a unique c:TQ→Q with cTq=qb. Precomposing the unit equation with the epimorphism q gives cηQ​q=q; precomposing the associativity equation with the epimorphism T2q gives equality by bT(b)=bμB​. Thus c is an algebra action. If an algebra map h:B→(Z,z) equalizes f,g, its unique underlying factor k:Q→Z satisfies kcTq=hb=zTh=zTkTq. Cancelling Tq shows kc=zTk. This proves monad algebra forgetful functor creates split coequalizers.
Now take any existing equalizing arrow e:B→D in D whose image is such a coequalizer. The lifting fact makes the unique compatible action on GD its already given action aD​, since Ke preserves that action. Hence Ke is a coequalizer of Kf,Kg in the algebra category. For any arrow h:B→Z equalizing the pair, its unique algebra factor KD→KZ lifts to an arrow D→Z by fullness of K; faithfulness gives its factorization equation and uniqueness. Therefore e is a coequalizer in D. We have proved the full comparison and reflection of split coequalizers equivalence
K is full and faithful⟺G reflects G-split coequalizers.​
