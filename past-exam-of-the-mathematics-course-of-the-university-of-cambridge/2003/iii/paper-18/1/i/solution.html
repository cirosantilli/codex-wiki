Write HA​=C(−,A) for a representable presheaf. The contravariant Yoneda lemma states that, for every presheaf on a category X, evaluation at the identity is a bijection
Nat(HA​,X)⟶X(A),α⟼αA​(1A​),​

natural in both A and X. Smallness of C ensures the relevant families and natural-transformation collections are sets.
For x∈X(A) define αBx​(h)=X(h)(x) for each h:B→A. This family is a natural transformation: for u:B′→B, X(u)αBx​(h)=X(u)X(h)x=X(hu)x=αB′x​(hu). Its identity value is x. Conversely, naturality of any α at h:B→A gives αB​(h)=X(h)αA​(1A​). Thus evaluation and the displayed construction are inverse.
For a map v:A→A′, precomposing a transformation HA′​→X with postcomposition HA​→HA′​ corresponds to applying X(v):X(A′)→X(A). For a transformation τ:X→Y, postcomposition corresponds to x↦τA​(x). These identities prove the stated naturality in both variables.
