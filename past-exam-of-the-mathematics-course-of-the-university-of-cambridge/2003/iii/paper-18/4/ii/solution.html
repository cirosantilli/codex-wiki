For fixed U, the categorical coend on the right is the quotient of ∐W​C(U,W)×X(W) by the identifications
(fh,x)V​∼(h,X(f)x)W​(h:U→W, f:W→V, x∈X(V)).
(8)
Define ΦU​([h,x])=X(h)x. This is well defined because the contravariant functor law gives X(fh)x=X(h)X(f)x. Its proposed inverse sends y∈X(U) to [1U​,y]. The composite to X(U) is the identity. In the other direction the coend relation, applied with f=h and initial map 1U​, gives [h,x]=[1U​,X(h)x]. Thus the inverse is genuine and
X(U)≅∫WC(U,W)×X(W).​
(9)
For a:U′→U, the coend map replaces h by ha; its image is X(ha)x=X(a)X(h)x, proving naturality in U. A morphism of presheaves acts on x and commutes with every X(h) by naturality, proving naturality in X. This is the density formula for presheaves.
