Efficient convection keeps the interior at nearly uniform specific entropy. For the fully ionized monatomic gas, the heat capacity ratio is 5/3, so the adiabatic process gives P=Kρ​ρ5/3, with Kρ​ spatially constant. The ideal gas relation P=RρT/μ then implies
P=KT5/2​,K=(μR​)5/2Kρ−3/2​.
(1)
The constant is spatial, not temporal: loss of specific entropy during contraction changes it. This is a stellar polytrope with polytropic index n=3/2. We neglect radiation pressure and take the thin photosphere as an atmospheric matching layer rather than a significant fraction of the stellar mass.
For a fixed dimensionless profile of the Lane-Emden equation, the stellar homology relations have the form
ρc​=Cρ​R3M​,Pc​=CP​R4GM2​,Tc​=Cρ​CP​​RRμGM​,
(2)
where Cρ​,CP​ are positive dimensionless structure constants. More explicitly, write ρ(r)=ρc​f(x) and m(r)=Mh(x) with x=r/R and fixed dimensionless profiles. Mass conservation gives M=4πρc​R3∫01​f(x)x2dx. Integrating the hydrostatic pressure support equation, with negligible surface pressure on the interior scale, gives Pc​=(GMρc​/R)∫01​h(x)f(x)x−2dx. Both integrals are fixed positive numbers, proving the two central scalings. Substituting these relations into K=Pc​/Tc5/2​ gives
K=K0​μ−5/2M−1/2R−3/2​,K0​=Cρ5/2​CP−3/2​R5/2G−3/2.
(3)
Thus the same polytropic index fixes K0​ throughout this homologous sequence, while the dimensional stellar polytrope constant changes.

L=−dtdE​=−7R23GM2​R˙.
