Using cases as disease haplotypes and controls as control haplotypes in the model requested here, the chromosome counts estimate
P(A∣d)=20/200=0.10,P(a∣d)=0.90,P(A∣D)=120/200=0.60,P(a∣D)=0.40.
(32)
These are conditional marker allele frequencies, not the reverse conditional probabilities P(d∣A). Under independent founder chromosomes, conditional on the father's Dd,Aa genotype, the phase AD/ad has weight proportional to 0.60⋅0.90=0.54, and Ad/aD has weight proportional to 0.10⋅0.40=0.04. Their normalized weights are 27/29 and 2/29. This conditions on the model's observed parental genotypes; the omitted disease-allele frequencies cancel in the phase-weight ratio.
Using the two phase-specific pedigree likelihoods already derived,
LLD​(θ)​=0.580.54L1​(θ)+0.04L2​(θ)​=8(0.58)θ(1−θ)​{0.54(1−θ)+0.04θ}=4.64θ(1−θ)(0.54−0.50θ)​.​
(33)
The normalizing factor is independent of θ, so maximize g(θ)=0.54θ−1.04θ2+0.50θ3. Its derivative gives exactly
1.5θ2−2.08θ+0.54=0.​
(34)
Only the smaller root lies in [0,1/2]:
θ=32.08−2.082−4(1.5)(0.54)​​≃0.34590.​
(35)
On this interval g′′(θ)=−2.08+3θ<0, so this stationary point is the unique maximum. The maximum LOD score, retaining the same linkage disequilibrium frequencies in numerator and denominator, is
Zmax​=log10​(1/2)(1/2)(0.54−0.25)θ(1−θ)(0.54−0.50θ)​≃0.05898.​
(36)
This is only a likelihood ratio of about 1.145 against θ=1/2, hence very weak evidence. The population frequencies are treated as fixed plug-in estimates, as requested; uncertainty in those estimates and possible disease-model differences between controls and normal-allele chromosomes would matter in a fuller analysis.
