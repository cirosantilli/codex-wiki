This includes q(ω)/q(ω′), not merely the posterior ratio. A particularly simple choice is ηi​=ϵi​, the weight prior: its factors cancel and the ratio is just the mixture likelihood ratio. A proposal concentrated around a pilot estimate can mix better, provided it retains positive density throughout the relevant simplex. Both target and proposal densities use the same k−1 independent simplex coordinates, so this direct weight proposal has no extra transformation Jacobian determinant.
