Fix a prime q of E over p, and a prime r of LE over q. Let P be its restriction to L. Inside the completed compositum, put
F=Kp​,J=Eq​,U=LP​.
(44)
The assumption means U/F is an unramified extension. Its residue degree is some f; the completed extension (LE)r​/J is the corresponding compositum UJ/J. It suffices to prove that this is unramified, regardless of the ramification of J/F.
Here is a proof of unramified extensions remain unramified under base change using the classification already established. Choose an integral generator α of U/F whose monic minimal polynomial g∈OF​[X] has irreducible separable reduction of degree f. Write kF​=Fq​ and kJ​=Fqr​. Let Jf​/J be the degree-f unramified extension. Its residue field Fqrf​ contains Fqf​, so gˉ​ has a simple root there. Hensel lemma lifts this root to a root of g in Jf​. This gives an F-embedding U↪Jf​.
An unramified extension of a local field is Galois, so all F-embeddings of U into a fixed algebraic closure have the same image. The original U therefore lies in Jf​, and UJ is an intermediate field of the unramified extension Jf​/J. Its ramification index is one. Since q and r were arbitrary,
e(r/q)=1 for every prime above p.​
(45)
This proof also explains why no linear-disjointness assumption on L and E, and no assumption that E/K is unramified, is needed.
