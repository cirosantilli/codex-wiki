Preserving synchronous gauge means T′+HT=0 and L′=kT. The first equation is (aT)′=0, and integration of the second gives the complete residual scalar transformation for each nonzero Fourier mode:
T=af​,L=kf∫τa(s)ds​+g.​

Changing the lower limit only changes g. Taking the trace and trace-free longitudinal part of Δhij​ gives
Δh=2kL−6HT,Δhs​=2kL.​
(23)
Consequently Δhs′​=2k2T and Δhs′′​=2k2T′. The cosmological Newtonian potential changes by
ΔΦ=2k2Δhs′′​+HΔhs′​​=T′+HT=0,
(24)
while its spatial counterpart changes by
ΔΨ=−6Δh−Δhs​​−2k2HΔhs′​​=HT−HT=0.
(25)
Thus both potentials are invariant under residual synchronous-gauge freedom, although h and hs​ individually are not.
