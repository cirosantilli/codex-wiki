Let E be the real span of the roots in h∗. The weight lattice is
ΛW​=P={μ∈E:μ(hα​)∈Z for every root α},​
(33)
where hα​ is the coroot in the root's sl2 subalgebra associated with a root, normalized by α(hα​)=2. It suffices to test the simple coroots. Equivalently, if ωi​(hαj​​)=δij​ defines the fundamental weights, then P=⨁i​Zωi​.
To prove integrality of representation weights on coroots, restrict a finite-dimensional module V to the root's sl2 Lie algebra. By the classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations, the eigenvalues of its Cartan element hα​ are integers. Thus every μ∈ΛV​ has μ(hα​)∈Z. The simple coroots form a basis of the real Cartan space, so these integral values also put μ in E. Therefore ΛV​⊆ΛW​​ for every finite-dimensional representation in this setting. The weight lattice need not coincide with the smaller root lattice.
