Use h=diag(1,−1), e=E12​ and f=E21​ in the sl2 Lie algebra, so hx=x, hy=−y, ey=x and fx=y. On the symmetric power, a monomial x3−kyk has weight 3−2k. The tensor product of Lie algebra representations adds weights, because h acts as h⊗I+I⊗h.
Put Ak​=x⊗x3−kyk and Bk​=y⊗x3−kyk for 0≤k≤3. These eight vectors are a basis of U; their weights are 4−2k and 2−2k. Thus the weight-space decomposition and a weight basis for each summand are
