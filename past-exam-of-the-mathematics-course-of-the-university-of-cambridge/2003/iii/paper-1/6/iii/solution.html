The double-bond arrow points to the middle node in the PDF. Since ∥α∥=1 and ∥β∥=2​, the short root α must occupy that middle node and the long root β the left node. The right node γ, joined to α by a single bond, is another short root. Thus the diagram is C3 root system type, labelled as follows.
The bonds require γ⋅β=0, γ⋅α=−1/2 and ∥γ∥2=1. Writing γ=(a,b,c) gives a=−1/2, b=a, and c2=1/2. One choice is therefore

The single-bond rank-two diagram is A2 root system type. Three suitable positive simple pairs for its root subsystems are
(α,γ),(α,α+β+γ),(α+β,γ).​

Each pair p,q has squared lengths one and inner product −1/2, so its angle is 120 degrees. Its plane meets the full root set in ±p,±q,±(p+q), with p+q respectively α+γ, 2α+β+γ and α+β+γ. These exhaust the roots of the plane, and their coordinates in the pair have one sign. Thus each pair is genuinely a simple basis of an A2​ subsystem.
The double-bond diagram is rank-two B2 root system type, equivalently C2​. Three suitable pairs, written short root first, are
(α,β),(α+γ,β),(γ,2α+β).​
