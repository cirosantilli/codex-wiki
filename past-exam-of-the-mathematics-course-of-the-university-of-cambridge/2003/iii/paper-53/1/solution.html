 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2003 iii Paper 53 Section A 1
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
Take {γa,γb}=2ηab and γab=γ[aγb]=[γa,γb]/2, with unit-weight antisymmetrization. The Clifford algebra first gives
γa​γmγa=(2−D)γm,γa​γb​γa=(2−D)γb​.
(1)
Expand the two antisymmetrized gamma matrices using γab​=γa​γb​−ηab​:
γab​γmγab=γa​γb​γmγaγb−Dγm.
(2)
Anticommuting γm past the next contracted factor gives
γa​γb​γmγaγb​=−γa​γb​γaγmγb+2γmγb​γb=−(2−D)γb​γmγb+2Dγm=[2D−(D−2)2]γm.​
(3)
Subtracting the remaining Dγm gives the gamma-matrix vector sandwich
α=−(D−1)(D−4).​
(4)
In particular it vanishes in four dimensions. The result is independent of the choice of Lorentz signature when all indices are contracted consistently. Defining the bivector without its factor 1/2 would multiply α by four.
