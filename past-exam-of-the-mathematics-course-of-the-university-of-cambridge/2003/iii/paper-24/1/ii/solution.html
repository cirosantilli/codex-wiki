The preceding dimension of level-one modular forms gives dimM16​=2 and dimS16​=dimM4​=1. Thus E4​Δ, which begins with q, spans S16​. A normalized weight-sixteen Eisenstein series and E44​ both have constant term one, so their difference is a multiple of E4​Δ.
There is a normalization error in the printed auxiliary expansion: for the usual normalized E16​, the coefficient multiplier is 16320/3617, rather than 255/(8⋅3617). Indeed, the Fourier expansion of a normalized Eisenstein series gives −32/B16​, and the Bernoulli number B16​=−3617/510 gives 16320/3617. The printed multiplier is smaller by a factor of 512. With the correct normalization, the first Fourier coefficients yield
E44​=1+960q+O(q2),E16​=E44​−36173456000​E4​Δ.

The Fourier coefficients of E44​ are integers, as are those of E4​Δ: E4​ has its integral divisor-sum expansion and the modular discriminant has the product q∏r≥1​(1−qr)24. Also 3456000+16320=960⋅3617, and gcd(16320,3617)=1. Reducing the identity modulo 3617 and cancelling the invertible multiplier proves the weight-sixteen Eisenstein congruence
cn​≡σ15​(n)(mod3617)(n≥1).​
(9)
The desired congruence is valid; it is the auxiliary normalization in the PDF that requires correction.
