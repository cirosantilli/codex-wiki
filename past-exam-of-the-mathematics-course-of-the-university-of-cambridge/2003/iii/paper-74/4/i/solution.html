Convolution with the retarded acoustic Green function, followed by two integrations by parts in the source coordinate, yields
ρ′(x,t)=∂xi​​∂xj​​∫4πc02​∣x−y∣Tij​(y,t−∣x−y∣/c0​)​dVy​.
(36)
For the acoustic compact-source approximation, replace the distance and the source retarded time by r=∣x∣ and τ=t−r/c0​ in the leading moment. Thus the integral is Sij​(τ)/(4πc02​r), where Sij​=∫Tij​dV. In the acoustic far field, each spatial derivative acts predominantly on retarded time: ∂xi​​τ=−ni​/c0​, where ni​=xi​/r. Derivatives of 1/r and of ni​ give lower powers of r. The two retarded derivatives therefore give
ρQ′​(x,t)∼4πc04​rni​nj​S¨ij​(τ)​=4πc04​r3xi​xj​S¨ij​(τ)​​.
(37)
This is the compact acoustic quadrupole field, retaining its angular stress projection.
For the compact acoustic quadrupole Mach-number scaling, let the source length be ℓ, typical fluctuation speed be U, and typical time be ℓ/U. The low-Mach number stress is T=O(ρ0​U2), so S=O(ρ0​U2ℓ3) and S¨=O(ρ0​U4ℓ). Consequently
ρQ′​/ρ0​=O(rℓ​m4),m=U/c0​​.
(38)
With the geometric ratio separated, this is the stated fourth-power dependence. It assumes an advective source time scale and compactness ωℓ/c0​=O(m)≪1. An externally driven source with an independent frequency does not acquire the same Mach power automatically.
