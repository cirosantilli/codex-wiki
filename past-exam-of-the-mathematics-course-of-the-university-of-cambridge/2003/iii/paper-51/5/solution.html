Use [Lm​,Ln​]=(m−n)Lm+n​+12c​(m3−m)δm+n,0​ and Ln†​=L−n​. A normalized highest-weight vector obeys L0​∣h⟩=h∣h⟩ and Ln​∣h⟩=0 for n>0. Its Virasoro descendants are products of negative modes. Their inner products are determined entirely by c,h and the Virasoro algebra; for example ∥L−1​∣h⟩∥2=2h, so unitarity requires h≥0. An irreducible unitary highest-weight Virasoro module is the Virasoro Verma module quotient by its null submodules, and its surviving inner product must be positive definite.
The classification for c<1 is the Unitary Virasoro discrete series:
c=1−m(m+1)6​,h=hr,s​=4m(m+1)[(m+1)r−ms]2−1​​,
(29)
where m is an integer at least 2, 1≤r≤m−1, and 1≤s≤m. The identification (r,s)∼(m−r,m+1−s) leaves m(m−1)/2 distinct modules. The two generating singular-vector levels are rs and (m−r)(m+1−s); all their Virasoro descendants are removed in the irreducible quotient. For m=2 the only module is the trivial c=h=0 module; for m≥3 these are the nontrivial unitary discrete-series modules. There are no other irreducible unitary highest-weight representations with c<1. This is the classification theorem, rather than a condition inferred only from finitely many descendant norms.
At c=1/2, m=3 and the possible weights are
h=0,h=21​,h=161​​.
(30)
Construct them with a single free chiral Majorana fermion:
ψ(z)=∑r​br​z−r−1/2,{br​,bs​}=δr+s,0​,br†​=b−r​.
(31)
The free chiral Majorana fermion conformal field theory has T=−:ψ∂ψ:/2. In the Neveu–Schwarz sector, r∈Z+1/2, while in the Ramond sector, r∈Z. With positive modes moved to the right and the zero-mode product antisymmetrized, the corresponding generators are
Ln​=21​∑r​(r−2n​):bn−r​br​:+aδn,0​,a=0 in NS,a=161​ in R.
(32)
The oscillator relation gives [Ln​,br​]=−(r+n/2)bn+r​. The stress-tensor operator product expansion, or the reordering of the quadratic generators, then gives the Virasoro algebra with c=1/2. One can check both constants directly in the ground states. In the NS sector,
L−2​∣0⟩=21​b−3/2​b−1/2​∣0⟩,∥L−2​∣0⟩∥2=41​=2c​.
(33)
In the R sector, b02​=1/2 and L−1​∣R⟩=21​b−1​b0​∣R⟩, whose norm squared is 1/8. The relation [L1​,L−1​]=2L0​ requires 2hR​=1/8, so hR​=a=1/16. Thus the Ramond sector zero-mode shift is essential.
The three constructions in Ising Virasoro modules from a Majorana fermion are explicit. The NS vacuum is a highest-weight vector of weight 0. The state b−1/2​∣0⟩ is a highest-weight vector of weight 1/2: applying any positive Ln​ gives −(n/2−1/2)bn−1/2​∣0⟩=0, including the zero coefficient for n=1. Since Ln​ is quadratic in fermions, the two cyclic modules lie in the even and odd fermion parity sectors, respectively. For R, choose a normalized ground state annihilated by all bn​ with n>0 and with b0​∣R⟩=±∣R⟩/2​. All positive Ln​ annihilate it and its weight is 1/16. The two zero-mode sign choices give isomorphic Virasoro modules; one choice suffices to construct the third module.
The oscillator inner products are positive, and each cyclic highest-weight representation just constructed is irreducible. Indeed any nonzero proper invariant graded submodule would have a lowest-energy vector w of weight greater than the cyclic ground weight. Every positive Ln​ annihilates w; thus w is orthogonal to every negative-mode descendant of the original ground vector, by moving the modes across the inner product. It is also orthogonal to that ground vector by its different L0​ eigenvalue. Since those Virasoro descendants span the cyclic module, w would have zero norm, a contradiction. This proves irreducibility directly inside the positive fermionic Fock space.
In fact the cyclic modules fill the entire even NS, odd NS and chosen R Fock spaces. Otherwise an orthogonal remaining lowest vector would be the ground vector of a further unitary highest-weight Virasoro module; its weight would lie in Z>0​, 1/2+Z>0​ or 1/16+Z>0​, respectively. None belongs to the classified list {0,1/2,1/16}. As a check, their Virasoro characters, defined by χh​(q)=TrqL0​−c/24, are
χ0​(q)χ1/2​(q)χ1/16​(q)​=2q−1/48​[n≥0∏​(1+qn+1/2)+n≥0∏​(1−qn+1/2)],=2q−1/48​[n≥0∏​(1+qn+1/2)−n≥0∏​(1−qn+1/2)],=q1/24n≥1∏​(1+qn).​
(34)
The NS signs project onto even and odd occupation number; the R product uses the single chosen ground state. Null vectors of the abstract Virasoro Verma modules are automatically zero in this construction. In particular L−1​∣0⟩=0, and the weight-1/2 and weight-1/16 ground states obey, respectively, (L−2​−43​L−12​)∣1/2⟩=0 and (L−2​−34​L−12​)∣1/16⟩=0, as is also verified by their level-two Gram matrices.
