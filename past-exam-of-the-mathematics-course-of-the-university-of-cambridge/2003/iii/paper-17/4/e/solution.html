PS4×T4​(t)=(1+t4)(1+t)4,

so b2​=6 and b4​=2. The proposed equality also gives b0​=b8​=1. Even if compactness was not stated separately, these two numbers force the putative eight-manifold to be connected and compact: the top ordinary real cohomology of a connected noncompact manifold is zero.
The auxiliary Hard Lefschetz theorem on de Rham cohomology says that, on a compact Kähler manifold of complex dimension n, Ln−k:Hk→H2n−k is an isomorphism, where Lα=[ω]⌣α. Here n=4, so L2:H2→H6 is injective. If Lα=0, then L2α=0 and therefore α=0. Thus L:H2→H4 is injective, forcing b2​≤b4​. The inequality 6≤2 is impossible. Hence these Betti numbers cannot occur for a Kähler eight-manifold.
