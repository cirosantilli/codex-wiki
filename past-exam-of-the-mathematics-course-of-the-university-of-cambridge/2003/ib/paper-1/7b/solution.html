Since u and v are harmonic conjugates, f=u+iv is a holomorphic function on D, with ux​=vy​ and uy​=−vx​. The entire function w↦eiw2 can be composed with f. Its value is
eif2=ei(u2−v2+2iuv)=e−2uv[cos(u2−v2)+isin(u2−v2)]=U+iV.
(13)
By the complex chain rule the composition is holomorphic on D, so its real and imaginary parts obey the Cauchy-Riemann equations. They are harmonic as well: differentiating those equations gives Uxx​+Uyy​=Vyx​−Vxy​=0 and likewise Vxx​+Vyy​=0. Hence U,V are a pair of harmonic conjugates on D. No simple-connectivity assumption is needed because the conjugate pair is already supplied globally. The PDF has e−2uv in both expressions; the differing TeX exponents are transcription defects.
