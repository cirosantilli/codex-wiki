The image contour has polar representation 2cos2θe3iθ. It is invariant under complex conjugation and under reflection through the origin. As θ runs from zero to 2π, the successive real-axis visits are
2, 0, 1, −1, 0, −2, 0, −1, 1, 0, 2,
(40)
at angles 0,π/4,π/3,2π/3,3π/4,π,5π/4,4π/3,5π/3,7π/4,2π. To specify the crossing direction, differentiate:
dθd​p(eiθ)=i(5p(eiθ)−4eiθ).
(41)
Its imaginary component is 6 at the point 2, −6 at −2, 3 at either visit to 1, and −3 at either visit to −1. At a visit to zero it is −4cosθ, nonzero for each of the four angles. Therefore all these visits are transverse crossings of the real axis, even though different arcs share the same image point.
