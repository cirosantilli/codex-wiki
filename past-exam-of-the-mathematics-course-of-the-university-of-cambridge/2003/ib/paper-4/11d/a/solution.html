Use separation of variables, ϕ=R(r)Θ(θ). After multiplying the Laplace equation by r2/(RΘ), the two separated equations are
Θ′′+λΘ=0,r2R′′+rR′−λR=0.
(26)
Single-valuedness requires 2π-periodicity. Thus the nonconstant angular modes have λ=n2, n=1,2,…, and angular factors cosnθ,sinnθ. Their radial factors are rn,r−n. The zero mode has constant angular factor and radial solution A+Blogr; a term proportional to θ would be multivalued.
Finiteness at the origin removes the logarithm and negative powers. In the disk the general regular harmonic expansion is
ϕ=A0​+n≥1∑​rn(An​cosnθ+Bn​sinnθ)​.
(27)
For the exterior, regularity at every finite radius allows
ϕ=C0​+D0​logr+n≥1∑​[(Cn​rn+Dn​r−n)cosnθ+(En​rn+Fn​r−n)sinnθ]​.
(28)
Coefficients must give convergent harmonic expansions on the domain and the intended boundary regularity at r=a. If “finite” is intended to mean bounded as r→∞, then D0​=Cn​=En​=0 and only the constant and decaying modes remain. Infinity is not included in the printed interval; moreover part (b)'s uniform background flow necessarily has an unbounded potential. Stating both interpretations avoids discarding that background mode.
