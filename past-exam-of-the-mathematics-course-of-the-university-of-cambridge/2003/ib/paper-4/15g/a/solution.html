The zero-composition subspaces of a linear map are subspaces because the defining equations are homogeneous linear equations in β: they hold for the zero map and are preserved by addition and scalar multiplication. A map with βα=0 vanishes on imα, so it factors uniquely through the quotient vector space V/imα. Conversely every map from that quotient to U gives such a β. Therefore
Ml(α)≅L(V/imα,U),dimMl(α)=(dimV−rankα)dimU​.
(50)
The condition αβ=0 says exactly that the image of β lies in kerα. Consequently
Mr(α)≅L(V,kerα),dimMr(α)=dimVdimkerα​.
(51)
Both dimension formulas use the fact that the space of maps between spaces of dimensions p,q has dimension pq: matrix units in any chosen bases supply a basis.
