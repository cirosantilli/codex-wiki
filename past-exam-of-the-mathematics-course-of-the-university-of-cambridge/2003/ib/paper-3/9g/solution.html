The discriminant of a binary quadratic form is d=b2−4ac; it is unchanged by a unimodular matrix change of integral variables and satisfies d≡0 or 1(mod4). For such a d, the discriminant criterion for prime representation by a binary quadratic form says that some integral binary quadratic form of discriminant of a binary quadratic form d represents an odd prime p exactly when d is a square modulo p, including zero. Equivalently,
there is an integer B with B2≡d(mod4p).​
(24)
These versions agree: a square root modulo p can be chosen even when d≡0(mod4) and odd when d≡1(mod4), by adding p if needed, and then the Chinese remainder theorem applies.
For completeness, if f(x,y)=p, then (x,y) is primitive, since any common divisor would have its square divide p. Complete (x,y) to a basis of Z2 with determinant one using Bézout's identity. The transformed binary quadratic form has leading coefficient p and discriminant of a binary quadratic form B2−4pC=d. Conversely, a suitable B makes [p,B,(B2−d)/(4p)] an integral binary quadratic form representing p at (1,0).
For x2+3y2, the discriminant of a binary quadratic form is −12. The prime 3 is represented. If p=3 is represented, reducing modulo 3 gives p≡x2≡1(mod3), which also excludes p=2. To prove sufficiency for p≡1(mod3), quadratic reciprocity gives
(p−3​)=(p−1​)(p3​)=(3p​)=1.
(25)
The criterion constructs a positive definite binary quadratic form [p,B,C] of discriminant of a binary quadratic form −12. It is primitive: a common divisor of its coefficients would divide p, and if that divisor were p, then p2 would divide 12, impossible for p=2,3.
We must still establish that this form is equivalent to x2+3y2, rather than merely some form of the same discriminant of a binary quadratic form. In a positive definite integral binary quadratic form, replace x by x+ky to arrange ∣b∣≤a. If c<a, apply (x,y)↦(−y,x) and repeat. Each such exchange strictly decreases the positive integer leading coefficient, so this reduction algorithm for a positive definite binary quadratic form terminates with ∣b∣≤a≤c. Then 12=4ac−b2≥3a2, so a≤2. With a=1, parity forces b=0, giving c=3. With a=2, the only integral choices are b=±2,c=2, and these are not primitive. Thus every primitive positive definite binary quadratic form of discriminant of a binary quadratic form −12 is equivalent to [1,0,3], and preserves its represented integers. Therefore
p=x2+3y2 for integers x,y⟺p=3 or p≡1(mod3).​
(26)
