 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2003 ib Paper 3 19G d
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
Take any positive definite inner product ⟨ , ⟩0​ on U and define the inner product adapted to an idempotent linear map
⟨u,v⟩ϕ​=⟨ϕu,ϕv⟩0​+⟨(I−ϕ)u,(I−ϕ)v⟩0​.​
(82)
It is symmetric and bilinear. If its quadratic value at u is zero, positive definiteness of the original inner product forces both ϕu=0 and (I−ϕ)u=0, hence u=0. Thus it is positive definite.
Using ϕ2=ϕ and (I−ϕ)ϕ=0 gives
⟨ϕu,v⟩ϕ​=⟨ϕu,ϕv⟩0​=⟨u,ϕv⟩ϕ​.
(83)
The map is therefore self-adjoint for this new inner product and remains idempotent, so it is an orthogonal projection for the explicitly constructed inner product. Geometrically the construction makes its complementary kernel and image orthogonal.
