Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2003 / ib / Paper 3 / 19G / b / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2003 ib Paper 3 19G b
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
For a normal operator ϕ, the adjoint operator identity and ϕ∗ϕ=ϕϕ∗ give
∥ϕu∥2=⟨ϕ∗ϕu,u⟩=⟨ϕϕ∗u,u⟩=∥ϕ∗u∥2.
(79)
Positive definiteness implies kerϕ=kerϕ∗. Also (imϕ)⊥=kerϕ∗, since orthogonality to every ϕv means ⟨ϕ∗u,v⟩=0 for every v. In finite dimension this gives imϕ=(kerϕ)⊥.
The idempotent linear map decomposes U=kerϕ⊕imϕ as in part (a), now orthogonally, and acts as zero on its kernel and identity on its image. Writing u=k+w and v=k′+w′ in these orthogonal summands,
⟨ϕu,v⟩=⟨w,w′⟩=⟨u,ϕv⟩.
(80)
Thus ϕ is self-adjoint, and together with idempotence this proves ϕ is an orthogonal projection.
