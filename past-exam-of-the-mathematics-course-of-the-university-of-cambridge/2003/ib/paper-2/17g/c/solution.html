Taking traces in the defining equation gives trC+trC=2trC, so the trace is real. Put a=trC/2. Then K=C−aI is traceless and satisfies K∗=−K, so −iK is a traceless Hermitian matrix. Hence every element has a unique representation
C=aI+i∑j​vj​Aj​,a,vj​∈R.

Conversely each such matrix satisfies the defining equation. This proves Q is a real four-dimensional vector space, with basis I,iA1​,iA2​,iA3​.
Substitution into the given map gives
Φ(aI+ij∑​vj​Aj​)=aI−j∑​vj​Aj​.​
(60)
Its values are Hermitian, and it acts on real coordinates by (a,v1​,v2​,v3​)↦(a,−v1​,−v2​,−v3​). This invertible real linear coordinate transformation proves the required isomorphism. The inverse sends aI+∑j​uj​Aj​ to aI−i∑j​uj​Aj​.
