the adiabatic sound speed is
c2=dρdp​=ρ2B2​,c=ρB​.
(280)
Since ∫(c/ρ)dρ=−B/ρ=−c, choose invariants that vanish in the equilibrium state (u,c)=(0,c0​):
R+​=u−c+c0​,R−​=u+c−c0​​.
(281)
Consequently
u+c=c0​+R−​,u−c=−c0​+R+​​.
(282)
In a right-moving simple wave with R−​=0, the R+​ characteristic speed is therefore u+c=c0​, independent of amplitude. The transport equation becomes (∂t​+c0​∂x​)R+​=0, so the whole velocity profile translates rigidly at the equilibrium sound speed c0​ without nonlinear distortion.
