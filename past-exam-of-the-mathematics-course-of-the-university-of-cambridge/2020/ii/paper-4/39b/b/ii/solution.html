Let τ be the time at which a right-running characteristic curve leaves the piston. The boundary velocity and the characteristic it emits are
u=aωcos(ωτ),x=asin(ωτ)+c0​(t−τ).
(283)
With
ϕ=ω(t−c0​x​),θ=ωτ,ϵ=c0​aω​<1,
(284)
these relations become
ϕ=θ−ϵsinθ,U(ϕ)=aωcosθ​.
(285)
Because dϕ/dθ=1−ϵcosθ>0 and ϕ(θ+2π)=ϕ(θ)+2π, U is single-valued and 2π-periodic behind the wavefront. Ahead of the first characteristic, where ϕ<0, the fluid remains undisturbed and U=0. The implicit relation is Kepler equation; unless ϵ=0, its inverse is not a pure sinusoid.
Series reversion gives
θ=ϕ+ϵsinϕ+ϵ2sinϕcosϕ+O(ϵ3).
(286)
Expanding the cosine then gives
cosθ=cosϕ−ϵsin2ϕ−23​ϵ2sin2ϕcosϕ+O(ϵ3),
(287)
and hence
U(ϕ)=aω(cosϕ−ϵsin2ϕ−23​ϵ2sin2ϕcosϕ+O(ϵ3))​(ϕ>0).
(288)
