Let N be the number of visits to the server made by one customer. Then N has the geometric distribution with success parameter δ, and its service periods E1​,E2​,… are independent exponential random variables of rate μ. For s≥0, the Laplace transform of the total service time T=E1​+⋯+EN​ is
Ee−sT=∑k≥1​δ(1−δ)k−1(μ+sμ​)k=s+δμδμ​.
(153)
Thus
T∼Exp(δμ)​.
(154)
At each service completion, a successful departure reduces the queue length by one and occurs at rate δμ while the queue is nonempty; feedback completions leave its length unchanged. The queue-length process is therefore the birth-death process of an ordinary M/M/1 queue with service rate δμ. It is positive recurrent exactly when λ<δμ, and then, with ρ=λ/(δμ),
πn​=(1−ρ)ρn,n≥0​.
(155)
