 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2020 ii Paper 4 22I b
OurBigBook.com Words: 141
Write C(K)=⋃n≥1​Sn​ with every Sn​ equicontinuous. Its closure En​=Sn​​ is still equicontinuous, because uniform limits preserve the same oscillation inequalities. The Baire category theorem applied to the Banach space C(K) shows that some En​ has nonempty interior. Hence there are f0​∈C(K) and r>0 such that the open ball B(f0​,r) lies in En​.
Fix x∈K. Equicontinuity of En​ gives a neighborhood U of x on which every member of En​ changes by less than r/4. If y∈U were distinct from x, the normality of a compact Hausdorff space and the Urysohn lemma would give h∈C(K) with h(x)=0, h(y)=r/2, and ∥h∥∞​=r/2. Both f0​ and f0​+h belong to En​, yet
∣h(y)−h(x)∣≤∣(f0​+h)(y)−(f0​+h)(x)∣+∣f0​(y)−f0​(x)∣<r/2,
(108)
a contradiction. Thus U={x}, so every point is isolated and K is a discrete space. A compact discrete space is finite, since its cover by singleton open sets has a finite subcover.
