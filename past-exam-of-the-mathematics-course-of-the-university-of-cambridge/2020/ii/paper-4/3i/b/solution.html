Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2020 / ii / Paper 4 / 3I / b / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2020 ii Paper 4 3I b
OurBigBook.com Words: 118
Perform every addition in the finite field F2​. From the two transmitted strings ci​=ai​+ki​ and di​=ai​+ki+1​ one obtains
ci​+di​=ki​+ki+1​(1≤i≤N).
(5)
Choosing one candidate value of k1​ recursively determines k2​,…,kN+1​, and hence determines the candidate plaintext ai​=ci​+ki​. There are only two candidates: changing k1​ complements every recovered key bit and every plaintext bit. The information that the message makes sense normally selects the intended candidate.
Once this ambiguity is resolved, any other ciphertext positions encrypted with the recovered segment k1​,…,kN+1​ can be decrypted. Key bits outside that segment remain independent and uniformly distributed, so this mistake gives no information about messages using only those bits. This is the two-time pad attack: reusing related one-time-pad key material destroys perfect secrecy.
