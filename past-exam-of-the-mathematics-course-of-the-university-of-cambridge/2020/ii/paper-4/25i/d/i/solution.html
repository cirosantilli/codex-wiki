False with the limsup printed in the paper. Let the even-indexed surfaces be one fixed unit sphere and the odd-indexed surfaces one fixed ring torus. Then
limsupn→∞​infx∈Sn​​Kn​(x)=1>0,

but infinitely many surfaces are tori. The two fixed surfaces lie in one ball and have uniformly bounded area. Their injectivity radii have a positive common lower bound, and compactness gives a common bound on ∣∇K∣, hence on every radial derivative ∣∂r​K∣. Thus this alternating sequence satisfies all four additional conditions as well.
