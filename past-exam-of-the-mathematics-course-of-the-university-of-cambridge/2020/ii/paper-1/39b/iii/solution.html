With B=0, the stated biharmonic stream function for planar Stokes flow is
ψ=Asin2θ+Crsinθ+rDsin3θ​.
(466)
Requiring it to vanish at r=2asinθ and r=4asinθ gives
A+2aC+2aD​=0,A+4aC+4aD​=0.
(467)
Thus A=−6aC and D=8a2C. Writing C=α yields
ψ=α(rsinθ−6asin2θ+r8a2sin3θ​)=rαsinθ​(r−2asinθ)(r−4asinθ).​
(468)
Using ur​=ψθ​/r and uθ​=−ψr​, differentiation shows that ur​cosθ+uθ​sinθ equals α on the inner boundary and −α/2 on the outer boundary. Both wall conditions hold precisely when
α=aΩ.​
(469)
This is the Stokes flow between touching counter-rotating cylinders.
