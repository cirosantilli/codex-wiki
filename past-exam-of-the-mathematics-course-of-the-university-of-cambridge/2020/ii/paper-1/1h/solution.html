An integral positive definite binary quadratic form
[a,b,c](x,y)=ax2+bxy+cy2
(1)
is reduced when
∣b∣≤a≤c,
(2)
with b≥0 when ∣b∣=a or a=c. Its discriminant is D=b2−4ac.
For D=−20, reduction gives 3a2≤20, so a=1 or 2. Since b2≡D(mod4), b is even. If a=1, then ∣b∣≤1 forces b=0, and c=5. If a=2, then b∈{−2,0,2}; integrality of
c=8b2+20​
(3)
leaves b=±2 and c=3, and the boundary convention retains only b=2. Hence the complete list is
[1,0,5]and[2,2,3]​.
(4)
Now suppose the prime p=5 is represented by x2+5y2. If p=2, the equation is impossible modulo four, so p is odd. The integers x and y then have opposite parity, and therefore
p=x2+5y2≡1(mod4).
(5)
Also x≡0(mod5), since otherwise 5∣p; hence
p≡x2≡1 or 4(mod5).
(6)
The Chinese remainder theorem gives the stronger conclusion
p≡1 or 9(mod20).
(7)
In particular,
p≡1,3,7, or 9(mod20)​,
(8)
as required.
