(344)
Its sample expected value is Rn​(F).
Because every f∈F takes values in {0,1}, replacing one sample point changes R by at most 1/n. Put
s=2nlog(3/δ)​​.
(345)
The lower-tail form of the Bounded differences inequality gives
Rn​(F)≤R(F(Z1:n​))+s
(346)
outside an event of probability at most δ/3.
For the excess-loss supremum G from part (b), replacing one observation changes G by at most 2/n. A second application of the same inequality gives
G≤EG+n2log(3/δ)​​=EG+2s

outside another event of probability at most δ/3. On the intersection of these two events, symmetrization and the union bound yield
