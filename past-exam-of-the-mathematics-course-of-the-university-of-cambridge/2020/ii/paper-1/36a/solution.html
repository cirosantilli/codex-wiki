Let two systems exchange energy inside an otherwise isolated composite system, so E1​+E2​=E is fixed. By the Second law of thermodynamics, equilibrium maximizes the total entropy
Stot​(E1​)=S1​(E1​)+S2​(E−E1​).
(407)
At an interior maximum,
0=dE1​dStot​​=∂E1​∂S1​​−∂E2​∂S2​​=T1​1​−T2​1​.
(408)
Thus T1​=T2​, which is entropy maximization under thermal contact and the condition for thermal equilibrium.
Stability requires this entropy maximum to be locally strict, so each ordinary subsystem has a concave entropy-energy relation. Since
∂E2∂2S​=∂E∂​(T1​)=−T21​∂E∂T​<0,
(409)
one has ∂T/∂E>0, or equivalently
∂T∂E​>0.​
(410)
This is the entropy concavity and positive heat capacity criterion.
Now let N↑​=αN and N↓​=(1−α)N. Because the spins are distinguishable, the number of microstates is the binomial coefficient
Ω=(αNN​)=(αN)!((1−α)N)!N!​.
(411)
The Boltzmann entropy S=kB​logΩ and the Stirling formula give, to leading order for large N,
S(α)=−NkB​[αlogα+(1−α)log(1−α)].​
(412)

E(α)=N(αϵ−(1−α)ϵ)=Nϵ(2α−1).​
(413)
Therefore
T1​=dE/dαdS/dα​=2ϵkB​​log(α1−α​).
(414)

0≤α<21​,​
