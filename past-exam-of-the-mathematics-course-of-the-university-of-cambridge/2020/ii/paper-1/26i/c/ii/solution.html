Consider the smooth map
Ψ:M4​(R)→Sym4​(R),Ψ(A)=ATMA.
(268)
Its derivative is
DΨA​(H)=HTMA+ATMH.
(269)
At A∈O(1,3) write H=AY. Then
DΨA​(AY)=YTM+MY.
(270)
This derivative is onto the ten-dimensional space of symmetric matrices: for a symmetric S, taking Y=21​MS gives YTM+MY=S. Hence M is a regular value. Since M4​(R) has dimension 16, the regular level set theorem gives
dimO(1,3)=16−10=6​.
(271)
The tangent vectors are exactly those for which the derivative vanishes:
TA​O(1,3)={AY:Y∈S},S={Y:YTM+MY=0}.​
(272)
Writing Y in one-plus-three block form shows explicitly that
S={(0v​vTΩ​):v∈R3, ΩT=−Ω}.
(273)
The three entries of v and three independent entries of the skew-symmetric matrix Ω again display dimension six. This is the Lie algebra of the Lorentz group.
