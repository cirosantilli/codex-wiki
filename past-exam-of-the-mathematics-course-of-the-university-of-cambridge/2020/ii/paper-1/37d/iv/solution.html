Because u0=c,dt/dτ=γc is constant, dt/dτ=γ. The coordinate speed along the circle is consequently
v=γR∣ω∣​,
(432)
so
R(v)=∣ω∣γ(v)v​=∣q∣Bγ(v)mv​.​
(433)
For the convention in the question, take qB>0, so ω>0 and Δτ=2π/ω.
On the circular solution, the free part of the relativistic charged-particle action is
Sfree​=−mc2Δτ=−ω2πmc2​.
(434)
In the gauge Ay​=Bx, the interaction is the closed line integral
Sint​=q∮Aμ​dxμ=qB∮xdy.
(435)
The orbit is clockwise for qB>0, so ∮xdy=−πR2. Thus
Sint​=−πqBR2=−ωπmγ2v2​.
(436)
Combining the two terms and using γ2v2=c2(γ2−1) gives the charged-particle action over one relativistic cyclotron orbit
S(v)=−ωπm​(2c2+γ(v)2v2)=−ωπmc2​(1+γ(v)2).​
(437)
Although the intermediate magnetic line integral depends on the chosen magnetic vector potential, the closed-orbit result is gauge invariant.
