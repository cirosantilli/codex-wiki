Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2020 / ii / Paper 1 / 17G / b / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2020 ii Paper 1 17G b
OurBigBook.com Words: 154
The sum of the degrees of all faces is 2e. Each of the t triangular faces contributes three. Because the graph is bridgeless and has no four-cycle, every other face has degree at least five, and therefore
2e≥3t+5(f−t)​.
(152)
No edge can border two triangular faces: two distinct triangles sharing that edge would have their other two edges form a four-cycle, while the same triangle on both sides would force the connected bridgeless graph to be that triangle, contrary to n≥4. Thus the 3t edge incidences belonging to triangular faces use distinct edges, so
e≥3t​.
(153)
Combining the inequalities gives
2e≥5f−2t≥5f−32e​,
(154)
and hence
f≤158e​​.
(155)
The Euler formula for a connected planar graph now yields
2=n−e+f≤n−e+158e​=n−157e​,
(156)
so the triangle-pentagon planar edge bound is
e≤715(n−2)​​.
(157)
Equality is possible. Cut each corner of a dodecahedron through the midpoints of its incident edges. The resulting icosidodecahedral graph has
n=30,e=60,t=20,f−t=12,
(158)
with every edge incident to one triangular and one pentagonal face. It has no four-cycle, and
60=715(30−2)​.
(159)
