The transformations
gs:(X,T,u)⟼(esX,e−sT,u)
(224)
form a one-parameter group with infinitesimal generator
V=X∂X​−T∂T​.
(225)
Under this scaling, ∂X​ has weight −1 and ∂T​ has weight 1, so uXT​ has weight zero, as does sinu. Thus uXT​=sinu is invariant and gs is a Scaling symmetry of the sine-Gordon equation.
The product
z=XT
(226)
is invariant under the group. A group-invariant solution therefore has the form u(X,T)=F(z). Since
uX​=TF′(z),uXT​=F′(z)+zF′′(z),
(227)
the Sine-Gordon equation reduces to
zF′′+F′=sinF.
(228)
Now put w=eiF. Then
F′=−iww′​,F′′=−i(ww′′​−w2(w′)2​),sinF=2iw−w−1​.
(229)
Substitution and multiplication by iw give
zw′′−zw(w′)2​+w′=2w2−1​.
(230)
Hence
w′′=w(w′)2​−zw′​+2zw2−1​​.
(231)
This is the Painlevé III equation with parameters (α,β,γ,δ)=(1/2,−1/2,0,0).
