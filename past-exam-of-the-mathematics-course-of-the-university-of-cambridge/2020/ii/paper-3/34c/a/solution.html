For a real short-range spherically symmetric potential, the stationary wavefunction must solve the Schrödinger equation, be regular at the origin whenever the potential is finite there, and satisfy the scattering boundary condition
ψ(r)∼eikz+f(θ)reikr​(r→∞).
(242)
Here eikz is the incident plane wave, the second term is an outgoing spherical wave, and its coefficient f(θ) is the scattering amplitude.
Comparing the stated asymptotic expression with the partial-wave expansion of a scattering amplitude gives
f(θ)=k1​∑l=0∞​(2l+1)fl​Pl​(cosθ),
(243)
where Pl​ is a Legendre polynomial. The outgoing-to-incoming coefficient in channel l is
Sl​=1+2ifl​.
(244)
Partial-wave unitarity requires
∣Sl​∣=1,
(245)
or equivalently
Imfl​=∣fl​∣2.
(246)
The real scattering phase shift δl​ is defined modulo π by
Sl​=e2iδl​,fl​=2ie2iδl​−1​=eiδl​sinδl​.​
(247)
