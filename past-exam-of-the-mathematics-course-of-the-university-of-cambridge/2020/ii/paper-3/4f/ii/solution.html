The language L={am2bm2:m≥0} is not context-free. Suppose it had pumping length p in the pumping lemma for context-free languages, and apply the lemma to w=ap2bp2. Write w=uvxyz with ∣vxy∣≤p, ∣vy∣>0, and uvixyiz∈L for every i≥0.
Let α and β be the respective numbers of as and bs in vy. If α=β, pumping with i=0 or i=2 destroys equality of the two block lengths. Hence a valid decomposition would require α=β=d>0. Pumping with i=2 would then produce ap2+dbp2+d, where 1≤d≤p. But
p2<p2+d<(p+1)2,
(10)
so p2+d is not a square number. This contradiction proves the square-count language is not context-free.
