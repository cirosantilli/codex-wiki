Gauss lemma states that if p∤a and m of the least positive residues of
a,2a,…,2p−1​a
(44)
exceed p/2, then (a/p)=(−1)m.
To prove it, replace each residue exceeding p/2 by its negative, obtaining numbers r1​,…,r(p−1)/2​ in {1,…,(p−1)/2}. No two of them are equal modulo p: such an equality would give ia≡±ja(modp), hence i=j in the allowed range. They therefore permute 1,…,(p−1)/2, and multiplication gives
a(p−1)/2(2p−1​)!≡(−1)m(2p−1​)!(modp).
(45)
Cancel the nonzero factorial and apply Euler's criterion to obtain the lemma.
For a=−1, all (p−1)/2 least positive residues p−1,p−2,…,(p+1)/2 exceed p/2. Hence the first supplementary law for quadratic reciprocity is
(p−1​)=(−1)(p−1)/2.​
