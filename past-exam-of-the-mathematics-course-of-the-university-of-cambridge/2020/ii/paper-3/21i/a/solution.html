For a bounded linear operator T:H→H, the spectrum is
σ(T)={λ∈C:T−λI has no bounded inverse},
(104)
while the point spectrum is the set of eigenvalues, equivalently the λ for which T−λI is not injective.
A compact operator maps the unit ball to a relatively compact set. A self-adjoint operator satisfies T=T∗, or ⟨Tx,y⟩=⟨x,Ty⟩ for all x,y∈H. A finite-rank operator has finite-dimensional image.
