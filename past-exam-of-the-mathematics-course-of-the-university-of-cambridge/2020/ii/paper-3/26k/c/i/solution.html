For a Borel set A⊆[0,1], the two inverse branches of the tent map are y↦y/2 and y↦1−y/2. Apart from their immaterial common endpoint,
T−1A=2A​ ∪ (1−2A​).
(159)
Each branch scales Lebesgue measure by 1/2, and their images lie in opposite half-intervals. Hence
m(T−1A)=21​m(A)+21​m(A)=m(A).
(160)
Also m([0,1])=1, so m is an invariant probability measure.
