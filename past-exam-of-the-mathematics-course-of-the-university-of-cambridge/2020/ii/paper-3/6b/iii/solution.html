trJ∗​=−(α+βI∗)<0,detJ∗​=βI∗(α+γ)>0.

The trace-determinant stability criterion shows that both eigenvalues have negative real part. The Endemic equilibrium of the SIR model with waning immunity is therefore locally asymptotically stable.
