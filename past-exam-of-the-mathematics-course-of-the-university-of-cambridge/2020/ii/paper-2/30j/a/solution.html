A sample x1:n​=(x1​,…,xn​) is shattered by F when every binary labeling is realized:
{(f(x1​),…,f(xn​)):f∈F}={0,1}n.
(218)
The shattering coefficient and VC dimension are
s(F,n)=supx1:n​∈Xn​∣{(f(x1​),…,f(xn​)):f∈F}∣,
(219)
VC(F)=sup{n:s(F,n)=2n}.
(220)
For the lower orthants in Rd, the d points e1​,…,ed​ are shattered: to realize a subset I, choose aj​=1 for j∈I and aj​=1/2 otherwise. Conversely, among any d+1 points choose, for each coordinate, one point attaining the maximum in that coordinate. At most d points have been chosen, so there is an unchosen point x. Any lower orthant containing every chosen coordinate maximizer must contain every sample point, including x. It cannot realize the labeling that includes all chosen points and excludes x. This proves
VC(F)=d​.
(221)
