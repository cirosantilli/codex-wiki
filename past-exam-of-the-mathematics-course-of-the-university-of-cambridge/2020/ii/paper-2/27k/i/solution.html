Write qi​=−gii​ for the total rate of leaving i. The jump chain of a continuous-time Markov chain X is the discrete-time chain Y=(Yn​) recording the states occupied after successive jumps. Its transition probabilities are
pij​=qi​gij​​(j=i),pii​=0,
(195)
whenever qi​>0.
The chain X is irreducible if every state can be reached from every other state with positive probability. A state i is recurrent if, after leaving i, the process returns to i with probability one; an irreducible chain is recurrent when one, and hence every, state is recurrent.
The continuous-time Markov chain and its jump chain have exactly the same successive states. If Y is transient, it visits each state only finitely often, so X is transient. If Y is recurrent, it visits a fixed state i infinitely often. At those visits, X has independent exponential holding times of rate qi​<∞. Their sum diverges almost surely, so explosion cannot occur before all those returns. Hence X returns to i infinitely often and is recurrent. Thus, for an irreducible chain,
X is recurrent⟺Y is recurrent.​
