For an iterate xm​, the Conjugate gradient method residual is
rm​=b−Axm​​.
(364)
Two fundamental properties are the Conjugate-gradient residual orthogonality conditions
rm​⊥Km​(A,r0​),rm​∈Km+1​(A,r0​).
(365)
Equivalently, the residuals generated before convergence are mutually orthogonal and span the successive Krylov spaces.
Since A has only s distinct eigenvalues, its minimal polynomial has degree at most s. Consequently
Ks+1​(A,r0​)=Ks​(A,r0​).
(366)
The two properties then put rs​ both inside and orthogonal to Ks​(A,r0​), so rs​=0. Hence Axs​=b, and
CG terminates after at most s iterations​.
(367)
