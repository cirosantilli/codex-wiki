Two square matrices are similar when B=P−1AP for some invertible matrix P. A Jordan normal form is a block-diagonal matrix whose blocks have one eigenvalue on the diagonal, ones on the superdiagonal, and zeros elsewhere; over an algebraically closed field every matrix is similar to such a form, unique up to reordering its Jordan blocks.

χA​(t)=χB​(t)=(t−1)3.

rank(A−I)=1,rank(B−I)=2,

A and B are not similar​.

An operator Q is Hermitian when
⟨ϕ,Qψ⟩=⟨Qϕ,ψ⟩
(10)
on its domain. Hermitian operators represent quantum observables because their expectation values and eigenvalues are real. In a normalized state,
(ΔQ)ψ​=⟨(Q−⟨Q⟩ψ​)2⟩ψ1/2​.

A statistic T is sufficient for θ if the conditional distribution of the full sample given T does not depend on θ. It is minimal sufficient if it is a function of every sufficient statistic.
The likelihood factors as
