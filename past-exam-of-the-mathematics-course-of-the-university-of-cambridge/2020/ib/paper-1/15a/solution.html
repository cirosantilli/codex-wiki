A stationary state has the form
Ψ(t)=e−iEt/ℏψ.
(85)
For a time-independent quantum observable Q, the global phases cancel in the expectation value:
⟨Q⟩Ψ(t)​=⟨e−iEt/ℏψ,Qe−iEt/ℏψ⟩=⟨ψ,Qψ⟩.
(86)
It is therefore independent of time.
For the infinite square well,
En​=2ma2n2π2ℏ2​.
(87)
The possible energy measurement results are
E1​=2ma2π2ℏ2​,E2​=ma22π2ℏ2​​,
(88)
with respective Born rule probabilities ∣c1​∣2 and ∣c2​∣2. Normalization gives ∣c1​∣2+∣c2​∣2=1.
At time t,
Ψ(x,t)=c1​e−iE1​t/ℏψ1​(x)+c2​e−iE2​t/ℏψ2​(x).
(89)
The required matrix elements are
⟨ψ1​,x^ψ1​⟩=⟨ψ2​,x^ψ2​⟩=2a​,⟨ψ1​,x^ψ2​⟩=−9π216a​.
(90)
Consequently
⟨x^⟩Ψ(t)​=2a​−9π232a​Re(c1∗​c2​e−iωt)​,
(91)
where the angular frequency is
ω=ℏE2​−E1​​=2ma23π2ℏ​​.
(92)
Finally, 2∣c1​c2​∣≤∣c1​∣2+∣c2​∣2=1, so
​⟨x^⟩Ψ(t)​−2a​​≤9π216a​​.
(93)
