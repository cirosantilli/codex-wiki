In polar coordinates,
vdt=ds=dr2+r2dθ2​.
(70)
With the change of radial coordinate u=logr, the detection functional becomes
P=λ∫rds​=λ∫du2+dθ2​.
(71)
It is therefore λ times ordinary path length in the (u,θ)-plane. The shortest path is the line segment from (logA,0) to (logB,α), so
u(θ)=logA+αlog(B/A)​θ.
(72)
Returning to r gives the logarithmic spiral
r(θ)=Aexp(αlog(B/A)​θ)​.
(73)
Its minimum detection probability is
Pmin​=λlog2(B/A)+α2​.
(74)
The functional depends only on the geometric path and not its parametrization, so tiptoeing and running give the same probability.
For the improved sensor, omit the irrelevant positive factor λ and use the Lagrangian
L=rr˙2​+rθ˙2.
(75)
The coordinate θ is cyclic, so its conjugate momentum is conserved. Equivalently,
h=rθ˙​
(76)
is constant. Because L has no explicit time dependence, the associated conservation of energy gives the constant
E=rr˙2​+rθ˙2​.
(77)
Multiplying by r and using h=rθ˙ gives the required equation
r˙2=Er−h2​.
(78)
