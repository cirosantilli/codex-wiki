Let the lattice spacing be d and write the central dipole as

Its two horizontal neighbours have moment p(cosθ,−sinθ). For either one, the expression in parentheses, after extracting d−3, is
−p2(1+cos2θ).
(100)
Its two vertical neighbours have moment p(−cosθ,sinθ), and each contributes instead
−p2(1+sin2θ).
(101)

(102)
The angle has cancelled, so the energy is independent of θ.
