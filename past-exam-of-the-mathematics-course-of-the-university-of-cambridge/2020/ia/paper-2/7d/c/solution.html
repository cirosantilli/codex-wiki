In the RSA cryptosystem, choose distinct large primes p,q, put N=pq, and choose e coprime to
ϕ(N)=(p−1)(q−1).
(36)
The decryption exponent is the modular multiplicative inverse
d≡e−1(modϕ(N)).
(37)
Thus ed=1+kϕ(N). For a message with gcd(m,N)=1, the Fermat-Euler theorem gives
cd≡med=m(mϕ(N))k≡m(modN).
(38)
For N=187=11⋅17, ϕ(N)=160. The extended Euclidean algorithm gives
1=37⋅13−3⋅160,
(39)
so
d=37​.
