Set p=t−λr using part (a). At every node,
f(yj​)−p(yj​)=λ(−1)j,∥f−p∥L∞(Y)​=∣λ∣.
(50)
If λ=0, the error vanishes. Otherwise any degree-at-most-n polynomial q with strictly smaller error would make q−p have alternating nonzero signs at the increasingly ordered nodes (reversed globally if λ<0). It would then have at least n+1 distinct roots by the intermediate value theorem, impossible. Hence
p=t−λr minimizes ∥f−q∥L∞(Y)​ over degq≤n.​
