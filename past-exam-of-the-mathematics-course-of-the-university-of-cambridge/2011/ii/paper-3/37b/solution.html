Write D=∂n​An​ and use repeated-index summation. Because every Ai​ is a harmonic function,
∂i​ui​=D−∂k​Ak​−xk​∇2Ak​=0,
(195)
and
∇2ui​=−2∂k​∂i​Ak​=−2∂i​D,∂i​p=−2μ∂i​D.
(196)
Thus μ∇2ui​−∂i​p=0, the incompressible Stokes equation. Moreover
∂j​ui​=∂j​Ai​−∂i​Aj​−xk​∂i​∂j​Ak​.
(197)
The antisymmetric first two terms cancel in the sum with ∂i​uj​, giving the Newtonian fluid Cauchy stress tensor
σij​=−pδij​+μ(∂j​ui​+∂i​uj​)=2μ(δij​D−xk​∂i​∂j​Ak​).​
(198)
For Ai​=Vi​(1−a/(2r)), harmonic outside the origin,
∂i​Ak​=2r3aVk​xi​​,ui​=Vi​(1−2ra​)−2r3axi​(V⋅x)​,p=−r3μa(V⋅x)​.
(199)
At r=a this gives u=21​[V−(V⋅n)n], where n=x/a, so the normal velocity is zero. Also
∂i​∂j​Ak​=2aVk​​(r3δij​​−r53xi​xj​​),σij​=r53μa(V⋅x)xi​xj​​.
(200)
Hence on the surface,
σij​nj​=a3μ​(V⋅n)ni​,​
(201)
a purely normal traction, appropriate to a shear-free sphere rather than a no-slip solid sphere. Finally rotational symmetry gives ∫S2​ni​nj​dΩ=(4π/3)δij​, so the fluid's force on the sphere is
F=∫r=a​σndS=4πμaV.​
(202)
This is the clean-bubble Stokes drag in the far-field velocity V.
