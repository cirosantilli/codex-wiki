Identify the Möbius group with PSL2​(C), the orientation-preserving isometry group of three-dimensional hyperbolic space. Here a properly discontinuous group action permits finite stabilizers: for each compact set K, only finitely many g satisfy gK∩K=∅.
Fix o∈H3. The set of isometries with d(o,go)≤R is compact. To see this, an orientation-preserving isometry is uniquely determined by the image of an oriented orthonormal frame at o. The image point lies in a compact closed hyperbolic ball and its frame lies in a compact rotation-group fiber. These choices parametrize the indicated set of isometries continuously.
A discrete subgroup of a topological group is closed: otherwise distinct subgroup elements accumulating at the same point would have quotients tending to the identity, contradicting isolation of the identity. Its intersection with a compact set is therefore finite. If K⊆B(o,R) and gK∩K=∅, choosing x,gx∈K gives d(o,go)≤d(o,gx)+d(gx,go)≤2R. Hence discreteness implies a properly discontinuous group action. Conversely, if the subgroup is not discrete, distinct gn​ tend to the identity. For a compact ball K with o in its interior, eventually gn​o∈K, so infinitely many gn​K meet K, contradicting proper discontinuity. This proves the equivalence.
Matrices of determinant one over the Gaussian integers are closed under multiplication and inversion, with inverse (d−c​−ba​). Quotienting by {±I} gives the specified group of Möbius transformations. The Gaussian integers form a discrete set in the complex plane, so these matrices form a discrete subgroup of SL2​(C), and the finite quotient remains discrete. Thus this group acts properly discontinuously on H3.
