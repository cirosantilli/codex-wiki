Suppose Xn​→X in probability. For a fixed real t and any δ>0, split according to ∣Xn​−X∣≤δ. The supplied exponential inequality and the bound ∣eitXn​−eitX∣≤2 give
∣φXn​​(t)−φX​(t)∣≤E∣eitXn​−eitX∣≤∣t∣δ+2P(∣Xn​−X∣>δ).
(67)
Let n→∞, then δ↓0. Thus the characteristic functions converge pointwise to φX​. The Lévy continuity theorem states that pointwise convergence of characteristic functions to a characteristic function continuous at zero implies convergence in distribution. Every characteristic function is continuous at zero, so
Xn​P​X ⟹ Xn​⇒X.​
(68)
