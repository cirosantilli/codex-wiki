A Hamiltonian system on a 2d-dimensional symplectic manifold is Liouville integrable when it has d first integrals, including the Hamiltonian, whose differentials are independent on a dense regular region and which pairwise have zero Poisson bracket. The Arnold-Liouville theorem states that a compact connected regular common level set is a d-torus; near it there are action-angle variables (I,θ) in which the Hamiltonian depends only on I and the motion is I˙=0, θ˙=∇I​H. Without compactness, the torus conclusion needs modification.
Here q1​ is a cyclic coordinate, so p1​=ℓ is conserved and {H,p1​}=0. The Hamilton equations are
q˙​1​=q22​p1​​,q˙​2​=p2​,p˙​1​=0,p˙​2​=q23​p12​​−q22​k​.
(101)
The differentials dH,dp1​ are independent away from the circular-orbit critical set, establishing integrability. Put r=q2​; on a fixed angular-momentum level,
p22​=2E+r2k​−r2ℓ2​.
(102)
For a bounded noncollision orbit require ℓ=0 and −k2/(2ℓ2)<E<0. Its two turning radii are
α=−2Ek−k2+2Eℓ2​​,β=−2Ek+k2+2Eℓ2​​.​
(103)
They are positive and α<β, and p22​=(−2E)(r−α)(β−r)/r2. In terms of u=1/r and the angular coordinate, eliminating time gives u′′+u=k/ℓ2. Thus
r=k[1+ecos(q1​−q∗​)]ℓ2​,e2=1+k22Eℓ2​<1.
(104)
After an angular change of 2π the phase-space state returns to itself, and the elapsed time is finite because r is bounded away from zero and infinity. This proves periodicity; a torus alone would only guarantee quasiperiodic motion. At E=−k2/(2ℓ2) the orbit is circular and remains periodic. For E≥0 the allowed radial motion is unbounded. The collision case ℓ=0 reaches the excluded boundary r=0 and is not a periodic orbit in the given phase space.
With the usual action convention (2π)−1∮pdq, the angular cycle gives I1​=ℓ, and the two radial legs give
I1​=p1​,I2​=γ∫αβ​r(r−α)(β−r)​​dr,γ=π−2E​​.​
(105)
For completeness, substitute r=(α+β)/2+(β−α)cosθ/2 to evaluate the integral as π[(α+β)/2−αβ​]. Therefore I2​=k/−2E​−∣ℓ∣ and H=−k2/[2(I2​+∣I1​∣)2]. The two frequencies have equal magnitude, consistent with the closed Kepler orbits. The circular limit has I2​=0 and α=β.
