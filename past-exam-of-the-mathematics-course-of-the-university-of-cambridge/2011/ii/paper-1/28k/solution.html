For a decision rule δ and loss L, its risk function is R(θ,δ)=Eθ​L(θ,δ(X)). It is an admissible estimator if there is no rule with risk everywhere no larger and somewhere strictly smaller. A Bayes decision rule minimizes ∫R(θ,δ)π(dθ) for a specified prior π. A minimax decision rule minimizes supθ​R(θ,δ) over all rules.
