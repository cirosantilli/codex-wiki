A path that reaches level c from below does so while in state +1, because Z is continuous and has slope X. At the first crossing time, the residual exponential holding time is memoryless and the Strong Markov property restarts the chain from +1. Conditional on ever reaching c, the probability of subsequently exceeding c+d is therefore exactly the original probability of exceeding d. Hence
ψ+​(c+d)=ψ+​(c)ψ+​(d),ψ+​(0+)=1.
(79)
The zero-level limit is one because the initial +1 holding time is positive almost surely. Also 0<ψ+​(c)≤1, and it is nonincreasing. Consequently h(c)=−logψ+​(c) is nondecreasing and additive. First h is linear on positive rationals, then monotonicity gives the same result on all positive reals. Thus
ψ+​(c)=e−Ac for some A≥0.​
(80)
Absorption can be viewed as killing at rate λ, independent of sign changes at rate q; this ensures the maximum is finite almost surely and makes the positive decay rate found in (iv) natural.
