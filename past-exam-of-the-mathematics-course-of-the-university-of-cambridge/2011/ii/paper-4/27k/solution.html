A random row vector has a multivariate normal distribution when every real linear combination of its coordinates is normally distributed, allowing a zero-variance constant. Its law is determined by its mean and covariance. For the conditional and testing formulas assume Σ is positive definite, so both coordinate variances and the conditional variance are positive; the given ratio already requires σXX​>0.
Put Z=Y−βX, with β=σXY​/σXX​. The pair (X,Z) is jointly normal and Cov(X,Z)=σXY​−βσXX​=0, so Z is independent of X. Its mean is μY​−βμX​, and its variance is σYY​−σXY2​/σXX​. Hence
