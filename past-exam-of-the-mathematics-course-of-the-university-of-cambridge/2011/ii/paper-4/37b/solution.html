In lubrication theory, the gap scale H is small compared with the axial scale L, so δ=H/L≪1 and wall slopes are small. Incompressibility makes the transverse velocity scale δU. The reduced Reynolds number is ρUH2/(μL)=δReH​, and must be small to neglect convection; the transverse viscous diffusion time must also be short compared with the wall-motion time to neglect unsteady inertia.
