Factorization over K gives the orbits of the Galois group on the three resolvent roots. If Q splits completely, the quotient is trivial and G=V4​. If Q is a linear factor times an irreducible quadratic, the quotient is C2​ and G=D8​.
If Q is irreducible, the quotient acts transitively on three roots, so it is C3​ or S3​. The product of their pairwise differences is a square root of the nonzero discriminant Δ and changes by the sign of the permutation. Thus Δ is a square in K exactly when the quotient contains only even permutations. The criterion is
