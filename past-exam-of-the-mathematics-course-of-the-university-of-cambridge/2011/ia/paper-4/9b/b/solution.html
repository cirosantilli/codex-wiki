For ψ=μ/r, the radial gradient gives
B=−∇ψ=r3μ​r.

The force −∇ϕ is radial and has no torque about the origin. For the remaining force, use the vector triple product:
dtd​(mr×r˙)=r×F=r3μ​r×(r˙×r)=rμ​(r˙−r˙r).
(47)
Meanwhile the radial unit vector obeys
dtd​r=rr˙−r˙r​.
(48)
Since ψr=μr, these derivatives cancel. Thus the generalized angular momentum in a radial monopole field is
L=mr×r˙−μr,L˙=0.​
(49)
The argument applies away from r=0, where the field is defined, for any radial ϕ. In contrast to ordinary angular momentum, this conserved vector has L⋅r=−μ; it fixes a cone rather than generally fixing a plane.
