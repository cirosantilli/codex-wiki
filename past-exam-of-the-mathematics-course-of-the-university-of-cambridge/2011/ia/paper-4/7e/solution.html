Define the binomial coefficient (in​) to be the number of i-element subsets of an n-element set. Counting ordered selections and dividing by the i! orders of each subset gives
(in​)=i!(n−i)!n!​,0≤i≤n.

Every subset has exactly one size, while each of the n elements can independently be included or omitted. Hence the power set has 2n members and

(1+x)n=∑i=0n​(in​)xi.
