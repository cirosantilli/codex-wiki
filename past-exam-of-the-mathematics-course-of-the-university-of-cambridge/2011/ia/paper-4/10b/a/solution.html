At P, use the local right-handed basis eE​,eN​,eU​ pointing east, north and vertically outward. The Earth's rotation vector has components
ω=ω(sinθeN​+cosθeU​),r=ReU​.
(56)
Initially r˙=0 in the rotating reference frame, so the Coriolis acceleration is zero. Evaluating the centrifugal acceleration gives
−ω×(ω×r)=ω2R(sin2θeU​−sinθcosθeN​).
(57)
The gravitational acceleration is −geU​. Hence
r¨(0)=−(g−ω2Rsin2θ)eU​−ω2RsinθcosθeN​.
(58)
The small angle δ from the downward vertical therefore satisfies
tanδ=g−ω2Rsin2θω2R∣sinθcosθ∣​,δ≃gω2R​∣sinθcosθ∣.​
(59)
For the northern hemisphere, 0≤θ≤π/2, this is the printed expression without absolute values. More generally, the printed signed meridional expression specifies the direction of the tilt; an unsigned angle uses its absolute value. The horizontal component is toward the equator and vanishes at both poles and at the equator.
