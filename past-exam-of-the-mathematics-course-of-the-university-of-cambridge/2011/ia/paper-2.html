An equilibrium of an autonomous differential equation is a constant y∗​ with f(y∗​)=0. Put y=y∗​+η. For differentiable f, the linear stability analysis gives
η′=f′(y∗​)η+o(η).
(4)

Pairwise independent events satisfy P(Ai​∩Aj​)=P(Ai​)P(Aj​) for every distinct pair. Independent events satisfy
P(⋂i∈I​Ai​)=∏i∈I​P(Ai​)
(14)
for every nonempty subset I of the indices. For three events this includes the triple intersection, not just the three pairwise intersections.
Write Ai​=Bi​∪C. Disjointness gives P(Ai​)=p+q, while every intersection of two or three distinct Ai​ is exactly C. Thus pairwise independence is equivalent to q=(p+q)2. Since p+q≥0,
p=q​−q.​
(15)
For 0≤q≤1/16, this is an admissible nonnegative p: putting u=q​∈[0,1/4] gives 3p+q=3u−2u2≤5/8<1. The four disjoint events can therefore have the stated probabilities, with the remaining probability assigned to their complement. This also covers q=0.
Full independence would additionally require q=(p+q)3. Together with q=(p+q)2, this forces p+q to be zero or one. The first case gives p=q=0; the second gives q=1,p=0. Neither allows positive p,q. There are no such mutually independent events with p>0 and q>0.
