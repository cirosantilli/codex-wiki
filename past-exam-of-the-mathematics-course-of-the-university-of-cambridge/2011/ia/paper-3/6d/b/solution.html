The general linear group GL2​(F5​) consists of invertible two-by-two matrices over the finite field F5​, under matrix multiplication. Such a matrix has two linearly independent columns. There are 52−1=24 choices for a nonzero first column, and 52−5=20 choices for a second column outside its one-dimensional span. Thus
∣GL2​(F5​)∣=24⋅20=480.​
(32)
The determinant is a group homomorphism
det:GL2​(F5​)⟶F5×​,
(33)
where F5×​={1,2,3,4} is the multiplicative group of nonzero field elements. It is surjective because diag(t,1) has determinant t. Its kernel of a group homomorphism is SL2​(F5​). The first isomorphism theorem gives a quotient of order four, whence
∣SL2​(F5​)∣=480/4=120.​
(34)
The nonzero squares form the subgroup {1,4} of F5×​. Its inverse image
H={M∈GL2​(F5​):detM∈{1,4}}​
(35)
is a subgroup with index two. Indeed, composing the determinant with the quotient map to F5×​/{1,4} makes H the kernel of a surjective group homomorphism to a group of order two. Thus ∣H∣=240.
