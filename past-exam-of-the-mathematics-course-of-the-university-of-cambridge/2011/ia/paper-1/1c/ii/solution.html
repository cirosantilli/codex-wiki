z=eπ/2+2πk,k∈Z.​
(5)
On the negative real axis, both z and z have principal complex argument π, so the left-hand side has modulus 2e−π, not two. There are no additional solutions there. The printed second term is the principal power of z, not the complex conjugate of zi.
