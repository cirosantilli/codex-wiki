The cofactor is Cij​=(−1)i+jdetA(ij), where A(ij) deletes row i and column j. The adjugate is the transposed cofactor matrix, adj(A)ij​=Cji​.
The (i,j) entry of adj(A)A is ∑k​Cki​akj​. For j=i, expansion along column i gives detA. For j=i, this same sum is the determinant of the matrix obtained by replacing column i by column j: deleting the replaced column leaves all its cofactors unchanged. The new matrix has two equal columns, so its determinant is zero. Thus
adj(A)A=(detA)In​.​
(28)
This proof does not assume invertibility and therefore also covers singular matrices.
