OurBigBook.com Words: 162
Associate free real dual variables ui​ and vj​ with the row and column equality constraints. The Lagrangian for the transportation problem is
L(x,u,v)=∑i​ai​ui​+∑j​bj​vj​+∑i,j​(cij​−ui​−vj​)xij​.
(25)
Its infimum over xij​≥0 is finite exactly when ui​+vj​≤cij​ for every pair. The linear programming duality therefore gives
maximize i∑​ai​ui​+j∑​bj​vj​subject to ui​+vj​≤cij​,ui​,vj​∈R​.
(26)
The dual variables have no sign restrictions because the primal constraints are equalities.
A feasible shipment x is optimal if and only if there exist feasible transportation dual potentials satisfying complementary slackness:
xij​(cij​−ui​−vj​)=0for every i,j​.
(27)
Thus every occupied route must have ui​+vj​=cij​. To see sufficiency explicitly, the primal minus dual objective is
∑i,j​xij​(cij​−ui​−vj​)≥0,
(28)
and complementary slackness makes it zero. This is the weak duality certificate. Necessity follows from strong duality for the feasible, bounded linear program. If the total supply M is positive, xij​=ai​bj​/M gives feasibility; if M=0, x=0 does. The feasible shipments form a closed bounded set, so the primal optimum is attained. This also covers zero supplies and demands. The one redundant equality merely leaves the dual gauge freedom (u,v)↦(u+t,v−t).
