γ=1+2c2∣v∣2​+O(∣v∣4/c4).

Consequently p=mv+O(m∣v∣3/c2), recovering ordinary momentum. At rest the four-momentum has energy E0​=mc2, so subtracting this rest energy gives the kinetic energy
K=E−E0​=(γ−1)mc2=21​m∣v∣2+O(m∣v∣4/c2)​.
(13)
Thus the nonrelativistic limit preserves the rest-energy offset as well as the usual kinetic energy.
