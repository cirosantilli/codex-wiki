A linear operator between Banach spaces is a compact operator when it maps the unit ball into a relatively compact set, equivalently when every bounded sequence of inputs has an image subsequence converging in norm. Such an operator is bounded. For a bounded linear operator on a complex Banach space, the resolvent set consists of the complex λ for which T−λI is bijective with bounded inverse; its complement is the spectrum. The point spectrum consists of those λ for which T−λI has a nonzero kernel, namely the eigenvalues.
