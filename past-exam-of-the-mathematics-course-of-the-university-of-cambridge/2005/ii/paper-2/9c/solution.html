Continuity preserves its initial sign for sufficiently small perturbations. Thus nonzero steady rotation is stable exactly about the largest or smallest principal moment, the intermediate axis theorem. It is not asymptotically stable, since energy is conserved. If Ω=0, energy makes the zero angular-velocity state stable for every ordering; the nonzero-rotation assumption is essential to the stated equivalence.
