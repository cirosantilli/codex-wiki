The given Riccati ansatz is v′=−iv2+iz/6. Differentiating it and substituting for v′ shows
v′′=−2ivv′+i/6=−2v3+zv/3+i/6.
(109)
It therefore selects C=i/6 in the preceding scaled Painleve equation. Write v=−iΨ′/Ψ. The quadratic terms cancel in the ansatz, leaving
−i(ΨΨ′′​+6z​)=0,Ψ′′+6z​Ψ=0.
(110)
Thus every nonzero solution of the indicated Airy equation gives the local mKdV solution
q(x,t)=−it−1/3Ψ(xt−1/3)Ψ′(xt−1/3)​,t>0,​
(111)
on intervals where Ψ=0. For example Ψ(z)=AAi(−z/61/3)+BBi(−z/61/3), with constants not both zero, has the required equation. This branch is generally complex and meromorphic; the printed ansatz itself includes i. A real v on a real interval would require simultaneously v′=0 and v2=z/6, which is impossible on an interval. Hence this particular ansatz does not furnish a nontrivial real-valued mKdV profile.
