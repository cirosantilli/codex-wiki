Use the original PDF's value p​=0.386; the TeX aid misreads its last digit. In the fitted conditional mixture, θ=0.575 means that about 57.5% are assigned to the structural-zero, or modelled cured, component. Its asymptotic 95% confidence interval of (0.30,0.81) shows substantial uncertainty about that fraction. An observed post-treatment zero alone is not proof of cure, since the non-cured Poisson distribution can also produce zero.
The parameter p is a conditional allocation probability, not the ratio of post-treatment to pre-treatment means. That ratio among non-cured patients is β=p/(1−p). Hence
β​=1−0.3860.386​≃0.629,estimated mean-count reduction among non-cured patients≃37.1%.​
(52)
The map p↦p/(1−p) is strictly increasing, so transform the endpoints of the supplied 95% confidence interval:
β∈(0.730.27​,0.480.52​)≃(0.370,1.083).​
(53)
No change in the non-cured mean is β=1, equivalently p=1/2, which lies in this interval. Thus the point estimate suggests a reduction, but this interval does not firmly establish one among the non-cured patients at the corresponding two-sided 5% level. It is not correct to describe p=0.386 itself as a 61.4% reduction in their mean count.
Under a full pre-conditioning mixture with cure independent of the baseline rate, the population mean ratio would be (1−θ)β, whose plug-in value is about 0.425(0.629)=0.267. This combines disappearance of counts in one component with a change of rate in the other; it is distinct from the conditional estimate's direct interpretation. The two marginal confidence intervals cannot simply be multiplied to obtain a calibrated joint 95% interval for this derived quantity.
Only 12 patients were observed, so the asymptotic intervals, particularly for a mixture parameter near a boundary, should be interpreted cautiously. The data describe one-minute counts and do not establish permanent cure. Nor does an uncontrolled before-and-after comparison alone prove a causal effect of the drug: natural count variability, secular changes and regression to the mean remain possible. The structural-zero interpretation and the reduction among non-cured patients are conclusions within the specified statistical model.
