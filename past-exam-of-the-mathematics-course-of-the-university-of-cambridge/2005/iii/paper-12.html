Take c>0 and e>0, as required for the displayed sparse scaling and the later normalization. A strictly balanced graph satisfies e(F)/v(F)<e/v for every proper nonempty subgraph F⊂H. This forces H to be connected and to have no isolated vertices: otherwise a component of at least the average density, or deletion of the isolated vertices, violates strict balance. Copies here are subgraphs, not induced subgraphs.
For fixed r, the factorial moment Er​(XH​) counts ordered r-tuples of distinct copies all present in the binomial random graph. The contribution of vertex-disjoint copies is exactly
ar(n)rv​​pre⟶(ace​)r.
(7)
The factor a−r removes the multiple embeddings of each copy coming from its graph automorphisms. For an overlapping tuple, let U be its union. There are O(nv(U)) placements of each fixed union type, and the probability its edges are present is pe(U). When adding a copy meeting the earlier union in f vertices and g edges, the exponent v(U)−(v/e)e(U) changes by
−f+(v/e)g≤0.
(8)
It is strictly negative if this intersection is a proper nonempty subgraph, and zero only for an empty intersection or a whole copy. In particular the first vertex-overlapping copy has a proper nonempty intersection: the preceding copies are vertex-disjoint, and a connected copy contained in their union would have to equal one of them, contrary to distinctness. Its contribution is therefore strictly negative; later additions cannot undo that loss. Only finitely many union types occur for fixed r, so their total contribution is o(1). We have proved all the factorial moment limits, and the allowed factorial-moment criterion for Poisson convergence gives

The lower bound follows because every vertex chooses two distinct neighbours inside its component. Let Zt​ denote the generation-t type-count vector, and let pi(t)​=Pri​(∣Zt​∣>0) from a single type-i ancestor. Every type-j child independently has probability pj(t)​ of having descendants in generation t. For a Poisson random variable N with mean λ, EsN=exp[λ(s−1)]. Independence across child types therefore gives
p(0)=1,pi(t+1)​=1−∏j​exp(−λij​pj(t)​)=Ti​(p(t)),Ti​(x)=1−e−(Λx)i​.
(27)
The events of nonempty successive generations decrease. Each individual has finitely many children almost surely, so the total tree is infinite precisely when no generation is empty. Consequently p(t)↓p, the actual survival probability of a branching process. Continuity of T yields
pi​=1−exp(−j∑​λij​pj​).​
(28)
The map T is coordinatewise increasing on nonnegative vectors. Every nonnegative fixed point p′ automatically lies in [0,1]l, since its coordinates equal 1−e−z with finite z≥0. This argument applies without irreducibility assumptions to the multitype Poisson branching process.

There are genuine normalization inconsistencies in this PDF. In the standard linearized chord diagram model Gn(1)​, time n gives n edges and total vertex degree 2n, with self-loops counted twice. The paper calls 2n the number of edges. Its right-endpoint hint also uses i/(2n)​ rather than i/n​. These cannot both describe the standard model; part (c)'s exponent is consequently inconsistent as well. We give the definition, prove the alternative construction and its limits, and explicitly identify the corrected conclusion rather than infer an invalid constant from the printed hint.
Start from the empty graph at time zero, or from one vertex with one self-loop at time one. To obtain Gt​ from Gt−1​, add vertex t and an edge directed from t to It​, where
Pr(It​=i∣Gt−1​)=2t−1di​(t−1)​ (i<t),Pr(It​=t∣Gt−1​)=2t−11​.​
(33)
A self-choice creates a self-loop. The old vertex degrees sum to 2(t−1), so these probabilities sum to one. This is exact preferential attachment, including the outward half of the new edge as weight one for the new vertex; it is not a rule normalized by 2t or a process adding two edges at each step. The degree of a vertex here is its total incoming plus outgoing vertex degree. The construction in part (a) has exactly one chord per vertex and makes the edge-count discrepancy explicit.

Draw n independent pairs of independent uniform random variables on [0,1]. In each pair call the minimum L and maximum R, then relabel whole pairs so R1​<⋯<Rn​. Merge all endpoints in (Ri−1​,Ri​] into vertex i, with R0​=0, and join the blocks containing the two endpoints of each pair, orienting from the right endpoint to the left. Self-loops are retained. This gives the uniform linearized chord diagram representation.
Before ordering, a pair has joint density 2 on 0<L<R<1. Consequently its maximum has distribution Pr(R≤r)=r2 and density 2r; conditional on R=r, its minimum is uniform on [0,r]. Independence and relabelling give the equivalent precise description
R12​<⋯<Rn2​ are the order statistics of n independent U(0,1) variables;Li​∣(R1​,…,Rn​) independently U(0,Ri​).​
(34)
Every one of the (2n)! relative orderings of the independently drawn labelled endpoints has the same probability. After forgetting pair labels and within-pair labels, each matching of 2n ordered points is produced by 2nn! orderings. Thus its matching is uniform among the (2n−1)!! pairings.
To prove equivalence with the recursive graph law, remove the last endpoint and its mate from a uniform pairing. The remaining ordered points have a uniform (n−1)-pairing, and the mate's old rank is uniform among the 2n−1 possible ranks, independently of that smaller pairing. Conversely insert the mate into a uniformly chosen one of its 2n−1 gaps, and pair it with the new final endpoint. For each old vertex block, the number of gaps assigned to it is exactly the number of endpoints already in that block, hence its old total vertex degree. These are the gaps immediately before each endpoint of that block. The last gap, after the last old endpoint, makes both new endpoints belong to the new final block and produces a self-loop. Therefore the attachment probabilities are di​/(2n−1) for old vertices and 1/(2n−1) for the new one. Induction proves the continuous construction and the recursive LCD model have the same distribution. Inserting a left endpoint into an old block raises exactly that vertex's vertex degree without splitting any old block.
