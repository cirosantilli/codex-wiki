Use target signature (−,+,…,+) and a closed-string spatial coordinate 0≤σ≤2π. Write Y for the compact coordinate and XA for the noncompact coordinates. In conformal gauge, the wave equation factorizes, so every coordinate is a sum of a function of τ+σ and a function of τ−σ. The closed-string boundary conditions are
XA(τ,σ+2π)=XA(τ,σ),Y(τ,σ+2π)=Y(τ,σ)+2πwR,w∈Z.

Single-valued wavefunctions of the center coordinate y∼y+2πR have momentum n/R, with n∈Z. Equivalently, the compact chiral worldsheet zero modes are α′pL​(τ+σ)/2 and α′pR​(τ−σ)/2, where

To classify all massless states, positivity of the two compact terms implies N+N≤2. When the sum is two, masslessness forces n=w=0, and closed-string level matching then forces N=N=1. At a generic radius the massless spectrum is the neutral 576-state spectrum above; at exceptional radii one adds exactly the states just listed. The neutral ground state remains tachyonic.
The map

Both the mass formula and compact-circle closed-string level matching are therefore unchanged, with a one-to-one map of the complete oscillator basis. This proves T-duality of the full spectrum, including winding, massive and tachyonic states.
At the self-dual circle the four single-oscillator charge families give 4⋅24=96 new massless states. Each family decomposes into a 25-dimensional gauge vector with 23 polarizations and one scalar field. There are also four massless ground states, with charges (±2,0) and (0,±2). Thus the massless spectrum at the bosonic self-dual circle has

Multiplying these currents by a noncompact oscillator produces six gauge vectors in total. The nine scalar string vertex operators JLa​JRb​ form the (3,3) representation, accounting for the radius scalar field and eight new scalar fields. The remaining neutral fields are the graviton, two-form and dilaton.

The other four extra scalar fields have masses 4/R2−4/α′ or 4R2/α′2−4/α′ and need not remain massless; one pair becomes tachyonic on either side of the self-dual point. Together with the original bosonic tachyon, this means that the gauge-symmetry interpretation is formal and does not assert a stable bosonic-string vacuum.
