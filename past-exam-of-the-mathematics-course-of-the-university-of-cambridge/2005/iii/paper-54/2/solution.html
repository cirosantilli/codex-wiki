Take 0≤σ≤π and split the coordinates into a=0,…,p along the branes and I=p+1,…,25 transverse to them. In conformal gauge the wave equation permits a separated mode e−iωτf(σ) with f′′+ω2f=0. For Neumann boundary conditions at both ends, f′(0)=f′(π)=0 selects cosmσ and integer m. The zero-frequency solution is independent of σ, with a constant position and a term linear in time. Therefore the open-string mode expansion is
Xa=xa+2α′kaτ+i2α′​∑m=0​mαma​​e−imτcosmσ.
(16)
The canonical momentum density integrated over this interval is ka.
For Dirichlet boundary conditions, subtract the static interpolation between fixed endpoint positions y0I​ and yπI​. The remaining field vanishes at both ends, selecting sinmσ with integer m and excluding a momentum worldsheet zero mode. The expansion is
XI=y0I​+πyπI​−y0I​​σ+2α′​m=0∑​mαmI​​e−imτsinmσ.​
(17)
Here αmI†​=α−mI​. There is no factor i in the sine series with this reality convention: changing m to −m reverses both the sine and the denominator. These are the integer-mode expansion with parallel D-brane endpoints; they are not the half-integer modes of a coordinate with a Neumann condition at one endpoint and a Dirichlet condition at the other.
The endpoints can move on (p+1)-dimensional worldvolumes but are fixed in the transverse directions. Thus these are open strings attached to D-branes, or stretched between parallel D-branes at y0​ and yπ​. The tangential momenta describe propagation on the brane; the transverse positions describe its embedding. Let L=∣yπ​−y0​∣. The linear spatial worldsheet zero mode contributes L2/(4π2α′) to L0​. Quantization with string intercept one gives
M2=4π2α′2L2​+α′N−1​.​
(18)
The first term is also the square of the classical stretched-string energy TL, since the string tension is T=1/(2πα′).
For two coincident D3-branes, label the ends of an oriented open string by i,j∈{1,2}. The four endpoint combinations give Chan-Paton factors λij​, which form the full algebra of 2×2 matrices. Under a change of brane basis these transform as λ↦UλU−1, the adjoint action of U(2). At L=0, the first oscillator level is massless. Its tangential polarization supplies a four-dimensional vector Aa​, while the 22 transverse polarizations supply real adjoint scalar fields ΦI. This is the bosonic D3-brane low-energy field content.
The endpoint matrices multiply in their boundary order in disk amplitudes. The difference between the two orders in the three-vector interaction gives the matrix commutator; the kinematic factor is the one-derivative Yang-Mills theory cubic vertex. Factorization and the vector gauge redundancy then supply the quartic interaction with the same commutator. Equivalently, the leading low-energy action is the dimensional reduction of 26-dimensional Yang-Mills theory, with
Fab​=∂a​Ab​−∂b​Aa​−i[Aa​,Ab​],Da​ΦI=∂a​ΦI−i[Aa​,ΦI],
(19)
Slow​=gYM2​1​∫d4xTr(−41​Fab​Fab−21​Da​ΦIDaΦI+41​[ΦI,ΦJ][ΦI,ΦJ])+⋯.
(20)
For Hermitian scalar fields the last displayed term corresponds to a nonnegative potential, because their commutators are anti-Hermitian. Higher-derivative terms are suppressed at energies well below 1/α′​. Ignoring the tachyon, the gauge sector is four-dimensional U(2) Yang-Mills theory, accompanied by 22 adjoint scalar fields. It is not pure Yang-Mills theory, nor the supersymmetric six-scalar D3-brane theory of a ten-dimensional superstring.
Separate the two branes and use the D-brane scalar-position normalization
⟨ΦI⟩=2πα′1​(y1I​0​0y2I​​).
(21)
A Yang-Mills gauge transformation preserves this expectation value only when it is diagonal if y1​=y2​. Thus the unbroken group is U(1)×U(1). The scalar field kinetic term supplies a mass for the off-diagonal vectors, since
[Aa​,⟨ΦI⟩]12​=2πα′y2I​−y1I​​(Aa​)12​.
(22)
Summing over I gives m122​=L2/(4π2α′2), exactly the mass of the stretched N=1 string. The two diagonal gauge vectors remain massless. This identifies brane separation with an adjoint Higgs mechanism, rather than an explicit deletion of the off-diagonal strings.
For the ground state of an open string joining the two different branes, N=0, so the bosonic stretched-string tachyon threshold is
M02​<0⟺L<2πα′​.​
(23)
At equality it is massless, and above it the stretched ground state has positive mass squared. The same-brane ground strings still have M02​=−1/α′; this calculation removes only the stretched-string instability, not the original instability of the bosonic branes.
