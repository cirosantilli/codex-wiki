Assume positive mass density bounded above and below and finite-energy fields in the ordinary bounded domain. The weighted space H=L2(D,ρdV)3 is a separable Hilbert space. Select one unit eigenfunction for each distinct squared frequency. They form an orthonormal set. Let {dj​} be a countable dense subset of H; assign to each eigenfunction a point dj​ within distance 1/3. Two orthogonal unit eigenfunctions have distance 2​, so the same dj​ cannot be assigned to both. This gives an injection into a countable set.
Hence there are at most countably many squared eigenfrequencies, and at most twice that many signed frequencies. Multiplicity at one frequency does not change the argument. A zero frequency associated with rigid translations or rotations is included. This proves the countability of elastic eigenfrequencies directly; proving discreteness or finite multiplicity would require additional spectral regularity arguments.
