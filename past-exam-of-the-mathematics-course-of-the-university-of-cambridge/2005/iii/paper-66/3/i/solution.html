Fix the indexing convention
Piℓ+1​=∑j​Mi−aj​Pjℓ​,tiℓ​=i/aℓ,
(14)
where the subdivision arity a>1 is an integer. A change of the initial control P00​ can influence only indices i=j1​ after one step, where Mj1​​=0. After ℓ steps the possible descendant indices are
i=aℓ−1j1​+aℓ−2j2​+⋯+jℓ​.
(15)
Let jmin​,jmax​ be the smallest and largest active subdivision mask indices. Dividing by aℓ gives the enclosure
a−1jmin​​(1−a−ℓ)≤tiℓ​≤a−1jmax​​(1−a−ℓ).
(16)
Therefore the support of a stationary subdivision scheme, in initial control-grid units, is enclosed by
[a−1jmin​​,a−1jmax​​],width a−1jmax​−jmin​​.​
(17)
For the stated symmetric subdivision mask with active extremes −w,w, the width is 2w/(a−1). For a nonzero compactly supported basic limit function ϕ, this is an exact endpoint calculation: its refinement equation is ϕ(x)=∑j​Mj​ϕ(ax−j). If β is its right support endpoint, only the largest active subdivision mask index can contribute sufficiently close to the rightmost point of this sum; all smaller indices have argument beyond β. Hence β=(β+jmax​)/a, giving β=jmax​/(a−1). The left endpoint follows in the same way. This argument also excludes cancellation at an extreme for signed subdivision masks. The support for initial control Pk​ is translated by k. If the nominal subdivision mask was padded by zero coefficients, use its actual extremes; and a limiting subdivision curve has to exist before its support is meaningful. This calculation describes the entire possible influence region, rather than the width of a single refinement stencil on its finer grid.
