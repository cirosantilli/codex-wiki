Suppose ϕj​∈Cr in u and ψk​∈Cs in v, including their respective knot joins. The mixed derivatives of the tensor-product surface basis are
∂up​∂vq​bjk​(u,v)=ϕj(p)​(u)ψk(q)​(v),p≤r, q≤s.​
(26)
These are continuous because each factor is continuous. A finite or locally finite control sum has the same property. In the usual total-order notation the parametric surface is therefore at least Cmin(r,s), and also has the stronger stated rectangular set of continuous mixed derivatives. Across a u-knot its guaranteed order is r, and across a v-knot its guaranteed order is s. No general improvement over a factor's smoothness is possible: setting Pjk​=Aj​ and using ∑k​ψk​=1 recovers the univariate curve ∑j​Aj​ϕj​(u) as a parametric surface constant in v.
