The comoving curvature perturbation R is the spatial curvature potential on uniform-field slices: choose T=δϕ/ϕˉ​′ and use (iii) to see that these slices also have zero scalar momentum density. For this spatial sign convention the intrinsic curvature perturbation is δR(3)=4a−2∇2R in that gauge; some conventions define the curvature perturbation with the opposite sign. The canonically useful Mukhanov-Sasaki variable is v=aQ.
