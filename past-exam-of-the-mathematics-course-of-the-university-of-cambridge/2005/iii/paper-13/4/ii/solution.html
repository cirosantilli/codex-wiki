Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2005 / iii / Paper 13 / 4 / ii / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2005 iii Paper 13 4 ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 190
Let P be a sphere with g+1 disjoint open disks removed, and form its double S by gluing two copies along the boundary. Then χ(S)=2χ(P)=2(1−g), so S is an oriented closed surface of genus g. The involution ι swapping the two halves fixes precisely the g+1 seam circles.
Use disjoint collars with coordinates (s,t)∈R/Z×(−η,η), where ι(s,t)=(s,−t). Choose a smooth even bump function χ(t) supported inside the collar, with χ(0)=1, and choose 0<ϵ<1. Define an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism R on each collar by R(s,t)=(s+ϵχ(t),t) and as the identity outside the collars. Scaling ϵ from zero gives an isotopy from the identity to R, so F=R∘ι is homotopic to ι.
A fixed point of F in a collar would satisfy −t=t, hence t=0. Its other coordinate would then require s+ϵ=s modulo one, impossible for the chosen ϵ. Outside the collars, F=ι exchanges the interiors of the halves and has no fixed points. Therefore ι has fixed circles but is homotopic to the fixed-point-free map F. This is the fixed-point-free perturbation of a doubled-surface reflection; the construction includes genus zero, where the double is a sphere.
