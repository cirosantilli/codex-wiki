The polarization identity for quadratic covariation, together with E[M±N]s​=E(Ms​±Ns​)2, yields
E[M,N]s​=E[Ms​Ns​]​.
(11)
The bracket identity for each zero-starting square-integrable martingale is the Itô isometry for integration of the constant integrand against that martingale. Polarizing both identities gives the displayed covariance identity for continuous square-integrable martingales. The bracket is integrable: its total variation is at most [M]s​[N]s​​ by the Kunita-Watanabe inequality, whose expectation is finite by the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality. Therefore condition (iii) is equivalent to (ii).
