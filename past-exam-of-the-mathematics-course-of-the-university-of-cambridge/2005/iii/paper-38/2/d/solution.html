For the integrable terminal bracket criterion, assume M0​=0, or more generally M0​∈L2. This initial-value assumption is necessary if it is not already part of the notation: an integrable but non-square-integrable random constant process has zero quadratic variation and still fails square-integrability.
For the zero-starting case, choose a localizing sequence τn​ bounding both ∣M∣ and its quadratic variation. The stopped processes are true square-integrable martingales, and the Itô isometry gives
