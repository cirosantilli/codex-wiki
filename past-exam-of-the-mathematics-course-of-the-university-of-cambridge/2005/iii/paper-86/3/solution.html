To obtain the rational-composition growth, represent the nonconstant degree-d sphere map by relatively prime homogeneous polynomials (P,Q) of degree d. Their joint norm on the unit sphere in C2 has a positive minimum and finite maximum: simultaneous vanishing would contradict relative primeness on the projective line. Homogeneity therefore gives constants 0<A≤B<∞ with

Its circular mean minus the value at zero is the spherical characteristic.
