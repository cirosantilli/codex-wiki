A sharper rejection envelope improves the algorithm without changing its proposal. For k>0, maximize the Poisson mass over λ=θL/2:
dλd​log(k!e−λλk​)=−1+λk​.
(35)
It increases until λ=k and decreases afterwards, so its maximum is Mk​=e−kkk/k!. For k=0, the maximum is M0​=1. Replace the acceptance rule by U≤ak​(θ,T)/Mk​. This is bounded by one, and its accepted density is unchanged because the factor 1/Mk​ cancels on normalization. Thus
αimproved​=Mk​mk​​,Mk​=k!e−kkk​ (k>0),M0​=1.​
(36)
For k>0 it is strictly more efficient, with improvement factor 1/Mk​∼2πk​ by Stirling's formula. For k=0 this envelope improvement gives no increase. Evaluating the Poisson mass and using a uniform draw also avoids generating a Poisson count only to discard it. A proposal concentrated near the posterior can improve efficiency further, but then its density and a valid rejection bound must be included.
