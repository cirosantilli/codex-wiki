Differentiate the product with the first factor close to the identity. A small displacement of its x′ coordinate adds to x; a small y′ displacement adds to y; and a small z′ displacement changes (x,y,z) by (−x,y,1) times that displacement. The infinitesimal left actions therefore give the right-invariant vector fields
R1​=∂x​,R2​=∂y​,R3​=∂z​−x∂x​+y∂y​.
(13)
For right multiplication, regard (x′,y′,z′) as the fixed point and the second factor as infinitesimal. Its displacements are (e−z′δx,ez′δy,δz); relabeling the coordinates of the fixed point gives the left-invariant vector fields
L1​=e−z∂x​,L2​=ez∂y​,L3​=∂z​.
(14)
The naming distinction matters: generators of left multiplication are right invariant, whereas generators of right multiplication are left invariant.
All mixed Lie brackets of vector fields vanish. For example, R3​(e−z)=−e−z cancels L1​(−x)=−e−z in [L1​,R3​], and R3​(ez)=ez cancels L2​(y)=ez in [L2​,R3​]. The brackets with R1​,R2​ are zero because the coefficients of the Li​ have no x or y dependence; [L3​,R3​]=0 because the coefficients of R3​ have no z dependence. Hence
[Li​,Rj​]=0for every i,j.​
(15)
Conceptually, the corresponding flows commute because (ag)b=a(gb), by associativity of the Lie group multiplication.
The dual coframe is
θ1=ezdx,θ2=e−zdy,θ3=dz,
(16)
which satisfies θi(Lj​)=δji​. Under left multiplication by (x0​,y0​,z0​), the transformed coordinates are X=x0​+e−z0​x, Y=y0​+ez0​y, Z=z0​+z. Therefore eZdX=ezdx, e−ZdY=e−zdy, and dZ=dz. Each one-form is a left-invariant differential form, so their sum of squares is the left-invariant metric
g=(θ1)2+(θ2)2+(θ3)2=e2zdx2+e−2zdy2+dz2.​
(17)
This is the standard metric of the Sol Lie group. As a sign check, [L3​,L1​]=−L1​, [L3​,L2​]=L2​, while dθ1=θ3∧θ1 and dθ2=−θ3∧θ2, in agreement with the Maurer-Cartan equation.
