The sine vectors vj(m)​=sin(jθm​), θm​=mπ/J, simultaneously diagonalize these two symmetric tridiagonal matrices. Their eigenvalues are
M:3h​(2+cosθm​),K:h2​(1−cosθm​).
(23)
Thus the semidiscrete decay rates are λm​=6(1−cosθm​)/[h2(2+cosθm​)]. The Forward Euler method multiplies each mode by 1−kλm​. Stability requires 0≤kλm​≤2 for every mode.
For a fixed finite Dirichlet grid the largest mode is θJ−1​=π−π/J. Consequently the exact finite-grid bound is
0≤μ≤3(1+cos(π/J))2−cos(π/J)​,μ=k/h2.​
(24)
As the mesh is refined, the upper bound decreases to 1/6. Hence the mesh-independent answer is 0≤μ≤1/6​. This is the forward Euler limit for a consistent hat mass matrix. The frequently seen 1/2 diffusion bound belongs to the standard second-difference scheme or a lumped mass matrix, and is not the bound for the consistent Galerkin equations derived here.
