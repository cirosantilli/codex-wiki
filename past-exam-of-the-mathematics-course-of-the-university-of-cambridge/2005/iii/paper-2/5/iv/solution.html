The weight lattice is
ΛW​={λ∈h∗:λ(Hα​)∈Z for every root α}=i=1⨁r​Zωi​,​
(56)
where the fundamental weights satisfy ωi​(Hαj​​)=δij​. The coroots use the normalization fixed above.
The Killing form defines the complex-linear map
B:h⟶h∗,B(H)(K)=B(H,K).
(57)
By nondegeneracy of the Killing form on a Cartan subalgebra, this is an isomorphism. For H∈hR​, expand its image in the dual fundamental-weight basis:
B(H)=∑i=1r​B(H,Hαi​​)ωi​.
(58)
The coefficients are real by the preceding positivity calculation, and the ωi​ are in ΛW​. Consequently
B(hR​)⊆RΛW​.​
(59)
In fact both spaces have real dimension r, so this inclusion is equality.
