Evaluate the Killing form on the root-space decomposition. For arbitrary K∈h,
B(Hα​)(K)=B(Hα​,K)=∑β∈Φ​β(Hα​)β(K).
(60)
Thus the Killing dual of a coroot is the root expansion
B(Hα​)=β∈Φ∑​β(Hα​)β=2β∈Φ+∑​β(Hα​)β.​
(61)
The factor two in the positive-root expression occurs because the contributions of β and −β are equal.
For sl3​, let a=L1​−L2​, b=L2​−L3​; the positive roots are a,b,a+b. On H12​ their values are 2,−1,1. Therefore
B(H12​)=2[2a−b+(a+b)]=6a,B(H12​)=6(L1​−L2​)=12ω1​−6ω2​.​
(62)
This uses the unscaled Killing form tr(adAadC). Changing that normalization would change the scalar six.
