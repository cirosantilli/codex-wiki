The Weyl group acts on the weight lattice by its root reflections and extends to ring automorphisms of the group ring of a weight lattice:

The second denominator expression is the Weyl denominator formula. Both alternants change sign under each root reflection; their quotient is invariant. Although written as a fraction, the theorem asserts that it is a finite element of Z[ΛW​] with the nonnegative coefficients given by weight multiplicities. The highest-weight classification specifies which λ label the irreducibles, while the root system and choice of positive roots determine every other term.
