Fix a finite-rank free abelian group HZ​. A pure integral Hodge structure of weight w is a decomposition
HC​=HZ​⊗C=⨁p+q=w​Hp,q,Hp,q=Hq,p.
(42)
Its Hodge numbers are hp,q=dimC​Hp,q, and its Hodge filtration is Fp=⨁r≥p​Hr,w−r. Equivalently, the filtration has these dimensions and satisfies
HC​=Fp⊕Fw−p+1.
(43)
The decomposition is recovered as Hp,q=Fp∩Fq.
A polarized Hodge structure additionally has a nondegenerate integral bilinear form Q satisfying Q(u,v)=(−1)wQ(v,u), extended complex-bilinearly, with
Q(Hp,q,Hr,s)=0unless (r,s)=(q,p).
(44)
We choose the geometric sign convention in which the positivity condition is
(−1)w(w−1)/2ip−qQ(v,vˉ)>0(0=v∈Hp,q).​
(45)
The expression is real by the symmetry and conjugation conditions. In particular it is iQ(v,vˉ)>0 on a weight-one (1,0) summand. Other conventions absorb the constant factor (−1)w(w−1)/2 into Q; a consistent choice matters in weight two.
Fix HZ​, Q and the Hodge numbers. The compact dual of a period domain is the projective flag variety
Dˇ={F∙:dimFp=∑r≥p​hr,w−r, Q(Fp,Fw−p+1)=0}.
(46)
The orthogonality conditions are algebraic. The Griffiths domain D is the subset whose filtrations also satisfy real opposition and the displayed positivity. It is open in the usual complex topology of Dˇ; the inequalities are not additional complex algebraic equations. Thus Dˇ retains the complex flag conditions, whereas D retains genuine polarized Hodge structures.
