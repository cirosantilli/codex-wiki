Throughout, the braces denote a multiset, so repeated splitting degrees retain their multiplicities. If the multisets agree after a common shift, permuting summands gives Ea​≅Eb​⊗O(c). Tensoring all the fibres of a vector bundle by the same line bundle leaves their one-dimensional quotient spaces unchanged. Hence
P(Ea​)≅P(Eb​).
(12)
This proves one implication in the isomorphism classification of abstract rational scrolls.
For the converse first take n≥3. Every divisor class on Fa​ has the form xL+yM. If its square is zero, restriction to a fibre Pn−1 gives
y2h2=0,
(13)
where h is the hyperplane class and h2=0 because n−1≥2. Thus y=0. It follows that L is intrinsically characterized as the primitive nonzero nef class whose square is zero. An isomorphism u:Fa​→Fb​ therefore carries Lb​ to La​; the sign is positive because both classes are nef and effective. The complete pencil ∣L∣ determines the ruling, so
πb​∘u=σ∘πa​
(14)
for an automorphism σ of P1.
On a fibre, the isomorphism preserves the positive generator OPn−1​(1) of its Picard group. Consequently
u∗Mb​=Ma​+cLa​
(15)
for some integer c. Push forward along the ruling, using π∗​O(M)=E and the projection formula for sheaves:
σ∗Eb​≅Ea​⊗OP1​(c).
(16)
Every automorphism of P1 preserves each O(d). The uniqueness of the splitting degrees in the Birkhoff–Grothendieck theorem now implies equality of the two multisets up to a shift. Replacing this c by −c gives the sign convention in the final formula. One can see that uniqueness numerically: for a split bundle,
h0(E(t))=∑i​max(ai​+t+1,0).
(17)
The first difference counts the degrees with ai​+t≥0, and the second difference counts the multiplicity of ai​=−t. Hence these dimensions determine every splitting degree, with multiplicity.
For n=2, subtract the smaller degree and write the normalized bundle as O⊕O(a), a≥0. Its surface is the Hirzebruch surface Fa​. Let B be its negative section and L its fibre, so B2=−a, BL=1, L2=0. The nef cone is generated by L and B+aL: a nef divisor xL+yB must have y≥0 from intersection with a fibre and x−ay≥0 from intersection with B, and these two inequalities also express it as a nonnegative combination of those generators. If a>0, a class uL+v(B+aL) with u,v≥0 has square
2uv+av2,
(18)
which vanishes only when v=0. Thus the ruling is again intrinsic, and the same bundle argument applies. If a=0, the surface is P1×P1 and has two nef square-zero rays, its two rulings. This distinguishes it from every a>0 surface. Isomorphisms may exchange its rulings, but both normalized splitting degrees are zero, so the stated classification is unchanged.
For n=1, every scroll is P1, and any two single splitting degrees differ by a common shift. Thus all ranks are covered:
F(a1​,…,an​)≅F(b1​,…,bn​)⟺{a1​,…,an​}mult​={b1​+c,…,bn​+c}mult​ for some c∈Z.​
(19)
Reading the braces as ordinary sets would lose information and make the assertion false when degrees repeat. For example, (0,0,1,2) and (0,1,1,2) have the same underlying set but not the same splitting multiset; their minima are both zero, so no nonzero common shift can reconcile them.
