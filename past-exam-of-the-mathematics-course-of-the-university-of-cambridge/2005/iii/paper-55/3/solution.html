Then Fμ5(n)​=−ikn​Bμ(n)​, so its term is a Proca field mass term +21​kn2​Bμ(n)​Bμ(−n). Equivalently, a periodic mode-dependent gauge transformation sets a(n)=0. The nonzero modes are massive vectors whose longitudinal polarization is supplied by a(n) through the Stueckelberg mechanism:
mn​=r∣n∣​(n=0).​

For each positive n there is one complex vector, equivalently the two real sine/cosine vectors, each with three physical polarizations. The negative mode is its conjugate, not another independent complex field. This is the infinite Kaluza-Klein tower of the Maxwell reduction on a circle.
At n=0, δa(0)=0 under periodic infinitesimal gauge transformations. The zero modes are a massless vector with two polarizations and a real massless scalar with one. The scalar is the circle component, or Wilson line degree of freedom; a generic constant value cannot be removed by a periodic gauge parameter. Both have m0​=0. Slow​=∫d4x[−41​Fμν(0)​Fμν(0)+21​∂μ​a(0)∂μa(0)].​

For the gravitational part, allow a circle radius field R(x) with vacuum value r, and use a dimensionless angle χ∼χ+2π in the Kaluza-Klein theory ansatz

The vector comes from off-diagonal metric components; R is the radion. Perform the coordinate transformation χ′=χ−κλ(x), leaving x unchanged. Since dχ=dχ′+κ∂μ​λdxμ, the metric has the same form with
Aμ′​=Aμ​+∂μ​λ,R′=R,gμν′​=gμν​.​
(22)
Thus a higher-dimensional diffeomorphism becomes an Abelian gauge transformation in the reduced theory. Changing the sign convention for the internal coordinate shift reverses the displayed sign without changing its content.
More generally, continuous internal isometries give lower-dimensional gauge symmetries, with vector fields from metric components along the corresponding Killing vector fields. The circle translation group gives U(1). A scalar mode ψn​(x)einχ transforms as ψn′​=einκλψn​, so Dμ​ψn​=(∂μ​−inκAμ​)ψn​ transforms covariantly. This derives Kaluza-Klein charge quantization in the chosen vector normalization. The metric vector is a graviphoton; if a five-dimensional Maxwell field is present as well, its zero-mode photon is a separate field originating from that Maxwell gauge symmetry.
