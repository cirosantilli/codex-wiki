Dimensional reduction of ten-dimensional super Yang-Mills theory on a six-torus gives the first: the vector yields one four-dimensional vector and six real scalar fields, and its Majorana-Weyl spinor yields four Weyl spinors. Reducing eleven-dimensional supergravity on a seven-torus gives the second: metric components supply one graviton, seven vectors and 28 scalars; the three-form supplies 21 vectors, 35 scalars and seven dualized two-form scalars; the gravitino supplies eight gravitini and 56 spin-half fields.
