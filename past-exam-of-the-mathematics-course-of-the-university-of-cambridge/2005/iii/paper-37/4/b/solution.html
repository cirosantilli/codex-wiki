There is a normalization error in the original PDF: the first term contains I rather than ρ. For a normalized qubit density operator, that printed expression has trace 2(1−p)+p=2−p, so it is not a quantum channel except at p=1. The intended depolarizing channel is the Pauli channel that leaves the input unchanged with probability 1−p and applies each nonidentity Pauli matrix with probability p/3, for 0≤p≤1. Its Kraus operators are
K0​=1−p​I,Kk​=p/3​σk​(k=1,2,3),
(40)
and ∑k=03​Kk†​Kk​=I, proving that it is trace-preserving and a completely positive map.
Write the input in its Bloch vector representation,
ρ=21​(I+r⋅σ),∣r∣≤1.
(41)
Conjugation by a Pauli matrix keeps its own Bloch vector component and reverses the other two. More explicitly, σk​σj​σk​=σj​ for j=k and −σj​ for j=k. Hence
∑k=13​σk​ρσk​=21​(3I−r⋅σ)=2I−ρ.
(42)
The corrected quantum channel therefore gives
Φp​(ρ)=(1−p)ρ+3p​(2I−ρ)=21​[I+(1−34p​)r⋅σ].
(43)
Thus its action is
r⟼(1−34p​)r.​
(44)
The Bloch sphere is sent to a sphere of radius ∣1−4p/3∣ about the origin of the Bloch ball. At p=0 the action is the identity. For 0<p<3/4 the Bloch vector shrinks without changing direction. At p=3/4 every input becomes the maximally mixed density operator I/2. For 3/4<p≤1, the Bloch vector reverses direction and shrinks, reaching factor −1/3 at p=1. The reversal is compatible with complete positivity because this remains a probabilistic mixture of Pauli matrices. The parameter here is the total nonidentity-error probability, not the retention parameter used in some definitions of the quantum depolarizing channel.
For completeness, retain the PDF literally and call its output Fp​(ρ). The same calculation gives
Fp​(ρ)=(1−2p​)I−6p​r⋅σ,TrFp​(ρ)=2−p.
(45)
This has no normalized-output Bloch ball interpretation as printed. If it is manually normalized, then
2−pFp​(ρ)​=21​[I−3(2−p)p​r⋅σ].
(46)
That different transformation sends p=0 to I/2, rather than to the input, and agrees with the intended answer only at p=1. The literal and corrected conventions therefore cannot be silently identified.
