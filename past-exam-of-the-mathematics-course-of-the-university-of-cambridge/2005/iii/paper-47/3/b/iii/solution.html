Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2005 / iii / Paper 47 / 3 / b / iii / Solution

OurBigBook.com Words: 76
Assume 0≤p≤1. Draw an independent component indicator with a Bernoulli distribution of success probability p. On success, generate the normal distribution from part (i) and return its absolute value; this has the half-normal distribution probability density function 2f(x) on x≥0. On failure, generate the Weibull distribution from part (ii). Use fresh independent uniforms for the chosen component.
The law of total probability gives the output probability density function
p2f(x)+(1−p)g(x)(x≥0),​
(23)
as required. The factor two belongs inside the half-normal component; the probability of choosing that component is p, not 2p.
