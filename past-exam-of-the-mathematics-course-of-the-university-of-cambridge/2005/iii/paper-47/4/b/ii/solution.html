For each bootstrap replicate form the studentized pivot
T∗=s∗θ∗−θ​.
(35)
Let t.025∗​,t.975∗​ be its empirical quantiles. The bootstrap-t confidence interval approximates
P(t.025∗​≤sθ−θ​≤t.975∗​)≃0.95.
(36)
Solving both inequalities for θ reverses the quantile order, giving
[θ−t.975∗​s,θ−t.025∗​s].​
(37)
Using the same fixed standard error in every bootstrap numerator would not implement this studentized procedure; each replicate needs its own s∗.
