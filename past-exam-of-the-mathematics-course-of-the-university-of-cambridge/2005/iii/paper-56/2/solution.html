The usual sigma-model lump sector adds the boundary condition ϕ(x)→ϕ∞​∈S2 uniformly as ∣x∣→∞. Defining the value at the added point to be ϕ∞​ then gives a continuous map S2→S2: neighborhoods of the point at infinity are precisely complements of compact subsets of the plane, and uniform convergence supplies continuity there. For the topological degree calculation using differential forms below, take a smooth compactified map, as in the regular lump sector.
