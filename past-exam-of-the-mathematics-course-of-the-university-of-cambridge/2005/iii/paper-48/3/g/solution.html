Expand the update in part (b). Its trinomial tree weights are
pi,dj​=2h2kσij,2​​−2hkbij​​,pi,mj​=1−h2kσij,2​​,pi,uj​=2h2kσij,2​​+2hkbij​​.​

They connect the current state to xi​−h,xi​,xi​+h at the next time. Their sum is one, their mean increment is bij​k, and their raw second increment moment is σij,2​k. Thus they match the log-price diffusion to first order in the time step.
All three weights are probabilities precisely when they are nonnegative:
h2kσij,2​​≤1,∣bij​∣h≤σij,2​,​
(23)
at every node and level. They are then automatically at most one. With positive σ−​, a simple sufficient uniform choice is k≤h2/σ+2​ and hsup∣r−σ2/2∣≤σ−2​. If volatility vanishes at a node while drift does not, the centered weights cannot all be nonnegative.
Nonnegative weights make the backward update a convex combination, proving maximum-norm stability and monotonicity: interior errors cannot exceed the larger of the terminal and boundary errors. This is a sufficient stability condition, not an equivalence with every possible stability notion. For constant coefficients on an unrestricted or periodic grid, let κ=σ2k/h2 and ν=bk/h. The Fourier amplification factor is
G(ϑ)=1−2κsin2(ϑ/2)+iνsinϑ.
(24)
Requiring ∣G∣≤1 for every frequency gives κ≤1 and ν2≤κ. Indeed, with s=sin2(ϑ/2), ∣G∣2−1=4s[(ν2−κ)+(κ2−ν2)s], so its two endpoint inequalities suffice. Probability weights require the stronger ∣ν∣≤κ. The distinction prevents interpreting a merely Fourier-stable scheme with negative weights as a probability tree.
