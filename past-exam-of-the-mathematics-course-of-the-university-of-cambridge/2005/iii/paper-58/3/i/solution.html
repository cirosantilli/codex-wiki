Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2005 / iii / Paper 58 / 3 / i / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2005 iii Paper 58 3 i
OurBigBook.com Words: 240
Number the physical wires 1,…,5 from top to bottom and write a computational basis ket in that order. The first four are ancilla qubits and the fifth carries the input. It is enough to follow the basis input ∣b⟩, b∈{0,1}, because the encoding quantum circuit acts linearly.
The first fan-out consists of four controlled-NOT gates from wire 5 to wires 1–4. It sends ∣0000b⟩ to ∣bbbbb⟩. The three Hadamard gates then give
8​1​∑a,c,d∈{0,1}​(−1)b(a+c+d)∣acdbb⟩.
(27)
The two Pauli X gates on wires 2 and 3, followed by a Controlled-Z gate and the same two X gates, restore the bits but contribute (−1)(1−c)(1−d): the sign is negative exactly when the original bits on both wires were zero. Finally, the right-hand controlled-NOT gates are 3→4, 2→5, 1→4 and 1→5. They leave the first three bits unchanged and turn the last two into b⊕a⊕d and b⊕a⊕c. Thus
∣bL​⟩=8​1​a,c,d∈{0,1}∑​(−1)b(a+c+d)+(1−c)(1−d)∣a,c,d,b⊕a⊕d,b⊕a⊕c⟩.​
(28)
Explicitly, the logical basis quantum states produced by the printed gates are
∣0L​⟩=8​1​(∣1L​⟩=8​1​(​−∣00000⟩+∣00110⟩+∣01001⟩+∣01111⟩−∣10011⟩+∣10101⟩+∣11010⟩+∣11100⟩),−∣00011⟩−∣00101⟩−∣01010⟩+∣01100⟩+∣10000⟩+∣10110⟩+∣11001⟩−∣11111⟩).​
(29)
Each has eight orthogonal terms of equal magnitude, and their supports are disjoint, so they are normalized and mutually orthogonal. The arbitrary input is therefore encoded as
α∣0⟩+β∣1⟩⟼α∣0L​⟩+β∣1L​⟩.​
(30)
This is a realization of the five-qubit error correcting code. The input amplitudes and their relative quantum phase are preserved in the logical subspace; the encoding is an entangled-state linear isometry, not five independent copies of the unknown input. The explicit signs and bit ordering above are fixed by this circuit, rather than by a convention for a different encoder.
