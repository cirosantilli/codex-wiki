Near the double-zero point, and with the stipulated absence of further bifurcations, the distinct open regions are:
  • λ<0: no equilibrium points or local periodic orbits; y˙​<0 wherever y=0, and the flow passes through the bottleneck region.
  • λ>0, μ<μh​: a saddle and a stable node or focus, with no local periodic orbit.
  • λ>0, μh​<μ<λ​: a saddle, a stable focus and an unstable periodic orbit surrounding the focus. This cycle is its local basin boundary.
  • λ>0, μ>λ​: a saddle and an unstable node or focus, with no small periodic orbit.
On the homoclinic curve the cycle becomes a saddle loop; its positive saddle trace μ+λ​ makes the loop repelling on the cycle side. At the Hopf curve the unstable cycle shrinks into the negative equilibrium point. The node–focus discriminant curves merely change the spiral appearance and are not additional bifurcations. The impossibility of a periodic orbit for λ<0 can also be seen without a picture: at a local minimum of periodic x(t) one would have y=0 and x¨=−λ+x2>0, but at its maximum the same strictly positive expression would be required to be nonpositive.
