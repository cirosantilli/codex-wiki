The Swift–Hohenberg equation has steady equation rw0​−B2w0​−w03​=0, and differentiation gives Lw0′​=0. On a fast periodic cell L is a self-adjoint operator. We assume the translation mode is its only zero mode; otherwise one must impose the corresponding additional Fredholm solvability conditions and retain additional amplitudes.
Since ∇2=∂ξ2​+ε2DY2​, its squared operator is B2+2ε2BDY2​+ε4DY4​. The time derivative first contributes at order ε4. At order ε2,
