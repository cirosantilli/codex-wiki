Put X=1+T and Y=Xp−1, so that ϕ(f)=f(Y). The essential integrality statement for the Frobenius substitution on cyclotomic power series is the decomposition into a finite free module:
R=⨁i=0p−1​Xiϕ(R).
(1)
To prove it, reduce modulo p. Since Y≡Tp, grouping the coefficients of a formal power series according to their exponents modulo p gives the unique expression ∑j=0p−1​Tjhj​(Tp). The change from 1,T,…,Tp−1 to 1,X,…,Xp−1 is a triangular matrix with diagonal entries 1, so this is also a basis over Fp​[[Tp]]. Lift these p coefficient series to R, subtract their contribution from f, divide the remaining error by p, and repeat. The sums of the successive lifts converge in the finer of the topologies on integral formal power series, because R is complete in that topology. This proves existence of the decomposition over R. A relation among its summands reduces to the zero relation modulo p, forcing each coefficient series to be divisible by p. Repetition forces divisibility by every power of p, hence every coefficient series is zero. This proves uniqueness and, in particular, injectivity of ϕ.
For f=∑i=0p−1​Xiϕ(fi​), define
S(f)=f0​.​
(2)
This is a Zp​-linear map. To calculate the average over roots of unity, work over O=Zp​[ζp​]. The substitutions T↦ζX−1 are legitimate: their constant terms are topologically nilpotent, so the defining coefficient sums converge in O. Since (ζX)p=Xp, each ϕ(fi​) is unchanged by the substitution. The sums of the powers of the roots of unity are p for i=0 and zero for 1≤i<p. Consequently
∑ζ∈μp​​f(ζX−1)=pϕ(f0​).
(3)
Thus the average is integral and belongs to ϕ(R), and the constructed Coleman trace operator has the required property. Injectivity of ϕ forces uniqueness of any map satisfying that property. Finally, the decomposition of ϕ(g) has f0​=g and all other coefficients zero, giving S∘ϕ=idR​.
We will also use the trace of multiplication by h on this finite free module. After extending scalars to split the conjugates, that trace is the sum just computed. Hence
TrR/ϕ(R)​(h)=pϕ(S(h)).
(4)
