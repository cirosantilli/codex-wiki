Take a compact subset K⊂Uα​, and choose finitely many such intervals covering it. The one-dimensional Wiener covering lemma selects pairwise disjoint intervals I1​,…,IN​ whose concentric triples cover K. For a finite family this follows by choosing a longest remaining interval and discarding all intervals intersecting it: each discarded interval has no greater length and lies in the triple of the chosen one.
