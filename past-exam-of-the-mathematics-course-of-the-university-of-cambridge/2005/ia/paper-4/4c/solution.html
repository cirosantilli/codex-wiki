On the horizontal road the total normal reaction is Mg. The kinetic friction force has magnitude μMg, so Newtonian mechanics gives u˙=−μg until rest. Thus
tdry​=μgU​,ddry​=2μgU2​.​
(12)
For the wet high-speed stage, add the four tyre drags: the total linear drag is λu, not λu/4. Hence Mu˙=−λu and u(t)=Ue−λt/M. At the switching speed u∗​=U/4, the elapsed time and distance are
t∗​=λM​log4,d∗​=∫0t∗​​Ue−λt/Mdt=4λ3MU​.
(13)
The remaining stage has the same constant kinetic friction deceleration as on a dry road, but starts at U/4. It therefore contributes time U/(4μg) and distance U2/(32μg). Adding both stages of the two-stage stopping under linear drag and dry friction gives
twet​=λM​log4+4μgU​,dwet​=4λ3MU​+32μgU2​.​
(14)
The exponential first stage reaches its positive switching speed in finite time; it need not reach zero under linear drag.
