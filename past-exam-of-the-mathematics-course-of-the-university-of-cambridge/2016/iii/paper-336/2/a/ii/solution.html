Write E=erfz, g=e−z2 and b=2/π​. The first correction in the inner expansion satisfies L0​Y1​=2zE. Direct differentiation verifies that zE+bg is a particular solution. Adding the constant and error function homogeneous solutions gives Y1​=zE+bg+A+BE.
The left boundary condition forces A=−b. Re-expanding the outer expansion at x=sz gives
yout​(sz)=1+sz+s2[2z2​−21​logz−21​logs]+⋯.
(18)
Therefore Y1​∼z, with no constant term in its large-z overlap. Since E→1 and g→0, this sets B=b. Thus the required half-order correction is
Y1​(z)=zerfz+π​2​[e−z2+erfz−1].​
(19)
It vanishes at zero and has the correct linear overlap. Keeping this O(ε​) term is essential even though the outer solution has no correction of that order.
