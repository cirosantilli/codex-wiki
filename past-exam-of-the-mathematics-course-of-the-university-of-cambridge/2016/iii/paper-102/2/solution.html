The form attached to V is BV​(x,y)=trV​(xy). More generally, for a Lie algebra representation ρ, the Trace form of a Lie algebra representation is
BV​(x,y)=trV​(ρ(x)ρ(y)).
(10)
The unqualified Killing form is the special case of the Adjoint representation,
B(x,y)=trg​(adxady).
(11)
The distinction matters: a Trace form of a Lie algebra representation can be degenerate even when g is semisimple, for example on the trivial Lie algebra representation.
The Trace form of a Lie algebra representation is bilinear and symmetric, because tr(AB)=tr(BA). It is an invariant bilinear form on a Lie algebra:
BV​([x,y],z)=BV​(x,[y,z]).
(12)
This follows by expanding both commutators and cyclically permuting factors under the matrix trace. Equivalently,
BV​([x,y],z)+BV​(y,[x,z])=0.
(13)
Its radical of a bilinear form is an ideal of a Lie algebra, since if BV​(y,−)=0, then BV​([x,y],z)=−BV​(y,[x,z])=0. The Killing form is also preserved by every automorphism of a Lie algebra, because the corresponding adjoint operators are conjugate. On a complex finite-dimensional Lie algebra, the Cartan criterion for semisimplicity says that the Killing form is nondegenerate exactly when the Lie algebra is semisimple. The Cartan solvability criterion says that g is solvable exactly when B(g,[g,g])=0.
We next construct the sl2 subalgebra associated with a root. Use the root-space decomposition
g=h⊕⨁β∈Φ​gβ​.
(14)
For u∈gβ​, v∈gγ​, invariance of the Killing form gives
(β(h)+γ(h))B(u,v)=0(h∈h).
(15)
Thus B(gβ​,gγ​)=0 unless β+γ=0, and B(gβ​,h)=0 for nonzero β. Nondegeneracy of B on g now implies that −α is a root and that B pairs gα​ and g−α​ nondegenerately.
Nondegeneracy of B∣h​ defines a unique tα​∈h by
B(tα​,h)=α(h)(h∈h).
(16)
Choose x∈gα​ and y∈g−α​ with B(x,y)=1. Their Lie bracket lies in the zero root space, namely h, and
B([x,y],h)=B(x,[y,h])=α(h)B(x,y)=α(h).
(17)
Therefore [x,y]=tα​.
The essential nonisotropic root lemma is that α(tα​)=0. Suppose instead that it vanished. Then [tα​,x]=[tα​,y]=0, so ⟨x,y,tα​⟩ would be a solvable Lie algebra with derived algebra Ctα​. Apply the Lie theorem to its action on g by the Adjoint representation. The commutator adtα​=[adx,ady] is strictly upper triangular in a suitable basis, hence nilpotent. But tα​∈h, so the root-space decomposition makes adtα​ diagonalizable. A diagonalizable nilpotent linear map is zero. Thus tα​ is central in g. The center of a Lie algebra of a semisimple Lie algebra is zero; equivalently a central element lies in the radical of the Killing form. This forces tα​=0, contradicting α=0.
Writing c=α(tα​), define
Xα​=x,Hα​=c2tα​​,Yα​=c2y​.​
(18)
The root-space decomposition and [x,y]=tα​ give
[Hα​,Xα​]=2Xα​,[Hα​,Yα​]=−2Yα​,[Xα​,Yα​]=Hα​.
(19)
The three vectors are linearly independent because they lie in the distinct summands gα​, h, and g−α​. Their span is therefore a copy of the sl2 Lie algebra.
The weight lattice consists of the functionals integral on all coroots. With Hα​ the coroot above, the weight lattice is
P={λ∈h∗:λ(Hα​)∈Z for all α∈Φ}=⨁i=1ℓ​Zωi​,
(20)
where the fundamental weights satisfy ωi​(Hαj​​)=δij​ for the simple roots αj​. Here P lies in the real span of the roots, viewed inside h∗.
The classification of finite-dimensional sl2 representations says that every finite-dimensional complex sl2 Lie algebra representation is a direct sum of irreducibles Lm​, m∈Z≥0​, on which the standard H has eigenvalues m,m−2,…,−m. Restrict any finite-dimensional Lie algebra representation of g to each sl2 subalgebra associated with a root. If v has weight λ, then Hα​v=λ(Hα​)v, so λ(Hα​) is an integer. Thus every weight lies in P. The same restrictions show that the commuting simple coroots act diagonalizably, justifying the simultaneous weight-space decomposition.
For sp(4), the roots are ±2ε1​, ±2ε2​, and ±ε1​±ε2​. Work on C4 with the alternating bilinear form having matrix
J=(0−I2​​I2​0​).
(21)
The symplectic Lie algebra is
sp(4)={M:MTJ+JM=0}={(AC​B−AT​):B=BT, C=CT}.
(22)
Using the matrix units Eij​, take the Cartan subalgebra
h=⟨h1​,h2​⟩,h1​=E11​−E33​,h2​=E22​−E44​.
(23)
Define εi​(a1​h1​+a2​h2​)=ai​. A regular diagonal element of h has centralizer precisely h, and every element of h acts diagonalizably. Thus it is a Cartan subalgebra. The requested Cartan decomposition is the root-space decomposition
sp(4)=h⊕⨁α∈Φ​CXα​.
(24)
Choose positive roots ε1​−ε2​, 2ε2​, ε1​+ε2​, 2ε1​. The symplectic root sl2 triple are given explicitly by
αε1​−ε2​ε1​+ε2​2ε1​2ε2​​Xα​E12​−E43​E14​+E23​E13​E24​​Hα​h1​−h2​h1​+h2​h1​h2​​Yα​E21​−E34​E41​+E32​E31​E42​​​

For the negative root spaces, use the corresponding Yα​. These eight root vectors, together with h1​,h2​, form a basis: the block description above has dimension 4+3+3=10, and the ten listed vectors are independent. Finally, the matrix unit identity
[Eij​,Ekl​]=δjk​Eil​−δli​Ekj​
