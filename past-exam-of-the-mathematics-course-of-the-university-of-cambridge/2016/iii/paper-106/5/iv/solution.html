Weak compactness of the adjoint forces weak compactness of the original operator. Assume T∗:Y∗→X∗ is weakly compact. Apply the already proved (i)⟹(ii) to this operator, rather than assuming the adjoint equivalence in advance. It gives

f(x)=(JX∗​f)(JX​x)=(T∗∗∗ψ)(JX​x)=ψ(T∗∗JX​x)=ψ(JY​Tx)=0.

ψ(T∗∗F)=0(F∈X∗∗)

The vector subspace JY​Y is closed in the norm topology in Y∗∗ because Y is complete and JY​ is an isometry. The Hahn-Banach theorem says that any point outside a closed linear subspace can be separated from it by a bounded linear functional vanishing on that subspace. Applying this in Y∗∗ shows that an element annihilated by all such ψ∈Y∗∗∗ must belong to JY​Y. Therefore T∗∗F∈JY​Y for every F, proving (iv)⟹(ii).
