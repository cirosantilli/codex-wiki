Conversely, every future unit timelike vector can be made the time axis of an orthonormal frame by a proper orthochronous Lorentz transformation, and that frame can be extended to normal coordinates at p. Requiring the displayed inequality in every such chart therefore gives the dominant energy condition for every future timelike vector; positive rescaling handles nonunit vectors. Future null vectors follow by continuity if they are included in the definition. The condition must hold in every local Lorentz frame; one chart alone is insufficient.
