One can take α=ω+1​. Finite induction gives Ln​=Vn​ for every finite n: every subset of a finite level is definable using its finitely many elements as parameters. Hence Lω​=Vω​, the set of hereditarily finite sets.
Now reason inside the constructible universe L, a model of ZFC. The set Lω​ is countable there, and there are countably many first-order formulas and finite parameter tuples. Thus Lω+1​=Def(Lω​) is countable in L. But the constructible power set PL(ω) is uncountable in L by the Cantor theorem. Choose
r∈PL(ω)∖Lω+1​.
(29)
This r is constructible and a subset of ω, so r⊆Vω​ and r∈Vω+1​. Therefore
r∈L∩Vω+1​∖Lω+1​​.
(30)
All comparisons were made inside L; this avoids assuming that the constructible reals are uncountable in the ambient universe. The witness remains valid externally because the two structures use the same r, the same Vω​, and the same constructible level. Thus constructible sets of low rank can appear at later stages.
