Use standard notation for forcing: q≤p means that q is stronger than p. A closed forcing order is λ-closed when every decreasing sequence ⟨pξ​:ξ<δ⟩ with δ<λ has a common lower bound:
∀δ<λ ∀ decreasing (pξ​)ξ<δ​ ∃q ∀ξ<δ (q≤pξ​).​
(36)
The chain condition for forcing is
every antichain in P has cardinality strictly below λ.​
(37)
An antichain in a forcing order consists of pairwise incompatible forcing conditions; incompatibility means having no common stronger extension. In particular, the ℵ1​-chain condition is the countable chain condition for forcing. These properties and their sequences or antichains are computed inside the ground model M.
