The Volterra integration operator is injective and has dense range. Its range of the Volterra integration operator is {f∈H1(0,1):f(0)=0}, and its Moore–Penrose inverse of an operator is differentiation on this domain. Thus the error estimate for K†f implicitly requires f(0)=0, in addition to the printed H2 assumption. For an arbitrary H2 function the same calculation bounds error against f′, but that function need not be in D(K†).

To prove convergence for every admissible datum, not just H2 data, let f=Ku with u∈L2(0,1) and extend u by zero. Each stencil is a forward, centred or backward average of u. Translation continuity in Lp makes each averaged difference from u tend to zero in L2(R). Restricting those three bounds to their respective intervals proves Rα​Ku→u.
