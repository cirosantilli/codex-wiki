For a continuous proper event time with cumulative hazard function H and survivor function F(t)=e−H(t), use the assumed inverse of H. For u≥0,
P(H(T)>u)=P(T>H−1(u))=F(H−1(u))=e−u.
(41)
Therefore the integrated-hazard transformation is unit exponential:
H(T)∼Exp(1).​
(42)
