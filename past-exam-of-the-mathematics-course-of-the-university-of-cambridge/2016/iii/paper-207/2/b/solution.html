Starting in state 1, the onset waiting time has the exponential distribution of rate λ. Thus the probability of onset before the next screen is 1−e−λt. This counts individuals who have already progressed to clinical disease as well as those still pre-clinical.
Conditional on onset at time u<t, the Markov property makes the remaining progression time exponential with rate ν. Thus the probability of clinical progression before the screen, conditional on that onset time, is 1−e−ν(t−u).
