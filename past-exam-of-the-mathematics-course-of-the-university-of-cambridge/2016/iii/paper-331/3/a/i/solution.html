Under the convention ei(kx−ωt), a mode's amplitude grows as eωi​t. The linear stability analysis of real-wavenumber disturbances is therefore summarized by
ωi,max​<0: strictly stable,ωi,max​>0: linearly unstable.​
(29)
At ωi,max​=0 there is marginal or neutral hydrodynamic stability: no normal mode grows exponentially, but some may fail to decay. If “linearly stable” includes neutral modes, its condition is ωi,max​≤0. These modal statements do not rule out transient growth from non-normal modes or algebraic growth.
