Normalize the discrete valuation on L by vL​(L×)=Z, set vL​(0)=+∞, and write OL​ for its valuation ring. For a finite Galois extension of local fields, define G−1​=G=Gal(L/K) and, for integers i≥0,
Gi​={σ∈G:vL​(σ(a)−a)≥i+1 for every a∈OL​}.​
(6)
Equivalently, Gi​ is the kernel of the action on OL​/mLi+1​. These are the lower ramification numbering of the ramification groups. In particular, G0​ is the inertia group, the kernel of the action on the residue field, and G1​ is the wild inertia group. They form a decreasing sequence of normal subgroups, eventually trivial.
For a real index t≥−1, extend by Gt​=G⌈t⌉​. Define the Herbrand function by
φL/K​(t)=∫0t​[G0​:Gs​]ds​(t≥0),φL/K​(t)=t(−1≤t≤0).
(7)
It is continuous, strictly increasing and piecewise linear; denote its inverse by ψL/K​. The upper ramification numbering is
Gu=GψL/K​(u)​(u≥−1).​
(8)
The definition includes the placement of the groups at break endpoints: the group at a break is the group before the drop. Lower numbering is compatible with subgroups; upper numbering is the one compatible with quotients. In a totally ramified extension, the uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups permits testing the defining valuation inequality on one uniformizer instead of on every integral element.
