A monomorphism m:A→B is left cancellable: mu=mv implies u=v for all parallel arrows into A. A strong monomorphism is a monomorphism with the following lifting property: if e:X→Y is an epimorphism and u:X→A, v:Y→B satisfy mu=ve, there is a diagonal d:Y→A with de=u and md=v. It is unique by monicity. An extremal monomorphism is a monomorphism m for which every factorization m=he with e an epimorphism forces e to be an isomorphism. We use the strong lifting formulation for the paper's terminology; below we also justify its equivalence with the extremal formulation under the complete, well-powered hypotheses. A regular monomorphism is an equalizer of two parallel arrows.
First, a strong monomorphism is extremal. In a factorization m=he, apply its lifting property with upper arrow 1A​ and lower arrow h. This gives d with de=1A​. Since e is an epimorphism, ede=e implies ed=1, so e is an isomorphism.
If m:A→B is an equalizer of r,s:B⇉C, consider a lifting square as above. The equation rve=smu=sve and the epimorphism property give rv=sv. The equalizer property therefore gives a unique d with md=v. Monicity then gives de=u. Hence every regular monomorphism is a strong monomorphism.
For the intersection of strong subobjects, let mi​:Ai​→B be strong and let pi​:P→Ai​ exhibit their intersection, with m=m1​p1​=m2​p2​. Given mu=ve with e an epimorphism, the two strong monomorphisms supply diagonals di​:Y→Ai​ satisfying di​e=pi​u and mi​di​=v. The pullback in a category property supplies d:Y→P with pi​d=di​. Its two projections show de=u, and md=v. Thus the intersection is strong. The same argument works for every existing small intersection of strong monomorphisms. Also, composites of strong monomorphisms are strong: first lift through the outer one, then through the inner one.
Write S for the full subcategory formed by every saturated object with respect to anodyne morphisms, using the paper's anodyne morphism in a category convention. Let m:C→B be a strong monomorphism and B saturated. For an anodyne morphism in a category e:X→Y and u:X→C, saturation extends mu to v:Y→B. Since e is an epimorphism, the strong lifting property supplies d:Y→C extending u. Therefore every strong subobject of a saturated object is saturated.
Now assume C is a complete category and a well-powered category, with the given saturated embeddings. Choose a monomorphism a:A→B into a saturated object. Intersect all strong subobjects of B through which a factors. They form a set by the well-powered category hypothesis, and the identity of B is one of them. Completeness constructs their intersection, so we obtain
AηA​​LAm​B,mηA​=a.
(1)
By the intersection of strong subobjects, m is strong; hence LA is saturated. Since a is a monomorphism, so is ηA​.
To prove that ηA​ is an epimorphism, take u,v:LA⇉D with uηA​=vηA​. Their equalizer j:E→LA is a regular monomorphism, and ηA​ factors through it. The composite mj:E→B is strong, so it belongs to the family defining LA. Minimality gives t:LA→E with mjt=m. Cancelling m gives jt=1LA​; since j is a monomorphism, also tj=1E​. Thus j is an isomorphism and u=v. We have proved
ηA​:A→LA is both monic and epic, and LA is saturated.​
(2)
This is the saturated reflection from a strong-subobject intersection.
The construction of a least strong subobject and the equalizer argument work for any morphism into any object, without requiring the source map to be monic or the target to be saturated. They give an epimorphism followed by a strong monomorphism. Applying this factorization to an extremal monomorphism makes the first factor invertible; thus the extremal and strong formulations agree under the present complete, well-powered hypotheses.
For every saturated S, saturation extends every f:A→S across the anodyne morphism in a category ηA​ to fˉ​:LA→S. Its extension is unique because ηA​ is an epimorphism. Consequently
S(LA,S)≅C(A,S),​
(3)
naturally in S and A. In particular, for h:A→A′ define Lh by LhηA​=ηA′​h; uniqueness proves the identity and composition laws. This constructs the reflector and proves that S is a reflective subcategory.
Finally we prove that S is a balanced category. If e:U→V is epic in S and u,v:V→X satisfy ue=ve, embed X by a monomorphism j:X→Y into a saturated object. Fullness makes ju,jv arrows of S, so ju=jv and hence u=v. Thus an epimorphism in S is also epic in C.
A monomorphism f:U→V in S is also monic in C: for a,b:X→U, extend them uniquely along ηX​ to aˉ,bˉ:LX→U. If fa=fb, cancellation of the ambient epimorphism ηX​ gives faˉ=fbˉ, so monicity in S gives aˉ=bˉ and a=b. Now if f is both monic and epic in S, it is an anodyne morphism in a category in C. Saturation of U extends 1U​ across f to r:V→U with rf=1U​. Ambient epicity gives fr=1V​. Fullness puts this inverse in S, proving
S is balanced.​
