Form the HNN extension which centralizes L:
GM​=⟨KM​,k∣k−1tk=t,k−1ri​k=ri​,k−1ℓj​k=ℓj​⟩.​
(44)
This is the extension associated to the identity isomorphism L→L; relations on the displayed finite generating set imply k−1hk=h for all h∈L. Since KM​ is finitely presented and I,J are finite, this is a finite group presentation.
For any g∈KM​,
k−1gk=g in GM​⟺g∈L.
(45)
The forward implication follows from Britton's lemma: if g∈/L, the word k−1gkg−1 is reduced with two stable letters and cannot be the identity. The reverse implication is the defining centralization relation. Consequently the computable word
W(r,s)=k−1t(r,s)kt(r,s)−1
(46)
satisfies
W(r,s)=1 in GM​⟺(r,s)∈H0​(M).​
(47)
An algorithm for the word problem for a group GM​ would therefore decide the nonrecursive set H0​(M), a contradiction. Hence GM​ has unsolvable word problem​.
