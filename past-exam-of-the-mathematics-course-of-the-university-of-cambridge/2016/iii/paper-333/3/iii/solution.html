For a zonal basic geostrophic flow U(y), take ψ0′​(y)=−U(y) and write ψ=ψ0​+ϕ. The flat-bottom shallow-water quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity gradient of the basic state is
Q0′​=β−U′′+RD−2​U.
(26)
Linearizing its material conservation gives the Rossby-wave equation for a sheared zonal current,
(∂t​+U∂x​)(∇h2​ϕ−RD−2​ϕ)+(β−U′′+RD−2​U)ϕx​=0.
(27)
For a general nonuniform jet, a global two-dimensional plane wave is not an exact normal mode: the coefficients depend on y. The exact zonal normal mode problem, ϕ=ϕ​(y)ei(kx−ωt), is
(U−ω/k)[ϕ​′′−(k2+RD−2​)ϕ​]+Q0′​ϕ​=0,k=0,​
(28)
with appropriate transverse boundary or radiation conditions. If the jet varies slowly compared with a wavelength, a local plane wave freezes these coefficients at y0​. Its local dispersion relation is
ω=kU−k2+l2+RD−2​k(β−U′′+RD−2​U)​.​
(29)
In the nondivergent Barotropic Rossby wave model under a rigid-lid approximation, this becomes ω=kU−k(β−U′′)/(k2+l2). It is exact for uniform U and otherwise a local relation. Retaining the free-surface term also requires retaining the basic surface slope in Q0′​; simply adding RD−2​ to the denominator while dropping RD−2​U from the numerator would describe a different prescribed-background model.
