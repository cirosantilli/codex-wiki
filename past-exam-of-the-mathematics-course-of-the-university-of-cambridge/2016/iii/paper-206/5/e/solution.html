The null model's 99 residual degrees of freedom imply n=100. Each predictor has a one-degree-of-freedom linear contribution in the parametric table, plus the reported nonlinear contribution. Consequently the termwise effective degrees of freedom are
edfexperience​≈1+8.8=9.8,edfeducation​≈1+0.4=1.4.​
(61)
Include the intercept once, giving about 12.2 total effective degrees of freedom. The more precise residual degrees of freedom in the output give
edftotal​=100−87.7644=12.2356.​
(62)
There is no conflict: 8.8 and 0.4 are rounded, so they cannot recover the precise total. Individual unrounded termwise values are not provided.
The package's residual mean-square estimate of error variance is
σ2=87.7644906437.7​≈10328.08,​
(63)
matching the rounded 10328 in the table. The nonlinear experience contribution is highly significant, while the smaller nonlinear education contribution has approximate p=0.03923.
This calculation uses the effective residual degrees of freedom reported by this fit. For a general non-idempotent linear smoother S, the noise contribution to expected RSS is σ2tr[(I−S)T(I−S)], and the expectation can additionally contain squared smoothing bias. Thus dividing by n−tr(S) is not a universal exact-unbiasedness theorem. The output's separation of linear and nonlinear term degrees of freedom is documented at cran.r-project.org/web/packages/gam/gam.pdf.
