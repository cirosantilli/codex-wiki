Let si​ be sunny days and li​ latitude. The R linear-model formula expands to a normal linear model with both main effects and their interaction term:
Yi​=β0​+βs​si​+βl​li​+βsl​si​li​+εi​,εi​∼iidN(0,σ2).
(25)
The fitted conditional mean is
μ​i​=1.619+0.01403si​+0.01438li​−4.872×10−6si​li​.​
(26)
The residual standard error is 0.2836, giving the usual variance estimate s2=0.08042896, and n=96+4=100.
The estimated interaction term is tiny relative to its standard error: t=−0.103 and the two-sided p-value is 0.91811. A sensible initial simplification is to remove the interaction and refit an additive linear model, lm(energy ~ sunny + latitude). Both main effects are significant in the supplied fit, and should initially be retained. Do not simply copy their old coefficient estimates into the new fit: removing a design column generally changes all estimates. Centering predictors makes intercepts and main-effect interpretations more useful, but does not change the fitted surface. The Box–Cox transformation in part (d) suggests a further response-scale improvement; after changing the response scale, reassess the interaction and diagnostics.
