 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2016 iii Paper 206 1 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 107
Use a common intercept and laboratory-specific slopes in the normal linear model. With ti​ denoting days and IBi​,ICi​ the indicator variables for laboratories B and C, the design matrix has row xiT​=(1,ti​,ti​IBi​,ti​ICi​), and
Yi​=β0​+βA​ti​+δB​ti​IBi​+δC​ti​ICi​+εi​,εi​∼iidN(0,σ2).
(1)
The interaction terms change slopes, without introducing laboratory-specific intercepts. Thus the estimated daily growth rates are
bA​=10.1404,bB​=10.1404+5.0770=15.2174,bC​=10.1404−3.1296=7.0108​
(2)
in millimetres per day, with fitted common intercept 0.6464 mm. The residual degrees of freedom are 30−4=26. The usual unbiased error variance estimate is the square of the residual standard error:
s2=15.092=227.7081 mm2.​
(3)
The small difference from 5921.7/26≈227.758 arises from rounding the standard error to two decimal places; the latter uses the more precise residual sum of squares supplied later. A maximum-likelihood estimate would instead divide that residual sum of squares by 30.
