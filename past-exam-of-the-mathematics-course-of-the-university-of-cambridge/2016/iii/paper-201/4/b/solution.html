The 2n increments are independent normal random variables, each distributed as 2−n/2B1​. Their squared means are 2−n and, using the permitted variance, their squared variances are 2⋅2−2n. Independence therefore gives
E[B]n​=2n2−n=1,var([B]n​)=2n2⋅2−2n=21−n.
(34)
Consequently the dyadic quadratic variation converges to one:
E([B]n​−1)2=21−n⟶0,[B]n​⟶1 in L2.​
(35)
This is dyadic quadratic variation of Brownian motion on the time interval of length one. The calculation requires independence only within each partition; increments from different resolutions need not be independent.
