The limiting polynomial is λ(λ+1)(λ+10/3), reproducing the three stated eigenvalues. The positive eigenvalue tends to zero, with λ∼−2f/3. The two negative modes decay as z−1 and z−10/3; the remaining mode is neutral at the limiting linear system. Its eigenvector is (1,2,3) in relative-flux coordinates.
The neutral buoyancy-flux mode of a pure plume has a simple interpretation: a constant surviving buoyancy flux fixes the amplitudes of a pure plume, with Q∝F1/3z5/3 and M∝F2/3z4/3. A small fractional change in F produces changes in Q,M in the ratio 1:2:3. It is retained rather than damped out.
There is a qualification at the exact endpoint. For fixed nonzero K and β=−8/3, taking Q∝z5/3 gives Fz​∝−z−1, not zero. The flux coefficients above also diverge as f→0. Thus the constant-flux powers are the formal limiting scaling, not an exact constant-buoyancy solution in a nonzero endpoint stratification; cumulative ambient effects can require logarithmic corrections.
