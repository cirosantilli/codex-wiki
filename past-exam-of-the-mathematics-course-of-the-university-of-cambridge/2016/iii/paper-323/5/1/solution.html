Use logarithms to base two, so Von Neumann entropy and quantum relative entropy are measured in bits. For density operators ρ,σ,
S(ρ)=−Tr(ρlog2​ρ),D(ρ∥σ)=Tr[ρ(log2​ρ−log2​σ)].​
(41)
The quantum relative entropy formula applies when the support of a positive operator ρ is contained in that of σ; otherwise D(ρ∥σ)=+∞. Use 0log2​0=0 in the Von Neumann entropy formula.
The binary density operator ω[p] is diagonal with eigenvalues 1−p,p. Its Von Neumann entropy is the binary entropy:
S(ω[p])=h2​(p)=−(1−p)log2​(1−p)−plog2​p.​
(42)
The two diagonal density operators commute, so their quantum relative entropy is
D(ω[p]∥ω[q])=(1−p)log2​1−q1−p​+plog2​qp​.​
(43)
A term with zero numerator has value zero; a positive numerator and zero denominator give +∞. In particular, D(ω[0]∥ω[0])=D(ω[1]∥ω[1])=0, whereas q=0,p>0 or q=1,p<1 gives +∞.
