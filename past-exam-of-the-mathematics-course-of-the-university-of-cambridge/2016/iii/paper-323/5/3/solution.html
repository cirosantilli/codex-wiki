For the distribution and POVM from part 2, apply the data-processing inequality for quantum relative entropy to the measurement channel. The quantum mutual information is the quantum relative entropy from the joint density operator to the product of its marginals, so for k≥2,
I(X:Q)ρ​​=D(ρXQ​∥ρX​⊗ρˉ​Q​)≥D(ω[ϵ]∥ω[1−1/k])=(1−ϵ)log2​k−h2​(ϵ)−ϵlog2​(1−1/k)≥(1−ϵ)log2​k−1.​
(47)
The last step uses h2​(ϵ)≤1 for the binary entropy and log2​(1−1/k)≤0. If 0≤ϵ<1, rearrangement and then the supremum over PX​ give the one-shot classical-quantum coding converse:
log2​k≤1−ϵI(X:Q)ρ​+1​≤1−ϵsupPX​​I(X:Q)ρXQ​​+1​.​
(48)
For k=1, the sole POVM effect is IQ​, forcing ϵ=0, and the bound is trivial. For ϵ=1, division by 1−ϵ is undefined: the undivided inequality remains valid and gives no size restriction. The displayed coding bound is therefore understood for ϵ<1, or with a vacuous +∞ right-hand side at ϵ=1.
An alternative uses the original decoder directly. Let M be the uniform message and M its measured estimate. For k≥2, Fano's inequality bounds the conditional entropy by H(M∣M)≤h2​(ϵ)+ϵlog2​(k−1). The Holevo bound gives
I(X:Q)ρ​≥I(M:M)=log2​k−H(M∣M)≥log2​k−h2​(ϵ)−ϵlog2​(k−1)≥(1−ϵ)log2​k−1.
(49)
Thus the same coding bound follows by bounding the classical mutual information obtainable from the POVM.
