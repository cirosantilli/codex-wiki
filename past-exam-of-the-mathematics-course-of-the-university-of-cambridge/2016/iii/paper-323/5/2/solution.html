Choose PX​ uniform on AM​ and zero outside it, so the marginal density operator of Q is ρˉ​Q​=k−1∑m​ρ(m)Q​. Define the binary test for quantum decoding success by
F(0)=m∈AM​∑​∣m⟩⟨m∣X​⊗E(m)Q​,F(1)=IXQ​−F(0).​
(44)
Each E(m) is a positive contraction. The computational basis blocks of F(0) are these effects on AM​ and zero elsewhere. Thus 0≤F(0)≤I, making F a POVM on the whole space, including labels outside AM​.
On the correlated classical-quantum state, its acceptance probability is
Tr[F(0)ρXQ​]=k1​∑m​Tr[E(m)ρ(m)]=1−ϵ.
(45)
On the product of the marginal density operators,
Tr[F(0)(ρX​⊗ρˉ​Q​)]=k1​∑m​Tr[E(m)ρˉ​Q​]=k1​Trρˉ​Q​=k1​,
(46)
since ∑m​E(m)=IQ​. The two requested probabilities are therefore 1−ϵ and 1/k. In the notation for binary output density operators, the measurement channel sends the joint input to ω[ϵ] and the product input to ω[1−1/k]; their parameters describe outcome one, not outcome zero.
