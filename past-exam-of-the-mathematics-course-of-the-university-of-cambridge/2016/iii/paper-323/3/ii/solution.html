Let Γ=TB​ denote the partial transpose. The partial transpose of a maximally entangled projector is
(ϕ+)Γ=d1​∑i,j​∣i⟩⟨j∣⊗∣j⟩⟨i∣=dS​,
(24)
where S is the swap operator, satisfying S†S=I and ∥S∥op​=1. If σ has positive partial transpose, then σΓ is a positive operator of trace one, hence ∥σΓ∥1​=1.
For a pure state ϕ+=∣Φ⟩⟨Φ∣, the unsquared quantum fidelity obeys F(ϕ+,σ)2=⟨Φ∣σ∣Φ⟩. Using the trace-adjoint identity for partial transpose and part (i),
F(ϕ+,σ)2=Tr(ϕ+σ)=d1​Tr(SσΓ)≤d1​∥σΓ∥1​=d1​.
(25)
The overlap is nonnegative, so taking its square root proves the PPT maximally entangled overlap bound:
F(ϕ+,σ)≤d​1​.​
(26)
The product state ∣00⟩⟨00∣ attains the bound, so it is sharp.
