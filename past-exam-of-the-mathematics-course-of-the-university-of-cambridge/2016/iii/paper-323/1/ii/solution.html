Write X and Y for the classical quantum registers X and Y. By the quantum mutual information formula I(U:V)=S(U)+S(V)−S(UV), expanding the right-hand side of the quantum mutual information balance identity gives
​I(X:B)+I(XB:D)−I(B:D)=S(X)+S(B)−S(XB)+S(XB)+S(D)−S(XBD)−S(B)−S(D)+S(BD)=S(X)+S(BD)−S(XBD)=I(X:BD).​
(7)
All Von Neumann entropies here are evaluated in ω.
We use three facts: quantum mutual information is nonnegative by nonnegativity of quantum relative entropy; a local quantum channel cannot increase it by data processing for quantum mutual information; and the quantum mutual information of a classical-quantum state is its ensemble's Holevo quantity. Write χ(T) for the Holevo capacity, the supremum of the output Holevo quantity over finite input ensembles.
The XB marginal is an output ensemble for E, with inputs ρ(x)A​=TrC​ρ(x)AC​. Consequently I(X:B)ω​≤χ(E). Also ωXBD​ is obtained from σXYD​ by a quantum channel on XY which retains X and prepares B from Y. Hence
I(XB:D)ω​≤I(XY:D)σ​.
(8)
To bound the latter even for entangled states ρ(x)AC​, exhibit the conditional input ensemble after a local measurement. Set
q(y∣x)=Tr[(Ey​⊗IC​)ρ(x)AC​],ρCx,y​=q(y∣x)TrA​[(Ey​​⊗IC​)ρ(x)AC​(Ey​​⊗IC​)]​
(9)
when q(y∣x)>0; zero-weight outcomes can be omitted. The numerator is a positive operator, its trace is q(y∣x), and the q(y∣x) sum to one. Thus
σXYD​=∑x,y​PX​(x)q(y∣x)∣x,y⟩⟨x,y∣⊗N(ρCx,y​).
(10)
This is a classical-quantum state with an output ensemble for N, so I(XY:D)σ​≤χ(N). Combining these bounds with I(B:D)ω​≥0 yields
I(X:BD)ω​≤χ(E)+χ(N),χ(E⊗N)≤χ(E)+χ(N).​
(11)
The last step takes the supremum over all input ensembles on AC. The left-hand channel direction is B←A, as established in part (i); the printed A←B in the last inequality is a typographical reversal. Independent product ensembles also give the reverse inequality, so this proves Holevo-capacity additivity for entanglement-breaking channels.
