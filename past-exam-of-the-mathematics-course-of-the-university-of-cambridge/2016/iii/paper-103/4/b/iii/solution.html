There are (k−1)! k-cycles in Sk​, and each has the same character value of a representation on Sλ. Taking traces in the cycle-sum identity for Young–Jucys–Murphy elements yields
(k−1)!χλ(k,1n−k)=∑T∈SYT(λ)​∏r=2k​cT​(r).
(48)
Only tableaux whose first k entries form a hook partition contribute.
Write such a shape as μb​=(k−b,1b), for 0≤b≤k−1. There are fμb​​=(bk−1​) ways to fill it: choose which b of the labels 2,…,k go below the first box. There are fλ/μb​ ways to fill the remaining skew diagram with the labels k+1,…,n, in standard order. The eigenvalue from part (ii) is (−1)b(k−b−1)!b!, so the factor
(k−1)!fμb​​(k−b−1)!b!​
(49)
is 1. Therefore the general character value is
χλ(k,1n−k)=0≤b≤k−1(k−b,1b)⊆λ​∑​(−1)bfλ/(k−b,1b)​.
(50)
Here fλ/μ counts standard skew Young tableaux, and is zero when μ is not contained in λ. In particular, for a full n-cycle,
χλ(n)={(−1)b,0,​λ=(n−b,1b),λ is not a hook.​​
(51)
For k=1, the general formula gives fλ/(1)=fλ​, the character value at the identity, as it should.
