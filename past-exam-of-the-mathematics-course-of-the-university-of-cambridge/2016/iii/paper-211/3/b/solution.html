For fixed γ, differentiation under the integral sign is justified by bounded P and X. At the unconstrained minimizer Hγ​, the first-order condition is
peγ(Hγ​⋅p−x)=E[Peγ(X−Hγ​⋅P)].
(26)
Define the strictly positive, bounded pricing kernel
Zγ​=eγ(Hγ​⋅p−x)eγ(X−Hγ​⋅P)​.
(27)
The first-order condition says E[Zγ​P]=p, so Zγ​∈Z. When taking the requested partial derivative, hold H fixed and only afterwards set H=Hγ​. Writing Aγ​=Hγ​⋅p−x, we obtain
∂γ​Fγ​(H)∣H=Hγ​​​=eγAγ​[Aγ​+E{Zγ​(X−Hγ​⋅P)}]=eγAγ​[E(Zγ​X)−x].​
(28)
The assumed dual bound applies to this particular pricing kernel. Thus
∂γ​Fγ​(H)∣H=Hγ​​≤0.​
(29)
No derivative of the map γ↦Hγ​ is needed; confusing a partial derivative with a derivative along the minimizing path would add an unnecessary hypothesis.
