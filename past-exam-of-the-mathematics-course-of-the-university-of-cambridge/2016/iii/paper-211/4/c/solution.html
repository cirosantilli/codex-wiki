Let b and h be the numbers of units of the numéraire and the stock in a replicating strategy. The two terminal payoffs of the European call option are 2 and 0, so
15b+20h=2,20b+15h=0.
(39)
Solving these simultaneous equations gives b=−6/35 and h=8/35. The initial replication cost is
C(1,18)=10b+10h=74​.​
(40)
As an independent pricing check, let q be the risk-neutral probability of the state (15,20) under the numéraire measure. The discounted stock must have initial value one and terminal mean one:
1=q1520​+(1−q)2015​,q=73​.
(41)
Risk-neutral valuation then gives 10(3/7)(2/15)=4/7. The physical probabilities 1/2,1/2 are not the numéraire-measure probabilities.
