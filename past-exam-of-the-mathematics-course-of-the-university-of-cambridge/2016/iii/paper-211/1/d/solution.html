A martingale with the specified terminal value must equal its conditional expectation. If St​=0, default has already occurred and the terminal European call option payoff is zero. If St​>0, put h=T−t. Conditional on survival to T,
ST​=St​eλhexp{−σ2h/2+σh​Y},

where Y has the standard normal distribution; the conditional probability of this survival is e−λh. The conditional expectation of the payoff in the enlarged filtration used in part (c) is consequently
e−λhSt​eλhF(σ2h,St​Ke−λh​).
(9)
This expression is already measurable with respect to the natural filtration of S, so the tower property of conditional expectation gives the same value there. The survival factor cancels the compensating growth factor:
Ct​=⎩⎨⎧​St​F(σ2(T−t),St​Ke−λ(T−t)​),0,​St​>0,St​=0.​​
(10)
The separate zero branch avoids division by zero. At t=T it gives the required payoff, using F(0,m)=(1−m)+; integrability follows from (ST​−K)+≤ST​.
