Assume the scalar order parameter can take either sign. The cubic term breaks m↦−m symmetry, and a positive quartic coefficient bounds the Landau free energy below. Stationary points satisfy
A′(m)=m(A2​+A3​m+A4​m2)=0,
(16)
so, besides the disordered point m=0, the possible ordered points are
m±​=2A4​−A3​±A32​−4A2​A4​​​.
(17)
The disordered point has curvature A′′(0)=A2​ and is locally stable when A2​>0. Nonzero stationary points first appear when
A2​≤4A4​A32​​.
(18)
That condition alone does not make them the equilibrium phase: one must compare their free energies.
At a nonzero stationary point, substitute A2​=−A3​m−A4​m2 to obtain
A(m)=−6A3​m3​−4A4​m4​=−12m3​(2A3​+3A4​m).
(19)
For an ordered state to coexist with A(0)=0, this requires
mcoex​=−3A4​2A3​​,A2,coex​=9A4​2A32​​.​
(20)
Both curvatures at phase coexistence are positive and equal to 2A32​/(9A4​). The barrier is at m=−A3​/(3A4​). In fact the potential at phase coexistence factorizes:
A(m)=4A4​​m2(m+3A4​2A3​​)2.
(21)
The two distinct minima are therefore explicit, with a finite jump in the order parameter whenever A3​=0.
To state the equilibrium inequalities for either sign of A3​, put m=−sgn(A3​)ρ with ρ≥0. This sign always has lower energy than the opposite sign of equal magnitude. Then
A(m)=ρ2[2A2​​−3∣A3​∣​ρ+4A4​​ρ2].
(22)
The bracket's minimum is A2​/2−A32​/(9A4​). It follows that
A2​>9A4​2A32​​A2​=9A4​2A32​​A2​<9A4​2A32​​​:m=0 is the unique global minimum,:m=0 and mcoex​ coexist,:the global minimum is ordered.​​
(23)
The favored ordered minimum, when it exists, is
mord​=−sgn(A3​)2A4​∣A3​∣+A32​−4A2​A4​​​.
(24)
Thus a first-order phase transition is possible and occurs when the coefficients cross the coexistence relation. There is no continuous transition between m=0 and the equilibrium ordered state while A3​ remains nonzero and A4​ remains finite and positive. The ordered state becomes globally favorable while A2​ is still positive, before the disordered curvature can vanish. Although a stationary solution tends to zero at A2​=0, it is not the global transition branch. A continuous transition would require eliminating the cubic term, outside the stated A3​=0 condition.
The spinodal points clarify the metastable region: for 2A32​/(9A4​)<A2​<A32​/(4A4​) the ordered minimum is metastable; for 0<A2​<2A32​/(9A4​) the disordered minimum is metastable. At A2​=0 the disordered state loses local stability. These local-stability limits should not be mistaken for the first-order transition in a cubic-quartic Landau potential.
