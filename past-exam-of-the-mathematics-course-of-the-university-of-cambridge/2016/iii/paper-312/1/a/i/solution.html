Write hij​ for the spatial metric tensor and lower the shift vector with hij​. For the negative-shift convention, the four-dimensional metric and its inverse have components
g00​=−N2+Ni​Ni,g0i​=−Ni​,gij​=hij​,g00=−N−2,g0i=−NiN−2.
(1)
The normal has covariant components nμ​=(−N,0,0,0), so nμ​nμ=−1 and it annihilates every tangent vector to the spatial slice. Since ni​=0, its covariant derivative gives ni;j​=NΓ0ij​ and therefore Kij​=−NΓ0ij​.
Compute the required Christoffel symbol using the symmetric connection formula. Its time-index term and spatial-index term combine to give
Γ0ij​=2N21​(h˙ij​+∂i​Nj​+∂j​Ni​−Nk(∂i​hjk​+∂j​hik​−∂k​hij​)).
(2)
The spatial expression in parentheses is h˙ij​+Di​Nj​+Dj​Ni​, where D is the covariant derivative of h. Thus the extrinsic curvature with a negative shift is
Kij​=−2N1​(h˙ij​+Di​Nj​+Dj​Ni​).​
(3)
This derivation fixes both signs from the actual 3+1 decomposition of spacetime, rather than transferring a formula using the opposite shift vector convention. The connection formula used here has gλκ,ν​ as its second differentiated term; the printed repeated-index version is a typographical error.
