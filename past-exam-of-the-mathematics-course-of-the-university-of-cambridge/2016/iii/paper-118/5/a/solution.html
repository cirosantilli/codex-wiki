This is the Poincaré residue of a meromorphic top form with a simple pole. Write z=z1​ and locally α=(dz/z)∧β, with β a holomorphic (n−1)-form. The proposed residue is the pullback of β to Y.
First it does not depend on the choice of β: if dz∧(β−β′)=0, then at each point the difference has a factor dz, and its pullback to Y is zero. Now change the local defining coordinate to t=az, where a is a nowhere-zero holomorphic function. Such a factor exists because both coordinates define the same smooth complex analytic hypersurface. If α=(dt/t)∧η, then

At points of Y, this is an equality in the ambient exterior-power fibre, and the last term is zero. It follows that dz∧(β−η)=0 there, so their pullbacks to Y agree. This also covers changes of the tangential coordinates.
The restrictions are holomorphic top forms on Y, and their agreement on overlaps makes them a global section of the canonical bundle. Thus the residue is independent of the adapted coordinates:
