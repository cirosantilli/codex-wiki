For the nonnegative Hodge Laplacian ΔdH​=dd∗+d∗d, the Kähler Laplacian identity gives ΔdH​=2Δ∂ˉ​. If u is a holomorphic function, then ∂ˉu=0, while ∂ˉ∗u=0 by degree. Therefore
Δ∂ˉ​u=(∂ˉ∂ˉ∗+∂ˉ∗∂ˉ)u=0,ΔdH​u=0.
(16)
The divergence-of-gradient convention printed in part (b) is Δd​=−ΔdH​ on functions. The sign changes no kernel, so the requested harmonicity of holomorphic functions on a Kähler manifold is
Δd​u=0.​
(17)
Holomorphic functions are harmonic without any compactness assumption. This is a local identity from the Kähler Laplacian identity, not an integration argument requiring a compact manifold.
