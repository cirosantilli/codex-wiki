OurBigBook.com Words: 204
Choose a constant positive Hermitian form on V. Its underlying flat Kähler metric is translation-invariant and descends through the Euclidean lattice to the compact complex torus X. The Dolbeault Hodge decomposition gives a unique Dolbeault Laplacian-harmonic representative of every Dolbeault cohomology class. By the Kähler Laplacian identity, these are also Hodge Laplacian-harmonic.
Choose a constant coframe on V. A lifted harmonic form is a sum of constant wedge basis forms with smooth periodic coefficients. For the flat metric, the Hodge Laplacian acts coefficientwise by the scalar constant-coefficient Laplacian. Each coefficient is a harmonic function on a compact flat real torus, hence is constant: integration by parts gives ∫∣Da∣2=0. Conversely every constant-coefficient form is harmonic. Thus the harmonic representatives are exactly the translation-invariant differential forms of type (p, q).
The constant (1,0) covectors form V∗=HomC​(V,C); the constant (0,1) covectors form the space of antilinear maps V∗=HomC​(V,C). Sending a constant wedge form to its cohomology class gives

The map is intrinsic and does not depend on the auxiliary flat metric: that metric proves every class has exactly one constant representative, while the inclusion of constant forms is defined directly by the quotient. The Dolbeault cohomology of a complex torus consists of constant forms, and in particular
hp,q(X)=(pg​)(qg​),g=dimC​V.
