 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2016 iii Paper 118 3 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 146
A connection on a vector bundle is a real-linear map (complex-linear for a complex bundle)
D:Γ(E)⟶A1(E),D(fs)=df⊗s+fDs.
(24)
It extends to the covariant exterior derivative by D(η⊗s)=dη⊗s+(−1)kη∧Ds when η has degree k. Its square is linear over smooth functions: in D2(fs) the two df∧Ds terms have opposite signs and d2f=0. The curvature form of a connection is therefore the endomorphism-valued two-form
Θ=D2∈A2(EndE).​
(25)
In a local frame with D=d+θ, it is Θ=dθ+θ∧θ.
Define the tensor product connection on decomposable sections by
D(s⊗t)=D1​s⊗t+s⊗D2​t.​
(26)
The Leibniz rule for D1​,D2​ makes this well-defined over smooth functions and makes it a connection on a vector bundle. Applying its covariant exterior derivative again gives
D2(s⊗t)=D12​s⊗t−D1​s∧D2​t+D1​s∧D2​t+s⊗D22​t.
(27)
The mixed terms cancel by the graded sign. Thus curvature adds on the two tensor factors:
Θ=Θ1​⊗I+I⊗Θ2​.​
(28)
Equivalently, the mixed terms in (θ1​⊗I+I⊗θ2​)∧(θ1​⊗I+I⊗θ2​) cancel because operators on the different factors commute. This also proves the curvature of a tensor product connection formula in local frames.
