Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2016 / iii / Paper 122 / 4 / a / i / Solution

 ... 2016 iii Paper 122 4 a i
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
For an object X of a braided monoidal category, take its self-braiding
y=cX,X​:X⊗X⟶X⊗X.​
(35)
It is invertible by definition. Suppress canonical associators using the monoidal coherence theorem, and put R12​=y⊗1, R23​=1⊗y. The hexagon identity gives
cX,X⊗X​=R23​R12​.
(36)
Apply naturality of this braiding to the morphism y:X⊗X→X⊗X in its second argument. It says
cX,X⊗X​(1⊗y)=(y⊗1)cX,X⊗X​.
(37)
Substitution gives
R23​R12​R23​=R12​R23​R12​.​
(38)
This is the equation for a Yang–Baxter operator. Restoring the uniquely determined associators gives the non-strict diagram in the paper. Every object therefore has the canonical Yang–Baxter operator supplied by its self-braiding.
