More generally, the printed family A+Be−ξ can have finite angular momentum at both ends only if A=0 and A+B=0, hence it is trivial. Thus the request for a nonzero finite constant Jd​ is incompatible with the stated viscosity exponent, similarity ansatz and infinite radial domain. A physical finite-domain or altered similarity problem would need different boundary data or assumptions; silently assigning a finite value to the divergent integral does not repair it.
