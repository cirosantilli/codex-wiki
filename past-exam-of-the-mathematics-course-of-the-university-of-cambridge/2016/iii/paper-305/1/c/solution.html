Keep the color matrix intact: Aμ​=Aμa​Ta. Under parity, invariance of its contraction with the vector current requires
Aμ​(x)⟼P​Λμ​νAν​(xP​).​
(9)
Under charge conjugation, the fermionic reordering in part (b) transposes the color matrix as well as the spinor matrix. For a fixed color matrix M, ψˉ​Mγμψ becomes −ψˉ​MTγμψ. Hence invariance of the interaction requires
Aμ​(x)⟼C​−Aμ​(x)T.​
(10)
This is not a rule that changes the sign of every individual color component: transposition also acts on the generators of the fundamental representation.
Write the gauge field strength as Fμν​=∂μ​Aν​−∂ν​Aμ​+ig[Aμ​,Aν​]. Since [AT,BT]=−[A,B]T, the charge conjugation rule gives Fμν​↦−FμνT​. Including the coordinate reflection in CP symmetry,
Fμν​(x)⟼CP​−Λμ​αΛν​βFαβ​(xP​)T.​
(11)
For generators normalized by Tr(TaTb)=κδab, the color contraction equals κ−1Tr(Fμν​Fρσ​). The two charge-conjugation minus signs cancel, and transposition reverses the order inside the trace without changing it. The Levi-Civita symbol acquires detΛ=−1 under the remaining reflection. Thus
Lθ​(x)⟼CP​−Lθ​(xP​).​
(12)
The Yang-Mills theta term is CP-odd; a generic fixed nonzero θ produces CP violation. Setting θ=0 removes this source. Although the density is a total derivative, it can affect the quantum theory through nontrivial topological sectors; the local transformation test is not rendered irrelevant by that fact.
