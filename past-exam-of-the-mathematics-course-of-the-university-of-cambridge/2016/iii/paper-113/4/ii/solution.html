For the closed inclusion f, the direct image sheaf has stalk Fx​ at x∈X and zero stalk at points outside X: outside the closed set there is an open neighbourhood disjoint from X. Hence f∗​ is exact, since exact sequences of sheaves are detected on stalks.
Take a flasque resolution F→I∙. Direct image preserves flasqueness, because restrictions are the restrictions on inverse-image opens. Exactness makes f∗​I∙ a flasque resolution of f∗​F. Moreover
Γ(Y,f∗​I∙)=Γ(X,I∙).
(14)
The complexes computing sheaf cohomology are identical. Therefore
Hi(Y,f∗​F)≅Hi(X,F)(i≥0).​
(15)
This is sheaf cohomology under a closed inclusion; the closedness hypothesis is used in the exactness of direct image.
