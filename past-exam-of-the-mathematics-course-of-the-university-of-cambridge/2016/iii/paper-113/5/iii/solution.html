Eliminate t3​ using the linear equation. The homogeneous coordinate ring becomes
B=C[t0​,t1​,t2​]/(t13​+t0​t22​).
(21)
The cubic is a nonzero element of a polynomial integral domain, hence a non-zero-divisor. It gives the exact sequence of graded modules
0⟶S(−3)t13​+t0​t22​​S⟶B⟶0,S=C[t0​,t1​,t2​].
(22)
Thus dimBm​=(2m+2​)−(2m−1​) for m≥3. Equivalently, use the Hilbert series (1−z3)/(1−z)3, or the sheafified exact sequence on P2. The Hilbert polynomial of the plane cubic is
ΦOX​​(m)=3m.​
(23)
Its degree is three and its arithmetic genus is one, although the curve is singular: its affine equation near [1:0:0] is the cusp t22​+t13​=0. The Hilbert polynomial records the arithmetic genus, rather than the genus of the normalization.
