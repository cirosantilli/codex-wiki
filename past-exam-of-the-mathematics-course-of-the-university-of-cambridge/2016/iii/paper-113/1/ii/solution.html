OurBigBook.com Words: 107
Put A=k[t1​,t2​]. The punctured affine plane is covered by the principal open subschemes D(t1​) and D(t2​). Since A is an integral domain, restriction identifies its global sections with
Γ(X,OX​)=At1​​∩At2​​⊂k(t1​,t2​).
(2)
This intersection is A: if a/t1r​=b/t2s​, then t2s​a=t1r​b, and unique factorization forces t1r​ to divide a. Therefore the inclusion j:X↪SpecA induces the identity on global regular functions.
If X were an affine scheme, affine scheme reconstruction from global sections would make j an isomorphism of schemes, because its global-section map is an isomorphism. But its image omits the closed point (t1​,t2​). The punctured affine plane is therefore not affine. This is a useful example of global regular functions failing to distinguish a nonaffine open subscheme from its affine ambient space.
