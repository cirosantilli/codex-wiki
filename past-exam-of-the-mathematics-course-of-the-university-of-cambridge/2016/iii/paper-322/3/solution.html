A symmetric streamline with opposite impact parameter has the opposite transverse velocity. Their shock wave removes the opposing transverse motion while preserving the common downstream component. In the frame where the undisturbed medium is stationary, adding the star's velocity gives zero velocity immediately after this idealized collision.
After transverse kinetic energy is dissipated, the remaining specific orbital energy is εafter​=v2/2−GM/b. It is negative if b<2GM/v2, or equivalently if the upstream impact parameter satisfies d<dcap​=2GM/v2.
