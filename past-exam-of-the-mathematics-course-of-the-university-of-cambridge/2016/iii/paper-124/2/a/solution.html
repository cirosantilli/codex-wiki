The event that infinitely many An​ occur is limsupn​An​=⋂m≥1​⋃n≥m​An​. For every m, the union bound and monotonicity of a probability measure give
0≤P(limsupn​An​)≤P(⋃n≥m​An​)≤∑n≥m​P(An​).
(14)
The right-hand side tends to zero as the tail of a convergent series. Therefore the first Borel-Cantelli conclusion is
P(nlimsup​An​)=0.​
(15)
This proves the Borel-Cantelli first lemma without any independence assumption.
