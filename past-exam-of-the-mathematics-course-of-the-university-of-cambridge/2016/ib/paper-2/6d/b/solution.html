For the field generated by the infinitely long, axially symmetric shell currents, axisymmetry and the right-hand rule give B=Bϕ​(r)eϕ​. A circular loop has ∮B⋅dℓ=2πrBϕ​(r). Its enclosed electric current is zero inside the inner shell, I between the shells and 2I outside both. Ampère's law therefore gives
B(r)=⎩⎨⎧​0,2πrμ0​I​eϕ​,πrμ0​I​eϕ​,​r<a,a<r<b,r>b.​​
(16)
The positive eϕ​ direction is counterclockwise viewed from positive z. The ideal current sheets make the tangential magnetic field discontinuous at each shell; these formulas specify the three open regions, with no additional imposed background field.
