For every closed piecewise smooth curve γ in U, the winding number of f∘γ about zero is the integer
mγ​=2πi1​∫γ​f(z)f′(z)​dz.
(45)
If f=gkk​ for a holomorphic kth root, gk​ is nowhere zero, and
mγ​=k2πi1​∫γ​gk​gk′​​dz.
(46)
Thus mγ​ is divisible by every k for which a root exists. An infinite subset of the positive integers is unbounded, so a fixed integer divisible by all those k must be zero. Hence all periods of the holomorphic function f′/f vanish.
Fix z0​∈U. Since a domain is connected and open, its points can be joined by piecewise smooth paths. Vanishing periods make
H(z)=∫z0​z​f(w)f′(w)​dw
(47)
independent of path. It is a holomorphic primitive with H′=f′/f. Differentiating gives (fe−H)′=0, so fe−H=f(z0​). Choose a complex number c with ec=f(z0​). Then
F=H+c is holomorphic on U,f=eF.​
(48)
This constructs a global holomorphic logarithm without requiring U to be simply connected.
