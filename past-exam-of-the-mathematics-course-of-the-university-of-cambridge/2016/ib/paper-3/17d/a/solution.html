For a moving closed circuit C(t) bounding an oriented surface S(t), Faraday's law is
Eem​(t)=∮C(t)​[E+v×B]⋅dl=−dtd​∫S(t)​B⋅ndS.​
(65)
Here E is the electric field, B the magnetic field, v the local velocity of a circuit element, and Eem​ the electromagnetic electromotive force per unit charge around the circuit. The line orientation and normal n obey the right-hand rule. The flux derivative is a total derivative and includes both changing field and changing surface. The term v×B is the motional electromotive force. A battery contributes an additional non-electromagnetic source electromotive force.
