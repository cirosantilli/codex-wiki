For a direct application of Lagrangian sufficiency theorem, use equality constraints hi​=xi​−1−∑k=1i​yk​ and multipliers μi​=−2xi∗​. The Lagrangian derivatives in xi​ vanish. Its derivative in yj​ is 4yj∗​+2∑i=jn​xi∗​, which also vanishes: putting Sj​=2yj∗​+∑i=jn​xi∗​, the interior recurrence gives Sj​−Sj+1​=0, and the terminal condition gives Sn​=0. The candidate is feasible, and the theorem proves it is the unique constrained optimal solution. Equivalently, strict convexity of the eliminated objective proves the same global conclusion.
