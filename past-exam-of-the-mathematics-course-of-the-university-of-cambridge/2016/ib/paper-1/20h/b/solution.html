An irreducible Markov chain has positive recurrent states if the expected first return time to a state is finite; this then holds for every state. A recurrent chain with infinite mean return time has null recurrent states.
An irreducible positive recurrent chain admits a stationary distribution. If such a distribution π existed for this walk, its stationarity equations would give
πj​=21​(πj−1​+πj+1​)(j∈Z).
(114)
Thus πj+1​−πj​=πj​−πj−1​, so πj​=A+Bj. Nonnegativity for all positive and negative integers forces B=0. A constant sequence cannot sum to one over Z, so no stationary probability distribution exists. Hence the walk is null recurrent, not positive recurrent: all its states are null recurrent states. The first-return generating function in part (c) also verifies directly that its mean return time is infinite.
