Evaluation at a real point defines a surjective ring homomorphism p↦p(a,b) from R[x,y] to R. Its kernel is (x−a,y−b): subtracting the constant p(a,b) expresses the remainder as a multiple of x−a plus a multiple of y−b, by successive polynomial division. The first isomorphism theorem therefore identifies the quotient ring with the field R, proving each Ia,b​ is maximal.
For a maximal ideal not on this list, take
J=(x2+1,y).​
(35)
Evaluation p(x,y)↦p(i,0) is onto C, and its kernel is J: first subtract a multiple of y, then divide the remaining polynomial in x by x2+1. Hence R[x,y]/J≅C, a field. Finally, if J=Ia,b​ for real a,b, the member x2+1∈J would vanish at (a,b), requiring a2+1=0, which is impossible over R.
