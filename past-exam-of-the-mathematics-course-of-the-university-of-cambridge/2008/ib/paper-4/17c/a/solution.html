The world lines in the train frame are xA​=L/2, xB​=−L/2 and xC​=−vt. In the station frame, length contraction gives
xA′​=vt′+2γL​,xB′​=vt′−2γL​,xC′​=0.
(64)
The emission event is O=(0,0) in both frames. The signal world lines in S′ are x′=ut′ and x′=−ut′; their intersections with the train endpoints define the reception events RA​,RB​. Their velocities in S, from the relativistic velocity-addition formula, are (u−v)/(1−uv/c2) and −(u+v)/(1+uv/c2).
The two platform-alignment events occur at (x′,t′)=(±L/(2γ),0) in S′. The Lorentz transformation puts them at (x,t)=(±L/2,∓vL/(2c2)) in S, on the tilted simultaneity line t=−vx/c2.
