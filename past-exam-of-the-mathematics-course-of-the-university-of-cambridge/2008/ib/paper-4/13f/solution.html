Two norms ∥⋅∥a​,∥⋅∥b​ are Lipschitz equivalent norms when constants c,C>0 satisfy c∥x∥a​≤∥x∥b​≤C∥x∥a​ for every vector x. Applying these inequalities to xj​−xk​ shows that a sequence is a Cauchy sequence for one norm exactly when it is so for the other; applying them to xj​−x likewise equates convergence. If one norm is complete, a Cauchy sequence for the other converges using the first norm and hence also the second. Interchanging the norms proves completeness is preserved in both directions.
