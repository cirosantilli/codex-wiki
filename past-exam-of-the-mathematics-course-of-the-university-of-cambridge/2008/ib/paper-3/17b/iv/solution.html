Vacuum Maxwell's equations are invariant under spatial rotations. Rotate the preceding coordinate system so that ez​ becomes the propagation direction k, and rotate the transverse electric direction to a unit polarization vector n. Dot and cross products transform covariantly under this rotation, so transversality and the field relation are unchanged. With ω=c∣k∣,
E(r,t)=E0​ei(k⋅r−ωt)n,B(r,t)=cE0​​ei(k⋅r−ωt)(k×n),k⋅n=0.​
(71)
These complex fields encode real fields through their real parts. The displayed form describes linear electromagnetic polarization; a general transverse complex amplitude can describe elliptical or circular electromagnetic polarization as well.
