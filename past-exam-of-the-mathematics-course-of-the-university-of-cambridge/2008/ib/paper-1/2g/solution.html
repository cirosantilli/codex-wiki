Let Q∈SO(3,R). Since QTQ=I and detQ=1,
det(Q−I)=detQdet(I−QT)=det(I−Q)=−det(Q−I),
(2)
so det(Q−I)=0. Choose a unit vector e with Qe=e. Its orthogonal complement is an invariant subspace: for v⊥e, e⋅Qv=(QTe)⋅v=e⋅v=0. In an orthonormal basis (e,e1​,e2​), Q therefore has a scalar block 1 and a two-dimensional orthogonal matrix block of determinant one. Such a block has columns (cosθ,sinθ)T and (−sinθ,cosθ)T. Consequently Q is a rotation in three dimensions about the axis Re through angle θ, including the identity when θ=0.
To construct the Householder reflections, let S0​ reflect in the plane spanned by e,e1​ and let Sψ​ reflect in the plane spanned by e,cosψe1​+sinψe2​. They fix e, while on e⊥ their matrices are
S0​=(10​0−1​),Sψ​=(cos2ψsin2ψ​sin2ψ−cos2ψ​).
(3)
Their product is the planar rotation matrix through 2ψ. Taking ψ=θ/2 proves Q=Sθ/2​S0​​. This gives the composition of two plane reflections explicitly; each is a Householder reflection I−2nnT for the corresponding unit plane normal n.
