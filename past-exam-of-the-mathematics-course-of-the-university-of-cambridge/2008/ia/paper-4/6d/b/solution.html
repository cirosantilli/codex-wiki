 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2008 ia Paper 4 6D b
OurBigBook.com Words: 105
Take a generator u of the multiplicative group of a finite field Fp×​, which has order p−1. Write x=ua. Since any possible nonzero base is y=ur, the condition of being a kth power is
x=yk⟺ua=ukr⟺kr≡a(modp−1).
(16)
By part (a), this linear congruence is soluble exactly when d=gcd(k,p−1) divides a. Meanwhile
x(p−1)/d=1⟺ua(p−1)/d=1⟺(p−1)∣a(p−1)/d⟺d∣a.
(17)
Thus the required test is
x is a kth power in Fp×​⟺x(p−1)/d≡1(modp).​
(18)
The exponent residue classes divisible by d are 0,d,…,p−1−d, so there are exactly (p−1)/d distinct nonzero kth powers. Equivalently, part (a) shows each value has exactly d preimages under the power map.
