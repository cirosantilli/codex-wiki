For the die, this is the coupon collector problem. When r scores are still missing, let Wr​ count rolls up to and including the next newly observed score. Every fresh roll has success probability r/6, so Wr​ has a geometric distribution of parameter r/6. These six successive waiting times are independent: conditional on the entire past at the start of a stage, its waiting-time distribution depends only on the number r, not on the identities of the missing scores. Iterated conditioning therefore factors their joint distribution. The total number of rolls is N=∑r=16​Wr​.
