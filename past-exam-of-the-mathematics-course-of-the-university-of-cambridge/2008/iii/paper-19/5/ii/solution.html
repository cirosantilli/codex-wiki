The product Riemannian metric has block matrix
g=(gM​(x)0​0gN​(y)​),g−1=(gM​(x)−10​0gN​(y)−1​).
(51)
In the given Christoffel symbols formula, every mixed coefficient vanishes. Off-diagonal metric entries are zero, ∂yα​gij​=0, and ∂xi​gαβ​=0. These facts give ΓiαA​=0 for every output index A, as well as Γijα​=0 and Γαβi​=0. The remaining pure blocks are precisely the Christoffel symbols of the respective factor metrics.
For the lifted fields of (i), ∂xi​Yα=0. Consequently
∇X​Y=∑i,α​Xi∂xi​Yα∂yα​+∑A,i,α​ΓiαA​XiYα∂A​=0.
(52)
Thus ∇X​Y=0​. The same calculation gives ∇Y​X=0; differentiating two fields from one factor gives the lifted factor covariant derivative. This identifies the product Levi-Civita connection with the product affine connection and proves the curvature splitting used in the root solution.
