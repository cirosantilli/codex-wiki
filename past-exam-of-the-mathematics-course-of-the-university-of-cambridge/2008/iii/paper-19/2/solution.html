If a vector bundle E→M has rank k, form the exterior power of a vector bundle fibrewise: its fibre at p is ΛrEp​. A vector bundle trivialization over U induces one with fibre ΛrRk. Transition matrices Aαβ​(p) induce ΛrAαβ​(p), which are smooth and obey the cocycle law because exterior powers preserve composition. They glue to a smooth bundle of rank (rk​); for r>k it is the zero bundle. At r=k it is the determinant line bundle.
A real line bundle is trivial precisely when it has a nowhere-zero smooth section: a section s gives the bundle isomorphism (p,t)↦ts(p) from M×R. Apply this to ΛnT∗M. Suppose first that an oriented atlas is given. Its local coordinate forms
να​=dxα1​∧⋯∧dxαn​
(11)
are positive multiples of one another on every overlap. Choose a subordinate smooth partition of unity ρα​≥0. Extend ρα​να​ by zero outside its chart and set ν=∑α​ρα​να​. Local finiteness makes this smooth. At any point at least one weight is positive, and all summands are nonnegative multiples of the same positive top form. Therefore ν never vanishes, proving triviality of the line bundle.
Conversely let ν be a nowhere-zero section of ΛnT∗M. In each sufficiently small connected chart write ν=aα​dxα1​∧⋯∧dxαn​. Its coefficient has constant nonzero sign. If necessary reverse the first coordinate, making aα​>0. On overlaps the equality of the two expressions gives
aα​det(∂xβ​∂xα​​)=aβ​,
(12)
so each transition determinant is positive. These charts cover M, proving
ΛnT∗M trivial⟺M admits an oriented atlas.​
(13)
For dimension zero both assertions hold with the empty exterior product equal to one.
For the orientability of a vector bundle total space, write π:E→M, with rank r and orientable base. The vertical tangent vectors at v∈Ep​ identify with Ep​ by differentiating translation in that fibre. The differential of π therefore gives the exact sequence of vector bundles on E
0⟶π∗E⟶TEdπ​π∗TM⟶0.
(14)
Taking determinant line bundles gives
detTE≅π∗(detE⊗detTM).
(15)
To see the determinant identification directly, wedge a vertical frame with any lifts of a base frame; altering a lift by a vertical vector does not change that top wedge. Since M is orientable, detTM is trivial. Consequently detTE≅π∗detE. If detE is trivial, so is its pullback, making the total space orientable. Conversely, if E is orientable as a manifold, its tangent determinant is trivial; restricting this isomorphism to the zero section gives triviality of detE. The tangent and cotangent determinant lines are dual, so either tests orientability. We obtain
E orientable as a manifold⟺ΛrE trivial,when M is orientable.​
(16)
For a cotangent bundle the base need not be orientable. If base coordinates change from x to y=y(x) with Jacobian J, fibre coordinates of the same covector change from ξ to η=J−Tξ. The full coordinate change on T∗M has block Jacobian
(J∗​0J−T​),detJdetJ−T=1>0.
(17)
Thus every induced cotangent coordinate change preserves orientation, even when the base change reverses it. These charts form an oriented atlas on the total space, proving every cotangent bundle is orientable. Equivalently the canonical symplectic form of the cotangent bundle has a nowhere-zero top exterior power, as in cotangent bundle orientation.
