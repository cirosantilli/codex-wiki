The age of an FLRW universe at an epoch is the elapsed cosmic time since the Big Bang, not the lookback time from today. For positive matter and positive cosmological constant, integrating dt=da/a˙ gives
t(a)=H0​1​∫0a​Ωm,0​+ΩΛ,0​a′3​a′​da′​.
(16)
Put u=ΩΛ,0​/Ωm,0​​a′3/2; then du=(3/2)ΩΛ,0​/Ωm,0​​a′​da′. The integral becomes the age of a flat matter-Lambda universe:
t(a)=3H0​ΩΛ,0​​2​∫0ΩΛ,0​/Ωm,0​​a3/2​1+u2​du​=3H0​ΩΛ,0​​2arsinh(ΩΛ,0​/Ωm,0​​a3/2)​.
(17)
Choose 0<θ<π/2 such that tanθ=Ωm,0​/ΩΛ,0​​a−3/2. The upper-limit value of u is cotθ, and
arsinh(cotθ)=log(cotθ+cscθ)=logsinθ1+cosθ​.
(18)
Consequently
t(z)=3H0​ΩΛ,0​​2​logsinθ1+cosθ​,tanθ=ΩΛ,0​Ωm,0​​​(1+z)3/2.​
(19)
The integration constant is fixed by t→0 as z→∞, where θ→π/2. Equivalently, differentiating u=cotθ changes the last integral to −∫dθ/sinθ, recovering the supplied trigonometric identity. The formula assumes positive ΩΛ,0​ and Ωm,0​; its zero-vacuum limit is regular even though this parametrization is singular.
