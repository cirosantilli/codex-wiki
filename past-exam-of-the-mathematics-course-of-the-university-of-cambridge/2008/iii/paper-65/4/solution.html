The given equation is a Maurer-Cartan equation, with the sign appropriate to the chosen invariant coframe. Write λ=(λij​) as an antisymmetric matrix of one-forms, so dλ=λ∧λ. Using the graded product rule for the exterior derivative,
d2λij​​=dλik​∧λkj​−λik​∧dλkj​=λiℓ​∧λℓk​∧λkj​−λik​∧λkℓ​∧λℓj​=0.​
(20)
The two sums coincide after exchanging their dummy indices k,ℓ. This is also associativity of matrix-valued wedge products of differential forms.
To make the relation to the Jacobi identity explicit, let θa be any independent coframe and write dθa=−21​cabc​θb∧θc. Its dual invariant vector fields satisfy [Eb​,Ec​]=cabc​Ea​, by the exterior derivative of a one-form evaluated on vector fields. Evaluating d2θa=0 on three basis fields gives the cyclic coefficient identity
cebc​caed​+cecd​caeb​+cedb​caec​=0.
(21)
These are exactly the components of [[Eb​,Ec​],Ed​]+[[Ec​,Ed​],Eb​]+[[Ed​,Eb​],Ec​]=0. Thus the structure constants obey the Jacobi identity.
For the real four-dimensional case, abbreviate a=λ12​, b=λ23​, c=λ31​, d=λ43​, e=λ41​, f=λ42​; here d without an argument is a one-form, whereas d(⋅) denotes the exterior derivative. Directly expanding the given equations gives
da=−b∧c−e∧f,dd=−e∧c−b∧f,​db=−c∧a+d∧f,de=−f∧a+d∧c,​dc=−a∧b−d∧e,df=−d∧b−a∧e.​
(22)
Define α1±​=a±d, α2±​=b±e, and α3±​=c±f. For example,
dα1+​=−b∧c−e∧f−e∧c−b∧f=−(b+e)∧(c+f).
(23)
The minus combination and the other two cyclic combinations give
dα1±​=−α2±​∧α3±​,dα2±​=−α3±​∧α1±​,dα3±​=−α1±​∧α2±​.​
(24)
The six forms are independent, since the original pairs are their half-sums and half-differences. Their dual vector fields Ei+​,Ei−​ consequently obey
[Ei±​,Ej±​]=ϵijk​Ek±​,[Ei+​,Ej−​]=0.
(25)
Each triple is a three-dimensional Lie algebra ideal with the SO(3) Lie algebra brackets; the two ideals commute and span the original six-dimensional algebra. This proves the real four-dimensional rotation algebra splitting
so(4)≅so(3)⊕so(3).​
(26)
This is a Lie-algebra statement. Globally SO(4) is a central quotient of SU(2)×SU(2); a direct product of the corresponding SO(3) groups is not asserted.
