For a general colored medium and unit plane-wave input E(0,s)=1, let UW​(x,0) be the propagator of ∂x​=iΔ⊥​/(2k)+ikμW(x,s). Then the solution is
Γ(x;s1​,s2​)=E[(UW​(x,0)1)(s1​)(UW​(x,0)1)(s2​)∗].
(37)
A time-ordered operator exponential defines UW​. Its expectation depends on the full longitudinal covariance; the initial Gaussian and transverse-stationarity assumptions do not specify a universal closed expression.
With the stated longitudinal Gaussian white noise and transverse statistical homogeneity, the moment depends only on ρ=s1​−s2​. Hence (Δ1​−Δ2​)Γ=0. The closed equation in the previous part and initial value Γ(0)=1 give
Γ(x,ρ)=exp{−k2μ2x[C(0)−C(ρ)]}.​
(38)
In particular Γ(x,0)=1: the ensemble mean intensity remains constant for plane-wave input, even though coherent attenuation in a white-noise random medium reduces the mean complex field.
Use a consistent transverse power spectrum convention,
C(ρ)=∫(2π)2d2κ​Φ(κ)eiκ⋅ρ.
(39)
For an isotropic spectrum, angular integration gives the isotropic transverse covariance spectrum, C(ρ)=(2π)−1∫0∞​Φ(κ)J0​(κρ)κdκ. Therefore
Γ(x,ρ)=exp[−2πk2μ2x​∫0∞​Φ(κ)(1−J0​(κρ))κdκ].​
(40)
The supplied two-dimensional Fourier transform definition has angular factor 2π, whereas the displayed radial transform pair omits it. The latter is a normalized Hankel transform pair. If its radial spectrum is denoted ΦH​=Φ/(2π), use ΦH​ in the integral and omit the prefactor 1/(2π). These are equivalent conventions; mixing them changes the attenuation rate.
