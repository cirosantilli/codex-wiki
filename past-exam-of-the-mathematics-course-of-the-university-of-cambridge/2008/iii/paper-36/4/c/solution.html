for a standard Brownian motion up to the image exit time. If that lifetime is finite, the stopped Brownian motion can be extended beyond it; only the stopped law is used here.
In this clock the image mapping-out functions obey the ordinary Chordal Loewner equation with driver 6​Bu​. Their generated trace therefore has the law of SLE with parameter six, stopped when it leaves N∗. Meanwhile the original image segment is exactly Φ(γ) up to exit from N. The clock change is increasing, so it disappears after passing to [γ]. This proves the local stopped-curve equality required in part (a).
