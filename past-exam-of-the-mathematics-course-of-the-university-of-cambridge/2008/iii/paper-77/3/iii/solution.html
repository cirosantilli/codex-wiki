Use unit knot spacing and the centered Cardinal cubic B-spline convention C(t)=∑i​Pi​M3​(t−i), where M3​ is supported on [−2,2]. On the span [i,i+1], with s=t−i, the active control points are Pi−1​,Pi​,Pi+1​,Pi+2​, and their B-spline weights are
(b0​,b1​,b2​,b3​)=61​((1−s)3,3s3−6s2+4,−3s3+3s2+3s+1,s3).
(10)
For t=11/4, take i=2, s=3/4. The weights and their derivatives are
(b0​,b1​,b2​,b3​)=3841​(1,121,235,27),(b0′​,b1′​,b2′​,b3′​)=321​(−1,−21,13,9).
(11)
The PDF has P3​=(0,0,0) and P4​=(0,1,0), so the point and tangent vector are
C(11/4)=384P1​+121P2​+235P3​+27P4​​,C′(11/4)=32−P1​−21P2​+13P3​+9P4​​.
(12)
Thus, under this centered convention,
C(11/4)=(19261​,967​,19261​),C′(11/4)=(−1611​,41​,−1611​).​
(13)
The original PDF specifies neither the knot origin nor its indexing relative to Pi​. If instead the convention associates span [i,i+1] with Pi​,Pi+1​,Pi+2​,Pi+3​, the identical local weights multiply P2​,P3​,P4​,P5​ at the requested parameter, giving
C(11/4)=(−19213​,192131​,967​),C′(11/4)=(−165​,1611​,41​).​
(14)
These are parameter translations of the same uniform construction, rather than contradictory evaluations with the same knots. A specified knot convention is necessary to select the numerical pair uniquely; changing the knot spacing would also rescale the derivative.
