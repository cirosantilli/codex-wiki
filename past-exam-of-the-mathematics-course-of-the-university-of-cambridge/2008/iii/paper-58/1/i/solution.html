Use base-two logarithms, so entanglement entropy is measured in bits. Write h2​(p)=−plog2​p−(1−p)log2​(1−p) for the binary entropy, with 0log2​0=0. Taking a partial trace over any two parties removes the off-diagonal terms: the two traced strings are orthogonal. Consequently all three one-party reduced density matrices of the first pure state are
ρA​=ρB​=ρC​=p∣0⟩⟨0∣+(1−p)∣1⟩⟨1∣.
(1)
Each division into two groups is one party versus the other two. The Schmidt coefficients are p​ and 1−p​, so the entanglement entropy across each of A:BC, B:AC and C:AB is h2​(p). For the second pure state, set p=1/2: every one-party reduced density matrix is I/2, and all three entanglement entropies are one bit.
For every pair XY, the first pure state instead gives the reduced density matrix
ρXY​=p∣00⟩⟨00∣+(1−p)∣11⟩⟨11∣.
(2)
This is a separable quantum state, explicitly a convex combination of two product states. Its relative entropy of entanglement is therefore zero: in ER​(ρ)=infσ∈Sep​D(ρ∥σ), choose σ=ρ to attain zero, while nonnegativity of quantum relative entropy excludes a negative value. The same argument with equal weights applies to the second pure state. Thus
ER​(ρAB​)=ER​(ρAC​)=ER​(ρBC​)=0for both states.​
(3)
The pairwise density matrices contain classical correlations, despite the entanglement of the global pure state; discarding a party loses the coherence between its two branches.
The entanglement entropy of n independent source copies across any cut is nh2​(p), whereas f(n) copies of the target GHZ state have entanglement entropy f(n). Average monotonicity of pure-state entanglement entropy under LOCC gives P(n)f(n)≤nh2​(p) for exact successful forward branches. In a reversible asymptotic conversion the reverse direction similarly prevents a first-order deficit, since n′/n→1. For asymptotically faithful outputs, a continuity bound for quantum conditional entropy with trivial conditioning changes these inequalities only by o(n). Hence the reversible leading-order rate is
f(n)=nh2​(p)+o(n),n→∞lim​nf(n)​=h2​(p).​
(4)
The zero pairwise relative entropies of entanglement are consistent with this conversion: no independent pairwise resource has to be supplied or removed.
There is a qualification to the literal finite-block formulation in the source. For 0<p<1, the Schmidt rank of the exact state of n′ source copies is 2n′, whereas f target GHZ states have Schmidt rank 2f. Every refined LOCC branch acts by a product of local linear operators and cannot increase this Schmidt rank. For p=1/2, the displayed rate has f<n′ for sufficiently large blocks when n′/n→1, so an exact successful reverse conversion is impossible, irrespective of its claimed probability. The exact dilution obstruction from Schmidt rank is avoided in the usual asymptotic formulation by allowing outputs approaching the desired pure state. The leading-order rate above and the exact forward protocol below remain valid; neither establishes the source's stronger exact round-trip claim. At p=1/2 the two states are identical. At p=0 or 1, the source is a known product state, the rate is zero, and local preparation supplies any desired number of source copies.
