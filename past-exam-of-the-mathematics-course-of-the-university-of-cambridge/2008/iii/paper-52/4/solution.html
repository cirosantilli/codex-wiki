Take ηαβ=diag(−1,1) with coordinate zero equal to τ and coordinate one to σ. In the supplied representation set
C=ρ0=(01​−10​),ρ1=(01​10​).
(22)
They satisfy the Clifford algebra {ρα,ρβ}=2ηαβ1. For anticommuting spinor components and an ordinary matrix B, relabelling the two summed indices gives
χTBψ=−ψTBTχ.
(23)
Now CT=−C, whereas Cρ0=−1 and Cρ1=diag(−1,1) are symmetric. Therefore the Majorana Grassmann bilinear interchange yields
ψˉ​1ψ2=ψˉ​2ψ1,ψˉ​1ραψ2=−ψˉ​2ραψ1.​
(24)
These signs require fermionic, Grassmann-odd fields; commuting spinors would have the opposite interchange signs.
In flat conformal gauge, suppress the common factor (4πα′)−1 and write the combined density as L=−∂α​X⋅∂αX+ψˉ​⋅ρα∂α​ψ. The supersymmetry variation including its odd parameter is an even derivation. From (ρα)TC=−Cρα we obtain
δψˉ​=−ϵˉρβ∂β​X.
(25)
Also (Cραρβ)T=−Cρβρα, so interchanging odd spinors gives χˉ​ραρβϵ=ϵˉρβραχ. The two density variations, with target contractions understood, are therefore
δLB​δLF​​=2ϵˉ∂β​X⋅∂βψ,=−ϵˉρβρα∂β​X⋅∂α​ψ+ϵˉψ⋅□X.​
(26)
In the second term the symmetric second derivative annihilates the antisymmetric part of ρβρα. Using 2ηαβ−ρβρα=ραρβ now gives the exact off-shell identity
δL=∂α​(ϵˉραρβψ⋅∂β​X).​
(27)
Thus the action is invariant when this boundary flux vanishes and the transformation preserves the field boundary conditions, for example with suitable falloff or compatible supersymmetric endpoint conditions. On a spatial circle, periodic fields admit the constant parameter; antiperiodic fermions with periodic bosons require the local superconformal treatment, since a nonzero constant parameter would make the boson variation antiperiodic. The constant parameter and ordinary derivatives describe rigid supersymmetry in flat gauge; a locally supersymmetric curved-worldsheet formulation includes the worldsheet gravitino. The result is the rigid worldsheet supersymmetry boundary term without using field equations.
For the commutator on X, keep the order of the odd parameters:
[δ1​,δ2​]X=(ϵˉ1​ραϵ2​−ϵˉ2​ραϵ1​)∂α​X=ℓα∂α​X​,ℓα=2ϵˉ1​ραϵ2​.​
(28)
For the fermion, the order is equally important. Moving ϵ2​ through both odd factors in (ϵˉ1​∂α​ψ)ϵ2​ incurs two minus signs. Consequently
[δ1​,δ2​]ψ=ραM∂α​ψ,M=ϵ1​ϵˉ2​−ϵ2​ϵˉ1​.
(29)
To check the spinor matrix explicitly, set ϵ1​=(a,b)T, ϵ2​=(c,d)T, with all four components anticommuting and monomials ordered a,b,c,d. Then
M=(ad+bc2bd​−2ac−ad−bc​),V0=ϵˉ1​ρ0ϵ2​=−ac−bd,V1=ϵˉ1​ρ1ϵ2​=−ac+bd.
(30)
Direct multiplication gives ραM+Mρα=2Vα1. Hence
[δ1​,δ2​]ψ=2Vα∂α​ψ−M(ρα∂α​ψ).
(31)
Variation of the fermion action gives the Dirac equation ρα∂α​ψ=0. On this equation the second term vanishes, proving
[δ1​,δ2​]ψ=ℓα∂α​ψ,ℓ0=−2(ac+bd),ℓ1=2(bd−ac).​
(32)
This is on-shell closure of rigid worldsheet supersymmetry; the action's invariance was off shell, but its fermionic transformation algebra closes in this field content only on shell.
The associated superconformal current is proportional to ψ⋅∂X, of conformal weight 3/2. Its fermionic modes Gr​ extend the stress-tensor generators to the N=1 super-Virasoro algebra:
[Lm​,Gr​]=(2m​−r)Gm+r​,{Gr​,Gs​}=2Lr+s​+3c​(r2−41​)δr+s,0​.
(33)
The modes are half-integral in the Neveu–Schwarz sector and integral in the Ramond sector. Their anticommutator giving a Virasoro generator is the conformal-mode counterpart of the translation commutator just derived. Ten coordinate bosons and ten Majorana fermions give matter central charge c=10+10/2=15, canceled by the usual reparametrization and superconformal ghosts in the critical RNS string.
