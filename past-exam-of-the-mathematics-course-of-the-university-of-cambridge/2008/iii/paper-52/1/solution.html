The local gauge symmetries of the Polyakov action are worldsheet diffeomorphisms and Weyl transformations. Under a reparametrization σα↦σ′α, the embedding coordinates Xμ transform as scalars and the worldsheet metric as a covariant tensor; the contracted integrand is a scalar density. Infinitesimally, with the active convention, δX=−ξα∂α​X and δγ=−Lξ​γ. A Weyl transformation sends γαβ​↦e2ωγαβ​ and leaves X fixed. In two dimensions the factors from −γ​ and γαβ cancel. Target-space Poincaré transformations are additional global symmetries, rather than worldsheet gauge symmetries.
These gauge freedoms put the metric locally in conformal gauge, γαβ​=ηαβ​. Its variation must still be imposed: it gives the worldsheet stress tensor constraints T++​=T−−​=0. They act on the oscillator Fock space, and physical spurious (null) states are quotiented out. Equivalently physical states are the suitable BRST cohomology classes. Positive modes constrain kets; their adjoints constrain bras, so one does not impose every positive and negative matter mode on the same ket. Subtracting the two zero-mode equations gives closed-string level matching, N=N, and adding their mass-shell content gives M2=4(N−1)/α′. Quantum consistency fixes the intercept to one and, for just these flat coordinate fields, d=26: the matter central charge d cancels the reparametrization ghost central charge −26. Other dimensions require additional worldsheet degrees of freedom or a noncritical completion.
For the residual symmetry, take σ±=τ±σ. The conformal Killing equations leave independent transformations σ+↦f(σ+) and σ−↦f​(σ−), with a compensating Weyl rescaling restoring the flat metric. The two-dimensional quantum-gravity interpretation of strings follows from what is integrated, not from an analogy: at genus h, the Polyakov path integral integrates over both X and the metric, divided by diffeomorphism and Weyl gauge volume. String perturbation theory sums these surface topologies, weighted by gs2h−2​ before external-state coupling factors. Gauge fixing leaves worldsheet ghost determinants and integrals over worldsheet moduli. Thus it is two-dimensional quantum gravity coupled to the d coordinate matter fields. A flat gauge is local; general higher-genus surfaces retain conformal-structure moduli and need not admit a globally flat representative.
Target-space gravity is a different conclusion. At N=N=1, the state ϵμν​α−1μ​α−1ν​∣k⟩ is massless, transverse in both indices, and has longitudinal gauge identifications. Its symmetric traceless transverse polarization is a graviton; the antisymmetric and scalar polarizations give the Kalb–Ramond field and dilaton. The graviton gauge identification is precisely the linearized metric diffeomorphism. Moreover the leading string-frame massless effective action contains ∫−G​e−2ΦR, and worldsheet Weyl invariance with constant dilaton and zero two-form requires Rμν​=0 at leading order in α′. Thus both the propagating massless spin-two state and its low-energy interactions reproduce gravity. Closed strings contain the graviton and yield Einstein gravity in their low-energy massless sector, together with other fields and higher-derivative corrections; the bosonic tachyon instability remains.
