Replace the two arms by three identical arms of length N attached to one central qubit. Label the hub c and the sites on arm a by (a,j), a=1,2,3, j=1,…,N. Use XY exchange interactions with coupling J0​/3​ on each hub-to-first-site link, and coupling Jj​ on each arm link (a,j) to (a,j+1). Initially excite only the hub: this is the product state ∣1⟩c​ with every arm qubit in ∣0⟩.
In the symmetric single-excitation subspace, define ∣s0​⟩=∣c⟩ and ∣sj​⟩=3−1/2∑a=13​∣a,j⟩. The hub coupling is
H∣s0​⟩=3​J0​​∑a=13​∣a,1⟩=J0​∣s1​⟩,
(28)
and the reverse coupling has the same coefficient. All other radial links have coefficients Jj​, just as in part (c). Thus the symmetric-arm reduction of an exchange Hamiltonian is again exactly HT​. At time π/2 its evolution is
∣s0​⟩⟼∣sN​⟩=3​∣1,N⟩+∣2,N⟩+∣3,N⟩​.
(29)
Every nontip qubit is zero, so tracing them out leaves the three tips in the pure W state
∣W3​⟩=3​∣100⟩+∣010⟩+∣001⟩​.​
(30)
The essential change is the additional identical arm and the normalization of each hub coupling by 3​ instead of 2​.
