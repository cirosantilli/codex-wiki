The state in part (b) is symmetric in the receiving qubits. Single out Bob using the Dicke state recursion. For k≥1, the two vectors are
∣DkN​⟩∣Dk+1N​⟩​=Nk+1​​∣0⟩∣DkN−1​⟩+Nk​​∣1⟩∣Dk−1N−1​⟩,=Nk​​∣0⟩∣Dk+1N−1​⟩+Nk+1​​∣1⟩∣DkN−1​⟩.​
(6)
Distinct excitation counts give orthogonal states of the other recipients. Taking their partial trace, the only surviving off-diagonal term comes from the common rest vector ∣DkN−1​⟩. The one-qubit reduction of Dicke-state superpositions is consequently
ρ=N1​(k+∣α∣2(k+1)α∗β​(k+1)αβ∗k+∣β∣2​),k=2N−1​.​
(7)
Put p=∣α∣2 and q=∣β∣2, so p+q=1. The overlap with the input is
⟨ψ∣ρ∣ψ⟩​=Np(k+p)+q(k+q)+2(k+1)pq​=Nk+p2+q2+2(k+1)pq​=Nk+1+2kpq​=2NN+1+2∣α∣2∣β∣2(N−1)​​.​
(8)
For N=1 there is no system to trace out, and the same matrix is ∣ψ⟩⟨ψ∣, with overlap one. For every N>1 the overlap is below one, even at its maximum p=q=1/2, so these are imperfect copies.
