During radiation domination, the effective number of relativistic energy degrees of freedom, denoted g∗​, gives the energy density
ρR​=30π2​g∗​T4.
(31)
The paper's Neff​ is this g∗​; it is not the conventionally normalized effective number of neutrino species. With the specified reduced Planck mass, the Friedmann equation reads H2=ρR​/(3MPl2​), whence
H=90π2g∗​​​MPl​T2​.
(32)
Write the weak interaction conversion rate as Γ=AGF2​T5. The ratio Γ/H scales as T3, so it falls during cooling: conversion eventually becomes too slow to maintain neutron-proton chemical equilibrium. Equating the conversion and expansion rates gives
TF​=[AGF2​MPl​π2g∗​/90​​]1/3.
(33)
On suppressing the numerical coefficient, the cosmological weak freeze-out temperature is consequently
TF​∼GF−2/3​Neff1/6​MPl−1/3​.​
(34)
The one-sixth power reflects a balance between a conversion rate proportional to T5 and a Hubble parameter proportional to g∗​​T2.
