Use units with c=1. For the flat FRW metric, the Friedmann equation is H2=8πGρ/3+Λ/3, where H=a˙/a is the Hubble parameter. The cosmological continuity equation for a separately conserved component with constant equation of state P=wρ gives ρ∝a−3(1+w). Consequently radiation in cosmology scales as a−4, pressureless matter as a−3, and cosmological constant energy is constant. With a0​=1, define the present critical density and cosmological density parameters by
ρcrit,0​=8πG3H02​​,Ωr​=ρcrit,0​ρr,0​​,Ωm​=ρcrit,0​ρm,0​​,ΩΛ​=3H02​Λ​.
(1)
The Hubble constant H0​ measures the expansion rate today. Here Ωr​ includes photons and relativistic neutrinos, Ωm​ includes baryons and cold dark matter, and ΩΛ​ measures the vacuum contribution. Spatial flatness implies Ωr​+Ωm​+ΩΛ​=1. Thus
H2=H02​(Ωr​a−4+Ωm​a−3+ΩΛ​).
(2)
Since conformal time satisfies dτ=dt/a, we have a′=aa˙=a2H. Multiplication by a4 proves
a′2=H02​(Ωr​+Ωm​a+ΩΛ​a4).​
(3)
For an exam-level estimate, H0​=100hkms−1Mpc−1 with h≃0.7, Ωm​≃0.3, ΩΛ​≃0.7, and the specified radiation in cosmology gives Ωr​=4.2×10−5h−2≃8.6×10−5. These are rounded illustrative flat-universe values; precision cosmological density parameters depend on the fitted model. For comparison, the base flat-model fit in Planck 2018 cosmological parameters gives H0​≃67.4kms−1Mpc−1 and Ωm​≃0.315.
