Floquet theory suggests a sequence of instability tongues near
ω=2m​,m=1,2,3,…,tongue width O(ϵm).​
(24)
At these limiting frequencies the unperturbed Floquet multipliers e±2πiω coincide. Each multiplication by cost changes a Fourier frequency by one, so m forcing interactions are needed to couple +m/2 and −m/2. This explains both the sequence of parametric resonances and the successive powers of ϵ in their widths.
There is no implication that the exact half-integers themselves lie in every finite-ϵ growing interval. The tongue centers generally shift by O(ϵ2), which exceeds the width when m>2. It is the narrow intervals near the shifted centers that contain exponentially growing solutions. Frequencies staying a fixed distance from every half-integer are stable for sufficiently small ϵ.
