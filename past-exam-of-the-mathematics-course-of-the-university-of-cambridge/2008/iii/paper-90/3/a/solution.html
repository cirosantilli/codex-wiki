Hindered settling is the reduction of the particle settling velocity by displaced-fluid backflow and interactions with other particles. In the specified model, the downward speed is Vs​ at vanishing concentration and falls to zero at the maximum packing fraction ϕmax​. The characteristic speed of a concentration disturbance will differ from this individual-particle speed.
Take z positive upwards. For a locally uniform horizontal suspension without diffusion or bulk fluid motion, the upward particle flux is
j(ϕ)=−ϕV(ϕ)=−Vs​ϕ+ϕmax​Vs​​ϕ2.

(30)
This is the quadratic hindered-settling flux. Along its characteristic curves,
dtdϕ​=0,dtdz​=c(ϕ)=−Vs​+ϕmax​2Vs​ϕ​.​

when the minimum is negative and no earlier boundary discontinuity is imposed. A discontinuous compressive initial state can instead contain a shock from t=0.
