Use the derivative to transport the planes:
ϕt∗​((x,v),L)=(ϕt​(x,v),Dϕt​(x,v)L).​
(38)
The derivative is an invertible linear map, so it sends a two-dimensional subspace to a two-dimensional subspace. The flow identity ϕt​∘ϕs​=ϕt+s​ gives
Dϕt​F(x,v)=F(ϕt​(x,v)).
(39)
Hence a plane containing the generator is sent to another plane containing the generator; the displayed formula is a well-defined smooth lift to Λ(SM). The chain rule gives ϕt+s∗​=ϕt∗​∘ϕs∗​, and ϕ0∗​ is the identity. Thus it is a genuine lifted smooth flow, with inverse ϕ−t∗​, rather than merely a family of maps of individual fibres. Its generator is F∗=∂t​ϕt∗​∣t=0​.
