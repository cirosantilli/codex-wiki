OurBigBook.com Words: 144
Let π:SM→M be the unit tangent bundle projection. Write X for the geodesic flow generator and V for the vertical vector field of a surface unit tangent bundle, positively rotating v toward iv. The acceleration prescribed in the question changes only this velocity angle, so the surface thermostat generator is
F=X+λV.
(36)
It never vanishes: dπ(F)=v=0. Also F and V are linearly independent, since dπ(V)=0.
The vertical Maslov cycle of a surface thermostat is
ΛV​={((x,v),L):V(x,v)∈L}={((x,v),RF(x,v)⊕RV(x,v))}.​
(37)
There is exactly one such plane in each fibre, so ΛV​ is the image of a smooth section. A two-plane containing F corresponds to a line in the rank-two quotient T(SM)/RF; thus Λ(SM) is a projective bundle with fibre RP1. Its total dimension is four and the cycle has dimension three, making it a smooth hypersurface. In this two-dimensional transverse space the Maslov cycle means the projective line meeting the distinguished vertical line, which here is just that distinguished line itself in each fibre.
