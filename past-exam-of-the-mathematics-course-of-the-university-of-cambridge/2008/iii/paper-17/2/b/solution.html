Evaluate the smooth one-form on unit velocities to obtain f(x,v)=θx​(v). Its integral along a periodic orbit of the geodesic flow is exactly the one-form integral along the corresponding closed geodesic. Negative curvature makes this geodesic flow an Anosov flow; the geodesic flow of a connected closed negatively curved surface is also topologically transitive.
