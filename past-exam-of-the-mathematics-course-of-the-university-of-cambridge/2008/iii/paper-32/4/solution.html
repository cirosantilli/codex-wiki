Realize the two-dimensional irreducible representation ρ as the standard representation of the symmetric group on
V={(z1​,z2​,z3​)∈C3:z1​+z2​+z3​=0},
(25)
with S3​ permuting coordinates. It is the complement of the trivial summand in the three-point permutation representation. For each prime the local factor of an Artin L-function is
Lp​(ρ,s)=det(1−p−sρ(Frobp​)∣VIp​​)−1,
(26)
where Ip​ is the inertia group and Frobenius means any lift of the residue-field Frobenius automorphism. On VIp​ the lift is independent of that choice. At an unramified prime, put t=p−s. The Local Artin factors of the standard representation of S3 are
Frobenius cycle type(1)(2)(3)(12)(123)​eigenvalues on V1,11,−1ζ3​,ζ32​​Lp​(ρ,s)(1−t)−2(1−t2)−1(1+t+t2)−1.​​
(27)
The polynomial discriminant of X3−3 is −243=−35, so only 3 can ramify in its splitting field. For p=3, the squarefree factorization of X3−3 modulo p determines the Frobenius cycle type on its roots.
At p=2,
X3−3≡(X+1)(X2+X+1)(mod2).
(28)
The quadratic is irreducible, so Frobenius is a transposition and
L2​(ρ,s)=1−2−2s1​=1+2−2s+2−4s+⋯.
(29)
Thus a2​=0, a4​=1 and a8​=0. At p=5,
X3−3≡(X−2)(X2+2X+4)(mod5).
(30)
The quadratic discriminant is 3(mod5), a nonsquare, so this is again a transposition. Consequently L5​(ρ,s)=(1−5−2s)−1 and a5​=0.
At p=7, the possible cubes are 0,1,−1, so X3−3 has no root modulo 7. Being cubic, it is irreducible; Frobenius is a three-cycle. Therefore
L7​(ρ,s)=1+7−s+7−2s1​=1−7−3s1−7−s​=1−7−s+O(7−3s),
(31)
and a7​=−1.
The ramified factor at 3 must be computed on inertia group invariants. Write α=31/3 and set π=(ζ3​−1)/α. Using (ζ3​−1)2=−3ζ3​ gives
π3=1+2ζ3​,π6=−3.
(32)
The Eisenstein polynomial X6+3 over Q3​ has degree six, so Q3​(π) is a totally ramified degree-six subfield of Q3​(ζ3​,α). The latter has degree at most [F:Q]=6, so the fields agree. This Eisenstein sextic presentation of the splitting field of X3 minus 3 over Q3 shows D3​=I3​=S3​. Since the only permutation-invariant vectors in C3 have all coordinates equal, their intersection with V is zero. Thus
VI3​=0,L3​(ρ,s)=1.​
(33)
In particular a3​=a9​=0; an unramified degree-two factor at 3 would give the wrong coefficients.
Finally the Euler product makes its Dirichlet series coefficients multiplicative for coprime indices. Its constant coefficient is a1​=1, and a6​=a2​a3​=0, a10​=a2​a5​=0. Every integer up to ten is now covered, giving
(a1​,a2​,a3​,a4​,a5​,a6​,a7​,a8​,a9​,a10​)=(1,0,0,1,0,0,−1,0,0,0).​
(34)
