For ϵ>0, the quartic interaction has a nonzero limiting value λ∗​=O(ϵ) instead of disappearing under coarse-graining. Long-wavelength thermal fluctuations are therefore interacting, so the fluctuation-free Landau approximation cannot give the generic critical powers. For example, its ordinary correlation-length critical exponent is 1/2, whereas the relevant thermal eigenvalue just derived gives

The tadpole is momentum independent, so the anomalous dimension begins at O(ϵ2). Combining this with the scaling relations yields γ=1+ϵ/6+O(ϵ2), β=1/2−ϵ/6+O(ϵ2), and α=ϵ/6+O(ϵ2), exhibiting the deviation from ordinary mean-field indices. These are controlled statements for small positive ϵ, not exact numerical exponents at arbitrary dimension. The Landau-Ginzburg theory itself is not invalid: integrating its fluctuations is precisely how the Wilson-Fisher fixed point was obtained. The invalid step is replacing that interacting long-distance theory by a uniform saddle and ignoring its fluctuation corrections.
