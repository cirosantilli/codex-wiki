For k>0, the surface presentation
π1​(Σ2​)=⟨a1​,b1​,a2​,b2​∣[a1​,b1​][a2​,b2​]=1⟩
(26)
has an epimorphism to Z/k sending a1​ to 1 and the other generators to zero. Its kernel has index k. By the classification of connected covering spaces, it determines a connected k-sheeted cover p:Σ→Σ2​. Pull back the orientation, so each local sheet has degree +1 and degp=k. Counting lifts of cells gives χ(Σ)=kχ(Σ2​)=−2k, hence 2−2g=−2k and g=k+1.
For k<0, use the ∣k∣-sheeted cover and compose with an orientation-reversing diffeomorphism of Σ2​, for instance reflection in a symmetric embedded genus-two surface. By multiplicativity of mapping degree, the degree becomes −∣k∣=k. For k=0 take a constant map from the torus. Thus the arbitrary-degree maps to a surface of genus two can be chosen with
g(k)=∣k∣+1,Σ∣k∣+1​⟶Σ2​ of degree k(k∈Z).​
