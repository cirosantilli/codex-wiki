For a real Lie group G, its Lie algebra is the real tangent space Te​G at the identity. Each tangent vector extends uniquely to a left-invariant vector field, and the commutator of those fields defines the Lie bracket. For a matrix group this bracket is [X,Y]=XY−YX.
For a complex affine algebraic group, the Lie algebra of an affine algebraic group is the complex Zariski tangent space Te​G=HomC​(me​/me2​,C). Its bracket is the commutator of left-invariant derivations. In a faithful matrix realization, one can compute the tangent space using dual numbers: X belongs to it exactly when I+εX satisfies the group equations modulo ε2. This is a complex Lie algebra, whereas compact groups such as Un​ and SUn​ have real Lie algebras.

Their dimensions are respectively n2,n2,n2−1,n2−1, with real dimensions for the first and third and complex dimensions for the second and fourth. In every case the Lie bracket is the matrix commutator.
An irreducible complex affine algebraic group is connected. It is a reductive algebraic group when its unipotent radical Ru​(G), the largest connected normal unipotent subgroup, is trivial. This is an algebraic condition; in characteristic zero it also implies complete reducibility of finite-dimensional rational representations, as proved in Question 2.
Examples are algebraic tori, GLn​(C) and SLn​(C), and products of these groups. A diagonal matrix that is unipotent must be the identity, so a torus has no nontrivial unipotent subgroup. For the two matrix groups, let N be a normal unipotent subgroup. The Kolchin theorem supplies a nonzero fixed vector in their standard representation. The subspace fixed by N is invariant under the whole group: for g∈G, n(gv)=g(g−1ng)v=gv. The standard representation is irreducible, so every vector is fixed by N; its faithfulness makes N trivial. A normal unipotent subgroup of a product projects to such a subgroup of each factor, proving the product assertion. By contrast the additive group and a nontrivial upper-unitriangular group are not reductive: their unipotent radicals are the whole group.
