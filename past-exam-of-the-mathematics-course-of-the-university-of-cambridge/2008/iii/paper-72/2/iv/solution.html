vˉϕ​(R)=[Vc2​(R)−σ2(R)R(Rd​1​+Rσ​1​)]1/2.

The sketch shows the cold gas following Vc​, rising in the inner galaxy and becoming approximately flat, while the stellar curve sits below it. Only radii R≥1kpc are used for the stellar approximation; a central galactic bulge needs a separate model.
At R=8kpc, A≃4.22 and σ=30kms−1, giving gas speed about 220kms−1, stellar speed about 211kms−1, and a lag about 9kms−1. These are representative scales for the requested schematic, not a fit to the Milky Way. Hotter old stellar populations would have a larger lag: doubling this dispersion at the same radius gives a stellar speed about 182kms−1. The specified radial laws and isotropy make the example reproducible while retaining the qualifications in the preceding derivation.
