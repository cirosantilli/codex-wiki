For the defining representation, the standard coordinate vectors e1​,e2​,e3​,e4​,e5​ are weight vectors of weights ε1​,ε2​,−ε1​,−ε2​,0, respectively. Thus the weight diagram has four axial points and the origin, each of multiplicity one.
For the Adjoint representation, the eight nonzero weights of a representation are the roots from part (ii), each of multiplicity one, with the displayed Xα​ as basis. The zero weight space is the Cartan subalgebra, with basis
H1​=diag(1,0,−1,0,0),H2​=diag(0,1,0,−1,0).
(36)
The Adjoint representation diagram therefore has the four axial points, four diagonal points, and the origin of multiplicity two. The multiplicities sum to ten, as they must.
