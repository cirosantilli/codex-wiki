The delta replication from a local-volatility pricing equation uses the asset-unit holdings
htS​=VS​(t,St​),htB​=Bt​V(t,St​)−St​VS​(t,St​)​.​
(33)
Their wealth is exactly V(t,St​). Moreover,
htB​dBt​+htS​dSt​=rV(t,St​)dt+σ(St​)St​VS​(t,St​)dWt​=dV(t,St​),
(34)
where the final equality is the calculation in part (i). Thus the holdings are a self-financing strategy with terminal wealth g(ST​), proving replication. Since V≥0, this replicating strategy has nonnegative wealth and is an admissible trading strategy. The equality of trading gains is unchanged by replacing the drift with its physical-measure value: the stock drift in Itô formula and in the stock holding changes by the same amount. If πt​ denotes wealth fractions, then, wherever V>0, its stock fraction is St​VS​/V and its bank fraction is 1−St​VS​/V.
