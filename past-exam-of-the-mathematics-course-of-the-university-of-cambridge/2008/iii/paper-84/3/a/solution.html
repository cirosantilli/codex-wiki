Take Q as the fluid volume flux per unit source length into one side of the aquifer. For a symmetric release with total rate Q, use Q/2 on each side. Introduce a constant porosity ϕ to distinguish Darcy velocity from the advecting interstitial velocity; if the given flux is already normalized to pore volume, this factor is absorbed in its definition. This depth-averaged equation requires transverse equilibration and longitudinal concentration variations slow enough for the cell expansion.
