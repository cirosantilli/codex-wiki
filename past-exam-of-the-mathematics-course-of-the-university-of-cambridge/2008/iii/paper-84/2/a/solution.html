Let k1​>k2​ and let both original and injected fluids initially have dynamic viscosity μ. Assume sharp displacement fronts, constant porosity and negligible exchange between layers. The imposed pressure difference gives Darcy velocities qi0​=ki​ΔP/(μL) and interstitial velocities ui0​=qi0​/ϕ. The two arrival times are therefore
ta​=k1​ΔPϕμL2​,ts​=k2​ΔPϕμL2​=k2​k1​​ta​.​
(12)
If Ai​ is the flow cross-section of layer i, its volume discharge is Qi0​=Ai​qi0​. Equal viscosities make the hydraulic resistance independent of front position, so the total fluid discharge Q10​+Q20​ stays constant. The injected-fluid component at the outflow well is
Qinj​(t)=Q10​1t≥ta​​+Q20​1t≥ts​​.
(13)
It jumps from zero to the high-permeability discharge at the first breakthrough, then to the total discharge at the second. Before a layer breaks through, its outflow is original reservoir fluid. Cross-sections are needed to state an absolute volume discharge or its partition; the arrival times do not require them.
