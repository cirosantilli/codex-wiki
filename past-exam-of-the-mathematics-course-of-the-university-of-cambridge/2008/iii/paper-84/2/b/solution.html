The fractional resistance increase is larger in the fast layer. An effective selective regime is Δt<ta​ and Δt<τ<ta​, with (λ−1)Δt/ta​ appreciable while (λ−1)Δt/ts​ is small; the full delay model handles the activation ramps and the later exits. The relative share entering the slow layer then increases, although its absolute discharge cannot increase at fixed pressure. The total discharge decreases during polymer occupancy and returns to its original value once the activated slugs leave. A fixed-total-injection-rate experiment would have a different pressure response and cannot be substituted for this fixed-pressure model.
