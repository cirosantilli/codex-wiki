Label the incoming momenta and spin states (p1​,r1​),(p2​,r2​) and the outgoing ones (p3​,r3​),(p4​,r4​), with p1​+p2​=p3​+p4​. Write ui​=uri​(pi​) and vi​=vri​(pi​). The Mandelstam variables are s=(p1​+p2​)2, t=(p1​−p3​)2 and u=(p1​−p4​)2. At lowest order, each connected Feynman diagram has two pseudoscalar Yukawa interaction vertices and one internal scalar propagator.
Define the scattering amplitude by Sficonnected​=i(2π)4δ4(p1​+p2​−p3​−p4​)M. Fix the external Fock state ordering as b1†​b2†​∣0⟩ to b3†​b4†​∣0⟩ for the two-fermion process. The t exchange connects 1 to 3 and 2 to 4; the u exchange connects 1 to 4 and 2 to 3. The two Feynman vertices contribute (−iλ)2, and the internal scalar propagator contributes i divided by its denominator. Exchanging the two identical final fermions supplies a relative fermionic sign. Thus
Mψψ​=−λ2[t−μ2+i0(uˉ3​γ5u1​)(uˉ4​γ5u2​)​−u−μ2+i0(uˉ4​γ5u1​)(uˉ3​γ5u2​)​].​
(26)
This changes sign when the two outgoing labels are exchanged, as identical-fermion antisymmetry requires. There is no s annihilation diagram for two incoming fermions: the interaction preserves Dirac fermion number conservation.
For the fermion-antifermion process take initial state b1†​d2†​∣0⟩ and final state b3†​d4†​∣0⟩. The t exchange uses bilinears (uˉ3​γ5u1​)(vˉ2​γ5v4​), and the s annihilation uses (vˉ2​γ5u1​)(uˉ3​γ5v4​). To fix the sign without a guess, expand the current J=:ψˉ​γ5ψ: in creation and annihilation operators. Its relevant terms have the signs
J⊃b†buˉγ5u−d†dvˉγ5v+dbvˉγ5u+b†d†uˉγ5v.
(27)
The antifermion sign of a normal-ordered bilinear makes the exchange contraction negative, whereas the product of annihilation and creation contractions is positive for the specified external ordering. Multiplying by (−iλ)2i=−iλ2 therefore gives
Mψψˉ​​=λ2[t−μ2+i0(uˉ3​γ5u1​)(vˉ2​γ5v4​)​−s−μ2+i0(vˉ2​γ5u1​)(uˉ3​γ5v4​)​].​
(28)
A different common phase convention for an external state can reverse the overall amplitude, but never the relative sign of its two contributions. Both results are tree scattering with pseudoscalar exchange and are of order λ2.
