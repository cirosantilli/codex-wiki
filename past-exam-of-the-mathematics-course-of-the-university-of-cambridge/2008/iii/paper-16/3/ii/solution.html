A dependence on torsion is necessary if a bound uniform over all finite abelian groups is intended. In G=F2m​, choose independent random signs f(x). A cube with eight distinct vertices has sign product of expectation zero. Each of the twenty-eight possible collisions has probability 1/∣G∣, so the expectation of ∥f∥U38​ is at most 28/∣G∣. Some sign function therefore has norm at most (28/∣G∣)1/8. But with factors (f,1,f,1), every arithmetic progression is (x,x+d,x,x+d) and its product is f(x)2=1. Thus unbounded torsion obstructs uniform control of four-term progressions. The stated fixed-group bound, and the uniform cyclic-group bound needed in part (iii), are unaffected.
