For a normalized state ∣ψ(tF​;f)⟩ driven by the admissible control f, maximize
JA​[f]=⟨ψ(tF​;f)∣A∣ψ(tF​;f)⟩.​
(16)
Here A is the specified Hermitian operator, tF​ the final time, and the dependence on f is through the Schrödinger equation. The objective is real and is exactly the desired expectation value, so increasing it increases the requested observable. A control-resource penalty may be included when the admissible set does not already constrain field strength or fluence.
