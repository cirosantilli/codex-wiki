Use pulse areas in the convention θk​=∫Ωk​dt/ℏ, with the other channel switched off for a single-channel pulse. Substituting θ=π/2 into part (c) shows
W1​=U1​(π/2,π/2),W2​=U2​(π/2,−π/2).​
(13)
Here the arguments denote area and phase. For W1​, the off-diagonal factors are −ie−iπ/2=−1 and −ieiπ/2=1. For W2​, the phase −π/2 reverses those two signs. Thus these choices implement the gates exactly on all basis states, not only on one population.
To implement W4​, let P1​=U1​(π/2,0) and P2​=U2​(π/2,0) and apply channel 1, channel 2, then channel 1 again. Each active swap contributes −i, so direct tracking gives
P1​P2​P1​∣1⟩=−∣3⟩,P1​P2​P1​∣2⟩=−∣2⟩,P1​P2​P1​∣3⟩=−∣1⟩.
(14)
Hence W4​=P1​P2​P1​​, including the overall minus sign and middle-state phase actually printed in the PDF. Alternatively equal zero-phase envelopes give H(t)=Ω(t)C, where C=E12​+E21​+E23​+E32​. Since C3=2C, its exponential is I+[(cos2​θ−1)/2]C2−i(sin2​θ/2​)C. One simultaneous pulse with θ=π/2​ gives I−C2=−P13​=W4​.
For population transfer alone, the two zero-phase pulses P1​ followed by P2​ take ∣1⟩↦−i∣2⟩↦−∣3⟩, so the target population is exactly one. If a positive final amplitude is desired, phases ϕ1​=ϕ2​=π/2 give ∣1⟩↦∣2⟩↦∣3⟩. Both choices require area π/2 on each channel.
