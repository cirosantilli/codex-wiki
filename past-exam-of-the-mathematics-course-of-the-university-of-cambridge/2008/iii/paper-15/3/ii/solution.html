Choose an independent set uniformly and let Xi​ indicate whether it contains vertex i. Then H(X)=log2​i(G). Put Pi​=N(i)∩[i−1] and bi​=∣Pi​∣. Form a multiset containing every nonempty Pi​ and bi​ copies of singleton {i}. Each coordinate j occurs exactly dj​ times: in dj​−bj​ later-neighbor sets and in bj​ singleton copies.

Apply the upper Madiman-Tetali inequality. Since every j∈Pi​ precedes i, one has dj​≥di​; enlarging that set's weight to 1/di​ and removing conditioning only enlarges its nonnegative contribution.
