The Balister-Bollobas inequality says that if ri​ is the coordinate multiplicity in a multiset A, and Lj​={i:ri​≥j}, then ∑j​H(XLj​​)≤∑A∈A​H(XA​). More generally, each union-intersection compression decreases the entropy sum, by entropy submodularity.
For an exact k-cover define A−=[minA−1] and A+=[maxA−1]∖A. Compressing the family of A∪A− gives the nested family of all A− together with k full sets. Rearranging the resulting bound gives the upper Madiman-Tetali inequality. Conversely, compressing the family of all A+ together with k full sets gives the nested family of A∪A+, yielding the lower bound. In conditional-entropy notation these are
A∈A∑​H(XA​∣XA+​)≤kH(X)≤A∈A∑​H(XA​∣XA−​).​
(11)
The coordinate multiplicities agree in each compression, because A is an exact k-cover. An equivalent proof expands the chain rule for information entropy contributions hi​=H(Xi​∣X[i−1]​) and uses conditioning reduces entropy. It also gives the upper inequality for arbitrary fractional covers and the lower inequality for fractional packings.
