For a continuous survival distribution, S(t)=e−H(t), h(t)=H′(t) and f(t)=h(t)S(t). Applying these identities gives the Weibull distribution
S(t)=e−(λt)p,h(t)=pλptp−1,f(t)=pλptp−1e−(λt)p,t>0.​
(10)
The survivor function has value one at zero; the hazard may diverge there when p<1, which does not invalidate the distribution.
For common shape p, the hazard ratio is h2​(t)/h1​(t)=(λ2​/λ1​)p, independent of time. Thus the two distributions lie in the same proportional hazards family. They also satisfy
S2​(t)=S1​(λ1​λ2​​t),T2​=dλ2​λ1​​T1​.
(11)
Hence the time multiplier is λ1​/λ2​ and the hazard multiplier is (λ2​/λ1​)p, establishing the common accelerated life family as well. Here λ is reciprocal scale, not the ordinary time-scale parameter.
