A square has even valuation, and the unit square criterion shows that the unit class is determined by its odd residue modulo eight. In characteristic different from two, completing the square in a quadratic minimal polynomial makes every quadratic extension K(d​) for a nonsquare d∈K. If K(d​)=K(e​), write e​=a+bd​ and square. The unique unramified extension in this list is Q2​(5​). To verify this, α=(1+5​)/2 is integral and satisfies X2−X−1=0. Its reduction X2+X+1 is irreducible over F2​, so its residue field contains F4​. The standard degree formula for finite extensions of complete discretely valued fields with perfect residue field is [L:K]=e(L/K)f(L/K). Here the field degree is two, so the residue degree is two and the ramification index is one.
Conversely an unramified quadratic extension has residue field F4​. A root there of X2+X+1 lifts by Hensel lemma to a root of X2−X−1, whose derivative is a unit. For this lift β, (2β−1)2=5, so the extension contains Q2​(5​) and must equal it. Every other field in the list is therefore ramified.
