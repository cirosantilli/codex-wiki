Choose an initial s(0)>0. At each iteration, evaluate V and m using s(t) and draw β(t+1) from the conditional multivariate normal distribution just derived. Next compute bt​=b+∥y−Xk​β(t+1)∥2/2 and draw
s(t+1)∼IG(a+n/2,bt​).
(43)
For example, generate G∼Γ(a+n/2,rate bt​) and set s(t+1)=1/G. A draw of the conditional multivariate normal distribution can be formed as m+LZ with LLT=V a Cholesky decomposition and Z a vector of independent standard normal distributions.
This two-block Gibbs sampler alternates the full conditional distributions of the independent normal and inverse-gamma regression priors posterior. The conditional-integration argument proves that its invariant distribution is the joint posterior distribution. Under the usual convergence conditions, retain the pairs (β(t),s(t)) after an initial transient as the requested dependent posterior sample. The new variance update uses the newly drawn coefficients, rather than the previous coefficients.
