The middle panel shows the Feynman diagram for the leptonic W boson decay. With the charged-current Lagrangian density sign in the question, its vertex Feynman rule is
−22​ig​γμ(1−γ5​).​
(17)
An overall field-phase convention can change the overall amplitude sign but cannot change the decay width. With the standard Dirac field current ordering, the amplitude is
M=−22​g​ϵμ​(p)u(q1​)γμ(1−γ5​)v(q2​).
(18)
The unusual order of barred fields in the printed description should be interpreted as this physical weak charged current matrix element.
For massless final fermions, the fermion spin sums are ∑s​us​us​=q1​ and ∑s​vs​vs​=q2​. The massive-vector spin average divides by three. Thus
∣M∣2​=24g2​Kμν​Tμν,Kμν​=−gμν​+pμ​pν​/p2,
(19)
with the chiral trace
Tμν=Tr[q1​γμ(1−γ5​)q2​γν(1−γ5​)].
(20)
Anticommuting γ5​ through the two following gamma matrices and using (1−γ5​)2=2(1−γ5​) gives its symmetric part
TSμν​=8(q1μ​q2ν​+q1ν​q2μ​−gμνq1​⋅q2​).
(21)
Its remaining part is an antisymmetric epsilon tensor from the gamma matrix trace identities; it vanishes against the symmetric polarization tensor K. Direct contraction gives −gμν​TSμν​=16q1​⋅q2​ and
p2pμ​pν​​TSμν​=8[p22(p⋅q1​)(p⋅q2​)​−q1​⋅q2​].
(22)
Hence
∣M∣2​=3g2​[q1​⋅q2​+p22(p⋅q1​)(p⋅q2​)​].​
(23)
No additional averaging over hypothetical right-handed neutrino states is appropriate: the chiral projector already enforces the physical helicity coupling.
On shell, p=q1​+q2​, p2=MW2​ and qi2​=0, so q1​⋅q2​=MW2​/2 and p⋅qi​=MW2​/2. The squared matrix element reduces to g2MW2​/3. In the parent rest frame, integrate the spatial momentum delta first in two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space. The remaining radial integral is
∫dΦ2​=(2π)21​∫4k2k2dkdΩ​δ(MW​−2k)=8π1​.
(24)
Consequently the massless leptonic W decay width is
Γe​=2MW​1​3g2MW2​​8π1​=48πg2MW​​≡Γℓ​.​
(25)
Lepton universality gives Γ(W−→μ−νμ​)=Γ(W−→τ−ντ​)=Γℓ​ in the same massless approximation. The muon and tau lepton masses produce small corrections if retained.
With an identity CKM matrix, the allowed quark channels are du and sc. The corresponding third-generation channel bt is closed by the top threshold. Each open channel has three orthogonal matching-color final states, so the color multiplicity in a decay width gives Γ(du)=Γ(sc)=3Γℓ​. There is no initial color average because the W boson is colorless. The tree-level W branching fractions are therefore:

The rounded percentages sum to 99% because of rounding. The exact fractions sum to one, with total leptonic fraction 1/3 and hadronic fraction 2/3. The total decay width is
ΓW​=9Γℓ​=16π3g2MW​​=2π2​3GF​MW3​​.​
(26)
As an illustrative numerical estimate, taking g=0.65 and MW​=80GeV gives Γℓ​≃0.224GeV, each open quark width ≃0.672GeV and ΓW​≃2.02GeV. These are tree-level model estimates with the stated illustrative inputs, not a fit to a measured width. Strong radiative corrections increase the inclusive quark widths.
The produced quark and antiquark radiate gluons, generating a parton shower. As their virtualities fall towards the strong-interaction scale, perturbation theory gives way to hadronization: confinement converts the colored partons into color-neutral hadrons, typically seen as two particle jets, with additional radiation sometimes producing resolved extra jets. Unstable hadrons subsequently decay; the primary quarks are not observed as free particles.
Including the CKM matrix, the charged current couples an accessible up-type flavour ui​ to every kinematically accessible down-type flavour dj​. Therefore
Γ(W−→dj​ui​)=3Γℓ​∣Vij​∣2,ui​=u,c,dj​=d,s,b,​
(27)
The sums over j remain 3Γℓ​ by row unitarity, so the massless tree-level total remains 9Γℓ​. Quark mixing redistributes the hadronic branching fractions; it does not create a new total color factor. If a family is above threshold, only its accessible channels enter, and the unitarity sum must not be applied to an incomplete set of final flavours.
For completeness, CKM parameter counting starts with N2 real degrees of freedom of an N×N unitary matrix. This follows by counting 2N2 real entries and N2 independent Hermitian constraints in V†V=I. The real-orthogonal subclass has N(N−1)/2 mixing angles; the remaining N(N+1)/2 parameters can be viewed as phases. Rephasing each up-type and down-type mass eigenfield changes Vij​ by e−iαi​eiβj​. Of the 2N phases, the common phase does nothing, so 2N−1 are removable. Thus
Nphysical​=(N−1)2,Nangles​=2N(N−1)​,NCP phases​=2(N−1)(N−2)​.​
(28)
For three generic nondegenerate families this is three angles and one CP-violating phase. Degenerate masses can allow additional changes of basis and reduce the count.
