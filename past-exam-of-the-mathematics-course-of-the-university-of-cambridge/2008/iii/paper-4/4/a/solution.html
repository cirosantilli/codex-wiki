For the conjugation action, fixed elements have coefficients constant on G-conjugacy classes. Therefore (OG)G=Z(OG). More generally an O-basis of (OG)H consists of sums of the H-conjugation orbits on G.
For a∈(OG)H define the relative trace, or transfer, by
TrHG​(a)=∑g∈G/H​gag−1.
(27)
The expression is independent of the coset representatives and is G-fixed. If z∈Z(OG), then zTrHG​(a)=TrHG​(za); hence its image is a transfer ideal of conjugation-fixed elements in the center of an associative algebra.
Let P be a Sylow p-subgroup of H. Trace transitivity gives TrPG​=TrHG​TrPH​, proving inclusion of the P-image in the H-image. Conversely [H:P] is a unit of O, and for an H-fixed element a,
TrPH​(a)=[H:P]a.
(28)
Thus TrHG​(a)=TrPG​(a/[H:P]), proving the reverse inclusion and
(OG)HG​=(OG)PG​.​
(29)
