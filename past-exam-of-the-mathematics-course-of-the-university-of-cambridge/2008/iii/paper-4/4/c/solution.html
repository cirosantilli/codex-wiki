Projection onto the subring in part (b), along the two-sided ideal, is the Brauer homomorphism:
BrD​:(kG)D→kCG​(D),x∑​ax​x⟼x∈CG​(D)∑​ax​x.​
(31)
It is a unital algebra homomorphism because it is the quotient by that ideal followed by the displayed subring identification.
Put N=NG​(D). The projection is N-equivariant, since N preserves the centralizer. Its restricted kernel is
ker(BrD​∣(kG)N​)=(kG)N∩E<D∑​TrED​((kG)E).​
(32)
Equivalently it is spanned by the sums of the N-conjugation orbits on G disjoint from CG​(D). Normality of D in N ensures that an N-orbit either lies wholly inside that centralizer or wholly outside it.
