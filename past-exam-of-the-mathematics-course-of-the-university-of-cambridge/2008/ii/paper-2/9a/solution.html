All successive angular gaps are 2π/N in equilibrium, so their two contributions cancel. Because the potential depends only on gaps, a uniform change of all angles costs no energy. Its Hessian therefore annihilates (1,…,1), giving a zero-frequency normal mode. The corresponding motion is rigid rotation, θi​(t)=θi(0)​+A+Bt, including a constant common angular shift.
