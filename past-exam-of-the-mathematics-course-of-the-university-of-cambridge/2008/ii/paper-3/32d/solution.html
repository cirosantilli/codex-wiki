Represent spin by Ji​=(ℏ/2)σi​ in the basis ∣↑⟩,∣↓⟩. The Pauli matrices are Hermitian and satisfy [σi​,σj​]=2iϵijk​σk​, giving the general angular momentum commutation relations [Ji​,Jj​]=iℏϵijk​Jk​. The particular representation has J2=3ℏ2I/4 and J3​ eigenvalues ±ℏ/2, the features specific to spin one-half.
In the stated order of two-particle basis vectors, the operator is
σ(A)⋅σ(B)=​1000​0−120​02−10​0001​​.​
(67)
Its eigenvalue-one vectors are ∣↑↑⟩, ∣↓↓⟩ and (∣↓↑⟩+∣↑↓⟩)/2​, all symmetric under interchange. The eigenvalue-minus-three vector is (∣↓↑⟩−∣↑↓⟩)/2​, antisymmetric under interchange. These are the triplet state and singlet state, respectively.
Identical spin-half particles are fermions. With no other degrees of freedom to carry antisymmetry, only the singlet is allowed. Indeed, for total angular momentum J=(ℏ/2)(σ(A)+σ(B)),
J2=4ℏ2​(6I+2σ(A)⋅σ(B)).
(68)
This gives 2ℏ2 on the triplet and zero on the singlet, corresponding to total spin one and zero, as required by addition of angular momentum.
