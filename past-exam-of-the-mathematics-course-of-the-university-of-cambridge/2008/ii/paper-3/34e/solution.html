At fixed particle number, expand the entropy differential in (T,p). The definition of heat capacity at constant pressure and the Maxwell relation (∂S/∂p)T​=−(∂V/∂T)p​ give
TdS=Cp​dT−T(∂T∂V​)p​dp.​
(71)
Similarly expansion in (T,V), using heat capacity at constant volume and (∂S/∂V)T​=(∂p/∂T)V​, gives
TdS=CV​dT+T(∂T∂p​)V​dV.​
(72)
At constant pressure, dV=VαdT. Also dV/V=αdT−κdp, so (∂p/∂T)V​=α/κ. Equating the two entropy expressions at constant pressure yields
Cp​−CV​=κTVα2​.​
(73)
For a mechanically stable system with positive isothermal compressibility, this also gives Cp​≥CV​.
