Consider G(z)=(eiz−1)/(iz), extending it at zero by G(0)=1. It is holomorphic in the upper half-plane and on its boundary, and on large upper semicircles G(z)=O(1/∣z∣) because ∣eiz∣≤1. For real t, indent the upper-half-plane contour above the pole of G(z)/(z−t). The clockwise indentation contributes −iπG(t), while the large semicircle contributes zero in the limit.
