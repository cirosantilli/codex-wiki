Use signature (+,−,−,−), which is required for the stated tensor convention. Then Ub​=γ(1,−v). The time component is F0bUb​=γE⋅v, while Fij=−ϵijk​Bk​ gives the spatial components γ(E+v×B). Thus
FabUb​=γ(E⋅v,E+v×B).​
(133)
The scalar ρ∗​=JbUb​ is the charge density in the medium's rest frame. In that frame U=(1,0), so J−ρ∗​U=(0,j) and FU=(0,E). The rest-frame Ohm's law is precisely their proportionality with rest conductivity σ. Both sides are four-vectors, so its covariant form is
Ja−(JbUb​)Ua=σFabUb​.​
(134)
The time and spatial components respectively give
ρ−γρ∗​=σγE⋅v,j−γρ∗​v=σγ(E+v×B).
(135)
Eliminate ρ∗​ to obtain
j=ρv+σγ[E+v×B−(E⋅v)v].​
(136)
If the rest-frame charge density vanishes, set ρ∗​=0, not necessarily ρ=0 in the lab frame. Then
ρ=σγE⋅v,j=σγ(E+v×B).​
(137)
The induced lab charge is the term that cancels the longitudinal correction in the general formula.
