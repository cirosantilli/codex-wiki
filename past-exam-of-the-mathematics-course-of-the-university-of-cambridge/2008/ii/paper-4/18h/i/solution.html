The automorphisms satisfy σn=τ2=1 and τστ=σ−1. The n rotations z↦ζnj​z and n inversion maps z↦ζnj​/z are all distinct, so the dihedral group G has order 2n, including the case n=1 when it has order two. The invariant
w=zn+z−n​
(53)
is fixed by both generators.
Use two standard field-theoretic results: the fixed-field theorem for a finite group of automorphisms gives [L:LG]=∣G∣ and makes L/LG Galois with group G; and a nonconstant rational function P(z)/Q(z) in lowest terms satisfies [C(z):C(P/Q)]=max(degP,degQ). Here w=(z2n+1)/zn has coprime numerator and denominator and degree 2n. Thus [L:C(w)]=[L:LG]=2n. Since C(w)⊆LG, the tower law proves the dihedral fixed field in one rational variable:
LG=C(zn+z−n).​
(54)
