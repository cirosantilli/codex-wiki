For a flow, a hyperbolic fixed point has no eigenvalue of its linearization with zero real part; a nonhyperbolic fixed point has at least one such eigenvalue. At (μ,0) the eigenvalues are −μ,1−μ, while at (0,±1) they are μ+1,−2. Thus nonhyperbolicity occurs at the two stated parameters. The following centre manifold reductions show the associated actual bifurcations.
Near (1,0) put X=x−μ, Y=y, ν=μ−1. The transformed equations are
X˙=(1+ν+X)(−X+Y2),Y˙=Y(−ν−X−Y2),ν˙=0.
(40)
At ν=0, the non-extended stable subspace is the X axis and the centre subspace is the Y axis. In the extended centre manifold, write X=h(Y,ν), with h(0,ν)=0. The symmetry Y↦−Y allows an even graph. At quadratic order its invariance equation has left side of degree at least three and right side −h+Y2, so h=Y2+O(νY2,Y4). Substitution gives
Y˙=−νY−2Y3+O(νY3,Y5).​
(41)
This is a supercritical pitchfork bifurcation in parameter −ν=1−μ. The central branch is stable for μ>1 and unstable for μ<1 near one; the two nonzero branches for μ<1 are stable. At the bifurcation itself the cubic term attracts on the centre manifold.
Near (0,1) put X=x, Y=y−1, ν=μ+1. Then
X˙=X(ν−X+2Y+Y2),Y˙=(1+Y)(−X−2Y−Y2),ν˙=0.
(42)
At ν=0, the stable subspace is X=0 and the centre line is Y=−X/2. Thus the leading extended centre graph is Y=−X/2+O(X2,νX), and the reduced flow is
X˙=νX−2X2+O(X3,νX2).​
(43)
This is a transcritical bifurcation: X=0 is stable for ν<0, while the branch X=ν/2+⋯ is stable for ν>0; the opposite branches are unstable. At the critical point the quadratic term is only one-sided attracting.
The exact additional branch in y>0 is
x=(μ+1)/2,y=(1−μ)/2​,μ<1.​
(44)
It meets (μ,0) at μ=1 and (0,1) at μ=−1, displaying the connecting pitchfork and transcritical branches in a quadratic-product flow. Its Jacobian has determinant 1−μ2 and trace (μ−3)/2. Hence it is stable for −1<μ<1 and a saddle for μ<−1. The negative-y companion is needed for the pitchfork sketch near (1,0); no separate analysis of (0,−1) is required.
