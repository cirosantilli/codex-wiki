The metric is nonsingular on r>0; r=0 is a coordinate boundary at which the displayed coefficients diverge. The nonzero Christoffel symbols are
Γtrt​=Γrtt​=−1/r,Γttr​=−1/r,Γrrr​=−1/r.
(105)
Writing dots for derivatives with respect to proper time, the geodesic equations are
t¨−2t˙r˙/r=0,r¨−(t˙2+r˙2)/r=0.​
(106)
The first gives t˙=kr2 for a constant k, chosen positive for a future-directed particle. Normalization of a timelike four-velocity gives (−t˙2+r˙2)/r2=−1, so r˙2=k2r4−r2. Dividing by t˙2 gives
(dr/dt)2=1−1/(k2r2).​
(107)
Integrating on each side of the turning point yields r2−k−2​=∣t−t0​∣, hence r2−(t−t0​)2=k−2. Choose t0​=0 and let τ=0 at the minimum radius. Then
kr=secτ,kt=tanτ,−π/2<τ<π/2.​
(108)
Differentiating verifies t˙=sec2τ/k=kr2, the normalization and the radial Geodesic equation. proper time therefore remains finite as the coordinate radius and time tend to infinity at the endpoints of this chart.
