For k=0, the first integral
E(x,y)=2y2​+2x2​−3x3​
(13)
has E˙=0. The equilibrium points are (0,0) and (1,0). The linearization has eigenvalues ±i at the origin and ±1 at (1,0). The origin is a center equilibrium: the sufficiently small positive level sets of E are closed curves, since its Hessian matrix there is positive-definite. The other equilibrium is a saddle equilibrium.
At the saddle, E=1/6, and the separatrix satisfies
y2=3(x−1)2(2x+1)​.
(14)
Its two branches with −1/2≤x≤1 form a homoclinic orbit through the saddle; its remaining branches extend to x>1. Inside the loop, the closed trajectories run clockwise: above the horizontal axis x˙=y>0. The saddle directions have slopes +1 and −1. Outside the loop there are unbounded trajectories.
