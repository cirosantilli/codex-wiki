The previous permutation calculation shows that every automorphism either preserves x3,y3 separately or interchanges them. Their sum and product are therefore fixed by the Galois group and belong to K, proving the quadratic assertion.
For the depressed cubic, α+β+γ=0, αβ+αγ+βγ=b, and αβγ=−c. Using 1+ζ+ζ2=0 gives
x+y=3α,xy=−3b,

Its discriminant is 729c2+108b3=−27D, where D=−4b3−27c2. Here −27 is a square in K, since [3(ζ−ζ2)]2=−27.
One can justify the group criterion directly even when one resolvent vanishes. The nonzero root-difference product
d=(α−β)(α−γ)(β−γ)
(58)
has d2=D and changes sign exactly under odd root permutations. It is fixed by the entire group exactly when the group is contained in A3​. If D is a square in K, then d equals one of its two square roots in K; conversely, a fixed d lies in K. Therefore
Gal(F/K)={C3​,S3​,​D∈K×2,D∈/K×2.​​
