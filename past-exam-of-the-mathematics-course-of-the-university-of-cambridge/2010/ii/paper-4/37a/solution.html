For axisymmetric incompressible flow without swirl, the Stokes streamfunction satisfies
uR​=R2sinθΨθ​​,uθ​=−RsinθΨR​​.​
(168)
Substitution into the divergence in spherical coordinates makes incompressibility an identity. Its only vorticity component is
ωϕ​=R1​[∂R​(Ruθ​)−∂θ​uR​]=−RsinθD2Ψ​.
(169)
Taking the curl of the Stokes equation eliminates pressure and gives ∇2ω=0. For an axisymmetric azimuthal vector, its scalar component obeys (Δ−1/(R2sin2θ))ωϕ​=0. Direct differentiation verifies
(Δ−R2sin2θ1​)Rsinθh​=RsinθD2h​.
(170)
Apply this to h=D2Ψ to obtain
D2(D2Ψ)=0.​
(171)
