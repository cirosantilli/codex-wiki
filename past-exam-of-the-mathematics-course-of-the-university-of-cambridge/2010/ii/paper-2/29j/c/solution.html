Vt+1​=Vt​+σ2Vt​σ2​,X^t+1​=Vt​+σ2σ2X^t​+Vt​Yt+1​​.

Taking reciprocal variance and dividing the mean by variance gives
Vt+1−1​=Vt−1​+σ−2,X^t+1​/Vt+1​=X^t​/Vt​+Yt+1​/σ2.
(63)
Thus
X^t​=σ2/V0​+tσ2X^0​/V0​+∑j=1t​Yj​​.
(64)
Since the process noise is zero and A=1, Xt​=X0​ and Yj​=X0​+ηj​. The strong law of large numbers gives t−1∑j​Yj​→X0​ almost surely. The prior's finite weight vanishes in the displayed formula, so both limits equal X0​ almost surely. A zero prior variance instead means X0​ is known, and the assertion follows directly without reciprocal-variance formulas.
