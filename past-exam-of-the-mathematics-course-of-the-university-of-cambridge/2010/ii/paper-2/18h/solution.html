For the first field, set K=Q(5​,i). Its field extension degree is four, since Q(5​) is real and adjoining i doubles its degree. The polynomial X3−5 is irreducible over K: a reducible cubic would have a root in K, but every root has degree three over Q by Eisenstein criterion, impossible by the tower law because 3∤4. Thus
[Q(35​,5​,i):Q]=12​.
(32)
For the second field, put α=45​>0. The splitting field is F=Q(α,i) and has degree eight, since X4−5 is Eisenstein and Q(α) is real. Define automorphisms r(α)=iα,r(i)=i and s(α)=α,s(i)=−i. They satisfy r4=s2=1 and srs=r−1, and give eight distinct automorphisms. Hence Gal(F/Q)≅D8​.
