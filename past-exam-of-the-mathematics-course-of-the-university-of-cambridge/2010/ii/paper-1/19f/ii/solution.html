Directly tracking the supplied permutations gives
a6=1,b2=a3,bab−1=a−1.
(62)
Thus every group element reduces to ak or bak, 0≤k<6. These are twelve distinct elements: a has order six, while b is not a power of a because it exchanges the two six-element supports that every power of a preserves. Therefore ∣G∣=12. This is the dicyclic group with parameter three; the calculations give its order-twelve dicyclic character table.
Conjugation by b inverts powers of a, while conjugation by a changes the exponent in bak by two modulo six. Conjugation by b preserves that exponent's parity. The complete conjugacy classes are
{1}, {a3}, {a,a5}, {a2,a4}, {b,ba2,ba4}, {ba,ba3,ba5}.​
(63)
Their sizes are 1,1,2,2,3,3.
The normal subgroup N=⟨a2⟩ has order three. The quotient is cyclic of order four, generated by bN, since (bN)2=aN. Inflating its characters gives χj​(b)=ij, χj​(a)=(−1)j, for j=0,1,2,3. These are all the linear characters: in any one-dimensional representation, the relation bab−1=a−1 forces the image of a to have square one, so a2 is in the kernel.

To construct the last two rows, put ζ=eiπ/3 and, for k=1,2, set

The matrices obey all three relations. The distinct eigenlines of ρk​(a) are exchanged by ρk​(b), so the representation is irreducible. Its traces on am are 2cos(kmπ/3) and on bam are zero, producing the rows above. Their weighted inner products are orthonormal, and the degree-square sum 4⋅12+2⋅22=12 proves completeness.
