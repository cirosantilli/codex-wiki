Choose the argument in (0,2π) on the slit annulus and let
Kj​={z:1/2≤∣z∣≤2,1/j≤argz≤2π−1/j}.
(4)
These compact sets increase to the slit annulus, and each has connected complement: the omitted angular corridor joins the inner disk to the outside of the outer disk. The polynomial Runge theorem says that a function holomorphic near a compact set with connected complement can be approximated uniformly there by polynomials. Choose pj​ with supKj​​∣pj​−f∣<1/j. Every compact subset K of the slit annulus has positive distance from the removed positive ray, so K⊆Kj​ for all sufficiently large j. Hence pj​→f uniformly on every such compact subset.
