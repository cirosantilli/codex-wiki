Cover the circle by two open arcs whose intersection has two components, and take their products with X. Both members of this cover retract onto X, while their intersection retracts onto X⊔X. The Mayer–Vietoris sequence has the map
Hj​(X)⊕Hj​(X)⟶Hj​(X)⊕Hj​(X),(a,b)⟼(a+b,−a−b),
(58)
after consistent identifications of the two overlap components. Its kernel and cokernel are each Hj​(X). Exactness therefore supplies
0⟶Hj​(X)⟶Hj​(S1×X)⟶Hj−1​(X)⟶0.
(59)
This splits because the last group is free abelian: lift a basis and extend linearly. With H−1​=0, the result is
Hj​(S1×X)≅Hj​(X)⊕Hj−1​(X).​
(60)
Starting from a point and iterating this decomposition, Pascal's identity yields
Hj​(Tn;Z)≅Z(jn​)(0≤j≤n),Hj​(Tn)=0(j>n).​
(61)
