The matrix trace calculation in (i) gives
tr(ATA)=∑i,j​Aij2​≥0.
(20)
A sum of nonnegative real numbers is zero exactly when each is zero. Thus tr(ATA)=0⟺A=0​, so the real Frobenius inner product is positive-definite.
Write α=tr(ATA), β=tr(BTB) and γ=tr(ATB). If B=0, then β>0, and applying the same nonnegativity to A−tB gives
0≤tr((A−tB)T(A−tB))=α−2tγ+t2β.
(21)
Taking t=γ/β yields α−γ2/β≥0. If B=0, the claimed inequality is immediate. Hence the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality here is
(tr(ATB))2≤tr(ATA)tr(BTB).​
(22)
When B=0, equality holds exactly when tr((A−(γ/β)B)T(A−(γ/β)B))=0, hence exactly when A=(γ/β)B. Together with the case B=0, the complete equality condition is A,B are linearly dependent​, including the case that either is zero.
