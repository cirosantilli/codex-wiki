The gradient components are
fx​=y(1−2x−y),fy​=x(1−x−2y).
(53)
If x=0 or y=0, their simultaneous zeros give (0,0),(0,1),(1,0). If xy=0, solving 2x+y=1, x+2y=1 gives (1/3,1/3). These are all the critical points. The Hessian matrix is
H(x,y)=(−2y1−2x−2y​1−2x−2y−2x​).
(54)
At each of (0,0),(1,0),(0,1) its determinant is −1, so its two eigenvalues have opposite signs. All three are saddle points of a scalar function. At (1/3,1/3),
H=(−2/3−1/3​−1/3−2/3​),spec(H)={−1,−1/3}.
(55)
This point is a strict local maximum, of value 1/27​.
The zero level set is exactly
{x=0} ∪ {y=0} ∪ {x+y=1}.​
(56)
These three lines form a triangle containing the maximum. Inside it f>0; near the maximum positive level sets are nested closed curves, with their leading shapes elliptical because the Hessian matrix is negative definite. At each vertex the zero lines cross, separating alternating signs, and nearby nonzero level sets have hyperbolic branches. For example, the leading quadratic terms are xy at (0,0), −η(ξ+η) for ξ=x−1,η=y at (1,0), and −ξ(ξ+η) for ξ=x,η=y−1 at (0,1).
