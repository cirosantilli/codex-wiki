This is the waiting time to observe both Bernoulli outcomes. Write q=1−p, and let S record the sex of the first captured animal. That first capture is always needed. If S is male, every subsequent trial independently produces the required female with probability q, so N−1 has a geometric distribution with parameter q. If S is female, N−1 has a geometric distribution with parameter p. By the law of total expectation,
