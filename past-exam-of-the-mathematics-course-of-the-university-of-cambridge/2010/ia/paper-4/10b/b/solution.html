At infinity the potential energy tends to zero, so the conserved mechanical energy is E=21​v∞2​. The impact parameter b is the perpendicular distance from the origin to the incoming asymptotic line. Conservation of angular momentum gives ∣h∣=bv∞​.
For b>0, a turning point of the radial motion satisfies
21​v∞2​=2r2b2v∞2​​−rQ​,v∞2​r2+2Qr−b2v∞2​=0.

Only the positive root is physical, giving

For Q=0 this reduces to the straight-line closest distance b.

rmin​=v∞2​2∣Q∣​(1+4η2​+O(η4)).

If Q>0, rationalize the numerator instead:
rmin​=Q2+b2v∞4​​+Qb2v∞2​​=2Qb2v∞2​​(1−4η2​+O(η4)).

Thus the requested leading distances are
rmin​∼v∞2​2∣Q∣​(Q<0),rmin​∼2Qb2v∞2​​(Q>0).​

Repulsion gives a finite barrier even for zero impact parameter; attraction allows much closer passage. At b=0, the attractive formula has the limiting closest distance zero, corresponding to collision with the singular centre rather than a positive turning point.
