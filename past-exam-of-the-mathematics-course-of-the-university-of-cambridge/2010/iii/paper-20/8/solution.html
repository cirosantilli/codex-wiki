Put MF​=(M∗)NF/UF​. If F⊆G, projection of coordinates induces
eFG​([h])=[h∘prGF​].

A truth test independent of a coordinate is unchanged by its dummy ultrafilter quantifier. Consequently pullback preserves exactly the UF​-large subsets, proving that the map is well defined and injective. The Łoś theorem, applied to every formula rather than just to equality, proves that it is an elementary embedding. Projections compose, so these elementary maps form a coherent directed system.
Take its directed limit of elementary embeddings N∗. Explicitly identify two stage elements when their images agree at a common larger stage, and interpret functions and relations there. For an existential formula true in the limit, its parameters and a witness occur at a common stage; elementarity then pulls truth back to the stage of the original parameters. This verifies that all stage maps into the limit are elementary.
Let ai​ be the image in this limit of the coordinate class [n↦bn​]∈M{i}​. These elements are distinct: in U{i,j}​, equality would require ni​=nj​, whose inner-coordinate section is a singleton and hence not in the nonprincipal U. For i1​<⋯<ir​, the truth of any expanded-language formula on (ai1​​,…,air​​) is exactly
Un1​⋯Unr​[M∗⊨ϕ(bn1​​,…,bnr​​)].
(33)
This expression depends on the formula and the tuple length, not on the chosen increasing indices. Thus (ai​)i∈I​ is an order-indiscernible sequence. The empty-coordinate stage embeds M∗ elementarily into N∗.
Their Skolem hull is elementary by the witness-function argument, and an order automorphism of I extends to it by transporting the generators in every term. Equality and relation preservation follow from the displayed uniform truth test, and the inverse order automorphism gives the inverse map. Therefore this proves the Ehrenfeucht-Mostowski theorem through ultrapowers and their directed limit, with no Ramsey-theoretic step.
