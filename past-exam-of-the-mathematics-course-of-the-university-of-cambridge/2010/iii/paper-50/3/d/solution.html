Both the drift and local Zk​ control commute with S, so [HS​+f(t)Zk​,S]=0 at every time. The propagator consequently obeys U(t)†SU(t)=S, as follows by differentiating this expression. Thus every reachable unitary operator preserves the magnetization sectors of a spin chain. If Pq​ is a spectral projector of S, then U commutes with Pq​ and Tr(Pq​ρ(t))=Tr(Pq​ρ(0)).
The eigenspaces are nontrivial proper subspaces: ∣0⋯0⟩ has eigenvalue N, while ∣10⋯0⟩ has eigenvalue N−2. One cannot transfer the first pure state to the second, although their density operators have the same spectrum. Nor can one implement a unitary that mixes these two sectors. Hence the full system has neither operator, density, nor pure-state controllability​. Its Dynamical Lie algebra lies in the block-diagonal commutant of S. Controllability within a fixed sector, such as the single-excitation subspace, is still possible and must be tested separately.
