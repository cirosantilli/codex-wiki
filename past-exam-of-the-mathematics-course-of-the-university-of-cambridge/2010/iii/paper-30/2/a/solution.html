A multiclass queue must specify its service discipline as well as its arrival and service rates. Here is an ordered-queue model that includes the usual class-independent exponential single-server queue. Let class-r arrivals be independent Poisson processes of rates αr​, and let a class-r service requirement have the exponential distribution of rate μr​. A state is a finite word x=(c1​,…,cn​) of customer classes.
Choose service fractions γi​(n)≥0 with ∑i=1n​γi​(n)=1, and insertion probabilities δi​(n)≥0 with ∑i=1n​δi​(n)=1. The customer at position i departs at rate μci​​γi​(n); a class-r arrival is inserted at position i at rate αr​δi​(n+1). All rates leading to the same word are added. For class-dependent μr​, impose the symmetric service discipline δi​(n)=γi​(n).
