Fix the input-index convention
Dei​=∑j​θij​⊗ej​,D2ei​=∑j​Θij​⊗ej​.
(11)
Extend the connection on a vector bundle by the graded Leibniz rule, D(α⊗s)=dα⊗s+(−1)degαα∧Ds. Applying it twice gives
D2ei​=∑j​dθij​⊗ej​−∑j,k​θij​∧θjk​⊗ek​,
(12)
so
Θ=dθ−θ∧θ.​
(13)
This is the Cartan curvature equation with input indices. In the more usual coefficient-column convention A=θt, and (A∧A)t=−θ∧θ because the entries have degree one. Thus F=dA+A∧A=Θt; the sign difference is entirely conventional.
The Chern connection of a Hermitian vector bundle with holomorphic structure is characterized by metric compatibility and D0,1=∂ˉE​. To construct it, use a holomorphic local frame and let hij​=⟨ei​,ej​⟩, with the Hermitian inner product linear in the first variable. The second defining property makes θ type (1,0). The first gives
dh=θh+hθt,∂h=θh,
(14)
and therefore the only possible matrix is
θ=(∂h)h−1.​
(15)
Conversely, this formula has type (1,0) and satisfies both type components of the metric identity, because h=ht. Under a holomorphic frame change e′=ge, h′=ghg​t and direct differentiation gives
θ′=∂gg−1+gθg−1.
(16)
This is precisely the transformation obtained by differentiating e′=ge. The locally constructed connections glue, proving existence, and the forced formula proves uniqueness of the Chern connection.
The derivative of h−1 gives ∂θ=θ∧θ. Consequently the Chern curvature is
Θ=∂ˉθ∈A1,1(EndE),∂ˉEndE​Θ=0.​
(17)
The second assertion is ∂ˉ2θ=0 in a holomorphic local frame; holomorphic transition functions make it intrinsic. Thus the curvature Dolbeault class is Ψ=[Θ]∈H1(M,ΩM1​⊗EndE) by the Dolbeault theorem. Here Ω1(EndE) denotes this tensor product. We use the identification with bundle-valued Dolbeault forms in which the antiholomorphic exterior factor is placed first, so ∂ˉθ has the stated positive descent sign.
For the stated transitions e(β)=gαβ​e(α), differentiating the frame relation yields
θβ​=gαβ​θα​gαβ−1​+(∂gαβ​)gαβ−1​.
(18)
Transport the alpha matrix to the beta frame before subtracting it. The difference is the holomorphic section
σαβ​=(∂gαβ​)gαβ−1​.​
(19)
On a triple intersection, gαγ​=gβγ​gαβ​, so
σαγ​=σβγ​+gβγ​σαβ​gβγ−1​.
(20)
This is exactly the Čech cocycle condition after transporting all representatives to the gamma frame.
To identify its class, let Dα​ be the local holomorphic connection making the alpha frame parallel. Then tα​=D−Dα​ is a genuine local section of A1,0(EndE), represented by θα​, with ∂ˉtα​=Θ. Its Čech coboundary, using tβ​−tα​, is the intrinsic section represented by σαβ​ in the beta frame. This local-primitive construction is the Čech–Dolbeault identification, and proves that (σαβ​) maps to Ψ. For an arbitrary trivializing cover use its natural map to sheaf cohomology; a Stein refinement gives the usual Čech computation. The resulting object is the Čech cocycle for the curvature Dolbeault class.
If the metric changes, the difference B=D′−D is a global smooth (1,0) form with values in EndE. The local curvature formulas give Θ′−Θ=∂ˉB, so Ψ is metric independent. If holomorphic frames change by eα′​=uα​eα​, the corresponding local tensors tα′​−tα​, expressed in the old frame, are Bα​=uα−1​∂uα​. They are holomorphic, and their differences change the cocycle by δB. Hence Ψ depends only on the holomorphic bundle, not on its metric or trivializations.
Apply exterior product and endomorphism composition to curvature to define
Ψ(k)=[Θ∧k]∈Hk(M,ΩMk​⊗EndE),trΨ(k)∈Hk(M,ΩMk​).​
(21)
These are closed for the Dolbeault operator by the product rule. Their independence can also be verified without commuting matrices. If Θ1​−Θ0​=∂ˉB, then
Θ1k​−Θ0k​=∂ˉ(∑j=0k−1​Θ1j​∧B∧Θ0k−1−j​).
(22)
This follows by the noncommutative telescoping identity and the even total degree of curvature. The matrix products are interpreted in the fixed frame convention; powers of the degree-two curvature transpose to the corresponding usual curvature powers. The trace contraction is invariant under frame changes, so its class is well defined. This proves metric independence of curvature Dolbeault powers; for k>dimM the forms and groups in question vanish.
Finally take a smooth unitary frame. Metric compatibility gives θ†=−θ, and the Cartan curvature equation with input indices gives Θ†=−Θ. Thus
tr(Θk)​=(−1)ktr(Θk),(2πi​)ktr(Θk)​=(2πi​)ktr(Θk).
(23)
The second expression is therefore a real differential form. It is also d-closed: differentiating Cartan's equation gives dΘ=θ∧Θ−Θ∧θ, and cyclicity of trace cancels the resulting commutator in dtr(Θk). On a compact Kähler manifold, the Hodge decomposition theorem for compact Kähler manifolds identifies Hk(M,ΩMk​) with the (k,k) component of complex de Rham cohomology. Hence
(2πi​)ktr(Ψ(k)) determines a class in HDR2k​(M,R).​
(24)
It is k! times the degree-2k component of the Chern character. Reality does not assert integrality of these individual power-sum classes.
