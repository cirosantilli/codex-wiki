Using the same Bayesian deviance convention, the deviance information criterion and Akaike information criterion are
DIC=D+pD​=D(θ)+2pD​,AIC=D(θ)+2p.​
(34)
Both combine a lack-of-fit term with a complexity penalty; smaller values are preferred among comparable models. Under the locally flat prior distribution and the regular multivariate normal distribution approximation, D(θ)≈D(θ) and pD​≈p, so
DIC≈D(θ)+2p=AIC.​
(35)
All fitted parameters counted in the likelihood, including unknown dispersion parameters, belong in p. The equivalence need not hold for informative priors, nonregular models, or different choices of the likelihood's latent-variable representation.
