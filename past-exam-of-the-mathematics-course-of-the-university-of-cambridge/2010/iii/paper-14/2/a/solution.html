The Livsic theorem for a topologically transitive Anosov flow says that a Hölder function h:N→R satisfies the periodic-orbit obstruction
∫0T​h(ϕt​x)dt=0whenever ϕT​x=x
(12)
if and only if there is a Hölder function u satisfying the flow coboundary identity
u(ϕt​x)−u(x)=∫0t​h(ϕs​x)ds​.
(13)
The solution is differentiable along orbits and Fu=h in that sense. For a smooth flow and smooth h, the smooth regularity version gives u∈C∞(N), so Fu=h holds smoothly. The solution is unique up to an additive constant: the difference of two solutions is invariant and must be constant on a dense orbit and then everywhere by continuity. Necessity of the periodic obstruction follows immediately by taking t=T in the displayed identity.
