Taking a curl and using the equation for v verifies B=eu(1−eu)/2; substitution verifies the other first-order equation. At infinity the phase winds N times, producing flux 2πN. Thus multi-vortex construction reduces to solving one scalar elliptic boundary-value problem for specified positions and multiplicities, followed by these explicit formulas.
For the topological branch, u≤0: a positive interior maximum would have Δu≤0, whereas the equation there gives Δu=eu(eu−1)>0. At infinity, the linearized scalar equation is Δu−u=0. Comparison barriers and elliptic estimates give the decaying tail for regular topological solutions, and hence the required decay of B,DΦ and 1−h. The nonlinear elliptic problem determines v; the reconstruction formulas do not constitute an arbitrary superposition of single-vortex profiles.
