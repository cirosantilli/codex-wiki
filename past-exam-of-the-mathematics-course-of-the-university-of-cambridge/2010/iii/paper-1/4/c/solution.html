Every coordinate not displayed stays fixed. Thus each generating Weyl reflection is a signed permutation, so the Weyl group is contained in the signed symmetric group. Conversely, the first row supplies each independent sign change and the second supplies every coordinate transposition; these generate the entire signed symmetric group.
