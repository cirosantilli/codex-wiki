Write an element of the symplectic Lie algebra in n×n blocks. Direct multiplication in AJ+JAT=0 gives
A=(PR​Q−PT​),QT=Q,RT=R.
(15)
The diagonal Cartan subalgebra consists of H=diag(h1​,…,hn​,−h1​,…,−hn​). Define εi​(H)=hi​. Under the Adjoint representation, the off-diagonal matrix units in P have weights εi​−εj​; the symmetric entries in Q have weights εi​+εj​, including 2εi​ on the diagonal; those in R have the negatives of these weights. All nonzero root spaces are one-dimensional, while the zero weight space is the diagonal Cartan subalgebra. Thus the Cn root system is
R={±εi​±εj​:i<j}∪{±2εi​:1≤i≤n}.​
(16)
Choose the positive roots and simple roots as
R+={εi​−εj​,εi​+εj​:i<j}∪{2εi​},Δ={αi​=εi​−εi+1​:i<n}∪{αn​=2εn​}.​
(17)
Their highest root and Weyl vector are
θ=2ε1​=2α1​+⋯+2αn−1​+αn​,ρ=21​α∈R+∑​α=i=1∑n​(n−i+1)εi​.​
(18)
For the last identity, each pair i<j contributes 2εi​ before division by two, and the long positive root contributes another εi​ afterward. Successive simple roots have equal length except for αn​, whose squared length is twice that of αn−1​.
