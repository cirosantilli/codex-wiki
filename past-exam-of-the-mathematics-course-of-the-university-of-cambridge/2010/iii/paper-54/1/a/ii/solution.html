The Levi-Civita connection has symmetric Christoffel symbols, so antisymmetrization removes its connection terms. Hence the Papapetrou electromagnetic field is
Fab​=∇a​Vb​−∇b​Va​=2∇a​Vb​,
(5)
where the last equality uses the Killing equation. As a differential two-form, F=dV♭, so the identity d2=0 for the exterior derivative proves ∇[a​Fbc]​=0. Contracting the preceding second covariant derivative of a Killing vector gives
∇a∇a​Vb​=−Rbd​Vd,∇aFab​=−2Rbd​Vd.
(6)
A vacuum spacetime here has Rab​=0, with no cosmological constant. Therefore both source-free Maxwell equations hold:
∇[a​Fbc]​=0,∇aFab​=0.​
(7)
This is a test electromagnetic field on the given vacuum spacetime; its own stress-energy tensor is not included in that background's Einstein field equations.
