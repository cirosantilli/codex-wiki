Take an affinely parametrized null geodesic congruence, ∇k​k=0. Choose the auxiliary null vector and a transverse orthonormal screen basis to undergo parallel transport along each ray. For the unprojected tensor Cab​=∇b​ka​, the product rule and the Ricci identity yield
kc∇c​Cab​​=∇b​(kc∇c​ka​)−(∇b​kc)(∇c​ka​)+kc[∇c​,∇b​]ka​=−(∇b​kc)(∇c​ka​)−Racbd​kckd.​
(40)
Here the Riemann curvature tensor convention is the one in Question 1; its last-index antisymmetry converts the covector commutator to the displayed curvature term. Because Cab​ka=Cab​kb=0, inserting the screen-space projector in the product term adds only terms killed by these contractions. Parallel propagation makes differentiation commute with the screen projection. Therefore, in screen indices A,B,
dλdBAB​​=−BAC​BCB​−RAcBd​kckd.
(41)
This is the optical matrix evolution equation. Its trace has curvature contraction qabRacbd​kckd=Rcd​kckd: the additional terms in qab−gab vanish by curvature antisymmetry. Write B=(θ/2)q+σ+ω using the null expansion, null shear, and null twist. Symmetric--antisymmetric cross terms have zero trace, σ is trace-free, and
tr(B2)=21​θ2+σab​σab−ωab​ωab.
(42)
The minus sign in the last trace comes from ωab​=−ωba​ and the positive screen metric. Taking the trace therefore derives the Null Raychaudhuri equation:
dλdθ​=−21​θ2−σab​σab+ωab​ωab−Rab​kakb.​
(43)
For a nonaffine tangent obeying ∇k​k=κk, the same product-rule derivation adds +κθ on the right; the projected derivative of κ multiplies ka​ and vanishes. In D dimensions the quadratic null expansion term is −θ2/(D−2).
