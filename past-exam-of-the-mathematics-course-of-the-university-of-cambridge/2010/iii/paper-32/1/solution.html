Put G=XTX and H=XG−1XT. The rank of a matrix assumption makes G invertible, and H is the orthogonal projection matrix onto the column space of the design matrix. The Gaussian likelihood has log-likelihood
ℓ(b,v)=−2n​log(2πv)−2v∥Y−Xb∥2​,v>0.
(1)
Minimizing the squared Euclidean norm gives the ordinary least squares normal equations Gβ​=XTY. Maximizing over v then gives the maximum-likelihood estimators
β​=G−1XTY,σ2=nRSS​,RSS=YT(I−H)Y.​
(2)
The divisor for the maximum-likelihood estimator of the variance is n, whereas the unbiased residual variance estimate is s2=RSS/(n−p). The exceptional event RSS=0 has probability zero under the Gaussian linear model with σ2>0; on that event the likelihood is unbounded as v↓0.
For the joint sampling distribution, write β​−β=G−1XTϵ and Y−Xβ​=(I−H)ϵ. Their cross-covariance matrix vanishes because XT(I−H)=0. These two vectors are jointly multivariate normal, so they are independent random variables. The residual orthogonal projection has rank n−p, hence the standard chi-squared distribution result gives
β​∼Np​(β,σ2G−1),σ2nσ2​∼χn−p2​,β​ and σ2 independent.​
(3)
Thus these two displayed marginal laws, with their independence, specify the full joint distribution.
The quadratic form
σ2(β​−β)TG(β​−β)​
(4)
has a chi-squared distribution with p degrees of freedom and is independent of RSS/σ2. Their ratio therefore supplies the exact F-distribution pivot
T(β)=RSS/(n−p)(β​−β)TG(β​−β)/p​∼Fp,n−p​.
(5)
If f1−α​ is the (1−α) quantile of that F-distribution, inversion gives the normal linear-model confidence ellipsoid
C1−α​(Y)={b∈Rp:(β​−b)TG(β​−b)≤ps2f1−α​}.​
(6)
For every β and every σ2>0, the probability that this confidence set contains β is exactly 1−α. To test the null hypothesis, reject precisely when β0​ lies outside this confidence ellipsoid, equivalently when T(β0​)>f1−α​. Under the null hypothesis the rejection probability is exactly α, independent of the unknown variance.
For the numerical calculation, the second column of the design matrix sums to zero and its sum of squares is 60. Direct calculation from the observations gives
G=(90​060​),XTY=(030​),YTY=22.
(7)
Consequently the ordinary least squares estimate, residual sum of squares and unbiased residual variance are
β​=(01/2​),RSS=22−(XTY)TG−1(XTY)=22−15=7,s2=7/7=1.
(8)
The maximum-likelihood estimator of the variance is instead 7/9. At the proposed null value the F-distribution statistic is
T(0)=7/715/2​=7.5>4.74.​
(9)
Therefore reject the null hypothesis at the 5% level. The relevant critical value has 2 numerator and 7 residual degrees of freedom.
