A flat module is one for which tensoring preserves injections. This is equivalent to exactness of the tensor functor, since the tensor product of modules is always right exact: its universal balanced maps identify tensoring a cokernel with the cokernel of the tensored map.
Suppose M is flat. For every inclusion N′↪N, the map N′⊗A​M→N⊗A​M is injective. The detection property proved above therefore makes its character-dual map surjective. Under the character Tensor-hom adjunction, this is exactly
HomA​(N,M∗)↠HomA​(N′,M∗).
