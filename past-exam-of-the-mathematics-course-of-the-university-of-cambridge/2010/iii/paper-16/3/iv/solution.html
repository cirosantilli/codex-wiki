Take the projective line X=Pk1​ and the coherent sheaves F=OX​ and G=OX​(−1), where the latter is a twisting sheaf on projective space. Both are line bundles, so each stalk is a free rank-one OX,x​-module and therefore Fx​≃Gx​ at every point.
They are nevertheless not isomorphic. On the standard charts with coordinates t and t−1, choose frames e0​,e1​ of O(−1) with e1​=t−1e0​. A global section would give polynomials a(t) and b(t−1) satisfying
a(t)=t−1b(t−1).
(29)
The left side has only nonnegative powers of t, and the right side only strictly negative powers. Thus both vanish, giving Γ(X,O(−1))=0. For the structure sheaf, the corresponding equality is a(t)=b(t−1), whose solutions are precisely the constants. Hence
H0(X,F)=k,H0(X,G)=0,F≃G.​
(30)
A morphism of sheaves which is an isomorphism on all stalks would be an isomorphism; this example only asserts the existence of separate stalk isomorphisms, not that they come from one morphism of sheaves.
