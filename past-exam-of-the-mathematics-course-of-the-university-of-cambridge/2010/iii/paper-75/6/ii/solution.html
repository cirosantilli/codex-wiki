Let A,B be fixed objects and form the ambient exponential object BA. For every X, the exponential adjunction identifies maps X→BA with maps X×A→B. Since L is left exact and LA=A,
L(X×A)≅LX×A,
(24)
and its unit corresponds to ηX​×1A​. Because B is fixed, every map X×A→B therefore extends uniquely to LX×A→B. Transposing back gives
E(LX,BA)≅E(X,BA).
(25)
The fixed-object criterion from part (i) shows that BA already belongs to L. The ambient evaluation and transpose universal property restrict to the full subcategory, proving the subcategory is cartesian closed. This constructs exponentials in a left-exact reflective subcategory, rather than merely reflecting an exponential and assuming its universal property survives.
