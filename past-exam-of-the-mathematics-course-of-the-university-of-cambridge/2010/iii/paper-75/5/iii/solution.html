A geometric embedding means f∗​ is full and faithful, equivalently that the adjunction counit f∗f∗​H→H is invertible for every H. At c, the explicit right-adjoint formula identifies this counit with
Nat(D(T−,Tc),H)⟶Nat(C(−,c),H)≅H(c),
(21)
induced by τc​:C(−,c)→D(T−,Tc), sending h to Th.
If T is full and faithful, every τc​ is an isomorphism, making all these counit components bijective and hence making f∗​ full and faithful. Conversely, if precomposition with τc​ is a bijection for every H, then τc​ is invertible by the Yoneda lemma applied in the presheaf category: taking H=C(−,c) gives a left inverse, and injectivity for H=D(T−,Tc) makes it a right inverse too. Evaluating τc​ at c′ therefore says that
C(c′,c)T​D(Tc′,Tc)
(22)
is bijective for all c,c′. This proves the full-faithfulness criterion for presheaf geometric embeddings:
f is an injection⟺T is full and faithful.​
(23)
