To prove the derivative of the matrix exponential formula, expand its power series. The coefficient linear in h in (A+hB)k is ∑j=0k−1​AjBAk−1−j. Finally differentiability means f(t+h)=A+hB+o(h); the locally bounded derivative of the exponential sends the o(h) error to o(h), proving the chain rule even when f′ is not continuous.
