Let (ϕj​) be a smooth orthonormal eigenbasis of the positive Laplace-Beltrami operator, with eigenvalues (λj​). The orthonormal eigenbasis defines
e−tΔf=∑j​e−tλj​⟨f,ϕj​⟩ϕj​.
(73)
The large-j lower bound of part (c), valid also after the finitely many zero modes of a disconnected compact manifold, gives λj​≥aj2/n for sufficiently large j. Hence
∑j​e−tλj​<∞(t>0),
(74)
by comparison with ∫0∞​e−tax2/ndx. The operator is positive and diagonal with these summable coefficients, so it is a trace-class operator and its operator trace is their sum.
Its proposed heat kernel is
Kt​(x,y)=∑j​e−tλj​ϕj​(x)ϕj​(y)​.
(75)
We verify smoothness and the kernel action. The elliptic regularity estimate established in Question 4(d) gives, by induction,
∥ϕj​∥H2r​≤Cr​(1+λj​)r.
(76)
The Sobolev embedding theorem therefore gives, for every derivative order a, constants Ca​, ra​ with ∥ϕj​∥Ca​≤Ca​(1+λj​)ra​. Every differentiated term of the kernel series is bounded by Ca,b​e−tλj​(1+λj​)ra​+rb​. An exponential absorbs any fixed power: for t≥τ>0 this is at most Ca,b,τ​e−τλj​/2, a summable bound. Time derivatives merely introduce more powers of λj​. Thus the series converges uniformly with all spatial and time derivatives on [τ,∞)×M×M. In particular Kt​ is smooth for every t>0.
Integrating its uniformly convergent series against f∈L2(M) is legitimate since the compact manifold has finite volume and L2(M)⊆L1(M). It gives exactly the spectral expression above, so
(e−tΔf)(x)=∫M​Kt​(x,y)f(y)dvolg​(y).
(77)
Finally Kt​(x,x)=∑j​e−tλj​∣ϕj​(x)∣2 is nonnegative. Integrating term by term, either by uniform convergence or by Tonelli theorem, and using ∥ϕj​∥2​=1 proves the heat kernel trace formula
Tre−tΔ=j∑​e−tλj​=∫M​Kt​(x,x)dvolg​(x),t>0.​
(78)
