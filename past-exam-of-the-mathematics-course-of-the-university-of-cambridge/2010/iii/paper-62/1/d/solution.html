The Weierstrass approximation theorem states that the polynomials are a dense subspace of C[0,1] in the supremum norm. By linearity and (c), Bn​p→p uniformly for every polynomial p. Given f∈C[0,1] and ε>0, choose a polynomial p with ∥f−p∥∞​<ε. The operator norm estimate in (a) gives
∥Bn​f−f∥∞​≤∥Bn​(f−p)∥∞​+∥Bn​p−p∥∞​+∥p−f∥∞​≤2ε+∥Bn​p−p∥∞​.
(7)
First let n→∞, then ε↓0. Hence ∥Bn​f−f∥∞​→0 for every continuous f. This is an instance of extension of approximation operators from a dense subspace.
