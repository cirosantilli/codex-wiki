For sk−1​<t<sk​, only the last term survives and is positive. The nonnegativity from the recurrence in (b) actually gives positivity throughout the interior: for each s0​<t<sk​, at least one lower-order B-spline covering t has a positive recurrence coefficient, and induction starts with positive order-one indicators. Hence suppMi​=[ti​,ti+k​] and Mi​∈Ck−2 for k≥2, with degree k−1 on its knot intervals. For k=1, the B-spline is the step function 1[s0​,s1​)​(t)/(s1​−s0​), so no continuity is asserted.
The unit-integral normalization of a B-spline also follows directly: integrating k(sj​−t)+k−1​ over s0​≤t≤sk​ gives (sj​−s0​)k. The kth divided difference of this monic degree-k polynomial is one. Thus ∫Mi​(t)dt=1, explaining its normalization.
