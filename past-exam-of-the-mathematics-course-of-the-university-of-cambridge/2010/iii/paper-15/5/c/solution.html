First verify the difference of affine connections is a tensor. Linearity over smooth functions in X follows from the affine connection axioms. In Y, the two derivative terms cancel:
A(X,fY)=∇X​(fY)−∇X′​(fY)=fA(X,Y).
(48)
Thus A is a smooth (1,2)-tensor field, and Ap​(u,v) depends only on the tangent vectors u,v at p. For any smooth curve, its two covariant accelerations satisfy
Dt​γ˙​−Dt′​γ˙​=A(γ˙​,γ˙​).
(49)
If A(X,Y)=−A(Y,X), its diagonal values vanish. The two geodesic equations are therefore identical, and the connections have the same parametrized geodesics.
For necessity, the required local geodesic existence theorem is this: for every smooth affine connection, p∈M and v∈Tp​M, a unique geodesic exists on some interval about zero with γ(0)=p and γ˙​(0)=v. Indeed the geodesic equation is the smooth first-order ordinary differential equation on position and velocity
x˙k=vk,v˙k=−Γkij​(x)vivj,
(50)
so local existence and uniqueness apply. If the two connections have the same parametrized geodesics, apply the acceleration identity to this geodesic at zero to obtain Ap​(v,v)=0 for every p,v. Expanding Ap​(u+v,u+v)=0 gives Ap​(u,v)+Ap​(v,u)=0. Therefore
same parametrized geodesics⟺A(X,Y)=−A(Y,X).​
(51)
This is the criterion that parametrized geodesics determine the symmetric part of an affine connection. It concerns equality with the same affine parameters. Agreement merely of unparametrized images is weaker: adding α(X)Y+α(Y)X to a connection, for a smooth one-form α, changes acceleration by a multiple of the velocity, which can be absorbed by reparametrization. Such a difference need not be antisymmetric.
Finally, since ∇X′​Y=∇X​Y−A(X,Y), expand the derivative of the metric tensor:
(∇X′​g)(Y,Z)=X(g(Y,Z))−g(∇X′​Y,Z)−g(Y,∇X′​Z)=(∇X​g)(Y,Z)+g(A(X,Y),Z)+g(Y,A(X,Z)).
(52)
Under the assumed metric compatibility of ∇, the first term is zero. Consequently
∇′g=0⟺g(A(X,Y),Z)=−g(Y,A(X,Z))for all X,Y,Z.​
(53)
Equivalently, for each fixed X, the endomorphism A(X,⋅) is skew-adjoint for the Riemannian metric. This metric criterion does not require the two connections to have the same geodesics.
