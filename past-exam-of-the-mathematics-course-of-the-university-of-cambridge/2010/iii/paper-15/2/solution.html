A smooth rank-r vector bundle over a smooth manifold M consists of a smooth total space E, a smooth projection π:E→M, and an r-dimensional real vector space structure on each fibre Ep​=π−1(p). Each point has a neighborhood U with a vector bundle trivialization
τU​:π−1(U)∼​U×Rr
(13)
that is a diffeomorphism, commutes with projection to U, and is a linear isomorphism on every fibre. The overlap maps have the form (p,v)↦(p,h(p)v) with h:U∩V→GLr​(R) smooth. For a complex vector bundle, replace R by C and require complex-linear fibre maps.
A connection on a vector bundle is an R-linear map
∇:Γ(E)⟶Ω1(M;E),∇(fs)=df⊗s+f∇s,
(14)
where Γ(E) is the module of smooth sections, Ω1(M;E) consists of vector-bundle-valued differential forms of degree one, and f is a smooth function. Equivalently, evaluating on a vector field X gives operators ∇X​ with ∇fX​s=f∇X​s and ∇X​(fs)=X(f)s+f∇X​s. A connection on a vector bundle is local: its value at a point depends on the germ of the section of a vector bundle, so the same definition applies to local sections.
