Let m=dimM, n=dimN and k=codimN​S. Fix p∈f−1(S) and choose the allowed slice chart for an embedded submanifold on a neighborhood U of f(p), so that S∩U is given by x1=⋯=xk=0. On f−1(U) define the smooth map
G=(x1,…,xk)∘f:f−1(U)⟶Rk.
(38)
The derivative L=d(x1,…,xk) at a point of S∩U is surjective and has kernel TS, by the slice description. The printed sum-of-spaces condition gives
L(dfr​(Tr​M))=Rkfor every r∈f−1(S∩U).
(39)
Indeed any target tangent vector is a sum of an image vector and a tangent vector to S, and L kills the latter. By the chain rule, dGr​=L∘dfr​ is therefore surjective. This condition is precisely transversality of a map to a submanifold; the original sum need not be direct.
We have G−1(0)=f−1(S)∩f−1(U). Part (b), applied to G, gives slice charts for an embedded submanifold of codimension k around every point of this zero set. Since the construction is available around every p∈f−1(S), the charts give its global structure as an embedded submanifold with the subspace topology. Its dimension is m−k=m−n+dimS​. Moreover,
Tp​(f−1(S))={v∈Tp​M:dfp​(v)∈Tf(p)​S}.​
(40)
This follows either from the slice coordinates or from Tp​G−1(0)=kerdGp​ and kerL=Tf(p)​S. No injectivity of df, closedness of S, or direct-sum hypothesis is required.
