Put C0​=Set. The initial free construction L0​:Set→C1​ sends A to A×N with α1​(a,k)=(a,k+1). A function f:A→UB extends uniquely by (a,k)↦β1k​(f(a)). Its induced monad is T0​A=A×N, with unit a↦(a,0) and multiplication (a,k,l)↦(a,k+l). An algebra for a monad is determined by the action of 1∈N, so its Eilenberg-Moore category is C1​. Thus this first free–forgetful adjunction is a monadic adjunction without needing the given assumption for n=0.
The monadic length counts successive Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor steps until the comparison becomes an equivalence. Fix 0≤m<n. For m≥1, the free construction in the preceding part adds the (m+1)st operation with no fixed points. For m=0, the free first operation also has no fixed points. Consequently the left adjoint Fn,m​ to the composite forgetful functor Un,m​:Cn​→Cm​ is obtained by the one-step free construction, followed by empty higher operations. In particular, its underlying object of Cm​, unit and multiplication are exactly those of the one-step adjunction:
Un,m​Fn,m​=Um​Lm​=Tm​
(19)
as monads, not merely as object functions. The multiplication agrees because both counits evaluate the same first new operation and the same chains; no later operation has a value on a free object.
By the assumed one-step monadic adjunction property, or the explicit argument above when m=0, the Eilenberg-Moore category CmTm​​ is equivalent to Cm+1​. Under this equivalence the comparison Cn​→CmTm​​ is precisely Un,m+1​. Indeed, its algebra action evaluates each newly adjoined chain using α1​, and evaluates the first new value using αm+1​; it retains that operation and ignores all higher ones. The comparison has a left adjoint by the same explicit free extension. Thus the whole monadic tower for nested partial unary operations is
Set=C0​,C1​,…,Cn​,
(20)
with the remaining comparison at stage m equal to Un,m​, and stage n the identity.
It does not terminate earlier. If m<n, take the same underlying two-element set in two objects of Cn​, making every operation up to m the identity. In the first object make all remaining operations identities; in the second make αm+1​ exchange the two elements, and leave all subsequent operations undefined. Both satisfy the nesting rule. The identity function is a morphism after forgetting to Cm​, but is not a morphism between the original objects. Thus Un,m​ is not a full functor, and cannot be an equivalence. Therefore
monadic length(Cn​→Set)=n.​
(21)
