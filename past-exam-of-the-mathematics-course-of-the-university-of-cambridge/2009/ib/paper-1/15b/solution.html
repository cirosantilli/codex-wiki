The parity operator acts by (Pψ)(x)=ψ(−x). For an even potential, differentiating twice and using V(−x)=V(x) proves HP=PH on the parity-invariant domain of the Hamiltonian operator. Therefore each energy eigenspace is invariant under P. The projectors (I+P)/2 and (I−P)/2 split it into even and odd subspaces, giving a basis of simultaneous eigenstates. An odd state obeys ψ(0)=−ψ(0), hence ψodd​(0)=0.
The infinite walls impose ψ(−a)=ψ(a)=0, with the wavefunction zero outside. It is continuous at the central delta potential. Integrate the stationary Schrödinger equation over [−ε,ε]; the energy integral tends to zero and the Dirac delta function contributes κψ(0). This gives
2mℏ2​(ψ′(0+)−ψ′(0−))=κψ(0).​
(25)
For the given piecewise positive-energy form, E=ℏ2λ2/(2m). Replacing x by −x swaps the two pieces and leaves their values unchanged, proving Pψ=ψ. The value at zero is A, and the one-sided derivatives are −Bλ and Bλ. The jump condition gives B=−mκA/(ℏ2λ). The wall at a gives Acos(λa)−Bsin(λa)=0, hence
tan(λa)=−mκℏ2λ​.​
(26)
For κ=0 the undivided wall equation instead gives the usual free even roots. For κ>0, set c=mκ/ℏ2>0 and kn​=(n+1/2)π/a. In every interval kn​<λ<(n+1)π/a there is exactly one root: writing δ=a(λ−kn​)∈(0,π/2) gives cotδ=λ/c, whose two sides are respectively strictly decreasing and increasing. These are all positive even roots; the quadratic form ℏ2∫∣ψ′∣2/(2m)+κ∣ψ(0)∣2 is nonnegative, so there are no negative eigenvalues. Thus λn​>kn​ and ηn​=ℏ2(λn2​−kn2​)/(2m)>0.
The printed limit of this absolute energy difference is false. The exact quantization equation gives δn​=arctan(c/λn​), so δn​→0 but λn​δn​→c. Consequently
λn2​−kn2​=a2λn​δn​​−a2δn2​​⟶a2c​,n→∞lim​ηn​=aκ​.​
(27)
Thus the high-energy shift from a central delta barrier is a finite positive constant, not zero. The corrected vanishing statement is the relative shift ηn​/En(0)​→0, or the wave-number difference λn​−kn​→0. Physically, highly energetic particles are weakly affected in relative terms by a fixed point barrier. The free even wavefunction normalized in [−a,a] has density 1/a at the origin, so the barrier's first-order expectation is κ/a, matching the exact limit.
Odd states vanish at zero, so the delta term and the derivative jump both vanish. Their normalized wavefunctions and energies are

with zero wavefunction outside. They do not depend on κ. These even and odd sectors describe the central delta barrier in a symmetric infinite well.
