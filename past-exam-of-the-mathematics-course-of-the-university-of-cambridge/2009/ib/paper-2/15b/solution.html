For small transverse displacement, the wave equation on a string is
ytt​=c2yxx​,c2=T/ρ,​

where T is the tension and ρ is mass per unit length. Using separation of variables, put y=X(x)F(t), the ratios X′′/X=F′′/(c2F) equal a constant. The fixed-end conditions X(0)=X(L)=0 exclude nontrivial solutions for nonnegative separation constants; for −κ2 they give X=sin(κx) and κ=nπ/L, n≥1. The associated angular frequencies are ωn​=nπc/L. Thus the general superposition of separated normal modes is
y(x,t)=n≥1∑​[An​cos(ωn​t)+Bn​sin(ωn​t)]sinLnπx​.​

gn​=L2v​∫l−a/2l+a/2​sinLnπx​dx=nπ4v​sinLnπl​sin2Lnπa​.
