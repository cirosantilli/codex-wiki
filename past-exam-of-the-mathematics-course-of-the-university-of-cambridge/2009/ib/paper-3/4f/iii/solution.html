False. The rational numbers Q with their subspace topology from R are Hausdorff. If a subset contains two distinct rationals a<b, choose an irrational c between them; intersecting the subset with (−∞,c) and (c,∞) gives a separation into two nonempty relatively open sets. Thus every nonempty connected subset of Q is a singleton, so it is totally disconnected. Yet no rational is isolated, because every interval around it contains another rational. Therefore Q is not a discrete space. Hausdorffness and total disconnectedness do not imply discreteness.
