Choose the photon direction in the x′–y′ plane. Its four-momentum in the stellar rest frame is
p′μ=chν′​(1,cosθ′,sinθ′,0).
(35)
Let β=v/c, γ=(1−β2)−1/2. The Lorentz transformation to S gives
p0=γ(p′0+βp′x),px=γ(p′x+βp′0),py=p′y,pz=p′z.
(36)
Using p0=hν/c and px=(hν/c)cosθ yields the relativistic Doppler effect and relativistic aberration:
ν=γν′(1+βcosθ′),cosθ=1+βcosθ′cosθ′+β​.​
(37)
For the oppositely directed transverse photons, their rest-frame spatial momenta cancel and their total energy is E=2hν′. Boosting this combined four-vector gives laboratory energy γE and momentum
p=c2γEv​x=c21−v2/c2​Ev​x.​
(38)
The star loses rest mass E/c2. At unchanged speed its momentum therefore decreases by γ(E/c2)v, exactly the photon momentum. Its laboratory energy loss is likewise γE. There is no rest-frame recoil from the symmetric emission, and constant speed is consistent with four-momentum conservation because the stellar mass decreases.
