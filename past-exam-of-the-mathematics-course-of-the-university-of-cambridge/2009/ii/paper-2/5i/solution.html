An exponential dispersion family has density or mass function
p(y;θ,ϕ)=exp{a(ϕ)yθ−b(θ)​+c(y,ϕ)},
(10)
with support independent of the natural parameter θ. Usually a(ϕ)=ϕ/w for a known weight w. For the Poisson distribution, identify
θ=logλ,b(θ)=eθ,a(ϕ)=1,c(y,ϕ)=−log(y!),y∈N0​.
(11)
The dispersion is fixed at one. Differentiating the normalizing identity gives EY=b′(θ); differentiating again gives VarY=a(ϕ)b′′(θ). Here both derivatives equal eθ=λ, so
EY=λ,VarY=λ,V(μ)=μ.​
(12)
The variance function expresses b′′(θ) in terms of the mean μ. The canonical link function maps the mean to the natural parameter, so the Poisson canonical link is g(μ)=logμ.
