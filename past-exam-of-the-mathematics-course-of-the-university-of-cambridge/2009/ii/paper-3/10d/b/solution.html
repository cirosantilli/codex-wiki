Use the uniform-density configuration as a trial configuration for the white dwarf. Write P=CF​h2n5/3/me​, with CF​>0 the numerical coefficient suppressed by the approximate equation of state. Its particle density is n=3M/(4πmp​R3), and the nonrelativistic degeneracy pressure has kinetic-energy density 3P/2. Hence
Ekin​=R2α​,α=23​CF​me​h2​(4π3​)2/3(mp​M​)5/3.
(21)
For uniform density m(r)=M(r/R)3, so integration of the shell energy gives
Egrav​=−Rβ​,β=53​GM2.
(22)
Both constants are positive and independent of R. Differentiating E=α/R2−β/R gives its unique stationary radius R=2α/β. The second derivative there is 2α/R4>0, and this is the global minimum since E→+∞ at zero and E→0− at infinity. Thus
RWD​=5CF​(4π3​)2/3Gme​mp5/3​h2M−1/3​ ∼ Gme​mp5/3​h2M−1/3​.​
(23)
The uniform-density approximation is used for this energy estimate; it does not require a spatially constant pressure to satisfy the exact hydrostatic equilibrium equation.
