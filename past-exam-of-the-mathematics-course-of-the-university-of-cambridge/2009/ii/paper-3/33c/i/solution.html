Addition of angular momenta one and j gives J=∣j−1∣,∣j−1∣+1,…,j+1​, with projection condition M=q+m​. The usual bounds q∈{−1,0,1}, −j≤m≤j, −J≤M≤J must also hold. For j=0 only J=1 occurs; for j=1/2 the values are 3/2,1/2.
For j=1/2, abbreviate the product state ∣1,q⟩∣1/2,m⟩ by ∣q,↑⟩ or ∣q,↓⟩. The highest-weight state is ∣3/2,3/2⟩=∣1,↑⟩. Applying the total lowering operator, the sum of the two individual lowering operators, and dividing by the required ladder coefficient gives the whole quartet:
∣23​,23​⟩∣23​,21​⟩∣23​,−21​⟩∣23​,−23​⟩​=∣1,↑⟩,=32​​∣0,↑⟩+31​​∣1,↓⟩,=32​​∣0,↓⟩+31​​∣−1,↑⟩,=∣−1,↓⟩.​
(92)
The normalized vector orthogonal to the M=1/2 quartet state is annihilated by total raising, hence is the highest state of the remaining spin-1/2 representation. Lowering it gives
∣21​,21​⟩∣21​,−21​⟩​=31​​∣0,↑⟩−32​​∣1,↓⟩,=32​​∣−1,↑⟩−31​​∣0,↓⟩.​
(93)
These six orthonormal states span the six-dimensional product space, and are therefore all the states. Their relative signs are consistent with the lowering convention; a common phase for either multiplet is immaterial. The coefficients are the corresponding Clebsch-Gordan coefficients.
