Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2009 / ii / Paper 3 / 12F / b / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2009 ii Paper 3 12F b
OurBigBook.com Words: 118
A compact set K⊂C has exactly one unbounded complementary component, since its complement contains the outside of a sufficiently large disk. The connected, unbounded open set Ω, being disjoint from K, lies in that component. In particular ζ belongs to the unbounded component.
Fill all bounded components of C∖K to obtain the polynomial hull K. It is a compact set with connected complement, and ζ∈/K. Therefore 1/(z−ζ) is holomorphic on a neighborhood of K. The Runge approximation theorem gives polynomials Pn​ converging uniformly to this function on K, hence on K. This proves the requested uniform polynomial approximation on K, even when K itself has holes. If K is empty, the assertion is vacuous.
