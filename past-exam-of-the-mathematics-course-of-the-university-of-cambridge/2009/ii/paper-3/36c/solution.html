Use c=1 and metric signature (−,+,+,+), consistent with the supplied rest potential. In the Lorenz gauge, the Maxwell equations reduce to wave equations for the four-potential. A point worldline has current ja(x)=q∫ua(s)δ(4)(x−y(s))ds, where ua=dya/ds. Convolution with the retarded wave Green function θ(R0)δ(R2)/(2π) gives the stated integral: its support is the future light cone of each source point and it excludes incoming advanced signals.
For uniform motion ya(s)=(γs,0,0,γvs), ua=γ(1,0,0,v). Let R=x−y(s). The delta-function argument has derivative d(R2)/ds=−2R⋅u. Only the retarded root survives the step function, so
Aa(x)=4πμ0​q​−R⋅uua​​ret​.
(105)

Δ=1−v2vZ+Z2+(1−v2)r⊥2​​​,−R⋅u=γ[(1−v2)Δ−vZ]=γZ2+(1−v2)r⊥2​​.
(106)
Therefore, away from the worldline,
ϕ=4π(z−vt)2+(1−v2)(x2+y2)​μ0​q​,Az​=vϕ,Ax​=Ay​=0.​

A Lorentz transformation gives A0=γA′0, Az=γvA′0, exactly the expression above because r′=γZ2+(1−v2)r⊥2​​. Restoring SI units would require restoring the factors of c in both the four-potential convention and the supplied electrostatic normalization.
