Each can be normalized and supplies a distinct momentum label at zero energy. Hence zero energy is infinitely degenerate. In an infinite x direction the individual plane waves are generalized momentum states; square-integrable superpositions of their normalized transverse zero modes give ordinary transverse states by the Fourier isometry. The stipulated zero z momentum is understood per unit longitudinal length, or with periodic longitudinal normalization.
