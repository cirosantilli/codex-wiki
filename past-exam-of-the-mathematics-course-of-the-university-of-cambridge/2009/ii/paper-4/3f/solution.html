Let C be the middle-third Cantor set and choose a positive integer d with dlog2/log3≥k; this is possible for every real k, including negative k. Set X=Cd⊂Rd. It is closed and bounded since each factor is compact. If a subset of X is connected, each coordinate projection is a connected subset of C and hence a singleton; therefore the subset itself is a singleton. Thus X is totally disconnected.
For completeness, the dimension of these Cartesian powers of the Cantor set can be checked directly. At stage n there are 2dn cubes of side 3−n, giving vanishing s-dimensional cover cost whenever s>dlog2/log3. Conversely put independent equiprobable zero-or-two ternary digits on every coordinate. Each stage-n cube has probability 2−dn. If 3−n≤r<3−n+1, a Euclidean ball of radius r meets at most a constant depending on d such cubes. Hence its probability is at most Cd​rdlog2/log3. For any sufficiently fine cover, summing this bound over covering sets shows that its cost at exponent dlog2/log3 is bounded below by a positive constant, since total probability is one. These upper and lower Hausdorff measure bounds prove
dimH​X=dlog3log2​≥k.​
