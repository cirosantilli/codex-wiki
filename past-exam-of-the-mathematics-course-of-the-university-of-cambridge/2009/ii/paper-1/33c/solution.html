The ladder operators obey [a,a†]=1 and N=a†a has [N,a]=−a, [N,a†]=a†. Since ⟨ψ,Nψ⟩=∥aψ∥2≥0, its eigenvalues are nonnegative. If N∣ν⟩=ν∣ν⟩, lowering gives eigenvalue ν−1 and squared norm ν. Repeated lowering cannot produce a negative nonzero eigenstate; the norm product ν(ν−1)⋯ forces ν to be an integer. The final lowered state has a∣0⟩=0. With a unique ground state all normalized states are
∣n⟩=n!​(a†)n​∣0⟩,En​=ℏω(n+21​)​,n=0,1,….
(82)
For time-independent perturbation theory, write H′=H+λW with W=ℏω(a2+a†2). The standard first correction is λ⟨n∣W∣n⟩ and the second is λ2∑j=n​∣⟨j∣W∣n⟩∣2/(En​−Ej​). The first vanishes. Only j=n−2,n+2 occur, with ladder coefficients n(n−1)​ and (n+1)(n+2)​, respectively. Therefore
ΔEn(2)​=λ2ℏω[2n(n−1)​−2(n+1)(n+2)​]=−λ2ℏω(2n+1).
(83)
The downward term is zero for n<2.
Inverting the given position/momentum expressions gives
a2+a†2=ℏmω​x2−mℏωp​2​.
(84)
Thus α=1−2λ, β=1+2λ​. For ∣λ∣<1/2 both are positive, and this is a quantum harmonic oscillator of effective mass m/α and frequency ωαβ​. Its exact energies are
En′​=ℏω1−4λ2​(n+21​).​
(85)
Expansion gives En′​=ℏω(n+1/2)[1−2λ2+O(λ4)], agreeing with both perturbative corrections.
