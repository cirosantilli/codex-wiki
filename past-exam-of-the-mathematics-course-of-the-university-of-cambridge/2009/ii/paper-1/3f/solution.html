For the unit sphere, project from its north pole N=(0,0,1) onto the equatorial plane, identified with the complex plane. The line through N and (X,Y,Z) meets the plane at the stereographic projection
u=1−ZX+iY​,(X,Y,Z)=1+∣u∣2(2Reu,2Imu,∣u∣2−1)​.
(4)
The north pole is omitted from the plane chart; adding its image infinity gives the Riemann sphere.
For a point whose image and antipodal image are finite, stereographic projection of (−X,−Y,−Z) gives v=−(X+iY)/(1+Z). Therefore
uv=−1−Z2X2+Y2​=−1,
(5)
using X2+Y2+Z2=1. Conversely uv=−1 implies v=−1/u. Substituting this into the inverse stereographic projection changes the signs of all three coordinates, proving the converse. Thus finite chart images correspond to antipodes exactly when uv=−1. On the extended chart the exceptional antipodal pair is 0,∞, for which the displayed ordinary complex product is not defined.
