For the natural filtration Ft​=σ(Xs​:s≤t), a stopping time T takes values in [0,∞] and satisfies {T≤t}∈Ft​ for every t. Its occurrence is decidable from information available by that time.
The Strong Markov property says that on T<∞, conditionally on XT​, the post-T process is independent of FT​ and has the law of the original continuous-time Markov chain started at XT​. In particular Ei​[f(XT+s​)∣FT​]=Ps​f(XT​) on T<∞. The chain is an irreducible Markov chain if every state communicates with every other: for any i,j some time has Pi​(Xt​=j)>0, equivalently for finite rates there is a path of positive off-diagonal jump rates between them.
