For the Minkowski metric J=diag(1,−1), preservation means (Ax)TJ(Ay)=xTJy for all real vectors x,y. This is equivalent to ATJA=J, or, entrywise,
a2−c2=1,b2−d2=−1,ab−cd=0.
(47)
The restriction a>0 puts the first column on the right branch of the unit hyperbola, so uniquely a=cosht, c=sinht for a real parameter t. The third equation makes the second column a scalar multiple of (sinht,cosht)T: this also covers t=0, when b=0. The second equation makes that multiple ε=±1. Thus all the required matrices are
A=H(t)Dε​=(coshtsinht​εsinhtεcosht​),t∈R,ε=±1.​
(48)
The hyperbolic function addition formulas give H(t)H(s)=H(t+s), while Dε​H(s)=H(εs)Dε​. Consequently
(H(t)Dε​)(H(s)Dη​)=H(t+εs)Dεη​.
(49)
The product remains of the required form and its upper-left entry is positive. The identity is H(0)D1​, and the inverse is H(−εt)Dε​. Together with associativity of matrix multiplication, these identities establish the group property. This is the time-orientation-preserving Lorentz group in one spatial dimension.
