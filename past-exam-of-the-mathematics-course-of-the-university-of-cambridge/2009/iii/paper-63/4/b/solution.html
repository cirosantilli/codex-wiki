To obtain the specified expansion, use the usual nova-ejection prescription: the escaping material is expelled isotropically from the white dwarf and carries its specific orbital angular momentum, with no additional torque. This is isotropic re-emission from a binary star. Circularity alone does not determine the angular momentum carried by the ejecta; the prescription is an additional physical assumption.
