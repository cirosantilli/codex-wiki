For q>0, divide the two sink terms by P=Cu​Sq2. Put
a=Cu​SCρ2​Lρ​N2​,b=Lu​Cu​S1​.
(39)
The requested ratio is
R(q)=PB+ϵ​=qa​+bq.​
(40)
Here b>0 and a≥0. With stable stratification a>0, R diverges at both q↓0 and q→∞, and has a single minimum at qm​=a/b​, with Rmin​=2ab​. Increasing the stable density-gradient magnitude increases a, lifting the minimum and moving it rightward. When the mass density gradient vanishes, a=0 and the curve reduces to the straight line R=bq, with zero only as a limiting value at q=0 where the original ratio is undefined.
The plot uses q​=bq=q/(Lu​Cu​S) and A=ab=[Lρ​Cρ2​/(Lu​Cu2​)]Ri, so R=q​+A/q​. The horizontal line R=1 selects equilibria: two positive intersections for 0<A<1/4, one double intersection at A=1/4, and none above it. The zero-gradient line has one positive equilibrium; its formal zero root before division by q is the non-turbulent state.
