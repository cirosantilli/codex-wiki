Let the mean streamwise velocity be U(y) and set S=dU/dy>0. The relevant covariance is u′v′<0 on this half of the channel. The work of the Reynolds stress against the mean shear rate gives
P=−u′v′dydU​.​
(25)
The eddy viscosity relation is −u′v′=νT​S, so
νT​=dU/dy−u′v′​,P=νT​(dydU​)2.​
(26)
These are the kinematic-stress conventions, with the covariance measured per unit mass; inserting a stress measured in force per area would require division by mass density.
