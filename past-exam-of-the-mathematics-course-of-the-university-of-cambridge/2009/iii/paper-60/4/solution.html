On a bosonic background, a Killing spinor is a nonzero spinor parameter that makes all fermionic supersymmetry variations vanish. It identifies an unbroken supersymmetry of the background; the number of independent solutions counts preserved supercharges. In simple ungauged minimal four-dimensional supergravity, with the gravitino set to zero, its equation is ∇μ​ϵ=0 and the bosonic field equations are Rμν​=0. In theories with additional fields or a cosmological deformation, the Killing-spinor equation instead uses the corresponding modified connection.
Use commuting spinors to form ordinary geometric bilinears, and orient the Dirac current to the future with ϵˉ=−ϵ†γ0^ in mostly-plus signature. Since the spinor and gamma matrices are parallel,
∇μ​Kν​=(∇μ​ϵˉ)γν​ϵ+ϵˉγν​∇μ​ϵ=0.
(21)
Thus K is actually parallel, and in particular is a Killing vector field. In a local orthonormal frame,
K0^=ϵ†ϵ>0,Ki^=ϵ†αiϵ,αi=−γ0^γi^.
(22)
For every spatial unit vector u, ui​αi is Hermitian and squares to one. Its eigenvalues are ±1, so ∣ui​Ki∣≤K0. Taking u along the spatial current gives
Kμ​Kμ=−(K0)2+∣K∣2≤0.​
(23)
A nonzero parallel spinor cannot vanish at an isolated point, so this causal Dirac current is nonzero everywhere on a connected solution. Its squared norm is constant: it cannot alternate between timelike and null regions.
If K is timelike, normalize its constant norm. Its parallel dual one-form is closed, and its orthogonal distribution is integrable. Adapted local coordinates put the metric in the product form
ds2=−dt2+hij​(x)dxidxj.
(24)
The time independence follows because K is Killing, and the unit lapse and zero cross terms follow from parallelism. The vacuum equations give Rij​(h)=0. In three dimensions the Riemann tensor is completely determined by the Ricci tensor and its scalar contraction, so h is flat. Therefore
Every timelike-current solution is locally Minkowski spacetime.​
(25)
Constant spinors in its flat frame provide all local Killing spinors. Global identifications are possible only when they preserve a compatible spin structure and the required spinors.
If K is null, its parallelism first gives a Brinkmann coordinate system with K=∂v​. The parallel-spinor condition further restricts the local spin holonomy to the stabilizer of that spinor. In four dimensions, for a nonzero Weyl component represented by (1,0)T, its stabilizer in SL(2,C) is the upper-triangular group (10​b1​), b∈C: the two real null rotations, with no transverse rotation. Consequently the transverse frame is flat and the allowed curvature is of the wave type Ruiuj​. Using the coordinate freedom to remove transverse cross terms gives the local plane-fronted gravitational wave form
ds2=−2dudv+dx2+dy2+H(u,x,y)du2,Hxx​+Hyy​=0.​
(26)
Here Ruiuj​=−Hij​/2 and the only potentially nonzero Ricci component is Ruu​=−(Hxx​+Hyy​)/2, so the last equation is exactly the vacuum equation. Conversely, in the coframe e+=du, e−=dv−Hdu/2, ei=dxi, the nonzero spin-connection matrices are proportional to Hi​γ+idu. Constant spinors with γ+ϵ=0 are therefore parallel. Every harmonic transverse profile, with arbitrary u dependence, gives such a supersymmetric vacuum wave; the flat profile is included. This verifies both the form and sufficiency in the parallel-spinor classification of vacuum four-geometries.
There is a reality-condition subtlety in interpreting the branches. A single commuting four-dimensional Majorana spinor has a null current of a commuting Majorana spinor: its two-component representation gives ∣ξ†σξ∣2=(ξ†ξ)2. A timelike current can instead come from a Dirac Killing spinor or a sum of independent real-spinor currents. The conditional timelike classification above remains valid, but does not assert a generic timelike current for a single real Majorana parameter.
For the final question use constant sectional curvature −1/a2, so Rμν​=−3gμν​/a2. The spinor curvature identity gives
[∇μ​,∇ν​]ϵ=−2a21​γμν​ϵ.
(27)
Define the Anti-de Sitter Killing-spinor connection Dμ​=∇μ​+γμ​/(2a). Since ∇μ​γν​=0 and the connection is torsion-free,
[Dμ​,Dν​]=−2a21​γμν​+4a21​[γμ​,γν​]=0.
(28)
Thus the modified connection is flat. On a simply connected region, transport any initial spinor with this connection; flatness makes the result path independent and supplies actual nonzero solutions, not merely a necessary integrability condition. Hence
∇μ​ϵ+2a1​γμ​ϵ=0​
(29)
has a full local space of solutions on Anti-de Sitter spacetime, and globally on its universal cover. Global quotients require compatible spinor monodromy. These are Killing spinors of the cosmological deformation, not parallel-spinor vacua of the undeformed zero-cosmological-constant theory.
