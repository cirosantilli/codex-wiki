Fix the coefficient-column convention: write the local frame as a row e=(e1​,…,er​) and a section as s=eu for a column u of functions. Define the connection matrix θ=(θij​) by
∇ej​=∑i​ei​θij​,∇(eu)=e(du+θu).
(48)
Each entry is a smooth one-form. Define the curvature form of a connection by
RE(X,Y)s=∇X​∇Y​s−∇Y​∇X​s−∇[X,Y]​s.
(49)
The same Leibniz cancellations as for tangent-bundle curvature show that this is linear over smooth functions in X,Y,s. Thus it is a two-form with values in the endomorphisms of E. Its curvature matrix Θ=(Θij​) is specified by RE(X,Y)ej​=∑i​ei​Θij​(X,Y).
The covariant exterior derivative on coefficient forms is d+θ∧. Squaring it on u gives
(d+θ∧)2u=dθu−θ∧du+θ∧du+θ∧θu.
(50)
Therefore the Cartan curvature matrix equation is
Θ=dθ+θ∧θ,Θij​=dθij​+k∑​θik​∧θkj​.​
(51)
Matrix-valued wedge products here combine matrix multiplication with the wedge product of differential forms, in the stated order.
Using d2=0 and the graded Leibniz rule,
dΘ=dθ∧θ−θ∧dθ.
(52)
On the other hand, associativity of the endomorphism-valued exterior product gives
Θ∧θ−θ∧Θ​=dθ∧θ+θ∧θ∧θ−θ∧dθ−θ∧θ∧θ=dθ∧θ−θ∧dθ.​
(53)
The cubic terms cancel, proving the requested Bianchi identity
dΘ=Θ∧θ−θ∧Θ.​
(54)
