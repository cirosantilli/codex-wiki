The theorem on asymptotic normality of a maximum likelihood estimator says that in a regular identifiable model with nonsingular information the estimator is approximately multivariate normal, centred at the true parameter and with covariance equal to the inverse total information. Here one can regard one observation from each group as an independent pair and apply its usual iid version to n such pairs. Therefore
ν ∼˙ N(ν,ne−α+e−α−ν​).​
(16)
For a direct check, the second score equation gives the fitted second-group mean YB​, and the first then gives the fitted first-group mean YA​. Thus the Poisson log-rate ratio estimate is ν=logYB​−logYA​. The delta method, using independent sample-mean variances a/n and b/n, gives the same variance (a−1+b−1)/n. If a group total is zero, its finite log-mean estimator fails to exist; with fixed positive true means this event has probability tending to zero exponentially as n grows.
