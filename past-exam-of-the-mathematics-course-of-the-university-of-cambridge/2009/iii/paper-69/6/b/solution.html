The Weierstrass M-test gives a continuous periodic Weierstrass function, since ∑a−k<∞. For 5m≤n<5m+1, the given best approximant leaves the tail
g−tn​=∑k=m+1∞​a−kcos(5kx).
(35)
Its supremum norm is at most the sum of its coefficients, and at x=0 all cosine terms equal one, attaining that bound. Consequently
En​(g)=k=m+1∑∞​a−k=a−1a−m​.​
(36)
Put α=lna/ln5. Then a−m=(5m)−α and n/5m∈[1,5), giving En​(g)≍n−α, with constants depending only on a.
For 1<a<5, part (a) gives ω(g,1/n)≤Ca​n−α. The first Jackson theorem for periodic approximation, En​(g)≤Cω(g,1/n), supplies the matching lower bound. Hence the modulus of continuity has the precise order
ω(g,δ)≍δlna/ln5,1<a<5,δ↓0.​
(37)
At a=5, the inverse theorem for trigonometric approximation gives the upper bound Cδln(1/δ), and part (c) proves the matching lower bound. Thus the critical Weierstrass modulus is
ω(g,δ)≍δln(1/δ),a=5.​
(38)
To pass from scales 1/n to all small δ, use monotonicity of the modulus of continuity and adjacent reciprocals, which are comparable to δ.
