Put d=k−1 and let em​ be the elementary symmetric polynomial of degree m in the d interior spline knots ti+1​,…,ti+d​, with e0​=1. Expanding the knot polynomial gives
ωi​(x)=∏r=1d​(x−ti+r​)=∑m=0d​(−1)mem​(ti+1​,…,ti+d​)xd−m.
(31)
Compare the coefficient of xd−m in the Marsden identity. The left coefficient is (−1)m(md​)tm; the right coefficient is (−1)m∑i​em​(ti+1​,…,ti+d​)Ni​(t). Cancelling the common sign therefore gives the monomial B-spline coefficients:
ai(m)​=(mk−1​)em​(ti+1​,…,ti+k−1​)​,0≤m≤k−1.​
(32)
Equivalently, for m≥1 the numerator is ∑1≤r1​<⋯<rm​≤k−1​ti+r1​​⋯ti+rm​​. In particular ai(0)​=1, so the order-zero case reproduces constants, and for k≥2 the linear coefficients are ai(1)​=(ti+1​+⋯+ti+k−1​)/(k−1), the Greville abscissae. At m=k−1, the coefficient is the product of all interior knots. Each formula holds on the basic knot interval used in the Marsden identity.
