In a two-dimensional conformal field theory, a quasi-primary operator of conformal weights (h,h) obeys
O′(z′,zˉ′)=(dzdz′​)−h(dzˉdzˉ′​)−hO(z,zˉ)
(12)
for global conformal, or Möbius, transformations. A primary operator obeys this law for every locally invertible holomorphic and antiholomorphic conformal coordinate change. Quasi-primary is therefore weaker: the stress tensor at nonzero central charge is quasi-primary, but its Schwarzian transformation term prevents it from being primary under a general map.
A primary state is a simultaneous eigenstate of L0​,L0​ with eigenvalues h,h, satisfying
Ln​∣O⟩=Ln​∣O⟩=0(n>0).​
(13)
A quasi-primary state need only satisfy the corresponding n=1 conditions. Negative modes of the Virasoro algebra generate descendants.
In radial quantization, a local operator at the origin prepares a state on a surrounding circle; logarithmic radius is Euclidean time. The state–operator correspondence is ∣O⟩=O(0)∣0⟩. Acting with a Virasoro generator means inserting its stress-tensor contour around that operator. For a primary operator, the operator product expansion is
T(z)O(0)∼z2hO(0)​+z∂O(0)​.
(14)
Multiplication by zn+1 and the contour residue give L0​∣O⟩=h∣O⟩, L−1​∣O⟩=∣∂O⟩, and Ln​∣O⟩=0 for n>0. Conversely the contour modes are the coefficients of the singular expansion; vanishing positive modes removes every pole of order three and higher. The remaining double and simple poles generate precisely the primary transformation law. The antiholomorphic argument is identical. This proves the state-operator correspondence for Virasoro primaries in both directions.
An integrated string vertex operator is integrated with d2z. The measure transforms with weights (−1,−1), so conformal invariance requires its matter operator to have weights (1,1). In old covariant string quantization the same requirement is Ln>0​=Ln>0​=0 and L0​=L0​=1. The ghost-dressed unintegrated vertex ccˉV has total weights (0,0). Physical states are identified modulo null string states, or more systematically by BRST cohomology; raw counting of all primary operators includes gauge redundancy.
For a free embedding boson the holomorphic stress tensor is T=−:∂X⋅∂X:/α′. Contracting it with :eik⋅X: gives weight α′k2/4; every holomorphic oscillator of level r adds r to that weight. Thus
h=N+4α′k2​,h=N+4α′k2​.
(15)
With Lorentzian target signature, M2=−k2, so the closed-bosonic-string mass shell from primary weights is
N=N,M2=α′4​(N−1).​
(16)
The ground state is tachyonic, level one is massless, and higher levels are massive. The primary conditions also constrain the polarization tensors; the two zero-mode conditions fix the mass and level matching.
For the commuting beta-gamma system, write r=z−w. The displayed exchange leaves the arguments attached to their respective fields. Exchanging the arguments instead gives
β(z)γ(w)∼−r1​,γ(z)β(w)∼+r1​.
(17)
This radial-ordering sign in commuting beta-gamma contractions must be kept when differentiating. Expanding the total derivative in the proposed stress tensor gives
T=(1−λ):∂βγ:−λ:β∂γ:.
(18)
The contraction γ(z)β(w)∼1/r and its derivative ∂γ(z)β(w)∼−1/r2 give
T(z)β(w)∼(1−λ)r∂β(z)​+λr2β(z)​=r2λβ(w)​+r∂β(w)​.
(19)
Similarly ∂β(z)γ(w)∼1/r2 and β(z)γ(w)∼−1/r give
T(z)γ(w)∼(1−λ)r2γ(z)​+λr∂γ(z)​=r2(1−λ)γ(w)​+r∂γ(w)​.
(20)
There are no higher poles. Both fields are holomorphic and Tˉ=0, so
(hβ​,hβ​)=(λ,0),(hγ​,hγ​)=(1−λ,0).​
(21)
To compute the central charge, put A=:∂βγ: and B=:β∂γ:. Only double Wick contractions can give the fourth-order pole in TT. Their coefficients are
A(z)A(w)∼r41​,B(z)B(w)∼r41​,A(z)B(w)∼r42​,B(z)A(w)∼r42​,
(22)
where only the double-contraction terms are shown. For example, the mixed contraction uses ∂β(z)∂γ(w)∼2/r3 and γ(z)β(w)∼1/r. All Wick pairings have bosonic signs. Therefore the fourth-order coefficient is
(1−λ)2+λ2−4λ(1−λ)=6λ2−6λ+1.
(23)
The stress-tensor operator product expansion has fourth-order coefficient c/2, yielding
c=12λ2−12λ+2,cˉ=0.​
(24)
This is the stress tensor of a commuting beta-gamma system calculation. Single contractions supply the usual 2T/r2+∂T/r terms, with the potential third-order terms canceling.
