gs2g−2+n​.​
(26)
This is the string genus expansion. Four external strings on a sphere give gs2​, as in the displayed tree amplitude.
Use the conventional complex-coordinate measure d2z=2d(Rez)d(Imz), with ∂=(∂x​−i∂y​)/2. The action then has the standard real-coordinate normalization (4πα′)−1∫(∇X)2d2x. In these conventions the free-boson worldsheet propagator is
⟨Xμ(z,zˉ)Xν(w,wˉ)⟩=−2α′​δμνlog∣z−w∣2,​

up to an additive infrared constant. Indeed the quadratic kernel after integration by parts is −(πα′)−1∂∂ˉ, and ∂∂ˉlog∣z−w∣2=2πδ(2)(z−w) for this measure. Their product gives the required delta function. A simultaneous change of the coordinate-measure convention and action coefficient leaves this physical normalization unchanged.

The distance-product factor attached to insertion i is ∣czi​+d∣−α′∑j=i​pi​⋅pj​. Momentum conservation changes its exponent to α′pi2​. Thus the total factor at each insertion, including the measure, is

On the tachyon mass shell pi2​=4/α′ it is one. This proves the Möbius invariance of on-shell closed-string vertex integrals. The correlator is covariant; the correlator times all four measures is invariant. This is the invariant integrand needed to quotient by the residual SL(2,C) transformations.
For the massless Type II superstring theory amplitude, use s=−(p1​+p2​)2 in Lorentzian target signature and s+t+u=0. At generic fixed t, the numerator factor Γ(−α′s/4) has simple poles at
s=Mn2​=α′4n​,n=0,1,2,….​
(31)
At these points Γ(1+α′s/4)=Γ(1+n)=n! is finite and nonzero. The t,u factors are also finite and nonzero for generic t, so the poles persist; isolated residue zeros at special kinematics do not remove the general mass level. Tree-level factorization interprets a pole at s=M2 as exchange of a state of that mass. The Type II gamma-factor mass poles therefore exhibit a massless level and an infinite tower of massive levels, with equally spaced squared masses 4/α′.
There is no negative-s pole in this exchanged tower. In particular, at negative integer values of α′s/4 the reciprocal s-channel denominator can instead have zeros. This is consistent with the absence of the bosonic tachyon in the Type II superstring mass spectrum. The factor alone does not determine degeneracies, spins or every possible uncoupled state; external polarizations and the rest of the amplitude control their residues.
