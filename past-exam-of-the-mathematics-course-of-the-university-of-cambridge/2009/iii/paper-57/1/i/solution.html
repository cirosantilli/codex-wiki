Use H=a′/a for the conformal expansion rate and H=a′/a2 for the physical Hubble parameter. A future unit normal to a constant-τ slice is
nμ=(a−1,0,0,0),nμ​=(−a,0,0,0).​
(1)
It has norm minus one, and the spatial projection tensor is Pμν​=diag(0,1,1,1). The nonzero connections needed here are Γ000​=H, Γ0ij​=Hδij​ and Γi0j​=Hδij​. Thus ∇i​nj​=−Γ0ij​n0​=a′δij​. Projection removes every normal component, yielding
K00​=K0i​=0,Kij​=a′δij​,Kij​=Hδij​,K=3H.​
(2)
This uses the positive-expansion convention for extrinsic curvature in the question. With the alternative convention that inserts an overall minus sign, all these extrinsic-curvature quantities change sign.
For a comoving cell, the physical volume is proportional to a3. Its logarithmic derivative in proper time is 3a˙/a=3H=K. Thus the trace is the expansion rate of the normal congruence: positive for expansion and negative for contraction. The isotropic tensor Kij​=Hδij​ has vanishing trace-free shear. It measures homogeneous expansion, not the intrinsic spatial curvature, which is zero in this flat model.
