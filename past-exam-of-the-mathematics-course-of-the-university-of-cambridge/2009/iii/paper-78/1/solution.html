Let χ=H(F) use the Heaviside step function, and δs​=∣∇F∣δ(F), so that ns​=∇F/∣∇F∣ points from the body into the fluid. The kinematic boundary condition gives Ft​+u⋅∇F=0 on the surface. In the exterior, mass conservation and the Euler equations for an inviscid fluid are
ρt​+∂i​(ρui​)=0,∂t​(ρui​)+∂j​(ρui​uj​+pδij​)=0.
(1)
Products with χ are understood distributionally, using ∂t​χ=Ft​δ(F) and ∂i​χ=Fi​δ(F). With ρ′=ρ−ρ0​, the extended continuity equation is
∂t​(ρ′χ)+∂i​(ρui​χ)=ρ0​u⋅∇Fδ(F).
(2)
The corresponding extended momentum equation is
∂t​(ρui​χ)+∂j​[(ρui​uj​+pδij​)χ]=pFi​δ(F),
(3)
because its advective surface term is ρui​(Ft​+u⋅∇F)δ(F)=0. Differentiate continuity in time and substitute the divergence of momentum. Subtracting c02​∇2(ρ′χ) gives the Ffowcs Williams-Hawkings equation
(∂t2​−c02​∇2)(ρ′χ)=∂t​(ρ0​u⋅∇Fδ(F))−∂i​(pFi​δ(F))+∂i​∂j​(Tij​χ),​
(4)
where the inviscid Lighthill stress tensor is
Tij​=ρui​uj​+(p−c02​ρ′)δij​.
(5)
If p denotes total pressure, this tensor includes the constant ambient pressure p0​. Its contribution p0​∇2χ cancels the corresponding surface term exactly. For radiation integrals it is convenient to subtract p0​ from both terms, making Tij​=ρui​uj​+(p′−c02​ρ′)δij​ with p′=p−p0​. The displayed source identity is unchanged by this consistent pressure-gauge subtraction.
Assume no incoming sound and no free initial acoustic field. Put Ry​=∣x−y∣, τy​=t−Ry​/c0​, Q=ρ0​un​δs​, and Pi​=p′ns,i​δs​. Convolution with the retarded acoustic Green function gives the three source integrals, for an observer outside the body:
ρm′​ρf′​ρS′​​=4πc02​1​∂t​∫R3​Ry​Q(y,τy​)​d3y,=−4πc02​1​∂xi​​∫R3​Ry​Pi​(y,τy​)​d3y,=4πc02​1​∂xi​​∂xj​​∫R3​Ry​Tij​(y,τy​)H(F(y,τy​))​d3y.​​
(6)
These are the surface thickness, surface loading and volume quadrupole contributions. The retarded surface Dirac delta distributions must remain inside the integrals: integrating them over a moving retarded surface produces the moving-surface retarded Jacobian, and is not generally the same as simply substituting one retarded time into an instantaneous surface integral.
For an acoustic compact-source approximation, suppose the dominant sources have size ℓ, source frequency scale ω, and ωℓ/c0​≪1. Also require r=∣x∣≫ℓ and the acoustic far field ωr/c0​≫1. Then Ry​≃r−n⋅y, 1/Ry​≃1/r, and the leading source integrals use the common time τ=t−r/c0​. Slow source motion relative to c0​ is implicit in this leading retardation approximation. Define
m(t)=ρ0​∫S(t)​un​dS,fi​(t)=−∫S(t)​p′ns,i​dS,Sij​(t)=∫F>0​Tij​d3y.
(7)
Thus m is net displaced mass per unit time, f is the fluid pressure force on the body, and Sij​ is the integrated excess Lighthill stress tensor. The minus sign in f fixes the requested loading convention; the force on the fluid is opposite. On the radiating 1/r terms, ∂xi​​≃−(ni​/c0​)∂t​, giving
ρ′(x,t)≃4πrc02​m˙(τ)​−4πrc03​ni​f˙​i​(τ)​+4πrc04​ni​nj​S¨ij​(τ)​.​
(8)
Spatial derivatives of 1/r produce near-field terms of orders r−2 and r−3, omitted here. Neglecting source delays is a separate approximation from discarding those observer near-field terms. If a leading integrated source vanishes by symmetry, its first nonzero delay correction can be as important as another retained multipole.
For the translating sphere, write its center as X(t)=a1​cos(ωt)e, with e a unit vector, and its velocity as V=X˙. Let V=4πa3/3, η=aω/c0​≪1 and d=a1​/a≪1. Impermeability gives un​=V⋅ns​. Since its volume is constant,
m=ρ0​V⋅∫S​ns​dS=0,ρm˙′​=0​

in the leading compact formula. The individual local thickness sources have scale ρ0​(a1​/r)η2, but their net monopole cancels.
At first order in amplitude and leading order in η, the exterior incompressible velocity potential is ϕ=−a3V⋅rs​/(2rs3​), where rs​ is measured from the mean center. It has ∂rs​​ϕ=V⋅ns​ at rs​=a. Linear Bernoulli equation gives p′=−ρ0​ϕt​=(ρ0​a/2)V˙⋅ns​ there. Since ∫ns,i​ns,j​dS=(4πa2/3)δij​,
f=−Ma​V˙,Ma​=21​ρ0​V=32π​ρ0​a3.

ρf˙​′​=4πrc03​Ma​n⋅V¨(τ)​=6rc03​ρ0​a3a1​ω3​(n⋅e)sin(ωτ).​
(11)
It scales as ρ0​(a1​/r)η3. In homentropic small-amplitude flow the volume stress is quadratic to leading order, Tij​=O(ρ0​V2) over volume O(a3), so the volume acoustic quadrupole has scale
ρS′​=O(ρ0​ara12​​η4).
(12)

For completeness, the exact thickness-source integral has a nonzero first delay correction even though m=0. Its first spatial moment is
Mj​=ρ0​∫S​yj​V⋅ns​dS=ρ0​VVj​.
(13)
Expand its integrand at τy​=τ+n⋅y/c0​. The leading nonzero translating-sphere acoustic thickness dipole is therefore
ρth′​=4πrc03​ρ0​Vn⋅V¨(τ)​=3rc03​ρ0​a3a1​ω3​(n⋅e)sin(ωτ).​

ρlinear′​=2rc03​ρ0​a3a1​ω3​(n⋅e)sin(ωτ),pfar′​=c02​ρlinear′​.​
