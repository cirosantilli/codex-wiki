Write t=logμ and use αi​=gi2​/(4π). The renormalization-group beta function implies
dtdαi​​=2πβi​​αi2​,dtdαi−1​​=−2πβi​​.
(15)
Integrating the one-loop running coupling equation gives
αi−1​(μ)=αi−1​(MZ​)−2πβi​​logMZ​μ​.​
(16)
Here the sign of βi​ is the sign convention in the question: a negative coefficient makes the inverse coupling increase towards high energy.
For gauge coupling unification, let L=log(MGUT​/MZ​)/(2π) and let aG​ be the common inverse coupling at the unification scale. The normalized hypercharge coupling is (5/3)α1​, so
53​α1−1​(MZ​)=aG​+53​β1​L,α2−1​(MZ​)=aG​+β2​L,α3−1​(MZ​)=aG​+β3​L.
(17)
Subtract the second relation from the first to determine L, then subtract it from the third. Provided 3β1​/5−β2​=0, this proves
α3−1​(MZ​)=α2−1​(MZ​)+3β1​/5−β2​β3​−β2​​[53​α1−1​(MZ​)−α2−1​(MZ​)].​
(18)
The factors 3/5 belong to the inverse normalized coupling; dropping them changes the prediction.
Use the Standard Model gauge group SU(3)C​×SU(2)L​×U(1)Y​ and convention Q=T3​+Y. One fermion family and one complex Higgs doublet have the following Standard Model representations. The left-handed quark and lepton doublets contain two Weyl spinors each; the right-handed fields are single Weyl species.
fieldQL​=(uL​,dL​)uR​dR​LL​=(νL​,eL​)eR​ϕ​SU(3)C​333111​SU(2)L​211212​Y1/62/3−1/3−1/2−11/2​spin1/21/21/21/21/20​chiralityLRRLRnot applicable​​
(19)
The row uR​ denotes the right-handed up quark. Equivalently an all-left-handed table replaces the three right-handed fields by uRc​:(3,1)−2/3​, dRc​:(3,1)1/3​, and eRc​:(1,1)1​; these are alternative descriptions, not extra fermions. The minimal model has no right-handed neutrino. For completeness, the family-independent gauge fields are the spin-one gluons (8,1)0​, weak gauge bosons (1,3)0​, and hypercharge gauge boson (1,1)0​; they are not counted once per family.
For the Standard Model one-loop gauge coefficients, include three fermion families and one Higgs doublet. With T(N)=1/2, the colour Dynkin index sum in one family is
∑f,one family​T3​(f)=2⋅21​+21​+21​=2.
(20)
The first factor two counts the two members of QL​; the colour trace is already included in the Dynkin index. There is no coloured scalar. Hence
β3​=−11+32​(3⋅2)=−7.​
(21)
For SU(2)L​, the three colours of QL​ and the lepton doublet give 3(1/2)+1/2=2 per family. The Higgs contributes T2​(ϕ)=1/2. Thus
β2​=−322​+32​(3⋅2)+31​21​=−619​.​
(22)
For U(1)Y​, sum squared hypercharges over every colour and doublet component. One family contributes
6(61​)2+3(32​)2+3(−31​)2+2(−21​)2+(−1)2=310​.
(23)
The two complex Higgs components contribute 2(1/2)2=1/2. There is no Abelian gauge self-interaction term, so
β1​=32​(3⋅310​)+31​21​=641​.​
(24)
This β1​ is for the unnormalized coupling gY​ used in the question. For g1GUT​=5/3​gY​, the corresponding coefficient is (3/5)β1​=41/10. This distinction is required for a consistent unification calculation.
