Use Minkowski metric signature +−−− and natural units. With no lepton mixing, choose the weak charged current convention
Jα=∑ℓ=e,μ,τ​νℓ​γα(1−γ5​)ℓ.
(1)
Its conjugate convention exchanges J and J† without changing the interaction. The relevant four-fermion interaction gives the muon decay amplitude, up to an overall phase,
M=2​GF​​[u(k)γα(1−γ5​)v(q)][u(q′)γα​(1−γ5​)u(p)].​
(2)
Here GF​ is the Fermi constant; the factors 1−γ5​ are twice the left chiral projector, so there is no additional factor of two to insert.
For the final-spin sum let ρμ​=(p+m)(1+γ5​s)/2, where m=mμ​. It already describes the specified initial polarization; there is no further initial-spin average. The two gamma matrix traces are
∑final spins​∣M∣2=2GF2​​LαβHαβ​,Lαβ=tr[(k+me​)γα(1−γ5​)qγβ(1−γ5​)],
(3)
Hαβ​=tr[q′γα​(1−γ5​)ρμ​γβ​(1−γ5​)].
(4)
The electron-mass term vanishes in its chiral trace. In the muon trace, anticommutation with γ5​ gives
(1−γ5​)ρμ​γβ​(1−γ5​)=(p−ms)γβ​(1−γ5​).
(5)
For the chiral fermion trace contraction, define
Kαβ(a,b)=aαbβ+aβbα−gαβa⋅b+iϵαβρσaρ​bσ​.
(6)
The supplied trace conventions imply Lαβ=8Kαβ(k,q) and Hαβ​=4Kαβ​(q′,r), where r=p−ms. The factor four in the second trace follows from the polarization projector. The symmetric parts contract to 2[(a⋅c)(b⋅d)+(a⋅d)(b⋅c)]. The Levi-Civita symbol contraction, including i2=−1, contributes 2[(a⋅c)(b⋅d)−(a⋅d)(b⋅c)]. Mixed symmetric-antisymmetric contractions vanish. Thus Kαβ(a,b)Kαβ​(c,d)=4(a⋅c)(b⋅d), and
∑∣M∣2=64GF2​(k⋅q′)q⋅(p−ms),A=64,B=−64.​
(7)
Now neglect me​ as well. Put Q=p−k and c=k⋅s. The given moment integral over two-neutrino relativistic two-body phase space yields
∫∣q∣∣q′∣d3qd3q′​δ4(Q−q−q′)(k⋅q′)(r⋅q)=3π​(k⋅Q)(r⋅Q)+6π​(k⋅r)Q2.
(8)
In the muon rest frame, k⋅Q=mE, k⋅r=mE(1+c), r⋅Q=m2−mE(1+c) and Q2=m2−2mE. Substitution gives
6πm3E​[(3−2x)+(1−2x)c],x=2E/m.
(9)
Combining this with the Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure, whose one-particle denominator is (2π)32E, and d3k=E2dEdΩ, gives the polarized muon decay distribution
dxdΩdΓ​=384π4GF2​m5​x2[(3−2x)+(1−2x)k⋅s],0≤x≤1.​
(10)
Consequently C=−2, D=−2, E=1. Integration over the electron direction removes the polarization term, and ∫01​x2(3−2x)dx=1/2, recovering Γ=GF2​m5/(192π3).
The spin vector is axial and the momentum direction is polar: under parity, s stays unchanged while k reverses. Their scalar product therefore changes sign. Its nonzero coefficient demonstrates parity violation in polarized muon decay, arising from the left-chiral weak charged current. At the upper endpoint the negative muon's electron is preferentially emitted opposite the spin, providing a sign check on the result.
