Suppose first that U is not contained in (Sλ)⊥. Since polytabloids span Sλ, there exist u∈U and a tableau t with ⟨u,et​⟩=0. The column antisymmetrizer identity from the preliminaries gives
κt​u=⟨u,et​⟩et​∈U.
(29)
Therefore et​∈U. Because U is an Sn​-submodule, it contains all translates get​=egt​, and these span the Specht module. Hence Sλ⊆U. If the initial supposition fails, U⊆(Sλ)⊥. This proves the James submodule theorem
U⊇SλorU⊆(Sλ)⊥.​
(30)
Now let W be a submodule of Sλ. Apply the dichotomy to W⊆Mλ. Either W=Sλ, or W⊆Sλ∩(Sλ)⊥. Positivity of the Hermitian form gives Sλ∩(Sλ)⊥={0}: a vector in this intersection has squared norm zero. The same intersection is zero for the bilinear form by the agreement of complements explained above. Since Sλ=0, the only submodules are 0 and itself. Therefore
Sλ is simple over C.​
(31)
This use of characteristic zero and nondegeneracy is why the conclusion does not automatically extend to every modular Specht module.
