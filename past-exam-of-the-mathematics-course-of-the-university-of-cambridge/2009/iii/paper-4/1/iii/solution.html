Let V afford the irreducible character χ. The positive character inner product ⟨χH​,1H​⟩H​ is the multiplicity of the trivial representation in V restricted to H, so there is a nonzero vector v fixed by every element of H. Since N⊆kerχ, every element of N acts as the identity on all of V. By G=NH, every element of G therefore fixes v.
The line Cv is a nonzero invariant subspace of the irreducible representation V, so it must equal V. The action on this line is trivial, proving
χ=1G​.​
(10)
The hypothesis on the kernel of a character is what makes an H-fixed vector into a G-fixed vector.
