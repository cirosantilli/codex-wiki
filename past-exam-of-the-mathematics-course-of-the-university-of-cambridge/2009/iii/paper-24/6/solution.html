Work internally with the Heyting algebra Ω and the truth value u of U↪1. For o(u)(p)=u⇒p, the identities
u⇒⊤=⊤,p≤u⇒p,u⇒(p∧q)=(u⇒p)∧(u⇒q),u⇒(u⇒p)=(u∧u)⇒p=u⇒p
(33)
prove that it is an open local operator.
For a monomorphism m:S↪X with characteristic map χ, density means u⇒χ=⊤, equivalently u≤χ. This says exactly that U×X is contained in S, or that the pullback mono U×S→U×X is invertible. Consequently
m is o(U)-dense⟺U∗(m) is an isomorphism.​
(34)
An object X is an o(U)-sheaf exactly when the canonical map ηX​:X→XU, taking a value to its constant function on U, is invertible. Indeed T×U↪T is dense for every T. The sheaf condition makes E(T,X)→E(T×U,X)≅E(T,XU) bijective for every T, which proves ηX​ invertible by the Yoneda lemma. Conversely, if ηX​ is invertible and m:S↪T is dense, the isomorphism S×U≅T×U gives
E(T,X)≅E(T×U,X)≅E(S×U,X)≅E(S,X),
(35)
so X is a sheaf.
The pullback functor L:E→E/U has right adjoint ΠU​, sending A→U to AU. Every map X×U→A is automatically over U, since U is subterminal object, proving this adjunction directly. Its counit U×AU→A is evaluation and is invertible. The inverse sends a∈A to its image in U together with the constant function U→A of value a; all elements of U agree, so the composites are identities. The right adjoint is therefore full and faithful, and its image consists of objects fixed by X→XU. The sheaf characterization above proves
sho(U)​(E)≃E/U.​
(36)
For a general local operator j, its dense monomorphism classifier is the ordered subobject
J={p∈Ω:jp=⊤}↪Ω.
(37)
A mono is j-dense exactly when its characteristic map factors through J. If j=o(u), then J={p:u≤p}, which has least element u. This is least internally over every parameter object, not just among global points.
Conversely suppose a global u:1→J is internally least. Upward closure of J gives jp=⊤ exactly when u≤p, in every context. For S↪X let S be its j-closure, with characteristic predicate jp. The mono S↪S is dense: restricting jp to S gives truth. Its characteristic map therefore lands in J, so leastness of u gives U∩S⊆S. Internally this is
u∧jp≤p,hence jp≤u⇒p.
(38)
On the other hand ju=⊤, and applying j to u∧(u⇒p)≤p gives j(u⇒p)≤jp. Inflationarity then gives u⇒p≤jp. Both inequalities yield
J has a least element u⟺j=o(U),j(p)=u⇒p.​
(39)
This proves the least dense truth characterizes an open local operator criterion without replacing its internal order condition by the weaker condition on global truth values.
