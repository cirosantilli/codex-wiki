The Pauli matrix multiplication law σi​σj​=δij​I+iϵijk​σk​ immediately gives σμσˉν+σνσˉμ=2ημνI: the 00 case is 2I, the mixed cases cancel, and the spatial cases give −2δij​I. The spinor indices of σαβ˙​μ​ express the correspondence between a Lorentz four-vector and a Hermitian 2×2 matrix. For X=x0I+xiσi​, detX=(x0)2−∣x∣2; X↦MXM† preserves this determinant for M∈SL(2,C), yielding the Lorentz spinor double cover.
In four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry, the odd supercharges Qα​ and Qˉ​α˙​=Qα†​ transform as conjugate Weyl spinors. Their Super-Poincaré algebra is
{Qα​,Qˉ​β˙​​}=2σαβ˙​μ​Pμ​,{Qα​,Qβ​}={Qˉ​α˙​,Qˉ​β˙​​}=0,[Pμ​,Qα​]=[Pμ​,Qˉ​α˙​]=0.​

The ordinary translation and Lorentz generators satisfy the Poincare algebra, and the Lorentz generators act on the supercharges by the corresponding group representations on Weyl spinors. Tracing the mixed anticommutator gives P0​=41​∑α​{Qα​,Qα†​}, proving energy positivity in global supersymmetry. For a massive rest-frame state, Pμ​=(M,0,0,0), the rescaled charges Qα​/2M​ obey the canonical anticommutation relations. Acting with their adjoints builds a supermultiplet containing states of alternating fermion parity.
