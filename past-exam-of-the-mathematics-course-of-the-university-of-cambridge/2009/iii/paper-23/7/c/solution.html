Define the free algebra functor by
FT(X)=(TX,μX​),FT(f)=Tf.
(44)
The monad laws say precisely that μX​ defines an monad algebra structure on TX, while naturality of μ makes Tf an monad algebra morphism. We construct the adjunction explicitly.
For an monad algebra (A,a) and a morphism f:X→A, put f​=aTf:TX→A. It is an monad algebra morphism because
f​μX​=aTfμX​=aμA​T2f=aTaT2f=aTf​.
(45)
Conversely, from an monad algebra morphism h:(TX,μX​)→(A,a) obtain hηX​:X→A. These operations are inverse: naturality of η and the monad algebra unit law give
aTfηX​=aηA​f=f,
(46)
and the algebra-morphism equation together with the monad unit law gives
aT(hηX​)=aThTηX​=hμX​TηX​=h.
(47)
The formulas commute with precomposition in X and composition with monad algebra morphisms in (A,a), so the bijections are natural. Therefore the free-forgetful Eilenberg-Moore adjunction is
FT⊣U,CT(FTX,(A,a))≅C(X,A).​
(48)
Its unit is ηX​ and its counit at (A,a) is the monad algebra action a.
