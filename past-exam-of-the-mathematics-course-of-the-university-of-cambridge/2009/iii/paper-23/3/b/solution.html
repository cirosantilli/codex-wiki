A Cartesian closed category has finite categorical products and, for every P,Q, an exponential object QP with an evaluation morphism ev:QP×P→Q such that composition with evaluation gives natural bijections
E(R,QP)≅E(R×P,Q).
(10)
Finite categorical products in the presheaf category are pointwise; in particular its terminal object is the constant singleton categorical presheaf. For categorical presheaves P,Q define
(QP)(c)=Nat(Hc​×P,Q).​
(11)
For u:d→c, restriction sends α to α∘(Hu​×1P​). Composition and identity laws follow from those of the Yoneda embedding, so this is a categorical presheaf.
Define evaluation at c by evc​(α,x)=αc​(1c​,x). For u:d→c, naturality of α gives Q(u)αc​(1c​,x)=αd​(u,P(u)x), which is the evaluation of the restricted pair. Hence evaluation is a natural transformation.
Given t:R×P→Q, its currying sends z∈R(c) to the transformation whose component at a is
(tc​(z))a​(v,x)=ta​(R(v)z,x)(v:a→c, x∈P(a)).
(12)
Naturality in a follows from that of t, and naturality in c follows by composing v with the relevant arrow. Evaluating t at (1c​,x) recovers tc​(z,x). Conversely, if s:R→QP, naturality of s along v:a→c says that evaluating sa​(R(v)z) at (1a​,x) gives (sc​(z))a​(v,x). Thus currying the evaluation of s recovers s. These constructions are inverse and natural, proving the universal property. This is the exponential in a presheaf category, and proves the presheaf category is Cartesian closed.
