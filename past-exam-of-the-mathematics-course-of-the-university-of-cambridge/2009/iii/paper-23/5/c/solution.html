For a categorical presheaf P on the open subsets, define the functors on objects by
L(P)=P(∅),R(P)=P(S).
(28)
To prove L⊣Δ, a natural transformation t:P→ΔA must satisfy
tU​=t∅​∘res∅U​
(29)
for every open U, since the restrictions of ΔA are identities. Thus it is determined by the function P(∅)→A. Conversely such a function defines all tU​ by that equation, and the categorical presheaf restriction laws make the components compatible. Hence
Nat(P,ΔA)≅Set(P(∅),A).
(30)
For the other adjunction, a transformation s:ΔA→P is determined by its component sS​:A→P(S), since
sU​=resUS​∘sS​.
(31)
Every function A→P(S) supplies compatible components by this formula. Therefore Nat(ΔA,P)≅Set(A,P(S)), giving the adjoints to constant presheaves on open sets:
ev∅​⊣Δ⊣evS​.​
(32)
These are adjoints of the stated constant-presheaf functor, with its value at every open set including the empty one; no sheafification is involved.
