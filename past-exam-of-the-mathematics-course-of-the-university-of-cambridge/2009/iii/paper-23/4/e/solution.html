By the canonical colimit presentation of a presheaf, write
P=colim(c,x)∈∫P​Hc​
(20)
with structure maps αc,x​:Hc​→P. Since P is a 0-presentable object, the canonical map
colim(c,x)​Nat(P,Hc​)⟶Nat(P,P)
(21)
is bijective. Every element of a set-valued colimit has a representative in some component. In particular 1P​ is the image of a transformation s:P→Hc​ for some (c,x), so
αc,x​s=1P​.
(22)
Thus P is a retract of Hc​. The representable retract criterion from part (c), using the assumed equalisers in C, now gives P is representable. Only existence of the representative of 1P​ is needed; no stronger uniqueness-of-factorization assertion about individual colimit injections has been assumed.
