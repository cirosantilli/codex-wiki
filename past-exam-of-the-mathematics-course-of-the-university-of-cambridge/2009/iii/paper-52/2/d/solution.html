Let G=−i[H,⋅]+∑d​D[Vd​] denote the Lindbladian. Substituting ρ=∑n​rn​σn​ and projecting with the Hilbert-Schmidt inner product gives
r˙m​=∑n​Tr[σm​G(σn​)]rn​.
(22)
For the Quantum Hamiltonian term, cyclicity of the trace yields
Tr(σm​[−iH,σn​])​=−iTr(σm​Hσn​−σm​σn​H)=iTr[H(σm​σn​−σn​σm​)]=Lmn​.​
(23)
For each Lindblad dissipator, the same cyclic rearrangement gives
Tr[σm​D[Vd​](σn​)]​=Tr(Vd†​σm​Vd​σn​)−21​Tr[Vd†​Vd​(σn​σm​+σm​σn​)]=Dmn(d)​.​
(24)
These are the required coefficient matrices, so r˙=(L+∑d​D(d))r. They are real because both generators map Hermitian matrices to Hermitian matrices, whose coordinates are real by (a).
The trace of a commutator vanishes. Also
Tr[D[V](ρ)]=Tr(V†Vρ)−21​Tr(V†Vρ+ρV†V)=0.
(25)
Thus the last row of L+∑d​D(d) is zero and r˙N2​=0​. It is trace preservation, not preservation of the purity of a density operator, that produces this constant coordinate.
Write G=L+∑d​D(d) and n=N2−1. Since rN2​=1/N​, the remaining coordinates obey the Affine Bloch equation
s˙=As+c,Amn​=Gmn​,cm​=Gm,N2​/N​(1≤m,n≤n).​
(26)
Because [H,I]=0 and D[V](I)=[V,V†], its offset can also be written
cm​=N1​Tr[σm​∑d​[Vd​,Vd†​]].
(27)
It vanishes for unital dynamics, but need not vanish for a general dissipative evolution.
