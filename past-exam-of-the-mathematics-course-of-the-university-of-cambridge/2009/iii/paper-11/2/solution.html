Zeros are counted with their prescribed finite multiplicities, and the function is not identically zero. The criterion for zero sets in the unit disc is local finiteness: no point of the disc is an accumulation point of the zero sequence, and no point has infinite multiplicity. Necessity follows from the identity theorem for holomorphic functions. For sufficiency, factor a finite number of zeros at zero as zm.
