For the slowly varying external potential, use the collective-coordinate effective Lagrangian method: retain the localized free profile but let its centre, boost and overall phase vary with time, with its norm fixed. Substitute this finite-parameter ansatz into the field Lagrangian, integrate over space and derive the Euler-Lagrange equations for the parameters. The potential contribution is the external potential averaged over the soliton's density; if it varies little across the profile, it is approximately its value at the centre times the conserved norm. This gives approximate particle-like centre motion while neglecting radiation and profile deformation. No solution of the full perturbed field equation is required for this approximation.
