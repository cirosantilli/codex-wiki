Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2009 / iii / Paper 20 / 3 / i / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2009 iii Paper 20 3 i
OurBigBook.com Words: 480
Use ordinary integer surgery coefficients, so the positive trace has intersection form (n). Let F0​⊂K0​ be the capped Seifert surface. Label the Spin-c structures by
⟨c1​(si​),[F0​]⟩=2i,i∈Z.
(22)
On the trace Xn​:S3→Kn​, let Fn2​=n and label its structures rj​ by ⟨c1​(rj​),[Fn​]⟩=2j−n. Restriction identifies tk​ with j≡k(modn). In these compatible labels the Integer-surgery exact triangle in Heegaard Floer homology is
⋯⟶HF−(S3)Ak​​i≡k (mod n)⨁​HF−(K0​,si​)Bk​​HF−(Kn​,tk​)Ck​​HF−(S3)⟶⋯.​
(23)
Here boldface denotes the U-adic completion of Heegaard Floer homology. Only finitely many zero-surgery structures contribute, by the genus bound on a capped Seifert surface, so the middle direct sum causes no difficulty. The infinite sums of trace maps do require completion. The printed minus notation should be understood in this completed sense; with the polynomial version, a sum over every extension need not even have values in Z[U]. The plus version has the same ordering and needs no completion.
The two ordinary surgery traces give Ak​ and Ck​. For X0​:S3→K0​, H2(X0​)=Z, and its Spin-c structures qi​ have determinant evaluations 2i on the capped Seifert surface. Their restrictions are the unique structure on S3 and si​ on K0​. Thus the component of Ak​ in the ith summand is FX0​,qi​−​ for i≡k. For the orientation-reversed and turned-around trace Vn​:Kn​→S3, H2(Vn​)=Z and Fn2​=−n. Its structures rj​ still have characteristic evaluations 2j−n, restrict to tjmodn​ on Kn​ and to the unique structure on S3, and
Ck​=∑j≡k (mod n)​FVn​,rj​−​.
(24)
Characteristic parity explains the allowed evaluations; changing a structure by the generator of H2 changes its evaluation by two, and the boundary restriction reduces the label modulo n.
For n>1, Bk​ has an auxiliary lens-space input. More precisely, take the three-boundary surgery cobordism, or equivalently a two-ended cobordism Zn​:K0​#Ln​→Kn​, where Ln​ is positive n-surgery on the unknot. Its incoming lens-space factor is fixed to the distinguished structure u0​ and its canonical top Floer generator ΘLn​​. Then
Bk​(ξ)=∑v∣K0​​=si​,i≡kv∣Ln​​=u0​,v∣Kn​​=tk​​​FZn​,v−​(ξ⊗ΘLn​​).
(25)
This describes the boundary restrictions exactly, including the boundary that must not be suppressed. A concrete Kirby diagram for Zn​ starts with the split incoming surgeries of coefficients 0 and n, then attaches a zero-framed circle linking each incoming component once. Its full linking matrix is (001​0n1​110​). Quotienting cohomology by the two incoming surgery columns gives H2(Zn​)=Z⊕Z/n. Accordingly its Spin-c structures may be labeled (i,q) with incoming restrictions (si​,uq​) and outgoing restriction ti−q​. In particular, fixing q=0 gives exactly one extension for each i, and its outgoing class is imodn. Thus Bk​ is a Heegaard Floer cobordism map with the fixed auxiliary generator, rather than an unqualified map of a trace from K0​ to Kn​. When n=1, Ln​=S3 and all three arrows become the familiar sums of ordinary two-ended Heegaard Floer cobordism maps.
