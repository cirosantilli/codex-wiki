Work on R with Lebesgue measure. Without a uniform Lp bound, use
fn​=n−11[0,nq]​.​
(14)
It has ∥fn​∥q​=1 and ∥fn​∥r​=nq/r−1≤1, with 1/r=0 for r=∞. Each function still belongs to Lp∩Lr, but its Lp norm is unbounded. For every fixed ε>0, its level set above ε is eventually empty, defeating any positive uniform M.
Without a uniform Lr bound, use instead
gn​=n1[0,n−q]​.​
(15)
Now ∥gn​∥q​=1 and ∥gn​∥p​=n1−q/p≤1, but for any fixed ε>0 the high-amplitude level set has measure n−q→0. These give the two distinct failures of the nonvanishing level-set bound between three Lp norms when either control is removed.
