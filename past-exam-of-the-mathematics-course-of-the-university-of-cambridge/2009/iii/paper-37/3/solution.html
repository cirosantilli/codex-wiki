Here ψ is the ultimate ruin probability and ϕ=1−ψ is the survival probability. The relative safety loading is θ=(c−λμ)/(λμ)>0.
To derive the survival integro-differential equation, condition on the first claim time T, which has the exponential distribution of rate λ. Immediately before that claim the available capital is u+cT. A claim x≤u+cT leaves survival probability ϕ(u+cT−x), since the future claim process restarts with the same independent law; a larger claim causes ruin immediately.
