For excess of loss reinsurance with retention M, the direct insurer caps its payment at M and the reinsurer pays the excess. Thus
Yi​=min(Xi​,M),Zi​=(Xi​−M)+​.​

Let Ii​=1{Xi​>M}​ and α=P(Xi​>M)=1−FX1​​(M)∈(0,1). These are independent Bernoulli random variables, independent of N. Since NR​=∑i=1N​Ii​, its conditional law given N=n is a binomial distribution. Conditioning on N therefore gives the Bernoulli thinning identity
E[zNR​∣N]=(1−α+αz)N,GNR​​(z)=GN​(1−α+αz).​
(18)
Here the probability generating function may be evaluated on 0≤z≤1, and the identity extends wherever the transforms converge.
The positive reinsurer marks have the conditional law W=dX1​−M∣X1​>M. Hence
FW​(w)=⎩⎨⎧​0,1−FX1​​(M)FX1​​(M+w)−FX1​​(M)​,​w<0,w≥0,​fW​(w)=1−FX1​​(M)fX1​​(M+w)​(w>0).​

The independent-mark representation is justified as follows. Conditional on any fixed count and pattern of exceedance indicators, the positive excesses are independent with this same conditional law; that law depends neither on the number of exceedances nor on their locations. Averaging over the indicator pattern thus leaves the positive mark sequence independent of NR​. We may extend it by additional independent copies if needed, giving SR​=∑j=1NR​​Wj​ with the stated independence.
Now write q=1−p. For exponential distribution claim sizes of mean μ,
α=e−M/μ,P(W>w)=e−M/μe−(M+w)/μ​=e−w/μ.
(20)
Thus W is exponential with the original mean μ, by memorylessness of the exponential distribution. The original zero-based geometric distribution count has GN​(z)=p/(1−qz). Consequently
GNR​​(z)=p+qα−qαzp​=1−qR​zpR​​,pR​=p+qαp​,qR​=p+qαqα​.
(21)
This is the geometric count under Bernoulli thinning, with
P(NR​=k)=pR​qRk​(k=0,1,…).​
(22)
Finally use the Laplace transform of a nonnegative random variable, keeping the mass at zero. For t≥0,
Ee−tSR​=GNR​​(1+μt1​)=pR​+μtpR​(1+μt)​=pR​+qR​pR​+μtpR​​.
(23)
The last expression is the transform of a mixture distribution: mass pR​ at zero and, with probability qR​, an exponential distribution of rate pR​/μ. Uniqueness of the Laplace transform identifies this zero-based geometric sum of exponential variables:
L(SR​)=pR​δ0​+qR​Exp(pR​/μ).​
(24)
Equivalently, P(SR​=0)=pR​ and its positive density is qR​(pR​/μ)e−pR​s/μ. Therefore the requested exceedance probability is
P(SR​>s)=p+(1−p)e−M/μ(1−p)e−M/μ​exp(−μ[p+(1−p)e−M/μ]ps​),s>0.​
(25)
The prefactor is essential: the aggregate is zero whenever no claim crosses the retention.
