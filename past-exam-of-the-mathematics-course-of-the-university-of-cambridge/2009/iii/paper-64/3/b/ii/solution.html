Let cL​=(γpL​/ρL​)1/2 and cR​=(γpR​/ρR​)1/2, with positive pressures and densities. A nontrivial single rarefaction wave has constant specific entropy and constant Riemann invariant, so for one family label σ=±1 the necessary matching conditions are
ρLγ​pL​​=ρRγ​pR​​=K,uR​−uL​=γ−12σ(cR​−cL​)​,σ(cR​−cL​)>0.​
(33)
The final inequality says that the fan opens rather than compresses. Its edge speeds are ξL​=uL​+σcL​ and ξR​=uR​+σcR​, and their difference is σ(cR​−cL​)(γ+1)/(γ−1)>0. The profiles in part (a) then interpolate the two end states and supply a sufficient construction. For the left family, cR​<cL​, hence ρR​<ρL​ and pR​<pL​; for the right family all three inequalities reverse. In both cases uR​>uL​. These are the single-rarefaction matching conditions for an ideal-gas Riemann problem.
For a concrete nontrivial example, take γ=5/3 and
(ρL​,pL​,uL​)=(1,3/5,0),(ρR​,pR​,uR​)=(1/8,3/160,3/2).
(34)
The adiabatic sound speeds are cL​=1 and cR​=1/2, with common K=3/5 and left-family invariant u+3c=3. The fan edges are ξL​=−1 and ξR​=1. The complete self-similar solution is uniform at these stated left and right values outside the fan, while inside −1<ξ<1,
u=43​(ξ+1),c=43−ξ​,ρ=c3,p=53​c5.​
(35)
It matches both states continuously, has u′=3/4=2/(γ+1), and has constant specific entropy. For fixed x=0, its t↓0 limit is the required initial step.
