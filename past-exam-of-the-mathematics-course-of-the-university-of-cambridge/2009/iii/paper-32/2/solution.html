The standard Snell envelope hypothesis includes integrable rewards; independence of random variables and positivity then give finite first moments of the factors. If that hypothesis is dropped, an infinite-mean factor gives an infinite continuation value, so finite constants need not exist.

The original probability measure is already such an equivalent martingale measure, proving no arbitrage.
