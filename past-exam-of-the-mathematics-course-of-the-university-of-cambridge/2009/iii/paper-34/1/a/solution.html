For every nonzero v∈Rn−p, full column rank of L gives Lv=0, and hence
vTLTLv=∥Lv∥2>0.
(1)
Thus the Gram matrix LTL is a positive-definite matrix and is invertible. Using BA=0 and A=LLT,
0=BLLTL(LTL)−1=BL,
(2)
so
LTL is positive definite,BL=0.​
(3)
A linear image of a multivariate normal vector is again multivariate normal. Therefore the stacked vector has mean (Bμ,LTμ)T and covariance
σ2(BBTLTBT​BLLTL​)=σ2(BBT0​0LTL​).
(4)
In particular,
Z∼Nn​((BμLTμ​), σ2(BBT0​0LTL​)).​
(5)
The two diagonal blocks are positive-definite by the rank assumptions. The two blocks are jointly Gaussian and have zero cross covariance. The fact that uncorrelated jointly Gaussian variables are independent makes BY and LTY independent. Since
YTAY=YTLLTY=∥LTY∥2
(6)
is a measurable function of the second block, independence is preserved under this transformation. This proves Gaussian independence of linear and quadratic statistics:
BY and YTAY are independent.​
