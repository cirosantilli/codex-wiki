For an asymptotically flat spacetime with future null infinity I+, its black-hole region is
B=M∖J−(I+),​

where the causal past is understood in the conformal completion. Choose the future orientation in the advanced chart so that −∂r​ is future null. For any future causal tangent X, the inequality g(X,−∂r​)=−X(v)≤0 gives X(v)≥0. The causality inequality is
−fX(v)2+2X(v)X(r)+R2γAB​XAXB≤0.
(7)
If X(v)>0, it implies X(r)≤fX(v)/2. Inside r=2m, f<0, so r strictly decreases. If X(v)=0, the angular tangent vanishes and future orientation makes X a positive multiple of −∂r​, again decreasing r. Thus no future causal curve from 0<r<2m can reach the exterior or future null infinity: it is inside the black-hole region.
Conversely, from any point with r>2m, the outgoing radial null geodesic has u constant and dr/dv=f/2>0. It continues to arbitrarily large r, with r∗​→+∞, and reaches the corresponding future null infinity. Hence B∩{r>2m}=∅​ in this advanced exterior chart. This argument concerns the black-hole interior covered by ingoing coordinates; the additional white-hole region of the maximal extension has a different future causal orientation.
