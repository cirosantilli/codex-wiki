Take M˙>0 to mean inward accretion rate, and let S count loss through both faces per unit projected area of the accretion disk. An annulus of width dR loses 2πRSdR to the wind. In steady state, its incoming mass flux at its outer edge equals the inward flux at its inner edge plus this loss. Hence mass conservation gives
dRdM˙​=2πRS.​

If a loss rate is instead defined per individual face, it must be doubled before substituting for S here.
