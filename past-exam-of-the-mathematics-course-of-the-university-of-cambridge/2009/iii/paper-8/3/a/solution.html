Hs​(Tn)={u∈D′(Tn):∥u∥s2​=k∈Zn∑​(1+∣k∣2)s∣u(k)∣2<∞}.​

Weighted Fourier coefficients identify it isometrically with ℓ2, so it is a Hilbert space and complete. This definition includes negative orders and distributions. For nonnegative integer s the norm is equivalent to the sum of squared L2 norms of weak derivatives through order s.
