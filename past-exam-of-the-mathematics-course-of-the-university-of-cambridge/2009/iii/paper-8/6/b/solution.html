The kernel and cokernel of an equivariant Fredholm operator are finite-dimensional representations. Define its equivariant Fredholm index in the representation ring of a compact group by
indG​S=[kerS]−[cokerS].​
(31)
For G=SU(2) write H=⨁​n​En​⊗Cmn​ and H′=⨁​n​En​⊗Cmn′​, with finite multiplicities. By Schur lemma an intertwiner has blocks IEn​​⊗sn​, where sn​:Cmn​→Cmn′​. If its rank is rn​, the kernel and cokernel multiplicities are mn​−rn​ and mn′​−rn​. Consequently
indG​S=n∑​(mn​−mn′​)[En​].​
(32)
Only finitely many coefficients can be nonzero, because the global kernel and cokernel are finite-dimensional. Closed range is part of the Fredholm hypothesis and supplies the uniform lower bound on the remaining blocks; multiplicity matching alone would not establish that hypothesis. The ordinary index is obtained by taking dimensions, indS=∑n​(n+1)(mn​−mn′​). This calculation applies to any compact group with its finite-dimensional irreducibles in place of the En​.
