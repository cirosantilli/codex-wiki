The small nonzero initial lateral offset breaks the exactly collinear configuration; the spheres can deflect sideways and pass with a positive gap rather than touch. Smooth surfaces, continuum hydrodynamics and the no-slip boundary condition are essential to the noncontact conclusion; the argument is not a model of surface roughness or molecular contact.
