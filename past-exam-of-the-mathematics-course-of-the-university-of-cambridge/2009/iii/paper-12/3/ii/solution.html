First use weak compactness of bounded sets in the Hilbert space L2(Ω) to pass to a subsequence with uk​⇀u globally in L2. The weak lower semicontinuity of the Hilbert norm gives ∥u∥L2(Ω)​≤K. This global step also applies if Ω is unbounded.
Choose a nested exhaustion Uj​⋐Uj+1​⋐Ω by bounded smooth open sets. Part (i) bounds uk​ in H1(Uj​) for every j. By the Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem, H1(Uj​) embeds compactly in L2(Uj​). Weak compactness in H1(Uj​) and a diagonal subsequence argument therefore give, for the same subsequence,
uk​⇀u in H1(Uj​),uk​⟶u in L2(Uj​)for every j.
(29)
The local limit agrees with the global weak L2 limit. Using smooth exhaustion sets avoids imposing any unprinted boundary regularity on a general Ω′.
For a smooth compactly supported test function, the weak equation passes to the limit: the fixed bounded coefficients multiply fixed test functions, and local weak gradient convergence handles both the principal and drift terms. The zeroth-order term passes by local L2 convergence. Thus Lu=f weakly, u∈Hloc1​(Ω), and the global bound already proved gives u∈SK​.
To improve convergence of the gradients, put wk​=uk​−u. Crucially the forcing is the same for all k, so Lwk​=0. Given Ω′⋐Ω, choose j with Ω′⊂Uj​. Apply part (i) on Uj​ to the homogeneous equation for wk​:
∥wk​∥H1(Ω′)​≤C∥wk​∥L2(Uj​)​⟶0.
(30)
This establishes strong local Sobolev compactness for a fixed elliptic equation and proves
u∈SK​,uk′​⟶u in W1,2(Ω′) for every Ω′⋐Ω.​
(31)
