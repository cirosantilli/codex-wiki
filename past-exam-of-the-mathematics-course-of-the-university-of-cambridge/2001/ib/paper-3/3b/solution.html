A convenient version of Rouché's theorem is: if f,g are holomorphic functions on a neighbourhood of a closed disk, and ∣g∣<∣f∣ on its boundary, then f and f+g have the same number of zeros, counted with multiplicity, in the disk. In particular neither has a boundary zero under this comparison.
