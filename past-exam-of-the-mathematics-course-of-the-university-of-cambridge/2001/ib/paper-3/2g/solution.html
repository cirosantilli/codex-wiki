Single-valuedness requires 2π-periodicity, so the nonconstant angular Fourier modes have integer n≥1 and are cosnθ,sinnθ. Their radial solutions are rn,r−n. The zero angular mode has radial solutions 1,logr. These give the harmonic Fourier expansions in planar concentric domains used below.
