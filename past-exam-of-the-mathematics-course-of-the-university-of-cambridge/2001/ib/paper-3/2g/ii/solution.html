With the usual exterior-boundary interpretation that the harmonic function remains bounded as r→∞, positive radial powers and logr are excluded. Hence
ϕ(r,θ)=A0​+n=1∑∞​r−n(Cn​cosnθ+Dn​sinnθ).​
(8)
The series must converge locally for r>1, with any required finite boundary values at r=1. These are the interior harmonic Fourier expansions in planar concentric domains after the inversion r↦1/r.
If “finite” means only finite at each finite point, without a condition at infinity, growing solutions such as rcosθ and logr are allowed. Under that literal reading the general exterior expansion is
A0​+B0​logr+∑n≥1​[(An​rn+Cn​r−n)cosnθ+(Bn​rn+Dn​r−n)sinnθ],
(9)
with local convergence. Boundedness at infinity is the condition selecting the decaying exterior expansion.
