This impulsively struck fixed-end string is understood as a distributional weak solution; the initial velocity is a distribution, not a classical continuous function.

For x<l/4, it also contains the negative reflected impulse at −l/4, so the contributions cancel. For l/4<x<3l/4 only the positive impulse contributes. For x>3l/4 neither contributes.
