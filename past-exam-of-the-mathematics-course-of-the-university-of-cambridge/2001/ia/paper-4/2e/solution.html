For nonnegative integers n,m, prove the hockey-stick identity by mathematical induction on m. The induction base case m=0 is (0n​)=(0n+1​)=1. If the formula holds through m−1, Pascal's identity gives
∑j=0m​(jn+j​)=(m−1n+m​)+(mn+m​)=(mn+m+1​).

This supplies the inductive step.
A binary string containing exactly n zeroes and j ones has length n+j. Choosing its j one-positions gives (jn+j​) possibilities. Different values of j give disjoint collections, so counting binary strings with bounded ones yields
j=0∑m​(jn+j​)=(mn+m+1​).​

This includes n=0: the m+1 strings are the empty string and the all-one strings of lengths one through m.
