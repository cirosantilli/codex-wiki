For (b), a three-win outcome is counted three times in the pair sum and must be counted once, so subtract twice its probability. For (c), only the overlapping pairs 12 and 23 are allowed, so subtract the three-win intersection once. For (d), the two allowed exact patterns are 110 and 011. For (e), add 101 as well.
