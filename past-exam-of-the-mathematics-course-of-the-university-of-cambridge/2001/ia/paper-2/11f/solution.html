This is product stationarity under single-coordinate Markov updates: the visited coordinate has the same Bernoulli distribution afterward, and its independent local random decisions do not affect the other coordinates. Thus the originally independent indicator random variables remain jointly independent after each scheduled visit, not merely equal in their marginal probabilities.
