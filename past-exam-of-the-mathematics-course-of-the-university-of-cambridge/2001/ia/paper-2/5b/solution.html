For the homogeneous linear recurrence relation, substitute yk​=rk. Its characteristic equation of a linear recurrence is r2−r+1=0, with roots eiπ/3 and e−iπ/3. Taking real linear combinations gives
yk​=Acos(kπ/3)+Bsin(kπ/3).​
(12)
These two real sequences are linearly independent: their values at k=1,2 form a matrix with determinant 3​/2=0. Thus their two constants account uniquely for arbitrary first two values.
Let Pk​=∑n=1k​an​/(k−n+1) and reindex as Pk​=∑j=1k​ak−j+1​/j. In Pk+2​−Pk+1​+Pk​, each term with 1≤j≤k has coefficient
ak−j+3​−ak−j+2​+ak−j+1​=0.
(13)
The term j=k+1 is (a2​−a1​)/(k+1)=0, and the remaining term is a1​/(k+2)=1/(k+2). This explicitly proves that Pk​ is the required particular solution, a discrete Green convolution for the period-six recurrence.
The unit-response coefficients have the homogeneous form just found. The values a1​=a2​=1 give zero cosine coefficient and sine coefficient 2/3​, so
an​=3​2​sin(nπ/3).
(14)
Subtracting this particular solution from any other solution leaves a homogeneous recurrence. Thus the complete answer is
yk​=Acos(kπ/3)+Bsin(kπ/3)+3​2​n=1∑k​k−n+1sin(nπ/3)​,k≥1.​
(15)
