Rotation invariance and the radial Laplacian give (rd−1G′)′=0 away from the origin. (5)
Thus for d=2 the required long-distance correlation function is

(6)
At d=2, the finite separation-dependent limit is
G(r)=−2πKˉ1​log(r/a)+C.​
(7)
The constant absorbs the divergent constant term in the d→2 limit. In low dimension the individual G(r) has no regulator-independent infinite-volume value; its differences do. This is why the phase-difference variance, rather than an absolute phase variance, is the useful observable.
