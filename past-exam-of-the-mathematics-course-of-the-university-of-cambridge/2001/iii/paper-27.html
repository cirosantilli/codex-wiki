A credibility factor is the weight assigned to a particular risk's observed experience when blending it with a population or manual premium. In the Bühlmann model, the blend is the best affine mean-square predictor, rather than automatically the full posterior mean.
Let v=E[σ2(θ)], the expected process variance, and assume the displayed second moments are finite. The law of total expectation gives EXi​=m. The law of total variance and law of total covariance give
VarXi​=v+a,Cov(Xi​,Xj​)=a(i=j).​
(16)
For the second formula, the conditional covariance is zero by conditional independence, while the covariance of the conditional means is Varμ(θ)=a, the variance of hypothetical means. Likewise
Cov(μ(θ),Xi​)=a,​
(17)
because E[Xi​−μ(θ)∣θ]=0.
To derive the Bühlmann credibility premium, minimize E[(μ(θ)−b0​−∑i​bi​Xi​)2]. Differentiating in b0​ sets the predictor's mean to m. The remaining normal equations are
(vI+a11T)b=a1.
(18)
For v>0, subtracting any two rows forces all bi​ to be equal, then gives bi​=a/(v+na). Thus the premium and factor are
μ​=ZX+(1−Z)m,Z=na+vna​=n+v/an​.​
(19)
The same affine premium predicts Xn+1​: conditional independence makes E[(Xn+1​−μ​)2]=v+E[(μ(θ)−μ​)2], so minimizers agree. If v=0, every year's amount equals μ(θ) almost surely, and Z=1 gives the exact risk mean even though the coefficient vector need not be unique.
For fixed a>0,n, ∂Z/∂v=−na/(na+v)2<0. Thus more within-risk variation decreases credibility: individual experience becomes less informative, Z→0 as v→∞, and the premium tends to m. Increasing the number of observed years has the opposite effect.

(21)
Conditionally, each within-risk sample variance has mean σ2(θs​); hence Ev=v. The risk sample means have variance a+v/n, so EB=a+v/n. Therefore a=B−v/n is an unbiased method of moments estimate of a. For a usable nonnegative variance estimate take a=max(0,a), noting that this truncation sacrifices exact unbiasedness.
The balanced-panel method-of-moments credibility estimate for risk s is consequently
Z=na+vna​,μ​s​=ZXs​+(1−Z)m.​
(22)
If a=0 and v>0, use zero credibility. If both estimates are zero, the within and between sums of squares are zero and all the observed amounts coincide; any blend gives that same premium. With only one year per risk or only one risk, these within/between estimators are unavailable, so external structural estimates or additional assumptions are necessary.

Record the discount level at the start of each policy year as the state. A no claims discount system is a discrete-time Markov chain when, conditional on this state, the next year's claim behavior and update rule do not depend on earlier history. Independent annual accident and loss mechanisms with a fixed state-based reporting rule give this property; one should not equate the probability of an accident with the probability of a submitted claim.
The density identifies Z=(logL−μ)/σ as a standard normal random variable: substitute x=eμ+σz and dx=σxdz. Therefore, for a>0,
P(L>a)=1−Φ(σloga−μ​).​
(23)
Here L is the numerical repair cost. The character preceding L in the PDF is a currency symbol, not a separate multiplier.
Order states as 0%,20%,30%. With the stipulated common annual probability p of no submitted claims, the transition matrix is
P=​1−p1−p1−p​p00​0pp​​.​
(24)
A claim resets any state to zero, while a no-claim year raises the discount one level or keeps the top level fixed. If claim probabilities depend on the state, the corresponding row instead uses pi​=P(no submitted claim∣state i); the same transition mechanism applies. The supplied single p is a simplifying model assumption.
For a policyholder currently at zero discount, the current premium C has the same value under either decision and cancels in the comparison. If the repair is self-funded and no later accidents occur, the next two premiums are 0.8C and 0.7C, giving a total cost L+2.5C including the current premium. If a claim is made, the next two premiums are C and 0.8C, giving total cost 2.8C, with the repair paid by the insurer. Thus the two-year no-claims threshold with reset to zero is
L>0.3C.​
(25)
At equality the holder is indifferent; it has probability zero under the continuous density. This assumes full reimbursement and no excess or deductible, as in the stated model.

The other two current levels both have next-two-year no-claim premiums 0.7C+0.7C, versus C+0.8C after a claim, so their thresholds are 0.4C. Consequently incorporating this behavioral decision into claim probabilities generally produces state-specific pi​, rather than deriving a common p from the severity density alone; no accident-frequency law is provided.
