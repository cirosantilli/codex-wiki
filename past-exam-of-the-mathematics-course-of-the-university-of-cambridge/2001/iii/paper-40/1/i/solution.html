Use the instantaneous recycling approximation and treat the interstellar medium as well mixed. If star formation initially consumes mass dM, returning a fraction β leaves locked stellar mass ds=(1−β)dM. The original metal mass removed from the interstellar medium is Zi​dM. Pristine galactic gas inflow can change g without contributing to d(gZi​); enriched galactic gas inflow would require an additional metal source. Here 0≤β<1, and 0≤f≤1.
