OurBigBook.com Words: 70
If both groups share the same survival function and the common fit is adequate, their complete Cox–Snell residuals have the same unit exponential distribution. Under independent censoring in each group, both groupwise Kaplan–Meier estimators should therefore be close to e−u and to each other, subject to ordinary sampling variation and the number of subjects still at risk. Different censoring patterns can change precision without changing the residual survival target.
Both group curves should approximately follow the same unit-exponential survival curve.
