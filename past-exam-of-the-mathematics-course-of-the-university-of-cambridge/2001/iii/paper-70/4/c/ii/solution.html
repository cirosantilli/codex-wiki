There are two normalization problems in the printed energy identity. In conformal time, the physical density of a homogeneous scalar is T00​/a2, and its kinetic contribution contains ϕ′2, not one power of ϕ′. The conformal density of a canonical scalar field follows directly from the stress-energy tensor:
ρϕ​=2a2ϕ′2​+V,pϕ​=2a2ϕ′2​−V,T00​=a2ρϕ​.
(45)
Writing K=ϕ′2/(2a2), the condition K−V=−(K+V)/3 gives V=2K, hence
K=ρϕ​/3,V=2ρϕ​/3,ϕ′2=32a2ρϕ​​=32T00​​.​
(46)
The reconstruction is consistent with this corrected identity; the unsquared printed identity cannot hold as an energy relation.
The constant-equation-of-state density scaling for this coasting fluid gives ρϕ​=ρX0​a−2, where ρX0​=3M2H02​ΩX​. Choose the branch on which ϕ increases. Then
ϕ′=2​MH0​ΩX​​,V=a22M2H02​ΩX​​.
(47)
Keeping all three density parameters explicitly, the Friedmann equations in conformal time give
a′2=H02​(Ωr​+Ωm​a+ΩX​a2),dϕda​=2​M1​a2+ΩX​Ωm​​a+ΩX​Ωr​​​.
(48)
For ΩX​>0, define
b=2ΩX​Ωm​​,A=ΩX​Ωm2​/4−Ωr​ΩX​​​.
(49)
The square root is (a+b)2−A2​. Integrating on the expanding branch gives
arcoshAa+b​=2​Mϕ−ϕ0​​+C,
(50)
where a(ϕ0​)=1. Thus the scalar-field reconstruction of a coasting component is
a=Acosh[B(ϕ−ϕ0​)+C]+D,B=2​M1​=mpl​4π​​,C=arcoshA1+b​,D=−b.​
(51)
Substitution into the potential gives the requested present-field parametrization:
V(ϕ)=[Acosh(2​Mϕ−ϕ0​​+C)−b]22M2H02​ΩX​​.​
(52)
The domain is the branch where a>0 and the hyperbolic argument is positive; reversing the direction of the field changes the sign of B. The actual discriminant condition is Ωm2​/4>Ωr​ΩX​. The supplied stronger bound suffices for physical density fractions 0<ΩX​≤1. No density parameter has been eliminated using the flatness sum.
As a check, ϕ′ is constant and V,ϕ​=−2V(a′/a)/ϕ′. Since a2V=ϕ′2 along this solution, ϕ′′+2(a′/a)ϕ′+a2V,ϕ​=0 identically. The reconstructed field therefore satisfies its equation of motion as well as the Friedmann equations and required equation of state.
