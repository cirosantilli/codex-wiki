Write C=∫R​f(x)dx, so 0<C<∞. The finite envelope condition means that f≤Mg almost everywhere; in particular, the proposal must cover the support of the target.
For each independent proposal Y∼g, generate an independent U∼Unif(0,1) and accept Y precisely when
U≤Mg(Y)f(Y)​.​
(1)
Discard a rejected proposal and continue. The ratio is in [0,1] and is needed only where g(Y)>0. Neither evaluation of C nor prior knowledge of the normalized target is required.
To prove the output law, for any measurable set D the joint probability of acceptance and a proposal in D is
P(Y∈D,accept)=∫D​g(y)Mg(y)f(y)​dy=M1​∫D​f(y)dy.
(2)
Dividing by the total acceptance probability C/M gives ∫D​f(y)/Cdy, the target probability. Independent proposal-uniform pairs give independent accepted observations. With a finite proposal list there can be no accepted value; with continued independent trials, acceptance eventually occurs almost surely because C/M>0.
