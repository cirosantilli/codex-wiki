The acceptance mass for an unnormalized rejection envelope follows by integrating the proposal-specific acceptance probability:
pacc​=Eg​[Mg(Y)f(Y)​]=MC​.​
(3)
The inequality f≤Mg also gives C≤M, so this is a valid probability. It is the same at every independent trial.
If Ji​ indicates acceptance of the ith proposal, then J1​,…,Jn​ are independent Bernoulli distribution variables with parameter C/M. Hence the accepted count K=∑i​Ji​ satisfies
K∼Bin(n,MC​),EK=MnC​=Mn​∫R​f(y)dy.​
(4)
Its variance is npacc​(1−pacc​). Conditional on any acceptance pattern, the accepted values have independent target probability density function h; mixing over patterns retains this product law conditional on their count.
