Assume a sharp interface, uniformly mixed warm upper air with fixed reduced gravity g′=gΔρ/ρ0​, cool ambient air entering below, no wind, no wall heat exchange, negligible source volume, and quasi-steady Boussinesq flow through the small vents. Neglect the interior approach velocity. The hydrostatic buoyancy head between the two vents is ρ0​g′h.
Both openings carry the same volume flux qv​. The discharge coefficient law gives a pressure loss ρ0​qv2​/(2Cd2​A2) at each opening. Adding the two losses gives qv​=Cd​Ag′h​, not the single-opening value with an extra factor 2​. Cool replacement air displaces the warm layer, so Sh˙=−qv​. With h(0)=H, integrate to obtain
h(t)=(H​−2SCd​Ag′​​t)2,0≤t≤te​=Cd​Ag′​2SH​​.​

The ideal model sets h=0 thereafter. The formula applies only while the parenthesis is nonnegative; squaring a negative value would invent renewed filling. A finite aperture or a diffuse final interface invalidates the idealization near exhaustion.
