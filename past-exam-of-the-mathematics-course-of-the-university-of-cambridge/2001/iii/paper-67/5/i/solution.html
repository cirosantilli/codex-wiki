The dot denotes a partial time derivative at fixed comoving position, evaluated along the zeroth-order null geodesic. It is not the total derivative of hij​ along the ray, so the integral cannot in general be replaced by a difference of hij​ at the endpoints. This is the metric part of the Sachs-Wolfe effect in the negative-spatial-perturbation convention; an intrinsic emission temperature fluctuation or an emitter velocity would add separate boundary terms.
