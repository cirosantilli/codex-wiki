Use Xi​=ni​/nB​, so XD​ counts deuterium nuclei per baryon, not baryons bound into deuterium. For the radiative capture reaction, chemical equilibrium and the zero photon chemical potential give μD​=μn​+μp​. Dividing the three nonrelativistic Maxwell--Boltzmann number densities removes all chemical potentials:
nn​np​nD​​=gn​gp​gD​​(mn​mp​mD​​)3/2(T2π​)3/2exp[−TmD​−mn​−mp​​].

Here gD​=3 and gn​=gp​=2. The binding convention in this PDF is a negative mass defect, BD​=mD​−mn​−mp​<0. The positive nuclear binding energy is εD​=−BD​, so the abundance is enhanced by e−BD​/T, not by eBD​/T.
Since XD​/(Xn​Xp​)=nB​nD​/(nn​np​), insert the baryon-to-photon ratio nB​=ηnγ​ and the photon number density nγ​=2ζ(3)T3/π2. This gives
Xn​Xp​XD​​=ηπ22ζ(3)​T343​(mn​mp​mD​​)3/2(T2π​)3/2e−BD​/T.​
(18)
At equal nucleon mass mN​ in the prefactors, mD​≃2mN​, the deuterium equilibrium abundance simplifies to
Xn​Xp​XD​​≃π​12ζ(3)​η(mN​T​)3/2e−BD​/T.​
(19)
The dimensionless prefactor is about 8.14. These expressions describe thermal equilibrium; residual primordial deuterium after nuclear reactions cease must instead be obtained from the reaction kinetics.
