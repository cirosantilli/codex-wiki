An algebraic addition theorem for a meromorphic function f means a nonzero polynomial P(U,V,W) with
P(f(z),f(w),f(z+w))=0

identically where the values are finite. A local relation extends meromorphically to the other regular arguments.

For the final assertion, consider f(z)=eez, an entire function. If c is a period, then eez(ec−1)=1 for every z. The continuous exponent must be a constant integer multiple of 2πi; its derivative forces ec=1. Hence its periods are exactly 2πiZ, making it a simply periodic function.
