A uniform pro-p group is a finitely generated powerful pro-p group whose lower p-series P1​=G, Pi+1​=Pip​[Pi​,G]​ has constant successive indices. For odd p, the powerful pro-p group condition means [G,G]⊆Gp, with closed generated powers understood. For p=2 it means [G,G]⊆G4. For finitely generated powerful pro-p groups, uniformity is equivalent to being a torsion-free group.
It is convenient to use general matrix size d, then put d=p. Let Um​=1+pmMd​(Zp​). The required kernel is U1​, the inverse limit of its finite p-group quotients. For odd p, the convergent matrix logarithm and matrix exponential series

Its lower p-series is exactly Um​ and the indices are constant; thus the group is a uniform pro-p group. Alternatively, the logarithm excludes nontrivial p-power torsion. Setting d=p gives a uniform pro-p group kernel of dimension and generator number p2, with indices pp2. This is the odd-prime principal p-adic congruence subgroup is uniform result.
