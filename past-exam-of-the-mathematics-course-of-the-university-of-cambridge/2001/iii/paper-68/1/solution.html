An affine connection on the tangent bundle of a smooth manifold assigns a vector field ∇X​Y to two vector fields, is R-bilinear, and satisfies
∇fX​Y=f∇X​Y,∇X​(fY)=X(f)Y+f∇X​Y.
(1)
Thus its first slot is tensorial and its second obeys the Leibniz rule. Its action on covectors is defined by differentiating the pairing, and this extends it to tensor fields. A torsion-free connection has ∇X​Y−∇Y​X=[X,Y].
Assume that g is a smooth nondegenerate metric tensor. Metric compatibility and a vanishing torsion tensor imply the Koszul formula,
2g(∇X​Y,Z)=Xg(Y,Z)+Yg(Z,X)−Zg(X,Y)−g(X,[Y,Z])+g(Y,[Z,X])+g(Z,[X,Y]).
(2)
The right-hand side is determined by g and the Lie bracket of vector fields. Nondegeneracy determines ∇X​Y uniquely; conversely this formula defines an affine connection with both required properties, proving existence as well as uniqueness. In a coordinate frame the brackets vanish. Combining the three differentiated metric tensor identities gives
Γabc​=21​gad(∂b​gdc​+∂c​gdb​−∂d​gbc​).​
(3)
This is the Levi-Civita connection. Positive definiteness is unnecessary: the argument also works for a pseudo-Riemannian metric.
For the other affine connection define S(X,Y)=∇ˉX​Y−∇X​Y. The derivative of f cancels between the two Leibniz rules, so S is C∞-linear in both slots. The difference of affine connections is a tensor: here S is a section of TM⊗T∗M⊗T∗M with components Γˉabc​−Γabc​. Both affine connections are torsion-free, so S(X,Y)=S(Y,X).
Agreement of geodesics means agreement of their unparametrized curves; different affine parameters are allowed. For every nonzero tangent vector v, existence of a geodesic with that initial velocity implies that S(v,v) is parallel to v. The symmetric bilinear diagonal-parallel lemma now determines S. Choose a basis ei​ and write S(ei​,ei​)=αi​ei​. Applying the parallelism condition to ei​+ej​ and ei​−ej​ gives
S(ei​,ej​)=21​αj​ei​+21​αi​ej​.
(4)
With V(ei​)=αi​/2, bilinearity yields
Sabc​=δba​Vc​+δca​Vb​=2δa(b​Vc)​,Vc​=n+1Saac​​,n=dimM.​
(5)
This is projective equivalence of affine connections. It also covers dimension one. For two Levi-Civita connections, the projective covector from metric volume densities gives the explicit answer
Vc​=2(n+1)1​∂c​log​detgdetgˉ​​​.​
(6)

This determinant expression requires nondegeneracy of gˉ​; the difference-tensor and earlier trace formula require only the two torsion-free affine connections.
Conversely, the displayed tensor gives ∇ˉγ˙​​γ˙​=2V(γ˙​)γ˙​ along an affinely parametrized ∇-geodesic. Choosing a new parameter t(τ) satisfying t′′/t′=2V(γ˙​) removes that tangential acceleration.
