Use integral singular chains. The inclusion Y⊆X identifies Ck​(Y) with the subgroup of Ck​(X) generated by the singular simplices whose images lie in Y. It is a chain subcomplex. Define
Ck​(X,Y)=Ck​(X)/Ck​(Y),∂ˉ(c+Ck​(Y))=∂c+Ck−1​(Y).
(17)
This boundary is well defined because ∂Ck​(Y)⊆Ck−1​(Y), and its square is zero because ∂2=0. Its homology is, by definition, Hk​(X,Y). A relative cycle has a representative c with ∂c∈Ck−1​(Y); representatives of the same relative homology class differ by ∂b+y, where b∈Ck+1​(X) and y∈Ck​(Y).
Inclusion and quotient induce homomorphisms i∗​:Hk​(Y)→Hk​(X) and j∗​:Hk​(X)→Hk​(X,Y). Define the relative homology connecting homomorphism by
δ:Hk​(X,Y)⟶Hk−1​(Y),δ[c]=[∂c].
(18)
Here ∂c is a cycle in Y since ∂2c=0. If c is changed to c+∂b+y, its boundary changes by ∂y, a boundary in Y, proving independence of representatives. The formula is additive, so δ is a homomorphism.
The long exact sequence in relative homology is
⋯⟶Hk​(Y)i∗​​Hk​(X)j∗​​Hk​(X,Y)δ​Hk−1​(Y)i∗​​Hk−1​(X)⟶⋯​
(19)
and ends in
H0​(Y)i∗​​H0​(X)j∗​​H0​(X,Y)⟶0.
(20)
We prove all three types of exactness directly, so no unproved abstract lemma about chain complexes is required.
At Hk​(X), j∗​i∗​=0 because a chain in Y is zero in the relative chain group. Conversely, let an absolute cycle c have j∗​[c]=0. The definition of a relative boundary gives c=∂b+y with y∈Ck​(Y). Since c is a cycle, ∂y=0. Thus [c]=i∗​[y], proving kerj∗​=imi∗​.
At Hk​(X,Y), δj∗​=0 because an absolute cycle has zero boundary. Conversely, suppose δ[c]=0. Then ∂c=∂y for some y∈Ck​(Y). Therefore c−y is an absolute cycle and j∗​[c−y]=[c]. Hence kerδ=imj∗​.
At Hk−1​(Y), i∗​δ=0 because ∂c is a boundary in X. Conversely, if a cycle y∈Ck−1​(Y) becomes zero in Hk−1​(X), then y=∂c for some c∈Ck​(X). That c is a relative cycle, and δ[c]=[y]. Thus keri∗​=imδ.
These arguments also prove the assertions at shifted indices. Finally, every zero-dimensional relative cycle lifts to a chain in C0​(X), and every zero-chain is an absolute cycle; this proves surjectivity onto H0​(X,Y). All maps, representative changes, compositions and exactness assertions have consequently been justified from the chain definitions.
