Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2001 / iii / Paper 13 / 5 / b / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2001 iii Paper 13 5 b
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
Identify S2b−1 with the unit sphere in Cb, and put ζ=e2πi/a. The generator of the cyclic group Z/a acts by
(z1​,…,zb​)⟼(ζz1​,…,ζzb​).
(24)
It preserves the sphere because ∣ζ∣=1. If its kth power fixes a point, then (ζk−1)zj​=0 for every j. Some coordinate is nonzero, so ζk=1, meaning k≡0(moda). Thus the scalar root-of-unity action is free. The case a=1 is the trivial group and also satisfies freeness. The quotient is a standard lens space.
