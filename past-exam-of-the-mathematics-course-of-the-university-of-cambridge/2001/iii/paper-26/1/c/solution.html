The scalar clipping map g(m)=min(b,max(a,m)) is continuous. The rate function under scalar clipping has endpoint costs infm≤a​J(m) and infm≥b​J(m). Since a<μ<b and the costs decrease towards μ from the left and increase to its right, both infima are attained at the corresponding endpoints.
