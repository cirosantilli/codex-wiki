Retain only fields independent of the circle coordinate y, with coordinate period 2πr0​. Use the string-frame metric gμν​ and the circle reduction of eleven-dimensional supergravity ansatz
ds112​=e−2Φ/3gμν​dxμdxν+e4Φ/3(dy−C1​)2,A3​=C3​+B2​∧dy.
(16)
The minus sign in the fibre one-form is a convention chosen to give the usual F4​=dC3​−C1​∧H3​ below; changing the sign of C1​ consistently changes no physics. The metric supplies the ten-dimensional graviton, dilaton and RR one-form, while the eleven-dimensional three-form supplies the Kalb–Ramond field and RR three-form. Define
η=dy−C1​,F2​=dC1​,H3​=dB2​,G4​=dC3​,F4​=G4​−C1​∧H3​.
(17)
Then F4(11)​=F4​+H3​∧η. In particular dF4​=−F2​∧H3​, the reduced Bianchi identity. The field strength, not bare dC3​ alone, is the gauge-covariant horizontal component.
For the gravitational term, the block determinant gives
−G​=e−8Φ/3−g​.
(18)
A useful two-step curvature calculation first removes the overall factor e−2Φ/3. The intermediate metric dsˉ2=dss2​+e2Φη2 has
Rˉ=R[g]−41​e2ΦF2,μν​F2μν​−2∇2Φ−2(∇Φ)2,∇ˉ2Φ=∇2Φ+(∇Φ)2.
(19)
The eleven-dimensional conformal-curvature identity then gives
R[G]=e2Φ/3[Rˉ+320​∇ˉ2Φ−10(∇ˉΦ)2].
(20)
Combining the terms yields
−G​R[G]=−g​{e−2Φ[R+314​∇2Φ−316​(∇Φ)2]−41​F2,μν​F2μν​}.
(21)
Integration by parts gives ∫−g​e−2Φ∇2Φ=2∫−g​e−2Φ(∇Φ)2 modulo boundary terms. Therefore the Einstein-Hilbert action produces
−g​[e−2Φ(R+4(∇Φ)2)−41​F2,μν​F2μν​].
(22)
This explicitly derives both the string-frame dilaton kinetic coefficient and the RR one-form kinetic term.
Use the normalized form norm ∣Fk​∣2=Fμ1​⋯μk​​Fμ1​⋯μk​/k!, interpreting the printed four-form square in this convention. The horizontal and fibre pieces are orthogonal in the η basis. Four horizontal inverse metrics contribute e8Φ/3 to ∣F4​∣112​. Three horizontal inverse metrics and one fibre inverse metric contribute e2Φ/3 to ∣H3​∧η∣112​. Thus
−G​∣F4(11)​∣2=−g​[∣F4​∣2+e−2Φ∣H3​∣2].
(23)
The NS-NS three-form has the same e−2Φ prefactor as gravity; the RR kinetic terms do not have it in the string frame.
Finally reduce the topological term using the coordinate decomposition, which makes its signs transparent. The part of A3​∧F4(11)​∧F4(11)​ containing exactly one dy is
[B2​∧G4​∧G4​+2C3​∧G4​∧H3​]∧dy.
(24)
The purely horizontal eleven-form vanishes on the ten-dimensional base. Since
d(C3​∧B2​∧G4​)=B2​∧G4​∧G4​−C3​∧H3​∧G4​,
(25)
integration over the base turns the bracket into 3B2​∧G4​∧G4​, modulo the displayed exact term. The reduction of the eleven-dimensional Chern-Simons term therefore gives −21​∫B2​∧G4​∧G4​.
Absorbing the coordinate circumference into κ102​=κ112​/(2πr0​), the complete bosonic massless type IIA supergravity action in these conventions is
S10​=2κ102​1​{​∫d10x−g​[e−2Φ(R+4(∇Φ)2−21​∣H3​∣2)−21​∣F2​∣2−21​∣F4​∣2]−21​∫B2​∧G4​∧G4​}.​​
(26)
Dropping boundary terms assumes appropriate boundary conditions; nontrivial flux bundles may require patchwise potentials. Higher circle modes and higher-derivative corrections are excluded by this low-energy zero-mode reduction. No Romans-mass term arises from the ordinary ansatz.
The M-theory origin of type IIA D-branes follows by momentum, wrapping and magnetic geometry:

An M2-brane wrapped around the circle gives the fundamental string, and an unwrapped M5-brane gives the NS5-brane. These are NS objects rather than D-branes. In physical string/Planck units, the M-theory circle duality relations are
R11​=gs​ℓs​,ℓp3​=gs​ℓs3​,ℓs2​=α′.
(27)
They involve the physical asymptotic radius, distinguished from the arbitrary coordinate period used in the reduction ansatz. For example, mD0​=1/(gs​ℓs​)=1/R11​ is one momentum quantum. With TM2​=1/[(2π)2ℓp3​] and TM5​=1/[(2π)5ℓp6​],
TD2​=TM2​,TD4​=2πR11​TM5​=(2π)4gs​ℓs5​1​,TF1​=2πR11​TM2​=2πℓs2​1​.
(28)
The D6-brane is purely gravitational in eleven dimensions, rather than an elementary six-dimensional membrane; the circle-fibration magnetic charge gives its ten-dimensional interpretation.
A D8-brane is the important qualification. It sources the zero-form flux of massive type IIA supergravity and is not obtained as an ordinary wrapped M2/M5 or Kaluza-Klein object of the undeformed circle reduction above. Its extended/generalized lifts are outside this simple dictionary. The massless-circle interpretation of the other branes is developed in Four Lectures on M-theory.
