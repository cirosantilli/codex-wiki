For a coalition S, its coalition excess at an allocation is e(S,x)=v(S)−x(S), measuring its complaint. Sort all these excesses of a coalition in decreasing order. The nucleolus is the imputation whose sorted vector is smallest in lexicographic order: minimize the greatest complaint first, then the next among ties, and continue. Including the empty and grand coalitions merely inserts two fixed zero entries and does not change the minimizing allocation.
For every efficient allocation, the excesses of a coalition of {2} and {1,3} sum to zero:

Since the core of a cooperative game from part (b) is nonempty, this first-stage minimum is exactly zero and its minimizers are precisely that core of a cooperative game.
