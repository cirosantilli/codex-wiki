Near ω=f, namely KRD​≪1 with the Rossby deformation radius RD​=c0​/f, cp​∼f/K is large while cg​∼c02​K/f is small. The K=0 limit is an inertial oscillation, for which a spatial phase speed is not defined. Far above f, both speeds approach c0​, the shallow water gravity-wave speed, with cp​>c0​ and cg​<c0​ at finite K.
On the zero-frequency branch, geostrophic balance gives

For a nonconstant plane wave, q​=−(gK2/f)ζ​: relative vorticity and elevation have opposite signs. They can both be nonzero and spatially structured, whereas the horizontal divergence vanishes identically. A constant elevation is also a zero-frequency solution, with zero velocity and zero relative vorticity.

The initial outward pressure force creates a cross-ridge current; the Coriolis acceleration turns that current and builds the along-ridge geostrophic flow. The remainder is radiated inertia-gravity waves, not a dissipative disappearance of energy.
For precision, use the Fourier transform ζ​(k)=∫ζ(x)e−ikxdx. Initial rest implies ζt​(x,0)=0, and the conserved anomaly gives

These formulas satisfy both initial conditions. They also show that the waves carry no linear potential vorticity anomaly: q=(f/h00​)(ζ−ζ0​) and δ=−ζt​/h00​ reconstruct the remaining fields, with u and v obtained from their derivatives. On the infinite line, dispersive wave packets propagate away and decay locally, leaving the balanced ridge and its oppositely directed flanking currents. Conservation of energy still holds globally. In a reflecting finite basin, standing waves may persist, so a permanent pointwise relaxation is not guaranteed without dissipation.
If L≫RD​, most of the initial elevation remains balanced: ζs​≃ζ0​ with small smoothing corrections. If L≪RD​, the balanced ridge spreads to width RD​, and away from the original narrow core

(23)
Thus the final central height is much smaller than the initial one and the wave component is substantial. The integral of the balanced elevation equals the initial integral, since the Green's function has unit integral; the oscillatory component has zero net added volume.
