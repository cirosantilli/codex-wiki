Take homologous profiles and hold composition, hence mean molecular weight, fixed. The stellar mass conservation equation and stellar hydrostatic equation give ρc​∝M/R3 and Pc​∝GM2/R4. With ideal gas pressure support, Tc​∝M/R. Integrating the specific nuclear rate through homologous profiles then gives
Lnuc​∝Mρc​Tc5​∝M7R−8.
(1)
The radiative diffusion in a star equation gives Lrad​∝RTc4​/(κc​ρc​). Here κc​∝ρc1/2​Tc−5/2​∝M−2R, so
Lrad​∝M5R−1.
(2)
Equality of generated and transported luminosity implies M7R−8∝M5R−1, whence
R∝M2/7,L∝M33/7.​
(3)
This is the radiative homology with density-half inverse-five-halves opacity scaling.
The Stefan–Boltzmann law defines the effective temperature by L=4πR2σSB​Teff4​. Therefore Teff​∝M29/28. In theoretical Hertzsprung-Russell diagram coordinates, the slope is
dlogTeff​dlogL​=29132​.​
(4)
The usual graphical convention puts increasing temperature to the left, so the line descends as one moves right. If the horizontal coordinate is −logTeff​, its plotted slope is −132/29.
