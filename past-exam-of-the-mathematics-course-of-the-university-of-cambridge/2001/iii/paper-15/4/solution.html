The Hermitian metric induces pointwise Hermitian inner products on the exterior powers of the complex cotangent bundle, with different bidegrees orthogonal, and a positive volume form dVg​. Thus the global L2 inner product on smooth differential forms of type (p,q) is
⟨α,β⟩L2​=∫M​⟨α(x),β(x)⟩g​dVg​.
(22)
Let ∂ˉ∗ be the formal adjoint of the Dolbeault operator, defined by integration by parts, and let Δ∂ˉ​=∂ˉ∂ˉ∗+∂ˉ∗∂ˉ. This Dolbeault Laplacian preserves bidegree, is elliptic and nonnegative, and has
⟨Δ∂ˉ​α,α⟩=∥∂ˉα∥2+∥∂ˉ∗α∥2.
(23)
On a compact Hermitian manifold without boundary, the Dolbeault Hodge decomposition on a compact Hermitian manifold says that the smooth harmonic space H∂ˉp,q​=kerΔ∂ˉ​ is finite-dimensional and
Ap,q=H∂ˉp,q​⊕⊥Δ∂ˉ​Ap,q.
(24)
More precisely, there are the orthogonal harmonic projection H and a Dolbeault Green operator G taking smooth forms to smooth forms, with G=0 on harmonics and Δ∂ˉ​G=GΔ∂ˉ​=I−H. Elliptic regularity ensures these are statements about smooth forms, not only L2 completions. The Green operator commutes with ∂ˉ and ∂ˉ∗, since the Laplacian does and its inverse is unique on the orthogonal complement of the kernel.
Expanding α=Hα+Δ∂ˉ​Gα gives
Ap,q=H∂ˉp,q​⊕∂ˉAp,q−1⊕∂ˉ∗Ap,q+1.​
(25)
The three summands are orthogonal: harmonic forms are killed by both adjoints, and ⟨∂ˉu,∂ˉ∗v⟩=⟨∂ˉ2u,v⟩=0. If α is ∂ˉ-closed, its coexact component is orthogonal to every closed form; taking its inner product with α shows that component has norm zero. Conversely the first two components are closed. Thus ker∂ˉ=H∂ˉp,q​⊕∂ˉAp,q−1​, with out-of-range form spaces taken to be zero.
