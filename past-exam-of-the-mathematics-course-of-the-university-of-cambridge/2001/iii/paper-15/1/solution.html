At a point [X]∈Pn, the complex tautological line bundle has fiber CX⊆Cn+1. The hyperplane line bundle is its dual, [H]=O(1), and O(m)=O(1)⊗m. On Ui​={Xi​=0} let ti​=X/Xi​ be the tautological frame and ei​ its dual. On overlaps ej​=(Xj​/Xi​)ei​. The sheaf of holomorphic sections of a vector bundle of this dual bundle is the sheaf OPn​(1).
If a nonzero linear form ℓ cuts out the projective hyperplane H, it gives a holomorphic section sH​ of O(1) with a simple zero along H. Dividing a local section s by sH​ gives a meromorphic function with no poles away from H and at most a simple pole on H. Conversely multiplication by sH​ turns such a function into a holomorphic section. These mutually inverse local constructions agree on overlaps and prove the holomorphic line bundle associated to a divisor identification
O(1)≅O(H).​

For m=0, higher sheaf cohomology vanishes by assumption and global holomorphic functions are constant by compactness and the maximum principle. At a line ℓ=CX, the middle bundle has fiber Hom(ℓ,Cn+1) and the holomorphic tangent bundle has fiber Hom(ℓ,Cn+1/ℓ). Send a homomorphism to its composition with the quotient.
