Set L=KP​ and F=kp​, with valuation rings S,R. Let e be the ramification index, f the residue-field degree, and p the residue characteristic. A base uniformizer π generates Pe in S, up to a unit. The free R-module S has rank ef. For an integral a∈S, its field trace is the matrix trace of multiplication by a on this module. Reduction modulo π preserves the matrix trace.
The quotient S/πS has a filtration
S/Pe⊃P/Pe⊃⋯⊃Pe−1/Pe⊃0.
(13)
Each of the e successive quotients is a one-dimensional vector space over κL​=S/P. On each, multiplication by a acts as multiplication by its residue aˉ, whose trace over κF​=R/πR is TrκL​/κF​​(aˉ). A basis adapted to the filtration makes the multiplication matrix block triangular, so its trace is the sum of these quotient traces. This proves trace reduction in a ramified local extension:
TrL/F​(a)​=eTrκL​/κF​​(aˉ).
(14)
Since the hypothesis says p∣e, the right side is zero in κF​. Thus
TrKP​/kp​​(a)∈pOkp​​.​
(15)
No normality hypothesis is needed. The original PDF only requires divisibility of e, not divisibility exactly once; the converted TeX has corrupted that condition and several local subscripts.
