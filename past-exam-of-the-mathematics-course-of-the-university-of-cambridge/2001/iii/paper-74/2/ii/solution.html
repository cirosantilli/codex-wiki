Let G=Gal(K/k). Each field automorphism preserves the ring of integers and the base prime ideal, so it permutes the prime ideals above p. Suppose there were more than one orbit, and fix one orbit C. By the Chinese remainder theorem for ideals, choose a∈OK​ such that
a≡0(modP)(P∈C),a≡1(modQ)(Q∈/C, Q∣p).
(4)
The field norm NK/k​(a)=∏σ∈G​σ(a) lies in Ok​. It lies in every prime ideal of C, since for a fixed member each conjugate of a is zero modulo that member. Thus its contraction lies in p. On the other hand, for Q outside C, every σ−1(Q) is also outside it, so every σ(a) equals one modulo Q. The product is consequently one modulo Q. This contradicts its membership in pOK​⊆Q. Therefore the Galois action on the primes above p is transitive. The normal extension of number fields is separable, so its conjugates are exactly the elements of G used in the product.
