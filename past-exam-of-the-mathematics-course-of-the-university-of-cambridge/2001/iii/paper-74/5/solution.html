The trace pairing is nondegenerate in this separable field extension, so this is a full fractional ideal of S. The relative different ideal is its inverse:

Every integral x has integral traces against S, so S⊆D−1 and D⊆S.
For k=Q, take an integral basis b1​,…,bn​ and its trace pairing matrix T=(Tr(bi​bj​)). In these coordinates the codifferent is T−1Zn, and
[D−1:S]=∣detT∣=∣dK​∣.

The ideal norm of D equals this index, by inversion of fractional ideal norms. Therefore the norm of the different ideal relation is
NK/Q​(DK/Q​)=∣dK​∣,dK​=(−1)r2​NK/Q​(DK/Q​).​

Here r2​ is the number of conjugate pairs of complex embeddings. The different is an ideal of the upper field, whereas the relative discriminant ideal is its ideal norm in the base field; over Q the ordinary signed field discriminant also records the signature.
