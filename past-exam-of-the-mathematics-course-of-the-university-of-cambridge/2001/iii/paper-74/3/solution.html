Here is why this definition is the intrinsic relative ideal norm from norms of elements, and why the residue weights occur. Localize at p. The finite torsion-free Rp​-module Sp​ is free of rank n, because Rp​ is a discrete valuation ring. Multiplication by a nonzero integral a has determinant NK/k​(a). The Smith normal form gives
vp​(NK/k​(a))=lengthRp​​(Sp​/aSp​).

Decompose the quotient at the prime ideals above p. In SP​ each quotient Pj/Pj+1 is one-dimensional over S/P, so has length fP​ over Rp​. Therefore
vp​(NK/k​(a))=∑P∣p​fP​vP​(a).
(7)
For an integral ideal I, all norms of elements of I have valuations at least ∑fP​vP​(I). These minima can be attained simultaneously at the finitely many prime ideals above a fixed p. In fact the Chinese remainder theorem for ideals, applied inside the finitely generated module I, chooses an a∈I with nonzero image in each I/PI. Equivalently vP​(a)=vP​(I) at all these primes. The generated ideal of the element norms consequently has exactly the displayed valuations. Clearing a denominator extends this description to fractional ideals.
Multiplication of fractional ideals adds their prime valuations. The displayed formula therefore proves
NK/k​(IJ)=NK/k​(I)NK/k​(J).​

It also gives NK/k​((a))=(NK/k​(a)).
Finally, reduction of the free rank-n module Sp​ modulo p has dimension n over R/p. The factorization pS=∏i​Piei​​ and the Chinese remainder theorem for ideals identify this reduction with the product of the quotients by Piei​​. Their filtrations have ei​ successive quotients of dimension fi​ over R/p. Adding dimensions proves the fundamental identity for prime decomposition:
[K:k]=i∑​ei​fi​.​
