An algebra involution is a conjugate-linear map a↦a∗ satisfying (ab)∗=b∗a∗ and (a∗)∗=a; on a unital algebra it fixes 1. In the Banach-star convention it is continuous, although the following spectral identity uses only its algebraic properties. An element a is invertible precisely when a∗ is: its inverse is (a−1)∗. A unital C-star algebra is a complex Banach algebra with an involution satisfying the C-star identity ∥a∗a∥=∥a∥2. A Normal element of a C-star algebra satisfies x∗x=xx∗. The norm identity for the nonzero identity implies ∥1∥=1.
