Let δ=2η/ω​ and q=(1+i)/δ. Within the magnetic skin layer, differentiation across the layer dominates differentiation along the wall by the factor b/δ. The leading resistive induction equation is therefore
iωBx​=η∂y2​Bx​,Bx​(x,0)=C(x),Bx​⟶0(y/δ⟶∞).
(6)
Because ηq2=iω and Req>0, its decaying solution is
Bx​=C(x)e−qy,Bz​=0.​
(7)
The solenoidal magnetic-field constraint determines the first smaller component,
By​=qC′(x)​e−qy,Bx​By​​=O(δ/b).
(8)
Corrections to Bx​ from longitudinal diffusion have relative order (δ/b)2. Thus the zero normal boundary field from the perfect conductor calculation is a leading-order condition, not an additional exact condition at finite η. A smaller exterior-field correction matches By​.
To leading order the electric current density is jz​=qCe−qy/μ0​. For two real harmonic fields, the period average of their product is half the real part of the product of one amplitude with the conjugate of the other. Thus the mean Lorentz force in a magnetic skin layer is
F=2μ0​1​Re{(∇×B)×B∗}.
(9)
Its normal component is
Fy​=2μ0​δC(x)2​e−2y/δ,F=Fy​ey​to leading order.​
(10)
The first possible tangential contribution from the smaller By​ vanishes: it is −CC′e−2y/δRe(q/q∗)/(2μ0​), and q/q∗=i. This cancellation matters in computing the tangential streaming flow.
