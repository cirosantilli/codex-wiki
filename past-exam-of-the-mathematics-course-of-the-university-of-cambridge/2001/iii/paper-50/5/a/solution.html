Use the conservative continuity equation and momentum balance, with σij​ the viscous stress:
ρt​+∂i​(ρui​)=0,∂t​(ρui​)+∂j​(ρui​uj​)=−∂i​p+∂j​σij​.
(46)
Differentiate the first equation in time and take the divergence of the second. Eliminating momentum gives
ρtt​=∂i​∂j​(ρui​uj​+pδij​−σij​).
(47)
Subtract c02​Δρ, and define the Lighthill stress tensor by
Tij​=ρui​uj​+[(p−p0​)−c02​(ρ−ρ0​)]δij​−σij​.​
(48)
The constant reference subtraction has no double divergence. The exact Lighthill acoustic analogy is
(∂t2​−c02​Δ)ρ′=∂i​∂j​Tij​,ρ′=ρ−ρ0​.​
(49)
This is an exact rearrangement; linearization is not used to eliminate the nonlinear source tensor.
Convolving with the retarded acoustic Green function and transferring the source derivatives by integration by parts gives
ρ′(x,t)=4πc02​1​∂xi​​∂xj​​∫∣x−y∣Tij​(y,t−∣x−y∣/c0​)​d3y.
(50)
The acoustic compact-source approximation requires source size ℓ to satisfy Ωℓ/c0​≪1 for its characteristic frequency Ω; the acoustic far field additionally requires r≫ℓ and Ωr/c0​≫1. Internal retardation is then negligible at leading order, and the radiating parts of the two observer derivatives act on the common retarded time. With ni​=xi​/r and Sij​=∫Tij​d3y, the far-field acoustic force and stress moments give
ρ′(x,t)∼4πc04​rni​nj​​S¨ij​(t−r/c0​).​
(51)
The two spatial derivatives supply two factors of 1/c0​, in addition to the 1/c02​ in the Green function.
For a subsonic fluctuating source of velocity scale V, size ℓ and turnover time ℓ/V, suppose its stress scale is Tij​=O(ρ0​V2). Then Sij​=O(ρ0​V2ℓ3) and S¨ij​=O(ρ0​V4ℓ). The compact acoustic quadrupole Mach-number scaling is therefore
ρ0​ρ′​=O(rℓ​(c0​V​)4)=O((ℓ/r)m4).​
(52)
This fourth-power statement concerns the acoustic density amplitude under the specified eddy-time and stress scaling. With an independently imposed frequency, the general estimate instead retains V2Ω2ℓ3/c04​r.
