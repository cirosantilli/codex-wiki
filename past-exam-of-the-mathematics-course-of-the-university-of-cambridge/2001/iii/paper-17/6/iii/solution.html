Write the Yoneda embedding as h:C→C. For any finite product in a category, its universal property gives
hA×B​(D)=C(D,A×B)≅C(D,A)×C(D,B),h1​(D)≅1.
(44)
These bijections are natural, so h preserves finite products. Now use the exponential in a presheaf category, followed by the Yoneda lemma and the exponential object property in C:
(hB​)hA​(D)​=Nat(hD​×hA​,hB​)≅Nat(hD×A​,hB​)≅C(D×A,B)≅C(D,BA)=hBA​(D).​
(45)
Every step is natural in D, so these component bijections form a natural isomorphism
hBA​≅(hB​)hA​.​
(46)
Under this isomorphism, the image of the evaluation BA×A→B is the presheaf evaluation: both correspond to the same identity of the exponential under the transpose bijection. Thus the Yoneda embedding preserves exponentials, including their evaluation maps.
