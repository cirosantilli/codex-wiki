Set n=6 in part (b). The quotient W=u⊥/⟨u⟩ has dimension four and a nondegenerate alternating form. The coordinate action therefore defines
ρ:S6​⟶Sp(W)≅Sp(4,2).
(30)
Prove it is faithful before comparing orders. If a permutation lies in the kernel of a group homomorphism, it fixes the quotient class of each two-element subset A. That class has exactly the two representatives A and A+Ω=Ω∖A. Their cardinalities are two and four. A coordinate permutation preserves cardinality, so it must fix A itself, not exchange it with its complement. Thus it fixes every two-element subset. Intersecting the fixed sets {i,j} and {i,k} shows it fixes each singleton {i}, so it is the identity. Hence ρ is injective.
By part (a),
∣Sp(4,2)∣=24(22−1)(24−1)=16⋅3⋅15=720=6!.
(31)
An injection between finite groups of equal order is surjective. Choosing a symplectic basis of W identifies the form with the standard one, so
Sp(4,2)≅S6​.​
(32)
This realizes the exceptional isomorphism through a concrete binary permutation module. Equality of orders alone would not establish it; the faithful form-preserving action supplies the required group homomorphism.
