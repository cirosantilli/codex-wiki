The jth coordinate plane has equation Xj=0, hence covector Fi​=δij​. The line-plane incidence in Plücker coordinates formula reduces to
P(j)i​=Lij(no sum over j).​
(5)
Thus the jth column of the upper-index Plücker coordinates contains the intersection point's homogeneous coordinates, up to projective scaling. Its jth entry vanishes as required. If that column is zero, both defining points have coordinate j zero and the entire line is contained in the coordinate plane; there is no distinguished intersection point. Reading rows instead changes only the irrelevant overall sign.
