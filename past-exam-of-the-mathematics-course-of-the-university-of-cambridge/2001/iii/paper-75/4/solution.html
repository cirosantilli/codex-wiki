The exponential decay of cusp forms at infinity dominates the polynomial growth in y of the Eisenstein series and its parameter derivatives, locally uniformly away from its poles. Thus its stated continuation can be passed through the integral. The completed series is meromorphic on C, with possible simple poles only at s=k−1,k, and its functional equation is

It is important to separate this completed statement from the raw F. Inverting the completion gives meromorphic continuation of F, but zeros of ζ(2s−2k+2) in the denominator can give additional possible poles unless canceled by the integral.
