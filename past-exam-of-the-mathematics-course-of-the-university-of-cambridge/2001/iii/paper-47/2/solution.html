Consequently the Lamé parameters from hyperelastic energy Hessian are
λ=ρ0​WA11​A22​​(I),μ=ρ0​WA12​A12​​(I),λ+2μ=ρ0​WA11​A11​​(I).​

the elastic stiffness tensor of linear elasticity. A prestressed reference state requires the corresponding initial-stress corrections; the displayed formula concerns the natural state.
For a plane elastic wave, u=u(X1​,t), put r=e1​+u,X1​​ and Ψ(r)=W([r,e2​,e3​]). Balance of momentum without body force becomes
ui,tt​=∂X1​​Ψri​​(r),rt​=v,X1​​,vt​=C(r)r,X1​​,v=ut​, C=D2Ψ.​

This is a material-coordinate equation: the reference density cancels between inertia and nominal stress. If Caα​=cα2​aα​, the first-order system has characteristic speeds ±cα​ and right eigenvectors (aα​,∓cα​aα​). Along a characteristic curve with dX1​/dt=s=±cα​, its differential compatibility relation is
aα​⋅dv−saα​⋅dr=0.

This follows by multiplying the system by the left eigenvector (−saαT​,aαT​). It does not assert the existence of globally integrable Riemann invariants for an arbitrary three-component constitutive law.
There is a necessary qualification to the finite-amplitude transverse request. Set u=(0,w,0) and q=w,X1​​, so r=(1,q,0). The longitudinal momentum equation requires
0=∂X1​​Ψr1​​(1,q,0).
(12)
Thus arbitrary nonuniform pure transverse profiles are possible only if the compatibility condition for pure nonlinear transverse waves holds:
Ψr1​​(1,q,0) is independent of q,Ψr1​r2​​(1,q,0)=0.​
(13)
The third component vanishes by the reflection symmetry implied by isotropy and objectivity. The shear equation then closes:
ρ0​wtt​=∂X1​​τ(q),τ(q)=dqd​W([e1​+qe2​,e2​,e3​]),c(q)2=ρ0​τ′(q)​>0.

W=2λ​(trE)2+μtr(E2),E=2ATA−I​,λ,μ>0.

For A=[(a,q,0)T,e2​,e3​], this becomes
W(a,q)=8λ+2μ​(a2+q2−1)2+2μq2​.

The compatible nonlinear shear energy class is nonempty and can be genuinely nonlinear. Define J=detA, S=tr(ATA)−2J, and, for h≥0, take
W=2μ​(S−1)+4h​(S−1)2+2λ+μ​(J−1)2.

This is objective and isotropic. Near A=I, S−1=2tr(e2)−(tre)2+O(∣H∣3), so it has the specified Lamé parameters. On the plane deformation above, S−1=(a−1)2+q2 and J=a. Hence P11​=0 at a=1, while
τ(q)=μq+hq3,c(q)=ρ0​μ+3hq2​​.

For positive λ,μ, the relevant incremental plane moduli are positive. This supplies an explicit stable pure transverse family and, when h>0, amplitude-dependent wave speed.
For any compatible family, let
H(q)=∫0q​c(z)dz,v=wt​.

Consider the initially undisturbed half-space X1​>0. A right-going simple wave has the incoming invariant v+H=0, so v=−H(q). Denote the prescribed boundary nominal stress by T(t)=P21​(0,t)=τ(qb​(t)). The physical boundary traction, whose outward normal is −e1​, is −T(t)e2​. Invert the monotone law τ to obtain qb​. The wave state emitted at boundary time s is transported along
X1​=c(qb​(s))(t−s),q(X1​,t)=qb​(s),v(X1​,t)=−H(qb​(s)).​
(21)
Ahead of the leading front X1​=c(0)t the state is zero. Before characteristic crossing, these equations determine the unique emission time s for each disturbed point. The displacement itself is also determined, rather than just its derivatives:
w(0,s)=−∫0s​H(qb​(ζ))dζ,w(X1​,t)=w(0,s)+{c(qb​(s))qb​(s)−H(qb​(s))}(t−s).

Differentiating the implicit emission relation verifies w,X1​​=qb​(s) and wt​=−H(qb​(s)), so this is the complete pre-shock simple wave solution.
Write cb​(s)=c(qb​(s)). The boundary-generated characteristic curves lose invertibility when
∂s​X1​=c˙b​(s)(t−s)−cb​(s)=0.
(23)
Thus the characteristic crossing in a boundary-generated elastic simple wave criterion is
c˙b​(s)>0,tsh​=c˙b​>0inf​(s+c˙b​cb​​),Xsh​(s)=c˙b​cb2​​.​
(24)
Here c˙b​=c′(qb​)T′/(ρ0​cb2​). A smoothly increasing boundary stress therefore steepens into a shock wave only when later states travel faster: c′(qb​)T′>0. Increasing stress alone is insufficient. For the compatible example with h>0 and qb​>0, c′(qb​)=3hqb​/(ρ0​cb​)>0, so a nonzero increasing ramp produces finite-time crossing. For h=0 all rays are parallel and no such shock forms. This criterion locates the first gradient singularity; subsequent discontinuous evolution additionally requires the jump conditions and an admissibility condition.
For a general vector simple wave, replace the pure-shear line by an integral curve

Generic finite-amplitude quasi-transverse waves may contain longitudinal displacement; arbitrary vector boundary forcing need not generate a single simple wave.
