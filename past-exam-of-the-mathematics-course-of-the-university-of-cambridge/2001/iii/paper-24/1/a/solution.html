Observing Xs​ reveals exactly whether ω<s. Thus the natural filtration is
Ft​=σ{(0,s):0≤s≤t}.
(2)
Every generating set either contains the entire tail [t,∞) or avoids it, so this tail remains one indistinguishable atom. Conversely, these initial intervals generate the full relative Borel sigma-algebra on (0,t): use rational endpoints below t, together with (0,t) itself. Therefore every Borel set contained in (0,t), and its complement, belongs to Ft​. We obtain
Ft​={B∈F:B⊆(0,t) or Bc⊆(0,t)}.​
(3)
The strict endpoint is essential: the value at ω=t is still Xt​=1, so it cannot be distinguished from a later lifetime.
