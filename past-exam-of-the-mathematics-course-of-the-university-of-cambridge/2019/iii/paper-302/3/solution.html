The root lattice is Q=∑i​Zα(i). The weight lattice is
P={λ:⟨λ,(α(i))∨⟩∈Z for every i}.

Because every Cartan integer ⟨α(j),(α(i))∨⟩ is integral, Q⊆P. The Dynkin labels of λ are
[m1​,…,mr​],mi​=⟨λ,(α(i))∨⟩.

There are three isomorphism classes of complex simple rank-three Lie algebras: types A3 root systemA3​, B3 root systemB3​, and C3 root systemC3​. Use the convention Aij​=⟨α(i),(α(j))∨⟩, and order the chain as 1--2--3 with ∣α(1)∣=∣α(2)∣.
  • For A3​, all roots have the same length and the diagram has two single edges. Its Cartan matrix and angles are
AA3​​=​2−10​−12−1​0−12​​,θ12​=θ23​=120∘,quadθ13​=90∘,quad∣α(3)∣∣α(2)∣​=1.
(19)
  • For B3​, take α(1)=e1​−e2​, α(2)=e2​−e3​, and α(3)=e3​. The double-edge arrow points to the short third root, and
AB3​​=​2−10​−12−1​0−22​​,θ12​=120∘,quadθ23​=135∘,quadθ13​=90∘,quad∣α(3)∣∣α(2)∣​=2​.
(20)
  • For C3​, take α(1)=e1​−e2​, α(2)=e2​−e3​, and α(3)=2e3​. The double-edge arrow points to the short second root, and
AC3​​=​2−10​−12−2​0−12​​,θ12​=120∘,quadθ23​=135∘,quadθ13​=90∘,quad∣α(3)∣∣α(2)∣​=2​1​.

For A3​ these are the weights of the defining representation of sl4​; for B3​ they are {±e1​,±e2​,±e3​,0} in the vector representation of so7​; for C3​ they are {±e1​,±e2​,±e3​} in the defining representation of sp6​. Hence the requested dimensions are 4, 7, and 6, respectively.
