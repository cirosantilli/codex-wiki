A Cartan subalgebra h of a finite-dimensional complex semisimple Lie algebra is a maximal abelian subalgebra consisting of semisimple elements. The root-space decomposition is
g=h⊕⨁α∈Φ​gα​,gα​={X:[H,X]=α(H)X for all H∈h},
(7)
and the nonzero functionals α are the roots. A Cartan-Weyl basis consists of a basis Hi​ of h and root vectors Eα​∈gα​. Its brackets have the form
[Hi​,Hj​]=0,qquad[Hi​,Eα​]=α(Hi​)Eα​,qquad[Eα​,E−α​]=Hα​,
(8)
and [Eα​,Eβ​]=Nαβ​Eα+β​ when α+β is a root, and zero when α+β is neither a root nor zero.
