The required small parameters have distinct roles. The Rossby number ϵR​=U/(∣f0​∣L) makes inertial acceleration small relative to the leading geostrophic balance. U∣ua,h​∣​∼ϵa​=RSDmin(D,H)f02​L2H​∣f0​∣LU​≪1.​

If ω>0 or ω<ω∗​, then m2<0. Boundedness excludes the growing solution and uniquely selects χ​=Ψb​e−−m2​z​. If ω=ω∗​, boundedness excludes the linear-in-z solution, leaving χ​=Ψb​; this is the zero-vertical-wavenumber threshold.

(28)
