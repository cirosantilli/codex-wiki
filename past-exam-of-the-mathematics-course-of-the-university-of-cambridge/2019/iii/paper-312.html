The early-time state is the adiabatic Bunch-Davies vacuum: modes well inside the sound horizon approach positive-frequency Minkowski modes, and the tilted early-time contour selects the interacting vacuum by damping unwanted excitations. The in-in formalism evolves this one initial state forward to the observation time and backward, computing ⟨Ω∣Q(τ)∣Ω⟩. An in-out calculation instead computes a transition amplitude between prescribed initial and final states.
In the interaction picture, the free quadratic Hamiltonian evolves both QI and the free fields inside HintI​. The background Hamiltonian determines the homogeneous solution; the cubic Hamiltonian supplies the perturbative interaction insertions. In particular, the interaction-picture fields evolve under the quadratic Hamiltonian.

A scalar Fourier mode selects one spatial direction, k^, and has no transverse vector or tensor polarization. Rotations about k therefore leave its temperature and polarization patterns unchanged. Only azimuthal number m=0 occurs in the corresponding spherical harmonic expansion.
Let μ=k^⋅e and use the paper's convention
Θ(η,k,e)=∑ℓ≥0​(−i)ℓΘℓ​(η,k)Pℓ​(μ),
(25)
which omits the (2ℓ+1) factor used in some other definitions of the photon temperature multipole. In Fourier space the transport term is ikμΘ. The Legendre polynomial recurrence relation makes its coefficient at multipole ℓ equal to
k(2ℓ+3ℓ+1​Θℓ+1​−2ℓ−1ℓ​Θℓ−1​).
(26)
The gravitational time derivative is purely monopolar, and the potential gradient and baryon velocity are dipolar. Thomson scattering removes the monopole from its relaxation term and adds the quadrupole polarization correction. Thus the photon Boltzmann hierarchy is
Θ˙ℓ​+k(2ℓ+3ℓ+1​Θℓ+1​−2ℓ−1ℓ​Θℓ−1​)=​−τ˙[(δℓ0​−1)Θℓ​−δℓ1​vb​+10δℓ2​​(Θ2​−6​E2​)]+δℓ0​ϕ˙​+δℓ1​kψ.​​
(27)
The ℓ=0 term containing Θ−1​ is understood as zero.

Dropping gravity and expansion from the photon Boltzmann hierarchy gives
Θ˙0​=−3k​Θ1​​,Θ˙1​=kΘ0​−52k​Θ2​+τ˙(Θ1​+vb​)​.
