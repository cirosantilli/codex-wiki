The Teichmuller lift identifies the cyclic group Fq×​ with the group μq−1​ of prime-to-p roots of unity in K, where p is the residue characteristic. Consequently the multiplicative group of a non-Archimedean local field decomposes as
K×≅Z×μq−1​×(1+m).​

If d=[K:Qp​], the p-adic logarithm identifies a sufficiently deep higher principal-unit group with the additive group of a rank-d Zp​-lattice. The remaining kernel is precisely the finite group μpa​(K) of p-power roots of unity in K. Thus the principal-unit group of a mixed-characteristic local field has the topological group structure
1+m≅μpa​(K)×Zpd​.​
