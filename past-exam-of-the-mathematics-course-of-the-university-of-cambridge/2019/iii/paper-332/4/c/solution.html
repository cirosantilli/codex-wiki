h=AtF(η),η=DA2t3x​,F−3ηF′=1−η1​(ηF3)′,F(∞)=1.

At times comparable to t∗​, ice transport becomes important across the mountain's accumulation region, carries ice below the snowline, and establishes an ablation zone. The cap then approaches the steady conical ice cap with a linear accumulation gradient, with xN​→3ℓ/2. For a moving ice-covered disk, mass conservation and its front condition give
dtdV​=2πAcosα(2xN2​​−3ℓxN3​​).
(53)
The total input vanishes at xN​=3ℓ/2, explaining the final extent. The narrow apex and front regions are governed by local adjustments to the bulk approximation; they do not create a further distinct large-scale time regime in this scaling description.
