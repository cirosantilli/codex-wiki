A procedure controls the familywise error rate at level α when, for every configuration of true nulls I,
P(at least one Hi​,i∈I, is rejected)≤α.
(1)
Let i∗​=minI. The step-down procedure can reject any true null only if it reaches and rejects Hi∗​​, which requires pi∗​​≤α. Since a valid p-value under its null satisfies P(pi∗​​≤α)≤α, the procedure controls FWER without any assumption on dependence among the p-values.
A distribution P has the global Markov property for a directed acyclic graph G when every D-separation statement A⊥G​B∣C implies the corresponding conditional independence ZA​⊥⊥ZB​∣ZC​ under P.
If G∈S has nonadjacent vertices j,k, choose a topological ordering. Suppose j precedes k. Then j is a non-descendant and non-parent of k, so the directed local Markov property gives
Zj​⊥⊥Zk​∣ZpaG​(k)​.
(2)
The reversed ordering case is analogous. Hence
H0​⊆⋃S⊆[p]∖{j,k}​HS​.

P(false rejection of H0​)≤P(pS∗​​≤α)≤α.
(4)
This is nontrivial because it rejects whenever every tested conditional independence is rejected.
