The holomorphic part of the free-boson worldsheet propagator gives
∂Xμ(z)∂Xν(w)∼−2α′​(z−w)2ημν​.
(1)
Apply Wick theorem to T(z)T(w). The two double contractions give D/[2(z−w)4], while the single contractions reconstruct T and its derivative. Thus the stress-tensor operator-product expansion is
T(z)T(w)∼(z−w)4D/2​+(z−w)22T(w)​+z−w∂T(w)​​
(2)
and the D embedding coordinates have central charge c=D​.
The holomorphic stress-energy tensor generates an infinitesimal conformal transformation through
δv​T(z)=2πi1​∮z​dwv(w)T(w)T(z).
(3)
Taking the three residues gives
δv​T=12c​∂3v+2(∂v)T+v∂T​.
(4)
The third derivative is the anomalous term that prevents T from transforming as an ordinary weight-two Virasoro primary operator.
With Virasoro algebra modes Ln​=(2πi)−1∮dzzn+1T(z), a second contour calculation gives
[Lm​,Ln​]=(m−n)Lm+n​+12c​m(m2−1)δm+n,0​​,
(5)
so A(m)=c(m3−m)/12=D(m3−m)/12. This Virasoro central extension is the quantum conformal anomaly. In string theory the matter and ghost contributions must cancel; cmatter​+cghost​=D−26=0 gives the critical dimension of string theory D=26 and makes the BRST operator nilpotent.

Split the string embedding map into its constant worldsheet zero mode and orthogonal fluctuations, Xμ=xμ+X′μ. The kinetic operator has no inverse on the constant mode, while its inverse on X′ is the worldsheet Green function G. Completing the square in the Gaussian functional integral gives
∫DX′e−S[X′]−∫J⋅X′∝exp(21​∫Σ×Σ​d2zd2wJ(z)G(z,w)J(w)).
(6)
The remaining ordinary integral is
∫dDxe−xμ∫Σ​d2zJμ​(z),
(7)
which proves (1), up to a source-independent functional determinant.
To insert n tachyon vertex operators, choose
Jμ​(z,zˉ)=−i∑j=1n​kjμ​δ(2)(z−zj​).
(8)
The zero-mode integral produces target-space momentum conservation,
(2π)Dδ(D)(∑j​kj​).
(9)
In the nonzero-mode integral, discard the coincident self-contractions by normal ordering. With G(z,w)=−(α′/2)log∣z−w∣2, the remaining pairwise contractions produce the Koba-Nielsen factor, so
⟨j=1∏n​eikj​⋅X(zj​,zˉj​)⟩∝(2π)Dδ(D)(j∑​kj​)i<j∏​∣zi​−zj​∣α′ki​⋅kj​​.
(10)
For three closed-string tachyons on the sphere, ki2​=4/α′ and ∑i​ki​=0, hence α′ki​⋅kj​=−2 for i=j. The matter correlator is therefore ∣z12​z13​z23​∣−2. Gauge fixing the sphere's Möbius transformation group fixes three insertion points and supplies the bc ghost system correlator ∣z12​z13​z23​∣2, exactly cancelling this position dependence. Thus
A3​=CS2​gc3​(2π)Dδ(D)(k1​+k2​+k3​)​.
(11)
Here each vertex contributes one closed-string coupling gc​, CS2​ contains the sphere vacuum normalization and conventions, the worldsheet zero mode gives the momentum delta function, the nonzero modes give the Koba-Nielsen factor, and the ghost determinant divides by the conformal Killing group. Since CS2​∝gs−2​ and gc​∝gs​, the net string genus expansion dependence is gs​.

The BRST operator QB​ is Grassmann odd and represents the gauge symmetry on the gauge-fixed state space. Requiring two successive BRST transformations to vanish means
QB2​=21​{QB​,QB​}=0.
(12)
This nilpotence makes physical states a BRST cohomology. If [QB​,S0​]=0 and Ψ is the gauge-fixing fermion, the graded Jacobi identity gives
[QB​,S]=[QB​,S0​]+[QB​,{QB​,Ψ}]=21​[{QB​,QB​},Ψ]=0,
(13)
so S=S0​+{QB​,Ψ} is BRST invariant.
A holomorphic field of conformal weight h has the Laurent expansion
ϕ(z)=∑n∈Z​ϕn​z−n−h.
(14)
Under the state–operator correspondence, ϕ(z)∣0⟩ must be regular at the origin. Terms with n>−h have negative powers, so
ϕn​∣0⟩=0(n>−h)​.
(15)
For the anticommuting bc system,
b(z)=∑n​bn​z−n−2,c(z)=∑n​cn​z−n+1,{bm​,cn​}=δm+n,0​.
(16)
Separating creation and annihilation modes and summing the geometric series for ∣z∣>∣w∣ gives the bc ghost operator-product expansion
b(z)c(w)∼z−w1​​.
(17)
The BRST current built from the matter and ghost stress tensors has an operator-product expansion with c whose residue gives
{QB​,c(z)}=c(z)∂c(z)​.
(18)
For a matter Virasoro primary operator Φ(z,zˉ) of weights (1,1), the two standard string vertex operators are
U(z,zˉ)=c(z)cˉ(zˉ)Φ(z,zˉ)​,V=∫Σ​d2zΦ(z,zˉ)​.
(19)
The first is the local, unintegrated vertex. Using QB​c=c∂c, QB​cˉ=cˉ∂ˉcˉ, and the weight-(1,1) transformation of Φ, the terms cancel pairwise and QB​U=0. For the integrated vertex,
{QB​,Φ}=∂(cΦ)+∂ˉ(cˉΦ),
(20)
so {QB​,V}=0 on a closed worldsheet because the variation is a total derivative. Both vertices therefore represent the same BRST cohomology class.

(22)
Hence
∂Xμ(z)∂Xν(w)∼−2α′​(z−w)2ημν​.

[αmμ​,αnν​]=mημνδm+n,0​​.

M2=α′4​(N−1)=α′4​(Nˉ−1),N=Nˉ.
(26)

MT2​=−α′4​,Mg2​=0.​
(27)

Its transversality, mass-shell condition, and polarization gauge redundancy are the worldsheet statements that the deformation is marginal and is defined up to a linearized spacetime diffeomorphism.
