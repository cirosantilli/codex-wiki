In four spacetime dimensions [ψ]=3/2, [ϕ]=1, and [∂μ​]=1. Hence
[ψˉ​γμψ∂μ​ϕ]=5,[λ]=−1.​

The coupling is an irrelevant coupling by power counting in quantum field theory, so it is a nonrenormalizable interaction that would ordinarily define only an effective theory with a cutoff. Average over the two initial electron spins and two initial photon polarizations, and sum over the final ones. The supplied fermion spin sum and photon polarization sum turn the result into gamma-matrix traces. The Clifford algebra implies
γμ​aγμ=−2a,γμ​abγμ=4a⋅b,
(23)
by anticommuting the outside matrix through the product. Together with
tr(abcd)=4(a⋅bc⋅d−a⋅cb⋅d+a⋅db⋅c),
(24)
the two channel squares reduce, at m=0, to −2e4u/s and −2e4s/u; the remaining cross terms cancel. Thus, in terms of the Mandelstam variables,
∣M∣2​=−2e4(us​+su​).​
(25)
Physical Compton kinematics has s>0 and u<0, so the displayed expression is nonnegative.

For bosonic fields, time ordering places operators with later time arguments to the left:
T{ϕ1​⋯ϕn​}=ϕπ(1)​⋯ϕπ(n)​,tπ(1)​≥⋯≥tπ(n)​.
(26)
Normal ordering places all creation operators to the left of all annihilation operators and is denoted by colons. A Wick contraction is
contr(ϕi​,ϕj​)=⟨0∣T{ϕi​ϕj​}∣0⟩=DF​(xi​−xj​).
(27)
Wick theorem states
T{ϕ1​⋯ϕn​}=:ϕ1​⋯ϕn​:+single contractions∑​:⋯:+double contractions∑​:⋯:+⋯,​
(28)
where each sum runs over inequivalent disjoint pairings and contracted fields are replaced by their Feynman propagator.

Write each free field as creation and annihilation parts, ϕi​=ϕi−​+ϕi+​. Moving every annihilation part to the right produces commutators [ϕi+​,ϕj−​], which are precisely the contractions. Applying the n=2 identity once and then normal ordering the remaining field gives
