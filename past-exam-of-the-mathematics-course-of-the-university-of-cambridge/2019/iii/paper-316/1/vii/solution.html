For weak drag and C>0, take the resonant-argument libration centre ϕ0​≃π. The pericentre geometry of an exterior resonance then places the three pericentres at angles π/3, π, and 5π/3 relative to the planet. The trajectory closes after three particle orbits and five planet orbits. It moves retrograde in the rotating frame on average but develops a prograde loop near each periapsis, because the periapsis angular speed exceeds the planet's angular speed.
For a finite drag offset ϕ0​=π+arcsinS, rotate the whole particle pattern through arcsinS/3. The sketch uses the eccentricity requested by the second-order model; e≃0.555 is not small, so the curve is an illustration of that approximation rather than a quantitatively accurate integration of the full dissipative resonance.
