At emax​=B​, torque balance gives
sinϕ0​=−Ca2(p+q)Bq/2A(1+3B)​=−Ca2(5p−q)Bq/25A​.​
(8)
The magnitude of the right-hand side must not exceed one. This fixes the dissipative offset of the resonant-argument libration centre.
For C>0, put S=5A/[Ca2(5p−q)Bq/2]. The two torque-balanced phases are ϕ0​=−arcsinS and ϕ0​=π+arcsinS, modulo 2π. In the usual weak-perturbation pendulum approximation, δϕ˙​≃−3(p+q)nδa/(2a) and δa˙≃−2(p+q)CaBq/2cosϕ0​δϕ. Restoring libration thus selects cosϕ0​<0, giving
ϕ0​≃π+arcsinS(C>0).​
(9)
It approaches π when drag is weak compared with resonant torque. For C<0, the corresponding restoring branch has cosϕ0​>0 and approaches zero. The sine equation is the direct result independent of this leading-order stability approximation.
