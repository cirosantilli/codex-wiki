The last term averages to zero over the internal circular orbit. On the nearly circular stellar orbit, V≃vK​θ^ and vg​=(1−η)vK​θ^, so the averaged tangential acceleration is −ηκb​vK​.
With specific orbital energy −GM⋆​/(2ab​), the orbit-averaged drag work is −ηκb​vK2​, where vK2​=GM⋆​/ab​. Thus gas-driven migration of a binary centre of mass gives
a˙b​=−2ηκb​ab​=−τb​ab​​,τb​=2η(m1​/ts1​+m2​/ts2​)m1​+m2​​.​

This uses the weak-drag, nearly Keplerian approximation implicit in applying the circular-orbit work relation. Strongly gas-coupled orbits require a coupled radial-azimuthal drift solution.
