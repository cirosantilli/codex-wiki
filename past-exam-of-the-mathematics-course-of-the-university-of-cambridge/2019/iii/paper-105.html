The Lax-Milgram theorem states that if H is a real Hilbert space, B:H×H→R is a bounded bilinear form, and there is an α>0 such that
B(v,v)≥α∥v∥H2​(v∈H),
(13)
then for every bounded linear functional F∈H′ there is a unique u∈H satisfying
B(u,v)=F(v)(v∈H).
(14)
Moreover, ∥u∥H​≤α−1∥F∥H′​​. Symmetry of B is not required.
