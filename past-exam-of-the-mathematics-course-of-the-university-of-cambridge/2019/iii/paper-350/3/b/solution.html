The notation P0​=N(0,I) denotes generalized Gaussian white noise over H=L2(R2), not a Gaussian measure supported on H: the identity is not a trace-class operator there. For example, realize the observation on a suitable space of tempered distributions. Its Cameron-Martin space of a Gaussian measure is H. For deterministic h∈H, the stochastic pairing Wm​(h) has normal distribution N(0,∥h∥H2​) under P0​ and is the isonormal Gaussian process indexed by H.
Interpret the stated maps between Sobolev spaces as bounded linear operators, as usual. In particular K:H→H2(R2)⊂H is bounded, so its adjoint operator K∗ is bounded on H. Hence A=K∗K maps H into H, which is the property needed here. The stronger smoothing follows by Sobolev duality: boundedness of K:H−4→H−2 gives K∗:H2→H4, and therefore A:H→H4⊂H. As Π(H)=1, the shift Au belongs to the noise Cameron-Martin space of a Gaussian measure for Π-almost every u.
