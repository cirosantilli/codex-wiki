Exhaust Z2 by finite boxes Gn​ and let Tn​ be a uniform spanning tree of Gn​. Every finite tree satisfies the handshaking lemma, so
∣V(Gn​)∣1​∑v∈V(Gn​)​degTn​​(v)=∣V(Gn​)∣2(∣V(Gn​)∣−1)​⟶2.
(30)
Choose the root uniformly from V(Gn​). The proportion of roots within any fixed distance of the boundary tends to zero, and the rooted trees converge locally to the uniform spanning tree T of Z2. Since every degree is at most four, expectations also converge. Translation invariance therefore gives
E[degT​(0)]=2.
(31)
The four edges incident to 0 have equal inclusion probability by the rotations and reflections of the square lattice. If that common probability is r, then 4r=EdegT​(0)=2. Consequently
P(e∈T)=21​​
(32)
for every edge e by translation invariance.
