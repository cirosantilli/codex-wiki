Use the standard sprinkling coupling for Bernoulli percolation: first expose the p-open clusters, then independently open each remaining edge with probability α=(q−p)/(1−p). Explore the q-cluster of the origin cluster by following sprinkled edges. Each discovered p-cluster has at most 2d times its number of vertices as many incident edges, so the exploration is dominated by a Galton-Watson process of mean at most 2dαχ(p)<1. This process dies out almost surely, and hence there is no infinite q-open cluster. Thus q≤pc​, and p<q implies p<pc​. Therefore
χ(p)<∞⟺p<pc​.​
