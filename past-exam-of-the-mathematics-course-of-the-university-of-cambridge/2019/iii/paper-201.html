An Rd-valued process (Xt​)t≥0​ is a Brownian motion started at x when:
  • X0​=x almost surely;
  • its sample paths are almost surely continuous;
  • increments over disjoint time intervals are independent; and
  • for 0≤s<t, Xt​−Xs​ has the centered multivariate normal distribution with covariance matrix (t−s)Id​.
