Choose homogeneous algebra generators x1​,…,xr​ of positive degrees d1​,…,dr​ for S; they exist because S is Noetherian. For a finitely generated graded module V=⨁j​Vj​, its Poincare series of a graded module is
PV​(t)=∑j​(dimk​Vj​)tj.
(39)
The Hilbert-Serre theorem states that
PV​(t)=∏i=1r​(1−tdi​)Q(t)​​
(40)
for a Laurent polynomial Q(t)∈Z[t,t−1].
We prove this by induction on r. For r=0, S=k and V is finite-dimensional, so its series is a Laurent polynomial. For x=xr​ of degree d=dr​, multiplication gives the exact sequence of graded modules
0⟶(0:V​x)(−d)⟶V(−d)x​V⟶V/xV⟶0.
(41)
Additivity of the Hilbert series yields
(1−td)PV​(t)=PV/xV​(t)−tdP(0:V​x)​(t).
(42)
Both modules on the right are finitely generated over k[x1​,…,xr−1​], so the induction hypothesis proves the formula.
For the finitely generated commutative algebra R, let Rj​ be the stated degree filtration. Its associated graded ring
grR=⨁j≥0​Rj​/Rj−1​
(43)
is a standard graded algebra generated by the initial forms of x1​,…,xn​. Since
dimk​Rj​=∑i=0j​dimk​(Ri​/Ri−1​),
(44)
Hilbert–Serre shows that the quantity dimk​Rj​ agrees with a polynomial for all sufficiently large j. The growth of a finitely generated commutative algebra has polynomial degree dimR, independently of the chosen finite generating set.
For
R=k[Y1​,Y1−1​,Y2​,Y2−1​]

dimk​Rj​=1+4r=1∑j​r=2j2+2j+1.​
