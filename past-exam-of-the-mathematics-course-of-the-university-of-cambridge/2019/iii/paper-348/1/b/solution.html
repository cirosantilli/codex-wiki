Given any admissible transport map T, form its graph transport plan
πT​=(Id,T)#​μ.
(5)
For Borel sets A⊆X and B⊆Y, the definition of a pushforward measure gives
πT​(A×Y)=μ(A),πT​(X×B)=μ(T−1(B))=ν(B).
(6)
Hence πT​∈Π(μ,ν). Integration against a pushforward measure also gives
K(πT​)=∫X​c(x,T(x))dμ(x)=M(T).
(7)
The Kantorovich optimal transport problem therefore has at least all the competitors of the Monge optimal transport problem, with exactly the same costs. Consequently
T#​μ=νinf​M(T) ≥ π∈Π(μ,ν)inf​K(π).​
(8)
If there is no admissible transport map, the left side is +∞ by the convention inf∅=+∞, so the conclusion still holds. No existence of an optimizer is needed.
