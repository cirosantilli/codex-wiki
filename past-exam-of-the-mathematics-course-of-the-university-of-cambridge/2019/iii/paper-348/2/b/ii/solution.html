Use the intended strong convexity bound γ⊤D2φ(x)γ≥λ∣γ∣2. The original PDF omits ∣γ∣2 on the right. The printed bound for every γ∈Rd is impossible when λ>0, as γ=0 shows. Equivalently, the intended bound is stated for unit vectors. The norm of D2ζ is the operator norm induced by the Euclidean norm, or any matrix norm that bounds it.
Set
φε​=φ+εζ,Tε​=∇φε​.​
(19)
This function is C2, and its Hessian matrix satisfies
γ⊤D2φε​(x)γ​=γ⊤D2φ(x)γ+εγ⊤D2ζ(x)γ≥(λ−ε∥D2ζ(x)∥op​)∣γ∣2≥0.​
(20)
Part (i) therefore proves that φε​ is a convex function. The perturbation can use up the entire positive lower bound defining a strongly convex function; only convexity, rather than strict positivity of the Hessian matrix, is asserted.
