Let Φ=φε​ and let Φ∗ denote its convex conjugate. Its Fenchel–Young gap
H(x,y)=Φ(x)+Φ∗(y)−x⋅y
(41)
is nonnegative by the Fenchel–Young inequality, and the hypothesis says ∫Hdπε​≤ε.
First justify the integrability needed for the certificate. Finite second moments and the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality imply, for every transport plan π,
∫∣x⋅y∣dπ≤(∫∣x∣2dμ)1/2(∫∣y∣2dν)1/2<∞.
(42)
Since Φ∈L1(μ) and H∈L1(πε​), the identity Φ∗(y)=H(x,y)−Φ(x)+x⋅y proves Φ∗∈L1(ν) by the marginal distribution property. In particular, no subtraction of infinite integrals is being used.
Set
u(x)=21​∣x∣2−Φ(x),v(y)=21​∣y∣2−Φ∗(y),D=∫udμ+∫vdν.
(43)
These are integrable Kantorovich potentials. The Fenchel–Young inequality gives u(x)+v(y)≤21​∣x−y∣2. More precisely, for every transport plan π,
K(π)=D+∫Hdπ≥D,K(πε​)=D+∫Hdπε​≤D+ε.
(44)
Taking the infimum in the first inequality therefore proves
K(πε​)≤π∈Π(μ,ν)inf​K(π)+ε.​
(45)
The factor 1/2 in the quadratic cost is essential for this exact gap identity.
