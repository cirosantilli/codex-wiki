To form an additive composite expansion, subtract the common overlap from the sum of the inner and outer expressions. Their retained common part is
foverlap​=r1​+ϵ(r1​−1)+ϵ2[2r​+logr+D].

Thus one composite is ϵF0​(ϵr)+ϵ2F1​(ϵr)−ϵ2(D+1/2)/r. The last term can be screened by multiplying it by e−ϵr without changing either retained expansion. This gives a useful exponentially decaying version:
