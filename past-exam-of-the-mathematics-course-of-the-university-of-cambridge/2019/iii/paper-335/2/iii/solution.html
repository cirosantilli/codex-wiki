If the full stated potential V=k02​(2μW+μ2W2) is retained, it is not Gaussian and has mean mV​=μ2k02​. To retain every term through order μ2 in the intensity of this first Rytov field, the cumulant expansion instead gives

Here CVcov​=E[(V−mV​)(V′−mV​)]; the raw autocorrelation differs by mV2​=O(μ4). The stated remainder uses Gaussian finite-dimensional statistics of W, for which odd total moments vanish. This mean term is absent if one first truncates the potential to 2μk02​W. Both formulas concern the first Rytov approximation, and exponentiating it does not supply the omitted higher-order scattering corrections.
