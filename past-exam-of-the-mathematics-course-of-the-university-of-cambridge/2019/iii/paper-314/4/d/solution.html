The transonic accretion in a dipolar flux tube values are
rs​=6c02​GM⋆​(7−5γ)​,cs​=c0​7−5γ2​​,ρs​=ρ0​(7−5γ2​)1/(γ−1).​
(44)
The mass density follows from c2/c02​=(ρ/ρ0​)γ−1 for the same polytropic equation of state. If A⋆​ is the total loaded area at the surface, mass conservation at the sonic point gives
M˙=A⋆​(R⋆​rs​​)3ρs​cs​.​
(45)
For two equal polar caps with surface angular radius θ⋆​, A⋆​≃2πR⋆2​θ⋆2​, so the total mass accretion rate is
M˙=27R⋆​c05​2πθ⋆2​ρ0​(GM⋆​)3​(7−5γ2​)1/(γ−1)−5/2.​
(46)
For only one loaded polar cap, divide this expression by two. Expressing the result first in A⋆​ also covers a bundle whose loading angle is defined at another radius. The smooth isothermal limit γ→1 has rs​=GM⋆​/(3c02​), cs​=c0​, and ρs​=e5/2ρ0​.
