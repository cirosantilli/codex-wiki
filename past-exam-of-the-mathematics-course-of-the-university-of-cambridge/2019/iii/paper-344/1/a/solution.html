The zero-average constraint excludes the uniform mode but allows ϕ=Acos(q0​z). At a=ac​ the modes on the nonzero-wavevector soft-mode sphere become soft; below it they have negative quadratic energy, so the homogeneous state loses stability. A modulation along any chosen direction is the simplest smectic phase pattern. The quadratic instability selects its wavelength; with B=0, a nonlinear stabilizing interaction would still be needed to determine its amplitude.
