For Phi-six theory, let D0​=DF​(0) and Dxy​=DF​(x−y). The Dyson series gives
Z[0]=⟨0∣T{S}∣0⟩=1−6!iλ​∫d4x⟨ϕ(x)6⟩0​+2(6!)2(−iλ)2​∫d4xd4y⟨ϕ(x)6ϕ(y)6⟩0​+O(λ3).
(30)
At one vertex, Wick theorem supplies 5!!=15 pairings. At two vertices let r be the number of propagators joining them. It must be 0,2,4, or 6, and the number of contractions is
Nr​=(r6​)2r!((5−r)!!)2,(N0​,N2​,N4​,N6​)=(225,4050,5400,720).
(31)
Therefore
Z[0]=​1−48iλ​∫d4xD03​+(−iλ)2∫d4xd4y[4608D06​​+256D04​Dxy2​​+192D02​Dxy4​​+1440Dxy6​​]+O(λ3).​​
(32)
The four Vacuum Feynman diagram types are shown below. The r=0 term is two disconnected copies of the order-λ three-tadpole graph; the other three are connected.
Define
B1​=−48iλ​∫d4xD03​
(33)
