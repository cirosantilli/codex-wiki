For axial gauge, Ga[A]=nμAμa​. Its Faddeev-Popov operator is
δαbδGa[Aα]​=g1​nμDμab​=g1​n⋅∂δab+nμfacbAμc​.
(51)
On the gauge slice n⋅Ac=0, the second term vanishes. Hence
ΔFP​[A]​n⋅A=0​=det(g1​n⋅∂),​
(52)
This functional determinant is independent of the gauge field and may be absorbed into the normalization of the path integral. Any introduced ghosts are free and decouple, so no ghost fields are needed in axial gauge.
