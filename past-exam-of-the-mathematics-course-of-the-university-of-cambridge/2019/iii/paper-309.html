At each point, apply the indefinite-signature version of the Gram-Schmidt process to any local coframe, choosing one timelike and three spacelike one-forms. This produces an orthonormal coframe in spacetime {θa} satisfying
g=ηab​θa⊗θb,ηab​=diag(−1,1,1,1).
(15)
It is unique only up to a local Lorentz transformation. The torsion-free connection 1-forms are then determined by Cartan's first structure equation and metric compatibility,
dθa+ωab​∧θb=0,ωab​=−ωba​.
(16)
Cartan's second structure equation gives the curvature 2-forms
Rab​=dωab​+ωac​∧ωcb​=21​Rabcd​θc∧θd,
(17)
and contraction gives the Ricci tensor Rbd​=Rabad​.
For the displayed metric, take
θ0=dt,θi=e−tdxi(i=1,2,3).
(18)
Then dθ0=0 and dθi=−θ0∧θi. The nonzero connection forms are therefore
ωi0​=−θi,ω0i​=−θi​,
(19)
with ωij​=0. A second application of the structure equations gives
Ri0​=θ0∧θi,R0i​=θ0∧θi,Rij​=θi∧θj.
(20)
Equivalently,
Rab​=θa∧θb​​,
(21)
so this is a constant sectional curvature spacetime with K=1. In four dimensions,
Rab​=3gab​,R=12,Gab​=Rab​−21​Rgab​=−3gab​.
(22)
The Vacuum Einstein equations with a cosmological constant are Gab​+Λgab​=0, and hence
Λ=3​.
(23)
The metric is the contracting flat slicing of de Sitter spacetime with curvature radius one.

For an affine parameter λ, the geodesic Lagrangian is
L=21​[−(1+r2)t˙2+1+r2r˙2​+r2θ˙2+r2sin2θϕ˙​2].

r¨+r(1+r2)t˙2−1+r2r​r˙2−r(1+r2)(θ˙2+sin2θϕ˙​2)=0,

θ¨+r2r˙​θ˙−sinθcosθϕ˙​2=0,ϕ¨​+r2r˙​ϕ˙​+2cotθθ˙ϕ˙​=0.

The rotational Killing vector fields of the spherical symmetry conserve the angular-momentum vector. Its direction is fixed, so every nonradial orbit lies in the plane through the symmetry centre orthogonal to that vector. A spatial rotation can make this the equatorial plane. Equivalently, the initial conditions θ=π/2 and θ˙=0 solve the θ equation uniquely. This is the planarity of geodesics in spherical symmetry; a radial geodesic has zero angular momentum and may be assigned any plane.
On θ=π/2, the cyclic coordinates t and ϕ produce geodesic conserved quantities from Killing vectors,
E=(1+r2)t˙,L=r2ϕ˙​​.
(28)
For a timelike geodesic parametrized by proper time, the proper-time normalization is gab​x˙ax˙b=−1. Substitution of E and L gives the first-order radial equation
r˙2=E2−(1+r2)(1+r2L2​)​.
(29)
This is the timelike geodesic effective potential for static Anti-de Sitter spacetime with curvature radius one.
A geodesic passing through r=0 must have L=0. If it moves away from the origin, E>1, and
r˙2=(E2−1)−r2.
(30)
Taking proper time s=0 at departure gives
r(s)=E2−1​sins(0≤s≤π).
(31)
It turns around at s=π/2 and first returns to the origin at
Δs=π​.
(32)
This energy-independent refocusing is the characteristic radial timelike geodesic in anti-de Sitter spacetime result; for curvature radius a, the answer is πa.

For the torsion-free Levi-Civita connection, cyclically summing the displayed coordinate expression and using Γabc​=Γacb​ gives the algebraic first Bianchi identity
Rabcd​+Racdb​+Radbc​=0​.
(33)
Apply the Jacobi identity to three covariant-derivative commutators, or differentiate the coordinate formula in normal coordinates. The third-derivative terms and the derivatives of quadratic connection terms cancel cyclically, giving the differential second Bianchi identity
∇e​Rabcd​+∇c​Rabde​+∇d​Rabec​=0​.
(34)
Contracting the first and third curvature indices and then contracting once more yields
∇aRab​−21​∇b​R=0,
(35)
or the contracted Bianchi identity
∇aGab​=0​.
(36)
For the metric variation hab​=δgab​,
δ−g​=21​−g​h,
(37)
while the two double-divergence terms in the supplied metric variation of scalar curvature become a boundary term after integration by parts for tensor fields. The Einstein-Hilbert action therefore contributes Gab​+Λgab​. Varying the Proca action with respect to the metric gives the Proca stress-energy tensor, and the full Einstein-Proca theory equation is
Rab​−21​Rgab​+Λgab​=8π[Fac​Fb​c−41​gab​Fcd​Fcd+m2Aa​Ab​−21​m2gab​Ac​Ac]​.
(38)
Since δFab​=2∇[a​δAb]​, variation with respect to Ab​ and one integration by parts give the covariant Proca equation
∇a​Fab−m2Ab=0​.
(39)
Taking its covariant divergence gives
m2∇b​Ab=∇b​∇a​Fab=21​[∇b​,∇a​]Fab=0,
(40)
because the resulting contractions pair the symmetric Ricci tensor with the antisymmetric electromagnetic field tensor. Since m=0, the Lorenz constraint in Proca theory follows:
∇a​Aa=0​.
(41)
Moreover, F=dA and d2=0, so the Electromagnetic Bianchi identity is
∇a​Fbc​+∇b​Fca​+∇c​Fab​=0​.
(42)
Finally, use that identity to differentiate the matter tensor. The result groups naturally as
∇aTab​=Fb​c(∇aFac​−m2Ac​)+m2Ab​∇a​Aa.
(43)
Both terms vanish by the Proca equation and its Lorenz constraint in Proca theory. Thus ∇aTab​=0, exactly as required by the contracted Bianchi identity applied to the Einstein field equations. The vector equation is therefore consistent with the gravitational equation.
