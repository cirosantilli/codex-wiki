Let Ψ3​=A3ψ3​ and Θ3​=A3θ3​ denote the full third-order coefficients. This notation keeps the detuning term, proportional to A, and the slow-time term, proportional to AT​, visible. Define
B=J(ψ1​,θ2​)+J(ψ2​,θ1​).
(44)
The equations at order ϵ3 are
∇2Ψ3​+Rc​Θ3x​=−Aθ1x​,∇2Θ3​−Ψ3x​=AT​θ1​+A3B.
(45)
Equivalently, where A=0, the question's coefficients satisfy ∇2ψ3​+Rc​θ3x​=−A−2θ1x​ and ∇2θ3​−ψ3x​=(AT​/A3)θ1​+B. Eliminate Θ3​ from the full coefficients:
(∇4+Rc​∂x2​)Ψ3​=−A∂x​∇2θ1​−Rc​AT​θ1x​−Rc​A3∂x​B.
(46)
Use the Fredholm solvability condition for a self-adjoint operator. With ⟨f,g⟩=∫01​∫02​fgdxdz, projection onto the critical eigenfunction ψ1​ makes the left side vanish. Therefore the Landau amplitude equation is
AT​=αA−βA3,α=−Rc​⟨ψ1​,θ1x​⟩⟨ψ1​,∂x​∇2θ1​⟩​,β=⟨ψ1​,θ1x​⟩⟨ψ1​,∂x​{J(ψ1​,θ2​)+J(ψ2​,θ1​)}⟩​.​
(47)
These integrals already answer the requested coefficient calculation. Evaluating them gives ⟨ψ1​,θ1x​⟩=1/4, ⟨ψ1​,∂x​∇2θ1​⟩=−π2/2, and ⟨ψ1​,∂x​B⟩=π2/32. Hence, for the prescribed normalization,
AT​=21​A−8π2​A3.​
(48)
The cubic term opposes growth, so the nonzero steady amplitudes A=±2/π are stable equilibria within the chosen roll phase. The conductive state A=0 is unstable for this positive detuning. This is supercritical saturation of Darcy convection rolls.
