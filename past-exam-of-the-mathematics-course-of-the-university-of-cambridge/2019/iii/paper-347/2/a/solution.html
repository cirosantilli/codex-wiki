For γ=5/3, n=3/2 and the Newtonian critical radius tends to zero while cc​ diverges. The limiting critical solution approaches Mach number one only as r→0; it has no finite-radius Newtonian sonic transition. This is a failure of the Newtonian inner approximation, not an obstruction to accretion onto a black hole. A physical Schwarzschild black hole has a finite event horizon, and the regular relativistic inflow becomes supersonic outside it.
The singular-looking factor in the rate has a finite limit. Writing ϵ=n−3/2, its logarithm is ϵlog[n/ϵ]→0, so the critical Bondi accretion rate for gamma equals five thirds is
