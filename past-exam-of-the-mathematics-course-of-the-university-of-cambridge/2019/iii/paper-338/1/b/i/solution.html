The echellogram contains nearly parallel traces, one for each diffraction order. Choose the main echelle grating dispersion to increase wavelength toward the right and the cross-disperser to increase wavelength upward. At a fixed horizontal coordinate, order m+1 has a shorter wavelength than order m, so it lies below order m in this convention.
