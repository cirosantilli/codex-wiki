Let xm​=mh be a uniform periodic mesh, with indices interpreted modulo the number of nodes. Write the finite element approximation as uh​(x,t)=∑j​Uj​(t)ϕj​(x) using the chapeau functions. The Galerkin method requires
∫01​∂t​uh​ϕm​dx=∫01​∂x​uh​ϕm​dx.
(18)
The mass matrix has Mmm​=2h/3 and Mm,m±1​=h/6. The spatial matrix Cmj​=∫ϕm​ϕj′​ has Cm,m+1​=1/2 and Cm,m−1​=−1/2, with zero diagonal. Therefore the semidiscrete equations are
6h​(U˙m−1​+4U˙m​+U˙m+1​)=21​(Um+1​−Um−1​),​
(19)
or equivalently MU˙=CU. The coefficients come from integrating the products of the two overlapping piecewise-linear hat functions on each interval; periodicity supplies the wraparound entries.
