At periapsis, the particle's mean longitude equals its longitude of periapsis: λ=ϖ. Hence the pericentre geometry of an exterior resonance gives
ϕ=p(ϖ−λp​),Δθperi​=pϕ+2πj​,j∈Z.​

Thus a restricted libration phase can prevent close encounters even when the radial ranges of the two orbits overlap: this is resonance protection.

For equal logarithmic width dlogD, the number of bodies is n(D)DdlogD. Their mass is therefore
dM=6πρK​D4−αdlogD.
(27)
The mass removed by catastrophic planetesimal collisions per logarithmic bin per unit time scales as
M˙loss​(D)∝D4−αRcc​(D).
(28)
Assume a steady collisional cascade with supply only at the large end, loss only at the small end, negligible reassembly or cratering contributions, and self-similar fragmentation that transfers mass through comparable logarithmic intervals. Mass conservation requires the downward mass flux across every intermediate size boundary to be the same; otherwise mass would accumulate or deplete there. Under the scale-invariant bin-transfer assumption, this is proportional by a size-independent factor to M˙loss​, so
D4−αRcc​(D)=constant.​
(29)
This is constant mass flux in a collisional cascade. Steady state alone fixes the net flux, while the identification with the destruction rate of each bin additionally uses the stated fragmentation assumptions.

Put m=m1​+m2​, μ=Gm, n=μ/a3​, v=na, and θ=nt. Work instantaneously in the inertial frame in which the centre of mass is at rest. The unperturbed circular relative orbit has
r=a(cosθ,sinθ),v=v(−sinθ,cosθ).

The individual velocities are v1​=−(m2​/m)v and v2​=(m1​/m)v. Linear gas drag gives Fi​/mi​=−(vi​−vg​y^​)/ti​, where ti​=tsi​ are the aerodynamic stopping times. Define

(36)
Then the gas drag on a planetesimal binary is
fx​=κvsinθ,fy​=−κvcosθ+Δvg​.​
(37)
Equivalently, f=−κv+Δvg​y^​. If the centre of mass moves, vg​y^​ here is its relative gas velocity, held approximately fixed during one short binary orbit.

For weak gas drag, evaluate the work on the unperturbed circular orbit and average over one orbital period. The gas velocity changes negligibly during that period and ⟨cosθ⟩=0. Hence gas-driven contraction of a planetesimal binary obeys
