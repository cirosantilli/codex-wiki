Apply the multidimensional Itô formula to ξt​=F(t,St​,vt​). The stated PDE cancels its drift to rFdt, leaving
dξt​=​rξt​dt+vt​​(St​FS​+cρFv​)dWt​+cvt​​1−ρ2​Fv​dZt​.​
(56)
Consequently e−rtSt​ and e−rtξt​ are local martingales under the physical measure P. Thus P itself is an equivalent local martingale measure for the augmented market relative to the bank account. The continuous-time fundamental theorem of asset pricing says that existence of such a measure for locally bounded prices implies no free lunch with vanishing risk, and hence no arbitrage. The terminal condition also gives ξT​=ST​​ as required.
