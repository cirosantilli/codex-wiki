QL​=(uL​,dL​):(2,3),uR​:(1,3),dR​:(1,3),

with no right-handed neutrino. For SU(3)C​, three generations give nL​=6 from the two left-handed quark flavors and nR​=6 from the two right-handed quark flavors, while ns​=0. Therefore
β3​=311​(3)−31​(6+6)=7.​
(29)

β2​=311​(2)−31​(12)−61​=619​.​
