Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2019 / iii / Paper 121 / 3 / ii / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2019 iii Paper 121 3 ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 130
Let λ≥κ be a cardinal number of M. If forcing collapsed λ, some condition would force a surjective function f˙​:μ→λ for an ordinal μ<λ. Replacing μ by its ground-model cardinality lets us assume that μ is a cardinal.
For each ξ<μ, choose in M a maximal antichain in a forcing order deciding f˙​(ξ). The chain condition for forcing makes its size less than κ, so the set Bξ​ of possible values of f˙​(ξ) has size below κ. This is the possible-values lemma for chain-condition forcing. Every interpreted range is contained in B=⋃ξ<μ​Bξ​. If μ<κ, the fact that κ is a regular cardinal gives ∣B∣<κ≤λ; if κ≤μ<λ, infinite cardinal arithmetic gives ∣B∣≤μ<λ. Either way B cannot contain the range of a surjection onto λ, a contradiction. By cardinal preservation by chain-condition forcing, P preserves every cardinal at least κ.
