At each point, apply the indefinite-signature version of the Gram-Schmidt process to any local coframe, choosing one timelike and three spacelike one-forms. This produces an orthonormal coframe in spacetime {θa} satisfying
g=ηab​θa⊗θb,ηab​=diag(−1,1,1,1).
(15)
It is unique only up to a local Lorentz transformation. The torsion-free connection 1-forms are then determined by Cartan's first structure equation and metric compatibility,
dθa+ωab​∧θb=0,ωab​=−ωba​.
(16)
Cartan's second structure equation gives the curvature 2-forms
Rab​=dωab​+ωac​∧ωcb​=21​Rabcd​θc∧θd,
(17)
and contraction gives the Ricci tensor Rbd​=Rabad​.
For the displayed metric, take
θ0=dt,θi=e−tdxi(i=1,2,3).
(18)
Then dθ0=0 and dθi=−θ0∧θi. The nonzero connection forms are therefore
ωi0​=−θi,ω0i​=−θi​,
(19)
with ωij​=0. A second application of the structure equations gives
Ri0​=θ0∧θi,R0i​=θ0∧θi,Rij​=θi∧θj.
(20)
Equivalently,
Rab​=θa∧θb​​,
(21)
so this is a constant sectional curvature spacetime with K=1. In four dimensions,
Rab​=3gab​,R=12,Gab​=Rab​−21​Rgab​=−3gab​.
(22)
The Vacuum Einstein equations with a cosmological constant are Gab​+Λgab​=0, and hence
Λ=3​.
(23)
The metric is the contracting flat slicing of de Sitter spacetime with curvature radius one.
