Use the one-loop matter power spectrum convention in which P13​ is one ordering of ⟨δ(1)δ(3)⟩. Then
P(k,t)=D2Plin​(k)+P22​(k,t)+2P13​(k,t)+⋯,
(11)
with the P22 contribution to the one-loop matter power spectrum
P22​=2D4∫(2π)3d3q​F2,s​(q,k−q)2Plin​(q)Plin​(∣k−q∣)​
(12)
and the P13 contribution to the one-loop matter power spectrum
P13​=3D4Plin​(k)∫(2π)3d3q​F3,s​(k,q,−q)Plin​(q)​.
(13)
The factors two and three count Wick contractions. The mixed P12​ contains an odd number of zero-mean Gaussian fields, so P12​=0.
For q≫k, the ultraviolet softness of the second-order density kernel gives F2,s​=O(k2/q2), so
P22UV​=O(D4k4∫(2π)3d3q​q4Plin​(q)2​).
(14)
The third-order kernel has F3,s​(k,q,−q)=O(k2/q2), giving
2P13UV​=O(D4k2Plin​(k)∫(2π)3d3q​q2Plin​(q)​).
(15)
These integrals expose the sensitivity to unresolved short-scale dynamics. The effective sound-speed counterterm in large-scale structure has exactly the required deterministic shape:
Pct​(k,t)=−2ceff2​(t)k2PL​(k,t)​,PL​=D2Plin​.
(16)
Here ceff2​ absorbs the time and normalization conventions and has dimensions of length squared. A stochastic correction begins at k4 and can absorb the corresponding short-scale sensitivity of P22​.
