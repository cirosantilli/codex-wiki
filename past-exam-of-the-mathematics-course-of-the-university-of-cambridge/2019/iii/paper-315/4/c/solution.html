Let Fabs​=σSB​Teq4​ be the globally averaged absorbed stellar flux. The single-layer greenhouse model has an atmosphere transparent to incoming visible light and with infrared absorptivity and emissivity α. Treat the surface as a blackbody and neglect internal heating. The layer emits ασSB​Ta4​ both upward and downward.
Atmospheric radiative balance gives
ασSB​Ts4​=2ασSB​Ta4​,Ta4​=Ts4​/2(α>0).
(25)
Surface radiative balance gives
σSB​Ts4​=σSB​Teq4​+ασSB​Ta4​.
(26)
Eliminating Ta​ yields
Ts​=Teq​(1−2α​)−1/4.​
(27)
The transparent limit has Ts​=Teq​, and a fully infrared-opaque layer has Ts​=21/4Teq​. The model omits convection, wavelength-dependent opacity, and atmospheric absorption of starlight, so it captures the simplest greenhouse energy balance rather than a full surface-temperature prediction.
