For a compact space X, K0(X) is the Grothendieck group of isomorphism classes of finite-rank complex vector bundles under direct sum. Tensor product descends to this group and makes it a ring, with [CX​] as its unit.
The hypothesis E0​⊕CX​≅E1​⊕CX​ says that E0​ and E1​ define the same stable class. Rank-d bundles are classified by maps to BU(d), and the stabilization BU(d)→BU is 2d-connected. Since the finite CW complex X has dimension k≤2d, stabilization is injective on [X,BU(d)]. Thus stable cancellation for complex vector bundles gives
E0​≅E1​.​
(1)
Now let E0​,E1​ have rank d over Complex projective space CPd and have the same Chern classes. Their Chern characters agree because each component of ch(E) is a universal rational polynomial in the Chern classes. The ring
K0(CPd)≅Z[t]/(td+1)
(2)
is torsion-free, while the Chern character becomes an isomorphism after tensoring with Q; it is therefore injective. Hence [E0​]=[E1​] in K-theory, so after adding trivial bundles they are isomorphic. Since CPd has real dimension 2d, cancellation applies once more:
c(E0​)=c(E1​)⟹E0​≅E1​.​
(3)

For a complex vector bundle E→X, the K-theory Euler class is the zero-section pullback of its K-theory Thom class:
eK(E)=λ−1​(E)=∑j=0rankE​(−1)j[ΛjE].
(10)
The cofibration of the disk and sphere bundles gives the K-theory Gysin sequence of a sphere bundle
⋯→Ki(X)⋅eK(E)​Ki(X)⟶Ki(S(E))⟶Ki+1(X)→⋯.
(11)
Let γ=γC1,n+1​ be the tautological bundle. The Euler sequence on complex projective space gives the bundle isomorphism
TCPn⊕C≅(n+1)γ​.
(12)
Put t=1−[γ], so K0(CPn)=Z[t]/(tn+1). From
λs​(TCPn)=1+s(1+sγ)n+1​
(13)

For a complex vector bundle E, define the kth Adams operation by the kth Newton polynomial in its exterior powers:
ψk(E)=Nk​(Λ1E,…,ΛkE).
(17)
After applying the splitting principle for complex vector bundles and writing E=L1​⊕⋯⊕Lr​, the Newton identities give
ψk(E)=L1k​+⋯+Lrk​.
(18)
This formula proves additivity. Applied simultaneously to splittings of E and F, it also gives
ψk(E⊗F)=∑i,j​(Li​Mj​)k=ψk(E)ψk(F).
(19)
Thus ψk extends to a natural ring endomorphism of K0(X) and, for a line bundle,
ψk(L)=Lk.​
(20)
Write X=CPkk+2​ and let x=[γ​]−1. Its reduced K-theory is freely generated by xk,xk+1,xk+2, with xk+3=0, and restriction to the bottom cell sends xk to a Bott element u∈K0(S2k) and the two higher powers to zero. If r:X→S2k retracts the bottom-cell inclusion, then
r∗(u)=xk+axk+1+bxk+2
(21)
for some integers a,b. Naturality and the Adams operation on a Bott class give
ψ2(r∗u)=r∗(ψ2u)=2kr∗u.
(22)
Since ψ2(x)=2x+x2, comparison modulo xk+3 first in degree k+1 and then in degree k+2 gives
a=−2k​,b=24k(3k+5)​.
(23)
The first equality makes k even. Then 3k+5 is odd, so integrality of b forces 8∣k; it is also congruent to 2 modulo 3, so 3∣k. Consequently
24∣k.​
