Let (xn​) be a Cauchy sequence in a compact metric space X. By sequential compactness of a compact metric space, it has a convergent subsequence xnj​​→x∈X. Given ε>0, choose N such that d(xm​,xn​)<ε/2 whenever m,n≥N, and then choose j such that nj​≥N and d(xnj​​,x)<ε/2. For every n≥N, the triangle inequality gives
d(xn​,x)≤d(xn​,xnj​​)+d(xnj​​,x)<ε.
(52)
Thus the whole sequence converges to x. Every Cauchy sequence converges in X, so X is a complete metric space.
