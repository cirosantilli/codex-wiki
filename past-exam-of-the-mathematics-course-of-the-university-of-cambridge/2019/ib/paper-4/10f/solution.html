For u∈U, define ϕ(u)∈U∗ by ϕ(u)(u′)=⟨u,u′⟩. If ϕ(u)=0, then in particular

so u=0. Thus ϕ is injective. Since a finite-dimensional vector space and its dual space have the same dimension, ϕ is also surjective and hence an isomorphism. This is the finite-dimensional real case of the Riesz representation theorem.
