(73)
Let Gret​(x,t;y) solve

The spatial Fourier transform turns this into the preceding oscillator equation with α=ck, so

Inverting with the supplied radial integral gives the three-dimensional retarded Green function
Gret​(x,t;y)=4πc∣x−y∣H(t)​δ(∣x−y∣−ct)=4πc2∣x−y∣δ(t−∣x−y∣/c)​​.

For zero initial displacement and initial velocity f, convolution with this kernel gives the Kirchhoff formula
u(x,t)​=∫R3​Gret​(x,t;y)f(y)d3y=4πt​∫S2​f(x+ctω)dΩ=t⟨f⟩t​.​

Thus the value at (x,t) depends only on the initial data on the sphere of radius ct, not on its interior. This is the Huygens principle and describes propagation at the finite wave speed c.
