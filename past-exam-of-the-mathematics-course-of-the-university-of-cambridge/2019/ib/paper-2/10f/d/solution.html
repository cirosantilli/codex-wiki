For every nonnegative integer k,
(BA)k+1=B(AB)kA.
(38)
Consequently, if mAB​(AB)=0, then
(BA)mAB​(BA)=BmAB​(AB)A=0.
(39)
By the divisibility characterization of the minimal polynomial,
mBA​(t)∣t,mAB​(t).
(40)
Interchanging A and B also gives mAB​(t)∣t,mBA​(t). Therefore the Minimal polynomials of AB and BA can differ only in the exponent of the factor t, and that exponent differs by at most one.
If AB is diagonalizable, its minimal polynomial has no repeated roots. The preceding divisibility says that every nonzero Jordan block of BA has size one and every zero-eigenvalue block has size at most two. Squaring BA preserves the one-dimensional nonzero blocks and sends every size-at-most-two nilpotent block to zero. Thus the Jordan normal form of (BA)2 is diagonal, so
AB diagonalizable ⟹ (BA)2 diagonalizable​.
(41)
