 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2019 ii Paper 3 24F a
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
Homogenizing the affine equation gives the projective closure
X=V(F)⊂P2,F(X,Y,Z)=Y3Z−X4−Z4.
(203)
Its partial derivatives are
FX​=−4X3,FY​=3Y2Z,FZ​=Y3−4Z3.
(204)
If all three vanished at a point of X, then X=0 and either Y=0 or Z=0. If Y=0, the equation F=0 forces Z=0; if Z=0, the equation FZ​=0 forces Y=0. Either conclusion gives the forbidden zero triple of homogeneous coordinates. The Jacobian criterion therefore shows that X is a smooth projective curve; more specifically, it is the projective closure of y cubed equals x to the fourth plus one, a smooth plane quartic.
