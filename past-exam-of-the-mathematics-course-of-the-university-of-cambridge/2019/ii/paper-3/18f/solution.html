Let L be a splitting field of f(X)=Xm−1 over k. Its formal derivative is
f′(X)=mXm−1.
(125)
The assumption on the characteristic of k says that m=0 in k. Any root α of f is nonzero, and hence f′(α)=mαm−1=0. Thus f and f′ have no common root, so f is a separable polynomial. Since it has degree m and splits over L, it has exactly m distinct roots. This is the separability of roots of unity.
