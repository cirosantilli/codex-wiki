A continuous-time Markov chain is a reversible Markov chain in equilibrium if, when started in a stationary distribution π, the process (Xt​:0≤t≤T) has the same finite-dimensional distributions as (XT−t​:0≤t≤T) for every T>0.
For transition rates qij​, the detailed balance for a continuous-time Markov chain equations are
πi​qij​=πj​qji​(i=j).​
(247)
They imply invariance directly. For each state j,
(πQ)j​​=i=j∑​πi​qij​+πj​qjj​=πj​i=j∑​qji​−πj​k=j∑​qjk​=0,​
(248)
because qjj​=−∑k=j​qjk​. Thus πQ=0, so π is an invariant distribution of a continuous-time Markov chain.
