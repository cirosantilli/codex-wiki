Use the continuity equation to write the axial momentum equation in conservative form:
∂z∂​(rw2)+∂r∂​(ruw)=ν∂r∂​(r∂r∂w​).
(374)
Integrating from the axis to infinity, regularity at r=0 and decay of u,w,wr​ as r→∞ eliminate the boundary terms. Therefore the conserved momentum flux of a laminar round jet satisfies
dzdM​=0.​
(375)
Let W(z) be the centre-line velocity scale and δ(z) the jet width. Conservation gives M∼W2δ2, hence Wδ∼M1/2. The axial inertial and viscous terms scale as
zW2​∼δ2νW​,
(376)
so Wδ2∼νz. Combining the two relations gives
δ(z)∼M1/2νz​,W(z)∼νzM​.​
(377)
Thus the width grows linearly and the centre-line speed decays as z−1. The volume flux scales as
Q(z)=∫0∞​rwdr∼Wδ2∼νz,
(378)
so it increases downstream. By fluid entrainment, the extra flux must be ambient fluid drawn radially into the jet.
