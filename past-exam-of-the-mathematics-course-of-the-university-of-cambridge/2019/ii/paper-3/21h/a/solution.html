Write B for the open unit ball. Because T is a linear map, the hypothesis scales to
sB⊆T(sB)​(s>0).

Fix y∈B. Choose numbers r and θ such that
∥y∥<r<1,0<θ<1−r;
(166)
then r/(1−θ)<1.
Set z0​=y. Inductively, if ∥zj−1​∥<rθj−1, the scaled density statement lets us choose
xj​∈rθj−1Bsuch that∥zj−1​−Txj​∥<rθj,
(167)
and we put zj​=zj−1​−Txj​. The geometric series gives
∑j=1∞​∥xj​∥<∑j=1∞​rθj−1=1−θr​<1.
(168)
Because X is a Banach space, x=∑j≥1​xj​ exists and lies in B. Since T is a bounded linear operator, it is continuous, while
y−T∑j=1N​xj​=zN​⟶0.
(169)
Therefore Tx=y. As y∈B was arbitrary,
T(B)⊇B.​
(170)
This is the dense open-unit-ball image criterion.
