Let S be the support of the weight-d codeword x, and let πS​ delete all coordinates in S. Its kernel consists of codewords supported in S. If 0=y∈kerπS​, then wt(y)≥d but ∣S∣=d, so y is nonzero in every coordinate of S. Over F2​ this forces y=x. Hence
kerπS​=⟨x⟩
(78)
has dimension one, and the punctured code C′=πS​(C) has length n−d and rank k−1 by the rank-nullity theorem.
To bound its minimum distance, take y∈C whose puncture y′ is nonzero. If y has r nonzero coordinates in S, then y+x has d−r there, while both words have the same wt(y′) nonzero coordinates outside S. Both y and y+x are nonzero codewords, so
wt(y′)+r≥d,wt(y′)+d−r≥d.
(79)
Adding gives 2wt(y′)≥d, and therefore
d′≥⌈2d​⌉​.
(80)
