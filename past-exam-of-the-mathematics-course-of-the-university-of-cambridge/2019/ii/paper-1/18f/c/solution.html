The actions on the ordered pair (x,y) satisfy
σ4=1,τ2=1,τστ=σ−1.
(146)
Thus every element of G has the form σi or τσi for 0≤i<4, so ∣G∣≤8. Their actions are
g(g(x),g(y))​1(x,y)​σ(y,−x)​σ2(−x,−y)​σ3(−y,x)​τ(x,−y)​τσ(−y,−x)​τσ2(−x,y)​τσ3(y,x).​​
(147)
Because x,y are algebraically independent elements over Q, these eight substitutions are distinct. Hence
∣G∣=8​,
(148)
and the presentation identifies G with the dihedral group D8​.
Put
u=x2+y2,v=x2y2.
(149)
Both generators σ and τ fix u and v, so for F=Q(u,v),
F⊆KG.
(150)
The elements x2 and y2 are the two roots of
T2−uT+v,
(151)
so [F(x2,y2):F]≤2. Adjoining first x and then y takes at most two quadratic extensions, and the tower law gives
[K:F]≤2⋅2⋅2=8.
(152)
Part (b) gives 8=∣G∣≤[K:KG]. Since F⊆KG⊆K, another use of the tower law gives
8≤[K:KG]≤[K:F]≤8.
(153)
All inequalities are equalities and [KG:F]=1. Therefore
KG=Q(x2+y2,x2y2)​,
(154)
the dihedral fixed field of a two-variable rational function field.
