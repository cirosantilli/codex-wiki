Choose a K-basis e1​,…,en​ of L and write
α=∑i=1n​xi​ei​,
(241)
thereby identifying L with AKn​. Let M(x) be the n×n matrix whose jth column is the coordinate vector of αj−1 in this basis, for 1≤j≤n.
Write the multiplication table as
ei​ej​=∑l=1n​cijl​el​
(242)
with fixed structure constants cijl​∈K. Repeated multiplication shows that every entry of M(x) is a polynomial in x1​,…,xn​. Hence
D(x)=detM(x)∈K[x1​,…,xn​].
(243)
By the criterion supplied in the question,
K[α]=L⟺1,α,…,αn−1 are a basis⟺D(x)=0.
(244)
Consequently
U=D(D)=AKn​∖V(D)​,
(245)
which is a distinguished open set, and therefore a Zariski-open set. This is the determinant construction showing that primitive elements form a principal Zariski-open set. No assertion that D is nonzero is needed.
