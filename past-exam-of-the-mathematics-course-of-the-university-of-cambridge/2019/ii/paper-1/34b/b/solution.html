Keep A=(0,Bx,0) and choose the scalar potential ϕ=−Ex. Then
H=2m1​[px2​+(py​−qBx)2+pz2​]−qEx.
(338)
For pz​=0, completing the square gives
H=2mpx2​​+2mωc2​​(x−xc​)2−BE​py​−2B2mE2​,xc​=qBpy​​+qB2mE​.
(339)
Hence the spectrum on R3 is
En​(k)=ℏωc​(n+21​)−BE​ℏk−2B2mE2​.​
(340)
For a translationally invariant dispersion, the group velocity is vy​=ℏ−1∂En​/∂k. Therefore
vy​=−BE​,​
(341)
in agreement with the electric-cross-magnetic-field drift E×B/B2.
In the rectangle, k=2πr/Ly​ as before. The energy now changes with r, so the guiding-centre degeneracy is lifted, while the number of states in each tilted band remains approximately ∣qB∣A/(2πℏ). Adjacent states within a formerly degenerate level have the electric-field splitting of a Landau level in a rectangle
ΔEelectric​=Ly​2πℏ​​BE​​.​
(342)
Thus this is the ground-to-first-excited gap when the lowest Landau band contains at least two allowed guiding centres and ΔEelectric​<ℏωc​. Without that implicit weak-field or large-sample condition, the exact full spectral gap is
ΔE=min{∣B∣Ly​2πℏ∣E∣​,ℏωc​}.​
(343)
