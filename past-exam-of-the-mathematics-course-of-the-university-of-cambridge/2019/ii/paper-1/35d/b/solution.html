At fixed volume, entropy from heat capacity obeys dS=C(T)dT/T. The Third law of thermodynamics fixes S(0)=0 when the ground state is nondegenerate, so below the transition
S(Tc−​)=∫0Tc​​TαT​dT=αTc​.
(347)
Above the transition,
S(T)=S(Tc+​)+∫Tc​T​T′β​dT′=S(Tc+​)+βlogTc​T​.
(348)
For a transition with no latent heat, entropy is continuous, and comparison with S=βlogT+γ gives the directly testable relation
γ=αTc​−βlogTc​.​
(349)
Thus calorimetric measurements of C(T) and Tc​ verify γ. More generally, if a measurable latent heat L is present, S(Tc+​)−S(Tc−​)=L/Tc​, and the formula becomes
γ=αTc​+Tc​L​−βlogTc​.​
(350)
