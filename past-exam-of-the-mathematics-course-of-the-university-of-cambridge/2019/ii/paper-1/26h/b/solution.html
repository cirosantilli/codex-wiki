Let Symn​ be the vector space of real symmetric matrices and consider
Φ:Mn​(R)⟶Symn​,Φ(A)=ATA.
(250)
At R∈O(n),
DR​Φ(H)=RTH+HTR.
(251)
This derivative is surjective: for S∈Symn​, choosing H=RS/2 gives DR​Φ(H)=S. Thus I is a regular value and the orthogonal group O(n)=Φ−1(I) is a submanifold of codimension
dimSymn​=2n(n+1)​.
(252)
The determinant takes only the values ±1 on O(n), so the special orthogonal group SO(n)={R∈O(n):detR=1} is an open-and-closed submanifold of O(n). Therefore
dimSO(n)=n2−2n(n+1)​=2n(n−1)​.
(253)
The tangent space is the kernel of DR​Φ:
TR​SO(n)={H:RTH+HTR=0}={RA:A∈so(n)}​,
(254)
where the Special orthogonal Lie algebra so(n) is the vector space of skew-symmetric matrices. This proves the special orthogonal group as a submanifold description.
