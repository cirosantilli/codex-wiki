Let n=2l−1 and let H be the l×n parity-check matrix whose columns are the distinct nonzero vectors of the finite field vector space F2l​. The binary Hamming code is
C=kerH={x∈F2n​:HxT=0}.
(10)
A perfect code has Hamming balls of radius t=⌊(d−1)/2⌋ about its codewords partitioning the whole ambient space, where d is its minimum Hamming distance of a linear code.
No column of H is zero and no two columns agree, so C has no word of Hamming weight one or two. Three suitable columns sum to zero, so d=3. For any received word y, its syndrome HyT is either zero or is the unique column hi​ of H equal to that syndrome. In the first case y∈C; in the second,
H(y+ei​)T=HyT+hi​=0,
(11)
so y is at Hamming distance one from a codeword. Uniqueness follows from d=3. Thus the radius-one balls partition F2n​, and the code is perfect.
For l=3, the dual code is the binary simplex code of length seven. Every nonzero dual word evaluates a nonzero linear functional on the seven nonzero vectors of F23​, and exactly four of those evaluations are one. Hence every nonzero word of C⊥ has weight
4​.
(12)
