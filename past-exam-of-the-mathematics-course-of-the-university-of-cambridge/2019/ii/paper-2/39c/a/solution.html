Let θk​=kπh=kπ/(m+1) and define qj(k)​=sin(jθk​). The artificial boundary values are q0(k)​=qm+1(k)​=0. For the symmetric tridiagonal Toeplitz matrix H=[β,α,β], the sine addition formula gives
(Hq(k))j​=αsin(jθk​)+β[sin((j−1)θk​)+sin((j+1)θk​)]=(α+2βcosθk​)qj(k)​.
(247)
Thus all such matrices have the same discrete sine transform eigenvectors, with eigenvalues
λk​=α+2βcos(kπh),k=1,…,m.​
(248)
The sine vectors are mutually orthogonal vectors and nonzero, so the matrix Q=(sin(ijπh))i,j=1m​ is invertible. If D=diag(λ1​,…,λm​), then HQ=QD and therefore
H=QDQ−1.​
(249)
