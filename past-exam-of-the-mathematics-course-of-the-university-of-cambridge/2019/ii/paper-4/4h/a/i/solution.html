Let
L1​={wn:w∈{a,b}∗, n≥2}.
(17)
Intersect it with the regular language
R=a+ba+b.
(18)
Every word of R contains exactly two copies of b. If it is a proper power, its exponent must therefore be two and its root must contain one b. Hence
L1​∩R={ambamb:m≥1}.
(19)
This language is not regular: if p were a pumping length, pumping any nonempty substring among the first p copies of a in apbapb would change only its first a-block. Since regular languages are closed under intersection,
L1​ is not regular.​
(20)
This is the proper-power language is not regular.
