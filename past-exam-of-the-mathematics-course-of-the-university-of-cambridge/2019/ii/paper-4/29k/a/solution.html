In the one-period building block of the Cox--Ross--Rubinstein model, the money-market account grows by a deterministic factor R>0, while the stock grows independently at each time by
Yt​={U,D,​up,down,​0<D<U.
(315)
Thus St0​=RtS00​ and St1​=S01​∏j=1t​Yj​. Equivalently, the discounted stock has multipliers u=U/R and d=D/R.
The model has no arbitrage precisely when the risk-free return lies strictly between the stock returns:
D<R<U,equivalentlyd<1<u.​
(316)
Indeed, failure of either strict inequality gives a one-period arbitrage, while under the strict inequalities the probability
q=Q(Yt​=U)=U−DR−D​=u−d1−d​​
(317)
lies in (0,1). Taking the moves independent under Q with up probability q makes the discounted stock a martingale, since qu+(1−q)d=1. This is the unique Risk-neutral probability in the Cox--Ross--Rubinstein model, hence the unique equivalent martingale measure.
