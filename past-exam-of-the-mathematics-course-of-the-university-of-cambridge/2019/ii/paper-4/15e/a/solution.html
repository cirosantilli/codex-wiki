A Lagrange top is a rigid body that is symmetric about a principal axis, has a point on that axis fixed in space, and has its center of mass on the same axis while gravity acts uniformly. Here I1​=I2​ is the transverse principal moment of inertia about the fixed point, I3​ is the moment about the symmetry axis, M is the total mass, and l is the distance from the fixed point to the center of mass.
The Euler angles for a symmetric top use θ for inclination, ϕ for precession, and ψ for spin about the body axis. Since ψ is a cyclic coordinate, its generalized momentum
