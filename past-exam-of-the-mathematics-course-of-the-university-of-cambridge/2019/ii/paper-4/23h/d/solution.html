Put H=H01​(Ω) with its usual Hilbert space inner product, and define
B(u,v)=∫Ω​(∇u⋅∇v+uv+uA⋅∇v).
(247)
The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality shows that this is a bounded bilinear form. For u∈H, Young inequality gives
​∫Ω​uA⋅∇u​≤∣A∣2​∥u∥2​∥∇u∥2​≤2∣A∣2​​(∥u∥22​+∥∇u∥22​),
(248)
and therefore
B(u,u)≥(1−2∣A∣2​​)∥u∥H12​.
(249)
Because ∣A∣2​<2, this is a coercive bilinear form.
The map u↦∫Ω​uf is a bounded linear functional on H. By the Riesz representation theorem, it equals ⟨u,F⟩H​ for some F∈H. Applying part (b) produces one and only one v∈H such that
∫Ω​(∇u⋅∇v+uv+uA⋅∇v)=∫Ω​uffor every u∈H01​(Ω).​
(250)
This is precisely the weak formulation of the stated Dirichlet problem, obtained by integration by parts in the Laplace operator term. Thus v is its unique weak solution; this is the constant-drift massive-Laplacian Dirichlet problem.
