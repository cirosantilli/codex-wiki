The inverse function theorem now shows that the image of ρ contains a neighbourhood of the identity in SO(3). Therefore the image is an open subgroup. The special orthogonal group SO(3) is connected, since every rotation can be deformed continuously to the identity by reducing its rotation angle. A connected topological group has no proper open subgroup, so
