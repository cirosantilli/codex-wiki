Apply the quantum Fourier transform QFTM​ to the first register and measure it, obtaining c. The supplied Fourier-transform theorem states that, with probability of order 1/loglogr, c is an integer nearest to jM/r for some j coprime to r. On that event,
​Mc​−rj​​≤2M1​≤2r21​.

The supplied continued-fraction theorem, applied through continued-fraction recovery in quantum order finding, therefore recovers the reduced fraction j/r from c/M. Because j and r are coprime, its denominator is the exact order r. The candidate is checked classically by verifying αr≡1(modN).
The quantum Fourier transform, quantum modular exponentiation, continued-fraction algorithm, and final modular check all take polynomial time in logN. Since r<N, the successful event has probability of order 1/loglogN, as required.
