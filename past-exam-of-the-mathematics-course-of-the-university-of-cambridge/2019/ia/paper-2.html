The Kolmogorov axioms for a probability measure P are nonnegativity, normalization P(Ω)=1, and countable additivity on pairwise disjoint events. For P(B)>0, conditional probability is defined by
P(A∣B)=P(B)P(A∩B)​.
(49)
Because the events A∩Bi​ are pairwise disjoint and their union is A, countable additivity gives the law of total probability
P(A)=∑i=1∞​P(A∩Bi​)=∑i=1∞​P(A∣Bi​)P(Bi​).
(50)
For any j with P(A)>0, the definition of conditional probability and this identity give Bayes' theorem:
P(Bj​∣A)=∑i​P(A∣Bi​)P(Bi​)P(A∣Bj​)P(Bj​)​​.
(51)

Let hk​ be the probability that the simple random walk started at k hits n before zero. The first-step analysis recurrence and boundary conditions are
hk​=phk+1​+qhk−1​,h0​=0,hn​=1.
