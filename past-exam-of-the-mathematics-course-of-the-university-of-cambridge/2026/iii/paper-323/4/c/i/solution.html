X=ρAB​−σAB​=X+​−X−​.

Because TrX=0 and ∥X∥1​=2ε, one has
TrX+​=TrX−​=ε.
(35)
Thus ΔAB​=X+​/ε is positive with trace one, hence is a density operator. Define
ωAB​=1+εσAB​+εΔAB​​=1+εσAB​+X+​​=1+ερAB​+X−​​.
(36)
It is a convex combination of states. The equation εΔAB′​=(1+ε)ωAB​−ρAB​ gives
ΔAB′​=X−​/ε,
(37)
which is likewise positive and has trace one.
