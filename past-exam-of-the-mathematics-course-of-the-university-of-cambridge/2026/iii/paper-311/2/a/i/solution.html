The Penrose diagram is the maximally extended Schwarzschild diagram modified by an ingoing null shell from the chosen I−. To its past the vacuum mass is M; to its future the exterior mass is M+E. Draw the shell as a 45∘ ingoing line from the chosen I−, crossing the future event horizon and ending on the future spacelike singularity. In that same exterior, label i− and i+ at the lower and upper timelike corners, i0 at the spatial corner, and I− and I+ as the past and future null boundaries.
The crucial teleological feature is that the final H+, traced backward before the shell, lies outside the old r=2M future horizon. It crosses the old past horizon H− at the bifurcation two-sphere and continues into the white-hole region, ending on the past spacelike singularity. Thus the diagram must not draw the relevant H+ as beginning at the old bifurcation sphere.
