The unitization of C0​(R) is C(R+) on the one-point compactification of R. The supplied homeomorphism identifies R+ with the circle T. Every character φ of C0​(R) extends to the unital character
φ​(f+λ1)=φ(f)+λ.
(19)
By part d, φ​ is evaluation at a point of R+. Evaluation at the point at infinity vanishes on C0​(R) and cannot restrict to the nonzero character φ. The point is therefore some x∈R, and φ=δx​. Conversely each δx​ is plainly a character, so
ΦC0​(R)​={δx​:x∈R}.​
