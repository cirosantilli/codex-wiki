The module L=Lω1​​ is the four-dimensional spin representation. Its weights are (±ε1​±ε2​)/2, and its crystal is
2ε1​+ε2​​1​2ε1​−ε2​​2​2−ε1​+ε2​​1​2−ε1​−ε2​​.
(45)
Every weight of V lies in the root lattice, so every weight of every tensor power V⊗n also lies in that lattice. But ω1​=(ε1​+ε2​)/2 represents the nonzero coset in the quotient of the weight lattice by the root lattice. Consequently no irreducible constituent of V⊗n can have highest weight ω1​, and L never occurs.
