Let (Xn​) be Cauchy in the norm
∥X∥=Esupt≥0​∣Xt​∣.
(12)
Choose a subsequence (Xnk​​) for which
∑k=1∞​∥Xnk+1​​−Xnk​​∥<∞.
(13)
Tonelli theorem implies
∑k​supt≥0​∣Xnk+1​​(t)−Xnk​​(t)∣<∞
(14)
almost surely. The subsequence therefore converges uniformly on [0,∞), outside one null event, to a continuous process X. For each t, Xt​ is the almost-sure limit of Ft​-measurable variables; completeness of the filtration makes the chosen version adapted. The same summable bound and monotone convergence theorem show that Esupt​∣Xt​∣<∞ and that Xnk​​→X in norm.
Since the original sequence is Cauchy, the usual triangle argument upgrades convergence of the subsequence to Xn​→X in norm. Thus the space of indistinguishability classes is a Banach space.

For the level-N dyadic partition, write
VN​(t)=∑k=12N​∣f(k2−N∧t)−f((k−1)2−N∧t)∣.

limN​(VN​(t2​)−VN​(t1​))≥∣f(t2​)−f(t1​)∣.

With zero interest rate, the bank account is constant. The risky asset S is a continuous local martingale by assumption, while the European contingent claim price
