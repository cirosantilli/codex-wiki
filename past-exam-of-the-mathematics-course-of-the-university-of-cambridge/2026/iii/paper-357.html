For a fixed scalar function f, the equation □g​f=0 is coordinate invariant. A coordinate label xλ, however, is not a geometrically specified scalar under an arbitrary change of chart: a nonlinear redefinition of harmonic coordinates need not remain harmonic. Thus □g​xλ=0 is a coordinate or gauge condition selecting a class of charts, rather than a tensor equation asserting the vanishing of a tensor field with index λ.
