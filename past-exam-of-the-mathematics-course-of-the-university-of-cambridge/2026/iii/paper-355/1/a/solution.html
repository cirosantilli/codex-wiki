On the upper half-filament, write r(s)=(h(s),s) with 0≤s≤L. To first order in slope, resistive-force theory supplies the uniform transverse load fx​=ζ⊥​U, and Euler--Bernoulli balance gives
Ah′′′′=ζ⊥​U.
(1)
Midpoint symmetry and clamping impose h(0)=h′(0)=0; the free-end force and moment conditions are h′′′(L)=h′′(L)=0. With H=h/L and ξ=s/L,
H′′′′(ξ)=Sp,Sp=Aζ⊥​L3U​.
(2)
Four integrations give
H(ξ)=24Sp​(ξ4−4ξ3+6ξ2)​.
(3)
In particular, H(1)=Sp/8 and H′(1)=Sp/6. The small-slope approximation first fails when the end slope is order one, giving the estimate Sp∼6; parametrically, breakdown occurs at Sperm number of order one.
