After replacing a class by a nonsingular representative, let A represent a Seifert form over a field F of characteristic different from two. Set
Q=A+AT,T=A−1AT.
(11)
A direct calculation gives TTQT=Q, so (F2g,Q,T) is an isometric structure. A metabolizer for A corresponds to a T-invariant metabolizer for Q, and stabilization gives the canonical homomorphism
ACF​⟶WF​.
(12)
Conversely, for an isometric structure with I+T invertible, define
A=Q(I+T)−1.
(13)
Then A+AT=Q and A−1AT=T. These constructions respect orthogonal sums and metabolic structures and are inverse on Witt classes, proving ACF​≅WF​.
For an irreducible symmetric Laurent polynomial δ, the primary component of an isometric structure is
Vδ​=kerδ(T)N
(14)
for large N. The primary decomposition is orthogonal, so restriction of Q and T to Vδ​ defines the projection
WF​⟶WFδ​.
(15)
Now take F=R and let δ have roots ω,ω on the unit circle, with ω=eiθ in the upper half-plane. The isomorphism WRδ​≅2Z sends a class to the even signature jump
Jω​=limε↓0​(σei(θ+ε)​−σei(θ−ε)​).
(16)
For the class of a knot, this is precisely the jump of its Levine-Tristram signature at the root ω; reversing the choice of side changes the overall sign convention.
