The alpha disk prescription is
ν=αcs​H≃αΩcs2​​​,μ=ρν,0<α≲1.
(23)
Equivalently, the turbulent shear stress is of order αP. Magnetorotational turbulence supplies a physical mechanism for correlated radial and azimuthal motions and magnetic stresses, while α parametrizes their uncertain efficiency.

The Toomre stability criterion for a razor-thin isothermal gas disk uses
Q=πGΣcs​κ​​.
(36)
Self-gravity, represented by GΣ, amplifies overdensities. Pressure, represented by cs​, suppresses short wavelengths, while epicyclic motion with frequency κ suppresses long-wavelength radial collapse. Axisymmetric disturbances are stable for Q≥1 and unstable for Q<1.
For a Keplerian disk, κ=Ω, H≃cs​/Ω, Ω2≃GM∗​/r3, and Σ∼MD​/(πr2). Therefore
Q∼GMD​HΩ2r2​∼rH​MD​M∗​​.
(37)

M∗​MD​​≳rH​​.
