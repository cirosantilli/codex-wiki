Choose a finite generating set h1​,…,hr​ for H. Under the standard classification of connected covering spaces, each hi​ is represented by a based combinatorial loop γi​ in Y. Let Z be the union of the images of these finitely many finite edge paths. Then Z is a finite connected subgraph containing y0​.
Part (b) makes the inclusion-induced map
π1​(Z,y0​)⟶π1​(Y,y0​)=H
(6)
injective. Its image contains every hi​, because every γi​ lies in Z, and therefore contains the subgroup they generate, namely all of H. The map is consequently an isomorphism.
