A one-dimensional commutative formal group law over R is a power series F(X,Y)∈R[[X,Y]] satisfying
F(X,0)=X,qquadF(X,Y)=F(Y,X),qquadF(F(X,Y),Z)=F(X,F(Y,Z)).
(1)
A homomorphism θ:F→G is a series θ(T)∈TR[[T]] such that
θ(F(X,Y))=G(θ(X),θ(Y)).
(2)
Write θ(T)=uT+O(T2). If u=θ′(0) is a unit, recursive comparison of coefficients constructs a unique compositional inverse ψ(T)∈TR[[T]] with ψ(θ(T))=T=θ(ψ(T)). Apply ψ to the homomorphism identity and substitute X=ψ(U), Y=ψ(V) to obtain
F(ψ(U),ψ(V))=ψ(G(U,V)).
(3)
Thus ψ is a homomorphism from G to F, so θ is an isomorphism.
