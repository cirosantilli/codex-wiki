Let Nδ​ count sample points in the intersection of [0,1]d with the ℓ∞ ball of radius δ around x. That intersection has volume at least δd, so Nδ​∼Bin(n,q) with q≥Mδd. The event ∥X(L)​−x∥∞​>δ implies Nδ​<L. Since Var(Nδ​)≤nq and nq≥nMδd≥L, Chebyshev inequality gives
P(Nδ​<L)≤(nq−L)2nq​≤(nMδd−L)2nMδd​.
(18)
Taking the minimum with the trivial bound one proves the result.
