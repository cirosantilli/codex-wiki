For three identical operators, conformal invariance gives
⟨O(x)O(0)O(y)⟩=∣x∣Δ∣y∣Δ∣y−x∣ΔC3​​.
(6)
When ∣x∣≪∣y∣, ∣y−x∣=∣y∣[1+O(∣x∣/∣y∣)], so
⟨O(x)O(0)O(y)⟩∼∣x∣Δ∣y∣2ΔC3​​.
(7)
Inserting the operator product expansion, the identity term has zero expectation with O(y), while the O(0) term gives
∣x∣ΔCOOOOPE​​⟨O(0)O(y)⟩=∣x∣Δ∣y∣2ΔCOOOOPE​C2​​,
(8)
which has precisely the required position dependence.
