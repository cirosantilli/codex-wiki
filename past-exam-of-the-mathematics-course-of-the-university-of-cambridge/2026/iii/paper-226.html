Cov(Yx​,Yx′​)=4d21​​{y:y∼x and y∼x′}​.

Write p(a)=P(Y0​≥a); because Y0​∼N(0,1+1/(2d)), p(a)→0 as a→∞. Every path in a graph of length n contains, by a greedy selection, at least n/Md​ vertices at mutual graph distance greater than two, where Md​=∣B2​(0)∣.
