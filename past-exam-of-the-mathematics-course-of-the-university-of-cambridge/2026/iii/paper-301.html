After using the Clifford algebra, integrations by parts and the Dirac equation, every allowed Lorentz-covariant symmetric rank-two expression with one derivative reduces, modulo terms vanishing on shell and identically conserved improvements, to an overall multiple of
ψˉ​γ(μ∂ν)ψ.
(3)
Thus the general nontrivial tensor in the stated class is
Tμν=C[ψˉ​γμ∂νψ+ψˉ​γν∂μψ−(∂νψˉ​)γμψ−(∂μψˉ​)γνψ].
(4)

iγρ∂ρ​ψ=mψ,i(∂ρ​ψˉ​)γρ=−mψˉ​,
(5)
makes the terms cancel pairwise, proving ∂μ​Tμν=0 on shell. The normalization C remains free at this stage.

Choose C=i/4. Expanding
Sλμν=21​ψˉ​[γλ,γμ]γνψ
(6)

Tμν=Tμν+4i​[∂λ​Sλμν−∂ν(ψˉ​γμψ)].

Dμρ=ημρ□−(1−α1​)∂μ∂ρ.

Let Gμν​(x,y)=⟨Ω∣TAμ​(x)Aν​(y)∣Ω⟩. Invariance of the normalized path integral under A↦A+δA gives the Schwinger-Dyson equation
Dxμρ​Gρν​(x,y)=iδνμ​δ(4)(x−y)+ejμ(x)⟨Aν​(y)⟩j​,

⟨Aμ​(x)⟩j​=−ie∫d4zΔμρ​(x−z)jρ(z).

For quantum electrodynamics, jμ=ψˉ​γμψ is an operator built from a dynamical Dirac field. Integrating over ψ,ψˉ​ generates arbitrarily high powers of e, so the Gaussian-source truncation fails.
For the connected photon two-point function, the required diagrams through e4 are: the bare photon line at e0; one fermion-loop photon vacuum polarization insertion at e2; and at e4, a photon line with two successive one-loop polarization insertions together with the two-loop one-particle-irreducible fermion loop whose loop contains one internal photon chord. The latter includes the cyclic placements conventionally interpreted as self-energy and vertex corrections. Counterterm insertions must be added in a renormalized calculation. Disconnected vacuum bubbles cancel against the vacuum normalization.

Mμν=2ημν+(p1​−k1​)2−m2(2p1​−k1​)μ(2p2​−k2​)ν​+(p1​−k2​)2−m2(2p2​−k1​)μ(2p1​−k2​)ν​.
