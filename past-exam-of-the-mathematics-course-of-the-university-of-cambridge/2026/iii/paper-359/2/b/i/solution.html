The Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem also gives strong convergence in the corresponding spaces with one fewer derivative. In particular, um​→u in L∞ and ∇ωm​→∇ω in L2, so
(um​⋅∇)ωm​⟶(u⋅∇)ωin L2.

−νΔω+γω+(u⋅∇)ω=g,u=∇⊥Ψ,−ΔΨ=ω.
