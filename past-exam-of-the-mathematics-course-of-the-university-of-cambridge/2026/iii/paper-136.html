d OurBigBook.com
The restriction to Q cannot be trivial: otherwise the infinitely many integers would be pairwise distance one inside a bounded ball, contradicting local compactness. By part (c), it induces the p-adic topology for one prime p. Since a locally compact valued field is complete, the embedding Q↪K extends to a closed embedding Qp​↪K.
Now K is a locally compact topological vector space over the nondiscrete local field Qp​. Such a vector space is finite-dimensional: a compact neighbourhood, together with a maximal linearly independent subset chosen at a fixed separation scale, is totally bounded only if that subset is finite, and its span is then open and closed; maximality makes it all of K. Thus [K:Qp​]<∞.

The residue field kK​=Fq​ has a unique degree-n extension Fqn​. Choose a monic irreducible polynomial fˉ​ defining it and lift fˉ​ to a monic f∈OK​[X]. Hensel's lemma shows that a root generates an unramified extension L/K of degree n with that residue field. Any two such extensions embed into a common algebraic closure and have the same Teichmuller lifts of Fqn​, which generate them; hence they coincide. This proves existence and uniqueness of the unramified extension.
