An idempotent morphism satisfies e2=e and splits if e=ir with ri=1. Form the relative Karoubi envelope C[Eˇ]: its objects are (A,e) for e∈E, and
C[Eˇ]((A,e),(B,f))={u:A→B:u=fue}.
(1)
The functor A↦(A,1A​) is full and faithful. Each e∈E splits through (A,e) using e in both directions. If a functor F:C→D comes with splittings F(e)=ie​re​, define its extension by (A,e)↦ the splitting object and u↦rf​F(u)ie​. This gives the required factorization, unique up to the unique compatible natural isomorphism.
A preorder R is reflexive and transitive, so R∘R=R: transitivity gives one inclusion and reflexivity the other. If it is an equivalence relation, the quotient relation q:X→X/R satisfies qopq=R and qqop=1, so it splits in category of relations Rel. Conversely, writing a split preorder as R=ir, ri=1 and using reflexivity and transitivity shows R=Rop; hence it is an equivalence relation.
Let B be the set of join-inaccessible elements of a completely algebraic lattice A, and put
i(a)={b∈B:b≤a},r(S)=⋁S.
(2)
Then ri=1A​, while r(S)≤a exactly when S⊆i(a), so r⊣i. Join-inaccessibility gives i(⋁j​aj​)=⋃j​i(aj​), so i preserves all joins. Conversely, given such r⊣i with ri=1, each r{b} is join-inaccessible: if r{b}≤⋁S, adjunction and preservation of joins put b in some i(s), whence r{b}≤s. Finally,
a=r(i(a))=⋁b∈i(a)​r{b},
(3)
so A is completely algebraic.
Under the supplied full embedding Rel→CSLat, a set goes to its power-set lattice and a preorder goes to the associated idempotent. Splitting those idempotents gives exactly the retracts of power sets by adjoint join maps characterized above. Therefore Rel[Eˇ] is equivalent to the full subcategory of complete join-semilattices consisting of completely algebraic lattices.
