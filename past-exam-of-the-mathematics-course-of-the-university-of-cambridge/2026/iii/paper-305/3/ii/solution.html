Classically the p antifundamentals admit U(p) flavour rotations and λ admits an independent phase rotation, giving SU(p)×U(1)ψ​×U(1)λ​ up to finite quotients. A U(1) with charges qλ​,qψ​ is free of the mixed SU(N)2U(1) anomaly when
(N−2)qλ​+pqψ​=0.
(25)
Using p=N−4, choose
qλ​=N−4,qψ​=−(N−2).
(26)
The orthogonal axial phase has a chiral anomaly, while this combination survives. The continuous quantum global symmetry is therefore SU(p)×U(1), again up to possible finite central quotients.
