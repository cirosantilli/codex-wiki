Words: 61
1 OurBigBook.com
A global symmetry is spontaneously broken when it preserves the action but does not preserve a chosen ground state. If G is broken to the stabilizer H, the degenerate vacua form a vacuum manifold G/H. The Goldstone theorem states that a relativistic theory has one massless scalar mode for each broken continuous internal generator, so the standard counting gives dimG−dimH Goldstone bosons.

i OurBigBook.com
The continuous symmetry is
G=U(N)L​×U(N)R​,M⟼LMR†.
(1)
Strictly, the diagonal central U(1) acts trivially, so the faithful group is the quotient by that common center. Since L and R are unitary matrices,
∂μ​M⟼L(∂μ​M)R†,M†M⟼R(M†M)R†.
(2)
Every term is therefore unchanged by cyclicity of the matrix trace:
Tr(∂μM†∂μ​M)⟼Tr[R(∂μM†∂μ​M)R†],
(3)
and the same conjugation argument applies to Tr(M†M) and Tr[(M†M)2].

Let xa​≥0 be the eigenvalues of the positive semidefinite matrix M†M. The potential is
V=∑a=1N​(kxa​+2λ​xa2​).
(4)
For k<0 and λ>0, each summand is minimized at

Every vacuum can be written as M=vU with U(x)∈U(N). Freezing the massive radial modes and substituting this parameterization into the action gives, up to a constant vacuum energy,
Seff​=v2∫d4xTr(∂μU†∂μ​U)+⋯.
(7)
Writing U=exp(iπaTa/v) displays the N2 Goldstone fields πa. The omitted terms contain more derivatives or arise from integrating out radial excitations, so this is the leading nonlinear sigma model on the vacuum manifold U(N).

For Hermitian M, the continuous transformations preserving the field space act by conjugation,
M⟼UMU†,U∈U(N),
(8)
with the central U(1) acting trivially; there is also the discrete symmetry M↦−M. The vacuum equation is M2=v21, so every vacuum is unitarily conjugate to
Mp​=vdiag(1p​,−1N−p​),p=0,…,N.
(9)
Because the integer p cannot change continuously, the vacuum manifold has disconnected components
Mp​=U(p)×U(N−p)U(N)​.
(10)
On the pth component the unbroken continuous group is U(p)×U(N−p), and the Goldstone theorem gives
N2−p2−(N−p)2=2p(N−p)
(11)
Goldstone bosons. The discrete sign symmetry exchanges the components p and N−p but produces no Goldstone mode.

With matrix-valued fields, charge conjugation acts as
C:Aμ​(t,x)↦−AμT​(t,x),Fμν​↦−FμνT​.
(13)
Under parity, A0​ is even and Ai​ is odd:
P:A0​(t,x)↦A0​(t,−x),Ai​(t,x)↦−Ai​(t,−x).
(14)
Consequently F0i​ is odd and Fij​ is even. Under time-reversal symmetry, which is antiunitary, A0​ is even and Ai​ is odd after t↦−t; hence F0i​ is even and Fij​ is odd. Equivalently, the non-Abelian electric and magnetic fields transform as
EB​C−ET−BT​P−E+B​T+E−B​​.
(15)

iii OurBigBook.com
Since δFμν​=−ig[Fμν​,α], both Fμν​Fμν and Fμν​⋆Fμν vary by a commutator. Cyclicity of the matrix trace therefore makes both terms gauge invariant. In electric and magnetic variables,
Tr(Fμν​Fμν) is proportional to Tr(B2−E2),Tr(Fμν​⋆Fμν) is proportional to Tr(E⋅B).
(16)
The Yang-Mills kinetic term is even under C, P, and T. The Yang-Mills theta term is even under C because both fields change sign and transpose, but odd under P and under T because exactly one of E,B changes sign.

Expanding Fμν​, using antisymmetry of ϵμνρσ, and cyclically rearranging the matrix trace gives
Tr(Fμν​⋆Fμν)=∂μ​Kμ,
(17)
where the Chern-Simons current is
Kμ=2ϵμνρσTr(Aν​∂ρ​Aσ​−32ig​Aν​Aρ​Aσ​).
(18)
The theta contribution to the action is therefore a boundary term. For variations that vanish at the spacetime boundary, its variation vanishes, so it contributes nothing to the classical Euler-Lagrange field equations. Its integral can still distinguish topologically nontrivial gauge configurations in the quantum theory.

A gauge anomaly makes the gauge symmetry inconsistent and must cancel. An Adler-Bell-Jackiw anomaly breaks a classical global chiral symmetry quantum mechanically but does not by itself invalidate the gauge theory. A 't Hooft anomaly is an obstruction to gauging a global symmetry; it remains invariant along renormalization-group flow and constrains the infrared spectrum or symmetry-breaking pattern.

In the normalization supplied, a left-handed Weyl fermion in the two-index antisymmetric representation has cubic SU(N) gauge-anomaly coefficient N−4, while an antifundamental has coefficient −1. Cancellation of the gauge anomaly requires
(N−4)−p=0,

p=N−4.

Classically the p antifundamentals admit U(p) flavour rotations and λ admits an independent phase rotation, giving SU(p)×U(1)ψ​×U(1)λ​ up to finite quotients. A U(1) with charges qλ​,qψ​ is free of the mixed SU(N)2U(1) anomaly when
(N−2)qλ​+pqψ​=0.
(25)
Using p=N−4, choose
qλ​=N−4,qψ​=−(N−2).
(26)
The orthogonal axial phase has a chiral anomaly, while this combination survives. The continuous quantum global symmetry is therefore SU(p)×U(1), again up to possible finite central quotients.
