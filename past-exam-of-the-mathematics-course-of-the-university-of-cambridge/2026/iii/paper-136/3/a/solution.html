For sufficiently large r, the convergent p-adic logarithm and p-adic exponential series are inverse homomorphisms
log:1+πrOK​⟷πrOK​:exp.
(9)
Their identities log(xy)=logx+logy and exp(x+y)=expxexpy follow first formally and then by convergence. Multiplication by πr identifies (OK​,+) with (πrOK​,+). Since (1+πrOK​) has finite index in OK∗​, the conclusion follows.
