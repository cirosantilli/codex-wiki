The eastern wall has no normal flow, so the quasi-geostrophic streamfunction is constant there. Choose ψ(L,y)=0. Integrating the Sverdrup relation gives
ψint​(x,y)=βρH02​f0​τ′(y)​(L−x)​.
(25)
Since geostrophic balance gives ψ=gη/f0​,
ηint​(x,y)=gβρH02​f02​τ′(y)​(L−x)​.
(26)
If this interior solution were extended to both walls, the west-minus-east height difference would be
ηint​(0,y)−ηint​(L,y)=gβρH02​f02​L​τ′(y)​.
(27)
The northward volume transport per unit meridional distance is
T=H0​∫0L​vdx=H0​∫0L​ψx​dx=f0​gH0​​[η(L,y)−η(0,y)].
(28)
Thus
T=−βρH0​f0​L​τ′(y)​.
(29)
This is the basin-integrated form of Sverdrup balance.
