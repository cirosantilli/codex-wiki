After using the Clifford algebra, integrations by parts and the Dirac equation, every allowed Lorentz-covariant symmetric rank-two expression with one derivative reduces, modulo terms vanishing on shell and identically conserved improvements, to an overall multiple of
ψˉ​γ(μ∂ν)ψ.
(3)

Tμν=C[ψˉ​γμ∂νψ+ψˉ​γν∂μψ−(∂νψˉ​)γμψ−(∂μψˉ​)γνψ].
(4)
It is manifestly symmetric. Differentiating it, commuting partial derivatives and using
iγρ∂ρ​ψ=mψ,i(∂ρ​ψˉ​)γρ=−mψˉ​,
(5)
makes the terms cancel pairwise, proving ∂μ​Tμν=0 on shell. The normalization C remains free at this stage.
