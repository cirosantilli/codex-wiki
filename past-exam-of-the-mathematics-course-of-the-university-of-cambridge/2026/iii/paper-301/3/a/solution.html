Dμρ=ημρ□−(1−α1​)∂μ∂ρ.
(18)
Its quantum field theory propagator should satisfy
Dxμρ​Δρν​(x−y)=iδνμ​δ(4)(x−y),
(19)
and inversion into transverse and longitudinal projectors gives the numerator
Πμνcorrect​=−ημν​+(1−α)k2kμ​kν​​.
(20)
The paper instead prints −ημν​+(α−1)kμ​kν​/k2. Except at α=1, that is not the inverse of the displayed Lagrangian's kinetic operator. Taken literally, for α=2 it is the Green function of
[ημρ□+2−αα−1​∂μ∂ρ]Δρν​=iδνμ​δ(4),
(21)
and at α=2 its longitudinal part is noninvertible. Thus the longitudinal sign in the printed propagator is a typographical error; the two forms coincide in Feynman gauge.
