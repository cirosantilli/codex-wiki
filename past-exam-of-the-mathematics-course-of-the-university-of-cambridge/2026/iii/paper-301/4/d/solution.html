Let p1​,p2​ be the incoming scalar and antiscalar momenta and k1​,k2​ the outgoing photon momenta, so p1​+p2​=k1​+k2​. Up to the common overall phase fixed by the S-matrix convention, the connected leading amplitude is
M=e2ϵ1μ∗​ϵ2ν∗​Mμν,
(27)
where
Mμν=2ημν+(p1​−k1​)2−m2(2p1​−k1​)μ(2p2​−k2​)ν​+(p1​−k2​)2−m2(2p2​−k1​)μ(2p1​−k2​)ν​.
(28)
On shell, the denominators are −2p1​⋅k1​=−2p2​⋅k2​ and −2p1​⋅k2​=−2p2​⋅k1​. Contracting with k1μ​ therefore gives
k1μ​Mμνepsilon2ν∗​=2k1​⋅ϵ2∗​−(2p2​−k2​)⋅ϵ2∗​−(2p1​−k2​)⋅ϵ2∗​=0,
(29)
using momentum conservation and k2​⋅ϵ2​=0. The same calculation with the photons interchanged gives k2ν​Mμνϵ1μ∗​=0. The two exchange diagrams and the seagull term are all required for this Ward identity.
