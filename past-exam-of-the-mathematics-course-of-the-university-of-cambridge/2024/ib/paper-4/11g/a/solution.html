The surface is obtained by revolving the graph z=f(x) about the x-axis. It has a central cylindrical section of radius b, two smooth transition collars, and unit spherical caps centred at (±3,0,0); its projection onto the (x,z)-plane is the region ∣z∣≤f(x).
For a surface of revolution, the Gaussian curvature of a surface of revolution is
K=−f(1+f′2)2f′′​.
(43)
Thus the cylindrical region has K=0. On each transition collar, K<0 where f′′>0, vanishes at the unique inflection circle, and is positive where f′′<0. The spherical caps have K>0.
