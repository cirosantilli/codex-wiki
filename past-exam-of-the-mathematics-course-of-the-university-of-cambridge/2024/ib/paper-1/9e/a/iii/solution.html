A Sylow 2-subgroup of A5​ has order four. Since A5​ has no element of order four, each is a Klein four-group. For every i∈{1,…,5}, take the identity and the three double transpositions fixing i. For instance, the subgroup fixing 5 is
{1,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)}.

These five point stabilizers are precisely the Sylow 2-subgroups. Indeed, the 15 double transpositions occur three to each such subgroup, so there are
