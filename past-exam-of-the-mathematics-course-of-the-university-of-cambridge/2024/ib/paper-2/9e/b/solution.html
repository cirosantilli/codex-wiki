The coefficient condition says precisely that every nonconstant monomial has positive powers of both variables. Thus
R=K+XYK[X,Y].
(75)
If c+XYf and d+XYg lie in R, so do their sum and their product
cd+XY(cg+df+XYfg),
(76)
so R is a subring of K[X,Y].
For n≥1, let
In​=(XY,XY2,…,XYn)R​.
(77)
Then In​⊆In+1​. The containment is strict because XYn+1∈/In​: multiplying a generator XYj by an element of R produces either a scalar multiple of XYj, or terms divisible by X2. It cannot produce the monomial XYn+1 when j≤n. Hence
I1​⊊I2​⊊I3​⊊⋯
(78)
is a strictly increasing ideal chain. The constant-plus-ideal non-Noetherian subring therefore satisfies
R is not Noetherian​.
