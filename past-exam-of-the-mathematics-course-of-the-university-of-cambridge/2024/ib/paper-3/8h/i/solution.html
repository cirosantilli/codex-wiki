The independent identically distributed sequence (Xn​) is itself a Markov chain, since the conditional law of Xn+1​ is its common marginal law and does not depend on the past.
Also,
Sn+1​=Sn​+Xn+1​.
(39)
Because Xn+1​ is independent of the past, the conditional law of Sn+1​ depends on the history only through Sn​. Thus the partial sums (Sn​) are a Markov chain.
