Dedekind's factorization/Frobenius theorem says that for a prime not dividing the discriminant, the degrees of the distinct irreducible factors modulo p give the cycle lengths of an element of the Galois group.
The polynomial is irreducible over Q by the rational-root test followed by a comparison of possible monic quadratic factors. Its discriminant is 5472, a square, so its transitive Galois group lies in A4​. Modulo five,
f(X)≡X(X3−X−2),
