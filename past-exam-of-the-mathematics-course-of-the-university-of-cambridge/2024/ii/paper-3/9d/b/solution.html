For an expanding universe with 0≤w≤1, the coefficient (1+3w)H is positive. Thus Ω=1 is unstable toward the future: an initial 0<Ω<1 decreases away from one toward zero, whereas an initial Ω>1 increases and eventually corresponds to recollapse. In radiation domination, w=1/3 and
Ω′=2HΩ(Ω−1),
(34)
so a phase-line sketch has arrows away from the fixed point 1 on both sides. Consequently the observed near-flatness today requires extremely fine tuning of the early value of Ω.
