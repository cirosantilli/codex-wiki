Choose a language L⊆{0,1}∗ that is not recursively enumerable; such a language exists because there are uncountably many languages but only countably many algorithms. Form L as in part (d), which satisfies the pumping lemma.
If a grammar generated L, enumerate its terminal derivations and retain precisely words of the form 2v with v∈{0,1}∗. Since such a word contains only one separator,
2v∈L⟺v∈L.

This would enumerate L, a contradiction. Hence L is the required language.
