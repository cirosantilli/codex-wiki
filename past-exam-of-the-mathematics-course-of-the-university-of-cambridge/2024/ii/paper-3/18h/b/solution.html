The primitive root ζn​ is a zero of the cyclotomic polynomial Φn​, of degree φ(n). Its minimal polynomial over K divides Φn​, so
[K(ζn​):K]∣φ(n),
(76)
where divisibility also follows from the embedding of the Galois group of the cyclotomic field into (Z/nZ)×. Over Q, Gauss's irreducibility theorem for cyclotomic polynomials says that Φn​ is irreducible. Therefore
[Q(ζn​):Q]=degΦn​=φ(n).
