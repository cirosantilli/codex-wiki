The Riemann-Roch theorem states
ℓ(D)−ℓ(K−D)=degD+1−g.
(102)
On a genus-one curve K∼0. If D has degree zero, then D+P0​ has degree one, and Riemann--Roch gives ℓ(D+P0​)=1. Its unique nonzero section has an effective divisor of degree one, say P, so
D∼P−P0​.
(103)
If also D∼Q−P0​, then P∼Q; a nonconstant function with divisor P−Q would define a degree-one map to P1, forcing the genus to be zero. Hence P=Q.
This identifies X with Pic0(X) by P↦[P−P0​] and defines P+Q=R through
[P−P0​]+[Q−P0​]=[R−P0​].

(106)
