tr(An)=Ln​,
(231)
where Ln​ is the nth Lucas number. It counts closed symbolic itineraries of length n. Since 7 is prime, the itineraries fixed by the seventh shift have least period either 1 or 7. Hence the number of cyclic classes of primitive length-seven itineraries is
7tr(A7)−tr(A)​=729−1​=4.
(232)
Distinct primitive cyclic itineraries give distinct cycles, so F has at least four distinct 7-cycles. This is the fibonacci transition-graph cycle count.
