For the Nyquist stability criterion, let L(s)=kG(s). The open loop has no right-half-plane poles, so P=0. On the clockwise right-half-plane contour, the winding number N of 1+L about zero, equivalently of L about −1, satisfies

L(iω)=1+iαωk​

If k>−1, this circle does not wind around −1, so N=0, Z=0, and the loop is stable. If k<−1, it winds once with N=−1, so Z=1, and the loop is unstable. Nyquist therefore gives precisely the same condition k>−1.
