Lorentz invariance requires Aμ and Jμ to transform as four-vectors, χ as a scalar, m and μ0​ as scalars, and the integration domain and boundary conditions to respect the transformation. Then every contracted term and d4x are Lorentz invariant.
Under Aμ​↦Aμ​+∂μ​χ, the field strength is unchanged. The source term changes by a boundary term plus
−∫χ∂μ​Jμ,d4x,
(283)
which vanishes for a conserved current and suitable boundary behaviour of χ. The mass term is not gauge invariant. Thus the action has the usual local gauge invariance when
m=0,∂μ​Jμ=0,​
(284)
with the gauge function or its boundary contribution suitably controlled. For m>0, the mass term breaks this gauge symmetry.
