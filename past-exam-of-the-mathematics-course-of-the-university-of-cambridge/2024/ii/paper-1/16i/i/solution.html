¬(px,y​∧px,y′​),¬(px,y​∧px′,y​).

The first family says that each x is paired with at most one y; the second says that each y is paired with at most one x. There is no existence clause, so the domain may be any subset of X. Thus the relations fv​ are exactly the injective partial functions from X to Y.
