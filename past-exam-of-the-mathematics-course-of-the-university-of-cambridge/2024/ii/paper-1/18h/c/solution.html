Let β satisfy βp=α. Since K=Q(α)⊆Q(β),
[Q(β):Q]=[K(β):K][K:Q].
(128)
The polynomial f(Xp) has degree p[K:Q] and has root β. It is irreducible exactly when [K(β):K]=p.
If α=γp for some γ∈K, then γ is a root of f(Xp) of degree at most [K:Q], so f(Xp) is reducible. Conversely, if it is reducible, then
d=[K(β):K]<p.
(129)
Since p is prime, 1≤d<p implies gcd(d,p)=1. Applying coprime-degree descent for powers to K(β)/K and βp=α shows that α is a pth power in K. Hence
f(Xp) is irreducible⟺α∈/Kp.
