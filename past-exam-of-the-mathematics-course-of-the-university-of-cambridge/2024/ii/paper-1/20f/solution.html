An algebraic number is an algebraic integer when it is a root of a monic polynomial in Z[X].
If α and β are algebraic integers, the ring Z[α,β] is a finitely generated Z-module. Multiplication by αβ preserves this module, so the algebraic integer module criterion shows that αβ is an algebraic integer.
