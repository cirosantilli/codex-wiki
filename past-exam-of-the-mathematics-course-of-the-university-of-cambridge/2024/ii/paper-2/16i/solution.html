The Hartogs theorem says that for every set X there is an ordinal that does not inject into X. Indeed, the well-orderings of subsets of X form a set, since each is a relation in P(X×X). By replacement, their order types form a set of ordinals. Its supremum plus one cannot be the order type of a well-ordered subset of X, and hence cannot inject into X. The least such ordinal is the Hartogs ordinal h(X).
