The Seifert-van Kampen theorem says that if X=U∪V, where U,V,U∩V are path connected open sets containing x0​, then
π1​(X,x0​)≅(π1​(U,x0​)∗π1​(V,x0​))/⟨⟨iU∗​(γ)iV∗​(γ)−1:γ∈π1​(U∩V,x0​)⟩⟩.

The cell attachment is the quotient
X∪f​Dn=(X⊔Dn)/(u∼f(u) for u∈∂Dn),
(111)
with the image of x0​=f(∗) as base point. For n=2, take one open set that deformation retracts onto X together with a boundary collar, and another that consists of the interior of the disc with a collar and is contractible. Their intersection deformation retracts onto S1. Its generator maps to [f] in the first set and to the identity in the second. Van Kampen therefore proves the fundamental group after attaching a 2-cell formula
π1​(X∪f​D2,x0​)≅π1​(X,x0​)/⟨⟨[f]⟩⟩​.

π1​(S1∨S1,∗)≅F(a,b).
