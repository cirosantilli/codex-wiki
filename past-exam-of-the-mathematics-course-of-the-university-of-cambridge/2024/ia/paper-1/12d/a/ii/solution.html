Let f be continuous on [0,1]. Compactness makes it uniformly continuous. Given ε>0, choose δ>0 such that
∣x−y∣<δ⟹∣f(x)−f(y)∣<ε.
(134)
Choose a partition with mesh smaller than δ. The oscillation Mj​−mj​ on every subinterval is then at most ε, so
U(f,P)−L(f,P)=∑j​(Mj​−mj​)(xj​−xj−1​)≤ε.
(135)
The Riemann integrability criterion proves that every continuous function on [0,1] is Riemann integrable.
