Let n=pk and take G=Cpk​. If it embedded in Sn−1​, that symmetric group would contain a permutation of order pk. The order of a permutation is the least common multiple of its cycle lengths. For this least common multiple to be divisible by pk, one cycle length must itself be divisible by pk, requiring at least pk=n points. This is impossible in Sn−1​. Thus Cn​ is the required group.
