Each completed visit to radius r2​ begins a new radial excursion. By part (a), the conditional probability that the following excursion reaches radius R before radius r1​ is
qR​=log(R/r1​)log(r2​/r1​)​.
(22)
The Strong Markov property at the successive stopping times τ2k​ makes these trials independent with the same success probability. Consequently N(R) has a geometric distribution on {1,2,…} with parameter qR​:
P(N(R)>k)=(1−qR​)k.
(23)
As R→∞, qR​→0 and
qR​N(R)d​Exp(1),qR​logR⟶log(r2​/r1​).
(24)
Slutsky theorem now gives
logRN(R)​d​Exp(log(r2​/r1​)).​
(25)
