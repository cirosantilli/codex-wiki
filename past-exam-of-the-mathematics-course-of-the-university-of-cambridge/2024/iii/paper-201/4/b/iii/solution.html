Under the Cameron-Martin theorem for a linear drift, the probability that Brownian motion with drift −μ reaches x is
P(S≥x)=e−μxE[e−μ2Tx​/2].
(30)
Using the supplied Laplace transform with λ=μ2/2 gives
E[e−μ2Tx​/2]=e−μx,
(31)
and hence
P(S≥x)=e−2μx.
(32)
This is the survival function of the exponential distribution with rate 2μ.
