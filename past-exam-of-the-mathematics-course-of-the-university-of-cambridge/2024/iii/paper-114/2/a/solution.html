Write F2​=Z/2. A double covering has a deck transformation τ that exchanges the two points in every fibre. Every singular simplex σ:Δq→Y has exactly two lifts σ and τσ. Define the mod-two transfer chain map of a double covering by
T(σ)=σ+τσ,
(5)
and let f#​ send a simplex of X to its composite with f. Uniqueness of lifted faces shows that T commutes with the boundary operator, so both maps are chain maps.
For each base simplex, its two lifts span a copy of F22​. On this summand, T is a↦(a,a) and f#​ is (a,b)↦a+b. Hence the sequence is exact in every degree:
0⟶C∗​(Y;F2​)T​C∗​(X;F2​)f#​​C∗​(Y;F2​)⟶0.​
