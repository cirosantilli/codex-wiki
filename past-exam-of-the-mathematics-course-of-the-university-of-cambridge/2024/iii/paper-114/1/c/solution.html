At a point of the open two-cell, the local link is a circle, so local homology in degree two has rank one. At a point of Z, a neighborhood consists of n half-discs sharing their boundary interval; its link is the Theta graph Θn​. The local homology from a link calculation gives
H2​(Y,Y∖{z};Z)≅H1​(Θn​;Z)≅Zn−1.
(4)
When n>2, this local rank characterizes the points of Z. Since local homology is invariant under a homeomorphism, every homeomorphism satisfies f(Z)=Z, and in particular f(Z)⊂Z.
For n=2, the two pages form an ordinary disc neighborhood, and Y is RP2. The distinguished circle Z is a projective line, but a projective linear homeomorphism can carry it to a different projective line. Thus the conclusion does not hold.
