Let Γ be a congruence subgroup. A modular form of integral weight k and level Γ is a holomorphic function f:h→C such that
f(γτ)=(cτ+d)kf(τ)
(24)
for every γ=(ac​bd​)∈Γ, and such that f is holomorphic at every cusp. In terms of the slash operator for modular forms, the first condition is f∣k​γ=f; the second says that f∣k​σ has a Fourier expansion of a modular form with no negative powers in the local parameter whenever σ∈SL2​(Z) sends infinity to a cusp.

The Poisson summation formula for the Gaussian gives the transformations of the Jacobi theta function
θ(τ+2)=θ(τ),θ(−1/τ)=(−iτ)1/2θ(τ),
(26)

(27)

The defining series converges locally uniformly, so θk is holomorphic on h, and its expansion at infinity has no negative powers of eπiτ.
