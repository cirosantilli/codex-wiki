The higher-order Euler-Lagrange equation for a functional depending on hx​ and hxx​ gives
kc​hxxxx​−σhxx​=0.
(1)
With the membrane elastic length
ξ=σkc​​​,
(2)
the general free-membrane profile is
h(x)=A+Bx+Cex/ξ+De−x/ξ.
(3)
Two integrations by parts, followed by use of the field equation, turn the energy of any free interval [a,b] into the boundary term
Em​[a,b]=21​[kc​(hxx​hx​−hxxx​h)+σhhx​]ab​.
(4)
Take the undeformed membrane to have h,hx​→0 at infinity. In the small-contact approximation, the cylindrical profile is
hc​(x)=hc​(0)−2Rx2​,hc′​(δo​)=−Rδo​​.
(5)
Continuity of height and slope at the right contact point and exponential decay then give
h(x)=Rξδo​​e−(x−δo​)/ξ,x≥δo​,
(6)
with its reflected copy on the left. The two free tails carry
Eout​=2⋅21​∫δo​∞​(kc​hxx2​+σhx2​)dx=ξR2kc​δo2​​.
(7)
Within the contact, hxx​=−1/R. Since δo​≪ξ, the tension contribution there is smaller than the bending contribution by O(δo2​/ξ2), so
Econtact​=R2kc​δo​​,Ea​=−2Uδo​.
(8)
Writing the dimensionless adhesion strength as U=R2U/kc​, the total energy is
E(δo​)=R2kc​​[ξδo2​​+(1−2U)δo​].
(9)
Its stationary point is
δo​=ξ(U−21​)​.
(10)
It is an admissible bound state only for U>1/2; otherwise the constrained minimum is the unbound state δo​=0. Substitution gives
Emin​=−R2kc​ξ​(U−21​)2​.
(11)
