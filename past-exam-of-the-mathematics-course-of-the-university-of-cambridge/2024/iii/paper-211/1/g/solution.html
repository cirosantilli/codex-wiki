Assume condition (1). Given an adapted cash-flow process X1​,…,XT​, construct the holdings backwards. Set HT+1​=0. Once Ht+1​ is known, the random variable
Xt​+Ht+1​⋅Pt​
(9)
is Ft​-measurable. Condition (1) supplies an Ft−1​-measurable Ht​ satisfying
Ht​⋅(Pt​+δt​)=Xt​+Ht+1​⋅Pt​.
(10)
Hence ξtH​=Xt​ for every t≤T, and setting later holdings to zero proves condition (2).
Conversely, let XT​ be any FT​-measurable random variable and apply condition (2) to the adapted process with cash flow XT​ at T and zero cash flow earlier. Since HT+1​=0,
XT​=ξTH​=HT​⋅(PT​+δT​),
(11)
where HT​ is FT−1​-measurable. This is condition (1). The conditions are therefore equivalent and describe market completeness.
