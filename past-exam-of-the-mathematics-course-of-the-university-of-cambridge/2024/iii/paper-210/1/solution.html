A centered random variable X is a Sub-Gamma random variable in the right tail with variance factor σ2 and scale parameter c when
logEeλX≤2(1−cλ)σ2λ2​(0≤λ<c−1).
(1)
The Chernoff bound with λ=x/(σ2+cx) gives
P(X≥x)≤exp{−2(σ2+cx)x2​}.
(2)
If cx≤σ2, the exponent is at least x2/(4σ2); if cx≥σ2, it is at least x/(4c). Hence
P(X≥x)≤max{e−x2/(4σ2),e−x/(4c)}.
(3)

Thus the sum is sub-Gamma with parameters (v,b/3), and the preceding Chernoff calculation proves Bernstein's inequality.
