Strictly, the normalized functions form the unit sphere rather than a vector space; let V=L2(R) and restrict to normalized states when interpreting wavefunctions. The group law gives
D(q,r,s)D(q′,r′,s′)ψ(x)=e−i(s+s′+qr′)ei(r+r′)xψ(x−q−q′)=D(gg′)ψ(x).
(7)
Translation preserves Lebesgue measure and both exponential factors have unit modulus, so D(g) preserves the inner product and is a unitary representation. This is the Schrödinger representation of the Heisenberg group: q translates position, r translates momentum, and s contributes the physically irrelevant overall phase.

The Lorentz group consists of linear maps x↦Λx satisfying ΛTηΛ=η. The Poincare group consists of affine isometries x↦Λx+a and has multiplication

Under sl(2)α​, a root xα+yβ has weight 2x−y. Counting the twelve root spaces and the two-dimensional Cartan subalgebra gives multiplicities one at weights ±2, four at ±1, and four at zero. Therefore
g↓sl(2)α​=d2​⊕4d1​⊕3d0​.
