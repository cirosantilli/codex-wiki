Write the optimized kernel as
J(q)=aˉ+κq2+γq4,aˉ=a+Δ.
(27)
Near the soft-mode sphere, the supplied asymptotic result says
I(aˉ)∼C(aˉ−ac​)−1/2,Δ∼6gπ2C​​(aˉ−ac​)−1/4.
(28)

The modulated minimum from part (b), however, has negative free energy for sufficiently low a and amplitude A∗​=π(ac​−a)/(8g). The order parameter consequently jumps, which is the Brazovskii fluctuation-induced first-order transition.

a OurBigBook.com
The thermodynamic order parameter is the conserved scalar composition difference ϕ of the binary fluid mixture. The momentum density ρv is an additional conserved hydrodynamic mode; it is not a symmetry-breaking order parameter, but it must be retained because momentum relaxes only through spatial transport.
Composition conservation gives a continuity equation,
(∂t​+v⋅∇)ϕ=−∇⋅J.
(30)
The current J=−M∇μ is the leading isotropic, dissipative constitutive law: it drives material down gradients of the chemical potential. For the Landau-Ginzburg theory
F[ϕ]=∫d3r(2a​ϕ2+4b​ϕ4+2κ​∣∇ϕ∣2),
(31)
the functional derivative is
μ=δϕδF​=aϕ+bϕ3−κ∇2ϕ.
(32)
These statements give the advective Cahn--Hilliard equation.
Constant mass density and incompressible flow require ∇⋅v=0. Momentum conservation gives the Navier-Stokes equation: material acceleration equals the sum of the Newtonian viscous force η∇2v, the pressure force −∇P, and the Korteweg force −ϕ∇μ. The pressure is the Lagrange multiplier enforcing incompressibility. Together these equations are Model H dynamics; isothermality removes the need for a separate energy equation.

Assume late-stage bicontinuous phase separation has one characteristic domain size L(t) and statistically self-similar morphology. The dynamical scaling of binary-fluid coarsening then estimates
∣v∣∼L˙,∣∂t​v∣∼L¨,∣(v⋅∇)v∣∼LL˙2​,∣∇2v∣∼L2L˙​.
(33)
The interface has surface tension σ. Its curvature pressure is of order σ/L, so its coarse-grained gradient, equivalently the transverse part of −ϕ∇μ, scales as σ/L2. Diffusive composition transport is neglected because hydrodynamic advection controls the late stage, and ρ,η,σ are treated as constants.
The pressure cannot simply be discarded: in an incompressible flow it cancels the longitudinal part of the other forces and contains both capillary and dynamic contributions. Applying the divergence-free projection to the momentum equation eliminates ∇P while retaining a solenoidal force with the same scaling. Geometry, projection, signs, and correlations are absorbed into dimensionless order-one coefficients. The result is
ρ(αL¨+βLL˙2​)=ηγL2L˙​+σL2δ​.​
(34)

For a specified trajectory, the nonconserved order-parameter dynamics equation determines the noise realization
fF​=p˙​+ΓδpδF​.
