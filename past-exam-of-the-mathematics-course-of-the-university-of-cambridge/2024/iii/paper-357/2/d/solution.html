The spatial metric is stationary and only βr=b(r)=Mr/(r+M)2 is nonzero. The evolution equation therefore says
2αKij​=(Lβ​γ)ij​,
(30)
the Lie derivative of the spatial metric along the shift vector. For the radial component,
2αKrr​=b∂r​γrr​+2γrr​∂r​b=−r(r+M)2M​,
(31)
so
Krr​=−r2M​.​
(32)
For the angular components,
2αKθθ​=b∂r​(r+M)2=r+M2Mr​,
(33)
and spherical symmetry supplies
Kθθ​=M,Kϕϕ​=Msin2θ.​
(34)
All off-diagonal components vanish.
Contracting with the inverse spatial metric gives the mean curvature
K​=γrrKrr​+γθθKθθ​+γϕϕKϕϕ​=−(r+M)2M​+(r+M)2M​+(r+M)2M​.​
(35)
Thus
K=(r+M)2M​.​
(36)
