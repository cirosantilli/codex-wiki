Each annulus loses specific angular momentum as its characteristic moves to smaller r. The negative magnetic torque transmits that angular momentum along the large-scale field to the anchored interstellar medium. Thus the disk's angular momentum decreases even before mass crosses r=0; angular momentum is conserved only after the field and its anchoring medium are included.
