For X(i)=X1:n​∖Xi​, Han's inequality for relative entropy states that, when P=⨂i=1n​Pi​,
D(Q∥P)≥n−11​∑i=1n​D(QX(i)​∥PX(i)​).
(11)
First prove the chain rule for relative entropy. For two coordinates,
logP(x,y)Q(x,y)​=logPX​(x)QX​(x)​+logPY∣X​(y∣x)QY∣X​(y∣x)​.
(12)
Taking expectation under Q gives
D(QXY​∥PXY​)=D(QX​∥PX​)+EQX​​D(QY∣X​∥PY∣X​),
(13)
and iteration proves the chain rule for any finite product.
Write Qi​(⋅∣x<i​) for the successive conditional distributions. Since P is a product, the chain rule gives
D(Q∥P)=∑i=1n​EQ​D(Qi​(⋅∣X<i​)∥Pi​).
(14)
For a fixed omitted coordinate j, applying the chain rule in the remaining coordinate order gives
D(QX(j)​∥PX(j)​)=∑i=j​EQ​D(Qi​(⋅∣X<i​∖Xj​)∥Pi​).
(15)
The convexity of Kullback-Leibler divergence implies that removing Xj​ from the conditioning can only decrease each averaged conditional divergence. Summing over j, each full conditional increment occurs for exactly the n−1 indices j=i, and therefore
∑j=1n​D(QX(j)​∥PX(j)​)≤(n−1)D(Q∥P),
(16)
which is the claimed inequality. Equivalently, this is Han's entropy inequality after expanding each divergence: the product-reference cross-entropy terms cancel because they are modular.

One useful form of the modified logarithmic Sobolev inequality is the following. For a function F of independent coordinates, let
Fi​(x(i))=infz∈[0,1]​F(x1​,…,xi−1​,z,xi+1​,…,xn​)
(39)
and V+(x)=∑i​(F(x)−Fi​(x(i)))2. If V+≤v and λ≥0, the inequality gives
Ent(eλF)≤2λ2v​EeλF,
(40)
and the Herbst argument yields
P(F−EF≥t)≤e−t2/(2v).
(41)

P(F−EF≤−t)≤e−t2/(2v).
(42)

We prove the Convex Poincaré inequality. For a differentiable convex function h:[0,1]→R and independent copies U,U′ supported on [0,1], convexity gives
(h(U)−h(U′))2≤h′(U)21{U>U′}​+h′(U′)21{U′<U}​.
(43)
Taking expectations and using Var(h(U))=21​E(h(U)−h(U′))2 gives
Var(h(U))≤Eh′(U)2.
(44)
Applying this conditional inequality coordinate by coordinate in the Efron–Stein inequality proves
Var(f(X))≤E∑i​(∂i​f(X))2=E∥∇f(X)∥2≤1.
(45)
Since −g is convex and has the same gradient norm as g, the same argument gives Var(g(X))≤1.
