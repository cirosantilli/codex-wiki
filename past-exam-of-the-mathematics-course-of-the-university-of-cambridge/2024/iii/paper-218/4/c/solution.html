This is a normalized perceptron algorithm. Because ∥Zi​∥2​=1, an update on a misclassified point obeys
∥γ(m+1)−γ​∥22​​=∥γ(m)−γ​+Yi​Zi​∥22​=∥γ(m)−γ​∥22​+1+2Yi​ZiT​γ(m)−2Yi​ZiT​γ​≤∥γ(m)−γ​∥22​+1+0−2=∥γ(m)−γ​∥22​−1.​
(29)
Here the two inequalities use the update condition Yi​ZiT​γ(m)≤0 and the assumed unit margin Yi​ZiT​γ​≥1. A squared distance cannot become negative, so there can be at most ∥γ(0)−γ​∥22​ updates. The algorithm then returns a vector that correctly separates every training point. The estimate γ​ is the comparison vector for the proof; without an additional uniqueness condition the returned separator need not equal that particular vector.
