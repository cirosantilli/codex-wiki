∥xi+1​−x∗∥22​​≤∥xi​−tgi​−x∗∥22​=∥xi​−x∗∥22​−2t⟨gi​,xi​−x∗⟩+t2∥gi​∥22​.​
(1)
The subgradient inequality gives ⟨gi​,xi​−x∗⟩≥f(xi​)−f∗, while Lipschitz continuity of the finite convex function gives ∥gi​∥2​≤G. Hence
2t(f(xi​)−f∗)≤∥xi​−x∗∥22​−∥xi+1​−x∗∥22​+t2G2.
(2)
Summing this telescoping inequality for 0≤i<k, and then bounding the smallest term by the average, yields
min0≤i<k​f(xi​)−f∗≤2tk∥x0​−x∗∥22​​+2tG2​.
(3)
Writing D=∥x0​−x∗∥2​, the right-hand side is minimized by the constant step size
t=Gk​D​.
(4)
Substitution gives
0≤i<kmin​f(xi​)−f∗≤k​GD​​.
(5)
If D=0, the initial point is already optimal and the result is immediate.
