Partition the range into planes xj​=x0​+jΔx. At each step, evaluate the refractive-index phase screen at xj​ or the midpoint, multiply the current envelope by eΔxSj​, transform in z, multiply each transverse Fourier component by the diffraction phase e−ikz2​Δx/(2k0​), and apply the inverse Fourier transform. Repeating this split-step Fourier method m times produces E(xm​,z) from E(x0​,z). The step size must continue to resolve both longitudinal medium variation and the Lie-Trotter splitting error.
