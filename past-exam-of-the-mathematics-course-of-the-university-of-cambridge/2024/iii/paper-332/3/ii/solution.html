ρi​Lf​V=kw​hTm​−Th​​.
(63)
Consequently the unperturbed lateral solidification speed is
V=ρi​Lf​hkw​​1+ϵϵ​(Tm​−Ta​)=ρi​Lf​δka​​1+ϵTm​−Ta​​​.
(64)
The material parameters are the thermal conductivities kw​,ka​, ice density ρi​, and specific latent heat Lf​; the geometric thermal length is δ.
