Write the spin-one-half Heisenberg antiferromagnet as
Hz​=J∑⟨ij⟩​Si​⋅Sj​,J>0,
(16)
on a bipartite lattice of coordination number z. A one-spin density operator is ρ=(I+r⋅σ)/2, where r is its Bloch vector and ∣r∣≤1. In a two-sublattice product state,
⟨Si​⋅Sj​⟩=41​rA​⋅rB​.
(17)
The minimum is −1/4, attained by pure antiparallel Bloch vectors, so the minimizing product state is a Néel state. In the z→∞ limit this mean-field approximation becomes exact and the ground-state energy per bond is therefore
ebond​=−4J​.
(18)
The phrase “energy density” requires a coupling convention. For the unscaled Hamiltonian above, every site belongs to z/2 bonds and
NE0​​=−8Jz​,
(19)
which diverges as z→∞. With the standard Kac normalization
HzKac​=zJ​∑⟨ij⟩​Si​⋅Sj​,
(20)
the finite energy density is
limz→∞​NE0​​=−8J​.
(21)
If the convention divides by the spatial dimension d=z/2 instead, the answer is −J/4 per site. A Hamiltonian written with σi​⋅σj​ rather than Si​⋅Sj​ multiplies all these energies by four.
