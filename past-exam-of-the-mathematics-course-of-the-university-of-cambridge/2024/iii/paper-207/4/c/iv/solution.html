The variance of an estimated hazard increment is large when its group has few individuals in the risk set. The log-rank weights are near zero when either rj(0)​ or rj(1)​ is small and are largest when both groups retain substantial information. They therefore suppress noisy late-event comparisons and weight each observed-minus-expected event by its available information. Unit weights would instead give equal influence to unstable increments from depleted risk sets.
