Because χ(H)=3, every bipartite graph is H-free. The balanced complete bipartite graph therefore gives
liminfn→∞​(2n​)ex(n,H)​≥21​.
(32)
For the reverse bound, fix δ>0 and suppose that G has more than (1/2+δ)(2n​) edges. Apply the Szemerédi regularity lemma with parameters much smaller than δ. Form the reduced graph whose vertices are the regularity classes and whose edges are the regular pairs of density above a small fixed threshold. Edges inside classes, irregular pairs, and regular pairs below the threshold account for oδ​(n2) edges. The remaining edges force the reduced graph to have more than m2/4 edges.
By the Turan theorem, the reduced graph contains a triangle. The three corresponding regular pairs all have positive density, and the graph embedding lemma for regular pairs embeds every fixed three-colourable graph, in particular H, across suitable repeated subclusters of these three classes. Thus every sufficiently large graph of density above 1/2+δ contains H. Letting δ↓0 gives
n→∞lim​(2n​)ex(n,H)​=21​.​
(33)
This is the chromatic-number-three case of the Erdős-Stone theorem.
