For a minimal integral Weierstrass equation, let E be the reduced cubic and Ens​(k) its nonsingular points, with their induced group law. Define the filtration of elliptic-curve points over a local field by
E0​(K)={P∈E(K):P∈Ens​(k)},
(25)
and
E1​(K)=ker(E0​(K)⟶Ens​(k)).
(26)
The parameter z=−x/y identifies E1​(K) with the formal group of an elliptic curve on πOK​. Part (a) therefore gives E1​(K)[n]=0. Reduction restricts to the exact sequence
0⟶E1​(K)⟶E0​(K)⟶Ens​(k)⟶0.
(27)
Its restriction to n-torsion has trivial kernel, yielding the injection
E0​(K)[n]↪Ens​(k).​
(28)
