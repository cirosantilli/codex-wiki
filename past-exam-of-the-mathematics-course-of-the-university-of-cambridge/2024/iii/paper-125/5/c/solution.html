The three-isogeny descent connecting map, identified through the Weil pairing with H1(Q,μ3​)=Q×/(Q×)3, is
α:E′(Q)⟶Q×/(Q×)3.
(57)
The long exact sequence attached to 0→E[ϕ]→Eϕ​E′→0 makes it a group homomorphism with
kerα=ϕE(Q).
(58)
The function f=y from part (b) gives the explicit formula
α(O)=1,α(T)=d−1=d2(mod(Q×)3),α(x,y)=y(mod(Q×)3)quad(P=O,T).
(59)
At T, the value d−1 is the leading coefficient of y relative to the local parameter x, since y(y+d)=x3 gives y/x3→d−1. At −T=(0,−d) the ordinary formula gives −d, whose class is the inverse of d−1 because −1 is a cube.
Let S be the primes dividing d. For a prime ℓ∈/S, use
y(y+d)=x3.
(60)
If vℓ​(y)>0, then y+d is an ℓ-adic unit, so vℓ​(y)=3vℓ​(x). If vℓ​(y)<0, then vℓ​(y+d)=vℓ​(y), so 2vℓ​(y)=3vℓ​(x). In either case vℓ​(y) is divisible by three; the special values at O and T have the same property. Therefore
imα⊆Q(S,3).​
(61)
When d=1, this power-class group is trivial: a rational number whose valuation at every prime is divisible by three is a cube up to sign, and −1=(−1)3. Thus α is trivial, its kernel is all of E′(Q), and
ϕ:E(Q)↠E′(Q).​
(62)
