A single prescribed classical gravitational potential gives a Hamiltonian of the form H1​[Φ]⊗I+I⊗H2​[Φ]. A semiclassical mean field sourced only by expectation values likewise gives each particle a local one-body potential and does not provide a quantum mediator carrying branch correlations.
