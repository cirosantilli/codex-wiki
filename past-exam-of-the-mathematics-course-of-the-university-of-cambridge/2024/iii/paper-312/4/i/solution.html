Let n point from the observer toward the emission point, so the photon propagation direction is p​=−n and
x(η)=x0​+(η0​−η)n.

Along this path D=d/dη=∂η​−n⋅∇. Substituting p​=−n into the Photon Boltzmann equation with Thomson scattering gives
D(Θ+Ψ)+Γ(Θ+Ψ)=Φ′+Ψ′+Γ(Θ0​+Ψ−n⋅ve​).

(49)
