In the convention (SU(3)c​,SU(2)L​)Y​, one Standard Model fermion generation including a right-handed neutrino is
QL​:(3,2)1/6​,uR​:(3,1)2/3​,dR​:(3,1)−1/3​,
(40)
LL​:(1,2)−1/2​,eR​:(1,1)−1​,νR​:(1,1)0​.
(41)
A bare Dirac mass term pairs left- and right-handed fields in the same gauge representation, but every charged left-handed Standard Model fermion is an SU(2)L​ doublet while its right-handed partner is a singlet. The Higgs field H:(1,2)1/2​ and H=iσ2H∗:(1,2)−1/2​ permit the gauge-invariant Yukawa interactions
yd​Qˉ​L​HdR​+yu​Qˉ​L​HuR​+ye​LˉL​HeR​+yν​LˉL​HνR​+h.c.
(42)
The neutral Higgs vacuum expectation value turns them into masses after electroweak symmetry breaking.
Because νR​ is a gauge singlet, it may also have a large Majorana mass term M. Together with its Dirac mass mD​, the seesaw mechanism gives a light neutrino mass mν​≃mD2​/M. If no right-handed neutrino is retained, the same low-energy physics is encoded by the dimension-five Weinberg operator (LL​H)(LL​H)/Λ.
Now let ϕ be an electroweak scalar triplet with Y=1. Its T3 weights are 1,0,−1, and electric charge is Q=T3+Y, so its components have charges
ϕ++:Q=2,ϕ+:Q=1,ϕ0:Q=0​.
(43)
In the given matrix convention,
ϕ++=2ϕ1​−iϕ2​​,ϕ+=2ϕ3​​,ϕ0=2ϕ1​+iϕ2​​​.
(44)
Thus the electromagnetic-neutral direction satisfies ϕ3​=0 and ϕ1​−iϕ2​=0.
The gauge-invariant type-II seesaw mechanism Yukawa interaction is
LY​=−21​yϕ​LLT​Ciσ2ϕLL​+h.c.​.
(45)
The two lepton doublets have total hypercharge −1, which is cancelled by Y(ϕ)=1, and the displayed SU(2) contraction is a singlet. Expanding it contains
−4yϕ​​(ϕ1​+iϕ2​)νLT​CνL​+⋯.
(46)
Therefore a neutral condensate ⟨ϕ0⟩=0 preserves electromagnetism and generates a left-handed-neutrino Majorana mass term proportional to yϕ​⟨ϕ0⟩.
