A ferromagnetic vacuum breaks the special unitary group SU(2) to the subgroup of rotations about its magnetization, so its vacuum manifold is the two-sphere S2. The massless field is therefore a unit vector n(x,t) with n2=1.
The only rotational scalar linear in n˙ that can be formed directly from n is n⋅n˙=21​∂t​(n2)=0. A local one-form A(n)⋅n˙ can describe the required Berry phase, but no choice of A is globally nonsingular and strictly rotationally invariant on S2. One therefore needs coordinate patches, an extension, or a redundant spinor parametrization.
