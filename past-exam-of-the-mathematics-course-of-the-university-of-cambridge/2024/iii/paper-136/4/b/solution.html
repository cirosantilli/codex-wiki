For sufficiently large r, the series for the p-adic logarithm and p-adic exponential converge on 1+πrOK​ and πrOK​ and are inverse homomorphisms. Hence the principal-unit logarithm gives
1+πrOK​≅(πrOK​,+)≅(OK​,+),
(34)
where the last map is division by πr.
Now take K=Q3​(ζ3​) and π=ζ3​−1. This is a uniformizer, the residue field is F3​, and its Teichmuller units are μ2​={±1}. Thus
OK×​≅μ2​×U1​,Ui​=1+πiOK​.
(35)
The image of ζ3​ generates U1​/U2​, and μ3​∩U2​=1, so U1​=μ3​×U2​. The logarithm and exponential already converge inversely on U2​, giving U2​≅(π2OK​,+)≅(OK​,+). Since μ2​×μ3​≅μ6​, this proves the unit group of Q3 zeta3 decomposition
OK×​≅μ6​×OK​.​
(36)
