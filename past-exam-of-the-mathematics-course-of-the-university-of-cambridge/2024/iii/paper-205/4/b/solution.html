One standard construction uses the Debiased Lasso. Starting from the Square-root Lasso estimate β​, estimate a vector Θj​ that approximately inverts the jth column of the empirical Gram matrix Σ=XTX/n, for example by a Nodewise Lasso. Define
β​j​=β​j​+ΘjT​nXT(Y−Xβ​)​,Vj​=ΘjT​ΣΘj​,
(33)
and estimate σ by σ=∥Y−Xβ​∥2​/n​. The approximate two-sided level-α test rejects Hjβ​ when
​σVj​​n​β​j​​​>z1−α/2​,
(34)
where z1−α/2​ is a standard normal quantile.
Sufficient high-dimensional conditions include a Compatibility condition for the Lasso bounded away from zero, γ≍logp/n​, logp=o(n), and
n​slogp​⟶0,
(35)
together with the corresponding sparsity and consistency conditions for the nodewise inverse-Gram estimate. Under these assumptions the Debiased-Lasso asymptotic normality makes the rejection probability under Hjβ​ tend to α.
