Let Ω(k) solve the kth diagonal-block problem and set
Ω=[Ω(1)0​0Ω(2)​].
(17)
Its inverse is block diagonal. On each diagonal block, the Graphical-Lasso Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions hold by the definition of Ω(k). On the off-diagonal blocks choose
Z(12)=−S(12)/λ,Z(21)=−S(21)/λ.
(18)
The assumed inequalities ∣Sij​∣≤λ ensure that every entry lies in [−1,1], exactly the allowed subgradient at a zero entry of Ω.
Thus −Ω−1+S+λZ=0 on every block. The KKT conditions and the fact that the objective is strictly convex prove that Ω=Ω, giving the claimed block decomposition.
