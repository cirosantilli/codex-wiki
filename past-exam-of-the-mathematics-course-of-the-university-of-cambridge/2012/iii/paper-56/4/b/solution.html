The displayed ea are one-forms, so they form an orthonormal coframe, dual to the orthonormal frame E0​=A−1∂t​, E1​=A−1∂x​, E2​=A−1∂y​, E3​=∂z​. Use ηab​=diag(−1,1,1,1) and abbreviate
a=AA′​,q=AA′′​=a′+a2.
(38)
For i=0,1,2, dei=ae3∧ei, while de3=0. The Levi-Civita connection is fixed by Cartan's first structure equation together with Lorentzian connection-form antisymmetry, ωab​=−ωba​, where ωab​=ηac​ωcb​. Its nonzero connection 1-forms are
ω03​=ae0,ω13​=ae1,ω23​=ae2,ω30​=ae0,ω31​=−ae1,ω32​=−ae2.​
(39)
In particular, the time-index pair has the same sign with one index raised; incorrectly imposing ωab​=−ωba​ would lose the Lorentzian sign. All diagonal and all other entries vanish.
Apply Cartan's second structure equation. For i=0,1,2,
Θi3​=d(aei)=(a′+a2)e3∧ei=−qei∧e3.
(40)
For distinct i,j in {0,1,2}, putting s0​=−1 and s1​=s2​=1 gives Θij​=ωi3​∧ω3j​=−sj​a2ei∧ej. Thus a complete independent set of curvature 2-forms is
Θ01​Θ03​​=−a2e0∧e1,=−qe0∧e3,​Θ02​Θ13​​=−a2e0∧e2,=−qe1∧e3,​Θ12​Θ23​​=−a2e1∧e2,=−qe2∧e3.​​
(41)
All diagonal entries vanish. The remaining entries follow from Θab​=−Θba​: Θji​=−si​sj​Θij​ for i,j≤2, and Θ3i​=−si​Θi3​. These relations and the displayed six entries specify all sixteen curvature 2-forms. This computation is Cartan curvature of a planar warped spacetime.
