Let u be the tangent vector to a geodesic with affine parameter λ. Its geodesic equation is ∇u​u=0. For a Killing vector field ξ, metric compatibility gives
dλd​(ξa​ua)=uaub∇b​ξa​+ξa​ub∇b​ua=uaub∇(a​ξb)​=0.
(35)
Thus
ξa​ua is constant along every affinely parametrized geodesic.​
(36)
This geodesic conserved quantity from a Killing vector is independent of whether the geodesic is timelike, spacelike or null. For a timelike geodesic one can use proper time; for a null geodesic choose an affine parameter. An arbitrary nonaffine tangent rescales the displayed quantity and need not make it constant.
