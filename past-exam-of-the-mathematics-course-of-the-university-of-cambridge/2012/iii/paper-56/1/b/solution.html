The torsion tensor of an affine connection is T(X,Y)=∇X​Y−∇Y​X−[X,Y], where the last term is the Lie bracket of vector fields. A torsion-free connection has T=0. Since a coordinate basis commutes, this is equivalent there to Γμνρ​=Γμρν​; in a general basis its commutator must also be included.
The Levi-Civita connection is the unique torsion-free connection with metric compatibility, ∇g=0. To derive its Christoffel symbols, lower the first index: Γσνρ​=gσμ​Γμνρ​. Metric compatibility gives
∂ρ​gσν​=Γνσρ​+Γσνρ​.
(2)
Write down this identity with the derivative indices ρ,ν,σ, add the first two identities and subtract the third. Symmetry in the last two indices, supplied by the torsion-free connection, cancels all terms except twice Γσνρ​. Raising the first index yields
Γμνρ​=21​gμσ(∂ρ​gσν​+∂ν​gσρ​−∂σ​gνρ​).​
(3)
This calculation proves uniqueness. Conversely, these Christoffel symbols are symmetric in ν,ρ and satisfy ∇g=0 by substitution, proving existence as well.
