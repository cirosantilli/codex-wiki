A covariant derivative on the tangent bundle is an affine connection: it assigns a vector field ∇X​Y to vector fields X,Y, is linear over smooth functions in X, is real-linear in Y, and obeys the Leibniz rule ∇X​(aY)=X(a)Y+a∇X​Y. Thus it differentiates a vector field while taking account of how its local basis changes. It extends to tensor fields by the Leibniz rule, compatibility with tensor contraction, and ∇X​a=X(a) for a scalar.
In a local basis eμ​, the connection components are defined by ∇eρ​​eν​=Γμνρ​eμ​. In a coordinate basis, eρ​=∂ρ​, so
(∇ρ​V)μ=∂ρ​Vμ+Γμνρ​Vν,(∇ρ​α)μ​=∂ρ​αμ​−Γνμρ​αν​.
(1)
A covariant derivative of a tensor field has tensorial transformation properties, but the connection components themselves do not: changing the coordinate basis introduces second derivatives of the coordinate change. They describe the connection in a chosen basis, rather than components of a tensor.
