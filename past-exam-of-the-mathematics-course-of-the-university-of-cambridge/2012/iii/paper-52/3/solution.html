Put sW​=sinθW​ and cW​=cosθW​. The left-handed doublet has
TL3​=(21​0​0−21​​),QL​=(00​0−1​),
(16)
while the right-handed charged-lepton singlet has TR3​=0 and QR​=−1. There is no weakly coupled right-handed neutrino in the stated field content. For a fermion f, write gLf​=T3Lf​−Qf​sW2​ and gRf​=T3Rf​−Qf​sW2​, with gRν​=0 here. Since fˉ​L​γμfL​=fˉ​γμPL​f and similarly for R,
fˉ​γμ(gLf​PL​+gRf​PR​)f=fˉ​γμ(cVf​−cAf​γ5)f,cVf​=2gLf​+gRf​​,cAf​=2gLf​−gRf​​.
(17)
Consequently the neutral-current vector and axial couplings in the specified overall normalization are
cVℓ​=−41​+sW2​,cAℓ​=−41​,cVν​=cAν​=41​.​
(18)
These coefficients accompany g/cW​. A convention with g/(2cW​) uses twice these vector and axial coefficients; mixing the two conventions would make the widths wrong by a factor of four. For the neutrino, cV​−cA​γ5=21​PL​, so the chiral projection is already built into the vertex.
For either massless final pair with momenta k1​,k2​, p=k1​+k2​, the invariant amplitude is, up to an irrelevant overall phase,
Mf​=cW​g​ϵμ​(p)uˉf​(k1​)γμ(cVf​−cAf​γ5)vf​(k2​).
(19)
The spin-summed fermion tensor is
Tfμν​=tr[k1​γμ(cVf​−cAf​γ5)k2​γν(cVf​−cAf​γ5)].
(20)
The symmetric part, using the four-gamma trace, is
(Tfμν​)sym​=4[(cVf​)2+(cAf​)2](k1μ​k2ν​+k1ν​k2μ​−ημνk1​⋅k2​).
(21)
The vector-axial interference is antisymmetric in μ,ν, so it drops out of the symmetric Z polarization sum. Also pμ​Tfμν​=0 for massless external fermions, by their Dirac equations; hence the pμ​pν​/mZ2​ part contributes zero. Using k1​⋅k2​=mZ2​/2 gives
∣Mf​∣2​=3cW2​g2​(−ημν​)Tfμν​=3cW2​4g2mZ2​​[(cVf​)2+(cAf​)2].​
(22)
The massive-vector spin average supplies the factor 1/3 for the three spin states of the massive Z. The massless two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space is dΦ2​=dΩ/(32π2), and so ∫dΦ2​=1/(8π). The spin-averaged amplitude above is angle-independent in the rest frame, giving
Γf​=12πcW2​g2mZ​​[(cVf​)2+(cAf​)2].​
(23)
The massless leptonic Z decay widths for one charged-lepton flavor and one active neutrino flavor respectively are
Γ(Z→ℓℓˉ)=96πcW2​g2mZ​​(1−4sW2​+8sW4​),Γ(Z→ννˉ)=96πcW2​g2mZ​​.​
(24)
There is no color factor for leptons and no extra final-state identical-particle factor for a particle-antiparticle pair. The neutrino projectors already exclude sterile helicity states, so no additional factor of two is needed. These are tree-level massless widths; electroweak radiative corrections and charged-lepton masses have not been included. If desired, using GF​=g2/(42​mW2​) and mW​=cW​mZ​ converts the common prefactor to GF​mZ3​/(122​π).
