The regression residual vector is e=Y−Xβ​=(I−H)Y, where H=X(XTX)−1XT is the hat matrix. Both H and I−H are symmetric idempotent matrices, and (I−H)X=0. Therefore e=(I−H)ε and the linear image of a multivariate normal vector gives
e∼Nn​(0,σ2(I−H)).​
(4)
This multivariate normal distribution is singular, with rank n−p: fitted-residual orthogonality forces XTe=0. Specifically, Var(ei​)=σ2(1−hii​) and Cov(ei​,ej​)=−σ2hij​ for distinct observations. Thus regression residuals are generally neither independent nor identically distributed, even though the model errors are. Also RSS/σ2∼χn−p2​ and is independent of β​, which justifies the standard errors and exact Student t-tests below.
