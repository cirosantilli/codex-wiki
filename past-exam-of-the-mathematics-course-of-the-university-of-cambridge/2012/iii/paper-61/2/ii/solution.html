The steady incompressible shearing sheet equation is
u⋅∇u+2Ωez​×u=−ρ−1∇p+2ΩSxex​.
(16)
The final term follows from the local tidal potential −ΩSx2. For the given linear velocity, the advective acceleration is −αβ(xex​+yey​), while the Coriolis acceleration is 2Ωβxex​+2Ωαyey​. Equating components gives
ρpx​​=[αβ+2Ω(S−β)]x,ρpy​​=α(β−2Ω)y.
(17)
The cross derivatives vanish, so the pressure Hessian of an elliptical shearing-sheet vortex integrates to
ρp​=21​Ax2+21​By2+constant,A=(r−1)2S2​−r−12ΩS​,B=(r−1)2S2​−r(r−1)2ΩS​.​
(18)
The additive constant is fixed by exterior matching or the pressure reference. No zero-pressure free-boundary condition is imposed on an incompressible vortex patch: it is embedded in a surrounding fluid.
