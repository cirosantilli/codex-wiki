For the static patch of de Sitter spacetime, r∗​=∫dr/F=(R/2)log∣(R+r)/(R−r)∣. With v=t+r∗​ or u=t−r∗​, respectively,
ds2=−Fdv2+2dvdr+r2dΩ22​,ds2=−Fdu2−2dudr+r2dΩ22​.
(10)
Both radial blocks have determinant −1 and smooth coefficients at r=R. The normal has norm grr=F, so that surface is null. There k=∂v​ has covector dr, or k=∂u​ has covector −dr, making it normal as well as tangent: it is a Killing horizon.
For the signed surface gravity of a de Sitter horizon, since ∂r​k2=−F′(R)=2/R, part a gives κ=−1/R in the ingoing chart on the past branch, and κ=+1/R in the outgoing chart on the future branch, with k future-directed in the static patch. The physical magnitude is ∣κ∣=1/R​, normalized by central proper time. A negative signed F′(R)/2 must not become a negative temperature.
