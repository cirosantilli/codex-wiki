The coefficients are those of the three-stage Lobatto IIIA method. Set e=(1,1,1)T, c=Ae=(0,1/2,1)T and C=diag(c). Direct calculation gives all eight fourth-order conditions for a Runge-Kutta method:
bTe=1,bTc=21​,bTc2=31​,bTAc=61​,bTc3=41​,bTCAc=81​,bTAc2=121​,bTA2c=241​.​
(8)
Powers of c are componentwise. These Butcher order conditions prove order at least four for a smooth general nonlinear vector field; matching a scalar test equation alone would not suffice. A necessary fifth-order condition fails:
bTc4=245​=51​.
(9)
Hence the method has order exactly four, with one-step local truncation error O(h5) and, under stability and smoothness hypotheses, global error O(h4).
