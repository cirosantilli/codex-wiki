Every convergent member is also A-stable. For 0<α<2, put ζ=eiθ and calculate its boundary-locus test for multistep A-stability:
ζρ(ζ)​=−α(1−cosθ)+i(2−α)sinθ,ζσ(ζ)​=(2−α)cosθ+i2α​sinθ.
(21)
The denominator never vanishes on the unit circle in this parameter interval. It follows that
Reσ(eiθ)ρ(eiθ)​=2[(2−α)2cos2θ+α2sin2θ/4]α(2−α)(1−cosθ)2​≥0.
(22)
Therefore no amplification root can cross the unit circle as z=hλ moves in the open left half-plane. For small negative real z, the root near one moves to 1+z+O(z2), inside the circle; the other begins at α−1, also strictly inside. The leading coefficient 1−(1−α/4)z never vanishes in the left half-plane. Continuity of polynomial roots and connectedness of that half-plane now keep both roots inside everywhere. On its imaginary boundary the same locus has zero real part only at ζ=1, corresponding to z=0; that root is simple because 2−α>0.
At α=0, the two interlaced subsequences follow the trapezoidal rule with step 2h:
ζ2=1−z1+z​.
(23)
The right side has modulus at most one for Rez≤0. Its unit-circle roots are simple; the double zero at z=−1 lies strictly inside and is harmless. Thus the endpoint is also A-stable. Combining this with part 1, the requested range is 0≤α<2, including the BDF2 method at α=4/3.
