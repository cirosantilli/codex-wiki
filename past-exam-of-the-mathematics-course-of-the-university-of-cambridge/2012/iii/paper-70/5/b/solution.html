For k=2 on an equidistant spline knot sequence, Ni​ is the triangular hat with value one at ti+1​ and support [ti​,ti+2​]. Its slopes on the two pieces are 1/h and −1/h, and Mi​=Ni​/h. Direct integration gives
∫Ni2​=2∫0h​(u/h)2du=32h​,∫Ni​Ni+1​=∫0h​(u/h)(1−u/h)du=6h​.

Nonadjacent hats have disjoint interiors of their supports, so their inner product vanishes. Dividing by h therefore gives
gij​=⎩⎨⎧​2/3,1/6,0,​i=j,∣i−j∣=1,∣i−j∣≥2.​​
(29)
This linear-spline mixed Gram matrix is tridiagonal. Every diagonal entry is 2/3, including the first and last: the distinct-knot hats have their full supports inside [0,1]. End rows simply lack one off-diagonal neighbor; there are no repeated endpoint spline knots here.
