For a number field K and a modulus of a number field m=m0​m∞​, let IK​(m) be the group of fractional ideals prime to m0​, and let PK,1​(m) consist of principal fractional ideals (a) with a≡1 at the finite modulus and a>0 at the real places in m∞​. The ray class group is Clm​(K)=IK​(m)/PK,1​(m).
The Main theorem of class field theory states that there is a unique finite abelian ray class field Km​/K for which the Artin reciprocity map induces
Clm​(K)≃Gal(Km​/K).​
(12)
It is unramified outside the finite modulus and has no ramification at real places omitted from m∞​. More generally, subgroups H with PK,1​(m)⊆H⊆IK​(m) correspond, with inclusions reversed, to intermediate abelian fields, with Galois group IK​(m)/H. Every finite abelian extension occurs for some modulus. Equivalently, Km​ is the maximal abelian extension with conductor of an abelian extension dividing m. This states both reciprocity and the existence theorem of global class field theory.
