For a direct check of unramified behavior, the cyclotomic polynomial Φ25​ divides X25−1. Modulo 7, the latter has derivative 25X24=4X24 and has no common root with that derivative. Thus Φ25​ is separable, so 7 does not divide its polynomial discriminant. The field discriminant divides this polynomial discriminant, and therefore 7 is unramified in Q(ζ25​). The Frobenius automorphism satisfies
Frob7​(ζ25​)=ζ257​.​
(10)
Indeed its residue action is seventh powering. The reduced 25th roots of unity are distinct because 7∤25, so the residue congruence identifies the exponent uniquely modulo 25.
The Galois group is (Z/25Z)×, of order φ(25)=20. Since 72=49≡−1(mod25), 74≡1 but 72≡1. Consequently the Frobenius automorphism has order four. An unramified decomposition group is generated by that element, and the orbit-stabilizer theorem gives
g=20/4=5,f=4,e=1.​
(11)
There are therefore five primes above 7, each with residue field F74​.
