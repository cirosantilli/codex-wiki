The precise helium fraction requires the nuclear-burning temperature, baryon abundance and properly normalized weak rates; the question supplies only approximate time laws. In particular, the large no-decay fractions in the table must not be interpreted as precise predictions for the observed primordial abundance. The robust effect is extra relativistic energy raises the primordial helium fraction, with an absolute change of order 10−2 for one thermally populated flavor.
