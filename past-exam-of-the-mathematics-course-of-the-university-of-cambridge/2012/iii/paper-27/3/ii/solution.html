For an integral Minimal Weierstrass equation, let E0​(Qp​) be the subgroup of points whose reduction is a smooth point of the reduced cubic. Its reduction is a homomorphism to the smooth-locus group of a singular Weierstrass cubic, or to the whole reduced elliptic curve in the good-reduction case. Define the formal kernel of a minimal Weierstrass equation by
E1​(Qp​)=ker(E0​(Qp​)⟶Ens​(Fp​)).​
(9)
It consists of the identity and the points reducing to the identity at infinity. For a nonidentity affine point this is equivalent to vp​(x)=−2r and vp​(y)=−3r for some positive integer r.
The local parameter t=−x/y, with t(O)=0, gives the group isomorphism
E1​(Qp​)≅E(pZp​),​
(10)
where E is the formal group of an elliptic curve. To see the local coordinates, put w=−1/y in the Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve. The resulting relation is w=t3+a1​tw+a2​t2w+a3​w2+a4​tw2+a6​w3, and its unit linear coefficient gives a unique integral series w(t)=t3+O(t4). Thus x=t/w and y=−1/w have valuations −2vp​(t) and −3vp​(t). The chord-and-tangent group law expressed in this parameter is precisely the integral formal group law E. This description includes bad reduction; only the smooth identity neighborhood is involved.
