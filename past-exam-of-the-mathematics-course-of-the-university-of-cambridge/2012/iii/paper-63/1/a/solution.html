Efficient convection makes the interior follow a nearly adiabatic process and maintains uniform specific entropy. For the fully ionized monatomic ideal gas, the specific-heat ratio is 5/3, so the adiabatic temperature gradient gives dlogT/dlogP=2/5. Integrating at a fixed time gives
P=K(t)T5/2.​
(1)
The coefficient is spatially constant because the composition and specific entropy are uniform. It can change as the star loses energy. Equivalently the gas is an adiabatic stellar polytrope with P=Kρ​ρ5/3 and polytropic index n=3/2; Kρ​ and the pressure-temperature coefficient K are different quantities.
This fixes the dimensionless Lane-Emden equation profile throughout a stellar homology sequence. Writing ρ(r)=ρc​f(r/R) in the mass integral gives ρc​=Cρ​M/R3, with a fixed positive shape constant Cρ​. Integrating the stellar hydrostatic equation through the same profile, with negligible surface pressure compared with central pressure, gives Pc​=CP​GM2/R4. Hence
ρc​∝M/R3,Pc​∝GM2/R4,Tc​=Rρc​μPc​​∝RRμGM​.​
