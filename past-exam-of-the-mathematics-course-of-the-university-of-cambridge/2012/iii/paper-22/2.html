A connection on a vector bundle is a C-linear map D:A0(E)→A1(E) satisfying D(fs)=df⊗s+fDs for smooth functions. Its extension is the covariant exterior derivative
D(η⊗s)=dη⊗s+(−1)degηη∧Ds.
(9)
Squaring gives a C∞-linear operator D2s=Θs, defining the vector-bundle curvature Θ∈A2(EndE).
For a line bundle, EndE is canonically the trivial line bundle, so this vector-bundle curvature is an ordinary global complex two-form. In a local frame with D=d+A, the scalar identity A∧A=0 gives Θ=dA. Therefore dΘ=0. The calculations in the two subparts also explain the gluing directly.
