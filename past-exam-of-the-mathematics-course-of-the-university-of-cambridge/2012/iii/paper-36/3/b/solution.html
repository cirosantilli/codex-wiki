Rn∗​≥4(θ−τ)2​(1−21​∥Pf(n)​−Pg(n)​∥1​).​

Here Rn∗​ is the minimax risk for this functional. The factor 21​∥Pf(n)​−Pg(n)​∥1​ is the total variation distance, so the same metric squared-loss two-point bound applies to a functional even when distinct density parameters have the same functional value.
