For a pathwise differentiable statistical functional, an influence-function representer is a mean-zero function φ∈L2(P) such that every admissible score function g satisfies DψP​(g)=P(φg). The efficient influence function, also called the canonical gradient, is the unique such representer in the statistical tangent space. Equivalently, it is the orthogonal projection of any representer onto that statistical tangent space. The Pythagorean theorem in an inner-product space shows that it has the smallest squared L2 norm among all representers.
