Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2012 / iii / Paper 67 / 3 / a / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2012 iii Paper 67 3 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 290
Use five resource qubits, all initially ∣+⟩, and prepare the graph state with edges {1,3}, {2,4}, {3,4}, {4,5}. The labels here identify resource vertices; logical wire 1 travels from vertex 1 to 3, and logical wire 2 travels from 2 to 4 to 5. Each edge is a Controlled-Z gate, and these entangling gates commute, so all can be applied when preparing the resource.
Measure vertex 1 in the equatorial qubit measurement basis at angle α, with outcome s1​, and vertex 2 at angle β, with outcome s2​. These measurements can be simultaneous. The one-bit teleportation lemma leaves the state on vertices 3 and 4, before the last teleportation, equal up to global phase to
X3s1​​Z3s2​​X4s2​​Z4s1​​E34​(J(α)⊗J(β))∣++⟩.
(18)
Indeed commuting the two initial X byproducts through E34​ creates precisely the crossed Z factors. The unused edge from 4 to the fresh vertex 5 is the last one-bit teleportation link.
Measure vertex 4 at angle θ=(−1)s2​γ, with outcome s4​. By the printed J gate propagation identities,
Xs4​J(θ)Xs2​Zs1​=eis2​θXs4​⊕s1​Zs2​J(γ).
(19)
Thus the remaining two-qubit quantum state is
(X3s1​​Z3s2​​)⊗(X5s4​⊕s1​​Z5s2​​)∣ψC​⟩,
(20)
up to global phase, where ∣ψC​⟩ is the desired circuit output relabelled onto vertices 3 and 5. The sign choice cancels the unwanted angle reversal caused by the X frame on vertex 4.
Finally measure vertices 3 and 5 in the computational basis, obtaining raw results t3​,t5​. The Pauli Z gate affects only phases, while the Pauli X gate flips a computational result. The five-vertex graph-state circuit simulation therefore outputs
b1​=t3​⊕s1​,b2​=t5​⊕s4​⊕s1​.​
(21)
This deterministic classical correction reproduces the ideal joint probability distribution for every preceding measurement branch. The final fixed-basis measurements may share the second layer with the adaptive measurement of vertex 4, because their projectors act on different vertices; measuring them afterwards is also valid.
