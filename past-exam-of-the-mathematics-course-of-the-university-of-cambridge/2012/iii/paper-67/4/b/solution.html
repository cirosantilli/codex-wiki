The quantum circuit has one control wire per binary digit and one target quantum register. Every control starts in ∣0⟩, receives a Hadamard gate, controls the corresponding power U2j, and then enters the collective inverse quantum Fourier transform. Only the control register is measured; the target stays in ∣ψ⟩.
The omitted intermediate wires continue the pattern j=0,1,…,m−1. The inverse quantum Fourier transform black box is defined on the integer basis ∣k⟩ with k=∑j​2jkj​, so the displayed ordering introduces no hidden bit reversal. The measured bits satisfy
x=j=0∑m−1​2jxj​,ϕ=x/2m.​

Each controlled power box represents 2j sequential controlled-U uses when only that primitive is available.
