Use A=S+Ω, where S is the rate-of-strain tensor and Ω is antisymmetric. For incompressible flow trS=0. Since Ω2=(ωωT−ω2I)/4, taking a trace gives
trA3=trS3+3tr(SΩ2)=trS3+43​ωi​ωj​Sij​.
(11)
This uses the positive convention R=trA3/3 specified here; a convention with the opposite sign for R must not be mixed into the calculation. For a compressible flow there is an additional term −3ω2trS/4, so the displayed incompressible formula is not literally valid for every flow.
One can exhibit the required divergence directly. Define
Fi​=uj​(∂j​uk​)(∂k​ui​)−21​ui​(∂j​uk​)(∂k​uj​).
(12)
Differentiating, terms containing ∂i​ui​ vanish, and the two terms with a second derivative cancel after relabeling indices. Therefore
trA3=∂i​Fi​,⟨trA3⟩=0
(13)
in homogeneous turbulence with finite differentiable moments, or under periodic averaging. This is the Betchov relation. The preceding trace identity then implies
⟨ωi​ωj​Sij​⟩=−34​⟨trS3⟩=−34​⟨a3+b3+c3⟩​.
(14)
The principal strain rates are the real eigenvalues of the symmetric matrix S. Incompressibility gives a+b+c=0, and the identity a3+b3+c3−3abc=(a+b+c)(a2+b2+c2−ab−bc−ca) gives
⟨ωi​ωj​Sij​⟩=−4⟨abc⟩​.
(15)
No isotropy is required for this averaged tensor identity: statistical homogeneity and incompressibility are the key assumptions.
