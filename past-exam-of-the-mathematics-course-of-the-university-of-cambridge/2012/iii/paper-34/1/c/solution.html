The stopping-interval indicator is an elementary predictable process with stopping-time intervals. Its stochastic integral is
(1(S,T]​⋅M)t​=Mt∧T​−Mt∧S​.​

It is zero before S, equals Mt​−MS​ between S and T, and equals MT​−MS​ after T. For a martingale with jumps, the jump at S is excluded and the jump at T is included, exactly as required by the half-open interval.
