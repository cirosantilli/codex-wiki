In an abelian category, for f:A→B set coimf=coker(kerf) and imf=ker(cokerf). The canonical map from coimage to image is an isomorphism. Thus the image factorization in an abelian category is
Ae​imfm​B,f=me,e epic,m monic,​
(22)
unique up to a unique compatible isomorphism.
The short five lemma states that in a commuting diagram of short exact sequences 0→L→M→R→0 and 0→L′→M′→R′→0 in an abelian category, if the vertical maps L→L′ and R→R′ are isomorphisms, then so is M→M′.
For the Five lemma, consider commuting exact rows
A1​d1​​A2​d2​​A3​d3​​A4​d4​​A5​,B1​d1′​​B2​d2′​​B3​d3′​​B4​d4′​​B5​,
(23)
with vertical maps vi​:Ai​→Bi​. We prove the usual stronger version: v1​ epic, v2​ and v4​ invertible, and v5​ monic imply that v3​ is invertible. In particular the conclusion holds if all four outer vertical maps are isomorphisms.
Put L=imd2​ and R=imd3​, and define L′,R′ similarly. Exactness gives short exact sequences
0⟶L⟶A3​⟶R⟶0,0⟶L′⟶B3​⟶R′⟶0.
(24)
The functoriality of abelian image factorization supplies their vertical outer maps. We show that both are isomorphisms.
First, L≅cokerd1​ and L′≅cokerd1′​: exactness at A2​ identifies imd1​ with kerd2​, and the image-coimage isomorphism for d2​ gives the claimed cokernel. Write q:A2​→L and q′:B2​→L′ for these categorical cokernels. To construct an inverse to the induced map ℓ:L→L′, observe
(qv2−1​d1′​)v1​=qv2−1​v2​d1​=0.
(25)
Cancellation of the epimorphism v1​ gives qv2−1​d1′​=0, so qv2−1​ factors uniquely as ℓˉq′ with ℓˉ:L′→L. From ℓq=q′v2​, composing with the epic q,q′ gives ℓˉℓ=1L​ and ℓℓˉ=1L′​.
Second, R≅kerd4​ and R′≅kerd4′​ by exactness at the fourth objects. Write their inclusions as j:R→A4​ and j′:R′→B4​. The equality d4′​v4​=v5​d4​ gives the induced map ρ:R→R′. Since
v5​d4​v4−1​j′=d4′​j′=0
(26)
and v5​ is a monomorphism, d4​v4−1​j′=0. Kernel universality gives ρˉ​:R′→R with jρˉ​=v4−1​j′. Cancelling the monic j,j′ proves ρˉ​ρ=1R​ and ρρˉ​=1R′​.
Apply the short five lemma to the two short exact sequences: their outer maps ℓ,ρ are invertible, so v3​ is an isomorphism. This five lemma via image factorization argument uses only universal properties and therefore works in any abelian category, without treating its objects as literal sets of elements.
