Again use integer coefficients after clearing denominators and put S=∑i​ai​. If S=0, choose any odd positive integer u and take all xi​=u. This gives a monochromatic solution in any colouring.
For necessity, suppose S=0 and choose K with 2K>∣S∣. Colour the odd positive integers by their residue classes modulo 2K. In any monochromatic candidate, xi​≡u(mod2K) for a common odd u. The equation would imply uS≡0(mod2K). Since u is coprime to 2K, this forces 2K∣S, impossible for nonzero S with ∣S∣<2K. Thus regular over odd positive integers⟺i∑​ai​=0​. This is one-equation partition regularity over odd integers; its obstruction concerns the full coefficient sum rather than just a zero-sum subset.
