A feasible dual vector is a convex combination of the slopes whose mean slope is zero. It gives a constant lower bound on the maximum of the affine functions. The primal is always feasible, by taking x=0 and sufficiently large t. If it has a finite optimum, linear programming duality gives attainment and equality with this dual; otherwise the dual is infeasible and the primal is unbounded below.
