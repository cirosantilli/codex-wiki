A completely depolarizing unital quantum channel sends a pure qubit state to I/2, giving spectra r=(1,0) and s=(1/2,1/2). The proposed r≺s fails already at the first partial sum, since 1>1/2. The supplied doubly stochastic characterization of majorization then yields s≺r, completing the intended corrected result. This proves spectral majorization under a unital quantum channel. A unital quantum channel can make a spectrum more uniform; it cannot generally make the output spectrum majorize the input. The counterexample resolves the false printed assertion rather than treating the reversed inequality as a convention change.
