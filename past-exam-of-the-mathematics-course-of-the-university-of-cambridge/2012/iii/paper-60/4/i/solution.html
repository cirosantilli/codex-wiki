Let δsc​≃1.686 be the linear spherical-collapse threshold and let D(t) be the linear growth factor, normalized to one at the epoch to which the random density field has been extrapolated. The time-dependent barrier is δc​(t)=δsc​/D(t). The smoothed matter density variance σ2(M) is the variance of the linear density contrast smoothed with a spherical top-hat window function whose comoving mass is M. Both quantities must refer to the same linear normalization; equivalently use ν=δsc​/σ(M,t).
Let N denote the comoving number density and write N(M,t)=dN/dM for the positive differential Press-Schechter halo mass function. With ρˉ​0​ the mean comoving mass density, the mass fraction obeys
f(>M,t)=ρˉ​0​1​∫M∞​M′N(M′,t)dM′,N(M,t)=−Mρˉ​0​​∂M∂f​.

Differentiating the complementary error function and using ν=δc​/σ gives
dMdN​=π2​​M2ρˉ​0​​νe−ν2/2​dlnMdlnσ​​.​
(19)
This is the requested mass function; MdN/dM is the abundance per unit natural logarithmic mass. For physical rather than comoving number density, multiply by (1+z)3. The normalization in the Press-Schechter formalism includes its factor of two relative to the positive Gaussian tail, so f(>M) tends to one as the smoothing mass tends to zero when σ diverges. It is a collapse prescription, not the probability that an arbitrary already nonlinear density field stays Gaussian.
