Take w constant and w=−1, with a barotropic perturbation δP=wδρ. Define the density contrast δ=δρ/ρˉ​ and use the scalar velocity potential convention vi​(k)=iki​θ(k), so ∂i​vi=−k2θ. This θ is a potential, not the sometimes-used velocity-divergence variable. In synchronous gauge in cosmology, the inverse metric is g00=−a−2 and gij=a−2(δij−hij) to first order. Discarding products of perturbations in the perfect fluid in general relativity gives
T00=a−2ρˉ​(1+δ),T0i=a−2(1+w)ρˉ​iki​θ,Tij=a−2wρˉ​[(1+δ)δij−hij].​
(17)
For the linearization, each hij​ must be small: the PDF's determinant condition alone is insufficient, since h=diag(L,0,0) has zero determinant even for large L.
Write H=a′/a. The time component of covariant conservation of stress-energy is
∂μ​T0μ+Γμν0​Tμν+Γμνμ​T0ν=0.
(18)
Here Γμ0μ​=4H+h′/2. To first order, Γij0​Tij=a−2wρˉ​[3H(1+δ)+h′/2]: the two metric-h contributions cancel. Multiplying the conservation equation by a2, including Γ000​T00, gives
ρˉ​′(1+δ)+ρˉ​δ′+3H(1+w)ρˉ​(1+δ)−(1+w)ρˉ​k2θ+21​(1+w)ρˉ​h′=0.
(19)
The background cosmological perfect-fluid continuity equation, ρˉ​′=−3H(1+w)ρˉ​, cancels the first and third terms. Thus
δ′−(1+w)k2θ+21​(1+w)h′=0.​
(20)
The h′ term accounts for perturbation of the spatial volume expansion in synchronous gauge in cosmology. The PDF's correct connection Γ0ji​=Hδji​+h′ij​/2 is garbled in the TeX aid.
