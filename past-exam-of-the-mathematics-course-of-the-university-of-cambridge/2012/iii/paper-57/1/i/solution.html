Use signature (−,+,+,+) and write hij​ for the spatial induced metric, Di​ for its spatial covariant derivative, and q2=hijDi​ϕDj​ϕ. The negative shift vector convention gives nμ=N−1(1,Ni) and Π=nμ∂μ​ϕ. Decomposing the scalar field derivative gives
∂μ​ϕ=−nμ​Π+Dμ​ϕ,gμν∂μ​ϕ∂ν​ϕ=−Π2+q2.
(1)
Varying the scalar field matter action with respect to the inverse metric tensor, including the variation of −g​, gives the stress-energy tensor
Tμν​=∂μ​ϕ∂ν​ϕ−gμν​[21​gαβ∂α​ϕ∂β​ϕ+V].​
(2)
Raising both indices gives the requested Tμν. Contracting with the unit normal twice, or once with the spatial projection tensor, gives
ρ=21​Π2+21​q2+V=N2T00,Ji​=−ΠDi​ϕ.​
(3)
Projecting both indices of the stress-energy tensor onto the spatial hypersurface gives
Sij​=Di​ϕDj​ϕ+hij​(21​Π2−21​q2−V),S=23​Π2−21​q2−3V,Sij​=Di​ϕDj​ϕ−31​hij​q2.​
(4)
Two printed identifications require correction. The canonical momentum of the displayed density is πϕ​=h​Π, not Π: explicitly Lm​=Nh​[Π2/2−q2/2−V] and differentiation with respect to ϕ˙​ supplies h​. Thus Π is the normal scalar-field momentum. Also, the printed trace-free stress has an extra factor 1/2 and uses δij​ where the general spatial induced metric requires hij​. In an orthonormal spatial frame the latter becomes δij​, but the coefficient is still one. For a gradient (q,0,0) in that frame, direct projection gives S11​=2q2/3, rather than the printed q2/3. The PDF's energy-density gradient is spatial ∂i​ϕ∂iϕ; the TeX aid's spacetime index there is an OCR defect.
