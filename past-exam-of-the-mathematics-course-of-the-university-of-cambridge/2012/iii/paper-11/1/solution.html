Here is a direct proof of the requested Lovász shadow bound, avoiding any unproved numerical interpolation of the Kruskal-Katona theorem. The real binomial coefficient means (rx​)=x(x−1)⋯(x−r+1)/r!. It is strictly increasing for x>r−1. Since the set family is nonempty and its size is an integer, necessarily x≥r.
Apply the usual left coordinate shifts of a set family until the set family is fixed by every shift.
