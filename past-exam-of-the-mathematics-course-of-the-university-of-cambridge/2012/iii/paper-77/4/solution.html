This is roll instability from broken up-down symmetry. A growing homogeneous amplitude perturbation already proves instability, without a sideband calculation. The source's slightly asymmetric setting means nonzero quadratic coupling. If γ=0 is allowed literally, the displayed leading equations instead have a continuous sphere of constant-total-intensity states and neutral orientation perturbations. They then establish marginality, not strict growing-mode instability; higher-order effects would decide further selection. Likewise α=0 would invalidate the assumed finite-kc​ triad scaling.
