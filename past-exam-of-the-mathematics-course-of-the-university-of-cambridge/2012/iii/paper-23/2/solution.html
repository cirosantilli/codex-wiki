At a point x∈X, all restrictions of the constant presheaf of sets are identities. Two representatives (U,t) and (V,t′) give the same germ precisely when t=t′, so its stalk is canonically T. The disjoint union of the stalks is therefore the set X×T.
The topology of the associated étale space of a presheaf has basic opens obtained from sections over open U. For the constant section t, that basic open is U×{t}. These are exactly the basic opens of the product topology with T discrete. Consequently the germ-space identification is a homeomorphism over X, and the projection
p:X×Tdisc​⟶X​
(13)
is a local homeomorphism.
A continuous section over V has the form x↦(x,f(x)), where f:V→Tdisc​ is continuous, equivalently locally constant. Its sections form the constant sheaf of sets:
ΔT(V)={f:V→T:f is locally constant}.​
(14)
The sheaf gluing axiom follows by gluing the functions; continuity is local. This includes ΔT(∅) being a singleton, even when the constant presheaf's value on the empty open set was T. The construction is its sheafification.
A function u:T→T′ acts by postcomposition on locally constant functions, defining a functor Δ:Set→Sh(X). It preserves identities and composition.
To prove the adjunction with the global sections functor, let F be a sheaf. A sheaf morphism λ:ΔT→F gives a function
T⟶F(X),t⟼λX​(constant function t).
(15)
Conversely, suppose global sections st​∈F(X) are prescribed for every t∈T. For a locally constant f:V→T, its fibres Vt​=f−1(t) are disjoint open sets covering V. Restrict st​ to Vt​ and glue these sections. They agree on intersections, which are empty, so unique gluing defines λV​(f). Restriction to smaller opens commutes with this construction, making λ a sheaf morphism.
Applying the two constructions successively returns the original data: a locally constant function is locally one of the constant functions, and sheaf morphisms respect those restrictions and unique gluing. Naturality in T and F follows from postcomposition and restriction. Hence
HomSh(X)​(ΔT,F)≅Set(T,ΓF),Δ⊣Γ.​
(16)
For an empty space the same proof works: all global sections sets are singletons, and the sheaf category is degenerate. Constant sheaves are generally locally constant rather than globally constant on disconnected opens.
