A linear regularization is a family of bounded operators Rα​:Y→X such that Rα​y→A†y for every y in the domain of the Moore–Penrose inverse of an operator as α↓0. For noisy data ∥yδ−y∥≤δ, a convergent regularization of an inverse problem also specifies a parameter choice α(δ,yδ) giving convergence to A†y, uniformly over the allowed noise at each fixed admissible exact datum.
