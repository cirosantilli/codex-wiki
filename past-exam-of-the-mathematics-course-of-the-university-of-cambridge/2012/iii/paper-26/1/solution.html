Put C=⋃r≥1​Q(μpr​), where μpr​ is the group of prth roots of unity. The compatible Galois groups of the cyclotomic fields give
Gal(C/Q)≅lim​r​(Z/prZ)×=Zp×​.
(1)
For odd p, the Teichmüller character gives
Zp×​=μp−1​×(1+pZp​).
(2)
The p-adic logarithm identifies 1+pZp​ with pZp​, which is topologically isomorphic to Zp​. More explicitly, a↦(1+p)a is an isomorphism from the additive p-adic integers onto 1+pZp​: the logarithm of 1+p has valuation one and generates pZp​. Take the fixed field of μp−1​ in C.
For p=2, use instead
Z2×​={±1}×(1+4Z2​).
(3)
The p-adic logarithm identifies the second factor with 4Z2​, and a↦5a identifies it with Z2​. The fixed field of {±1} in C is the union of the maximal real subfields of the cyclotomic fields of 2-power conductor. Thus in both cases the cyclotomic Zp-extension exists:
Q[p∞]=CΔp​,Gal(Q[p∞]/Q)≅Zp​,​
(4)
where Δp​=μp−1​ for odd p and Δ2​={±1}.
The closed subgroup pnZp​ is the unique subgroup of index pn, so the Galois correspondence supplies the unique degree-pn field Q[pn]. In the odd case it is the Δp​-fixed field in Q(μpn+1​); in the even case it is the real subfield of Q(μ2n+2​). These finite layers are all totally real number fields.
Now set A=Q[pn], B=Q[pm], and let HA​ be the ordinary Hilbert class field of A. Thus HA​/A is abelian, unramified at finite primes, and split at every real place, with degree h(A). If m=n there is nothing to prove. Otherwise the unique prime over p is totally ramified in B/A, by the permitted total-ramification fact and multiplicativity of the ramification index. Any nontrivial intermediate field of B/A is ramified at that prime. Hence
HA​∩B=A.
(5)
It follows that the two extensions are linearly disjoint field extensions, and
[BHA​:B]=[HA​:A]=h(A).
(6)
Unramified extensions remain unramified after base change. The real places also remain split: HA​ and B are totally real, so their compositum is totally real. Therefore BHA​/B lies inside the ordinary Hilbert class field HB​/B. Its degree divides [HB​:B]=h(B), proving
h(pn)∣h(pm).​
(7)
This is class number divisibility with split real places; using a class-field statement restricted to imaginary fields would unnecessarily omit the present real layers.
