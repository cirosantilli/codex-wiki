A field is irrotational when ∇×F=0. In R3 define
V(x)=−∫γ​F⋅dℓ,
(2)
where γ is any path from x0​ to x. The difference between two choices is the circulation around a closed loop. By the Stokes theorem it is the flux of ∇×F through a spanning surface and hence zero. Thus V is well-defined, V(x0​)=0, and differentiation gives F=−∇V.
If ∇×A0​=B, every solution is
A=A0​+∇χ

for an arbitrary differentiable scalar χ, since the difference is curl-free. A necessary condition is ∇⋅B=0, because the divergence of a curl vanishes.
