OurBigBook.com Words: 98
Extend a grid vector v by zero to the Dirichlet boundary. Pairing contributions along undirected nearest-neighbour edges gives the discrete energy identity
vTAv=−{r,s}∈E∑​(vr​−vs​)2,​
(21)
where E includes edges from an interior node to a boundary node. This is the three-dimensional version of summation by parts for the seven-point Dirichlet Laplacian.
The right side is nonpositive. If it vanishes, every pair of neighbouring values agrees; connectivity of the grid and the zero boundary values then imply v=0. Thus vTAv<0 for every nonzero v, so A is negative definite. In particular, zero is not an eigenvalue, and therefore
A is nonsingular.​
(22)
