A balanced category is one in which every morphism that is both a monomorphism and an epimorphism is an isomorphism. A faithful functor reflects monomorphisms and epimorphisms: cancellation after applying the functor can be pulled back by injectivity on hom-sets. Therefore, if F:C→D is faithful, C is balanced, and F(f) is an isomorphism, then f is both monic and epic and hence is an isomorphism. Thus F reflects isomorphisms.
Now let F:C⇄D:G be an adjunction with unit η and counit ε. Under the adjunction bijection
D(FX,FA)≅C(X,GFA),
(8)
the morphism Fu corresponds to ηA​u. If F is faithful and ηA​u=ηA​v, then Fu=Fv and hence u=v, so every ηA​ is monic. Conversely, if every ηB​ is monic and Fu=Fv for u,v:A→B, naturality gives
ηB​u=GF(u)ηA​=GF(v)ηA​=ηB​v,
(9)
and monicity gives u=v. This proves the faithful left adjoint criterion.
Assume next that C is balanced, every arrow in D factors as a strong epimorphism followed by a monomorphism, and both η and ε are pointwise monic. The unit criterion makes F faithful. The triangle identity
εFA​F(ηA​)=1FA​
(10)
makes the monomorphism εFA​ a split epimorphism, hence an isomorphism. Thus F(ηA​) is an isomorphism. The first paragraph shows that F reflects isomorphisms, so ηA​ is an isomorphism for every A. By the fully faithful adjoint criterion, F is full and faithful.
To prove closure under strong quotients, let q:FA↠B be a strong epimorphism. Naturality gives
εB​FG(q)=qεFA​.
(11)
The right side is a strong epimorphism, while εB​ is monic. The lifting property supplies s:B→FGB with
εB​s=1B​.
(12)
Since εB​ is also monic, it is an isomorphism. Hence B≅F(GB) lies in the essential image of F.
Conversely, assume F is full and faithful and its image is closed under strong quotients. Then η is an isomorphism and in particular pointwise monic. Factor a counit component as
FGBe​Cm​B,
(13)
with e strong epic and m monic. Closure under strong quotients gives C≅FA′ for some A′. After choosing this isomorphism, fullness writes e=Fh for a map h:GB→A′. Since e is epic and F is faithful, h is epic. The transpose of
εB​=mFh
(14)
is 1GB​, so
G(m)ηA′​h=1GB​.
(15)
Thus h is also monic. Balancedness makes h an isomorphism, hence e is an isomorphism and εB​=me is monic. This proves the pointwise-monic unit-and-counit criterion.
Balancedness is necessary. Let C be the two-element poset 0<1, viewed as a category, and let D be the terminal category. The unique F:C→D is left adjoint to the functor G selecting 1. Every morphism in a poset is monic, so the unit and counit are pointwise monic. But F is not full: the unique arrow F1→F0 has no preimage 1→0. This is the pointwise-monic adjunction over a non-balanced poset.
