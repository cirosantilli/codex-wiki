Linearizing the cosmological Euler equation gives
v′+Hv=−∇ϕ.
(41)
Taking the curl removes the gradient force, so the vorticity w=∇×v obeys
w′+Hw=0​.
(42)
Since H=a′/a, its solution is
w∝a−1​.
(43)
Thus expansion dilutes linear vorticity; in an Einstein-de Sitter universe, a∝τ2 and w∝τ−2.
