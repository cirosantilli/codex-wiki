∅=Y−1​⊂Y0​⊂⋯⊂Ym​=Y

K−1(Yr​)=0

and a short exact sequence whose new summand in K0(Yr​) is free abelian on the newly attached cells. Every such extension splits as an extension of free abelian groups, so K0(Y) is free, with one generator for each cell. This proves the Complex K-theory of an even-cell complex result.
The exterior product defines
K0(Y)⊗Ki(X)⟶Ki(Y×X).
(23)
For a point it is the identity. Attaching one layer of even cells gives corresponding exact sequences on the source and target; the sphere case is the suspension isomorphism, and induction with the Five lemma proves that the product map remains an isomorphism. This is the Künneth theorem for complex K-theory with an even-cell factor.
