Let p:P(E)→X be the projectivization of a real vector bundle, let LE​ be its tautological bundle, and put
u=w1​(LE​)∈H1(P(E);F2​).
(1)
The mod-two projective bundle formula says that H∗(P(E);F2​) is a free H∗(X;F2​)-module on 1,u,…,ud−1. The Projective bundle definition of Stiefel–Whitney classes is the unique relation
ud+p∗w1​(E)ud−1+⋯+p∗wd​(E)=0.
(2)
Apply the splitting principle for real vector bundles. After an injective pullback, write
E=L1​⊕⋯⊕Ld​,E′=L1′​⊕⋯⊕Ld′′​.
(3)
If xi​=w1​(Li​), the projective-bundle relation factors as
∏i=1d​(u+xi​)=0,
(4)
so
w(E)=∏i=1d​(1+xi​).
(5)
The line summands of E⊕E′ are the union of the two lists, hence
w(E⊕E′)=w(E)w(E′).
(6)
Comparing the degree-k components proves the Whitney product formula for Stiefel–Whitney classes
wk​(E⊕E′)=∑i+j=k​wi​(E)wj​(E′).
(7)
Injectivity of the splitting pullback returns the identity to X.
For real line bundles, the transition functions take values in O(1)={±1}. Tensor product multiplies these signs, while the identification {±1}≅Z/2 turns multiplication into addition. The corresponding degree-one characteristic classes therefore satisfy the First Stiefel–Whitney class of a tensor product of real line bundles formula
w1​(L⊗L′)=w1​(L)+w1​(L′).
(8)
Equivalently, this follows from the classification of real line bundles by H1(X;F2​).
Now take M=RPn and E=kγR1,n+1​. Since
E=γR1,n+1​⊗Rk,
(9)
a line in Ex​ is the fixed line γx​ tensored with a line in Rk. Thus the projectivization of copies of the real tautological line bundle is
P(E)≅RPn×RPk−1.
(10)
Let x and v be the degree-one generators pulled back from the first and second factors. The mod-two cohomology ring of real projective space and the Künneth theorem give
H∗(P(E);F2​)≅F2​[x,v]/(xn+1,vk).
(11)
The tautological line LE​ is the tensor product of the two tautological lines, so w1​(LE​)=x+v. In the alternative generator u=w1​(LE​), the same ring is
F2​[x,u]/(xn+1,(u+x)k).
(12)
The stable tangent-bundle identity
TRPn⊕1≅(n+1)γR1,n+1​
(13)
gives
w(TRPn)=(1+x)n+1.
(14)
For the vertical part of the tangent bundle of a projectivized real vector bundle,
1⊕Hom(LE​,ωE​)≅LE∗​⊗p∗E.
(15)
Each of the k line summands on the right has first Stiefel–Whitney class
w1​(LE∗​⊗p∗γ)=w1​(LE​)+x=v.
(16)
The Whitney product formula for Stiefel–Whitney classes therefore yields
w(TP(E))=(1+x)n+1(1+v)k,
(17)
the Total Stiefel–Whitney class of the projectivization of copies of the tautological line.
