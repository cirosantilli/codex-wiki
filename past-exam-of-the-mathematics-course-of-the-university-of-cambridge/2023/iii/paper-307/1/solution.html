The Kähler potential K(Φ,Φ†) is a real function integrated over all four fermionic coordinates, ∫d4θK. Its complex Hessian gives the scalar Kähler metric and therefore the kinetic terms. The superpotential W(Φ) is holomorphic and is integrated over chiral superspace, ∫d2θW+h.c.; its derivatives determine Yukawa couplings and the F-term scalar potential.
