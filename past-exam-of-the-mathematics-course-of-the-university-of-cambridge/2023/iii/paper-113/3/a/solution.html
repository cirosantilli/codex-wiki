An OX​-module F is a quasi-coherent sheaf when every affine open V=SpecA⊆X has F∣V​≅M for some A-module M. For a morphism f:X→Y, its direct image sheaf is
(f∗​F)(V)=F(f−1V),
(13)
while the pullback of a sheaf of modules is
f∗G=OX​⊗f−1OY​​f−1G.
(14)
If i:Z↪X is a closed immersion of Noetherian schemes, then on an affine open V=SpecA⊆X one has Z∩V=Spec(A/I). The restriction of i∗​OZ​ corresponds to the cyclic A-module A/I, so it is finitely generated. Hence i∗​OZ​ is a coherent sheaf; more generally this is the direct image of a coherent sheaf under a closed immersion.
Coherence need not survive an arbitrary pushforward. Let
j:D(x)=Speck[x,x−1]↪Speck[x]
(15)
be the open immersion. The sheaf OD(x)​ is coherent, but
Γ(A1,j∗​OD(x)​)=k[x,x−1]
(16)
is not a finitely generated k[x]-module. Therefore j∗​OD(x)​ is not coherent.
