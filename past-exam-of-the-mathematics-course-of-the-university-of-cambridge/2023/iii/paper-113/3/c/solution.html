Let the degree-d projective hypersurface Xd​ be defined by the homogeneous polynomial F. Multiplication by F identifies its ideal sheaf with OPn​(−d), giving the ideal-sheaf sequence of a projective hypersurface
0⟶OPn​(−d)⋅F​OPn​⟶i∗​OXd​​⟶0.
(20)
In particular, the kernel in the question is OPn​(−d).
For n=2, additivity of the Euler characteristic in this short exact sequence and the line-bundle formula
χ(P2,O(m))=(2m+2​)
(21)
give
h0(Xd​,OXd​​)−h1(Xd​,OXd​​)=χ(OXd​​)=1−(22−d​)=1−2(d−1)(d−2)​.
(22)
Here H2(Xd​,OXd​​)=0 by the preceding cohomological-dimension argument. Moreover H0(P2,O(−d))=H1(P2,O(−d))=0 for d>0, so the long exact sequence gives H0(Xd​,OXd​​)≅k. Consequently
h1(Xd​,OXd​​)=2(d−1)(d−2)​,
(23)
which is the genus-degree formula for a projective plane curve.
