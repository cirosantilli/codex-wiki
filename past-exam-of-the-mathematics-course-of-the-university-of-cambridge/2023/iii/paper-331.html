Write the Rayleigh equation for inviscid shear flow as
ϕ′′−α2ϕ−U−cU′′​ϕ=0,ϕ(0)=ϕ(1)=0.
(1)
Multiply by ϕ∗, integrate, and use integration by parts:
∫01​(∣ϕ′∣2+α2∣ϕ∣2)dz+∫01​∣U−c∣2U′′(U−c∗)​∣ϕ∣2dz=0.
(2)
Its imaginary part is
ci​∫01​∣U−c∣2U′′​∣ϕ∣2dz=0.
(3)
For an unstable mode ci​>0, the integral can vanish only if U′′ changes sign somewhere in the flow. Thus the velocity profile must have an inflection point. This is Rayleigh's inflection-point theorem; it is necessary, not sufficient, for inviscid instability.

Away from z=0,±1, the base profile is linear or constant, so U′′=0 and ϕ′′−α2ϕ=0. Across a corner z=z0​, integration of the Rayleigh equation gives the jump condition
(U(z0​)−c)[ϕ′]=[U′]ϕ.
(9)
For the even mode, at z=0 this gives
ϕ′(0+)=−1−cϕ(0)​.
(10)
For 0<z<1, therefore,
ϕ(z)=A[cosh(αz)−α(1−c)sinh(αz)​].
(11)
For z>1, decay requires ϕ∝e−α(z−1). The jump at z=1, where [U′]=1, gives
ϕ′(1−)=(c1​−α)ϕ(1).
(12)
Substitution and elementary simplification yield
2α2c2+α(1−2α−e−2α)c−[1−α−(1+α)e−2α]=0​.
(13)
The coefficients are real, so instability occurs when the quadratic discriminant is negative. At α=1 it is
(1+e−2)2−16e−2<0,
(14)
whereas at α=2, using e−4≃1/55, it is positive. By continuity there is a first threshold 1<αs​<2 at which the discriminant vanishes. Hence one conjugate root has ci​>0 for
1≤α<αs​<2​.
(15)

Dot the momentum equation with u, multiply the temperature equation by σRaθ, and integrate over V. The pressure term vanishes by incompressibility, while skew-symmetry of incompressible transport removes both nonlinear advection terms. Integration by parts and the boundary conditions give
dtdE​=σ∫V​[2Rawθ−∣∇u∣2−Ra∣∇θ∣2],dV​,
(21)

E=21​∫V​(∣u∣2+σRaθ2)dV.
(22)

dtdE​=−σ∫V​∣∇u∣2dV≤0,
(23)
so viscosity monotonically dissipates kinetic energy.

The linear normal-mode problem is
λu=−∇p+sigmaRaθz+σ∇2u,λθ=w+∇2θ,∇⋅u=0.
(25)
At λ=0 it is exactly the variational Euler--Lagrange system from part ii. Therefore the energy-stability threshold coincides with the stated linear neutral threshold Racrit​. After the pressure enforces incompressibility, let L denote the displayed linear operator. Integration by parts gives
⟨Φi​,LΦj​⟩=−σ∫V​∇ui​:∇uj​dV−σRa∫V​∇θi​⋅∇θj​dV+σRa∫V​(wi​θj​+θi​wj​)dV.
