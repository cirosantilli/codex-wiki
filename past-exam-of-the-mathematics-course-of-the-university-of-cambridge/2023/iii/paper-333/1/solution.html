f0​∣βy∣​,f0​∣ζ∣​,H0​∣hb​∣​,H0​∣η∣​≪1.
(1)
At leading order, geostrophic balance gives
ug​=(−ψy​,ψx​),ζ=∇2ψ,η=gf0​​ψ.
(2)
Expanding the reciprocal depth in the shallow-water potential vorticity gives
q=H0​−hb​+ηf0​+βy+ζ​≃H0​f0​​(1+f0​βy​+f0​∇2ψ​+H0​hb​​−gH0​f0​ψ​).
(3)
This is the stated shallow-water quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity Pg​. The term ∇2ψ is the parcel's relative vorticity, while −f02​ψ/(gH0​)=−ψ/LD2​ is vortex stretching caused by free-surface displacement, where
LD​=f0​gH0​​​
(4)
is the barotropic deformation radius.
For hb​=αy, omit the irrelevant constant factor f0​/H0​. The active PV anomaly is
Q=∇2ψ−LD2​ψ​+(β+H0​f0​α​)y.
(5)
Linearizing Qt​+J(ψ,Q)=0 about rest and using ψ∝ei(kx+ly−ωt) gives the Topographic Rossby-wave dispersion relation
ω=−k2+l2+LD−2​(β+f0​α/H0​)k​​.
(6)
A northward displacement raises planetary PV when β>0 and raises topographic PV when the bottom rises northward, α>0. PV conservation then requires anticyclonic relative vorticity, producing westward phase propagation. A negative slope opposes β and reverses propagation when f0​α/H0​<−β.
Under a rigid lid, h=H0​−hb​ is fixed but need not be close to H0​. With q=(f+∇2ψ)/h(y), linearization of Dq/Dt=0 gives
∂t​∇2ψ+(β−fhhy​​)ψx​=0​.
(7)
For h=H0​eγy and over a region where f≃f0​, plane waves obey
ω=−k2+l2(β−f0​γ)k​​.
(8)
The planetary-vorticity gradient dominates when ∣β∣≫∣f0​γ∣, while exponential depth variation dominates when ∣f0​γ∣≫∣β∣. If the variation of f=f0​+βy across the region is retained, this is the corresponding local WKB approximation with effective gradient β−fγ.
