A real (1, 1)-form is a form φ∈Ω1,1(X) satisfying φˉ​=φ. In holomorphic coordinates it has the form
φ=i∑j,k​hjkˉ​dzj∧dzˉk,hjkˉ​=hkjˉ​​​.
(19)
It is a positive real (1, 1)-form when
−iφ(ξ,ξˉ​)>0
(20)
for every nonzero tangent vector ξ of type (1,0), equivalently when the Hermitian matrix (hjkˉ​) is positive definite.
A holomorphic local trivialization of a holomorphic line bundle L→X is equivalently a nowhere-zero holomorphic local frame e. A connection A is unitary when it preserves the fiberwise Hermitian inner product:
dh(s,t)=h(∇As,t)+h(s,∇At).
(21)
The Chern connection is the unique unitary connection whose (0,1) part is the bundle's Dolbeault partial connection ∂ˉL​.
In a holomorphic frame, put h=h(e,e). The local formula for the Chern connection on a line bundle is
∇Ae=(∂logh)e,F(A)=∂ˉ∂logh.
(22)
The curvature therefore has type (1,1). Since a unitary connection has imaginary curvature, F(A)​=−F(A), and hence
iF(A)​=iF(A).
(23)
Thus iF(A) is a real (1,1)-form.
For connections A on L and A on L, the tensor product connection is defined on decomposable local sections by
∇A⊗A(s⊗s)=∇As⊗s+s⊗∇As.
(24)
The curvature of a tensor product connection on line bundles is additive:
F(A⊗A)=F(A)+F(A).
(25)
Consequently
iF(A⊗A)=iF(A)+iF(A),
(26)
which is positive whenever both summands are positive.
For the final assertion, simultaneously diagonalize the positive Hermitian matrices of φ and ψ by congruence at the chosen point. In the resulting coframe,
φ=i∑j​aj​dzj∧dzˉj,ψ=i∑j​bj​dzj∧dzˉj,aj​,bj​>0.
(27)
A direct wedge-product calculation gives
(φ∧ψ)(ξ,η,ξˉ​,ηˉ​)=∑j<k​(aj​bk​+ak​bj​)∣ξj​ηk​−ξk​ηj​∣2.
(28)
If ξ and η are linearly independent, at least one of these 2×2 minors is nonzero. Every coefficient is positive, so the sum is strictly positive. This is wedge positivity for two positive (1, 1)-forms.
