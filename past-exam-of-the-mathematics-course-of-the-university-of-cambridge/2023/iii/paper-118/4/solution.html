The Kähler manifold structure gives a Riemannian metric, its volume form, the complex orientation, and a Hermitian inner product on complex differential forms. The complex Hodge star operator is the complex-linear map characterized by
α∧∗βˉ​=⟨α,β⟩volg​.
(29)

d∗=(−1)m(r+1)+1∗d∗,
(30)
equivalently the formal adjoint of d for the L2 inner product. Similarly ∂ˉ∗ is the formal adjoint of ∂ˉ. Define the Hodge Laplacian and Dolbeault Laplacian by
Δd​=dd∗+d∗d,Δ∂ˉ​=∂ˉ∂ˉ∗+∂ˉ∗∂ˉ.

Expanding d=∂+∂ˉ, the Kähler identities make the mixed anticommutators vanish and imply Δ∂​=Δ∂ˉ​. Hence the Kähler Laplacian identity is
Δd​=2Δ∂ˉ​.
(32)

[L,Δ∂ˉ​]=0.
