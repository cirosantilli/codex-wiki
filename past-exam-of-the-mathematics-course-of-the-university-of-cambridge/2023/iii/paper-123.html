A modulus of a number field is a formal product
m=m0​m∞​,
(1)
where m0​=∏p​pnp​ is a nonzero integral ideal and m∞​ is a product of distinct real places. Only finitely many exponents np​ are nonzero.

Let L/K be a finite abelian extension and let m be an admissible modulus, so every ramified prime divides m0​ and the Artin reciprocity map
θL/K​:IK​(m)⟶Gal(L/K)
(2)
is defined. If p∤m0​, then the ideal-theoretic decomposition law says that its residue degree is
f(P/p)=ordθL/K​(p),
(3)
and, since p is unramified, it has
g(p)=ordθL/K​(p)[L:K]​
(4)
prime factors in L.
Indeed, θL/K​(p) is the Artin symbol, whose restriction to the residue field is x↦xNp. The Galois group of the finite residue-field extension is generated by this Frobenius automorphism and has order f(P/p). The fundamental identity efg=[L:K], with e=1, gives the formula for g.

Let fL/K​ be the conductor of an abelian extension and let m be divisible by it. For any σ∈Gal(L/K), the Chebotarev density theorem supplies infinitely many unramified prime ideals p with
(pL/K​)=σ.
(27)

The adele ring is the restricted product
AK​=∏v′​Kv​={(xv​)v​:xv​∈Ov​ for all but finitely many finite v}.
(30)
Addition and multiplication are componentwise. If xv​,yv​∈Ov​ outside finite sets, then xv​+yv​,xv​yv​∈Ov​ outside their union, so these operations are closed on AK​. The componentwise ring axioms make it a ring. Its restricted product topology has basic open sets ∏v​Uv​ with each Uv​⊆Kv​ open and Uv​=Ov​ at all but finitely many finite places.

Let G=Gal(L/K) and let
M=L[G,G]
(38)
be the fixed field of the commutator subgroup. Then M/K is the maximal abelian subextension of L/K, with
Gal(M/K)≅Gab.
(39)
The nonabelian norm-residue kernel theorem identifies the kernel of the global reciprocity map
CK​⟶Gab
(40)

NL/K​CL​=NM/K​CM​,
(41)
