For slow mass loss, the adiabatic invariance of an orbital action applies. Spherical symmetry conserves L exactly, and adiabatic evolution conserves the radial action. For a Kepler orbit,
Jr​+L=GMa​.
(13)
Consequently Ma and e remain constant. When the central mass halves, every semimajor axis doubles while each eccentricity is unchanged:
a1​=2a0​,e1​=e0​​.
(14)
No orbit becomes unbound as long as the final mass remains positive, so
funbound​=0​.
(15)
Because the action mapping changes no eccentricity and introduces no orientation preference, the initially isotropic distribution remains isotropic rather than becoming radially or tangentially anisotropic.
