Let (vn​) be a minimizing sequence. The coercive lower bound from part 2 makes it bounded in the harmonic-oscillator energy space and in L4. After taking a subsequence, vn​ converges weakly in both spaces. J(v)≤liminfn→∞​J(vn​).
