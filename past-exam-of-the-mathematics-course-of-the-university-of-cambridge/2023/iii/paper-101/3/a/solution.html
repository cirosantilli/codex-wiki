Zariski lemma says that a field which is a finitely generated algebra over a field k is a finite algebraic extension of k. The Strong Hilbert Nullstellensatz says that, for an ideal I⊆k[T1​,…,Tn​] over an algebraically closed field,
I(V(I))=I​.
(13)
Let the unique point of V(a) be x=(x1​,…,xn​) and let
m=(T1​−x1​,…,Tn​−xn​).
(14)
The Nullstellensatz gives a​=m, so a⊆m. Each of the finitely many generators ui​=Ti​−xi​ of m has some power uiei​​∈a. If
r=1+∑i​(ei​−1),
(15)
then every monomial of total degree r in the ui​ is divisible by one of the uiei​​. Hence
mr⊆a⊆m.
(16)
Thus the assertion is true; algebraically, the quotient defines a punctual scheme supported at x.
