The left cosets Γ∞​\Γ(1) correspond to primitive bottom rows (c,d)∈Z2, up to simultaneous sign. Put
r=2Res+k.
(37)

Im(γτ)=∣cτ+d∣2y​,j(γ,τ)=cτ+d

show that the absolute value of a summand is
yRes∣cτ+d∣−r.
(39)
The hypothesis Res>(2−k)/2 says precisely that r>2.
If τ ranges over a compact subset K⊂h, the positive-definite quadratic form ∣cτ+d∣2 has a uniform lower bound
∣cτ+d∣2≥CK​(c2+d2)
(40)
for some CK​>0. The summands are therefore bounded uniformly on K by a constant times
(c2+d2)−r/2.
(41)
The corresponding two-dimensional lattice sum converges for r>2. The Weierstrass M-test proves absolute and locally uniform convergence. This is the absolute convergence of a weight-k real-analytic Eisenstein series.
