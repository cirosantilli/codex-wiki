A subcritical droplet must make a rare thermal fluctuation from the metastable basin over the free-energy barrier F∗. Its probability carries the Boltzmann factor e−F∗/(kB​T), so the nucleation rate has the Arrhenius form
Γ∼Γ0​e−F∗/(kB​T).
(45)
For a volume containing order one candidate subcritical droplet, the waiting time for a supercritical droplet and subsequent macroscopic growth is therefore
τ∼τ0​eF∗/(kB​T),​
(46)
up to an algebraic kinetic prefactor. This is the Arrhenius nucleation time.
