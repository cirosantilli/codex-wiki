Choose complete orthonormal sets of complex Klein-Gordon solutions {uiin​} and {uiout​} that have positive frequency with respect to the asymptotic timelike Killing fields in the remote past and future. Their normalization uses the conserved Klein-Gordon inner product:
(ui​,uj​)KG​=δij​,(ui∗​,uj∗​)KG​=−δij​,(ui​,uj∗​)KG​=0.
(46)
The real quantum field has either mode expansion of a free field
ϕ=∑i​(aiin​uiin​+aiin†​uiin∗​)=∑i​(aiout​uiout​+aiout†​uiout∗​).
(47)
Completeness relates the mode bases by the Bogoliubov transformation
uiout​=∑j​(αij​ujin​+βij​ujin∗​),
(48)
and hence
aiout​=∑j​(αij∗​ajin​−βij∗​ajin†​).
(49)
Preservation of the canonical commutation relations requires
αα†−ββ†=I,αβT=βαT.
(50)
The past and future notions of positive frequency therefore define the generally different in-vacuum and out-vacuum. Nonzero β means that the in-vacuum is a squeezed state containing out-particles.
