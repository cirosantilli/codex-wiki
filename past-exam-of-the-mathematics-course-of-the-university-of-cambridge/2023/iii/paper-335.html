Let (σj​,uj​,vj​) be a singular system of a compact operator, with
Avj​=σj​uj​,A∗uj​=σj​vj​.

(47)
defined when the Picard criterion holds, with the component in kerA∗ sent to zero. It is the minimum-norm least-squares solution of Af=g.
A regularization of an inverse problem consists of bounded maps Rα​:Y→X and a parameter rule α=α(δ,g(δ)) such that
α→0,Rα​g(δ)→A†g

(49)

Rα​g(δ)=(A∗A+αI)−1A∗g(δ)=∑j​σj2​+ασj​​⟨g(δ),uj​⟩vj​.
