i OurBigBook.com
For an affine algebraic group G, its Lie algebra is the tangent space at the identity,
g=Te​G.
(1)
Equivalently, it is the space of left-invariant derivations of the coordinate ring C[G]. The Lie bracket is the commutator of derivations,
[X,Y]=XY−YX.
(2)
It is antisymmetric because [Y,X]=−[X,Y]. Associativity of composition gives
[X,[Y,Z]]+[Y,[Z,X]]+[Z,[X,Y]]=0
(3)
after all six triple products cancel in pairs, proving the Jacobi identity. This construction is the Lie algebra of an affine algebraic group.

The finite-dimensional irreducible rational representations of SL2​(C) are
Vn​=Symn(C2),n≥0,
(5)
of dimension n+1. The central element −I acts on Vn​ by (−1)n. Therefore precisely the even-indexed representations
V2m​,m≥0,
(6)

A bilinear form B on a Lie algebra is invariant when
B([x,y],z)=B(x,[y,z]),
(7)
equivalently B([x,y],z)+B(y,[x,z])=0.
Choose a basis (xi​) of g and its B-dual basis (xi). The Casimir element is
Ω=∑i​xi​xi∈U(g),
(8)
where U(g) is the universal enveloping algebra. The tensor ∑i​xi​⊗xi corresponds under B:g≅g∗ to the identity endomorphism, so invariance of B makes it fixed by the diagonal adjoint action. Applying multiplication U(g)⊗U(g)→U(g) gives
[x,Ω]=0
(9)
for every x∈g. Thus Ω lies in the center of an associative algebra of U(g).

e=(00​10​),f=(01​00​),h=(10​0−1​)

Ω=ef+fe+21​h2.
(11)
On a highest-weight vector v in the (n+1)-dimensional irreducible module, ev=0 and hv=nv. Since efv=[e,f]v=nv and fev=0,
Ωv=(n+2n2​)v=2n(n+2)​v.
(12)

The Cartan solvability criterion states that a finite-dimensional complex Lie algebra g is solvable exactly when
κ(g,[g,g])=0,
(13)
where κ is the Killing form. In the matrix form of the criterion, a Lie subalgebra g⊆gl(V) is solvable if
tr(xy)=0
(14)
for all x∈[g,g] and y∈g.

A torus t in a Lie algebra is an abelian subalgebra whose elements act semisimply in the Adjoint representation. Its weight-space decomposition is
g=⨁γ∈t∗​gγ​,gγ​={x:[t,x]=γ(t)x for every t∈t}.
(15)
Invariance of the nondegenerate Trace form of a Lie algebra representation gives
(gγ​,gδ​)V​=0unlessγ+δ=0.
(16)
Nondegeneracy therefore forces β=−α. The standard sl2 subalgebra associated with a root argument gives one-dimensional opposite root spaces with vectors e∈gα​, f∈g−α​ and hα​∈t satisfying
[hα​,e]=2e,[hα​,f]=−2f,[e,f]=hα​.
(17)
Their span is a copy of sl2​.
Set
h=ker(α:t→C).
(18)
Then dimh=dimt−1, t=Chα​⊕h, and every element of h commutes with e, f, and t. Hence h is abelian and
g=sl2​⊕h
(19)
as a direct sum of commuting Lie algebras. This is the two-root decomposition with a nondegenerate trace form.

Write an element of the diagonal torus as
diag(t1​,…,tn​,−tn​,…,−t1​)
(22)
and let εi​ extract ti​. The root-space decomposition is
so2n​=t⊕⨁1≤i<j≤n​(gεi​−εj​​⊕gεi​+εj​​⊕g−εi​+εj​​⊕g−εi​−εj​​),

R={±εi​±εj​:i<j}.
(24)
The upper-triangular choice gives
R+={εi​−εj​, εi​+εj​:i<j}.
(25)
A compatible simple system is
αi​=εi​−εi+1​(1≤i<n),αn​=εn−1​+εn​.
(26)
The highest root and Weyl vector are
θ=ε1​+ε2​=α1​+2α2​+⋯+2αn−2​+αn−1​+αn​,ρ=∑i=1n​(n−i)εi​.
(27)
The fundamental weights are
ωk​=ε1​+⋯+εk​(1≤k≤n−2),

ωn−1​=21​(ε1​+⋯+εn−1​−εn​),ωn​=21​(ε1​+⋯+εn​).

Let V=C4. The Special linear Lie algebra sl(V) acts on Λ2V. The wedge product
Λ2V×Λ2V⟶Λ4V≅C
(32)
is a nondegenerate symmetric bilinear form, and the action preserves it because sl(V) acts trivially on Λ4V. This gives an injective homomorphism
sl4​⟶so(Λ2V)≅so6​.
(33)
Both Lie algebras have dimension 15, so the map is an isomorphism. This realizes the Isomorphism between so6 and sl4.

Choose a short simple root α1​ and a long simple root α2​. The G2 root system has positive roots
α1​, α2​, α1​+α2​, 2α1​+α2​, 3α1​+α2​, 3α1​+2α2​.
(39)
The two hexagons formed by the short and long roots give the usual twelve-root diagram. The fundamental weights are
ω1​=2α1​+α2​,ω2​=3α1​+2α2​.
(40)
The second is the highest root, so the irreducible module L(ω2​) is the Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra.
The seven weights of L(ω1​) are zero and the six short roots, each with multiplicity one. Its crystal, with arrows denoting the lowering operators f​i​, is the chain
ω1​1​α1​+α2​2​α1​1​01​−α1​2​−(α1​+α2​)1​−ω1​.

For type Bn​, the spinor representation has highest weight
ωn​=21​(ε1​+⋯+εn​)
(43)
and its weights are the 2n sign vectors
21​∑j=1n​ij​εj​,ij​∈{±1}.
(44)
Each weight has multiplicity one. Along the simple root αj​=εj​−εj+1​, the Kashiwara operator ej​ can raise a weight exactly when (ij​,ij+1​)=(−1,+1), when it replaces that pair by (+1,−1). For the short root αn​=εn​, en​ replaces a final −1 by +1. This proves the stated crystal by the root-string property of a crystal.
For n=3, the complete list of raising edges is
(−,−,−)3​(−,−,+)2​(−,+,−),(−,+,−)1​(+,−,−),(−,+,−)3​(−,+,+),(+,−,−)3​(+,−,+),(−,+,+)1​(+,−,+),(+,−,+)2​(+,+,−)3​(+,+,+).​
(45)
The tensor product of crystals has four highest-weight connected components, of highest weights
0,ω1​,ω2​,2ω3​.
(46)
Consequently, for the eight-dimensional spin representation S of so7​,
S⊗S≅C⊕V(ω1​)⊕V(ω2​)⊕V(2ω3​),
(47)
with dimensions
64=1+7+21+35.
(48)
Equivalently these summands are Λk(C7) for 0≤k≤3.
