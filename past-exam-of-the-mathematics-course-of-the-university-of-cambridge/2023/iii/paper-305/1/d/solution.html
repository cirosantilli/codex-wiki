For a null momentum qμ, impose transversality
qμ​ϵμ(q,λ)=0.
(10)
This removes one of four vector components. The remaining equivalence
ϵμ∼ϵμ+αqμ
(11)
removes a second component, leaving two transverse polarizations. For momentum along the third axis they may be chosen as
ϵ±μ​=2​1​(0,1,±i,0),
(12)
which carry helicity ±1.
The equivalence is a gauge redundancy: vectors that differ by a multiple of qμ describe the same physical state rather than distinct measurable configurations. A Lorentz transformation of a chosen transverse representative can require a compensating gauge transformation. Therefore the amplitude
M=ϵμ​Mμ
(13)
must be invariant under ϵμ​↦ϵμ​+αqμ​. For arbitrary α, this is exactly the Ward identity
qμ​Mμ=0.​
(14)
