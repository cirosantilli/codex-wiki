a OurBigBook.com
Because X and Y are independent random variables, conditioning on Y merely translates X. The conditional entropy under a deterministic change of variables and the fact that conditioning reduces entropy therefore give
H(X+Y)≥H(X+Y∣Y)=H(X∣Y)=H(X).
(1)
Interchanging X and Y similarly gives H(X+Y)≥H(Y), and hence
H(X+Y)≥max{H(X),H(Y)}.
(2)
The proof applies to countable alphabets whenever the displayed information entropies are finite.

For the direct half of the code-distribution correspondence, let L be the length function of a binary prefix code and put
K=∑x​2−L(x)≤1,R(x)=K2−L(x)​.
(21)
Then R is a probability mass function and
−log2​R(x)=L(x)+log2​K≤L(x).
(22)
Thus every prefix code determines a distribution whose ideal description lengths do not exceed the codeword lengths.
Conversely, given a probability mass function R, set
L(x)=⌈−log2​R(x)⌉
(23)
for R(x)>0. Then 2−L(x)≤R(x), so the Kraft inequality is satisfied. Its converse supplies a binary prefix code with these lengths, and
−log2​R(x)≤L(x)<−log2​R(x)+1.
(24)
If real lengths are allowed, the ideal choice L(x)=−log2​R(x) satisfies Kraft with equality.

Put pi​=P(Xi​=1) and λ=∑i​pi​. A relative-entropy form of the Poisson approximation bound for dependent Bernoulli variables is
De​(PSn​​∥Poisson(λ))≤∑i=1n​pi2​+∑i=1n​He​(Xi​)−He​(X1​,…,Xn​).
(30)
The last two terms form the total correlation; they vanish when the Bernoulli variables are independent.
Here De​ and He​ use natural logarithms.
To prove the bound, let Qi​ be the Poisson distribution with mean pi​ and let Q=⨂i​Qi​. Expanding the Kullback-Leibler divergence against this product law gives
De​(PX1n​​∥Q)=∑i​De​(Bernoulli(pi​)∥Poisson(pi​))+∑i​He​(Xi​)−He​(X1n​).
(31)
The supplied one-dimensional estimate bounds the first sum by ∑i​pi2​. Under the addition map, PX1n​​ becomes PSn​​, while the Sum of independent Poisson random variables under Q has the Poisson distribution with mean λ. The data processing inequality for relative entropy proves the displayed result.
If a bound directly in the paper's unhalved total-variation norm is desired, Pinsker's inequality also gives
∥PSn​​−Poisson(λ)∥1​≤2{i∑​pi2​+i∑​He​(Xi​)−He​(X1n​)}​.​
