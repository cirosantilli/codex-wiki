The symmetric group acts transitively on the Young tableaux of a fixed shape. Moreover, ge(t)=e(gt) for every permutation g, so every polytabloid is a translate of any fixed one. Hence the Specht module is a cyclic module generated by e(t).
It remains to see that e(t)=0. In its expansion, the coefficient of the tabloid {t} is one: if π∈C(t) and {πt}={t}, then π∈R(t)∩C(t)={1}. Thus the generator, and therefore the module, is nonzero.

Write the transposition as τ=rc with r∈R(t) and c∈C(t). If x is not one of the two entries moved by τ, then r(c(x))=x. The entries x and c(x) lie in one column of t, while c(x) and r(c(x))=x lie in one row. A Young diagram has only one cell at the intersection of a specified row and column, so c(x)=x and then r(x)=x.
Consequently both r and c fix every entry outside the support of τ. On the two remaining entries each is either the identity or their transposition. They cannot both transpose them, since then rc=1, and they cannot both be the identity. Exactly one of r,c is therefore τ, proving that τ lies in exactly one of R(t) and C(t).

Use a beta set X on an e-runner partition abacus. A hook of length divisible by e corresponds to a bead x and a gap x−eh on the same runner. Divide both runner positions by e. They become a bead and a gap at distance h in the runner partition λ(s), and hence determine a hook there. This gives the required bijection, with ∣H∣=e∣f(H)∣.
Removing H replaces the bead x by the gap x−eh. On its runner this is exactly the bead move that removes f(H), while every other runner is unchanged. Thus hook removal commutes with the construction and
Qe​(λ∖H)=Qe​(λ)∖f(H).
(20)
This is the abacus divisible-hook correspondence.

Let bt​ be the Column antisymmetrizer of a Young tableau. For a tabloid {u}, if two entries from one column of t lie in one row of u, their column transposition fixes {u} and pairs every term of bt​{u} with its negative. Thus bt​{u}=0.
Otherwise each row of u meets each column of t at most once. Matching entries within columns then gives a column permutation c∈C(t) for which {cu}={t}. Reindexing the antisymmetrizer gives bt​{u}=sgn(c)e(t). Hence every basis tabloid maps into Fe(t), while bt​{t}=e(t), and therefore
bt​Mλ=Fe(t).​
