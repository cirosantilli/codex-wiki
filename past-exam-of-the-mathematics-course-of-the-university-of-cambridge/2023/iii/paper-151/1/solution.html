A group extension of G by the ZG-module M is an exact sequence
1⟶Mi​Eπ​G⟶1
(1)
whose conjugation action on i(M) agrees with the prescribed action of G on M. It is a split group extension when π has a group-homomorphic section s:G→E. Two such extensions are equivalent group extensions when an isomorphism of their middle groups is the identity on M and induces the identity on G. Transporting a section through that isomorphism proves that every extension equivalent to a split extension is split.
Choose a set-theoretic section s with s(1)=1. Its failure to preserve multiplication is the normalized two-cocycle
c(g,h)=s(g)s(h)s(gh)−1∈M.
(2)
Associativity gives the two-cocycle identity, and replacing s changes c by a group coboundary. The resulting class [c]∈H2(G,M) is therefore intrinsic to the extension, as expressed by second group cohomology classifies group extensions.
Now write G=⟨x,y⟩≅Z2 and let I be the augmentation ideal of ZG. The Koszul resolution for a rank-two free abelian group gives, after applying HomZG​(−,M), the last coboundary
M2⟶M,(a,b)⟼(1−y)a+(x−1)b.
(3)
Its image is IM. For M=ZG/I2 this yields the second cohomology of a rank-two free abelian group with truncated group-ring coefficients calculation
H2(G,M)=M/IM≅(ZG/I2)/(I/I2)≅ZG/I≅Z.
(4)
The canonical map M→Z induces the identity on these final quotients, so H2(G,M)→H2(G,Z) is surjective; indeed it is an isomorphism.
Let F2​=⟨x,y⟩ and let γi​(F2​) be its lower central series. The class-two quotient F2​/γ3​(F2​) is the Integer Heisenberg group. In the class-three free nilpotent group N=F2​/γ4​(F2​), the module γ2​(F2​)/γ4​(F2​) is cyclic over ZG on [x,y] and is isomorphic to ZG/I2. Quotienting it by I/I2 gives the central kernel ⟨[x,y]⟩≅Z of the Heisenberg group. The kernel of
N⟶F2​/γ3​(F2​)
(5)
is γ3​(F2​)/γ4​(F2​), freely generated by [[x,y],x] and [[x,y],y], and is central. Thus it is Z2. This is the central nonsplit extension of the integer Heisenberg group by Z2. If it split, centrality would give N≅H(Z)×Z2, whose abelianization has rank four; but N has abelianization Z2. Hence the extension is nonsplit.
