ωn​=21​(ε1​+⋯+εn​)

and its weights are the 2n sign vectors
21​∑j=1n​ij​εj​,ij​∈{±1}.

For the short root αn​=εn​, en​ replaces a final −1 by +1. This proves the stated crystal by the root-string property of a crystal.
