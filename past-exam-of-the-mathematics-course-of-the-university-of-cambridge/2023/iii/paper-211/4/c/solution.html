Define the risk-neutral claim value
V(t,s)=e−r(T−t)∫R​g(se(r−σ2/2)(T−t)+σT−t​z)ϕ(z)dz.
(22)
Then V(0,S0​)=x, V(T,s)=g(s), and the Black-Scholes equation holds. Differentiation under the integral gives the delta
∂s​V(t,s)=e−rτE[g′(se(r−σ2/2)τ+στ​Z)e(r−σ2/2)τ+στ​Z],τ=T−t.
(23)
A Gaussian shift Z↦Z+στ​ rewrites this as
∂s​V(t,s)=∫R​g′(se(r+σ2/2)τ+στ​z)ϕ(z)dz,
(24)
which is exactly the stated θt​ at s=St​.
Apply Itô formula to V(t,St​). The PDE gives
dV(t,St​)=r{V−θt​St​}dt+θt​dSt​.
(25)
This is the same wealth equation as part a, with the same initial value x. Uniqueness therefore gives Xtx,θ​=V(t,St​) and hence
XTx,θ​=V(T,ST​)=g(ST​).​
(26)
