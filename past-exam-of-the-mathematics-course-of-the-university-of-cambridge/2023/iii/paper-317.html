Let
δ=−(∂logT∂logρ​)P,μ​,ϕ=(∂logμ∂logρ​)P,T​,∇μ​=dlogPdlogμ​.
(50)
A displaced fluid element remains in pressure balance, changes temperature adiabatically, and retains its composition. Comparing its density with the environment after an upward displacement gives the Ledoux criterion for stability,
∇<∇ad​+δϕ​∇μ​​.
(51)
The reverse inequality causes convection. For uniform composition, or if composition is ignored, ∇μ​=0 and this reduces to the Schwarzschild criterion
∇<∇ad​​
(52)
for stability. A molecular weight increasing inward has ∇μ​>0 and stabilizes the stratification.
