y′=λy.
(135)

yn+1​=R(z)yn​,z=hλ,
(136)

S={z∈C:∣R(z)∣≤1}​.
(137)

{z:Rez≤0}⊆S​,
(138)
so it does not amplify any mode that the exact differential equation does not amplify.
