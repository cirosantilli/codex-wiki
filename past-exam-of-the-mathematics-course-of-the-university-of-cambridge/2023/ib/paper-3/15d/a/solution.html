The magnetic field is B=∇×A. The magnetostatic Ampère-Maxwell equation is ∇×B=μ0​J, so the curl of the curl identity gives
∇(∇⋅A)−∇2A=μ0​J.
(112)
A gauge transformation A↦A+∇χ does not change B. We may therefore choose the Coulomb gauge ∇⋅A=0 by solving the Poisson equation ∇2χ=−∇⋅A. It follows component by component in a Cartesian coordinate system that
∇2A=−μ0​J​.
(113)
The free-space Green function of the Laplacian then gives
A(r)=4πμ0​​∫R3​∣r−r′∣J(r′)​d3r′​.
(114)
For a localized steady current density, conservation of electric charge gives ∇′⋅J=0; integration by parts confirms directly that this integral has zero divergence and hence obeys the Coulomb gauge.
