A bilinear form B on V is nondegenerate when
B(v,w)=0for every v∈V⟹w=0.
(1)
In finite dimensions this is equivalent to nondegeneracy in the first argument.
Define the linear map
β1​:V⟶V∗,β1​(w)=B1​(−,w).
(2)
Nondegeneracy makes β1​ injective. Since V and its dual space have the same finite dimension, β1​ is an isomorphism. Similarly define β2​(w)=B2​(−,w) and set
α=β1−1​β2​​.
(3)
Then α is linear and
B1​(v,αw)=β1​(αw)(v)=β2​(w)(v)=B2​(v,w).
(4)
If w∈kerα, this identity gives B2​(v,w)=0 for every v. Conversely, if B2​(v,w)=0 for every v, then B1​(v,αw)=0 for every v, so nondegeneracy gives αw=0. Hence
{w∈V:B2​(v,w)=0 for every v∈V}=kerα​.
(5)
This is the representation of a bilinear form relative to a nondegenerate bilinear form.
