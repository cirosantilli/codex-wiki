Write the equally spaced one-particle spectrum as
En​=E0​+nΔ.
(375)
The two-particle level labelled by N has energy
EN(2)​=2E0​+NΔ
(376)
and receives contributions from orbital pairs (n1​,n2​) with n1​+n2​=N.
For every unequal unordered pair n1​<n2​, there is one symmetric and one antisymmetric spatial combination. The symmetric one combines with the six-dimensional symmetric spin space; the antisymmetric one combines with the three-dimensional antisymmetric spin space. Thus each unequal pair contributes nine states.
If N is odd, all pairs are unequal and there are (N+1)/2 unordered pairs. Therefore
gN​=29(N+1)​,N odd.​
(377)
If N is even, there are N/2 unequal pairs and the additional equal pair n1​=n2​=N/2. The latter has only a symmetric spatial state and therefore contributes six spin states. Hence
gN​=92N​+6,N even.​
(378)
This is the degeneracy of two identical spin-one bosons with equally spaced orbital levels.
