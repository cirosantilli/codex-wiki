Let L=Q(ζn​). We use the standard cyclotomic containment lemma: every root of unity in Q(ζn​) has order dividing lcm(2,n). One proof uses the conductor of a rational cyclotomic field. The conductor attached to a primitive mth root is m, except that it is m/2 when m≡2(mod4); containment in Q(ζn​) forces this conductor to divide n, which gives m∣lcm(2,n).
If n is even, lcm(2,n)=n, and the powers of ζn​ already give all n possible roots of unity in L. If n is odd, −ζn​ has order 2n, so L contains all 2nth roots of unity, and the containment lemma shows there are no others. Thus the roots of unity in a rational cyclotomic field number
{n,2n,​n even,n odd.​​
