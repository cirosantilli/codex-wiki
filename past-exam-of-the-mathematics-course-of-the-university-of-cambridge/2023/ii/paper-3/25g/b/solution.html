A plane has both principal curvatures zero, hence K=H=0. A circular cylinder of radius R has principal curvatures 0 and ±R−1, hence
K=0,∣H∣=2R1​>0.

Because the absolute value of mean curvature is preserved even if one reverses the chosen orientation, no Euclidean motion can carry any open piece of the cylinder to an open piece of the plane. Thus the condition K≡0 does not characterize planar pieces.
