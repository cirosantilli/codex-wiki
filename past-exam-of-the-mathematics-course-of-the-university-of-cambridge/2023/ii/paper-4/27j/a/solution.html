A Non-homogeneous Poisson point process with intensity function λ is a random counting measure N such that, for every finite family of pairwise disjoint bounded Borel sets A1​,…,Ak​, the counts N(A1​),…,N(Ak​) are independent random variables and
N(Ai​)∼Poisson(∫Ai​​λ(x)dx).
(284)
The assumed local integrability makes each Poisson parameter finite. This count law and independence characterize the process.
