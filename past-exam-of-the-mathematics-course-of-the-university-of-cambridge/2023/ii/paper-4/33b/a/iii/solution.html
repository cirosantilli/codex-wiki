∣J,J⟩=∣j1​,j1​⟩1​∣j2​,j2​⟩2​.

The orthonormal vector in the same two-dimensional m=J−1 subspace is
∣J−1,J−1⟩=Jj2​​​,∣j1​,j1​−1⟩1​∣j2​,j2​⟩2​−Jj1​​​,∣j1​,j1​⟩1​∣j2​,j2​−1⟩2​.
(353)
