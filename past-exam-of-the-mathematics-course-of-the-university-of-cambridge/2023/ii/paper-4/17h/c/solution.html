⋃i∈S​Ai​⊆⋃j∈N(S)​Bj​.
(164)
Using finite additivity of Lebesgue measure and the equal-volume hypotheses,
k∣S∣​=vol(⋃i∈S​Ai​)≤vol(⋃j∈N(S)​Bj​)=k∣N(S)∣​.
(165)
Hence ∣N(S)∣≥∣S∣ for every S. The Hall marriage theorem supplies a perfect matching, which has the form Ai​Bσ(i)​ for a permutation σ of [k]. Every matched edge means precisely that Ai​∩Bσ(i)​=∅.
