For the standard dot product on F2n​, the dual code is
C⊥={y∈F2n​:y⋅c=0 for every c∈C}.​
(19)
It is an intersection of kernels of linear functions, and is therefore a linear code.
Let σ denote cyclic right shift. If y∈C⊥ and c∈C, then
(σy)⋅c=y⋅(σ−1c).
(20)
A cyclic code is closed under both σ and σ−1, so σ−1c∈C and the right-hand side vanishes. Hence σy∈C⊥, proving directly that the dual of a cyclic code is cyclic.
Identify words with polynomials in F2​[X]/(Xn−1). If the generator polynomial of a cyclic code is the monic divisor g(X) and
g(X)h(X)=Xn−1,
(21)
then the generator polynomial of C⊥ is the monic reciprocal
g⊥(X)=h∗(X)=Xdeghh(X−1)/h(0).​
(22)
Equivalently, the parity-check polynomial of one code becomes, after reversal, the generator polynomial of its dual.
