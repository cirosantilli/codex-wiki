For X=x, the last flip must be the rth head. Among the preceding x+r−1 flips, exactly x are tails and r−1 are heads. There are (xx+r−1​) choices for their positions, and every resulting sequence has probability (1−p)xpr. The negative binomial stopping argument therefore gives
P(X=x)=(xx+r−1​)(1−p)xpr​,x=0,1,…,

so X has the failures-before-the-rth-success negative binomial distribution.
To put the mass function into exponential family form, set
θ=log(1−p),−∞<θ<0.
(42)

Pθ​(X=x)=(xx+r−1​)exp{θx+rlog(1−eθ)}=h(x)exp{θT(x)−A(θ)},
