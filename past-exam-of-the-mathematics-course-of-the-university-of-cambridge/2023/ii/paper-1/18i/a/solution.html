A finite extension L/K is normal when every irreducible polynomial in K[T] having one root in L splits completely over L.
Suppose first that L/K is normal. Choose α1​,…,αm​ with L=K(α1​,…,αm​), and let fj​ be the minimal polynomial of αj​ over K. Every fj​ splits in L by normality, and L is generated by their roots because it is already generated by the particular roots αj​. Thus L is the splitting field of f1​⋯fm​.
Conversely, suppose that L is the splitting field of f∈K[T]. Every K-embedding σ:L→K permutes the roots of f, so σ(L)=L. By the embedding characterization of normality, L/K is normal. This proves the finite normal extension as a splitting field criterion.
