Put α=47​. The roots of T4−7 are α,iα,−α,−iα, so its splitting field is
L=Q(α,i).
(186)
The polynomial is irreducible over Q by the Eisenstein criterion at 7, and Q(α)⊂R does not contain i. The tower law for field extensions therefore gives [L:Q]=8.
Define automorphisms
r(α)=iα,quadr(i)=i,qquads(α)=α,quads(i)=−i.

Finally, the normal subextension criterion says that normal intermediate fields correspond exactly to normal subgroups.
