Write
Y=μ+ε,Y∗=μ+ε∗,
(121)
where ε and ε∗ are independent, centered, and have covariance matrix σ2In​. Then
HY−Y∗=−(I−H)μ+Hε−ε∗.
(122)
The deterministic term is orthogonal in expectation to the two centered random terms, and the random terms are independent. Since the hat matrix is a symmetric idempotent projection of rank p,
E∥Hε∥2=σ2tr(HTH)=σ2trH=pσ2,
(123)
while E∥ε∗∥2=nσ2. Therefore
E∥HY−Y∗∥2=∥(I−H)μ∥2+(n+p)σ2​.
(124)
In this bias-variance decomposition for linear prediction, ∥(I−H)μ∥2 is squared model bias, pσ2 is variance from fitting p coefficients, and nσ2 is irreducible noise in the future response. Enlarging the model tends to reduce the first term while increasing the fitted-model variance, which is the bias-variance tradeoff.
