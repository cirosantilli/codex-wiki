A set A⊆W is a Sigma-1 set when it is the domain of a partial computable function: there is a partial computable f such that
w∈A⟺f(w)↓​.
(110)
Equivalently, w∈A iff ∃tR(w,t) for some computable predicate R; one may take R(w,t) to say that the truncated computation function observes a halt within t steps.
