Part (b) gives paths with the same endpoint z but integral values
H(z),H(z)+4K,H(z)+2iL.
(112)
Applying the local inverse and then continuing it shows that
H(w+4K)=H(w),H(w+2iL)=H(w).
(113)
Since K,L>0, the first period is real and the second is purely imaginary, so they are linearly independent over R. Hence H is an elliptic function with two periods
4Kand2iL​.
(114)
Indeed, it is the Jacobi elliptic sine, and these generators give its period lattice.
