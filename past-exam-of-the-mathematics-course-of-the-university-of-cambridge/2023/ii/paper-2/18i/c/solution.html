Let y1​,…,yr​ be the distinct roots of g in its splitting field L. For each i, let xi​ be the unique pth root of yi​ in an algebraic closure. Then
Tp−yi​=(T−xi​)p,
(182)
so the roots of
f(T)=g(Tp)
(183)
are precisely the xi​. Hence
M=L(x1​,…,xr​).
(184)
But yi​=xip​, so L=K(y1​,…,yr​)⊆K(x1​,…,xr​). It follows that
M=K(x1​,…,xr​),
(185)
which proves that M is also the splitting field of f over K, as in the splitting field of a polynomial obtained by Frobenius substitution.
Moreover xip​=yi​∈L, so every root of f is purely inseparable over L. In fact M/L is purely inseparable.
Now take σ∈Aut(L/K). The extension theorem for field embeddings extends σ to a K-embedding of M into an algebraic closure. Since M is a splitting field of f over K, its image is again M, so this extension is an automorphism τ of M. It is unique: for every root xi​,
τ(xi​)p=σ(yi​),
(186)
and the Frobenius endomorphism gives only one possible pth root. Since the xi​ generate M over L, τ is uniquely determined. This is the unique embedding extension through a purely inseparable extension.
