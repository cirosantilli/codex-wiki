A one-time pad over the finite additive group Zn​ uses a key K that is uniform on Zn​, independent of the plaintext X, as long as the plaintext, and never reused. Encryption and decryption are
C=X+K(modn),X=C−K(modn).
(80)
For independent random variables X,Y, conditioning reduces entropy and translation by a known element of the finite additive group preserves entropy, so
H(X+Y)≥H(X+Y∣Y)=H(X∣Y)=H(X).
(81)
Interchanging X and Y gives H(X+Y)≥H(Y), hence
H(X+Y)≥max{H(X),H(Y)}​.
(82)
This is the entropy of a sum of independent finite-group variables.
The result explains why adding independent pad symbols cannot reduce uncertainty. For a uniform pad, X+K is itself uniform and is independent of X, which is the perfect-secrecy property. Independence is necessary: if X is nonconstant and Y=−X(modn), then X+Y=0 is constant, so
H(X+Y)=0<H(X)=H(Y).
(83)
