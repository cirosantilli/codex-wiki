Let γ be a C3 regular curve parametrized by arc length, so ∣γ′(s)∣=1. Define the Frenet frame
T=γ′,κ=∣T′∣,N=κT′​,B=T×N,
(292)
and define the torsion by
τ=−B′⋅N.
(293)
Thus curvature requires two derivatives, while N,B, and torsion require κ>0; the displayed classical torsion requires three derivatives. At a point where κ=0, N,B, and τ are not defined by this construction.
Since (T,N,B) is an orthonormal frame, differentiating its inner products shows that its derivative matrix is skew-symmetric. The definition gives T′=κN. Next,
N′⋅T=−N⋅T′=−κ,N′⋅N=0,
(294)
so N′=−κT+cB. As B′=T×N′ and T×B=−N, we have B′=−cN; hence c=−B′⋅N=τ. The Frenet-Serret formulas are therefore
T′=κN,N′=−κT+τB,B′=−τN.​
(295)
