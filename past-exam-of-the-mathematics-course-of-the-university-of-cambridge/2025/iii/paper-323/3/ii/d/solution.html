Extend the quantum conditional entropy from normalized states to positive operators by
F(XAB​)=−Tr(XAB​logXAB​)+Tr(XB​logXB​).
(29)
Since TrXAB​=TrXB​, the two terms involving logt cancel under X↦tX, so F(tX)=tF(X). Thus F is positively homogeneous, and part (b) extends its concavity from states to the positive cone.
Apply part (c) with X=σAB​ and Y=ρAB​. Differentiating the matrix logarithm under the trace gives
dtd​​t=0​F(σ+tρ)=−Tr(ρlogσ)+Tr(ρB​logσB​).
(30)
The inequality from part (c), after moving F(ρ) to the left, becomes
D(ρAB​∥σAB​)≥D(ρB​∥σB​)​.
(31)
This is the data-processing inequality for quantum relative entropy under partial trace. Tensoring each output with the appropriate maximally mixed state does not change either side, so it also proves data processing under normalized partial traces. Singular σ follows by approximation on its support.
