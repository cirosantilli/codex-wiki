The total variation distance is
dTV​(μ,ν)=supB∈B(X)​∣μ(B)−ν(B)∣.

Hadamard well-posedness requires existence, uniqueness, and continuous dependence of u on f. A compact operator with infinite-dimensional range cannot have closed range: otherwise its inverse on the orthogonal complement of its kernel would be bounded, making the identity on an infinite-dimensional space compact. Hence the inverse on ranA is unbounded. If kerA={0} uniqueness also fails, and data outside the range have no exact solution. In every case at least stability fails, so the inverse problem is ill posed.
