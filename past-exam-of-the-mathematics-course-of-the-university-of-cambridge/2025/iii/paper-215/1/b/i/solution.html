One shuffle removes the ordered top k cards and inserts them, in a uniformly random person-order, into successively chosen uniform slots. The transition rule depends on a deck only through relabeling of its cards, so its transition matrix on the symmetric group is doubly stochastic. Hence the uniform distribution on all n! permutations is invariant.
