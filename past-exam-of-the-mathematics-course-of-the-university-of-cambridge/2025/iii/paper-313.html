The potential is even, so field reflection has α=−1; spatial reflection has β=−1. If K(x)=ϕ(0,1)(x), the four oriented interpolating solutions, allowing a common translation, are

E=21​∫R2​(∣∂x​ϕ∣2+∣∂y​ϕ∣2)dxdy.
