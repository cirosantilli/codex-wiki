If every wt(Ea​)<d/2, then
wt(Ea†​Eb​)≤wt(Ea​)+wt(Eb​)<d.
(22)
By the definition of the distance of a stabilizer code, no Pauli operator of weight below d is a nontrivial logical operator. Part (b) therefore proves the local correctability of a stabilizer code for this error set.
The converse fails because pairwise products, rather than individual weights, control correctability. For example, let P be a high-weight Pauli outside C(S) and take the error set {I,P}. If P anticommutes with a stabilizer, then ΠPΠ=0, while P†P=I; the KL conditions hold even when wt(P)≥d/2. A still simpler singleton set containing any known unitary Pauli error is always reversible regardless of its weight.
