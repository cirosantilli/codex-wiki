For the chiral transformation ψ↦eiαγ5ψ, one also has ψˉ​↦ψˉ​eiαγ5. The massless kinetic and vector-current terms are invariant because {γ5,γμ}=0. Since γ5 commutes with [γμ,γν], the Pauli bilinear transforms with e2iαγ5 and is not invariant. Thus the continuous classical axial symmetry requires λ=0.
A fermion mass also breaks the symmetry, so in the massive theory one additionally needs m=0, which contradicts a genuinely massive fermion. Making α local produces (∂μ​α)ψˉ​γμγ5ψ. No choice of the vector coupling e or Pauli coupling λ cancels this term; gauging it requires an axial gauge field, and at the quantum level one must also address the chiral anomaly.
