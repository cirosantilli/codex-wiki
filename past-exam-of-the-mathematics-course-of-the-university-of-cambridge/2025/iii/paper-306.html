The string embedding coordinates form a nonlinear sigma model whose target-space metric is the spacetime metric Gμν​. A consistent quantum string background must preserve worldsheet Weyl invariance, so all sigma-model beta functions must vanish, and the total matter-plus-ghost central charge must cancel. For a bosonic string with only a metric, this requires D=26 and, to leading order in α′, βμνG​=α′Rμν​+O(α′2)=0; thus the target metric must be Ricci-flat at leading order.
