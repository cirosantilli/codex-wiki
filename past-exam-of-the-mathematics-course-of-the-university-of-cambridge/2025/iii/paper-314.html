a OurBigBook.com
The approximation requires a nonrelativistic monatomic gas whose particles collide often enough for local thermodynamic equilibrium, with a mean free path much shorter than every macroscopic scale. Translational motion must dominate its heat capacity, giving the adiabatic exponent γ=5/3. Heat conduction, viscosity, shocks, radiative heating and cooling, ionization, chemical reactions, and excitation of internal degrees of freedom must be negligible over the flow time. Under those conditions entropy is advected and the gas behaves as an adiabatic perfect gas.

The function
f(M)=M3/2+3M−1/2
(7)
has its unique minimum at M=1, where f(1)=4. At large r, mass conservation and the reservoir boundary conditions give u∝r−2 and hence M→0. The physical solution therefore begins on the subsonic branch 0<M<1. It cannot pass smoothly to M>1 without reaching the minimum, where the two algebraic branches meet. For γ=5/3 that meeting can occur only in the limiting central behavior of the critical solution, so the flow remains subsonic for every r>0.

A force-free magnetic field obeys
(∇×B)×B=0,
(44)
equivalently j×B=0: its current is parallel to its magnetic field. In a very low-density exterior, material pressure and inertia are too small to balance a finite Lorentz force. Quasistatic force balance therefore drives the magnetic field toward a force-free configuration.
