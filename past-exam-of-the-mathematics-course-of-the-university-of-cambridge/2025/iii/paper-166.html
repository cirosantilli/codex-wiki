Let K be a number field. Its places consist of its real embeddings, conjugate pairs of complex embeddings, and the finite places associated with nonzero prime ideals p of its ring of integers of a number field. At a real or complex place use the usual absolute value. If p lies over the prime number p with ramification index ep​, normalize its absolute value by
∣x∣p​=p−ordp​(x)/ep​.
(17)
These normalizations extend the standard absolute values on Q.
Write dv​=[Kv​:Qv​] for the local degree of a place. Thus dv​ is 1 at a real place, 2 at a complex place, and ev​fv​ at a finite place. The Absolute multiplicative Weil height is
H(α)=∏v∈MK​​max{1,∣α∣v​}dv​/[K:Q].
(18)
The product formula shows that this is unchanged when K is replaced by a larger number field containing α.
