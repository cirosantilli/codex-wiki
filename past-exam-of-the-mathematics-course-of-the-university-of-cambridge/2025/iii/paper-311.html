An asymptotically flat spacetime at future null infinity admits a smooth conformal completion (M,gˉ​) with the following properties. The physical spacetime M is the interior of M, its metric obeys

Let na​=∇ˉa​Ω. Multiplying the supplied conformal Ricci relation by Ω2, using Rab​=0, and taking the limit to I+ gives

The second law of black-hole mechanics states that the area of spatial cross-sections of a future event horizon cannot decrease toward the future, provided the null energy condition and the standard global assumptions hold.
Let ka be an affinely parametrized horizon generator. It is hypersurface-orthogonal, so its null twist vanishes. The Null Raychaudhuri equation in D dimensions and the Einstein equation give
dλdθ​=−D−2θ2​−σab​σab−Rab​kakb≤−D−2θ2​.
(30)
If θ(λ0​)<0, integration implies that θ diverges to −∞ within affine distance at most (D−2)/∣θ(λ0​)∣. The resulting focal point would make the generator leave the achronal boundary that defines the event horizon, contradicting its assumed future completeness. Hence θ≥0 everywhere. Since null expansion obeys
dλdA​=θA,
(31)
every horizon area element, and therefore every complete cross-section area, is nondecreasing.
