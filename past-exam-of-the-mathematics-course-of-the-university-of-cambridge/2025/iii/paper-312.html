Write a filled vertex with n incoming linear fields for the nth-order peculiar-velocity divergence. Gaussian initial conditions require every linear field to be paired. Through sixth order in the linear density there are exactly five connected topologies: the tree diagram B211​ and the one-loop triangle B222​, vertex-correction diagram B321I​, propagator-correction diagram B321II​, and four-leg diagram B411​.
