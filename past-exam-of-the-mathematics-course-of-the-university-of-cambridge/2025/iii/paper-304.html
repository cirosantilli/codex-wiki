The Quantum effective action contains the following diagrams. For Γ2​, there are the tree inverse propagator, the one-loop tadpole at order λ, and at order λ2 the two-loop sunset and the two-loop tadpole with a tadpole insertion. For Γ4​, there are the tree vertex −iλ and, at order λ2, the three one-loop bubble diagrams in the s, t, and u channels. Disconnected and one-particle-reducible graphs do not occur in the quantum effective action.

An infinitesimal change of gauge-fixing fermion, Ψ↦Ψ+δΨ, changes the action by the BRST-exact term QδΨ. If O is gauge invariant, then QO=0. BRST invariance of the measure gives the BRST Ward identity ⟨QX⟩=0, so the normalized variation is
δ⟨O⟩=i⟨O,QδΨ⟩c​=i⟨Q(OδΨ)⟩c​=0.
(24)
Therefore correlation functions of gauge-invariant operators do not depend on the choice of gauge, provided there is no BRST anomaly or boundary contribution.
