Put S=A∖p and define
Bp​=S−1B,
(22)
where A acts on B through the given inclusion. By the prime ideal correspondence for localization, primes of Bp​ correspond to primes q of B satisfying q∩S=∅, equivalently q∩A⊆p.
The localized extension Ap​⊆Bp​ remains integral. If q∩A=p, then
Bp​/S−1q
(23)
is an integral domain integral over the field Ap​/pAp​. An integral domain integral over a field is a field, so S−1q is maximal.
Conversely, if n is maximal in Bp​, the contraction of a maximal ideal under an integral extension is maximal in the local ring Ap​, hence equals pAp​. Contracting once more to A gives p. Extension and contraction are inverse under localization, proving the required fiber primes of an integral extension bijection.
