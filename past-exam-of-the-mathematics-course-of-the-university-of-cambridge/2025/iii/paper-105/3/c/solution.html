With g=0, pull the bounded measurable initial value back along the characteristic flow map:
u(t,x)=u0​(X(0;t,x)).
(38)
The flow is measurable and invertible, so u is measurable and ∥u∥∞​≤∥u0​∥∞​. Choose smooth u0(k)​ converging to u0​ in Lloc1​ with uniformly bounded essential suprema, and define
u(k)(t,x)=u0(k)​(X(0;t,x)).
(39)
Part a makes each u(k) a classical, hence weak, solution. On every compact subset of spacetime, the C1 change-of-variables formula for the flow and its locally bounded Jacobian determinant give u(k)→u in L1. Passing to the limit in the weak identity by dominated convergence proves that u is a bounded weak solution with initial datum u0​.
