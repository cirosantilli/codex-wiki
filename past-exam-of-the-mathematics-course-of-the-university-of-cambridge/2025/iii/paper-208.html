Hoeffding lemma states that if a≤X≤b almost surely, then for every real λ,
logEeλ(X−EX)≤8λ2(b−a)2​.
(1)
Convexity of eλx bounds it on [a,b] by the secant joining its endpoint values. Taking expectations reduces the centered moment-generating function to that of a two-point variable on {a,b} having the same mean. After rescaling to [0,1], its logarithm is
−λq+log(1−q+qeλ),
(2)
where q is its mean. Twice differentiating in λ shows that the second derivative is a Bernoulli variance and hence at most 1/4. The value and first derivative vanish at zero, so Taylor's theorem gives at most λ2/8. Rescaling proves the claim.
