A pair of boundary circles has one dimensionless invariant under the conformal field theory group. If their radii are R1​,R2​ and their center separation is
d=R1​+R2​+L,
(33)
a convenient invariant is the inversive distance
χ=2R1​R2​d2−R12​−R22​​.
(34)
The choice between connected and disconnected bulk surfaces can depend only on χ. Let the transition occur at the single theory-independent numerical value χ=X, with X>1. Then
(R1​+R2​+C)2=R12​+R22​+2XR1​R2​,
(35)
so
C(R1​,R2​)=R12​+R22​+2XR1​R2​​−R1​−R2​​.
(36)
All dependence on the two radii is fixed by conformal symmetry; only the pure number X requires the explicit minimal-surface calculation.
