The hierarchy problem is visible in the one-loop top-quark contribution to the Higgs boson mass parameter,
δmH2​∼−8π23∣yt​∣2​Λ2.
(11)
In a supersymmetric theory the corresponding stop loop has the opposite quadratic term. If supersymmetry is softly broken, the remaining correction is roughly
∣δmH2​∣∼8π23∣yt​∣2​mt2​logmt​Λ​.
(12)
The two relevant one-loop Feynman diagrams are a Higgs two-point function with a closed top-quark loop and the analogous stop loop. Requiring the residual correction not to exceed the electroweak scale by many orders of magnitude gives the naturalness expectation mt​, and broadly other sparticle masses, at a few TeV or below. This is an order-of-magnitude argument rather than a mass bound.
R-parity is
Rp​=(−1)3(B−L)+2s,
(13)
where B is baryon number, L is lepton number and s is spin angular momentum. Standard Model particles have Rp​=+1, while their superpartners have Rp​=−1. The renormalizable R-parity-violating superpotential of the Minimal supersymmetric Standard Model is
WRPV​=21​λijk​Li​Lj​Ekc​+λijk′​Li​Qj​Dkc​+21​λijk′′​Uic​Djc​Dkc​+κi​Li​Hu​,
(14)
with λijk​=−λjik​ and λijk′′​=−λikj′′​.
Combining λi1k′​Li​Q1​Dkc​ with λ11k′′​U1c​D1c​Dkc​ produces proton decay through exchange of a virtual right-handed down-type squark. The tree diagram has u and d entering the baryon-number-violating vertex, an internal dRk​ line, and the lepton-number-violating vertex emitting νˉi​ and a light quark. Hadronization gives
p⟶π++νˉi​.
(15)
The invisible particle is an antineutrino of any family i=1,2,3, and electric-charge conservation requires a positively charged pion. The antisymmetry of λ11k′′​ requires k=2 or 3.
Integrating out a squark of mass mq​​ gives a dimension-six interaction with coefficient C∼λ′λ′′/mq​2​. Ignoring order-one hadronic and phase-space factors,
Γp​∼8π∣λ′λ′′∣2​mq​4​mp5​​.
(16)
For mp​∼1 GeV and mq​​∼104 GeV, the factor before the couplings is about 10−18--10−17 GeV. A lifetime above 1034 years is about 1065 GeV−1, so Γp​≲10−65 GeV and
∣λ′λ′′∣≲10−24​
(17)
at order-of-magnitude accuracy. Hadronic matrix elements and flavour choices alter the numerical coefficient, not the conclusion that simultaneous baryon- and lepton-number violation is extraordinarily constrained.
The matter-parity factor (−1)3(B−L) is odd for every quark or lepton superfield and even for either Higgs superfield. Each term in WRPV​ contains an odd number of matter superfields, so R-parity forbids all four types of term. Standard MSSM Yukawa interactions contain two matter superfields and remain allowed.
Finally, the requested four-point interaction contains a squark, an antisquark, a W boson and a gluon. It comes from the squark gauge-covariant kinetic term

2gs​g2​Q​†TatIQ​Gμa​WIμ,

2igs​g2​gμν(Ta)ji​(tI)BA​,
