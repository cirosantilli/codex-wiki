The Freiman-Ruzsa theorem over a finite field states that if A⊆Fpn​ and ∣A+A∣≤K∣A∣, then A is contained in a vector subspace H with
∣H∣≤K2pK4∣A∣.
(1)
After translating A, assume 0∈A. Put S=A−A, and choose X⊆A+S maximal subject to the translates x+A, x∈X, being pairwise disjoint. Since X+A⊆2A+S=3A−A, the Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality gives
∣X∣∣A∣=∣X+A∣≤∣3A−A∣≤K4∣A∣,
(2)
so ∣X∣≤K4.
Maximality gives A+S⊆X+S: if y∈A+S is not already in X, then (y+A)∩(x+A)=∅ for some x∈X, whence y∈x+A−A=x+S. Inductively, mA+S⊆⟨X⟩+S for every positive integer m. Because 0∈A, every element of ⟨A⟩ belongs to some mA in the finite vector space, and therefore
H:=⟨A⟩⊆⟨X⟩+S.
(3)
Finally, ∣⟨X⟩∣≤p∣X∣ and ∣S∣≤K2∣A∣ by the Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality, so
∣H∣≤p∣X∣∣S∣≤K2pK4∣A∣.​
(4)
