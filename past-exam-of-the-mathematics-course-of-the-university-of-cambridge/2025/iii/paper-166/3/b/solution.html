Write
P(X)=∑j=0n​pj​Xj,Q(X)=∑j=0n​qj​Xj,
(41)
so there are N=2(n+1) unknown integer coefficients. Let t be the number of integers r with
0≤r<d(2−κ)n​−1.
(42)
For each such r, impose the linear equation over K=Q(α)
Lr​(p,q)=Dr​(P+αQ)(α)=0,
(43)
where Dr​ is the normalized derivative of a polynomial. The coefficient of pj​ is (rj​)αj−r and that of qj​ is (rj​)αj−r+1. The local definition of projective height, together with (rj​)≤2n, gives
Hp​(Lr​)≤C0n​
(44)
for a constant C0​ depending only on α.
There are M=t forms over the degree-d field K, and
dM<(2−κ)n−d<N.
(45)
Moreover N−dM≥κn+2. Applying Siegel lemma gives a nonzero integral coefficient vector with
max{H(P),H(Q)}≤(NC0n​)dM/(N−dM)≤C1n​,
(46)
because the exponent is bounded in terms of κ and every fixed power of n is at most exponential in n. These P,Q have all the required vanishing normalized derivatives.
