If OK​ is Noetherian, its maximal ideal is finitely generated. Ideals in a valuation ring are totally ordered, so every finitely generated ideal is generated by one of its generators; write m=(π). Then v(π) is the smallest positive element of the value group. Subtracting integral multiples of this value shows that every value is an integral multiple of v(π), so v is discrete.
