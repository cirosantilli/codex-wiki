An orthonormal wavelet is ψ∈L2(R) such that {2j/2ψ(2jx−k)}j,k∈Z​ is an orthonormal basis. A multiresolution analysis is a nested family of closed spaces Vj​ with trivial intersection, dense union, dyadic scaling, integer-translation invariance of V0​, and a generator ϕ whose integer translates form an orthonormal basis of V0​. The Meyer-Mallat theorem says every such analysis has an orthonormal wavelet whose translates span Vj+1​⊖Vj​.
