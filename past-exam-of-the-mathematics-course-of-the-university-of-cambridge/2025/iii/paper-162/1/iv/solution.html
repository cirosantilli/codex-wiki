The exceptional divisor is E≅Pr−1, so its Chow groups are generated by the linear subspaces [Γk​]. The complement X∖E is isomorphic through the blow-up map to Pr∖{p}. The localization sequence for {p}⊆Pr shows that the latter's Chow groups are generated by the restrictions of linear spaces Hj​ through p for 1≤j≤r; its zero-dimensional Chow group vanishes.
Under the complement isomorphism, Hj​∖{p} corresponds to the open part of the strict transform Hj​. A second localization sequence, now for E⊆X, shows that A∗​(X) is generated by the lifts [Hj​] together with the images [Γk​] from A∗​(E), exactly as claimed.
