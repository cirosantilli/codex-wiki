We induct on the Coxeter length ℓ(w). There is nothing to prove when ℓ(w)=0. Otherwise choose a simple root α such that
ℓ(wsα​)=ℓ(w)−1;
(20)
equivalently, wα is negative. Since λ and μ=wλ lie in the Closed dominant Weyl chamber,
0≤⟨λ,α∨⟩=⟨μ,(wα)∨⟩≤0.
(21)
Therefore ⟨λ,α∨⟩=0, and the simple reflection sα​ fixes λ. Moreover,
(wsα​)λ=wλ=μ.
(22)
The induction hypothesis writes wsα​ as a product of simple reflections that fix λ. Multiplying on the right by sα​ gives the required expression for w. This is the Weyl stabilizer of a dominant point lemma.
