Take
φ∗=φM,
(179)
the formula relativization to a class: replace each ∃yψ by
∃y(y∈M∧ψM)
(180)
and each ∀yψ by
∀y(y∈M⇒ψM),
(181)
leaving atomic formulas unchanged. If M is given by a defining formula, insert that formula wherever y∈M occurs; this is why the map may depend on M.
For z and any parameters in M, induction on formulas gives
V⊨φM(z)⟺M⊨φ(z).
(182)
Therefore φ∗-closure puts
{z∈x:V⊨φM(z)}={z∈x:M⊨φ(z)}
(183)
in M for every x∈M. This is exactly the relativized closure criterion for separation, so (M,∈) satisfies the φ-instance of separation.
