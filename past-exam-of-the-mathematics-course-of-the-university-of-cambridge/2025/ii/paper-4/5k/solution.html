For a model with k fitted parameters, the Akaike information criterion is
AIC=−2ℓ(θ)+2k,
(36)
where ℓ is the log-likelihood evaluated at the maximum-likelihood estimate.
In the normal linear model with known σ2,
−2ℓ(β)=nlog(2πσ2)+σ21​∥Y−Xβ∥2.
(37)
The maximum-likelihood estimator is the ordinary least-squares estimator, with fitted mean μ​=HY, where
H=X(XTX)−1XT.
(38)
Since only the p components of β are fitted,
AIC=nlog(2πσ2)+σ21​∥Y−μ​∥2+2p.
(39)
After multiplying by σ2 and discarding the model-independent constant, this is exactly Mallows Cp:
Cp​=∥Y−μ​∥2+2pσ2​.
(40)
To compare it with prediction error, write Y=μ+ε and Y∗=μ+ε∗, where μ=Xβ, the two errors are independent, and both have covariance σ2I. Since Hμ=μ, H is an orthogonal projection of rank p, and
Y∗−μ​=ε∗−Hε.
(41)
The cross term has zero expectation, so
MSPE=E∥ε∗∥2+E∥Hε∥2=nσ2+pσ2.
(42)
On the other hand,
E∥Y−μ​∥2=E∥(I−H)ε∥2=σ2tr(I−H)=(n−p)σ2.
(43)
Therefore the unbiased prediction-error identity for ordinary least squares gives
E(Cp​)=(n−p)σ2+2pσ2=(n+p)σ2=MSPE​.
(44)
