Immediately after the reversal the state populations have not changed, but the new Hamiltonian satisfies
H(−B)=−H(B).
(496)
Thus
e−βH(B)=e−(−β)H(−B),
(497)
so the same state is canonical for the reversed field with
β′=−β​.
(498)
This is the magnetic-field reversal and negative temperature construction. Since
β′μB′=(−β)μ(−B)=βμB,
(499)
the partition function is unchanged. Therefore
F′=−F​,C′=C​.
(500)
A gas has kinetic energy unbounded above. Its partition function diverges for β<0, so it cannot have a normalizable negative temperature canonical state and cannot satisfy the zeroth-law equilibrium condition βgas​=β′<0.
If an amount δQ>0 flows from the spins into a positive-temperature gas, the total entropy change is
δStotal​=δQ(βgas​−β′)>0.
(501)
The second law therefore selects heat flow
from the negative-temperature spin system to the gas​.
(502)
This is the general ordering expressed by heat flow from negative to positive temperature.
