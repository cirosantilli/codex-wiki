Take the plane of incidence to be the xy-plane, so all three electric polarizations point along z. Write their signed scalar amplitudes as EI​,ER​,ET​. Tangential-E continuity gives
EI​+ER​=ET​.
(521)
For each plane wave,
H=μω1​k×E.
(522)
Continuity of its tangential component gives
n−​cosθI​(EI​−ER​)=n+​cosθT​ET​,
(523)
where the common proportionality between ∣k∣ and n cancels. Solving the two equations gives the transverse-electric Fresnel reflection coefficient
EI​ER​​=n−​cosθI​+n+​cosθT​n−​cosθI​−n+​cosθT​​.
(524)
Using Snell's law to eliminate θT​,
n+​cosθT​=n+2​−n−2​sin2θI​​.
(525)
Since ϵ+​>ϵ−​ and μ is common, n+​>n−​. Therefore
(n+​cosθT​)2−(n−​cosθI​)2=n+2​−n−2​>0.
(526)
The numerator is consequently strictly negative throughout
0≤θI​≤π/2, and
∣EI​∣∣ER​∣​=n+2​−n−2​sin2θI​​+n−​cosθI​n+2​−n−2​sin2θI​​−n−​cosθI​​>0​.
(527)
Thus transverse-electric polarization has no Brewster angle in this setting.
