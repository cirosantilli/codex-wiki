Z(β)=∑s​e−βEs​,β=(kB​T)−1.

It packages the equilibrium probabilities Ps​=e−βEs​/Z and hence all canonical thermodynamics. In particular,
F=−kB​TlogZ,⟨E⟩=−∂β∂logZ​,(ΔE)2=∂β2∂2logZ​,
