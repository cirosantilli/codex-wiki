OurBigBook.com Words: 93
Use the shuffle bound: if a well-order is partitioned into subsets of order types ξ and η, its order type is at most the Hessenberg natural sum ξ#η. This follows by merging the two induced well-orders and comparing their Cantor-normal-form terms.
If both X and Y had order type strictly below ωδ, each would have leading Cantor exponent below δ. Their natural sum would still be below ωδ. The shuffle bound would then give
ωδ≤otp(X)#otp(Y)<ωδ,
(89)
a contradiction. Thus at least one part has order type ωδ.
