Fix a K-embedding σ:Li−1​→K. If mi​(t) is the minimal polynomial of αi​ over Li−1​, an extension of σ to Li​ is determined by the image of αi​, which must be a root of the polynomial obtained by applying σ to the coefficients of mi​. Conversely, each distinct root gives one extension. Since K is algebraically closed, there is at least one such root, and there are at most di​ distinct roots.
