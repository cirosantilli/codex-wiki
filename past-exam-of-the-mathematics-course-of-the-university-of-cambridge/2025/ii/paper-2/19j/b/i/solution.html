An invariant bilinear form B defines a linear map
ΦB​:V⟶V∗,ΦB​(v)(w)=B(v,w).
(181)
Invariance gives
ΦB​(gv)(w)=B(gv,w)=B(v,g−1w)=(gΦB​(v))(w),
(182)
so ΦB​ is an G-homomorphism. Its kernel is a G-subrepresentation of irreducible V. Hence either ΦB​=0, so B=0, or ΦB​ is injective and therefore a linear isomorphism V→V∗. In the latter case B is a nondegenerate.
If B and C are two nonzero invariant forms, then
ΦC−1​ΦB​∈EndG​(V).
(183)
By Schur lemma, this endomorphism is scalar, so B=λC. Cases involving the zero form are immediate. Thus any two invariant bilinear forms are proportional.
