Take Tf​ to consist of T together with the following sentences.
  • f is injective and surjective:
∀x∀y(f(x)=f(y)⇒x=y),∀y∃xf(x)=y.
(143)
  • For every n-ary operation symbol ω of L,
∀x1​⋯∀xn​f(ω(x1​,…,xn​))=ω(f(x1​),…,f(xn​)).
(144)
This includes f(c)=c for each constant symbol c.
  • For every n-ary relation symbol R of L,
∀x1​⋯∀xn​(R(x1​,…,xn​)⟺R(f(x1​),…,f(xn​))).
(145)
The first pair of axioms makes the interpretation ϑ a bijection. The remaining schemes say exactly that it preserves every operation and relation, so (M,ϑ)⊨Tf​ exactly when M⊨T and ϑ∈Aut(M).
Finally, T is consistent and hence has a model M by part (b). Expanding M by interpreting f as the identity automorphism gives a model of Tf​, so Tf​ is consistent.
