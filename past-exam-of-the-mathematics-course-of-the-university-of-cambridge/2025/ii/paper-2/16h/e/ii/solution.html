Suppose TG​ is inconsistent. By the compactness theorem, some finite subset Δ⊆TG​ is inconsistent. Only finitely many added symbols fg​ occur in Δ; let X⊆G be the finite set of their indices. Every sentence of Δ belongs to the sublanguage LX​, so
Δ⊆TG​∩LX​.
(148)
Consequently TG​∩LX​ is inconsistent.
