The compactness theorem states that a set T of first-order sentences has a model if and only if every finite subset of T has a model.
The forward implication follows by using the same model for every finite subset. Conversely, suppose every finite subset of T has a model. If T were inconsistent, a formal derivation of ⊥ from T would use only finitely many assumptions, say those in T0​⊆T. Then T0​ would be inconsistent. By part (b), T0​ would have no model, contrary to the hypothesis. Thus T is consistent, and part (b) gives a model of T.
