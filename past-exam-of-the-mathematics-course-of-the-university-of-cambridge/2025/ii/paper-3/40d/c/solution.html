For the wave scheme, use vmn​=Gneimθ. After multiplying by G, the recurrence becomes
G2+(4μs−2ρ)G+1=0,s=sin2(2θ​).

Equivalently,
G+G−1=2(ρ−2μs).
(482)
Because the two roots have product one, both lie on the unit circle precisely when
∣ρ−2μs∣≤1.
(483)
This must hold for every s∈[0,1]. At s=0 it requires ρ≤1. Since the question assumes 1≤ρ≤2, stability is possible only for ρ=1. The condition at s=1 then becomes
∣1−2μ∣≤1,
(484)
or 0≤μ≤1. With the stipulated μ>0, the centered three-level wave scheme is stable exactly when
ρ=1,0<μ≤1​.
(485)
