Let L be the tridiagonal matrix with diagonal entries −2 and adjacent off-diagonal entries 1 on the interior grid points. The finite-interval scheme is
(I−2μ​L)un+1=(I+2μ​L)un.
(486)
For J interior points, the dirichlet discrete Laplacian has eigenvalues
λj​=−4sin2(2(J+1)jπ​)<0,1≤j≤J.
(487)
The corresponding eigenvalues of the amplification matrix are
qj​=1−(μ/2)λj​1+(μ/2)λj​​.
(488)
Since λj​<0 and μ≥0,
∣qj​∣≤1
(489)
for every j. The matrix is real symmetric in the sine eigenbasis, so its induced 2-norm is the largest ∣qj​∣. This proves the crank-Nicolson stability on a finite Dirichlet interval and gives
stability for every μ>0​.
