Let E=Q(α,β). Since
3​=α2−3,6​=αβ,2​=3​6​​,
(154)
part (b) gives [E:Q]=8. The roots of X4−6X2+6 are ±α,±β, all in E, so E is its splitting field and is Galois. Its action on the four roots is transitive; the order-eight entry in the list from part (a) is D8​. Therefore
Gal(E/Q)≅D8​.
(155)
For an explicit dihedral Galois action on four radical roots, define
r(α)=β,r(β)=−α,s(α)=α,s(β)=−β.
(156)
Then r4=s2=1 and srs=r−1. The subgroup lattice is determined by
D8​⟨r⟩⟨r2,s⟩⟨r2,rs⟩⟨r2⟩⟨s⟩⟨r2s⟩⟨rs⟩⟨r3s⟩{1}.​
(157)
Here ⟨r2⟩ lies in all three order-four subgroups; ⟨s⟩,⟨r2s⟩ lie in ⟨r2,s⟩; and ⟨rs⟩,⟨r3s⟩ lie in ⟨r2,rs⟩.
Reversing inclusions under the Galois correspondence gives the complete field lattice
EQ(2​,3​)Q(α)Q(β)Q(α+β)Q(α−β)Q(2​)Q(3​)Q(6​)Q.​
(158)
The incidence is likewise reversed: Q(2​) lies in Q(2​,3​); Q(3​) lies in that field, Q(α), and Q(β); and Q(6​) lies in that field, Q(α+β), and Q(α−β). The hint verifies the last two quartic fields through α+β=2​γ and α−β=2​δ.
