The two eigenvalues are the points at arguments 0 and γ. The counterclockwise arc from 1 to eiγ has length γ, while the complementary clockwise arc has length 2π−γ. The shorter of the two contains the spectrum. Therefore
θ(Uγ​)=min{γ,2π−γ}={γ,2π−γ,​0≤γ<π,π≤γ<2π.​​

The two geometric cases are respectively the short counterclockwise arc and the short complementary clockwise arc; at γ=π both semicircles have length π.
