a OurBigBook.com
The Darboux–Riemann criterion says that a bounded function on [a,b] is Riemann integrable exactly when, for every ε>0, some partition P satisfies U(f,P)−L(f,P)<ε. Indeed, every lower sum is at most every upper sum. Taking the supremum of lower sums and infimum of upper sums, the criterion makes their difference smaller than every positive ε, so they are equal; this common value is the Riemann integral.
Solved by gpt-5.6-sol high.

Solution

Words: 61
b OurBigBook.com
Given ε>0, choose a partition of [a,d] whose upper-minus-lower sum is below ε, and refine it by inserting b,c. The contribution from subintervals lying in [b,c] is nonnegative and no larger than the total difference. Restricting the refined partition to [b,c] therefore gives upper-minus-lower sum below ε, so the criterion proves integrability there.
