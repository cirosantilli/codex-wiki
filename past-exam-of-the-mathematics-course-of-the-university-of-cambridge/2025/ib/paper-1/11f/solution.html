An allowable parametrisation is a smooth homeomorphism from an open subset of R2 onto an open subset of S, with derivative of rank two. Away from the axis, the rotation orbit has nonzero tangent. A transverse curve supplied by the submanifold theorem, followed by the rotation action, gives
σ(u,v)=(f(u)cosv,f(u)sinv,g(u)),
(23)
with ∣v∣<π and (f′,g′)=(0,0).
For a ruled parametrisation, regularity is exactly
ψs​×ψt​=(a′+tb′)×b=0.
(24)
Rotate and translate along the axis so the specified ruling is
L(t)=(d,st,ct),d>0.
(25)
Here s=0 because the line is not parallel to the axis, and c=0: if c=0, rotating the horizontal tangent line makes the ruled parametrisation singular at its closest point. Rotating L gives
x2+y2=d2+c2s2​z2,
(26)
a one-sheet hyperboloid. Rotation invariance puts this whole surface in Σ. Connectedness and the fact that a complete embedded hyperboloid cannot be a proper subset of another connected embedded surface force equality. Rescaling radial and axial coordinates gives a diffeomorphism with x2+y2=1+z2.
