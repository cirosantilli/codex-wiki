Let k=∣G/H∣. The coset action gives a nontrivial homomorphism G→Sk​ whose kernel is normal. Simplicity makes it injective. The sign map is trivial on the nonabelian simple image, so G embeds in Ak​. For k≤4, Ak​ is solvable, as are its subgroups, whereas a nonabelian simple group is not. Hence k≥5.
