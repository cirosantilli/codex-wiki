Every value is a finite real number. In any nondegenerate interval [a,b]⊆[0,1], its interior contains infinitely many rational numbers, whereas there are only finitely many reduced fractions in [0,1] with denominator at most any fixed M. Some rational number in (a,b) therefore has denominator exceeding M. Hence the function is unbounded on every such interval. It reverses the positive values of the Thomae function rather than requiring an infinite value at any point.
