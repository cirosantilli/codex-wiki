Its fixed points are u=0 and u∗​=1−1/λ. The fixed-point multipliers are F′(0)=λ and F′(u∗​)=2−λ. Since λ>1, zero is always unstable. The positive fixed point is stable for 1<λ<3 and unstable for λ>3. For λ>3, a small displacement obeys ϵn+1​≃(2−λ)ϵn​: its sign alternates and its amplitude grows, giving an oscillatory instability whose period is 2δt.
This is an artifact of Euler discretization of logistic growth. Exact small perturbations decay by the positive factor e−rδt per time step; a one-dimensional autonomous differential equation cannot have a nonconstant periodic orbit, since its solutions move monotonically between equilibrium points. The artificial instability occurs when rδt>2, beyond the stable step range of this discretization.
