OurBigBook.com Words: 89
For every a∈R, the nonempty order interval
Ua​=⟨(a,−1),(a,1)⟩={a}×(−1,1)
(36)
is open in the lexicographic order topology on the real plane. The sets Ua​ are pairwise disjoint.
Suppose {Bn​:n∈N} were a countable basis of a topology. For each a, the basis of a topology property gives a member containing (a,0) and contained in Ua​. Let n(a) be the least index of such a member. The same nonempty Bn​ cannot lie in two disjoint Ua​, so a↦n(a) is an injection from the uncountable set R into N, a contradiction. This topology is not second countable.
