Part (ii) shows that α∗v=λˉv for every v∈Eλ​, so Eλ​ is invariant under α∗. Apply the adjoint criterion for an invariant orthogonal complement with α∗ in place of α, using (α∗)∗=α. It follows that
α(Eλ⊥​)⊆Eλ⊥​​.
(62)
The same argument interchanging α and α∗ shows that Eλ⊥​ is invariant under both operators, a fact needed for the induction in part (iv).
