For the right-triangle identity, normalize its right vertex to i and one leg to the vertical geodesic, with endpoint iy, y>1. The other leg lies on the unit semicircle, with endpoint s+it, s2+t2=1, t>0. These geodesics meet orthogonally at i, since the metric is conformal to the Euclidean metric. The semicircle is a geodesic because a real Möbius transformation sends it to a vertical line; vertical lines are minimizing by the same length bound. The stabilizer rotations above can align any given tangent direction, giving this normal form for every right triangle.
