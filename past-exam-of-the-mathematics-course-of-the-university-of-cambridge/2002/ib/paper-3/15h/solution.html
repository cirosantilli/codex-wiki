Introduce surplus variables s1​,s2​, slack s3​, and artificial variables a1​,a2​, all nonnegative. The two-phase simplex standard-form equations are
x1​−x2​−s1​+a1​=1,4x1​−x2​−s2​+a2​=10,x2​+s3​=6.
(49)
Phase I maximizes w=−a1​−a2​. An initial basic feasible solution has basic variables (a1​,a2​,s3​)=(1,10,6) and all others zero. Its simplex dictionary is
a1​=1−x1​+x2​+s1​,a2​=10−4x1​+x2​+s2​,s3​=6−x2​,w=−11+5x1​−2x2​−s1​−s2​.
(50)
Let x1​ enter. The simplex ratio test gives 1 from the a1​ row and 10/4 from the a2​ row, so a1​ leaves. After this pivot,
x1​=1+x2​+s1​−a1​,a2​=6−3x2​−4s1​+4a1​+s2​,s3​=6−x2​,w=−6+3x2​+4s1​−5a1​−s2​.
(51)
Choose x2​ to enter; the limiting ratios are 6/3=2 and 6, so a2​ leaves. The resulting dictionary is
x1​x2​s3​w​=3−31​s1​+31​s2​+31​a1​−31​a2​,=2−34​s1​+31​s2​+34​a1​−31​a2​,=4+34​s1​−31​s2​−34​a1​+31​a2​,=−a1​−a2​.​
(52)
Thus wmax​=0​, attained at (x1​,x2​,s3​)=(3,2,4) with s1​=s2​=a1​=a2​=0. Since w≤0 for every feasible Phase I point, this proves feasibility and completes Phase I.
Delete the artificial variables and restore the original objective z=−2x1​+3x2​. The resulting Phase II dictionary is
x1​=3−31​s1​+31​s2​,x2​=2−34​s1​+31​s2​,s3​=4+34​s1​−31​s2​,z=−310​s1​+31​s2​.
(53)
Let s2​ enter; only s3​ decreases, and it reaches zero at s2​=12. Pivoting gives
x1​=7+s1​−s3​,x2​=6−s3​,s2​=12+4s1​−3s3​,z=4−2s1​−s3​​.
(54)
