For a linear map T:U→V, its rank of a linear map is dimimT, and its nullity is dimkerT. The rank-nullity theorem states dimU=rankT+nullityT. One obtains it by extending a basis of the kernel to a basis of U; the images of the added vectors form a basis of the image.
Restrict β to A=imα. Its image is im(βα) and its kernel is A∩kerβ. Thus
rank(βα)=rankα−dim(A∩kerβ).
(7)
The intersection dimension is at most dimkerβ=dimV−rankβ, proving the lower bound. It is nonnegative, proving the upper bound by rankα, while im(βα)⊆imβ proves the other upper bound. Consequently the rank inequality for a composition is
rankα+rankβ−dimV≤rank(βα)≤min(rankα,rankβ).​
