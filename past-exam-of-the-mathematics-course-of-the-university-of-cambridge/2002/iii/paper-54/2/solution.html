An elastic deformation is reversible: after removal of the deforming loads the material returns to its reference state, and its instantaneous stress is determined by its current deformation rather than a plastic or viscous history. In the infinitesimal approximation the displacement field ui​ gives the infinitesimal strain tensor eij​=(ui,j​+uj,i​)/2. The Cauchy stress tensor is defined by traction ti​=σij​nj​ on a plane of unit normal n; absence of body couples makes σij​=σji​.

Here the derivative is the gradient on the space of symmetric tensors, with the double-contraction inner product. This convention accounts for the two occurrences of each off-diagonal entry; treating six independent coordinates without their corresponding factors would give an erroneous factor of two in shear components.
Take a stress-free reference state of mass density ρ0​. The Taylor series of the elastic energy starts quadratically:
E(e)=E(0)+2ρ0​1​cijkl​eij​ekl​+O(∣e∣3).
(10)
Using ρ=ρ0​+O(∣e∣) gives σij​=cijkl​ekl​+O(∣e∣2), the relation of linear elasticity. If the reference state has initial stress, the first-order expansion also retains that constant stress. Symmetry of stress and strain gives the minor symmetries cijkl​=cjikl​=cijlk​, while equality of the mixed second derivatives of elastic energy gives the major symmetry cijkl​=cklij​. Thus the elastic stiffness tensor is a symmetric map on a six-dimensional strain space: there are 6⋅7/2=21 independent coefficients.
For twofold elastic symmetry, choose the symmetry axis as x3​. Rotation through π is Q=diag(−1,−1,1), and transforms the infinitesimal strain tensor into QeQT. Components e11​,e22​,e33​,e12​ are even and e23​,e13​ are odd. Invariance of elastic energy excludes every coupling between these parity classes.
