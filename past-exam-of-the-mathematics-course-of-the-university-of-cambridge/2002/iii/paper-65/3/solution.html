The renormalization-group beta function is

At one-loop accuracy, integrating dλ/dlogμ=bλ2 gives

For a positive weak coupling and b>0, the coupling increases toward the ultraviolet and decreases toward the infrared. Extrapolation predicts a Landau pole at μ=μ0​exp[1/(bλ(μ0​))], where the weak-coupling approximation fails. For b<0, the positive coupling decreases toward zero as μ→∞: this is asymptotic freedom. Toward the infrared it instead grows, so perturbation theory eventually fails. The zero coupling is a fixed point. If negative couplings are allowed mathematically, their direction of motion still satisfies dλ/dlogμ=bλ2, but the side on which the pole occurs reverses: for example b>0,λ<0 approaches zero from below in the ultraviolet. Negative quartic coupling does not give a stable scalar potential.
