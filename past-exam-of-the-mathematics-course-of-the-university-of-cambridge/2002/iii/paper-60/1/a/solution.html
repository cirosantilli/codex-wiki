Put h=1/(m+1) and impose homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions, as required by the stated continuum eigenvalues. Let T=tridiag(1,−2,1) be the unscaled one-dimensional Dirichlet discrete Laplacian. For 1≤k≤m, the vectors vj(k)​=sin(jkπh) satisfy
Tv(k)=τk​v(k),τk​=2cos(kπh)−2=−4sin22kπh​.
(1)
The identity follows by adding the two neighboring sines; the values at j=0,m+1 vanish. These m mutually orthogonal vectors form the discrete sine transform basis. Consequently their tensor products give all m2 two-dimensional modes, not merely a few candidate eigenvectors.
In a compatible grid ordering the axial operator is A5​=h−2(T⊗I+I⊗T). The nine-point operator is
A9​=A5​+6h21​T⊗T.
(2)
Indeed, the added Kronecker product supplies the diagonal neighbors with weight 1/6, subtracts 1/3 from each axial neighbor and adds 2/3 to the central coefficient. Thus it has exactly the coefficients of the second discretization. On v(k)⊗v(l) the eigenvalues are
λ5,kl​=−h24​(sin22kπh​+sin22lπh​),​
(3)
and
λ9,kl​=λ5,kl​+3h28​sin22kπh​sin22lπh​,1≤k,l≤m.​
(4)
Equivalently, h2λ9,kl​=−10/3+4[cos(kπh)+cos(lπh)]/3+2cos(kπh)cos(lπh)/3.
