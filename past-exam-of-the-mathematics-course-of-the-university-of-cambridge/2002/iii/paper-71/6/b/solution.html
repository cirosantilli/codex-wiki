Take a periodic comoving box of volume L3, with k=2πn/L. A real field has the expansion
δϕ^​(x,t)=∑k​[wk​(t)a^k​eik⋅x+wk∗​(t)a^k†​e−ik⋅x],

This opposite momentum is required by reality; the single-oscillator shorthand with the same label on both operators must not be read literally as every traveling-wave Fourier coefficient.
The quadratic action has canonical momentum π^=a3δϕ^​˙​. Its equal-time field commutator requires
a3L3(wk​w˙k∗​−wk∗​w˙k​)=i​.

wk​=L−3/22k3​H​(i+aHk​)eik/(aH)​.

This is the existing Bunch-Davies mode normalization in a finite comoving volume. It is exact for a massless minimally coupled test field in de Sitter and the leading approximation for a light slowly evolving field. The homogeneous zero mode needs separate treatment.
