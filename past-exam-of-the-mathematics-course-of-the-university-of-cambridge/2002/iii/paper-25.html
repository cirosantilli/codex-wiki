For completeness, the contour estimate used in Question 2 follows from these very formulas. Subtract the complex logarithmic derivative expansion at 2+it from that at σ+it, with 1−b/(2log(T+3))≤σ≤2 and ∣t∣≤T. Terms with ∣t−γ∣>1 have differences O((t−γ)−2), whose sum is O(log(∣t∣+3)) by the kernel bound. There are O(log(∣t∣+3)) remaining terms. Fix a positive integer q and an integer a coprime to q. We use Dirichlet characters modulo q, extended by zero on integers not coprime to q, and write χ0​ for the principal Dirichlet character. L(s,χ0​)=ζ(s)∏p∣q​(1−p−s)
(52)
has a simple pole at one, with residue φ(q)/q.
