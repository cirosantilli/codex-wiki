A sufficient existence condition is HdR1​(P)=0; more generally one assumes the action is weakly Hamiltonian and resolves its moment-map equivariance obstruction.

For a left action, fundamental vector fields obey [ξP​,ηP​]=−[ξ,η]P​. Together with the Hamiltonian-field identity, this makes

A useful concrete sufficient condition is a common fixed point x0​ and the normalization μξ​(x0​)=0. The constant c is zero there because all fundamental fields vanish, and hence zero everywhere on connected P. This is fixed-point normalization removes the moment-map cocycle. If the action has an ineffective Lie-algebra kernel, the map to observables can have that kernel; equality of brackets alone is not a claim of a faithful representation.
For the oscillator, the printed complex coordinates give idzi​∧dzˉi​=dpi​∧dqi and {zi​,zˉj​}=iδij​. For an anti-Hermitian matrix ξ∈u(3), the fundamental field is δz=ξz. Direct contraction yields

Consequently the unitary moment map of an isotropic oscillator in our declared convention is
μξ​(z)=−iz†ξz.​

Unitary invariance of H=z†z proves conservation of all nine real matrix components. Under z↦Uz, the matrix transforms as zz†↦Uzz†U−1, exactly the coadjoint transformation in this pairing. The origin is the required fixed point.
