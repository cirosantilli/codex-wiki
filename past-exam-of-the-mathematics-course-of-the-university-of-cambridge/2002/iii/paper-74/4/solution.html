Define the raw Killing form by
K(X,Y)=Tr(adXadY).​
(21)
For a finite-dimensional real or complex Lie algebra in characteristic zero, it is nondegenerate exactly when the algebra is a semisimple Lie algebra. For a real compact semisimple algebra it is negative definite. Consequently the positive compact Killing metric is g=−K: for example on su(2) with [ei​,ej​]=εijk​ek​, Kij​=−2δij​ and −K gives a Riemannian metric on SU(2). An overall minus sign in the term “Killing metric” is common here; raw trace K itself is negative definite in this example.
For a Lorentzian metric use sl(2,R) with [h,e]=2e, [h,f]=−2f, [e,f]=h. Direct traces give
K(h,e,f)​=​800​004​040​​,
(22)
whose metric signature is (2,1). For a non-Abelian degenerate example, the three-dimensional Heisenberg Lie algebra has [x,y]=z central. Every product of two adjoint maps is zero, so K=0.
For any adjoint-invariant nondegenerate metric on a Lie group, the Koszul formula on left-invariant fields simplifies to
2g(∇X​Y,Z)=g([X,Y],Z),∇X​Y=21​[X,Y].​
(23)
Take R(X,Y)Z=∇X​∇Y​Z−∇Y​∇X​Z−∇[X,Y]​Z. The Jacobi identity gives
R(X,Y)Z=41​([X,[Y,Z]]−[Y,[X,Z]])−21​[[X,Y],Z]=−41​[[X,Y],Z].
(24)
Taking the trace of Z↦R(Z,X)Y=−adYadX(Z)/4 yields
Ric(X,Y)=−41​K(X,Y).​
(25)
Thus the metric g=K is a Killing-form Einstein metric with Einstein constant −1/4. With g=−K the same connection has constant +1/4. This proves the Einstein manifold assertion for every semisimple group, including products when the unscaled Killing form is used on all factors.
There is also a different, flat metric-preserving connection. Declare the global left-invariant frame parallel:
DX​(Yaea​)=X(Ya)ea​.
(26)
Its curvature is zero because applying two derivatives to coefficient functions and subtracting their commutator gives zero. Its metric derivative vanishes since gab​ is constant in this frame. Its torsion tensor on invariant fields is
T(ea​,ec​)=−[ea​,ec​],Ta​bc​=−Ca​bc​.​
(27)
For each fixed c, antisymmetry gives Ta​bc​=Cc​ba​, so this is the matrix of adec​. The torsion contractions of a left-parallel Lie-group connection therefore are
Ta​ac​=Tr(adec​)=0,Ta​bc​Tb​ad​=Kcd​.​
(28)
The zero trace follows because a semisimple algebra is perfect: every element is a sum of brackets, and the adjoint of a bracket is a matrix commutator with zero trace. If the metric convention is g=−K, the second contraction is −gcd​. These flat translation connections on a Lie group are not the torsion-free Levi-Civita connection; nonzero torsion permits flatness alongside the curved Killing metric.
