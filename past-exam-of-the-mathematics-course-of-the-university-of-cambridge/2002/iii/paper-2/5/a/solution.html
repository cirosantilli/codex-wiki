Let V afford the given group representation of H. Its induced representation is the left G-module
IndHG​V=C[G]⊗C[H]​V​.
(23)
Choose representatives t of the left cosets G/H; then it is the direct sum of the spaces t⊗V. If gt=t′h with h∈H, the action sends t⊗v to t′⊗Ψ(h)v. This is independent of the representatives up to equivalence, and its dimension is [G:H]dimV.
Only cosets fixed by g contribute to its trace. Their contribution is ψ(t−1gt), giving the induced character formula
ψG(g)=tH∈G/Ht−1gt∈H​∑​ψ(t−1gt)=∣H∣1​x∈Gx−1gx∈H​∑​ψ(x−1gx)​.
(24)
For an arbitrary class function f on H, the same averaging formula defines its induced class function on G. Equivalently, extend f by zero outside H and average ∣H∣−1∑x​f(x−1gx). This definition is linear and does not require f to be an actual character.
For a complex character ψ of a finite group G afforded by a representation ρ, its determinant character is
(detψ)(g)=detρ(g)​.
(25)
Multiplicativity of the determinant makes this a linear character; it does not mean the determinant of the scalar value ψ(g). The definition depends only on the character because characters determine the equivalence class of a finite-dimensional complex representation. In particular the determinant character is trivial on the derived subgroup G′.
