The correct connected-diagram statement does hold, in particular for every knot diagram. If sA​,sB​ count the all-A and all-B bracket smoothing state circles, changing j smoothings increases the circle count by at most j. The maximum and minimum A exponents therefore lie between n+2sA​−2 and −n−2sB​+2. This gives the Kauffman bracket breadth bound brA​⟨D⟩≤2n+2sA​+2sB​−4. For a connected projection, the Turaev surface has Euler characteristic sA​+sB​−n≤2, so the bound is 4n. Writhe of a link diagram normalization changes only a monomial, and t=A−4 divides the breadth by four, giving brVL​≤n.
For a diagram with k projection components, sum the same Euler characteristic inequality over the components. Then sA​+sB​≤n+2k and the Jones polynomial breadth bound for a disconnected diagram is brVL​≤n+k−1. The extra k−1 is indispensable, as the unlink shows.
