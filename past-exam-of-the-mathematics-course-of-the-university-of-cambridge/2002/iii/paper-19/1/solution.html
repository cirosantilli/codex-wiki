⟨D⟩=s∑​Aa(s)−b(s)δ∣s∣−1,​

where a(s) and b(s) count its A and B smoothings and ∣s∣ is the number of state circles. Resolving all crossings proves that the recursive rules define the same polynomial independently of the order of expansion.
Under a positive Reidemeister move of type I, the two resolutions give Aδ+A−1=−A3 times the straight strand; the negative curl gives A−1δ+A=−A−3. Thus the bracket itself is not an unframed link invariant. For type II, use the adjacent cap-cup generator e of the Temperley-Lieb algebra, with e2=δe. RR−1=1+(A2+A−2+δ)e=1.

Hence the bracket is invariant under types II and III.
For an oriented diagram, let w(D) be its writhe of a link diagram, the sum of its signed crossings. Types II and III preserve writhe of a link diagram, while a positive or negative curl changes it by one or minus one. The Jones polynomial is therefore
VL​(t)=(−A3)−w(D)⟨D⟩,t=A−4,V◯​=1.​

The normalization cancels type I, so all Reidemeister moves preserve it. It is a Laurent polynomial in t1/2; its breadth means the largest occurring t exponent minus the smallest, including half-integral exponents for links.
