For an unrestricted binary relation R, the extension axioms permit arbitrary directed edges and loops. For each m≥0 and each choice of bits ϵi​,δi​,η∈{0,1}, require that every tuple of distinct x1​,…,xm​ has a fresh element y with
y=xi​for all i,R(y,y)η∧⋀i=1m​(R(y,xi​)ϵi​∧R(xi​,y)δi​).

Here P1 denotes P and P0 denotes ¬P; the notation is a literal abbreviation, not exponentiation of a relation. Incoming and outgoing choices are independent, and the loop bit is also specified. These are the extension axioms for an unrestricted binary relation. If one instead restricts to undirected loop-free graphs, the allowable patterns reduce to the random-graph extension axioms of Question 2; no such restriction is imposed here.
