Write the interaction matrix as J to distinguish it from its scalar strength. The printed pair sum contains both orientations and has no factor 1/2. For a positive-definite matrix J, completion of the square gives the Hubbard–Stratonovich transformation

Insert this identity into the Ising model partition sum and interchange the finite spin sum with the convergent Gaussian integral. The spin variables now factor independently:

The factor two in the spin-field coupling is required by the double pair sum; replacing it by one would decouple a statistical Hamiltonian with a different normalization. The diagonal Jii​σi2​=J is a spin-independent energy.
