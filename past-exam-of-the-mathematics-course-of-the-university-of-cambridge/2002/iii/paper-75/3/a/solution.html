Take the causally unwrapped universal cover of Anti-de Sitter spacetime, with curvature radius L. In global coordinates,
ds2=cos2ρL2​[−dT2+dρ2+sin2ρdΩd−22​],0≤ρ<π/2,T∈R.
(19)
The ordinary radial Penrose diagram is a half-strip: the left edge ρ=0 is a regular center and the right edge ρ=π/2 is a timelike conformal boundary. For displaying signed spatial motion, the figure doubles the radial diameter to −π/2<ρ<π/2. Its two ends represent antipodal spatial directions of the higher-dimensional boundary; they are not a claim that the full AdS boundary has two disconnected components.
The vertical edges are spatial infinity at every finite global time, forming the timelike boundary R×Sd−2. The arrows mark the future and past timelike ends T→±∞, conventionally i+ and i−. There are no separate null-infinity edges like Minkowski I±: radial null rays satisfy dT=±dρ and reach the timelike boundary in finite global time. Their physical affine parameter is nevertheless infinite there. The drawing is a finite excerpt of an infinitely tall strip, not an identification of its top and bottom. On the original hyperboloid global time is periodic and there are closed timelike curves; unwrapping it is essential to the usual physical maximally extended AdS spacetime.
