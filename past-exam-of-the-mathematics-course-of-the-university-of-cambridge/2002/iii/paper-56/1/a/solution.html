The equilibria have y=0 and x(λ+x2)=0. Thus
O=(0,0) for all parameters,P±​=(±−λ​,0) for λ<0.​
(1)
At O, the linearization has trace κ and determinant λ. Hence O is a saddle equilibrium when λ<0, a sink equilibrium when λ>0,κ<0, and a source equilibrium when λ>0,κ>0. The sink equilibrium/source equilibrium may be a node equilibrium or a focus equilibrium; the node-focus discriminant is not a bifurcation curve. At P±​, the trace and determinant are κ+λ and −2λ. These points are sink equilibria for κ<−λ and source equilibria for κ>−λ.
The local bifurcation curves are therefore
λ=0(pitchfork),κ=0, λ>0(Hopf at O),κ=−λ, λ<0(Hopf at P±​).​
(2)
For κ=0 on the pitchfork curve, a leading centre-manifold equation is x˙=(λx+x3)/κ+⋯. For κ<0, with unfolding parameter −λ, the pitchfork is supercritical and creates two sink equilibria from a sink equilibrium that becomes a saddle equilibrium. For κ>0 it is subcritical in the centre direction; the nonzero branches are unstable, and the transverse eigenvalue is also positive. The origin of parameter space is a reflection-symmetric double-zero degeneration.
To determine Hopf criticality at the origin, use the energy E=y2/2+λx2/2+x4/4, whose derivative is (κ−x2)y2. For a small nearly harmonic oscillation x=Rcos(λ​t), averaging gives R˙=κR/2−R3/8+⋯. Thus this Hopf bifurcation is supercritical and creates an attracting periodic orbit for κ>0. By the supplied opposite-criticality fact, the simultaneous Hopf bifurcations at the two nonzero equilibria are subcritical. A direct calculation confirms this: writing x=−λ​+X, Y=−y/−2λ​ puts the cubic radial coefficient at 1/4>0, including the contribution from the quadratic terms.
