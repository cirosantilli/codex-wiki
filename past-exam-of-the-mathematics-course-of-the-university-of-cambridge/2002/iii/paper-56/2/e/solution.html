OurBigBook.com Words: 150
The four nearby portrait types in u>0 can be organized by increasing μ at fixed λ>1/2 close to 1/2:
  • A: μ<−1/4. There is no interior equilibrium; trajectories approach the stable boundary equilibrium at the origin.
  • B: −1/4<μ<μH​. The lower interior equilibrium is a saddle equilibrium and the upper one a sink equilibrium. The saddle equilibrium's stable manifold separates trajectories approaching the sink equilibrium from those approaching the origin.
  • C: μH​<μ<μG​. The upper equilibrium is repelling, enclosed by an attracting periodic orbit. The saddle equilibrium's stable manifold separates the periodic orbit's basin from the origin's basin.
  • D: μ>μG​, still in the local neighborhood with μ<0. The saddle equilibrium and repelling upper equilibrium remain, but the periodic orbit has disappeared. Generic trajectories ultimately approach the origin.
The no-cycle region D continues around the double-zero point onto the λ<1/2 side. The sketches distinguish the boundary origin from the two interior equilibria and show the periodic orbit only in region C.
