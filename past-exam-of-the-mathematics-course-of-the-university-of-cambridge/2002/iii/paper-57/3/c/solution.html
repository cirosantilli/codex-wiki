The homogeneous comparison has size μ in both transmission factors; it must not be assigned the heterogeneous second moment. The general conclusions are enhanced invasion and selective removal of large susceptible farms. The final unweighted fraction of farms infected has no universal ordering relative to the homogeneous comparison: higher transmission and stronger heterogeneity-induced depletion compete, and the displayed final-size equations determine the answer for a specified distribution.
