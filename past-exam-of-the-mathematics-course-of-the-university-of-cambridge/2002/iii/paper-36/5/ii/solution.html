With the negative-binomial size θ>0 held fixed, collect all mean-independent terms into c(yi​,θ). The cell log-likelihood is
ℓi​(μi​)=c(yi​,θ)+yi​logμi​−(yi​+θ)log(μi​+θ).
(39)
Its derivative is
ℓi′​(μi​)=μi​yi​​−μi​+θyi​+θ​=μi​(μi​+θ)θ(yi​−μi​)​.
(40)
For yi​>0 it is positive below yi​ and negative above yi​, so the saturated maximum is at μi​=yi​. For yi​=0, the maximum is the boundary limit μi​↓0. Evaluating the saturated-minus-fitted log-likelihood gives the negative binomial deviance
Dn​=2i∑​yi​logei​yi​​−2i∑​(yi​+θ)logei​+θyi​+θ​​.
(41)
All gamma-function and factorial terms cancel because the same known θ is used in both models. The zero-count logarithmic term is interpreted as zero; its remaining cell contribution is 2θlog(1+ei​/θ)≥0. No intercept score cancellation is required for this formula, so it applies to the specified design whether or not its covariates include an intercept.
For completeness, the fitted coefficients obey the score equations of the fixed-size negative binomial generalized linear model,
∑i​xi​θ+ei​θ(yi​−ei​)​=0,ei​=exp(β​Txi​).
(42)
The logarithmic link is not the canonical link for this family, but it produces the requested positive means. As a check, when θ→∞,
(yi​+θ)logei​+θyi​+θ​⟶yi​−ei​,
(43)
so the formula tends to the full Poisson deviance, including the linear terms when an intercept-balance equation is unavailable. The size θ used here is the negative-binomial size parameter; it is distinct from the random-multiplier variance denoted by the same letter in Question 4.
