Take the symmetric scalar Landau free energy with u>0, r=at for a positive constant a, and leading equation of state
H=rM+uM3.
(13)
At zero conjugate field, minimization gives M=0 for t>0 and M=±−r/u​ for t<0. Selecting the positive pure thermodynamic phase,
M=ua​​(−t)1/2.
(14)
Consequently the order-parameter critical exponent is β=1/2​.
Differentiate the equation of state at fixed temperature, within the selected phase:
χ=∂H∂M​​t​=r+3uM21​={(at)−1,(2a∣t∣)−1,​t>0,t<0.​
(15)
Both sides diverge as ∣t∣−1, so the magnetic-susceptibility critical exponent is γ=1​. The leading above-to-below magnetic susceptibility amplitude ratio is two. These are the ordinary mean-field critical exponents, valid under the fluctuation qualification explained above.
