This belongs to the lattice-ordered permutation group. At αi​, all terms in its ith inner meet equal βi​. At αk​, every inner meet is at most βk​, because its j=k term takes that value. The outer join is consequently exactly βk​ there. Thus h maps the whole tuple to its target. This finite interpolation by lattice operations proves every finite order-transitivity without assuming the group permits arbitrary cut-and-paste restrictions of its elements.
