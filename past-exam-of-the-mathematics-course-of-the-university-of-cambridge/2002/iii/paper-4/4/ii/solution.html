OurBigBook.com Words: 196
Construct the conjugacy of positive real-line bumps using u=v=α. Since both αf and αg are greater than β, choose the fundamental-interval map to be identity on [α,β] and then increase from β to the respective right endpoints. Equivariant extension along f and g gives a conjugator on their supports fixing [α,β]. Extend it over the remaining portions of J=[γ,γh], fixing the endpoints of J; both support intervals lie strictly inside J. Call the resulting interval bijection b0​. It satisfies fb0​=b0​g on J.
The point γ lies in a positive orbital of h, because γh>γ. Its half-open cells Jhn, n∈Z, partition that orbital. Extend by
(thn)b=(tb0​)hn,γ≤t<γh,
(20)
and set b to identity outside the orbital. Since b0​ fixes the cell endpoints, this is an order automorphism and satisfies bh=hb. Each cell is preserved; f and g are identity on all cells other than the original one, so fb=bg holds everywhere. Finally b fixes [α,β] pointwise, and a is supported inside that interval. Hence ba=ab. Thus
b−1fb=g,ba=ab,bh=hb​.
(21)
This is conjugating positive bumps while preserving a fundamental interval; merely choosing an arbitrary conjugator would not ensure either commutation relation.
