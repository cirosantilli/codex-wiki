For the complex scalar field, the canonical momenta are π=ϕ˙​† and π†=ϕ˙​. Split H=H0​+HI​. The fields therefore obey the free Klein-Gordon equation, while the states evolve through HI​(t). The canonical quantization of a complex scalar field gives
ϕI​(x)=∫dΠp​[a(p)e−ip⋅x+b†(p)eip⋅x],dΠp​=(2π)32Ep​d3p​.

The adjoint expansion contains a† and b. Particle and antiparticle modes are independent; their nonzero commutators are
[a(p),a†(q)]=[b(p),b†(q)]=(2π)32Ep​δ(3)(p−q).​

All cross-species commutators, and all pairs of creation operators or of annihilation operators, vanish. These formulas reproduce [ϕ(x),π(y)]=[ϕ†(x),π†(y)]=iδ(3)(x−y) and the remaining vanishing equal-time commutators.
There are no derivative interactions, so the interaction Hamiltonian is HI​(t)=−∫d3xLI​(x). Differentiating the interaction picture state gives i∂t​∣ΨI​⟩=HI​(t)∣ΨI​⟩. Its time-evolution operator satisfies
U(t,t0​)=I−i∫t0​t​dt1​HI​(t1​)U(t1​,t0​).

Iteration gives ordered integration regions t1​>⋯>tn​. Each is 1/n! of the full region with a time-ordered product, yielding the Dyson series
U(t,t0​)=∑n=0∞​n!(−i)n​∫t0​t​dt1​⋯dtn​T[HI​(t1​)⋯HI​(tn​)].

With the usual asymptotic scattering prescription, taking t0​→−∞ and t→+∞ proves
S=Texp(i∫d4xLI​(x)).​

Time ordering is essential because different interaction Hamiltonians need not commute.
At first order, use the connected tree scattering amplitude, equivalently the normal-ordered quartic vertex with the vacuum and one-particle mass contributions removed. This qualification matters if the local product is interpreted literally without normal ordering: single-vertex tadpole diagrams and vacuum diagrams are not the requested connected two-particle amplitude. The complex scalar quartic contact vertex is obtained from
S−I=−4iλ​∫d4x:ϕ†2(x)ϕ2(x):+O(λ2).

In a fully connected matrix element, an incoming particle and outgoing antiparticle attach to the two ϕ factors; an outgoing particle and incoming antiparticle attach to the two ϕ† factors. Each pair has two assignments, giving 2!2!=4. The external relativistic normalization of a one-particle state cancels each mode measure: for example [ϕ(x),a†(p1​)]=e−ip1​x and [b(q2​),ϕ(x)]=eiq2​x. Consequently the connected integrand is 4e−i(p1​+q1​−p2​−q2​)⋅x, and
⟨p2​q2​∣(S−I)∣p1​q1​⟩conn​=−iλ(2π)4δ(4)(p1​+q1​−p2​−q2​)+O(λ2).

Thus the invariant connected amplitude is
T=−λ+O(λ2).​

The Dirac delta function expresses four-momentum conservation.
For the identity contribution, commute a(p2​) through a†(p1​) and independently b(q2​) through b†(q1​); all terms leaving a creation operator against the left Fock vacuum or an annihilation operator against the right Fock vacuum vanish. Hence
