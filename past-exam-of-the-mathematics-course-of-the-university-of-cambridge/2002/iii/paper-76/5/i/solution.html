The syntax operation (s,t)↦#(ST), where s=#S,t=#T, is a total computable function on valid codes: decode the finite syntax trees, form their application, and encode it again. Extend it arbitrarily on invalid codes if necessary. Similarly n↦#(cn​) is total computable because the finite numeral syntax can be generated effectively from n. The preceding characterization of lambda-definable functions supplies closed representatives A,C with

Here d(S)=c#S​ is lambda quotation, not a code of a beta equivalence class. In particular C quotes the syntax of the numeral itself; it is not simply successor or an identity operation.
