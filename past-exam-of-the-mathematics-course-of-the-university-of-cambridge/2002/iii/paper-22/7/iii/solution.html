Let yn​=C(−,n). Suppose two sections f,g∈yn​(m) have equal restrictions on a covering sieve on a category R on m. Such a sieve on a category is nonempty, so choose h:l→m in it. Then fh=gh. Applying hypothesis (b) with the two right-hand factors both h gives f=g, since f,g have the same source and target. Hence every representable is separated for J.
In the associated-sheaf construction, sections of F+ are matching families modulo agreement after covering refinement. The map F→F+ identifies two original sections only if they agree on a covering sieve on a category. Thus it is monic when F is separated. The plus construction is itself separated, and applying it a second time gives the associated sheaf; its second unit is monic as well. Applying this to yn​ proves the unit is a monomorphism
yn​↪An​=aJ​yn​.​

The same construction shows the unit image is dense: a section of F++ is locally represented by sections of F+, which are in turn locally represented by sections of F, and transitivity combines the two covering refinements.
