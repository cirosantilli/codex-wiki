Choose the poloidal magnetic field convention Bp​=∇×(Ay^​)=(−Az​,0,Ax​). The term αB regenerates this poloidal flux function from the toroidal magnetic field through the alpha effect. It represents a component of the mean-field electromotive force produced by correlated velocity and magnetic fluctuations. The shear G=πV/d stretches the poloidal vertical component Bp,z​=Ax​ into toroidal field, giving GAx​: this is the Omega effect. Both η∇2 terms diffuse and damp the field. In a turbulent mean-field model, η can denote an effective diffusivity including turbulent transport. The alpha-Omega dynamo approximation retains the shear-dominated toroidal source, and the kinematic dynamo assumption prescribes the velocity and coefficients without their Lorentz force feedback.
For a horizontal wavenumber k and vertical wavenumber π/d, set K2=k2+π2/d2 and seek amplitudes proportional to est. The two modal equations are
(s+ηK2)A=αB,(s+ηK2)B=ikGA.
(9)
A nonzero mode requires the dispersion relation
(s+ηK2)2=iαGk.
(10)
For real nonzero αGk, the root with positive real square-root part is
s+​=−ηK2+2∣αGk∣​​(1+isgn(αGk)).
(11)
The other root has strictly negative real part. For k=0 there is no oscillatory feedback and both roots have real part −ηπ2/d2; the possible triangular-system prefactor cannot yield exponential growth.

ηπ2/d2ℜs+​​=−(1+h2)+2∣D∣h​​.
(12)
Consequently the plane-layer alpha-Omega dynamo threshold at fixed h is
∣D∣>Dc​(h):=h2(1+h2)2​.
(13)
To find its least value, differentiate Dc​(h)=2(h−1+2h+h3):
Dc′​(h)=2(−h−2+2+3h2)=0⟺3h4+2h2−1=0.
(14)
The unique positive solution is h2=1/3. The threshold diverges at both ends h↓0 and h→∞, so this is its global minimum. Hence
∣D∣c​=33​32​,∣kc​∣=3​dπ​.​
(15)
With the usual positive-D convention, the onset value is Dc​=32/(33​) and exponentially growing waves exist for D>Dc​. Exactly at the threshold the real part is zero, so it is a neutral dynamo wave: the quoted minimum is the onset threshold, or infimum for strict growth, not a growing solution at equality. If signed D is allowed, the growth condition concerns ∣D∣; there is no smallest signed negative value among all growing cases.
The real field is obtained by taking the real part of a Fourier mode. Its phase is kx+(ℑs+​)t+πz/d, so the horizontal phase speed is −(ℑs+​)/k. Reversing the sign of αV reverses propagation, while leaving the growth threshold unchanged. Reversing k gives the conjugate real-field representation.
