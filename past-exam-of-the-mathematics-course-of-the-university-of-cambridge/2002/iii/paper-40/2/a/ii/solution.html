The unknown common factor Zg​/Zf​ cancels. Under full target probability support and integrability, the numerator and denominator sample means converge to ∫f​θ/Zg​ and ∫f​/Zg​, respectively, whose ratio is μ. The same construction applies if only one normalization is unknown by taking the other probability density function already normalized. Sampling from g itself is assumed available, even when its normalization is not explicitly known.
This ratio estimator is generally biased at finite n, unlike the ordinary normalized-weight estimator. Also, its variance is not the formula in the next part: that formula concerns the ordinary unbiased estimator. Full probability support for f is needed here because the denominator estimates the entire target normalizing integral, not just the probability support of fθ.
