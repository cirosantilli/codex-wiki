v(m+1)=n1​i=1∑n​j=1∑2​wij(m)​(xi​−μj(m+1)​)2.​
(45)
The residuals use the new component means, while the weights remain from the old parameters. The divisor is n, not n−2, because this is a maximum-likelihood update. Starting with 0<α<1 and v>0 gives positive responsibilities and positive nj​; a zero effective component size at a boundary would leave its mean unidentified. These are the EM for Gaussian mixtures with a common variance transitions.
