The order prior and probabilities of choosing upward or downward moves are not specified, so include them explicitly. Let ρk​ be the model-order prior probability, bk​ the birth-selection probability at order k, and dk+1​ the reverse death-selection probability. Denote the correctly normalized coefficient prior by pk​(a) and the probability density function of z∼N(0,σa2​) by q(z). Common variance-prior factors cancel since v=σ2 is unchanged.
Put m=n−1∑i​xik+1​, r=y−Xk​a, and wi​=xik+1​−m. The centered polynomial birth move in reversible-jump sampling gives Xk+1​a′=Xk​a+zw, so the residual-sum-of-squares change is
∥r−zw∥2−∥r∥2=z2wTw−2zwTr.
(25)
The map (a0​,a1​,…,ak​,z)↦(a0​−mz,a1​,…,ak​,z) is triangular with determinant one. Thus the reversible-jump Markov chain Monte Carlo ratio is
R=ρk​bk​ρk+1​dk+1​​pk​(a)q(z)pk+1​(a′)​exp(2v2zwTr−z2wTw​),α↑​=min(1,R).
(26)
