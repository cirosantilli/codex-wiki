In the Bühlmann model, one policy has a random risk parameter Θ. Conditional on Θ, its annual observations Xi​ are independent and identically distributed random variables, with mean m(Θ) and variance v(Θ). Put m0​=E[m(Θ)], let v=E[v(Θ)] be the expected process variance, and let a=Var(m(Θ)) be the variance of hypothetical means. Assume the required second moments are finite.
