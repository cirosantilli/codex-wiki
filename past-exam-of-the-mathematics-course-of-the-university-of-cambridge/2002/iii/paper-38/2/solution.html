For an interest parameter ψ and nuisance parameter λ, the profile likelihood is Lp​(ψ)=L(ψ,λψ​), where λψ​ maximizes the likelihood at fixed ψ. Its maximum occurs at the joint maximum-likelihood estimate of ψ. It preserves the optimized likelihood ratios for different interest values and is unchanged by one-to-one reparametrization of the nuisance at each fixed ψ. It generally is neither a marginal sampling density nor a conditional likelihood, and eliminating nuisance parameters by maximization can introduce appreciable small-sample distortion. Under the regular conditions of Wilks theorem, twice the profile log-likelihood drop has an asymptotic chi-squared distribution with the dimension of the interest parameter as its degrees of freedom.
