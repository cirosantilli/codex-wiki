For completeness, the contour estimate used in Question 2 follows from these very formulas. Subtract the complex logarithmic derivative expansion at 2+it from that at σ+it, with 1−b/(2log(T+3))≤σ≤2 and ∣t∣≤T. Terms with ∣t−γ∣>1 have differences O((t−γ)−2), whose sum is O(log(∣t∣+3)) by the kernel bound. There are O(log(∣t∣+3)) remaining terms. After reducing b, the zero-free region of the Riemann zeta function separates every such zero horizontally from the half-width boundary by ≫1/log(T+3): their heights satisfy ∣γ∣≤T+1. Thus on the left edge, and on the horizontal edges at t=±T, each reciprocal is O(log(T+3)). The Gamma function and pole terms satisfy the same required bound; at small heights on the left edge the pole distance is ≫1/log(T+3). This proves ∣ζ′/ζ∣≪log2(T+3) on those edges and justifies the contour shift without choosing heights that dodge unknown zeros.
