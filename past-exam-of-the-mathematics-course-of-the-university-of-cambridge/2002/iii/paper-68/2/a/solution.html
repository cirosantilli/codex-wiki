At fixed nonzero four-momentum, take the finite physical supermultiplet and write PF​=(−1)F for fermion parity. Each supercharge is odd, so PF​Qα​=−Qα​PF​ and the same holds for its adjoint. By the cyclic property of the trace,
Tr(PF​Qα​Qα†​)=Tr(Qα†​PF​Qα​)=−Tr(PF​Qα†​Qα​).
(10)
Consequently Tr[PF​{Qα​,Qα†​}]=0. Sum the two spinor components of the N=1 Super-Poincaré algebra. Since Tr2×2​σi=0, their sum is 4H. On a fixed-energy representation, H=EI, yielding
0=4ETrPF​=4E(nB​−nF​).
(11)
For a nonzero physical one-particle momentum, E>0, including the massless case. Hence the bosonic and fermionic physical state counts agree:
nB​=nF​.​
(12)
Equivalently a nonzero normalized supercharge and its adjoint define an odd involution pairing the two subspaces. This is supertrace pairing at positive energy; it counts spin polarizations, not merely distinct names of particles.
The phrase “every supermultiplet” requires this positive-energy qualification. A zero-energy supersymmetric vacuum with all supercharges acting as zero is a one-dimensional bosonic representation with no fermionic partner. It is a counterexample to the unrestricted statement, because division by E is then impossible. It does not contradict the requested equality for particle supermultiplets.
