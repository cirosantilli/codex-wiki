Here spectral mapping theorem follows by factoring p(z)−μ: its commuting factors are invertible exactly when every root avoids the Banach algebra spectrum.
The rule p(x)↦p(λ) is well-defined by the displayed bound, multiplicative and bounded on the dense polynomial subalgebra. It extends to a unital character φλ​ on B. Since φλ​(λ1−x)=0, that element cannot have an inverse in B. Thus U⊆σB​(x). Together with part (iii), this proves that the Banach algebra spectrum of a polynomial-generated Banach subalgebra is exactly the original Banach algebra spectrum with all its bounded complementary components filled in.
