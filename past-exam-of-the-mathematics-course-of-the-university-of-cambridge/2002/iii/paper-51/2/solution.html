The scalar-gradient inclination in a steady planar strain is the tangent of the angle between the scalar's normal and the horizontal plane. Equivalently, it is the cotangent of the inclination of an isoconcentration surface to that plane. Large α corresponds to nearly horizontal scalar surfaces; small α corresponds to nearly vertical ones.

If the strain varies slowly, the horizontal gradient must align with the current growing direction, and the vertical-to-horizontal ratio must adjust before that strain changes. Both adjustment times are of order c−1. Thus the local condition is cτσ​≫1, and the typical condition for the specified statistics is
