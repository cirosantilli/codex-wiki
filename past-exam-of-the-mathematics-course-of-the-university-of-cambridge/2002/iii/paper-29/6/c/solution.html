Let Ds​=exp(−∫0s​Ru​du), where Ru​=Fu,u​ is the short rate. At maturity the zero-coupon bond pays one, so ZT,T​=DT​. If the discounted bond price is a martingale, its terminal conditional expectation is
Ds​Ps,T​=Zs,T​=E[DT​∣Fs​].
(49)
The positive factor Ds​ is measurable with respect to the current filtration. Dividing it out gives
Ps,T​=E[exp(−∫sT​Ru​du)​Fs​].​
(50)
Conversely, this identity says Zs,T​=E[DT​∣Fs​]. The tower property gives E[Zs,T​∣Fr​]=Zr,T​ for every r≤s, establishing the martingale condition. The integrated short rate is a Gaussian random variable with finite variance, so the Gaussian moment-generating function proves that DT​ is integrable. Together with the preceding subparts, this proves all three descriptions equivalent.
