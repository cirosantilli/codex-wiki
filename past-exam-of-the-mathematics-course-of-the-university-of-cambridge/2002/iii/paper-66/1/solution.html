Use Q=T3​+Y and Ta​=τa​/2. For each generation r=e,μ,τ, the leptons are
Lr​=(ℓrL​νrL​​): (2,−21​),Rr​=ℓrR​: (1,−1).
(1)
Both are color singlets. The conjugate antilepton fields transform in the conjugate representations and have opposite hypercharge; they are already included by the conjugate fields in the Lagrangian. The two components of Lr​ have electric charges 0,−1, and Rr​ has charge −1. There is no right-handed neutrino representation in this field content. These assignments use the electroweak representation and hypercharge table in the same hypercharge normalization as the scalar doublet.
For a doublet of hypercharge Y, define
Dμ​=∂μ​−ig2τa​​Aaμ​−ig′YBμ​.
(2)
The SU(2) term is absent for a singlet. Under the local transformation ψ↦eiYωUψ, the gauge fields transform so that Dμ​ψ↦eiYωUDμ​ψ. Consequently the required gauge-covariant kinetic terms are
Lkin​=∑r​(iLˉr​γμDμ​Lr​+iRˉr​γμDμ​Rr​)+(Dμ​ϕ)†Dμϕ.
(3)
The fields of definite chirality are defined with PL,R​=(1∓γ5​)/2. Since Lˉ has hypercharge +1/2, the Yukawa interaction
LY​=−∑r,s​Lˉr​ϕ(Yℓ​)rs​Rs​+h.c.
(4)
is invariant: its hypercharges add to zero, and Lˉϕ contracts the two doublet indices. A bare charged-lepton mass violates these assignments. No renormalizable neutrino Yukawa term is available without a right-handed neutrino. A gauge-invariant scalar potential depending on ϕ†ϕ can select the nonzero vacuum, without altering these kinetic and Yukawa constructions.
There is an explicit local unitary gauge transformation whenever rϕ​=(∣ϕ1​∣2+∣ϕ2​∣2)1/2=0:
Uϕ​=rϕ​1​(ϕ2​ϕ1∗​​−ϕ1​ϕ2∗​​),Uϕ†​Uϕ​=I,detUϕ​=1,Uϕ​ϕ=(rϕ​0​).
(5)
Thus, with the real field ρ=2​rϕ​−v,
ϕ=2​v+ρ​(10​).​
(6)
This proves the polar coordinates for the electroweak Higgs doublet constructively. The gauge choice is local about the nonzero vacuum; it need not be nonsingular at zeros of the doublet.
The three identical-generation kinetic terms permit unitary flavor changes. A singular value decomposition gives UL†​Yℓ​UR​=diag(ye​,yμ​,yτ​) with yr​≥0. Rotate both components of each left doublet together, and the right singlets independently. In this basis,
LY​=−r∑​mr​(1+vρ​)ℓˉr​ℓr​,mr​=2​yr​v​.​
(7)
The left/right cross terms combine into this Dirac mass. Since the neutrinos have no mass matrix, their simultaneous left rotation preserves a diagonal charged current. This is charged-lepton Yukawa matrix diagonalization; the neutrinos remain massless in the stated renormalizable model.
The scalar kinetic term gives
(Dμ​ϕ)†Dμϕ=21​(∂μ​ρ)2+8(v+ρ)2​[g2(A1μ​A1μ​+A2μ​A2μ​)+(gA3μ​−g′Bμ​)2].
(8)
Introduce sW​=g′/g2+g′2​ and cW​=g/g2+g′2​. The combinations W+=(A1​−iA2​)/2​, W−=(W+)∗, Z=cW​A3​−sW​B and Aγ​=sW​A3​+cW​B therefore diagonalize the quadratic terms:
Lmass​=MW2​Wμ+​W−μ+21​MZ2​Zμ​Zμ,MW​=2gv​,MZ​=2vg2+g′2​​=cW​MW​​,Mγ​=0.​
(9)
The factor 1/2 applies to the real neutral field, not to the complex charged field. The massless photon corresponds to the unbroken generator T3​+Y, which annihilates the vacuum. This gives the electroweak doublet gauge-boson mass matrix and its diagonal spectrum.
