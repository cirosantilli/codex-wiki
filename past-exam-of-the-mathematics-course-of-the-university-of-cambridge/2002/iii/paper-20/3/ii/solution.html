OurBigBook.com Words: 103
Let 1 be a terminal object. The pullback in a category of X→1←Y has precisely the universal property of the binary product in a category X×Y: every pair of morphisms to X,Y is compatible over 1. Repeated binary products in a category, together with 1 as the empty product, give all finite products in a category.
For f,g:X⇉Y, form the pullback in a category of the categorical diagonal ΔY​:Y→Y×Y along ⟨f,g⟩:X→Y×Y. Its projection e:E→X satisfies fe=ge. If h:T→X satisfies fh=gh, the pair h,fh gives a unique morphism into this pullback in a category. Conversely any such morphism must project to that pair. Hence e is the equalizer of f,g. The preceding construction now gives all finite limits.
