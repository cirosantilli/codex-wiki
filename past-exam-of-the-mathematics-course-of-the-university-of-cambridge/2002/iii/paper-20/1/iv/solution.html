We first justify the relevant pointwise epimorphism in a functor category criterion. A componentwise surjective function gives an epimorphism, because equality of two composites can be cancelled at each component. Conversely, for a natural transformation t:X→Y, form the pointwise pushout P=Y⨿X​Y. Restrictions of Y preserve the identifications imposed by t, so these pointwise sets and restrictions form a categorical presheaf. The two inclusions j1​,j2​:Y→P satisfy j1​t=j2​t. If t is epic, j1​=j2​. At an object U, an element outside the image of tU​ would retain two distinct copies in P(U), contradicting this equality. Thus every tU​ is surjective.
