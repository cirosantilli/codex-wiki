The category of elements ∫X has objects (W,x) for x∈X(W), and morphisms f:(W,x)→(V,y) with X(f)y=x. Send (W,x) to the representable presheaf HW​, and a morphism f to H∙​(f). The element x supplies a natural transformation HW​→X with component h↦X(h)x, by the Yoneda lemma. These transformations form a cocone.
Pointwise at U, the colimit of this diagram in a category has representatives (W,x,h:U→W), with relation (W,X(f)y,h)∼(V,y,fh). These are exactly the relations of the preceding categorical coend. Its proved natural isomorphism with X(U) therefore identifies the cocone with the universal colimit cocone. More directly, every representative equals (U,X(h)x,1U​) through the morphism h:(U,X(h)x)→(W,x); two representatives with the same image therefore agree. As C is small, ∫X is small, so this is an ordinary small colimit. Consequently
