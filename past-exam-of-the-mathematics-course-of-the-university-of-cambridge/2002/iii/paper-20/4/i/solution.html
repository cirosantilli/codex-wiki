For D:I→C, define the functor Cone(−,D):Cop→Set by
Cone(X,D)=Nat(ΔX,D),
(7)
where ΔX is the constant diagram functor; a morphism into X acts by precomposition on every categorical cone leg. A categorical limit is a representation of a functor of the form
C(−,L)≅Cone(−,D).​
(8)
The image of 1L​ is the universal categorical cone λ:ΔL→D. By naturality of the representing isomorphism, the image of h:X→L is λ∘Δh. Thus this definition is exactly existence and uniqueness of factorization of every categorical cone through λ. Conversely that universal property gives the representing natural bijection.
