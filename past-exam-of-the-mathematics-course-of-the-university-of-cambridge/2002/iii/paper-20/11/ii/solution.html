Write the adjunction unit and adjunction counit of F⊣G as η,ε. Its induced monad is T=GF, with multiplication μ=GεF​. The Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor is
K(d)=(Gd,ad​),ad​=Gεd​:T(Gd)→Gd,K(h)=Gh.
(47)
The unit law for ad​ is Gεd​ηGd​=1Gd​, a triangle identity for an adjunction. Naturality of ε at εd​ gives εd​FGεd​=εd​εFGd​; applying G gives ad​Tad​=ad​μGd​. Naturality at h gives the monad algebra morphism equation for Gh. Thus K is well-defined.
For each d, consider the fork in D
FGFGd εFGd​​FGεd​​​ FGdεd​​d.
(48)
It commutes by the naturality equation just used. Its image under G is the fork T2(Gd)⇉T(Gd)→Gd of the preceding question, with parallel morphisms μGd​,Tad​ and final map ad​. It is split using s=ηGd​ and t=ηT(Gd)​. By the assumed reflection, εd​ is the coequalizer of this pair in D.
For faithfulness, if Gh=Gk for h,k:d→e, naturality gives hεd​=εe​FGh=εe​FGk=kεd​. A coequalizer is an epimorphism, so h=k.
For fullness, let u:K(d)→K(e) be a monad algebra morphism. Thus u:Gd→Ge satisfies uad​=ae​Tu. Set v=εe​Fu:FGd→e. We verify explicitly that v equalizes the two morphisms of the displayed fork. The monad algebra morphism equation gives
vFGεd​=εe​F(uGεd​)=εe​FGεe​FGFu.
(49)
Naturality of ε first at Fu and then at εe​ gives
vεFGd​=εe​εFGe​FGFu=εe​FGεe​FGFu.
(50)
The coequalizer therefore supplies a unique h:d→e with hεd​=v. Applying G yields Ghad​=Gv=ae​Tu=uad​. The map ad​ is split epic with section ηGd​, so cancellation gives Gh=u. This proves fullness, and the earlier cancellation proves faithfulness. Hence
K:D→CT is full and faithful.​
(51)
No claim that K is essentially surjective is needed; that stronger conclusion needs the corresponding existence hypotheses.
