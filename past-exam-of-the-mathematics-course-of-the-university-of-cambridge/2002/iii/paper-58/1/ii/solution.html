At the double threshold, Lψ0​=Lψ1​=0. For j≥2, qj2​≥9π2/4, so ∣K∗−qj2​∣≥13π2/8>3π2/8 and every remaining eigenvalue is strictly negative. There is a spectral gap separating this two-dimensional kernel from the stable modes. The centre manifold theorem therefore gives a two-dimensional state centre manifold, with
ψ=Aψ0​+Bψ1​+h(A,B),h=O((∣A∣+∣B∣)2),⟨h,ψj​⟩=0(j=0,1).
(3)
If the two detuning parameters are adjoined as variables with zero time derivatives, the extended centre manifold has total dimension four; its state fibers still have dimension two.
The only nontrivial spatial symmetry of the bounded interval is the reflection x↦−x. Since ψ0​ is even and ψ1​ odd, its group action on the amplitudes is (A,B)↦(A,−B). Thus equivariance requires A˙ to be even and B˙ odd in B. There is no sign symmetry ψ↦−ψ, because the quadratic nonlinearity breaks it. The general smooth form is A˙=F(A,B2), B˙=BG(A,B2), with the zero solution preserved. The quadratic even-odd mode interaction follows on truncation:
A˙=λ1​A+a1​A2+a2​B2+O(3),B˙=λ2​B+a3​AB+O(3).
(4)
Writing m=μ−μ∗ and κ=K−K∗, the exact linear growth rates are m−3π2κ/4−κ2 and m+3π2κ/4−κ2. To first order in detuning,
λ1​=m−43π2​κ,λ2​=m+43π2​κ.​
(5)
The orthogonal projection of the quadratic term onto the neutral eigenfunctions determines the leading coefficients. Both eigenfunctions have squared L2 norm one, and parity kills the unwanted products. The quadratic correction h contributes no term through Lh, since L is self-adjoint and the projected modes lie in its kernel. Consequently
a1​=∫−11​ψ03​dx,a2​=∫−11​ψ0​ψ12​dx,a3​=2∫−11​ψ0​ψ12​dx.
(6)
These projections are also a concrete way of computing the normal form. In this normalization they give a1​=8/(3π), a2​=32/(15π) and a3​=64/(15π), consistent with the stated coefficient ratios.
