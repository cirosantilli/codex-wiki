The Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra acts on the algebra itself: adX​(Y)=[X,Y]. In the basis Tb​, its matrices are
(Taad​)cb​=ccab​.​
(27)
For any algebra element X, the Jacobi identity gives
[Taad​,Tbad​]X=[Ta​,[Tb​,X]]−[Tb​,[Ta​,X]]=[[Ta​,Tb​],X].
(28)
Therefore the D×D matrices satisfy the same Lie bracket relations:
[Taad​,Tbad​]=ccab​Tcad​.​
(29)
This construction does not require that the algebra be simple or that the adjoint action be faithful.
For the field calculation, write Aμ​=Aμa​Ta​ and λ=λaTa​. In the derivative-plus-connection convention the Yang-Mills field strength is
Fμν​=∂μ​Aν​−∂ν​Aμ​+[Aμ​,Aν​],δAμ​=∂μ​λ+[Aμ​,λ].
(30)
The sign of the parameter here is the negative of the matter-field parameter in the usual gauge transformation in the derivative-plus-connection convention. Work with the stated λ throughout. Expanding the variation gives
δFμν​=​[∂μ​Aν​−∂ν​Aμ​,λ]+[Aν​,∂μ​λ]−[Aμ​,∂ν​λ]+[∂μ​λ,Aν​]+[Aμ​,∂ν​λ]+[[Aμ​,λ],Aν​]+[Aμ​,[Aν​,λ]].​
(31)
The second derivatives cancel by commutativity of partial derivatives, and all remaining derivatives of λ cancel by antisymmetry of the Lie bracket. The Jacobi identity makes the last two terms [[Aμ​,Aν​],λ]. Hence
δFμν​=[Fμν​,λ],δFμνa​=cabc​Fμνb​λc.​
(32)
Unlike the connection, the curvature has no inhomogeneous derivative term: it transforms in the Adjoint representation.
The quadratic form κab​=tr(Taad​Tbad​) is the Killing form. Its symmetry follows from cyclicity of the matrix trace. Using the just-proved adjoint commutators,
cdca​κdb​+cdcb​κad​​=tr([Tcad​,Taad​]Tbad​+Taad​[Tcad​,Tbad​])=tr[Tcad​,Taad​Tbad​]=0.​
(33)
Thus
cdca​κdb​+cdcb​κad​=0.​
(34)
Equivalently, each adjoint generator is skew with respect to this invariant bilinear form. This invariance holds even when the Killing form is degenerate; no inverse form is needed here.
The spacetime metric used to raise Lorentz indices is unaffected by the internal gauge transformation. Since δF=−adλ​F, invariance gives
δ(κab​FaμνFμνb​)​=κ(δFμν,Fμν​)+κ(Fμν,δFμν​)=−λc[cdca​κdb​+cdcb​κad​]FaμνFμνb​=0.​
(35)
Therefore the Killing-form contraction of the field strengths is gauge invariant, as required for the internal contraction in a Yang-Mills action.
