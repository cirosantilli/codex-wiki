Let (qR​,qS​,qP​) be player two's mixed strategy in Rock paper scissors. The respective expected payoffs from choosing rock, scissors and paper are 1−p, −p and 2p−1. Thus player two solves the linear programming problem
max (1−p)qR​−pqS​+(2p−1)qP​,qR​,qS​,qP​≥0,qR​+qS​+qP​=1.
(30)
A linear objective on this probability simplex is maximized by a largest-payoff pure response, or by any mixture of tied maximizers. Scissors is never a maximizing response: rock exceeds it by one. Comparing the other two gives 1−p⋛2p−1 according as p⋚2/3. Therefore
⎩⎨⎧​(qR​,qS​,qP​)=(1,0,0),(qR​,qS​,qP​)=(q,0,1−q), 0≤q≤1,(qR​,qS​,qP​)=(0,0,1),​p<2/3,p=2/3,p>2/3.​​
(31)
The optimal expected payoff is 1−p below the tie and 2p−1 above it, with value 1/3 at the tie.
