With metric signature (+,−,−,−), four-momentum is pμ=(E/c,p); for a particle of rest mass m it satisfies pμpμ​=m2c2. For a massive particle pμ=muμ, where uμ is its four-velocity. The total four-momentum is the sum of the individual four-momenta. If that sum is timelike, its center-of-momentum frame exists and the total energy in that frame is
W=(E1​+E2​)2−c2∣p1​+p2​∣2​.​
(47)
This follows because the spatial momentum is zero in that frame and the Minkowski norm is invariant under a Lorentz boost. A null total momentum, such as parallel photons, would not have such a rest frame.
For the photon striking an electron initially at rest, the initial invariant gives W2=me2​c4+2me​c2Eγ​. The three final particles have total rest energy 3me​c2. Their center-of-momentum energy is at least this value, with equality when all have zero relative momentum. Energy-momentum conservation therefore requires
me2​c4+2me​c2Eγ​≥9me2​c4,Eγ​≥4me​c2.​
(48)
The photon-induced triplet pair-production threshold is consequently four electron rest-energy units. This is attainable as a kinematic threshold: at equality the center-of-momentum speed in the laboratory is 4c/5, and boosting the three resting final particles gives each energy 5me​c2/3 and momentum 4me​c/3. Their totals are the initial 5me​c2 and 4me​c. Recoil of the original electron is why twice the rest energy is insufficient.
