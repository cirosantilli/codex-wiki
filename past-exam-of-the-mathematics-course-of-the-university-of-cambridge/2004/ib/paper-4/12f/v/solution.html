Use the usual convention that a ring homomorphism preserves the identity. Such a map from the quotient ring to C fixes Q: it fixes integers, and preservation of inverses fixes rational numbers. It is therefore determined by the image ζ of the class of x. The defining relation forces ζ3=1. Conversely, evaluation at any such ζ annihilates (x3−1) and hence factors through the quotient. Thus there are exactly three unital homomorphisms, namely
[f]⟼f(ζ),ζ∈{1,e2πi/3,e4πi/3}.​
(30)
If the convention permits nonunital ring homomorphisms, add the zero map. These are the only extra possibilities: the image of 1 is an idempotent in C, hence either zero or one, and image zero forces the whole map to vanish.
