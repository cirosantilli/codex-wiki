A direct construction, valid for every 0≤t<1, is
a1​a2​a3​​=(1,0,0),=(t,1−t2​,0),=(t, t1+t1−t​​, 1+t(1−t)(1+2t)​​).​​
(33)
The first coordinates give a1​⋅a2​=a1​⋅a3​=t, and
a2​⋅a3​=t2+t(1−t)=t.
(34)
The first two norms are one. For the third,
∣a3​∣2=t2+1+tt2(1−t)+(1−t)(1+2t)​=1.
(35)
Thus the required Gram matrix is (1−t)I+t11T. The column matrix is upper triangular and has determinant
det(a1​ a2​ a3​)=(1−t)1+2t​>0,​
(36)
so these vectors are linearly independent. This is an explicit instance of unit vectors with a common inner product; at t=0 it reduces to the standard orthonormal basis.
