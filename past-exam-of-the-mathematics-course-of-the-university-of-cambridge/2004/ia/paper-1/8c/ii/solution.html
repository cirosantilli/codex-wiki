No. For every n≥2, take
aj​=ej​(1≤j≤n−1),an​=e1​+⋯+en−1​

in Rn. These form a minimally dependent vector family: the full family is dependent because an​−∑j=1n−1​aj​=0.
If the omitted vector is an​, the remaining coordinate vectors are independent. If the omitted vector is ek​, a relation among the retained vectors has an ek​ coordinate coming only from an​, so its coefficient must be zero; the coefficients of all retained coordinate vectors must then be zero. Thus every (n−1)-element subfamily is linearly independent, as required for a counterexample. When n=1, the empty subfamily is independent while the one-vector family consisting of the zero vector is not, so the answer remains negative.
