For the divisibility assertion, 13+5⋅1=6. The change when n is replaced by n+1 is
(n+1)3+5(n+1)−(n3+5n)=3n(n+1)+6.
(6)
One of the consecutive integers n,n+1 is even, so the displayed change is divisible by 6. If n3+5n is divisible by 6, its successor is too. Thus induction proves 6∣n3+5n​ for every n≥1.
