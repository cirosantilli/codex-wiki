No attractive contact force is available, so N cannot be negative. Over a whole oscillation its minimum is m(g−ω2∣Az​∣). Thus strictly positive contact loading throughout requires ω2∣Az​∣<g​, or the printed form with Az​ taken as a nonnegative vertical amplitude. If ω2∣Az​∣>g, contact cannot persist: it would require N<0 during part of the cycle. At equality, N vanishes only at isolated instants and the ideal constrained motion can still remain in contact. Hence the strict inequality characterizes positive loading; mere nonnegative contact permits the marginal equality case.
