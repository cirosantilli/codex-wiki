The Bézout's identity from part (a) makes 19 the inverse of 79 modulo 100. Multiplication by 19 therefore gives x≡133≡33(mod100). Thus all integer solutions are x=33+100k, k∈Z​. Equivalently, the three solution classes modulo 300 are 33,133,233; using modulus 300 must not discard two of these classes.
