The imaginary coefficient of the antisymmetric term comes from Euclidean continuation; the Lorentzian action has the corresponding real coupling. For constant Φ0​, the Gauss-Bonnet theorem gives SΦ​=Φ0​χ(Σ), with Euler characteristic χ=2−2g on a connected closed oriented surface. Thus dilaton Euler-characteristic weighting gives

Adding one handle multiplies a contribution by gs2​, identifying gs​ as the string coupling. With canonically normalized external closed-string states, n vertices add gsn​, giving the string genus expansion gs2g−2+n​.
Before gauge fixing, the Polyakov path integral for inserted string vertex operators is schematically
An​=∑g​∫Vol(Diff×Weyl)DhDX​e−SE​[X,h]∏r=1n​∫Σ​d2zr​Vr​(zr​).
(21)
The quotient removes descriptions related by worldsheet diffeomorphisms and Weyl transformations. Gauge fixing has a nontrivial Jacobian, the Faddeev-Popov determinant. Anticommuting vector ghosts ca and symmetric trace-free antighosts bab​ exponentiate this determinant as a local worldsheet ghost action. These worldsheet ghost fields implement the gauge quotient; they are not additional spacetime particles. Their zero modes must be treated separately rather than included in an invertible determinant.
Three kinds of geometry enter the scattering calculation. The worldsheet moduli describe genuinely different conformal shapes that remain after local gauge fixing. Each such shape must be integrated over. The mapping class group consists of orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms modulo those continuously deformable to the identity; it identifies different markings of the same conformal surface, so only one representative of each class is counted. The worldsheet conformal Killing group is the continuous residual conformal symmetry of a chosen surface. It moves insertion positions without changing the geometry and must be divided out, usually by fixing enough vertex positions.
For a torus, write z∼z+1∼z+τ, with Imτ>0. Its shape modulus is τ, its mapping class group acts through the modular group, and its connected worldsheet conformal Killing group acts by translations. Thus the shape integral runs over the standard fundamental domain of the modular group, while one insertion position can be fixed by translation. On the sphere, three positions can instead be fixed by Möbius transformations; for genus at least two the continuous conformal Killing group is trivial.
Now use the half-normalized worldsheet diffeomorphism ghost operator in the question. A vector field va can preserve the representative metric after a compensating Weyl transformation precisely when P1​v=0. The trace of the metric variation sets δω=21​∇a​va, leaving the Conformal Killing equation
∇a​vb​+∇b​va​−hab​∇c​vc=0.​
(22)
The kernel of this operator gives the infinitesimal worldsheet conformal Killing group.
For a surviving infinitesimal worldsheet modulus, choose its metric variation tab​ orthogonal to all gauge variations. Orthogonality to Weyl transformations requires habtab​=0. On the closed surface, the formal adjoint of the worldsheet conformal Killing operator follows by integration by parts:
∫h​tab(P1​v)ab​=−∫h​(∇a​tab)vb​.
(23)
Orthogonality to every diffeomorphism variation is therefore the adjoint-kernel condition, giving
habtab​=0,∇atab​=0.​
(24)
In a local conformal coordinate these imply ∂zˉ​tzz​=0 and its conjugate: the moduli variations are represented by holomorphic quadratic differentials. Multiplying the definition of P1​ by two changes its adjoint by two but changes neither kernel.
For four tachyon vertex operators on the torus, there is one complex shape modulus, four complex insertion positions and one complex translation to remove. The worldsheet integration count after conformal gauge fixing is therefore
1+4−1=4 complex integrations=8 real integrations.​
(25)
Fix the fourth insertion at z4​=0. A schematic gauge-fixed expression is
A1,4​=gs4​∫F​dμ(τ)∫Tτ3​​∏r=13​d2zr​⟨(b,μτ​)(bˉ,μˉ​τ​)ccˉV4​(0)∏r=13​Vr​(zr​)⟩.
(26)
Here the antighost pair supplies the modulus measure, and the ghost pair at the fixed vertex absorbs the translation zero modes. The three unfixed positions and τ are the four complex integrations. The punctured Riemann surface moduli dimension gives the same result, 3g−3+n=4. Target-spacetime zero-mode integrals producing momentum conservation are separate from this requested count of worldsheet integrations.
