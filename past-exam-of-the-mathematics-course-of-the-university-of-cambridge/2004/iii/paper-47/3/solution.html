For a compact nonabelian simple factor CA​>0, the coupling decreases at high energy: asymptotic freedom. In contrast, positive scalar λ increases toward the ultraviolet. Its one-loop extrapolation has a Landau pole, indicating breakdown of weak-coupling perturbation theory, not a proof by itself of nonperturbative triviality. Ultraviolet poles should be extracted at infrared-safe mass or nonexceptional kinematics; a scaleless massless zero-momentum integral cannot be used to read off this ultraviolet counterterm without separating its infrared behavior.
