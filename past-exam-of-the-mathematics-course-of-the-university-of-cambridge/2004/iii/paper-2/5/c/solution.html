Translation X↦I+X is a bijection from nilpotent matrices to unipotent matrices. We can count the former without requiring a further partition identity. Let an​ be their number, with a0​=1.
Every linear operator T has a unique decomposition from the Fitting lemma
V=kerTn⊕imTn,
(33)
on which it is respectively nilpotent and invertible. To justify the decomposition, kernels and images stabilize by step n; if Tnv∈kerTn, then v∈kerT2n=kerTn, so Tnv=0. The dimensions then show the sum is all of V, and stabilization makes T invertible on the image. These two summands are determined by T, so there is no overcounting.
For a nilpotent summand of dimension k, the number of ordered complementary subspaces is ∣GLn​(q)∣/(∣GLk​(q)∣∣GLn−k​(q)∣), by transporting two fixed bases. The restrictions of T have ak​ and ∣GLn−k​(q)∣ choices. Summing all qn2 matrices gives
∣GLn​(q)∣qn2​=∑k=0n​∣GLk​(q)∣ak​​.
(34)

∣GLn​(q)∣an​​=ϕn​(q−1)1​−ϕn−1​(q−1)1​=ϕn​(q−1)q−n​.
(35)
