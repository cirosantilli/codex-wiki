Composition through a different length acquires a factor of f when it returns to the original length, so these reductions define an algebra homomorphism. It is onto, and its kernel is nilpotent. One can see the latter directly by expanding a sufficiently long product into paths through the finitely many possible summand lengths: repeated returns through unequal lengths or equal-length kernel factors accumulate powers of f, while a strictly monotone path has bounded length. Eventually every path vanishes in every summand.
