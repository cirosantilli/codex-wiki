Write f(u) for the physical free energy per site excluding the identity term, so F/N=C+f(u). Blocking sends u to u′=Rb​u and the identity coefficient to C′=bDC+c(u). Since the total partition function is unchanged and N′=N/bD,
C+f(u)=b−D[C′+f(u′)],f(u)=b−Df(u′)+g(u),g(u)=b−Dc(u).​
(22)
The function g records the field-independent energy generated by eliminated modes, including their entropy and normalization. It is the identity-operator contribution to renormalization-group free energy; it must be tracked to recover the complete thermodynamics, even though it does not change normalized spin probabilities.
Iterating the additive free-energy recursion under blocking gives
f(u0​)=b−nDf(un​)+∑j=0n−1​b−jDg(uj​).
(23)
When a regular background A(u) solves A(u)=b−DA(u′)+g(u), the singular remainder fs​=f−A satisfies the homogeneous scaling equation fs​(u)=b−Dfs​(u′). Thus the full free energy contains an inhomogeneous contribution, while the background-subtracted singular part is homogeneous. A finite initial identity energy C0​ adds a regular normalization, not a new magnetization singularity.
