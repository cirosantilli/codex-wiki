The unmatched haplotype-relative-risk counts satisfy w=a+b, x=c+d, y=a+c and z=b+d. For the raw table this gives
(w,x,y,z)=(13,8,6,15),OR=xywz​=1665​=4.0625.​
(27)
The Pearson chi-squared test of independence in this two-by-two table has statistic
χ2=(w+x)(y+z)(w+y)(x+z)(w+x+y+z)(wz−xy)2​=4372058​≃4.709.
(28)
Thus the unmatched calculation gives nominal evidence at 5%, with p≃0.030. It differs from the transmission disequilibrium test because it uses an unmatched table, and it also inherits the missing-parent selection issue from the raw counts.
For the ten complete trios, (w,x,y,z)=(12,8,6,14), giving odds ratio 7/2 and the unmatched Pearson chi-squared test of independence statistic 40/11≃3.636, with p≃0.057. That calculation does not reject at 5%. The nominal significance from the raw table therefore should not be presented as a robust association conclusion without a justified model for incomplete families and the unmatched-table assumptions.
