In vacuum without a cosmological constant, the ambient Ricci tensor and scalar vanish. A totally umbilic hypersurface has Kab​=λhab​, so K=3λ and Kab​Kab=3λ2. Therefore the scalar curvature of an umbilic vacuum hypersurface is 6ελ2.
To express the requested nonnegative version, take Lorentz signature (+−−−), so the timelike unit normal has ε=+1 and h is the induced negative-definite spatial metric. Then
(3)R=6λ2=32​K2≥0.​
(14)
If instead one uses signature (−+++) and its positive-definite spatial metric with the same curvature-slot convention, the scalar is −6λ2. Replacing h by −h reverses its scalar curvature. Thus the printed inequality has a signature/induced-metric convention built into it; the general formula above makes that choice explicit rather than losing the sign. The metric in the separate final question explicitly uses the mostly-plus signature.
