There is also an L2 norm proof. The diffusion matrix Lh​ is symmetric and satisfies
vTLh​v=−h−2∑m=0M−1​am+1/2​(vm+1​−vm​)2,v0​=vM​=0.

Hence the eigenvalues of I+kLh​ lie in [−1,1] under the same step restriction, and the spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices gives an L2 norm contraction. These stability estimates are valid for the printed recurrence, although they cannot repair its inconsistency with the printed advection equation.
