With sufficiently accurate starts and smooth derivative evaluation maps, convergence of a zero-stable multiderivative method gives sixth-order error on fixed time intervals. The first Dahlquist barrier for ordinary linear multistep methods does not apply, because this is a multiderivative multistep method.
