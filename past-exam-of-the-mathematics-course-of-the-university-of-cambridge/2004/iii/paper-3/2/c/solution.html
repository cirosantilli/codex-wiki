The Weyl group of Dn consists of all permutations of the n coordinates together with an even number of coordinate sign changes. Equivalently,
W(Dn​)={t↦(δi​tσ(i)​)i=1n​:σ∈Sn​, δi​∈{±1}, i∏​δi​=1}≅(Z/2Z)n−1⋊Sn​.​

Its order is 2n−1n!. The difference-root reflections give ordinary transpositions; composing a sum-root reflection with the corresponding difference-root reflection negates precisely the two selected coordinates. These describe the generators of all even signed permutations. For n=2 the group has four elements; for the toral case n=1 it is trivial.
