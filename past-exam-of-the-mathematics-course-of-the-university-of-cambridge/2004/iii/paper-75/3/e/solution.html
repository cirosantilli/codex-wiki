The undisturbed reservoir remains at h=H, u=0, while the particle budget gives ϕ=ϕ0​e−Vs​t/H. The edge of the rarefaction wave advancing into that reservoir travels with the local left-running characteristic speed, −b0​H​e−Vs​t/(2H). Thus
xr​(t)=−Vs​2Hc0​​[1−e−Vs​t/(2H)]​.
(22)
This is the disturbance head into the initially resting reservoir, also called the leading edge of the rarefaction wave; it reduces to −c0​t as Vs​→0. The rightmost fan/shelf junction is the rarefaction tail, not this reservoir edge. Its trajectory in the full sedimenting system cannot be obtained by retaining the constant-buoyancy Riemann invariants.
For the requested near-front approximation, hold hf​=4H/(F+2)2 fixed and take the shelf's concentration initially to be ϕ0​. The locally uniform shelf approximation gives bf​=b0​e−Vs​t/hf​, so
x˙f​=Fb0​hf​​e−Vs​t/(2hf​),xf​(t)≃Vs​2Fhf​b0​hf​​​[1−e−Vs​t/(2hf​)]​.
(23)
This tends to uf​t at small settling and predicts a finite limiting front location within the fixed-depth closure. It is not an exact solution of the spatially varying variable-buoyancy shallow water equations: different material histories and resulting buoyancy gradients eventually invalidate a perfectly uniform shelf. No exact finite-time stoppage is claimed.
