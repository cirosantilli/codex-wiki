For positive integer n and any positive integer N, the finite geometric series identity
1−t1​=∑j=0N−1​tj+1−ttN​
(21)
gives an exact decomposition along the indented contour. The polynomial terms have no pole and may be returned to the positive real axis; their Gamma function integrals are
I(λ,n)=eλ∑r=nn+N−1​λrΓ(r)​+I(λ,n+N).
(22)
Equivalently, expansion about the endpoint t=0 in the Laplace transform integral gives the same coefficients. The resulting formal asymptotic expansion is
I(λ,n)∼eλr=n∑∞​λrΓ(r)​​.
(23)
The ratio of consecutive terms is r/λ, so the infinite series is factorially divergent. It should be truncated, not treated as a convergent sum; the pole prescription controls the contribution beyond its algebraic orders. Although the source initially calls n an integer, the ordinary lower endpoint integral requires n>0, as is already implicit in the large-positive-n limit of part (i).
