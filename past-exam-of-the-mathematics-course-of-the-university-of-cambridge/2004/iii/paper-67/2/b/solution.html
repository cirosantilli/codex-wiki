The full spherical collapse reverses its expansion at tm​ and reaches zero radius at t=2tm​, corresponding to θ=2π. In the ideal cold, pressureless model this makes the nonlinear density contrast infinite. Extrapolating the linear growing mode to that same time instead gives the finite linear spherical-collapse threshold
