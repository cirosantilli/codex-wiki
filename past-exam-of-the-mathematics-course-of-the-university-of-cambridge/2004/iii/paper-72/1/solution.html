Write Biα​=A˙iα​ and assume that the material constraint is regular, so F,A​=0. Differentiating the constraint shows that admissible rates of the deformation gradient satisfy F,Aiα​​Biα​=0. The given stress-power identity says that W,A​−NT annihilates this tangent hyperplane. Its orthogonal complement is the one-dimensional span of F,A​, so a Lagrange multiplier gives the constraint reaction in hyperelastic stress:
Nαi​=W,Aiα​​+qF,Aiα​​.​
(1)
The sign of q is a convention; the virtual work identity determines no constitutive value for this reaction. Regularity matters: a singular equation describing the same constraint need not have a nonzero gradient.
For incompressibility, take F=detA−1. Differentiating the expression using the Levi-Civita symbol gives
∂Aiα​∂detA​=21​ϵαβγ​ϵijk​Ajβ​Akγ​=(detA)(A−1)αi​.
(2)
The middle expression is the corresponding entry of the cofactor matrix. Since detA=1, the nominal stress is therefore
Nαi​=W,Aiα​​+q(A−1)αi​.​
(3)
For the neo-Hookean solid, W,Aiα​​=μAiα​, so in the material-first convention N=μAT+qA−1. The identity matrix satisfies the prescribed dead loading with q=T−μ, proving that the undeformed cube is always an equilibrium.
To obtain the homogeneous tensile bifurcation of a neo-Hookean cube, first seek A=diag(λ,λ,λ−2), with λ>0. This has unit determinant. The two distinct diagonal stress equations are μλ+q/λ=T and μλ−2+qλ2=T. Eliminating q gives
(λ3−1)[μ(λ+λ−2)−T]=0.
(4)
Thus the nontrivial branch is
t=λ+λ−2,λ3−tλ2+1=0,q=μ/λ,t=T/μ.​
(5)
The function h(λ)=λ+λ−2 decreases on (0,21/3) and increases on (21/3,∞), because h′=1−2λ−3. Its minimum is h(21/3)=3/22/3. Above this threshold there are two positive roots λ−​<21/3<λ+​. For t=2, neither root is one, and the three choices of the exceptional coordinate for each root give six distinct diagonal deformation gradients, in addition to I.
At t=2, however, λ−​=1 and the three smaller-root matrices coincide with I: it would be incorrect to count those as three new deformations. The requested existence of seven distinct homogeneous deformations nevertheless holds even at this value. For any unit vector n, put
A(n)=λ+​I+(λ+−2​−λ+​)nnT.
(6)
Its positive eigenvalues are λ+​,λ+​,λ+−2​, and its determinant is one. Since A(n) is an orthogonal conjugate of the diagonal solution, the same q=μ/λ+​ gives μA(n)T+qA(n)−1=TI. Choose the six directions
e1​, e2​, e3​, 2​e1​+e2​​, 2​e1​+e3​​, 2​e2​+e3​​.
(7)
Their outer products are distinct, and λ+​>21/3>1, so the six A(n) are distinct from one another and from I. Constant nominal stress satisfies equilibrium and the prescribed surface tractions. Hence seven distinct homogeneous equilibria exist whenever T/μ>3/22/3. This is an existence count, not an exhaustive classification: rotated principal axes actually give a continuous family. The conventional seven axis-aligned positive-stretch branches coalesce to four distinct matrices at T/μ=2.
