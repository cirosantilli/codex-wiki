An exact Gibbs sampler update is available despite the non-Gaussian intercept: draw θ′ from the conditional gamma distribution law in part (ii), then set
μ′=logθ′.​
(49)
Together with conditional updates of the two remaining effects, this targets the required joint posterior in the log-intercept coordinates. The log-gamma prior for a Poisson log-intercept includes the change-of-variable factor: with dθ/dμ=eμ,
π(μ∣a2​,b2​,x)∝exp{(a+T)μ−(b+E)eμ}.
(50)
Using (a+T−1)μ instead would omit the Jacobian and give the wrong conditional law for μ.
