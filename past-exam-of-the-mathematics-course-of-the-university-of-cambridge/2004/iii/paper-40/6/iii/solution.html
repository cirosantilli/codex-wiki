For observed data x and missing data z, let pθ​(x,z) be the complete-data model. Starting at θ(0), the expectation-maximization algorithm alternates

The E-step computes the conditional expectation of the complete-data log-likelihood, using the current parameter for the missing-data conditional distribution. The M-step maximizes that expected log-likelihood over a new parameter. Maximizing the likelihood of imputed mean data is equivalent only when the complete-data log-likelihood has the necessary linear dependence on the missing sufficient statistics.
