Denote the density process by Lt​=exp(Mt​−[M]t​/2). The given identities for all A∈Fs​ imply
EP​(Lt​∣Fs​)=Ls​,EP​Lt​=1.
(37)
Thus L is a strictly positive true martingale, not merely a local martingale. The Itô formula gives dL=LdM, and hence
d[L,X]t​=Lt​d[M,X]t​.
(38)
First work with the zero-starting corrected increments U=X−X0​−[X,M]. Integration by parts gives
d(LU)​=UdL+LdU+d[L,U]=LUdM+LdX−Ld[X,M]+Ld[M,X]=LUdM+LdX.​
(39)
The finite-variation bracket has zero quadratic covariation with L, so no other term appears. Thus LU is a P-local martingale.
To justify the change of measure, stop U at τn​=inf{t:∣Ut​∣≥n}, with optional further localization if needed. The product Lt​Ut∧τn​​ remains a P-local martingale: before the stopping time the cancellation above applies, and afterwards only its bounded constant factor multiplies dL. Its absolute value is bounded by nLt​. On a finite horizon the stopped values of L are uniformly integrable, so Q1(c) upgrades this product to a true martingale. The Bayes formula for conditional expectation now gives
EQ​(Ut∧τn​​∣Fs​)=Ls​EP​(Lt​Ut∧τn​​∣Fs​)​=Us∧τn​​.
(40)
Finite-horizon equivalence of the measures transfers the localizing sequence to Q. Therefore U is a continuous Q-local martingale. If X0​∈L1(Q), adding its constant-in-time process proves
X=X−[X,M] is a continuous Q-local martingale.​
(41)
This proves the Girsanov theorem assertion under the usual zero or deterministic initial-value convention. More generally the normalization M0​=0 makes L0​=1, so P and Q agree on F0​ and the required initial integrability is automatic.
There is a literal initial-integrability qualification if neither convention is imposed. On positive integers let P(n)=2−n, Q(n)=c/n2, with c=(∑n≥1​n−2)−1, and let every filtration equal the full sigma-algebra. Constant processes Mt​=log(c2n/n2) and Xt​=n are P-martingales, and eMt​ is precisely the prescribed density. But EQ​∣X0​∣=∞ and [X,M]=0, so the asserted Q-local martingale cannot have an integrable initial value. This is why initial integrability under a density change is part of the precise formulation.
