Parametrize the nonconstant closed geodesic at unit speed on [0,L]. Its parallel transport around the loop fixes the tangent γ˙​(0). Since the manifold is orientable, it preserves orientation on the full tangent space, and hence has determinant one on the normal space. This normal space has odd dimension because the manifold has even dimension.
An odd-dimensional special orthogonal transformation has a fixed vector. Indeed, nonreal eigenvalues of a real orthogonal transformation occur in conjugate pairs with product one; the real eigenvalues are ±1, and determinant one makes the number of −1 eigenvalues even. Odd dimension therefore requires a +1 eigenvalue. Transport a unit fixed normal vector around the geodesic to obtain a periodic parallel field V, with Dt​V=0 and V⊥γ˙​.
