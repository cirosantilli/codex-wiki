Surjectivity was proved in part (b). Fix q∈N and choose a normal ball B=BN​(q,ε) on which expq​ is a diffeomorphism from the tangent ball. For each p∈f−1(q) define a section
sp​(expq​v)=expp​((dfp​)−1v),∣v∣<ε.
(8)
Completeness of M guarantees that the source Riemannian exponential map is defined. Preservation of geodesics and uniqueness of their initial-value problem give f∘sp​=idB​. Since df is invertible, differentiation of this identity shows that sp​ is a local diffeomorphism; it is injective because it is a section. Its image Up​ is open, and f:Up​→B is a diffeomorphism.
These images are disjoint. If sp​(y)=sp′​(y), lift the unique radial geodesic from y back to q starting at that common point. Both section constructions give this same lift, and uniqueness forces its endpoint to be both p and p′. Conversely, for any x∈f−1(B), lift that reversed radial geodesic from f(x) to q starting at x. Completeness supplies its full finite interval; its endpoint is some p∈f−1(q). Reversing the lift shows x=sp​(f(x)). Consequently
f−1(B)=p∈f−1(q)∐​Up​,f∣Up​​:Up​⟶B is a diffeomorphism.​
(9)
Every point has such an evenly covered neighborhood, proving that the complete local isometry is a covering. This argument does not assume a uniform positive source injectivity radius.
