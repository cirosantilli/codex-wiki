Write the expected value as μT=(μ1T​,μ2T​) and assume the relevant covariance matrix V22​ is invertible. Remove the linear predictor from the first block by defining
Y=X1​−μ1​−V12​V22−1​(X2​−μ2​).
(1)
A linear image of a multivariate normal vector is again multivariate normal. Direct covariance calculation gives
Cov(Y,X2​)=V12​−V12​V22−1​V22​=0.
(2)
Thus Y and X2​ are independent: uncorrelated jointly Gaussian variables are independent. Expanding the covariance matrix of Y, using V21​=V12T​, gives
Cov(Y)​=V11​−V12​V22−1​V21​−V12​V22−1​V21​+V12​V22−1​V22​V22−1​V21​=V11​−V12​V22−1​V21​.​
(3)
Conditioning on X2​=x2​ translates Y by the fixed vector μ1​+V12​V22−1​(x2​−μ2​) and leaves its covariance matrix unchanged. Consequently the conditional multivariate normal distribution is
X1​∣X2​=x2​∼N(μ1​+V12​V22−1​(x2​−μ2​), V11​−V12​V22−1​V21​​).
(4)
The conditional covariance is the Schur complement and does not depend on x2​.
