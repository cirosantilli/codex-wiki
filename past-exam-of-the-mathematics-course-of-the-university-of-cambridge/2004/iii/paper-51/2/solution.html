Dμ​Fμν=[DνΦ,Φ],Dμ​DμΦ+m2Φ=0.​

In particular the sign of the scalar mass term follows from the plus sign in the supplied trace Lagrangian density; it should not be guessed from a different metric convention. This is the adjoint Yang-Mills-Higgs trace variation.
For the static Bogomolny-Prasad-Sommerfield monopole, take the usual purely magnetic extension ∂0​Ai​=∂0​Φ=0 and temporal gauge A0​=0. At m=0, the equations reduce to
Dj​Fji​=[Φ,Di​Φ],Di​Di​Φ=0,
(12)
with the ν=0 equation identically zero. We now verify both spatial equations from Bi​=Di​Φ without assuming them. The gauge-theory Bianchi identity gives Di​Bi​=0, hence immediately Di​Di​Φ=0. Also Fji​=εjik​Bk​, so
Dj​Fji​​=εjik​Dj​Dk​Φ=21​εjik​[Dj​,Dk​]Φ=21​εjik​[Fjk​,Φ]=−[Bi​,Φ]=[Φ,Di​Φ].​
(13)
Here εjik​εjkℓ​=−2δiℓ​. This proves that the Bogomolny equations imply all static zero-mass Yang-Mills equations and the scalar equation. The specification of A0​ matters: the spatial Bogomolny equations by themselves do not constrain an arbitrary extra electric potential, so the implication is for their standard purely magnetic static extension.
