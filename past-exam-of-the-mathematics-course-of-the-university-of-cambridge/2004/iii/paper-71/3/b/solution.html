and equality holds at x=0. Thus its supremum norm error is exactly Am​.
At the 2Q points xj​=jπ/Q, 0≤j<2Q, every omitted frequency is an odd integer multiple of Q. Indeed for k≥m+1,
cos(5kxj​)=cos(5k−m−1jπ)=(−1)j.

Uniform convergence permits summing these values, giving
