The permitted transitions form the following diagram. Each arrow is labelled by its fitted transition intensity in units of inverse years; the absence of an arrow is a structural restriction, not an estimated small effect.
State 1 can move directly to 2, 3 or 5; state 2 can move to 4 or 5; state 3 can move to 4 or 5; and state 4 can move only to 5. There are no recovery transitions, and state 5 is an absorbing state. The rates of leaving the four living states are the sums of their outgoing arrows:
λ1​=0.085,λ2​=0.181,λ3​=0.369,λ4​=0.513per year.​
(45)
The diagonal generator entries are their negatives, so every row of Q sums to zero. These diagonal entries are not arrows or negative event rates. For example the direct 1→5 rate is 0.004 per year, while death after reaching another state can occur at a much larger rate. The printed fit has no covariates on the intensities, so it estimates one pooled rate system and contains no treatment-effect comparison, despite the trial's treatment randomization. The reported −2ℓ describes the fitted likelihood level; by itself it is not a goodness-of-fit test without a reference or competing model.
