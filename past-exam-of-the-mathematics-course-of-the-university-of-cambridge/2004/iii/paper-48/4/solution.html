At leading order each accessible quark flavor contributes independently. Away from thresholds and narrow resonances, quark-hadron duality relates the inclusive parton rate to the observed inclusive electron-positron annihilation into hadrons, giving
σhad(γ)​≃3s4πα2​3f maccessible∑​Qf2​.​

This formula concerns the photon channel, assumes s​ is large compared with the relevant quark masses and the strong-interaction scale, and does not include Z boson exchange.
Beyond leading order, an exactly exclusive pair of colored quarks is not an observable asymptotic state because of confinement and hadronization. Moreover its virtual corrections have infrared divergences; real soft or collinear gluon emission cannot be excluded if a finite rate is wanted. The inclusive sum includes both the corrected two-parton channel and channels such as qqˉ​g, with their degenerate soft and collinear limits. Real and virtual infrared divergences then cancel for an infrared-safe observable. A specified jet observable can also be meaningful if it defines an infrared-safe observable; a bare two-quark final state is not.

Thus the principle of minimal sensitivity, applied to this expression with nonzero coupling and β0​=0, selects the one-loop stationary scale for the hadronic annihilation correction
μ∗2​=sexp[−11−2nf​/34(1.99−0.11nf​)​].​

This is a prescription based on the residual order-a3 scale dependence of an order-a2 approximation. At strictly order a2, the derivative already vanishes for every renormalization scale; the prescription does not assert exact scale dependence of the observable. It is useful only while the selected running coupling remains perturbative. If a=0 or β0​=0, the stated derivative imposes no unique scale.
