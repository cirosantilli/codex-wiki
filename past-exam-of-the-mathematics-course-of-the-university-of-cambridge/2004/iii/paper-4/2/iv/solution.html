Choose a finite set S of topological generators of G and let Γ=⟨S⟩ denote the abstract subgroup it generates. Then Γ is dense in G. For an open subgroup H, the abstract subgroup K=Γ∩H is dense in H, since every nonempty open subset of H is open in G. Every coset of H meets Γ, so [Γ:K]=[G:H]=m<∞.
