The primary vorticity Jacobian determinant vanishes because ∇2ψ=−sψ. The quadratic temperature Jacobian determinant is
J(ψ,bcosαxsinπz)=2απab​sin2πz.
(23)
The mean temperature correction therefore obeys c˙=−4π2c−απab/2. Its Jacobian determinant with the primary streamfunction is
J(ψ,csin2πz)=απaccosαx(sin3πz−sinπz).
(24)
Projecting on the retained primary modes gives, with D=s+Qπ2/s,
a˙=−σDa+σRαb/s,b˙=−sb+αa+απac,c˙=−4π2c−απab/2.
(25)
For a nonzero steady roll, b=(s2+Qπ2)a/(Rα) and c=−αab/(8π). Substitution in the second equation gives, within this truncation,
a2=s2+Qπ28(R−Rc​)​,c=−πRR−Rc​​.​
(26)
The positive denominator ensures branches only on the supercritical side for every Q≥0. To verify dynamical stability as well as existence, the critical right eigenvector is (1,α/s); normalizing the left eigenvector gives linear eigenvalue derivative
K=s(s+σD)σα2​>0.
(27)
Quadratic slaving gives c=−α2a2/(8πs), and projection of its feedback gives the cubic amplitude equation
a˙=K[(R−Rc​)a−8s2+Qπ2​a3]+⋯.​
(28)
Its nonzero branches are stable in the center direction, while all other eigenvalues remain negative at a simple onset. Reflection about the mid-width sends a→−a, so the bifurcation is a supercritical pitchfork bifurcation for all Q, in the generic case of a single critical roll mode.
The cubic saturation of quasistatic magnetoconvection result is identical in Fourier truncation and perturbation theory at the orders needed here. At quadratic order the only forced mode is precisely the retained horizontally uniform sin2πz temperature mode. At cubic order its feedback projects on the primary mode with the same coefficient. The generated third vertical harmonic is orthogonal to that primary projection and contributes only to higher-order corrections. Thus both methods give the same linear threshold and cubic solvability condition; the truncated finite-amplitude branch is not an exact solution of the full PDE at all amplitudes.
