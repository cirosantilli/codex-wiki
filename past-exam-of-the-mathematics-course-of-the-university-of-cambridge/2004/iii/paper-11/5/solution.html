Independent continuous samples follow by refining finite coordinate discretizations; the strict-order pattern stabilizes almost surely when ties have probability zero. A uniform random permutation is the rank sequence of independent continuous samples, so it has the same subsequence-length concentration. Arbitrarily dependent random sequences are not covered by the product-space assumption.
