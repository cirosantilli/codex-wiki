Write ω=u+iv, with v>0, and a chosen square root kβ​=a+ib. Squaring gives
a2−b2=β2u2−v2​−k2,ab=β2uv​.
(32)
One cannot have b=0: the second relation would force u=0, and then the first would require the square of a real number to be negative. Consequently
Repβ​=Reu+iva+ib​=∣ω∣2au+bv​=∣ω∣2v​(b+β2bu2​).
(33)
The bracket has the sign of b, proving the causal vertical wavenumber branch equivalence
Imkβ​>0⟺Repβ​>0.​
(34)
This selects the square root analytically in the upper frequency half-plane. At ω=iv it is kβ​=iv2/β2+k2​; continuation supplies the outgoing branch for other frequencies. The expression β−2−p2​ must use this inherited branch rather than an independently chosen square root. For positive real frequency its limit is positive real for a propagating wave and positive imaginary for an evanescent wave.
