Use local coordinates with the interface z=0, solid below and fluid above, and choose the P-SV displacement potentials convention ux​=ϕx​−ψz​, uz​=ϕz​+ψx​. Let the reflected P and SV potential wave amplitudes be A,B, while the transmitted P wave amplitude is TP​. All four waves have the same tangential slowness
p=αsinθP​​=βsinθS​​=αf​sinθf​​,qP​=αcosθP​​,qS​=βcosθS​​,qf​=αf​cosθf​​.
(18)
The incident and transmitted retarded times have negative qP​z,qf​z, while elastic waves have positive qP​z,qS​z. At the interface all waveform derivatives therefore have the same argument. displacement field continuity gives
qP​(1−A)+pB=qf​TP​.(1)
(19)
With Lamé parameters μ=ρβ2 and λ=ρ(α2−2β2), the shear and normal stresses follow directly from differentiation:
σxz​=μ(2ϕxz​+ψxx​−ψzz​),σzz​=λΔϕ+2μ(ϕzz​+ψxz​).
(20)
The fluid has no shear traction, so the coefficient of f′′ in σxz​ must vanish:
α2sin2θP​​(1−A)−β2cos2θS​​B=0.(2)
(21)
The coefficient of f′′ in the solid stress is ρ[cos2θS​(1+A)−sin2θS​B]; in the fluid it is ρf​TP​. Thus
ρ[cos2θS​(1+A)−sin2θS​B]=ρf​TP​.(3)
(22)
These are the three required matching conditions; tangential displacement field need not be continuous at a solid–inviscid-fluid boundary.
Put C=cos2θS​ and X=1−A. From (2), B=(β2/α2)(sin2θP​/C)X. In (1), the coefficient of X simplifies because C+2(β2/α2)sin2θP​=1. Hence X=(αCcosθf​)/(αf​cosθP​)TP​. Substitute this into (3) to obtain
2ρC=[ρf​+αf​cosθP​ραcosθf​​(C2+α2β2​sin2θP​sin2θS​)]TP​.
(23)
Therefore the solid-fluid P-wave transmission coefficient is
TP​=ρf​αf​cosθP​+ρα[cos22θS​+(β2/α2)sin2θP​sin2θS​]cosθf​2ραf​cos2θS​cosθP​​.​
(24)
Division by C was only an elimination device; the final expression extends continuously to C=0. The normal-incidence value is 2ραf​/(ρα+ρf​αf​). This is a potential coefficient; longitudinal displacement field transmission additionally multiplies it by α/αf​. The original PDF's bracket lies in the denominator and contains β2/α2 and cosθf​. The converted TeX misplaces the bracket, substitutes θ2 for β2, and loses the final cosine.
