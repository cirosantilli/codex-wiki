Indifference means equality of the risky expected utility and the utility of the sure reduced wealth. A second-order Taylor expansion, using Eϕ=0, gives
EU(W(1+ϕ))=U(W)+21​W2U′′(W)Eϕ2+o(Eϕ2).
(17)
For the sure alternative,
U(W(1−ψ))=U(W)−WU′(W)ψ+O(ψ2).
(18)
Equating these and retaining the leading terms produces the small multiplicative risk premium
ψ≃−21​U′(W)WU′′(W)​Var(ϕ).​
(19)
For increasing concave utility functions this is positive. The approximation assumes genuinely small shocks with controlled Taylor remainders and positive final wealth; small variance alone would not justify it for rare large shocks.
