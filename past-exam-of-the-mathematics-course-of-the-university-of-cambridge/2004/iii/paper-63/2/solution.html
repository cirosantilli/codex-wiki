Write u=cosθ for the direction cosine and B=j/κ=σT4/π for the radiative transfer source function. Integrating the radiative transfer equation u∂τ​I=I−B over solid angle gives
dτdF​=∫(I−B)dΩ=4π(J−B).
(8)
Radiative equilibrium makes the radiative flux F constant, so J=B=j/κ.
For I=A+Cu, the radiation-field moments are
J=21​∫−11​Idu=A,F=2π∫−11​uIdu=34πC​,cPr​=2π∫−11​u2Idu=34πA​.
(9)
Consequently the Eddington closure approximation holds. Substituting into the radiative transfer equation, with B=J=A, gives uA′+u2C′=Cu. Equality for every u requires A′=C and C′=0, with C=3F/(4π). The stipulated inward hemispheric radiative flux at the surface is
Fin​=2π∫−10​u(A0​+Cu)du=−πA0​+32πC​.
(10)
Putting this equal to zero fixes A0​=2C/3, hence A=C(τ+2/3). Using A=σT4/π and the effective temperature definition F=σTe4​ gives the grey atmosphere law
T4=43​Te4​(τ+32​)​,T0​=2−1/4Te​​.
(11)
The positivity limitation of a linear Eddington intensity matters here: the formal surface intensity I(0,u)=C(2/3+u) is negative for u<−2/3. Thus this angular ansatz gives the requested Eddington surface boundary condition for approximate moments; vanishing integrated inward flux is not an exact, nonnegative, pointwise no-incoming radiation field.
Neglecting radiation pressure, the stellar hydrostatic equation and the definition of optical depth give hydrostatic equilibrium in optical depth, dP/dτ=g/κ. Since T4=T04​(1+3τ/2), putting v=T4 gives
dvd(Pα)​=3κ0​T04​2αg​vβ−1.
(12)
Taking P(0)=0 and α>0, the grey-atmosphere pressure with power-law opacity is therefore
Pα=3βκ0​T04​2αg​(T4β−T04β​)​(β=0).
(13)
The pressure formula printed in the PDF is missing the factor 1/β. Differentiating its right-hand side would otherwise give dP/dτ=βg/κ. For β=0 the correct limit is Pα=2αglog(T4/T04​)/(3κ0​T04​).
At the specified matching location, the luminosity relation makes F=Lr​/(4πr2)=σT4, hence T=Te​, T4=2T04​ and τ=2/3. Multiplying the corrected pressure relation by κ0​T4−4β/g gives
gPκ​=3β4α​(1−2−β)​.
(14)
This agrees with the PDF's final surface boundary condition for a power-law-opacity grey atmosphere. It also extends to negative β wherever the model applies; at β=0, its continuous limit is Pκ/g=4αlog2/3.
