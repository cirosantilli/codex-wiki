Since the donor loses mass, this is positive for q<4/3: the donor shrinks relative to its Roche lobe, so transfer shuts off without an external driver. The Roche lobe itself expands only for q<5/6; for 5/6<q<4/3 it shrinks more slowly than the donor.
Gravitational-wave emission from a binary system provides such a driver: the orbit emits gravitational waves carrying energy and angular momentum, and the shrinking Roche lobe can maintain contact. Allowing J˙<0, the binary mass-transfer contact equation becomes

The donor does not change mass in the eruption and moves inside its expanded Roche lobe: this is nova-induced binary detachment.
