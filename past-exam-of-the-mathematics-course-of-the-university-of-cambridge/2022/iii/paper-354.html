Under the state–operator correspondence, let ∣Oij​⟩ be the state of the antisymmetric conformal primary operator Oij​. In radial quantization Pi†​=Ki​, while primarity gives Ki​∣Ojk​⟩=0. The norm of the level-one descendant obtained by taking a divergence is therefore determined by the conformal algebra commutator
[Ka​,Pb​]=2i(δab​D−Mab​).
(1)
Using the two-form action of Mab​ gives, up to a positive normalization,
∥Pi∣Oij​⟩∥2∝(Δ−d+2)∥O∥2.
(2)
Positivity of norm yields the two-form conformal unitarity bound
Δ≥d−2(d≥4).​
(3)
At saturation the descendant is null, and the operator obeys the conservation equation ∂iOij​=0.
In d=3, Hodge duality turns the two-form into the vector primary Vk​=ϵkij​Oij/2. The vector divergence descendant has norm proportional to Δ−d+1=Δ−2. Consequently the stronger bound is
Δ≥2(d=3),​
(4)
rather than the formal two-form value d−2=1.

Varying the two-form action gives the gauge-invariant equation
∇a​Fabc=0,F=dA.​
(5)
Choose radial gauge Azi​=0 and boundary-transverse gauge ∂iAij​=0. In the Poincare patch of AdS6​, −g​=z−6, while raising the three indices of F contributes z6. The boundary components consequently obey
(∂z2​+ηkl∂k​∂l​)Aij​=0.​
(6)
The near-boundary indicial equation is α(α−1)=0, so
Aij​(z,x)=Jij​(x)+zAij(1)​(x)+⋯.
(7)
Under the bulk dilation (z,x)↦(λz,λx), a two-form component carries two powers of inverse length. Thus the leading coefficient has dimension 2, while the coefficient of z has dimension 3:
ΔJ​=2,ΔO​=3.​
(8)
The leading freely specifiable, nonnormalizable coefficient is the CFT source Jij​. The subleading normalizable coefficient is its canonical response and determines ⟨Oij​⟩. This agrees with the saturated two-form unitarity bound ΔO​=d−2=3 in the five-dimensional boundary CFT and with the holographic dictionary for a massless bulk gauge field.

Translation invariance supplies the momentum-conserving delta function. Lorentz invariance, symmetry of Tij​, stress-tensor conservation, and tracelessness then fix the remaining tensor structure to the transverse traceless spin-two projector shown in the question, up to an overall theory-dependent coefficient. Since the stress-energy tensor has scaling dimension d, its momentum transform has dimension zero. The delta function has momentum dimension −d, so scale invariance requires
β=d.​
(9)
For the Wightman function, the spectral condition restricts support to the appropriate future-directed timelike momenta, with convention-dependent distributions on the null boundary. It vanishes for spacelike momentum. A time-ordered or Euclidean correlator is obtained by analytic continuation and exists more broadly, but polynomial contact terms are renormalization-scheme dependent; in even dimensions the nonlocal power is accompanied by a logarithm.
In a large-N holographic CFT, the single-trace state Tij​(p)∣0⟩ is dual at leading order to a one-graviton bulk state with the matching boundary momentum and polarization. Multiparticle intermediate states and graviton interactions enter at subleading orders in 1/N.

The stated finite value
Δt1​Iren​=−12c​
(19)
is the Casimir energy of a two-dimensional conformal field theory on the unit spatial circle. The plane-to-cylinder conformal map shifts the Hamiltonian by −c/12, so the global AdS3​ vacuum is dual to the CFT cylinder vacuum rather than to a state of zero cylinder energy. The sign assigned directly to the Lorentzian on-shell action depends on whether it is identified with the action density or with the vacuum-energy generating functional; the universal boundary datum is the vacuum energy E0​=−c/12 supplied in the question.
