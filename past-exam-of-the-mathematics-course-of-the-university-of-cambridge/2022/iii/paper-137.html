For an integer k and a congruence subgroup Γ≤SL2​(Z), the space Mk​(Γ) consists of holomorphic functions f:h→C satisfying
(f∣k​γ)(τ)=(cτ+d)−kf(γτ)=f(τ)
(1)
for every γ=(ac​bd​)∈Γ, and which are holomorphic at every cusp. This is the space of modular forms of weight k and level Γ.

If γ∈Γ(N), then (x,y)γ≡(x,y)(modN), so part c gives invariance. It remains to check the cusps. For any σ∈SL2​(Z),
Gk(x,y)​∣k​σ=Gk(x,y)σ​.

For fixed τ=x+iy, the set {∣cτ+d∣:(c,d)∈Z2∖{0}} has a positive minimum. Choose a primitive pair (c,d) attaining it and complete it to a matrix γ∈SL2​(Z). Since
Im(γτ)=∣cτ+d∣2y​,
(8)
this point has maximal imaginary part in the orbit. Translate by a power of T:τ↦τ+1 to arrange ∣Reτ∣≤1/2. If now ∣τ∣<1, applying S:τ↦−1/τ strictly increases the imaginary part, a contradiction. Thus ∣τ∣≥1, proving that every orbit meets the standard fundamental domain of the modular group F.

b OurBigBook.com
The point 1/N+Λτ​ has exact order N, and changing τ by Γ1​(N) changes the pair only by a complex scaling, so the stated map is well-defined.
Conversely, scale a lattice to write it as Zτ⊕Z. A point of exact order N is represented by (rτ+s)/N, where (r,s) is primitive modulo N. The group SL2​(Z) acts transitively on primitive vectors modulo N, so a basis change carries this point to 1/N. This proves surjectivity. Two resulting normalized pairs are similar precisely when their basis-change matrix fixes (0,1) modulo N, namely when it lies in Γ1​(N). This proves injectivity and the Gamma 1 level structure on a complex lattice bijection
Γ1​(N)\h≅C×\L(N).​
(15)

Index-p overlattices Λ′ correspond to order-p subgroups of (p−1Λ)/Λ≅Fp2​, hence to the p+1 lines in that vector space.
If p∤N, the order of v+Λ′ cannot decrease: its decrease would have a factor dividing both p=[Λ′:Λ] and N. Thus all p+1 overlattices are counted.
If p∣N, the element (N/p)v+Λ is a nonzero point of order p in C/Λ. Exactly one of the p+1 overlattices contains it; in that overlattice the image of v has order N/p, while in every other one it retains order N. Therefore

This is the Prime-index overlattices preserving a Gamma 1 level structure count.

Λ∨={y:⟨x,y⟩∈Z for every x∈Λ}.

Put Lτ​=y−1/2(Zτ+Z), a covolume-one lattice, and define
Θk,τ​(t)=∑0=z∈Lτ​​z,ke−πt∣z∣2.

Termwise Mellin transformation in the initial half-plane gives
Gk​(τ,s)=Γ(s+k)πs+ky−k/2​∫0∞​Θk,τ​(t)ts+k−1dt.
(23)
Split the integral at t=1. The integral over [1,∞) is entire in s because the theta sum decays exponentially. Apply the Poisson summation formula for a Euclidean lattice and the Fourier eigenfunction calculation from part b to the interval (0,1], then substitute t↦1/t. This rewrites the small-time integral as another exponentially convergent integral over [1,∞) plus explicit elementary Mellin terms. Those terms are meromorphic, but for positive even k their apparent poles are canceled by the zeros of 1/Γ(s+k). The displayed formula therefore continues holomorphically to every s∈C, proving the analytic continuation of a weight-k real-analytic Eisenstein series.
