A formula ϕ(xˉ,yˉ​) has the order property for T when, for every finite n, some model contains tuples ai​,bj​ with
ϕ(ai​,bj​)⟺i<j.
(5)
Compactness realizes this pattern indexed by any linear order.
Fix κ≥∣T∣ and let λ be least with 2λ>κ. Use the order property along I=Qλ, and take parameters
B={bj​:j∈J},
(6)
where part i gives ∣B∣≤κ. For distinct i,i′∈I, choose j∈J strictly between them. Then ϕ(ai​,bj​) and ϕ(ai′​,bj​) have different truth values, so the types tp(ai​/B) are distinct. There are ∣I∣=2λ>κ such types over at most κ parameters. Enlarging B to size exactly κ if necessary preserves them. Hence T is not κ-stable. This is the order property implies instability argument.
