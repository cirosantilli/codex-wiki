Perform the infinitesimal local change of integration variables
δψ(x)=iα(x)ψ(x),δψˉ​(x)=−iα(x)ψˉ​(x)
(23)
in the Euclidean path integral for ⟨jμ(x)ψ(x1​)ψˉ​(x2​)⟩. The vector transformation has no quantum anomaly, so its functional measure is invariant. The action variation supplies −i∫α∂μ​jμ, while varying the two charged insertions supplies contact terms at x1​ and x2​ with opposite signs. Since a change of integration variables cannot change the integral, the coefficient of the arbitrary function α(x) vanishes:
∂μ​⟨jμ(x)ψ(x1​)ψˉ​(x2​)⟩=−[δ(4)(x−x1​)−δ(4)(x−x2​)]⟨ψ(x1​)ψˉ​(x2​)⟩​.
(24)
This Schwinger-Dyson equation is the position-space Ward-Takahashi identity.
