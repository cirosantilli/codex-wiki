For x(P)=A/B2 in lowest terms, take the logarithmic naive height h(P)=logmax{∣A∣,B2}. Its required properties are
h(2P)=4h(P)+O(1)
(46)
and
h(P+Q)+h(P−Q)=2h(P)+2h(Q)+O(1),
(47)
with constants depending only on the curve. Define the canonical height of an elliptic curve by
h(P)=limr→∞​4−rh(2rP).
(48)
The first bounded-error relation makes this a convergent telescoping correction to h(P). Apply the second relation to 2rP,2rQ, divide by 4r, and let r→∞ to obtain
h(P+Q)+h(P−Q)=2h(P)+2h(Q).
(49)
Also h(2P)=4h(P), and the parallelogram identity then gives h(nP)=n2h(P) for every integer n. Thus h is a quadratic form.
