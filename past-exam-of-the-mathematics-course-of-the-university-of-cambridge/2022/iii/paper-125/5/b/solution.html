The change of variables X=5x, Y=25y identifies E5​ with
Y2=X3−25X=X(X−5)(X+5).
(43)
A full two-isogeny descent, equivalently the standard full 2-descent for a cubic with three rational roots, gives
E5​(Q)/2E5​(Q)=⟨(0,0),(1,0),(−4/5,6/25)⟩F2​​.
(44)
The local conditions at 2, 5, and infinity leave exactly these eight square-class combinations; primes outside {2,5} have even valuations and contribute none. Hence the quotient has dimension three. Since the rational 2-torsion has dimension two, the rank is one.
The Lutz–Nagell theorem on the integral model, or reduction at two good primes, excludes odd torsion and torsion of order greater than two. Therefore
E5​(Q)tors​={O,(0,0),(1,0),(−1,0)}≅(Z/2Z)2,
(45)
and (−4/5,6/25) generates the free part. This is the Mordell-Weil group of the congruent number curve for five.
