For an adiabatic cosmological perturbation, the baryon and photon fractional density perturbations obey δb​=3δγ​/4. Since the pressure of the photon-baryon fluid is supplied by the photons,
dP=31​dρˉ​γ​,dρˉ​=dρˉ​γ​+dρˉ​b​=(1+4ρˉ​γ​3ρˉ​b​​)dρˉ​γ​.
(1)
The photon-baryon sound speed is therefore
cs​=cdρˉ​dP​​=3​c​(1+4ρˉ​γ​3ρˉ​b​​)−1/2.​
(2)
Use ρˉ​b​∝a−3 and ρˉ​γ​∝a−4 in the Comoving Jeans length
λJ,com​=acs​​Gρˉ​b​π​​.
(3)
Well before matter-radiation equality, photon inertia dominates, cs​≃c/3​, and
λJ,com​∝a1/2∝t1/4.
(4)
Once baryon loading dominates while tight coupling still holds, cs​∝a−1/2, so λJ,com​ is approximately constant. At cosmological recombination, photon pressure support disappears and the baryonic Jeans scale drops sharply. For a subsequently adiabatic monatomic gas, Tb​∝a−2 and cs​∝a−1, giving
λJ,com​∝a−1/2∝t−1/3
(5)
in an Einstein-de Sitter universe. The requested graph therefore rises as t1/4, flattens before recombination, jumps downward there, and then decreases as t−1/3.
For collisionless matter, the same instantaneous estimate uses its one-dimensional velocity dispersion σv​ instead of cs​; more precisely, suppression is described by collisionless free streaming. Thus
λJ,com​∼aσv​​Gρˉ​cdm​π​​.
(6)
While the particles are relativistic, σv​≃c and λJ,com​∝a1/2. Once nonrelativistic but thermally coupled to radiation, Tcdm​∝a−1, so σv​∝a−1/2 and the scale is constant. After kinetic decoupling, momentum redshifts as a−1, so σv​∝a−1 and λJ,com​∝a−1/2. The second graph joins these three power laws at tNR​ and tdec​; unlike the baryonic graph, its final decline begins at dark-matter kinetic decoupling rather than recombination.
