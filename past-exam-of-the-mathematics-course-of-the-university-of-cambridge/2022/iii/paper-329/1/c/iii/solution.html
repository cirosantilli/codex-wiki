Ω2​=8πμa3G2​​=−2R3a3​[3R2(Ω⋅R)R​−Ω].

The resulting rotlet advects the force-free sphere 1. Since the field is harmonic, Faxén's first law gives
U1​=a3R3Ω2​×(−R)​=−2R6a6​Ω×R​.
(20)
The returned rotlet has velocity O(Ωa6/R5) and rate of strain O(Ωa6/R6) at sphere 1. That strain induces a stresslet of strength O(μΩa9/R6), whose velocity at sphere 2 is O(Ωa9/R8). This method of reflections for Stokes flow gives the stated order of the next correction to U2​.
