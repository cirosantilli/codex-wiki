A magnetostatic force-free magnetic field is in equilibrium when its electric current exerts no Lorentz force density:
J×B=0.
(1)
Thus J is pointwise parallel to B, so for some scalar α(r),
J=αB.
(2)
Using the magnetostatic Ampère's law, ∇×B=(4π/c)J, gives
∇×B=c4π​αB​.
(3)
Taking the divergence and using ∇⋅B=0 yields
B⋅∇α=0​.
(4)
The force-free parameter is therefore constant along each magnetic field line.

Use the material forms of the ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation and momentum equation:
DtDB​=(B⋅∇)u−B∇⋅u,

DtDu​=−∇(h+Φ)+T∇s+4πρ(∇×B)×B​.

DtDhc​​=−B⋅∇(h+Φ−2u2​)+TB⋅∇s−hc​∇⋅u.
(38)
Since ∇⋅B=0, this is the cross-helicity conservation law
∂t∂hc​​+∇⋅Fc​=Sc​​,
(39)
with
Fc​=uhc​+B(h+Φ−2u2​),Sc​=TB⋅∇s​.
(40)
