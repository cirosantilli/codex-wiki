At the characteristic vector of a set 1B​, this polynomial vanishes for B=A and is nonzero for B=A. Hence the restricted functions PA​ are linearly independent. On the r-slice, every square-free monomial of degree below s can be raised to degree s using the relation ∑i​xi​=r, so the degree-at-most-s function space is spanned by the (sn​) square-free degree-s monomials. Linear independence gives the theorem.
