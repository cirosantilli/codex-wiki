[Du]α;Br​​+∣u∣α;Br​​≤η[D2u]α;B​+Cη​∣u∣2;B​.

[D2u]α;B1/2​​≤δ[D2u]α;B​+C(n,α,β,δ)∣u∣2;B​.​

The standard iteration lemma for nested balls absorbs the first term. The remaining C2 norm is bounded by the interior derivative estimate and interpolation, producing
∣u∣2,α;B1/2​​≤C(n,α,β)∣u∣0;B​.​
