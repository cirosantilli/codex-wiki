Put N=Sd/X and let p=q(X). The restriction
q:Sd∖X⟶N∖{p}
(21)
is a homeomorphism. At every point of N∖{p}, transport the local orientation of Sd through this homeomorphism. The localization of the global class q∗​[Sd] is therefore a generator at one, and hence every, point of that connected open set. The localizations of a global homology class form a section of the orientation local system; because the manifold N is connected, this generator extends across p. Thus q∗​[Sd] is a fundamental class for an orientation of N.
Choose [N]=q∗​[Sd]. By the degree of a map between oriented manifolds,
q∗​[Sd]=1[N],
(22)
so degq=1​.
