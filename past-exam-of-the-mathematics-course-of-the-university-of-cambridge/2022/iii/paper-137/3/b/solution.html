The point 1/N+Λτ​ has exact order N, and changing τ by Γ1​(N) changes the pair only by a complex scaling, so the stated map is well-defined.
Conversely, scale a lattice to write it as Zτ⊕Z. A point of exact order N is represented by (rτ+s)/N, where (r,s) is primitive modulo N. The group SL2​(Z) acts transitively on primitive vectors modulo N, so a basis change carries this point to 1/N. This proves surjectivity. Two resulting normalized pairs are similar precisely when their basis-change matrix fixes (0,1) modulo N, namely when it lies in Γ1​(N). This proves injectivity and the Gamma 1 level structure on a complex lattice bijection
Γ1​(N)\h≅C×\L(N).​
