R2⟶C,(c,d)⟼cτ+d
(2)
has inverse norm bounded uniformly for τ∈C. Hence there is AC​>0 such that
∣cτ+d∣≥AC​c2+d2​.
(3)
Therefore the absolute value of the defining series is bounded locally uniformly by
AC−k​∑(c,d)=(0,0)​(c2+d2)−k/2,
(4)
which converges for k>2. The Weierstrass M-test gives locally uniform absolute convergence, so termwise holomorphy proves that Gk(x,y)​ is holomorphic.
