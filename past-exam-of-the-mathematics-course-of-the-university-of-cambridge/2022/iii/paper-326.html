For a bounded operator A:X→Y between Hilbert spaces, the Moore--Penrose inverse of a Hilbert-space operator is defined on
D(A†)=R(A)⊕R(A)⊥
(1)
and has range R(A†)=N(A)⊥. Its Penrose equations are
AA†A=A,A†AA†=A†,(AA†)∗=AA†,(A†A)∗=A†A.
(2)
Equivalently, A†A=PN(A)⊥​ and AA† is the restriction of PR(A)​​ to D(A†).

Solution

ii OurBigBook.com
The assertion is understood for every η∈R and for a topology in which sequentially closed sets are closed, in particular the norm topology of a Banach space. Suppose first that f is sequentially lower semicontinuous and xn​∈Ωη​={x:f(x)≤η} converges to x. Then
f(x)≤liminfn​f(xn​)≤η,
(18)
so x∈Ωη​ and the sublevel set is closed. Conversely, if all sublevel sets are closed but lower semicontinuity fails, there are xn​→x and a real η<f(x) with a subsequence satisfying f(xnk​​)≤η. Closedness of Ωη​ would put x in that set, a contradiction. This proves the closed-sublevel-set characterization of lower semicontinuity.
