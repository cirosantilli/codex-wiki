Give ∂X the outward-normal-first boundary orientation. The Generalized Stokes theorem states that, for every compactly supported (n−1)-form ω,
∫X​dω=∫∂X​F∗ω.
(8)
Choose an oriented coordinate cover by charts into Rn or the half-space Hn={x1≥0}, and choose a partition of unity (ρi​) subordinate to it. Since the family is locally finite and ω has compact support, only finitely many ρi​ω are nonzero. It is therefore legitimate to write both integrals as finite sums and prove the identity for a form supported in one chart.
In an interior chart the integral of an exact compactly supported top form is zero by the fundamental theorem of calculus. In a boundary chart write
ω=∑j=1n​(−1)j−1aj​dx1∧⋯∧dxj∧⋯∧dxn.
(9)
Integrating dω=(∑j​∂j​aj​)dx1∧⋯∧dxn coordinate by coordinate kills every tangential derivative. The normal derivative leaves precisely the restriction to x1=0, with the sign selected by the outward-normal-first convention. This is ∫∂X​F∗ω, proving the theorem.
