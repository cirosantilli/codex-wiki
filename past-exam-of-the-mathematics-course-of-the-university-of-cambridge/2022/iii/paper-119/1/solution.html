A monomorphism is a left-cancellable morphism. A strong monomorphism m:S→A has the right lifting property against every epimorphism: from a commutative square
X↓eY​u​v​​S↓mA​
(1)
with e epic, one obtains d:Y→S satisfying de=u and md=v. A regular monomorphism is an equalizer of a parallel pair.
Suppose m:S→A equalizes r,s:A⇉C. In the square above,
rve=rmu=smu=sve.
(2)
Since e is epic, rv=sv, so the universal property of the equalizer gives the required d. Thus every regular monomorphism is strong.
Let mi​:Si​→A be strong for i=1,2, and let P=S1​×A​S2​. Given a lifting square against P→A, compose its top map with each projection. Strength of mi​ produces maps di​:Y→Si​ with mi​di​=v. The pair (d1​,d2​) has equal composites to A, hence induces d:Y→P. Therefore intersections of strong subobjects are strong.
Call a morphism anodyne when it is both monic and epic, and call an object saturated when it is injective with respect to every such morphism. Let m:S→B be a strong subobject of a saturated object. Given an anodyne e:X→Y and u:X→S, saturation of B extends mu to some v:Y→B. Strength of m applied to mu=ve lifts v to d:Y→S with de=u. Hence S is saturated.
Now embed A as a subobject of a saturated object B. Since the category is well-powered, the strong subobjects of B through which A→B factors form a set; completeness supplies their intersection j:R→B. The same coordinatewise lifting argument used for two factors shows that j is strong, so R is saturated.
The induced map η:A→R is monic. To prove it epic, let f,g:R⇉C satisfy fη=gη. Their equalizer q:E→R is regular and hence strong. Composites of strong monomorphisms are strong, so jq:E→B is a strong subobject containing A. Minimality of the intersection forces R→B to factor through E, which implies f=g. Thus η is epic and therefore anodyne.
For every saturated S, each map a:A→S extends across η to a map aˉ:R→S. This extension is unique because η is epic. Consequently A↦R is left adjoint to the inclusion of saturated objects: the full subcategory S is reflective. This is the saturated reflection from a strong-subobject intersection.
It remains to prove that S is balanced. First let e:X→Y be epic in S. If f,g:Y⇉Z are morphisms in the ambient category, embed Z into a saturated object T. Equality fe=ge then implies equality after composing with Z→T; epicity in the full subcategory gives equality there, and monicity of Z→T gives f=g. Thus e is epic in the ambient category.
If e:X→Y is also monic in S, it is monic in the ambient category as well. Indeed, for u,v:W⇉X with eu=ev, reflect W by an anodyne map W→RW. Saturation extends u and v to uˉ,vˉ:RW⇉X; ambient epicity of W→RW and monicity of e inside S give uˉ=vˉ, hence u=v. Therefore e is anodyne in the ambient category. Saturation of X extends 1X​ across e to a retraction r:Y→X. Since e is epic, ere=e implies er=1Y​, so e is an isomorphism. Hence S is balanced.
