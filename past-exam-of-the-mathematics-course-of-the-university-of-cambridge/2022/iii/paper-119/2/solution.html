Let B be a subterminal object in a Cartesian closed category. For every X and A, the exponential object adjunction gives
C(X,BA)≅C(X×A,B).
(3)
The set on the right has at most one element because B is subterminal. Hence BA is subterminal, proving that Sub(1) is an exponential ideal.
Now let D⊆C be reflective, with reflector L and unit ηA​:A→LA. Recall the useful form of its universal property: an object D lies in D precisely when every map f:A→D factors uniquely through ηA​.
Suppose first that D is an exponential ideal. Reflective subcategories are closed under ambient limits, so LA×LB lies in D. Given f:A×B→D with D∈D, curry in the first variable to obtain A→DB. Since DB∈D, this factors uniquely through ηA​ and uncurries to LA×B→D. Curry once more, now in B; since DLA∈D, the result factors uniquely through ηB​. Thus every f factors uniquely through
ηA​×ηB​:A×B⟶LA×LB.
(4)
This makes LA×LB a reflection of A×B, so uniqueness of reflections gives
L(A×B)≅LA×LB.
(5)
Conversely, suppose L preserves binary products, and take D∈D. To prove DA∈D, start with f:X→DA and let f​:X×A→D be its transpose. Since D is reflective, f​ factors uniquely through
ηX×A​:X×A⟶L(X×A)≅LX×LA.
(6)
Composing the resulting map LX×LA→D with 1LX​×ηA​ and currying produces an extension LX→DA of f. Product preservation identifies the unit on LX×A with 1LX​×ηA​, so the same universal property proves uniqueness. Therefore DA is reflective, and D is an exponential ideal. This proves the reflector product criterion for an exponential ideal.
Finally consider the arrow category [2,Set]. For arrows a:A0​→A1​ and b:B0​→B1​, let
F={(u0​,u1​):u0​:A0​→B0​,u1​:A1​→B1​,bu0​=u1​a}.
(7)
Then the exponential ba is the arrow
F⟶B1A1​​,(u0​,u1​)⟼u1​.
(8)
Indeed, a commutative square from c:C0​→C1​ to this arrow is, after currying, exactly a commutative square a×c→b. This establishes the required exponential adjunction and proves that [2,Set] is cartesian closed.
If b is injective, two elements (u0​,u1​) and (u0′​,u1​) of F have
bu0​=u1​a=bu0′​,
(9)
so injectivity gives u0​=u0′​. Hence F→B1A1​​ is injective. The category of injective functions is therefore an exponential ideal in the arrow category. Its terminal object and binary products are inherited pointwise, so these same exponential objects make Mono(Set) cartesian closed.
