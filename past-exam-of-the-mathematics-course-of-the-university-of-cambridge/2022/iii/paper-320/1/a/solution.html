Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2022 / iii / Paper 320 / 1 / a / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2022 iii Paper 320 1 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
Choose intrinsic Cartesian coordinates in which the oblate spheroid is
X2+Y2+q2Z2​=1.
(1)
Let ℓ be distance along the line of sight and let y be the sky coordinate in the plane containing the line of sight and the symmetry axis. A rotation through the inclination i gives
Y=ycosi−ℓsini,Z=ysini+ℓcosi.
(2)
Substitution into the ellipsoid equation and minimization over ℓ, equivalently requiring the quadratic in ℓ to have zero discriminant on the projected boundary, gives
X2+cos2i+q2sin2iy2​=1.
(3)
Therefore the projected axis ratio of an oblate spheroid is
Q2=cos2i+q2sin2i​.
(4)
It correctly gives Q=1 face-on and Q=q edge-on.
