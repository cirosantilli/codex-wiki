On an oriented pseudo-Riemannian vector space (Rn,η,vol), the Hodge star operator is the unique linear map
∗:Λp⟶Λn−p
(19)
satisfying
α∧∗β=⟨α,β⟩η​vol
(20)
for all p-forms α,β. If s is the number of negative metric directions, then
∗2=(−1)p(n−p)+s
(21)
on p-forms under this convention. For the two-forms in the question,
σ∧μ=(∗σ)∧μ=⟨μ,σ⟩η​vol,
(22)
whereas
σ∧μ=−σ∧(∗μ)=−⟨σ,μ⟩η​vol.
(23)
The induced inner product is symmetric, so these expressions are negatives of one another. Hence a self-dual differential form and an anti-self-dual differential form are orthogonal and
σ∧μ=0​.
(24)
Write w=x1+ix2 and z=x3+ix4. The metric is conformal to the standard Euclidean metric, and the Hodge star on middle-degree differential forms is conformally invariant. Taking eij=dxi∧dxj, the three real forms are
ω1​=e13−e24,ω2​=e14+e23,ω3​=2(e12+e34),
(25)
because ω1​+iω2​=dw∧dz. For the orientation specified by
dw∧dz∧dwˉ∧dzˉ, one has
∗e13=−e24,∗e14=e23,∗e12=e34.
(26)
The corresponding relations for the complementary basis forms immediately give
∗ωk​=ωk​,k=1,2,3​.
(27)
Thus these forms give a real basis of the self-dual two-forms.
Let Dα​=∂α​+Aα​ be the gauge covariant derivative. In these complex coordinates the Anti-self-dual Yang-Mills equations are
Fwz​=0,Fwˉzˉ​=0,Fwwˉ​+Fzzˉ​=0.
(28)
Introduce the spectral parameter λ and the linear operators
L(λ)=Dw​−λDzˉ​,M(λ)=Dz​+λDwˉ​.
(29)
Their commutator is
[L,M]=Fwz​+λ(Fwwˉ​+Fzzˉ​)+λ2Fwˉzˉ​.
(30)
Therefore the Lax pair for the anti-self-dual Yang-Mills equations
L(λ)Ψ=0,M(λ)Ψ=0
(31)
is compatible for every λ exactly when the ASDYM equations hold.
In particular, Fwz​=0 says that the connection restricted to each (w,z) surface is a flat connection. On a simply connected coordinate patch, the compatible equations
(∂w​+Aw​)g=0,(∂z​+Az​)g=0
(32)
have an invertible solution g. Applying the associated gauge transformation sets
Aw​=Az​=0​.
(33)
This conclusion is local; global topology can obstruct a single such gauge over the whole space.
