A nonzero linear functional ℓ∈(Cn+1)∗ restricts on each projective line to define a global holomorphic section sℓ​ of O(1). Its zero divisor is the projective hyperplane Hℓ​={[z]:ℓ(z)=0}, so every hyperplane is an effective divisor of O(1).

Compatibility with the other projective charts gives the linear growth bound in the question along every complex affine line. The supplied Liouville-type result makes f affine-linear, and homogenizing it gives a linear functional ℓ on Cn+1. Thus D=Hℓ​.

mathbbCn={(z,[v])∈Cn×CPn−1:z∈Cv},σ(z,[v])=z.

Hence the construction is independent of coordinates up to biholomorphism.
