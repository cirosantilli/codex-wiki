A holomorphic line bundle is a complex line bundle with holomorphic transition functions, and a holomorphic section is one whose coefficient in every holomorphic local frame is holomorphic. Given a Hermitian metric on a holomorphic vector bundle h, its Chern connection is the connection ∇ satisfying
∇0,1=∂ˉL​,dh(s,t)=h(∇s,t)+h(s,∇t).
(7)
Let e be a nonvanishing holomorphic local frame, put H=h(e,e)>0, and write ∇e=Ae. The first condition forces A0,1=0, while metric compatibility forces
A=H−1∂H=∂logH.
(8)
This determines ∇ uniquely and also constructs it. If e′=ge for a nowhere-zero holomorphic function g, then A′=A+g−1∂g, exactly the connection one-form transformation law, so the local constructions glue.
For a line bundle, A∧A=0, and the curvature form of a connection is
F(∇)=dA=∂ˉ∂logH=−∂∂ˉlogH.
(9)
It has type (1,1). Under e′=ge, the extra term g−1∂g is closed, so the curvature is unchanged and therefore global. This is the local formula for the Chern connection on a line bundle.
Any other Hermitian metric has the form h=euh for a global smooth real function u. Its local squared norm is H=euH, whence
F(∇)−F(∇)=∂ˉ∂u.
(10)
Connections ∇1​ and ∇2​ induce the tensor product connection
(∇1​⊗∇2​)(s1​⊗s2​)=∇1​s1​⊗s2​+s1​⊗∇2​s2​.
(11)
Its connection form in a product frame is A1​+A2​, so the curvature of a tensor product connection is F1​+F2​. For Chern connections, equip L1​⊗L2​ with the product metric
h(s1​⊗s2​,t1​⊗t2​)=h1​(s1​,t1​)h2​(s2​,t2​).
(12)
The tensor product connection has the correct (0,1) part and preserves this metric, so uniqueness identifies it with the Chern connection of h.
