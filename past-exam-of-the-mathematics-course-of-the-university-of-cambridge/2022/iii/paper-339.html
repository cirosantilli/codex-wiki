A vector g is a subgradient of the convex function f at x when
f(y)≥f(x)+gT(y−x)
(1)
for every y. The set of all such vectors is the subdifferential ∂f(x). The proximal operator satisfies
u=proxf​(x)⟺x−u∈∂f(u).​
(2)
More generally, u=proxtf​(x) exactly when (x−u)/t∈∂f(u).

A map T is a firmly nonexpansive mapping when
∥Tx−Ty∥2≤(Tx−Ty)T(x−y)
(23)
for all x,y. Put p=proxf​(x) and q=proxf​(y). Then x−p∈∂f(p) and y−q∈∂f(q). Monotonicity of the subdifferential gives
[(x−p)−(y−q)]T(p−q)≥0,
(24)
which rearranges to
∥p−q∥2≤(p−q)T(x−y).​
(25)
