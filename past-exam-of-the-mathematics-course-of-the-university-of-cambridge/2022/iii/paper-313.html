Write the scalar field energy as E=T+V, where
T=21​∫−∞∞​(ϕ′)2dx,V=∫−∞∞​U(ϕ)dx.
(1)
Under the Derrick scaling ϕλ​(x)=ϕ(λx), a change of variables gives
E(λ)=λT+λ−1V.
(2)
A finite-energy solution of the Euler-Lagrange equation is stationary under this admissible variation. The Derrick virial identity is therefore
0=E′(1)=T−V,21​∫−∞∞​(ϕ′)2dx=∫−∞∞​U(ϕ)dx​.
(3)
The static field equation is ϕ′′=U′(ϕ), so integration gives
U(ϕ)=21​ϕ6−ϕ4+21​ϕ2+C=21​ϕ2(1−ϕ2)2+C.
(4)
The polynomial before C is nonnegative and vanishes, so requiring the minimum to be zero fixes C=0. Hence the vacuum manifold is
U−1(0)={−1,0,1}​,
(5)
which has three elements. A finite-energy scalar-field kink can join only adjacent vacua: a solution cannot cross the intermediate vacuum at finite x because its first integral would have ϕ=ϕ′=0 there and the Picard-Lindelof theorem would make it constant. There are therefore four oriented topological sectors,
−1→0,0→−1,0→1,1→0,
(6)
comprising two increasing kinks and their two antikinks. Symmetry under ϕ↦−ϕ and spatial reflection generates all four from one profile.
For the 0→1 sector, completing the square gives the Bogomolny bound
E​=21​∫−∞∞​[ϕ′−ϕ(1−ϕ2)]2dx+∫−∞∞​ϕ′ϕ(1−ϕ2)dx≥∫01​ϕ(1−ϕ2)dϕ.​
(7)
Equality holds for the Bogomolny equation
ϕ′=ϕ(1−ϕ2)​.
(8)
With y=ϕ2, this becomes the logistic differential equation y′=2y(1−y). Translation invariance supplies an arbitrary center x0​, and the explicit kink in a phi-six model is
ϕ(x)=1+e−2(x−x0​)​1​​.
(9)
It tends to 0 and 1 at the two spatial ends and saturates the bound. Its mass is consequently
M=∫01​ϕ(1−ϕ2)dϕ=41​​.
(10)

Finite energy requires ∣ϕ∣→1 and Dϕ→0 on the circle at spatial infinity. Writing ϕ∼eiχ there gives A∼dχ. The vortex number is the winding number
N=2π1​∮S∞1​​dχ∈Z.
(11)
By Stokes theorem, the magnetic flux is quantized:
∫R2​Bdx1∧dx2=∮S∞1​​A=2πN​.
(12)
For the rotationally symmetric Abelian Higgs vortex ansatz, A=f(r)dθ and ϕ=h(r)eikθ give
B=rf′​,∣Dϕ∣2=(h′)2+r2(k−f)2h2​.
(13)
After the angular integration, the Abelian Higgs model energy becomes
E(f,h)=π∫0∞​[r(f′)2​+r(h′)2+r(k−f)2h2​+4r​(1−h2)2]dr.
(14)
Completing the square in the two pairs of terms gives
πE​=​∫0∞​[r(h′−rk−f​h)2+r1​(f′−2r​(1−h2))2]dr−[(k−f)(1−h2)]0∞​.​
(15)
Regularity at the origin and approach to the vacuum at infinity require
f(0)=0,h(0)=0,f(∞)=k,h(∞)=1​.
(16)
More precisely, h(r)=O(rk) and f(r)=O(r2) near the origin. The boundary term is k, so
E≥kπ​.
(17)
The bound is saturated exactly when both squares vanish, giving the radial Bogomolny vortex equations
h′=rk−f​h,f′=2r​(1−h2)​.
(18)
The asymptotic value f(∞)=k also makes the flux 2πk, so the ansatz has vortex number N=k.

On an oriented pseudo-Riemannian vector space (Rn,η,vol), the Hodge star operator is the unique linear map
∗:Λp⟶Λn−p
(19)
satisfying
α∧∗β=⟨α,β⟩η​vol
(20)
for all p-forms α,β. If s is the number of negative metric directions, then
∗2=(−1)p(n−p)+s
(21)
on p-forms under this convention. For the two-forms in the question,
σ∧μ=(∗σ)∧μ=⟨μ,σ⟩η​vol,
(22)
whereas
σ∧μ=−σ∧(∗μ)=−⟨σ,μ⟩η​vol.
(23)
The induced inner product is symmetric, so these expressions are negatives of one another. Hence a self-dual differential form and an anti-self-dual differential form are orthogonal and
σ∧μ=0​.
(24)
Write w=x1+ix2 and z=x3+ix4. The metric is conformal to the standard Euclidean metric, and the Hodge star on middle-degree differential forms is conformally invariant. Taking eij=dxi∧dxj, the three real forms are
ω1​=e13−e24,ω2​=e14+e23,ω3​=2(e12+e34),
(25)
because ω1​+iω2​=dw∧dz. For the orientation specified by
dw∧dz∧dwˉ∧dzˉ, one has
∗e13=−e24,∗e14=e23,∗e12=e34.
(26)
The corresponding relations for the complementary basis forms immediately give
∗ωk​=ωk​,k=1,2,3​.
(27)
Thus these forms give a real basis of the self-dual two-forms.
Let Dα​=∂α​+Aα​ be the gauge covariant derivative. In these complex coordinates the Anti-self-dual Yang-Mills equations are
Fwz​=0,Fwˉzˉ​=0,Fwwˉ​+Fzzˉ​=0.
(28)
Introduce the spectral parameter λ and the linear operators
L(λ)=Dw​−λDzˉ​,M(λ)=Dz​+λDwˉ​.
(29)
Their commutator is
[L,M]=Fwz​+λ(Fwwˉ​+Fzzˉ​)+λ2Fwˉzˉ​.
(30)
Therefore the Lax pair for the anti-self-dual Yang-Mills equations
L(λ)Ψ=0,M(λ)Ψ=0
(31)
is compatible for every λ exactly when the ASDYM equations hold.
In particular, Fwz​=0 says that the connection restricted to each (w,z) surface is a flat connection. On a simply connected coordinate patch, the compatible equations
(∂w​+Aw​)g=0,(∂z​+Az​)g=0
(32)
have an invertible solution g. Applying the associated gauge transformation sets
Aw​=Az​=0​.
(33)
This conclusion is local; global topology can obstruct a single such gauge over the whole space.
