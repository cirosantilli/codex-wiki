The integrand is nonnegative. If m>0, both terms can vanish only when u=0, so the solution of the modified Helmholtz equation is unique.
If m=0, the identity only forces ∇u=0. On a connected region, u may be any constant. Thus solutions of the Laplace equation with prescribed normal derivative are unique only up to an additive constant, so
uniqueness fails when m=0​.
