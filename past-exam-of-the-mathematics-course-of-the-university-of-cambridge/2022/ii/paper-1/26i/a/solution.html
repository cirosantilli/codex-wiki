The Gauss map sends p to the chosen unit normal N(p). Differentiating ⟨N,N⟩=1 shows that dNp​ maps Tp​S into itself. The shape operator Sp​=−dNp​ is self-adjoint because its associated second fundamental form is symmetric. Its eigenvalues k1​,k2​ are the principal curvatures. A point is umbilical when k1​=k2​, and the surface is minimal when its mean curvature H=(k1​+k2​)/2 vanishes everywhere.
In an orthonormal principal basis, dNp​ has diagonal matrix diag(−k1​,−k2​). It is conformal exactly when
⟨dNp​u,dNp​v⟩=λ(p)⟨u,v⟩,λ(p)>0,
