On a probability space carrying an infinite IID sequence (Xn​) with law m, map ω to (X1​(ω),X2​(ω),…). Its pushforward measure μ satisfies
μ(∏n≥1​An​)=∏n≥1​m(An​)
(141)
for every cylinder set. Cylinder sets form a pi-system generating σ(C), so the sigma-finite uniqueness theorem for measures proves uniqueness. This is the countable product measure m⊗N.
For a cylinder A=A1​×⋯×AN​×R×⋯,
μ(θ−1A)=m(R)m(A1​)⋯m(AN​)=μ(A).
(142)
The same generating-class argument extends this equality to every measurable set, so θ is a measure-preserving transformation.
If A=θ−1A, then A=θ−nA for every n, so membership in A is independent of the first n coordinates. Thus A belongs to the tail sigma-algebra. The zero-one law gives μ(A)∈{0,1}, proving that the shift is ergodic.
