At negligible Reynolds number, the Stokes equations and their no-slip boundary conditions are linear in velocity. Scaling or superposing a prescribed rigid translation therefore scales or superposes the resulting stress and total hydrodynamic force. Hence, for a fixed rod orientation,
F=−R⋅U​.
(361)
The minus sign records that viscous drag opposes motion, and R is the translational hydrodynamic resistance matrix.
