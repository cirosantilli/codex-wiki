For a real symmetric matrix, suppose that one eigenvalue λ∗​ is uniquely closest to s and that x0​ has a nonzero component in its eigenspace. Since the iteration is the power method applied to (A−sI)−1, the normalized vectors converge, up to sign, to an eigenvector for λ∗​.
