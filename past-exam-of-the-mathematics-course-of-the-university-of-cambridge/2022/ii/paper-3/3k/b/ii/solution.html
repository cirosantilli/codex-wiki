A square-free monomial in d variables of degree j≤d−1 evaluates to one at exactly 2d−j points of F2d​, an even number. Since parity of Hamming weight is a linear functional over F2​, every sum of such evaluation words also has even weight. Hence every word in RM(d,d−1) has even weight.
If f and g have degrees at most r and d−r−1, then fg has degree at most d−1. The binary inner product of their evaluation words is the parity of the weight of the evaluation of fg, and is therefore zero. Thus
RM(d,d−r−1)⊆RM(d,r)⊥.
(18)
Finally, binomial symmetry gives
dimRM(d,r)+dimRM(d,d−r−1)=2d,
(19)
so the inclusion has equal dimensions and
RM(d,r)⊥=RM(d,d−r−1)​.
(20)
This is the Dual of a Reed-Muller code.
