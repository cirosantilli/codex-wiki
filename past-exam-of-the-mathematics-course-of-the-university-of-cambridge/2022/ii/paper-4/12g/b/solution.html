It is continuous. The map T∘f:D→D has a fixed point x by the Brouwer fixed-point theorem.
If ∣x∣<1, the identity T(f(x))=x can hold only with f(x)=x, because the projection of a point outside D lies on its boundary. If ∣x∣=1, the hypothesis gives ∣f(x)∣≤1, so T(f(x))=f(x) and again f(x)=x. Thus f has a fixed point.
