There are two bifurcations in 0≤k≤1/2, both at k=0.
Near (0,0,0), append k˙=0. The plane y=0 is invariant and tangent to the extended center subspace, so it is an extended center manifold. The reduced equation is exactly
x˙=x(−k−3x+x2),k˙=0.
(327)
Its leading terms −kx−3x2 have two branches, x=0 and x=−k/3+O(k2), which cross and exchange their center-direction stability. Hence this is a transcritical bifurcation.
For the bifurcation at (1,2,0), set
X=x−1,v=y−2−X.
(328)
The extended system becomes
X˙=(1+X)(v−k+X2),v˙=v+v2+(1+X)(k−X2),k˙=0.
(329)
Solving the centre-manifold invariance equation for v=h(X,k) gives
h(X,k)=−k+X2−5Xk+9k2+O((∣X∣+∣k∣)3).
(330)
Substitution into the X equation yields
X˙=2(X2−k)+O((∣X∣+∣k∣)3).
(331)
For k>0 this has two nearby equilibria X=±k​+O(k), while for k<0 it has none. It is therefore a saddle-node bifurcation. These reductions are collected in bifurcations of the 2022 Cambridge quadratic-cubic system.
