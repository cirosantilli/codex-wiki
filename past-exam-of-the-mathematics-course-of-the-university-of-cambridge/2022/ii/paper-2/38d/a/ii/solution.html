Along the constant latitude, use ϕ as parameter. The parallel transport equations are
dϕdVθ​−sinθcosθVϕ=0,dϕdVϕ​+cotθVθ=0.
(261)
With W=sinθVϕ, these become
dϕdVθ​=cosθW,dϕdW​=−cosθVθ.
(262)
The initial components (1,0) therefore evolve as
Vθ(ϕ)=cos(ϕcosθ),Vϕ(ϕ)=−sinθsin(ϕcosθ)​.
(263)
After one circuit, the parallel transport around a latitude of the unit sphere gives
(Vθ,Vϕ)=(cos(2πcosθ),−sinθsin(2πcosθ)​)​.
(264)
