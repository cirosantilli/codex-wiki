The discrete logarithm problem asks, given g and h≡ga(modp), to recover a modulo p−1. In the Diffie-Hellman key exchange, Alice sends ga, Bob sends gb, and both compute gab. An enemy able to compute discrete logarithms recovers a or b from the public messages and hence obtains the key.
For three participants with private exponents a,b,c, circulate three tokens around a directed ring. Start with ga,gb,gc; whenever a participant receives a token, they raise it to their private exponent and pass it on.
