The Reynolds number is
Re=νUL​​.
(269)
When it is large, viscosity is negligible in most of the flow, but the inviscid outer solution cannot generally satisfy the no-slip boundary condition at a rigid wall. A thin boundary layer resolves this mismatch: tangential gradients remain of outer scale L, normal gradients have the much shorter scale δ, and the small viscous coefficient is offset by uyy​=O(U/δ2). Matching a viscous inner solution to an inviscid outer solution retains the leading viscous effects without solving the full equations uniformly everywhere.
