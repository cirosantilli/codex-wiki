(296)

(297)
For an inviscid elastic liquid, μ=0, cP2​=λ/ρ, and σ=λ(∇⋅u)I. Continuity of normal displacement and normal traction at z=0 gives
cosθ(1−R)=Tcosθ′,λk(1+R)=λ′k′T.
(298)
