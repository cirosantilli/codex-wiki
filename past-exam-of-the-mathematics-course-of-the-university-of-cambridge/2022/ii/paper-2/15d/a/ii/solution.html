Alice holds the unknown input ∣α⟩ and one qubit of a shared ∣ϕ+⟩ pair; Bob holds the other. Alice performs a Bell measurement on her two qubits and sends its two-bit outcome to Bob. For outcomes
ϕ+,ϕ−,ψ+,ψ−, Bob's qubit is respectively
∣α⟩,Z∣α⟩,X∣α⟩,XZ∣α⟩

up to global phase. Bob applies respectively I,Z,X,ZX and obtains ∣α⟩. This is quantum teleportation.
