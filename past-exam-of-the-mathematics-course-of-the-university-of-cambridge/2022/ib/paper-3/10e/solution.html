Matrices over a Euclidean domain are equivalent when B=PAQ for invertible matrices P,Q, equivalently when one can pass between them by invertible elementary row and column operations.
For a nonzero matrix, move a nonzero entry to the top left and use Euclidean division and row or column operations to replace it by any nonzero remainder. Repeating terminates with an entry d1​ dividing every entry; otherwise adding an offending entry into its row would permit one more strict Euclidean reduction.
