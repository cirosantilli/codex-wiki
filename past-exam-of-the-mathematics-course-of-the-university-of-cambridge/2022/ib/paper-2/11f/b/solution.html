For an oriented regular surface with unit normal N, the Gauss map sends each point p to N(p)∈S2. The outward normal to the catenoid is
N(u,v)=(sechucosv,sechusinv,−tanhu).
(54)
Its image is the open southern hemisphere, apart from its limiting equator and south pole. The Gauss map is one-to-one after taking v modulo 2π and reverses orientation. Since the Jacobian of the Gauss map is the Gaussian curvature, its signed spherical area is the total Gaussian curvature:
∫S′​KdA=−area(hemisphere)=−2π​.
(55)
Equivalently, KdA=−sech2ududv, whose integral over u>0 and 0≤v<2π is −2π.
