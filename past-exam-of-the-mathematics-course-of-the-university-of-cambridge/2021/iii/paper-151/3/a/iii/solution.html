By part ii, Gn​ is the intersection of finitely many open subgroups, so it is open. Conjugation permutes the subgroups of each index, hence Gn​ is also a normal subgroup. The groups Gn​ form a descending family. If U is any open normal subgroup and [G:U]=m, then Gm​⊆U. Thus (Gn​) is cofinal among the open normal neighborhoods of the identity. Part i now gives
G≅nlim​​G/Gn​​.
