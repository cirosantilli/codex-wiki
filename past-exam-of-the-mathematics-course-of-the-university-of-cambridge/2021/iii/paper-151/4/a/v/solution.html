For the trivial action, crossed homomorphisms G→Q are ordinary group homomorphisms, while principal crossed homomorphisms vanish. The image of a finite group in the torsion-free additive group Q must be trivial, so
H1(G,Q)=0​.
(34)
Degree-zero cohomology is the invariant submodule; the action is trivial, so
H0(G,Q)=Q​.
