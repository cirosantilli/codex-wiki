By the Fundamental theorem of finitely generated abelian groups, write
A≅Zr⊕F
(11)
with F finite. Taking profinite completions gives
A≅Z,r⊕F.
(12)
If A≅Z, this is plainly Z.
Conversely, suppose A≅Z. Reduction modulo a prime p gives
A/pA≅(Z/pZ)r⊕F/pF,
(13)
whereas Z/pZ≅Z/pZ. Choosing p∤∣F∣ first shows r=1. If F=0, choosing a prime divisor p of ∣F∣ makes F/pF=0, a contradiction. Thus F=0 and A≅Z.
