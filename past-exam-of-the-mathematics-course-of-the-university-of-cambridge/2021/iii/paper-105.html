The boundedness in the Sobolev space H1(U) and the weak sequential compactness of bounded sequences in a reflexive Banach space give a subsequence converging weakly to some u∈H1(U). For each integer m≥2, the Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem makes H1(U)↪Lm(U) compact because the dimension is two. Repeated extraction followed by the diagonal argument gives one subsequence unk​​ converging strongly to u in every Lm(U) with integral m≥2.
For any finite real p≥1, choose an integer m≥max{2,p}. Since U has finite measure, the Lp inclusion on a finite measure space gives
∥unk​​−u∥Lp(U)​≤∣U∣1/p−1/m∥unk​​−u∥Lm(U)​⟶0.
(12)
The same subsequence therefore works for every finite p.
