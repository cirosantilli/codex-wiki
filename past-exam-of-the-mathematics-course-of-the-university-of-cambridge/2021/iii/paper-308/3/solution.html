In the Rational map approximation for Skyrmions, stereographic coordinate z describes the direction x∈S2, and a degree-B rational map R(z)=p(z)/q(z) defines a unit vector nR​. The Skyrme model field is approximated by
U(r,z)=exp[if(r)nR​(z)⋅τ],f(0)=π,f(∞)=0.

They are the face centers of the dodecahedral B=7 Skyrmion, equivalently the vertices of its dual icosahedron, and make its icosahedral symmetry visible directly in the rational map.
