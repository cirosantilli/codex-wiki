By part ii every subset of the Baire space of sequences is 2ℵ0​-Suslin. If 2ℵ0​≤ℵ2​, part i would make every such set ℵ2​-Suslin. Therefore
Ψℵ2​​⟹2ℵ0​>ℵ2​.​
(8)
Now suppose 2ℵ0​>ℵ2​. The axiom of choice gives a set B⊆ωω of cardinality exactly ℵ2​. If B were ℵ1​-Suslin, the Aleph-one-Suslin decomposition into analytic sets would write it as a union of ℵ1​ analytic sets. If all those analytic sets were countable, their union would have cardinality at most ℵ1​, so one of them is uncountable. The perfect set property for analytic sets then makes that member, and hence B, have cardinality 2ℵ0​, contradicting
∣B∣=ℵ2​<2ℵ0​.
(9)
Thus B is not ℵ1​-Suslin, and
2ℵ0​>ℵ2​⟹Ψℵ1​​.​
(10)
