A regular category has finite limits, every morphism factors through its image of a morphism in a regular category as a regular epimorphism followed by a monomorphism, and regular epimorphisms are stable under every pullback in a category. A cover is a strong epimorphism. Every regular epimorphism is strong: if e is the coequalizer of r,s and a square has e on the left and a monomorphism m on the right, monicity shows that the upper arrow coequalizes r,s. It therefore factors through e, and the epimorphism property of e shows that this factor is the required diagonal. Conversely, factor a strong epimorphism f as f=me with e regular epic and m monic. The lifting property gives a two-sided inverse to m, so m is an isomorphism and f is regular epic. Thus regular epimorphisms and covers coincide.
Let L:C→D be the left-exact reflector and let m:A′↪A. Since L preserves finite limits, Lm is monic. Define cA​(A′) as the pullback
cA​(A′)↓A​⟶ηA​​​LA′↓LmLA.​
(27)
Naturality of the unit supplies a map A′→cA​(A′) over A, proving A′≤cA​(A′). A factorization A′≤A′′ induces LA′≤LA′′ and therefore cA​(A′)≤cA​(A′′), so cA​ is order-preserving.
Apply L to the defining pullback. Left exactness and the fact that LηA​:LA→LLA is an isomorphism identify L(cA​(A′)) with LA′. Pulling back once more therefore gives
cA​(cA​(A′))≅cA​(A′).
(28)
For a map f:B→A, left exactness identifies L(f∗A′) with (Lf)∗(LA′). Pasting the two pullback squares then yields
cB​(f∗A′)≅f∗cA​(A′),
(29)
so this closure operation induced by a left-exact reflector commutes with pullback.
Assume A lies in D, so ηA​ is an isomorphism. If A′ also lies in D, its unit is an isomorphism and the defining square gives cA​(A′)≅A′. Conversely, if A′ is closed, that square expresses A′ as a finite limit of A, LA′, and LA, all fixed by L. Fixed objects of a left-exact reflective subcategory are closed under finite limits, so A′ belongs to D.
Finally suppose C is regular. The fixed objects have finite limits. For f:A→B in D, factor it in C as
Ae​Im​B.
(30)
Applying L gives ALe​LILm​B. The map Le is regular epic because a left adjoint preserves the coequalizer presenting e, and Lm is monic because L is left exact. Thus D has image factorizations. Their image subobject is the closure cB​(I). A map in D is regular epic exactly when this closure is all of its codomain. Images in C commute with pullback, and the closure operation also commutes with pullback, so this condition is pullback-stable. Hence D is regular, as stated by the left-exact reflective subcategory of a regular category theorem.
