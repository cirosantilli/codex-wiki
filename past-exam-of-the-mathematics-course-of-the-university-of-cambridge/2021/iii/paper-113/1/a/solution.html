For every homogeneous s∈S+​,
D+​(φ(s))={p∈ProjT:φ(s)∈/p}.
(1)
Hence
U=⋃s∈S+​ homogeneous​D+​(φ(s)),
(2)
so U is open in the Zariski topology.
If p∈U, then φ−1(p) is a homogeneous prime ideal that does not contain the irrelevant ideal of a graded ring S+​. Thus
f(p)=φ−1(p)
(3)
defines a map U→ProjS. On every Standard affine open of Proj the graded homomorphism induces
(Ss​)0​⟶(Tφ(s)​)0​,
(4)
and therefore an affine scheme morphism
D+​(φ(s))⟶D+​(s).
(5)
These morphisms agree after localization on overlaps, so they glue to the required morphism of schemes f:U→ProjS.
