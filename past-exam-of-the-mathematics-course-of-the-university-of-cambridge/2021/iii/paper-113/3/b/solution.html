Because X is a Noetherian scheme, choose a finite affine cover X=⋃i​Ui​. Every intersection Ui​∩Uj​ is quasi-compact, so choose a finite affine cover Ui​∩Uj​=⋃k​Uijk​. If ji​:Ui​↪X and jijk​:Uijk​↪X are the inclusions, the sheaf axiom gives an exact sequence
0⟶F⟶⨁i​(ji​)∗​(F∣Ui​​)⟶⨁i,j,k​(jijk​)∗​(F∣Uijk​​),
(23)
where the last arrow is the difference of the two restrictions to each overlap chart.
Applying the left-exact direct image sheaf functor identifies f∗​F with the kernel of
⨁i​(fji​)∗​(F∣Ui​​)⟶⨁i,j,k​(fjijk​)∗​(F∣Uijk​​).
(24)
Every map Ui​→Y and Uijk​→Y is a morphism between affine schemes. Part (a) shows that all sheaves in the two finite sums are quasi-coherent sheaves. Kernels of morphisms of quasi-coherent sheaves on the affine scheme Y are quasi-coherent, because they correspond to kernels of module homomorphisms. Therefore f∗​F is quasi-coherent. This is the quasi-coherence of direct image under a quasi-compact quasi-separated morphism in the present Noetherian case.
