Write A=k[x,y], X=SpecA, and U=X∖Z. Since A is an integral domain, no nonzero global section is supported only at the origin, so
HZ0​(X,OX​)=0.
(26)
The punctured affine plane has the affine cover U=D(x)∪D(y). Its Čech cochain complex for the structure sheaf is
0⟶Ax​⊕Ay​(a,b)↦a−b​Axy​⟶0.
(27)
Consequently
H0(U,OU​)=A,H1(U,OU​)=Ax​+Ay​Axy​​,Hi(U,OU​)=0(i≥2).
(28)
Because X is affine, the higher sheaf cohomology of OX​ vanishes. The long exact sequence for local cohomology therefore gives
HZ1​(X,OX​)=0,HZ2​(X,OX​)≅k[x±1,y]+k[x,y±1]k[x±1,y±1]​,
(29)
and HZi​(X,OX​)=0 for i≥3. The nonzero group has the k-basis
{x−ay−b:a,b≥1}.
(30)
This computes the local cohomology of the affine plane supported at the origin in every degree.
