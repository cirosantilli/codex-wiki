If the filtration is stable from degree ℓ, then M∗ is generated over R∗ by finite generating sets for M0​,…,Mℓ​. Conversely, let homogeneous elements of degrees at most ℓ generate M∗. In every degree n>ℓ, each expression for an element of Mn​ uses a positive-degree coefficient from R∗, so Mn​=IMn−1​. Hence finite generation is equivalent to stability.
For M′⊆M, take
Mn​=InM∩M′.
(49)
Then M∗ is a graded submodule of the finite Rees module ⨁InM. Since R is Noetherian and I is finitely generated, R∗ is Noetherian; hence M∗ is finite and the filtration is stable. Therefore, for some ℓ and all n≥ℓ,
InM∩M′=In−ℓ(IℓM∩M′).​
(50)
This is the Artin-Rees lemma.
