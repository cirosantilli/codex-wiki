For Si​=[n]∖{i}, the chain rule for information entropy gives
H(XSi​​)=∑j∈Si​​H(Xj​∣XSi​∩[j−1]​).

Each summand is at least H(Xj​∣X1j−1​) because conditioning reduces entropy. Summing over i, each j occurs n−1 times:

The three-point identity for relative entropy is
D(P∥Q)=D(P∥R)+D(R∥Q)+a∑​{P(a)−R(a)}logQ(a)R(a)​​.

The binary Kraft inequality states that codeword lengths l(a) of a prefix code, or more generally a uniquely decodable code, satisfy
∑a​2−l(a)≤1.
(20)
Conversely, positive integer lengths obeying this inequality can be realized by a binary prefix code.
