The Poisson tensor has rank two there and is tangent to each level set, so it inverts to a symplectic form; each sphere is a symplectic leaf. Rotations preserve both F and the alternating tensor ϵabc​, hence preserve the restricted Poisson tensor and its inverse symplectic form. The SO(3) action therefore restricts to a symplectic action on every sphere SR2​⊂R3.
