Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2021 / iii / Paper 144 / 1 / c / Solution

OurBigBook.com Words: 111
Enumerate the countable atomic model as M={a0​,a1​,…} and fix any N⊨T. We recursively construct finite partial elementary maps fn​ from the first n elements of M into N.
Suppose fn​(aˉ)=bˉ. Since M is atomic, choose a formula θ(xˉ,y) isolating tp(aˉ,an​). Its existential consequence ∃yθ(xˉ,y) belongs to tp(aˉ), so partial elementarity gives
N⊨∃yθ(bˉ,y).
(1)
Choose a witness bn​. Because θ isolates the complete joint type, extending fn​ by an​↦bn​ remains partial elementary. The union of the recursive maps is an elementary embedding M→N. Thus every countable atomic model is a prime model, proving the countable atomic model is prime result.
