For a∈k and each n, choose a lift xn​∈OK​ of the unique pnth root ap−n, which exists because k is a perfect field. Define
[a]=limn→∞​xnpn​.
(11)
Changing xn​ by an element of the maximal ideal changes its pnth power by an element whose valuation tends to infinity, so the limit exists and is independent of all choices. In characteristic p, the Frobenius endomorphism satisfies (x+y)pn=xpn+ypn, making [⋅] a ring homomorphism lifting the identity on k. If s:k→OK​ is any other such section, then
s(a)=s(ap−n)pn
(12)
for every n, and the same limiting construction forces s(a)=[a]. This proves uniqueness of the Teichmuller lift.
Choose a uniformizer t. Repeatedly subtracting the lift of the residue and dividing by t gives every x∈OK​ a unique convergent Teichmuller expansion
x=∑j≥0​[aj​]tj.
(13)
Because the lift is a ring map, this identifies OK​ with k[[t]] and its fraction field with the Laurent series field k((t)). This is the equal-characteristic complete discretely valued field classification.
If K is locally compact, its compact valuation ring has only finitely many disjoint residue-class balls. Thus k is finite, as also follows from the local compactness criterion for a complete non-Archimedean field.
