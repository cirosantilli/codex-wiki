The angle
ϕ=(p+q)λ2​−pλ1​−qΩ2​
(53)
obeys the D'Alembert characteristic. Because reflection in the reference plane makes the disturbing function even in inclination, the nodal coefficient must be even, and therefore
q is even​.
(54)
At astronomical conjunction, λ1​=λ2​=λc​, so
qϕ​=λc​−Ω2​​.
(55)
Thus ϕ/q is the angular distance from the ascending node of M2​ to the conjunction. When M2​ is at that node, λ2​=Ω2​, and
pϕ​=Ω2​−λ1​​.
(56)
Up to the chosen sign convention, ϕ/p is the angular separation between the inner body and the node when the outer body crosses the reference plane. These two views explain geometrically why libration confines both conjunction and node-crossing phases.
