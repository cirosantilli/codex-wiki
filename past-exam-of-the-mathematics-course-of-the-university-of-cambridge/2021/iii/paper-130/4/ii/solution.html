Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge / 2021 / iii / Paper 130 / 4 / ii / Solution

 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2021 iii Paper 130 4 ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 170
Assume condition i and call a set B⊆N bad when it contains no member of S. No finite collection of bad sets covers N: if it did, assigning each integer to the first bad set containing it would give a finite coloring whose color classes are bad, contrary to condition i. Consequently
{N∖B:B is bad}
(23)
has the finite-intersection property. It generates a proper filter on a set, which the ultrafilter lemma extends to an ultrafilter U. If some A∈U were bad, then N∖A would also belong to U by construction, contradicting propriety. Hence every A∈U contains a member of S, proving condition ii.
For the final question, let S consist of the pairs {x,2x} and {x,3x}. Color n by
v2​(n)+v3​(n)(mod2).
(24)
Multiplication by either two or three reverses this parity, so this two-coloring has no monochromatic member of S. Condition i fails, and the equivalence just proved shows that no ultrafilter with the stated property exists.
