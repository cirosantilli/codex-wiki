The James submodule theorem says that for every FSn​-submodule U≤Mλ, either
Sλ⊆UorU⊆(Sλ)⊥,

where orthogonality is taken with respect to the tabloid bilinear form.
Fix a λ-tableau t. Part a shows that for every tabloid {s}, the vector bt​{s} is either zero or a signed copy of the polytabloid e(t). Comparing the coefficient of {t} gives the precise identity
bt​u=⟨u,e(t)⟩e(t)(u∈Mλ).
(4)
If U⊈(Sλ)⊥, choose u∈U and a tableau t with ⟨u,e(t)⟩=0. Since U is a submodule, the identity puts e(t) in U. Every polytabloid of shape λ is an Sn​-translate of e(t), so their span Sλ lies in U. If no such u,t exist, then by definition U⊆(Sλ)⊥. This proves the theorem over the arbitrary field F.
