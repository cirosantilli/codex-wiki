codimkerθ=dimSλ=dimSλ′=codim(Sλ′)⊥,
(12)
and hence
kerθ=(Sλ′)⊥​.
(13)
Fix the original tableaux t,u. For a λ-tableau w, let gw​ be the unique permutation satisfying w=gw​t and put εw​=sgn(gw​). Since
θ({w′})=εw​e(w)⊗e(u),
(14)
the quotient pairing Mλ′/(Sλ′)⊥≅(Sλ′)∗ gives the explicit conjugate Specht module as a sign-twisted dual isomorphism
e(w)⊗e(u)longmapsto[e(v′)⟼εw​⟨{w′},e(v′)⟩]
(15)
