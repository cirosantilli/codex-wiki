 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2021 ii Paper 1 25I Non-isomorphism of the two unions of lines
OurBigBook.com Words: 129
The three coordinate axes are the three irreducible components of
V=V(xy,xz,yz)⊆Ak3​.
(311)
Their common intersection is the origin. Likewise, if ℓ1​,ℓ2​,ℓ3​ are distinct linear forms defining the three lines in Ak2​, then
W=V(ℓ1​ℓ2​ℓ3​),
(312)
and its three irreducible components have only the origin in common. Any isomorphism of algebraic varieties would permute irreducible components and therefore map the origin of V to the origin of W.
Every defining polynomial xy,xz,yz has zero differential at the origin, so
T0​V=k3anddimT0​V=3.
(313)
The defining polynomial of W is homogeneous of degree three, so its differential also vanishes at the origin, giving
T0​W=k2anddimT0​W=2.
(314)
An isomorphism induces an isomorphism of Zariski tangent spaces, which is impossible here. Hence V and W are not isomorphic. This is the tangent-space obstruction between two unions of three lines.
