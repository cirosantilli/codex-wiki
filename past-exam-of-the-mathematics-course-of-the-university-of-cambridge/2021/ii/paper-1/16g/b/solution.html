Suppose the finitary set of formulae S is equivalent to the finite set
F={f1​,…,fm​}.
(156)
For each fj​, the relation S⊢fj​ has a finite proof, so it uses a finite set Rj​⊆S of assumptions. Put
R=⋃j=1m​Rj​.
(157)
This is a finite subset of S, and R⊢fj​ for every j, hence R⊢F. Since equivalence also gives F⊢S, transitivity of deduction yields
R⊢S​.
