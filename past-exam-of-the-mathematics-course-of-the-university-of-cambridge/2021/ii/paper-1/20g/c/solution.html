g​(T)=T(T2+T−1).
(223)

(224)

Modulo 2,
g​(T)=T(T2+T+1),
(225)

(2)=(2,β)(2,β2+β+1)​.
(226)
This is a product of two distinct proper prime ideals, so (2) is not prime. These decompositions are collected in prime ideals above two and three in the cubic field of discriminant minus 307.
