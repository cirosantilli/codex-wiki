An analytic continuation of f1​ from D1​ to D2​ is a holomorphic function f2​ on D2​ whose restriction to the nonempty overlap D1​∩D2​ agrees with f1​.
Suppose f2​ and f​2​ are two such continuations. Both equal f1​ on D1​∩D2​, so
f2​=f​2​
(127)
on that nonempty open set. Since D2​ is connected, the identity theorem gives
f2​=f​2​ throughout D2​​.
(128)
Thus analytic continuation to D2​ is unique.
