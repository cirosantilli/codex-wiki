Suppose there were no singular point in the Riemann sphere. Then every finite point would be ordinary, so p and q would be entire functions. Ordinary behavior at infinity would force p(z)=2/z+O(z−2) and q(z)=O(z−4). In particular p(z)→0, so Liouville theorem would give p≡0, contradicting the required leading term 2/z. Thus infinity cannot also be ordinary, and every such differential equation has at least one singular point in C.
