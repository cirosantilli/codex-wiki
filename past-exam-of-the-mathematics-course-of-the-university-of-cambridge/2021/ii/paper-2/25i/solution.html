A morphism Pn→P1 is represented by homogeneous polynomials F,G of the same degree with no common projective zero. If the degree is positive and n≥2, the Projective dimension theorem forces the two hypersurfaces V(F) and V(G) to meet. Thus the degree is zero and the morphism is constant.
