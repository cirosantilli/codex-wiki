For cardinals, κ<λ means that there is an injection κ→λ but no bijection. Under the axiom of choice, every set is well-orderable, so every infinite cardinal is the initial ordinal ℵα​ for a unique ordinal α. Cantor theorem gives
ℵα​<2ℵα​,
(74)
and minimality of the successor cardinal gives
ℵα+1​≤2ℵα​.
(75)
The strict inequality ℵα+1​<2ℵα​ printed in the conversion is not provable and can be false under the generalized continuum hypothesis; the intended symbol must be ≤.
