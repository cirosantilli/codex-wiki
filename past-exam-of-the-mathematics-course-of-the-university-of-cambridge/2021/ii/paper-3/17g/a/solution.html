The diagonal Ramsey number R(k) is the least n such that every red-blue coloring of Kn​ contains a monochromatic Kk​. The standard recursion
R(s,t)≤R(s−1,t)+R(s,t−1)
(80)
gives
R(k)≤(k−12k−2​)<4k.
(81)
Thus the converted k4 is a superscript-order error; that bound is false for large k.
In a red-blue coloring, if the red spanning graph is connected it has a red spanning tree. Otherwise its red components are joined pairwise by blue edges, making the blue graph connected, so it has a blue spanning tree.
The result fails for three colors. Color the six edges of K4​ by its three perfect matchings, one color per matching. Every monochromatic graph then consists of two disjoint edges and has no spanning tree.
