Expand det(A+tB) by multilinearity of the determinant in its columns. The coefficient of tj is a sum of determinants obtained by choosing j columns from B and the remaining columns from A. Since the columns of B span a space of dimension k, every choice of more than k columns from B is linearly dependent. All coefficients of tj for j>k therefore vanish, so
degdet(A+tB)≤k.
(57)
If k=n, then B is invertible and the coefficient of tn is detB=0. Hence the degree is exactly n.
For a zero-polynomial example with k=1, take
A=(00​00​),B=(10​00​).
(58)
Then rankB=1, while det(A+tB)=0 for every t.
