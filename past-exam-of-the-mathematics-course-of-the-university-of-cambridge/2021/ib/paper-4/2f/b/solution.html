If f:X→Y is continuous, then f∘π is continuous because the quotient map π is continuous by definition.
Conversely, suppose f∘π is continuous. For every open set V⊆Y,
π−1(f−1(V))=(f∘π)−1(V)

is open in X. The definition of the quotient topology then says that f−1(V) is open in X. Hence
f is continuous⟺f∘π is continuous​.
