For 0≤u≤1, independence gives
P(U>u)=P(X>u,Y>u)=(1−u)2.
(5)
Thus U has probability density function fU​(u)=2(1−u), and its expected value is
EU=∫01​2u(1−u)du=31​.​
(6)
Pointwise, U+V=X+Y and UV=XY. By linearity of expectation, EV=1−1/3=2/3; by independence, E(UV)=EXEY=1/4. Consequently the covariance of two uniform order statistics is
Cov(U,V)=E(UV)−EUEV=41​−92​=361​.​
(7)
The order statistics are dependent despite the independence of the original observations.
