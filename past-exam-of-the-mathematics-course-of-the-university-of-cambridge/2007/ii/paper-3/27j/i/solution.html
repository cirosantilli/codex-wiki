At each nondegenerate binary node, no arbitrage requires the riskless gross return R=1+r to lie strictly between the two stock returns. The three intervals are (4/5,2) at time zero, (6/5,3/2) at the upper node, and (5/6,4/3) at the lower node. Their intersection gives
1/5<r<1/3.​
(59)
At R=5/4, the upper-branch risk-neutral probabilities are (RS−Sd​)/(Su​−Sd​). They are 3/8 initially, 1/6 after the up move, and 5/6 after the down move. All are strictly between zero and one, so the martingale measure is equivalent and unique. The four terminal probabilities in the PDF's order 45,36,16,10 are 1/16,5/16,25/48,5/48.
