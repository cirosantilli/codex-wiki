In the instantaneous rest frame the total radiation has zero net three-momentum, so its emitted four-momentum is proportional to the four-velocity. A Lorentz boost multiplies its energy by γ, while the elapsed coordinate time for the source is also multiplied by γ. Their ratio, total instantaneous radiated power, is therefore invariant. This concerns total emitted power, not the directional flux or an observer's photon arrival rate.

U˙=(γ3α,γ3αv+γv˙).​
