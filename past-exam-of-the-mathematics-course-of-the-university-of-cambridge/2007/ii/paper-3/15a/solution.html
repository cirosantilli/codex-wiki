For a nonrelativistic ideal gas, the virial relation is Egrav​=−2Ekin​ and total energy is negative. Under adiabatic compression its kinetic energy scales as R−2, faster than the magnitude R−1 of gravity, providing restoring support; its adiabatic index 5/3 exceeds 4/3. For an ultrarelativistic gas, Egrav​=−Ekin​ and both terms scale as R−1, giving marginal stability at adiabatic index 4/3 in this idealized Newtonian model.
