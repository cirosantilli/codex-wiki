Away from the turning points, the Liouville–Green approximation gives in the allowed region
ψ∼q−1/4[Acos(λ∫ax​q​ds)+Bsin(λ∫ax​q​ds)].
(89)
The bound-state condition discards the growing exponential in each forbidden region, leaving
ψL​∼CL​(−q)−1/4exp[−λ∫xa​−q​ds],ψR​∼CR​(−q)−1/4exp[−λ∫bx​−q​ds].
(90)
These approximations require variation slow compared with the local wavelength, and must be replaced near a,b by Airy approximations.
Near a, set z=−λ2/3q′(a)1/3(x−a). The local differential equation becomes ψzz​−zψ=0. Decay on the left selects Airy function Ai(z) rather than its growing companion. Comparing the supplied asymptotics across the turning point yields
ψ∼2CL​q−1/4cos(Sa​(x)−4π​),Sa​(x)=λ∫ax​q​ds.
(91)
At b, use z=λ2/3[−q′(b)]1/3(x−b); decay on the right similarly yields 2CR​q−1/4cos(Sb​(x)−π/4), where Sb​=λ∫xb​q​ds. Let J=Sa​+Sb​=λ∫ab​q​ds. The left expression has equal cosine and sine coefficients in the variable Sa​; the right expression has those coefficients proportional to cos(J−π/4) and sin(J−π/4). They can describe the same nonzero wave only when J−π/4=π/4+nπ. Therefore the WKB quantization condition is
λ∫ab​q(x)​dx=(n+21​)π,n=0,1,2,….​
(92)
The two simple turning points contribute the two quarter-phase shifts, giving the half-integer correction.
