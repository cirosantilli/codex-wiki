Label the two displacements in cell n by un​,vn​, with cell length 2a. Choose the K spring within a cell and the G spring between cells. The equations are
mu¨n​=−(K+G)un​+Kvn​+Gvn−1​,mv¨n​=−(K+G)vn​+Kun​+Gun+1​.
(97)
A Bloch wave (un​,vn​)=(u,v)ei(2aqn−ωt) gives a two-by-two eigenvalue problem, whose determinant is
(K+G−mω2)2−(K+Ge−2iaq)(K+Ge2iaq)=0.
(98)
Thus the alternating-spring chain dispersion relation is
ω±2​(q)=mK+G±K2+G2+2KGcos(2aq)​​.​
(99)
Periodic boundaries require e2iaqN=1, so q=πj/(Na) modulo π/a. There are N distinct wavenumbers in the Brillouin zone −π/(2a)≤q<π/(2a) and two branches, accounting for 2N modes. The minus branch is acoustic, with zero frequency and in-phase motion at the centre; the plus branch is optical, with opposite cell displacements.
Expanding the square root near zero gives
ω−​(q)∼am(K+G)2KG​​∣q∣,ω+​(q)∼m2(K+G)​​[1−2(K+G)2KGa2q2​].​
(100)
At the zone boundary the square root is K−G, so the acoustic maximum is 2G/m​ and the optical minimum is 2K/m​. The frequency gap is therefore
Δω=2K/m​−2G/m​.​
(101)
Quantizing each harmonic mode gives phonons, bosonic excitations of energy ℏω±​(q) and crystal momentum ℏq defined modulo a reciprocal lattice vector. Their group velocity is dω/dq; acoustic phonons carry long-wavelength sound, while optical phonons have a nonzero centre frequency. There is one longitudinal polarization per branch here, and phonon number is not conserved in thermal equilibrium.
