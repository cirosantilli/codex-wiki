For massive motion put ℓ=h2/(GM). The Newtonian solution is u0​=(1+ecosϕ)/ℓ. The resonant cosine forcing in 3GMu02​ is 6GMecosϕ/ℓ2, producing the secular correction 3GMeϕsinϕ/ℓ2. Resumming it into a shifted phase gives the precessing-ellipse approximation
