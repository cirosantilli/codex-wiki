For a fixed amount of simple compressible material, dU=T,dS−p,dV and G=U−TS+pV gives dG=−S,dT+V,dp. Equality of mixed partial derivatives of G therefore yields the Maxwell relation
(∂p∂S​)T​=−(∂T∂V​)p​.​
(84)
In the insulated throttling process, piston work into the gas is p1​V1​ and work out is p2​V2​. Neglecting changes in macroscopic kinetic and gravitational energy, the first law gives U2​−U1​=p1​V1​−p2​V2​. Thus U1​+p1​V1​=U2​+p2​V2​, so enthalpy is conserved. The irreversible process is not assumed isentropic.
Since dH=T,dS+V,dp and dS=(Cp​/T)dT−(∂V/∂T)p​dp, one has dH=Cp​,dT+[V−T(∂V/∂T)p​]dp. Therefore the Joule-Thomson coefficient is
μJT​=(∂p∂T​)H​=Cp​T(∂V/∂T)p​−V​=Cp​V(αT−1)​.​
(85)
For an ideal gas, V∝T at fixed pressure and α=1/T, so μJT​=0. A pressure drop has dp<0, so cooling, dT<0, requires μJT​>0 in the operating range.
