Use simplicial homology with a fixed coefficient group or ring. The Mayer–Vietoris sequence is
⋯⟶Hi+1​(X)δ​Hi​(A∩B)α​Hi​(A)⊕Hi​(B)β​Hi​(X)δ​Hi−1​(A∩B)⟶⋯.
(46)
The inclusion maps define α([z])=([z],−[z]) and β([a],[b])=[a+b]. To define δ, split a simplicial cycle c∈Ci+1​(X) as c=a+b with a a chain in A and b in B. Since ∂a=−∂b, this boundary lies in Ci​(A∩B), and δ[c]=[∂a]. At degree zero the unreduced sequence ends with H0​(A)⊕H0​(B)→H0​(X)→0.
If [z]=δ[c], its representative is a boundary in A and the negative of a boundary in B, so α[z]=0. Thus imδ⊆kerα.
Conversely if α[z]=0, choose a∈Ci+1​(A) and b∈Ci+1​(B) with ∂a=z=∂b. The chain c=a−b is a simplicial cycle in X. Its splitting into the A chain a and B chain −b gives δ[c]=[z]. Hence imδ=kerα, the requested exactness at Hi​(A∩B).
