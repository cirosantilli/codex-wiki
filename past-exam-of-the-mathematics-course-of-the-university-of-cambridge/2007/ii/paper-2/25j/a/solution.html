The first Borel-Cantelli lemma says that ∑n​Pr(An​)<∞ implies Pr(An​ infinitely often)=0, without an independence assumption. Indeed, for every N,
Pr(⋃n≥N​An​)≤∑n≥N​Pr(An​)⟶0.
(53)
The decreasing intersection of these tail unions is the infinitely-often event; continuity of probability from above proves the assertion. The second lemma says that for independent An​, divergence of the probability sum implies Pr(An​ infinitely often)=1.
