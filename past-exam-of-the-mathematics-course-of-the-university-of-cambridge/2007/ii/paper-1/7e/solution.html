Introduce the vector x=(t,y,y′,…,y(k−1))∈Rk+1. Its autonomous ordinary differential equation is
x˙=(1,x2​,x3​,…,xk​,g(x0​,x1​,…,xk​)),

where the coordinate indices start at zero. Appending the clock removes the explicit time dependence.
For a vector field with local existence and uniqueness, its flow ϕt​(x) is the solution at elapsed time t starting at x. The uniqueness theorem for ordinary differential equations gives ϕs​(ϕt​(x))=ϕs+t​(x) whenever both sides are defined. The orbit is O(x)={ϕt​(x):t lies in its maximal interval}. For a forward-complete solution its omega-limit set is
ω(x)=⋂T≥0​{ϕt​(x):t≥T}​,
(10)
equivalently the set of limits along sequences tn​→∞. A homoclinic orbit is a nonconstant complete orbit approaching the same equilibrium as t→−∞ and t→∞. This last definition concerns a general autonomous system; the appended clock itself cannot approach an equilibrium.
