Let D=Q/V0​ be the dilution rate. Population loss is DN, and nutrient loss by consumption is NK(C)/V0​, so mass conservation gives
N˙=γNK(C)−DN,C˙=D(C0​−C)−V0​NK(C)​.
(6)
Use the dimensionless variables τ=Dt, c=C/K0​, and n=NKmax​/(QK0​). Substitution into the Monod equation gives the stated system with
α=QγKmax​V0​​,β=K0​C0​​.​
(7)
For a positive equilibrium, the first equation requires αc/(1+c)=1. Hence c∗​=1/(α−1) and n∗​=α(β−c∗​), both positive exactly under the specified inequalities.
Writing A=αn∗​/(1+c∗​)2>0, the Jacobian matrix at this equilibrium is
J=(0−1/α​A−1−A/α​),specJ={−1,−A/α}.​
(8)
Both eigenvalues are negative, so the state is locally asymptotically stable. The Monod chemostat equilibrium and relaxation also makes one decay rate transparent: z=c+n/α satisfies z′=β−z, independently of the uptake kinetics.
