The initial data require Φ(ξ)=exp[−(2ν)−1∫x∗​ξ​U(s)ds], up to a positive constant that cancels from u. Substituting this into the heat kernel formula gives the exponent −G/(2ν) with
G(x,ξ,t)=2t(x−ξ)2​+∫x∗​ξ​U(s)ds.​
(49)
Differentiate the kernel integral in x and apply the Cole-Hopf transformation. Its constant prefactor cancels, leaving
u(x,t)=∫R​e−G/(2ν)dξ∫R​(x−ξ)t−1e−G/(2ν)dξ​.
(50)
The choice of x∗​ adds an irrelevant constant to G; the usual convergence assumptions on the initial data justify the integrals and differentiation.
