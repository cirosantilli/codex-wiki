The Wilson-Fisher fixed point of the full scalar theory also has a thermal relevant direction at the Wilson-Fisher fixed point; approaching the critical theory requires mass tuning. For negative initial coupling, the quartic potential is unstable: the same formula tends to zero from below in the ultraviolet and reaches a pole at finite scale toward the infrared.
