The solenoidal magnetic-field constraint implies
∇⊥​⋅δB⊥​=−∂z​δB∥​.
(18)
With δB/B0​=O(ϵ) and ∂z​=O(ϵ∇⊥​), the right-hand side is O(ϵ2k⊥​B0​). Thus the leading, order-ϵ perpendicular field is divergence-free. On a simply connected perpendicular patch, or for the nonzero perpendicular Fourier modes of a periodic domain, it has a stream function. Choose its normalization by
δBx​=−vA​B0​​∂y​Ψ,δBy​=vA​B0​​∂x​Ψ.
(19)
Consequently the leading reduced electron magnetohydrodynamics representation is
B0​δB​=vA​1​z^×∇⊥​Ψ+z^B0​δB∥​​+O(ϵ2).​
(20)
The error denotes an order-ϵ2 field relative to B0​. The parallel perturbation is an independent scalar and is not removed by leading perpendicular solenoidality.
For clarity, the representation is asymptotic rather than an exact decomposition of an arbitrary three-dimensional solenoidal field. If δB∥​ depends on z, exact solenoidality can be restored by adding ∇⊥​χ to δB⊥​, where ∇⊥2​χ=−∂z​δB∥​. Its amplitude is O(ϵ2B0​), so it lies beyond the retained field order. A perpendicular harmonic component can be absorbed into the guide field or fixed separately by boundary conditions.
