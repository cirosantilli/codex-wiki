∂t​δB=B0​∂z​∂t​ξi​,δB=B0​∂z​ξi​=ik∥​B0​ξi​.

Project both momentum equations with P=I−kkT/k2. This removes the pressure gradients. The magnetic tension is already transverse to k, and, with a=k∥​vA​, the projected equations are
ξ¨​i​=−a2ξi​−μ(ξ˙​i​−ξ˙​n​),ξ¨​n​=−μ(ξ˙​n​−ξ˙​i​).

Here vA​=B0​/4πρ​ uses the ion density, as stipulated. For either of the two independent transverse polarizations, the equal-density ion-neutral Alfvén dispersion relation follows from
(ω2−a2+iμω−iμω​−iμωω2+iμω​)(ξi​ξn​​)=0.
(4)
Its determinant is ω[ω3+2iμω2−a2ω−iμa2]. Removing the static neutral-displacement factor gives
ω3+2iμω2−a2ω−iμa2=0.​
(5)
The removed factor is a time-independent neutral displacement with no velocity or magnetic perturbation, a relabeling of the homogeneous neutral fluid rather than a fourth physical wave. Direct elimination in the first-order system for (ui​,un​,δB) gives exactly the same cubic. This also handles the zero-frequency limits without introducing a displacement-label mode.
