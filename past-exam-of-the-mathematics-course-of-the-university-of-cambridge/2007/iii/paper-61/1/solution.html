To prove the static Killing field is a Ricci eigenvector, use the permitted alternative of adapted coordinates. Hypersurface orthogonality supplies hypersurfaces orthogonal to k. Choose spatial coordinates on one such hypersurface and carry them along the flow of the Killing vector, using its flow parameter as t. The flow preserves both the metric tensor and k, so it preserves orthogonality to k. Consequently the mixed metric components vanish, and the Killing equation makes all coefficients independent of t:
