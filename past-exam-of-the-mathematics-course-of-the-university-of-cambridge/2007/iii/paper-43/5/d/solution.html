Let A=Aλ​ be the influence matrix, so y​=Ay. Its trace is the effective degrees of freedom, accounting for shrinkage of the fitted smooth rather than just counting basis coefficients. The generalised cross-validation score is
GCV(λ)={1−tr(Aλ​)/n}2∥y−Aλ​y∥2/n​={n−tr(Aλ​)}2n∑i​(yi​−y​i​)2​.​
(34)
For a fixed smoothing parameter, the exact leave-one-out residual of this penalized linear fit is (yi​−y​i​)/(1−Aii​), obtained by removing its observation's contribution to the normal equations. Thus Leave-one-out cross-validation uses the average of their squared values. GCV replaces the individual leverages Aii​ by their average tr(A)/n. This avoids separate refits and gives a criterion balancing the small training residuals of a flexible fit against its larger complexity.
Evaluate the score across positive λ, or optimize it numerically, and choose λ minimizing GCV. Then refit or retain the spline at this value. In the full natural smoothing-spline family the trace decreases from n towards 2 as smoothing increases. The selected parameter is intended to give good prediction, rather than necessarily to pass through every point.
