factor(day) treats the nine days as categorical rather than imposing a numerical time trend. rep(400,27) supplies the number of trials for every dish, and prop is the observed surviving proportion. The contrast setting uses treatment coding with day 1 as reference; the ordered-factor polynomial option is unused. Supplying these proportions to glm with the binomial family and weights 400 gives precisely the grouped-binomial logistic regression likelihood for the counts. The weights represent trial counts, not additional independent dish observations. The binomial family's default link is the logit link and its dispersion is fixed at 1.

The intercept is the first day's logit, and each other coefficient is its day's logit minus that reference. These are additive effects on log odds, not additive changes in survival probability.
The analysis of deviance adds the eight day indicators after an intercept-only model. Its residual deviance compares a fitted model with the saturated statistical model assigning one separate probability to each of the 27 dishes. For fitted dish probabilities p​ij​, the binomial deviance is
