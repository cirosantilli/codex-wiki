Work in the smooth or piecewise-linear category; embedded spheres and disks are locally flat. A closed connected orientable three-manifold is an irreducible three-manifold when every embedded S2 bounds an embedded three-ball. It is a prime three-manifold when M≅P#Q forces P≅S3 or Q≅S3. A separating sphere gives such a connected sum after capping its two sides with balls. Irreducibility therefore implies primeness, but a prime manifold can still have a nonseparating sphere.
A geometrizable three-manifold is one whose prime summands, after sphere splitting and capping, can be cut along disjoint essential tori into pieces whose interiors admit complete geometric structures modeled on the eight Thurston geometries:
S3,R3,H3,S2×R,H2×R,SL2​(R),Nil,Sol.
(12)
Here a geometric structure is a locally homogeneous quotient structure modeled on the stated simply connected space. The original manifold need not itself carry one geometry before decomposition.
A Heegaard splitting is M=H1​∪ϕ​H2​, where H1​,H2​ are handlebodies with their boundaries identified. Its genus is the common boundary genus. The Heegaard genus gH​(M) is the least possible genus of such a splitting. Thus “genus g” as a minimum requires both a genus-g splitting and the nonexistence of one of smaller genus; an arbitrary genus-g splitting only proves gH​(M)≤g.
