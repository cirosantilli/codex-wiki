Write δ=−A2−A−2. Normalize the reduced Kauffman bracket by ⟨◯⟩=1 and ⟨D⊔◯⟩=δ⟨D⟩. At a positive braid crossing choose the smoothing rule Ri​=A1+A−1ei​, where 1 is the two parallel strands and ei​ joins the adjacent top endpoints and adjacent bottom endpoints. At its mirror crossing the coefficients are interchanged. This convention fixes the two smoothing labels. Equivalently, for a nonempty diagram,
⟨D⟩=s∑​Aa(s)−b(s)δc(s)−1,​
(17)
where each bracket smoothing state chooses one smoothing at each crossing, a,b count the choices, and c counts the resulting circles. It is a Laurent polynomial in A and is independent of the order in which crossings are resolved.
We prove the local invariance needed for the Jones polynomial. In the Temperley-Lieb diagram algebra, stacking the smoothing tangles gives ei2​=δei​, ei​ei+1​ei​=ei​ and its reflected version. These identities follow just by following the planar strands; only the first removes an extra circle. The inverse crossing is Ri−1​=A−11+Aei​, and
Ri​Ri−1​=1+(A2+A−2+δ)ei​=1.
(18)
Thus the second Reidemeister move preserves the bracket. Expanding three crossings gives
R1​R2​R1​−R2​R1​R2​=A−1(A2+δ+A−2)(e1​−e2​)=0,
(19)
so the third Reidemeister move does too; inverse versions follow by multiplying by inverse crossings. A positive curl resolves to Aδ+A−1=−A3 times the straight strand, and a negative curl gives A−1δ+A=−A−3. Thus the first move changes the bracket by the indicated factor.
Orient the diagram and let w(D) be its writhe, the sum of the signed crossings. Define
VD​(t)=(−A3)−w(D)⟨D⟩​t=A−4​.​
(20)
The first move changes w by +1 or −1, exactly cancelling the bracket's curl factor. The other two moves leave writhe unchanged. Planar isotopy leaves both quantities unchanged. The Reidemeister theorem states that diagrams of ambient-isotopic tame oriented links are related by planar isotopy and these three local moves; therefore VD​ depends only on the oriented link.
This substitution really lies in Z[t1/2,t−1/2]. If there are N crossings, each state exponent a−b has parity N, and w(D) also has parity N. Expanding each power of δ adds only even exponents, so every exponent of the normalized bracket has parity N−3w(D)=0(mod2). Every even power of A is an integral power of t1/2. The normalization gives Vunknot​=1.
