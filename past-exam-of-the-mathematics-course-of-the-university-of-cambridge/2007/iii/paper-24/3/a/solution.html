For tame or smooth embeddings f0​,f1​:S1↪S3, ambient isotopy means there is a continuous family Ft​:S3→S3, 0≤t≤1, of homeomorphisms, with F0​=id and F1​∘f0​=f1​. Equivalently the map (x,t)↦(Ft​(x),t) is a homeomorphism of S3×[0,1] preserving the time coordinate. In the smooth category one uses diffeomorphisms and a smooth isotopy. If only unparametrized images are compared, the last condition is F1​(f0​(S1))=f1​(S1); for oriented knots it must also preserve the strand orientation.
A link is an embedding of a finite disjoint union of circles. Choose a point of S3 outside it and identify the complement of that point with R3. A generic planar projection gives an immersion with only finitely many transverse double points, no triple points and no singularities of the projected strands. A link diagram is this planar immersed union together with the over/under information at each double point, recording which strand has the larger height. An oriented link diagram also records each component's orientation. The crossing data are indispensable: the planar immersion alone does not specify the link.
