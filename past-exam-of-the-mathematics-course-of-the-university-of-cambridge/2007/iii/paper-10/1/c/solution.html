A strongly continuous unitary representation of the circle group decomposes as H=⨁​m∈Z​Hm​, with Uz​ξ=zmξ on Hm​. Its positive energy unitary representation of the circle condition is that the occurring integers are bounded below. Equivalently Ueit​=eitL with a self-adjoint generator bounded below. An integer character can shift the lowest weight to zero.

For k=0, applying it or its adjoint to a nonzero lowest-energy vector produces a vector below the lowest energy, a contradiction. Thus Tk​=0 for every k=0, while T0​ is scalar.
Fejér sum averaging of the strongly continuous orbit Uz​TUz∗​ converges strongly to T. Its Fourier coefficients have just shown every such average is T0​, so T=T0​ is scalar.
