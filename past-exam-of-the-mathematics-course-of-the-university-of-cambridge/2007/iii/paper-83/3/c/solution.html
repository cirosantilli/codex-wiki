The turbulent Schmidt number compares turbulent momentum and concentration diffusivities:
ScT​=κT​νT​​=2.​
(39)
The mean fluid has no vertical velocity. Taking z positive upward and Ws​>0, the settling flux is −Ws​ϕ, while the given turbulent particle flux is −κT​ϕz​. Local particle-volume conservation therefore gives
ϕt​+∂z​J=0,J=−Ws​ϕ−κT​ϕz​.
(40)
Consequently the evolution equation is
ϕt​=∂z​(Ws​ϕ+κT​ϕz​).​
(41)
For constant settling speed this is ϕt​=Ws​ϕz​+∂z​(κT​ϕz​). In the logarithmic region, κT​=Cz/2 from the preceding part. The sign is consistent with downward settling: without diffusion, a profile evolves as ϕ(z,t)=ϕ(z+Ws​t,0). The dilute suspension approximation neglects feedback on the mean flow and particle interactions; deposition or erosion must be imposed through the bed boundary flux.
