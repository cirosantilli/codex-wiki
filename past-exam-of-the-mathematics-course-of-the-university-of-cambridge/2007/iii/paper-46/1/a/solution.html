Treating the two-year observation period as approximately complete, divide total person-time at risk by two to estimate the average number of children being observed. Dividing again by the number of families gives
2(1700+1700)3595+3888​≃1.10.
(1)
Thus the estimate is about 1.1 eligible children per family. This is a person-time-based approximation for children contributing to the study, not a count of older children or an exact reconstruction of family composition if follow-up or eligibility changed.
