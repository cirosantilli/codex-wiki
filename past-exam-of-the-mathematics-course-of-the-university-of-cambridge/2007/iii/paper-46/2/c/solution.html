Define the difference as control minus intervention, positive for fewer deaths under intervention assignment. The observed difference per thousand is
Δ=1000(30000148​−30000123​)=3025​≃0.833.
(9)
For independent binomial distributions, its unpooled standard error per thousand is
SE=100030000p​C​(1−p​C​)​+30000p​I​(1−p​I​)​​≃0.547.
(10)
The approximate 95% confidence interval is therefore
0.833±1.96(0.547)≃(−0.24, 1.91) fewer deaths per thousand.​
(11)
The rare-event Poisson standard error 148+123​/30 gives almost the same interval. A reduction of 1.5 per thousand is consistent with the data, since it lies inside the interval. Zero also lies inside, so a nonzero benefit is not established at the two-sided 5% significance level. This does not establish equivalence or prove the proposed effect size, and cannot by itself establish treatment contamination.
