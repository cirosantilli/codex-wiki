Use the spatial-potential convention of this paper and assume ρˉ​+Pˉ=0. A passive time displacement changes the spatial scalar by Ψ−Ψ=NˉHξ0 and changes the energy density by
δρ​−δρ=−ρˉ​˙​ξ0=3NˉH(ρˉ​+Pˉ)ξ0.
(9)
The two contributions to the uniform-density curvature perturbation therefore cancel:
ζ​−ζ=−NˉHξ0+3(ρˉ​+Pˉ)3NˉH(ρˉ​+Pˉ)ξ0​=0.
(10)
A spatial scalar displacement changes E but not this combination. Hence ζ is gauge invariant at linear order. Pure cosmological-constant matter, for which ρˉ​+Pˉ=0, does not supply a energy density clock and this formula is not defined for that component alone.
Now take the definite constant-w barotropic equation of state and use physical-time differentiation D=Nˉ−1∂t​. Put A=ρˉ​+Pˉ. Background stress-energy conservation gives DA=−3H(1+w)A. Differentiating ζ=−Ψ+δρ/(3A), then substituting the two supplied perturbation equations, gives
Dζ​=−DΨ+3ADδρ​+H(1+w)Aδρ​=HΦ−3κ​−3Δχ​−HAδρ+δP​+3κ​−HΦ−3AΔu​+H(1+w)Aδρ​=−AH​(δP−wδρ)−31​Δ(χ+Au​).​
(11)
Thus the lapse and expansion perturbations cancel without imposing a gauge. In terms of the non-adiabatic pressure perturbation δPnad​=δP−wδρ,
Nˉζ˙​​=−ρˉ​+PˉHδPnad​​−31​Δ(χ+ρˉ​+Pˉu​).​
(12)
For the stipulated barotropic equation of state, δP=wδρ, so the first term vanishes. For a Fourier mode, Δ=−k2/a2. For regular long-wavelength solutions the remaining gradients are suppressed when k≪aH. Consequently ζ˙​≃0 on superhorizon scales, with exact conservation in the leading zero-gradient limit. It is not an exact finite-k identity. Entropy perturbations or a singular gradient expansion can invalidate that conclusion; the supplied adiabatic assumption is what removes the unsuppressed pressure source.
This superhorizon conservation of uniform-density curvature is useful because inflationary fluctuations leave the horizon during cosmic inflation and may re-enter only much later. In an adiabatic single-clock evolution, the conserved uniform-density curvature perturbation transports their initial amplitude through reheating and the later radiation in cosmology and matter eras without solving every short-distance process. It then determines the initial conditions for large-scale structure and Cosmic microwave background anisotropy. Extra fluctuating fields or fluids can introduce isocurvature perturbations and convert them into curvature, so conservation must be checked rather than assumed during such transitions.
