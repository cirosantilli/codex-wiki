Thus fixing the lapse function and shift vector perturbations does not exhaust the scalar coordinate freedom. The functions C,D describe residual synchronous-gauge freedom; an integration-origin change in the integral is absorbed into D.
To move from synchronous gauge to Newtonian gauge, choose λ=−ES​ to set E=0. Requiring B=0 then gives
