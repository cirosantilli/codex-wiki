For a regular, spherical star, hydrostatic equilibrium gives dP/dr=−Gm(r)ρ(r)/r2. Multiply by 4πr3 and integrate to the surface. Integration by parts gives
∫0R​4πr3drdP​dr=4πR3P(R)−3∫0R​4πr2Pdr.
(26)
The central boundary term vanishes for finite central pressure. Since dm=4πr2ρdr, the right-hand side of the integrated hydrostatic equation is
−∫0R​4πGmρrdr=−∫0M​r(m)Gm​dm=Ω.
(27)
The last expression is the gravitational energy: adding a thin mass shell dm to the interior mass m contributes −Gmdm/r, counting each interacting pair once. Hence
3∫0M​ρP​dm+Ω=4πR3P(R).
(28)
With the specified zero surface pressure this proves the stellar virial theorem in the required form,
3∫0M​ρP​dm+Ω=0.​
(29)
