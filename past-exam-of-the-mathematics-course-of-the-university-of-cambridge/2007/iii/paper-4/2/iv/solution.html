Label the weight vertices by 1,…,n,0,nˉ,…,1ˉ, with weights e1​,…,en​,0,−en​,…,−e1​. The crystal of the defining odd-orthogonal representation is the chain
11​22​⋯n−1​nn​0n​nˉn−1​n−1​⟶⋯1​1ˉ.
(19)
Each arrow of color i subtracts the simple root αi​. The two arrows through zero form the length-two short-root string.
Here is a complete grid description of the tensor product of crystals, valid for arbitrary n. Place the vertex a⊗b in row a, column b, in the above chain order; there are (2n+1)2 vertices. Let εi​(a) and φi​(a) count incoming and outgoing steps along the color-i string. For i<n, the two strings are i→i+1 and i+1​→iˉ, each of length one. For i=n, the string is n→0→nˉ, with pairs (εn​,φn​) equal to (0,2),(1,1),(2,0). Other vertices have both counts zero.
Using the crystal tensor-product rule, draw every color-i edge by
f​i​(a⊗b)={f​i​(a)⊗b,a⊗f​i​(b),​φi​(a)>εi​(b),φi​(a)≤εi​(b).​​
(20)
The first case is a downward arrow to the next row and the second a rightward arrow to the next column; omit an arrow when its indicated factor has no outgoing edge. Thus the rule specifies every vertex and every arrow of the general tensor-square diagram, including the boundary nodes. The figure displays the resulting entire grid for n=3, alongside the vector crystal; the generator can draw any positive rank.
The raising rule chooses the first factor when φi​(a)≥εi​(b) and the second otherwise. For n≥2, its only highest vertices are
1⊗1: 2e1​,1⊗2: e1​+e2​,1⊗1ˉ: 0.​
(21)
Indeed a highest tensor must have highest first factor, hence a=1. For colors i>1, the second factor cannot have an incoming edge. For color one, its incoming count must be at most φ1​(1)=1. Inspecting the strings leaves precisely b=1,2,1ˉ.
These three connected components encode the Irreducible Lie algebra representations
V⊗V≅S02​V⊕Λ2V⊕C,highest weights 2e1​, e1​+e2​, 0.​
(22)
Here S02​V is the traceless symmetric square, Λ2V is the Adjoint representation, and the invariant symmetric form supplies the trivial summand. Their dimensions are n(2n+3), n(2n+1), and one, summing to (2n+1)2. In fundamental-weight notation the highest weights are 2ω1​,ω2​,0 for n≥3, but 2ω1​,2ω2​,0 for n=2.
For n=1 the vector crystal is 11​01​1ˉ. The same tensor rule gives highest vertices 1⊗1,1⊗0,1⊗1ˉ, of weights 2e1​,e1​,0, or 4ω1​,2ω1​,0, respectively. Its summand dimensions are 5,3,1.
