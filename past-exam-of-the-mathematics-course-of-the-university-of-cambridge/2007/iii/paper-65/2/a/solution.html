Let the equilibrium ideal gas be at rest, with uniform ρ0​,p0​ and B0​=B0​ez​. This local wave calculation neglects gravity, viscosity and magnetic diffusion. Define the adiabatic sound speed c≡vs​=(γp0​/ρ0​)1/2, the Alfvén speed va​=B0​/(μ0​ρ0​)1/2, and a wavevector k=k(sinθ,0,cosθ), where k=∣k∣ and θ is its angle to the equilibrium magnetic field. Perturbations have dependence eik⋅x−iωt and phase speed v=ω/k.
The linearized ideal magnetohydrodynamic equations for mass conservation, pressure, induction and momentum give

The relation between pressure and mass density follows from eliminating k⋅u′ between the two material evolution equations; it applies to the nonzero-frequency wave sector. The induction result automatically has k⋅b=0.
The y MHD wave polarization is perpendicular to both the field and wavevector. It has δp=δρ=0 and obeys

Taking its determinant gives v4−(c2+va2​)v2+c2va2​cos2θ=0. Solving this quadratic equation in v2 derives both magnetoacoustic wave phase speeds,
vf,slow2​=2c2+va2​​±4(c2+va2​)2​−c2va2​cos2θ​​.

The plus sign is the fast magnetosonic wave, the minus sign the slow magnetosonic wave. The radicand is nonnegative, since it equals [(c2−va2​)2+4c2va2​sin2θ]/4. Both squared speeds are nonnegative. Each nonzero mode has forward and backward propagation; an advected entropy disturbance is a separate zero-frequency mode in this static equilibrium.
