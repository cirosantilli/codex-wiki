All invariants in this part refer to the selected magnetic field line. Its squared poloidal Alfvén number is
MAp2​=Bp2​μ0​ρup2​​=ρμ0​k2​.
(43)
At the Alfvén surface crossing it equals unity, so ρa​=μ0​k2 and MAp2​=1/y. Subtract the two azimuthal integrals in part (a) to find
Rω−Rℓ​=μ0​kBϕ​​(1−ρμ0​k2​),vϕ​≡uϕ​−Rω=y−1Rω−ℓ/R​.
(44)
Finite azimuthal fields and velocity at y=1 require the Alfvén-surface regularity condition for an axisymmetric wind ℓ=ωRa2​. Hence
vϕ​=ωRa​y−1x−x−1​,∣up​∣=ρa​Ra2​x2y∣k∣C​=μ0​ρa​​Ra2​x2yC​.
(45)
Here vϕ​ denotes the azimuthal velocity relative to field-line rotation. For gravity of a point mass at z=0, Φ=−GM/R=−GM/(Ra​x).
Insert these quantities in the corotating energy integral with w=0, and divide by the gravitational energy scale GM/Ra​. This gives the cold radial magnetohydrodynamic wind integral,
f(x,y)=2α​[(y−1x−x−1​)2−x2]+2x4y2β​−x1​=GMεRa​​​,
(46)
with
α=GMω2Ra3​​,β=μ0​ρa​GMRa3​C2​​.
(47)
Both are dimensionless. The apparent 0/0 at x=y=1 must be evaluated on the smooth solution branch, not assigned an arbitrary value.
A constant-energy curve obeys fx​+fy​dy/dx=0. At a nondegenerate magnetosonic point, the vanishing coefficient of the mass density derivative requires simultaneous vanishing of its numerator:
fy​=0,fx​=0,f=fwind​​.
(48)
The curve must admit a real crossing slope. Expanding about such a point gives fyy​m2+2fxy​m+fxx​=0, m=dy/dx, so a regular saddle point of a scalar function crossing has fxy2​−fxx​fyy​>0. These are the form of the slow and fast regularity conditions in a warm wind, with the enthalpy contribution retained in f.
In the strictly cold approximation used to obtain the displayed f, the slow speed is zero. There is a cold-limit degeneracy of a slow magnetosonic point: a finite-speed interior slow crossing need not survive. Indeed,
fy​=−(y−1)3α(x−x−1)2​−x4y3β​<0(y>1, β>0),
(49)
so this cold equation has no ordinary sub-Alfvénic slow critical point. A small retained enthalpy is needed to locate that point, which can approach the launching boundary as the sound speed tends to zero. The fast critical point still obeys the simultaneous conditions above wherever the reduced expression is nonsingular.
