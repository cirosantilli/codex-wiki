For 0<q<1, every direction is contracted by the same positive factor. The pure-state Bloch sphere becomes a concentric sphere of radius 1−q, and the Bloch ball contracts towards the maximally mixed density operator I/2. The lack of a preferred direction explains the name depolarizing channel.
At q=1 all inputs become I/2. For the allowed Pauli-error range 1<q<4/3, the factor is negative, so there is also an antipodal reversal; the radius is q−1≤1/3. Thus it would be incorrect to describe the whole printed p∈(0,1) range as positive radial contraction.
