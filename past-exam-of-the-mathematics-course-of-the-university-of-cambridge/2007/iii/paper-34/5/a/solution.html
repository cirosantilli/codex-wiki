The generalized measurement postulate specifies Kraus operators Miα​, where i is the recorded outcome and α permits unobserved alternatives for that outcome, satisfying
i,α∑​Miα†​Miα​=I.​
(54)
For a density operator ρ, outcome i has probability and conditional state
pi​=Tr(ρα∑​Miα†​Miα​),ρi​=pi​∑α​Miα​ρMiα†​​(pi​>0).​
(55)
With one operator per outcome, this is the usual formula pi​=Tr(Mi​ρMi†​), ρi​=Mi​ρMi†​/pi​. The positive operators Ei​=∑α​Miα†​Miα​ form a POVM, while the state-update maps form a quantum instrument.
The full postulate reduces to the usual projective measurement with the Lüders update when there is one operator per outcome and Mi​=Pi​, with Pi​=Pi†​=Pi2​, Pi​Pj​=0 for i=j, and ∑i​Pi​=I. Then pi​=Tr(Pi​ρ) and ρi​=Pi​ρPi​/pi​. Projective effects Ei​=Pi​ alone ensure these probabilities but do not fix the state update: an additional outcome-dependent unitary could still be applied. The choice Mi​=Pi​ makes the required projective state-update condition explicit.
