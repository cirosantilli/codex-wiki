Omit indices of probability zero. The support of a positive operator of every remaining ρi​ lies within that of ρ​, because ρ​ is a positive sum containing pi​ρi​. Thus D(ρi​∥ρ​) is finite.
First suppose suppρ​⊆suppσ, so all quantities are finite. Expand the trace definitions of quantum relative entropy:
i∑​pi​D(ρi​∥σ)−i∑​pi​D(ρi​∥ρ​)​=i∑​pi​Trρi​(log2​ρ​−log2​σ)=Trρ​(log2​ρ​−log2​σ)=D(ρ​∥σ).​
(10)
Therefore Donald's identity is
i∑​pi​D(ρi​∥σ)=i∑​pi​D(ρi​∥ρ​)+D(ρ​∥σ).​
(11)
If the support condition on σ fails, at least one positive-weight ρi​ also fails it. The left side and the final term on the right are then +∞, while the first right-hand sum remains finite. The equality is valid in this extended sense, without taking any undefined difference of infinities.
