For a block with nonzero typical dimension, write P=Pε(n)​ and Q=I−P. Let the compressed register be a copy of the typical space plus a one-dimensional orthogonal failure flag. Choose an isometry V:PH⊗n→Kn​ onto the successful code sector, extended by zero on Q, and a normalized flag ∣f⟩ orthogonal to its range. Choose any normalized vector ∣φ⟩ in the source Hilbert space. Define
Cn​(X)=VPXPV†+Tr(QX)∣f⟩⟨f∣,

Dn​(Y)=V†YV+⟨f∣Y∣f⟩∣φ⟩⟨φ∣.
(22)
These are CPTP maps: the first performs the projective test and either encodes or prepares a flag; the second embeds the successful sector and prepares a fixed state on failure. Their trace identities follow from V†V=P and VV†+∣f⟩⟨f∣=IKn​​. Thus typical-subspace compression with a failure flag implements
Λn​(X)=PXP+Tr(QX)∣φ⟩⟨φ∣.
