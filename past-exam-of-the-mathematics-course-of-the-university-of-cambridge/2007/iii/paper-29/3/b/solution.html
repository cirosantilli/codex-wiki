Equivalently, one can check just S,T in the next cancellation, since they generate the group. Substituting these formulas into

Dk​:Mk​⟶Mk+2​.​
(23)
This operator is the Serre derivative.
If f=∑n≥0​an​qn, then its derivative has constant coefficient zero, while E2​ has constant coefficient one. Therefore
a0​(Dk​f)=−12k​a0​(f).
(24)
For k=0 this vanishes exactly when a0​(f)=0, proving the claimed cusp form equivalence in the intended positive-weight setting. At weight zero the printed equivalence is false: f=1 is a noncuspidal weight-zero modular form, but D0​f=0 belongs to S2​. Indeed all weight-zero forms are constants by compactness of X(1). Negative-weight level-one modular forms are zero by the valence formula for the modular group, so there is no further nonzero exception. The transformation and holomorphy conclusion for Dk​f remains true at weight zero.
