Reduction modulo N has kernel Γ(N) and is onto SL2​(Z/NZ). To see surjectivity, use the Chinese remainder theorem to reduce to Z/paZ. In that local ring a unimodular column has a unit entry. Elementary row operations move a unit to the upper position and clear the lower one; diagonal matrices diag(u,u−1) are products of elementary matrices as well. Explicitly, with E12​,E21​ denoting elementary shears,
(0−u−1​u0​)=E12​(u)E21​(−u−1)E12​(u),
(51)
and multiplying this by the same matrix with u=−1 gives diag(u,u−1). Thus elementary matrices generate the special linear group over each local ring. Chinese remaindering their parameters gives generation over the product ring, and every such parameter lifts to an integer. This proves the reduction is onto.
Over Fp​, there are p2−1 choices for a nonzero first column and p choices for a second column with determinant one, so ∣SL2​(Fp​)∣=p(p2−1). Each reduction from modulus pa+1 to pa has kernel represented by I+paB, with B modulo p and trB=0, of size p3. Hence, for N>1,
[SL2​(Z):Γ(N)]=∣SL2​(Z/NZ)∣=N3∏p∣N​(1−p−2).
(52)
For N=1 the index is one. Since −I∈Γ(N) exactly when N=1,2,
μ=⎩⎨⎧​162N3​p∣N∏​(1−p−2)​N=1,N=2,N≥3.​
(53)
For N≥2 there are no nontrivial effective elliptic stabilizers. Indeed A=I+NB∈Γ(N) with determinant one has trB=−NdetB, and therefore
trA=2−N2detB≡2(modN2).
(54)
A noncentral elliptic element of SL2​(Z) has trace 0 or ±1, none congruent to 2 modulo N2 for N≥2. The central element −I at level two acts trivially and is not an elliptic point of the effective quotient. Thus ν2​=ν3​=0 for N≥2, while at level one both numbers are one.
The subgroup Γ(N) is normal, so all its modular cusps have the same effective width as infinity. That width is N: the shear Th belongs to ±Γ(N) exactly when N∣h; for N≥3 its diagonal entries exclude the minus sign, and for N=1,2 that sign changes nothing. Since modular cusp widths sum to μ,
ν∞​=Nμ​=⎩⎨⎧​132N2​p∣N∏​(1−p−2)​N=1,N=2,N≥3.​
(55)
For N≥3 the resulting principal congruence modular curve genus is
g(X(N))=1+24N2(N−6)​p∣N∏​(1−p−2).​
(56)
For clarity, the small levels, including all exceptional terms, are
N123456​[SL2​:Γ(N)]162448120144​μ1612246072​ν2​100000​ν3​100000​ν∞​13461212​g000001​​
(57)
For N>6, every factor in the genus correction is positive, so g>1. Therefore the genus-zero levels are exactly N=1,2,3,4,5.
