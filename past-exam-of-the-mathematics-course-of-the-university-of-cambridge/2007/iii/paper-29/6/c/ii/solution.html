The transformation condition is necessary by the definition of a cusp form. For sufficiency, fix σ∈SL2​(Z) representing any modular cusp, and set g=f∣k​σ. By item (i), g is holomorphic in the upper half-plane. Choose an actual period h with σThσ−1=γ∈Γ. Associativity of slash operations and the given transformation law give
g∣k​Th=f∣k​σTh=f∣k​γσ=(f∣k​γ)∣k​σ=g.
(62)
Thus g(τ+h)=g(τ) and g is a single-valued holomorphic function on a punctured disk with coordinate qh​=e2πiτ/h. The calculation in part (b) transports the original uniform bound to this translate:
∣g(τ)∣yk/2=ϕ(στ)≤B.
(63)
This puts every modular cusp in exactly the situation needed in item (iii). No assumption that a single modular cusp represents all of Γ's modular cusps is made.
