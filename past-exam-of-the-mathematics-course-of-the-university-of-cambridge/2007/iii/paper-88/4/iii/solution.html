Label its vertices of a hypergraph 1,…,v and give vertex of a hypergraph i a nonempty host part Vi​. For each hypergraph edge e={i,j,k}, let he​ be the corresponding host hypergraph edge indicator function of subset density pe​, with ∥he​−pe​∥□3​≤α1/8. The normalized labelled hypergraph copy count is
tF​=Ex1​∈V1​,…,xv​∈Vv​​∏e∈E(F)​he​(xe​).
(48)
If the host parts are disjoint, every such vertex of a hypergraph choice is automatically injective. Enumerate the m hypergraph edges as e1​,…,em​ and telescope
∏j=1m​hej​​−∏j=1m​pej​​=∑j=1m​(∏i<j​pei​​)(hej​​−pej​​)(∏i>j​hei​​).

In the term for ej​={a,b,c}, fix all variables outside this triple. Since F is simple and three-uniform, every other hypergraph edge intersects {a,b,c} in at most two vertices of a hypergraph. Its remaining factor therefore depends on at most two of xa​,xb​,xc​. Group all two-variable factors according to the pairs (a,b),(a,c),(b,c). Absorb each one-variable factor into any pair containing its variable, and constants into any group. The three resulting pair functions remain bounded by one.
The balanced factor is now multiplied by exactly the sort of pair functions handled in part (ii). Its average has absolute value at most α1/8, independently of the fixed outside variables. Averaging those variables and summing the m errors gives
∣tF​−e∈E(F)∏​pe​∣≤mα1/8.​
(50)
Equivalently,
​#F−(e∏​pe​)(i=1∏v​∣Vi​∣)​≤∣E(F)∣α1/8i=1∏v​∣Vi​∣.​
(51)
This counts labelled copies preserving the specified vertex of a hypergraph classes, for arbitrary intersection patterns among the hypergraph edges, and includes the three-uniform tetrahedron as a special case.
For a common host vertex of a hypergraph set of size n, the same product average counts homomorphisms. At most (2v​)nv−1 assignments identify a pair of distinct labelled vertices of a hypergraph, by the union bound. Thus restricting to injective copies changes the estimate by at most this amount. With a common normalized subset density p the injective count is therefore pmnv+O(mα1/8nv+v2nv−1). For induced copies, include the nonhypergraph edge indicator functions as additional factors; their balanced functions are negatives of the corresponding edge-balanced functions and have the same box norm. The same proof applies provided all the relevant triple parts satisfy the stated quasirandomness condition.
