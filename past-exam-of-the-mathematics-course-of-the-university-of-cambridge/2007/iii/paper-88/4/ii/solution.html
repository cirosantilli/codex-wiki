First prove the pair-factor correlation bound for the three-dimensional box norm. For real g and three functions a(y,z),b(x,z),c(x,y) bounded in absolute value by one, let
T=Ex,y,z​g(x,y,z)a(y,z)b(x,z)c(x,y).
(40)
Apply the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality in (y,z), placing the x average inside and removing a. This gives
∣T∣2≤Ey,z​∣Ex​g(x,y,z)b(x,z)c(x,y)∣2.
(41)
Expand this square, with independent x0​,x1​. Apply Cauchy-Schwarz in (x0​,x1​,z), removing the product b(x0​,z)b(x1​,z). The result is
∣T∣4≤Ex0​,x1​,z​∣Ey​g(x0​,y,z)g(x1​,y,z)c(x0​,y)c(x1​,y)∣2.
(42)
Expand with independent y0​,y1​ and apply Cauchy-Schwarz in (x0​,x1​,y0​,y1​), removing the four c factors. Then
∣T∣8≤Ex0​,x1​,y0​,y1​​​Ez​∏i,j∈{0,1}​g(xi​,yj​,z)​2=∥g∥□38​.
(43)
Therefore ∣T∣≤∥g∥□3​, with no independence assumption on the three pair functions.
In this multipartite hypergraph, a simplex is a three-uniform tetrahedron: one vertex of a hypergraph from each of X,Y,Z,W, with all four possible triples present. Write the corresponding hypergraph edge indicator functions as h1​(x,y,z),h2​(x,y,w),h3​(x,z,w),h4​(y,z,w), of subset densities p,q,r,s. Its normalized count is
t=Ex,y,z,w​h1​h2​h3​h4​.
(44)
Telescope the product exactly:
h1​h2​h3​h4​−pqrs=(h1​−p)h2​h3​h4​+p(h2​−q)h3​h4​+pq(h3​−r)h4​+pqr(h4​−s).
(45)
For the first term fix w. The remaining three factors are bounded pair functions of (x,y), (x,z) and (y,z), so the inequality just proved bounds its (x,y,z) average by ∥h1​−p∥□3​≤α1/8. Averaging over w preserves that bound. For each other term fix the vertex of a hypergraph outside its balanced triple and apply the same argument, allowing an unused pair factor to be identically one. The subset density prefactors are at most one. Hence
∣t−pqrs∣≤4α1/8.​
(46)
Multiplying by ∣X∣∣Y∣∣Z∣∣W∣ gives
∣#simplices−pqrs∣X∣∣Y∣∣Z∣∣W∣∣≤4α1/8∣X∣∣Y∣∣Z∣∣W∣.​
(47)
Thus one may take the absolute constant C=4. This proves the complete-pair-support case of the tetrahedron counting lemma directly; no lower bound on p,q,r,s is needed.
