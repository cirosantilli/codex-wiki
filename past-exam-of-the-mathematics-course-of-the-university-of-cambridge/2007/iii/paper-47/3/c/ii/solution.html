Each of the three displayed correlation eigenvalues exceeds the average eigenvalue one, but that alone does not show all remaining eigenvalues are below one. Their sum is 9−6.78=2.22, and a fourth eigenvalue just above one is compatible with both that sum and the ordering. A complete eigenvalue sequence, a plot of successive eigenvalues, and the stability and interpretation of the directions would help decide the cutoff. With only 25 countries, estimated directions also have sampling uncertainty. The retained covariance and correlation dimensions should therefore be judged against their own variance criterion and substantive aim, not chosen solely because three columns were printed.
