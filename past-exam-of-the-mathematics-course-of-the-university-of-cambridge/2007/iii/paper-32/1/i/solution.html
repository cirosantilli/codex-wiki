In the truncated tensor algebra, let gN(V) be the linear span of all iterated Lie brackets of elements of V of length at most N, with bracket [a,b]=a⊗b−b⊗a. Brackets of length greater than N vanish. The free step-N nilpotent Lie group is
GN(V)=expgN(V)⊆1+⨁k=1N​V⊗k.
(2)
The exponential and logarithm are the finite series
exp(ℓ)=∑j=0N​j!ℓ⊗j​,log(1+a)=∑j=1N​j(−1)j+1a⊗j​.
(3)
Its multiplication is the tensor multiplication above, its identity is e=(1,0,…,0), and its inverse is
(1+a)−1=∑j=0N​(−a)⊗j,(expℓ)−1=exp(−ℓ).

The truncated Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula shows that this multiplication stays in the exponential of the Lie algebra. This is the connected simply connected Lie group of the free step-N nilpotent Lie algebra, rather than a discrete free nilpotent group.

The first-layer Lie brackets span the full Lie algebra, so horizontal curves reach every endpoint. Thus this infimum is finite; it is a left-invariant distance inducing the usual manifold topology. Dilations δλ​(a)(k)=λka(k) give dCC​(δλ​g,δλ​h)=λdCC​(g,h) for λ>0. Equivalently dCC​(e,g) is the least length of a bounded variation control whose step-N path signature is g.
For p≥1 set N=⌊p⌋. A weak geometric p-rough path is a continuous path X:[0,T]→GN(V), usually based at e, with
Dsup​[u,v]∈D∑​dCC​(Xu​,Xv​)p<∞.​
(7)
Its increments Xs,t​=Xs−1​⊗Xt​ obey Xs,u​=Xs,t​⊗Xt,u​. Equivalently their degree-k tensor components have finite p/k-variation and satisfy the path signature algebraic constraints. This is a group-valued definition; being a limit of smooth path signatures in the same p-variation topology is the stronger definition of a geometric p-rough path.
