Use positive carrier wavenumber k and retain waves traveling mainly in the positive x direction. Factor out their rapid wave phase by writing ψ=eikxE(x,z). Substitution into the Helmholtz equation is exact and gives
Exx​+2ikEx​+Ezz​=0.
(13)
For a transverse Fourier mode eiνz at a small angle, ∣ν∣/k≪1 and its forward longitudinal wavenumber is
kx​=k2−ν2​=k−2kν2​+O(ν4/k3).
(14)
Thus the envelope varies along x on scale k/ν2, much more slowly than the carrier wavelength, and ∣Exx​∣/∣kEx​∣=O(ν2/k2). Dropping this smaller term derives the parabolic wave equation,
2ikEx​+Ezz​=0,Ex​=2ki​Ezz​​.
(15)
With transform E(x,ν)=∫E(x,z)e−iνzdz, its free propagation is
E(x,ν)=E(0,ν)e−iν2x/(2k),E(x,z)=2π1​∫E(0,ν)eiνz−iν2x/(2k)dν.
(16)
This is a one-way paraxial approximation, not a replacement for backward waves or large-angle Helmholtz components. Its Fourier multiplier has unit modulus and therefore does not attenuate any admitted component.
