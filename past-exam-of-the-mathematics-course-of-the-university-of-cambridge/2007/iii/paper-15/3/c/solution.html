Use the covariant exterior derivative on the endomorphism bundle and remember that F has degree two. The Bianchi identity is dA​F=dF+A∧F−F∧A=0. The local formula from part (b) gives
dFA∧F−F∧A​=d(dA+A∧A)=dA∧A−A∧dA,=A∧dA+A∧A∧A−dA∧A−A∧A∧A=A∧dA−dA∧A.​
(25)
The two lines cancel, establishing
dA​F=0.​
(26)
Because this is an endomorphism-valued equation and the operators transform covariantly under a change of local frame, this local calculation proves the global identity.
For a line bundle over k=R or C, every fibre endomorphism is multiplication by a scalar. Thus EndE is canonically the trivial scalar bundle, even when E itself is nontrivial. In a local frame A is a scalar one-form, so A∧A=0 and F=dA locally. Also A∧F=F∧A, since their degrees are one and two. The Bianchi identity therefore reduces to the ordinary closedness condition
dF=0.​
(27)
This does not assert that the local scalar one-forms A combine into a global one-form.
For two connections on a vector bundle ∇,∇′, the difference is C∞(M)-linear in the section: the two df⊗s terms cancel. It is consequently a global endomorphism-valued one-form a. In rank one, a is a global ordinary scalar one-form. Locally A′=A+a, and the scalar cross terms and a∧a vanish, giving
FA′​−FA​=d(A+a)−dA=da.
(28)
This is a global equality, so the two closed differential forms differ by an exact differential form. It proves the connection-independent curvature class of a line bundle:
[FA′​]=[FA​]∈HdR2​(M;k).​
(29)
