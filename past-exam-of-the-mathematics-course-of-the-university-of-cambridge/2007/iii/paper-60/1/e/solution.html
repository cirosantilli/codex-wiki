Write the unitary conjugation as ρ(t)=U(t)ρ(0)U(t)†. The identity component is unchanged, and the generalized Bloch vector transforms by

These coefficients are real, by the same trace argument as in part (a). Unitary conjugation preserves the Hilbert-Schmidt inner product, so the conjugated traceless generators form another orthonormal basis. It follows that O(U)TO(U)=I. The unitary group is connected, O(I)=I, and the determinant of an orthogonal matrix is ±1; hence detO(U)=1. Thus Hamiltonian evolution rotates the generalized Bloch vector by an element of SO(N2−1).
Equivalently, in units ℏ=1, the Von Neumann equation gives

Cyclicity of the trace shows Akℓ​=−Aℓk​, so A is a real skew-symmetric matrix. This is the generator form of Hamiltonian rotations of Bloch vectors, valid also for a time-dependent Quantum Hamiltonian. For N>2, Hamiltonians generally realize only a proper subgroup of all rotations of this real space; they preserve the whole density-operator spectrum, not merely its purity of a density operator.
