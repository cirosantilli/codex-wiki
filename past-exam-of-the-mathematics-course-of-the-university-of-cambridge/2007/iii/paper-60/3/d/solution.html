OurBigBook.com Words: 88
Write X=σx​, Y=σy​, Z=σz​. The specified diagonal two-qubit gate has the exact factorization
Cphase​=−Z⊗Z=Rz​(π)⊗Rz​(π).
(30)
Thus one exact implementation is to set the controllable Ising coupling to zero and perform the two local rotations about the z-axis, synthesized from the available x,y pulses as in part (c). Alternatively, turn off the local fields and choose the interaction area ∫J12​(t)dt=π/2. Then
UI​=e−iπZ⊗Z/2=−iZ⊗Z=iCphase​,
(31)
which implements the same physical gate up to global phase. The printed gate is local, not entangling; the pulse construction does not change that fact.
