We prove the Birkhoff–Grothendieck theorem by induction on the rank of the vector bundle E. Rank zero is immediate; rank one is the classification Pic(Pk1​)≅Z established above.
First there is a largest integer a with H0(E(−a))=0. To see existence and boundedness directly, trivialize E on the two standard affine charts: the associated finite projective modules over the principal ideal domains k[t] and k[t−1] are free. Let A(t)∈GLr​(k[t,t−1]) be its transition matrix, so a section of E(−a) is a pair satisfying
p(t)=t−aA(t)q(t−1),p∈k[t]r,q∈k[t−1]r.
(16)
If a exceeds the largest exponent in any entry of A, the right side has only strictly negative powers; the left side has nonnegative powers. Hence both vanish. For a sufficiently negative, take a nonzero constant vector p and put q=taA(t)−1p; all its exponents are nonpositive, so it supplies a nonzero section. Thus a largest a exists.
Such a section gives a nonzero map O(a)→E. It is injective, since it is nonzero generically and the source is torsion-free. Saturate its image. The resulting line subbundle is O(a+d) for the degree d≥0 of the effective divisor of zeros of the original map. If d>0, its inclusion in E would give a nonzero section of E(−a−d), contradicting maximality. Therefore d=0 and
0⟶O(a)⟶E⟶Q⟶0
(17)
has a locally free quotient. By induction, Q≅⨁j​O(bj​).
Twist this exact sequence by O(−a−1). The outer groups H0(O(−1)) and H1(O(−1)) are zero by the Čech cohomology of twists on the projective line. The long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology therefore gives
H0(E(−a−1))≅H0(Q(−a−1)).
(18)
The first is zero by the choice of a, so each H0(O(bj​−a−1)) vanishes. Thus bj​≤a for every j. The extension class lies in
Ext1(Q,O(a))≅⨁j​H1(O(a−bj​))=0,
(19)
since a−bj​≥0. The identification of this Ext functor with cohomology can be seen without additional duality: local splittings of a vector-bundle extension differ by a Čech cocycle in Hom(Q,O(a)), and a coboundary changes them to compatible splittings. Vanishing therefore gives a global splitting. Consequently
E≅O(a)⊕j⨁​O(bj​),​
(20)
completing the induction over any field k.
The degrees can be arranged in nonincreasing order and their multiset is unique. Indeed, for any such decomposition,
h0(E(m))−h0(E(m−1))=#{i:ai​≥−m},
(21)
so the dimensions of the twisted global sections recover the number of summands of every degree.
