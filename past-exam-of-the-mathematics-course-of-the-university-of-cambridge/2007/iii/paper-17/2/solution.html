First suppose Δ has full dimension in MR​. Form the toric cone over it in MR​⊕R and the graded semigroup algebra
RΔ​=⨁l≥0​ ⨁m∈lΔ∩M​kχmtl,deg(χmtl)=l,
(7)
where degree zero contains only k. The lattice semigroup is finitely generated by the Gordan lemma. The projective toric variety of a lattice polytope is PΔ​=ProjRΔ​. Using all lattice points in all dilates avoids any assumption that the degree-one lattice points generate the ring.
For each vertex v of Δ, the degree-zero part after inverting χvt is
(RΔ​[(χvt)−1])0​=k[cone(Δ−v)∩M].
(8)
Indeed its exponents are m−lv with m∈lΔ. Conversely, every lattice point of the tangent toric cone has this form for sufficiently large integral l. These charts cover the Proj construction: each point of a dilate is a rational convex combination of vertices, so some power of its monomial is a product of vertex monomials. Their dual cones are the vertex toric cones of the inward normal fan of a polytope. The normal toric fan is complete because every linear functional on Δ attains its minimum on a convex face. This describes PΔ​ as a complete toric variety. Translating Δ by m0​∈M gives the graded isomorphism (m,l)↦(m+lm0​,l), so containing the origin is unnecessary. If Δ is not full-dimensional, translate a lattice vertex to zero and use M∩spanR​(Δ−Δ); the construction is then complete of dimension dimΔ.
For the simplex in question put Q=∏i​qi​. Its inequalities are xi​≥0 and ∑i​qi​xi​≤Q, so its inward normal toric rays are R+​ei​ and R+​(−q1​,…,−qn​). Here the ei​ in the dual space denote the standard dual basis. Apply the real linear map A(ei​)=ei​/qi​. It carries these toric rays and their convex face incidences to the standard simplex fan, with toric rays e1​,…,en​,−∑i​ei​. The transported lattice is
N′=i=1⨁n​qi​1​Zei​⊇Zn,PΔ​≅XΣ,N′​.​
(9)
With gcd(q1​,…,qn​)=1, the primitive vectors of this toric fan in N′ are vi​=ei​/qi​ and v0​=−∑i​ei​. The latter is primitive because λ∑i​ei​∈N′ requires each λqi​ to be integral, whose smallest positive solution is λ=1. Their unique primitive relation is
v0​+q1​v1​+⋯+qn​vn​=0.
(10)
Thus mutual coprimality beyond the displayed common gcd condition is not needed.
For a general complete toric variety, introduce one variable zρ​ for each toric ray. Its toric homogeneous coordinate ring, or Cox ring, is
S=k[zρ​:ρ∈Σ(1)],degzρ​=[Fρ​]∈Cl(XΣ​).​
(11)
The toric divisor class sequence is 0→M→ZΣ(1)→Cl(XΣ​)→0, where the first map is m↦(⟨m,vρ​⟩)ρ​. Thus the graded piece Sα​ has basis the monomials ∏zρbρ​​ with bρ​≥0 and ∑bρ​[Fρ​]=α. For an invariant divisor D=∑aρ​Fρ​, monomials in degree [D] correspond to algebraic torus characters χm through bρ​=aρ​+⟨m,vρ​⟩≥0. This also identifies that graded piece with the global sections of O(D).
In our example M′=(N′)∗=⨁i​qi​Zei∗​, and its map to Zn+1 sends ∑i​qi​bi​ei∗​ to (−∑i​qi​bi​,b1​,…,bn​). The quotient is therefore Z, via (c0​,…,cn​)↦c0​+∑i​qi​ci​. In particular degz0​=1 and degzi​=qi​, giving the explicit graded isomorphism
S ∼ ​k[Y0​,Y1q1​​,…,Ynqn​​],z0​↦Y0​,zi​↦Yiqi​​.​
(12)
The target has the grading inherited from ordinary total degree in k[Y0​,…,Yn​]. Its displayed generators are algebraically independent, which proves injectivity as well as surjectivity onto that subring.
To obtain the quotient, the toric irrelevant ideal is generated by ∏ρ∈/σ(1)​zρ​. Here each maximal toric cone omits exactly one toric ray, so it is (z0​,…,zn​), and the open set is An+1∖{0}. The diagonalizable algebraic group with algebraic torus character group Cl(X)=Z is k∗. Its action on the Cox coordinates is dictated by their degrees, namely
λ⋅(y0​,…,yn​)=(λy0​,λq1​y1​,…,λqn​yn​),PΔ​≅P(1,q1​,…,qn​).​
(13)
Here the yi​ name the independent Cox coordinates; the graded-subring realization above does not change their weights. More concretely, the quotient chart where yi​=0 has coordinate ring (S[yi−1​])0​, the invariant ring for this action. These charts glue to ProjS and to the specified toric fan charts. Positive weights make each orbit closed inside the punctured affine space, and in characteristic zero the finite stabilizers cause no failure of this geometric quotient. This is the weighted projective space construction.
