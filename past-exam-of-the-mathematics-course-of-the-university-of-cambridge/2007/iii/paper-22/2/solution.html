A global holomorphic section composed with that inclusion has n+1 component holomorphic functions on compact connected Complex projective space. Each is constant: its absolute value attains a maximum; in a coordinate ball the one-variable maximum modulus principle along complex lines makes it locally constant, and connectedness propagates that constant globally. Thus the section is a fixed vector v∈Cn+1 lying in every fibre, namely every line CZ. For n≥1 the intersection of all these lines is zero. Hence
