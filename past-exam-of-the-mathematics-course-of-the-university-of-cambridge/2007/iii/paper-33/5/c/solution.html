Set Wt−​=Bh−t​−Bh​ and Wt+​=Bh+t​−Bh​, 0≤t≤h, with h=1/2. Both start at zero and are continuous. Increments of W− over consecutive disjoint time intervals are negatives of Brownian increments over corresponding disjoint past intervals, read in reverse order. They are independent centered normal random variables with covariance equal to the interval length times the identity. The same properties hold for W+ using future increments. Every past increment is independent of all the future increments after deterministic time h, so the two whole processes are independent, by finite-dimensional independence and generation by rational-time evaluations. They are the independent Brownian arms at a deterministic time.
