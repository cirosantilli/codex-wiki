Write η(A)=∫A​X∞​dλ. This is a finite positive measure. For A∈Fn​, the conditional expectation inequality gives
η(A)=∫A​E[X∞​∣Fn​],dλ≤∫A​Xn​dλ=μ(A).
(5)
The union A=⋃n​Fn​ is an algebra of sets generating B([0,1)), since half-open dyadic intervals generate all open sets and hence all Borel sets. The class {A:η(A)≤μ(A)} is closed under increasing and decreasing limits, by continuity of these finite measures. The Monotone class theorem extends the inequality from A to every Borel set. Approximation by nonnegative simple functions and monotone convergence then give
μ(f)≥∫fX∞​dλfor every nonnegative measurable f.​
(6)
The difference ν=μ−η is therefore a finite nonnegative measure, not merely a signed measure. For A∈Fn​,
ν(A)=∫A​(Xn​−E[X∞​∣Fn​])dλ.
(7)
Consequently its restricted Radon-Nikodym derivative is
Yn​=Xn​−E[X∞​∣Fn​].​
(8)
Choose the cell-average versions of all the conditional expectations. Then Yn​ is constant on each level-n cell and equals 2nν(Dkn​) there, so these versions are nonnegative at every point.
