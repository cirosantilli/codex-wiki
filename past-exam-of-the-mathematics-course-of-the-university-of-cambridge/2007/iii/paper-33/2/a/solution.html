Write the residual process as
Mt​=∑i=1n​θi​1−t1{Ui​>t}​​.
(15)
For s<t<1, the filtration tells us whether each Ui​ has arrived by time s, and its exact arrival time if it has. On {Ui​>s}, its conditional law is uniform on (s,1). Independence of the original Ui​ means that the histories of the other arrivals do not alter this law. Hence
E[1{Ui​>t}​∣Fs​]=1{Ui​>s}​1−s1−t​.
(16)
This conditional identity can also be checked against products of bounded functions of the censored arrival times and extended to Fs​ by the Monotone class theorem. Summing the identity gives E[Mt​∣Fs​]=Ms​.
Each Mt​ is adapted and integrable, with
EMt​=∑i​θi​=S.
(17)
Its paths are smooth between the finitely many arrival times and have downward jumps of size θi​/(1−Ui​) at arrivals before time one. The indicators use Ui​>t, so the value at a jump is the post-jump value: paths are right continuous with finite left limits on [0,1). Thus M is a càdlàg martingale, specifically the uniform-arrival residual mass martingale.
