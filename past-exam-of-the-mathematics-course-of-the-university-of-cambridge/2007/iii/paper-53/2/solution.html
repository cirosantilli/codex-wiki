Use metric signature (+−−−) and average over the four initial spin states, summing over final spins and the Nc​=3 quark color charge states. The Electrons carry no color charge, so their initial color charge average is trivial. A final color charge average would not give the inclusive relativistic cross-section requested.
Photon exchange gives the tree amplitude, up to an irrelevant overall phase,
M=se2Qq​​δab​[vˉ(p1​)γμu(p2​)][uˉ(q1​)γμ​v(q2​)],e2=4πα.
(9)
Massless spin sums give ∑uuˉ=p and ∑vvˉ=p. Therefore
∣M∣2​=4s2Nc​e4Qq2​​tr(p1​γμp2​γν)tr(q1​γμ​q2​γν​).
(10)
The color charge multiplicity is ∑a,b​∣δab​∣2=Nc​, not Nc2​. Each trace equals 4(aμbν+aνbμ−gμνa⋅b). Contracting them, the terms proportional to (p1​⋅p2​)(q1​⋅q2​) cancel, leaving 32[(p1​⋅q1​)(p2​⋅q2​)+(p1​⋅q2​)(p2​⋅q1​)]. Thus
∣M∣2​=s2384π2α2Qq2​​[(p1​⋅q2​)(p2​⋅q1​)+(p2​⋅q2​)(p1​⋅q1​)]​.
(11)
In the centre-of-momentum frame every energy is s​/2. Let θ be the angle between p1​ and q2​. Then
p1​⋅q2​=p2​⋅q1​=4s​(1−cosθ),p1​⋅q1​=p2​⋅q2​=4s​(1+cosθ).
(12)
Consequently ∣M∣2​=Nc​e4Qq2​(1+cos2θ).
The supplied two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space formula has a factor-of-two error. Deriving the normalization explicitly,
dΦ2​=32π2dΩ​,incident flux=4p1​⋅p2​=2s,
(13)
so the massless two-body scattering flux normalization is
dΩdσ​=64π2s∣M∣2​​=128π2p1​⋅p2​∣M∣2​​.
(14)
For example, integrating the spatial Dirac delta in dΦ2​ leaves [4(2π)2]−1dkdΩδ(s​−2k); its radial integral supplies the factor 1/2. The printed denominator 64π2p1​⋅p2​ would double the relativistic cross-section and cannot reproduce the requested result with the standard scattering amplitude.
The correctly normalized differential and total results for electron-positron annihilation into a quark pair are
dΩdσ​=4s3α2Qq2​​(1+cos2θ),∫dΩ(1+cos2θ)=316π​,
(15)
and hence
σq​=s4πα2​Qq2​​.
(16)
At energies where photon exchange and the parton description apply, sum over kinematically accessible quark flavors. The produced quarks undergo hadronization, so the leading inclusive hadronic rate is
σhad​=s4πα2​∑q​Qq2​,R(s)=σ(e+e−→μ+μ−)σhad​​=3∑q​Qq2​.
(17)
This is the hadronic R ratio. Perturbative QCD adds a leading relative correction αs​(s)/π; thresholds, narrow resonances and, near the Z boson scale, electroweak exchange require additional treatment. The calculation is a photon-exchange, massless result from a tree-level Feynman diagram.
