There is a sign inconsistency in the printed model. For the ordinary cross product let J(a)z=a×z. The vector triple-product identity gives [J(a),J(b)]=J(a×b). Consequently the printed Dμ​=∂μ​+eJ(Aμ​) has gauge curvature
[Dμ​,Dν​]Φ=eFμν+​×Φ,Fμν+​=∂μ​Aν​−∂ν​Aμ​+eAμ​×Aν​.
(27)
Its gauge curvature cannot instead contain the printed negative quadratic term. For a noncommuting pure gauge potential for this gauge covariant derivative, F+=0 whereas F−=−2eAμ​×Aν​=0. Thus the printed gauge kinetic term assigns a nonzero value to a connection obtained by applying the gauge-field transformation law to the zero connection. Literally the printed action has global rotations but not the requested local triplet gauge symmetry. The cross-product convention for an adjoint covariant derivative makes the minimal repair explicit: change the gauge field strength quadratic term to a plus sign, retaining the printed gauge covariant derivative. Changing the sign in the gauge covariant derivative instead would be another consistent convention. The remaining calculation uses the first repair.
The corrected theory has local SO(3) gauge symmetry, equivalently the adjoint-field realization of the SU(2) Lie algebra; its adjoint scalar fields and gauge fields are insensitive to the center of a group of SU(2). Explicitly, Φ↦R(x)Φ and
eJ(Aμ​)↦ReJ(Aμ​)R−1−(∂μ​R)R−1.
(28)
Both Dμ​Φ and Fμν​ transform as triplets, so their dot products and the scalar potential are invariant. Assume λ>0, e=0 and take v≥0 without loss of generality. For v>0 the minima have Φ2=v2. Choose the scalar-field vacuum Φ0​=v(0,0,1), whose stabilizer consists of rotations around the third axis: the residual gauge symmetry is SO(2), locally U(1).
In unitary gauge write Φ=(0,0,v+h), Aμ​=Aμ3​ and Wμ±​=(Aμ1​∓iAμ2​)/2​. These are the neutral massless gauge field, two charged massive gauge fields and a real radial scalar field. Define
fμν​=∂μ​Aν​−∂ν​Aμ​,Cμν​=Wμ+​Wν−​−Wν+​Wμ−​,
(29)
Gμν±​=(∂μ​∓ieAμ​)Wν±​−(∂ν​∓ieAν​)Wμ±​.
(30)
Substitution gives Fμν3​=fμν​−ieCμν​ and (Fμν1​∓iFμν2​)/2​=Gμν±​. The entire physical-field Lagrangian density is therefore
L=−41​(fμν​−ieCμν​)(fμν−ieCμν)−21​Gμν+​G−μν+21​∂μ​h∂μh+e2(v+h)2Wμ+​W−μ−8λ​[(v+h)2−v2]2.

In particular Dμ​Φ=(e(v+h)Aμ2​,−e(v+h)Aμ1​,∂μ​h); this directly verifies the scalar field kinetic and vector mass terms. Expanding the scalar potential gives λv2h2/2+λvh3/2+λh4/8. Comparing the quadratic terms with standard real scalar field and charged-vector mass terms yields the adjoint triplet Higgs spectrum,
mW±2​=e2v2,mA​=0,mh2​=λv2​.
(32)
This also rewrites the purely vector interactions, which are already contained in the displayed gauge field strengths rather than omitted from the theory.
The original symmetry remains unbroken at v=0. The unique minimum is then Φ=0, with three massless gauge fields and three massless real scalar field components; the quartic scalar field interaction remains. The physical degrees of freedom number 3×2+3=9. At v>0 the two broken generators supply two Goldstone bosons, eaten to give longitudinal gauge-boson polarizations of the charged vectors. The physical count is 2×3+2+1=9: two massive vectors, one massless vector and one radial scalar field. The scalar components eaten in unitary gauge are not additional physical particles.
For the broken phase all interactions containing the physical scalar field are
