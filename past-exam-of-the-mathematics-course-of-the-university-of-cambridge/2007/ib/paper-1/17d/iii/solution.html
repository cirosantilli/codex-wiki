(61)
Equivalently,
h=2gU02​​[1−(ρA0​U02​(1−cosα)Mg​)2].
(62)
For a nonnegative support height the ratio in parentheses must not exceed one, and the prescribed h≪U02​/(2g) requires that ratio to be close to one. If the weight exceeds the lift available at the nozzle, this model has no supported height above the nozzle.
