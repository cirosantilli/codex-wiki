Use the position four-vector Xa=(ct,x) and Minkowski signature (+,−,−,−). Along a massive particle's timelike path,
c2dτ2=c2dt2−∣dx∣2,dτ=dt1−∣v∣2/c2​=dt/γ.
(8)
Thus its four-velocity is Ua=dXa/dτ=γ(c,v), and multiplying by the invariant rest mass gives
pa=MUa=(Mγc,Mγv).​
(9)
Its temporal component is E/c, and the invariant relation is (E/c)2−∣p∣2=M2c2.
For a massless particle, use the null energy–momentum relation rather than substituting M=0 into Mγ: a massless particle has no proper-time parametrization of a timelike rest frame. With positive energy, papa​=0 implies E/c=∣p∣. Hence
pa=(∣p∣,p),E=c∣p∣.​
(10)
The original PDF retains the spatial vector in this expression; the TeX conversion incorrectly replaced it by its magnitude.
