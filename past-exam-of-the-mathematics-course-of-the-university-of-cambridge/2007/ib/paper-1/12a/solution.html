The product topology on X×Y has as a basis the rectangles U×V, where U is open in X and V is open in Y. Equivalently, it is the coarsest topology making both coordinate projections continuous.
To show that the product of two Hausdorff spaces is Hausdorff, take distinct points (x1​,y1​) and (x2​,y2​). If x1​=x2​, choose disjoint open neighborhoods U1​,U2​ in X; the rectangles U1​×Y and U2​×Y separate the product points. If the first coordinates agree, the second coordinates differ, and the same argument uses disjoint open neighborhoods in Y. Thus every pair of distinct product points has disjoint open neighborhoods.
