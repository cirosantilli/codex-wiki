Communication is reflexive because (P0)ii​=1, and symmetric by its definition. If i↔j and j↔k, choose positive-probability paths i→j in m steps and j→k in n steps. The Chapman-Kolmogorov equation gives
(Pm+n)ik​≥(Pm)ij​(Pn)jk​>0.
(24)
Combining the reverse paths gives a positive-probability path k→i as well. Thus communication is transitive and is an equivalence relation.
To transfer recurrence, first establish the recurrence criterion by return probabilities. Put ri​=Pi​(Ti+​<∞) and let Ni​=∑n≥0​1{Xn​=i}​ under Pi​. After each return the Strong Markov property restarts the chain at i, so Pi​(Ni​≥k+1)=rik​. Therefore
∑n≥0​(Pn)ii​=Ei​Ni​=∑k≥0​rik​.
(25)
For ri​<1 this equals (1−ri​)−1; for ri​=1 it is infinite. The expectation identity follows by summing the nonnegative indicators, so remains valid when the expectation is infinite. Consequently i is a recurrent state exactly when its return-probability series diverges.
If i↔j, choose a,b with (Pa)ji​>0 and (Pb)ij​>0. For every n≥0,
(Pa+n+b)jj​≥(Pa)ji​(Pn)ii​(Pb)ij​.
(26)
Summing over n, recurrence of i forces the return-probability series for j to diverge. The proved criterion shows j is recurrent, so recurrence is a property of the communicating class.
