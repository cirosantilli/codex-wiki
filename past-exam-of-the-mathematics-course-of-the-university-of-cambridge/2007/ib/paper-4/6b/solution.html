For the wavefunction of a particle of mass m, the probability density and probability current are
ρ=Ψ∗Ψ,j=2miℏ​(Ψ∗Ψx​−ΨΨx∗​)=mℏ​Im(Ψ∗Ψx​).​

For a real potential V, the Time-dependent Schrödinger equation and its complex conjugate read
iℏΨt​=−2mℏ2​Ψxx​+VΨ,−iℏΨt∗​=−2mℏ2​Ψxx∗​+VΨ∗.
(12)
Multiplying by Ψ∗ and Ψ respectively yields
ρt​=2miℏ​(Ψ∗Ψxx​−ΨΨxx∗​)=−jx​.
(13)
The potential terms cancel because V is real. This is the continuity equation, expressing local conservation of probability.
For the given superposition the common time phase cancels in Ψ∗Ψx​, and
Ψ∗Ψx​=ik(1−∣R∣2)+ik(R∗e2ikx−Re−2ikx).
(14)
The expression in the second parentheses is purely imaginary, so the second term is real and contributes nothing to the probability current. Thus
j=mℏk​(1−∣R∣2).​
(15)
It is the incoming probability current minus the reflected probability current.
