At the initial instant the circulation around every closed curve is zero because the fluid is at rest. A smooth material-flow map carries closed curves bijectively between times. Conservation of circulation around every material curve therefore makes the line integral of the velocity zero around every closed curve at the current time, not just small contractible loops. Fix a reference point in a connected fluid region and define

The vanishing closed-curve integrals make this independent of the path. Thus u=∇ϕ: the fluid has a globally defined velocity potential. Its incompressible flow condition is ∇⋅u=0, hence ∇2ϕ=0. This argument establishes the global potential without silently assuming the fluid region is simply connected.
