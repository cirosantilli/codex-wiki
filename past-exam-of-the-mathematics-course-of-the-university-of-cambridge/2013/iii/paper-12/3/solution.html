We prove edit-distance stability for clique-free graphs by induction on r, using the symmetric difference of edge sets as the distance. For r=1, a K2​-free graph is edgeless, and there is nothing to change. For r≥2, choose a vertex of maximum degree d, let A be its neighbourhood and put B=V(G)∖A. Then G[A] is Kr​-free. Write
D=tr​(n)−e(G),DA​=tr−1​(d)−e(G[A]),b=e(G[B]),m=∣A∣∣B∣−eG​(A,B).
(15)
Both deficits are nonnegative by the Turan theorem. Since every vertex of B has degree at most d=∣A∣,
eG​(A,B)+2b≤∣B∣d,m≥2b.
(16)
The complete r-partite graph formed from an (r−1)-partite Turán graph on A and the new class B has at most tr​(n) edges. Thus L=tr​(n)−tr−1​(d)−∣A∣∣B∣≥0, and direct subtraction gives
D=L+DA​+m−b.
(17)
By induction, edit G[A] into a complete (r−1)-partite graph using at most 3DA​ changes. Delete the b edges inside B, and add the m missing edges between A and B. The resulting graph is complete r-partite on the same vertex set, and
3DA​+b+m≤3DA​+3(m−b)+3L=3D≤3k,
(18)
where the inequality uses m≥2b. The required edit distance is at most 3k. Empty partition classes are allowed; a sufficiently dense nondegenerate case has the usual full complement of classes.
For the odd-cycle conclusion, use two standard consequences of the Szemerédi regularity lemma, stated explicitly. The triangle removal lemma says that for every α>0, some β>0 ensures that a graph with at most βn3 triangles in a graph can be made triangle-free by deleting at most α(2n​) edges, for all sufficiently large n. Also, for each fixed odd length ℓ≥3 and each β>0, there is ζ>0 such that a graph with at least βn3 triangles in a graph contains at least ζnℓ copies of Cℓ​.
For clarity, the latter odd-cycle copies from positive triangle density consequence follows by applying regularity with error small relative to β, removing exceptional, irregular and very sparse pairs, and retaining a triangle in a graph among the remaining regular dense pairs. The graph embedding lemma for regular pairs counts a positive constant times nℓ embeddings of any fixed graph properly three-coloured into those three clusters, including Cℓ​. Dividing by the fixed number of descriptions of a cycle gives the same conclusion for unlabelled copies.
Apply triangle in a graph removal with α=ϵ, and take the resulting β. A C2013​-free graph cannot have βn3 triangles in a graph for large n, by the preceding consequence. Delete at most ϵ(2n​) edges to obtain a triangle-free G′. With N=(2n​),
e(G′)≥(1/2−2ϵ)N,t2​(n)−e(G′)≤2ϵN+n/4.
(19)
Apply the proved stability result with r=2. The resulting complete bipartite graph H satisfies
∣E(G)△E(H)∣≤ϵN+3(2ϵN+n/4)=7ϵN+3n/4.
(20)
Choose n0​(ϵ) also large enough that 3n/4≤4ϵN. Then the distance is at most 11ϵ(2n​).
For the unheaded continuation, use the same β and its associated ζ, and set δ=ζ/2. Having at most δn2013 cycles rules out βn3 triangles in a graph just as before. The identical deletion and stability calculation applies. A sufficiently small δ=δ(ϵ)>0 gives the same 11ϵ bound.
