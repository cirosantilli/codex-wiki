Take N to be an even number for a perfectly bipartite periodic chain and make the bipartite spin rotation by π about the x axis on alternating sites. In the local frame the Néel state has all spins up, while a bond becomes

Every site has two neighbours. Fourier transform gives ∑⟨ij⟩​(ni​+nj​)=2∑k​nk​ and pairing coefficient γk​=cosk, so
H=−NJS2+2JS∑k​ak†​ak​+JS∑k​γk​(ak​a−k​+ak†​a−k†​)+O(S0).

H=−NJS(S+1)+JSk∑​(ak†​,a−k​)(1γk​​γk​1​)(ak​a−k†​​)+O(S0).​
