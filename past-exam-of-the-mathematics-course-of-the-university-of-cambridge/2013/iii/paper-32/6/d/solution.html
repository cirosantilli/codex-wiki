For example, the second stop is 33.1−24.8=8.3, while its entry is 27.8−24.8=3. Thus subsequent rows begin at gap age three, not zero. The first row retains time since treatment started. Keep the patient and episode labels, and preferably the original calendar endpoints as auxiliary fields: gap ages in different episodes are not chronological treatment times. The value 7.1 for episode three being smaller than 8.3 for episode two does not reverse their occurrence order. This gap-time recurrent-event model forms risk sets on the episode-age scale while preserving each episode's historical eligibility.
