A diamond sequence on a regular uncountable λ implies 2ℵ0​≤λ. For every x⊆ω, regard x as a subset of λ. A correct guess above ω is exactly x, so the sequence's at most λ guesses include every real.
Force with Add(ω,λ+) using finite conditions. The delta-system lemma and agreement on the finite root prove the countable chain condition. It preserves cardinals and the regularity of λ, while adding at least λ+ distinct reals. The reals on distinct coordinates differ on a dense set of conditions, so their number is indeed at least this ground cardinal. In the extension,
2ℵ0​≥λ+>λ,¬◊λ​.​
(42)
This gives the requested relative consistency over a model with the indicated regular uncountable cardinal, with the usual forcing-theoretic consistency interpretation.
