Let p∈G force that D˙ is a diamond sequence. For each α<ω1​, construct in M the family
Eα​={x⊆α:∃q≥p (q⊩D˙α​=xˇ)}.
(40)
This family is countable in M: choose a witnessing condition for each distinct value; different values require incompatible conditions, and the forcing is CCC It is not being asserted that every generic value of D˙α​ is a ground subset. Only values forced equal to a ground subset enter Eα​.
For any ground X⊆ω1​ and ground club set C⊆ω1​, the CCC preserves ω1​ and C remains a club set. Since p forces diamond, some strengthening q≥p forces D˙α​=Xˇ∩αˇ for an ordinal α∈C, deciding the ordinal witness if necessary. Thus X∩α∈Eα​. The ground set of such α meets every ground club set and is stationary in M.
Consequently ⟨Eα​⟩ witnesses the countable-family diamond assertion in M. By the fully proved countable-family diamond equivalence, with λ=ω1​ and its stationary index set, it follows that
M⊨◊.​
(41)
Thus CCC forcing cannot create diamond.
