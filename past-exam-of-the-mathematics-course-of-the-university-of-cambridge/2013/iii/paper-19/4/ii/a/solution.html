The generic extension is M[G]={τG:τ∈M is a forcing name}. It is transitive: if x∈τG, an active pair in τ supplies a subname σ∈M with x=σG. Ground sets belong to it by their canonical names. Without assuming a weakest condition, use aˇ={(bˇ,p):b∈a, p∈P}; nonemptiness of G gives aˇG=a.
Induction on forcing name rank gives rank(τG)≤nrk(τ). The right side is an ordinal of M. If α∈M[G] is an ordinal, its rank equals α, so it is at most a ground-model ordinal. Transitivity of M then implies α∈M. Conversely every ground ordinal remains the same ordinal in the transitive extension, since membership is unchanged. Hence
Ord∩M[G]=Ord∩M.​
(25)
This proves forcing preserves ordinals directly from ranks, without assuming cardinal preservation.
