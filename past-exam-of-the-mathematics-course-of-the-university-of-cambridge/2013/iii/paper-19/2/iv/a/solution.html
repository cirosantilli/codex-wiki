For the intended infinite regular cardinal κ, set S0​(δ)=0. Suppose 0<ζ<κ+ and the functions for its predecessors have been constructed. Since ∣ζ∣≤κ, choose a surjection eζ​:κ→ζ and define
Sζ​(δ)=η<δsup​(Seζ​(η)​(δ)+1).​
(12)
The supremum is below κ: it uses fewer than κ ordinals below κ, and κ is regular. This defines a function κ→κ at each stage of the recursion.
For any α<ζ, choose η with eζ​(η)=α. Whenever δ>η, the displayed supremum includes Sα​(δ)+1, so Sα​(δ)<Sζ​(δ). Therefore the recursion yields a long chain under eventual domination of length κ+.
