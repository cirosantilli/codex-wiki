The required boundary model is a fracture that drains after the storm, with h(0,t)=0, and a current of finite extent whose flux vanishes at its advancing nose. The drainage volume decay in a dipole porous current shows that the printed positive rate cannot describe drainage and its factor is also inconsistent with the conserved-moment scaling. This solution supplies a direct counterexample to that rate and the corrected one.
