Take increasing to mean strict growth on every nonempty time interval; otherwise a constant family has no uniquely determined driving point. The half-plane-capacity parameterization imposed below guarantees strict growth. For s<t, define the increment hull in the mapped domain by
Ks,t​=H∖gs​(H∖Kt​).
(38)
This definition includes filling and boundary conventions automatically. Its mapping-out function is gs,t​=gt​∘gs−1​. The Loewner local growth property means
0≤t≤Tsup​rad(Kt,t+h​)⟶0(h↓0)​
(39)
for every finite T within the parameter range. Equivalently, the mapped increments have uniformly vanishing diameter on compact time intervals.
For fixed t, the nonempty compact Euclidean closures Kt,t+h​ are nested as h decreases. Their diameters tend to zero, so their intersection is a singleton. Its point lies on the real axis: the imaginary part of any point in a hull is at most its enclosing radius. Define the Loewner transform by
{ξt​}=h>0⋂​Kt,t+h​.​
(40)
Since ξt​ lies in each closure, every point of Kt,t+h​ is at distance at most 2rad(Kt,t+h​) from it.
To prove continuity, choose z∈Kt+2h​∖Kt+h​ and write w=gt​(z), w′=gt+h​(z). Then w∈Kt,t+2h​, w′∈Kt+h,t+2h​ and w′=gt,t+h​(w). The continuity estimate from part (i) gives
∣ξt+h​−ξt​∣​≤∣ξt​−w∣+∣w−w′∣+∣w′−ξt+h​∣≤2rad(Kt,t+2h​)+3rad(Kt,t+h​)+2rad(Kt+h,t+2h​).​
(41)
The right-hand side tends to zero uniformly on compact time intervals. Applying the same inequality with the earlier time as the base proves left continuity as well. Thus the Loewner transform is continuous.
Now impose hcap(Kt​)=2t. The half-plane-capacity composition rule follows by composing Laurent expansions at infinity and gives
hcap(Ks,t​)=2(t−s).
(42)
For a point not yet swallowed, put zs​=gs​(z) and rs,t​=2rad(Ks,t​). The increment is contained in the half-disc of radius rs,t​ centred at ξs​. The continuity estimate first proves continuity of s↦gs​(z): its increment is bounded by 3rad(Ks,t​). The differentiability estimate for a mapping-out function then gives, when t−s is small enough,
gt​(z)−gs​(z)=gs​(z)−ξs​2(t−s)​+O(∣gs​(z)−ξs​∣2rs,t​(t−s)​).
(43)
On a compact interval before swallowing the denominator stays away from zero. Divide by t−s and let t↓s. The local-growth property makes the error tend to zero. The analogous backward quotient has the same limit, using continuity of gs​(z) and ξs​. Thus the Chordal Loewner equation follows:
∂t​gt​(z)=gt​(z)−ξt​2​,g0​(z)=z.​
(44)
The initial value follows from hcap(K0​)=0, hence K0​=∅. Without the capacity parameterization, the same argument gives dgt​(z)=dhcap(Kt​)/(gt​(z)−ξt​), interpreted with the capacity clock.
