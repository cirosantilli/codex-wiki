Acting on the column (x,1)T gives the faithful Matrix Lie group representation
(eα0​β1​)(x1​)=(eαx+β1​).
(25)
Multiplication and inversion are
(α,β)(a,b)=(α+a,β+eαb),(α,β)−1=(−α,−e−αβ).
(26)
Thus G is the real affine group. Differentiating the matrices at the identity gives its Lie algebra
g={(a0​b0​):a,b∈R}.
(27)
With
D=(10​00​),T=(00​10​),
(28)
the matrix commutator is [D,T]=T, and [D,D]=[T,T]=0.
The left Maurer-Cartan form and its right analogue are
θL​=g−1dg=(dα0​e−αdβ0​)=Ddα+Te−αdβ,
(29)
θR​=dgg−1=(dα0​dβ−βdα0​)=Ddα+T(dβ−βdα).
(30)
For a constant h, left translation leaves θL​ unchanged, and right translation leaves θR​ unchanged. Taking their dual vector fields gives
DL​=∂α​,TL​=eα∂β​​,DR​=∂α​+β∂β​,TR​=∂β​​.
(31)
The first pair consists of left-invariant vector fields; the second consists of right-invariant vector fields. These are the invariant frames of the real affine group. Computing their Lie brackets of vector fields yields
[DL​,TL​]=TL​,[DR​,TR​]=−TR​.
(32)
All brackets of a field with itself vanish. The sign difference is necessary: evaluation at the identity identifies left-invariant vector fields with the matrix Lie algebra as a homomorphism, whereas right-invariant vector fields realize the opposite Lie algebra. Equivalently, ξ↦−ξR​ is a homomorphism for the same matrix commutator. There is no convention in which these particular coordinate fields both have the positive structure constant while retaining the usual Lie bracket of vector fields.
The Maurer-Cartan equation gives the same check. If θL​=DℓD+TℓT, then
dℓD=0,dℓT=−ℓD∧ℓT.
(33)
For θR​=DrD+TrT, instead
drD=0,drT=rD∧rT.
(34)
Thus the left equation is dθL​+θL​∧θL​=0 and the right equation is dθR​−θR​∧θR​=0, explaining the opposite bracket signs directly.
Finally, the two given matrix curves are exp(ϵT) and exp(ϵD). Their left action on a general group element is
g1​(ϵ)g(α,β)=g(α,β+ϵ),g2​(ϵ)g(α,β)=g(α+ϵ,eϵβ).
(35)
Differentiating at ϵ=0 gives TR​ and DR​ respectively. Hence Left translations on a Lie group are generated by right-invariant vector fields. More generally, the velocity of exp(tξ)g is (dRg​)e​ξ, which is right-invariant because left and right multiplication commute. In the other direction, the flows gexp(tξ) generate left-invariant vector fields and are right translations on a Lie group.
