 ... Past exam of the mathematics course of the University of Cambridge 2013 iii Paper 57 3 b
OurBigBook.com Words: 114
Apply the modulo operation to the eigenvalues of ZA​+ZB​. The product eigenstates ∣00⟩,∣01⟩,∣10⟩,∣11⟩ have ordinary eigenvalues 2,0,0,−2, respectively, and residues 2,0,0,2 modulo 4. The resulting observable is
O=2Pe​=I+ZA​⊗ZB​​.
(23)
Use the entanglement-assisted nondemolition parity measurement from part (a). Equal local meter records give O=2; unequal records give O=0. Its conditional measurement in quantum mechanics maps are ρ↦Pe​ρPe​ and ρ↦Po​ρPo​, with normalization by their probabilities. The quantum nondemolition measurement preserves every vector within each degenerate eigenspace, including superpositions of ∣00⟩ and ∣11⟩. Measuring the two system spins separately would destroy that even-sector coherence and would therefore not realize the same Lüders rule instrument. The nonlocal eigenvalue again becomes known only after local operations and classical communication compares the local records.
