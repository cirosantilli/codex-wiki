The fitted progressive illness-death model has three allowed arrows: 1→2 with estimated transition intensity 0.1849, 1→3 with 0.01935, and 2→3 with 0.06143, all in years−1. State 3 is an absorbing state, and state 2 has no return arrow.
Once in state 2, the holding time has an exponential distribution with rate q23​, so the mean holding time from a transition intensity matrix is
E(T2​)=0.061431​=16.28 years.​
(39)
Apply a confidence interval for an inverse rate to the printed rate interval (0.03552,0.1063): since inversion reverses order, the approximate 95% interval for the mean is
(0.10631​,0.035521​)=(9.41,28.15) years.​
(40)
The time-homogeneous Markov property makes the future depend only on the current state; the exponential distribution also has the memoryless property. For a person currently in state 2,
p23​(2)=1−e−2(0.06143)=0.11561.​
(41)
Equivalently, p22​(2)=p22​(1)2≃0.94041632. Thus the fitted two-year death probability is approximately 11.6%.
