For y=0,1,2,… and λ>0, the Poisson distribution has mass
Pr(Y=y)=y!e−λλy​=exp{ylogλ−λ−log(y!)}.
(17)
Match this to the exponential dispersion family with
θ=logλ,b(θ)=eθ,ϕ=1,c(y,1)=−log(y!).​
(18)
Then μ=b′(θ)=eθ=λ and V(μ)=b′′(θ)=μ. The canonical link function expresses the natural parameter in terms of the mean, so the Poisson canonical link is g(μ)=logμ. Its conditional variance equals its conditional mean.
