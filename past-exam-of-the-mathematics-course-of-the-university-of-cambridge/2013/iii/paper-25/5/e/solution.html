Fix T>0. The given normal distribution and part (d) imply
Ee−λ⟨X⟩T​=e−λT(λ≥0).
(53)
One can deduce determinism without any moment assumption on the bracket. Put Z=e−⟨X⟩T​. Taking λ=1,2 gives EZ=e−T and EZ2=e−2T, so Var(Z)=0. Therefore ⟨X⟩T​=T almost surely. Applying this at every rational time and using continuity of quadratic variation gives ⟨X⟩t​=t simultaneously for all t≥0 outside a single null set.
The Lévy characterization of Brownian motion states that a continuous local martingale starting at zero with this bracket is Brownian motion in its filtration. To see the independent-increment conclusion directly, the Itô formula shows that eiθXt​+θ2t/2 is a martingale on any fixed bounded time interval: it is a local martingale with a deterministic bound on its modulus. Thus
E[eiθ(Xt​−Xs​)∣Fs​]=e−θ2(t−s)/2.
(54)
The deterministic conditional characteristic function identifies an N(0,t−s) increment independent of Fs​. Together with the given path continuity and X0​=0, this proves X is Brownian motion.
