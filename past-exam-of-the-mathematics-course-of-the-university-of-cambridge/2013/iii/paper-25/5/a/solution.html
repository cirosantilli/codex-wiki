For a deterministic t≤T, conditional symmetry makes the conditional characteristic function of XT​−Xt​ invariant under θ↦−θ. The bounded real and imaginary parts of the exponential are legitimate test functions. Hence
e−2iθXt​Mt​=e−iθXt​E[eiθ(XT​−Xt​)∣Ft​]=e−iθXt​E[e−iθ(XT​−Xt​)∣Ft​]=E[e−iθXT​∣Ft​].
(46)
The right side is a bounded complex martingale. Both sides have continuous versions by the assumptions; equality on rational times and continuity make them indistinguishable. Thus e−2iθXt​Mt​ is a martingale on [0,T]. Complex martingale assertions mean the corresponding assertions for both real and imaginary parts.
