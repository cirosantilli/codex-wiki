Continuity and adaptedness make the first boundary hit a stopping time. A continuous path starting in the open domain cannot leave it before meeting its boundary. Thus Xt∧T​∈D∪∂D, with the usual interpretation when T=∞. If ∣u∣≤K on this set, then

The stopped process is a bounded local martingale; the bounded local martingale criterion makes it a martingale and, in fact, a uniformly integrable martingale. The martingale convergence theorem gives almost sure and L1 convergence as t→∞.
Its limit can also be identified pathwise. On {T<∞} the stopped process is eventually constant at e−λTu(XT​). On {T=∞} its absolute value is at most Ke−λt and hence tends to zero. Thus the limit is e−λTu(XT​)1{T<∞}​.
