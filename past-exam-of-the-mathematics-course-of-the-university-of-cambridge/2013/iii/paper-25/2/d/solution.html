Multiply the representation in part (c) by ∫0∞​αu​dWu​ and take expectations. This Itô integral has mean zero, and the bilinear form of the Itô isometry gives
E[ϕ(WT​)∫0∞​αu​dWu​]=E∫0∞​βu​αu​du.
(22)
Equating this with part (b), and writing both ordinary integrals with the same time variable, yields
E∫0∞​(βu​−ϕ′(WT​)1{u≤T}​)αu​du=0.​
(23)
All terms are integrable by the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, the assumed square integrability of α, and boundedness of ϕ′. This is an orthogonality statement against predictable processes; its second term need not itself be predictable.
