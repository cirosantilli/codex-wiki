Independent Poisson processes of flow arrivals and independent exponential distributions of document sizes make the count vector a continuous-time Markov chain. By the memoryless property, a surviving residual size with exponential distribution still has rate μr​ per unit of transferred data, so each route-r flow completes at instantaneous rate μr​xr​(n).
