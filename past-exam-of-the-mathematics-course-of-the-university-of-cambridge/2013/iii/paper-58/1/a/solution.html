The PDF's small superscript is essential: f(x)=axmodN. Interpret r as the least positive period, the multiplicative order of a modulo N. If one permitted an unspecified nonminimal period, the answer would not be identifiable; for example, a=1,N=8 gives the same constant function with period one or period eight. Under the promised periodic modular-exponential interpretation, ar≡1(modN), so a is a unit. Thus
ax≡ay(modN)⟺r∣(x−y).
(1)
In particular, different residue classes modulo r have different function values, which is necessary for the usual quantum period finding coset argument.
Prepare N−1/2∑x​∣x⟩∣1⟩ by applying the quantum Fourier transform to the first register. Use repeated squaring to precompute a2jmodN, then multiply the value register by that known number controlled on bit j of x. These controlled modular multiplications are reversible because a is a unit; their inverses use the modular inverses of the same constants. This produces N−1/2∑x​∣x⟩∣axmodN⟩ with a polynomial number of the assumed arithmetic operations. Known work registers can be uncomputed.
Measure the value register. Since r∣N, its fiber is exactly one coset, and the first register becomes
N/r​1​∑ℓ=0N/r−1​∣x0​+ℓr⟩.

The quantum Fourier transform of a periodic coset state is supported uniformly on the r outcomes c=jN/r, 0≤j<r. Indeed the inner geometric series ∑ℓ=0N/r−1​e2πiℓrc/N is zero unless (N/r)∣c, and each allowed amplitude has modulus 1/r​.

This is two-sample exact period recovery. The Chinese remainder theorem makes divisibility of independent uniform residues independent across the different prime divisors of r. For each such prime ℓ, both j1​ and j2​ are divisible by ℓ with probability ℓ−2. Therefore
Pr(r=r)=∏ℓ primeℓ∣r​​(1−ℓ−2)≥∏ℓ prime​(1−ℓ−2)=ζ(2)1​=π26​>21​.

The number-theoretic facts used here are the Chinese remainder theorem, the Euler product for the Riemann zeta function at two, and ζ(2)=π2/6. The candidate can also be certified by ar≡1(modN): it divides the least period, so this equality holds exactly when it is the full period. Each preparation, reversible evaluation, QFT, measurement and greatest common divisor calculation has the assumed or standard polynomial time cost in logN. Two runs suffice for the desired constant success probability. Quantum period finding recovers the least period with probability at least 6/π2 in polynomial time.
