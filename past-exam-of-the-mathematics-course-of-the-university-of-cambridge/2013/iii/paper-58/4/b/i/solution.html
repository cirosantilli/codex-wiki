For a unitary operator W, WW†=I. Thus for every nonnegative integer j,
(W†HW)j=W†HjW.
(28)
Insert this into the convergent matrix exponential series in the finite-dimensional qubit setting:
W†eiHW=∑j=0∞​j!ij​W†HjW=∑j=0∞​j!ij​(W†HW)j.
(29)
Consequently unitary conjugation commutes with the exponential:
W†eiHW=eiW†HW.​
(30)
For an unbounded self-adjoint Hamiltonian, the same identity follows from the spectral theorem for normal operators, with the domain transported by W; no unbounded power-series manipulation is needed.
