The crude monadicity theorem in its reflexive-coequalizer form says: if F⊣G:D→C, G is a conservative functor, D has reflexive coequalizers, and G preserves them, then the Eilenberg-Moore comparison functor K:D→CT, for T=GF, is an equivalence of categories. Requiring all coequalizers to exist and be preserved is a stronger sufficient form.
For an algebra (A,a), form in D the coequalizer
FTAFa​εFA​​​FAq​L(A,a).
(16)
The pair is a reflexive pair with common section FηA​: both composites are the identity by the algebra unit law and the triangle identity. An arrow u:FA→B transposes to v=GuηA​:A→GB. The two composites uFa and uεFA​ transpose to va and GεB​Tv, respectively. Indeed the latter transpose is GuμA​ηTA​=Gu, while GεB​Tv=GuμA​TηA​=Gu by counit naturality. Thus u equalizes the pair exactly when v is an monad algebra morphism (A,a)→K(B). The coequalizer property gives natural bijections
D(L(A,a),B)≅CT((A,a),K(B)).
(17)
These define L on arrows by uniqueness, so L⊣K. Its unit has underlying arrow β=GqηA​:A→GL(A,a).
By preservation, Gq coequalizes Ta and μA​ in C. The action a:TA→A is a split coequalizer of that pair: take s=ηA​ and t=ηTA​, with μA​t=1TA​ and Tat=ηA​a. Thus there is a unique isomorphism α:GL(A,a)→A with αGq=a. We have αβ=aηA​=1A​. Also
Gq=GqμA​ηTA​=GqTaηTA​=GqηA​a=βa.
(18)
Consequently βαGq=Gq, and epimorphic cancellation gives βα=1. The adjunction unit β is an monad algebra morphism; its invertible underlying arrow has an algebra-morphism inverse. Thus the unit of L⊣K is an isomorphism.
For its counit cB​:LK(B)→B, the triangle identity gives K(cB​)βK(B)​=1K(B)​. Hence K(cB​), and therefore G(cB​), is an isomorphism. Since G is a conservative functor, cB​ is an isomorphism too. Both unit and counit of L⊣K are invertible, which proves the claimed equivalence. This proof uses only reflexive coequalizers in D and split coequalizers in C.
