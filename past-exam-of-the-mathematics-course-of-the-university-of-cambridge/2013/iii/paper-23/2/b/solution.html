For nonprincipal primitive Dirichlet characters, termwise Mellin transformation initially in ℜs>1 gives
Λ(s,χ)=21​∫0∞​θχ​(x)x(s+a)/2xdx​.
(13)
The Dirichlet character theta function decays exponentially at infinity; its transformation makes it decay faster than any power at zero. Hence the integral is entire in s. For even parity, substitute x=1/y and the theta transformation to obtain
Λ(s,χ)=εχ​Λ(1−s,χ​).​
(14)
The same calculation with the extra x−1 power gives the odd functional equation with its corresponding root number.
The gamma function has no zeros and has simple poles at nonpositive integers. Thus the nontrivial zeros of L and Λ coincide with multiplicities. The trivial zeros of a Dirichlet L-function are 0,−2,−4,… for a nonprincipal even character, and −1,−3,−5,… for an odd character. They cancel the gamma poles and are not zeros of Λ: the functional equation takes these points to the zero-free right-hand region, including the standard nonvanishing of nonprincipal Dirichlet L-functions at one at the even endpoint. The canceled zeros are simple.
The principal primitive Dirichlet character has conductor of a Dirichlet character equal to one and L=ζ. In that case Λ=π−s/2Γ(s/2)ζ(s) is meromorphic with poles at zero and one. Its canceled trivial zeros begin at −2, while ζ(0)=−1/2 is not zero. Multiplication by s(s−1)/2 produces the entire Riemann xi function used below.
