Use natural units and the Minkowski metric (+−−−), and work at tree level with on-shell final particles. Write M=mH​ and, for a final particle of mass m, βm​=1−4m2/M2​. In the Higgs boson rest frame the Lorentz-invariant phase-space measure reduces to
dΦ2​=16π2M∣k∣​dΩ=32π2βm​​dΩ,∫dΦ2​=8πβm​​.
(13)
To obtain this, integrate the momentum delta function to set k2​=−k1​; the energy delta function is δ(M−2k2+m2​), whose radial Jacobian is E/(2k). Hence for an angle-independent final-state spin sum S=∑∣M∣2,
Γ=16πMβm​S​.
(14)
There is no initial-state spin average for a scalar, and neither charged-particle pair here requires an identical-particle factor of 1/2.
For Higgs decay to two W bosons, the Feynman vertex gives M=(2mW2​/v)ε1∗​⋅ε2∗​, up to an overall phase. Contracting the two massive vector polarization sums gives
SW​=v24mW4​​[2+mW4​(k1​⋅k2​)2​],k1​⋅k2​=2M2−2mW2​​.
(15)
The constant 2 follows from 4−1−1 in the contraction, with the last term coming from the two momentum projectors. Putting xW​=mW2​/M2, the full massive result is
Γ(H→W+W−)=16πv2M3​1−4xW​​(1−4xW​+12xW2​),M≥2mW​.​
(16)
The on-shell two-body width is zero below this threshold; decays through off-shell W bosons into more particles are different channels.
For Higgs decay to a fermion pair, put yb​=mb​/v. The amplitude is M=−yb​uˉ(k1​)v(k2​), up to an overall phase and a color Kronecker delta. The fermion spin sums and gamma-matrix trace give
Sb​=Nc​yb2​tr[(k1​+mb​)(k2​−mb​)]=4Nc​yb2​(k1​⋅k2​−mb2​)=2Nc​yb2​(M2−4mb2​).
(17)
The color multiplicity in a decay width is Nc​=3: only a quark and antiquark with matching colors contribute, so the factor is three rather than nine. Therefore
Γ(H→bˉb)=8πv23mb2​M​(1−M24mb2​​)3/2,M≥2mb​.​
(18)
Again the on-shell two-body width is zero below threshold. This is a partonic tree-level answer with every mass retained; hadronization is outside the specified calculation.
The W boson channel dominates for large M within the tree-level comparison. The widths scale as M3/v2 and mb2​M/v2, respectively, and
Γ(H→bˉb)Γ(H→W+W−)​∼6mb2​M2​.​
(19)
The enhancement comes from longitudinal polarization of a massive vector boson: its polarization vector grows as momentum divided by mW​. Thus the longitudinal pair survives the apparently small mW2​ factor in the interaction. At masses so large that the scalar sector is strongly coupled, the tree-level extrapolation itself needs corrections.
