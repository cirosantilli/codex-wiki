Variation of the inverse metric relation gacgcb​=δab​ gives δgab=−hab. Hence the metric variation of scalar curvature is
δR=−Rabhab​+gabδRab​.
(31)
Put h=gabhab​. The connection variation gives the contractions
gabCcab​=∇a​hca−21​∇ch,Ccac​=21​∇a​h.
(32)
Using metric compatibility and the Palatini identity,
gabδRab​=∇c​∇a​hca−∇c​∇ch.
(33)
Renaming dummy indices gives
δR=−Rabhab​+∇a∇bhab​−∇c∇c​(gabhab​).​
(34)
No interchange of covariant derivatives on a tensor is needed in this derivation, so no hidden curvature-commutator term is discarded.
