Write the Cartesian comonad as (G,ε,δ), with G preserving finite limits. A coalgebra for a comonad is (A,a) with a:A→GA, εA​a=1A​ and δA​a=Gaa. Its forgetful functor U:EG→E is faithful, creates finite limits, and has the cofree coalgebra right adjoint R(X)=(GX,δX​). We construct the two remaining topos structures explicitly.
For exponential objects, take coalgebras (A,a),(B,b) and start with R(BA). Put X=G(BA), with structure ξ=δBA​, and let
e:X×A⟶B,e=ev(εBA​×1A​).
(4)
Transpose the following two maps X×A→GB into maps u,v:X→(GB)A:
be,Ge∘(ξ×a),
(5)
using G(X×A)≅GX×GA. The cofree adjunction transposes u,v once more into coalgebra morphisms R(BA)⇉R((GB)A). Let Q be their equalizer. A coalgebra map (C,c)→R(BA) corresponds to an arbitrary ambient map h:C×A→B. It factors through Q precisely when
bh=Gh∘(c×a),
(6)
which is exactly the condition that h be a coalgebra morphism. Therefore Q represents EG(C×A,B) and is the required exponential in a coalgebra topos.
For the subobject classifier, let κ:GΩ→Ω classify the mono G⊤:G1≅1↪GΩ. In the cofree coalgebra RΩ, form
ΩG​=Eq(1RΩ​,GκδΩ​).
(7)
Both arrows are coalgebra morphisms. The cofree transpose of ⊤:1→Ω factors through this equalizer and gives its true arrow. To verify classification, let S↪X have ambient characteristic map χ:X→Ω. It supports a subcoalgebra of (X,x) exactly when it is invariant under x, equivalently
S=x−1(GS),χ=κGχx.
(8)
The counit proves the reverse containment in the first equation; the forward containment supplies the restricted structure map, whose coalgebra laws follow through the mono. Under the cofree adjunction, the second equation says exactly that Gχx:X→RΩ factors through ΩG​. Pulling back its true arrow recovers S, since κGχx=χ. This proves the universal property of the subobject classifier of a coalgebra topos. Hence EG is a topos.
Now let f:E→F be a geometric morphism, with L=f∗⊣H=f∗​. The comonad G=LH is Cartesian: L preserves finite limits and the right adjoint H preserves limits. Put D=EG, already a topos. The forgetful adjunction U⊣R defines p:E→D with p∗=U, so p∗ is faithful.
The comparison functor
K:F→D,K(Y)=(LY,LηY​)
(9)
preserves finite limits. It has a right adjoint J, given on a coalgebra (A,a) by
J(A,a)=Eq(Ha,ηHA​:HA⇉HGA).
(10)
Indeed, the transposed arrow Y→HA corresponds to a coalgebra map KY→(A,a) exactly when it equalizes these two maps. Applying the finite-limit-preserving L shows that LJ(A,a) is the equalizer of
Ga,δA​:GA⇉G2A.
(11)
That equalizer is a:A→GA. If t:Z→GA equalizes the pair, then aεA​t=εGA​Gat=εGA​δA​t=t, proving the claimed universal property. Consequently the counit KJ(A,a)→(A,a) is invertible. The fully faithful adjoint criterion makes J full and faithful.
Thus K⊣J defines a geometric embedding i:D→F, and UK=L identifies the composite with f. The requested factorization is
E p ​D i ​F,f=i∘p,​
(12)
where p is a surjective geometric morphism and i∗​=J is full and faithful.
