Take the normal vector outward from the cylinder-and-cone solid. The surface consists of the cylindrical side and conical roof, with no bottom disk; this convention fixes the otherwise unspecified orientation of a surface. The boundary is the radius-two circle at z=−2. Reversing every normal vector reverses the final sign.
For F=(yz2,0,0), its curl is (0,2yz,−z2). For the cylindrical parametrized surface, use rc​=(2cosθ,2sinθ,z), with −2≤z≤2, and use rc,θ​×rc,z​=(2cosθ,2sinθ,0) as the outward area vector. Its flux is

The total surface integral is therefore −16π with the outward convention, or +16π for the opposite convention.
For Stokes theorem, the induced bottom-circle orientation is counterclockwise as viewed from above, opposite to the orientation of an outward bottom cap. Use r=(2cosθ,2sinθ,−2) with increasing θ. Then
∮∂S​F⋅dr=∫02π​(8sinθ)(−2sinθ)dθ=−16π,​

agreeing with the two parametrized fluxes. The seam at z=2 is internal and cancels between the two pieces; the cone tip does not supply another boundary curve.
