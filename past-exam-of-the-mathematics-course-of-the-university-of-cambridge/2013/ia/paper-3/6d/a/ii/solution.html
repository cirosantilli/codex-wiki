To rule out all conjugators when p=5, suppose PAP−1=B and write P=(rt​su​). Comparing PA=BP gives t=0 and r=3u. Its determinant condition is ru=1, so r2=3. The nonzero squares modulo five are 1 and 4, and therefore no such P exists. The two matrices are not conjugate in SL2​(5). This is the square-class obstruction in unipotent conjugacy in SL2 over a finite field; checking only diagonal conjugators would not by itself exclude every possible conjugator.
