B(r)=4πμ0​I​∮C​∣r−r′∣3dr′×(r−r′)​.​

This is exactly the sign convention in the printed form using r′−r.
Let v=(r−r′)/∣r−r′∣3. Away from the wire, ∇⋅v=0. The supplied divergence and curl of a cross product therefore gives ∇×(dr′×v)=−(dr′⋅∇r​)v. But ∇r​v=−∇r′​v, so the integrand is the total differential of v along the source loop. Its closed-loop integral is zero. Thus ∇×B=0 at every point outside C, as demanded by the current-free magnetostatic Ampère's law.
