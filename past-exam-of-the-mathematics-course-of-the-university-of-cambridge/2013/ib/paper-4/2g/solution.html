The conjugacy class of x is its orbit under the conjugation action, and its stabilizer is the centralizer CG​(x). The orbit-stabilizer theorem gives
∣ClG​(x)∣=[G:CG​(x)].
(3)
By Lagrange's theorem, the order of the centralizer divides pn=∣G∣, so the index is a power of p. A conjugacy class has size one exactly when its element belongs to the center of a group.
Separate these singleton classes in the class equation:
pn=∣Z(G)∣+∑x∈/Z(G)​[G:CG​(x)],
(4)
where the sum takes one representative of each nonsingleton conjugacy class. Every summand is divisible by p. Thus p∣∣Z(G)∣, and the identity ensures ∣Z(G)∣>0. Consequently ∣Z(G)∣≥p. This is the nontrivial center of a finite p-group property.
