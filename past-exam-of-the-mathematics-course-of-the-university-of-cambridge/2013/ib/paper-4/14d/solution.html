On the outer boundary this is one. On the inner boundary, ∣z∣2=4x/5, and the ratio is four. More directly, inside the outer circle the numerator exceeds the denominator by 3(1−∣z∣2)>0, whereas outside the inner circle four times the denominator exceeds the numerator by 3(5∣z∣2−4x)>0. Thus the image is 1<∣w∣<2. The inverse transformation is z=(w−2)/(2w−1), so this is a bijective conformal map onto that annulus.
