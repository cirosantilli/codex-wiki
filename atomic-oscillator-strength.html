Atomic oscillator strength (fij​)

 Home Physics Atomic physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
An atomic oscillator strength is a dimensionless measure of an optical transition's absorption strength relative to a classical electron oscillator. It is related to its spontaneous radiative rate through the transition energy and the statistical weights of the two levels. The absorption convention fij​ fixes the lower state i and upper state j; interchanging that convention can lose a weight factor.
