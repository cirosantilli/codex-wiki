Morrey inequality on a cube (∣v(y)−vQ​∣≤Cr1−n/p∥Dv∥Lp(Q)​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space Sobolev embedding theorem

For a cube Q of side length r in Rn, p>n, and a continuously differentiable function v, its average vQ​=∣Q∣−1∫Q​v satisfies
∣v(y)−vQ​∣≤Cn,p​r1−n/p∥Dv∥Lp(Q)​(y∈Q).
(32)
Averaging the fundamental theorem of calculus along a line segment from y to z∈Q and changing variables gives ∣v(y)−vQ​∣≤Cn​∫Q​∣Dv(w)∣∣w−y∣1−ndw. The Holder inequality applies because (n−1)p′<n, and the kernel norm is O(r1−n/p). Approximation extends the estimate to the continuous representative of a Sobolev space function. On Rn it yields a Hölder continuous function of exponent 1−n/p representing every W1,p class.
