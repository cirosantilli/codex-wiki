Kolmogorov first similarity hypothesis (S2​(r)=vη2​F(r/η))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Energy cascade Kolmogorov 1941 theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
In the locally isotropic equilibrium range, the small-scale velocity increment statistics depend on the separation, mean viscous dissipation and kinematic viscosity, independently of large-scale forcing details. Dimensional analysis leaves the Kolmogorov microscales as normalization scales and gives the displayed second-order longitudinal structure function. Universality of F is part of the hypothesis, not something proved by dimensional analysis.
