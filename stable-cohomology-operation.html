Stable cohomology operation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Cohomology Cohomology operation
OurBigBook.com Words: 497 Articles: 10
A cohomology operation is stable if it commutes with the suspension isomorphism on reduced cohomology. Ordinary cup products become zero on a suspension, but stable operations can still connect its nonzero classes. The Steenrod squares and Steenrod reduced powers are examples.
