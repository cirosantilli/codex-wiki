Diffraction thermal-expansion thermometry (d(2θ)/dT=−2αL​tanθ)

OurBigBook.com Words: 57
At fixed radiation wavelength, differentiating Bragg's law for an isotropically expanding cubic specimen gives the displayed angular sensitivity, with angles in radians. Accurate temperature inference requires a calibrated lattice-parameter versus temperature curve, stable wavelength and angular zero, and separation of thermal expansion from stress or composition shifts. Counting statistics can permit centroid precision finer than the observed diffraction-line width.
