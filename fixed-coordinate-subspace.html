Fixed coordinate subspace (Rkp​={θ:θj​=0 (j>k)})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Vector subspace
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The fixed coordinate subspace of dimension k in Rp consists of vectors whose coordinates after the first k vanish. Restricting a quadratic form to it uses only the leading k by k matrix block. It specifies one fixed support; allowing every support of size k would introduce another combinatorial counting step.
