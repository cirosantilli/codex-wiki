Spectral projector

OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A spectral projector is the value E(B) of the projection-valued measure of a normal operator on a measurable part B of its spectrum. In finite dimension, the spectral projector onto an eigenspace extracts the corresponding eigenvector component.
