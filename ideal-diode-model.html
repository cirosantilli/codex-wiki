Ideal diode model (ID​≥0, VD​≤0, ID​VD​=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Diode
OurBigBook.com Words: 122
The ideal diode model sets the conducting electric potential difference to zero and the blocking electric current to zero. With the stated orientation,
conducting:blocking:​VD​=0,ID​≥0,ID​=0,VD​≤0.​
(2)
[ref]
The surrounding electrical circuit chooses the state and remaining value. The two branches meet at VD​=ID​=0. Their product vanishes, so the ideal diode dissipates no power: PD​=VD​ID​=0.
The ideal diode model omits forward drop, reverse leakage, diode reverse breakdown and switching delay.
