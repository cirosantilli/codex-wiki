Coulomb blockade

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Potential barrier Quantum tunnelling
OurBigBook.com Words: 269 Articles: 6
Suppression of tunnelling because adding or removing a discrete island charge costs more energy than the accessible transport and thermal energy window. Weak junction coupling and small island capacitance make it observable.
