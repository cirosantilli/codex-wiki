Relative safety loading (ρ=c/(λμ)−1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Classical risk model
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
If the expected claim outflow per unit time is λμ, the relative safety loading is ρ=c/(λμ)−1. Positive loading means expected premium income exceeds expected claim outflow. This is the net profit condition in the classical risk model.
