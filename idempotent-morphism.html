Idempotent morphism

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category
OurBigBook.com Words: 283 Articles: 5
An endomorphism e:A→A is idempotent when e2=e. It splits when e=ir for maps r:A→B, i:B→A satisfying ri=1B​.
