OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For x′′+ε∣x∣x′+x=0 and unit initial amplitude, averaging uses ⟨∣cosψ∣sin2ψ⟩=2/(3π). Thus AT​=−2A2/(3π) and the displayed algebraic envelope follows. The leading phase has no first-order drift. The dissipation is amplitude dependent, so an exponential envelope with a constant decay rate would give the wrong long-time scale.
