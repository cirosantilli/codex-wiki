Representation of a functor

 ... Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Functor Functor category Representable functor
OurBigBook.com Words: 149 Articles: 3
A representation of F:C→Set is an object A together with a universal element a∈F(A) such that
C(A,B)⟶F(B),f⟼F(f)(a)
(3)
is a bijection for every B, naturally in B. Equivalently, it is a specified natural isomorphism C(A,−)≅F.
