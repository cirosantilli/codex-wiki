Radiometer thermal link (Qlink​≃G(T−Tb​), τ=C/G)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Radiometry Electrical substitution radiometer Electrical substitution heat balance
OurBigBook.com Words: 97
A radiometer thermal link is the intentional path for thermal conduction from receiver to reference block. Near a chosen operating point, write Qlink​≃G(T−Tb​) with local conductance G in WK−1. A weaker link increases the steady temperature rise per added power, δT≃δP/G, but also increases the uncontrolled relaxation time τ=C/G when the link dominates other heat transfer. These are local linear approximations: actual thermal conductivity depends on temperature, and active feedback control introduces its own response. NIST explicitly describes the tradeoff between weaker links and smaller receiver heat capacity.[ref]
