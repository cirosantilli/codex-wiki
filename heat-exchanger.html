Heat exchanger

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Heat transfer
OurBigBook.com Words: 50 Articles: 1
A heat exchanger transfers heat between a fluid and another fluid or its surroundings. In measuring the Joule-Thomson effect with a porous plug, an upstream coil restores the inlet temperature before the measurement throttle; it belongs outside the approximately insulated measurement control volume.
