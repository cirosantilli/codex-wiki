Gauge-covariant time translation (δAν​=αF0ν​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic vector potential Gauge transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For the Maxwell field, the variation δAν​=αF0ν​=α(∂0​Aν​−∂ν​A0​) is time translation combined with a gauge transformation. With constant α, δFμν​=α∂0​Fμν​, and the Lagrangian changes by α∂0​L. Its Noether current is directly the time component of the gauge-invariant Maxwell stress-energy tensor, providing the physical energy density without a potential-dependent improvement step.
