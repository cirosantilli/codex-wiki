Isothermal sphere (r−2[r2(lnρ)′]′=−4πGρ/K)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Ideal-gas atmosphere in uniform gravity Hydrostatic equilibrium
OurBigBook.com Words: 552 Articles: 8
An isothermal sphere is a spherically symmetric self-gravitating gas equilibrium with equation of state p=Kρ and constant K. The hydrostatic equilibrium equations p′=−GMρ/r2 and M′=4πr2ρ yield the title equation. Its regular finite-central-density solution is distinct from the singular isothermal sphere. A finite outer radius requires confinement because the regular density does not vanish at finite radius.
