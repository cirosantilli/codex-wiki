Reliable improvement

 Home Science Psychology Psychometrics Reliable change index
OurBigBook.com Words: 57 Articles: 1
Improvement exceeding a specified measurement-error criterion, with any additional no-deterioration rule made explicit. It differs from crossing a symptom cutoff and from improvement attributable to treatment.
