Branching from B3 to A3 (7↦6⊕1,8↦4⊕4)

OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For B3 root system simple roots e1​−e2​,e2​−e3​,e3​, choose the D3 root system simple roots in chain order e2​−e3​,e1​−e2​,e2​+e3​. The Dynkin labels project by (a,b,c)↦(b,a,b+c). This separates the vector weights {0,±ei​} into the six-dimensional exterior square of the defining SU(4) representation and a singlet representation. The spinor cube vertices 21​(±e1​±e2​±e3​) split into its defining representation and its dual representation.
