SO(3) group (SO(3))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Orthogonal group Special orthogonal group
OurBigBook.com Words: 62 Articles: 1
The SO(3) group consists of real three-by-three orthogonal matrices with determinant one. It acts by rotation in three dimensions. Its group manifold is RP3, since the Adjoint double cover from SU(2) to SO(3) identifies antipodal points of the three-sphere.
