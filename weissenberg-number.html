Weissenberg number (Wi=τγ˙​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
The Weissenberg number measures deformation accumulated over a viscoelastic relaxation time, often as the product of that time and a shear rate or an extension rate. Its precise rate convention should be stated. Unlike a Deborah number based on externally imposed temporal variation, it can remain nonzero in a steady flow. A weak-flow constitutive expansion generally requires both slow temporal change and small deformation over the memory time.
