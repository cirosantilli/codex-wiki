Spectral density of a stationary process (fX​(λ))

When its autocovariance is absolutely summable, a weakly stationary process has spectral density f(λ)=(2π)−1∑h​γ(h)e−ihλ. The inverse relation is γ(h)=∫−ππ​eihλf(λ)dλ. A linear filter multiplies this density by the squared modulus of its frequency response.
