Normal ultrafilter on small subsets

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Filter (set theory) Ultrafilter Fine ultrafilter
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
An ultrafilter U on Pκ​(λ) is normal when each function with f(x)∈x on a U-large set is constant on a U-large subset. This is the small-subset version of normality; together with fineness and kappa-completeness it gives the measure characterization of a supercompact cardinal.
