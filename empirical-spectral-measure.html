Empirical spectral measure (LN​=N−1∑i=1N​δλi​​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Random matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
For an N-by-N Hermitian matrix, the empirical spectral measure puts mass 1/N at each eigenvalue, counted with multiplicity. It is a probability measure. Integrating a test function against it gives the average of that function evaluated at the eigenvalues.
