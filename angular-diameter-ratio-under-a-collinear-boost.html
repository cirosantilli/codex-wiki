Angular-diameter ratio under a collinear boost (∣v∣/c=(r2−1)/(r2+1))

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Relativistic aberration
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For two coincident observers with relative motion along the sightline to a small distant source, its angular diameter changes by a rapidity factor. A boost toward the source compresses its incoming angular cone: tan(ϑ′/2)=e−ηtan(ϑ/2) for angular radius ϑ. For a small source the larger-to-smaller diameter ratio is r=e∣η∣, giving the displayed relative speed. A finite angular size requires the half-angle formula, and arbitrary transverse relative motion cannot be inferred from one diameter ratio alone.
