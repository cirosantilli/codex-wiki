Conditional-expectation martingale

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale
OurBigBook.com Words: 78 Articles: 1
For an integrable random variable Z and an increasing filtration (Fn​), the process Mn​=E[Z∣Fn​] is a martingale. The tower property of conditional expectation gives the martingale identity.
