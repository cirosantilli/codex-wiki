Neutrino decoupling

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmological entropy conservation Thermal decoupling in cosmology Cosmic neutrino background
OurBigBook.com Words: 189 Articles: 4
Neutrino decoupling occurs when weak interactions become slower than cosmic expansion. The neutrino distribution then free streams and cools as Tν​∝a−1.
