Rapid artery-ventricle pressure equilibration (p^​a​=[Ca​pa+​+V+−V0​]/(Ca​+1/Es​))

 Home Biology Physiology Heart Left ventricle Ventricular elastance
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
If an open aortic valve equilibrates pressure much faster than peripheral outflow, the arteries and ventricle share a common pressure while their combined blood volume is conserved during the short adjustment. Let arterial vascular compliance be Ca​ and systolic ventricular elastance be Es​. Equating the initial and final arterial-plus-ventricular stored volumes gives the displayed pressure. Subsequent peripheral drainage sees the combined compliance Ca​+1/Es​; during filling a closed aortic valve removes the ventricular contribution.
