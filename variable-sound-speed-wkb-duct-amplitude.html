Variable-sound-speed WKB duct amplitude (A=Ck0​/kN​,N=∫−RR​F2dy)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis WKB approximation WKB amplitude in a slowly varying duct
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For ∇⋅(k0​(X)−2∇p)+p=0 in a slowly varying rigid duct, a real local transverse normal mode F with squared norm N(X) has longitudinal wavenumber k(X). The WKB approximation is p=A(X)F(y,X)exp[−iϵ−1∫Xk(ξ)dξ]. The moving-wall Neumann boundary condition and next-order solvability give 2kNk0−2​AX​+(kNk0−2​)X​A=0, hence the displayed amplitude. For F=cos(nπy/R), N=R for n≥1 and N=2R for n=0. The factor k0​ is essential when the coefficient in the divergence-form equation varies. This approximation requires a mode separated from cutoff and from other modes.
