Four-dimensional Majorana bilinear vanishing criterion ((Cγ(n))T=−(−1)n(n+1)/2Cγ(n))

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Weyl spinor Majorana spinor
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For a Majorana spinor λ with odd Grassmann variables as components, λTMλ sees only the antisymmetric part of M. With CT=−C and (γa)T=−CγaC−1, reversing an antisymmetrized gamma product gives the displayed transpose rule. Thus the same-spinor vector and bivector bilinears vanish, whereas scalar, pseudoscalar and three-form bilinears can be nonzero. Four-dimensional Hodge duality gives the same vanishing result for a bivector multiplied by γ5​. Commuting spinors instead see the symmetric part, so their selection rule is different.
