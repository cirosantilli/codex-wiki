Azimuth (η)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Horizontal coordinate system
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
Azimuth is the angle about the local vertical measured in the astronomical horizon. A common convention increases eastward from north; some astronomical formulae use westward from north. The convention changes the sign in the hour angle transformation.
