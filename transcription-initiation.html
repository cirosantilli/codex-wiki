Transcription initiation

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Gene expression Transcription (biology)
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 2
The initial stage of transcription, including RNA polymerase association with a promoter, local DNA opening, and the first RNA synthesis events before productive elongation. Open-complex formation, abortive synthesis and promoter escape are distinct transitions; an unwound bubble need not disappear when escape occurs.
