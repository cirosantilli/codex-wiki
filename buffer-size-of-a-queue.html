Buffer size of a queue (b)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory Queue (queueing theory)
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
The maximum stored work or number of waiting jobs, according to the model's convention. A bufferless queue has no room to retain excess input for a later slot.
