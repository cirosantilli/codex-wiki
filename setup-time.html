Setup time (tsetup​)

 Home Electrical engineering Digital electronics Sequential logic Flip-flop Setup and hold time
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The minimum interval before an active clock edge during which the data input of a flip-flop must remain stable for specified operation. It constrains the latest arrival of new data at the receiving flip-flop.
