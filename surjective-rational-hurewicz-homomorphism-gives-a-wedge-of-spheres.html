Surjective rational Hurewicz homomorphism gives a wedge of spheres

OurBigBook.com Words: 63
If X is simply connected and h∗​:π∗​(X)⊗Q→H∗​(X;Q) is onto, choose a homogeneous homology basis and representing sphere maps into XQ​. Their wedge sum gives a rational homology equivalence, hence a rational homotopy equivalence by the rational Whitehead theorem. All positive cup products therefore vanish. In a minimal model, the induced map from cohomology to the indecomposable quotient is injective; replacing a set of generators by cocycle representatives produces a quasi-isomorphism to the square-zero cohomology algebra, proving formality of a topological space.
