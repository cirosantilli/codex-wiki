Wavelength (λ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Wavenumber
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
The wavelength is the spatial period of a monochromatic wave. For angular wavenumber k, it is λ=2π/∣k∣.
