For HR​=λ(kx​σy​−ky​σx​) with λ>0, the lower- and upper-band spinors of unit norm can be chosen as
u−​=2​1​((ky​+ikx​)/k1​),u+​=2​1​(−(ky​+ikx​)/k1​),k>0.
(60)
Their spin expectation values are ⟨S⟩±​=±(ℏ/2)(−ky​,kx​,0)/k. They are tangential to circles of constant momentum magnitude, opposite between branches, and reversed at opposite momenta as required by time-reversal symmetry. When EF​>0, the outer contour belongs to the lower branch and the inner one to the upper branch. For EF​<0 the two Fermi contours both belong to the lower branch and have the same tangential spin orientation at a given angle in polar coordinates. At k=0 the Hamiltonian has quantum degeneracy in spin, and no unique spin texture direction is selected.
