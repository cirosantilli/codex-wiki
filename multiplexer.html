Multiplexer (mux(s,a,b)=(¬s∧a)∨(s∧b))

 ... Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Circuit complexity Boolean circuit Combinational logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
A selector that routes one of several inputs to its output according to control bits. The displayed two-input version selects a when s=0 and b when s=1. A word selector applies the same control bit to parallel one-bit multiplexers; its circuit size grows with the word width while its added circuit depth remains constant.
