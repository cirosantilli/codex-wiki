Cardinal arithmetic

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cardinal number
OurBigBook.com Words: 788 Articles: 18
Cardinal sums are cardinalities of disjoint unions, products are cardinalities of Cartesian products, and exponentials κλ count functions from a set of size λ to one of size κ.
