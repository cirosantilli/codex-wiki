External vertex boundary (∂v​S)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Graph neighbourhood
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The external vertex boundary of a vertex set S is
∂v​S=N(S)∖S=N[S]∖S.
(41)
For comparisons at fixed ∣S∣, minimizing ∣∂v​S∣ is equivalent to minimizing ∣N[S]∣.
