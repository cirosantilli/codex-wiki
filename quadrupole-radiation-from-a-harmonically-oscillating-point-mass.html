Quadrupole radiation from a harmonically oscillating point mass (⟨P⟩=15c516Gm2L4ω6​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Gravitational wave Mass quadrupole moment Quadrupole formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For a point mass moving as z=Lcosωt along one fixed axis, the trace-free mass quadrupole moment is mL2cos2ωtdiag(−1/3,−1/3,2/3). Its radiating component has twice the source angular frequency. The quadrupole formula gives ⟨P⟩=16Gm2L4ω6/(15c5) for this contribution in the leading slow-motion weak-field approximation. Any physical driver or support has its own quadrupole contribution; this expression is for the prescribed mass alone.
