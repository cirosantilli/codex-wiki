Feature-conjunction account of perirhinal cortex

 Home Biology Neuroscience Brain Medial temporal lobe Perirhinal cortex
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
An account in which the region represents complex combinations of object features, reducing interference when individual features are shared across objects. It predicts effects in both memory and demanding perceptual discriminations, rather than restricting its contribution to a separate memory box.
