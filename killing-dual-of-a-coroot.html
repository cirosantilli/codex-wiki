Killing dual of a coroot (B(Hα​)=∑β∈Φ​β(Hα​)β)

Let B(H)=B(H,⋅) be the Killing form identification of a Cartan subalgebra with its dual. The root-space decomposition gives B(H,K)=∑β∈Φ​β(H)β(K), and therefore the displayed root expansion. On the real coroot span its coefficients are real, proving B(hR​)⊆RP. For sl3​ and H12​=E11​−E22​, the formula gives B(H12​)=6(L1​−L2​). The normalization is B(X,Y)=tr(adXadY), not a rescaled trace form.
