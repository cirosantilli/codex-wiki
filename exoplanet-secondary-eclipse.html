Exoplanet secondary eclipse

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 314 Articles: 8
Secondary eclipses are one of the methods of detecting exoplanets and also probe planetary emission.
During secondary eclipse the planet passes behind its star. The lost system light measures the planet-star flux ratio from reflected starlight and planetary thermal emission.
