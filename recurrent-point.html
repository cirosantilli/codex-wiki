Recurrent point (Tnj​x→x,nj​→∞)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Topological dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 107 Articles: 1
A point of a dynamical system is recurrent if some sequence of positive times tending to infinity returns its orbit arbitrarily close to the point. In a compact metric space, this is equivalent to the displayed condition. A periodic point is recurrent, but a recurrent point need not be periodic.
