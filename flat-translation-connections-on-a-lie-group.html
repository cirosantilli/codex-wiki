Flat translation connections on a Lie group (∇X−​Y=0,∇X+​Y=[X,Y])

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Left and right translation on a Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 183 Articles: 2
Declare a global left-invariant, respectively right-invariant, frame parallel to obtain two flat affine connections on a Lie group. For left-invariant vector fields, their derivatives are as displayed and their torsion tensors are −[X,Y] and +[X,Y]. The tensors vanish on Abelian groups; two genuinely torsionful connections are not asserted there.
