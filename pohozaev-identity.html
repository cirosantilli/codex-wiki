Pohozaev identity

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Nonlinear analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 59 Articles: 1
A Pohozaev identity is an integral identity for a nonlinear partial differential equation, obtained by multiplying the equation by a scaling vector field such as x⋅∇u and applying integration by parts. It relates kinetic, potential, and nonlinear energies.
