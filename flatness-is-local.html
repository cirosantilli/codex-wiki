Flatness is local

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Flat module
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
An R-module M is flat if and only if Mp​ is flat over Rp​ for every prime ideal p, equivalently for every maximal ideal.
