Contrastive representation learning

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical learning Self-supervised learning
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
Learning a representation by relating selected matching examples or views and contrasting them with other examples. The augmentation, matching and sampling rules determine which distinctions are retained. Brain-like prediction in a trained model does not establish the exact objective or plasticity mechanism in cortex.[ref]
