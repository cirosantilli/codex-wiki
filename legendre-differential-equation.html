Legendre differential equation

OurBigBook.com Words: 353 Articles: 11
The equation (1−x2)y′′−2xy′+ℓ(ℓ+1)y=0 has polynomial solutions for nonnegative integer ℓ.
The equation (1−x2)y′′−2xy′+ℓ(ℓ+1)y=0 has the Legendre polynomial of degree ℓ as its normalized regular polynomial solution when ℓ is a nonnegative integer.
