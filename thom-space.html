Thom space (Th(ξ)=D(ξ)/S(ξ))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 127 Articles: 1
For a real vector bundle over a compact base, its Thom space is the quotient of its disk bundle by its sphere bundle, with the collapsed sphere bundle as basepoint. Equivalently it is the one-point compactification of the total space. An oriented bundle's Thom class and Thom isomorphism identify the reduced cohomology of this space with shifted cohomology of the base.
