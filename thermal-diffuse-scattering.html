Thermal diffuse scattering

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering Scattering amplitude Diffraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
Diffuse scattering generated by fluctuating lattice displacements. Resolved one-phonon creation and annihilation transfer both energy and crystal momentum, with Bose factors nB​+1 and nB​. An energy-integrating diffraction measurement can record their summed intensity without resolving the small energy transfer. Phonon polarization, soft-mode frequencies and atomic scattering weights determine the pattern; static disorder supplies a distinct possible diffuse contribution.
