Coleman norm contraction (N(1+pkR)⊆1+pk+1R)

 ... Iwasawa theory Iwasawa module p-ramified Iwasawa module Class-field unit sequence Coleman power series Coleman norm operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For odd p, the Coleman norm operator satisfies the displayed inclusion for every k≥1. Write Mh​ for multiplication by h on the finite free module R over ϕ(R). Its trace is pϕ(S(h)), where S is the Coleman trace operator. In det(I+pkMh​) the linear term is therefore divisible by pk+1, and all higher terms are divisible by p2k. Congruence reflection for Frobenius substitution gives the assertion. A norm-fixed element of 1+pR lies in 1+pkR for every k and hence equals 1.
