Quark model

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron
OurBigBook.com Words: 110 Articles: 1
Ordinary baryons have three valence quarks, and ordinary mesons have a quark and an antiquark. Additive quantum numbers such as electric charge, baryon number and strangeness are sums of constituent values. This valence description organizes flavor symmetry multiplets while allowing sea constituents and gluons.
