Quark model

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 9
Ordinary baryons have three valence quarks, and ordinary mesons have a quark and an antiquark. Additive quantum numbers such as electric charge, baryon number and strangeness are sums of constituent values. This valence description organizes flavor symmetry multiplets while allowing sea constituents and gluons.
The quark model makes a large catalogue manageable: the same few quark flavors generate many different hadrons. It predicts relations between electric charge, strangeness and isospin, rather than assigning these independently to each new particle. It is especially useful for asking which combinations are possible and which additional measurements could distinguish them. A constituent list alone does not determine a mass, a decay rate or whether a proposed combination forms a bound state.
