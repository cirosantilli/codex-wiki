Damped free drift of an iceberg (v˙+fJv+γv=F/m)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Iceberg Iceberg free drift
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For a constant wind force F, still water, and linear water drag force −mγv, let Jv=z^×v. The Coriolis parameter f gives equilibrium v∞​=(γF−fJF)/[m(γ2+f2)]. The deviation evolves as e−γtR(−ft)(v(0)−v∞​), a damped inertial oscillation. Actual drag is commonly quadratic, so this is a linear illustrative model rather than a universal iceberg speed law.
