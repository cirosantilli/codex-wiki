Holding-time reversal along a fixed CTMC jump path

Conditioned on a finite jump path i0​,…,in​, the holding times are independent exponentials with the corresponding rates. Multiplying the probability of occupying in​ at time t by qin​​ turns its simplex density into a product symmetric under reversal of the time portions and the state sequence.
