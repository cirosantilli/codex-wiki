Heegaard Floer chain complex (CF−(Y,s))

OurBigBook.com Words: 662 Articles: 5
For an admissible pointed Heegaard diagram, CF− is a free Z[U] chain complex with one generator for each point of Tα​∩Tβ​. Each generator is an unordered selection containing exactly one intersection on each alpha circle and exactly one on each beta circle. After choosing coherent orientations,
∂−x=∑y​∑ψ∈π2​(x,y)μ(ψ)=1​​#M(ψ)Unz​(ψ)y.
(8)
Here M is the moduli space modulo strip translation, μ is the Maslov index, and nz​ is multiplicity at the basepoint. Holomorphic representatives have nonnegative domain multiplicities. The differential lowers grading by one and U lowers it by two. Boundary strata of one-dimensional moduli spaces give the cancellation proving ∂2=0. Its homology is HF−.
