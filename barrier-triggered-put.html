Barrier-triggered put (τ=inf{u≥t:Su​≤B}∧T)

 ... Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fundamental theorem of asset pricing Contingent claim Put option American put option
OurBigBook.com Words: 148 Articles: 1
A put with a prescribed exercise rule: exercise at the first touch of B<K, or at expiry if no touch occurs. Its value is bounded above by the American put option, which optimizes over exercise times. With zero interest rate, its value equals the down-and-out put value plus (K−B) times a cash-at-expiry one-touch option value. At positive interest rate the rebate is paid at touch, so replacing it by an expiry payment would generally be incorrect.
