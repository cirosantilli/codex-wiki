Uniformizer criterion for lower ramification groups

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Ramification group
OurBigBook.com Words: 458 Articles: 6
If L/K is a finite totally ramified Galois extension and πL​ is a uniformizer, then
Gi​={σ:vL​(σ(πL​)−πL​)≥i+1}
(21)
for every i≥0.
