Temperature ratio of an early-decoupled neutrino (TX​/Tγ​=[43/(11g∗s,D​)]1/3)

 ... Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmological entropy conservation Thermal decoupling in cosmology Cosmic neutrino background Neutrino decoupling
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A relativistic species decoupling before ordinary neutrino decoupling cools as TX​∝a−1. Cosmological entropy conservation in the remaining bath gives TX​/Tν​=[10.75/g∗s,D​]1/3, where g∗s,D​ counts that bath just after the early species has left it. Subsequent electron-positron annihilation in cosmology gives Tν​/Tγ​=(4/11)1/3. For the ideal high-temperature relativistic entropy content of the Standard Model, the resulting ratio is about 0.332.
