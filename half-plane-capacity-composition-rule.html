Half-plane-capacity composition rule

OurBigBook.com Words: 34
If compact H-hulls satisfy A⊆C and D is the bounded filling of gA​(C∖A), then
gC​=gD​∘gA​,hcap(C)=hcap(A)+hcap(D).
(132)
In particular, inclusion is strict exactly when the capacity increase is positive.
