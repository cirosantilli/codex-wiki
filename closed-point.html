Closed point ({x} is closed)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological space
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
A point of a topological space is closed when its singleton is a closed set. In a T1 space every point is closed. The closed points of an affine scheme SpecA are its maximal ideals; the closure of the point corresponding to p is V(p).
