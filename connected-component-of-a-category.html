Connected component of a category (π0​(C))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Connected category
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
Two objects of a category lie in the same connected component if a finite zigzag of morphisms, with either orientation allowed, joins them. This is an equivalence relation; its classes give full subcategories, each a connected category. For a small category, the collection of components is a set denoted π0​(C). A functor induces a function on components. Empty categories have no components.
