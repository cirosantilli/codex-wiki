Uniform pure-qubit average (dμ=4πsinθdθdφ​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Bloch vector Bloch sphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
The rotation-invariant average of pure qubit states is uniform area measure on the Bloch sphere, induced by Haar measure on unitary transformations. For a uniformly distributed unit Bloch vector r, rotational symmetry gives Eri​=0 and Eri​rj​=δij​/3. Uniform polar angle without the factor sinθ is a different distribution.
