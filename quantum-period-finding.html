Quantum period finding

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum theory Quantum Fourier transform
OurBigBook.com Words: 164 Articles: 2
For a function of period r dividing N, prepare a uniform superposition of inputs, evaluate the function into a second register, and measure that register. The first register becomes a periodic coset state. Applying the quantum Fourier transform and measuring gives
c=srN​,0≤s<r.
