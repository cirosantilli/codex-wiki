Ulam sequence

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Sequence Integer sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
Starting with two distinct positive integers a<b, repeatedly append the smallest integer larger than the previous term that is expressible in exactly one way as the sum of two distinct earlier terms. Requiring distinct summands and counting unordered pairs are both essential parts of the definition.
