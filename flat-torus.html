Flat torus (Rd/Λ)

 ... Differential geometry Second fundamental form Gauss–Codazzi equations Gauss equation Sectional curvature Flat manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 265 Articles: 3
A flat torus is Rd/Λ with its quotient Euclidean metric, where Λ is a full-rank Euclidean lattice. Its volume is the lattice covolume. The dual lattice indexes its complete Fourier series of Laplacian eigenfunctions.
