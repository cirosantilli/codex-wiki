Wang homomorphism (Δ:Hi​(F)→Hi+n−1​(F))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Serre spectral sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 54 Articles: 1
For a fibration over Sn, the only possible differential in the homological Serre spectral sequence is dn​. Identifying its two nonzero columns with the homology of the fiber gives the Wang homomorphism Δ.
