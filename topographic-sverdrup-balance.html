Topographic Sverdrup balance

 ... Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Ocean circulation Wind stress Ocean gyre Sverdrup balance
OurBigBook.com Words: 209 Articles: 3
When depth varies but the Coriolis parameter is constant, forcing can balance advection of f/H rather than advection of planetary vorticity. This topographic potential-vorticity gradient plays the role normally played by the beta effect.
