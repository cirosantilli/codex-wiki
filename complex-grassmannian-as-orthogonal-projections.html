Complex Grassmannian as orthogonal projections (P=P∗=P2,trP=k)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Grassmannian Grassmannian as projection matrices
OurBigBook.com Words: 184 Articles: 2
The complex Grassmannian of k-planes in Cn identifies with the displayed Hermitian orthogonal projection matrices. A plane is recovered as the image of its projection. Near a plane E, a neighboring plane is the graph of a complex linear map Z:E→E⊥. Its projection is W(W∗W)−1W∗ with W=(I,Z)T. The unitary group acts transitively by conjugation, and its tangent directions are [A,P] with A∗=−A.
