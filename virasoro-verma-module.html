Virasoro Verma module (M(c,h))

The Virasoro Verma module M(c,h) is the universal highest-weight representation with central charge c and ground weight h. The Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt theorem gives a basis L−n1​​⋯L−ns​​∣h⟩ with n1​≥⋯≥ns​>0. Its invariant Hermitian form is fixed by Ln†​=L−n​ and the norm of the ground vector. Quotienting by its maximal proper submodule produces the irreducible highest-weight module; in the unitary cases this removes the null vectors.
