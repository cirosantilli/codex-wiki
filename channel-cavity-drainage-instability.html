Channel-cavity drainage instability (NR′​(QR​)>∣NK′​(QK​)∣)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Glaciology Subglacial drainage
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
At fixed total discharge QR​+QK​, remove a small amount ε from a channel and send it through connected cavities. The equilibrium channel effective pressure drops by NR′​ε, while the cavity pressure difference drops by ∣NK′​∣ε. Under the displayed inequality the cavity system leaves a common effective pressure higher than the reduced-flux channel can sustain. Excess creep closure then decreases channel discharge further, amplifying the perturbation.
