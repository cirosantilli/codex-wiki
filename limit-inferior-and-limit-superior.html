Limit inferior and limit superior (liminf, limsup)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 58 Articles: 1
For a real sequence (an​), the limit inferior is the limit of the infima of its tails and the limit superior is the limit of the suprema of its tails. These extended-real bounds describe its lowest and highest limiting behavior.
