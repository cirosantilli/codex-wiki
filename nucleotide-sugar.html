Nucleotide sugar

 Home Chemistry Molecule Nucleotide
OurBigBook.com Words: 227 Articles: 8
A nucleotide sugar carries an activated sugar linked to a nucleotide-containing group. It supports selective glycosyl transfer; donor identity alone does not specify the linkage or final glycan structure.
