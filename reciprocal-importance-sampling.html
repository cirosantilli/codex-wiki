Reciprocal importance sampling (Z−1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Monte Carlo method Importance sampling
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
Let r be an unnormalized density, Z=∫r with 0<Z<∞, and π=r/Z. Given independent draws θt​∼π and an evaluable normalized density q supported where r>0, reciprocal importance sampling estimates Z−1 by T−1∑t​q(θt​)/r(θt​). Its expectation is Z−1∫q=Z−1 and its variance is (TZ2)−1(∫q2/π−1). Broad tails of q relative to π can make this infinite; reversing the importance sampling ratio reverses the dangerous tail mismatch. Taking the reciprocal of the estimate is generally biased for Z.
