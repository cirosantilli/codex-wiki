Service discipline

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 157 Articles: 3
A service discipline specifies which customers receive the server's capacity and where newly arriving customers join the queue. First come first served and processor sharing give different sample paths even when their equilibrium populations agree.
