Polymer adsorption

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymer physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 300 Articles: 5
Polymer adsorption binds a polymer near an attractive interface through contacts of some of its monomers. The binding energy competes with the loss of conformational entropy. A chain need not lie flat: noncontacting loops and tails can extend into the solvent. In a weak-adsorption blob model, the layer thickness exceeds the Kuhn length and local chain statistics remain approximately those of the free chain below that thickness.
