Vertically averaged viscous disk equations (∂t​Σ+r−1∂r​(rΣvr​)=0)

In an axisymmetric thin disc with no vertical mass or torque flux, integrate mass and conservation of angular momentum over height. With fixed height-independent l=r2Ω, the equations are ∂t​Σ+r−1∂r​(rΣvr​)=0 and ∂t​(Σl)+r−1∂r​(rΣvr​l−r3νˉΣΩ′)=0. Their difference gives rΣvr​l′=(r3νˉΣΩ′)′. The density-weighted viscosity of a disk supplies the integrated stress.
