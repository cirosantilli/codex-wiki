Space of continuous functions on a compact space (C(K))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Space of continuous functions vanishing at infinity
OurBigBook.com Words: 350 Articles: 6
For a compact Hausdorff space K, C(K) consists of continuous scalar-valued functions with the supremum norm. It is a Banach space and, with complex scalars, pointwise multiplication and conjugation make it a commutative unital C-star algebra.
