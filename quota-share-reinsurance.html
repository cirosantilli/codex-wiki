Quota share reinsurance (SI​=αS)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Reinsurance
OurBigBook.com Words: 291 Articles: 4
Quota share reinsurance retains a fixed proportion α∈[0,1] of each claim. The annual retained aggregate is αS, whose expected value and variance are αES and α2Var(S).
