Weak lower semicontinuity of convex gradient energies (uk​⇀u in H1⟹∫F(Du)≤liminfk​∫F(Duk​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Calculus of variations Direct method in the calculus of variations
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
Suppose the domain has finite measure and F∈C1 is convex, with ∣F(p)∣≤C(1+∣p∣2) and ∣DF(p)∣≤C(1+∣p∣). The supporting inequality F(p)≥F(q)+DF(q)⋅(p−q), integrated with q=Du, gives ∫F(Duk​)≥∫F(Du)+∫DF(Du)⋅(Duk​−Du). The last term tends to zero by weak convergence of the gradients and the fixed L2 test field DF(Du). This proves weak lower semicontinuity without requiring pointwise convergence of gradients.
