Frequency (ν=1/T)

 Home Physics Wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
Frequency is the number of repetitions of a periodic phenomenon per unit time. For a wave of period T, its frequency is ν=1/T, measured in hertz. The angular frequency is ω=2πν.
