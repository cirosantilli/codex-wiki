Hydraulic press (F2​/F1​=A2​/A1​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure Pascal's law
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
Two connected pistons apply Pascal's law to convert force and displacement. For an ideal incompressible fluid, A1​s1​=A2​s2​ and F1​/A1​=F2​/A2​, hence F1​s1​=F2​s2​: output force gain is not an energy gain.
