Qudit shift and phase operators (X∣j⟩=∣j+1⟩,Z∣j⟩=ωj∣j⟩)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state Qudit
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
For an n-level qudit and primitive root of unity ω, these unitary operators obey Xn=Zn=I and ZX=ωXZ. The inverse of XsZ−r is ZrX−s, with the order reversed. These operators implement the receiver corrections in qudit teleportation.
