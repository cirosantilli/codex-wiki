Effective norm-form height estimate

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Diophantine approximation Linear form in logarithms of algebraic numbers Baker lower bound for a homogeneous linear form in logarithms
OurBigBook.com Words: 107
Let L be a fixed number field and let V⊂L be a fixed rational linear subspace for which the associated norm form has no unit-family degeneracy. There are effective constants A,C>0 such that every z∈V∩OL​ with nonzero field norm m satisfies
H(z)≤C∣m∣A.
(131)
To prove this, factor the principal ideal (z), write its generators as a bounded factor times powers of fundamental units, use the linear relations defining V, and apply the Baker lower bound for a homogeneous linear form in logarithms to bound the unit exponents by O(log∣m∣). For L=Q(α)K with [Q(α):Q]=3, [K:Q]=2, and V=spanQ​{1,α,ω} for a basis 1,ω of K, the coprime degrees rule out the degeneracy.
