Polymer scattering function (g(0)=N)

For N equal scattering sites on one polymer, define g(k)=N−1∑m,n​⟨eik⋅(Rn​−Rm​)⟩. It has g(0)=N; its normalized form factor is g/N. Under a rotationally invariant joint distribution, each separation vector has a uniform direction at fixed magnitude r. The angular average is 21​∫−11​eikrμdμ=sin(kr)/(kr), with value one at r=0. Thus g depends only on k=∣k∣, and g(k)=N−1∑m,n​⟨sin(k∣Rn​−Rm​∣)/(k∣Rn​−Rm​∣)⟩. The diagonal self terms are essential in a finite chain and give the large-k limit one for a discrete Gaussian chain.
