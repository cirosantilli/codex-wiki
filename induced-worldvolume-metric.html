Induced worldvolume metric (hμν​=∂μ​Xa∂ν​Xbgab​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Brane Worldvolume
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The induced worldvolume metric is the pullback of the target metric tensor by a brane embedding. For a nondegenerate timelike embedding, −deth​ is its invariant worldvolume volume density. The p=1 specialization is the induced worldsheet metric.
