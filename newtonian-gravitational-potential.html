Newtonian gravitational potential (Φ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field
OurBigBook.com Words: 442 Articles: 10
The Newtonian gravitational potential is the potential energy per unit test mass. Outside a spherical mass M it is Φ(r)=−GM/r after choosing zero at infinity, and the gravitational acceleration is −∇Φ.
