Polynomial Lax representation of the Nahm equations (L˙(λ)=[L(λ),M(λ)])

Put P=A1​+iA2​, Q=A1​−iA2​ and R=A3​, and define
L(λ)=P+2Rλ−Qλ2,M(λ)=−iR+iQλ.
(25)
Direct expansion gives [L,M]=−i[P,R]+i[P,Q]λ+i[R,Q]λ2. The Nahm equations imply P˙=−i[P,R], 2R˙=i[P,Q] and −Q˙​=i[R,Q], so L˙=[L,M]. In any finite-dimensional matrix representation, cyclicity of the matrix trace gives
dtd​TrLp=pTr(Lp−1[L,M])=0(p=1,2,…).
(26)
Thus every coefficient of the degree-at-most-2p matrix trace polynomial is conserved. At projective infinity the polynomial is interpreted as a section of O(2p), not a globally holomorphic scalar function on the complex projective line.
