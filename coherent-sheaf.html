Coherent sheaf

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Quasi-coherent sheaf
OurBigBook.com Words: 399 Articles: 7
On a locally Noetherian scheme, a coherent sheaf is a quasi-coherent sheaf that is locally associated with a finitely generated module. Kernels, cokernels, and finite direct sums of coherent sheaves remain coherent.
