Pressure-anisotropy-modified magnetic tension (T=B2/(4π)+p⊥​−p∥​)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure Pressure tensor Pressure anisotropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
In Gaussian units, the gyrotropic pressure tensor adds directional stress to the magnetic tension. A transverse displacement of a uniform magnetic field obeys ρ∂t2​ξ⊥​=T∂∥2​ξ⊥​. Positive T gives a restoring force; negative T gives the fluid firehose instability. This follows by combining −∇⋅P with the divergence of the Maxwell stress tensor. In SI replace B2/(4π) by B2/μ0​.
