Kraus operator

 ... Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Positive linear map Completely positive map Kraus representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
The operators Kj​ in a Kraus representation are Kraus operators. For a measurement outcome i, its associated Kraus operators determine both the outcome probability and the conditional post-measurement state.
