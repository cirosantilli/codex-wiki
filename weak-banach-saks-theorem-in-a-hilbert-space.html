Weak Banach–Saks theorem in a Hilbert space

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Hilbert space Weak convergence in a Hilbert space
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
If xn​⇀x in a Hilbert space, some subsequence (xnk​​) has Cesaro means converging in norm to x. For x=0, choose the subsequence so successive vectors have summably small inner products; expansion of the squared norm of its averages then proves convergence.
Thus Hilbert spaces have the weak form of the Banach-Saks property.
