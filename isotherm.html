Isotherm

 ... Statistical physics Thermodynamics Second law of thermodynamics Carnot's theorem Carnot cycle Isothermal process
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
An isotherm is a curve of equilibrium states at fixed temperature, often drawn in the pressure-volume plane. For an ideal gas of fixed amount it obeys pV=constant. The curve is not itself a specification of the rate, reversibility or experimental mechanism by which the states are visited.
