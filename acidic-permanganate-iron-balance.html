Acidic permanganate-iron balance

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Redox Oxidation Oxidation state
OurBigBook.com Words: 225
An increase in oxidation state records oxidation; a decrease records reduction. Thus Fe2+→Fe3+ raises the iron oxidation state by one, while MnO4−​→Mn2+ lowers the manganese oxidation state from +7 to +2. Five iron ions supply the five-Electron inventory required per permanganate ion.
In an acidic chemical solution, the complete half-reactions are
Fe2+MnO4−​+8H++5e−​⟶Fe3++e−,⟶Mn2++4H2​O.​
(104)
First balance oxygen with four water molecules, then hydrogen with eight protons, and finally electric charge with five Electrons. Multiply the iron half-reaction by five and add; the exchanged Electrons cancel:
MnO4−​+8H++5Fe2+⟶Mn2++4H2​O+5Fe3+.​
(105)
Both sides contain one manganese, four oxygen, eight hydrogen and five iron chemical atoms, with net electric charge +17e. The H+ notation represents the acidic aqueous proton inventory; it does not imply bare protons moving independently of water. These half-reactions and their sum are the acidic balance used in the documented permanganate-iron titration.[ref]
The coefficients predict amounts consumed, not a collision of five ions or an elementary reaction. Bulk chemical solution also contains compensating counterions, which are omitted from this net chemical equation. The hydrogen and oxygen oxidation states are unchanged; balancing their inventories remains essential even though they do not account for the exchanged Electrons.
