Group the normalizing sum by its odd and even flow counts. The coefficient sums are hp​(ρ1​,ρ3​)=∑i=0p​ρ1i​ρ3p−i​ and the analogous hq​ for even routes. If a and b are the two maximum loads, then ap≤hp​≤(p+1)ap and similarly for b. The generating identity ∑p,q​(pp+q​)apbq=(1−a−b)−1 proves necessity and sufficiency of the displayed strict inequality. It is equivalent to strictly subunit offered load on each of the four resources.
