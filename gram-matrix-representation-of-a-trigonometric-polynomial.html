Gram matrix representation of a trigonometric polynomial (pk​=∑i−j=k​Mij​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier series Trigonometric polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 76 Articles: 1
Under the displayed diagonal-sum convention, set w(z)=(1,z−1,…,z−d)T on the unit circle. A Hermitian matrix M then gives
p(z)=w(z)∗Mw(z).
(4)
Consequently M⪰0 proves nonnegativity. Conversely the Fejér–Riesz theorem produces the rank-one spectral-factor Gram matrix. This gives a semidefinite programming representation of nonnegative trigonometric polynomials. The reversed convention j−i=k instead uses the unconjugated monomial vector.
