Platinum resistance thermometer

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Temperature Thermometer Resistance thermometer
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 6
A resistance thermometer using platinum as its sensing material. It provides the temperature readout in the saturation-pressure equilibrium measurement.
Transduction turns electrical resistance into a temperature indication. A uniform wire has R=ρel​ℓ/A, with electrical resistivity ρel​, length ℓ, and cross-sectional area A. Stronger atomic vibrations at higher temperature scatter electrons more, increasing platinum's electrical resistivity; impurities and crystallographic defects also contribute.[ref]
A small known electric current I and measured electric potential difference V give R=V/I through Ohm's law; a resistance bridge can instead compare the element with a reference resistor. Inverting the calibrated response then gives the sensing element's temperature. This makes electrical readout convenient, but the platinum resistance thermometer lead resistance error, resistance thermometer self-heating, and resistance thermometer immersion test explain why a stable display alone is insufficient. The gas thermometer supplies a contrasting route through an equation of state; the saturation-pressure equilibrium measurement shows why a trustworthy independent temperature readout matters.
