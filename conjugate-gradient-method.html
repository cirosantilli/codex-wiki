For a positive-definite matrix A, the conjugate gradient method chooses mutually A-conjugate search directions. Starting with r0​=b−Ax0​ and p0​=r0​, it uses
αk​=pkT​Apk​rkT​rk​​,xk+1​=xk​+αk​pk​,rk+1​=rk​−αk​Apk​,

and pk+1​=rk+1​+βk​pk​ with
βk​=rk+1T​rk+1​/(rkT​rk​).
