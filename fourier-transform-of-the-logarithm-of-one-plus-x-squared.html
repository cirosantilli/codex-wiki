Fourier transform of the logarithm of one plus x squared (F[21​log(1+x2)])

For u(x)=21​log(1+x2), the angular-frequency transform is the tempered distribution
⟨u,φ⟩=−π∫R​∣ξ∣φ(ξ)−φ(0)​e−∣ξ∣dξ.
(17)
The numerator cancels the singularity at zero and makes this integral absolutely convergent. Differentiating u and using (1+x2)−1​=πe−∣ξ∣ determines this expression up to a Dirac delta distribution. That delta coefficient is zero: testing with the expanding Gaussian φn​(x)=(2π​)−1e−x2/(4n) makes both the proposed expression and ⟨u,φ​n​⟩ tend to zero, while φn​(0) stays fixed. Changing the subtraction convention would change the delta coefficient.
