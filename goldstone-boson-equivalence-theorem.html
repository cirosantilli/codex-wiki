Goldstone-boson equivalence theorem (M(VL​⋯)=M(χ⋯)+O(mV​/E))

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Electroweak interaction Higgs field Higgs mechanism
OurBigBook.com Words: 151 Articles: 1
At tree level and energies much greater than a gauge-vector mass, an amplitude with longitudinal external gauge bosons equals the corresponding amplitude with their would-be Goldstone bosons, up to conventional external-state phases and relative O(mV​/E) corrections for a nonzero leading amplitude. In a renormalizable gauge, the broken-symmetry identities relate kμ​Mμ to mV​Mχ​; inserting εLμ​=kμ/mV​+O(mV​/E) explains the result. Loop-level versions include field-normalization factors. This is different from an equivalence theorem for field redefinitions.
