Complex quadratic Abelian gauge condition (F[A]=∂μ​Aμ​+iAμ​Aμ​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing Nonlinear Abelian gauge fixing
OurBigBook.com Words: 189 Articles: 2
The formal variation of this condition is δF=(∂2+2iA⋅∂)α. Thus its Faddeev-Popov ghost field operator can be chosen as OA​=−∂2−2iA⋅∂. With Dμ​=∂μ​+iAμ​, one has D2=∂2+2iA⋅∂+iF[A], so OA​=−D2 on the formal gauge slice. The reality obstruction for a complex Euclidean gauge condition prevents interpreting this slice as an ordinary real Euclidean gauge fixing without an additional complex-contour prescription.
