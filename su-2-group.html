SU(2) group (SU(2))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Unitary group Special unitary group
OurBigBook.com Words: 58 Articles: 1
The SU(2) group is the group of two-by-two unitary matrices of determinant one. It is a compact three-dimensional Lie group whose underlying manifold is a three-sphere.
