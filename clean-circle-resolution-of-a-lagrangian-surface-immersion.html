Clean-circle resolution of a Lagrangian surface immersion

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Lagrangian submanifold Lagrangian surgery
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
Perturb a clean self-intersection circle of a Lagrangian immersion by a Morse function on S1. Its minimum and maximum give two transverse double points. Resolving both by Lagrangian surgery adds two one-handles and changes the Euler characteristic by −4. One neck may be glued with the orientation-reversing choice, so the resolved surface is nonorientable.
