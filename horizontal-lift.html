Horizontal lift

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Connection (vector bundle)
OurBigBook.com Words: 387 Articles: 7
A horizontal lift is determined by the horizontal distribution of a principal connection or the corresponding connection on a vector bundle. A horizontal lift of a base curve is a curve in the bundle projecting to it and tangent to the horizontal distribution. In a local frame its fiber coordinate solves a linear ordinary differential equation.
