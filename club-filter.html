Club filter (Dκ​={A⊆κ:∃ club C⊆A})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Ordinal Club set
OurBigBook.com Words: 126 Articles: 2
On a regular uncountable cardinal number κ, the club filter consists of all subsets containing a club set. It is a kappa-complete filter by club filter completeness. Its members are stationary sets, although a stationary set need not be a filter member, and a filter member need not itself be closed.
