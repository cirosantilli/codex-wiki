Specht filtration

 ... Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young tableau Tabloid Polytabloid Specht module
OurBigBook.com Words: 228 Articles: 3
A Specht filtration is a finite chain of submodules whose successive quotients are Specht modules. In positive characteristic it need not split and is not a composition series, because its Specht factors may themselves be reducible. Dimensions and ordinary lifted characters can still be added along the filtration.
