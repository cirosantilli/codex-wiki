Section compression in binary order (Ci​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Analysis of Boolean functions Boolean hypercube Binary order on the discrete cube
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
Fix a coordinate of the Boolean hypercube, delete it in each of the two sections, and replace each section by a binary initial segment of the same size. This preserves the total size. Mathematical induction on dimension proves that it does not decrease contained edge counts; the crossing contribution is the size of the section intersection, maximized by nested initial segments.
