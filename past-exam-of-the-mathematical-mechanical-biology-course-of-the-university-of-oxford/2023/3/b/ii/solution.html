Write Wi​=∂W/∂αi​, evaluated at (α−1/2,α−1/2,α). Applying the chain rule to the reduced strain energy density gives
W^′(α)=−21​α−3/2W1​−21​α−3/2W2​+W3​.
(50)
Multiply by α and use α1​=α2​=α−1/2:
αW^′(α)=α3​W3​−21​α1​W1​−21​α2​W2​.
(51)
The transverse symmetry specified in the hint has W1​=W2​. Thus
αW^′(α)=α3​W3​−α1​W1​.​
(52)
The lateral faces have zero traction, so their diagonal Cauchy stress tensor components satisfy t1​=t2​=0. The hydrostatic pressure enforcing elastic incompressibility is therefore p=α1​W1​=α2​W2​, and the axial component simplifies to
t3​=α3​W3​−p=αW^′(α).​
(53)
This elimination of the Lagrange multiplier p is what makes the reduced strain energy density useful for the spring balance.
