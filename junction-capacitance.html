Junction capacitance (Cj​)

 Home Physics Semiconductor P-n junction Depletion region
OurBigBook.com Words: 144 Articles: 1
The incremental capacitance associated with changes of electric charge around a p-n junction. Its depletion contribution is often estimated by Cj​≃εs​A/W, so an independent junction capacitance measurement can constrain the width W of the depletion region under the model assumptions. Other stored electric charge, including injected minority carriers, can also contribute to a measured terminal capacitance.
