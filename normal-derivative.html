Normal derivative (∂N​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Normal vector Unit normal
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
The normal derivative of a differentiable function u along a hypersurface with unit normal N is the directional derivative
∂N​u=N⋅∇u.
(30)
Higher normal derivatives at a point x may be defined by differentiating the one-variable function s↦u(x+sN(x)) repeatedly at s=0.
