Modular form on a finite-index subgroup (Mk​(Γ))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form
OurBigBook.com Words: 178 Articles: 2
A modular form on an arbitrary finite-index subgroup Γ≤SL2​(Z) is a holomorphic function on the complex upper half-plane, invariant under the weight-k slash operator for modular forms, and holomorphic at a cusp at every cusp of a modular group for Γ. If σ∈SL2​(Z) maps infinity to a cusp, choose a genuine translation period h>0 with σThσ−1∈Γ. Then f∣k​σ has a convergent series in e2πiz/h with nonnegative exponents. A cusp form has zero constant term at every cusp. This definition allows noncongruence subgroups and avoids sign ambiguities in odd weights when a smaller width is defined only modulo the center.
