Orbital angular momentum quantum number (l)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state Quantum number Angular momentum quantum number
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
The nonnegative integer l for which a spherical harmonic has squared orbital angular momentum ℏ2l(l+1). The angular part of a regular scalar wavefunction has l=0,1,2,… and magnetic quantum number m=−l,…,l.
