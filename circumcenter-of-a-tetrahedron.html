Circumcenter of a tetrahedron (O=A+2u⋅(v×w)∣u∣2(v×w)+∣v∣2(w×u)+∣w∣2(u×v)​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Polyhedron Tetrahedron
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
For independent edge vectors u=B−A, v=C−A, w=D−A, the displacement x=O−A solves x⋅u=∣u∣2/2, x⋅v=∣v∣2/2, x⋅w=∣w∣2/2. The reciprocal cross-product basis gives the displayed formula. Its squared distances to all four vertices are equal. It need not lie inside the tetrahedron.
