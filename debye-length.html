Debye length (λD​=ϵ0​kB​Te​/(ne​e2)​)

 Home Physics Plasma (physics) Plasma screening
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 2
For mobile thermal electrons with a fixed neutralizing background, the Debye length is the characteristic distance over which plasma screening suppresses an electrostatic perturbation. Linearizing the Boltzmann density response and using Poisson's equation gives the displayed scale. Other mobile species contribute additively to λD−2​ through ns​qs2​/(ϵ0​kB​Ts​).
