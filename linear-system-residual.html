Linear-system residual (r=b−Ax)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Gradient descent Conjugate gradient method
OurBigBook.com Words: 74 Articles: 1
The residual of an approximate solution x of Ax=b is r=b−Ax. It is the unbalanced right-hand side, while the solution error is x∗​−x=A−1r. The conjugate gradient method makes residuals orthogonal to successive Krylov subspaces.
