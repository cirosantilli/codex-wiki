Moon

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Natural satellite
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 20
The Moon is Earth's natural satellite. Its synchronous rotation keeps approximately the same near side of the Moon facing Earth, while both the near side of the Moon and the far side of the Moon experience sunlight and darkness. See illumination of a synchronously rotating moon for the geometry. [ref].
