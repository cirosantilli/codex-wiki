For slender hard rods of length L and diameter D≪L, the leading pair excluded volume is the displayed function of their mutual angle γ. Alignment lowers mutual obstruction and increases positional entropy, while narrowing the orientation distribution lowers orientational entropy. Their competition can drive an isotropic-to-nematic transition without attractive forces. The natural concentration scale is cL2D of order one. Finite rod diameter, flexibility and interactions beyond the hard-core second-virial approximation change quantitative predictions.
