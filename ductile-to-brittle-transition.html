Ductile-to-brittle transition (TDBTT​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Fracture toughness
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A change from predominantly ductile deformation and fracture to predominantly brittle fracture as temperature decreases under specified loading and specimen conditions. A quoted transition temperature depends on its criterion: absorbed impact energy, fracture appearance and fracture toughness need not give the same number. In ferritic steel, irradiation hardening can raise the transition temperature.
