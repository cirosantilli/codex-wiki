Bose gas

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Bose-Einstein statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A gas whose indistinguishable constituents are bosons. The ideal model follows Bose-Einstein statistics, while interactions modify its excitations and thermodynamics. Macroscopic ground-state occupation can occur under suitable dimension and density conditions, but it does not alone determine a superfluid response.
