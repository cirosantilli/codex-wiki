Use the real zero-trace Sobolev space H01​(Ω) with norm ∥∇u∥2​. The Poincaré inequality on the bounded smooth domain gives ∥u∥2​≤CP​∥∇u∥2​, making this an equivalent Sobolev norm. The weak formulation is to find u∈H01​(Ω) with u≥0 almost everywhere and
∫Ω​∇u⋅∇vdx=∫Ω​(1+u​)vdxfor every v∈H01​(Ω).​
(22)
The zero Dirichlet boundary condition is encoded in the Sobolev trace. Formally, the identity follows by multiplying the partial differential equation by a test function and using integration by parts; it then extends to all v∈H01​(Ω) by density.
To check all terms, boundedness of Ω gives finite Lebesgue measure m=∣Ω∣. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality gives u∈L1(Ω) and
∥u​∥22​=∫Ω​udx≤m1/2∥u∥2​,∥1+u​∥2​≤m1/2+m1/4∥u∥21/2​.
(23)
Thus the right side is a bounded linear functional of v, since its absolute value is at most ∥1+u​∥2​CP​∥∇v∥2​. The left side is finite by the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality. These estimates work for every stated n≥2.
