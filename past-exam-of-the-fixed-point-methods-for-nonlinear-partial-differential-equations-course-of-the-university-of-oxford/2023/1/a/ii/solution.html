This monotone sequence is bounded below by zero, so it has a limit a∈[0,1]. The continuity of the sine gives a=sina, and the strict inequality above forces a=0.
Hence xn​→0, and 0 is a fixed point of the sine. It is the only real fixed point: every such point has absolute value at most one, where the preceding strict inequality applies. Thus
