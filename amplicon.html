Amplicon

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Polymerase chain reaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
An amplicon is a nucleic acid product of amplification. In ordinary PCR amplification without added primer tails, its boundaries eventually correspond to the outer ends of the two PCR primer binding sites. First-cycle extensions on long starting templates can run beyond the opposite site; exact-length single strands appear in cycle 2 and exact-length duplex products in cycle 3 under complete extension. This explains why the bounded product becomes dominant even though the initial extensions need not have its length.
