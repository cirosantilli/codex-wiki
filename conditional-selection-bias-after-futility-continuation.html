Conditional selection bias after futility continuation (E(S∣X≥c)−δ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Clinical trial Group sequential design Futility boundary
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 2
In a two-stage normal trial, conditioning on a first-stage sample mean exceeding c raises that mean. With stage standard error s, mean parameter δ, and combined mean S, the estimator bias after continuation is E(S∣X≥c)−δ=(s/2)λ((c−δ)/s), where λ is the upper-tail Inverse Mills ratio. It is positive and tends to zero as continuation becomes virtually certain.
