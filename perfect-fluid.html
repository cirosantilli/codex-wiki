Perfect fluid

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
A perfect fluid has no viscosity or heat flux in its local rest frame; its stress is specified by energy density and isotropic pressure. Its relativistic realization is the perfect fluid in general relativity.
