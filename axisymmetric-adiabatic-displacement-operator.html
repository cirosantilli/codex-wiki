Axisymmetric adiabatic displacement operator (Fξ=(δρ/ρ2)∇p−∇δp/ρ−κ2ξR​eR​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Stellar oscillation
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 2
In the Cowling approximation, an axisymmetric meridional fluid displacement in a cylindrically rotating barotropic star obeys −ω2ξ=Fξ, with δρ=−∇⋅(ρξ) and δp=−ξ⋅∇p−γp∇⋅ξ. Conservation of specific angular momentum supplies the epicyclic term, with κ2=R−3d(R4Ω2)/dR. The Cowling energy principle for a rotating barotropic star gives its symmetric quadratic form.
