 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics
OurBigBook.com Tags: Terrestrial atmosphere Words: 2k Articles: 9
The upper part of the terrestrial atmosphere includes regions where ionization makes the response to electromagnetic waves depend strongly on mobile electrons. The ionosphere describes that electrical structure; its boundaries need not coincide with the boundaries defined by temperature in the mesosphere and thermosphere.
