Partial pressure (pi​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Ideal gas
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The partial pressure of a component of a gas mixture is the pressure it would exert alone at the same temperature and total volume. For an ideal mixture, pi​=ni​RT/V and p=∑i​pi​. The chemical potential of an ideal gas uses pi​ for that component rather than the total pressure p.
