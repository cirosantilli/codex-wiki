Free chiral Majorana fermion conformal field theory (hψ​=1/2,c=1/2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Worldsheet Two-dimensional conformal field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 128 Articles: 1
A real chiral Majorana fermion with contraction ψ(z)ψ(w)∼1/(z−w) has stress tensor T=−:ψ∂ψ:/2. Fermionic Wick contractions give T(z)ψ(w)∼ψ(w)/(2(z−w)2)+∂ψ(w)/(z−w). Double contractions in TT give 1/(4(z−w)4), so the central charge is 1/2. Fermion bilinears of different species are weight-one currents; equal-species undifferentiated bilinears vanish by antisymmetry.
