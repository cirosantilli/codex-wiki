Isenthalpic process

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Enthalpy
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
An isenthalpic process preserves enthalpy for a fixed amount of material, or preserves specific enthalpy between inlet and outlet when comparing a flowing fluid of fixed composition. It need not preserve temperature or entropy. An insulated throttle satisfying the steady-flow energy balance assumptions is an important example.
