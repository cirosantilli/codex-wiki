Pollination

 Home Biology Reproduction Sexual reproduction
OurBigBook.com Words: 719 Articles: 7
In angiosperms, pollination transfers pollen from an anther to a stigma. Fertilization is a later fusion of gametes; seed development follows. Pollination may use animals, wind or water, so an insect visit is neither required for every flower nor sufficient evidence of fertilization.[ref]
