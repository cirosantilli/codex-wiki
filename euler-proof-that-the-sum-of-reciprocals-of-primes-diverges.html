Euler proof that the sum of reciprocals of primes diverges

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory Dirichlet series Euler product
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
For a finite set of primes,
∏p≤x​(1−p1​)−1
(50)
expands as the sum of 1/n over positive integers whose prime factors are at most x. It contains every term with n≤x, so these partial products dominate the harmonic series and diverge. Since
−log(1−t)=t+O(t2)
(51)
uniformly for 0≤t≤1/2, convergence of ∑p​1/p would force convergence of the logarithms of these products, a contradiction.
