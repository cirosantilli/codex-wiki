ADM formalism

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Numerical relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The Arnowitt-Deser-Misner formalism expresses general relativity as constrained Hamiltonian evolution of a spatial metric and its conjugate momentum after a 3+1 decomposition of spacetime. Lapse and shift enforce the Hamiltonian and momentum constraints. For an asymptotically flat spacetime its boundary Hamiltonian gives the Arnowitt-Deser-Misner energy.
