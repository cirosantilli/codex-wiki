Stimulated Raman transition (ΔE=ℏ(ω1​−ω2​),Δp=ℏ(k1​−k2​))

 Home Physics Atomic physics Atomic measurement methods Raman transition
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
Coherent absorption from one applied beam and stimulated emission into another, transferring an atom between two long-lived states. The energy transfer uses the frequency difference and the momentum transfer the wavevector difference. It is distinct from an absorption-absorption process.
