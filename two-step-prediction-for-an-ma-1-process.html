Two-step prediction for an MA(1) process (XT+2∣T​=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series Best linear prediction from a finite past
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
In a centered MA(1) process, XT+2​ is uncorrelated with every observation through time T. Its best linear predictor from that information is therefore zero, with error variance σ2(1+θ2). One-step innovation formulas using XT+1​ cannot be substituted when that observation is unavailable.
