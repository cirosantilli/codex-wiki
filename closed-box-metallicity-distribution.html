Closed-box metallicity distribution (dMs​/dZ=(M0​/Y)e−Z/Y)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy evolution Galactic chemical evolution Closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For a pristine closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution, Mg​dZ=YdMs​ and dMs​=−dMg​ imply Mg​=M0​e−Z/Y independently of the star formation rate. The resulting metallicity distribution function is exponential on 0≤Z≤Zf​. Its normalized density is e−Z/Y/[Y(1−e−Zf​/Y)]. A fixed initial mass function and survival selection are needed to turn locked stellar mass into a stellar number distribution. Logarithmic abundance intervals have the additional factor (ln10)Z.
