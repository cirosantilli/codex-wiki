Division field of an elliptic curve (K(E[n]))

 ... Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Torsion point of an elliptic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 2
The n-division field is obtained by adjoining to K the coordinates of every point of E[n]. Over a finite field its degree is the order of Frobenius acting on E[n].
