For orthonormal Legendre polynomials on [−1,1], the polynomial qn​ represents evaluation at zero on polynomials of degree at most n: ⟨r,qn​⟩=r(0). This follows by expanding r in the orthonormal basis, or from the Riesz representation theorem. With M=⌊n/2⌋ and cm​=(m2m​)/4m, orthonormality gives
∥qn​∥22​=21​∑m=0M​(4m+1)cm2​=2(2M+1)2cM2​​.
(35)
The last identity telescopes using cm​/cm−1​=(2m−1)/(2m). The norms are unbounded: cm​≥1/(2m+1) makes the sum dominate a divergent harmonic series. Thus the evaluation identities hold eventually for every fixed polynomial but cannot extend to convergence for every L2 vector, by the Uniform boundedness principle. Point evaluation is also not defined on L2 equivalence classes.
