Electric flux (ΦE​=∫S​E⋅ndS)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric field
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
The electric flux through an oriented surface is the surface integral of the normal component of its electric field. For a closed surface with outward normal, Gauss's law makes the electric flux equal to enclosed charge divided by ε0​ in SI units. It depends on surface orientation and may be negative.
