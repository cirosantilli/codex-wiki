Basic construction of a Coxeter group (U(W,K))

For a mirrored chamber K, the basic construction is
U(W,K)=(W×K)/∼,
(19)
where (w,x)∼(w′,x) when w−1w′ belongs to the subgroup generated by the mirrors containing x.
