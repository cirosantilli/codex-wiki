Dimension of the preimage of a vector subspace (dimT−1(X)=dim(X∩imT)+dimkerT)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Image and preimage of a function Preimage
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
Let T:U→V be a linear map between finite-dimensional vector spaces and X a vector subspace of V. The preimage T−1(X) is a vector subspace containing kerT. To compute its dimension, choose a basis k1​,…,ks​ of kerT and a basis y1​,…,yd​ of X∩imT. Lift each yj​ to uj​∈U with Tuj​=yj​. Then k1​,…,ks​,u1​,…,ud​ is a basis of T−1(X): applying T proves linear independence, and subtracting a suitable linear combination of the lifts reduces every element of the preimage to the kernel. This proves the displayed formula without using the rank-nullity theorem. Taking X=V then proves the rank-nullity theorem itself.
