Centre-of-momentum energy (s​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Four-momentum Center-of-momentum frame
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The centre-of-momentum energy is the total energy measured in the centre-of-momentum frame. For a scattering process with total four-momentum P, it is the Lorentz-invariant quantity P2​=s​ in the mostly-minus convention.
