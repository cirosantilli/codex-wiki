Pion (π)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 368 Articles: 6
A pion is one of the light pseudoscalar mesons produced by chiral symmetry breaking in quantum chromodynamics. The charge states are π+, π0 and π−.
