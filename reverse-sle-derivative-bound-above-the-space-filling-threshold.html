Reverse SLE derivative bound above the space-filling threshold

OurBigBook.com Words: 43
If κ>8, then for every 0<α<(1−8/κ)/2 and fixed T, there is an almost surely finite random C such that
∣hT′​(x+iy)∣≤Cy−1+α
(146)
for ∣x∣≤1 and 0<y≤1. The proof combines the derivative martingale, Markov inequality, a dyadic lattice, the Borel-Cantelli lemmas, and the Koebe distortion theorem.
