Nicholson-Bailey model (X′=rXe−aY,Y′=cX(1−e−aY))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Population dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
The Nicholson-Bailey model describes seasonal host-parasitoid interaction. Hosts surviving random attacks reproduce, while parasitoid offspring are proportional to attacked hosts. For r>1, its positive equilibrium is Y∗​=(logr)/a, X∗​=rY∗​/[c(r−1)]. Counting attacked rather than surviving hosts makes its predator reproduction law different from the annual pulse-breeding predator-prey model.
