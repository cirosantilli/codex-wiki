Limited excess of loss reinsurance (LM,A​(X)=min{(X−M)+​,A})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Reinsurance Excess of loss reinsurance
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 2
A reinsurance layer with attachment M and payment limit A pays nothing below M, the excess between M and M+A, and A above that. Its expected recovery is ∫MM+A​P(X>x)dx, by the tail integral formula for moments. For continuous claims, conditioning on penetration of the layer divides this expectation by P(X>M).
