Constructive empiricism

 Home Philosophy Philosophy of science
OurBigBook.com Words: 69 Articles: 1
Acceptance of a scientific theory involves belief in its empirical adequacy and practical commitment to its use, without requiring belief in its full truth about unobservables. This concerns epistemic attitudes, whereas the distinction between a constructive theory and a principle theory concerns explanatory organization.[ref]
