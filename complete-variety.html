Complete variety

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Proper morphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
A variety over a field k is complete when its map to Speck is universally closed: after any base change, projection sends closed subsets to closed subsets. For a separated finite-type variety, this is equivalent to being proper. In the classical algebraically closed setting one may test projections X×Y→Y for arbitrary varieties. Images of rational points over a non-algebraically-closed field must not replace geometric images.
