Yang-Mills theory is the non-Abelian gauge theory with field strength
Fμν​=∂μ​Aν​−∂ν​Aμ​−ig[Aμ​,Aν​].
(17)
Under an infinitesimal gauge transformation it transforms covariantly as δFμν​=−ig[Fμν​,α].
