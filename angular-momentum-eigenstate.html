Angular momentum eigenstate (∣j,m⟩)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
A joint eigenstate ∣j,m⟩ of J2 and Jz​, with eigenvalues ℏ2j(j+1) and ℏm. Its allowed labels satisfy j∈{0,1/2,1,…} and m=−j,−j+1,…,j.
