Bowen ball (Bn​(x,ϵ)={y:dn​(x,y)<ϵ})

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Discrete dynamical system Topological entropy Bowen metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A radius-ϵ ball for a Bowen metric consists of the points whose first n iterates stay within ϵ of the corresponding iterates of the centre. Balls at a fixed small radius distinguish forward orbit segments at that observational resolution.
