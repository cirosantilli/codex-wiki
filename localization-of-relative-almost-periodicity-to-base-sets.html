Multiplying a relatively almost periodic observable by a base-set indicator preserves relative almost periodicity: on each fiber an orbit value is either unchanged or zero, so add zero to the finite list of centers. Conditional norm covariance under a factor map also proves closure under convergence in the uniform conditional L2 norm.
