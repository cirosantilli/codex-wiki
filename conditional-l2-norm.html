Conditional L2 norm (∥f∥y​=(∫∣f∣2dμy​)1/2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Conditional expectation
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 2
Disintegrate over a factor map and take the L2 norm using the conditional probability on each fiber. Its square integrates to the global squared norm. The uniform conditional L2 norm takes the essential supremum of these fiber norms.
