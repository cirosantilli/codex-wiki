Closed decomposable complex volume forms are integrable

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Almost complex structure from a decomposable volume form
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
If Ω=θ1​∧⋯∧θn​ and dΩ=0, differentiating θj​∧Ω=0 gives dθj​∧Ω=0. Only the (0,2) component of dθj​ can survive this wedge product; multiplication by Ω is injective on that component. Thus no dθj​ has a (0,2) part. The bracket and differential-form criteria for integrability make the induced almost complex structure integrable. Local decomposability is part of the hypothesis and is not implied solely by nonvanishing of Ω∧Ω.
