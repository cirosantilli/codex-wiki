Nonmonotone monetary utility

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Utility function Expected utility
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
A utility function on monetary amounts need not be increasing merely because preferences obey the expected utility hypothesis. The outcome labels do not themselves supply a preference order. For example, values u(1)=2, u(2)=0, u(4)=3 define consistent expected utility comparisons even though the amount 2 is least preferred. Preferring more money is an additional assumption. It is distinct from risk aversion, which concerns the effect of spreading uncertainty for a specified increasing utility function.
