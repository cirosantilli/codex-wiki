Half-line NLS spectral functions (s=(a♯b♯​ba​),S=(A♯B♯​BA​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Integrable systems Defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 87 Articles: 1
Initial scattering gives s and boundary-time scattering gives S. The defocusing reduction and unit determinants give the displayed matrix forms. The initial functions a,b are analytic in the upper half-plane, while the boundary functions A,B are bounded in the first and third quadrants. Their finite-horizon compatibility is the half-line NLS global relation.
