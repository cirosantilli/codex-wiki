Additive category with truncated primary torsion (Ap​)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Enriched category Semi-additive category Additive category
OurBigBook.com Words: 116
Fix a prime number p. Take the full subcategory of finitely generated abelian groups with no element of order p2. It is closed under finite direct sums and subgroups. If tp​(G) is the p-primary torsion subgroup, the map G→R(G)=G/(ptp​(G)) is a reflection into this subcategory: every map to an allowed group kills ptp​(G). Therefore ordinary kernels in a category and reflected cokernels in a category supply all finite categorical limits and colimits. No nonzero finitely generated group has zero reflection, so the epimorphisms are precisely surjections and are all conormal epimorphisms. Multiplication by p2 on Z is a monomorphism but not a normal monomorphism: its cokernel here is Z/pZ, whose kernel is pZ, not p2Z. Multiplication by p is normal; composing it with itself gives this nonnormal monomorphism.
