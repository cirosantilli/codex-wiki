Two-sheeted extension of superextremal Kerr spacetime (ρ2/(r2+a2)+z2/r2=1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Analytic extension of a spacetime
OurBigBook.com Words: 108
For 0<M<a, Δ=(r−M)2+a2−M2>0, so no horizon blocks need adjoining. The oblate Cartesian coordinates of the Kerr metric satisfy the displayed relation, where ρ2=x2+y2. Keeping only the positive root r leaves a cut along the disk z=0, ρ<a. Continue r through zero away from its boundary, the Kerr ring singularity, onto a negative-radius sheet. Equivalently cut two Cartesian sheets along the disk and join opposite banks, so r changes sign continuously. The metric is analytic at the disk interior in signed oblate coordinates since Σ=a2cos2θ=0 there. The extension has asymptotically flat ends at r→+∞ and r→−∞; only the singular ring remains inextendible by an analytic Lorentz metric.
