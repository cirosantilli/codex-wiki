Shallow-water approximation

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Shallow water equations
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The shallow-water approximation applies when a fluid layer's vertical scale is much smaller than its horizontal scale. Vertical acceleration is then negligible to leading order, pressure is hydrostatic pressure, and the horizontal velocity can be represented by a depth average.
