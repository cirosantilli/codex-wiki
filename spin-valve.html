Spin valve

 ... Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Electrical resistance and conductance Electrical resistance Magnetoresistance
OurBigBook.com Words: 185 Articles: 3
A device with a reference magnetic layer whose magnetization is comparatively fixed and another layer whose magnetization responds more readily to an applied field. The resistance reads their relative alignment. Fert's account describes how such giant magnetoresistance sensors entered hard-disk read heads in 1997.[ref]
