Lyman-alpha absorption (1s+γ→2p)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Hydrogen atom Lyman-alpha line
OurBigBook.com Words: 507 Articles: 8
A neutral hydrogen atom absorbs a photon near the Lyman-alpha resonance by excitation from 1s to 2p. Absorption lines in a quasar spectrum can therefore locate hydrogen along the sightline by their redshift, subject to peculiar-velocity and line-profile effects.
