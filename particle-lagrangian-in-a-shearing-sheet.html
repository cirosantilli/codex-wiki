Particle Lagrangian in a shearing sheet (L2​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Gravitational instability of an astrophysical disk Shearing sheet
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
Expand a unit-mass particle's Lagrangian about an axisymmetric circular orbit of radius r0​ and frequency Ω0​, using r=r0​+x and φ=Ω0​t+y/r0​. Circular-orbit balance removes the linear radial term; total-time-derivative invariance of a Lagrangian removes r0​Ω0​y˙​. To second order,
L2​=21​(x˙2+y˙​2+z˙2)+2Ω0​xy˙​−Φt​,Φt​=−qΩ02​x2+21​Ωz2​z2.
(5)
Here q is the orbital shear parameter and Ωz​ is the vertical epicyclic frequency. The Euler-Lagrange equations retain the local Coriolis acceleration and tidal gravity.
