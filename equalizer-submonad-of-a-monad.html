Equalizer submonad of a monad (βA​:RA⟶TA)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Monad
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
In a category with equalizers, the equalizer of ηTA​,TηA​ defines a functor R by βB​Rf=TfβA​. It has unit α and multiplication θ characterized by
βA​αA​=ηA​,βA​θA​=μA​TβA​βRA​.
(10)
Cancelling the monomorphisms βA​ reduces its unit and associativity laws to those of T, so β:R⇒T is a monad morphism. The split equalizers associated with a monad show that αTA​ is invertible. If every TβA​ is monic, then every TαA​ is invertible, the naturality square of β at βA​ is a pullback in a category, and R is an idempotent monad. This monicity hypothesis is essential to this last argument; arbitrary functors need not preserve monomorphisms.
