Sum of the largest eigenvalues (Fk​(X)=∑i=1k​λi​(X))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex set Support function
OurBigBook.com Words: 215 Articles: 3
For a real symmetric matrix X with eigenvalues λ1​≥⋯≥λn​, the sum of its k largest eigenvalues is the support function of the fantope:
Fk​(X)=max{tr(XY):0⪯Y⪯I, trY=k}.
(31)
Here ⪯ is the Loewner order. In an orthonormal eigenbasis for X, the objective depends only on the diagonal entries of Y, which lie in the capped simplex. Thus the identity reduces to the sum of the largest components of the eigenvalues.
