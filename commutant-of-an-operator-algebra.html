Commutant of an operator algebra (A′=EndA​(T))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Centralizer of a subalgebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 358 Articles: 4
The commutant of A⊆End(T) is A′={b:ba=ab for all a∈A}. This centralizer is an operator algebra. The double-centralizer theorem for semisimple operator algebras and Schur–Weyl duality use this meaning, which is distinct from the centralizer of a single element of a group.
