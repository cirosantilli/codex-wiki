Algebraic structure

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 256 Articles: 4
An algebraic structure is a set equipped with specified operations satisfying selected axioms. Groups, rings and vector spaces are examples. Its homomorphisms preserve the operations belonging to that structure.
