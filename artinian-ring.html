Artinian ring

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Noetherian ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 341 Articles: 7
An Artinian ring satisfies the descending chain condition on ideals. A commutative ring is Artinian exactly when it is Noetherian and has Krull dimension zero.
