Equality of top-hat jet parity growth rates (γ=21​∣kV∣1−e−4∣k∣L​)

 ... Fluid mechanics Shear layer Vortex sheet Kelvin-Helmholtz instability Top-hat planar jet Equal-density top-hat planar-jet dispersion relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For a top-hat planar jet in stationary fluid of the same mass density, odd and even velocity potentials have the same temporal growth rate. Put s=tanh(∣k∣L). Their dispersion relations use s and 1/s, but s​/(1+s)=1/s​/(1+1/s). Their real phase velocities are respectively Vs/(1+s) and V/(1+s). The common growth rate is strictly below ∣kV∣/2 for finite ∣k∣L>0, approaching the isolated vortex sheet value when the sheets decouple.
