Time-ordered product (T{O1​⋯On​})

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Wick's theorem Time ordering
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A time-ordered product sorts operator insertions from latest time on the left to earliest time on the right. Reordering odd fermionic operators contributes the sign of their permutation. For bosonic A,B, T[A(t)B(t′)]=θ(t−t′)A(t)B(t′)+θ(t′−t)B(t′)A(t). Vacuum expectations of free time-ordered products are evaluated by the Wick theorem.
