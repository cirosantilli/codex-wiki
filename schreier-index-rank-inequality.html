Schreier index-rank inequality

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Nielsen–Schreier theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
If H has finite index d in a finitely generated group G, then Schreier's lemma gives
d(H)≤1+d(d(G)−1).​
(29)
Equality holds when G is a free group of finite rank.
