Reduced equations for an anisotropic rotating fluid (D⊥​∇⊥2​Φ=2Ω∂z​w,D⊥​w=−2Ω∂z​Φ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Rotating fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
Under critical balance in a rapidly rotating fluid, define u⊥(0)​=z×∇⊥​Φ and D⊥​=∂t​+{Φ,⋅}. Leading horizontal incompressibility gives zero divergence, but at the next order ∇⊥​⋅u⊥(1)​=−∂z​w. Its Coriolis acceleration contribution survives in the vertical vorticity equation, producing the displayed coupled system. The vertical momentum equation uses the leading pressure p0​=2ΩΦ. A flow that stays purely horizontal is independent of z in this reduced system; the assertion is not an exact theorem for arbitrary fast solutions of the full rotating Euler equations.
