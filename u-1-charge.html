U(1) charge (Q∈Z)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Unitary representation Representation of the circle group
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For the circle group parameterized by eiθ with period 2π, a one-dimensional representation acts as eiθ↦eiQθ, and its integer label Q is its charge. Periodicity forces Q∈Z. A tensor product representation adds U(1) charges, a dual representation negates them, and restriction to the finite cyclic group generated by e2πi/n retains only Qmodn. Integer normalization must be fixed before discussing a largest finite subgroup left by a condensate.
