Implication introduction rule (→I)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Natural deduction
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The implication introduction rule discharges a temporary assumption A from a derivation of B, producing A→B. Under the Curry-Howard correspondence, a body t:B under a:A gives the lambda abstraction λaA.t:A→B. Unused assumptions may also be discharged.
