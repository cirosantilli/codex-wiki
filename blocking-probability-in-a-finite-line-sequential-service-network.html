Represent N telephone lines by a closed migration process with free-line, operator and automated-service colonies. Accepted arrivals have rate ν when a free line exists, c operators complete at rate λmin(k,c), and i automated calls complete at rate μi. Define Dc​(k)=∏h=1k​min(h,c) and
Hc​(n)=∑i=0n​Dc​(n−i)i!(ν/λ)n−i(ν/μ)i​.
(7)
The product-form stationary distribution of a closed migration process and Poisson arrivals see time averages give the displayed blocking probability. This extends a single-operator model without treating the independent operators as a server with rate cλ when fewer than c calls are present.
