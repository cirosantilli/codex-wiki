Symmetric power (SnV)

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor product Tensor algebra Symmetric algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 493 Articles: 8
The nth symmetric power SnV is the quotient of V⊗n that identifies tensors differing by a permutation of their factors. Equivalently, over characteristic zero it is the subspace of symmetric tensors.
