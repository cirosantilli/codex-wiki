Fourier coefficient of a measure on the circle (μ​(n)=∫e−inxdμ(x))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier transform Fourier transform of a finite measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
For a finite complex measure on the additive circle group R/(2πZ), its Fourier coefficients are integrals against the group characters e−inx, n∈Z. They obey ∣μ​(n)∣≤∥μ∥, where the norm is the total variation norm of a measure. A probability measure has zeroth coefficient one. The normalized Haar measure m(dx)=dx/(2π) has all nonzero-frequency coefficients zero. A bounded family of finite Radon measures converges in the weak-star topology exactly when its coefficients converge to those of the proposed limit: extend linear combinations to all continuous tests using the density of trigonometric polynomials and the common variation bound.
