Letter to Foscarini by Robert Bellarmine (1615-04-12)

Primary letter in English translation, with its three numbered arguments. The date is 12 April 1615. Hypothetical mathematical use, scriptural interpretation and an actual physical demonstration are distinguished. The examination prints 1616; that is not the date of this document.
