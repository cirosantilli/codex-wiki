Measured output (ym​(t))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Control theory Plant (control theory) Output (control theory)
OurBigBook.com Words: 79 Articles: 1
The measured output is the information about the plant output available to the controller. It may differ from the actual plant output because of measurement noise or feedback delay. In the ideal examples ym​=y=x, so the controller observes the actual current state exactly.
