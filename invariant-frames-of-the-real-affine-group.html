In exponential scale coordinates, the real affine group has matrices (eα0​β1​). The left and right Maurer-Cartan forms are respectively Ddα+Te−αdβ and Ddα+T(dβ−βdα). Their dual vector fields obey [DL​,TL​]=TL​ and [DR​,TR​]=−TR​, displaying the opposite bracket sign for right-invariant vector fields.
