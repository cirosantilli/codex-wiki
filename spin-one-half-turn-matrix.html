Spin-one half-turn matrix (e−iπJ2​=​001​0−10​100​​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Spin Irreducible spin representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
In the ordered spin-one basis (m=1,0,−1), the second angular momentum operator satisfies J23​=J2​. Therefore its matrix exponential reduces to I−isinθJ2​+(cosθ−1)J22​. A half-turn exchanges the two outer weights and negates the middle weight. Applied to the pion isospin triplet, this fixes the rotation factor in G parity.
