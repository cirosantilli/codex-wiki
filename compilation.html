Compilation

 Home Computer science Programming language Compiler
OurBigBook.com Words: 455 Articles: 2
Compilation is the translation process performed by a compiler, which may include checking and transforming an abstract syntax tree. Translation can finish before execution, or happen during execution through just-in-time compilation. Reusing the translated representation can avoid repeated parsing and repeated constant folding when only the inputs change. This does not guarantee a speedup: translation itself costs work, and the choice of representation and execution mechanism matters.
