Mapping-telescope realization of the rational group with square-free denominators (Hn​(T)≅Qsq​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Mapping cylinder Mapping telescope
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
List the primes as p0​,p1​,… and form the mapping telescope of degree-pi​ maps Sn→Sn. Its reduced homology vanishes outside degree n, while
Hn​(T;Z)≅lim​(Zp0​​Zp1​​⋯)≅Qsq​.
