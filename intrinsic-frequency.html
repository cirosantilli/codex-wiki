Intrinsic frequency (ω)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave Atmospheric internal gravity wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 157 Articles: 3
The intrinsic frequency is the frequency observed in a frame moving with the basic flow. For a wave of frequency ω and horizontal wavenumber k in a uniform current U, it is ω=ω−kU.
