Pants decomposition ({γi​}i=13g−3​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Hyperbolic geometry Hyperbolic surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 344 Articles: 6
A collection of disjoint essential simple closed curves that cuts a closed genus-g surface into 2g−2 surfaces of type pair of pants. There are 3g−3 curves. Geodesic representatives are used for Fenchel–Nielsen coordinates.
