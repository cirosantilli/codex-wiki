Trait

 Home Computer science Programming language Object-oriented programming
OurBigBook.com Words: 114 Articles: 1
A reusable type and collection of members that can be composed into a class in object-oriented programming. In Scala, a trait can declare required members and provide concrete implementations. Several traits can contribute mixin behavior to one class, subject to linearization and member-conflict rules.
