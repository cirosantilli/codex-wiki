Bilateral exponential forcing of an overdamped oscillator (f(t)=e−∣t∣)

OurBigBook.com Words: 95
For a>b>0 with a,b=1, the solution of (Dt​+a)(Dt​+b)y=e−∣t∣ that decays at both infinities is
y(t)=⎩⎨⎧​(a+1)(b+1)et​,(a−1)(b−1)e−t​+a−b2​(a2−1e−at​−b2−1e−bt​),​t≤0,t≥0.​
(69)
Its Fourier transform is 2/[(1+k2)(a+ik)(b+ik)]. The lower half-plane contains only the pole −i, while the upper half-plane contains i,ia,ib. Inverting by the residue theorem gives the two expressions. Direct substitution verifies the equation separately on each side, and y,y′ match at zero, so there is no spurious impulsive source. The negative-time response is nonzero because the forcing already acts before zero, even though the Green function is causal. Coinciding rates a=1 or b=1 require limits with polynomial times exponential terms.
