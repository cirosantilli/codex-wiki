For slowly depositing particles, Bx​=−vs​B/(uh) and local adjustment gives u∼β∗​B1/3 with the constant-speed entraining gravity current on a slope coefficient. At leading order h∼h0​+E(x−x0​) and B1/3=Bo1/3​−[vs​/(3β∗​E)]ln[1+E(x−x0​)/h0​]. Its validity requires settling slow compared with the current and, for small E, compared with entrainment.
