Seifert form (θF​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Knot Seifert surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 822 Articles: 23
For oriented curves x,y on a Seifert surface, the Seifert form is
θF​([x],[y])=lk(x+,y),
(4)
where x+ is a positive normal push-off of x.
