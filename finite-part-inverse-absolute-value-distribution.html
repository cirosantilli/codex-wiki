Finite-part inverse-absolute-value distribution (Pf(1/∣k∣))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Distribution theory Distribution (mathematical analysis) Hadamard finite-part integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
Subtract a test function's value at zero before integrating 1/∣k∣ near zero, and retain the ordinary tail integral. This defines an even tempered distribution. Changing the subtraction cutoff adds a multiple of the Dirac delta distribution. Multiplication by k gives sgnk.
