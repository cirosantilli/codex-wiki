Dirichlet domain (DG​(p))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Properly discontinuous group action
OurBigBook.com Words: 199 Articles: 1
For a group of isometries acting properly discontinuously on the hyperbolic plane, and a point p with trivial point stabilizer, the closed Dirichlet domain is
DG​(p)={x:ρ(x,p)≤ρ(x,gp) for every g∈G}.
(26)
It is the intersection of the closed half-planes bounded by perpendicular bisectors between p and its other group orbit points. The group orbit is locally finite and the hyperbolic plane is a proper metric space, so every point has a nearest group orbit point. This shows that the translates of DG​(p) cover the plane. Distinct interiors are disjoint, since being strictly closer to two different group orbit points is impossible. Boundary ties are allowed in the closed domain. Thus it is a geometric fundamental region.
