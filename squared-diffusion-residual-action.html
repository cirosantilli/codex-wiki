Squared diffusion-residual action (S[h]=∫(ht​−ν∇2h)2ddxdt)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Diffusion equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 149 Articles: 1
For constant ν, variation of this positive functional gives (∂t​+ν∇2)(∂t​−ν∇2)h=0, or htt​−ν2∇4h=0. The diffusion equation ht​=ν∇2h gives zero-residual minima, but is only a subset of the stationary equations of the squared functional. Each nonzero spatial Fourier mode has both growing and decaying temporal solutions in the stationary equation. Boundary data decide which stationary paths are admissible. In two spatial dimensions, the bulk action is invariant under the anisotropic scale invariance x↦bx, t↦b2t, h↦h.
