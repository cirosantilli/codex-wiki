Jones-Wenzl idempotent (fn​)

 ... Geometry and topology Knot theory Link Link diagram Kauffman bracket Temperley-Lieb diagram algebra

The Jones-Wenzl idempotent has identity-diagram coefficient one, satisfies fn2​=fn​, and annihilates each adjacent cap-cup generator on either side. With δ=−A2−A−2 and Δ0​=1, Δ1​=δ, Δn​=δΔn−1​−Δn−2​, it is constructed recursively by fn​=F−(Δn−2​/Δn−1​)Fen−1​F, where F=fn−1​⊗1. The required denominators must be nonzero. Its closed bracket trace is Δn​=(−1)n[n+1].
