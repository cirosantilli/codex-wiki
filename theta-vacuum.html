Theta vacuum (∣θ⟩)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Yang-Mills theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 391 Articles: 9
A theta vacuum is a coherent state ∣θ⟩=∑n​einθ∣n⟩ formed from gauge vacua of different winding numbers. Large gauge transformations shift n and act on this state by a phase. The Yang-Mills theta term gives the corresponding weighting of spacetime topological sectors.
