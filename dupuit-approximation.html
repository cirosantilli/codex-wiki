Dupuit approximation

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Aquifer Unconfined aquifer
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
The Dupuit approximation treats flow in a shallow unconfined aquifer as predominantly horizontal, with hydrostatic pressure p=ρg(h−z) and horizontal pressure gradient independent of depth. Integrating Darcy's law over the saturated depth then gives a volume flux per unit width depending only on the groundwater height and its slope.
