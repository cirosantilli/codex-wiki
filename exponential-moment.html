Exponential moment

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Moment-generating function
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
An exponential moment of a random variable X is an expectation E[eθX] or E[eθ∣X∣] at a specified parameter θ. Its finiteness controls tail decay and the domain of the moment-generating function.
