Homologous vertical structure of a constant-opacity alpha disk

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Accretion disk Alpha disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 206 Articles: 2
Let R=k/(μm​mp​) and choose h6=3ακΣ2R4/(16σΩ5). Set z=hζ, ρ=(Σ/h)D, p=ΣΩ2hP, T=(Ω2h2/R)Θ and F=αΣΩ3h2Q. Vertical hydrostatic equilibrium, viscous dissipation, radiative diffusion and the ideal gas law reduce to P′=−Dζ, Q′=9P/4, Θ′=−DQ/Θ3, P=DΘ. Midplane symmetry gives Q(0)=0; a regular radiative-zero surface has P=Θ=0 at a universal ζs​, with 2∫0ζs​​Ddζ=1. All physical parameters have disappeared. Uniqueness of this normalized boundary-value problem makes every physical profile a rescaling of one dimensionless solution.
