Use the convention Auxx​+2Buxy​+Cuyy​=0, with discriminant B2−AC. Here A=1, B=0, C=sign(y).
For y>0, the discriminant is −1<0, so the equation is an elliptic partial differential equation, already in the canonical Laplace equation form uxx​+uyy​=0.
For y<0, the discriminant is 1>0, so it is a hyperbolic partial differential equation, the wave equation uxx​−uyy​=0. Introduce characteristic coordinates
ξ=x+y,η=x−y.
(57)
Then ∂x​=∂ξ​+∂η​ and ∂y​=∂ξ​−∂η​, giving
uxx​−uyy​=4uξη​=0.
(58)
On y=0, the discriminant vanishes and the principal part has rank one; the degenerate parabolic type is uxx​=0. This is an interface line, rather than an open parabolic subdomain. Thus
