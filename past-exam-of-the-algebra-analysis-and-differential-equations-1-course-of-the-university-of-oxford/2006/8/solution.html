G(x,s)=sinh2sinh(1+min(x,s))sinh(1−max(x,s))​.​

It vanishes at x=±1, is continuous at x=s, and its first derivative has jump −1. Thus LG(⋅,s)=δs​ in the distributional derivative sense, with the sign appropriate to L=−d2/dx2+1. Integration against f gives the required boundary-value solution. The kernel is positive and symmetric, as is also apparent in the figure.
