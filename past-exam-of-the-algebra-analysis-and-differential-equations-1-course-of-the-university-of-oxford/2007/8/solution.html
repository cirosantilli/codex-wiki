Choose characteristic parameter t with initial value at t=0. The characteristic equations are
x˙=x,y˙​=2y,z˙=xy,(x,y,z)(0)=(s,s,s2).
(29)
The first two integrate immediately; substitution into the third gives
x=set,y=se2t,z=3s2​(2+e3t),t∈R.​
(30)
Eliminating t gives the projected parabola
y=sx2​,x/s>0.​
(31)
The sign restriction selects the half-parabola actually reached from the initial point.
The projection map (s,t)↦(set,se2t) has Jacobian determinant se3t=0, so the initial curve is noncharacteristic for every allowed s. Its global inverse is
s=yx2​,t=log(y/x),
(32)
defined precisely when x,y have the same nonzero sign. Every such point is reached by exactly one characteristic. Hence the region of unique determination from these initial data projects onto
{(x,y):xy>0},​
(33)
the first and third quadrants. The axes are not reached for finite parameter from s=0, and the other two quadrants have no method of characteristics issued from this initial curve.
As a direct check, substitution of the inverse parameters gives the solution
z(x,y)=3y22x4​+3xy​(xy>0).
(34)
It has z(s,s)=s2, and differentiating gives xzx​+2yzy​=xy. This is quadrant uniqueness for unequal dilation characteristics.
