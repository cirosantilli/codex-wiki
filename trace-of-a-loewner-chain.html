When the indicated limit exists continuously and the hulls are generated by its past, it defines the trace of the Loewner chain. The hull Kt​ includes the curve up to time t and any regions it disconnects from infinity. Therefore swallowing a point by Kt​ is different from visiting that point by the Loewner trace. For SLE, the continuous trace is part of its basic existence theory.
