Absolute-frequency conservation in steady shear (ω˙=0,ω=ω−kU)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Hydrodynamic stability Absolute hydrodynamic instability Absolute frequency
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A time-independent ray Hamiltonian conserves the stationary-observer absolute frequency. A horizontally uniform background also conserves horizontal wavenumber. The intrinsic frequency ω=ω−kU(z) nevertheless changes when the ray moves through shear. These follow from the Hamiltonian ray-tracing equations and are essential to identifying a critical level of an internal gravity wave.
