Repeated singleton-removal allocation

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution Multinomial distribution Uniform balls-in-bins allocation
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
In repeated uniform balls-in-bins allocation, remove each ball that is alone in its bin and independently reallocate all remaining balls in the next round. If Bj​ balls remain, the conditional expectation of the next count is Bj​[1−(1−1/n)Bj​−1] for Bj​≥2, and zero for Bj​≤1. The surviving-ball indicator random variables need not be independent. For two balls and two bins, both remain with probability 2−j after j rounds, so EBj​=21−j.
