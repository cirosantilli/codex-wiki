Magnetic tweezers

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetostatics Magnetic dipole
OurBigBook.com Words: 269 Articles: 5
A generic instrument for force and torque uses a magnetically responsive bead in a controlled magnetic field, with optical tracking. Translational force requires a field gradient. Permanent or anisotropic moments can feel orienting torque; an isotropic induced moment parallel to the field has no such torque. This definition concerns the physical instrument, without biological applications.
