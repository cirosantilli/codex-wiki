Coeval binary population

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Binary star
OurBigBook.com Words: 241 Articles: 3
A coeval binary has components formed at the same time. A population of such binary stars can still have a distribution of system ages. If component masses have independence and the state at age t is determined separately for each component, the states have conditional independence given t. If g(t) and w(t) are the conditional probabilities of two states, the unconditional unordered pair fractions are E[g(t)2] and 2E[g(t)w(t)], not generally products of marginal state fractions.
