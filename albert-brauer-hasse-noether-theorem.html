Albert-Brauer-Hasse-Noether theorem (0→Br(K)→⨁v​Br(Kv​)∑invv​​Q/Z→0)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Central simple algebra Brauer group
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
For a number field K, localization of the Brauer group is injective. Its image is exactly the finite-support tuples of local classes whose local Brauer invariants sum to zero. The non-Archimedean summands have invariant group Q/Z, real summands have {0,1/2}, and complex summands are zero. For a cyclic character χ and parameter a, the invariant of the local cyclic algebra is χ(ArtKv​​(a)). Thus the zero-sum condition on principal parameters is the additive form of Artin reciprocity.
