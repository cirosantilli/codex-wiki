Chiral-superfield component expansion

With left Grassmann derivatives, a chiral superfield is a finite expansion in chiral coordinates yμ=xμ+iθσμθˉ:
Φ(y,θ)=ϕ(y)+2​θψ(y)+θ2F(y).

Here ϕ is a complex scalar field, ψ a Weyl spinor and F an auxiliary field. The translation form of its ordinary-coordinate expansion is
Φ(x,θ,θˉ)=exp(iθσμθˉ∂μ​)(ϕ(x)+2​θψ(x)+θ2F(x)).

Φ=ϕ+2​θψ+θ2F+iθσμθˉ∂μ​ϕ−2​i​θ2∂μ​ψσμθˉ−41​θ2θˉ2□ϕ,□=ημν∂μ​∂ν​.
