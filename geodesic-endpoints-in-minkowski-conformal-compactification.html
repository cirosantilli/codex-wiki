Geodesic endpoints in Minkowski conformal compactification

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Asymptotically flat spacetime Conformal completion Minkowski conformal compactification
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
A complete inertial timelike geodesic in Minkowski spacetime has x=b+vt with ∣v∣<1. Under p=arctan(t−r), q=arctan(t+r), both p,q tend to the same endpoint, giving past timelike infinity and future timelike infinity. A complete null geodesic has one finite limiting null coordinate at each end and reaches past null infinity and future null infinity. Complete spacelike geodesics have r>∣t∣ asymptotically and both ends approach spacelike infinity. The endpoints have finite compactified coordinates but occur at infinite physical affine parameter. A conformal transformation preserves null paths as unparametrized geodesics; it does not generally preserve timelike or spacelike geodesic paths.
