Simon's problem

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum Fourier transform Hidden subgroup problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 271 Articles: 5
Simon's problem asks for the hidden string p∈{0,1}n promised by f(x)=f(y) exactly when x⊕y∈{0n,p}. It is the hidden subgroup problem on (Z2​)n with hidden subgroup {0n,p}.
