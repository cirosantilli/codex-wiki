Lorentz boost (t′=γ(t−vx),x′=γ(x−vt))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Lorentz transformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 223 Articles: 4
A Lorentz boost relates inertial frames moving at a constant relative velocity without an additional spatial rotation. In units with light speed one, the displayed transformation preserves t2−x2, with Lorentz factor γ=(1−v2)−1/2. Applying it to a static classical field-theory soliton produces a uniformly moving solution in a Lorentz-invariant theory.
