Gas-driven migration of a binary centre of mass

For a nearly circular stellar orbit of radius ab​ in gas moving at (1−η) times the local circular speed, the averaged tangential gas drag on the centre of mass is −ηκb​vK​. Orbit-averaged drag work gives
a˙b​=−2ηκb​ab​,τb​=2ηκb​1​.
(6)
If m2​/m1​=ϵ≪1 and aerodynamic stopping time scales as mk, 0<k<1, then τ/τb​≃ηϵk≪1. Internal contraction can outpace stellar migration. Tightly gas-coupled drift requires solving radial and azimuthal motion together instead of this weak-drag approximation.
