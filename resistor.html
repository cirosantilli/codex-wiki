Resistor

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Electrical resistance and conductance Electrical resistance
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A resistor is an electrical circuit component whose intended behavior is electrical resistance. An ideal ohmic resistor obeys Ohm's law, V=IR, and converts electrical energy into heat at power P=I2R through Joule heating. It provides the dissipative term in a series RLC circuit.
