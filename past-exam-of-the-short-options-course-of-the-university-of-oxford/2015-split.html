The original cover dates this examination to 22 June 2015, 9.30–11.00am. The archive stores it under 2014/T/a13752w1.pdf; the cover determines the year. Source: released examination archive.
The intact copy in the Mathematics Part A release, member FOI-20210708-1 - Data/a13752w1 (4).pdf, restores the previously missing printed page 3 and supplies a complete readable counterpart. Questions 1–2 had already been recovered and answered; those answers and all answers to questions 5–8 are retained. The solutions now cover all eight questions and their 37 leaf subparts. The earlier source provenance above is unchanged.
