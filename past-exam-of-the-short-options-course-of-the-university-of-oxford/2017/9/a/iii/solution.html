Infection grows above the susceptible threshold St​=1/α and declines below it. A wave depletes susceptibles; subsequent recruitment can restore the threshold and permit renewed growth. In the neutral linear regime, perturbations around the positive fixed point oscillate, so repeated small waves occur for sufficiently small perturbations over any fixed finite observation period.
For example, in scaled variables x=αS, y=αI, the choice r=αB=0.2, x0​=1.03, y0​=0.19 produces the repeated positive waves illustrated below. This finite trajectory illustrates recurrence of outbreaks without claiming a nonlinearly attracting periodic orbit from the linearization alone.
