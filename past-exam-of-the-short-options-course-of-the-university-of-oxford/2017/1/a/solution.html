For distinct odd primes p,q, quadratic reciprocity states
(qp​)(pq​)=(−1)(p−1)(q−1)/4.​
(1)
Here the Legendre symbol records whether a nonzero congruence class is a quadratic residue. The first supplementary law for quadratic reciprocity and second supplementary law for quadratic reciprocity give
(p−1​)=(−1)(p−1)/2,(p2​)=(−1)(p2−1)/8.
(2)
Thus minus one is a square exactly when p≡1(mod4), and two is a square exactly when p≡1 or 7(mod8).
To test the specified congruence class, factor 111=3⋅37. Since 2017≡1(mod4), multiplicativity of the Legendre symbol and quadratic reciprocity yield
(2017111​)​=(20173​)(201737​)=(32017​)(372017​)=(31​)(3719​)=(1937​)=(1918​).​
(3)
There is no sign change on swapping 19 and 37 because 37≡1(mod4). Finally 18=2⋅32, so multiplicativity of the Legendre symbol gives (18/19)=(2/19)=−1, since 19≡3(mod8). Therefore
x2≡111(mod2017) has no integer solution.​
(4)
