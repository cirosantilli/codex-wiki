Euler's totient function counts the integers in a complete complete residue system that are coprime to the modulus:
ϕ(n)=#{1≤a≤n:gcd(a,n)=1}.​
(8)

In particular, ϕ(2017)=2016. Adjoining the prime factor 2 changes neither the odd factor nor its count of units, since ϕ(2)=1. The product formula gives
n=4034=2⋅2017,ϕ(4034)=(2−1)(2017−1)=2016.​
(10)
This is a positive integer distinct from 2017, as required.
