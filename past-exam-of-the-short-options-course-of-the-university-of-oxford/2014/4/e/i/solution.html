 ... Short options course of the University of Oxford Past exam of the short options course of the University of Oxford 2014 4 e i
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
Use the six-dimensional vector space of real symmetric 3×3 matrices as the target, rather than all nine matrix entries independently. Define
F:M3​(R)⟶Sym3​(R),F(A)=ATJA−J.
(25)
Its derivative is
DFA​(H)=HTJA+ATJH.
(26)
The given J is symmetric with J2=I. Every A∈G is invertible, since taking determinants of ATJA=J gives (detA)2=1. For any symmetric target S, choose
H=21​AJS.
(27)
Then ATJH=21​JJS=S/2, and its transpose is also S/2. Thus DFA​(H)=S and the derivative is surjective at every point of G.
The regular level set theorem now gives
dimG=9−6=3.​
(28)
It also proves smooth embeddedness in the entire ambient vector space of matrices. The target symmetry is what gives the correct codimension.
