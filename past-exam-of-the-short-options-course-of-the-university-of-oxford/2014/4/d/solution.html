Apply the regular level set theorem to F(A)=detA−1 on the n2-dimensional vector space of real matrices. At a point with determinant one, part (b) gives DFA​(H)=tr(A−1H). Choosing H=A/n gives DFA​(H)=1, so this derivative is surjective.
Hence the special linear group SL(n,R) is a smooth submanifold of dimension n2−1. At the identity,
TI​SL(n,R)={H:trH=0}.​
(24)
This is the special linear Lie algebra. When n=1, the group is a single point and the tangent space is zero, consistent with the same formula.
