At a constrained critical point, the directional derivative of f vanishes along every admissible tangent velocity:
Dfa​(v)=0(v∈Ta​M).
(45)
For a regular zero locus, Ta​M=kerDga​. A linear functional vanishing on this kernel lies in the row space of Dga​. Equivalently there are Lagrange multipliers λ1​,…,λk​ such that
∇f(a)=DgaT​λ=j=1∑k​λj​∇gj​(a),g(a)=0.​
(46)
Geometrically, ∇f is orthogonal to the tangent space, so it lies in its orthogonal complement, spanned by the independent constraint gradients. Thus the level set of f has no first-order change in a tangent direction. The equations provide candidates for constrained extrema; they must still be classified and compared, and boundary or singular cases must be treated separately. The condition is necessary for a regular constrained extremum, not a guarantee of a maximum.
