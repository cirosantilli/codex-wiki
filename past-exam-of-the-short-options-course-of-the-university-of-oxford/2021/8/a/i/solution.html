Differentiate the worldline with respect to θ:
dθdt​=2​Tcosθ>0,dθdx​=−2​cTκsinθ,c1​dtdx​=−κtanθ.
(86)
Since ∣tanθ∣≤1 and 0<κ<1, this is a future-directed timelike curve. The proper time element satisfies c2dτ2=c2dt2−dx2, hence
dθdτ​=2​Tcos2θ−κ2sin2θ​.
(87)
Alice's total proper time is therefore
τA​=22​T∫0π/4​1−(1+κ2)sin2θ​dθ.​
(88)
This definite integral is an exact answer; it is an elliptic integral of the second kind. In notation E(α∣m)=∫0α​1−msin2u​du, it is 22​TE(π/4∣1+κ2). Although this parameter exceeds one, the integrand is real on the stated interval because its minimum squared value is (1−κ2)/2>0.
The endpoint events have (t,x)=(0,0) and (2T,0). Sam stays at x=0 in his inertial frame, so his elapsed proper time is 2T. For κ>0, the integrand is strictly less than cosθ except at θ=0, and τA​<2T.
