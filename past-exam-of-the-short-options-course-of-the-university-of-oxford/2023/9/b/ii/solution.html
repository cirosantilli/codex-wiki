The non-axis nullclines are u1​+Au2​=1 and Bu1​+u2​=1. For A>1, B<1, the first has intercepts (1,0),(0,1/A) and the second has (1/B,0),(0,1). Their axis intercept order rules out a positive intersection. Below the first line u1​ increases and above it decreases; below the second u2​ increases and above it decreases. Thus S2​ is a saddle point and S3​=(0,1) is stable.
For strictly positive initial populations, both coordinates stay positive and are bounded above by their individual logistic equations. There are no interior periodic orbits: the Dulac function 1/(u1​u2​) makes the divergence of the multiplied vector field −1/u2​−ρ/u1​<0, so the Bendixson-Dulac theorem applies. The stable manifold of S2​ is the invariant red-only axis, and S1​ is repelling. The phase portrait, or the Poincaré-Bendixson theorem with these boundary directions, therefore gives
(u1​,u2​)⟶(0,1)​.
(57)
The qualification u2​(0)>0 matters: a grey population identically absent cannot drive extinction. In dimensional variables, positive grey presence leads to N1​→0, N2​→K2​ in this no-hunting case.
