OurBigBook.com Words: 121
The terms ri​Ni​ give intrinsic per-capita population growth. The terms −ri​Ni2​/Ki​ model intraspecific crowding in a logistic equation, while −ri​bij​Ni​Nj​/Ki​ model interspecific competition. Here Ki​ is the single-species carrying capacity, and bij​ measures the competitive effect of one unit of species j relative to one unit of species i in that species' crowding term. The last term −δN2​ is per-capita removal of grey squirrels: constant effort, rather than a constant number removed per unit time.
The intrinsic rates r1​,r2​ and removal rate δ have units of inverse time. The capacities K1​,K2​ have the same density units as the respective Ni​; with both densities expressed in the same units, b12​,b21​ are dimensionless. The two equations form a Competitive Lotka-Volterra model with density regulation and harvesting.
