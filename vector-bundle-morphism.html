Vector bundle morphism

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 294 Articles: 5
A morphism of vector bundles over one base is a smooth map between their total spaces that commutes with projection to the base and is linear on each fiber. In local vector bundle trivializations it is multiplication by a smoothly varying matrix.
