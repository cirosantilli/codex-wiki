Photocurrent (Iph​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Photodetector Photodiode
OurBigBook.com Words: 292
Photocurrent is the electric current attributable to illumination. Here Iph​≥0 denotes its magnitude after background subtraction. For a photodiode without internal multiplication, let P be incident radiant flux concentrated at wavelength λ, and let η be external quantum efficiency, including losses before collection. An incident photon has energy Eγ​=hc/λ, with Planck constant h and speed of light c. Dividing energy per second by energy per photon gives the incident photon rate Φγ​. Multiplying by η gives the mean collected pair rate N˙pair​; multiplying by elementary charge q gives
Φγ​=hcPλ​,N˙pair​=ηΦγ​,Iph​=qN˙pair​,R(λ)=PIph​​=hcηqλ​.
(40)
The factor is q: each collected pair replenishes an electron at one contact and removes an electron at the other. A cross-section of the external wire therefore counts one transferred electron per completed pair, consistent with charge conservation. The electron and electron hole are two internal charge carriers, not two independent transfers through that wire.
Choose an illustrative photodiode model with λ=650nm, η=0.75 and P=1.00μW. Using the exact SI values h=6.62607015×10−34Js, c=299792458ms−1 and q=1.602176634×10−19C gives
Eγ​Φγ​N˙pair​R(650nm)Iph​​=3.05607055×10−19J,=3.27217577×1012s−1,=2.45413183×1012s−1,=0.393195267AW−1,=0.393195267μA.​
(41)
A mean fraction 0.75 does not require each fourth photon to be lost in a repeating pattern. It specifies a mean conversion rate. Nor does 0.75 mean 0.75AW−1: quantum efficiency is dimensionless, whereas spectral responsivity compares electric current with power.
At fixed radiant flux, longer-wavelength photons have less energy, so more arrive per second. This explains the rising straight line when quantum efficiency is held constant. A real photodiode's quantum efficiency varies with wavelength; the plotted assumption cannot be extended past its band gap absorption cutoff. At fixed incident photon rate instead, Iph​=ηqΦγ​ has no additional factor of λ.
