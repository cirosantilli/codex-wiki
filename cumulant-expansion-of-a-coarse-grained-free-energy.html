Cumulant expansion of a coarse-grained free energy

Writing the interaction as V and averaging over the fast modes, which have a Gaussian distribution, gives the cumulant expansion Feff​=F0​+⟨V⟩−21​(⟨V2⟩−⟨V⟩2)+⋯. The second cumulant selects connected Feynman diagrams.
