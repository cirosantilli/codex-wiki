Half-range Fourier transform (F±(k)=∫R±​​eikxf(x)dx)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier transform
OurBigBook.com Words: 200 Articles: 2
With the eikx convention, a right-supported decaying function has a transform analytic in the upper half-plane and a left-supported one in the lower half-plane. Incident modes continued with small imaginary frequency can have poles outside the initial convergence strip; these poles must be tracked separately from outgoing scattered waves.
