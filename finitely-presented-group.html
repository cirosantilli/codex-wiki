Finitely presented group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Group presentation Finite group presentation
OurBigBook.com Words: 505 Articles: 7
A finitely presented group is a group admitting a finite group presentation. Finite presentability concerns the existence of such a presentation, not the decidability of its word problem for a group.
