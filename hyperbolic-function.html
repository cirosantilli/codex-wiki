Hyperbolic function (sinh,cosh,tanh)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Exponential function
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 10
Hyperbolic functions combine the exponential function to describe a hyperbola much as trigonometric functions describe a circle. Their two basic members are the hyperbolic cosine and hyperbolic sine, defined for every real number t by
cosht=2et+e−t​,sinht=2et−e−t​.
(51)
Their derivatives exchange the two functions:
dtd​cosht=sinht,dtd​sinht=cosht.
(52)
These formulas follow by applying differentiation to the exponential function definitions. [ref]
Adding and subtracting gives cosht+sinht=et and cosht−sinht=e−t. Multiplying these equalities proves the hyperbolic Pythagorean identity:
cosh2t−sinh2t=1.​
(53)
Consequently the parametric equation (x,y)=(cosht,sinht) traverses the entire right branch of the unit hyperbola x2−y2=1, where x≥1. For example, t=0 gives (1,0), while t=±1 gives approximately (1.543,±1.175). Compare (cosθ,sinθ) on the unit circle, where the squared coordinates add to one. The minus sign changes the geometry from a closed circle to an unbounded hyperbola.
The other members are ratios and reciprocals: hyperbolic tangent is tanht=sinht/cosht, hyperbolic cotangent is cotht=cosht/sinht, and hyperbolic secant is secht=1/cosht; denominators must be nonzero. The hyperbolic sine and hyperbolic cosine both solve the second-order linear differential equation f′′=f. In fact f(t)=Acosht+Bsinht has f(0)=A and f′(0)=B, making this basis convenient for an initial value problem. Their geometry also makes hyperbolic substitution useful for integrals containing 1+u2​, and the hyperbolic cosine gives the physical catenary of a hanging chain.
