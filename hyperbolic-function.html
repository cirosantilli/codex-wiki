Hyperbolic function

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Exponential function
OurBigBook.com Words: 239 Articles: 8
Hyperbolic functions are combinations of the exponential function analogous to trigonometric functions. The hyperbolic cosine and hyperbolic sine satisfy cosh2x−sinh2x=1 and parametrize a unit hyperbola.
