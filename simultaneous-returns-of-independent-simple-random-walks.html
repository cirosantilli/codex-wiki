Simultaneous returns of independent simple random walks

OurBigBook.com Words: 38
For d independent symmetric walks on Z, the probability that all are at the origin at time 2n is
(4n(n2n​)​)d≍n−d/2.
(106)
The Borel-Cantelli lemmas therefore show that simultaneous returns occur only finitely often almost surely when d≥3.
