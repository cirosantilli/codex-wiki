For the linear dispersive Stokes equation, ω(ν)=ω(k) factors as (ν−k)(ν2+kν+k2−1)=0. In D+​={Imk>0,Reω(k)<0} the two displayed other roots lie in the lower half-plane, so both can be used in the global relation. To check the root locations, at k=ib they have imaginary part −b/2. A root could reach the real axis only where Reω(k)=0, so continuity preserves their signs throughout this connected domain. The branch points ±2/3​ lie outside its closure. The weights A1​=(k−ν2​)/(ν1​−ν2​) and A2​=(ν1​−k)/(ν1​−ν2​) obey A1​+A2​=1 and νj′​=−Aj​.
