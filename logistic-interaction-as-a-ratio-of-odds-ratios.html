Logistic interaction as a ratio of odds ratios (eδ=ORA∣B=1​/ORA∣B=0​)

For binary predictors A,B, a logistic regression with linear predictor a+bA+cB+δAB has conditional odds ratios eb at B=0 and eb+δ at B=1. Their ratio is eδ. The same interaction can be computed from the four cell logit link values as δ=η11​−η10​−η01​+η00​. Independent positive event and nonevent counts in each cell give an estimated variance for this contrast equal to the sum of all eight reciprocal counts. An interaction on the odds ratio scale is not an interaction on the absolute-risk scale.
