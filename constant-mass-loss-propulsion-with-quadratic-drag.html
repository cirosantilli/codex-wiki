Constant mass-loss propulsion with quadratic drag (mu˙=QV−αu2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Momentum Rocket equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
A body ejecting mass at constant rate Q>0 and relative exhaust speed V>0, against quadratic drag αu2, has remaining mass m=m0​−Qt. A short-interval momentum balance gives mu˙=QV−αu2. From rest, separation of variables gives
u(t)=αQV​​tanh(QαV​​logm0​−Qtm0​​).
(7)
For final mass mf​>0, this formula applies until t=(m0​−mf​)/Q. The zero-drag limit is the rocket equation uf​=Vlog(m0​/mf​). Two dimensionless variables are αV/Q and m0​/mf​.
