Homology relations for radiative stars

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure Stellar homology
OurBigBook.com Words: 763 Articles: 12
For chemically uniform, gas-pressure-supported stars with homologous dimensionless profiles, mass conservation and hydrostatic pressure support equation give ρc​∝M/R3 and Tc​∝M/R. Suppose opacity scales as κ∝ρaTb and specific nuclear energy generation as ϵ∝ρcTd. Then
Lnuc​∝M1+c+dR−3c−d,Lrad​∝M3−a−bR3a+b.
(17)
The second scaling follows from radiative diffusion in a star, L∝RTc4​/(κc​ρc​). Equating the two gives
R∝M(a+b+c+d−2)/(3a+b+3c+d)
(18)
when the denominator is nonzero. Fixed composition and self-similar profiles are essential; these are relations between idealized models rather than universal stellar laws.
