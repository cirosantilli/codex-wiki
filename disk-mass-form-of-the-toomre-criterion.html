Disk mass form of the Toomre criterion (Q∼(H/r)(M∗​/MD​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Gravitational instability of an astrophysical disk Toomre's stability criterion
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
In a star-dominated Keplerian disk with H≃cs​/Ω and characteristic disk mass MD​∼πr2Σ, the Toomre stability criterion becomes Q∼(H/r)(M∗​/MD​). Thus instability can occur at a disk-to-star mass ratio comparable to the small disk aspect ratio. The numerical coefficient depends on whether MD​ means a local annular estimate or integrated enclosed mass.
