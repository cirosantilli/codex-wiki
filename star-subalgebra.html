Star-subalgebra

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Star-algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A linear subalgebra closed under the involution. In bounded operators, this means closure under the adjoint operator; neither norm closure nor operator-topology closure is part of the definition. These distinctions matter in the Von Neumann double commutant theorem and the Kaplansky density theorem.
