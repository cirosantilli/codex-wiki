Double series (∑m​∑n​amn​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Series (mathematics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
A double series is a series whose terms carry two indices. For nonnegative terms, define its sum as the supremum of all finite subsums; iterated sums in either order give this same value, possibly infinity. For absolutely summable terms, both orders also agree and give a finite sum. Without these hypotheses, an order of summation must be specified and changing it can change the result. A triangular index set, such as 0≤n≤m, can be represented by setting the other terms to zero.
