Tensile failure criterion (σ3′​=−T0​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Brittle fracture
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
In the simplest strength-cutoff model, opening begins when the least compressive effective stress reaches minus the tensile strength: σ3′​=−T0​ with compression positive. In Mohr's circle, this is contact with the vertical line σn′​=−T0​ at τ=0. The criterion represents an opening mechanism and differs from frictional shear failure.
