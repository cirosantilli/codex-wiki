For a finitely generated torsion Iwasawa module, its characteristic ideal is the product of the height-one prime ideals raised to the lengths of the corresponding localizations. In the elementary-divisor description it is generated by p∑ai​∏fjbj​​. It is unchanged by finite modules and multiplicative in short exact sequences of torsion modules.
