Step-2 planar group coordinates ((v,a)(w,b)=(v+w,a+b+21​(v1​w2​−v2​w1​)))

OurBigBook.com Words: 52
Write a group element as exp(v+a[e1​,e2​]). The step-two Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff formula gives the displayed multiplication and inverse (−v,−a). The scalar a is the antisymmetric second signature coordinate, which equals the signed area for a closed planar path. The Carnot-Carathéodory distance is comparable to ∣v∣+∣a∣1/2 near the identity, with the same homogeneous scaling globally.
