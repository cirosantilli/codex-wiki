Standalone expository note by Paul Garrett, verified from the full-name byline on p. 1. Hosted on his University of Minnesota faculty website; the host is not the author.
Online text: www-users.cse.umn.edu/~garrett/m/algebra/cantor_schroeder_bernstein.pdf
The document header is dated 19 February 2005; this does not establish a first publication date.
Cited passages: p. 1, theorem for two injections between arbitrary sets and introductory no-choice statement; pp. 1–2, chain types and forward or reversed pairings. The article independently proves its alternative complement pairing.
