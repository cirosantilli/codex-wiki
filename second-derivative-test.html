Second-derivative test

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Hessian matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 1
At an interior local maximum of a twice differentiable function, its Hessian matrix is negative semidefinite; at a local minimum it is positive semidefinite. For a scalar function on Euclidean space, the Laplacian is therefore nonpositive at a local maximum and nonnegative at a local minimum.
