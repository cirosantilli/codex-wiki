Formal logarithm (logF​)

 ... Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Formal group law
OurBigBook.com Words: 459 Articles: 6
Over a characteristic-zero field, a one-dimensional commutative formal group law has a unique strict isomorphism to the additive formal group, called its formal logarithm. It satisfies logF​(F(X,Y))=logF​(X)+logF​(Y).
