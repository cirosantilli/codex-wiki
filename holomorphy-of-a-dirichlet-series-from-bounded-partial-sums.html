Holomorphy of a Dirichlet series from bounded partial sums (supx​∣∑n≤x​bn​∣<∞⟹∑bn​n−s holomorphic for Res>0)

Writing B(x)=∑n≤x​bn​, the Abel summation formula gives ∑bn​n−s=s∫1∞​B(x)x−s−1dx. If B is bounded, the integral and its differentiated integrands converge uniformly on compact subsets of Res>0, giving a holomorphic function. A nonprincipal Dirichlet character has bounded partial sums because it is periodic and its sum over a period is zero.
