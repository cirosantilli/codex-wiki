Mersenne number (Mk​=2k−1,k≥1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Integer Positive integer
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
A Mersenne number has the form Mk​=2k−1 for a positive integer k. Its binary representation consists of k ones. For example, M4​=15 is composite, while M5​=31 is prime. If k=ab with a,b>1, the finite geometric series factorization
2ab−1=(2a−1)(1+2a+⋯+2a(b−1))
(8)
has two factors greater than one. Consequently a Mersenne prime requires a prime exponent, but M11​=23⋅89 shows that this condition alone is insufficient.
