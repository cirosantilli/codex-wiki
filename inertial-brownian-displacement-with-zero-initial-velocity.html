Inertial Brownian displacement with zero initial velocity (⟨x2⟩=2D[t−2τ(1−e−t/τ)+τ(1−e−2t/τ)/2])

A free particle following Langevin dynamics initialized at zero position and velocity has relaxation time τ=m/ζ and thermal diffusivity D=kB​T/ζ. Its position is the convolution of the random force with [1−e−t/τ]/ζ. The fluctuation-dissipation relation for a Langevin particle gives force covariance amplitude 2ζkB​T, and integrating the squared kernel yields the displayed variance. At short times it grows as 2Dt3/(3τ2) because the initial velocity is cold; at long times it grows as 2Dt. A Maxwellian initial velocity produces a different short-time transient.
