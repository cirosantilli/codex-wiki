For R>0, a spherical ultraviolet cutoff gives the following exact examples of the one-loop critical-mass subtraction integral:
J3​(R)=2π2R​1​arctanR​Λ​,J4​(R)=16π21​logRΛ2+R​,J5​(R)=12π31​(Λ−R​arctanR​Λ​).
(35)
The areas of the unit spheres in dimensions three, four and five are 4π, 2π2 and 8π2/3. Dividing by (2π)D reduces the integrals to constants times ∫0Λ​dp/(p2+R), ∫0Λ​pdp/(p2+R) and ∫0Λ​p2dp/(p2+R) respectively. Elementary integration proves the formulas. As R→0, the first has a power divergence, the second a logarithmic divergence, and the third a finite limit. These examples make the ordinary upper critical dimension four visible without treating a self-consistent one-loop equation as an exact interacting critical exponent prediction.
