Fermionic bilinear charge algebra ([QA​,QB​]=Q[A,B]​,QA​=∫d3x:ψ†Aψ:)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Supersymmetric quantum mechanics Fermionic Fock space Canonical anticommutation relations
OurBigBook.com Words: 141 Articles: 1
For constant internal matrices acting on Dirac fields, the equal-time canonical anticommutation relations give
[ψi†​ψj​,ψk†​ψl​]=δjk​ψi†​ψl​−δil​ψk†​ψj​
(61)
with the corresponding spatial delta functions for fields. The quartic terms cancel after fermion reordering. Integrating proves the displayed algebra for well-defined global Noether charges, with normal ordering and a regulator that preserves the nonanomalous internal symmetry. Orthogonal chiral projectors make the left/right mixed commutators zero. This reduces current-charge closure to a finite matrix calculation.
