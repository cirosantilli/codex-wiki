Left Ore set

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Noncommutative algebra Ore condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
A multiplicative subset S is left Ore when, for every s∈S and r∈R, there are s′∈S and r′∈R such that s′r=r′s.
