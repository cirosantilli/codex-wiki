Direct Riemann integrability

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Riemann integration Riemann integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
A function g on [0,∞) is directly Riemann integrable when its upper and lower sums on an equal-width mesh are absolutely finite and converge to the same finite integral as the mesh width tends to zero. A locally absolutely continuous integrable function with integrable derivative has this property: the upper-minus-lower sum is bounded by mesh width times its total variation. The derivative bound therefore gives bounded variation as well as tail control. This stronger form of integrability is a hypothesis of the key renewal theorem.
