Classification of integral matrices satisfying the third cyclotomic polynomial

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Structure theorem for finitely generated modules over a principal ideal domain Finitely generated torsion-free module over a principal ideal domain
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
An integral matrix satisfying A2+A+I=0 makes Zn a module over the Eisenstein integers by letting the primitive cube root act as A. This module is finitely generated and torsion-free, hence free. Since the Eisenstein integers have integer rank two, such matrices exist only for n=2m; for that dimension every one is integrally conjugate to
(01​−1−1​)⊕m.
