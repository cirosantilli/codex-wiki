Inverse beta decay (νˉe​+p⟶e++n)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Radioactive decay Beta decay
OurBigBook.com Words: 788 Articles: 4
In the reaction displayed here, an electron antineutrino interacts with a proton to produce a Positron and a neutron. It turns an otherwise hard-to-observe incident particle into two products with different detection signatures. A prompt Positron signal followed by neutron capture can identify a candidate much more selectively than a single energy deposit. Inverse beta decay is a weak interaction; the incident electron antineutrino is detected through its reaction products.
