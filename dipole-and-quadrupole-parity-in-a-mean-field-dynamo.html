Dipole and quadrupole parity in a mean-field dynamo

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Dynamo action Mean-field dynamo
OurBigBook.com Words: 182 Articles: 2
In a reflection-symmetric mean-field dynamo model, dipole parity can be represented by an even poloidal-potential amplitude and an odd toroidal-field amplitude; quadrupole parity reverses these assignments. The names describe the reflection classes appropriate to the model, not arbitrary electric multipoles. A complex amplitude P=A+iB allows a phase rotation by i to interchange the two classes when the unperturbed equations have that phase symmetry.
