The displayed descending filtration combines powers and group commutators. In a pro-p group, take the closure of the generated product at each step. For a powerful p-group these layers are Pi​(G)=Gpi−1, with iterated closed power subgroups in the infinite case. The successive quotients are elementary abelian p-groups.
