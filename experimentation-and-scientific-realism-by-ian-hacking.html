Experimentation and Scientific Realism by Ian Hacking (1982)

Original article, Philosophical Topics 13(1), 1982, pp. 71–87. The opening distinguishes experimental entities used as tools from belief in the truth of whole theories. A related chapter appears in the 1983 book Representing and Intervening; its pagination is different.
