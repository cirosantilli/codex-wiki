Assume a constant annual force of infection λ>0 across ages and calendar years. For independent sampled people with ages ai​>0 and binary results yi​, fit
Yi​∼Bernoulli(pi​),pi​=1−e−λai​.
(31)
Applying the complementary log-log link gives
ln[−ln(1−pi​)]​=ln(λai​)=β0​+lnai​,β0​=lnλ.​
(32)
Therefore fit a binomial regression with the complementary log-log link, an intercept, and lnai​ as an offset with coefficient fixed at one. Age is measured in years. An ordinary freely fitted coefficient of age, or even a freely fitted coefficient of lnai​, would describe a different exposure model. The annual force of infection estimate is
λ=eβ​0​ year−1​.
(33)
An infection-naive person's one-year infection probability is 1−e−λ, which is approximately λ only when that rate is small.
The log-likelihood being maximized is, apart from constants,
ℓ(β0​)=∑i​[yi​ln{1−exp(−ai​eβ0​)}−(1−yi​)ai​eβ0​].
(34)
A confidence interval for β0​ can be exponentiated to give a positive interval for λ. For groups of nj​ people at a common age aj​, use Yj​∼Binomial(nj​,1−e−λaj​) with the same offset. If an age band is broad, use individual ages or average 1−e−λa over the band's age distribution instead of treating its midpoint as exact. No numerical estimate is possible without the survey observations.
The biological interpretation assumes that immunoglobulin G positivity records previous infection, persists over the relevant ages, and is not substantially produced by maternal antibodies or vaccination. These are conditions to check, rather than conclusions from the fitted curve. Use known-positive and known-negative serum controls to assess the assay threshold, and validate specificity against relevant other infections. Cross-reactive antibodies to other flaviviruses can produce positive dengue virus serology, so a positive result need not be a marker specific to dengue virus exposure.[ref]
If independently established assay diagnostic sensitivity and diagnostic specificity are Se and Sp, the observed positive probability is
pi,obs​=Se(1−e−λai​)+(1−Sp)e−λai​.
(35)
Use that observation model in the binomial likelihood; it is generally no longer the simple intercept-plus-offset complementary log-log link fit. With only the stated survey, one cannot freely estimate Se, Sp, antibody loss and an arbitrary infection history simultaneously. A single binary result also does not identify how many subsequent infections a person has experienced.
