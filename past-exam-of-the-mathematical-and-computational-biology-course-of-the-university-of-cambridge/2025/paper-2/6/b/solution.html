Under the conventional σ>1 criterion, the supplied summaries classify it as a small-world network: clustering is about 2.071 times the reference value, while path length is only about 1.021 times the reference value. Two reference random graphs permit a numerical comparison but do not establish the variability or statistical significance of that classification.
There is a literal consistency problem with describing these summaries as those of a four-vertex simple unweighted graph. Any finite graph distance is at most three, whereas the supplied mean path lengths exceed three. A local clustering coefficient with at most three neighbours can only be 0, 1/3, 2/3 or 1 under the usual definition, not 0.7 or 0.8. Thus the result above is the intended calculation from the given summaries; they cannot all describe an ordinary four-node simple unweighted network. A larger network or a separately specified weighted convention would be needed for a literal network interpretation.
