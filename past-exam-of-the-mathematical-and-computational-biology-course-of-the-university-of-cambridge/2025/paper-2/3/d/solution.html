The two descendant branches of an equal-rate Jukes-Cantor model compose to a single branch of duration 2T. More explicitly, the model is symmetric, so summing over the ancestral base gives ∑a​πa​Pai​(T)Paj​(T) and a total mismatch probability 43​(1−e−2μT). Thus use the pairwise difference fraction directly with t=2T, rather than halving the differences and correcting the halves separately:
μ​​=−29×106ln(1−4×0.29/3)​=1.68568×10−8 year−1​.​

This is the parameter in the printed formula of (c). For a mutation rate measured as actual changes per site per year, the conversion derived in (b) gives
r=43​μ​=1.26426×10−8 site−1year−1​.
(28)
The latter is directly comparable to the naive estimate in (a): allowing repeated changes raises that estimate by about 26.4%. The exponent parameter and actual event rate are two conventions for the same fitted process, not two different biological estimates.
