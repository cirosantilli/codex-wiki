This is an immigration--death process, equivalently an M-M-infinity queue: admission occurs at rate α regardless of occupancy, and each of n animals is discharged independently at rate δ. Its state-transition diagram through four animals is

Every upper arrow points right and every lower arrow points left. Thus qn,n+1​=α and qn,n−1​=nδ for n≥1, with no discharge from zero. Four is only the limit of the requested sketch, not an absorbing state or a capacity boundary.
