Admissions add to the expected value at rate α, while the expected aggregate discharge rate is δE[X(t)]. Consequently
N′(t)=α−δN(t)​.
(42)
Multiplying this linear ordinary differential equation by the integrating factor eδt gives
dtd​[eδtN(t)]=αeδt.
(43)
Integrating from zero, using N(0)=0, gives eδtN(t)=α(eδt−1)/δ. Hence
N(t)=δα​(1−e−δt)​.
(44)
It starts at zero, has initial slope α, and approaches α/δ as t→∞, as the admission and discharge rates balance.
