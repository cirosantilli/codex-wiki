OurBigBook.com Words: 111
Build an FM-index: store the Burrows-Wheeler transform, cumulative character counts C, and a structure supporting fast rank queries for Occ(c,r). Then each step of backward search uses two rank queries instead of rescanning the last column. A wavelet tree or suitably checkpointed counts can supply these queries while keeping memory manageable. The counting cost is proportional to query length times the rank query cost, rather than repeatedly scanning the whole genome.[ref]
To recover genomic positions, store a sampled suffix array and follow the last-to-first mapping to a sampled position, adding the number of steps taken. More samples trade storage for faster position recovery. Index character counts and sample positions, rather than storing the quadratic-size full cyclic rotation matrix.
