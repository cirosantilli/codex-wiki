For a cell of length ℓ aligned with the capillary, the steady linear gradient produces the front-to-back concentration difference
Δc=ℓ​dxdc​​=Lc0​ℓ​.
(61)
At the given initial length, L=5×10−3 m, this is 100(20×10−6)/(5×10−3)=0.4 nM, above the stated threshold. Requiring Δc≥0.1 nM gives
L≤0.1 nM(100 nM)(20×10−6 m)​,Lmax​=2.0×10−2 m=2.0 cm​.
(62)
The diffusion coefficient does not enter this steady-gradient threshold. It does constrain experimental waiting time: for fixed reservoir concentrations, the slowest decaying mode of the diffusion equation has timescale L2/(π2D). With the supplied D, this is about 7.04 hours at 0.5 cm and 4.69 days at 2 cm; several such times may be required for an accurate steady state. Thus the maximum from the detection threshold is usable only if the reservoirs and cells remain suitable while the profile develops.
