The Jacobian matrix of the update map is
J(H,P)=(re−aPb(1−e−aP)​−arHe−aPabHe−aP​),J(0,0)=(r0​00​).
(21)
Its eigenvalues are r and zero. The extinction equilibrium is locally asymptotically stable for 0<r<1 and unstable for r>1, by linear stability analysis for a discrete dynamical system. In the latter case, even a small perturbation along P=0 grows as Ht​=rtH0​.
At r=1, an eigenvalue equals one, so the strict linear criterion does not decide stability. Directly, 0≤Ht+1​≤Ht​ and 0≤Pt+1​≤bHt​. Starting sufficiently close to extinction therefore keeps both coordinates close for all time, proving Lyapunov stability in the nonnegative state space. It is not asymptotically stable, since every nearby (H,0) is another equilibrium. This distinction makes the statement “stable when r>1 fails” precise.
