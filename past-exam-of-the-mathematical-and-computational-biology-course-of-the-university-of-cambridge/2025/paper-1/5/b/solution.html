The function is strictly concave on x>0. Its derivative is positive for 0<x<1, zero at x=1, and negative for x>1. Thus it rises from the limiting value −1 as x↓0, touches the horizontal axis at its unique maximum (1,0), and then decreases to −∞. It never crosses above the axis. The left endpoint is not included, and the slope tends to +∞ there.
