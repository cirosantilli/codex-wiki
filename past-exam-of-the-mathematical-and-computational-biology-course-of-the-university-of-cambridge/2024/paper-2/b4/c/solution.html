The averaging axis must be zero: average over sampled patterns while keeping the site index. A provisional choice for this particular trace is B=3000, retaining 2000 states:
def mean_methylation(meths, burnin):
    if not 0 <= burnin < len(meths):
        raise ValueError("burn-in must leave at least one state")
    return np.asarray(meths)[burnin:].mean(axis=0)

burnin = 3000

The fixed endpoints have estimate exactly one; adjacent sites are often smoothed into methylated or unmethylated stretches despite individual conflicting observations. The rescaled observations are only a visual comparison and are not another estimate of the posterior probability.
This is a finite-chain estimate for the conditional-product target defined in part (a). A burn-in chosen by inspection does not establish convergence, and the long excursions in this short Markov chain leave substantial uncertainty in individual-site estimates; several longer runs would be needed for precise inference. To sample the coherent joint alternative instead, pass score=joint_methylation_score and inspect that new trace before choosing its own burn-in.
