Use reproducible random starts and retain the best successful result:
rng = np.random.default_rng(2024)
starts = rng.uniform(-4, 4, size=100)
results = [opt.minimize(lambda z: f(z[0]), x0=[x0]) for x0 in starts]
successful = [result for result in results if result.success]
best = min(successful, key=lambda result: result.fun)
print(best.x[0], best.fun)
The estimate is xmin​≃−0.309213, fmin​≃0.0192409. This random-restart optimization improves coverage of the different attraction regions, but their number alone does not guarantee global optimality.
Here an analytic check is possible. For ∣x∣≥π/5, the envelope gives f(x)≥1−e−π2/125≃0.0759, above the candidate. On [0,π/5], f≥1. In the remaining interval [−π/5,0], the derivative condition is equivalent, with z=−x, to cot(5z)=0.08z. The left side decreases strictly from +∞ to −∞ while the right side increases, giving one minimum. This certifies the selected well as global rather than relying only on the restart experiment.
