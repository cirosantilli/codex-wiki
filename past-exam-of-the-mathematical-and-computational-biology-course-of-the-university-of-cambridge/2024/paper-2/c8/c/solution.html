For each alignment column, ignore gaps, form the empirical distribution of its amino acids, and calculate its Shannon entropy in bits. The requested alignment column information content is
Ri​=log2​(20)−Hi​=a:pia​>0∑​pia​log2​(20pia​).​
(6)
Thus it is the relative entropy from the column distribution to the uniform distribution on twenty amino acids, not the ordinary Shannon entropy itself. A column containing one residue type has the maximum value log2​(20)=4.321928 bits.
information = []
for j in range(A.shape[1]):
    counts, n = emission_counts(A[:, j])
    p = np.array(list(counts.values()), dtype=float) / n
    H = -np.sum(p * np.log2(p))
    information.append(float(np.log2(20) - H))
print(np.round(information, 4))
