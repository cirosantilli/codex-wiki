Under the stated topology probabilities, P(A)=2/3 and P(B)=1/3. Averaging the branch lengths from part (b), the expected length producing the folded 3:1 class is
B3:1​=32​(4E4​+2E3​+2E2​)+31​(4E4​+2E3​)=4E4​+2E3​+34​E2​.
(44)
The factor 2E2​ in type A includes both the one-descendant and three-descendant root branches. The expected length producing the 2:2 class is
B2:2​=32​E3​+31​(E3​+2E2​)=E3​+32​E2​.
(45)
With a common mutation rate per unit branch length, it cancels from the ratio of expected counts in this folded site frequency spectrum:
R=E3​+32​E2​4E4​+2E3​+34​E2​​.
(46)
For the Yule process,
RYule​=1/3+1/31+2/3+2/3​=27​=3.5​.
(47)
For Kingman's coalescent,
Rcoal​=1/3+2/32/3+2/3+4/3​=38​≈2.67​.
(48)
These are ratios of ensemble expected mutation counts, not the expected value of the random ratio in one tree. The stipulated topology weights apply to both processes, and their waiting-time rates depend only on the current number of branches.
