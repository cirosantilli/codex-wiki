The experiment follows phonon peaks as both momentum and energy transfer are varied. In inelastic neutron scattering, q=ki​−kf​ locates the lattice modulation and ℏω=Ei​−Ef​ measures the mode energy, so a softened mode can be distinguished from static diffraction alone. Measurements on deuterated TTF-TCNQ between 60 and 300K found a growing Kohn anomaly near 2kF​ below about 150K as the 54K transition was approached.[ref]
