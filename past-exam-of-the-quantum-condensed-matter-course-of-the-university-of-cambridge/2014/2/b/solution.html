The Drude model treats mobile electric charge carriers as independent particles with density n, signed electric charge q, isotropic mass m, and momentum relaxation time τ. In uniform steady fields,
0=q(E+v×B)−τmv​,J=nqv.
(10)
At zero magnetic field the drift velocity is qτE/m, giving electrical conductivity and electrical resistivity
σ0​=mnq2τ​,ρ0​=nq2τm​.​
(11)
The electrical conductivity is positive for either carrier sign. Scattering dissipates directed drift energy into the environment; a finite steady electric current is compatible with a short-lived drift momentum. The independent-carrier relaxation-time approximation omits detailed band anisotropy, quantum interference and microscopic collision correlations.
For electric current along x and magnetic field Bz^, the magnetic force deflects carriers toward one side of the sample. electric charge accumulates until a transverse Hall field cancels that force in an open transverse circuit. With Jy​=0, Ey​=vx​B, so
RH​=Jx​BEy​​=nq1​,ρyx​=nqB​.​
(12)
Electrons have negative RH​ in this convention, whereas positive carriers have positive RH​. For a strip of thickness d along z, the magnitude of the Hall electric potential difference across its width is ∣VH​∣=∣IB/(nqd)∣. Thus Hall measurements determine carrier sign and, within this one-carrier model, density, while combining RH​ with σ0​ determines charge carrier mobility μ=∣q∣τ/m=σ0​∣RH​∣.
Keeping both in-plane electric current components, the resistivity tensor is
ρ=(ρ0​B/(nq)​−B/(nq)ρ0​​),σ=ρ−1.
(13)
This fixes the off-diagonal sign convention and shows that inversion is not componentwise reciprocation. Under the Hall boundary condition Jy​=0, the ideal one-carrier isotropic model gives Ex​/Jx​=ρ0​, independent of magnetic field: there is no ordinary longitudinal magnetoresistance in this minimal model. Multiple carrier types or anisotropic bands can change both this conclusion and the simple RH​=1/(nq) inference. Their electrical conductivity must be added before inverting to obtain the total electrical resistivity.
