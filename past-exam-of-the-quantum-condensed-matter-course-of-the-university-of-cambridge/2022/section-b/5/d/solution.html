At nonzero band hybridization, the crossing becomes an avoided crossing. The separation at kc​ is 2α. If α≪Efl​, appreciable mixing occurs only where ∣Ak2−Efl​∣≲α; away from that region each branch retains nearly its original orbital character. For α∼Efl​, the avoided crossing is broad and both branches are substantially reshaped, as shown in the figure.
Use E−​ for the lower branch and E+​ for the upper one. For α>0,
E−​(k)<Efl​<E+​(k),mink​E+​(k)=E+​(0)=2Efl​+Efl2​+4α2​​.
(25)
The lower branch increases towards Efl​ from below. Thus an indirect gap exists in the exactly flat-band model. In the unbounded-parabola limit its lower bound is
Δind​=2Efl2​+4α2​−Efl​​≃Efl​α2​(α≪Efl​).​
(26)
A finite Brillouin zone gives a finite maximum of E−​ below Efl​ and hence a slightly larger indirect gap. This hybridization gap differs from the local direct gap 2α.
The material can therefore be a band insulator when the lower band is full and the upper band empty. With two spin-degenerate orbitals per unit cell, the required filling is
2 electrons per unit cell, i.e. half of the combined four-state capacity.​
(27)
With spin degeneracy removed, replace two by one for each independent spin sector. Other fillings are metallic in this noninteracting model.
