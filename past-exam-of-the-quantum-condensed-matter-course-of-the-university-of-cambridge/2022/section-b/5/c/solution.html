Write the dispersive band as Ep​=Ak2 with A>0 and define kc​=Efl​/A​. The uncoupled sketch is an upward-opening parabola and a horizontal flat band at positive energy. They cross on the sphere k=kc​ in three dimensions, or at ±kc​ in a one-dimensional cut:
Ep​(kc​)=Efl​,α=0.​
(24)
Without mixing the crossing is allowed. The left panel of the figure shows this case.
For dimensional clarity A=ℏ2/(2mℓ​) in the usual band effective mass convention. The printed product mp​ℏ2k2 is consistent only if mp​ is a curvature coefficient with inverse-mass units; it cannot simultaneously denote the effective mass itself. Using A avoids relying on that notation.
