Here the osmotic pressure is a two-dimensional surface pressure, with units of energy per area. In the dilute regime, independent whole chains provide the translational entropy, so
Π∼ℓ2kB​T​Nϕ​,ϕ≪ϕ∗.​
(20)
In the semidilute polymer solution, use a correlation blob containing g monomers. Within it the chain still follows the planar good-solvent law ξ∼ℓg3/4. Its area fraction is ϕ∼gℓ2/ξ2∼g−1/2, so g∼ϕ−2 and ξ∼ℓϕ−3/2. There is about one independent thermal contribution per blob area. The semidilute osmotic pressure of a two-dimensional polymer solution is thus
Π∼ξ2kB​T​∼ℓ2kB​T​ϕ3,ϕ∗≪ϕ≪1.​
(21)
It is independent of total chain length once each chain contains many blobs. The two estimates agree at ϕ∗∼N−1/2, where each gives (kB​T/ℓ2)N−3/2.
