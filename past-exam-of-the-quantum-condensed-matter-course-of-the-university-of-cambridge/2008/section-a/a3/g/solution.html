An entropic network has approximately one thermal energy of elastic cost per mesh volume, giving G0​∼kB​T/ξ3. At T=300 K, the supplied mesh size and viscosity give
G0​∼0.033 Pa,τ=η/G0​∼0.030 s.​
(12)
The intermediate-time plateau in the three-dimensional mean squared displacement is
⟨Δr2⟩plateau​∼πaξ3​=4.0×10−14 m2,rrms​∼0.20 μm.​
(13)
After the omitted fast initial transient, the log-log sketch is approximately flat for t≪τ, crosses over near τ, and grows linearly for t≫τ. The figure shows the Maxwell compliance prediction over this range.
An ordinary 25–30 frame-per-second recording samples intervals of about 0.03–0.04 s, comparable to τ. It therefore has little access to a clear plateau at lags well below the crossover, even if its positional precision resolves a displacement of a few tenths of a micrometre. Higher frame rates and calibrated localization noise would improve the modulus measurement. The bead radius is only twice the mesh size, so treating the network as a homogeneous continuum is also an approximation.
