A plasmon is a quantum of a collective longitudinal oscillation of electron density against the positive background. A small charge displacement creates an electric restoring field; Newton's law then gives the plasma angular frequency. Assuming approximately one conduction electron per atom and spacing d=3 A˚ gives n∼d−3=3.7×1028 m−3. Hence
ωp​=ε0​me​ne2​​∼1.1×1016 s−1.​
(25)
The ordinary frequency is about 1.7×1015 Hz, with energy ℏωp​∼7 eV. A different conduction-electron count per atom changes this estimate by its square root; dielectric and band-structure corrections also modify the simplest free-electron result.
