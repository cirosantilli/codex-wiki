A donor-state overlap estimate compares this radius with the mean dopant spacing d∼ND−1/3​. Taking d∼a∗ yields
ND​∼(a∗)−3=3.91×1024m−3=3.91×1018cm−3.​
(7)
There is no sharp geometrical onset: hydrogenic wavefunctions have exponential tails. If “overlap” is defined by neighbouring orbital diameters touching, d∼2a∗, the corresponding estimate is 4.88×1023m−3, smaller by a factor of eight. These are conventions for the onset of appreciable orbital overlap, not an exact critical density for a metal-insulator transition.
