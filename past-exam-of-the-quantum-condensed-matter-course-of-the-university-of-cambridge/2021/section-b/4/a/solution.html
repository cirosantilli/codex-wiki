For a Hamiltonian with a lattice-periodic potential V(r+R)=V(r), its simultaneous energy eigenstates and lattice-translation eigenstates can be chosen in the Bloch wave form
ψnk​(r)=eik⋅runk​(r),unk​(r+R)=unk​(r).​
(9)
Equivalently, ψnk​(r+R)=eik⋅Rψnk​(r). The index n labels an energy band; crystal momentum ℏk is defined modulo a reciprocal lattice vector. These are the content of Bloch's theorem.
