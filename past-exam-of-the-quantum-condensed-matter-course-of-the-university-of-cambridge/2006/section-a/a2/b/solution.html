For two species of chains with the same polymerization degree N, the number of independently translating objects per occupied site is reduced by N. The symmetric Flory-Huggins polymer blend has
kB​Tf​=Nϕlnϕ+(1−ϕ)ln(1−ϕ)​+χϕ(1−ϕ),kB​Tf′′​=Nϕ(1−ϕ)1​−2χ.

The symmetry still places the thermodynamic critical point at ϕc​=1/2, but now
χc​=N2​.​

The entropy of mixing per monomer is much smaller than for simple liquids, so even a weak unfavorable interaction can cause phase separation for long chains. If the energetic interaction parameter is χ=A/T with A>0, this model gives Tc​=AN/2; the conclusion relies on that specified temperature law.
