OurBigBook.com Words: 87
Write zc​ for the lattice coordination number and ϵAA​,ϵBB​,ϵAB​ for contact energies. Counting the excess of unlike contacts relative to the unmixed reference gives the Flory interaction parameter
χ=kB​Tzc​​[ϵAB​−2ϵAA​+ϵBB​​].​
(8)
Unlike contacts that are less favorable than the mean like contact give χ>0. For temperature-independent contact energies, χ is proportional to 1/T, so heating weakens this energetic preference relative to the entropy of mixing. Effective parameters in real solutions can also include entropic terms, often represented schematically by A/T+B; the pure contact-energy model does not explain every possible temperature dependence.
