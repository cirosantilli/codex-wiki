The gain bandwidth must be expressed as a frequency range:
Δν=c(600nm1​−700nm1​)≃7.14×1013Hz.
(5)
The gain bandwidth limit on a laser pulse then gives
Δt∼Δν1​≃1.4×10−14s,of order 10fs.​
(6)
The numerical coefficient depends on the pulse shape and width definition. For example a Gaussian pulse whose intensity spectral FWHM actually filled this width would have a transform-limited intensity duration about 6.2fs. A hard gain interval does not by itself specify such a FWHM. Reaching the estimated scale requires coherent mode locking and control of group velocity dispersion; the cavity round-trip time determines the pulse separation. An intensity autocorrelation measurement can estimate the pulse duration once its shape is specified.
