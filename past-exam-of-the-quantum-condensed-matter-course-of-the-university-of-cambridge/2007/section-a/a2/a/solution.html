 ... Quantum Condensed Matter course of the University of Cambridge Past exam of the quantum condensed matter course of the University of Cambridge 2007 Section A A2 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 130
Long anisometric molecules cannot pack independently. In the Onsager excluded-volume mechanism, two slender rods of length L and diameter D exclude a relative-position volume approximately 2L2D∣sinγ∣, where γ is their mutual angle. Aligning rods reduces this excluded volume, increasing the translational entropy available at a fixed number density. It simultaneously reduces orientational entropy. At sufficiently high concentration the positional gain wins, producing a nematic liquid crystal even for purely repulsive rods.
The nematic director is apolar: n and −n describe the same orientation. The molecules retain liquid-like positional disorder, in contrast to a smectic phase with layered order. A typical dimensionless concentration controlling the slender-rod competition is cL2D, although finite aspect ratio, flexibility and attractions change the transition. The mechanism illustrates that ordering can increase total entropy: the lost orientational freedom is compensated by less mutual obstruction.
