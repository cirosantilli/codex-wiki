For a smooth homogeneous solid and a partially wetting liquid at equilibrium, tangential balance at the contact line gives Young's equation
γSV​=γSL​+γLV​cosθ.​
(14)
The angle is measured through the liquid. Replacing solid-vapour area by solid-liquid plus liquid-vapour area changes free energy by −S per wetted area, where the spreading coefficient is
S=γSV​−γSL​−γLV​.​
(15)
For partial wetting, S=γLV​(cosθ−1)<0. Positive S favours a spreading film and zero macroscopic equilibrium contact angle; the finite-angle Young construction cannot be extrapolated to cosθ>1.
