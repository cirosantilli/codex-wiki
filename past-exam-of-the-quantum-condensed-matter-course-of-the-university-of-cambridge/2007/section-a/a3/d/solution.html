For a concentration blob with g ideal segments, ξ∼ag​ and concentration matching gives ϕ∼ga3/ξ3∼g−1/2. Thus the concentration-blob size in a theta solvent is
ξ∼ϕa​=6.7nm,g∼ϕ−2=100.​
(11)
The whole chain contains about ten blobs, so the separation of scales is moderate. Below ξ, theta-solvent segments have ideal chain statistics; above it, screening also gives Gaussian random-walk statistics for the sequence of blobs. There is no good-solvent self-avoiding regime to insert in this particular theta-solvent calculation. Density correlations and segment dynamics can still cross over at the blob scale.
