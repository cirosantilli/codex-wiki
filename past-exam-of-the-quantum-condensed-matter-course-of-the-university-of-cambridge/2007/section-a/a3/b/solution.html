Here R denotes a typical chain size, such as an rms end-to-end distance of a polymer chain, rather than the mean end-to-end vector of an isotropic solution, which is zero. The Flory approximation gives R∼aN3/5 in a good solvent, an ideal chain has R∼aN1/2 in a theta solvent, and a compact polymer globule has size R∼aN1/3 in a strongly poor solvent. These are scaling estimates with order-one prefactors omitted, treating the stated N as the segment count associated with the supplied statistical length.
