For a spherical micelle of sodium dodecyl sulfate with n molecules and a sharp tail-filled core of radius R, volume and head-group area give nv=4πR3/3 and na0​=4πR2. Dividing gives the spherical micelle geometry
R=a0​3v​=1.84nm,n=a0​4πR2​≃75.​
(2)
There is a consistency limit: R exceeds the supplied maximum tail length 1.67nm. Equivalently the amphiphile packing parameter is v/(a0​lc​)=0.368>1/3, outside the strict spherical-core bound. Thus the requested spherical assumption gives these estimates, but the three molecular dimensions cannot all be exact within that ideal sharp-core geometry. Effective head area, core structure or aggregate shape must adjust; silently replacing R by lc​ would no longer satisfy both packing equations.
