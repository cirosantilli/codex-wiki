The hydrogenic donor state radius is the Bohr radius rescaled by screening and effective mass:
aB∗​=a0​m∗/me​εr​​=(0.0529nm)0.216​=4.23nm.​
(19)
The donor-state overlap estimate compares the mean spacing nD−1/3​ to this radius. Taking the spacing as aB∗​ gives nD​∼(aB∗​)−3=1.32×1025m−3=1.32×1019cm−3. Taking it as the orbital diameter gives a factor eight less, 1.65×1018cm−3. Thus a geometric estimate is roughly 1018–1019cm−3, with its contact convention stated. The exponential tails do not have a sharp overlap boundary, and this estimate should not be identified with a precise metal-insulator transition threshold.
