Uniform absolute continuity for a finite measure

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Absolute continuity of measures
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
If μ(Ω)<∞, then μ≪ν exactly when for every ε>0 there is δ>0 such that ν(A)<δ implies μ(A)<ε. For necessity, a contrary sequence with ν(An​)<2−n and μ(An​)≥ε has tail unions decreasing to a ν-null limsup; continuity from above for finite μ contradicts absolute continuity.
