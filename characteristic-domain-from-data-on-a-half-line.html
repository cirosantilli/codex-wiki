Characteristic domain from data on a half-line

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Domain of dependence
OurBigBook.com Words: 133
For Noncharacteristic Cauchy data parameterized by t≥0, the method of characteristics produces a surface Φ(s,t)=(x,y,z). The part uniquely fixed as a classical graph through the data must retain both the allowed footpoint range and a nonsingular branch of the projected map. On a branch where inversion gives (s(x,y),t(x,y)), require t(x,y)≥0, together with the branch condition and a nonzero Jacobian determinant. The strict inequality t>0 describes its open interior; the endpoint characteristic t=0 supplies a boundary trace. A fold caustic can supply another boundary.
An algebraic formula obtained by eliminating s,t can extend beyond this domain of dependence. Its values there need not be selected by the original data, because their characteristic footpoints can lie on the unprescribed half of the data curve. Tracking the parameter range is therefore part of the uniqueness argument, separately from selecting a real square-root branch.
