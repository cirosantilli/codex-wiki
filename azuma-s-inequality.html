Azuma's inequality (P(MN​−M0​≤−t)≤e−t2/(2∑i​ci2​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
If a martingale has increments with ∣Mi​−Mi−1​∣≤ci​ almost surely for deterministic ci​, then each one-sided deviation of magnitude t>0 from M0​ has probability at most exp(−t2/(2∑i​ci2​)). If all ci​ vanish, the martingale is constant. The inequality gives concentration from bounded increments without requiring independent increments.
