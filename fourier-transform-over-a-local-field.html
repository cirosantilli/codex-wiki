Fourier transform over a local field (f​(y)=∫F​f(x)ψ(xy)dx)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier transform
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 3
For a local field F, an additive character ψ, and a Haar measure dx, the Fourier transform is
f​(y)=∫F​f(x)ψ(xy)dx.
(104)
It maps the Schwartz-Bruhat space to itself.
