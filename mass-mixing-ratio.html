Mass mixing ratio (q=ρa​/ρ)

 Home Chemistry Mixture Mass fraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
The mass mixing ratio used in a dilute radiative transfer model is absorber mass density divided by total atmospheric mass density. A hydrostatic pressure interval contributes absorber mass path qdp/g. Some meteorological conventions divide by dry-air mass instead; the convention must be stated. A volume or mole mixing ratio instead contributes absorber moles xdp/(gM), with background molar mass M.
