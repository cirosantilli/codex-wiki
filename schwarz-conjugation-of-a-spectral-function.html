Schwarz conjugation of a spectral function (F♯(k)=F(k)​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Complex number Complex conjugate Complex conjugation
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
This operation reflects a holomorphic function across the real axis while preserving holomorphic dependence on the new argument. A transform of real data with real kernel coefficients satisfies F♯=F. In a polygonal global relation, complex rotation factors are conjugated as well; an explicit prefactor i changes sign.
