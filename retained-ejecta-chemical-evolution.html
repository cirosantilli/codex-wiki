Retained-ejecta chemical evolution (d(gZ)/ds=−(1−βf)Z/(1−β)+yf)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy evolution Galactic chemical evolution Instantaneous recycling approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 1
Under the instantaneous recycling approximation, forming mass dM locks ds=(1−β)dM into stars. If a fraction f of returned material remains in the interstellar medium, the retained original metal mass is βfZdM, and retained newly made metal mass is fyds, where y is the stellar yield per locked mass. Subtracting the metal mass ZdM initially consumed gives the displayed equation. Pristine galactic gas inflow adds gas but no metals; enriched inflow adds its metal mass separately. The retained fraction applies to both old and newly made metals in this idealization.
