Cohomology operation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Cohomology
OurBigBook.com Words: 534 Articles: 11
A cohomology operation is a natural transformation between cohomology functors. Thus for every continuous map f, its pullback commutes with the operation: θ(f∗a)=f∗θ(a). This makes operations additional invariants of homotopy equivalence, beyond the additive groups and their cup products.
