Discrete location game

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Normal-form game
OurBigBook.com Words: 259 Articles: 1
A discrete location game can model firms choosing sites from {1,…,k} while customers at those sites choose a nearest firm. The customer masses, distance rule, permission to share a location and rule for ties are part of the model. For two firms, customer value two per site and equal splitting of ties, the payoff to a firm at x against one at y is
u(x,y)=⎩⎨⎧​x+y−1,k,2k−x−y+1,​x<y,x=y,x>y.​
(24)
For x<y, counting sites strictly left of the midpoint and assigning half of an equidistant site's value gives x+y−1, whether x+y is even or odd. The other firm receives the remainder, so this is a constant-sum game with total 2k. Changing tie priority can fundamentally change its Nash equilibria, especially when co-location is permitted.
