Support-preserving right-hand-side changes in linear programming (x∗ optimal, suppx⊆suppx∗, x≥0⟹x optimal for Ax)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Linear programming Linear programming duality Complementary slackness
OurBigBook.com Words: 130
For the linear program maximizing cTx subject to Ax=b, x≥0, let x∗ be optimal and let y∗ be an optimal dual linear program solution. Complementary slackness makes AjT​y∗=cj​ on every positive coordinate of x∗. Any nonnegative x supported on those coordinates therefore has
cTx=(y∗)TAx.
(5)
The same y∗ is dual feasible when the right-hand side is changed to b=Ax. Equality of feasible objectives and weak duality prove optimality of x for that modified problem.
There is also a direct primal proof. If z≥0 had Az=Ax and cTz>cTx, then x∗+ε(z−x) would remain feasible and nonnegative for sufficiently small ε>0. On zero coordinates of x∗, support inclusion makes its perturbation nonnegative; on positive coordinates, smallness preserves positivity. Its objective would improve, contradicting optimality of x∗.
