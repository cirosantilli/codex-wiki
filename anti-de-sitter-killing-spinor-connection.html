Anti-de Sitter Killing-spinor connection (Dμ​=∇μ​+γμ​/(2a))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supergravity Killing spinor
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
For constant sectional curvature −1/a2, spin curvature is [∇μ​,∇ν​]=−γμν​/(2a2). The added Clifford term in D has the opposite commutator, making D flat. On a simply connected region, parallel transport of any initial spinor is path independent and constructs solutions of Dϵ=0. Thus Anti-de Sitter spacetime and its universal cover admit the associated Killing spinors locally and, with appropriate global spin data, globally.
