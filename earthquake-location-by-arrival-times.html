Earthquake location by arrival times (ti​=t0​+Ti​(x))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Seismology
OurBigBook.com Words: 336 Articles: 6
Arrival-time location estimates a hypocenter and origin time by comparing picked phase arrivals with predicted travel times. Linearization gives residual changes δti​=δt0​+∇Ti​⋅δx. Poorly distinct derivative columns cause tradeoffs, while incorrect wave speeds or phase labels cause systematic bias even with many stations.
