The impulsive disruption by stellar mass loss threshold is F=1/2, with unique finite root α≃1.79101236. Decreasing α shifts mass to larger stars and increases F, so the idealized system is globally unbound for α<αcrit​. The derivation requires rapid, well-mixed loss and absence of a confining external potential.
