Four-force (Kμ=dpμ/dτ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Four-momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The four-force is the proper time derivative of four-momentum. For constant rest mass and coordinate three-force F=dp/dt, its components are Kμ=γ(F⋅v/c,F) in the convention pμ=(E/c,p). Thus it packages the power transfer and three-force into one Lorentz-covariant vector.
