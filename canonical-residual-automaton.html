Canonical residual automaton

 ... Foundations of mathematics Formal language theory Finite-state machine Deterministic finite automaton DFA minimization Minimal deterministic finite automaton
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The canonical residual automaton of a regular language has its distinct left quotients of a formal language as states, initial state L, accepting states those containing the empty word, and letter transition R↦a−1R. It is a complete minimal deterministic finite automaton; every other complete minimal deterministic finite automaton for that language is isomorphic to it.
