Tempered distribution (S′(Rn))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Schwartz space
OurBigBook.com Words: 645 Articles: 12
A tempered distribution is a continuous linear functional on the Schwartz space. Tempered distributions include functions of at most polynomial growth and admit a Fourier transform by duality.
