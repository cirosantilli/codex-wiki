Steady quasi-geostrophic response to localized heating (ψ=−βN2f0​​∫x∞​Rz​(X,y,z)dX)

In the weak-forcing steady response about rest, diabatically forced quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity gives βψx​=(f0​/N2)Rz​. For β=0, an eastern zero-flow condition fixes the horizontal circulation through ψ=−(f0​/βN2)∫x∞​Rz​dX, up to a horizontally uniform pressure gauge. The thermodynamic equation gives w=R/N2. A western zonal wake can remain even though the heating and vertical velocity vanish there. This is a formal steady interior solution: specified initial boundary buoyancy can prevent it from being the attained steady state. For example, under a rigid lid where the heating vanishes, initially uniform boundary buoyancy remains uniform at linear order.
