Geometrization of the Lorentz force by an affine connection (Γitt​=−Ei,Γitj​=ϵijk​Bk)

For smooth stationary fields on R3, set Γitt​=−Ei and Γitj​=Γijt​=ϵijk​Bk, with all other Christoffel symbols zero. This torsion-free connection makes the equation x¨=E+2B×x˙ a geodesic equation with time as affine parameter. Arbitrary smooth fields suffice; a metric realization imposes additional potential conditions.
