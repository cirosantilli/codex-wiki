Driftless square-root diffusion (dXt​=Xt​​dWt​)

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Stochastic differential equation Markov diffusion
OurBigBook.com Words: 192 Articles: 1
A driftless square-root diffusion process is the nonnegative solution of dXt​=Xt​​dWt​, with zero an absorbing boundary. For elapsed time h>0, starting from x≥0, its Laplace transform is
Ex​[e−uXh​]=exp(−1+uh/2xu​),u≥0.
(26)
To prove this, fix the final time h and set F(t,x)=exp[−xu/(1+u(h−t)/2)]. Differentiation gives Ft​+21​xFxx​=0, while F(h,x)=e−ux. By Itô formula, F(t,Xt​) is a local martingale; it takes values in [0,1], so localization and bounded convergence make it a true martingale. Taking expectations at the two endpoints proves the formula. The Markov property gives the same formula conditionally at any starting time.
