Tate twist (M(1)=M⊗Zp​​Zp​(1))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation Galois representation Cyclotomic character
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The Tate twist M(1) multiplies a Galois representation's action by the cyclotomic character. Here Zp​(1) is the inverse limit of p-power roots of unity under power maps. For a one-variable Iwasawa module, twisting changes characteristic power series by the corresponding change in the generator's action.
