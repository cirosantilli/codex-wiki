Weak-pressure Couette-Poiseuille expansion (u=hU​y+2ηd​(U/h)G​(h2−y2)+⋯,Q=3ηd​(U/h)2Gh3​+⋯)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Generalized Newtonian fluid Differential shear viscosity
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
Perturb the Couette flow between walls moving at +-U by a small pressure gradient -G. The mean shear rate remains U/h, and the stress variation across the gap is -Gy. Linearizing the monotone generalized Newtonian fluid law divides this variation by differential viscosity, giving the displayed velocity and flux per unit width. Require Gh≪(U/h)ηd​(U/h) and a smooth constitutive law on the perturbed rates. For a power-law fluid, the denominator is nk(U/h)n−1.
