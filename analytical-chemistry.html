Analytical chemistry

 Home Chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 392 Articles: 22
The branch of chemistry concerned with identifying substances in a sample and measuring their quantities or concentrations. A bicinchoninic acid assay, for example, estimates protein content through an optical measurement.
