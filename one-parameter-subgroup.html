One-parameter subgroup

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 149 Articles: 2
A one-parameter subgroup of a Lie group G is a smooth homomorphism γ:(R,+)→G. Every such subgroup has the form γ(t)=exp(tX) for a unique X in the Lie algebra of G.
