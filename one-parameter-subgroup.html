One-parameter subgroup (γX​(t)=exp(tX))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 304 Articles: 3
A one-parameter subgroup of a Lie group G is a smooth group homomorphism γ:(R,+)→G: it satisfies γ(s+t)=γ(s)γ(t), with γ(0)=e and γ(−t)=γ(t)−1. Every such map has the form γ(t)=exp(tX) for a unique X=γ′(0) in the Lie algebra of G. Thus its initial tangent vector determines the whole motion, for all real times. The map need not be injective; the word subgroup describes its image. See MIT 18.745, Lecture 7, Proposition 7.1.
