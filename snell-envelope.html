Snell envelope

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale

The finite-horizon Snell envelope of rewards Zn​ is defined backward by VN​=ZN​ and Vn​=max{Zn​,E[Vn+1​∣Fn​]}. It is the least supermartingale dominating Z.
