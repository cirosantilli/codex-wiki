Ester

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound
OurBigBook.com Words: 290 Articles: 23
The carboxylic acid-derived ester linkages in a triglyceride or typical glycerophospholipid have the form R−C(=O)−O−R′. Here the acyl group is attached through oxygen to the glycerol-derived part. See IUPAC Blue Book, P-65.6: esters.
