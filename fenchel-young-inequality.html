Fenchel–Young inequality

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Convex conjugate
OurBigBook.com Words: 66 Articles: 1
For a proper convex function f and its convex conjugate f∗,
f(x)+f∗(p)≥p⋅x.
(16)
Equality holds exactly when p∈∂f(x), or equivalently x∈∂f∗(p).
