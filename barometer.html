Barometer

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 5
An instrument measuring atmospheric pressure. A mercury barometer compares the hydrostatic pressure of a measured mercury column with the pressure above it. Its equation is patm​=pt​+ρgh, with h the vertical separation of the liquid levels.
