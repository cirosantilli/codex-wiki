Wilson loop (Wγ​=trPexp(−∮γ​A))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Wilson line
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
With gauge covariant derivative D=d+A, a Wilson loop is the matrix trace in a specified group representation of parallel transport around a closed curve. The holonomy of a connection changes by conjugation under gauge equivalence of principal connections, so its trace is gauge-invariant. It detects global connection data even when the local gauge curvature is zero.
