Symmetric Poisson difference process

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Poisson process
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The difference of two independent rate-λ Poisson processes is a continuous-time simple symmetric random walk with total jump rate 2λ and variance 2λt at time t.
