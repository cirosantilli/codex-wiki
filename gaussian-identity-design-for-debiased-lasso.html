Gaussian identity design for debiased Lasso

In the normal linear model, assume ε∼Nn​(0,σ2I) is independent of X. Independent rows of X with multivariate normal distribution Np​(0,Ip​) give a simple random design for Debiased Lasso. Let p→∞, logp=o(n) and slogp=o(n​), with fixed positive noise scale. Choose the Lasso penalty and all Nodewise Lasso penalties as sufficiently large constant multiples of logp/n​, including the noise scale in the former. Uniform sample cross-correlations are bounded by this scale, so zero is the nodewise solution with probability tending to one. Column squared norms divided by n converge uniformly to one. For s≥1, entrywise concentration of a Gaussian Gram matrix gives η≤D0​logp/n​ with probability tending to one for sufficiently large fixed D0​. The stated sparsity condition implies sη→0 on this event, so one can take ϕ2≥1/2 eventually. The conditional Gaussian tail bound for the noise score and the Basic inequality for the Lasso then give ∥β​−β0∥1​≤Cslogp/n​ with probability tending to one. Consequently the debiased-Lasso remainder bound is at most Aslogp/n​ with probability tending to one, for a fixed constant A.
