Fermionic state counting (dimΛNCM=(NM​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 126
Choosing N different complete one-particle states from an orthonormal basis of size M gives (NM​) independent Slater determinants. This is the binomial coefficient because only the occupied subset matters: permutations change the sign of the Slater determinant, not the physical quantum state. Summing over particle numbers gives 2M states in the finite fermionic Fock space.
For the diagram's two spatial orbitals A,B, take energies 0,Δ with Δ>0, each independent of spin. In the complete-state order (A↑,A↓,B↑,B↓), the six N=2 occupation number strings are 1100,1010,1001,0110,0101,0011. Their total energies are 0,Δ,Δ,Δ,Δ,2Δ, giving energy multiplicities 1,4,1. For N=3, the ground state energy is Δ with two independent ground states, according to which spin orbital in B is occupied. Across N=0,1,2,3,4, the counts are 1,4,6,4,1, summing to 16. These are exact counts for this model, not measured atomic energies.
