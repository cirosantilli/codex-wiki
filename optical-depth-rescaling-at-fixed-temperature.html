Optical-depth rescaling at fixed temperature (B(τ)=B(τ/a))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Radiative transfer
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
Multiplying an absorber abundance by a with fixed cross sections and physical temperature profile multiplies optical depth by a. The Planck function as a function of the new coordinate must therefore be composed with division by a. A term proportional to τn acquires coefficient a−n. Holding its old optical-depth coefficient fixed would instead change the physical temperature profile. This is the immediate radiative comparison before thermal adjustment.
