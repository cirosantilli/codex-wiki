Digital register

 Home Electrical engineering Digital electronics Sequential logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 2
A fixed-width collection of flip-flops storing a binary representation. A synchronous load-enabled digital register captures all its input bits on the active clock edge when load is asserted and otherwise retains its old value. One multiplexer before each D flip-flop can select new data or feedback from its old output. A processor register is a particular use inside a processor; temporary state in a standalone binary multiplier is another.
