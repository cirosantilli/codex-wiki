Photoelectric threshold frequency (ν0​=Φ/h)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Photon Photon absorption Photoelectric effect Einstein photoelectric equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
The photoelectric threshold frequency is the lower frequency boundary allowed by the ideal single-photon energy balance. With vacuum wavelength λ=c/ν and speed of light c, its corresponding longest wavelength is λ0​=hc/Φ. The chosen 2.30eV work function gives ν0​≃5.561375×1014Hz and λ0​≃539.062nm. These are model parameters, not a measured threshold for a named metal. Detectable photocurrent need not begin abruptly at the ideal boundary: collection, finite spectral width, thermal broadening and surface inhomogeneity matter.
