Write ω=∇×u and use the vector identity (u⋅∇)u=∇(∣u∣2/2)−u×ω. Taking the curl of the momentum equation eliminates all pure gradients, including gravity and the ∇(∇⋅u) viscous contribution. Before invoking barotropy, the pressure term gives
∇×(−ρ1​∇p)=−∇(1/ρ)×∇p=ρ2∇ρ×∇p​.
(16)
This is baroclinic vorticity generation. For a barotropic fluid, p=p(ρ), so ∇p is parallel to ∇ρ and this source vanishes. Constant ν permits the curl and Laplacian to commute. The result is
∂t∂ω​=∇×(u×ω)+ν∇2ω​.
(17)
No incompressibility assumption was used. Since ∇⋅ω=0, an equivalent form is
DtDω​=(ω⋅∇)u−ω∇⋅u+ν∇2ω.
(18)
The terms represent vortex stretching/tilting, compression/dilatation and viscous diffusion. In particular, removing the baroclinic source does not make the vorticity of a three-dimensional parcel constant. A spatially varying entropy-dependent K in p=Kργ need not define a single barotropic law; the common-pressure-law assumption matters.
