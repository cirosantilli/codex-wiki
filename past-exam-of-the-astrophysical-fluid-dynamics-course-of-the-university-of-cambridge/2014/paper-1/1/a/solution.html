For an ideal gas of fixed composition, the central temperature satisfies Tc​∝pc​/ρc​=Kρc1/n​. Keeping Tc​ fixed therefore requires K∝ρc−1/n​, rather than keeping the polytropic constant fixed. Substitution in the Lane-Emden equation scalings gives
M∝ρc(3/n−1)/2​ρc−3/(2n)​=ρc−1/2​,R∝ρc(1/n−1)/2​ρc−1/(2n)​=ρc−1/2​.
(1)
Consequently the homologous polytropes at fixed central temperature obey
M∝R.​
(2)
The polytropic index and mean particle mass must be common to this homologous sequence: varying either changes the dimensionless structural coefficient. The fixed-central-temperature assumption is the idealization being used here, rather than a universal property of real stars.
