Insert the pressure-negligible velocity law into the steady spherical mass flux. With x=r/Re​,
ρ(r)=ρe​r2v(r)Re2​ve​​=ρe​x−3/2.
(35)
An adiabatic equation of state pρ−γ=constant and the ideal gas law give
p(r)=pe​x−3γ/2,T(r)=Te​x−3(γ−1)/2.​
(36)
For a monatomic gas with γ=5/3,
p∝r−5/2,T∝r−1.​
(37)
The normalization Te​, and hence pe​=ne​kB​Te​, was not specified and remains a boundary parameter. If it is large enough for the pressure gradient to affect acceleration, replace the ballistic velocity by the full adiabatic Bernoulli equation solution. The general mass-flux and thermodynamic relations still hold, but these particular radius powers then need not hold.
