Using a center-to-edge sound-crossing time ts​=R/cs​ and the pressureless uniform-sphere free-fall time of a uniform sphere tff​=3π/(32Gρ)​, equality gives
Rcross​=cs​32Gρ3π​​∼Gρ​cs​​.​
(4)
On scales larger than this estimate, pressure information cannot cross the region before its gravitational dynamical evolution. It therefore identifies the order of the Jeans length and separates gravity-dominated from pressure-supported behavior in the simple comparison. It is not an exact normal-mode stability calculation: with the standard Jeans wavelength λJ​=cs​π/(Gρ)​, this radius is 3/32​λJ​. Using the diameter rather than the radius as the crossing distance changes the numerical coefficient. Boundary conditions, density structure, turbulence and magnetic support also matter for an actual cloud.
