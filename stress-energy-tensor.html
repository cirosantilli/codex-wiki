Stress-energy tensor

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 58
The stress-energy tensor describes local energy density, momentum density, and stress. Compatibility with the Einstein field equations requires covariant conservation ∇μTμν​=0.
