Plastic strain (e=ee+ep)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Plasticity (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
In small-strain elastoplasticity, total infinitesimal strain tensor splits into an elastic part and a generally irreversible plastic part. The elastic constitutive equation determines stress from ee, while a yield condition and a plastic flow law determine the evolution of ep. Removing external load need not remove plastic strain, so using Hooke's law on total strain after yielding is insufficient.
