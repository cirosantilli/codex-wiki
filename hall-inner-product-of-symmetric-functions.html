Here pα​ is a product of power-sum symmetric functions and zα​=∏r​rmr​mr​! for part multiplicities mr​. The Frobenius characteristic map identifies this product with the ordinary character inner product. Schur functions are orthonormal, and the adjoint of multiplication by pk​ is k∂/∂pk​. These properties turn a power-sum multiplication formula into the Murnaghan–Nakayama rule.
