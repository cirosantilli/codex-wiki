Photolysis rate coefficient (J=∫Fλ​σλ​Φλ​dλ)

 Home Chemistry Chemical bond Photodissociation
OurBigBook.com Words: 73 Articles: 1
The first-order rate coefficient for a specified photodissociation channel. It integrates spectral actinic flux, absorption cross section and channel quantum yield. Absorption without dissociation does not contribute to that channel's rate. Its units are inverse time.
