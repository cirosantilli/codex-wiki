Fully observed Markov source

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Markov information source
OurBigBook.com Words: 513 Articles: 3
Each emitted symbol identifies the state of a stationary finite Markov chain. Here the alphabet is the state space itself. Its transition matrix P and stationary distribution π determine the entropy rate of a fully observed Markov source.
