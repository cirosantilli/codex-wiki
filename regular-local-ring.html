Regular local ring

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Local ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 308 Articles: 4
A Noetherian local ring (R,m) is regular when the minimal number of generators of its maximal ideal equals its Krull dimension. Every regular local ring is an integral domain and a unique factorization domain.
