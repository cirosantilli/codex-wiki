Affine Galois group of a prime-radical splitting field (Gal(Q(ζℓ​,λ1/ℓ)/Q)≃Fℓ​⋊Fℓ×​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Semidirect product Affine group over a finite field
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
For distinct or equal prime numbers ℓ,λ, the polynomial Xℓ−λ is Eisenstein at λ. Its splitting field over Q(ζℓ​) has Galois group a subgroup of Cℓ​, which is nontrivial because a degree-ℓ radical cannot belong to a degree-(ℓ−1) cyclotomic field. Thus the full Galois group has order ℓ(ℓ−1). On roots indexed by j∈Fℓ​, all automorphisms act as j↦uj+b. Equality of orders identifies the group with the full affine group over a finite field.
