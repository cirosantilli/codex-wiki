Hermite interpolating polynomial (H(xi​)=yi​,H′(xi​)=di​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial interpolation error Hermite interpolation
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
At distinct interpolation nodes x0​,…,xn​, prescribed values yi​ and first derivatives di​ determine exactly one Hermite interpolating polynomial of polynomial degree at most 2n+1. The homogeneous data force a double root of a polynomial at every interpolation node. Hence the kernel of a linear map given by these 2n+2 conditions is zero: a nonzero polynomial with those double roots of a polynomial would have polynomial degree at least 2n+2. Equal finite dimensions of a vector space then give existence as well as uniqueness. This interpolation polynomial is distinct from the Hermite polynomials forming a classical orthogonal polynomial family.
