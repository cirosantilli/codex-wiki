Bounded-bracket criterion for a stochastic exponential ([M]∞​≤C)

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Doléans-Dade exponential Novikov's condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
For a zero-start continuous local martingale M with [M]∞​≤C deterministically, its stochastic exponential is a uniformly integrable martingale. For each p>1, supt​EE(M)tp​≤ep(p−1)C/2. Its terminal value is strictly positive, so it defines an equivalent infinite-horizon change of measure by the Girsanov theorem.
