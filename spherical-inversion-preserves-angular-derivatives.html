Spherical inversion preserves angular derivatives (r×∇r​=r×∇r​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Harmonic function Kelvin transform
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For r=a2r/r2, the chain rule gives ∇r​=(a2/r2)(I−2rrT)∇r​. The radial term is killed by crossing with r, giving the identity. Purely angular vector spherical harmonics are therefore unchanged as angular basis functions. This is distinct from pushing forward a physical vector field by the inversion Jacobian.
