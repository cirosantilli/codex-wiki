Plant SI model with logistic total population

OurBigBook.com Words: 80 Articles: 1
The plant-disease model
S˙=(S+I)(1−S)−βIS,I˙=−(S+I)I+βIS
(27)
has total population N=S+I and disease prevalence θ=I/N satisfying
N˙=N(1−N),θ˙=θ{βN(1−θ)−1}.
(28)
Thus infection does not alter total-population growth. The invasion threshold at the carrying capacity N=1 is β=1.
