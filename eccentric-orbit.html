Eccentric orbit

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Kepler orbit Orbital eccentricity
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
An orbit with nonzero orbital eccentricity. A bound Kepler orbit with 0<e<1 is a noncircular ellipse; e=1 and e>1 describe the parabolic and hyperbolic unbound cases. A semi-major axis fixed by Kepler's third law need not equal the time average of the orbital radius.
