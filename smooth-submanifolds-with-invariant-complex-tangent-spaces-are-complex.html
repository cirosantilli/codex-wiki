Smooth submanifolds with invariant complex tangent spaces are complex (J(TY)=TY)

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Complex submanifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
Let Y be a embedded submanifold of a complex manifold. If its tangent spaces are invariant under the almost complex structure induced by a complex atlas, choose ambient holomorphic coordinates making its tangent space at one point Ck×0. The first k coordinates restrict to a local real diffeomorphism by the inverse function theorem, so Y is locally the graph of a smooth function F. Invariance of the tangent graph gives dF(iv)=idF(v), the Cauchy-Riemann equations. Thus F is holomorphic and these graph coordinates give the induced complex submanifold structure. This proves the result directly, without needing the general integrability theorem as an unproved step.
