Twistor line (Lx​=P(Wx​)≅CP1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Twistor theory Twistor correspondence
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The projective line associated to a two-plane Wx​ representing a point of complexified compactified Minkowski space. Two distinct twistor lines intersect precisely when their spacetime points are null separated. On the Lorentzian real slice, the line lies entirely in projective null twistor space and parametrizes the null geodesics through the point.
