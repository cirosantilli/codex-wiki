Homotopy class ([f]∈[X,Y])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
An equivalence class of continuous maps under homotopy, with any endpoint or boundary restrictions specified. Unbased loop classes are free homotopy classes; periods of a closed differential form are constant on such classes.
