Short exact sequence in an abelian category (0→A→B→C→0)

 ... Category theory Enriched category Semi-additive category Additive category Abelian category Exact sequence in an abelian category
OurBigBook.com Words: 138 Articles: 2
A short exact sequence in an abelian category has one nonzero morphism entering the middle object and one leaving it: 0→Ai​Bp​C→0. Equivalently, i is the kernel in a category of the epimorphism p. It splits if p has a section; in that case the additive category splitting argument identifies B with the biproduct A⊕C. Categories of modules give familiar examples.
