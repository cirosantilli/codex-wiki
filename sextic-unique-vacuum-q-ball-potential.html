Sextic unique-vacuum Q-ball potential (U(s)=s−s2+s3,s=∣ϕ∣2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field-theory soliton Non-topological soliton Q-ball
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
This nonnegative potential has its only vacuum at s=0, since U(s)=s[(s−1/2)2+3/4]. For the complex kinetic normalization 21​∣∂ϕ∣2, the small-amplitude mass squared is 2U′(0)=2, whereas mins>0​2U(s)/s=3/2. The radial Q-ball equation has a positive localized profile for frequencies 3/2<ω2<2, for example in three spatial dimensions. It is a standard non-topological soliton potential: the effective profile potential U(f2)−ω2f2/2 is negative somewhere, while its quadratic coefficient near zero is positive. The conserved Noether charge, not vacuum winding, supplies the stabilization mechanism.
