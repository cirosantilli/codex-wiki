Conserved momentum flux of a porous thermal plume (M=βFh​/Ch​,Q=(36κMz)1/3,B=gαFh​/Ch​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Darcy law Porous thermal plume
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
For a slender porous thermal plume, Darcy law gives w=β(T−T0​), with β=Kgα/ν. If Ch​=ρ0​cp​ and Fh​=Ch​∫w(T−T0​)dx is the conserved excess convective heat flux per span, then the kinematic momentum flux is M=∫w2dx=βFh​/Ch​, and the buoyancy flux is B=∫gα(T−T0​)wdx=gαFh​/Ch​. The hyperbolic-secant porous plume profile has Q=(36κMz)1/3. Multiplication of Q and M by ρ0​ gives physical mass flux and the density-weighted Darcy second moment. Intrinsic pore-velocity momentum flux instead has an additional inverse-porosity factor. Momentum conservation here follows from the heat/velocity proportionality, not from an inertia balance: porous drag balances buoyancy locally.
