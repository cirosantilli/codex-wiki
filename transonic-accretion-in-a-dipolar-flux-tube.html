Transonic accretion in a dipolar flux tube

OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 2
The dipolar flux-tube area has A(r)=A⋆​(r/R⋆​)3. Applying transonic accretion in a power-law tube with n=3 and a polytropic equation of state gives
cs2​=7−5γ2c02​​,rs​=6c02​GM(7−5γ)​,ρs​=ρ0​(7−5γ2​)1/(γ−1).
(23)
Here c0​ and ρ0​ are the reservoir sound speed and mass density, and cs​ is the sound speed at the sonic point. The mass accretion rate is
M˙=A⋆​(R⋆​rs​​)3ρs​cs​.
(24)
For two equal polar caps of surface angular radius θ⋆​≪1, A⋆​≃2πR⋆2​θ⋆2​. One loaded cap gives half the total rate. The finite-radius transonic branch exists for 1<γ<7/5, must cross outside the star (rs​>R⋆​), and requires θ⋆2​rs​/R⋆​≪1 for the sonic region to remain a narrow flux tube.
