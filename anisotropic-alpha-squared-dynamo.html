Anisotropic alpha-squared dynamo (∂t​B=∇×(αB)+η∇2B)

OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 1
An alpha-squared dynamo may use a tensorial alpha effect rather than an isotropic coefficient. For constant alpha tensor α and magnetic diffusivity η>0, a steady nonzero Fourier mode with wavevector K satisfies ηK2B=iK×(αB) and K⋅B=0. Anisotropy can change both the critical alpha magnitude and the preferred wavevector direction.
