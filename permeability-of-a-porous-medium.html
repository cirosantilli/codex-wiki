Permeability of a porous medium (k)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Darcy law
OurBigBook.com Words: 286 Articles: 6
The permeability of a porous medium measures its ability to transmit fluid. In Darcy's law, k has dimensions of area and depends on the geometry and connectivity of the pore space.
