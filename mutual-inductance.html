Mutual inductance (M)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Inductance
OurBigBook.com Words: 299 Articles: 5
For two fixed linear coils, mutual inductance M relates magnetic flux linkage in one coil to electric current in the other: Λ21​=MI1​. The induced electromotive force is E2​=−MdI1​/dt with consistent winding orientations. For reciprocal coils, ∣M∣=kL1​L2​​, where L1​,L2​ are self-inductances and 0≤k≤1 measures magnetic coupling. Its SI unit is the henry.
