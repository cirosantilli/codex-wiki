Massless induced representation of the Poincare double cover

 ... Special relativity Lorentz transformation Lorentz group Poincaré group Little group Massless particle little group
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For a future null standard momentum k, choose Lorentz transformations L(p)k=p and a unitary irreducible representation d of its little group. The Wigner rotation R(Λ,p)=L(Λp)−1ΛL(p) acts on the internal state. The resulting induced representation acts on wavefunctions with invariant measure d3p/(2p0). Trivial little-group translations give fixed helicity; nontrivial translations give a continuous-spin representation with infinitely many polarization states.
