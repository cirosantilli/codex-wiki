Sheaf associated with a graded module (M∣D+​(f)​=(Mf​)0​​)

 ... Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes Proper morphism Projective scheme Proj construction
OurBigBook.com Words: 149 Articles: 2
A graded module M over a graded ring S determines a quasi-coherent sheaf on the Proj construction ProjS by taking the degree-zero part of its localization on each standard affine open of Proj. Localization identifies these module sheaves on overlaps. The construction is exact, by exactness of localization and exactness of taking a fixed graded component.
