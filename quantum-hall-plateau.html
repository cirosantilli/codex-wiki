The degeneracy of a Landau level is eBA/h per resolved internal branch in area A. The filling factor f=ns​h/(eB) varies continuously. Additional localized eigenstates need not carry bulk transport, allowing an integer plateau over an interval of f; a mobility gap need not be an empty spectral gap.[ref]
The plot is an original phenomenological construction, not measured data or a calculation of Anderson localization. It stipulates dimensionless transverse sheet conductivity k(f)=1+21​∑m=15​[1+tanh((f−m−1/2)/w)] and longitudinal sheet conductivity s(f)=a∑m=15​sech2((f−m−1/2)/w), with chosen w=0.06, a=0.08. Both are measured in units e2/h. The quantum Hall conductivity tensor inversion produces ∣ρyx​∣/RK​=k/(s2+k2) and ρxx​/RK​=s/(s2+k2). Regions near k=N, s=0 yield plateaus near RK​/N; the model alone does not explain their microscopic robustness. Each step represents one resolved branch, without assuming unresolved spin or valley multiplicities.
