Geometric quantization

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 415 Articles: 6
Geometric quantization associates quantum states and observables with a symplectic manifold. A first step uses a complex line bundle with a connection on a vector bundle whose curvature is proportional to the symplectic form divided by ℏ; its existence imposes an integrality condition. A real polarization is an integrable half-dimensional distribution with Lagrangian submanifolds as leaves. Requiring sections to be covariantly constant along the leaves reduces the variables to a configuration-space description. Restricting the curvature to a Lagrangian leaf gives zero, permitting local flat sections; global sections can require trivial holonomy. Exact Lagrangian graphs also describe local semiclassical phases through their generating functions.
