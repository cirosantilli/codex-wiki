Dalton (unit, Da=u)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element Isotope Atomic mass
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
The unified atomic mass unit and dalton are names for the same mass unit:
1u=1Da=12ma​(12C)​,
(16)
where the carbon-12 chemical atom is free, at rest, and in its nuclear and electronic ground state. Its conversion to SI mass units is determined experimentally. See The International System of Units (SI), ninth edition, SI Brochure (Table 8, footnote (f), printed p. 146: dalton and unified atomic mass unit).
