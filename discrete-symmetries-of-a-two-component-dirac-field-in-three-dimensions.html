Discrete symmetries of a two-component Dirac field in three dimensions (P:Sm​↦S−m​,T:Sm​↦S−m​,C:Sm​↦Sm​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
For gamma matrices γ0=σ3​, γ1=iσ1​, γ2=iσ2​ and Minkowski metric diag(1,−1,−1), a one-axis spatial reflection acts with γ1 on the two-component Dirac field. It preserves the kinetic term and reverses the Dirac mass term. Time reversal acts with γ2 and includes conjugation of the operator coefficients; it has the same mass reversal. Their composition preserves a real mass. Charge conjugation can use ψC​=γ1ψ∗, because γμγ1=−γ1γμ∗. The mass bilinear is charge-conjugation even when Grassmann field exchange signs are included, and the kinetic action is invariant up to a boundary term. These statements concern the classical free action.
