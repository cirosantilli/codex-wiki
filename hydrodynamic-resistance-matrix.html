Hydrodynamic resistance matrix

 Home Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
At fixed orientation, linearity of Stokes flow relates a rigid body's translational velocity U to the hydrodynamic force F by
F=−RU.
(26)
The Lorentz reciprocal theorem makes R symmetric, and positive viscous dissipation makes it positive definite.
