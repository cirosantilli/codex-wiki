Hegerfeldt theorem

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
Let H be a self-adjoint Hamiltonian bounded below, A a bounded positive operator and pA​(t)=⟨e−iHtψ,Ae−iHtψ⟩. The Hegerfeldt theorem states that either pA​(t) vanishes identically or it is nonzero for almost every real time. In particular, vanishing throughout an open interval forces identically vanishing. The proof uses analytic continuation of A1/2e−iHtψ into the lower half-plane. Applied to an assumed sharp localization effect, this obstructs strict finite-speed localization for positive-energy one-particle dynamics. It does not identify a controllable superluminal signal: the localization observables and admissible local preparation procedures need separate analysis.
