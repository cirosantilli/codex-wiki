Equatorial Rossby-wave dispersion relation

 ... Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Equatorial wave Equatorial Rossby wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
Under meridional geostrophic balance, the mode with Hermite index n≥1 has ω=−kc/(2n+1). The full shallow-water equations bend this long-wave line into a dispersive Rossby branch.
