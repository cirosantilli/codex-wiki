Smooth representation of a locally profinite group

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 321 Articles: 7
A complex group representation π:G→GL(V) is smooth when every vector is fixed by some compact-open subgroup. Equivalently each vector's stabilizer is open. Smoothness is local constancy of orbit maps with the vector space regarded discretely, not ordinary continuity into a finite-dimensional complex topology.
