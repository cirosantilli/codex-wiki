Thus the quadratic form is Σ=∑t≤L​h(t)yt2​. Möbius inversion gives g(d)ρd​=∑d∣t≤L​μ(t/d)yt​. On the permitted squarefree integers, the constraint ρ1​=1 becomes ∑t​μ(t)yt​=1. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality yields Σ≥1/J, where J=∑t​1/h(t) over those integers; equality holds at yt​=μ(t)/(Jh(t)).
