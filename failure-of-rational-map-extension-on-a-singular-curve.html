Failure of rational-map extension on a singular curve

 ... Algebraic geometry Algebraic variety Projective space Projective variety Smooth projective curve Extension of a rational map from a smooth projective curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
Let ν:C→C be the normalization of a nodal curve. Its rational inverse C⇢C cannot extend over the node: the two branches give two distinct points of C, whereas a morphism can assign only one image to the node.
