Given Gaussian random intercept b0​ and random slope b1​, let the conditional incidence rate be eβ0​+β1​s+b0​+b1​s. The normal moment-generating function gives the displayed marginal rate, with Var(b0​+b1​s)=τ02​+2sτ01​+s2τ12​. Thus the conditional annual rate ratio for a cluster is eβ1​+b1​, whereas the marginal rate ratio also contains covariance and variance terms. Centering s at a chosen year makes intercept heterogeneity refer directly to that year.
