Orbit determination

 ... Branches of physics Classical mechanics Energy Potential energy Escape velocity Spacecraft trajectory
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
Orbit determination infers a body's position and velocity from observations and an equation of motion. It is an inverse problem; a residual of an inverse problem compares an observed readout with its modeled prediction.
