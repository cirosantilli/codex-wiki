Cygnus X-1

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Binary star X-ray binary Wind-fed black-hole binary
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A stellar black-hole binary with a massive stellar companion. A 2011 dynamical analysis combined optical photometry and spectroscopy with a distance constraint and inferred 14.8±1.0 solar masses for the compact object. This is that study's historical estimate, not a claim about the latest measurement.[ref]
