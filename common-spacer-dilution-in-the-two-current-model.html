Common-spacer dilution in the two-current model (m=p2/[(1+s)2−p2])

OurBigBook.com Words: 149
Define a dimensionless channel contrast p=(b−a)/(a+b) and spacer ratio s=r/(a+b). Positive a,b give ∣p∣<1, and substitution gives the displayed expression. Equal channel responses, p=0, eliminate the model's magnetic-configuration signal. Increasing the common spacer resistance at fixed a,b increases the denominator and decreases m.
This is a controlled comparison within the model. In an actual multilayer, changing a spacer thickness can change the magnetic coupling and the interfaces as well as electrical transport. Spin-flip scattering also mixes the channels that the calculation kept independent.
