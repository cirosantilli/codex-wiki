Mass conservation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
Mass conservation states that matter is neither created nor destroyed. For mass density ρ and velocity v, its local form is ∂t​ρ+∇⋅(ρv)=0.
