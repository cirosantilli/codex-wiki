Conservative Thomson scattering does not thermalize radiation

 ... Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Photon-baryon fluid Thomson scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Coherent Thomson scattering by cold electrons changes a photon's direction without changing its frequency in the electron rest frame. A monochromatic incident radiation field therefore remains monochromatic after arbitrarily many such scatterings. Large scattering optical depth can isotropize radiation but does not imply a Planck photon distribution. True absorption and emission, or suitable additional inelastic processes and photon production, are needed to establish a blackbody spectrum independently of the initial radiation field.
