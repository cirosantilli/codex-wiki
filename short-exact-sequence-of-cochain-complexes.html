Short exact sequence of cochain complexes (0→A∗→B∗→C∗→0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Homological algebra Cochain complex
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A degreewise short exact sequence of cochain complexes induces a long exact sequence from a coefficient sequence in the coefficient case, and a long exact cohomology sequence generally. Its degree-one connecting homomorphism lifts a quotient cocycle, takes its coboundary, and identifies that result in the subcomplex.
