Triangle inequality

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Topological analysis Metric space
OurBigBook.com Words: 14
A metric satisfies d(x,z)≤d(x,y)+d(y,z). A norm satisfies the corresponding inequality ∥u+v∥≤∥u∥+∥v∥.
