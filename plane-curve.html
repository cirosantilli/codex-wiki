Plane curve

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 877 Articles: 16
A plane curve is a curve contained in a plane. In algebraic geometry, one studies plane curves defined by polynomial equations. General plane curves, such as an Archimedean spiral, need not be algebraic curves.
