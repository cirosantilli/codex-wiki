Type space (Sn​(A))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Model theory Elementary extension Complete type
OurBigBook.com Words: 408 Articles: 9
With its standard topology, this type space is a Stone space. The type space SnM​(A) is the set of complete n-types over a parameter set A that are consistent with the complete theory of M together with its diagram over A.
