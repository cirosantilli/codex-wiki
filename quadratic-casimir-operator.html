Quadratic Casimir operator (∑a​ta​ta​=C2​(R)I)

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Casimir element
OurBigBook.com Words: 276 Articles: 3
For a Lie algebra representation with generators referred to an orthonormal invariant form, the sum ∑a​ta​ta​ commutes with every generator. Indeed, the commutator is proportional to a contraction of antisymmetric structure constants of a Lie algebra with the symmetric product ta​tb​+tb​ta​. The Schur lemma makes it scalar in an irreducible complex representation; that scalar is its quadratic Casimir eigenvalue. With trace normalization tr(ta​tb​)=C(R)δab​, taking the trace gives C(R)dimg=C2​(R)dimR.
