Character space of continuous functions on the circle (ΦC(T)​≅T)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Space of continuous functions vanishing at infinity Space of continuous functions on a compact space
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
A maximal ideal M of C(T) has a common zero: otherwise finitely many fj​∈M have no simultaneous zero, and ∑j​∣fj​∣2∈M is invertible, a contradiction. If the common zero is z, then M is the kernel of evaluation at z by maximality. Thus every algebra character is point evaluation. Continuous functions separate distinct points of the unit circle, and the evaluation bijection is a homeomorphism for the Gelfand topology.
