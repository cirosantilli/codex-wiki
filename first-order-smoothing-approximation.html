First-order smoothing approximation

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Dynamo action Mean-field dynamo
OurBigBook.com Words: 419 Articles: 5
First-order smoothing neglects the fluctuating nonlinear velocity-magnetic-field product in the fluctuation resistive induction equation, but retains ⟨u×b⟩ in the mean-field electromotive force. For a constant test field it gives ∂t​b−η∇2b=(B0​⋅∇)u. Its validity requires the omitted terms to be small compared with the retained forcing and diffusion terms.
