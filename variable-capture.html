Variable capture

 ... Foundations of mathematics Computability theory Lambda calculus Untyped lambda calculus Beta reduction Capture-avoiding substitution
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
An unintended change of binding caused by moving or substituting a term beneath a binder. In first-order logic, substituting y for the free x in ∀yS(x,y) without renaming would yield ∀yS(y,y), incorrectly binding the substituted occurrence. First rename the bound y to a fresh z, obtaining ∀zS(x,z), and then substitute to get ∀zS(y,z). The paper's condition that a term be free for a variable excludes such capture.
