Turbulent kinetic energy (et​=21​ui′​ui′​​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 236 Articles: 3
Kinetic energy per unit mass in velocity fluctuations. Mean shear, buoyancy and transport supply or redistribute it, while molecular viscosity dissipates it. Its local budget motivates the local turbulent kinetic energy balance when storage and transport are negligible.
