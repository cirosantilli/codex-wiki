With the convention m∗γ=γ∗n and invertible bialgebra scalar cocycle γ for n, the same coalgebra has n=γˉ​∗m∗γ, or equivalently m=γ∗n∗γˉ​. Thus n(a,b)=∑γˉ​(a(1)​,b(1)​)m(a(2)​,b(2)​)γ(a(3)​,b(3)​). Specifying the equation prevents opposite twist conventions from being conflated.
