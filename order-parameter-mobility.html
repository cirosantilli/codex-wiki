Order-parameter mobility (M)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Order parameter Conserved order-parameter dynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The positive order-parameter mobility M relates the diffusive transport flux to the chemical potential gradient: J=−M∇μ. Combining this law with local conservation gives the Cahn--Hilliard equation when μ is the functional derivative of a composition free energy.
