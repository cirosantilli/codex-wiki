Delayed neutron-capture coincidence window (ϵ(W)=1−e−W/τ)

 ... Physics Nuclear physics Radioactive decay Beta decay Inverse beta decay Cowan-Reines reactor antineutrino experiment
OurBigBook.com Words: 367 Articles: 1
Why not accept every delayed pulse? A longer window captures more genuine neutron capture signals, but also admits more accidental coincidences. Consider an original model with a capture waiting time following an exponential distribution of mean τ>0. Assume detection after a prompt signal accepts 0<t<W, with perfect time measurement and no lower cut. Integrating the normalized density gives
ϵ(W)=∫0W​τe−t/τ​dt=1−e−W/τ.
(12)
This assumes that moderation, competing capture channels, boundaries and electronic recovery can be represented or neglected in a single effective waiting-time model. It is not a fitted historical capture law.
Let independent prompt-like and delayed-like background streams have Poisson process rates Rp​ and Rd​. The accidental coincidence pair rate in a directed window of full width W is Rp​Rd​W. Electronics producing at most one accepted delayed match per prompt instead give Rp​(1−e−Rd​W). These definitions coincide to first order when Rd​W≪1. Correlated backgrounds, such as one external neutron producing both a prompt recoil and a later capture, require separate controls; an independent-stream estimate cannot remove them.
For chosen τ=20μs and W=50μs, the accepted fraction is 1−e−2.5≃0.918. With chosen Rp​=40s−1 and Rd​=30s−1, the pair rate is 0.0600s−1, while the at-most-one rate is approximately 0.05996s−1. These are teaching parameters, not reported Savannah River rates. A measured uncorrelated background can be tested using displaced time windows; a reactor-off measurement addresses a different question and cannot by itself characterize every reactor-correlated background.
