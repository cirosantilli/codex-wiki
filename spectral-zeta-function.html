Spectral zeta function (ζL​(s)=∑λj​>0​λj−s​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Spectral geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 138 Articles: 1
For a nonnegative elliptic operator L on a closed manifold, sum its positive eigenvalues with multiplicity. The defining series converges in a right half-plane. If n0​=dimkerL and K(t)=Tr(e−tL) is the heat trace, then
ζL​(s)=Γ(s)1​∫0∞​ts−1(K(t)−n0​)dt.
(1)
Subtracting finitely many terms of the short-time heat kernel expansion gives a meromorphic continuation. Zero eigenvalues must be excluded: assigning them a nonexistent inverse power does not define the spectral zeta function.
