Nontrivial valuation in the local compactness criterion

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Local compactness criterion for a complete non-Archimedean field
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
An infinite field with the trivial absolute value on a field is discrete, hence locally compact, but its residue field is the original infinite field. Thus local compactness forces a finite residue field and a discrete nontrivial value group only when the valuation is assumed nontrivial.
