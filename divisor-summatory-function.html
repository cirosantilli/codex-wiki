Divisor summatory function (D(x)=∑n≤x​τ(n))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Arithmetic function Dirichlet convolution Dirichlet hyperbola method
OurBigBook.com Words: 120
The divisor summatory function adds the divisor function up to a cutoff x≥1. It counts all ordered pairs of positive integers whose product is at most x. For N=12, the twelve individual divisor function values are
(τ(1),…,τ(12))=(1,2,2,3,2,4,2,4,3,4,2,6),D(12)=35.
(66)
Counting columns of lattice points instead gives 12+6+4+3+2+2+1+1+1+1+1+1=35. The Dirichlet hyperbola method needs only the first three column counts:
D(12)=2(12+6+4)−32=44−9=35.
(67)
The arithmetic work of the Dirichlet hyperbola method is much smaller even though the answer is identical.
This is useful for elementary averages: individual divisor function values fluctuate, but their summation has the regular behavior given by the divisor summatory asymptotic. Dividing that estimate by a positive integer N gives
N1​∑n=1N​τ(n)=logN+2γ−1+O(N−1/2)(N⟶∞),
(68)
where log is the natural logarithm, γ is the Euler--Mascheroni constant, and O uses big O notation. This describes an average, rather than an estimate for every individual divisor function value. [ref]
