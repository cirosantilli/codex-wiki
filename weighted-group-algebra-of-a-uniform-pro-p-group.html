Choose ordered minimal topological generators a1​,…,ad​ of a uniform pro-p group for odd p, put bi​=ai​−1, and choose 1/(p−1)<s<1. Ordered monomials bα=b1α1​​⋯bdαd​​ give power-series coordinates in the integral completed group algebra. The displayed Non-Archimedean absolute value filtration gives a complete normed Qp​-algebra after completion of the rational group algebra. The power relation raises valuation by one, and interchanging bi​,bj​ adds terms of valuation at least 1+s>2s, so multiplication does not decrease total valuation. The p-adic logarithm and p-adic exponential are inverse on the relevant small balls. In particular logG is a faithful realization of the Lie algebra of a uniform pro-p group, useful for verifying its intrinsic limit operations and the Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff formula without assuming the correspondence in advance.
