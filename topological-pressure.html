For a finite full shift and a potential with summable variations, the displayed symbolic pressure exists and does not depend on the reference tail η. The almost-additive partition-function limit proves existence. The function γ↦P(γU) is convex; finite-volume second derivatives are energy variances divided by block length. If −B≤U≤−b<0, its increments for δ>0 lie between −Bδ and −bδ, so it is Lipschitz continuous, strictly decreasing, and has one positive zero.
