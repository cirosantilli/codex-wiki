Protoplast

 Home Biology Biological cell Cell wall
OurBigBook.com Words: 278 Articles: 1
A protoplast is the living contents of a biological cell bounded by its cell membrane after removal of the cell wall. Removing the cell wall need not immediately destroy the cell membrane; stability depends on the preparation and surroundings. Lysozyme acts on susceptible bacterial peptidoglycan, not on cellulose or chitin, so it is not a universal wall-removal enzyme.
