Bucharest Early Intervention Project

 Home Science Psychology Psychological research methods
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
A randomized study of a supported foster-care intervention for young children already living in institutions. The randomized offer supplies evidence about changing care; subsequent care changes, adherence and attrition affect the estimand and analysis. Mediator claims about attachment need assumptions beyond randomization of the whole package.[ref]
