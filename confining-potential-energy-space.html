For a nonnegative potential V(x)→∞ as ∣x∣→∞, the confining-potential energy space is
ΣV​={u:∇u∈L2, V1/2u∈L2},∥u∥ΣV​2​=∥∇u∥22​+∥V1/2u∥22​.
(34)
A local Poincaré inequality controls the missing L2 term, so this energy norm makes ΣV​ a Hilbert space.
