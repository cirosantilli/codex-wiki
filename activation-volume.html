The pressure derivative of the standard Gibbs energy of activation: ΔV‡∘=(∂ΔG‡∘/∂p)T​. In the corresponding rate convention, ΔV‡∘=−RT(∂lnk/∂p)T​. A negative value makes increasing pressure accelerate the reaction. Solvent reorganization can contribute as well as the intrinsic reacting geometry.
