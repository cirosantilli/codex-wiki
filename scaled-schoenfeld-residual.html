Scaled Schoenfeld residual

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis Cox proportional-hazards model Schoenfeld residual
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A scaled Schoenfeld residual rescales a Schoenfeld residual using fitted information so that its trend can diagnose departures from a constant coefficient in a Cox proportional-hazards model. A smooth trend against transformed event time estimates a time-dependent coefficient pattern; a flat trend supports the working proportional hazards assumption test.
