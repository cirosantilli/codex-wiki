Internal-wave ray in uniform vertical shear (m(t)=m0​−kst,z(t)=(ω−ω(t))/(ks))

For U=sz, N>0, k>0 and the rising branch m0​<0, the Hamiltonian is ω=ksz+Nk/k2+m2​. It gives constant ω,k and m=m0​−kst. The height approaches zc​=ω/(ks) as time tends to infinity. With θ=arctan(∣m∣/k), the ray slope is dx/dz=tanθ+ksz/(Nsinθcos2θ). The intrinsic and absolute frequencies must not be confused.
