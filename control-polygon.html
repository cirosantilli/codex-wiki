Control polygon

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Computer aided geometric design Control point
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
A control polygon joins successive control points by line segments. It gives an editable geometric skeleton, not usually the exact curve. Positive partition-of-unity basis functions place the curve inside the convex hull of its active control points; subdivision can refine this polygon to approximate the limit curve with a certified error.
