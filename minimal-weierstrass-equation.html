Minimal Weierstrass equation

 ... Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 17
Minimality here concerns the discriminant valuation of an integral Weierstrass equation of an elliptic curve over a discretely valued field.
Over a discretely valued field, a minimal Weierstrass equation has integral coefficients and has least possible discriminant valuation among integral Weierstrass equations related by admissible changes of variables.
