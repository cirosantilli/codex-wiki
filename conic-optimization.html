Conic optimization

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 17
Optimization of a linear function subject to affine constraints and membership in a closed convex cone. Choosing the positive semidefinite cone gives semidefinite programming; other choices include copositive optimization and completely positive optimization. Dual cones produce scalar-product bounds on feasible objectives.
