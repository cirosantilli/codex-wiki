Coming down from infinity of the Kingman coalescent

 ... Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Continuous-time Markov chain Kingman's coalescent Kingman's infinite coalescent
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For t>0, the number Bt​ of blocks is finite almost surely. On a finite restriction, the expected time to reach m blocks is at most ∑j>m​2/[j(j−1)]=2/m. Markov inequality, followed by increasing the restriction, gives P(Bt​>m)≤2/(mt). Let m→∞, then use rational times and monotonicity to obtain the assertion simultaneously for every t>0.
