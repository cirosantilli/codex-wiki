Mid-edge facet centroid invariance (p​(r+1)=p​(r))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Computer aided geometric design Subdivision surface Mid-edge subdivision
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
The descendant of a face with cyclic vertices p0​,…,pn−1​ has vertices pi′​=(pi​+pi+1​)/2. Their arithmetic mean is unchanged. On the cyclic Fourier basis, a mode of frequency j is multiplied by (1+e2πij/n)/2. Every nonconstant mode has modulus less than one, so the descendant face shrinks to the original vertex centroid. This puts that centroid on the subdivision limit, without requiring the original face to be planar.
