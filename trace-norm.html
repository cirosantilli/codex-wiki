Trace norm (∥A∥1​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Normed vector space
OurBigBook.com Words: 218 Articles: 5
The trace norm of a finite-dimensional operator A is the sum of its singular values:
∥A∥1​=TrA∗A​.
(22)
For a Hermitian operator A=A+​−A−​, it equals TrA+​+TrA−​.
