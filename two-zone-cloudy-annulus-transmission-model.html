Two-zone cloudy annulus transmission model

 ... Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Transit spectroscopy Exoplanet transmission spectrum Annulus model for transmission spectroscopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
An exoplanet cloud deck divides the projected atmospheric annulus into cloudy and clear radial zones. Their contributions to the exoplanet transmission spectrum add as projected areas multiplied by 1−e−τλ​ for each zone's slant optical depth.
