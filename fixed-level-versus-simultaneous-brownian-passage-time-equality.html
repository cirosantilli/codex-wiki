Fixed-level versus simultaneous Brownian passage-time equality

 ... Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Hitting probability First-passage time Brownian first-passage time
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
For standard Brownian motion, set Ha​=inf{t:Bt​≥a} and Ta​=inf{t:Bt​>a}. The Strong Markov property and immediate positive excursions give Ta​=Ha​ almost surely for each fixed a≥0. However, these level-indexed processes are not indistinguishable stochastic processes. Almost surely the random level A=supt≤1​Bt​ is positive and exceeds B1​. It is first attained before time one but is not exceeded until after time one, so HA​<1<TA​. The simultaneous-equality event therefore has probability zero. The strict passage process Ta​ is the right-continuous choice needed for a subordinator.
