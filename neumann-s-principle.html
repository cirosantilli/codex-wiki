Neumann's principle

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Point group
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
A physical property tensor must be invariant under every symmetry operation of the material's point group. For a polar rank-r tensor, Ti1​…ir​​=Ri1​j1​​⋯Rir​jr​​Tj1​…jr​​. Taking R=−I proves that an odd-rank polar tensor vanishes in a centrosymmetric material. A susceptibility of order n has rank n+1, so local bulk even-order electric-dipole response is forbidden there. The name refers to Franz Ernst Neumann.
