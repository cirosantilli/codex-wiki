Transverse miss on a rotating platform (d≃4ωa2/v)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Reference frame Rotating reference frame Coriolis force
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
A projectile thrown across a rotating disk's diameter at speed v relative to the thrower inherits the thrower's tangential velocity. During its approximate flight time 2a/v, a receiver at the opposite rim moves tangentially in the opposite direction. The relative sideways displacement is consequently 4ωa2/v to leading order in aω/v. Integrating the Coriolis force in the disk's frame gives the same result.
