Electron transfer and ionic character

 Home Chemistry Chemical bond Ionic bonding
OurBigBook.com Words: 132
The bookkeeping steps Na→Na++e− and Cl+e−→Cl− conserve electric charge and describe the Electron inventories of separated species. They do not establish a chronological mechanism in which a labeled Electron jumps between two pre-existing chemical atoms and becomes a classical object owned exclusively by one of them. In a bound material, quantum mechanics determines the electron density and the total energy.
Ionic bonding and a covalent bond are useful limiting descriptions with a continuum between them. Electronegativity helps anticipate unequal sharing of electron density, but a numerical cutoff does not measure a universal fraction of ionic bonding. A partial atomic charge depends on how the electron density is partitioned; integer formal charges are a bookkeeping convention. Using ±e in a point-charge model therefore does not claim that every partition of the actual electron density assigns exactly those values.[ref][ref]
