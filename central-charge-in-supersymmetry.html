Central charge in supersymmetry (ZAB=−ZBA)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry Super-Poincaré algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 206 Articles: 3
A central charge in four-dimensional extended supersymmetry is a Lorentz scalar even generator appearing in {QαA​,QβB​}=ϵαβ​ZAB. It commutes with the displayed translation, Lorentz algebra, and supercharge generators. An added R-symmetry automorphism can act on the central charges.
