Critical-point slope of barotropic spherical accretion (C=ρd(cs2​)/dρ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Barotropic fluid Barotropic spherical accretion equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
At a regular sonic point, differentiating the vanishing coefficient and numerator yields (2+C/cs2​)(u′)2+4Cu′/(ru)+(4C+2cs2​)/r2+Φ′′=0. A real slope compatible with the desired branch is needed in addition to the sonic equalities. For constant sound speed this reduces to (u′)2=−cs2​/r2−Φ′′/2.
