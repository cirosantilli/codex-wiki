Jordan decomposition in an affine algebraic group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Affine algebraic group
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
Every element g of an affine algebraic group has unique commuting semisimple and unipotent parts gs​,gu​ such that g=gs​gu​. The construction agrees with the multiplicative Jordan decomposition in every rational representation.
