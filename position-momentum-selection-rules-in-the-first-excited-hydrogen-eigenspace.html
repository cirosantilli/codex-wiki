Position-momentum selection rules in the first excited hydrogen eigenspace

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Hydrogen atom Hydrogen energy eigenbasis First excited hydrogen eigenspace
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 1
The operators X3​ and P3​ commute with L3​, so they preserve m. Both are odd under parity, so they connect only states of opposite orbital parity. Within the n=2 eigenspace, only the pair ∣2,0,0⟩ and ∣2,1,0⟩ can therefore have nonzero matrix elements.
