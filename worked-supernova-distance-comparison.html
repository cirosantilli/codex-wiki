Stipulate H0​=70kms−1Mpc−1 and c=299792.458kms−1, so c/H0​=4282.7494Mpc. A megaparsec is 106 parsecs. Neglect radiation in cosmology. Compare an accelerating flat model (Ωm,0​,ΩΛ,0​)=(0.3,0.7), an open matter-only model (0.3,0) and the Einstein-de Sitter universe (1,0). Their present deceleration parameters are respectively −0.55, 0.15 and 0.5. For these models,

Here χ is dimensionless; the radial null geodesic in FLRW spacetime supplies its integral and spatial curvature of an FLRW universe supplies Sk​. The observer-to-source comoving radial distance is (c/H0​)χ, whereas the transverse distance uses Sk​. Omitting the latter distinction would bias the open-model comparison.
At z=0.5, numerical integration gives χ=0.440984335 for the flat accelerating model and χ=0.392227378 for the open matter-only model. Thus

The accelerating model is 0.21559 mag fainter than the open matter-only prediction at the same redshift and standardized absolute magnitude, corresponding to 1.10438 times the luminosity distance and 0.81991 times the radiative flux. These are model calculations, not observations from either collaboration.
The Milne model has q=0, Ωm,0​=ΩΛ,0​=0 and Ωk,0​=1, giving χ=log(1+z) and dL​=(c/H0​)(z+z2/2). The Einstein-de Sitter luminosity-distance relation independently checks the flat matter-only integral. At z=0.5, the accelerating model exceeds the Milne model by only 0.12317 mag, illustrating the dependence of a residual on the chosen reference. Over a longer redshift interval, luminosity distance integrates past deceleration as well as recent acceleration; a residual need not increase forever.
Multiplying H0​ by f>0 at fixed density fractions divides every dL​ by f and shifts every distance modulus by −5log10​f. The predicted apparent magnitude is unchanged if MB,0​ simultaneously increases by 5log10​f. This is the absolute magnitude–Hubble constant degeneracy. For example, 70→77kms−1Mpc−1 shifts μ by −0.20696 mag and MB,0​ by +0.20696 mag. Relative curves at the same H0​ retain their separation; determining H0​ requires an external absolute magnitude calibration.
