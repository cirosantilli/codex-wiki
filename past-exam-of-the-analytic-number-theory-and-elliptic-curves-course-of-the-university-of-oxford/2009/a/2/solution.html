This is the three-four-one inequality for Euler products applied to the real character modulo four.
Suppose t=0 and L(1+it)=0. Holomorphy gives L(σ+it)=O(σ−1) as σ↓1. The only pole of the Riemann zeta function is at one, so ζ(σ+2it) remains bounded, while ζ(σ)3=O((σ−1)−3). The displayed product is then O(σ−1), contradicting its lower bound. Hence L(1+it)=0 for t=0.
