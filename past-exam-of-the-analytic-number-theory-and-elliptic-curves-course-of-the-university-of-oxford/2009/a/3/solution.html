We use the standard facts that ζ has no zeros on σ≥1, has a simple pole at one, and is meromorphic with no other pole. The function (s−1)ζ(s) is holomorphic function and nonzero on the compact set {1+it:∣t∣≤T}, including its removable value one at s=1. It remains nonzero in a neighborhood of this compact set.
