In the real series of p-adic absolute values, the block pk≤n<pk+1 contains (p−1)pk equal terms of size p−k. Its sum is p−1, independently of k. Infinitely many such positive block sums force divergence. This exhibits p-adic convergence without summable absolute values: the criterion is that the terms tend to zero, rather than real absolute summability.
