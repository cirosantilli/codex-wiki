The discriminant of y2=x3−9 is −16⋅27⋅81=−2437, so five and seven are primes of good reduction of an elliptic curve. Count solutions over their residue fields.
Modulo five the values of x3−9 for x=0,1,2,3,4 are 1,2,4,3,0. They yield 2,0,2,0,1 affine points, respectively, and the point at infinity gives #E(F5​)=6.
Modulo seven the values for x=0,…,6 are 5,6,6,4,6,4,4. Only the three values four are nonzero squares, giving six affine points and #E(F7​)=7.
For a rational elliptic curve at an odd prime of good reduction of an elliptic curve, the kernel of reduction on Qp​ has no torsion element: it is the formal group on pZp​, whose formal logarithm embeds it in the additive group pZp​. Thus the entire torsion subgroup injects into each of the two finite reduction groups. Its order divides both six and seven, hence is one:
E(Q)tors​={o}.​
(40)
Equivalently one may use prime-to-residue-characteristic injection at both primes; it gives the same coprime bound. This is a two-prime bound on rational elliptic torsion.
