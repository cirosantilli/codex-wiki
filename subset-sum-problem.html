Subset sum problem (∑i∈S​si​=t)

 ... Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Polynomial-time reduction Polynomial-time many-one reduction NP-hardness NP-completeness
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
Given finitely encoded nonnegative integers si​ and target t, the subset sum problem asks whether some subset sums to t. Taking equal item weights and profits reduces it to testing whether a 0-1 knapsack problem with capacity t attains value t.
