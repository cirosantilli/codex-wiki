Consumption allocation (f:A→R+L​)

 Home Mathematics Mathematical economics General equilibrium theory Exchange economy
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
In an economy with a measure space of consumers, a consumption allocation is a measurable, integrable function assigning each consumer a feasible bundle. Exact aggregate feasibility means ∫A​fdμ=∫A​ωdμ.
