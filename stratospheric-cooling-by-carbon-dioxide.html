Stratospheric cooling by carbon dioxide

 ... Astrophysics Planetary science Planet Earth Terrestrial atmosphere Stratosphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
An increase of atmospheric carbon dioxide can cool the stratosphere through enhanced infrared cooling relative to non-infrared heating and through changes in the spectrum of upwelling radiation. A local emissivity-only explanation is incomplete because absorption and emission both change. The response depends on ozone solar heating, spectral opacity, altitude and adjustments of the layers below.[ref]
