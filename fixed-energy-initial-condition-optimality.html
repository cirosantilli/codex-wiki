Fixed-energy initial-condition optimality (PH​∇J(u0​)=λu0​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Control theory Optimal control Direct-adjoint looping
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For a differentiable objective on an admissible real Hilbert space H, with ∥u0​∥2/2=E0​>0, stationarity on the sphere in a normed vector space of fixed kinetic energy means that the projected gradient is normal to the sphere in a normed vector space. Thus PH​∇J=λu0​ for a real Lagrange multiplier. It follows by testing all tangent variations orthogonal to u0​. The multiplier need not be positive; second-order conditions are still needed for a local minimum.
