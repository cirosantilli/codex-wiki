For the entropy balance of a thermostatted resistor, electrical work becomes heat Q=I2Rt=2.0×107J. At fixed temperature the resistor has no net change of thermodynamic state; it exports this heat to its thermostat. Treating the electrical supply as a source of work,
ΔSresistor​=0,ΔSuniverse​=TI2Rt​=6.67×104JK−1.​
(1)
Entropy is produced by Joule heating even though it does not accumulate in the resistor. Additional entropy production inside a nonideal power supply is outside the specified model.
