The field law. In the Biot-Savart law, dB is the contribution to the magnetic flux density at the observation point from a directed wire element dℓ carrying steady conventional electric current I. The vector R points from that element to the observation point, R=∣R∣, and R=R/R. The constant μ0​ is the vacuum permeability; the cross product sets the direction by the right-hand rule. Integrating the elements gives the total field, in teslas.
The moment of the shown loop. The arrows in the original PDF run counterclockwise viewed from +z. Thus the magnetic dipole moment is
m=IΔxΔyz.​
(22)
Its units are Am2. Reversing the current reverses both this moment and the field.
