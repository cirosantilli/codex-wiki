Use source coordinates (x′,y′,0) and observation point (0,0,z). The axial numerator in the Biot-Savart law is
[dℓ′×(−x′,−y′,z)]z​=x′dy′−y′dx′.

For ∣z∣ much larger than the loop dimensions, replace the denominator [z2+x′2+y′2]3/2 by ∣z∣3 to leading order. Transverse components cancel between opposite sides. The loop integral is twice its oriented area, so
Bz​≃4π∣z∣3μ0​I​∮(x′dy′−y′dx′)=4π∣z∣3μ0​I​(2ΔxΔy).
(24)

B(0,0,z)≃2π∣z∣3μ0​m​.​
(25)
