 ... Past exam of the physics B course of the University of Cambridge 2018 Paper 1 Section C C11 Corotating asteroid
OurBigBook.com Words: 239
The extra radial term. A circular Kepler orbit has Ωorb2​=GM/R3. In the frame rotating with the synchronously spinning body, centrifugal acceleration increases by Ωorb2​a across a radial offset a. This adds the outward differential acceleration
Δacent​=R3GM​a.​
(70)
Equivalently, in the inertial frame the farther corotating point needs an extra inward centripetal acceleration Ωorb2​a, which gravity alone does not supply. The total radial stretching relevant to the static corotating body is therefore 3GMa/R3. This is the corotating point-mass tidal field. The source's “force” expression is per unit mass.
Self-gravitational survival. Denote the asteroid radius by s, distinct from the orbital radius R. Its mass is ma​=4πρs3/3 and its surface self-gravitational acceleration is Gma​/s2=4πGρs/3. Compare this restoring acceleration with radial tidal stretching 3GMs/R3:
34πGρs​≳R33GMs​.
(71)
The corotating tidal-disruption estimate for a uniform sphere is
Rmin​≃(4πρ9M​)1/3.​
(72)
This is the requested spherical local force-balance estimate, not an exact fluid Roche figure. It neglects deformation and material cohesion.
Why the asteroid radius cancels. At fixed density, self-gravitational surface acceleration scales as ma​/s2∝s. The linear tidal acceleration also scales as s. Their ratio is therefore independent of s, provided s≪R so that the tidal expansion is valid. Density, rather than asteroid size, controls this estimate.
The terrestrial value. Convert ρ=2 gcm−3 to 2000 kgm−3 and use ME​=6.0×1024 kg:
Rmin​≃[4π(2000)9(6.0×1024)​]1/3≃1.29×107 m=1.29×104 km.​
(73)
This is distance from Earth's centre, not height above its surface.
