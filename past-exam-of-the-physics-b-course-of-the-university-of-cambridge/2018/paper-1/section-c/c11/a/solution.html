For a point mass, g(R)=−GMR/R3. Along the outward radial direction, gR​=−GM/R2 and
dRdgR​​=R32GM​.
(66)
Thus a small radial displacement a=aR has differential acceleration
T(a)≃R32GM​a.​
(67)
The farther point falls inward less strongly, so its acceleration relative to the reference point points outward. The formula is first order in ∣a∣/R.
