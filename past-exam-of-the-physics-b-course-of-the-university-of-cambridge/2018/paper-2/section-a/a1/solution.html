For the usual hollow rectangular electromagnetic waveguide, let the propagation axis be z and the interior be 0<x<a, 0<y<b. A transverse magnetic polarization has Bz​=0 and Ez​=0. At a perfect conductor wall the tangential electric field vanishes. Since Ez​ is tangential to all four side walls, it satisfies a homogeneous Dirichlet boundary condition around the rectangle.
Separating the transverse Helmholtz equation therefore gives the transverse magnetic mode of a rectangular waveguide
Ez​=E0​sin(anπx​)sin(bmπy​)ei(kz​z−ωt),n,m=1,2,….
(1)
A zero value of either index makes Ez​ identically zero, rather than producing a nonzero field uniform along that direction. The transverse electric and magnetic fields of this TM family are obtained from transverse derivatives of Ez​, so they also vanish. Thus both TM indices must be nonzero: neither TMn0​ nor TM0m​ exists in this rectangular hollow-guide model. In contrast, transverse electric modes obey a Neumann boundary condition on Bz​ and may have one zero index. The claim is about this waveguide geometry, not all possible guides.
