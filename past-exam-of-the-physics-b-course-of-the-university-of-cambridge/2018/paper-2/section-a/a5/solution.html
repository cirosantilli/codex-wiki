For attraction U<0, the singly occupied state never minimizes the grand energy. At the crossing of the empty and doubly occupied states, μ∗​=ϵ+U/2, its grand energy is −U/2>0, while both competing states have zero grand energy. Hence
N(μ)={0,2,​μ<ϵ+U/2,μ>ϵ+U/2.​​
(11)
For U=0 the two thresholds coincide at μ=ϵ, giving a direct 0→2 step. At a threshold, distinct occupations are degenerate; zero-temperature minimization alone does not select a unique occupation. A grand canonical ensemble at small positive temperature smooths the steps, with
Ξ=1+2e−β(ϵ−μ)+e−β(2ϵ+U−2μ),⟨N⟩=Ξ2e−β(ϵ−μ)+2e−β(2ϵ+U−2μ)​.

In particular, the equal-weight empty/pair limit for U<0 gives mean occupation one exactly at its crossing; this is not a finite-width singly occupied plateau.
