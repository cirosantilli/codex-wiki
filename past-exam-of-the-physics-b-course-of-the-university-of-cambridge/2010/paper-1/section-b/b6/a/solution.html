A charging parallel-plate capacitor illustrates the physics. For plate area A, Q=ϵ0​AE, so the gap displacement current is Id​=ϵ0​AE˙=Q˙​, equal to the conduction current in its leads. An Ampère contour therefore gives the same magnetic circulation whether its spanning surface crosses the wire or the gap. No electrons have to cross an ideal vacuum gap.
