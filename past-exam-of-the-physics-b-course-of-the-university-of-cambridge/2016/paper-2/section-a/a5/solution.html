At fixed temperature, equilibrium blackbody radiation has fixed energy density u, with radiation pressure p=u/3. Doubling the volume therefore doubles its internal energy, so ΔU=U0​. The work done by the radiation is
W=∫V0​2V0​​pdV=3u​V0​=3U0​​.
(12)
The first law of thermodynamics, with positive work done by the radiation, gives
Q=ΔU+W=34​U0​=1000 μJ=1.00 mJ.​
(13)
Thus 1.00 mJ is absorbed from the walls. The walls must replace the energy used to do work as well as supply the extra stored energy; using only ΔU=750 μJ would omit that work. This is a reversible isothermal expansion of a photon gas, not an adiabatic expansion.
