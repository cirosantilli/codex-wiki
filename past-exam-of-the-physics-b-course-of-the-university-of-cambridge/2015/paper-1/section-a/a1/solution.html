For a smooth isolated conducting sphere, Gauss's law gives the surface electric field Es​=Q/(4πϵ0​a2) and electric potential V=Q/(4πϵ0​a)=aEs​. At the specified dielectric strength, Vmax​=3.0×103 V. The capacitance of an isolated conducting sphere is C=4πϵ0​a, so its capacitor energy is
Umax​=21​CVmax2​=2πϵ0​a3Es2​=5.0×10−7 J≃0.50 μJ.​
(1)
This is the ideal breakdown-limited energy of a conducting sphere, taking air's permittivity approximately equal to vacuum permittivity and neglecting nearby objects and surface irregularities. The vacuum permittivity used numerically is 8.8542×10−12 Fm−1.[ref]
