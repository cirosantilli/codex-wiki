After the photon has thermalized, its energy Eγ​=hc/λ belongs to the macroscopic body's internal energy. The body's heat capacity makes the resulting temperature change negligible. Its equilibrium entropy therefore increases by
ΔS≃λThc​.​
(6)
The Boltzmann entropy S=kB​lnΩ then gives the microstate multiplier from photon absorption:
Ωi​Ωf​​=exp(ΔS/kB​)≃exp(λkB​Thc​).​
(7)
For scale, explicitly choosing a representative visible wavelength λ=500 nm and room temperature T=300 K gives ΔS=1.32×10−21 JK−1, or ΔS/kB​=95.9, and a multiplier approximately 4.5×1041. These are illustrative choices, not additional supplied data: the exact multiplier depends exponentially on the particular visible wavelength and temperature. The exact Planck constant, speed of light, and Boltzmann constant fix the conversion.[ref]
