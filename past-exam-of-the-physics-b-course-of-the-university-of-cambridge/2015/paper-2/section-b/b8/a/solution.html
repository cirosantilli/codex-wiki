e˙3​=I⊥​L​×e3​,Ωspace​=L/I⊥​.​

Its angle to L is constant, with cosθ=I∥​ω3​/L. The body-frame and space-frame frequencies of free symmetric-top precession describe different frames. A spherical body has ω∥L and no corresponding wobble.
