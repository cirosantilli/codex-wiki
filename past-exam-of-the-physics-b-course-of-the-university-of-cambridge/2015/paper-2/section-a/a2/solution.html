For the image force on a line charge above a conducting plane, with no imposed external electric field, use the method of images: replace the plane by a parallel image line of charge −λ at height −h. The image is 2h from the real line, and its field at the real line has magnitude λ/(4πϵ0​h). Therefore
lengthF​=−4πϵ0​hλ2​z^.​
(3)
Here z^ points upward from the plane. The force is attractive and normal to the conductor, for either sign of λ. The image reproduces the induced surface charge field; it is not an extra physical charge, and no factor 1/2 is inserted in the force on the actual line.
