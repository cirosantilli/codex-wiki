For a fixed magnetic dipole moment in an externally imposed uniform magnetic field, the torque is τ=m×B. Its component in the direction of increasing θ is −mBsinθ. Since τθ​=−dU/dθ, integration gives
U(θ)=−m⋅B=−mBcosθ,​
(8)
where the arbitrary additive constant has been chosen so that U=0 at θ=π/2.
The aligned orientation θ=0 has the minimum U=−mB and is stable against small rotations; the anti-aligned orientation θ=π has the maximum U=+mB and is unstable. The graph repeats every 2π. Rotating quasistatically from alignment to anti-alignment requires external work ΔU=2mB.
