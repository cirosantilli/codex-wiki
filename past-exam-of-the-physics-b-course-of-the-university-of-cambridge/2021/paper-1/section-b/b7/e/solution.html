As R→0, the short joining fibre is very stiff and the lower normal mode has ϕ1​≃ϕ2​. The spheres therefore behave as one torsional load with combined moment of inertia 2I, suspended by the upper fibre of torsion constant Kt​/ℓ1​. Its angular frequency satisfies
ω−2​→2IKt​/ℓ1​​=2ω02​​.​
(32)
The high-frequency opposite-twist mode costs increasingly large elastic energy and moves to arbitrarily high frequency in the massless-fibre model.
As R→∞, the joining fibre is very compliant. In the higher normal mode the lower sphere scarcely moves: the amplitude ratio tends to A2​/A1​≃−1/R→0. The upper sphere approaches the original single torsion pendulum, giving
ω+2​→ω02​.​
(33)
The other mode is then a slow twist of the almost freely suspended lower sphere. These interpretations assume the formal limits do not invalidate the thin massless-fibre approximation.
