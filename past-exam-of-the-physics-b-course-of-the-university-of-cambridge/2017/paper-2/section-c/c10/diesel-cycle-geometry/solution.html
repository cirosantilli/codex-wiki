 ... Past exam of the physics B course of the University of Cambridge 2017 Paper 2 Section C C10 Diesel-cycle geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 147
Label the initial state 1, the end of compression 2, the end of constant-pressure heating 3, and the end of adiabatic expansion 4. The volume sequence is V1​→V2​→V3​→V1​→V1​, with V2​<V3​<V1​. The Diesel cycle has a compression adiabat 1→2, a horizontal constant-pressure heat-input segment 2→3, an expansion adiabat 3→4, and a vertical constant-volume cooling segment 4→1.
For an ideal gas, a reversible adiabat obeys pVγ=constant, so it falls more steeply than an isotherm. The expansion adiabat lies above the compression adiabat at equal volume between V3​ and V1​, giving a clockwise cycle with positive work output.
