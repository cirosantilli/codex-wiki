The symmetry is electromagnetic reciprocity applied to mutual inductance. For thin closed circuits in vacuum, the magnetic vector potential generated by circuit B is
AB​(r)=4πμ0​IB​​∮B​∣r−rB​∣dℓB​​.
(10)
Stokes theorem converts the magnetic flux through circuit A into its magnetic vector potential circulation. Dividing by IB​ gives Neumann's mutual-inductance formula,
MAB​=IB​ΦA←B​​=4πμ0​​∮A​∮B​∣rA​−rB​∣dℓA​⋅dℓB​​.
(11)
The dot product and separation are unchanged by interchanging A and B, so
MAB​=MBA​.​
(12)
Each has units WbA−1=H. The winding orientations must be used consistently to determine the common sign. The same reciprocity holds in ordinary linear reciprocal magnetic media; it is not an unrestricted statement about biased nonreciprocal materials. Equivalently, a reversible magnetic energy function has equal mixed derivatives with respect to the two currents.
