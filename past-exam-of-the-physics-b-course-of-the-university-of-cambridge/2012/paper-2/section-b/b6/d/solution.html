Thus transverse waves propagate above the plasma frequency, while below it the wavenumber is imaginary and a semi-infinite lossless plasma reflects an incident wave with an evanescent interior field. In the cold electrostatic model, a longitudinal density oscillation instead has frequency ωp​.

The simple dielectric function is a dynamical electron-inertia response under stated assumptions.
