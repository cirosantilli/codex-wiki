For an attractive inverse-square force, write V(r)=−κ/r with κ>0, and take nonzero conserved angular momentum J. The conic section orbit has
r=1+ecosψJ2/(mκ)​,e2=1+mκ22EJ2​.
(36)
The physically allowed cases are ellipse for −mκ2/(2J2)≤E<0, parabola for E=0, and hyperbola for E>0. The minimum allowed energy gives the circular special case e=0. Here E is measured relative to infinity. Zero angular momentum instead gives radial collision or escape trajectories, interpreted as degenerate limits rather than regular closed ellipses.
