For fixed, magnetically linear geometry, self-inductance relates a coil's own magnetic flux linkage to its electric current, Λi​=Li​Ii​. mutual inductance relates the linkage in one coil to the current in the other, Λ21​=MI1​. These coefficients enter the induced electromotive forces through Ei​=−dΛi​/dt; reversing a chosen winding orientation reverses the sign assigned to M.
In the ideal confined-field toroid, a circular magnetostatic Ampère law contour encloses N1​I, so
Hϕ​(r)=2πrN1​I​(a<r<b).​
(14)
Write w=b−a for the height of the square cross-section. The inductance of a rectangular-section toroid follows by integrating the magnetic flux density over that cross-section; it cannot be replaced by its value at an arbitrary mean radius:
Φ=w∫ab​μ0​μr​Hϕ​(r)dr=2πμ0​μr​N1​Iw​lnab​.
(15)
Consequently, for winding normals chosen consistently,
Li​=2πμ0​μr​Ni2​(b−a)​lnab​,M0​=2πμ0​μr​N1​N2​(b−a)​lnab​=L1​L2​​.​
(16)
The equality means complete shared magnetic flux linkage; with leakage one instead has ∣M∣<L1​L2​​.
For the crack, use the radial-path approximation for a cracked toroid, with an air length d on each path and core length 2πr−d. Continuity of the normal magnetic flux density at a crack face gives Hg​=μr​Hc​. Hence
N1​I=μ0​μr​B(r)​(2πr−d)+μ0​B(r)​d,B(r)≃2πr+(μr​−1)dμ0​μr​N1​I​.
(17)
Integrating the radial paths yields
Md​≃2πμ0​μr​N1​N2​w​lna+qb+q​,q=2π(μr​−1)d​,M0​Md​−M0​​≃ln(b/a)ln[(b+q)/(a+q)]​−1=−0.529.​
(18)
Here q=1.5915cm and Md​/M0​=0.47079: about a 53% reduction. The small ratio d/a does not justify expanding in μr​d/a, which is about ten. The crack's low magnetic permeability gives appreciable magnetic reluctance. This is an estimate within a confined-flux model; actual redistribution near the slit is outside its accuracy. Narrow-gap circuit models assume small gap width relative to transverse dimensions and approximately directed flux.[ref]
For the intact ring, ideal poloidal winding currents and the no-leakage boundary condition admit H=N1​Iϕ​/(2πr) with zero radial and vertical components. It is curl-free inside the current-free core, divergence-free for uniform magnetic permeability, tangent to the core boundary, and has the required circulation; uniqueness of the Neumann problem removes an additional homogeneous scalar-potential field. This explains zero non-azimuthal components in the stipulated intact-ring idealization. A literal slit breaks that rotational symmetry, so zero transverse components everywhere is not exact for the cracked ring: magnetic fringing and flux redistribution occur. In fact, extending the approximate B(r)∝1/(r+q) unchanged around a current-free core would give [∇×H]z​=0. Thus that radial-path calculation and a claim of exact purely azimuthal field in the cracked geometry cannot both be exact. The numerical answer above retains the intended circuit approximation while making its limitation explicit.
