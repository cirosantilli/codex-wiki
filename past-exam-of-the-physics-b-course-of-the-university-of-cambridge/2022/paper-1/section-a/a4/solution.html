The quoted capacitance is that of the filled parallel-plate capacitor, Ci​=1.0×10−6 F. Disconnecting the source imposes charge conservation on the isolated plates:
Q=Ci​Vi​=(1.0×10−6)(2500)=2.50×10−3 C.
(10)
On removing the dielectric, the capacitance falls by its relative permittivity, so Cf​=Ci​/5=2.0×10−7 F. The capacitor energy at fixed electric charge is U=Q2/(2C). Therefore
Ui​=21​Ci​Vi2​=3.125 J,Uf​=2Cf​Q2​=5Ui​=15.625 J,
(11)
and
ΔU=Uf​−Ui​=+12.5 J.​
(12)
The increase is supplied by the external work needed to withdraw the dielectric against its attraction into the electric field, in the lossless quasistatic limit. The final electric potential difference is Q/Cf​=12500 V, not the original charging value.
