The Poynting vector is the local electromagnetic energy flux density:
N=E×H.​
(40)
Here E is the electric field and H the magnetic field intensity. For an oriented area element dS, N⋅dS is the electromagnetic power crossing that element in its positive normal direction. Thus ∫S​N⋅dS is outward power through a closed surface, in watts. The Poynting vector has units Wm−2, and its direction describes local electromagnetic energy transport.
