 ... Past exam of the physics B course of the University of Cambridge 2022 Paper 2 Section C C8 a
OurBigBook.com Words: 156
Use an ideal gas to illustrate the Carnot cycle, with hot and cold heat baths at Th​=T2​ and Tc​=T1​. The four processes are all reversible thermodynamic processes. Lettered states below belong only to this illustration; the engine later in the question uses numbered states.
Starting at A, perform the following four stages:
  • A→B: isothermal expansion at Th​. The gas absorbs heat Qh​ from the hot heat bath and does work. Its internal energy is unchanged, so Qh​=WAB​=nRTh​ln(VB​/VA​).
  • B→C: reversible ideal-gas adiabat during expansion. Insulation prevents heat transfer; work done by the gas reduces its internal energy and lowers its temperature from Th​ to Tc​.
  • C→D: isothermal compression at Tc​. Work done on the gas is rejected as heat Qc​ to the cold heat bath, with Qc​=nRTc​ln(VC​/VD​)>0.
  • D→A: insulated adiabatic compression, performed reversibly, raises the temperature back to Th​ and closes the cycle.
The clockwise enclosed area in the pressure-volume diagram is the positive net work per cycle, W=∮pdV=Qh​−Qc​.
