Random-walk exit bound from a positive-increment block (ET≤m/pm)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Stopping time Geometric tail bound from a uniform escape probability
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For a random walk with independent and identically distributed increments and an exit time from (0,r), suppose p=P(X1​>r/m)>0 for some integer m≥1. A block of m such positive increments forces an exit. Conditional on survival, every successive block has success probability at least pm, so P(T>km)≤(1−pm)k and ET≤m/pm. This is an instance of the geometric tail bound from a uniform escape probability; centering of the increments is not required.
