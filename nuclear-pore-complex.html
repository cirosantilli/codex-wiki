Nuclear pore complex

 Home Biology Biological cell Cell nucleus Nuclear envelope
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A nuclear pore complex is an assembly of proteins spanning the nuclear envelope and regulating passage between the cell nucleus and cytosol. It is a selective gateway, not simply an unregulated hole: messenger RNA export and import of nuclear proteins require appropriate transport machinery. See Alberts et al.: nuclear pore complexes.
