Molecular symmetry

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Point group
OurBigBook.com Words: 599 Articles: 19
The spatial symmetry of a fixed molecular geometry. Its point group acts on atomic positions, orbitals and vibrational coordinates; these actions need not have the same group representation.
