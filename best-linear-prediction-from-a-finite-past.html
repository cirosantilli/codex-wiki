Best linear prediction from a finite past (Xt∣T​=projspan(X1​,…,XT​)​Xt​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series
OurBigBook.com Words: 365 Articles: 6
The best mean-square linear predictor is orthogonal projection onto the span of the available centered observations, plus the known mean. Orthogonal innovations give a convenient basis of that span. Prediction at a future horizon must use only actually observed variables; it need not be a one-step predictor.
