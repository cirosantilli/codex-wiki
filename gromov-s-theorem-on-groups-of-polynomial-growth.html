Gromov's theorem on groups of polynomial growth

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Growth function of a discrete metric space Growth function of a finitely generated group Polynomial growth of a group
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
Every finitely generated group of polynomial growth of a group has a virtually nilpotent group structure. Applying the one-scale virtual nilpotence theorem gives a finite normal subgroup with nilpotent quotient; centralizing that finite subgroup produces a nilpotent subgroup of finite index.
