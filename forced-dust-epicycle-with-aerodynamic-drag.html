Let vx​,vy​ and ux​,uy​ be the dust and gas velocity perturbations after subtracting the common background shear, suppressing primes. Put ts​>0 for the aerodynamic stopping time. Linear axisymmetric dust perturbations about a Keplerian shearing sheet obey Dϵ​vx​−2Ωvy​=ϵΩux​ and Dϵ​vy​+Ωvx​/2=ϵΩuy​, with ϵΩ=ts−1​>0. Eliminating vy​ gives

Thus a gas epicycle ux​=ucos(kx−Ωt), uy​=usin(kx−Ωt)/2 supplies both 2ϵΩ2usin(kx−Ωt) and ϵ2Ω2ucos(kx−Ωt). Keeping the latter is necessary for an exact linear equation beyond first order in drag. The homogeneous roots are −ϵΩ±iΩ, separating damping from epicyclic frequency.
