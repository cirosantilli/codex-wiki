Depression

 Home Science Psychology Clinical psychology
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
Depressive mood and related symptoms, or a context-dependent shorthand for a depressive disorder. Research should specify whether the outcome is symptom severity, a current diagnosed episode, first onset or recurrence.
