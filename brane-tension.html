Brane tension (Tp​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Brane
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The brane tension is the energy per unit spatial brane volume and multiplies its geometric action. In units ℏ=c=1, a p-brane tension has mass dimension p+1. The p=1 case is the string tension.
