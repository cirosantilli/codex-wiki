Finite-ring antiferromagnetic Ising correlation (Cn​=(tn+tN−n)/(1+tN),t=−tanh(βJ))

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Spin model Ising model Antiferromagnetic Ising model Antiferromagnetic Ising ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 1
The exact zero-field two-point correlation function Cn​=⟨S1​Sn+1​⟩ in the finite canonical ensemble of an antiferromagnetic Ising ring. The spin-chain transfer matrix has eigenvalues λ+​=2cosh(βJ) and λ−​=−2sinh(βJ). In their eigenbasis, an Ising spin insertion exchanges the two eigenvectors. The trace with two insertions is therefore λ−n​λ+N−n​+λ+n​λ−N−n​, while the partition function is λ+N​+λ−N​, proving the formula. Its two terms retain both paths around the ring. It has C0​=1 and Cn​=CN−n​. At fixed n and finite positive inverse temperature its thermodynamic limit is (−tanhβJ)n.
