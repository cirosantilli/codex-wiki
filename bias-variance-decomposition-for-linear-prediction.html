If Y,Y∗ are independent N(μ,σ2In​) vectors and H is a rank-p orthogonal projection, then
E∥HY−Y∗∥2=∥(I−H)μ∥2+(n+p)σ2.
(157)
The first term is squared approximation bias, while pσ2 is fitted-model variance and nσ2 is irreducible new-response noise.
