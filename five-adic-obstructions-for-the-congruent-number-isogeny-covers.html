Five-adic obstructions for the congruent-number isogeny covers (α′(E5′​)={1,5})

OurBigBook.com Words: 130
For E5′​:y2=x3+100x, the two-isogeny descent classes 2 and 10 have no rational covering points. Their primitive integral quartics are respectively N2=2U4+50V4 and N2=10(U4+V4), with gcd(U,V)=1.
For the first equation, reduction modulo 5 forces 5∣U and 5∣N, since 2 is not a square modulo 5 and a nonzero fourth power is 1. Put U=5U1​,N=5N1​. Dividing by 25 gives N12​=50U14​+2V4, again a nonsquare modulo 5 because 5∤V. For the second equation, a primitive pair has U4+V4 nonzero modulo 5: it is 1 or 2. The right side has five-adic valuation exactly 1, impossible for a square. Since (5,25) lies on E5′​, its square-class image is exactly {1,5}. Combined with the rank bound for a prime congruent-number elliptic curve computation, the two-isogeny rank formula yields rank exactly 1 for E5​:y2=x3−25x.
