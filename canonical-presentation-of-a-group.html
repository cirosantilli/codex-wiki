Canonical presentation of a group (⟨G∣ker(F(G)→G)⟩)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Group presentation
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
The canonical presentation of a group uses one symbol for each group element and all words that evaluate to the identity element as relators. Evaluation is a surjective group homomorphism from the free group on the underlying set; the first isomorphism theorem proves that the resulting group presentation recovers the original group. Equivalently the multiplication-table relators sg​sh​sgh−1​ normally generate its kernel: they reduce every word to the symbol for its value. [ref]
