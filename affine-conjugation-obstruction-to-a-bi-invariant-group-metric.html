Affine conjugation obstruction to a bi-invariant group metric (Da​Tb​Da−1​=Tab​)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Left-invariant group metric Bi-invariant group metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
In the orientation-preserving affine group of the real line, translations T1/n​ converge to the identity element, but conjugation by dilation Dn​ sends each one to T1​. A compatible bi-invariant group metric would give d(e,T1/n​)=d(e,T1​)>0 for every n, contradicting convergence. The group is nonetheless metrizable in its ordinary two real coordinates.
