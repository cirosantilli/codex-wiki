Radial distance in a normal neighbourhood (r(q)=∣expp−1​(q)∣)

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Geodesic Exponential map (Riemannian geometry) Normal neighbourhood
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
In a normal neighbourhood U=expp​(V) with V star-shaped, define r(q)=∣expp−1​(q)∣. Away from p, the Gauss lemma gives ∇r=(dexpp​)v​(v/∣v∣) for v=expp−1​(q). Thus ∣∇r∣=1 and this unit vector field is tangent to unit-speed radial geodesics. The function r is not smooth at p, but r2/2 is smooth and ∇(r2/2)=(dexpp​)v​v extends by zero. On a sufficiently small normal ball, r equals the Riemannian distance from p.
