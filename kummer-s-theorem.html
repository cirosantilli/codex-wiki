Kummer's theorem (vp​(kn​)=(dp​(k)+dp​(n−k)−dp​(n))/(p−1))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory P-adic valuation
OurBigBook.com Words: 185
For a prime number p and integers 0≤k≤n, the P-adic valuation of the binomial coefficient (kn​) equals the number of arithmetic carries in the base-p addition of k and n−k.[ref] Indeed, subtract the two denominator factorial valuations in the Legendre formula:
vp​(kn​)=vp​(n!)−vp​(k!)−vp​((n−k)!)=p−1dp​(k)+dp​(n−k)−dp​(n)​.
(54)
Each arithmetic carry accounts for one unit of this quotient by subadditivity of the base-p digit sum. In particular, the binomial coefficient is divisible by p exactly when at least one carry occurs.
For n=50,k=17,p=3, the factorial counts give
v3​(1750​)=22−(5+1)−(11+3+1)=1.
(55)
The matching base-p expansions are 17=(0122)3​ and 33=(1020)3​. Their units add to two; the next column gives 2+2=4=1+3, sending one carry into the nines column. That column then gives 1+0+1=2, and no further carry occurs. The result is (1212)3​=50. The base-p digit sums check the same result: (5+3−6)/(3−1)=1. Consequently (1750​) is divisible by three but not by nine.
