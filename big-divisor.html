Big divisor

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Cartier divisor Positivity of divisors
OurBigBook.com Words: 943 Articles: 11
A Cartier divisor D on an integral n-dimensional projective variety is big when h0(X,mD)≥cmn for some c>0 and infinitely many positive integers m. Equivalently, its Iitaka dimension is n. Kodaira's lemma characterizes bigness by an ample-plus-effective decomposition, and the birational linear system criterion for bigness shows why this growth captures the full variety.
