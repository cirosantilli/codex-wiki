Introduction to Fourier Transform Spectroscopy by Julius Cohen (1986; NBSIR 86-3339)

 Home Physics Physics bibliography
OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by Julius Cohen Words: 22
Institutional report on the Michelson interferometer, interferograms, Fourier transform spectroscopy, interferogram apodization, resolution and sampling. Chapters 13–16 derive the reconstruction and discuss finite-window and sampling limitations.
