Nonpolarizing beam splitter

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Beam splitter
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
A nonpolarizing beam splitter is designed for approximately equal power splitting ratios for the two orthogonal linear polarizations, at specified wavelengths and angle of incidence. It need not be 50:50, lossless, or have equal transmission and reflection wave phases for both linear polarizations. A scalar beam splitter scattering matrix therefore still requires a specified electromagnetic polarization and operating range. Polarizing beam splitters instead aim to separate those linear polarizations into different paths.
