Between-study heterogeneity (τ2)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Meta-analysis Random-effects meta-analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 292 Articles: 5
Variation in the underlying study effects, additional to sampling error. The usual normal random-effects meta-analysis models it by a between-study variance τ2. It can reflect effect modification, design differences or within-study bias; it is not automatically a biological treatment difference.
