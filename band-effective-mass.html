Band effective mass (m∗)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bloch's theorem Electronic band structure Energy band
OurBigBook.com Words: 402 Articles: 6
In one dimension the band effective mass is defined by 1/m∗(k)=E′′(k)/ℏ2. Together with v=E′(k)/ℏ and ℏk˙=F, this gives v˙=F/m∗. Negative band curvature produces negative effective mass for the electron wave packet; near a filled-band maximum the equivalent missing-electron description uses positively responding holes. In several dimensions the inverse mass is the Hessian ∂i​∂j​E/ℏ2.
