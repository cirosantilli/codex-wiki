Differentiability from continuous partial derivatives (Df(a)h=∑j​∂j​f(a)hj​)

If all partial derivatives exist near a point and are continuous there, successive coordinate increments and the one-variable mean value theorem express f(a+h)−f(a) as ∑j​hj​∂j​f(a+ηj​(h)) with ηj​(h)→0. Subtracting the displayed linear map leaves o(∥h∥), proving Fréchet differentiability. In two dimensions the absolute remainder is at most ϵ(∣h1​∣+∣h2​∣)≤2​ϵ∥h∥ for small enough increments.
