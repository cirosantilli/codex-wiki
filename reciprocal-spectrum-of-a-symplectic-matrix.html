Reciprocal spectrum of a symplectic matrix (χM​(x)=x2mχM​(x−1))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic group Symplectic matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
For a symplectic matrix M of size 2m with determinant one, the defining identity MTJM=J gives M−1=J−1MTJ. Since similarity and transposition preserve the characteristic polynomial, χM−1​=χM​. Factoring xI−M=M(xM−1−I) then proves the displayed reciprocal identity for x=0. If χM​(z)=(z−λ)dR(z) with R(λ)=0, this identity gives χM​(x)=x2m−d(1−λx)dR(1/x) near x=λ−1. Thus reciprocal eigenvalues have the same algebraic multiplicity. The fixed points ±1 require the stronger parity argument in even multiplicities at fixed points of reciprocal root pairing.
