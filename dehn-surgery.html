Dehn surgery

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 330 Articles: 6
Dehn surgery removes tubular neighborhoods of a link in a three-dimensional manifold and performs Dehn filling on their boundary tori with specified primitive meridian slopes. Dehn surgery on a framed link is the integral-slope special case.
