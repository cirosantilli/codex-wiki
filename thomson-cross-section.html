Thomson cross-section (σT​=q4/(6πϵ02​m2c4))

A free nonrelativistic charge driven by a weak plane electromagnetic wave has peak acceleration ∣q∣E0​/m. Its time-averaged Larmor formula power is q4E02​/(12πϵ0​m2c3). Divide by incident light intensity ϵ0​cE02​/2 to obtain the displayed cross section, independent of frequency. For an electron, it is 8πre2​/3. The approximation assumes negligible Compton recoil kinematics and small driven velocity compared with the speed of light.
