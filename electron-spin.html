Electron spin (s=21​,Sn​=±ℏ/2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Spin Spin one-half
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
An electron has spin one-half. Its intrinsic spin angular momentum has two possible components, ±ℏ/2, along a specified unit vector n. These are the two eigenvalues of the corresponding spin measurement; the spin is distinct from the electron's orbital angular momentum.
