A Schauder estimate bounds a solution in a Hölder norm two derivatives stronger than the forcing term. For a uniformly elliptic Dirichlet problem with Cα coefficients,
∥u∥C2,α(Ω)​≤C(∥Lu∥Cα(Ω)​+∥u∥C0(Ω)​).
