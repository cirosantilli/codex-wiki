Three-region calcium nullcline (J(u)−K(u)v2/(1+v2)−δv=0)

 Home Biology Signal transduction Calcium signaling Two-pool calcium model
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
For J=βu2/(1+u2) and K=u4/(1+u4) with small leak δ, the positive nullcline is unique at each u because its derivative with respect to v is strictly negative. Its asymptotic regions are a high-store balance v≃(J−K)/δ, a crossing balance near J(uc​)=K(uc​), and a negligible-leak balance v2/(1+v2)≃J/K. The crossing relation is (Kc′​−Jc′​)(u−uc​)≃Kc​/v2−δv. These describe one continuous curve, not three distinct equilibria at a fixed cytoplasmic concentration.
