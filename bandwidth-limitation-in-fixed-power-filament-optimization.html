At fixed leading power of a periodic planar filament, the family asin(mkx+Ωt) has propulsive force of a periodic planar filament increasing with spatial harmonic m. The formal quadratic objective is unbounded without a spatial cutoff; large harmonics eventually invalidate the small-slope model. With a finite admissible Fourier series band, force divided by power is a weighted average of the signed ratios −(ξ⊥​−ξ∥​)kj​/(ξ⊥​Ωj​). An allowed extremal ratio is attained by a travelling wave mode, or by a superposition with one common phase speed.
