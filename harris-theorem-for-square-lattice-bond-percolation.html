Harris theorem for square-lattice bond percolation (θ(1/2)=0)

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Percolation theory Percolation probability Percolation critical probability Harris-Kesten theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
Uniform fixed-aspect rectangle crossing in bond percolation bounds at the self-dual parameter yield a positive lower bound for closed dual circuits in geometrically spaced disjoint annuli. The independent annular barriers for percolation imply that an origin-to-infinity open graph path has probability zero. Parameter monotonicity gives zero percolation probability for every p≤1/2.
