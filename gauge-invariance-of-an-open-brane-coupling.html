Gauge invariance of an open-brane coupling (S=q∫W​X∗C+q∫∂W​X∗B)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Brane Wess-Zumino brane coupling
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For an oriented brane worldvolume W, a gauge transformation C↦C+dΛ changes the Wess-Zumino brane coupling by q∫∂W​X∗Λ, by the Generalized Stokes theorem. If the brane boundary lies on a manifold carrying a form B of one lower degree, adding the displayed boundary term and transforming B↦B−Λ cancels this variation. The sign uses the induced boundary orientation. A closed worldvolume needs no boundary term; noncompact cases require appropriate support or decay conditions.
