Polynomial invariant ring (C[W]G)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation Invariant theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 512 Articles: 8
For a group acting linearly on W, act on the coordinate ring by (g⋅f)(w)=f(g−1w). The polynomial invariant ring is the fixed subalgebra C[W]G. It inherits the degree grading. Even over the complex numbers it need not be a unique factorization domain, as the quadratic cone invariant ring demonstrates.
