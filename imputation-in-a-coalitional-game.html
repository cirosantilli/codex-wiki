Imputation in a coalitional game (I(v)={x:x(N)=v(N), xi​≥v({i})})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Cooperative game theory Transferable utility game
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
An imputation is an efficient, individually rational allocation in a transferable utility game. Its total is the grand-coalition value and each player receives at least its singleton value. The set is nonempty exactly when v(N)≥∑i​v({i}), and is then compact. This individual-rationality condition distinguishes the nucleolus from the prenucleolus.
