Born–Infeld soliton

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory D-brane Dirac-Born-Infeld action
OurBigBook.com Words: 269 Articles: 4
A supersymmetric spike of a D-brane combines a worldvolume gauge field with a transverse scalar profile. Its geometry and charge represent strings ending on the brane. Electric spikes represent fundamental strings; magnetic spikes on a D3-brane represent D1-branes. The original construction is Brane Dynamics From the Born-Infeld Action.
