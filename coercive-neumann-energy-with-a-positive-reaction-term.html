Coercive Neumann energy with a positive reaction term (B[u,u]≥min(ν,μ)∥u∥H12​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Boundary condition Neumann boundary condition
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
A symmetric uniformly elliptic divergence-form energy with zeroth-order coefficient at least μ>0 controls the full H1 norm, including constants. Its quadratic energy minus a bounded load has a unique minimizer by the direct method, and vanishing first variation gives the natural Neumann weak equation. Unlike a pure Neumann Laplacian, this problem needs no zero-mean compatibility condition. The exact energy difference from the weak solution is half the bilinear energy of the difference.
