Aerodynamic stall

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Airfoil
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A loss of the approximately attached-flow relation between angle of attack and lift coefficient, associated with substantial flow separation. A stalled airfoil can still produce lift; its behavior must be measured or modeled beyond thin airfoil theory.
