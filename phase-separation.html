Phase separation

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Phase transition Phase coexistence
OurBigBook.com Words: 395 Articles: 8
A system can split into macroscopic regions with distinct local order parameters, densities or compositions. In a short-range system at phase coexistence, regions of two phases with means M1​,M2​ and volume fractions q,1−q have spatial mean M=qM1​+(1−q)M2​. Their interface contribution is subextensive, so their thermodynamic free-energy density is the corresponding convex combination of the phase densities. This can lie below the homogeneous-branch Landau free energy at the same M. The equilibrium constrained order-parameter free energy is consequently convexified, with a straight coexistence segment described by the Maxwell construction. A nonconvex local potential remains useful for homogeneous branches, metastability and interfaces, but must not be identified with the full equilibrium constrained density throughout a coexistence interval.
