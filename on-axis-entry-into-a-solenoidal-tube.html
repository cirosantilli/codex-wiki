On-axis entry into a solenoidal tube (rmax​=2mv⊥​/(∣q∣B))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Charged particle in a uniform magnetic field Guiding center
OurBigBook.com Words: 141 Articles: 1
A particle entering a uniform axial magnetic field on the tube axis follows a transverse circle through that entry point. Its guiding centre is one Larmor radius from the axis, so the maximum radial displacement from the axis is twice the Larmor radius. For tube radius a, clearance requires v⊥​≤∣q∣Ba/(2m) when the transit samples a complete gyration. If the transit is shorter, the actual sampled arc determines clearance instead. The axis is revisited after each full cyclotron period, not after each half-period.
