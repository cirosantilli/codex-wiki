Linear Jahn-Teller selection rule (ΓQ​⊂Sym2Γel​)

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry Ligand field Jahn-Teller effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For a real nonmagnetic electronic multiplet, a vibrational coordinate can couple linearly through a real symmetric electronic operator only if its representation occurs in the symmetric square. Remove the fully symmetric component when asking about symmetry-lowering distortion. In Td​, both Sym2T1​ and Sym2T2​ equal A1​+E+T2​, so E and T2​ modes are symmetry-eligible for either triplet; coupling strengths are not determined by this decomposition.
