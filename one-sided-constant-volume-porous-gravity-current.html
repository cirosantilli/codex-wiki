One-sided constant-volume porous gravity current (L3=9λVt/ϕ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Porous gravity current
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
A localized release of actual fluid volume V per unit width on a reflecting half-line x≥0 has the Barenblatt solution h=[L2−x2]+​/(6λt), where L3=9λVt/ϕ and λ=kΔρg/(ϕμ). The mass conservation normalization is ϕ∫0L​hdx=V. Zero slope at the reflecting boundary gives zero Darcy flux. For symmetric spreading on the full line, the same formula has L3=9λVt/(2ϕ) when V denotes the total released volume. A finite initial patch is not determined by volume alone; this exact source profile has a concentrated initial release.
