Cyclotomic character (κ:GF​→Zp×​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation Galois representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 1
The cyclotomic character is defined by g(ζ)=ζκ(g) on compatible p-power roots of unity. On the full cyclotomic tower of Q, it identifies the Galois group with Zp×​. Its finite-order part is the Teichmüller character and its pro-p part acts through 1+pZp​, or 1+4Z2​.
