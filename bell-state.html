Local flag correction of a Bell-state phase mixture

Words: 69
Bell state OurBigBook.com
Suppose Alice holds a classical orthogonal flag F for the phase of a Bell state:
ρABF​=p∣Φ+⟩⟨Φ+∣AB​⊗∣0⟩⟨0∣F​+(1−p)∣Φ−⟩⟨Φ−∣AB​⊗∣1⟩⟨1∣F​.
(1)
Her local controlled phase correction U=IAB​⊗∣0⟩⟨0∣F​+(ZA​⊗IB​)⊗∣1⟩⟨1∣F​ maps this to ∣Φ+⟩⟨Φ+∣AB​⊗[p∣0⟩⟨0∣+(1−p)∣1⟩⟨1∣]F​. Thus Alice and Bob recover an exact Bell pair without revealing its bit value. Even if Eve knows the flag, purity decouples a subsystem from its purification, so the corrected pair can supply a private bit. Discarding the flag first can instead yield a separable quantum state, as happens at p=1/2.

A Bell pair consists of two qubits prepared jointly in a Bell state. It is a maximally entangled two-qubit resource with Schmidt rank two. Two independent Bell pairs have resource Schmidt rank four across the same party partition.

No-communication theorem

Words: 90 Articles: 1
Reduced density matrix OurBigBook.com
A trace-preserving operation on subsystem B cannot change the reduced density matrix of a disjoint subsystem A when its unobserved outcomes are averaged. Local measurements on A therefore cannot reveal which operation was chosen on B.

Remote state invariance under a local trace-preserving operation (TrA​[(EA​⊗idB​)(ρAB​)]=ρB​)

Words: 56
No-communication theorem OurBigBook.com
For local Kraus operators Ai​ with ∑i​Ai†​Ai​=I, let ρAB′​=∑i​(Ai​⊗I)ρAB​(Ai†​⊗I). For every remote operator B, cyclicity of the trace gives Tr[(I⊗B)ρAB′​]=∑i​Tr[(Ai†​Ai​⊗B)ρAB​]=Tr[(I⊗B)ρAB​]. Hence the remote reduced density matrix is unchanged. In particular every remote POVM has the same outcome probabilities, proving the no-communication theorem from the measurement rule rather than assuming it as a physical postulate.

Entanglement distillation

Words: 95
Entangled state OurBigBook.com
Entanglement distillation uses local operations and classical communication on many noisy entangled states to produce fewer high-fidelity Bell pairs. It is also called entanglement purification. The protocol may fail or abort; naming a purification step does not guarantee successful output for arbitrary inputs. If the output is an exact pure state of Bell pairs, every purification of a density operator factors between those pairs and the environment, so measuring each pair in the computational basis gives a shared uniform bit independent of an eavesdropper. Approximate privacy follows when the entire retained output is sufficiently close to that pure target. A separable quantum state cannot be distilled into a Bell pair by LOCC, because each local branch preserves separability.

Entanglement dilution

Words: 213 Articles: 2
Entangled state OurBigBook.com
Entanglement dilution uses local operations and classical communication to prepare partially entangled states from maximally entangled states, such as Bell pairs. For many identical bipartite pure targets, the asymptotic cost in Bell pairs per target, allowing a vanishing approximation error, is their entanglement entropy in bits. The quantum typical subspace of the target's Schmidt coefficients has almost all its weight and dimension at most 2m(S+δ). A maximally entangled state of this dimension can be converted to the normalized truncated target by Nielsen's pure-state conversion theorem. The resulting state approaches the full target as the retained weight tends to one. Average monotonicity of pure-state entanglement entropy and entropy continuity give the converse lower bound. This is the reverse asymptotic task to entanglement concentration.
Exact dilution obstruction from Schmidt rank (m≥nfor exact GHZ⊗m→Ψp⊗n​)
Words: 54
Entanglement dilution OurBigBook.com
For 0<p<1, every bipartition of Ψp⊗n​ has Schmidt rank 2n, while m copies of a GHZ state have Schmidt rank 2m. Schmidt-rank contraction under product operators therefore forbids any exact successful LOCC branch when m<n. Approximate entanglement dilution avoids this obstruction by retaining only a high-weight quantum typical subspace, whose dimension has logarithm nh2​(p)+o(n). Exact finite-block conversion and asymptotically faithful conversion are different tasks.
Deterministic two-qubit entanglement dilution (∣Φ+⟩⟶u∣00⟩+v∣11⟩)
Words: 61
Entanglement dilution OurBigBook.com
For nonnegative u,v with u2+v2=1, Alice applies Kraus operators M0​=diag(u,v) and M1​=diag(v,u) to one half of a Bell state. Their squared effects sum to the identity, and both outcomes have probability one half. The first branch is the target; the second becomes the target after both parties apply Pauli X gates. Sending the outcome by classical communication makes this exact deterministic entanglement dilution, rather than a postselected conversion.

An entanglement monotone quantifies entanglement without increasing on average under local operations and classical communication. The average is over recorded measurement in quantum mechanics outcomes: individual successful branches can have more entanglement, provided their probabilities compensate for that increase. The relative entropy of entanglement is the smallest quantum relative entropy from a density operator to a separable quantum state, with the separable set specified for the relevant division into parties. Schmidt-tail entanglement monotone (E2​(χ)=1−λmax​(ρA​))
Words: 100
Entanglement monotone OurBigBook.com
For a bipartite pure state, the squared Schmidt coefficients are the nonzero eigenvalues of either reduced density matrix. Their sum except the largest defines E2​=1−λmax​(ρA​). The largest eigenvalue is a convex function, since λmax​(ρ)=max∥v∥=1​⟨v∣ρ∣v⟩; consequently E2​ is concave. If Alice measures locally and records outcome r, Bob's conditional reductions satisfy ∑r​pr​ρB,r​=ρB​. Concavity gives ∑r​pr​E2​(χr​)≤E2​(χ). For Bob's measurements use Alice's unchanged average reduction instead. Iteration proves average monotonicity under LOCC. For Schmidt rank at most two, E2​ is the smaller squared Schmidt coefficient. A conversion succeeding with probability p therefore obeys pE2​(ϕ)≤E2​(ψ), since failure branches contribute nonnegative amounts.

Tripartite Schmidt projection entanglement concentration (R=h2​(p))

Asymptotic pure-state entanglement conversion rate (R(ψ→ϕ)=S(ρAψ​)/S(ρAϕ​))

Words: 182 Articles: 1
Local operations and classical communication OurBigBook.com
For bipartite pure source and target states with positive target entanglement entropy, the optimal rate of LOCC conversion with vanishing output error is the ratio of their entanglement entropies. Here vanishing error means that the trace distance between the output and ∣ϕ⟩⟨ϕ∣⊗m tends to zero; a recorded failure probability must also tend to zero. Entanglement concentration first provides nS(ρAψ​)−o(n) Bell pairs, and entanglement dilution needs mS(ρAϕ​)+o(m) such pairs. This achieves the ratio. Conversely, refine the output into pure branches. Average monotonicity of pure-state entanglement entropy bounds their average entropy by the input entropy; continuity bound for quantum conditional entropy, with trivial conditioning, makes the output entropy loss per target tend to zero. This forbids a larger rate. The convention permits approximations for finite blocks: exact deterministic conversion of every finite block can obey stricter majorization constraints. The reversible concentration construction is given in the original concentration paper.

Collective advantage over independent two-qubit filtering (h2​(s)/h2​(t)>s/t(0<s<t<1/2))

For two-qubit pure states with smaller squared Schmidt coefficients s<t, optimal stochastic conversion of a two-qubit pure state succeeds independently with probability s/t. The asymptotic pure-state entanglement conversion rate of collective LOCC is instead h2​(s)/h2​(t), where h2​ is binary entropy. Optimal stochastic conversion of a two-qubit pure state (pmax​=min{1,s/t})

Words: 96
Local operations and classical communication OurBigBook.com
Write a source and target in Schmidt decomposition with squared coefficients (1−s,s) and (1−t,t), where 0<s,t≤1/2. If s≥t, Nielsen's pure-state conversion theorem allows deterministic conversion. If s<t, the Schmidt-tail entanglement monotone gives p≤s/t. Alice attains this bound with a local two-outcome measurement in quantum mechanics whose success Kraus operator is Ks​=diag(s(1−t)/(t(1−s))​,1) and whose failure operator is Kf​=diag(1−s(1−t)/(t(1−s))​,0). Local-unitary invariance of reduced-state spectra (ρA​↦UA​ρA​UA†​)

Words: 74
Local unitary operation OurBigBook.com
Under a product unitary operator UA​⊗UB​, the reduced density matrix of A becomes UA​ρA​UA†​. The unitary on B cancels under the partial trace. Unitary conjugation preserves eigenvalues, so the squared Schmidt coefficients of a bipartite pure state are unchanged. Conversely, two normalized bipartite pure states with the same squared Schmidt coefficients, including multiplicities and zero padding, have Schmidt decompositions carried into each other by local unitaries mapping their Schmidt bases. Thus reduced-state spectra completely classify bipartite pure states up to local unitary operations.

Nielsen's pure-state conversion theorem (∣ψ⟩LOCC​∣ϕ⟩⟺λ(ψ)≺λ(ϕ))

Words: 73
Local operations and classical communication OurBigBook.com
An exact deterministic LOCC conversion of a bipartite pure state to another is possible exactly when the input vector of squared Schmidt coefficients is majorized by the output vector. Sort the two probability vectors decreasingly and pad with zeros to a common length before applying majorization. A maximally entangled state may therefore be converted to a product state, while the reverse conversion is forbidden. The criterion does not assert conversion by postselection or with a catalyst.

Exact teleportation resource criterion

Words: 80
Quantum teleportation OurBigBook.com
A shared resource enables deterministic exact transmission of an arbitrary unknown qubit using only local operations and classical communication if and only if it can produce a shared Bell pair deterministically by such operations. Sufficiency follows from quantum teleportation. For necessity, prepare a Bell pair locally at the sender and apply the purported transmission protocol to one half: exact identity-channel action leaves a shared Bell pair between the retained reference and the receiver. A separable shared resource cannot satisfy this condition because local operations and classical communication preserve separability.

No-programming theorem

A deterministic finite-dimensional universal programmable quantum gate cannot implement every unitary exactly: programs for physically distinct unitaries would have to be mutually orthogonal, but a finite-dimensional program register contains only finitely many mutually orthogonal states.

Quantum dense coding uses one shared Bell pair and one transmitted qubit to communicate two classical bits.
