Lepton

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 23
A lepton is a Standard Model fermion without color charge. The charged leptons have electric charge −1 and their corresponding neutrinos have charge zero. There are three generations; their antiparticles carry opposite electric charge and lepton number. Charged leptons and neutrinos have lepton number +1, with the corresponding antiparticles carrying −1.
