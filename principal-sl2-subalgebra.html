Principal sl2 subalgebra (sl2pr​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 154 Articles: 2
A principal sl2 subalgebra of a semisimple Lie algebra has semisimple generator hpr​=2ρ∨. Restriction to it packages a representation's weights into ordinary sl2​ weight strings.
The infinitesimal embedding is a subalgebra of the semisimple Lie algebra; integrating it to a Lie-group homomorphism is a separate group-level construction.
