Milnor square

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic K-theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 226 Articles: 3
A cartesian square of unital rings A=A1​×A0​​A2​ is a Milnor square when at least one map Ai​→A0​ is surjective. Finitely generated projective modules over A can then be assembled from projective modules over the two upper rings together with an isomorphism of their reductions over A0​.
