Investment portfolio (H=(H1,…,Hn))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance
OurBigBook.com Words: 506 Articles: 11
A collection of holdings in traded assets. Its value is the dot product of the holdings with the asset prices. A self-financing portfolio is an investment portfolio whose trading requires no external funding.
