One-magnon Bloch states on a ring (E(q)−E0​=J[1−cos(qa)])

OurBigBook.com Words: 147
For a periodic spin-one-half Heisenberg model H=−(J/ℏ2)∑n=1N​Sn​⋅Sn+1​, J>0, the fully aligned state has E0​=−NJ/4. In its single-excitation subspace, let ∣n⟩ denote a down spin at site n in an otherwise up background. Direct action of each bond gives
H∣n⟩=(E0​+J)∣n⟩−2J​(∣n−1⟩+∣n+1⟩).
(94)
The normalized translation eigenstates are ∣q⟩=N−1/2∑n​eiqna∣n⟩, with q=2πℓ/(Na) modulo 2π/a. Their energy eigenvalues are E0​+J[1−cos(qa)]. This is an exact one-magnon calculation, not a many-magnon harmonic approximation. Near q=0 the energy above E0​ is Ja2q2/2.
The wavenumber restriction comes from requiring a periodic plane-wave coefficient pattern, equivalently requiring this vector to be an eigenvector of a one-site translation with eigenvalue e−iqa. The identity TN=I alone imposes no restriction on a general state: it already holds throughout the ring Hilbert space. An arbitrary finite sum with coefficients eiqna still returns after N translations, but need not be a one-site translation eigenstate unless eiqNa=1.
