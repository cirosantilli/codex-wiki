General-position hemisphere count (N−d≥2r−1)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Sphere Hemisphere Open hemisphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
Choose N points of Sd such that every d+1 of their position vectors are linearly independent. Any equatorial hyperplane then contains at most d points. If N−d≥2r−1, at least one of each opposite pair of open hemispheres contains r points. Such configurations exist: take distinct real parameters ti​ and normalize (1,ti​,…,tid​); the Vandermonde determinant proves the required independence. This weaker one-of-two guarantee already suffices for the topological lower bound in Lovász theorem on Kneser graphs.
