Electric field of a uniformly moving point charge

Let R join the instantaneous position of charge q to the field point, with constant β=v/c. Transforming its rest-frame Coulomb field gives
E=4πϵ0​[R2−∣β×R∣2]3/2q(1−β2)R​,B=v×E/c2.
(9)
The electric field is radial from the current position, reduced along the velocity and enhanced transversely. This present-position form is compatible with the causal retarded solution specifically because motion is uniform. There is no acceleration-dependent radiation field, although the moving charge has a magnetic field and transports electromagnetic energy.
