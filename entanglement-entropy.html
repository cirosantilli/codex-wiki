Entanglement entropy (SA​=−Tr(ρA​logρA​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Von Neumann entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 559 Articles: 11
For a bipartite pure state, the entanglement entropy is the Von Neumann entropy of either reduced density matrix. The two reduced states have the same nonzero eigenvalues, so their entropies agree.
