The ideal voltage source drives a series L–R–C branch and a separate resistor R in parallel. At ω0​=1/LC​, the series branch is purely resistive, so its peak current is Ip​=V0​/R. Its stored energies are
UL​=21​LIp2​sin2ω0​t,UC​=21​LIp2​cos2ω0​t.
(11)
Their sum is constant in sinusoidal steady state, with value Umax​=LV02​/(2R2). Each resistor dissipates average power V02​/(2R), including the resistor directly across the source. Over one cycle,
Wdiss​=ω0​2π​RV02​​,Wdiss​Umax​​=4πRω0​L​=4πR1​CL​​.​
(12)
Omitting the parallel resistor would double this ratio. The expression concerns the driven steady state, not a switch-on transient.
