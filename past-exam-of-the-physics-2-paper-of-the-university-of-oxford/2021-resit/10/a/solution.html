For a circular current loop, the Biot-Savart law is
dB=4πμ0​I​s3dℓ×s​,
(51)
where s points from a current element to the field point. Choose the loop in the yz plane, with observation point xx. A parametrization consistent with positive axial magnetic field is
r′(φ)=(0,acosφ,asinφ),dℓ=(0,−asinφ,acosφ)dφ,s=(x,−acosφ,−asinφ).
(52)
Then s=a2+x2​ and
dℓ×s=(a2,axcosφ,axsinφ)dφ.
(53)
The transverse contributions cancel around the circle, leaving the on-axis magnetic field of a circular current loop
B(x)=2(a2+x2)3/2μ0​Ia2​x.​
(54)
The right-hand rule fixes its direction; reversing the electric current reverses the magnetic field. At the centre this becomes μ0​I/(2a), and far away it decays as ∣x∣−3, as expected for a magnetic dipole.
