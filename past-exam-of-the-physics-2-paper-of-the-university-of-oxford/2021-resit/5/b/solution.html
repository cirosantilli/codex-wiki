For a sinusoidal plane electromagnetic wave in vacuum, write the maximum electric field as E0​. The magnetic field maximum is B0​=E0​/c, so the cycle-averaged Poynting vector magnitude, or light intensity, is
I=μ0​E0​B0​​⟨cos2ωt⟩=21​ϵ0​cE02​,E0​=ϵ0​c2I​​.​
(21)
Each photon carries energy hf, where h is the Planck constant. Therefore the photon number flux density, namely the number per unit normal area per unit time, is
ΦN​=hfI​=2hfϵ0​cE02​​.​
(22)
Multiplying by an illuminated normal area gives the photon arrival rate. Each absorbed photon transfers momentum hf/c at normal incidence, giving the radiation pressure on a perfectly absorbing surface
p=cI​=21​ϵ0​E02​.​
(23)
These are time-averaged quantities. At oblique angle of incidence θ, the normal radiation pressure is Icos2θ/c; the simple displayed expression assumes normal incidence.
