A distant satellite supplies an approximately collimated incident beam. A concave spherical mirror of radius of curvature Rc​=1.5m has paraxial focal length fm​=Rc​/2. Put the receiving feed at the principal focus, on the optical axis in front of the dish:
fm​=0.750m from the dish vertex.​
(29)
The radius of curvature relation is a paraxial approximation. A practical dish is usually a parabolic reflector, which focuses an on-axis collimated beam without spherical aberration; a literal spherical dish has spherical aberration, so an exact optimum over its full aperture would need a specified focusing criterion.
