A feature of transverse size s=0.03m at height H=2.0×105m subtends
Δθ≃Hs​=1.5×10−7rad.
(37)
Choose a representative visible wavelength λ=550nm. For an unobstructed circular mirror, the first Airy disk minimum gives ΔθR​=1.22λ/D, so
D≃s1.22λH​=4.47m(about a 4–5 m mirror).​
(38)
Using the single-slit scaling alone would give D∼λH/s=3.67m; it gives the same metre-scale estimate but omits the circular-aperture factor. Choosing visible wavelengths between 400 and 700nm changes the circular-aperture estimate to roughly 3.3–5.7m. These are diffraction-only estimates, not a claim that resolving one stroke guarantees reading arbitrary text with limited contrast or noise.
