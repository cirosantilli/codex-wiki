Each long wire lies in the other's magnetic field, B=μ0​I/(2πd). The magnetic Lorentz force per unit length has magnitude
F′=2πdμ0​I2​,opposite currents repel.​
(26)
For the two-wire circuit's self-inductance, a nonzero wire radius is essential. Introduce a radius aw​≪d. Between the wires their magnetic fields add. The external magnetic flux linkage per unit length through the connecting ribbon is, to leading order in aw​/d,
Φ′=∫aw​d−aw​​2πμ0​I​(x1​+d−x1​)dx=πμ0​I​lnaw​d−aw​​.
(27)
It follows that the leading thin-wire loop self-inductance per unit length is
Lext′​≃πμ0​​lnaw​d​.​
(28)
It is the loop value for the pair, not the contribution of just one conductor. Corrections from the wire interior depend on the current distribution: for nonmagnetic round wires with uniform direct electric current, the pair contributes another μ0​/(4π); for a surface current the internal contribution is zero. The leading logarithm is common to both models.
