This is an RC low-pass filter: at small frequency the capacitor has large electrical impedance and the output nearly equals the input; at large frequency it increasingly shunts the signal to ground. The amplitude then falls as 1/f, and the phase shift approaches −90∘.

A finite load changes the transfer function and must be included if its electrical impedance is comparable with the circuit's output electrical impedance.
