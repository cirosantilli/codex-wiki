The two wavelengths have independent interference patterns. Their interference fringe spacings are
βg​=100×10−6546×10−9×0.5​=2.730mm,βb​=100×10−6437×10−9×0.5​=2.185mm.​
(43)
Different optical frequencies do not maintain a fixed mutual phase over the detector's integration time, so their intensities add. For narrow equal slits, suppressing the slowly varying diffraction envelopes, one may write
I(y)=Ig,max​cos2(βg​πy​)+Ib,max​cos2(βb​πy​).
(44)
The relative line strengths are not supplied; the figure illustrates equal peak intensities rather than inferring a quantitative brightness ratio. At y=0 both colours are bright, producing a blue-green mixture. Away from the centre the green and blue interference fringes progressively separate, creating alternating colour and brightness variations; places where one line is dark can still be bright in the other. Neither two-line mixing nor its central maximum is generally white.
Across −15mm≤y≤15mm, the green maxima occur at y=2.730mmm for m=−5,…,5, and the blue maxima at y=2.185nmm for n=−6,…,6. Near y=±10.92mm, the fourth green and fifth blue maxima nearly coincide: 4βg​=10.920mm and 5βb​=10.925mm. The 5μm mismatch shows that these are near coincidences, not exact ones for the given wavelengths. The plotted central interval includes all these maxima.
