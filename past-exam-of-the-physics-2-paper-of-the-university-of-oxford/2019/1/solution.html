Take an electric current I1​ in the long solenoid. Away from its ends, Ampère's circuital law gives the approximately uniform magnetic field
B1​=μ0​LN1​​I1​.
(1)
Here μ0​ is the vacuum permeability; an air core is understood. Each turn of the short surrounding coil encloses the magnetic flux B1​πR2. Thus its magnetic flux linkage is Λ2​=N2​B1​πR2, and the mutual inductance is
M=I1​Λ2​​=μ0​LN1​N2​πR2​.​
(2)
The short coil lies in the nearly uniform central magnetic field; its own axial length therefore does not enter. With consistent winding orientations, Faraday's law of induction gives E2​=−MdI1​/dt. Reversing a winding reverses this signed coupling, while the stated magnitude is unchanged.
