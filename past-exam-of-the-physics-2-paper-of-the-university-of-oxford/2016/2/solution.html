The Lorentz force is
F=q(E+v×B)​.
(3)
For E=0, the magnetic part is perpendicular to the velocity, so its instantaneous power F⋅v vanishes. The speed and kinetic energy therefore remain constant. Decompose the initial velocity into v∥​ along the uniform magnetic field and v⊥​ perpendicular to it. The parallel component is unchanged, while the perpendicular component rotates uniformly.
For a nonrelativistic particle with qB=0, the resulting helical motion has
rL​=∣q∣Bmv⊥​​,ωc​=m∣q∣B​,T=∣q∣B2πm​,h=v∥​T​.
(4)
Here rL​ is the Larmor radius, ωc​ the cyclotron frequency in angular units, and h the axial advance per turn. Opposite signs of electric charge give opposite senses of rotation. Pure perpendicular motion gives a circle; pure parallel motion gives a straight line. If q=0 or B=0, there is no magnetic deflection. At relativistic speeds, replace m in the radius and period by γm; γ stays constant because the magnetic force does no work.
