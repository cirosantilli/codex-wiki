Neglect fringing field near the edges of the parallel-plate capacitor. Put the plates at z=0,d, with prescribed electric potentials VA​,VB​. Translational symmetry parallel to a plane makes the electrostatic potential independent of the coordinates parallel to the plates. Thus Laplace's equation reduces to d2V/dz2=0. Its general solution is V=A+Bz, and the two boundary conditions determine both constants:
V(z)=VA​+dVB​−VA​​z,E=dVA​−VB​​z^.​
(4)
The electric field is uniform and points toward decreasing electric potential. No plate voltages are specified, so a numerical electric potential cannot be fixed; choosing one plate as the reference only fixes the arbitrary additive constant.
