For the convex vacuum void, use internal refractive index 1, external refractive index n, and the original α>0 in the immersed thin lens formula:
fvoid​=2α(1−n)n​=−2α(n−1)n​.​
(69)
A concave glass thin lens of the same curvature magnitude in vacuum has αconcave​=−α and therefore
fconcave​=−2α(n−1)1​,fvoid​=nfconcave​.​
(70)
Both elements diverge, but their physical focal lengths differ by a factor n for n>1. For example, n=1.5 makes the void's focal length magnitude 1.5 times that of the concave glass element. Their reduced optical powers noutside​/f are equal; physical focal length also depends on the surrounding refractive index.
