The electric dipole moment is p=−qdz^. The circle a=2​h is the zero of the leading normal electric field and hence of the induced surface charge density. Inside it, induced surface charge density is negative; outside it, positive. The tangential electric field is zero everywhere on the grounded electrical conductor, so the leading full electric field vanishes on that circle. The corresponding dipole-induced charge on a grounded plane integrates to zero over the whole plane: the negative central region is balanced by the positive exterior.
