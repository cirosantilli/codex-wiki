The positive lamp power comes from extra work by the primary current source. The secondary electric current creates a changing magnetic flux through the primary, so maintaining the same prescribed primary electric current requires an additional terminal voltage. This is the reflected impedance of the secondary load. The self-inductance and mutual inductance terms exchange stored magnetic energy, while the lamp dissipates it.
