Consequently the relative wave amplitude is
∣H∣=(1−ω2LC)2+(ωRC)2​1​.​
(45)
For input V0​cosωt, the output is V0​∣H∣cos(ωt+ϕ), where the continuous phase shift is
ϕ=−atan2(ωRC,1−ω2LC).​
(46)
For positive frequency and R>0, this phase shift goes from 0 towards −π, passing through −π/2 at ω0​. A one-argument arctangent without a quadrant correction would wrongly predict a positive phase shift above ω0​. The capacitor follows a slowly varying input voltage, while its output falls as 1/(ω2LC) at high frequency.
