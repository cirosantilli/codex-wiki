For a uniformly illuminated single slit of width b, summing the wave amplitudes across the slit gives the Fraunhofer diffraction profile
I(θ)=I(0)(βsinβ​)2,β=λπbsinθ​.
(26)
The first minimum occurs at sinθ=λ/b. The Rayleigh criterion places one source's central maximum at the other source's first minimum; hence the slit gives Δθ≃λ/b. A circular aperture instead produces an Airy disk, whose first minimum is at 1.22λ/D. For the camera's circular lens,
Δθ=D1.22λ​=4.88×10−4rad=0.0280∘≃101 arcseconds.​
(27)
The factor 1.22 belongs to circular-aperture diffraction, rather than the single-slit profile.
