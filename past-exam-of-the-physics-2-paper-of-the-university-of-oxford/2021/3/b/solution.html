For a plane electromagnetic wave with linear polarization, write E=E0​xcos(kz−ωt) and H=E0​y​cos(kz−ωt)/(μ0​c). The Poynting vector is instantaneous energy flux:
S=μ0​cE02​​cos2(kz−ωt)z.
(13)
The light intensity is its cycle average, not its peak:
I=⟨∣S∣⟩=2μ0​cE02​​=21​ϵ0​cE02​.​
(14)
An incident electromagnetic wave transports longitudinal momentum E/c per energy E. Perfect reflection back along the incident direction reverses that momentum, transferring 2E/c to the mirror. Dividing by illuminated area and time gives the mean radiation pressure
p=c2I​=c2⟨∣S∣⟩​.​
(15)
Here S denotes the incident travelling wave. The average net Poynting vector of the incident-plus-reflected standing wave is zero, although its radiation pressure is nonzero.
