Use nonrelativistic dynamics, take B>0 along +y, and neglect radiation. The Lorentz force qv×B initially points along +z. It does no work, so the speed stays v and the trajectory inside the field is circular, with
r=qBmv​,Ω=mqB​.
(16)
Starting from the entry point, the orbit can be parametrized as x=rsinθ, z=r(1−cosθ), where θ=Ωt. Its largest possible x before turning back is r. Therefore it enters the strict region x>b exactly when
r>b,B<qbmv​​.
(17)
For that case, the first boundary crossing satisfies sinθb​=b/r. The velocity components are vx​=vcosθb​ and vz​=vsinθb​, so the outgoing angle above the +x direction is
θb​=sin−1(mvqBb​),0≤θb​<2π​​.
(18)
The zero-field limit is straight motion. At r=b the particle only touches the far boundary tangentially and does not enter x>b; for r<b it turns around before reaching that boundary and eventually returns through the entrance side. The equality case must therefore not be included in a crossing condition.
