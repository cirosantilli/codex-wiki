In the chosen phasor convention, output phase minus input phase is
ϕ(ω)=−arctan[3ωRC−(ωRC)−1​],ω>0.​
(55)
The real part of the denominator is positive, so this principal arctangent is continuous throughout the positive-frequency range. Below ω0​, the output leads the input, approaching +π/2 at low frequency. Above ω0​, it lags, approaching −π/2 at high frequency. At the maximum,
ϕ(ω0​)=0.​
(56)
The input and output are in phase there, with the positive one-third voltage ratio found above.
