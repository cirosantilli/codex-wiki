For the uniform space charge in a grounded spherical cavity, grounding sets the metal's electric potential to zero. With no external sources and zero electric potential at infinity, the exterior electric potential is also zero by the uniqueness theorem for the electrostatic potential. The original shell electric charge is no longer fixed: electric charge can flow to or from earth.
