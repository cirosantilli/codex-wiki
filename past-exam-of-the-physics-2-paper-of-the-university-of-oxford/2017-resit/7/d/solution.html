For a steady filamentary electric current I, the Biot-Savart law is
B(r)=4πμ0​​∫∣r−r′∣3Idℓ′×(r−r′)​.​
(27)
Here r is the observation position, r′ the source position, dℓ′ points in the direction of positive electric current, and μ0​ is the vacuum permeability. A circular loop has centre magnetic field μ0​I/(2r): the cross product has magnitude rdℓ′ and a constant axial direction, and ∫dℓ′=2πr.
Introduce the constant signed angular velocity Ω about the positive axis. A ring's electric charge dq=2πσrdr crosses any radial cut once per rotation, so its electric current is dI=Ωdq/(2π)=σΩrdr. Adding loop contributions yields the magnetic field of a rotating charged annulus:
B(0)=x^∫ab​2rμ0​​σΩrdr,B(0)=2μ0​σΩ(b−a)​x^=2π(a+b)μ0​QΩ​x^.​
(28)
The direction follows the right-hand rule for positive electric charge; reversing either Q or Ω reverses the magnetic field. The source supplies no rotation rate, so the answer necessarily retains Ω.
