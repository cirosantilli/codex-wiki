Transmitted and untransmitted alleles under multiplicative penetrance

 Home Biology Genetics Genetic association Transmission disequilibrium test Family pseudo-control genotype
OurBigBook.com Words: 94
Under random mating and Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, label each parental allele by whether it is transmitted. Let (A,B) and (C,D) be the transmitted/untransmitted pairs of two parents. Before ascertainment all four draws are independent with population frequencies π. If offspring disease weights the transmitted pair by ψA​ψC​, then
P(A=a,B=b,C=c,D=d∣offspring disease)=πa∗​πc∗​πb​πd​.
(3)
The transmitted pair is an affected-case genotype and the untransmitted pair a population family pseudo-control genotype under this homogeneous model. For unordered genotypes, compatible labelled transmissions must be summed. Conditioning on parental genotypes instead removes nuisance population frequencies and gives the usual transmission disequilibrium test.
