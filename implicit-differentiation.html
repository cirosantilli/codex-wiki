Implicit differentiation

 ... Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Differentiable map Inverse function theorem Implicit function theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 2
Implicit differentiation differentiates an identity F(x,y(x))=0 along a differentiable branch and uses the chain rule to solve for its derivative:
Fx​+Fy​y′=0,y′=−Fy​Fx​​(Fy​=0).
(116)
The partial derivatives Fx​,Fy​ treat x,y as independent coordinates before evaluating on the branch. The implicit function theorem justifies the branch's local existence and regularity under its hypotheses; differentiating alone does not establish either. For the unit circle at (3/5,4/5), this calculation yields the tangent line y−4/5=−(3/4)(x−3/5).
