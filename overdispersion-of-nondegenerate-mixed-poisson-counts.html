Overdispersion of nondegenerate mixed Poisson counts (VarN=EL+VarL)

If N∣L has Poisson mean L, conditional expectation and the law of total variance give the display. A positive variance of the random mean prevents the unconditional count from being Poisson. For Poisson parents of density λ and offspring mean μ, the count in A has excess variance λμ2∫κ(A−z)2dz, which is positive for a bounded positive-area set and a nontrivial model.
