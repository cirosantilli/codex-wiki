Electrical conductivity (σ)

 ... Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Electrical resistance and conductance Electrical resistance Ohm's law
OurBigBook.com Words: 534 Articles: 9
Electrical electrical conductivity relates current density to the local electric field in the rest frame of an isotropic ohmic medium: J=σE. It is a bulk material coefficient, distinct from a sheet surface electrical conductivity. A moving conductor instead uses the moving-conductor Ohm law.
