Seismic moment tensor (fi​=−∂j​[Mij​(t)δ(x−y)])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Elastic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 753 Articles: 17
A localized zero-resultant seismic moment tensor represents the leading force-dipole part of an elastic wave source through the displayed equivalent body force. For a prescribed displacement jump bi​ across a surface with normal nj​, its moment density is cijkl​bk​nl​, where c is the elastic stiffness tensor. Spatial integration gives the point-source moment when the source is small compared with the wavelengths.
