OurBigBook.com Words: 51
The human gene encoding a transcription factor implicated in speech and language development through rare pathogenic variants. Related genes in other vertebrates provide mechanistic models, but the human phenotype is not equivalent to any single animal motor task. A gene-to-phenotype relationship does not by itself isolate one affected brain structure.[ref]
