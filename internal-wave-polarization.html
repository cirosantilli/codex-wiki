Internal-wave polarization

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave Internal-wave phase and group velocity
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 1
For a propagating, non-cutoff two-dimensional internal gravity wave proportional to ei(kx+mz−ωt), incompressibility gives U=−mW/k. Thus the oscillatory velocity field is perpendicular to the wavevector and parallel to the group velocity, whereas the phase velocity is parallel to the wavevector.
