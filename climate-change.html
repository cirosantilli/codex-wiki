Climate change

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 44
Climate change is a persistent alteration of the statistics of a planet's climate. External radiative forcing, internal variability and climate feedbacks all matter, and the timescale must be specified. A climate model connects mechanisms to observable changes; the radiative-convective climate column separates vertical heat transfer from whole-planet conservation of energy, while the Hasselmann stochastic climate model explains how fast weather can drive slow thermal variability.
