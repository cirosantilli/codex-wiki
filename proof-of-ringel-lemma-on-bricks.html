Proof of Ringel lemma on bricks

 ... Module theory Module (mathematics) Module homomorphism Endomorphism ring Brick module Ringel lemma on bricks
OurBigBook.com Words: 117
The Fitting lemma supplies a nonzero nilpotent endomorphism f of a non-brick indecomposable X. Minimize its nonzero rank; nilpotence and minimality give f2=0. Set I=imf⊂K=kerf=⨁j​Kj​. Choose a nonzero component u:I→Kj​. The square-zero endomorphism Xf​Iu​Kj​↪X has rank at least dimI, so u is injective.
If Ext1(I,Kj​)=0, the projection K→Kj​ extends to X→Kj​ by the long exact sequence of Ext groups, giving a module retraction and contradicting indecomposability. Thus this extension group is nonzero. Since path algebras are hereditary, u induces a surjection Ext1(Kj​,Kj​)↠Ext1(I,Kj​). Hence Kj​ is a proper indecomposable submodule with self-extensions. Iterate until a brick module is reached; dimensions strictly decrease.
This minimal-rank argument is given in section 2 of William Crawley-Boevey's quiver lectures.
