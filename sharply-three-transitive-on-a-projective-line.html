Sharply three-transitive on a projective line (PGL2​(F) acts regularly on ordered distinct triples of P1(F))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory General linear group over a finite field Projective general linear group action on the projective line
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For any field F, a projective linear group element is uniquely determined by its images of three distinct points of the projective line, and any ordered distinct target triple is attainable. Given representatives vz​,vx​ forming a basis and vy​=Avz​+Bvx​, the matrix with columns Avz​,Bvx​ sends (0,1,∞) to (x,y,z). Distinctness forces A,B=0. The matrices realizing one projective map differ by a nonzero scalar; over Fp​ there are p−1 such matrices.
