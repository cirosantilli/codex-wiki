Kähler covariant derivative of a superpotential (Di​W=∂i​W+Ki​W)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supergravity Supergravity F-term potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The Kähler covariant derivative of a superpotential transforms by the same holomorphic factor as the superpotential under a Kähler transformation. This follows by differentiating e−fW and cancelling the extra derivative of f against the shift of Ki​.
