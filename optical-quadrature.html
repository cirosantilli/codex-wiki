Optical quadrature (Xϑ​=(ae−iϑ+a†eiϑ)/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 307 Articles: 5
A phase-selected Hermitian field observable. With annihilation operator a and [a,a†]=1, define X1​=(a+a†)/2 and X2​=(a−a†)/(2i). Then [X1​,X2​]=i/2 and the Fock vacuum has variance 1/4 in either quadrature. This dimensionless convention gives Xϑ​=X1​cosϑ+X2​sinϑ. Balanced homodyne detection selects ϑ through the reference wave phase.
