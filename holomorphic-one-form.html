Holomorphic one-form (ω∈H0(X,KX​))

 ... Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Canonical bundle Holomorphic differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
A section ω of the canonical bundle of a Riemann surface, locally ω=ϕ(z)dz with holomorphic ϕ. A nonzero form on a compact genus-g surface has total zero order 2g−2. Its integral supplies flat coordinates away from zeros.
