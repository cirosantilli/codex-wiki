Color charge

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 347 Articles: 6
Color charge is the gauge group charge of Quantum chromodynamics, associated with its SU(3) symmetry. A quark transforms in the fundamental three-dimensional representation, an antiquark in its conjugate, and a lepton is a singlet. The word color is a label for this charge, unrelated to visible light.
