Right-inverse characterization of the axiom of choice

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Function Inverse function Right inverse
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
The assertion that every surjective function has a right inverse is equivalent to the axiom of choice. Choice selects one member of each nonempty fiber of a function, giving a right inverse. Conversely, for a family (Xi​)i∈I​ of nonempty sets, the projection from the disjoint union {(i,x):i∈I, x∈Xi​} onto I is surjective. A right inverse selects one element from each Xi​, giving a choice function.
