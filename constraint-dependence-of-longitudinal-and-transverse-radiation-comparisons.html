Constraint dependence of longitudinal and transverse radiation comparisons (P⊥​/P∥​=γ−2 at fixed a, γ2 at fixed F)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic radiation Larmor formula Liénard radiated-power formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For one particle at a fixed Lorentz factor, the Liénard radiated-power formula gives P∥​=Cq​γ6a∥2​ and P⊥​=Cq​γ4a⊥2​, where Cq​=q2/(6πϵ0​c3). Consequently equal coordinate acceleration magnitudes make transverse motion radiate less by γ2. Equal applied laboratory force magnitudes instead give a∥​=F/(mγ3) and a⊥​=F/(mγ), so P⊥​/P∥​=γ2. These opposite orderings concern distinct constraints and are consistent. An energy budget without a time, length, force, or acceleration constraint cannot by itself select a unique minimum-loss accelerator design.
