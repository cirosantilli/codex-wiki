Periodic continued fraction of a quadratic irrational

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Continued fraction
OurBigBook.com Words: 82 Articles: 4
The simple continued fraction of a quadratic irrational is eventually periodic. For a nonsquare positive integer D, the continued fraction of D​ is periodic after its integer part.
