Solar-cell fill factor (FF=Pmax​/(Voc​Isc​))

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Photon Photon absorption Photovoltaic effect Solar cell
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The solar-cell fill factor compares the maximum delivered electrical power with the rectangle formed by the open-circuit electric potential difference and short-circuit electric current. It measures the shape of the illuminated current-voltage characteristic. The conversion efficiency is η=Voc​Isc​FF/Pin​.
