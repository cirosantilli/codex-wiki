OurBigBook.com Words: 46
For a compact H-hull and a Borel subset of the intrinsic boundary of a simply connected domain, hydrodynamic normalization at infinity and the Poisson kernel for the upper half-plane give this limit as y→∞ with x/y→0. After multiplying the kernel by πy, it tends pointwise to one and is eventually uniformly bounded. Dominated convergence proves the finite-measure case and Fatou's lemma proves the infinite-measure case.
