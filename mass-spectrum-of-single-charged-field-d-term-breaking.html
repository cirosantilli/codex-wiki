Mass spectrum of single charged-field D-term breaking (mϕ2​=gqξ,mψ​=mλ​=mA​=0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 98
For canonical kinetic terms, vanishing superpotential, g>0 and qξ>0, the D-term scalar potential V=21​(ξ+gq∣ϕ∣2)2 has its minimum at ϕ=0. Both real scalar modes have squared mass gqξ, while the chiral Weyl fermion, photon and gaugino are massless; the gaugino is the goldstino. The scalar-to-fermion squared-mass splitting is gqξ. On the other branch qξ<0, v2=−ξ/(gq) cancels D and preserves supersymmetry; the Higgsed vector, remaining real scalar and Dirac fermion have common squared mass 2g2q2v2. A single nonzero-charge chiral fermion has a gauge anomaly, so a quantum realization requires an anomaly-cancelling completion; these are the stated classical vacuum spectra.
