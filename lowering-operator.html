Lowering operator

 ... Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Highest-weight representation Ladder operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A lowering operator is a negative-root vector. Acting on a weight vector of weight μ either gives zero or a weight vector of weight μ−α.
