Angular dipole (ℓ=1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Spherical harmonic
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
The angular dipole is the degree-one spherical harmonic component. A real angular dipole is a function d⋅n for a fixed vector d. Direction-dependent observer velocity and gravitational endpoint terms can therefore be discarded from higher-multipole calculations when they have this form.
