Latent heat from a vapor-pressure slope (Lm​≃−Rdln(p/p∗​)/d(1/T))

Within the Ideal-vapor Clausius-Clapeyron approximation, a local slope of the natural logarithm of saturation pressure against inverse temperature estimates the molar latent heat:
Lm​(T)≃−Rd(1/T)dln(p/p∗​)​.
(196)
In the stipulated vapor-pressure calculation, the slope is −4811.16K, which recovers 40.0kJmol−1. For variable Lm​(T), a two-point estimate gives an average weighted by T−2, rather than the latent heat at an arbitrary midpoint. Differentiating noisy pressure measurements amplifies their errors. Outside the ideal-vapor regime, use independently determined molar volumes in Lm​=TΔvm​dp/dT; a straight-looking graph alone cannot establish that the gas is ideal.
