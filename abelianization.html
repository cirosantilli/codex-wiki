Abelianization (Gab)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Commutator subgroup
OurBigBook.com Words: 197 Articles: 3
The abelianization of a group is the quotient Gab=G/G′ by its commutator subgroup. It is the largest abelian quotient of G.
