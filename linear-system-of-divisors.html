Linear system of divisors (P(V))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Weil divisor
OurBigBook.com Words: 327 Articles: 5
A linear system of divisors is a projective family of effective Weil divisors linearly equivalent to a fixed divisor. A complete linear system of a divisor contains every effective representative of that class; a projective subspace gives a subsystem. For a Cartier divisor, the family is obtained from global sections of its associated line bundle. The algebraic curve case recovers the usual moving family of effective divisors.
