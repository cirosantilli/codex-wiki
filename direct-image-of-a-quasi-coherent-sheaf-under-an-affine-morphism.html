Direct image of a quasi-coherent sheaf under an affine morphism

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Direct image sheaf
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
For an affine morphism f:X→Y, the direct image of every quasi-coherent sheaf on X is quasi-coherent. On affine charts this is the fact that restriction of scalars turns the module sheaf M into the module sheaf associated with the same underlying module over the target ring.
