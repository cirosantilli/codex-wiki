Absolute irreducibility of a group representation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Irreducible representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
A group representation over a field is absolutely irreducible if it remains irreducible after every field extension. For a finite-dimensional representation it is enough to check an algebraic closure. Irreducibility over the original field alone is weaker: the two-dimensional real rotation representation of the cyclic group of order three splits into two lines over the complex numbers.
