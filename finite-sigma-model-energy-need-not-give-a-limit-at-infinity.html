Finite sigma-model energy need not give a limit at infinity

For arbitrary maps ϕ:R2→S2, integrability of the Dirichlet energy does not imply continuous extension to the one-point compactification. For large r, take
ϕ(r,ϑ)=(sina(r),0,cosa(r)),a(r)=loglogr,
(2)
and interpolate smoothly to a constant on a bounded disk. Its exterior energy is
21​∫∣x∣≥R​∣∇ϕ∣2d2x=π∫R∞​rlog2rdr​=logRπ​<∞.
(3)
The field has no limit because a(r) keeps winding. A continuous extension at the point at infinity instead requires ϕ(x)→ϕ∞​ uniformly outside sufficiently large disks. This fixed-limit boundary condition is standard for a sigma-model lump, but is extra information beyond finite energy for arbitrary fields.
