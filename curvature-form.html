Curvature form (F(A))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 442 Articles: 10
The curvature form of a connection on a vector bundle is F=dA+A∧A in a local frame. A principal connection has the corresponding Lie algebra-valued form F=dA+21​[A∧A]. It measures the failure of horizontal directions to be integrable.
The curvature is F(A)=dA2​ and in a local frame satisfies F(A)=dA+A∧A.
