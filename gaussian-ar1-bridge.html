For a stationary autoregressive process of order one with mean μ, autoregressive coefficient ϕ and innovation variance σ2, an interior observation conditional on its neighbors has
Xt​∣Xt−1​,Xt+1​∼N(μ+1+ϕ2ϕ{Xt−1​+Xt+1​−2μ}​,1+ϕ2σ2​).
(8)
The Markov property makes the same law valid when all other observations are also conditioned on.
