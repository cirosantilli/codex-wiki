Restricted maximum likelihood

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Normal linear model
OurBigBook.com Words: 71 Articles: 1
Restricted maximum likelihood estimates covariance matrix parameters from linear combinations of the data whose distribution does not involve the fixed mean coefficients. It differs from a restricted maximum-likelihood estimator obtained by maximizing an ordinary likelihood function under a null hypothesis.
