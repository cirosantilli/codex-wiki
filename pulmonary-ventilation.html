Pulmonary ventilation

 Home Biology Physiology
OurBigBook.com Words: 328 Articles: 14
Movement of air into and out of the lungs. Changes in breathing can influence cardiac timing and can accompany emotion or nonemotional physical demands. Breathing must therefore be measured or controlled when interpreting appropriate psychophysiology recordings.
