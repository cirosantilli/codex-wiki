Integral membrane protein

 Home Biology Biological cell Cell membrane Membrane protein
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 2
An integral membrane protein is firmly incorporated into a membrane, commonly through contact with the hydrophobic interior of the lipid bilayer. Extraction behavior is an operational distinction: it resists treatments that remove many peripheral membrane proteins. Transmembrane proteins are one class; integral does not mean spanning.[ref]
