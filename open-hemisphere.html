Open hemisphere ({x∈Sd:u⋅x>0})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Sphere Hemisphere
OurBigBook.com Words: 166 Articles: 2
For a nonzero vector u, the open hemisphere of Sd with direction u is {x∈Sd:u⋅x>0}. Its boundary is excluded, so opposite open hemispheres are disjoint.
