Positive time change of a smooth flow (F=fF, f>0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Smooth flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
Write ρ=1/f and t=∫0s​ρ(ϕr​x)dr. For an Anosov stable bundle, the new stable directions are v+λs​(x,v)F(x), where λs​=ρ(x)−1∫0∞​dρϕr​x​(Dϕr​v)dr. Exponential contraction makes this integral uniformly convergent. Differentiating the clock equation shows that its flow-direction coefficient after time t is ρ(ϕs​x)−1∫s∞​dρ(Dϕr​v)dr, which decays exponentially and has the same graph form at the new point. Negative-time integrals give the unstable graph. Compactness and positivity of f compare old and new times uniformly, preserving the Anosov flow property.
