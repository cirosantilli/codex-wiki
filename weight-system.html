Weight system

 ... Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Highest-weight representation Weight vector Weight space Weight-space decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 1
The weight system of a finite-dimensional Lie algebra representation is its collection of weights with their weight multiplicities. Restriction to a Lie subalgebra projects each weight to the smaller Cartan subalgebra, retaining and combining weight multiplicities. Grouping the resulting weights into irreducible weight systems computes the branching rule.
