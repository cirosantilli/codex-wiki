A reflecting bottom beneath a jump in buoyancy frequency creates a standing field from repeated transmitted and reflected internal gravity waves. For ray angles θ1​=π/3 and θ2​=π/6, the downward particle-displacement amplitude has magnitude 3​∣ηi​∣/[1+8sin2(m2​H)]1/2. It reaches 3​∣ηi​∣ when m2​H is an integer multiple of π. The upper reflected wave has the same amplitude magnitude as the incident wave because the lossless closed cavity admits no net downward energy flux.
