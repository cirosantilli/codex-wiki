Infection

 Home Biology Medicine
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
An infection involves invasion and multiplication of an infectious agent in a host. Bacteria are one possible cause; an agent capable of causing disease is a pathogen. The National Cancer Institute defines infection through invasion and growth in the body. [ref].
In a SIR model, acquiring infection moves a member of the susceptible compartment into the infectious compartment. That immediate transition is a model assumption: the SEIR model instead includes an exposed stage before infectiousness. Specify these states when translating a biological process into compartmental models.
