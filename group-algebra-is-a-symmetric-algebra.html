Group algebra is a symmetric algebra

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Modular representation theory Symmetric algebra (Frobenius algebra)
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 4
For a finite group G, the coefficient of the identity in xy defines a nondegenerate symmetric associative bilinear form on kG. Hence kG≅(kG)∗ as bimodules and the group algebra is a symmetric algebra.
