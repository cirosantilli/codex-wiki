XY exchange interaction (E=(X⊗X+Y⊗Y)/2)

The isotropic XY exchange interaction between two qubits is E=(X⊗X+Y⊗Y)/2. Its action is
E∣00⟩=E∣11⟩=0,E∣01⟩=∣10⟩,E∣10⟩=∣01⟩.
(81)
Indeed, X∣0⟩=∣1⟩, X∣1⟩=∣0⟩, Y∣0⟩=i∣1⟩, and Y∣1⟩=−i∣0⟩. The two terms cancel on equal bits and add on unequal bits. Thus a network Hamiltonian operator H=∑a,b​Kab​Eab​ preserves excitation number; in the single-excitation subspace, it is the weighted adjacency matrix, moving an excitation from a to b with hopping coefficient Kab​.
