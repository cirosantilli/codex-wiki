Dedekind zeta function as a permutation Artin L-function (ζK​(s)=LQ​(C[Hom(K,E)],s))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Dedekind zeta function
OurBigBook.com Words: 105
Let E be a finite normal closure of the number field K. The permutation representation of its Galois group on the embeddings of K into E has one trivial constituent, because the action is transitive. At a prime, the inertia group invariant basis consists of sums over inertia orbits of embeddings; Frobenius automorphism permutes them in cycles of lengths equal to the residue degrees of the primes of K. A cycle of length f contributes det(1−Tρ(Frob))=1−Tf. Hence the local factors equal those of the Dedekind zeta function, including at ramified primes. If nontrivial irreducible Artin L-functions are holomorphic and nonzero at one, this factorization and the simple pole of the Riemann zeta function show that ζK​ has a simple pole there.
