Algebra for a monad ((A,a))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Monad
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 4
An algebra for a monad (T,η,μ) is an object A with a morphism a:TA→A satisfying aηA​=1A​ and aT(a)=aμA​.
