For an entire function f, use the complex logarithm L with argument in (0,2π). The unit-circle integral in the title uses the branch's one-sided boundary values at its crossing with the cut. On a keyhole contour, the outward upper bank has L=logx, and the inward lower bank has L=logx+2πi. Their combined integral of f(L−iπ) is −2πi∫ε1​f(x)dx. The inner circle contributes O(ε∣logε∣), which vanishes. The Cauchy integral theorem on the slit plane therefore gives the identity.
On z=eiθ, ∣L−iπ∣=∣θ−π∣≤π. The estimation lemma consequently gives

Subtracting the middle branch value iπ centres the weight and produces this bound. The contour crosses the cut only in the limiting boundary-value interpretation, so it is not a closed contour inside the logarithm's domain.
