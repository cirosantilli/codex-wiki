Neutral-hydrogen column-density distribution (f(N,z)=∂2N/(∂N∂z))

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Hydrogen atom Lyman-alpha line Lyman-alpha absorption
OurBigBook.com Words: 210 Articles: 2
The neutral-hydrogen column-density distribution counts Lyman-alpha absorption systems per unit column density and redshift. A count in logarithmic column bins is Nln(10)f(N,z). At low forest columns an approximate power law f∝N−β with β near 1.5 is useful, but the slope and normalization depend on column range and epoch. Lyman limit systems are optically thick at the hydrogen photoionization edge; damped Lyman-alpha systems have strong radiative damping wings. A distribution per absorption distance instead removes the geometrical path factor.
