Rotating saddle trap (∣Ω∣>ω)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Reference frame Rotating reference frame
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 1
A planar quadratic saddle mω2(x2−y2)/2 rotated at angular speed Ω becomes linearly confining for the displayed strict inequality. Its rotating-frame mode frequencies obey ϵ±2​=Ω2±ω2. Equality has a zero-frequency secular mode and is not robust bounded-motion stability. This is a rotating potential model; a rolling ball on an actual surface has additional constraints.
