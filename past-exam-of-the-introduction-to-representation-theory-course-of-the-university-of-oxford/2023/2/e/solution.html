Let ε denote the sign representation and take the explicit two-dimensional G-module
U=1⊕ε.
(25)
Its character is χU​=(2,0,2,2,0). The character of a tensor product of group representations is the pointwise product of their characters, because tr(M⊗N)=tr(M)tr(N). Consequently
χC⊗U​=(3,−1,−1,0,1)(2,0,2,2,0)=(6,0,−2,0,0)=χV​.
(26)
By Maschke's theorem both complex representations are semisimple representations, and characters distinguish finite-dimensional complex semisimple representations. Therefore
V≅C⊗(1⊕ε)≅C⊕(C⊗ε).​
(27)
This is the induction of the planar square representation to the symmetric group on four letters.
The dimension of the fixed-point subspace of a group action is the multiplicity of the trivial representation. Its character inner product here is
dimVG=⟨χV​,1⟩G​=246+3(−2)​=0.
(28)
Hence VG=0. Equivalently, Frobenius reciprocity identifies this multiplicity with ⟨χW​,1H​⟩H​=(2−2)/8=0.
