Informative censoring

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis Censoring (statistics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 1
Informative censoring occurs when censoring carries information about the future event time beyond the variables conditioned on in the analysis. It can invalidate ordinary Kaplan–Meier estimator and survival-model residual interpretations.
