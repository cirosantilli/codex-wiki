Subdivision arity (a)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Computer aided geometric design Subdivision surface Subdivision mask
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The subdivision arity is the integer factor a>1 by which a stationary refinement shrinks parameter-grid intervals: control index i has coordinate i/aℓ after ℓ levels. It is distinct from the mask width and from the number of neighbors in a stencil.
