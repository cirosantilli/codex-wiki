Causal Green function of an overdamped oscillator (G(t)=H(t)a−be−bt−e−at​,a>b>0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Damped harmonic oscillator
OurBigBook.com Words: 198 Articles: 1
For L=(Dt​+a)(Dt​+b) with a>b>0, H denotes the Heaviside step function. This Green function vanishes at negative time and satisfies LG=δ(t). Its positive-time part solves the homogeneous ordinary differential equation, is zero at the origin, and has right derivative one. Thus it is continuous with a unit derivative jump, which gives precisely the Dirac delta function in its second distributional derivative.
With the negative-exponential Fourier transform convention,
G(k)=(a+ik)(b+ik)1​=a−b1​(b+ik1​−a+ik1​).
(68)
Both poles lie in the upper half-plane. Contour integration closes above for positive time and below for negative time, giving the title expression. When the integral converges, convolution y(t)=∫−∞t​G(t−s)f(s)ds supplies the response with no freely added homogeneous transient growing toward negative infinity. The continuous limit a→b is G(t)=H(t)te−bt.
