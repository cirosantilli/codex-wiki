Uniform integrability of conditional expectations

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Convergence of random variables Uniform integrability
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For one integrable random variable Y, the family ZG​=E[Y∣G], over arbitrary sigma-algebras G, has uniform integrability. On A={∣ZG​∣>K}, the defining property of conditional expectation gives E[∣ZG​∣1A​]≤E[∣Y∣1A​], while P(A)≤E∣Y∣/K by the Markov inequality. The integrable random variable Y has uniformly small absolute integrals over events of sufficiently small probability.
