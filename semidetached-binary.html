Semidetached binary

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Binary star
OurBigBook.com Words: 44 Articles: 1
A semidetached binary has one component filling its Roche lobe while the other remains inside its own lobe. Algol binaries and most ordinary cataclysmic variables are examples.
