Radial magnetic induction scalar (P=B⋅x)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Resistive induction equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 124 Articles: 1
For solenoidal velocity and magnetic field, with constant magnetic diffusivity η, the resistive induction equation implies
∂t​P+u⋅∇P=B⋅∇Q+ηΔP,Q=u⋅x.
(20)
The product rule gives Dt​P=x⋅Dt​B+u⋅B and ΔP=x⋅ΔB+2∇⋅B. Consequently the radial velocity, through Q, is the source term for the radial magnetic field.
