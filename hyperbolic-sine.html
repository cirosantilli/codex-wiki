Hyperbolic sine (sinh)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Exponential function Hyperbolic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 100
The hyperbolic sine is the odd function
sinhx=2ex−e−x​.
(61)
Its derivative is coshx≥1, so it increases strictly from −∞ to ∞ and is a bijection of R onto itself. For example, sinh0=0, sinh1≈1.175201, and sinh(−1)≈−1.175201. It is the vertical coordinate on the unit hyperbola parametrized by (coshx,sinhx).
The Taylor series
sinhx=x+3!x3​+5!x5​+⋯
(62)
shows that sinhx≈x near zero. Its tangent line there is y=x; unlike the ordinary sine, the hyperbolic sine grows without bound. Its antiderivative is coshx+C. In the catenary y=acosh(x/a), the hyperbolic sine gives both the slope, through the derivative y′=sinh(x/a), and the arc length s(x)=asinh(x/a) from the lowest point for x≥0.
