Convex roof extension (F(ρ)=infρ=∑j​pj​πj​​∑j​pj​μ(πj​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Quantum state ensemble
OurBigBook.com Words: 161 Articles: 1
A convex roof extension extends a function μ on pure states to arbitrary density operators by minimizing its ensemble average over all pure state decompositions. It agrees with μ on pure states: every positive-weight state in a decomposition of a rank-one density operator has the same one-dimensional support. Concatenating nearly minimizing ensembles proves that F is a convex function. Moreover, every convex function that is no larger than μ on pure states is no larger than F, by applying convexity to each decomposition. Infima need not be attained; an arbitrarily small additive error suffices when F is finite.
