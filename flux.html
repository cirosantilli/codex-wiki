Flux

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Conservation law
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 2
A flux records transport of a quantity through a surface per unit time. A flux density j gives total transport rate ∫S​j⋅ndS, where n is the unit normal vector. A conservation law relates its divergence to the local change in the quantity's density.
