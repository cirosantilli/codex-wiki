Silsesquioxane ((RSiO3/2​)n​)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound Organosilicon compound
OurBigBook.com Words: 83 Articles: 3
A silicon–oxygen framework with ideal fully condensed composition (RSiO3/2​)n​. Each silicon has one organic substituent and three siloxane bridges. Incompletely condensed cages retain silanol groups.
