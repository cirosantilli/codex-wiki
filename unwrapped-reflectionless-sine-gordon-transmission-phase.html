Unwrapped reflectionless sine-Gordon transmission phase (δN​(0)=πN,δN​(∞)=π(N+1)/2)

For the transmitting product with factors cosh[(θ−iπj/N)/2]/cosh[(θ+iπj/N)/2] and prefactor (−1)N, the continuous phase on θ≥0 is πN−2∑j=1N−1​arctan[tanh(θ/2)tan(πj/(2N))]. Its derivative is −∑j​sin(πj/N)/[coshθ+cos(πj/N)]. A Riemann sum at fixed positive rapidity gives (2N/π)logtanh(θ/2) at leading order. The endpoints depend on the stated unwrapped branch; setting the high-energy value to zero silently changes the constant.
