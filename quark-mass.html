Quark mass (Lm​=−∑f​mf​qˉ​f​qf​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Quark
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
A quark mass is the coefficient of its Dirac mass term. This term couples left- and right-handed quarks, so nonzero quark masses explicitly break independent rotations of the two chiralities. For two equal masses, the vector flavor symmetry survives, while the nonsinglet axial current obeys the flavor-nonsinglet axial Ward identity with equal quark masses. In the renormalized theory the mass parameter depends on the subtraction scale and scheme, while measurable hadron masses are physical observables.
