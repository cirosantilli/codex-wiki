Adsorption chemical potential (μads​=kB​Tln[θ/(1−θ)]−kB​Tlnz)

 Home Chemistry Physical chemistry Adsorption Adsorption isotherm Langmuir adsorption model
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The chemical potential of an ideal single-occupancy lattice gas with occupied-site internal partition function z and surface coverage θ. The ratio θ/(1−θ) accounts for the entropy of distributing particles among occupied and vacant adsorption sites.
