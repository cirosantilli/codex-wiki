Large solitary pulse on a cylindrical film (Hmax​∼2κ(3c)2/3)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Lubrication theory Thin-film equation Thin liquid film on a vertical cylinder
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
For a travelling wave of large positive speed in the cylindrical film equation, the leading core is proportional to 1−cosx over an axial interval of length 2π. Edge coordinates X=(3c)1/3(x−xe​) reduce the transition equation to H3HXXX​=H−1. Its real positive exponent gives a monotone trailing approach and its complex stable exponents give damped capillary waves ahead, with physical wavelength 4πa/(35/6c1/3). Matching the two quadratic edge expansions selects the uniform film thickness; this is a matched asymptotic expansion, valid while the pulse itself remains thin relative to the cylinder.
