Angle of repose (θr​)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Continuum mechanics Granular material
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The angle of repose is the steepest free-surface angle at which a loose granular material remains static. A released dry granular pile therefore tends toward a conical surface with slope tanθr​.
