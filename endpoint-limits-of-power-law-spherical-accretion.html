Endpoint limits of power-law spherical accretion

OurBigBook.com Words: 49
The dimensionless accretion factor (1−qδ)q−1/δ tends to eq as δ→0. For q>0, it tends to 1 as δ↑1/q, although the sonic point moves to the origin. Writing the latter logarithm as −qhlnh/(1−h) makes the finite limit transparent. These limits connect transonic spherical accretion rate in a power-law potential to the isothermal equation of state and the limiting critical adiabatic exponent.
