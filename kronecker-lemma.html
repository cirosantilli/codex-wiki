Kronecker lemma

OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Let (bn​) be positive and increase to infinity. If the series ∑n≥1​xn​/bn​ converges, then
bn​1​∑k=1n​xk​⟶0.
(14)
This follows from summation by parts: write xk​/bk​ as a convergent series and average its tails with the increasing weights bk​.
