Normal mean proportional to standard deviation model (Xi​∼N(μ,μ2),μ>0)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Exponential family Curved exponential family
OurBigBook.com Words: 130
This one-parameter normal distribution model has positive mean equal to its standard deviation. It is a curved exponential family: both the linear and quadratic natural parameters depend on the same scalar statistical parameter. It combines information about the center and spread, so the sample mean alone does not use all available Fisher information.
For a statistical sample of size n, let A=∑i​xi​ and B=∑i​xi2​. Up to an additive constant the log-likelihood function is ℓ(μ)=−nlogμ−B/(2μ2)+A/μ. Its score function is (B−Aμ−nμ2)/μ3. When B>0, the numerator changes from positive to negative at its unique positive root, so the global maximum-likelihood estimator is
μ​=2n−A+A2+4nB​​.
(1)
The all-zero observation vector has zero probability and is an exceptional case with an unbounded likelihood function near zero. Since EA=nμ and EB=2nμ2, the expected Fisher information is In​(μ)=3n/μ2. Thus regular asymptotic normality of a maximum likelihood estimator gives n​(μ​−μ)d​N(0,μ2/3). The optimal asymptotic combination of a normal sample mean and standard deviation attains the same first-order precision.
