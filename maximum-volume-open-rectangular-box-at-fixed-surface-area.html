Maximum-volume open rectangular box at fixed surface area (Vmax​=(S/3)3/2/2)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Lagrange multiplier
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For a lidless rectangular parallelepiped with positive dimensions l,w,h and prescribed external surface area S=lw+2lh+2wh, the largest volume is attained at l=w=2h=S/3​. The arithmetic-geometric mean inequality applied to lw,2lh,2wh gives
4(lwh)2≤(S/3)3,
(82)
with equality exactly when these three face-area terms are equal. This proves both the claimed global bound and its attainable equality case. Lagrange multipliers produce the same dimensions from the interior stationarity equations. The weighted open-box minimization is the corresponding fixed-volume formulation with general positive face weights.
