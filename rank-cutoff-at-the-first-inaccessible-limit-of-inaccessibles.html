Rank cutoff at the first inaccessible limit of inaccessibles (Vι​⊨ZFC+∞IC+¬ILI)

OurBigBook.com Words: 54
If ι is the least inaccessible limit of inaccessibles, Vι​ models ZFC and internally has unboundedly many inaccessible cardinals, while none of its ordinals is an inaccessible limit of inaccessibles. Rank agreement makes inaccessibility below ι absolute. If a model of unboundedly many inaccessibles has no such limit in the first place, no cutoff is needed. This proves the corresponding relative-consistency nonimplication.
