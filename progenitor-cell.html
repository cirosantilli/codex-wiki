Progenitor cell

 Home Biology Biological cell
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 4
A progenitor cell is a precursor with relatively restricted differentiation potential and, generally, more limited self-renewal than a stem cell. Usage depends on the lineage and assay; an expression marker alone does not establish renewal capacity.
