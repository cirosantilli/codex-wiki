Splitting of rational primes in a quadratic field

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Ring of integers of a quadratic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 105 Articles: 2
For a quadratic field of discriminant D and a rational prime p, the ideal (p) splits into two distinct prime ideals, remains prime, or ramifies according as the quadratic character χD​(p) is 1, −1, or 0. The corresponding prime-ideal norms are (p,p), p2, and p, respectively.
