Thermal diffusion length (ℓ(t)=αt​)

 ... Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Thermal conduction Thermal diffusivity Thermal diffusion time
OurBigBook.com Words: 173
The thermal diffusion length ℓ(t)=αt​ is the characteristic length over which thermal conduction changes a temperature distribution during time t in the constant-thermal diffusivity model. It is a scale, not a sharp front or distance travelled at a constant speed: the heat equation has infinite propagation speed mathematically. Different definitions of penetration depth introduce numerical factors. In particular, the normalized one-dimensional heat kernel has spatial variance 2αt, so its root-mean-square width is 2αt​.
At t=10s, the model materials have thermal diffusion lengths 4.47 and 2.24mm. For L=10mm their thermal diffusion times are 50 and 200s. These values describe the same heat equation as the thermal diffusion rate of a Fourier mode; the different numerical factors come from the definition of the scale.
