Worldsheet moduli (Mg,n​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Polyakov action Polyakov path integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 183 Articles: 3
Worldsheet moduli parameterize inequivalent conformal structures and marked insertion points after worldsheet diffeomorphisms and Weyl invariance are fixed. At each genus, string perturbation theory integrates over the corresponding moduli space.
