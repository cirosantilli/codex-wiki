Gauge transformation in cosmological perturbation theory (xμ=xμ+ξμ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Linear cosmological perturbation theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 238 Articles: 4
A cosmological gauge transformation changes the identification of a perturbed spacetime with its homogeneous background. Individual field perturbations can change without changing physical observables. At first order the metric change is the linear metric gauge-transformation law; matter scalars obey the corresponding background-shift rule.
