Mach cone (sinθ=1/M)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Compressible flow Normal shock wave Oblique shock Mach angle
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
The cone forming the envelope of sound wavefronts from a source moving supersonically at Mach number M>1. In the source frame the ray-velocity sphere is centered at −Mc0​ex​ and has radius c0​. Its tangent rays make angle sin−1(1/M) to the backward flight direction; all other emitted acoustic rays lie inside that cone.
