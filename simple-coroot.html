Simple coroot (αi∨​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 34
For chosen simple roots, the corresponding coroots are the simple coroots. They form the simple system of the dual root system. The fundamental weights are defined by ⟨ωj​,αi∨​⟩=δij​, so pairings with simple coroots record coordinates in the fundamental-weight basis.
