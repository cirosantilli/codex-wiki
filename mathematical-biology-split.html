Mathematical biology

 Home Physics Branches of physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 24k Articles: 460
Mathematical biology uses mathematical models to connect biological mechanisms with quantitative predictions. Differential equations can track changing quantities, while population dynamics, enzyme kinetics and gene regulatory networks ask how growth, reactions and feedback shape living systems. A useful mathematical model states its assumptions and units: a simple chemostat, for example, reveals how nutrient supply and removal jointly determine whether a culture persists.
