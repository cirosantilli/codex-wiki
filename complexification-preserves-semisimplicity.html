Complexification preserves semisimplicity (rad(gC​)=(radg)C​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Complexification of a Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For a finite-dimensional real Lie algebra g, the solvable radical of its complexification of a Lie algebra is invariant under complex conjugation. Its real fixed subspace is a solvable ideal of g, and complexifying that fixed subspace recovers the whole radical. Conversely the complexification of any real solvable ideal is solvable. These inclusions prove the displayed identity; in particular g is semisimple if and only if gC​ is semisimple.
