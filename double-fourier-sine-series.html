Double Fourier sine series (f(x,y)=∑m,n≥1​cmn​sin(mπx/Lx​)sin(nπy/Ly​))

On a rectangle (0,Lx​)×(0,Ly​), products of the one-dimensional Fourier sine basis give an orthogonal basis of L2 space. Fourier orthogonality in each variable gives
cmn​=Lx​Ly​4​∫0Lx​​∫0Ly​​f(x,y)sinLx​mπx​sinLy​nπy​dydx.
(19)
For a constant function one, these coefficients are 4[1−(−1)m][1−(−1)n]/(π2mn): only odd indices occur. The product modes vanish on the rectangle's boundary, making them natural Dirichlet Laplacian eigenfunctions. In the heat equation, their amplitudes decay as e−κπ2(m2/Lx2​+n2/Ly2​)t. Initial data not vanishing at the boundary are still represented in the interior and in the square-integrable sense; the positive-time boundary values need not agree continuously with the initially assigned boundary values.
