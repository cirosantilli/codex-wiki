Factorial-moment criterion for Poisson convergence (E(Xn​)k​→λk)

 ... Markov chain Continuous-time Markov chain Birth-death process Birth-death master equation Factorial moment Poisson factorial moments
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
If nonnegative integer-valued random variables Xn​ have all fixed factorial moments converging to λk, then their convergence in distribution is to the Poisson distribution with mean λ. The first-moment bound gives uniform tightness, and bounds on higher moments give uniform integrability of each fixed lower moment. Every subsequential limit therefore has the indicated factorial moments. For such a limit X and t≥0, the nonnegative binomial expansion and the monotone convergence theorem give E(1+t)X=∑k≥0​tkλk/k!=eλt. This identifies its moment-generating function as that of the Poisson distribution, uniquely determining the limit.
