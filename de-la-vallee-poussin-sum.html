This average of Fourier partial sums reproduces every degree-at-most-n trigonometric polynomial. With σr​=r−1∑j=0r−1​sj​,
vn,m​=m(n+m)σn+m​−nσn​​,∥vn,m​∥∞​≤1+m2n​.

The bound follows because Fejér summation is a uniform-norm contraction. For n=0, the term involving σ0​ is omitted.
