Factorial-tail irrationality proof for a hyperbolic cosine

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Exponential function Hyperbolic function Hyperbolic cosine
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
For a fixed positive integer q, cosh(1/q)=∑k≥0​q−2k/(2k)! is irrational. If it were rational with denominator b, choose N large enough that b∣(2N)!. After multiplication by q2N(2N)!, the finite partial sum is an integer and the remaining positive tail is bounded by a geometric series whose ratio is at most 1/[q2(2N+1)(2N+2)]. For sufficiently large N, this tail is strictly between zero and one, contradicting its integer value. Only a finite integer partial sum is used; convergence of an infinite series of rationals does not itself prove irrationality.
