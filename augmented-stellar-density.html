Augmented stellar density (ρ(r,ψ))

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics Galactic distribution function
OurBigBook.com Words: 81 Articles: 1
An augmented density ρ(r,ψ) treats radius and relative potential as independent variables and reduces to the physical density on ψ=ψ(r). Its extension away from that curve contains additional orbital-anisotropy information. Different extensions can give the same physical density; physical realizability additionally requires a nonnegative galactic distribution function.
