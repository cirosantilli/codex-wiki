Square-root stock implied volatility (Σ(t,T)2=−T−t8​logSt​​C(t,T)​)

For a square-root stock claim priced by C(t,T)=E[ST​​∣Ft​], the constant-volatility geometric Brownian motion price is St​​exp(−σ2(T−t)/8). If t<T, St​>0 and 0<C(t,T)≤St​​, its unique nonnegative inverse volatility is the displayed Σ(t,T). For bounded predictable volatility in dS=SσdW, bounds 0≤a≤σ≤b give a≤Σ≤b, without independence from the Brownian motion. At zero stock price the claim price is also zero and no volatility is identified by this equation; a separate convention is needed.
