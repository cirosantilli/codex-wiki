Localization of a Noetherian ring

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Localization of a ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 67 Articles: 1
Every localization of a Noetherian ring is Noetherian. Each ideal J⊆S−1R is the extension of its contraction to R, whose finite generating set consequently generates J.
