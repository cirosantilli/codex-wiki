Shifted Poisson data fidelity (Dg​(u)=∫[Tu−glog(1+Tu)])

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Regularization of an inverse problem Variational regularization Poisson data fidelity
OurBigBook.com Words: 251 Articles: 2
For u≥0 and a positivity-preserving operator, this is the negative log-likelihood for independent Poisson observations with intensity 1+Tu, up to constants depending only on the data and the unit background. For bounded g≥0, its scalar derivatives are 1−g/(1+s) and g/(1+s)2 on s=Tu≥0. It is convex, and strictly convex in the predicted intensity when g>0 almost everywhere. On a unit-area domain, the Jensen inequality gives Dg​(u)≥∥Tu∥1​−∥g∥∞​log(1+∥Tu∥1​). The shift makes log(1+Tu) integrable whenever Tu∈L1; no integrability of logu is needed.
