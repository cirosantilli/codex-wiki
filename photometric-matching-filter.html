Photometric matching filter

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Photometry (optics) Photometer
OurBigBook.com Words: 16
A photometric matching filter shapes a photodiode's combined spectral responsivity toward the photopic luminous efficiency function. Matching is imperfect, so photometric calibration must retain spectral information. [ref]
