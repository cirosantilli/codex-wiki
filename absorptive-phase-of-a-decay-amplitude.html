Absorptive phase of a decay amplitude (δ)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Decay width Decay amplitude
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
An absorptive phase arises from physical intermediate states or rescattering in a decay amplitude. For a pair of CP symmetry conjugate reactions with otherwise symmetric dynamics, this phase is the same in both amplitudes, whereas a weak phase changes sign. Their differing transformation laws allow interference to produce a direct CP asymmetry.
