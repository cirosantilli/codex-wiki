Trace-power witness optimization (λmax​(F)d≤Tr(Fd)≤Dλmax​(F)d)

 ... Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Quantum complexity theory QMA Witness acceptance operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For a positive witness acceptance operator on dimension D, large trace powers approximate its largest eigenvalue. With an m-qubit QMA quantum witness and d=2m+2, the usual NO instances give Tr(Fd)<2−d and YES instances give a value above 2−d. A closed product-index expansion computes the trace by recomputation with polynomial storage, avoiding logarithms and roots in a real-arithmetic implementation.
