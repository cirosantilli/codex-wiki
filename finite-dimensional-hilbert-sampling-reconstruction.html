Finite-dimensional Hilbert sampling reconstruction (f​=(PS​∣T​)−1PS​f)

 ... Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Linear operator Projection (linear algebra) Oblique projection
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
For equal finite-dimensional closed subspaces of a Hilbert space T,S, positivity of cosθT,S​ makes PS​∣T​ invertible. The displayed reconstruction is the unique element of T whose orthogonal projection onto S matches that of f. It is the oblique projection onto T along S⊥. Its operator norm is the value of the secant function at the directed subspace angle, and its error is at most that secant function value times the best orthogonal projection error. The lower error bound follows from the Pythagorean identity. Zero-dimensional cases are handled directly.
