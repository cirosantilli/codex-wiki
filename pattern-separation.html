OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A transformation that makes representations of similar inputs less overlapping, helping reduce interference between stored events. It must retain enough information for useful downstream learning and retrieval. The dentate gyrus is a proposed substrate, with quantitative input–output evidence from spatial cue-conflict experiments.[ref]
