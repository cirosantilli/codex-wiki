The series is locally uniformly convergent. Integrate πcot(πζ)/(ζ2−z2) around the square with sides Reζ,Imζ=±(N+1/2). The cotangent is uniformly bounded there, so the integral tends to zero. The residue theorem then gives πcot(πz)/z+∑n∈Z​(n2−z2)−1=0, which is the expansion. Differentiation generates higher integer-lattice pole sums. Setting w=e2πiz gives πcotπz=πi(w+1)/(w−1). On 0<∣z∣<1, expansion of each paired summand gives πcotπz=z−1−2∑m≥0​ζ(2m+2)z2m+1, relating this trigonometric function to the Riemann zeta function.
