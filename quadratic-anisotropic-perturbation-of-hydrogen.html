Quadratic anisotropic perturbation of hydrogen (V′=α(x2+y2)+βz2)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Hydrogen atom
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
An axially symmetric quadratic potential energy perturbation preserves parity and the z component of orbital angular momentum. For 1s,2s,2p0​,2p±1​, its first-order energy shifts are respectively (2α+β)a02​, 14(2α+β)a02​, (12α+18β)a02​, and (24α+6β)a02​. They follow by multiplying the hydrogen radial second moment by the angular average of α+(β−α)cos2θ, whose cos2θ averages are 1/3,1/3,3/5,1/5. Negative β makes the globally extended potential unbounded below; the shifts then describe local perturbative resonance poles or a potential stabilized at large distance.
