Wind-driven Roche-lobe overflow (q<3(1−n)1+3n​)

 ... Binary star Roche lobe Roche-lobe overflow Nonconservative binary mass transfer Donor-wind angular-momentum loss Donor-wind Roche-lobe response
OurBigBook.com Words: 138 Articles: 1
A fast escaping Jeans-mode mass loss wind can drive a giant into Roche-lobe overflow because its radius changes as it loses mass, even though the wind itself is not substantially captured. This differs from slow Wind Roche-lobe overflow. For Rd​∝Md−n​ and RL​∝a(Md​/M)1/3, wind alone gives dlog(Rd​/RL​)/dt=M˙[3(1−n)q−(1+3n)]/[3Md​(1+q)], where q=Md​/Ma​ and M˙<0 is the escaping wind rate. The displayed inequality is exactly the condition that overfill increases.
