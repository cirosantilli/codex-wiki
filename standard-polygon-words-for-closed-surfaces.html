Standard polygon words for closed surfaces

OurBigBook.com Words: 149
Read a boundary word counterclockwise, giving every paired edge a label. A positive exponent means the edge arrow agrees with the boundary traversal, and an inverse means it disagrees. Identify equally labelled edges in their arrow directions. The sphere has a square word abb−1a−1; the vertex count is three, giving Euler characteristic 3−2+1=2. A closed orientable surface of genus g≥1 has word
∏j=1g​aj​bj​aj−1​bj−1​.
(2)
A nonorientable surface which is the connected sum of surfaces of k real projective planes has word c1​c1​⋯ck​ck​. For k=1, this is a digon; the square word abab, identifying antipodal boundary points, is another model of the real projective plane. The Klein bottle has square word aba−1b, representing the nonorientable case k=2. The classification theorem for closed combinatorial surfaces says that these orientable and nonorientable families exhaust the connected closed cases.
