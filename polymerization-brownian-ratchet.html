Polymerization Brownian ratchet

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biopolymer mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 205 Articles: 2
A polymerization Brownian ratchet advances a filament by incorporating a monomer when thermal motion opens enough space between its tip and a resisting load. The irreversible chemical step rectifies the load's fluctuations. The elementary load advance a depends on tip geometry; two staggered strands can give a=δ/2, whereas two aligned strands do not automatically give this step. An effective addition rate must refer to the same elementary advance.
