Exclusive joint removal has a diffusing difference mode (Var(X1​−X2​)=Var(X1​(0)−X2​(0))+2λt)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Chemical reaction network Mean-field joint removal of two chemical species
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
If individual removal is absent, simultaneous removal of one molecule from each species leaves X1​−X2​ unchanged. Independent constant-rate births change it by plus or minus one, each at rate λ. The difference is therefore a symmetric continuous-time random walk on the integers with variance growing as displayed. It has no normalizable stationary law. This explains why a deterministic attracting line of equilibria does not provide a stationary stochastic distribution.
