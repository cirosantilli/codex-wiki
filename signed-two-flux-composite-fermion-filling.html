Signed two-flux composite-fermion filling (ν∗=ν/(1−2ν))

 ... Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force Hall effect Quantum Hall effect Fractional quantum Hall effect Composite fermion
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
Attach two statistical flux quanta Φ0​=h/e per electron at density ns​. For B>0, B∗=B−2ns​Φ0​=B(1−2ν) and the signed effective filling is ν∗=ns​Φ0​/B∗=ν/(1−2ν). Inversion gives ν=ν∗/(2ν∗+1). Integer ν∗=p gives p/(2p+1), while ν∗=−p gives p/(2p−1). Using nonnegative effective filling instead requires explicitly tracking the effective-field sign. At half filling the mean effective field vanishes.
