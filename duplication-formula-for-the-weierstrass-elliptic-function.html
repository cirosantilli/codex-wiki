The Weierstrass elliptic function satisfies the displayed meromorphic function identity. To prove it, the Riemann-Hurwitz formula shows that its derivative has exactly three simple zeros modulo its period lattice, at the nonzero two-torsion points of a complex torus. At each such half-period h, evenness around h gives ℘′′/℘′=1/(z−h)+O(z−h), so the right side has principal part 1/(4(z−h)2), matching ℘(2z). At zero, ℘=z−2+O(z2) and ℘′′/℘′=−3/z+O(z3) give the same principal part and zero constant term. The difference is a holomorphic elliptic function, hence constant by compactness, and its limit at zero is zero.
