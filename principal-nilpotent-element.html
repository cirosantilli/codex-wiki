Principal nilpotent element

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Principal sl2 subalgebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A principal nilpotent element of a semisimple Lie algebra is a nilpotent element whose centralizer has dimension equal to the rank. For nonzero simple-root vectors ei​, the sum e=∑i​ei​ is principal nilpotent and forms the raising element of a Principal sl2 subalgebra.
