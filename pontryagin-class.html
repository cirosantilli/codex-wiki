Pontryagin class (pk​(E)=(−1)kc2k​(E⊗R​C))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Chern-Weil homomorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For a real vector bundle E, its Pontryagin classes are the even-index Chern classes of its complexification with the displayed alternating signs. They lie in integral cohomology of degrees 4k and obey a total-class product formula under direct sums. If a metric connection on a vector bundle has curvature matrix R, its first real representative is p1​(E)=−tr(R∧R)/(8π2). The Bianchi identity makes this representative closed, and Chern-Weil connection transgression makes its de Rham cohomology class independent of the connection. This normalization gives degree-four A-hat form −p1​/24.
