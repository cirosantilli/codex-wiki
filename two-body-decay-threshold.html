Two-body decay threshold (M≥m1​+m2​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Four-momentum Relativistic two-body decay
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
In the rest frame of the parent particle, four-momentum conservation requires M=E1​+E2​≥m1​+m2​. For two equal masses the daughter momentum magnitude is 21​M2−4m2​; the two-body decay phase space closes at M=2m. For a finite constant decay amplitude, the decay width tends to zero at this threshold.
