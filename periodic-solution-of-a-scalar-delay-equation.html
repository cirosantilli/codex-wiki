Periodic solution of a scalar delay equation

OurBigBook.com Words: 20
Purely imaginary characteristic roots produce oscillatory solutions. In particular, rτ=−π/2 gives λ=±iπ/(2τ) and the real solution cos(πt/(2τ)).
