Integrated Clausius-Clapeyron relation (ln(p2​/p1​)≃−(Lm​/R)(1/T2​−1/T1​))

If the molar latent heat Lm​ is approximately constant on the chosen temperature interval, the Ideal-vapor Clausius-Clapeyron approximation integrates to
lnp1​p2​​≃−RLm​​(T2​1​−T1​1​),p(T)≃p1​exp[RLm​​(T1​1​−T1​)].
(193)
For Lm​>0, higher absolute temperature gives higher saturation pressure. A plot of ln(p/p1​) against inverse temperature 1/T is a straight line of slope −Lm​/R. One reference pair (T1​,p1​) fixes its intercept. On a per-unit-mass basis replace Lm​/R by L/Rs​, with Rs​=R/M and M the molar mass; using L in Jkg−1 with the molar R would give incompatible units.
If only the constant-latent heat approximation is relaxed, the same ideal-vapor model instead gives
lnp1​p2​​≃R1​∫T1​T2​​T2Lm​(T)​dT.
(194)
Consequently an apparent straight-line fit across a broad temperature range need not represent a single material constant. The simple exponential also approaches a finite value as T→∞; this is a property of the stipulated formula, not a prediction that liquid–gas phase coexistence extends beyond a thermodynamic critical point.
