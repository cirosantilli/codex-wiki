For the moving semi-infinite crack, take the branch cut of z​ on the negative real axis and x​>0 ahead of the tip. With steadily moving antiplane shear, face tractions σ23±​=−f(x) imply ImF′±=f/μ. The antisymmetric displacement response gives F′−=−F′+. Hence G=z​F′ has jump G+−G−=−2−x​f(x)/μ. The Sokhotski–Plemelj theorem gives the displayed Cauchy integral, assuming convergence and no extra remote homogeneous loading. For x>0 it is purely imaginary, so σ23​=∫−∞0​−s​f(s)/(x−s)ds/(πx​). At fixed co-moving loading this ahead-of-tip stress is independent of speed; the displacement still has the factor 1/β.
