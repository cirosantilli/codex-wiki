Planning fluent

 Home Computer science Artificial intelligence
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
A fact whose truth can change as actions execute in automated planning. Ground location facts and an empty-container marker are examples. In STRIPS, static facts such as an object's fixed type are never added or deleted, while planning fluents can appear in action effects.
