Minimally coupled massless scalar field (S=−21​∫ddx−g​gμν∂μ​ϕ∂ν​ϕ)

The displayed action functional contains no mass term and no Rϕ2 curvature coupling. Its Euler-Lagrange field equation is ∂μ​(−g​gμν∂ν​ϕ)=0. In a flat FLRW metric, a Fourier mode obeys ϕk′′​+2(a′/a)ϕk′​+k2ϕk​=0 in four dimensions. The expansion can therefore freeze these modes; the curvature term of a conformally coupled scalar field changes that conclusion.
