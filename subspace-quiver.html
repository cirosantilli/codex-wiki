Subspace quiver (Sr​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Quiver
OurBigBook.com Words: 259 Articles: 4
This quiver has r source vertices, each with one arrow to a common sink. Its path algebra has the vertex idempotents and the arrows as basis. After extracting source kernels from a representation, its remaining arrows identify the source spaces with subspaces of the sink.
