Quartic hilltop inflation (ns​≃1−3/N)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic inflation Slow-roll approximation Hilltop inflation
OurBigBook.com Words: 153 Articles: 2
Near a flat quartic maximum, V≃ασ4−3αϕ4. With reduced Planck mass M, the slow-roll approximation gives N≃σ4/(24M2ϕN2​), ηV​≃−3/(2N) and ϵV​≃σ4/(192M4N3). Hence ns​≃1−3/N, nT​≃−2ϵV​, and PR​≃8αN3/π2. The result is dominated by the near-maximum part of the trajectory; extending slow roll to a distant minimum is unnecessary.
