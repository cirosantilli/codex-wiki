Coefficient extraction ([x1a1​​⋯xnan​​]P)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
Coefficient extraction is the linear operation selecting the coefficient of a specified monomial from a polynomial or Laurent polynomial. Multiplying by the inverse of that monomial reduces the operation to taking a constant term. The coefficient form of the Combinatorial Nullstellensatz expresses certain coefficient extractions as weighted sums of evaluations on a finite product set.
