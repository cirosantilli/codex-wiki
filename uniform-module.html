Uniform module

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 154 Articles: 2
A nonzero module is uniform if every pair of nonzero submodules has nonzero intersection. Equivalently, every nonzero submodule is an essential submodule. A right Noetherian domain is uniform as a right module: if aA∩bA=0 for nonzero a,b, the right ideals biaA, i≥0, form an infinite direct sum, contradicting the ascending chain condition.
