Equally spaced normal curvatures average to mean curvature (∑j=0m−1​kn​(θ+jπ/m)=mH)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Second fundamental form Normal curvature Euler formula for normal curvature
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
For directions spaced in the same rotational sense, Euler formula for normal curvature gives kn​(θ)=H+(k1​−k2​)cos(2θ)/2. The sum of the m equally spaced complex exponentials e2iθ+2πij/m is zero when m≥2, proving the formula. Consecutive unsigned angles alone do not imply this arrangement: they permit directions to double back.
