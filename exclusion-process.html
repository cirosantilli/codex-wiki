Exclusion process (Lf(η)=∑x,y​p(x,y)η(x)(1−η(y))[f(ηxy)−f(η)])

Particles on a countable lattice attempt jumps at the rates p(x,y), and an attempt into an occupied site is suppressed. Thus each site contains at most one particle and the number of particles is conserved when finite. Here ηxy interchanges the occupations at x and y. Bounded nearest-neighbour rates give a well-defined Markov process. A symmetric jump kernel gives the symmetric simple exclusion process; an unequal left-right kernel gives the asymmetric simple exclusion process.
