Koebe quarter theorem

 ... Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Uniformization theorem Riemann mapping theorem Koebe distortion theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 1
If f is a univalent function on the unit disc, then B(f(0),∣f′(0)∣/4)⊆f(D). The constant 1/4 is sharp, as shown by the Koebe function. This gives useful comparisons between the derivative of a conformal map, its conformal radius, and its distance to the domain boundary.
