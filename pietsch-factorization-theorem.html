Pietsch factorization theorem

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Topological vector space Continuous linear operator Absolutely p-summing operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 105
For an absolutely p-summing operator T:E→F, there is a probability measure μ on the dual unit ball with its weak-star topology such that ∥Tx∥≤πp​(T)(∫∣ϕ(x)∣pdμ(ϕ))1/p. Thus T factors through the closed span of the evaluation functions in Lp(μ), with the final operator having norm at most πp​(T). Conversely this factorization implies the summing inequality by summing under the integral. Existence of μ follows from finite-dimensional Hahn-Banach separation theorem: failure for finitely many vectors would contradict the summing inequality for a nonnegative weighted family. Compactness of probability measures then combines the finite constraints. The final operator need only be defined on the indicated closed subspace, not on all of Lp(μ).
