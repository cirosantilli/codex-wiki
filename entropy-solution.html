Entropy solution

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation Scalar conservation law Inviscid Burgers equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 584 Articles: 9
An entropy solution is a weak solution of a nonlinear scalar conservation law that also satisfies entropy inequalities selecting physically admissible shocks. For convex fluxes, this excludes expansion shocks and restores uniqueness for bounded initial data.
