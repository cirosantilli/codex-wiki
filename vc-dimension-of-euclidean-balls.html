VC dimension of Euclidean balls (VC(Bd​)=d+1(d≥1))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Statistical learning theory Shattering coefficient VC dimension
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
The positive-radius closed Euclidean balls in Rd have VC dimension d+1 for d≥1. Vertices of a regular simplex are shattered: interpolate any desired signs by an affine function a⋅x+b, then perturb a sphere through the vertices to the score 1+ηb+ηa⋅x−∥x∥2. For the upper bound, take an affine dependence λ among d+2 points and orient it so that Q=∑i​λi​∥xi​∥2≥0. Labelling the positive coefficients inside and the negative coefficients outside would force ∑i​λi​(r2−∥xi​−c∥2)>0, while the affine dependence makes this sum equal −Q≤0. The weaker d+2 upper bound from unrestricted lifted affine linear classifiers loses their fixed quadratic coefficient.
