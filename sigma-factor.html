Sigma factor

 ... Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Gene expression Transcription (biology) RNA polymerase
OurBigBook.com Words: 27 Articles: 1
A protein that confers promoter-recognition specificity on a bacterial-type RNA polymerase. Plastid sigma factors can be nuclear encoded and imported.
