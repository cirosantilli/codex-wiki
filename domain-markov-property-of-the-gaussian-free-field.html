Domain Markov property of the Gaussian free field

 ... Stochastic process Gaussian process Gaussian random field Gaussian free field Continuum Gaussian free field Zero-boundary Gaussian free field
OurBigBook.com Words: 86 Articles: 1
For an open U⊂D, the field decomposes as h=hU​+hD∖U, where hU​ is an independent zero-boundary Gaussian free field on U and hD∖U is harmonic on U and agrees with the outside field in the distributional sense.
