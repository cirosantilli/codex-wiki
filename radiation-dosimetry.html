Radiation dosimetry

 Home Physics Radiation Ionizing radiation
OurBigBook.com Words: 161 Articles: 7
Measurement or calculation of energy imparted by ionizing radiation, together with explicitly specified weighting conventions. Absorbed dose, equivalent dose and effective dose are different quantities.
