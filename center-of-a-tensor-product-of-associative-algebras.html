Center of a tensor product of associative algebras (Z(A⊗k​B)=Z(A)⊗k​Z(B))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor product Tensor product of associative algebras
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For associative algebras of finite dimension over a field k, write a central tensor as x=∑j​aj​⊗bj​ in a basis of B. Commutation with every a⊗1 forces each aj​ into the center of an associative algebra Z(A). Reexpand in a basis of Z(A) and commute with every 1⊗b to force the second coefficients into Z(B). The reverse inclusion follows by multiplying pure tensors.
