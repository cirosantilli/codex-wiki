Keep the electromagnetic units of the supplied Lagrangian, including its factor c, and regard e as the signed coupling used there. Set aB​=eΦB​/(2πc). The canonical momentum is pϕ​=Iϕ˙​+aB​, so the Legendre transform gives
H=2I(pϕ​−aB​)2​,H^=2I1​(−iℏ∂ϕ​−2πceΦB​​)2.​

Single-valued periodic wavefunctions have normalized eigenstates einϕ/2π​ with integer n. Defining dimensionless flux f=eΦB​/(hc) gives En​=ℏ2(n−f)2/(2I), and the trace is therefore
Z=n∈Z∑​exp[−2Iβℏ2​(n−f)2].​
(34)
The flux-threaded quantum rotor spectrum is periodic under f↦f+1 after relabelling n, corresponding to flux period hc/∣e∣ in these units. A local gauge change can remove the magnetic vector potential from the differential operator but then imposes a twisted boundary condition with phase e−2πif; it cannot remove the flux dependence from both the Hamiltonian and boundary condition simultaneously. In SI conventions the flux coupling has no c, so one must not mix the two unit systems.
