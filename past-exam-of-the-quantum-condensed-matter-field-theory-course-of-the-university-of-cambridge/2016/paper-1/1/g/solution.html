For α>0, the dispersion is periodic and has minima at k=0,π in the rotated full-zone convention, with
ϵmin​=2JS(1+α)2−1​=2JSα(2+α)​.​
(11)
The maximum is 2JS(1+α) at k=±π/2. The easy-axis state has no continuously broken symmetry, so a gap is allowed. Near a minimum the energy behaves as 2JSα(2+α)+(δk)2​. At α=0 it becomes ϵk​=2JS∣sink∣, linear near its zeros, with small-k slope 2JS in the chosen units. Restoring lattice spacing a and ℏ gives spin-wave speed 2JSa/ℏ. Zone folding reorganizes the same modes into the magnetic Brillouin zone.
These are linear spin-wave predictions, not a statement that the exact one-dimensional quantum chain always has long-range Néel order. At the isotropic point the zero-point spin reduction contains N−1∑k​(∣sink∣−1−1)/2, which diverges logarithmically as the infrared cutoff vanishes. This failure of the assumed ordered reference must not be mistaken for a calculation of the exact interacting spectrum.
