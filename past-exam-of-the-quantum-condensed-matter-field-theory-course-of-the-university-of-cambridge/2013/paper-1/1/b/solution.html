For each opposite-momentum pair use the Bogoliubov transformation
bk​=uk​αk​−vk​α−k†​,uk2​−vk2​=1,uk2​+vk2​=Ek​Ak​​,2uk​vk​=Ek​Bk​​.
(4)
Equivalently uk​=coshθk​, vk​=sinhθk​, with tanh(2θk​)=Bk​/Ak​. The identity uk2​−vk2​=1 preserves the canonical commutation relation. Vanishing anomalous terms require Bk​(uk2​+vk2​)−2Ak​uk​vk​=0, and hence the Bogoliubov quasiparticle dispersion is
Ek​=Ak2​−Bk2​​=ϵk​(ϵk​+2nVk​)​.​
(5)
Substitution and normal ordering give
H2​=Emf​+21​k=0∑​(Ek​−Ak​)+k=0∑​Ek​αk†​αk​.​
(6)
This is the requested form after putting each Ek​/2 inside the oscillator term. By momentum-pair counting in bosonic diagonalization, all sums cover the full nonzero-momentum set; no further factor of two is needed. The bosonic Bogoliubov zero-point shift combines the two vacuum sums before taking a continuum limit.
If Vk​→V0​>0 smoothly, the low-energy excitations are gapless phonons:
Ek​≃ℏc∣k∣,c=nV0​/m​.​
(7)
For contact repulsion, the healing length ξ=ℏ/2mnV0​​ separates this regime from the particle-like regime Ek​≃ϵk​+nV0​. Stability requires ϵk​+2nVk​>0 for every nonzero mode. A general momentum-dependent interaction need not share the contact model's entire curve. An unregularized contact model also makes the vacuum-energy shift ultraviolet divergent; a physical finite-range interaction or scattering-length renormalization is needed for that absolute ground-state energy.
