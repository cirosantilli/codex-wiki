The independent barred and unbarred Grassmann variables satisfy antiperiodic boundary conditions at 0,β. Adjacent time-slice labels in the normal-ordered symbol fix the continuum prescription and thermal normalization. The interaction coefficient is 3U, not the U of a differently normalized Hubbard model.
