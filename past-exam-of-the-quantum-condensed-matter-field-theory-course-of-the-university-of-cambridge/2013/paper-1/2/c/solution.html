For conventional superconductivity, an attractive interaction pairs opposite-spin fermions. A complex pairing Hubbard–Stratonovich transformation introduces a Cooper pair field Δ. The fermionic fields then appear quadratically, giving the pair-field effective action of a superconductor
Seff​[Δ,A]=∫g∣Δ∣2​−TrlnG−1[Δ,A]+SEM​,g>0.
(21)
The trace convention counts each independent spin-pair block once. The uniform saddle is the BCS gap equation; the fermionic excitations have Ek​=ξk2​+∣Δ∣2​ in the fully gapped case. A slow-field expansion near the transition yields Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity. Normalize its pair field Ψ so ∣Ψ∣2 is an effective pair density, with pair mass m∗​ and signed charge q=−2e:
F=∫d3x[a∣Ψ∣2+2b​∣Ψ∣4+2m∗​1​∣(−iℏ∇−qA)Ψ∣2+2μ0​B2​],b>0.
(22)
For a<0, the uniform minimum has ρ0​=∣Ψ∣2=−a/b, with arbitrary constant phase. Writing Ψ=(ρ0​​+h)eiθ separates the amplitude mode h from the phase. The local potential has curvature term 2∣a∣h2 and coherence scale ξ=ℏ/2m∗​∣a∣​. A neutral condensate has a gapless phase Goldstone mode when suitable dynamics is included.
The charged theory is invariant under
θ↦θ+qχ/ℏ,A↦A+∇χ,ϕ↦ϕ−∂t​χ.
(23)
The gauge-invariant condensate momentum is ℏ∇θ−qA. Only ℏ∇θ−qA and ℏθ˙+qϕ enter physical phase dynamics. At nearly constant amplitude, varying F gives
j=m∗​qρ0​​(ℏ∇θ−qA),λ−2=m∗​μ0​q2ρ0​​.​
(24)
For a smooth phase, taking the curl and using Ampère's law gives ∇2B=B/λ2: the Meissner effect screens a static bulk magnetic field over the London penetration depth λ. In a gauge with θ=0, the phase stiffness is a quadratic term in A, the superconducting version of the Higgs mechanism.
To identify the dynamical mode, a simple lossless long-wave superconducting phase-density action is
L=2χp​​(ℏθ˙+qϕ)2−2m∗​ρ0​​(ℏ∇θ−qA)2+2ϵ0​E2​−2μ0​B2​,χp​>0.
(25)
Here χp​ is the pair compressibility coefficient. Eliminating the longitudinal electric potential gives
ω2=ωp2​+cs2​k2,ωp2​=ϵ0​m∗​q2ρ0​​,cs2​=m∗​χp​ρ0​​.​
(26)
Thus the neutral sound mode becomes a gapped longitudinal plasma oscillation; transverse electromagnetic waves acquire ω2=cvac2​k2+cvac2​/λ2 in this simple bulk model. This redistribution of the phase degree of freedom into electromagnetic collective modes is the Anderson-Higgs mechanism in a superconductor. Local gauge symmetry is a redundancy, not an independently observable symmetry whose breaking creates another massless particle. [ref]
The static functional alone does not determine a frequency or damping rate. The displayed mode dispersions require the added dynamical assumptions; real screening, normal carriers, boundaries and dissipation modify them. The amplitude fluctuation is distinct from the phase mode and is not removed by the gauge choice.
