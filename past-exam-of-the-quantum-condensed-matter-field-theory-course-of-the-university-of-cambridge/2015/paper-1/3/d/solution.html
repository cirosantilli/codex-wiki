The convergent Gaussian integral can be performed by a contour shift or by its Fourier-transform identity. Its determinant is independent of φ at this order. Combining that normalization with the saddle action into a constant S0​ gives

The saddle contribution to S0​ is −βVμ2/(2g); the additional measure-dependent constant need not be assigned an invented numerical value. The density Gaussian extends the fluctuation integration beyond its exact positivity domain, which is justified only while fluctuations around ρ0​>0 are small. The smooth zero-winding assumption is also required as discussed above. A constant global phase zero mode gives a finite compact-group volume, handled separately from nonzero phonon modes.
Fourier transforming the Bose-gas phase-only action gives a kernel ωn2​/g+(ρ0​/m)q2. Analytically continuing ωn​↦−i(ω+i0+) gives its real-frequency poles,
ω(q)=c∣q∣,c=gρ0​/m​.​
(38)
In the present units the excitation energy equals ω. Each real Fourier coordinate is a harmonic oscillator with inertia coefficient 1/g and spring coefficient ρ0​q2/m, hence frequency equal to the square root of their ratio. This is the direct relation to a quantum harmonic chain. The mode is a gapless sound phonon; its finite speed reflects both the compressibility ∂ρ/∂μ=1/g and the phase stiffness ρ0​/m.
Retaining the density-gradient term provides a useful check. Its integration instead gives a temporal coefficient 1/Aq​, so
ω2(q)=mρ0​q2​(g+4mρ0​q2​)=c2q2+4m2q4​,ω(q)=ϵq​(ϵq​+2gρ0​)​,ϵq​=2mq2​.
(39)
This is the Bogoliubov spectrum. Its small-q limit is the derived sound branch; at large q within the contact model it crosses towards particle-like behaviour. The crossover in the squared dispersion is q∼2mgρ0​​, precisely the scale beyond which the discarded density gradient is not small.
The same action checks the dimensional restriction in the saddle analysis. At nonzero temperature its zero Matsubara mode gives phase fluctuations proportional to ∫ddq/q2; at zero temperature integrating the frequency gives ∫ddq/∣q∣. Their infrared divergences invalidate uniform true long-range order for d≤2 at nonzero temperature and for d=1 at zero temperature. The long-wave collective description can remain useful for correlations even where a global condensate saddle is not a literal symmetry-broken state. The contact coupling here is an effective low-energy parameter; this calculation does not determine ultraviolet renormalization or trap-specific surface modes.
