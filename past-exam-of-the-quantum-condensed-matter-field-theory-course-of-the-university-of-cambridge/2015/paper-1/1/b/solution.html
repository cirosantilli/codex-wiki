The spin commutation relations give [Stotz​,Sn±​]=±Sn±​. Using the product rule for a commutator,
[Stotz​,Sn+​Sn+1−​]=Sn+​Sn+1−​−Sn+​Sn+1−​=0,
(2)
and the conjugate term also vanishes. The longitudinal field commutes with every Snz​, so [H,Stotz​]=0. The Hamiltonian is invariant under the global spin rotation U(θ)=e−iθStotz​: its continuous symmetry group is U(1). Simultaneous energy and total-magnetization eigenstates can be chosen, and evolution preserves each fixed-magnetization sector. Local magnetization can still flow between sites; generally [H,Snz​]=0. In the fermion representation the conserved number will be Nf​=N/2−Stotz​.
