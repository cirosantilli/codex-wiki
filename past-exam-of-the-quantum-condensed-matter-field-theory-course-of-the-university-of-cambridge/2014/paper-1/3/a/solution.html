The ground state is the lower symmetric combination of localized well states: tunnelling lifts the apparent classical degeneracy. This splitting is nonperturbative in ℏ, beyond a power series about either minimum. In a metastable well a bounce, with a negative fluctuation mode, describes decay rather than the stable doublet splitting. Finite temperature, overlapping events or a low barrier require improvements beyond the dilute approximation.
