With Dμ​=∂μ​+igAμ​ and [Ta,Tb]=ifabc​Tc, the Yang-Mills field strength has the sign Fμνa​=Gμνa​−gfabc​Aμb​Aνc​. Expanding −Fμνa​Faμν/4 gives a cross term (g/2)Gμνa​fabc​AbμAcν; antisymmetry makes its two derivative terms equal, proving the cubic expression. Squaring the nonlinear term gives the quartic expression. Both vertices arise from the same gauge coupling, rather than independent free parameters.
