Riemannian volume form (dvolg​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 3
On an oriented Riemannian n-manifold, the volume form is the unique positive n-form taking value one on every positively oriented orthonormal frame. In positive local coordinates,
dvolg​=det(gij​)​dx1∧⋯∧dxn.
