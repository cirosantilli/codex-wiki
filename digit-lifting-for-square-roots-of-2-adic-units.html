Digit lifting for square roots of 2-adic units (xn+1​=xn​+εn​2n−1)

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field p-adic unit 2-adic unit
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
An odd square is congruent to one modulo eight. Conversely, for u≡1(mod8), begin with x3​=1 and xn2​≡u(mod2n). For n≥3, adding εn​2n−1 changes the square by εn​2nxn​ modulo 2n+1. Since xn​ is odd, one choice of εn​∈{0,1} gives the next congruence. The corrections tend to zero 2-adically, so completeness produces an odd square root. This directly proves the unit square criterion without needing a unit derivative in Hensel lemma.
