Control point (Pi​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Computer aided geometric design
OurBigBook.com Words: 161 Articles: 2
A control point is geometric data used to specify a curve or surface through a basis or refinement rule. In a linear representation C(t)=∑i​bi​(t)Pi​, moving Pi​ by h changes the curve by bi​(t)h. The support of bi​ is therefore precisely its influence region. A control point need not lie on the curve: Bézier curves interpolate their endpoint controls, whereas generic interior B-spline controls are approximated.
