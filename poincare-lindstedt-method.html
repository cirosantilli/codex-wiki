Poincaré-Lindstedt method

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Singular perturbation
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For a weakly perturbed harmonic oscillator, expand both its periodic solution and its angular frequency, using the stretched time τ=ωt. At each order, choose the frequency correction to remove fundamental-harmonic forcing that would produce a secular term. For a self-excited periodic orbit, cancellation of the independent sine and cosine terms can determine both the leading amplitude and the frequency correction. Actual existence follows when the associated Poincaré return map has a simple displacement zero, by the implicit function theorem.
