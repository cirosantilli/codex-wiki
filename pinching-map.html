Pinching map (P(ρ)=∑i​Pi​ρPi​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum theory Measurement in quantum mechanics Projective measurement Lüders rule Nonselective projective measurement
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For mutually orthogonal projectors summing to the identity, a pinching map discards coherences between their ranges while preserving each diagonal block. It is a quantum channel with Kraus operators Pi​, and a unital quantum channel. A state is fixed by pinching exactly when it commutes with every projector.
