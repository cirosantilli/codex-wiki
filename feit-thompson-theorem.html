Feit–Thompson theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
Every finite group of odd order is a solvable group. In particular, a nonabelian simple group has even order and therefore has an involution by Cauchy's theorem for finite groups. This is a deep theorem, not an elementary consequence of the Sylow theorems.
