Countably closed forcing (ω1​-closed)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Forcing Closed forcing
OurBigBook.com Words: 93 Articles: 2
Every descending countable sequence of conditions has a common stronger condition. This is ω1​-closed forcing and adds no countable ordinal sequences.
