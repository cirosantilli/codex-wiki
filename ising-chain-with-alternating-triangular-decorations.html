Ising chain with alternating triangular decorations (gM​=3M+1)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Spin model Ising model Antiferromagnetic Ising model
OurBigBook.com Words: 112
Take 2M base Ising spins on a ring and add one apex Ising spin over every other base bond. Give all base and apex bonds the antiferromagnetic energy Jss′ with J>0. There are 3M sites, and adjacent triangles are connected by a single undecorated bridge rather than sharing a corner. With K=J/(kB​T), summing the apex spins yields alternating spin-chain transfer matrices
Ast​=2e−Kstcosh[K(s+t)],Bst​=e−Kst,Z=Tr(AB)M.
(12)
Their common symmetric eigenvector gives the leading cell eigenvalue 3+e−4K+e−2K+3e2K. A minimum-energy triangle has one frustrated bond and energy −J; its connecting bridge can have energy −J. Requiring the bridge to be antiparallel leaves the counting transfer matrix (21​12​) on successive first base spins. Its eigenvalues 3,1 give ground-state degeneracy gM​=3M+1 and ground-state energy −2JM. The residual entropy per site is kB​ln3/3.
