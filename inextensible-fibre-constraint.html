Inextensible fibre constraint (∣Fm0​∣=1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Finite elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 269 Articles: 3
A unit reference fibre direction m0​ retains its length under this constraint on the deformation gradient. Its reaction contributes an undetermined tension along the current fibre direction, independently of the pressure reaction enforcing incompressibility.
