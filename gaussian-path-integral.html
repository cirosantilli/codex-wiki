Gaussian path integral (∫Dηei⟨η,Aη⟩∝(detA)−1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Path integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 2
A quadratic path integral becomes a product of Gaussian integrals after diagonalizing its fluctuation operator. One real coordinate per mode gives an inverse square root of the functional determinant; one complex coordinate, or two real coordinates, gives its inverse. A regulator and normalization are essential: determinant ratios are often meaningful before an absolute determinant is defined.
