Axial intensity gain of an amplitude zone plate (If​/I0​≃4No2​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Fresnel diffraction Zone plate
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
For uniform coherent plane-wave illumination, an ideal binary amplitude zone plate transmits alternate Fresnel half-period zones. If No​ complete odd zones are open, every one contributes approximately twice the unobstructed axial complex amplitude, in the same phase, and If​/I0​≃4No2​. This is a local light intensity gain relative to removing the plate and outer stop. It differs from comparison with a finite clear aperture: an aperture containing an odd integer number of half-period zones has axial intensity 4I0​, giving plate-to-aperture ratio No2​. Neither gain is a total power efficiency.
