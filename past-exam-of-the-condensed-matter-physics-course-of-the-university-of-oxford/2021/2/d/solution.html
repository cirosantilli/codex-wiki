Use the long-wavelength linear dispersion relation ω≃vL​∣k∣ as an estimate. The phonon energy is then
E=ℏω≃ℏvL​a2π​=0.5431×10−9mh(8433ms−1)​,E≃0.0642eV.​
(21)
This extrapolates the measured low-wavevector speed of sound to the X point, the first Brillouin zone boundary along [100]. It is not an exact phonon dispersion relation there: the discrete crystal structure generally bends the branch, and the initial slope alone cannot determine its boundary energy. In particular, (2π/a,0,0) is not a reciprocal lattice vector of the face-centered cubic lattice; the smallest such axial vector is (4π/a,0,0). There is therefore no reason for this acoustic phonon energy to vanish.
