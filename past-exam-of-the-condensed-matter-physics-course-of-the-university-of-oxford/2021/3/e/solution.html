For the degenerate electron gas, let Dg​=Am/(πℏ2) include both spin states. The Sommerfeld expansion gives
Ug​(T)−Ug​(0)=6π2​Dg​(kB​T)2,Cv​=3π2​Dg​kB2​T.
(34)
For the gapless symmetric intrinsic semiconductor, part (d) gave the same coefficient with Ds​=Am∗/(πℏ2). Thus for equal sample areas and equal spin and valley degeneracies,
Cv​Cvtot​​=mm∗​;Cv​Cvtot​​=1 if m∗=m.​
(35)
The equality often intended in this comparison requires the same density of states. The source sets me∗​=mh∗​ but does not explicitly equate them to the electron gas mass m. Without that additional assumption the ratio is m∗/m, or more generally Ds​/Dg​ if sample areas or degeneracy factors differ. For example, m∗=2m with equal areas satisfies the stated equal-effective mass condition in the semiconductor and gives ratio two. The filled Fermi sea does not add a factor Ne​/Nc​: only the thermal shell near its Fermi energy contributes to its heat capacity.
