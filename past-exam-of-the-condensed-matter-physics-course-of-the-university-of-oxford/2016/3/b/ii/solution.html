The 2 K sample is about 93.3% of its saturation magnetization, with about 96.6% of the moments in the aligned state. The two percentages describe different observables. A linear extrapolation would predict M/Ms​≃x=1.679, exceeding saturation and therefore contradicting the bounded two-state population.
For completeness, at this 2 K, 5 T bias the dimensionless secant susceptibility is approximately χ0​tanhx/x=0.01199, while the differential susceptibility is χ0​sech2x=0.002805 under the same prescribed-field approximation. Neither equals the zero-field Curie slope 0.02159. Thus saturation reduces the incremental response even while the magnetization itself is large.
