The phrase “fraction aligned” is ambiguous if it is used to mean net alignment. Half the moments are already in the parallel state at zero field, but the net magnetization is then zero because the antiparallel half cancels them. The table reports both the literal parallel-state population and the imbalance that produces magnetization. At this weak field even the 2 K sample is far from saturation, so the Curie approximation is accurate within this independent-spin model.
