For one spin-degenerate parabolic two-dimensional band, kF​=2πn​=1.3729×108 m−1. The free-electron model with m=me​ gives
EF​=me​πℏ2n​=7.182×10−4 eV=0.7182 meV,TF​=8.334 K.​
(20)
The given 103 Ω is a sheet resistivity, not a bulk resistivity in ohm-metres. The two-dimensional Drude sheet conductance is ne2τ/me​, so
τ=ne2ρ□​me​​=1.183×10−11 s.​
(21)
Thus vF​=ℏkF​/me​=1.589×104 m/s and
ℓF​=vF​τ=1.880×10−7 m=188 nm.​
(22)
The sample temperature is not given. This is specifically the Fermi-surface mean free path, appropriate in the degenerate regime T≪8.3 K for the bare-mass model. At higher temperatures the characteristic thermal speed should replace vF​ for a thermal transport distance. If a real two-dimensional band has mass m∗, the stated results scale as EF​,TF​∝1/m∗, τ∝m∗ and vF​∝1/m∗; the inferred ℓF​=ℏkF​/(ne2ρ□​) is mass independent under these same Drude assumptions. No semiconductor effective mass or valley degeneracy is silently introduced.
