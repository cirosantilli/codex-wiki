For the reciprocal lattice vector Ghkl​=(2π/a)(h,k,l), sum the atomic scattering factors with the atomic basis phase factors. Taking the positive phase convention gives the structure factor
Shkl​=fAu​(Ghkl​)+fCu​(Ghkl​)[eiπ(k+l)+eiπ(h+l)+eiπ(h+k)].
(3)
Changing the phase convention complex-conjugates the structure factor and leaves the diffraction wave intensity unchanged. Since the Miller indices are integers,
αhkl​=(−1)k+l+(−1)h+l+(−1)h+k.
(4)
If all three Miller indices have the same integer parity, all pairwise sums are even. Otherwise exactly one pairwise sum is even and two are odd. Consequently
αhkl​={3,−1,​h,k,l all even or all odd,mixed parity.​​
(5)
The corresponding structure factors are fAu​+3fCu​ and fAu​−fCu​. Reflections with mixed integer parity are superlattice reflections that reveal chemical ordering in alloys.
