Use the same atomic basis phases as in the ordered structure factor, replacing each atomic scattering factor by its site average. For the reflection with equal integer parity,
S111​=⟨fc​⟩+3⟨ff​⟩=fAu​+3fCu​.
(20)
Conservation of the total composition removes the alloy sublattice order parameter from this amplitude. For the reflection with mixed integer parity,
S110​=⟨fc​⟩−⟨ff​⟩=ρ(fAu​−fCu​).
(21)
The coherent powder diffraction wave intensities consequently satisfy
I111​(ρ)=I111​(1),I110​(ρ)=ρ2I110​(1).​
(22)
Here the lattice constant, atomic scattering factors, powder diffraction multiplicities, and omitted experimental factors are held fixed. The ratio of a superlattice reflection to a fundamental reflection can therefore measure the magnitude of the alloy sublattice order parameter.
