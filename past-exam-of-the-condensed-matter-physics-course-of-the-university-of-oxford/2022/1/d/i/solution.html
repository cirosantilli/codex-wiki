Specify the normalization of the alloy sublattice order parameter rather than assuming that its endpoint values uniquely define it. Let pc​ be the probability of gold occupation at a corner site and define
ρ=1−1/4pc​−1/4​,pc​=41+3ρ​.
(17)
The complementary copper probability is 3(1−ρ)/4, so the corner-site atomic scattering factor is
⟨fc​⟩=41​[(1+3ρ)fAu​+3(1−ρ)fCu​].​
(18)
The stoichiometry provides one gold chemical atom per four-site unit cell. If all three face sublattices are equivalent, their gold occupation probability pf​ obeys pc​+3pf​=1. Therefore
pf​=41−ρ​,1−pf​=43+ρ​,⟨ff​⟩=41​[(1−ρ)fAu​+(3+ρ)fCu​].​
(19)
At ρ=1 the corner and face sites carry pure gold and pure copper; at ρ=0 they have identical statistical compositions. These formulas describe the branch with 0≤ρ≤1 and equivalent face-site averages.
