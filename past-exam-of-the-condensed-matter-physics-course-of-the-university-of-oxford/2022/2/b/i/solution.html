For a long chain of length L with periodic boundary conditions, each spin has Ldk/(2π) quantum states in a wavenumber interval dk. Including two spin states, a full energy band contains 2L/a electrons. The state counting in a Bloch band therefore gives the energy density
U(ϵ)=π1​∫one zone​E−​(k)dk=aEZB​​∫−11​[1+y2−4y2+ϵ2​]dy.
(27)
The integral of 1+y2 is 8/3. For ϵ>0, the even square-root integral is
∫−11​4y2+ϵ2​dy=4+ϵ2​+2ϵ2​lnϵ2+4+ϵ2​​.
(28)
For example, this follows by setting u=2y, applying the supplied antiderivative, and evaluating both endpoints; the lower-end logarithm contributes lnϵ. Hence
U(ϵ)=aEZB​​[38​−4+ϵ2​−2ϵ2​lnϵ2+4+ϵ2​​].​
(29)
The continuous zero-coupling limit is U(0)=2EZB​/(3a). This agrees with directly integrating the one-dimensional free electron model up to kF​=π/a at two electrons per unit cell.
