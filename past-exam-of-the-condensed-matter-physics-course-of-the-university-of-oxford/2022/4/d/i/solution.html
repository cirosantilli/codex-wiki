 ... Condensed matter physics course of the University of Oxford Past exam of the condensed matter physics course of the University of Oxford 2022 4 d i
OurBigBook.com Words: 137
The given electron configuration has an inert closed [Xe] core and two 4f electrons. The Aufbau principle identifies the occupied subshells; the free-ion term within the open 4f2 configuration is then selected by Hund's rules. A closed core contributes L=S=0.
Each f electron has orbital angular momentum quantum number l=3. Place the two spins parallel, so S=1. The Pauli exclusion principle then requires distinct orbital projections. The largest allowed total projection is ML​=3+2=5, obtained from ml​=3 and ml​=2, giving the maximum allowed L=5. A larger ML​=6 would place two parallel-spin electrons in the same atomic orbital and is forbidden.
The f subshell holds fourteen electrons; f2 is less than half filled. The third of Hund's rules therefore selects J=L−S=4. Hence
L=5,S=1,J=4,ground term 3H4​.​
(70)
The term symbol uses 2S+1=3 and the spectroscopic letter H for L=5. The praseodymium ion retains an orbital contribution, so a spin-only estimate would be inappropriate.
