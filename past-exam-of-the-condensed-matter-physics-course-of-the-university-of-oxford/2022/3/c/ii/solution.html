These are graphical estimates, not high-precision fitted material parameters. To extract activation energies consistently with part (a), retain the temperature powers in the effective density of states factors.
On the semiconductor carrier freeze-out tail, put x=1/T. Then
log10​n=constant−43​log10​x−2kB​ln10Ei​​x.
(56)
For example, the right-hand graph gives roughly log10​[n/(m−3)]≃17.7 at x=0.10K−1 and 13.0 at x=0.30K−1. Subtract the known prefactor contribution before interpreting the slope:
Ei​≃2kB​ln100.20K−14.7−(3/4)log10​3​≃8.6×10−3eV.
(57)
Thus Ei​≃9meV​, to the accuracy of the drawing. Ignoring the weak prefactor gives about 9.3meV, a similar rough estimate.
For the intrinsic branch, use
lnn(T1​)n(T2​)​=23​lnT1​T2​​+2kB​Eg​​(T1​1​−T2​1​).
(58)
The left-hand graph gives n(100K)≃2.5×1021m−3 and n(500K)≃1.5×1024m−3, both far above ND​. Therefore
Eg​≃(1/100−1/500)K−12kB​[ln600−(3/2)ln5]​≃0.086eV,Eg​≃0.09eV.​
(59)
Here kB​=8.6173×10−5eVK−1. Omitting the T3/2 prefactor over this wide temperature interval would instead give approximately 0.14eV, so it is not a negligible correction for the band-gap extraction from intrinsic carrier activation. The source graph warrants an approximate narrow band gap, rather than several significant figures.
