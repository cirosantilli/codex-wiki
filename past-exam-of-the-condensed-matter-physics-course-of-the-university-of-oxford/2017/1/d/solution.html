The X-ray wavelength is λ=hc/E=1.54980×10−10m. Write the amplitude of one conventional unit cell as the face-centred cubic selection rule times the two-atom atomic basis amplitude:
Fhkl​=[1+eiπ(k+l)+eiπ(h+l)+eiπ(h+k)][fGa​+fAs​eiπ(h+k+l)/2].

The first bracket is four when the indices have equal parity and zero otherwise. Among the allowed Bragg reflections, the zinc blende structure factor therefore gives
∣Fhkl​∣2=16⎩⎨⎧​fGa2​+fAs2​,(fGa​+fAs​)2,(fGa​−fAs​)2,​h,k,l odd,h+k+l≡0(mod4),h+k+l≡2(mod4).​

Here the atomic scattering factors are taken as real. For the requested rough estimate set fGa​≃31 and fAs​≃33. The weak difference reflections remain allowed: unequal species do not have the extra exact extinction of the equal-species diamond limit.

The first intensity column is the geometry-independent multiplicity-and-structure estimate. The second additionally assumes an unpolarized beam and a common symmetric powder-diffractometer geometry, with Lorentz-polarization factor
LP(θ)∝sin2θcosθ1+cos2(2θ)​.

An overall constant cancels in either normalization. A particular measured intensity set is not fixed by the atomic numbers alone: actual atomic scattering factors decrease with momentum transfer, anomalous scattering and absorption can matter, and the instrument geometry and polarization determine angular corrections. The two columns state the approximation rather than asserting identical peak heights for all instruments.[ref]
