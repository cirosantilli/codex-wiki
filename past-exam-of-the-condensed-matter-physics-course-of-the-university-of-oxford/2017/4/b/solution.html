For an undoped neutral intrinsic semiconductor, n=p. Equating the two expressions from a and taking logarithms gives
lnNc​−kB​TEc​−μ​=lnNv​−kB​Tμ−Ev​​,
(37)
and hence the intrinsic-semiconductor chemical potential
μi​(T)=2Ec​+Ev​​+2kB​T​lnNc​Nv​​=2Ec​+Ev​​+43​kB​Tlnme∗​mh∗​​.​
(38)
Therefore α=43​ln(mh∗​/me∗​)​. The logarithm is temperature-independent for fixed parabolic effective masses. The chemical potential is exactly at midgap only if the two effective density of states values coincide; for mh∗​>me∗​ it moves above midgap. Additional valley multiplicities would add their logarithmic contribution.
