At 1000K, kB​T=0.08617eV, approximately 3.3 times this binding energy. Dilute donors are consequently expected to be largely ionized, rather than frozen out. At sufficiently high temperature, intrinsic electron-hole production can dominate the total carriers even though donors are ionized.
