Let μa​=2.13μN​ be the magnitude of the saturated nuclear moment of helium-3, with μN​=eℏ/(2mp​). A spin-one-half nucleus has the two Zeeman energies ∓μa​B. For independent nuclei the one-site partition function and magnetization density are
z=2cosh(kB​Tμa​B​),M=n3​μa​tanh(kB​Tμa​B​).
(51)
In the weak-field limit μa​B≪kB​T, expand the hyperbolic tangent:
χ3,nuc​=kB​Tμ0​n3​(2.13μN​)2​=3ukB​Tμ0​ρ3​(2.13μN​)2​.​
(52)
The nuclear moment's negative sign means that its moment points oppositely to its spin; it does not make the thermal magnetic susceptibility negative. The response is paramagnetism governed by Curie's law.
There is no extra factor 1/3 here: the stated 2.13μN​ is the moment of a fully polarized spin-one-half state. Equivalently, writing the moment operator as gN​μN​I requires gN​=−4.26, and gN2​μN2​I(I+1)/3=(2.13μN​)2. The formula presumes independent localized nuclear moments; exchange correlations invalidate it close to the much lower ordering scale discussed in part (e).
