Spherical law of sines (sinA/sina=sinB/sinb=sinC/sinc)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Spherical geometry Spherical trigonometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
For a nondegenerate convex minor spherical triangle, its scalar triple product τ=u⋅(v×w) satisfies ∣τ∣=sinbsincsinA, and cyclically. Dividing by sinasinbsinc proves the spherical law of sines.
