Homogeneous harmonic inversion (Δ(r−(2n+1)Hn​)=r−(2n+1)ΔHn​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Spherical harmonic Solid harmonic Irregular solid harmonic
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
A degree-n homogeneous polynomial satisfies Euler homogeneous function theorem r⋅∇Hn​=nHn​. The product rule gives Δ(rsHn​)=rsΔHn​+s(2n+s+1)rs−2Hn​ in three dimensions. Taking s=−(2n+1) gives the displayed identity and the equivalence of harmonicity on r>0. The origin is excluded; even 1/r has a distributional Dirac delta function source there.
