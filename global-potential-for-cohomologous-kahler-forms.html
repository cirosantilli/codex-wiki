Global potential for cohomologous Kähler forms (ω−ω=i∂∂ˉf)

OurBigBook.com Words: 61
On a compact Kähler manifold, the difference of cohomologous Kähler forms is a real, de Rham cohomology exact (1,1)-form. The ddbar lemma gives ω−ω=∂∂ˉh. Reality implies ∂∂ˉhˉ=−(ω−ω), so f=(h−hˉ)/(2i) is real and has the displayed potential equation. The difference of two real potentials is a pluriharmonic function, hence constant on each connected component. On a connected manifold the only ambiguity is one additive constant.
