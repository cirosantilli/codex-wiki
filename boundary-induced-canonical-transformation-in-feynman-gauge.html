In mostly-minus signature, adding the boundary term ∂μ​Kμ with Kμ=21​(Aν​∂νAμ−Aμ∂ν​Aν) to the subtracted Feynman gauge density changes πμ=−A˙μ to the displayed canonical momenta. For fields with vanishing spatial boundary terms, ∫K0d3x=∫A0​∂i​Ai​d3x, whose functional derivatives are these momentum shifts. They depend only on coordinates, so [Aμ​,πν]=iδμ​νδ3 is unchanged. The only potentially nonzero mixed momentum bracket is [π0(x),πi(y)]=i(∂yi​+∂xi​)δ3(x−y)=0. Thus both density representatives define canonical commutation relations, although their momenta must not be equated componentwise.
