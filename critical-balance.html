Critical balance (τnl​∼τwave​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Magnetohydrodynamic turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Critical balance is a scale-dependent ordering in which the wave-propagation time and nonlinear interaction time are comparable. For strong Alfvénic turbulence, k∥​vA​∼k⊥​δu⊥​. For electron magnetohydrodynamics, the linear rate is vA​di​k∥​k⊥​ and the nonlinear rate is vA​di​k⊥2​δB/B0​, so critical balance instead gives δB/B0​∼k∥​/k⊥​. This assumption is an estimate of decorrelation dynamics, not an exact equality at every point.
