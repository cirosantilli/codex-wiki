Self-paired spinless fermion mode (k≡−k(mod2π))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Canonical transformation of mode operators Bogoliubov transformation Nambu spinor
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
In a unit-spacing periodic spinless chain, self-paired momenta are k=0 and, when available, k=π. An anomalous pair of the same creation operator vanishes by the canonical anticommutation relations. Such a mode is a single normal number level, filled or empty according to its energy sign, rather than a two-distinct-mode Bogoliubov transformation. This excludes the paired-angle construction at those special momenta.
