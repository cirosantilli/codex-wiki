Connected union with a connected hub

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Connected space Connected subset
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
Suppose connected subsets Uj​ all meet one connected member U1​. A separation of their union would place U1​ entirely on one side. Each Uj​ is also entirely on one side and intersects U1​, so every Uj​ lies on the same side, contradicting nonempty separation. Thus the union is connected. If the subsets are path-connected, join two points by a path to U1​, a path inside U1​, and a path back out. This needs pairwise contact with the hub, not one point shared by every subset.
