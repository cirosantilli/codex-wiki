The Structure of Some Crystals as Indicated by their Diffraction of X-rays by William Lawrence Bragg (1913 publication)

OurBigBook.com Tags: Work by William Lawrence Bragg Words: 92
By William Lawrence Bragg, credited as W. L. Bragg. Published in Proceedings of the Royal Society of London, Series A 89, 248–277 (1913). The first page records receipt on 21 June and reading on 26 June 1913. The communication credit to William Henry Bragg is distinct from authorship.
DOI: doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1913.0083
Inspected scan: scispace.com/pdf/the-structure-of-some-crystals-as-indicated-by-their-1ugl7pojiz.pdf
Cited passages: p. 248, authorship and communication credits; pp. 262–263, Figures 8–9 and Tables VII–IX, sodium chloride sections, orientations, thickness comparison and spot intensities; pp. 264–265, Figure 10, interpenetrating face-centered arrays and the remaining atomic-assignment uncertainty. The historical scattering-weight interpretation is not modern X-ray scattering theory.
