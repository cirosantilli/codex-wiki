Bath cooling followed by insulated equilibration (T∞​=L1​∫0L​T(x,tb​)dx)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Diffusion equation Heat equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
Let a homogeneous plate 0<x<L begin at uniform temperature T0​, cool with zero-temperature Dirichlet boundary conditions until time tb​, then switch to insulated Neumann boundary conditions. For thermal diffusivity κ>0, the Fourier sine series during cooling is T=(4T0​/π)∑n≥1, n odd​sin(nπx/L)e−κ(nπ/L)2t/n. Integrating the insulated heat equation shows that its integral average is conserved. All nonconstant Fourier cosine series modes then decay, proving that the final uniform temperature is T∞​=(8T0​/π2)∑n≥1, n odd​e−κ(nπ/L)2tb​/n2. Its limits are T0​ at zero bath time and zero at infinite bath time. Instantaneous changes of boundary condition can create initial-boundary corner incompatibilities without invalidating the positive-time solution.
