The fixed-design nonparametric regression model observes Yi​=m(xi​)+σϵi​ at deterministic design points xi​. For independent and identically distributed random variables ϵi​ with the standard normal distribution, comparing two mean functions has Kullback-Leibler divergence ∑i​(m1​(xi​)−m0​(xi​))2/(2σ2).
