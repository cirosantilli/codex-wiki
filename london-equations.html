London equations

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Magnetic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 16
The London equations relate the superconducting electric current to the electromagnetic field: ∂t​J=E/(μ0​λL2​) and ∇×J=−B/(μ0​λL2​). In a gauge with constant condensate phase the second follows from J=−A/(μ0​λL2​); it implies exponential magnetic field screening.
