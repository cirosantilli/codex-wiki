Taylor–Goldstein equation

The Taylor–Goldstein equation governs linear two-dimensional disturbances of an inviscid, vertically sheared, stably stratified parallel flow. For horizontal phase velocity c, base velocity U(z), buoyancy frequency N(z), and horizontal wavenumber k,
