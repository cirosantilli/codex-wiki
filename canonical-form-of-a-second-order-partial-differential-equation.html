Canonical form of a second-order partial differential equation (Uξη​,Uξξ​+Uηη​,Uξξ​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Linear partial differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 330 Articles: 2
For a second-order real linear partial differential equation in two variables, an invertible local change of variables simplifies its principal part. At regular points, the hyperbolic case has mixed-derivative principal part Uξη​, the elliptic case has principal part Uξξ​+Uηη​, and the parabolic case has rank-one principal part Uξξ​, after multiplication by nonzero factors. Lower-order terms and forcing must also be transformed with the chain rule; simplifying only the principal part does not in general give the full canonical equation. In the hyperbolic partial differential equation case the new variables can be chosen constant along the two characteristic families.
