The complex Hahn-Banach theorem extends every bounded complex-linear functional on a subspace to the whole normed space with exactly the same norm. Let d=dist(x0​,M)>0 and define on M+Cx0​
g0​(y+λx0​)=λd.

(F∘)∘​=spanF​.​

The dual-side double annihilator corresponds to weak-star closure, which need not equal norm closure.
