There is a further issue: no L1 function has exactly this Fourier series. Here is a direct proof using strongly lacunary polynomial L1-L2 bound. For a trigonometric polynomial p supported on {±j!:j≥3}, consecutive positive frequencies have ratio at least four. Every zero sum of four signed frequencies pairs into cancelling equal frequencies: a largest unpaired frequency would exceed the sum of the other three.
