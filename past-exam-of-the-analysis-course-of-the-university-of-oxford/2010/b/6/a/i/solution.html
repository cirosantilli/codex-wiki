For finite subsets F⊂Z, orthonormality gives
​∑n∈F​αn​en​​2=∑n∈F​∣αn​∣2.
(33)
For SN​=∑∣n∣≤N​αn​en​, the squared norm of SN​−SM​ is the corresponding finite tail sum. Since ∑n∈Z​∣αn​∣2<∞, these tails tend to zero. Thus (SN​) is a Cauchy sequence in the complete Hilbert space and converges.
The same estimate for arbitrary finite tails proves that the sum is unconditional, so the bilateral orthogonal series converges in X independently of its ordering. This is the Riesz-Fischer theorem for an orthonormal system.
