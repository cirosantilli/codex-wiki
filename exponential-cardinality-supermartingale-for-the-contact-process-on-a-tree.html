Exponential cardinality supermartingale for the contact process on a tree (νρ​(A)=ρ∣A∣)

For the contact process with per-edge infection rate λ and recovery rate one on a degree-(d+1) regular tree, the infinitesimal generator gives Lνρ​(A)=(1−ρ)νρ​(A)(∣A∣/ρ−λ∣∂E​A∣). The edge boundary of a finite forest in a regular tree implies Lνρ​≤0 whenever ρλ(d−1)≥1, including the absorbing empty state where the generator is zero. Thus the displayed functional is a bounded supermartingale, and the extinction probability from A is at most ρ∣A∣.
