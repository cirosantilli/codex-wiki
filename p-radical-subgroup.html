p-radical subgroup (D=Op​(NG​(D)))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Finite p-group p-subgroup
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A p-subgroup is p-radical if it equals the p-core of its normalizer. Every defect group of a block is p-radical by defect groups are centralizer-conjugate Sylow intersections. Being an intersection of arbitrary Sylow subgroups alone is weaker than the centralizer-conjugate statement used in that argument.
