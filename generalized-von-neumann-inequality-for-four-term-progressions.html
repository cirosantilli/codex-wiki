Generalized von Neumann inequality for four-term progressions (∣Ex,d​∏j=03​fj​(x+jd)∣≤minj​∥fj​∥U3​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 254 Articles: 2
For functions bounded by one on a cyclic group of prime order greater than three, three applications of Cauchy-Schwarz bound the four-term arithmetic progression average by the smallest Gowers U3 norm of its factors. Zero extension yields the corresponding interval bound with an absolute factor. This makes Gowers U3 norm a measure of uniformity relevant to four-term arithmetic progression counts.
