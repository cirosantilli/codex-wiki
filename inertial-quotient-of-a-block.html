Inertial quotient of a block (E(B)=NG​(D,bD​)/(DCG​(D)))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Modular representation theory Block of a group algebra Defect group of a block
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
For a maximal Brauer pair (D,bD​) of a block of a group algebra, the inertial quotient is the displayed quotient of its pair stabilizer by DCG​(D). It has order coprime to p, and its order is the inertial index of a block. For abelian D, conjugation embeds it in Aut(D).
