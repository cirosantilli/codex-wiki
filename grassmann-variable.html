Grassmann variable

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Exterior algebra Grassmann algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
A Grassmann variable is an odd generator θ of a Grassmann algebra. In characteristic zero it obeys θ2=0, so functions of finitely many Grassmann variables have finite Taylor expansions.
