Reusing LU factors for multiple right-hand sides (AX=B,LY=PB,UX=Y)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Numerical linear algebra LU decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 186
When a fixed matrix A is used with several forcing or measurement vectors, compute its Pivoted LU decomposition once. Collect r right-hand sides into the columns of B=(b(1) ⋯ b(r)), and the corresponding solutions into X. Then
AX=B,LY=PB,UX=Y.
(82)
Each column undergoes the same row permutation, forward substitution and back substitution. Neither L, U nor P depends on B. This is the useful separation between preparing a linear system and applying its solution procedure repeatedly. LAPACK implements it by taking previously computed factors, permuting all right-hand sides, and performing the two triangular solves.[ref]
For the unpivoted factors in Computed three-by-three LU decomposition, take
B=​72326​024​46−8​​,Y=​796​02−2​4−22​​,X=​123​01−1​2−11​​.
(83)
Direct matrix multiplication checks all three identities LY=B, UX=Y and AX=B. The first column reproduces the previous solve; the other two use exactly the same factors. New right-hand sides can also arrive one at a time. If A changes, these stored factors generally need to be recomputed.
