Smoothed matter density variance (σ2(M,z)=2π21​∫0∞​k2P(k,z)∣W(kR)∣2dk)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Linear cosmological density perturbation Density contrast
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
The smoothed matter density variance measures the root-mean-square linear density contrast after applying a window corresponding to a Lagrangian mass scale. For a homogeneous isotropic field it is σ2=(1/(2π2))∫k2P(k)∣W(kR)∣2dk. The variance convention, growth epoch and smoothing mass-density relation must agree with the collapse barrier used in a halo abundance calculation.
