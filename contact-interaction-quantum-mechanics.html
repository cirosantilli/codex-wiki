In a one-dimensional identical-particle model, a contact interaction has two-body potential U(x,x′)=gδ(x−x′), using the Dirac delta distribution. For bosons, its second quantization is the displayed normally ordered Hamiltonian operator. Projection onto orthonormal modes gives Hint​=(g/2)∑ijkl​Iijkl​ai†​aj†​ak​al​, where Iijkl​=∫φi∗​φj∗​φk​φl​dx. The overlap integrals determine which mode-changing processes survive. A finite-mode model describes that projection; discarded modes can still matter to the full physical system.
