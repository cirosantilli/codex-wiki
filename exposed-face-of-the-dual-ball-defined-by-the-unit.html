Exposed face of the dual ball defined by the unit (SA​={x∈BA∗​:Rex(1A​)=1})

 ... Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra C-star algebra Positive functional on a C-star algebra State on a C-star algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
For a nonzero unital C-star algebra, a positive functional on a C-star algebra obeys ∥x∥=x(1A​), and conversely this equality for a bounded functional implies positivity. Therefore its state on a C-star algebra functionals are exactly the maximizers of the real linear evaluation x↦Rex(1A​) on the closed dual unit ball. If a convex combination reaches that maximum, each constituent must reach it: each real part is at most one, and their weighted mean is one. The modulus bound then forces evaluation to equal one, so each constituent is again a state. Hence the state space is an exposed face and a face of a convex set.
