Free subgroups of every finite rank in the free product of Z and C2

OurBigBook.com Words: 153
Let G=⟨x,y∣y2=1⟩. For each n≥2, a free basis of a subgroup of rank n is
{xn−1} ∪ {xjyxyx−j:0≤j≤n−2}.
(31)
A covering-space proof uses r=n−1 spheres double-covering the real projective plane, with antipodal points aj​,bj​ over the wedge point. Add lifted x edges in a cyclic order through the aj​, and an x loop at each bj​. This is a connected 2r-sheeted cover of a circle wedged with a projective plane. After collapsing one connecting arc in each simply connected sphere, its fundamental group is that of a graph with r vertices and 2r edges, hence free of rank r+1.
A spanning tree along the first r−1 cyclic edges gives the displayed words: the closing cycle is xr, and the loop reached through aj​,bj​ is xjyxyx−j. Injectivity of the induced fundamental-group map of a covering map proves they form a free basis in G.
