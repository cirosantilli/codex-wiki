Estimation of a beta shape parameter (θ=−n/∑i​logXi​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Beta distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 116
For an independent statistical sample from the beta distribution with parameters (θ,1), θ>0, the probability density function is θxθ−1 on (0,1). Its raw moments are EXr=θ/(θ+r) for r>−θ, by direct integration. Consequently the method of moments gives T1​=Xˉ/(1−Xˉ), whereas the maximum-likelihood estimator is T2​=−n/∑i​logXi​.
The second formula follows by setting the score function n/θ+∑i​logXi​ to zero; its derivative is negative. Also −logXi​ has an exponential distribution of rate θ, since P(−logXi​>u)=e−θu. The central limit theorem and delta method give
n​(T1​−θ)d​N(0,θ+2θ(θ+1)2​),n​(T2​−θ)d​N(0,θ2).
(17)
Thus the asymptotic relative efficiency of T1​ relative to T2​ is 1−(θ+1)−2. The transformed sum has a gamma distribution of shape n and rate θ, which also gives ET2​=nθ/(n−1) for n>1 and Var(T2​)=n2θ2/[(n−1)2(n−2)] for n>2.
