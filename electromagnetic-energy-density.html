Electromagnetic energy density (u=21​(E⋅D+B⋅H))

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Poynting vector Poynting theorem Electromagnetic energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
In a linear isotropic nondispersive medium with time-independent permittivity ϵ and magnetic permeability μ, the stored electromagnetic energy per volume is u=ϵ∣E∣2/2+∣B∣2/(2μ). For a short derivation, the Maxwell equations in matter and the divergence identity for a cross product give ∇⋅(E×H)=−H⋅∂t​B−E⋅∂t​D−Jf​⋅E. The constitutive relations turn the first two terms into −∂t​u, proving the Poynting theorem. Choose u=0 when the electric field and magnetic field both vanish. Dispersive or time-dependent material response requires a different accounting of stored energy.
