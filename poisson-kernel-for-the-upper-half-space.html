Poisson kernel for the upper half-space (Pz​(u,v)=z/[2π(u2+v2+z2)3/2])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Poisson's equation Poisson kernel
OurBigBook.com Words: 802 Articles: 11
In three dimensions the Poisson kernel for the upper half-space is
Pz​(u,v)=2π(u2+v2+z2)3/2z​,z>0.
(36)
It gives the harmonic function ϕ(x,y,z)=∫R2​Pz​(x−ξ,y−η)f(ξ,η)dξdη for compatible boundary data. The method of images constructs the zero-boundary Dirichlet Green function by subtracting the reflected-source Green function of the Laplacian from the source kernel. On the boundary plane the outward unit normal is −ez​, and −∂n​G=Pz​, giving the integral formula through Green's second identity.
The kernel is positive and has integral one: in polar coordinates this is z∫0∞​r(r2+z2)−3/2dr=1. Its mass outside any fixed disk tends to zero as z↓0. It is therefore an approximate identity, recovering bounded boundary data at continuity points. At a jump across a smooth curve, the vertical limit gives the average of the two adjacent values; assigning a different value only on that curve does not change the harmonic extension.
