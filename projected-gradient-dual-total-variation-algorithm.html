Minimize F(p)=21​∥g−D∗p∥22​ over P={p:∣pi​∣2​≤λ} using
pk+1=ΠP​(pk+τD(g−D∗pk)).
(22)
The pointwise Euclidean projection onto a convex set sends a block r to r/max(1,∣r∣2​/λ). The gradient of F has Lipschitz constant ∥D∥2, so finite-dimensional projected gradient descent converges for 0<τ<2/∥D∥2. On an unscaled square grid with N>1, 0<τ<1/4 is safe. Reconstruct uk=g−D∗pk; unused dual directions may make the minimizing p nonunique while the primal minimizer is unique.
