Bottom-heavy spherical-cell orientation dynamics (p˙​=B−1(k−p3​p)+ω×p/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biological fluid dynamics Gyrotaxis
OurBigBook.com Words: 345 Articles: 4
For a spherical cell with its centre of mass displaced by −ℓg​p from its geometric centre, gravity produces torque mgℓg​p×k. Rotational resistance ζr​ gives angular velocity ω/2+(mgℓg​/ζr​)p×k. Taking its cross product with p gives the displayed orientation equation with B=ζr​/(mgℓg​). Steady orientation therefore requires the two terms on the right to sum to zero; placing the vorticity term alone on the other side changes its sign.
