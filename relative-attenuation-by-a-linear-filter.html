Relative attenuation by a linear filter (η=∣H(ωn​)∣/∣H(ωs​)∣)

 Home Electrical engineering Signal processing Linear filter
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For a linear time-invariant system, a sinusoidal wanted signal at ωs​ and an unwanted sinusoidal component at ωn​ acquire their separate frequency response factors. The ratio of their output amplitudes, divided by their input amplitude ratio, is η=∣H(ωn​)∣/∣H(ωs​)∣. An amplitude attenuation factor of F=1/η corresponds to a factor F2 in squared-amplitude power ratios under a common impedance convention. The unwanted component's own absolute attenuation is ∣H(ωn​)∣, which need not equal its attenuation relative to the wanted signal.
