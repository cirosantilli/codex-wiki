For a linear filter with chosen nonzero reference voltage gain H0​, define its effective noise bandwidth by
Bn​=∣H0​∣21​∫0∞​∣H(f)∣2df.
(59)
This is the width of an ideal rectangular linear filter with gain magnitude ∣H0​∣ passing the same mean square of input white noise. Consequently
vo,rms​=∣H0​∣4kB​TRBn​​.
(60)
Use the full voltage transfer from the open-circuit voltage source to the readout, including any electrical load, when applying this formula. A nominal signal bandwidth stated between two cutoff frequencies does not specify the integral: gradual tails and a nonuniform passband matter. For an ideal rectangular unit-gain passband [f1​,f2​], Bn​=f2​−f1​. For an unloaded RC low-pass filter with H0​=1 and cutoff frequency fc​=1/(2πRC),
Bn​=∫0∞​1+(f/fc​)2df​=2π​fc​=4RC1​.
(61)
The last equality follows by substituting x=f/fc​ and integrating 1/(1+x2) as arctanx. Using the nominal fc​ as the noise bandwidth underestimates the variance by a factor π/2, or the root mean square voltage by π/2​.
