Multivariable Taylor expansion (f(a+h)=f(a)+Df(a)h+21​hTHf​(a)h+O(∥h∥3))

For a scalar function with continuous third derivatives near a, apply the one-dimensional Taylor theorem to g(t)=f(a+th). The chain rule gives g′(0)=Df(a)h and g′′(0)=hTHf​(a)h, proving the displayed formula. Here Hf​ is the Hessian matrix. The finite polynomial part is the Multivariate Taylor polynomial; the remainder controls its accuracy.
