Bregman divergence (DJp​(u,v))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Inverse problem Regularization of an inverse problem Variational regularization
OurBigBook.com Words: 341 Articles: 5
For a convex functional J and p∈∂J(v), the Bregman divergence is
DJp​(u,v)=J(u)−J(v)−⟨p,u−v⟩.
(28)
It is nonnegative but need not be symmetric or satisfy the triangle inequality.
