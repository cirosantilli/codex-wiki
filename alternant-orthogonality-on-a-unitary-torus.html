Alternant orthogonality on a unitary torus (∫T​Aκ​Aη​​dt=n!δκη​)

 ... Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Highest-weight representation Weyl character formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 99
For strictly decreasing integer tuples κ,η, let Aκ​(z)=det(ziκj​​) on the diagonal maximal torus of the unitary group. Expanding both determinants, Fourier orthogonality makes the integral zero unless the exponent tuples have the same permutation orbit. Strict decreasing order makes that equivalent to κ=η; the n! matching permutation terms then each contribute one. An alternating Laurent polynomial is a finite linear combination of these alternants. For an irreducible character χλ​, its highest monomial gives coefficient one for Aλ+δ​ in χλ​Aδ​, with δ=(n−1,…,0). The Weyl integration formula and Schur orthogonality relations force the sum of squared coefficients to be one, so all other coefficients vanish. This proves the unitary Weyl character formula directly.
