Crista

 Home Biology Biological cell Organelle Mitochondria Inner mitochondrial membrane
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
A crista is a fold of the inner mitochondrial membrane into the mitochondrial matrix. Its membrane remains continuous with the surrounding inner mitochondrial membrane; it is not a separately inserted sac like a thylakoid.[ref]
The diagram idealizes access through the fold's opening. Connectivity alone does not imply instant mixing or identical concentrations throughout the intermembrane space. Narrow geometry and molecular interactions can matter to transport. Increasing surface area also does not, by itself, prove a proportional increase in ATP production: the supply of substrates, available oxygen and the state of the coupling machinery must also support the flux.
