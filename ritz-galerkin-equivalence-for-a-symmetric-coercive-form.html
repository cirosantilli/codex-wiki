Ritz-Galerkin equivalence for a symmetric coercive form (J(v)−J(u)=21​a(v−u,v−u))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Finite element method Rayleigh-Ritz method
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 1
For a bounded symmetric coercive bilinear form on a Hilbert space, a solution of a(u,w)=ℓ(w) minimizes J(v)=a(v,v)/2−ℓ(v). The displayed identity proves strict minimality and uniqueness. Restricting to a conforming finite element space makes the discrete Ritz method equivalent to the Galerkin method. Galerkin orthogonality gives exact best approximation in the energy norm; the Céa lemma translates it into a reference-norm estimate.
