Contravariant power-object functor (P:Eop→E)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Elementary topos Power object
OurBigBook.com Words: 207 Articles: 3
An arrow f:A→B induces Pf:PB→PA by inverse image of predicates. Transposition of predicates on A×B gives E(A,PB)≅E(B,PA) and the adjunction Pop⊣P. The induced monad on the topos is double power-object formation; it is distinct from the covariant powerset monad on sets.
