Beta barium borate

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Borate Barium borate
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The low-temperature crystalline phase of barium borate, used as a nonlinear optical crystal. A properly phase-matched and compensated source can use it for spontaneous parametric down-conversion; the choice of collected modes and compensation determines the resulting polarization entanglement.
