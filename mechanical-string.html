Mechanical string

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A slender flexible body modeled by a moving curve that transmits tension while its bending stiffness is neglected. For a uniform stretched string with constant tension T and linear mass density μ, small transverse oscillations obey μytt​=Tyxx​; see the wave equation on a string.
