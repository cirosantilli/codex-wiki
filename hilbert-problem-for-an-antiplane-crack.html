Hilbert problem for an antiplane crack (G++G−=−2p)

 ... Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Antiplane shear Complex stress potential for antiplane shear
OurBigBook.com Words: 387 Articles: 4
For a crack on the real axis under applied shear traction p, write G=μF′ for the additional complex stress potential for antiplane shear derivative. Reflection of the induced displacement gives G−=G+. Traction-free crack faces therefore require G++G−=−2p; intact portions have G+=G−. This is a scalar Riemann-Hilbert problem. Endpoint regularity, single-valued displacement and the absence of additional remote loads fix its homogeneous terms.
