Quantum phase

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state
OurBigBook.com Words: 278 Articles: 5
Multiplying a quantum state by one global complex phase does not change any measurement probability; relative phases between components can affect interference.
The global phase is common to the whole state; relative phases control interference between state components.
