Electrostatic induction

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge
OurBigBook.com Words: 128 Articles: 2
Electrostatic induction is redistribution of electric charge in response to an external charged object. An isolated electrical conductor can acquire positive and negative surface regions while its total electric charge stays fixed; see the contact control in electric charge.
