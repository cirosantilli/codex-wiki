Uniform-source vacuum functional and spacetime volume (F=Vd​μdf(λ,m2/μ2,h/μ3))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Generating functional Normalized vacuum generating functional
OurBigBook.com Words: 120
A translation-invariant connected generating functional with a constant external source is extensive in spacetime volume Vd​. Its total logarithm is dimensionless, while its density has mass dimension d. Consequently its dimensionless representation at fixed physical volume must retain μdVd​. For a free massive scalar with source coupling μ−ϵ/2hϕ, completing the square gives F=Vd​μ−ϵh2/(2m2). This already disproves a literal rewriting of the full uniform-source logarithm using only λ,m2/μ2,h/μ3 at fixed volume. In infinite volume the total functional is generally infinite; use the density, a source of compact support, or an explicit volume regulator. After writing the density as μdf, its renormalization group equation has the engineering term +df. Ultraviolet subtraction alone does not establish infrared finiteness for a massless constant source.
