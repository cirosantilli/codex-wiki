Preorder traversal

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Tree (graph theory) Depth-first traversal of a tree
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
Visit a rooted tree's root before recursively visiting its child subtrees in their specified order. For a binary tree, the order is root, left subtree, right subtree. Unlike inorder traversal, this interpretation stores an internal node's label before either child's labels.
