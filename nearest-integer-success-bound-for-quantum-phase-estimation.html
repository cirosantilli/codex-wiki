Nearest-integer success bound for quantum phase estimation (Pr(nearest phase label)≥4/π2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum Fourier transform Quantum phase estimation
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
For a unitary operator eigenphase ϕ, an n-qubit quantum phase estimation register of size L=2n has output probability amplitude
am​(ϕ)=L1​∑x=0L−1​eix(ϕ−2πm/L).
(55)
Choose the nearest integer label m to Lϕ/(2π), with labels understood modulo L, and put d=Lϕ/(2π)−m, taking the representative ∣d∣≤1/2. The finite geometric series gives ∣am​∣=∣sin(πd)∣/[L∣sin(πd/L)∣]. For 0<∣d∣≤1/2, concavity of sine gives ∣sin(πd)∣≥2∣d∣, while ∣sin(πd/L)∣≤π∣d∣/L. Thus the probability of the nearest label is at least 4/π2. At d=0 it is exactly one. If two labels tie for nearest, each satisfies the bound.
