Dimensionless quantity (dimq=1)

 Home Physics Physical quantity
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 1
A dimensionless quantity has zero exponent for every base physical dimension. It can be formed by dividing two quantities with the same dimensions, such as x=r/R or q=m/M. Its value is independent of the choice of units for those dimensions. The quantities GM2/R and Lt both have dimensions of energy, so RLt/(GM2) is dimensionless. A dimensionless variable is such a quantity used as an unknown or independent variable in an equation.
