Momentum loss from uniform potential-vorticity mixing (⟨δU⟩=−βW2/12)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Potential vorticity Potential-vorticity evolution equation Potential-vorticity conservation
OurBigBook.com Words: 152 Articles: 1
If quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity initially has gradient β across a channel of width W and is homogenized, a height-independent change of zonal velocity obeys δQ=−δUy​. Requiring δU(0)=δU(W)=0 gives uniform final potential vorticity equal to its original channel mean, δU=βy(y−W)/2, and channel-mean momentum change −βW2/12. The negative change for β>0 is wave drag associated with mixing, not a loss of total momentum in an isolated unforced system.
