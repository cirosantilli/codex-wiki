Higgsino (H)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supersymmetry breaking Soft supersymmetry breaking Sparticle
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
A higgsino is the Weyl spinor partner of a Higgs scalar field in a chiral superfield. After a supersymmetric Higgs mechanism, its combination along a broken gauge direction mixes with the gaugino in the resulting massive N=1 vector multiplet.
