Suppose both extensions were inconsistent. By the explosion argument in (i), the extension by ϕ proves ¬ϕ, and the extension by ¬ϕ proves ϕ. The deduction theorem for propositional logic therefore yields
Γ⊢ϕ→¬ϕ,Γ⊢¬ϕ→ϕ.
(15)
The identity theorem gives ⊢¬ϕ→¬ϕ and ⊢ϕ→ϕ. Apply E to the first displayed logical implication and the identity logical implication with antecedent ¬ϕ to obtain Γ⊢¬ϕ. Apply E to the identity logical implication with antecedent ϕ and the second displayed logical implication to obtain Γ⊢ϕ. This contradicts propositional consistency of Γ.
Consequently at least one of the two one-formula extensions is propositionally consistent. The formal proof uses the source's own calculus, including its rule E, rather than assuming an unproved completeness theorem for this presentation.
