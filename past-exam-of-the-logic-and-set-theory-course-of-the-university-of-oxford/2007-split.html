This University of Oxford Part B paper is cross-listed for Mathematics, Mathematics and Computer Science, Mathematics and Philosophy, and Mathematics and Statistics. Section A concerns mathematical logic and section B concerns set theory. [ref] The archive contains a complete editable five-page transcription. Bounded official-source searches found no advertised answers to this exact paper. All 29 source leaves and the attached unnumbered tasks are covered below. In Q7(v), the convergence condition is retained exactly as printed, without adding absolute-value bars.
