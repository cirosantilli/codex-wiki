For each term t define an L2​-formula Et​(z) expressing its value. Use fresh bound variables at every step:
Ex​(z):=(z=x),Ec​(z):=Q(z),Ef(t)​(z):=∃y[Et​(y)∧P(y,z)].
(12)
Freshness includes avoiding all relevant free variables of the formula being translated. Define the atomic translation by
σ(t1​=t2​):=∃u∃v[Et1​​(u)∧Et2​​(v)∧u=v],
(13)
and extend through the logical operations: σ(¬θ)=¬σ(θ), σ(θ→η)=σ(θ)→σ(η), and σ(∀xθ)=∀xσ(θ). Fresh witnesses prevent variable capture and ensure the translation preserves the original free-variable set.
For an L1​-structure A, let Ar have the graph relation of fA and singleton relation {cA}. Induction on terms proves that Et​(z) holds precisely when z is the value of t. Induction on formulas then proves
A,s⊨α⟺Ar,s⊨σ(α).
(14)
Conversely every L2​-structure satisfying ϕ,ψ is uniquely the relational version of an L1​-structure on that same domain. Hence, for arbitrary possibly infinite Γ, semantic entailment on the left is equivalent to semantic entailment from σ(Γ)∪{ϕ,ψ} on the right, with assignments retained in the free-formula case. Applying soundness theorem for first-order logic and Gödel's completeness theorem gives
Γ⊢α⟺σ(Γ)∪{ϕ,ψ}⊢σ(α).​
(15)
The prohibition on completeness in the preceding direct equality question is local to that proof; here it supplies the clean semantic-to-syntactic transfer after the actual translation and model correspondence have been established.
