First eliminate implication and biconditional using the logical equivalences
A→B≡¬A∨B,A↔B≡(¬A∨B)∧(¬B∨A).

Push negations inwards using De Morgan's laws and eliminate double negations. Structural induction leaves only literals joined by conjunction and disjunction.
Now induct on that remaining expression. A literal is already in conjunctive normal form. A conjunction of two conjunctive normal forms is again one after associating its clauses. For a disjunction, write the two inductively obtained forms as ⋀i​Ci​ and ⋀j​Dj​ and use distributivity:
(i⋀​Ci​)∨(j⋀​Dj​)≡i,j⋀​(Ci​∨Dj​).​
(6)
Each Ci​∨Dj​ is a clause. All operations preserve logical equivalence, proving the required form exists.
