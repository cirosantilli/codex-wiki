The new system's first axiom is an old axiom, its replacement axiom is derivable in the old system by part (iii), modus ponens rule is shared, and its deduction rule is admissible in the old system by the theorem allowed in the source. Induction on a new-system derivation therefore translates it into an old-system derivation with the same assumptions.
The reverse logical implication is false for the displayed systems. Take Γ=∅ and let ϕ be an instance of the old contraposition axiom, for example with distinct propositional variables in place of α,β. It is an old-system theorem, but the matrix countermodel in part (ii) refutes its derivability in the new system. Thus the requested two-way equivalence cannot be concluded from these printed axioms.
