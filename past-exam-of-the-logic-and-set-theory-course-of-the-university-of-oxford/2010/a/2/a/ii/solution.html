The requested derivation does not follow from the printed axiom set. Use a two-valued matrix interpretation with ordinary material logical implication and designated value 1, but interpret the unary symbol ¬ by the constant function N(0)=N(1)=0.
Every instance of the first axiom α→(β→α) is valid. In the replacement axiom, both N(β)→α and N(α)→β have value 1, so its whole logical implication is valid as well. Modus ponens rule preserves the designated value. The deduction rule is sound for semantic consequence under this interpretation because logical implication is the ordinary truth-functional logical implication. Induction on derivations, allowing arbitrary sets of assumptions, therefore makes every theorem valid in this matrix.
