In the Henkin construction, first extend the consistent sentence set to a complete consistent theory with witnesses in an expanded language containing fresh constants. Its term model has closed terms modulo provable equality as elements:
[t]=[u]⟺T⊢t=˙u.
(13)
Functions are interpreted by applying their symbols to representative terms, and an atomic predicate holds of term classes exactly when the corresponding atomic sentence belongs to T. Equality axioms make these definitions independent of representatives. The Henkin witness property handles the existential step in the truth lemma, so the resulting structure satisfies T and hence the original Σ.
An arbitrary sentence set need not already have enough closed terms or witnesses. Calling this a term model for Σ means a term-model construction of a model satisfying Σ, usually through that witnessed completion; it does not assert that every arbitrary Σ itself already supplies the construction.
