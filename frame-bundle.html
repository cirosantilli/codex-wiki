Frame bundle (Fr(TM))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Principal bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
The frame bundle of an n-dimensional smooth manifold has fiber over x consisting of linear isomorphisms u:Rn→Tx​M. Right composition by GL(n,R) makes it a principal bundle. A global frame of a vector bundle is a global section of a fiber bundle and trivializes the tangent bundle. A metric restricts the allowed frames to the orthonormal frame bundle.
