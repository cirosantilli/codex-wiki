A Fab fragment contains one antibody binding site, allowing comparison with the two-site intact IgG in the antibody surface plasmon resonance experiment.
A Fab fragment combines one complete immunoglobulin light chain with one immunoglobulin heavy chain VH​,CH1​ portion, including antibody constant regions.[ref]
The single paratope makes a Fab fragment useful for separating antibody affinity from two-arm avidity. If an intact IgG attaches strongly to a surface but its matched Fab fragments detach more readily, loss of the second arm is a candidate explanation even if the individual paratope is unchanged. Comparing the formats asks a structural question; it does not by itself establish which binding mechanism dominates.
