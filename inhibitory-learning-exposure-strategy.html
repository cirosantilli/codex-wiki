Inhibitory-learning exposure strategy

 Home Science Psychology Clinical psychology Cognitive behavioral therapy Exposure therapy
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
A strategy intended to strengthen learning that competes with a threat association and improve its later retrieval. Expectancy testing and varied contexts are examples. A coherent learning rationale does not itself demonstrate the optimal clinical implementation for every developmental group.[ref]
