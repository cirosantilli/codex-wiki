Hall bar

 ... Electromagnetic field tensor Lorentz force Hall effect Quantum Hall effect Integer quantum Hall effect von Klitzing-Dorda-Pepper quantum Hall experiment
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
A Hall bar is a strip with current contacts at its ends and additional voltage contacts along its sides. Opposite-side contacts measure a transverse electric potential difference; same-side contacts at different longitudinal positions measure a longitudinal electric potential difference. Separate voltage leads reduce the influence of current-contact electrical resistance when their input electric currents are negligible.
