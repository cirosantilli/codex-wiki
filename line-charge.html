Line charge (μ=dQ/dℓ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Charge density
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A line charge is a charge distribution idealized as lying on a curve, described by charge per unit length. An infinite straight line with constant density μ produces the radial electric field Er​=μ/(2πε0​r) by Gauss's law, and a logarithmic electrostatic potential.
