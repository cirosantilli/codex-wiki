Phonon scattering

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Bose-Einstein statistics Bose-Einstein distribution Phonon
OurBigBook.com Words: 239 Articles: 3
Phonon scattering changes the occupation or propagation of lattice-vibration normal modes through interactions with boundaries, defects or other phonons. Anharmonic terms permit phonon-phonon scattering. Different processes need not relax heat flow equally: normal processes conserve total crystal momentum within the first Brillouin zone, whereas Umklapp scattering changes its representative by a nonzero reciprocal lattice vector.
