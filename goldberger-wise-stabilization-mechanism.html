The Goldberger–Wise stabilization mechanism generates a radion potential in the Randall–Sundrum model using a weakly backreacting bulk scalar field. On 0≤y≤L, its static profile obeys χ′′−4kχ′−mχ2​χ=0, hence χ=Ae(4+ϵ)ky+Be−ϵky, where ϵ=4+mχ2​/k2​−2. Boundary potentials approximately fix χ(0)=v0​, χ(L)=vL​. For ϵ≪1 and kL≫1, the leading radius-dependent potential is proportional to e−4kL(vL​−v0​e−ϵkL)2. Its leading minimum satisfies kL≃ϵ−1log(v0​/vL​). Thus a modest ratio of boundary values can generate a large warp exponent. Corrections shift this minimum; the approximation requires small scalar field backreaction, and the four-dimensional cosmological constant still needs adjustment.
