Posterior predictive distribution (p(y∗∣y))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Bayesian posterior
OurBigBook.com Words: 185 Articles: 2
The distribution of a new or missing quantity integrates its model over the Bayesian posterior: p(y∗∣y)=∫p(y∗∣ϕ,y)p(ϕ∣y)dϕ. Both parameter and residual uncertainty should enter predictive draws used for multiple imputation.
