Normalized vacuum generating functional (Z[J]=Z[J]/Z[0])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Generating functional
OurBigBook.com Words: 207 Articles: 2
For a vacuum generating functional Z[J], normalization gives Z[J]=Z[J]/Z[0] and Z[0]=1. Functional derivatives of Z give normalized vacuum expectation values. The linked-cluster theorem shows that this ratio cancels Vacuum Feynman diagrams, while −ilogZ[J] gives connected correlators. Components attached to external insertions are not removed merely by the normalization.
