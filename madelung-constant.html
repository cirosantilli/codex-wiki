Madelung constant (αM​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 83
The Madelung constant, named for Erwin Madelung, is a dimensionless geometrical factor in the electrostatic binding of an ionic crystal. For a neutral lattice of electric charges ±e with nearest unlike-ion distance r0​, Coulomb's law gives the potential energy per ion pair as −αM​e2/(4πϵ0​r0​). At a positive reference site, αM​=−∑j=0​zj​r0​/rj​, with neighboring electric charges zj​e. Both attraction and repulsion enter this lattice sum; summing with a neutral-bulk prescription is essential. Short-range overlap repulsion and other contributions must be added before inferring a stable spacing or a thermodynamic lattice energy.
