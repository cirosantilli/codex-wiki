Exoplanet occurrence rate (fB​=⟨NB​⟩)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
The mean number of planets per surveyed star in a specified parameter bin. It need not equal the fraction of stars hosting at least one such planet. Inferring it from transits requires accounting for geometric transit probability, transit survey completeness and false positives.
