Borel hierarchy (Σα0​,Πα0​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Sigma-algebra Borel set
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For a topological space, Σ10​ is the class of open sets and Πα0​ consists of complements of Σα0​ sets. For countable α>1, Σα0​ consists of countable unions of sets in ⋃β<α​Πβ0​. These countable levels contain every Borel set. Continuous inverse images preserve each level, by induction using openness, complementation and countable unions.
