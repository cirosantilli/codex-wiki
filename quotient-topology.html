Quotient topology

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 254 Articles: 5
For an equivalence relation R on a topological space X, the quotient topology on the set X/R declares U⊆X/R open exactly when its inverse image under the projection q:X→X/R is open in X.
