Electronic heat capacity (Cmel​≃γT)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Heat capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 324 Articles: 6
Electronic heat capacity is the contribution from electronic excitations to heat capacity. A simple normal metal with a smooth density of states near its Fermi energy has Cmel​≃γT well below its electron Fermi temperature. Only a narrow energy interval near the Fermi energy changes occupation on heating, explaining the small fraction of active electrons. The Debye T-cubed test separates this contribution from lattice phonons. Interactions, a superconductivity transition or other electronic structure can invalidate a constant γ description.
