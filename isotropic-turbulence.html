Isotropic turbulence

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Homogeneous turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 759 Articles: 18
Isotropic turbulence has statistics invariant under rotations as well as translations. With no mean velocity, its two-point velocity correlation tensor depends on separation length and on longitudinal/transverse directions, and its one-component velocity variances are equal.
