Spline interpolation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation Spline (mathematics) Spline approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 297 Articles: 4
Spline interpolation matches prescribed function values at distinct sites by an element of a finite-dimensional spline space. In a B-spline basis its existence and uniqueness for all data are equivalent to invertibility of the B-spline collocation matrix. Distinct ordered sites satisfying the Schoenberg–Whitney theorem give this condition.
