Residual-versus-fitted plot

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Normal linear model Linear regression
OurBigBook.com Words: 82 Articles: 1
A residual-versus-fitted plot displays residuals vertically against predicted responses horizontally. Curvature suggests an incorrect conditional-mean model; a fan-shaped spread suggests heteroscedasticity, while isolated large residuals may indicate unusual observations. It does not alone measure leverage or influence: regression leverage records unusual predictor combinations, and Cook's distance combines leverage and residual size.
