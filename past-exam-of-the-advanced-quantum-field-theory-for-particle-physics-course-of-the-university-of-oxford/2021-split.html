Use natural units, the Minkowski metric g=diag(1,−1,−1,−1) and the Feynman i-epsilon prescription. In question 1 use dimensional regularization with d=4−2ϵ and write Cϵ​=1/(16π2ϵ); in the alternative convention d=4−ϵ, replace Cϵ​ by 1/(8π2ϵ). Only ultraviolet poles are retained there.
