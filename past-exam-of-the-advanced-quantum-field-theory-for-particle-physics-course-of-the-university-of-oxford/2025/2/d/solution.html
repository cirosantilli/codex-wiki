In the right panel of part a, p enters the fermion line, p′=p+q leaves it, and a virtual photon of momentum k bridges the external photon vertex. Write Γμ=γμ+Λμ and F1​=1+F1(1)​+⋯. The one-loop amplitude is
−ieΛμ=​∫k​(−ieγα)(p′−k)2−m2+i0i(p′−k+m)​(−ieγμ)(p−k)2−m2+i0i(p−k+m)​(−ieγβ)k2+i0−igαβ​​,Λμ=−ie2∫k​[k2+i0][(p′−k)2−m2+i0][(p−k)2−m2+i0]γα(p′−k+m)γμ(p−k+m)γα​​.​​
(30)
This Feynman integral has a logarithmic superficial degree of divergence. Its ultraviolet pole is independent of the external momenta and m, because every such numerator insertion or denominator correction lowers the large-k degree. Preserve an auxiliary positive mass M while extracting the ultraviolet pole; setting everything to zero would create a scaleless integral and conceal the cancellation of ultraviolet and infrared divergences.
The leading numerator is γαkγμkγα​. Symmetric integration replaces kρ​kσ​ by gρσ​k2/d. Two contractions of gamma matrices then give
γαkγμkγα​ ⟼ d(d−2)2​k2γμ,tr[γαkγμkγα​γμ​]=4(d−2)2k2.
(31)
For the auxiliary-mass extraction of ultraviolet poles,
JM​=∫k​(k2−M2+i0)3k2​=∫k​[(k2−M2+i0)21​+(k2−M2+i0)3M2​],JM​​UV​=16π2ϵi​.
(32)
The second term is ultraviolet convergent. Thus
4d1​tr(Λμγμ​)​UV​=−ie2d(d−2)2​JM​​UV​,F1(1)​(q2)​UV​=16π2ϵe2​.​
(33)
The ultraviolet pole of the QED vertex is therefore Λμ∣UV​=e2γμ/(16π2ϵ). The Pauli structure has zero trace against γμ​ and no ultraviolet pole at this order.
Form-factor qualification. The two electron electromagnetic vertex form factors in the source describe matrix elements between external on shell Dirac spinors. A general off shell vertex has more structures, so the unprojected trace is not a projector for the complete finite on-shell F1​. It does correctly isolate the divergent local γμ coefficient, which is all that is required here.
