The proper quark-gluon vertex colour factors come from two one-particle-irreducible Feynman diagrams, shown in the upper-middle and upper-right panels. One has a virtual gluon joining the open quark line on opposite sides of the external gluon insertion. The other has two virtual gluons meeting the external gluon at a three-gluon Feynman vertex, with their other ends joined by an internal quark. A Faddeev-Popov ghost field has no direct quark coupling, so it supplies no additional one-loop Feynman vertex graph here.
Let Ta be the Hermitian generators of the fundamental representation of SU(N), with tr(TaTb)=δab/2, [Ta,Tb]=ifabcTc, ∑b​TbTb=CF​1, and fabcfdbc=CA​δad. Here CF​=(N2−1)/(2N) and CA​=N are quadratic Casimir operators evaluated in the fundamental representation and the Adjoint representation.
For the first graph, the ordered product is ∑b​TbTaTb. Expand the double commutator:
∑b​[Tb,[Tb,Ta]]=2CF​Ta−2∑b​TbTaTb=CA​Ta.
(15)
Thus its colour factor is
b∑​TbTaTb=(CF​−2CA​​)Ta=−2N1​Ta=−61​Ta(N=3).​
(16)
For the second graph choose the orientation of the three-gluon Feynman vertex so that its group tensor is fabc and the ordered product along the quark line is TbTc. Antisymmetry removes the anticommutator part:
fabcTbTc=21​fabc[Tb,Tc]=2iCA​​Ta=23i​Ta(N=3).​
(17)
The corresponding real group coefficient is CA​/2=3/2 after the conventional factor of i is incorporated into the remaining Feynman rules. If the vertex is written with ifabc, its raw matrix product is instead −CA​Ta/2. Reversing the ordered product reverses its sign together with the orientation-dependent three-gluon Feynman rule; these conventions describe the same scattering amplitude. External-leg corrections and local counterterms are separate from these two proper one-loop graphs.
