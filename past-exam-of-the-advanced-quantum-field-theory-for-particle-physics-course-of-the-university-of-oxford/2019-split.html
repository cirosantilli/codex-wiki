The bundle filename says 2018, but the paper's cover establishes Trinity Term 2019, 24 April 2019, and formal code A15272W1. These are solutions to that 2019 paper. Use natural units, the Minkowski metric g=diag(1,−1,−1,−1), and the Feynman i-epsilon prescription. Write p=γμpμ​. In question 1, D=4−ϵ, gD​=μϵ/2g, and the amputated gluon self-energy insertion is iΠμν,ab. This fixes the overall sign and factor of i in the results below; defining the insertion as −iΠ instead reverses all corresponding definitions of Π. The quadratic Casimir operator in the Adjoint representation is CA​=Nc​ for SU(Nc​), hence CA​=3 in QCD.
