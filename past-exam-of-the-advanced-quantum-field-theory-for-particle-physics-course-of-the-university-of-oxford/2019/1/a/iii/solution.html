There is one four-gluon tadpole diagram. Temporarily replace its internal gluon propagator by −igρσ​δcd/(l2−mg2​+i0). Contract the supplied four-gluon Feynman vertex with gρσ​δcd. Its first colour factor vanishes because fcce=0. Each of the other two terms contributes CA​δab(D−1)gμν, so
δcdgρσ​V4μνρσ,abcd​=−2igD2​CA​δab(D−1)gμν.
(7)
Include the symmetry factor 1/2 and the factor −i from the internal gluon propagator:
iΠ4gμν,ab​=−gD2​CA​δab(D−1)gμνJ1​(mg2​).
(8)
The exact regulated tadpole integral is
J1​(mg2​)=−(4π)D/2i​Γ(1−D/2)(mg2​)D/2−1.
(9)
In particular,
iΠ4gμν,ab​​UV​=−8π2ϵig2CA​δab​(D−1)mg2​gμν.
(10)
The entire expression is proportional to (mg2​)1−ϵ/2 and vanishes as the auxiliary mass tends to zero near four dimensions. Equivalently, its expanded terms are proportional to mg2​/ϵ, mg2​logmg2​ and mg2​, all of which disappear when the regulator is removed. Hence
Π4gμν,ab​=0(mg​→0).​
(11)
This is the massless scaleless integral result obtained directly in dimensional regularization. The artificial mass is only an intermediate device for this graph; retaining it in the full theory would modify the gauge symmetry and cannot be used when applying the massless transversality identity in part (iv).
