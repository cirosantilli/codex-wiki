Gauge invariance and power counting permit the same local operators as in the original renormalizable action. With renormalized fields, a convenient counterterm parametrization is
Lct​=−δZϕ​(Dμ​ϕ)†Dμϕ−δM2ϕ†ϕ−4μϵδλ​(ϕ†ϕ)2−4δZA​​Fμν​Fμν.​
(1)
Here Dμ​=∂μ​−iμϵ/2eAμ​. Expanding the first term supplies kinetic, scalar-scalar-photon, and seagull counterterms with the relative coefficients required by the Ward identity. Equivalently introduce separate vertex constants first and then impose that identity. For ϕ0​=Zϕ1/2​ϕ, A0​=ZA1/2​A, and e0​=μϵ/2Ze​e, it gives Ze​ZA1/2​=1. Thus charge renormalization can be represented through the photon field factor, rather than adding an independent gauge-noninvariant interaction.
The mass coefficient defined here is δM2=Zϕ​m02​−m2; it must be distinguished from m02​−m2. Gauge fixing may require the corresponding gauge-parameter redefinition, and vacuum diagrams can require an additive vacuum-energy counterterm. No photon mass counterterm is allowed by the unbroken gauge symmetry. The quartic scalar counterterm remains necessary for the full theory even at a scale where the renormalized λ is zero, because gauge interactions generate scalar four-point divergences.
