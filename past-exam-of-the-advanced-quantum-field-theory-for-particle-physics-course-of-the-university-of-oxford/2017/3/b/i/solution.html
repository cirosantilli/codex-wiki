∂ϕk​∂ϕi​∂2V​​v​(Ta)ij​vj​+∂ϕi​∂V​​v​(Ta)ik​=0.

Whenever Tav=0, this is a nonzero vector in the kernel of the scalar mass-squared matrix, so it is a zero-eigenvalue direction. It is tangent to the degenerate vacuum orbit. In a global theory it is a Goldstone direction; in the gauged theory the corresponding would-be Goldstone field supplies a longitudinal polarization of a massive vector.
For the specific potential, stationarity at a nonzero vacuum gives v2=m2/(4λ), assuming m2>0 and λ>0. Its Hessian is
(Mϕ2​)ij​=(−m2+4λv2)δij​+8λvi​vj​=8λvi​vj​.
