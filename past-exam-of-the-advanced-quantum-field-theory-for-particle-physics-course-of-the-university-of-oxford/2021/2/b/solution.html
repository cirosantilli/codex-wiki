There are exactly four tree-level Feynman diagrams: one four-photon contact graph and one exchange graph for each of the three partitions 12∣34, 13∣24 and 14∣23 of the external legs. The exchange graphs contain two cubic Feynman vertices. A fermion loop or a closed Faddeev-Popov ghost line has positive loop order, so neither enters here.
Write p1​+p2​+p3​+p4​=0 and take physical photon polarization vectors εi​ with pi​⋅εi​=0 and pi2​=0. At the cubic Feynman vertex attached to legs i,j, the two terms containing pi​⋅εi​ or pj​⋅εj​ vanish. If q=pi​+pj​ flows into the internal line, the remaining factor at that end is
−2λqμ(εi​⋅εj​).
(23)
At the other end the internal incoming four-momentum is q rather than −q, giving +2λqν(εk​⋅εl​). Multiplication by the photon propagator gives
iMij∣kl​=(−2λqμ)q2−igμν​​(2λqν)(εi​⋅εj​)(εk​⋅εl​)=4iλ2(εi​⋅εj​)(εk​⋅εl​).
(24)
The cancellation holds first at generic nonzero channel Mandelstam invariants and then by continuation at degenerate kinematics. Summing the three exchange graphs produces 4iλ2P, where
P=(ε1​⋅ε2​)(ε3​⋅ε4​)+(ε1​⋅ε3​)(ε2​⋅ε4​)+(ε1​⋅ε4​)(ε2​⋅ε3​).
(25)
The quartic Feynman vertex contributes −4iλ2P. Therefore
Mγγ→γγtree​=0.​
(26)
This is a cancellation for physical on shell photon polarization vectors. The off shell four-photon correlation function need not vanish. Physical light-by-light scattering first occurs through charged-particle loop Feynman diagrams.
