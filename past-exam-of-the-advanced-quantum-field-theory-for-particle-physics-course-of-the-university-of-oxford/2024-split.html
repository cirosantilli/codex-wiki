Solutions to all seventeen lettered parts. Use natural units, the Minkowski metric gμν​=diag(1,−1,−1,−1), Feynman slash notation p=γμpμ​, and the Feynman i-epsilon prescription in internal propagators. Define the scattering amplitude by Sfi​=δfi​+i(2π)4δ(4)(pf​−pi​)Mfi​. The gauge covariant derivative convention in question 3 is specified there to match the printed three-boson Feynman rule.
