The tree-level Feynman diagrams are the two ordered quark-exchange graphs and the s-channel gluon-exchange graph with a three-gluon vertex. There is no two-gluon-two-quark contact term in the ordinary QCD Lagrangian density. Let q=k1​+k2​=p1​+p2​, rt​=p1​−k1​, ru​=p1​−k2​, and s=q2, t=rt2​, u=ru2​. Define iM as the product of the stated Feynman rules.
Suppressing external colour indices, the quark graphs are
Mtμν​=−g2uˉ(p1​)TaTbγμtrt​​γνv(p2​),Muμν​=−g2uˉ(p1​)TbTaγνuru​​γμv(p2​).
(7)
The massless Dirac equations give uˉp1​=0 and p2​v=0. Consequently
tuˉk1​rt​k2​v​=−uˉk2​v=uˉk1​v,uuˉk2​ru​k1​v​=−uˉk1​v.
(8)
The last equality uses uˉqv=0. The quark-exchange sum is therefore −g2uˉk1​[Ta,Tb]v.
For the gluon graph, use all incoming momenta (k1​,k2​,−q) in the three-gluon tensor:
Vμνρ=(k1​−k2​)ρgμν+(k2​+q)μgνρ−(q+k1​)νgρμ.
(9)
Its double contraction is k1μ​k2ν​Vμνρ=s(k2​−k1​)ρ/2. The vertex and gluon propagator give
Msμν​=−sig2fabc​uˉγρ​TcvVμνρ,k1μ​k2ν​Msμν​=ig2fabcuˉk1​Tcv.
(10)
Combining the graphs yields
k1μ​k2ν​Mμν​=−g2uˉ(p1​)k1​([Ta,Tb]−ifabcTc)v(p2​).​
(11)
Thus the requested spinor proportionality factor is −g2uˉ(p1​)k1​v(p2​) when the colour matrix is displayed separately; retaining it inside the spinor bilinear makes its external colour indices explicit. For a convention calling the Feynman-rule product itself M, rather than iM, the entire result gains one factor of i.
