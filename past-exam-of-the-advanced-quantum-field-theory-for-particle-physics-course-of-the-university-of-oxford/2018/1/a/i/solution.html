Let ϕ0​=Z2​​ϕ, A0μ​=Z3​​Aμ​, and define the renormalized coupling by e0​=eD​Z1​/(Z2​Z3​​). Write Z2​m02​=m2+δm2 and Z22​λ0​=μϵ(λ+δλ). The complete renormalized Lagrangian density, with its counterterms already included, is
L=​Z2​∂μ​ϕ∗∂μϕ−(m2+δm2)ϕ∗ϕ−4μϵ(λ+δλ)​(ϕ∗ϕ)2−4Z3​​F2−ieD​Z1​Aμ​[ϕ∗∂μϕ−(∂μϕ∗)ϕ]+eD2​Z4​A2ϕ∗ϕ+Lgf​.​
(1)
Separating off the classical renormalized terms leaves the counterterm Lagrangian
Lct​=​δ2​∂μ​ϕ∗∂μϕ−δm2ϕ∗ϕ−4μϵδλ​(ϕ∗ϕ)2−4δ3​​Fμν​Fμν−ieD​δ1​Aμ​[ϕ∗∂μϕ−(∂μϕ∗)ϕ]+eD2​δ4​Aμ​Aμϕ∗ϕ,Zi​=1+δi​.​​
(2)
The seagull vertex factor is thus 2ieD2​Z4​gμν. The bare covariant derivative fixes Z4​=Z12​/Z2​, rather than supplying an unrelated fourth charge parameter. The scalar QED Ward identity, for a symmetry-preserving regularization, gives Z1​=Z2​ and hence Z4​=Z2​.
For an invertible quadratic gauge field operator, also include Lgf​=−(∂⋅A)2/(2ξ). One may choose ξ0​=Z3​ξ, so this term needs no independent counterterm. The strict transverse limit is ξ→0. Even if the renormalized quartic coupling is set to zero at one scale, its counterterm need not vanish: photon graphs generate a four-scalar interaction. The renormalized Lagrangian density must retain this allowed operator.
