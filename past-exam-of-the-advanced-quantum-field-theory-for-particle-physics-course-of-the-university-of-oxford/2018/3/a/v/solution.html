The massive radial scalar changes the magnitude of the vacuum field; the massless Goldstone boson changes its orientation. The symmetry is global here, so no gauge field eats that mode.
The genuinely new scalar cubic interactions are −λvh3−λvhπ2. Including identical-field factorials, their Feynman vertex factors are
hhh: −6iλv,hππ: −2iλv.​
(35)

There are no derivative interactions from the linear Cartesian field shift.
