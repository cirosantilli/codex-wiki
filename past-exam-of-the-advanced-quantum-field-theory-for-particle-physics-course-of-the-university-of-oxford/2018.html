For the proper four-point interaction vertex, retain one-particle-irreducible Feynman diagrams with two labelled external photons and the two ends of a directed scalar line. Put C for a cubic scalar–photon vertex and S for a seagull vertex. The one-loop counting identities require VC​+2VS​=4. There are three possibilities, giving the nine proper scalar-QED seagull-vertex diagrams:
  • Four cubic vertices: two box graphs, with scalar-line order Cγ1​γ2​C or Cγ2​γ1​C, where the end vertices are joined by the internal photon.
  • Two cubic vertices and one seagull: one triangle with order CSC, both external photons on the middle vertex; two with order SCC, distinguished by which external photon is on the seagull; and two with order CCS, with the same two choices.
  • Two seagulls: two bubble graphs with order SS, distinguished by the external-photon assignment.
Each graph has Feynman-diagram symmetry factor one with these external labels: the internal photon and scalar are different species, and the charged scalar endpoints cannot be exchanged. The list is complete because the internal photon must join the ends of the open scalar chain for the graph to remain connected after any one internal line is cut. If it joined two interior points, an outside scalar segment would be a bridge and the graph would be one-particle reducible.
External-leg self-energy insertions contribute to an unamputated four-point correlation function, not this proper vertex. The local four-point counterterm from i is included separately when constructing the renormalized answer; it is not another one-loop topology. Abelian Faddeev-Popov ghosts do not interact in this linear gauge and add no diagram.

The double-longitudinal Ward identity for gluon fusion uses the defining Lie algebra identity [Ta,Tb]=ifabcTc makes the boxed amplitude vanish:
k1μ​k2ν​Mμν​=0.​
(12)
In QED, both external gauge bosons are photons, the charge matrices commute, and there is no three-photon vertex. The two fermion-exchange graphs then cancel without a third graph. In QCD, omitting the three-gluon interaction would leave a generally nonzero colour commutator: gauge-boson self-interaction is essential to the cancellation.
This is a tree-level manifestation of a Ward identity and, more generally in a gauge-fixed non-Abelian theory, the Slavnov-Taylor identities. They connect the matter representation, the structure constants and the gauge interactions, ensuring that auxiliary longitudinal polarizations do not enter physical probabilities. With all other external legs physical and on shell, replacing one external massless gauge-boson polarization by its momentum leaves zero. The double-contraction result proved here is one consequence, not by itself a proof that every open-index amplitude tensor is transverse off shell. At loop order the corresponding non-Abelian identities also involve Faddeev-Popov ghosts and a consistent symmetry-preserving renormalization.

Db​(p)∼−ξMA2​i​⟶0.
(41)
The angular field becomes infinitely heavy in this gauge and is absent from the unitary gauge propagating description. The qualification about fixed momentum matters: loop integrations probe arbitrarily large p, so one cannot automatically interchange the ξ→∞ limit with ultraviolet integration. If gv=0, there is no massive Higgs phase and the asserted heavy-field limit does not follow. A separate radial Higgs boson may remain; its mass depends on the unspecified potential and is not determined by this gauge-fixing term.
