Couple a nondynamical background gauge field Bμ​ to the Dirac current, choosing a plus sign for the source term:
Z[B]=N∫Dψˉ​Dψexp{i∫d4x[ψˉ​(i∂−m)ψ+Bμ​ψˉ​γμψ]}.​

There is no integration over Bμ​. The gauge covariant derivative is ∂μ​−iBμ​, so the simultaneous transformations are
ψ′=eiα(x)ψ,ψˉ​′=ψˉ​e−iα(x),Bμ′​=Bμ​+∂μ​α.
(7)
The kinetic term changes by −jμ∂μ​α and the source term changes by +jμ∂μ​α. The action is therefore invariant, exactly for finite α as well as infinitesimally. Changing integration variables and using the invariant functional measure from part c proves the background gauge invariance of a current generating functional:
Z[B+∂α]=Z[B].​
(8)
The allowed transformations preserve the vacuum boundary conditions. Its infinitesimal version is ∂μ​δZ/δBμ​=0. Because Bμ​ is only a source, this statement needs no gauge fixing or photon kinetic term. The absence of a vector gauge anomaly is essential to the change-of-variables argument.
