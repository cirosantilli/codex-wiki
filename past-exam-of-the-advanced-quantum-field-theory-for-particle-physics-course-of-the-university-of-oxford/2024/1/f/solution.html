This is the chiral flavour symmetry of massless Dirac fields. Its eight Noether currents are jL,Aμ​=ℓ†σˉμTAℓ and jR,Aμ​=r†σμTAr. The sum jR​+jL​ is the vector current, and jR​−jL​ is the axial current, for each flavour generator. This statement concerns the free theory in flat spacetime; an interacting theory can have a chiral anomaly.
