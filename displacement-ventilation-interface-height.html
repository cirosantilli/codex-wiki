Displacement-ventilation interface height (h)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Natural ventilation Displacement ventilation
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
At steady state, the displacement-ventilation interface lies where the total plume volume flux equals the imposed ventilation flux. For one triangular-profile wall line plume with kinematic buoyancy flux B0​, entrainment coefficient α, and ventilation flux A per unit span,
h=(3α2​)2/3B01/3​A​.
