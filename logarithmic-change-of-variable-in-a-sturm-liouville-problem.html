Logarithmic change of variable in a Sturm-Liouville problem (t=logx,x2y′′+xy′=Y′′(t))

 ... Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Linear ordinary differential equation Second-order linear differential equation Cauchy-Euler equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 256 Articles: 2
For a Cauchy-Euler equation, logarithmic coordinates turn scale changes in x>0 into translations in t. With Y(t)=y(et), the chain rule gives xy′=Y′ and x2y′′=Y′′−Y′. Consequently the Sturm-Liouville operator −x2y′′−xy′−cy becomes −Y′′−cY. Its natural weighted inner product has measure dx/x=dt, so this change of variable also preserves the norm when the transformed problem uses ordinary integration in t. On 1<x<eℓ with Dirichlet boundary conditions, the orthonormal eigenfunctions are 2/ℓ​sin(nπlogx/ℓ) and the eigenvalues are (nπ/ℓ)2−c, for n≥1.
