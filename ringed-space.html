Ringed space

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 124
A ringed space (X,OX​) is a topological space X equipped with a sheaf of rings OX​. A morphism consists of a continuous map f:X→Y and a compatible morphism f−1OY​→OX​.
