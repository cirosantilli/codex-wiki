Integrability (∫∣f∣dμ<∞)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Lebesgue integrable function
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
A real or complex measurable function is integrable for a measure μ when its absolute value has finite integral. For a random variable, this is integrability with respect to its probability measure, and makes its expectation finite.
