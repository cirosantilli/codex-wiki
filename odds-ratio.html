Odds ratio (OR)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling
OurBigBook.com Words: 242 Articles: 3
The odds ratio is the ratio of two event odds. For rare events it is close to the corresponding risk ratio, because p/(1−p)≃p when p is small.
