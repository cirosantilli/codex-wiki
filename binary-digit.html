Binary digit (bj​∈{0,1},n=∑j​bj​2j)

A binary digit is a zero or one in the base-two positional representation of an integer. The sum s2​(n)=∑j​bj​ determines the Thue–Morse sequence and is the Hamming weight of the binary representation.
