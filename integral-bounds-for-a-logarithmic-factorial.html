Integral bounds for a logarithmic factorial (NlnN−N+1≤ln(N!)≤(N+1)ln(N+1)−N+1−2ln2)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Asymptotic analysis Asymptotic equivalence Stirling formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
For integer N≥2, write ln(N!)=∑k=2N​lnk. Monotonicity gives ∫k−1k​lnxdx≤lnk≤∫kk+1​lnxdx. Summing and evaluating the endpoint integrals proves the displayed bounds. They also hold with equality when N=1. The lower integral starts at one and the upper at two; confusing those endpoints loses the constant in the upper bound. The bounds imply ln(N!)=NlnN−N+O(lnN), while Stirling's formula determines the logarithmic and constant corrections.
