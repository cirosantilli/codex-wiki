Kitamasa algorithm

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear recurrence relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
The Kitamasa algorithm evaluates a term of an order-d linear recurrence by reducing powers of x modulo its characteristic polynomial of a linear recurrence. If the recurrence relation is an+d​=∑j=0d−1​cj​an+j​, reduce xN modulo xd−∑j=0d−1​cj​xj by exponentiation by squaring; the resulting coefficients give aN​ as a linear combination of a0​,…,ad−1​. Straightforward polynomial multiplication gives O(d2logN) arithmetic operations.
