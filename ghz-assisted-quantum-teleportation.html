GHZ-assisted quantum teleportation (∣χs,t,c​⟩B​=XtZs⊕c∣χ⟩)

Share a GHZ state on A,B,C and let the unknown input Q be ∣χ⟩. Alice applies CNOT from Q to A, then a Hadamard gate to Q, and measures Q,A computationally, giving s,t. Charlie measures C in the Hadamard basis, giving c. Bob's conditional state is
XtZs⊕c∣χ⟩,
(6)
up to a global phase. Alice's two bits and Charlie's one bit therefore specify Bob's inverse correction Zs⊕cXt.
To prove the phase rule, write ∣χ⟩=α∣0⟩+β∣1⟩. After Alice's result, BC is α∣tt⟩+(−1)sβ∣(1⊕t)(1⊕t)⟩. Taking Charlie's Hadamard basis partial inner product multiplies its second term relative to its first by (−1)c. The remaining common phase is (−1)ct, which is physically irrelevant. All eight result triples have probability 1/8. Charlie's record localizes a known-phase Bell pair, allowing the standard Bell-basis teleportation identity to complete the transfer.
