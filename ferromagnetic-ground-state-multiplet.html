Ferromagnetic ground-state multiplet (Stot​=NS,dim=2NS+1)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Spin Heisenberg model Heisenberg ferromagnet
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
A connected isotropic spin-S ferromagnet with positive exchanges has a fully polarized ground state and a maximal-total-spin multiplet. For N sites the total spin is NS, with 2NS+1 magnetic states generated by total-spin lowering. Global rotations span the same degenerate space. Slowly varying reorientations produce gapless ferromagnetic magnons; finite-volume degeneracy must be distinguished from a thermodynamic statement about spontaneous order.
