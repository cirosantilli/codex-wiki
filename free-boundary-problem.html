Free boundary problem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 1
A free boundary problem determines both a field and part of its domain boundary. A glacier terminus, for example, separates positive ice thickness from bare bed. Mass conservation, nonnegative thickness, a rule for ice ablation only where ice exists, and suitable interface or entropy solution conditions are needed; a formal zero of one characteristic branch alone need not give the selected physical boundary.
