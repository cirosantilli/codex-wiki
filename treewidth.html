Treewidth (tw(G)=minT​(maxB∈T​∣B∣−1))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Tree decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
The minimum width of a tree decomposition. A tree has width at most one, while a clique of k vertices forces width at least k−1. Every pair of vertices in the clique shares a bag; their connected bag connected subgraphs of a tree have a common intersection by the Helly property of connected connected subgraphs of a tree, placing the whole clique in one bag. Small treewidth is useful for dynamic programming on finite-domain constraint satisfaction problems.
