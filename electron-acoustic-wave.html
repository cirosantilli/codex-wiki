Electron acoustic wave

 Home Physics Plasma (physics) Plasmon Plasma oscillation
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
An electron-supported electrostatic branch with approximately linear small-wavenumber dispersion relation ω≃kua​. A velocity plateau can support such a completely undamped mode even when ion response is neglected. A single Maxwellian electron population generally damps the corresponding acoustic response strongly. Distinct electron populations provide another setting for electron acoustic behavior, with different assumptions and damping properties.
