Surface acoustic impedance (Z=P/V)

 ... Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Elastic wave in an isotropic solid Acoustic impedance Normal acoustic impedance
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
At an acoustic boundary, the surface acoustic impedance is the pressure amplitude divided by the velocity amplitude directed into that boundary. With upward surface velocity −V and harmonic convention eiωt, it is Z=P/V. Reflection compares this impedance with the incident fluid's normal acoustic impedance. The velocity orientation and harmonic convention must be fixed before interpreting signs of inertial, elastic, and radiation terms.
