Radar cross section (σback​=4π(dσ/dΩ)back​)

 Home Electrical engineering Radar
OurBigBook.com Words: 86 Articles: 1
The monostatic radar cross section is 4π times the backward differential scattering cross-section. Equivalently, σback​=limr→∞​4πr2Sback​/Sinc​. It is a directional scattering measure with dimensions of area, not generally the geometric area or the total scattering cross-section. The definition may additionally specify the transmitted and received polarizations.
