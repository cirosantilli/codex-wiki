Pointwise minimax rate for Hölder density estimation

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical inference Nonparametric statistics Density estimation Kernel for density estimation Kernel density estimation
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
Over a one-dimensional Hölder density class of smoothness β, the optimized pointwise mean-squared error has order n−2β/(2β+1). A kernel of sufficiently high order balances squared bias h2β against variance (nh)−1.
