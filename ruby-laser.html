Ruby laser

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Laser Three-level laser
OurBigBook.com Words: 328 Articles: 1
A ruby laser uses chromium ions in ruby as its amplifying medium. Maiman's report identifies a metastable state fed by optical excitation and red emission near 694.3nm.[ref] The pump stores excitation in the medium; stimulated emission transfers it into the optical field. The simplified three-level laser rate equation explains why the stored population and the output pulse evolve together.
