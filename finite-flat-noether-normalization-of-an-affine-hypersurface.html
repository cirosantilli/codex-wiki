Finite flat Noether normalization of an affine hypersurface (X→Akn−1​ finite and flat)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Krull dimension Noether normalization lemma Noether normalization by weighted substitutions
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For a nonconstant polynomial over any field, use triangular substitutions xi​=yi​+ynBi​, xn​=yn​, where B exceeds every exponent in its support. Base-B weights distinguish all original monomials, so the transformed polynomial has a unique highest yn​ power with coefficient in k×. Rescale to make it monic. The monic polynomial quotient is finite free over k[y1​,…,yn−1​], yielding a finite flat projection. The nonlinear substitution works over finite fields as well as infinite ones; a generic linear-direction argument alone would not do so.
