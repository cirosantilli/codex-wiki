Pure Yang-Mills one-loop beta function (β(g)=−11CA​g3/[3(4π)2]+O(g5))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Renormalization Charge renormalization
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For pure Yang-Mills theory with a compact simple gauge group and canonically normalized gauge field, the one-loop renormalization-group beta function is μdg/dμ=−11CA​g3/[3(4π)2]+O(g5), where CA​>0 is the Casimir operator eigenvalue in the Adjoint representation. This coefficient includes the gauge-field and Faddeev-Popov ghost field loops; it is the standard pure-gauge input when adding matter-loop contributions. Its negative sign gives asymptotic freedom. Integrating the leading equation gives g(μ)−2=g(μ0​)−2+11CA​log(μ/μ0​)/[24π2], while perturbation theory is valid. Combining this term with the adjoint Dirac contribution to the Yang-Mills beta function gives the integer obstruction to one-loop marginality with adjoint Dirac fermions.
