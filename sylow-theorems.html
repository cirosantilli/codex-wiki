Sylow theorems

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 998 Articles: 16
If ∣G∣=pam with p∤m, then G has subgroups of order pa, every p-subgroup lies in one, and all such Sylow subgroups are conjugate. Their number satisfies np​≡1(modp) and np​∣m.
