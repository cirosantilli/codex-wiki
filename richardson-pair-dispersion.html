Richardson pair dispersion (⟨r2(t)⟩∼gϵt3)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Turbulent mixing
OurBigBook.com Words: 213 Articles: 3
Richardson pair dispersion concerns relative separation of two marked fluid elements, rather than their centre-of-mass motion. In an inertial range, scale-local relative diffusivity is of order ϵ1/3r4/3. A self-similar closure gives d⟨r2⟩/dt=Aϵ1/3⟨r2⟩2/3 and hence a t3 regime after loss of memory of initial separation. Stationarity and inertial-range separation alone do not imply this law at arbitrarily short times.
