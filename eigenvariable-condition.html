Eigenvariable condition

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Natural deduction
OurBigBook.com Words: 181 Articles: 2
An eigenvariable condition ensures that a variable chosen as arbitrary or as an existential witness cannot smuggle extra information into a quantifier inference. For the universal quantifier introduction rule, the generalized variable must not occur free in any undischarged assumption. For the existential quantifier elimination rule, the fresh witness must not occur free in the resulting conclusion or in the other undischarged assumptions. Temporary assumptions may contain it if they are discharged before the final inference.
