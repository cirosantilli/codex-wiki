Neon (Ne, Z=10)

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical element
OurBigBook.com Words: 36 Articles: 3
The chemical element with atomic number 10. Neon burning uses photodisintegration and alpha capture, while neon-22 can provide neutrons through 22Ne(α,n)25Mg.
