Geometric correction for heliocentric velocity dispersion (A(r,R⊙​))

 ... Galaxy Galaxy dynamics Stellar dynamics Galactic distribution function Velocity dispersion Heliocentric projection of a spherical velocity dispersion
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For tracers sampled uniformly on a Galactocentric shell of radius r, with the Sun at radius R⊙​, the correction in the heliocentric projection of a spherical velocity dispersion is
A=4r2r2+R⊙2​​−8r3R⊙​(r2−R⊙2​)2​ln​r−R⊙​r+R⊙​​​.
(30)
The limiting value at r=R⊙​ is 1/2; it tends to 2/3 as r/R⊙​→0 and to 2R⊙2​/(3r2) at large radius. Uniform heliocentric solid angle at fixed r>R⊙​ instead gives A=2R⊙2​/(3r2) exactly. Specify the selection measure when applying the correction.
