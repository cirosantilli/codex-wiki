Contraction principle for large deviations (J(y)=inff(x)=y​I(x))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Convergence of random variables Large deviation principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 229 Articles: 3
If XN​ satisfies a large deviation principle in E with good rate function I and f:E→F is a continuous map, then f(XN​) has the same large-deviation speed and good rate function
J(y)=inf{I(x):f(x)=y}.
(16)
The preimages of open and closed sets give the two large deviation principle inequalities. The identity {J≤M}=f({I≤M}) follows from compactness and lower semicontinuity by taking minimizing sequences in the fibres; hence J is good.
