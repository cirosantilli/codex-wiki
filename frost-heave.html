Frost heave

 ... Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Stefan problem Phase boundary Premelting
OurBigBook.com Words: 332 Articles: 4
Frost heave is displacement caused by segregated ice growth supplied by liquid flow, rather than solely by the expansion of water on freezing. A premelting film allows liquid access beneath the ice; thermomolecular pressure draws supply against viscous resistance and load.
