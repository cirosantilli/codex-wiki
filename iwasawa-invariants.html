Iwasawa invariants (μ(M)=∑i​ai​,λ(M)=∑j​bj​degfj​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 2
The invariants μ,λ of a finitely generated torsion Iwasawa module measure its p-power elementary factors and distinguished-polynomial factors. Vanishing of μ means that the module is finitely generated over Zp​, up to finite error. It does not mean that the module is finite: a factor Λ/(T) has μ=0 and is Zp​.
