Cell culture passage accounting (Nseed​=fNharvest​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 206
In an original constructed example, seed 300000 biological cells in 6mL of cell culture medium. Suppose the recoverable count becomes 1200000 before the next subculture. Transfer a well-mixed quarter of that harvest into a new vessel and bring its final volume to 6mL. Assume complete recovery, no loss during transfer and no division during handling. The selected portion contains
Nseed​=41​(1200000)=300000,cseed​=6mL300000​=50000mL−1.
(1)
The other 900000 biological cells remain outside the tracked vessel. The lower count after transfer is selection of a portion, not evidence of death. Adding fresh cell culture medium lowers the number density further without changing the selected count.
If the next interval again changes 300000 to 1200000, the two growth intervals contribute 2+2=4 net cell line population doublings. At the second harvest there has been just one intervening passage. Another quarter-transfer would add another passage without adding a growth interval. Thus neither passage number nor the logarithm of the final count divided by the very first seed count records all growth when portions are removed.
