Mechanical redistribution of sea ice (ψ(h))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Sea ice Sea-ice thickness distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
Mechanical redistribution of sea ice changes thickness-class area through ridging, rafting and lead formation. In the conservative thickness-distribution equation, local volume conservation gives ∫hψ(h)dh=0; area normalization including open water gives ∫ψ(h)dh=∇⋅v when thermodynamic boundary fluxes are accounted for consistently.
