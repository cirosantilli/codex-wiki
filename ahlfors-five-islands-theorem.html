Ahlfors five islands theorem

 ... Classification of isolated singularities Pole Meromorphic function Nevanlinna theory Ahlfors covering surface theory Ahlfors second fundamental theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
Every nonconstant meromorphic function on the complex plane has a simple island over at least one of any five Jordan domains on the Riemann sphere with disjoint closures. The Ahlfors second fundamental theorem, area comparison over target domains, and a length-area exhaustion of the complex plane prove this: without a simple island every counted island has degree at least two, forcing 3S≤(5/2)S+O(L).
