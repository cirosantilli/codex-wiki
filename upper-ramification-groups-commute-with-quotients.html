Upper ramification groups commute with quotients ((G/H)u=GuH/H)

 ... Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Ramification group Lower ramification numbering Herbrand function Upper ramification numbering
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
For a normal subgroup H of a finite local Galois group, the upper ramification numbering on its quotient is obtained by projecting the upper groups. The Herbrand function is the change of variable that ensures this compatibility. In contrast, the lower ramification numbering restricts directly to subgroups.
