Nucleon

 Home Physics Subatomic particle Hadron Baryon
OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 36
The proton and neutron form the nucleon isospin doublet, with spin angular momentum 1/2 and baryon number one. A unit Skyrmion with the fermionic Finkelstein-Rubinstein constraints gives these quantum numbers in its lowest rotational multiplet.
