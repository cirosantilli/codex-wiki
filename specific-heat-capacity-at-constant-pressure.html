Specific heat capacity at constant pressure (cP​)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Heat capacity Specific heat capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 78 Articles: 1
The specific heat capacity at constant pressure is cP​=(∂h/∂T)P​, where h is specific enthalpy. It includes expansion work as well as the change in internal energy.
