Quantifier (∀,∃)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A quantifier binds a free variable in a first-order formula and specifies how its truth depends on elements of a first-order structure: universal quantification ∀ requires every element, while existential quantification ∃ requires at least one.
