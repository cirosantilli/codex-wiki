Sign behavior of fixed-shift inverse iteration

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Power method Inverse iteration Convergence of fixed-shift inverse iteration
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
If the closest eigenvalue λ∗​ lies above the shift, the dominant eigenvalue (λ∗​−s)−1 of the inverse is positive and normalized iterates approach one fixed sign of its eigenvector. If λ∗​<s, that inverse eigenvalue is negative, so the direction converges but consecutive normalized vectors alternate signs. At a shift exactly midway between two simple eigenvalues, the two inverse eigenvalues have equal modulus and opposite signs, so generic iterates alternate between two mixtures instead of selecting one eigenvector.
