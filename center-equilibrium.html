Center equilibrium

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Autonomous planar system Equilibrium point of a dynamical system Linear stability of a planar equilibrium
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
A center equilibrium of a planar dynamical system is surrounded by closed trajectories. It is stable but not asymptotically stable.
