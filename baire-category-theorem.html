Baire category theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Topological analysis Metric space Complete metric space
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 22
Every complete metric space is a Baire space: every countable intersection of open dense subsets is dense. Equivalently, no nonempty open subset is a countable union of nowhere-dense subsets.
To prove the dense-intersection form, let V be nonempty and open and let G1​,G2​,… be open dense sets. Successively choose closed balls
B(xn​,rn​)⊂B(xn−1​,rn−1​)∩Gn​,0<rn​<2−n,
(10)
starting with a ball contained in V∩G1​. The centres form a Cauchy sequence. Its limit belongs to every closed ball, and hence to V∩⋂n​Gn​.
