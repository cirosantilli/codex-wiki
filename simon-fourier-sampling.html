Simon Fourier sampling (Pr(z)=21−n1z⋅s=0​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum Fourier transform Hidden subgroup problem Simon's problem Simon's algorithm
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Apply the Hadamard transform to a Simon coset state. The probability amplitude of ∣z⟩ is 2−(n+1)/2(−1)x⋅z[1+(−1)s⋅z]. Destructive quantum interference eliminates exactly those z with z⋅s=1. A computational-basis measurement therefore samples the kernel s⊥ uniformly, independently of the representative x.
