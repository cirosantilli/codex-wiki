Gauge-invariant electron Yukawa mass (me​=∣ye​∣v/2​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Yukawa interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
The Standard Model lepton doublet L=(νL​,eL​)T has hypercharge −1/2, the electron singlet eR​ has −1, and the Higgs doublet has 1/2. Therefore −ye​LˉϕeR​+h.c. is gauge-invariant even though a bare eˉL​eR​ mass is not. The vacuum expectation value generates a Dirac mass ∣ye​∣v/2​. In unitary gauge, the Higgs boson coupling is −(me​/v)heˉe after rephasing the electron.
