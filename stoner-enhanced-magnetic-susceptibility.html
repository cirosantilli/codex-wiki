Stoner-enhanced magnetic susceptibility (χ=χP​/[1−Ugs​(EF​)])

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum magnetism Hubbard model Itinerant ferromagnetism Stoner criterion
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
Let gs​(EF​) be the single-spin density of states per site per energy, and let the on-site Hubbard model repulsion have energy U. For a weak uniform magnetic field H, the half-splitting of the two spin bands is Δ=Uδn/2+μ0​μB​H, where δn=n↑​−n↓​. Constant density of states gives δn=2gs​Δ. Therefore
HμB​δn​=1−Ugs​2μ0​μB2​gs​​.
(91)
This is the response per site; multiplying by the site number density gives the dimensionless SI volume magnetic susceptibility. With spin-summed density of states g=2gs​, the denominator is 1−Ug/2. Its vanishing is the mean-field Stoner criterion. The unpolarized linear-response expression is stable only for Ugs​<1.
