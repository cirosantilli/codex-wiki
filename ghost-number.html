Ghost number (gh(c)=1,gh(cˉ)=−1)

 ... Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing Faddeev-Popov determinant Faddeev-Popov ghost
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 1
The additive grading assigns ghost number +1 to c and −1 to cˉ. Physical observables have ghost number zero. This grading is distinct from spin or the Adjoint representation.
