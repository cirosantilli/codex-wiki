N-variable Lindenbaum algebra (Fn​/En​(T))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Lindenbaum algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
Take formulas whose free variables lie among a fixed n-tuple and identify them when their universal equivalence follows from T. Conjunction, disjunction and negation induce a Boolean algebra; its order is implication modulo T. Complete types correspond to ultrafilters and isolated complete types to atoms. For a complete theory in a countable language, finiteness of these algebras for every finite n characterizes countable categoricity.
