One-step method (yn+1​=yn​+hΦ(xn​,yn​,h))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 47
A one-step method approximates an ordinary differential equation using the current approximate solution and evaluations within the next step. The function Φ is its increment function. A Runge-Kutta method may use several intermediate stages while remaining a one-step method: it does not require additional previous solution values as a linear multistep method does. Its consistency error is the exact-start update defect divided by h.
