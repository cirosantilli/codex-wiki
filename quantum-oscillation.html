Quantum oscillation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Landau level
OurBigBook.com Words: 137 Articles: 1
A quantum oscillation is an oscillatory contribution to a metal's response as a magnetic field moves quantized orbital levels past the Fermi energy. For a closed extremal Fermi surface cross-section of wavevector-space area Aext​, Onsager quantization gives inverse-field period Δ(1/B)=2πe/(ℏAext​). Oscillations of magnetization and electrical resistivity are respectively the de Haas-van Alphen effect and Shubnikov-de Haas effect. Low temperature and long quantum lifetime are needed so thermal smearing and disorder do not wash out the Landau levels.
