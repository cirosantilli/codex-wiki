Weyl equation (i(∂t​∓σ⋅∇)χL/R​=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Weyl spinor
OurBigBook.com Words: 90 Articles: 1
The free massless relativistic field equation for one chiral two-component spinor. With mostly-minus metric, the left-chiral equation has the minus sign and the right-chiral equation the plus sign. The positive-energy particle helicities are respectively −1/2 and +1/2; antiparticle helicities are opposite.
