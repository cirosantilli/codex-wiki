OurBigBook.com Words: 86
For an orthogonal group gauge transformation R∈O(3), a scalar vector transforms as ϕ↦Rϕ. The gauge connection belongs to the Adjoint representation, identified with axial vectors: the displayed cross-product identity makes its homogeneous transformation A↦(detR)RA. The matrix connection also has the usual inhomogeneous derivative term. Thus Dϕ transforms as an ordinary vector, including under improper orthogonal transformations. Treating both fields as ordinary vectors would fail because a cross product changes sign under an orientation reversal. A nonzero vector vacuum has stabilizer subgroup O(2); its connected part is SO(2).
