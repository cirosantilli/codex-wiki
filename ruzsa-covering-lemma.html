Ruzsa covering lemma

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Additive combinatorics Approximate group
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
If finite sets satisfy ∣S+T∣≤K∣T∣, then a maximal family of disjoint translates x+T, with x∈S, has at most K members and gives
S⊆X+T−T,∣X∣≤K.
