Uniform-reference information projection maximizes entropy (D(q∥u)=lnm−He​(q))

For a uniform distribution on a finite set of m outcomes, direct expansion of relative entropy gives
D(q∥u)=∑i​qi​ln(mqi​)=lnm−He​(q),
(93)
where He​ is information entropy in nats. Therefore minimizing relative entropy from this particular reference is equivalent to maximizing information entropy subject to the same constraint. This statement depends on the uniform reference; an arbitrary reference contributes its own weights.
In the three-outcome example, He​(q∗)=ln3−D(q∗∥p)≈0.901235 nats. The mean fixes one aggregate quantity, and information projection chooses how to distribute the remaining uncertainty. This extends maximum entropy on a finite alphabet, whose unconstrained maximizer is the uniform law itself.
