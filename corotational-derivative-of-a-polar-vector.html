Corotational derivative of a polar vector (Dt​p+Ωp)

 ... Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity Conformation tensor Objective time derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
With velocity gradient Aij​=∂i​vj​, set Ω=(A−AT)/2. A vector rigidly rotating with the material has material derivative Dt​p=−Ωp, so its corotational derivative vanishes. Its unit rotational coefficient is fixed by an objective time derivative; adding −ξDp describes the material's flow alignment of a polar order parameter.
