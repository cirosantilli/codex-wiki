Lucas number (L0​=2, L1​=1, Ln+2​=Ln+1​+Ln​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The Lucas numbers obey the same second-order recurrence as the Fibonacci numbers but start with 2,1, so their sequence is 2,1,3,4,7,11,…. For n≥1, Ln​=Fn−1​+Fn+1​. Substitution in the Fibonacci addition formula gives F2n​=Fn​Ln​. Consecutive Fibonacci numbers are coprime by the Euclidean algorithm; since Ln​=Fn​+2Fn−1​, Bézout's identity gives gcd(Fn​,Ln​)=gcd(Fn​,2)≤2.
