A lossless low-energy charged-condensate action has a positive compressibility coefficient χp​, effective pair density ρ0​, mass m∗​ and signed charge q. Coupling its phase to the electromagnetic potentials through the displayed combinations preserves gauge symmetry. Adding the Maxwell action and eliminating the longitudinal electric potential gives ω2=q2ρ0​/(ϵ0​m∗​)+[ρ0​/(m∗​χp​)]k2 in a simple homogeneous bulk model. Removing the electromagnetic coupling leaves neutral sound. Static Ginzburg-Landau theory of superconductivity alone cannot determine these frequencies or their damping. [ref]
