Skew polynomial ring of an automorphism (A[t;σ])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Polynomial ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 244 Articles: 3
For an automorphism σ of a unital ring A, this ring consists of finite sums ∑j≥0​aj​tj, with multiplication determined by ta=σ(a)t. Both left and right coefficient normal forms are available because σ is invertible. The noncommutative Hilbert basis theorem implies that it is a right Noetherian ring when A is, and a left Noetherian ring when A is. Invertibility of σ matters in this two-sided formulation.
