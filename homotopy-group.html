Homotopy group (πn​(X,x0​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 40
The nth homotopy group consists of based homotopy classes of maps Sn→X. It is a group for n≥1 and an abelian group for n≥2.
