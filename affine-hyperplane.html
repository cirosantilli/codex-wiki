Affine hyperplane (H={v∈V:ℓ(v)=c},ℓ=0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Affine subspace
OurBigBook.com Words: 68
In a finite-dimensional real vector space, a level set of a nonzero linear functional is an affine hyperplane. Choosing v0​ in that level set gives H=v0​+kerℓ, so its dimension is one less than that of the ambient space. With a Euclidean inner product, translation followed by an orthonormal basis of kerℓ identifies it isometrically with the lower-dimensional Euclidean space. The coordinate-sum constraint on balanced cut vectors is an example.
