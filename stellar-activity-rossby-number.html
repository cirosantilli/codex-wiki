Stellar activity Rossby number (Ro∗​=Prot​/τc​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Rossby number
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For a convective star, the activity convention compares its rotation period with a convective turnover time. It differs by fixed factors from the fluid Rossby number estimated from speed, length and angular velocity. Small Ro∗​ denotes strong rotational influence on convection. Magnetic activity usually rises as this ratio decreases in the unsaturated regime, but levels off in a saturated rapid-rotation regime.
