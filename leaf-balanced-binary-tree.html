Leaf-balanced binary tree

 Home Computer science Data structure Binary tree Balanced binary tree
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
A leaf-labelled binary tree is leaf-balanced when the leaf counts of the two subtrees at every internal node differ by at most one. Splitting a finite nonempty list into pieces of sizes ⌊n/2⌋ and ⌈n/2⌉ and applying the same construction recursively preserves the original leaf order and gives this balance condition. Both pieces are nonempty for n≥2 and strictly shorter than the input; induction proves termination and balance. This leaf-count convention is different from demanding equal subtree heights.
