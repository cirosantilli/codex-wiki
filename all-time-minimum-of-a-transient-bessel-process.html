All-time minimum of a transient Bessel process (Px​(inft≥0​Xt​≤r)=(r/x)δ−2)

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Bessel process Bessel power local martingale
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For a Bessel process of dimension δ>2 started at x>0, its scale function of a one-dimensional diffusion gives Px​(τr​<∞)=(r/x)δ−2 for 0<r<x. In particular, inft≥0​Xt​>0 almost surely. The minimum is attained because the process tends to infinity; the hitting formula alone already implies positivity of the infimum.
