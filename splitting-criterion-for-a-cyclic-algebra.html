Splitting criterion for a cyclic algebra ((L/K,σ,a) splits⟺a∈NL/K​L×)

 ... Algebra over a field Associative algebra Central simple algebra Brauer group Crossed-product algebra of a Galois extension Cyclic algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
If a=NL/K​c, let L act on itself by multiplication and let the extra generator act by cσ. Its nth power is multiplication by a, and these operators give the cyclic algebra as EndK​(L). Conversely, if the algebra is a matrix algebra on an n-dimensional vector space, its embedded degree-n field L makes that space one-dimensional over L. The extra generator is then cσ in an L-basis, so its nth power forces a=Nc.
