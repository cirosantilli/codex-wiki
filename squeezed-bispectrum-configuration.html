Squeezed bispectrum configuration (kL​≪kS​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Field theory in cosmology Primordial non-Gaussianity Primordial bispectrum
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A primordial bispectrum is squeezed when one external Fourier mode has much smaller wavenumber than the other two. Momentum conservation then makes the two short wavevectors almost opposite. This configuration measures the response of short-scale correlations to a long perturbation.
