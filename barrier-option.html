Barrier option

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fundamental theorem of asset pricing Contingent claim
OurBigBook.com Words: 439 Articles: 7
A barrier option activates or expires according to whether the underlying price reaches a prescribed level during its lifetime. Its state must include whether the barrier has already been reached; the current price alone generally does not determine its remaining payoff.
