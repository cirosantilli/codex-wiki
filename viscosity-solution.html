Viscosity solution

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Solution of a partial differential equation Weak solution
OurBigBook.com Words: 170 Articles: 1
For a partial differential equation F(x,u,Du,D2u)=0 whose operator is nonincreasing in the Hessian variable, a continuous viscosity subsolution satisfies F(x0​,u(x0​),Dϕ(x0​),D2ϕ(x0​))≤0 whenever a smooth test function ϕ touches it from above. A viscosity supersolution satisfies the reverse inequality for tests touching from below. A viscosity solution satisfies both conditions. For F=−trD2u, these conditions are respectively Δϕ≥0 and Δϕ≤0. They make sense even when u has no classical second derivatives.
