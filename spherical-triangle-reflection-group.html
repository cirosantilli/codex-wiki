Spherical triangle reflection group (∣G∣=1/p+1/q+1/r−14​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Spherical geometry Spherical triangle
OurBigBook.com Words: 97
Reflections in the sides of a spherical triangle of angles π/p,π/q,π/r generate a finite group when its images tessellate the sphere. The triangle area is π(1/p+1/q+1/r−1) by the spherical excess formula. Label its three vertex types and continue those labels across each reflected side; they are consistent around every vertex and, because the sphere is simply connected, globally. The generated group preserves these labels, so a chamber stabilizer fixes all three vertices and is trivial. Dividing sphere area by chamber area gives its order. Up to permutation the possibilities are (2,2,n) for n≥2, and (2,3,3),(2,3,4),(2,3,5), of orders 4n,24,48,120.
