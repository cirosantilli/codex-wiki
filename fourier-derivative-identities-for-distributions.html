Fourier derivative identities for distributions

OurBigBook.com Words: 41
For Dj​=−i∂j​, the Fourier transform of a tempered distribution satisfies
Dαu=ξαu,xβu=(−1)∣β∣Dβu.
(16)
The base cases follow from ⟨Dj​u,φ⟩=i⟨u,∂j​φ⟩ and integration by parts in the Fourier integral. Iterating gives the multi-index formulas. The D convention matters: for the unscaled derivative, ∂j​u​=iξj​u.
