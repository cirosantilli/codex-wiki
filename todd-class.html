Todd class (td(E)=∏j​1−e−xj​xj​​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Intersection theory Chern class
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
Here the xj​ are the formal Chern roots of a vector bundle. The degree-four term is (c12​+c2​)/12. Applied to the tangent bundle of a compact complex surface, Riemann–Roch for the structure sheaf gives the Noether formula.
