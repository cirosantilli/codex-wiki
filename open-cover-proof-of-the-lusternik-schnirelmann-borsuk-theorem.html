Open-cover proof of the Lusternik-Schnirelmann-Borsuk theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Borsuk-Ulam theorem Lusternik–Schnirelmann theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For a finite open cover U0​,…,Ud​ of Sd, take continuous nonnegative functions ϕi​ summing to one and positive only in Ui​. Such a partition of unity comes from normalizing the distance to a set functions d(x,Sd∖Ui​). The Borsuk-Ulam theorem applied to (ϕ1​,…,ϕd​) gives equal values at an antipodal pair. The sum condition makes ϕ0​ equal too, and some common positive value places both points in one Ui​.
