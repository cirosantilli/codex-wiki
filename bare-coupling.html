Bare coupling (λ0​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Renormalization
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
A bare coupling is an interaction coefficient in a regulated action. It is related to a renormalized coupling through counterterms and any required powers of the renormalization scale. For quartic scalar theory in 4−ϵ dimensions, its mass dimension is ϵ, and λ0​=μϵ[λR​+aλR2​/ϵ+⋯]. At fixed regulator, changing μ while leaving the theory unchanged keeps λ0​ fixed; the corresponding change of λR​ defines its renormalization-group beta function.
