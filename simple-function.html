Simple function (s=∑j=1m​aj​1Aj​​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Lebesgue integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 105
A simple function is a measurable function whose image is a finite set. Its value sets Aj​={x:s(x)=aj​} form a finite measurable partition. For nonnegative aj​, its Lebesgue integral is ∑j​aj​μ(Aj​), with the convention 0⋅∞=0. The pieces need not be intervals, or even connected sets. See MIT lecture 10, Definitions 100 and 106.
For example, on [0,1] let s be 2 on [0,1/4), 1 on [1/4,3/4), and 0 on [3/4,1]. Its Lebesgue integral is 2(1/4)+1(1/2)=1. Its superlevel sets have Lebesgue measure 3/4 for 0≤t<1, 1/4 for 1≤t<2, and 0 thereafter. The layer cake representation gives the same answer: (3/4)(1)+(1/4)(1)=1. These two calculations are the building blocks of the general Lebesgue integral.
