Nuclear magnetic dipole moment (μI​=gI​μN​I)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Atomic nucleus Nuclear spin
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The magnetic dipole moment associated with nuclear spin, using dimensionless angular momentum operators and the nuclear magneton μN​. The quoted scalar moment is its expectation in the stretched quantum state, μI​=gI​μN​I. In the same electronic energy level, the leading magnetic-dipole hyperfine structure constant is proportional to μI​/I, apart from isotope-dependent electronic and nuclear-size corrections.
