Internal-wave momentum deposition (u=(k/ω)E)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 1
A slowly established upward plane internal gravity wave has u′w′=−m∣a∣2/(2k). The mean horizontal conservation of momentum equation gives ut​=−∂z​u′w′. Using envelope transport and an initially vanishing mean gives u=−m∣a∣2/(2kcgz​). Its energy density per unit mass is E=(k2+m2)∣a∣2/(2k2), so u=(k/ω)E. This is the mean flow left behind the advancing wave envelope, rather than a claim that an established uniform wavetrain has nonzero local stress divergence.
