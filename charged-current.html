Charged current

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Weak interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 535 Articles: 10
The weak charged current couples a W boson to two members of a left-handed electroweak doublet. In the quark mass basis its flavor coefficients are entries of the Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix.
