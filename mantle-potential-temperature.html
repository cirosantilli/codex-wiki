Mantle potential temperature (Tp​)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planet Earth Earth mantle
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
The temperature a parcel of mantle material would attain after a reversible adiabatic process bringing it to a specified reference pressure, without changing its composition or phase assemblage. It removes the compressional contribution when comparing sources at different depths. Within one phase and with depth z positive downward, the adiabatic process gives the gradient is dT/dz=αgT/cp​, so
Tp​=T(z)exp(−∫0z​cp​(s)α(s)g(s)​ds).
(2)
Absolute temperature is used inside this formula. Recovering a source Tp​ from erupted magma also requires accounting for melting, latent heat, ascent and fractional crystallization; an eruption temperature is not automatically a mantle potential temperature.
