Jackknife variance estimator (VJ​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Jackknife resampling
OurBigBook.com Words: 91 Articles: 1
For leave-one-out estimates θ(−i)​ and their average θJ​, the Jackknife variance estimator is VJ​=(n−1)n−1∑i​(θ(−i)​−θJ​)2. For a sample mean it equals the sample variance divided by n. Its general interpretation as an estimated sampling variance requires appropriate smoothness of the statistic.
