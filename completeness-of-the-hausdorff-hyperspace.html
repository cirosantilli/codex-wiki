Completeness of the Hausdorff hyperspace

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Topological analysis Metric space Hausdorff distance
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
For any nonempty complete metric space X, the family of its nonempty closed bounded subsets is a complete metric space under the Hausdorff distance. Boundedness of the entire ambient space is unnecessary in this general form.
For a Cauchy sequence (Fn​) of these sets, define An​=⋃m≥n​Fm​​ and δn​=supp,q≥n​dH​(Fp​,Fq​). A Cauchy sequence in any metric space is bounded, so its tail sets are contained in a common finite-radius neighbourhood of one bounded FN​; hence every An​ is bounded. The distance to a set is one-Lipschitz, so estimates pass to the closure, giving dH​(Fn​,An​)≤δn​ and dH​(An​,Am​)≤δn​ for m≥n. Choose nj​ with δnj​​≤2−j. The Summably nested Hausdorff intersection theorem gives Anj​​→G=∅. The triangle inequality now gives Fn​→G, proving completeness.
