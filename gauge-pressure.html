Gauge pressure (pg​=p−patmosphere​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Hydrostatic pressure
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
Gauge pressure is pressure measured relative to the surrounding atmospheric pressure; absolute pressure is instead measured relative to an ideal vacuum. At an open surface psurface​=patmosphere​, so the constant-mass density hydrostatic pressure law becomes pg​=ρf​gh. Here pg​=0 at the surface denotes equality with atmospheric pressure. It does not denote zero absolute pressure. [ref]
