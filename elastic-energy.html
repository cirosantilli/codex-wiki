Elastic energy is the recoverable energy stored by deforming an elastic material. In the small-deflection model of an elastic filament, the bending contribution is a positive quadratic functional of the curvatures, such as 21​∫(Ay′′2+Bz′′2)dx. Work by applied loads must also be included when minimizing the total potential energy.
