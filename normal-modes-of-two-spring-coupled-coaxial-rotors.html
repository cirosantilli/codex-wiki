Normal modes of two spring-coupled coaxial rotors (ωrel2​=kaA(1/I1​+1/I2​)cosδ0​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 121
For point masses on concentric horizontal rings in parallel planes, set I1​=ma2, I2​=MA2, and δ=θ−φ. A spring of zero natural length gives V=constant−kaAcosδ. The inertia-weighted angle q=(I1​θ+I2​φ)/(I1​+I2​) separates the Lagrangian into a free common rotor with inertia I1​+I2​ and a relative rotor with inertia Ir​=I1​I2​/(I1​+I2​). Static equilibria have δ0​=0 or π. Their normal modes are the common displacement (1,1) with zero frequency and the relative displacement (I2​,−I1​) with the displayed squared frequency. The aligned family has a restoring relative mode; the antipodal family has an exponentially growing one. The zero mode is required by rotational symmetry. Small nonzero total angular momentum produces common drift, so stability of the aligned family modulo rotations should be distinguished from Lyapunov stability of one fixed equilibrium point.
