The Schwinger boson representation satisfies Sn2​=(Nn​/2)(Nn​/2+1), where Nn​ is the local total boson number operator. Thus a prescribed local spin s requires the displayed operator constraint at every site. A condition on ⟨Nn​⟩ alone is insufficient: for ⟨Nn​⟩=2s, ⟨Sn2​⟩=s(s+1)+Var(Nn​)/4. Projecting onto the required eigenspace at each site removes the unwanted local irreducible spin representations.
