Stern-Volmer equation (Φ0​/ΦQ​=1+kQ​τ0​[Q])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Fluorescence Quenching (fluorescence)
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
For purely dynamic quenching of a single emitting population, Φ0​/ΦQ​=τ0​/τQ​=1+kQ​τ0​[Q]. The slope measures kQ​τ0​, so determining a bimolecular quenching constant requires the unquenched lifetime or equivalent independent rate information.
