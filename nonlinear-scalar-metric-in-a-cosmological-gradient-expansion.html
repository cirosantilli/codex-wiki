Nonlinear scalar metric in a cosmological gradient expansion (hij​=abg2​e2ζδij​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 41
With vectors and independent tensors neglected, finite scalar cosmological perturbations of the local spatial scale can be encoded by hij​=abg2​e2ζδij​. Scalar lapse and shift potentials complete its 3+1 decomposition of spacetime. The exact shift-square term is hijNi​Nj​, independently of the parametrization of the scalar shift.
