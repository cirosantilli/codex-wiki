Automorphism invariance of parameter-free definability (σ[D]=D)

 ... Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic First-order structure Definable set Parameter-free definability
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
Every parameter-free definable set is preserved setwise by every automorphism of a first-order structure. For atomic formulas, preservation follows from the definition of a structure automorphism. Logical connectives preserve that equivalence, and a bijection transports existential and universal witnesses in both directions. Induction on first-order formulas therefore gives M⊨φ(a) exactly when M⊨φ(σa). Applied to a defining formula, this proves the displayed equality. With parameters from B, restrict to automorphisms fixing B pointwise. An automorphism moving a proposed singleton or taking a proposed function-graph point outside the graph is consequently a direct obstruction to definability.
