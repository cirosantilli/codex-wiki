Linear thermal expansion coefficient (αL​=L−1(∂L/∂T)P​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Thermal expansion
OurBigBook.com Words: 136 Articles: 1
The linear thermal expansion coefficient is the fractional change in a specified length per change in temperature, at fixed external pressure for a sample free to expand. For small changes, ΔL/L≃αL​ΔT. An isotropic sample with the same response in all directions has volumetric coefficient of thermal expansion α=3αL​, since differentiating V∝L3 gives dV/V=3dL/L. Direction-dependent materials require a specified measurement direction. [ref].
