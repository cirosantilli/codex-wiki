Entropy lower bound from the largest atom

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Information entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 99
For a finite probability distribution with largest atom Π, its base-two Shannon entropy satisfies H≥2(1−Π). If Π≥1/2, coarsen the distribution to the indicator of a largest atom: Shannon entropy cannot increase under a deterministic function, so H≥h2​(Π). The concavity of binary entropy places it above the chord from (1/2,1) to (1,0), giving h2​(Π)≥2(1−Π). If Π≤1/2, every nonzero probability p has −log2​p≥−log2​Π, so H≥−log2​Π. The function −log2​Π−2(1−Π) vanishes at 1/2 and has negative derivative on (0,1/2], proving the same bound. Equality in the combined bound occurs for a point mass or a distribution uniform on two points.
