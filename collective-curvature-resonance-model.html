Collective-curvature resonance model (ω02​=ωL2​+K/I)

Consider an original scalar response model for a small collective angle u. Stipulate effective inertia I>0, restoring curvature K≥0, damping rate Γ>0, and a drive torque τ0​cosωt:
Iu¨+2IΓu˙+(IωL2​+K)u=τ0​cosωt.
(32)
The undamped resonance scale is ω0​=ωL2​+Ω2​, where Ω2=K/I. This illustrates the role of an additional collective restoring energy. It is not the full coupled spin dynamics of either helium phase, and its parameters are not fitted to historical observations.
The steady response and mean absorbed power follow directly:
χ(ω)=ω02​−ω2−2iΓω1/I​,P=2τ02​ω​Imχ(ω)=(ω02​−ω2)2+4Γ2ω2τ02​Γω2/I​.
(33)
With constant damping, differentiating this last expression places its positive-frequency maximum exactly at ω0​. Setting chosen Ω=1 and ωL​=2 moves the peak from 2 to 5​≃2.236, about 11.8%. At ωL​=10 the same curvature moves it only to 101​≃10.050, about 0.50%. At large ωL​, the shift is Ω2/(2ωL​) to leading order. A smaller shift at larger field therefore need not mean that the restoring curvature vanished.
