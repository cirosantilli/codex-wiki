Entropy generation in finite-temperature heat transfer (dStot​=dQ(1/T−1/TR​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Second law of thermodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 1
When a system at instantaneous temperature T absorbs heat dQ from a reservoir at TR​, with no other entropy production in the system, its entropy increases by dQ/T while the reservoir changes by −dQ/TR​. The total is positive for spontaneous heat transfer across a finite temperature difference. A quasistatic mechanical path can therefore be thermally irreversible.
