Cauchy distribution (Cauchy(x0​,γ))

The Cauchy distribution with location x0​ and scale γ>0 has probability density function
f(x)=πγ(1+((x−x0​)/γ)2)1​.
(50)
The standard Cauchy distribution has x0​=0, γ=1, characteristic function e−∣t∣, and infinite first absolute moment.
