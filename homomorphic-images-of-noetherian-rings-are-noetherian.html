Homomorphic images of Noetherian rings are Noetherian

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Noetherian ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
Let ϕ:R→S be a surjective ring homomorphism and let R be a Noetherian ring. For every ideal J of S, its inverse image is a finitely generated ideal of R. If ϕ−1(J)=(a1​,…,ar​), then J=(ϕ(a1​),…,ϕ(ar​)). Thus S is a Noetherian ring. In particular, every quotient ring of a Noetherian ring is a Noetherian ring. For a ring homomorphism that is not surjective, apply the same argument with its image as codomain.
