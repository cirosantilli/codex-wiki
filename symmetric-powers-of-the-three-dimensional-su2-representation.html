Symmetric powers of the three-dimensional SU2 representation (Symn(V2​)=⨁j=0⌊n/2⌋​V2n−4j​)

 ... Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor product Tensor algebra Symmetric algebra Symmetric power
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
The three-dimensional irreducible representation V2​ of SU(2) has weights 2,0,−2. Thus the character of its nth symmetric power is ∑a+b+c=n​t2(a−c). Its weight 2ℓ has multiplicity ⌊(n−∣ℓ∣)/2⌋+1 for ∣ℓ∣≤n. Subtracting adjacent weight multiplicities gives one copy of each V2n​,V2n−4​,…. The invariant subspace is one-dimensional for even n and zero for odd n.
