Quantum phase estimation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum Fourier transform
OurBigBook.com Words: 946 Articles: 12
Given controlled powers of a unitary U and an eigenstate U∣ψ⟩=e2πiϕ∣ψ⟩, quantum phase estimation outputs an approximation to ϕ. If the input is a superposition of eigenstates, measurement selects an eigenphase with probability equal to the squared magnitude of its coefficient.
