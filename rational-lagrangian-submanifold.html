Rational Lagrangian submanifold

OurBigBook.com Words: 144 Articles: 1
For a Lagrangian submanifold L in an exact symplectic manifold with primitive λ, its period subgroup is {∫c​λ:c∈H1​(L;Z)}⊂R. It is rational when this subgroup is discrete. If it is nonzero, it is γZ for a unique γ>0. Some conventions reserve the word rational for this nonzero case. The zero subgroup corresponds to an exact differential form λ∣L​; its least positive period is interpreted as +∞ if that endpoint convention is admitted.
