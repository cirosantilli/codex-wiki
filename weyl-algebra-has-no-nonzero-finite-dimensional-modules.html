Weyl algebra has no nonzero finite-dimensional modules (tr[∂1​,x1​]=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Noncommutative algebra Weyl algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
For the complex Weyl algebra An​ with n≥1, a unital finite-dimensional module would give matrices satisfying [∂1​,x1​]=I. Their matrix trace gives 0=dimC​M, so the module is zero. Characteristic zero and n≥1 are essential.
