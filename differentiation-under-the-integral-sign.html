Differentiation under the integral sign

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 83 Articles: 2
If g(t,x) and its partial derivative Di​g(t,x) are continuous for t in a compact interval and x in an open set, then
Di​∫ab​g(t,x)dt=∫ab​Di​g(t,x)dt.
(81)
Apply the fundamental theorem of calculus to the difference quotient in the ith coordinate and use uniform continuity on a compact neighbourhood.
