Algebraic alpha quenching (α(B)=α0​−p∣B∣2)

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Dynamo action Dynamo quenching
OurBigBook.com Words: 212 Articles: 2
Algebraic alpha quenching prescribes a field-dependent alpha effect as a closure for nonlinear mean-field dynamo feedback. The polynomial law α=α0​−p∣B∣2 with p>0 decreases alpha, so it can saturate positive alpha but makes negative alpha more negative. It therefore differs importantly from the sign-preserving rational law α0​/(1+∣B∣2/B∗2​). Its solutions must satisfy nonnegative squared-amplitude conditions; an assertion based only on large ∣α0​∣ need not hold for both signs.
