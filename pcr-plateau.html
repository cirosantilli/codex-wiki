PCR plateau

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Polymerase chain reaction PCR efficiency
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
A PCR plateau is the late phase in which further cycles add little product. Depletion of substrates and accumulation of reaction by-products can reduce PCR efficiency. Consequently, similar final amplicon amounts need not mean similar initial template amounts. In the stipulated green model, 0<Nn​<K implies Nn​<Nn+1​<K, and growth approaches the imposed ceiling. That illustrates loss of starting-amount information without claiming to identify the limiting chemical process in an actual tube.[ref]
