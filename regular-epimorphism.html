Regular epimorphism

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Epimorphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
A regular epimorphism is a morphism that is the coequalizer of some parallel pair. Every regular epimorphism is a strong epimorphism.
