Milnor–Moore theorem (U(π∗​(ΩY)⊗Q)≅H∗​(ΩY;Q))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Loop space Pontryagin ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For simply connected Y, rational Hurewicz homomorphism identifies π∗​(ΩY)⊗Q with the primitive elements of a Hopf algebra of H∗​(ΩY;Q) and induces an isomorphism U(π∗​(ΩY)⊗Q)≅H∗​(ΩY;Q) of graded Hopf algebras. The bracket on homotopy is the Samelson product. The underlying algebra theorem says that a connected graded cocommutative Hopf algebra over characteristic zero is the universal enveloping algebra of its primitive Lie algebra. These statements let a free tensor algebra of loop-space homology detect a free graded Lie algebra of homotopy.
