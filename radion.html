Radion

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Compactification (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 194 Articles: 2
A radion is a lower-dimensional scalar field describing the size or separation modulus of compact extra dimensions. In a two-brane Randall–Sundrum model, its expectation determines the inter-brane separation and the warp-generated hierarchy. A background size alone does not supply a radion potential or a stabilization mechanism.
