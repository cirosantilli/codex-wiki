Density-variance dissipation rate (χ=κρ​∣∇ρ′∣2​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence Eddy diffusion
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
Molecular scalar diffusion removes density-fluctuation variance at the positive rate displayed here. With this convention the budget for one half of the variance has dissipation term −χ; the full variance budget has −2χ. In a stationary locally homogeneous shear flow the half-variance budget gives ρ′w′​ρ​z​+χ=0. An integral-scale model writes χ=ρ′2​q/Lρ​, absorbing any normalization constant into Lρ​.
