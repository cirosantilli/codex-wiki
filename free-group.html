Free group (Fn​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 3
The free group F(S) on a set S consists of reduced words in letters s±1, with multiplication followed by cancellation. Every map from S to a group extends uniquely to a homomorphism from F(S).
