Terrestrial atmosphere

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planet Earth
OurBigBook.com Words: 134 Articles: 4
The terrestrial atmosphere has regions with opposite signs of the atmospheric lapse rate: the troposphere usually cools upward, the stratosphere warms upward through ultraviolet heating, the mesosphere cools, and the thermosphere warms. A monotone atmospheric pressure-temperature profile can approximate one such interval, but cannot describe all their transitions with one fixed branch.
