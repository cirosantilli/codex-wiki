Skorokhod embedding theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Stopping time
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 2
The Skorokhod embedding theorem represents a centered probability distribution μ on the real numbers with finite variance as BT​∼μ for a stopping time T of Brownian motion. One may choose T so that the stopped process (Bt∧T​) is uniformly integrable and ET=∫x2μ(dx).
