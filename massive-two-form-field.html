Massive two-form field (∂mFmnp​=μ2Anp​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Classical field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 78 Articles: 1
An antisymmetric tensor Amn​ with F=dA obeys ∂mFmnp​=μ2Anp​ for μ=0. Taking a divergence yields ∂mAmn​=0, and the remaining equation is the Klein-Gordon equation for each component. The physical particle polarizations form the exterior square of the massive little group vector representation, of dimension (D−1)(D−2)/2. Unlike a two-form gauge field, it has physical longitudinal polarizations.
