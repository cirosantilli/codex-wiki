Closed matter-dominated Friedmann solution (a=A(1−cosu),t=A(u−sinu)/k​)

A positively curved pressureless matter universe with zero cosmological constant has the cycloid parametrization displayed above. In natural units, if the Friedmann equation is a˙2=C/a−k with C,k>0, then A=C/(2k) and u=k​τ for conformal time measured from the Big Bang. Substituting in a′2=Ca−ka2 verifies the scale factor; integrating dt=adτ gives cosmic time. The universe reaches maximum size at u=π and recollapses into a Big Crunch at u=2π.
