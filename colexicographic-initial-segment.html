Colexicographic initial segment (Ir​(a))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Colexicographic order
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
The first a sets of a fixed size in colexicographic order form a colexicographic initial segment. Its lower shadow is again a colexicographic initial segment at the next smaller size.
