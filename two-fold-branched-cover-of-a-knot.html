Two-fold branched cover of a knot (Σ2​(K))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 89 Articles: 1
The two-fold branched cover of a knot is the double cover of S3 branched along the knot. Its first homology has a nonsingular linking form and, when finite, has order detK.
Away from the knot this is a two-sheeted covering space; at the knot it has the standard local model of a branched covering.
