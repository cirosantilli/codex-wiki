Tait conjectures

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Knot Knot diagram
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The Tait conjectures concern reduced alternating knot diagrams: they minimize the crossing number of a knot, have an invariant diagram writhe, and are related by flypes. These formerly conjectural assertions are now theorems; the first is the Tait crossing-number theorem.
