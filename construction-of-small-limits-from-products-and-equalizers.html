Construction of small limits from products and equalizers

 ... Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Diagram (category theory) Cone over a diagram Categorical limit
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
If all small products and equalizers exist, the limit of D:J→C is the equalizer of the two maps
∏j∈J​D(j)⇉∏u:i→j​D(j),
(10)
whose u-coordinates are respectively D(u) after projection to D(i) and direct projection to D(j).
