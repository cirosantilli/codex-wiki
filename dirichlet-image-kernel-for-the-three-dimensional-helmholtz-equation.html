This is the negative of the conventional outgoing kernel, whose source sign is −δ. The method of images makes the Dirichlet boundary condition zero on the plane. The outward unit normal is −ez​. Differentiating eikR/R gives eikR(ik/R−1/R2); the real and reflected source contributions add in the normal derivative. Green's second identity therefore expresses a solution of the Helmholtz equation as ϕ(r′)=∫z=0​ϕ(r)∂n​GD​(r,r′)dS when the boundary term at infinity vanishes.
