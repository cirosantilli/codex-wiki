Signal peptidase

 Home Biology Protein Protein targeting Signal peptide
OurBigBook.com Words: 132
A signal peptidase removes a cleavable signal peptide; a suitable cleavage site and access are required.[ref]
For the stipulated 180-amino acid residue precursor under ER signal sequence, removal of the first 20 amino acid residues leaves 160. Now stipulate that positions 101–120 of that precursor are instead retained as a stop-transfer sequence. Their positions in the mature chain become 81–100, because the numbering origin changes when the first 20 positions are removed. The mature chain still has 160 amino acid residues, but its membrane-spanning segment is retained. A second hypothetical chain with an uncleaved internal signal anchor loses no positions at all. These are conditional bookkeeping examples, not predictions from invented biological sequences.
