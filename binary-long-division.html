Binary long division (x=qy+r,0≤r<y)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Integer Euclidean division Long division
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
For integers x≥0 and y>0, an integer division algorithm determines the binary numeral system digits of a quotient by testing and conditionally subtracting successively halved multiples of the divisor. The loop invariant x=qy+r, together with 0≤r<y2j, makes each step a halving of the remaining quotient interval. Start with q=0,r=x and j large enough that x<y2j. To decrease j by one, subtract y2j−1 from r and add 2j−1 to q exactly when r≥y2j−1. Both branches preserve the displayed equality and establish 0≤r<y2j−1. When j=0, the quotient and remainder conditions hold.
