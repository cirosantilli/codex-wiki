Fractional entropy of a relativistically decoupled species (sX​/stotal​=g∗s,X​/(g∗s,X​+g∗s,bath,D​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmological entropy conservation Thermal decoupling in cosmology
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
If a species and the remaining bath share a temperature at decoupling, their initial entropy ratio is the ratio of their entropy-weighted degrees of freedom. Subsequent conservation of each sector's comoving entropy keeps that fraction constant, even when the bath heats relative to the relic. For a two-state neutrino species leaving an ideal Standard Model bath, the fraction is (7/4)/(106.75+7/4)≃1.61%.
