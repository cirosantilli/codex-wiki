Juan Fernández Ridge

 ... Geophysics Solid Earth Mantle convection Plate tectonics Tectonic plate Nazca Plate
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A volcanic ridge system entering the South American subduction zone near central Chile, associated with the southern transition of the Pampean flat-slab region. Its crustal structure, hydration and past geometry constrain explanations of the slab dip; present-day bathymetry alone does not give the buoyancy of its already subducted continuation.
