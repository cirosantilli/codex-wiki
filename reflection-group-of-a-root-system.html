Reflection group of a root system (W(Φ))

 ... Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Cartan subalgebra Root-space decomposition Root system Weyl reflection
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 5
The reflection group of a root system Φ is the subgroup of the orthogonal group generated by the reflections sα​ for α∈Φ. It permutes the roots and acts freely and transitively on the fundamental systems.
