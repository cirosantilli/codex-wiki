Epsilon zero (ε0​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Ordinal Ordinal exponentiation
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
The ordinal ε0​ is the least fixed point of α↦ωα. It is the supremum of 1,ω,ωω,ωωω,…, and is countable.
