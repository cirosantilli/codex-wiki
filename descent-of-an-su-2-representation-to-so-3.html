Descent of an SU(2) representation to SO(3)

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group Adjoint representation of a Lie group Adjoint double cover from SU(2) to SO(3)
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
A SU(2) representation descends to the SO(3) group exactly when −I acts trivially. In Symn(C2) it acts by (−1)n, so descent holds precisely for even n, or integer spin j=n/2. This is the inflation of a group representation criterion for the kernel {±I}. Half-integer-spin representations instead remain representations of the covering group.
