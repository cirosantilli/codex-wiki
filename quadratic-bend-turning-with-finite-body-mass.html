Quadratic-bend turning with finite body mass (θ∞​=2UAC+Mb​(B1​−M0​)C​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biological fluid dynamics Lighthill elongated-body theory Recoil correction in elongated-body theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 98
Put Bj​=[m(x)(x−xˉ)j]0L​, Mj​=∫m(x)(x−xˉ)jdx, A=∫α(t)dt, and C=B0​(B2​−M1​)−B1​(B1​−M0​). For quadratic bending h0​=−α(t)(x−xˉ)2, the linear recoil correction in elongated-body theory with a stable post-stroke relaxation gives the displayed angle. It follows by setting time derivatives to zero in the integrated lateral and angular momentum equations. The simpler 2UA is exact in the zero-body-mass limit, or when B1​=M0​, but is not a generic finite-body-mass identity. For L=1, xˉ=1/2, and m(x)=m∗​x2, it becomes 2UA/(1+2Mb​/m∗​). Body deformation inertia affects derivative forcing during the stroke, not this final integral relation when the deformation and its time derivative return to zero.
