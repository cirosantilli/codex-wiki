Neutral gas density from an absorption distribution (ΩHI​=cρcrit,0​H0​mH​​∫NfX​(N,z)dN)

 ... Physics Mathematical physics Hydrogen atom Lyman-alpha line Lyman-alpha absorption Neutral-hydrogen column-density distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
Let fX​=∂2N/(∂N∂X) count neutral hydrogen absorbers per column density and absorption distance. The summed column per unit proper light-path length is the mean neutral number density. Since dℓ=cdz/[(1+z)H(z)] and dX/dz=H0​(1+z)2/H(z), that density is (H0​/c)(1+z)3∫NfX​dN. Dividing the proper mass density by (1+z)3 gives the comoving density in the displayed formula. The denominator is today's critical density; including neutral helium requires a stated mass correction. For a distribution per redshift, replace H0​fX​ by H(z)fz​/(1+z)2.
