Maxwell Lagrangian (L=−41​Fμν​Fμν)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 117 Articles: 2
The source-free electromagnetic Lagrangian density is quadratic in the electromagnetic field tensor. Its Euler-Lagrange field equation is ∂μ​Fμν=0. Antisymmetry of F makes the density invariant under gauge transformations, and contraction of its indices makes it a Lorentz scalar.
