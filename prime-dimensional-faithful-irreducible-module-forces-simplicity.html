Prime-dimensional faithful irreducible module forces simplicity (dimV prime,V faithful irreducible ⟹ L simple)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 106
Let a semisimple complex Lie algebra L=⨁i​Li​ act faithfully and irreducibly on a space V of prime dimension. Complete reducibility for L1​ splits V into isotypic components. The commuting other ideals preserve these components, so irreducibility leaves just one, of the form W1​⊗HomL1​​(W1​,V). Repeating with the other factors gives V≅⨂i​Wi​, with each Wi​ an irreducible module for its simple ideal.
Faithfulness makes every factor nontrivial. A nonabelian simple Lie algebra has only the trivial one-dimensional module, so dimWi​≥2. Their product is the prime dimV, forcing just one simple ideal. Hence L is simple. This is a representation-dimension obstruction, independent of the classification of root systems.
