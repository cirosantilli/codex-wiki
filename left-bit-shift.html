Left bit shift

 Home Computer science Programming language Bit shift
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A bit shift toward more significant positions, filling low positions with zeros. On an unbounded nonnegative integer, shifting left by k places multiplies it by 2k; fixed-width representations may discard overflowing high bits.
