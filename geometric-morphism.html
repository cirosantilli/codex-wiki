Geometric morphism (f∗⊣f∗​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Elementary topos
OurBigBook.com Words: 987 Articles: 18
A geometric morphism f:E→F is an adjunction f∗⊣f∗​ with finite-limit-preserving inverse image f∗:F→E. Inverse image also preserves colimits as a left adjoint. An extra left adjoint f!​⊣f∗ makes it essential.
