Exponential of covariant set-valued functors ((HG)(c)=Nat(C(c,−)×G,H))

 ... Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Adjoint functors Cartesian closed category Exponential object
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
For a small category, this formula defines the exponential object in its covariant set-valued functor category. An arrow c→d acts by precomposition with C(d,−)→C(c,−). Evaluation applies a natural transformation to (1c​,z); currying a map P×G→H sends p∈P(c) to the transformation (f,z)↦θ(P(f)p,z). The formulas prove the universal property directly, without an adjoint existence theorem.
