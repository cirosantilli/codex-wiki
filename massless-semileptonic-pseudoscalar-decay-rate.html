Massless semileptonic pseudoscalar decay rate (dΓ/ds=GF2​∣V∣2λ3/2∣f+​(s)∣2/(192π3M3))

For a pseudoscalar meson of mass M decaying into a pseudoscalar meson of mass m and two massless leptons through a weak charged current, the transverse massless leptonic current selects f+​(s). With hadronic current normalized as (p+k)μf+​(s)+(p−k)μf−​(s) and flavour coefficient V,
dsdΓ​=192π3M3GF2​∣V∣2​λ(s,M2,m2)3/2∣f+​(s)∣2,0≤s≤(M−m)2.
(2)
The Källén function has mass dimension four, and the coefficient has mass dimension −7. This decay rate assumes the stated form-factor normalization and no additional isospin factor. It follows by the integrated massless leptonic tensor and the pion momentum change of variable in the parent's centre-of-momentum frame.
