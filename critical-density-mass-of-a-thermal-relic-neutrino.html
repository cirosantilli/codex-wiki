A standard thermal Cosmic neutrino background with one populated neutrino and one antineutrino helicity per species has nν​+nνˉ​=3nγ​/11 after electron-positron annihilation in cosmology. If this species is nonrelativistic today and dominates a spatially flat matter universe, equate mν​(nν​+nνˉ​) to the critical density 3H02​MP2​/(8π), using the unreduced Planck mass. The photon number density gives the displayed conditional mass. Extra populated states or several massive species change the abundance count; vacuum-dominated cosmology changes the density available for matter.
