Leaky porous gravity current

 Home Physics Branch of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Porous gravity current
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
A leaky porous gravity current loses flux through a localized fracture or a distributed permeable boundary. At a point fracture, mass conservation makes the horizontal Darcy flux discontinuous by exactly the leakage rate.
