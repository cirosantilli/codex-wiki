Global section functor (Γ(X,−))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules
OurBigBook.com Words: 222 Articles: 6
The global section functor sends a sheaf F on X to Γ(X,F). It is left exact, and its right derived functors are sheaf cohomology.
