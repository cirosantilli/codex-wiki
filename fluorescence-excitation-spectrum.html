Fluorescence excitation spectrum (Fex​(λex​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Fluorescence
OurBigBook.com Words: 142
A fluorescence excitation spectrum scans excitation wavelength while detecting fluorescence in a fixed emission band. A fluorescence emission spectrum does the converse. The inspected spectrofluorometer example uses an excitation monochromator scan with the emission monochromator fixed, and reverses their roles for emission.[ref]
An operational model explains why this need not reproduce absorption. For one dilute, unsaturated emitting species, write a(λ) for absorbed fraction, Rin​(λ) for incident photon rate, Φ(λ) for fluorescence quantum yield, and ηband​(λ) for the fraction of emitted photons counted in the selected band. The background-subtracted count rate is
Cex​(λ)=Rin​(λ)a(λ)Φ(λ)ηband​(λ).
(1)
Only after correcting incident photon rate, and with constant yield and emission-band collection, is this proportional to absorbed fraction. A nonfluorescent absorber contributes to absorption but not this readout. Thick samples, multiple emitting species, and reabsorption of fluorescence complicate the model.
