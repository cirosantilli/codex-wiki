For s>1/2, restriction to the coordinate hyperplane extends uniquely to a bounded linear map
γ:Hs(Rn)⟶Hs−1/2(Rn−1).
(35)
In Fourier variables, Cauchy--Schwarz bounds the integral over the normal frequency because (1+t2)−s is integrable exactly when s>1/2.
