Fermionic coherent state (a∣θ⟩=θ∣θ⟩)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Coherent state
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 1
For a single fermionic annihilation operator a and an independent odd Grassmann variable θ, a fermionic coherent state is an extended state obeying a∣θ⟩=θ∣θ⟩. With {a,θ}=0 one may take ∣θ⟩=exp(−θa†)∣0⟩. Its overlap is exponential in the Grassmann labels. A resolution of identity with a Berezin integral gives the time-slice construction of a fermionic path integral. These formal eigenstates use Grassmann-valued coefficients.
