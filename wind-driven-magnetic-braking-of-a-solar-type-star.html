Wind-driven magnetic braking of a solar-type star (J˙≃−M˙w​ΩRA2​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar evolution Stellar wind
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 1
A magnetized stellar wind extracts angular momentum through matter transport and Maxwell stress tensor. The Alfvén radius supplies an effective lever arm larger than the stellar radius, with geometry factors absorbed into an averaged lever arm. Dynamo action couples the torque to rotation by supplying the magnetic field. For a simplified radial wind of fixed speed, RA2​ scales as B∗2​R∗4​/(μ0​M˙w​vw​), so unsaturated B∗​∝Ω gives a torque proportional to −Ω3. Magnetic topology and wind thermodynamics can alter this scaling.
