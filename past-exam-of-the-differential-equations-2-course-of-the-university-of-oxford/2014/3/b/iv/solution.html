Write y(x)=Y(X) with X=(1−x)/ϵ​. The transformed equation is
Y′′=Y−(1−ϵ​X)2+ϵ(2+Y2).
(38)
Since the rescaled forcing contains ϵ​, an inner expansion naturally uses powers of ϵ​. Its leading term satisfies
Y0′′​=Y0​−1,Y0​(0)=0.
(39)
The asymptotic matching condition with the leading outer solution is
Y0​(X)⟶y0​(1)=1(X→∞).
(40)
The general solution is Y0​=1+AeX+Be−X. Matching eliminates AeX, and the boundary condition gives B=−1. Hence
Y0​(X)=1−e−X,yinner​(x)∼1−exp(−ϵ​1−x​).​
(41)
This tends to the boundary value zero at x=1 and to the matching value one on the interior side of the layer. The negative sign in the exponential follows from measuring the inner variable inward from the right endpoint.
For an optional uniform leading approximation, combine the leading inner and outer terms and subtract their common overlap value one. The additive composite expansion is
ycomp​(x)=x2−exp(−ϵ​1−x​).​
(42)
It satisfies the right boundary condition exactly; its left-end discrepancy is the exponentially small value −e−1/ϵ​. The requested leading inner expansion is Y0​ above.
