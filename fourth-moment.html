Fourth moment (E[X4])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Expected value Moment
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The fourth moment is E[X4]. Its finiteness is equivalent to square integrability of X2. For centered variables, dividing it by the squared variance gives kurtosis.
