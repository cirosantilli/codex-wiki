Spherical pendulum (L=21​mr2(θ˙2+sin2θϕ˙​2)+mgrcosθ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Simple pendulum
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 1
A point mass constrained to a fixed distance r from a pivot and free to move in three dimensions under uniform gravitational acceleration. With the vertical axis downward and polar angle θ measured from it, the Lagrangian is the expression in the title. The azimuth ϕ is a cyclic coordinate, so mr2sin2θϕ˙​ is conserved angular momentum about the vertical axis. A rigid massless rod enforces the constraint without the loss-of-tension limitation of a string.
