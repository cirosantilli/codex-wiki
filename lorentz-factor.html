Lorentz factor (γ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 3k Articles: 19
For a relative speed 0≤v<c, the Lorentz factor is
γ=1−v2/c2​1​.
(4)
At v=0.6c, γ=1/1−0.36​=1/0.8=1.25. Thus a moving clock accumulates 0.8 seconds of proper time per laboratory second, and a parallel moving rod has 0.8 times its rest length. The Lorentz factor approaches one at small speed and grows without bound as v approaches the speed of light; a massive object's rest frame never requires a boost at v=c.
