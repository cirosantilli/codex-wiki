Power boundedness of a two-level Fourier scheme

For a two-level Fourier recurrence un+1=a(θ)un+b(θ)un−1, the amplification matrix is
T(θ)=(a(θ)1​b(θ)0​).
(13)
Stability requires its powers to be uniformly bounded in the time index and the mesh frequencies. If its two amplification roots have modulus at most one and their separation has a positive mesh-independent lower bound, the eigenvectors (ξj​,1)T give a uniformly bounded diagonalization of a matrix, proving stability. A repeated unit-modulus root of this companion matrix instead produces a Jordan block and linear growth in time. Thus checking only the moduli of the roots is insufficient.
