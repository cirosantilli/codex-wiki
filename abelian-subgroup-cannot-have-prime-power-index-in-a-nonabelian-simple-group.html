Abelian subgroup cannot have prime-power index in a nonabelian simple group

 ... Algebra Group theory Group Finite group Burnside's theorem Prime-power conjugacy-class obstruction to simplicity
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
If an abelian subgroup A has index pa, the centralizer of any 1=g∈A contains A, so the class size of g divides pa. The prime-power conjugacy-class obstruction to simplicity rules this out. If A=1, the group is a p-group and has nontrivial center of a group, also ruling out nonabelian simplicity.
