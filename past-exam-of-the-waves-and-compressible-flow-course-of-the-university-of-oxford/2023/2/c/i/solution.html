W(k,z,0)=πhCe−ϵh∣k∣sin(πz/h),Wt​(k,z,0)=0.

The transformed evolution is a harmonic oscillator for each k, with
ω(k)=k2+π2/h2+α2/4​αg​k​.
(19)
Therefore inversion gives
W(x,z,t)=2hC​sin(hπz​)∫−∞∞​e−ϵh∣k∣cos(ω(k)t)eikxdk​.
(20)
