Generalized linear model

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling
OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 109
A generalized linear model takes independent responses from an exponential dispersion family and relates their means to linear predictors by g(μi​)=xiT​β. The response distribution, systematic component, link, dispersion, and known observation weights together specify the model.
