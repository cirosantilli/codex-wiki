Jump of a fractional power across a branch cut (F−​=e−2πiαF+​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Branch cut
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
For an entire function f, let F(z)=f(z)z−α with noninteger real α, and choose the branch of the complex logarithm with ϕ<argz<ϕ+2π. On the cut ray z=reiϕ, the bank whose argument approaches ϕ has value F+​=f(reiϕ)r−αe−iαϕ, while the other bank has argument approaching ϕ+2π and value F−​=e−2πiαF+​. The signed jump is therefore (1−e−2πiα)F+​ when defined as F+​−F−​. Stating the branch and order of subtraction removes the sign ambiguity. A keyhole contour uses this phase relation to convert its two bank integrals into a constant multiple of one real or radial integral.
