Noether normalization

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 167 Articles: 2
Every finitely generated algebra A over a field has algebraically independent elements y1​,…,yd​ such that A is finite as a module over the polynomial subalgebra k[y1​,…,yd​]. The integer d is the Krull dimension of A.
