Dominant weight multiplicity formula for sl3 (ma,b​(p,q)=max{0,1+min(a,b,r,s)})

 ... Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Highest-weight representation Formal character of a weight module sl3 interlacing character formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 120
For the irreducible highest-weight representation of the special linear Lie algebra sl3​ with Dynkin labels (a,b), let (p,q) be a dominant integral weight. Put r=(2a+b−2p−q)/3 and s=(a+2b−p−2q)/3. If these are not integers, the weight multiplicity is zero; otherwise it is max{0,1+min(a,b,r,s)}.
To derive the formula, use the sl3 interlacing character formula with top row (a+b,b,0). The target diagonal exponents are (s+p+q,s+q,s), and an interlacing middle row (P,Q) has P+Q=2s+p+2q, with bottom entry s+p+q. Thus
max{b,2s+p+2q−b,s+p+q}≤P≤min{a+b,2s+p+2q}.
(45)
Every integral P in this interval gives exactly one Gelfand–Tsetlin basis vector. The upper bound minus the lower bound is the minimum of six differences: a,s,r,r+p+q,b,s+q. Since p,q≥0, this minimum reduces to min(a,b,r,s). Counting the interval proves the formula, including absent weights of a representation. This converts a two-dimensional weight diagram calculation into four elementary boundary distances.
