de Sitter spacetime

 Home Physics Branch of physics General relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 261 Articles: 8
De Sitter spacetime is the maximally symmetric Lorentzian spacetime of constant positive curvature. Its expanding flat slicing has scale factor a(τ)=−1/(Hτ) in conformal time τ<0.
