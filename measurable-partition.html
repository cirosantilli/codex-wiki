Measurable partition (ξ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 252 Articles: 6
A countable measurable partition is a countable family of pairwise disjoint measurable sets whose union has full measure. Its members are its atoms; null atoms can be discarded in statements made almost everywhere.
