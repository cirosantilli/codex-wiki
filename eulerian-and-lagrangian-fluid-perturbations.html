Eulerian and Lagrangian fluid perturbations

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 3
For a background scalar field f and fluid displacement ξ, the Lagrangian perturbation follows the displaced fluid element while the Eulerian perturbation compares fields at one fixed position. To first order,
ΔL​f=δf+ξ⋅∇f.
