Pullback tangent bundle (f∗TN)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Pullback vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 208 Articles: 3
For a smooth map between manifolds f:M→N, the pullback vector bundle f∗TN has fibre Tf(p)​N at p∈M. A smooth section is a vector field along a map. It allows one to differentiate maps and curves without requiring them to be invertible.
