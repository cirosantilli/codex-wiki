Unbounded typewriter sequence with vanishing L1 norm (∥fn​∥1​→0,limsupn​fn​(x)=∞)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Lp space Typewriter sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 97
For each m≥1, enumerate the m2 half-open intervals [j/m2,(j+1)/m2) partitioning [0,1) and use the functions m times their indicator functions. Concatenate these finite blocks in increasing m. Every function in block m has L1 norm 1/m, which tends to zero. Every x∈(0,1) is in one interval of every block, so the values at that point have a subsequence increasing to infinity. Thus small integral norm does not bound the pointwise upper limit, even when every function is nonnegative. In contrast, Fatou's lemma prevents a strictly positive lower limit everywhere on an interval when the norms tend to zero.
