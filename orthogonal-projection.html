Orthogonal projection (P)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Hilbert space Hilbert projection theorem Orthogonal decomposition by a closed subspace
OurBigBook.com Words: 321 Articles: 5
The orthogonal projection PF​ onto a closed subspace F sends x to the component in the decomposition x=PF​x+(I−PF​)x with (I−PF​)x∈F⊥. It is a contraction: ∥PF​x∥≤∥x∥.
