Autocovariance of an AR(1) process (γ(h)=σ2ϕ∣h∣/(1−ϕ2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 79
For the stationary causal AR(1) with ∣ϕ∣<1, matching terms in its infinite moving-average representation gives, for h≥0,
γ(h)=σ2∑j=0∞​ϕjϕj+h=1−ϕ2σ2ϕh​.
(16)
Symmetry of the autocovariance extends this to negative lags. Dividing by γ(0) gives the autocorrelation ρ(h)=ϕ∣h∣. The geometric series explains both the enlarged stationary variance and the diminishing memory: all past disturbances contribute to the present, but their squared weights have a finite sum. At ϕ=0, the process reduces to shifted white noise and all nonzero-lag autocorrelations vanish. See Bartlett, lecture 4: AR(1) representation and mean-square convergence.
