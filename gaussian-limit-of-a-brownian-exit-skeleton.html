Gaussian limit of a Brownian exit skeleton (Bσ1k​​/k​ ⇒ N(0,1))

 ... Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Brownian exit time Brownian exit from an interval Brownian exit-time skeleton
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
The position is the sum of k independent symmetric signs. Its characteristic function after division by k​ is cos(u/k​)k→e−u2/2. The Lévy continuity theorem gives the normal distribution limit. Scaling the same skeleton to radius 2−n and using Brownian exit-skeleton clock convergence identifies its other limit as BEσ1​​, forcing Eσ1​=1.
