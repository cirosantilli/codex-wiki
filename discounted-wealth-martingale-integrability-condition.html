Discounted wealth martingale integrability condition

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Arbitrage Self-financing portfolio Discounted wealth equation in discrete time
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
An integrable predictable strategy whose discounted gains ϕn​ΔSn​ are integrable preserves the martingale property of discounted prices in finite discrete time. Bounded holdings suffice. Without integrability the claim is false: let S0​=3/2, S1​=1+U, S2​=1+U+ε/2, where U is uniform on (0,1) and ε is an independent fair sign. Holding zero stock first and 1/U shares in the second period, funded by the bank, gives zero-initial-cost terminal wealth ε/(2U), which is not integrable. Replication pricing requires the corresponding true-martingale condition, not merely formal self-financing.
