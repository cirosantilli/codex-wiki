Lick Observatory iodine-cell radial-velocity method

 ... Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Exoplanet atmospheric observatory Doppler spectroscopy Iodine-cell Doppler calibration
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
At Lick Observatory, the Hamilton spectrograph records starlight with an imprinted iodine reference. The reference tracks instrumental wavelength and profile changes during the science exposure. Barycentric correction and a time series of relative stellar shifts then support a dynamical companion model.
