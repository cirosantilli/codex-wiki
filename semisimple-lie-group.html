Semisimple Lie group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie group
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
A real or complex Lie group is semisimple when its Lie algebra is semisimple. For a real group, the Killing form then defines a nondegenerate bi-invariant pseudo-Riemannian metric. Compact semisimple groups have negative-definite Killing form; noncompact examples such as SL(2,R) have indefinite Killing form. A disconnected group can also have a semisimple Lie algebra, so connectedness is a separate hypothesis when integrating infinitesimal actions.
