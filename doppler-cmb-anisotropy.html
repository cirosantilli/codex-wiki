Doppler CMB anisotropy

 ... Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Photon temperature multipole Photon monopole Sachs-Wolfe combination
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The Doppler CMB anisotropy is the line-of-sight velocity contribution from the last-scattering plasma. With the convention used here it contributes −n⋅ve​ to the observed fractional temperature perturbation.
