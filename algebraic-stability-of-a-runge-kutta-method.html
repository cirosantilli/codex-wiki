Algebraic stability of a Runge-Kutta method

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Runge-Kutta method Implicit Runge-Kutta method Collocation Runge-Kutta method

A Runge-Kutta method is algebraically stable when bi​≥0 and the matrix M with Mij​=bi​aij​+bj​aji​−bi​bj​ is positive semidefinite. Algebraic stability implies contractivity for dissipative differential equations.
