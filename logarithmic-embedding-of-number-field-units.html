Logarithmic embedding of number field units

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Dirichlet unit theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 148 Articles: 3
Choose the real embeddings σi​ and one embedding τj​ from each complex-conjugate pair. The weighted logarithmic embedding is
Log(u)=(log∣σ1​u∣,…,log∣σr​u∣,2log∣τ1​u∣,…,2log∣τs​u∣).
(19)
For a unit, the sum of these coordinates is log∣NK/Q​(u)∣=0.
