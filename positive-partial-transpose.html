Positive partial transpose (PPT)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Separable quantum state Partial transpose
OurBigBook.com Words: 84 Articles: 1
A bipartite density operator has positive partial transpose when ρTB​≥0. Every separable quantum state has this property, since transposing one factor of a positive product state preserves positivity and the separable sum preserves it as well. The property does not depend on which local basis defines the transpose.
