Axis stabilizer in the full octahedral symmetry group (GRe3​​≅D8​×C2​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group action Symmetry group of a cube
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
An opposite-vertex line in the full octahedral symmetry group is an unoriented coordinate axis. Its stabilizer subgroup has the block form diag(A,ε), where A is a signed two-dimensional permutation matrix and ε=±1. The eight possibilities for A form the dihedral group of a square, of order eight; the independent axial sign gives a cyclic group of order two. Thus the stabilizer is their direct product, of order sixteen. It is larger than the stabilizer of an individual vertex, because exchanging the two endpoints still fixes the line.
