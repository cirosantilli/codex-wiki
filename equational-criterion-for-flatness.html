Equational criterion for flatness (∑i​ai​mi​=0⟹mi​=∑j​bij​nj​,∑i​ai​bij​=0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Flat module
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
For a flat module M over a commutative ring, every finite relation among elements of M is a finite combination of relations among elements of the ring itself, in the displayed sense. To obtain this, tensor the kernel of the linear map Ar→A, (ui​)↦∑i​ai​ui​, with M. Flatness says that this tensor maps onto the kernel in Mr. Writing an element of that tensor as a finite sum gives the columns bij​ and the elements nj​. The converse follows by applying this condition to relations in a tensor product to prove that tensoring preserves injections.
