For a planar tangential charge-one nematic disclination, ∇⋅Q=−(λ′/2+λ/r)er​. This follows by differentiating the tensor dyad, using ∇⋅eθ​=0 and (eθ​⋅∇)eθ​=−er​/r. With bulk density aλ2/2+bλ4/4 and divergence elastic density K(λ′/2+λ/r)2/2, radial variation of 2π∫rfdr gives
aλ+bλ3−4K​(λ′′+λ′/r−4λ/r2)=0.
(5)
For a<0, a regular melted core has λ=O(r2) and an ordered far field has λ0​=−a/b​. Linearizing the bulk derivative gives λ/λ0​=1−K/(2∣a∣r2)+O(r−4). The core scale is of order K/∣a∣​; the amplitude disturbance has an integrable algebraic tail, rather than exactly compact support.
