Meromorphic continuation of a Mellin transform from an asymptotic expansion (Ress=−σj​​Mf(s)=cj​)

Suppose f decays faster than every power at infinity and, near zero, f(y)=∑j=1r​cj​yσj​+O(yσr+1​) for every r, with σj​ strictly increasing and tending to infinity. Subtracting finitely many terms in the integral over (0,1) gives
Mf(s)=∫1∞​f(y)ys−1dy+∑j=1r​s+σj​cj​​+∫01​(f(y)−∑j=1r​cj​yσj​)ys−1dy.
(6)
The last integral is holomorphic on Res>−σr+1​. These expressions continue the Mellin transform meromorphically to the whole plane, with simple poles precisely at −σj​ for nonzero cj​.
