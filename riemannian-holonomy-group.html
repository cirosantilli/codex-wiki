Riemannian holonomy group (Holp​(g)⊂O(Tp​M,gp​))

 ... Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Principal bundle Connection (principal bundle) Horizontal distribution of a principal connection Holonomy
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 3
For a connected Riemannian manifold, the full holonomy group consists of parallel transport maps around all piecewise smooth loops based at p. Metric compatibility makes them orthogonal. Basepoint changes conjugate this group by parallel transport. This is full holonomy, not just the subgroup using contractible loops.
