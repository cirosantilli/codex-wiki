Conformal boundary (g​=Ω2g,Ω=0,dΩ=0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Conformal map Conformal geometry Conformal compactification
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
A boundary obtained by extending the rescaled metric g​=Ω2g smoothly and nondegenerately to a hypersurface on which Ω vanishes with nonzero derivative. Its causal character is measured using the extended Lorentzian metric, not the divergent physical metric. In signature (+−−−), a timelike normal makes the boundary spacelike, a spacelike normal makes it timelike, and a null normal makes it null. These cases lead to different causal and initial-value properties. The conformal cylinder of two-dimensional de Sitter spacetime has spacelike past/future boundaries; the conformal strip of two-dimensional anti-de Sitter spacetime has two timelike boundaries.
