Cubic radial coefficient of a quadratic planar map (ℓ=Re(λg21​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory Neimark–Sacker bifurcation
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
In a complex coordinate write z′=λz+f20​z2+f11​zz+f02​z2, with ∣λ∣=1. Away from strong resonances of a planar map, the quadratic change z=w+h20​w2+h11​ww+h02​w2 has h20​=f20​/(λ2−λ), h11​=f11​/(1−λ) and h02​=f02​/(λ2−λ). Substituting and collecting w2w gives g21​=2f20​h11​+f11​h20​+f11​h11​+2f02​h02​. The cubic radial coefficient is ℓ=Re(λg21​); ℓ<0 gives a supercritical crossing when the multiplier modulus increases through one.
