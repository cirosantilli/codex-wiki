Stored excitation in a pulsed laser

OurBigBook.com Words: 309
A short optical pulse can draw on previously stored excitation. In the original calculation below, C=100, K=20, β=10−4, u(0)=s(0)=0, and r=3 for 0≤x<4, followed by r=0. The cavity field initially remains small while pumping fills the upper state. Once sufficient gain develops, the photon population grows and depletes the stored inversion. Continuing pump action can refill it, giving relaxation oscillations in this simple model. This is not a fit to Maiman's recorded output.

The model also preserves 0≤u≤1 and s≥0: at each boundary its derivatives point inward or along the boundary.
