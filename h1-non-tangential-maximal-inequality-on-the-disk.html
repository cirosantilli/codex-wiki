For an analytic H1 space on the unit disk function, its fixed-aperture non-tangential maximal function obeys the displayed strong L1 bound. First replace f by fR​(z)=f(Rz), R<1. The fact that modulus powers of holomorphic functions are subharmonic gives ∣fR​∣1/2≤P[∣f(Rξ)∣1/2]. Since a Poisson maximal function is dominated by the Hardy-Littlewood maximal function, the strong L2 Hardy-Littlewood maximal inequality yields ∫NA​fR​≤CA​∫∣f(Rξ)∣≤CA​∥f∥1​. Pointwise interior convergence and the Fatou lemma remove R. Analyticity permits this endpoint estimate; general L1 boundary data have only a weak L1 maximal bound.
