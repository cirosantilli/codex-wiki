Magnus effect

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
The Magnus effect is a transverse force on a spinning body in relative motion through a fluid. Rotation changes the surrounding circulation, pressure distribution and wake. In a two-dimensional cylinder model, the Kutta–Joukowski theorem gives force per unit span F′=ρU×(Γz^), with relative fluid velocity U and circulation Γ positive anticlockwise viewed from +z. Real viscosity and boundary-layer behavior help determine the circulation; impermeability alone does not determine it from spin rate.
