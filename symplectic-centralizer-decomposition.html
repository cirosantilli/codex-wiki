Symplectic centralizer decomposition (Csp(V)​(a)≅sp(V0​)⊕⨁{λ,−λ}​gl(Vλ​))

Let a be a toral subalgebra and an abelian Lie algebra in the symplectic Lie algebra, over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Its defining representation has a weight decomposition. The symplectic Lie algebra identity gives (λ(a)+μ(a))ω(v,w)=0 for v∈Vλ​, w∈Vμ​, so ω(Vλ​,Vμ​)=0 unless μ=−λ. Since ω is a nondegenerate bilinear form, its restrictions give a perfect pairing between Vλ​ and V−λ​, and a nondegenerate bilinear form on V0​.
An element of the centralizer of a Lie subalgebra preserves each weight space. On V0​ it is any element of sp(V0​). On each nonzero pair, its action A on Vλ​ is arbitrary, and the perfect pairing uniquely forces the action on V−λ​ to be the negative dual map −A∗. These independent choices preserve Lie brackets, proving the displayed direct sum of Lie algebras; the sum runs over one representative per nonzero pair.
This centralizer of a Lie subalgebra is an abelian Lie algebra precisely when V0​=0 and all the nonzero weight spaces are one-dimensional. Indeed, glm​ is nonabelian for m≥2 by [E11​,E12​]=E12​, while every nonzero symplectic vector space contains a two-dimensional symplectic subspace on which [diag(1,−1),E12​]=2E12​. In the stated case the surviving summands are copies of gl1​ and commute. Repeated weights of a representation, including a zero one, are therefore exactly what obstructs this commutativity.
