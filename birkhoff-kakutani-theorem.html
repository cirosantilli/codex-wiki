Birkhoff-Kakutani theorem (G metrizable⟺G first countable)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Left-invariant group metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 163 Articles: 1
A Hausdorff topological group admits a compatible left-invariant group metric exactly when it has a countable neighbourhood basis at the identity element. Choose symmetric neighbourhoods of the identity element Un​ with Un+13​⊂Un​ and trivial intersection. Assign a step in Un​ cost 2−n, and take the infimum of total costs of chains joining two points. The dyadic product estimate for group neighbourhoods makes this a genuine compatible metric. The Hausdorff assumption separates distinct points; without it the same construction can give only a pseudometric.
