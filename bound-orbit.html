Bound orbit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Orbit (dynamical system)
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
An orbit that remains within a bounded spatial region for all times for which the motion is defined. In an attractive inverse-distance central potential with zero potential energy at infinity, a noncolliding orbit is bound when its total mechanical energy is negative. That sign test depends on the potential and its energy origin; it is not a universal definition of bounded motion. A closed orbit repeats its position and velocity, whereas a general bound orbit can precess without closing.
