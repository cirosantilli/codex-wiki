Electric multipole expansion

 Home Physics Branch of physics Electromagnetism Electrostatics
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 4
For a localized charge distribution, the far electric potential is an inverse-radius expansion whose first terms are the total charge monopole, the electric dipole moment, and the electric quadrupole moment.
