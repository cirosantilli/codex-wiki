Spectral norm bound for a centered Bernoulli adjacency matrix (E∥A−EA∥op​≤Cn​)

For independent Bernoulli distribution upper-triangular entries of a symmetric zero-diagonal adjacency matrix of a graph, put W=A−EA. For a unit vector x, x⊤Wx=2∑i<j​xi​xj​Wij​ has sub-Gaussian variance proxy at most ∑i<j​xi2​xj2​≤1/2, by the Hoeffding lemma. Hence P(∣x⊤Wx∣>u)≤2e−u2. The volumetric bound for Euclidean metric nets gives a 1/4-metric net of the unit sphere with at most 9n points. The quadratic form net bound gives ∥W∥op​≤2maxx​∣x⊤Wx∣ on that net. The union bound gives P(∥W∥op​>2nlog9+log2+s​)≤e−s; integrating this tail proves the displayed expectation bound.
