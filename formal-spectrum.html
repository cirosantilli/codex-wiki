Formal spectrum (SpfA)

For a complete ring with an adic topology defined by I, the formal spectrum has the prime ideals containing I as its points and structure sheaf lim​n​OSpec(A/In+1)​. On a principal open subset D(f) its sections form the completed localization of an adic ring, not generally the ordinary localization Af​.
