Local isometry

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry First fundamental form
OurBigBook.com Words: 418 Articles: 8
A local isometry between Riemannian manifolds is a local diffeomorphism f with f∗gN​=gM​. Equivalently, its differentials are isometric linear isomorphisms between tangent spaces. It preserves the Levi-Civita connection, intrinsic sectional curvature, and affinely parametrized geodesics. A map whose differentials are merely isometric injections is an isometric immersion; the local diffeomorphism requirement distinguishes these notions.
