P-adic valuation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 68 Articles: 2
For a prime p and a nonzero integer N, the p-adic valuation is
vp​(N)=max{a∈Z≥0​:pa∣N}.
(8)
It extends to nonzero rational numbers by vp​(a/b)=vp​(a)−vp​(b).
