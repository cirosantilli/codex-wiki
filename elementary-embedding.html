Elementary embedding (j:M→N)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 423 Articles: 11
An elementary embedding preserves every first-order formula with parameters from its domain. Its critical point crit(j) is the least ordinal moved by a nonidentity embedding.
