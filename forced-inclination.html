Forced inclination (wf​)

 ... Planetary science Planetary system dynamics Disturbing function Secular perturbation Laplace-Lagrange secular theory Complex inclination
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
The particular, forced part of a complex inclination solution. For a fixed, circular, inclined inner planet and an exterior test ring, linear Laplace-Lagrange secular theory gives w˙=−iν(w−wp​), so wf​=wp​ and w=wp​+(w(0)−wp​)e−iνt. The ring describes a circle about the planetary tilt in an Argand diagram; relative to the planet, its mutual inclination stays constant while its ascending node precesses.
