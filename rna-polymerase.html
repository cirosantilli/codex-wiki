RNA polymerase

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Gene expression Transcription (biology)
OurBigBook.com Words: 150 Articles: 8
An RNA polymerase used in transcription is an enzyme that synthesizes RNA from a DNA template. Unlike a replicating DNA polymerase, it can start a new strand without a pre-existing paired strand end; initiation still requires the appropriate DNA signals and associated machinery. See Alberts et al.: RNA polymerase and transcription initiation.
