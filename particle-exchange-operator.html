Particle exchange operator (P12​)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum mechanics Tensor product of quantum systems Multiparticle quantum state
OurBigBook.com Words: 192 Articles: 6
For two particles, the exchange operator sends ∣α⟩⊗∣β⟩ to ∣β⟩⊗∣α⟩. It has eigenvalues +1 on symmetric states and −1 on antisymmetric states.
