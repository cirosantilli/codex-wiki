Picard injection for holomorphic projective bundles ((M,n)↦p∗M⊗L⊗n)

 ... Ringed space Sheaf of modules Locally free sheaf Line bundle Picard group Holomorphic Picard group
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
For a rank-two holomorphic vector bundle on a nonempty complex manifold, this map from Pichol​(X)×Z to Pichol​(P(E)) is injective. Fibre degree detects n. If p∗M is trivial, a nonvanishing holomorphic section is constant along every compact projective fibre in a local bundle chart, so it descends to a nonvanishing section of M. The proof requires no global section of p.
