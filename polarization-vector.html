Polarization vector (εμ(p,λ))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field
OurBigBook.com Words: 198 Articles: 4
A Lorentz four-vector coefficient selecting a spin-one polarization in a mode expansion of a free field. Its allowed components depend on the field equations and gauge fixing.
