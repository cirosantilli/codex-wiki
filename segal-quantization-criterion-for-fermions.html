Segal quantization criterion for fermions (u implementable⟺[P,u]∈S2​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Supersymmetric quantum mechanics Fermionic Fock space Polarized fermionic Fock space
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For the irreducible representation of the canonical anticommutation relations determined by P, a unitary one-particle map u is unitarily implementable precisely when P−uPu∗ is Hilbert-Schmidt, equivalently when [P,u] is Hilbert-Schmidt. The implementer is unique up to a scalar phase. The equivalent representation criterion for two polarizations is that their projections differ by a Hilbert-Schmidt operator. This yields a continuous projective unitary representation of the restricted unitary group.
