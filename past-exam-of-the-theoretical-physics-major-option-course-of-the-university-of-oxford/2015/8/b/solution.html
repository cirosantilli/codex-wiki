Use ημν​=diag(1,−1,−1,−1) and define the physical electromagnetic source by ∂ν​Fνμ=Jμ. For a constant infinitesimal phase δφ=ieϵφ, the matter Noether current is
Jμ=ie[φ(Dμφ)∗−φ∗Dμφ]=ie(φ∂μφ∗−φ∗∂μφ)−2e2∣φ∣2Aμ.​
(69)
A definition using phase parameter δφ=iϵφ instead divides this expression by e; the Maxwell source includes the electric coupling shown here. Current signs must be tied to the printed Dμ​=∂μ​−ieAμ​ convention.
To verify the equation of motion, vary the matter kinetic term with respect to Aμ​:
δLmatter​=−JμδAμ​.
(70)
Varying −F2/4 and integrating by parts gives (∂ν​Fνμ)δAμ​. The stationary action therefore requires
∂ν​Fνμ=Jμ.​
(71)
The potential has no explicit dependence on Aμ​ and contributes no extra source term. Antisymmetry of Fνμ implies ∂μ​Jμ=0 on the equations of motion; the same conservation law follows from the scalar equations. The source current is gauge-invariant although its expanded derivative and vector-potential pieces are not separately invariant.[ref]
