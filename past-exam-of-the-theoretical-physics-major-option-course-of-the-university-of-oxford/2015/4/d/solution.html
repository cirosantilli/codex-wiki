At zero field the normalized transfer matrix for a classical spin chain eigenvectors and eigenvalues are
∣+⟩=2​1​(11​),∣−⟩=2​1​(1−1​),λ+​=2coshK,λ−​=−2sinhK.​
(31)
The spin insertion exchanges the two eigenvectors: σz∣+⟩=∣−⟩ and conversely. Evaluating the trace in c therefore gives, with x=λ−​/λ+​=−tanhK,
gN​(r)=1+xNxr+xN−r​.​
(32)
This is the finite-ring result. In the thermodynamic limit, or for 1≪r≪N away from the wrap-around image, it becomes
g(r)=(−tanhβJ)r=(−1)re−r/ξ,ξ=−logtanhβJ1​.​
(33)
Here ξ is in lattice-site units. The alternating sign is antiferromagnetic order at short distances; the exponential envelope shows the absence of long-range order at every positive temperature in this one-dimensional finite-range model. As T→0, ξ diverges, asymptotically ξ∼21​e2βJ. For the even ring the zero-temperature result is g(r)=(−1)r, consistent with the two alternating ground configurations. At fixed finite N, “large distance” must still respect periodicity: gN​(r)=gN​(N−r) rather than decaying indefinitely beyond the ring.
