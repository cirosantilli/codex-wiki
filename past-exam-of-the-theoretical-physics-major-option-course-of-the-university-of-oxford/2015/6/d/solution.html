In four space-time dimensions a canonically normalized scalar has mass dimension one, so g has mass dimension one. The amplitude starts at order g, a rate is quadratic in that amplitude, and the only mass scale in the massless-daughter decay is m. Those facts already require Γ∝g2/m, leaving only a dimensionless numerical factor to calculate.
For low-energy charged-scalar scattering through a neutral scalar, label the incoming particle and antiparticle as 1,2 and their outgoing counterparts as 3,4. Charge flow allows s-channel annihilation and t=(p1​−p3​)2 exchange of the real massive scalar. It forbids a separate u-channel graph for this fixed assignment of distinguishable final charges. The tree amplitude is
M=−g2(s−m21​+t−m21​)=m22g2​+O(m4g2s​,m4g2t​).​
(55)
At low energy it is approximately angle independent. The massless two-body scattering formula then gives
σtot​≃16πs∣M∣2​=4πm4sg4​.​
(56)
In particular the requested scale estimate is σ∼g4/(m4s), not g4/m6 with the incoming-energy dependence omitted. Integrating out ϕ gives Leff​=g2(φ†φ)2/(2m2) at this order, whose complex-scalar four-leg combinatorics reproduce the same 2g2/m2 amplitude. These are weak-coupling perturbative results about the specified vacuum; possible ultraviolet stabilizing interactions are not determined by the cubic term alone.
