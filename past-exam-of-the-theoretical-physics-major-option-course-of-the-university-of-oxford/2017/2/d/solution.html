HLSW​=k∑​ϵ(k)bk†​bk​+21​k∑​[ϵ(k)−αk​],​

ϵ(k)=αk2​−Δk2​​=S(Hf​+2J)[Hf​+2J(1−cosk)]​.​
(25)
The field-polarized planar ferromagnetic spin wave has energy gap ϵ(0)=SHf​(Hf​+2J)​. The momenta run over the whole Brillouin zone once, including the self-inverse momenta 0 and, for even L, π; these use single-mode squeezing rather than introducing an extra independent partner.
The zero-field case has a zero-mode obstruction to bosonic Bogoliubov diagonalization. Its nonzero-momentum modes have ϵ(k)=22​JS∣sin(k/2)∣, but α0​=Δ0​=JS, so no finite squeezing angle exists for the uniform mode. With Q0​=(a0​+a0†​)/2​, its Hamiltonian is JSQ02​−JS/2, not an identically zero oscillator. For example, its matrix element between the vacuum and the two-boson number state is JS/2​, whereas ϵ(0)b0†​b0​ would vanish. Consequently the requested oscillator diagonalization is literal for nonzero field; at zero field treat the collective orientation separately or introduce a field regulator before taking a limit.
