The action is dimensionless and [d4x]=−4, so [L]=4. A canonical scalar kinetic term then requires 2+2[ϕ]=4, and the electromagnetic kinetic term requires 2[Fμν​]=4. Thus
[Φ]=[a]=[b]=1,[Fμν​]=2.​
(21)
The brackets here denote mass dimension. The Lagrangian has no explicit coordinate dependence, and all indices are contracted as Lorentz scalars. Its continuous spacetime symmetry is the Poincaré group. Noether's theorem gives conserved four-momentum from translations and conserved angular momentum, including the boost generators, from Lorentz transformations. The nonzero mass M and dimensionful couplings prevent a dilation symmetry.
Sign qualification: the printed electromagnetic kinetic term has the opposite healthy relative sign to the scalar kinetic terms. With the stated mostly-minus Minkowski metric, a physical Maxwell Lagrangian is −Fμν​Fμν/4. The printed plus sign instead gives photon Hamiltonian density −(E2+B2)/2; a nonzero purely electric configuration already has negative energy, unbounded below as its amplitude grows. The physical decay widths below use the healthy Maxwell sign with the given interaction normalization. The dimensional and spacetime-symmetry conclusions themselves do not depend on this sign correction.
