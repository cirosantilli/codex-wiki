For the sextic polynomial to be bounded below at large ∣m∣ while allowing either sign of b, its leading coefficient must obey
c>0.​
(68)
If c<0, the free energy tends to −∞ as ∣m∣→∞, so it cannot define an equilibrium within this truncation. The exceptional case c=0 is stable for b>0 and gives the ordinary quartic Landau theory; it cannot stabilize the negative-b region required for the full sextic phase diagram. Higher positive powers could stabilize a negative c, but they are absent from this model.
