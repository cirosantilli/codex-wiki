With outgoing physical photon polarization vectors, the Feynman rule gives
Aλ1​λ2​​=iga​ϵμνρσ​q1ρ​q2σ​ϵ1μ∗​ϵ2ν∗​.
(56)
The overall phase does not affect the decay width. Replacing either polarization vector by its own four-momentum gives zero by antisymmetry. Thus the Ward identity permits replacement of each physical photon polarization sum by −ημν inside this amplitude. The printed sum is not an equality of free-standing physical two-polarization tensors: for a photon moving along z, its physical 00 component is zero whereas −η00​=−1. Gauge-dependent longitudinal terms cancel only after contraction with the transverse amplitude.

Γ(a→γγ)=2!1​64π2ma​4π​2ga2​ma4​​=64πga2​ma3​​.​
