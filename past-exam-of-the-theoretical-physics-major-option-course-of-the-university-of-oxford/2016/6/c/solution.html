The angular domain is one sphere: 0≤φ<2π and −1≤cosθ≤1, equivalently 0≤θ≤π. The printed negative-to-positive polar-angle interval is not a second independent coverage of the sphere.
For identical daughters, integration over the full labelled two-body phase space counts each physical final state twice. One must therefore include an additional factor 1/2!, or equivalently integrate over an unordered half-domain. This distinction is necessary for the two identical photons in solution.
