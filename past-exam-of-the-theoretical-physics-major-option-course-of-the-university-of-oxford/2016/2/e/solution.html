Use the unitary discrete Fourier transform aj​=L−1/2∑k​eikjak​, where k=2πn/L. Then
HLSW​=∑k​[αk​ak†​ak​+2βk​​(ak​a−k​+ak†​a−k†​)],
(19)
with αk​=S[2Jx−(Jy+Jz)cosk] and βk​=−S(Jy−Jz)cosk. The factor 1/2 accounts for the duplicated k,−k pair in the full momentum sum.
Make a bosonic Bogoliubov transformation ak​=coshθk​bk​+sinhθk​b−k†​. Its coefficients preserve the canonical commutation relation because cosh2θk​−sinh2θk​=1. The coefficient of the pair term vanishes when tanh2θk​=−βk​/αk​. Thus
HLSW​=k∑​εk​bk†​bk​+constant,εk​=2S(Jx−Jycosk)(Jx−Jzcosk)​.​
(20)
The dropped constant is 21​∑k​(εk​−αk​) in addition to the classical ground-state energy. Since Jx>Jy>Jz>0, αk​>∣βk​∣ at every k, so the bosonic Bogoliubov diagonalization is stable. The minimum occurs at k=0 and gives the energy gap 2S(Jx−Jy)(Jx−Jz)​. In the isotropic limit the pair coefficient vanishes and the familiar quadratic small-k ferromagnetic dispersion relation is recovered.
