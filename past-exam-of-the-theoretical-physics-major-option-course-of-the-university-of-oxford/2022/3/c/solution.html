Set the time derivatives in the master equation to zero. The first and third rows give f1​p0​=f3​pB​ and f2​pA​=(f3​+f4​)pB​. Normalization then gives, with N=f2​f3​+f1​(f3​+f4​)+f1​f2​,
p0st​=Nf2​f3​​,pAst​=Nf1​(f3​+f4​)​,pBst​=Nf1​f2​​.​
(21)
These expressions assume positive rates, or a limiting choice for which N=0 and the stationary distribution is unique. If rates vanish so that multiple closed classes appear, the initial condition can matter.
The word “equilibrium” in this part must mean a stationary distribution. With positive f1​,f2​,f3​ the one-way contact transitions support a nonzero stationary electric current, so the process is not in thermodynamic equilibrium and does not satisfy detailed balance. In particular, 0→A carries positive probability flow while the direct reverse rate is zero.
