Let R=J2+D2​ and k0​=arctan(D/J). Since Jcosk+Dsink=Rcos(k−k0​), the continuum minimum of the dispersion relation is
Δ=2S[J(1+u)−R],uc​=1+(D/J)2​−1.​
(17)
For D>0, the energy gap closes at a positive uc​, at wave vector k0​. Below it, the quadratic expansion about the polarized state has a negative mode and is no longer a stable ground state approximation; higher-order terms and a different ordered reference state are needed.
Two qualifications are required by the actual assumptions. First, D=0 is permitted, in which case uc​=0; it is a concrete counterexample to the printed strict inequality uc​>0. Second, a finite ring only has discrete momenta. Its finite-ring spin-wave threshold with antisymmetric exchange is
uc​(L)=ℓ=0,…,L−1max​[cosL2πℓ​+JD​sinL2πℓ​]−1.​
(18)
This approaches uc​ in the thermodynamic limit and equals it when k0​ is allowed. For example, L=4 and 0<D<J give uc​(L)=0, since the maximum of 1,D/J,−1,−D/J is one. Thus the continuum formula and its positive threshold require the large-ring interpretation. The two symmetry-related polarized ground states already have equal exact energy; the calculated energy gap refers to excitations about one chosen orientation.
