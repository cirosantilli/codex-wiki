The real scalar field action has spacetime translations and Lorentz invariance, together giving Poincaré symmetry. With a real scalar transformation law it also has parity and time reversal. A generic polynomial potential has no continuous internal symmetry. The additional field reversal ϕ↦−ϕ is a symmetry precisely when every nonzero odd-power coupling vanishes. The free massless special case also admits a constant shift of ϕ; generic interactions remove it.
In mass units, [∂μ​]=1 and the dimensionless action requires [L]=D. Normalizing the kinetic term fixes the classical mass dimension of the scalar field:
2+2[ϕ]=D,[ϕ]=2D−2​,[m]=1.

For each interaction, [λn​]+n[ϕ]=D, so
[λn​]=D−2n(D−2)​.​
(64)
These are classical or engineering scaling dimensions; quantum corrections can change the scaling of operators and couplings. The scale transformation prescribed later has field weight one, which equals this canonical weight only at D=4.
