Choi's theorem on completely positive maps

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Positive linear map Completely positive map
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
A finite-dimensional linear map is completely positive if and only if its Choi matrix is positive semidefinite. One direction applies the map tensored with the identity to a maximally entangled positive operator. For the converse, factor the positive Choi matrix into rank-one terms and reshape their vectors into matrices; the spectral Kraus decomposition reconstructs the map as a Kraus representation, which is completely positive.
