Aquifer A requires half the pumping power at equal throughput, or half the energy to inject the same volume over the same time. This compares the excess pressure-volume work dissipated by the Darcy flow; any different baseline compression or other plant costs need separate accounting.
The imposed comparison matters. At fixed Δp, A takes twice the volumetric flow rate and twice the instantaneous power, but the same excess energy per injected volume.
