Commutator of covariant derivatives ([∇α​,∇β​]Vμ=Rμναβ​Vν)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Riemann curvature tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 136 Articles: 1
For a torsion-free connection, the commutator of coordinate covariant derivatives vanishes on a scalar field and acts on a vector field through the displayed Riemann curvature tensor. On a covector field it instead gives [∇α​,∇β​]Aμ​=−Rνμαβ​Aν​. The curvature commutator is a tensor derivation, so each index of a general tensor contributes independently with the corresponding sign. Including the lower derivative index in the second covariant derivative is essential for this identity.
