Pushdown automaton configuration ((q,w,α))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Formal language theory Pushdown automaton
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
The control state q, unread input word w, and stack contents α of a pushdown automaton. A fixed convention must say which end of α is the top. With the top on the left, replacing top X by γ changes Xβ into γβ.
