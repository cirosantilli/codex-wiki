Quasi-coherent sheaf

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules
OurBigBook.com Words: 967 Articles: 16
A sheaf of modules on a scheme is quasi-coherent when every affine chart SpecA restricts it to the sheaf associated with an A-module. On an affine scheme it is determined by its module of global sections.
