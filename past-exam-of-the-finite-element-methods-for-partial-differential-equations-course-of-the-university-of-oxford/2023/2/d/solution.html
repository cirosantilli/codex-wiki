Let e=u−uh​. Subtracting the two weak formulations gives Galerkin orthogonality, a(e,vh​)=0 for every vh​∈Vh​. Since a is the first-order Sobolev inner product, for every vh​∈Vh​ we have
∥e∥12​​=a(e,u−vh​)+a(e,vh​−uh​)=a(e,u−vh​)≤∥e∥1​∥u−vh​∥1​.​

If e=0, division proves ∥e∥1​≤∥u−vh​∥1​; if e=0, the same conclusion is immediate. Thus the Céa lemma has constant one in this case.
Use the quadratic nodal interpolant Ih​u. The assumed H3(Ω) regularity gives continuous nodal values by the Sobolev embedding theorem, and its zero Sobolev trace makes the nodal values on ΓD​ zero. The quadratic Lagrange triangle construction then gives Ih​u∈Vh​. The allowed quadratic finite element interpolation estimate on a shape-regular mesh is
∥u−Ih​u∥1​≤CI​h2∥u∥H3(Ω)​,
(31)
where CI​ depends on the fixed triangle and shape regularity, but not on h. Therefore
∥u−uh​∥H1(Ω)​≤∥u−Ih​u∥H1(Ω)​≤C1​h2∥u∥H3(Ω)​,C1​=CI​.​
(32)
This step uses the inclusion Ih​u∈Vh​ explicitly permitted by the question. Arbitrary smaller vector subspaces without the same approximation property would not give this rate.
