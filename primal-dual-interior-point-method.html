Primal-dual interior-point method

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Conic optimization Interior-point method
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
A primal-dual interior-point method solves perturbed KKT conditions while updating both the primal variables and Lagrange multipliers. For inequalities written as ci​≥0, a logarithmic-barrier path has positive ci​,yi​ and perturbed complementarity ci​yi​=μ. A Newton system for these coupled equations gives primal and dual corrections together, with damping to preserve positivity. This can avoid explicitly forming the most ill-conditioned eliminated barrier Hessian, although scaling and regularity still matter.
