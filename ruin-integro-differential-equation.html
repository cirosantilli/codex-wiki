Ruin integro-differential equation (λc​ψ′(u)=ψ(u)−(fX​∗ψ)(u)−FX​(u))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Classical risk model Ultimate ruin probability
OurBigBook.com Words: 134 Articles: 1
In the classical risk model, condition on the first arrival of the Poisson process. If available capital just before it is v, ruin has probability FX​(v)+∫0v​fX​(x)ψ(v−x)dx. Integrate this expression at v=u+ct against λe−λt and change variables from t to v. Differentiating the lower limit gives the displayed equation. The claim-density convolution is continuous for bounded ψ, so the first-arrival representation also supplies the differentiability required by the equation.
