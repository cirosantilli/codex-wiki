Ruby pressure scale (Δλ=λR1​​−λR1​,0​)

 ... Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure Pressure measurement High-pressure experimental metrology Diamond anvil cell
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 4
The ruby pressure scale converts a shift of a ruby fluorescence spectral line into pressure. Excitation light produces a fluorescence emission spectrum; fitting its R1​ line gives a wavelength λR1​​. The reference λR1​,0​ must be measured or assigned at specified reference pressure and temperature. The difference Δλ is an observable; interpreting it as pressure requires a pressure calibration and suitable stress conditions.
The original 1972 report found sharp R1​,R2​ emission shifting toward lower photon energy as pressure increased; it tentatively associated broadening with nonhydrostatic conditions.[ref] In use, excitation rejection, a calibrated wavelength axis and a fitted background matter because leakage or background can move an apparent peak. A brighter trace improves counting statistics but does not alone establish a correct pressure scale.
