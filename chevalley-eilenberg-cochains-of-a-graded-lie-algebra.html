Chevalley–Eilenberg cochains of a graded Lie algebra (C∗(L))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Graded Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 92
For a positively graded Lie algebra L over Q, finite-dimensional in each degree, its cochain algebra is free graded commutative on the graded dual of sL, with differential dual to the Lie bracket and its Koszul signs. The Jacobi identity gives d2=0. A Lie element of degree r contributes a cochain generator of degree r+1. Its cohomology computes ExtU(L)​(Q,Q) with total grading; the standard free enveloping-algebra resolution gives this identification. For a free graded Lie algebra, the tensor-algebra augmentation has a length-one free resolution, explaining the square-zero cohomology of a rational wedge sum of spheres.
