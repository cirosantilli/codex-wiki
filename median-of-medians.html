Median of medians

 Home Computer science Algorithm
OurBigBook.com Words: 64
For deterministic linear-time selection, group keys into fives, compute the median of each group, and recursively select the median of those medians as a pivot. Apart from bounded end effects, at least 3n/10 keys lie on either side. Thus the selection cost satisfies T(n)≤T(⌈n/5⌉)+T(7n/10+O(1))+O(n)=O(n), since the fractions sum to 9/10<1. A strong induction with sufficiently large linear coefficient absorbs the lower-order terms.
