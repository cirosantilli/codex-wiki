Reaction coordinate (q)

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Chemical kinetics Reaction mechanism
OurBigBook.com Words: 419 Articles: 4
A reaction coordinate parametrizes changes in molecular configuration along a chemical reaction path. It can involve a bond length, a bond angle, or a combination of geometric changes. It is not elapsed time and is not the bulk extent of reaction: one describes configurations, while the other accounts for how much material has converted. The precise choice matters when interpreting an energy profile. See the IUPAC Gold Book: reaction coordinate.
