Cramér's theorem

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Moment-generating function Cumulant-generating function
OurBigBook.com Words: 371 Articles: 6
For independent and identically distributed random variables with a moment-generating function finite on an open interval containing zero, the empirical mean satisfies a large deviation principle with rate function given by the Legendre transform of a cumulant-generating function. The exponential-moment hypothesis is part of this theorem; mere integrability does not guarantee this full large-deviation principle.
