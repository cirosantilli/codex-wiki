Expected utility (E[u(X)])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Utility function
OurBigBook.com Words: 318 Articles: 5
The expected value of the utility function applied to a random outcome, using the decision-maker's probabilities. For a finite lottery it is ∑x​p(x)u(x). Under risk neutrality and quasilinear utility, auction utility is valuation minus payment upon winning and zero upon losing, so its expectation is the winning probability times the payoff conditional on winning.
