Sine-Gordon Bäcklund transformation (wξ​=a−1sind,dη​=asinw)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Real scalar field Sine-Gordon theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 264 Articles: 4
For the angular field u=βϕ and coordinates ξ=m(x+t)/2, η=m(x−t)/2, let w=(v+u)/2 and d=(v−u)/2. The displayed relations imply uξη​=sinu and vξη​=sinv. They define a Bäcklund transformation with the reciprocal parameter convention used in the Bianchi permutability for sine-Gordon Bäcklund transformations. Physical-field trigonometric arguments include the coupling β.
