Parent in a rooted tree

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Tree (graph theory) Rooted tree
OurBigBook.com Words: 17
For a nonroot vertex, its adjacent predecessor on the unique graph path from the root of a tree. The root of a tree has no parent.
