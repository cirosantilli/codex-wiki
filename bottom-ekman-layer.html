Bottom Ekman layer

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Ocean circulation Wind stress Ekman layer
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A viscous no-slip boundary below a geostrophic current creates a bottom Ekman layer of thickness δ=(2ν/∣f∣)1/2. Its depth-integrated ageostrophic transport has a component opposite the geostrophic current and a transverse component set by the sign of f.
