Diode reverse breakdown

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Diode
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A sufficiently large reverse electric potential difference can produce a rapidly increasing reverse electric current. Both the ideal diode model and the unextended Shockley diode equation omit this behavior.[ref]
