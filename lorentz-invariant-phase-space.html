Lorentz-invariant phase space (dΦ2​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Decay width
OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 2
For two outgoing particles the Lorentz-invariant phase space is dΦ2​=(2π)4δ(4)(p−k−k′)d3k/((2π)32Ek​)d3k′/((2π)32Ek′​). With massless daughters in their center-of-mass frame, dΦ2​=dΩ/(32π2) and ∫dΦ2​=1/(8π). This fixes normalization factors in decay widths and scattering rates.
