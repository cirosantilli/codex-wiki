Passing-base fraction for the Fermat test (αN​=∣{a∈U(N):aN−1=1}∣/φ(N))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Carmichael number
OurBigBook.com Words: 180
Among the units U(N) modulo N, the passing residue classes form the kernel of a group homomorphism a↦aN−1. If each trial samples independently and uniformly from those units, with replacement, the probability that r trials all pass is αNr​. This statement assumes that sampling rule; it does not describe arbitrary or dependent choices of bases.
For 341=11⋅31, the Chinese remainder theorem identifies the unit group with cyclic groups of orders 10 and 30. The number of solutions of x340=1 is gcd(340,10)gcd(340,30)=100, among φ(341)=300 units. Thus α341​=1/3. The Carmichael numbers 561 and 1105 have passing fractions 320/320 and 768/768, so their conditional false-pass probability remains one regardless of the number of independent trials.
