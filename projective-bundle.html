Projective bundle

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 818 Articles: 13
The projective bundle of a real or complex vector bundle E→X has fiber the space of one-dimensional subspaces of Ex​. It carries a tautological bundle λ⊂p∗E; in the real case this is the projectivization of a real vector bundle.
The same construction applies to an algebraic vector bundle over an algebraic variety: local trivializations glue products with projective space. In the lines convention its universal tautological bundle is a line bundle; the alternative projectivization by quotients convention parametrizes one-dimensional quotient spaces. Both conventions give dimension dimX+rank(E)−1 and a proper morphism to the base.
