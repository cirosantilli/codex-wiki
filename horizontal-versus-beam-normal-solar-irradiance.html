Horizontal versus beam-normal solar irradiance

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Radiometry Irradiance
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A parallel beam incident at elevation α has horizontal irradiance Fh​=F⊥​sinα. Its path optical depth in a plane-parallel atmosphere is τv​/sinα. Confusing these projections changes both attenuation and intercepted power.
