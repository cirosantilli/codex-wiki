Cylinder premeasure from a positive functional (ℓ(A)=ϕ(1A​))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Measure Premeasure
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A normalized positive linear functional on C(Ω) for Cantor space gives a finitely additive set function on the algebra of clopen sets. If a countable disjoint union of such sets is itself clopen, compactness gives a finite subcover; all other terms are empty. Finite additivity therefore gives the required countable additivity, making ℓ a premeasure. The Caratheodory extension theorem produces a unique Borel probability measure.
