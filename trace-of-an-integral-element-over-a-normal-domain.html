Trace of an integral element over a normal domain

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Field trace
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
For a normal domain A with fraction field K, the field trace of an integral element in a finite separable field extension L/K belongs to A. Its conjugates are integral, so their sum is integral; it lies in K and hence in A by normality. This places the integral closure inside a trace-dual lattice.
