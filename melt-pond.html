Melt pond

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Sea ice
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
A melt pond is liquid water collected in a depression on melting ice. Its lower albedo than snow-covered ice can strengthen ice-albedo feedback. Pond drainage and connected pores can redistribute an oil spill under sea ice during warming.
