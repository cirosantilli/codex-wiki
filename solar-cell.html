Solar cell

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Photon Photon absorption Photovoltaic effect
OurBigBook.com Words: 543 Articles: 11
A solar cell converts incident light into electrical power through the photovoltaic effect. A semiconductor p-n junction separates and collects photogenerated electrons and electron holes. An ideal illuminated junction has output current density
J(V)=Jsc​−J0​(eeV/(kB​T)−1),Voc​=ekB​T​log(1+J0​Jsc​​).
(46)
Here Jsc​ is the short-circuit current density, J0​ is the diode saturation current, and Voc​ is the open-circuit electric potential difference. The conversion efficiency is the largest delivered VJ divided by the incident power per unit area; the short-circuit and open-circuit operating points themselves deliver zero power.
