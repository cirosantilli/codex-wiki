Travelling solidification front in a pure supercooled melt (Tl​=T∞​+ΔTe−Vξ/κ,S=1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Stefan problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 174 Articles: 3
For equal thermal properties and bounded travelling profiles on two deep half-spaces, the solid temperature is Tm​ and the liquid profile is exponential. The Stefan condition requires L=cp​ΔT. This balances the latent heat of solidification with warming of the initially supercooled liquid.
