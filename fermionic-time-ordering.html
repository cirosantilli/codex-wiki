Fermionic time ordering (T[A(x)B(y)]=θ(x0−y0)A(x)B(y)−θ(y0−x0)B(y)A(x))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Wick's theorem Time ordering
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
For two odd fermionic operators, time ordering contributes a minus sign whenever the chronological rearrangement exchanges them. For several fields the sign is the sign of a permutation restricted to the odd fields. This convention makes the free Dirac propagator a Green function of the Dirac equation with its contact term fixed by the canonical anticommutation relations.
