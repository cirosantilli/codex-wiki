Dopamine

 Home Biology Neuroscience Neuron Synapse Neurotransmitter
OurBigBook.com Words: 74 Articles: 1
A neurotransmitter involved in multiple neural functions. Some phasic dopamine-neuron responses are consistent with reward-prediction-error signalling, and timed interventions can affect learning. This does not mean that every dopamine neuron or every timescale carries one identical scalar error.[ref]
