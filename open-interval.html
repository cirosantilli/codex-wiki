Open interval

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Open set
OurBigBook.com Words: 47 Articles: 1
This is the open-boundary case of an interval.
An open interval in the real line is a set (a,b)={x:a<x<b}, allowing either endpoint to be infinite.
