Invariant-normalized scalar mode extraction (a(p)=i∫d3xfp∗​∂t​​ϕ)

For a free real scalar field expanded with measure d3p/[(2π)32Ep​] and fp​=e−ip⋅x, the displayed Klein-Gordon inner product extracts its annihilation operator. Indeed (fp​,fq​)=(2π)32Ep​δ3(p−q) and (fp​,fq∗​)=0; spatial Fourier inversion removes the measure. Taking the Hermitian adjoint gives a†(p)=−i∫d3xfp​∂t​​ϕ. The equal-time canonical commutation relation then gives [a(p),a†(q)]=(2π)32Ep​δ3(p−q).
