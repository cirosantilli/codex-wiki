Rigidity of a negative curve (∣C∣={C})

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic surface Smooth algebraic surface Smooth projective surface Negative curve on a projective surface
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
If an effective divisor D is linearly equivalent to a negative curve C, then D=C. Otherwise D⋅C=C2<0 forces C to be a component of D, since distinct irreducible curves have nonnegative intersection multiplicities. Write D=C+D′ with D′ effective and linearly equivalent to zero. An ample divisor intersects D′ positively unless D′=0, while linear equivalence forces that intersection to vanish.
