Polar order parameter

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Order parameter
OurBigBook.com Words: 310 Articles: 6
A polar order parameter is a vector whose direction distinguishes head from tail. In an isotropic medium without an external polar field, spatial inversion makes the free energy even under p↦−p.
