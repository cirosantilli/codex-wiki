Momentum balance for a material fluid element (ρDu/Dt=−∇p)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Cauchy momentum equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For an inviscid material volume element, neglecting body forces, Newton's second law gives D(ρuδV)/Dt=−∇pδV. Since mass conservation keeps ρδV constant, the result is ρDu/Dt=−∇p. Cancelling δV before differentiating would incorrectly omit its changing volume. A fixed control volume instead requires the advected momentum flux, yielding ∂t​(ρu)+∇⋅(ρu⊗u)=−∇p.
