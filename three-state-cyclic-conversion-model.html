Three-state cyclic conversion model (A˙=−aA+cC,B˙=aA−bB,C˙=bB−cC)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Linear ordinary differential equation Linear system of differential equations
OurBigBook.com Words: 165 Articles: 1
Three nonnegative amounts convert in the directed cycle A→B→C→A at positive first-order rates a,b,c. The linear system of ordinary differential equations conserves A+B+C=N. Its characteristic polynomial is λ[λ2+(a+b+c)λ+ab+bc+ca]. The two nonzero eigenvalues have negative real part, so every trajectory converges to the unique steady state with that total:
(A∞​,B∞​,C∞​)=ab+bc+caN​(bc,ca,ab).
(50)
This is also a column-convention continuous-time Markov chain after normalizing by N>0: every component loses to one successor and receives from one predecessor. A complex-conjugate pair produces damped oscillations about equilibrium even though the amounts remain nonnegative. A repeated nonzero eigenvalue is defective and requires a Jordan chain.
