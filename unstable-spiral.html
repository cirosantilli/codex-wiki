Unstable spiral (λ=α±iω,α>0,ω=0)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Autonomous planar system Equilibrium point of a dynamical system Linear stability of a planar equilibrium
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A planar equilibrium point whose Jacobian matrix has nonreal conjugate eigenvalues with positive real part. Its nearby linearized trajectories rotate while their amplitude grows exponentially; the smooth nonlinear system has the same local qualitative behavior when the equilibrium is hyperbolic.
