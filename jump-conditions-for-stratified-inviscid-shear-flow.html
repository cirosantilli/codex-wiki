At a material interface without surface tension, continuity of displacement and of the pressure evaluated on the displaced interface gives
[U−cw​]=0,[(U−c)w′−U′w−ρ0​gρ​U−cw​]=0.
(7)
The first follows from the kinematic boundary condition ik(U−c)η​=w. The second uses p​=ρ0​[(U−c)w′−U′w]/(ik) and hydrostatic displacement [p​−gρη​]=0. These conditions apply to density, velocity and vorticity jumps within the Boussinesq approximation.
