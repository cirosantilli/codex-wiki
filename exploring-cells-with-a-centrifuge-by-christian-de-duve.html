Nobel lecture by Christian de Duve, delivered 12 December 1974. This is the lecture date, not an established online publication date. NobelPrize.org hosts the official document; the inspected text was a transcription on Doczz because the official PDF could not be fetched.
Official lecture PDF: www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/duve-lecture.pdf
Inspected transcription: doczz.net/doc/8221300/christian-de-duve---nobel-lecture
Cited passages: printed pp. 152–159, Figures 1–7, fractionation and enzyme latency; pp. 161–164, microscopy. This is a primary investigator’s retrospective account; it does not replace direct inspection of the original assay paper.
