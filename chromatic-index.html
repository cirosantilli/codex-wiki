Chromatic index (χ′(G))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Graph coloring Edge coloring
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
The chromatic index is the least number of colors in an edge coloring for which edges sharing an endpoint have different colors. It is at least the maximum degree of a graph. In a bipartite graph it equals the maximum degree: for an uncolored edge, choose colors missing at its endpoints and swap the two colors on a suitable alternating component. If the missing colors differ, that component cannot connect the two endpoints, since the necessary even-length path would put them in the same bipartition class.
