Formula relativization to a class

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Class (set theory) Transitive class
OurBigBook.com Words: 976 Articles: 23
The relativization φM of a formula φ is obtained recursively by restricting every quantifier to M:
(∃xψ)M=∃x(x∈M∧ψM),(∀xψ)M=∀x(x∈M⇒ψM).
(13)
For parameters in M, the ambient statement φM holds exactly when the structure (M,∈) satisfies φ.
