Degree of an isogeny (degϕ)

 ... Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Isogeny of elliptic curves
OurBigBook.com Words: 480 Articles: 5
The degree of an isogeny is the degree of its induced finite extension of function fields. On End(E) it is a quadratic form: deg(nϕ)=n2degϕ, and
deg(ϕ+ψ)+deg(ϕ−ψ)=2degϕ+2degψ.
