Dual arrangement of a planar point set

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic variety Projective space Projective plane Point-line duality
OurBigBook.com Words: 283 Articles: 4
Dualize a noncollinear finite point set in the real projective plane to projective lines. Their intersection vertices and consecutive line segments form an embedded graph. A vertex incident to k lines has graph degree 2k and corresponds to a primal line containing k points. Ordinary lines correspond to degree-four vertices. The faces are polygons with at least three sides.
