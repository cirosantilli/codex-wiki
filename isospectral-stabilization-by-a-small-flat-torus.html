Isospectral stabilization by a small flat torus (Mi​×(Rk/εZk))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Riemannian product Product Laplacian eigenspace decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 102
Two isospectral manifolds remain isospectral after taking a Riemannian product with the same closed factor: eigenvalues add, with multiplicities given by the product Laplacian eigenspace decomposition. For nonisometric closed connected factors M1​,M2​, choose ε<δ<mini​inj(Mi​). Every closed product geodesic of length less than δ has constant projection to Mi​, since a nonconstant closed geodesic shorter than the injectivity radius is impossible. The short coordinate geodesics of the small flat torus span exactly its tangent factor at every point. Any product Riemannian isometry must preserve that intrinsically defined smooth distribution and its orthogonal complement, hence restrict to an isometry of the original factors. Thus these products are still nonisometric. Taking Mi​ to be four-dimensional tori proves the higher-dimensional stabilization statement without assuming a cancellation theorem for arbitrary products.
