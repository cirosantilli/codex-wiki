Quarter-wave Bragg stack (nH​dH​=nL​dL​=λ0​/4)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Distributed Bragg reflector
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
At normal incidence, alternating layers with indices nH​,nL​ and optical thicknesses nH​dH​=nL​dL​=λ0​/4 give reinforcing reflected waves near design wavelength λ0​. Their periodic transfer matrix has a stop band when its Bloch phase is complex. A finite stack has nonzero transmission and its exterior impedances matter. Introducing a suitably phased central defect can support a transmission resonance within the stop band; a one-dimensional stop band is not a complete three-dimensional photonic gap.
