Concavity of the logarithm ((logs)′′=−1/s2<0)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Exponential function Logarithm
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The logarithm is strictly concave on positive real numbers, as its second derivative is negative. Thus the Jensen inequality gives ∫logfdμ≤log∫fdμ for a probability measure and an integrable positive f, whenever the left side is defined. It also controls the negative logarithmic part of convex combinations of strictly positive functions.
