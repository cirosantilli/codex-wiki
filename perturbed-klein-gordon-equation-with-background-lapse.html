Perturbed Klein-Gordon equation with background lapse (δΠ=Dδϕ−ΠˉΨ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Linear cosmological perturbation theory Scalar cosmological perturbation
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
Let D=Nˉ−1∂t​ and Πˉ=Dϕˉ​. For negative-expansion extrinsic curvature of a spatial hypersurface, its trace perturbation is κ. With δΠ=Dδϕ−ΠˉΨ, scalar evolution is DδΠ−ΨDΠˉ+3HδΠ−κΠˉ−Δδϕ+V,ϕϕ​δϕ=0. Combining background acceleration terms produces the standard lapse source −Πˉ(κ+DΨ−3HΨ)+2ΨV,ϕ​.
