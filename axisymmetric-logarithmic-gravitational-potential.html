Axisymmetric logarithmic gravitational potential (Φ=21​V2log(R2+z2/q2))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field Newtonian gravitational potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 75 Articles: 1
An axisymmetric logarithmic Newtonian gravitational potential has spheroidal equipotential surfaces and a flat galaxy rotation curve: RΦR​(R,0)=V2. The singular model is defined away from the origin; a fixed reference length makes the logarithm dimensionless. Its central r−2 mass density cusp is locally integrable, but its total mass diverges at large radius.
