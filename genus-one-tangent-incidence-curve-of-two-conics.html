Genus one tangent incidence curve of two conics (E={(ℓ,x):ℓ tangent to C1​, x∈C2​∩ℓ})

If two smooth plane conics over C meet at four distinct points, their tangent incidence curve is a smooth projective genus one curve. Projection to C2​ is a double cover branched at those four intersections. The tangent equation becomes a quadratic whose discriminant cuts out C1​∩C2​; its four simple zeros make the cover smooth and connected. The Riemann-Hurwitz formula then gives genus one.
