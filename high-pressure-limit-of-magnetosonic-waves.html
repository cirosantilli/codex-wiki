High-pressure limit of magnetosonic waves (vs2​≫va2​)

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Magnetohydrodynamic wave Magnetosonic wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 1
For sound speed vs​ much larger than Alfvén speed va​, and angle θ between the wavevector and magnetic field, the fast magnetosonic wave has vf2​=vs2​+va2​sin2θ+O(va4​/vs2​). The slow magnetosonic wave has vslow2​=va2​cos2θ[1−(va2​/vs2​)sin2θ+O(va4​/vs4​)]. The fast mode is predominantly longitudinal sound. The slow mode becomes almost incompressible, polarized in the plane of the wavevector and magnetic field; the Alfvén wave is polarized perpendicular to that plane. The latter two are predominantly magnetic-tension waves with equal leading phase speeds.
