Holomorphic need for two Higgs chiral doublets

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Minimal supersymmetric Standard Model
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
A renormalizable MSSM superpotential is a holomorphic function of chiral superfields and cannot use their conjugates. Gauge-invariant up-type Yukawa couplings require Hu​ of hypercharge +1/2, while down-type and charged-lepton Yukawa couplings require Hd​ of hypercharge −1/2. A single doublet and its conjugate cannot play both roles in the superpotential. This is distinct from higgsino anomaly cancellation.
