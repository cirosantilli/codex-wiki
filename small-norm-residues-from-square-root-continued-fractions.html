Small norm residues from square-root continued fractions (∣pk2​−Nqk2​∣<2N​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Diophantine approximation Continued fraction Continued fraction convergent
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For nonsquare N>1, write the complete quotients after the initial term as (N​+m)/d. They exceed one, while their algebraic conjugates lie between minus one and zero. Thus d<N​+m<2N​. Comparing coefficients in N​=(pk​αk+1​+pk−1​)/(qk​αk+1​+qk−1​) gives pk2​−Nqk2​=dk+1​(pk​qk−1​−qk​pk−1​), and the last determinant has absolute value one. These small residues are useful candidates for smooth numbers in congruence-of-squares factorization.
