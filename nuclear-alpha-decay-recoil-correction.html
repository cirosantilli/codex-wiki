Nuclear alpha-decay recoil correction (Tα​≃Qα​Md​/(Md​+mα​))

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Radioactive decay Alpha decay
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
In two-body alpha decay of a parent at rest, momentum conservation makes daughter and alpha momenta equal and opposite. The nonrelativistic kinetic energies therefore satisfy Td​/Tα​=mα​/Md​, giving
Tα​=Qα​Md​+mα​Md​​,Td​=Qα​Md​+mα​mα​​.
(9)
An excited daughter reduces the available Q value. To leading recoil order, an alpha line separation ΔTα​ corresponds to excitation Ex​=(1+mα​/Md​)ΔTα​. Thus ratios of excitation energies inferred from line separations do not need the common recoil factor. These formulas assume kinetic energies small compared with rest masses; an exact relativistic two-body decay calculation can replace them when necessary.
