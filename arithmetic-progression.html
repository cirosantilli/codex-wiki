Arithmetic progression

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Arithmetic
OurBigBook.com Words: 127 Articles: 3
An arithmetic progression is a sequence a,a+r,…,a+(m−1)r with constant successive difference r. It is nonconstant when r=0.
