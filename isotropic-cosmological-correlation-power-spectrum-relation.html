For the Fourier transform convention δk​=∫d3xδ(x)eik⋅x and covariance ⟨δk​δk′​⟩=(2π)3P(k)δD​(k+k′), inversion gives ξ(r)=∫d3kP(k)eik⋅r/(2π)3. Isotropy reduces the angular integral to 4πj0​(kr), where j0​(x)=sinx/x is the order-zero Spherical Bessel function. At zero separation this gives the variance if the spectrum integral converges. A nonnegative spectrum can produce negative correlations away from the origin.
