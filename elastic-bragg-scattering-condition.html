Elastic Bragg scattering condition

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering Scattering amplitude Crystal scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 7
For elastic scattering with k−k′=q∈Λ∗ and ∣k∣=∣k′∣=k,
2k⋅q=∣q∣2,∣q∣=2ksin2θ​.
(24)
The smallest nonzero diffraction angle is determined by the shortest reciprocal-lattice vector.
This reciprocal-lattice condition is equivalent to Bragg's law, expressed in terms of the lattice-plane spacing and the wavelength.
