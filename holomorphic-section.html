Holomorphic section

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Holomorphic vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
A section of a holomorphic vector bundle is holomorphic when its coordinate functions in every holomorphic local trivialization are holomorphic.
It is a section of a fiber bundle satisfying holomorphicity.
