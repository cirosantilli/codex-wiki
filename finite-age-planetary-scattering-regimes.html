At fixed stellar mass M⋆​, planetary mass density ρp​, age t⋆​ and initial ratio κ=a0​/ap​, the order-unity Safronov number comparison and comet energy diffusion give boundaries
Mesc​=32π3​​M⋆3/2​ρp−1/2​ap−3/2​,Mage​=M⋆​100t⋆​Pp​​​κ−1/4.
(15)
Their logarithmic slopes in planetary mass versus radius are −3/2 and 3/4. Above both, strong kicks and a short estimated diffusion time favour ejection; below the escape boundary, repeated weak encounters favour collision statistically. Below the age boundary, the stated diffusion mechanism has not completed on its characteristic clock. Collision lifetimes require additional cross-section and encounter-rate information, so this map alone cannot establish retention or accretion within the age.
