Maximum bound for a monotone reaction term (∥u∥∞​≤h−1(∥f∥∞​))

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Elliptic boundary value problem Uniformly elliptic operator Weak maximum principle for elliptic operators
OurBigBook.com Words: 103
Let h:R→R be continuous, odd, strictly increasing and onto. Suppose u∈C2(Rd) tends to zero at infinity and satisfies −Δu+h(u)=f, with bounded f. At a positive maximum m of u, −Δu≥0, so h(m)≤f≤∥f∥∞​. Apply the same argument to −u to obtain the displayed bound, using the maximum-point argument of the weak maximum principle for elliptic operators. For h(s)=s+sins, strict monotonicity follows by integrating h′=1+coss, whose zeros are isolated. Thus ∥u∥∞​≤h−1(∥f∥∞​)≤∥f∥∞​+1. The stronger bound by ∥f∥∞​ holds when that norm is at most π, but need not hold for larger sources.
