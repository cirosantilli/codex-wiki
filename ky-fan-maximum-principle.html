Indeed Y=QQT is a rank-k orthogonal projection matrix in the fantope, and projecting onto the eigenvectors of the largest eigenvalues attains the support function maximum. Equivalently, the largest matrix trace of a compression to a k-dimensional vector subspace is the sum of the largest eigenvalues.
