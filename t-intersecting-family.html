t-intersecting family (∣A∩B∣≥t)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Set family Intersecting family
OurBigBook.com Words: 410 Articles: 6
A set family is t-intersecting if every pair of its members has at least t common elements. For r-sets on an n-point ground set, the whole level is t-intersecting when n≤2r−t. Ordinary intersecting families are the case t=1.
