Centralizer of a stabilizer group

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum error correction Stabilizer code
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
The Pauli centralizer C(S) consists of Pauli operators commuting with every stabilizer. Elements of C(S)∖S, modulo stabilizers and phases, are the nontrivial logical Pauli operators.
