Cauchy–Riemann operator (∂ˉJ​u=21​(du+J∘du∘j))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Pseudoholomorphic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 272 Articles: 4
This first-order operator vanishes exactly on J-holomorphic curves. Linearization at a map u gives a Fredholm operator between suitable Sobolev spaces of sections. At a constant map it is the Dolbeault operator on the trivial pulled-back tangent bundle.
