Far side of the Moon

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Natural satellite Moon
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
The far side of the Moon is the hemisphere directed approximately away from Earth. “Far” describes the direction to Earth; it does not describe illumination by the Sun. The far side of the Moon experiences both sunlight and darkness during the lunar phase cycle. [ref].
