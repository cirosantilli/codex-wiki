Baryon Euler equation with Thomson drag (v˙b​+Hvb​+kψ=(τ˙/R)(vb​−vγ​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic microwave background Cosmic microwave background anisotropy Photon-baryon fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
For the velocity convention vγ​=−Θ1​ and τ˙<0, momentum-conserving Thomson scattering gives v˙b​+Hvb​+kψ=(τ˙/R)(vb​−vγ​). Here R is the baryon loading parameter. Its collision force is opposite to the photon force when both are weighted by their enthalpies.
