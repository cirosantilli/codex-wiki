Centromere

 Home Biology Genetics Chromosome
OurBigBook.com Words: 206 Articles: 3
A centromere is the chromosome region where a kinetochore assembles.[ref] Following DNA replication, both sister chromatids have their own copied centromere regions; the counting convention groups the attached sisters as one replicated chromosome. Cohesin maintains sister association: a centromere is not an extra DNA bridge joining two separate DNA molecules.[ref]
