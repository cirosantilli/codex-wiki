Lyapunov quantum control (V˙=−f2≤0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Control theory Quantum control
OurBigBook.com Words: 156 Articles: 2
For ρ˙​=−i[H0​+fH1​,ρ] and ρ˙​d​=−i[H0​,ρd​], the Hilbert-Schmidt distance gives V=∥ρ−ρd​∥2/2. The choice f=Tr(ρd​[−iH1​,ρ]) yields V˙=−f2. Decrease alone does not prove convergence: orthogonal pure eigenstates of a diagonal H0​ give zero input for a transverse Pauli control even though their distance remains nonzero. Such a law can be simulated to produce an open-loop control.
