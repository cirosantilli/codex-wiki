Frame of a vector bundle (e=(e1​,…,er​))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Vector bundle trivialization
OurBigBook.com Words: 165 Articles: 2
A frame over an open set U is a list of smooth sections e1​,…,er​ of a rank-r vector bundle whose values form a basis in every fibre over U. It is equivalent to a vector bundle trivialization by (x,v)↦∑a​ea​(x)va. Two local frames differ by a smooth map g:U→GL(r), written e′=eg.
