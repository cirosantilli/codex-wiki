Bilinear degree pairing for elliptic-curve homomorphisms (B(ϕ,ψ)=21​(deg(ϕ+ψ)−degϕ−degψ))

OurBigBook.com Words: 69
With zero degree assigned to the zero element of the homomorphism group of elliptic curves, the degree parallelogram law makes this symmetric pairing additive in both arguments. Its diagonal is the degree of an isogeny; it is positive definite on every finite-dimensional rational span after extension to the real numbers. The integral polarization 2B(ϕ,ψ) is integer-valued, while B can be half-integral. For instance, if an elliptic curve has an automorphism ω with ω2+ω+1=0, then 1+ω=−ω2 has degree one, so B(1,ω)=−1/2.
