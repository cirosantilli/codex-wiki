Amplitude damping channel (A0​=∣0⟩⟨0∣+1−p​∣1⟩⟨1∣,A1​=p​∣0⟩⟨1∣)

A zero-temperature energy-relaxation channel transfers the excited population of a qubit to its ground state. Its Kraus operators are A0​=diag(1,1−p​) and A1​=p​∣0⟩⟨1∣ for 0≤p≤1. They satisfy ∑j​Aj†​Aj​=I. The excited population is multiplied by 1−p and coherences by 1−p​. For nonzero p, it is not a unital quantum channel.
