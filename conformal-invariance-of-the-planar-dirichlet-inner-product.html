Conformal invariance of the planar Dirichlet inner product

 ... Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space First-order Sobolev space Zero-boundary Sobolev space Dirichlet inner product
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For a conformal bijection f:D→D and smooth u,v of compact support on D, (u∘f,v∘f)∇,D​=(u,v)∇,D​​. The Jacobian matrix of f is a rotation times ∣f′∣. Consequently the chain rule introduces ∣f′∣2 into the gradient pairing, while the change of variables formula introduces precisely the same factor into area. They cancel. The identity extends by completion to the Dirichlet energy spaces; it does not preserve the L2 term of an inhomogeneous Sobolev norm.
