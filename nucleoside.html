Nucleoside

 Home Chemistry Molecule
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 4
A nucleoside combines a nucleobase with a sugar, without the phosphate groups of a nucleotide. Adenosine is the nucleoside built from adenine and ribose. See Alberts et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell: nucleosides and nucleotides.
