NLS ground state (Q)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Nonlinear analysis Nonlinear Schrödinger blowup analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 265 Articles: 6
For the mass-critical focusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation in dimension d, the NLS ground state is the positive radial solution of
−ΔQ+Q−Q1+4/d=0.
(22)
Its symmetry orbit consists of the optimizers of the sharp Gagliardo-Nirenberg inequality.
