Remote sensing

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Radiometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 836 Articles: 23
Inferring properties of a distant object from measured signals, often electromagnetic radiation. Spatial sampling, spectral information and observation cadence must jointly resolve the physical process of interest. Received radiance alone need not uniquely separate temperature, emissivity and atmospheric attenuation.
