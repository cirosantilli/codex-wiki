Drift estimator (bT​(x,h))

 ... Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Stochastic differential equation Itô diffusion Drift coefficient
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
From a continuously observed Itô diffusion, a local drift estimator divides ∫0T​1[x−h,x+h]​(Xt​)dXt​ by the corresponding diffusion occupation time. Its error separates into a local Hölder continuity bias and an Itô integral divided by occupation time. The Itô isometry gives a pointwise noise scale (Th)−1/2 when the invariant density is locally positive.
