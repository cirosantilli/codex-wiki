Bertrand's postulate (n<p<2n(n>1))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Prime number
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
For every integer n>1, there is a prime number strictly between n and 2n. This supplies cyclic prime moduli comparable to an integer interval's length in additive combinatorics.
