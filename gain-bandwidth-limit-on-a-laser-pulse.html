Gain bandwidth limit on a laser pulse (Δt∼1/Δν,Δν=c(1/λmin​−1/λmax​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Light Optical pulse Time-bandwidth product
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
A transform-limited pulse needs coherent phases across its contributing frequency range. A laser's usable gain bandwidth therefore sets a duration scale through the time-bandwidth product, as displayed. The wavelength interval must first be converted to frequency; the simple conversion cΔλ/λ2 is only a narrow-band approximation. Numerical constants depend on pulse shape and width convention: a Gaussian intensity envelope has ΔtFWHM​ΔνFWHM​=2ln2/π≃0.441. A hard gain interval is not automatically a spectral FWHM. Mode locking can arrange the longitudinal modes' phases, while group velocity dispersion must be controlled to approach the transform limit. The cavity round-trip time sets pulse spacing, rather than this bandwidth-limited width.
