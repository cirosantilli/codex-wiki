Theta function with characteristics (θ[α,β](z,τ)=∑n∈Z​eπi(n+α)2τ+2πi(n+α)(z+β))

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Theta function Jacobi theta function

For real characteristics α,β and Imτ>0, Gaussian decay gives normal convergence, an entire function of z and holomorphic dependence on τ. Integer shifts of alpha leave the function unchanged; a unit shift of beta multiplies it by e2πiα. Its spatial quasi-periods are θ(z+1)=e2πiαθ(z) and θ(z+τ)=e−πiτ−2πi(z+β)θ(z). Binary characteristic labels often mean alpha and beta divided by two, so the convention must be stated.
