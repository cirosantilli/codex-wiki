Stretched coordinates for quasi-geostrophic inversion (Z=(N/∣f∣)z,Q−f=ψxx​+ψyy​+ψZZ​)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Potential vorticity Potential-vorticity inversion Stratified quasi-geostrophic inversion
OurBigBook.com Words: 107 Articles: 1
For constant nonzero Coriolis parameter and positive constant buoyancy frequency, the anisotropic quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity operator becomes the ordinary three-dimensional Laplacian in the displayed coordinates. Its decaying whole-space inverse is ψ(X)=−∫[Q(X′)−f]/(4π∣X−X′∣)d3X′. Boundary or far-field data are needed to exclude arbitrary harmonic additions.
