FRET optical crosstalk

 Home Biology Biophysical measurement Förster resonance energy transfer FRET sensitized emission
OurBigBook.com Words: 195
FRET optical crosstalk includes FRET donor emission leaking into the FRET acceptor detection band and direct excitation of the FRET acceptor by the nominal FRET donor excitation light. Measure donor-only, acceptor-only and unlabeled preparations with the same fluorescence microscope settings to separate these contributions and background.[ref]
An explicit linear measurement model makes the controls' purpose clear. Let D be background-subtracted donor-band counts under donor excitation, A acceptor-band counts under acceptor excitation, and F acceptor-band counts under donor excitation. Donor-only and acceptor-only preparations determine leakage coefficients a and b. Then
S=F−aD−bA
(27)
is a corrected FRET sensitized emission signal under the assumed linear, fixed optical response. In a synthetic example D=100, A=80, a=0.10, b=0.05 and F=34 give S=20. Even a nontransferring sample would give F=14 from these two contaminants. Correcting that signal still does not convert S into FRET efficiency without a calibration of excitation, yields and detection.
A biological comparison also needs a pairing-negative preparation and a known transferring positive preparation. Match label abundance and localization where possible; changing concentration can change encounter frequency as well as brightness. Check that attaching the labels preserves the protein function being investigated. Otherwise the measurement can faithfully report a perturbation introduced by the labels themselves.
