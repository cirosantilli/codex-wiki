Ring torus

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Gauss-Bonnet theorem Gaussian curvature of a torus
OurBigBook.com Words: 19
A ring torus with major radius R and minor radius r<R has parametrization
X(θ,ϕ)=((R+rcosθ)cosϕ,(R+rcosθ)sinϕ,rsinθ)
(57)
and Gaussian curvature K(θ)=cosθ/[r(R+rcosθ)].
