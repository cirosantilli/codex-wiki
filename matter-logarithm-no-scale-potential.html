Matter-logarithm no-scale potential (V=eK(s2∣DS​W∣2+3t​∣WC​∣2))

For s=S+Sˉ>0, t=T+Tˉ−∣C∣2>0, K=−logs−3logt and a holomorphic superpotential independent of T, the exact F-term potential is V=eK[s2∣DS​W∣2+(t/3)∣WC​∣2]. Inverting the mixed T,C metric gives KTTˉ=t(t+∣C∣2)/3, KTCˉ=tCˉ/3, KCTˉ=tC/3, and KCCˉ=t/3. Substitution of DT​W=−3W/t and DC​W=WC​+3CˉW/t cancels all mixed terms, leaving 3∣W∣2+(t/3)∣WC​∣2; the first term cancels the supergravity negative term.
