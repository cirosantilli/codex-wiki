Nevanlinna characteristic (T(r,f)=m(r,f)+N(r,f))

 ... Complex analysis Isolated singularity Classification of isolated singularities Pole Meromorphic function Nevanlinna theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 206 Articles: 3
The characteristic of a meromorphic function is the sum of its Nevanlinna proximity function and Nevanlinna integrated counting function. It treats large values and actual poles together. Replacing f by f−a changes the characteristic by at most log(1+∣a∣).
