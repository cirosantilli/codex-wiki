Ubiquitination

 Home Biology Protein Ubiquitin
OurBigBook.com Words: 61 Articles: 5
Ubiquitination covalently attaches ubiquitin to a substrate, usually through successive action of a ubiquitin-activating enzyme, ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme and ubiquitin ligase. The modification can change degradation, localization or signalling; attachment alone does not specify which outcome occurs.
