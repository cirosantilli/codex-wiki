Gärtner–Ellis theorem (I=Λ∗)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Convergence of random variables Large deviation principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 117
Let XN​∈Rd and aN​→∞. Define the scaled cumulant-generating function
ΛN​(θ)=aN−1​logEeaN​⟨θ,XN​⟩.
(14)
Suppose its pointwise limit Λ exists, is lower semicontinuous, has 0 in the interior of its effective domain, and is essentially smooth: it is differentiable on that interior, which is nonempty, and its gradient norm diverges at every finite boundary point approached from the interior. Then XN​ satisfies a large deviation principle with good rate function given by the Legendre-Fenchel transform
Λ∗(x)=supθ​{⟨θ,x⟩−Λ(θ)}.
(15)
The Chernoff bound supplies the upper estimate. The lower estimate uses exponential tilting at a parameter whose gradient selects the required mean; essential smoothness supplies the boundary approximation needed for the full lower bound. A finite boundary slope can leave an affine branch of Λ∗ outside this argument, so a separate lower-bound proof is then necessary.
