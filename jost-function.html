Jost function (F(k))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering S-matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
For radial quantum scattering, the incoming coefficient of the regular solution defines the Jost function, with convention S(k)=F(−k)/F(k). A zero at k=iκ, κ>0, removes the exponentially growing component and produces a bound state, subject to regularity and normalizability. Poles of the outgoing numerator alone need not produce bound states.
