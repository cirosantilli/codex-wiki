Paczyński-Wiita circular orbit

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical black hole Black-hole accretion Paczyński-Wiita potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 2
Let rS​=2GM/c2. In the Paczyński-Wiita potential, circular orbit balance and the radial epicyclic frequency formula give
Ω2=r(r−rS​)2GM​,h=r−rS​GM​r3/2​,κr2​=r(r−rS​)3GM(r−3rS​)​.
(39)
Thus the innermost stable circular orbit lies at r=3rS​. Its specific orbital energy is −c2/16, giving a zero-torque radiative efficiency of black-hole accretion of 1/16. The model reproduces the Schwarzschild inner orbit exactly but approximates its relativistic energy and frequency. Abramowicz's derivation explains the potential's relation to the Schwarzschild effective potential.
