Bühlmann model (m=E[m(Θ)], v=E[v(Θ)], a=Var(m(Θ)))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 605 Articles: 12
Given a latent risk parameter Θ, observations are independent and identically distributed random variables with conditional expectation m(Θ) and conditional variance v(Θ). The Bühlmann credibility premium estimates m(Θ) by an affine function of past observations under mean squared error. Its population parameters are m=Em(Θ), expected process variance v=Ev(Θ), and variance of hypothetical means a=Var(m(Θ)).
