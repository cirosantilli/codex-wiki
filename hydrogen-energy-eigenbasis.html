Hydrogen energy eigenbasis

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Hydrogen atom
OurBigBook.com Words: 210 Articles: 6
Neglecting spin, bound hydrogen eigenstates are labelled ∣n,ℓ,m⟩, where ℓ=0,…,n−1 and m=−ℓ,…,ℓ. Their unperturbed energy depends only on n.
