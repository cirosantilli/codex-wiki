Bus ownership

 Home Computer science Computer bus Bus arbitration
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
The permission to use a particular computer bus resource during a specified phase. A pending grant need not authorize immediate use. Arm's AMBA Specification, revision 2.0 (section 3.11.3, figures 3-15–3-17) makes address-path handover depend on completion, and delays data bus ownership relative to address bus ownership. Consequently, a single whole-bus transaction owner is a useful simple model, but does not describe every overlapping implementation.
