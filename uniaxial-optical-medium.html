Uniaxial optical medium (ε=ε0​diag(no2​,no2​,ne2​))

A transparent, nonmagnetic linear anisotropic dielectric is uniaxial when two principal permittivities agree. With the optic axis along z, its permittivity tensor has the displayed diagonal matrix form. Here no​ and ne​ are principal refractive indices at a specified frequency; ne​ is not the extraordinary wave's index in every direction.[ref]
