A Rabi oscillation is a coherent oscillation of populations in a driven two-level quantum system.
A resonant drive can transfer a quantum state from one energy eigenstate to another and then bring its population back. This is unitary time evolution: the drive exchanges energy with the selected two-level system, while total probability stays one. One complete Rabi cycle has population period 2π/Ω; halfway through, a Rabi pi pulse transfers the whole population in the ideal model. This makes Rabi oscillations useful for calibrating resonant quantum control, preparing quantum superpositions and supplying the pulses of Ramsey interferometry.
