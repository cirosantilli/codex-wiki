Haar wavelet (ψ=χ[0,1/2)​−χ[1/2,1)​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Wavelet Orthonormal wavelet
OurBigBook.com Words: 570 Articles: 9
The Haar wavelet has one vanishing moment and compact support of unit length. Its scaling function is χ[0,1)​, and its refinement coefficients are h0​=h1​=1/2​. The approximation spaces consist of functions constant on dyadic cells. Its discontinuity makes it effective for jump localization but limits smooth-function approximation.
