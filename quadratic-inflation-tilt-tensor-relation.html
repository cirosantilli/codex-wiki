Quadratic-inflation tilt-tensor relation (r=4(1−ns​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmic inflation Slow-roll approximation Quadratic-potential slow-roll solution
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The quadratic potential has equal potential slow-roll parameters, ϵV​=ηV​=1/(2N+1). Consequently ns​=1−4/(2N+1) and r=16/(2N+1) at leading order. The relation r=4(1−ns​) is independent of the inflaton mass, so a satisfactory scalar tilt does not by itself guarantee a satisfactory tensor prediction.
