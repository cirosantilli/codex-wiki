Complex tautological line bundle (OPn​(−1))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Tautological bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 394 Articles: 7
Over complex projective space, its fibre at a line ℓ⊆Cn+1 is ℓ itself. It is a holomorphic line bundle contained in the trivial bundle. Its dual is the hyperplane line bundle.
