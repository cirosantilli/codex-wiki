Eddy diffusivity (K)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
An eddy diffusivity is a transport closure representing unresolved turbulence by a mean scalar flux proportional to a negative mean concentration gradient. A local estimate is K∼Uℓ, where U and ℓ are turbulent speed and length scales. In a line plume with constant characteristic speed and width proportional to z, this gives K∝z. Its coefficient is an independent closure parameter; fluid entrainment conservation alone does not determine it.
