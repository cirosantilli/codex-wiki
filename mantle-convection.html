Mantle convection

 Home Physics Branch of physics Geophysics Solid Earth
OurBigBook.com Words: 233 Articles: 5
Mantle convection is the slow buoyancy-driven motion of a rocky planetary mantle. On geological timescales the solid mantle behaves as a highly viscous Newtonian fluid to first approximation.
