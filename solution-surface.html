OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The function graph of a scalar solution of a differential equation with two independent variables. For a first-order partial differential equation, the method of characteristics constructs it from lifted characteristic curves; invertibility of the projected parametrization is required for the surface to define a single-valued function.
