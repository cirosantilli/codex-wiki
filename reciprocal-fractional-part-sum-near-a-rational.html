Reciprocal fractional-part sum near a rational

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Analytic number theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
If (a,q)=1, ∣α−a/q∣≤q−2, and M,R≥2, then uniformly in m0​,
∑m0​≤m<m0​+M​min(R,∥αm∥−1)≪log(2q)(qMR​+M+R+q).
(16)
Split the interval into blocks of length at most q/2. Within a block the points αm are ≫1/q-separated modulo one; ordering their distances from the nearest integer gives O(R+qlogq) per block.
