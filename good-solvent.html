Good solvent

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry) Polymer solution
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
A good solvent favors contact with polymer segments strongly enough that the effective excluded volume is positive. A long dilute flexible chain swells relative to an ideal chain; the three-dimensional size exponent is approximately 0.588, often replaced by 3/5 in a scaling estimate. This designation depends on the polymer, solvent and temperature, rather than on the solvent alone.
