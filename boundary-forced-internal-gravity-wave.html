Boundary-forced internal gravity wave

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Gravity wave Internal wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 456 Articles: 7
A moving boundary excites an internal gravity wave through its kinematic boundary condition. For wavenumber k and angular frequency ω, the vertical velocity field satisfies W′′+k2(N2/ω2−1)W=0. The radiation condition selects outward group velocity; when ω>N, the bounded response is an evanescent wave.
