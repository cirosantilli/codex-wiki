L1 space (L1(μ))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Lp space
OurBigBook.com Words: 224 Articles: 2
The Lp space for p=1 consists of equivalence classes of integrable functions, with L1 norm ∥f∥1​=∫∣f∣dμ. Conditional expectation is a contraction in this norm. Convergence in this space is L1 convergence, which controls the change in every integral over a measurable subset.
