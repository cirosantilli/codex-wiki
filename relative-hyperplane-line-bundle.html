Relative hyperplane line bundle (L=S∗,L∣P(Ex​)​=O(1))

 ... Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Projective bundle Holomorphic projectivization by lines Relative tautological line bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
Dualize the relative tautological line bundle to obtain the positive relative hyperplane line bundle. It restricts to O(1) on each projective fibre. This sign depends on the convention that projectivization parametrizes lines; fixing the convention avoids confusing it with the tautological line itself.
