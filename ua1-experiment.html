UA1 experiment

 Home Physics Particle detection W and Z discovery experiments
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
The 1983 UA1 experiment paper used proton–antiproton collisions at s​=540GeV. A high-transverse momentum electron candidate required a track matched to an electromagnetic particle shower, with little energy behind it in the hadronic particle calorimeter. Test beams checked electron and pion responses; ordinary events and particle jet samples checked the imbalance resolution. Two searches, beginning with isolated electrons and with missing transverse energy, converged on the same candidates. Conversion pairs, hadrons and heavy-flavor decays were examined as backgrounds.[ref]
