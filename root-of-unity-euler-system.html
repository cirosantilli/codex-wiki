Root-of-unity Euler system

 ... Algebra Galois theory Cyclotomic polynomial Cyclotomic field Cyclotomic unit Cyclotomic Euler system
OurBigBook.com Words: 338 Articles: 3
Fix a finite excluded set S containing two, and let WS​ contain the roots of unity of orders coprime to every prime in S. A root-of-unity Euler system is a nonzero algebraic-valued function Φ on WS​ that is even and Galois equivariant, satisfies ∏ρq=1​Φ(ρζ)=Φ(ζq) for q∈/S, and satisfies Φ(ρζ)≡Φ(ζ)(modq) when the order of ζ is prime to q, for every prime q over q. Values are required to be locally integral units where the congruence is used. These distribution and congruence properties provide auxiliary cyclotomic extensions, beyond a single norm-compatible p-power sequence.
