Malus' law (I=I0​cos2θ)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Plane electromagnetic wave Polarization of an electromagnetic wave Linear polarization
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
An ideal linear polarizer transmits the fraction cos2θ of incident linearly polarized light when its axis makes angle θ to the polarization. For a single photon, the corresponding conditional transmission probability is ∣⟨θ∣0⟩∣2=cos2θ, by the Born rule; rejection has probability sin2θ. The classical field calculation projects its electric-field amplitude onto the transmission direction and squares that amplitude to obtain intensity. This is a conditional probability given the incident polarization and is not automatically the joint probability of passing one analyzer and failing another.
