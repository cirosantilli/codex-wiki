Coordinate subspace (EB​=span{ei​:i∈B})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Vector subspace
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
For a chosen basis (e1​,…,en​), the vector subspace spanned by a specified collection of basis vectors. In the standard basis of Euclidean space, its vectors have zero coordinates outside B. The block coordinate gradient is the orthogonal projection of the full gradient onto this subspace. The fixed coordinate subspace consisting of the first k coordinates is a particular example.
