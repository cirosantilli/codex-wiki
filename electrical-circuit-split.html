Electrical circuit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 15k Articles: 162
An electrical circuit connects components that carry electric current and sustain electric potential difference differences. Its dynamics may couple electrical resistance, inductance and capacitance.
