Acoustic eikonal equation in a shear flow ((ω−Ukx​)2=c02​∣k∣2)

 Home Physics Acoustics
OurBigBook.com Words: 148 Articles: 1
For a slowly varying parallel shear flow U(y)ex​, the leading WKB method neglects mean-velocity gradients while retaining local advection. The continuity equation and linearized Euler equations then give the displayed acoustic dispersion relation. On the positive intrinsic-frequency branch, ω=Ukx​+c0​∣k∣ and the group velocity is Uex​+c0​k/∣k∣. A wavefront normal is parallel to k; the acoustic energy-ray direction generally differs because of the advection term.
