Homotopy inverse limit of a tower (holimn​Xn​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
For a sequence of maps Xn​→Xn−1​, its homotopy inverse limit records points xn​ and paths from the image of xn​ to xn−1​, with the usual homotopy-coherent compatibility. A fibrant replacement tower computes it by an ordinary inverse limit. For a tower whose homotopy groups are eventually constant in each degree, the limit has those stable groups: the Milnor inverse-limit exact sequence has zero derived-limit term. In particular X→holimPn​X is a weak equivalence for the Postnikov tower of a connected CW complex.
