Transcendence theory

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Diophantine approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 13
Transcendence theory proves that particular numbers are not algebraic numbers, or that several numbers satisfy no nonzero polynomial relation with algebraic coefficients. A recurring method constructs an auxiliary expression which is simultaneously too small analytically and too large arithmetically if the proposed values were algebraic.
