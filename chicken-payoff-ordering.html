Chicken payoff ordering (T>R>S>P)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Game theory Evolutionarily stable strategy Hawk-Dove game
OurBigBook.com Words: 85
The Chicken payoff ordering describes a symmetric two-player bimatrix game in which aggression earns temptation T against yielding, mutual yielding earns R, yielding to aggression earns S, and mutual aggression earns P, with the displayed strict ordering. Aggression is a best response to yielding, and yielding is a best response to aggression. Thus the two asymmetric action pairs are pure strategy Nash equilibria. The Hawk-Dove game has this ordering in its positive-resource regime where the damage cost exceeds the resource value. In the prisoner's dilemma, the ordering of S and P is reversed, making defection a strictly dominant strategy instead.
