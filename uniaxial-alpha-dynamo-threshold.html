Uniaxial alpha dynamo threshold (γ2=(q2+m2)2/(m2+δq2))

OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For α=diag(α0​,α0​,α1​), set γ=α0​/η, δ=α1​/α0​, and q2=k2+l2. A steady nonzero Fourier mode obeys the displayed condition when the denominator is nonzero. At fixed q>0 and 0<δ<1, minimizing over m2≥0 gives m∗2​=(1−2δ)q2 and γmin2​=4(1−δ)q2 for δ<1/2; for δ≥1/2 it gives m∗2​=0 and γmin2​=q2/δ. This is a boundary-constrained threshold of an anisotropic alpha-squared dynamo.
