Scale factor in a conformal-time density parameter (Ωi​=8πGa2ρˉ​i​/(3H2))

For physical density ρˉ​i​ and conformal time, H=a′/a=aH. Substitution into the definition Ωi​=8πGρˉ​i​/(3H2) gives the displayed formula. Omitting a2 gives Ωi​/a2, which need not equal one even in a spatially flat matter-only universe. In particular, the conformal growth source 23​H2Ωm​ equals 4πGa2ρˉ​m​ with the physical density parameter.
