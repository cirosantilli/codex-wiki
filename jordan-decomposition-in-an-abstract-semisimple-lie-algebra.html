Jordan decomposition in an abstract semisimple Lie algebra (x=xs​+xn​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 83
A complex semisimple Lie algebra has an intrinsic Jordan–Chevalley decomposition x=xs​+xn​ characterized by [xs​,xn​]=0, a diagonalizable Adjoint representation operator adxs​, and a nilpotent Adjoint representation operator adxn​. The Adjoint representation is faithful because the centre is zero. Closure of semisimple matrix Lie algebras are closed under additive Jordan decomposition applied to this faithful representation supplies the parts and their uniqueness. For every finite-dimensional Lie algebra representation ρ, the operators ρ(xs​) and ρ(xn​) are the semisimple and nilpotent parts of ρ(x). Thus the decomposition is independent of a matrix embedding; for a nonfaithful representation it passes to the semisimple quotient by its kernel.
