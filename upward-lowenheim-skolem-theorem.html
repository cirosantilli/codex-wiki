Upward Lowenheim-Skolem theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic Lowenheim-Skolem theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
If an L-theory has an infinite model, then it has models of arbitrarily large cardinality. For a cardinal κ, add constants cα​ for α<κ and all inequalities cα​=cβ​. Every finite fragment is satisfiable in the given infinite model, so compactness gives a model with at least κ elements. Applying the downward theorem produces a model of exactly κ when κ≥∣L∣+ℵ0​.
