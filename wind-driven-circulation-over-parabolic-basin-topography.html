Wind-driven circulation over parabolic basin topography (H=ax(Lx​−x))

OurBigBook.com Words: 68
With f=f0​+βy and parabolic depth, the topographic potential-vorticity pseudovelocity is (−β/H,−fHx​/H2). For positive f,β, it points west and turns south on the western slope, north on the eastern slope. Its characteristics are the parabolas y=Qax(Lx​−x)/β−f0​/β, where Q=f/H. Wind-driven ocean gyres are steered by these depth gradients; drag and boundary data determine the actual ocean transport streamfunction. The zero-depth shoreline is a singular limit that requires a separate physical regularization.
