Demand correspondence (x(p,w))

 Home Mathematics Mathematical economics General equilibrium theory Exchange economy
OurBigBook.com Words: 155 Articles: 3
A demand correspondence assigns a nonempty set of chosen consumption bundles to each price and wealth pair. Every chosen bundle satisfies the budget constraint. A utility function rationalizes the demand correspondence when the chosen bundles are exactly its maximizers over the budget set.
