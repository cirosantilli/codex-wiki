Commutator expansion for exponential conjugation (eABe−A=∑n≥0​adAn​(B)/n!)

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Lie bracket Commutator

For bounded linear operators, or formally in an associative algebra,
eλABe−λA=∑n≥0​n!λn​adAn​(B),adA​(B)=[A,B].
(9)
Differentiate the two exponentials using the product rule: the result is eλA[A,B]e−λA. Induction gives the nth derivative eλAadAn​(B)e−λA, and the Taylor series at zero proves the formula. For bounded operators convergence follows from ∥adAn​(B)∥≤(2∥A∥)n∥B∥. For unbounded quantum operators, use a common invariant domain and justify the series there, or use the finite-dimensional system of commutators when their span closes.
