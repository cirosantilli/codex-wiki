Unsmoothed matter-density reduced skewness (S3​=34/7)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Standard perturbation theory in cosmology Standard perturbation theory density kernel
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
For Gaussian initial conditions and the Einstein-de Sitter second-order standard perturbation theory density kernel, the unsmoothed density contrast obeys
⟨δ3⟩=734​σ4+O(σ6),σ2=⟨δ2⟩.
(9)
There are three choices of the second-order field and two connected Wick contractions; the angular average of F2​ is 5/7+(2/7)/3=17/21. Thus the reduced skewness is 6(17/21)=34/7. Smoothing changes this result, and the unsmoothed calculation requires a finite variance.
