ΓIR​=A2u​+3E1u​.​
(67)
The A2u​ fundamental couples to the out-of-plane dipole component and each E1u​ fundamental to the two in-plane components. Counting one frequency for each degenerate pair, the expected number is 1+3=4 fundamental bands, containing seven vibrational coordinates.
This is the symmetry count for the full molecule, including hydrogen displacements. The experimental compilation likewise lists the out-of-plane A2u​ fundamental and three E1u​ fundamentals; anharmonic resonances and weak intensity borrowing can produce additional observed features beyond the ideal fundamental count.[ref]
