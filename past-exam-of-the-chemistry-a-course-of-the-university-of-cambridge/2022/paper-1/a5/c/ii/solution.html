The overall vibrational ground state is invariant under every molecular symmetry operation, so it transforms as A1g​. Combining it with the electronic ground state gives
Γinitial​=A1g​⊗A1g​=A1g​.​
(68)
For a degenerate harmonic normal mode, the zero-quantum wavefunction depends on the invariant sum Qx2​+Qy2​, so the ground vibrational symmetry remains totally symmetric even when excited vibrational states form multiplets.
