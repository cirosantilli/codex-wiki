Let P12​ exchange the complete labels of the two electrons. The spatial factors obey P12​Φ±​=±Φ±​. The singlet state spin factor is antisymmetric, while each triplet state spin factor is symmetric. Thus in all four cases
P12​Ψ=−Ψ,​
(17)
as required by the Pauli exclusion principle. In particular, the MS​=0 triplet state remains a triplet despite having opposite measured spin projections in each product term.
For a spin-independent Hamiltonian and the same fixed spatial atomic orbitals, direct evaluation gives
Esinglet​=ha​+hb​+Jab​+Kab​,Etriplet​=ha​+hb​+Jab​−Kab​,
(18)
where Jab​ is the direct Coulomb integral and Kab​>0 is the exchange integral for the helium 1s,2s pair. Therefore
Esinglet​−Etriplet​=2Kab​>0.​
(19)
The antisymmetric spatial wavefunction vanishes when the two positions coincide and reduces their Coulomb repulsion. The lower energy of the maximum-spin member of this fixed electron configuration is the first of Hund's rules.
