The all-positive combination is unchanged by every vertical mirror but changes sign under σh​, giving A2u​. The alternating combination changes sign under both sorts of vertical mirror, as required for A1u​. The two remaining sign patterns distinguish B1u​ from B2u​ in the printed axes. The signs refer to the upper pz​ lobes; the lower lobes have opposite quantum phases, and neither colour denotes an electric charge.
