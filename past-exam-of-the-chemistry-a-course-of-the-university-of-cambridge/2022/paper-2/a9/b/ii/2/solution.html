For the regime 0<γ<t considered in part (iii), the crossing becomes an avoided crossing with gap 4γ at k=π/(2a). The lower band has its maximum there and the upper band its minimum. Their orbital characters exchange smoothly across this region, with equal s/p weights at its centre. The lower-right panel gives the sketch.
The zero-mixing limit recovers the crossing of case (1). A useful special limiting case is γ=t, where the square root is independent of wavevector and both bands are flat at E=±2t. This flat-band limit is outside the strict γ<t assumption made in part (iii).
