The opposite spatial parity of the s orbital and axial p orbital gives opposite signs for left and right s–p hopping. With real βsp​, one consistent Bloch-basis convention gives
H(k)=(Es​(k)−2iβsp​sin(ka)​2iβsp​sin(ka)Ep​(k)​).
(48)
The resulting energies are
E±​=2Es​+Ep​​±(2Es​−Ep​​)2+4βsp2​sin2(ka)​.​
(49)
Thus s-p mixing in a one-dimensional tight-binding chain lowers the lower branch and raises the upper branch wherever the coupling is nonzero. For separated atomic levels, the lower branch remains mainly s and the upper mainly p, with increasing admixture where their uncoupled energies approach.
The upper-right panel of the figure in part (a)(ii) compares the mixed curves with dashed unmixed curves. The mixing vanishes at both k=0 and π/a, so those endpoints remain unchanged. If the unmixed curves approach or cross in the interior, the nonzero coupling produces an avoided crossing.
