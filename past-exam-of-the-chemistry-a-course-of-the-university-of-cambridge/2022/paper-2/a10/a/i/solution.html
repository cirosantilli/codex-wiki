A typical intrinsic semiconductor has a nearly full valence band ending at Ev​ and a nearly empty conduction band starting at Ec​, separated by
Eg​=Ec​−Ev​>0.​
(56)
At finite temperature, the equilibrium Fermi level lies near the middle of the gap. In the nondegenerate parabolic-band approximation,
EF​=2Ec​+Ev​​+2kB​T​lnNc​Nv​​,​
(57)
where Nc​,Nv​ are the two effective density of states values. Equal densities of states put it exactly at midgap; unequal masses or valley multiplicities shift it slightly. The left panel gives the requested energy-level sketch, with doped cases for comparison.
