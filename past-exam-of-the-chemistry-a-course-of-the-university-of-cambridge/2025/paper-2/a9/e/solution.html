The Fermi temperature is
TF​=EF​/k=2.21414×105 K​.
(32)
Inserting it into the question's quoted estimate gives
TF​2R(4 K)​=3.0041×10−4 Jmol−1K−1​.
(33)
As explained in (d), that count is per mole of conduction electrons. Using the stipulated six electrons per tungsten atom gives 1.8025×10−3 Jmol−1K−1 per mole of tungsten in the same rough model. The accurate free-electron Sommerfeld expansion coefficient would instead give 4.4474×10−3 Jmol−1K−1 per mole of tungsten. These are different approximations and counting conventions, not three interchangeable exact answers.
