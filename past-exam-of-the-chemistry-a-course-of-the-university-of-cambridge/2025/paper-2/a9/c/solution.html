The Fermi sphere volume in reciprocal space is
VF​=34π​kF3​=46.9429 A˚−3​.
(29)
The primitive real-space cell has volume a3/2, so the first Brillouin zone has volume
VBZ​=a3/2(2π)3​=15.6476 A˚−3​,VF​=3VBZ​​.
(30)
Each band accommodates two electrons per primitive cell when spin is counted. Six electrons therefore require three such zone-volumes of occupied reciprocal-space states in the free-electron counting picture. The extended-zone sphere is not confined to the first zone; folding it produces multiple bands. It does not imply that the actual tungsten Fermi surface is spherical.
