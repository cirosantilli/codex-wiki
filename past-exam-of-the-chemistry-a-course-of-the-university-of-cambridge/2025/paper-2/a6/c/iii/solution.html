For one mole, Nk=R, so the three-level Morse oscillator result is
Um​=RT2q3​q3′​​=124.21 Jmol−1​.
(10)
For the full quantum harmonic oscillator with the given wavenumber,
Um,harm​=e215/(0.695⋅100)−1R(215/0.695)​=122.16 Jmol−1​.
(11)
The specified anharmonic calculation is about 1.7% higher. Anharmonicity lowers the first excitation spacings and increases excited-state occupation. Both results remain small because 100 K is well below the vibrational temperature. The comparison also mixes a three-level anharmonic truncation with an infinite harmonic sum; omitted higher Morse levels would make a further small positive correction at this temperature.
