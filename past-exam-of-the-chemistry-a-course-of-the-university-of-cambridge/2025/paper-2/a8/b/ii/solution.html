The primed fTS′​ is the corresponding transition state partition function per unit volume with the unstable reaction-coordinate degree of freedom omitted. Its replacement by the crossing-frequency factor kT/h is already outside the partition-function ratio. For a diatomic atom-pair transition state, the omitted coordinate is the bond stretch, leaving translation and rotation.
