 ... Past exam of the chemistry A course of the University of Cambridge 2025 Paper 1 A3 d iii
OurBigBook.com Words: 98
Read the stronger members of the inset doublets at about 2945, 2926 and 2906 cm−1. Their spacings are roughly 19–20 cm−1, so the common-B estimate is
B≃21​Δν≃10 cm−1.​
(28)
Using the harmonic wavenumber from the preceding calculation and labelling lines by the lower-state J,
νP1​​≃2886.89−2(10)=2867 cm−1,νR1​​≃2886.89+4(10)=2927 cm−1.​
(29)
These are graph-based estimates. Finite reading precision and different upper/lower rotational constants limit their accuracy to a few cm−1; extra decimal places would not be justified. The R1 result refers to J=1→2, while R0 is the line nearest the high-wavenumber side of the band gap.
