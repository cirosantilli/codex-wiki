OurBigBook.com Words: 82
For operations ordered as (E,C2​(z),σxz​,σyz​), the characters of the twelve Cartesian displacements are
χ3N​=(12,−2,4,2).
(32)
Only atoms left fixed contribute: all four for E and σxz​, but only C and O for C2​ and σyz​. Multiplying their counts by the Cartesian traces 3,−1,1,1 gives these numbers. Subtract
χtrans​=(3,−1,1,1),χrot​=(3,−1,−1,−1)
(33)
to obtain χvib​=(6,0,4,2). The character table reduction gives
Γvib​=3A1​+2B1​+B2​.​
(34)
The dimensions sum to 6=3N−6, as required. The B2​ mode is the out-of-plane wag with this axis convention.
