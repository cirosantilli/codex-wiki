For two eigenfunctions, apply the same Hermitian operator identity across the two states:
qn​⟨ψm​,ψn​⟩=⟨ψm​,Q​ψn​⟩=⟨Q​ψm​,ψn​⟩=qm​⟨ψm​,ψn​⟩.
(5)
Hence (qn​−qm​)⟨ψm​,ψn​⟩=0. Distinct eigenvalues therefore imply orthogonal eigenfunctions. Within a degenerate eigenspace, orthogonality is a possible basis choice, rather than a consequence for every pair of vectors.
