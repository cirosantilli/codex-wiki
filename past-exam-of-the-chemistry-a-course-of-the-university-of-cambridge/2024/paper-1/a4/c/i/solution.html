Ideal gaseous phosphorus pentachloride is a trigonal bipyramid with D3h molecular point group symmetry. There are five chlorine sites and three 3p orbitals per site, hence a fifteen-dimensional basis. A moved atom contributes zero to the character; each fixed chlorine contributes the trace of the three-dimensional polar-vector matrix acting on its local p orbitals.

The dimension check is 2+1+6+2+4=15. As an additional transparent separation, the two axial chlorines supply A1′​+E′+A2′′​+E′′, while the three equatorial ones supply A1′​+A2′​+2E′+A2′′​+E′′. These are all chlorine 3p orbitals, including both radial and tangential functions; a five-orbital ligand-sigma basis would answer a different question.
