The half-zone volume reflects one electron per primitive cell and two spin states per Bloch wavevector: a completely filled single band would require two electrons per cell. Volume alone does not establish containment inside the polyhedral zone. Here the nearest reciprocal-lattice points are of type (111), so their zone faces are a distance π3​/a from the origin. The free-electron radius obeys
