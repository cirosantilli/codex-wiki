Differentiation of the restricted free energy gives the magnetization
Mrestricted​​=−V1​(∂B∂Amag​​)T,V​=VNμ​tanhkB​TμB​​.​
(34)
In the weak-field limit ∣μB∣≪kB​T, tanhx≃x, so
Mrestricted​≃VkB​TNμ2B​=VkB​T4NμB2​B​.​
(35)
The printed coefficient is inconsistent with the stated moment μ=2μB​. The requested expression without the factor four would follow from μ=μB​ in this restricted model. This discrepancy is present in the original PDF, rather than introduced by its transcription.
For completeness, differentiating the full thermal free energy gives
Mfull​=VNμ​1+e−βΔcoshxe−βΔsinhx​.​
(36)
Its weak-field form is
Mfull​≃VkB​TNμ2B​1+eθ/T1​=VkB​T(1+eθ/T)4NμB2​B​.​
(37)
At kB​T≫Δ this approaches 2NμB2​B/(VkB​T), so restoring the two nonmagnetic ground states does not recover the printed coefficient either. The inverse-temperature Curie law form is valid, but the population factor and moment squared must be retained consistently.
