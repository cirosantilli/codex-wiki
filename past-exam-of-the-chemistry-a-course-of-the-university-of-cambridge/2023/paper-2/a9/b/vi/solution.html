There are L primitive cells and therefore L lattice points. The specified reciprocal-space density LAc​/(2π)2 counts distinct allowed wavevectors; each supports two electron spin states. Filling the disk at zero temperature gives
Ne​=2(2π)2LAc​​πkF2​.
(62)
Using Ac​=2a2 and kF2​=π2/(2a2),
LNe​​=2πAc​kF2​​=2π​≃1.571 electrons per lattice point​.
(63)
The circle fills a fraction π/4 of the first-zone area, while one spin-degenerate band has two states per primitive cell; these give the same result. A single-spin count would instead be π/4, omitting the physical twofold spin degeneracy of this model. The circle has only just reached the sides, so the zone corners remain unfilled: first contact does not mean filling the entire first band.
