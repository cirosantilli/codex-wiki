Construct Bragg planes by taking the midpoints between the reciprocal origin and the other reciprocal lattice points. In one dimension these are points at k=mπ/a, for nonzero integer m.
The first Brillouin zone is the interval bounded by the nearest two such points:
−aπ​≤k≤aπ​​.
(53)
The second Brillouin zone consists of the next intervals on both sides:
−a2π​≤k≤−aπ​oraπ​≤k≤a2π​​.
(54)
Boundary points may be assigned to one adjacent zone to avoid double counting. The first zone is reached from the origin without crossing a Bragg point; the second is reached after crossing one. Each zone has total length 2π/a. In particular, the two separated pieces of the second zone are not the entire translated first-zone interval centered at 2π/a.
