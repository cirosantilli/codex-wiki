For an ideal mixture, the Helmholtz free energy is a sum over species. At fixed T,V and all other populations, the molecular partition function qi​ does not depend on Ni​. Differentiating the contribution written using each species' own energy reference gives
∂Ni​∂​[−Ni​kB​Tlnqi​+kB​T(Ni​lnNi​−Ni​)]=−kB​Tlnqi​+kB​TlnNi​.
(25)
The desired additive energy term requires a consistent energy reference in molecular partition functions. Let ε0,i be the energy per molecule of species i in its reference state, measured relative to a common chemical energy zero, and let qi​ sum excitations above that reference. Then
qiabs​=e−ε0,i/(kB​T)qi​,Ai​=Ni​ε0,i−Ni​kB​Tlnqi​+kB​T(Ni​lnNi​−Ni​).
(26)
Therefore
μi​=(∂Ni​∂A​)T,V,Nj=i​​=ε0,i−kB​TlnNi​qi​​​.
(27)
One convenient choice is each species' rovibrational ground state, so the molecular reference includes its zero-point energy. A different consistent reference is possible, but the corresponding partition function must change. The offset cannot arise from differentiation of a formula that has already omitted it, and it must not be counted twice. These chemical potentials are per particle; molar expressions instead use the molar gas constant.
