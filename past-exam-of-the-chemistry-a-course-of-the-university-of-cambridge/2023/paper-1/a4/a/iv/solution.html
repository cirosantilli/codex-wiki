Each cyanide contributes charge −1, so the formal nickel oxidation state is +2 and the metal has d8. The requested diagram retains only the five principally metal-3d molecular orbitals; the mainly ligand bonding molecular orbitals lie below them and are omitted.
In the square, dx2−y2​ points directly towards all four ligand donors and gives the highest, strongly antibonding molecular orbital, B1g​. The dz2​ function also has an allowed A1g​ interaction, but its in-plane overlap is smaller. Including the allowed nickel 4s mixing gives a principally d A1g​ level with relatively weak antibonding character. The B2g​ and Eg​ metal functions have no ligand partner in the sigma bond donor basis and are nonbonding in this minimal model. The useful qualitative ordering is therefore
D4h​:(B2g​,Eg​)nb​<A1g​<B1g​.​
(34)
The three nonbonding functions are shown together because the sigma-only model does not determine their finer splitting. Additional electrostatic and pi backbonding interactions can change the ordering of the lower d levels; molecular symmetry alone does not fix these energy gaps.[ref]
In the tetrahedron, the ligand T2​ combinations interact with the metal T2​ functions, producing higher d-derived antibonding molecular orbitals. The metal E pair has no sigma donor partner and remains nonbonding:
Td​:Enb​<T2,antibonding​.​
(35)
The diagram fills both arrangements with eight metal electrons; the square is shown in its fully paired state.
