The angular factors of the nickel 3d atomic orbitals transform like quadratic Cartesian functions. In the D4h molecular point group, the assignments are
orbitaldz2​dx2−y2​dxy​(dxz​,dyz​)​D4h​A1g​B1g​B2g​Eg​​​​
(30)
All five are even under inversion symmetry, hence the g suffixes. The pair (dxz​,dyz​) is mixed by a quarter-turn and spans a two-dimensional irreducible representation.
In the Td molecular point group, the corresponding result is
(dz2​,dx2−y2​)∼E,(dxy​,dxz​,dyz​)∼T2​.​
(31)
There is no inversion label because Td​ has no inversion centre. In both cases the dimensions add to five.
