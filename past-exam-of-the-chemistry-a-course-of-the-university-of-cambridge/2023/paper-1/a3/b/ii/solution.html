In the printed geometry, one B–F bond lies along the in-plane axis and contributes zero. The other two fluorines have perpendicular distance Rsin60∘=3​R/2; boron is again on the axis. Therefore
Ib​=2mF​(23​R​)2=23​mF​R2=2Ic​​.​
(25)
The other in-plane principal moment is the same by threefold symmetry. Hence Boron trifluoride is an oblate symmetric top with Ic​>Ib​ and C=B/2.
