The squared angular momentum operator and one selected component are commuting operators, so a complete set of common eigenfunctions can be chosen: for the z component these are the usual ∣j,m⟩ angular momentum eigenstates. Low rotational energy spacings can be excited in cold interstellar gas when electronic and most vibrational excitations are frozen out. A polar molecule such as carbon monosulfide has allowed electric dipole transition lines whose frequencies depend on its moment of inertia, providing a distinctive pattern for rotational spectral line assignment. The nitrogen-centered highest occupied molecular orbital of ammonia supplies a lone pair; planar borane has an empty out-of-plane p-derived lowest unoccupied molecular orbital. Approach along this acceptor axis gives constructive orbital overlap and donation of the nitrogen pair into the boron-centered acceptor. A symmetry orbital is a linear combination of atomic orbitals transforming as a component of an irreducible representation of the molecular point group. A molecular orbital is a one-electron wavefunction for the molecule, obtained as an eigenfunction of the chosen effective one-electron Hamiltonian operator. Symmetry organizes the eigenproblem, but a symmetry label alone does not determine its coefficients.

ii

Words: 74 Articles: 1
a OurBigBook.com
Solution
Words: 74
ii OurBigBook.com
A symmetry orbital is a molecular orbital when it is also an eigenfunction of the effective one-electron Hamiltonian operator. If an irreducible representation occurs once in a complete invariant model space, a symmetry-invariant Hamiltonian acts as a scalar on that copy by Schur's lemma. Its basis functions are then degenerate eigenfunctions. When the same symmetry occurs in several copies, those copies can mix; one must diagonalize the corresponding block. Merely having a one-dimensional symmetry label is insufficient if other orbitals have that same label.

This is a deliberately simplified model: unequal atomic energies or unequal overlaps require a generalized calculation.
