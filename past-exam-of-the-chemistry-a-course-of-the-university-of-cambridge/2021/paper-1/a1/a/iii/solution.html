Use the commutator derivation identity [A,BC]=[A,B]C+B[A,C] and the orbital angular momentum commutation relations. They give
[j^​z​,j^​x2​][j^​z​,j^​y2​][j^​z​,j^​z2​]​=iℏ(j^​y​j^​x​+j^​x​j^​y​),=−iℏ(j^​x​j^​y​+j^​y​j^​x​),=0.​
(4)
The first two expressions cancel on addition, so
[j^​z​,j^​2]=0.​
(5)
The same argument works for each component of the angular momentum operator.
