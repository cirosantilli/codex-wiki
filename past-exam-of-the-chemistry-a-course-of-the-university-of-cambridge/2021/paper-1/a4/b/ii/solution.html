For planar ammonia, the nitrogen valence atomic orbitals have symmetries 2s:A1′​, (2px​,2py​):E′ and 2pz​:A2′′​. The hydrogen symmetry orbitals span A1′​⊕E′. Consequently the in-plane orbitals form a bonding a1′​ orbital, a bonding e′ pair and their antibonding partners. The out-of-plane 2pz​ has no matching hydrogen combination and remains a nonbonding orbital. With the core omitted, the conventional valence occupation is
(2a1′​)2(1e′)4(1a2′′​)2.​
(37)
The preceding ammonia molecular orbital diagram shows the correlation. On pyramidalization, A1′​ and A2′′​ both correlate with A1​ of the C3v point group, permitting s–p orbital mixing. The pure planar lone pair can acquire bonding character, lowering its energy and helping stabilize the pyramid. Symmetry alone does not provide a numerical inversion barrier.[ref]
