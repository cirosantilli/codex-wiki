For an axis through the center of mass perpendicular to the bond, the point-mass moment of inertia is
I​=mC​r12​+mS​r22​=(mC​+mS​)2mC​mS2​+mS​mC2​​r2=mC​+mS​mC​mS​​r2​.​
(26)
The prefactor is the reduced mass μ; this is the standard reduction of a two-body linear rigid rotor to I=μr2.
