For kB​T≫δ, the Boltzmann factors of the orientations become almost equal, giving 3N nearly equiprobable microstates. The Boltzmann entropy of that distribution approaches
Smax​=NkB​ln3.​
(6)
The exact limiting value is reached only as T→∞ for a nonzero splitting. The useful crossover scale is T∼δ/kB​; one needs T≫δ/kB​ for a good saturation approximation. The binding offset ε is not an additional population scale here because all sites remain occupied and it shifts every orientation equally.
