Using dx/dT=−x/T in the internal energy gives
CV​=NkB​(2+e−x)22x2e−x​,x=kB​Tδ​.​
(7)
This is a Schottky anomaly with unequal degeneracies, with lower-level state degeneracy two and upper-level state degeneracy one. Its limiting forms are
CV​∼2NkB​​x2e−x(T→0),CV​∼92NkB​​x2(T→∞).​
(8)
It vanishes at low temperature because exciting the upper orientation is exponentially unlikely. It also vanishes at high temperature because the populations have nearly saturated and further heating hardly changes the internal energy. Between these limits it has one smooth maximum. The original plot shows its shape; numerical maximization gives kB​Tmax​/δ≃0.449 and Cmax​/(NkB​)≃0.241.
