Similarity ansatz (u(x,t)=tαF(x/tβ))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Similarity solution
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A similarity ansatz assumes a profile invariant under a prescribed rescaling, commonly in the displayed power-law form. Substitution into a partial differential equation fixes compatible exponents and can reduce it to an ordinary differential equation for F. Boundary conditions and conserved quantities must also be compatible with the scaling; the ansatz alone does not establish existence of a solution.
