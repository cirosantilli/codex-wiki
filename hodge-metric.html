Hodge metric

 ... Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Kähler manifold Kähler metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
A Hodge metric is a Kähler metric whose Kähler form represents an integral cohomology class in real de Rham cohomology. Equivalently, its periods over integral two-cycles are integers. Some conventions require the class of ω/(2π) to be integral instead; this changes the normalization but not existence.
