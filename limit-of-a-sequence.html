Limit of a sequence (limn→∞​xn​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Convergent sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 1
A real sequence (xn​) has limit of a sequence L∈R when for every ε>0 there is N such that ∣xn​−L∣<ε for all n≥N. Its limit of a sequence is unique. Extended-real convergence to −∞ means that every real upper bound eventually exceeds all the terms; convergence to +∞ is the corresponding lower-bound condition. The extended-real Fekete lemma is an example where the limit of a sequence need not be finite.
