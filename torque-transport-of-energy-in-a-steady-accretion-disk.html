Torque transport of energy in a steady accretion disk (Da,b​=Ba,b​+Ω(a)G(a)−Ω(b)G(b))

OurBigBook.com Words: 101
For a steady Keplerian accretion disk with inward accretion rate M˙ and vanishing inner torque, the outward angular-momentum torque is G=M˙(GMr​−GMrin​​). It transports energy outward at rate ΩG. The binding-energy acquisition rate between radii b and a is Ba,b​=GMM˙(1/a−1/b)/2. Integrating the vertical viscous dissipation and then the annulus gives
Da,b​=GMM˙[23​(a1​−b1​)−rin​​(a−3/2−b−3/2)].
(28)
Since ΩG=GMM˙[r−1−rin​​r−3/2], subtraction proves the displayed energy balance. Far from the inner edge Da,b​≃3Ba,b​, whereas the boundary torque powers vanish for the complete disk and D=B=GMM˙/(2rin​). Local dissipation is therefore not supplied solely by local changes in orbital binding energy.
