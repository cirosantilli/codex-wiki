Continuous bijection (f continuous and bijective)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological space Continuous map
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
A continuous bijection is a continuous map that is a bijection. It need not be a homeomorphism, since its inverse function need not be continuous. The compact-to-Hausdorff continuous bijection theorem supplies a useful sufficient condition for the stronger conclusion.
