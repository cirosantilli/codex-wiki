For a quantum state ensemble of pure vectors and an orthogonal projection P, measure {P,I−P}, preserve the successful subspace, and decode failure to a fixed normalized vector φ. The resulting quantum channel is Λ(X)=PXP+Tr[(I−P)X]∣φ⟩⟨φ∣. For αk​=∥Pψk​∥, its squared quantum fidelity on the kth source vector is αk4​+(1−αk2​)∣⟨ψk​∣φ⟩∣2. The inequality a2≥2a−1 on [0,1] proves the displayed average bound. It depends only on the source density operator's weight in P and does not require the emitted vectors to be orthogonal.
