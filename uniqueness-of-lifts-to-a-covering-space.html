Uniqueness of lifts to a covering space

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Covering space
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Let p:X→X be a covering map and let Y be path-connected. If two lifts f​1​,f​2​:Y→X of the same map f:Y→X agree at one point, they agree everywhere. Join that point to any other by a path and apply uniqueness in the path lifting theorem.
