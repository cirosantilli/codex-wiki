BABAR time-dependent CP violation experiment

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Standard Model fermion Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 388 Articles: 1
BABAR used asymmetric electron-Positron collisions near the Upsilon meson 4S resonance to prepare neutral B meson pairs. It reconstructed one decay into charmonium plus a kaon, and used flavour tagging on the other B meson with charged leptons or kaons. Silicon tracking reconstructed decay vertices for the decay-time difference. Self-tagging decays calibrated mistags and time resolution; charged decays provided a control. The tagged time distributions established CP violation, limited by backgrounds, mistags and vertex alignment.[ref]
The Lorentz boost converts vertex separation to Δt≃Δz/(βboost​γboost​c) at leading order. Unequal beam energies thus make a time difference measurable as a distance.[ref]
