Ellipse

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Conic section

An ellipse is the locus of points whose distances from two fixed foci have constant sum. If the focal distance is d and the sum is c>d, its semimajor and semiminor axes are c/2 and c2−d2​/2.
