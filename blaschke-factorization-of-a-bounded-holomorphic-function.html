Blaschke factorization of a bounded holomorphic function (f=Bg,∥g∥∞​≤∥f∥∞​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Holomorphic function Blaschke product
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
Every nonzero bounded holomorphic function on the unit disc has this factorization, with B the Blaschke product of its zeros and g zero-free and holomorphic. The Blaschke condition follows from Jensen's formula. Dividing by any finite partial zero product gives a holomorphic function; the maximum modulus principle and the finite product's boundary modulus one bound that quotient by ∥f∥∞​. Locally uniform convergence of the partial products gives the same bound for g, with removable extensions at their zeros. This factorization alone does not make g constant or a full inner-outer factorization.
