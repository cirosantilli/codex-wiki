Grain size

 ... Thermodynamics State of matter Solid Crystal Polycrystalline material Grain in a polycrystal
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A measure of the spatial scale of crystal grains. Its definition must be stated for elongated crystal grains; size, aspect ratio and orientation distribution have distinct physical effects.
