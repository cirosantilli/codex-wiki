Feeling Safe Programme

 Home Science Psychology Clinical psychology Cognitive behavioral therapy Cognitive behavioral therapy for psychosis
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
A personalised intervention for persistent persecutory delusions, combining modules selected to address relevant maintaining processes. Its active control trial compared it with same-therapist befriending alongside usual care. The continuous conviction result and categorical recovery comparison require separate interpretation.[ref]
