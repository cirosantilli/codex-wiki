Scale-dependent halo bias from local non-Gaussianity (Δb(k)=2fNL​δc​bL​/α(k))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Dark matter halo Peak-background split
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
For a universal abundance depending on halo peak height, the peak-background split gives a potential-response coefficient bφ​=2fNL​δc​bL​, with bL​ the linear Lagrangian halo bias. If δ=α(k)φ, the corresponding deterministic galaxy bias correction is Δb=bφ​/α(k), proportional to k−2 on sufficiently large scales. For a physical Eulerian tracer, bE​=1+bL​, so bφ​=2fNL​δc​(bE​−1). The deterministic tracer power spectrum is (bE​+Δb)2Pm​; it remains nonnegative even if a negative correction changes the sign of the tracer-matter correlation.
