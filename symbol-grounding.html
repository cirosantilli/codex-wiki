Symbol grounding

 Home Computer science Artificial intelligence
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The problem of connecting internal symbols to what they designate in perception, action or other meaningful relations. Supplying an intelligent agent with perfectly identified objects and relations can conceal this problem rather than solve it.
