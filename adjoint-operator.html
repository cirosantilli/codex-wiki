Adjoint operator

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Hilbert space Riesz representation theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 48
For T∈L(H), the adjoint T∗ is the unique bounded operator satisfying
⟨Tx,y⟩=⟨x,T∗y⟩.
(5)
Riesz representation applied for each y proves existence; in an orthonormal basis its matrix is the conjugate transpose of the matrix of T.
