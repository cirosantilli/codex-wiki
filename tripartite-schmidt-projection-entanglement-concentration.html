For p​∣000⟩+1−p​∣111⟩, a projective measurement of the number of ones in one party's n local qubits gives a common type class of size dk​=(kn​). The conditional pure state is dk−1/2​∑∣x∣=k​∣x,x,x⟩. It can be converted exactly and deterministically to a uniform common-label state of any dimension m≤dk​: one party measures an m-element subset S with Kraus operator ΠS​/(m−1dk​−1​)​, and all parties relabel their common retained labels after receiving S. Choosing m=2f gives f independent GHZ states. Stirling's formula and the weak law of large numbers give f/n→h2​(p) in probability. Measuring every local bit separately would instead destroy the coherence and leave a product state.
