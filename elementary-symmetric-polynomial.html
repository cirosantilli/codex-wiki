Elementary symmetric polynomial (ek​(x1​,…,xn​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 910 Articles: 6
The kth elementary symmetric polynomial adds every product obtained by choosing k distinct positions from x1​,…,xn​:
ek​(x1​,…,xn​)=∑1≤i1​<⋯<ik​≤n​xi1​​⋯xik​​.
(15)
The conventions are e0​=1 and ek​=0 for k>n. The positions must be distinct, but their values may coincide. Each permutation of the variables merely rearranges the summands, so this is a symmetric polynomial.
For x1​=1, x2​=2, x3​=3, we obtain e1​=1+2+3=6, e2​=1⋅2+1⋅3+2⋅3=11, and e3​=1⋅2⋅3=6. Expanding the product gives
(z−1)(z−2)(z−3)=z3−6z2+11z−6​.
(16)
In general, ∏i=1n​(z−xi​)=zn−e1​zn−1+e2​zn−2−⋯+(−1)nen​. Thus the coefficients of a monic polynomial collect elementary symmetric polynomials of its roots of a polynomial, with alternating signs. These are the Vieta formulas.[ref]
