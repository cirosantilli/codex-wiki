Excess of loss reinsurance (g(x)=min(x,M))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Reinsurance
OurBigBook.com Words: 413 Articles: 7
Per-claim reinsurance under which the insurer retains each claim up to a level M and the reinsurer pays its positive part (x−M)+​. The annual retained amount is a sum of capped claims, so it is generally different from the cap of the annual sum in aggregate stop loss reinsurance.
