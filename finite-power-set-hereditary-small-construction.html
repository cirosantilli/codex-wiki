Finite-power-set hereditary-small construction

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cumulative hierarchy Transitive closure
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
Let z0​=ω and zn+1​=P(zn​). Call x small when it injects into some zn​, and let HS contain exactly the sets all of whose hereditary members are small. Then every zn​ and the set y={zn​:n∈ω} belong to HS, but ⋃y does not. Consequently (HS,∈) fails the Axiom of union.
