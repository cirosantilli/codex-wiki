Tangent space (Tp​M)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Tangent vector
OurBigBook.com Words: 352 Articles: 7
The tangent space Tp​M is the vector space of tangent vectors to a smooth manifold M at p. For an embedded manifold M⊆RN, it is
Tp​M={γ′(0):γ:(−ε,ε)→M is smooth and γ(0)=p}.
(26)
For a regular parametrized surface X(u,v), it is spanned by Xu​ and Xv​.
