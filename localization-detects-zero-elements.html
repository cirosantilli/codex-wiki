Localization detects zero elements

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Localization of a ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
An element a of a commutative ring R is zero if and only if its image in every localization at a prime ideal is zero. If a=0, its proper annihilator lies in a maximal ideal m. No element outside m can annihilate a, so a/1=0 in Rm​. This also applies to elements of an R-module using localization of a module.
