Infimal convolution (E□F)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Convex analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 3
The infimal convolution is (E□F)(u)=infv​{E(v)+F(u−v)}. It optimizes a decomposition of u between two costs. It is convex when both costs are convex functions, and its convex conjugate is E∗+F∗ whenever the extended-real operations are well defined.
