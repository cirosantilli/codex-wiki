Dipolar perturbations of a critically rotating cylinder (uR​=a(ω+kR),W=iaR[(kR)2−ω2])

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Critically rotating self-gravitating cylinder
OurBigBook.com Words: 100
The regular m=1 velocity family satisfies the displayed formulas. The surface pressure condition and gravitational matching give Ws​=−Ωs2​R0​η, while the kinematic condition is i(ω+Ωs​)η=uR​(R0​). If the material radial displacement uR​/[i(ω+kR)] is continued regularly to the boundary, then η=−ia and ω2=0: translation of an isolated cylinder has no restoring force. Using only the Eulerian velocity conditions instead leaves ω2(ω+Ωs​)=0. The additional surface-corotation case has η=0, Φ′=0, uR​=a(kR−Ωs​) and W=iaR[(kR)2−Ωs2​]; it satisfies the velocity equations and zero surface pressure. Excluding that degenerate branch is an extra admissibility assumption, not a valid division by a nonzero frequency at every surface.
