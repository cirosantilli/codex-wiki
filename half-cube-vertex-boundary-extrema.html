Half-cube vertex-boundary extrema (min∣∂A∣=(⌊n/2⌋n​),max∣∂A∣=2n−1)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Analysis of Boolean functions Boolean hypercube Vertex-isoperimetric inequality in the discrete cube
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For n≥1, a family of 2n−1 vertices in the Boolean hypercube has these minimum and maximum external vertex boundary sizes. The Harper theorem gives the minimum: in odd dimension take the lower half of the levels, and in even dimension add the middle-level sets containing coordinate one to all lower levels. The family of even Hamming weight attains the maximum, since every remaining vertex has an adjacent even-weight vertex.
