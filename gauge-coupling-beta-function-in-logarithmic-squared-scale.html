Gauge-coupling beta function in logarithmic squared scale (μdg/dμ=cg3+O(g5)⟹dα/dlnμ2=4πcα2+O(α3))

For a four-dimensional dimensionless gauge coupling, the one-loop correction is cubic in g. With α=g2/(4π), the chain rule and d/dlnμ2=(1/2)d/dlnμ give the displayed conversion. In QCD, c=−(11N−2nf​)/(48π2), hence dαs​/dlnμ2=−(11N−2nf​)αs2​/(12π)+O(αs3​). Other types of coupling or several interacting couplings can have different leading expansions; the squared-scale convention must not be mixed with the unsquared one.
