Radiative temperature gradient (∇rad​)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Irradiated planetary atmosphere Radiative-convective boundary
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The radiative temperature gradient is the logarithmic pressure gradient ∇rad​=dlogT/dlogP required to carry the flux by radiation. Convection begins when it exceeds the relevant adiabatic gradient.
