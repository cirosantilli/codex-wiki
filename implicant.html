Implicant

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Analysis of Boolean functions Boolean hypercube Boolean function
OurBigBook.com Words: 73 Articles: 1
A logical conjunction T of literals is an implicant of a Boolean function f if every Boolean valuation making T true also makes f true. Every term in a disjunctive normal form is an implicant of the represented Boolean function. To rule out a one-literal term, it suffices to find a false Boolean valuation of f where that literal is true.
