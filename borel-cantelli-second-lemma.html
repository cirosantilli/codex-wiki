If the events An​ are mutually independent events and ∑n=1∞​P(An​)=∞, then P(limsupn​An​)=1. For an infinite family, mutual independence means every finite subfamily satisfies the factorization defining independent events.[ref]
Proof outline: for M≥m, independent events and 1−x≤e−x give
P(⋂n=mM​Anc​)=∏n=mM​(1−P(An​))≤exp(−∑n=mM​P(An​))⟶0.

Continuity of probability makes each infinite tail union have probability one; their countable intersection has probability one as well.
