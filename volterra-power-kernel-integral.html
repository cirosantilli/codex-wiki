Volterra power-kernel integral ((Vβ​f)(x)=∫0x​(x−y)β−1f(y)dy,β>0)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Integral operator Volterra operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 221 Articles: 1
For nonnegative Lebesgue measurable f on (0,1), the displayed Volterra power-kernel integral is defined for every 0<x<1 as a nonnegative extended Lebesgue integral. The factor is finite and continuous on the open integration interval (0,x), and an endpoint value can be assigned arbitrarily. The result may initially be infinite; for example f(y)=1/y makes every value infinite. At β=1, it reduces to the ordinary Volterra operator.
For joint integration, use the integral kernel (x−y)β−1 on 0<y<x<1 and zero elsewhere. This piecewise prescription avoids the singular diagonal and never raises a negative number to a noninteger power. The L1 bound for a Volterra power-kernel integral gives an integrable output, finite almost everywhere, whenever f is integrable.
