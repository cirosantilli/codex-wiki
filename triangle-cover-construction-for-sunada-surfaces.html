Triangle cover construction for Sunada surfaces (χ(M)=∣T∣(p−1+q−1+r−1−1))

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Spectral geometry Isospectral manifolds Sunada theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 1
Let a finite group T be generated by elements of exact orders p,q whose product has exact order r, with p−1+q−1+r−1<1. The epimorphism from the hyperbolic triangle group sending its generators to these elements has torsion-free kernel. Its quotient of the hyperbolic plane is a closed surface with a T action. A subgroup acts freely precisely when it meets no conjugate of a nonidentity power of the three elliptic generators. Free almost conjugate subgroups give isospectral manifolds by the Sunada theorem.
