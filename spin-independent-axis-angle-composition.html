Spin-independent axis-angle composition (D(S)(g1​g2​)=D(S)(g1​)D(S)(g2​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The axis and angle of a product of rotations are properties of the group elements, independent of which spin representation acts on a state. Higher-spin generators do not obey the two-term Pauli exponential, but representations preserve the resulting rotation product. The central 2π rotation acts as (−1)2SI.
