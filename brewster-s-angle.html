Brewster's angle (tanθB​=n2​/n1​)

 ... Electromagnetism Electromagnetic wave Plane electromagnetic wave Interface conditions for electromagnetic fields Dielectric-interface boundary conditions Fresnel equations
OurBigBook.com Words: 309 Articles: 4
For an interface between two lossless, isotropic, nonmagnetic media with unequal refractive index values n1​,n2​, Brewster's angle is the incidence angle at which the reflected amplitude in transverse magnetic polarization vanishes. The Fresnel equations and Snell's law give tanθB​=n2​/n1​; the reflected and transmitted rays are perpendicular there. The usual formula assumes equal magnetic permeabilities.
