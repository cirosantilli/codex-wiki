Finite-time Friedmann singularity under the strong energy condition

 ... Physics Branch of physics Cosmology Friedmann equation Friedmann acceleration equation Strong energy condition in a Friedmann universe
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
If H>0 at one time, the inequality d(H−1)/dt≥1 forces H→+∞ and a→0 within finite time to the past. If H<0, it forces H→−∞ and a→0 within finite time to the future.
