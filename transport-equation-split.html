Transport equation (ut​+b⋅∇u=0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 7k Articles: 103
A transport equation carries a quantity along the flow of a velocity field b. For a smooth solution of ut​+b⋅∇u=0, the value of u is constant along each characteristic curve. With constant speed c, the chain rule gives dtd​u(x0​+ct,t)=ut​+cux​=0, hence u(x,t)=g(x−ct). This is exact translation: it preserves the shape rather than smoothing it, as the periodic heat and transport comparison shows.
