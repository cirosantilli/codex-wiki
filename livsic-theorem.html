Livsic theorem (Fu=h)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Smooth flow Anosov flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For a topologically transitive Anosov flow, a Hölder function has a Hölder flow coboundary precisely when its integral around every periodic orbit vanishes. The coboundary identity is u(ϕt​x)−u(x)=∫0t​h(ϕs​x)ds. For smooth flow and smooth h, the smooth regularity theorem gives smooth u. Necessity follows by setting t equal to a period. The solution is unique up to a constant by density of an orbit.
