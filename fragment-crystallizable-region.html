The Fc fragment comprises paired immunoglobulin heavy chain CH2​,CH3​ portions, interacts with immune partners and lacks paratopes. Its name records historical crystallization.[ref]
For example, Fc fragments can bind components of the complement system, illustrating a function distinct from epitope recognition.[ref]
An isolated Fc fragment therefore separates one set of protein interactions from target recognition. In the connectivity example under Papain digestion of IgG, the Fc fragment contributes one connected product but zero antibody binding sites. Retaining it preserves different interaction surfaces; it does not restore either severed arm. Conversely, the two Fab fragments preserve the stipulated epitope-recognition sites while losing their connection to this common stem.
