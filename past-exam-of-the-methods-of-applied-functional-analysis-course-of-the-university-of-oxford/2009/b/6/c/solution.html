For the log-cosh obstacle variational inequality, the admissible set is convex: for any v∈M, u+t(v−u) remains above the obstacle and in H01​ for 0≤t≤1. Minimality and the right derivative at zero therefore give
∫Ω​∇u⋅∇(v−u)+tanh(u)(v−u)dx≥0(v∈M).​
(34)
The one-sided sign comes from admissibility, which permits motion toward every v∈M but does not permit an arbitrary negative variation at a contact point.
Now fix ϕ∈Cc∞​(D). Smoothness on D and continuity of the obstacle give a strictly positive minimum of u−g on the compact support of ϕ. Hence u+tϕ is admissible for both signs of sufficiently small t. Applying the inequality to both variations yields
∫D​∇u⋅∇ϕ+tanh(u)ϕdx=0.
(35)
Integration by parts gives −Δu+tanhu=0 distributionally on D, and smoothness makes it pointwise. Thus
−Δu+tanhu=0where u>g.​
(36)
On the contact set the variational inequality, rather than unrestricted stationarity, remains the governing statement. Convexity of the energy also makes the inequality sufficient for minimization; strict monotonicity implies at most one minimizer, although existence was already stipulated in this part.
