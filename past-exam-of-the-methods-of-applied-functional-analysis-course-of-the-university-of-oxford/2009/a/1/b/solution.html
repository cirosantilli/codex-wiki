Weak convergence uj​⇀u means F(uj​)→F(u) for every F∈X∗. Weak-star convergence Tj​⇀∗T means Tj​(x)→T(x) for every x∈X, viewing X as the predual. The Sequential Banach-Alaoglu theorem for a separable predual says that every norm-bounded sequence in X∗ has a weak-star convergent subsequence. Separability is what turns the bounded weak-star compactness statement into this sequential form.
For the weak-star limit of periodic rescalings, apply it with X=L1(R), which is separable, and X∗=L∞(R). The rescaled functions have uniform essential-supremum bound ∥θ∥∞​.
