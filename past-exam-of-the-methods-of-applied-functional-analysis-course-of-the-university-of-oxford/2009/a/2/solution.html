To verify lower semicontinuity directly, put wj​=vj​−u. Boundedness of B makes B[u,wj​]→0 under weak convergence, and symmetry gives
B[vj​,vj​]=B[u,u]+2B[u,wj​]+B[wj​,wj​]≥B[u,u]+o(1).

Also F(vj​)→F(u). Therefore I(u)≤liminfI(vj​), so the absolute minimum is attained.
For every h∈H1 the real function t↦I(u+th) has a minimum at zero. Its derivative there is B[u,h]−F(h), yielding the required weak equation for every h. If two weak solutions exist, their difference z satisfies B[z,h]=0 for all h; choose h=z and use coercivity to obtain z=0. In fact the weak equation also gives the exact energy identity
I(v)−I(u)=21​B[v−u,v−u]≥2κ​∥v−u∥H12​.​
(10)
Thus the minimizer and weak solution are both unique. The natural boundary condition is encoded by testing in the whole H1 space, without needing pointwise conormal derivatives for rough coefficients.
