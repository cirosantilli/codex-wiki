An integrable example on the whole real line is the tent function
u(x)=(1−∣x∣)+​,Du(x)=1(−1,0)​(x)−1(0,1)​(x).​
(10)
It is not differentiable at −1,0,1. For a test function φ, split the integral into (−1,0) and (0,1) and use integration by parts. The endpoint terms at −1,1 vanish because u=0, and those at zero cancel because u is continuous. The result is ∫uφ′=−∫(Du)φ. Thus the corners obstruct classical differentiability but not this weak derivative.
