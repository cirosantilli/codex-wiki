Quarter-wave impedance transformer

 ... Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Electrical impedance Impedance matching
OurBigBook.com Words: 110
A lossless intermediate section of positive real characteristic impedance ZM​ matches unequal real exterior characteristic impedances ZL​,ZR​ when ZM2​=ZL​ZR​ and its propagation wave phase is an odd multiple of π/2. The length is an odd quarter of the internal wavelength. This principle also applies to the characteristic impedance of a stretched string.
For a uniform intermediate string of length l, put δ=kM​l. Continuity of displacement and transverse force gives the displacement reflection coefficient
r=(ZL​+ZR​)cosδ−i(ZM​+ZL​ZR​/ZM​)sinδ(ZL​−ZR​)cosδ+i(ZM​−ZL​ZR​/ZM​)sinδ​.
(34)
At real positive mechanical impedances, the denominator never vanishes. Its numerator vanishes only if (ZL​−ZR​)cosδ=0 and (ZM2​−ZL​ZR​)sinδ=0. Thus unequal exterior strings require the quarter-wave condition. Equal exterior strings with a different middle string instead transmit perfectly at integer half-wavelengths; if all three mechanical impedances agree, every frequency is matched.
