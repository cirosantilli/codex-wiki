Postnikov invariant (kn​)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Homotopy group Postnikov tower Principal Eilenberg–MacLane fibration
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The nth Postnikov invariant is the cohomology class kn​∈Hn+1(Pn−1​X;πn​X) whose representing map has homotopy fibre Pn​X. It specifies how the new homotopy group is attached to the previous stage. A zero invariant gives the product Pn​X≃Pn−1​X×K(πn​X,n). Merely tensoring homotopy groups with Q does not describe rationalization of a topological space unless these classes are also transported.
