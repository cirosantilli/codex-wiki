Stokes' paradox

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
The unbounded two-dimensional Stokes flow past a no-slip circular cylinder has no solution approaching a nonzero uniform stream. With radius and speed scaled to one, the forced angular mode has stream function ψ=f(r)sinθ. The biharmonic equation gives f=Ar3+Brlogr+Cr+D/r. Uniform flow at infinity forces A=B=0, C=1; impermeability forces D=−1, but then f′(1)=2 violates no slip. The failure belongs to a nonuniform small-Reynolds number limit. In a distant outer region inertia must be retained through an Oseen approximation or the full Navier-Stokes equations, matched to an inner Stokes region. A finite outer wall changes the problem and removes this particular infinity obstruction.
