Point vortex in a wedge

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Vorticity Vortex (fluid mechanics) Line vortex
OurBigBook.com Words: 98
For a point vortex of circulation Γ in the wedge 0<θ<π/n, with positive integer n, the conformal map z↦zn sends the domain to the upper half-plane. An opposite image vortex gives the complex potential
w(z)=−2πiΓ​logzn−c,nzn−cn​.
(4)
Remove the singular self-induced velocity before setting z=c. The resulting complex velocity is
dtdc​=2πiΓ​(−2cn−1​+cn−c,nncn−1​).
(5)
The finite first term follows from the expansion of (zn−cn)/(z−c) at c. In polar coordinates this becomes r˙=nΓcot(nθ)/(4πr) and θ˙=−Γ/(4πr2). Therefore rsin(nθ) is constant along a vortex trajectory. The case n=1 is motion parallel to a plane wall, while n=3 describes a sixty-degree wedge.
