Electric force (FE​=qE)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric field
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 1
The electric force on an electric charge q is FE​=qE, using the electric field of the other sources and excluding the charge's own self-field. Positive electric charge gives force along the electric field; negative electric charge reverses it. A field E=400N/Cx therefore gives FE​=−8×10−7Nx on q=−2nC. This is the electric part of the Lorentz force; the magnetic contribution is qv×B. [ref].
