Perfect quantum state transfer (e−iHt∗​∣a⟩=eiχ∣b⟩)

 ... Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state Observable Hamiltonian (quantum mechanics) Unitary time evolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
A Hamiltonian operator has perfect transfer between basis states ∣a⟩,∣b⟩ at time t∗​ if e−iHt∗​∣a⟩=eiχ∣b⟩ for a known global phase χ. In an excitation-conserving qubit network whose vacuum is fixed, the initial product state with α∣0⟩+β∣1⟩ at site a and zeros elsewhere evolves to α∣vac⟩+βeiχ∣b⟩. A local phase gate at b removing χ restores the unknown qubit there. This explains why transfer of the single-excitation basis state suffices for qubit transfer when the vacuum phase is also accounted for.
