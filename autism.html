Autism

 Home Biology Medicine Psychiatry Mental disorder Neurodevelopmental disorder
OurBigBook.com Words: 128 Articles: 3
A heterogeneous neurodevelopmental condition involving characteristic differences in social communication and restricted or repetitive patterns of behaviour or interests. Sensory differences and varying support needs matter; an autistic individual’s empathy, language or mental health cannot be inferred from the label alone.
