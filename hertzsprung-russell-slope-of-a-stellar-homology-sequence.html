Hertzsprung-Russell slope of a stellar homology sequence (dlogL/dlogTeff​=4Y/(Y−2X))

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure Stellar homology Homology relations for radiative stars
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
For a stellar homology sequence with R∝MX and L∝MY, the Stefan–Boltzmann law gives Teff​∝M(Y−2X)/4. Eliminating mass gives the displayed Hertzsprung-Russell diagram slope when Y=2X. The usual horizontal axis decreases in temperature toward the right, reversing the visual left-to-right sign. The relation is between logarithmic variables and depends on the specified structural assumptions.
