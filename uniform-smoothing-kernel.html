Uniform smoothing kernel (K(u)=21​1{∣u∣≤1}​)

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical inference Nonparametric statistics Nonparametric regression Local polynomial regression Kernel for nonparametric regression
OurBigBook.com Words: 67 Articles: 1
The uniform smoothing kernel assigns equal weight inside the window [x−h,x+h] and zero weight outside. Its Nadaraya–Watson estimator is the average of observations in that window.
