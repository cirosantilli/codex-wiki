Additivity of Holevo capacity (χ∗(Λ⊗Γ)=χ∗(Λ)+χ∗(Γ))

 ... Quantum theory Quantum information theory Positive linear map Completely positive map Quantum channel Holevo capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 97 Articles: 1
A channel's Holevo capacity is additive with another channel when their joint capacity equals the sum of the individual capacities. If this holds with every other channel, the channel's tensor powers have capacity nχ∗(Λ) and regularization does not increase its unassisted classical capacity of a quantum channel. Additivity is a special property, not a general rule for quantum channels.
