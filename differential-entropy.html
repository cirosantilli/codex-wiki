Differential entropy (h(q)=−∫q(x)logq(x)dx)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Information entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 198 Articles: 4
The differential entropy of a probability density function is −Eq​[logq(X)] when this expected value is defined. Unlike discrete information entropy, it can be negative and depends on the coordinates and reference measure. It is the entropy term in the evidence lower bound for continuous variational inference.
