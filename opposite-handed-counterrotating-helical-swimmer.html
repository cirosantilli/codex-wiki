Opposite-handed counterrotating helical swimmer (U=−AD(1+n)2−B2(1−n)22nBDω​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Microswimmer
OurBigBook.com Words: 168 Articles: 3
Two coaxial opposite-handed helices linked by an internal motor share a translation speed but counterrotate. In an additive local-drag model, a length ratio n multiplies the second helix's diagonal resistances by n and reverses its coupling. With Ω1​−Ω2​=ω, force and torque balance give U=−2nBDω/[AD(1+n)2−B2(1−n)2].
