Longitudinal gauge propagator contraction (paGab​=ξpb​/p2)

 ... Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing Covariant gauge Positive-sign covariant gauge-fixing inverse
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For the positive-sign covariant gauge-fixing inverse, paGab​=ξpb​/p2. It is not identically zero for fixed ξ=0 and is a nonzero vector at a nonzero non-null four-momentum. Null momenta require the chosen pole prescription, and individual zero components are possible. The covariant Landau gauge limit suppresses this longitudinal part. The Ward identity removes longitudinal contributions from physical conserved-current amplitudes.
