Quadratic bosonic Hamiltonian (H=a†Aa+21​(aTBa+h.c.))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Canonical transformation of mode operators Bogoliubov transformation Bosonic Bogoliubov diagonalization
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A quadratic bosonic Hamiltonian contains terms of degree two in bosonic creation operators and bosonic annihilation operators, with an optional constant. The number-conserving matrix A is Hermitian and the pair matrix B may be taken symmetric. Positive stable forms can be diagonalized by a canonical Bogoliubov transformation; the strict stability condition matters at zero modes.
