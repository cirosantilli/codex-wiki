Gluino (Ga)

OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The gluino is the spin-one-half sparticle paired with a gluon. In the MSSM, the eight adjoint color components form a Majorana spinor in (8,1,0) under SU(3)c​×SU(2)L​×U(1)Y​. Its gauge representation is real, so the opposite-helicity antiparticle states are already included in the adjoint multiplet.
