Free-draining polymer friction (ζN​=Nζ0​)

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymer physics Rouse model
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
With additive drag from N identical monomers, ζN​=Nζ0​ and centre-of-mass diffusion is DN​=kB​T/(Nζ0​). For monomer Stokes law ζ0​=6πηa, this gives DN​∝N−1. The model neglects long-range hydrodynamic coupling and does not alone describe the long-time spatial diffusion of a strongly entangled chain.
