Cyclotomic character of a finite cyclotomic extension (χm​:Gal(Q(ζm​)/Q)≃(Z/mZ)×)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Galois theory Cyclotomic polynomial Cyclotomic field
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
For a primitive mth root of unity ζm​, every Galois automorphism sends ζm​ to a unique ζma​ with a invertible modulo m. The assignment σ↦a is a group homomorphism, since composition multiplies the exponents, and is injective because ζm​ generates the cyclotomic field. The irreducibility of cyclotomic polynomials gives degree φ(m), equal to the order of the target, so the map is an isomorphism. The cyclotomic character on an absolute Galois group factors through this finite quotient and generally has a nontrivial kernel.
