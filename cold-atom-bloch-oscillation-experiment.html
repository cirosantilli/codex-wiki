Cold-atom Bloch oscillation experiment

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bloch's theorem Bloch oscillation
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
In a 1996 experiment, ultracold cesium atoms were loaded into the lowest band of an optical standing wave made with 852nm light. Chirping the frequency difference of its counterpropagating beams accelerated the lattice, producing a constant inertial force in its frame. Switching off the lattice after different evolution times allowed momentum-distribution measurements. The observed oscillations tested the periodic band velocity underlying a Bloch oscillation; the lattice period and imposed acceleration controlled the oscillation frequency.[ref]
