Thermal exponent from a discrete renormalization map (ν=logb/logλt​)

 ... Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Renormalization group Renormalization-group relevance Relevant operator Relevant direction of a fixed point
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A discrete renormalization group with length factor b>1 and positive relevant thermal multiplier λt​>1 sends t to λt​t and the correlation length to ξ/b. Combining this with ξ∼∣t∣−ν gives the displayed correlation-length critical exponent. The corresponding scaling dimension is yt​=logλt​/logb=1/ν. Relevance of discrete multipliers is determined by magnitude relative to one, unlike the sign criterion for continuous-flow eigenvalues.
