Nonlinear optical coherence length (ℓc​=π/∣Δk∣)

 ... Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Interference (wave propagation) Phase matching Phase mismatch
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The distance over which a constant phase mismatch accumulates phase difference π. In unseeded, undepleted sum-frequency generation, the generated intensity first reaches a maximum at this length and returns to zero at 2ℓc​. This length concerns relative phases of interacting waves, rather than the temporal optical coherence length of one source.
