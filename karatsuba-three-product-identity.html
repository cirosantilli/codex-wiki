Karatsuba three-product identity (xy=acB2m+((a+b)(c+d)−ac−bd)Bm+bd)

 Home Computer science Algorithm Karatsuba multiplication
OurBigBook.com Words: 94
Compute exactly three smaller products,
p2​=ac,p0​=bd,ps​=(a+b)(c+d).
(68)
The distributive property gives ps​=ac+ad+bc+bd, so the cross coefficient is h=ps​−p2​−p0​=ad+bc. Recombine by
xy=p2​q2+hq+p0​,q=Bm.
(69)
The saving is that ad and bc are never separately multiplied. Multiplying a digit block by q or q2 shifts its position in positional notation; it does not require another general integer multiplication. Addition of the shifted results still requires Karatsuba carry handling.
