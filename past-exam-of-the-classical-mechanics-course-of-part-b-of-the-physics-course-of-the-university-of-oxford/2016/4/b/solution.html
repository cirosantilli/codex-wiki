St​+2m1​(Sx2​+Sy2​+Sz2​)+mgz=0.​
(34)
Because x,y and time are cyclic in the Hamiltonian, use separation S=ax+by−Et+W(z). The remaining equation is
(W′)2=2m(E−mgz)−a2−b2≡Q(z).

S=ax+by−Et−3m2gs​[2m(E−mgz)−a2−b2]3/2,s=±1.​

It is real in the classically allowed region Q≥0, and pz​=sQ​. At a turning point one matches the ascending and descending branches. If g=0, the integral is simply W=pz​z with constant pz​.
A convenient equivalent complete integral, expressed using initial-momentum constants α, is globally polynomial in time:
S=​αx​x+αy​y+(αz​−mgt)z−2mαx2​+αy2​+αz2​​t+2αz​g​t2−6mg2​t3.​​
