Lichnerowicz spinor-square formula (∇2=∇2−R/4)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Dirac field Dirac equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 273 Articles: 4
With {γμ,γν}=2gμν and spin curvature [∇μ​,∇ν​]=Rμνab​γab/4, the Dirac equation operator squares to the rough Laplacian minus R/4. In the curvature contraction the four-gamma term vanishes by the first Bianchi identity, the two-gamma terms vanish by Ricci symmetry, and the scalar term is −2R. In Riemannian signature i∇ is the self-adjoint convention and its square is −∇2+R/4.
