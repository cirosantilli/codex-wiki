Arbitrarily slow Fourier coefficient decay (limsupn→∞​k(n)∣g​(n)∣=∞)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Riemann-Lebesgue lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
Given any positive k(n)→∞, choose increasing integers nj​ with k(nj​)≥j2j. The absolutely uniformly convergent series g(t)=∑j​2−jeinj​t is continuous, and its Fourier coefficient at nj​ is exactly 2−j. The displayed unbounded limsup follows, although every individual continuous function still has coefficients tending to zero by the Riemann-Lebesgue lemma.
