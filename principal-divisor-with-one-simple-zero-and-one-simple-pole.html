Principal divisor with one simple zero and one simple pole

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Divisor on an algebraic curve Principal divisor on an algebraic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
If distinct points p,q on a smooth projective curve satisfy (f)=p−q, then f:C→P1 is a finite morphism of degree one. Such a morphism between smooth projective curves is an isomorphism, so C has genus zero. Hence no divisor p−q on a curve of positive genus is principal.
