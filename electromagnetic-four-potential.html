Electromagnetic four-potential

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 34
The four-potential combines the electric potential and magnetic vector potential as Aμ=(ϕ/c,A). The electromagnetic four-potential Aμ​ transforms under
Aμ​↦Aμ​+∂μ​χ,
(66)
while the antisymmetric field tensor Fμν​=∂μ​Aν​−∂ν​Aμ​ is unchanged.
