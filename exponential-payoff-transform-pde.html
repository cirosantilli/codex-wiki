For log-price dynamics dX=−σ2dt/2+σdWX and volatility dynamics dσ=A(σ)dt+B(σ)dWσ, with correlation ρ, the exponential payoff ansatz U=eθXV reduces the backward partial differential equation to the displayed one-dimensional equation. The terminal value is V(T,σ)=1. Correlation changes the volatility drift, while the log-price drift supplies the negative θ term.
