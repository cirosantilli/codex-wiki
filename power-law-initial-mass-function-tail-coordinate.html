Power-law initial-mass-function tail coordinate (X(M)=(Mmin​/M)p−1)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Star formation Initial mass function
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
For an initial mass function proportional to M−p on M≥Mmin​ with p>1, integration and normalization give the displayed upper-tail fraction. The probability integral transform makes X uniform on [0,1] for a randomly sampled birth mass. Evolutionary lifetime regions can then be measured as ordinary areas when the independent age coordinate is also uniform.
