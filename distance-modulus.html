Distance modulus (μ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astronomical magnitude
OurBigBook.com Words: 15
The distance modulus is the difference between apparent magnitude and absolute magnitude. For distance d in parsecs,
μ=m−M=5log10​(d/10pc).
