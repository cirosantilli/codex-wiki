Keller--Segel aggregation threshold (D1​f0​>D2​(κ−a0​f0′​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Chemotaxis Keller--Segel model
OurBigBook.com Words: 171 Articles: 1
For a homogeneous Keller--Segel model with nonnegative chemical production, linearized cell diffusion D2​>0, attraction coefficient D1​>0, and chemical diffusion Dρ​>0, put c=κ−a0​f0′​ and κ=(ρk(ρ))′∣ρ0​​. A Fourier mode with squared wavenumber z has determinant z[D2​(c+Dρ​z)−D1​f0​]. If c≥0, a growing mode on the infinite plane exists exactly when D1​f0​>D2​c, and 0<z<(D1​f0​−D2​c)/(D2​Dρ​) is the unstable band. If c<0, the homogeneous chemical mode is already unstable. Dividing the threshold by D2​κ to obtain a sum of two ratios is valid only when κ>0. Positivity of k does not guarantee positivity of (ρk)′.
