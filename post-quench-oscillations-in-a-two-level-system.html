Post-quench oscillations in a two-level system (P0​(t)=1−sin2θsin2(Δf​t/(2ℏ)))

After the quantum quench, hold Hf​ fixed and evolve without an intervening energy measurement. The unitary time evolution is
∣ψ(t)⟩=ce+iΔf​t/(2ℏ)∣gf​⟩−se−iΔf​t/(2ℏ)∣ef​⟩.
(14)
Projection onto the original ∣0⟩ gives probability amplitude c2e+iΔf​t/(2ℏ)+s2e−iΔf​t/(2ℏ). Squaring its modulus yields the two-level quantum return probability
P0​(t)=1−sin2θsin2(2ℏΔf​t​),P1​(t)=sin2θsin2(2ℏΔf​t​).
(15)
Their sum is one. For θ=π/3, the first minimum of P0​ is 1/4 at t=πℏ/Δf​, and the return is complete at T=2πℏ/Δf​. At θ=0 or π there is no oscillation, because the initial quantum state is already a final energy eigenstate.
Changing measurement basis reveals the evolving relative quantum phase. Measuring the final energy first instead destroys this quantum coherence between final energy eigenstates in the unconditioned ensemble: its later original-basis return probability is c4+s4=1−21​sin2θ, equal to 5/8 for this example. That constant differs from the oscillation of the unmeasured pure state. The work protocol and the oscillation protocol therefore use separate preparations.
