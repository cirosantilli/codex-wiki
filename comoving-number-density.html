Comoving number density (np​=(1+z)3nc​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Scale factor (cosmology) Comoving coordinate Comoving volume
OurBigBook.com Words: 87 Articles: 1
Comoving number density is object count per comoving volume. With scale factor normalized to unity today, proper density is np​=a−3nc​. A conserved population moving with homogeneous expansion has constant nc​ despite declining proper density. Formation, mergers or changing sample selection can make nc​ evolve.
