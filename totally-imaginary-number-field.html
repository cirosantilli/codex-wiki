Totally imaginary number field

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Number field
OurBigBook.com Words: 159 Articles: 2
A number field is totally imaginary if it has no embedding into the real numbers. Its embeddings into the complex numbers occur entirely in conjugate pairs. This is also called totally complex; it does not assert that every element of the field is nonreal.
