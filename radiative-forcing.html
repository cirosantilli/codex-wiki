Radiative forcing (ΔF)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Climate change
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 1
Radiative forcing is a change in the net downward radiative flux caused by an imposed perturbation, evaluated with a specified adjustment convention before the surface climate responds. State the reference period and whether the diagnostic is at the tropopause or top of the atmosphere. A local seasonal ocean heat flux is not directly interchangeable with global annual radiative forcing.
