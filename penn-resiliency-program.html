Penn Resiliency Program

 Home Biology Medicine Public health Prevention of mental disorders Universal prevention
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
A cognitive-behavioral prevention program studied in school and other settings. A trial with a nonspecific-program comparison found heterogeneous school results and no overall average depressive-symptom benefit in the full sample; program identity alone does not guarantee prevention of disorder.[ref]
