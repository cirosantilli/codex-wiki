Fejér monotonicity (∥zk+1−p∥≤∥zk−p∥)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
A sequence is Fejér monotone with respect to a nonempty set C if ∥zk+1−p∥≤∥zk−p∥ for every p∈C. It is bounded. If it has a cluster point zˉ∈C, its nonincreasing distance to zˉ and that convergent subsequence force the entire sequence to converge to zˉ.
