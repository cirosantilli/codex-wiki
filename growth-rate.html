Growth rate (Reσ)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Dispersion relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
For a normal mode proportional to eσt, the real part Reσ is its exponential growth rate. A positive growth rate signals linear instability.
