For the unit-width symmetric box kernel for density estimation, suppose the design density is positive and differentiable at x, the regression function is twice differentiable there, and the conditional variance and design density are bounded near x. Write Gn​=(nh)−1∑i​(Yi​−m(x))1{∣Xi​−x∣≤h/2}​. Symmetry cancels its linear Taylor term, giving EGn​=O(h2); independence gives VarGn​=O((nh)−1). The absolute error bound for a ratio estimator with a controlled denominator gives the displayed rate if the small-denominator contribution is negligible. The balance h≍n−1/5 gives O(n−2/5). Interior design positivity matters: support boundaries can prevent the linear cancellation.
