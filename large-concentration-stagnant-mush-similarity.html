For C=C0​/ΔC≫1 and inverse Stefan number S=O(C), a stagnant mushy-layer model has nearly constant effective heat capacity, giving Ω2θt​=κθzz​ with Ω2=1+S/C. With negligible liquid solutal diffusivity, roof value θ=−1, positive liquid superheat θ∞​, and edge h=2λκt​, the mush profile is θ=−1+erf(Ωη)/erf(Ωλ). Matching heat flux requires Ωeλ2erfcλ=θ∞​eΩ2λ2erf(Ωλ). The square on Ω in the exponential is essential. The porosity C/(C−θ) records the leading composition balance; the linear thermal profile is asymptotic, not exact at finite C.
