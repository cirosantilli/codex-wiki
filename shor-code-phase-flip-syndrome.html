Shor-code phase-flip syndrome ((X1⊗3​X2⊗3​, X2⊗3​X3⊗3​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 81
Group the nine physical qubits into three blocks and write Br​ for the product of three Pauli X gates in block r. The commuting stabilizer generators B1​B2​ and B2​B3​ have eigenvalue +1 on both logical codewords. A Pauli Z gate in blocks one, two or three gives syndromes (−,+), (−,−) or (+,−) respectively. Apply a Z gate to any one qubit of the indicated block to recover the logical state. Z errors at distinct locations within a block act identically on the code, so this correction is degenerate.
