Regular value

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 295 Articles: 6
A value is regular when the derivative is surjective at every point of its preimage.
Every value of a submersion is regular, but a map can have some regular values without being a submersion everywhere.
