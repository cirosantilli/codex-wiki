Molar solubility (s)

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Precipitation (chemistry) Solubility product
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
The molar solubility s is the amount of substance of a specified solid dissolved per volume of saturated chemical solution, under stated conditions. It counts dissolved formula units, not the sum of all dissolved ion concentrations. For AB with no initial A or B and no competing chemical reactions, one-to-one stoichiometry gives cA​=cB​=s. For an AB2​ solid, the corresponding concentrations are s and 2s.[ref]
