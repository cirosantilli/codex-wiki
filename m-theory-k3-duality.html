M-theory K3 duality (M on K3⟷heterotic on T3)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory M-theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 2
M-theory on a K3 surface and the heterotic string on a three-torus have matching seven-dimensional moduli and charge lattices. The K3 intersection lattice supplies twenty-two vector charges; an M5-brane wrapped on K3 becomes the heterotic fundamental string. The strong-coupling interpretation and low-energy matching are derived in section 4.7 of String Theory Dynamics in Various Dimensions.
