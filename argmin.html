Argmin (argminx∈S​f(x))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 1
The set of minimizers of an objective:
argminx∈S​f(x)={x∈S:f(x)≤f(y) for every y∈S}.
(3)
It is a set of inputs, whereas the minimum is an objective value. The argmin can be empty if an infimum is not attained, a singleton, or a larger set.
