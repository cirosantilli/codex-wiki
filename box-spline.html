Box spline (MΞ​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation Spline (mathematics) Spline approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 331 Articles: 3
For a spanning direction matrix Ξ=(ξ1​,…,ξm​) in Rd, the box spline is the density of the pushforward of uniform measure on [0,1]m under t↦Ξt. Equivalently,
∫Rd​MΞ​(x)φ(x)dx=∫[0,1]m​φ(Ξt)dt.
(9)
Its support is the zonotope ∑j​[0,1]ξj​. It is piecewise polynomial of total degree at most m−d. For integer directions, splitting each integration interval in half yields its binary refinement symbol
2d∏j=1m​21+zξj​​,
(10)
up to the monomial specifying the choice of origin. If r is the smallest number of directions whose removal leaves a nonspanning set, the usual box spline smoothness criterion gives Cr−2 regularity. Repeated directions increase smoothness while enlarging support.
