Set K=βJ. The transfer matrix for the one-dimensional Ising model assigns a Boltzmann factor to each bond. In the Ising spin order (+1,−1), it is
Tss′​=e−Kss′,T=(e−KeK​eKe−K​).
(24)
Multiplying these bond factors and summing every Ising spin implements the canonical ensemble. The periodic boundary conditions close the product into a trace:
ZN​=∑S1​,…,SN​​∏j=1N​TSj​Sj+1​​=trTN.
(25)
The eigenvectors ∣+⟩=(1,1)T/2​ and ∣−⟩=(1,−1)T/2​ form an orthonormal basis, with eigenvalues
λ+​=e−K+eK=2coshK,λ−​=e−K−eK=−2sinhK.
(26)
The negative second eigenvalue encodes alternating correlations. Defining q=λ−​/λ+​=−tanhK, the exact partition function and total Helmholtz free energy are
ZN​=(2coshK)N+(−2sinhK)N=(2coshK)N(1+qN),​
(27)
FN​=−β1​lnZN​=−βN​ln(2coshK)−β1​ln(1+qN).​
(28)
The parity-dependent term must be retained for finite N; in particular it nearly cancels the leading term of ZN​ at low temperature when N is odd.
Represent insertion of one Ising spin by Σ=diag(1,−1). In the eigenbasis of T, this matrix swaps ∣+⟩ and ∣−⟩. Its diagonal entries in that eigenbasis vanish, giving the exact one-point correlation function
⟨S1​⟩=ZN​tr(ΣTN)​=0.​
(29)
Equivalently, configurations pair with their opposites under spin inversion symmetry, with the same Boltzmann factor and opposite S1​.
Two insertions separated by n bonds give the two-point correlation function
⟨S1​Sn+1​⟩​=ZN​tr(ΣTnΣTN−n)​=λ+N​+λ−N​λ−n​λ+N−n​+λ+n​λ−N−n​​.​
(30)
Thus the exact finite antiferromagnetic Ising-ring correlation is
CN​(n):=⟨S1​Sn+1​⟩=1+qNqn+qN−n​,q=−tanh(βJ),0≤n≤N−1.​
(31)
The two numerator terms represent the two arcs between the sites. The formula has CN​(0)=1 and CN​(n)=CN​(N−n), as required by the periodic boundary conditions. Since the one-point correlation function is zero, this is also the connected correlation function. At K=0, every Ising spin is independent, so CN​(n)=0 for distinct sites and CN​(0)=1; powers with exponent zero are understood as one.
