Joint entropy (h(X1​,…,Xn​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Information entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The information entropy of the joint probability distribution of a random vector. For discrete variables it is −∑x1​,…,xn​​p(x1​,…,xn​)logp(x1​,…,xn​), with zero-mass terms omitted. Factoring joint probabilities into conditional probabilities gives the chain rule for information entropy, so joint entropy measures all coordinates together while accounting for their statistical dependence.
