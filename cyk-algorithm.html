CYK algorithm

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Formal language theory Context-free language Context-free grammar Chomsky normal form
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
The CYK algorithm decides membership in a context-free language given by a grammar in Chomsky normal form. It records, for every substring, the nonterminals that derive that substring and combines adjacent substrings using binary productions. For a word of length n and a fixed grammar, it runs in O(n3) time.
