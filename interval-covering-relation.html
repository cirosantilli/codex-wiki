Interval covering relation (J⟶K)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Dynamical systems Discrete dynamical system Interval map
OurBigBook.com Words: 146 Articles: 3
For closed intervals J and K, write J⟶K under F when F(J)⊇K. The intermediate value theorem implies that there is a closed subinterval L⊆J with F(L)=K.
