Dual cube complex of a wallspace (C(S,W))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Cubical complex Wallspace
OurBigBook.com Words: 214 Articles: 5
The vertices of the dual cube complex are consistent choices of one halfspace of every wall that differ from a principal choice on only finitely many walls. Two vertices are joined when their choices differ on one wall, and higher cubes fill the resulting hypercubes. The result is a CAT(0) cube complex.
