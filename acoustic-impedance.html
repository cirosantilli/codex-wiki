Acoustic impedance (Z=ρc)

 ... Physics Branch of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity Linear elasticity Elastic wave in an isotropic solid
OurBigBook.com Words: 63 Articles: 1
The acoustic impedance of a fluid is Z=ρc. At an oblique planar interface, zero reflection requires a matching condition involving the normal impedances Z/cosθ or, equivalently for displacement amplitudes, Zcosθ.
