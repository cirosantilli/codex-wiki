Conjugacy of Cartan subalgebras of the symplectic Lie algebra (ghg−1=h′,g∈Sp(V))

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Cartan subalgebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 115
Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, any two Cartan subalgebras of the symplectic Lie algebra are conjugate by the symplectic group. A Cartan subalgebra h is an abelian Lie algebra and equals its centralizer of a Lie subalgebra. The symplectic centralizer decomposition therefore forces all its weight spaces in V to be one-dimensional, nonzero, and paired by opposite weights of a representation. Choose a basis vector in each space, normalizing the perfect pairing with its opposite to one. These vectors form a symplectic basis B. In this basis, h is contained in the Lie subalgebra of diagonal matrices with entries (t1​,…,tn​,−t1​,…,−tn​). All those diagonal matrices commute with h, so equality with its centralizer of a Lie subalgebra forces equality with the entire diagonal Lie subalgebra. Repeating for h′ gives a second symplectic basis. The linear map sending one symplectic basis to the other belongs to the symplectic group and conjugates the two diagonal Lie subalgebras, proving the claim.
