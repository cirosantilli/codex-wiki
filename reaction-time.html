Reaction time (RT)

 Home Science Psychology Psychological research methods
OurBigBook.com Words: 156 Articles: 3
The elapsed time between a specified event and a response. The event, response criterion and time units must be defined; trial exclusion and aggregation affect its analysis. Repeated trials from one participant are not independent participant-level observations.
