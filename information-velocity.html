Information velocity

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Dispersion relation Phase velocity and group velocity
OurBigBook.com Words: 100
An information velocity must specify what counts as a newly distinguishable message and how it is detected. In a causal model, receivers cannot distinguish two otherwise identical preparations before their difference can arrive. Front velocity therefore bounds the arrival of genuinely new changes when the model has a finite domain of dependence. Group velocity can estimate the delay of a weakly distorted narrow wave packet, but it is not a universal speed of information: damping, amplification and reshaping can move the peak without advancing the first possible response. A Gaussian peak shift alone does not test relativistic causality.[ref]
