Three-point amputation in the connected-action convention (G3c​=iΓ(3)(G2c​)⊗3)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Effective action One-particle-irreducible vertex
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
Use the Minkowski generating functional Z=eiW, source +Jϕ, mean field φ=δW/δJ, and quantum effective action Γ=W−∫Jφ. Then W(2)=iG2c​, W(3)=−G3c​, and Γ(2)=−(W(2))−1. Differentiating this inverse gives Γ(3)=(W(2))−1⊗3W(3)=−i(G2c​)−1⊗3G3c​, with integration over the repeated spacetime arguments. Thus amputation of external propagators gives iΓ(3) in this convention. The full connected two-point functions, rather than bare propagators, perform the exact amputation.
