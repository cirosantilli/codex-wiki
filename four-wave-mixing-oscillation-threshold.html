Four-wave-mixing oscillation threshold (κL=π/2)

 ... Branches of physics Optics Nonlinear optics Four-wave mixing Degenerate four-wave mixing Backward four-wave-mixing boundary problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The first zero of cosκL makes the homogeneous opposite-end boundary problem singular. Its undepleted-pump treatment admits a nontrivial field without an imposed signal, indicating a parametric oscillation threshold. Depletion, saturation and the actual noise-seeded dynamics determine finite fields near threshold.
