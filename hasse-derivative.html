Hasse derivative (f[ℓ](x)=∑j≥ℓ​(ℓj​)fj​xj−ℓ)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
The Hasse derivatives of a polynomial are the coefficients in f(x+t)=∑ℓ≥0​f[ℓ](x)tℓ. A point a has multiplicity at least m exactly when f[ℓ](a)=0 for 0≤ℓ<m. Unlike iterated formal derivatives divided by factorials, this definition works in positive characteristic, where factorials can vanish. For example, in characteristic two (x+1)2 has zero first formal derivative but nonzero second Hasse derivative.
