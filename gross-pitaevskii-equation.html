Gross–Pitaevskii equation (iℏ∂t​ψ=(−ℏ2∇2/(2m)+g∣ψ∣2+V)ψ)

The Gross–Pitaevskii equation describes a dilute interacting condensate through a complex order parameter ψ, normalized so that ∣ψ∣2 is number density. Repulsive contact interactions have g>0. In a frame rotating at the condensate frequency, the operator includes −μ, with chemical potential μ. It is generated by a Hamiltonian functional; its homogeneous positive-density state has n0​=μ/g when g,μ>0.
