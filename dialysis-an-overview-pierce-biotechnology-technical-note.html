Manufacturer scientific technical note issued by Pierce Biotechnology, with copyright 2008 Thermo Fisher Scientific. Neither first publication nor revision date is established. The guidance describes membrane retention conventions and limitations, rather than guaranteeing recovery for every sample.
Cited passages: Dialysis: an overview, Tech Tip 20, p. 1, Dialysis is Diffusion Through a Semi-Permeable Membrane; Tech Tip 20, p. 1, Factors Affecting Dialysis Rate: MWCO definition and globular reference molecules; Tech Tip 20, p. 2, Other Factors in Dialysis: water transport, volume change and nonspecific membrane binding; Tech Tip 20, pp. 1-2, renewed gradients and Other Factors in Dialysis.
