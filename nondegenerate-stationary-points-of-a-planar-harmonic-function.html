Nondegenerate stationary points of a planar harmonic function (detD2u=−uxx2​−uxy2​<0)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Harmonic function
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
For a planar harmonic function, uyy​=−uxx​. Its Hessian matrix is therefore (ab​b−a​), with eigenvalues ±a2+b2​. At a stationary point with nonzero Hessian matrix determinant, both signs occur, so the point is a saddle point. A zero determinant requires separate treatment and is not covered by this nondegenerate test. The result is the local quadratic counterpart of the maximum principle for harmonic functions.
