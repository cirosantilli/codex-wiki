Partial-wave scattering

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 138 Articles: 2
Partial-wave scattering decomposes a scattering state into partial waves with definite orbital angular momentum. For a central potential, each channel has its own scattering phase shift and Partial-wave S-matrix.
