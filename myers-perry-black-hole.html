Myers-Perry black hole

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 2
A Myers-Perry black hole is the higher-dimensional asymptotically flat rotating vacuum generalization of the Kerr black hole. In six dimensions, a solution with one nonzero rotation parameter a and horizon radius r+​ has mass parameter μ=r+​(r+2​+a2).
