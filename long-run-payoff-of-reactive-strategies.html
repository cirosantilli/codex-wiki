Long-run payoff of reactive strategies (x∗​=[q1​+(p1​−q1​)q2​]/[1−(p1​−q1​)(p2​−q2​)])

OurBigBook.com Words: 68
With initially independent actions, same-round independence is preserved by reactive updates: each next action uses the other independent previous action and fresh independent randomization. Marginal cooperation follows x′=q1​+(p1​−q1​)y, y′=q2​+(p2​−q2​)x. If the product of response slopes has modulus below one, these recurrences converge and the joint distribution is their product. Averaging R,S,T,P over that distribution gives the long-time payoffs. At deterministic response slopes of modulus one, initial-memory-specific cycles must instead be averaged directly.
