Fibre spheres in a sphere-torus product (F=[S2×{q}])

 ... Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Symplectic manifold Pseudoholomorphic curve J-holomorphic sphere Gromov compactness for spheres
OurBigBook.com Words: 95
In a product symplectic manifold S2(a)×T2n−2, every compatible almost complex structure admits a J-holomorphic sphere in class F through each point. Here S2(a) has area a, and the torus carries a constant symplectic form. All spherical classes are integer multiples of F, so a curve in class F cannot split into positive-area nonconstant spheres. For the product complex structure the fibre through a point is unique and regular. Its one-point count modulo two is one; generic transversality and the compact one-dimensional parameterized moduli spaces preserve this count. Approximation and Gromov compactness for spheres remove the genericity requirement. This gives the area-a curve used in the proof of the Gromov non-squeezing theorem.
