Projective dimension (pdR​M)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Projective module
OurBigBook.com Words: 417 Articles: 5
For a nonzero module, the projective dimension pdR​M is the shortest length of a projective resolution of the module M, or infinity if no finite projective resolution exists. Equivalently, it is the supremum of degrees with nonzero ExtRn​(M,N) as N ranges over all modules. This equivalence follows by dimension shifting and the characterization of projective modules by vanishing Ext1.
