Slowly rotating weak-field metric

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Linearized gravity
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
Far from a slowly rotating isolated body of mass m and angular momentum J, the scalar part of the metric is the linearized point-mass field while g0i​ is the dipole field of J. For rotation about the z-axis, the cross term is 4Jr−3(ydx−xdy)dt in a common convention.
