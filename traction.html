Traction (t=σn)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Constitutive equation Stress
OurBigBook.com Words: 109 Articles: 1
Traction is the force per unit area transmitted across an oriented surface. For unit normal vector n, the stress tensor gives t=σn. Its normal component is normal stress, and its tangential component is shear stress.
