Maximum principle for harmonic functions

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Maximum principle
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A continuous function harmonic on a bounded domain attains its maximum and minimum on the boundary. Apply the strictly subharmonic result to u+ϵ∣x∣2 and let ϵ↓0, then apply the same argument to −u.
