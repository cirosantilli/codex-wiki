Darcy law

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 38
Darcy's law relates the Darcy velocity, the fluid flux per unit total cross-sectional area relative to a solid skeleton, to the driving pressure gradient. With no body force, u=−(k/μ)∇p, where k is permeability of a porous medium and μ is dynamic viscosity. With gravity, the pressure gradient is replaced by ∇p−ρg.
