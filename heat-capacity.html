Heat capacity (CX​=(δQ/dT)X​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 13
Heat capacity measures the heat supplied per small temperature increase along a specified process:
CX​=(dTδQ​)X​.
(138)
The subscript X records the constraint, such as fixed volume or fixed pressure. The units are JK−1. The symbol δQ emphasizes that heat is a transfer along a path, rather than a state function with a differential dQ. Consequently, an unspecified heat capacity does not determine the required heat.
For a closed system of fixed composition undergoing a quasistatic process, assume that only pressure-volume work occurs, with no friction and no change in bulk kinetic energy or potential energy. The first law of thermodynamics and H=U+pV, where U is internal energy and H is enthalpy, then give
δQ=dU+pdV=dH−Vdp,CV​=(∂T∂U​)V​,Cp​=(∂T∂H​)p​.
(139)
These partial derivatives define heat capacity at constant volume and heat capacity at constant pressure, with the amount and composition also held fixed. Integrating along the selected constraint gives Q=∫T1​T2​​CX​(T)dT within a single thermodynamic phase; Q=CX​ΔT requires constant CX​. [ref]
For a computed example, take a monatomic gas in the classical ideal gas regime, with amount of substance n=1mol and stipulated molar gas constant R=8.314Jmol−1K−1. The equipartition theorem gives U=3nRT/2, and the ideal gas law gives pV=nRT. Thus
CV​=23​nR=12.471JK−1,Cp​=25​nR=20.785JK−1.
(140)
For the same temperature increase ΔT=10K, the two processes require
QV​=124.71J,Qp​=207.85J,ΔUV​=ΔUp​=124.71J,WV​=0,Wp​=pΔV=nRΔT=83.14J.
(141)
Here work done by the gas is positive. At fixed pressure, the extra heat supplies expansion work; the internal energy increase is the same because internal energy of an ideal gas depends only on temperature. Their final volume values differ. [ref]
The practical value of heat capacity is that a measured heat input and temperature response can determine it. Once its constraint and temperature dependence are known, it predicts heating requirements and supplies entropy changes through entropy from heat capacity. [ref]
