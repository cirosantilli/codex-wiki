Promoter (genetics)

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Gene expression
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 4
A gene promoter is a DNA region involved in initiating transcription. Moving a coding sequence into a nuclear genome does not guarantee that its original gene promoter can drive nuclear gene expression.
