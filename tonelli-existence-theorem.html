Tonelli existence theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Calculus of variations Direct method in the calculus of variations
OurBigBook.com Words: 116
For a scalar fixed-endpoint problem on a bounded interval, suppose the continuous integrand f(x,y,p) is convex in p and satisfies f(x,y,p)≥Θ(∣p∣)−C, where Θ is nonnegative convex and Θ(r)/r→∞. If there is one admissible absolutely continuous function of finite energy, a global minimizer exists in W1,1.
The lower bound gives uniform integrability of derivatives in a minimizing sequence. Fixed endpoint values then give boundedness and equicontinuity of the functions. Extract uniform convergence of the functions and weak L1 convergence of their derivatives; the limit retains the endpoint values. Convexity in the derivative gives weak lower semicontinuity of convex gradient energies, so the limit attains the infimum. Strict convexity of the full integrand on a convex admissible class additionally gives uniqueness.
