Axial coupling of an elliptic helix (Fz​/Ω=Gz​/W=−cABb∫−ππ​(s′)−1dθ)

For a slender centreline X(θ)=(Acosθ,Bsinθ,bθ), −π≤θ≤π, let s′(θ)=(A2sin2θ+B2cos2θ+b2)1/2 and use local force density f=c(2I−tt)v, t=dX/ds, c=2πμ/ln(L/R). Rotation with angular speed Ω gives t⋅v=ΩAB/s′, hence
Fz​=−cΩABb∫−ππ​s′(θ)dθ​.
(12)
Axial translation with speed W gives exactly the same coefficient for the torque: Gz​=−cWABb∫dθ/s′. Indeed (X×t)z​=AB/s′. Both forces and torques here are exerted on the fluid. In the circular case A=B=a, this reduces to Fz​/Ω=Gz​/W=−2πca2b/a2+b2​. The equality is also a consequence of the Lorentz reciprocal theorem, since the local resistance tensor is symmetric.
