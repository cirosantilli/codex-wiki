Green-theorem proof of exactness on a disc

 ... Geometry and topology Differential form Exterior derivative Closed and exact differential forms Closed differential form Poincaré lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
On a star-shaped planar domain containing zero, let η be a smooth closed differential one-form and set f(x)=∫[0,x]​η. Green theorem on the triangle with vertices 0,x,x+h gives f(x+h)−f(x)=∫[x,x+h]​η. Dividing by a displacement and taking its limit proves df=η. Thus every such form is an exact differential form, a degree-one version of the Poincaré lemma which uses only the planar integral theorem.
