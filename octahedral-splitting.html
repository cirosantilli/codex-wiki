Octahedral splitting (Δo​)

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry Ligand field
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
In an ideal octahedral molecular geometry, the metal d orbitals divide into lower t2g​ and higher eg​ sets. Their separation is Δo​. Whether electrons pair in the lower set depends on that separation relative to pairing costs; observed many-electron transition energies need not equal Δo​ exactly.
