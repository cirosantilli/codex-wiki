Cofinality (cf(α))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cardinal number
OurBigBook.com Words: 456 Articles: 12
The cofinality of an ordinal α is the least order type of an unbounded subset of α, equivalently the least ordinal β admitting a cofinal function β→α.
