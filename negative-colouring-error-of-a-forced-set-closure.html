Negative colouring error of a forced-set closure (P(Gcolour​∈δ∨​)≤nℓ+12−r)

OurBigBook.com Words: 96
Independently colour each vertex with one of m−1 colours and join differently coloured vertices. This complete multipartite graph has no m-clique. If a forced set W is rainbow but its r witnesses are not, condition on the colours of W. The disjoint petals make those failure events conditionally independent. For each witness of size at most ℓ, the probability of a colour collision is at most (2ℓ​)/(m−1). If this is at most 1/2, one closure step causes an error with probability at most 2−r. There are at most nℓ+1 possible steps; apply the union bound.
