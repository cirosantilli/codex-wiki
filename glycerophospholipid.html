Glycerophospholipid

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Lipid Phospholipid
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 3
Glycerophospholipids are phospholipids built on a glycerol-phosphate scaffold. The illustrated two-acyl group form is common, but the subclass also includes other substitutions and one-chain derivatives. See IUPAC–IUB lipid nomenclature, Lip-2.2 and Lip-2.6.
