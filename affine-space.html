Affine space (A)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Affine geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 464 Articles: 9
A nonempty set A equipped with a group action of the additive group of a vector space V, such that for every x,y∈A there is a unique v∈V with x+v=y. There is no distinguished origin. Choosing one identifies A with V; changing it gives a translation.
