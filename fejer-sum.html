Fejér sum (σn​(f))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier series Fejér kernel
OurBigBook.com Words: 336 Articles: 6
The nth Fejér sum is the arithmetic mean of the first n Fourier partial sums. Equivalently, it is the convolution of the function with the Fejér kernel.
