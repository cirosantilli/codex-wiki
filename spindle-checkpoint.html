Spindle checkpoint

 Home Biology Biological cell Cell cycle M phase Mitosis
OurBigBook.com Words: 291 Articles: 5
The spindle checkpoint can delay anaphase while kinetochore attachment is incomplete. This couples progression to the state of the spindle apparatus, rather than requiring every biological cell to separate its sister chromatids after a fixed waiting time. Alignment in a picture alone is not a demonstration that this control is satisfied.[ref]
