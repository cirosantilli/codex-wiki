Kac collision operator (Q=(2N​)−1∑i<j​(2π)−1∫Uij,θ​dθ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Kinetic theory Kac model
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 2
For N≥2, Uij,θ​F=F∘Rij,θ​ uses the genuine two-coordinate rotation (vi​,vj​)↦(vi​cosθ+vj​sinθ,−vi​sinθ+vj​cosθ). Average it uniformly over angles and pairs to obtain Q. Rotation invariance of Lebesgue measure makes Q a bounded self-adjoint operator of norm one on L2(RN). Each pair average is an orthogonal projection, but their full average is not in general a projection.
