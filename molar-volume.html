Molar volume (vm​=V/n)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Volume (thermodynamics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
The volume per amount of substance, with n measured in moles. For a uniform pure substance, vm​=M/ρ, where M is its molar mass and ρ its mass density. The Clapeyron equation pairs a difference of molar volumes with latent heat per mole.
