Spherical-collapse model

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Dark matter halo
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 23
The spherical-collapse model applies a spherical top-hat filter to an overdense region and predicts collapse when its linearly extrapolated smoothed overdensity exceeds the threshold δc​≃1.686 in an Einstein-de Sitter universe.
