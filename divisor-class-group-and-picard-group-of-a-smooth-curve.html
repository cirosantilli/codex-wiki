Divisor class group and Picard group of a smooth curve (Cl(X)≅Pic(X)≅H1(X,OX∗​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Weil divisor Divisor class group
OurBigBook.com Words: 155 Articles: 1
For a smooth irreducible algebraic curve, send a divisor on an algebraic curve D=∑P​nP​P to the line bundle associated to a divisor defined locally by OX​(D)P​=tP−nP​​OX,P​. A nonzero rational section of a line bundle gives its inverse construction, and changing that section changes its divisor by a principal divisor. Thus the divisor class group is the Picard group. The quotient of the sheaf of nonzero rational functions on an irreducible variety by the sheaf of units of the structure sheaf has stalk Z at each closed point, via the discrete valuation. Its global sections are finite sums of points. The corresponding long exact sequence in sheaf cohomology, and the vanishing for the rational-function sheaf, identify both groups with H1(X,OX∗​).
