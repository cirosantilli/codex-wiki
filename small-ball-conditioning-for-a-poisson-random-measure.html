For a Poisson random measure on Rd with Lebesgue measure intensity and a nonnegative compactly supported continuous function f, put mr​=μ(B(0,r)) and ar​=∫B(0,r)​e−fdμ. Continuity gives ar​/mr​→e−f(0). With L=Ee−M(f), the count-weighted Laplace functional of a Poisson random measure and independent inside/outside counts give respectively
P(M(B(0,r))≥1)E[M(B(0,r))e−M(f)]​=L1−e−mr​ar​​,E[e−M(f)∣M(B(0,r))≥1]=L1−e−mr​1−e−ar​​.
(36)
Both converge to the displayed value. The conditioning is meaningful for every r>0; it is not conditioning on a positive-probability point at the origin.
