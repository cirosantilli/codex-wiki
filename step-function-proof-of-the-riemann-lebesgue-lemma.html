Step-function proof of the Riemann-Lebesgue lemma (∫ab​f(t)cos(ωt)dt→0)

For a continuous real function f on [a,b], uniform step approximation on a compact interval gives a finite step function g=∑j​cj​1Ij​​ with small uniform error. Each cosine integral over an interval has absolute value at most 2/∣ω∣. Therefore
​∫ab​f(t)cos(ωt)dt​≤(b−a)∥f−g∥∞​+∣ω∣2​∑j​∣cj​∣.
(34)
First choose g to make the first term small, then let ∣ω∣ grow with g fixed. This proves the cosine version of the Riemann-Lebesgue lemma without differentiating f; the sine and complex-exponential versions follow in the same way.
