Planetary quadrupole coefficient (J2​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field Newtonian gravitational potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 131 Articles: 1
The dimensionless coefficient J2​ describes the leading axisymmetric departure of a planet's external Newtonian gravitational potential from a monopole: Φ=−GMp​/r+[GMp​J2​Rp2​/r3]P2​(cosϑ), where ϑ is colatitude from the spin axis. An oblate mass distribution has positive J2​. The disturbing function is minus the nonspherical potential term. On a circular inclined Kepler orbit, cosϑ=sinIsinM, so orbit averaging gives RJ2​​=GMp​J2​Rp2​a−3(1/2−3sin2I/4). The exact circular nodal rate is −3nJ2​(Rp​/a)2cosI/2; its small-tilt limit is −3nJ2​(Rp​/a)2/2.
