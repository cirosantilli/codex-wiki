In an incompressible shearing sheet with background velocity −2Axey​, take both the perturbation velocity and the entire magnetic field to be real single Fourier modes with a common real wavevector and transverse amplitudes. Then every quadratic directional derivative vanishes, including terms involving complex conjugates. There is no uniform background magnetic field and consequently no linear magnetic-tension coupling between the amplitudes. Advecting the phase requires k˙x​=2Aky​, k˙y​=k˙z​=0. The velocity amplitude obeys v˙−2Avx​ey​+2Ω×v=−ikψ​−νk2v; the magnetic amplitude obeys the displayed equation. The modified pressure includes magnetic pressure; the physical pressure can contain a mean and second harmonic even when the modified pressure is a single mode.
