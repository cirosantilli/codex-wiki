Circular orbit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Circular motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 278 Articles: 4
A circular orbit maintains constant distance from its centre of attraction. Its inward radial acceleration has magnitude v2/r, so the radial force must satisfy Fr​=−mv2/r.
