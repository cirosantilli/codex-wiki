Circular orbit

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Circular motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 650 Articles: 8
A circular orbit maintains constant distance from its centre of attraction. Its velocity changes direction even when its speed stays constant. The inward centripetal acceleration has magnitude v2/r, so the radial force must satisfy Fr​=−mv2/r. For a Newtonian gravitational field with standard gravitational parameter μ, this gives v=μ/r​.
