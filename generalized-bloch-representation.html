Generalized Bloch representation (ρ=I/N+∑k=1N2−1​sk​σk​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Density matrix Bloch vector
OurBigBook.com Words: 382 Articles: 6
Choose a Hilbert-Schmidt inner product orthonormal Hermitian operator basis with final element I/N​. A density operator has real coefficients rk​=Tr(σk​ρ) and fixed final coefficient 1/N​. The remaining coefficients form its generalized Bloch vector. Positivity restricts these real vectors to a compact convex body; for N>2 this body is not the entire Euclidean ball allowed by a purity bound.
