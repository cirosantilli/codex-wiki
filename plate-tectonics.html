Plate tectonics

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Solid Earth Mantle convection
OurBigBook.com Words: 854 Articles: 25
A mobile-lid mode of planetary evolution in which coherent pieces of the lithosphere move, form at spreading regions and can recycle through subduction. Slab pull, ridge push and mantle flow compete with deformation resistance. It affects heat transport, volcanism, earthquakes and the carbonate-silicate cycle. It is not guaranteed solely by an exoplanet's mass.
