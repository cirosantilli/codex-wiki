Frameshift mutation

 ... Genetics Molecular genetics Gene expression Translation (biology) Genetic code Reading frame
OurBigBook.com Words: 139
A frameshift mutation changes downstream grouping into codons through insertion or deletion of a number of nucleotides not divisible by three. In the constructed messenger RNA example, insert C immediately after AUG while retaining the same initiation position: AUGCGCUUUCUG(A). The first codon is preserved, but the original downstream groups GCU, UUC and UGA disappear. The displayed fragment now has no complete standard stop codon; what happens later depends on its continuation.
The short proof is positional: every downstream nucleotide moves by k positions after an insertion of length k. Boundaries recur every three positions, so they retain the original phase exactly when k is divisible by three. An insertion of three nucleotides can still change the peptide, but it does not shift the downstream reading frame. This distinction helps interpret genetic variants without predicting a particular phenotype from grouping alone.[ref]
