Stokes settling velocity (W)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Suspension (chemistry) Settling velocity
OurBigBook.com Words: 21
The Stokes settling velocity is the terminal relative velocity obtained by balancing gravity, buoyancy, and the Stokes drag law for a dilute isolated sphere.
