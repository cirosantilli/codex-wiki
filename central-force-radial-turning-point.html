Central-force radial turning point

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Momentum Angular momentum Conservation of angular momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 83 Articles: 1
For unit mass in a central force with potential Φ(r) and conserved angular momentum L, a radial turning point r∗​ has zero radial speed and therefore satisfies
E=2r∗2​L2​+Φ(r∗​).
