Einstein-Cartan theory ((ea,ωab))

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Einstein field equations Einstein-Hilbert action
OurBigBook.com Words: 170 Articles: 2
An independent vierbein and metric-compatible spin connection allow spin density to source a torsion tensor. The connection equation from the Einstein-Hilbert action is algebraic in torsion. In the absence of connection-dependent matter it fixes the Levi-Civita connection. For a minimally coupled Dirac field, eliminating the connection generates a local four-fermion interaction. Thus its equivalent torsion-free second-order action includes this contact term; simply dropping it defines a different matter theory.
