Hubbard–Stratonovich transformation (∫dMe−M2/(2U)+Mj∝eUj2/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Path integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 95 Articles: 1
A Gaussian auxiliary-field identity replacing a quadratic expression in a composite field by a linear coupling to a new field. Completing the square in M2/(2U)−Mj yields an exponential eUj2/2 after integrating M, with normalized Gaussian prefactors. It converts suitable quartic field interactions into bilinear couplings and permits fermionic fields to be integrated out.
