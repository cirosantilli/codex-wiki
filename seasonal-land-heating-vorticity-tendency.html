Seasonal land-heating vorticity tendency

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Potential vorticity Ertel potential vorticity Isentropic potential vorticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
Heating over land often favours a deeper cyclonic thermal low, while cooling favours a shallower anticyclonic thermal high. Diabatic heating and surface friction violate the assumptions of simple parcel PV conservation; a vertical profile is needed for a quantitative statement.
