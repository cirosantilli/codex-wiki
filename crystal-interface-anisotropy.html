Crystal interface anisotropy (γ(n),M(n))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Stefan problem Phase boundary
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 1
A crystal's interfacial free energy and attachment kinetics depend on the interface normal n. The orientation-dependent energy γ and mobility M select directions even when bulk diffusion is isotropic. This supplements the transport mechanism of crystal dendrites; isotropic transport alone does not specify the number of preferred arms.
