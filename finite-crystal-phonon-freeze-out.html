Finite-crystal phonon freeze-out (Nph​∼gmin​e−ℏωmin​/(kB​T))

OurBigBook.com Words: 51
An internal harmonic vibration spectrum with lowest positive frequency ωmin​ has the displayed low-temperature thermal phonon population, where gmin​ is its degeneracy. The continuum bulk law fails when thermally occupied wavelengths reach the sample size. Mechanical boundary conditions determine ωmin​; rigid translation of the entire solid is not an internal phonon mode.
