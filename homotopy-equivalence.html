Homotopy equivalence

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 200 Articles: 4
Spaces X and Y are homotopy equivalent when there are continuous maps
f:X→Y,g:Y→X
(20)
such that g∘f is homotopic to idX​ and f∘g is homotopic to idY​.
