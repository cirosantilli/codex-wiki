Photometric passband

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astronomical magnitude Photometric system
OurBigBook.com Words: 103 Articles: 1
A photometric passband is the wavelength-dependent response used for a brightness measurement. An energy-weighted measurement is I=∫ηλ​Fλ​dλ, with spectral flux density Fλ​. Photon-counting conventions can instead include a factor λ/(hc); the calibration must use the same convention as the measurement.
