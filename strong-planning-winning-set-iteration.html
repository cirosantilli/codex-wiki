Strong planning winning-set iteration (Wt+1​=Wt​∪Pre∀​(Wt​))

For a finite nondeterministic planning problem with goal set G, initialize W0​=G and put
Wt+1​=Wt​∪{s:∃a, ∅=Results(s,a)⊆Wt​}.
(2)
A state in Wt​ has a strong plan reaching G within t steps. The proof is mathematical induction: an action witnessing admission to Wt+1​ has only successors with plans of depth at most t. Conversely, the first action of any guaranteed plan of depth t+1 has every successor in Wt​, again by mathematical induction. Since Wt​ only grows inside a finite state space, the iteration stabilizes after at most the number of states many strict expansions. Recording a witnessing action at first admission gives a policy whose rank decreases at every nonterminal step.
