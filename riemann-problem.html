Riemann problem (U(x,t)=U(x/t))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation System of conservation laws
OurBigBook.com Words: 248 Articles: 2
A Riemann problem for a one-dimensional system of conservation laws ∂t​U+∂x​f(U)=0 has two constant initial states, UL​ for x<0 and UR​ for x>0. The data and equation are invariant under (x,t)↦(λx,λt). When the physically admissible entropy solution is unique, this invariance makes it a self-similar solution depending on x/t. For ideal-gas compressible flow, the characteristic speeds are u−c,u,u+c. The two acoustic families carry shock waves or rarefaction waves, while the middle family carries a contact discontinuity. Sufficiently strong separating velocities can instead create a vacuum region.
