L2 norm convergence (∥fn​−f∥2​→0)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Normed vector space Norm topology Norm convergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
A sequence in L2 space converges in its L2 norm when ∫∣fn​−f∣2dμ→0. This concerns the integral of the squared error and need not imply pointwise convergence. In particular a complete Fourier sine basis reconstructs each odd L2 function in this norm, while pointwise claims about its Fourier series require additional hypotheses.
