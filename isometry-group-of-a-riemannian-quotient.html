Isometry group of a Riemannian quotient (Isom(X/Γ)=NIsom(X)​(Γ)/Γ)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Isometry Isometry group
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For a complete simply connected Riemannian manifold X and a free properly discontinuous deck group Γ, each isometry of X/Γ lifts to an isometry of X normalizing Γ. Conversely such a normalizing isometry descends; two lifts differ by a deck transformation. This proves the formula, including orientation-reversing isometries if the full normalizer is used.
