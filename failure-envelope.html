Failure envelope

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Constitutive equation Stress Mohr's circle
OurBigBook.com Words: 202 Articles: 4
The locus of critical combinations of normal stress and shear stress. Contact of a Mohr circle with this boundary identifies an ideal failure threshold; it does not by itself describe post-failure dynamics.
