Derivative-weighted call delta martingale (Mt​=Zt​πt​)

Suppose a′ is bounded and let dZt​=Zt​a′(St​)dWt​, Z0​=1. The Novikov condition makes Z a strictly positive true martingale. For πt​=U(t,St​) solving the call delta equation for a driftless local volatility diffusion, the product formula gives
d(Zt​πt​)=Zt​(a(St​)US​(t,St​)+a′(St​)U(t,St​))dWt​.
(51)
The two drift terms cancel through quadratic covariation. Thus Zπ is a local martingale. If it is a true martingale on the closed horizon, its terminal value is ZT​1{ST​≥K}​, so
πt​=Zt​E[ZT​1{ST​≥K}​∣Ft​]​,0≤πt​≤1.
(52)
The upper bound uses E[ZT​∣Ft​]=Zt​. Equivalently, the option delta is the terminal-exceedance probability under the measure with density ZT​.
