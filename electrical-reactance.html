Electrical reactance (X=ImZ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Electrical impedance
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 2
Electrical reactance X is the signed imaginary part of electrical impedance Z=R+iX, measured in ohms. It describes the part of the terminal electric potential difference in phase quadrature with the electric current in a sinusoidal steady state. In the eiωt convention, an ideal inductor has XL​=ωL>0 and an ideal capacitor has XC​=−1/(ωC)<0 for ω>0. A large ∣X∣ reduces electric current at fixed applied voltage, but an ideal purely reactive component returns the energy it receives over each cycle. This is why electrical reactance and electrical resistance have the same units yet describe different physical behavior. [ref].
In a series RLC circuit, electrical impedances add because the same electric current passes through every component. For ideal positive inductance L, capacitance C, and total electrical resistance RΣ​>0,
X(ω)=ωL−ωC1​,∣Z∣=RΣ2​+X2​,Irms​=∣Z∣Vrms​​,ϕ=arctanRΣ​X​.
(40)
Here ϕ is voltage wave phase minus current wave phase. Below ω0​=1/LC​, X<0 and electric current leads terminal voltage; above it, X>0 and electric current lags. At ω0​, the two signed electrical reactances cancel, leaving Z=RΣ​ and the maximum electric current at fixed terminal voltage. This is the resonant series RLC response. Electrical resistance does not cancel: the toy circuit gives f0​=ω0​/(2π)=1591.55Hz and Irms​=9.091mA there. These numbers follow directly from the stipulated component values. [ref].
Cancellation concerns terminal voltage, not the disappearance of stored energy. In the toy circuit at resonance, the individual inductor and capacitor voltages are each 0.9091V in root mean square magnitude, with opposite wave phases, while their sum is zero. Their peak stored magnetic energy and capacitor energy are each 0.8264μJ, attained at different times. With smaller positive RΣ​, either component voltage can exceed the applied voltage. This ability to select a frequency while sustaining internal energy exchange connects electrical reactance to tuning and frequency response.
For an ideal inductor or capacitor, the absorbed instantaneous power equals the rate of change of stored energy:
pL​=vL​i=dtd​(21​Li2),pC​=vC​i=dtd​(21​CvC2​).
(41)
Positive power stores energy; negative power returns it. Over a periodic steady state, each stored energy returns to its initial value, so its cycle-averaged absorbed power is zero. An ideal resistor instead has pR​=Ri2≥0, transferring energy into Joule heating. More generally, the average terminal power of this series RLC circuit is
p​=Vrms​Irms​cosϕ=Irms2​RΣ​.
(42)
Zero average power in an ideal reactive component does not imply zero instantaneous power, zero electric current, or zero stored energy. Real coils and capacitors have losses, so zero ideal reactive loss is a model assumption rather than a claim about every component. [ref].
