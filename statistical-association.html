Statistical association

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Causal inference
OurBigBook.com Words: 199 Articles: 5
A statistical association is a systematic dependence between variables. It need not represent a causal effect, because it may arise from confounding, selection, or reverse causation.
