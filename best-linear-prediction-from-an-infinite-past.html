Best linear prediction from an infinite past (Xt+1​=projHt​​Xt+1​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Time series
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
The best mean-square linear predictor from a semi-infinite past is the orthogonal projection onto the closed linear span of past observations in the random-variable Hilbert space. For a causal invertible ARMA representation, past observations and past innovations generate the same closed span. The next linear innovation is orthogonal to it and is therefore the prediction error.
