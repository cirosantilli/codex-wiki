Projective measurement

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Measurement in quantum mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 389 Articles: 10
A projective measurement is specified by mutually orthogonal projections Pi​ with ∑i​Pi​=I. On a state ρ, outcome i has probability Tr(Pi​ρ).
This is the projection-valued special case of a measurement in quantum mechanics; general POVM effects need not be projections.
