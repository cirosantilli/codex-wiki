Residual electron freeze-out (αeff​ne​≲H)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Recombination (cosmology)
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
When the effective recombination rate per Electron becomes smaller than the expansion rate, the small remaining ionized fraction can no longer track chemical equilibrium. A nonzero Electron fraction remains, exceeding the exponentially small Saha equation prediction. The effective rate includes atomic radiative bottlenecks; chemical freeze-out is distinct from photon decoupling by scattering.
