Hitting time of a downward-biased walk reflected at an upper barrier (EN​T0​=q−pN​−(q−p)22pq​(1−(p/q)N))

On {0,…,N}, stop at zero, move left with probability q>1/2 and right with probability p=1−q at interior states, and move deterministically from N to N−1. For mi​=Ei​T0​, the first-step analysis gives m0​=0, mi​=1+qmi−1​+pmi+1​ and mN​=1+mN−1​. Setting Di​=mi​−mi−1​ gives qDi​−pDi+1​=1, DN​=1, hence Di​=[1−2p(p/q)N−i]/(q−p). Summing these differences proves the displayed formula, and more generally mi​=i/(q−p)−2pq(p/q)N−i[1−(p/q)i]/(q−p)2. A downward block has a uniformly positive probability of reaching zero, which supplies a geometric tail bound and justifies finiteness of these expectations.
