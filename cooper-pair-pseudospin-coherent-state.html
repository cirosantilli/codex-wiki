Cooper-pair pseudospin coherent state

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Fermi liquid BCS theory BCS ground state
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
The empty and doubly occupied states of a paired momentum block form a two-level space. Define Sk+​=ak↑†​a−k↓†​, Sk−​=a−k↓​ak↑​ and Skz​=(nk↑​+n−k↓​−1)/2. They obey the spin algebra on that space. The normalized factor uk​∣0⟩+vk​∣pair⟩ is a spin-one-half coherent state; the product over blocks is the BCS ground state. Pauli exclusion prevents interpreting a block as an unrestricted harmonic-oscillator pair mode.
