Critical radius for a supercooled spherical seed (ac​=Γ/(Ts​−T∞​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Planetary ice shell Stefan problem Quasistatic spherical Stefan problem with capillarity
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
The Gibbs--Thomson relation depresses a spherical interface's equilibrium temperature by Γ/a. For a>ac​, its interface is warmer than the supercooled ambient and heat loss drives growth. For a<ac​, its interface is colder than the ambient and heat gain drives melting. The equilibrium radius is unstable because the derivative of the quasistatic radius velocity at ac​ is positive.
