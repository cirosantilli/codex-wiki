Gaussian momentum-shell scaling (r′=b2r,h′=b(D+2)/2h)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Renormalization group Gaussian fixed point
OurBigBook.com Words: 174 Articles: 2
After integrating a free-field momentum shell, restore the cutoff with x′=x/b and ϕ′(x′)=b(D−2)/2ϕ<​(bx′). The gradient coefficient is invariant, the mass changes as r′=b2r, and a uniform conjugate field changes as h′=b(D+2)/2h. This follows by counting measure, derivative and field factors in the quadratic Hamiltonian. A nonuniform source instead obeys h′(x′)=b(D+2)/2h(bx′), with the source on the right projected onto the retained Fourier modes. The field has engineering dimension (D−2)/2 and zero anomalous dimension.
