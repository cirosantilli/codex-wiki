Straight-rod resistance in a linear flow (C=4πμ/∣logϵ∣)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Slender-body theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 2
For a straight rod X=sp, −L<s<L, leading slender-body theory uses f=C(I−pp/2)[ΔU+s(ΔΩ×p−Ep)]. Integration gives F=2CL(I−pp/2)ΔU and G=(2CL3/3)[(I−pp)ΔΩ−p×Ep]. Axial spin is outside this centreline approximation.
