Weight diagram

 ... Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Highest-weight representation Weight vector Weight space Weight-space decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 181 Articles: 3
A diagram placing the weights of a representation in the real span of its weight lattice, recording each weight multiplicity. Coincident weights must retain their multiplicity: for an Adjoint representation the zero-weight multiplicity is the dimension of its Cartan subalgebra.
