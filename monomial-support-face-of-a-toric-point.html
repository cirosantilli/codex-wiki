Monomial support face of a toric point

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Toric geometry Toric variety Orbit-cone correspondence
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
At a point p of Speck[C∩M], the exponents m with χm(p)=0 form a semigroup face. They are the lattice points of one face of C. Points with that same support face form a single algebraic torus orbit: their nonzero monomial evaluations extend to homomorphisms from the lattice generated by the face into k∗, and restriction from the full torus is surjective.
