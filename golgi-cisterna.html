Golgi cisterna

 Home Biology Biological cell Organelle Golgi apparatus
OurBigBook.com Words: 135 Articles: 1
A Golgi cisterna is a flattened membrane-enclosed sac. Stacking several sacs does not make their interiors one open space: membrane separates Golgi lumen from intervening cytosol.[ref]
