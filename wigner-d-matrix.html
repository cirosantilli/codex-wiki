Wigner D-matrix (Dmm′(j)​(ϕ,θ,ψ))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Spin Irreducible spin representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For active rotations with U=e−iϕJ3​e−iθJ2​e−iψJ3​, the irreducible spin representation matrix is Dmm′(j)​=e−imϕdmm′(j)​(θ)e−im′ψ, where dmm′(j)​=⟨j,m∣e−iθJ2​∣j,m′⟩. Its spin-one-half middle matrix is (cos(θ/2)sin(θ/2)​−sin(θ/2)cos(θ/2)​). A 2π rotation acts by (−1)2j; only integer-spin representations descend to the special orthogonal group in three dimensions.
