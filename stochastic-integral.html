Stochastic integral (∫HdX)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus
OurBigBook.com Words: 922 Articles: 19
The stochastic integral ∫HdX integrates a predictable process against a semimartingale. It extends pathwise integration against finite-variation processes and the Itô integral against local martingales.
