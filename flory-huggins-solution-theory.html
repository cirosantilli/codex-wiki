Flory–Huggins solution theory (f/(kB​T)=(ϕ/N)lnϕ+(1−ϕ)ln(1−ϕ)+χϕ(1−ϕ))

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry) Polymer solution
OurBigBook.com Words: 592 Articles: 8
Flory–Huggins solution theory is an incompressible lattice description of mixing a polymer with a solvent. A chain occupies N connected sites, while a solvent molecule occupies one; the displayed mixing free energy is per site. The first two terms are translational entropy of mixing contributions and the last is a mean-field excess contact interaction. A positive interaction parameter χ penalizes polymer–solvent contacts. If the site volume is v0​, the physical free-energy density is f/v0​ and the polymer volume fraction gives monomer number density ϕ/v0​. Composition-dependent interaction parameters require extra derivative terms in stability calculations.
