Kernel of reduction of an elliptic curve (E1​(K))

OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 2
For an elliptic curve with good reduction over a local field, the kernel of reduction consists of the points reducing to the identity. The parameter t=−x/y identifies it with the maximal ideal equipped with the formal group of an elliptic curve.
