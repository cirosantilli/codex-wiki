Handedness reversal of helical hydrodynamic resistance (B↦−B,A,D unchanged)

OurBigBook.com Words: 34
Mirroring the handedness of a helix reverses its axial translation-rotation coupling coefficients while preserving its pure translation and rotation resistances. With t=cosθez​−sinθeφ​ for a left-handed helix and the force-on-body convention, B=C=ℓa(ξ⊥​−ξ∥​)sinθcosθ.
