Charge continuity equation (∂t​ρ+∇⋅J=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Maxwell equations Charge conservation from Maxwell equations
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
The local form of conservation of electric charge relates charge density to current density. Taking the divergence of the Ampère-Maxwell equation and substituting Gauss's law proves the equation, using that the divergence of a curl is zero.
