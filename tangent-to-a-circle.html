Tangent to a circle

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological space Circle
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
At a point T on a circle with centre O, the tangent line is perpendicular to the radius OT. For example, differentiating ∣r(s)−O∣2=R2 along a parametric curve on the circle gives (r−O)⋅r′=0, showing that the radial vector and tangent direction are orthogonal vectors. Conversely, the perpendicular straight line through T has only that point in common with the circle: for a nonzero displacement v along it, ∣T+v−O∣2=R2+∣v∣2>R2.
