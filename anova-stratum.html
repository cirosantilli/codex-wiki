ANOVA stratum

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Normal linear model Linear regression Analysis of variance
OurBigBook.com Words: 82 Articles: 1
An ANOVA stratum is an orthogonal response subspace representing one level of experimental variation, such as between blocks or within blocks. In a covariance model scalar on each such subspace, its eigenvalue supplies the common variance scale for its projected coordinates. Treatment and residual sums of squares in ANOVA must be compared within appropriate strata.
