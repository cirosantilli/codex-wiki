Frobenius factorization through a finite normal cover (Lp⊆KorK⊆Lp)

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Scheme of characteristic p Absolute Frobenius morphism
OurBigBook.com Words: 108
For a finite surjective map of normal integral schemes with function fields K⊆L in characteristic p, the condition Lp⊆K lets the Absolute Frobenius morphism of the source factor through the map. On an affine chart A⊆B, send b to bp∈A: it lies in K, is integral over A, and A is integrally closed. The condition K⊆Lp instead lets the map factor after the source Frobenius. Send a to its unique pth root in L; that root lies in the normal ring B because it satisfies a monic polynomial over B. These rules localize and glue; injectivity of Frobenius in the function field supplies uniqueness.
