Matrix summability method (Ai​(x)=∑j​aij​xj​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Space of sequences converging to zero Convergent sequence space
OurBigBook.com Words: 353 Articles: 5
A matrix summability method takes the ordinary limit of a sequence of the row sums Ai​(x), whenever every row series converges and the resulting sequence has a finite limit. For rows in the absolutely summable sequence space with uniformly bounded norms, the matrix defines a bounded linear operator from the l-infinity sequence space to itself.
