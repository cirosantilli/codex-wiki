Invariant theory

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Group representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 19
Invariant theory studies functions and tensors unchanged by a group action. For a linear representation, the polynomial invariant ring records invariant polynomial functions on the representation space. Tensor invariants and commuting algebra actions connect it with Schur–Weyl duality.
