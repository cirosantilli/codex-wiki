Linear Alfvén wave equations (ω=±k∥​vA​)

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics Magnetohydrodynamic wave Alfvén wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 234
Take a static uniform fluid of mass density ρ>0, constant gas pressure, and background magnetic field B0​=B0​ez​ with B0​>0. Use SI units, vacuum permeability μ0​, zero electrical resistivity and zero viscosity. Write B=B0​+b⊥​(z,t) and u=u⊥​(z,t), with both disturbances perpendicular to ez​. Retain only linear order in their amplitudes. There is no background fluid flow, so the linear material derivative is ∂t​.
The continuity equation has ∇⋅u⊥​=0, hence ∂t​δρ=0; choosing no initial mass density fluctuation gives δρ=0. For this shear mode the gas pressure fluctuation is also zero. In the ideal magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation, the Lorentz force density can be written
μ0​(∇×B)×B​=μ0​(B⋅∇)B​−∇2μ0​B2​.
(11)
Because B0​⋅b⊥​=0, the perturbed magnetic pressure starts at quadratic order. The surviving transverse magnetic tension gives the first equation below. Expanding the ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation gives the second:
ρ∂t​u⊥​=μ0​B0​​∂z​b⊥​,∂t​b⊥​=B0​∂z​u⊥​.
(12)
Differentiate the first in time and substitute the second. Both velocity and magnetic field disturbances obey the one-dimensional wave equation:
∂t2​u⊥​=vA2​∂z2​u⊥​,vA​=μ0​ρ​B0​​.
(13)
For a plane wave proportional to ei(k∥​z−ωt), this gives ω2=k∥2​vA2​, or ω=±k∥​vA​. The magnetic and velocity amplitudes satisfy b⊥​=−(B0​k∥​/ω)u⊥​: they have opposite signs for propagation along +ez​ and equal signs for propagation along −ez​. The general uniform-background shear polarization of waves, perpendicular to both k and B0​, gives the same dispersion relation with k∥​=k⋅ez​. Its group velocity is ±vA​ez​, whereas its phase velocity normal to an oblique wavefront has magnitude vA​∣k∥​∣/∣k∣.
