Bogomolny degree bound for the O3 sigma model (E≥4π∣Q∣)

For E=21​∫(∣∂1​n∣2+∣∂2​n∣2), completing the square gives E=21​∫∣∂1​n±n×∂2​n∣2±4πQ, hence E≥4π∣Q∣. Saturation yields the Bogomolny equations of the sigma-model lump. With the oriented stereographic projection n=(2Rew,2Imw,1−∣w∣2)/(1+∣w∣2), holomorphic rational maps have positive degree and saturate the bound; antiholomorphic maps have negative degree.
