For per-capita birth and death rates B and D, the rates are bn​=Bn and dn​=Dn. If μ=ENt​ and σ2=varNt​, then
μ′=(B−D)μ,(σ2)′=2(B−D)σ2+(B+D)μ.
(81)
The expected value sees only the net growth rate B−D, whereas the variance also sees the total event rate B+D.
