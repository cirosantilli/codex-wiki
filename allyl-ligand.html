Allyl ligand (η3-C3​H5−​)

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry Ligand in coordination chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
An allyl ligand can bind as η3 C3​H5−​, donating four electrons in ionic electron counting. Protonation can release an alkene and make coordination sites available.
