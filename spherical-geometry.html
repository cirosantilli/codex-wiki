Spherical geometry

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 474 Articles: 9
Spherical geometry studies distances and angles intrinsically on a sphere. On the unit sphere, the shorter great circle arc between non-antipodal points with unit position vectors u,v has length arccos(u⋅v).
