Cas9

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Genetic engineering CRISPR genome editing
OurBigBook.com Words: 47 Articles: 1
A guide-directed DNA nuclease. Target recognition depends on a guide RNA and an appropriate protospacer adjacent motif; cleavage is followed by cellular repair, so cleavage alone does not specify the final allele.
