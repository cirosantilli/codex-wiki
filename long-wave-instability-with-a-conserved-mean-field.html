Long-wave instability with a conserved mean field

OurBigBook.com Words: 78
Putting λ=l2ν into the sideband cubic and retaining l4 gives
rν2−[(μ−σ−1)r+2q2]ν−[(μ−σ)r+2σq2]=0.
(28)
For r=1−q2>0, μ/σ>(1−3q2)/(1−q2) makes the constant coefficient negative. There is a positive real ν, so sufficiently small nonzero l grows. On a finite period L, however, only l=2πj/L are allowed. For q=0, μ=2, σ=1, L=1, every nonzero allowed mode has negative growth rates despite this long-wave inequality. Thus an arbitrarily-small-wave-number conclusion cannot be asserted without the domain-size qualification.
