Relative volatility (α=x/(1−x)y/(1−y)​)

 Home Chemistry Distillation
OurBigBook.com Words: 311 Articles: 1
For a two-component mixture, let x be component A's mole fraction in the liquid and y its mole fraction in the coexisting vapor. At interior compositions, define the relative volatility of A with respect to B by the ratio of composition odds:
α=x/(1−x)y/(1−y)​.
(34)
Thus α>1 means A is enriched in the vapor, while α=1 means y=x. Solving this definition gives
αx(1−y)=y(1−x),y=1+(α−1)xαx​.
(35)
Here a constant relative volatility is a stipulated model of vapor-liquid equilibrium, not a measured property of a named chemical solution. Real relative volatility can vary with composition, temperature, and pressure. The relation specifies no absolute temperature scale: even fixing α=2 does not determine a boiling-temperature curve or a heat requirement.
