Cyclopentadienyl ligand (Cp−)

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry Ligand in coordination chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 2
The cyclopentadienyl ligand is based on C5​H5−​. In its common η5 mode it binds through five ring carbons and donates six electrons in ionic electron counting. Ring substitution can create distinct NMR environments.
