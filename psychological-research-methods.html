Psychological research methods

 Home Science Psychology
OurBigBook.com Words: 939 Articles: 27
Methods for defining questions, obtaining observations and evaluating claims in psychology. Design of experiments can identify effects of assigned conditions; an observational study measures existing variation. Qualitative research methods examine meanings and processes in context. The method must fit the question and the intended inference.
