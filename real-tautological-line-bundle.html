Real tautological line bundle (γn​)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Tautological bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
Over Real projective space RPn, the real tautological line bundle is
γn​={(ℓ,v):v∈ℓ}⊆RPn×Rn+1.
(51)
On the standard chart Ui​={[x]:xi​=0}, let si​([x])=x/xi​. A local trivialization sends ([x],t) to ([x],tsi​([x])), with inverse ([x],v)↦([x],vi​). These maps prove that this is a real line bundle. Its unit sphere bundle is the antipodal map quotient Sn→RPn. Its mod-two Euler class of a real line bundle is the degree-one generator of the mod-two cohomology ring of real projective space.
