Invariant bilinear-form construction of a Yang-Mills action

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Yang-Mills theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
If a Lie algebra has an invariant symmetric nondegenerate form h, its curvature F transforms in the Adjoint representation and L=−h(Fμν​,Fμν)/4 is gauge invariant. Infinitesimal invariance follows from h([θ,X],Y)+h(X,[θ,Y])=0. For compact generators with tr(TaTb)=δab/2, this gives −tr(Fμν​Fμν)/2=−Fμνa​Faμν/4. An arbitrary index contraction is not gauge invariant for an arbitrary Lie algebra.
