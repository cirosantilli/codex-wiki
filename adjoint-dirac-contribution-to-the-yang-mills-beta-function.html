Adjoint Dirac contribution to the Yang-Mills beta function (βDirac,ad​(g)=4CA​g3/[3(4π)2])

One adjoint Dirac spinor contributes the displayed screening term to the four-dimensional renormalization-group beta function in the modified minimal subtraction scheme. The fermion contribution to the gluon self-energy insertion has pole −8g2CA​(k2δμν​−kμ​kν​)/(3ϵ(4π)2). The corresponding background gauge-field counterterm is δZA​=−8g2CA​/(3ϵ(4π)2); the background gauge-field renormalization identity Zg​=ZA−1/2​ gives g0​=μϵ/2(g+4CA​g3/[3ϵ(4π)2]). Differentiation at fixed g0​ yields the screening coefficient. The mass-independent subtraction includes massive fields until effective-theory threshold matching is performed.
