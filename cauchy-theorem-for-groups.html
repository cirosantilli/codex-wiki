Cauchy theorem for groups

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 144 Articles: 1
If a prime divides the order of a finite group, the group contains an element of that prime order.
One proof lets a cyclic group of order p rotate the tuples
(x1​,…,xp​)withx1​⋯xp​=1.
(10)
The tuple set has size ∣G∣p−1, and its fixed tuples are precisely (x,…,x) with xp=1. Counting nonfixed orbits modulo p forces a nonidentity fixed tuple.
