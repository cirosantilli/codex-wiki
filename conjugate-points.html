Conjugate points

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Geodesic
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
Two points along a geodesic are conjugate when a nonzero Jacobi field along it vanishes at both points. This detects singularity of the geodesic exponential map and governs loss of local length minimization.
