Discriminant ideal (disc(S/R))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Field trace Trace pairing
OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 2
For the integral closure S of a Dedekind domain R in a finite separable extension, the discriminant ideal is locally generated by
det(TrL/K​(αi​αj​)),
(54)
where (αi​) is a local R-basis of S. Intrinsically it is the image of (detR​S)⊗2 under the determinant of the trace pairing.
