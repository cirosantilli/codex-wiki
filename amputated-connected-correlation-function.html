Amputated connected correlation function

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Correlation function Connected correlation function
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
An amputated connected correlation function is obtained by removing external propagators. Amputation using full propagators eliminates external self-energy decorations; internal exchange diagrams remain. It is distinct from a one-particle-irreducible correlation function. In a scalar theory with vanishing one-point function, at zero momentum the four-point quantity satisfies A4​=−Γ(4)+3[Γ(3)]2/Γ(2) with a convention in which connected diagram vertices are minus effective-action derivatives. For a constant scalar source, the Legendre relation J=Γ′(ϕ) gives G′′=1/Γ′′ and G′′′′=−Γ′′′′/(Γ′′)4+3(Γ′′′)2/(Γ′′)5. Dividing by the four full external propagators proves the formula.
