Zero-temperature correlation of an odd antiferromagnetic Ising ring (Cn​=(−1)n(1−2n/N))

 ... Statistical physics Spin model Ising model Antiferromagnetic Ising model Antiferromagnetic Ising ring Finite-ring antiferromagnetic Ising correlation
OurBigBook.com Words: 80
At fixed odd N, the zero-temperature canonical ensemble equally weights the 2N ground states of an antiferromagnetic Ising ring. The single frustrated bond in an antiferromagnetic Ising ring lies on a chosen n-bond path with probability n/N. The Ising spin product is (−1)n if the path excludes that bond and −(−1)n if it includes it, proving the displayed two-point correlation function. The loss of staggered alignment upon averaging persists at zero temperature and is caused by geometric frustration, rather than a positive density of thermal defects. The expression remains symmetric under n↦N−n for odd N.
