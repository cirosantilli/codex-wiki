Electromagnetic matching for an alpha-Omega interface (A=a,Az​=az​,B=b,ηBz​=ηbz​)

 ... Star Sun Solar magnetic field Solar dynamo Interface solar dynamo Equal-diffusivity interface dynamo
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
For a planar interface, equal permeability and no singular sheet current make all magnetic components continuous. A nonzero horizontal Fourier wavenumber then gives the first three conditions in the title. Continuity of the tangential electric field supplies the last: continuous mean velocity cancels its advective contribution, and the retained alpha mean-field electromotive force is along the toroidal direction, so the other tangential electric component is −U∂x​A−η∂z​B. The given alpha-Omega equations retain only toroidal-to-poloidal alpha generation; adding a full isotropic alpha contribution would alter both the bulk equation and the matching problem.
