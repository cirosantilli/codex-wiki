Top-hat plume model

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulent plume
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 42
A top-hat plume model takes velocity and reduced gravity to be uniform across the plume and zero outside it. Integral volume, momentum, and buoyancy fluxes then depend only on plume width and these uniform values.
