The endpoint Laplace method for a Legendre polynomial ceases to localize when nx−1​=O(1). On the scale x=1+ν/n2, its real integral representation gives

The Modified Bessel function of the first kind equals one at ν=0 and has large-ν behavior e2ν​/2π2ν​​, matching the Hyperbolic Debye asymptotic for Legendre polynomials. This boundary layer resolves an increasingly steep function at an endpoint where its value remains fixed.
