Scheme

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space
OurBigBook.com Words: 8k Articles: 172
A scheme is a locally ringed space covered by open subsets isomorphic to spectra of commutative rings. It retains both the points defined by prime ideals and the local algebra of functions around them.
