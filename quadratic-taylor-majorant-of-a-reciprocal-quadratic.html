Quadratic Taylor majorant of a reciprocal quadratic (4x2−4x+5​−1+x21​=4(1+x2)(x−1)4​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Taylor polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
The quadratic Taylor polynomial of f(x)=1/(1+x2) at x=1 is p(x)=(x2−4x+5)/4. Multiplying p−f by 4(1+x2) gives (x−1)4, proving the exact displayed identity. Thus p≥f for every real x, with equality only at one. In particular, a claim that f>p to the right of one has the wrong sign. The Lagrange remainder also proves this direction there, since f′′′(x)=24x(1−x2)/(1+x2)4<0 for x>1. The exact identity is stronger: it controls the sign globally and shows the fourth-order contact at the expansion point.
