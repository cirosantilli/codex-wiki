Local Artin reciprocity

Local Artin reciprocity gives a continuous homomorphism ArtK​:K×→Gal(Kab/K) with dense image. For every finite abelian extension L/K, it induces an isomorphism
K×/NL/K​(L×)≅Gal(L/K).
