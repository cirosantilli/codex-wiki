Random sum of independent claims (ES=ENEX)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Aggregate claims model
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 22
For independent claim sizes with common expected value m and variance v, independent of the nonnegative integer count N, the aggregate satisfies ES=mEN and Var(S)=vEN+m2Var(N). Its moment-generating function is GN​(MX​(t)) wherever finite. These identities follow from the law of total expectation and law of total variance.
