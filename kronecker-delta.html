Kronecker delta (δij​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 22
The Kronecker delta is δij​=1 when i=j and zero otherwise. It is the component matrix of the identity map in a basis.
