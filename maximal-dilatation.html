Maximal dilatation (K(f)=1−∥μf​∥∞​1+∥μf​∥∞​​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Riemann surfaces Quasiconformal mapping
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
The essential supremum of the ratio of the largest to the smallest infinitesimal singular value of a quasiconformal map. It equals (1+∥μf​∥∞​)/(1−∥μf​∥∞​) in terms of the Beltrami coefficient.
