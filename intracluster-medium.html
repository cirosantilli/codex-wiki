Intracluster medium

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Galaxy cluster
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
The intracluster medium is diffuse hot plasma between the galaxies of a galaxy cluster. Its X-ray radiation includes thermal bremsstrahlung and atomic line emission. Measured temperature and density profiles supply a cluster hydrostatic mass estimator when approximately thermal hydrostatic equilibrium holds. The medium supplies much of a cluster's baryonic mass, but this need not equal its total gravitating mass.
