Diatomic molecule

 Home Chemistry Molecule Linear molecule
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 16
A diatomic molecule contains two chemical atoms; hydrogen chloride and carbon monoxide are examples. Their fixed-separation moment of inertia is I=μr2 in the linear rigid rotor model.
