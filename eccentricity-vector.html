Eccentricity vector (e)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Celestial mechanics Kepler orbit Orbital eccentricity
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
For an attractive Kepler orbit with gravitational parameter GM, velocity v and specific angular momentum h=r×v, the eccentricity vector is
e=GMv×h​−r.
(45)
Its magnitude is the orbital eccentricity, and it points toward periapsis for a noncircular orbit. It is a normalized attractive Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector; it remains constant under the pure inverse-square force.
