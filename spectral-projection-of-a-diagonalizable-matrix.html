Spectral projection of a diagonalizable matrix (Pλ​=∏μ=λ​(A−μI)/(λ−μ))

 ... Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Eigenvalue Diagonalizable matrix Spectral decomposition
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For a diagonalizable matrix with distinct eigenvalues λ,μ,…, this polynomial is the projection operator onto the eigenspace for λ along the direct sum of the other eigenspaces. It is one on its own eigenspace and zero on the others. Consequently Pλ​Pμ​=0 for different eigenvalues, ∑Pλ​=I and A=∑λPλ​. These projections need not be orthogonal projections; that stronger property holds for a normal matrix. This is the polynomial interpolation form of the spectral decomposition.
