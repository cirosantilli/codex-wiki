The observations, the sample mean and the confidence interval all have different meanings. The bar estimates the population mean; it is not an interval intended to contain 95% of individual observations. In repeated independent samples of size five from the assumed normal distribution, 95% of intervals constructed using the exact t quantile cover the same fixed μ. This is not a 95% posterior probability that the fixed statistical parameter lies in this particular realized interval. Such a probability requires a separate Bayesian inference model.
