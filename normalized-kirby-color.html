Normalized Kirby color (Ω=D−1∑a​da​a)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Colored link Kirby color
OurBigBook.com Words: 201 Articles: 3
The Kirby color divided by the total quantum dimension. A zero-framed closed loop with this color evaluates to D in the isotopy normalization of anyon diagrams. An encircling loop acts on a simple charge x by Dδx1​, the Kirby killing property. Normalization must be tracked explicitly when using a surgery formula.
