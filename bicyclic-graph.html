Bicyclic graph (∣E∣=∣V∣+1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Graph excess
OurBigBook.com Words: 209 Articles: 2
A connected bicyclic graph has two independent cycles, equivalently excess one. Its 2-core has the same excess and every removed vertex lies in a tree attached to the core. The graph kernel captures the finite collection of possible branching structures.
