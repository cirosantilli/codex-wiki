Carrier density in a graphene cone (n=sgn(EF​)EF2​/[π(ℏvF​)2])

Assume zero temperature, the linear Dirac cone, and both real-spin states and both electronic valleys. Define n as excess electron number per unit area, negative for an excess of electron holes, relative to neutrality. A filled circular region of radius kF​ in wave vector space contains
∣n∣=4(2π)2πkF2​​=πkF2​​,∣EF​∣=ℏvF​kF​.
(23)
Thus the excess carrier density grows quadratically with the Fermi energy, unlike the constant density of states of a parabolic two-dimensional band. Differentiating gives the positive density of states per unit area, D(E)=2∣E∣/[π(ℏvF​)2]. Both the geometry of occupied states and the fourfold degeneracy matter to the coefficient.
