Lewis acid-base adduct

 Home Chemistry Acid-base chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 38 Articles: 1
A Lewis acid-base adduct forms when an electron-pair donor binds an acceptor through a coordinate covalent bond. A ring nitrogen can bind GaCl3​ without displacement of a chlorine substituent from phosphorus.
