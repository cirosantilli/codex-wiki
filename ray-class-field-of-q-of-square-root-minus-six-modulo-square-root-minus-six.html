Ray class field of Q of square root minus six modulo square root minus six (K(−6​)​=Q(2​,−3​))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Class field theory Existence theorem of global class field theory Ray class field
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
For K=Q(−6​), reduction of Z[−6​] modulo (−6​) gives Z/6Z. Its unit group is covered by the units {1,−1} of K. The ray class exact sequence therefore identifies the ray class group with the ordinary ideal class group, of order two. Consequently this ray class field is the Hilbert class field of Q of square root minus six. To verify its lack of ramification, the integral basis candidate 1,2​,b,2​b, where b=(1+−3​)/2, has discriminant 82(−3)2=576. The relative discriminant formula also forces the field discriminant to be divisible by (−24)2=576. Equality proves that the relative discriminant ideal is trivial.
