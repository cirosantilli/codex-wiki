Sparse norm preservation from entrywise Gram control

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Inner product Gram matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
If E=ATA/d−I has entrywise maximum norm at most t, every vector z supported on at most s coordinates satisfies ∣zTEz∣≤t∥z∥12​≤st∥z∥22​. Thus the squared norm distortion is at most st on all such sparse vectors. Use the quadratic inequality at z=0, where a ratio would be undefined.
