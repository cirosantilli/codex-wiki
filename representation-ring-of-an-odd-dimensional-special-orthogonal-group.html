Representation ring of an odd-dimensional special orthogonal group (R(SO(2m+1))≅Z[z1±1​,…,zm±1​](Z/2)m⋊Sm​)

For the block-rotation maximal torus in the special orthogonal group SO(2m+1), the Weyl group acts by permutations and independent inversions of zi​. Its invariant Laurent polynomials are the symmetric polynomials in xi​=zi​+zi−1​, hence the polynomial ring in their elementary symmetric polynomials e1​,…,em​. Restriction of the group representation ring of a compact group is injective because every group element is conjugate into the torus and characters distinguish virtual representations. For the defining complexified representation V, the exterior-power characters satisfy ∑k​χΛkV​tk=(1+t)∏i​(1+xi​t+t2). For k≤m, its coefficient is ek​ plus an integral linear combination of ej​ for j<k. Induction puts every ek​ in the restriction image, proving equality with the invariant ring and giving polynomial generators [Λ1V],…,[ΛmV].
