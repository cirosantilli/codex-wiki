Climate sensitivity (ΔTeq​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Climate change Climate model
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
Climate sensitivity quantifies the equilibrium temperature response to a specified change in forcing. Distinguish the response to doubled carbon dioxide from the gain per unit radiative forcing: for a linear net restoring coefficient λ>0 and constant forcing F0​, the gain is 1/λ, whereas the equilibrium warming is ΔTeq​=F0​/λ. The forcing convention and the climate feedbacks permitted to adjust are part of the definition. A transient warming before the ocean heat content has adjusted is a different quantity.
