Yoneda embedding (y:C→[Cop,Set])

 ... Category theory Category Functor Functor category Representable functor Yoneda lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 179 Articles: 3
The Yoneda embedding sends A to C(−,A). The Yoneda lemma identifies natural transformations yA→yB with morphisms A→B, so the embedding is full and faithful. It preserves every limit that exists in C.
