Saturating closed-box enrichment law (Zg​=1−μY)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy evolution Galactic chemical evolution Closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
A finite-metal-fraction variant of the closed-box model of galactic chemical evolution uses effective stellar yield Y(1−Zg​), rather than constant yield. With pristine initial gas and Y>0, metal accounting gives dZg​/dlog(1/μ)=Y(1−Zg​) and hence Zg​=1−μY. The mass-weighted mean stellar metallicity is [Y−(Y+1)μ+μY+1]/[(Y+1)(1−μ)]. At final gas fraction μf​, the metallicity distribution function is (1−z)1/Y−1/[Y(1−μf​)] on 0<z<1−μfY​. For trace abundances, Zg​≃−Ylogμ and the usual closed-box metallicity distribution is recovered to leading order.
