Fluorescence instrument response (R(t))

OurBigBook.com Words: 183
A fluorescence instrument response is the distribution of recorded delays for an effectively prompt optical signal. Finite excitation-pulse width and timing variation in the photodetector and electronics contribute. With a normalized response R, intrinsic fluorescent photon rate I, and background rate B, the expected trace is
M(t)=∫−∞∞​R(u)I(t−u)du+B(t),∫−∞∞​R(u)du=1.
(4)
Fit a candidate decay after this convolution with the measured response; treating the broadened leading edge as the intrinsic decay can bias fluorescence lifetime. The accessible technical note describes measuring prompt scattered excitation with a nonfluorescent scattering sample.[ref]
