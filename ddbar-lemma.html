ddbar lemma (∂∂ˉ-lemma)

On a compact Kähler manifold, a pure-type differential form that is d-closed and is either ∂-exact or ∂ˉ-exact is ∂∂ˉ-exact. In particular, if η is ∂ˉ-exact and ∂η=0, then
η=∂ˉ∂ϕ
(354)
for a form ϕ of bidegree one lower in each component.
