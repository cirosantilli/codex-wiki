Reaction intermediate

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Chemical kinetics Reaction mechanism
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
A reaction intermediate is formed in one step of a reaction mechanism and consumed in a later step, cancelling from the net chemical equation. A reaction intermediate can connect the transformations in energy coupling; it is distinct from a transition state. See the IUPAC Gold Book: intermediate.
