Inverse-at-infinity criterion for local boundedness of a mapping-out function

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Schramm–Loewner evolution Compact H-hull Mapping-out function of a compact H-hull
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
The inverse of a hydrodynamically normalized mapping-out function has expansion f(w)=w+O(1/w) at infinity. If bounded-domain points zn​ had unbounded images wn​=g(zn​), then zn​=f(wn​)=wn​+O(1/wn​) would be unbounded, a contradiction. Thus g is bounded even near finite rough boundary points. Its own expansion at infinity then makes g(z)−z uniformly bounded throughout the domain. Reflection is needed only near infinity, not along the entire hull boundary.
