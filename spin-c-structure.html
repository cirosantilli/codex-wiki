Spin-c structure (Spinc)

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Riemannian manifold Spin manifold Spin structure
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
A Spin-c structure on an oriented rank-n real vector bundle is a lift of its oriented frame bundle to Spinc(n)=(Spin(n)×U(1))/{(1,1),(−1,−1)}. Its determinant line bundle is associated with [s,z]↦z2; its First Chern class reduces modulo two to the second Stiefel–Whitney class. When such structures exist, their isomorphism classes form an affine space over H2; tensoring by a complex line bundle of first Chern class h changes the determinant class by 2h. On a closed oriented three-manifold they always exist. On a four-manifold, the determinant class is characteristic: ⟨c1​(s),v⟩≡v⋅v(mod2).
