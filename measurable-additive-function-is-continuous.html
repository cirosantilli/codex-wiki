Measurable additive function is continuous

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional equation Cauchy's functional equation Additive function
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
Every Lebesgue-measurable additive function f:Rn→Rm is continuous. Small-value preimages are measurable; one of their integer dilates has positive measure, so the Steinhaus theorem puts a neighbourhood of zero in their difference set. Additivity then makes f small on that neighbourhood.
