For a photon in ds2=a2[−dτ2+(δij​+hij​)dxidxj], the null geodesic condition cancels the homogeneous 2Hp0 term in the derivative of q=a2p0. The remaining first-order geodesic equation gives the displayed momentum change. Its direction is constant at zeroth order, so first-order redshift and brightness sources can be evaluated on the unperturbed ray; direction deflection multiplied by an already first-order anisotropy is second order.
