Comet energy diffusion (x=1/a)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Planetary system dynamics Planetary scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 190 Articles: 1
Model successive uncorrelated planetary kicks as a random walk in inverse semi-major axis x=1/a, proportional to negative specific orbital energy. If the root mean square kick per periapsis passage is sx​ and the initial value is x0​, the characteristic number of passages to reach the escape scale is N∼(x0​/sx​)2. For sx​=10Mp​/(ap​M⋆​) and passage interval P(a0​),
tej​∼100P(a0​)​(a0​ap​​)2(Mp​M⋆​​)2.
(14)
The quantity sx​ has inverse-length units, not the units of a variance-per-time diffusion coefficient. With ⟨(Δx)2⟩=2Dx​t, that coefficient is Dx​=sx2​/(2P). This estimate assumes many independent encounters; changing orbital periods and resonant correlations can invalidate the clock. It is not an exact mean first-passage time.
