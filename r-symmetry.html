R-symmetry

 Home Physics Branches of physics Supersymmetry Supersymmetric gauge theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 124 Articles: 4
An R-symmetry acts nontrivially on the supersymmetry generators. In four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry, the superspace coordinate θ has R-charge one, so a superpotential must have R-charge two.
