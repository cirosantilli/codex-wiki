Charge carrier mobility (μ=∣q∣τ/m)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electric charge Electric current Current density Charge carrier
OurBigBook.com Words: 147 Articles: 3
Charge carrier mobility is the magnitude of steady drift velocity per unit weak electric field in a scalar transport model. In the Drude model, vd​=sgn(q)μE and σ=n∣q∣μ. The positive charge carrier mobility separates ease of motion from the sign of electric charge; its SI unit is m2/(Vs).
