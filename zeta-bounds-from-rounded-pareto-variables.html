Zeta bounds from rounded Pareto variables (1/(s−1)≤ζ(s)≤s/(s−1))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Continuous probability distribution Pareto distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
Let X have survival function P(X≥x)=x−s for x≥1, with s>1. Its expected value is s/(s−1). The tail-sum formula for expectation gives
E[⌊X⌋]=∑k≥1​k−s=ζ(s).
(20)
The absolutely continuous probability distribution gives zero mass to the integers, so ⌈X⌉=⌊X⌋+1 almost surely and E[⌈X⌉]=ζ(s)+1. Taking expectations of ⌊X⌋≤X≤⌈X⌉ gives ζ(s)≤s/(s−1)≤ζ(s)+1, which rearranges to the displayed bounds on the Riemann zeta function. Finiteness is justified by ⌊X⌋≤X and ⌈X⌉≤X+1.
