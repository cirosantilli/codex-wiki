Supportive Parenting for Anxious Childhood Emotions

 ... Science Psychology Clinical psychology Child and adolescent clinical psychology Youth anxiety treatment Parent involvement in youth anxiety treatment
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A parent-based treatment that targets accommodation and supportive responses to a child's anxiety. A randomised trial compared it with child CBT using prespecified noninferiority criteria; the tested age range and margins constrain the conclusion.[ref]
