Quantum enveloping algebra of sl2 (Uq​(sl2​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Hopf algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 139 Articles: 2
Over a field with q=0 and q2=1, this Hopf algebra has generators E,F,K,K−1 and relations KE=q2EK, KF=q−2FK and EF−FE=(K−K−1)/(q−q−1). One comultiplication convention is ΔE=E⊗K+1⊗E, ΔF=F⊗1+K−1⊗F and ΔK=K⊗K. Its counit takes E,F to zero and K to one. The antipode takes E to −EK−1, F to −KF and K to K−1.
