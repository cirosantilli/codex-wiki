Sagnac fringe shift worked example (N≃4Ω⋅A/(cλ0​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Sagnac effect Sagnac area law
OurBigBook.com Words: 302
For a stationary rotating apparatus driven by a source with optical coherence and proper frequency ν0​, define λ0​=c/ν0​. The source wave phase advances by 2πν0​Δτ between the emission times of the two contributions that meet at one readout spacetime event. Define the signed phase lag Δϕ of the + contribution and the corresponding displacement N in units of one interference fringe by
Δϕ=2πν0​Δτ,N=2πΔϕ​≃cλ0​4Ω⋅A​.
(45)
The sign of a photographed spatial displacement also depends on the imposed fringe orientation; this definition fixes the theoretical sign through the delayed beam. In an equal-amplitude output, a representative light intensity is I(x)=Imax​[1+cos(Kx−Δϕ)]/2, where K sets the chosen spatial interference fringe spacing 2π/K. Moving Δϕ by 2π shifts the pattern by one fringe. A fixed output exactly at a bright or dark fringe has zero first-order intensity slope, so a known phase bias or a spatial fringe readout is useful for determining a small signed delay.
Choose a square of side 0.30m, a rectangle 0.40m by 0.20m, λ0​=633nm and Ω=+10rads−1, with angular velocity parallel to the positive normal vectors. Both perimeters are 1.20m, but the areas are 0.090m2 and 0.080m2. Using c=299792458ms−1, the leading predictions are
Δtsquare​Δtrectangle​​≃4.006×10−17s,≃3.560×10−17s,​Nsquare​Nrectangle​​≃0.0189705,≃0.0168626.​
(46)
These are original model choices and predictions, not measured points. The square has 9/8 times the response despite the equal path lengths. Reversing from +Ω to −Ω gives N(−Ω)−N(+Ω)=−2N(+Ω): the displacement magnitude between opposite rotations is twice the displacement magnitude from rest. Confusing these two comparisons introduces a factor-of-two error.
