Saddle-node bifurcation on an invariant circle (T∼π/η​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Bifurcation theory Saddle-node bifurcation
OurBigBook.com Words: 151 Articles: 1
When a saddle-node lies on a returning invariant circle, removing the equilibria creates passage around a periodic orbit through a slow bottleneck. For x˙=x2+η with η>0, crossing a fixed interval [−h,h] takes 2arctan(h/η​)/η​∼π/η​. If the remaining orbit takes bounded time, this is also its leading period. Strong transverse contraction makes the newborn periodic orbit attracting. This global saddle-node mechanism differs from a local fold occurring away from an already existing cycle.
