For a faithful representation of C(K) by integration against a regular projection-valued measure P, every nonempty open subset U satisfies P(U)=0. Choose a nonzero continuous function supported inside U, using compact Hausdorff normality. If P(U)=0, its spectral integral vanishes, contradicting faithfulness. Regularity is understood through the scalar spectral measures.
