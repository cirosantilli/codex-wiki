Electrical impedance (Z(ω)=V/I)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 3
Electrical impedance is the ratio of voltage and current complex amplitudes for sinusoidal steady response at a specified angular frequency. With time dependence eiωt, an ideal resistor, inductor and capacitor have ZR​=R, ZL​=iωL and ZC​=1/(iωC). Their electrical impedances add in a series RLC circuit, giving Z=R+i(ωL−1/(ωC)). The current amplitude is V0​/∣Z∣, so cancellation of the imaginary terms explains the resonant series RLC response. [ref].
