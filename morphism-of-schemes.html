Morphism of schemes

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 5k Articles: 102
A morphism of schemes is a morphism of locally ringed spaces. On affine schemes it is contravariantly equivalent to a homomorphism of their coordinate rings.
