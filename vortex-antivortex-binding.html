Vortex-antivortex binding

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Lower critical dimension Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless transition
OurBigBook.com Words: 159
In the phase with quasi-long-range order, vortex defects of opposite winding remain bound in pairs. Their unbinding produces the disordered phase with a finite correlation length.
For two unit vortices of opposite charge separated by R, the vortex Coulomb gas gives E+−​(R)≃2Ec​+2πρs​log(R/a) before screening. At distances much greater than R, their phase gradients cancel at leading order: the pair field decays as R/r2, whereas a lone phase vortex has a 1/r gradient. A neutral pair therefore has finite energy as L→∞ at fixed R. Bound does not mean absent: small pairs remain thermally excited and reduce the phase stiffness through their response to a twist.
