Antisymmetry ((xRy and yRx)⟹x=y)

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Finitary relation Binary relation Antisymmetric relation
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
The property of a binary relation that opposite directed relationships between two elements can hold only when those elements are equal. It does not require every pair to be comparable and does not prohibit a longer directed cycle whose reverse edges are initially absent. Taking reflexive transitive closure can therefore destroy this property.
