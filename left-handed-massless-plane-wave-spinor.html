Left-handed massless plane-wave spinor (λL​(p)=N(0χ−​(p​)​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 70
In the standard Weyl representation of the gamma matrices and mostly-minus metric signature, the left chirality component obeys i(∂t​−σ⋅∇)χL​=0. A plane wave with p0>0 exists precisely on the massless mass shell p0=∣p∣. Its coefficient is a negative helicity eigenspinor. For direction angles ϑ,φ, one normalized choice is
χ−​=(−e−iφsin(ϑ/2)cos(ϑ/2)​),(σ⋅p​)χ−​=−χ−​.
(7)
The nonzero solution is unique up to overall normalization and phase. Different patches are required for a continuous phase choice over all directions; the direction of zero momentum is undefined.
