Rational principal part (Rat(F)/FP​)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Rational section of a sheaf of modules
OurBigBook.com Words: 242 Articles: 2
For a locally free sheaf on an irreducible variety, the stalk inclusion identifies sections regular near P inside its rational sections. The quotient records the obstruction to regularity at that point. This definition does not assume a nonsingular point or a discrete valuation parameter.
