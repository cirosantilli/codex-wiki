Odd antiferromagnetic Ising ring (g0​=2N,E0​=−(N−2)J)

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Spin model Ising model Antiferromagnetic Ising model Antiferromagnetic Ising ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
An odd periodic nearest-neighbour Ising model with antiferromagnetic coupling has unavoidable geometrical frustration. For k parallel bonds, H=−NJ+2Jk, while ∏j​Sj​Sj+1​=1 forces N−k to be even. Thus odd N requires at least one parallel bond. A ground state has exactly one, with N possible locations and two global spin inversion symmetry choices, proving the displayed ground-state degeneracy and ground-state energy. The zero-temperature canonical ensemble distributes that defect uniformly. A directed arc of n bonds contains it with probability n/N, giving two-point correlation function (−1)n(1−2n/N). The finite antiferromagnetic Ising-ring correlation gives the corresponding positive-temperature result.
