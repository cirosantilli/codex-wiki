Molar heat capacity (Cm​=C/n)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Heat Heat capacity
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
Molar heat capacity is heat capacity divided by amount of substance n, with units Jmol−1K−1. Its constraint must still be specified: CV,m​=CV​/n and Cp,m​=Cp​/n. For the classical monatomic gas example in heat capacity, these are 3R/2 and 5R/2, where R is the molar gas constant. Some textbooks use CV​ and Cp​ for these per-mole quantities; the convention here reserves them for the whole sample.
