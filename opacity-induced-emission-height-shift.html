Opacity-induced emission-height shift (Δz=Hlna)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Radiative transfer
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
If optical depth is τ(z)=τs​e−z/H, multiplying opacity by a raises any fixed-optical-depth surface by Hlna. In a constant lapse-rate layer this changes its temperature by −ΓHlna.
