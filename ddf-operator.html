DDF operator ([Ami​,Anj​]=mδijδm+n,0​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Covariant quantization of the bosonic string
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
DDF operators are transverse physical string oscillators constructed as contour integrals of weight-one string vertex operators. Choose a null reference momentum q and transverse polarizations ϵi​ orthogonal to it and to a reference on-shell momentum. The integrand is proportional to ϵi​⋅∂Xeinq⋅X; its normalization and q⋅p are fixed so the operators obey the oscillator algebra in the title and commute with the positive-mode Virasoro constraints. For an open-string tachyon seed, one can use 2α′q⋅p=1. Acting with negative modes shifts momentum and raises oscillator level while preserving the physical mass-shell condition. Their transverse Fock space has positive norm and makes the physical content of the no-ghost theorem for the critical bosonic string explicit.
