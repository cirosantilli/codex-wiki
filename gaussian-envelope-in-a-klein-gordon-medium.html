Rigid translation requires ∣β∣tσ2≪1. The quadratic approximation can allow that quantity to be order one, but still requires the accumulated neglected cubic wave phase to be small over the appreciable spectrum, for example (t/6)sup∣q∣≤3σ​∣ω′′′(k0​+q)∣(3σ)3≪1. This sufficient check controls only the stated three-width spectral window; the Gaussian tails must also be negligible at the required accuracy. Higher derivatives or very long times require returning to the exact spectral integral. The width L(t) is therefore a controlled local approximation; it neither predicts front velocity nor establishes a universal identity between group velocity and information velocity.
