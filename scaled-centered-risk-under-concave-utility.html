Scaled centered risk under concave utility

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Utility function Expected utility maximization
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
If U is concave and EZ=0, then
G(s)=E[U(m+sZ)]
(28)
is concave in s. By Jensen inequality, G(s)≤G(0); concavity then makes G nonincreasing on [0,∞).
