Continuous dependence of the fixed point of a uniform contraction

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fixed-point theorem Contraction mapping theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
Suppose Tλ​ are contractions of one complete metric space with a common constant k<1, and Tλ​x depends continuously on λ for each fixed x. Their fixed points xλ​ depend continuously on λ, because for a fixed parameter λ0​,
d(xλ​,xλ0​​)≤1−kd(Tλ​xλ0​​,Tλ0​​xλ0​​)​.
