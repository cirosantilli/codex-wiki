Homogenisation on a uniform layer

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial Multilinear polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 99
On the uniform layer of the Boolean cube Ωr​, every multilinear polynomial of polynomial degree at most s≤r is in the span of the degree-s square-free monomials. For T⊆[n], ∣T∣=j≤s, and xT​=∏i∈T​xi​, the identity is
xT​=(s−jr−j​)−1∑S⊇T∣S∣=s​​xS​on Ωr​.
(8)
At the characteristic vector of a set A of size r, both sides vanish if T⊈A. Otherwise exactly (s−jr−j​) summands are 1. Hence the dimension of the restricted polynomial space is at most (sn​). No assertion of independence of the spanning monomials is needed, so no condition r+s≤n is required. Unlike ordinary homogenization, this operation preserves degree bounds by using the fixed-weight evaluation domain.
