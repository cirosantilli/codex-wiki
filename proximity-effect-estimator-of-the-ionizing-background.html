Proximity-effect estimator of the ionizing background (NHI​=NHI(0)​/(1+ω),ω=ΓQ​/Γbg​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 83
In photoionization equilibrium at fixed gas density and temperature, the extra quasar photoionization rate reduces neutral-hydrogen column density by the displayed factor. Its local rate is calculated from the quasar flux, while the isotropic background rate is Γbg​=4π∫Jν​σHI​(ν)/(hν)dν. Fitting the absorption suppression versus proper distance constrains the cosmic ionizing background. For an unperturbed column-density distribution proportional to N−p with p>1, the line abundance above a fixed threshold is reduced by (1+ω)1−p. Density enhancement, quasar age, anisotropy and redshift errors must be distinguished from this ionization effect.
