Derivative of the determinant (D(det)A​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Determinant
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 2
For an invertible matrix A and any matrix H,
D(det)A​(H)=det(A)tr(A−1H).
(25)
Indeed det(A+tH)=det(A)det(I+tA−1H), and multilinearity shows that the linear coefficient of det(I+tB) is trB. At a singular matrix the derivative is instead written tr(adj(A)H); this polynomial formula holds for all A.
