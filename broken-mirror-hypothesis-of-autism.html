Broken mirror hypothesis of autism

 ... Biology Medicine Psychiatry Mental disorder Neurodevelopmental disorder Autism
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A proposal that dysfunction of action observation–execution matching explains autistic social differences. Its broad form requires direct evidence connecting the mechanism to the relevant behaviour, not merely atypical activation or an imitation score.
