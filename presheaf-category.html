Presheaf category ([Cop,Set])

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Functor Functor category
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 25
The presheaf category on a small category C is the functor category [Cop,Set]. Its limits and colimits are computed pointwise.
Each object is a presheaf on a category, and its morphisms are natural transformations.
