Minimum matching at a polytropic sonic point (minF=minG)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar evolution Stellar wind Polytropic stellar wind
OurBigBook.com Words: 59
Put y=ρr4/(γ+1) and α=4(γ−1)/(γ+1). The polytropic stellar wind equations become F(r)=Crα+GMrα−1=G(y)=γKyγ−1/(γ−1)+M˙2/(32π2y2). At a regular sonic point both derivatives vanish. Matching their minima allows the two positive-y branches to join with finite slope; unequal minima either leave a forbidden radial interval or separate the subsonic and supersonic branches. This reduces a singular differential-equation crossing to a geometric minimum comparison.
