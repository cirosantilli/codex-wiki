Rankine-Hugoniot conditions (σ[u]=[f(u)])

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation Scalar conservation law Shock wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
The Rankine-Hugoniot condition expresses conservation laws across a moving discontinuity of a weak solution. Across a discontinuity x=σt in ut​+f(u)x​=0, with left and right states u−​ and u+​, conservation requires
σ(u+​−u−​)=f(u+​)−f(u−​).
(19)
Writing [u]=u+​−u−​ and [f(u)]=f(u+​)−f(u−​) gives σ[u]=[f(u)]. The same relation holds componentwise for a system of conservation laws; in compressible flow, conservation of mass, momentum and energy supplies the jump conditions for a shock wave. Integrating the weak solution identity over a thin space-time region crossing the discontinuity yields this balance between the swept-up conserved quantity and the difference of fluxes.
