Capillary condensation

 ... Thermodynamics Thermodynamic potential Thermodynamic free energy Gibbs free energy Phase coexistence curve Kelvin equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 28
Condensation in a wetting confined geometry at a vapor pressure below planar-interface saturation. A concave liquid meniscus has lower equilibrium vapor pressure in the capillarity approximation. Gap size, contact angle and surface adsorption affect the actual onset.
