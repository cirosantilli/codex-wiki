Atmosphere

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science
OurBigBook.com Words: 231 Articles: 5
A gravitationally retained gaseous envelope of a planet. Its pressure, composition and temperature determine its radiative transfer and circulation. A pure saturated water atmosphere follows the saturation pressure along a phase coexistence curve when condensate is locally available.
