Titration blank (Vblank​)

 ... Acid-base chemistry Acid (chemistry) Weak acid Weak acid dissociation measurement Acid-base titration Titrant standardization
OurBigBook.com Words: 89
A titration blank runs the background procedure without the intended reacting sample, matching relevant water, indicator quantity and titration endpoint conditions. Its titrant demand estimates a background contribution to subtract. The inspected EPA procedure uses such a phenolphthalein blank; this is a distinct control, rather than an instruction present in Columbia's Part 3.[ref] A blank must match the relevant background to justify subtraction; it does not remove sample-specific side reactions or a mismatch between titration endpoint and equivalence point.
