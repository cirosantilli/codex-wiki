Simple linear regression

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Normal linear model Linear regression

Simple linear regression uses one predictor and an intercept, Yi​=α+βxi​+εi​, with E(εi​∣xi​)=0. The regression coefficients describe a straight conditional-mean line: α is its value at zero and β its rate of change per unit of the predictor. When zero is outside the observed range, predictor centering can make the intercept refer to a more representative predictor value.
For fixed predictors and uncorrelated mean-zero errors of common variance σ2, with Sxx​>0,
Var(β​)=Sxx​σ2​,Var(α)=σ2(n1​+Sxx​xˉ2​).
(10)
A larger predictor spread improves slope precision under this model. In the worked example Sxx​=10 and n=5; the estimated error variance is s2=RSS/(n−2)=0.8/3. A normal linear model gives the slope pivot (β​−β)/(s/Sxx​​) a Student t-distribution with n−2 degrees of freedom. The distinction between a fitted line and a reliable uncertainty statement is precisely the role of the error assumptions.
