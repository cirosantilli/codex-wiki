Covariance (Cov(X,Y))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Expected value Variance
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 41
The covariance of square-integrable random variables is
Cov(X,Y)=E[(X−EX)(Y−EY)].
(10)
It determines the variance of a difference through
Var(X−Y)=Var(X)+Var(Y)−2Cov(X,Y).
(11)
