Neglect radiation in cosmology and take pressureless matter plus a separately conserved dark energy component with pDE​=wρDE​c2, where ρ here denotes mass-equivalent energy density. The Friedmann acceleration equation gives

Thus w<−1/3 for a positive-density component is necessary for that component to accelerate expansion, but the total must also overcome pressureless matter. For a cosmological constant, w=−1 and q0​=Ωm,0​/2−ΩΛ,0​, so acceleration requires ΩΛ,0​>Ωm,0​/2. Spatial curvature of an FLRW universe enters the distance relation but has no separate term in this acceleration criterion. A Hubble diagram alone does not identify a microscopic dark energy mechanism.
