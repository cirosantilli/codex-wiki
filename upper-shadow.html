Upper shadow (∇A)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Set family shadow
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
The upper shadow of A⊆[n](r) is
∇A={B∈[n](r+1):A⊂B for some A∈A}.
(11)
Among families of fixed size, an initial lexicographic segment minimizes its upper shadow.
