Pauli eigenstate

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum circuit Pauli group Pauli operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 61 Articles: 1
One of the six pure single-qubit eigenstates of X,Y,Z. Their Bloch vectors are the six coordinate unit vectors with both signs. Their convex hull is the stabilizer octahedron.
