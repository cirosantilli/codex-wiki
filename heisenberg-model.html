Heisenberg model

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Spin
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 24
The Heisenberg model couples neighboring or more general pairs of spins through σi​⋅σj​. For all-to-all equal coupling, the Hamiltonian can be written in terms of the square of the total spin.
