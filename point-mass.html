Point mass (ρ=mδ(3)(x−z))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Mass
OurBigBook.com Words: 31
A point mass idealizes a localized body's mass density as ρ(x,t)=mδ(3)(x−z(t)), using the Dirac delta function. Its second mass moment tensor is Iij​=mzi​zj​. The approximation ignores its internal size and structure.
