Non-colliding gravitational velocity-kick bound (∣Δv∣max​)

For a local two-body hyperbolic encounter with central parameter μ and allowed centre separation rmin​≥r0​, conservation of energy gives equal asymptotic speeds. The gravitational scattering angle therefore implies
∣Δv∣=1+rmin​v∞2​/μ2v∞​​≤r0​μ​​=2​vesc​(r0​)​.
(47)
Put z=v∞​rmin​/μ​ and use 2z/(1+z2)≤1. The optimizing grazing orbit has z=1, orbital eccentricity two and deflection π/3. For finite spherical bodies r0​ is the sum of their radii; excluding actual contact makes the upper bound a supremum. Without a finite exclusion radius the ideal point-mass problem has no finite bound.
