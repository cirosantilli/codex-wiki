Generic extension

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Forcing Generic filter
OurBigBook.com Words: 529 Articles: 12
If G is a generic filter over a countable transitive model M, the generic extension M[G] consists of the interpretations by G of all forcing names in M.
