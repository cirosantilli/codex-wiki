Nuclear decay constant (λ)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Radioactive decay
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
In the constant-rate mathematical model, an undecayed atomic nucleus has conditional probability
Pr(T≤t+Δt∣T>t)=1−e−λΔt=λΔt+O((λΔt)2)
(14)
of decaying during the next positive interval Δt. The nuclear decay constant has units of inverse time. Its value describes a rate; it does not specify a scheduled death time for one atomic nucleus. The conditional probability is independent of the age t, the memorylessness of the exponential distribution.
