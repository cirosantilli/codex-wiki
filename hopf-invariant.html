Hopf invariant (a⌣a=H(ϕ)b)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Homotopy group
OurBigBook.com Words: 323 Articles: 3
For ϕ:S2q−1→Sq, q>1, its mapping cone has integral cohomology generators a in degree q and b in degree 2q. The integer defined by a⌣a=H(ϕ)b, with fixed cell orientations, is the Hopf invariant. It measures how an attaching map changes multiplication without changing the additive groups. The complex Hopf fibration has invariant one because its mapping cone is CP2; a constant attaching map has invariant zero.
