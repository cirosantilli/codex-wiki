Spontaneous emission (Pe​(t)=Pe​(0)e−Γt)

 Home Physics Atomic physics
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 21
Spontaneous emission is radiative decay of an excited matter quantum state by emission of a photon, without an applied resonant drive. In a simple Markov decay model the excited-level population obeys P˙e​=−ΓPe​. During driven control, the expected emission count is governed by Γ∫Pe​(t)dt; suppressing the lossy level's population is therefore the purpose of dark-state adiabatic transfer.
