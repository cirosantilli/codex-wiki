Square

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Polygon Quadrilateral Rectangle
OurBigBook.com Words: 77 Articles: 2
A rectangle with all four side lengths equal. It is a regular four-sided polygon. Rotations and translations preserve this definition; its sides need not be parallel to the coordinate axes.
