Van der Waals critical point ((vc​,Tc​,pc​))

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Pressure Equation of state Van der Waals equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 182 Articles: 1
The liquid-gas critical point of the Van der Waals equation occurs where its two subcritical stationary points merge into a horizontal inflection point. The conditions are (∂p/∂v)T​=(∂2p/∂v2)T​=0. The short calculation is
−(vc​−b)2kB​Tc​​+vc3​2a​=0,(vc​−b)32kB​Tc​​−vc4​6a​=0.
(18)
Dividing these relations gives 2/(vc​−b)=3/vc​, hence
vc​=3b,Tc​=27bkB​8a​,pc​=27b2a​.
(19)
Substitution gives the Van der Waals law of corresponding states and the parameter-independent ratio pc​vc​/(kB​Tc​)=3/8. That ratio belongs to this equation of state; reducing a real fluid's variables does not enforce it.[ref]
An exact rearrangement of the reduced critical Van der Waals isotherm makes its flatness visible:
q(x,1)−1=−x2(3x−1)3(x−1)3​.
(20)
The first nonzero departure near x=1 is −3(x−1)3/2. On the critical particle volume approached from t>1, (∂q/∂x)x=1​=−6(t−1) and
κT​=6pc​(t−1)1​.
(21)
The divergent isothermal compressibility and cubic flattening explain why the critical region is mechanically soft. These are mean-field theory predictions; thermal fluctuations limit their use for real fluids very close to a thermodynamic critical point.
