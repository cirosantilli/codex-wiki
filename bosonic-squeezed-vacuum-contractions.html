Let bk​=coshθk​ak​+sinhθk​a−k†​ for real even θk​, and let every bk​ annihilate a normalized vacuum state. Inverting the Bogoliubov transformation gives ak​=coshθk​bk​−sinhθk​b−k†​. Only ⟨bk​bq†​⟩=δkq​ survives, proving the displayed vacuum expectation values. Taking the Hermitian adjoint gives the same real negative pair amplitude for two creation operators. The normal occupation number is quadratic at small θk​, whereas the pair amplitude is linear. For one opposite-momentum pair the two-mode squeezed vacuum is sechθ∑n≥0​(−tanhθ)n∣n,n⟩, fixing the sign convention directly.
