Mean-field electromotive force (E=⟨u×b⟩)

 ... Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Resistive magnetohydrodynamics Dynamo action Mean-field dynamo
OurBigBook.com Words: 481 Articles: 6
The mean-field electromotive force is the vector correlation E=⟨u×b⟩. Its curl enters the mean resistive induction equation. It is a local vector with units of velocity times magnetic field, not the scalar circuit voltage called electromotive force. A space-time average of periodic modes is often used to measure its transport coefficients.
