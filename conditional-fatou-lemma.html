Conditional Fatou lemma

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Conditional expectation
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
For nonnegative random variables Xn​ and a sigma-algebra G,
E[liminfn​Xn​∣G]≤liminfn​E[Xn​∣G]
(13)
almost surely. It follows from the ordinary Fatou lemma after testing both sides on every event in G.
