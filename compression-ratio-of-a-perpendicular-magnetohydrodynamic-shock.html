Put c12​=γp1​/ρ1​ and vA12​=B12​/(μ0​ρ1​). The mass, momentum and energy jump conditions for a perpendicular magnetohydrodynamic shock factor into the unchanged state x=1 and
(2−γ)vA12​x2+[(γ−1)u12​+2c12​+γvA12​]x−(γ+1)u12​=0.
(44)
For the compressive physical branch x>1, positive downstream pressure implies x<(γ+1)/(γ−1) when γ>1 and the upstream sound speed or Alfvén speed is nonzero. Indeed the polynomial implies
ρ1​p2​​=(γ+1)−(γ−1)xc12​[(γ+1)x−(γ−1)]/γ+(γ−1)vA12​(x−1)3/2​.
(45)
It then gives u12​>c12​+vA12​: the upstream flow exceeds the perpendicular fast magnetosonic wave speed. The exactly cold unmagnetized strong-shock limit reaches the upper compression bound.
