Radiative shift of an isothermal breeze maximum (rM′​=[GM−σL/(4πmˉc)]/(2a2))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Parker wind Subsonic isothermal breeze
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
For unattenuated isotropic radiation from a point source, the radiation pressure force on each absorbing particle is σL/(4πcr2). Dividing by its mass mˉ changes the effective inverse-square inward acceleration to [GM−σL/(4πmˉc)]/r2. The wind equation therefore places the maximum of a subsonic isothermal breeze at the displayed radius, provided the bracket is positive. If it is nonpositive, the positive-temperature subsonic outflow decreases in speed throughout its domain and has no interior speed maximum. A cross section per particle requires that particle's mass; an opacity per unit mass instead absorbs this conversion.
