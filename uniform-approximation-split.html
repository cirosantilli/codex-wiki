Uniform approximation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 7k Articles: 118
Uniform approximation minimizes the supremum norm of the difference between a target function and an approximant.
The error is measured uniformly over the domain using the supremum norm. A sequence of approximants whose uniform error tends to zero converges by uniform convergence; the optimization problem and the convergence notion are distinct.
