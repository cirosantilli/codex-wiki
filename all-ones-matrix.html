All-ones matrix (J)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Linear map Matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
The all-ones matrix J=eeT has every entry equal to one, where e=(1,…,1)T. On Rn, the matrix J/n is the orthogonal projection matrix onto span{e}.
