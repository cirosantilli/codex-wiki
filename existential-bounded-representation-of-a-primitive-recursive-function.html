Existential bounded representation of a primitive recursive function

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Computability theory Primitive recursive function
OurBigBook.com Words: 212 Articles: 3
The graph of every primitive recursive f:Nk→N has a formula
y=f(x)⟺∃zδ(y,x,z)
(7)
in the language of ordered rings, where every quantifier inside δ is bounded. Composition uses existentially quantified intermediate values, while primitive recursion uses a boundedly checked code for the finite sequence of intermediate values.
