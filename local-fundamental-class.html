OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For a finite Galois extension L/K of non-Archimedean local fields, its relative Brauer group is the kernel of restriction Br(K)→Br(L) and identifies with H2(Gal(L/K),L×). Restriction multiplies the local Brauer invariant by [L:K]. This kernel is therefore cyclic of order [L:K], and the indicated class is its distinguished generator. In an unramified extension with arithmetic Frobenius generator it is represented by the cyclic algebra with parameter a uniformizer.
