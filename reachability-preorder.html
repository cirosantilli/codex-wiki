Reachability preorder (u⪯v⟺a directed path from u to v exists)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Set Preorder
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
Reachability in a directed graph, allowing length-zero paths, is a preorder: zero steps give reflexivity and concatenation gives transitivity. Distinct mutually reachable vertices violate antisymmetry. Its equivalence classes under mutual reachability are the strongly connected components; the induced reachability relation on those classes is a partial order. Equivalently, contracting the classes gives the acyclic condensation graph.
