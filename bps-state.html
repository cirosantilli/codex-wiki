BPS state

 ... Branches of physics Supersymmetry Four-dimensional N=1 supersymmetry Super-Poincaré algebra Central charge in supersymmetry BPS bound in supersymmetry
OurBigBook.com Words: 125 Articles: 1
A BPS state saturates a BPS bound in supersymmetry. The zero-norm combinations of supercharges and their adjoints annihilate its entire irreducible supermultiplet. Thus part of the supersymmetry is preserved. For a positive-mass representation, its massive supermultiplet is shortened; massless supermultiplets require the separate null-momentum construction. Charge conjugation can require a separate conjugate multiplet in the physical spectrum.
