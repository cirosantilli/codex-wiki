Principal part of a meromorphic function

 ... Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Complex submanifold Complex analytic hypersurface Divisor on a complex manifold Sheaf of meromorphic functions on a complex manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 185 Articles: 2
At a point x of a Riemann surface, the principal part of a meromorphic function is the negative-degree part of its Laurent series in a local coordinate. It is equivalently a germ in the quotient Kx​/Ox​.
The principal part at a pole is the finite sum of negative-degree terms of its Laurent series. Prescribing such parts is the problem solved by the Mittag-Leffler theorem.
