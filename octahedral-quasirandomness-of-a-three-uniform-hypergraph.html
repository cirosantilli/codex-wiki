Octahedral quasirandomness of a three-uniform hypergraph (∥1H​−p∥□38​≤α)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Hypergraph Uniform hypergraph
OurBigBook.com Words: 203 Articles: 1
For a tripartite three-uniform hypergraph on nonempty finite parts X,Y,Z, let h:X×Y×Z→{0,1} be its hypergraph edge indicator, let p=Eh and put g=h−p. In the eighth-power convention, α-quasirandomness means
∥g∥□38​=Ex0​,x1​,y0​,y1​,z0​,z1​​∏i,j,k∈{0,1}​g(xi​,yj​,zk​)≤α.
(1)
All averages are uniform and independent, with repeated sampled vertices of a hypergraph allowed. This expression is nonnegative because it equals Ex0​,x1​,y0​,y1​​(Ez​∏i,j​g(xi​,yj​,z))2. It measures correlations across the eight faces of a tripartite octahedral configuration. If instead the norm itself is used as the quasirandomness parameter, the parameter in this definition is its eighth power.
