Type II gamma-factor mass poles (s=4n/α′,n=0,1,…)

 Home Physics Branches of physics String theory Type II string theory Type II superstring mass spectrum
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
At generic fixed t, the factor Γ(−α′s/4) has simple poles at the displayed nonnegative values of s. Its denominator Γ(1+α′s/4) is finite there. The t,u factors do not generically cancel those poles under s+t+u=0. Factorization identifies massless and massive intermediate states at those masses. The factor has no negative-mass-squared poles; special polarization or momentum choices can suppress individual residues and cannot establish the degeneracy of an entire level.
