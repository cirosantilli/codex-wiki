Formaldehyde

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Aldehyde
OurBigBook.com Words: 41
The planar molecule H2​CO. With the C=O bond along z and the molecule in the xz plane, its C2v​ vibrational representation is 3A1​+2B1​+B2​. Interchanging the x and y axes interchanges the B1​ and B2​ labels; physical activity is unchanged.
