Radiative-convective climate column

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics Climate change Climate model
OurBigBook.com Words: 584 Articles: 1
A radiative-convective climate column applies a radiative-convective equilibrium model to the vertical thermal structure relevant to climate change. Radiative transfer determines heating and cooling; convective adjustment represents unresolved vertical redistribution when the chosen atmospheric lapse rate is too steep. Radiative equilibrium alone need not give a physically admissible lower-atmospheric profile.
In 1967, Syukuro Manabe and Richard T. Wetherald computed equilibrium with prescribed relative humidity, allowing atmospheric water vapor to change with temperature. Their average-cloud case warmed by 2.36K for carbon dioxide increasing from 300 to 600 parts per million, versus 1.33K with fixed absolute humidity. These are model outputs, not measured warming. The comparison isolates an important water-vapor feedback.[ref]
An original two-temperature calculation exposes the bookkeeping. Let an opaque surface absorb solar irradiance Q and emit blackbody thermal radiation σTs4​. A single isothermal atmospheric layer transmits all sunlight and absorbs a fraction ϵ of surface infrared emission, emitting ϵσTa4​ both upward and downward. This is a grey thermal-band emissivity, with 0<ϵ≤1. Let H be upward nonradiative heat flux, and let Cs​,Ca​>0 be heat capacities per unit area. Then conservation of energy gives
Cs​T˙s​=Q+ϵσTa4​−σTs4​−H,Ca​T˙a​=ϵσTs4​−2ϵσTa4​+H.
(9)
The two equations sum to
dtd​(Cs​Ts​+Ca​Ta​)=Q−L,L=(1−ϵ)σTs4​+ϵσTa4​.
(10)
Here L is outgoing longwave radiation. Both H and downward atmospheric emission cancel internally: they redistribute energy without creating it. The factor two in atmospheric emission counts two faces. Dropping the downward face would spoil the surface budget. Each right-hand term has units Wm−2; each C has units Jm−2K−1.
Choose Q=240Wm−2, H=80Wm−2 and ϵ=1. At equilibrium, L=σTa4​=240Wm−2 and σTs4​=400Wm−2, giving Ta​≃255.06K and Ts​≃289.81K. Direct substitution gives surface balance 240+240−400−80=0 and atmospheric balance 400−480+80=0. These deliberately chosen fluxes illustrate the model; they are not an observed global budget or a reconstruction of the 1967 calculation. Prescribing H illustrates heat transfer accounting but does not close a realistic convection law.
For fixed Q,H,ϵ, the positive-temperature equilibrium is locally stable. Put a=4σTs3​>0 and b=4ϵσTa3​>0. The Jacobian matrix of the temperature equations has trace −a/Cs​−2b/Ca​<0 and determinant ab(2−ϵ)/(Cs​Ca​)>0. Thus both eigenvalues have negative real part. Temperature-dependent climate feedbacks alter this test. A single grey layer also omits the optical spectrum, cloud structure and the independent thermal behavior of the troposphere and stratosphere.
