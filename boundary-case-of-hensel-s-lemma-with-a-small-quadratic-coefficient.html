Boundary case of Hensel's lemma with a small quadratic coefficient (∣f(a)∣=∣f′(a)∣2)

 ... Area of mathematics Arithmetic Non-Archimedean analysis Local field Hensel's lemma Strong form of Hensel lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 133
Let R be a complete discrete valuation ring, f∈R[X], and d=∣f′(a)∣ satisfy 0<d<1 and ∣f(a)∣=d2. Suppose the second Taylor coefficient of f at every point of R has norm below one. One Newton iteration over a valued field step has correction h=−f(a)/f′(a) of norm d. Its residual has quadratic term of norm strictly below d2, and all higher terms have norm at most d3<d2. The derivative changes by a term of norm below d, so its norm remains d. The strong form of Hensel lemma therefore applies from the new point, producing a root within distance d of the original point. The second Taylor coefficient is f′′/2 when that quotient is meaningful, or the division-free binomial-coefficient Taylor expression in arbitrary characteristic. A Newton sequence that reaches a root is thereafter constant; residuals decrease strictly only before that event.
