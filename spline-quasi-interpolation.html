Spline quasi-interpolation (Qf=∑i​λi​(f)Ni​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 80
A spline quasi-interpolant synthesizes a B-spline expansion from local bounded linear functionals, instead of solving a global interpolation system. Suppose each functional is bounded by ck​∥f∥C[ti​,ti+k​]​ and the operator reproduces every spline. On a knot cell [tj​,tj+1​], only indices j+1−k≤i≤j contribute. Their supports lie in [tj+1−k​,tj+k​], and subpartition of unity for B-splines proves local operator norm at most ck​. Reproduction of degree-k−1 polynomials and a local Taylor theorem remainder then give error at most (1+ck​)(2k−1)khk∥f(k)∥∞​/k!, on the basic knot domain with the usual completed boundary basis.
