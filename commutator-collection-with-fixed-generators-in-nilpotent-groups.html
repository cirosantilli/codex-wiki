Commutator collection with fixed generators in nilpotent groups (G′=[G,a1​]⋯[G,ad​])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Solvable group Nilpotent group
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
If a nilpotent group is generated by a1​,…,ad​, every element of its commutator subgroup is a product [x1​,a1​]⋯[xd​,ad​]. The sets in the displayed formula need not be subgroups. Induct on the nilpotency class c. Collection in G/γc​(G) gives the desired product with a central error. Since γc​(G)=[γc−1​(G),G], that error is a product [y1​,a1​]⋯[yd​,ad​] with yi​∈γc−1​(G). These factors are central, and γc−1​(G) centralizes G′, so replacing xi​ by xi​yi​ absorbs the error. For a finitely generated pro-p group, applying this formula in all finite quotients and using compactness makes its algebraically generated commutator subgroup closed.
