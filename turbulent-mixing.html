Turbulent mixing

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 685 Articles: 12
Turbulent mixing redistributes a transported scalar, such as temperature or particle concentration, through irregular fluid motions and eventual molecular diffusion. A top-hat plume model assumes this mixing keeps the scalar approximately uniform within a selected cross section; that uniformity is an additional closure, not a consequence of thin geometry alone.
