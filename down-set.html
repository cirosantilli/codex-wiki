Down-set

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Set family Boolean lattice
OurBigBook.com Words: 189 Articles: 3
A down-set is a family D such that A∈D and B⊆A imply B∈D. It is the complement of an up-set within the same Boolean lattice.
This is the downward-closed Boolean-lattice case of upper and lower sets.
