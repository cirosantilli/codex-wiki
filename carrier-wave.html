Carrier wave (ei(k0​x−ωc​t))

 ... Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Dispersion relation Phase velocity and group velocity Wave packet
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
A carrier wave is the rapid reference oscillation in a modulated wave. In the narrow-packet representation ψ=A(x,t)ei(k0​x−ωc​t), choose k0​ near the centre of the frequency spectrum and ωc​=ω(k0​) on its dispersion relation. Its constant-wave phase reference moves at phase velocity ωc​/k0​. This reference need not coincide with a local maximum of the complete real wavefield when the wave envelope varies. Moving the reference k0​ shifts some slow wave phase into A without changing ψ.
