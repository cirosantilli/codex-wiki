Electric-dipole selection rule

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Time-dependent perturbation theory Electric-dipole interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 133 Articles: 3
Because the position operator has odd parity, an electric-dipole matrix element vanishes between states of the same parity. For orbital-angular-momentum eigenstates, the full rule is Δℓ=±1 and Δm=0,±1, with the field polarization selecting the allowed change in m.
