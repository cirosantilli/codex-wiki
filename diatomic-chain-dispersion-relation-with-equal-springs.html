Diatomic-chain dispersion relation with equal springs

For alternating masses m,M at spacing d, equal nearest-neighbour spring constants K, and cell length 2d, a two-component normal mode gives
ω±2​=K(m1​+M1​)±K(m1​+M1​)2−mM4​sin2(kd)​,∣k∣≤2dπ​.
(48)
The minus sign is the acoustic phonon branch and the plus sign is the optical phonon branch. For M>m the edge frequencies are 2K/M​ and 2K/m​, leaving a gap. At k=0 they are zero and 2K(1/m+1/M)​.
