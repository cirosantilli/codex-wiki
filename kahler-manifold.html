Kähler manifold

 ... Geometry and topology Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 51
A Kähler manifold is a complex manifold with a positive real closed (1,1)-form ω. The associated Riemannian metric is g(u,v)=ω(u,Jv).
