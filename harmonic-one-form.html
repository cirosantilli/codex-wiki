Harmonic one-form (H1(M))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential form Hodge star operator Harmonic differential form
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
A harmonic one-form is a differential one-form α satisfying Δα=0 for the Hodge Laplacian. On a closed manifold, the Hodge decomposition theorem identifies these forms with degree-one de Rham cohomology. The Bochner-Weitzenbock formula for one-forms implies that harmonic one-forms are parallel under nonnegative Ricci curvature.
