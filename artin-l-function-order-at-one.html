Artin L-function order at one (ords=1​LK​(χ,s)=−⟨χ,1G​⟩G​)

Assume that every nontrivial one-dimensional Artin L-function over the intermediate fields is holomorphic and nonzero at one, while the trivial one is the base Dedekind zeta function with a simple pole. By Brauer induction, χ=∑j​nj​IndHj​G​ψj​ with integers nj​ and linear characters ψj​. Compatibility of Artin L-functions with induced representations expresses LK​(χ,s) as the corresponding product of LLHj​​(ψj​,s)nj​. Its order at one is −∑j​nj​⟨ψj​,1Hj​​⟩. Frobenius reciprocity turns this sum into −⟨χ,1G​⟩. Hence every nontrivial irreducible character gives order zero and a holomorphic nonzero function near one. This local conclusion does not assert global Artin holomorphy.
