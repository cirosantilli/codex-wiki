Gauge-covariant integration by parts (∂μ​κ(Y,Z)=κ(Dμ​Y,Z)+κ(Y,Dμ​Z))

OurBigBook.com Words: 44
For Lie-algebra-valued fields and a constant invariant bilinear form on a Lie algebra, the connection terms in the displayed identity cancel. Indeed, invariance gives κ([A,Y],Z)+κ(Y,[A,Z])=0. Ordinary integration by parts then yields ∫κ(Dμ​Y,Z)=−∫κ(Y,Dμ​Z) when boundary terms vanish, for example for compactly supported variations. This preserves covariance in variational calculations of Yang-Mills equations.
