Simulation of a computable diagonal Hamiltonian

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Hamiltonian simulation
OurBigBook.com Words: 120 Articles: 1
Let A∣x⟩=a(x)∣x⟩, where a reversible circuit C computes a(x) into a clean register. Then applying e−ita(x) as a phase on that register and performing uncomputation implements e−itA on the data register. For a(x)=(−1)f(x), a Boolean output qubit suffices: C†(I⊗e−itZ)C acts as e−itA when that qubit starts in ∣0⟩. This exact construction avoids a Lie-Trotter product formula because the eigenvalue is computed directly.
