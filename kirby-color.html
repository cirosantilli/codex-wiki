Kirby color (Ω=∑j=0r−2​dj​f​j​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Knot theory Colored link
OurBigBook.com Words: 284 Articles: 3
The Kirby color is the weighted sum of annular closures of the surviving Jones-Wenzl idempotents at a root of unity. Its weights are their closed bracket traces. The fusion of Jones-Wenzl colors gives aΩ=da​Ω in the annular fusion ring. Inserting the corresponding fusion resolutions along a band gives the Kirby color handle-slide identity, the central ingredient of surgery invariance.
