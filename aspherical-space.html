Aspherical space

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Homotopy group Eilenberg–MacLane space
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A path-connected space is aspherical when its higher homotopy groups vanish, equivalently when its universal cover is contractible. An aspherical space with fundamental group G is a K(G,1), so based homotopy classes of maps into it are controlled by homomorphisms of fundamental groups.
