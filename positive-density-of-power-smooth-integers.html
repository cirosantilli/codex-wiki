Positive density of power-smooth integers (#{n≤H:P+(n)≤Hη}≫η​H)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Smooth number
OurBigBook.com Words: 110
Fix η=1/k with k≥2. Choose k primes in [Hη−η2,Hη], repetitions allowed, and an integer cofactor m so that mp1​⋯pk​≤H. The product is between H1−η and H, so m≤Hη and all resulting integers are Hη-smooth. The Mertens theorem for reciprocal primes makes the reciprocal prime sum tend to −log(1−η)>0. Since ⌊H/(p1​⋯pk​)⌋≥H/(2p1​⋯pk​), there are ≫η​H ordered representations. Each integer has only Oη​(1) prime factors in that interval, counted with multiplicity, so representation multiplicities are bounded by a constant depending on k. This proves the density bound. For arbitrary fixed positive η, choose a smaller reciprocal-integer exponent.
