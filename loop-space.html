Loop space (ΩX)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 625 Articles: 13
The loop space ΩX is the space of based loops in X. It is the homotopy fiber of the inclusion of the base point into X.
