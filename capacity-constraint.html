Capacity constraint

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Flow network
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
If a directed edge e has capacity c(e), a feasible flow satisfies 0≤f(e)≤c(e). In an antisymmetric-flow convention, this is encoded together with the residual capacity of the reverse orientation.
