Hydrolysis

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 833 Articles: 11
Hydrolysis is a chemical reaction involving water that breaks a chemical bond. The free energy released by suitably coupled ATP hydrolysis can drive active transport; the available free energy depends on the actual composition and conditions.
