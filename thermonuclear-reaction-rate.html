Thermonuclear reaction rate (r12​=n1​n2​⟨σv⟩/(1+δ12​))

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar nuclear fusion
OurBigBook.com Words: 196 Articles: 3
A thermal distribution of relative velocities weights a nuclear cross-section to determine the reaction rate per volume. Identical reactants require the denominator two to avoid counting each pair twice. Specific deposited power is r12​Qdeposited​/ρ. Charged-particle quantum tunnelling through a Coulomb barrier produces a Gamow peak, while resonances and plasma screening can alter the rate.
