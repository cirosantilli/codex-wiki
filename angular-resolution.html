Angular resolution (θres​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Observational astronomy
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
Angular resolution describes the smallest angular separation or structure an observation can distinguish. A physical scale r at distance D subtends approximately r/D radians when r≪D. Resolving a black-hole sphere of influence therefore involves distance as well as its physical radius, and useful kinematics also require adequate surface brightness.
