Symmetric algebra (S(V))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Multilinear algebra Tensor product Tensor algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 516 Articles: 9
The symmetric algebra is the quotient of T(V) by the ideal generated by v⊗w−w⊗v. It is graded by symmetric powers:
S(V)=⨁n≥0​SnV.
