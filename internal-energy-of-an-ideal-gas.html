Internal energy of an ideal gas

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Ideal gas
OurBigBook.com Words: 27
For a fixed amount of ideal gas, internal energy depends only on temperature. If CV​ is constant, dE=CV​dT and hence E=CV​T after a choice of energy zero.
