Cumulant expansion

The cumulant expansion writes
logE[etX]=∑n≥1​n!tn​κn​(X),
(62)
where κn​ is the nth cumulant. Applied to a fluctuating interaction, it expands the logarithm of the averaged Boltzmann weight in connected expectation values.
