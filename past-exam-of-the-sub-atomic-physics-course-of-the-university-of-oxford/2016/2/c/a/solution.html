Use c=1 and let E denote the total center-of-momentum energy, not one beam's energy. Opposite equal momenta give E=2Ebeam​=498 MeV, so the kaon is at its stipulated resonance mass mK​=498 MeV. Write the unspecified selected exit branching fractions as bρ​ and bK​. The supplied line shape gives

The distinction between inclusive and exclusive resonance branching factors matters here. For inclusive resonance formation, sum over all decays, so bρ​=bK​=1. At E=mK​ the small kaon width cancels from the peak value:
Rinclusive​(498)=0.693​(4982−7692)2+4982147249821472​=0.1890.​
(16)
If instead the measured cross section is specifically resonant elastic π+π−→π+π−, its exit branching fractions equal the entrance ones: bρ​=1, bK​=0.69. Then
Rππ​(498)=0.1890/0.69=0.2740.​
(17)
The source says production without specifying an observed decay channel, so both conventions are made explicit rather than silently squaring a branching fraction for an inclusive measurement. The quoted kaon width and two-pion branching fraction identify the short-lived propagation state KS​; a pure flavor state K0 is a superposition of short- and long-lived states. This exercise uses the stipulated KS​ resonance model and equal omitted proportionality factors.
