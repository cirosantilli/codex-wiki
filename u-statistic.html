U-statistic ((mn​)−1∑i1​<⋯<im​​H(Xi1​​,…,Xim​​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Estimator
OurBigBook.com Words: 301 Articles: 6
A U-statistic averages a symmetric function of m distinct sample observations over all unordered m-tuples. For independent and identically distributed observations it is an unbiased estimator of EH(X1​,…,Xm​). Omitting repeated indices can remove diagonal contributions in quadratic plug-in estimators.
