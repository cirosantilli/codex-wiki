Dirichlet energy

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The Dirichlet energy of a differentiable scalar function is one half the integral of its squared gradient. On a Euclidean domain it is E(u)=21​∫∣∇u∣2dx; the Dirichlet energy on a Riemannian manifold uses its metric and volume measure. Its stationary functions satisfy the Laplace equation with fixed boundary values.
