Galilean invariance

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Galilean transformation Principle of Galilean relativity
OurBigBook.com Words: 119 Articles: 1
A dynamical symmetry under Galilean transformations. For an isolated pair with constant masses, a force law depending only on relative positions and relative velocities has this invariance. Equal-and-opposite forces alone do not imply it: their magnitude can depend on centre of mass velocity relative to a preferred frame.
