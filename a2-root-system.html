A2 root system (A2​)

 ... Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Cartan subalgebra Root-space decomposition Root system
OurBigBook.com Words: 58 Articles: 1
The A2​ root system has equal-length simple roots α1​,α2​ with angle 2π/3, and roots ±α1​, ±α2​, ±(α1​+α2​). Its Cartan matrix is (2−1​−12​). The corresponding complex simple Lie algebra is sl3​(C), of dimension eight.
