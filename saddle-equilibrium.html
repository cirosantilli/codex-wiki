Saddle equilibrium

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Autonomous planar system Equilibrium point of a dynamical system Linear stability of a planar equilibrium
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
A planar hyperbolic equilibrium is a saddle when the Jacobian determinant is negative. It has one stable and one unstable eigendirection and corresponding one-dimensional invariant manifolds.
