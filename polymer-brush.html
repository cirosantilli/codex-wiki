Polymer brush

 Home Chemistry Polymer Polymer physics Grafted polymer chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 441 Articles: 7
A layer of sufficiently crowded tethered polymer chains stretched away from their attachment surface. In a good solvent, overlap increases the interaction contribution to free energy; extension relieves this crowding at a cost of chain entropy. The mushroom-to-brush crossover occurs at σg​R2∼1, where R is an isolated coil size. In a simple good-solvent scaling model, the brush height is H∼Nb(vσg​/b)1/3 for effective segment excluded volume v and statistical segment length b; if v∼b3, this becomes H∼Nb(σg​b2)1/3. The scaling requires strong stretching relative to the isolated coil but extension well below the contour length Nb.
