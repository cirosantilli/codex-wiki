Travelling-wave stationarity of planar filament propulsion (c=(ξ⊥​−ξ∥​)/(Γξ⊥​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Mathematical biology Biological fluid dynamics Resistive-force theory Propulsive force of a periodic planar filament
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 1
For the leading periodic propulsive force of a periodic planar filament and power of a periodic planar filament functionals, the Euler-Lagrange equation for Fx​+Γ(W˙−W˙0​) gives (ξ⊥​−ξ∥​)yxt​+Γξ⊥​ytt​=0. For nonzero thrust, yt​ obeys an advection equation and the active travelling wave is f(x−ct), with c=(ξ⊥​−ξ∥​)/(Γξ⊥​). A static periodic shape may be added without changing leading force or power. Stationarity is a necessary extremum condition, not by itself an existence theorem for a global maximum.
