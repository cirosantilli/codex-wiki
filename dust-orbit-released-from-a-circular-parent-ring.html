Dust orbit released from a circular parent ring

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Planetary science Interplanetary dust Radiation-pressure coefficient
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
If release imparts no velocity kick, a grain's initial Kepler orbit under reduced stellar attraction has q=rb​, Q=rb​/(1−2β), and e=β/(1−β) for 0≤β<1/2. Its pericentre distance remains at the dust birth ring, while its apocentre distance grows with the radiation-pressure coefficient.
