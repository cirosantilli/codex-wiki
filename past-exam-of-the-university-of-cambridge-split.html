Exam sources held for the University of Cambridge, checked on 18 October 2026. Each year range means at least one held paper in every listed year; it does not promise every paper or module for that year. Links lead to existing course or exam articles; bold course names have no dedicated article yet. Download links lead to public originals, sometimes inside larger releases.
Solutions: officially advertised student answers are sufficient to skip new solutions, including suggested answers on Moodle or in a departmental library; access may require sign-in or payment. “Unverified” means that availability or exact-year coverage has not been established, rather than that no answers exist. Marking schemes, selected answers and examiner reports are distinguished where that is all the evidence establishes. Existing wiki drafts are retained and are separate from University answer availability.
For Physics courses, the official past-paper search requires University sign-in; its exact-year solution holdings remain unverified except for the public sets identified below.
