Stinespring representation of a completely positive map (N(X)=TrE​(VXV†))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Quantum information theory Positive linear map Completely positive map
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
In finite dimensions, a completely positive map has the form N(X)=TrE​(VXV†) for V:HQ​→HQ′​⊗HE​. From a Kraus representation take V=∑j​Kj​⊗∣j⟩. The observable-picture adjoint is N†(Y)=V†(Y⊗IE​)V. A general completely positive map need not have isometric V; it is a linear isometry of Hilbert spaces precisely when the map is trace preserving.
