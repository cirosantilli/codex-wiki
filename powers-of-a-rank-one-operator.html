Powers of a rank-one operator (Km=φ(a)m−1K(m≥1))

 ... Analysis Functional analysis Compact operator Hilbert-Schmidt operator Trace-class operator Rank-one operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 107 Articles: 1
Let Kx=aφ(x) on a vector space, where φ is a linear functional. Its image of a linear map lies in the span of a. Applying K twice gives K2x=aφ(a)φ(x)=φ(a)Kx. Mathematical induction then proves the displayed power formula. If φ(a)=0, K2=0, even when K=0; this is a nilpotent operator. If φ(a)=0, then K/φ(a) is an idempotent linear map. In a real inner product space, the special case φ(x)=b⋅x gives φ(a)=a⋅b.
