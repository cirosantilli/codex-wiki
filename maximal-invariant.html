Maximal invariant (A(y)=A(z)⟺z=gy for some g)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Transformation model
OurBigBook.com Words: 205 Articles: 2
A statistic is invariant if A(gy)=A(y); it is maximal when it also separates different group orbits. Consequently every invariant procedure factors through the maximal invariant, subject to the usual measurability conditions on the orbit space. Standardizing a sample by an equivariant estimator of location and positive scale gives a maximal invariant for the positive affine group.
