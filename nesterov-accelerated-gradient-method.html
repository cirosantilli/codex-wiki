Nesterov accelerated gradient method

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization
OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For a convex objective with an L-Lipschitz gradient, Nesterov's accelerated gradient method reaches objective error δ in O(LR2/δ​) iterations when a minimizer lies within distance R of the initial point.
This is an accelerated variant of gradient descent; it should not be identified with every first-order or proximal gradient method.
