Take the upward-oriented graph z=c1−x2/a2−y2/b2​ over 0≤x≤Lx​, 0≤y≤Ly​, where a,b,c,Lx​,Ly​>0, A=Lx2​/a2, B=Ly2​/b2 and A+B<1. The surface flux of the vector field F=(−y,x,0) is the displayed formula. Indeed the vector surface element is (−zx​,−zy​,1)dxdy, so
Φ=c(b21​−a21​)∫0Lx​​∫0Ly​​1−x2/a2−y2/b2​xy​dydx.
(67)
The change of variables u=x2/a2, v=y2/b2 changes xydxdy to a2b2dudv/4. Integrating (1−u−v)−1/2 over [0,A]×[0,B] gives 34​[1−(1−A)3/2−(1−B)3/2+(1−A−B)3/2], proving the result. When a=b, the value of the vector field is a tangent vector to the ellipsoid at every point, and the surface flux vanishes pointwise.
