Gamma decay (Eγ​≃Ei​−Ef​)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Atomic nucleus Nuclear structure Nuclear de-excitation
OurBigBook.com Words: 414 Articles: 6
Gamma decay is electromagnetic nuclear de-excitation by photon emission. It preserves mass number and atomic number, while changing the nuclear state. Angular momentum and parity constrain the allowed radiation multipoles. The gamma ray topic is broader than this nuclear process.
