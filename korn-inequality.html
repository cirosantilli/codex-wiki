Korn inequality (∥∇v∥L2​≤C∥ε(v)∥L2​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Sobolev space
OurBigBook.com Words: 105
The zero-boundary Korn inequality controls the full displacement gradient tensor by the infinitesimal strain tensor for v∈H01​(Ω;Rd). On a bounded Lipschitz domain, combining it with the Poincaré inequality makes a positive linear elasticity energy a coercive bilinear form in the first-order Sobolev norm. The zero Dirichlet boundary condition removes the otherwise uncontrolled rigid displacements.
For this zero-boundary version one can prove an exact identity. For a smooth compactly supported vector field, two applications of integration by parts give
∑i,j​∫Ω​∂j​vi​∂i​vj​=∫Ω​(divv)2.

Expanding the symmetric gradient and using density in the zero-boundary Sobolev space therefore yields
2∥ε(v)∥L22​=∥∇v∥L22​+∥divv∥L22​.

In three dimensions this also gives the stronger trace-free version

Here the matrix-valued L2 norm uses the Frobenius inner product. This explains why the trace-free form printed in the examination suffices for the usual Korn inequality estimate.
