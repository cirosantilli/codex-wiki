Prime-power binomial divisibility ((jpr​)≡0(modp)(0<j<pr))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Binomial coefficient
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For a prime number p and integer r≥1, every interior binomial coefficient in row pr is divisible by p. The polynomial identity (1+x)p≡1+xp(modp) iterates under the Frobenius endomorphism to (1+x)pr≡1+xpr(modp). This concerns divisibility by p, not necessarily by pr.
