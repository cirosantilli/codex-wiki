A charged scalar field coupled to an Abelian gauge field through Dμ​=∂μ​−ieaμ​. Under ϕ↦eiαϕ and aμ​↦aμ​+e−1∂μ​α, its gauge covariant derivative transforms with the same phase. The potential determines whether the perturbative spectrum has a massless photon or exhibits the Higgs mechanism.
