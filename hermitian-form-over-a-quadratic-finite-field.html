Hermitian form over a quadratic finite field (B(v,w)=B(w,v)​,aˉ=aq)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Sesquilinear form Hermitian form
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 2
Over the finite field K=Fq2​, a sesquilinear form relative to a↦aq is linear in its first argument and conjugate-linear in its second. It is Hermitian when B(w,v)=B(v,w)​. Its diagonal values belong to Fq​; it is nondegenerate when B(v,w)=0 for every w forces v=0. This definition applies in characteristic two as well.
