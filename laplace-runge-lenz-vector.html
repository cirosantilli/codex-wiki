Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector (A)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Inverse-square force
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 2
For the signed inverse-square force F=−κr/r3 on a particle of mass m, with Hamiltonian H=p2/(2m)−κ/r, the Laplace-Runge-Lenz vector A=p×L−mκr is conserved. Attraction has κ>0 and repulsion has κ<0. It obeys A⋅L=0 and A2=m2κ2+2mHL2. For unit mass in a Kepler orbit, κ=GM.
