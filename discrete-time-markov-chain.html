Discrete-time Markov chain (P(Yn+1​=j∣Fn​)=PYn​j​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
A time-homogeneous discrete-time Markov chain has transitions at integer times with the displayed conditional probabilities and a row-stochastic transition probability matrix P. A probability row vector π is a stationary distribution when πP=π. It is reversible when πi​Pij​=πj​Pji​; multiplying these equations along a finite path proves equality of its forward and reversed stationary path probabilities.
