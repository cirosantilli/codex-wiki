Nonlinear endpoint layer with a quadratic reaction (Y′′+qY+Y2=0)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Differential equation Ordinary differential equation Singular perturbation Matched asymptotic expansion
OurBigBook.com Words: 162 Articles: 1
For q>0, matching Y→−q, Y′→0 fixes the first integral to (Y′)2=(q−2Y)(Y+q)2/3. Hence endpoint values above q/2 cannot match this outer branch. The upper family is Y=−q+(3q/2)sech2[q​(ξ−ξ0​)/2]. An endpoint value strictly between −q and q/2 admits two translations, one initially approaching −q and one initially moving towards the turning value q/2. Values below −q give the unique lower family Y=−q−(3q/2)csch2[q​(ξ+ξ0​)/2] with ξ0​>0. Matching to zero instead gives (Y′)2=−qY2−2Y3/3, which forbids a nonconstant real approach to zero. These are leading boundary layer classifications, not exact multiplicity theorems at degenerate endpoint data.
