Past null infinity (I−)

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole Penrose diagram Null infinity
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
Past null infinity is the ideal boundary from which incoming null geodesics arrive in an asymptotically flat spacetime. In the Minkowski conformal compactification it is T−R=−π, with the retarded coordinate tending to −∞ and the advanced coordinate finite.
