Polynomial multiplication map to complex projective space ((CP1)m→CPm,(L1​,…,Lm​)↦∏i​Li​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Complex projective space
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
Identify CPm with nonzero homogeneous binary forms of degree m, modulo nonzero scalar multiplication. Multiplication of m projective linear forms gives a well-defined holomorphic map: no product is zero, and scaling one form scales the entire coefficient vector. A form with m distinct roots has exactly m! ordered factorizations. In affine root coordinates the derivative is a Vandermonde determinant, nonzero at distinct roots. Each such preimage contributes +1 with complex orientations, so the mapping degree is m!. This also realizes the least positive degree allowed by the factorial degree obstruction for products of two-spheres.
