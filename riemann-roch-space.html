Riemann-Roch space (L(D))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Divisor on an algebraic curve
OurBigBook.com Words: 101 Articles: 1
The Riemann-Roch space of a divisor D is the finite-dimensional vector space
L(D)={f∈k(X)×:(f)+D≥0}∪{0}.
(43)
Its elements are the rational functions whose poles are bounded by the negative coefficients of D.
