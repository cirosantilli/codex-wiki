There are two one-loop Feynman diagrams: the two orders of the labelled photons around a top quark fermion loop, with one Yukawa interaction insertion for the outgoing Higgs boson. They are added, rather than identified by a Feynman-diagram symmetry factor. Each has the same color charge trace Nc​=3.
In the first routing, traverse the fermion loop from the p2​ insertion to the p1​ insertion with momentum l, then to the Higgs boson insertion with momentum l+p1​, then back to the p2​ insertion with momentum l−p2​. Conservation at the Higgs boson insertion gives ph​=p1​+p2​. The second routing exchanges (p1​,μ) and (p2​,ν): its three internal momenta are l, l+p2​ and l−p1​. Reversing the orientation is another way to represent this second ordering. Two vector insertions give no Furry theorem cancellation.
