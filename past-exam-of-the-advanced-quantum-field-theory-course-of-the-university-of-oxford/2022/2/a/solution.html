At the stated order the renormalization-group beta function is separable:
∫αi​αf​​α2dα​=b0​∫μi​μf​​μdμ​.

Consequently
αf​1​=αi​1​−b0​lnμi​μf​​,αf​=1−b0​αi​ln(μf​/μi​)αi​​.​

For a positive initial running coupling, this expression is used on the connected interval containing μi​ where its denominator is positive and perturbation theory remains valid.
If b0​<0, the running coupling decreases at high renormalization scale and tends to zero: this is asymptotic freedom. Decreasing the renormalization scale increases the running coupling, with a formal infrared pole at Λ=μi​exp[1/(b0​αi​)]. Near Λ, perturbation theory fails; the pole alone is not a proof of confinement. Both QCD and the SU(2) weak gauge coupling have this sign for the Standard Model particle content above the relevant thresholds.
If b0​>0, the running coupling decreases towards low renormalization scale and grows towards high renormalization scale. The formal ultraviolet Landau pole is μL​=μi​exp[1/(b0​αi​)]. The pole signals failure of this perturbative evolution, rather than a controlled physical infinity. The hypercharge gauge coupling and ordinary QED with charged matter have this sign.
If b0​=0, the running coupling is constant at this order; higher-order terms may still make it run. No Standard Model gauge factor has a vanishing one-loop order coefficient for the usual particle content.
