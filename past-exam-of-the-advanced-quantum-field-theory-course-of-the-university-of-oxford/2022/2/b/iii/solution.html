Both s-channel annihilation and t-channel gluon exchange are now allowed. Their spin sum factors are the Bhabha scattering factors with e↦g. Their color charge tensors, with the external indices of Solution, are
cs​=(Ta)ji​(Ta)kl​,ct​=(Ta)ki​(Ta)jl​.

∑a​(Ta)ij​(Ta)kl​=21​(δil​δjk​−Nc​1​δij​δkl​).

∣M∣2​=g42Nc2​Nc2​−1​[s2t2+u2​+t2s2+u2​−Nc​2​stu2​].​

∣M∣2​=94g4​[s2t2+u2​+t2s2+u2​]−278g4​stu2​.​
