Tautology (⊨A)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic Classical propositional logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
A tautology is a propositional formula true under every Boolean valuation in classical propositional logic, such as the law of excluded middle p∨¬p. A primitive proposition p is not a tautology, even if it represents a true statement. See forall x: Calgary, section 12.1.
