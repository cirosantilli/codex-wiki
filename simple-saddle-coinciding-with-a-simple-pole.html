Let G′(z0​)=0, G′′(z0​)=0, and let F,G be analytic. Assume a contributing contour can be deformed onto two descent arms of the method of steepest descent with one clockwise semicircular avoidance of the pole at z0​, and that its other portions are negligible. The odd pole term has zero Cauchy principal value on the real arms and integral −iπF0​ on the clockwise indentation. The constant regular term integrates to 2π/λ​ times its coefficient, proving the expansion and generic correction order. A degenerate saddle has a different local width and possibly a different indentation angle; analyticity and a single critical point alone do not imply this result. If F0​=0, retain the additive expansion rather than writing asymptotic equivalence to zero.
