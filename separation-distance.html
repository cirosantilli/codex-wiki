For a finite Markov chain with stationary distribution π, the separation distance from an initial state x is
sx​(t)=maxy​(1−π(y)Pt(x,y)​).
(386)
If τ is a strong stationary time, then sx​(t)≤Px​(τ>t), and total variation distance is at most separation distance.
