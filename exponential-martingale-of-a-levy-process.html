Exponential martingale of a Lévy process (Mtu​=exp(iuXt​−tψ(u)))

OurBigBook.com Words: 56
With the convention EeiuXt​=etψ(u), this complex process is a martingale for each real u. Its absolute value is the deterministic finite number e−tReψ(u). Conditioning on the past at time s and using independent increments multiplies eiuXs​−tψ(u) by e(t−s)ψ(u), giving Msu​. Under the opposite exponent convention the sign of the compensating term reverses.
