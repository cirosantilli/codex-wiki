Harmonic expansion on a disk (u(r,θ)=a0​+∑n≥1​rn(an​cos(nθ)+bn​sin(nθ)))

A real harmonic function on the unit disk has the displayed expansion, convergent locally uniformly with its derivatives in the interior. If its continuous boundary data have Fourier coefficients A0​,An​,Bn​ in the convention f(θ)=A0​/2+∑n≥1​(An​cos(nθ)+Bn​sin(nθ)), its harmonic extension has a0​=A0​/2, an​=An​, bn​=Bn​.
To obtain the radial factors, separation of variables in the Laplace equation in polar coordinates gives r2R′′+rR′−λR=0 and Θ′′+λΘ=0. Angular periodicity restricts nonconstant modes to λ=n2 for positive integers n. The radial factors are rn,r−n, with 1,logr for the constant angular mode. Boundedness at the origin removes r−n and logr. Each surviving factor rncos(nθ) or rnsin(nθ) is a harmonic polynomial, the real or imaginary part of (x+iy)n. The expansion can also be derived from the Poisson integral on the unit disk for the disk.
