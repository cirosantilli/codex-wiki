Cluster gravitational-lensing mass estimate (κ=Σ/Σcrit​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Large-scale structure of the universe Galaxy cluster
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
Gravitational lensing constrains a galaxy cluster's projected mass independently of its light output or dynamical equilibrium. The lensing convergence is projected surface mass density divided by Σcrit​=c2Ds​/(4πGDd​Dds​), using the appropriate angular diameter distances. Strong gravitational lensing constrains the central mass through multiple-image geometry, and weak gravitational lensing constrains larger-scale projected mass through coherent image distortions. Converting these observations into a three-dimensional mass needs geometrical modelling and the relevant lensing degeneracies.
