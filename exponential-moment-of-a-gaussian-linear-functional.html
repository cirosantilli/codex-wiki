Exponential moment of a Gaussian linear functional

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Gaussian process Gaussian random field
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 1
If a linear functional Z=∫a(r)V(r)dr of a real Gaussian random field exists in mean square, it is a Gaussian random variable. Completing the square in its density gives
EeZ=exp(EZ+21​VarZ),VarZ=∬a(r)a(r′)Cov[V(r),V(r′)]drdr′.
(64)
For a complex linear functional ϕ, apply this to Z=2Reϕ; then 21​VarZ=E∣ϕ−Eϕ∣2+ReE(ϕ−Eϕ)2.
