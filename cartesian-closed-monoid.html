Cartesian closed monoid (M=End(D))

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Category theory Category Adjoint functors Cartesian closed category
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
A monoid encoding a one-object product and exponential structure: pairing is inverse to the map z↦(πz,π′z), and currying is inverse to y↦ε⟨yπ,π′⟩. These bijections imply pairing naturality ⟨x,y⟩z=⟨xz,yz⟩ and currying naturality (x⟨zπ,π′⟩)∗=x∗z. The element e=(π′)∗ then satisfies ez=e for every z, hence is idempotent. Splitting e supplies a terminal object and a two-object Cartesian closed category whose endomorphisms at the original object are M. For the original object to be nonterminal, M must be nontrivial.
