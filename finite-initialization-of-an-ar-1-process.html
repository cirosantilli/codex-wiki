Finite initialization of an AR(1) process (Xt​−μ=ϕt(X0​−μ)+∑j=0t−1​ϕjεt−j​)

For t≥1, iterating an AR(1) from a fixed value X0​=x0​ gives the displayed finite sum. With centered white noise of variance σ2 and ∣ϕ∣<1,
E[Xt​]=μ+ϕt(x0​−μ),Var(Xt​)=1−ϕ2σ2(1−ϕ2t)​.
(17)
For h≥0, its covariance is Cov(Xt+h​,Xt​)=ϕhVar(Xt​), which depends on t. Even setting x0​=μ leaves a changing variance. At ϕ=0.7, σ2=1, and x0​=μ, the variances at times zero through three are 0,1,1.49,1.7301, approaching 100/51; this finite initialized record is not weakly stationary.
To initialize in weak stationarity, give X0​ mean μ, variance σ2/(1−ϕ2), and zero covariance with every future driving noise value. The full stationary construction sets X0​−μ=∑j≥0​ϕjε−j​. For discrete Gaussian white noise, equivalently draw X0​ from N(μ,σ2/(1−ϕ2)) independently of future noise. This gives exact strict stationarity from the first plotted point; it is the initialization used in the figure in time series. Matching these first two moments alone does not guarantee strict stationarity for non-Gaussian inputs. See also stationary initialization of a scalar linear state-space model.
