Vertical epicyclic frequency (Ωz​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Astrophysical disk Thin disk Vertical structure of an astrophysical disk
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The vertical epicyclic frequency is the frequency of a small vertical oscillation about a circular orbit. In an axisymmetric potential, Ωz2​=∂z2​Φ at the midplane; in a spherical potential it equals the circular orbital frequency.
