Equation of motion

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
An equation of motion determines how a physical state evolves. A variational action gives Euler-Lagrange equations; a phase-space action instead gives canonical Hamiltonian mechanics equations together with any imposed constraints.
