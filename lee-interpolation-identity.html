Lee interpolation identity (ℓi+1​−ℓi​=ωi​Ni​)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Uniform approximation Spline (mathematics) Spline approximation B-spline
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For distinct increasing knots, let ℓi​(⋅,t) interpolate (⋅−t)+k−1​ on ti​,…,ti+k−1​ and let ωi​(x)=∏r=1k−1​(x−ti+r​). Adjacent interpolants share k−1 values; their difference is a multiple of ωi​. Its leading coefficient, by the divided difference recurrence, is the order-k B-spline Ni​(t). Thus ℓi+1​(x,t)−ℓi​(x,t)=ωi​(x)Ni​(t). Telescoping proves the Marsden identity. Repeated knots require the well-defined confluent or limiting convention.
