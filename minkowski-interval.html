Minkowski interval (s2=ημν​ΔxμΔxν)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Special relativity Minkowski spacetime Minkowski metric
OurBigBook.com Words: 246 Articles: 2
The squared interval between two events in Minkowski spacetime is the quadratic form of their coordinate difference under the Minkowski metric. In signature (−,+,+,+) it is −c2Δt2+∣Δx∣2. Lorentz transformations preserve this quadratic form; timelike, spacelike and null separation correspond to negative, positive and zero values.
The opposite metric signature (+,−,−,−) reverses the sign of the Minkowski interval, giving c2Δt2−∣Δx∣2; the physical classification and proper time stay the same. State the convention before using a sign to identify a separation. With the mostly-plus convention here, a straight clock segment has s2=−c2Δτ2, while a light signal has s2=0. [ref].
