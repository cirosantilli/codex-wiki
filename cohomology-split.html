Cohomology (H∗(X;R))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 96
Cohomology is the contravariant counterpart of homology, formed from cochains and coboundaries. A coefficient ring equips it with the cup product and hence with a graded ring structure.
