Stopped martingale in discrete time

For a discrete martingale and stopping time T,
M(n+1)∧T​−Mn∧T​=1{T>n}​(Mn+1​−Mn​).
(18)
The indicator is measurable at time n, so the stopped process is a martingale. If it is uniformly bounded and T<∞ almost surely, bounded convergence gives EMT​=EM0​ directly.
