Algebraic curve

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Algebraic variety
OurBigBook.com Words: 844 Articles: 14
An algebraic curve is a one-dimensional algebraic variety. On a smooth algebraic curve, the local ring at every closed point is a discrete valuation ring, which allows orders of zeros and poles to be recorded as a divisor on an algebraic curve.
