Primary Greek and accompanying historical English text. Book II.3, treatment of cardiac affections: impending collapse, the rationale for selective depletion, and the assigned sequence of venesection, cupping, wine, poultices and enemas. Distinguish this work from the existing Causes and Signs of Chronic Diseases.
