Posterior variance (Var(θ∣y))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics Bayesian posterior
OurBigBook.com Words: 99 Articles: 1
A posterior variance is the variance of a parameter under its Bayesian posterior, namely E[(θ−E[θ∣y])2∣y] when finite. It describes remaining uncertainty after observing data and is distinct from the repeated-sampling variance of a fitted estimator.
