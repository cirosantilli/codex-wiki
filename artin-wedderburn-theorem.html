Artin–Wedderburn theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Central simple algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 187 Articles: 1
Every finite-dimensional semisimple algebra is a finite product of matrix algebras over division algebras. In particular, every central simple algebra over k is isomorphic to Mr​(D) for a finite-dimensional central division algebra D over k. The integer r and the isomorphism class of D are unique.
Indeed, decompose the right regular module of a finite-dimensional semisimple algebra A as
AA​≅⨁i=1s​Sini​​,

where the Si​ are pairwise nonisomorphic simple modules. The Schur lemma makes Di​=EndA​(Si​) a division ring and gives HomA​(Si​,Sj​)=0 for i=j. Left multiplication identifies
A≅EndA​(AA​)≅∏i=1s​Mni​​(Di​).
(3)
Conversely, the column module over Mn​(D) is simple and the regular module is a direct sum of n copies of it, so finite products of such matrix rings are semisimple. The simple-module decomposition also proves uniqueness up to permuting the factors.
