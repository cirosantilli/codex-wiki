Independent-volume supernova quenching (q(eνt−1)=B(r))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy Galaxy evolution Stellar feedback
OurBigBook.com Words: 149 Articles: 2
Before local expulsion, a linear star-formation law ψ=νρg​ without return gives ρg​=ρ0​e−νt. If q=γESN​ is the energy supplied per unit stellar mass, accumulated energy is qρ0​(1−e−νt). A fixed binding energy per unit remaining gas mass B(r) gives the threshold q(eνt−1)=B(r). This assumes a specified retained gravitational profile and neglects exchange between volumes.
