If a smooth real phase ϕ has a unique nondegenerate interior maximum at t0​, then
∫a(t)exϕ(t)dt∼a(t0​)exϕ(t0​)x∣ϕ′′(t0​)∣2π​​.
(224)
For a maximum at an endpoint b with ϕ′(b)>0, the leading factor is a(b)exϕ(b)/(xϕ′(b)).
