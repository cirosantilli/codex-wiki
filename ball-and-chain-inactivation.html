Ball-and-chain inactivation

 Home Biology Biological cell Membrane potential Voltage-gated ion channel Ion-channel inactivation
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A tethered intracellular particle blocks an open ion channel from the cytoplasmic side. In classical Shaker-type channels the particle is N-terminal: removing it suppresses fast inactivation, while cytoplasmic application of a corresponding peptide can restore block. The pore-blocking mechanism differs from ordinary activation-gate closure.
