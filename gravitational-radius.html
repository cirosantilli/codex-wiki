Gravitational radius (Rg​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Virial theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 107 Articles: 1
For a self-gravitating system of mass M and Newtonian gravitational potential energy W<0, its gravitational radius is defined by
Rg​=−WGM2​.
(20)
It converts the total binding energy into a characteristic size without assuming a particular density profile.
