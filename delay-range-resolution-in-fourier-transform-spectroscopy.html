Delay-range resolution in Fourier transform spectroscopy (δλ∝λ2/L)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Spectrometer Fourier transform spectroscopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
A Michelson interferometer mirror displacement x changes round-trip optical path difference by 2x, giving delay τ=2x/c. An interferogram recorded over a finite delay range is multiplied by a window; the recovered spectrum is the true spectrum convolved with that window's Fourier transform. Its characteristic frequency resolution scales inversely with delay range. Consequently a mirror travel range L gives δν∝c/L and δλ≃(λ2/c)δν∝λ2/L. Numerical width factors depend on the scan definition, window and resolution criterion; the inverse-travel scaling does not.
