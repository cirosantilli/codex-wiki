Up-set

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Set family Boolean lattice
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
An up-set is a family U such that A∈U and A⊆B imply B∈U.
An up-set is upward closed under inclusion: if A belongs and A⊆B, then B belongs. This is the Boolean-lattice case of upper and lower sets.
