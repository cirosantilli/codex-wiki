Volume averages in a shearing box (⟨∂x​Q⟩=⟨∂y​Q⟩=⟨x∂y​Q⟩=⟨∂z​Q⟩=0)

OurBigBook.com Words: 35
Periodic azimuthal and vertical boundary terms vanish. Radial boundary terms cancel after the azimuthal translation prescribed by the shearing-periodic boundary condition. Consequently the volume average of a divergence is zero, enabling integration by parts for the smooth shearing-periodic fields.
