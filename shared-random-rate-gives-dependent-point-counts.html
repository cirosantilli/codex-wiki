Shared random rate gives dependent point counts (Cov(Π(A),Π(B))=Var(R)∣A∣∣B∣)

Draw one random variable R, taking values zero and two with equal probabilities. Conditional on R, generate a Poisson point process on the real line with constant rate R. This is a Cox process directed by the random measure Rdx: the same rate is shared by every window. It is a locally finite simple point process and a stationary point process. The law of total expectation gives μ(A)=E[R]∣A∣=∣A∣; the law of total variance and law of total covariance give, for bounded Borel sets A,B with A∩B=∅,
Var(Π(A))=∣A∣+∣A∣2,Cov(Π(A),Π(B))=∣A∣∣B∣.
(15)
The conditional covariance vanishes by independence, but the shared conditional means fluctuate together. This distinguishes conditional from unconditional independence.[ref]
For a window of length L, the exact probability mass function is
P(NW​=k)=21​1{k=0}​+21​e−2Lk!(2L)k​,k=0,1,2,….
(16)
In particular, its void probability functional is (1+e−2L)/2, compared with e−L for the unit-rate Poisson point process. Whole realizations can be empty or comparatively crowded, while their mean density remains one. This example demonstrates shared-rate statistical dependence, rather than a model of short-range offspring clusters.
