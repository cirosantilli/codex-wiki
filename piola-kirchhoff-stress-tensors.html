Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensors

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Constitutive equation Stress
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 3
These stress measures refer force or area to the reference configuration. Different index-order conventions distinguish a first Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor from its transposed nominal stress tensor. The second Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor has two material indices.
