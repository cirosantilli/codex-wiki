Least dense truth characterizes an open local operator (J has least u⟺j(p)=u⇒p)

OurBigBook.com Words: 76
For j=o(U), the dense monomorphism classifier consists of the truth values above u, so has least element u. Conversely, if it has least element u, density of a monomorphism is exactly containment of the pulled-back subterminal U. For a subobject S⊆X, the mono S→S is dense, so U∩S⊆S and j(p)≤u⇒p. Since j(u)=⊤, applying j to u∧(u⇒p)≤p gives u⇒p≤j(p). Hence equality holds.
