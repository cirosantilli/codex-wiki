Witt bordism classification

OurBigBook.com Words: 148 Articles: 1
For k≥1, the rational middle intersection pairing induces an isomorphism Ω4kWitt​≅W(Q). The restriction k>0 matters: the degree-zero group is Z. In positive dimension congruent to zero modulo four, a space is Witt null-bordant precisely when its middle intersection form is metabolic.
