Semisimple algebra

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
A finite-dimensional associative algebra is semisimple when its left regular module is a direct sum of simple modules. Equivalently, every left ideal is a direct summand. In particular, a semisimple algebra has no nonzero square-zero left ideal: if L2=0 and a module projection A→L exists, its value e at 1 satisfies e∈L and e2=e, forcing e=0.
