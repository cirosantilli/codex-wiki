Astronomical seeing

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Atmospheric optics Atmospheric turbulence
OurBigBook.com Words: 138 Articles: 4
Astronomical seeing is the atmospheric blurring of a stellar image, conventionally expressed as the angular full width at half maximum of its long-exposure point spread function, usually in arcseconds. For a large aperture in the ideal Kolmogorov model, it is approximately 0.98λ/r0​ radians, with Fried parameter r0​.
