Helical symmetry ((θ,z)↦(θ+ϵ,z+bϵ))

 Home Physics Symmetry (physics) Continuous symmetry
OurBigBook.com Words: 143 Articles: 1
A helical symmetry combines an axial rotation and a proportional axial translation. A helix with z=bθ is unchanged as a set by this transformation. If a mechanical Lagrangian is invariant, the Noether conserved quantity for a mechanical point symmetry is Lz​+bPz​, where the two momenta include all moving components. Pure axial rotation and pure axial translation need not separately be symmetries. The helical symmetry of a potential is a related special case in which the constraint is replaced by an invariant potential.[ref]
