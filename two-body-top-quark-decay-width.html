Here x=mW2​/mt2​, the bottom quark mass is neglected, and the W boson is on shell. The weak charged current gives the spin-averaged squared scattering amplitude gW2​∣Vtb​∣2(mt2​−mW2​)(mt2​+2mW2​)/(4mW2​). The two-body Lorentz-invariant phase space has p∗=(mt2​−mW2​)/(2mt​); multiplying by p∗/(8πmt2​) gives the displayed decay width. Its longitudinal-vector contribution explains the large-mt​ cubic growth, and its threshold factor makes the width vanish as mt​↓mW​. The formula requires mt​>mW​ and receives finite-mass and radiative corrections.
