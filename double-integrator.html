Double integrator (p˙​=v,v˙=u)

OurBigBook.com Words: 157
The double integrator is the linear time-invariant system in which one control input is integrated into velocity, and velocity into position. It is a mathematical idealization of one-dimensional motion under commanded acceleration; for a constant mass m and net force F, Newton's second law gives u=F/m. The worked examples here use normalized dimensionless quantities and assume no control disturbances, rather than describing a laboratory record. [ref]
