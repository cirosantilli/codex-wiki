Structural induction on lazy lists

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical proof Structural induction
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
For an admissible predicate on lazy lists P, prove P(⊥) and P([]), and prove P(xs)⇒P(x:xs) for every possibly partial head x. Constructor induction then establishes P on every finite partial approximation, and admissibility extends it to limits, including infinite lists. Without the bottom value case and the limit condition, ordinary finite structural induction does not justify a claim about infinite data.[ref]
