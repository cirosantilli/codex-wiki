Large-deviation rate of a minimum of independent copies (J(m)=I(m)+(k−1)infx≥m​I(x))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Convergence of random variables Large deviation principle Contraction principle for large deviations
OurBigBook.com Words: 136 Articles: 1
For fixed k independent random variables whose families have the same large deviation principle with good rate function I, the product large-deviation principle gives the sum of coordinate costs. Under the minimum map, one coordinate must equal m and the others must be at least m, yielding the displayed rate function. If I is convex with unique zero μ, then J(m)=I(m) for m≤μ and J(m)=kI(m) for m≥μ.
