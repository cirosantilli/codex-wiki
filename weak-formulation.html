Weak formulation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Solution of a partial differential equation Weak solution
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
A weak formulation multiplies a differential equation by a test function, integrates, and uses integration by parts to move derivatives away from the unknown function. Natural boundary conditions appear in the resulting boundary terms.
