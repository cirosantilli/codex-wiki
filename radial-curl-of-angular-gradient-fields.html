Radial curl of angular gradient fields (r⋅∇×f=r−1ΔS2​F)

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Geodesic Surface covariant derivative Surface gradient
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
Let ∇tan​F=∇F−er​∂r​F be the tangential surface gradient on a sphere of radius r>0. For smooth scalar fields, set f=Rer​+∇tan​G+er​×∇tan​F. Its radial curl detects only F:
(∇×f)⋅er​=r21​ΔS2​F,ΔS2​=sinθ1​∂θ​(sinθ∂θ​)+sin2θ1​∂ϕ2​.
(12)
To prove this, use the radial curl formula (rsinθ)−1[∂θ​(sinθfϕ​)−∂ϕ​fθ​]. The radial component R never enters it. The G terms cancel as Gϕθ​−Gθϕ​=0 by symmetry of second derivatives, while the F terms give the unit-sphere Laplace-Beltrami operator. Taking the dot product with the position vector r=rer​ gives the displayed r−1 version. This tangential gradient decomposition does not assert that the whole vector field is solenoidal.
