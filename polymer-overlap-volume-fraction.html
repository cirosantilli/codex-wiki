Polymer overlap volume fraction (ϕ∗∼Nv0​/R3)

 Home Chemistry Mixture Solution (chemistry) Polymer solution Polymer overlap concentration
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
The polymer overlap volume fraction counts the segment volume of one chain divided by its coil volume. If the segment volume is v0​ and R∼bNν, then ϕ∗∼(v0​/b3)N1−3ν. The site-volume convention v0​∼b3 gives N−4/5 for the three-dimensional Flory approximation and N−1/2 for an ideal chain. A geometric coil volume such as 4πRg3​/3 changes a prefactor; segment length and segment volume are not generally interchangeable without a model convention.
