Quadratic Lagrange triangle (P=P2​(K))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Finite element method Finite element
OurBigBook.com Words: 91
The quadratic Lagrange triangle uses degree-at-most-two polynomials on a nondegenerate triangle, with point evaluation at its three polygon vertices and three edge midpoints as the six finite element degrees of freedom. If λi​ are the affine functions of its barycentric coordinate system, the cardinal shape functions are
Ni​=λi​(2λi​−1),Nij​=4λi​λj​(i<j).
(13)
At a polygon vertex, one coordinate in the barycentric coordinate system is one and the others vanish; at an edge midpoint, two are 1/2 and the third is zero. These values prove the cardinal property. Since dimP2​(K)=6, this is an unisolvent finite element. Matching the two endpoint values and midpoint value on a shared edge of a polygon matches the whole quadratic restriction, producing a continuous global conforming finite element space.
