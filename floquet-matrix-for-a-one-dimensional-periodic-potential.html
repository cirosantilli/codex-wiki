Floquet matrix for a one-dimensional periodic potential

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum theory Bloch theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 62 Articles: 1
For a potential of period a, the Floquet matrix M(E) maps Cauchy data through one cell:
(ψ(x+a)ψ′(x+a)​)=M(E)(ψ(x)ψ′(x)​).
(51)
For a real Schrodinger equation, detM=1.
