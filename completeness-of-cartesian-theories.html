Completeness of Cartesian theories

 ... Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic First-order logic Cartesian theory Cartesian syntactic category
OurBigBook.com Words: 55
If a Cartesian sequent fails to factor its antecedent subobject A↪C through its consequent, the covariant representable functor CT​(A,−) is a set-valued countermodel. It preserves finite limits, and the element named by the antecedent inclusion belongs to the antecedent but cannot belong to the consequent. Hence validity in every set model implies Cartesian derivability.
