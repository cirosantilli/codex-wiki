Orthogonal complement for a bilinear form (W⊥={v:B(w,v)=0 for all w∈W})

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Bilinear form
OurBigBook.com Words: 69
For a bilinear form on a finite-dimensional vector space, the displayed right bilinear orthogonal complement is a subspace. Nondegeneracy identifies the ambient space with its dual and gives dimW⊥=dimV−dimW. If the form is symmetric or alternating, orthogonality is reciprocal and (W⊥)⊥=W. For a general nonsymmetric form, left and right complements must be distinguished. A restricted symmetric or alternating form has radical of a bilinear form W∩W⊥; quotienting by that radical of a bilinear form gives a nondegenerate induced form.
