Thermal and electrical relaxation times in metals (κe​/(σT)=L0​τκ​/τσ​)

 ... Thermodynamics Heat Thermal conduction Fourier's law Thermal conductivity Wiedemann-Franz law
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
For a degenerate isotropic parabolic electron band, electrical conductivity is σ=ne2τσ​/m and electronic thermal conductivity is κe​=π2nkB2​Tτκ​/(3m). Their ratio is κe​/(σT)=L0​τκ​/τσ​, where L0​=(π2/3)(kB​/e)2 is the Lorenz number. Nearly elastic scattering that relaxes charge and heat currents similarly gives τκ​≃τσ​ and the Wiedemann-Franz law. Inelastic scattering can relax heat current and directed momentum differently, invalidating the simplest identification even in a good metal.
