Density positivity for an axisymmetric logarithmic potential (q2≥1/2)

 ... Branches of physics Classical mechanics Newton's law of universal gravitation Newtonian gravitational field Newtonian gravitational potential Axisymmetric logarithmic gravitational potential
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
The Poisson equation for Newtonian gravity gives
ρ=4πGq2V2​(R2+q−2z2)2R2+(2−q−2)z2​.
(3)
For V>0, nonnegative mass density away from the origin is equivalent to q2≥1/2. Strict positivity requires q2>1/2. This condition does not by itself guarantee a nonnegative galactic distribution function.
