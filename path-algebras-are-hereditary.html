Path algebras are hereditary (ExtkQn​(V,W)=0(n≥2))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Module theory Hereditary ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 45
The standard projective resolution of a quiver representation has length one, even for quivers with oriented cycles. Thus every path algebra is a hereditary ring, and higher extension groups vanish. Applying a long exact sequence of Ext groups to 0→I→K→C→0 gives a surjection Ext1(K,W)↠Ext1(I,W), since the next term Ext2(C,W) is zero. This is the hereditary step in the Ringel lemma on bricks.
