Strict positivity implies coercivity for an elliptic Dirichlet form

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Elliptic boundary value problem Uniformly elliptic operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
Let B[u,v]=∫U​aij∂i​u∂j​v+cuv on H01​(U) for a bounded smooth domain, smooth symmetric uniformly positive coefficients, and bounded real c. If B[u,u]>0 for every nonzero u, then B[u,u]≥α∥u∥H12​ for some α>0. If this failed, choose ∥uj​∥H1​=1 with B[uj​,uj​]→0. The Rellich-Kondrachov compactness theorem gives a subsequence weakly converging in H01​ and strongly in L2. The principal energy has weak lower semicontinuity, and the potential term converges, so strict positivity forces the limit to be zero. Uniform ellipticity then gives ∥Duj​∥2​→0, contradicting normalization. Merely being a positive semidefinite operator is insufficient: −Δ−λ1​ has a nonzero Dirichlet eigenfunction of zero energy.
