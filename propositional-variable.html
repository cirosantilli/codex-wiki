Propositional variable (pi​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Mathematical logic Propositional logic
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A propositional variable is an atomic symbol assigned a truth value by a Boolean valuation. A propositional formula is built from these symbols by logical connectives. The variables carry no quantifiers over individuals; their values may be chosen independently.
