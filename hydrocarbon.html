Hydrocarbon

 Home Chemistry Chemical substance Chemical compound
OurBigBook.com Words: 353 Articles: 18
A hydrocarbon contains only carbon and hydrogen. A fatty acid also contains oxygen; calling its long chain a hydrocarbon region does not make the whole fatty acid a hydrocarbon. See IUPAC Gold Book: hydrocarbons.
