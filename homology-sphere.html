Homology sphere

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Cohomology Cap product Fundamental class Poincare duality
OurBigBook.com Words: 212 Articles: 3
An integral homology d-sphere is a space with the same integral homology as Sd. Removing one point from a closed oriented integral homology sphere produces an acyclic space by the long exact sequence of the pair and the local orientation isomorphism in degree d.
