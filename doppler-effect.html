Doppler effect

 Home Physics Wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 2
The Doppler effect changes the observed frequency of a wave when source and observer move relative to one another. For light and a small radial recession speed vr​≪c, Δλ/λ≃vr​/c, where λ is wavelength. This is the basis of Doppler spectroscopy.
