Renormalization of the tent map near its fixed point (h∘Fμ2​∘h−1=Fμ2​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Discrete dynamical system Interval map Tent map
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 1
For 1<μ≤2​, the interval J=[1/(μ+1),μ/(μ+1)] is invariant under the second iterate of the tent map. The affine coordinate h(x)=(μ/(μ+1)−x)/((μ−1)/(μ+1)) conjugates that restriction to the tent map with parameter μ2. Its two linear pieces give the identity directly. This turns the two-iterate horseshoe threshold into a four-iterate threshold and explains why chaotic invariant sets need not have periodic points near the original nonzero fixed point.
