Maser discovery experiment

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Maser Ammonia maser
OurBigBook.com Words: 147
Ammonia gas entered a vacuum chamber through fine channels; the selected beam entered a cavity with input and output waveguides.[ref] With external microwaves swept across a molecular resonance, the 1954 experiment read an increase in transmitted power; with sufficient beam emission, it sustained oscillation.[ref]
The 1955 readout mixed two maser signals with a local oscillator in a crystal detector, amplified them at an intermediate frequency, and combined them in a diode detector. An oscilloscope showed their beat frequency. Changing cavity tuning, source pressure or focuser field tested sensitivity; excessive detuning stopped oscillation.[ref] The beat tests relative frequency stability; it does not by itself establish an absolute frequency or an intrinsic linewidth independent of detector noise. Detuning also changes the field–molecule interaction, so the threshold model's constant G cannot describe arbitrary tuning scans.
