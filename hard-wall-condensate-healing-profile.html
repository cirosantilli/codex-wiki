A repulsive condensate next to an impenetrable planar wall has Dirichlet boundary condition ψ(0)=0. Writing ψ=n0​​f reduces its stationary Gross–Pitaevskii equation to ℓh2​f′′+(1−f2)f=0. The bulk conditions f→1,f′→0 give ℓh2​(f′)2=(1−f2)2/2 and hence the displayed number density. Omitting n0​ describes normalized number density, not dimensional number density.
