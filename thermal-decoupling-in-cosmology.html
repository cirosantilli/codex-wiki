Thermal decoupling in cosmology

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmological entropy conservation
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 27
A particle species thermally decouples when its interaction rate falls below the Hubble parameter. Its collisionless relativistic distribution subsequently redshifts with temperature proportional to a−1, while annihilations among still-coupled species can heat the remaining plasma.
