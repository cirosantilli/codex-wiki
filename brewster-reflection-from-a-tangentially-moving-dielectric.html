Brewster reflection from a tangentially moving dielectric (∣βB​∣=n/1+n2​)

OurBigBook.com Words: 67
A vacuum ray incident normally on a dielectric moving parallel to its surface becomes oblique in the material rest frame, with sini=∣β∣. For a transparent nonmagnetic dielectric, the rest-frame Brewster angle satisfies taniB​=n, giving ∣βB​∣=n/1+n2​. The reflected electric field is perpendicular to the plane containing the ray and the velocity. A real refractive index n=1 is assumed; dispersion makes the condition implicit through the rest-frame frequency, and absorption generally removes exact extinction.
