sl2 matrix commutator example (sl2​(k)=kH⊕kE⊕kF)

OurBigBook.com Words: 170
Over a field k, the trace-zero 2×2 matrices form the special linear Lie algebra sl2​(k). The matrix trace of a commutator is zero, so this is a Lie subalgebra of the general linear Lie algebra. A basis is
H=(10​0−1​),E=(00​10​),F=(01​00​).
(17)
In fact (ac​b−a​)=aH+bE+cF, uniquely. The individual products are
HE=E,EH=−E,HF=−F,FH=F,EF=(10​00​),FE=(00​01​).
(18)
Subtracting in the stated order gives
[H,E]=2E,[H,F]=−2F,[E,F]=H.
(19)
For characteristic different from two, this is the standard sl2 triple; see Etingof, Section 11.4. The products remain valid in characteristic two, but there H=I is central and 2E=2F=0, so the familiar characteristic zero structure changes.
The following table gives all nine basis brackets. Each entry is [row,column]; the diagonal vanishes by alternation, and exchanging row and column reverses the sign.
[ , ]HEF
H02E−2F
E−2E0H
F2F−H0
Bilinearity now computes any commutator, for example
[H+E,2F−E]=2[H,F]−[H,E]+2[E,F]=2H−2E−4F.
(20)
The three structure constants of a Lie algebra relations encode all of this multiplication. They also show why the sl2 triple is useful in Lie algebra representations: the actions of E and F move between eigenvalues of the action of H in steps of two.
