At the active clock edge, the next state is Q+=J¬Q∨¬KQ: inputs 00 hold, 01 reset, 10 set, and 11 toggle. In particular, K=0 gives Q+=J∨Q, whereas J=K=1 gives Q+=¬Q. The standard function table is illustrated by a positive-edge device in the [ref]. Its asynchronous reset is active high; another circuit may specify an active-low clear.
