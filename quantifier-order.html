OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 2
In general, exchanging a universal and an existential quantifier changes a formula's meaning. A uniform witness satisfies a condition for every input, while a pointwise witness may vary with the input. For the equality relation on a two-element set, ∀x∃y(x=y) holds but ∃y∀x(x=y) fails.
