Second mass moment tensor (Iij​=∫ρ(x)xi​xj​d3x)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Gravitational wave Mass quadrupole moment
OurBigBook.com Words: 80 Articles: 1
The second mass moment tensor records the quadratic spatial distribution of mass. Its trace-free part is the mass quadrupole moment Qij​=Iij​−δij​Ikk​/3. The mechanical moment-of-inertia tensor instead equals Jij​=δij​Ikk​−Iij​. A rigid rotation L transforms the second mass moment tensor as I(t)=L(t)I(0)L(t)T.
