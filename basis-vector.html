Basis vector (ei​∈B)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Basis
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
A basis vector is one of the vectors in a specified basis of a vector space. Its coordinate column in that basis has one entry equal to one and all other entries zero. Columns of a matrix of a linear map are the images of domain basis vectors, expressed in the chosen codomain basis.
