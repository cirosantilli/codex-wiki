Localization length (∣ψn​∣∼e−∣n−n0​∣a/ξ)

The localization length ξ measures the decay scale of a localized eigenstate's wave amplitude envelope. Here a is the site spacing and n0​ its centre. With this amplitude convention, probability decays on half that scale:
∣ψn​∣∼e−∣n−n0​∣a/ξ,∣ψn​∣2∼e−2∣n−n0​∣a/ξ.
(10)
Individual random tails fluctuate; this notation describes their typical envelope, not exact equality at every site. A localization length much greater than a finite sample's length makes a state look extended inside that sample. The participation ratio below is an effective site count, not an independent fitted value of ξ.
