Image smoothing

 Home Computer science Image processing Diffusion image processing

Image smoothing suppresses rapid fluctuations attributed to noise. Linear heat equation image smoothing attenuates Fourier frequencies by e−t∣ξ∣2 but also blurs image edges. Nonlinear filtering uses image signal geometry to distinguish fluctuations from meaningful transitions.
