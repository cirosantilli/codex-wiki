Galaxy stellar mass (M∗​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Galaxy
OurBigBook.com Words: 430 Articles: 8
Galaxy stellar mass is the total mass in a galaxy's stars and stellar remnants. It is usually inferred from luminosity and colour or a spectral-energy-distribution fit, so it depends on stellar-population models, dust treatment, and the adopted initial mass function.
