Evaluation of a forcing name (val(τ,G))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Forcing Forcing name
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
Interpret a forcing name recursively by val(τ,G)={val(σ,G):∃p∈G ((σ,p)∈τ)}. The collection of interpreted ground-model names is the generic extension.
