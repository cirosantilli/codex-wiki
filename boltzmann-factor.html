Boltzmann factor (e−βE)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Canonical ensemble
OurBigBook.com Words: 20
At inverse temperature β=1/(kB​T), the Boltzmann factor e−βE is the relative canonical ensemble weight of a microstate with energy E.
