Rotational spectrum

 Home Chemistry Molecule Molecular rotation Rotational spectroscopy
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A set of spectral lines associated with transitions between molecular rotational energy levels. A polar linear rigid rotor has electric-dipole transitions with ΔJ=±1; in the ideal model adjacent absorption frequencies are separated by twice the rotational constant in frequency units.
