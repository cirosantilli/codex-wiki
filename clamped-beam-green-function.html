For a unit-length elastic beam with unit bending stiffness, the Green function of Dx4​ with G=Gx​=0 at both endpoints is the displayed kernel. It is piecewise cubic and continuous through its second derivative, while Gxxx​(ξ+,ξ)−Gxxx​(ξ−,ξ)=1. A constructive form is G=ax2+bx3+(x−ξ)+3​/6, with a=ξ(1−ξ)2/2 and b=−(1−ξ)2(1+2ξ)/6. The right endpoint conditions determine these coefficients. Thus y(x)=∫01​G(x,ξ)f(ξ)dξ solves the clamped static beam problem.
