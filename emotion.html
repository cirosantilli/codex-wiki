Emotion

 Home Science Psychology
OurBigBook.com Words: 904 Articles: 32
A coordinated, context-sensitive pattern involving appraisal, subjective feeling, bodily changes and action tendencies. These components can dissociate; a facial expression is evidence about emotion rather than an infallible definition of it.
