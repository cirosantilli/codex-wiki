Expected distortion (E[d(X,X)])

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Rate-distortion theory Rate-distortion function Distortion measure (information theory)
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Expected distortion is the expectation of the reconstruction cost under the joint probability distribution of X and X. It weights common source outcomes more strongly than rare ones. A bound on expected distortion is therefore different from a bound on the cost for every input, or on the probability of exceeding a specified cost.
