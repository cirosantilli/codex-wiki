Salt-rejection function for a saline Stefan front (F(λ)=1−π​λeλ2erfcλ)

For salt-free ice and a liquid concentration profile proportional to erfc[x/(2Dt​)], salt rejection at a=2λDt​ gives Ci​F(λ)=C0​, where D is the salt diffusion coefficient, and Ci​,C0​ are the interface and far-field concentrations. This follows from −DCx​(a+)=a˙Ci​. The function is positive and strictly decreasing for all real λ, since an integration by parts gives
F(λ)=2∫0∞​ue−u2−2λudu,F′(λ)=−4∫0∞​u2e−u2−2λudu<0.
(18)
Consequently Ci​=C0​/F(λ) increases with λ, equals C0​ at zero, and is enriched for freezing and diluted for melting. The positivity proof also prevents spurious negative interface concentrations when using the complementary error function formula.
