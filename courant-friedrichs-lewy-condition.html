Courant–Friedrichs–Lewy condition

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Finite difference Finite difference method Courant number
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
A Courant–Friedrichs–Lewy condition requires the numerical domain of dependence to contain the differential equation's physical domain of dependence. It commonly bounds a ratio such as cΔt/Δx for an explicit hyperbolic scheme.
