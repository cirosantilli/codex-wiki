Reflection lower bound for two-point percolation ((gk​/(8k))2≤h2k​≤g2k​)

For bond percolation on the square lattice, write hn​=Pp​(0↔(n,0)) and gk​=Pp​(0↔∂[−k,k]2). A maximal-probability boundary graph vertex can be placed at (k,j) by symmetry. Reflection about x=k identifies its connection probability from 0 with that from (2k,0). The Harris-FKG inequality yields (gk​/(8k))2≤h2k​≤g2k​ for k≥1.
