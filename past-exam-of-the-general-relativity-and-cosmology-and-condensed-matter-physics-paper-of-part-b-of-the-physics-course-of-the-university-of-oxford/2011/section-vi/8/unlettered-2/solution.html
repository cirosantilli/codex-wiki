Use the paper's stipulated face-centered cubic lattice, which has twelve nearest neighbours. The saturated one-ion alignment scale is
(gμB​J)Be,sat​=J+13J​kB​TC​≃0.135eV.
(40)
Dividing the effective field among twelve equivalent neighbours gives the per-bond exchange interaction scale
Ebond​≃12gμB​JBe,sat​​≃1.12×10−2eV=11.2meV.​
(41)
To specify the convention, write the Heisenberg model as H=−J∑⟨ij⟩​Ji​⋅Jj​, with dimensionless angular-momentum operators and each bond counted once. Then gμB​Be​=12J⟨Jz​⟩, so J=3kB​TC​/[12J(J+1)]≃11.2meV here. A convention with −2J′ instead has J′=5.62meV.
