Near an isolated Bragg scattering degeneracy, ordinary nondegenerate perturbation theory fails because the free energies coincide. Retain the resonant plane waves ∣k⟩, ∣k+G⟩ and apply degenerate perturbation theory. Absorb the constant V0​ into the energy origin.
