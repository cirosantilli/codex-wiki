Messuage lemma for groups

OurBigBook.com Words: 87
In the effective group form used for recognition problems, a finite group presentation of K and a word w yield, by an algorithm, a finite presentation of a group Kw​. If w=1 in K, then Kw​ is trivial; if w=1 in K, then the specified generators embed K in Kw​. One can additionally arrange that the normal closure of the image of w is all of Kw​. The finite presentation can be written without first deciding which case holds. This is the collapse-versus-embedding construction behind the Adian–Rabin theorem.
