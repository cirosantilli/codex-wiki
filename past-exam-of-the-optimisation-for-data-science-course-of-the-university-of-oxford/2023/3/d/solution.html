O(γLf​​logεΔ0​​) epochs,O(γnLmax​​logεΔ0​​) using Lf​=nLmax​.​​

The common coordinate-based guarantees favour blocks by a factor n/m=ℓ in epochs. In fact, with the conservative constants β=mLmax​ and Lf​=nLmax​, both methods have the same guaranteed contraction per update, while a block update uses only m coordinate partial derivatives.
This compares the bounds justified by the given constants. For a particular objective function, sharper constants give the comparison β/γ against Lf​/γ; an actual speedup of ℓ is not universal. Computing a block coordinate gradient also need not cost exactly m/n of a full gradient evaluation. The epoch complexity comparison assumes that coordinate partial derivative counts are a useful measure of work, and does not itself establish a wall-clock comparison.
