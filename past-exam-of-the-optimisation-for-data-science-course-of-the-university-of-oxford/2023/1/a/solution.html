Put z=x−α∇f(x) and τ=αλ. The proximal operator for the L1 norm separates into independent scalar minimization problems:
pi​=argminu∈R​{τ∣u∣+21​(u−zi​)2}.
(1)
The subgradient optimality condition is 0∈pi​−zi​+τ∂∣pi​∣. By the subgradient of the absolute value, a positive solution must satisfy pi​=zi​−τ>0, and a negative solution must satisfy pi​=zi​+τ<0. At zero, the subgradient optimality condition holds exactly when ∣zi​∣≤τ. The squared term makes the objective function a strictly convex function, so these cases give its unique minimiser.
Thus the proximal operator is the soft-thresholding operator
Sτ​(t)=⎩⎨⎧​t−τ,0,t+τ,​t>τ,∣t∣≤τ,t<−τ,​pi​=Sτ​(zi​).
(2)
Consequently the requested proximal gradient mapping, rather than just the proximal operator, is
[Gα​(x)]i​=αxi​−Sαλ​(xi​−α∂i​f(x))​,i=1,…,n.​
(3)
The corresponding proximal gradient method update is xi+​=Sαλ​(xi​−α∂i​f(x)). Small coordinates are set exactly to zero. When λ=0, the soft-thresholding operator is the identity and the proximal gradient mapping reduces to ∇f(x).
