G parity (G=Ce−iπJ2​)

 ... Branches of physics Quantum field theory Standard Model Quantum chromodynamics Flavor symmetry Isospin
OurBigBook.com Words: 203 Articles: 3
The product of charge conjugation and a half-turn in isospin is G parity. Both conjugations act on the isospin generators by (J1​,J2​,J3​)↦(−J1​,J2​,−J3​), so their product commutes with every generator. The Schur lemma therefore gives a common G-parity value throughout each irreducible isospin multiplet on which the operator acts internally. It can classify a charged multiplet even though individual charged states are not eigenstates of charge conjugation.
