Supremum error (En​=supx∈X​∣fn​(x)−f(x)∣)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Uniform convergence
OurBigBook.com Words: 60
For functions taking values in real numbers on a nonempty domain of a function X, the supremum error is the supremum of the absolute value of the approximation error over all inputs. It may be infinite and need not be attained. When finite, it is the supremum norm of fn​−f; uniform convergence is exactly En​→0. Bounding this one quantity gives an accuracy guarantee for every input, including inputs that change with n.
