Uncapacitated minimum-cost flow (min{cTf:Bf=b, f≥0})

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Minimum-cost flow problem
OurBigBook.com Words: 205 Articles: 3
An uncapacitated minimum-cost flow has nonnegative edge flows without finite upper capacities. The flow balances prescribe net supply at each vertex. For the oriented incidence matrix convention with tail +1 and head −1, vertex network dual potentials have network reduced costs cij​−πi​+πj​. Nonnegative network reduced costs and complementary slackness certify a feasible optimum. A feasible negative-cost directed cycle makes the problem unbounded below.
