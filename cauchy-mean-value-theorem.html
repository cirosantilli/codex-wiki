Cauchy mean value theorem

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Mean value theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
For a<b and real-valued functions f,g that are continuous on the closed interval [a,b] and differentiable on the open interval (a,b), there exists ξ∈(a,b) with
[f(b)−f(a)]g′(ξ)=[g(b)−g(a)]f′(ξ).
(70)
Apply Rolle's theorem to the linear combination h(x)=[f(b)−f(a)]g(x)−[g(b)−g(a)]f(x), which has equal endpoint values. The displayed product identity permits zero derivatives. For the ratio form, additionally assume g′(x)=0 for every x∈(a,b). Then Rolle's theorem ensures g(b)=g(a), so both denominators below are nonzero:
g(b)−g(a)f(b)−f(a)​=g′(ξ)f′(ξ)​.
(71)
Neither derivative needs to be continuous, and f′ may vanish. Choosing g(x)=x recovers the mean value theorem; the ratio form also gives the zero-over-zero endpoint case of L'Hôpital's rule. [ref]
