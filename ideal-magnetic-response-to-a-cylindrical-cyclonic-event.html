For an axisymmetric velocity u=(0,rf(r),g(r)) independent of z, write B=Bz​z+∇×(Az). Ideal induction becomes
(∂t​+f∂ϕ​)A=0,(∂t​+f∂ϕ​)Bz​=rg′​∂ϕ​A.
(41)
The first equation expresses magnetic flux freezing of the transverse flux function, while the second is axial stretching Br​g′. Starting from a uniform field B0​x gives A(r,ϕ,t)=B0​rsin(ϕ−f(r)t) and Bz​(r,ϕ,t)=B0​tg′(r)cos(ϕ−f(r)t). In complex azimuthal-mode notation, A=B0​re−ift and Bz​=iB0​tg′e−ift, obtained by elementary integrating factors. The transverse field has Br​=r−1Aϕ​ and Bϕ​=−Ar​, preserving zero divergence.
