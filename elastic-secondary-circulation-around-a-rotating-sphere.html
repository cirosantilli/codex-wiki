Elastic secondary circulation around a rotating sphere (Ψ=H(r)sin2θcosθ)

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Rheology Non-Newtonian fluid Viscoelasticity Second-order fluid
OurBigBook.com Words: 101
For a sphere of radius a rotating with angular velocity Ω, the primary Stokes flow has vϕ​=Ksinθ/r2, K=Ωa3. In the weak, inertialess second-order fluid correction, put c=ψ1​+2ψ2​. The stream function in the title has
H(r)=4μa3cK2​[1−3(a/r)2+2(a/r)3],vr​=r2H​(3cos2θ−1),vθ​=−rH′​sinθcosθ.
(11)
Both meridional components vanish at r=a and decay at infinity. Indeed, Erϕ​=−3Ksinθ/(2r3) gives [∇×∇⋅(2cE2)]ϕ​=72cK2sinθcosθ/r8, and the curl of the momentum equation reduces to (d2/dr2−6/r2)2H=72cK2/(μr7). For c>0 the secondary flow enters towards the equator and leaves near the poles; c<0 reverses it and c=0 removes this correction. The meridional circulation is even in the sign of sphere rotation. Inertial secondary flow is excluded from this calculation.
