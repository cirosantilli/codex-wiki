Guided parent-delivered cognitive behavioral therapy

 ... Science Psychology Clinical psychology Child and adolescent clinical psychology Youth anxiety treatment Parent involvement in youth anxiety treatment
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
A therapist supports a caregiver in delivering structured CBT activities with their child. Guidance includes reviewing practice and overcoming barriers, rather than merely distributing information. Evidence for a specified guided programme does not establish that an unsupported manual has the same effect.[ref]
