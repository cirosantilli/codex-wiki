Valuation ring

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Local ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 686 Articles: 12
A valuation ring is an integral domain R such that, for every nonzero element x of its fraction field K, either x∈R or x−1∈R. Equivalently, its ideals are totally ordered by inclusion.
