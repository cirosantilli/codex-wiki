Heterogeneous two-server queue (μ=μ1​+μ2​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Queueing theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 93
A queue with a Poisson process of arrivals and two independent service times with exponential distributions of different rates must distinguish the two possible single-customer states. If an arrival chooses either idle server with probability 1/2, the continuous-time Markov chain is reversible. Write a,b for the states with only server one or two busy, and n≥2 for total population. Its detailed balance equations give πa​=π0​ν/(2μ1​), πb​=π0​ν/(2μ2​) and πn​=π0​ν2(ν/μ)n−2/(2μ1​μ2​). These weights normalize exactly when ν<μ. In equilibrium, reversed upward transitions have total rate ν in every state, so time reversal of a continuous-time Markov chain makes the departure stream a Poisson process of rate ν. This output conclusion requires stationarity.
