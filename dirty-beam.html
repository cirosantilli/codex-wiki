Dirty beam (B(θ))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Observational astronomy Radio astronomy Aperture synthesis
OurBigBook.com Words: 489 Articles: 2
The dirty beam is the point spread function obtained by directly inverting the weighted sampled complex visibilities. For sampling positions uk​ and nonnegative weights wk​ with W=∑k​wk​>0, choose
B(θ)=W1​∑k​wk​e2πiuk​θ,B(0)=1.
(60)
Include each uk​ with −uk​ and the same weight so that B is real. Its unit peak convention means that an isolated unit point source contributes a shifted unit-height response. It is not a unit-integral convention. Sparse sampling can leave substantial subsidiary peaks, even when the longest interferometric baseline is large.
