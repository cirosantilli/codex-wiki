Excluded volume

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Pair potential Hard-sphere gas
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
The excluded volume is the volume of relative positions forbidden to another particle. For hard spheres of radius r0​, that forbidden region is a sphere of radius 2r0​.
