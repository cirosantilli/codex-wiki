Proper morphism

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Morphism of schemes
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 27
A morphism is proper when it is separated, of finite type, and universally closed. Properness is stable under base change and composition.
