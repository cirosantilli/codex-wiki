Intersecting shadow lemma (∣∂F∣≥∣F∣)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Extremal set theory Set family Intersecting family
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
For an intersecting family of r-sets with r≥1, its lower shadow has at least as many members as the family. Apply elementary set shifts and then the ballot reflection injection to obtain this bound.
