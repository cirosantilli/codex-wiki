Gravity anomaly

 Home Physics Branches of physics Geophysics
OurBigBook.com Words: 294 Articles: 8
A difference between observed gravity and a specified reference field after stated reductions. Free-air, Bouguer and isostatic anomalies remove different modeled effects; they are not interchangeable raw measurements.
