Selmer exact sequence and rank bound (Sel(n)(E/K))

For an elliptic curve over a number field K, global and local Kummer maps of an elliptic curve give 0→E(K)/nE(K)→Sel(n)(E/K)→Sha(E/K)[n]→0. Indeed a Selmer class maps to a locally trivial torsor; conversely any locally trivial torsor killed by n has a global Kummer-sequence lift whose local restrictions lie in the local Kummer images. For a prime ℓ, the Mordell-Weil theorem therefore gives dimFℓ​​Sel(ℓ)=rankE(K)+dimFℓ​​E(K)[ℓ]+dimFℓ​​Sha(E/K)[ℓ]. Thus a Selmer computation supplies a rigorous upper rank bound, while independent rational points supply a lower bound; the gap need not represent undiscovered rational points.
