Fundamental matrix of an absorbing continuous-time Markov chain ((−QT​)−1)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis Continuous-time multi-state model
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
For a finite absorbing continuous-time Markov chain with transient subgenerator QT​, the entry (−QT​)ij−1​ is the expected time spent in transient state j before absorption when starting from state i.
