Cyclic vector (V=span{v,Tv,T2v,…})

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 177 Articles: 2
For a linear operator T, a cyclic vector is a vector whose iterates span the entire vector space. In finite dimension d, the vectors v,Tv,…,Td−1v then form a basis: the first linear dependence makes the preceding span invariant under T, so later iterates cannot enlarge it. In topological settings one often requires the iterates to have dense span instead; that closure convention must be stated.
