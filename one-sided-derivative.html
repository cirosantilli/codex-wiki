One-sided derivative (f−′​(a),f+′​(a))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 51
The right and left one-sided derivatives are the limits of the difference quotient restricted respectively to positive and negative increments:
f+′​(a)=limh↓0​hf(a+h)−f(a)​,f−′​(a)=limh↑0​hf(a+h)−f(a)​.
(25)
At an interior point, differentiability is equivalent to existence and equality of these two finite limits. For the absolute value function, f+′​(0)=1 and f−′​(0)=−1, so it is continuous at zero but not differentiable there.
