Prescribed-zero construction of planar Abelian Higgs vortices (Δh+1−eh=4π∑a​ma​δza​​)

 ... Classical field-theory soliton Gauge-theory soliton Abelian Higgs model Nielsen-Olesen vortex Bogomolny vortex equation Taubes equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 86
Choose positions za​ and positive integer multiplicities ma​. Solve the Taubes equation with h→0 at infinity and h=2ma​log∣z−za​∣+O(1) near each position. The planar existence theorem supplies such a solution for every finite multiset of positions; see the original existence theorem. Set χ=∑a​ma​arg(z−za​), Φ=eh/2+iχ and A=∇χ−J∇h/2, with J(v1​,v2​)=(−v2​,v1​). The singular terms cancel in A, while Φ has precisely the selected zeros. These fields satisfy the Bogomolny vortex equations, with B=(1−∣Φ∣2)/2. Their vortex number is ∑a​ma​. Complex conjugation of Φ together with A↦−A gives antivortices.
