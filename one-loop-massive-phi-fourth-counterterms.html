One-loop massive phi-fourth counterterms (δm2=λm2/(16π2ϵ),δλ=3λ2/(16π2ϵ))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Real scalar field Quartic interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 116 Articles: 1
With interaction −μϵλϕ4/4! and d=4−ϵ, the two-point tadpole diagram has symmetry factor 1/2. The Euclidean massive loop integral gives its pole through Id,1​=−m2/(8π2ϵ)+O(1). The three four-point bubble diagrams each have symmetry factor 1/2, and the massive scalar bubble pole in four dimensions gives the quartic counterterm. The tadpole is independent of external momentum, so no one-loop wave-function renormalization is required. A vacuum-energy counterterm is additionally needed if vacuum diagrams are retained.
