Complex velocity (q=W′=u−iv)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Potential flow Complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 383 Articles: 5
For a complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow W=ϕ+iψ in the coordinate z=x+iy, the Cauchy-Riemann equations give W′=ϕx​−iϕy​=u−iv. A material point with physical velocity (u,v) therefore satisfies z˙=W′(z)​. Complex conjugation matters when deriving a pathline from a complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow.
