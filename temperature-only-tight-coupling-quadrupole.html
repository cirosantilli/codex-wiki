Temperature-only tight-coupling quadrupole (Θ2​=−20kΘ1​/(27τ′))

OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For Θ(μ)=∑ℓ​(−i)ℓΘℓ​Pℓ​(μ) and a Thomson collision operator omitting polarization feedback, the quadrupole relaxes with rate (9/10)ΓT​, where ΓT​=−τ′>0. Balancing its leading streaming source gives Θ2​=20kΘ1​/(27ΓT​). This differs from photon quadrupole in tight coupling with polarization; the two formulas expand different collision operators.
