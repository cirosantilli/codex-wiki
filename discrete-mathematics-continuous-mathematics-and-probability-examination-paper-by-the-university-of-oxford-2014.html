Discrete Mathematics, Continuous Mathematics and Probability examination paper by the University of Oxford (2014) (4223, 2014-06-26)

 Home Computer science Computer science bibliography
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
The ten-page University of Oxford Computer Science Prelims paper dated 26 June 2014. The public released examination archive contains it as 4223.pdf. Question 1's displayed function requires a domain restriction. Question 3's fractional-size recurrence relation needs a base case and an integer-rounding convention. Question 6's parameter range includes negative values, so the critical-point classification requires a split at α=−1 as well as α=1; its final derivative request also has a typographical superscript on f. These points were checked against printed pp. 2, 4 and 7.
