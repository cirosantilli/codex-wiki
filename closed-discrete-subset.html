Closed discrete subset

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Discrete space Discrete subset
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 1
A closed discrete subset D of a topological space X is a closed set whose subspace topology is the discrete topology. Every point of D therefore has a neighbourhood meeting D at that point alone. Closedness excludes all accumulation points outside D as well.
In a plane domain, a closed discrete subset is exactly a set meeting every compact subset in finitely many points. This local finiteness is necessary for a nonzero holomorphic function to have the subset as its entire zero set. A merely isolated-point subset can have an accumulation point outside the subset but inside the domain, as {1/n:n≥1}⊂C does.
