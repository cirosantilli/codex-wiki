Free scalar four-point function (⟨Tϕ1​ϕ2​ϕ3​ϕ4​⟩)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Wick's theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
For a centered free real scalar field, its vacuum time-ordered product has expectation ⟨Tϕ1​ϕ2​ϕ3​ϕ4​⟩=G12​G34​+G13​G24​+G14​G23​, where Gij​ is the Feynman propagator between insertions i,j. These are the three complete pairings in the Wick theorem. All remaining normal-ordered products have zero vacuum expectation. No fermionic interchange signs occur for a bosonic field.
