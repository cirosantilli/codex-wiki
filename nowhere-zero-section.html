Nowhere-zero section

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Fiber bundle Vector bundle Section of a vector bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 203 Articles: 4
A nowhere-zero section of a vector bundle is a section s with s(x)=0 in every fiber. Every trivial vector bundle of positive rank has one.
