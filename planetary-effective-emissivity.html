Planetary effective emissivity (ϵeff​=Fout​/(σTs4​))

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Irradiated planetary atmosphere Planetary equilibrium temperature
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
The ratio of top-of-atmosphere outgoing thermal flux to the blackbody flux at a chosen representative surface temperature. In global equilibrium with a uniform surface, ϵeff​=(Teq​/Ts​)4. It parametrizes the relation between surface and radiating temperatures and need not equal the material emissivity of the surface or an atmospheric layer. An effective value below one is compatible with a black surface when colder atmospheric layers contribute to radiation escaping to space.
