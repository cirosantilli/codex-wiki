Stationary Rossby waves in a sinusoidal zonal jet (k2+l2=(π/L)2)

OurBigBook.com Words: 59
For a sinusoidal mean jet on an f plane, its QG potential-vorticity gradient is (π2/L2+kd2​)U. Ground-stationary modes satisfy the displayed circle and can solve the full stationary shear equation even though their scale does not meet a local short-wave assumption. The local stationary group vector is 2Uk(k,l)/(k2+l2+kd2​), reducing to a zonal scalar formula only for l=0.
