Genetic marker

 Home Biology Genetics
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
A genetic marker is an observed inherited genomic state used to track chromosomes, test genetic association, or perform genetic linkage analysis. Genetic marker alleles need not themselves cause the phenotype. Several genetic markers on one nonrecombining region form a single observed haplotype.
