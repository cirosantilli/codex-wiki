Trivial formal deformation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Associative algebra Formal associative deformation
OurBigBook.com Words: 102 Articles: 1
A formal associative deformation is trivial if a k[[t]]-linear automorphism continuous for the adic topology T=id(modt) fixing the identity transports the deformed multiplication to the original one. This is equivalence by a formal change of coordinates, stronger than merely an abstract algebra isomorphism between unspecified middle terms.
