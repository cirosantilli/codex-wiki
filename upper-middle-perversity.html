Upper middle perversity (nˉ(k)=⌈(k−2)/2⌉)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Intersection homology Perversity
OurBigBook.com Words: 19
The upper middle perversity is nˉ(k)=⌈(k−2)/2⌉. It differs from the lower middle perversity by one at odd codimensions, and is complementary to it.
