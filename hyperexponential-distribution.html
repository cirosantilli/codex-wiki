Hyperexponential distribution (F(x)=∑i​wi​e−ri​x)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Mixture distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
A finite mixture distribution of exponential distributions with rates ri​>0 and nonnegative weights summing to one. Its expected value is ∑i​wi​/ri​ and its moment-generating function is ∑i​wi​ri​/(ri​−t) on the common finite domain. The mixture chooses one component for each observation; it is not a sum of independent exponential variables.
