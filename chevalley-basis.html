Chevalley basis

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 201 Articles: 3
A Chevalley basis of a complex semisimple Lie algebra consists of suitable coroot elements and one vector eα​ in each root space, chosen so all Lie bracket structure constants are integers. In particular [eα​,eβ​]=Nα,β​eα+β​ when the sum is a root, with Nα,β​=±(p+1) where β−pα is the last negative-direction root in the root string. For independent roots with no root sum the bracket is zero. The integral lattice makes reduction to other fields possible.
