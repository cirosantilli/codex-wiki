Odd-cycle copies from positive triangle density (#K3​≥βn3⟹#Cℓ​≥ζnℓ)

OurBigBook.com Words: 54
For any fixed odd length ℓ≥3, positive triangle in a graph density forces positive density of ℓ-cycles. A Szemerédi regularity lemma partition retains a triangle in a graph of regular dense pairs after the sparse and exceptional pairs are removed. Count embeddings of a proper three-colouring of Cℓ​ into those three clusters. The constants depend on β and ℓ, not on graph order.
