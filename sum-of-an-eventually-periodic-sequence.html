Sum of an eventually periodic sequence (∑j=0L−1​uN+j​=qC+∑j=0r−1​uN+j​,L=qT+r)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Sequence Eventually periodic sequence
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
Suppose a numerical sequence has period T from index N, and let C=∑j=0T−1​uN+j​. For L=qT+r with 0≤r<T, split the required sum into q complete periods and a tail of length r. Every complete period contributes C, proving the formula in the title. Precomputed prefix sums of one period make the tail lookup constant time. If this periodic sequence consists of the first differences of another sequence, the same formula extrapolates that original sequence by adding the initial value at N.
