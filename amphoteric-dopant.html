Amphoteric dopant

 Home Physics Semiconductor Semiconductor doping
OurBigBook.com Words: 71
An amphoteric dopant can act as a donor impurity or an acceptor impurity according to its incorporated lattice site or defect configuration. Silicon in gallium arsenide is the standard example: substitution on the gallium sublattice gives a shallow donor, while substitution on the arsenic sublattice gives a shallow acceptor. Its electrical effect therefore depends on growth conditions as well as total silicon concentration.[ref]
