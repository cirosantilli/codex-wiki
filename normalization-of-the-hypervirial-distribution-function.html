Normalization of the hypervirial distribution function (C=2(p+5)/2π5/2Γ(p/2)Γ((3p+3)/2)(p+1)Γ(2p+2)​)

In units G=M=a=1, velocity integration of F=CLp−2E(3p+1)/2 gives C=(p+1)Γ(2p+2)/[2(p+5)/2π5/2Γ(p/2)Γ((3p+3)/2)]. The beta function separates the radial-speed and angular integrals. This constant is positive for every p>0.
