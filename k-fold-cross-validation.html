K-fold cross-validation

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Statistical learning Cross-validation
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
K-fold cross-validation partitions observations into K folds, trains K times while holding out one fold at a time, and combines the held-out losses.
