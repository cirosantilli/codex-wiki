Ambient restriction formula for the spherical Laplacian (ΔSd​(F∣Sd​)=ΔF−Frr​−dFr​)

For a smooth ambient function near the unit sphere, the chain rule along the great circles γi​(t)=pcost+ei​sint gives (F∘γi​)′′(0)=D2F(ei​,ei​)−DF(p). Sum over an orthonormal basis of the tangent space and supplement it by the radial vector p. The geodesic trace formula for the Laplace-Beltrami operator then gives the displayed formula, evaluated at radius one. In particular a degree-ℓ harmonic polynomial restricts to a spherical harmonic with positive eigenvalue ℓ(ℓ+d−1).
