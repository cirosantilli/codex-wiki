Counting nilpotent matrices by the Fitting decomposition

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Nilpotent linear map Nilpotent matrix
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
There are qn2−n nilpotent matrices of size n over Fq​. Every matrix has a unique invariant direct sum on which it is nilpotent and invertible. Counting those ordered decompositions gives qn2/∣GLn​(q)∣=∑k=0n​ak​/∣GLk​(q)∣. Subtraction at n and n−1 yields an​=qn2−n. Translation by the identity counts unipotent matrices.
