Mean squared displacement (⟨∣X(t+τ)−X(t)∣2⟩)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion Diffusion coefficient
OurBigBook.com Words: 145 Articles: 2
A mean squared displacement is the expected value of a squared position change over a specified time lag. For isotropic free diffusion in d dimensions, it equals 2dDτ. A nonzero mean drift contributes its squared displacement as well as the displacement variance; specifying the tracked coordinates and whether the mean is subtracted is essential.
