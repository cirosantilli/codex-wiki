Risk-neutral pricing (Vt​=Bt​EQ​[X/BT​∣Ft​])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Risk-neutral measure
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 2
In a finite-state discrete-time financial market, a replicable terminal financial payoff X has the displayed value under any equivalent martingale measure Q for the bank account numéraire. Here Ft​ represents current information and conditional expectation averages over future states. The self-financing portfolio's discounted portfolio wealth is a martingale, so its current value equals the discounted expectation of its terminal value. In an incomplete market, nonreplicable claims need not have a unique price; continuous-time extensions require further conditions on admissible trading strategies and integrable discounted wealth.
