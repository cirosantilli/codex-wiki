One-parameter Poisson-Dirichlet distribution (PD(θ)=PD(0,θ))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Random partition Poisson-Dirichlet distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 239 Articles: 3
Take a Poisson random measure on (0,∞) with intensity θe−udu/u and arrange its atoms as J1​>J2​>⋯. The sum T=∑i​Ji​ is finite and positive almost surely, has Gamma distribution with shape θ and rate one, and the law of (Ji​/T) is PD(θ). The order is decreasing, so the first component is the largest; a size-biased selection of a component has a different law.
