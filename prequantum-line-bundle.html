Prequantum line bundle (F∇​=iω/ℏ)

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Symplectic geometry Geometric quantization Prequantization
OurBigBook.com Words: 46
A prequantum line bundle is a Hermitian line bundle over a symplectic manifold with unitary connection on a vector bundle of the displayed curvature. With ω=−dθ, a local connection is ∇=d−iθ/ℏ. Reversing the Hamiltonian-vector-field convention or passing to the dual line bundle reverses the corresponding curvature convention. Integrality of ω/(2πℏ) on closed two-cycles is the topological existence condition.
