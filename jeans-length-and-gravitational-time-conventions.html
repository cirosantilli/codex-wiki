Jeans length and gravitational time conventions (ℓJ​/cs​=32/3​tff​)

 ... Branches of physics Cosmology Linear cosmological density perturbation Jeans wavenumber Jeans instability Jeans length
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
The physical Jeans length is ℓJ​=cs​π/(Gρ0​)​. A pressureless uniform sphere of initial density ρ0​ has free-fall time of a uniform sphere tff​=3π/(32Gρ0​)​, giving the title relation. Thus a full-wavelength sound-crossing time and this exact free-fall convention agree in scaling, not numerical value. Using the scale kJ−1​ instead gives 1/(cs​kJ​)=1/4πGρ0​​, the inverse long-wave static Jeans growth-rate spectrum limit. At the threshold itself pressure balances gravity and the net growth rate vanishes; the pressureless free-fall time must not be confused with that marginal mode's infinite growth time.
