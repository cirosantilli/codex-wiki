Meridional ray bend threshold (Rc​=n2​r/(n1​cosβ−n2​))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Optical fiber Step-index optical fiber
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For a light ray launched on the centreline in the plane of a circular optical fiber bend, let R be centreline radius, r core radius and β the internal launch angle from the tangent. The straight segment's distance from the bend centre is Rcosβ. Thus its outer-wall angle of incidence satisfies sini=Rcosβ/(R+r). Equating this to n2​/n1​ gives the limiting total internal reflection threshold in the title, provided n1​cosβ>n2​. Radially displaced or nonplanar launches need different geometry; this is not a universal wave-optical loss cutoff.
