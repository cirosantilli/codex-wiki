Basis pursuit (min{∥x∥1​:Ax=y})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Sparse optimization Compressed sensing
OurBigBook.com Words: 525 Articles: 7
Basis pursuit minimizes an ℓ1 norm subject to exact linear measurements. Basis pursuit with a noise tolerance replaces the equality by ∥Ax−y∥2​≤η. A unique minimizer has linearly independent active columns, and hence at most rankA nonzero coordinates: a null direction on its support would make the objective locally affine on a feasible line, contradicting minimality or uniqueness.
