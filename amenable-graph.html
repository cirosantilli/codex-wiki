Amenable graph

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
A bounded-degree infinite graph is amenable when there are finite nonempty vertex sets Wn​ such that ∣∂+Wn​∣/∣Wn​∣→0, where ∂+Wn​ is the set of vertices outside Wn​ adjacent to it. These are graph Følner sets. Amenability makes surface contributions negligible compared with volume. The square lattice and any periodic finite decoration of it are examples.
