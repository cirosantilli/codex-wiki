The empty set is bounded. Otherwise choose p in the compact metric space X. The open balls B(p,n), n=1,2,…, form an open cover. A finite subcover exists by compactness; because the balls are nested, the largest one already covers X. Hence X⊆B(p,N) for some N.
For x,y∈X, the triangle inequality gives d(x,y)≤d(x,p)+d(p,y)<2N. Thus
diamX≤2N<∞,so X is bounded.​
(2)
The proof uses only compactness and the metric, not a coordinate system.
