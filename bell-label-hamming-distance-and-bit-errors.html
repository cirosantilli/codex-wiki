Bell-label Hamming distance and bit errors (pbit​=ϵw/2)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bell state Superdense coding Two-component Bell resource in dense coding
OurBigBook.com Words: 56
If a wrong Bell-label partner occurs with probability ϵ and differs from the intended two-bit label in w positions, its contribution to the error probability of a uniformly selected bit is ϵw/2. Relabeling the Bell states can change w while preserving the physical wrong-state probability. Thus symbol error probability and bit error probability are different quantities.
