Conditional inference in a location-scale family (Lμ,σ​(Yˉ,s∣A=a))

OurBigBook.com Words: 86
For normalized residuals Ai​=(Yi​−Yˉ)/s, Fisherian conditional inference uses the conditional distribution of (Yˉ,s) given the observed A=a. For almost every attainable ancillary value, the joint conditional density of Yˉ=m and s=t>0 is proportional to
tn−2σ−n∏i=1n​f0​(σm+tai​−μ​).
(2)
The factor tn−2 is the radial Jacobian on the (n−1)-dimensional centered residual space. After setting u=(m−μ)/σ and v=t/σ, the conditional density is proportional to vn−2∏i​f0​(u+vai​), which is parameter-free. Conditioning on a continuous ancillary uses a regular conditional distribution, rather than division by the probability of a singleton ancillary value.
