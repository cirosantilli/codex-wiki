Stress glut tensor (Γ)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Elastic wave Seismic moment tensor
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
A stress glut tensor describes a seismic source through an equivalent body-force density f=−∇⋅Γ. For a symmetric stress glut tensor supported away from the boundary, integration by parts makes its coupling to a normal mode equal to ∫Γ:e∗dV, where e is that mode's strain.
