Worldsheet Weyl anomaly

 ... Branches of physics String theory Bosonic string theory Polyakov action String nonlinear sigma model Sigma-model beta function
OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 1
A worldsheet Weyl anomaly is the quantum failure of the gauge-fixed string path integral to remain invariant under a Weyl transformation. Its local coefficients are the sigma-model beta functions, while its constant part is controlled by the total central charge.
