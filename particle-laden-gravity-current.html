Particle-laden gravity current

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Reduced gravity Gravity current Gravity-current box model
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 19
A particle-laden gravity current is driven by the excess density of suspended particles. Deposition decreases its reduced gravity, so a finite-volume current can approach a finite runout length.
