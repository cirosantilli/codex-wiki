Conjugates of one free generator freely generate

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Geometric group theory Free group
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
If a,b freely generate, then aiba−i for any distinct integer indices freely generate their subgroup. Group a reduced word in these new generators into blocks with nonzero exponents and different successive indices. Expansion produces alternating nonzero powers of b and interior powers aij+1​−ij​, so it cannot freely reduce to the empty word. Consequently every finite-rank free group embeds in a rank-two free group; free rank is not monotone under subgroup inclusion.
