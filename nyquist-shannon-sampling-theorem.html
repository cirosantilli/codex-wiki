Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 225 Articles: 3
A suitably band-limited function can be reconstructed from equally spaced samples when the sampling rate exceeds twice its largest ordinary frequency. Sampling more slowly aliases distinct frequencies in the Fourier transform. For spatial sampling, the same statement relates sample pitch to the shortest resolvable wavelength.
