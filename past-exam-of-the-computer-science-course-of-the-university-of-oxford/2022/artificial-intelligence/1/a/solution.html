Use a,w,g,r for the agent, monster, gold and arrow, and abbreviate sqij​ to sij​. A physically safe STRIPS plan is
​Move(a,s11​,s21​),Move(a,s21​,s22​),Shoot(a,s22​,r,w,s23​),Move(a,s22​,s23​),Move(a,s23​,s13​),Take(a,g,s13​),Move(a,s13​,s23​),Move(a,s23​,s22​),Move(a,s22​,s21​),Move(a,s21​,s11​).​
(1)
Every movement follows a listed adjacency predicate. The shot is taken from s22​ while the arrow is held, and kills the monster before entry into s23​. The pit at s12​ is never entered. The pickup establishes possession of the gold, and the last four movements restore the exit location while preserving that possession. Thus ten actions achieve the formal goal and the intended safe return; arriving at the exit is the source model’s representation of leaving.
