Write P=999/1000. If
t>P⟺n+3n​>1000999​⟺n>2997​,
(9)
then both players prefer buying against a buyer, since (t−P)/2>0. Both prefer not buying against a nonbuyer: 2t/3<2/3<P and t/3<1/3<P. These strict opposite best responses give the interior crossing of a coordination game.
An explicit fully mixed Nash equilibrium follows from indifference. Let p be X's probability of buying and q Y's. X is indifferent when
(1−q)(32t​−P)+q2t−P​=0,
(10)
and Y is indifferent when
(1−p)(3t​−P)+p2t−P​=0.
(11)
Solving gives
p=P+t/32(P−t/3)​,q=P−t/32(P−2t/3)​,t=n+3n​, n>2997.​
(12)
Both numerators and denominators are positive. For p<1 the required inequality is 2P−2t/3<P+t/3, equivalent to P<t; the same comparison for q<1 again reduces to P<t. Thus p,q∈(0,1), and the equations make both actions best responses for each player. By linearity of expected payoff, neither player can profit by any other mixed strategy. At n=2997 these formulas reach p=q=1 and are not fully mixed; the strict threshold matters.
