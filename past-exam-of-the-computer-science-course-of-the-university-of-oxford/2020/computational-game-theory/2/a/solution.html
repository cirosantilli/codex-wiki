The assignment is Pareto optimal. Write ai​ for the room selected by the ith student. Suppose another assignment a′ makes every student at least as well off. The first student already selected their uniquely most valuable room, so a1′​=a1​. Once the rooms of students 1,…,i−1 are fixed, the rooms available to student i in the original serial dictatorship are exactly the rooms that remain after those fixed assignments. Student i chose the unique maximum among them, so weak improvement requires ai′​=ai​.
Mathematical induction fixes every student's room. Thus a′=a and nobody can be strictly better off, contradicting the definition of a Pareto improvement. Strict room rankings and the absence of allocation externalities are the essential assumptions: a student's utility is determined solely by their own room.[ref]
