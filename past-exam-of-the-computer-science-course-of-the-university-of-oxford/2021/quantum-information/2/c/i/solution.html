Either experiment is possible. In the sequential experiment choose U=X. The first computational basis measurement on I/2 returns a uniform bit j and prepares ∣j⟩. The Pauli X gate changes it to ∣1−j⟩, so the second result is always opposite. The two possible records have probabilities Pr(0,1)=Pr(1,0)=1/2.

The Born rule gives the same two possible records, again with probability 1/2 each. The observed computational basis anticorrelation alone does not identify whether the correlation was generated sequentially or shared between two qubits.
