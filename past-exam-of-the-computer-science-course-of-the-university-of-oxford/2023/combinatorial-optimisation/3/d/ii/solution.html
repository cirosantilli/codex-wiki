 ... Past exam of the computer science course of the University of Oxford 2023 Combinatorial optimisation 3 d ii
OurBigBook.com Words: 75
First test for an isolated vertex, reporting infeasibility if one exists. Otherwise compute a maximum cardinality matching M using a general-graph polynomial-time algorithm, then add one arbitrary incident edge for each unmatched vertex in a matching. The proof in (i) shows that the resulting edge cover has minimum cardinality ∣V∣−∣M∣.
The final scan takes O(∣V∣+∣E∣) time with adjacency lists; the maximum cardinality matching computation is polynomial. One may obtain it from a general-graph maximum-weight matching routine with every weight equal to one.[ref]
