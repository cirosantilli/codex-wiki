Before using no-waste allocation to restrict the constraint domains, there is one constraint graph vertex for each of A,B,C,D,E. Every distinct pair has a disjointness binary constraint, and also the two directional envy-free allocation constraints. Thus the constraint graph is the complete graph K5​, with all ten edges.
The nonempty-bundle and no-waste allocation requirements are unary and add no edges. The original all-item coverage constraint has been removed because this variant permits unallocated items.
