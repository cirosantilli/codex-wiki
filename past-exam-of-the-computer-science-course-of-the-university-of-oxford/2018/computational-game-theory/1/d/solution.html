OurBigBook.com Words: 73
Under the intended positive rewards 0<h<d, a hunter at the all-hare strategy profile receives h. A unilateral switch to deer leaves only one participant. Since the integer threshold satisfies k>n/2 with n≥3, we have k≥2, so that deer hunt fails and pays zero. Thus no player can gain by switching, and the all-hare profile is a Nash equilibrium. At h=0 it remains an equilibrium through indifference; negative h would invalidate the claim.
