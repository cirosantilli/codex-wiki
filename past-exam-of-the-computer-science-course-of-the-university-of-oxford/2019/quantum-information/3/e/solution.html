One Grover iteration returns a marked label with certainty. Prepare ∣s⟩=N−1/2∑x​∣x⟩; apply the supplied phase oracle to negate amplitudes on the N/4 marked inputs; apply the Grover diffusion operator D=2∣s⟩⟨s∣−I; and measure in the computational basis. If F prepares ∣s⟩ from ∣0⟩, the reflection is D=F(2∣0⟩⟨0∣−I)F†, requiring no further oracle query.
After the oracle the amplitudes are 1/N​ on the 3N/4 unmarked labels and −1/N​ on the marked labels. Their average is
μ=N1​(4N​3N​−4N​N​)=2N​1​.
(32)
The reflection performs inversion about the mean, ax​↦2μ−ax​. It therefore sends every unmarked amplitude to zero and every marked amplitude to 2/N​. The resulting normalized state is
N​2​f(x)=1∑​∣x⟩.​
(33)
Each marked label has Born rule probability 4/N, every unmarked label has probability zero, and the marked probabilities sum to (N/4)(4/N)=1. This is one-step exact Grover search with a quarter of the inputs marked, using one reduced-oracle query. The positive integer N must be divisible by four; a qubit implementation may take N=2m, but the stated reflection proof does not assume that register convention.
