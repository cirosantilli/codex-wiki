Put δ=π/(2n). Between successive measurement bases, the squared overlap is c=cos2δ for retaining the same label and s=sin2δ for changing label. The first step obeys the same rule if we regard the initial state as label 0 at angle zero. Thus the recorded labels form a Markov chain with transition matrix (cs​sc​).
Let qk​ be the probability of label 0 after step k. Then q0​=1 and
qk​=cqk−1​+s(1−qk−1​),2qk​−1=(c−s)(2qk−1​−1).
(11)
Since c−s=cos(π/n), iteration yields
qn​=21​[1+cosn(nπ​)].​
(12)
This is the total final-outcome probability, including histories that change label an even number of times. The smaller quantity cos2n(π/(2n)) counts only the history with label 0 at every step and is not the requested total. For example, at n=3 the total is 9/16, whereas the uninterrupted history has probability 27/64. At n=1, the final basis is {∣1⟩,∣0⟩} up to signs, so the total probability of label 0 is zero, as the formula also gives. This distinguishes nonselective measurement-guided Zeno transfer from postselection on one record.
