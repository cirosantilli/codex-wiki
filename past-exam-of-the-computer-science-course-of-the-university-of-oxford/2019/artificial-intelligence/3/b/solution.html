Use direct-transfer STRIPS actions: one action includes picking up a block and putting it down again. There is no separate holding state. Ground b,y as distinct blocks and x as a support, either a block or the table, with x=b and x=y.
For Move(b,x,y), take
PreAddDel​={Block(b),Block(y),On(b,x),Clear(b),Clear(y)},={On(b,y)}∪({Clear(x)} if x=τ),={On(b,x),Clear(y)}.​
(13)
The preconditions ensure that b can be picked up and that the destination has room. Moving it frees a block source and occupies the destination, while Clear(b) persists. When x=τ, table availability remains true; its redundant addition is simply omitted. Since x is fixed in each ground action, this case distinction constructs ordinary add lists rather than introducing conditional action effects. The search transition model is (S∖Del)∪Add.
