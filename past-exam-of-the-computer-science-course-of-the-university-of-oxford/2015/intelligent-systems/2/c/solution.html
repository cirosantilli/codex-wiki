Every moved coin is exposed, and the last two moves put a smaller coin on a larger one. Thus
5<23−1=7 moves.​
(18)
Five is also optimal here: before the first move of c3​, both smaller coins must leave s1​. Neither can make its only move directly to the final destination and remain there, because it would obstruct the eventual arrival of c3​. Each smaller coin therefore needs at least two moves. Those four small-coin moves and at least one move of c3​ give the matching lower bound.
