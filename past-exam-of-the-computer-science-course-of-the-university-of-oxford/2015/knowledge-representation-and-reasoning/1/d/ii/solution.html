The usual transformation to clausal normal form preserves equisatisfiability, but not necessarily logical equivalence​. Removing implications and distributing logical conjunction and logical disjunction preserve logical equivalence. Skolemization introduces new symbols and chooses witnesses as functions of preceding universally quantified variables.
For example,
φ=∀x∃yR(x,y)⟼ψ=∀xR(x,f(x)).
(11)
On the domain {0,1}, let R be equality. Then φ is true. Expand this same first-order structure by interpreting f as the constant function with value 0. The resulting first-order structure falsifies ψ at x=1. Interpreting f as the identity instead satisfies ψ.
Thus every model of ψ has a reduct satisfying φ, and a model of φ can be expanded with suitable witnesses to satisfy ψ. That model-expansion property gives equisatisfiability, not logical equivalence for every interpretation of the added function. If “CNF” means a conjunctive matrix with existential quantifiers retained, Skolemization has not yet happened and an equivalent form is possible; this is why the convention must be specified.
