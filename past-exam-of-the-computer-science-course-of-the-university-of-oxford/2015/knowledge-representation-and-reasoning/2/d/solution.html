For EL with bottom, the eligible axioms are (2),(4),(5)​. Axioms (1) and (3) contain universal restrictions, and (3) also contains a disjunction, so they are outside its syntax. For plain EL, whose constructors are ⊤, atomic names, conjunction and existential restriction, the eligible axioms are (2),(5)​; (4) is excluded because it uses ⊥. These conventions should not be conflated.
Here is a normalized TBox for the bottom-inclusive version. Introduce one fresh description logic concept X for ∃R.A, and use
∃R.AXA⊓BC​⊑X,X⊑∃R.A,⊑∃R.X,⊑⊥,⊑∃P.⊤,∃P.⊤⊑C.​​
(27)
These are the usual normalized forms: an existential restriction on the left with an atomic right-hand side, an atomic left-hand side with an existential restriction on the right, or a conjunction of atomic names implying ⊥. Here ⊤ is permitted as a filler.
The first two general concept inclusions force X≡∃R.A. The third then says ∃R.A⊑∃R.∃R.A, exactly (2). Conversely, any model of (2) extends by X=(∃R.A)I to satisfy all three normalized general concept inclusions. The last two are the two directions of (5). For plain EL, delete just A⊓B⊑⊥ from this normalized TBox.
