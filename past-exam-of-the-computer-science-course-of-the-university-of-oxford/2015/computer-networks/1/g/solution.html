All the following polynomial coefficients and sums lie in the field with two elements, so a+a=0. For the constant polynomial,
a=(x+1)⋅0+a.
(8)
For the linear polynomial, subtracting the leading multiple means adding it:
ax+b=(x+1)a+(a+b).
(9)
For the quadratic polynomial, first cancel ax2 using ax(x+1), leaving (a+b)x+c; then cancel (a+b)x using (a+b)(x+1). Thus
ax2+bx+c=(x+1)(ax+a+b)+(a+b+c).
(10)
The requested polynomial quotients and polynomial remainders are therefore
aax+bax2+bx+c​:::​​Q=0,Q=a,Q=ax+a+b,​​R=a,R=a+b,R=a+b+c.​​
(11)
For a general polynomial P(x)=∑i=0n​pi​xi, write P=(x+1)Q+R with constant R. Since 1+1=0, polynomial evaluation at x=1 gives P(1)=R. This is the polynomial remainder theorem with −1=1 in the field with two elements. Hence
P(x)mod(x+1)=P(1)=p0​+p1​+⋯+pn​.​
(12)
The polynomial remainder is precisely the parity bit obtained by taking the exclusive or of all the polynomial coefficients.
