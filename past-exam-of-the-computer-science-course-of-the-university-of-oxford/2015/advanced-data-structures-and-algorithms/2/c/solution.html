For any feasible circulation with vertex demands, sum its defining flow balance over all vertices:
∑v∈V​dv​=∑v∈V​∑u:uv∈E​fuv​−∑v∈V​∑w:vw∈E​fvw​.
(19)
Every directed edge ab∈E contributes +fab​ exactly once to the first sum, at its head b, and −fab​ exactly once to the second, at its tail a. The two sums therefore contain exactly the same terms. They cancel, giving
v∈V∑​dv​=0.​
(20)
This is global flow conservation: internal transport cannot create or destroy the total amount. Lower bounds on network flow and capacity constraints restrict which flows are feasible but do not affect the cancellation. The condition is necessary, not sufficient: insufficient flow network edge capacities can still prevent satisfying balanced demands.
