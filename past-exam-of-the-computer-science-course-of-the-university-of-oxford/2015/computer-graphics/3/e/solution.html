A blocked light loses both its diffuse and specular contributions. The simple ambient approximation remains. Opaque point lights produce hard shadow boundaries. Use a consistent sidedness policy for occluders: an opaque closed solid blocks from either crossing side, so a two-sided blocker test is appropriate even when primary visibility uses back-face culling.
