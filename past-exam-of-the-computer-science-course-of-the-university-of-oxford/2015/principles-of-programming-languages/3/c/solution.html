Add the abstract syntax tree alternative Fix Ident Expr. Its syntactic sugar expansion and the semantic equations from part (a) give
eval(Fix x e)ρ​=eval(Let (Rec x e) (Variable x))ρ=defn(Rec x e)ρ▹(λρ′.result(lookupEnv(ρ′,x)))=result(ρ∗)▹(λρ′.result(lookupEnv(ρ′,x)))=result(v),​
(26)
where ρ∗=extend(ρ,[(x,v)]) and v=close(e,ρ∗). The final step uses left identity from the monad laws and the lookup rule for the newly bound identifier.
Thus a direct evaluator equation is:
eval (Fix x e@(Lambda xs body)) rho = result v
  where
    rho' = extend rho [(x,v)]
    v = close e rho'
Equivalently, spelling out programming closure construction,
eval(Fix x (Lambda xs b))ρ=result(v),v=Function(λargs.eval(b)(extend(ρ∗,zip(xs,args)))).​
(27)
The result is a recursive closure, not the result of calling its body. Its self-binding is visible inside that body but does not extend the caller's surrounding semantic environment.
