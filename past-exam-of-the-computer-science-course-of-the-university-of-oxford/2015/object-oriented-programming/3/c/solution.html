For an integer cell coordinate, occupancy is an existential predicate:
contains(W,x,y)⟺∃i:y=yi​ ∧ xi​≤x<xi​+wi​.​
(16)
The first occupied unit cell of brick i is at xi​, and its last is at xi​+wi​−1. Thus the required support predicate, with the baseplate explicitly included, is
supportive(W)⟺∀i:yi​=0 ∨ (yi​>0 ∧ contains(W,xi​,yi​−1) ∧ contains(W,xi​+wi​−1,yi​−1)).​
(17)
For width one, the two support cells coincide. For a wider brick, its middle may bridge an empty gap; the question only requires support beneath its two ends. Checking xi​+wi​ would incorrectly examine a cell beyond the brick's right end. The potentially infinite baseplate supplies every required support at height zero.[ref]
