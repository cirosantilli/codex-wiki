Choose a circular fillet that removes the sharp convex tip at B. The unit directions from B toward A and C are (−3​/2,−1/2) and (−1,0). Therefore the prescribed tangent points are
M=(2−43​​,43​),N=(23​,1).
(8)
The circle centre K lies on the normal line to BC through N, so K=(3/2,k). Its radius to M must be perpendicular to AB, whose direction vector is (3​,1). Thus
(M−K)⋅(3​,1)=0 ⟹ 3​(21​−43​​)+43​−k=0 ⟹ k=23​​.
(9)
Consequently
K=(23​,23​​),r=1−23​​=22−3​​.​
(10)
Indeed ∣M−K∣=∣N−K∣=r. The interior angle ABC is 30∘, and the same radius follows from r=21​tan15∘. The selected circular arc runs from M to N through angles −60∘≤θ≤90∘ around K:
γ(θ)=K+r(cosθ,sinθ).
(11)
The 150∘ circular arc facing B is the rounding; the complementary arc is not part of the boundary.
Define the disk and a half-plane through the chord MN by
Dr​={fc​≤0},fc​=(x−23​)2+(y−23​​)2−r2,
(12)
Hg​={g≤0},g=x−23​+(2−3​)(y−1).
(13)
Both M,N satisfy g=0, and g(B)=1/2>0. The clipping half-space Hg​ retains the part away from B, while the disk restores the rounded cap. The required local constructive solid geometry tree is
Tblend​=Hg​∪Dr​,Prounded​=P∩Tblend​.​
(14)
Only the portion of P on the B side of MN can be removed. To see the locality, g is negative at every original vertex of a polygon except B, and vanishes on the two adjacent sides exactly at M,N. In the resulting triangle BMN, the disk keeps the curved cap next to MN and discards the tip at B. A global set intersection with Dr​ alone would destroy most of the seat. The outward normal vector on the new boundary is ∇fc​=2((x,y)−K); at M,N it has the same direction as the outward normal vectors of AB,BC.
The tangent constraints alone do not insist on a circle; other conic sections can give different bulges. For example, a positive-weight rational quadratic Bézier curve with handles M,B,N has the required tangent directions:
Rw​(s)=(1−s)2+2ws(1−s)+s2(1−s)2M+2ws(1−s)B+s2N​,0≤s≤1,w>0.
(15)
Varying w changes the bulge while fixing the tangent points; the circular choice has w=cos75∘. Once a circular fillet and these two tangent points are chosen, its centre and radius have no remaining freedom. The half-plane sign and arc selection are also essential: reversing them chooses a different retained region. This construction gives matching tangent directions, or first-order geometric continuity, at M,N; the curvature jumps from zero on each straight side to 1/r on the circle.
