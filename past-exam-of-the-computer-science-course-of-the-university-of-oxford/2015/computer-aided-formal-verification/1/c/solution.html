For a complete binary decision tree with variable ordering of a binary decision diagram z<y<x, first test z, then y, then x on every branch. The four Boolean cofactors after the first two tests are

011

111
The eight terminal nodes, read from left to right in the figure, are 0,0,1,1,0,1,1,1​. The repeated tests are intentional: this is the full binary decision tree before any reduction.
For example, the branch z=1,y=0,x=1 ends at one, whereas z=0,y=0,x=1 ends at zero. This checks the dependence on z that must survive the later reduction.
