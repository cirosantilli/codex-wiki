In the moving stretched coordinates, ∂t​=∂τ​−s′(τ)ε−1∂ξ​ and ∂x​=ε−1∂ξ​. Multiplying the damped Burgers equation by ε gives
ε(uτ​+u)+(u−s′)uξ​=uξξ​.
(29)
Discarding the order-ε terms yields the leading Viscous Burgers travelling shock equation (u−s′)uξ​=uξξ​​. Integrate across a nonconstant transition whose derivative vanishes at either end:
[2u2​−s′u]−+​=0.​
(30)
Multiplication by u and a further integration by parts give
[3u3​−2s′u2​]−+​=−∫−∞∞​uξ2​dξ<0.​
(31)
The first identity is the Rankine-Hugoniot condition, giving s′=(u−​+u+​)/2. Substitution into the second makes its left side (u+​−u−​)3/12, so the viscosity-selected entropy shock has u−​>u+​.
After the first characteristic collapse, the shock is fed by the left constant initial state and the right zero state. Their damped values are u−​=2e−τ and u+​=0. Therefore s′=e−τ. Matching to s(log2)=0 yields
s(τ)=21​−e−τ,τ>log2.​
(32)
These matching states are consistent with the characteristic feet: the left foot is s−2(1−e−τ)=−3/2+e−τ<−1, while the right foot is s>0. The formula describes the leading thin-layer position in the small-viscosity limit.
