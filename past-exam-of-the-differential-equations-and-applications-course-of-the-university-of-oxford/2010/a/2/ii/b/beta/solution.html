OurBigBook.com Words: 62
Every image of L is a linear combination acos2x+bsin2x. The function cosx is not such a combination. Indeed these functions are continuous, so equality almost everywhere would imply equality everywhere; evaluation at the endpoints would require simultaneously a=1 and a=−1. Therefore the forcing is outside the image and there is no solution. Orthogonality to the kernel is the equivalent image test for this self-adjoint finite-rank operator.
