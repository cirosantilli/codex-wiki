Assume first that n≥m. Substituting Rodrigues' formula for Ln​ gives
Inm​=n!1​∫0∞​Lm​(x)dxndn​(xne−x)dx=n!(−1)n​∫0∞​Lm(n)​(x)xne−xdx.
(23)
Here integration by parts was performed n times. Each boundary term vanishes: at infinity a polynomial times e−x tends to zero; at zero every derivative of xne−x of order less than n vanishes. If n>m, the derivative Lm(n)​ is zero, proving orthogonality. The case m>n follows by exchanging the two factors.
For m=n, the leading coefficient found above gives Ln(n)​=(−1)n. Therefore
Inn​=n!1​∫0∞​xne−xdx=n!Γ(n+1)​=1.
(24)
The same conclusion includes n=0 directly. Thus the full Laguerre orthonormality relation is
∫0∞​Ln​(x)Lm​(x)e−xdx=δnm​.​
