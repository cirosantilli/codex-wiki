The coefficient matrix of (uy​,vy​)T is A=(vu−3​uv​). Its distinct eigenvalues are λ±​=v±1/u for every u>0, so the system is strictly hyperbolic. Define D±​=∂x​+(v±1/u)∂y​. Then

the derivative terms cancel, as do the sources u−u. Hence R=v−1/u is constant on the plus-family characteristics.
The data give R=0, so throughout their domain of dependence v=1/u. The minus-family speed is then v−1/u=0: the other characteristics are the horizontal lines y=a. Substitution into the second PDE, or its minus-characteristic compatibility relation, gives vx​=1/v. Integrate at fixed y using v(0,y)=y and retain the positive branch:
v(x,y)=y2+2x​,u(x,y)=y2+2x​1​.​

For x≥0 the lower edge of the region determined solely by the given data is the plus characteristic from (0,1). Write it y=b(x), where the exact boundary characterization is
b′(x)=2b(x)2+2x​,b(0)=1.​
(31)
The determined region is x≥0, y≥b(x)​, with strict inequality for its interior. Both backward characteristic families there reach the supplied initial half-line. Below this boundary a backward plus characteristic would require initial data below y=1, which were not supplied; extending the displayed formulas there gives a possible continuation but is not forced by the data. An elementary expression for b is unnecessary: its initial-value ODE defines it uniquely and the requested other family already has the explicit form y=a. The sketch integrates only this boundary for illustration.
