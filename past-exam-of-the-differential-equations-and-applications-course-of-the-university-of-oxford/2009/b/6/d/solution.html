The forcing contains x/y, so matching it with a single similarity variable requires β=1. For u=yαf(η), η=x/y, substitution gives
xuxx​−uy​=yα−1[ηf′′+ηf′−αf].
(36)
Matching the source y−1ηe−η selects α=0, β=1​ and gives f′′+f′=e−η. Multiplying the first-order equation for f′ by eη yields f′=(η+C)e−η, so f=−(η+1+C)e−η+D. The limits at zero and infinity give D=0, C=−1. Hence
u0​(x,y)=−yx​e−x/y.​
(37)
For fixed positive x, the initial limit is also zero because the exponential dominates x/y. The function −ηe−η decreases to −1/e at η=1 and increases thereafter. Therefore
minu0​=−1/e,attained at every x=y>0.​
(38)
The axis limits are zero, but there is no joint limit at (0,0): approaching along x=y gives −1/e. This does not violate any of the printed fixed-variable limits. It is this bounded similarity solution whose minimum was calculated; the unbounded corner-source additions in (b) need not have the same minimum.
