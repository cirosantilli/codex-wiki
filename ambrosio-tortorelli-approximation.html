Ambrosio–Tortorelli approximation (Eε​(u,v))

 Home Computer science Image processing Variational image processing Mumford–Shah functional
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
The Mumford–Shah functional can be approximated by an auxiliary edge field 0≤v≤1 and energy
Eε​=∫(u−g)2+α(v2+ηε​)∣∇u∣2+β[ε∣∇v∣2+4ε(1−v)2​],0<ηε​=o(ε).
(1)
The field is near one inside regions and near zero across an image edge, weakening smoothing there. The profile v(s)=1−e−∣s∣/(2ε) has unit edge energy per unit length in this normalization. The 1990 construction is a Gamma-convergence approximation; its global-minimizer limit does not guarantee global optimality of an alternating numerical solve. Each fixed-field subproblem is quadratic, but the joint energy is nonconvex.
