Complex spectral Fourier transform (Q(k)=∫C​ekˉz−kzˉq(z)dA(z))

A complex-coordinate parametrization of the two-dimensional Fourier transform. With z=x+iy, k=k1​+ik2​, and (ξ1​,ξ2​)=(2k2​,−2k1​), its exponent is −iξ⋅(x,y). Since dξ=4dA(k), its inverse is q(z)=π−2∫ekzˉ−kˉzQ(k)dA(k). The spectral equation (∂zˉ​−k)F=q gives ∂kˉ​F=−ekzˉ−kˉzQ(k)/π, linking Fourier inversion to a spectral dbar equation.
