Artiodactyla

 ... Mammaliaform Mammal Theria Eutheria Placental mammal Laurasiatheria
OurBigBook.com Words: 459 Articles: 28
In the broad phylogenetic usage, a clade including Cetacea together with the traditional even-toed ungulates. Whale ancestry is therefore artiodactyl ancestry, despite the loss of the adult terrestrial hoofed body plan.
