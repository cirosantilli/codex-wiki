Recursive call

 Home Computer science Algorithm Recursion (computer science)
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
An invocation made by a recursive procedure of itself, directly or through another procedure. Each invocation has its own arguments and local variables; a returning invocation supplies an output to its caller.
