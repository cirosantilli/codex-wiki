Axiom of countable choice (An​=∅ ∀n⇒∏n​An​=∅)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Axiom of choice
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 1
The axiom of countable choice allows one to choose an element from each member of a countable family of nonempty sets. It is a restricted form of the axiom of choice. In the usual proof that a countable union of countable sets is countable, it suffices to choose one enumerating injection for each set; if those enumerations are already supplied, that proof requires no further choice.
