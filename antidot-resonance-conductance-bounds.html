Antidot resonance conductance bounds (G=(e2/h)(Ndir​+T))

 ... Hall effect Quantum Hall effect Integer quantum Hall effect Quantum Hall edge state Quantum Hall antidot Resonant antidot backscattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
A spin-resolved resonant path adds at most e2/h to the electrical conductance of directly transmitted spectator channels. Conversely, resonant transfer into a returning edge removes at most e2/h from an otherwise open channel. For an isolated level, T(E)=ΓL​ΓR​/[(E−ϵd​)2+(ΓL​+ΓR​)2/4] reaches one only for equal couplings. Integer offsets follow the channel connectivity and do not follow from a local filling factor alone. temperature, asymmetry, coherent background paths and inelastic processes can change the apparent resonance height and shape.
