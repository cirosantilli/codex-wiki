Fixed-pressure planar Darcy displacement (μh​LX+21​(μ−μh​)X2=kΔPt/ϕ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Darcy law
OurBigBook.com Words: 158 Articles: 1
For two incompressible fluids separated by a planar material front X(t), the Darcy flux is uniform and the pressure drop sums the resistances of the two layers: U=kΔP/[μX+μh​(L−X)]. The front moves at the pore velocity X′=U/ϕ. Integrating from X(0)=0 yields the displayed relation. The formula holds up to breakthrough X=L, with either sign of the viscosity difference; equal viscosities give constant speed.
