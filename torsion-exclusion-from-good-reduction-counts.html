For an elliptic curve over Q, reduction at a good prime p is injective on the ℓ-primary torsion subgroup when ℓ=p: its kernel is a formal group, where multiplication by ℓ is bijective. Hence a rational point of order ℓ forces ℓ∣#E(Fp​). If for every possible prime ℓ some good p=ℓ has a point count not divisible by ℓ, rational torsion is trivial. In particular two distinct good primes p,r with coprime counts, p∤#E(Fr​) and r∤#E(Fp​) suffice: the coprime counts exclude other primes, and the two cross conditions exclude p,r. This argument does not incorrectly apply prime-to-p injectivity to p-power torsion.
