Product rule

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Calculus Derivative
OurBigBook.com Words: 80 Articles: 3
If f and g are differentiable at a, then
(fg)′(a)=f′(a)g(a)+f(a)g′(a).
