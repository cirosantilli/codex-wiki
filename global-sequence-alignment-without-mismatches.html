Global sequence alignment without mismatches (Di,j​=max(Di−1,j​−g,Di,j−1​−g,Di−1,j−1​+1 if xi​=yj​))

 Home Biology Genetics Molecular genetics Sequence alignment
OurBigBook.com Words: 96
Align two complete strings using matching letters or a letter opposite a gap, with no unequal-letter columns and no double-gap columns. Give each match score one and each gap penalty g≥0. The dynamic programming table for prefixes starts with Di,0​=−gi and D0,j​=−gj. Its last-column cases give the displayed recurrence, where the diagonal candidate is omitted on a mismatch. Construction takes O(mn) time for positive lengths and O(mn) space with full alignment traceback.
An alignment with ℓ matches has m+n−2ℓ gaps and score (1+2g)ℓ−g(m+n). Therefore a longest common subsequence determines its optimum. A finite mismatch penalty would solve a different problem.
