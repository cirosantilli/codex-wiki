The Green-Lagrange strain tensor has eigenvalues (λj2​−1)/2. Its Frobenius norm and trace are invariant under the orthogonal principal-axis change, so
W(F)=4μ​j=1∑3​(λj2​−1)2+8λ​(j=1∑3​λj2​−3)2.​

For the homogeneous dilation F=ρI, put K=μ+3λ/2. Then E=(ρ2−1)I/2. Differentiating the energy through E gives the second Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor 2μE+λtr(E)I=K(ρ2−1)I, and multiplication by F gives the first Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor. Thus
T=ρK(ρ2−1)I,S=J−1TFT=ρK(ρ2−1)​I.​
(24)
The normals of the reference and deformed spheres point in the same radial direction. As ρ↓0, the reference traction magnitude ∣TN∣ tends to zero, while the current traction magnitude ∣Sn∣ tends to infinity, provided K>0, as for physically stable Lamé parameters. The negative signs of the stress coefficients for 0<ρ<1 indicate compression.
There is no contradiction: the current area is ρ2 times the reference area, so the same force divided by the vanishing current area has the larger stress. Indeed TN=ρ2Sn. The limiting map at ρ=0 is not itself an admissible deformation in continuum mechanics; the statement concerns the approach through positive ρ. A degenerate choice K=0 would not have the asserted growth without bound.
