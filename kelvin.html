Kelvin (K)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Thermodynamics Temperature
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
The kelvin is the SI unit of thermodynamic temperature, with symbol K. Its definition fixes the numerical value of the Boltzmann constant; it is written without a degree sign.[ref]
