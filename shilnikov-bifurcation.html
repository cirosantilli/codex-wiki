Shilnikov bifurcation

 Home Physics Branches of physics Dynamical systems Dynamical system Homoclinic orbit
OurBigBook.com Words: 260 Articles: 2
A Shilnikov bifurcation is a homoclinic connection to a saddle-focus in three dimensions, with one real unstable eigenvalue λ+​>0 and a stable complex pair λ−​±iω. The eigenvalue ratio δ=−λ−​/λ+​ distinguishes a contracting return for δ>1 from the oscillatory expanding return associated with positive saddle value when δ<1. Generic local and global maps yield infinitely many saddle periodic orbits at a connection in the latter case.
