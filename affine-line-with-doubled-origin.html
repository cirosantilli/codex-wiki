Affine line with doubled origin (A1⨿D(t)​A1)

 ... Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme Spectrum of a commutative ring Affine scheme Affine line
OurBigBook.com Words: 104
Glue two copies of the affine line over a base along the principal open subset D(t) by the identity. This scheme has two copies of the zero section and a canonical morphism of schemes to the original affine line. Over Z, its functor of points assigns to a commutative ring R the pairs (r,e) with e an idempotent in R/(r). Indeed, r gives the map to the affine line, while e partitions V(r) into the two clopen sets assigned to the zero sections. Complementing the opposite part gives two open subsets covering SpecR, whose intersection is D(r); the coordinate r defines maps on them which glue. This also proves that no lifting of e to an idempotent of R is necessary.
