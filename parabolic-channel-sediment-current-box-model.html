Parabolic-channel sediment-current box model (h3/2L=constant,ϕ˙​=−3Ws​ϕ/(2h))

A well-mixed particle-laden gravity current in a prismatic channel with b(z)=Cz​ has volume V=2Ch3/2L/3. Negligible entrainment gives fixed V. Vertical particle deposition flux is Ws​ϕb(h)L, so ϕ˙​=−3Ws​ϕ/(2h). Close the front by L˙=FGϕh​, where G=g(ρp​−ρ0​)/ρ0​. Eliminating time gives
ϕ​+8FG​h03/2​L0​3Ws​L2​=ϕ0​​+8FG​h03/2​L0​3Ws​L02​​.
(5)
The concentration tends to zero at a finite limiting runout length of a gravity current, although reaching that limit takes infinite time in this model.
