Brownian bridge

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Brownian motion
OurBigBook.com Words: 250 Articles: 5
A standard Brownian bridge is the centered Gaussian process B(t)=W(t)−tW(1) on [0,1]. Its covariance is min(s,t)−st and it satisfies B(0)=B(1)=0.
