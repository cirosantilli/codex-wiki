Nonlinear Abelian gauge fixing

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Gauge field Gauge fixing
OurBigBook.com Words: 296 Articles: 4
For an Abelian gauge theory with Aμ​↦Aμ​+∂μ​α, a nonlinear gauge functional F[A] has Faddeev-Popov determinant det(δF[A+∂α]/δα). This operator can depend on A even though the Adjoint representation of U(1) is trivial. Consequently Faddeev-Popov ghost fields need not decouple in a nonlinear Abelian gauge, unlike a field-independent linear gauge condition.
