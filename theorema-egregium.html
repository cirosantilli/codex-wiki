Gaussian curvature depends only on the first fundamental form and is therefore preserved by local isometries. For a parametrized surface with metric coefficients gij​, decompose its second derivatives by the Gauss formula
Xij​=Γijk​Xk​+hij​N.
(434)
Taking tangent inner products determines the lowered Christoffel symbols solely from the metric:
Γijk​=21​(∂i​gjk​+∂j​gik​−∂k​gij​).
(435)
Comparing Xijk​ and Xikj​ gives the Gauss equation
R1212​=h11​h22​−h122​.
(436)
Consequently
K=det(gij​)h11​h22​−h122​​=det(gij​)R1212​​,
(437)
and the final expression uses only gij​ and its first two derivatives.
