Two-outcome LOCC dilution of a Bell pair (M0​=diag(c,s),M1​=diag(s,c))

Let c,s≥0 and c2+s2=1. Starting from the Bell state (∣00⟩+∣11⟩)/2​, Alice applies the measurement in quantum mechanics with Kraus operators M0​,M1​ displayed above. Completeness follows from M0†​M0​+M1†​M1​=I. Each outcome has probability 1/2. The normalized outputs are c∣00⟩+s∣11⟩ and s∣00⟩+c∣11⟩. Communicating the outcome allows both parties to apply a Pauli X gate in the second branch, making both outputs equal to the first. Thus this LOCC protocol deterministically converts one Bell pair into any two-qubit pure state with these Schmidt coefficients, after appropriate local unitary operations. It includes the product state endpoint without postselection.
