Phi cubed theory (ϕ3 theory)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Scalar field theory Real scalar field
OurBigBook.com Words: 540 Articles: 9
Phi cubed theory has interaction −gϕ3/3!, giving a cubic vertex −ig. In four spacetime dimensions the scalar field has mass dimension one and g has mass dimension one.
