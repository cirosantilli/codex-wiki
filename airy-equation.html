Airy equation (qt​+qxxx​=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Integrable systems Lax pair
OurBigBook.com Words: 733 Articles: 11
The Airy equation is the linear dispersive partial differential equation qt​+qxxx​=0. Under the spatial Fourier transform, each mode evolves by multiplication by eik3t.
For positive time its fundamental solution is (3t)−1/3Ai(x/(3t)1/3), connecting this evolution equation to the Airy function without identifying the two concepts.
