Contracted nonlinear susceptibility notation (jk↦J∈{xx,yy,zz,yz,zx,xy})

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Nonlinear optics Nonlinear optical susceptibility Nonlinear-susceptibility tensor symmetry
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
For identical input frequencies, intrinsic permutation symmetry of nonlinear susceptibility makes the last two Cartesian indices symmetric. Store their six independent pairs as one index, so the quadratic response uses a three-by-six array diJ​. In the convention dijk​=χijk(2)​/2, the contracted input vector is (Ax2​,Ay2​,Az2​,2Ay​Az​,2Az​Ax​,2Ax​Ay​)T. The factors of two preserve the sum over both off-diagonal orderings. This bookkeeping does not assert the stronger Kleinman symmetry and does not change the tensor's physical order.
