Fermi level (μ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 30
The Fermi level is the chemical potential controlling electron occupations. For an ideal Fermi gas at zero temperature it equals the Fermi energy; at nonzero temperature the chemical potential need not equal that zero-temperature energy.
