Thermally damped quasi-geostrophic mountain wave

 ... Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Quasi-geostrophic approximation Three-dimensional quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity Quasi-geostrophic mountain wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 35
Thermal damping makes the vertical wavenumber of a stationary topographic Rossby wave complex. A vertically propagating wave then decays while oscillating, whereas an inviscidly evanescent disturbance acquires both decay and phase tilt; both can exert wave drag.
