Schneider-Lang theorem (∣S∣≤[K:Q](ρ1​+ρ2​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Diophantine approximation Transcendence theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
Let f1​,…,fs​ be meromorphic functions with fj′​∈K[f1​,…,fs​] for a fixed number field K. Suppose f1​,f2​ are algebraically independent over C and have finite orders ρ1​,ρ2​. The set S of points where every fj​ is finite and belongs to K satisfies the displayed bound. The orders are growth orders for meromorphic functions. For f1​=z, f2​=ez, the orders are zero and one and the functions are algebraically independent; an algebraic value of eα at nonzero algebraic α would produce infinitely many such points nα.
