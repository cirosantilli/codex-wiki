Magnetic field between circular capacitor plates (Bϕ​(s)=−iωμ0​ϵ0​V0​s/(2d))

OurBigBook.com Words: 61
For a circular air-filled parallel-plate capacitor with voltage phasor V0​e−iωt, uniform gap d and radius a, the Ampère-Maxwell equation on a circular loop of radius s<a gives the displayed magnetic field. The enclosed displacement current is proportional to s2, whereas the circulation is 2πsBϕ​. Consequently its amplitude grows linearly from zero on the axis. This assumes negligible fringing fields and a quasistatic approximation ωa/c≪1.
