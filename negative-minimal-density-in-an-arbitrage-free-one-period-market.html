Negative minimal density in an arbitrage-free one-period market

OurBigBook.com Words: 61
Let a discounted gain Y take values −1,1,10 with physical probabilities 1/10,4/5,1/10. Then m=17/10 and C=801/100, so the minimal martingale measure in a one-period market has signed density Z∗​(10)=−610/801. Nevertheless (109/200,89/200,1/100) is a strictly positive pricing probability with zero mean gain. Thus absence of arbitrage does not ensure positivity of the minimal density. The minimum-norm identity holds for signed pricing densities; interpretation as a dominated martingale measure requires nonnegativity.
