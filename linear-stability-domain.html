Linear stability domain

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 5
For a one-step method applied to y′=λy, write yn+1​=R(hλ)yn​. Its linear stability domain is
S={z∈C:∣R(z)∣≤1}.
(215)
Forward Euler has R(z)=1+z, while backward Euler has R(z)=(1−z)−1 and is A-stable.
