ADHM factorization identity (Dz​Dz†​=12​⊗f−1)

OurBigBook.com Words: 104
With z1​=x1+ix2, z2​=x3+ix4, form
Dz​=(B2​−z2​−B1†​+zˉ1​​B1​−z1​B2†​−zˉ2​​IJ†​).
(18)
The two ADHM construction constraints say that Dz​Dz†​ has zero off-diagonal blocks and equal diagonal blocks, so it equals 12​⊗f−1. Assume it is invertible everywhere. Choose an orthonormal kernel bundle frame Ψ, so Dz​Ψ=0 and Ψ†Ψ=1N​, and set A=Ψ†dΨ. The orthogonal complement projector is 1−ΨΨ†=Dz†​(12​⊗f)Dz​. Differentiating the kernel equation gives
F=Ψ†dDz†​(12​⊗f)∧dDz​Ψ.
(19)
Its space-time differential two-form entries are combinations of dz1​∧dzˉ1​−dz2​∧dzˉ2​, dz1​∧dzˉ2​, and dzˉ1​∧dz2​, all anti-self-dual. Thus the constructed gauge curvature satisfies the Anti-self-dual Yang-Mills equations.
