Optical spectrum

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 24
An optical spectrum describes the distribution of optical radiation over wavelength or frequency. A spectrograph records it by separating wavelengths, while a photometric passband integrates a chosen part.
