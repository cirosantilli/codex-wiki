Weak-star topology

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Weak topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 606 Articles: 11
The weak-star topology σ(X∗,X) is pointwise convergence of functionals on X. If X is nonreflexive, it is strictly weaker than the weak topology σ(X∗,X∗∗) on X∗.
