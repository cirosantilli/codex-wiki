Likelihood reconstruction from posterior simulation

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Bayesian statistics
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For data x, a positive prior distribution density π and likelihood function ℓ, Bayes' theorem gives posterior density h(θ∣x)=π(θ)ℓ(θ)/m(x). Thus h/π reconstructs the likelihood up to a constant. Estimate the posterior density from simulation, divide by the known prior density and maximize over the search region. A posterior mode alone instead estimates a maximum a posteriori estimate; it estimates a likelihood maximizer only when the prior is constant there. Prior support must include the likelihood maximizer of interest.
