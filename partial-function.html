Partial function (f:X⇀Y)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Function
OurBigBook.com Words: 127 Articles: 2
A partial function from X to Y assigns at most one value in Y to each element of X; it may be undefined outside a subset of X.
