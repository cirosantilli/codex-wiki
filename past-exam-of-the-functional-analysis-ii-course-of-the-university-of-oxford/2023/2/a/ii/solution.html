Composition with t↦t1+αn​ preserves continuity, so An​ maps the space of continuous functions on a compact space C([0,1]) into itself and is linear. Since t↦t1+αn​ maps [0,1] onto itself,
∥An​x∥∞​=∥x∥∞​,∥An​∥=1.
(12)
For 0<t≤1, the mean value theorem applied to r↦t1+r gives
0≤t−t1+αn​≤αn​t∣logt∣≤eαn​​.
(13)
The same bound holds at t=0. Each x∈C([0,1]) has uniform continuity by the Heine-Cantor theorem. With its modulus of continuity ωx​(δ)=sup∣s−t∣≤δ​∣x(s)−x(t)∣, we obtain
∥An​x−x∥∞​≤ωx​(αn​/e)⟶0.​
(14)
Alternatively, the bound proves convergence on every polynomial; the Weierstrass approximation theorem makes these a dense subset of C([0,1]), and the Banach-Steinhaus convergence theorem with ∥An​∥=1 gives the same conclusion.
