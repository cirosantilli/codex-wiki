Stresslet reflection between two force-free and forced spheres

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Stokes flow Hydrodynamic interaction
OurBigBook.com Words: 149 Articles: 1
Consider two widely separated rigid spheres of equal radius a, with separation Rn, in Stokes flow. Apply a force 6πμaU0​ to the first sphere and let the second be force-free and torque-free. The second sphere samples the rate-of-strain tensor of the first sphere's Stokeslet, E=3a(U0​⋅n)(I−3nn)/(4R2). Its leading disturbance is the stresslet of a sphere in a uniform straining Stokes flow. Evaluating that disturbance back at the first sphere gives
ΔU=−415​(Ra​)4(U0​⋅n)n.
(4)
The hydrodynamic mobility matrix decreases at fixed force, consistently with the increase of resistance caused by an additional rigid inclusion.
