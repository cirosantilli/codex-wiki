Brauer character

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Modular representation theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 186 Articles: 5
Let M be a representation over a splitting field k of characteristic p. For a p-regular element g, the eigenvalues of g on M have order prime to p. Lift them to characteristic-zero roots of unity by the Teichmuller lift; their sum is the Brauer character value χM​(g).
