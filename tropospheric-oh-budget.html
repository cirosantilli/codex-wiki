Tropospheric OH budget ([OH]=P/K)

 Home Chemistry Atmospheric chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 87 Articles: 1
A local or box-model balance between hydroxyl-radical production and effective losses. For fixed production P and pseudo-first-order total loss frequency K, [OH]=P/K. Additional hydrogen, carbon monoxide or methane consumption can lower hydroxyl radical if production and other terms stay fixed; radical recycling and spatial transport can alter that simple result.
