Geometric realization of a simplicial complex (∣K∣)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Simplicial complex
OurBigBook.com Words: 670 Articles: 6
Take one copy of the standard geometric simplex Δσ for every face σ of an abstract simplicial complex K, label its vertices by σ, and identify equally labelled common faces. Give the result the quotient topology from the disjoint union of all these simplexes. Equivalently, a point is a finitely supported family of barycentric coordinates λv​≥0 with ∑v​λv​=1 and {v:λv​>0}∈Σ.
The weak topology of a simplicial realization makes U open precisely when U∩∣σ∣ is open in every closed simplex. This definition determines the topology even for infinite complexes, when a metric realization of a simplicial complex may give a different topology.[ref]
