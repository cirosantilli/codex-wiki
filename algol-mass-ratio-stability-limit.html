Algol mass-ratio stability limit (qcrit​≃2/3)

 ... Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Binary star Roche lobe Roche-lobe overflow Algol binary
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
A simplified conservative model with a fully convective donor gives long-lived stable Roche-lobe overflow only for donor-to-accretor mass ratio q<2/3. This follows from the Roche-lobe radius response exponent and the donor's adiabatic stellar radius response exponent −1/3. The boundary is model-dependent; radiative donors, evolved core structure and mass or angular-momentum loss change it. Van Rensbergen and collaborators' Algol population study includes reported Algol mass ratios above 0.7 and discusses observational uncertainties.
