Gravitational neutron bound-state transmission

 ... Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Quantum system Quantum state Bound state Quantum bouncer
OurBigBook.com Words: 105
A horizontal neutron-reflecting mirror supplies the lower boundary of a quantum bouncer, with the Earth's approximately linear gravitational potential above it. An absorber above the mirror removes neutrons with sufficient vertical extent. Scanning the mirror-absorber gap and counting transmitted neutrons probes the lowest allowed gravitational states. The 2002 work led by Valery V. Nesvizhevsky reported this confinement; a subsequent detailed report describes suppression below the lowest-state height.[ref][ref]
This is a count-versus-gap experiment, not a direct image of a wavefunction. Surface scattering, absorption and the incident vertical-motion distribution enter a quantitative transmission model.
