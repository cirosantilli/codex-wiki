For an affordable nonnegative terminal claim X, represent the conditional-expectation martingale Nt​=E[ζT​X∣Ft​] as dNt​=ht​dWt​. Then wt​=Nt​/ζt​ and the displayed dollar amount in the stock replicate X. The Itô product rule gives d(ζw)=ζ(σθ−κw)dW, proving the formula; share holdings are θt​/St​.
