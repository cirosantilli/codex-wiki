Cyclic coordinate

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Lagrangian mechanics
OurBigBook.com Words: 15
A generalized coordinate absent from the Lagrangian is cyclic. Its conjugate generalized momentum is conserved by the Euler-Lagrange equations.
