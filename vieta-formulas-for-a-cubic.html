Vieta formulas for a cubic

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial Vieta's formulas
OurBigBook.com Words: 134
Take the stipulated polynomial roots −2,1,5. Their first three elementary symmetric polynomials are
e1​=−2+1+5=4,e2​=(−2)(1)+(−2)(5)+(1)(5)=−7,e3​=(−2)(1)(5)=−10.
(16)
Their monic polynomial is therefore
(x+2)(x−1)(x−5)=x3−4x2−7x+10.​
(17)
The constant coefficient is 10, while the product of the three roots of a polynomial is −10: for a cubic polynomial, these have opposite signs. Expanding the factors directly verifies the same polynomial identity.
For the nonmonic polynomial −3x3+12x2+21x−30, the same ratios give sum −12/(−3)=4, pairwise-product sum 21/(−3)=−7, and product −(−30)/(−3)=−10. It is −3 times the monic polynomial above, so its roots of a polynomial are the same. Checking only the sum and product would leave the middle elementary symmetric polynomial unchecked; all three are needed to recover a monic polynomial of polynomial degree three.
