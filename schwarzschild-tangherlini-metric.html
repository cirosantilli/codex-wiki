Schwarzschild-Tangherlini metric (f(r)=1−μ/rD−3)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 1
For D≥4, the static spherical vacuum metric ds2=−fdt2+f−1dr2+r2dΩD−22​ has an event horizon at rh​=μ1/(D−3). With time normalized at infinity its ADM mass is M=(D−2)ΩD−2​μ/(16πGD​), where ΩD−2​ is the unit-sphere area. For D=5 and μ=2m, rh​=2m​ and M=3πm/(4G5​). The spherical orbits are three-spheres, even though the radial-time Penrose diagram has the same causal pattern as its four-dimensional counterpart.
