Bias spectroscopy of one-dimensional subbands (En+1​−En​=e∣V∣)

OurBigBook.com Words: 72
In a rigid spectrum, source and drain threshold lines in a bias-versus-gate map satisfy En​(Vg​)=μS​ or μD​. Where adjacent thresholds align with opposite edges of the bias window, their spacing equals the window width e∣V∣. For symmetric bias and spin degeneracy, neighboring channel counts at those edges give a differential electrical conductance halfway between integer 2e2/h plateaus. The dc ratio I/V is a different observable; barrier motion and heating can modify the interpretation.
