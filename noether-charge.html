Noether charge (Q)

 Home Physics Branch of physics Quantum field theory Classical field theory Noether current
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
A Noether charge is the spatial integral Q=∫j0d3x of a Noether current. It is time-independent when the current has vanishing flux through the spatial boundary, and in a Hamiltonian formulation it generates the associated continuous symmetry.
