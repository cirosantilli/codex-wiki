Discrete-time Markov chain martingale characterization (Mnf​=f(Xn​)−f(X0​)−∑j<n​(Pf−f)(Xj​))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 72
An adapted process on a finite or countable state space has the Markov chain transition matrix P exactly when the displayed process is a martingale for every bounded test function f. The forward implication follows by taking the conditional expectation of its increment. Conversely, the martingale property gives E[f(Xn+1​)∣Fn​]=(Pf)(Xn​); testing individual-state indicators recovers the entire conditional transition law. Stopping these martingales turns equations (P−I)h=0 and (P−I)v=−1 into hitting probabilities and expected hitting times.
