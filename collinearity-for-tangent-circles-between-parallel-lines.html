Collinearity for tangent circles between parallel lines

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Topological space Circle Tangent circles
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
Let two positive-radius circles have external tangency at Q and lie in the closed strip between two parallel lines, touching opposite boundaries at P,R. Then P,Q,R are collinear. To prove this, choose coordinates with the boundaries x=0,d, put P=(0,0) and R=(d,h), and write the centres as O1​=(r1​,0), O2​=(d−r2​,h). The contact point is
Q=r1​+r2​r2​O1​+r1​O2​​=r1​+r2​r1​​R.
(11)
The closed line segment PR is contained in the union of the two closed disks: its portions from P to Q and from Q to R are chords. The strip and external tangency hypotheses are essential to this particular formulation.
