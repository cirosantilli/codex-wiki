Continuous functional calculus (f↦f(a))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Banach algebra C-star algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 332 Articles: 5
For a Normal element of a C-star algebra a in a unital C-star algebra, the Commutative Gelfand--Naimark theorem identifies C(σ(a)) isometrically with the closed unital algebra generated by a and a∗. The coordinate function maps to a, defining f(a) for every continuous f on the spectrum of an element.
