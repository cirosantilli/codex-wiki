Affine lift of a holomorphic map between one-dimensional complex tori

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Complex torus
OurBigBook.com Words: 98
Let πΛ​:C→C/Λ and πΛ′​:C→C/Λ′ be the universal covering maps of two one-dimensional complex tori. Every holomorphic map f:C/Λ→C/Λ′ has a holomorphic lift between one-dimensional complex tori F:C→C satisfying πΛ′​∘F=f∘πΛ​, and every such lift is an affine function
F(z)=az+b
(23)
with aΛ⊆Λ′. Indeed, for λ∈Λ, the continuous function F(z+λ)−F(z) takes values in the discrete set Λ′ and is therefore constant. Hence F′ is Λ-periodic. It is bounded on a compact fundamental parallelogram and therefore on the complex plane, so the Liouville theorem makes F′ constant. If f preserves the identity and the lift is chosen with F(0)=0, then b=0 and the lift is a linear map.
