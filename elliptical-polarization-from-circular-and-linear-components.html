Elliptical polarization from circular and linear components

 Home Physics Wave Polarization (waves) Elliptical polarization
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
Two coherent waves with common propagation direction and frequency can combine into a polarization ellipse. Take circular components Ex​=Acosψ, Ey​=Asinψ and add Bcosψ along x. The resulting semiaxes are ∣A+B∣ and ∣A∣. Choosing positive A,B gives a nondegenerate ellipse with unequal axes. Uncorrelated relative phases do not describe the same fixed pure polarization.
