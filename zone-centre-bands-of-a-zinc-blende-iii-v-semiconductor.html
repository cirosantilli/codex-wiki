Zone-centre bands of a zinc-blende III-V semiconductor

 Home Physics Semiconductor III-V semiconductor
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
A common unstrained cubic III-V band ordering consists of an s-like Γ6c​ conduction doublet, a p-like Γ8v​ quartet and a lower spin-orbit split-off Γ7v​ doublet. In the cubic limit the quartet gives heavy-hole and light-hole bands that meet at the Gamma point; different curvatures and direction-dependent mixing distinguish them away from it. The split-off valence band is separated by Δso​. This common ordering is a model to check against the actual material, not a consequence of chemical stoichiometry alone.
