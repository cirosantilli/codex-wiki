Internal variable (ξr​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Continuum thermodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 132 Articles: 3
An internal variable records a material state beyond its instantaneous deformation gradient and temperature, such as a relaxing microstructural state. A constitutive equation specifies its evolution and its contribution to the free energy.
