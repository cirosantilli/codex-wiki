Electron fugacity in the Saha equation (ze​=eμe​/kT)

OurBigBook.com Words: 55
For classical nondegenerate Electrons, the free-particle Boltzmann occupancy has the form ze​exp[−p2/(2mkT)], with chemical potential measured relative to its rest energy. Counting the two spin states in phase space gives ne​=2ze​(2πmkT/h2)3/2. A bound level of energy −In​ has relative occupancy (gn​/g+​)ze​eIn​/kT against its parent ion. Eliminating ze​ gives the level-specific Saha ionization equation.
