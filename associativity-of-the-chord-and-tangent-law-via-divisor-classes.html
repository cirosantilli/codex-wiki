Associativity of the chord-and-tangent law via divisor classes

 ... Algebraic geometry Normalization of an algebraic curve Geometric genus Genus one curve Elliptic curve Chord-and-tangent group law
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
For a smooth genus one curve with origin O, the map P↦[(P)−(O)] identifies geometric points with the degree-zero Picard group of a curve. Riemann-Roch theorem gives a unique effective representative for each divisor class of degree one: its Riemann-Roch space has dimension one. A line cuts a plane cubic in a divisor linearly equivalent to 3(O) when O is the Weierstrass flex. The two successive line intersections in the chord-and-tangent group law therefore give addition of the corresponding divisor classes. Associativity follows by injectivity of the identification and associativity of divisor-class addition, including tangent intersection multiplicities.
