Identity component (G∘)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Connected space Connected component
OurBigBook.com Words: 52
The identity component is the connected component containing the identity element. It is a closed normal subgroup: products and inverses of connected sets through the identity remain in that component, and conjugation preserves it. It need not be open; in the additive group Q with its usual topology it is the nonopen singleton {0}.
