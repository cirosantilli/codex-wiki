Fubini-Study metric

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Kähler manifold
OurBigBook.com Words: 266 Articles: 5
The Fubini-Study metric is the standard unitary-invariant Kähler metric on Complex projective space, induced from the unit sphere by the Hopf fibration. Its associated Kähler form is the Fubini-Study form; scaling conventions determine the area of a projective line.
