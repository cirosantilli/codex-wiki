Compact Hausdorff space

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Compact space
OurBigBook.com Words: 259 Articles: 3
A compact Hausdorff space is both compact and Hausdorff. Such a space is normal, and every real-valued continuous function on it is bounded.
