physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/Handbook/Tables/strontiumtable5.htm
Tabulated strontium electron configurations, atomic terms, total angular momentum and energy levels in cm−1. Multiplication by 100 converts to m−1. The entries for 5s5p and 5s6s independently test assignments inferred from rounded optical wavelengths.
