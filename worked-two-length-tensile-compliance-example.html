Check the reconstruction at 400 N. The machine contribution is Cm​F=0.040 mm, and the two specimen contributions are 0.100 mm and 0.200 mm. Adding the respective offsets gives the stipulated 0.145 mm and 0.248 mm. An extensometer spanning Lg​=25.0 mm on either ideal uniform specimen would instead read 0.050 mm extension: engineering strain 0.00200, at engineering stress 200 N/mm2=200 MPa. Their ratio is again 100 GPa. Ignoring machine compliance gives apparent moduli 71.43 GPa and 83.33 GPa even after removing the offsets. No yield point or real material identity is implied by these stipulated linear inputs.
