Zero-rate distortion threshold (Dzero​=minx^​E[d(X,x^)])

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Rate-distortion theory Rate-distortion function
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
For the finite alphabets considered here, the zero-rate distortion threshold is the smallest expected distortion obtainable by always outputting one fixed reconstruction. Such a reconstruction is independent of the source, so its mutual information is zero. A randomized output that is independent of the source averages the costs of fixed reconstructions and cannot improve their minimum. Thus R(D)=0 exactly when D≥Dzero​. For a fair bit under Hamming distortion, Dzero​=1/2; for a Bernoulli random variable of parameter p, it is min{p,1−p}. The threshold depends on the source probability distribution, rather than only the alphabet.
