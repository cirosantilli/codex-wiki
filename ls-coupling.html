LS coupling (J=L+S)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Addition of angular momentum
OurBigBook.com Words: 167 Articles: 2
An atomic addition of angular momentum scheme in which the individual orbital angular momentum vectors first combine into L and the individual spins into S, then these combine into total angular momentum J. When spin-orbit coupling is weak compared with the interactions between electrons described by electrostatics, L and S remain useful approximate quantum numbers. The Landé g-factor describes the resulting weak-field magnetic splitting.
