Kummer irreducibility criterion

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 29
Over a field containing the relevant roots of unity, Xn−a is irreducible when the valuation data prevent a from being a proper prime-divisor power, with the standard fourth-power exception.
