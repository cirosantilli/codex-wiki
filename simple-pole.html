Simple pole

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Complex analysis Isolated singularity Classification of isolated singularities Pole
OurBigBook.com Words: 23
A simple pole is a pole of order one. Its residue is the nonzero value of the holomorphic extension of (z−a)f(z) at the pole.
