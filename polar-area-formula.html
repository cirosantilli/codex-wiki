Suppose 0≤r1​(θ)≤r2​(θ) and the region is described without overlap by α≤θ≤β and r1​(θ)≤r≤r2​(θ). Integrating the polar area element gives its area:
A=∫αβ​∫r1​(θ)r2​(θ)​rdrdθ=21​∫αβ​(r2​(θ)2−r1​(θ)2)dθ.
(90)
For a region bounded by a single radial graph and two rays, take r1​=0. If the two bounding graphs cross, split the angular interval at their intersections so that the larger radius is used consistently.
