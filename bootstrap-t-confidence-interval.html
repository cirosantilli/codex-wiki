Bootstrap-t confidence interval ([θ−q.975​s,θ−q.025​s])

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Bootstrapping (statistics) Bootstrap confidence interval
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For each bootstrap sample, form T∗=(θ∗−θ)/s∗, using that sample's estimated standard error, possibly from a nested bootstrap. If qp​ are conditional quantiles of T∗ and s is the original estimated standard error, invert the studentized inequalities to obtain the displayed interval. The quantile order is reversed by subtraction. Its coverage is approximate and requires a nondegenerate statistic and suitable bootstrap regularity.
