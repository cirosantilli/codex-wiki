Delta jump at activation of a down-and-in call

 ... Mathematical optimization Mathematical finance Fundamental theorem of asset pricing Contingent claim Barrier option Down-and-in claim
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
For strike c>b, write C(s,c,τ) for the ordinary European call option value. Before activation the down-and-in claim is I(s,τ)=(b/s)αC(b2/s,c,τ), where α=2ρ/σ2−1. Consequently its left-limit option delta at the barrier is −αC(b,c,τ)/b−Cs​(b,c,τ), whereas after activation it is Cs​(b,c,τ). Their difference is strictly positive for τ>0: differentiating the killed normal transition density at its boundary gives the integral of a strictly positive function against the positive call payoff. The value is continuous, so changing the bank holding finances the stock rebalance.
