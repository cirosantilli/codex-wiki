Mechanical energy (E=T+V)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Classical mechanics Energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 118 Articles: 2
Mechanical energy is the sum of kinetic energy and potential energy. It is conserved when all forces are conservative and the potential has no explicit time dependence.
