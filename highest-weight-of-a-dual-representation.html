Highest weight of a dual representation (−w0​λ)

 ... Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Lie algebra homomorphism Lie algebra representation Dual Lie algebra representation
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
For a finite-dimensional irreducible representation L(λ) of a complex semisimple Lie algebra, its dual Lie algebra representation has highest weight −w0​λ, where w0​ is the longest Weyl-group element. Indeed duality negates all weights, and w0​λ is the lowest weight of the original module. In particular w0​=−I makes every such irreducible module self-dual.
