Two-generator HNN embedding (G↪E=⟨a,t⟩)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Group embedding
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
For G=⟨gi​∣R⟩, put B=G∗F(a,b), u0​=a, v0​=b, ui​=b−iabi and vi​=gi​a−ibai for i≥1. The ui​ freely generate by free conjugate bases in a rank-two free group. The vi​ do too: projection killing G maps them to a free conjugate basis, so no reduced word in them is trivial. The HNN extension adjoining t−1ui​t=vi​ embeds G by Britton's lemma. Since b=t−1at and gi​=t−1ui​t(a−ibai)−1, its generators reduce to a,t. For finitely many gi​ and R this is a finite group presentation; for effective countably many generators and enumerable R it is a two-generator recursive presentation of a group.
