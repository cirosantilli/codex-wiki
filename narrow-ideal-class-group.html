Narrow ideal class group (Cl+(K))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebraic number theory Ideal class group
OurBigBook.com Words: 44
The narrow ideal class group of a number field is the group of nonzero fractional ideals modulo principal fractional ideals generated by totally positive elements. It is the ray class group for the modulus consisting of all real Archimedean places and trivial finite part. The forgetful map to the ordinary ideal class group is surjective, with kernel {±1}r1​/sgn(OK×​) under the unit signature map.
