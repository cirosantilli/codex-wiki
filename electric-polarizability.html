Electric polarizability (dind​=αE)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrostatics Electric dipole moment
OurBigBook.com Words: 528 Articles: 7
Electric polarizability relates an induced electric dipole moment to an applied electric field. In a weak-field model with linearity, dind​=αE. A tensor response allows different electric field directions to induce electric dipole moments in different directions. A response that changes with molecular rotation enables rotational Raman spectroscopy.
