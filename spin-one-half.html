Spin one-half (S=σ/2)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum mechanics Angular momentum operator Spin
OurBigBook.com Words: 149 Articles: 3
In units ℏ=1, spin one-half is the two-dimensional irreducible spin angular momentum representation with components Si​=σi​/2. The Pauli matrices give eigenvalues ±1/2 along every unit axis. The normalized x-axis states are (∣↑z​⟩±∣↓z​⟩)/2​.
