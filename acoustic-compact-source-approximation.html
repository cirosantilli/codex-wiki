Acoustic compact-source approximation (k0​ℓ≪1)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Linear acoustics
OurBigBook.com Words: 39
An acoustic source is compact when its size is much smaller than the acoustic wavelength. The propagation delay across the source can then be neglected in leading source integrals. This condition is distinct from the radiation-region requirement k0​r≫1.
