Cantor space ({0,1}N)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Product topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 528 Articles: 9
Cantor space is the binary sequence space with the product topology of discrete two-point factors. Here the Bernoulli-space synonym refers to this topology, without selecting a particular probability measure. The metric d(x,y)=∑j≥1​2−j∣xj​−yj​∣ induces the topology. A diagonal subsequence argument gives compactness. Fixing a finite prefix gives a clopen cylinder set. The map x↦∑j​2xj​3−j is a homeomorphism to the usual Cantor set.
