Number density of a relativistic neutrino relative to photons (nν​/nγ​=83gν​​(Tν​/Tγ​)3)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Cosmological entropy conservation Thermal decoupling in cosmology Cosmic neutrino background
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
With negligible chemical potential, the Fermi-Dirac distribution has 3/4 of the boson number density per degree of freedom at equal temperature. A species with gν​ populated states therefore has nν​/nγ​=(3gν​/8)(Tν​/Tγ​)3, since photons have two states. This remains the number-density ratio after the neutrino becomes massive if its comoving particle number is conserved.
