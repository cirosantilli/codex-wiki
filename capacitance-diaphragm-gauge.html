Capacitance diaphragm gauge

 ... Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Kinetic theory Thermal transpiration Thermal transpiration experiment with a glass microtube
OurBigBook.com Words: 43
A capacitance diaphragm gauge senses pressure through deflection of a diaphragm that changes a capacitor's capacitance. Its head can have a different temperature from the vessel being measured, allowing thermal transpiration in the connecting tube to change the head pressure. [ref]
