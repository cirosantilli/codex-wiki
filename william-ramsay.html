William Ramsay (1852–1916)

 Home Chemistry Chemist List of chemists
OurBigBook.com Words: 61 Articles: 1
A chemist who led the chemistry department at University College London from 1887 to 1913. His isolation of argon followed John William Strutt, 3rd Baron Rayleigh's observation that atmospheric nitrogen had greater density than chemically prepared nitrogen.[ref]
