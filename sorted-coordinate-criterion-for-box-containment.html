Sorted-coordinate criterion for box containment (X fits Y⟺ai​<bi​ for all i)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Dynamic programming Box nesting
OurBigBook.com Words: 81
Let a1​≤⋯≤ad​ and b1​≤⋯≤bd​ be the separately sorted dimension vectors. Matching corresponding ranks proves the forward construction when every ai​<bi​. Conversely, if ak​≥bk​, then the d−k+1 source dimensions from rank k onward require distinct targets strictly larger than ak​, but only the last d−k target ranks could qualify. The pigeonhole principle rules out such a permutation. Strict inequality matters: equal side lengths do not fit under this convention. Sorting and checking gives an O(dlogd) decision algorithm.
