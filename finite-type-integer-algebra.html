Finite-type integer algebra (R=Z[r1​,…,rm​])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 121 Articles: 1
A unital commutative ring generated by finitely many elements under addition and multiplication, starting from the image of the integers. Equivalently it is a quotient of a finite-variable polynomial ring over Z. localization of a ring at one element remains finite type, since R[1/r]≅R[T]/(rT−1). Generation here is as an algebra, not merely as a field extension when the ring happens to be a field.
