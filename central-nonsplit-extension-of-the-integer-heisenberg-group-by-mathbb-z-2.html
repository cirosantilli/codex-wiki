The quotient map
F2​/γ4​(F2​)⟶F2​/γ3​(F2​)
(65)
is a central nonsplit extension of the Integer Heisenberg group by γ3​(F2​)/γ4​(F2​)≅Z2. It cannot split because the source has abelianization Z2, whereas a central splitting would give an abelianization containing an additional direct factor Z2.
