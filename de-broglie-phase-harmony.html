de Broglie phase harmony

OurBigBook.com Words: 189
In the 1923 note Waves and Quanta, Louis de Broglie proposed a periodic process associated with rest energy, remaining in phase with a wave along the moving particle's path. He distinguished the internal frequency slowed by time dilation from the wave frequency based on total energy.[ref] The proposal developed into his 1924 thesis.[ref] In the 1929 lecture The wave nature of the electron, he reconstructed the argument using a Lorentz transformation.[ref]
Here is that quantum phase argument in modern notation. Set ω0​=mc2/ℏ in the rest frame. A Lorentz transformation to a frame where the particle has velocity v gives
t′=γ(t−c2vx​),γ=1−v2/c2​1​,ϕ=ω0​t′=ℏEt−px​,E=γmc2,p=γmv.
(2)
Along x=vt, proper time is τ=t/γ, so
ϕ(vt,t)=ℏ(E−pv)t​=γℏmc2t​=ω0​τ.
(3)
This is the harmony: evaluating the spatial wave at the moving particle gives the slower clock phase. At fixed x, its angular frequency is instead E/ℏ=γω0​. Since k=p/ℏ, the de Broglie wavelength follows as λ=2π/k=h/p. The proposed clock was a theoretical construction, not an observed mechanical vibration.
