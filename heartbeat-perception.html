Heartbeat perception

 Home Biology Neuroscience Sensory system Perception Interoception
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
Perception or estimation of one's heartbeat. A mental tracking score can depend on timing, beliefs about ordinary heart rate and response strategy as well as sensitivity to actual cardiac signals; such a score is not automatically a pure measure of interoceptive accuracy.[ref]
