For independent and identically distributed trials with constant success probability 0<q<1, let pn​ be the probability of avoiding two consecutive successes in the first n trials. An acceptable sequence starts either with a failure, leaving n−1 unrestricted fresh trials to check, or with a success followed by a failure, leaving n−2. These disjoint cases prove the recurrence for n≥2.
