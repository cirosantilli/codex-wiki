Nonorthogonal nearest-neighbour tight-binding band

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum theory Bloch's theorem Tight binding
OurBigBook.com Words: 65
For one localized orbital per site in a chain, take on-site matrix elements Hjj​=E0​, nearest-neighbour matrix elements Hj,j±1​=h, overlaps Sjj​=1 and Sj,j±1​=s, with other terms neglected and h,s real. A Bloch state reduces the generalized eigenvalue problem Hc=ESc to
E0​+2hcos(ka)=E[1+2scos(ka)],E(k)=1+2scos(ka)E0​+2hcos(ka)​.
(24)
The overlap metric must be positive for the retained model; ∣s∣<1/2 is sufficient for all k. Dropping it yields the cosine band of the orthogonal tight-binding approximation.
