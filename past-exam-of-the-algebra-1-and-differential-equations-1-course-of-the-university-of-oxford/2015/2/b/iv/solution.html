On each nonzero eigenspace Vλ​, the restricted linear map Sλ​=S∣Vλ​​ is diagonalizable. Indeed mS​(Sλ​)=0, so the minimal polynomial of Sλ​ divides mS​. A divisor of a product of distinct linear polynomials has the same property, proving the diagonalizable restriction lemma.
Choose a basis of Sλ​-eigenvectors in each Vλ​. Every chosen vector is simultaneously a T-eigenvector, with eigenvalue λ. The direct sum in part (ii) shows that joining these bases gives a basis of V. Therefore S and T have a common eigenbasis, which is exactly simultaneous diagonalization.
