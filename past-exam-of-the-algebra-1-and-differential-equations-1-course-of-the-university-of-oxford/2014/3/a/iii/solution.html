That is precisely membership of the orthogonal complement for a bilinear form. Therefore
U⊥=(imΦ∣U​)∘.​
(29)
The dimension formula in part (ii) gives
dimU⊥=n−rank(Φ∣U​)≥n−dimU,
(30)
so dimU+dimU⊥≥n​. More precisely, the rank-nullity theorem gives
dimU+dimU⊥=n+dim(U∩kerΦ).
(31)
This is the orthogonal complement dimension for a degenerate bilinear form. The possible excess comes from vectors annihilated by the form against all of V.
