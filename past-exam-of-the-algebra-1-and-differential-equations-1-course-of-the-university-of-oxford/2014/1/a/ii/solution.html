Suppose E2=E. Every vector has the decomposition
v=Ev+(v−Ev),Ev∈imE,v−Ev∈kerE.

If w=Ez also belongs to kerE, then w=E2z=Ew=0. Hence
V=imE⊕kerE.​
(3)
On its image of a linear map, E acts as the identity; on its kernel of a linear map, it acts as zero. Concatenate a basis of the image with a basis of the kernel. The resulting matrix is diag(Ir​,0), where r=rankE.
Conversely, this block matrix squares to itself, so any linear map having that matrix in a basis satisfies E2=E. Thus projection operators are precisely the linear maps with this block form. This is an algebraic projection; no inner product or orthogonality is required.
