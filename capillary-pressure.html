Capillary pressure (pc​=γκ)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Surface tension
OurBigBook.com Words: 87 Articles: 1
Capillary pressure is the pressure jump produced by a curved interface. For constant surface tension it is proportional to the interface curvature according to the Young–Laplace equation.
