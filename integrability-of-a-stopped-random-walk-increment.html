Integrability of a stopped random-walk increment (E∣XT​∣≤E∣X1​∣ET)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Stopping time
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
Let Xn​ be integrable independent and identically distributed random variables, and let T≥1 be a stopping time for their natural filtration, with ET<∞. Since {T≥n} is measurable before Xn​ is observed, independence and the Tonelli theorem give E∣XT​∣≤∑n​E[∣Xn​∣1{T≥n}​]=E∣X1​∣ET. The event T=n need not be independent of Xn​, so the selected increment can be biased.
