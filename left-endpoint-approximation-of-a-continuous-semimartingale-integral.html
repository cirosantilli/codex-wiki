Left-endpoint approximation of a continuous semimartingale integral (∑k<⌊t/h⌋​Hkh​(X(k+1)h​−Xkh​)u.c.p.​∫0t​Hs​dXs​)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Stochastic calculus Stochastic integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 79
For a left-continuous locally bounded adapted process H and a continuous semimartingale X, the elementary predictable left-endpoint approximations tend pointwise to H. Decompose X into a continuous local martingale and a continuous finite-variation process. After localization, dominated convergence with respect to the variation measure handles the finite-variation integral. The Itô isometry and Doob L2 maximal inequality handle the martingale integral. The omitted partial last interval is bounded by the local bound on H times the modulus of continuity of X. This proves uniform convergence on compacts in probability and is the grid-sum foundation of the Itô product rule.
