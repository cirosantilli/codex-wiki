Step potential (V(x)=V0​Θ(x))

For constant mass m, a finite jump from potential energy zero at x<0 to V0​>0 at x>0 requires continuity of the wavefunction and its first derivative. With incidence only from the left and E>V0​, put k=2mE​/ℏ and q=2m(E−V0​)​/ℏ. Matching A+B=C and k(A−B)=qC gives reflected probability amplitude B/A=(k−q)/(k+q) and transmission amplitude C/A=2k/(k+q). The transmission probability is (q/k)∣C/A∣2, conserving probability current.
For 0<E<V0​, replace q by iκ, where κ=2m(V0​−E)​/ℏ is the tunnelling decay constant. The right-hand wavefunction is Ce−κx, with zero probability current. Its probability density decays on length 1/(2κ), while its amplitude decays on length 1/κ. The semi-infinite raised region gives total reflection, unlike a finite potential barrier allowing quantum tunnelling into a second allowed region.
