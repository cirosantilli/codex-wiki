Graded Jacobi identity

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Lie superalgebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 18
The Jacobi identity of a Lie superalgebra includes parity signs. In particular, for odd c and even ϕ it gives [c,[c,ϕ]]=21​[[c,c],ϕ].
