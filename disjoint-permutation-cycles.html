Disjoint permutation cycles

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Symmetric group Permutation cycle
OurBigBook.com Words: 16
Permutation cycles with disjoint supports commute. The order of their product is the least common multiple of their lengths.
