Phonon density of states (g(ω)=dN/dω)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Density of states
OurBigBook.com Words: 122 Articles: 2
The number of lattice-vibration modes per unit angular frequency. For a three-dimensional isotropic monatomic crystal with three linear acoustic branches of a common speed vs​, wavevector-shell counting gives g(ω)=3Vω2/(2π2vs3​) below a suitable cutoff. Different branch speeds replace 3vs−3​ by vL−3​+2vT−3​.
