Finite pro-p presentation (G=Fp​(x1​,…,xd​)/⟨r1​,…,rs​⟩Fp​​)

 ... Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Topological group Profinite group Pro-p group Free pro-p group
OurBigBook.com Words: 128 Articles: 1
A finite generating set and finitely many relators in a free pro-p group, divided out by their closed normal closure. Relators are elements of the free pro-p group; they need not be finite ordinary words. A pro-p group with such a presentation is finitely presented in the pro-p category.
