Inverse Bernstein approximation on a fixed-degree polynomial space (∥Bn−1​f−f∥∞​→0)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis Stone-Weierstrass theorem Weierstrass approximation theorem Bernstein polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
On the fixed finite-dimensional space of polynomials of degree at most d, uniform convergence of Bn​ on each monomial and equivalence of norms in finite dimensions imply εn​=∥Bn​−I∥op​→0. A Neumann series then gives ∥Bn−1​−I∥op​≤εn​/(1−εn​) for sufficiently large n. Consequently the displayed convergence holds. A strictly positive polynomial has positive minimum on the compact interval, so its inverse Bernstein approximants are also strictly positive for all sufficiently large n.
