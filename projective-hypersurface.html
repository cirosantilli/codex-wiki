Projective hypersurface (Xd​)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry
OurBigBook.com Words: 623 Articles: 14
A degree-d projective hypersurface Xd​⊆Pkn​ is the closed subscheme cut out by one nonzero homogeneous polynomial of degree d.
This is a homogeneous-polynomial hypersurface in projective space.
