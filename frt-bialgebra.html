FRT bialgebra (CT1​T2​=T1​T2​C)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Algebra over a field Hopf algebra RTT construction
OurBigBook.com Words: 58
Given an invertible braiding C on V⊗V, the universal bialgebra coacting on V for which C is a comodule map is generated by the matrix coefficients tij​. Its comultiplication is Δtij​=∑k​tik​⊗tkj​. Colinearity gives quadratic relations. Writing R=τC turns the relation into the familiar RTT construction with the appropriate ordered tensor factors. A Yang-Baxter R-matrix supplies a scalar coquasitriangular structure.
