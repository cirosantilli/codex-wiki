Radial elastic compatibility (drd​trτ=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
In a homogeneous isotropic solid without body force, a radial displacement field satisfies (λ+2μ)(u′+2u/r)′=0. For nonzero λ+2μ, its volumetric strain is constant. Since Hooke's law gives trτ=(3λ+2μ)(u′+2u/r), its stress tensor has constant trace. After plasticity in continuum mechanics has occurred, this conclusion applies to purely elastic increments during elastic unloading, and need not apply to the total residual stress.
