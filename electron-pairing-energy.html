Electron pairing energy (P)

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry Ligand field
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The electron pairing energy represents the energetic penalty for putting electrons into already occupied orbitals rather than suitable separate orbitals. A simple comparison with octahedral splitting explains high-spin and low-spin alternatives.
