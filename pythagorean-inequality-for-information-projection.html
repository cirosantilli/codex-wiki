Pythagorean inequality for information projection (D(r∥p)≥D(r∥q∗)+D(q∗∥p))

Under the finite-alphabet, positive-reference, closed-convex set assumptions above, every feasible law r∈C satisfies
D(r∥p)≥D(r∥q∗)+D(q∗∥p).​
(92)
Thus a feasible law's excess relative entropy above the optimum is at least its relative entropy from the optimizer. The information projection supplies a quantitative comparison with every other feasible law, not merely the location of a minimum.[ref]
For affine expected value constraints, the result becomes a Pythagorean identity for relative entropy. In relative entropy minimization under a mean constraint, the logarithm of qi∗​/pi​ is an affine function of the outcome ai​. Any feasible r has the same normalization and mean as q∗, making the residual in the three-point identity for relative entropy zero.[ref]
