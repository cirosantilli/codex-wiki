Continuous-time symmetric simple random walk (EeiθXt​=e−t(1−cosθ))

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Lévy process Compound Poisson process
OurBigBook.com Words: 129 Articles: 1
At unit jump rate, this is Xt​=∑k=1Nt​​ξk​, where N is a rate-one Poisson process and the independent marks are uniform signs. Equivalently it is the difference of two independent rate-1/2 Poisson processes. It is a Lévy process with characteristic function exp{t(cosθ−1)}, mean zero, variance t, and Lévy characteristic exponent 1−cosθ in the negative-exponent convention. A general total jump rate rescales time.
