Wien displacement constant (b)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Radiative transfer Planck function Wien's displacement law
OurBigBook.com Words: 47
The Wien displacement constant is the constant of proportionality b in λmax​T=b. From the exact International System of Units values of the Planck constant, speed of light and Boltzmann constant,
b=(1.380649×10−23)(4.9651142317…)(6.62607015×10−34)(299792458)​mK≃2.897771955×10−3mK.
(34)
The units follow from hc/kB​: (Js)(ms−1)/(JK−1)=mK. The defining constants are exact; the displayed decimal is rounded. [ref]
