Nutrient

 Home Biology
OurBigBook.com Words: 34 Articles: 4
A nutrient supplies material or energy used by an organism. In mathematical biology, a nutrient concentration can be transported, produced and consumed, and can influence population dynamics or act as a chemoattractant.
