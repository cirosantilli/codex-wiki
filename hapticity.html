Hapticity (ηn)

 Home Chemistry Coordination chemistry Ligand in coordination chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 60 Articles: 1
Hapticity, written ηn, specifies the number of contiguous ligand atoms involved in attachment to a metal. It differs from denticity, which counts donor sites in a chelate.
