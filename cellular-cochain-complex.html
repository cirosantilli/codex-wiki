Cellular cochain complex (Ccellq​(X;A)=Hom(Cqcell​(X;Z),A))

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homology (mathematics) Chain complex Cellular chain complex
OurBigBook.com Words: 213 Articles: 3
For a CW complex, applying Hom(−,A) to its integral cellular chain complex gives a cochain complex. The differential is precomposition with the cellular boundary. For finitely many cells it is represented by the transpose of the boundary matrix, reduced in the coefficient group when appropriate.
