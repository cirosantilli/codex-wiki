Fault-plane ambiguity

 ... Analysis Partial differential equation Wave equation Elastic wave Seismic moment tensor Focal mechanism
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
Interchanging the slip direction and fault normal leaves a double-couple moment tensor unchanged. First-motion polarities therefore cannot identify the physical fault plane without information such as mapped rupture, aftershock geometry or geodetic displacement.
