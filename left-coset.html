Left coset (gH)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Coset
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
For a subgroup H of a group G and g∈G, its left coset is gH={gh:h∈H}. Left multiplication h↦gh is a bijection from H onto gH. Left and right cosets agree for every g exactly when H is a normal subgroup.
