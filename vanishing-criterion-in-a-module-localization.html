Vanishing criterion in a module localization (m/1=0 in S−1M⟺sm=0 for some s∈S)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Localization of a ring Localization of a module
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
The fraction relation defining localization of a module gives this criterion. For localization at powers of a single element f, a fraction vanishes exactly when some power of f annihilates its numerator. Finitely many such vanishing relations can be cleared with one common exponent. This is distinct from detecting global zero by localizing at every prime ideal.
