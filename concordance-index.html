Concordance index (C)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Survival analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 54
A survival concordance index measures agreement between predicted risk ordering and observed event ordering over comparable subject pairs, with half credit for prediction ties. A pair is ordinarily comparable when the earlier observed follow-up ends in an event. Censoring-adjusted versions can reduce dependence on follow-up patterns. Good discrimination does not imply good survival prediction calibration or correct proportional hazards.
