Hydrostatic volume strain from Young's modulus and Poisson's ratio (ΔV/V=−3(1−2ν)p/E)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Continuum mechanics Elasticity (physics) Linear elasticity Bulk modulus
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
For an isotropic linearly elastic solid, equal compressive principal stresses −p produce three equal normal strains −(1−2ν)p/E. Adding them gives the displayed volume strain, or bulk modulus K=E/[3(1−2ν)]. It is a small-strain relation using the pressure change, not an arbitrary absolute pressure reference.
