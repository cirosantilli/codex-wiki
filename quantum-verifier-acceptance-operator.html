OurBigBook.com Words: 50
A verifier with clean ancillas and a designated output measurement induces an effect 0≤M≤I on the witness register. Its acceptance probability is Tr(Mρ), and the largest possible acceptance is ∥M∥. Spectral bounds on M make the soundness quantifier over all witnesses, including entangled repeated witnesses, explicit.
