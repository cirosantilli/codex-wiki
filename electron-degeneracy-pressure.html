Electron degeneracy pressure

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Quantum ideal-gas statistics Fermi-Dirac distribution Fermi gas Degeneracy pressure
OurBigBook.com Words: 166 Articles: 3
Electron degeneracy pressure supports dense objects when electrons fill momentum states up to a Fermi surface. In the nonrelativistic limit, a fixed-mass self-gravitating object has the characteristic degenerate scaling R∝M−1/3.
