Abrikosov vortex lattice

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Superconductivity Type-II superconductor Superconducting mixed state Abrikosov vortex
OurBigBook.com Words: 402 Articles: 3
A periodic array of Abrikosov vortices. In the isotropic local theory near the upper critical magnetic field, the triangular array has a lower Helmholtz free energy than the square array.[ref]
The useful distinction is between vortex existence, array geometry, and vortex mobility. Quantized winding does not force every array to be triangular or defect-free. A stationary array and vortices moving under an applied electric current also have different transport consequences; flux pinning matters for carrying useful electric currents.
