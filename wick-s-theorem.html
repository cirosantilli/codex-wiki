Wick's theorem

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 714 Articles: 14
Wick theorem expresses a time-ordered product of free fields as its normal-ordered product plus the sum over every possible contraction. It turns the Dyson series into Feynman diagrams.
