Dyadic partition (IJ,k​=[k2−J,(k+1)2−J))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Riemann integration Partition of an interval
OurBigBook.com Words: 184 Articles: 2
A dyadic partition of [0,1] at level J consists of 2J equal cells of length 2−J. Each cell splits into two cells at the next level. Use left-closed, right-open cells, adding the endpoint 1 to the final cell when a pointwise representative is needed. These nested partitions underlie the Haar scaling functions and Haar wavelets.
