Group ring (R[G])

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra Ring
OurBigBook.com Words: 373 Articles: 5
The group ring R[G] consists of finite formal sums ∑g∈G​rg​g with coefficients in a ring R. Addition is coefficientwise and multiplication extends the group operation and multiplication in R distributively. A group algebra is the case in which R is a field.
