Support of a vector (suppx)

 ... Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis Sparse optimization Compressed sensing Sparse vector
OurBigBook.com Words: 98 Articles: 1
The support of a finite vector is the set of indices of its nonzero coordinates. It is the support of a function on a finite discrete index set, and its cardinality defines sparsity.
