Gravitational-wave energy and angular-momentum balance (E˙=ΩJ˙)

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Gravitational wave Mass quadrupole moment Quadrupole formula
OurBigBook.com Words: 120
For a slowly evolving circular binary star with separation a, reduced mass μ and orbital angular frequency Ω, the leading gravitational wave losses satisfy E˙=ΩJ˙. One direct check uses the standard quadrupole angular-momentum loss law
J˙i​=−5c52G​ϵijk​⟨Q¨​jℓ​Q...​​kℓ​⟩,
(10)
where Q is the mass quadrupole moment and ϵijk​ is the Levi-Civita symbol. For r=a(cosΩt,sinΩt,0), put A=μa2; then Qxx​=A(1/6+cos(2Ωt)/2), Qyy​=A(1/6−cos(2Ωt)/2), Qxy​=Asin(2Ωt)/2, and Qzz​=−A/3. Substitution gives J˙z​=−32GA2Ω5/(5c5) and the quadrupole formula gives E˙=−32GA2Ω6/(5c5). Their ratio proves the balance relation. The same relation follows from the rotating wave's angular harmonic number two and frequency 2Ω. The additional angular-momentum flux law, not the energy flux alone, is needed to justify preservation of secular circularity.
