Noncompact counterexample to the Šura-Bura lemma

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Connected space Connected component Quasi-component Šura-Bura lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 90
Set p=(0,0), q=(1,0), εm​=1/(m+2), and let Am​ be the polygonal arc joining (εm​,0), (εm​,m), (1−εm​,m), and (1−εm​,0). In the planar subspace Z={p,q}∪⋃m​Am​, each arc is clopen. The component of p is therefore just {p}. Any clopen neighborhood of p contains the left endpoints of all sufficiently high arcs, hence those entire connected arcs, and by closedness also the limit q of their right endpoints. Thus its quasi-component is {p,q}. Higher arcs escape to infinity while their endpoints approach the two distinguished points.
