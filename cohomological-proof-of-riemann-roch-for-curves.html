Cohomological proof of Riemann-Roch for curves (χ(O(D))=degD+1−g)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Divisor on an algebraic curve Riemann-Roch theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 38
On a smooth projective curve, the exact sequence 0→O(D−P)→O(D)→k(P)→0 increases the Euler characteristic by one. Iteration for positive and negative coefficients gives χ(O(D))=degD+1−g. Serre duality identifies h1(O(D)) with h0(O(K−D)), proving ℓ(D)−ℓ(K−D)=degD+1−g.
