Conditional expectation of a summand given future partial sums

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Measure theory Conditional expectation
OurBigBook.com Words: 26
For independent and identically distributed random variables Xj​ that are integrable random variables, with Sn​=∑j=1n​Xj​, symmetry gives E[X1​∣Sn​,Sn+1​,…]=Sn​/n. The decreasing sigma-algebras generated by these future sums permit application of the reverse martingale convergence theorem.
