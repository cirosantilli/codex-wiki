Smooth endoplasmic reticulum

 Home Biology Biological cell Organelle Endoplasmic reticulum
OurBigBook.com Words: 63
Smooth endoplasmic reticulum lacks attached ribosomes. Specialized regions support lipid metabolism and processing of foreign compounds; the endoplasmic reticulum also stores and releases calcium ions. Regional specialization does not split the network into two separate organelles, nor make every smooth region perform every function.[ref]
