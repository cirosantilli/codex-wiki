Principal curvature (k1​,k2​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Differential geometry Second fundamental form Shape operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 313 Articles: 2
At a point of a smooth surface, a normal section of a surface can bend by different amounts depending on its tangent vector. The principal curvatures are the largest and smallest signed normal curvatures among unit tangent vectors. Equivalently they are the two eigenvalues k1​,k2​ of the shape operator, with an orthonormal basis e1​,e2​ of corresponding principal directions. Here the labels follow the selected directions, without requiring k1​≤k2​. [ref]
For a unit tangent vector making angle θ with e1​, Euler formula for normal curvature gives
v=cosθe1​+sinθe2​,kn​(θ)=II(v,v)=k1​cos2θ+k2​sin2θ.
(20)
The normal curvature is a weighted average of the principal curvatures; the extremes occur in the principal directions. If the principal curvatures coincide, every direction in the tangent plane is a principal direction: this is an umbilical point. The Gaussian curvature and mean curvature summarize the pair by
K=k1​k2​,H=2k1​+k2​​.
(21)
For an outward-oriented sphere of radius R, both principal curvatures are −1/R, so the normal curvature is −1/R in every direction. For an outward-oriented circular cylinder, choose e1​ around the circumference and e2​ along the axis. The principal curvatures are −1/R,0, giving kn​(θ)=−cos2θ/R; halfway between the principal directions, kn​(π/4)=−1/(2R).
At the origin of the upward-oriented hyperbolic paraboloid z=(x2−y2)/2, the principal directions are the coordinate axes and the principal curvatures are 1,−1 in the chosen length unit. Thus kn​(θ)=cos(2θ), with zero normal curvature at θ=π/4,3π/4. Zero normal curvature in these directions coexists with Gaussian curvature −1: the shape operator is not zero. The mean curvature is zero at this point; this does not say that the whole hyperbolic paraboloid is a minimal surface.
Each graph uses angle θ in the tangent plane, measured from the indicated first principal direction. The sphere and circular cylinder have R=1; the saddle graph refers only to the origin. Reversing the unit normal reflects each normal curvature graph through zero, negates both principal curvatures and the mean curvature, and preserves their product, the Gaussian curvature.
