Equal-brightness binary-star visibility (g(Y)=g⋆​(Y)cos(πβY/λ))

 ... Branches of physics Optics Physical optics Interferometer Astronomical interferometer Michelson stellar interferometer
OurBigBook.com Words: 191 Articles: 2
Let identical spatially incoherent stellar brightness profiles of equal integrated flux be displaced by projected angles ±β/2 along an interferometer baseline. The Fourier shift property makes their normalized signed coherence g(Y)=g⋆​(Y)cos(πβY/λ). The observed visibility is its absolute value. The slow single-star envelope depends on each star's brightness profile, while the oscillatory factor records projected separation. Unequal flux prevents complete cancellation at the binary minima.
