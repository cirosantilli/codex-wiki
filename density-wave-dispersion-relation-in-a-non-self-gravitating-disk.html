Density-wave dispersion relation in a non-self-gravitating disk (ω2=κr2​+cs2​k2)

Let cs2​=(dP/dΣ)0​ be the barotropic closure of a razor-thin disk sound-speed squared and κr​ the radial epicyclic frequency, with κr2​=2(2−qr​)Ω2 for orbital shear parameter qr​. For a radial axisymmetric normal mode in a uniform barotropic shearing sheet, let Ux​,Uy​ be velocity amplitudes and σ the surface-density amplitude. Linear momentum gives −iωUx​=2ΩUy​−ikcs2​σ/Σ0​ and −iωUy​=−κr2​Ux​/(2Ω); mass conservation gives ωσ=kΣ0​Ux​. Eliminating the amplitudes for ω=0 proves the displayed dispersion relation. The rotational term is epicyclic restoration; the pressure term is acoustic restoration. Without a perturbed gravitational potential, no self-gravity term belongs in the formula.
