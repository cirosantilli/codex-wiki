Eilenberg–MacLane space (K(A,n))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Homotopy group
OurBigBook.com Words: 373 Articles: 7
A connected Eilenberg–MacLane space K(G,n), n≥1, has πn​≅G and all other positive-degree homotopy groups trivial. The group G must be abelian when n≥2, but may be nonabelian when n=1. For an abelian group, the Dold–Kan correspondence constructs a simplicial model from the chain complex concentrated in degree n. A classifying space of a discrete group gives the general K(G,1) case.
