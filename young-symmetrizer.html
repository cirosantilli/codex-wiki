Young symmetrizer (ht​=ct​rt​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Representation theory Representation theory of the symmetric group Partition of an integer Young tableau
OurBigBook.com Words: 144 Articles: 1
For a Young tableau t, let rt​ sum its row permutations and ct​ be the signed sum of its column permutations in the group algebra of the symmetric group. The product ht​=ct​rt​ is a Young symmetrizer. The other order is another conventional realization. It satisfies ht2​=Hλ​ht​, where Hλ​ is the hook product of a partition, so division by that scalar gives a primitive idempotent in characteristic zero. Acting on a tensor power constructs a Schur module.
