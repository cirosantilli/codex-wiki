Exponential of a Pauli vector (e−ian⋅σ=Icosa−in⋅σsina)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Pauli matrices
OurBigBook.com Words: 92 Articles: 1
For a real unit vector n, the Pauli matrix multiplication law gives (n⋅σ)2=I. Separating the even and odd powers in the matrix exponential proves the displayed identity. For spin one-half, the physical rotation through angle θ has a=θ/2.
