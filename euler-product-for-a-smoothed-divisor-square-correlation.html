Euler product for a smoothed divisor-square correlation (E(z,z′)=ζ(1+z+z′)H(z,z′)/(ζ(1+z)ζ(1+z′)))

For Rez,Rez′>0, the series ∑d,e​μ(d)μ(e)d−ze−z′/[d,e] has local factor 1−p−1−z−p−1−z′+p−1−z−z′. Dividing by the displayed zeta function ratio leaves a holomorphic function H near zero with H(0,0)=1. The local factor of H is (1−a−b+c)(1−c)/((1−a)(1−b)), where a=p−1−z, b=p−1−z′, c=p−1−z−z′, and equals one exactly at zero. With z=(1+it)/logX, the zeta function poles produce the kernel (1+it)(1+it′)/((2+i(t+t′))logX) that determines the smooth divisor-square sieve asymptotic.
