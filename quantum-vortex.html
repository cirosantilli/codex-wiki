Quantum vortex

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Order parameter Topological defect Phase vortex
OurBigBook.com Words: 459 Articles: 6
A quantum vortex has integer winding number of a condensate's complex argument. For atomic mass m, its superfluid velocity gives circulation 2πℏq/m for integer q. A straight vortex has a density-depleted core and, outside it, tangential velocity qℏ/(mr). Its long-range flow energy is proportional to q2log(R/a), while its angular momentum has the sign of q.
