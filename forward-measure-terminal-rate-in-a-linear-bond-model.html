Forward-measure terminal rate in a linear bond model (EQT[rT​∣Ft​]=e−(T−t)rt​/P(t,T))

For the bounded rate diffusion of linear bond pricing in a bounded short-rate diffusion, Dt​e−(T−t)rt​ is a bounded martingale with terminal value DT​rT​. Dividing its conditional expectation by Dt​P(t,T) through the Bayes formula for conditional expectation gives the displayed forward-measure expectation. It lies between zero and one.
