Gustaf Dalén (1869–1937)

 Home Engineering Mechanical engineering Mechanical engineer List of mechanical engineers
OurBigBook.com Words: 100 Articles: 1
A mechanical engineer whose work connected gas lighting to automatic operation. Chalmers records his mechanical-engineering qualification in 1896.[ref] After an accident left him blind in 1912, he returned to AGA in 1913 and continued leading the company.[ref]
The sun valve links radiometric absorptance, thermal expansion and mechanical motion to a useful decision: whether a beacon should receive its main gas supply. The Dalén light combines this daylight response with repeated flashes. The Nobel Prize in Physics in 1912 recognized his automatic regulators for lighthouse and buoy lighting.
