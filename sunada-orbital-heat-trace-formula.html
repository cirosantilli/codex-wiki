Sunada orbital heat-trace formula (ZU\N​(t)=∣U∣−1∑u∈U​It​(u))

 ... Geometry and topology Differential geometry Riemannian geometry Spectral geometry Isospectral manifolds Sunada theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 78
For a finite subgroup U of a group T of Riemannian isometries of a closed manifold N, assume U acts freely and define It​(g)=∫N​HN​(t,x,gx)dV(x). Invariance of the Riemannian heat kernel and change of variables show that It​ is a class function on T. For U≤T, the heat kernel on a finite isometric quotient and integration over a fundamental domain give the displayed heat trace formula. Grouping by conjugacy classes proves equality for Gassmann equivalent subgroups. More generally the same argument allows fixed points of T outside the freely acting subgroups; only the quotient under consideration must be smooth.
