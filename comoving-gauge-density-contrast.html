Comoving-gauge density contrast (Δ=δ−3H(1+w)v)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Linear cosmological density perturbation Density contrast
OurBigBook.com Words: 191 Articles: 3
For a perfect fluid with P=wρ, in Newtonian gauge in cosmology the density perturbation on fluid-comoving slices is the displayed combination of the fractional density perturbation and velocity potential. The Einstein energy and momentum constraints combine into ∇2ϕ=4πGa2ρˉ​Δ when scalar anisotropic stress vanishes. This gives a relativistic Poisson constraint valid beyond the subhorizon limit.
