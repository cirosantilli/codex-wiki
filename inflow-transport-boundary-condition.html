Inflow transport boundary condition (u(t,0)=f(t))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Partial differential equation Transport equation Linear transport equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 115 Articles: 1
For a transport equation on a domain, prescribe boundary data only where the velocity points into the domain. On x>0 with speed a>0, a bounded weak solution with initial data u0​ and inflow data f satisfies
∫u(φt​+aφx​)+∫u0​φ(0,x)+a∫f(t)φ(t,0)=0.
(16)
The integrals are over the quadrant and its two boundary rays. Backtracking characteristic curves gives u0​(x−at) when x≥at and f(t−x/a) otherwise. Corner values need not match for a bounded weak solution.
