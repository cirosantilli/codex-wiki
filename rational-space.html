Rational space

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology Homotopy Rational homotopy theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 200 Articles: 3
In the simply connected homotopy category, a rational space is a simply connected CW complex whose positive homotopy groups are vector spaces over Q. Multiplication by every nonzero integer on these groups is invertible. More generally one uses nilpotent spaces with localized fundamental group and higher homotopy groups; the simply connected version avoids local-coefficient twisting.
