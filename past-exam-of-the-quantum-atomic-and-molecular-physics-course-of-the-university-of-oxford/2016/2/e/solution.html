For the second isotope, consecutive gap differences are approximately 56.4 MHz, but the smallest gap is about 1.5 times this value. The higher-F values for the five gaps are therefore 3/2,5/2,7/2,9/2,11/2. The six levels run from F=1/2 to 11/2. The electronic state is unchanged, so J=5/2 as in d; the endpoints give

The positive transition frequencies do not fix either coupling sign. If a signed ratio is required, equal signs must be supplied as an additional assumption or determined by an ordering-sensitive measurement. No absolute nuclear g-factor is inferred without a calibrated electronic field coefficient. The second ladder in b illustrates the inferred half-integer F sequence without turning that sign ambiguity into a falsely measured ordering.
