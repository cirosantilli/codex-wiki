The limit near 121.8 nm lies about hc/(121.8 nm)≃10.18 eV above its lower term. Subtracting from the approximately 11.88 eV ground-state ionization energy puts that lower term near 1.70 eV: the 3d2D3/2,5/2​ pair. The first upper levels are the already identified 4p2P1/2,3/2​ pair. Their allowed decays are

The fourth possible pairing, P1/2​→D5/2​, has ∣ΔJ∣=2 and is forbidden as an electric dipole transition. The three lines therefore arise from doublet terms, not a spin-triplet term: “triplet series” counts three spectral lines.
For example, the first two wavelengths together with the 4p3/2​ energy give E(3d3/2​)≃1.693 eV and E(3d5/2​)≃1.700 eV. The longer 854.2 nm wavelength from the same upper level places its lower D level higher. The 866.2 nm transition independently returns the lower D energy within the rounding precision. Increasing the upper principal quantum number gives np2P→3d2D triplets with two nearby lower-term ionization limits near 121.8 nm. The arrows are included in b; their emission direction is P to D, not the reverse. The independent tabulation confirms the D-term position and ordering.[ref]
