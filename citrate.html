OurBigBook.com Words: 38
Citrate denotes an ion derived from citric acid. The commonly specified form has electric charge equal to that of three electrons and chemical formula C6​H5​O73−​; it results from removing three hydrogen ions from citric acid. Forms with additional hydrogen ions attached through protonation also occur. [ref]
