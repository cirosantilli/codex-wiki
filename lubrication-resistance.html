Lubrication resistance (F∼μa2U/h)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Viscous fluid flow Lubrication theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 260 Articles: 3
Lubrication theory predicts large viscous resistance when fluid must be squeezed through a narrow gap. For normal approach of smooth curved surfaces with curvature scale a and minimum separation h≪a, the resisting force scales as μa2U/h. Under a bounded normal force this gives ∣h˙∣=O(h), so ideal smooth surfaces do not make contact in finite time. Tangential and rolling motions have different resistance laws; the normal squeeze law should not be applied to them.
