Jordan chain (v1​,…,vr​)

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Operator theory Jordan normal form Generalized eigenvector
OurBigBook.com Words: 112 Articles: 1
A Jordan chain of a linear operator T for an eigenvalue λ is a list of nonzero vectors satisfying (T−λI)v1​=0 and (T−λI)vj​=vj−1​ for 2≤j≤r. These vectors are linearly independent: in a vanishing linear combination, applying the largest relevant power of T−λI isolates the last coefficient times v1​, then descending induction removes all coefficients. On their span the operator has one Jordan block. This is different from a generalized eigenfunction that solves (T−λI)u=0 outside the original function space.
