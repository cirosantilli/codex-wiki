The henry is the SI unit of inductance:
1H=1AWb​=1AVs​.
(153)
A coil with self-inductance 1H has magnetic flux linkage 1Wb at electric current 1A. A rate of change of electric current 1A/s produces induced electromotive force of magnitude 1V. The weber measures magnetic flux and magnetic flux linkage; the henry measures their ratio to electric current. [ref].
