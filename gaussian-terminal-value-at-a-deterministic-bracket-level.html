Gaussian terminal value at a deterministic bracket level ([M]T​=a⟹MT​∼N(0,a))

 ... Probability theory Martingale Continuous-time martingale Local martingale Continuous local martingale Dambis-Dubins-Schwarz theorem
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
Let M be a zero-starting continuous local martingale and let T<∞ be a stopping time with [M]t∧T​≤a and [M]T​=a for a deterministic a. The complex exponential exp(iuMt∧T​+u2[M]t∧T​/2) is a bounded local martingale, hence a true martingale. Its terminal expectation is one, giving the characteristic function EeiuMT​=e−u2a/2. Conditional optional sampling between two bracket levels also gives independent Gaussian increments in inverse-bracket time; Gaussian one-time marginals alone do not establish that independence.
