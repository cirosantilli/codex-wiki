Hypergraph complement (E(H)=(ℓV​)∖E(H))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Hypergraph Uniform hypergraph
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The complement of an ℓ-uniform hypergraph on V has all ℓ-subsets of V that are not hyperedges of the original hypergraph. An edge in every r-set of one hypergraph is equivalent to the absence of a complete ℓ-uniform r-vertex hypergraph in its complement.
