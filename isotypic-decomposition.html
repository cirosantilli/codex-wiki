Isotypic decomposition

A finite-length semisimple module has a canonical decomposition
M=⨁i​Mi​
(19)
in which Mi​ is the sum of all simple submodules isomorphic to a fixed simple module Si​. Each Mi​ is isomorphic to a finite direct sum Simi​​.
The decomposition is intrinsic to the semisimple module: the isomorphism types of its simple submodules determine the summands.
