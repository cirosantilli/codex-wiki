Josephson coupling energy (UJ​=−EJ​cosϕ,EJ​=ℏIc​/(2e))

The ideal Josephson junction has a phase-dependent energy
UJ​(ϕ)=−EJ​cosϕ,EJ​=2eℏIc​​.
(21)
Differentiating and using the voltage–phase relation yields
dtdUJ​​=EJ​sinϕϕ˙​=VIs​.
(22)
Thus the ideal pair element stores and returns energy. At V=0 it carries a static electric current without electrical power loss. Under a nonzero periodic drive its instantaneous power need not vanish, but its energy returns after a full phase cycle. Real junctions also have a capacitance, quasiparticle transport and an external circuit; their total losses cannot be inferred from this conservative pair term alone.
