Chlorine monoxide dimer cycle (ClO+ClO+M⇌ClOOCl+M)

 Home Chemistry Atmospheric chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 130 Articles: 2
A low-temperature catalytic ozone destruction pathway involving ClO association to ClOOCl, photodissociation of the dimer and regeneration of chlorine atoms. Combined with two Cl + O3 reactions its net effect is 2O3​→3O2​. Low temperature suppresses thermal dimer dissociation, while sunlight is needed for the photolytic step. Polar stratospheric clouds can activate reservoir chlorine before the sunlit cycle becomes rapid.
