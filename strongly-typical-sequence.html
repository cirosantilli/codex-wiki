Strongly typical sequence

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Asymptotic equipartition property
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
For a finite-alphabet IID random variable source, a strongly typical word has empirical symbol frequencies within the chosen tolerance of every p(x), and contains no zero-probability symbols. Unlike a weakly typical sequence, this definition controls the individual symbol counts. Both definitions give sets whose probability tends to one for fixed positive tolerance.
