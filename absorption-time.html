Absorption time (TA​=inf{m≥0:Xm​∈A})

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Hitting probability First-passage time
OurBigBook.com Words: 77
For a discrete-time Markov chain and a set A of absorbing states, its absorption time is the hitting time TA​=inf{m≥0:Xm​∈A}. It is zero when the initial state already lies in A, and infinite on a sample path that never enters A. Finiteness almost surely need not imply a finite expected value. For a finite-state model, a uniform positive probability of absorption within a fixed number of steps gives a geometric series bound on the survival probabilities and proves that the mean is finite. The mean absorption time of a lazy symmetric random walk is an explicit example.
