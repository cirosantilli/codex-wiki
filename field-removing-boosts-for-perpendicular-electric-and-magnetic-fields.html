Field-removing boosts for perpendicular electric and magnetic fields

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Relativistic electromagnetism Electromagnetic four-potential Electromagnetic field tensor Electromagnetic field invariants
OurBigBook.com Words: 105
For perpendicular electric field E and magnetic field B, the sign of E2−c2B2 determines which field can be removed by a Lorentz boost. If positive, w=c2E×B/E2 gives B′=0; if negative, w=E×B/B2 gives E′=0. Both results follow from E⊥′​=γ(E+w×B) and B⊥′​=γ(B−w×E/c2); the relevant strict inequality ensures ∣w∣<c. A nonzero null crossed field cannot be reduced to a single field by a finite Lorentz boost. A particle initially at rest in the laboratory has initial velocity −w in the chosen inertial frame, which must be retained when solving its trajectory.
