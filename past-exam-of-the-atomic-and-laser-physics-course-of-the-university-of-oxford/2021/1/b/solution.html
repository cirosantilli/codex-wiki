Within a fixed L,S,J angular momentum multiplet, the component of L along J follows from 2L⋅J=J2+L2−S2. The corresponding result for S follows by interchanging L and S. For the quantum state ∣LSJMJ​⟩,
⟨Lz​⟩⟨Sz​⟩​=MJ​2J(J+1)J(J+1)+L(L+1)−S(S+1)​,=MJ​2J(J+1)J(J+1)+S(S+1)−L(L+1)​.​
(4)
These are dimensionless expectation values in the convention of part (a). Substituting into the first-order time-independent perturbation theory formula ΔE=⟨HZ​⟩ gives
ΔE=gJ​μB​BMJ​,gJ​=1+(gs​−1)2J(J+1)J(J+1)+S(S+1)−L(L+1)​.​
(5)
For gs​=2, the Landé g-factor becomes
gJ​=1+2J(J+1)J(J+1)+S(S+1)−L(L+1)​.​
(6)
The magnetic sublevels must first be diagonalized within a degenerate multiplet; choosing MJ​ along the magnetic field already does this. For J=0 the displayed fraction is undefined, but all vector expectation values vanish and there is no first-order Zeeman splitting. A higher-order shift may remain.
