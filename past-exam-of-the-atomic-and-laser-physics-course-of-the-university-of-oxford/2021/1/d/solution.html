Neglect hyperfine structure, spin-orbit coupling and the diamagnetic term, and set b=μB​B, ε=αa02​. The Zeeman energy is b(LB​+2SB​). The electron spin is free, so every spatial result below is duplicated with an added ±b.
For a magnetic field parallel to z, the old magnetic quantum number m remains good and the first-order energy levels are
E1s,±​E2p0​,±​E2s,±​E2pm​,±​​=−Ry+23​ε±b,=−Ry/4+3ε±b,=−Ry/4+21ε±b,=−Ry/4+21ε+mb±b,m=±1.​​
(11)
The two s orbitals have zero orbital angular momentum, so only their electron spin contributes at this order.
For a magnetic field parallel to x, the expectation of Lx​ in each individual pm​ orbital vanishes. One must still check the degenerate m=±1 subspace: its projected Lx​ is zero as well. Thus there is no first-order orbital splitting in a sufficiently weak transverse magnetic field. Spin still contributes ±b.
The transverse orbital Zeeman mixing can be seen without assuming its size. Put E0​=−Ry/4+3ε, Et​=−Ry/4+21ε, and Δ=Et​−E0​=18ε. In the real px​,py​,pz​ orbital basis, px​ is unaffected while py​,pz​ have off-diagonal matrix elements of modulus b. Diagonalizing this two-dimensional Hamiltonian gives the spatial energy eigenvalues
Et​,2Et​+E0​​±(2Δ​)2+b2​.​
(12)
When b≪Δ, their shifts relative to the earlier levels are 0,+b2/Δ,−b2/Δ, with ±b added for electron spin. The displayed square-root expression also handles b comparable to Δ, provided coupling to other principal shells stays negligible. The 2s orbital retains zero orbital correction.
These quadratic orbital estimates keep the enhanced contribution from mixing the split 2p levels. Minimal magnetic minimal coupling also produces e2B2r⊥2​/(8me​); its expectation value is positive and must be added for a full order-B2 calculation. For example it gives e2B2a02​/(4me​) for 1s. It is parametrically smaller than the 2p mixing term when ε≪Ry.
