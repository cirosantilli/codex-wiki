The printed magnetic dipole moment uses dimensionless angular momentum operators: their physical counterparts are ℏL and ℏS. Choose the magnetic field along z. Then the Zeeman energy is HZ​=μB​B(Lz​+gs​Sz​), whose characteristic size is μB​B. In LS coupling, treating this as a time-independent perturbation theory requires it to be much smaller than the relevant fine structure separation, rather than merely smaller than the Coulomb interaction energy.
For a concrete hydrogen atom estimate take the 2p1/2​,2p3/2​ pair. The hydrogen fine-structure correction is
Enj​=−n2Ry​−n3Ryαfs2​​(j+1/21​−4n3​),
(1)
where αfs​ is the fine-structure constant, distinguished from the later potential coefficient. Thus
ΔE2p​=16Ryαfs2​​≃4.53×10−5eV,μB​ΔE2p​​≃0.78T.
(2)
Consequently a suitable weak-magnetic field condition for this pair is
B≪0.8T;B∼10−2T is comfortably perturbative.​
(3)
There is no single threshold for every hydrogen energy level: the relevant fine structure separations decrease with n. The 1s energy level has no same-n spin-orbit interaction partner, so it cannot itself define this comparison. This calculation neglects hyperfine structure; resolving that smaller scale requires an additional weak-magnetic field condition.
