The closed 1s22s2 shells have L=S=0, leaving the equivalent 2p2 electrons. Addition of angular momentum and the Pauli exclusion principle permit the atomic terms 3P,1D,1S. Their term symbols use 2S+1LJ​. Hund's rules put the triplet state below the singlet states; for a less-than-half-filled p shell the smallest J in 3P is lowest. Thus
