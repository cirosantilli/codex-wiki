The 4f7 shell carries a large spin (its ground atomic term under Hund's rules has L=0,S=7/2), and the 6s electron penetrates the inner shells. Its nonzero probability density at the atomic nucleus supplies a potentially large Fermi contact interaction with the nuclear magnetic dipole moment. The 6s shell here is singly occupied, so the obligatory paired-6s2 cancellation is absent. Both stable europium isotopes have nonzero nuclear spin, as the spectrum below also establishes.
The term symbol 8D11/2​ specifies S=7/2, L=2, J=11/2. Its large J, combined with appreciable nuclear spin, permits a wide range of total angular momentum F and hence a substantial total hyperfine structure span. For I=5/2, F=3,…,8 and the span is 30∣a∣. The penetrating 6s contribution and the available range of F explain the large hyperfine structure; the term symbol alone does not determine its constant, which also depends on electronic recoupling, radial wavefunctions and configuration interaction.
