For 5s5d, addition of angular momentum gives L=2 and S=0,1. The even-parity atomic terms and atomic fine-structure levels are
1D2​,3D1​,3D2​,3D3​.​
(5)
The electric-dipole selection rules exclude all connections to the even-parity 5s2 and 5s6s electron configurations. To the odd-parity 5s5p electron configuration, all seven allowed connections in pure LS coupling are
1D2​3D1​3D2​3D3​​↔1P1​,↔3P0​,3P1​,3P2​,↔3P1​,3P2​,↔3P2​.​​
(6)
Here a connection is listed independently of whether the 5s5d energy level is above or below its partner.
In a weak magnetic field, use the Landé g-factor
gJ​=1+2J(J+1)J(J+1)+S(S+1)−L(L+1)​.
(7)
For the three 3DJ​ energy levels, this gives 1/2,7/6,4/3; for 3P1​,3P2​ it gives 3/2. The six-component spectral line is therefore
3D1​↔3P1​.​
(8)
A component has frequency displacement (μB​B/h)(mD​/2−3mP​/2). The electric dipole transition with mD​=mP​=0 has zero matrix element, since the relevant J=1↔1 rank-one Clebsch-Gordan coefficients vanish there. The remaining allowed pairs give
polarizationπσσ​mD​−mP​0+1−1​hΔν/(μB​B)−1,+1+1/2,+3/2−3/2,−1/2​​
(9)
Thus all six frequency positions ±1/2,±1,±3/2 are distinct. Perpendicular to the magnetic field, both the π and σ components are visible, with mutually perpendicular linear polarizations. Along the magnetic field the π components disappear. This is the weak-field Zeeman effect, before the Paschen-Back effect changes the coupling scheme.
