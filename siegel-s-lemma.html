Siegel's lemma

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Number theory Diophantine approximation
OurBigBook.com Words: 418 Articles: 5
Let K be a number field of degree D, and let L1​,…,LM​ be linear forms in N>DM variables with coefficients in K and projective height at most H≥1. There is a nonzero x∈ZN satisfying every Lj​(x)=0 and
∥x∥∞​≤(NH)DM/(N−DM).
(83)
For K=Q, this follows by applying the pigeonhole principle to the images of the integer box {0,…,Y}N under (L1​,…,LM​). Expanding coefficients in a rational basis of K gives the factor D in the general count.
