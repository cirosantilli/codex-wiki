Quantum collision finding

 Home Computer science Theoretical computer science Computational complexity theory Quantum complexity theory Quantum query complexity
OurBigBook.com Words: 167 Articles: 1
Given a function f with an m-bit output, accessed through the unitary operator Uf​∣x⟩∣y⟩=∣x⟩∣y⊕f(x)⟩, quantum collision finding asks for distinct inputs with equal outputs. Here ⊕ is bitwise exclusive or on the m-bit answer quantum register. The distinctness requirement excludes the uninformative pair (x,x). For a two-to-one function on N inputs, Grover search algorithm methods yield a cube-root quantum query complexity, despite a square-root cost for finding a partner of just one preselected input.
