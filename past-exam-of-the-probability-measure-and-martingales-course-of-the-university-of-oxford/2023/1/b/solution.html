For m≥1, fix signs x1​,…,xm​. The constraints Xj​=xj​ leave exactly two choices for the (m+1) signs Y0​,…,Ym​: choose Y0​ freely, then set Y1​=Y0​x1​ and Yj​=xj−1​xj​ for 2≤j≤m. Each input tuple has probability 2−(m+1) by independence. Consequently
P(X1​=x1​,…,Xm​=xm​)=2−m=∏j=1m​P(Xj​=xj​).
(4)
Every finite subfamily is a marginal distribution of one such initial segment, so the entire sequence is independent, with fair sign probability distributions.
Using the usual everywhere sign-valued representatives, for every n≥1 we have
Y0​=Xn​∏j=1n​Yj​.
(5)
The right side is measurable with respect to σ(Y,Tn​). Therefore Y0​ is measurable with respect to their intersection G.
Write T=⋂n​Tn​. The Kolmogorov zero-one law applies to the independent random variables (Xn​), so every event C∈T has probability zero or one. Also Y0​ is independent of Y: this follows first for finite intersections of sign events from the given independence, then for their generated sigma-algebra by part (a).
The sets D∩C with D∈Y and C∈T form a pi-system generating H. If P(C)=0, the event D∩C has probability zero and is independent of every event in σ(Y0​). If P(C)=1, it differs from D by a null set, so the same independence follows from that of Y0​ and Y. Another application of part (a) proves
Y0​ is G-measurable,σ(Y0​) and H are independent.​
(6)
In particular H cannot contain the nontrivial event {Y0​=1}, whereas G does. Thus adjoining a sigma-algebra need not commute with a decreasing intersection.
There is a small version qualification if the assumptions specify only the probability distributions, allowing exceptional values other than signs. Put qn​=Xn​/(Y1​⋯Yn​) when the denominator is nonzero, and qn​=0 otherwise. For k≥n, qk​ is measurable with respect to σ(Y,Tn​). The limit superior of (qk​), after scalar clipping to [−1,1], is therefore G-measurable and equals Y0​ almost surely. Literal measurability of an arbitrary version of Y0​ need not follow without choosing sign-valued representatives or adjoining the ambient null sets. All the independence conclusions are unaffected.
