After τ, the token product remains zero, whereas the deterministic term nN/3 continues to increase. Combining this with the pre-elimination calculation in part (ii),
E[Mn+1​−Mn​∣Fn​]=3N​1{τ≤n}​.​
(45)
The stochastic process is adapted and integrable at each fixed time, and the displayed conditional expectation is nonnegative. Thus (Mn​) is a submartingale.
For N>0 it is neither a martingale nor a supermartingale. If a,b,c>0, there is positive probability that the first player transfers a token to the second in each of the next a rounds. Until the last such round all three remain active, so this event has probability 6−a>0 and entails τ≤a. At time n=a the displayed conditional expectation is therefore strictly positive on an event of positive probability, ruling out both the zero drift required of a martingale and the nonpositive drift required of a supermartingale. If an initial holding is zero, τ=0 and the same conclusion follows immediately. The degenerate case N=0 gives a constant martingale.
