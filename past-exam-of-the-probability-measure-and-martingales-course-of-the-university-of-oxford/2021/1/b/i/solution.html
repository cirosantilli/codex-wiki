For every n, Fn​ is a sigma-algebra contained in F, and including one more random variable among its generators can only enlarge it. Hence Fn​⊆Fn+1​; the trivial F0​ is contained in every sigma-algebra. These are exactly the requirements for a filtration.
The constant X0​=x is F0​-measurable. Suppose Xn​ is Fn​-measurable. The pair (Xn​,Un+1​) is then Fn+1​-measurable, and the event {Un+1​≤Xn​} is Fn+1​-measurable because comparison of two real coordinates defines a Borel set. Define its indicator function by Bn+1​. The recursion becomes
Xn+1​=2Xn​+Bn+1​​.
(7)
It is a measurable function of that pair. Mathematical induction proves Xn​ is Fn​-measurable for every n, so (Xn​) is an adapted process. The same recursion also proves 0<Xn​<1 at every finite time, a useful bound for the later integrability arguments.
