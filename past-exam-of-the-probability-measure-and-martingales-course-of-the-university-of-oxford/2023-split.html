Solutions to every part of all three questions of the probability, measure and martingales course of the University of Oxford Part B examination, sat on 5 June 2023. Source: original released PDF, bundle pages 288–291; paper pages 1–4. The native PDF confirms the endpoint defect in Question 2: its slope formula evaluates f outside [0,1] when X=1. We specify values on that null set below. Question 1(b)'s literal measurability assertion uses everywhere sign-valued random variables; for arbitrary versions with the stated probability distributions, it holds up to null sets, as explained in the solution.
