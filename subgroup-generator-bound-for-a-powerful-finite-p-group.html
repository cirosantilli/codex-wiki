Subgroup generator bound for a powerful finite p-group (d(H)≤d(G))

 ... Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Finite p-group Powerful p-group
OurBigBook.com Words: 88
For odd p, a subgroup H of a finite powerful p-group G satisfies d(H)≤d(G). Here d is the dimension of the Frattini quotient. Set K=H∩Gp, V=G/Gp, W=Gp/Gp2, and A=HGp/Gp. The onto power map θ:V→W sends A into the image of Hp in K/Φ(K). Since Φ(K)≤Gp2, this image has dimension at least dimθ(A). Also Φ(K)≤Φ(H)≤K. Induction on ∣G∣, applied to the powerful subgroup Gp, gives d(K)≤dimW. Consequently
d(H)≤dimA+dimW−dimθ(A)≤dimW+dimkerθ=dimV=d(G).
(2)
The last inequality is rank-nullity theorem.
