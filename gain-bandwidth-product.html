Gain-bandwidth product (ft​)

 ... Branches of physics Electromagnetism Electrical circuit Electronic amplifier Operational amplifier Open-loop gain
OurBigBook.com Words: 70
For a dominant-pole voltage-feedback operational amplifier with A(s)=A0​/(1+s/ωa​) and A0​≫1, the unity-gain angular frequency is approximately ωt​=A0​ωa​. In the range where this pole dominates, a roughly constant closed-loop noise gain GN​ gives a bandwidth approximately ft​/GN​. This approximation depends on the amplifier model and feedback control circuit; it is not a universal statement about all amplifiers or all frequency-dependent feedback networks. [ref]
