Jordan decomposition of a function of bounded variation

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Riemann integration Total variation of a function
OurBigBook.com Words: 32
Every real function f of bounded variation is the difference of two nondecreasing functions. Writing Vf​ for its cumulative total variation, one may take
f+​=2Vf​+f​,f−​=2Vf​−f​,

up to constants chosen for the desired normalization.
