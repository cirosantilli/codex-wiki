Wavefunction matching at a finite potential step (ψ(a−)=ψ(a+),ψ′(a−)=ψ′(a+))

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Schrödinger equation Time-independent Schrödinger equation
OurBigBook.com Words: 254 Articles: 2
For a finite jump in a one-dimensional potential energy, with constant mass and no delta potential, both the wavefunction and its first spatial derivative are continuous. Integrating the time-independent Schrödinger equation across a vanishing interval makes the jump in ψ′ vanish, since the integral of (V−E)ψ vanishes. A jump in ψ itself would produce a derivative of a Dirac delta function in ψ′′, with no matching term in the equation. The second derivative can jump. This matching allows transmission and quantum tunnelling; a finite step is not a hard wall.
