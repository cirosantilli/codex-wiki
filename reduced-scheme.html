Reduced scheme

 ... Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic geometry Ringed space Locally ringed space Scheme
OurBigBook.com Words: 156 Articles: 3
A scheme is reduced when every local ring OX,x​ is a reduced ring, equivalently when its structure sheaf has no nonzero nilpotent element. The affine scheme SpecA is reduced exactly when A is reduced.
