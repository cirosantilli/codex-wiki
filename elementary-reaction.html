Elementary reaction

 Home Chemistry Chemical reaction Chemical kinetics Reaction mechanism
OurBigBook.com Words: 106 Articles: 1
An elementary reaction is a single step in a reaction mechanism. The law of mass action models its directional reaction rate using reactant concentrations raised to the step's stoichiometric multiplicities. Applying that rule requires evidence that the step is elementary; a net chemical equation alone supplies no such evidence.
