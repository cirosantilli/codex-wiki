Killing energy with positive time signature (E=g(P,K))

 ... Physics Branches of physics General relativity Killing vector field Geodesic conserved quantity from a Killing vector Killing energy
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
With metric signature (+−−−) and a future timelike stationary Killing vector K, positive energy is E=g(P,K) for future physical momentum P. A common (−+++) convention instead uses −g(P,K). The normalization of K fixes the observer or asymptotic time scale.
