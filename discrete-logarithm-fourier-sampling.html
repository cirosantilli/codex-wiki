Discrete-logarithm Fourier sampling (c2​≡−yc1​(modM))

For a generator of a group g of order M and x=gy, the function f(a,b)=gax−b hides the subgroup {(yb,b):b∈ZM​} of ZM2​. Each fiber is an affine coset {(yb+k,b):b∈ZM​}. Measuring the function register gives its normalized coset state. Applying the positive-exponent quantum Fourier transform to both input registers gives amplitude
MM​e2πikc1​/M​∑b=0M−1​e2πib(yc1​+c2​)/M.
(47)
The finite geometric series vanishes off c2​=−yc1​ and equals M on that line. Thus each allowed output pair has probability 1/M, independently of the coset offset. If gcd(c1​,M)=1, a modular inverse recovers y=−c2​c1−1​.
