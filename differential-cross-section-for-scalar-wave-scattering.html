Differential cross-section for scalar wave scattering (dσ/dΩ=∣f∞​∣2)

 Home Physics Wave Wave scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 148 Articles: 1
If a unit incident plane wave has scattered field ψs​=eikRf∞​(r,r0​)/R+O(R−2), the differential wave scattering cross-section is
dΩdσ​=∣f∞​(r,r0​)∣2.
(48)
It is the far-field limit of R2 times the scattered-to-incident energy flux ratio. The common physical amplitude normalization cancels when the incident and scattered waves are in the same background medium. The formula assumes that the 1/(4π) Green-function factor, if present, is already included in the far-field pattern.
