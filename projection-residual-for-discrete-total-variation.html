Projection residual for discrete total variation (u=g−PC​g)

For C=D∗{p:∣pi​∣2​≤λ}, discrete total variation denoising is the proximal operator of the support function of C. The Moreau decomposition gives the displayed projection residual. It is generally not itself a metric projection onto a closed convex set: such a projection is idempotent, but TV denoising shrinks a sufficiently large two-level jump again when reapplied.
