Minimal-subtraction two-point counterterms in cubic scalar theory (δZ=−g2/[6(4π)3ϵ],δm2=−g2m2/[(4π)3ϵ])

OurBigBook.com Words: 43
With additive counterterms 21​δZ(∂ϕ)2+21​δm2ϕ2, their Euclidean propagator insertion is −(δZk2+δm2). These minimal subtraction scheme coefficients cancel the cubic bubble insertion. A bare mass shift also subtracts m2δZ after wavefunction renormalization; it is not the same coefficient as the additive mass counterterm.
