Transportation dual certificate with capacity inequalities (ui​≤0,ui​+vj​≤cij​)

 ... Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Linear programming Linear programming duality Transportation problem Transportation dual potentials
OurBigBook.com Words: 74
For shipments xij​≥0 with factory row sums at most capacities si​ and shop column sums exactly demands dj​, the displayed dual potentials give ∑cij​xij​≥∑i​ui​si​+∑j​vj​dj​. The sign ui​≤0 ensures ui​∑j​xij​≥ui​si​. Equality proves optimality. It occurs when every used cell is tight and every row with spare capacity has ui​=0, the relevant complementary slackness conditions. A dummy demand column with zero costs converts unused capacity into a balanced transportation problem.
