Additional conditioning reduces entropy (H(X∣Y,Z)≤H(X∣Y))

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Information theory Conditional entropy Conditioning reduces entropy
OurBigBook.com Words: 101
For finite discrete random variables X,Y,Z, retaining Y and also observing Z gives
H(X∣Y)−H(X∣Y,Z)=I(X;Z∣Y)≥0.
(11)
Equality means conditional independence of X and Z given Y. Both sides average over reports; the additional observation is retained alongside Y.[ref]
A short proof of the original inequality uses Kullback-Leibler divergence:
H(X)−H(X∣Y)=I(X;Y)=∑y:pY​(y)>0​pY​(y)D(pX∣Y=y​∥pX​)≥0.
(12)
Gibbs inequality makes each term nonnegative, vanishing exactly when pX∣Y=y​=pX​: independence for every possible report. Applying this within each Y=y and averaging proves the additional-conditioning inequality.[ref]
