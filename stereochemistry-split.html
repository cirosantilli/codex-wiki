Stereochemistry

 Home Chemistry
OurBigBook.com Words: 4k Articles: 78
Stereochemistry studies the spatial arrangement of chemical atoms in molecules. A Fischer projection records this information at successive stereocenters, whereas a molecular formula only counts chemical atoms. See McMurry: representing carbohydrate stereochemistry.
