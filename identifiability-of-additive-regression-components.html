Identifiability of additive regression components (∑i​fj​(xij​)=0)

 ... Probability and statistics Statistical model Statistical modelling Generalized linear model Generalized additive model Additive regression model
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 1
In an additive regression model, each component can transfer a constant to the regression intercept, so impose empirical mean-zero constraints, or population mean-zero constraints when appropriate. Even after centering, nonzero component perturbations hj​ with ∑j​hj​(xij​)=0 at every observed point make the additive decomposition nonidentifiable. Extra smoothing penalties may resolve the fitted decomposition, but centering alone does not remove concurvity.
