The nonconstant invariant factors of a linear operator satisfy d1​∣⋯∣dk​=mA​. By part (a)(i), mA​=X2+1 is an irreducible polynomial, so every dj​ must equal X2+1. Their polynomial degrees sum to n, giving n=2k.
The companion matrix of X2+1, in the basis (1,X) of Q[X]/(X2+1), is
C=(01​−10​).
(36)
Therefore, for each fixed even n, every such A has rational canonical form
diag(n/2 blocksC,…,C​​)​.
(37)
There are no such matrices when n is odd. This is an instance of the rational canonical form with irreducible minimal polynomial principle; equality of minimal polynomials alone does not determine rational canonical form in general.
