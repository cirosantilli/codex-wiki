Standard basis (e1​,…,en​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Linear algebra Vector space Basis
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The standard basis of the vector space Rn consists of the vectors ei​ whose ith coordinate is one and whose other coordinates are zero. Every vector x has the unique expression x=∑i​xi​ei​ in this basis.
