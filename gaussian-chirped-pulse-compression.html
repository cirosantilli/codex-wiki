The calculation's integrated energy agrees in time and frequency through Parseval's identity, while its centroid and variance agree with these independent moment formulas.

Perfect spectral phase correction cannot recreate the suppressed spectral amplitude. Real gain narrowing and saturation require a material-dependent amplifier model; a constant multiplier is only a limited idealization.
