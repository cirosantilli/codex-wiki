Urysohn function from closure-nested open sets (f(x)=inf{t:x∈Ut​})

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Topology Normal space Urysohn's lemma
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
Let open sets Ut​ be indexed by a dense subset of [0,1], with Us​​⊆Ut​ for s<t. Taking the infimum of membership indices, with value one outside their union, gives a continuous function. The strict sublevel sets are unions of Ut​; strict superlevel sets are unions of complements of Ut​​. These identities prove continuity.
