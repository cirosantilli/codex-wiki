For the structure sheaf of a complex manifold, local holomorphic logarithms give the short exact sequence of sheaves
0→Z​→OM​exp(2πi⋅)​OM∗​→1.
(21)
The connecting map H1(M,OM∗​)→H2(M,Z) sends a holomorphic line bundle to its First Chern class. In particular, it is onto when H2(M,OM​)=0.
