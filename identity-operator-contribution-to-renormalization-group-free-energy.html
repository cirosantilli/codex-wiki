Identity-operator contribution to renormalization-group free energy (f(u)=b−Df(Rb​u)+g(u))

 ... Branches of physics Statistical physics Critical phenomenon Renormalization group Real-space renormalization group Normalized blocking kernel
OurBigBook.com Words: 214 Articles: 2
If an energy per site C multiplies the identity operator, blocking sends C to bDC+c(u). For free energy per site F=C+f(u), exact partition function invariance gives the displayed inhomogeneous equation with g=b−Dc. It records eliminated-mode entropy, vacuum contributions and measure normalization. Removing a suitable regular background leaves homogeneous singular scaling; additive logarithmic resonances can obstruct a strictly analytic subtraction.
