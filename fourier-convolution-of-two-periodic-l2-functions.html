Fourier convolution of two periodic L2 functions (∫−ππ​f(s)g(t−s)ds=∑n​⟨f,en​⟩⟨g,en​⟩eint)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Fourier analysis Convolution Periodic convolution operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 67
With en​(s)=(2π)−1/2eins, periodic L2 functions have the displayed convolution identity for every real t. Apply the polarized Parseval identity to f and g(t−⋅)​; a change of variable gives the latter function's pairing with en​. The Cauchy-Schwarz inequality bounds the sum of absolute coefficient products by ∥f∥2​∥g∥2​, so the resulting series converges absolutely and uniformly. This proves continuity of the convolution even though neither original Fourier series need converge pointwise.
