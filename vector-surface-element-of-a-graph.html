Vector surface element of a graph (dS=(−gx​,−gy​,1)dxdy)

 ... Analysis Real analysis Calculus Multivariable calculus Vector calculus Surface integral
OurBigBook.com Words: 42
The graph z=g(x,y) has upward-oriented vector area element dS=(−gx​,−gy​,1)dxdy and scalar area element dS=1+gx2​+gy2​​dxdy. These follow from the cross product of the parametrization derivatives (1,0,gx​) and (0,1,gy​). The vector element is used in flux surface integrals and Stokes theorem; reversing orientation changes its sign.
