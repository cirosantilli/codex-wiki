Falling factorial ((t)j​=t(t−1)⋯(t−j+1))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Combinatorics Factorial
OurBigBook.com Words: 66
The falling factorial is the product of j successive descending factors, with (t)0​=1. Another notation is tj​; this article uses (t)j​ for descending factors, whereas the rising factorial uses the same notation for ascending factors. For a nonnegative integer t, it counts ordered selections of j distinct objects and vanishes when j>t. Powers expand as tj=∑ℓ=0j​S(j,ℓ)(t)ℓ​, where S(j,ℓ) is a Stirling number of the second kind.
