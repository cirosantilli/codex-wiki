Residual extrema when response extrema are attained (L−M≤emin​≤L−m,U−M≤emax​≤U−m)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Regression residual Residual constraints from a bounded response
OurBigBook.com Words: 87
For a finite sample, let L=mini​yi​, U=maxi​yi​, m=mini​y​i​ and M=maxi​y​i​. The regression residuals are ei​=yi​−y​i​. Every residual is at least L−M and at most U−m. At an observation attaining yi​=L, its residual is at most L−m, so emin​≤L−m. At an observation attaining yi​=U, its residual is at least U−M, so emax​≥U−M. These observations prove all four bounds. Merely knowing theoretical response limits, without attainment, would not justify the two inner bounds.
