Azimuthal brightness of a birefringent crystallite (I∝sin2(2α))

 ... Physics Branches of physics Optics Optical instrument Optical microscope Polarizing microscope
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
Rotating a uniform crystallite around a fixed propagation direction changes the crossed-polar intensity factor sin2(2α). Extinctions repeat every90degrees and maxima occur45degrees away. Similar interference colour can coexist with different brightness if retardation is similar but azimuth differs.
