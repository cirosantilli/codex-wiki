Boundary force in an infinite square well (Pj​=ℏ2∣∂x​ψ(xj​)∣2/(2m))

For a normalized sufficiently regular solution of the Schrödinger equation on [0,L] with Dirichlet walls, the instantaneous outward forces on the walls have magnitudes
P0​=2mℏ2​∣ψ′(0,t)∣2,PL​=2mℏ2​∣ψ′(L,t)∣2.
(106)
A wall displacement changes the kinetic-energy quadratic form by the boundary term −Pj​dLj​, giving the local force. Equivalently, direct integration by parts in the time derivative of the mean particle momentum gives
dtd⟨p⟩​=2mℏ2​(∣ψ′(0,t)∣2−∣ψ′(L,t)∣2)=P0​−PL​.
(107)
The force on the particle is the opposite of the net force −P0​+PL​ on the walls. For a stationary energy eigenstate each magnitude equals 2En​/L. For the equal superposition of the first two box states, writing C=E1​/L and Ω=3E1​/ℏ gives P0​=C(5+4cosΩt) and PL​=C(5−4cosΩt). Both time averages equal 2⟨E⟩/L, the classical mean force on one wall at that energy, even though the quantum forces vary continuously in time.
