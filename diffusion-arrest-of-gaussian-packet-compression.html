Diffusion arrest of Gaussian packet compression (ℓB​∼κ/λ​)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Scalar transport Passive scalar Gaussian scalar packet in a linear incompressible flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 211 Articles: 1
For a chaotic incompressible planar flow with Lyapunov exponent pair λ,−λ, an initially broad Gaussian scalar packet in a linear incompressible flow stretches and compresses exponentially while diffusion is negligible. Its compressed variance stops decreasing around κ/λ, the Batchelor scalar microscale balance, while the larger variance continues to grow. In a constant principal strain, the normalized covariance eigenvalues obey j˙​±​=±2λj±​+2κ, proving that the compressed one approaches κ/λ. Random finite-correlation strain instead gives a fluctuating compressed width; stationarity and integrability assumptions are required to turn this into exponential statistics of the packet amplitude.
