Homotopy

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Algebraic topology
OurBigBook.com Words: 1k Articles: 51
A homotopy between maps f,g:Y→X is a continuous map H:Y×[0,1]→X with H(y,0)=f(y) and H(y,1)=g(y).
