Dirichlet test

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Real analysis Sequence and series Convergent series
OurBigBook.com Words: 84
If the partial sums of ∑bn​ are bounded and an​ decreases to zero, then ∑an​bn​ converges. The uniform version holds for functions bn​(x) when their partial sums have one bound M independent of x. Indeed partial sums starting at index N have absolute value at most 2M, and summation by parts bounds every weighted tail from N to K by 2MaN​. The uniformly Cauchy sequence criterion proves uniform convergence. This justifies integrating a conditionally convergent Fourier series on intervals staying away from its endpoint jump.
