Matter-era growing and decaying density modes (δ=Aτ2+Bτ−3)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Linear cosmological density perturbation
OurBigBook.com Words: 111 Articles: 2
During matter domination in a flat FLRW metric, the cosmological continuity equation gives ρ∝a−3, and the Friedmann equation gives a∝τ2 and 8πGρa2=12/τ2. The CDM density equation in a matter-radiation universe, with radiation neglected, is therefore δ′′+2δ′/τ−6δ/τ2=0. Its Euler-Cauchy equation powers satisfy (p−2)(p+3)=0, proving the displayed general solution. The growing density contrast is proportional to the scale factor, while the decaying mode is proportional to a−3/2. This all-scale form uses the CDM-comoving synchronous gauge; other density slices require their corresponding gauge transformation.
