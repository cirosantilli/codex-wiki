Linearized curvature of a two-potential weak-field metric (ds2=−(1+2Φ/c2)(dx0)2+(1−2Ψ/c2)dx2)

For metric signature (−,+,+,+), x0=ct and small dimensionless potentials, the metric perturbation is h00​=−2Φ/c2, hij​=−2Ψδij​/c2. Substitution into the linearized Ricci tensor and scalar formula gives
R00​=c2∇2Φ+3∂02​Ψ​,R0i​=c22∂0​∂i​Ψ​,Rij​=c2∂i​∂j​(Ψ−Φ)+δij​(∇2Ψ−∂02​Ψ)​.
(11)
The contraction is R=(4∇2Ψ−2∇2Φ−6∂02​Ψ)/c2, so G00​=−2∇2Ψ/c2. These signs use Rμναβ​Vν=[∇α​,∇β​]Vμ. Static zero-anisotropic-stress boundary conditions give Φ=Ψ and the Newtonian limit; without those assumptions the two potentials and their time derivatives must be retained.
