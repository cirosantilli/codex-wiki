Polynomial evaluation (Ea​(p)=p(a))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Polynomial
OurBigBook.com Words: 94 Articles: 1
Polynomial evaluation replaces the indeterminate of a polynomial by an input value. For a fixed scalar a, the rule Ea​:p↦p(a) is a linear functional on a vector space of polynomials, because evaluating a linear combination gives the same linear combination of the values. For p(t)=1+2t+3t2, evaluation at zero gives one and evaluation at two gives seventeen. Polynomial interpolation uses these measurements to reconstruct a polynomial subject to a polynomial degree bound.
