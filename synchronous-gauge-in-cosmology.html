Synchronous gauge in cosmology

 Home Physics Branches of physics Cosmology Linear cosmological perturbation theory Scalar cosmological perturbation
OurBigBook.com Words: 451 Articles: 8
Synchronous gauge sets the lapse and shift perturbations to zero, h0μ​=0. Its coordinates follow freely falling observers, but residual coordinate freedom produces gauge modes that must be distinguished from physical perturbations.
