Stopped process (Xtτ​=Xt∧τ​)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Martingale Stopping time
OurBigBook.com Words: 93 Articles: 1
The stopped stochastic process follows X until the stopping time τ and thereafter keeps the value Xτ​ when τ is finite. For a pathwise right-continuous adapted process, the stopped process is adapted, by progressive measurability and measurable evaluation at t∧τ. When X is a martingale, further stopping and integrability conditions determine whether its stopped process is a martingale.
