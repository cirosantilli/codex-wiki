Newton root-finding iteration (tr+1​=tr​−F(tr​)/F′(tr​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Numerical analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 268 Articles: 4
The Newton root-finding iteration solves the local tangent-line approximation to a scalar equation F(t)=0. For a twice continuously differentiable function near a simple root, with derivative bounded away from zero, Taylor's theorem gives ∣tr+1​−t∗​∣≤C∣tr​−t∗​∣2 once the iteration is sufficiently close. Global convergence is not automatic. A sign-changing bracket can safeguard the method by replacing unsuitable steps by the bisection method.
