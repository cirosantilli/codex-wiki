Spatial resolution

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics
OurBigBook.com Words: 48
The ability of an imaging or measurement system to distinguish nearby spatial features. A resolution criterion must specify the features and measurement conditions: diffraction, noise and sampling can all limit the distinction. A small pixel spacing alone does not establish high spatial resolution; electron microscope resolution illustrates the role of wavelength and numerical aperture.
