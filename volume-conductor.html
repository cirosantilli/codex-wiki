Volume conductor

 Home Physics Branches of physics Electromagnetism Bioelectromagnetism
OurBigBook.com Words: 273 Articles: 4
A volume conductor is a conducting three-dimensional medium through which electric currents flow. In the quasistatic bioelectric model, the total current density is J=Jp−σ∇u, comprising an impressed source current and its Ohmic return current. Electric charge conservation gives ∇⋅(σ∇u)=∇⋅Jp. An insulating boundary has zero normal total current.
