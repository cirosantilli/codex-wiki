The word "projection" does not mean ordinary perpendicular projection in the plotted Euclidean space. For the same reference and mean constraint, minimizing the squared Euclidean distance in the three probability coordinates instead gives
∑i​(qi​−1/3)2=6x2−x+61​,xE​=121​.
(90)
Thus the Euclidean minimizer is qE​=(1/12,1/3,7/12), whereas the information projection has q−∗​≈0.116204. The squared Euclidean objective at its own minimum is 1/8; its relative entropy is approximately 0.210918 nats, larger than the optimum 0.197378 nats. Both distributions satisfy the same constraint.
The reference and objective encode what "least change" means. Relative entropy is asymmetric and is not a metric; its minimization answers a different question from linear least squares. The useful geometric analogy comes from a Pythagorean inequality for information projection, rather than from an assertion that the colored contours are circles.
