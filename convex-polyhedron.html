Convex polyhedron

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Geometry and topology Euclidean geometry Polyhedron
OurBigBook.com Words: 81 Articles: 1
A polyhedron that is also a convex set. A bounded full-dimensional convex hull of finitely many points is a convex polyhedron; each face lies in a supporting plane, with the whole solid in its inward half-space.
