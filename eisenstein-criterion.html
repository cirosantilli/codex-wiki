Eisenstein criterion

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Commutative algebra
OurBigBook.com Words: 155 Articles: 2
For a primitive polynomial
F(X)=an​Xn+⋯+a0​∈Z[X],
(39)
if a prime p does not divide an​, divides every aj​ for j<n, and has p2∤a0​, then F is irreducible. If F=GH, reduction modulo p forces both G and H to be positive-degree monomials. Their constant terms are consequently divisible by p, making p2∣F(0), a contradiction. Gauss lemma for polynomials transfers the result between Z[X] and Q[X].
