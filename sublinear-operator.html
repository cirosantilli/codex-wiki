Sublinear operator

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Analysis Functional analysis
OurBigBook.com Words: 370 Articles: 6
A nonnegative function-valued operator S is sublinear if S(f+g)≤S(f)+S(g) and S(cf)=∣c∣S(f) almost everywhere. This pointwise domination notion includes maximal one-sided interval averaging operators and differs from requiring an ordinary linear map.
