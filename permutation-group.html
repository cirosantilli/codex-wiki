Permutation group

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Algebra Group theory Finite group theory Symmetric group
OurBigBook.com Words: 104 Articles: 1
A subgroup of the symmetric group of a set, acting faithfully by permutations. A finite permutation group specified by generators can be enumerated by repeatedly multiplying the elements already found by those generators until no new element appears. Its orbits and conjugacy classes can then be computed directly.
