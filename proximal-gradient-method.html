Proximal gradient method

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Mathematical optimization Convex optimization Proximal operator
OurBigBook.com Words: 270 Articles: 4
The proximal gradient method minimizes g+h, where g has an L-Lipschitz gradient and h is convex with a tractable proximal operator, by xr+1​=proxαh​(xr​−α∇g(xr​)). A standard choice 0<α≤1/L gives objective error O(1/r) in the general convex case.
This basic forward-backward update is one of the proximal gradient methods for learning.
