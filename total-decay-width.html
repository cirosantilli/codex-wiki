Total decay width (Γtot​=∑i​Γi​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Relativistic quantum field Decay width
OurBigBook.com Words: 34
The sum of the partial decay widths over mutually exclusive final channels. It determines the unstable state's mean lifetime through τ=ℏ/Γtot​. A new open channel increases the total width even when an existing channel's partial width remains unchanged.
