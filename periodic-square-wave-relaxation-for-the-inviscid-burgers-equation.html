Periodic square-wave relaxation for the inviscid Burgers equation (u(x,t)=21​+t−1({x−t/2}−21​),t≥1)

Take period-one data equal to one on [k−1/2,k] and zero on (k,k+1/2). Before t=1, the separate rectangular pulse solutions of the inviscid Burgers equation do not overlap in their interiors. At t=1 each shock meets the two neighboring fans. Afterward, on the interval between shocks sk−1​ and sk​, the solution remains (x−k+1/2)/t. The Rankine-Hugoniot condition gives sk′​=(sk​−k)/t and sk​(1)=k+1/2, hence sk​=k+t/2.
The title formula uses the fractional part and chooses right-hand shock values. Its left and right states are 1/2+1/(2t) and 1/2−1/(2t), making every shock a causal entropy shock of speed 1/2. Therefore supx​∣u(x,t)−1/2∣≤1/(2t): the periodic entropy solution converges uniformly to its conserved mean even though it has a jump at every finite time. Periodizing a nonlinear solution is valid here only before the supports interact; after interaction the matching shock calculation is essential.
