Holomorphic p-form on a complex manifold (α∈H0(X,ΩXp​))

OurBigBook.com Words: 96 Articles: 1
A differential form of type (p, q) of type (p,0) is holomorphic when its local coefficients in holomorphic coordinates are holomorphic functions, equivalently ∂ˉα=0. It need not be d-closed on every complex manifold. Top-degree holomorphic forms are closed by bidegree. On a compact complex surface, holomorphic forms of all degrees are closed, even without a Kähler metric.
