Stokes Raman scattering (νs​=ν0​−νv​)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Optics Scattering of light Raman scattering
OurBigBook.com Words: 57
In Stokes Raman scattering, matter gains internal energy and the photon loses the same amount. For a quantum harmonic oscillator representing one molecular vibration, Ev​=hνv​(v+1/2) with v=0,1,…. The transition v→v+1 gives νs​=ν0​−νv​, requiring ν0​>νv​ for an outgoing photon. Even a molecule initially in its vibrational ground state can undergo this process. The shared name does not turn it into the absorption/emission-band Stokes shift used for fluorescence.
