Constant-flux porous gravity current (tc​=ϕKb​Q/U3)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Porous-media flow Porous gravity current Porous gravity current with background flow
OurBigBook.com Words: 108 Articles: 1
Constant actual volume input Q into a porous gravity current with background flow gives early symmetric scales h∼[(Q/ϕ)2t/a]1/3 and x∼[a(Q/ϕ)t2]1/3. For U>0, drift dominates after tc​=ϕKb​Q/U3. The late interior depth is Q/U and the steady upstream edge is x−​=−Kb​Q/U2, with upstream linear profile h=U(x−x−​)/Kb​. Upstream volume flux is zero because background drift balances gravity spreading.
