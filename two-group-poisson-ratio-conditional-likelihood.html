For independent aggregate Poisson distributions with means nλ and nλeβ, conditioning on the total eliminates λ. Independent Poisson conditioning gives the displayed binomial distribution. Its conditional likelihood is proportional to eβY1​(1+eβ)−m, also the profile likelihood after maximizing over the unrestricted positive baseline rate. Unequal exposures replace the binomial success probability by n1​eβ/(n0​+n1​eβ).
