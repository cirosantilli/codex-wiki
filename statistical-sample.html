Statistical sample ((X1​,…,Xn​))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference
OurBigBook.com Words: 215 Articles: 3
A collection of observed random variables used to infer a statistical model or its parameters. A common assumption is that the observations are independent and identically distributed random variables; dependence must otherwise be included in the model. Its randomness induces the sampling distribution of a statistic.
