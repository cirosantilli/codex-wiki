Dust-grain Eddington limit (LEdd,d​)

 ... Branches of physics Astrophysics Stellar astrophysics Stellar structure Mass-luminosity relation Eddington luminosity
OurBigBook.com Words: 82
An isolated compact dust grain of radius rd​ and material density ρd​ has opacity per unit dust mass κd​=3Qpr​/(4ρd​rd​), where Qpr​ is its radiation-pressure efficiency. Its Eddington luminosity is 4πGMc/κd​. Matching the electron-scattering opacity gives rd,crit​=3Qpr​/(4ρd​κes​), approximately 1.9cm for Qpr​=1, ρd​=1gcm−3, and κes​=0.4cm2g−1. At that electron-scattering luminosity, smaller grains feel a net outward force and larger grains remain attracted. This is an isolated-grain force balance, not the opacity of an arbitrary gas-dust mixture.
