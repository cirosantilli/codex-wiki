Sections of the degree-one line bundle on the projective line (H0(CP1,O(1))≅C2)

 ... Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Holomorphic vector bundle Holomorphic line bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 73
A global holomorphic section of O(1) on CP1 is a linear homogeneous polynomial in the two projective coordinates. On one patch its coefficient is an entire function p(λ); regularity on the other patch says p(λ)/λ is regular at infinity. Hence p has at most linear growth. Cauchy estimates make its second and higher derivatives zero, so p=a+bλ. The same argument entrywise applies to matrix-valued sections and makes the logarithmic derivative in the Penrose-Ward correspondence linear in the primed two-component spinor.
