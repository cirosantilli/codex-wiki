Holding time

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Markov process Markov chain Continuous-time Markov chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 113 Articles: 2
The holding time of a continuous-time Markov chain is the time spent in its current state before the next jump. In state i it has an exponential distribution of rate equal to the total outgoing rate qi​.
