Atmospheric carbon-to-oxygen ratio (C/O)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Astrophysics Exoplanet Exoplanet atmosphere Atmospheric metallicity of a giant planet
OurBigBook.com Words: 88 Articles: 1
The atmospheric carbon-to-oxygen ratio compares the elemental abundances of carbon and oxygen in the atmosphere. It influences molecular composition under chemical equilibrium, but condensation, disequilibrium chemistry in an exoplanet atmosphere, and incomplete spectral coverage complicate its inference from molecular bands.
