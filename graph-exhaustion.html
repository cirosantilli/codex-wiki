Graph exhaustion (V1​⊆V2​⊆⋯,⋃n​Vn​=V)

 ... Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Graph theory Degree (graph theory) Locally finite graph
OurBigBook.com Words: 49
An exhaustion of a countable graph is an increasing sequence of finite connected graph vertex sets whose union is the whole graph vertex set. In a locally finite graph, every prescribed finite graph path together with all graph neighbours of its visited graph vertices lies in a sufficiently large exhaustion set. This permits coupling finite-volume and infinite-volume simple random walks up to a finite stopping time.
