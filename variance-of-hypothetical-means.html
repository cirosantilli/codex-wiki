Variance of hypothetical means (a=Var(E[X∣Θ]))

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Actuarial statistics Bühlmann model
OurBigBook.com Words: 37
The variance of hypothetical means in the Bühlmann model is the between-risk variance of the conditional claim expected value. It creates the shared covariance a between different years of the same risk and determines how much individual experience should influence the premium.
