With Fourier convention cn​=N−1/2∑k​e−iknck​, the Transverse-field Ising model has H=J∑k​Ψk†​[(g−cosk)σz​−sinkσx​]Ψk​. Expanding the spinor retains the constant −JgN and pairs k with −k. The physical quasiparticle energy is twice the positive matrix eigenvalue times J, because both momentum partners occur in the Nambu sum.
