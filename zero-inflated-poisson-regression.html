Zero-inflated Poisson regression (logμ=xTβ,logitπ=zTγ)

 ... Probability and statistics Probability theory Probability distribution Discrete probability distribution Poisson distribution Zero-inflated Poisson distribution
OurBigBook.com Words: 250 Articles: 3
A zero-inflated Poisson regression specifies a structural-zero probability π and a susceptible-component Poisson distribution mean μ. In this parametrization,
P(Y=0)=π+(1−π)e−μ,P(Y=y)=(1−π)e−μμy/y!(y>0).
(12)
The mean is (1−π)μ. Logistic predictors for π and logarithmic predictors for μ allow covariates to affect component membership and count intensity differently. A susceptible individual can still generate a zero count; susceptibility is not equivalent to an observed positive response.
