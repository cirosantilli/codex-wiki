Irreducible characters of the affine semidirect product of orders eleven and five

OurBigBook.com Words: 50
For G=C11​⋊C5​ with faithful action, the abelianization G/C11​≅C5​ supplies five linear characters. The two orbits of size five on the nontrivial characters of C11​ induce two irreducible characters of degree five. Their squared degrees satisfy
5⋅12+2⋅52=55,
(78)
so these seven characters are all the irreducible characters of G.
