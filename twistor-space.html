Twistor space (T≅C4)

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Twistor theory
OurBigBook.com Words: 433 Articles: 8
In flat twistor theory, a twistor is a pair Z=(ωA,πA′​) of two-component spinors. The twistor incidence relation with a spacetime point is ωA=ixAA′πA′​. The nonzero vector Z and its projective class play different roles: the latter belongs to projective twistor space.
