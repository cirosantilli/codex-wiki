Canonical gradient (φeff​=ΠT​φ)

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Statistical inference Statistical functional Pathwise differentiability of a statistical functional Influence-function representer
OurBigBook.com Words: 53
The canonical gradient is the influence-function representer in the statistical tangent space T. It equals the orthogonal projection onto T of any representer. Every other representer differs from it by an element of T⊥, so the Pythagorean theorem in an inner-product space gives it minimum variance. For ψ(P)=Pa in an unrestricted density model with bounded a, it is a−Pa.
