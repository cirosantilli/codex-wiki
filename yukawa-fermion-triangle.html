Yukawa fermion triangle (tr[(a+m)(b+m)(c+m)]=4m[m2+a⋅b+a⋅c+b⋅c])

 ... Physics Branches of physics Quantum field theory Perturbative quantum field theory Feynman diagram Fermion loop
OurBigBook.com Words: 126 Articles: 1
Three scalar Yukawa interactions on a closed Dirac field loop give two cyclic orientations. Odd gamma matrix traces vanish, while tr(γμγν)=4gμν gives the displayed numerator. For external invariant mass squares M2,μ2,μ2, write Da​=a2−m2 and similarly for the other two lines. The numerator becomes 4m[Da​+Db​+Dc​+4m2−μ2−M2/2], reducing the fermion loop to three scalar bubbles and a scalar triangle Feynman integral. The closed fermion loop sign must be included. A massive fermion produces a logarithmic local divergence.
