Bullet Cluster

A merging galaxy cluster exhibiting the bullet Cluster dark-matter separation: peaks of weak gravitational lensing mass lie nearer the galaxies than the displaced X-ray gas. Combining the components supports a dominant less-collisional gravitating component under the usual gravitational model.[ref]
