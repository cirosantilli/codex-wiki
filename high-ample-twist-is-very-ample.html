High ample twist is very ample

 ... Algebraic geometry Ringed space Sheaf of modules Locally free sheaf Line bundle Ample line bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 33
If A is an ample line bundle on a projective variety and L is any line bundle, then A⊗m⊗L is very ample for all sufficiently large integers m. Taking L to be trivial also makes A⊗m very ample.
