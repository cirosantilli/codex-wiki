Set δ=2κ/α​, R=αh/V and S=L/[cp​(T0​−TE​)]. In the regular limit R≫S≫1, at fixed finite thermal strain number αh2/κ and bounded phase fraction, the heat equation reduces to κT′′+αzT′=0. Integration gives the displayed error function profile. Matching the roof temperature gradient G yields
G=π​δ2(T0​−TE​)​erf(h/δ)e−(h/δ)2​.

The product composition is Cs​=C0​−α(T0​−TE​)δ[1−e−(h/δ)2]/[mVπ​erf(h/δ)]. The asymptotic reduction requires admissible phase fractions. If the thermal strain number grows singularly with R, the two ordering inequalities alone do not control boundary-layer errors; the full strain-driven steady mush equations retain the latent-heat term.
