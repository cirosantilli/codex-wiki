Five-dimensional equal-spin Myers-Perry black hole

 Home Physics Branches of physics General relativity Black hole Myers-Perry black hole
OurBigBook.com Words: 61
A five-dimensional Myers-Perry black hole with equal rotational parameters has a metric depending on a single radial coordinate. In a fibered coordinate system, its radial functions can be written f=1−r02​/r2+α2r02​/r4, h=1+α2r02​/r4, and Ω=αr02​/(r4h). For 0<α<r0​/2 there are outer and inner Killing horizons; at equality they form a degenerate Killing horizon. With the standard sphere identifications and G5​=1, its Komar mass is 3πr02​/8.
