A single Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution has a nonzero slope at every finite nonzero u and has no velocity plateau. The would-be electron acoustic wave is therefore subject to strong Landau damping, so there is no corresponding completely undamped acoustic branch in a single Maxwellian electron population with immobile ions. At its only stationary point u=0, a Maxwellian has ∫f0′​(v)/vdv<0, making the static dielectric function positive rather than zero. This statement does not exclude acoustic modes of suitably distinct electron populations or an ion-supported mode when ion dynamics is retained.
