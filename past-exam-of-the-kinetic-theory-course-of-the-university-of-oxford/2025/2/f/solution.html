For k>0, the upper Langmuir wave starts at ω≈+ωpe​ and approaches the line ω=kV from above. The lower Langmuir wave starts at ω≈−ωpe​ and approaches ω=−kv0​ from below. The electron acoustic wave starts at the origin with slope v1​+δv12​/v02​ and approaches ω=kv1​ from above; it stays inside kv1​<ω<kv2​. These are the only completely undamped branches, as shown by the cubic equation in b.
The small asymmetry between the outer branches reflects the extra positive-velocity population introduced by the velocity plateau. The negative-k sketch follows by rotating the displayed branches through the origin, since ϵ(−ω,−k)=ϵ(ω,k) on these undamped solutions. One should not impose symmetry about the ω=0 axis at fixed positive k.
