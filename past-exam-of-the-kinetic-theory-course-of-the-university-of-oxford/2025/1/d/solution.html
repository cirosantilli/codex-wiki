Write ci​=vi​−ui​ and introduce the raw third velocity moment Mijk​ and the third central velocity moment Qijk​:
Mijk​=∫vi​vj​vk​fd3v,Qijk​=∫ci​cj​ck​fd3v.​
(14)
The expansion of their product gives
Mijk​=ρui​uj​uk​+ui​Pjk​+uj​Pik​+uk​Pij​+Qijk​.
(15)
The heat flux is qi​=Qijj​/2 for unit mass.
Multiply the BGK model by vi​vj​. The external force term is
−∂k​U∫vi​vj​∂vk​​fd3v=ρ(uj​∂i​U+ui​∂j​U).
(16)
Therefore the raw second velocity moment satisfies
∂t​Πij​+∂k​Mijk​=−ρ(ui​∂j​U+uj​∂i​U)−τΠij​−Πij(0)​​.
(17)
The matching local Maxwellian has Πij(0)​=ρui​uj​+ρΘδij​, so
Πij​−Πij(0)​=Pij​−Pij(0)​,Pij(0)​=ρΘδij​.
(18)
To remove the bulk motion, let Dt​=∂t​+uk​∂k​ be the material derivative. The continuity equation and the momentum conservation equation imply
ρDt​ui​=−∂k​Pik​−ρ∂i​U,
(19)
and hence
∂t​(ρui​uj​)+∂k​(ρui​uj​uk​)=−uj​∂k​Pik​−ui​∂k​Pjk​−ρ(ui​∂j​U+uj​∂i​U).
(20)
Subtract this identity from the raw second velocity moment equation and insert the expansion of Mijk​. The terms ui​∂k​Pjk​ and uj​∂k​Pik​ cancel by the product rule. The external force terms also cancel, since a common acceleration changes the local mean velocity without directly changing the relative velocities. What remains is
∂t​Pij​+∂k​(uk​Pij​+Qijk​)+Pik​∂k​uj​+Pjk​∂k​ui​=−τPij​−ρΘδij​​.​
(21)
The trace of the relaxation term is zero because Pii​=3ρΘ. Thus the BGK model relaxes anisotropy of the pressure tensor while preserving the local random kinetic energy. The evolution is still a velocity-moment hierarchy, since Qijk​ requires further information about f.
