Define the unperturbed orbital angular frequency vector by
Ω(J)=∂J∂H0​​.​
(33)
It is the rate of advance of the angle variables along the unperturbed Hamiltonian flow; the action variables are constant. Expand the Collisionless Boltzmann equation to first order in the perturbations. Since f0​ and H0​ depend only on the action variables, [f0​,H0​]=0, and
∂t​δf+[δf,H0​]+[f0​,δΦ]=0,∂t​δf+Ω⋅∂θ​δf−∂J​f0​⋅∂θ​δΦ=0.
(34)
The omitted Poisson bracket [δf,δΦ] is quadratic in the perturbations. Use the Fourier series convention
δf=∑k∈Z3​δfk​(J,t)eik⋅θ,δΦ=∑k∈Z3​δΦk​(J,t)eik⋅θ.
(35)
For each harmonic the linear ordinary differential equation is
∂t​δfk​+ik⋅Ωδfk​=ik⋅∂J​f0​δΦk​.
(36)
Multiplication by the integrating factor eik⋅Ωt and use of δfk​(0)=0 give the causal linear response in action-angle variables:
δfk​(J,t)=ik⋅∂J​f0​∫0t​δΦk​(J,t′)e−ik⋅Ω(t−t′)dt′.​
(37)
The zero harmonic stays zero at this order. This formula retains the entire forcing history; replacing it immediately by a resonant Dirac delta function would discard the transient response.
