Screening-length convention. The source defines λDeexam​=3​Ve​/ωpe​, so its long-wavelength condition is equivalent to κ≪1. We retain that definition when expressing answers in its symbols. The actual electron Thomas-Fermi screening length obtained from the static response is instead
ϵe​(0,k)=1+k2Ve2​3ωpe2​​=1+k2λTF2​1​,λTF​=3​ωpe​Ve​​=3λDeexam​​.​
(27)
For a concrete comparison, at k=ωpe​/Ve​, the static dielectric function is 4; using the printed length as the physical Thomas-Fermi screening length would give 1+1/3=4/3. Calling the printed length the Debye length thus differs from the physical degenerate-electron screening convention by a factor of three. It does not affect the boxed dimensional dispersion relation.
