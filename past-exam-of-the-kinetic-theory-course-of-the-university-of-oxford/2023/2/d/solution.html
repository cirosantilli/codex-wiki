For the positive-frequency branch write s=1+δ, δ>0, and Q=k2Ve2​/(3ωpe2​)=κ2/3. The degenerate-plasma dielectric response is equivalent to
1+21+δ​log2+δδ​=−Q.
(28)
At large Q, a logarithmic divergence is needed to balance the right-hand side; hence δ≪1. Keeping the constant as well as the large term gives
log2δ​=−2(1+Q)+O(δ∣logδ∣),δ∼2exp[−2−3ωpe2​2k2Ve2​​].​
(29)
More precisely the relative correction is O((1+Q)e−2(1+Q)). The additive constant −2 in the exponent fixes the leading prefactor 2e−2 and should not be discarded.
The negative-frequency branch follows from the even real dielectric function outside the occupied interval. Thus
ω±​=±kVe​(1+δ),δ∼2exp[−2−92​(kλDeexam​)2].​
(30)
Using the physical Thomas-Fermi screening length instead gives δ∼2e−2−2(kλTF​)2. This is degenerate-plasma zero-point sound: the phase velocity lies exponentially close to, but strictly beyond, the edge of the Fermi sphere for every finite k in this approximation. The limit is a property of the supplied classical-trajectory kinetic theory; applying it at microscopic wavelengths would require checking quantum recoil, which that equation omits.
