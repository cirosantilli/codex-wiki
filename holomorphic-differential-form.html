Holomorphic differential form

 ... Complex geometry Complex structure Almost complex manifold Integrable almost complex structure Complex manifold Canonical bundle
OurBigBook.com Words: 606 Articles: 15
On a Riemann surface, a holomorphic differential form is a holomorphic section of the canonical bundle. In a local complex coordinate z it has the form f(z)dz with f holomorphic.
