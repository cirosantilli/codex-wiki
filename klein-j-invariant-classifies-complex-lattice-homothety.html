Klein j-invariant classifies complex lattice homothety (SL2​(Z)\Hj​C)

 ... Area of mathematics Number theory Modular function Modular form Meromorphic modular form Klein j-invariant
OurBigBook.com Words: 76
The valence formula for the modular group shows that Δ=(E43​−E62​)/1728 has no zeros in the half-plane. For every a∈C, the weight-twelve form E43​−aΔ has total weighted zero order one. At a=0 its sole zero is the order-three zero at the cubic elliptic orbit; at a=1728 its sole zero is the order-two zero at the quadratic elliptic orbit. Otherwise it has one simple ordinary zero orbit. Thus each value of the Klein j-invariant occurs on exactly one modular orbit and classifies one homothety of complex lattices.
