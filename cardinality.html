Cardinality (∣X∣)

 Home Mathematics Area of mathematics Foundations of mathematics Set theory Cardinal number
OurBigBook.com Words: 25
The cardinality ∣X∣ of a set X is the cardinal number shared by all sets in bijection with X. For a finite set, it is the number of elements.
