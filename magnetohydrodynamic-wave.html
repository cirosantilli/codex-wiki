Magnetohydrodynamic wave (MHD wave)

 ... Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Astrophysical fluid dynamics Magnetohydrodynamics Ideal magnetohydrodynamics
OurBigBook.com Words: 2k Articles: 35
A magnetohydrodynamic wave is a linear disturbance of a conducting fluid and its magnetic field. A uniform ideal plasma supports an Alfvén mode and fast and slow magnetosonic modes.
