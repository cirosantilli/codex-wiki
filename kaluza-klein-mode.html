Kaluza-Klein mode (mℓ​=2π∣ℓ∣/L)

 Home Physics Mathematical physics Compactification (physics)
OurBigBook.com Words: 327 Articles: 5
A Kaluza-Klein mode is a coefficient in a field expansion in eigenfunctions on compact extra dimensions. On a circle of circumference L, momentum is 2πℓ/L, and a higher-dimensional massless field has lower-dimensional squared mass (2πℓ/L)2. Nonzero modes give exponentially suppressed static-potential corrections at distances much larger than L.
