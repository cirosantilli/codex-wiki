Domain Markov property of a chordal Loewner chain

 ... Area of mathematics Probability and statistics Probability theory Stochastic process Schramm–Loewner evolution Loewner chain
OurBigBook.com Words: 137 Articles: 1
Conditionally on the entire past up to t, the future domains mapped and centered by gt​−Ut​ have the original chain law and are independent of that past. For Schramm–Loewner evolution this follows from independent stationary Brownian motion increments and the composition rule for chordal Loewner driving functions. The Strong Markov property gives the version at almost surely finite stopping times.
