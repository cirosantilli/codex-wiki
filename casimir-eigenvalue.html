Casimir eigenvalue (cλ​=(λ,λ+2ρ))

 ... Algebra Diagonal dominance Lie theory Lie algebra Semisimple Lie algebra Casimir element
OurBigBook.com Words: 36
The Casimir operator formed from the Killing form acts on an Irreducible Lie algebra representation with highest weight λ by the scalar (λ,λ+2ρ), where ρ is the half-sum of positive roots. It is zero on the trivial Lie algebra representation and nonzero on every nontrivial finite-dimensional irreducible complex representation of a semisimple Lie algebra.
