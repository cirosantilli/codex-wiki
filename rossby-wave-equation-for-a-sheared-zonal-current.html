Rossby-wave equation for a sheared zonal current ((∂t​+U∂x​)(∇h2​−RD−2​)ϕ+Qy​ϕx​=0)

 Home Physics Branches of physics Fluid mechanics Geophysical fluid dynamics Rossby wave
OurBigBook.com Words: 141 Articles: 2
Linearizing shallow-water quasi-geostrophic potential vorticity about a zonal current U(y) gives Qy​=β−U′′+U/RD2​. A normal mode ϕ​(y)ei(kx−ωt) obeys (U−ω/k)[ϕ​′′−(k2+RD−2​)ϕ​]+Qy​ϕ​=0. A global transverse plane wave is generally unavailable for an arbitrary nonuniform jet; boundary conditions or a local short-wavelength approximation are required.
