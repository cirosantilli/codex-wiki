Gamow-Teller transition (MGT​=⟨f∣∑k​σk​τk±​∣i⟩)

 Home Physics Nuclear physics Radioactive decay Beta decay Beta decay transition
OurBigBook.com Words: 40
The spin-dependent axial-vector contribution to an allowed beta decay. Its rotational rank is one, permitting ΔJ=0,1 with unchanged parity, but excluding 0→0. Here σk​ acts on nucleon spin and τk±​ on isospin. Higher-orbital spin-dependent contributions are conventionally described as Gamow-Teller-type forbidden transitions.
