Flow definition of the Lie derivative of a tensor field (LX​T=dtd​​0​ϕt∗​T)

 ... Fiber bundle Vector bundle Tensor product of vector bundles Tensor field Tensor derivation Lie derivative of a tensor field
OurBigBook.com Words: 62
For the local flow ϕt​ of a smooth vector field X, pull a tensor field T back to the fixed base point before differentiating. Pullback uses dϕt−1​ on vector factors and the dual of dϕt​ on covector factors. The chain rule gives LX​f=Xf. In coordinates, dϕt−1​Y(ϕt​(x))=Y+t(dYX−dXY)+O(t2), so LX​Y=[X,Y]. Differentiation of pullback preserves tensor contractions and obeys the Leibniz rule for tensor products; hence this is the tensor derivation defining the Lie derivative of a tensor field.
